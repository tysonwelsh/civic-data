Welcome
Mayor Mike Caldwell welcomed Wasatch Front Regional Council members and guests and
introductions were made.
Consent Agenda [00:02:38]
ACTION: Minutes and Financial Statements
Councilmember Michael Jensen moved to approve the minutes of the WFRC meeting held March
23, 2017, the financial statements for March and April and the Budget Expenditure Report to date.
Mayor Tom Dolan seconded the motion. All items were accepted unanimously.
Chairman’s Report [00:03:01]
Information: Transportation Governance and Funding Task Force
Mayor Caldwell introduced Task Force co-Chair, Representative Mike Schultz, who gave an
overview of the work of the Task Force thus far. He, along with other WFRC members who are
serving on the Task Force will continue to update the Council on the progress and invited
discussion about the goals and objectives of the group.
Public Comment [00:32:17]
Mayor Caldwell opened the meeting up to public comment. There were no public comments.
Budget Committee [00:32:30]
Mayor Caldwell turned the time to Mayor Tom Dolan, Chair of the Budget Committee. Mayor
Dolan noted that a public hearing was needed regarding the WFRC FY18 Budget and Unified
Planning Work Program (UPWP) and called for a motion. Mayor Ben McAdams motioned to open
the public hearing and Councilmember Michael Jensen seconded the motion. The affirmative vote
was unanimous. Mayor Dolan explained the updates to the budget asked the group if there were
any public comments. Since no questions or comments regarding the documents were raised,
Mayor JoAnn Seghini motioned to close the public hearing and Commissioner Bret Millburn
seconded. The affirmative vote was unanimous. [00:34:55] Mayor Dolan then motioned that the
Council approve the WFRC FY18 Budget and Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP).
Commissioner Bret Millburn seconded and the affirmative vote was unanimous. Mayor Dolan then
asked Andrew Gruber, WFRC, to give an update on WFRC’s office relocation. Mr. Gruber also

explained the need for the Council Chair, in consultation with the WFRC Budget Committee, to
have the authorization to negotiate and execute documents related to the sale of the current
offices and the purchase or lease of a new space. [00:43:55] Mayor Dolan motioned to approve
the authorization as requested and the second was made by Mayor Bob Stevenson. The vote in
the affirmative was unanimous.
Regional Growth Committee [00:44:51]
Mayor Ben McAdams briefly reviewed the outcomes of the Regional Growth Committee (RGC)
meeting held on May 18, 2017. Potential Regional Transportation Plan (RTP) amendments are
available for public comment between July 1 through August 5, 2017. These amendments will be
considered by the Council during the August 2017 meeting. There was discussion on shifting
demographics along the Wasatch Front and the impact the aging population could affect our
transportation needs for the future.
[00:47:17] Mayor McAdams introduced Callie New, WFRC, who gave an update on the process
of Wasatch Choice 2050 and Regional Transportation Plan 2019-2050. Ms. New presented the
input that was collected from the Scenario Workshops held around the region during February
and March 2017.
[00:56:30] Mayor McAdams turned the time to Megan Townsend, WFRC, who reviewed the
Transportation and Land Use Connection (TLC) Program. Ms. Townsend then discussed the
sixteen projects that are to be funded during the 2017 award year. Ms. Townsend also introduced
Alex Roy, a new employee hired in the TLC Program at WFRC.
Transportation Committee (Trans Com) [01:07:46]
Mayor Derk Timothy informed the Council of the items that were discussed in the Trans Com
meeting held on April 20, 2017.
Information: Board Modifications to the 2017-2022 Transportation Improvement Program
Amendment Six (TIP) [01:07:46]
Mayor Timothy reported that WFRC received requests to modify the current 2016-2021
Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). The modifications required action from the
Transportation Committee (TransCom) since Wasatch Front Regional Council was not meeting,
but did not require a new air quality conformity analysis or a 30-day public comment period. Mayor
Timothy referred members to the packet for detailed information on Amendment Six and asked if
there were any questions.
ACTION: Board Modifications to the 2017-2022 TIP Amendment Seven [01:09:00]
Mayor Timothy then turned the time to Ben Wuthrich, WFRC, who presented information
regarding Amendment Seven to the 2017-2022 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). This
amendment is necessary in order to move these transportation projects forward. [01:19:05]
Mayor Derk Timothy moved to approve the resolution to modify the 2017-2022 TIP as requested.
Mayor Toby Mileski seconded the motion and the voting was unanimous in the affirmative.
ACTION: Surface Transportation Program (STP), Congestion Mitigation Air Quality
(CMAQ), and Transportation Alternatives Program (TAP) projects for 2018-2023 TIP
[01:19:25] Mr. Wuthrich continued his presentation by referring the group to the spreadsheets in
the packet and briefly discussing the projects in the three programs (STP, CMAQ, TAP) which
are to be considered for funding. [01:36:25] Mayor Derk Timothy moved to approve the
highlighted STP, CMAQ, and TAP projects to be added to the draft FY 2018-2023 STP and CMAQ
Programs, and the draft FY 2019 TAP Program. Mayor Tom Dolan seconded the motion and the
voting was unanimous in the affirmative.
Active Transportation Committee (ATC) Report [01:36:45]
Mayor Don Carroll reported on ATC since their last meeting on May 10, 2017. The ATC created
the Active Transportation Plan Standards to help local communities in the development of their
bicycle and walking plans. These standards will be used to guide the development of new plans

funded through MAG, WFRC, and UDOT. Upcoming Mobile Active Transportation Tours:
Murray/Midvale, June 14, 2017 10:30AM at the Murray Central Transit Station - This ride will focus
on first and last mile connections to transit, and Southwest Davis County, August/September,
2017 - This ride will focus on suburban connections to backbone trail networks, and will be
scheduled pending the completion of the Legacy Trail reconstruction. Golden Spoke Event: The
final area of the Jordan River Trail bridge at North Temple is under construction with a tentative
completion date at the end of November 2017.
Air Quality Committee Report [01:40:40]
Mayor JoAnn Seghini provided an update on the Air Quality Committee. The annual Clear the Air
Challenge, which has usually been held in July or August, will be held in February 2018. Tesoro
Oil Refinery is expecting to be producing sulfur-free (Tier 3) fuel by 2020.
Executive Director’s Report [01:42:23]
Andrew Gruber, WFRC, reported that the federal government has proposed their FY18 budget
and it is unclear how future transportation funding will be impacted. Also, WFRC no longer has
an appointment with the Utah Communications Authority but has been asked to participate on an
advisory committee to provide input regarding public communications for the state.
Other Business [01:44:17]
Mayor Mike Caldwell asked if there were any other items to discuss. With no further business,
Commissioner Bret Millburn moved that the meeting be adjourned at 3:45pm. Councilmember
Michael Jensen seconded the motion and the voting was unanimous.
