CITY OF EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL
ORDINANCE NO. 2025-O0-02 DATE: May 27, 2025
AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF EMIGRATION CANYON APPROVING AND
ADOPTING ENGINEERING STANDARD DRAWINGS AND SPECIFICATIONS FOR
THE CITY OF EMIGRATION CANYON

WHEREAS, following its incorporation as a municipality in 2017, the City of Emigration
Canyon (“Emigration Canyon”) has received its engineering services from Salt Lake County
Public Works Engineering through the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District’s (“MSD”)
master interlocal agreement with Salt Lake County; and

WHEREAS, because Emigration Canyon received its engineering services from Salt Lake
County, Emigration Canyon has followed the Salt Lake County Engineering and Flood Control
Standard Drawings and Specifications; and

WHEREAS, effective January 1, 2025, the MSD established its own engineering
department to provide engineering services directly to its member entities, including Emigration
Canyon; and

WHEREAS, now that the MSD is providing engineering services directly to Emigration
Canyon, the City Council desires to adopt updated and revised engineering standard drawing
standards to replace the County’s standards.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON CITY COUNCIL
AS FOLLOWS:

A. Adoption of Engineering Standards. The attached document titled “Emigration
Canyon Engineering Standard Drawings and Specifications” is adopted as the official standards
for the City of Emigration Canyon (the “Standards’”).

B. Direction to Staff. The Mayor and applicable Emigration Canyon staff and
contractors authorized and directed to take such steps as may be needed: (a) for this Ordinance to
become effective under Utah law, including but not limited to compliance with the requirements
of Utah Code §§ 10-3-711 and 10-3-712; and (b) to use and apply the Standards in accordance
with applicable laws, regulations, and ordinances.

C. Severability. If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any part of this
Ordinance is unconstitutional or invalid, then such portion of this Ordinance, or specific
application of this Ordinance, shall be severed from the remainder, which shall continue in full
force and effect.

D. Effective Date. This Ordinance will take effect immediately upon its posting
pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-712.

ADOPTED AND APPROVED at a duly called meeting of the Emigration Canyon City

Council on this 27" day of May 2025.

Emigration Canyon Municipal Code
Chapter 14 - Highways, Sidewalks and Public Places
14.16.010 Right-Of-Way--Excavation And Structures Prohibited When

A. No right-of-way of any Township road shall be dug up or excavated and no approach,
road, driveway, pole, pipeline, conduit, sewer, ditch, culvert, billboard, advertising
sign, or other structure or object of any kind or character shall be placed,
constructed or maintained within any such right-of-way except as permitted by and
in accordance with the regulations of the county engineering division acting for the
Township.

B. Cuts or open excavations on newly constructed, paved, or overlaid rights of way are
not allowed for three years. If an emergency cut or excavation occurs, the
responsible persons shall comply with any special conditions imposed by the
Township regarding restoration of the roadway as detailed in Section C.

C. Any person making any excavation, bore or boring in or under any Township right-of-
way shall, at their own expense restore the surface to its same type and depth of
pavement as which is adjoining, including the gravel base material (at a minimum of
3: Bituminous surface and 8" untreated base course). All restoration shall conform
to the Township standards and all restorations shall be accomplished within
seventy-two (72) hours from the time the excavation, unless additional time is
granted in writing by the Township. If, the road has been constructed, reconstructed,
or overlaid within the preceeding three (3) years, or the pavement has been chip
sealed or slurry sealed within the preceding two (2) years, the permittee shall be
responsible for the following:

1. New Streets, Reconstructed Streets, or Overlaid Streets: The asphalt surface
shall be milled down a minimum depth of two inches from curb (or edge of
asphalt) to center of road and resurfaced with asphalt. If the cut extends
through the center-line of the road the milling and paving will be from curb to
curb (or edge of asphalt to edge of asphalt). Milling and paving shall extend to
a length of 15' in each direction from the east side of the cut. The County
Engineer or designee reserves the right to require a length greater than fifteen
feet in each direction and a depth greater than two inches if there are special
circumstances.

2. Slurried Streets or Chip-sealed Streets: The pavement shall be re-slurry
sealed with an approved type II slurry from curb (or edge of asphalt) to center

of road and a minimum of 15’ in each direction from each side of the cut. If
the cut extends through the center-line of the road the application of slurry
seal will be from curb to curb (or edge of asphalt to edge of asphalt).

3. Excavation of Township rights-of-way during the winter months (herein
defined as October 15 to April 15) will be allowed only if the work is a new
service connection, required maintenance, emergency or otherwise
approved by the Township. Permanent patching of a Township right-of-way
excavated in winter shall be delayed until April 15 or later and the outdoor
ambient temperature is 50 degrees and rising. The permittee will maintain a
temporary patch until such time as a permanent surfacing is accomplished.
The temporary patch will provide a smooth driving surface and be restored
within 72 hours of being notified that repairs are required.

Any person who violated the provisions of this section is guilty of a Class Cc
misdemeanor.
