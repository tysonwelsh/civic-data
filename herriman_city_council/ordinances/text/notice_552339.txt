PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on August 14, 2019 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2019-26 adopting an Impact Fee Enactment that imposes a Public Safety Impact Fee. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
