1. Sustainabihty             Action           Plan     Review;          Samantha            DeSeelhorst,              Sustainability        Analyst
Samantha         DeSeelhorst           said the purpose                 of the Sustainability               Action       Plan was to: 1) coordinate
sustainability        efforts       among          the tri-city      region     of Millcreek,              Holladay,        and Cottonwood            Heights;
2) provide       a consistent          sustainability             vision     for the future;           3) clarify        city sustainability         goals for
interested       community            members;             and 4) qualify           for various         sustainability         grants and incentives.
She reviewed          the timeline              of the plan which             started       in the surnrner            of 2019.      She said the plan
attempts      to balance          the needs and desires                  of each community.                   To be effective,           it must be general
enough       to apply       to the whole             tri-city     region,     while      also being           specific     enough       to represent          areas
that each city is passionate                      about.    Some strategies             and policies           may be more             applicable     to one
city than others.           Each community                  can determine             which         areas they wish          to focus on, and at
what speed/timeline.                She anticipated               that staff would            use the plan to make regular
recommendations               on areas of sustainability                     focus.      She then went over the updates                         to the plan
since it was last presented                    to the Council.             In the City        Content         section,     she added additional
projects     to each city's           lists of accomplishments.                      In the Development                   section,     she added language
related    to considering             solar and electric             vehicle         infrastructure           during      development           processes.       In
the Energy        Use section,            she altered           Community            Renewable             Energy       Program        language     to
Millcreek           City     Council           Meeting           Minutes                                                  9 August          2021        Page      2 of 7



encourage          program           engagement                rather     than     strict     participation.           In the Landscaping                    section,        she
altered      tree inventory             language           to specify          coordination            with       partner     agencies.          In the Waste
Management             section,         she added              language        related       to consideration               of recycling            market
development            zones         and language               related       to existing        conservation              plans      from      water      providers.             In
the Plan Partners                 section,      she added           the Utah         Rivers       Council          and Tree         Utah,       and updated             several
staff    titles    for accuracy.


Mayor        Silvestrini          said DeSeelhorst                 did a fine job            incorporating            the feedback              the Council            had
provided.


2. Planning           Matter          First        Reading:            ZT-21-006,            Technical            Corrections            Regarding
Definitions,          Noticing,           and       Planning            Commission               Membership;                 Robert          May,      Planner
Robert       May      said staff        was requesting                  the City       Council         adopt       several      technical          amendments                to the
zoning       ordinance,            to clarify         requirements,              and to update             the code to bring              it into      compliance              with
recent      changes         in state statute.             He then reviewed                   the amendments.                 In 2018,        the Planning
Coinmission            increased          from         seven       to nine       members,           so section        10.05.060           needed         to reflect          that
five    members            constituted          a quorum.            The major           newspapers            in Salt Lake            County         have      gone         to a
weekly        publication           rather      than daily,             so the 2021          Utah      Legislature           passed       S.B.      201 to eliminate
the noticing         requirement               in newspapers.               Millcreek          code would            be updated             to reflect        that.     Section
19.04.439          of the Zoning              Ordinance             currently        defines      "Public          Notices"           and the noticing
requirements.              However,           staff     felt    that having          the noticing           requirements              of "Public         Notices"            in
the definitions             section      of the zoning              ordinance            was not the proper                 place      for it and should               instead
be in the administration                      section      of the ordinance.                 He said staff           proposed          to have       two      new
definitions         for "Public          Hearing"              and "Public          Meeting"           in sections          19.04.438           and 19.04.439                 and
move      the current             sections      of "Public             Hearings        and Meetings"                (19.04.438)           and "Public            Notices"
(19.04.439)          to Chapter           19.02         General         Provisions           and Administration                 in two        new      sections
19.02.150          of 19.02.160.              In the new            section      19.02.160,           the requirement                 for publication            in a
newspaper           would         be eliminated.               In addition,         staff     recornrnended               moving         the noticing
requirements           for street        vacations              to Chapter         14.48       of the Millcreek               Ordinance,            in the Highways,
Sidewalks,          and Public           Places.


May      said "noncoinmercial                      greenhouses             associated          with      residential         uses"       would       be added           to the
19.04.020          Agriculture           definition            which       would       enable       the owner          of a single-family                 residential             lot
to maintain          a noncornmercial                   greenhouse            associated          with      a residential           use. He said, "existing
grade"      and "human-caused"                        terins       would      be added          to 19.04.095              Building,         Height       Of definition.

to tie these terms             together.           The 19.94.230               Family         definition          would      have      the word "unrelated"
removed           to meet      state code.            The 19.04.265               Garage,        Private       definition           would       have     the words
"roof     or" removed              so that      garages          that shared         a common              wall     with     the dwelling            would        be

five    feet wide          from     twenty         feet to meet            the current         roadway            standard.        The    new       definition          of
19.04.093          Building,          Footprint          Of would           be added          to read, "Footprint                   of building'         means          the
