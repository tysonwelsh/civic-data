Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order

Commission Chair Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2) Discussion Item

2.1) Update on Staff approved site plans.

Katie Bussell presented updated information on Holbrook Site G. Kim Struthers also provided
information on the site plans.

This was not on the recording.

2.2) Discussion of Sjors van Duren active transportation video.

The discussion focused on implementing a balanced approach to bike and car infrastructure,
emphasizing safety for all road users. Mike West advocated for a mindset shift among leaders

and the integration of walking, biking, and transit. Financial concerns, regional collaboration,
and funding opportunities were explored. Scott Bunker expressed skepticism based on Provo's
experience, noting that past bike infrastructure didn't significantly increase ridership. The
Commissioners acknowledged the challenge of fostering a cultural shift towards sustainable
transportation.

2.3) Discussion of detached ADU Development Code Amendment.

The discussion revolved around proposed changes to accessory dwelling units (ADUs). Key
points included a suggested maximum size, setback considerations, and debates over restrictions
on structures with windows. Concerns were raised about potential misuse and conversion over
time. The Commissioners discussed the need for a permanent foundation and enforcing
restrictions through building permits. The proposal underwent further review with potential
modifications.

5) City Business

There was no city business to discuss.

6) Adjournment

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Eyre moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:45 p.m.
