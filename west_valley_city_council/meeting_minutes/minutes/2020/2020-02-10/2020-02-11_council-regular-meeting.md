B.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION Z-4-2019, FILED
     BY VAHID SADEGHI, REQUESTING A ZONE CHANGE FROM M
     (MANUFACTURING) TO R-1-8 (SINGLE UNIT DWELLING
     RESIDENTIAL, MINIMUM LOT SIZE 8,000 SQUARE FEET) FOR
     PROPERTY LOCATED AT 4155 SOUTH 6000 WEST
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled February 11, 2020, in order for the City Council to
     hear and consider public comments regarding application Z-4-2019, filed by Vahid
     Sadeghi, requesting a Zone Change from M (manufacturing) to R-1-8 (single unit
     dwelling residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) for property located at 4155
     South 6000 West.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            Vahid Sadeghi has submitted a zone change application for a 0.86 acre
            parcel located at 4155 South 6000 West. The property is currently zoned M
            (manufacturing) with a General Plan designation of low density residential
            (3 to 4 units/acre). The proposed zone is R-1-8 (single unit dwelling
            residential, minimum lot size 8,000 sq. ft.).

            Surrounding zones include R-1-8 to the south, east and west and M to the
            north. Surrounding land uses include single unit dwellings to the east and
            west, a church to the south and a recently approved Maverik convenience
            store to the north. The subject parcel is vacant.

            UDOT acquired the subject parcel along with what is now a road to the
            south to provide access to the Hunter Cove subdivision and Hunter High
            School to the east. The subject property is now UDOT surplus. The
            applicant is planning to subdivide the property into four lots as seen in the
            attached concept plan.

            The Zoning Ordinance requires that “All new Subdivisions involving a
            rezone of property, or a PUD, shall participate in a Development Agreement
            that addresses housing size, quality, exterior finish materials, Streetscapes,
            Landscaping, etc.” The applicant has committed to build homes that meet
            the City’s design standards.

     Mayor Bigelow opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
     or opposition, Mayor Bigelow closed the Public Hearing.

RESOLUTION 20-16: AUTHORIZE THE EXECUTION OF AN INTERLOCAL
COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND SALT LAKE
COUNTY FOR THE PURCHASE OF AERIAL PHOTOGRAPHY
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-16 that would authorize the execution
of an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between the City and Salt Lake County for the
purchase of aerial photography.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       This resolution authorizes the City to enter into an agreement for the purchase and
       use of digital orthorectified aerial photography and oblique imagery of West Valley
 MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING – FEBRUARY 11, 2020
                         -8-

       City. Under this agreement, the West Valley City agrees to participate in the
       purchase of a commercial aerial photography license of high-resolution Imagery
       data. The option to buy both is available, in order to get the oblique’s with access
       to the browser based “ConnectExplorer” and up to 5 licenses to view and measure
       the photos, the ortho photography must be purchased first. The delivery for the
       ortho photos will include image files of the city/area tiled into PLSS sections as
       well as access to a WMTS web service.

       Current, accurate digital orthorectified photography has many uses within the City.
       The photos are used within the majority of City Departments to visualize and
       identify growth; compare urban changes with aerial photography from previous
       years; as a reference for new spatial information; as a background for online map
       viewers and printed maps; for measurements of impervious surfaces; and more.
       The format is compatible with existing GIS applications that are used and
       developed by City Staff. By partnering with other agencies, costs for aerial
       photography are significantly reduced.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City

RESOLUTION 20-18: AUTHORIZE THE EXECUTION OF A COST SHARING
INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND MAGNA WATER
DISTRICT FOR THE 6750 WEST ROADWAY PROJECT
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-18 that would authorize the execution
of a cost sharing Interlocal Agreement between the City and Magna Water District for the
6750 West Roadway Project.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
 MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING – FEBRUARY 11, 2020
                         -10-

follows:

       The Public Works Department recently completed construction on the 6750 West
       Roadway Project. 6750 West is a small cul-de-sac that provides access to the newly
       constructed Parks Department maintenance building, Magna Water’s drinking
       water treatment plant and a few residential properties.

       The agreement came to be as the city and the water district coordinated efforts to
       improve access to both properties. The roadway will be platted and dedicated for
       public use, creating a public road to access adjoining properties. The city and the
       water district share design and construction costs for the new road. The water
       district is also paying for a few miscellaneous waterline improvements completed
       with the city projects.
