   2. Considering a Site Plan for LDS Church Parking Lot Addition to be used by
      Building 1,2,7. The parking lot is located at 245 S Main, includes parcel numbers
      XA00-0074-(.68 acres), and XA00-0076-2 (.13 acres) The project is located in the
      CU1 (Combined Use 1) zone.
             a. Staff Presentation
                Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day
                Saints has filed an application for a private parking lot for an addition of the
                Nephi 1,2,7 Church building, located at 245 S. Main. Parcels for this project
                are: XA00-0074-XXX (.68 acres), and XA00-0076-2 (.13 acres). Current
                zoning for these parcels is currently in the CU1 (Combined Use 1) zone. A
                private parking lot is an allowed accessory use for a primary building and use
                of a church in this zone. Demolition of a previous log building (King’s
                Nursery) on the site has already started.

                  It is the opinion that after Mark Greenwood ALM & Associates, Civil
                  Engineers, and Roger Knell of Knell Architects, P.C. who are representing the
                  Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints, meet any UDOT access or
                  encroachment permission for this project that this project be approved.

   3. Discussion regarding concept zoning along Highways 28 and 132 regarding the CU1
      and CU2 zones.
         a. Staff Presentation
                Mrs. Shauna Talbot continues the discussion from the May 8, 2024, Planning
                commission meeting. This item concerns the General Plan done in 2022, and
                the things they have heard back from the community about keeping Main
                Street in Nephi to have more of a rural feel. Amongst the information
                presented are the traffic flow and loads, commercial building along main
                street, and how pods along Main Street may achieve these items.
                The city would like a long-term plan to help with this area and mix combined
                use and areas in the proposed areas on the map. (see attachment 1)
                Proposed areas are the following:
                    • Purple areas on map be turned to Commercial Use 2 (CU2)
                    • North side starting at 700 North to 400 North (Wasp Way) to
                         Commercial Use 2 (CU2).
                    • Alternate after between Commercial Use 1 (CU1) and Commercial
                         Use 2 (CU2) out.
                    • 100 South to 200 South as Commercial Use 1 (CU1)
                    • 200 South to 700 South as Commercial Use 2 (CU2)
                    • 700 South and to the end of town would be Highway Commercial Use
                         Zones.

          b. Commission Discussion
             Commissioner Heather Robertson raised the question of the South area in the map
             (see attachment item 1), and what changing the area zoning map will do for future
             requests and building for the Nephi City staff. Staff member, Mrs. Shauna Talbot,
             responded that changing the zoning map and zone areas will assist staff currently
             and future staff in clarifying what areas are recommended for growth.
             Commissioner Robertson also commented on the high-density housing outlook
             and stated that it may not be seen favorably by the community.

              Commissioner Cory Thomson suggested to the staff that on 5th South instead of
              creating pods of areas, that half block on both sides to assist traffic and future
              traffic coming from the west into Nephi City.

              Commissioner Fran Petersen raised the question and technicality of the CU-1 and
              CU-2 zones and usage of rights. Staff member Shauna Talbot clarified that
              current zone areas are listed as CU-1 is residential only or single-family housing,
              and the restriction is business above housing. CU-2 allows for the creation and
              business above of residential.

              Chairwoman Ann Peterson commented on that by adopting the current map that it
              will help assist with future traffic flow, as well as create a pattern during business
              hours. Staff member, Mrs. Shauna Talbot, clarified that the future hope for
              downtown and main street areas are to make them functional, traffic flows that
              support the city and do not create bottle necking during hours of the day as has
              been shown in other cities i.e. Lehi City, Park City, and additional locations that
              are currently facing this.

              Alternate Commissioner Terry Cook commented that the idea will work and
              prevent the traffic issue that currently Logan City is facing, and asked staff to
              clarify if business owners who are currently not fitting into the pod they are in or
              who would like to change it in the future what options they will have. Staff
              member, Mr. Seth Atkinson, clarified they can come in and request an zone
              change for that particular space, however once changed it will need to conform to
              the new zoning area unless a planning commission changes the zoning map.

              Alternate Commissioner John Ford explained that he views the pod idea of pros
              and cons and views this as a necessary change to assist the narrative of what we
              want to see and expect for the future of our main street and traffic areas.

              Mr. Seth Atkinson, staff member, presented to the Commission by adopting a
              format as seen on the map (see attachment 1) for zoning it will establish the
              philosophy of having a plan for Nephi City. Thereby assisting future staff,
              planning commissions and future prospective businesses see a plan and narrative
              of what as a city our residents, city staff, and this planning commission had in
