        Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Annual Action Plan
                   City Administration is proposing the Council adopt a resolution that approves the Annual Action Plan (AAP) and budget
        for fiscal year 2022. Fiscal year 2022 is the second year of the five-year Consolidated Plan (ConPlan) period. The amounts in the
        Annual Action Plan budget as approved by the Council are incorporated into the Major Grants section of the City Budget. The
        Annual Action Plan budget consists of an estimate of entitlement funding, program income funds, and carry-over funding as well
        as the Administration's proposal on how those funds will be expended throughout the next fiscal year.
                  Project Coordinator Fuentes used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide additional infonnation regarding the
        FY21-22 AAP. The City's current five-year ConPlan encompasses the period of July 1, 2020 to June 30, 2025, and is the
        mechanism by which the City receives Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) funds. It is a strategic planning
        tool that outlines the City's plans for HUD funding over a five-year period. If a project is not specified in the ConPlan, HUD funds

Quality Neighborhood Initiative Program Renewal
          The City's Community and Economic Development Department is proposing the City Council, Mayor and
Redevelopment Agency renew the Quality Neighborhoods Initiative. The Quality Neighborhoods Initiative was first adopted in
November 2015 as a way to coordinate various community and economic development programs and strategies into a
consolidated vision designed to help stabilize and revitalize neighborhoods in Ogden. The Initiative uses several programs,
including the city's five-year Consolidated Plan and Annual Action Plans, the City's Redevelopment Agency, and other city and
state programs, to establish a coordinated pattern of public investment into the overall health of the community. The program uses
several funding sources, which include Community Development Block Grants, Business Depot Ogden (BDO) lease revenue,
state and other local funding sources. An update to the BDO lease revenue appropriation is addressed in a separate but related
Council item. The Council reviewed the proposal at its March 9, 2021, work session meeting. The work session review on April6
will allow further discussion of the proposed renewal.
          Community Development Manager Ogden stated the primary functions of the Initiative are to:
              •    Create a framework of principles and guidelines for decision making regarding location, nature, and intended
                   outcomes of revitalization projects and programs.
              • Identify and facilitate coordination of resources, including funding, ordinances, and interdepartmental priorities,
                   and coordination of other services for individuals and families directly impacted by changes brought about by
                   the implementation of the initiative.
          Mr. Ogden noted the initiative outlines a framework of objectives and strategies for implementing its goals; there are
several changes proposed with the renewal of the initiative, including:
              • Removing a reference to create a master urban renewal area.
              • Updating a reference to New Bridge School instead of Dee School.
              • Updating the property targeting language to clarify that many programs are available city-wide.
              • Updating language to focus on historic properties, when possible, when purchasing, renovating, or reselling
                   properties.
              • Removing the HUD ACA program reference as this program is no longer functional.
              • Adding new language under Property Targeting to allow new and renovated housing to demonstrate best
                   practices in implementing codes and ordinances to facilitate a variety ofhousing types like accessory dwelling
                   units.
          Funding for the Initiative primarily comes from federal funding included in the five-year Consolidated Plan and Annual
Action Plans, existing infrastructure spending within the target area coordinated with other projects, and BDO lease revenue.

         Discussion of the proposal among the Council and Mr. Ogden centered on topics such as statistics the City has collected
regarding the influence the initiative has had on neighborhood stability and a shift from rental properties to owner-occupied
properties; rent rates stability amidst current housing market trends; the target demographic and area for Initiative funding; and
data that can be relied upon as an indicator of the success of the program. Council member Choberka suggested that data
regarding the Quality Neighborhoods Initiative be located on the City's website for easier access for residents.
         There was then a review of the projects that have been completed in the Quality Neighborhoods Initiative target area,
after which the Council thanked Mr. Ogden for his willingness to discuss this matter further before moving the proposal to a City
Council meeting agenda for action. Council member Hyer stated that he feels it is important to improve the messaging and
marketing regarding the program to address the concern addressed by some residents that the City is focusing on one area of the
City and ignoring others.
         Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reviewed the schedule of proceeding with action items; ifthere are no objections,
Council staff will add necessary items to the Aprill3, 2021, City Council and Redevelopment Agency (RDA) meeting agendas.
No objections were voiced.

Council Business
         Council Executive Director Eller-Smith discussed efforts of Council staff to develop a plan to allow in-person attendance
during future City Council meetings; staff proposes a hybrid format that combines in-person and electronic participation in order
to keep participation numbers below 25 for each meeting and to allow for social distancing. Staff will continue to test the plan to
ensure that meetings are accessible and effective.


         The meeting adjourned at 5:45p.m.
