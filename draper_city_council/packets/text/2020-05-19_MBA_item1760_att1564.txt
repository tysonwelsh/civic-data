Business Meeting

1.0    Call to Order: Boardmember Walker, Temporary Chair

2.0    Motion: Approval of May 14, 2019, Draper City Municipal Building Authority
       Meeting Minutes.

2.1    Boardmember Vawdrey moved to accept the May 14, 2019, Draper City Municipal
       Building Authority Meeting Minutes. Boardmember Lowery seconded the motion.

4.1    Boardmember Lowery moved to adjourn to a Redevelopment Agency Meeting.
       Boardmember Vawdrey seconded the motion.

4.2    A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Vawdrey, and Walker
       voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.
