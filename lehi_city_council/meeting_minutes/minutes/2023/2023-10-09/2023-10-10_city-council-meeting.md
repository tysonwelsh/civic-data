136   9. Consideration of Ordinance #59-2023 a Development Code Amendment to Chapter 26,
137      Accessory Uses, changing the lot size requirement for detached ADUs from 1/2 to 1/3
138      acre.
139      The discussion revolved around housing elements, particularly focusing on detached
140      accessory dwelling units (ADUs). The conversation covered the analysis of properties
141      eligible for ADUs, parking requirements, setback regulations, and ADU size limitations.
142      There was debate about whether the ADU size should be determined by a percentage of the
      Lehi City Council Meeting                       3                                 October 10, 2023
143       primary dwelling or a set square footage. Concern was raised about infrastructure strain due
144       to the potential increase of an ADU over 1300 square feet.
145
146       Mayor Johnson asked for public input.
147
148       Jamie Ostler stated that she owns property just under a third of an acre and proposed that the
149       Council consider including a quarter-acre lot size for accessory dwelling units (ADUs) due to
150       the ample space available on their large lot. She emphasized the benefits of ADUs, including
151       providing housing for family members and reducing outdoor water usage. She was aiming to
152       address the housing problem and allow young people to remain in Utah.
153
154       The Council discussed the possibility of reducing the minimum lot size for the allowance of
155       Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) from a third of an acre to a quarter of an acre. Concerns
156       were raised about the potential impact on infrastructure, including sewer and water demands.
157       Some council members suggested a cautious approach, considering the potential impacts on
158       infrastructure and proposing to revisit the matter after some time to gather more data. There
159       was also discussion about existing regulations for setbacks, height limits, and the potential of
160       internal ADUs.
161
162       Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to a p p r o v e Ordinance #59-2023 a
                      Development Code
163                   Amendment to Chapter 26, Accessory Uses, changing the lot size requirement for
164                   detached ADUs from 1/2 to 1/3 acre; but with the modification to Section 26.040
165                   1A, striking the existing provision of either 50% of the primary dwelling or 1200
166                   square feet and replace it with a flat cap of 1300 square feet for ADUs; and
167                   including all DRC and Planning Commission comments. Councilor Albrecht
168                   seconded the motion.
169
170       Various perspectives were discussed regarding the appropriate size restrictions for ADUs and
171       their potential impact on infrastructure and community. The concern was that on a large lot
172       with a large primary home, the ADU could also be rather large which may place more burden
173       on infrastructure. The Council was also willing to have this discussion again in the future
174       after this change has been implemented and they can collect more data.
175
176       Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
177       Councilor Koivisto, No; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed 4-1.
