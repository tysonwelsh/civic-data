          Land Use Development and Management Act (LUDMA)

                    o Provide health, safety and welfare
                    o Promote prosperity
                    o Improve morals, peace and good order now and for the future.
             • General Plan
                    o Sets the Land Use Policies for the community
                    o Intended to be a living document, meaning it can change and focus
                       when things change in the community. For your community by the
                       community.
             • Land Use Ordinances
                    o Laws written and adopted by the legislative community.

             •   Legislative vs. Administrative Decision Making
                    o Administrative decisions involve applying existing codes for
                        development, based on individual facts and circumstances. In our city
                        subdivisions will no longer go to the city council. Conditional use
                        permit, Site plan, building permit, variances will.
             •   Consideration when Making Administrative Decisions
                    o Land use authority must apply plain language
                    o If an application meets the requirements, it should be timely approved.
                    o If there is a regulation “does not plainly restrict he land use
                        application” or could be reasonable read to support different
                        interpretations, the land use must interpret and apply the regulation to
                        “favor” the proposals.
                    o Land use authority must support its decision with “Substantial
                        evidence.”
             •   Considerations when making Administrative Decisions
                    o Role of the Pubic Administrative Decisions
                             Land use authority may not rely on public opinion or
                                 preferences when making an administrative decision.
                             Land use authority may receive input from the public for the
                                 purpose of gathering facts and evidence to support its
                                 conclusions and decision, in accordance with adopted
                                 standards.
             •   Annexation
                    o Process of bringing property into a city’s limits. Annexation for
                        urbanization in areas with municipal services
                    o Annex territory by legislative action, regulation and requirements.
                        Always review with legal counsel BEFORE action.
                    o Each agency must have an annexation policy
                    o State codes and local regulations completed with legal counsel
                    o Section 403 effective November 2024
             •   Conditional Use Permit
                    o Local Code requires conditional uses. “Objective standards set forth”
                    o Conditional use permit entitled if:
                             Proposed use complies
                             Reasonable conditions are proposed, or imposed, to mitigate
                                 any “reasonable anticipated detrimental effects” “in accordance
                                 with applicable standards”
                             An application for conditional use permit is entitled to
                                 approval, if:
                                     • Proposed complies with applicable requirements
                                     • Reasonable conditions are proposed or can be imposed,
                                          to mitigate reasonably anticipated detrimental effect in
                                          accordance with applicable standards.
                             Examples of Standards that may address potential detrimental
                                 effects:
                                     • Impacts on street service levels and/or traffic patterns

             •   Exactions
                    o An exaction is a mandatory contribution required by a governmental
                        entity as a condition of approval for a development application and
                        may include:
                            1. Dedication of property
                            2. Construction and dedication of public improvements

             •   Exactions-The Rough Proportionality Test
                    o A municipality may impose an exaction on development proposed in a
                        land use application when it satisfies the following two-part “rough
                        proportionality” test:
                             An essential link exists between a legitimate governmental
                                interest and each exaction; and
                             Each exaction is roughly proportionate, both in nature and
                                extent, to the impact of the proposed development.
             •   Tools for Thinking About Rough Proportionality
                    o System Improvements vs. Project Improvements
                             System improvement-understood to be a public facility that is
                                intended to provide services to larger regions.
                             Project Improvements-an improvement planned and designed
                                to provide primarily to the proposed development and is
                                necessary for the use and convenience of future users.
             •   Finding the Right Balance
                    o It starts by following your ordinances and development standards.
                    o Requiring a developer to build improvements that will clearly serve
                        only, or at least primarily, the proposed development, typically
                        satisfies the rough proportionality test, and is lawful
                    o Requiring the developer to bear the burden of constructing a system
                        improvement that will benefit and serve a larger region, or the
                        community, likely violates the rough proportionality test, and is
                        unlawful
             •   Variances
                    o The Appeal Authority may grant a variance only if:
                             literal enforcement of the ordinance would cause unreasonable
                                hardship for the applicant that is not necessary to carry out the
                                general purpose of the land use ordinances;

                             There are special circumstances attached to the property that do
                              not generally apply to other properties in the same zone;
                            granting the variance is essential to the enjoyment of a
                              substantial property right possessed by other property in the
                              same zone.
                     o The Appeal Authority may grant a variance only if:
                            the variance will not substantially affect the general plan and
                              will not be contrary to the public interest; and
                            the spirit of the land use ordinance is observed and substantial
                              justice done.
                     o Unreasonable hardship may not be self-imposed or economic and must
                       be “peculiar” to the property.
                     o Special circumstances must relate to the hardship and must deprive the
                       property of privileges others in the same zone enjoy.
