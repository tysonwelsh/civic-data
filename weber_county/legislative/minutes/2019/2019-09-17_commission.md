      2. RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE FILING OF CROSS-APPEALS FOR 2019 CENTRALLY ASSESSED PROPERTIES
         – RESOLUTION 53-2019.
            Courtlan Erickson, Deputy County Attorney, noted that there are certain types of properties in the State that are
            centrally assessed by the State Tax Commission Property Tax Division but the county receives the property tax
            revenue. When these property owners appeal property values through the State Tax Commission, counties have
            the opportunity by statute to file a cross-appeal or request to be joined as a party in those appeals. This resolution
            addresses six of those appeals. The County Attorney’s Office received prior Commission approval to file the
            appeals for CenturyLink and PacifiCorp and these are on this resolution for ratification. He asked for approval
            for the county to join as a party in the litigation for the remaining four—T-Mobile, Union Pacific Railroad
            Company, Utah Central Railway, and Utah Railway Company. Commissioner Harvey thanked Mr. Erickson
            for his work on Weber County’s participation with other counties on these centrally assessed tax issues.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 53-2019 authorizing the filing of cross-appeals for 2019
            centrally assessed properties; Chair Jenkins seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

    6. CONTRACT WITH HOOPER CITY TO ESTABLISH TERMS RELATED TO THE HOOPER CITY 5500 SOUTH
       PROJECT AMENDED REQUEST.
         Brooke Stewart, of County Community Development, stated that the City ran into unexpected utility relocation
         expenses on this project. The City has franchise agreements in place with all of the utility companies to move
         their utilities at their expense for any road widening/construction projects. However, the City did not realize
         that those franchise agreements do not apply to work done on State highways. Because 5500 S. is along a State
         highway, the City incurred unexpected costs associated with relocating those utilities. UDOT has committed
         to cover 50% of those expenses and WACOG recommended awarding the City $94,846 from Sales Tax funds
         at its August meeting. Chair Jenkins serves on the Transportation Committee and said that this had been
         approved.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Hooper City to establish terms related to the Hooper
         City 5500 South project amended request; Chair Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
