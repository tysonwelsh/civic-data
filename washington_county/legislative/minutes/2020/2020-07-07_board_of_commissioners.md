JULY 7, 2020

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on July 7, 2020, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist,
Commissioner Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, County Administrator
Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2020-1158-O, AN ORDINANCE
VACATING A PORTION OF A COUNTY ROAD GENERALLY LOCATED ON

THE NORTHERLY END OF KOLOB RESERVOIR IN WASHINGTON COUNTY

Public Works Director Ron Whitehead explained that a road located on the northerly end
of the Kolob reservoir formerly passed along side of the reservoir across private property.
Washington County recently realigned the road to pass around the private property. As a
condition of the realignment, the County agreed to vacate the old portion of the road that
is no longer needed for access.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2020-1158-
O, An Ordinance Vacating a Portion of a County Road Generally
Located on the Northerly End of Kolob Reservoir in Washington
County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Hurricane Valley Fire Chief Tom Kuhlmann explained that there were 53 brush fires
reported between 9:00 PM and midnight on July 4, 2020. The County is brittle due to the
dry conditions in the area from lack of rain. Mr. Kuhlmann cautioned citizens to remain
vigilant in regards to fire safety and prevention.

Fireworks may be discharged between July 21-27, 2020. Some areas of the County are
