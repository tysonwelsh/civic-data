          DETERMINATION REGARDING CONDUCTING TOWN OF ALTA PUBLIC MEETINGS
                            WITHOUT AN ANCHOR LOCATION

The Mayor of the Town of Alta hereby determines that conducting a meeting with an anchor
location presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be present at the
anchor location pursuant to Utah Code section 52‐4‐207(4) and Resolution 2020‐R‐12. The
facts upon which this determination is based include: The percent and number of positive
COVID‐19 cases in Utah has been significantly higher since May 27, 2020. The seven day
average of cases has been over 300 since June 5, 2020. COVID‐19 patients in Utah hospitals
have increased during the same time period. Almost half the state cases are in Salt Lake
County. As of August 3, 2020, there have been 317 deaths in Utah due to COVID‐19.

This meeting will not have a physical anchor location. All attendees will connect remotely.
Attendees may join the webinar by registering for the meeting through the Town website
www.townofalta.com or the meeting can be watched on YouTube. Additionally, comments
may be emailed to the Town Clerk plever@townofalta.com

This determination will expire in 30 days on September 10, 2020.
