
Meeting Minutes
Work Session 11-24-20
7:00  p.m.
Nephi City Council Chambers


	•	Code Enforcement Officer Andrea Holm addressed the council about her efforts to enforce the code at the RV Parks within the city.  Alex Johnson, owner of two of the RV Parks in the city, expressed his appreciation for Ms. Holm working with him in helping to contact tenants and getting his RV Parks compliant with the city code.  He stated that it is difficult to balance justice with mercy because the people living in the parks do need a place to stay.  Mr. Johnson wanted the council to know that progress is being made towards cleaning up the RV parks. 

	•	 Chief of Police Michael Morgan, Juab County Sheriff Douglas Anderson, and Millard County Sheriff Richard Jacobsen presented a request for an additional officer from the Nephi City Police Department exclusively devoted to the Drug Task Force that serves Millard and Juab County.   

	•	Chief of Police Michael Morgan requested from the council some additional officers and office help to aide in the workload of the department. 

	•	 City Recorder Lisa Brough presented to the mayor and council the certification of the Nephi South Commercial Annexation petition.  

	•	The council discussed the procedures necessary to fill the vacancy of the Nephi City Justice Court Judge Sharla Williams who recently passed away.  The council also discussed changing prosecuting services for the Justice Court due to the resignation of Perry Davis as the court’s prosecutor.  

	•	

	•	Adjournment 9:30 p.m.
