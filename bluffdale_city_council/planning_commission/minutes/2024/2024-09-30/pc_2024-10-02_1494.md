       Proposed Text Amendment to Create a Definition for Satellite Communications

Planning Manager, Caitlyn Tubbs, presented the Staff Report and stated that the SD-X Gateway
Zone covers 6.78 acres at 383 West 14600 South at the intersection of 14600 South and Pony
Express Road. The SD-X zone entitles the property to a full-service hotel, which includes
conference spaces, restaurants, a bar, and a spa. They are also entitled to develop three residential
towers up to a total of 500 units with a minimum building height of 10 stories and no maximum
height. As it is part of a Special Development Zone, the Project Plan was also adopted into the
ordinance.


The applicant proposed the utilization of approximately 0.5 acres on the southern tip ofthe site to
develop a satellite communications station, which would include approximately 40 two-meter
antennas. That would require an amendment to Section 11.11.110.010 of City Code to include
Satellite Communication Stations as Permitted Uses in the SD-X Gateway Zone. It would also
amend Section 11.20.020 to establish the following definition of the Satellite Communication
Stations Land Use:


       A station and/or facility located on the Earth's surface or within the major portion
       ofthe Earth's atmosphere intended for communication with one or more stations of
       the same kind by means of one or more reflecting satellites or other extraterrestrial
       objects in space. A satellite communication station shall include any exterior
       equipment two meters in diameter or in width or in length used to receive direct
       broadcast satellite service, including but not limited to direct-to-home satellite
       service; to receive or transmit fixed wireless signals via satellite; to receive video-
       programming service via multipoint distribution services; multichannel multipoint
       distribution services; to receive or transmit fixed wireless signals other than via

       satellite; or to receive broadcast communication signals. Such term shall include
       any mount, pole, or other elevating device supporting the same.

Staffrecommended that the Planning Commission consider the following questions:

   • Is the proposal in keeping with the purpose of the SD-X Gateway Zone?

   • Should additional design standards, landscaping requirements, and/or setbacks be enacted
       for the proposed Land Use due to the fact that it is a unique and visible property?

Notice ofthe public hearing was published on the Utah Public Notice and Bluffdale websites and
posted at City Hall. No public comments were received. Staff recommended that the Planning
Commission review the proposal, conduct a public hearing, and consider either forwarding a
recommendation to the City Council or continuing the item if additional discussion was needed.

In response to a question from Chair Cragun, Ms. Tubbs stated that the applicant is a separate
entity that applied with permission from the property owner. They are not associated with the
hotel developer. The proposed satellite facility would be on the south end of the parcel, adjacent
to the full-service hotel. It could potentially impact the underground parking originally approved
for the hotel as well as the site landscaping. With regard to visibility from the proposed hotel and
residential towers, the submitted site plan includes an eight-foot metal fence surrounding the 0.5-
acre parcel. The remainder ofthe site would be filled with gravel.

In response to a question from Commissioner Flynn, Ms. Tubbs stated that she believes it would
be a remotely managed, standalone facility.         There would not be any employees onsite.
Commissioner Flynn asked if a generator will be installed in case of power outages. Ms. Tubbs
stated that the applicant will be required to provide information regarding utility services when the
final site plan is submitted. The concept site plan was received with a Building Permit application,
at which time the applicant was informed that an Ordinance change would be required in order to
proceed with the project.

6.     PUBLIC HEARING. CONSIDERATION. AND RECOMMENDATION on a
       Proposed Text Amendment Revising Section 11.160,140 to Allow Additional Fencing
       Height in Front Yards for Properties that Changed Lot Configuration Following a
       Subdivision. City of Bluffdale, Applicant(Application 2024-34L Caitlvn Tubbs,staff
       presenter.


Ms. Tubbs presented the Staff Report and stated that the City Council directed Staff to prepare
amendments to the Fencing Code on July 16, 2024. Bluffdale's fencing height standards are
outlined in Bluffdale Code Section 11.160.140, which regulates both sight-obscuring and non-
sight-obscuring fences in front yards. Under the current code, the maximum height of a sight-
obscuring (solid)fence is 42 inches. Non-sight-obscuring(open)fences can be up to 60 inches in
height. The proposed Text Amendment would address properties that undergo subdivision and,
through the subdivision, the orientation ofthe lot changes. Ms. Tubbs provided an example ofthe
potential subdivision where the former side yard of an existing home because the front because
that it is the portion of the lot that is closest to the new right-of-way. The proposed amendment
would allow a preexisting home in this situation to retain the former side yard fence in its new
front setback.


The proposed Amendment reads as follows:

       2.        Notwithstanding the foregoing, a parcel or lot that has been determined to
                 have changed front yard orientation to a newly created right of way through
                 an approved subdivision plat AND which had an existing dwelling at the
                 time of subdivision may construct fencing within the front setback in
                 accordance with the orientation of the existing dwelling up to six (6') in
                 height. All requirements for clear view for intersections and adjacent
                 driveways shall still apply.


Notice of the public hearing was published on the Utah Public Notice website and the City of
Bluffdale website and posted at City Hall. No comments were received. City Staffreconunended
that the Planning Commission hold a public hearing and forward a positive recommendation to the
City Council for review at the November 13,2024, City Council Meeting.
