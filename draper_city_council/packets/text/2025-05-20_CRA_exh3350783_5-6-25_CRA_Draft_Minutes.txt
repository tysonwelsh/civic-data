2.b   Public Hearing: CRA Resolution #25-02, establishing the Community
      Reinvestment Agency of Draper City Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year
      2025-2026 ending June 30, 2026 and setting the public hearing date of May
      20, 2025 to receive comment on the Tentative Budget
      Finance Director John Vuyk presented a Community Reinvestment Agency
      Tentative Budget for FY2026, and said budget adoption was tentatively

      one come forward.

      Boardmember F. Lowry moved to approved CRA Resolution #25-02
