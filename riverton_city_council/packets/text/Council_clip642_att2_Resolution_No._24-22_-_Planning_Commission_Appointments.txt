                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-22

 A RESOLUTION RE-APPOINTING COMMISSION MEMBERS TO THE RIVERTON
                    CITY PLANNING COMMISSION

       WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance 2-95-020, the Mayor shall
appoint with advice and consent of the City Council residents of Riverton City to serve on the
Planning Commission; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the re-appointment of
Monique Mortensen-District 1, Shelly Cluff-District 2, Troy Rushton-District 3 and Evan
Matheson-At-Large; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the re-appointments of the Mayor's
nominations to the Planning Commission.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the re-appointment of Monique
          Mortensen, Shelly Cluff, Troy Rushton and Evan Matheson as Planning
          Commissioners, which terms shall end January 31, 2028.
       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of
February, 2024 by the following vote:
