                                 ORDINANCE NO: 40-2025


        AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE LEHI CITY DEVELOPMENT CODE
        CHAPTER 39, DEFINITONS DWELLING, TWO-FAMILY, EQUIPMENT
            SALES AND RENTAL, PUBLIC/CIVIC USE, HEALTH CARE
                 FACILITIES, AND RETAIL SALES, GENERAL

       WHEREAS, it has become necessary to amend Chapter 39 of the Lehi City Development
Code; and

        WHEREAS, this amendment seeks to clarify that an ADU is not a duplex; and

        WHEREAS, this amendment seeks to eliminate unnecessary descriptions of equipment
sales and rental; and

        WHEREAS, this amendment seeks to clean up public and civic use; and

        WHEREAS, this amendment seeks to clarify that Health Care Facilities can have a helipad;
and

      WHEREAS, this amendment seeks to add mail services and copy centers to Retail Sales,
General; and

       WHEREAS, following a public hearing on May 8, 2025, the Lehi City Planning
Commission reviewed the proposed revisions and forwarded a positive recommendation to the
City Council; and

        WHEREAS, on June 10, 2025, the City Council held a duly noticed meeting to receive
public comment and ascertain the facts regarding this matter, which facts and comments are found
in the hearing record and which include the staff report, minutes from the Planning Commission
meeting of May 8, 2025, and the positive recommendation of the Planning Commission; and,

        WHEREAS, after considering the facts and comments presented to the Municipal Council,
the Council finds: Chapter 39 of the Lehi City Development Code should be amended; and such
action furthers the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Lehi.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Lehi City, Utah as
follows:

PART I:

        Chapter 39 of the Lehi City Development Code is hereby amended.

PART II:

       C.      The City Council hereby directs that the official copy of the Lehi City Code be

       D.      This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after being posted or published as

        Approved and Adopted by the City Council of Lehi City this 10th day of June 2025.
