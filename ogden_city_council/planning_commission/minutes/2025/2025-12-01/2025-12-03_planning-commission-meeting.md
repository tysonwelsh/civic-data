3. Beacon Hills project, 756 30° St
a. Final Group Dwelling, consisting of 9 single-family dwellings

b. Preliminary Private Subdivision, consisting of 9 footprint lots

today as the project will provide affordable starter homes for the community.

5. Special Exception, painted brick in East Central, 2743 Liberty Ave
Petitioner was not present.
Planner Jennifer Patrick stated that though the painting of brick is prohibited in the East
Central Historic District, the brick had been previously been painted white and the petitioner
wanted to re-paint the existing brick. Testing proved that attempted paint removal of the raked
brick would be very difficult and potentially damaging, therefore staff recommended approval,
with conditions to remove additional mailboxes and units to assure it is converted back to the
duplex it was originally zoned for.

COMISSIONER SOUTHWICK MOVED TO APPROVE THE SPECIAL EXCEPTION SUBJECT
TO STAFFS AND THE COMMISSIONERS RECOMMENDED CONDITIONS BASED ON FINDING
THE APPLICABLE CRITERIA ARE MET. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COMMISSIONER ROSS
AND PASSED UPON BY THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE — COMMISSIONERS
AKHMEDOV, ROSS, SANDAU, SHALE, SOUTHWICK, WILLIAMS, AND CHAIR SHINODA. VOTING
NO — COMMISSIONER AABERG AS HE FEELS IT SHOULD BE CONSISTANT WITH SIMILAR
DECISIONS IN THE PAST.

6. Public Hearing, Zone Change, R-1-10 to R-1-8, 1425 Kingston Dr
Petitioner Gary Cannon shared some statistics and wants to provide nice single-family homes.
Assistant Planning Director Joseph Simpson said that creating 10 single-family lots with a cul-
de-sac is in compliance with the provisions of the General Plan and the Mount Ogden
Community Plan and that staff recommended approval of the rezone petition.
Residents Shawn Mathewson, Sam Hunter, Jonathon Cornell, Jenny Nagy and Jeff Preston
were all in full support of the project baring there is enough parking and that home heights are
consistent with the current homes. Meredith Lund through Zoom disagreed and thought the
plan called for too many lots.

COMMISSIONER SHALE MADE A MOTION TO CLOSE THE PUBLIC HEARING.
COMMISSIONER ROSS SECONDED THE MOTION. ALL IN FAVOR - AYE

COMISSIONER AABERG MOVED TO APPROVE THE ZONE CHANGE. MOTION WAS
SECONDED BY COMMISSIONER SANDAU AND PASSED UPON BY THE FOLLOWING ROLL
CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE —- COMMISSIONERS AABERG, ROSS, SANDAU, SHALE, WILLIAMS,
AND CHAIR SHINODA. VOTING NO — NONE. COMMISSIONER SOUTHWICK RECUSED
HIMSELF DUE TO BEING INVOLVED WITH THE PROJECT. COMMISSIONER AKHMEDOV WAS
GONE AS HE HAD TO LEAVE EARLY.
