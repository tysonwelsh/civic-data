1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair Jacob called the meeting to order at 7:52 pm

2. PUBLIC HEARING
   a. Final Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of the City of West Jordan, Utah for
      Fiscal Year 2022
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck reported that the tentative budget was adopted on
May 26, 2021 and no changes had been made. The purpose of the hearing was to receive public
comment and the final adoption would take place on June 23, 2021.

Chair Jacob opened a public hearing at 7:53 pm
Citizen Comments:
None
Chair Jacob closed the public hearing at 7:53 pm
