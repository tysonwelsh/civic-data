WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
                        WORK MEETING MINUTES
                            MARCH 26, 2026

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Commissioner Iverson on March 26, 2026, in the Washington County
Commission Conference Room 421 in the Washington County Administration Building,
111 E Tabernacle Street, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Commissioner Adam
Snow, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales, Slade
Hughes-Veyo, Culinary Water, Zach Renstrom-Washington County Water Conservation
District, John Cazier-Diamond Valley Water, Joni Flint-Pine Valley/Brookside, Raymond
Kunz-Pine Valley/Brookside, Alan Davis-Winchester Hills, Scott Messel-Washington
County Community Development, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy
County Clerk Jayanne Lewis.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY WATER CONSERVATION
DISTRICT, LOCAL WATER MANAGERS, AND THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
REGARDING WATER USE AND ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT PERMITING

Commissioner Iverson welcomed all in attendance and thanked them for coming. Deputy
County Attorney Victoria Hales shared that the county currently allows Accessory
Dwelling Units (ADU) up to 2,500 square feet. With the current allowed ADU size, it could
potentially be taxing on the current water systems. There were several concerns shared
regarding requiring a separate water connection, the amount of water used by the ADU,
and what possible outcomes could come with users using more than their allotted water
shares.

A discussion on regulations for future ADU building permits raised concerns about having
“blanket” rules for the water companies. Each water company representative shared their
thoughts on how to address securing water for the ADU during the permit process. It was
determined that the regulation needed to come from each individual water company. A
second or separate will serve letter would be the only additional requirement from
Washington County.
