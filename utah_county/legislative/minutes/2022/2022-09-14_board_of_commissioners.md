COMMISSIONER LEE - ANNOUNCED THE UTAH COUNTY EMPLOYEE OF THE
MONTH FOR SEPTEMBER, 2022 - JEREMY SHEPHERD (READ NOMINATION):

| would like to nominate Jeremy Shepherd for the Utah County employee of the month!
Jeremy has been working in the Fleet Services Division as the Fleet Warehouse
Administrator for about a year and a half now. In that time he has really shown a perfect
example of the right attitude and work ethic along with making workplace interactions
enjoyable and fun. He shows up to work ready to accomplish what ever tasks are assigned
to him. He has been charged with learning a new software system where there are
aspects that are his sole responsibility. He has risen to that call by not only using the new
system and what tools are available to him there but learning the obsolete system well
enough to cross check needed items for vehicle routine maintenance and repairs. He will
then have the needed materials and parts billed and to the technician as fast as possible,
often before the parts are needed to complete the repairs.

Jeremy has already on several occasions jumped in to help in any capacity that he noticed
was needed. For example, more than once, when it was close to quitting time or a driver
of a county vehicle needed the vehicle, he has put on a pair of gloves and just started
helping mount and balance a set of tires for the vehicle. He also is constantly being
proactive on projects going on in the shop and asking his supervisors if they need anything
for it or if they want him to go pick up anything needed to finish the project. He then
organizes trips for parts to multiple vendors for the most efficient use of time.

Last but definitely not least. Jeremy has an awesome attitude and makes everyone he
deals with feel like their needs, no matter how large or small, are a top priority. He gets
along with everyone and seems to perfectly balance work ethic and social skills to
improve the work environment. He always strives to understand peoples requests when
purchasing parts and materials and will often recommend things that may also be needed
or may make the repair process more efficient or cost effective. He in general works
extremely hard to improve the work experience as a whole. We are very lucky to have him
here at Utah County!

JEREMY SHEPHERD - I’D LIKE TO THANK ALL THE GUYS I WORK WITH. WITHOUT
THEM, I WOULDN'T HAVE ANYTHING TO DO SO THEY KEEP ME PRETTY BUSY.
THANK YOU!
