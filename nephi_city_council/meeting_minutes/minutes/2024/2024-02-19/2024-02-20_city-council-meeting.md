GOLF SUPERINTENDENT JOHN BALE HONORED FOR HIS RETIREMENT:

    Mayor Seely honored retiring Golf Superintendent John Bale.  Mr. Bale had worked for Canyon Hill’s Golf Course since April 2008.  He had played an important role in many of the improvements to the course, including numerous new tee boxes and bunkers and the construction of the new practice area.  Mr. Bale had been responsible for supervising the summer help.  Mr.  Bale is an active member of the Men’s Golf Association and one of his best qualities is his ability to socialize well with the golfers and make them feel welcome on the course.  He was presented with a plaque of recognition and a retirement gift.  Mr. Bale thanked the City for the support he received and the equipment the city had provided for him to do his job.  He said that Nephi had great employees and he had enjoyed working for the City of Nephi.

CONTRACT WITH CREWS & ASSOCIATES APPROVED:

     Finance Director/City Recorder Lisa Brough updated the Council that at the last regular City Council meeting, the staff shared the results of a committee review for a municipal advisor request for proposals.  The committee recommended Crews & Associates as the City’s next Municipal Advisor.
      A Municipal Advisor, or MA, assists cities when they issue debt in the form of a bond or loan.  The MA provides advice during a bond process as well as oversees the bidding process.  If a bond rating is needed, the MA helps the city prepare for a bond rating call.  The MA will also review documents related to a bond issuance and assist a city in the selection of bond counsel.  Their fees generally come from the issuance of a debt instrument.  These services will be valuable as the city navigates funding options for electrical, sewer, gas, and general fund projects in the near future.
      Councilor Travis Worwood asked if Crews & Associates would be able to do impact fee analysis.  Ms. Brough was not sure of that and if they were not able to do the analysis, we would go out for another RFP to someone that could do that work. 
  A 3-year professional services contract had been reviewed by City Attorney Wright.   If the city is satisfied with the services provided by Crews & Associates, the contract could be extended in 1-year increments twice.  Councilor Travis Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the contract with Crews & Associates.  Councilor Callaway seconded the motion.  The motion passed unanimously.
