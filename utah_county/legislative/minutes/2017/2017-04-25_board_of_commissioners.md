7.    ADOPT A RESOLUTION AMENDING PROVISIONS OF THE UTAH COUNTY PROCUREMENT
      RULES AND REGULATIONS
(Track A, 41:35)
Commissioner Graves expressed interest in comparing the Utah County Procurement Rules and Regulations to other
counties, and the state, and suggested continuing this item for one week. Utah County Civil Division Chief Deputy
Attorney David Shawcroft mentioned a pending Request for Proposals (RFP) which could be dependent on the
adoption of this resolution. Utah County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson asked for clarification on the section
addressing ‘further negotiations,’ cautioning that future commissioners may interpret the paragraph differently.
Utah County Purchasing Manager Robert Baxter noted the intent is to not depart from the scope of the original RFP,
and discussed language options with the commissioners and Mr. Shawcroft to better clarify that meaning.
Commissioner Lee also commented on the difference between choosing three or five of the best responses to the
proposals, with the Board agreeing the top five should be the final pool.

(Track A, 51:55)
Utah County Purchasing Manager Robert Baxter mentioned he would rather not see the adoption of this resolution.
Mr. Baxter would prefer a selection committee from the respective departments completing the Request for
Proposals process, with the commissioners having the ability to veto a decision if needed. He expressed this
suggested procedure gives too much arbitrary power to the commissioners, and that is unsettling. The Board had
further discussion.

Utah County Health Department Director Ralph Clegg summarized the county and entire state continue to fail in
meeting a fine particulate standard for air quality as set by the Federal Clean Air Act. He introduced John Bird and
Bill Rees of the Utah State Division of Air Quality, and Mr. Rees talked about how Utah County was initially
classified as a ‘moderate’ area but the standards continually evolve and the county has now been elevated to
‘serious’ status. The commissioners discussed collaborating with the state to re-visit and potentially create a new
clean air plan.
