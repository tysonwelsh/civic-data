Presentation - Silver Creek

Principal Planner,
presented a request to amend Block 9 and Parcel A of the Silver Creek Unit l Subdivision for discussion and approval.

Presentation - Last Chance Phase 1
Tiffanie N. Robinson,
Senior Planner,
presented the Last Chance Phase l Subdivision located in White Pine Canyon for public hearing and approval.
Spencer White,
Owner,
briefly addressed the planning commission.
Commission Chair Mooney opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:28 p.m.
Tim Lewis was called to speak.
Diane Tanner was called to speak.
Aleisha Verbaas was called to speak.
Commission Chair Mooney closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:33 p.m.
Commissioners addressed the concerns that were raised during the public hearing. Mr. White and Planner Robinson provided additional information to the discussion based on comments made by members of the public.
Commissioners had additional questions for the applicant. Mr. White, Attorney Viti, Director Barnes, and Planner Robinson responded to the commissioner’s questions.
David Smith with TCFC provided additional information to the discussion.
Matthew Nagie made a motion to approve the Last Chance Phase 1 Subdivision pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval outlined in the staff report. Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Thomas Cooke.
