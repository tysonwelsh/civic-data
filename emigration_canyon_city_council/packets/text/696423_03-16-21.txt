                         The Meeting May Be Closed for Reasons Allowed by Statute

Pursuant to Utah Code 52-4-202 the undersigned, as Chair of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council,
hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the electronic meetings of the Council will present a substantial
risk to the health and safety of those who may be present at the anchor location for the following reasons:

      The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under a “High Level of Transmission” alert stage for
the COVID-19 pandemic; and

      The regular meeting place for the Council (the Fire Station in the Canyon) does not have sufficient space in
the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing to keep people safe from infection.
