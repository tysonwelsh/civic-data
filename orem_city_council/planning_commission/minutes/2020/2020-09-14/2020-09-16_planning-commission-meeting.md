REGULAR MEETING

NOTICE OF ALL-ELECTRONIC MEETING AND OF OPPORTUNITY FOR PUBLIC PARTICIPATION
Pursuant to Planning Commission Chairman Mickey Cochran’s Proclamation dated September 16, 2020, the Orem
City Planning Commission meeting on September 16, 2020 will be an all-electronic meeting. There will be no
anchor location for the meeting. The City cannot ensure the recommended minimum of six feet of social distancing
for meeting participants at the usual anchor location in the Orem City Council Chambers. Therefore, holding the
meeting at the City Council Chambers would present a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be
present at the meeting and may contribute to the spread of COVID-19. There will be no public access to the Orem
City Council chambers. Those desiring to view or participate in the meeting must do so electronically.

To view the meeting: Please go to orem.org/meetings.

To make a comment: Please submit your comment through: orem.org/comments OR
PlanningCommissionPublicComments@orem.org

Comments will be accepted before and during the meeting. All comments received will be entered into the record
and forwarded to each Planning Commission member for review. Comments will also be read aloud during the live
meeting when required by law or when time permits. Once an item has been voted on, that item will be closed to
additional comments.

Thank you for your cooperation during these challenging times.

Chair Cochran asked Mr. Harding to introduce Agenda Item 3.1:

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Jared Osmond for the Planning Commission to approve the site plan of Osmond
Retail Orem located at 1296 West University Parkway in the PD21 zone.

                                               Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to build a small
                                               1300 square foot building for Roxberry Juice with a drive-thru. They
                                               will remove the current CNG awning.

                                               Architecture: The proposed one-story structure will be 1,300 square
                                               feet.

Parking: They have met all the required parking requirements. Six spaces were required, and they have provided two
existing plus four new places for a total of six parking spaces including a handicap space.

Dumpster: The dumpster is to the north of the project and enclosed as required by code.

Landscaping: The overall landscape plan complies with applicable City and PD21 codes. The existing landscaping
along University Parkway will remain.

Transportation/Engineering: No Concerns.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the site plan of Osmond Retail Orem located at 1296
West University Parkway in the PD21 zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Harding.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to speak via telephone. Mr. Osmond introduced himself. He said Roxberry Juice
has signed the contract. Mr. Cochran asked if there is not a need for a natural gas station there. Mr. Osmond said he
understands that they have sold the business and have moved the equipment off site, but he does not know what
happened or why they sold it. Ms. Jensen asked which side of the building the drive-thru is on. Mr. Cochran said it
will be on the University Parkway side.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did,
he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Roberts moved to approve the site plan of Osmond Retail Orem located at 1296
West University Parkway in the PD21 zone. Mr. Cook seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Mickey Cochran,
Carl Cook, Camille Jensen and Barry Roberts. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Cochran asked Mr. Harding to introduce Agenda Item 3.4:

Agenda Item 3.4 is a request by Cameron Davis for the Planning Commission to vacate Lots 10 and 18, Elysian F
final plat approval of Elysian Fields Plat E located at 882 N R8 zone.

                            Staff Presentation: The applicant lives in the proposed Lot 1 (882 North 1000 East),
                            Jackie Wood lives on the proposed Lot 2 (862 North 1000 East), and the Wollaston
                            family lives on the proposed Lot 3 (1029 East 850 North).

                            The applicant is interested in constructing a garage on his property in which to store
                            his boat. After talking with the planning department, he came to the conclusion that
                            in order to get a structure the size he needed, he would need to purchase additional
                            land from his neighbor, Jackie Wood. This would allow him enough space to build
                            the addition and be able to meet the required setbacks.

Ms. Wood has been cooperative and has agreed to sell the portion of land that the applicant needs. As part of the
review of this plat, it was discovered that the Wollaston family had an illegal nuisance strip they purchased from
Ms. Wood. The City required them to fix the issue, and they decided it would be best to be a part of this plat
amendment.

There is an existing easement on Lot 1 that the applicant determined needed to be vacated to build the garage. All
other letters of vacation were obtained a few months ago. However, it was discovered that CenturyLink had an
existing line in that easement. After working with them for a few months, they have moved the line to the proposed
easement and have provided a letter of vacation.

All the proposed lots meet the minimum lot size and width requirements.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Lots 10 and 18, Elysian Fields Plat A and final plat
approval of Elysian Fields Plat E located at 882 North 1000 East in the R8 zone.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to speak via telephone. Cameron Davis introduced himself. He said he purchased
