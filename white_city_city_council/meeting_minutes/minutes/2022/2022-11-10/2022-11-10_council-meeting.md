                                             Workshop

Engineering Projects and Draft Walk White City Plan

                 Shane Ellis, Engineer, presented the Walk White City Plan. An open house will
be held on November 17, 2022, from 6:00 pm – 8:00 pm at Alta View Elementary School. The
draft plan will be presented to the community, he will review the comments and provide input for
the final draft.

Design work for 9400 South is being drafted. There is not enough funding to complete both sides
of the road so the east side will be completed. The project will go out to bid this winter and
construction will begin in spring.

Wall Consultant Group (WCG) continues to work on the design for the Sego Lily project. The road
is not in terrible condition; therefore, it is recommending a 4 inch mill and overlay instead of 2
inches. He has asked for additional information on a 2 and 3 inch mill and overlay. The bid for
the 2 inch mill and overlay came in at $3 million. He will get an updated cost estimate for 4 inches.

              Mayor Flint stated she will need to request additional funding from the Greater
Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD).

               Mr. Ellis stated he will look at the cost and see how much more funding will be
needed. During the Sego Lily workshop meeting, WCG stated they would not be able to finish the
project design until summer 2023; however, he has since pushed them to get it done sooner.
They anticipate the project design to be done in March 2023. Once the design is finished, a

Dimple Dell Advisory Board Report

                Marc Perry, White City Metro Township Liaison, Dimple Dell Advisory Board,

Unified Fire Authority

                Captain Ken Aldridge stated the Wildland Division responded to 75 wildfires in
the western United States, completed 100’s of hours of grant and mitigation work. With
Thanksgiving coming up, the safety message this month is kitchen safety. He recommends
staying alert while prepping meals, unattended cooking is the leading cause of fires. There were
43 calls for the month of October. Chief Klein from Sandy Fire is retiring and is in the process of
looking for his replacement. After the replacement is hired, they will introduce themselves to the
Council.

Unified Police Department

               Sergeant Luis Lovato reviewed the stats for October, stating there were 41 calls
for service. Vehicle burglaries are trending up and residents are reporting suspicious activities
more. This time of year, individuals are warming up their cars unattended and that increases
stolen cars. UPD completed its take back initiative and received 55 ½ pounds of medication for
destruction. UPD and Jiffy Lube have partnered to offer a service where the catalytic converter is
engraved with a VIN number and painted to help deter thefts. Shop with a Shield will be held on
December 10, 2022. If the Council knows anyone that could benefit from Shop with a Shield,
notify Chief Thomas.

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Municipality pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-202 provides that any two or more public
agencies
may enter into an agreement with one another for joint or cooperative actions; and

       WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-214 provides that any public agency may convey
property to or acquire property from any other public agencies for consideration as may be agreed
upon; and

       WHEREAS, the City and the County are public agencies as contemplated in the above
referenced sections of the Utah Code (more specifically referred to as UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-
13-101, et seq. - Interlocal Cooperation Act); and

      WHEREAS, the conveyance of property provided herein is an interest in real property as
contemplated in the Interlocal Cooperation Act; and

        WHEREAS, the County owns a parcel of real property known as Big Bear Park (hereinafter
referred to as the “Park”); and

        WHEREAS, the Park is located at approximately 930 East Onyx Lane, White City, Utah
(Parcel Nos. 28-08-179-014, 28-08-179-013, 28-08-179-015), and as further described in the
quitclaim deed (hereinafter the “Quitclaim Deed”, attached hereto as Exhibit A, and incorporated
herein by this reference); and

        WHEREAS, the Council desires to formally accept the transfer and conveyance the Park
to the White City Metro Township from County, for the purposes and on the terms and conditions
set forth in this Agreement.

        NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the White City Metro Township Council that the
Mayor is authorized to execute the Interlocal Agreement with Salt Lake County accepting the
transfer of the Big Bear Park to the White City Metro Township as set forth in Exhibit “A” attached
hereto.

South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                  Council Member Price stated the budget meeting will be on December 12, 2022,
at 6:00 pm. The Audit Committee completed a fraud risk assessment, and the results came in as
low risk. There were five human cases of West Nile Virus in Utah. A total of four workers
compensation incidents were reported. The number of acres that were treated in 2022 were
average and traps were down to approximately 5,000. The manager is going to retire in 2023 and
the District will be working to fill that vacancy.
