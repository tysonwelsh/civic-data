Fiscal Items

              Mayor Clayton reviewed the bill in the amount of $689.50 for the law enforcement
survey and the bill in the amount of $5,497 for legal services for the month of November.

Update on Metro Township Bill

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, stated the bill is in
good shape and Representative Jordan Teuscher has committed to keeping it clean and not
allowing any further amendments. It should move during the early part of the session.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Law Enforcement Community Survey

                Council Member Stitzer delivered a PowerPoint presentation entitled Copperton
Metro Township Citizen Survey Results 2023. She reviewed the methodology used, the
responses received to the following questions:
   • How Important are fast responses from first responders to emergent calls?
   • How important is it to see your police officers engaged with our community outside their
       field of work?
   • Would you be in favor of a tax or fee increase if that meant better police services?
   • Would you be supportive of an increase in police patrols in our community?
   • Do you feel we need more police officers than we currently have?
She also reviewed the analysis and recommendations, and a summary of the survey.

Current UPD Interlocal Agreement

                Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, stated the draft is
not ready for review by the full Utah Transit Authority (UTA) Board. The next draft is expected to
be sent to the attorneys involved by January 5, 2024, prior to the Zoom meeting scheduled for
11:00 a.m. on January 11th. It would be helpful to know whether Copperton Metro Township
wants to be a part of Unified Police Department (UPD) to ensure Copperton’s needs are reflected
in the agreement.

Later in the meeting, after the closed session, he explained the Interlocal Agreement allows for a
member to withdraw with 12 months’ notice, subject to cooperation provisions.

    Bingham Cemetery Board

               Council Member Stitzer stated the Wreaths Across America ceremony was held

Copperton Metro Township Council Member Reports

    Mayor Clayton

       Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)
       Council of Governments (COG)

               Mayor Clayton stated most of the conversation has been budget and Justice
Court related. The MSD is trying to recuperate $1.5 million that has been paid to the Justice
Court. He and Mayor Kelly Bush, Kearns Metro Township, would rather see that money
reallocated to the metro townships than have it in the MSD’s General Fund. They have been
going back and forth with legal counsel.

    Council Member Olsen

       Unified Police Department (UPD)
       Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

                Council Member Olsen stated the UPD Board meeting regarding the interlocal
agreement was last week and the SLVLESA meeting will be tomorrow. The main issue is that
Sheriff Rivera’s requested funding for more officers to patrol those areas of the Southwest parks
up to July 2024. The Board has not acted, and Council Member Olsen anticipates it will ultimately
vote to deny the request. The Board could vote on the interlocal agreement as soon as January
18th. The other members of the Board feel the need to move forward regardless of Copperton’s
stance.

    Council Member Bailey

       Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
       Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

               Council Member Bailey stated most of the discussion during both meetings was
focused on budgets. There was a 12 percent increase for the upcoming fiscal year on the agenda,
and it passed. There will probably be a similar increase next year.

    Council Member Stitzer

       Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

              Council Member Stitzer stated the Board of Trustees has not met since the last
Council meeting, but there will be a budget amendment hearing on Wednesday, December 27 to
