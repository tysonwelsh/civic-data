                                      RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       ORDINANCE NO. 20-23

 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 15 OF THE RIVERTON CITY CODE, ADOPTING
THE ENGINEERING STANDARDS AND SPECIFICATIONS GOVERNING DEVELOPMENT
  OF PROPERTY, ACCOMPANYING APPENDICES, AMENDMENTS THERETO AND
                         RELATED MATTERS

        WHEREAS, Title 15, of the Riverton City Code sets uniform standards for construction of
buildings and facilities within Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Engineering Department has drafted a uniform set of standards
to regulate public development of facilities which will ultimately be dedicated to the City, or will
otherwise connect to City- owned facilities; and

        WHEREAS, recent developments in Utah law require formal adoption of said standards by the
Riverton City Council; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the
citizens of Riverton City to amend Title 15 of the Riverton City Code, replacing Exhibit A with the
2020 Standard Specifications and Plans enclosed herein;

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

        Section 1. Chapter 070 of Title 15 is hereby amended as described in the attached
                   Exhibit “A” incorporated herein by reference. The City Recorder is hereby
                   directed to codify said standards in a manner consistent with the Riverton
                   City Code.
        Section 2. All other ordinances in conflict with or inconsistent with this ordinance are
                   hereby repealed.
        Section 3. This Ordinance shall take effect upon publication or upon posting as
                   required by law.
