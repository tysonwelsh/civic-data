                               RESOLUTION #2024 – 05 – 03
     A RESOLUTION CREATING EMIGRATION CANYON COMMUNITY COUNCIL
    COMMITTEE AND DESIGNATING OFFICIALS AND MEMBERS OF COMMITTEE

        WHEREAS, municipalities may create boards, commissions, or committees to administer
statutes, ordinances, and programs of the municipality under Utah Code Ann §§ 10-3b-104 (Power
& Duties of Mayor), 10-3b-403 (Power & Duties of Council); and

       WHEREAS, starting in the 1960s, the community of Emigration Canyon has been served
by several community groups and private non-profit entities; and

        WHEREAS, one such group, the Emigration Canyon Community Counsel, LLC filed
for dissolution in May 2024 and transferred all assets remaining after dissolution to the
municipality of Emigration Canyon; and

       WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon desires to continue serving its residents by creating
a municipal community council committee to carry out various projects and business of the
municipality.

    NOW, THEREFORE, THE FOLLOWING IS RESOLVED BY THE EMIGRATION
CANYON CITY COUNCIL EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

       Section I: The Emigration Canyon Community Council Committee (“Committee”) is
hereby created.

       Section II: The Emigration Canyon City Council hereby appoints Councilmember
Catherine “Rin” Harris to supervise the Committee and any volunteers accepted to the
Committee thereafter.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the Emigration Canyon City Council, Utah, this 28th day
of May in the year 2024.
                                           EMIGRATION CANYON




                                           Joe Smolka, Mayor
