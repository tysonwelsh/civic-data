CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting 8-2-22, work session minutes 8-9-22, a list of claims on the warrant
register dated 8-16-22 and the sale of burial rights to Lloyd Olpin. Councilor Memmott seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

    Juab County Travel Council Director Shanna Memmott addressed the council and informed them
abut a public meeting that will be held on September 1st from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. The meeting is
sponsored by the Forest Service and the purpose is to receive public comment on the proposed fees to
four areas up the canyon that will directly affect the residents of Juab County. Ms. Memmott said that
residents can also go online and give comment. Ms. Memmott also said that she has recently been
appointed to the Scenic Byway Committee by the Governor and she has received a $95,000 grant for the
purpose of rebranding the county.
   Mayor Seely thanked Ms. Memmott for keeping them informed and suggested that all the council
join him in making comments and in attending the meeting.

LOVELESS ESTATES PLAT A SUBDIVSION FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson updated the council that previously the Planning Commission and
City Council had approved the Loveless Estates PUD development. With the recent changes in the
economy and the housing sector, the developers from Loveless are looking to phase their development.
The development has now been split into a Phase A and B.
  The Development Review Committee had reviewed the two proposed phases and have recommended
deed restrictions for snow removal and garbage pickup on the homes facing 900 N and 815 N. Once the
loop for Loveless Estates is completed in Phase B, these deed restrictions could be removed. 200 West
will also need to be completed in Phase A. The Planning Commission reviewed the new Phase A and
recommended approval with these requirements from the Development Review Committee.
  Councilor Worwood moved to approve the final plat for Loveless Estates. Councilor Memmott
seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

NORTH RIDGE ESTATES PLAT “C” ZONE CHANGE APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that Mt. Peak Development, owned by Terry Messersmith, is
seeking approval for a rezone of a portion of property located at 100 East and 1500 North that had
recently been rezoned from CU-1 to R-3-PUD, now the city and Mr. Messersmith feel a R-3-Z zoning
would be more appropriate for a proposed phase of his development (Plat C) that would include six (6)
individually owned lots with three (3) twin-homes on the lots, for a total of six (6) individual units. This
would be in keeping with other phases of this development that are also this zoning and use. The
developer does not want these planned twin homes to be part of the PUD HOA. Mr. Atkinson stated
that this makes some sense because Phase C is separated from the rest of the planned PUD
development by 1500 North.
   Councilor Parady moved to approve Ordinance 08-16-2022 amending the zone map of Nephi City and
changing North Ridge Estates Phase C from R-3(PUD) to R-3(Z). Councilor Ostler seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote .

NORTH RIDGE ESTATE PLAT C FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council the final plat for North Ridge Estates Plat “C”. Councilor
Worwood moved to approve the final plat. Councilor Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed
on a unanimous vote.

IT SERVICES AGREEMENT WITH ROCKY MOUNTAIN TECHNOLOGY SERVICES (RMT) RATIFIED:

   Mr. Atkinson explained that for several months, staff had been looking for an Information
Technology solution for the city. Through a proposal process, a staff committee examined the pros and
cons from two different vendors. Ultimately, the committee recommended Rocky Mountain Technology
Services (RMT).
  RMT serves several neighboring cities with high praise from their references. It is hopeful that the city
will have a dedicated technician to work with the city on our IT needs. They also have a strong
partnership with CentraCom who provides VOIP and internet services for the city. They have BCI
certification as well so they can work with our Police Department.
   Councilor Parady moved to ratify the Mayor’s signing of the IT services agreement with RMT.
Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR AND CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

 The council reviewed two business license application for LMV Company and Ha Ha Powersports LLC.
 Mr. Atkinson reminded the council about the annual Utah League of Cities and Towns conference to
be held October 5th-7th.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn regular council meeting and to convene into executive session to
discuss the character and competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
Meeting adjourned at 7:40 p.m.

WORK SESSION CONVENED AT 7:55 p.m.

    1.  The council discussed the Racoon Eye Annexation zone. Mr. Atkinson said that he had talked
       to Mr. Humpreys and they worked out a solution that will work for him and work well with the
       city’s conceptual zone map.
    2. The council discussed the agreement with Nephi Irrigation Company about measuring the
       amount of water they use out of the irrigation pond. Mr. Atkinson stated that there is a
       verbal agreement that we will keep the pond full if we can use the water from Bradley Springs.
    3. Mr. Atkinson asked the council if they would like him to prepare a FAQ web page for
       misconceptions about the role and authority of local government. The council directed him to
       do so.
    4. Meeting adjourned at 8:45 p.m.
