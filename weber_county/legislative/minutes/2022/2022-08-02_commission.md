D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: I’ve recently listened to a radio announcement about being kind.
    I had a lot of great examples in my life that taught me being angry and offended is a choice. But also that being
    kind is a choice. We often don’t know what going on in the life of someone we are communicating with, to walk
    in their shoes, to live in their home, live with their circumstances. Many times if we had to fill in someone else’s
    shoes we’d keep the same pair we have on when we find out what’s really going on with them. Choose to be kind
    and stay focused on that.

     1. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 37-2022 APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE TRAPPERS LOOP
        WATER IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT.
            Stacy Skeen (Commission Office): Recently the Commission created the Trappers Loop Water Improvement
            District. At that time, two board members were appointed, leaving one position to fill. The position was
            reposted and one application was received from Brian Woolsey. This would complete the Trapper’s Loop
            board.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 37-2022 appointing members to the Trappers Loop Water
            Improvement District; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
     2. APPROVAL OF CONTRACT WITH CITYGATE, LLC FOR A PARAMEDIC SERVICE STUDY.
            Eli Johnson (Sheriff): Part of the paramedic program with the three primary fire departments that provide
            paramedic services for the county; they put together a 10 year spending plan to help maintain and manage a
            budget provided by the county. The plan includes a periodic outside audit of the services being provided. This
            is a contract to bring in an outside eyes to audit those services. This contract will include a study previously
            approved by the Commission.

            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a with CityGate, LLC for a Paramedic Service Study; Commissioner
            Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
