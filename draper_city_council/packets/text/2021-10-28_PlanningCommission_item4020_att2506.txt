SUMMARY and BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a plat amendment application for
approximately 9.8 acres located at approximately 12197 S. 300 East (Exhibit B). The
property is currently zoned RM2. The applicant is requesting a plat amendment to allow
for a new 54 lot single-family residential subdivision.

The subject property has historically been utilized as agricultural or farming land since at
least 1978 when the City incorporated. There have been previous applications to rezone
the property and the most recent one was in earlier this year. An application in the fall


NCS Residential Development                                          App. # SUBD-094-2021
Plat Amendment Request                        1
2020 was for a commercial zoning, and there were concerns expressed about that request
from the public. The applicant submitted a subsequent application earlier this year and
the site was rezoned to RM2. Based on concerns raised at the Planning Commission
hearing for that rezone, a Development Agreement was proposed and adopted by the City
Council (Exhibit E).



ANALYSIS
General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
High Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is
characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the RM2 zoning classification (Exhibit D). Draper City
Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020(B)(5) states: “the purpose of the RM1 and RM2
zones is to permit well designed apartments, townhomes, twin homes, and condominiums
at relatively high densities that are appropriately buffered from and compatible with
surrounding land uses.” Although single family detached homes are not stated in the


NCS Residential Development                                         App. # SUBD-094-2021
Plat Amendment Request                        2
purpose of the zone, the zoning designation allows single-family lots as a permitted use
and the applicant has received approval of a Development Agreement (Exhibit E) to allow
up to 63 single family residential detached lots.

The property is surrounded by a mix of zoning designations. To the east is the CSD-DD
(Day Dairy Commercial Special District) and CC (Community Commercial) zone. The areas
to the south are zoned CC. On the west side, across 300 East, is CR (Regional Commercial)
zoning. To the north, the residential area is zoned RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000
square foot lot minimum).

Subdivision Layout. The applicant is proposing a 54 lot single family development (Exhibit
F). There will be one public road near the center of the subdivsion that connects 12200
South on the east to the roundabout at 300 East on the west boundary. All of the homes
will be located on private streets laid out through the subdivsion. The lots will be
developed with single family homes, with no multi-family or attached units proposed. The
largest lot proposed is 9,844 square feet and the smallest lot is 4,063 square feet. There is
no proposed connection with any of the developments to the north, south or east.

The development agreement allows for up to 63 single family lots with a minimum of 4,000
square feet for each lot. Setbacks were also outlined in the Development Agreement and
are as follows:

                 Front Yard ........................................... 20 feet
                 Side Yard Interior............................... 5 feet
                 Side Yard Collector ............................ 15 feet
                 Side Yard Private Street .................... 10 feet
                 Rear Yard ............................................ 15 feet

One final item in the Development Agreement was that all road widths comply with the
DCMC.

Because this will be developed as a single family subdivsion, there are no minimum design
requirements for the homes.

Criteria For Approval.
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for
such requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by
        signing an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use
        Authority finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no
        public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application
based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff
report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
         Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
         and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
2.       Once final plat has been approved, a Land Disturbance Permit is required prior to
         construction activities onsite. Permit is obtained through the Engineering Division.
3.       Any work in a public right-of-way will require an Encroachment Permit obtained
         through the Engineering Division.
4.       If the plat amendment is not recorded within one year from the date of approval or
         construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced,
