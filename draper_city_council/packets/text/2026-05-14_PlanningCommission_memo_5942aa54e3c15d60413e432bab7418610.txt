Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Preliminary Subdivision Plat and Conditional
Use Permits for property fencing and a gated entry for approximately 11.67 acres located
on east side of 1700 East and South of 12600 South, at approximately 12696 S. 1700 E.
The property is currently zoned RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 ft² min. lot size). The
applicant is requesting that a Preliminary Subdivision Plat be approved to allow for the
development of the currently vacant site into 18 lots. The applicant is also requesting
approval of Conditional Use Permits for fencing over six (6) feet in height and for a gated
entry to the development.
