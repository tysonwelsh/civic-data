BUSINESS MEETING
Meeting began at – 6:00 p.m.
Commissioner Pinon read the Chairs Opening Statement.
1) General Plan Review and Revisions
Ms. Davies provided an update and advised of feedback on five main items to address in the plan.
1. Too long and repetitive - Ms. Davies is working on reducing it. Went through the items removed
and reference on SWOT and APAE analysis. Cut out images and pages with only a small amount
of information to condense into fewer pages.
2. Too much context and background - Turned more context into information boxes.
3. Opinions vs. Facts - Larger issues to be worked through with the Council. Value statements are
appropriate. Implementation and projections are published facts. Commissioner Karkut said he
supports the plan, and this is a values-based document and would like to see the community
going forward in the future. Mr. Long said this has been a long process. Recommendation to the

has responsibility to do with it as they please. Recognize the Planning Commissioners scope of

4. Water issues – Commissioner Karkut said in favor of actions that maintain or promote water
quality. This is an admiral goal. None of their jurisdictions can manage water quality. Should
use words like manage, but can use the word promote, it is values based. Instead of water rights
and quantity equality, instead maybe addressing concerns of water rights and quantity.
Discussing challenges, maybe use considering and addressing water quality concerns.
Recognition of issues raised by the community. Write up of recommendations have not included
water and moved under the topic of sustainability. Character areas include a vision, main
canyon road character area, with possible neighborhood zone. Neighborhood descriptions were
moved to the appendix. All the language was left in that includes the neighborhood descriptions.
Helpful to have description of each area in the body of the text, followed by the vision
implementation. Adding additional pages in the main document is supported, modify paragraph
instead of neighborhood descriptions included in the Main Canyon Road Character Area. Can
be included in the glossary appendix and add a hotlink.
Environment chapter is where water will live since it’s been pulled out of other places. Remove
or rework build limits have been under consideration based on water access for many years
(reference to docs where this is discussed) limitations highlighted sentence on page 128
regarding watershed, as the figure is aged.
5. Working groups – Could remove reference to working group. If it doesn’t make sense to keep it,
the Council can organize the groups at a later date. Language to suggest implement working
groups. Something will happen to implement the working group and if the council wants to say
how this will move forward and the planning commission should say it the way they see
appropriate.
Land Use Work Program - Implementation of working groups is up to the Council to determine.
Need to be conscious of avoiding requirements and need to establish a working group as
appropriate.
Action c – Develop a working group at the councils discretion.
Action f - In adopting ordinances, consider working group recommendations.
Action h – Limit rather than prohibit.
Ms. Davies said image and map revisions aren’t in the plan yet, but should be finished by Monday,
January 11th.
