BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 2.71
acres located on the east side of State St., at approximately 12033 South State St. (Exhibit
B). The property is currently zoned CR. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment
be approved to allow for the division of the land into two lots.

The property is Lot 3 of the Factory Stores of America Subdivision Plat, recorded in 1995.
There are two businesses located on the property. On the western half is an existing hotel,
Quality Inn. This hotel was built in 1996. An adjacent business, Cowabunga Bay, utilizes the
eastern half of the property for additional outdoor seating space for the water park.

The property has been assigned the CR zoning classification (Exhibit D). According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the CR zone is to
“provide areas where a combination of destination oriented business, retail commercial,
entertainment, and related uses may be established, maintained and protected to serve both
residents and nonresidents of the city. Typical uses in this zone include large scale, master
planned commercial centers with outlying commercial pads, big box stores, and offices.” The
subject property is adjacent to the CR zone on the north, east, and south sides. To the west
is State Street and I-15.

Subdivision Layout. The subdivision plat will create two lots (Exhibit E). The western lot, Lot
105, will house the existing hotel building and half the existing parking lot. This lot is 1.33
acres in size. The eastern lot, Lot 106, will house the Cowabunga Bay facilities and the other
half of the parking lot. This lot is 1.38 acres in size. The hotel will retain enough parking to
comply the City’s parking standards of one parking space per hotel room. The parking on
Lot 106 will provide parking for future development of that property.
