Re: Distracted Driving Policy Comments: Draper City is committed to providing employees with a safer work environment. The addition of Section 10350 will promote safe driving habits and modify behavior to minimize or prevent motor vehicle incidents/accidents due to distracted driving.   Our liability insurer, Utah Risk Management Agency (URMA), has also encouraged member cities to address the causes of vehicle accidents by way of training, policies, etc.
The proposed policy has been reviewed by legal counsel and has also been reviewed by department directors. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Resolution 20-55 Adopting Section 10350 - Distracted Driving 12/7/2020 Cover Memo
