This meeting was held electronically.
This meeting was held as an electronic meeting pursuant to Draper City Resolution #14-54. As restrictions
take place across the State for limiting public gatherings, we want to make sure everyone interested in
participating in City Council meetings can do so in a manner that they feel most comfortable. Individuals
could participate by:

      1. Listening through the Draper City Website: https://www.draper.ut.us/95/Agendas-Minutes
      2. Emailing comments to public.comment@draper.ut.us by 5 p.m. on the day of the meeting.
         These will become part of the public record.
      3. Requesting the Zoom Meeting ID from public.comment@draper.ut.us by noon, May 18, 2020,
         to be able to speak during the Public Hearing portions of the meeting.


Business Meeting

1.0      Call to Order: Board Chair - Troy K. Walker

2.0      Motion: Approval of June 11, 2019, and June 18, 2019, Municipal Board Authority
         Meeting Minutes.

2.1      Boardmember Marsha Vawdrey moved to accept the June 11, 2019, and the June 18,
         2019, Municipal Board Authority Meeting Minutes. Boardmember Fred Lowry
         seconded the motion.

2.2      A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts, and
         Vawdrey voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.0      Action Item: Resolution # MBA 20-01 (Legislative Action), Adopting the FY2020-2021
         Tentative Budget and setting a public hearing date for June 2, 2020, to take public comment
         on the Municipal Building Authority Tentative Budget.
3.1   Bob Wylie, Finance Director, said that within the Municipal Building Authority there was
      only one expenditure line item for $229,230. This was the bond payment for the series
      2016 MBA funding bond. The original bond that it was issued for was the Suncrest Fire
      Station.

3.2   Boardmember Mike Green moved to approve Resolution #MBA 20-01, adopting the
      FY2020-2021 Tentative Budget. Boardmember Marsha Vawdrey seconded the
      motion.

3.3   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts, and
      Vawdrey voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

4.0   Adjourn to a Redevelopment Agency of Draper City Meeting.

4.1   Boardmember Mike Green moved to adjourn to a Redevelopment Agency of Draper
      City Meeting. Boardmember Tasha Lowery seconded the motion.

4.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts,
      Walker, and Vawdrey voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.


4.3   The meeting adjourned at 8:44 p.m.
