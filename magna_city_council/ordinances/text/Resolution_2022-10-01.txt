MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.:.2 2-10-01 DATE: October 11, 2022
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL DESIGNATING AN EMERGENCY MANAGER FOR
THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RECITALS
A. The Magna Metro Township ("Magna City") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-
2a-401 et seq.
B. House Bill 96 adopted in April, 2021 requires each Metro Township to designate an
Emergency Manager, and
C. The Emergency Manager shall oversee the contract with Salt Lake County Emergency
Management and Unified Fire Authority for planning, response, mitigation and recovery
efforts; and
D. The Emergency Manager will coordinate with Unified command and responding agencies
during emergency incidents that reach the level of activation; and
E. Overall operational response should fall to the appropriate agency(s) based on the type of
incident with the Magna Metro Township included by participating in the unified command;
and
F. The Emergency Manager shall disseminate policy level priorities of the township to Unified
command; and
G. The policy group representative should follow the interim successors process by authority
being the Mayor then the Deputy Mayor, then any available council member; and
H. The Magna Metro Township Council, the legislative body of Magna City elected by the
voters of Magna City, has determined it is in the best interest of Magna City to designate
Council Member Audrey Pierce to serve as the Magna Metro Township Emergency
Manager.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township Council, the Council
formally designate Council Member Audrey Pierce to serve as the Emergency Manager for the
Magna Metro Township,
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED the Council hereby directs staff to provide a copy of this
Resolution to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District to inform it of the appointment.
