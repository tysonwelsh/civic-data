    2. PUBLIC HEARING – to accept public comment on adoption of a Transportation Master Plan
       Element of the General Plan
          a. STAFF PRESENTATION
              Shauna Talbot, City Planner, explained her desire to implement a master transportation
              plan for Nephi City that would include a map. This information would be helpful in
              making decisions for future development, utilities, etc. Shauna introduced Micklane
              Farmer who was instrumental in acquiring a grant to help fund the project and is
              overseeing the project details.
   Mr. Farmer explained his background with Jones and Demille and his experience
   developing transportation plans. He detailed the process of data collection, planning,
   and the public open house that have been part of the Transportation Master Plan
   process. He presented an overview of the information contained on the city’s master
   plan website. (Link to Transportation Master Plan). He explained the document tab and
   the links included to the municipal code, zoning maps, the city’s general plan, and Nephi
   parks and recreation.
   Mr. Farmer discussed the roadway classification map, potential future interchanges, and
   the coordination with R6 for the agri park development. He highlighted the importance
   of the grid network expansion and the potential for a belt loop circulation with
   interchanges.
   The commissioners discussed whether location of arterial collector roads on the map
   has weight to keep someone from building and if the area around the airport would stay
   an industrial zone. It was determined flexibility would be necessary in the plan due to
   potential future developments. The city’s data would also be shared with the county to
   help with their planning.
b. PUBLIC HEARING
   OPEN: 7:25 pm. Chairman Thomson opened the public meeting and explained the rules
   and procedures.

3. PUBLIC HEARING - to accept public comment on changes to Section 11.6.12(5)(h)(1) Site Plan
   Review to establish maximum noise levels in industrial and commercial zones, (this applies to
   data centers within the new ID4 zone and can also be applied in all industrial or commercial
   zones).
       a. STAFF PRESENTATION
           Shauna Talbot, City Planner, introduced the proposal of adding noise limitations to the
           city’s development code for industrial and commercial zones. When site plan is
           approved, it will be established which parameters can be used to determine compliance
           with our noise ordinance. The maximum allowable level is to be measured at the
           property line. Verification can be conducted by the city with the specified equipment, or
           the city could request a special inspection of the developer at time of building permit.
           Testing could also occur if concerns were raised after the operations have commenced.
           Ms. Talbot detailed the process of collecting data using a decibel meter. She shared the
           results of the measurements taken at various locations, including Eagle Mountain data
           center, industrial parks, and commercial areas.
           The planning commission asked questions concerning penalties for not complying with
           the noise ordinance, where measurements were taken, and the time that noise was
           measured. Penalty is currently a Class C Misdemeanor with the possibility to add more
           penalties to an agreement with a developer if desired. Measurements would be taken at
           the property line for 30 seconds. The commission discussed the importance of accurate
           noise measurements and suggested measuring randomly, at various times of day and
           unannounced. The possibility of requiring a decibel radar on sight and having a third
           party test if discrepancy among city and owner was mentioned.
       b. PUBLIC HEARING
           OPEN: 8:06 pm. Chairman Cory Thomson opened the public hearing.
           PUBLIC COMMENT: None
           CLOSED: 8:07 pm. Public hearing was closed.
       c. COMMISSION DISCUSSION
           The planning commission was grateful for the testing Shauna did to show examples of
           noise range and measurements. Discussion continued with the possibility of additional
           parameters including measuring multiple boundaries and doing a sound study which
           may require mitigation if sound levels measure above maximum level allowed.
