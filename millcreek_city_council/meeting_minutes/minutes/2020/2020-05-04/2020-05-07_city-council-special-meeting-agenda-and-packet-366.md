Minutes of the
Millcreek City Council
May 7, 2020
4:30 p.m.
Special Meeting

The City Council of Millcreek, Utah, met in a special public meeting on May 7, 2020 at City
Hall, located at 3330 S. 1300 E., Millcreek, UT 84106. The meeting was conducted
electronically per Millcreek State of Local Emergency Directive No. 2 of 2020 and live streamed
via the City’s website with an option for online public comment.

SPECIAL MEETING - 4:30 p.m.
TIME COMMENCED: 4:35 p.m.

1. Closed Session per Utah Code Ann. §52-4-205(d) Strategy Sessions to Discuss the
Purchase, Exchange, or Lease of Real Property
Mayor Silvestrini called the meeting to order. He noted that Council Member Uipi was
participating electronically. He said the purpose of the meeting was to have a closed session
because staff had been informed that property in the City Center had been listed for sale and
the Council wanted to discuss the possible purchase of it.

Council Member Jackson moved to go into closed session per Utah Code Ann. §52-4-205(d)
strategy sessions to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property at 4:36 p.m.
Council Member Catten seconded. The Recorder called for the vote. All Council Members
voted yes. The motion passed unanimously.

Council Member Catten moved to go back into the open meeting at 5:24 p.m. Council
Member Jackson seconded. The Recorder called for the vote. All Council Members voted
yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2. Discussion and Consideration of Resolution 20-17, Authorizing the Mayor to Submit
Offers to Purchase and Negotiate the Purchase of Approximately 1.61 Acres of
Improved Real Property Located at 1350 East Miller Avenue, 0.2 Acres of Improved
Real Property Located at 1311 East Woodland Avenue, and 0.17 Acres of Improved
Real Property Located at 1337 East Woodland Avenue

Council Member Jackson moved to adopt Resolution 20-17, Authorizing the Mayor to
Submit Offers to Purchase the below listed properties. Council Member Catten seconded.
The Recorder called for the vote. All Council Members voted yes. The motion passed
unanimously.

Mayor Silvestrini said the Council had a regular meeting on Monday, May 11", and Council
Member Uipi would be participating electronically in that meeting.

ADJOURNED: Council Member Jackson moved to adjourn the meeting at 5:27 p.m.
Council Member Marchant seconded. Mayor Silvestrini called for the vote. All Council
Members voted yes. The motion passed unanimously.
