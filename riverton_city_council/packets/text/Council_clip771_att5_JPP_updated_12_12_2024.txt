                                   JOINT PROTECTION PROGRAM

In consideration of the payment of "funds owing" to the Agency, the mutual promises set forth
in the First Amended and Restated Interlocal Agreement and the provisions of the Bylaws, the
Agency provides the Members the following Joint Protection Program effective July 30, 2021.

The following terms shall have the meaning hereinafter set out regardless of whether they
are bolded, capitalized or appear inside quotation marks:

   1.       “Agency” shall mean the Utah Risk Management Agency, which was created by

                                      ARTICLE II: COVERAGES

Subject to and limited by the terms, definitions, application, conditions and exclusions of this
Program, the Agency shall pay on behalf of the Member and covered individuals (as defined
in Article V hereof) those monies that the Member or covered individual becomes legally
obligated to pay as damages by reason of liability imposed by law or assumed by contract
because of "Bodily Injury,” "Property Damage,” "Personal Injury,” "Public Officials’ Errors and
Omissions,” and "Employee Benefit Liability" as those terms are herein defined and to which
this Program applies, caused by an "occurrence." The monies due for payment may come from
Agency funds, Member payments, deductibles or from the proceeds from other insurance
products, such as, but not limited to, reinsurance or excess insurance purchased to protect
the assets of the Agency.

Section 1:     Core Protection Program

Coverages, as approved by the Agency, may be offered and required as a part of the
mandatory participation in the Agency’s Core Protection Program which consists of:

       Coverage A: Third Party Liability. Subject to the exclusions contained in this Program,
       this coverage consists of:

              1. “Bodily Injury”;

              2. “Property Damage”;

              3. “Personal Injury”;

              4. “Public Officials’ Errors and Omissions”;

              5. “Employee Benefit Liability”; and

              6. “Theft or Crime Insurance”.

       Coverage A is claims made and applies only if a claim for damages is first made in writing
       against the Member or covered individuals during the term of the Member’s coverage
       period. A claim by a person or organization seeking damages will be deemed to have
       been made when written notice of the claim is received by the Member or covered
       individual. This Program covers only those occurrences for which a claim is made
       during the effective period of this coverage and no coverage is provided for events,
       offenses, or occurrences prior to the effective date of coverage or after the
       termination date of coverage except as outlined in Article II, Section 5.

       Even though Coverage A is claims made, the Agency will respond to occurrences as
       requested by a Member. All costs associated with this response to these occurrences
       will be treated as if the claim had formally been received by the Agency.

       Coverage B: Group-Purchased General Property. Members are required to purchase
       general property coverage through the Group Property Purchase Plan established by
       the Agency.

       Coverage C: Cyber Liability. URMA will group-purchase cyber liability coverage for its
       Members. The limits, terms and conditions for this coverage are subject to approval by
       the Board.

       Cyber liability coverage will be subject to a $25,000 deductible per occurrence. URMA
       will pay the first $25,000 of each claim and Members will reimburse that amount
       through the Repayment Program. Payments made above the $25,000 will not be
       subject to the Repayment Program.

Section 2:      Optional Coverage and Programs

    1. Auto Physical Damage Package. This coverage is for physical auto damage
       (comprehensive and collision) on Member-owned or leased vehicles valued at less than
       $50,000 for which a Member has paid $270 for each covered vehicle to URMA.
       Coverage shall extend to newly acquired vehicles not previously covered provided the
       Member notifies URMA within 30 days of the acquisition. Owned vehicles may be
       added and removed from coverage, effective the date of notice to URMA.

        A one-time tow is limited to $250, only if the vehicle is disabled at a distance greater
        than 50 miles from Member’s city. No other towing or shuttle fees are covered. URMA
        will pay labor and repairs based on appraisal from an outside adjuster or collision repair
        facility. Annual labor rates will be reviewed and set by URMA based on industry rates.
        URMA will pay the amount of an outside adjuster appraisal or the collision repair
        facility’s final bill upon completion of work, to the Member or the collision repair facility,
        based on Member’s preference. URMA will cover actual replacement cost (verified by
        invoice) of damaged after-market installed equipment, depreciated by twenty percent
        (20%) per year (based on IRS schedule). Member fleet managers will handle the sale
        and salvage of totaled vehicles. Reimbursement will be made less the salvage amount
        or appraisers estimate. The coverage is limited to the cash value of the vehicle (not
        replacement value). The deductible is $5,000 per vehicle. This coverage is not subject
        to the Repayment Program contained in Article II, Section 7, nor is it required for any
        Member which chooses to be self-insured for auto physical damage.

    2. Cyber Liability. Members may purchase additional cyber liability coverage through
       URMA and its brokers.

    3. Safety Cooperation Program. URMA has established a Safety Cooperation Program for
       purposes of providing workers compensation coverage through the Workers
       Compensation Fund of Utah. The Program emphasizes risk management principles
       espoused in URMA’s liability program, and to that end provides educational and
       training opportunities as well as claims adjusting, monitoring and review. Moreton &
       Co. acts as a broker to assist in each Member’s efforts to prevent and reduce claims
       and lower experience modification factors (Emods). Members that participate in the
       Safety Cooperation Program may receive credits towards their annual inspection
       scores as set forth in the Inspection Program adopted by the Board. While
       participation in the Program is strongly encouraged, it is not mandatory. Members
       who participate in the Program shall be known as the Safety Cooperative Group.

Section 3:     Terminated Coverage

Workers’ compensation coverage was terminated effective April 1, 1995. The Agency will
continue to provide coverage for “claims made” during the period that workers’ compensation

Section 4:     Previous Coverages

Coverage previously offered by the Agency shall be handled within the terms of the Joint
Protection Program that was in effect when the claim was made.

Section 5:     Coverage Prior to and After Membership

The Agency shall also provide, at the option of and cost to the requesting Member, coverage
prior to membership and after membership.

The Agency may determine costs of such coverage from an actuarial study or other study
acceptable to the Executive Committee.

Section 6:     Deductibles

Members may choose to have an amount billed as a “deductible” as part of their risk
management strategy for liability and property damage claims. URMA will bill these
deductibles quarterly. Deductible payments will be credited toward the 5-year repayment
program for the respective claim (s).

Section 7:     Repayment Program

Members shall reimburse the Agency for all claims costs above the deductible paid on their
behalf by the Agency as follows:

    A. In addition to paying an annual contribution, the Member shall reimburse the Agency
       for one hundred percent (100%) of losses above the deductible; and

    B. This reimbursement shall be made in an amount equal to twenty percent (20%) of all
       payments above the deductible made per year during the previous five (5) years on
       behalf of the Member; and

    C. The amount of the Member’s single largest loss repayment during a given year, that
       exceeds the Member’s shared loss repayment calculation, which is twenty-five percent
       (25%) of the Member’s annual funds owed to URMA. The loss is shared at that amount
       and URMA does not require repayment above that amount; and

    D. If a Member has a shared loss and suffers another large loss, the Member may petition

Section 8:      Transfer of Risk

Members shall transfer risk contractually whenever possible.

Section 9:      Coverage Territory

This JPP applies anywhere in the world with the exception of any country or jurisdiction which
is subject to trade or other economic sanction or embargo by the United States of America, but
only if a claim is made for such in the United States of America.


                                      ARTICLE III: DEFENSE

The Agency shall defend any claim which in the opinion of the Agency is covered by this
Program. The Agency may investigate, defend, and settle or compromise any claim, and the
manner and method thereof shall be solely within the discretion of the Agency. The Agency
shall not be obligated to further defend or pay any costs of any claim after the coverage limits
of this Program have been exhausted by legal fees, expenses and costs of defense, payment
of judgments, and/or settlements.

Section 1:      Claims Settlement

Except for the situations outlined in Section 2 of this Article, the Agency shall not settle any
claims against the Member or covered individual without advising the Member or covered
individual of the proposed settlement. The Member or covered individual shall consider the
proposed settlement within fifteen (15) days of a request by the Agency; however, it is
generally expected that the Member will choose one of the following options and consider the
request as soon as possible. The Member shall choose one of the following options:

    A. The Member agrees with the proposed settlement. In this instance, the Agency shall
       attempt to settle the claim as proposed;

    B. The Member or covered individual disagrees with the proposed settlement. In this
       instance, the Member or covered individual shall have seven (7) days to appeal the
       proposed settlement in writing to the Claims Committee. If the Member or covered
       individual disagrees with the decision of the Claims Committee, the Member or covered
       individual may by formal City Council resolution and written agreement with the
12 | P a g e                           UTAH RISK MANAGEMENT AGENCY | JOINT PROTECTION RROGRAM
                                                                        Update December 12, 2024
         Agency, waive coverage for the claim in question and defend the claim at its own
         expense. The Agency shall, thereafter, be liable only for the amount for which the
         claim could have been settled, with costs of defense incurred to that date, and
         anticipated to be incurred through the conclusion of the settlement. Any subsequent
         settlement, verdict, award or judgment in excess of the amount for which the claim
         could have been settled, and all costs of defense, shall be the sole responsibility of the
         Member or the covered individual; or

    C. The Member does not approve or disapprove the proposed settlement within the
       fifteen-day (15) time-frame. In this instance, the Agency shall take whatever action it
       deems necessary to settle the claim.

If, under all three options outlined above, the Member delays the decision beyond the time
allotted to the Member and this delay costs the Agency money beyond what a quick and timely
decision would have cost, the Member shall be responsible for all of these additional costs and
shall immediately reimburse the Agency for these costs when billed by the Agency. The
Member may appeal this requirement that the Member pay the additional costs to the
Executive Committee.

Section 2:       Claims Resolution

Where it is clear that repayment of a paid loss will be capped under the Repayment Program,
the Agency will aggressively resolve claims without specific approval of the Member, even
though the Member may want the Agency to do otherwise.

Section 3:       Claims Reporting

Each Member shall report to the Agency immediately all incidents or occurrences which could
be expected to result in the Agency being required to consider a claim against a Member, its
employees, or for casualty losses to the Member's property within the scope of coverages
provided by the Agency.

Section 4:       Cooperation and Assistance

Each Member shall cooperate with and assist the Agency, the Agency's attorneys, claims and
litigation manager, insurers of the Agency, and any other agent, employee, or officer of the
Agency for occurrences which could be expected to result in the Agency being required to
consider a claim against the Member, its agents, officers, or employees, or for casualty losses
to the Member’s property within the scope of coverages provided by the Agency in all matters
relating to the Agency, and comply with all Bylaws, the terms of this Agreement, the First
Amended and Restated Interlocal Agreement and/or rules and regulations adopted by the
Board of Directors.

Section 5:     Information and Assistance

Each Member shall provide the Agency with all information or assistance necessary for the

Section 6:     Legal Representation

Each Member shall allow attorneys designated by the Agency to represent the Member in the

Outside attorney’s fees not associated with a claim are covered by the respective Member.
When there is an occurrence and the Member desires URMA’s assistance regarding outside
attorney’s, URMA may pay outside attorneys’ fees if agreed upon by the Member and the
URMA Executive Director.

Section 7:     Claims Management Services

Lowther and Associates services are covered with regard to occurrences and claims, if URMA
is notified by the Member. Notifying Lowther and Associates is considered notification to
URMA.

                                 ARTICLE IV: LIMITS OF LIABILITY

The coverage provided to Members and covered individuals under this Joint P rotection
Program is limited as follows:

    A. The existence of this Program, the contracting for benefits and the Member’s payment
       into the Agency's reserves and surpluses shall not constitute a waiver or any
       relinquishment of any rights or immunities provided by constitution, statute, or
       common law, including the immunities and limitations extended by the Governmental
       Immunity Act of Utah.

    B. This Program does not provide coverage under Article II, Section 1 (Core Protection
       Program) for "Bodily Injury" and/or “Property Damage” in excess of the damage limits
       established in the Governmental Immunity Act of Utah, except in an action wherein it
       is authoritatively determined that the immunities and/or the liability limits of the
       Governmental Immunity Act of Utah are unconstitutional, unenforceable, inapplicable
       or otherwise of no force and effect, for a reason other than the fact of the existence of
       this Program.

    C. Regardless of the number of individuals or Members covered under this Program, or

14 | P a g e                           UTAH RISK MANAGEMENT AGENCY | JOINT PROTECTION RROGRAM
                                                                        Update December 12, 2024
         the number of persons or organizations making claims or bringing suits, or the
         number of claims made or suits brought, the Agency's liability, in the event of an
         authoritative holding of the inapplicability or unenforceability of the liability limits of
         the Governmental Immunity Act of Utah, is limited to the sum of Six Million dollars
         ($6,000,000) per occurrence, except as to those persons covered under Article IV,
         paragraph L hereof. This limit is the most the Agency will pay because of any one
         "occurrence.” Costs, legal fees, and expenses attendant to the defense of the claim are
         included in determining the Agency's limit of liability.

    D. The coverage for asbestos liability is limited to an annual aggregate of One Hundred
       Thousand dollars ($100,000).

    E. The payment for Personal Injury Protection shall be limited to the statutory minimum
       required by Utah law.

    F. The payment for Uninsured Motorist Coverage and Underinsured Motorist Coverage
       Protection shall be limited to the statutory minimum. This coverage amount
       supersedes any previous endorsement, agreement or coverage document for this
       coverage.

    G. Cyber liability shall be limited to the amounts set forth in Article II, Section 1, Coverage
       C.

    H. For purposes of claims brought by victims of sexual assault, a perpetrator’s conduct
       shall constitute one single occurrence, in spite of the fact that there may be multiple
       victims (one occurrence per perpetrator). The date of occurrence is the date of the
       first assault perpetrated.

    I. Theft or Crime Insurance is covered only as required in Utah Code Section 10-3-831.

    J. sUAS (Drone) Coverage is third-party: bodily injury, personal injury, or property damage
       liability arising out of the operation, maintenance, or use of any sUAS as defined in
       Article 1, subsection 31, owned or operated by a Member. sUAS operations are covered
       only to the extent that the Member has procured the proper licensing through the FAA
       (Certificate of Authority for City or Part 107 for Pilot), and has adopted a use policy in
       accordance with federal and state law and regulations, which is specific to each
       department that has a sUAS, and the sUAS at the time of the occurrence is being used
       by a licensed operator within the policy.

    K. Small Transit Coverage includes third-party: bodily injury, or property damage liability
       arising out of the operation, maintenance, or use of any transit authority, transit
       system, or public transportation system owned or operated by the Member.

                             ARTICLE VIII: WITHDRAWING MEMBERS

Section 1:      Financial Obligations

Members who have withdrawn or are in the process of withdrawal along with former Members
who have had their membership canceled pursuant to the provisions of the First Amended and
Restated Interlocal Agreement which created the Agency shall still be responsible for paying,
repaying and/or reimbursing the Agency for all "funds owing" the Agency.

Section 2:      Repayment Options

For those Members who have given the Agency their withdrawal notice in accordance with the
requirements of the First Amended and Restated Interlocal Agreement, the following two (2)
options are available to the withdrawing Member under this Repayment Program to pay for
losses and future claims expenses the withdrawing Member owes the Agency:

    A. The withdrawing Member must give at least one-year’s notice in writing before
       withdrawing. The coverage shall terminate on June 30th after the one-year waiting
       period passes. After termination of coverage the standard Repayment Program will
       continue until all losses are repaid under the program. Any loss that has been shared
       pursuant to the Repayment Program will no longer be shared after the withdrawal, so
       that URMA does not share any loss of a withdrawn Member.

    B. The one-year waiting provision shall be waived upon the request of a Member provided
       that the Member complies with the requirements listed below. These are the
       conditions for withdrawal and the conditions of coverage for the withdrawing
       Member:

           1. The Member must give written notice of its desire to withdraw by March 31st for
23 | P a g e                            UTAH RISK MANAGEMENT AGENCY | JOINT PROTECTION RROGRAM
                                                                         Update December 12, 2024
               a June 30th termination. No withdrawal shall be allowed prior to June 30th.

           2. The withdrawing Member shall pay all amounts owed under the Repayment
              Program by July 10th immediately following withdrawal. This includes payments
              the Member would have paid in the year following the withdrawal plus all
              payments that URMA has already covered that would normally be paid over the
              next five years.

           3. For pending claims that URMA will still be defending after the withdrawal, the
              Member may reimburse URMA for monies expended after the withdrawal date
              pursuant to the normal Repayment Program; provided, however, no loss will be
              shared once the Member has withdrawn, and the Member will be required to
              repay the full loss. URMA shall not share in losses once the Member has
              withdrawn.

           4. The Member shall pay the equivalent of fifty percent (50%) of its projected
              contributions for the fiscal year that immediately follows the withdrawal as a
              penalty for early withdrawal. This payment shall also be made by July 10th. The
              payment shall include all amounts the Member would have owed as contribution,
              the Repayment Program amount, assessments, deductibles, membership fees,
              premiums, and registration fees.

           5. Coverage shall terminate on June 30th. URMA will only cover claims that have
              been filed against the Member and presented to URMA by that date.

           6. URMA may settle or otherwise dispose of pending claims without input or
              direction from the withdrawing Member.

           7. The withdrawing Member shall be responsible for all deductibles associated with
              its claims.

           8. The withdrawing Member shall sign a contract by June 30th with the following
              provisions:

                a) A statement accepting the terms for early withdrawal as stated in this
                   Section 2, paragraph B, and

                b) A covenant not to sue and full release of URMA for any liability arising out
                   of the Member’s membership in URMA.
