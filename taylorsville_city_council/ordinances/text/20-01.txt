              SUMMARY:     Consideration of Ordinance 20- 01 to amend the 2006 General Plan from
                           Community Commercialto Low-Density Residential for the properties generally
                           located 6581 South Redwood Road and 6621 South Redwood Road to facilitate the
                           development of a nine- lot subdivision.

           BACKGROUND:     Lynn Rindlisbacher on behalf of Dave Wilson is requesting a General Plan
                           amendment and subsequent ZoningMap amendment to facilitate the development of
                           a nine-lot subdivision for the parcels generally located at 6581 South Redwood Road
                           Parcel ID 21224010140000), 6621 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID
                           21224010150000), 6581 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010080000), 6573
                           South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21222570160000), 6579 South Redwood Road
                           Parcel ID 21222570120000), 6579 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID
                           21222570100000), 6579 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010060000), 6577
                           South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010070000), and 6577 South Redwood Road
                           Parcel ID 21222570180000). The General Plan amendmentwill be requested in two
                           different ordinances( 20- 01 and 20- 02), as the parcels located within the subdivision
                           have different General Plan designations( see Exhibit A and B).


                           The property is owned by the Wilson family and they were granted Preliminary Plat
                           approval fora nine- lot subdivision by the Planning Commission on 12/ 10/ 19,
                           contingent upon the General Plan and Zoning Map amendments being approved by
                           the City Council. The Wilson families will remain in Lots 4, 6, and 8 of the
                           proposed subdivision, which is why they are requesting a General Plan amendment
                           that is consistent with a single- family home development ratherthan a commercial
                           or multi-family development. The proposed subdivision will have lots varying in
                           size from 11, 754 square feet to 29, 358 square feet, which is congruent with the Low-
                           Density Residential General Plan designation.

                           The proposed subdivision has nine parcels, with five different addresses.
                           Additionally, the parcels have two different General Plan and Zoning Map
                           designations. The two parcels that serve as the access to the property and are
                           adjacent to Redwood Road are designated as Community Commercial( Ordinance
                           20- 01). The internal seven lots are designated as Medium- Density Residential
                            Ordinance 20- 02). The requested General Plan amendment will provide continuity
                           for all the parcels within the subdivision by amendingthe designation for all nine
                           parcels to   Low-Density Residential.
                               In addition to Ordinance 20- 01, the applicant has requested a General Plan
                               amendment for seven parcels from Medium-Density Residential to Low-Density
                               Residential( Ordinance 20- 02) a Zoning Map amendment from Limited Commercial
                               to R- 1- 10( Ordinance 20- 03), and R- 1- 15 to R- 1- 10( Ordinance 20- 04).


                               The parcels and addresses that will be amended by Ordinance 20- 01 are outlined
                               below:

                                        6581 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010140000)

                                        6621 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010150000)


                               The requested amendment is consistent with the provisions outlined in Section 1. 4
                                Amending the Plan" page 1- 8 of the 2006 General Plan, and aligns with Goals 7. 1,
                               7.2, and 7.3 of the General Plan( GP page 7- 3).


                               The Planning Commission has sent a positive recommendation to the City Council
                               to amend the aforementioned parcels from Community Commercial to Low-Density
                               Residential via a vote of 7- 0 on 12/ 10/ 19.

     AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A GENERAL PLAN
     AMENDMENT FROM COMMUNITY COMMERCIAL TO LOW-DENSITY RESIDENTIAL


        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on January 8,
2020, to consider, among other things, approving an amendment to THE TAYLORSVILLE 2006 GENERAL
TO AMEND THE PROPERTIES OUTLINED IN EXHIBIT A FROM COMMUNITY COMMERCIAL TO LOW- DENSITY
RESIDENTIAL; and


        WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 05. 030( A)(2), notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published in the Deseret News and the Salt
Lake Tribune on November 30, 2019; and


        WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was also
published on the State Public Notice Website on November 26, 2019; and


        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on the
proposed amendment on December 10, 2019, and the Planning Commission voted 7- 0 to recommend that
the City Council approve the proposed amendment; and

      WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy public
comment on the proposed amendment during its regularly scheduled meeting on January 8, 2020, and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best interest
of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed amendment.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the proposed
amendment to TAYLORSVILLE 2006 GENERAL PLAN is hereby approved and is designated in Exhibit
A.
        THIS ORDINANCE, assigned Ordinance No. 20- 01, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the city recorder, and
accepted as required herein.
