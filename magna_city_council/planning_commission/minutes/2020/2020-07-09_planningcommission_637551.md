31183- Cris Kohles is requesting Conditional Use approval to Manufacture Landscaping Materials.
Acreage: Approximately 4.85 acres. Location: 2360 South 7200 West. Zone: M-2 (Manufacturing).
Planner: Diana Martinez. (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on this application prior to acting on
the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioner Richards asked if they have an office, do not they need restrooms. Ms. Martinez said they
should have to because people will be in that office on a daily basis and will have potable water on site and
discuss port-a-pottys.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding the type of processing.

five to six average, less most times an hour during peak season, average full year would be less.
Commissioner Collard asked hours of operation. Mr. Kohles said 7am to 5pm, some Saturdays 8am to
noon. Commissioner Cripps asked about security. Mr. Kohles said has cameras all over and reason for

(Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on this application prior to acting on
the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioner Richards asked if it will be dug out or on grade. Ms. Martinez said on grade. Commissioner
Richards asked if there is a height requirement. Ms. Martinez said would need to go through grading.
Commissioner Weight said has water on site. Ms. Martinez said would go through grading supervisor and
may have contacted Army Corp, grading and urban hydrologists would need to work on that but did not
note anything. Ms. Martinez said construction land site, demolished materials. Fire has reviewed and has

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Rob Reynolds
Address: 6891 South 700 West, Midvale
Comments: Mr. Reynolds said wetlands, Terracon employed to do analysis, kersey creek is to the west.
Health and state would mandate they take care of wetlands. Sewer and water are on adjoining site with
restrooms and water. Drew a well for dust suppression or fire with dirt and water on site. Health department
has been contacted and will have an update after this meeting. State and Kennecott are supportive. Dominion
is supportive for their barrier. Landfill has a mandated height of 250 feet, piping and drainage taken care
of. No additional traffic will go down California to the main landfill. Landfill now is fenced around, grading,
and daily cover. Materials are construction and demolition debris, and recycling, crush concrete, no
structure. Airport is reserved for future landfill and would enhance if there was an earthquake.

Commissioner Richards asked height of the current facility. Mr. Reynolds said only in phase two but goes
up to six, current elevation is 125 feet. Mr. Reynolds said water truck, trash compactor and dozer are
equipment utilized and emergency truck. Commissioner Richards asked if it needs to be lined underneath.
Mr. Reynolds said does not need to be lined, clay ground is impervious to water seeping down, cannot fill
until five feet above the high-water line.
