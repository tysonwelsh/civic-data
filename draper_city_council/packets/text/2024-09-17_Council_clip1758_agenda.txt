7:00 PM BUSINESS SESSION

1.     Call to Order

2.     Pledge of Allegiance

3.     Oath of Office: Fire Department

4.     Public Comments
       To be considerate of everyone attending the meeting, public comments will be
       restricted to items that are not listed on this or a future agenda and limited to three
       minutes per person. Comments which cannot be made within these limits should be
       submitted in writing to the City Recorder prior to noon the day before the meeting.
       Comments pertaining to an item on the agenda should not be given at this time but
       should be held until that item is called.

5.     Consent Items

       5.a Approval of Resolution #24-47
           A Resolution appointing a member to the Draper City Building Board of
           Appeals.

6.     Items for Council Consideration
     6.a Public Hearing: Ordinances #1621 and #1622
         An Ordinance amending the official Land Use Map of Draper City for
         approximately 0.75 acres of property from Residential Low/Medium Density
         to Residential Medium Density and an Ordinance amending the official
         Zoning Map of Draper City for approximately 0.75 acres from RA2
         (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot lot minimum) to R3 (Single
         Family Residential, 13,000 square foot lot minimum), for the property
         located at approximately 1201 E. 13200 South, otherwise known at the
         Norman Land Use Map and Zoning Map Amendments. Staff report by
         Maryann Pickering.

     6.b Public Hearing: Ordinance #1623
         An Ordinance of Draper City amending the text of Chapter 4 of the Draper
         City General Plan to replace a Moderate Income Housing Plan strategy. Staff
         report by Todd Taylor.

     6.c Public Hearing: Ordinance #1624
         An Ordinance of Draper City amending the text of the Land Use and
         Development Code (Title 9) of the Draper City Municipal Code relating to
         development agreements and residential rear yard setbacks. Staff report by
         Todd Taylor.

     6.d Action Item: Resolution #24-25
         A Resolution of the Draper City Council approving the trail and open space
         projects for 2024-2025. Staff report by Rhett Odgen.
