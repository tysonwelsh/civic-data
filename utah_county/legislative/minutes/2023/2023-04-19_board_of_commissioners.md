                                    PUBLIC HEARING
1. PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER A PETITION TO VACATE A PORTION OF THE PLAT
OF ELBERTA TOWNSITE, SPECIFICALLY VACATING THE PORTION OF THE RIGHTS-OF-
WAY IDENTIFIED ASALL OF FIRST SOUTH STREET FROM LAY AVENUE TO MAIN
STREET (HIGHWAY 68). ALL OF “WHITNEY AVENUE” AND “WILSON AVENUE” GOING
NORTH FROM SECOND SOUTH TO THE RAILROAD RIGHT OF WAY. ALL OF “DURKEE
AVENUE”, “BARD AVENUE”, “BAISCH AVENUE” AND “BOLLER AVENUES” GOING
NORTH FROM FIRST SOUTH STREET TO THE RAILROAD RIGHT OF WAY. ALL OF FIRST,
SECOND, AND THIRD NORTH STREETS WEST OF MAIN STREET (HIGHWAY 68). ALL OF
“VARNER AVENUE”, “LAY AVENUE”, “WHITNEY AVENUE” AND “WILSON AVENUE”
NORTH OF THE RAILROAD RIGHT OF WAY. ALL THE ALLEYS IN BLOCKS 5, 6, 7, 10, 11,
12, 26, 27, 30, 31, 37, 38 AND 39, PURSUANT TO UTAH CODE SECTIONS 17-27A-208
AND 72-3-108. (PUBLIC HEARING SET MARCH 1, 2023).
       -Robert J. Moore, Attorney

(Timestamp: 2:45)
BEN STANLEY, DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY – STATED THAT UTAH COUNTY HAS
RECEIVED A REQUEST TO VACATE CERTAIN ROADS THAT WERE NEVER BUILT IN
THE HISTORIC 1908 ALBERTA TOWN SIDE PLATTE. THE REQUEST HAS COME
FROM THE CHURCH OF JESUS CHRIST OF LATTER-DAY SAINTS. SOME COUNTY
DEPARTMENTS HAVE RESERVATIONS ABOUT DOING THIS AND THERE ARE SOME
OPEN QUESTIONS THAT NEED TO BE ADDRESSED. THE GOAL OF TODAY’S PUBLIC
HEARING IS TO LISTEN TO WHAT THE PUBLIC HAS TO SAY ABOUT THE PETITION.

WADE EVA, RESIDENT OF ELBERTA – ASKED WHY THEY ARE ONLY DOING THIS ON
CHURCH PROPERTY AND NOT THROUGHOUT THE RESIDENTIAL AREA. HE IS
CONCERNED THERE WILL BE DEAD-END ROADS IF THIS REQUEST IS GRANTED.
KENT JORGENSON, FARMLAND RESERVE – STATED THAT FARMLAND RESERVE
WISHES TO CLEAN UP THE AREA.
JOSEPH ROST, FARMLAND RESERVE – STATED THAT A HEARING WAS HELD A YEAR
AGO ABOUT THE REQUEST, AND IT WAS SUGGESTED BY ONE OF THE
COMMISSIONERS THAT FARMLAND RESERVE CHECK WITH OTHER RESIDENTS IN
THE AREA TO SEE IF THEY WOULD LIKE TO BE INCLUDED. FARMLAND RESERVE
REACHED OUT TO THE LOCAL PEOPLE, BUT NO ONE WISHED TO JOIN.
