                              KEARNS CITY COUNCIL

          RESOLUTTON #B2q26-10                         DATE: March 9.2026

  A RESOLUTION APPOINTING LYNDSAY LONGTIN AS THE ALTERNATE
      REPRESENTATIVE APPOINTEE OF KEARNS TO THE BOARD OF
  DIRECTORS OF THE UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY AND THE UNIFIED FIRE
                          SERVICE AREA

       WHEREAS, the City of Kearns is a member entity of the Unified Fire Authority (the
"IJFA"), an interlocal entity, and the Unified Fire Service Area ("UFSA"), u special district;
and

        WHEREAS, the Kearns City Council (the "Council") may appoint a Councilmember
plus an alternate representative to UFA's Board of Directors pursuant to UFA's bylaws and to
UFSA's Board of Trustees pursuant to Title l78, Chapter 3, Part 3 of the Utah Code; and

         WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint Lyndsay Longtin as Kearns' alternate
 representative to the UFA Board and the UFSA Board, should Council Member Chrystal
 Butterfield be unable to attend any board meetings in the future.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS CITY COTJNCIL
EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

      1. The Council appoints Lyndsay Longtin as Kearns'alternate representative on the
UFA Board and the UFSA Board.

         2. The Kearns City Recorder is authorized to provide UFA and UFSA with this
resolution along with a copy of a letter announcing Lyndsay Longtin's appointment as the
alternate representative and notifying UFA and UFSA of their contact information.
