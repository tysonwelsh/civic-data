50            Ordinance No. 21-13* - Amending and Updating Riverton City's Ordinance
51            Regulating Development on Private Lanes, Proposed by Riverton City
     Page | 6 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     June 1, 2021



1    Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, said that this public hearing was for a text
2    amendment which was initiated by Riverton City. He said that the amended ordinance
3    addresses several key issues relating to development on private lanes. He said it clarifies that
4    all required setbacks shall apply, and shall be measured from the edge of the private lane,
5    rather than property lines. He said it also stipulates that the private lane area will not be
6    counted towards determining the total buildable area for the main dwelling and for accessory
7    structures. He said “flag” lots, or lots where a portion of the lot area is made up solely by the
8    private lane, may not count the narrow portion of the lot occupied only by the private lane
9    towards the minimum lot area.

10   Mr. Lethbridge said the amendment allows the maximum number of lots utilizing a private lane
11   to be determined during Conditional Use permit approval. He said the minimum width
12   standards are set, and the City has the discretion during approval to require additional width and
13   infrastructure, including curb, gutter, and sidewalk, based on the number of lots and the
14   circumstances of development. He said the ordinance also clarifies that required infrastructure
15   must be installed prior to issuance of a building permit.

16   Councilmember Stewart said that there may be easements that are undevelopable and he
17   doesn’t want to restrict areas that are not able to be developed. Mr. Lethbridge said that 1,500
18   feet is very lengthy and this would fall well under this 1500 feet. Mr. Lethbridge said he thought
19   the City would be protected even if the 1,500 feet language was removed.
20
21   Mayor Staggs called for public comments; there being none, he closed the Public Comment
22   period.
23
24   Councilmember Stewart MOVED that the City Council approve Ordinance No. 21-13* -
25   Amending Sections 18.(20,25,30,35,&40).070, Lots Located on Private Lanes, as
26   described in Exhibit “A” and remove the 1,500 feet maximum. Councilmember McDougal
27   SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the motion; there being none,
28   he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as follows: Councilmembers Buroker-Yes, McCay-
29   Yes, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
