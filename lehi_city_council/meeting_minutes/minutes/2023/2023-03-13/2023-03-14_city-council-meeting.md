6. Consideration of Preliminary subdivision approval for the Fox Canyon Plats A1 and I1
   including 41 single family lots located at approximately 5400 North Fox Canyon Road
   with the Flex Land Use Designations in the Traverse Mountain Planned Community.
   Items 6 and 7 were heard simultaneously. Mayor Johnson asked for a motion to untable items
   6 and 7.

    Motion:     Councilor Koivisto moved to untable items 6 and 7. Councilor Albrecht seconded
                the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


    Merlin Huff was representing Perry Homes. He noted the changes that have been made since
    the last meeting to address concerns. He stated that the density was decreased, and the road
    alignments were adjusted to improve safety at the intersections.

    Lorin Powell stated that the City’s Traffic Engineer reviewed the updated traffic study and
    agreed with it’s findings. There was discussion about the intersection that will require a
    signal to bring it up to the city’s standards and when that signal would be installed.

    There was discussion about the block lengths and connectivity standards and if any
    exceptions were made. Mr. Struthers stated that exceptions are allowed, but they could look
    specifically at those.

    Mayor Johnson invited the public to make comments.

    Rob Ludlow stated that there’s a high level of standards in Traverse Mountain and those
    standards should be met without exceptions. He appreciates the modifications, but it’s not
    enough.

    Josh Cornia also expressed concerns with the development.

    Shanny Capp was concerned with the added traffic and safety.

    Rich Kindred was concerned with additional traffic and asked that the density be moved to a
    different location.

    Lee Denning had concerns with emergency response because of the location and how it’s
    dangerous to have so many people at that high elevation with snow storms and wildland fires.

    There were concerns with the lack of a presentation being made by the applicant and that it’s
    difficult for the Council to have all of their questions answered.


    Motion:     Councilor Koivisto moved to table item 6 and 7.
Lehi City Council Meeting                       3                                   March 14, 2023
    There was discussion about what direction the Council should give the applicant. Councilor
    Koivisto stated that she would have liked to see more of a presentation to address the previous
    concerns including answers to resolve the traffic.

    The Motion failed due to a lack of a second.

    Councilor Albrecht was concerned with the timing of when the traffic signal would be
    installed. She would prefer that it be installed before the intersection is at a failed level. She
    stated that she would be uncomfortable with the density of item #7 without a traffic signal
    being installed first. The Mayor suggested that as part of the motion, the Council could direct
    staff to install the traffic signal at a specific time.

    Mr. Powell stated that they would need to confirm the block lengths, but it appears they are
    meeting the connectivity requirements.

    Mr. Huff stated that he felt that all the concerns were addressed.


    Motion:     Councilor Koivisto moved to table items 6 and 7. Councilor Albrecht seconded the
                motion.

    Councilor Hancock stated that they should provide some direction to the applicant as to what
    the Council expects. It sounds like there’s some miscommunication among Perry Development
    employees.

    Councilor Koivisto stated that she would like to see more clarification from the traffic study.
    Councilor Hancock was concerned with the density at this location if there were to be wildfires
    or large snowstorms. He said that the added density could compound the issues that are already
    there. He suggested that the applicant look at changing the townhomes to single family homes
    and move that density to the Perry Planning District.

    Mayor Johnson stated that a density transfer may result in more apartments and that may not
    be the right direction for the City. He stated that townhomes allow younger people to build
    equity.

    Amended Motion: Councilor Koivisto amended her motion to include Councilor Hancock’s
    and Mayor Johnson’s comments.

    Councilor Albrecht stated that she would like to have a plan for the traffic signal moving
    forward and also noted that the applicant may entertain some changes if it were to come back.

    2nd Amendment to the Motion: Councilor Koivisto amended her motion to include Councilor
    Albrecht’s comments and remove Mayor Johnson’s comment from the pervious amended
    motion, and note that she would like to see the density moved.

    Mr. Wood stated that this is an administrative preliminary subdivision approval, so they can
    ask the applicant to move the density, but if they deny it for the density, then they need a reason
    why it doesn’t meet code.

    the Transverse Mountain Area Plan, which is the governing document, it appears they are
