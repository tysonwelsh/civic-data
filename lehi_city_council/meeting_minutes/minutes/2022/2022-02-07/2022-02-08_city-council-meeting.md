27   2. Presentations and Report
28   2.1 Discussion of Revisions to the Proposed Thanksgiving Station Concept Plan
29   Jonathan Bankhead stated that he would like to address their revised concept plan and ensure they
30   are moving in a comfortable direction as they are working through resubmitting the plan. He noted
31   that it was made clear at the previous meeting that 5200 units was too much and that significant
32   improvements would need to be made in order to justify anything above 2000 units.
33
34   Mr. Bankhead stated that they are working more closely with staff as they address some of the
35   concerns from the previous meeting which included infrastructure plans, transportation, cost, and
36   triggers. The traffic needs to improve, but the cost should not come from the City.
37
38   Right now, their plan is to include 2000 units with the bulk of it being within a 1/3 mile radius of
39   the FrontRunner Station. Electric Park and Farm Country will remain in the newly proposed plan.
40   He stated that the Clubhouse Drive extension is a much needed component regardless of this
41   development. There is proposed legislation that would transfer the ownership of Clubhouse Drive
42   to the State, making it part of SR92.
43
44   Mr. Bankhead stated that they would like to submit another concept plan by the end of March, so
45   they want to make sure that the Council is comfortable with their general direction before they
46   design something.

51   2.2 Cold Springs Infrastructure Reimbursement Discussion
52   Den Lundell, Finance Director, briefed the Council on an upcoming budget amendment. He said
53   that they need to reimburse about 4 million in infrastructure improvements for the Cold Springs
54   Development. He stated that the projects were completed faster then expected, so they will need
55   to do an amendment to the budget.
56
57   2.3 Discussion of Telecommunications Franchise Agreement with Wasatch Broadband.
58   Spencer Wangsgard, representing Wasatch Broadband, informed the Council that he was
59   approached by a developer in Holbrook Farms to address the lack of internet options. He said
60   that they plan to minimize the scope of their project in the area, but can hopefully provide more
61   options to residents. Mayor Johnson suggested that they work with Strata and coordinate fiber
62   installation if possible.
63
64   3. Agenda Questions
65   None.

67   4. Administrative Report
68   David Kitchen, HR Director, informed the Council that they are moving to a digital timecard
69   process, but they are having some issues because the Council and Commission are paid on a
70   different schedule. They are paid monthly instead of bi-weekly like all other employees, so he
71   suggested that the Council move to the bi-weekly system which would be 26 pay periods per
72   year. The Council agreed.
73
74   5. Mayor and Council Reports
75   Mayor Johnson stated that they receive a donation from Patterson Homes every year that
76   historically they have used for Library purchases, but it could be used for other things. He would
77   like to see this year’s donation be used toward the construction of a dog park for the raw
78   materials, then volunteers can help with the construction. The Council reached consensus that the
79   donation this year should be used toward the construction of a dog park.
80
81   Mayor Johnson suggested implementing some procedures that would assist with efficiency
82   during a Council meeting. He recommended that each Councilmember state their opinion and
83   give their analysis once per application. It’s appropriate to ask questions, but he would like to
84   avoid a debate. The Council agreed that this procedure would be beneficial.

108   Eric Sabin expressed concerns with the Holbrook Farms Park concept plan that was recently
109   reviewed by the Planning Commission. He asked that the design be altered to adjust the grass
110   and natural grass areas. He also stated that it does not meet the code requirements for the number

114   because it looks like weeds.

117   correctly, and that public input is considered.
118

120   felt that it could be designed in a more efficient way and was concerned that public comment
121   was not allowed at the Planning Commission meeting.
