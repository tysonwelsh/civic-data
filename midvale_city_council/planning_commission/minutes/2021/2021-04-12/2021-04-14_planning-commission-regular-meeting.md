Mr. Jason Binks stated that the applicant, Tyson Williamson representing the Will Group, requests
arezone of 4 adjacent parcels at 24, 30 and 52 W Center St and 7710 State St from Transit-Oriented
Development (TOD) to Transit-Oriented Development Overlay (TODO). The proposed rezoned
parcels total 3.39 acres. The applicant also proposes an approximately 248-unit, vertical mixed use
development that is yet unnamed. A mixed-use development is a conditionally permitted use in |
the proposed TODO zone using Chapter 17-7-17 standards.
The proposed rezone is consistent with the 2016 General Plan and meets requirements of Midvale
Code Chapter 17-3-1. Therefore, Staff recommends the Planning Commission recommend that
City Council approve the proposed rezone at 24-52 W Center St and 7710 S State St to Transit-
Oriented Development Overlay (TODO).
With the conditions of approval listed in the staff report, the application complies with Midvale
City Municipal Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the conditional use
permit and preliminary site plan for the high-density State and Center Housing development, with
the conditions of approval listed in the staff report.

3. REZ — 21-25-226-019; REZONE FROM REGIONAL COMMERICAL (RC) TO
CLEAN INDUSTRIAL (CD); 7037 S. HIGH TEXH DR.; KEVIN ROGERS/GRIP6
LLC (APPLICANT & PROPERTY OWNER): JANA WARD, PLANNER 1
(STAFF).

Ms. Ward stated that B.J. Minson and Kevin Rogers, representing Grip6 LLC, are requesting to

rezone 2.5 acres located at 7037 S High Tech Dr. from Regional Commercial (RC) to Clean

Industrial (CI).

Staff concluded that the rezone request does not meet any of the required criteria for a rezone.

| | The proposal is not necessary to comply with the general plan nor does it reflect an unanticipated

| community need; the City already has areas where manufacturing uses are permitted. The

: property has been commercially zoned since at least 1983 and was not the result of a clerical
error or mistake and the surrounding environs have changed very little in that time. The
surrounding properties have been consistently occupied in accordance with RC zone
requirements. Because Staff believes the request is not supported by the 2016 Midvale City

General Plan or the requirements of Chapter 17-3-1 of the Midvale City Municipal Code,

Planning Staff recommends forwarding a recommendation to the Midvale City Council to deny

the request to rezone the property located at 7037 S High Tech Dr. to the Clean Industrial (CI).

Ms. Ward concluded by stating the City staff really wants to work with Grip6 and are looking for

alternatives, like a text amendment, to address their needs.
