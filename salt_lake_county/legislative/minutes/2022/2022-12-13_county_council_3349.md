A JOINT COMMEMORATIVE RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE 
COUNTY COUNCIL AND THE SALT LAKE COUNTY MAYOR 
COMMENDING THE CAREER AND SERVICE OF CLERK SHERRIE 
SWENSEN
WHEREAS, Sherrie Swensen, along with her three siblings, was raised by 
her parents, Sterling and Ethel, in Murray, Utah.  Sherrie graduated from 
Murray high school in 1966, and directly entered the workforce, beginning a 
succession of office and real estate jobs; and
WHEREAS, Sherrie began her career in public service as an administrative 
assistant with the Community Action Program, where she worked with the 
State of Utah on legislative matters.  Sherrie eventually took a position with 
the Utah Democratic Party where she continued to serve the public - all while 
raising her two boys, Shane and Lane; and
WHEREAS, party officials took notice of Sherrie ’s efficiency and energy, 
and urged her to run for public office - which she initially demurred; and
WHEREAS, after studying how difficult it was for residents to register to 
vote, Sherrie thought that “a democracy meant that more people should be 
representative of the masses of the population” and decided that it was 
important to do something to improve voting access; and 
WHEREAS, Sherrie agreed to run for office and was elected Salt Lake 
County Clerk in 1990, and was subsequently re -elected in 1994, 1998, 2006, 
2010, 2014, and 2018, and has diligently served the residents of Salt Lake 
County as its elected County Clerk for a total of 32 years; and
WHEREAS, when Sherrie began her first term, she noticed how much voter 
outreach was needed.  Sherrie spent many of her Friday nights and Saturday 
mornings at community events like boat shows, autoramas, and other 
Page 2 of 27
County Council Meeting Minutes December 13, 2022
community gatherings, letting eligible Salt Lake County residents know how 
they could become registered to vote; and
WHEREAS, in addition to outreach, Sherrie also made registration as 
accessible to as many voters as possible, placing boxes of registration forms 
in grocery stores, apartment buildings, all post offices, libraries, college 
campuses, and everywhere else she could think of;  Sherrie went to high 
schools and registered 18 year olds, and to senior citizen centers and 
registered 81 year olds; and
WHEREAS, in the 1992 election, the first major election of Sherrie ’s 
clerkship, the number of registered voters increased dramatically - a trend 
that has continued throughout her tenure; and
WHEREAS, to help ensure continuity in elections, Sherrie began to offer 
contracts to the municipalities in 1995 to provide election services to them .  
Sherrie was the first Clerk in Utah to offer this service, believing that Clerks 
should serve their constituents whenever and wherever possible.  Salt Lake 
County initially conducted municipal elections for South Jordan, Sandy, and 
Salt Lake City in 1995.  By 2013, all of the county ’s municipalities were on 
board - the last being Alta, as it only had one precinct; and
WHEREAS, Sherrie has continue to be a champion of voting accessibility, 
promoting vote by mail opportunities and legislation, and was a pioneer in 
offering no -excuse vote by mail (VBM) options to Salt Lake County 
residents and municipalities.  In 2013, Sherrie was able to offer municipal 
elections utilizing VBM, and the first such elections were held in West Jordan 
and Cottonwood Heights.  Sherrie first provided VBM in the 2014 midterm 
election and by 2017, all municipalities were VBM.  Voter participation 
skyrocketed under Sherrie ’s tenure, which she credits in large part to the ease 
and accessibility of VBM; and
WHEREAS, Sherrie has faithfully administered 70 elections to date, 
including the 2022 midterm General Election, and has seen to it that every 
vote has been counted fairly and accurately; and 
WHEREAS, Sherrie has excelled in discharging her other statutory functions, 
which include issuing marriage licenses, processing passport applications, 
and keeping minutes and records of the County Council and other county 
boards and meetings.  During her tenure, Sherrie ’s office has prepared over 
Page 3 of 27

5,000 Commission/Council meeting minutes, and issued over 278,479 
marriage licenses; Sherrie has personally officiated over 1,000 marriages and 
has administered oaths to scores of elected officials and countless numbers 
of their deputies; and
WHEREAS, Sherrie ’s biggest loves of her life are her family including her 
two sons, wonderful daughters ’-in-law Katherine and Kelly, and eight 
incredible grandchildren.  Upon retirement Sherrie plans to stay busy, travel, 
spend time with her family, and focus on things that she is passionate about; 
and
WHEREAS, Sherrie also has tremendous affection for the residents of Salt 
Lake County, and her proudest legacy has been ensuring that they have the 
opportunity to marry who they love and have their voices heard in in our 
community through elections; and
WHEREAS, Sherrie ’s career in public service reminds us all of the singular 
power and value of the right to vote in our constitutional republic - as 
President Lyndon Johnson noted when signing the Voting Rights Act, “the 
vote is the most powerful instrument ever devised for breaking down 
injustice and destroying the terrible walls which imprison [people] because 
they are different from other [people]”;
NOW, THEREFORE, the undersigned do hereby resolve that the records of 
the County Council show that the County Mayor, County Council, and their 
respective staff send their best wishes and regards to Clerk Sherrie Swensen 
as she pursues her future endeavors.  The Council and Mayor further 
resolves in thanking Clerk Swensen for her service to the people of Salt Lake 
County.

RESOLUTION NO. 6042
RESOLUTION APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION 
AGREEMENT BETWEEN SALT LAKE COUNTY AND THE 
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF SALT LAKE CITY SETTING 
FORTH THE COUNTY ’S PARTICIPATION IN THE STATE STREET 
COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT PROJECT AREA
RECITALS
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County and the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake 
City (the “Agency”) are “public agencies” as defined by the Utah Interlocal 
Cooperation Act, Utah Code §§ 11-13-101 to -608, and are therefore 
authorized to enter into an Agreement to act jointly and cooperatively in a 
manner that will enable them to make the most efficient use of their resources 
and powers; and
WHEREAS,  the Agency is a community reinvestment agency created and 
existing under the Limited Purpose Local Government Entities - Community 
Reinvestment Agency Act, Utah Code §§ 17C-1-101 to -5-406 (the “CRA 
Act”); and
WHEREAS, the County and the Agency are authorized under the Interlocal 
Act and the CRA Act to enter into this Agreement to act jointly and 
cooperatively in a manner that will enable them to make the most efficient use 
of their resources and powers and implement a community reinvestment 
project area plan; and 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. R-25-2018 adopted by the Agency 
Page 16 of 27
County Council Meeting Minutes December 13, 2022
on August 21, 2018 and Ordinance No. 51-2018 adopted by the Salt Lake 
City Council on October 2, 2018, the State Street Community Reinvestment 
Area Plan (the “Project Area Plan”) has been approved; and
WHEREAS, under the Project Area Plan, the Agency desires to support 
commercial corridors and employment hubs to support living -wage jobs; 
attract and expand neighborhood retail and services; provide an array of 
housing choices to meet the needs of current residents while attracting new 
residents including new families; and increase neighborhood livability and 
safety; and
WHEREAS, Section 17C-4-201 of the CRA Act authorizes the County, as a 
taxing entity, to consent to the payment to the Agency of its share of tax 
increment (as defined in Section 17C-1-102(61) of the Act) generated from 
the Project Area for the purposes set forth in the Project Area Plan; and
WHEREAS, the County and the Agency wish to enter into an Interlocal 
Cooperation Agreement to set forth the County ’s contribution of tax 
increment; and
WHEREAS, it has been determined that the best interests of the County and 
the general public will be served by execution of the attached Interlocal 
Cooperation Agreement; and 
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the attached Interlocal Cooperation Agreement is hereby 
approved; and the Mayor is hereby authorized to execute said agreement, a 
copy of which is attached as Attachment “A” and by this reference made a 
part of this Resolution.
