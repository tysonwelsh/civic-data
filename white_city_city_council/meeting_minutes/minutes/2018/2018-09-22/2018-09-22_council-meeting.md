                                            Field Trip

                Council Members met the Planning Commission and Salt Lake County Planning
and Development Services staff at the White City Water District Offices to tour White City. The
tour travelled to various locations within White City to educate the Council, Planning Commission,
and planning staff to the existing development conditions in White City to prepare for future
general plan discussions.

Introduction of Lupita McClenning

               Lupita McClenning introduced herself and stated she has been a Planner and
Director for coastal areas for over 20 years.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Overview of the 60 & 90-day plan for Salt Lake County Planning & Development Services Division

                 Lupita McClenning stated she has done a work force analysis to determine what
skills the Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services Division has. There will need to
be discussions on how to maintain and retain good employees.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Training on the Roles & Responsibilities of the Planning Commission and Council in Regard to
Land Use & the Creation & Importance of the General Plan

                Lupita McClenning stated all land use amendments and recommendations come
from the Planning Commission. All elected and appointed officials should go through a yearly
training to stay up to date on current laws and how it will affect the community. When creating a
new general plan, she recommends including the Planning Commission, one Council Member,
and a leader in the community.

               Paul Ashton stated the Planning Commission is the recommending body. It
makes recommendations and decisions for policies already in place. The Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) is hiring a land use attorney. He recommends looking at
the old general plan.

              Alison Weyher stated she looked at the general plan from 1994 and a community
plan from 2004. She added a transportation and moderate-income housing sections to the general
plan.

              Mr. Ashton stated if the most current general plan can be adopted by the end of
October then the Council can apply for grants. He also recommends having someone from the
White City Water District attend general plan meetings.

               Mayor Flint stated water is very important for White City and that resource needs
to be protected.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Discussion & Staff Direction on the Removal of the Moratorium & Creation Process of the General
Plan

                Mayor Flint stated the moratorium that was placed on the general plan will be
lifted at the Councils October meeting. She would like staff to work with the Planning Commission
to determine the dates to hold public hearings and meetings, so the general plan can be adopted
by the end of October.
