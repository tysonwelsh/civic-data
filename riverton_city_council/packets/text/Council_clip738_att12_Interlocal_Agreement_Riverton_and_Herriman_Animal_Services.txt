                          ANIMAL SHELTER AGREEMENT

      This Animal Shelter Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Herriman City, a
Utah municipal corporation (“Herriman”), and Riverton City, a Utah municipal
corporation (“Riverton”). This Agreement is effective on the date that the last party
executes this Agreement as indicated by the date stated under that party’s signature
line.

       1.    Purpose. The purpose of this Agreement is to outline the terms and
conditions under which Herriman will provide shelter services for animals brought to
Herriman’s animal shelter by Riverton animal services.

      2.     Scope of Services.

           a.     Riverton animal services shall contact Herriman animal services
      when Riverton needs to house an animal found within Riverton’s city limits.

             b.      Herriman agrees to accept only dogs and cats from Riverton animal
      services.

             c.    The parties shall coordinate a time for the animal to be dropped off
      at Herriman’s animal shelter located at [Address]. Unless an emergency exists,
      drop-off hours shall be during normal business hours.

             d.      For good cause, Herriman City may refuse any animals brought to
      the facility by Riverton. Good cause may include insufficient space, multiple
      quarantined animals, sick or injured animals, unavailable personnel, etc.

             e.     Herriman will provide appropriate shelter and care for these
      animals while in Herriman’s custody, unless the animals are injured and/or sick
      before entering Herriman’s animal shelter, at for which Riverton will be
      responsible for providing medical attention to the animals.

             f.     Herriman shall not provide any additional animal services to
      Riverton, including picking up the animals in Riverton, locating animal owners,
      or arranging adoptions.

      3.     Fees.
      a.      Riverton agrees to pay Herriman the following fees for housing the
animals:
              i.     $50 per full day of shelter per animal (over 8 hours).

              ii.    $25 per partial day of shelter per animal (under 8 hours).

      b.    Herriman may increase the fees in the Section to cover its costs.
Any change in fees shall be in effect within 15 days of written notice to Riverton.

       c.      Herriman will provide Riverton with monthly billing no later than
the 5 day of the month following. Herriman will collect the fees directly from
Riverton. Riverton is responsible for collecting any fees from its residents or pet
owners.

4.     Holding and Release of Animals.

       a.    Every week the parties shall report to each other on the status of
the animals being held by Herriman.

       b.      Riverton Animal Services must coordinate with Herriman Animal
Services on any animals needing to be picked-up or dropped-off. Riverton
animal services must approve in writing (email okay) any animal release prior to
release of said animal by Herriman animal services.

      c.     Herriman will not allow pick-ups or drop-offs beyond its normal
business hours, unless agreed to by Herriman on a case-by-case basis.

       d.     If an animal is injured while being held at Herriman’s animal
shelter and the injury is caused by the negligence of the City, Herriman shall be
responsible for those costs.

      e.      All animals being released from the Herriman animal shelter shall
include a release of claims by the person picking up the animal.

5.     Termination of Agreement.

       a.      Riverton expects that they will need to utilize Herriman’s animal
shelter for approximately six months. Accordingly, the term of this Agreement
shall be for six months from the Effective Date. The parties may mutually agree
to extend this term by written Agreement.
            b.     Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason by
      providing the other party with at least thirty (30) days’ written notice.

            c.     Any animals currently being held by Herriman must be removed
      by Riverton no less than 24 hours before the termination date.

            d.      If Riverton fails to remove the animals within the notice period,
      Riverton will be liable for any additional holding fees incurred after the notice
      period.

      6.     Breach of Agreement.

              a.     If Riverton breaches any term of this Agreement, Herriman may, at
      its sole discretion, impose the following penalties:

                    i.     Payment of all outstanding fees due under this Agreement.

                   ii.   A penalty fee of $100 per day for each day Riverton fails to
             comply with the terms of this Agreement after written notice of the
             breach.

                  iii.  Any other remedies available under the law or this
             Agreement.

            b.     In the event of a breach by Riverton, Herriman reserves the right to
      suspend or terminate the Agreement immediately, with written notice to
      Riverton, and to seek additional damages as allowed by law.

       7.     Indemnification. Riverton agrees to indemnify and hold harmless
Herriman, its officers, employees, and agents, from any claims, liabilities, damages, or
expenses arising from Riverton’s actions, omissions, or breaches of this Agreement,
including but not limited to actions related to the animals brought to Herriman’s
shelter.

      8.     Miscellaneous.

             a.     This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the
      parties and supersedes any prior agreements or communications related to the
      subject matter hereof.
            b.     Any amendments to this Agreement must be in writing and
      executed by both parties.

            c.    If any provision of this Agreement is deemed invalid or
      unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

            d.     This Agreement and the rights and obligations of the parties shall
      be governed and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the State of Utah.

              e.     In the event any action or proceeding is taken or brought by either
      party concerning this Agreement, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover
      its costs and reasonable attorneys’ fees, whether such sums are expended with or
      without suit, at trial, on appeal or in any bankruptcy or insolvency proceeding.
