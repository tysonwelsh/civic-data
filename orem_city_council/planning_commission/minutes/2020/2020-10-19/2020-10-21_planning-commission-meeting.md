                  Chair Cochran asked Mr. Bench to introduce Agenda Item 3.1:

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Business Licensing for the City to amend Appendix A of the Orem City Code by
amending Standard Land Use Code 6413 Automobile Repair to Automobile Repair (Mechanical Only) and enacting
Standard Land Use Code 6414 Auto Body Repair and Paint as permitted uses in the C2, HS, M1 and M2 zones.




Staff Presentation: There is currently a Standard Land Use (SLU) Code for Automobile Repair (6413) in Appendix
A of the Orem City Code which has covered all auto repair. It has recently been brought to the attention of Business
Licensing that auto body work needs a special license from the State and they have special safety restrictions that are
not required with mechanical auto repair. After working with several businesses who are less than cooperative with
the Neighborhood Preservation Officer, it was decided that a separate SLU code was needed to help mitigate the
potential issues from non-licensed paint booths to operate without proper approvals. Several businesses are doing auto
painting that is getting overspray on vehicles in the area, they are not getting permits for their paint booths, they do
not have the proper ventilation, they do not have the required State license and they do not have health department
regulations in place. It is a nuisance to neighboring businesses and can cause property damage.

Recommendation: Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City
Council to amend Standard Land Use Code 6413 Automobile Repair to Automobile Repair (Mechanical Only) and
enact Standard Land Use Code 6414 Auto Body Repair and Paint as permitted uses in the C2, HS, M1 and M2 zones.

There was discussion on the item. Some of the key issues that were discussed were:
    ● How does this text amendment affect current businesses?
    ● The actual enforcement of the issue would be with Business Licensing.
    ● There are currently people who run auto shops out of their house. That is not permitted.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Cook moved to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to amend
Standard Land Use Code 6413 Automobile Repair to Automobile Repair (Mechanical Only) and enact Standard Land
Use Code 6414 Auto Body Repair and Paint as permitted uses in the C2, HS, M1 and M2 zones. Mr. Spencer seconded
the motion. Those voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Camille Jensen, Carl Cook, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer, and Mike
Staker. The motion passed unanimously.

                   Chair Cochran asked Mr. Bench to introduce Agenda Item 3.3:

Agenda Item 3.3 is a request by Tom Stuart Construction to approve the site plan of Tear Apart Auto Recycling
located at 520 North Geneva Road in the M2 zone.




Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to build a small 1500 square foot office building and use the rest of
the acreage to store autos prior to being transferred to the facility in Salt Lake City. The main facility for this business
is in Salt Lake City where they specialize in buying and selling cars, as well as providing used auto parts.

At the facility in Orem they will not be selling autos or parts. This will be a short term storage facility, usually a
vehicle will be on this site for only two or three days. It will then be transported to the facility in Salt Lake City.

Architecture: The proposed one-story structure will be 1,500 square feet. Materials will be corrugated metal siding,
stucco and reclaimed wood. The storage area for autos will be reclaimed asphalt.

Parking: They have met all the required parking requirements. Six spaces were required (1 per 250 square feet), and
they have provided nineteen parking stalls including a handicap parking stall.

Dumpster: The dumpster is to the north of the project and enclosed as required by code.

Landscaping: The overall landscape plan complies with applicable City and M2 zone codes. The existing wall and
landscaping along Geneva Road will remain.

Transportation/Engineering: No Concerns.

                   Chair Cochran asked Mr. Bench to introduce Agenda Item 3.4:

Agenda Item 3.4 is a request by Davies Design Build to amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of
Orem by changing the zoning of approximately 2.16 acres from the OS5 zone to the C2 zone at approximately 2000
South Geneva Road.

Existing Zoning:




Proposed Zoning:




Staff Presentation: The applicant is requesting the zone change to allow for a neighborhood convenience store and
gas station. The proposed gas station will be patterned after the existing gas station at approximately 1700 West Main
Street in Lehi. It is proposed to have internal restaurants, a convenient store, and 6-12 gas pumps.

The properties in question were originally designated in the General Plan as LDR residential. However, a recent
change was made to the South-West area in the General Plan that designates this corner as Community Commercial
(CC). This change was made understanding that this applicant was interested in this rezone.

The proposed rezone sits at the corner of Geneva Road and 2000 South. The Development Review Committee
requested that the applicant go through preliminary review for his curb cuts since 2000 South is a Provo street and
Geneva Road is a UDOT road. Preliminary UDOT and Provo City reviews have been completed. Provo is very likely
to grant the request. However, UDOT has not indicated whether or not the request will be granted. Once the applicant

applies for site plan approval, he will need to get full approval from both jurisdictions in addition to Orem City for the
curb cuts. UDOT is planning on putting in a light at the intersection in the near future.

As the property was originally planned to be residential, the water and sewer infrastructure was also originally planned
to go through this property to service the future residential properties to the east of this corner. In order to be able to
still accommodate that plan, staff will require the applicant to continue those utilities through the property to the east

The applicant was required to hold a neighborhood meeting prior to applying for the rezone. 44 residents attended the
meeting. Below is a list of some prominent concerns with the applicant’s response to each one.
    ● Why this particular location instead of elsewhere?
             ○ Demand for a convenience store in this area is increasing. The closest commercial to this location
                 is at Geneva Road and University Parkway.
    ● What kind of fence will border the gas station?
             ○ An 8’ stamped concrete wall.
    ● If approved for the zone change, what is the likelihood of something other than a gas station going into the
         property?
             ○ The cost of the land would prohibit anything other than a gas station from going in.
    ● How tall will the structure be?
             ○ 32 feet.

During the neighborhood meeting, the applicant asked for an informal vote of all in attendance as to whether they
approved of the proposal or not. The vote was 42 in favor, 1 abstained, and 1 against. The general feel from the meeting
was that the residents were excited about the proposal.

Recommendation: Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City
Council to amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone of approximately
2.16 acres from the OS5 zone to the C2 zone at approximately 2000 South Geneva Road.

There was discussion on the item. Some of the key issues that were discussed were:
    ● Traffic and access. The applicant received preliminary approval from Provo City Engineering for access on
        2000 South. The applicant has not yet received such approval from UDOT for access on Geneva Road, but
        has had a conversation with them regarding it and anticipates receiving that approval if granted site plan
        approval from Orem City.
    ● Where was the future Lakeview Parkway going to go in relation to this site? About a third of a mile west of
        this corner.
    ● Had the applicant considered this future road in his traffic study? Yes.
    ● Had a market study been conducted to determine if this site was viable for a gas station? Yes. The applicant
        conducted a market study and determined that this site was very viable for a gas station.
    ● The annexation of this property in the City happened about five years ago. At that time, residential uses made
        sense for this corner. What changed? The Church of Jesus Christ announced a temple to be placed near this
        site. The City changed the general plan recently for this area to attempt to be more in harmony with that site.
        As part of that change, this corner was designated to be commercial.
    ● What did the previous general plan designate this corner to be? Low Density Residential.
    ● Did the buyers of the new homes to the north of this site know that a gas station was potentially going in at
        this corner? The applicant indicated that the realtors and contractors of many of those homes attended the
        neighborhood meeting back in March and were aware of the change. The developer of those homes also
        attended that meeting and indicated that he was afraid that this gas station might compromise the closing of
        the sales of those homes. Most of these homes were not built at the time that neighborhood meeting was held.
        There is no way of knowing for sure, but these realtors and contractors may or may not have communicated
        that information to the home buyers. It is evident in the many public comments these residents made that
        they were probably not informed.
    ● Public Comments included some of the following concerns:
              ○ Many of the neighboring Provo residents expressed strong opposition to the change.
