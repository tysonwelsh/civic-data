                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 26-02

         A RESOLUTION APPOINTING A CITY REPRESENTATIVE TO THE
                  ASSOCIATION OF MUNICIPAL COUNCILS

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is a part of the Association of Municipal Councils which; and

      WHEREAS, the City is entitled to appoint a representative to serve on the Association of
Municipal Councils; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Councilmember
Spencer Haymond is willing to serve as the City’s representative on the Association of
Municipal Councils.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointment of
          Councilmember Spencer Haymond to serve as the City’s representative on the
          Association of Municipal Councils until a successor is appointed.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 20th day of
January 2026 by the following vote:
