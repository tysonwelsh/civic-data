2. PUBLIC COMMENT
Mr. Oscar Arvizu  stated he supported the Rio Grande Plan, which would bury and box 
the train tracks along 400 West, creating a more seamless east -to-west connection in the 
city.  This plan would create open stretches of land for housing, parks, and businesses.  It 
would unify neighborhoods and create a dynamic connection and vibrant downtown core .  
It could potentially improve public transit, traffic congestion, and air quality.  He urged the 
Council to take the first steps in funding an impact analysis.
Council Member Stringham  asked Council Member Winder Newton if this would 
qualify for fourth quarter funding.
Council Member Winder Newton stated she would look into that.

woman elected to state senate in the United States.  She ran against her husband 
and won, and served one term.  She created the State Board of Health and the 
School for the Deaf and Blind.  She was the first female surgeon in Utah, and had 
to receive her schooling separately, because to attend alongside the men in her class 
was deemed inappropriate at the time.  She was also a suffragette.  
Ms. Erin Litvack , Deputy Mayor of County Services, stated there was a public art 
piece in the Salt Palace Convention Center in honor of Martha Hughes Cannon.  It 
was made by a local artist from carpet remnants.  She recommended taking a trip to 
see it.

counselor.

4.1 Selection of Council Leadership for 2025 25-2429
Attachments: Staff Report
(Approx. 3:45PM, 5 Min.)
Discussion/Direction
A motion was made by Council Member Harrison, seconded by Council 
Member Winder Newton, that Council Member Theodore be appointed as 
Council Chair, Council Member Stringham be appointed as Council Vice Chair, 
Council Member Winder Newton be appointed as Council Pro Tempore, and 
Council Member Brandshaw be appointed as Minority Caucus Chair. The 
motion carried unanimously, with Council Members Stewart and Moreno 
abstaining.
Council Member Stringham  asked Legal Counsel if Council members 
were allowed to abstain from voting in this scenario.  
Mr. Mitchell Park , Legal Counsel, Council Office, stated statute required 
Council members to abstain if there was a conflict of interest, but did not 
state whether abstaining was allowable in other situations.  Robert’s Rules of 
Order had additional language about the rules for abstaining, but he could not 
cite them off the top of his head.
