                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-22

       A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL FILLING A
       MIDTERM VACANCY AND APPOINTING A MEMBER OF THE
       ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD FOR THE TRAVERSE RIDGE
       SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. (UCA) Section 17-D-1-301 et seq. Draper City
has, by Ordinance No. 1124 and Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 3-1-290, created
an administrative control board and delegated certain powers thereto to administer the
responsibilities of the Traverse Ridge Special Service District; and

        WHEREAS, DCMC Section 3-1-290(e) requires the Administrative Control Board be
comprised of five members appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City
Council, and that each member shall be a registered voter within the special service district or be
an officer or employee of Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, Cal Roberts, who was appointed on January 22, 2019, as a member of the
Administrative Control Board, is vacating a four year appointed position which expires December
31, 2022; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to UCA Section 20A-1-512 the City prepared a notice of vacancy
for the vacant board member position, posted the notice in at least three public places within the
local district at least two weeks before the deadline for accepting nominees for the appointment,
published the notice in a newspaper of general circulation and on the Utah Public Notice website,
and otherwise complied with all requirements of the law for filling midterm vacancies; and

      WHEREAS, the City now desires to appoint a qualified person to fill the mid-term
vacancy on the Administrative Control Board;

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        The following registered voter of the Traverse Ridge Special Service District or officers or
employees of Draper City shall be and is hereby appointed to fill the remainder of a vacant four-
year term which shall expire on December 31, 2022:
