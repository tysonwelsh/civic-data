                               RESOLUTION CRA-25-06

     A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AGENY
  DISSOLVING THE DRAPER GATEWAY COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT AREA

       WHEREAS, the Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City (the “Agency”)
was created to transact the business and exercise the powers provided for in the
Limited Purpose Local Government Entities - Community Reinvestment Agency Act,
UTAH CODE ANN. 17C-1-101 et seq. and any subsequent, replacement or amended law
or act (the “Act”); and

       WHEREAS, by Ordinance of the City Council of Draper City, the Agency’s
governing board (the “Board”) adopted a community development plan (the “Plan”)
for the Draper Gateway Community Development Project Area (the “Project Area”)
pursuant to Sections 17C-4-102 and 103 of the Act; and

       WHEREAS, the Project Area’s tax increment collection period has expired, and
all funds collected within the Project Area have been expended in accordance with
the Plan; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to section 17C-1-702 of the Act, the Agency seeks to
dissolve the Project Area, with final dissolution to occur upon the adoption of an
ordinance by the Draper City Council; and

       WHEREAS, the Board met on October 21, 2025 to consider, among other
things, adopting Resolution CRA-25-06 to dissolve the Draper Gateway Community
Development Project Area and all other necessary actions to effectuate this
resolution;

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the governing board of the Draper City
Community Reinvestment Agency as follows:

       1.    Dissolving of Project Area. It has become necessary and desirable to
dissolve the Project Area, specifically the Draper Gateway Community Development
Project Area with a map attached hereto in Exhibit A. The Agency hereby directs the
Draper City Council to consider and adopt an ordinance pursuant to Utah Code Ann.
17C-1-702 to effectuate the dissolution.

       2.     Notification of Taxing Entities. Staff are authorized and directed to
provide any required notices to taxing entities with interlocal agreements related to
the Project Area (i.e., Canyons School District, Central Utah Water Conservancy
District, Draper City, Jordan Valley Water District, South Valley Sewer District, Draper
City, Salt Lake County) and each taxing entity that levies or imposes a tax on property
within the dissolved Project Area, as necessary to comply with such agreements and
required notices upon dissolution.

       3.      Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
its adoption. The Project Area shall be dissolved upon the adoption of an ordinance
of the City’s legislative body.
