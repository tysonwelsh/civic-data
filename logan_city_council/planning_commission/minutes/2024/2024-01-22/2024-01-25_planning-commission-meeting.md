Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session on
Thursday, January 25, 2024. Commissioner Guth called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Jordy Guth, Roylan Croshaw, Jessica Lucero, David Lewis, Sara Doutre,
Eldon Peterson

Commissioners Excused: Ken Heare

Staff Present: Mike DeSimone, Russ Holley, Tanya Rice, Amanda Pearce, Mohammed Abdullahi,
Aaron Smith, Jeannie Simmonds (City Council) and Mike Johnson (City Council).

Commissioner Peterson made a motion to approve the minutes from the January 11, 2024 meeting.
Commissioner Doutre seconded the motion. The motion was approved unanimously.

Commissioner Lucero made a motion to elect Commissioner Doutre as Planning Commission Chair
and Commissioner Guth as Vice Chair. Commissioner Lewis seconded the motion. The motion
was approved unanimously and will become effective at the February 8, 2024 meeting.

Robinson Ranch Subdivision, PC 24-003, and LDC Amendment Engineering Design Standards, PC
24-004 to the February 8" meeting. Commissioner Peterson seconded the motion. The motion was
approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING:

PC 24-005 Land Development Code Amendment — Alcohol, Chapters 17.11, 17.62 [Code
Amendment] Aaron Smith/Logan City is requesting a code amendment to the Land Development
Code, Chapters 17.11 and 17.62 that include changes to the administrative procedures for alcohol
licensing to better conform with state and local laws; and make changes to local restrictions regarding
alcohol uses.

STAFF/PROPONENT: Aaron Smith, Business Licensing Administrator reviewed the proposed
amendments to the Land Development Code Chapters 17.11 District and Corridor Zone Uses,
updating the local restrictions regarding alcohol uses, including bars, taverns, brew restaurants,
package agencies, off-premise beer, manufacturing, and distribution, and the proposed amendments
to Chapter 17.62 Definitions adding new definitions for Bar, Small Brewery, Brewery, Distillery, and
Winery.

The proposed package of code amendments related to the regulation of alcohol uses also includes
changes to the Municipal Code Section 5.10 Alcoholic Beverage, including updated administrative
procedures for alcohol licensing to better conform with state and local laws, policies, and procedures.
The City Council is the decision making authority on Municipal Code amendments, so therefore the
Planning Commission will only make a recommendation on the Land Development Code
amendments.


The proposed staff changes to the Land Development Code Chapter 17.11 Use Table include the
following:

PUBLIC: Chris Sisneros-Kidd who is looking to start the first Home Range Brewing in Logan spoke
about the difficulty finding a location in the TC-1 zone that meets the proximity requirements and said
that leaving Taprooms as a permitted use in the TC-1 zone would incentivize business in the area
and not create additional steps in an already heavily regulated industry.

Commissioner Peterson asked what areas in town Mr. Sisneros-Kidd believes would be ideal for a

Taproom. Mr. Sisneros-Kidd said that the Island would be a good location or something near 1000
West in the Commercial Service area.

COMMISSION: The Commission discussed the proposed use table and recommended the following
adjustments:

Change Bar and Taproom uses to Permitted in the Town Center One (TC-1) and to Conditionally
Permitted in the Town Center Two (TC-2) and Mixed Use (MU) zones.

Change Bar in conjunction with manufacturing, and Tavern in conjunction with manufacturing, to
Permitted in the Commercial Service (CS) zone.

Change Brew Restaurant to Conditionally Permitted in the Community Commercial (CC) zone and
to Permitted in the Gateway (GW), Commercial Service (CS), Industrial Park (IP), and Airport (AP)
zones.

Change Taproom and Tavern uses to Permitted in the Town Center One (TC-1) zone and to
Conditionally Permitted in the Town Center Two (TC-2) and Mixed Use (MU) zones.

Change Brewery, Distillery or Winery uses to Conditionally Permitted in the Industrial Park (IP) zone.
Change “Small” Brewery, Distillery, or Winery to Permitted in the Commercial Service (CS) zone.
The Commission also discussed updating the definition of Small Brewery to Small Brewery, Distillery,

or Winery, removing the alcohol volume limitation and only using a square footage limitation of
10,000 square feet or less.


MOTION: Commissioner Lucero made a motion to forward a recommendation of approval to the
Municipal Council with the changes discussed on code amendment PC 24-005 with the findings for
approval as listed below. Commissioner Lewis seconded the motion.

FINDING EOR APPROVAL

1. Utah State Law authorizes local Planning Commission to recommend ordinance changes to the
legislative body (Municipal Council).

2. The Code Amendments are done in conformance with the requirements of Title 17.51 of the
Logan Municipal Code.

3. The proposed Code Amendments are consistent with the Logan City General Plan.

4. The proposed land use changes contained in LDC Chapters 17.11 and 17.62 are consistent with
the goals of the various zones.

5. No public comment has been received regarding the proposed amendments.

Moved: J. Lucero Seconded: D. Lewis Approved: 6-0
