Public Hearing and recommendation to the County Council for an amendment to Chapter 10-4-10, Use Table of the Snyderville Basin Development Code to allow Gasoline Service Station with a Convenience Store in the Service Commercial (SC) zone. Maverick Incorporated, Applicant. Project # 23-173. Legislative Review. Ray Milliner, Principal Planner.
(6:05 P.M.)
Attachment:
January 23, 2024 Staff Report Use Table Amendment.pdf
Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner
, presented for public hearing and recommendation to the County Council for an amendment to Chapter 10-4-10.
Richard Piggott with Maverick Inc., briefly addressed the Commission.
Clarifying questions were asked by members of the Commission.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:13 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:14 p.m.
Additional questions were asked of Planner Milliner and the applicant.
Christopher Conabee made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council per the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval in the staff report. DJ Hubler seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
