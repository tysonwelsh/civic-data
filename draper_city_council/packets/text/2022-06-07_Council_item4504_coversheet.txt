Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: June 7, 2022
Re: Ordinance 1542 Comments: Parades in Draper are traditionally well attended and spectators claim their viewing spots along the parade routes days, if not, weeks, in advance by a variety of methods, including placing chairs and roping off areas in the right-of-way and parking motorhomes and similar large vehicles along the parade route.
In order to balance the public interest in observing a parade, the property rights of homeowners along the parade route and the City’s authority to regulate city-owned rights-of way, the City wishes to implement regulations regarding when and how spectators can occupy viewing areas on public property along a parade route.  ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Ordinance 1542 6/6/2022 Cover Memo Exhibit - Ord. 1542 6/6/2022 Cover Memo Exhibit - Ord. 1542 - redlined 6/6/2022 Cover Memo
