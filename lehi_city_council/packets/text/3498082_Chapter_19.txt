  APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 04.060. Criteria for Approval.
No amendment to this Code, or the Zoning District Map(s), may be recommended for approval by the Commission
nor approved by the City Council unless such amendment or conditions thereto are consistent with the General Plan.
In considering a Code amendment, or Zoning District Map(s) amendment, the applicant shall identify, and the City
Staff, DRC, Commission, and the City Council may consider the following factors, among others:

    1.   The effect of the proposed amendment on the character of the surrounding area;
    2.   Consistency with the goals and policies of the General Plan.
    3.   Consistency and Compatibility of the proposed zone with the General Plan land uses of nearby and adjoining
         properties.
    4.   The suitability of the properties for the uses requested and their suitability for the existing uses identified by
         the General Plan;
    5.   Whether a change in the uses allowed for the affected properties will unduly affect the uses, or proposed uses
         for nearby and adjoining properties;
    6.   The gain to the public health, safety and welfare from the existing classification to the proposed amendment;
         and
    7.   The overall community benefit of the proposed amendment.


  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests review and recommendation of proposed amendments to Chapter 19, Renewable Energy Sys-
tem Regulations, to remove references and requirements for conditional uses. This update is a comprehensive update
of this Chapter to try to match with current efforts to remove conditional uses where possible. The primary changes
relate to wind energy facilities, small wind energy facilities, and roof-mounted wind energy systems as follows below:

Wind Energy Facility
          o Clarifies that this is not a conditional use (to be finalized with the upcoming Table of Uses update)
          o Removes the requirement for a noise analysis study since there are already Municipal Code allow-
              ances for noise
          o Clarifies fencing requirements by removing variability and just requiring the 6-foot fence with lock-
              ing gate

Small Wind Energy Facility
           o Clarifies that this is not a conditional use
             o    Establishes a maximum height limit directly in the Code (no Planning Commission review required)

Roof-Mounted Wind Energy System
          o Clarifies that this is not a conditional use

These changes aim to improve clarity and consistency in the interpretation and application of the Development Code.

The DRC had no comments on the proposed Development Code amendment.


  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development Code Amendments are legislative items giving the Planning Commission a large amount of discretion.
They can recommend approval, denial or any changes they would like to see.

The public can also speak to this item and share any of their concerns.


  POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS

The Planning Commission may move to recommend approval, approval with modifications or denial of the pro-
posed amendments to the Lehi City Development Code. Any motion should include findings. Possible findings
include:

    1.   The proposed Development Code does/ does not seek to ensure the health, safety and welfare of existing and
         future Lehi Residents.
    2.   The proposed Development Code amendment is/is not in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provi-
         sions of the General Plan and its various elements.
    3.   Additional findings based upon information presented during the public hearing.

Narrative –Chapter 19 Development Code Amendment

The Planning Division is proposing a Development Code Amendment to clean up and update
sections of Chapter 19, Renewable Energy System Regulations, of the Development Code.
Specifically, we are proposing a comprehensive update to match with the Table of Uses and
remove references to Conditional Uses.

The primary changes made with the comprehensive update are as follows:
   • Wind Energy Facility-
          o Clarified that this is not a conditional use. This change will be finalized with a
              change to the Table of Uses that is upcoming
          o Removed requirement for a noise analysis study
          o Clarified fence requirements
   • Small Wind Energy Facility
          o Clarified that this is not a conditional use
          o Sets the maximum height in Code and not subject to Planning Commission
              decision
   • Roof-Mounted Wind Energy System
          o Clarified that this is not a conditional use

We believe this will help clarify the interpretation of the Code.

Please contact the Planning Division if you have any questions.
