Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Vice Chairman Everett welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Discussion of Bike and Pedestrian Master Plan update
Discussed after item 3.

Mike West presented a video, "Do your buses get stuck on traffic?" Mike provided information on
Bike and Pedestrian Master Plan updates. Mike presented information on bike safety and the issues
that bike infrastructure is facing.

3. Review public noticing requirements
Discussed noticing requirements and whether or not the physical noticing for every public hearing
item was necessary. Jacob and the Commissioners agreed that the importance of transparency was
necessary even though it was not required. Engage Lehi now offers scanning QR codes as well.
The planning staff does take a great deal of time to conduct physical noticing. The Commission

feels there is value in physical noticing and knows that online noticing will eventually take the
place of that.

4. Discussion of draft accessory dwelling unit standards
Brittney Harris presented new information of draft dwelling unit standards. The Commission
discussed lighting, setbacks, height, conversion of conditional use permits, and other
requirements.

5. Discussion of Maker Space standards

    Commissioner Ellis motioned to extend the meeting to 7:15 p.m. Commissioner Eyre
    seconded. The motion passed unanimously.

Sierra and McKay presented information on Maker Space Standards and Flex Use Development.

3. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Ellis moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Hemmert seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:15 p.m.
