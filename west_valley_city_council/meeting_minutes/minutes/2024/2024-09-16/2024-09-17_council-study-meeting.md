REVIEW AGENDA FOR REGULAR CITY COUNCIL MEETING OF
SEPTEMBER 17, 2024
Steve Pastorik discussed recent changes made to the development agreement as follows:
       - No basement entrances permitted for the townhomes in the project.
       - A maximum of 30% of the housing units could be designated as rentals, a
           provision to be enforced by the HOA through the CC&Rs.
       - The single-family homes along 1950 West will be required to be at least 8,000
           square feet, have an 80-foot frontage, and include three-car garages.
       - No more than four unrelated individuals could reside in a single home, which
           would also be enforced by the HOA through the CC&Rs.
       - Edge Homes, the developer, would not retain ownership of any units for rental
           purposes, ensuring all units would be sold.

Councilmember Harmon asked if walk out basements could also be banned in the single-
family units. Steve replied that he would talk to the developer to see if they were amenable
to this. Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if there is a definition of “related individuals”.
Steve replied that the City doesn’t define this in Code but the HOA would need to define
that in their covenants. Steve briefly explained enforcement procedure in these types of
communities. Councilmember Huynh expressed concern regarding the amenities and
stated that he would like to see those improved so they are similar to the DAI project at
Decker Lake.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Lang, members of the Council had no further questions or concerns
regarding items listed on the Agenda for the Regular City Council meeting scheduled later
this night.


ORDINANCE 24-40: AMEND SECTIONS 3-11-103, 3-11-105. AND 3-11-106 OF
THE WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO AMEND CERTAIN
PROVISIONS GOVERNING THE EMPLOYEE DISCIPLINE HEARING

Eric Bunderson, City Attorney, presented proposed Ordinance 24-40 that would amend
Sections 3-11-103, 3-11-105. and 3-11-106 of the West Valley City Municipal Code to Amend
Certain Provisions Governing the Employee Discipline Hearing Officer.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       cases. Key changes include clarifying appeal filing requirements, establishing a

RESOLUTION 24-141: APPROVE THE PURCHASE OF NETWORK SECURITY
PRODUCTS AND RELATED EQUIPMENT
Ken Cushing, IT Director, presented proposed Resolution 24-141 that would approve the
Purchase of Network Security Products and Related Equipment.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       1. Network Security Enhancements from Manage Engine:
              Description: The implementation of Endpoint Central will enable the
                 management of all desktops, laptops, and mobile devices, allowing
                 employees to install approved software and request IT helpdesk
                 assistance. AD Audit will monitor and analyze login and network access
                 activities, as well as changes to user accounts. ADSelfService Plus
                 Professional will provide a centralized platform for password
                 management and policy enforcement.
              Quoted Cost: $40,000
       2. Server Upgrades and Virtualization:
              Description: This project involves replacing outdated servers used for
                 virtualization to improve resource utilization and maintain redundancy,
                 ensuring the City's IT infrastructure remains robust and efficient.
              Quoted Cost: $27,424.54
       3. Network Infrastructure Expansion:
              Description: Many network switches and related equipment at city
                 facilities are outdated, with some no longer receiving security patches
                 or supporting modern features. Upgrading this equipment will enhance
                 security and reliability by enabling the use of modern features and
                 ensuring the ability to apply necessary patches.
              Quoted Cost: $252,840.88

REVIEW AGENDA FOR SPECIAL REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY MEETING
SCHEDULED SEPTEMBER 24, 2024

      RDA RESOLUTION 24-12: AUTHORIZE THE AGENCY TO EXECUTE A
      PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT WITH ABILITY INCLUSION
      SERVICES
      Jonathan Springmeyer, ED Director, presented proposed Resolution 24-12
      authorize the Agency to Execute a Purchase and Sale Agreement With Ability
      Inclusion Services.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:

             Ability Inclusion Services has submitted a signed agreement for the
             purchase of three parcels of land owned by the WVC Redevelopment
             Agency. The purchase amount is $350,000. The purpose of the land will
             be for the development of an independent living facility for persons with
             disabilities. The facility will be owned and operated by Ability Inclusion
             Services.

             Ability Inclusion Services (AIS) is a non-profit organization located in West
             Valley City that serves persons with disabilities providing community
             opportunities for independence and individual growth. AIS wishes to
             purchase this property for the construction of a new independent living
             facility for adult persons with disabilities. The facility will be owned and
             operated by AIS.

      Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if there are any other proposals or alternate ideas
      for the use of this property. Jon replied no. Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if
      there would be any concern regarding the types of tenants and their proximity to
      schools. Jon replied no and stated that this organization focus solely on adults with
      disabilities.

      The Redevelopment Agency will consider Resolution 24-12 at the Special Meeting
      scheduled September 24, 2024 at 6:30 P.M
