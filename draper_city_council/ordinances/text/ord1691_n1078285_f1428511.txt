City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1691

On December 16, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1691 amending

at approximately 231 E. 13800 S. known as the Openshaw Draper 138 Zoning Map

County Recorder on May 5, 2026. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
