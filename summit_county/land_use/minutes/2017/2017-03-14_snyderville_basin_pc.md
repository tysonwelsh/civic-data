TRANSPORTATION REVIEW 
In attendance was Caroline Rodriguez, Summit County Transportation Director.  With 
the CVMA was Scott Perkes (manager of Planning and Transportation) with the CVMA, 
and Brian Modrowsky (Executive Director), also with the CVMA.  Ms. Rodriguez said 
that the CVMA has been great to work with in meeting the County’s transportation 
goals.  She thinks they are a strong partner.   
 
Mr. Modrowsky said they will go through a power point presentation.  Following the 
presentation will be a question and answer period.  He said the power point was shown 
to the County Council in January 2016.  They worked for six months to create a plan 
that would be acceptable to both the CVMA and the County Council.  The bottom line 
objective is to ensure they can mitigate the 27% traffic reduction within the SPA when 
it is fully developed.   
 
In 2014, it was shown that they were at about 24%.  This helped them to ascertain what 
they had to do to reach 27%.  The power point showed different figures of traffic 
mitigation.  They predict that at 100% build-out, they will be at 27% mitigation; 
however, it was realized that additional strategies will need to be implemented in order 
to reach that number.   
 
Mr. Modrovsky described how they reached 16% traffic mitigation.  He expanded that 
the cabriolet, a gondola, and the transit center were put in, along with pedestrian 
pathways.  They have a regional bus system.  There are carpooling incentive programs.

“Development within Critical Lands Prohibited: Development within

1. Discussion regarding The County Resource Management Plan – Sean Lewis, 
County Planner  
 
Planner Sean Lewis said approximately two years ago, the State Legislature passed a 
bill requiring counties to develop a resource management plan.  It required an outline 
of how resources should be managed on Federal Lands.  That legislation was amended 
in 2016.  It allowed an extension of time.  The State of Utah has allocated funds for this 
to be accomplished.  The goal is to combine the 29 counties’ plans into a larger State-
wide plan.   
 
Planner Lewis said he spent the second half of 2016 working with the Mountainland 
Association of Governments.  A link to this information is on the County’s web site.  At 
the completion of that process, Summit County hired consultants from the firm of 
Logan-Simpson Design.  Attending the meeting are the consultants.  They are Whitney 
May, Alison Eddie, and Chrissie Gilmore.    
 
Under the direction of the Summit County Council, the consultants are writing a Summit 
County Resource Plan.  It is not a Snyderville Basin or Eastern Summit County plan.  
They are working with Councilmember Carson and Councilmember Armstrong.  The 
State requires this will be referenced in the General Plan.  Referencing it will allow the 
plan to be amended independently.
