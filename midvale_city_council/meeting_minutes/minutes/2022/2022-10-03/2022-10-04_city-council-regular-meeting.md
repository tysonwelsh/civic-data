    . DISCUSSION REGARDING SETBACKS FOR GARAGES, CARPORTS, AND
       ACCESSORY STRUCTURES W|THIN THE SF.1 ANO SF-2 ZONES
Adam Olsen said the Planning Staff proposes amendments related to setbacks for
attached garages, carports, accessory structures and EADU's within the SF-1 and SF-2
zone districts. The amendments will allow flexibility in setbacks for expansion of existing
attached garages and/or carports that could not otherwise occur. Current Code requires
a side setback of four feet (4') for an attached garage or carport. Multiple areas within the
SF-1 and SF-2 zones have existing garage/carport structures which encroach into the
required 4' setback, rendering many of these unable to expand. These structures are
classified as "non-conforming." The amendments, as proposed, will allow a side
expansion to 1' of a property line, as well as the ability to expand deeper into the lot at
the current non-conforming setback line.

Setbacks for unoccupied accessory structures and External Accessory Dwelling Units
(EADU's) will remain al 2' frcm side and rear property lines. However, the proposed
amendments will allow the ability for a 0' setback, provided certain criteria are met
(identified in draft language).

           FOR PARKING LOTS
Wendelin Knobloch said Midvale City proposes amendments to the Main Street Form-
Based Code (MS-FBC) to harmonize the code with current best practice and allow limited
usage of neon elements within signage on Main Street.

The landscaping section of the code requires improvements to the interiors of surface
parking lots, and it has become apparent that innovative development patterns of mixed-
use developments create surface parking lots with building or roof overhangs that make
landscaping impracticable in certain situations. The code language has been adjusted to
accommodate for this type of case.

The amended code language in the signage section allows limited usage of "neon signs"
if the project location is on Main Street within the MS-FBC area and is associated with a
historic structure, or a parcel that used to host a historic structure. The amendment, if
approved, will facilitate redevelopment in an authentic manner because neon signage
was a part of historic main streets throughout northern America.

Public notice was sent to affected entities for the planning commission and city council
hearings as required in Section 17-3-9 of the Midvale Municipal Code. At the time of this
writing no public comment has been received.

E. Criteia/Required Findings. The city's zoning is the result of a detailed and

proposed rezoning is consistent with the policies and goals of the general plan and that
the applicant has demonstrated that the:

1      Proposed rezoning is necessary either to comply with the general plan proposed

higher density housing options and taller buildings in the area around the TRAX station."
