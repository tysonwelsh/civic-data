Nephi City Work Session
June 25, 2024
7:00 p.m.
21 E 100 N
Nephi, UT 84648


	•	 Four members of the Ute Stampede Committee met with the Council to discuss the details of parades, concessions, law enforcement, and other concerns of the Ute Stampede celebration.  Mayor Seely expressed concern about the added Wednesday night to the celebration and how that adds additional expense to the police department.

	•	 City Administrator Seth Atkinson showed the Council a new look to the Nephi City Logo.  Rowdy Voorhees, the summer intern, had made the changes.  The Council gave their approval for the new colors on the logo.  

	•	 Administrative and City Council Updates:
	•	Mr. Atkinson reported that it is time for his annual evaluation by the Council
	•	The golf course appraisal was scheduled to happen on Wednesday, June 25, 2024.
	•	Mr. Atkinson reported that the Arts Grant application period is coming up.
	•	The total sales tax for fiscal year 2024 came in just under $2,000,000.
	•	City Attorney Kasey Wright showed the signs for OHV and golf cart safety created by the Zero Fatalities organization.
	•	City Attorney Kasey Wright reviewed a parking lot ordinance draft,
	•	City Attorney Kasey Wright updated the council about the cooling system at the Hive.  The system has not been working well but staff is working with a company to help solve the problem.
	•	Councilor Travis Worwood reported that Arts in the Park was a success.
	•	Mayor Seely reminded the Council about the open house for the power plant.
	•	Councilor Parady reported that the Farmer’s Market was a successful event at the Pioneer Park.
	 
	•	 Councilor Worwood moved to convene into closed session to discuss the character and competence of an individual, real property, and litigation.  Councilor Parady seconded the motion.  The motion passed on a unanimous vote.  

	•	 The meeting adjourned at 7:58 p.m.

	•	 Councilor Travis Worwood moved to adjourn the meeting.  Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion.  The motion passed unanimously.  The meeting adjourned at 9:49 p.m.
