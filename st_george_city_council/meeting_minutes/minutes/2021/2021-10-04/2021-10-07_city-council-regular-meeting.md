PUBLIC HEARING/AMEND FISCAL YEAR 2021-2022 BUDGET/RESOLUTION:
     Public hearing and resolution to review and approve amendments to the
     Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Budget.

      Assistant City Manager Deanna Brklacich outlined the proposed amendments as
      follows: 1) To carryover funding from two federal grants received by the Police
      department in prior years but not fully expended as anticipated. The grants should
      be completely spent during this current fiscal year; 2) To increase the golf course
      budgets for unanticipated cost increases in rye and fescue seeds. The prices
      increased substantially due to reduced supply from the growers created by a
      combination of slow production caused by the pandemic and also drought conditions;
      3) To increase Southgate Golf Course budget for a few final remaining costs related
      to the Clubhouse Renovation project that were approved
      in the previous year but not completed until the current year. The costs will be
      covered by revenues from the Snack Bar which is now in full operation; 4) To
      increase the Economic Vitality & Housing department's budget to recognize a
      donation from TDS for the City to use towards research, initiatives, projects and
      other activities which supports the attainable housing goals of the Housing Action
      Coalition; 5) To carryover a portion of the $10,000 funding approved in the previous
      year towards the Art Museum Outdoor Sign project. We received a grant for $7,500
      in the prior year towards this project which will be applied, in addition to $425
      coming from Miscellaneous Revenues; 6) To recognize receipt of the City's
      Proportionate Share Distribution from the Utah Communications Authority (UCA) in
      the amount of $223,066 of which $97,800 is requested to be used towards phone
      network equipment for 3 consoles. The remainder of the funds will be used towards
      other one-time capital costs which are to be determined; 7) To increase the
      Coronavirus Relief Fund budget for Federal CARES Act COVID19 funds that were
      unspent in the previous year at $305,563, and COVID funds from the Utah
      Communications Authority (UCA) at $9,455, for a total of $315,018. These funds will
      be expended in the current year towards additional grant-eligible supplies and

LIFT DAYTIME IRRIGATION RESTRICTIONS:
      Consider lifting the daytime irrigation restrictions for those that irrigate
      with culinary water.

      Water Services Director Scott Taylor explained this is an item that takes place
      annually as the watering is expected to be less in the winter months. He noted the
      City has done a lot to encourage water conservation. As a result, the year-to-date
      water consumption is down 2% compared to last year. He mentioned that the
      secondary irrigation water used on City facilities decreased 12% from last year.
      Staff encourages citizens to be wise with water use. Although staff is requesting to
      lift the daytime irrigation restrictions, he asked that residents remain wise with their
      use of water.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Smethurst to lift the daytime
                    irrigation restrictions for those that irrigate with culinary water.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember McArthur.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.
