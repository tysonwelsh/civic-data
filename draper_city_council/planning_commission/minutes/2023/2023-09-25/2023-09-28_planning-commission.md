       C.      Public Hearing: Specialized Manufacturing Land Use and Zoning Map
               Amendment (Legislative Action).
               On the request of Bill Gould, representing Concord Properties and TETS, LLC, for
               a Land Use Map Amendment from Neighborhood Commercial to Community
               Commercial and a Zoning Map Amendment from CC (Community Commercial)
               to CG (General Commercial) on 3.89 acres of property located at 687 W 12300 S.
               The property is located in the CC zone. Application 2023-4554-MA, 2023-4558-
               MA. Staff contact is Tevai Haunga, 801-576-6522, tevai.haunga@draperutah.gov.

Chair Adams reported that the above item is the only public hearing on the meeting agenda. City
Planner, Tevai Haunga, presented the Staff Report and explained that the applicant is requesting
approval of a Land Use and Zoning Map amendment. The property is located at 687 West 12300
South. She shared aerial images of the site. The current land use designation is Neighborhood
Commercial and the proposed land use designation is for Community Commercial. The current
zoning is Community Commercial (“CC”) and the proposed zoning is for General Commercial
(“GC”). The applicant response letters were shown. Ms. Haunga explained that no comments
from the public had been received regarding the application.

Commissioner Fowler believed the Planning Commission had seen the property before. Planning
Manager, Todd Draper, confirmed this. The property came before the Commission for a rezone
to the current CC zone; however, there is now a request for the CG Zone so there could be light
manufacturing use on the property. Community Development Director, Jennifer Jastremsky, noted
that a Site Plan was previously approved for an office building but that project was no longer
moving forward.

The applicant’s representative, Bob Simons, introduced himself and reported that he represents
Specialized Manufacturing, Inc. If the project moves ahead, it will be their fourth location in
Draper since 1997. Mr. Simons reported that they manufacture cable and harness assemblies,
primarily for aircraft and aviation. They also do sheet metal and machining in support of those
aviation products. When the business first moved to Draper in 1997, there were two employees.
There are now 70. He discussed the different buildings that the business has utilized over the years
and explained that there was a desire to remain in Draper. The business needs to expand and the
property will allow for a building almost double in size of their current one. Their interest is in
building a 60,000-square-foot office warehouse on the proposed site.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

Chair Adams reminded the Commissioners that there would be a recommendation made to the
City Council. The request was for a Land Use Map amendment and a Zoning Map amendment.
Commissioner Fowler believed the use would fit in well with what is in the surrounding area.
There is nearby residential but she felt that separate enough that the use would not disturb those
residents. Commissioner Squire explained that her biggest issue was the adjacent residential.
Putting a commercial zone next to a residential zone could be problematic. Chair Adams asked
for additional information about the residential use. Mr. Draper reported that there are multiple
residentially zoned parcels, but only one home. City Attorney, Mike Barker, reported that the City
used to have a right-of-way in the vicinity but it was vacated. The Commissioners looked at a map
of the area. Mr. Simons pointed out that the actual use is light manufacturing. It is self-contained
and the use is already occurring within Draper in a CG Zone.

There was discussion about the proposed zone. Light manufacturing is allowed in the newly
requested zone. Chair Adams pointed out that the Commission needs to consider the worst-case
scenario when considering applications. Commissioner Nixon referenced the list of allowed uses
and wondered if this would fall under “Manufacturing, limited.” Mr. Draper confirmed this. It is
a commercial zone but allows for light manufacturing as an allowed use. Chair Adams agreed
with the comments shared. It appeared to be an appropriate use for the site but it is also important
to consider any adjacent residential uses. Commissioner Fowler noted that the residential area
appears to be in transition but it is not possible to guess about future uses there.

Commissioner Nixon believed the main difference between the CC and CG Zones relate to auto
rental, auto repair, and self-storage units. Commissioner Fowler noted that there is not a significant
difference between the current zone and the proposed zone. Commissioner Ogden stated that an
Enterprise car rental agency was to the east of the property. It seemed to him that the proposed
use made sense in this location.

Motion: Commissioner Fowler moved to FORWARD A POSITIVE RECOMMENDATION
to the City Council for the proposed Land Use Map Amendment, as requested by Bill Gould,
representing Concord Properties and TETS, LLC, for Application 2023-4554-MA based on
the following findings:

       1.      The proposed amendment is consistent with the goals, objectives, and policies
               of the City’s General Plan.

       2.      The proposed amendment is harmonious with the overall character of existing
               development in the vicinity of the subject property.

       3.      The proposed amendment is consistent with the standards of any applicable
               overlay zone.

       4.      The proposed amendment will not adversely affect adjacent property.
