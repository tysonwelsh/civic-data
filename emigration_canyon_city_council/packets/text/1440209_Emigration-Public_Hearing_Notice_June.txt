                           NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

             EMIGRATION CANYON PLANNING COMMISSION
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN Pursuant to Utah Code Title 10, Chapter 20, Section 205, that
the Emigration Canyon Planning Commission (the “Commission”) will hold a public hearing
on June 11, 2026, beginning at 8:30 AM (Meeting location on the back page). The purpose of
the hearing is to receive public comments regarding:

   •   CUP2025-001542 - Gregory Palis is applying for a dwelling group. Location: 6771 East
       Emigration Canyon Road. Zone: FR-0.5. Acres: 1.08. Planner: Justin
       Smith (Discussion, Action)

   •   OAM2026-001636 - Consideration of a resolution amending Emigration’s land use fee
       schedule. Presenter: Brian Hartsell (Discussion, Hearing, Action)

   •   OAM2026-001637 - Consideration of an ordinance enacting Chapter 19.03,
       Development Agreements. Planner: Brian Tucker, Planning Manager (Discussion,
       Hearing, Action)

   •   OAM2026-001638 - Consideration of an ordinance repealing and replacing Chapter
       19.84, Conditional Uses. Planner: Brian Tucker, Planning Manager (Discussion,
       Hearing, Action)


If members of the public wish to comment but cannot attend the meeting, comments may be
sent to Wendy Gurr (WGurr@msd.utah.gov). Prior to opening the public hearing, the Planning
Commission will hear a presentation from MSD Staff regarding the ordinance amendments.

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed public hearing items after taking comments
from the public during the public hearing. The public is also invited to review and inspect the
proposed change at https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html.

You may also submit written comments prior to the public hearing by contacting Wendy Gurr
at wgurr@msd.utah.gov or (385) 391-8268. Individuals needing special accommodation
(including ancillary communicative aids and services) during this public hearing should
contact Ms. Gurr three days before the public hearing.
