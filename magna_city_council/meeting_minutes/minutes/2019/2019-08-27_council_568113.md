       DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS AREA MAP

BE IT HEREBY ORDAINED by the Magna Township Council as the legislative body of the Magna
Metro Township as follows:

       1.    Amendment to Special Development Standards Areas Map regarding Parcel No.
14-33-328-013-0000.

The Special Development Standards Areas Map, which was adopted by Section 15.14.010 of the
Magna Code of Ordinance 2017 depicting the overpressure areas surrounding the Northrop
Grumman (formerly Hercules and ATK) Baccus Works, is modified and amended, by the
Amended Map, attached as Exhibit A, as to Parcel No. 14-33-328-013-0000, so that the 0.5 PSI
overpressure area shall only be the area within a 3470 foot radius of the following GPS coordinate,
and the 0.3 PSI overpressure area shall be the area of Parcel No. 14-33-328-013-0000 outside
the 0.5 PSI overpressure area but within a 5200 foot radius of the following GPS coordinate:

               Area 1 – N40d40’38.3” W112d04’20.0”

              The Amended Map, attached as Exhibit A, depicts the 0.5 PSI overpressure area
and the 0.3 PSI overpressure area, as amended, for Parcel No. 14-33-328-013-0000.

        2.       This ordinance will take effect 15 days after its passage and upon at least one
publication in a newspaper publishes in and having general circulation in Magna Metro Township
of Salt Lake County, and if not so published within 15 days then it will take effect immediately
upon its first publication.

Council Business

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

                 Council Member Peel stated survey results have been posted on WFWRD’s
website. Responses from the Magna community showed that the majority do not want recycling
services. An app is being developed to help residents determine what items can and cannot be
recycled. The board is looking at moving toward becoming a local district rather than a special
district. Each board member will be going back to their council to see if this is something that they
are in favor of. If all of the district members are in favor of the move to a local district, an interlocal
agreement will need to be put in place. After the interlocal agreements are in place, the board will
try and push legislation through.

RESOLUTION NO. 19-08-02                                                     Date: August 27, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN MAGNA AND
       SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR THE PROVISION OF PERSONNEL FOR THE
       MAGNA “COMMUNITY THAT CARES” PROGRAM

                                            RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
      401 et seq.

                                                 5
DATE          THURSDAY                                 AUGUST                           27, 2019


   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to Utah Code Title 17 – “Counties.”

   D. The County, as a part of its Human Services portfolio, provides certain Youth Service
      Programs for Youth Substance Abuse Prevention and Control.

   E. As a part of the Council meeting on July 9, 2019, the Council approved the grant
      agreement of a Substance Abuse Grant of $88,000 to commence the creation of the
      Magna “Community That Cares” Program (the “Program”).

   F. As the next step in the creation of the program, Magna desires to use the approved grant
      funding to contract with Salt Lake County Youth Services for the services of a person to
      manage the program.

   G. Pursuant to Magna’s desires, Salt Lake County has offered the attached Interlocal
      Cooperation Agreement to Magna for that purpose (“Exhibit A”).

                                         RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves, and authorizes the Mayor
to sign, the attached Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with Salt Lake County hereinafter known
as Exhibit A, subject to approval as to form, to procure the services of an employee of Salt Lake
County Youth Services to manage the Magna “Community That Cares” Program.
