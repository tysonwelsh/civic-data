                                   TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                   ORDINANCE NO. 24-10

     AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING TEXT
   AMENDMENT TO TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE CHAPTER 13.38 SSD-X BENNION
                           POINT ZONING DISTRICT.
      WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council (the “Council”) met in regular session on
October 2, 2024, to consider, among other things, approving a text amendment to Taylorsville
Municipal Code Chapter 13.38 SSD-X Bennion Point Zoning District; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13.35.040, notice of a
public hearing before the City’s Planning Commission was posted on Taylorsville City’s website
on August 29, 2024; and

        WHEREAS, the City’s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on September 10, 2024, during which the Planning Commission voted
6-1 to recommend that the Council approve the attached text amendment; and

        WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on October
2, 2024; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed text amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE CHAPTER 13.38, as articulated in
Exhibit “A,” attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference, are hereby approved,

       This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 24-10, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited, and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.
