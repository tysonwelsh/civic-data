MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 19-08-01 DATE: August 13, 2019
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
APPROVING A FIVE-YEAR SERVICE CONTRACT BETWEEN MAGNA AND
ONSOLVE, FOR USE OF THE CODE RED ALERT SYSTEM.
| RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401
et seq.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

C. Onsolve, LLC (the “Company”) is a provider of an emergency alert and information
dissemination system known as CodeRED.

D. One of the key components required in the development of the Magna Emergency Operations
Plan is the establishment of a community emergency alert and information dissemination
system.

E. Based on referrals from some local communities and the Emigration Metro Township, Magna
contacted representatives of Onsolve LLC to inquire about contracting to use Onsolve’s
CodeRED alert system.

F. Based on discussions with Company representatives, and Onsolve’s presentation to the
Council on May 28, 2019, the Council directed staff to pursue a five-year contract with the
Company for CodeRED.

G. The Company responded with the attached contract for services (Attachment “A”),

H. Use of CodeRED will allow Magna to effectively communicate important information to its
residents during emergencies and other important non-emergency incidents.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the 5-year service contact
between Magna and Onsolve LLC, hereinafter known in Attachment A with exhibits, and
authorizes the Mayor to sign the contract to affect the same.
