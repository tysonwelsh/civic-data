The purpose of the work session was to review the agenda for the City Council meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.;
review the Ogden Nature Center Annual Report; review the Ogden Downtown Alliance Annual report; discuss the Fiscal Year
2021 Audit and Annual Comprehensive Financial Report; receive an update regarding the Marshall White Center; discuss
proposed amendments to the Own in Ogden Program guidelines; discuss Council business, including proposed amendments to
City Code pertaining to Council member qualifications and Council meeting schedule; and hear Council Comments.

Agenda Review
Chair Nadolski and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting

scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m. There was a focus on the agenda items related to a proposed mixed-use project proposed for the
corner of 25" Street and Porter Avenue.

Ogden Nature Center Annual Report

Ogden Nature Center Executive Director Mary McKinley used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize the
annual report for the Ogden Nature Center. The presentation touched on topics such as 2021 accomplishments; operating income
and expenses for 2021; volunteer support at the Center; land and facilities improvements; an update on the secondary water
system as the Center; habitat restoration; and leadership transition at the Center. Ms. McKinley reported she will be retiring as the
Center’s Executive Director after 25 years with the organization; she thanked the Council and Administration for their partnership
and support of the Center during that time and reported a formal announcement on her replacement will be made in March of this
year.

The Council engaged in discussion with Ms. McKinley regarding events and programming at the Center and several
Council members expressed their gratitude to Ms. McKinley for all she does for the community.

Ogden Downtown Alliance Annual Report

Ogden Downtown Alliance (ODA) Executive Director Kim Bowsher used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to
summarize the annual report for the ODA. The presentation touched on topics such as ODA’s economic impact; strategic planning
efforts for years 2016-2021; strategic planning goals for 2022-2026; events scheduled for 2022; and the sponsors that provide vital
support to the ODA.

Several Council members expressed their appreciation to Ms. Bowsher and the ODA for their efforts to improve Ogden’s
downtown. Ms. Bowsher stated she appreciates the partnership of the Council and Administration of Ogden City.

Fiscal Year 2021 Audit

Representatives of HBME, the City’s auditing firm, presented the Fiscal Year (FY) 2021 Audit Report. They
emphasized they have issued a clean opinion regarding the City’s financial situation and government accounting practices, which
is the best opinion available to cities.

Fiscal Year 2021 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report

Senior Accountant Jensen and Comptroller Stout reviewed the fiscal year (FY) 2021 Annual Comprehensive Financial
Report (ACFR). They briefly touched on contributions to the Airport Fund; contributions to the Golf Fund; interfund loans;
General Fund balance, and Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Fund balance. They also acknowledged findings of the FY2021 audit
and summarized the corrective actions that have been taken to respond to those findings. They then discussed highlights of the
report, including the capital asset investment; fleet and facilities fund balance improvements and investments; and reduction of
long-term debt. Finally, they concluded by discussing the Dinosaur Park and Golf Course funds, both of which have a cash
deficit.

Mayor Caldwell commented on the efforts of Ms. Stout and her staff to improve the City’s financial position.

Marshall White Center Update

Shawn Bishop, Chair of the Marshall White Center (MWC) Advisory Committee reported on upcoming open house and
canvassing events scheduled to solicit input from the public regarding their desires for the MWC. He added that the Advisory
Committee’s meetings are public meetings, and all are invited to attend.

Recreation Manager Bridge also discussed planned open house events and indicated that the feedback provided at the
open houses will be used to inform the recommendations that will be formalized by VCBO architects.

Chair Nadolski thanked Mr. Bishop and Mr. Bridge, as well as Mayor Caldwell, for regular updates regarding the MWC;
he looks forward to a full report of all open houses and indicated he feels great progress is being made.

Proposed Amendments to the Own in Ogden Program Guidelines

Community Development Manager Ogden reported Administration is proposing to amend the Own in Ogden program
guidelines to increase the amount available for zero interest, deferred payment loans. The proposal increases the amount available
for persons purchasing their primary residence in Ogden from $5,000 to $10,000, income-qualified Ogden City employees and
new hires from $10,000 to $15,000, and an increase from $10,000 to $15,000 for qualifying full-time, state-certified K-12
classroom teachers and administrators. These changes are the result of increased housing costs in Ogden.

The Council engaged in discussion with Mr. Ogden regarding the criteria that will be used to evaluate Own in Ogden
loan applications; the benefit to Ogden City employees; and the impact the program adjustments will have on the average
taxpayer.

Council Business

Proposed Amendments — Council member qualifications and Council meetings schedule: Council Executive Director
Eller-Smith reviewed proposed amendments to Title 2 of the Ogden Municipal Code to allow a Council member to retain their
municipal district seat if a change in the boundary lines of the municipal district results in the Council member residing outside the
new district boundaries. She also referenced the Code change regarding the reduction of the number of regular Council meetings
from three meetings per month to two meetings per month. Redevelopment Agency meetings are not affected.

The Council thanked Council staff for their proactiveness in addressing these issues.

Legislative Update: Council member White and Management Services Director Brown discussed legislative bills moving
through the current Legislative session that may impact Ogden City or residents of the community.

The meeting adjourned at 5:50 p.m.
