 1            Pressurized Irrigation (Secondary Water) Fee and Connection Discussion
 2
 3   Mayor Pro Tem McCay asked Kevin Hicks, Administrative Services Director, to start the
 4   discussion on the pressurized irrigation fee and connection.
 5
 6   Mr. Hicks explained that there had been some misunderstanding about the pressurized
 7   irrigation fee. If residents have access to canal shares and are current, the City will give them a
 8   reduced rate regardless of lot size.
 9
10   Mr. Hicks said that the City was made aware of this issue when a resident on Lovers Lane
11   wanted to connect to the secondary water system. Staff looked at this resident’s situation and
12   determined what it would take to allow them to connect and also examined the cost for this
13   connection. After determining these things, staff looked to the fee schedule to determine what
14   the monthly rate would be and that’s when they realized there was a problem with the fee
15   schedule. The fee schedule provides a fee for properties that are 0.75 to 1.0 acres but does not
16   specify the fee for properties over 1 acre.
17
18   Mr. Hicks said there were 112 properties that fit into the category of lots larger than 1-acre. He
19   said that 40 of these properties would not see a fee increase due to various assessments and
20   72 properties could see a monthly fee increase if the fee schedule was adjusted. Mr. Hicks
21   asked the Council to decide what they would like to do with properties that are greater than one
22   acre so that they can add an additional line to the fee schedule. Staff has recommended that
23   residents pay a “per acre” fee by multiplying $40.87 by the number of acres they own. Mr. Hicks
24   explained the increase in fees for each property if this recommendation was followed.
25
26   Mr. Brickey said he was hoping to provide transparency to the community and he wants to be
27   fair and transparent through the process. He said this fee adjustment could be delayed until
28   December but he doesn’t think this is neighborly.
29
30   Councilmember Wells said that many of his neighbors were not notified and believed their rate
31   would go up double. He would like notices that give more answers than questions and asked if
32   there is a legal reason that a small amount of information is included on the notice.
33
34   Mr. Hicks said it depends on the type of notice and he thought this notice provided enough
35   information. Another issue is residents saying they did not get the notice. He knows that
36   notices were taken to the post office but whether they are delivered is out of his control.
37
38   Mr. Brickey said that notice was not required. However, the Mayor said to send notice to any
39   residents who owned property over one acre.
40
41   Councilmember Wells asked additional clarifying questions of staff.
42
43   Councilmember Buroker said there were two separate items, the connection fee and the usage
44   fee. Mr. Hicks said that was correct and if the Council agreed with his interpretation of the
45   connection fees, then that is already in the fee schedule. However, he didn’t know if there was
46   a prior agreement that the City committed to connect this resident.
47
48   Councilmember Buroker asked if residents that haven’t connected are included in the numbers
49   presented by Mr. Hicks. Mr. Hicks responded that the numbers he presented are residents that
50   are already connected to the system.
