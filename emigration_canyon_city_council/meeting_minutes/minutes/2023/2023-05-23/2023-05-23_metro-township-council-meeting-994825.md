Community Development Block Grant Program Participation
Cameron Platt stated the Community Development Block Program (CDBG) is run
by Salt Lake County. If the Council opts in, it will be part of the program for three years. Emigration
Canyon Metro Township is not qualified to get funding on its own but if Salt Lake County identifies
a project that includes Emigration Canyon, then it could get funding.
Council Member Smolka, seconded by Council Member Pinon, moved to opt into
an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement to participate in the Salt Lake County Urban County
Community Development Block Grant Program, Home Investment Partnership Program, and
Emergency Solutions Grant Program. The motion passed unanimously.

H.B. 374 County Sheriff Amendments
Mayor Smolka stated a meeting with the Mayors and the Salt Lake Valley Law
Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA) will be held on June 2, 2023.
Cameron Platt stated there have been several meetings with Salt Lake County
and members of SLVLESA to discuss what the metro townships are going to do about law
enforcement services. It appears that SLVLESA is planning on continuing to exist. SLVLESA can
contract with the Salt Lake County Sheriff’s Office for base services, and it can also contract with
other law enforcement agencies as necessary. The metro township’s options are to continue with

It may be a good idea to go to the legislature and present the idea of the metro townships
becoming towns. That would allow different funding and the metro townships could contract
directly with the Salt Lake County Sheriff’s Office or SLVLESA. The process to convert from a
