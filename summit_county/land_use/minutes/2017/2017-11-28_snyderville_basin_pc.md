2. Public Hearing regarding a proposed Rezone and Conditional use Permit for  
property located at 24000 West Rasmussen Road; Parcel PP-53; Peter Corroon, 
applicant – Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner     
 
Planner Strader said the purpose of this meeting is to get public comment and then 
receive clear direction from the Commission for the applicant.  The property is located 
on Rasmussen Road and is west of the RV Park.  It contains 22.72 acres and is zoned 
Rural Residential and Hillside Stewardship.  The proposed Community Commercial 
zone will enable the applicant to develop a mixed-use affordable housing project.

Planner Strader said the application was reviewed by the Planning Commission in work 
sessions.  These were held in May and August of this year.  It is proposed to have a total 
of 102 residential units dispersed among six apartment buildings.  An 82,000 square-
foot retail building is also proposed.   
 
Planner Strader said the Code requires 20% of any development to be affordable.  
Based on 62 market-rate units being provided in conjunction with a commercial 
component, a minimum of 13.57 affordable units are required.  The applicants are 
proposing a total of 40 affordable units.  They are targeting the 60%-80% AMI level.  
Mr. Corroon has said the AMI level may be lower depending on the final number of 
units.  Each unit is designed to meet the minimum requirements for affordable housing.        
 
Planner Strader said the previous application included 24 temporary cabins located at 
the west end of the property.  Those structures have been removed from the site plan 
due to a potential traffic roundabout on the future I-80 interchange.  If the roundabout 
is built, it could impact a portion of the applicant’s property.  Removing the cabins will 
allow the applicant to proceed with the project.  It leaves the potential right of way 
available in the future.    
 
The apartments and the retail building were pointed out on the site plan.  Detail 
drawings were provided by the applicant.  These verify they are compliant with the 
setbacks, height, and other requirements.  A bus stop is being proposed on Rasmussen 
Road.  The details concerning the bus stop will have to be worked out.  Discussions are 
taking place about the electric bike-share program and amenities for regular bikes. 
 
Planner Strader said that Basin Rec is requesting the applicant provide a trail easement 
on the north side of the creek.  The applicant is willing to work with Basin Rec on the 
details.   The applicant has agreed to meet other service providers’ requirements.  Staff 
is comfortable that the development will fit on the site.

Findings of Fact: 
1. The Silver Creek Properties is located at 6450 Silver Creek Drive in the 
community Commercial (CC) zone. 
2. There is an existing retail sales building with outdoor storage on site. 
3. The building and use were originally approved and built as a permitted use 
in November 2001. 
4. In 2004, the County Commission approved Chapter 10-2-7 of the Snyderville 
Basin Development Code.  This states that outdoor storage yards may be 
appropriate in connection with a primary use on a property, provided said 
yard is reviewed by the Planning Commission as a Conditional Use.   
5. Because the use has never received Conditional Use approval from the 
Planning Commission, it is a legal non-conforming use in the CC zone. 
6. The purpose of the application is to bring the existing outdoor storage use 
into compliance with the Snyderville Basin Development Code and 
Community Commercial Zone requirements.   
7. The Silver Creek Properties building is located in an area with commercial 
uses such as storage units, manufacturing, and retail sales. 
8. The use has operated in its current location for approximately 12 years, with 
little documented impact on the neighboring uses. 
9. No official complaints relating to noise or other disturbances have been filed 
with either the Sherriff’s Department or the Community Development Code 
Enforcement division. 
10. No additions or expansions of the use are proposed as part of this proposal. 
11. All necessary public facilities are available on site, including water, septic, 
and roads. 
12. The use contributes to the General Plan encouraging a diversity of 
commercial uses in appropriate locations in the Snyderville Basin.
