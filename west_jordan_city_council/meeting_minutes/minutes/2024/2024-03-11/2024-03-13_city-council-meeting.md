 4. PUBLIC COMMENT
     Chair Jacob opened the public comment period at 6:18 pm.
     Comments:
     Rick Sterner, resident of West Jordan in District 2, expressed a complaint about the
     commercialization of a residential property in his neighborhood. He said he sent photographs
     to the City last July of debris placed on a residential property. Mr. Sterner said some of the
     material had been removed with the help of the Mayor, but he was told by the City that
     remaining material would probably be permitted to stay. Mr. Sterner stated the material
     belonged in a land fill, said his property value would be affected, and asked why the City was
     not enforcing Code in that situation.

     Laura Shin, resident of West Jordan in Copper Field Phase One, acknowledged new Council
     Members and invited the Council to walk through her neighborhood with her on Saturday, April
     13th at 2:30 pm. She expressed a desire to share thoughts and concerns with the Council
     regarding planned expansion of 9000 South.

     Amy Motta, Salt Lake City resident and volunteer with Bailing out Benji, spoke of the Animal
     Welfare Act (AWA) and puppy mills. Ms. Motta expressed the opinion that the Federal law was
     flawed, and encouraged the Council to consider a humane pet store ordinance.

     Jerry Leder, West Jordan resident, said he represented Sugar Creek Condos. He said the
     development had problems with trees along the Bingham Creek Fence line. Mr. Leder said staff
     had indicated the trees would be taken care of in May. He emphasized that the trees were a
     problem, knocking down fences and growing under the fences. Mr. Leder said the neighborhood
     also had a large problem in the last few years with someone vandalizing and knocking down
     fences. He spoke of the cost to the HOA every time a fence was knocked down. Mr. Leder said
     he had reported the problem to the police and was waiting for a call back. Mr. Leder asked for
     help from the City in communicating with a neighbor regarding trees on a neighboring property
     that needed to be trimmed to prevent additional fence damage.

     Trevor Roseberry, West Jordan resident, said he had noticed grounds were being taken care of
     better since implementation of a highland fee, and thanked the Mayor. Mr. Roseberry said he
     did not love the design of Daybreak, and was not a fan of high density. He said he understood
     the need for affordable housing, but did not want a design like Daybreak or high-density
     housing in his area. Mr. Roseberry suggested the City entice restaurants closer to his area.

 5. REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. City Council Reports
          Council Member Green reported on recent events at schools in West Jordan, and
          mentioned that a portion of Jordan River Trail was closed due to flooding.

           Council Member Whitelock commented that the Special Education teacher at Copper Hills
           High School was amazing. She said she always enjoyed attending the We the People
           program, and said she appreciated teachers inviting the Council to be present at school
           events.

           Council Member Shelton said he met with representatives from the West Jordan Police
           Department Fraternal Order of Police, and shared that each member of the Council was
           invited on a ride-along. He expressed support for the Police Department.

           Chair Jacob spoke fondly of the Special Education Department and the Pals Program at
           Copper Hills High School. He shared his daughter’s experience participating in the class
           and subsequent career in the same field during and after college. He believed the program
           benefited both the community and future generations.

       b. Council Office Report
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson referenced Council Office Reports provided in
          meeting packets and requested feedback. Mr. Anderson spoke of items expected to appear
          on future meeting agendas the upcoming Utah League of Cities and Towns (ULCT)
          conference in St. George. He reported on finishing touches in Council Chambers at City
          Hall.

       c. Mayor’s Report
          Mayor Dirk Burton reported being appointed as a member of the Utah Uniform Building
          Code Commission and spoke of events in the community. He mentioned the Employee
          Health Center grand opening, and that staff were exploring alternative options for green
          waste collection.

       d. City Administrator’s Report
          None
