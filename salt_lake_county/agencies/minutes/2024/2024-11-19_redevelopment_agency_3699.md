4.1 2025 Proposed Redevelopment Agency Budget Presentation 24-2329
Attachments: Staff Report
2025 RDA Budget Presentation
Ms. Dina Blaes , Director, Office of Regional Development, delivered a 
PowerPoint presentation entitled 2025 Proposed Redevelopment Agency 
(RDA) Budget. She reviewed the budget summary; budget detail; FTE 
requests; and fee increases. 
Council Member Stringham  stated Magna City has asked for the RDA to 
be transferred to it by the end of 2024. She asked what the process was and 
what was being discussed.
Ms. Blaes stated she received a letter from Adam Long, Attorney, Smith 
Hartvigsen PLLC, requesting the RDA to be transferred in 90 days. Based 
on past experiences with transferring RDA ’s to other jurisdictions, it takes
