3.1 Public hearing and recommendation of Ken Garff Automotive’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Chapter 23, Signs, to allow larger sign area and height on
pylon signs for multi-tenant projects.
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    The applicant, Mr. Godfrey, was present. He stated there were two reasons they were asking
    for the amendment. The first reason was for visibility for their sign and facility. The second
    reason is current code allows for a similar sign on all three of their parcels to reduce sign
    clutter by putting it on one sign.

    Commissioner Hemmert asked if there was a way to look at an exception for freeway
    frontage where the location for the sign is sunk down from the freeway. Kim Struthers stated
    there is some flexibility built into the code with certain language where additional height is
    allowed near a freeway overpass. Commissioner Hemmert stated he typically does not like to
    amend code unless it is a site specific issue.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged comments.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Hemmert reiterated he was not in favor of a code amendment but suggested
    the applicant submit an application pursuant to 4B. Commissioner Nielsen agreed with
    Commissioner Hemmert and does not want to change the code where there is language that
    allows other avenues. Kim Struthers clarified the code. Commissioner Peterson agreed with
    eliminating the sign clutter, as does Commissioner Ellis.

Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a negative recommendation of Ken Garff
Automotive’s request for review of a Development Code Amendment to Chapter 23, Signs,
to allow larger sign area and height on pylon signs for multi-tenant projects, based in the
findings in the report that the amendments are not necessary to accomplish what the applicant is
requesting. Commissioner Peterson seconded. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Adam Albrecht’s request for review of the
Yeomans & Morgan General Plan Amendment, changing the land use designation from
LDR (low density residential) & MDR (medium density residential) to IHDR (intermediate
high density residential) on approximately 5 acres of property located at 143 South 1100
West.
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.


    Commissioner Everett asked if Staff had given general direction with the draft general plan
    map in consideration of the 5 to 7 acres that is being proposed tonight.

    The applicant, Adam Albrecht, was present. He stated he has reviewed the infill PRD and
    feels he has met the requirements. Kim Struthers stated the PUD is required and the only way
    it can be developed is by staying within the existing designation. Mr. Albrecht stated he has a
    concept plan that is available.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged comments.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen asked if infrastructure was in place to support the development. Ross
    Dinsdale stated the City would have a better idea with an updated general plan and it was
    being looked at right now. Commissioner Nielsen stated the development is near commercial
    and a major roadway with walkability, as well as being a transitional community.
    Commissioner Everett stated there is property to the East that is agricultural and would be an
    abrupt change, especially with a transportation corridor and he didn't feel that the
    development would sway UTA’s decision. Commissioner Peterson expressed concern for the
    kids that can’t walk on Main Street or get on the bus to ride to school, as well as trying to get
    to Vet’s Park and the he doesn’t feel that it is an infill project. He feels that public comment
    should be taken into consideration and that he is opposed to the change. Commissioner
    Hereth stated he would not be in support of this item with the revised general plan update.
    Commissioner Bunker asked about a time frame with the general plan and when it would be
    done. He likes the idea of mixed development neighborhoods. Kim Struthers stated it would
    be within the next few months. Ross Dinsdale stated a traffic analysis was in process and
    would give a better perspective on what to expect.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to forward a negative recommendation of Adam
Albrecht’s request for review of the Yeomans & Morgan General Plan Amendment,
changing the land use designation from LDR (low density residential) & MDR (medium
density residential) to IHDR (intermediate high density residential) on approximately 5
acres of property located at 143 South 1100 West, finding that it is not consistent with the
neighborhood, or meets the infill requirement needed for the PUD as well as the negative
comments from the neighborhood. Commissioner Everett seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, no; Commissioner Bunker, no; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The vote passed 4-3 with a negative recommendation.

3.4 Public hearing and recommendation of Fieldstone Homes’ request for preliminary
subdivision review of Hidden Canyon Plat D, a 99-lot single-family development located
approximately north of Fox Canyon Road.
    Sierra presented information on the agenda item. There was a prior preliminary plan that was
    approved but there have been significant changes, resulting in a new revision.

    Commissioner Everett stated he thought there were 100 lots as opposed to the 99 lots that
    were presented. Commissioner Everett noted a comment was made about the trail
    intersecting in front of lots. Kim Struthers clarified that there were 120 total units, so there
    would be 100 total units.

    The applicant, Matthew Loveland, was present and gave a short presentation on the item.
    Commissioner Nielsen asked if the trail easement was in the right of way. Ross Dinsdale
    stated that he would look and see where the right of way started so it didn't create issues or
    impede traffic. Grading will take place so material won't be left undone or unkept. Kim
    Struthers stated the max block length may be approved for a longer block length with
    exception from DRC and Council.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged comments.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen was concerned about the length of driveway effecting the trail system.
    Kim Struthers suggested recessing the garages back as far as possible to make the driveways
    longer. Kim stated there were several reasons to have a block length standard.


Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to recommend approval of Fieldstone Homes’ request for
preliminary subdivision review of Hidden Canyon Plat D, a 99-lot single-family
development located approximately north of Fox Canyon Road; including all DRC
comments and findings stated in the report adding that when the final layouts of the driveways

Commissioner Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.10 Consideration of Holiday Oil’s request for approval of the Holiday Oil Site Plan
located at 1225 West Sandalwood Drive in a commercial zone.
    Commissioner Everett is employed by the State Agency that regulates underground storage
    tanks, but does not regulate the sighting of these facilities that is on this specific agenda item.

    Sierra presented information on the agenda item. The proposed site shows a fifty foot access
    width, which exceeds the regular access width code. Our code does allow wider width if it is
    justified, and our Engineering department has reviewed the request but does not feel it is
    justified. The DRC had comments on the carwash and recommended adding windows or
    other features to break up the building’s architecture.

    The applicant, John Linton, was present. The applicant addressed the driveway width and
    needing the width for safety and convenience stating the thirty six foot access isn’t enough
    width to allow that with trailers and fuel trucks coming in and out. He stated Ryan Hales
    competed a traffic study and he recommended a wider access. Ross stated he would have the
    City's Traffic Engineer look at the width of the drive again.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to approve Holiday Oil’s request for approval of the
Holiday Oil Site Plan located at 1225 West Sandalwood Drive in a commercial zone;
including all DRC comments, specifically the comment that the applicant work with staff
regarding the aesthetics of the carwash wall, the width of the access drive aisle, and all other
findings stated in the report. Commissioner Hemmert seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
