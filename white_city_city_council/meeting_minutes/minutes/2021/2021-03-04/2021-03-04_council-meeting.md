Reports from other Municipal Providers

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

               Captain Kenneth Aldridge stated community events will resume with all COVID
safety protocols in place. The Chief is reviewing the budget and will present it to the finance
committee in April. Recruit camp is in week 4. Bills at the legislature for first responder mental
health are being monitored.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Chief Randy Thomas reviewed the calls for service for February. There was a
good community contact made that led to a long-term investigation on drug activity. The SafeCam
system has been successful and provided video of an arson. Sandy City Police picked up a
robbery suspect that lives in White City on Sego Lily.

Council Business

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution considering the appointment of
Christopher Spagnuolo and Christy Seiger-Webster as Planning Commission members for a
three-year term ending February 28, 2024:

RESOLUTION NO. 21-03-01                                                      Date: March 4, 2021

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO COUNCIL APPROVING THE
       APPOINTMENT OF CHRISTOPHER SPAGNUOLO AND CHRISTY SEIGER-
       WEBSTER AS PLANNING COMMISSION MEMBERS FOR A THREE-YEAR
       ENDING FEBRUARY 28, 2024

                                           RECITALS


                                                2
DATE            THURSDAY                       MARCH                                   4, 2021


   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. Pursuant to Ordinance 17-06-02, Planning Commission Members are appointed by the
      Metro Township Council to serve a three-year terms;

   C. On this date, the White City Metro Council desires to appoint Christopher Spagnuolo and
      Christy Seiger-Webster as Planning Commission members with a term ending February
      28, 2024.

                                        RESOLUTION

      THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the Council
approves the appointment of Christopher Spagnuolo and Christy Seiger-Webster as Planning
Commission Members for White City with the term ending February 28, 2024.

         APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this 4th day of March 2021.

                                                           By: /s/ KAY DICKERSON
                                                               Mayor Pro-Tempore
ATTEST

By: /s/ SHERRIE SWENSEN
    Salt Lake County Clerk
    Metro Township Clerk/Recorder

                Council Member Little motioned to table the appointment of Christopher Spagnuolo
and Christy Seiger-Webster to the White City Planning Commission to allow more time to notify
the public of the openings. The motion failed due to a lack of a second.

              Council Member Perry, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to approve the
Resolution. The motion passed 3 to 1, showing Council Member Little voted in opposition.
