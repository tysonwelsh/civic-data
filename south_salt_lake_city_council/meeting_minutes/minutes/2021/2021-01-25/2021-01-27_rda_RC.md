meeting at an anchor location presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who

United States, The Governor of Utah, the County Health Department and Mayor, and the

      1. Bills, Claims, and Communications. None.

      2. Report of the Executive Director. Mayor Wood reported that staff is continuing to
      work with the other taxing entities on tax increment participation for the South City
      Project. Reception has been positive. The goal is to obtain their commitment to
      participate no later than April of this year. She is very appreciative of the other taxing
      partners being willing to meet and discuss the importance of the project.

      The environmental cleanup of the Belo Terra property has been completed. Staff is
      working with the State to finalize the issuance of the documents required to close on the
      property. The goal was to close at the end of January but that has been extended. Boyer,
      the buyer of the property, has meet with the City to discuss options for the development
      of the property.

      The plan for the western portion of the 3300 South Master Plan from I-15 west to 1000
      West has been completed. Staff will be setting up meetings with the Board members to
      review the results of the plan in the near future.

      Tonight they will be participating in an update of the Economic Development Strategic
      Plan. Staff will be updating them on the goals and objectives they have completed. While
      they’ve had some success, they still have significant opportunity to continue to move the
      city forward.

      Mayor Wood also requested the RDA staff prepare an incentive policy to assist and guide
      them with the use of incentives. It’s important when they offer their public dollars that
      they have considered the benefit they will receive and that they have adequate dollars for

      New Business

          1. Discussion regarding goals and objectives for economic development
             strategic plan. Economic Development Consultant, Randy Sant, reviewed the
             Economic Development Strategic Plan accomplishments with the Board. A copy
             is attached to these minutes and incorporated by this reference. These were
             prioritized by the Board back in 2018.

              A developer come that has shown interest in looking at the Central Valley sewer
              property to determine if the they could do a higher end development that would
              produce a lot more revenue than is currently receive. It would also be a significant
              development that would benefit the community. They have been working with the
              District for about seven months now and an MOU has been signed that will allow
              them to start pursuing and looking into opportunities to bring in better
              development for the City that will increase the revenue for the sewer district.

              The Board discussed the goals and priorities that they would like to see the
              Economic Development Strategic Plan focus on over the next five years.

              Director Pinkney would like to see a focus on night life. She doesn’t feel there is
              as much as there could be.

              Director Siwik would like to focus on a “buy local” campaign to create more
              support for the businesses in the community. He also suggested bringing more
              development to the 2700 South area.

              Director Bynum agreed that they need more night life. She also asked if
              something could be done about the abandoned, vacant buildings in the city.

              Director Mila agreed with adding night life and doing something about the vacant
              buildings.

              Director deWolfe would like to focus on State Street. Not just getting good
              businesses there but also with beautification. He would like it to be attractive and
              visually appealing. That applies to all the project areas. He would also like to
              continue to focus on transportation, making it easy to get around in the city.

              Director Huff likes the focus on the downtown area, making it walkable and easy
              to get around. She would also like to see the 3300 South area developed.

              Director Siwik suggested creating some economic development projects along
              500 West as well as looking long-term at some development on 900 West from
              State Route 201 down to 2400 South.
