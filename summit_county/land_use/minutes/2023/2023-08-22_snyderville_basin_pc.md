3)
Project # 22-287: Public hearing and action for a Conditional Use permit for a Major Accessory Building at 449 Wasatch Way, Summit County, Utah; Parcel SL-E-313 (2.53 acres). Applicant: Charles Ochello.
Planner: Ray Milliner.
(6:21 P.M.)
Ray Milliner,
Planner
, presented information regarding a Conditional Use permit for a Major Accessory Building.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions prior to the Public Hearing. Charles Ochello, Applicant, responded to a question regarding gravel surrounding the building.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:32 P.M.
Laura Hoffman was called to speak.
Jed Palmacci was called to speak.
Allison Wyler was called to speak.
Frank Butler was called to speak.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:47 P.M.
Commissioners addressed the questions and comments introduced during the public hearing. Planner Milliner provided information and responded to the questions and concerns raised.
Commissioners asked additional questions to which the applicant, Charles Ochello, responded.
John Kucera made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit for the Major Accessory Building according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in the staff report with the additional condition of some form of landscaping buffer to be staff approved on the west side of the dwelling. Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Christopher Conabee.

Project #23-071: Address: 4600 N Silver Springs Dr., Silver Springs, Summit County, UT; Parcel PP-116-B-X (10.49 acres). Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) for a ~9,800 sq. ft. addition to the Parley's Park Elementary School; Applicant: Todd Hansen, on behalf of Park City School District.
Senior Planner: Amir Caus, AICP
(7:29 P.M.)
Amir Caus, AICP,
Principal Planner
, presented a Conditional Use Permit for an addition to the Parley's Park Elementary School.
Commissioners asked brief clarifying questions.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for Public Hearing at 7:38 P.M.
Bob West was called to speak.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for Public Hearing 7:41 P.M.
Commissioners asked additional clarifying questions to which Planner Caus and Applicant, John Simmons with Moca Systems responded.
John Kucera made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit for Parley's Park Elementary School expansion according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval as found in the staff report. Thomas Cooke seconded, and the motion carried, (5-1).
Tyann Mooney voted AYE
