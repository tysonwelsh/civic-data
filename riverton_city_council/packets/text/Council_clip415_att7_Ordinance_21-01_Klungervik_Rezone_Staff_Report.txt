Background

This application is for rezone of property located at 2998 West 12600 S. The property is an
approved commercial development currently zoned C-PO. A single building has been built, with
several open pads remaining. The project is approximately 1.4 acres.The property to the north
and east is zoned RR-22, although a portion of the property to the east is the Riverton High
School site. The property to the west is zoned C-PO.

The development was designed and approved with five pads, intended for medical office use.
Following the construction of the existing building, the owner has not had tenant interest
sufficient to fill the other pad spaces. He has approached the City with potential tenants, but
many have been more retail in nature than the Professional Office zone allows. In response,
owner has submitted this application requesting a zone change from Professional Office to
Commercial Gateway to expand the range of uses available to the project.

Commercial Gateway is an applicable zone in this area. The existing commercial project just to
the east of this property is zoned Commercial Gateway, and the commercial zoning on the
south side of 12600 South in this area is a mix of Professional Office and Commercial Gateway.
Should the rezone be approved, uses within this project would still be limited by available
property and parking. The Commercial Gateway zone has identical setback requirements to the
Professional Office zone relative the adjacent residential properties.

       C.     REZONE, GORM KLUNGERCIK IS REQUESTING A REZONE OF
              PROPERTY LOCATED AT 2998 WEST 12600 SOUTH, THE CURRENT
              ZONE IS PROFESSIONAL OFFICE (C-PO) AND THE REQUESTED ZONE
              IS GATEWAY (C-G).

Chair Cannon opened the public hearing.

Mr. Prestwich reported that this is a rezone of an existing approved office development west
of the High School. The property is located at 2998 West 12600 South. The property location
was identified on a map displayed. Mr. Prestwich reported that what has been built so far is
the parking and one pad. Five pad sites are available. The applicant built the first building
but struggled to fill space. It was planned as a medical office complex.

The applicant approached the City with ideas for different tenants. Mr. Prestwich stated that
the problem was that the Commercial Professional Office Zone is more limited in terms of
uses that are allowed. As the applicant was exploring potential tenants for the site they
determined that it is was in their best interest to apply for a rezone to Commercial Gateway.

Mr. Prestwich identified the Commercial Gateway on the map and stated that it is not out of
character with the area. The General Plan designation is Business Park, which makes both
the Professional Office zone and the Gateway Zone viable uses within it. Commercial
Gateway uses allow for a more of a retail mix than the Professional Office. Mr. Prestwich
stated that while the available uses would change with the zoning, the site would still be
limited by available parking and the configuration of the site. Staff recommended approval.

Commissioner Rushton asked if the changes would require the applicant to apply for another
site plan in case the result would differ from the existing site plan. Mr. Prestwich responded
in the affirmative and explained that there will be no significant changes to the site plan. The
changes would be mainly to the uses. Commissioner Rushton observed that there is
residential to the north of the property and asked about the height restrictions. Mr. Prestwich
stated that the height restriction is the same between the two zones and includes a limitation
of 20 feet. There is an additional one foot for one every foot in building height separation.
The impact on the neighbors will not change because of similar height limitations in both
zones.

Commissioner Rushton asked if there is a mechanism for an Interconnect Agreement.
Mr. Prestwich stated that the original site plan included a floating easement and there is a
cross-connection easement. The applicant, Gorm Klungervik confirmed that there is an
Riverton City Planning Commission Meeting                                                  5

easement that connects to the western portion of the property. It was an existing easement
that was required by the Utah Department of Transportation (“UDOT”).

Chair Cannon asked the applicant if he has a good relationship with his neighbor to the east.
Mr. Klungervik reported that he met the neighbor once and had spoken to him. He explained
that they will not be applying for anything extreme on the property, however, there has been
an increase in requests for daycare centers, restaurants, and gym type facilities.

Commissioner Lefgren asked if the parking would change with the zone change.
Mr. Prestwich stated that the parking ordinance is based on use so it will be the same.
Commissioner Lefgren expressed concern with parking if the use is something like a
restaurant. Mr. Prestwich stated that he made it clear to the applicant that there will be
constraints beyond just the zoning itself with the number of parking stalls. The applicant
confirmed that he was aware of that.

There were no public comments. The public hearing was closed.

Commissioner James recommended APPROVAL of Application PLZ-20-4005, the
Klungervik Rezone, rezoning the subject properties located at 2998 West 12600 South
from C-PO (Commercial-Professional Office) to C-G (Commercial Gateway).
Commissioner Hansen seconded the motion. The motion passed with the unanimous
consent of the Commission.

zoning is Residential. Across 3600 West is Commercial Regional.
