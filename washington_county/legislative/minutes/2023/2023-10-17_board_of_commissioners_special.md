WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
OCTOBER 17, 2023

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Snow at 1:00 p.m. on October 17, 2023, in the Commission Chamber
Overflow , 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Chairman
Adam Snow, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County
Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, Members of the Western
Fire District board, Residents of the SR 18 Corridor and a Representative from Gold Cross
Ambulance service.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION ON EMERGENCY MEDIAL TRANSPORTATION SERVICES BY GOLD
CROSS AMBULANCE SERVICE.

Commissioner Snow started the meeting by updating those in attendance about the
previous meeting with Gold Cross Ambulance service.

There was a dialogue about budget and staffing concerns regarding Gold Cross Ambulance
services providing assistance in emergency medical calls and/or transportation.

Response times for calls requiring emergency medical services were also addressed, as this
affects overall budget numbers.

Another Work Meeting will be held to continue discussions on the matter.
There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at

02:49 p.m.

JWYANNE DEWIS : ADAM SNOW
DEPUTY CLERK CHAIRMAN
