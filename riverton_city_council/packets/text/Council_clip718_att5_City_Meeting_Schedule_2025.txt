                                                RIVERTON CITY
                                           2025 MEETING SCHEDULES

Pursuant to § 52-4-202(2) Utah Code Annotated, Riverton City hereby gives notice of the following meeting schedules
for the Year 2025:
      City Council Meetings are generally held on the first and third Tuesday of each month with Work Sessions
         beginning at 6:00 p.m. and City Council Meetings beginning at 7:00 p.m., at Riverton City Hall, located at
         12830 South Redwood Road, Riverton, Utah.
      Planning Commission Meetings are generally held on the second and fourth Thursdays of each month
         beginning at 6:30 p.m. at Riverton City Hall, located at 12830 South Redwood Road, Riverton, Utah.
      Riverton Fire Service Area (RFSA) Meetings are held in May, June and December on the first Tuesday of
         the month following City Council Meetings.
      Riverton Law Enforcement Service Area (RLESA) Meetings are held in May, June and December on the
         third Tuesday of the month following City Council Meetings.
      Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Meetings, Special and/or Emergency Meetings, in addition to those
         specified, may be held as circumstances require; best practicable notice will be given for such meetings.
      Board of Adjustment Hearings may be held as circumstances require; best practicable notice will be given
         for such meetings.
      Historic Preservation Commission Meetings are held monthly on the first Thursday of the month at 6:00
         p.m. at Riverton City Hall, located at 12830 South Redwood Road, Riverton, Utah.

Meetings may be canceled; however, best practicable notice will be given for canceled meetings. Meetings will not
be held on a State or Federal Holiday or on an Election Day. All meetings are open to the public unless closed, pursuant
to the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act. Accessible parking and entrance are located on the south end of Riverton
City Hall, with elevator access to the City Council Chambers which are located on the second floor. Please contact
the City Recorder to make special accommodations at (801) 208-3128.
