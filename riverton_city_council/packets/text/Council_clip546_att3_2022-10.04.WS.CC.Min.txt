33            Update Regarding Transportation Issues and Literary Opportunities
34
35   Mayor Staggs explained that he was appointed in February 2022 to be on the UTA Advisory
36   Board. The three trustees are empowered to handle the budget and the local advisory board
37   does not have much authority. In the south end of the valley, UTA has pulled out all bus routes
38   and implemented the Via app for transit. Salt Lake City and the Southwest cities of South
39   Jordan, Riverton, Herriman and Bluffdale have roughly the same population which equates to
40   each of these two areas representing 17% of the county population. However, in comparing
41   Salt Lake City to the Southwest cities, the bus stops are 32% compared to 2%, the rail stations
42   are 48% compared to 5%, the riders are 50% compared to 2%. Sales tax revenue for mass
43   transit totals $260 million for Salt Lake County, $75 million comes from Salt Lake City, $27
44   million comes from the Southwest cities. In the operational budget, the expenses are shown by
45   mode and it was determined that the costs in the southwest are only $6 million whereas, Salt
46   Lake City is $103 million in costs.
47
48   Mayor Staggs explained that he would like to encourage the Southwest neighbors to reach out
49   to the trustees while they are considering the budget this year about these discrepancies.
50

15            Ordinance No. 22-22 – The Master Development Agreement for Riverton Ranch
16            Estates, establishing requirements, standards, and parameters for development of
17            approximately 3.2 acres located at 13040 S Redwood, and including approval of an
18            eight (8) lot private lane subdivision on that property
19
20   Mr. Lethbridge explained this item was continued from the September 20, 2022 City Council
21   Meeting. Riverton City is proposing approval of a Development Agreement establishing
22   requirements, standards, and parameters for development of approximately 3.2-acres located at
23   13040 S Redwood Road, including approval of an eight (8) lot subdivision of that property, with
24   all lots accessing a private lane.
25
26   Mr. Lethbridge said the property is currently zoned R-3-SD, a single-family residential zone, with
27   1/3 minimum lot sizes. The zoning and general plan designations were previously approved by
28   Riverton City. The subdivision accesses Riverton Ranch Road via a private drive, and the
29   applicant has proposed a gate as part of the development. The lane and configuration and
30   location of the gate has been reviewed by the Riverton City’s Unified Fire Authority
31   representative.
32
33   Ryan McDougal, Applicant, commented that he wanted to continue the hearing until the
34   development agreement was correct. He said the redlines he submitted are still outstanding
35   and he would prefer to have 100% of the redlines signed off before entering into the master
36   development agreement.
37
38   Councilmember McCay said she would like to have the gate on lot 104 removed. Mr. McDougal
39   said the gate was placed there to minimize the traffic going in and out of the neighborhood.
40
41   Mr. Lethbridge explained the redlines were clean up items, the conditions are small and will be
42   completed by the engineer and sent to the applicant tomorrow.
43
44   Councilmember Buroker said that approving this project at this time would move the project
45   along quicker and said that continuing the public hearing would be very confusing for residents.
46
47   Mayor Staggs asked if the redlines would significantly impact this development. Mr. Lethbridge
48   said no.
49
50   Mayor Staggs called for public comments;
51
