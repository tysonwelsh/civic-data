ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS

Z-8-2024 Petition by West Valley City requesting a zone change to remove the Decker Lake Station
Overlay Zone and change the zoning from M (Manufacturing) to C-2 (General Commercial). The
property is located at 3022, 3038, and 3074 S. Decker Lake Dr., and at 2964 S. Tundra Ln. on 13.4 acres.
This petition also includes a zone change to remove the Decker Lake Station Overlay Zone from the
property at 2212 West 3100 South, which is 0.44 acres.

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented. There were no questions or comments.

ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS

ZT-10-2024 Petition by West Valley City requesting an amendment to the zoning ordinance by
modifying Section 7-6-301, adding Sections 7-10-400P through 7-10-402, and removing Sections 7-14-
100P through 7-14-104. These changes would remove the Decker Lake Station Overlay Zone and add
development standards for properties along Decker Lake Drive between 3100 South and 3500 South.

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented. There were no questions or comments.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS

CA-5-2024 Petition by Rasoul Gassmepour requesting a conditional use amendment to add automobile
service to the Ark Plaza development. The property is located at 1927 West 3600 South on 1.03 acres
and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial) Continued from September 11, 2024 Public Hearing.

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented. Since the Public Hearing on September 11th, the Applicant has
finished the landscaping and removed two auto tenants which has improved that parking significantly.
Jody is still working on getting definite square footage numbers to calculate parking and issue business
licenses. Commissioner Woodruff pointed out the original Conditional Use was approved for 49%
warehouse and 51% retail; he wanted to know if that’s a violation as his mixed use is more than 51%
retail. Jody replied that the mixed uses are not a violation she would press as long he meets the
parking requirement. However, he cannot accommodate the mixed uses as far as business licenses go.
Chair Lovato asked if the Applicant needs another two weeks to allow him more time to comply with
the requirements. Jody said she would leave that up to the Commissioners.


CA-6-2024 Petition by Wheeler Machinery requesting conditional use amendment approval for the
HyMac project which includes construction of a 34,500 SF shop building for engine and equipment
service as well as frontage improvements along 2100 South. The project is located at 4901 West 2100
South on 7.62 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing).

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented. There were no questions or comments.


CA-7-2024 Petition by Gerald Higgins requesting a conditional use amendment for an expansion of an
automobile service business. The property is located at 3899 S. Redwood Road on 4.52 acres
(expansion area 0.33 acres) and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial).

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented on behalf of Brock Anderson. There were no questions or
comments.


CA-8-2024 Petition by HourTaing Enterprise, LLC requesting conditional use amendment approval for
an auto service use in the Kim Plaza development. The project is located at 1798 West 3600 South, Unit
on 1.22 acres and is zoned C2 (General Commercial).

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented. There were no questions or comments.
