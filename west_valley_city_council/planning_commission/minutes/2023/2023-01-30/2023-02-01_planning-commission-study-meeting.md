CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-1-2022 Petition by ERIC MALMBERG WITH AWA requesting conditional use approval for a
              new industrial building. The property is located at 5000 S U-111 Hwy on 11.32 acres and
              is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
              brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that this and the subsequent application, C-2-2023,
              have the same applicant, and both applications are proposing the addition of a
              new building at the Northrup Grumman facility. He said this proposal is for a new
              manufacturing building to construct rocket motors. Brock stated that the metal
              structure would be 46,844 square feet. The disturbed area for construction is over
              10 acres, which makes it a conditional use and, therefore, is coming before the
              Planning Commission today. He pointed out that the proposed building includes a
              locker room, staff breakroom, and a couple of offices, with most of the space used
              to construct the rocket motors. Brock noted that they would be meeting the
              parking requirements. He explained that historically, Northrup Grumman had not
              been required to add formal landscaping to buildings more than 300 feet internally
              on the site, so it would not be required for this proposal.


C-2-2022      Petition by ERIC MALMBERG WITH AWA requesting conditional use approval for a
              new industrial building. The property is located at 5000 S U-111 Hwy on 13.5 acres and
              is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
              brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson said this proposal is for a new warehouse building to store and distribute
              finished rocket motors. He stated that the warehouse would be 58,576 square feet. Brock
              explained that they would need 29 stalls to meet the parking requirements. He pointed out
              that the applicant is proposing 26 stalls, and they are confident that will be sufficient
              parking for the employees. However, they could add the three additional stalls if the
              Commission would like them to meet the requirement. Brock noted that no formal
              landscaping would be required, and they would not need to meet the Commercial Design
              Standards.



There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:37 p.m.
