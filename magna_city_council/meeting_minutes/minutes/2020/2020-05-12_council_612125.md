Citizen Public Input

              Greg Schulz stated he received an email from Mark Elieson who said unlicensed
salespeople were going door to door in the middle of the pandemic. He had asked what the
Council could do about that.

             Mark Elieson stated that is a correct analysis of his email. These salespeople are
not wearing masks nor social distancing.

                Paul Ashton stated the Council has the authority to talk to the salespeople,
especially if they are not licensed and not complying with health regulations. He suggested Mr.
Elieson get information from them and call in a complaint.

              Council Member Prokopis suggested Mr. Elieson call code enforcement since
the salespeople were operating without a business license.

                Lieutenant Levi Hughes, Unified Police Department (UPD) stated there is a
statute that allows the UPD to cite door to door salespeople who do not have licenses. A lot of
them come from out of state, and the sooner the UPD starts citing them, the quicker they leave.

Reports from Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Lieutenant Levi Hughes delivered a presentation on the Magna Precinct
reviewing calls for service, general offenses, calls for officers, response times, the violent crime
report, death investigations, robberies, felony assaults, sex abuse of children, adult sex abuse,
missing persons cases, runaway juvenile cases, high risk operations, deployments, and juvenile
crime statistics.

He also reviewed the first quarter report, which included the number of cases and the arrests, of
which there were 18. That is significant and a result of detectives doing great work. Officers from
the Magna Precinct have been involved in the initial stages of Magna’s Communities that Care
program, and they are meeting this week via ZOOM. The detective unit facility and inside of the
police station are being remodeled to make room for the new detectives. The detectives who
have been working from home due to COVID-19 are now back in the office as well. A couple of
search warrants were done earlier in the year resulting in homes being shut down from narcotics
distributions as well as nuisance homes. There have also been some gang issues. So, the
Magna Precinct has been working with school resource officers to help kids stay on track. The
traffic unit has increased by one officer, so there are now two motor officers and their only job in
Magna is traffic That unit also received a new radar trailer, which is ready to be used.

               Mayor Peay stated that garbage has been being dumped at the recreation center.

                Lieutenant Hughes stated the UPD is aware of that and trying to keep it cleaned
up, so it does not become an eyesore or encourage others to dump.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                Council Member Prokopis stated the UFA should have a definitive answer by
Friday whether the crew that was displaced from Station 102 would be allowed to return. If they
are allowed back, they will return on about Monday. The UFA Board is talking about the budget
process and will be considering a 2 percent increase for services for next year’s budget. Twenty
wildland firefighters were brought on board earlier this year, due to the dry climate. It is expected
there will be an early fire season. Thirty-one recruits who have been training will graduate
Thursday, and they will get their first assignments afterward. He reviewed the Utah Department
of Health’s COVID tracker, which recently started tracking by zip code. Two days ago, 116 people
in the 84044 zip code tested positive. Compared to other cities, Magna is in the middle – Salt
Lake City has over 600 cases and West Valley City is approaching 300 cases.

Resolution 20-05-01

               Paul Ashton reviewed the resolution requesting the reopening of a cemetery fund
account by the GSLMSD to manage the finances of the Pleasant Green Cemetery. He noted the
GSLMSD only has to pay expenses for the cemetery on revenue that the Magna Metro Township
actually provides to it.

RESOLUTION NO. 20-05-01                                                      Date: May 12, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       REQUESTING THE OPENING OF A CEMETERY FUND ACCOUNT BY THE
       GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT TO MANAGE THE
       FINANCES OF THE PLEASANT GREEN CEMETERY

                                          RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body for
      Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District that
      provides certain municipal-type services to Magna which include accounting and
      financial management of Magna’s accounts.

   D. The Pleasant Green Cemetery Preservation and Development Association is a Utah
      nonprofit organization (the “Nonprofit”).

   E. In October 2019, the nonprofit reached out to Magna regarding the municipality’s
      possible interest in taking over the ownership, operation, and management of the
      Pleasant Green Cemetery (the “Cemetery”).

   F. Pursuant to discussions between the nonprofit and Magna, it was decided the Cemetery
      ownership, operation and management would be transitioned to the municipality.




                                               5
DATE             TUESDAY                                MAY                              12, 2020

   G. Transitioning the ownership, operation, and management of the cemetery to the
      municipality insures the long term viability of the cemetery as a locally operated entity.

   H. Pursuant to commencing operation of the cemetery, Magna requires the MSD open a
      fund account for the cemetery to manage the revenues and expenses of the cemetery.

                                         RESOLUTION

      THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township, the Mayor and Council
hereby requests the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District open an account for the
Pleasant Green Cemetery to accept revenues and to pay from those revenues the expenses of
the Cemetery necessary for its operation and management.
