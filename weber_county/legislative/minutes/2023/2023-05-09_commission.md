D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: I had the opportunity to represent our county at the Catholic
Community Services function this morning. I have been told in the past that Utah is the number one state in the nation
for volunteering. If that’s true of the state then the governor should give Weber County recognition for the highest
number of volunteers for any county in this state. It’s not government’s job to do everything that volunteers and people
can do for themselves. Just last month 10,000 people have been served including meals for school kids, even on
weekends. It was great to visit with the Catholic Community Services and to see volunteerism is alive and well in this
county.

H. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. APPROVE A GRANT AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE OF UTAH DEPARTMENT OF
       ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY, DIVISION OF WATER QUALITY FOR THE WEST WEBER SITE
       SEWER INFRASTRUCTURE GRANT PROGRAM.
         Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): This is a request received an allocation last year for
         ARPA funding for sewer improvements at LMSA lagoon. Contract between county and the division of Water
         Quality that received the funding. Contract outlines terms and conditions and the way we would interface with
         the Division of Water Quality as we proceed to assist LMSA with the renovation of their lagoon system. One
         caviat to the contract is the $5M grant has specific restrictions only being used for construction costs but not
         administrative. Any leftover funds can go through the lift station at 5900.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a grant agreement with the State of Utah Department of
         Environmental Quality, Division of Water Quality for the West Weber Site Sewer Infrastructure Grant Program;
         Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye

    2. APPROVE RESOLUTION 25-2023 INITIATING THE CREATION OF A GOVERNING
       DOCUMENT    FOR   THE    PROPOSED        PROMONTORY COMMERCE CENTER
       INFRASTRUCTURE DISTRICTS NOS. 1, 2, AND 3.
         Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): PIDs allow developers to pursue funding for needed
         infrastructure in particular areas. We have been working through our policies and procedures aligning with
         the state’s code. Promontory’s request has gone through the Economic Finance Committee which recommends
         approval of the resolution authorizing creation of the PID governing document.

    3. APPROVE WEBER COUNTY TAX REVIEW COMMITTEE TAX REFUND RECOMMENDATION
       REGARDING PARCEL #20-114-0003.
         Deedee Kimber (Assessor’s Office): Received request for a refund from Randy Reese for property purchased
         in 2016 when requirements were a little different than today. Pineview Summer Homes does not receive mail
         so he did not receive his valuation notices or his tax bills. He has since updated his address with the county
         this February to his PO box. The Tax Review Committee recommends a refund of one year refund for 2022.
         Commissioner Harvey: What does ‘timely’ mean?
         Ms. Kimber: Statute says you can apply for a late appeal up to March 31st for appealing the prior year. The
         state rules keep changing. We can only go back the one year which is the committee’s recommendation.
         Courtlan Erickson (Attorney): Between Title 59 and our own county Ordinance, you have the authorization
         to go back up to 4 years of repayment. What the Tax Review Committee recommended is to go back one year
         beyond what the Board of Equalization (BoE) would do. They are already recommending that you exercise
         your authority under State code section 59-2-1347 to grant a one year refund beyond what the Board of
         Equalization would do.
         Commissioner Bolos: The Tax Review Committee recommends a one year refund. Can you tell me about the
         conversation on how you got to one year?
         Ms. Kimber: We did talk about the four years but based on the county Ordinance reading that the taxable
         status change allowed up to one year drove the discussion. Normally you can make a solid case and just do it
         but recent changes in laws have made it difficult.
         Mr. Erickson: I know some of the discussion committee members had related to a property owner’s
         responsibility to be aware of their obligations under state law and to check the mail, provide the right addresses
