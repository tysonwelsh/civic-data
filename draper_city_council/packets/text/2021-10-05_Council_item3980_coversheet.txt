From:
CC:  Date: October 5, 2021
Re:  Comments: Under MLUDMA, the city may require or permit developers to enter into an agreement and provide improvement completion assurances for public infrastructure and landscaping requirements found in the city code. This amendment clarifies acceptable methods of improvement completion assurances and more accurately uses legislative language as opposed to contractual language to define under what circumstances an improvement completion assurance may be posted. The findings are as follows:
The proposed amendments are consistent with goals, objectives, and policies of the city's general plan;
The amendment will not create a conflict with any other section of the city code;
The amendment is in the city's best interest; and
The amendment implements the best current professional practices of urban planning, design, and engineering.
