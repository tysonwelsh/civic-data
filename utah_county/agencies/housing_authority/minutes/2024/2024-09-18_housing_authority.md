B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-09-02 Approve Springlake 2025 Budget 
Steve made a motion to approve the 2025 Springlake Budget. April seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
 
Springlake is 13-unit property that receives subsidy through the USDA. Each year the USDA 
requires an annual budget be submitted after HAUC Board of Commissioners approves the 
budget. The property is stable and is anticipated to continue through 2025. In the budget for 
2025 HAUC has asked for a rent increase on each of the 13 units to keep up with the fair 
market rents in Utah County. 
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-09-03 Approve Non-Profit Entity Creation 
Sara made a motion to approve the Non-Profit Entity Creation. Suzanne seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC has been working with legal counsel to create a non-profit entity. The proposed name 
for the entity is Utah County Communities (UCC).  Legal counsel provided the by-laws and is 
moving forward with the creation. HAUC staff is seeking approval to complete all the 
necessary steps to create the non-profit entity. Such as file for 501c3 status with the IRS, bank 
account creation, transfer of funds into the bank account.
