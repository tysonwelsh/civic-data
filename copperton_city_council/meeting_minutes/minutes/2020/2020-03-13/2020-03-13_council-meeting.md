Council Emergency Business

Copperton State of Emergency - COVID-19 (Coronavirus)

               Council Member Pazell stated he is proposing a resolution adopting procedures
to address and respond to the Coronavirus Disease of 2019 (COVID-19). This is a prudent move
both for public health and for the free flow of federal and state dollars. Costs associated with
COVID-19 can be recouped provided Copperton takes the necessary measures and precautions.

            Mayor Clayton described the Copperton Plan of Action and Declaration of
Emergency. These plans are not needed currently but should be on file and ready to go.

              The Council discussed amendments to the documents.
DATE           FRIDAY                             MARCH                                 13, 2020


               Nathan Bracken amended the documents as discussed.

                 Council Member Pazell stated his suggestion for a food drive (non-perishable
food, water, and medical supply drive) will keep the community together, reduce panic, and create
resources in case of radical need. He will push this idea on social media. Council Member Bailey
will lead this effort.

               A motion was made by Council Member Pazell, seconded by Council Member
Stitzer, to approve the following Resolution, Emergency Response Plan, and Declaration of
Emergency as amended. The motion passed unanimously.

                                 RESOLUTION # 03-01
                                   March 13, 2020

      A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
      ADOPTING PROCEDURES TO ADDRESS AND RESPOND TO THE
      CORONAVIRUS DISEASE OF 2019 (COVID-19)

      WHEREAS, Chapter 2.86 of the Copperton Metro Township Code (the “Code”) authorizes
the Mayor to declare a state of emergency and sets forth the actions that the Copperton Metro
Township (“Copperton”) can take to respond to an emergency; and

        WHEREAS, Section 2.86.050(E) of the Code specifically authorizes the Mayor to declare
a state of emergency at the request of the director of the Salt Lake County Health Department or
the Utah Department of Health; and

       WHEREAS, on January 21, 2020, the Utah Department of Health activated its Department
Operations Center in response to the evolving COVID-19 global outbreak and recognized COVID-
19 as an imminent threat to the health and safety of the residents of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, on February 28, 2020, the State Emergency Operations Center raised its
activation level to Level 3-Elevated Action, and the Utah Division of Emergency Management and
the Utah Department of Health have activated a Joint Information System for public information;
and

        WHEREAS, the Centers for Disease Control (“CDC”) identifies the potential public health
threat posed by COVID-19 both globally and in the United States as “high,” and has advised that
person-to-person spread of COVID 19 will continue to occur globally, including within the United
States; and

       WHEREAS, the Utah Department of Health recognizes that confirmed community
transmission in the United States significantly increases the risk of exposure and infection to the
State of Utah’s general public and creating an extreme public health risk that may spread quickly;
and
DATE            FRIDAY                             MARCH                                  13, 2020

       WHEREAS, due to the identification of the COVID-19 cases in Utah, COVID-19 has
created conditions that are or are likely to be beyond the control of local resources and require the
combined forces of other political subdivisions to combat; and

       WHEREAS, although Copperton does not currently have a confirmed case of COVID-19,
the Copperton Metro Township Council (“Council”) desires to adopt a plan of action that will guide
Copperton’s efforts to respond to COVID-19; and

       WHEREAS, the Council has coordinated with the Salt Lake County Health Department to
prepare a recommended plan of action (ADDENDUM 1) and has prepared a draft declaration of
emergency (ADDENDUM 2) that may be used or modified as necessary by the Mayor in
accordance with the plan of action or as the Council or Mayor may otherwise deem necessary.

       NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY PROCLAIMED AND ORDERED, by the Copperton
Metro Township Council that effective immediately:

      1. The plan of action attached as ADDENDUM 1 is adopted and shall be implemented,
as needed, by the Council and all applicable Copperton staff and agents; and

       2. If needed or as otherwise contemplated in the plan of action, the Mayor is authorized
to use and modify the draft declaration of emergency attached as ADDENDUM 2 pursuant to
Chapter 2.86 of the Copperton Metro Township Code and all other applicable laws, regulations,
and ordinances;

       3. Nothing in this resolution, the draft declaration of emergency, or the plan of action shall
limit, abrogate, or otherwise modify any authority or action under applicable law, regulation,
ordinance, or policy that Copperton, the Council, the Mayor, or any of Copperton’s staff or agents
may take to respond to COVID-19, in compliance and conjunction with state and federal law;

      4. The plan of action will be posted to the Copperton Metro Township’s website, its social
media accounts, and the Utah Public Notice website, and will be otherwise distributed to
Copperton residents as the Council or Mayor may deem necessary; and

       5. Councilmember Bailey is authorized and instructed to develop and lead an emergency
committee that will work with other stakeholders to organize a drive that will inventory and collect
non-perishable food, water, and medical and other supplies needed to respond to COVID-19.

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Copperton Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah

A DECLARATION OF A PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY FOR THE CORONAVIRUS DISEASE
                            OF 2019 (COVID-19)

       WHEREAS, Chapter 2.86 of the Copperton Metro Township Code (the “Code”)
authorizes the Mayor to declare a state of emergency and sets forth the actions that the
Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) can take to respond to an emergency; and

        WHEREAS, Section 2.86.050(E) of the Code specifically authorizes the Mayor to declare
a state of emergency at the request of the director of the Salt Lake County Health Department
or the Utah Department of Health; and

       WHEREAS, on January 21, 2020, the Utah Department of Health activated its Department
Operations Center in response to the evolving COVID-19 global outbreak and recognized COVID-
19 as an imminent threat to the health and safety of the residents of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, on February 28, 2020, the State Emergency Operations Center raised its
activation level to Level 3-Elevated Action, and the Utah Division of Emergency Management
and the Utah Department of Health have activated a Joint Information System for public
information; and

        WHEREAS, the Centers for Disease Control (“CDC”) identifies the potential public health
threat posed by COVID-19 both globally and in the United States as “high,” and has advised that
person-to-person spread of COVID 19 will continue to occur globally, including within the United
States; and

        WHEREAS, the Utah Department of Health recognizes that confirmed community
transmission in the United States significantly increases the risk of exposure and infection to the
State of Utah’s general public and creating an extreme public health risk that may spread quickly;
and

        WHEREAS, due to the identification of the COVID-19 cases in Salt Lake County, COVID-
19 has created conditions that are or are likely to be beyond the control of local resources and
require the combined forces of other political subdivisions to combat; and

      WHEREAS, the mobilization of local resources, ability to coordinate interagency
response, accelerate procurement of vital supplies, and use of mutual aid will be critical to
successfully responding to COVID-19; and

       WHEREAS, these conditions do create a “State of Emergency” within the intent of the
Disaster Response and Recovery Act found in Title 53, Chapter 2a of the Utah Code Annotated
1953, as amended; and necessitate that Copperton proclaim the existence of a local public
health emergency.

        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY PROCLAIMED AND ORDERED, by the Mayor of
the Copperton Metro Township pursuant to Chapter 2.86, that:

       1.     A local public health emergency exists in Copperton, and shall remain in effect for
DATE           FRIDAY                             MARCH                                13, 2020

        no longer than 30 days unless ratified by the Copperton Metro Township Council and
        continued for up to an additional 30 days; and

        2.    The Council and all applicable Copperton staff and agents will implement the
        corresponding portions of the Copperton COVID-19 plan of action adopted on March 13,
        2020; and

        3.     During the local public health emergency, all applicable provisions of Chapter 2.86
        of the Copperton Code needed to prevent or contain the outbreak and spread of COVID-
        19 will apply, including but not limited to the following actions needed to:

                    a. All government sponsored public meetings, including but not limited to
               planning and zoning, are canceled until further notice with the exception of the
               Copperton Metro Township Council, which will meet remotely pursuant to its
               electronic meetings policy;

                    b. COVID-19 advisory notices will be posted on all public locations that host
               public events, including the Lions Club, Churches, and Parks; and

                  c. The Copperton Park is closed for the duration of the declaration of the
               emergency; and

         4.    This declaration will be: (a) posted to the Copperton Metro Township’s website, its
        social media accounts, and the Utah Public Notice website; (b) distributed to Copperton
        residents in the most expeditious manner possible as determined by the Mayor; and (c)
        disseminated to the appropriate news media pursuant to Section 2.86.050(D) of the
        Copperton Code.
