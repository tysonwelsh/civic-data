Re: General Plan Amendment - Moderate Income Housing Comments: A discussion regarding Chapter 4 - Housing of the current General Plan. The intent of the discussion is to identify changes that may need to be made to the General Plan relative to  the requirements of House Bill (H.B) 462 passed by the Utah State Legislature during the recent 2022 legislative session.  Staff can then formulate possible changes to be reviewed at a future date through the public hearing process.
Attached is Chapter 4 of the General Plan with highlights denoting the selected Moderate Income Housing strategies currently incorporated by Draper City into the General Plan and a copy of the now applicable State Code statutes. The statute requires the City to choose five of the listed strategies and adopt them into the housing element of the General Plan along with implementation measures of how the City can achieve said strategies.
