APPROVED                               CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL
                               JOINT MEETING WITH CACHE COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION
            March 30, 2022 at 3:00 p.m. - Cache County Multipurpose Room at 179 North Main, Logan, Utah.
 In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
appear and speak at a County Council meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinions or purported facts.
             The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


MEMBERS PRESENT: Barbara Tidwell, Paul R. Borup, Karl Ward, David Erickson, Gina Worthen, Nolan P. Gunnell
MEMEBERS EXCUSED: Gordon Zilles
PLANNING COMMISSION PRESENT: Chris Sands, Melinda Lee, Brady Christensen, Lane Parker, Jason Watterson, Brandon
Spackman, Nate Daugs
STAFF PRESENT: Development Services Director Chris Harrild, Planner Lauren Ryan, Trails Coordinator Carly Lansche, Planning
Manager Tim Watkins, Chief Deputy Executive Dirk Anderson, Economic Development Director Shaun Milne, Public Works
Director Matt Phillips, Clerk/Auditor Jess Bradfield, Chief Deputy Clerk/Auditor Bryson Behm
OTHER ATTENDANCE:

Council Meeting
1. Call to Order 3:00p.m. – Chair Barbara Tidwell

2. Discussion 18:48 The Cache County Council and Planning Commission discussed the County’s General Plan with an emphasis
   in the following areas (given in order of discussion):

   Discussion began with talks about potential development and rezoning options for various lots and parcels of land for
   residential use. The group discussed best practices and potential plans for how county land will or should be used in the
   future, if land should be conserved, or developed and rezoned for residential or commercial use. 33:34

   The group changed topics and the Commission and Council discussed County water conservation, irrigation, and water use.
   1:14:08

   The discussion then shifted to regional collaboration and efforts to coordinate planning with municipalities; this included
   discussion on collaborated efforts in transportation and roads. 1:25:15

   The Commission and Council then discussed outdoor recreation and activity; protecting agriculture, trails, and outdoor areas.
   1:36:10

   Finally, the group discussed economic effects of development and options for affordable housing, commercial use areas, and
   potential options for managing and sustaining growth moving forward. 1:45:04


 Adjourn: Approximately 5:00 PM
