               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Cutler, moved open the

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       AUTHORIZING AND REGULATING THE USE OF ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES
       FOR EFFECTIVE AND EFFICIENT PROCESSING OF CERTAIN DOCUMENTS
       FOR THE METRO TOWNSHIP

The White City Metro Township Council does hereby ORDAIN as follows:

                                           RECITALS

   1. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   2. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of
      White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   3. Utah Code § 46-4-201 allows for the use of electronic signatures on legally binding
      documents and contracts, unless otherwise disallowed.

   4. The Utah State Legislature uses electronic signatures for the effective and efficient
      processing of legislation and other legal work of the legislature.

   5. Municipal Code Online is a Corporation that hosts the White City Code of Ordinances, and
      among the features of its software can allow for the secure use of electronic signatures in
      the approval of Ordinances pertaining to changes, additions, and/or repeals of White City’s
      Code.

   6. Certain legal and business documents processed by the administrative agents of White
      City require the efficiency and effectiveness provided by using electronic signatures.

   7. It would be a benefit to White City and its residents to use this regulate and use feature.

                                         RESOLUTION

       BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council does hereby
adopt the following ordinance, to be added to the White City Metro Township Code of Ordinance:

                       CHAPTER 2.85 – ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES

2.85.010 – Purpose

The ordinance codified in this chapter regulates the use of electronic signatures by elected
officials and other parties in approving legislation, contracts, and/or other legally-binding
documents of White City.

2.85.020 – Uses of Electronic Signatures Allowed


                                                4
DATE            THURSDAY                               NOVEMBER                           15, 2018


In any document accepted by White City in which a signature is required or used, White City may
authorize the use of an electronic signature so long as it complies with the requirements of the
most currently adopted version of Utah Code § 46-4: Uniform Electronic Transactions Act.

2.85.030 – Rules Regarding Force and Effect of Electronic Signatures

The use of an electronic signature shall have the same force and effect as the use of a “wet” or
manual signature if:

   A. The signature is capable of verification;

   B. The signature is under the sole control of the person using it; and

   C. The signature is linked to the data in such a manner that it is readily ascertainable if the
      data is changed after the signature is applied.

2.85.040 – Determination of Approved Technologies, Vendors, and Uses of Electronic Signatures

Upon the recommendations of Staff, the Council shall determine and approve acceptable
technologies and vendors under this section consistent with industry best practices to ensure the
security and integrity of the data and the signature. The Administrator shall further determine and
recommend to the Council the documents for which White City will accept electronic signatures.

2.85.050 – Enactment Date

This Ordinance shall be effective on the 31st day after the date of its adoption by the Council.

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area

                Council Member Cutler stated Magna and Kearns Metro Townships shared a
precinct with one Chief. They have decided to separate the precincts; Chief Jason Ackerman is
assigned to Magna and Chief Brian Lohrke is assigned to Kearns. Six new officers are in the
training and will be on the road in 6-8 weeks. Budget amendments and budget adjustments were
made.

The Council observed a moment of silence for UPD Sergeant Cory Peterson who passed away
from an unforeseen illness.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                 Council Member Perry UFA and other agencies have sent fire fighters to
California to assist with the fires. The Facilities Committee for UFSA met and toured 2 new West
Valley fire stations and the new Salt Lake City fire station to look at the pros and cons and what
will work for UFA.

South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

              Council Member Price stated seasonal employees are done for the year. A new
set of seasonal employees will be hired in the spring of 2019. The Utah Mosquito Abatement
Association (UMAA) meeting was held and discussed DEET protection and the use of drones.
The board looked into getting its own lab but has since decided not to move forward.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

               Mayor Flint stated the GSLMSD Board voted against a tax increase. Mayor
McAdams will be removed as CEO of the GSLMSD in the upcoming legislation. Every department
that has presented a budget to the GSLMSD has been required to present an itemized accounting
of what the funds are for. Cathy Jensen was hired as the GSLMSD Financial Manager. The
GSLMSD is coming into its own.
