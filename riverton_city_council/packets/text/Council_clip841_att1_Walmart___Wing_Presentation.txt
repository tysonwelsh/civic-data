                               Walmart & Wing Partnership
                               Drone delivery is one of many ways Walmart is offering faster,
                               more flexible delivery options. Wing & Walmart are taking
                               last-mile delivery to new heights, with a new level of speed,
                               reliability, sustainability, and cost-effectiveness.
                               Our partnership is helping to meet the demand for last-mile
                               delivery with Walmart leading operations for local stores and
                               Wing leading logistics of the delivery drones at each store.
                               Since partnering in 2023, Wing and Walmart now serve
                               thousands of shoppers every week from dozens of
                               Walmart stores across Dallas-Fort Worth, Metro Atlanta, and
                               Greater Houston.
