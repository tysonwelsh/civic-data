                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-47

                A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY
     COUNCIL APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN
  INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY, TO
  OBTAIN HIGH RESOLUTION ORTHOGONAL AERIAL PHOTOGRAPHS OF THE
               MUNICIPAL TERRITORY OF RIVERTON CITY.

        WHEREAS, Salt Lake County (the “County”) has obtained oblique imagery and high-
resolution orthogonal aerial photographs of County territory; and

        WHEREAS, in obtaining the aforesaid imagery and photographs, the County has retained
the rights to share the same with other governmental entities in the state of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that obtaining a set of aerial
photographs of Riverton City is useful as a tool for, without limitation, conducting meetings
where group participation is required to vet land development strategies within Riverton City;
and

        WHEREAS, under the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Annotated, 11-13-
101 et seq. (2020) any two or more public agencies may enter into agreements with one another
for joint or cooperative action and may also contract with each other to perform any
governmental service activity or taking which each public agency entering into the contract is
authorized by law to perform; and

        WHEREAS, an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement (“Agreement”) has been prepared for
approval and execution by and between the County and Riverton City, which states the purposes
thereof, and the extent of the required participation of the parties and the rights, duties,
responsibilities, and obligations of the parties in the acquisition and use of aerial photographs
needed by Riverton for the uses described in this Resolution.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

   1. The attached Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Riverton City and the County
      County is hereby approved by the Council and the Mayor is hereby authorized to execute
      the same on behalf of Riverton City, once the same is approved as to legal form by the
      Riverton City Attorney.
