                                   ORDINANCE NO: 64-2024


      ORDINANCE AMENDING THE LAND USE ELEMENT OF THE LEHI CITY
          GENERAL PLAN AND THE GENERAL PLAN LAND USE MAP
               AT APPROXIMATELY 9861 WEST 9600 NORTH

      WHEREAS, on October 22, 2024, following all necessary public hearings, the Lehi City
Council adopted an amendment to the Lehi City General Plan which included the 2022 Land Use
Element together with the Lehi City General Plan Land Use Map; and

       WHEREAS, the Lehi City Planning Commission held a public hearing on September 26,
2024, to review and make a recommendation on the General Plan Amendment located at
approximately 9861 West 9600 North changing the land use designation from Very Low Density
Residential Agriculture (VLDRA) to Low Density Residential (LDR), as shown on Exhibit “A”
and forwarded their recommendation to the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council held a public meeting on October 22, 2024, pursuant to the
requirements for amendment of the Land Use Element of the Lehi City General Plan and General
Plan Land Use Map from Very Low Density Residential Agriculture (VLDRA) to Low Density
Residential (LDR).

     NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF LEHI CITY,
UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

        PART 1:

       The Land Use Element of the Lehi City General Plan and General Plan Land Use Map is
hereby amended to change the subject property from VLDRA to LDR. The total amendment will
include approximately 5.18-acres of property located at approximately 9861 North 9600 West,
more specifically shown on Exhibit “A” attached hereto.

        PART II:

       If a provision of this Ordinance 64-2024 conflicts with a provision of a previously
adopted ordinance concerning the same title, chapter, and/or section number amended herein, the
provision in this Ordinance shall prevail.

        A.     This ordinance and its various section, clauses and paragraphs are hereby declared
to be severable. If any part, sentence, clause or phrase is adjudged to be unconstitutional or invalid,
the remainder shall not be affected thereby.

       B.      The City Council hereby directs that the official copy of the Lehi City General Plan
be updated to reflect the provisions enacted by this Ordinance.
