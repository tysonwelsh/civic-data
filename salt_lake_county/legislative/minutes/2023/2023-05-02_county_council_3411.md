A Proclamation of the Salt Lake County Council Proclaiming the Valuable 
Contributions of the Paraguayan Community to our County Cultural, 
Economic, and Civic Life
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County is home to a vibrant and diverse community 
of individuals from all corners of the world; and 
WHEREAS, the Paraguayan community has made significant cultural, 
economic, and civic contributions to the fabric of Salt Lake County society; 
and 
WHEREAS, Paraguayans have enriched our community through their unique 
culture, language, food, music, dance, and arts, sharing their heritage with all 
residents of Salt Lake County; and 
WHEREAS, the Paraguayan community has also contributed to the 
economic development of our County by creating businesses, generating 
employment, and increasing the tax base; and
WHEREAS, Paraguayan residents have participated actively in civic life, 
contributing to the betterment of our County by volunteering, serving in 
public office, and advocating for freedom, family traditions, and religious 
freedom.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT Proclaimed:
We, the members of the Salt Lake County Council do hereby recognize and 
acknowledge the valuable contributions of the Paraguayan community to 
our county cultural, economic, and civic life; and
We encourage all residents of Salt Lake County to celebrate and embrace 
the diversity of our community, recognizing that the identity and values of 
the Latin American people make us stronger and more dynamic.
Dated this 1st day of May, 2023.

5.1 Public Hearing on May 2, 2023 at 4PM, to Hear Comment on 
the Formation of the Salt Lake County Conventions and

A motion was made by Council Member Harrison, seconded by Council 
Member Bradshaw, to open the public hearing.  The motion carried by a 
unanimous vote.
The following citizens spoke in favor of the formation of the Salt Lake County 
Conventions and Tourism Assessment Area (CTAA) for the following reasons: 
Visit Salt Lake ’s Board of Directors analyzed this for two years before going to the 
Legislature asking it to allow this, and it endorses this unanimously; the CTAA will help 
the tourism industry rise to a higher standard for the benefit of thousands who depend on 
tourism to pay bills and raise children; it will support 48,000 jobs; it will give the 
hospitality industry another tool to bolster the economy; it presents an opportunity to 
bring fresh money into the community; the hospitality industry is just rebounding from the 
pandemic, and the assessment is a way to garner more business; most tier one convention 
cities trying to be competitive have an assessment in place or are pursuing one, and those 
that have a tourism assessment have seen a positive impact in obtaining new business; it is 
an opportunity to be competitive with other markets; it will bring new tourism to Salt Lake 
County for the hotels, which have a commitment to their employees to keep their rooms 
full and give them a safe and secure working environment; the hospitality industry brings 
people together, and hotels host various groups that would not otherwise come to Salt 
Lake; convention attendants and tourist visitors are high spenders in Salt Lake County, 
and high -sped visitors supercharge the economy, as they spend money at shops, 
restaurants, and bars, and they are tenants in commercial properties; there is a ripple effect 
on the economy when tourists spend money, as conventions and trade shows hire local 
trade persons, and those trade persons then spend into the economy again; tourism is vital 
for the success of the City Creek Center, as visitor spending can be as much as 40-60

7.1 Consideration of a Resolution of the Salt Lake County 
Council Authorizing Execution and Delivery of a Real Estate 
Purchase Contract with the Utah Department of 
Transportation and Approving Conveyance of a Parcel of 
Real Property and a Temporary Construction Easement
23-0491
Attachments: Staff Report
Resolution to Convey Property to UDOT - Government 
Center (04.25.23) - RAFL
RESOLUTION NO. 6093
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
AUTHORIZING EXECUTION AND DELIVERY OF A REAL ESTATE 
PURCHASE CONTRACT WITH THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF 
TRANSPORTATION AND APPROVING CONVEYANCE OF A 
PARCEL OF REAL PROPERTY AND A TEMPORARY 
CONSTRUCTION EASEMENT
RECITALS
A. Salt Lake County (the “County”) owns a certain real property located 
at approximately 2100 South State Street, Salt Lake City, Utah, also 
identified as Parcel No. 16-18-355-002-2000 (the “Property”).
B. The Utah Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) is constructing 
roadway improvements along State Street in Salt Lake City (the “Project”) 
and would like to acquire a 6-square-foot portion of the Property (the 
“Parcel”) from the County and a temporary construction easement as part of 
the Project.
C. UDOT has submitted a Real Estate Purchase Contract (the “REPC”) 
to the County for the acquisition of (1) the Parcel via a quit claim deed (the 
“Deed”) and (2) the temporary construction easement via a separate 
document (the “Easement”).
D. The total amount offered by UDOT for acquisition of the Parcel and 
the Easement is $2,000.00, which amount has been reviewed and approved 
by the Salt Lake County Real Estate Section as full and adequate 
consideration.
Page 10 of 14
County Council Meeting Minutes May 2, 2023
E. It has been determined that the best interests of County and the general 
public will be served by executing the REPC, the Deed, and the Easement 
attached to this Resolution.  These actions will be in compliance with all 
applicable state statutes and county ordinances.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the grant and conveyance of the Parcel and the 
Easement to UDOT pursuant to the REPC is hereby approved, and the 
Mayor is authorized and directed to execute the REPC, attached hereto as 
Exhibit 1, and any other related document necessary to complete this 
transaction.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED by the Salt Lake County Council that the 
Mayor and County Clerk are hereby authorized, consistent with the terms of 
the REPC to execute the Deed and the Easement attached to the REPC and 
to deliver the fully executed documents to the Salt Lake County Real Estate 
Section for delivery to UDOT upon payment of the agreed upon purchase 
amount.
