                                                     MINUTES
                                         WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                        Tuesday, January 16, 2018 - 10:00 a.m.
                               Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
 In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
 appear and speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported
 facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: James H. “Jim” Harvey, Kerry W. Gibson and James Ebert.
OTHER STAFF PRESENT: Lynn Taylor, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s Office; Bryan Baron, Deputy County
Attorney; and Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME – Chair Harvey
B. INVOCATION – Kris Lewis
C. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Steffani Ebert
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY – Commissioner Ebert
E. CONSENT ITEMS:
   1. Warrants #1320-1322 and #424622-424783 in the amount of $734,341.60.
   2. Purchase orders in the amount of $216,412.58.
   3. Minutes for the meetings held on January 2 and 9, 2018.
   4. Surplus a 2005 Polaris Ranger 6x6 from the Weber County Sheriff's Office.
   5. Retirement Agreement with Chris Ward.
   6. Severance Agreement with Douglas Larsen.
      Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the consent items; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
      Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey - aye

F. ACTION ITEMS:
    1. CONTRACT WITH PAPER LANTERN INVESTMENTS, LLC, DBA BEAN-A-COLADA TO RENEW
       COMMERCIAL LEASE SPACE AT THE PLEASANT VALLEY BRANCH.

         Bryan Baron, Deputy County Attorney, presented this contract renewal for three years.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the contract with Paper Lantern Investments, LLC, DBA Bean-a-
         Colada to renew commercial lease space at the Pleasant Valley Branch; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey - aye
    2. REQUEST FOR APPROVAL OF A TAX REVIEW COMMITTEE RECOMMENDATION TO REFUND $663.25 TO
       ROBERT SCOTT BUCKLEY, PARCEL NUMBER 17-062-0037.
         Joe Olsen, of the County Assessor’s Office, representing the Tax Review Committee, stated that the
         property’s legal description includes part of the road. Mr. Buckley approached the county in 2017 with the
         issue. The county’s policy allows going back four years, which is for $663.25.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the Tax Review Committee recommendation to refund $663.25 to
         Robert Scott Buckley, parcel number 17-062-0037; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey - aye
    3. RESOLUTION MAKING APPOINTMENTS TO THE WEBER AREA DISPATCH 911 AND EMERGENCY
       SERVICES DISTRICT ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD – RESOLUTION 5-2018.
         Commissioner Gibson noted that the county advertised for the two midterm vacancies. WACOG reviewed
         the applications and recommended appointing Jim Truett and Russell Porter.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to adopt Resolution 5-2018 appointing Jim Truett (through 12/31/2018) and
         Russell Porter (through 12/31/2020) to the Weber Area Dispatch 911 and Emergency Services District
         Administrative Control Board; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey - aye
    4. INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH HARRISVILLE CITY FOR ANIMAL CONTROL SERVICES.
         Ashley Haslam, with County Animal Control, presented this contract extension. There are no other changes.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the Interlocal Agreement with Harrisville City for animal control
         services; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey - aye
   5. CONTRACT AMENDMENT WITH SUMMIT ENERGY, LLC, TO MODIFY MONTHLY INVOICES.
       Steffani Ebert, with the County Sheriff's Office, noted that the county has a contract with Summit Energy.
       The Sheriff’s Complex is on a different gas rate. This amendment modifies how the county is invoiced.
       Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the contract amendment with Summit Energy, LLC, to modify
       monthly invoices; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey - aye
   6. TRAINEE REIMBURSEMENT AGREEMENTS WITH THE FOLLOWING:
       COLTON VIGIL     ALONZO WADE
       TREVOR LEBARON ZACHARY LEHR
       CALEB RIVERA     DEZMOND PERKINS
       Steffani Ebert, with the County Sheriff's Office, presented these trainee standard reimbursement agreements
       to reimburse academy costs. The individuals agree to remain employed with the county for a minimum of
       two years.
       Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the trainee reimbursement agreements with Colton Vigil, Trevor
       LeBaron, Caleb Rivera, Alonzo Wade, Zachary Lehr and Dezmond Perkins; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey - aye

   7. CONTRACT WITH CRS ENGINEERS FOR ADDITIONAL WORK ON THE JUVENILE DETENTION FACILITY ROAD.
       Jared Andersen, County Engineer, presented this contract for the added work requirements requested by
       the county for the new road, Depot Drive, for this facility.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the contract with CRS Engineers for additional work on the Juvenile
       Detention Facility road; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
       Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey - aye

G. PUBLIC COMMENTS:
       John Ott, of South Ogden, thanked the Commission for working with him when he performed the flag
       retirement ceremony and would like to schedule another ceremony for the County Fair.

H. ADJOURN
      Commissioner Gibson moved to adjourn at 10:21 a.m.; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
      Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
