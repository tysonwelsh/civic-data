New Business

   1. Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Tentative Redevelopment Agency Budget Presentation.
      Finance Director, Crystal Makin, reviewed the tentative proposed RDA budget with the
      Directors, as well as an overview of what the Redevelopment Agency is and how it works.

       The RDA’s purpose is to access resources that help municipalities pursue and encourage
       economic development, create jobs that will generate tax revenue, eliminate blight, and
       facilitate the creation of affordable housing stock in the City.

       Revenues are earned through Tax Increment Funding (TIF), which is allocated by State statute
       through the creation of a project area, its administration, and housing.

       The current project areas are:
           a. 3900 South Project Area
           b. Market Station Project Area
           c. Central 15 Project Area
           d. HTRZ Project Area

       Other operating funds that the RDA’s funding is tied into are the administration fund, housing
       fund, and the debt service fund.

       The total tentative proposed budget for the 2024-2025 fiscal year is $33,011,100.

       The Board Members will be able to submit questions so that staff can work on getting additional
       information back to them.

       A copy of the presentation is to be attached and incorporated by this reference.

       There will be a Public Hearing scheduled and noticed to take public comment on the tentative
       proposed 2024-2025 RDA Budget for the May 22nd RDA Meeting.
