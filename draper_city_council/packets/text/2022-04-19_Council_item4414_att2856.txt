RECOMMENDATION:
Staff recommends the City Council approve resolution No. 22-18 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule
regarding the fees charged for Encroachment Permits.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule shows fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and administrative
service costs.

The proposed changes will update the existing “Street Cut Permits Fees” to “Encroachment Permits”. This will
make it uniformed throughout the Consolidated Fee Schedule. The Encroachment Permit fees are assessed to
reflect the increased in costs for maintenance and repairs to the city streets as these encroachments reduce the
useful life of the road.

       A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE
       OF DRAPER CITY REGARDING ENCROACHMENT PERMIT FEES.

       WHEREAS, the Mayor and City Council wish to update certain fees shown in the
Draper City Consolidated Fee schedule to better recover the cost of providing related services;
and

        WHEREAS, the City desires to update and clarify the permitting fees for work within
the city public ways to cover the cost to the city for administration and inspection services; and

       WHEREAS, the City desires to add encroachment permit fees related to narrow trench
excavation with the city public way; and

        WHEREAS, the proposed amendments comply with state law and benefit the health,
safety, and welfare of Draper residents.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH:

      Section 1. Approval. The City Council hereby amends the Draper City Consolidated Fee
Schedule as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid,
or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this
Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

       Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, ON THIS 19th DAY OF APRIL 2022.
