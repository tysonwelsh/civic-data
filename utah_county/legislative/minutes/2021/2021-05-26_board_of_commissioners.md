LEGISLATURE PASSED A SET OF REFORMS COLLECTIVELY CALLED TRUTH IN
TAXATION. ONE OF THE THINGS THAT TRUTH IN TAXATION DOES UNDER STATE
LAW IS REQUIRES THE STATE TAX COMMISSION TO CERTIFY THE TAX RATES
THAT ARE ADOPTED BY LOCAL GOVERNMENT. THIS IS KNOWN AS THE CERTIFIED
RATE AND EACH YEAR LOCAL GOVERNMENT CITIES AND COUNTIES WILL ADOPT
THIS CERTIFIED RATE. THE REASON THAT IT’S IMPORTANT THAT THE RATE IS
CERTIFIED IS THAT THE STATE TAX COMMISSION GOES THROUGH A VERY
COMPLEX CALCULATION PROCESS TO CERTIFY THE FACT THAT THE
JURISDICTION IS ONLY GOING TO COLLECT THE SAME NOMINAL DOLLAR
AMOUNT OF REVENUE THAT THEY COLLECTED THE YEAR BEFORE PLUS ANY
ADDITIONAL REVENUE RELATED TO NEW GROWTH IN VALUATION, NEW
GROWTH AND PROPERTIES.

FOR EXAMPLE: IF YOU HAD A COUNTY THAT COLLECTED A MILLION DOLLARS IN
YEAR 1 AND IN YEAR 2 NOTHING CHANGED MEANING NO NEW PEOPLE MOVED IN,
NO NEW PROPERTIES WERE CREATED, NO NEW UNDEVELOPED LAND WAS
DEVELOPED - EVERYTHING STAYED PRECISELY THE SAME, THEN THAT COUNTY
WOULD ONLY COLLECT A MILLION DOLLARS. EVEN IF EVERY PROPERTY IN THE
COMMUNITY WERE TO DOUBLE IN VALUE, THAT LOCAL COUNTY WOULD ONLY
COLLECT A MILLION DOLLARS IN YEAR 2. SO, THIS CERTIFIED TAX RATE

THE WAY THE TABOR RULE WORKS IN COLORADO, IS THAT THE INCREASE TO
PROPERTY TAXES IS CAPPED BASED ON A FORMULA OF POPULATION AND
INFLATION - WHAT THEY CALL ‘POPUFLATION’. SO, ALL AROUND THE COUNTRY

MECHANISM. WHAT THAT REQUIRES THEN, GOING BACK TO THE TRUTH IN

INFLATION IT WOULD LIKE TO RECAPTURE WHEN IT COLLECTS MORE TAXES IN
