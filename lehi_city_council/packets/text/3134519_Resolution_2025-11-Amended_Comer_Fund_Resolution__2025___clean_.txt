                                    RESOLUTION #: 2025-11

                 A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE FRANCES MARTENS COMER
                              PERPETUAL LIBRARY FUND.


        WHEREAS, in 2000, the Municipal Council established the Frances Martens Comer
Perpetual Library Fund (“Fund”) to honor Mrs. Frances Comer and her contribution to the
literacy and education of the children of Lehi; and

       WHEREAS, the resolution creating the Fund granted authority over the Fund to the
Library Board (“Board”) and established procedures for how the Fund could be used; and

       WHEREAS, on October 3, 2024, the City Council approved Resolution 2024-53
authorizing certain changes to the procedures established in the original resolution, and desires
now to rescind that resolution and amend the original resolution; and

       WHEREAS, the Lehi City Library Board desires to have these procedures amended in a
manner that continues to protect the Fund principal while allowing the Board more flexibility in
using the fund to benefit the library; and

       WHEREAS, currently the Board is authorized to use only 85% of the interest earnings of
the preceding fiscal year and requests the authority to use up to 100% of the interest earnings
of the preceding fiscal year, 100% of the profits earned through the Spring Gala, and 100% of
the profits earned through other Board fund-raising activities; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has authority to amend the procedures that govern the
Frances Martens Comer Library Fund and is willing to do so as described below;

       NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Municipal Council of Lehi City, Utah, that that
Resolution 2024-53 is hereby repealed and Resolution 02-08-00.2 is amended as shown on the
attached Exhibit A.

          Approved and Adopted by the Municipal Council of Lehi City this 11th day of February,
2025.

                                        EXHIBIT A
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 02-08-00.2

     RESOLUTION OF THE LEHI CITY COUNCIL ESTABLISHING THE FRANCES MARTENS
                        COMER PERPETUAL LIBRARY FUND

       Whereas, Frances Martens Comer has faithfully and diligently served the City of Lehi as
a member of the Lehi City Council from January 1996 through December 1999. In that
capacity she served as the Council representative to the City Library Board (“Board”) where she
was instrumental in expanding the Library’s budget and improving conditions for the staff and
patrons; and,

       Whereas, Mrs. Comer has contributed immeasurably to the literacy and education of
many hundreds of children who attended the Lehi Junior High School during her twenty-five
year career as a teacher and librarian where she helped instill in many young minds the
enrichment that can come by reading good books; and

        Whereas, it is the desire of the Lehi City Council to gratefully acknowledge the
significant contributions of Mrs. Comer throughout her career and particularly during her
tenure as a member of the Lehi City Council by establishing a library fund in her name for the
purpose of providing additional and ongoing financial support to the Lehi City Public Library.

        Now therefore, be it resolved by the Lehi City Council that a library fund is hereby
established and funded in the sum of $25,000.00 and designated as the FRANCES MARTENS
COMER LIBRARY FUND (“Fund”). In order to ensure that the fund remain in perpetuity the
following restrictions and rules apply:

1.      Neither the principal appropriation of $25,000.00 nor any portion of the accumulated
        interest will be available for use until July 1, 2005. Beginning on that date, 100% of the
        earned interest for the fiscal year immediately preceding, may be distributed to the
        Library Board for its use as described below (“Disbursement”). Each year thereafter,
        subject to the conditions set forth below, the Library Board will be entitled to the use of
        the Disbursement as it votes to do so. Whatever amount of Disbursement, if any,
        remains after the Board votes will be added to the perpetual Fund each year.

2.      Disbursements may be used for library materials and programs including but not
        limited to books, periodicals, electronic media, building improvements and library
        activities. All Disbursements must be approved by the Board.
3.   The money generated by the Board’s fund-raising activities (including the Spring Gala)
     may be used by the Board, including saving the money for future projects. In addition,
     all other funds raised by the Board through additional fund-raising activities may be
     used by the Board, including saving the money for future projects.

4.   Private contributions may be made payable to the Lehi City Corporation, Comer Library
     Fund. All private contributions become part of the fund balance.

5.   The Fund is to be for the ongoing support of the Library and its programs.

6.   The City Treasurer is directed to invest all funds in accordance with the Utah Money
     Management Act.

7.   The City Administrator, or designee, shall provide the Board with an annual financial
     statement showing the current balance of the Fund.
