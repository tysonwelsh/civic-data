Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Hemmert welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner
Peterson.

2. Consent Agenda

2.1 The Commission reviewed the minutes from April 23, 2020. Commissioner Everett moved to
approve the minutes Commissioner Hereth seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

3. Regular Agenda

3.1 Recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development Code amendment
to Chapter 20, adding a home office with mobile services as an allowed use. (Tabled from the
April 23, 2020 meeting)


    The Commissioners discussed the Development Code amendment. Chairman Hemmert felt
    that K5 wasn’t needed since it was stated in K1.

Motion: Commissioner Nielsen recommended approval and also include that K5 be removed as
it’s repetitive to K1.; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the staff report.
Commissioner Eyre seconded the motion.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Laura Allred’s request for review of RA-1
(residential/agriculture) zoning on the Allred B Annexation, approximately .57-acres located
at approximately 968 East Mesa Avenue.
    Tony Train was present on behalf of the applicant.

    No questions were asked of the applicant.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Laura Allred’s request for
review of RA-1 (residential/agriculture) zoning on the Allred B Annexation, approximately .57-
acres located at approximately 968 East Mesa Avenue; including all DRC comments and findings
stated in the report, with the recommendation that the developer find ways to incorporate more
green space and reduce the parking. Commissioner Hereth seconded the motion.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.3 Public hearing and recommendation of Richard Ellsworth’s request for review of the
Ellsworth Zone Change from TH-5 (transitional holding) to RA-1 (residential/agriculture)
on 0.34 acres located at 1015 East 1840 North.
    The applicant Richard Ellsworth was available for questions.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Nielsen moved to approve Richard Ellsworth’s request for review of the
Ellsworth Zone Change from TH-5 (transitional holding) to RA-1 (residential/agriculture) on
0.34 acres located at 1015 East 1840 North, including all DRC comments and findings stated in
the report. Commissioner Everett seconded the motion.


Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.4 Public hearing and consideration of ACRM Architects request for approval of the First
Utah Bank site plan with a conditional use for a drive-thru located at 3601 Digital Drive.
    The applicant David was present, but had technical difficulties so he called in.

    Commissioners asked clarifying questions to the staff. Commissioner Nielsen was concerned
    about whether they hit the required landscape requirement.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Nielsen moved to recommend approval of ACRM Architects request for
approval of the First Utah Bank site plan with a conditional use for a drive-thru located at 3601
Digital Drive, including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Also stating that
they look for ways to add additional landscape on the front side of the building. He also verified
that the dumpster works. Commissioner Everett seconded the motion.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.5 Public hearing and consideration of Method Studio’s request for conditional use
approval of the Dancing Moose Montessori School site plan, a commercial development
located at 2700 North Ashton Blvd.
    The applicant Michael Sevitt discussed the staggered drop off times to help with traffic.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Method Studio’s request for
conditional use approval of the Dancing Moose Montessori School site plan, a commercial
development located at 2700 North Ashton Blvd., including all DRC comments and findings
stated in the report. Also noting that a hard surface exception was granted for privacy concerns.
Commissioner Eyre seconded the motion.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.6 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of Development
Code Amendments to Chapter 12 and 26, adding requirements for storage containers on
residential property.
    Commissioners asked clarifying questions to the staff.
    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request for review of
Development Code Amendments to Chapter 12 and 26, adding requirements for storage containers
on residential property, including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Also, with
change that the lot sizes/zones TH-5, A-5, A-1, RA-1, and R-1-22 be restricted. Commissioner
Bunker seconded the motion.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.7 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code amendment to Table 37.070 adding a parking requirement for Short Term Rentals.
    Commissioners asked clarifying questions about parking requirements to the staff.
    Commissioner Nielsen was concerned about the 1:1 ratio for every room. He asked about the
    difference between someone who has 5 bedrooms with children compared to someone with 5
    bedrooms that is a more of a party house.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request for review
of a Development Code amendment to Table 37.070 adding a parking requirement for Short Term
Rentals, including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Ellis
seconded the motion.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed.

4. City Business
There was no city business to discuss.

5. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Hemmert moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Ellis seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 8:30 p.m.
