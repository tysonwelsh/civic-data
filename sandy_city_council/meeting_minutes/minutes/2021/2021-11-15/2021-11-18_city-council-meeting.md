City Council Meeting Minutes November 18, 2021

1. 21-461 The Sandy City Council, acting as the Sandy Board of Municipal
Canvassers, meeting to re-canvass certain returns for the 2021 General
Election following the required recount of the Mayoral election.

Attachments: Click here to eComment on this item

City Recorder Wendy Downs present the election results as recounted by Salt Lake
County on November 17, 2021.
The Chair opened the item to public comment:
Tane Wanlass expressed excitement to participate in meeting, praised Council Member
Zoltanski's efforts, and also encouraged the City to help increase awareness of meetings.
Zachary Frankel congratulated Council Member Zoltanksi and expressed appreciation for
the governing process.
Brent Allsup congratulated Council Member Zoltanski. He disagreed with the process of
the recount.
Kathy Walker congratulated the winners of the election, especially Council Member
Zoltanski.
Maddi Stufflebeam spoke about her relationship with Council Member Zoltanski and
anticipation for Council Member Zoltanski's administration.
Steve Van Maren congratulated Council Member Zoltanaski and Council Member
Robinson.
Terri Hrechosy congratulated Council Member Zoltanski. She also spoke to the
professionalism of Sherrie Swenson's administration of the election.
Jim Edwards congratulated all candidates who ran for office, and those who won. He
thanked the City Recorder's Office for their effort assisting candidates and residents.
Public Comment closed.
Council Member Sharkey raised concern about the recount process. Lynn Pace and
Tracy Cowdell spoke to the questions raised. Council discussion ensued.
Council Member Sharkey made a motion which was seconded by Council Member
Houseman to decline approval of the election results and that a full recount has not been
conducted as Sandy City is entitled to per statute and to request/order County Elections
to conduct a more complete recount. The vote was as followed:
Sharkey: yes
Houseman: yes
Robinson: no
Christensen: no
Stroud: no
Nicholl: no
Zoltanski: abstain
The motion failed.

City Council Meeting Minutes November 18, 2021
Council Member Robinson made a motion to accept and certify the results of the 2021
mayoral election which was seconded by Council Member Stroud. The vote was as
follows:

Robinson: yes
Stroud: yes
Nicholl: no
Zoltanski: abstain
Sharkey: no
Houseman: no
Christensen: yes
The motion failed.
Staff requested a brief pause regarding the reconsideration of motions.
Council Chair Stroud made a motion to reconsider Council Member Sharkey's original
motion. It was seconded by Council Member Christensen. The vote was as follows:
Stroud: yes
Christensen: yes
Nicholl: yes
Zoltanski: yes
Sharkey: yes
Houseman: yes
Robinson: yes
The motion to reconsider passed.
A vote was taken again on Council Member Sharkey's motion: Council Member Sharkey
made a motion which was seconded by Council Member Houseman to decline approval
of the election results and that a full recount has not been conducted as Sandy City is
entitled to per statute and to request/order County Elections to conduct a complete
recount and request the County to expedite that request so the Council can consider the
results at a special meeting next Monday. The vote was as follows:
Stroud: yes
Zoltanski: yes
Sharkey: yes
Houseman: yes
Robinson: no
Christensen: yes
Nicholl: yes
The motion passed.
The meeting adjourned.
