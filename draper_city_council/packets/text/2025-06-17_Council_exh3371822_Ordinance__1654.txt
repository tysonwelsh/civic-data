                                    ORDINANCE NO. 1654


AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLE 6-6 OF THE
DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE PERTAINING TO FIRE PREVENTION AND
LICENSES


        WHEREAS, Utah State Code 15A-5-202.5 gives municipalities the authority to restrict
the discharge of fireworks in certain parts of municipal boundaries; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City needs to ensure the restrictions on fireworks discharge are
properly conveyed to the public; and

      WHEREAS, the Fireworks Restrictions Map is subject to change and it is appropriate to
amend the map through ordinance rather than a code text amendment; and

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council finds good cause to amend DCMC Chapter 6-6;
and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed ordinance, and has found that the
amendments contained herein serve the best interests of the health safety and welfare of the City
of Draper.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following findings
regarding the amendment of Chapter 6-6 of the Draper City Municipal Code attached hereto as
Exhibit A: 1) There is a discrepancy between the map present in Chapter 6-6 and the map
approved by Ordinance 1582 which represent the desired boundaries of fireworks discharge; 2) it
is more efficient to amend the Fireworks Restrictions Map through ordinance rather than a code
text amendment; and 3) the required certificate of insurance monetary amounts are insufficient
and outdated. The proposed amendments serve the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare
of the citizens of Draper.

      Section 2. Amendment. Chapter 6-6 of the Draper City Municipal Code is hereby
amended to reflect the changes in the attached Exhibit A.

        Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to correct any
punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis errors prior to submitting the Ordinance
to Sterling Codifiers.

        Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.
       Section 5. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final
passage.




PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 3RD DAY OF JUNE, 2025.

6-6-010: DEFINITIONS:
All words used in this chapter shall mean the same as they are defined in Utah Code
Annotated 53-7-202, as amended. (Ord. 969, 5-3-2011)
6-6-020: SALE, DISCHARGE AND POSSESSION OF FIREWORKS RESTRICTED:
A. License Required: It is unlawful and shall constitute a class B misdemeanor for any
person to sell or offer for retail sale any fireworks without first obtaining a license from the
city and paying the required regulatory fee as set forth in the city's consolidated fee
schedule.
B. Permitted Fireworks: It is a class B misdemeanor for any person to sell, offer for retail
sale, discharge, or have in their possession any fireworks in Draper City, other than class
C common state approved explosives as authorized by Utah Code Annotated 53-7-201
et seq., as amended, the rules adopted by the Utah fire prevention board pursuant
thereto, and this chapter, except as otherwise permitted by state law.
C. Discharge Distance To Structures: It shall be unlawful to discharge any fireworks
within twenty feet (20') of any residence, dwelling or other structure.
D. Prohibited Discharge In Specified Areas: Due to perennial extreme hazardous fire
conditions in certain portions of Draper City, the discharge of fireworks is prohibited east
of 1300 East Street, south of 13800 South Street, west of Interstate 15, and in the area
from 12300 South running north along 1000 East Street to the north city boundary, then
following the city boundary west to 700 East Street, then south to the UTA TRAX line,
then following the TRAX line to 12300 South Street, to be known as the Mehraban
Wetlands area, from the Wetlands Area following the TRAX line east to 1300 East Street,
all as shown on the following map: City Council in consultation with the Draper City fire
chief, shall determine boundaries where fireworks are prohibited based on historical
hazardous environmental conditions. A map showing the boundaries for discharge of
fireworks in Draper City with any changes shall be published by June 1st of each calendar
year, and shall be maintained for public inspection at City Hall.
E. Displays Require Permit: It is unlawful and shall constitute a class B misdemeanor
for any person to discharge display fireworks, special effects, or flame effects
performances, without first receiving a Draper City permit therefor. Evidence shall also be
provided that the display operator, special effects operator, or flame effects operator who
will set up and discharge the display has received a license from the state fire marshal,
division department of public safety. The operator shall also provide a certificate of
insurance naming the city as an additional insured in the minimum amount of two million
dollars ($2,000,000.00) three million dollars ($3,000,000) for flame effects or five million
dollars ($5,000,000) for firework displays and special effects. and which names the city
as an additional insured.
F. Sky Lanterns And Similar Devices Prohibited: It shall be unlawful and shall constitute
a class B misdemeanor for any person to discharge or launch a "sky lantern" or similar
type of unmanned fire. "Sky lantern" means a small unmanned hot air balloon with an
opening at the bottom where a fire is suspended. "Sky lanterns" are sometimes called
"Chinese lanterns", "sky candles" or "fire balloons" and usually consist of a wire frame
with a paper shell and a candle or other fuel, by which the burning candle or fuel creates
hot air that makes the lantern float and glow. (Ord. 1052, 6-18-2013; amd. Ord. 1444, 7-
7-2020)
Editor’s Note:
  Ordinance 1582, adopted June 6, 2023, temporarily supersedes division (D) of this

6-6-030: APPLICATION FOR LICENSE TO SELL FIREWORKS:
A. Requirements: All applications for a license to sell fireworks shall:
1. Set forth the proposed location where the fireworks are to be sold and whether the
location is a temporary stand, trailer, tent, or permanent structure;
2. Be accompanied by certificates of insurance evidencing:
a. Liability coverage in the minimum amounts of two hundred thousand dollars/four
hundred thousand dollars ($200,000.00/$400,000.00), property damage coverage in the
minimum amount of two hundred thousand dollars ($200,000.00) with each naming the
city as an additional insured;
b. Products liability coverage in a minimum amount of five hundred thousand dollars
($500,000.00);
c. A statement that Draper City will be given ten (10) days' written notice prior to any
cancellation of the insurance by the insurance company; and
3. Be accompanied by a copy of the applicant's Utah sales tax license.
B. Time To File Application: Applications shall be made at least three (3) business days
prior to when the applicant wishes to begin selling fireworks within the time constraints
set forth in state law. (Ord. 969, 5-3-2011)
6-6-040: POSTING OF LICENSE AND MAP:
A. License Display Required: Licenses issued pursuant to this chapter shall be posted
in a conspicuous place in a temporary stand, trailer or tent, and in a conspicuous place in
the vicinity of the fireworks in a permanent structure.
B. Presentation Upon Request: The city license shall be available for presentation upon
request to duly authorized officials.
C. Map Display Required: A map showing the boundaries for discharge of fireworks in
Draper City shall be posted at all retail fireworks locations. (Ord. 969, 5-3-2011)
6-6-050: ENFORCEMENT:
A. Responsibility Of Officials: Every officer charged with the enforcement of state and
city laws, including the fire marshal, is hereby charged with responsibility to enforce this
chapter.
B. Seizure And Destruction; Revocation Of License: Fireworks sold or offered for sale in
violation of this chapter may be seized and destroyed and the license of the person selling
or offering fireworks for sale may be revoked.
C. Violation; Nonrenewal: Any violation of this chapter may serve as a basis for
nonrenewal of a license in future years. (Ord. 969, 5-3-2011)
6-6-060: TEMPORARY STANDS, TRAILERS, AND TENTS:
A. Building Code Applicability: Temporary stands, trailers, and tents used for the retail
sale of fireworks shall meet the requirements of the latest edition of the building code
adopted by the city. Each licensee shall pay the inspection fee and each licensed
premises shall be inspected prior to any sales.
B. Removal: Temporary fireworks stands, trailers, and tents shall be removed within
seven (7) days after the fireworks sales period has ended. (Ord. 969, 5-3-2011)
