Proposal 
On July 29, the Community Development Department received a plat amendment application 
requesting approval to vacate approximately 2 acres from Lot  5 in the Wilderness Acres # 15 
Subdivision and add that acreage to Lot 39 in the Wilderness Acres #20 Subdivision. Parcel WA-
15-5 is owned by Poll Enterprise, LLC and currently contains approximately 6.97 acres. Parcel 
WA-20-39 is owned by Lynn D. and Kathleen B. Poll and currently contains approximately 1.91 
acres. The purpose of the amendment is to adjust the common boundary between the two 
parcels. The plat amendment would not create any additional lots. 
 
Project Description 
Project Name: Lynn and Kathleen Poll Plat Amendment   
Applicant(s): Lynn D. Poll

Location: 4363 Highline Rd.; 4518 Alpine Circle

Background 
The Wilderness Acres #20 Subdivision was legally established through a Summit County land -
division process recorded under Entry No. 147127. Lot 39, owned by Lynn D. and Kathleen B. 
Poll, consists of approximately 1.91 acres and is developed with an existing residence. The 
Wilderness Acres #15 Subdivision was legally established through a Summit County land -
division process recorded under Entry No. 147122. Lot 5, owned by Poll Enterprises LLC, 
consists of approximately 6.97 acres and is currently undeveloped. 
 
The applicant is requesting approval to vacate approximately 2 acres from Lot  5 in the 
Wilderness Acres #15 Subdivision and add that acreage to 39 in the Wilderness Acres #20 
Subdivision. Parcel WA-20-39 is currently 1.91 acres and Parcel WA-15-5 is 6.97 acres. Following 
approval of this plat amendment, Parcel WA-20-39 would increase to approximately 3.91 acres, 
and Parcel WA-15-5 would be reduced to approximately 4.97 acres. 
 
Under Section 11-4-5 of th e Eastern Summit County  Development Code, the Eastern Summit 
County Planning Commission serves as the Land Use Authority for any amendment to a 
recorded subdivision plat involving a lot  vacation. Community Development staff, in 
coordination with applicable service providers, has reviewed the application and determined 
that the project complies with all relevant requirements of the Eastern Summit County 
Development Code. 
 
Analysis and Findings 
Chapter 4 – Subdivision Plat Amendment (11-4-5) 
Public Notice, Meetings and Comments  
The project was publicly noticed as a public hearing and potential action item before the 
Eastern Summit County  Planning Commission ( ESCPC). The notice was published in the Park 
Record and all required locations. Courtesy postcards were mailed to property owners within 
1,000 feet of Parcel WA-20-39 and WA-15-5. As of the date of this Staff Report, no public 
comments have been received by Planning Staff. 
 
Recommendation 
Staff has reviewed the application for compliance with all standards in the Eastern Summit 
County Development Code and found that it meets the requirements for approval.  Staff 
recommend that the ESCPC review the proposed  lot vacation , conduct a  public hearing, and 
approve the  application, pursuant to the following findings of fact, conclusions of law , and 
conditions of approval.

additional lot into the subdivision, located at 1300 W Hobson Ln (Parcel RMTN-A), Hoytsville,

Project Name:   Rees Mountain Subdivision Re-Plat Preliminary Subdivision 
Applicant(s):   Kathy Kercher

Raymond Rees and Son Land and Livestock Inc. (Parcel RMTN-A)

Zone District:   AG-10 and AG-20 
Parcel Number and Size: RMTN- 1 (6 acres) and RMTN-A (24 acres) 
Type of Process:   Administrative 
Final Land Use Authority: Eastern Summit County Planning Commission (Preliminary

Background 
 
The existing Rees Mountain Subdivision was recorded on June 21, 2024 (Entry #1221567) and 
consists of 1-lot and a Remainder Parcel contained within. The subdivision is located in the AG-
10 (10.77 acres – 1.077 units of density) and AG-20 Zone (19.23 acres – 0.9615 units of density). 
The subdivision is currently being utilized for grazing on the steeper slopes and cultivated for 
alfalfa on flatter areas.

2 
 
 
Vicinity Map  
 
 
Analysis and Findings 
 
Summit County Ordinance 983 (Exhibit D) updated subdivision requirements in the Eastern 
Summit County Development Code (Code). Ord 983 is still pending codification in the online 
code but will be in Section 11-4-5. Per Ordinance 983; If a Preliminary Subdivision complies with 
the applicable standards and specifications, the Planning Commission shall approve the 
preliminary Subdivision Plat. Additionally, the Ordinance states; Because of the limited time 
frame for preliminary review, compliance with standards may be written in the form of 
conditions of approval to be met during the Subdivision Plan Review part of the Final Subdivision 
Plat. Staff have reviewed the application for compliance below. 
 
Chapter 4 – Lot/Parcel Requirements for Development, Subdivision, and/or Uses 
For development purposes and in order to apply for development, a parcel must be a 
Conforming Parcel. 
 
Analysis: Parcel RMTN-A was created as a legal Remainder Parcel that is eligible for 
future development should the zoning densities allow for it. The subdivision is located in 
the AG-10 (10.77 acres – 1.077 units of density) and AG-20 Zone (19.23 acres – 0.9615 
units of density) which is sufficient for two units of density overall and therefore would 
comply with the Code definition of a Conforming Parcel. COMPLIES 
 
Subdivision Review Standards 
Per Ordinance 983 of the Code, the proposed subdivision shall comply with the following;  
 
Standard 1: The Subdivision is compliant with the Eastern Summit County General Plan.  
 
12
3 
 
  
Analysis: The Eastern Summit County General Plan Section 2.1 Policy C states the 
following: Work to ensure that single-family residential development minimizes 
disturbance and is clustered where appropriate. The proposed additional lot will be 
within an existing residential subdivision and clustered with the existing residential lot. 
COMPLIES 
 
Standard 2: The Subdivision is designed to fit into the topography of the site. Lots shall be sited 
to best fit into the natural terrain, to minimize excessive site grading and to mitigate impacts on 
the natural environment and resources of the surrounding area. The subdivision layout shall 
demonstrate the preservation of watercourses, drainage areas, wooded areas, rough terrain 
and similar natural feature areas.  
 
Analysis: The proposed lot slopes West to East with slopes significantly less than 30% 
and no sensitive natural feature areas which. This will help minimize excessive grading. 
The remainder of Parcel RMTN-A would still be utilized for alfalfa. COMPLIES 
 
Standard 3: The Subdivision considers adjacent land uses. Site design shall mitigate any 
potential adverse impacts including but not limited to flooding, erosion, subsidence, sloping of 
the soil or other dangers or nuisances.  
 
Analysis: The adjacent uses are agricultural and residential. Appropriate utility 
easements are shown on the subdivision plat. Staff and service providers have not 
identified any adverse impacts related to flooding, erosion, subsidence, sloping of the 
soil, or other dangers and nuisances. COMPLIES AS CONDITIONED 
 
Standard 4: Where appropriate, subdivisions shall be designed to accommodate a variety of 
transportation modes. Vehicle/pedestrian/bicycle/equestrian circulation shall be demonstrated, 
and where possible pedestrian/bicycle/equestrian circulation should be separated from 
vehicular circulation.  
   
Analysis: Hobson Ln does not have any existing pedestrian/bicycle facilities or 
amenities. All lots will be accessed via a private driveway shown on the plat. Given the 
very low-density area the subdivision is located in, there is little opportunity for multi-
modal transportation. COMPLIES 
 
Standard 5: Each proposed lot shall have legal access through a recorded Right-Of-Way or 
easement. The Applicant shall demonstrate that adequate access to the property from a public 
road may be granted by the State or County, whichever is applicable.  
 
Analysis: All lots will be accessed via a private driveway shown on the plat. COMPLIES 
AS CONDITIONED

this title.
