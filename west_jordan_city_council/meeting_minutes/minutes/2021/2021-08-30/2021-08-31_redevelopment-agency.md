REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair Jacob called the meeting to order at 7:03 pm
2. CLOSED MEETING
At 7:04 pm, Board Member Kelvin Green moved that the Redevelopment Agency Board recess
to convene in a closed meeting to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property as
provided by Utah Code Annotated §52-4-205 and adjourn from there.
Board Member Kayleen Whitelock seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Council Chair Zach Jacob                   Yes
Council Vice Chair Kelvin Green            Yes
Council Member Chad Lamb                   Yes
Council Member Chris McConnehey            absent
Council Member David Pack                  Yes
Council Member Kayleen Whitelock           Yes
Council Member Melissa Worthen             absent
The motion passed 5-0

Those present during the closed session included Board Members Kelvin Green, Chad Lamb, David
Pack, Kayleen Whitelock, Melissa Worthen (via Zoom), and Chair Zach Jacob, Mayor Dirk Burton, City
Administrator Korban Lee, and City Attorney Rob Wall, Economic Development Director Chris Pengra
and Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson.

Board Members discussed the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property as follows:
Item #1: 7:05 pm to 7:48 pm

Board Member David Pack left the meeting at 7:14 pm and rejoined via Zoom at the same time.
