Proposal 
Henefer Town is proposing a diversion from the Weber River for secondary irrigation water. 
Water will be funneled from the Weber through an underground water pipe in the S. Henefer 
Rd. right of way (ROW) to a dam and storage reservoir located on Parcel NS-836-X. The project 
begins within the Town boundaries around Interstate 84 and goes into the unincorporated 
County where Old Ditch Rd. meets S. Henefer Rd.

Background 
Currently, Henefer residents use culinary water for irrigation. Because of water shortage 
concerns the Town had a moratorium on new building permits from 2018 through November 
2024. Henefer Town proposes a secondary water system that includes an underground water 
pipe, dam, and storage reservoir to free up the culinary water supply. The project straddles 
Henefer Town and the unincorporated County. Any proposed work within the unincorporated 
County is within Henefer’s Annexation Declaration Area.  
 
The dam, access to the dam, and most of the reservoir are proposed within the unincorporated 
County on Parcel NS-836-X, which is owned by the Town of Henefer. The proposed reservoir 
will hold about 3.5-acre feet of water at capacity. The dam will be accessed from Peaks Rd. by a 
proposed 12 ft. wide gravel road with 3’ shoulders. Peaks Rd. is a private road. A small portion 
of the reservoir will extend into Parcel PEAKS-AG, which is within Henefer Town.  
 
The water pipe will run from NS-836-X into the ROW of Peaks Rd. on an unnamed Parcel that 
includes the Henefer Cemetery. Then the pipe will follow the ROW of S. Henefer Rd. where it 
will cross into the Henefer Town boundary along Old Ditch Rd. on Parcel HT-142. Both S. 
Henefer Rd. and Old Ditch Rd. are County roads. The pipe will follow the Weber River within the 
Town along Parcels HT-142 and HT-142-B where it will terminate at a pumphouse on parcel HT-
846-1-X. Only work within the unincorporated County is subject to this Conditional Use Permit 
(CUP).
  
 
The proposed dam is classified in the Eastern Summit County Development Code (Code) as a 
Utility Structure and Related Facilities. The water pipe is considered an Underground 
transmission line exceeding 12 inches diameter. Both uses are considered Conditional Uses in 
the AG-5 Zone.  
 
Construction of the project began without appropriate County permits, and Summit County 
Code Enforcement has instructed all construction related to this CUP to stop until appropriate 
permits have been obtained. Additionally, Henefer intends to annex Parcel NS-836-X and the 
unnamed Parcel into the municipal boundaries. A complete annexation petition was received 
on April 4, 2025 for both properties (Exhibit F). 
 
The purpose of this CUP is to
 allow construction to begin again as soon as possible for the 
benefit of County and Henefer residents while the annexation is ongoing. Additionally, a 
portion of the project, particularly the water pipe along S. Henefer Rd., will remain in the 
unincorporated County even after the annexation is approved, therefore, the CUP is still 
necessary.  
 
This project was first reviewed by the ESCPC on April 17, 2025 where a public hearing and 
discussion was held. The ESCPC recognized the importance of the infrastructure project for the 
Town and hoped to move the project along quickly. However, there was insufficient 
documentation to show the potential risks to County infrastructure and residences if the dam 
3

were to break, overflow, or need to be drained. Without the documentation to outline the 
potential risks, the ESCPC was unable to condition the project in a way that could mitigate

applicant time to provide additional information. The applicants did not provide the required
