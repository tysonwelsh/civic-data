BACKGROUND: Council previously approved via Ordinance NO. 1416 the vacation of a portion of
real property known as the 12100 South public right-of-way in front of Parcel# 28-30-283-002. When
this action was recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office, instead of recording as a separate
parcel, they inadvertently combined to the existing parcel, thus creating a new Parcel# 28-30-283-004.
This resolution provides a mechanism to receive payment for the vacated portion of right-of-way that SL
County Recorder’s Office added in the creation of the new parcel.
