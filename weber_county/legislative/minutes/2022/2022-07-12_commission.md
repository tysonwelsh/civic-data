G. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. WEBER COUNTY BOARD OF CANVAS - CERTIFICATION OF RESULTS OF THE JUNE 28, 2022
       2022 PRIMARY ELECTION.
         Lauren Shafer (Elections Director): 68,826 ballots sent to active, registered voters; total number of votes cast
         was 29,533, about 42.9%. By-mail votes were the largest form of returns. Of all the returned ballots, 375
         ballots had issues where 175 were corrected through mail contact. 200 ballots could not be not counted; 140
         signatures did not match voter records, 43 ballots were unsigned, and 17 ballots were signed by someone other
         than the voter. 52 provisional ballots cast, of which 45 were counted; 7 were wrong party affiliation. 2018
         Republican turnout was 54%, this year was 41%; non-partisan turnout in 2018 was 14%, this year was 17%;
         by-mail and drop box returns in 2018 was 94%, this year was 97%; in person turnout in 2018 was 5%, this
         year it was 2%; undeliverable ballot rate in 2018 was 3%, this year was 1.4%.
         Jim Owens, Riverdale 4 Precinct chair: Will these numbers be available to the public?
         Ms. Shafer: Yes, they will be posted today after the meeting.
         Chair Jenkins: To Ms. Winham’s question, she said when the machines were turned on, the monitor instantly
         showed a count.
         Ms. Shafer: We actually have the full election period to begin scanning ballots. We started to count some of
         them the Friday before which is why those vote totals were in there. So the numbers she viewed were from
         ballots already received by mail or in a drop box. While no election observers were present for that count, the
         schedule was posted at least one week in advance of what our processes were each day.
         Ms. Winham: This conflicts with what was told by Ricky to Daniel Badal. It doesn’t seem wise practice to
         count with no one to witness.
         Chair Jenkins: Were any votes loaded when times were not open (to witness)?
         Ms. Shafer: No, we did everything according to our processing schedule, per statute posted ahead of time.
         Commissioner Harvey: You and your team are to be commended with regards to what goes on. I would like
         to see a little friendlier way to navigate the election website. It makes sense to me to load as the statute
         allows.
         Mr. Owens: How long would it have taken to wait to process those less than 15k votes? And is there really
         an advantage to processing early?
         Ms. Shafer: We never know what our volume is going to be. We process early to ensure that the votes that
         come in between the weekend and election day can be processed in time to post results Tuesday at 8pm.
         Waiting to count those 15k votes take about an hour to count but a whole day to get through all the ballot
         processing steps.

         As the Weber County Board of Canvassers, Commissioner Froerer moved to certify the results of the June 28,
         2022 Weber County Board of Canvas - for the 2022 Primary Election; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Weber County Board of Canvassers: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins
         – aye
