D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Bolos: This is an exciting time with all the high school graduations going
on. I thought to look back at the years my parents graduated. My dad graduated from Ogden High in 1961 and my
mother from Ben Lomond High School in 1962. I looked up information dating back to those dates I thought I would
share. When my dad graduated, gas was $0.27 a gallon, eggs were $0.30 a dozen, bacon, which I love, was $0.67 a
pound, and a house cost $12,500. The next year when my mom graduated, gas had gone up a penny, eggs went up two
cents, there was no information on bacon, a Ford Cortina, whatever that is, was $591, and house prices were the same.
I look back on this and I thought things had changed a lot from the time they graduated to the time I graduated. But
what has not changed is the optimism of those graduates when they get out of high school with their whole lives ahead
of them and opportunities they may not even know about yet. I wish those graduates the best of luck in their lives and
their many successes.

     1. WEBER COUNTY TAX REVIEW COMMITTEE RECOMMENDATION ON PARCEL #04-040-
        0043- OWNER: ESPINOZA.
            Dakota Wurth (Clerk/Auditor’s Office): The state mandates the county annually hold a tax sale for properties
            that have delinquent taxes five years in arears. We also afford a Tax Review Committee to review
            circumstances for second and additional extensions. This first parcel had a change in ownership with a
            demonstrable effort and actualization in paying off delinquent taxes. There is evidence of an active loan in
            process to pay the taxes in the near future. The Tax Review Committee voted 3-1 to recommend a third
            extension for this parcel.

            Commissioner Bolos: I know there are three properties you are working with him on, with him getting two
            properties up to compliance. That effort shows that in good faith he will take care of the third one.

            Chair Froerer: I want to thank the Tax Review Committee to analyze these and for their work on these. It
            appears that parts are in place to bring this to fruition.
