Angel Castillo addressed the conversation that took place in tonight’s work session regarding proposed amendments to
the City’s manufacturing zones; as a former Planning Commissioner, she understands how important land use regulations are and
former Planning Manager Montgomery once told her to think of a use change as a ‘forever change’ because once a use is allowed,
it will remain in place for at least 30 to 50 years. She stated that there were some matters presented to the Council tonight that
deserve more time and consideration; she is concerned that these types of recommendations are made by City Administration at
the last minute with a sense of urgency. She agreed with many of the comments made by the Council during the work session
about the need to preserve private property rights and she was crushed that during the discussion, the Community and Economic
Development staff never mentioned local/existing businesses and that is because their focus is on bringing in outside businesses
and industry, which will stress the City’s natural resources and challenge its housing needs. There are already many small
businesses in the community that could use the kind of support Community and Economic Development is offering to outsiders.
Linda Sanchez stated tonight’s work session was great and she appreciated the Council standing up for their constituents
and local businesses. It was the best work session that she has listened to, and she thanked the Council for keeping checks and
balances in mind. She looks forward to hearing the next discussion of the issue. She also congratulated those individuals who were
successful in the recent Primary Election.

Council member Comments
: Council member Hyer referenced flags that have been placed around the Municipal Building block and noted they were

Council member White thanked City Recorder Hansen and the staff at the County Clerk’s Office for administering a ,
smooth Primary Election; their attention to detail is impressive and Ms. Hansen’s office should be considered a model for others.
Vice Chair Richey echoed Council member White’s comments and stated that the efforts Ms. Hansen and the Weber
County Clerk’s Office expend to ensure all precautions are in place to preserve election integrity are impressive. He also

not moving forward to the General Election for their efforts.
Council member Nadolski also thanked all candidates for all seats in the City. He specifically acknowledged Council
member Blair and highlighted the class with which he has conducted his campaign.
Council member Hyer thanked Ms. Sanchez for her positive comments. He stated that each Council member is serving
because they love Ogden, and it is nice to be recognized in a positive manner for their service.
