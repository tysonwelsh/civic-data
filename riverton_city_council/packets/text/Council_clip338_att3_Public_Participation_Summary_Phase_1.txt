Mapping Our Places
         Summary
     OVERVIEW
     From October through December 2019, an online mapping tool has
     collected input on our Southwest Vision Website. The interactive
     tool allowed participants to drag and drop “pins” on a map of the
     study area, providing a unique look at the places in Southwest Salt
     Lake County that we love, the places we see potential for change,
     and the places that need improvement. While this mapping tool
     was available, we received a total of 95 pins. Participants marked
     a total of 25 places they love, 34 places that need improvement,
     and 31 areas that have opportunities for change.

     Many participants identified open space, parks, and recreation
     that they love. This aligns closely with the online questionnaire
     results and interviews with stakeholders during this process, with
     many residents stating that open space and recreation amenities
     are among their favorite aspects of their community.

     Opportunity areas largely clustered at key intersections and
     along major corridors with comments that suggest there is
     room for redevelopment and infill potential, especially near strip
     malls. Several pins were placed in areas that resident see future
     employment opportunities and recreational trail connections as
     well.

     Of the input for places that need improvement, nearly all comment
     were placed on main east/west roads and at busy intersections,
     asking for traffic congestion solutions. Some of specific ideas
     that were presented included: an east/west freeway, more public
     transportation, and flex lanes. Many comments pointed out
     locations for major arterials to connect to Mountain View Corridor
     and some called for better intersections to improve safety for
     pedestrians, cyclists, and vehicles.

     The comments shown have been modified slightly in an effort to
     clarify and consolidate.
