 VARIANCES
 B-6-2024                     Randy Debenham, is requesting a variance from Section 7-6-202 of the City Code. This
                              section requires that setbacks for accessory buildings in the A (Agriculture Zone) be 10 feet.
                              The applicant is seeking a 6.5-foot setback on the south property line and a 5-foot variance on
                              the east property line. The property is located at 2565 South Fargo Street and is zoned A.
                              (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)


 NON CONFORMING USES
 B-4-2024   Mohamed Ahmed, is requesting consideration to enlarge an existing non-conforming building.
            The request before the Board is to allow an addition to the existing single-family dwelling that
            has been in existence since 1999. The proposed addition will be a new dwelling unit that
            complies with provisions of the R-2-8 zone. The property is located at 2919 West 2730 South
            and is zoned R-2-8. (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

 B-5-2024                     Randy Debenham, is requesting non-conforming use determination regarding a second single-
                              family dwelling on property located at 2565 South Fargo Street. The applicant is requesting
                              that the Board determine the non-conforming status of the second dwelling located to the rear
                              of the original dwelling. The property is located at 2565 South Fargo Street and is zoned A.
                              (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)
