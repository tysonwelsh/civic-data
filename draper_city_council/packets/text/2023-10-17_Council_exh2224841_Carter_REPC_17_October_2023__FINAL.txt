                           REAL ESTATE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Real Estate Purchase Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the _____ day
of October 2023 by and among DRAPER CITY, a Utah municipal corporation, 1020 E. Pioneer
Road, Draper, UT 84020 (“City” or “Seller”), and LEROY S. CARTER, an individual Utah limited
(“Buyer”), 690 E 12100 S, Draper, UT 84020.

                                              RECITALS

WHEREAS, Buyer is offering to purchase property owned by Draper City for $21,526.56; and

WHEREAS, the property measures approximately 0.055 acres (2,391.84 sq. ft.); and

WHEREAS, the City previously vacated, by Ordinance No. 1416, this property as public right-of-
way; and

WHEREAS, the City finds under the terms of this Agreement the City will be receiving fair value for
such property by accepting the offer of $21,526.56.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and the agreements set forth below,
as well as the mutual benefits to be derived from this Agreement, the parties hereto agree as follows:

                                                TERMS

1. Sale and Purchase: The Draper City Council has declared the Property as surplus and has
authorized the sale of the Property to the Buyer, and Buyer agrees to purchase, acquire, and receive
from the City, the Property described in Exhibit A, all subject to and upon the terms and conditions of
this Agreement. Buyer accepts the Property in “as-is” condition.

2. Purchase Price: The total “Purchase Price” to be paid by the Buyer for the acquisition of the
Property shall be $21,526.56 and shall be paid in one lump sum at closing. Closing shall take place no
later than fifteen (15) business days following the mutual execution of this Agreement by Buyer and
Seller. Seller agrees to make final conveyance at closing by Warranty Deed.

3. Water: No water rights or shares are included in this Agreement.

4. Non-transferability: This Agreement is non-transferable, except to an entity or trust owned or
under common control with, or established by, Buyer, or to Buyer’s principal.

5. Taxes: Buyer shall be responsible for any and all taxes levied against the Property after the recorded
conveyance of the Property to Buyer.
