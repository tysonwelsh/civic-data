A ORDINANCE AMENDING THE CURRENT APPLICABLE CODE TO REPEAL THE
STORM WATER FACILITES IMPACT FEE
RECITALS

A. Utah State Code section 10-2a-414 provides that when a metro township is
incorporated that each county ordinance that is in effect on the day that metro township is
incorporated will remain in effect as a metro township ordinance until the metro township
council amends or repeals the ordinance.

B. The Township is currently updating and readopting portions of the effective
county code to apply specifically to the Township.

C. It has been recommended by staff of the service district and the County that the
provisions in the current code found in Title 17; Chapter 17.20; Article VII regarding storm
water facilities impact fees should be repealed for the Township.

ORDINANCE

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY ORDAINED, by the Council of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township:

I. The current code Title 17; Chapter 17.20; Article VII Storm Facilities Impact Fees is hereby
repealed in its entirety.

II. This ordinance shall take effect upon publication and posting as required in Utah Code 10-3-
711.

Ill. The recorder shall give this ordinance a number, if the Township Council has not already so
done.

IV. Immediately following this ordinance, the recorder shall make or cause to be made a
certificate stating the date of passage and of the date of publication or posting, as required.
