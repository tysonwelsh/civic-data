BUSINESS MEETING
Meeting began at – 8:31 a.m.
1) Emily Plewe is requesting a front yard fence fronting Emigration Canyon Road. The fence exceeds
the limits set forth in the ordinance, which the planning commission has authority to approve.
Location: 5798 East Emigration Canyon Road. Planner: Jim Nakamura (Discussion only)
Ms. Plewe advised she bought this property two years ago and has since started landscaping.
Commissioners, staff, and Ms. Plewe had a brief discussion regarding her plans, snow plowing,
rocks, option B proposal favored, and height. Deferred public hearing to the July 10th planning
commission meeting as an action item with a staff report.
2) Approval of the March 13, 2025, Planning Commission Meeting Minutes. (Motion/Voting)
Motion: To approve March 13, 2025, Planning Commission Meeting Minutes as amended.
Motion by: Commissioner Karkut
2nd by: Commissioner Wallace
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimously in favor (of commissioners present)
3) Emigration Canyon Planning Commission and Staff will continue discussion on updates to Title 19.
Topics may include: The path and timeline toward adoption of Title 19 in 2025, Fencing, Tree and
Vegetation Protection, Wildlife Habitat Protection, and Traffic Studies. Planner: Brian Tucker
