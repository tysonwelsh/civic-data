CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1205-0, AN ORDINANCE AMENDING
THE WASHINGTON COUNTY CODE MODIFYING VARIOUS TITLE 10 ZONING

REGULATIONS, INCLUDING DEFINITIONS, ESTABLISHING HEARING OFFICERS
APPEAL PROVISIONS, ADDING UTILITY REQUIREMENTS FOR ZONING

DETERMINATIONS, ADDING NON-OWNER OCCUPIED PLANNED

DEVELOPMENT __— SHORT __TERM__ RENTAL __ PROVISIONS, _ REVISING

ENFORCEMENT PROVISIONS, AND OTHER MISCELLANEOUS CHANGES TO
TITLE 10 AND TITLE 9 TO CONFORM WITH THE NEW PROVISIONS

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the County desires to improve
provisions in the Title 10 10 zoning regulations including various definitions. New provisions
include establishing hearing officers, and procedures to hear and determine appeals from
administrative decisions of the land use authority, variance appeals, and appeals from decisions of
the building department, the building official or fee appeals under Title 9 or Title 10 of county
regulations.

Additional changes require that a zone change be compatible with infrastructure to more accurately
and uniformly match zones to the availability of water, sewer, fire, and power at the zone change
location.

An additional zone will be added to Title 10 called Planned Development Short Term Rental in
order to enable the short-term rental of non-owner occupied residences in specified areas
appropriate for the zone, and in a manner that will not have a negative impact on surrounding
property and residences. The Commissioners requested some time ago to allow non-owner
occupied short term rentals in some circumstances, and this accomplishes it. Existing regulations
regarding short-term rental appeals and inspections, as well as the enforcement and penalty
provisions, also needed some changes

Deputy Attorney Victoria Hales stated that there was language that needed to be altered at lines
513 and 516. Striking ‘proof of and instead saying ‘Evidence acceptable to the County’ and
‘Evidence shall include the following’. These changes can be approved as the final draft of the
proposed Title 9 and Title 10 revisions.

TWO PROFESSIONAL SERVICE AGREEMENTS FOR TORTOISE SURVEYS TO BE
CONDUCTED IN ZONE 6

Mr. Rognan explained that Zone 6 was created in the HCP renewal by the expansion of the Red
Cliffs Desert Reserve. The area is west of St. George near the Bloomington area and consists of

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2931, A RESOLUTION FORMALLY
RECOMMENDING ANNEXATION OF A COUNTY PENINSULA INTO ADJACENT
HURRICANE CITY PURSUANT TO UTAH CODE ANN. 10-2-418(8)(C)

Ms. Hales explained that there is a peninsula of County land surrounded on three sides by
Hurricane, and the city has expressed an intent to seek annexation. The area is approximately 470
acres and encompasses Adobe Hills, Cliffdweller Ranch, Copper Cliffs, Grassy Meadows Sky
Ranch, and Hurricane Cliff Estates subdivisions. Without annexation of the peninsula, future
development of the area would be limited, due to the County being unable to provide the types of
services that would be better provided by a municipal entity. Considering the facts, circumstances,
and location, the County formally recommends the annexation under state law.

01/21/2022 @ 1930, Stranded Persons — General Steam - Man Hours — 22

SAR responded to assist National Forest Service on two subjects stranded in a Jeep on
General Steam Road. They were in an area with no cell or radio service. The Ranger and
SAR attempted several times to get the subjects back to Enterprise Reservoir. SAR
finally took the two subjects back to Enterprise. Their vehicle was left on General Steam

Road.

01/22/2022 @ 1530, Stranded Motorist — General Steam — Man Hours — 27

SAR responded to two subjects stranded in a Toyota Tacoma on General Steam Road.
The subjects had to hike over a mile to get cell signal to call for help. SAR located them
in almost the exact location of the rescue the day prior. SAR work to get them back to the
paved roadway. They were released. No EMS needed.
01/29/2022 @ 1420, Injured Mtn Biker, LaVerkin Twist Trail — Man Hours — 42

SAR was dispatched to an injured Mountain Biker on the LaVerkin Twist Trail. SAR,
EMS, and LaVerkin PD located the subject. He had potential head and neck injuries. He
was taken to a UTV and driven back to the IC. He was taken by ambulance to SGRH.

01/29/2022 @ 1700, Climber Fall, Virgin River Gorge — Man Hours — 48

SAR responded to a 45 year old male climber that fell about 40 feet. SAR hiked into the
canyon and located the injured male. They stabilized the subject. The Arizona DPS
helicopter arrived and hoisted the subject out. He was transferred to Mercy Air and
flown to SGRH for treatment.

02/02/2022 @ 1420, Injured Mtn Biker, Desert Canyon Area — Man Hours — 24

SAR, SGPD and Gold Cross responded to a report of a Mountain Biker that crashed on a
trail. EMS and SAR worked on the 75 year old male and found he was having a medical
issue. They loaded him into a UTV and took him to an ambulance. He was taken to
SGRH. Unfortunately the male didn’t survive.

02/04/2022 @ 1715, Injured Hiker, Scout Cave Trail — Man Hours — 26

SAR was called to a 12 year old female that fell 8 feet while hiking the Scout Trail. SAR,
SGFD, and Gold Cross hiked in. She was located and stabilize with a suspected leg
fracture. She was carried to a UTV and driven out to the IC. She was taken to SGRH for

treatment.

03/11/2022 @ 2000 — Lost Hikers — Water Canyon — Man Hours - 72

SAR responded to two lost hikers on Canaan Mountain. The hikers said they were lost,
getting cold, and had no way to light a fire. SAR units hiked up Water Canyon and use
UTV’s from the Coral Pink area. They located the hikers. They were loaded into UTV’s
are driven back to the Incident Command. They denied EMS and were released.

03/12/2022 @ 1700 — Lost Persons — Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 20

SAR Members responded to a couple who had gotten lost in Sand Mountain area in their
Jeep. Incident Command was established at the water tanks. Several SAR members
responded from Incident Command to locate the individuals. The individuals were
located in their vehicle and guided back to incident command. Both individuals both
refused medical attention. All SAR members were accounted for.
