Request
 With the permission from the property owners
 (Metro California, LLC), the applicant, Betsy Bolivar,
 is requesting a conditional use permit for a bilingual
 daycare center at the property located at 8029 S. 700
 E. The request is to convert the vacant building into
 a childcare facility within the Boulevard Commercial
 (BC) zone (applicant letter, Exhibit “A”).

Background
 The subject property is located on the east side of 700
 East Street, a major arterial state highway. This is a
 single-story vacant building that was originally built
 in 1981, with a second building added in 1999 and
 created two separate lots. The entire site area is a net
 total of 18,871 square feet, with the vacant building
 compromising 2,095 square feet of net space.

 Properties directly west are zoned BC. The property
 directly behind the proposed daycare is an office
 building. To the northeast and southeast are homes in
 the R-1-8 zone and used as single-family residences.

Analysis
The applicant is proposing to utilize the space for a daycare center.
That use is a conditional use within the BC Zone. The entire building
which comprises 2,043 square feet of the 18, 871 square foot lot that
is zoned Boulevard Commercial (BC). The daycare is proposed to be
a bilingual center that operates Monday-Friday from 6:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. The applicant is not proposing any exterior
changes or site modifications to accommodate this use. Should future changes be proposed, those would need to be reviewed
through a modified site plan review.

 applicable to this particular request and merit discussion or additional consideration by the Planning Commission (staff
 analysis is found below in italics):

Sec. 21-33-4. Conditions.
      In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City may impose conditions that address:

 That the site already has an ingress and egress that is cohesive for a drop off and pick up use for a daycare.

Staff Concerns
  Staff has no other concerns with the proposed use.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for a daycare center to operate in the
  BC zone as described in the staff report for the property located at 8029 S. 700 E. based on the following findings and
  subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. The proposed use meets the intent of the BC zone.

    2.    The proposed use should have minimal to no impact on the surrounding uses.
