Pre-Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
Mayor Pro-Tempore Southwick welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were
present. Councilor Condie gave the invocation.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Presentation on a Disc-Golf Course on the Willis Estates Wetlands
Skyler Beltran, with the Parks, Trails and Trees Committee stated that they are interested in
adding a disc-golf course in the open space near Willis Estates. He stated that installation is
affordable, and it would be on land that has no other use.

Curtis Clements stated that he is a dis golfer and there is a greater need for courses in this area.
He said that it’s easy to maintain and doesn’t require a lot of upkeep. He said that local residents
are happy to maintain the course. He said that the wetlands would be a great location on
otherwise unusable property. He broke down the cost estimates to the Council for the course
installation. The Council was interested in designating some funds for the installation. The
Council also suggested that Mr. Clements do some fundraising to help cover costs.

2.2 Discussion of the FY2022 Budget and Capitol Projects
There was discussion on the need for sidewalk improvements to downtown. Dave Norman,
Public Works Director, stated that they are requesting an increase to the line item used for
improvements, but noted that that money is also used all over town to address maintenance
concerns and ADA requirements. Lorin Powell, City Engineer stated that they have created a
map showing the priority list for adding sidewalks to downtown. Council Albrecht wanted to
ensure that the downtown area is not neglected and suggesting using grant money to help fund

the projects. Mr. Norman stated that they could also use other department line items in the
budget to help fund more sidewalks.

Councilor Condie stated that he would also like to see another school resource officer, more
book funding for the library, and paved parking at Vets ballpark. He also requested that there be
funding for an all abilities park at Family Park. He would prefer that funding be dedicated to
Family Park rather then Mellor Rhodes. Councilor Albrecht also suggested adding a position for
a part time grant writer.

Jason Walker, City Administrator, stated that they could look at moving some funds to account
for the requests, and that they will report back to the Council at the next meeting.

2.3 Discussion of Water Restrictions
Mr. Norman and Matt Dalton, with the Water Department, suggested implementing water
restrictions. Mr. Dalton stated that the soil moisture content was so low that the soil soaked up all
the snowpack, so the water didn’t make it into the streams and reservoirs. He said that rivers are
running at historically low rates. Mr. Norman stated that they will need to make some changes to
the Water Conservation plan.
