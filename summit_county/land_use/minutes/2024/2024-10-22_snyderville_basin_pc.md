Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner,
reintroduced the project at Quinn's Junction and described the changes being introduced for discussion. The items were an increase in parking on the site, a typo in the memo from the applicant, access for pedestrians and the Rail Trail, and the addition of car chargers.
Richard Piggot with Maverik briefly addressed the Commission.
Commissioner Mooney asked a brief clarifying question to which Mr. Piggot responded.
Commissioner Cooke asked a question about the rail trail. Mr. Piggot answered the question.
Commissioners commented on the project, provided feedback, and asked questions.
James Copeland,
Maverik Civil Design Engineer
, responded to a question raised by Commissioner Cooke.
Bruce Carmichael made a motion to approve the application pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Tyann Mooney seconded, and the motion carried, (4-3).
Tyann Mooney voted AYE
Bruce Carmichael voted AYE
Christopher Conabee voted AYE
Matthew Nagie voted AYE
Makena Hawley voted NAY
Thomas Cooke voted NAY
Megan Recher voted NAY
Discussion was held on the motion.
Commissioner Hawley suggested an amendment to the motion to have max of 28 total parking spaces. If in two years Maverick finds it wouldn't work, they could request to the CDD to allow for additional parking, and best efforts to include a pedestrian bridge from the Maverick to the Rail Trail.
The amendment was not accepted, and the original motion was voted on and passed.

Laura Kuhrmeyer,
Planner
, provided a brief history of the proposed project and presented the accessory structure for public hearing and possible recommendation to the Community Development Director.
Commissioners Mooney and Cooke asked brief clarifying questions to which Planner Kuhrmeyer responded.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 7:41 p.m.
Ricardo Velarde was called to speak.
Susan Goldsmith was called to speak.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 7:53 p.m.
Commissioners and Director Barnes responded to the questions raised during the public hearing.
Commissioners provided feedback, suggestions, and commentary on the proposed project.
Forest Seaman and Olivia D’Agostino responded to comments made during the public hearing.
Matthew Nagie made a motion to approve the proposed low impact permit at 8845 Silver Creek Road to the Community Development Director pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report with two additional conditions of approval: lights be required to be turned off from sunset to sunrise as well as a waste plan being submitted to the Community Development Director as part of the permit. Tyann Mooney seconded.
Discussion was held on the motion by Commissioner Carmichael with respect to condition number 4 to have the two accessory buildings be removed prior to the building of the greenhouse.
Commissioner Nagie accepted Commissioner Carmichael's amendment.
Matthew Nagie made a motion to approve the proposed low impact permit at 8845 Silver Creek Road to the Community Development Director pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report with three additional conditions of approval: lights be required to be turned off from sunset to sunrise, a waste plan being submitted to the Community Development Director as part of the permit and the two accessory buildings be removed prior to the building of the greenhouse. Tyann Mooney seconded, and the motion carried, (6-1).
Tyann Mooney voted AYE
Makena Hawley voted AYE
