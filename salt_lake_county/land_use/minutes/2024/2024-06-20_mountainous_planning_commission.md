PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Citizen 
Name: Bill Clayton 
Address: Wasatch Resort at the mouth of Little Cottonwood Canyon 
Comments: Mr. Clayton said he is speaking on behalf of several neighbors. He likes the idea of squaring 
the defensible space. Three things to add to the changes :  First, private roads in the community area were 
deforested, and there has been enormous re-growth with narrow roads, and he envisions potential hazards; 
he requests adding enablement to create a reasonable zone along private roads  to allow vegetation 
management. Second, removal of diseased trees from fungus, bark beetle, etc. should be allowed. He likes 
the ability to remove trees from private property. Third, with land above b uried utilities, don’t want to 
replace trees on top of the utility.  
 
Speaker # 2: Citizen 
Name: Ed Marshall 
Address: 6451 Millcreek Canyon Road 
Comments: Mr. Marshall said the definition of WUI geographic area is presented as a clearer definition. 
The current definition, where all areas within FCOZ  are in the WUI area,  presents a dilemma  where all 
existing property is arguably subject to WUI . Ordinance should just apply to  all undeveloped areas. If the 
definition should be broad, keep in mind it applies to all WUI codes. 
 
Commissioner Omer motioned to close the public Hearing, Commissioner Staker seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED 
Commissioners, staff, and counsel  had a brief discussion regarding fire issues and defensible space, 
removal of and replacement of dead and diseased trees, WUI developed and undeveloped areas, driveways 
and private roads, allowing tree removal to adjoining private roads and what is the area of buffer feet, 
creation of areas above defensible space and avoiding replacement above buried utilities and parking strips 
and the ramifications of the same , giving discretion to staff as issues come forward and challenges  that 
poses without concrete standards, and limits of disturbance and placement of clustered trees. 
 
Motion: To recommend  approval to County Council with staff recommendations of  file # OAM2024-
