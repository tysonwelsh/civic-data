RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body
for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. On November 26, 2019, pursuant to state law, the Council approved and adopted the
2020 Magna Budget.

D. On May 13, 2020 ownership and operational control of the Pleasant Green Cemetery
(the “Cemetery”) was transferred to the municipality.

E. The operation of the cemetery does require the collection of revenues and payment of
expense as a par of its operation, requiring it be made a part of the budget to comply
with state law.

F. On September 22, 2020, the Council tentatively approved the Amended 2020 Magna
Budget to include a budget for the Cemetery (Attachment A) and set the public

1

hearing to be held on June 23, 2020 at 7:00 PM to accept input from the public
regarding the amendment to the Budget to add the Cemetery Budget.
G. On September 30, 2020, a notice of the public hearing and a copy of the proposed
| amendment to the Magna Budget to add the Cemetery Budget were posted on the
municipality’s website (www.magnametrotownship.org), the Utah Public Meeting
Notice website (www.utah.gov/pmn), and posted at the Webster Center (8952 W
Magna Main St Magna, UT, 84044) which fulfills the noticing requirements under
State Law.
H. The Cemetery Budget is necessary for the conduct of the business of the Cemetery
and to comply with State Law.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township, the Mayor and
Council hereby approves and adopts the amendment to the 2020 Magna Budget and
include the 2020 Pleasant Green Cemetery Budget to the 2020 Magna Budget that
