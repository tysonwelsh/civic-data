PRESENTATIONS:

GPZ-5-2025 Petition by Grow Development LLC requesting a General Plan change from
Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre)
and a zone change from A-2 (Agriculture, minimum lot size 2 acres) to R-1-8 (Single Unit
Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) and A (Agriculture, minimum
lot size ½ acre). The property is located at 3801 S 6400 W on 1.86 acres. (Staff – Steve
Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented a request to amend the General Plan and Zoning Map for
a property at 3801 South 6400 West, proposing a change from large lot residential to low
density residential. The applicant plans to keep two existing homes and build six new homes
ranging from 1,500 to 2,200 square feet with basements. The development agreement includes
exceptions for frontage requirements, rear setbacks, and parking spaces, with the applicant
proposing two-car garages instead of three-car garages. The applicant would add side parking
spaces to accommodate 5 cars per home. Curb and gutter would be installed along the
existing homes and in the cul-de-sac. Commissioner Porter asked about the necessity of
including the lots with existing homes, to which Steve explained was due to area requirements
for the zoning compliance. The group also discussed why 2-car garages are proposed since the
proposed lots are wide enough to accommodate 3-car garages, with Steve suggesting that the
applicant can provide more input on this issue in the next meeting. Commissioner Drozdek
inquired about the zoning of surrounding areas, which Steve confirmed are R-1-7 and R-1-8.
Commissioner Durfee didn’t feel there was a sufficient reason to allow two-car garages.

Z-7-2025 Petition by Roderick Enterprises requesting a zone change from LI (Light
Industrial) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 2444 and 2476 South Anna
Caroline Drive, on 7.39 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Steve Pastorik said the applicant requested this be moved to the December 10th
Public Hearing.

S-4-2025 Ana Po’oi is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Niu Haven
Meadows Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 1.38 acres. The
subdivision is located at 4404 South 5400 West and is zoned A. (Staff – Steve Lehman at
801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented a flag lot subdivision request, where a single-family home
would be built over half an acre between two existing stubbed streets. Public Works decided
not to require connecting the two streets, as building one home doesn't justify the high
construction costs of a new road. The new home would be accessed from 5400 West, and the
fire department requirement for access would include the possibility of installing a gate with a
knox box instead of a turnaround or a new hydrant. The shallow water table was noted as a
potential concern, with the Building Official allowing basements to be 2 feet deep based on a
ten year old report from the adjacent subdivision. Deeper basements could be requested with
a new soils report.

DISCUSSION ITEM: Central Jordan River Reconnect Project Presentation

Nick, from the consulting firm PORT, and Angelo, from Salt Lake County Parks, presented a
regional draft plan for the Jordan River Recreation Area being developed by Salt Lake County
Parks in collaboration with West Valley City, Taylorsville, Millcreek, Murray, and South Salt Lake.
Established through House Bill 173 in 2022, the Jordan River Recreation Area was officially
designated as a recreation zone extending 250 yards on each side of the river between SR-201
and 5400 South. The preferred plan has been developed over the past year with input from
stakeholders and the public and is intended to be adopted by the jurisdictions as part of their
General Plans or parks and recreation open space plans. Nick pointed out that the plan aligns
well with West Valley City's ongoing parks master plan and General Plan updates.

The identified priorities for the Central Jordan River Reconnect is connected communities,
healthy river corridors, water recreation, equitable and safe access, and operation
management. The discussion gave specific attention to three segments in West Valley City:
Four Corners Commons, Tracy Aviary area, and Destination Park/Oxbow area. In these areas the
focus is river ecology restoration, additional trails links, creating side channels, floodplain
connections, and new road connections. Implementation would require additional studies and
permitting.

It was mentioned that Salt Lake County Parks and Recreation has recently completed a
feasibility study to replace the Redwood Recreation Center with a new facility, so there is the
possibility of creating water sports areas and vehicular connections in the Redwood Nature
Area.

Nick emphasized the need for a regional third-party entity to manage and maintain the area,
as current funding and jurisdictional challenges make it difficult for individual municipalities to
handle these responsibilities. Given the state's designation of the area as a regional park, Salt
