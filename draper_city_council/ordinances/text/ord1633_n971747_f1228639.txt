City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1633

On February 4, 2025, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1633 amending
the official Zoning Map of Draper. Otherwise known as the South Willow Zoning Map
Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office
and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 5th day of February 2025. Laura
Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
