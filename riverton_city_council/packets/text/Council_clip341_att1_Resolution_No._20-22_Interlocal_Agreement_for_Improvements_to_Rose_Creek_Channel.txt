                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO.20-22


RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN INTERLOCAL
COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN SALT LAKE COUNTY AND RIVERTON CITY
FOR IMPROVEMENTS TO ROSE CREEK CHANNEL WITHIN RIVERTON CITY
BOUNDARIES



       WHEREAS, Rose Creek is a natural channel which crosses property owned by Suburban Land
Reserve (SLR) between Mountain View and 4250 W; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton entered into a Development Agreement with SLR wherein the City
agreed to relocate the Rose Creek Channel to a location agreed upon by both parties; and

        WHEREAS, to obtain a permit to relocate said channel, the Army Corps of Engineers is requiring
the City to improve the Rose Creek channel to carry the 100 year storm event; and

       WHEREAS, Rose Creek is a Salt Lake County stormwater facility; and

        WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has agreed to partially fund improvements included in the
relocation project by providing $225,000 from their 2020 budget; and,

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has prepared an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement for execution
by the City to obtain these funds; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council does hereby determine that it is in the best interests of the
health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to execute said Agreement.

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council that the aforesaid
Resolution authorizing the Mayor to execute an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement to receive from Salt
Lake County the sum of $225,000 for improvements to the Rose Creek channel within Riverton
boundaries, copies of which are incorporated herein by reference, be, and hereby is, approved, and the
Mayor of Riverton is hereby authorized to execute the same.
