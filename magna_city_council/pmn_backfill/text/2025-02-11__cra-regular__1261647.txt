B. Salt Lake County Redevelopment Agency Transfer Update - Board Member Eric Barney
CRA OF MAGNA BOARD MEMBERS
ERIC BARNEY, AUDREY PIERCE, TRISH HULL,
STEVE PROKOPIS, MICK SUDBURY

Board Member Eric Barney met with Kirsten Swinyard from Salt Lake County to discuss the
property transfers, they are all on board.
Board Member Pierce joined the meeting.
All properties previously discussed are being transferred, that includes the three parcels on
9150, also known as Magna Neighborhood Park. Two of those parcels are owned by the RDA,
one is owned by Salt Lake County. Those parcels will be transferred straight to the city from
the current owner entities, as well as the detention basin next to Copper Park. There was no
specific time frame given, as there is some legislative work that has to happen related to the
County Council, but they all appear to be on board. The attorneys on the call did not indicate
they saw any issues holding this up. Regarding the properties on Main Street within the RDA
area, even if county owned, will transfer to the RDA.
Paul Ashton noted that when the county transfers parks, they include a clause that the
property remain a park.
Board Member Barney indicated that in discussion with the attorneys, they share that clause is
not present in any of the current agreements for the above-mentioned park properties.
Regarding the Arbor Park area, there are two RDA-type entities in that area. One that is active
currently, with funding being collected and distributed on. The other has been floating out there
and should have been terminated but wasn’t, so once discovered they are terminating that
property. Since all the increment in the Arbor Park RDA has all been allocated there isn’t really
anything left to do other than let it run its time out.
David Brickey shared the county is sending the Arbor Park RDA to the city, but at the present
time they are terminating the increment to be paid out which is one of the reasons for the
delay. They are doing it this year so the city can see how that allocation takes place and is
calculated, so next year when the city has the obligation there is an example to follow.
Board Member Sudbury asked about the issue with the ditch, piping and cap. Didn't they get
that stamped off?
David Brickey responded that Don submitted some numbers yesterday, Ms. Allen reviewed
them, and he indicated the rest could be discussed during a closed session.
Board Member Barney indicated that was city business to be discussed in another meeting,
and the matter was dropped.
