  INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND
   HERRIMAN CITY FOR THE MICROSURFACE SEAL AND STRIPING OF 13400
          SOUTH STREET FROM MOUNTAIN VIEW TO 5600 WEST


 THIS INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY
AND HERRIMAN CITY FOR THE MICROSURFACE SEAL AND STRIPING OF 13400
               SOUTH STREET FROM MOUNTAIN VIEW TO 5600 WEST
       (this “Agreement”) is entered into this _____ day of May, 2020, by and between
RIVERTON CITY, a Utah municipality (“Riverton”), and HERRIMAN CITY, a Utah
municipality (“Herriman”).

                                            R E C I T A L S:

       A.      Riverton and Herriman each own and/or are responsible for maintenance of a
portion of 13400 South Street between 5600 W and Mountain View Corridor (“13400 Street”).

       B.          13400 Street is in need of repair including microsurface and lane striping
(“Project”).

         C.        Riverton and Herriman desire to jointly proceed with the Project.

         D.        Herriman has solicited bids for the Project.

        E.     Riverton and Herriman are public agencies and are therefore authorized by the Utah
Interlocal Cooperation Act, Section 11-13-101, et seq., U.C.A., to enter into agreements with each
other which will enable them to make the most efficient use of their powers

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the recitals above and the mutual covenants,
promises, and agreements contained herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the
receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:

       Section 1.      Description of the Project. The Project shall consist of a microsurface
treatment and lane striping as more fully described on attached exhibit “A.”

       Section 2.    Obligations of Herriman. Herriman shall be responsible for procuring
and advancing the Project

        Section 3.     Obligations of Riverton. Riverton shall reimburse Herriman for one-half
the cost to complete the Project, which is estimated at $100,000. After the Project is complete,
Herriman shall send Riverton an invoice for one-half of the cost of the Project, which Riverton
agrees to pay within thirty days of receiving the referenced invoice.

       Section 4.         General Provisions. The following provisions are also an integral part of
this Agreement:
