Legal Updates/Information

Kearns Code Updates – Title 3 – Revenue and Finance

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated he would like
feedback from the Council regarding Title 3 of the Kearns Metro Township, which deals with
revenue and finance. The State Legislature passed a bill this year, which allows metro townships
to impose a Municipal Energy Use Tax and a Telecommunication Tax. He is not proposing the
Council impose these taxes but would like to include them in this section so the Council can see
what that looks like. Before the Council could impose either of these taxes, it would have to go
through a Truth-in-Taxation process, which includes holding a public hearing. He would like the
Council’s input before he did additional work on this.

               The Council indicated it was okay with Mr. Bracken moving forward with this idea.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) Bond and Interlocal Agreement

                Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated there are
several capital improvement projects that the Municipal Services District (MSD) will need to fund.
To get the necessary funding, the MSD would have to issue a $20 million bond. The cost would
be spread across all members of the MSD. Kearns will need approximately $9 million from this
bond to work on 4700 South. If there are any funds left over, it could use them for other projects,

Formal Letter Declaring the Use of Land Transferred to Kearns Metro Township

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated there are 3.5
acres of vacant land located at 6015 South 5600 West, which is owned by the Utah Department
of Transportation (UDOT) and a small sliver of land owned by Salt Lake County. UDOT can give
Kearns this land at no cost if it is used for a road or public park. The contract with UDOT for this
land would include a revisionary clause that if the land is used for something else, the title of the
property would revert to UDOT. For this to happen, the Council would have to authorized Mayor
Bush to sign a letter indicating that Kearns would use the property for the above uses.

               Mayor Kelly Bush stated this would be a good investment. The property would
be under the control of Kearns and later, the township could purchase if it wanted to use the
property for something other than a park or road.

              Council Member Butterfield, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
authorize the Mayor to sign the letter to transfer the land from UDOT to the Kearns Metro
Township. Roll was called, showing the vote to be Council Member Butterfield “Aye,” Council
Member Schaeffer “Aye,” Council Member Peterson “Aye,” and Mayor Bush “Aye.”
