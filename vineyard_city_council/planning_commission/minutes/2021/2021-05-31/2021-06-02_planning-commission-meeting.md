     Call to Order
Chair Brady called the meeting to order at 6:04pm.

1.    INVOCATION/INSPIRATIONAL THOUGHTS/PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE –
Commissioner Gudmundson gave an inspirational thought regarding the new community garden.

2.       OPEN SESSION -
Resident David Lauret asked for the POD 1 Overlay District meaning to be explained. Mr. Brim
replied that the Overlay District was created to help the city negotiate with the developer on issues
such as setbacks and open space.

3. MINUTES REVIEW AND APPROVAL:

THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.

4.      BUSINESS ITEMS
    4.1 DISCUSSION AND ACTION – POD 1 Clubhouse Site Plan
Mr. Perez gave a brief explanation of the proposed clubhouse and site plan. He gave staff’s
recommendation for approval. Mr. Olsen with Home Center Construction answered questions
from the Commission. He estimated work to begin later this summer.
