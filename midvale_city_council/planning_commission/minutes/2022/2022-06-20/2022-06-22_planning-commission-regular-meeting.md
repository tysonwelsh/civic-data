PUBLIC HEARINGS

     1. Amendments to Title 17 of the Midvale City Municipal Code to address licensure of
        Child Care Centers and Child Care Facilities within the City.

The city proposes an amendment to the above listed chapters of the Municipal Code. The
amendment removes the requirement for proof of a state childcare license and requires in its
place a city business license. HB 15, approved this past legislative session, states in part, that a
municipality cannot “enact or enforce an ordinance that: (a) imposes licensing or certification
requirements for a childcare program…”. To ensure compliance with this recent revision
enacted by HB 15, the city proposes to remove the requirement in our Code for proof of state
licensure.

Public notice has been sent to affected entities as required in 17-3-9. B of the Municipal Code.
No
comments have been received as of the writing of this report.

If the draft ordinance is in favor with the Commissioners, it will go onto the City Council.

code.

   3. Natalie Reynolds-Owen requests Conditional Use Permit approval to allow for a
      daycare facility located at 237 Tennyson Ave in the Single Family Residential (SF-1)
      zone.

The applicant requests conditional use permit approval to allow for a home daycare facility
located at 237 Tennyson Ave in the Single Family Residential (SF-1) zone. The daycare facility
would allow for up to twelve children to attend.

This proposal has been reviewed by Planning Staff, the City Engineer, and the Unified Fire
Authority for compliance with the respective guidelines, policies, standards, and codes. Staff
finds the proposal complies with requirements outlined in Midvale City Municipal Code 17-7-1.

Public notice has been sent to property owners within 500 feet of the subject parcel. No
Objections have been received as of the writing of this report. *As of June 22, 2022, we’ve
received three comments against the proposal. *

Mrs. Arnold describes a home daycare with the maximum of 12 children.

Staff recommends approval.

DISCUSSION:

   1.   Potential Code Text Amendment for a Duplex to be an allowed use as opposed to a
        Conditional Use Permit (CUP) within the SF-1 (DO) zone.

Mr. Olsen describes the CUP application process being an administrative task. The Planning &
Zoning Department would like to change the CUP process for the duplex/twin home structure
approval to an allowed use.

Chair Snow asks for clarification on the difference between the twin home/duplex requirements.

Commissioner Benson asks for clarification as well. He asks if this is defined as a homeowner
changing their own home into a duplex/twin home.

Mr. Olsen describes the process as being identical to other processes in other zoning areas. They
are not being seen in the other zones but in the SF-1 (DO) zone it is brought before Planning
Commission and then onto City Council. The department would like to ask for the administrative
power to treat this as other zones would.

Commissioner Liedtke sees this as an opportunity to not waste time in Planning Commission and
City Council meetings.

Vice Chair Anderson agrees.

Chair Snow agrees.

This will be drawn up and voted on, then taken to the City Council.
