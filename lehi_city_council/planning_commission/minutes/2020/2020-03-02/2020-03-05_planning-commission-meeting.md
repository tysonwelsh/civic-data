Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Vice Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Discussion Items

    2.1 Transit Master Plan

    The Planning Staff did a presentation on the Transit Master Plan.

    2.2 General Plan Land Use Update

    Mike West and Kim Struthers did an update on the General Plan. They discussed the goals
    and strategies for the General Plan.

3. Adjournment


With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Nielsen moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Ellis seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:00 p.m.
