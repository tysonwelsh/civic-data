Guests: Myrna Trauntvein (Press), Donald W. Ball, Kalea Pauole, Chad Williams, JD Parady, Craig Oswald,
Jeremy Lynn and Ruth Bonzo.

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City
Attorney Kasey Wright offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  Chad William, representing Central Utah Counseling, led the Pledge of Allegiance and then gave an
explanation of the services provided by his office.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes for the regular council meeting held on 1-19-21, work session held on 1-19-21, work session
held on 1-26-21, and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 2-2-21. Councilor Memmott seconded
the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

  Councilor Kent Jones reviewed two business license applications. The first one was a ownership
change for One Man Band and the second one was for a home occupation license for L J Crafts.
Councilor Jones moved to approve both license applications. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

SAVAGE BLUFFS SUBDIVISION ZONE CHANGE APPROVED:

   Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 02-02-2021: AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE ZONE
MAP OF NEPHI CITY, STATE OF UTAH. The property known as Savage Bluffs Subdivision was changed
from Residential R1-H to Residential R1. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed
on the following vote:
                               NAY: Kent Jones
                               Yes: Skip Worwood
                                      Nathan Memmott
                                      Larry Ostler
                                      Justin Seely

PROSECUTION SERVICES AGREEMENT WITH KASEY WRIGHT APPROVED:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson explained to the council that the city’s prosecutor, Perry Davis, was
unable to continue prosecution services for the Nephi Justice Court. Kasey Wright has temporarily taken
                                                                                              Page 1 of 2
over those duties through February. The city staff has worked with Mr. Wright to determine a viable
long-term approach to prosecution services at a cost of approximately $2,333 per month. This price
includes the cost of an E-prosecutor software. Councilor Jones moved to authorize the Mayor to sign
the Prosecution Services Agreement between Nephi City and Kasey Wright. Councilor Memmott
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

OUT OF STATE TRAVEL APPROVED FOR THREE DEPARTMENTS:

   Mr. Atkinson reported that the recreation, police, and electric departments all have conferences,
training, or testing that will be held out of state. The recreation department has a national Athletic
Business Conference in San Antonio, Texas in October that Recreation Director Craig Oswald would like
to attend. Chief of Police Michael Morgan requested to attend FBI training that the date and location
have not yet been determined. Testing for the electric department staff will occur in Mesquite, Nevada
in March. Councilor Jones moved to giver permission to all three requests to attend out-of-state
conferences. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

LARRY O’DONNAL APPOINTED TO THE LIBRARY BOARD:

    Councilor Justin Seely, who represents the city council on the Library Board, moved to appoint Larry
O’Donnal as a Library Board member. Mr. O’Donnal would be filling the vacancy of board member
Bonnie Ingram. Mr. O’Donnal has been a resident of Nephi for the past 14 years and is a frequent
patron of the library. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson reported that the USU sponsored Well-Being Survey has had low participation,
particularly of male participation.
  Mr. Atkinson updated the council on the CentraCom fiber installation project.
  Mr. Atkinson stated that the budget priority setting work session will be held on February 9, 2021 at
6:30 p.m. at the golf course clubhouse.
  Mr. Atkinson gave a legislative update on several house bills of interest to the city.
  Councilor Seely reported on the success of the library sponsored Information Fair and then invited
Library Director Ruth Bonzo to report on the fair. Ms. Bonzo handed out prizes to the council member
who had attended the Information Fair.
  Councilor Jones spoke favorably about the meeting the council had had with the county officials.
Although, he said that he was confused at times of who was running the meeting and that people were
talking at the same time.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual, and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed
on a unanimous vote.


   The meeting adjourned at 8:45 p.m.
