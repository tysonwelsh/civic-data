PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on March 28, 2018 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2018-15 amending Title 2 Chapters 1-6 of the Herriman City Code of Ordinance regarding Boards and Committees and adding a new chapter 7 creating a Trails Committee. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
