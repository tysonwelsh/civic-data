CONSIDERATION OF PROCLAMATION NO. 2023-29, A PROCLAMATION
DECLARING WASHINGTON COUNTY AS AMERICA’S COUNTY

Commissioner Iverson spoke about the upcoming Liberty Village and the County’s excitement for
the upcoming events.

Commissioner Almquist discussed the beliefs discussed within the Proclamation and each
Commissioner’s contribution to the document.

Commissioner Snow stated the purpose of this Proclamation is to let everyone know that
Washington County will always be a beacon for freedom.

Chief Executive Officer of United We Pledge Dennis Leavitt stated hope that the spirit of America
will carry on with the rising generation.

Commissioner Snow read the Proclamation aloud.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Proclamation No. 2023-29, A
Proclamation Declaring Washington County as America’s County. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3135, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
APPRAISAL PROPOSALS REQUIRED FOR THE WARNER VALLEY LAND
EXCHANGE

Mr. Clarke explained when the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve was created, large portions of land were
privately owned. The federal government agreed to acquire privately owned land within the
reserve, and Washington County has agreed to facilitate those transactions. The BLM and
Washington County Water Conservancy District would like to make a three way exchange that
would result in the BLM’s acquisition of privately owned property remaining in the reserve.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3135,
A Resolution Approving Appraisal Proposals Required for the Warner Valley
Land Exchange. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3137, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_CONTRACT FOR THE DESIGN OF THE CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER
EXPANSION

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw requsted the next two items be combined as they are
related. She stated that in 2012, the Children’s Justice Center (CJC) was constructed by MRW and
Watts Construction. Through the growth of the County, there is a need to increase the mental health
services through the CJC. Watts and MRW Design will provide services at cost in order to facilitate
these efforts.

Mr. Clarke thanked the Commissioners for their work in supporting the CJC.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3137,
A Resolution Approving a Contract for the Design of the Children’s Justice
Center Expansion. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
