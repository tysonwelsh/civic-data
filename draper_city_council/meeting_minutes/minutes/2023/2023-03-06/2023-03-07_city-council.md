6.0   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1577, an Ordinance approving the second
      amendment to the Master Development Agreement for EPG Pony Express
      for approximately 26.93 acres of property located at approximately 13004
      South Pony Express Road within Draper City

6.1   Planner Todd Draper explained the proposed second amendment to the
      Master Development Agreement (MDA) for EPG Pony Express would allow
      signage exceeding Draper City Municipal Code allowances. He said the
      Planning Commission reviewed the request and forwarded a positive
      recommendation. Mr. Draper answered questions from the Council.

6.2   Mark McKee, representing the applicant, said the request was for signage
      fitting the main façade of the building, and consistent with Living Spaces stores
      nationwide. Responding to a question from Councilmember F. Lowry, Mr.
      McKee said Living Spaces had not signed a tenant agreement at the time of the
      original MDA.

6.3   Mayor Walker opened a public hearing, and closed the public hearing
      seeing that no one came forward to comment.

6.4   Councilmember Green moved to approve                      Ordinance      #1577.
      Councilmember F. Lowry seconded the motion.

6.5   A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
