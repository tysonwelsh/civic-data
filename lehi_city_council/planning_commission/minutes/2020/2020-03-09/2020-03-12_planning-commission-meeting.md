3.2 Public hearing and consideration of Yesco’s request for conditional use approval of an
Electronic Display Pylon Sign for Ken Garff located approximately 2302 North Aston Blvd.
    Brittney presented information on the agenda item. The applicant is asking for exceptions on
    signage allowance of 70 feet in height, 794.31 sqft. in size, and 400 sqft. electronic display
    area. DRC recommended a condition of no other Pylon signs allowed on property if approved.

    Commissioner Hemmert asked for definition of multi tenant in commercial. Kim stated he
    believes where it is different auto dealerships it would qualify as multi tenant. He also stated
    the signage would be distinct. Commissioner Hemmert stated he was struggling with the lot
    and signage size. Commissioner Hemmert asked for clarification on City Code and if it was
    the only regional Commercial Zone. Kim stated it was the only development in regional
    Commercial Zone.


    The applicant, Dan Pace with Yesco, was present. He stated the site plan has three parcels and
    rather than having a sign for each that they would combine signs. Commissioner Hemmert
    stated there is some topography that hinders line of sight. Commissioner Everett asked how
    high the sign was near I-15. The applicant was unsure of the height of I-15. Commissioner
    Peterson asked what the height of standard billboard was. Kim stated it varies on location.
    Commissioner Peterson asked for clarification on the sign and why it needs to be so high. The
    applicant stated the 70 feet is within the Code exceptions. Ross stated Southbound view is
    obstructed quite a bit and that obstruction from Northbound and Southbound traffic varies.
    Commissioner Nielsen stated the sign looked like it was preparing for four signs. The applicant
    stated he wasn’t sure if it would be standalone or what would be on the other lots.
    Commissioner Hemmert opened public hearing on agenda item 3.2
    Rob Ludlow stated he felt billboards were a problem and have created light pollution. He asked
    that the code be enforced without exceptions and sets a terrible precedent. He stated there were
    exceptions, but that this should not be considered because it is not a community benefit.
    Kenny Nuvery stated he agreed with Mr. Ludlow. His property is affected by the Outlet sign
    and is concerned what this new signage will bring. He stated the distance is a little over one
    mile away, but the distance to the stop light from his house in comparison to the brightness of
    the sign is four stop lights. He stated in terms of impact it is very reasonable to assume it will
    not be beneficial. Kenny stated the outlet signs are more impactful than this proposal. He asked
    for a negative recommendation.
    Commissioner Hemmert stated the Sign Ordinance was reviewed a while ago and time and
    research were spent crafting language for new sign standards that are in line with other
    communities. He stated when the outlets sign was approved the standards were not put in place.
    He stated the City is trying to protect the I-15 corridor with signage.
    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing on agenda item 3.2
    The applicant stated the new signage is controlled remotely through a cell modem and would
    fully expect to abide by the code. Commissioner Hemmert asked about the sign functions. The
    applicant stated the light is focused where it should go.
    Commissioner Hemmert stated rights to signage have been allowed before but is concerned
    with a consolidation that is this large with all the combined signs.
    Commissioner Peterson stated the Commission could allow height exceptions but jumping to
    70 feet because the code allowed seems extreme. He asked what a reasonable sign height would
    be. He also asked about parking and signage on buildings and was inclined to deny the request
    because he hasn’t seen anything that shows it has to be this large to be efficient.
    Commissioner Hemmert stated he was also concerned about the size of the sign and reflected
    on a previous approval.
    Commissioner Eyre stated there is a radio tower that is unsightly and suggested three signs that
    would fit the code rather than one large consolidated sign.


    Commissioner Nielsen stated he agreed with the comments and expressed that he does not like
    the signage code and standards and thinks it is getting ridiculous with the signage that is being
    allowed. He stated a variance is not something that should be considered. He also stated the
    view is obscured on I-15, as well as the landscape and scenery, and would rather have three
    signs as opposed to one large sign. He stated the justification of the sign for four dealerships
    was not being met for the signage that is being proposed.
    Commissioner Hemmert asked about the lighting standards and when they needed to be
    submitted for review. Kim stated he would look for the plans in the submittal.
    Commissioner Everett stated none of the exceptions made sense to him.
    Commissioner Hemmert stated if it is not approved it needs to have facts to back it up as to
    why it isn’t approved. The Commission went through code at length and which standards need
    to be met for approval. The Commission and Staff discussed the agenda item.
    Commissioner Hemmert entertained a motion.
Motion: Commissioner Nielsen moved to deny Yesco’s request for conditional use approval of an
Electronic Display Pylon Sign for Ken Garff located approximately 2302 North Aston Blvd;
including the DRC comments from February 19, 2020 and based on findings in six of the items
that were not met which were 1, 2, 4 and 5, the proposed signs exceptions are in conflict with the
intent of the chapter, the section of the code does not meet the intent due to the overlarge size of
the sign, and that preponderance of evidence was not met and does not justify a 70 foot sign.

Commissioner Everett suggested to include Chapter 12.4 EDS Illumines Review in the motion.

Amended Motion: Commissioner Nielsen moved to amend and include Chapter 12.4 EDS
Illumines Review to his motion. Commissioner Everett seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion
passed unaminously.
