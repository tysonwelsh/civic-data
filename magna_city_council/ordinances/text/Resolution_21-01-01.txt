                                MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP


RESOLUTION NO.: 21-01-01                                           DATE: January 12, 2021


             A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
             APPROVING THE THIRD AMENDED CONTRACT BETWEEN
             MAGNA AND GOVERNMENT CONSULTANTS LLC TO
             PERFORM MUNICIPAL ADMINISTRATION WORK FOR MAGNA



                                          RECITALS



A. The Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code§§ 10-2a-401

   et seq.



B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the "Council") is the municipal legislative body for

   Magna pursuant to Utah Code§ 10-3b-501.


C. Government Consultants LLC (the COMPANY) is the contracted service provider to Magna

   for Municipal Administration.


D. During the creation of the 2020 Magna Budget, the Council decided an increase in the

   monthly pay to Company was desired to reflect their appreciation for the company's work and

   to begin transitioning the company contract to market.


E. To affect the new compensation rate requires amending the contract with the Company.


F. The attached amended contract (Attachment "A") reflects those changes.
                                        RESOLUTION

   THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the amended contract
between Magna and Government Consultants LLC, and authorizes the Mayor to sign the contract
to affect the same.
