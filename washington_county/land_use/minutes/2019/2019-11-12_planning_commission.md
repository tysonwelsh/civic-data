Washington County Planning Commission Meeting
                                  November 12, 2019

The Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting was held on Tuesday, November 12, 2019
in the Council Chambers of the Washington County Administration Building at 197 E. Tabernacle,
St. George, UT. Chairman Slade Hughes opened the meeting at 1:30 p. m. He led the Pledge of
Allegiance and then explained meeting protocol and how the public hearing would be handled.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Dan Briggs (Alternate), Shayne Durrant, Kevin
Jones, Mark Owens, Reed Scow (Alternate), Doug Solstad, and Jon Walter.

Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Natalie Nelson, Deputy County
Attorney; Brian Graff, Deputy Attorney; Kayla Eppley, Administrative Assistant; Kurt Gardner,
Building Official; Gil Almquist, County Commissioner.


1. CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT. Review request for conditional use permit approval of a 3
million gallon culinary water tank on BLM Land. The property is located in the southern area of
Warner Valley off Warner Valley Road and east of the Southern Utah Parkway. The applicant is
Washington County Water Conservancy District.

Mr. Messel provided maps and documentation of the proposal. He explained that the Washington
County Water Conservancy District has applied for a conditional use permit to construct a 3
million gallon water tank to be located on BLM land in the southern end of Warner Valley, off
Warner Valley Road and east of the Southern Utah Parkway. The property is currently zoned OSC-
20 (Open Space Conservation 20 acre minimum lot size).

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the requested conditional use permit application for the 3 million gallon water
storage tank to be located in the OSC-20 (Open Space Conservation 20 acre minimum lot size)
zone based on the following findings and conditions:

 1. Public Utilities are a conditional use in the OSC-20 (Open Space Conservation 20 acre
    minimum lot size) zone.
 2. The proposed water tank will help provide culinary water to residents within Washington
    County.
 3. The proposal is consistent with the General Plan.
 4. The proposal meets the applicable Washington County Codes.

Todd Olsen of Bush and Gudgell representing the Water Conservancy District reported they have
gone through the permitting process with BLM and received approval for the location of the water
tank. He answered questions from the planning commission.

MOTION:        Commissioner Durrant MOVED to approve the conditional use permit

2. FINAL PLAT APPROVAL. Review and approval of the Cemetery Hill Subdivision final
plat. The 9 acre parcel, 4033-A-1-C-1-HV, will be divided into 6 lots. The property is located in
the RA-1 (Residential Agricultural 1 acre minimum lot size) zone at approximately 250 S. Silver
Valley Road near Leeds. The applicant is DHP Investment, LLC.

Mr. Messel reported that back in November 2017, the applicant, DHP Invest, LLC, submitted an
application and preliminary plat for a six lot subdivision on approximately 9 acres in Leeds. The
Parcel, 4033-A-1-C-1-HV, is located at the top of the hill and will be accessed off Silver Valley
Road at approximately 250 S. Silver Valley Rd. The zoning designation is RA-1 (Residential
Agricultural 1 acre minimum lot size). When the original request was submitted, there several
items and documents that needed to be addressed and provided before the project could move
forward for preliminary plat approval. The applicants submitted the required changes and
documents to the county and during the August 21, 2019 meeting, received preliminary plat
approval. The applicants have submitted the final plat and required documents and are now ready
to move forward to record the subdivision. However, due to septic issues, only four of the six lots
are being approved under phase one of the project.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the final plat and necessary documents and would suggest that the planning
commission approve the final subdivision plat of Phase 1 of the Cemetery Hill Subdivision based
on the following findings:

   1. The public hearing for the proposed subdivision was properly noticed and approved.
   2. The public hearing was held on March 13, 2018.
   3. The final plat meets the applicable Washington County Codes.
   4. The subdivision will meet the applicable requirements of the RA-1 (Residential
      Agricultural 1 acre minimum lot size) zone.
   5. The proposed subdivision request is in harmony with the General Plan.

3. PUBLIC HEARING/PROJECT PLAN AND ZONE CHANGE. Review of a project plan
and zone change for a master planned commercial development request on Parcels 2011-A-1-NS,
2011-A-2-NS, 2011-A-4-NS, and KBR-7A-391-NS. The request is to change the parcels from the
exiting A-20 (Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size) zone and the RA-5 (Residential Agricultural
5 acre minimum lot size) zone to the PDC (Planned Development Commercial) zone. The
combined parcels total approximately 30.14+/- acres in size and are located off Exit 40 on Old
Highway 91 and Kolob Canyon Road and Badger Way in New Harmony. The applicants are
Juniper Investors, LLC./Stephan Swindle.

Mr. Messel reported that Several months ago, the Planning Commission reviewed the concept plan
for a PD Commercial project located on the west side of Exit 40 on I-15. The exit provides access
to the Kolob Canyon portion of Zion National Park. Washington County annually welcomes more
than 4 million visitors. They come for many reasons including such as the weather, world class
outdoor and recreational opportunities. The proposed project consists of parcels 2011-A-1-NS,
2011-A-2-NS, 2011-A-4-NS and KBR-7A-391-NS. The parcels are currently zoned A-20
(Agriculture 20 acre minimum lot size) and RA-5 (Residential Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot
size). The request, as part of the PD project process, is to rezone the subject parcels to the PD
Commercial Zone. The PD Commercial Zone is tied to the project plan and supporting documents.
The project is approximately 21.84 acres in size. The project plan is divided into 8 “Areas”. The

project plan documents outline the possible uses in each area. The uses include: hotel, gas station/
convenience store, food services, RV camping, open space, general retail and commercial uses.
The developer will dedicate the required water rights to Mountain Springs Water Company. Ash
Creek Special Service District has provided a will serve letter. Utility plans are included in the
project plan packet. More detailed construction plans will be reviewed as each “Area” is
developed.

The General Plan for the New Harmony Valley states that “Commercial development in the New
Harmony Valley should be kept to the minimum needs to serve the convenience needs of the
residents and to provide for the needs of the traveling public on the Interstate Freeway.” It also
states under Policies, “Limit highway commercial development to convenient locations adjacent
to the freeway interchanges.” A majority of all developed Interstate 15 interchanges the length of
the State of Utah have commercial businesses and zoning.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the requested PD Commercial Zone Change and Project Plan and recommends
approval, based on the following findings and conditions:

    1.  The public hearing for the PD zone change and Project Plan was properly noticed.
    2.  The public hearing was held on November 12, 2109 in the regularly scheduled Planning
        Commission Meeting.
    3. The traffic study has been reviewed and accepted by staff.
    4. The storm drainage plan has been reviewed and accepted by county engineering and
        planning staff
    5. The proposed commercial project will provide needed convenience services to the
        residents and visitors to Washington County and Zion National Park.
    6. The zone change would allow the applicant a means to utilize the property in a beneficial
        way.
    7. The proposed project plan and zone change are consistent with the General Plan.
    8. Commercial development is an appropriate land use at Interstate 15 interchanges.
    9. That connectivity of pedestrian and bike paths and sidewalks must be integrated both
        externally and internally as each designated area on the project plan is developed.
    10. That bike racks must be installed in each development area.
    11. Site lighting will be night sky friendly, while also providing safe and appropriate lighting
        for the developed uses.
    12. That no building exceeds the two-story building height unless, adequate fire and
        emergency services codes, standards, and equipment are reasonably mitigated and
        approved by the Washington County Commission.
    13. The architecture, landscape, and site design are similar to design elements used in
        Springdale near the entrance to Zion National Park and meet or exceed County Standard.
    14. The project will be an economic benefit to the region.
