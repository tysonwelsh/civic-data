Work Session Items

1.         21-299                 Administrative Services Department providing an update on the fourth
                                  quarter for fiscal year 2021.
                   Attachments:   AMENDED FY 2021 Q4 Budget Update.pdf

                                  Brett Neuman with the Finance Department presented a FY 2021 Q4 Budget Update -
                                  July 2020 through June 2021. This is a preliminary report; the revenues and expenses in
                                  each fund are not finalized yet. He provided a financial overview of the fourth quarter of
                                  fiscal year 2021. The City Council thanked Mr. Neuman for his presentation.

2.         21-298                 Administration updating the Council on the results of the July 6 - July 22,
                                  2021 Alta Canyon Sports Center Survey
                   Attachments:   2021 Alta Canyon Survey Results_Full Report.pdf

                                  Dan Medina, Parks & Recreation Director, reviewed the community survey recently
                                  completed on Alta Canyon Recreation Center. The survey was open from July 6 through
                                  July 22, 2021. There were 2,029 responses. He summarized key items that stood out to
                                  him. The pool is an important feature of the center. Respondents also wanted a more
                                  open, light center with newer amenities. Council questions and comments followed.

3.         21-300                 Administrative Services Department providing an update and
                                  recommendation on use of American Rescue Plan Act funding.
                   Attachments:   ARPA Information Update

                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director, provided an overview and update of the American Rescue
                                  Plan Act funding (ARPA) and made recommendations to the Council. The City will
                                  receive $9,151,064. Fifty percent of those funds have been received. He reviewed the key
                                  goals of the ARPA funding and eligible expenses and recommended allocations of
                                  funds.Council questions and comments followed.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Housman thanked the Police and Fire Department staff for joining and
                    participating in a neighborhood carnival and helping to make great memories for the kids.
                    She thanked the Sandy Rotary Club for holding a food drive for the Sandy Club through
                    Amazon Wish List. She gave an update from the Healthy Sandy Steering Committee and
                    thanked Charles Otis and Eric Richards for their help.

                    Council Member Zoltanski thanked the Police Department staff for sponsoring the recent
                    Night Out Against Crime event. She spoke about the wonderful performance which was
                    part of the Hale Center Theater Cultural Celebration and thanked the Hale Center Theatre
                    staff for organizing the event. The Community Arts production of Freaky Friday was very
                    funny and enjoyable. She encouraged all residents to provide feedback and comments on
                    the UDOT EIS. Comments can be submitted through Friday, September 3rd.

                    Council Member Christensen gave an update from the Sandy Historic Committee. She
                    spoke about the selection of a provider who will be developing an app for a walking tour of
                    Historic Sandy. She provided an update on the RFP process related to the neighborhood
                    plan.

                    Council Member Sharkey gave an update from the Association of Municipal Councils and
                    spoke about a presentation from the Kem C. Gardner Policy Institute. The committee has
                    been focusing on the housing crisis in Utah and she shared housing statistics and
                    trends. In one year's time, 40% of Utah households were priced out of the home buying
                    market. That figure has jumped to 55% one year later. In the renter market, 63% of
                    renters were priced out of the home buying market in June of 2020 and one year later,
                    77% of renters were priced out of the home buying market. She spoke about the supply
                    and demand of housing in Utah. At the rate of growth we are currently experiencing, we
                    will run out of entitled land to build on before we run out for the need to build and it is
                    important to make better use of what we have.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Bradburn provided a Sandy specific drought update. All of this information is on the
                    Sandy City website. Our overall use of water is trending down by approximately 10%
                    which equates to approximately 65 Bell Canyon Reservoirs of water saved. He thanked
                    the community and acknowledged the efforts of all residents to conserve water during this
                    drought period.

CAO Report

                    Matt Huish, CAO, spoke about the opening of the candidate filing period for the upcoming
                    election. Seven candidates have filed for Mayor. He provided an update on the Google
                    Fiber process and the Community Arts/Sandy Amphitheater.
