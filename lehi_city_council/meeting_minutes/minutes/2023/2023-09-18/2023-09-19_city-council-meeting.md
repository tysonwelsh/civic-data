Pre Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present, noting that
Councilor Koivisto attended virtually for the first part of the meeting and will attend in person
soon. Councilor Condie gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1. Fiber Network Update
Shaye Ruitenbeek gave an update to the Council on the Fiber Network project. She stated that
there are a total of 112 customers connected to the network, with a total of 232 orders. She
expressed that cabinet 18 is doing well. Shaye also asserted an Open House at Sage Vista Park was
recently held with a successful turnout.

2.2) Radon Information Presentation from the Utah County Health Department
Wendy Wright with the Utah County Health Department gave a presentation on radon. She
presented basic information on radon gas. She encouraged the Council, and the public, to install
radon detectors in their homes.

Councilor Koivisto joined the meeting in person at 5:58 p.m.

The council members asked questions regarding radon, the materials that create radon, and how
Utah County participates in the program. Todd Munger will also help distribute the information
to the residents of Lehi to help people understand what a test kit looks like. Mayor Johnson also
encouraged the City to provide information about radon.
