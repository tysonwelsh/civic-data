Budget Committee Charter

The proposed budget committee charter included for the council’s consideration in the March
meeting is roughly similar to the charter presented in February, with substantive changes in
response to discussion in the meeting and other changes to reduce the volume of text. Below
are key details on the changes. Click here to view the staff report from the February meeting
and click here to view the draft charter from February, which is attached to a resolution in the
document posted online.

    •   In the February 2024 meeting, the council expressed reluctance to include members of
        the public on the committee, and the updated charter no longer provides for such
        membership
    •   Town manager, budget officer, and treasurer serve on the committee ex-officio, or “as a
        result of one’s position”
    •   The purpose of the committee is similar to what it has been in previous years, and
        similar to the purpose defined in the February 2024 draft charter, but with key
        differences related to the Town’s transition to a town manager-by-ordinance form of
        government. Generally, the purpose of the committee is to review draft proposed
        budget documents and assist the town manager, and staff, in preparing budgets for
        recommendation to the council. The committee also reviews elements of the Town’s
        financial planning and reporting processes and makes recommendations for their
        improvement.
    •   The committee does not have formal decision-making authority other than to approve
        meeting minutes.

Recommendation to create the Town of Alta Budget Committee and adopt the Budget
Committee Charter via Resolution 24-R-6

Staff recommends the council discuss any proposed edits to the updated Town of Alta Budget
Committee Charter and adopt it during the March 13, 2024 council meeting. Staff will contact
potential budget committee members to discuss meeting scheduling options as soon as
possible.
