FROM:          Craig Calvert, Purchasing Director

ITEM:          Request approval to enter into a contract with Firestorm Pyrotechnics, Inc for the
               July 4, 2020 Town Days Fireworks Show.

SUMMARY:

The Recreation Department wanted to change the fireworks show to a Computer
Choreographed / Computer Lit firework show. They would also like to increase the amount of
the fireworks show from $17,000 to $25,000.

Because this contract is for services in the 20/21 Fiscal Year Budget, by approving this contract
Council will need to approve $25,000.00 for the fireworks show in the 20/21 FY Budget.

Purchasing has issued a Request for Proposal for this service and an intent to award has been
issued.




FISCAL IMPACT: $25,000.00

FUNDING SOURCE: 10-65-530 - $25,000

ATTACHMENTS:
   Contract 19-1065-06

RECOMMENDED MOTION OR ACTION:

I move the City Council Approve Resolution No. 20-04 - Authorizing the City to enter into a
contract with Firestorm Pyrotechnics, Inc for the July 4, 2020 Town Days Fireworks Show.
