2.     Loveless Estates Subdivision Plat A (33 lots) amendment to the previously
       approved Final Plat to show newly proposed phasing.

       a.    Staff Presentation
       Shauna presented the amendment to the commission.

       b.      Commission Discussion
       Fran asked Brett Ludlow if the change was favorable, to which he confirmed that it was.

3.   Roderick Business Park Subdivision (7 lots) - Preliminary Plat, represented by
     Ben Wheat

     a.    Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented the Preliminary Plat to the Commission.

     b.      Commission Discussion

     between lots 6 & 7. He plans on having the site plan for Lot 1 ready for the next meeting.

4.   Rezone request by Mt. Peak Development, LLC a UT LLC, (Terry Messersmith) for
     property located at approximately 100 E. 1500 N. from R3-PUD overlay (previously
     CU-1) to an R3-Z (residential with zero lot-line overlay).

     a.      Open Public Hearing
     Public hearing opened at 7:08 p.m.
     Richard Peterson asked if this request was discussed at the last meeting. Shauna told
     him that it was originally discussed and matches the original plan. Joye Cannell asked
     how far the lot line is and voiced her concern about the transient aspect of small homes.
     Alan said that these lots are for sale, not for rent. Richard P. asked if the lot line includes
     the easement. Heather said that the easement will actually be in the front. Joye C. asked
     if we don’t own the easement anymore. Heather said that there will be a 15 foot alley, so
     no one can build on it. Mike Cannell asked if the property previously owned by the
     school district is being developed. Shauna confirmed, explaining that there was a land
     swap with The Wright Direction for a 42 lot subdivision with ¼ acre lots. Joye C. stated
     that her house has been rattled during the construction behind her home. Mike C. said
     he has noticed sediment in their toilets, as well as decreased water pressure. Shauna
     told them that she would pass this information on to the Public Works Director. Mike C.
     said they didn’t have any of these problems prior to the construction. Maria Petersen

     said she has had a lot of problems with her electricity. Cory Thomson stated the need to
     refocus on the matter at hand. Richard P. asked if the 35’ height agreement is still
     happening. Shauna said that an R3 zone allows for 35’, which is the usual house size.
     Seth commented that the allowable height in the R3 zone is actually 45’ Richard P. said
     that building height was a concern at a previous meeting. Could there be a variance?
     Seth told him that there can’t be a variance because that would require a zone change.
     Fran said that a variance can’t be approved based on public comment. Shauna assured
     that the developer is vested in our community. Seth said that anything over 35’ should
     be reviewed by the commission. Richard P. stated that he has noticed the last 3
     meetings have been geared more towards high density housing. Alan asked what the
     original zoning was for this property, to which Shauna responded CU-1. Alan said that
     without a zone change, there would be a potential for businesses. Therefore we looked
     at a zone change. Richard P. asked for clarification of the property boundary and voiced
     his concern about how the increase in housing would affect our water supply. He
     wondered when a moratorium would be appropriate. Alan stated that his questions lead
     to another topic, and Mr. Petersen is welcome to attend the commission meetings and
     review the general plan. Richard P. continued with his concerns and said he just doesn’t
     want the property to go any farther east. Fran said that it’s important to remember that
     the commission has to approve anything over 35’. She assured that the commission
     understands the public’s feelings and they agree.

     b.    Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented the rezone request to the commission.

     c.     Commission Discussion (Close Public Hearing)
     Public Hearing was closed at 7:28 p.m.

     d.    Action Item
     Motion: The motion was made to approve the rezone request by Mt. Peak
     Development, LLC.
     Made by: Cory Thomson Second: Karl Brough Outcome: Passed unanimously

5.   Northridge Estates Phased Subdivision

     a.     Plat C Subdivision (6 lots) - Vicinity, Preliminary, and Final Plat (combined
            consideration). Proposal includes three (3), twin-homes on six (6) lots with
            each lot separately owned.

            i.    Staff Presentation
            Shauna and Seth presented the proposal to the commission.

            ii.    Commission Discussion
            Seth pointed out that it’s unique to consider a preliminary plat and a final plat at

     the same time, but everything we are usually waiting on is done. Heather asked
     about a potential stoplight, and Brett L. said that was in UDOT’s hands. Fran
     asked if the twin homes would have the required 3 parking spots. Shauna
     responded that the city hasn’t approved the parking requirements yet, so 2 spots
     per unit will be required. Shauna continued that the application for this
     subdivision was submitted long before we even started discussing parking. Karl
     asked how the units will be oriented, and Brett L. said that they will share a
     property line side by side.

     iii.   Action Item
     Motion: The motion was made to accept the Plat C Subdivision Vicinity,
     Preliminary, and Final Plat with the DRC notes.
     Made by: Cory Thomson Second: Alan Hancock Outcome: Passed
     unanimously

b.   Plat D PUD Subdivision (40 units) - Vicinity Plan. Proposal includes five (5)
     Eight-plex condominium units. Mandatory HOA.

     i.    Staff Presentation
     Shauna and Seth presented the proposal to the commission.

     ii.     Commission Discussion
      Brett L. asked if this was the preliminary or vicinity. He said he was under the
     impression that the vicinity had already been approved. Shauna said this is for
     the vicinity plan. It was mentioned that we are working with the chief of police on
     the addressing of homes for emergency purposes. Karl wondered about the
     verbiage in the proposal. He pointed out that it should be 1500-1620, and the
     plan shows 1500-1600. Shauna said that at the preliminary it will change to
     1620. Karl said that his only issue is that of speed. Is there any way to offset the
     entrances or provide more signage? Seth said that it’s not a city street, and
     Shauna said they can only make suggestions to the developer. Brett said that the
     entrance to the subdivision will look like a driveway, rather than a road. Karl
     asked for the name of the subdivision, to which Brett answered, “Northridge.”
     Karl asked if there will be an identifying name at the entrance. There were a few
     other questions from the commissioners. Script and recording are available for
     review of additional comments and questions.

     iii.   Action Item
     Motion: The motion was made to accept the Plat D PUD Subdivision Vicinity
     Plan with DRC notes.
     Made by: Alan Hancock Second: Heather Robertson Outcome: Passed
     unanimously

6.   Title 10 Updates

     a.    Open Public Hearing
           Public Hearing was opened at 7:57 p.m.

           i.       Considering adjustments to all zones for the minimum lot size,
                    frontage, density, building height, and setbacks in all zones
                    (including accessory buildings)
           Skip asked if moving a building because of height will really make a difference.
           Fran stated that it doesn’t change the view, but it does decrease the usability.
           Shauna said that she had been thinking about Shari C.’s comments last week
           about windows. She wondered if windows need to be limited at a certain height?
           Fran asked if we are going to get to a point that the state will require certain
           things due to the housing shortage. Heather asked if we would need an 8’
           setback if the state says we have to allow secondary housing. Alan said he is
           still conflicted with this subject. Skip wondered if we could restrict the height, and
           Alan wondered if we could base the height on the primary structure. Skip asked
           if we could set a cap on the height, to which Karl said we can’t limit the height
           Skip responded that we absolutely can. Ann and Skip both expressed that they
           are not comfortable with basing height on primary structures. Fran asked if we
           have a current requirement, to which Seth responded 35’. Skip said his issue is
           with obnoxiously large buildings and thinks 35’ is too high. Heather asked if we
           can base building size on lot size. Skip said we need to set a standard, and Alan
           wondered if it could be based on a percentage of the property. Heather said she
           likes the lot size idea and Cory said that he would like to see them 10’ from the
           primary dwelling of the neighbors. Karl offered to come up with a visual example
           for the commission using Google Earth to determine lot coverage.

           ii.    Updating some uses in zones
           iii.   Updating Definitions and General Terms (Land-locking property and
                  flag-lots prohibited)

           iv.    Eliminating the Planned Residential Development (PRD) code

     b.    Staff Presentation

     c.    Commission Discussion

     d.    Action Item
           Motion: A motion was made for a continuance of the public hearing until the next
           regular meeting.
