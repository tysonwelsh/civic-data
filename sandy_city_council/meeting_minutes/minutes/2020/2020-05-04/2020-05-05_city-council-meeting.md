Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Robinson thanked the community for working Project Protect mask
                                  creation.

                                  Council Member Stroud thanked the Police Department for their educational video.

                                  Council Member Zoltanski thanked all of the residents and participants of the first Virtual
                                  Town Hall meeting last week. She asked about the reopening of restroom facilities in both
                                  County and City parks.

Mayor's Report

                                  Mayor Bradburn provided an operational update on COVID-19 response. The soft opening
                                  of City buildings occurred yesterday. Communication has happened to residents through
                                  the City website and City social media channels. Most services will occur over the next
                                  two weeks by appointment only.

CAO Report

                                  CAO Matt Huish noted that next week the new City website will go live (May 16). He also
                                  noted that stringent cleaning guidelines make it difficult to reopen parks restroom, but the
                                  Parks Department is working to reopen them in a methodical way.
