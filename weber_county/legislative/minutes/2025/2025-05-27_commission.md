                                                    Tuesday, May 27th, 2025 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Sharon Bolos, Gage Froerer, and James “Jim” H. Harvey
STAFF PRESENT: Jason Horne, of the Clerk/Auditor’s office; Chris Crockett, Deputy County Attorney; and Craig
D. Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME: Chair Bolos
B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: All in attendance were invited to recite the Pledge.
C. INVOCATION/MOMENT OF SILENCE: Commissioner Froerer: Yesterday was very important day for honoring
   our veterans and so I thought this morning would be an appropriate time to have a moment of silence. We all have
   special connections with those that served and family members going back to 1776 when this all started. Over a
   million veterans have sacrificed for our freedom. I think a lot on Memorial Day about my dad who served in the
   Merchant Marines. Merchant Marines don't get a lot of attention and credit. Everybody's heard of West Point
   back on the east coast where the US Army Academy is located; not too many people have heard of Kings Point
   where the Merchant Marine Academy is located. My dad graduated from Kings Point. Merchant Marines provide
   food, supplies, and ammunition to the areas it needed but they didn't have typically any weapons so they were
   escorted. I would suggest we have a moment of silence.
D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
     Commissioner Harvey: Memorial Day started as Decoration Day designed to honor those who paid the ultimate
     sacrifice for our freedom. When I was younger I didn’t want to visit the graves but now I look forward to it. My
     wife and I find great joy each year and spend the weekend traveling around.
     Chair Bolos: There are a lot of great traditions. My grandfather was a veteran of World War I and would invite us
     to place flags on graves in Ogden cemetery with him.

G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH RUNBECK ELECTION SERVICES FOR THE PRINTING AND
      POSTAGE SERVICES RELATED TO ELECTION MATERIALS.

         Lauren Shafer (Elections Director): Through competitive bid process we have selected Runbeck for our new
         printing and postage vendor. While we have a great working relationship with our previous vendor, K&H,
         Runbeck’s proposal was too good to pass up. The contract is for two years with three one year options for a
         possible total of five years. They are a nationally respected elections partner and we are excited to work with
         them. The pricing model in this contract should lead to a lot of long-term savings.

         Commissioner Froerer: Thank you for keeping the best interest of the taxpayers in mind and making sure it
         was a competitive bid.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a contract with Runbeck Election Services for the printing and postage
         services related to election materials; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye
