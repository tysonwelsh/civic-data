A Key Vision Element

YESTERDAY OUR BOARD APPROVED OUR ISSUING OF AN RFP DOCUMENT TO THE
DEVELOPMENT COMMUNITY TO SELECT OUR INITIAL DEVELOPMENT PARTNERS.
THAT WILL BE A MULTI-MONTH PROCESS WHICH WILL BE OPEN, TRANSPARENT
AND RIGOROUS. WE'LL SELECT PARTNERS THAT ARE EXPERIENCED AND HAVE
THE SKILL AND FINANCIAL WHEREWITH TO DO THIS BUT ALSO WHO SHARE OUR
VISION FOR DOING SOMETHING BOLD WHILE THINKING FOR THE STATE AND
WHO CARES ABOUT UTAH AS MUCH AS WE DO.

THE INMATES WILL BE MOVED FROM THE PRISON SITE PROBABLY NEXT JUNE
(2022) AND WE ANTICIPATE THAT DEMOLITION AND ABATEMENT WILL START
AROUND AUGUST OR SEPTEMBER (2022). ONCE THE SITE IS PREPARED THEN
THE STATE WILL BEGIN SOME WORK ON THE BACKBONE INFRASTRUCTURE FOR
THE SITE AND PREPARE IT FOR THOSE INITIAL DEVELOPERS TO COME IN AND
START ON PHASE ONE.
