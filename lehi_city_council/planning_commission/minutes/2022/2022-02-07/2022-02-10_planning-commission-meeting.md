3.1) Consideration of Gardner Company’s request for conditional use approval of the Lewis
Landing Industrial site plan, an 11.81-acre industrial development located at approximately
4150 West Waterbury Drive. (withdrawn from previous meeting)
Sierra presented information on the agenda item and clarified that this was not a conditional use.
The applicant would like an exception to that requirement.

Commissioner Nielsen asked if there were any questions. Commissioner Eyre clarified nothing
had changed from last meeting other than the conditional use.

The applicant, Ben Seastrand with the Gardner Company, was present. He stated there was a
concern with traffic last time he was present, and they have looked at the traffic study. He stated
they do not foresee traffic going through the neighborhood as a main traffic route. A couple of
reasons why is the access off 2100 North will be a seamless access and will provide them the
access. The other reason is any group they lease to they will have a firm trucking route
established and they will encourage the tenants to adhere to the route. The traffic engineer that
provided the traffic study, Ryan Hales from Hales Engineering, presented information on the
traffic study where they will be able to channel the truck routes to the East and West. He stated
trucks traveling through the neighborhood will provide a slower travel route and it will not be a
time savings. Ryan stated naturally, the neighborhoods are not a desirable place for the trucks to
travel. Truck restriction is also an option to force trucks down Waterbury drive.

Commissioner Britton asked about the housing units and the 31 acre park that was recently
approved and how the traffic near Drexler will impact that area. Ryan stated a traffic study had
not been ran near that area. Ryan stated there are options that can provide traffic calming
measures and they are working on the transportation master plan and connectivity and can work
with Ross Dinsdale with this issue. Commissioner Newall asked about signage to stop trucks
from using those neighborhood roads. Ryan stated those are designated truck routes and can
become problematic. Commissioner Everett asked how many trips per day. Ryan stated there
were 2500 trips per day: 226 morning peak and 233 evening peak.

Commissioner Nielsen clarified this was not a public hearing item.

The Commission and Mr. Hales discussed the traffic study and the traffic impact and landscape
requirements.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to grant approval of the Lewis Landing Industrial
        site plan, an 11.81-acre industrial development located at approximately 4150 West
        Waterbury Drive granting the condition that it will not be a formalized road in the
        center of the property, noting the DRC comments. Adding the CC&R’s to the truck route
        to avoid a formalized traffic route through the residential zones. Commissioner Ellis
        seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.


3.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Park Place Suites request for review of a General
Plan Amendment from Commercial to Mixed Use on 0.51 acres located at approximately
2600 North 1200 West.
3.2 and 3.3 were heard simultaneously

Sierra Pierson presented information on the agenda item.

Ross Dinsdale stated Glade Kirkham’s main concern with his DRC comment is the main access to
the power substation is through this property and if they need to get equipment through there it
could be an issue. Ross stated his DRC comment is directed to TOD areas for any new density.
This is a mid-point for future TOD’s but is not TOD on the General Plan. Any property along this
area along 2100 North can make the same argument and he is concerned about setting a precedent
if this is amended to mixed-use. The property owner was concerned about traffic and on street
parking. Kim Struthers also added that they are in the middle of defining mixed use and there could
be different ways to approach that and how the new ordinance is written. Ryan Hales stated the
road and parking aisle would be able to accommodate a WB-62 truck and the power company
would be able to access the power substation. There would be 90 degree parking on the street.

The applicant, Ryan Hales, was present. He stated they had purchased the property and had plans
for development but stopped progress due to COVID. Ryan showed a brief presentation of the
development and the surrounding area. He stated HDR, SFR, and Commercial surrounded the
property. There is a future TOD, UTA/BRT route, a Historic South Rail Trail, the 1200 West major
arterial and I-15 frontage road system as appealing features. He clarified this was a vertical mixed
use project and would house Hales Engineering Offices, have underground parking, residential
units, and have lock/walk-in units. There will be rooftop access that will have residential units and
accessible by an elevator. Ryan stated there will be parking stalls in the parking garage, as well as
bike parking and storage. There would not be access provided to the rail trail through the property,
as it is not allowed, but would be accessible through a wider walk at 1200 West.

3.5) Public Hearing and consideration of Robert and Diane Myer’s request for conditional
use approval of the Myer’s Flag lot located at 2269 North 600 West.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant will have to submit a conditional use application if all requests are met.

Commissioner Everett wanted to make sure all setbacks will be met. Sierra stated it does appear
all will be met. Commissioner asked about the existing garage.

The applicant, Diane and Robert Myers, were present. They stated the garage was put to the side
of the house instead of the front because it previously got denied.

Kailynne Osborne was present and stated she lives to the south of the proposed flag lot and is
opposing the request. In a previous meeting, Council discussed preserving low density and
splitting larger lots and denied the change. She stated they meet the flag lot approval, but does
not meet all the requirements. It is a safety issue because it is at an angle and is across the street
from a park. The safety issue is the biggest concern for her. She feels it takes away diversity and
lot sizes does not comply with TOD lot sizes. She would like to see the already developed areas
left alone. Would like to see the lot left alone where it has animal rights, especially for future
property owners. She wants to make sure the 20 foot widening of the roadway will happen. She
has the same concerns as the ADU with the increased traffic, noise, dust, and privacy. She would
like to point out that her backyard sits lower than the property that is going to be developed, and
noise and dust is not always the same to others and she is concerned it will not be taken care of
because not everyone has the same definitions. She stated she wants to know the definition of all
weather and hard surface roadways and would like it in writing for clarification so there would
not be a way around it for variances and so it would be clear and specifically defined. In the
concept plan it shows that house facing south, she would like the house to face the street and not
face her property. She would like to know if the house could be angled at a different direction.
She understands this is just a concept. She inquired if there is a code for the mailbox and if it has
to be on their property line because it will impede the driveway. She stated she would like to see
a requirement that there be a height cap to the house, no higher than the house to the West, not to
impede the view. She would like the motion to include a privacy fence of 8 feet and would ask
the Commission to clearly describe the type of 8 foot fence and feels it is reasonable. She had
concerns with the 12 parking spaces in the residential zone and would like to know why so many
are needed. She is asking the Commissioners to deny the request, or at least table the item. She
wants the motion to include conditions, height restrictions to the house, 8 foot privacy fence,
restrict an ADU on the flag lot. She would like the definition for the flag lot hard surfaced road
to be clear.

Doug Osborne had a concern about the water shares that were turned over. He knew that Farren
Collins built a shop and was going to try and build a house and knew he needed water. He was
told if he wanted to build a house, he needed to turn over water shares. He thinks it will be unfair
if it is subdivided if they are forced to turn over water shares, especially with the water problems
in the state.
Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged the online comments that were in favor.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Nelsen opened the public hearing and acknowledged the email that came through
that was against the request.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen asked City Staff to address water rights, hard surfaced/all weathered road,
elevation for water flow, angled drive, and house height.

Ross Dinsdale stated with water rights there is an annexation agreement they will have to
dedicate culinary water. The driveway at an angle is different because you can use more of the
driveway to straighten car since it is your own driveway. There are sight distance requirements,
but there are no concerns with the City with this driveway. The building code requires each lot to
retain their own lot drainage. They may have to put in a curb. The City will look at that when the
plan is submitted. Ross stated in regard to house height, commissioners can apply conditions.
Sierra stated conditions can be imposed, but by standard it would need to meet height maximum.
Ross stated that is why there is a landscape buffer. In regard to the fence, it would need to have a
zone approval, and would need to be made as a condition to come back and apply for a fence. A
hard surface is defined as asphalt or concrete. A typical flag lot has concrete or asphalt to the flag
stem and then continues with gravel or concrete. Only two stalls are required for parking.
Each flag lot is determined by location and will have to meet yard setbacks. This is beyond
regulations of the code. The state and local guidelines determine ADU regulations.

The applicant, Robert and Diane Myer's, addressed the concerns from the Commissioners and
the public comment. The direction of the home, because of the setbacks, is the only options for
the direction of the house, they are fine to put asphalt or concrete for the hard surface. The fire
department did not have issues. They submitted eight foot privacy fence application. The
driveway is being used daily.

Commissioner Bunker stated he is not worried about orientation of the house or an 8 foot privacy
fence because the distance of the house. Commissioner Newall agreed with Commissioner
Bunker, especially where the applicants stated they are happy to install the eight foot fence.
Commissioner Ellis stated he would rather see landscaping than fence. Commissioner Britton
stated it is well within the property owners’ rights to subdivide, even if it means losing animal
rights.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve of Robert and Diane
        Myer’s request for conditional use approval of the Myer’s Flag lot located at 2269
        North 600 West; including all DRC comments. With the findings that the proposed flag
        lot meets the requirements of the R-1-12 zone, can have reasonable conditions put
        on it to mitigate against nuisances to the neighbors, will not be detrimental or
        injurious to property or improvements in the vicinity, will be located and conducted
        in a manner in compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General
        Plan and the purposes of this Code, that the property on which the use, building, or
        other structure is proposed to be established is of adequate size and dimensions to
        permit construction of the facilities and the conduct of the use in such a manner
        that it will not be detrimental to adjoining properties and the area; hard surface
        roadway defined as asphalt, concrete, bricks or pavers, that the applicants will retain the
        drainage on their property, with the recommendation that the applicant work with the
        neighbors regarding an 8 foot fence. Commissioner Jackson seconded the motion.
        Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
