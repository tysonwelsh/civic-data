THE SOUTHERN UTAH REGIONAL TRAIL STANDARDS AS_ PART OF THE
WASHINGTON COUNTY TRANSPORTATION MASTER PLAN WHICH IS AN
ELEMENT OF THE GENERAL PLAN

Destination Development Manager Leslie Fonger explained that St. George City received a federal
grant to review paved trail standards. The proposed standards have guidelines for construction and
development of paved trails, including trail dimensions, permitted uses, levels of service, signage,
trail and street interface, and active transportation facilities generally.

the General Plan. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3313, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE UDWR FOR RAVEN MANAGEMENT
FUNDING

Mr. Schijf explained with the Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP), the desert tortoise is protected. In
recent years, raven densities have increased to a level where they are above recommended levels.
This agreement allows for raven control measures to be implemented in order to ensure adequate
survival rates of juvenile tortoises, with the Utah Department of Natural Resources (UDWR)
reimbursing Washington County up to $12,000 for raven control efforts,

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3313, A
Resolution Approving a Cooperative Agreement with the UDWR for Raven
Management Funding. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow
voting aye.
