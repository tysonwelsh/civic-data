CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION
CA-2-2020 Petition by LONESTAR BUILDERS requesting approval for a conditional use
          amendment for a carwash in a shopping center. The property is located at 5668 W.
          Parkway Blvd. on 1.08 acres and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody
          Knapp at 801-963-3497)

            Discussion:

            Jody explained the applicant is looking to add a Quick Quack Carwash to the WinCo
            Shopping Center. She stated the parcel they are looking to build on was initially
            approved to be a parking lot and has since been divided to create a parcel for the
            carwash. Jody explained that the east-west and north-south pedestrian circulation would
            be changed. She explained the east-west connection will remain, however most of the
            curb cut access sites will be closed. Jody stated the north-south connection will be re-
            routed as part of the development. Jody stated the applicant is wanting to add a
               monument sign, making 4 monument signs for the shopping center. Jody explained this
               will still meet the original guidelines.

               Commissioner Fuller asked if the current parking lot will be enough for the carwash.
               Jody stated they currently have adequate parking and will not need additional parking
               spaces.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:48 p.m.
