 61   SITE PLAN – Approving the site plan of the Orem City Center located at 56 North State Street in the C2 zone.
 62
 63   Staff Presentation:
 64   Orem City requested plans be drawn up and a new City Center built on the existing City Center location at 56 North
 65   State Street. After many meetings and plan reviews, the project is ready to begin. The current City Center building
 66   will remain standing and open for all Orem residents while the construction of the new City Center building is built.
 67   Once the new building is open, the old building will be demolished, and a new courtyard constructed.
 68
 69   The new building will be located next to Center Street with a small portion fronting State Street. This way the
 70   construction on the new building can go forward while the existing building stays standing and open for City use.
 71
 72   Architecture: The exterior of the building will be masonry brick that is dark and then light toned brick which is a
 73   blend of three (3) colors. This meets the building materials requirements outlined in Section 22-8-8(B)(5)(d)(i) for
 74   the State Street Corridor.
 75
 76   Height: The new City Center building will be forty-
 77   eight (48) feet high with a four (4) foot high parapet
 78   along the roof line for a total of fifty-two (52) feet in
 79   height. This additional four feet in height for
 80   municipal buildings in a C2 zone was approved by
 81   the City Council on February 28, 2023. (22-8-8(A)
 82
 83   Landscaping: There will be a landscaped/sitting area
 84   between the new building and the existing library.
 85
 86   Parking: There will be a new parking area between the existing library, Public Safety and the new building. Based
 87   on the size of the new building and all existing buildings located at the City Center a total of eight hundred thirty-
 88   three (833) parking stalls are required and there are eight hundred eighty-nine (889) proposed parking stalls. (22-15-
 89   4(B)
 90
 91   Recommendation:
 92   The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City Code. Staff recommends
 93   the Planning Commission approve the site plan of the Orem City Center located at 56 North State Street in the C2
 94   zone.

119   PUBLIC HEARING – REZONE – Amending Section 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by
120   changing the zone of approximately 4.86 acres from the C3 zone to the C2 zone located generally at 877 North State
121   Street.
122
123   Staff Presentation:
124   There is a large parcel of vacant land at approximately 877 North State
125   Street. The smaller parcel that fronts State Street is in the C2 zone. The
126   larger parcel that front 400 West are in the C3 zone. The applicant would
127   like it all to be one zone, the C2 zone. The total acreage is 7.10 acres and
128   the applicant would like to rezone approximately 4.86 acres from the C3
129   zone to the C2 zone. (22-1-5)
130
131   As this entire piece of property is owned by one developer, it is difficult to
132   deal with two different zones. The new commercial projects will be tied
133   together with through access from State Street to 400 West. Having the
134   entire property be one zone will make it easier to work the entire piece of
135   property as one development.
136
137   This request is for the rezone only, not the preliminary and final plat. If the City Council approves the rezone
138   application, the applicant will need to apply for a preliminary and final plat. However, a concept plan is provided to
139   show potential development including the access from State Street to 400 West. (17-4-5)
140
141   Recommendation:
142   Staff recommends that the Planning Commission forward positive a recommendation to the City Council to amend
143   Section 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone of approximately 4.86 acres from
144   the C3 zone to the C2 zone located generally at 877 North State Street.

180   PUBLIC HEARING – TEXT AMENDMENT – Amending Appendix A of the Orem City Code by enacting
181   Standard Land Use Code 6266 Pediatric Palliative Care Facility and enacting 22-6-9(N) Pediatric Palliative Care
182   Facility in Chapter 22 of the Orem City Code.
183
184   Staff Presentation:
185   The applicant is requesting these additions to the Orem
186   City Code in an effort to have the opportunity to convert
187   the property located at 370 West 500 North in Orem to a
188   Pediatric Palliative Care Facility.
189
190   This property is currently zoned for a 26 bed facility for
191   ambulatory elderly. This change would allow the facility
192   to be changed to a 26 bed facility for individuals, typically
193   children, with medical challenges. The facility would
194   provide overnight respite, transitional care from hospital
195   to home and care at times of increased medical need. It
196   also would serve as a facility for patients who are living
197   at home to receive outpatient treatments.
198
199   According to the applicant the families and children who need these services have to leave Utah County to receive
200   this kind of care. Currently there are five of these facilities in the country.
201
202   Recommendation:
203   Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to amend
204   appendix A of the Orem City Code by enacting Standard Land Use Code 6266 Pediatric Palliative Care Facility and
205   to enact 22-6-9 (N) Pediatric Palliative Care Facility in Chapter 22 of the Orem City Code.
206
