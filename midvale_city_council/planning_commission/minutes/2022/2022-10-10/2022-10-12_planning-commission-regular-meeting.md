GENERAL SESSION

Chair Snow called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. He
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS

   2. Midvale City requests amendments to Title 17-7-1 of the Midvale City Municipal
      Code to address the Duplex approval process.

        Staff proposes an amendment to the above listed chapter and table of the Municipal
        Code, permitting duplexes in the SF-1(DO) as an allowed use rather than a conditional
        use. Duplex proposals currently go to the Planning Commission for approval, through
        the conditional use process. Staff proposes removing them from conditional use
        approval to staff level approval. All code requirements related to placement, height, etc.
        will remain the same. This change will allow the Planning Commission to focus on
        larger, city-wide planning issues; given that duplex approvals are administrative in
        nature (if all the boxes are checked, they must be approved), while expediting the
        approval process with staff level approval.

        Public notice has been sent to affected entities as required in 17-3-9. B of the Municipal
        Code. No comments have been received as of the writing of this report.

        Staff recommends approval.

   1. Upcoming Meetings and Projects.
         a. Main Street Project updates
               • Mr. Knobloch reviews a video of Main Street. He explains that the
                  Planning Commissioners are a part of maintaining the form-based code for
                  Midvale. It tightly regulates the area to strictly regulate/maintain the
                  desired look for the city.
               • Currently there is a focus on the Downtown area in Midvale. The RDA is
                  completing a branding study to help market the city.
               • The Planning Department is working on an urban design study that
                  focuses on streetscape improvements to create a more inviting atmosphere.
               • The RDA has a moderate-income housing program that will be supported
                  by specific developers that allows dwelling units to be more affordable.
               • The Court project will be a mixed-use building with both commercial and
                  residential space. The current basketball court sale had city involvement.
               • The RDA has begun a mural festival to initiate the Art District here in
                  Midvale. This will be an annual event.
               • There is also an Art House Program in the work that will provide a home
                  for artists and for the community to gather, see their work, and witness the
                  creations in the process.
               • The Corner & 6th project is located at Main Street and 6th Avenue. Here
                  there will be a mixed-use building established. Mixed-use of main level
                  commercial use and upper floors as residential use.
               • The West Main Apartments project will be located on Depot Street. No
                  development process has been made besides the developer securing the
                  lot.
               • The former bank building on the corner of Main & Center will be under
                  renovations. Not only on the façade but also a lot of structural
                  improvements.
               • Downtown Programming is being activated to increase exposure to the
                  area and inspire investors in the private sector.
               • The RDA annual events draw in many Utah residents from all over the
                  valley. This includes Trick-Or-Treat on Main Street, Light Up Main
                  Street, Midvale Mural Festival, and the Car Show.
               • The Planning Department has been working with the CDC on an
                  urbanization and walkability project to improve pedestrian activity in both
                  experience and safety.
