                                          Nephi City
                                    Work Session Minutes
                                     December 14, 2021
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                 Nephi City Council Chambers




1. The council discussed the Gordon McPherson land agreement and talked about the
   possibility of annexing the area and different zoning options. The council agreed for the city
   to initiate the annexation process since some of the property in the annexation area is
   owned by the city.
2. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the public hearing for the W. Wright Industrial
   Annexation will be held December 21, 2021, at 7:00 p.m. An annexation agreement is being
   drafted and will need council approval before completing the annexation.
3. Mr. Atkinson reviewed the Powell Yards subdivision agreement with the council.
4. Mr. Atkinson reviewed the need for a zone change for the Gardner Village Subdivision from
   a zero-lot line overlay to a PUD overlay.
5. Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the Planning and Zoning Commission is reviewing the
   CU Zone and are working on conceptual zoning of the city’s annexation area.
6. Mr. Atkinson reviewed financing options for the Hive project. The city will need
   approximately $2.7 million in a sales tax bond to complete the project. The acquisition of
   the bishop’s storehouse that is located west of the Hive was discussed. Superintendent
   Kodey Hughes addressed the council and said that the storehouse could be used for
   additional storage needs, parking, and possibly some classrooms. The council was generally
   in favor of pursuing purchasing the storehouse. Mr. Atkinson showed debt service payment
   amounts if the city borrowed the $2.7 million and the debts service payment if the city
   borrowed an additional $500,00 for the storehouse.
7. City Recorder Lisa Brough asked the council if they wanted to keep the same meeting
   schedule for next year. The council agreed to keep the schedule the same and directed Ms.
   Brough to bring the 2022 annual meeting schedule for official approval to next week’s
   council meeting.
8. Work Session adjourned at 8:25 pm.

Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielsen, Councilor Larry Ostler, Councilor Justin Seely, Councilor Skip
Worwood, Councilor Nathan Memmott, City Administrator Seth Atkinson, City Attorney Kasey
Wright, Lisa Brough, JD Parady, Dr. Kodey Hughes, John Bradley, Craig Oswald, Terry Cook.
