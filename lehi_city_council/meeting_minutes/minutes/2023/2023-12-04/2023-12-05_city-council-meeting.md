5:00 PM, Broadbent Community Room, 128 N 100 E

1. Welcome and Roll Call

2. Christmas Dinner with City Council, Planning Commission, and staff.

3. Presentation and Discussion of the North Lehi Station Area Plan (formerly Vistas Area
Plan)
The consultants presented on the Vistas Area Plan, emphasizing the importance of transit-
supportive development, connectivity, and public input. They also noted that there's a strong desire
for a mix of housing types, including affordable housing, the desire for more retail, entertainment,
and dining options in the area. Survey participants also expressed interest in having green spaces
and recreational opportunities. Connectivity and walkability were highlighted as important
aspects, given the grade changes and topography of the area.

Regular Session of the City Council
Immediately Following the Joint Session, Council Chambers, 153 N 100 E, Lehi

1. Welcome and Roll Call
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present.


2. Consideration of Resolution #2023-70 requesting the recertification of the Lehi City
Justice Court.
Judge Morgan Cummings was available to answer any questions.

Motion: Councilor Southwick moved to approve Resolution #2023-70 requesting the
recertification of the Lehi City Justice Court. Councilor Condie seconded the motion.

Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


3. Consideration of Resolution #2023-71 a Resolution of the Board of Canvassers certifying
the Official Canvassers' Report of the November 21, 2023 General Municipal Election for
Lehi City, Utah.

Motion: Councilor Southwick moved to approve Resolution #2023-71 a Resolution of the
Board of Canvassers certifying the Official Canvassers' Report of the November 21, 2023 General
Municipal Election for Lehi City, Utah. Councilor Hancock seconded the motion.

Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


4. Consideration of Adjourning into a Closed Session to discuss the character,
