                                  Brian McCuistion, Planning Direct gave a presentation on the creation of the Kuwahara
                                  Wholesale Proposed Special Development Zone.

                                  Council questions followed.

                                  The Chair opened Public Comment for this item. Dustin Fratto provided instruction to
                                  attendees on how to comment.

                                  Alex and Sara Kuwahara expressed their thanks to the City staff and Council for the new
                                  zone.

                                  Steve Van Maren expressed a concern about dropping the SD designation from two acres
                                  to one acre, and recommended an alternative if certain conditions are met. He also noted
                                  a concern with the surface materials of the parking area, and the requirement to retain
                                  storm water on the property.

                                  Sharon Jensen spoke in favor of Kuwahara Wholesale as a positive amenity to the
                                  neighborhood.

                                  Public Comment closed. City Attorney Lynn Pace brought the Council's attention to the
                                  narrow uses of the property. Also, the Planning Commission created two different
                                  temporary use windows for Farmers Markets.

                                  Council questions and comments followed.




                                  A motion was made by Brooke Christensen, seconded by Monica Zoltanski, that
                                  this That the City Council adopt the proposed ordinance #21-13, which are
                                  amendments to the Land Development Code and shown in Exhibit "A", for the
                                  following reasons:

                                  1.           The proposal complies with the Purpose of the Land Development
                                  Code as stated in section 21-01-03.

                                  2.            Compliance with the Goals and Policies of the General Plan by
                                  establishing appropriate development standards for all uses and zoning

                                  foothills of the City.

                                  The Chair opened the item to public comment. Dustin Fratto provided instruction.

                                  Ellen Birrell expressed support for the ordinance if it were to be amended to all of the
                                  property on the east side of Wasatch Blvd to the northern-most edge of Sandy

                                  Megan Sieverts also expressed support if the ordinance was amended to include all of
                                  the land to the northern edge of the City.

                                  Leslie Kovach also recommended extending the ordinance to the northern-most edge of
                                  Sandy City.

                                  Public comment closed. Council Member Nicholl spoke to the limitation of the ordinance
                                  to areas of steep slopes. Council discussion followed regarding the mechanics of
                                  expanding the area included in the ordinance.
