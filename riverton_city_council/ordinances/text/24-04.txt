                                          RIVERTON        CITY, UTAH
                                           ORDINANCE NO. 24- 04


      AN ORDINANCE           ADOPTING      UPDATES        TO RIVERTON     CITY' S STANDARDS       AND
         SPECIFICATIONS           MANUAL, ORDINANCE               PROPOSED   BY RIVERTON      CITY.


       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has held a public hearing to consider said standards and
specifications and forwarded a recommendation to the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said standards and
specifications; and


        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
adopt the updated Riverton City Standards and Specifications Manual as described in Exhibit " A".

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as follows:

       Section   1.   The updated Riverton City Standards and Specifications Manual, shall be, and hereby is,
                      adopted as shown in Exhibit " A."


       Section 2. Section 3. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 16th day of April, 2024
