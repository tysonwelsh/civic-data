C-10-2020
Mele Tile (Outside Storage)
3650 W. 2150 S.
M Zone (.43 acres)

The applicant, Jeff Titmus representing the Mele Tile, is requesting conditional use approval for
outside storage at 3650 W. 2150 S. This property is zoned M, manufacturing and the West
Valley City General Plan designates this area as light manufacturing. The surrounding zone is M,
manufacturing on all sides. The surrounding uses include light industrial, warehouse/office and
car sales/repair uses.
As part of the City’s ongoing clean-up efforts a thorough site inspection was conducted of this
property during a new business license review. During that inspection there were several
violations noted on this property, including outside storage. The new business on site, for Mele
Tile, would like to utilize the north portion of the property for outside storage. Therefore, they
have submitted a conditional use application to get that violation resolved.

This location is used as the tile/granite fabrication shop for Mele Tile. They do not have office or
retail operations from this location. So, the front portion of the site would be the parking area for
the 6 fabrication employees. Then the rear yard will be used for storage. Currently that portion of
the site is in need of maintenance/resurfacing and the applicant has indicated that will be
completed on 6/22, prior to the public hearing for this use. The entire storage area will be
surfaced with asphalt and must screened from public view.

The landscaping and building façade/signage is also in disrepair and in need of maintenance and
the existing dumpster on site needs to be relocated or screened in a masonry enclosure.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:

           1. The approved use for this application is for outside storage of granite materials
              and supplies for Mele Tile.

           2. The outside storage area must be paved with asphalt and the appropriate access
              shall be maintained throughout the site.

           3. Outside storage is only permitted on the north side of the site as outlined in the
              approved site plan and must be screened from public view.

           4. The existing landscaping on site shall be maintained with a minimum of 50% live
              plant material and kept free of weeds. A tree is required in the landscaping island
              on the east side of the entrance. If the large existing tree on the west side of the
              entrance is removed, then trees are required at a spacing of one for every 30’ of
              frontage.

           5. The dumpster location must meet the requirements set forth in 7-2-118 of the
              West valley City Municipal Code.

           6. The exterior façade of the building shall be maintained and repaired, including but
              not limited to all signage and damage caused from previous signage.

           7. Must meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies.

           8. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.
Discussion:   Jody Knapp presented the application. Joy Miya, representing the applicant,
