       JACOB ATKIN – PROPOSED THAT WE STAY WITH THE POTENTIAL $1.8 MILLION TO BE REIMBURSED AFTER
       EXPENSES ARE INCURRED. IF THE 32% OF THE PROJECT IS LESS THAN $1.8 MILLION, THEN THE COUNTY WOULD
       ONLY BE BILLED FOR A TOTAL OF 32% OF THE REPLACEMENT PROJECT.

       COMMISSIONER LEE – MOTION TO APPROVE AND SIGN THE REVISED AGREEMENT WITH THE STRIKING OUT OF
       THE LINE MENTIONED ABOVE UNDER SECTION 5B AND ALSO WITH AN UNDERSTANDING THAT THE $1.8
       MILLION BEING CONTRIBUTED, IF IT EXCEEDS THAT AMOUNT, THAT IT REMAINS THE 1.8 FOR UTAH COUNTY
       AND IF IT COMES IN LOWER, THEN THE 32% WILL REFLECT.

       AYE: ALL IN FAVOR
