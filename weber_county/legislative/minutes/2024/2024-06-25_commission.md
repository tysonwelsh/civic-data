D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: Since we are coming close to Pioneer Day, I recently had the
   opportunity to visit southeastern Utah. Missionaries from southwestern Utah were called on a mission to
   southeastern Utah. Part of that journey involved going over some very rugged territory northwest of Bluff called
   Hole in the Rock where there was a 10-12 foot space in the cliffs leading down a steep slope down to the river.
   They spent six weeks lowering all the equipment and supplies for the 260 people. I do not know if we quite
   understand the hardships they went through and how dedicated and tough they were.
