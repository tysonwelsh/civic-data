Analysis and Findings 
Section 10-3-18 (I) of the Snyderville Basin Development Code sets the following grounds for 
vacating or changing a plat: 
 
1. If the county manager or land use authority is satisfied that neither the public nor any 
person will be materially injured by the proposed vacation, alteration or amendment, 
and there is good cause for the vacation, alteration or amendment, the county manager 
or land use authority may vacate, alter or amend the plat, any portion of the plat, or any 
road or lot. 
  
Analysis: The applicant is permitted to build six detached single-family homes on the 
current lots of record. However, given the size and location of these lots, development 
is unlikely to be feasible. Furthermore, their dimensions and placements do not 
facilitate efficient delivery of services. Applicable service providers have reviewed and 
provided confirmation they can provide services for the proposed lots. Staff finds no 
evidence that the public nor any person would be materially injured by the amendment. 
COMPLIES 
 
2. No plat amendment shall be approved which results in an increase in density. 
 
Analysis: Density is defined in the Code as, “The number of dwelling units per acre with 
respect to residential land uses.” The applicant is requesting to amend the location of 6 
existing lots of record that are currently eligible for the development of detached single-
family residences. The amendment would not result in an increase in density. COMPLIES
