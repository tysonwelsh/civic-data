- Ralph Clegg, Health Department Executive Director —

COMMISSIONER AINGE, WANTS A BRIEF OVERVIEW AND WANTS TO KNOW WHAT THE
OFFSET AMOUNT IS FOR.

RALPH CLEGG, HEALTH DEPARTMENT, WEBER STATE UNIVERSITY HAS AGREED TO
TAKE THE ALL THE DATA FROM THE VEHICLE EMMISSION PROGRAMS IN THE STATE.
COMBINE IT INTO ONE DATABASE AND USE THE DMV DATABASE AS WELL TO TRACK
THE VEHICLES THAT HAVE AN UNKNOWN OUTCOME. USUALLY THESE ARE VEHICLES
THAT HAVE FAILED THE EMMISSIONS TEST AND OFTEN WILL GO TO ANOTHER COUNTY
TO REGISTER RATHER THAN THE COUNTY THEY RESIDE. THIS WILL HELP US TRACK
THAT AND WORK WITH THE DMV TO MAKE SURE WE ARE CAPTURING THOSE VEHICLES.
THEY MAY STILL BE OPERATING HERE EVEN THOUGH THEY ARE REGISTERED
SOMEWHERE ELSE. US EPA AS PART OF THE STATE IMPLEMENTATION PLAN AND THE
CLEAN AIR ACT REQUIRES THE EMMISSIONS PROGRAMS. THE HAVE A HARD TIME
APPROVING DE-CENTRALIZED PROGRAMS. THE MUCH PREFER TO HAVE GOVERNMENT
RUN PROGRAMS BECAUSE THE FEEL THEY HAVE BETTER CONTROL THAN THE PRIVATE
GARAGE LOCATIONS. THIS WILL HELP US BRING THE LEVEL DOWN WHICH IS HIGHER
IN UTAH THAN THE EPA LIKES TO SEE. THIS IS OUR EFFORT TO TRY AND ADDRESS THAT.
