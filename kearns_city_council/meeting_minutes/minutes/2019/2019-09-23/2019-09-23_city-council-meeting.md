Council Business

Ordinance – Title 2

            The Council reviewed an ordinance repealing and replacing Title 2 of the Kearns
Metro Township Code regarding council administration and had concerns with the following
sections:

                    Section 2.04.180
                    Section 2.04.190

This item will come back for consideration at a future meeting.

Interlocal Agreements

    First Amendment of the Master Interlocal Agreement

             The Council reviewed the First Amendment of the Master Interlocal Agreement
between the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD), Salt Lake County and the
Metro Townships for municipal, administrative, and operational services.

              Council Member Peterson, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to approve
the First Amendment of the Master Interlocal Agreement. The motion passed unanimously.

    Second Amendment of the Master Interlocal Agreement

             The Council reviewed the Second Amendment of the Master Interlocal Agreement
between the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD), Salt Lake County, and the
Metro Townships for municipal, administrative, and operational services.

               No vote was taken.

    Addressing Services

              The Council reviewed the Interlocal Agreement between the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District (GSLMSD), Salt Lake County, and the Metro Townships for
addressing services.

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to approve
the Addressing Services Agreement. The motion passed unanimously.
