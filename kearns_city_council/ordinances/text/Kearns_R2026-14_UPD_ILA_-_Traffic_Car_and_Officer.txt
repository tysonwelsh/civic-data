                       INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT
                                            BETWEEN
             UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT OF GREATER SALT LAKE
                                               -AND-
                                       CITY OF KEARNS



       THIS INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT (“Agreement”) is made and

entered into the _______ day of __________________, 2026 (“Effective Date”), by and between

UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT OF GREATER SALT LAKE, an interlocal entity of the State

of Utah (“UPD”), and CITY OF KEARNS, a municipal corporation of the State of Utah (the

“CITY”). UPD and the City may be referred to collectively as the “Parties” and may be referred

to individually as a “Party.”

                                           WITNESSETH:

       WHEREAS, UPD provides law enforcement services to the City; and

       WHEREAS, UPD requested that the City contribute funds to assist with the funding needed

for UPD to deploy a dedicated traffic officer and vehicle in the Kearns Precinct (“Traffic Costs”);

and

       WHEREAS, UPD also requested that the City transfer funds from the Kearns Precinct fund

balance (“SLVLESA Funds”) that the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area

(“SLVLESA”) manages to cover increased costs associated with UPD’s Kearns Precinct

(“Additional Precinct Costs”) in fiscal year 2026-2027.

       NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises and covenants hereinafter

contained, the Parties agree as follows:

       1.      Term. The Term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and

continue for a period of three (3) years (“Initial Term”). At the expiration of the Initial Term, this




                                                  1
Agreement shall automatically renew on an annual basis (“Renewal Term(s)”), unless terminated

in accordance with Section 9 of this Agreement.

       2.      Definition of “Fiscal Year.” For the purposes of this Agreement, the term “fiscal

year” means July 1 through June 30.

       3.      Traffic Costs. The City shall contribute monies in the amount of Two Hundred

Nine Thousand Dollars ($209,300) to UPD for fiscal year 2026-2027, and One Hundred Seventy

Nine Thousand and Thee Hundred Dollars ($179,300) to UPD for the remaining fiscal years (plus

any adjustment due to salary increase or reduction) of the Term to pay the Traffic Costs. UPD shall

provide the City with a written invoice for the Traffic Costs by July 30 of each fiscal year, and the

City shall pay such invoice within thirty (30) days of receiving the written invoice. The City shall

have full decision-making authority to determine how to pay the Traffic Costs, including using

monies the City owns or otherwise controls or by seeking approval from SLVLESA to allocate

SLVLESA Funds to UPD.

       4.      Additional Precinct Costs.

               (a)     By July 30, 2026, UPD shall submit a written invoice to the City for the

       Additional Precinct Costs for fiscal year 2026-2027, which shall not exceed One Hundred

       Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($125,000.00). Within thirty (30) days of receiving the

       written invoice from UPD, the City shall either pay directly or from Kearns Precinct Fund

       Balance held by UPD or obtain approval from SLVLESA to pay the amount owing to UPD

       for the Additional Precinct Costs.

               (b)     UPD may request Additional Precinct Costs in subsequent fiscal years

       during the Term, and the City shall consider such requests, provided that the City shall

       have no obligation to grant such requests under this Agreement. If the City approves the

       payment of Additional Precinct Costs in addition to those authorized under Subsection 4(a),

       the City shall approve such requests by June 30 of the fiscal year in question and UPD shall


                                                 2
       submit a written invoice to the City by July 30 of that fiscal year. Within thirty (30) days

       of receiving the written invoice from UPD, the City shall either pay directly or from Kearns

       Precinct Fund Balance held by UPD or shall obtain approval from SLVLESA to pay the

       amount owing to UPD for the Additional Precinct Costs.

       5.        Annual Budgeting. UPD will give notice of the amount of any salary and benefit

changes for subsequent fiscal years that may impact the Traffic Costs or Additional Precinct Costs

as soon as can be reasonably estimated but no later than April 30 of each fiscal year of the Term.

The City shall consider the information UPD provides under this Section when developing its

budget for the next fiscal year, but shall not be obligated to modify any payments it makes to UPD

under this Agreement

       6.      Return of Unexpended Funds. After the conclusion of each fiscal year of the

Term, the City may direct UPD in writing to return any Traffic Funds or SLVLESA Funds that

UPD did not expend during the fiscal year.

       7.      Kearns Service. All funds contributed or transferred under this Agreement shall

be expended for a vehicle and law enforcement services in the City of Kearns only and exclusively

for the benefit of the City of Kearns but may serve other temporary UPD duties as assigned.

       8.      Records. The Parties shall comply with the Government Records Access and

Management Act, Utah Code §§ 63G-2-101 et seq. and all other relevant local, state and federal

laws regarding this Agreement and any records generated or created related to this Agreement.

       9.      Termination. Either Party may cancel or terminate this Agreement for any reason

by delivering notice thereof to the other Party at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the

Initial Term or any Renewal Term.

       10.     Legal Compliance. The Parties, as part of the consideration herein, shall comply

with all applicable federal, state and local laws, rules, policies, and regulations.

       11.     Interlocal Agreement. In satisfaction of the requirements of the Interlocal

Cooperation Act, Title 11, Chapter 13, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended (“Interlocal Act”),

in connection with this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

               (a)    This Agreement shall be approved by each Party, pursuant to § 11-13-202.5

       of the Interlocal Act;

               (b)    This Agreement shall be reviewed as to proper form and compliance with

       applicable law by a duly authorized attorney on behalf of each Party, pursuant to Section

       11-13-202.5 of the Interlocal Act;

               (c)    A duly executed original counterpart of the Agreement shall be filed with

       the keeper of records of each Party, pursuant to § 11-13-209 of the Interlocal Act;

               (d)    Each Party shall be responsible for its own costs of any action done pursuant

       to this Agreement, and for any financing of such costs; and

               (e)    No separate legal entity is created by the terms of this Agreement. To the

       extent that this Agreement requires administration other than as set forth herein, it shall be

       administered by the City designee and UPD designee, acting as a joint board. No real or

       personal property shall be acquired jointly by the Parties as a result of this Agreement. To

       the extent that a Party acquires, holds, and disposes of any real or personal property for use

       in the joint or cooperative undertaking contemplated by this Agreement, such Party shall

       do so in the same manner that it deals with other property of such Party.

       12.     General Provisions. The following provisions are also integral parts of this

Agreement:

              12.1    Binding Agreement. This Agreement shall be binding upon and shall inure

       to the benefit of the successors and assigns of the respective Parties hereto.

              12.2     Captions. The headings used in this Agreement are inserted for reference

       purposes only and shall not be deemed to define, limit, extend, describe, or affect in any


                                                 4
way the meaning, scope, or interpretation of any of the terms or provisions of this

Agreement or the intent hereof.

       12.3    Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed in any number of counterparts

with the same effect as if the signatures upon any counterpart were upon the same

instrument. All signed counterparts shall be deemed to be one original.

       12.4     Severability. The provisions of this Agreement are severable, and should

any provision hereof be void, voidable, unenforceable, or invalid, such void, voidable,

unenforceable, or invalid provision shall not affect the other provisions of this Agreement.

       12.5    Waiver of Breach. Any waiver by either Party of any breach of any kind or

character whatsoever by the other, whether such be direct or implied, shall not be construed

as a continuing waiver of or consent to any subsequent breach of this Agreement.

       12.6    Cumulative Remedies. The rights and remedies of the Parties hereto shall

be construed cumulatively, and none of such rights and remedies shall be exclusive of, or

in lieu or limitation of, any other right, remedy, or priority allowed by law.

       12.7    Amendment. This Agreement shall not be modified except by an instrument

in writing signed by the Parties hereto.

       12.8    Time of Essence. Time is the essence in this Agreement.

       12.9    Governing Law and Venue. This Agreement shall be interpreted, construed,

and enforced according to the substantive laws of the state of Utah.

       12.10   Legal Review. Both Parties represent that they fully understand their right

to review all aspects of this Agreement with counsel and that they have had the opportunity

to consult with counsel; that they have carefully read and fully understand all the provisions

of this Agreement; and that they are freely knowingly and voluntarily entering into this

Agreement. The rule that such an agreement is to be construed against the drafter shall not

be applied to this Agreement.

       12.11    Exhibits and Recitals. The Recitals set forth above and all exhibits to this

Agreement are incorporated herein to the same extent as if such items were set forth herein

in their entirety within the body of this Agreement.

       12.12 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding and

agreement by and among the Parties hereto, and supersedes all prior agreements,

representations, or understandings by and among them, whether written or oral, pertaining

to the subject matter hereof.

       12.13    Governmental Immunity. Both Parties are governmental entities under the

Governmental Immunity Act, Utah Code § 63G-7-101, et seq. (the “Immunity Act”).

Consistent with the terms of the Immunity Act, the Parties agree that each Party is

responsible and liable for any wrongful or negligent acts which it commits, or which are

committed by its agents, officials, or employees. Neither Party waives any defenses or

limits of liability otherwise available under the Immunity Act and all other applicable laws,

and both Parties maintain all privileges, immunities, and other rights granted by the

Immunity Act and all other applicable law.

       12.14    Ethical Standards. The Parties hereto represent that they have not: (a)

provided an illegal gift or payoff to any officer or employee, or former officer or employee,

or to any relative or business entity of an officer or employee, or relative or business entity

of a former officer or employee of the other Party hereto; (b) retained any person to solicit

or secure this Agreement upon any contract, agreement or understanding for a commission,

percentage, brokerage or contingent fee, other than bona fide employees of bona fide

commercial agencies established for the purpose of securing business; (c) breached any of

the ethical standards; or (d) knowingly influenced, and hereby promise that they shall not

knowingly influence, any officer or employee or former officer or employee to breach any

of the ethical standards set forth in State statute, City, or County ordinances.
