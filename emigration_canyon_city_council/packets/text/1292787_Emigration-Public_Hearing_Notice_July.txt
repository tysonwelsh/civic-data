                           NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

             EMIGRATION CANYON PLANNING COMMISSION


NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN Pursuant to Utah Code Title 10, Chapter 9a, Section 205, that the
Emigration Canyon Planning Commission (the “Commission”) will hold a public hearing on
Thursday, July 10, 2025, beginning at 8:30 AM. (Meeting location on last page)

The purpose of the hearing is to receive public comments regarding:

   •   OAM2025-001430 - Consideration of an ordinance eliminating the agricultural exemption
       to the requirement for building and land use permits in Section 19.02.090 of the Emigration
       Canyon Code. Planner: Brian Tucker (Discussion/Action)
   •   OAM2025-001433 – Consideration of an ordinance repealing Chapters 19.08, 19.44,
       19.56, and 19.86 of Title 19, the Emigration Canyon Zoning Ordinance. Planner: Brian
       Tucker (Discussion/Action)
   •   OAM2025-001434 – Consideration of an ordinance repealing and replacing Chapter
       19.02, General Provisions and Administration, of the Emigration Canyon Code. Planner:
       Brian Tucker (Discussion/Action)
   •   OAM2025-001435 – Consideration of an ordinance repealing and replacing Chapter
       19.88, Nonconforming Uses and Noncomplying Structures, of the Emigration Canyon
       Code. Planner: Brian Tucker (Discussion/Action)


If members of the public wish to comment but cannot attend the meeting, comments may be sent
to Wendy Gurr (WGurr@msd.utah.gov). Prior to opening the public hearing, the Planning
Commission will hear a presentation from planning staff regarding the ordinance amendments.

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed public hearing items after taking comments
from the public during the public hearing. The public is also invited to review and inspect the
proposed amendment at: https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html.

You may also obtain copies of the ordinance amendments and submit written comments prior to
the public hearing by contacting Wendy Gurr at wgurr@msd.utah.gov or (385) 391-8268.
Individuals needing special accommodation (including ancillary communicative aids and services)
during this public hearing should contact Ms. Gurr three days before the public hearing.
