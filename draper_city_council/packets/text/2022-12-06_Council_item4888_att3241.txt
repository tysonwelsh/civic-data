BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Text Amendment to Draper City Municipal
Code (DCMC) Section 9-13-080, Table 9-13-2 in order to modify the setback requirements
for manufacturing zones. The applicant is looking to develop the property located at 15101
South Minuteman Dr with storage units.

Text Amendment. The proposed text amendment will alter the setbacks in the M1 and M2
zones, as shown below and in Exhibit B of this report. The existing front setback doesn’t
function the way it is written. It requires the greater of a 15-feet front setback or 2.5-feet of
setback for every foot of building height. There is no circumstance in which the 15-foot
setback would apply, as that would only be applicable for buildings that are 6-feet or less in
height. The proposed language creates a setback standard between 10-feet and 25-feet for
the M1 zone and 20-feet to 35-feet for the M2 zone. A greater setback is required as a
building gets taller. The maximum building height allowed in the M1 and M2 zones is 45-
feet. The language also clarifies the side and rear setbacks.

Criteria For Approval.
A Text and Map Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City
Council and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation
to the City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in
Section 9-5-060(E) of the DCMC:

         2. Text Amendments:
              a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies
                  of the city's general plan;
              b. Whether a proposed amendment furthers the specific purpose statements of the
                  zoning ordinance;
              c. Whether the proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the request
                  and there is sufficient justification for a modification to the zoning ordinance;
              d. The proposed amendment will not create a conflict with any other section or part
                  of this title or the general plan;
              e. Whether the potential effects of the proposed amendment have been evaluated
                  and determined not to be detrimental to public health, safety, or welfare and
                  represents an overall community benefit; and
              f. The extent to which a proposed text amendment implements best current,
                  professional practices of urban planning, design, and engineering practices.

public comment prior to making a decision.
