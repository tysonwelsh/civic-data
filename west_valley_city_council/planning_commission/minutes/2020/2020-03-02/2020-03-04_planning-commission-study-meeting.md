GENERAL PLAN ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
GPZ-2-2020 Petition by SCOTT HELM requesting a General Plan change from Large Lot Residential (2 to
           3 units/acre) to Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre) and a zone change from A
           (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-10 (Single unit dwelling residential, minimum
           lot size 10,000 sq. ft.). The property is located at 4625 West 4100 South on 1.985 acres. (Staff
           – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545)


             Discussion: Steve Pastorik explained that back in 2015 the City reviewed all lots over 2-acres
             and zoned those lots to one of the A zones. Steve then explained the property in question has
             a large driveway that placed them over the 2-acre mark. Steve explained the driveway could
             not be used for another type of development. Therefore, the acreage left to develop would be
             less than the 2-acres. Steve explained the applicant is looking to combine 2 lots to create a
             subdivision with a total of 7 lots. Steve stated the surrounding properties are zoned R-1-8 and
             the proposal for R-1-10 zoning will be like the surrounding residential area. Steve stated the
             applicant will comply with the latest housing standards. Commissioner Lovato asked who
             will have ownership of the driveway. Steve Lehman stated that most of the homes are owned
            by one family so, they will probably share the ownership of the driveway. Commissioner
            Winters asked if the applicant was going against any ordinances, or is he just wanting to
            request the zone change? Steve Pastorik stated the applicant is just requesting the zone
            change. Steve stated the property in question was originally zoned R-1-8 and the City changed
            the zone to A when the driveway placed the property over the 2-acre minimum.
            Commissioner Fuller asked if this driveway will now go to a new subdivision. Steve Pastorik
            confirmed the driveway will go to the new subdivision.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION
C-3-2020 Petition by PEDRO ALBARRAN requesting conditional use approval for storage within
         an accessory building associated with a home occupation. The property is located at 3790
         S 3600 W on 0.42 acres and is zoned R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff -
          Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361)

            Discussion: Brock explained the application was proposed by a gentleman with a
            landscaping business wanting a home office. Brock stated the owner is wanting to build a
            garage for storage. Brock explained he will need to present site plans that meet the code
            because the current size of the garage is larger than the home and does not meet code. Brock
            stated the owner would like to store landscaping equipment in the accessory building. Brock
            told the applicant that whatever would fit on the back of a trailer would be allowed in the
            accessory building. Brock explained there is some equipment currently on the lot that does
            not meet the standards and will need to be moved and the applicant will need to obtain a
            building permit. Brock stated the property will be cleaned up as well to meet the title 7
            codes.
            Commissioner Woodruff explained that a landscaping business located up the street from the
            lot in question currently has box trucks and other large equipment in and out of the home
            business. Steve P and Brock will investigate this to make sure they are meeting code.
            Commissioner Winters asked if we should care what he puts in the garage? Brock explained
            that the code states he can only have one truck and trailer stored in the garage.
            Commissioner Lovato asked you can only have one truck for the business? Steve Pastorik
            explained they can only have one business truck, not including personal vehicles.
