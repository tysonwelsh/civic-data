COMMISSIONER LEE - I’LL EXPLAIN A LITTLE MORE. WE WROTE INTO OUR
ORDINANCE IF THERE WERE LESS THAN 5 COMPLAINTS OR LETTERS OVER A 3-
YEAR PERIOD OF TIME THEN IT IS EASY FOR US TO MOVE IT ON AND SAY THE
AGRICULTURAL PROTECTION JUST HAPPENS. THE REASON WHY WE DID THAT IS
BECAUSE THE STATE LAW HAD A ‘SHALL’ IN THERE WHICH WAS A FINANCIAL
COST TO THE COUNTY DUE TO NOTICING, SIGNAGE, ETC. WE ASKED THE STATE
TO MOVE THAT FROM ‘SHALL’ TO ‘MAY’. THEN IF WE DON’T HAVE ANY
COMPLAINTS, THEN IT EASILY ALLOWS US TO ADOPT IT AND MOVE IT ON TO THE
NEXT 20 YEARS. BUT IF THERE IS A CONCERN, THEN WE WANT IT TO BACK
THROUGH THE PROCESS WITH THE ADVISORY BOARD AND THE PLANNING
COMMISSION TO SEE IF THOSE ARE JUST LETTERS OF NUISANCE OR IF THERE’S
LEGITIMATE REASONS AND THEN IT WOULD COME BACK TO US AGAIN.
