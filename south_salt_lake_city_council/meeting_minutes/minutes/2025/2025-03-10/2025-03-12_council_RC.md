       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          Several residents shared public comments in opposition to short term rentals in the
          City.

              Former Council Member Siwik shared about March as colon cancer awareness
              month.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood shared that 23 families graduated from the ‘Baby and You’ program
          last weekend and that there are 12 more families signed up for the next session.
          Mayor Wood noted that the City now has a ‘cradle to career’ pipeline of City
          programs that provide support, classes, and resources for families and children in
          the City, making South Salt Lake one of the only cities that has never received
          federal Promise neighborhood grant funds achieve this for its residents.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.

       6. INFORMATION.
             a. Proclamation: City of Kindness, One Kind Act a Day
                Mayor Wood, alongside OKAAD Chief Growth Officer, Curtis Bennett, read
                the proclamation in its entirety that states the City’s pledge to be a city of
                kindness in partnership with Communities of Kindness.

                   A copy of the proclamation is attached and incorporated by this reference.

               b. Promise South Salt Lake Presentation: CADCA Trip and YAGI
                  Promise Deputy Director, Edward Lopez, alongside Promise staff and several
                  Youth City Council Members, gave a presentation that gave an overview of
                  the grant funds that the Youth City Council received from the Salt Lake
                  County Health Department.

                   The funds allowed three City staff members and five Youth City Council
                   Members to attend the Community Anti-Drug Coalitions of America
                   National Leadership Forum in Washington D.C. and to implement an
                   advocacy project made up of four training courses that will help build
                   leadership skills and prevention knowledge for participating youth.

                   A copy of the presentation is attached and incorporated by this reference.

               c. Critical Home Repair Program & Partnership
                  LMI Housing Navigator, Rebecca Yund, alongside Habitat for Humanity CEO,
                  Carin Crowe, gave a presentation about the Critical Home Repair Program
                  and how it works.

                   The organization’s team performs a 100-point inspection of the home and
