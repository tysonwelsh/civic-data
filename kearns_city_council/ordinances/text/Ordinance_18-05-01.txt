


ORDINANCE NO. 18-05-01								May 14, 2018
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 11.20.135 OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP MUNICIPAL CODE CHANGING THE DEFINITION OF ABANDONED VEHICLE FROM ONE PARKED UNATTENDED FOR 24 HOURS TO THAT OF 48 HOURS TO CONFORM THE VEHICLE PROVISIONS TO THE PROVISIONS OF STATE CODE

RECITALS

	•	Utah State Code section 10-2a-414 provides that when a metro township is incorporated that each county ordinance that is in effect on the day that metro township is incorporated will remain in effect as a metro township ordinance until the metro township council amends or repeals the ordinance.

	•	Utah State Code section 10-3B-504(3) provides that in a metro township that is in a municipal service district that the township council may adopt by ordinances reasonably related to the safety, health, morals and welfare of the metro township inhabitants which includes regulating parking on public streets and the abandonment of vehicles on public streets.

	•	The Keams Metro Township Council recognizes the need to amend the existing section 11.20.135 of its code, as incorporated from the Salt Lake County Code, to conform it with the State of Utah Traffic Code.

ORDINANCE

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY ORDAINED, by the Council of the Keams Metro Township that section 11.20.135 of the existing Keams Metro Township code is amended by deleting the struck through language and adding the underlined language as follows:

11.20.135 - Long-term parking prohibited.

No person owning, possessing, controlling or having custody of a vehicle shall permit it to remain standing upon any county street or alley for a consecutive period of more 48 hours.
