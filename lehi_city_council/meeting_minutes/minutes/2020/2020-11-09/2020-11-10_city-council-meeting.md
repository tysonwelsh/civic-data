2. Presentations and Reports
2.1) Discussion of Ranked Choice Voting
Stan Lockhart was there to present information on Ranked Choice Voting and gave a power point
presentation to Council Members and Staff. He stated that a survey was conducted to take a poll
on voter experience for those who had used Ranked Choice Voting with positive results. He said
Legislators are encouraging cities and towns throughout Utah to adopt Ranked Choice Voting. Mr.
Lockhart suggested that Governor Herbert would also support Ranked Choice Voting.

Mr. Lockhart cited reasons why Ranked Choice Voting is popular, such as saving taxpayers
money, being shorter and less expensive for a City campaign, and easy for cities to implement.
Utah County has used Ranked Choice Voting and he encouraged cities to contact Amelia Powers
with Utah County Auditor’s Office for information. Council Member Albrecht asked Stan to speak
about the "Spoiler Effect". Stan and Council Members discussed the differences between regular
voting and Ranked Choice Voting. Stan stated there were other cities in Utah County that are
planning on joining Ranked Choice Voting this upcoming year.

2.2) Discussion of Improvements at Dry Creek Reservoir
Jason Bond presented information on the Dry Creek Reservoir with a twofold purpose of design
and funding and a direction for operational purpose. Council Member Southwick asked what
needed to be fixed or addressed from the last plan. Jason stated parking was insufficient and
compared to its other reservoirs where parking would be inadequate. Council Member Hancock

have an effect on those residents. Council Member Condie asked about the grass and sand

2.3) Discussion of golf cart usage on city streets
Darren Paul, Chief of Police, presented information on the use of street legal golf carts for cost
effective commuting. Darren spoke with Mark Robinson who crafted a bill for the use of golf
carts on the street. Mark stated the bill was not intended to cover communing, but rather just to
get back and forth from the golf course so they didn't have to trailer the golf carts.

Darren suggested golf carts fall under a “low speed vehicle” that requires safety equipment and a
Driver’s License to operate. The Council and Darren discussed foreseeable problems such as
confusion with parents unknowingly allowing their children to drive, being unable to safely
commute on the streets, golf carts that end up on sidewalks, and enforcement of the law being
challenging for Officers. Council decided they will not consider golf carts being allowed to
operate on the streets for the reasons listed above.

2.4) Financial Update- Dean Lundell, Finance Director
Dean Lundell, Finance Director for Lehi City, presented a financial update on the quarterly
report that was previously sent out to Council.

The City has larger expenses than revenue, but things are coming in as they should. Dean does
not have any concerns with the revenue and expenditure outlook. The Council and Dean
discussed ongoing fiscal year and prospective fiscal year goals. The general fund balance was
holding at a little under $12 million. The Road Tax money has increased and is holding well.
Revenues have increased across Utility Fund Revenue and Cash Reserves.

3. 20 Minute Citizen Input
Scott Wilson asked about Traverse Mountain Blvd to Triumph Mtn Connection and the delay in
the construction process. He had been informed that it would not be completed until Spring of
this year. Scott reached out to Council Member Hancock with report of the delay. Scott is still
upset because the road is still not complete, and he would like it to be finished before the
snowfall of this year.

Dave Norman responded that the delays are because of sewer lines. Dave stated Lorin Powell
would be the responsible person to talk to. Lorin informed Council and Scott there is an
easement that needs to be installed and without that easement there cannot be an approval. There
is a pre con scheduled and if the weather permits paving can take place soon. The developer is
ready to continue, but timing has been an issue.
