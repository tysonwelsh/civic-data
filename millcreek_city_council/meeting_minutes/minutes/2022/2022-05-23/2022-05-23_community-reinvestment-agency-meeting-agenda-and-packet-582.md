L Status      Update        on Wasatch            Community         Reinvestment             Project       Area
   Assistant City Manager Francis Lilly reported that a car dealership working on the Wasatch
   Community Reinvestment        Project Area site backed out of their negotiations with the property
   owner. There were some complex land issues due to UDOT owning land. The developer was
   still potentially interested. Staff would be meeting to talk about the village center concept part
   of the General          Plan that could be used in the area.
