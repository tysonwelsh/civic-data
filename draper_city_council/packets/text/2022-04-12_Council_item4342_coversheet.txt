Re: Ordinance 1530 Amending DCMC 2-4-060 -- Constitutional Taking Issues Comments: Utah Code 63L-4-101, et seq. is known as the “Constitutional Takings Issues Act (the “Act”). The Act requires Draper City to enact an ordinance establishing guidelines to assist the City in identifying actions involving the physical taking or exaction of private real property that may have constitutional taking issues. Draper City enacted Draper City Municipal Code (“DCMC”) 2-4-060 to comply with the Act.
Staff is recommending the City Council amend DCMC 2-4-060 to clarify the factors staff should follow in analyzing a potential constitutional taking issue, to clarify appeals and designate the city’s Appeals and Variance Hearing Officer as the individual to hear appeals of constitutional taking issues in accordance with the Act and to make other minor edits to the text. The findings are as follows: The proposed amendments comply with state law and will benefit the helath, safety and welfare of Draper residents.
