Requested Agenda Title:
Public Hearing to Consider the Sandy Hills General Plan
Requested Agenda Item Description: The drafted Sandy Hills General Plan is a long-term plan for the
unincorporated area of Sandy Hills, an unincorporated island surrounded by Sandy City and represented by the
Sandy Hills Community Council. The plan meets the required State Code for General Plan content including
the elements of Land Use and Transportation, along with an additional Chapter covering Resilience and
Infrastructure. Staff will present the drafted Plan to the Council, and the presentation will be followed by a
public hearing. After the public hearing, the Council may choose to make a motion regarding adoption of the
Plan.
