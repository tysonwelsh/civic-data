5.     Items for Council Consideration
5.a    Public Hearing: Ordinance #1596, an Ordinance amending the Official
       Zoning Map of Draper City for approximately 0.48 acres of property
       from RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot minimum lot
       size) to CC (Community Commercial) located at approximately 11853
       South 700 East, otherwise known as the Tunbridge Zoning Map


                                                                        2|Page
      Amendment. Continued from the December 6, 2023 City Council
      meeting.
      Tevai Haunga, a city planner, showed maps of the subject area and explained
      the applicant’s request to rezone from RA2 to CC to make the property easier
      to sell. She said the Planning Commission reviewed the application and
      forwarded a positive recommendation.

      Kim Tunbridge, the applicant, said her husband applied for the rezone in
      2023 and had since passed away. She said she did not have plans for the
      property at that time.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing, and closed the public hearing
      seeing no one come forward.

      Responding to a question from the Council, Community Development
      Director Jen Jastremsky said RA2 and CC Zones had different setback
      standards.

      Councilmember T. Lowery moved to approve Ordinance #1596.
      Councilmember Roberts seconded the motion.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.

5.b   Public Hearing: Ordinances #1605 and #1606, an Ordinance amending
      Official Land Use Map and Zoning Map of Draper City for approximately
      1.19 acres from Community Commercial to Neighborhood Commercial
      and from RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot minimum lot
      size) to CN (Neighborhood Commercial), located at approximately
      11743 South 700 East within Draper City, otherwise known at the
      Federal Heights Holdings LLC Zoning and Land Use Amendments.

      Maryann Pickering, a city planner, showed maps of the subject area and said
      the applicant proposed to change both the Land Use designation and Zoning

      Map for the property. If approved, the applicant may improve the property
      to commercial standards to operate a business. The Planning Commission
      forwarded a unanimous positive recommendation.

5.d   Action Item: Resolution #24-02, a Resolution authorizing the Mayor to
      sign a real estate purchase agreement for the purchase of parcel #28-
      33-203-007 and parcel #28-33-203-010 located at approximately 12695
      South 1700 East consisting of approximately 10.87 acres.
      City Manager David Dobbins explained the proposal to purchase
      approximately 10 acres from the Canyons School District, with the intention
      to pursue the option of using five acres for cemetery space and selling the
      other half for single-family home development. The cemetery portion of the
      purchase would be paid out of the General Fund. Mr. Dobbins presented
      three amendments to the real estate purchase agreement requested by the
      School District.

      Councilmember F. Lowry moved to approve Resolution #24-02, subject
      to the following amendments: the City would equally share in any
      proceeds above the purchase price, the disclosure deadlines changed
      to April 1, 2024, and the settlement date to April 30, 2024, and the
      acceptance date changed to March 15, 2024. Councilmember Roberts
      seconded the motion.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
