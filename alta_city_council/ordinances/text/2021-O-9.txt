                                  ORDINANCE #2021– O –9
   ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING TRAFFIC ENFORCEMENT ON QUASI-PUBLIC
                   ROADS AND PARKING AREAS

        Whereas, the Town of Alta (“Town”); and Alta Ski Lift Company (“ASL”) have
historically worked jointly manage parking for safety, snow removal, and access to property
within the town; and
       Whereas, the Town and ASL desire to jointly manage parking for various users,
including guests, residents, businesses employees and operators, and other visitors; and
       WHEREAS, the Town and ASL have executed a Memorandum of Understanding that
allows a parking program to be put in place on Forest Service land subject to a Special Use
Permit held by ASL ; and
       WHEREAS, ASL has "quasi-public roads or parking areas" which means privately
owned and maintained road or parking area that is generally held open for use of the public for
purposes of vehicular travel or parking; and
       WHEREAS, a map of the area covered by this Ordinance is attached as Exhibit A;
       WHEREAS, ASL as owner and special use permittee of this area agree that the area in
Exhibit A is subject to Utah Code Title 41, Chapter 6a, Traffic Code;
       WHEREAS, the Town Council held a public hearing on November 10, 2021.
    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE TOWN OF ALTA TOWN
COUNCIL OF, UTAH, THAT:
        SECTION I: The recitals above are incorporated herein as findings of fact. Pursuant to
Utah Code Ann. § 41-6a-214, the quasi-public roads and parking areas of ASL are hereby
subject to traffic and parking laws enforceable by the Town of Alta.

       SECTION II: This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon publication.
     PASSED AND APPROVED by the Town of Alta Town Council, Utah, this 10th day of
November in the year 2021.
                                             TOWN OF ALTA
