MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
Date: 2024-10-10
Re: Public Hearing: Patterson Subdivision Plat Amendment (Administrative Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for approximately 0.92
acres located on the north side of Patterson Lane, at approximately 1571 E. Patterson Lane. The
property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting to resize the storm drainage retention
easements on two of the three lots in the subdivision. The purpose for the change to the size of the
storm drainage retention easements is to accommodate the applicants desired house plans for
development of the lots.




Findings for approval:



1. There is good cause for the amendment, and no public street or municipal utility easement will
be vacated or amended.



Findings for denial:




1.   Good cause does not exist for the amendment.
