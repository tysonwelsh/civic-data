BUSINESS ITEMS
3.1       PUBLIC HEARING – Tentative Fiscal Year 2019-2020 Budget
    The RDA Board will hear public comment concerning the adopted Tentative Fiscal Year
    2019-2020 RDA Budget. The RDA Board may review any items that were the proper subject
    of consideration in the Public Hearing. Approval of the final budget is tentatively scheduled
    for June 26.

Chair Fullmer called for a motion to open the public hearing.

Motion: BOARDMEMBER JUDD MOVED TO OPEN THE PUBLIC HEARING AT 6:58 PM.
BOARDMEMBER FLAKE SECONDED THE MOTION. CHAIR FULLMER,
BOARDMEMBERS FLAKE AND JUDD VOTED AYE. BOARDMEMBERS EARNEST AND
RILEY WERE ABSENT. MOTION CARRIED WITH TWO ABSENT.

Chair Fullmer turned the time over to City Manager/Finance Director Jacob McHargue.

Mr. McHargue explained that nothing had changed since they had adopted the tentative budget.
He gave a brief overview of the budget. Highlights were:
Revenue                                     $8,434,400
Major Expenditures
   • Personnel                              $255,400
   • Contracted Services                    $119,100
   • TIFF Payments                          $3,812,400
   • Bond Payments                          $4,395,700
Capital Projects
   • Remediation Westside                   $7,000,000
   • Main Street                            $2,500,000
   • Rail Spur removal                      $2,500,000

There was a discussion about property taxes.

Chair Fullmer called for further questions. Hearing none, she called for a motion to close the
public hearing.

Motion: BOARDMEMBER FLAKE MOVED TO CLOSE THE PUBLIC HEARING AT 7:00
PM. BOARDMEMBER JUDD SECONDED THE MOTION. CHAIR FULLMER,
BOARDMEMBERS FLAKE AND JUDD VOTED AYE. BOARDMEMBERS EARNEST AND
RILEY WERE ABSENT. MOTION CARRIED WITH TWO ABSENT.

      Boardmember Judd asked for an update on Geneva Nitrogen. Mr. McHargue explained that
they had applied for a permit and were doing the demolition. Mr. Reid said that he was impressed
with the company doing the demolition. He mentioned that he checked the site twice a week.

Boardmember Judd asked if they could get US Steel to give them an update. Mr. McHargue
replied that he would set something up.
