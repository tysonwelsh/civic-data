DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Rick Bentley, along with other neighbors adjacent to the Challenger School site,
is requesting the reconsideration of Condition of Approval number 4 that was modified during
the preliminary site plan review by the Planning Commission on August 20, 2020.

BACKGROUND
The Challenger School Site is located in the CN Zone (Planned Center – Neighborhood District),
which is a commercial zone. It abuts properties in multiple residential zones. City ordinance
dictates that an eight-foot masonry wall be constructed between commercial and residential
zones. However, it also allows the Planning Commission to approve a wall less than eight feet
tall if the adjacent neighbors request it.

During the Planning Commission meeting held August 20, 2020, the Commission found
preliminary site plan review to be complete, but amended Condition of Approval number 4 to
read “That an eight-foot masonry wall be constructed along the length of the west property line
where no retaining wall is present and that a minimum of six-foot masonry wall be constructed
along the portion of the boundary where a retaining wall is present, subject to confirmation and
discussion of the exact height based on coordinating with the adjacent residents the overall
height, appearance and purpose of that wall.”

This allows a wall less than eight feet tall along the portion of the site where the retaining wall is
proposed, but gave no leeway for a lower wall for the remaining residents to the south that are
adjacent to the development.

Since the meeting, the remaining residents to the south have officially requested a wall less than
eight feet in height. The request is attached to the staff report. A map of the properties requesting
the reconsideration of Condition of Approval number 4 is also attached.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500 foot radius of the subject parcels as per Sandy
City Land Development Code requirements, to notify them of the Planning Commission meeting.

ANALYSIS
City ordinance allows for residents adjacent to a commercial zone to request a lower wall height.
The ordinance states:

Sec. 21-23-7. - Screening at Boundaries of Residential Districts.

(a) Masonry Wall. For commercial and industrial developments abutting residential districts
(except recognizable holding zones for future commercial or industrial development), an opaque
masonry wall shall be installed and maintained along all district boundaries, other than streets,
where the premises abut areas zoned for residential uses.

(b) Height. Except where otherwise provided, the opaque masonry wall shall be a minimum of
eight feet in height. If requested by the adjacent residents, the Planning Commission may
approve a lower wall based upon unusual circumstances (e.g., views, landscaping, etc.). A lower
height wall may be required adjacent to a front property line for sight distance and traffic safety.

The applicant already has a masonry wall along the rear of his property. Double-fencing along his
wall would not be ideal. It would create a space in between the walls that would not be reasonably
maintainable, and it would also collect trash deposited by wind. Additionally, he does not wish to
have a fence that towers over his and obscure his view. The other residents also want to reduce the
obstruction of their views. A masonry wall can be tied into the applicant’s wall and then proceed
to the south at a consistent height of approximately six to seven feet. The wall can be constructed
of the same, or similar, split face stone as the applicant’s wall to allow for a consistent architecture
across the entire length of the wall. Challenger School did meet with Sandy City staff and the
adjacent residents, and they all agree on the type and style of wall to be installed.

   CONCERNS
   Staff’s primary concern is that the wall will only extend approximately 25 feet along the rear of
   the Thacker property, which is the southernmost adjacent property to the Challenger School site.
   The future development that borders the rest of their rear yard is in a residential zone, and would
   not be required by ordinance to install a masonry wall. This means that the Thackers could have a
   completely different fence along part of their property that extends to the masonry wall. This issue
   may be addressed by the Planning Commission when the development to the south comes before
   the Planning Commission for preliminary site plan review.

   STAFF RECOMMENDATION
   Staff recommends that the Planning Commission modify Condition of Approval number 4 to read
   “That a masonry wall no less than six feet tall be constructed along the western property line, and
   that the developer work with the adjacent residents to construct a wall of a primarily consistent
   height along the length of the entire wall.”
