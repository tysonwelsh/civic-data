City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1683

On November 18, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1683 amending
the official zoning map of draper city for approximately 2.28 acres of property from
ra1 (residential agricultural) to ra2 (residential agricultural), located at approximately
1360 east 13200 south and 13239 south 1300 east within draper city, otherwise
known as the plummer zoning map amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at
the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
