                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-52

      A RESOLUTION TO DECLARE VEHICLE NO. RSV-1 AS SURPLUS, AND
            AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO DISPOSE OF
                       AS DEEMED APPROPRIATE

         WHEREAS, the City Fleet Manager and Operations Services Director has determined
that it is more cost effective to trade this vehicle in on a new vehicle; and

        WHEREAS, the vendor selling the City the new vactor truck will provide the City with a
truck to use as needed, until the new truck is received, which is anticipated to be approximately
10 months; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets that are no
longer needed as surplus in a public meeting.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council declares vehicle no. RSV-1 as surplus, authorizing the
          City Manager to dispose of as deemed appropriate.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 21st day of
July 2020 by the following vote:
