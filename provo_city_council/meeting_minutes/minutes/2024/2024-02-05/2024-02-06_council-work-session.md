John Borget, Director of Administrative Services, presented.

   2. A discussion regarding the East Bay Shopping Center Sales Tax Reimbursement
      Agreement - fourth payment - budget appropriation (24-011) (00:06:30)

Keith Morey, Development Services Assistant Director, presented.

There are three agreements that he wants to talk about. Several years ago Provo City entered into
sales tax reimbursement agreements with various shopping centers. The idea was to partner with
the retail shopping centers and agree that if they were going to invest in their property with new
renovations, then they would increase the sales tax and the City would share back a portion of
the increase they could potentially generate. If every year, they can show thee was an increase,
then they would share back a portion for a period of time.

The agreement meant that they would make over two million dollars of improvement. They
would look every year at the sales tax and if there was an increase they would give a portion
back. Every year, the City comes to the Council to share the tax amounts and show that shopping
centers have behaved in a way that they would deserve a portion of their sales tax back. The
target reimbursement amount as per the agreement is $1,154,385.00 which is one
half of the total out-of-pocket expenses which were $2,308,770.00 This is the fourth payment in
the ten-year agreement, that is $59,113.00 which represents the amount over the baseline of
$90,099.00. The total received in 4 years is $241,098.00. Out of all the partnerships, this is been
one of the most productive things they have done. Every one of them has significantly increased
their sales tax.

Ms. Katrice MacKay asked him to go through all three agreements.

This will go into Agenda Items 3 and 4.
