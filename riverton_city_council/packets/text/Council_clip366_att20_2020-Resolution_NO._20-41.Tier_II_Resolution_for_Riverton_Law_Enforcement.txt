                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-41

    A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING RIVERTON CITY TO PAY EMPLOYEE
 CONTRIBUTIONS ON BEHALF OF EMPLOYEES IN THE TIER 2 PUBLIC SAFETY
               AND FIREFIGHTER RETIREMENT SYSTEM
       WHEREAS, Riverton City employs employees who are eligible for and participate as
members in the New Public Safety and Firefighter Tier II Contributory Retirement System
administered by the Utah Retirement Systems;
        WHEREAS, in accordance with federal and state law, including Section 414(h)(2) of the
Internal Revenue Code, employers may take formal action to pick-up required employee
contributions, which will be paid by the employer in lieu of employee contributions;
       WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to formally pick-up a portion of the employee
contributions required to be paid under Subsection 49-23-301(2)(c), as enacted in S.B. 56, Public
Safety and Firefighter Tier II Retirement Enhancements (2020 General Session), for all City
employees participating in the New Public Safety and Firefighter Tier II Contributory Retirement
System; and
        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council is duly authorized to take this formal action on
behalf of Riverton City as a participating employer with the Utah Retirement Systems:
     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
    1. The Riverton City Council and Mayor herewith declares that beginning July 1, 2020,
       Riverton City shall prospectively pick-up and pay required employee contributions for
       all City employees who are members of the New Public Safety and Firefighter Tier II
       Contributory Retirement System; provided, however that the City’s contribution shall
       not exceed the required contributions for Tier I Public Safety Service Employees.

    2. The picked up contributions paid by the employer, even though designated as employee
       contributions for state law purposes, are being paid by the City in lieu of the required
       employee contributions.

    3. The picked up contributions will not be included in the gross income of the employees
       for tax reporting purposes, that is, for federal or state income tax withholding taxes, until
       distributed from the Utah Retirements Systems, so that the contributions are treated as
       employer contributions pursuant to Section 414(h)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code.

    4. The picked up contributions are a supplement and not a salary reduction to the City
       employees who are eligible for and participating members in the New Public Safety and
       Firefighter Tier II Contributory Retirement System.
   5. From and after the date of this pick-up, a City employee may not have a cash or deferred
      election right with respect to the designated employee contributions, including that the
      employees may not be permitted to opt out of the pick-up and may not be entitled to any
      option of choosing to receive the contributed amounts directly instead of having them
      paid by the City on behalf of its employees to the Utah Retirement Systems.

   6. To bring parity between the election of the Riverton City Council and the Riverton
      Employee Policies and Procedures Manual (“Manual”), the Riverton City Council
      hereby amends Section 4.7 of the Manual as described in Exhibit A, incorporated herein
      by reference.

   7. This resolution takes effect on July 1, 2020, and is contingent upon the Riverton City
      Council adopting a budget which allocates sufficient funding for expenses associated
      with adoption of this Resolution.

RETIREMENT BENEFIT FOR PUBLIC SAFETY SERVICE EMPLOYEES WHO BECOME MEMBERS OF
URS ON OR AFTER JULY 1, 2011 (TIER II).


URS arranges individual retirement accounts for each Tier II Public Safety Service Employee, and
requires employers to contribute annually to the Public Safety Service Employee’s retirement account.
If a Public Safety Service Employee’s first employer is Riverton City, then the Public Safety Service
Employees shall be required to select whether to participate in what is known as a “hybrid retirement”
or a “defined contribution retirement” within one year from the Public Safety Service Employee’s date
of hire. Public Safety Service Employees are directed to consult with URS to learn the benefits offered
under both systems before making a selection. Employees shall become members of the hybrid
retirement system by default, if no selection is made by the employee. Tier II Public Safety Service
Employees who have already become a member of hybrid retirement or a defined contribution
retirement shall continue to be a member of their same plan after coming to work for Riverton City.

Riverton City annually contributes a percentage of a Public Safety Service Employee’s compensation to
URS as that Employee’s retirement benefit. The City’s contribution is an amount equal to that required
by the Utah State Retirement and Insurance Benefit Act, under the direction of URS. In addition to
amounts required to be paid by URS, Riverton City contributes an annual amount to a Public Safety
Service Employee’s individual retirement account in an amount equal to the difference between the
required employer contribution for Tier 1 employees less the required employer contribution for Tier II
employees less 2% (provided the result is not a negative number) of the Public Safety Service
Employee’s compensation. If the Public Safety Service Employee is required to contribute, the City will
elect to pick-up the employee’s contributions by reducing this additional contribution into the Public
Safety Service Employee’s individual retirement account. However, the combined contributions for Tier
II employees will not exceed the required contributions for Tier I Public Safety Service Employees.

RETIREMENT SYSTEM
In place of Social Security, Riverton City has elected to participate in the retirement program offered by
the Utah Retirement Systems (“URS”), which provides retirement benefits for member-employees. URS
arranges individual retirement accounts for each member employee, and requires employers to
contribute annually to the employee’s retirement account. The amounts contributed by employers
equal a certain percentage of an employee’s salary, and may be adjusted from year to year.
DEFINITIONS


As used in this Manual:



   1. “Compensation,” as applied to calculating retirement benefits, means the total amount of
      payments that are includable in gross income received by a Public Safety Service Employee as
      base income for the regularly scheduled work period. City administration shall establish the
      regularly scheduled work period. Base income shall be determined prior to the deduction of any
      amounts the Public Safety Service Employee authorizes to be deducted for salary deferral or
      other benefits authorized by federal law. “Compensation” includes performance-based bonuses
      and cost-of-living adjustments. When calculating retirement benefits, “Compensation” does not
      include:
      a. overtime;
      b. sick pay incentives;
      c. retirement pay incentives;
      d. the monetary value of remuneration paid in kind, as in a residence, use of equipment or
          uniform, travel, or similar payments;
      e. a lump-sum payment or special payment covering accumulated leave; and
      f. all contributions made by a participating employer under this system or under any other
          employee benefit system or plan maintained by a participating employer for the benefit of a
          member or participant.

   2. “Dispatcher” means a Public Safety Service Employee who is certified in accordance with Utah
      Code Section 53-6-303 or successor statute.

   3. “Public Safety Service Employee” means an employee of Riverton City who performs a public
      safety service and is employed for an average of 2080 hours of regularly scheduled employment
      per year, and is a:
      a. Law enforcement officer; or
      b. Dispatcher.

   4. “Law Enforcement Officer” means a Public Safety Service Employee who is a sworn and certified
      peace officer, is an employee the Riverton City Police Department, and whose primary and
      principal duties consist of the prevention and detection of crime and the enforcement of
      criminal statutes or ordinances of this state or any of its political subdivisions. A Law
      Enforcement Officer must meet the statutory requirements of a Utah Code Section 53-13-103 or
      successor statute to qualify as a Law Enforcement Officer under this Manual.
   5. “Tier I” means a system or plan provided under the Utah State Retirement and Insurance Benefit
      Act for which an employee is eligible to participate if the employee initially enters regular full-
      time employment before July 1, 2011.

   6. “Tier II” means a system or plan provided under the Utah State Retirement and Insurance
      Benefit Act in lieu of a Tier I system or plan for an employee who does not have Tier I service
        credit in a system or plan under this title, if the employee initially enters regular full-time
        employment on or after July 1, 2011. “Tier II” includes:
        a. the Tier II hybrid system established under Utah State Retirement and Insurance Benefit Act:
                i.    Chapter 22, Part 3, Tier II Hybrid Retirement System; or
               ii.    Chapter 23, Part 3, Tier II Hybrid Retirement System; and
        b. the Tier II Defined Contribution Plan (Tier II DC Plan) established under Utah State
            Retirement and Insurance Benefit Act:
                i.    Chapter 22, Part 4, Tier II Defined Contribution Plan; or
               ii.    Chapter 23, Part 4, Tier II Defined Contribution Plan.



RETIREMENT BENEFIT FOR PUBLIC SAFETY SERVICE EMPLOYEES WHO BECOME MEMBERS OF
URS BEFORE JULY 1, 2011 (TIER I).


Typically, a Tier I Public Safety Service Employee is a member of either a contributory or noncontributory
retirement plan, under the Utah State Retirement Systems and Insurance Benefit Act, before s/he comes
to work for Riverton City. Riverton City will continue make all contributions required by URS to maintain
the existing retirement accounts of Public Safety Service Employees who are also Tier I members of URS
when they come to work for Riverton City. Riverton City makes said contributions to URS annually, as a
percentage of a Public Safety Service Employee’s compensation as the Employee’s retirement benefit.
The City’s contribution is an amount equal to that required by the Utah State Retirement and Insurance
Benefit Act, under the direction of URS. All terms and conditions of a Tier I employee’s retirement plan
shall continue to be governed by URS. Public Safety Service Employees shall be required to cooperate
with the City in maintaining the Employee’s retirement plan with URS.



In addition to URS benefits, Riverton City annually contributes to a 401(a) retirement program which is
managed by ICMA-RC. For every Riverton City Public Safety Service employee, Riverton City will
contribute to each employee’s ICMA-RC retirement account an amount equal to 6.5% of an employee’s
gross wage.

RETIREMENT BENEFIT FOR EMPLOYEES OTHER THAN PUBLIC SAFETY SERVICE EMPLOYEES WHO
BECOME MEMBERS OF URS BEFORE JULY 1, 2011 (TIER I).


Riverton City will continue to make all contributions required by URS to maintain the existing retirement
accounts of employees who are also Tier I members of URS. All terms and conditions of a Tier I
employee’s retirement plan shall continue to be governed by URS. Employees shall be required to
cooperate with the City in maintaining the Employee’s retirement plan with URS.

In addition to URS benefits, Riverton City annually contributes to a 401(a) retirement program which is
managed by ICMA-RC. For every Riverton City employee working 20+ hours per week, Riverton City will
contribute to each employee’s ICMA-RC retirement account an amount equal to 22.06% of an
employee’s gross wage, less the amount required to be contributed into an employee’s URS retirement
account.

EMPLOYEES WHO HAVE OPTED OUT OF RETIREMENT PLANS WITH URS WHILE EMPLOYED WITH
RIVERTON CITY.


Riverton City will follow the Utah State requirements for employees that have opted out of the URS
system or are post-retired.

EMPLOYEES WORKING LESS THAN 20 HOURS PER WEEK FOR RIVERTON CITY.


Employees working less than 20 hours per week or who are temporary employees will have an amount
equal to 7.5% of the employee’s gross wage paid into a 457 plan for the employee which is managed by
URS. For purposes of URS coverage, the City classifies all elected officials as part-time. Eligibility for
retirement coverage under Utah Retirement Systems shall be administered in accordance with the
statutory rules governing Utah Retirement Systems.
