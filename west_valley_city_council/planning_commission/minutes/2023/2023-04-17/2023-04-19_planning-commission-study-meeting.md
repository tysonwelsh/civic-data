GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-1-2023 Petition by RUSS NELSON requesting a General Plan change from Non-Retail
           Commercial to General Commercial and a zone change from RB (Residential
           Business) to C-2 (General Commercial). The property is located at 4543 and 4547
           S 4000 W on 0.73 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov) Continued from the March 22, 2023 public hearing.

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained that this is a continued item. He noted the subject
               property is developed as a daycare and zoned Residential Business. Steve said the
               applicant is requesting a zone change to commercial to add a carwash. He stated
               that the concern is regarding the proposed access on 4000 West. The Public
               Works Department will not allow another access, so it was suggested that the
               applicant reach out to Home Depot to see if they could share the existing access.
               However, they wanted to keep the access private. Steve said he tried to reach the
               applicant, but they have not responded. He is unsure if they will proceed with this
              proposal or withdraw the application.

              Commissioner McEwen asked if they could share one of the other access points
              on 4000 West. Steve explained that in a situation where they are removing the
              existing structure to redevelop the property, the current codes would apply to the
              new development.

              Commissioner McEwen suggested looking at the zoning on the subject property
              and considering a rezone so there is more opportunity for development.


ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-3-2023 Petition by HH-SALT LAKE, LLC requesting amendments to Sections 7-1-103
          and 7-6-301 of the zoning ordinance to remove the definition for Lingerie Store
          and to remove Lingerie Store from the Commercial and Manufacturing Use
          Table. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)
          Continued from the April 12, 2023 public hearing.

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik said this item was continued from last week. He explained that the
              applicant requested to change two sections of the Code. The first change would be
              to remove the definition of a Lingerie Store from the ordinance, and the second
              would be to remove the term Lingerie Store from the Commercial and
              Manufacturing Use Table. Therefore, it would no longer exist in the Code.

              Brandon Hill, Assistant City Attorney, said that during last week's meeting,
              Commissioner Woodruff had wanted more information regarding the background
              of this application. He explained that when the store first went in, the focus was if
              it was a sexually oriented business and met all of the associated requirements.
              Brandon stated that it was determined this was not an SOB. He explained that
              there was little conversation around a lingerie store. Brandon said that within the
              ordinance, an SOB covers things such as strip clubs and other similar businesses.
              He pointed out that the term lingerie refers to an article of clothing.


              Chair Fuller said Commissioner Woodruff was also concerned about the
              ramifications of approving this proposal. Brandon explained that the SOB in the
              ordinance would cover most of the problematic businesses.


              Commissioner Wood mentioned that she needs help understanding why they
              would have put a Lingerie Store within the Overlay Zone, knowing they could not
              sell lingerie. Brandon said when staff discussed the store with the applicant; the
              focus was on a potential SOB, not a lingerie store. He noted that the applicant was
              likely unaware of the store not being allowed in this location and believes it was
              an oversite.
              Commissioner Lovato asked if something within the ordinance would catch a
              potential issue, so we do not have a similar situation in the future. Brandon stated
              as staff, we try to capture everything from the applicant upfront and provide them
              with the City's expectations upfront. He said this makes the process more
              effective.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
S-2-2023  Petition by GREY GARZA is requesting preliminary approval for the Grove
          South Industrial Park Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 13.8
          acres. The proposed subdivision is located at 5551 South U-111 and is zoned M
          (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-
          ut.gov).

Discussion:   Steve Lehman presented an aerial photo that showed the subject property and
              explained that the applicant owns the subject location and some connecting the
              property to the north and the east that the applicant has already acquired or will
              acquire. Steve noted that the Kennecott Copper Railroad runs between these
              properties. He explained that a bridge for the railroad was going over U-111 and
              had been removed, and the rail line either was abandoned or will be abandoned.
              Steve explained that once that land is cleaned-up, the applicant wants to acquire
              it. Steve noted that this proposal is to subdivide the subject property with a single
              access into two lots, with a dedicated right-of-way and a road extension to the
              east.

              Commissioner Fuller asked if they are planning to keep the property zoned
              Manufacturing. Steve said he believes the zone will remain the same, and the
              applicant intends to build industrial/warehouse buildings on the property.
