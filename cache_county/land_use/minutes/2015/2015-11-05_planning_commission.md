#1 Barber Conditional Use Permit (Andrew & Shelly Barber) 
 
Harrild reviewed Mr. Andrew Barber’s request for a conditional use permit (CUP) to allow an accessory 
apartment in a single family dwelling located on 50.25 acres of property at 6189 south 2400 West, east of 
Wellsville (Agricultural, A10 Zone). The intent for the apartment is for a potential semi-private living 
space for members of their family at their own discretion, such as elderly parents or married children.  
The proposed apartment does not have its own entrance, utilities, water, septic, or parking area. The 
proposed use must comply with the requirements for definition 1120 – Accessory Apartment. 
 
Mr. Andrew Barber if you change the process for this, can I get my money back? 
 
Harrild unfortunately, no. 
 
Mr. Andrew Barber there was some angst over this issue because as I filled out the application with 
staff I was told to answer “does not apply” for all the questions.

#4 Musselman Airport Conditional Use Permit (Greg Musselman) 
 
Harrild reviewed Mr. Musselman’s request for approval of a conditional use permit (CUP) to allow a 
private airport located on 114.32 acres of property at 11800 South 1300 East, Avon (Agricultural, A10 
Zone). This item was continued from October’s meeting.  Staff and commission discussed the items the 
FFA deemed did not apply to the type of aircraft that will be using this runway and also discussed the 
location of the nearest home. The flight pattern is setup to not impact or fly over that home unless it is an 
emergency situation. The road was the main issue and staff was waiting on the review of the fire district 
at the time of the last planning commission. The Fire Marshall has reviewed the project and has stated 
that the proposed airstrip, the access road shall be a minimum of 12’ wide, all-weather surface such that 
fire apparatus and emergency medical vehicles are able to access the site in a minimal amount of time 
under weather conditions common to the area. The other thing to note is the fire district has stated that 
during the winter the Fire District does not have apparatus capable of traveling over snow and will not be 
able to provide fire protection in the event of an accident. Furthermore, medical assistance will be delayed 
and will require a response by the Cache County Sherriff’s office Search and Rescue Team. 
 
Staff and commission discussed the necessary improvements on the road. Mr. Musselman will have to 
lay gravel down to meet the requirements of the Fire District. Some members expressed concerns with 
applying public road requirements when fire protection and medical personal will not be able to access 
this site always. Staff upholds the requirements of the Fire Marshall and he has to enforce the code.   
 
Mr. Greg Musselman I actually had an opportunity to talk with the Fire Marshall about this and he wrote 
a letter to the Development office. My issue is, and Mr. Olsen is aware of the road, the road is just a dirt 
road and people can drive around on the grass to get past things. But I did scrape the ground today and I 
sent pictures to Chris.  My argument with this is, if the road isn’t passable I won’t be going up there and 
there won’t be a need for emergency services. Also, in the wintertime if we use this, the Fire Department 
has already said they won’t be able to get up there. I actually propose that we strike #5 from the
