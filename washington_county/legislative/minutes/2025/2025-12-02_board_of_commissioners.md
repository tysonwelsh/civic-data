WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
                                 MEETING MINUTES
                                  DECEMBER 2, 2025

 The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
 Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on December 2, 2025, in the Washington County
 Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance
 were Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin
 Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

         INVOCATION BY: Sharon Shores, Universal Life Church Ministries

         PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Jason Bradley, Emergency Operations Director

 CONSENT AGENDA:

 The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
 commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
 the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
 items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

    •   Consideration of Auditor-Approved Claims for Payment for November 19, 2025, through
        December 2, 2025
    •   Consideration of Regular Commission Meeting Minutes of November 18, 2025
    •   Consideration of Special Commission Meeting Minutes of November 18, 2025
    •   Consideration of Administrative Adjustments: Real Property
    •   Consideration of Administrative Adjustments: Personal Property
    •   Consideration of Proof of Publication of Ordinances No. 2025-1309-0 and 2025-1310-0,
        Published on Sunday, November 23, 2025
    •   Consideration of Amended Plat Map for Pine Valley Ranchos
    •   Consideration of the 2026 Participation Agreement for the Utah Cooperative Wildfire
        System

MOTION:         Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve the Consent Agenda as Outlined.
                Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
                with Commissioners Iverson, Snow, and Almquist voting aye.

 CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2025-1311-O, AN ORDINANCE REZONING A
 CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY
 1850 W MILLS LANE NEAR LEEDS FROM THE A-20 ZONE (AGRICULTURAL 20
 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE), TO THE PDR ZONE (PLANNED DEVELOPMENT
 RESIDENTIAL), HEREINAFTER FULLY DESCRIBED IN THIS ORDINANCE
 (APPLICANT - SOLARA COMMUNITIES, LLC)

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained the next two items are in regards to the
same project. He stated there is a 10 acre portion of a nearly 200 hundred acre parcel near Leeds
that will include a total of 74 residential units consisting of 60 townhomes and 14 single family
lots. This is a rezone of 10 acres only and isn' t dependent on any other rezone or the proposed
PID. Mr. Messel explained these first 75 lots can be approved with one access, but any further
development would need to add further access points in the future.

MOTION:         Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Ordinance No. 2025-1311-0, An
                Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County Located at
                Approximately 1850 W Mills Lane Near Leeds from the A-20 Zone
                (Agricultural 20 Acre Minimum Lot Size), to the PDR Zone (Planned
                Development Residential), Hereinafter Fully Described in This Ordinance
                (Applicant - Solara Communities, LLC). Motion seconded by Commissioner
                Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow
                and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3631. A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE PLANNED DEVELOPMENT PROJECT PLAN AND DEVELOPMENT
AGREEMENT FOR SOLARA 75 (APPLICANT - SOLARA COMMUNITIES, LLC)

Mr. Messel reviewed descriptive slides and stated the project plan includes all details including
construction plans, density phasing, open space and landscaping plans for the project. The
development agreement has to be approve concurrently with the other approvals and controls the
legal aspects of the development.

 CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3632, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
 A REIMBURSEMENT AGREEMENT WITH WASHINGTON COUNTY FLOOD
 CONTROL AUTHORITY REGARDING THE UPPER SANTA CLARA RIVER
 PROJECT

Public Works Director Todd Edwards explained this project has been ongoing with the Flood
Control Authority for some time. Washington County agrees to complete flood control
improvements including vegetation and wood removal from the Santa Clara River channel, with
the Flood Control Authority agreeing to reimburse the County for the cost of the project up to
$80,000.

 MOTION:       Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2025-3632,
               A Resolution Approving a Reimbursement Agreement With Washington
               County Flood Control Authority Regarding the Upper Santa Clara River
               Project. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous
               vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3633, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A CONTRACT WITH GENASYS FOR EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE
SERVICES

Emergency Operations Manager Jason Bradley stated Washington County has needed evacuation
zones for some time with Genasys software creating those evacuation zones for the public in case
of emergency. He explained thi s is a homeland security reimbursement project, with the County
being able to recoup most of these costs.

MOTION:       Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2025-3633, A
              Resolution Approving a Contract with Genasys for Emergency Management
              Software Services. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
              by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow, and Almquist voting
              aye.

COMMISSIONER'S REPORT

Nothing presented.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:36 p.m.
