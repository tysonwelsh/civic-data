CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2599, A RESOLUTION
RATIFYING THE AGREEMENT WITH SWITCHPOINT FOR EMERGENCY

FUNDS DUE TO COVID-19

Chairman Iverson explained that due to the COVID-19 pandemic that Switchpoint has not
been able to hold their annual fundraiser. Switchpoint requested funding so they could
continue to be operational during the pandemic as it is a critical service offered to the
citizens of Washington County. Funds will be used to continue to provide services to the
low-income and homeless population including: soup kitchen, emergency shelter,
emergency food pantry, social detox, residential treatment, affordable housing options and
thrift store and other COVID-19 related needs.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2020-2599,
A Resolution Ratifying the Agreement with Switchpoint for Emergency
Funds Due to COVID-19. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist,
and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2600, A RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING _ EXPENDITURES OF _ FUNDS COLLECTED _AS

WASHINGTON COUNTY'S SHARE OF THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND
PARKS SALES TAX

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that in 2014 the Recreation, Arts, and
Parks (RAP) initiative was placed on the ballot for approval. It was approved for a ten (10)
year period, wherein, citizens would pay 1/10" of one percent (1%) that would be
distributed to the various municipalities based on point of sale and population.

As part of the established Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), the unincorporated
areas of the County also receive funding based on the distribution guidelines. Several
communities in the unincorporated area have applied for funding including: Diamond
Valley, Silver Reef, and Veyo.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Commissioner Almquist stated that Washington County along with neighboring Counties
petitioned the Governor seeking to move Washington County from an orange designation
to a yellow designation in regards to COVID-19 restrictions. The Governor denied the
request to move to yellow, however, Commissioner Almquist cautioned all the citizens to
continue being mindful of their behaviors for safety purposes.

Chairman Iverson expressed frustrations with the Governor and stated that it should be up
to the counties to determine the various health risks within each County in regards to
mandated restrictions.

Commissioner Cox stated that social distancing and self-quarantine measures have
flattened the curve of the spread of COVID-19, however, the safety of each individual is a
personal responsibility.

Ms. Felshaw mentioned that video broadcast of Commission Meetings has been streamed
on Facebook and via Zoom since the beginning of the COVID-19 pandemic. It is likely for

that to continue moving forward as it has proven to be a valuable resource for citizens to
gain access to the meetings remotely.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:36 p.m.
