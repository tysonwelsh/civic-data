     Recognizing Ogden Pride for their successful Ogden Pride Virtual
     Festival and support of the LGBTQ+ community
               A memo from Council staff regarding recognition of Ogden Pride for their successful Ogden Pride Virtual Festival and
     support of the LGBTQ+ community came before the Council for consideration. Council member Nadolski read the certificate of
     recognition issued by the Ogden City Council and Mayor Caldwell and presented Ogden Pride President Brian Barnum with the
     certificate.
               Mr. Barnum thanked the Council for this recognition; he recognized his Board for being innovative and proactive in
     finding ways to hold the annual Ogden Pride Festival in virtual format during a pandemic.
                Council member White stated that Ogden Pride is relatively young organization, yet they have done so much for Ogden
     City and the LGBTQ+ community; she noted that suicide rates in Utah continue to increase and many of those suicides are young
     people who identify as LGBTQ+ and it warms her heart to know that this organization is here and providing much needed
     advocacy work in the community. She is grateful there are people that can rely upon Ogden Pride for their support and she hopes
     it is possible to hold a live festival next year.

Natural Resources and Sustainability Advisory Committee Annual Report
          The Natural Resources and Sustainability Advisory Committee annual report came before the Council for consideration.
Committee Chair Heather Root used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide the Council with an overview of the report,
including accomplishments, cunent projects underway, and future plans and goals of the committee.
          Council member Lopez thanked the Committee for their efforts.
          Council member White stated that this Committee has far exceeded the vision that she had when she initially proposed
the creation of this body; she is excited about the policy impacts that their work will have on the entire community.
          Council member Nadolski asked ifthere is one issue that gamers the greatest amount of enthusiasm from the Committee.
Ms. Root stated that the entire group is very enthusiastic about air quality.
          Chair Choberka asked if the Committee has considered opportunities for facilitating a community outreach campaign in
an emergency situation. Ms. Root stated that is something that the Committee has discussed at length and there are concerns
relating to the problems caused when individual members attempt to speak on behalf of the entire group or even the City; this is an
issue that the Committee will continue to work on over the course of the next several months as it is very important. Chair
Choberka stated that if the Council can offer any support for those efforts, she would be willing to consider such. a request.
          COUNCIL MEMBER HYER MOVED TO ACCEPT THE NATURAL RESOURCES AND SUSTAINABILITY
ADVISORY COMMITTEE ANNUAL REPORT FOR REVIEW. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL
MEMBER LOPEZ, ALL VOTING AYE. Council member Stephens was not present when this vote was taken.

Public Comments
          Heath Satow stated that he read the documentation regarding the recycling program that was presented during tonight's
work session; the documentation indicated that the program was suspended March 3, but it was 19 weeks later, on July 13, when
the City provided a press release regarding that fact. Press releases are intended to inform citizens of what the City is up to, but
what was not mentioned in the recycling update is that the press release was issued almost a week after the newspaper article was
published regarding the City's problems with recycling. It is also his understanding that the City's Sustainability Committee
learned about the program's suspension via that same news article. He stated that issuing press releases only after being 'busted'
by local newspaper is not how good government works. He requested that the City Administration be more transparent on literally
every issue in the City, particularly when the information is embanassing. The citizens should be treated like adults as they can
handle the truth. The City should also actually use its advisory committees to make better decisions. He read an abbreviated
description of the role of the Sustainability Committee: "the members ofthis Committee provide policy and program
