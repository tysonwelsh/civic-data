                                     PUBLIC NOTICE

Please be advised that November 7, 2024, the City Council of the City of St George
approved the following ordinance(s):



Ordinance No. 2024-066 to vacate a portion of an existing municipal utility easement
located on the eastern boundary of Lots 11 and 30 of the Woodhaven Estates Subdivision.

Ordinance No. 2024-067 to vacate a portion of an existing public utility and drainage
easement located on the southern boundary of Lot 12 of Ancestor Estates Phase 2.

Ordinance No. 2024-068 to vacate Lot 21 from the Ancestor Estates Phase 3 subdivision.

Ordinance No. 2024-069 to amend the City zoning map from A-20 (Agricultural, 20-acre
minimum lot size) to PD-R (Planned Development Residential) on approximately 35.16 acres
generally located southeast off exit 6 along Southern Parkway at the eastern terminus of
Desert Canyons Parkway.

Ordinance No. 2024-070 to amend an approved PD-C (Planned Development Commercial)
on approximately 3.44 acres located on the northeast corner of Black Ridge Drive and 250
West.
