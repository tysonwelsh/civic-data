WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
OCTOBER 9, 2025

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 1:00 p.m. on October 9, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow,
County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

INVOCATION BY: Commissioner Gil Almquist
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Commissioner Adam Snow

CONSENT AGENDA:

The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3598, A RESOLUTION ADOPTING
THE UPDATED ZION SCENIC BYWAY CORRIDOR MANAGEMENT PLAN

Commissioner Iverson stated this item was previously tabled to allow for further review and
consideration of the plan. The commission has had adequate time for review and consideration,
and it is ready to be adopted.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3598, A
Resolution Adopting the Updated Zion Scenic Byway Corridor Management
Plan. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3599, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE SANTA CLARA RIVER WATERSHED PLAN AGREEMENT BETWEEN NRCS
AND WASHINGTON COUNTY

Public Works Director Todd Edwards stated this agreement is to receive fourteen million dollars
from the Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) to assist with flood control along the
Santa Clara River areas in Diamond Valley, Dammeron Valley and the Shivwits Reservation.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3599, A
Resolution Approving the Santa Clara River Watershed Plan Agreement
Between NRCS and Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and
Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3600, A RESOLUTION PROVIDING

SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that this Resolution is to authorize the county
commission to temporarily fill vacancies on the Dixie Deer Special Service District Administrative

Control Board.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3600, A
Resolution Providing the Temporary Appointment of Board Members in the
Dixie Deer Special Service District. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow

CONSIDERATION __OF_ _ RESOLUTION NO. ___R-2025-3601, _A _RESOLUTION
CANCELLING THE NOVEMBER 2025 ELECTION FOR DIXIE DEER SPECIAL
SERVICE DISTRICT

Chief Deputy Clerk Genna Goodwin stated the Dixie Deer SSD has three current vacancies and
lacks a quorum to hold a meeting to fill the vacancies, so it is necessary to cancel the November
2025 election because there were no statutorily required declarations of candidacy submitted.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3601, A
Resolution Cancelling the November 2025 Election for Dixie Deer Special
Service District. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3602, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE EXPENDITURE OF TRT FUNDING FOR AN EVENT MARKETING
AGREEMENT FOR THE COPPER ROCK CHAMPIONSHIP EVENT

Greater Zion Sports and Adventure Marketing Manager Chad McWilliams explained the Tourism
Advisory Board (TAB) has recommended the use of $100,000 of Tourism Tax Revenue (TRT) for
an event marketing package for the LPGA Golf Event at Copper Rock Golf Course on May 13-
16, 2026.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3602, A
Resolution Approving the Expenditure of TRT Funding for an Event
Marketing Agreement for the Copper Rock Championship Event. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

PHASE 1 OF THE BUENA VISTA TRAIL

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3604, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND THE
TOWN _OF SPRINGDALE FOR TOURISM TAX FUNDING TO ASSIST IN THE
CONSTRUCTION OF THE GEORGE A. BARKER RIVER EXPANSION

Ms. Fonger explained Springdale recently acquired one acre of land near the George A. Barker
Park to expand the bathroom, grass and walking areas. This park will be expanded in two phases,
with the TAB recommending contributing $1,250,000 in TRT and/or TRCC funding for the

expansion.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3604, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Agreement Between Washington County
and the Town of Springdale for Tourism Tax Funding to Assist in the
Construction of the George A. Barker River Expansion Subject to Attorney
Review. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3605, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_GRANT AGREEMENT WITH THE OFFICE OF TOURISM FOR THE UTAH
COOPERATIVE MARKETING PROGRAM

Ms. Fonger stated Washington County received grant funding in the amount of $182,250 for Utah
Tourism entities to promote and market the film industry to out-of-state visitors. Greater Zion will
provide matching funds.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3605, A
Resolution Approving a Grant Agreement with the Office of Tourism for the
Utah Cooperative Marketing Program. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and
Almquist voting aye.

Ms. Lafaele stated this agreement is for a talent broker organization that will assist in talent
acquisition for the big-ticket events.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3607, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement with Variety Attractions, Inc. for Talent
Acquisition for the 2026 Washington County Fair. Motion seconded by

WASHINGTON COUNTY FAIR
