From: Travis VanEkelenburg
CC:  Date: June 2, 2020
Re: Ordinance #1439 Stevens Lot Rezone Comments: Applicant, Bryan Flamm, has submitted this application on behalf of Kim Stevens; owner of the subject property. This property is accessed by a private lane and is approximately 1.0 Acre in size and is not part of a recorded subdivision. The property is currently zoned RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 ft² lot minimum).  The applicant is seeking a change in zoning designation from RA1 to RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 ft² lot minimum) with the intention of purchasing the north half of the property and obtaining an amended subdivision plat to include the property as part of his rear yard. The applicant’s property is contiguous to the subject property and located within the Kennington Estates Subdivision.
The Planning Commission, at their regularly scheduled meeting on May 14, 2020, forwarded a positive recommendation with a vote of 4-1.  The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. That Section 9-5-060 of the Draper City Municipal Code allows for the amendment of the city’s zoning map;
2.  That all findings for a zone map change, as contained in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E), are satisfied;
3.  That adequate facilities and services exist to serve the subject property, including but not limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, storm water drainage systems, water supplies, and waste water and refuse collection.
