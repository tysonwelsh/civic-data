2. 24-290 City Council interviewing Britney Ward to serve as the Sandy City
"Assistant Director of Public Works / City Engineer"

Attachments: Asst Director of PW_City Engineer Job Specifications

Resolution 24-38C
Mayor Zoltanski introduced Britney Ward to the Council for their consideration for advice
and consent of the appointment of Ms. Ward to the position of City Engineer/Assistant
Director of the Public Works Department. The Mayor reviewed Ms. Ward's background
and qualifications for this position along with her recommendation for the appointment.
Ryan Kump, Public Works Director, further discussed Ms. Ward's background as well as
his intended restructuring of the department that aligned with this proposed appointment.
The City Council interviewed Britney Ward regarding her background and experience. Ms.
Ward responded to Council questions and spoke about the combined role of city engineer
and deputy public works director. She has been currently acting as the Assistant City
Engineer and she spoke about her responsibilities associated with this position as well
as her managerial qualifications. Council acknowledged her work on various projects
including the traffic light scynchronization program and her responsiveness to council and
residents concerns and questions. Ms. Ward spoke about the importance of working
collaboratively, using a fiscally conservative approach, and the importance of
understanding the problem and listening to others. She also mentioned that she has a
similar approach to the current public works director and works well with him. She spoke
about balancing her work load and relying on her team to delegate tasks. The Council
thanked Ms. Ward for her interest and service to the city and will reach out to her if they
need additional information.

3. 24-292 Presentation of RDA Consulting Agreement with Cottonwood Business
Consulting, LLC, for Small Business Liaison services.

Attachments: Small Business Liaison Staff Report (8.20.24)

2024.08.05 Consulting Agreement
Proposal Cottonwood Business Consulting Small Business Liasion
Services RDA 2024
Kasey Dunlavy, Economic Development Agendcy Director, presented to the Council the
consulting agreement with Cottonwood Business Consulting for small business liaison
services rendered to the Redevelopment Agency for the upcoming fiscal year. He spoke
about the outreach to small businesses in Sandy City, the business retention program,
and the positive feedback received from businesses. He spoke about the expansion of the
business connect program and the responsiveness and service his department provided
to small businesses. Other cities have a dedicated full time staff in this position and he
spoke about the efficiencies of hiring a consultant for this program. A Request for
Proposal (RFP) was issued by the city for this liaison position. Based on the responses
received, Cottonwood Business Consultant was the recommended firm for the position.
Council questions and comments followed. Council saw the need to provide and support
small businesses in Sandy, but expressed some concerns that this program may be
outside the scope of services a city typically provides. Council asked about the program
goals and marketing program. Mr. Dunlavy spoke about the benefits of having a small
business liaison. There are 6,000 small businesses in Sandy and it would be impossible
for a department staff of 3 to provide substantial outreach services to these small
businesses without a liaison. He believed this outreach strengthened our community.
Council requested that the RDA do an analysis based on the Perato Principle to measure
the effectiveness of this outreach program and felt additional information needed to be
done to support the need to hire a consultant. Other questions included the focus on
small businesses and asked what resources and outreach was done for medium and
large businesses. Council also had questions regarding the $40,000 cost associated with
the program consultant. Council asked if the South Valley Chamber was better equipped
to provide outreach to small businesses. Expenses related to small business outreach
and development are allowed by RDA's under the Utah State Code. Mr. Dunlavy thought
Ms. Johnson did an excellent job servicing our small business community in Sandy. Mr.
Dunlavy thanked the Council for their consideration and asked them to reach out to him if
they needed any additional information.

Council Member Business
Council Member Marci Houseman expressed appreciation to the community and the
Sandy Police Department for their response to a recent incident involving a Jordan High
School student. She also acknowledged the Council and Administration for their efforts to
bring a Gold Star Memorial to Sandy City. Draper City just recently installed their own
Gold Star Memorial. Yesterday was the three year anniversary of the 13 Marines killed in
Afghanistan. We have not forgotten their bravery and courage. We have not forgotten Staff
Sergeant Taylor Hoover. She appreciated and acknowledged the Gold Star Memorials
installed in Utah and around the country to honor the families and relatives of those who
died while in active military service.
Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked and congratulated the Fire Department and Chief
McConaghie for holding a wonderful swearing in ceremony last night. He also supported
the recent management and leadership decisions made in the department which he felt
provided greater depth and opportunities for the department personnel.
Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer spoke about the conflict that arises when as a council
member you have a duty to represent your constituents and then a higher form of
government does something that negatively impacts your constituents. He spoke about
the Legislature's recent actions regarding the Supreme Courts unanimous decision. He
feels he has a duty to speak out on this on behalf of his constituents who could be hurt
by this decision and encouraged residents to uphold judicial integrity, uphold the state
constitution, and to not be political about gerrymandering and boundary maps. He also
encouraged all to retain the full say of the people to override bills that don't represent us.
Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledged the Sandy Police Officers and Fire
Department staff for their responsiveness to the Jordan High School Community. He has
a student at Jordan High School and he thanked all the first responders for their great
response to the high school community. He supports our city first responders and their
needs. This was an incredibly difficult incident for the community and first responders. He
requested a presentation from the Police regarding how gangs and gang violence is
impacting our schools. He would like to know what is being done about this issue. The
Fire Department swearing in ceremony was a wonderful event. The South Valley Chamber
golf event was a great event and offered a great opportunity to meet local business
owners in Sandy.

the incident at Jordan High School. She thanked the Fire Chief for the wonderful swearing

presentation from a youth council member regarding air soft guns and how the toy gun
can be confused for a real gun if the orange tip has been removed. She spoke about an
initiative by Mayor Wilson regarding this matter. She also spoke about the proposed $570
million dollar bond that will be on the ballot this year for the county. She can forward more
information to you. She provided an update from the Healthy Sandy and Historic
Committee. The Heritage Festival will be held on September 28th. All are welcome.
