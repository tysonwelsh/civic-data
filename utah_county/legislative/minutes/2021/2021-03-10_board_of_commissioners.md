MARCH 2021 EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH: NICK LANCE (COMMISSIONER LEE READ
NOMINATION):

I can't think of a County employee more deserving of the Employee of the Month recognition
than Nick Lance with Public Works. Nick has been our (the Children’s Justice Center — CJC)
Building Maintenance Specialist since he started his county employment. The CJC’s facility has
been in tip-top shape since he took over the responsibilities of maintaining our building. Nick
responds quickly to our work orders and has an amazing skill set to be able to quickly fix all the
issues this old house throws his way. He is always prompt and professional while working in our
building.

We serve children and families who are experiencing the crisis and chaos that comes with the
disclosure of significant abuse, and Nick is conscientious of that when there are clients in the
building. Once we had a family inquire about Nick and what job he had at the center. The family
commented that when they were walking in the building, he had held the door open and greeted
them as they entered the center. They genuinely appreciated his warm welcome. It meant a lot to
them as they were nervous coming to the center for their appointment that day.

In March of 2020 (at the start of the COVID craziness) we started a flooring replacement project
at the Center. Our carpeting/flooring was 20+ years old and very much looked it. As the person over
our building, Nick was responsible for playing musical office furniture, sometimes twice a day,
every day for nearly 4 weeks while the flooring was being replaced. He would arrive early in the
morning (sometimes with additional help, oft-times by himself with only a hand truck) to move all
the office furniture from the offices the flooring would be worked on; only to return it either later
that day, or early the next to its original spot. (Not just office furniture, this included appliances,
toilets, large storage shelves, etc.) This work was tedious (you don’t realize how much furniture is
in your office, until you must empty it out), cramped, heavy, awkward, and quite frankly never
ending. Nick showed up every morning upbeat, positive, and NEVER had a word of complaint. He
took great care in moving all the items and remembering where they needed to be returned to. He
worked so incredibly hard in our building every day for 4 plus weeks and still had to tackle his
multitude of other important responsibilities. The entire flooring project would have been a true
logistical nightmare if it wasn’t for Nick. His hard work, positive attitude, and attention to detail is
reflected in how great the CJC looks. He does stellar work and we literally wouldn't have this
amazing building without him working hard to keep it this way.

For this reason and so many more we could list Nick Lance is deserving of the Employee of the
Month Award.

Submitted by: Children's Justice Center Staff

BRYCE ARMSTRONG - THERE ARE A COUPLE LAND USES THAT PLAY INTO THIS
SPECIFIC FACILITY.

THE UTAH COUNTY LAND USE ORDINANCE WHICH DESIGNATES WHAT DEFINED
AS A PUBLIC PARK SO THIS PARK WE CALL IT THE NORTH COUNTY EQUESTRIAN
PARK. IT’S FIRST AND FOREMOST A PUBLIC PARK. THE CURRENT ORDINANCE
LISTS A PUBLIC PARK AS PERMITTED USE SUBJECT TO MEETING CERTAIN
CONDITIONS OR REQUIREMENTS THAT ARE SPELLED OUT IN THE ORDINANCE
INCLUDING THE SUBMITTAL OF A SITE PLAN, AN OPERATIONS DISCLOSURE
STATEMENT THAT INCLUDES AN OPERATIONS PLAN.

THERE’S ALSO WHAT’S TERMED A PUBLIC PARK FACILITY WHICH IS A
RECREATIONAL TYPE USE LOCATED WITHIN A PUBLIC PARK. PRIOR TO 2008,
THE UTAH COUNTY LAND USE ORDINANCE REQUIRED THOSE TYPE OF USES OR
FACILITIES TO BE APPROVED THROUGH THE CONDITIONAL USE PROCESS AND SO
SPECIFICALLY THE EQUESTRIAN ARENA WAS SUBJECT TO THAT CONDITIONAL
USE PROCESS. IT WENT THROUGH THAT PROCESS BEGINNING IN 2001.

IN 2008 THE UTAH CANYONLANDS ORDINANCE WAS AMENDED TO INCLUDE A
PUBLIC PARK FACILITY AS A PERMITTED USE MEANING IT COULD BE APPROVED
THROUGH AN ADMINISTRATIVE PROCESS SHOWING THAT IT MEETS THE
REQUIREMENTS OF THE ORDINANCE WHICH INCLUDES A SITE PLAN, AN
OPERATIONS PLAN SIMILAR TO PREVIOUSLY. IT JUST DID NOT REQUIRE TO GO
THROUGH THE CONDITIONAL USE PROCESS AFTER 2008,

LET ME RUN THROUGH SOME OF THE APPROVALS ASSOCIATED WITH THE
EQUESTRIAN PUBLIC PARK FACILITY ELEMENT OF THIS PUBLIC PARK. IN 2001
THERE WAS A CONDITIONAL USE THAT WAS APPROVED FOR A PUBLIC PARK
FACILITY THE EQUESTRIAN FACILITY COMPONENT SUBJECT TO THE CONDITIONS
OF THAT THE ANNUAL REVIEW OF THE PERMIT BE CONDUCTED UNTIL A REVIEW
WAS NO LONGER NEEDED. A FINAL OPERATION PLAN SUBMITTED IDENTIFIED
SOME TREES NEEDED TO BE PLANTED AS A BUFFER AND THEN ALSO THEY HAD
SOME OPERATION TIMES THAT WERE INCLUDED SPECIFICALLY SO THOSE WERE
