ORDINANCE NO. 2020-0-20
AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING TEMPORARY MONUMENT SIGN
STANDARDS IN THE MAIN STREET FORM-BASED CODE (MS-FBC)
ZONE.

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 10-9a-504 Utah Code Annotated, Midvale City (“the
City”) may, without prior consideration of or recommendation from the planning commission,
enact an ordinance establishing a temporary land use regulation for any part or all of the area
within the municipality if the City Council makes a finding of compelling, countervailing public
interest; and

WHEREAS, Subsection 17-7-11.10(E) of the Midvale City Municipal Code currently
prohibits monument signs taller than 6’ in height with a sign area of more than 50 square feet;
and

WHEREAS, the City Council desires a monument sign to be placed on the City Hall
property to provide citizens and visitors with emergency information about the ongoing
Coronavirus-19 pandemic; and

WHEREAS, although the City has sought to shrink the monument sign to comply with
the existing zoning regulations, its contractor has indicated that it cannot reduce the sign any
further and meet the communication needs of the City; and

WHEREAS, the City Council desires to adopt a temporary land use ordinance with the
minimum dimensions necessary to construct a monument sign to provide emergency information
about the ongoing Coronavirus-19 pandemic to citizens and visitors.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Midvale City, Utah
as follows:

Section |. Finding of compelling, countervailing public interest. This ordinance
incorporates the recitals and finds that the recitals constitute a compelling, countervailing public
interest which justifies the temporary land use regulation set forth in the Section 2 below.

Section 2. Temporary Zoning Regulation. From the effective date of this ordinance and
during the period of the temporary land use regulation, Subsection 17-7-11.10(E) of the Midvale
City Municipal Code is amended to reflect the language in Exhibit A.

Section 3. Duration. This temporary land use regulation shall remain in effect for a
period of six months from the effective date of this ordinance or until the effective date of a City
Council action adopting revisions to monument sign standards in Subsection 17-7-11.10(E) of
the Midvale City Municipal Code, whichever occurs first.

E. Monument Signs.
1. Intent. A monument sign is freestanding and located in a front or side yard of a lot.
2. Regulations.
a. Monument signs shall be developed according to the standards in Table 10.04.
b. Multiple tenant buildings on a lot with a width of greater than three hundred feet,
measured across the front property line, may have signage with the following
parameters:
i. Up to two monument signs on one frontage.
ii. Signs shall be at least one hundred fifty feet apart.
ili. Monument signs may not be pole mounted.
iv. Manually changeable text is not permitted for monument signs.
v. | May serve multiple purposes such as seating.
vi. If placed closer than five feet from the front and corner side property lines,
signs must meet clear view requirements.
vil. Double-sided signs are permitted.

c. Buildings on a lot with a width of greater than six hundred feet, measured across a
primary frontage, may have signage with the following parameters along such
frontage:

i, Up to 9” tall and a maximum area of 75 SF per sign face.

ii, __ Up to two monument signs on one frontage.

iii, _ Signs shall be at least three hundred feet apart.

iv. _ Monument signs may not be pole mounted.

vy. __ Manually changeable text is not permitted for monument signs.
vi. __ May serve multiple purposes, such as seating.
