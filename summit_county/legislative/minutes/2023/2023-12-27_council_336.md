Advice and consent of Council regarding the County Manager's recommendation to appoint a Justice Court Judge (4:22 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Manager Shayne Scott explained the process for his selection of the proposed Summit County Justice Court Judge.
Attachment: 122723 Ltr to Council re Justice Court Judge.pdf
Roger Armstrong endorsed the Manager’s recommendation based on his participation on the selection committee. (4:24 PM)
Malena Stevens also endorsed the appointment based on her experience with Janet Elledge. (4:26 PM)
Margaret Olson also spoke on behalf of Janet Elledge’s appointment. (4:28 PM)
Canice Harte made a motion to approve the County Manager's recommendation to appoint Janet Elledge Summit County Justice Court Judge. (4:29 PM). Christopher Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Janet Elledge thanked those involved. (4:30 PM)
