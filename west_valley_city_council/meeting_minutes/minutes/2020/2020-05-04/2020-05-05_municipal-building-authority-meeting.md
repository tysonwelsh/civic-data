     APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF ANNUAL MEETING HELD JANUARY 7, 2020
     The Board considered Minutes of the Annual Meeting held January 7, 2020. There were
     no changes, corrections or deletions.

     Mr. Fitisemanu moved to approve the Minutes of the Annual Meeting held January 7, 2020.
     Ms. Lang seconded the motion.

     A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

     20-02: ADOPT A TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR THE SUPPORT OF THE
     BUILDING AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1,
MINUTES OF BUILDING AUTHORITY REGULAR MEETING – MAY 5, 2020

 2020 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2021; SET JUNE 2, 2020 AS THE DATE FOR THE
 PUBLIC HEARING
 Wayne Pyle, Chief Executive Officer, discussed proposed Resolution 20-02 that would
 adopt a tentative budget for the support of the Building Authority for the fiscal year
 commencing July 1, 2020 and ending June 30, 2021; set June 2, 2020 as the date for the
 Public Hearing.

 Written documentation previously provided to the Building Authority included
 information as follows:

        This resolution adopts the tentative budget for the Building Authority of West
        Valley City for the 2020-2021 fiscal year and sets a date for public comment at a
        hearing on June 2, 2020.

        The Building Authority will adopt a tentative budget that will be made available
        for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s Recorder’s office and
        gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment, before the final adoption of
        this tentative budget for FY 2020-2021.

 Upon inquiry by Chairman Nordfelt there were no further questions from members of the
 Authority, and he called for a motion.

        Mr. Huynh moved to approve Resolution 20-02.

        Mr. Buhler seconded the motion.

        A roll call vote was taken:

        Mr. Fitisemanu                Yes
        Ms. Lang                      Yes
        Mr. Buhler                    Yes
        Mr. Huynh                     Yes
        Mr. Bigelow                   Yes
        Mr. Christensen               Yes
        Chairman Nordfelt             Yes

        Unanimous.


 MOTION TO ADJOURN
 Upon motion by Mr. Buhler all voted in favor to adjourn.
