MEMO
To: City Council
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
Date: 2024-03-12
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinances #1605 and #1606


Comments:

This application is a request for approval for zoning and land use map amendments for
approximately 1.19 acres located on the east side of 700 East, at approximately 11743 S.
700 East. The property is currently zoned RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot
minimum lot size). The applicant is requesting that the zoning and land use map
amendments are approved to allow for an office to operate in the existing home on the
site.

The property owner applied for a business license on March 10, 2022 to locate a software
company at the property in the existing home. However, because the business owner
does not intend to live at the home, they do not meet the qualifications for operating as a
home-based business. Currently, the applicant has indicated he is operating in another
city and is awaiting the outcome of these applications before he potentially moves the
business to Draper. This application proposes to change the zoning to a commercial zone
so that they can convert the home to a commercial use and operate as an office use from
this location. The business license is pending until the outcome of the zoning and land
use map amendment request. If the applicant is successful in obtaining the land use and
zoning map changes, they will still have to obtain approval of an amended site plan to
add the minimum required parking to the property for the intended use.

The Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation on both items on
February 8, 2024 on a 5-0 vote.
