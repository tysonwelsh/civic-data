City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On December 7, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1569, amending the
text of the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code
relating to Manufacturing Zone setbacks and Box N Lock Storage. The complete
ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 7th day of December 2022. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
