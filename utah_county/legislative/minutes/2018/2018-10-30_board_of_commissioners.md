2. ADOPT (OR DENY) AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND SECTION 3-2 OF THE UTAH COUNTY LAND USE
ORDINANCE RELATED TO A PROPOSED EXCEPTION TO REQUIRED YARD SPACE FOR FREE-
STANDING SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS FOR RESIDENTIAL USE APPURTENANT TO AN ON-SITE
DWELLING

- Bryce Armstrong, Community Development -

BRYCE ARMSTRONG, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT, AS YOU RECALL THIS MATTER WAS
REFERED BY THE COMMISSION TO THE PLANNING COMMISSION FOR A LOOK AT OUR SET
BACK REQUIREMENTS AS IT RELATES TO SOLAR PEDISTALS, ARRAYS PERTINATE TO A
DWELLING. AT THE BEGINING OF THE YEAR WE HAD A SIMILAR TEXT AMMENDMENT
RELATED TO GENERATORS, HAC EQUIPMENT. SECTION 2-3 OF OUR LAND USE ORDINANCE
REQUIRES THE SET BACK AREA TO BE CLEARED FROM THE GROUND TO THE SKY. THERE
ARE SEVERAL EXCEPTIONS OF EQUIPMENT, FENCES, POOLS THINGS OF THAT NATURE. WE
ALSO INCLUDE AN EXCEPTION FOR GROUND MOUNTED SOLAR ARRAYS. THIS TEXT
AMMENDENT CREATES THAT EXCEPTION. GENERALLY THIS IS NOT GOING TO BE A BIG
IMPACT ON THE COMMUNITY OR NEIGHBORS. IT HAS BEEN PROPOSED THAT THE HEIGHT
BE LIMITED TO 12 FEET. THE SURFACE AREA OF THE ACTUAL PANELS BE LIMITED TO 1000
SQ FEET. RESEARCH WAS DONE TO ESTABLISH CAPACITY AND WE FEEL THE 1000 SQ FEET
TO BE ADEQUATE. THEN IT WAS REQUESTED THAT WE PROVIDE AN EXCEPTION TO ALLOW
BIGGER SIZES UNDER CERTAIN CONDITIONS.

COMMISSIONER GRAVES MAKES MOTION TO ADOPT ORDINACE. COMMISSIONER LEE

3. ADOPT OR (DENY) AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 3-11-9 OF THE UTAH COUNTY LAND
USE ORDINANCE TO DELETE THE TERM 'CLASS B'
- Peggy Kelsey, Community Development -

PEGGY KELSEY, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT, A BREIF HISTORY OF THIS CHANGE. IN THE
LAND USE ORDINANCE IT IS REQUIRED THAT COMMERCIAL BUSINESS, BUILIDING FRONT ON
A PAVED ROAD. IN SECTION 3-11 THERE ARE EXCEPTIONS TO THE RULE. IN 2010 AN
ORDINANCE WAS PASSED IT ADDED SUB SECTION 9 FOR EXCEPTIONS FOR MINING
OPERATIONS SO THEY COULD FRONT ON AN UNPAVED COUNTY ROAD. THIS PUTS MINING
OPERATIONS FURTHER FROM POPULATED AREAS. IN 2016 WE WERE UPDATING COUNTY
ROADS ON MAPS. IN DOING SO WE LOOKED AT LAND USE RECORDS. TO BE CONSISTANT WE
TOOK TERM COUNTY ROAD AND CHANGED IT TO CLASS B ROAD. THIS SECTIONS
ORDINANCE WAS NOT LOOKED AT CLEARLY ENOUGH AT THAT POINT IN TIME.
ESSINTIALLY WHAT HAPPENED IS IT TOOK COUNTY ROAD TO COUNTY CLASS B ROAD AND
IN EFFECT IT DEFEATED THE WHOLE PURPOSE OF HAVING THE EXCEPTION FOR THE
MINING OPERATION. THIS TEXT AMMENDMENT WILL CLEAR THAT UP.
