WHEREAS, the City Council of Kearns, Utah (respectively, the “Council” and “Kearns”)
held a public hearing on February 10, 2025 (the “Public Hearing”), to consider, among other
things, an ordinance adopting a stipend for the Kearns Planning Commissioners, adjusting
compensation for the Mayor of Kearns, and adopting compensation for a Kearns City Manager
position; and

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-3-818 requires, among other things, that Kearns publish
public notice and hold a separate public hearing on proposed compensation increases for executive
municipal officers and employees before adopting those increases; and

WHEREAS, the Council desires to attract and retain talented individuals and understands
that fair and marketable compensation is required to do so; and

WHEREAS, the Council intends to: (1) approve a stipend in the amount of $ Jo per
meeting attended by the Planning Commissioners; (2) adjust the annual salary for the Mayor to
$10,000- ; and (3) adopt an annual salary for the City Manager of $18,000 _; and

WHEREAS, on February 3, 2025, the Council posted notice of the Public Hearing pursuant
to Utah Code § 10-3-818(2)(b); and

WHEREAS, proper noticing has been given and public comment has been taken.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:
