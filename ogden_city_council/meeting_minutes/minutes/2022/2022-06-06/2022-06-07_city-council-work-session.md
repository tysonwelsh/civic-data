Fiscal Year 2022 Third Quarter Financial Report

Comptroller Stout summarized the Fiscal Year (FY) 2022 third quarter financial report; this report summarizes the City’s
budgetary expenditures, revenues, and general financial information as of March 31, 2022. Revenues are projected to exceed
budget by more than $3.25 million. She provided charts illustrating the third quarter expenditures by fund, the breakdown of the
City’s revenue sources, and tax revenue detail in the general fund. She engaged in discussion with the Council as they sought
information regarding specific details for individual funds to determine if revenues and expenses are on target when considering
the total fiscal year budget.

Fiscal Year 2022-2023 City Budget

General Fiscal Year 2023-2027 Capital Improvement Plan Discussion: Council Deputy Director Symes stated that the
intent of this agenda item was to give the Council time to provide feedback regarding Administration’s proposed Fiscal Year (FY)
2023-2027 Capital Improvement Plan (CIP); he facilitated discussion among the Council to solicit that feedback. There was
discussion among the Council, Council staff, and Administrative staff regarding project overruns and the potential need to identify
additional funding for each project to ensure they can be completed within budget; opportunities to order supplies/materials now
to avoid increased costs or inflation at a later date.

General Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Budget Discussion: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith stated she has heard some
feedback from a few Council members regarding their desired changes to the budget; she facilitated discussion among the entire
Council to determine if there is consensus for various budget amendments or additions that have been recommended; there was
brief discussion about staffing at the Ogden Airport; hangar costs at the Airport; Recreation staffing levels; reducing salary and
benefits allocations for each Department to account for unspent money when a position is vacant. Specific to the matter of
reducing salary and benefits allocations for each Department, Chief Administrative Officer Johnson indicated Administration
understands the concerns that the Council has regarding providing funding that may not be needed in the fiscal year but asked that
the budget requests be approved with the understanding that Administration will come to the Council ifany Department desires to
utilize unspent compensation funding for another purpose in their budget. There was discussion among the Council and
Administration regarding the topic, with brief focus on the potential for other cities to increase their employee compensation,
which may impact the City’s employment pool and ability to retain current employees. The Council ultimately concluded to
provide funding adequate to compensate all currently created positions in each Department of the City.

Chair Nadolski called for a recess at 5:20 p.m.
The meeting reconvened at 5:30 p.m.

Council Business (cont,)

Proposed Amendments to Elected Official Salary Ordinances: Executive Director Eller-Smith presented the Council
with a draft ordinance intended to amend the current elected official salary provisions in the City Code; she summarized the
current compensation provisions, which indicate that the Council and Mayor will receive any cost-of-living adjustment (COLA)
provided to regular employees. The amendments will provide the Council the ability to adopt a contrary salary ordinance to allow
for variation from the compensation adjustments provided to all employees. She noted Council staff has separated all salary
schedules for the purposes of taking independent action on the regular employees’ salary schedules, the City Council salary
schedule, and the Mayor’s salary schedule. The Council thanked Ms. Eller-Smith for her work on this matter.
