 VARIANCES
 B-3-2020                     MR. LANCE WALKER, representing Granger Medical Clinic, is requesting a variance from
                              the West Valley City Code. The variance request is from Section 7-6-408(4)(b) which
                              requires that the area limitation for wall signs on side or rear facades be 5% of the first story
                              building face or the side or rear façade in the City Center Zone. Mr. Walker is requesting a
                              variance to allow one wall sign to be 10% on the west façade of the new Granger Medical
                              Clinic. This property is located at 2965 West 3500 South on 3.79 acers and is zoned CC (City
                              Center). (Staff - Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311)
