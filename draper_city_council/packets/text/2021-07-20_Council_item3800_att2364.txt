                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-39

       A RESOLUTION APPOINTING JAN SHORT, ANNEKE CANNON, AND STEPHANIE
       SHERRELL AS REGULAR MEMBERS OF THE DRAPER CITY SPECIAL EVENT
       ARENA COMMITTEE

     WHEREAS, the City is authorized to establish subordinate bodies with the city as
deemed appropriate by the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee are
appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

        WHEREAS, Jan Short, Anneke Cannon, and Stephanie Sherrell have expressed a
willingness to serve as members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor recommended Jan Short, Anneke Cannon, and Stephanie
Sherrell to serve as members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        The City Council hereby provides its advice and consent to the Mayor’s appointment
of Jan Short to serve as a member of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee.

       Section 1. Appointment. Jan Short, Anneke Cannon, and Stephanie Sherrell are
hereby appointed to serve as members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee
according to the laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the Draper City Special Event
Arena Committee and its members for the term specified herein.

       Regular Member:
       Jan Short                           July 20, 2021 through July 31, 2025
       Anneke Cannon                       July 20, 2021 through July 31, 2025
       Stephanie Sherrell                  July 20, 2021 through July 31, 2025

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be
severable.

        Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
its passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THIS THE 20TH DAY OF JULY 2021.
