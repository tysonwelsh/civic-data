As the Public Works team, we would like to nominate Travis Taylor as the employee of the month. Travis
is the type of employee that everyone wants to have on their team. He is approachable, friendly,
knowledgeable, willing to help and always there when he is needed. He is generally just an all-around
great individual. Travis came to the County about 7 years ago as an Engineer Tech I over traffic signs. He
demonstrated his willingness to get in and get dirty by taking ownership of the signs and the shape that
they were in as reflection of him as an employee. He very quickly became the resource on the appropriate
installation and sign usage for both street signs and trail signs. Travis’s ability to quickly learn and
master signs helped him as he decided to increase his knowledge by furthering his education. In order to
meet the demands of his job and still be efficient, he attended classes after hours. He took the information
he obtained at school and applied it to his job here at the County. Over the course of 3 or 4 years due to
his diligent efforts and increased knowledge, Travis had the opportunity to advance in the Engineering
Division and eventually became the Engineering Technician Supervisor. He is one of those employees that
isn’t OK just doing a mediocre job but seeks for perfection in his duties and strives to assist and motivate
others. Travis took his knowledge and his desire to serve others and brought the County from the archaic
age to a modern digital age by diligently working to get permits digitized and available online for the
public’s convenience. Travis is excellent when dealing with the public. He treats them respectfully and
honestly while striving to meet their needs and still keeping the county’s best interest at heart. Often
individuals come frustrated with what they call the county processes and by the time they finish with
Travis, the situation is diffused, and they leave with an action plan and a smile on their face. Travis also
took his skills and his desire to make things more efficient and better by assisting roads in developing a
tracking system to provide a better means of determining actual costs for projects. Most recently one of
the highlights that Travis completed for Public Works was that he developed a video to illustrate the
accomplishments of the team that was presented as part of Public Works Week. If you have seen the
video, you can honestly say a picture is worth a thousand words. Not only is Travis good with the office
stuff but when time calls for getting the job done he is not afraid to throw the boots on, climb in a stream,
remove a log jam, fill and place sand bags, and get dirty. The only hesitation we have in nominating
Travis Taylor as employee of the month is that we don’t want other departments to try and poach him
from us.

TRAVIS TAYLOR – STATED THAT HE WAS VERY SURPRISED.
