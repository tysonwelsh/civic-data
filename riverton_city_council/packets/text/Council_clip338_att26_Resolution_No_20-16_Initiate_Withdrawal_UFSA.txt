                                    RIVERTON CITY
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-16

A RESOLUTION TO INITIATE THE PROCESS BY WHICH THE RIVERTON CITY
COUNCIL OR VOTERS OF RIVERTON CITY MAY CONSIDER WITHDRAWAL OF
 THE MUNICIPAL TERRITORY OF RIVERTON CITY FROM THE UNIFIED FIRE
                          SERVICE AREA


        WHEREAS, Riverton City obtains fire protection, paramedic, and emergency response
services (known as “First Responder Services”) from Unified Fire Authority, under the terms of
an Interlocal Agreement executed by and between Riverton City, Salt Lake County, and several
municipalities within Salt Lake County; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City pays for the First Responder Services it receives from the
Unified Fire Authority through its membership in the Unified Fire Service Area (“UFSA”),
which it became a part of by UFSA’s adoption of Resolution No. 5 on December 30, 2005; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City residents and property owners pay the UFSA for the services
rendered by the Unified Fire Authority through the levy of ad valorem a property taxes upon
owners of property within the City, which is levied by UFSA; and

        WHEREAS, by comparing the operating budget expenses of the Unified Fire Authority
in Riverton with the revenue generated by ad valorem property taxes levied within Riverton City
for Firs Responder Services, the Riverton City Council is concerned that tax revenues generated
within the City may be subsidizing the budget of the Unified Fire Authority to provide First
Responder Services outside of the territorial jurisdiction of Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, the prospect of the UFSA incurring bonded indebtedness for the
construction of facilities outside of Riverton City, which would be repaid in part by the ad
valorem property taxes generated within Riverton City, adds to the concern that Riverton tax
revenues may subsidize the cost of Providing First Responder Services outside the territorial
jurisdiction of Riverton City; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that, owing to stated
concerns regarding the imbalance of revenue generated in Riverton City against the expense of
the Unified Fire Authority to provide First Responder Services to Riverton, the voters of
Riverton City should be asked to determine whether or not the City should remain within the
boundaries of SLVLESA; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §17B-1-505, the UFSA is a “First Responder
District” for the purposes of understanding procedures required to withdraw from the UFSA; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §17B-1-505 (3)(a) states, “The process to withdraw a
municipality from a first responder district or municipal services district may be initiated by a
resolution adopted by the legislative body of the municipality;” and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §17B-1-505 (5)(a) states, “A municipality shall be
withdrawn from a first responder district if: (i) the legislative body of the municipality adopts a
resolution initiating the withdrawal under Subsection (3)(a); and (ii) (A) whether before or after
the effective date of this section, the municipality and first responder district agree in writing to
the withdrawal; or (B) except as provided in Subsection (5) (b) and subject to Subsection (6), the
voters of the municipality approve the withdrawal at an election held for that purpose;” and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §17B-1-505 (6) states, “An election under Subsection
(5)(a)(ii)(B) may not be held unless: (a) a feasibility study is conducted under Section 17B-1-
505.5; and (b) (i) the feasibility study concludes that the withdrawal is functionally and
financially feasible for the municipality and the first responder district; or (ii) (A) the feasibility
study concludes that the withdrawal would be functionally and financially feasible for the
municipality and the first responder district if conditions specified in the feasibility study are met;
and (B) the legislative body of the municipality adopts a resolution irrevocably committing the
municipality to satisfying the conditions specified in the feasibility study, if the withdrawal is
approved by the municipality’s voters;” and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that to verify whether ad
valorem property taxes generated within Riverton are subsidizing First Responder Services in
communities outside Riverton, it: 1) shall adopt a resolution initiating withdrawal; and 2) likely
shall undertake a feasibility study required under Utah Code §17B-1-505; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has considered this resolution in a public
meeting, following public notice required by law,


      NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF RIVERTON
CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby initiates the withdrawal of the municipal territory of
          Riverton City from the Unified Fire Service Area. The Mayor is authorized and directed
          to submit written notice of the adoption of this resolution, accompanied by a copy of the
          resolution, to the Unified Fire Service Area within 10 days following adoption of the
          same.

       2. The Mayor, with the participation of the Riverton City Board Representative of the
          Unified Fire Service Area, is authorized to undertake any and all steps necessary to
          obtain a feasibility study; unless the Mayor and the Unified Fire Service Area are able to
          agree in writing to withdrawal without a feasibility study. If an agreement in writing
          cannot be obtained between Riverton City and the Unified Fire Service Area to complete
          withdrawal from the Unified Fire Service Area, all further action undertaken by Riverton
          City to complete withdrawal shall be contingent upon the Riverton City Council’s
          approval of a completed feasibility study.

      3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
