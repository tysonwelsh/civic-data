Mayor Justin Seely opened the regular council meeting and welcomed all present.

PRAYER: Mayor Justin Seely

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
   Dwayne Horton, a therapist at Central Valley Medical Center led the pledge of allegiance and then
spoke about mental health issues. He said there is a shortage of therapist. He emphasized the fact that
people need to have hope. He stated that all ages of people are suffering from mental health problems.
He said he has been hired by CVMC as a full-time therapist and people can get an appointment with him
by calling CVMC.


CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting 4-19-22, work session held on 4-26-22, a list of claims on the warrant
register dated 5-3-22 and the sale of burial rights to Kevin Broadhead. Councilor Parady seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

  Mr. Joel Cowan commented that from the discussion at the last meeting about setting height limits
for buildings in the revised Industrial Zone, he and the fire department had run some drills with the
ladder truck to see the height it could reach when fully extended. He said that 55 feet is the most
desired height for the buildings with the collapse zone figured in.

RACCOON EYE ANNEXATION PETITION ACCEPTED:

      City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed the Raccoon Eyes Annexation petition with the council.
He reminded them that an earlier petition for the same property had been rejected by the City Recorder
because of some boundary line issues. Mr. Joe Humphrey, the petitioner, had since resolved the
property boundary issues and is now re-petitioning for annexation. The property is approximately one
half of a mile west of Main Street on the north side of Highway 132. Councilor Worwood moved to
accept the Raccoon Eyes Annexation petition and allow the annexation process to continue. Councilor
Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BLAKE AND GULLEY PRELIMINARY/FINAL PLAT AND DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that when the Powell Park subdivision was approved, it was
anticipated that some of the homeowners next to the Powell Park subdivision would merge their lot
with the Red Cliffs Estates with the adjacent Powell Park lots. This is the case for the Blake and Gulley
                                                                                                 Page 1 of 3
Subdivision. In this case, the homeowners would like to vacate three lots from the Powell Park
Subdivision, merge the three lots into two and divide the two lots between two homeowners in the Red
Cliffs Subdivision. They would also then like to merge the two lots from the Powell Park Subdivision into
the lots from the Red Cliffs Estates Subdivision.
    To accomplish this purpose, first the three lots in the Powell Park Subdivision must be vacated, as
well as two lots in the Red Cliffs Estates Subdivision. Once this is accomplished, the Blake and Gulley
Fields Subdivision can occur. He stated that this is a laborious administrative process but needed to be
done through a development agreement and a re-subdivision.
 Councilor Parady moved to approve the preliminary and final plats for the Blake and Gulley re-
subdivision and to authorize the Mayor to sign the development agreement. Councilor Memmott
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

UMPA AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that Utah Municipal Power Agency Board had approved the
proposal of UMPA providing a cash security deposit to PacifiCorp for network upgrade costs. The
deposit is for $6,540,000 which PacifiCorp will place in an interest-bearing account. The project will
bring a second point of delivery from PacfiCorp, which will allow Nephi to meet the electrical demands
caused by growth in the City.
  Councilor Memmott moved to accept the UMPA agreement with all exhibits. Councilor Worwood
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

REIMBURSEMENT RESOLUTION 05-03-2022 ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson explained that the city may need to pay approximately $790,000 to PacifiCorp toward a
redundant southern connection fairly soon after UMPA supplies the security deposit on our electrical
line project. This payment will occur well before the city is able to secure bond financing for the
project. For the city to be potentially reimbursed for these costs, the city needed to pass a
reimbursement resolution. The resolution would enable the city to use future bond proceeds for
project related expenses that occur prior to obtaining the bond proceeds.
   Councilor Callaway moved to adopt the Reimbursement Resolution 05-03-2022. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATES:

  Mr. Atkinson reminded the council that there would not be a work session next Tuesday, but the
council would be attending a Regional Summit in Richfield hosted by the Six County Association of
Governments.
  City Recorder Lisa Brough reported that open enrollment meetings would be held with each
department next week and that she would be involved in a Workers Compensation audit on Tuesday of
next week.
  City Attorney Kasey Wright updated the council on the status of the Hive agreement.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Councilor Callaway reported on the JRDA meeting. He stated that the scale house is operational.
JRDA is in the process of hiring an operator for the scale house and the job opening closes on Friday.
The tentative day for weighing loads is June 1, 2022.
  Mayor Seely said that he has had a report of parties being held in many different areas of the city
which is creating the problem of vehicles blocking people from their driveways.
  Councilor Parady informed the council that the Chamber of Commerce will be holding a lunch at the
Senior Citizen Center on Thursday. The candidates for County Commission will be the speakers.
  Councilor Ostler said he had heard complaints about people blocking driveways at the soccer fields.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Memmott moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session to
discuss the character and competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Regular council meeting adjourned at 7:50 p.m.

          Work Session convened at 8:05 p.m.

    1.    The council discussed the condition of the road by the Maverik construction project. Public
