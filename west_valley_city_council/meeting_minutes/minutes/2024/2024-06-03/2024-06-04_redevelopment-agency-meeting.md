      A.     ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING THE FY 2024-2025 BUDGET
             Chairman Nordfelt informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
             Redevelopment Agency Meeting scheduled June 4, 2024, in order for the Board
             to hear and consider public comments regarding the FY 2024-2025 Budget.

             Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
             information as follows:

                    This resolution adopts the final budget for the Redevelopment Agency of
                    West Valley City for the 2024-2025 fiscal year after receiving public
                    comment at a hearing on June 4, 2024.

                    The Redevelopment Agency adopted a tentative budget that was made
                    available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s
                    Recorder’s office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment,
                    before adopting this final budget for FY 2024-2025.

             Chairman Nordfelt opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
             or opposition, Chairman Nordfelt closed the Public Hearing.

             ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 23-08, ADOPT THE ANNUAL
             BUDGET OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
             FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1, 2024 AND ENDING
             JUNE 30, 2025
             The Redevelopment Agency previously held a public hearing regarding proposed
             Resolution 23-08 that would adopt the annual budget of the West Valley City
             Redevelopment Agency for the fiscal year commencing July 1, 2024 and ending
             June 30, 2025.

      Upon inquiry by Chairman Nordfelt there were no further questions from members of the
      Authority, and he called for a motion.

             Mr. Whetstone moved to approve Resolution 24-08.

             Mr. Fitisemanu seconded the motion.

             A roll call vote was taken:
