Care Allocation Discussion: Presenters: Brandon Nelson, Finance Director &
Trevor Bell, Budget Manager

Mr. Nelson reported on the CARE meetings for 2023. This year there were 32 applicants
that applied for the CARE tax. The total amount asked for is $1,174,175 the available budget is
$1,177,333. Discussions were held about how the organizations were chosen and the amount of
money each business was awarded. Mr Spencer and Mr Lambson serve on the committee and
explained the process.

‘ity Social Media Sites: Presenter: Steve Earl,

Proposed Polic
City Attorney

Mr. Earl said this policy is to direct the type of flags that would be appropriate to be
flown on City-owned flag poles. The list includes the United States Flag, the Armed Forces
Flags, the POW/MIA Flag, the State Flag, the Ceremonial State Flag, the UVU Flag and the
Orem City Flag. The City shall have exclusive control over which of the said flags are displayed
on any City Flagpole at any given time. This policy will include banners as well as flags. The
City Council recommended adding Oremfest banners to the policy. Mr. Adam Robertson, from
SCERA, asked about how an art district banner might work in the policy. Mr. Earl explained that
these banners could be added by resolution. Discussions were held about an arts addition to the

policy.

Mr. Earl moved on to the policy concerning social! media accounts. The policy would
limit the logo and emblems used in city social media. The policy states that the profile picture
should be limited to the approved City logos and emblems. No logos, emblems, or flags may be
displayed as part of the City’s profile picture on any City Social Media Account other than the
Orem City Flag, the City Logo, the City Seal, the United States Flag, the State Flag, the
Ceremonial State Flag, the Armed Forces Flags, the POW/MIA Flag, the UVU Flag, the official
Orem Police and Fire Department badges and other Orem City logos and badges approved in the
Orem City Brand Usage and Style Guide approved by the City Council. This provision is limited
to the profile picture on Orem Social Media Accounts and does not apply to posts made by
Covered Persons on City Social Media Accounts (as opposed to the profile picture display) or to
comments made by individuals in connection with any such posts. This policy does not apply to
posts, it is specific to the profile picture. This helps protect the Orem City brand.

Mr. Earl will bring this resolution to the next city council meeting on May 16, 2023. The
council members were supportive of the resolution.

Orem Logo at Intersections: Presenter: Taylor Forbush, Traffic Operations Section Manager
and Tyler Peay, Public Service Division Manager

Mr. Peay introduced a new possible branding effort for the City of Orem. Mr. Forbush
discussed the thermoplastic branding that can be installed in intersections. The product is melted
down into the road and will last about 5 years. UVU has some thermoplastic branding currently
in Orem. Five designs were shown and the size and amount of colors will determine the cost. A
20 ft X 20 ft size with 2 colors would cost about $5,300. They are proposing 3 intersections
State & 800 N, State & Center St. and State and University Pkwy.

Rental Dwelling Licensing: Presenter: Ryan Clark, Acting Assistant City Manager/Development
Services Director
Mr. Clark has written up the Rental Dwelling Code for Rental Dwelling Licensing at the

recommendation of the City Council. 12-9-6 License Fee-The proposed change is that the owner
of a house with a legal Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) is required to pay the licensing fee for
the initial license application and is not required to pay the licensing fee on an ongoing basis. If
a house with a legal ADU is sold to a new owner, the new owner is required to apply for a new
license and pay the initial application fee. This ordinance change will be presented to the City
Council on May 16, 2023.

Business Safety & Neighborhood Refresh Grant: Presenters: Jared Weech, COP Detective, Kathi
Lewis, Economic Development Division Manager, and Kena Mathews, Community Service
Manager

Mr. Weech is here with Ms. Lewis and Ms. Mathews to talk about the Business Safety
and Security Grant. The Neighborhood In Action had funds left over and this group is proposing
using these funds to give grants to businesses for security. The program will allow businesses to
improve the safety and security of Orem businesses.

The Neighborhood Refresh Grant Program will be available for Orem residents for
improvements at their homes and would be allotted $20,000. The Business Safety and Security
Grant Program would be allotted $40,000 for Orem businesses to use for security upgrades.

The Orem Neighborhood Commission recommended that the management and leadership
fund the new grant programs with the outstanding matching grant program funding. No CDBG
funds will be used for the Business Safety and Security Grant Program. Many benefits were
presented for these programs.

Possible future program funding could be matching grants, utilize volunteer support to
help make project dollars go further, research possible supplemental grant funding, and leverage
CDBG funding and other federal and state dollars and programs whenever possible.

The council members had questions and discussions about the two programs and how
they will be implemented and communicated. Updates on the program have been requested by
the city council.

Public Hearing/Resolution: Adopting the City of Orem Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Tentative
Budget Presenter: Brandon Nelson, CIP, Finance Director

Mr. Nelson is presenting the Tentative Budget today for Fiscal Year 2023-2024. The
proposed budget will total $152,344,861. The budget has been reviewed on multiple occasions.
The highlights are:

* Projected FY 2023-2024 revenues increased $11,724,998 over FY 2022-2023
budgeted revenues, primarily due to sales tax, property tax, and interest earnings
revenues.

¢ Funded the city’s compensation program including step adjustments and merit
increases for all other benefited employees.

¢ Funded market adjustments for employees based on a market analysis completed
by the city’s HR division.

* Health Insurance increase at 8%

Fleet Replacement totaled $2,939,000

* Capital Projects totaled $14,094,298

* Created new Development Fees Fund

Our budget over the past 4 years has grown from $125,326,548 in FY 2021 to the current
$152,344,861. Mr. Nelson went over the revenues to Orem City including sales tax, property tax
and franchise tax. In answer to Ms. Lauret’s question, if the revenues are lower than expected
there are 3 ways to adjust for this. First, evaluate other revenues sources, are they doing better
than expected. Second, there is contingency money in the budget . Third, we would use the
surplus money.

Mr. Nelson continued on to City-Wide Expenditures. This included personnel, operations
and capital. The additions to the general fund were also presented by Mr. Nelson. He also went
over the expenditures in the fleet and street departments. Mr. Tschirki reported that 950 North
street reconstruction will need to wait for utilities installed but issues will be addressed as
needed. Mr. Ryan reported on 400 West in the 1600 N area. This project is funded and at design.
The project should start this fall. Mr. Nelson continued to explain debt services funds, internal
service funds, and the water fund. In a comparison of water fees, Orem City is $47.82. The
average is $74.43. In a comparison of sewer fees, Orem City is at $35.79 and the average is
$42.70. In a comparison of storm water fees, Orem City is at $8.20 and the average is at $8.48.

Mr. Nelson spoke on the recreation fund and that the new facility is covering their own
cost. Solid Waste through Waste Management will have a minimal increase in rates from $12.55
to $12.95 for trash and from $4.40 to $4.65 for recycling. In a comparison of solid waste fee,
Orem City is $17.60 and the average is $18.29. In total utilities, excluding gas and electric,
Orem is at $109.41 and the average is at $143.90. Mr. Earl informed the group that a date needs
to be set for the final budget as part of adopting the tentative budget.
