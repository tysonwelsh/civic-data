                               ORDINANCE 2023-06-01

              Ordinance No. 2023-06-01                  Date: June 27, 2023

     AN ORDINANCE OF THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP
      COUNCIL AMENDING SECTION 19.73.110 PROVISIONS ON NIGHT
                            LIGHTING


                                       RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District provides services to the
five Metro Townships in the Salt Lake Valley, unincorporated areas, and the Town of
Brighton; and
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township adopted each county ordinance in
effect at the time of its incorporation pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-2a-414(3)
and has authority to amend or repeal the county ordinance when it determines it is
necessary; and
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township is a municipality and has authority
to regulate Night Lighting in general pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-3c-103
(2);
WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township has authority to adopt amendments to
ordinance language controlling night lighting pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-501 in
accordance with the Municipal Land Use, Development, and Management Act,
(“MLUDMA”), Title 10, Section 9a, Utah Code, to encourage lighting practices that
minimize light pollution, glare, light trespass, and skyglow to preserve and enhance views
of the night sky; and
WHEREAS, the Council deems it necessary to amend its ordinances in order to maintain
the rural atmosphere and community charter of the metro township; protect ecological
conditions and nocturnal wildlife within the Metro Township; Maintain nighttime safety,
utility, and security; encourage lighting practices that promote energy conservation; and
for the protection and preservation of the public health, safety, and general welfare.
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE Emigration Canyon METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
as follows:
1.     Section 19.73.110 is repealed and replaced to read as follows:
 [LAND USE ORDINANCE]: The following Section 19.73.110 is hereby repealed and
 replaced in its entirety with the revised Section 19.73.110 attached hereto as Exhibit A |
 Land Use Amendment Resolution | 19.73.110 Night Lighting.
 2.      Severability. If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any part of this
 Ordinance is unconstitutional or invalid, then such portion of this Ordinance, or specific
 application of this Ordinance, shall be severed from the remainder, which shall continue in
 full force and effect.
 3.     Effective Date. This Ordinance will take effect immediately upon posting and
 publication as required by law.
 PASSED AND ADOPTED this 27th day of June 2023.

EXHIBIT A
                                 SUMMARY OF
                     Emigration Canyon METRO TOWNSHIP
                            Ordinance No. 2023-06-01


 On [Date], the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council enacted Ordinance No.
 2023-06-01, adopting Section 19.73.110 Night Lighting, and repealed Section
 19.73.110 Night Lighting the prior ordinance of the Salt Lake County Council
 adopted for Emigration Canyon in 2012, prior to Emigration Canyon’s
 incorporation as a municipality in 2017. This complies with general guidance and
 Work Program elements of the general plan. The intent of the ordinance is: To
 encourage lighting practices that minimize light pollution, glare, light trespass,
 and sky glow to preserve and enhance views of the night sky. To maintain the
 rural atmosphere and community character of the Metro Township. To protect
 ecological conditions and nocturnal wildlife within the Metro Township. To
 maintain nighttime safety, utility, and security. And to encourage lighting
 practices that promote energy conservation.
