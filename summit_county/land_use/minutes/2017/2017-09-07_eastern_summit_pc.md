1. Discussion regarding a possible Village Overlay Zone. – Planning Staff   
 
 
Director Putt stated when the Planning Commission forwarded a recommendation for 
Chapter 3: The Zoning Districts, there were two zones that were set aside to spend more 
time with. One was the RC Zone and the other was the Village Overlay Zone. The County

necessary adjustments could be made and brought to the Planning Commission at that time 
to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council for the Village Overlay Zone. 
An Overlay Zone is a specific set of process, standards or regulations that overlay the 
existing zoning that is in place in a particular area at a given time. The overlay is done to 
achieve specific purposes. This village overlay would create the potential to look at some of 
the unincorporated communities and find a way to incentivize necessary redevelopment or 
growth opportunities that address a mix of uses, including business activity and economic 
development, while addressing infrastructure. 
The first step would be a decision by the County Council to establish a planning boundary 
around an unincorporated area. The boundary area could be nominated by the County 
Council, the Planning Commission, or at the request of property owners. A specific land use 
plan would then be studied, reviewed and ultimately adopted to result in a rezoning of that 
study area. 
Some parameters this rezoning would allow for are a potential increase in residential 
density (up to five units per acre), a mix of single-family and multi-family structures, allow 
for mixed uses of residential, commercial, office and civic uses. The existing zones are 
single-use zones. 
There should be a discussion about limiting the Village Overlay to the historic village areas. 
There is an existing option for the other areas with the Resort SPA (Specially Planned Area) 
process.
