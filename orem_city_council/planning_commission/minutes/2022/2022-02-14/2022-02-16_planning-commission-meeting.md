Chair Sakar asked Ms. Stevens to introduce Agenda Item 3.1: (click here for recording)

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Kenzy Fogle for the Planning Commission to vacate Lot 7 of Chelsea Subdivision
and approve Chelsea, Plat B located at 1920 South 50 West in the R8 zone.

                                        Staff Presentation: The applicant is seeking to vacate the existing public
                                        utility easement which runs along the south boundary of the property so
                                        she can build a six foot high CMU fence along the property line. That
                                        would not be possible unless the easement is vacated. There will be no
                                        changes to the stormwater drainage, landscaping, exteriors of buildings,
                                        traffic, etc. The applicant has received letters from each of the five utilities
                                        vacating the easement.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City

located at 1920 South 50 West in the R8 zone.

Chair Sakar opened the public hearing and invited those from the audience who had come to speak to this item to

Madeline Komen, Murray Low, Amber Pope, Barry Roberts and Haysam Sakar. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Sakar asked Ms. Stevens to introduce Agenda Item 3.2: (click here for recording)

Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Bracken Atkinson for the Planning Commission to approve the overall preliminary
plat and site plan of Orem Technology Center Buildings 6 and 7, including Orem Technology Campus, Plat B
located generally at 1340 N 1380 W in the PD36 zone, with the condition the developer will coordinate with Utah
Department of Transportation for the design and construction of a traffic signal at 1200 N Geneva Road in
connection with this project, and a bond for the completion of the traffic signal prior to issuing a building permit.

                                       Staff Presentation: Orem Technology Center currently has four buildings
                                       used as office and warehouse space. The applicant is proposing to add two
                                       buildings to the campus. This will be the final phase of development for the
                                       Orem Technology Center.

                                       Architecture:
                                       Both the buildings are two-story structures that will contain office warehouse
                                       space. Building 6 will contain 136,100 square feet with approximately 18,000
square feet being used as office space. Building 7 will contain 89,423 square feet with approximately 8,750 square
feet being used as office space. The number of units will vary depending on how much space each tenant wants. For
example, the existing buildings contain a variety of the number of tenants and space that is leased. The proposed
finishing materials will match the existing buildings and include bronze framing, wood stained concrete, and four
different colors of painted concrete as shown on the elevations. Each building will be two stories in height with an
overall height of 37 feet. The PD-36 zone allows an overall height of 125 feet.

Parking: The project requires 334 parking stalls based on the following requirements:

Building 6:
18,000 square feet (sf) of office at 1 stall/250 sf requires 72 stalls
117,450 sf of warehouse at 1 stall/750 sf for the first 50,000, 1/1,000 sf for the second 50,000, and 1/1,500 sf for any
additional space requires 129 stalls
Building 1 requires a total of 203 stalls

Building 7:
8,750 sf of office at 1 stall/250 sf requires 35 stalls
80,091 sf of warehouse at 1 stall/750 sf for the first 50,000 and 1/1,000 sf for the second 50,000 requires 98 stalls
Building 2 requires a total of 133 stalls

Added together, this comes to a total of 334 required parking stalls. The site plan contains 336 spaces.

Landscaping: The landscaping plan will contain the same plants and materials as the rest of the site. The overall
landscape plan complies with applicable City Codes.

Transportation/Engineering: The proposed buildings will generate enough traffic to warrant a traffic signal at 1200
N Geneva Road. There is a condition of approval which the developer must coordinate with Utah Department of
Transportation for the design and construction of a traffic signal. A bond for the completion of the traffic signal
must also be posted before a building permit will be issued.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the overall preliminary plat and site plan of Orem
Technology Center Buildings 6 and 7, including Orem Technology Campus, Plat B located generally at 1340 N
1380 W in the PD36 zone, with the condition the developer will coordinate with Utah Department of Transportation
for the design and construction of a traffic signal at 1200 N Geneva Road in connection with this project, and a bond
for the completion of the traffic signal prior to issuing a building permit, and pay for the installation of the traffic
signal.

Chair Sakar asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Stevens. Mr. Low asked if something
changed due to further conversations, would this item come back to the Planning Commission for reapproval. Mr.
Bench said if they meet the conditions of the approval it would not come back again. If there were a dispute they
would need to come back to modify the condition.

Chair Sakar invited the applicant to come forward. Bracken Atkinson introduced himself. He asked for clarification
on how the traffic signal would be bonded as there is a big unknown with UDOT. Mr. Kelly said they would discuss
that after the approval on the staff level.

Chair Sakar opened the public meeting and invited those from the audience who had come to speak to this item to
come forward to the microphone.

When no one came forward, Chair Sakar closed the public meeting and asked if the Planning Commission had any
more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did, he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Sakar moved to approve the overall preliminary plat and site plan of Orem
Technology Center Buildings 6 and 7, including Orem Technology Campus, Plat B located generally at 1340 N
1380 W in the PD36 zone, with the condition the developer will coordinate with Utah Department of Transportation
for the design and construction of a traffic signal at 1200 N Geneva Road in connection with this project, and a bond
for the completion of the traffic signal prior to issuing a building permit, and pay for the installation of the traffic
signal. Mr. Roberts seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Jim Condie, Madeline Komen, Murray Low, Amber
Pope, Barry Roberts and Haysam Sakar. The motion passed unanimously.

Minutes: The Planning Commission reviewed the minutes from the previous meeting. Chair Sakar then called for a
motion to approve the minutes of February 2, 2022. Mr. Low moved to approve the meeting minutes for February 2,
2022. Ms. Komen seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Jim Condie, Madeline Komen, Murray Low, Amber
Pope, Barry Roberts and Haysam Sakar. The motion passed unanimously.

Adjourn: Chair Sakar called for a motion and moved to adjourn. Mr. Roberts seconded the motion. Those voting
aye: Jim Condie, Madeline Komen, Murray Low, Amber Pope, Barry Roberts and Haysam Sakar. The motion
passed unanimously.
