2.
Project #: 23-027. Administrative Process: Public hearing and action regarding a Plat Amendment application to modify an existing building pad, 7068 Canyon Drive, Summit County, Utah; Parcel PB-PR-110 (1.27 acres). Applicants: Jeff and Elana Greer. Madlyn McDonough, County Planner. 6:03 P.M.
Madlyn McDonough,
County Planner,
presented a Plat Amendment proposal to modify an existing building pad in the Pineridge Subdivision. Commissioner Hubler asked a brief clarifying question to which Planner McDonough, Elana Greer,
Applicant
and Alex Arie,
Architect,
responded.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:13 P.M.
Scott Scofield spoke in favor of the proposal at 6:13 P.M.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:15 P.M.
Commissioners asked more in depth questions. Architect, Alex Arie, addressed the questions and concerns raised by Commissioners.
DJ Hubler made a motion to approve the Plat Amendment pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in the staff report. John Kucera seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Christopher Conabee, Thomas Cooke.
