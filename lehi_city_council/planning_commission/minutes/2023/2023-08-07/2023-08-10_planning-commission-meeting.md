Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commissioner Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Conner Atkin’s request for conditional use
approval of a 70-foot flagpole for the Tagg-N-Go located at 3482 West 1940 North.

Applicant was not present during his item. He arrived shortly after item 2.3 and asked if there
was any way to move the flagpole to the east side. The commissioners had informed him that is
was approved for the opposite and that they couldn’t change it.

Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Conner Atkin’s request for conditional use
approval of a 70-foot flagpole for the Tagg-N-Go located at 3482 West 1940 North.;
including the DRC comments, and the findings that the proposed concept is consistent with Lehi
City development code and that the proposed concept does conform the goals and policies of the
Lehi General Plan. Commissioner Bunker seconded.


Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Jason Boal’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Table 05-030-B, allowing limited drive through facilities
in the Neighborhood Commercial Zoning District.
Applicant Jason Boal was present he mentioned that they met with DRC and felt that they have
made some good modifications with the concerns that were stated at DRC.

Chairman Newall opened the public hearing.

Staff read over the three comments that were sent through the Engage Lehi and Granicus.

Chairman Newall closed the public hearing.

The Commissioners discussed some of their thoughts with the use and changing code.

Mr. Boal felt that this suggestion or route was the one that was preferred by the neighbors, but
they understand the concerns about being comfortable with the approach. He explained what
they did to make this an appropriate proposal that meets the needs of the neighbors and is
supported by the neighbors.

Glen Goings, property developer-gave some context to why they chose to do it this way.

Commissioners discussed what was reasonable to recommend to city council and if a
development code was needed.

Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Jason Boal’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Table 05-030-B, allowing limited drive through facilities
in the Neighborhood Commercial Zoning District.; including all DRC comments and findings
stated in the report and that the proposed code amendment does conform the goals and policies
of the Lehi General Plan. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.3) Public hearing and recommendation of DR Horton’s request for review of the Skye
Area Plan Second Amendment, adding 883 acres and 300 residential units to the Area Plan
located at approximately SR-92 and Center Street.
Applicant Scott Bishop was available for questions. He passed out comments and concerns from
DRC and the adjustments.

Chris Jenson with DR Horton shared the Skye Master plan. ''

Chairman Newall opened the public comment.

Brian Patello-would like for DR Horton to give the residents ownership of property behind their
homes because there are gophers, and no one is maintaining the area.
Chairman Newall closed the public hearing.

Staff and commissioners discussed what could be done with the open area that was brought
forward by Officer Patello.

The commissioners continued to ask clarifying questions to DR Horton.

Jeff Stevenson with DR Horton clarified about the apartment properties and where they are
bringing those in.

Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to positive recommendation of DR Horton’s request
for review of the Skye Area Plan Second Amendment, adding 883 acres and 300 residential
units to the Area Plan located at approximately SR-92 and Center Street. including all DRC
comments and findings stated in the report; With the findings that the council strongly consider
facilitating the sale of the small strip/pan handle of the Bermuda triangle to the private residents
represented by Officer Patello. They recommend that the language on no restrictions be clarified
with timing restrictions on applications. They cannot landscape or sell the property until they
have water rights. The commissioners are comfortable with the discussion with the garages along
with a 20% landscape. With the changes they would be striking #2 from the DRC comments.
Commissioner Eyre seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, no;
Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed.

2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Chapter 26, Accessory Uses, changing the lot size
requirement for detached ADUs from 1/2 to 1/3 acre

Chairman Newall opened the public hearing.

Ms. Bussell read the comment from online.

Chairman Newall closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Eyre was concerned about the ½ addresses because of the post office. The staff
let them commissioners know that it was because of the emergency services so that they would
know there was another tenant in the home.

Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to table Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code Amendment to Chapter 26, Accessory Uses, changing the lot size requirement for
detached ADUs from 1/2 to 1/3 acre stating that there might need to work out some of the
items that were discussed tonight with improvements. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.


2.5) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Table 05.040.A and Table O5.030.A, adding twin homes
as a permitted use in the R-2 zone.

Chairman Newall opened the public hearing.

Chairman Newall closed the public hearing.

Commissioners asked question and discussed the item.

Commissioner Bunker motioned to extend the meeting. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request for
review of a Development Code Amendment to Table 05.040.A and Table O5.030.A, adding
twin homes as a permitted use in the R-2 zone.; including all DRC comments and findings
stated in the report; Commissioner Everett seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, no. The motion passed unanimously.

3. City Business

Mr. Struthers let them know that there is a conference that the commissioners can attend in the
fall, and he will send them more information. There was no other city business to attend to at
this meeting.

4. Adjournment

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Jackson moved to adjourn the meeting. Chairperson Newall seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 10:33 p.m.
