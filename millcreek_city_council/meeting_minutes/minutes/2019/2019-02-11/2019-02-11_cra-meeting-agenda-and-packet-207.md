REGULAR MEETING -— 7:00 p.m.

Chair Silvestrini called the meeting to order. He asked for public comment. There was none.

1.1 Discussion and Consideration of Resolution 19-01, Initiating the Process of
Adopting One or More Community Reinvestment Project Area Plans by
Designating a Survey Area Known as the Millcreek Center Survey Area;
Mike Winder, Economic Development Director
Mike Winder said in May of 2018, the Agency adopted resolutions to designate
various survey areas. He said since the boundaries have significantly changed
since the original designation, state law requires a re-designation of the survey
area known as Millcreek Center Survey Area. Winder said the new area was
smaller but did incorporate some previously missing areas. Board Member
Jackson asked how many times the map can be changed. Winder said as many
times as the Agency wants if they go through the process.

Board Member Jackson moved to approve Resolution 19-01, Adopting One More
Community Reinvestment Project Area Plan. Board Member Catten seconded. Chair
Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes. The motion passed
unanimously.
