B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-07-02 HAUC Bylaws Update 
Amelia made a motion to approve the HAUC Bylaws Update. Suzanne seconded the 
motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
Current HAUC Bylaws permit the agency to amend the current Bylaws with board approval. 
Currently HAUC Bylaws indicate that a current County Commissioner will be a member of the 
Board. The increased demands placed on the appointed Commissioner make it extremely 
difficult to consistently attend HAUC board meetings. HAUC staff recommends the 
amendment to the current Bylaws allowing staff members in specific Utah County positions be 
appointed by the County Commission as a Board member when a potential County 
Commissioner schedule is not likely to allow regular board meeting attendance.

D. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-07-04 Interfund Write-off 
Steve made a motion to approve the Interfund Write-off. Amelia seconded the motion. 
The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC purchased a 57-unit property in Orem in 2023. Twenty-one of the units in the main 
building received much needed rehabilitation. The rehab and property transition process 
lasted just over 1 year to February 2024. Due to expected loss of rent from the 21 units being 
rehabbed, the property operated at a loss.   An interfund balance was created holding various 
costs related to the rehab and other operating expenses during the transition phase. The 
property is now stabilized. The resolution approves the discharge of the full interfund balance. 
 
E. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-07-05 Boundary Line Adjustment 
Sara made a motion to approve the Boundary Line Adjustment. Amelia seconded the 
motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC Board of Commissioners approved the purchase of three parcels located in Orem. A 
survey of the parcels identified multiple boundary line gaps along the north, east, and west 
property boundaries on the county records. Other related issues were found along the western 
property boundary located in the right-of-way, all of which appear to be caused by old 
measuring techniques. This resolution allows the Executive Director to work with legal 
counsel, a surveyor, title company, and the city to sign the necessary agreements, 
amendments, and the like to address and correct such matters.
