      A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION ZT-3-2020,
           FILED BY WEST VALLEY CITY, REQUESTING A ZONE TEXT
           CHANGE TO AMEND THE RESIDENTIAL BUILDING DESIGN
           STANDARDS IN THE CC (CITY CENTER) ZONE
           Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
           Council Meeting scheduled June 16, 2020, in order for the City Council to hear and
           consider public comments regarding application ZT-3-2020, filed by West Valley
           City, requesting a Zone Text Change to amend the residential building design standards
           in the CC (City Center) Zone.

             Proposed Ordinance 20-26 related to the proposal to be considered by the City
             Council subsequent to the public hearing, was discussed as follows:

             ACTION: ORDINANCE NO. 20-26, AMEND SECTIONS 7-6-405 AND 7-11-
             402 OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO AMEND
             CERTAIN REGULATIONS APPLICABLE WITHIN THE CITY CENTER
             ZONE
             Steve Pastorik, CED, discussed proposed Ordinance 20-26 that would amend
             Sections 7-6-405 and 7-11-402 of the West Valley City Municipal Code to amend
             certain regulations applicable within the City Center Zone.

             Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
             information as follows:

                     On March 10, 2020 the City Council adopted Ordinance # 20-13 that
                     included several revisions to the City’s multi-unit residential design
                     standards found in Chapter 7-11 of the Zoning Ordinance. The City Center
                     zone also includes design standards for residential buildings that are very
                     similar to the multi-unit residential design standards. The purpose of this
                     proposed zone text change is to align the standards for residential buildings
                     in the City Center zone with the recently updated design standards for
       MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – JUNE 9, 2020

               multiple unit dwelling residential in Chapter 7-11.

       Councilmember Lang asked if this change will bring City Center Zone standards
       into exact compliance with RM zoning elsewhere in the City. Steve replied yes with
       a few exceptions. Councilmember Lang asked if amenities are the same. Steve
       replied yes.

       The City Council will consider Ordinance 20-26 at the Regular Council Meeting
       scheduled June 16, 2020 at 6:30 P.M.

RESOLUTION 20-78: AWARD A CONTRACT TO STAKER PARSONS
COMPANIES FOR THE 2020 ASPHALT OVERLAY PROJECT
Dan Johnson, Public Works, presented proposed Resolution 20-78 that would award a
Contract to Staker Parsons Companies for the 2020 Asphalt Overlay Project.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Bids were opened for the project on June 2, 2020. A total of three (3) bids were
       received. The lowest responsible bidder was Staker Parson Companies, at
       $1,192,443.

       The project consists of asphalt pavement improvement, along with miscellaneous
       pipe or pedestrian access ramp improvements on the following streets:

       Minor Street Asphalt Pavement Overlays:
       Beaver Circle
       Market Street and 3785 South
       3060 West, 3875 South and Campfire Circle
       3835 South
       Marcus Road
       Redwing Street
       Whitehall Drive

       Staker Parson Companies was the lowest responsible bidder and is qualified to
       perform the work.

Mayor Bigelow clarified that regular asphalt will be used. Dan replied yes.

The City Council will consider Resolution 20-78 at the Regular Council Meeting scheduled
June 16, 2020 at 6:30 P.M.

CONSENT AGENDA SCHEDULED FOR JUNE 16, 2020
A.  RESOLUTION 20-79: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO ENTER INTO A
    RIGHT OF WAY PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND ACCEPT A
    WARRANTY DEED AND TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION EASEMENT
    WITH AND FROM MATTHEW PHILLIPS FOR PROPERTY LOCATED
    AT 3015 WEST 3875 SOUTH
    Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-79 that would authorize the City
    to enter into a Right of Way Purchase Agreement and accept a Warranty Deed and
    Temporary Construction Easement with and from Matthew Phillips for property
    located at 3015 West 3875 South.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:

             The Matthew C. Phillips property is one of several properties affected by
             the required removal and replacement of pedestrian ramps to meet current
             ADA standards as part of the 2020 Asphalt Overlay Project. As part of this
             project several City streets will be overlaid full width. Any existing ADA
             ramps which do not comply with the current standards and regulations must
             be removed and replaced.

             The Warranty Deed and the Grant of Temporary Construction Easement
             will facilitate construction of the project. Compensation in the amount of
             $400.00 was negotiated based upon an appraisal reports prepared by Integra
             Realty Resources.

      Upon inquiry, there were no further questions or concerns expressed by members
      of the City Council.

      The City Council will consider Resolution 20-79 at the Regular Council Meeting
      scheduled June 16, 2020, at 6:30 P.M.

     Mayor Bigelow stated that there is a large crown between 4800 West and 5600

     evaluated as part of the design. Councilmember Nordfelt noted that bike lanes will

     replied that the lanes will shrink from 5 lanes to 3 lanes because there is not the
     traffic volume needed for what is currently in place.

B.   COVID-19 UPDATE

       Paul Isaac stated that there have been 1,091 cases in West Valley City, 561 of which
       are active. The City has increased from 17 to 22 cases per day on average. Paul
       noted that there has only been one City employee that has tested positive which
       indicates the precautions employees are taking are effective. Mayor Bigelow noted
       that 84119 and 84120 have higher cases, along with high cases in Logan, that were
       reported by the media today. Councilmember Huynh asked for numbers by district.
       He asked if this information is available to the public. Paul replied the numbers are
       provided by the County and are accessible to those who are interested.
       Councilmember Lang asked if the employee that tested positive was working from
       home. Paul replied yes. Councilmember Lang asked if there is education on how to
       maintain safety for residents related to COVID. Paul replied that there are several
       educational efforts that the City is moving forward with. Nicole Cottle, Assistant
       City Manager, stated that she is working closely with MyHomeTown to educate
       and assist residents in that community that are being impacted. Sam Johnson, Public
       Relations, detailed efforts that are being done including signage, outreach to
       Hispanic communities, social media, etc. Councilmember Christensen noted that
       there is also information being provided via Healthy West Valley. Councilmember
       Huynh expressed concern about confusion or lack of communication to different
       ethnic groups. Mayor Bigelow stated he would like information related to
       geographic locations and demographics.
