#3 Deep Canyon Trailhead Conditional Use Permit 12 
Smith reviewed the staff report for the Deep Canyon Trailhead Conditional Use Permit (CUP). 13 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the requested variance for the setback, the number of trash cans, trail 14 
development on steep slopes, and the number of parking spots. 15 
Scott Peterson spoke as a consultant for the County on the parking study. 16 
Sands asked about the variance and why the parking lot wasn’t move to the east. 17 
Mr. Peterson commented on the variance for the setback and how the cost for the parking was prohibitive of 18 
moving it to the east. 19 
Staff and Commissioners discussed setback concerns, the vault toilet, concerns raised by citizens for road 20 
work currently happening, and a little bit of history on the project. 21 
Phillips explained some of the history of the trail and road impacts, the current needed road improvements to 22 
provide safe access to the current trailhead, and some wording additions to the trash, vault toilet conditions, 23 
and if year-round maintenance will be needed. 24 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the grant funding, steep slopes, and future trails. 25 
Jon Hardman commented as representative from Mendon City Council and commented on the watershed 26 
and water protection zone issues and locating the parking lot out of those areas, and revegetation plans. 27 
Jessa Watkins commented on the road improvements, concerns regarding the removal of trees, the wildlife 28 
corridor, and artificial lights. 29 
Daugs commented that concerns with tree removal can be discussed with the County Road Department. 30
