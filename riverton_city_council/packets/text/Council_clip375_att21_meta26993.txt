FROM:          Trace Robinson, Public Works Director/City Engineer

ITEM:          Approval to authorize the Mayor to sign the Memo of Understanding (MOU) with
               UDOT indicating that The City of Riverton will commit to provide $1,000,000 for
               the construction of the Bangerter/12600 S Interchange Project.

SUMMARY:

Over the past several months the City of Riverton has been working with UDOT regarding the
proposed Bangerter interchange at 12600 S. During the process, several modifications have been
made to the interchange to accommodate the desires of the City. The adjustments resulted in a
configuration which included the City’s requests. This concept was presented to the City Council
during the August 6th Council meeting. To construct the hybrid interchange, UDOT will require a
commitment from the City to provide a Betterment of $2,000,000. UDOT has provided a MOU for
the City to execute indicating the City is willing to provide the Betterment funding. The execution
of the MOU is time sensitive and will be required before a design build contract can be awarded
for the Bangerter 3 Project. The award is scheduled for mid-January 2019.

As a side note, City, County, UDOT, and State officials have work together to obtain assistance
from Salt Lake County for the first $1,000,000 of the of the Betterment. An Interlocal Agreement
(ILA) for $1,000,000 is scheduled to be on the County Council agenda for approval on December
3, 2019.

                          MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Date:                   August 12, 2019

To:                     Trace Robinson, Riverton City – Public Works Director/City Engineer

From:                   Marwan Farah, UDOT - Bangerter Highway Project Director

Subject:                Project No.: S-0154(84)14 – Bangerter Three Interchanges
                        Location: 12600 South and Bangerter Highway Interchange

 UDOT is preparing to request proposals for and award a design-build contract for the highway project identified
 as Project Number S-0154(84)14, this Project includes the construction of a grade separated interchange
 with Bangerter Highway going over 12600 South, in Riverton City (CITY), as shown in the State
 Environmental Study (SES). The CITY is requesting that UDOT modify its current SES plan and
 construct the grade separated interchange with lowering Bangerter Highway and 12600 South, as
 presented at Riverton City meeting on August 6, 2019. As UDOT continues to move forward with the
 design plans for this Project, the CITY agrees as follows:

      •    CITY will pay a share of the differential cost increase associated with lowering Bangerter
           Highway and 12600 South. The differential cost for this change to be paid by CITY is an amount
           not to exceed One Million Dollars ($ 1,000,000.00). UDOT anticipates that the total differential
           cost associated with lowering Bangerter Highway and 12600 South will be Two Million Dollars
           ($2,000.000.00). UDOT understands and agrees that it shall obtain additional funding directly
           from Salt Lake County in the amount of One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) to pay the total
           differential cost.

      •    CITY agrees to allow UDOT to close 12600 South access for traffic crossing Bangerter Highway,
           for the construction of the new interchange. Anticipated closure duration will be up-to six months
           of the construction (only right-lane movements on- and off Bangerter Highway might be
           allowed).
