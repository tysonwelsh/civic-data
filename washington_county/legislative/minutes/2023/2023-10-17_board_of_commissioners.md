PRESENTATION: 20 YEAR WATER PLAN: WASHINGTON COUNTY WATER
CONSERVANCY DISTRICT

Zach Renstrom, General Manager of the Washington County Water Conservancy District
(WCWCD) presented on the WCWCD’s 20-year water plan. Renstrom explained that the Lake
Powell Pipeline will not be built any time soon, causing WCWCD to reexamine ways to ensure
water availability for generations to come. These include new homes requiring to be water
efficient, grass removal rebates, water reuse systems and reservoirs.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3185, A RESOLUTION AWARDING
FUNDING TO THE WASHINGTON COUNTY WATER CONSERVANCY DISTRICT
FOR THE DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF TOQUER RESERVOIR

Commissioner Iverson stated that the American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) of 2021 sent money to
local governments in 2021. As a County, the Commissioners were dedicated to invest these
moneys in the community. ARPA funds are being used for Purgatory projects, an Emergency
Operations storage facility, and the construction and design of Toquer Reservoir.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3185, A
Resolution Awarding Funding to the Washington County Water Conservancy
District for the Design and Construction of Toquer Reservoir. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3189, A RESOLUTION PROPOSING
TO ANNEX ADDITIONAL AREAS INTO DAMMERON VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL
SERVICE DISTRICT; PROPOSING TO AUTHORIZE ADDITIONAL SERVICES, AND
OTHER MATTERS

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated this Resolution is regarding the consolidation of fire
districts along SR-18. This Resolution would expand the boundaries of the Dammeron Valley Fire
Special Service District, and would annex the communities of Brookside, Veyo, Gunlock and
Diamond Valley into Dammeron Valley Special Service District. There will be a hearing on the
proposed annexation in December 2023, with the final annexation resolution proposed to be
adopted in February, 2024.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3189,
A Resolution Proposing to Annex Additional Areas into Dammeron Valley
Fire Special Service District; Proposing to Authorize Additional Services, and
