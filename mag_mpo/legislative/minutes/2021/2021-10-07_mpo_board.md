UDOT TIP Modification – Bob Allen, MAG SR-92 widening from Manor Drive to Spruce Drive in Highland, add center turn lane This is a UDOT selected project using $600,000 of UDOT funds. Because MAG has jurisdiction over the TIP, a recommendation and vote are required. People traveling toward American Fork Canyon on SR-92 that want to turn onto Spruce Drive must turn from the eastbound through lane. This is causing congestion because this section of road has higher speed and volume of traffic. The SR-92 Center Turn Lane Extension in Highland project will extend the center turn from Manor Drive to Spruce Drive allowing for safer left turns. MAG Staff and TAC recommend the project be modified as indicated by UDOT. Mayor Mann moved that the SR92 Center Turn Lane Extension project be added to the TIP. Mayor Stout seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Herbert Utah Valley Chamber of Commerce MAG Staff: Shawn Seager Melanie Haws Andrew Jackson Michelle Carroll Robert Allen Shawn Eliot MPO Regional Planning Committee October 7, 2021 Page 3 Pleasant Grove Blvd/North County Blvd, funding adjustment, intersection improvements This is a UDOT selected project using UDOT funds. Because MAG has jurisdiction over the TIP, a recommendation and vote are required. This project was selected in 2020 through the TIP selection process at UDOT. During the design of the project, a recent pavement project had skipped this area and wasn’t accounted for in the original project scope estimate. Since the time the project was selected in 2020, there have been some changes resulting in cost increases. Two new signals were added to the project, improvements were made to coordinate with the Pleasant Grove 1300 West realignment project, and construction and property increases. The scope of the project is to increase capacity at the interchange and North County Blvd area. Current funds: $9,100,000 Additional funds: $4,160,000 New project total: $13,260,000 MAG Staff and TAC recommend the project be modified as indicated by UDOT. Mayor Wright moved that RPC recommend that $4,160,000 be added to the PG Interchange Area Capacity Project. Mayor Andersen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 2022 TIP Project Selection Process Kick-off - Bob Allen MAG will be selecting projects to be added to the TIP in 2022. Approximately $80 million in State, County and Federal funds will be available. The majority of those funds will be available in 2025 and 2026. A review of the selection process and the schedule for each step was discussed. Based on discussions with multiple TAC members, proposed changes were recommended to adjust the process and make it better. Proposed changes include: 1. No vote will be taken at the project idea phase. MAG staff will categorize projects based on identified criteria. 2. At concept stage, the total score will be 50% staff score, 50% TAC score. MAG has a unique voting process. MAG MPO TAC has one vote each to approve each project on the project list and voting members are doing the technical review and voting on their own projects. No other MPO, that MAG can find, has project sponsors select their own projects. MPO Regional Planning Committee October 7, 2021 Page 4 Mayor Staheli expressed concern that there is not enough time for him to prepare and submit project ideas on behalf of the Town of Goshen. Bob Allen stated that MAG Staff are available to assist small cities with the process. MAG Staff and TAC recommends the process be updated as proposed. Mayor Staheli moved that the project selection be amended as presented. Mayor Johnson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
