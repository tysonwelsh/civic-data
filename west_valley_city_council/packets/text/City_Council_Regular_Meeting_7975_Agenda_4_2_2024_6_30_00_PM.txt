                6.        Comment Period:
                          (The comment period is limited to 30 minutes. Any person wishing to comment shall limit
                          their comments to three minutes. Any person wishing to comment during the comment period
                          shall request recognition by the Mayor. Upon recognition, the citizen shall approach the
                          microphone. All comments shall be directed to the Mayor. No person addressing the City
                          Council during the comment period shall be allowed to comment more than once during that
                          comment period. Speakers should not expect any debate with the Mayor, City Council or
                          City Staff; however, the Mayor, City Council or City Staff may respond within the 30-minute
                          period. Speakers shall refrain from personal attacks against fellow citizens, city staff, or
                          members of the City Council.)
