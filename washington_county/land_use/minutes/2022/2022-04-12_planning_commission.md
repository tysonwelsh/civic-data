Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting

The Public Meeting of the Planning Commission of Washington County was called to order by
Chairman Leuck at 1:30 p.m. on April 12, 2022, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers and via Zoom, at 197 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah. Commissioners in

Recreation Grounds and Facility Project located in Kolob on Parcels 102-A-49-1-B-HV,

Transition, Open Space Conservation, and Seasonal Forest Residential. The applicant has

existing provisions for Rural Recreation Grounds and Facilities. The proposed units
include cabins, yurts, tree house units, glamping units, improved campgrounds and

denied. The applicant is Zions Gate Management Co.

This is a public meeting, not a public hearing item. The public is welcome to attend, and
written comments may be submitted to the Community Development Department any
time 24 hours in advance of the meeting. This is a continuation of an item from a prior
Planning Commission meeting to this date certain.

The applicant has requested that this item be continued again. The Planning Commission
will consider the applicant’s continuance request at the April 12, 2022 Planning
Commission meeting.

Mr. Messel explained that this item has been previously presented to the Planning
Commission and been tabled to dates certain. He stated that Staff recommends that the
Planning Commission table the item and that the item be noticed at a later date. Ms.
Hales explained details on the project are possibly changing and the item would most
likely need to be re-noticed.

ZONE CHANGE / PUBLIC HEARING. Review and hold a public hearing for a zone
change request from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the A-5
(Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone for Parcel 7004-A-1-F-NC near Enterprise.

Mr. Messel explained the general location of the parcel and stated that the parcel does
not currently comply with zoning standards and must be brought into compliance before
issuing a building permit for a home on the property. He went on to explain that the road
also needs to be dedicated before the county can issue a building permit. The applicant
has submitted a record of survey to show where Bench Road will be dedicated to meet
the approval conditions of the county. The adjacent parcel to the east is included on the
record of survey to show Holt Canyon Road being dedicated to the county, as well.

Commissioner Anderson asked about the road between the lots and the acreage on the
smaller side of the lot. Mr. Messel explained that even though the road separates the
parcel, it’s still technically one parcel. It wouldn’t be possible to build a dwelling on the
smaller portion of the parcel on the north side and that the septic density approval
through Washington County Water Conservancy District is only approved for one
dwelling, and the applicant could do agricultural buildings on the smaller section. Ms.
Hales suggested using a deed restriction and appreciates the applicant’s willingness to
dedicate the road.

Mr. Edwards stated that the record of survey proposes that 28’ be dedicated to the county
for Holt Canyon Road and he would like them to dedicate 50’ if they are agreeable to
that. Mr. Messel also explained that the applicant would still meet the minimum lot size
zone requirements if so. Chairman Leuck asked what obligations the applicant has to the
county if they dedicate the road. Ms. Hales stated that in the case of a subdivision, they
would need to do all the improvements but, in this case, we work with each applicant
and these applicants have been working with the county. Mr. Messel clarified the
difference between the subdivision and a platted plat, such as in this case, and why it is
considered one lot, even with Bench Road cutting through.

Chairman Lueck asked the applicant what his thoughts are on giving the 50’ road
dedication for Bench Road. Mr. Brayden Preston; the applicant, stated that he is not
wanting to give up any more property. Mr. Edwards asked the applicant if he is agreeable
to dedicating Bench Road and Mr. Preston agreed that he is. Mr. Kasey Preston stated he
spoke with Mr. Whitehead; Public Works Director, and he approved the dedication of
Bench Road and Holt Canyon Road as proposed on the record of survey. Mr. Edwards
stated that the county is always happy to get whatever road dedications we can get and
explained the desire for road widths as far as safety and access.

ZONE CHANGE / PUBLIC HEARING. Review and hold a public hearing for a zone
change request from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the FR-
13.5 (Forest Residential 13,500 Sq. Ft. minimum lot size) zone for Parcel 6199-A-1 in
Pine Valley.

Mr. Messel explained the parcel size and location. He went on to explain that this agenda
item could allow for up to 25 lots on this property if rezoned to the proposed FR-13.5 lot
size zone. He also explained the adjacent Thiriot subdivision, and that the applicant had
broken up the zoning designations within the parcel to allow the back portion of the
parcel to remain open. He stated that the Planning Commission may feel it appropriate
to require utility will-serves before the zone change approval. The applicant is not
requesting a subdivision, just a zone change at this time.

Commissioner Owens asked for clarification that the parcel to the east is currently zoned
A-20 and the parcel to the west stayed A-5 in the back and the front zoning approval was
FR-13.5. Mr. Messel confirmed.

Commissioner Owens asked the applicant why he did not request a zone change similar
to the adjacent property. Mr. Jennings stated that he is proposing the same zone as the
first 5 lots on the adjacent property and would like to rezone because it’s a reasonable
zone. He went on to explain that Pine Valley water shares are limited and it’s not fair to
request water at this time.

Mr. Edwards explained that the county is struggling with water and utilities — septic
systems included. This may require an advanced septic system. Mr. Jennings stated that
he is aware and is agreeable to moving forward with an advanced system in combination
with the adjacent Thiriot Subdivision. He explained that he is planning to build 3 houses
at this time, depending on water availability. Water availability and zoning options were
discussed between Staff, Planning Commission and the applicant. Discussion continued
about zoning density and water supply.

Emily Neilson, representative for a committee of Pine Valley residents, proceeded to give
a presentation concerning the Pine Valley area and its history. She explained the goals of
the Pine Valley Overlay Zone proposal. The proposal would allow the county to focus on
a small area with specific needs. The community of Pine Valley was given opportunity to
give comments and a survey was sent out to the residents of the area before contracting
with a professional planner to develop the overlay draft. The proposed overlay suggests
standards to follow as new developments come in. Ms. Neilson thanked the Planning

reviewed by the Pine Valley board before approaching the county for approvals.

Discussion was had about how the Pine Valley Board would relate to County Staff and
the Planning Commission. The General Plan was explained and how an overlay zone
would differ from that. Commissioner Owens suggested having someone independent
for the appeal process like what the county has done for any appeals of Board decisions.

Mr. Messel explained the next steps to move forward for the proposed overlay zone.
Commissioner Anderson expressed concern about holding the 5% STR. He stated that it
could lead to the current STR owners holding all the cards, and he would like to see them
look at all options regarding STRs in the valley. Chairman Leuck thanked everyone for
coming to this meeting and is encouraged at the support of the community. Discussion
was had about accommodating current and future residents and setting up ordinances
that are fair to everyone. Chairman Leuck asked how we can support and move this
forward. Mr. Messel explained that zoning ordinances are living documents and need to
be continually examined and changed. Discussion was had about state, county and the
overlay zones not contradicting each other.

Chairman Leuck thanked Ms. Neilson for her work and the community of Pine Valley for

DISCUSSION ITEM: HURRICANE ANNEXATION.

Mr. Messel explained that there is an annexation in process right now for a portion of the
unincorporated county to be incorporated into Hurricane City. The area is an

the ability to ask for the annexation. This is a different process than what we are used to.
Hurricane City will be doing the hearing on this. The county has provided area maps and
the annexation process can be very time consuming.
