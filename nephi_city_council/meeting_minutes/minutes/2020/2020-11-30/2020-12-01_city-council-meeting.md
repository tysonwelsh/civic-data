Guests: Myrna Trauntvein (Press), Donald W. Ball, Travis Kenison, Gerratt Bethers, Toby Kingston, Craig
Oswald.

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then
Councilor Worwood offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  Councilor Kent Jones led the pledge of allegiance.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes for a public hearing held on 11-17-20, the regular council meeting held on 11-17-20, work
session held on 11-24-20, sale of a burial rights to the Debbie P. Howarth Trust, Tyson and Amanda
Ashworth, and Lorie and David Ashworth, and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 12-1-20
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BEER LICENSE APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones reviewed a beer license application for Nephi Oil, Inc. Councilor Jones moved to
approve the beer license. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

PAUL DAVIS HIRED AS NEPHI CITY PATROL OFFICER:

  Councilor Jones moved to ratify the hiring of Paul Davis as a patrol officer. Mr. Davis had been
working for the police department as an unpaid reserve and the past few months as a paid reserve
officer. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

LIBRARY DIRECTOR RUTH BONZO REPORTED ON TWO LIBRARY GRANTS:

  Library Director Ruth Bonzo reported to the council about two grants that the library had recently
received. The first grant was received from the Utah Educational Network to purchase six additional
WiFi hot spots making 12-total available to the public. The hot spots help people that do not have
access to the internet.
  The second grant that Ms. Bonzo reported on was a $5,000 pass-through grant received from Central
Utah Public Health Department. The money is to be used for substance abuse prevention services. The
funds can also be used to help build life skills to help with things like bullying prevention and mental
health. The library staff plans on creating substance abuse story kits for all ages. They are also planning
two community events.

ORDINANCE 12-01-2020 -MALMGREN ANNEXATION ADOPTED:

  Councilor Jones moved to adopt Ordinance 12-01-2020 : AN ORDINANCE ANNEXING CERTAIN
PROPERTIES TO NEPHI CITY AND ESTABLISHING ZONING CLASSIFICATION. The property described in the
ordinance is known as the Malmgren Annexation. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

2006 GARBAGE TRUCK DECLARED AS SURPLUS PROPERTY:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the need to declare the 2006 Sterling
Condor Garbage Truck as surplus property. The Solid Waste department had recently purchased a new
garbage truck leaving the need to dispose of the oldest truck in the fleet. The truck will be put on the
internet site govdeals.com to be sold to the highest bidder. Councilor Ostler moved to declare the 2006
Garbage Truck as surplus property. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION 12-01-2020 APPOINTING TEMPORARY JUDGES ADOPTED:

   City Recorder Lisa E. Brough reported to the council that due to the passing of Judge Sharla Williams,
the Nephi City Council needed to adopt a resolution allowing for the appointment of temporary judges.
Councilor Jones moved to adopt Resolution 12-01-2020: A RESOLUTION OF THE NEPHI CITY COUNCIL
APPOINTING TEMPORARY JUDGES TO THE JUSTICE COURT. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

E-PROSECUTOR AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson reported that due to some staffing changes at the County Attorney’s office, the city
needed to contract prosecution services from another source. While the city is looking for a long-term
solution, City Attorney Kasey Wright had agreed to provide services in the interim. Part of the services
needed include the use of a software called E-Prosecutor. The software provides a method for
prosecuting attorneys to compile information required by the state for the justice court. The software
was used by the previous prosecutor for the city and can be used by any prosecutor the city may choose
in the future. The cost of the software is $1,600 per year per user. Councilor Memmott moved to
authorize the Mayor to sign the E-Prosecutor Agreement. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

    Mr. Atkinson and several councilors expressed their appreciation to all those that helped with the
Illuminate the Night festivities that turned out to be a huge success.

   Councilor Jones shared a historic document that outlined the 1954 dedicatory services for the Nephi
4th Ward Chapel and Seminary Building that currently houses the city’s police department. He expressed
his mixed feelings about the need to improve our police force and department building but was hesitant
about raising property taxes. He asked if the council could discuss the drug problem and police
department expansion further at a future work session.

  The council agreed to cancel the work session scheduled for December 22, 2020.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Ostler moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session to
discuss the character and competency of an individual, litigation, and investigative proceedings.
Councilor Worwood seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:05 p.m.
