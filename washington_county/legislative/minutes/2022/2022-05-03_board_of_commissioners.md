CONSIDERATION OF PUBLIC WORKS REPORT

Public Works Director Ron Whitehead explained that the Road Department is focusing on road
prep work for projects moving forward, specifically in Kolob and on Antelope Road.

In preparation for Ironman, the passing lane at Winchester Hills area was completed in order to
accommodate athletes and motorists during the event.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Accept the Public Works Report as
Presented. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1207-O, AN ORDINANCE ADDING THE
COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR AS A MEMBER OF THE ACCIDENT REVIEW BOARD

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that the Accident Review Board is a
recommending body to the Commission regarding accidents involving county vehicles and
property. Currently, the Board is comprised of one county commissioner, one member from the
Attorney’s Office, one member from the Sheriff’s Office, and one representative from the Human
Resources Department. The County would like to add an additional seat that is held by the County

Administrator.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2022-1207-O, An
Ordinance Adding the County Administrator as a Member of the Accident
Review Board. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

Destination Development Manager Leslie Fonger explained that the Tourism Tax Advisory Board
(TAB) recommended funding for St. George City to expand their pickleball court in Little Valley
complex. The project would expand the complex from twenty-four (24) to thirty-four (34) courts,
including a championship court with stadium seating. The TAB recommended funding the project
for $1,000,000 from the TRT fund.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2940, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Agreement Between Washington County
and the City of St. George for TRT Funding to Expand the Little Valley
Pickleball Complex. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

Chairman Iverson explained that Washington County would like to provide bee inspection services

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2949, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AGREEMENT WITH TRAILS UTAH TO HELP FUND A NEW MOUNTAIN BIKE

TRAIL SYSTEM IN WASHINGTON COUNTY

Ms. Fonger explained that two (2) mountain bike trail systems were recently approved for
construction consisting of fifty-five (55) miles. Trails Utah will administer the first six (6) miles
of trail for the Spring Hollow trail system, including adding trailheads, signage, facilities, event
staging areas, race start and finish areas, parking and improved dispersed camping areas.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2949, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement with Trails Utah to Help Fund a New
Mountain Bike Trail System in Washington County. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

THE USE OF THE PEACE OFFICER BASIC TRAINING REIMBURSEMENT
AGREEMENT AT THE SHERIFF’S OFFICE

Sheriff Nate Brooksby explained that the County will pay for the basic core academy and
corrections or peace officer academies through the Utah Peace Officers Standards and Training
Division (POST), so long as the employee will remain in the continuous full-time employment of
the County as a member of the Sheriffs Office for a period of two (2) years following the date of

the completion of training.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Chairman Iverson reminded everyone of the upcoming Ironman Championship taking place on
Saturday, May 7, 2022.

Commissioner Adam Snow reminded those communities affected by the route of the Ironman that
the economic impact from the event saves each county resident approximately $1,400 dollars in
tax each year and to watch out for those who are training this week on the roads.
