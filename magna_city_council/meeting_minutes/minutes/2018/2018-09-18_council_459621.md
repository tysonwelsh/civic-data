Development of the Operational Organization of the Metro Township

                Council Member Hull stated the Council does not have a policy or best practice
on what boards Council Members sit on and how long their terms are. She would like to create a
policy that defines how the Council chooses who sits on what board and for how long.

Utah League Cities and Towns Convention Update

               Council Member Hull stated there is a transportation land use connection
program grant that the Council can apply for. It will fund a person to help rewrite codes and
ordinances to make them fit Magna. She will also look into what the following organizations have
to offer: National Main Street Program, Roger Brooks International Downtown Action Plan,
Destination Development Association, and Center for Neighborhood Technology.

                  Council Member Peel stated he would support the grant if the Council could hire
the individual.

2019 Legislative Update

               Paul Ashton stated language in the Metro Township clean-up bill includes
changing the relationship with independent elected officials from obligatory to discretionary, and
adding a section to include the definition of a metro township and that it will be considered a city
or town unless otherwise stated. The bill needs to be sent to the legislative research committee.
