Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council held Tuesday, October 4, 2022, at Lehi
City Hall, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at approximately 3:30 PM.

Present:     Mark Johnson, Mayor
             Paige Albrecht, Council Member
             Paul Hancock, Council Member
             Mike Southwick, Council Member

Excused:     Chris Condie, Council Member
             Katie Koivisto, Council Member

Others Present:     Jason Walker, City Administrator; Cameron Boyle, Assistant City
Administrator; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Joel Eves, Power Director; Andy Gowans,
Management Analyst; and Teisha Wilson, City Recorder

1. Welcome
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that Councilmembers Koivisto and Condie were
   excused. Councilor Albrecht attended virtually.

2. Tour of Glass Recycling Facility
   Council and staff participated in a tour of the glass recycling facility. The Council returned at
   approximately 6:30 p.m.

3. Discussion of Power Plan Expansion
   Joel Eves presented on the power plant expansion. He stated that some of the factors that drive
   this decision include worsening conditions, such as increased heat, gas price volatility, and
   increased demand for services. This could lead to more savings and better resource diversity.
   He stated that they shouldn’t continue to buy contracted 5-year products. They need to look at
   sustainability long term that meets the City’s needs. Mr. Eves recommended they add 3
   additional 2.4 MW units and 2 new 3.89 MW units.

   Mr. Eves stated that they need to acquire the air permit and then Council will need to approve
   the contract. They will also have to comply with federal regulations. He stated that they will
   need an additional staff member as a generator technician. He said the building will not change
   because they planned for this expansion. He would like to begin as soon as possible. The cost
   is around $19 million.

4. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time the meeting adjourned
   at approximately 7:00 p.m.
