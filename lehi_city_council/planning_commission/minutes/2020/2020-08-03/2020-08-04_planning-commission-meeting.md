Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Vice Chair Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Discussion Items

    2.1 Review and discussion of the Planning Division’s downtown parking study
    Mike West reviewed the parking study that was conducted. The Commission and Planning
    Staff discussed the study.

    2.2 Discussion of downtown way-finding signage plan
    Jacob Curtis showed the Commission a presentation of waling apps and how they could
    be tied into general plan strategies, as well as a revitalization plan to improve the way
    finding in Lehi City.

    2.3 Review of City Civic Campus Master Plan


    Kim Struthers presented information to the Commission about the Campus Master   Plan
    and the development updates for the plan.

3. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Nielsen moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Everett seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
