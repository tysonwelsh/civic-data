       MINUTES OF WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING
                                 JUNE 18, 2025

THE WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION MET FOR STUDY SESSION ON WEDNESDAY,
JUNE 18, 2025 AT 3:30 PM IN WEST VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600 CONSTITUTION BLVD, SUITE 220,
WEST VALLEY CITY, UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY
CHAIR MATTHEW LOVATO.

      Mathew Lovato, Chair

PRESENTATIONS:

GP-3-2025 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a change to the City’s General Plan to provide an
update to the Fairbourne Station Vision. Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-
ut.gov.

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented the City’s proposal to update the Fairbourne Station Vision Plan for the
areas generally between 3500 and 3650 South, and 3200 to 2700 West. The most significant proposed change
is adding the Valley Fair Mall property with the option for mixed-use development, particularly residential,
to revitalize the mall area. The City would also update the Plan’s text to reflect accomplishments from the
past 13 years, specifically, streets and office uses.

Z-2-2025 Petition by CARL AND JENNIFER NEWLAND requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture,
minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-7 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 7,000 sq. ft.). The
property is located at 5173 West 3500 South on 0.86 acres. Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented an application requesting to rezone a portion of the applicant’s property
from A to R-1-7 to build a new home, with the existing home remaining on the property. The request requires
two exceptions: a 65 foot frontage instead of the required 70 feet, and an 83.5 foot width instead of the
required 100 feet. While an alternative flag lot configuration is possible, the owners prefer to locate the new
home closer to the street. The Planning Commission discussed the setback distance and driveway placement,
noting that while the home could be set back further, the driveway would need to be addressed in the
subdivision process.
Z-3-2025 Petition by Tyler Jones, representing IHC HEALTH SERVICES INC. requesting a zone change
from B/RP (Business Research Park) to C-2 (General Commercial). The property is located at 3930 West
Parkway Boulevard on 11.25 acres.
Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented an application requesting a zone change from BRP to C-2 for property
in Lake Park to accommodate a new Salt Lake Behavioral Health mental health hospital. The C-2 zone
allows hospitals, whereas the BRP zone does not. The applicant plans to convert the existing two-story
building into a layout that would be more efficient and cost-effective than their current five-story building in
Salt Lake City. The facility would primarily serve military personnel and public safety workers with trauma-
related issues, though the exact services offered and transportation logistics remain unclear. Since the
immediate plans only include the existing building, there was discussion about only rezoning the developed
portion of the lot instead of the entire lot that includes vacant land.

Z-4-2025 Petition by EVY COLLIN requesting a zone change from R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling
Residential, min. lot size 8,000 sq. ft.) and C-1 (Neighborhood Commercial) to R-1-7 (Single Unit Dwelling
Residential, min. lot size 7,000 sq. ft). The property is located at 4048 South 4800 West and is 0.19 acres in
size. Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented an application to change the zoning from R-1-8 and C-1 to R-1-7 for
two parcels totaling 10.19 acres at 4048 South 4800 West to build a house. The property is surrounded by
single-unit dwellings and has a complex history due to multiple boundary changes without City approval.
The applicant is also asking for frontage exceptions due to the property's narrow width, and approval for a 2-
car garage instead of the required 3-car garage, committing to include a parking pad to the side of the garage.
Steve Pastorik indicated that since the lot is likely too small for a commercial use, building a home would be
better than leaving it vacant in staff’s opinion. The applicant committed to a 2,700 square feet foot home,
fiber cement siding, and stone/stucco materials.

SMI-5-2025 Petition by VTRE DEVELOPMENT is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Vantrust
WVC Industrial Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots and 1 parcel on 39.5 acres. The
subdivision is located at approximately 6051 West Beagley Road and is zoned M (Manufacturing). Staff -
Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented a proposal to make 2 lots roughly 9 acres each. The remaining property
will be developed in the future, due to having to realign the Ritter Canal. The exact business use is unknown
but would be developed as large office flex.

C-8-2025 Petition by COAL CREEK CONSULTING requesting a conditional use for roof mounted
antennas. The property is located at 2933 South 5600 West on 3.36 acres and is zoned C-2 (General
Commercial). Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented a proposal for a conditional use permit from Coal Creek Consulting to
install roof-mounted cell antennas on the Burlington building. The antennas are currently on a light pole
stealth facility across the street. The plan is to remove the pole and install the upgraded equipment on the
Burlington building’s roof. The new antennas’ height would meet Code at 14.5 feet and be placed a minimum
of 10 feet away from the edge of the building’s roof behind the parapet. The proposal includes three different
antenna arrays; initially, two antennas will be installed on each, with additional antennas added as needed in
the future. The antennas are on a sled mount with a visual screening barrier that will be finished in a neutral
color to match the Burlington building.
