BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of an Amended Subdivision Plat for approximately
2.87 acres located at approximately 12271 S 900 E (Exhibits B & C). The property is currently
zoned CN. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to allow for the
creation of three (3) lots from six (6) existing parcels.

The subject property is a part of Lots 2, 3, and 4 of block 20 of the Draperville Plat (Exhibit F).
According to County records, the initial structures on the property were built in 1964, with
an expansion of the structures occurring in 1996.

Subdivision Layout.
The applicant is proposing to create three (3) lots on the property located within the existing
Draperville Plat (Exhibits F and G). The lots range in size from 0.54 to 1.72 acres in size. There
are existing easements that include a 16 ft wide right -of-way easement, a signal pole
easement, and a 5 ft wide telecommunications easement. These easements will all remain
and are shown on the proposed plat (Exhibit G).

The proposed plat includes new 7 ft wide public utility easements along the front, rear, and
side property lines of the new lots. It will also add a 25 ft wide cross access easement running
north/south between all three lots, and a 10 ft wide private sewer easement across Lot 3 for
the use of Lot 2.

A cross access easement is needed to provide vehicular and pedestrian connectivity between
the lots within the amended subdivision plat and the existing parcel to the east in order to
comply with DCMC Section 17-5-035. The applicant has submitted a proposed reciprocal
access easement agreement to staff for review. The applicant is required to record this
easement and note the recordation information for the easement on the plat prior to final
approval and signature of the Zoning Administrator on the Final Mylar Plat and recordation
of the amended subdivision plat.

Criteria For Approval.
The grounds for review and potential approval of an amended subdivision plat request are
found in DCMC Section 17-9-040. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat if the
        Planning Commission finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment,
        and no public street or municipal utility easement has been vacated or amended.
