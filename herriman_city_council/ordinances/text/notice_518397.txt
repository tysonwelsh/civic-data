PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on February 27, 2019 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2019-04 adopting a restructured fee schedule for the Herriman City Police to provide case reports, fingerprint services, clearance letters and other miscellaneous services to include a fee specific to a state mandate to maintain a child abuse registry. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
