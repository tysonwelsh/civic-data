       Public Hearing – 6:05 p.m. (Or as soon thereafter as possible)
       To consider adopting the annual budget of the City of South Salt Lake Redevelopment Agency
       for the period commencing July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2024.

       Community & Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, and Finance Director,
       Crystal Makin, were both present to talk to the Board of Directors about the RDA’s goals and
       plans for the upcoming year. A copy of the PowerPoint presentation is attached and
       incorporated by this reference.

       They also gave an overview of the budget changes that have been made since the last RDA
       meeting.

       Mr. Weidenhamer said that one of the main goals for him as the Community & Economic
       Development Director is to have the RDA stand alone and be able to operate on its own. The
       RDA does currently use existing City staff from the Planning Department, Attorney’s Office, as
       well as some others, because it is more cost efficient that way. The intent for this next year is to
       re-orient what economic development is for the City with its existing business community and
       residents. He shared that his focus is on providing more community amenities like trails, biking,
       and open space, as well as land acquisition for parks and other community amenities. He also
       wants to focus on being more involved in community events, specifically in the Creative Industry
       Zone.

       He talked about the Housing and Transit Reinvestment Act and how they are currently pursuing
       an application to leverage new State legislation that would encourage affordable housing and
       redevelopment by sharing property taxes with developers and having that also be a funding
       source for the City to bond against.

       He then went over the 2023-2024 RDA budget and the changes that have been made. A big
       change is regarding the funds for the Chamber of Commerce. Another change is funding $2
       million dollars for land acquisition and noted that this is a strategy that staff has been working
       on internally.

       The second slide in the PowerPoint shows a line for new revenues from renting out any acquired
       properties back out under the free market. It also shows a fund balance for the sale of the Bella
       Terra property where the Porsche site may be.

       New expenses show funding for the Chamber, and the other lines show what it would take to
       purchase, own, and operate potential land pieces that the City could have.

       Regarding the Chamber’s fund, there would be an added $40,000 to the $5,000 that the City

        currently funds. He said that he feels that this is an opportunity for the RDA to recommit and
        reinvest into the Chamber Board.

        Chamber President, Gary Birdsall, and his staff are currently in the process of contacting every
        business in the City to compilate what their needs are. He also said that it’s his goal to make a
        significant commitment to the Women in Business network.

        Lastly, he said that another budget goal is to add staff to assist Gary in the tasks to keep up with
        the Chamber needs and goals.

        Director Siwik asked about the $2 million dollars to fund land acquisition.

        Finance Director, Crystal Makin, stated that there is a budget of $2 million dollars in the City’s
        Capital Fund for ‘potential land purchases’ in the coming year. This line item in the RDA budget
        mirrors that and provides a cushion when opportunities open.

        Director Bynum said that there have been many past conversations regarding the Chamber. She
        pointed out that Chambers from other cities are independent but noted how South Salt Lake,
        although being a smaller city, had bigger needs and feels that this is a good strategy.

        Director Mila opened the Public Hearing at 6:11 p.m.

        Chamber of Commerce President, Gary Birdsall, addressed the Board of Directors and shared his
        being on board with the RDA goals for fiscal year 2023-2024.

        He also took the time to share about Director and Council Chair Bynum and how she stepped up
        to help a local business, Pat’s BBQ, and was impressed with her service and attentiveness when
        they went out and sat with them. She went out of her way to get in touch with the right City
        staff to address the issues they had. He invited the rest of the Directors to reach out to him to
        schedule a time to go out and meet with business owners.

        Director Mila closed the public hearing at 6:14 p.m.

        Director Huff made a motion to approve Resolution for the RDA Budget for the 2023-2024 fiscal
        year.
