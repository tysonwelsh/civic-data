       A request to amend the Boulder Creek Commons PD (Planned Development) in order to review the conceptual
       site plan and elevations on approximately seven acres located on the south-east corner of 1450 South and River
       Road. The applicant is RCSG, and the representative is Richie Webb. The project will be known as River
       Crossing – Phase 1. Case No. 2021-ZCA-081 (Staff – Dan Boles)

       Dan Boles presented the following:

       Dan Boles – You have likely seen this before, as Boulder Creek Commons. This was all zoned PD-C in about
       2015. There is an approval for a Quench It across the street. The use list is in place, and they are not asking to
       change it at all. Elevations will come in later for buildings A and B, we are looking at buildings C-G. It appears
       they are meeting the landscaping requirement we will make sure of that when the site plan comes through. They
       meet the parking requirement at the worst-case scenario. If retail comes in then they will have extra parking. The
       buildings are similar in design and material. Staff is recommending approval of the application.

       Commissioner Larsen – It looks like there are room for drive thrus and stacking.

       Dan Boles – About a year ago or so they brought this before you because they were limited to 3 drive thrus on the
       site, City Council took that away, that limitation. But they gave them some requirements for drive thru. I’ve
       looked at those requirements. It appears that they are meeting the requirements that City Council gave them.
