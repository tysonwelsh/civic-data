Planning Commission Work to be Suspended

               The Council reviewed the following resolution placing a moratorium on the work of
the White City Metro Township Planning Commission on the White City Metro Township General
Plan.

RESOLUTION NO. 18-04-03                                                         Date: April 5, 2018

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL PLACING
       A MORITORIUM ON THE WORK OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
       PLANNING COMMISSION ON THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
       GENERAL PLAN

                                            RECITALS

           A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
              Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.




                                                 2
DATE        THURSDAY                            APRIL                                 5, 2018

       B. The White City Metro Township Council (“Council”) is a duly elected Metro
          Township pursuant to UCA §§ 10-3b-501 et al.

       C. The White City Metro Township Planning Commission (“Planning Commission”) is
          the Planning Commission for White City pursuant to UCA §§ 10-9a-301 et al.

       D. Utah State Legislature is the legislative branch of the State of Utah.

       E. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a county pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Title 17
          Counties, and is the contracted service provider of certain municipal-type services
          via the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”).

       F. White City is required to develop and adopt a White City General Plan pursuant to
          UCA §§ 10-9a-401 et al.

       G. The Planning Commission is charged to develop a recommended Draft White City
          General Plan to the Council for their consideration and action.

       H. 2nd Substitute House Bill 259 “Moderate Income Housing Amendments,”
          (“HB259”), approved by 2018 Utah Legislature and signed by the Governor,
          requires municipalities to develop moderate income housing plans as a part of their
          General Plans by July 1, 2019.

       I.   At the time of this resolution, no administrative or legal guidance has been given
            by the State on how municipalities who were land locked and built out should
            create or use economic information and other related land use information to
            create a moderate income housing plan in accordance with 2nd Substitute House
            Bill 259.

       J. The County developed a Moderate Income Housing Plan (“County Plan”) that
          included White City, the other Metro Townships, Millcreek City, and the
          Unincorporated Areas, and presented it to the County Council in February 2017.

       K. The County Plan has not been reviewed or accepted and adopted by the White
          City Council and it is unknown at this time whether or not the County Plan comports
          with 2nd Substitute House Bill 259.

       L. Contracted Staff used the County Plan to guide discussions of the Planning
          Commission during the formulations of the land use maps that were to be
          considered for use in the General Plan and may have influenced work product and
          maps being considered by the Planning Commission.

       M. As part of the General Plan process, it has been determined that contracted staff
          and the planning commission could benefit from additional training to assist them
          in the development of the General Plan. In addition, it has been determined that
          additional legal support is needed by the Planning Commission as it undertakes
          its duties.


                                            3
DATE           THURSDAY                              APRIL                                  5, 2018


           N. As the legislative and administrative authority of White City, the Council has the
              right to impose a moratorium on work on the General Plan until such time as
              clarification is received on the effect of 2nd Substitute House Bill 259 on White City,
              and additional training and legal support is provided to the Planning Commission.

                                          RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, that effective
the date hereof, all work of the Planning Commission on the White City General Plan shall cease
and not recommence until such time as the Council is satisfied that the effects of 2nd Substitute
House Bill 259 have been taken into consideration and additional training and legal support has
been provided to members of the Planning Commission and contracted staff. This matter shall be
reconsidered in six months. The Planning Commission shall continue to fulfill its other duties and
responsibilities.

APPROVED and ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, State of Utah
on this 5th day of April, 2018.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

DATE           THURSDAY                             APRIL                                  5, 2018


                Matt Rhoades, Battalion Chief, Unified Fire Authority, stated Heavy Rescue and
Hazmat were called out to do a body recovery in Eureka. A rollover accident occurred in front of
the Heavy Rescue and Hazmat crews on their way home; the driver and passenger had to be
extricated from the vehicle. The accident was in a remote area and it would have taken an hour
to get extraction equipment to the area. If the crews had not been there at that moment, the driver
and passenger would have died.

House Bill 238 – Firework Restrictions passed, which will restrict the number of days fireworks
can be used and increase penalties for using fireworks illegally. UFA is getting ready for wildland
season and anticipates an average summer. The safety message for the month is a reminder to
change the batteries in smoke detectors twice a year.

Sandy Canal Trail

             The Council reviewed the following resolution approving an interlocal agreement
between White City and Sandy City for the design of the White City-Sandy Canal Trail.

RESOLUTION NO. 18-04-02                                                         Date: April 5, 2018

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING AND ADOPTING AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN
       WHITE CITY AND SANDY CITY FOR THE DESIGN OF THE WHITE CITY-
       SANDY CANAL TRAIL

                                           RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (“Council”) is a Metro Township Council pursuant
      to UCA §§ 10-3b-501 et al.

   C. Sandy City (“Sandy”) is a City and neighboring municipality to White City.

   D. Sandy and White City desire to promote the development of a trail along the confluence
      of the decommissioned Sandy Canal.

   E. As a part of the development of the trail, a design of the trail is needed to determine trail
      aesthetics and the overall costs to construct.

   F. Partnering with Sandy via an interlocal agreement allows White City and Sandy to more
      effectively and efficiently achieve work towards the common goal of constructing the trail.

                                         RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the Council
approves and adopts the attached Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between White City and
Sandy City for the design of the White City-Sandy Canal Trail, hereinafter to be known as
Attachment “A” to this resolution, for the mutual benefit of White City and Sandy.

APPROVED and ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, State of Utah
on this 5th Day of April, 2018.
