Citizen Public Input

                Betzaida Perez spoke under “Citizen Public Input” stating she was trying to build
a carport next to her house, but the Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services
Division told her she could not do it because she would not have a setback of eight feet from the
finished carport to the property line. She felt there was no reason for this.

                                               1
DATE           MONDAY                        JULY                                         9, 2018


               Curtis Woodward, Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services
Division, stated the setback is required due to fire concerns. Every home on the block has a
minimum distance between the wall of the house or carport to the property line to create a natural
fire break. There are two ways the property owner can get around this requirement. The first way
would be an ordinance change to allow for smaller setbacks. The second way is to request a
variance where the property owner would have to establish a hardship that is unique to the
property allowing for an exception to the setback requirement.

             Council Member Snow asked what the distance was to the property line from
where the edge of the wall would be.

               Robert Perez stated it was eight feet in the front and five feet in the back. The
variance option would cost a lot of money, which he did not have.

                Mr. Woodward stated it is possible to have a detached carport closer to the
property line if the structure is built at least six feet away from the house and completely in the
backyard.

             Mr. Perez stated that option would cost him an additional $5,000 to extend the
driveway. He did not have the money do to that.

               Mr. Woodward asked if the property line was at an angle. He asked if that was
why the structure was closer to the property line in the back than it was in the front.

               Mr. Perez stated no; the property line is straight.

               Mayor Bush asked if someone from the Planning and Development Services
Division could go out and look at this property to see if the issue could be resolved.

Public Hearings

              Application #30647 - Randy Moore on behalf of the Church of Jesus Christ of
Latter-day Saints (LDS) requesting a rezone from R-1-43 to R-1-5. The 5.73 acre property is
located at 5944 South Cougar Lane.

               David White, Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services Division,
stated the rezone request follows the general plan for this area. Concerns have been received
relating to the layout of the development and who owns what parcels. The Kearns Metro
Township Planning Commission recommended approval of this request.

              Randy Moore stated this development would fit with the area. The adjacent
property owner does not have any issues with this. Access to this property would be off Cougar
Lane between the Youth Village and the LDS chapel.

DATE           MONDAY                        JULY                                        9, 2018

              Council Member Snow asked if the road would back the existing homes or if
additional homes would be built and then the road put in.

             Mr. Moore stated many different options have been looked at, but right now the
road would come off Cougar Lane and then transition quickly to the north so the existing homes
would be backed with new homes.

             Mr. White stated the hearing tonight is for the Council to consider the rezone
request. Density, roadway, and the subdivision plot would come before the Kearns Metro
Township Planning Commission for review.

               Council Member Snow asked what the lot size would be.

              Mr. Moore stated 5,000 square feet. The area is currently zoned for agricultural
use with one acre lots.

               Mayor Bush asked if the lots would be bigger or smaller than the adjacent
property.

               Mr. White stated they would be 2,000 square feet smaller.

               The public hearings were opened.

               No one appeared in favor of or in opposition to this request.

              Application #30648 – Brian Weyman on behalf of the Church of Jesus Christ of
Latter-day Saints (LDS) requesting acceptance of a roadway dedication plat located at
approximately 5025 West 4865 South.

                Curtis Woodward, Salt Lake County Planning and Development Division, stated
this is road dedication that should have taken place several years ago when the Camp Kearns
industrial area was first approved. It was a requirement by the planning commission at that time.
However, it was never dedicated. When going through the Camp Kearns dedication plat, the LDS
Church realized it owned the road, but did not want it. The dedication of this street would follow
up with a requirement that was made years ago.

               No one appeared in favor or in opposition to this request.

               Council Member Brown, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to approve
Application #30648 accepting a roadway dedication plat located at approximately 5025 West 4865
South. The motion passed unanimously.

               Application #30665 – Salt Lake County to vacate a portion of a public street
located at approximately 5375 South Street between 4250 and 4280 West to accommodate the
development of a new public library campus.

Division, stated the request is to vacate a portion of a public street for the Kearns Library Campus.
The location of the new library is across the street from the current library. The current library will
be torn down to accommodate parking. If the road was not closed, then patrons would have to
cross the street to get to the new library. This would not be safe.

Street between 4250 and 4280 West to accommodate the development of a new public library
campus. The motion passed unanimously.

            The Council reviewed the following ordinance vacating a portion of a public street

ORDINANCE NO. 18-07-01                                                        Date: July 9, 2018




                                               7
DATE           MONDAY                         JULY                                           9, 2018

       AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PORTION OF A PUBLIC STREET KNOWN AS
       5375 SOUTH STREET BETWEEN APPROXIMATELY 4250 AND 4280 WEST IN
       THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP

       The Kearns Metro Township Council, as the legislative body of Kearns Metro Township,
State of Utah, ordains as follows:

       Section I:

   1) A portion of the platted street, which is more fully described in Exhibit A attached hereto
      and incorporated herein by reference, is hereby vacated.

   2) The purpose of the vacation is to allow Salt Lake County to incorporate the land into its
      existing adjacent parcels for the purposes of developing a new library.

   3) This ordinance is based upon a determination by the Kearns Metro Township Council
      following a public hearing on June, 2018, that due and proper notice of the hearing to
      vacate the platted street segment was duly given according to law; that good cause exists
      for the vacation; and that neither the public interest nor any person will be materially injured
      by the vacation; that fair and adequate consideration shall be provided; and that the metro
      township has no present or foreseeable future public need for the portion of the alley being
      vacated, or for any public purpose other than the library.

   4) Pursuant to Section 14.48.030 of the Kearns Metro Code of Ordinances, the vacation of
      this portion of the street to accommodate a safer library campus is adequate
      compensation for the metro’s interest.

   5) All right, title and interest in and to the specified portions of the street being vacated are
      to revert or otherwise be conveyed, by operation of state law and local ordinances, to the
      abutting property owner, Salt Lake County.

   6) This ordinance shall have no force or effect to impair any other existing easements or
      rights-or-way for public utilities, public franchises, irrigation, storm drainage, or other such
      easements, as presently exist under, over, or upon the vacated portions of said street, or
      as are or may be shown on the official plats and records of the County.

   7) The Salt Lake County Recorder is hereby directed to record this ordinance and
      accompanying exhibits, and to make the necessary changes on the official plats and
      records of the County to reflect said ordinance.

        Section II:    This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon
at least one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in Kearns
Metro Township, and if not so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall take effect
immediately upon its first publication.

      IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Kearns Metro Township Council has approved, passed and
adopted this ordinance this 9th day of July, 2018.
