PUBLIC HEARINGS
Hearings began at – 8:40 a.m.
30939 – (Continued from July 11, August 15, and September 12, 2019) - Ordinance Amendments –
Enactment of an administrative code enforcement process to be codified in Title 12 of the Emigration Metro
Township Municipal Code.
Commissioners had a brief discussion regarding changes made with their comments and incorporated
changes. Commissioners had additional changes to the code.
1) 12.3.320 (1) a. Should read - Does not abate a violation.
2) 12.3.320 (1) b. Delete “The responsible person”.
3) 12.4.410 should read – The fund shall be reimbursed by collection from the responsible person
and/or property owner. Delete the word property from the sentence.
Commissioner Karkut motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Wallace seconded that motion.

2) General Plan Discussion
Mr. Quintanilla provided the changes made last night to the Vision Statement. Emigration Trails working
group. 2007 has workable trails and will be proposed to the council and there were no changes.
Commissioner Pinon asked if the trails working group would present to council and Commissioner
Wallace’s concern on bikers and hikers effect the wildlife. Commissioner Karkut asked priority on
trailheads and trails. Commissioner Pinon said plan allows for anything to be a study but no prioritization.
Commissioner Wallace said easiest to complete is Perkins Flat and others were lower priority and a lot
harder to put together. Commissioner Pinon said additional trails in the canyon on private property and
not part Perkins Flat plan, and is not part of Utah open lands portfolio, and the easement were never
dedicated. Next meeting is focused on section three, looking at goals and objectives on chapter one, and
looking at chapter three the projects posted in 2012 and what is on the docket for the future, five through
eleven. Mr. Quintanilla said trails working group on priorities they have a color-coded recommendation
system. Confirmed next general plan steering committee meeting is December 3rd and planning
commissioner December 12th.
3) Other Business Items (as needed)
Welcomed Commissioner Harpst to the planning Commission. Ms. Davies introduced herself as a
new long-range planner with the MSD.
Commissioner Harpst motioned to adjourn, Commissioner Karkut seconded that motion.
