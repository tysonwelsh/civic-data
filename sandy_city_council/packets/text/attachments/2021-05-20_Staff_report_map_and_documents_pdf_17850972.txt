REQUEST
Ms. Natalie Nichols, of Anderson, Wahlen and Associates Engineers, representing Spencer
Connelly, of The DRH Company, is requesting preliminary commercial Site Plan Review
for a proposed Dutch Bros Coffee Drive-up Window Restaurant, located in the
Community Commercial (CC) zoning district. The site is currently used as a Chevron brand
self-service gas station and convenience store with a car wash, located at 9401 S. 700 East
Street, which will be demolished to make way for this new project. A drive-up window
restaurant is a permitted use in the (CC) zoning district. Building architectural design
review approval is also required. The Planning Commission is the land-use authority for
these requested actions.


BACKGROUND
The site consists of two parcels totaling 0.44 acres. This gas station has existed on the northwest
corner of the Sandy Mall development since 1979, but is a stand-alone property, never having been

included in the Sandy Mall Development nor in the subsequent Sandy Mall Subdivision which was
platted in 2016, due to different property ownerships. The property was recently purchased by The
DRH Company from the prior owner, in anticipation of this new use. All property in this area of 700

Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify

due to the lack of residential zoning in the area.

ANALYSIS
Pedestrian and Vehicle Access and On-site Circulation. All street improvements and utilities
are existing from the prior service station development and a previous UDOT widening project along
700 East Street. However, the City Engineer has a street intersection widening project in a
preliminary design stage for this intersection of 9400 South and 700 East. This project is not finalized
nor is it funded but is expected to be ready for construction in the next several years. As such,
additional street right-of-way will be needed from the north side of this project area (approximately
4 feet) for 9400 South Street widening. The proposed site plan has been adjusted to reflect the future
acquisition of the necessary street widening property on the north side of the site. However, with
the City street widening project pending, the City Engineer has elected to allow the current cub, gutter
and sidewalk improvements to remain in place until reconstruction occurs by the City project.

With the current gas station use, customers can enter the property via two driveway approaches: one
from 700 East that is located about mid-property and a second driveway approach located as a
shared driveway with the Sandy Mall property, from 9400 South Street. There is also reciprocal
access from the parking lot of the Sandy Mall property to the east of this property, which will remain
with the new design. The existing driveway approach from 700 East Street is too close to the
intersection and the City Transportation Engineer is enforcing its elimination in the new project.

The new drive-up window coffee restaurant use will do the vast majority of its business through the
drive-up window. Thus the customer vehicle queuing lanes will dominate the new site. The building
is being pushed to the north to accommodate the maximum vehicle queuing possible on the property.
Double drive-up lanes are proposed to enable sufficient vehicle stacking capacity. Although there is
no inside seating in the prototype 950 square foot building, there will be a walk-up window for
pedestrian customers located on the north side of the building.

Pedestrian access will be greatly improved when compared to the existing development. ADA
compliant pedestrian sidewalks will connect to the 9400 South Street sidewalk and to the mainly
employee parking located in the center of the site. Pedestrian access will be adequate for the
development.

Zoning Review. The project will meet all of the requirements of the (CC) zoning district and the
Sandy City Development code. Parking for this project will meet the City requirements on site.
All required City Departments and Divisions have reviewed and recommend approval, subject to
their usual requirements and conditions.

Architectural Design Review. The building's design, materials and colors were reviewed by
the Sandy Planning Staff as to compliance with the Sandy City Architectural Design
Requirements. This "Branded" building fully meets the Sandy City Architectural Design

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan review is
complete for the proposed Dutch Bros Coffee Drive-up Window Restaurant project, and
approve the building's architectural design, materials and colors as proposed, based upon the
following two findings and the following seven conditions:

FINDINGS.
    A.  That the proposed project meets or will meet all the applicable requirements of the
        Sandy City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as
        to building architectural design, materials and colors.
    B.  That re-development of this property is beneficial at this intersection location and
        long term vehicle traffic will be improved by the elimination of the non-compliant
        700 East Street drive approach and the intersection improvement planned by the
        City.

CONDITIONS.
    1.  That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior
        to the start of any construction. The final site plan shall comply with all Development
        Code requirements and any modifications required or allowed by the Planning
        Commission.

    2.  That the development will comply with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety
        Codes applicable to this type of use.

    3.  That the developer be responsible to meet all provisions of the (CC) zoning district
        and the Sandy City Architectural Design Requirements with the details finalized
        with staff during final site plan review.

    4.  All existing utility boxes (i.e. transformers, switchgear, telephone, cable TV, etc.) shall
        be shown on the site plan and all new development required utility boxes shall be
        placed underground or moved behind the front setback of the buildings and screened
        from view. Each box shall be shown in its exact location and shall be noted with its
        exact height, width and length. Building utility meters shall be mounted to the side or
        rear elevation and screened.

    5.  That the developer be responsible for the placement of a temporary 6 foot high chain
        link fence around the perimeter of the project during the construction phase of the
        project for security. Said fence shall also be required to include fabric to prohibit
        blowing dust problems, if it becomes necessary or if it is required by the Community
        Development Department during Site Plan Review.

    6.  That the applicant comply with all department requirements as noted in all
        Preliminary Review letters prior to submittal for final site plan review with staff.
