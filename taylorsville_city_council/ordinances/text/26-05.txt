                                TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                 ORDINANCE         NO. 26- 05


    AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE AMENDING
 TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE TITLE 11: VEHICLES AND TRAFFIC

        WHEREAS,      the Taylorsville   City Council ( the " Council")   met in regular session

on April 15, 2026, to discuss, among other things, approving proposed amendments to
Title 11 Vehicles and Traffic of the Taylorsville City Code and;

        WHEREAS, the City of Taylorsville maintains regulations governing stopping,
standing, and parking on public streets and rights- of-way to promote public safety, reduce
obstructions to traffic and emergency access, improve neighborhood livability, and ensure
fair and consistent enforcement; and


        WHEREAS, the existing regulations in Title 11. 20 were last significantly
updated several years ago, and the City has determined that updates are necessary to
clarify definitions, correct inconsistencies, modernize certain parking restrictions,
improve enforcement provisions, and better address current community needs such as
recreational vehicle parking, commercial vehicle restrictions, and snow removal; and

        WHEREAS, the proposed amendments to Title 11 Vehicles and Traffic are set
forth in the attached hereto as Exhibit" A" and incorporated herein by this reference; and

        WHEREAS, the City Administration has presented the proposed amendments to
the Council and recommends their approval; and


        WHEREAS, the effective management and enforcement of parking regulations is
essential for traffic safety, emergency vehicle access, and the overall quality of life in
Taylorsville;   and


          WHEREAS, after due consideration and public input, the Council has determined
that it is in the best interest of the general health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of
Taylorsville to approve the proposed amendments to Title 11.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed amendments to Title 11 Vehicles and Traffic of the Taylorsville City Code, as
set forth in Exhibit" A" attached hereto, are hereby approved.



        This Ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 26- 05, shall take effect upon passage and
approval.


        PASSED AND APPROVED by the Taylorsville City Council this 15th day of
April, 2026.
