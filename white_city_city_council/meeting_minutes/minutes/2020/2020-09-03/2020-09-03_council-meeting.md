       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN INTERLOCAL
       COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY, TOWN OF ALTA,
       TOWN OF BRIGHTON, BLUFFDALE CITY, COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP,
       COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY, DRAPER CITY, EMIGRATION CANYON
       METRO TOWNSHIP, HERRIMAN CITY, HOLLADAY CITY, KEARNS METRO
       TOWNSHIP, MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP, MIDVALE CITY CORP., CITY OF
       MILLCREEK, MURRAY CITY, RIVERTON CITY, AND CITY OF SOUTH SALT
       LAKE RELATING TO THE CONDUCT OF THE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT
       BLOCK GRANT PROGRAM, EMERGENCY SOLUTIONS GRANT PROGRAM
       AND THE HOME INVESTMENT PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM

The White City Metro Township Council, acting as the legislative governing body of the White City
Metro Township, resolves as follows:

        WHERAS, Salt Lake County (“County”) participates as an “urban county,” as defined by
federal regulation, in the Community Development Block Grant (“CDBG”), Emergency Solutions
Grant (“ESG”), and the HOME Investment Partnership (“HOME”) programs administered by the
U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (“HUD”); and

       WHEREAS, the County has previously entered into three distinct interlocal cooperation
agreements with participating municipalities within Salt Lake County that did not receive separate
CDBG, ESG, and HOME program entitlement grants governing the Parties participation in the
CDBG, ESG and HOME programs which fell short of strictly complying with all HUD-imposed
requirements; and

        WHEREAS, the County now desires to proceed with a single interlocal agreement with all
participating municipalities, including White City Metro Township, which does strictly comply with
all HUD-imposed requirements and which supersedes and terminates effective upon all CDBG,
ESG, and HOME funds and income received in the three-year period ending June 30, 2021 are
expended and the funded activities completed, the following interlocal agreements between the
County and the above referenced cities, which pertain to participating in the urban county for
CDBG, ESG, and HOME Investment Partnership Programs: Salt Lake County Contract No.
BV9303C, Salt Lake County Contract No. BV03192C, and Salt Lake County Contract No.
BV043108; and

      WHEREAS, an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement (“Agreement”) has been prepared for
approval and execution by and between County and participating municipalities, which states the
purposes thereof, and the extent of the required participation of the parties and the rights, duties,
responsibilities, and obligations of the parties in the conduct and administration of the CDBG,
ESG, and HOME programs as specified therein; and

         WHEREAS, under the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Annotated, 11-13-101
et seq. (2020) any two or more public agencies may enter into agreements with one another for
joint or cooperative action and may also contract with each other to perform any governmental
service activity or taking which each public agency entering into the contract is authorized by law
to perform.

Council Member Reports

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

              Council Member Perry stated UFA is requesting $8,380 in CARES Act funding
from White City Metro Township to cover personnel and PPE. UFA adopted a new code of ethics
policy and is working on the strategic plan presentations. UFA discussed the updates with
Versaterm, the computer aided dispatch (CAD) system, and worked out any potential issues.

UFA has a training facility in Magna with structures that are nearing the end of its useful life and
need replacement. Ivory Homes wants the training facility property and is willing to exchange
property. The current UFA property is near a residential property and the potentially new property
would be near a more industrial area.

UFA has a lot of good support and behavior and wellness program specialists that help firefighters
and staff address situation they face every day.

The current UFSA District Manager has taken a job out of state which will leave a vacancy for this
position. Analysis of fire stations to determine if they need to be replaced or rebuilt continues to
move forward. Herriman City is leaving UFSA and will be contracting with UFA directly.

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

             Mayor Flint stated with Taylorsville City leaving UPD and SLVLESA the board is
reviewing how big of an impact that will have. A five-year budget plan is being discussed and
worked through. UPD has been able to give a small cost of living and merit increase to officers.
There are some problematic concerns that have warranted a committee to study the issues.

                                   −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                 Council Member Price stated a committee to audit the fraud risk assessment was
created and will have a proposal in December. Conflict of interest disclosures will need to be
completed annually. There have been two reported West Nile virus areas in Salt Lake County
with five total areas in Utah and no human cases.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

                Mayor Flint stated the board has a lot of meetings lately to discuss the budget.
Salt Lake County departments have been presenting its budget requests. There has been an
impact on sales tax; however, White City has seen an increase in sales tax because more people
are ordering items online and that sales tax goes to White City. The 2021 budget has not been
finalized but should be by the end of this month.
