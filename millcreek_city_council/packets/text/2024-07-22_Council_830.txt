   b. ZT-24-004,             Proposed             Updates          to the Wireless            Telecommunication                      Facilities      Code in
         Chapter        18.75;         Brad      Sanderson,             Current       Planning          Manager
         Sanderson         said wireless              telecorninunication             facilities,      wl'ffle     infrequently          applied      for, can be
         contentious         depending            on their location             and design.          The last major           installation         was a 60-
         foot-tall      facility      near Home            Depot,       which     has been noted for its unobtrusive                          appearance.
         The current         code regulating               these facilities         has not seen significant                  updates        in decades and
         mostly       involves         minor      changes,         primarily       to definitions.         Due to federal             regulations            by the
         FCC,        cities have limited               authority      over safety          and design,        leading        to the proposal            of
         removing         many         conditional         use requirements                in favor     of c}e:arer standards.              The updated
         code will       now include              specific       guidelines        for wall-mounted,                roof-mounted,            freestanding
         steel structures,            and monopoles,               eliminating        conditional          uses. Wall          and roof mounts                 will
         have stricter         standards          regarding         height      and placement,            with      anteru'ias required             to be close
         to the wall        and set back further                 from roof        edges. New           provisions           include      restrictions          on the
         visibility      of these structures                  and introducing         standards         for freestanding              structures        like
         flagpoles        and water towers,                designed       to blend         into the environment.                The aim is to ensure
         that installations                are aesthetically         pleasing       while     meeting         necessary        operational           standards.


         Mayor        Silvestrini           asked about regulating                poles differently              when       they are located          on
         sensitive       land or natural               landscapes.       There      was an existing               one that was controversial                     on
