1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner
Britton from the meeting.

as Planning Commissioner and thanked him for his time and commitment for serving as a member

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Presentation by Allen Matheson on The Point development.
Alan Matheson came to discuss the Point of the Mountain. RFQ and RFP process. Alan had a
PowerPoint that he used to present to the Planning Commission. thepointutah.org has a lot of the

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of the Diane & Robert Myers request for the
Myers’ Zone Change on 0.97-acres of property located at 2269 North 600 West changing the
zoning from R-1-12 to R-1-8.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Diane and Robert Myers, was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Kaylynn Osborne does not feel that the request meets the requirements of the general plan and
goals in the City because of the City Animal Rights code. She stated the applicants asked for a
variance which was granted. She inquired if the item is passed if the ADU is void because the
request would be different. She asked for some clarification on the application process.

Michelle Stallings stated she is aware that the discussion is a zone change, but she is concerned
about the flag lot that has the same intent. She asked where the driveway would be located for the
ADU and asked about the ADU conditional use permit. She stated there would be three access
points to the lot but feels it will create issues.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Craig Chambers stated it would need to be looked at in the future but would not affect this specific
item.

The applicant, Diane and Robert Myers, addressed the Commissioners and answered clarifying
questions.

Commissioner Nielsen re-opened the public hearing.

Kaylynn Osborne asked about the measurements of the sliver of property next to the applicants.
She stated the County does not have it recorded and needs to be readjusted.

Commissioner Nielsen re-closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to recommend approval of the zone change with the
    findings and DRC Comments. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.8) Public hearing and recommendation of Howland Partners’ request for review of the
Lehi Pointe Phase 4 concept, a 125-unit residential condominium and townhome
development on approximately 14.5 acres located at 3450 North Digital Drive in a
Commercial zone.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Everett asked if the 125 units were part of the proposal. Brittney clarified they were
included. Commissioner Nielsen asked about parking and open space and if they meet the
requirements. Commissioner Eyre asked about the BRT rail. Brittney stated the intent was for the
BRT rail to be built. Mike West stated they will help with connection to the rail trail and are
working out the details with UTA.

The applicant, Tina Franco with Howland Partners, was present. She stated they would like to see
if they can get a walkout onto the trail at the third floor of the building. There is a stub connection
and she stated it will be revised due to a 30 foot wall. Lehi City suggested angled parking. Tina
stated they have included a revised plan with what the architect is intending on doing with that
area. The development is planning on two levels of parking for the units. Commissioner Newall
liked the idea of having a garage on the second level.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Dusty Pyne stated he would like clarification with the stalls and parking and if the lot to the East
is considered shared parking.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

The applicant, Tina Franco, stated Longhorn requires 127 stalls for this site and that parking would
be for Longhorn. All 316 parking spaces are under the apartments and whichever tenant occupies
the space will take the North parking area. Commissioner Hereth asked about a prior parking
structure that was approved. Tina stated they decided to go another direction with that.

Commissioner Eyre asked about a pedestrian bridge. Ross Dinsdale stated pedestrian bridges are
costly, but that UTA has shifted focus and it may be a possibility.

    Motion: Commissioner Hereth moved to send a positive recommendation of Howland
    Partners’ request for review of the Lehi Pointe Phase 4 concept, a 125-unit residential
    condominium and townhome development on approximately 14.5 acres located at 3450
    North Digital Drive in a Commercial zone; with the findings that the proposed concept will
    provide a walkable, mixed-use development near a regional trail system and in close proximity
    to future transit, that the concept is consistent with the Lehi City Development Code, and
    conforms to the goals and policies of the General Plan. Including all DRC comments.
    Commissioner Ellis seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
