CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to Insituform Technologies for the 2023
        Sewer Relining Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: As part of the Wastewater Collections
          on-going maintenance program, old and failing clay sewer collection pipes are
          identified through our video inspection program and scheduled to be re-lined with
          a PVC pipe liner. While re-lining the pipeline is typically more cost per foot than
          the conventional open-dig method, it is a viable and cost-effective solution as it
          can be done without cutting the asphalt and digging in the streets, which requires
          repaving and restoration of surface improvements. Staff recommends awarding
          the bid to Insituform Technologies.

      b. Consider approval to award bid to Feller Enterprises for the construction
         of the concrete flatwork for the Bloomington Hills Playground
         Replacement Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Last year (2022), the primary play
          structure at Bloomington Hills Park (Redwood Tree Park) had to be removed for
          safety issues due to: decay and failure of the structure’s protective safety
          coatings, the age of the structure, replacement parts were no longer available.
          This bid award is for the playground replacement/renovation, phase 1. The scope
          of work for this part includes: 30’ feet of curb and gutter on the west side of
          Redwood Tree Circle, an ADA ramp, a sidewalk up to the play area, a 6’ foot wide
          sidewalk apron around the play area, and a connecting sidewalk to the existing
          pavilion. It also includes the associated excavation work required to prepare the
          play area for the installation of the new play equipment and safety surfacing.
          Staff recommends awarding the bid to Feller Enterprises.

      c. Consider approval to award bid to Interstate Rock Products Inc. for the
         construction of the Little Valley Park Artificial Turf Fields.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The soccer fields at Little Valley were
           first installed in 2016 and have hosted many sports events and practices since.
           The replacement of Fields 1, 2 and 3 with synthetic turf has been designed by
           Park Planning with Civil Science and Lloyd Sports. The project was put out to bid
           on January 30, 2023; four (4) bids were received and opened on February
           17,2023. Interstate Rock Products is the responsible low bidder in the amount of
           $3,365,956.13. This amount includes the Base Bid (Fields 1 & 2 and Additive
           Alternate 2 (Field 3). Staff recommends approval pending adoption of the
           associated budget amendment.

      d. Consider approval to purchase P25 Compliance 700/800Mhz public-
         safety radios for the Saint George Police Department and 911 Center
         pursuant to State Contract # AR3841.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The state of Utah is currently replacing
           its public-safety radio system which includes the core infrastructure but also end
           user equipment which has been in service since the 2002 Winter Olympics.
           These radios are sole sourced and being purchased through Utah State Contract
           # AR3841. Staff recommends approval pending adoption of the associated
           budget amendment.

      e. Consider approval of the renewal of the Concessions Agreement with the
         Ice Cream Factory Vending for the airport terminal.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Ice Cream Factory Vending has been
           providing vending services at the airport since 2016. This is a renewal of the
           concessions agreement. Staff recommends approval.

      f.   Consider approval of fee waiver for Leadership Dixie Graduation at the
           Dixie Academy.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Leadership Dixie has asked to have the
           waived for the use of Dixie Academy on May 11, 2023 from 4 pm to 10 pm. The
           fee waiver would be $600.

      g. Consider approval of a fee waiver for the Utah League of Cities and
         Towns BBQ event on Thursday, April 20, 2023 at Vernon Worthen Park.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City of St. George is a member of
           the Utah League of Cities and Towns and is the host city for this annual mid-year
           conference. In the past, the City has waived fees (special event fee and park
           rental fee) for this BBQ which has taken place at Town Square, Confluence and
           Worthen Park. Staff recommends waving the 1) Park reservation fee of $400
           and 2) the Special Event fee of $150.
