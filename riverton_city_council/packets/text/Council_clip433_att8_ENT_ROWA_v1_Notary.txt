IN CONSIDERATION of the foregoing hereinafter set forth, it is mutually agreed by the parties as follows:

1. The grantor hereby agrees to convey and sell by Warranty Deed a portion (approximately 3,089 square feet or
    about 0.071 acres) of the parcel of land known as parcel number 27-31-200-062. This is a voluntary sale to
    Riverton City. It is not subject to condemnation.
2. Grantor shall transfer and convey the property free of all liens and encumbrances except recorded easements.
3. Grantor shall indemnify and hold harmless Grantee from and against any and all claims, demands and actions,
    including costs, from lien holders or lessees of the property.
4. Grantor shall leave the property in the same condition as it was when this contract was signed. No work,
    improvement, or alteration will be done to the property other than what is provided for in this agreement.
5. All fixtures are to remain with the property.
6. As this is a voluntary sale, the Grantors waive any right they have to a “first right of refusal” on any surplus
    property not used for the proposed highway or other city purposes.
7. The purchase price is based upon an Appraisal Report prepared by Nexus Realty dated October 27, 2017.
8. Riverton City will select a Title Insurance Company to complete this transaction.
9. Riverton City will pay closing costs for this transaction such as recording fees, buyer’s title insurance policy
    premiums, and closing fees.
10. Riverton will NOT pay fees or expenses related to the Grantors obtaining or recording of releases or re-
    conveyance of existing liens or other encumbrances.
11. The grantors may continue to use the property conveyed to Riverton City in a manner similar to the existing use
    until Riverton City is prepared to take possession. The City may take possession at any time and is not liable for
    lost or damaged improvements across the City.
12. The property is part of the future planned widening of 12600 South Street. Any discussion between the grantor
    and the City regarding the design and construction of 12600 South Street was preliminary or conceptual in
    nature and is subject to change.
13. This Agreement is a complete agreement and final understanding between the parties. Other prior discussion
    representations, agreements or other considerations expressed between the parties but not made a part of this
    Agreement are not binding upon any party to this Agreement. Grantor understands this agreement is an option
    until approved by Riverton City.
