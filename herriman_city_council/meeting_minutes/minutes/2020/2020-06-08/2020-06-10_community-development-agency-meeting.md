                                  COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND
                                RENEWAL AGENCY OF HERRIMAN CITY
                                           MINUTES
                                                  Wednesday, June 10, 2020
                                                   Approved July 28, 2020

The following are the minutes of the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City. The
meeting was held on Wednesday, June 10, 2020 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West
Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City
Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair David Watts

Directors Present:          Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith

Staff Present: City Manager Brett geo. Wood, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Assistant City Manager Wendy
               Thomas, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Alan Rae, Community Development
               Director Blake Thomas, Chief of Police Troy Carr, City Planner Michael Maloy, and
               Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette.

7:00 PM: (OR AS SOON AS POSSIBLE THEREAFTER)
   1. Call to Order
Chair Watts called the meeting to order at 7:45 p.m.

                  1.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes of June
                        10, 2020
Director Smith moved to have the minutes of June 10, 2020 be prepared and distributed to each member of the Community
Development and Renewal Agency Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary,
if after ten days, there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes
are made and the board is in agreement. Director Henderson seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

    2. Discussion and Action Items
           2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution of the Community Development and Renewal
                Agency of Herriman City to adopt the final budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2020
                and ending June 30, 2021 – Alan Rae, Finance Director
Finance Director Alan Rae indicated the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City has
three differed Community Development Project Areas: Herriman Towne Centre, Herriman Industrial Park and
Anthem. He noted all of the bond payments have been being paid through the Tax Increment Fund and added the
Herriman Business Park does receive property taxes. The City is currently waiting for supporting documentation
from a developer to clarify costs associated with the project Impact Fees and the Tax Increment Fund.

Director Rae noted the Anthem project area has not been budged to distribute expenditures. Documentation has
been received and verified, and it is being requested those expenditures be approved as part of the budget.

Director Shields moved to approve Resolution No. R2020-04 adopting the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman
City Final Budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2020 and ending June 30, 2021 with the inclusion to distribute $244,937.17
Anthem Development. Director Ohrn seconded the motion.

The vote is recorded as follows:
Director Jared Henderson                           Aye
Director Steven Shields                            Aye
Director Sherrie Ohrn                              Aye
Director Clint Smith                               Aye
Chair David Watts                                  Aye
The motion passed unanimously.

     3. Adjournment
Director Smith moved to adjourn the Community and Development Renewal Agency meeting at 7:49 p.m. Director Henderson
seconded the motion, and all voted aye.


I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true, accurate
and complete record of the meeting held on June 10, 2020. This document constitutes the official minutes for the
Community Development and Renewal Agency Meeting.
