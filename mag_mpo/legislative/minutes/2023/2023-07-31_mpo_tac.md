Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Items – Bob Allen, MAG 2024 TIP Adoption The Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) MAG is responsible for preparing and approving a Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) for the area. The TIP lists transportation projects and programs within the MPO for the fiscal years 2024–2029. Conceptual projects are listed through 2030. All regional projects, regardless of the selection agency, must be approved by the MPO committees and be listed in the TIP. MPO Technical Advisory Committee July 31, 2023 Page 3 TIP includes more than 200 projects totaling over $1.7B. Major projects include 2100 North in Lehi, Mountainview Corridor, FrontRunner improvements, Payson Main St interchange, and numerous trail improvements. The TIP can be viewed at: mountainland.org/tip Andy Spencer moved to recommend that the 2024 TIP be adopted as presented. Brad Kenson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. TIP Modification - Lehi 2300 West Approval In 2020, Lehi was awarded $7,376,300 in Local Transportation Tax (Count) funds to widen 2300 West from 2100 North to Main Street. The project is 1.85 miles long and includes a 5 -lane cross-section and intersection improvements at 2100 North. The project is intended to begin construction in January of 2024, and costs have risen considerably since the project was initially selected due to inflation. Primarily costs related to: • Traffic Control and Mobilization • Earthwork (embankment, grading, and base course) • Precast Concrete (irrigation pipe and boxes) • Asphalt Surfacing Lehi has already acquired additional funds from UDOT. However, an additional $4.85M is needed to complete the project. Lehi also requests a minor scope change to add a HAWK signal in front of North Point Elementary School and either a Hawk signal at the Waist Ditch Trail or trail improvements at 300 North to accommodate Westfield Jr High. These minor scope changes total $390,000. Study and Project Updates Pioneer Crossing Study - Claire Woodman, Horrocks UDOT, in partnership with MAG; the Cities of American Fork, Lehi, Saratoga Springs, and Eagle Mountain; and Utah County, initiated the Pioneer Crossing Arterial Study in December 2022 to develop and evaluate concepts for expansion along this critical east-west corridor in northern Utah County. The study is a planning-level effort intended to gather data and information on potential expansion concepts to further understand high-level trade-offs related to cost, travel time, travel demand, property impacts, and other related factors. The study team has also developed and evaluated concepts for Meadows Crossing, in proximity to Pioneer Crossing. See presentation slides for details Safe Streets for All federal grant update - Jim Price, MAG In February of 2023 MAG was awarded $1,000,000 from the USDOT to complete a Safety Action Plan (SAP) covering Utah, Wasatch, and Summit Counties. The SAP is a prerequisite for applications for future SS4A Implementation Grants. Staff conducted a competitive Request for Proposals process and awarded the contract to Parametrix Consultants. Parametrix is teaming with Avenue Consultants and Toole Group. MAG has also executed a funding agreement with USDOT and will administer the grant. The Project timeframe for completion is approximately July 2023 to September 2024. See presentation slides for details RAISE First/Last Mile federal grant update - Jim Price, MAG Rebuilding American Infrastructure with Sustainability and Equity (RAISE) is a USDOT Transportation Infrastructure grant program that has also been known as TIGER in past years. In 2015 MAG partnered with UDOT, UTA, and Wasatch Front Regional Council to apply for and receive approximately $20 million for a series of First Mile/Last Mile infrastructure projects along the Wasatch Front. Both the Lehi Rail Trail Bridge over SR 92 and the Provo FrontRunner Pedestrian MPO Technical Advisory Committee July 31, 2023 Page 5 Bridge were recipients of these funds. The Lehi Bridge was completed in August 2021 and the Provo Bridge is under construction with a scheduled completion of November 2023. In 2022 these same partners worked together to apply for RAISE funds for similar projects along the Wasatch Front and were awarded $24.5 million. In the MAG area, this will provide approximately $5.5 million for bicycle and pedestrian improvements along the Provo University Avenue and 200 W corridors to increase safe access to UVX. UDOT will be administering the funds and will work closely with Provo City and MAG on project design and delivery. Transportation Investment Fund (TIF, TTIF) Nomination Process - LaNiece Davenport, MAG Local governments and transit districts are invited to nominate qualifying transportation projects for prioritization and possible funding by the Utah Transportation Commission. The Utah Transportation Commission uses a project prioritization process to in form funding decision -making for the Transportation Investment Fund (TIF) and the Transit Transportation Investment Fund (TTIF) which includes this project nomination process. Visit the UDOT Project Prioritization to learn more. Visit the UDOT Project Nomination page to nominate a project. Nominations are required for TIF Active Transportation and the two TTIF programs since a match is required as part of the eligibility. For the TIF Highway and TTIF Transit funding programs, phase 1 projects from the Utah Unified Transportation Plan are automatically considered which includes phase 1 fiscally constrained projects from each of Utah’s MPOs including MAG and the UDOT Long Range Transportation Plan. Eligible projects may be considered for funding by the Utah Transportation Commission at any time once ranked lists have been developed. UDOT is accepting nominations now through August 31, 2023.
