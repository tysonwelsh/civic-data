          A memo from the Management Services Department regarding proposed New Markets Tax Credit (NMTC) came before
the Board for consideration. The memo stated Administration is proposing the Board consider a resolution authorizing the
President to execute a Leverage Loan in the amount of $6.9 million to Broadstreet Fund 28, LLC as part of the NMTC process.
The loan funds will funnel through the NMTC process to Marshall White Community Center (MWCC) NMTC SPE, Inc, a special
purpose entity controlled by the City, and used to reimburse the Municipal Building Authority for the loan. The resolution also
authorizes the execution of all other documents, instruments or agreements reasonably related to the transaction including, but not
limited to, documents relating to leases, indemnities, guarantees, the option agreement, payments, and reimbursements.
          Cody Rogers, an attorney from Stinson LLP, summarized the memo and used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to
expound on NMTC financing opportunities for the MWCC project. He presented a diagram to provide an overview of the
structure of the NMTC Program, which highlighted the role of private investors in the Program. Private investors are incentivized
to make investments in struggling communities in exchange for tax credits that reduce their tax liability. This improves their
return on investment (ROI). He then discussed the amount of funding that has historically been allocated annually for the NMTC
Program, the process an entity must follow to secure NMTC funding, NMTC compliance requirements, and the anticipated net
benefit to Ogden City ($2.1 million) as a result of participation in the Program. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see
the information packet for the meeting.
          An additional memo from the Management Services Department regarding the proposed budget amendment related to
the NMTC loan came before the Board for consideration. The memo stated the proposed amendment totaling $6.9 million dollars
will fund a leverage loan to Broadstreet Investment Fund 28, LLC that will benefit NWCC NMTC SPE, Inc., a special non-profit
entity controlled by the City. The loan will be repaid by MWCC NMTC SPE, Inc, with proceeds from the New Markets Tax
Credit program. Through the federal NMTC program, the City will receive approximately $2.1 Million in investor funding. This
funding will be used to complete elements of the MWCC project that were removed from the scope of work due to budgetary
constraints.
          Finance Director Sorensen summarized the memo and used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to identify the revenue
source in the Municipal Building Authority budget.
