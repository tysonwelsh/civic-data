Lehi City Pony Express Corridor Preservation Request – Jim Price, MAG (1:15:21) The cities of Lehi and American Fork are working on a joint MAG TIP request for funds to construct a portion of the Pony Express Parkway from Lehi Center Street to the proposed Vineyard Connector at approximately 570 W in American Fork. The project is a Phase 1 RTP project and is a Corridor Preservation Approved Corridor (#23). If approved, the project funds would be available in FY 2025 and FY 2026. The preferred alignment for the project impacts multiple properties. Lehi City has begun to contact those owners to understand which ones would be willing sellers in order to utilize Corridor Preservation Funds to purchase as much ROW as possible. Purchasing said properties along this corridor would be within the purpose and need of the Corridor Preservation Fund policy. To expedite the process while assuring all properties are purchased in accordance with applicable regulations, MAG staff recommends that TAC and RPC consider a Pony Express acquisition program using Corridor Preservation Funds similar to those utilized in 2007 for North County Boulevard and in 2019 for Orem 1600 North. For those programs, RPC authorized a total of $6.5 million for MAG staff to purchase needed properties as they became available, without having to seek approvals for individual acquisitions. Staff reported back to TAC and RPC all expenses and acquisitions at the end of the program. For this program, Lehi City requests authorization for up to $6 million and for MAG staff to oversee the acquisition process. Staff will report back to TAC and RPC on program status within 60 days of any expenditure, and at the annual Corridor Preservation Fund Update. Lehi City will become the property owner for all parcels. MAG Staff recommends approval of the proposed ROW acquisition program and that this program be authorized for a period not to exceed 24 months. Corridor Preservation Fund Balance - $9.8 million. Richard Nielson moved that the Technical Advisory Committee favorably recommend approval to the Regional Planning Committee of the described program of ROW acquisition for Pony Express Parkway.
