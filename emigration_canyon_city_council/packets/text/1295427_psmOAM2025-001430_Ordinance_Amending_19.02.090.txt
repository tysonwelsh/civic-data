  AN ORDINANCE OF THE EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL AMENDING
      SECTION 19.02.090 BUILDING AND USE PERMITS REQUIRED


                                        RECITALS
       WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon is a municipality and has authority to adopt land
use regulations, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-9a-501 in accordance with the Municipal Land
Use, Development, and Management Act, Title 10, Section 9a, Utah Code; and
       WHEREAS, the Council deems it necessary to amend a section of the code so that
no uses are exempt from permit;
      WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Municipal Code was adopted based on a
County Code which is not always applicable to the municipality.
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON CITY COUNCIL that:
Section 1. Amended. Title 19, Chapter 02, Section 090 of the Emigration Canyon Code is
hereby amended as follows:
19.02.090 Building And Use Permits Required
Construction, alteration, repair or removal of any building or structure, or any part thereof,
as provided or as restricted in this title, shall not be commenced or proceeded with except
after the issuance of a written permit for the same by the building ofﬁcial. The use of the
land shall not be commenced or proceeded with except upon the issuance of a written
permit for the same by the development services division director or designee. No use
permit shall be required for land used for agricultural purposes, as deﬁned in this title,
and/or for the keeping or raising of animals or fowl.

Section 2. Effective Date. This Ordinance will take effect immediately upon publication
pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-712.
PASSED AND ADOPTED this __ day of _________ 2025.
