Public Comment No comments MAG MPO Planning Certification Review Report - Steve Call, FHWA FHWA and FTA evaluates and certifies the planning process for the Mountainland MPO, UDOT and UTA every four years. The certification review process is an opportunity for both FHWA and FTA to provide advice and guidance to the MPO. The review process ensures that the planning requirement of federal law are being satisfactorily implemented. Staff has answered all the 2011 recommendations. Based on the overall findings FHWA and FTA certify the planning process for the Mountainland MPO and their planning partners UDOT and UTA. Commendations:  MPO, UDOT and UTA collaborate and coordinate all of their planning activities  Use cutting edge visualization techniques to help the public and elected officials understand issues  Made environmental mitigation and integral part of their plan development process  Collaborative a formulated financial plan for the Utah Unified Plan and MPO plans  Joint planning effort in operating and maintaining their surface transportation system and addressing performance based planning in the Utah Unified Plan development  Provided active transportation choices Recommendations:  Provide an annual performance and expenditure report  Increase emphasis on the state’s Strategic Highway Safety Plan priorities  Update Public Participation Plan every four years and add current Plan on the website  Utilize advanced mapping techniques to analyze present transportation benefits  Continue to enhance the website Contact Nan for the complete copy of the Certification Review of MAG MPO and Findings Report. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning May 5, 2016 Page 3 Adopt the Unified Planning Work Program - Shawn Seager The Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP) is the annual budget for Regional Planning and illustrates where the planning resources are allocated. It describes the planned work, budget and funding sources that develops a coordinated transportation planning process. The FY-16-17 $2,367,961 budget is 13% less when compared to last year’s $2.7 m budget. Due to a decrease in pass-through contracts and special studies. $1,677,251 or 70% of the budget is for 12 full time equivalent MAG MPO staff. Contracts and new studies:  Travel Demand Modeling Contact  Joint planning studies Point of the Mountain with UDOT, UTA, and WFRC  Public Participation Any questions contract Shawn Seager or Steve Johnson. Mayor Chris Pengra moved to adopt the Executive Summary of the MAG MPO FY-16-17 Unified Planning Work Program. Mayor J.H. Hadfield seconded the motion and the motion carried unanimously. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning May 5, 2016 Page 4 Again MPO staff met with all the sponsors to review their ideas prior their submission of concept reports. In March, 33 Concept Reports were received for $85 m. Staff reviewed each report and gave a score to each project bases on technical merits. On April 11th, TAC went on a field trip to review each project. On April 18th, TAC met to review the Project Ideas, Concept Reports, Staff Technical Scores, and the Field Trip to create the Project Priority List. After RPC approves the Project Priority List, staff will apply the available MPO and County transportation funds ($40m) to create a Draft Funding List. TAC will review the Draft Funding List in May and will recommend to Regional Planning the final Funding List for the 2019-2010 TIP for the June 2, 2016 Regional Planning Meeting. Mayor Richard Brunst moved the MPO Regional Planning Committee approve the Project Priority List as recommended by the MPO Technical Advisory Committee and MPO staff. This priority list will be used by MPO staff to create a Draft Funding List that will have final approval by this body and county funded projects by the Utah County Commission.
