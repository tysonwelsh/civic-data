RECOMMENDATION:
Staff recommends approving Resolution #23-77 Adopting a process to follow for approving new trail
and open space project

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
City Council has requested a known and approved process to follow for new trail and open space projects on
city property. This process will ensure that city council goals are met for each project, that input has been
taken and received by the Parks, Trails and Recreation Committee, and that is new project is funded and
approved.


PREVIOUS LEGISLATIVE ACTION:
Draper City Council Meeting – November 7, 2023 – Study Session discussion regarding the new process.

FISCAL IMPACT: Funding source and budget for each project approved will needed to be approved.

                                       “EXHIBIT A”

      PROCESS FOR APPROVING NEW TRAIL AND OPEN SPACE PROJECTS


   Beginning in each new year (January/February), The Parks, Trails and Recreation
    Committee (PTR) will meet and recommend new trail and open space projects. These
    would include new trail construction, existing trail reconstruction, and open space
    improvements to meet established objectives and goals of the city. This will ensure that
    the PTR Committee is heard and input is valued.

   Draper City Staff, including the Parks and Recreation Department, administration or City
    Council, may also make recommendations for new trail and open space projects to be
    considered.

   Working together, City staff and the Parks, Trails and Recreation Committee will
    establish a finalized list of new trail projects to be presented to the Draper City Council.

   An annual joint work session with the Draper City Council and the Parks, Trails and
    Recreation Committee will be scheduled in the spring to review the projects and ensure
    a full understanding of proposed new projects.

   Draper City Council will evaluate each project and make sure each recommendation
    meets objectives and goals established by the city. (examples: a new “foot only” trail
    proposal which will ease trail user conflict, or a new “bike only” trail to improve flow or
    reduce conflict, etc.)

   Funding sources and budgets for each new trail project will be identified and be
    approved by City Council in either operational department budgets or in CIP budget, by
    following the established budget cycle for Draper City. Some projects may be completed
    by city staff or funded by approved non-city agencies or foundations.

   Each new project and accompanying budget and funding source will be approved by
    resolution by the Draper City Council.

   Approved projects will follow the fiscal year, July – June of each year.
