City Council Meeting Minutes May 10, 2022
1. 22-155 General Citizen Comments
Attachments: Click here to eComment on this item

Council Chair Marci Houseman invited the public to participate in General Citizen
Comments:
Ms. Deb Pecorelli spoke about the Pebble Brook Sugar Creek rezone. She expressed
support of the R1-10 Rezone that the Council is considering. She did express concerns
regarding the development plan and design and suggested changes to the overall plan.
She has sent in ecomments to the Council.
Mr. Damon Martin lives in the same neighborhood as Ms. Pecorelli. He expressed
concerns about the lack of green space and for the wildlife that lives there. He is in favor
of the R1-10 Rezone.
Ms. Patricia Jones spoke about the March 10th CDBG meeting minutes and asked for
information regarding the difference between the distributions to organizations and the
amounts these organizations requested.
Mr. Benjamin Hurst expressed support for the Sugar Creek rezone. The proposed maps
show some unsafe road layouts that will impact their neighborhood. He also spoke about
the lack of green space in the proposed development.
Mr. Donald Cutshaw lives by the proposed development and expressed concerns with the
new road that will lead into the development and is also concerned about the lack of
green space.
Mr. Bill Rigley spoke about the Sugar Creek proposed development and the high density
of the project.
Mr. Chandler Whipple expressed concern with the loss of recreational space and the
access to the proposed development.
Ms. Jane Irwin spoke about the impact of the proposed road into the new development
but is in favor of the development.
Mr. Andrew Luker spoke about the trail by the golf course which is treasured open space
by the neighbors and spoke about having some open space in the new development.
Mr. Joey Krueger and Ashley live by Dry Creek and spoke about the crossing at Creek
Runway Road and requested that a pedestrian crosswalk be installed at that intersection.
Ms. Cariann Jones spoke about Item 4 Rezone. She spoke about the impact of the
proposed development and the lack of open space.
Public Comment closed.

3. 22-148 Public Utilities Department providing an overview of the Localscapes
Program
Attachments: Public Utilities Localscapes Memo to Council
Localscapes Presentation
Ms. Nikki Wyman, Public Utilities, presented on Localscapes which consists of resilient
and water efficient landscaping suitable for this area in Utah. Localscapes allows for more
efficient use of water and improves the function of our yards. She reviewed the five
components of Localscapes landscaping and provided information about rebate programs
available to residents. Council questions and comments followed.

City Council Meeting Minutes May 10, 2022

5. 22-143 Community Development Department presenting the Citizen Advisory
Committee's recommendation for the use of Community Development
Block Grant funds as will be incorporated in the 2022 Annual Action Plan
for the 2022 program year.

Attachments: CDBG Committee Recommendation Summary
Minutes from March CDBG Committee Meeting
Jake Warner, Community Development, presented on the CDBG Committee
recommendations for the distribution of funds for the Program Year 2022 (Fiscal Year
2023). He provided background of the Community Development Block Grant organization
and the Home Program. He described the purpose and use of the utilization of these
funds. CDBG receives approximately $400,000 from HUD. The CDBG Committee reviews
the applications and makes recommendations for the distribution of these funds. Council
questions and comments followed.

6. 22-149 Human Resources Department presenting the Tentative FY 2022-23
Employee Compensation Plan

Attachments: CC Compensation Presentation May10'22.pdf
Katrina Frederick, Human Resource Director, provided a review of the Employee
Compensation Plan proposal. She spoke about the sources used to collect information
and data for the Compensation Study, the goals for the Compensation Plan proposal, and
the FY 2023 Compensation Plan Proposal Summary. The Plan proposal includes Cola
increase to all positions city-wide. Council questions and comments followed. Council
thanked Ms. Frederick for her presentation.

7. 22-154 City Administration, City Council, Administrative Services, and
Non-Departmental presenting their FY 2022-23 tentative department
budgets.

Attachments: Presentation

The Budget presentations for this evening were as follows:
Cliff Strachan, CAO, presented the Administration budget for Fiscal Year 2023. He
reviewed Administration's objectives and goals and reviewed the budgets for the Mayor's
Office, Chief Administrative Officer, Communications, Sandy Amphitheater, and
Non-departmental budgets. Council questions followed.
Dustin Fratto, Council Executive Director, reviewed the proposed fiscal year 2023 budget
for the Council Office. He reviewed the changes in the detailed line items.
Brian Kelley, Finance Director, reviewed the proposed budgets for Fiscal Year 2023 for
the following departments: Human Resources, Information Technology, and Facilities.
Council questions followed.
