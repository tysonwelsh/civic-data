SV-1-2024
Clifford Street Vacation
2300 South 5660 West

BACKGROUND

Guy Williams, representing various property owners along Clifford Street, is requesting
consideration to vacate all of Clifford Street which is located at 5660 West and north of 2300
South. Clifford Street was platted as part of the Hayne’s Addition Plat E Subdivision and was
approved in May 1889.

The street in question has never been formally improved. Properties that adjoin this street on both
the east and west have been using the right of way for access to their properties for many years.
As the salvage yards have become fewer and fewer, a result of the Mountain View Corridor, and
with the subsequent adoption of the Light Industrial Zone in 2016, land uses are starting to change
in this area.

The site east of Clifford Street was originally occupied by outside storage and salvage type
businesses. It has historically been an enforcement issue for the City. The City approved the
Wasatch Fleet facility in 2019. One of the driving factors in vacating Clifford Street was that
Wasatch Fleet would like to access the west portion of their operation through the vacated right-
of-way. The other businesses adjacent to Clifford Street will remain for the time being but are
likely to transition over time.

One concern of staff is how the vacated right-of-way will be treated going forward. To answer

     2. The applicants have provided a conceptual site plan showing a new drive approach coming

To conclude, land previously occupied by the unimproved street can be better utilized by the new
business fronting 5600 West and for other properties along this right-of-way as well. Long term
opportunities may allow for future and improved development having improvements that better
serve these properties. The street does contain existing utilities that will be preserved by the
vacating ordinance

According to City Ordinance, streets and/or alley vacations shall be reviewed by the Planning
Commission with a recommendation to the City Council.

Maverik West Valley Subdivision
2390 South 5600 West
LI Zone
1 Lot
4 Acres

BACKGROUND

AWA Engineering, representing Maverik Inc., is requesting preliminary subdivision approval for
the Maverik West Valley Subdivision. The subject property is located at the northwest corner of
2400 South 5600 West. The proposed subdivision will amend all of lots 7-12 and 37-48 and a
portion of lots 1-6 in block 1 and all of lots 1-12 and 46-47 and a portion of lot 48 of block 2
Hayne’s Addition Plat E Subdivision. The proposed plat also includes previously vacated alleys,
and a portion of Clifford Street abutting said lots.

ISSUES:

The Maverik West Valley Subdivision is being proposed to create a new 4-acre lot for a new
Maverik store. As mentioned previously, the existing site contains a number of lots, vacated alleys
and streets from the original Haynes Addition Plat E Subdivision. These will all be consolidated
to create one new lot.

Access will be gained from 5600 West and 2400 South. The access on 5600 West will need to be
coordinated with UDOT, which process is well under way with the Conditional Use application.
As discussed in the study session, this site is larger than most Maverik sites due to this location
being used not only for regular vehicular fueling but for large semi-trucks and an RV dumping
station.

As part of the subdivision, the developer will be dedicating property along 5600 West and a smaller
portion along 2400 South. The applicant will need to coordinate improvements along both rights
of way with UDOT and the City as part of the conditional use application.

When this area was platted in 1889, various alleys and Clifford Street were dedicated as part of
that plat. Over the years, these alleys and the Clifford Street right-of-way have been vacated.
However, the utility easements that were located within said alleys and street were not. As part of
this plat, and in order to develop the site as proposed, these will be vacated as part of the
subdivision plat. The applicant will need approvals from any existing utility companies regarding
this matter.

Cobalt Landing PUD - Amended
1585 West Neon Way
RM Zone

BACKGROUND: Russ Platt is requesting consideration to amend portions of the Cobalt Landing
PUD. The proposed application will amend lots 101, 115-138, Parcels C & E and will create
Parcels H, I, J and K.

STAFF/AGENCY CONCERNS: There are no staff or agency concerns with this application.

ISSUES: The Cobalt Landing PUD was recorded in January 2018. This phase of the project
consisted of 72 units. A combination of 2- and 3-unit buildings comprises the residential mix in
the project. The request of the applicant is to amend the 2-unit buildings along the entire south
and east boundaries of the development. This was actually a concept that was raised during the
original approval process, but a decision was made to postpone the idea until residential units were
sold and occupied.

Now that all units are occupied, the applicant and HOA have been working with residents to amend
the plat by creating and extending a property line from the back of the units to the corresponding
boundaries on the south and east sides of the development. The majority if not all property owners

C-21-2024
Take 5 Oil Change (Wildcat Real Estate Holdings, LLC)
5619-5635 W. 3500 S.
C-2 zone, .74 acres
The applicant, Wildcat Real Estate Holdings, LLC, representing Take 5 Oil Change, is requesting
conditional use approval for a drive-thru auto service center at 5619-5634 W. 3500 S. The zoning for
this property is C-2, General Commercial and the General Plan designation is Hunter Town Center.
The surrounding zone is C-2 on the north and east. The zoning to the west is R-1-8 (single dwelling
unit residential 8,000 sf min) and C-1, neighborhood commercial to the south. The surrounding uses
include fast food to the north (Burger King) and east (Arby’s), mixed retail and office to the south.
To the west a portion of the residential is occupied by residential homes and the remainder along
3500 S. is vacant and owned by Questar Gas.

The project will occupy two parcels. The west parcel was originally part of the Hunter Park Plaza
project. The east parcel was a 7-11. These lots were modified as part of a Utah Department of
Transportation (UDOT) project and the existing buildings were demolished for that road widening
and then the remainder parcels were recently sold as UDOT surplus property.

The proposal is to construct a 1,585 square foot 3-bay auto service building on the east lot. The
services include oil changes and emissions where the customers remain in their car and drive through
the bays for the service, which takes approximately 10 minutes to complete. The typical hours of
operation for this use vary daily but range from 7:00 am – 8:00 pm, 7 days a week.

The building will be constructed of stone and stucco with peaked roof and column accents. The
building must comply with the Commercial Design Standards. This is a corner lot and the only
customer entrance is for vehicles so Staff recommends that the north elevation (non-overhead door
side) also provide elements as a primary façade for the requirements of the Design Guidelines for
Commercial Buildings.

The current layout of the project has the Take 5 Oil Change located on the east lot with a shared
access off 3500 South for the west lot that connects to the center to the south. There will be four to
five employees, and 9 parking spaces provided (9 required). The service bay doors are located on the
east and west sides of the building and are not directly adjacent to the residential zone/uses. There is
also an existing masonry wall along the west edge that separates the residential zone so that will
remain however, it is need of maintenance and will need to be cut back further out of the 20’ setback
per City Code. The west lot will be maintained and landscaped with live plant material to match the
east lot however it may be redeveloped in the future as another building lot. Parking and site design
will then be re-evaluated at that time.

The lots are located along two High Image Arterials (3500 South and 5600 West) so they will be
landscaped per the 7-10 standards. There will be signage on the building that will be required to meet
the Title 11 standards.

There will be a dumpster provided for this building that will be located within a 6’ masonry
enclosure that is constructed to match the building.

CA-10-2024
Hunt Electric Campus - Outside Storage and Heavy Manufacturing
1811, 1863, 1897, 1920, 1935, 1940 W. Alexander and 2421 S. Progress Dr.
M Zone (11.49 acres)

The applicant, Hunt Electric, is requesting a conditional use amendment for outside storage and
heavy manufacturing on all Hunt Electric parcels. The zoning for this area is M, Manufacturing.
The West Valley City General Plan anticipates light manufacturing uses for this area. The
surrounding zone is manufacturing on all sides. The surrounding uses include Desert Tech
(gun/ammunition manufacturing) to the north, Lindquist Sales (heating equipment supplier) to the
south, UPS to the west.

There have been several applications that have been completed, modified or expired and the intent
of this application is to clarify all of the uses and improvements on all of the parcels associated
with the Hunt Electric campus.

The two primary uses subject to conditional use approval are Outside Storage and Heavy
Manufacturing, which also includes surfacing and screening requirements.

Heavy Manufacturing: Includes light assembly of electrical components outside of the buildings
on the campus. This is completed on both gravel and paved surfaces as outlined in more detail
below for each individual lot. This work is clean, dust free electrical work that includes:
- Electrifying the interiors of 53’ semi-trailers and installing a patented pop-up control tower and
associated wiring.
