White City Metro Township
RESOLUTION NO.: 2 2-05-01 DATE: May 5, 2022
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO COUNCIL
APPROVING THE APPOINTMENT OF CHRIS HUNTZINGER
AND GENE WILSON AS FULL-TIME PLANNING COMMISSION
MEMBERS AND WESTON MILLEN AS AN ALTERNATE
MEMBER OF THE PLANNING COMMISSION FOR THREE-
YEAR TERMS ENDING FEBRUARY 28, 2025
RECITALS

A. The White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah Code 8§
10-2a-401 ef seq.

B. Pursuant to Ordinance 17-06-02, Planning Commission Members are appointed by the Metro
Township Council to serve three-year terms;

C. On this date, the White City Metro Council desires to appoint Chris Huntzinger and Gene Wilson as
Full-Time Planning Commission Members and Weston Millen as an Alternate Member of the
Planning Commission with terms ending February 28, 2025.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the

Council approves the appointment of Chris Huntzinger and Gene Wilson as Full-Time Planning

Commission Members and Weston Millen as an Alternate Member of the Planning

Commission with terms ending February 28, 2025.
