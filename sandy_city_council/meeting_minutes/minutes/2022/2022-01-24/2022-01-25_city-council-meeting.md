City Council Meeting Minutes January 25, 2022
Prayer / Pledge of Allegiance
Council Chair Marci Houseman read a statement regarding conducting the City Council
meeting virtually without an anchor location. She welcomed those in attendance.
Lynn Pace, City Attorney, offered the Prayer.
Council Member Cyndi Sharkey led the Pledge.
Work Session Items
1. 22-001 Trans Jordan Landfill providing the City Council with their 2022
Organizational Update
Attachments: Presentation.pdf
Mike Gladbach, Public Works Director, introduced Jaren Scott, Executive Director with
Trans-Jordan Member Cities, who provided an overview of the organization and reviewed
the primary function and purpose of the organization: best value at low rates,
environmental best practices, and long term commitment. Council questions followed.
Council moved to Time Certain Events and heard General Citizen Comments.
2. 22-002 Waste Management presenting their 2022 Recycling and Smart Truck
Recap to the City Council
Attachments: Presentation
Mike Gladbach introduced Blake Leonelli with Waste Management who provided an
overview of the transition to every other week recycling plan. He reviewed the statistics
relative to recycle contamination. He also spoke about Smart Truck Technology. Council
questions and comments followed. Council thanked Mr. Leonelli for his presentation.
3. 22-004 Administrative Services Department providing the City Council with a
quarterly budget update
Attachments: FY 2022 Q2 Budget Update.pdf
Brett Neuman, Finance Department, provided an update on the current fiscal year Q2
budget. Council comments and questions followed.
Council moved to Standing Reports.
4. 22-012 Possible Closed Session: character, professional competence, or physical
or mental health of an individual.
Council convened a Closed Session to discuss the character, professional competence,
or physical or mental health of an individual. The City Council meeting adjourned following
the closed session.
A motion was made by Ryan Mecham, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey to convene a
Closed Session in the Council Conference Room to discuss the character,
professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual and
Adjourn the City Council meeting immediately following the Closed Session...The
motion carried by the following vote:

Standing Reports
Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report
Dustin Fratto, Interim Director, thanked Administration for their collaborative efforts over
the past month. Next week's meeting is virtual. He reviewed upcoming agenda items with
the Council.
Council Member Business
Council Member Sharkey updated the Council on her meeting with Wasatch Front &
Recycling District and Legislative Policy Committee. She spoke about upcoming
legislation this year.
Council Member Scott Earl spoke about his swearing in ceremony.
Council Member Marci Houseman provided an update from the Public Utilities Advisory
Board (PUAB) meeting and about a recent water audit conducted - the results were very
positive for the department. She shared resident feedback with Mike Gladbach regarding
the city's decision making process related to the scheduling of snow plow service. Mike
Gladbach responded to the question and provided details on the process.
Council Member Ryan Mecham provided a brief update from the CDBG committee. He
asked if there would be a review of the committee assignments at a future city council
meeting.
Mayor's Report
Mayor Zoltanski provided information about the City's recent surveys. She spoke about
forming an Economic Advisory Committee to discuss business growth in Sandy. Mayor
Zoltanski shared with the Council that she was elected as Secretary to the Council Of
Governments (COG) and she updated the Council on reent meetings she had with
legislative representatives.
CAO Report
Kim Bell, Interim CAO, spoke about the collaborative efforts between the Council Office
and the Administration and provided the Council with some updates.
