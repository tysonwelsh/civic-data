REGULAR SESSION  
 
1. General Public Input  
 
The public hearing was opened.  There were no comments made and the public hearing 
was closed.  
 
2. Public hearing and possible action regarding an amendment to the Silver Creek 
Estates Unit F Subdivision Plat to vacate portions of Wasatch Way; 299 East 
Wasatch Way; SS-18-C-1-C; SS-18-C-A; SL-F-354-A; LDRS-1; LDRS-2,; Leon Day, 
applicant– Sean Lewis, County Planner     
 
Planner Lewis distributed an updated version of the proposal.  He said this is a two-part 
project.  One is a plat amendment that will amend two lots of the Silver Creek Estates 
subdivision.  There are two parcels not within the boundaries of the subdivision.  The 
applicant is requesting to combine these four pieces of property into two.  The other 
request is to vacate a portion of Wasatch Way Road.

A vicinity map was shown.  The existing property boundaries were pointed out along 
with the proposed property boundaries.  There will be no change in density.  Planner 
Lewis said this action occurred via deed approximately 20 years ago, but because of the 
subdivision it was never platted.      
 
Planner Lewis pointed to the plat map to show the portion of Wasatch Way that was 
never constructed.  The applicant is requesting this part of the road be vacated, which 
requires an ordinance by the County Council.   The Commission will make a 
recommendation to the County Council.   
 
Planner Lewis explained the reason this has taken so long to come before the 
Commission is because the property owner to the north did not agree with vacating the 
road; however, the property has recently sold.  The property is known as Last Dance 
Ranch Subdivision.  The new owner is agreeable to vacating the road.   
 
Commissioner Fine verified that only the highlighted portion of Wasatch Way will be 
vacated.  Attorney Thomas asked if the portion of Wasatch Way, not included in the 
request, has been indicated on the plat map.  He believes the County Council will be 
more inclined to vacate the requested portion of Wasatch Way if it there is a road that 
replaces it.  Planner Lewis said there is an existing road.  Chair Peck asked if this is a 
dirt road.  She was told that is correct.   
 
Chair Peck requested that written permission from the land owner of Last Dance 
Ranch be included with the Staff Report.  The access for Last Dance Ranch was pointed 
out.  The parcels that will be included in the Silver Creek Estates subdivision were 
pointed.  Planner Lewis said that Staff is recommending that the Planning Commission 
forward a positive recommendation to the County Council.   
The public hearing was opened.

Leon Day (the applicant) spoke.  He said the previous owner of Last Dance Ranch would 
not agree to vacate the road.  He explained the reason why the road was never built is 
because the location is very wet.  He agrees with what Planner Lewis has stated and is 
available to answer questions.   
 
Dan Fuller pointed to his property on the vicinity map.  He has lived on Wasatch Way 
for 20 years.  He doesn’t have a lot of objections, but does have questions about the 
wetlands on the property.  He has seen water run on the properties in question during 
spring run-off.  He asked if there is an acknowledgement that there are wetlands.   
 
Planner Lewis answered it is known there are wetlands, but a plat amendment 
doesn’t require a wetlands delineation; however, it will be required before a house is 
built.  Mr. Fuller had additional questions.  Planner Lewis said these questions will be 
answered as part of the building permit process.   
 
Mr. Fuller asked if approved, will each lot will have a building right regardless if there 
are wetlands or not.  Planner Lewis answered that is correct.  He said the building 
rights already exist.  Mr. Fuller expressed concern that all structures in the area are on 
septic.  Has that been taken into account with the wetlands?  Chair Peck said if 
wetlands are identified on the property, the setbacks and building pads will be 
adjusted to accommodate them.   
 
Chair Peck said although the questions are valid, they are premature.  The properties 
already have building rights.  Mr. Fuller said the process seems backwards.  The cart is 
before the horse.   
 
Sam Ferron said he owns the property just south of Mr. Fuller.  He has not yet built his 
home.  This seems like a worthwhile effort.  He is in support of the action.   
The public hearing was closed.

COMMISSION COMMENTS AND QUESTIONS          
Commissioner Cooke asked if the current road is platted.  He also asked if the trail 
easement carries through the subdivision.  Planner Lewis said the trail easement is 
shown on the amendment, but not past that point because it is not needed for this 
application.  He believes the trail easement continues; however, he is unable to confirm 
that.  If there isn’t an easement recorded, this plat grants them one.  Mr. Day pointed to 
the trail easements on the plat map.  There are three recorded trail easements in place. 
 
Commissioner Stevens made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the 
County Council for the plat amendment and road vacation as outlined in the Staff 
Report and as follows.  Commissioner Cooke seconded the motion.   
 
FINDINGS OF FACT 
1. Harold Bywater is the listed fee title owner of Parcel SS-C-1, SS-18-C-1-C, SL-F-
354-A, AND SL-F-355-A.  
2. Jeff Jonas is the listed fee title owner of Parcels SS-18, now known as the Last 
Dance Ranch Subdivision, LDRS-1-LDRS-2.  
3. Parcel SS-C-1, SS-18-C-1-C, SL-F-354-A, and SL-F-355-A are respectively 5.75, 
2.30, 1.39, and 1.47 acres in size. 
4. Parcels LDRS-1-LDRS-2 are a combined 47.04 acres in size. 
5. Parcels SS-C-1, SS-18-C-1-C, SL-F-354-A, AND SL-F-355-A are located at 
approximately 299 E. Wasatch Way.  
6. Parcels SS-18, SS-18-C-1, SS-18-C-1-C, SL-F-354-A, AND SL-F-355-A are located in 
the Rural Residential Zoning District.  
7. The Silver Creek Estate Unit F Subdivision plat was recorded in Summit County 
on March 3, 1965 and included Lot 354, containing 2.87 acres, and Lot 355, 
containing 2.87 acres.   
8. The Unit F plat was amended on November 22, 1994 to dedicated Wasatch Way 
in its built location.  Among other changes, the November 22, 1994 amendment

modified the boundaries of lot 354 and 355 reducing their size to 1.39 acres and 
1.47 acres respectively.  
9. In 2004, The Summit County Community Development Department determined 
that Assessor parcels 22/18-C-1 and SS-18-C-1-C are not considered to be “Lots 
of Record.”    
10. The proposed plat amendment would amend the boundaries of Lots 354 and 
355 to incorporate Assessor Parcels SS-18-C-1 and SS-18-C-1-C into the 
boundaries of the Silver Creek Estate Unit F Subdivision which would result in 
two Lots of Record that would be eligible for the construction of a single family 
home on each lot.    
11. The proposed amendment would also vacate the unused portion of Wasatch 
Way that bisects Lots 354 and 355. 
12. The proposed amendment would vacate the portion of the Silver Creek Estates 
Unit F Subdivision that is currently located entirely within the Last Dance Ranch 
Subdivision. 
13. The boundaries of LRDS-1 and LRDS-2 do not change as a result of the 
amendment. 
14. Per the Summit County Code requirements for road vacation, this item was 
publicly noticed via the March 17, 24, 31, and April 7, 2018 issue of The Park 
Record Newspaper.   
 
CONCLUSIONS OF LAW 
1. There is good cause for the amendment of the Silver Creek Estate Unit F 
Subdivision plat as the proposed plat amendment would formally amend the 
Silver Creek Estates Unit F plat to reflect changes that occurred via recorded 
deed a generation ago.  The vacation of the Wasatch Way right-of-way would 
not impact or materially injure any person as the road has not been built in a 
different configuration.   
2. No increases in density would occur as a result of this amendment.
