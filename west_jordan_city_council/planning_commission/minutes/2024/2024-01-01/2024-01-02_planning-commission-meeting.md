6. Good Day Catering; 7872 South Old Bingham Highway; Conditional Use Permit for
Reception Center; P-C Zone; Magic Clean Car Wash LLC/Karl Mason (applicant)
[#31562; parcel 21-32-226-010]
Christine Mason, owner of Good Day Catering, gave a summary of their current catering business and
listed some of the venues where they are approved to cater at, and which take a fee from each event.
That is why it is important for them to have their own event space. They are planning to add 4,000
square feet of event space and 1,000 square feet of storage/staging area to their property that currently
houses their catering operations. Hours of operation are anticipated to be from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. seven
days a week, excluding holidays. Most of their events last until 10:00 p.m. with time to clean up. There
will be 48 parking spaces in front of the building and 23 spaces as part of a shared parking agreement
with the property to the north, which is her husband’s automotive business. They are not interested in
obtaining a banquet or full alcohol license. However, clients would be allowed to rent one of their
certified bartenders. They have not had any problems in the past, so she did not anticipate any at this
location. Security staff would be present during events as well as an event coordinator and kitchen
staff. She felt that West Jordan needs this type of business, because the only venue similar in style and
size in West Jordan would be Gardner Village. This would be an opportunity to keep dollars in West
Jordan with a beautiful facility. Responding to questions she explained what a banquet license is and
noted that occupancy of the building is determined by the fire department, but events would probably
be limited to about 200 persons.
Megan Jensen reviewed the size, location, and surrounding uses of the property. The Planned
Community zone allows for a reception center with a conditional use permit. She reviewed the
proposed building addition site plan, which is currently being reviewed by staff. There will be a
covered patio for smaller receptions. The parking regulations require 52 spaces, but with the proposed
layout, the flow of patrons to these kinds of events, and the shared parking agreement, staff and the
traffic engineer did not have any concerns. The recommended conditions of approval relate to noise
mitigation and safety, which she reviewed.
Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the Conditional Use Permit for Good Day
Catering, located at 7872 South Old Bingham Highway in a P-C zone, provided that all Conditions of
Approval can be fully adhered to at all times.

Jay Thomas estimated that 75 parking stalls would be needed based on 150 guests with two people per

vehicle plus staff. He asked if that would be adequate.

Larry Gardner said the parking requirement calculation is based on the ordinance. The site shows 48

spaces with the requirement of 52. However, with the cross-access easement parking should not be a

concern.

Duncan Murray said he reviewed the cross-access and parking agreement recorded against the land, so

even if the ownership changes it is still in force and those spaces can be counted. If the property to the

north changes its use, then the parking for that property would be reevaluated, but the approval of this
property would remain.

Paul Brockbank spoke to the question about emergency access for the site. The fire department

reviewed the site and although it is a snug area, it meets the restrictions. Also, the applicant plans to

install a fire sprinkler system in the whole building, which alleviates most of the fire code concerns.

It was pointed out that there is a shoulder on the adjacent street where cars can park, but those areas

cannot be used in the parking calculation. The dumpster location is existing. Clarification was given

regarding wall location and setbacks.

Karl and Christine Mason provided information regarding site lighting. The parking lot is well lit, but

the lights are not directed toward the apartments. The courtyard in the back is primarily for small

ceremonies with reception activities being conducted inside. There will be lights on the building, but it
will be soft lighting in the back.

Larry Gardner said the code requires that light cannot cross the property line, so what the applicant is

describing is acceptable. However, if it does become a problem then it would be an enforcement issue.

Ts Station Area Plans; Recommendation to City Council for adoption of Three Station Area
Plans; Bingham Junction at 7387 South Bingham Junction Boulevard (Midvale); West
Jordan City Center at 8021 South Redwood Road; Historic Gardner at 1127 West 7800
South; Small Area Plan(s); City of West Jordan (applicant)

Tayler Jensen said staff had been working for 1 % years on this project along with Midvale City, UTA,

and UDOT. The goal of the station area plan is to create a vibrant place in the community that will be

uniquely West Jordan. He presented the three station area plans, which are required by HB462 for a

one-half mile radius from all Trax Stations. A small area plan is like a general plan for the area with

ideas of what will happen in the catalyst sites. Any approvals for zoning or site development come to

the Planning Commission and City Council in the future. He reviewed the project goals and each of the

preferred concepts that involve city or public properties. The hope is that once they develop, other

properties near them will then begin to redevelop.

Comments and questions from the Commission were discussed, which included concern with single-
family right next to Trax, involvement with the Jordan School District for relocation of their
warehousing activities, partnerships with UTA, benefits of added residential along the Trax line in
Midvale that could bring an influx of workers to West Jordan, and the level of involvement the State
has. Implementation of the plan could begin immediately and continue for 20-30 years. An official
plan can be a benefit when the city is applying for grants or other funding for improvements to these
areas.
Ammon Allen asked about plans for the other stations.
Tayler Jensen said West Jordan has funding from WFRC for the 5600 West Old Bingham Highway
station, which they hope to have adopted by the end of the year. Some of the station area plans might
get an exemption to the requirement because the areas are built out and do not have many
redevelopment opportunities. The goal is to have all seven of the plans adopted by the end of 2025.
Staff recommended that the Planning Commission make a Recommendation of Approval
(positive recommendation) to the City Council for the Three Stations, Station Area Plan.
