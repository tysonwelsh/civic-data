Description of the Proposal-Blue Sky Sporting Clay Lodge Complex 
The applicant, Michael Phillips, is the owner and operator of the 1007-acre Blue-Sky Ranch outside of 
Wanship, and he is requesting approval of a Conditional Use permit that will allow him to provide 
additional facilities and amenities at the Ranch. 
Background: 
 The Ranch was issued a Conditional Use Permit on October 23, 2006, for a corporate retreat located in 
Wanship. The use was considered to be a “guest ranch/lodge intended to attract visitors/patrons on a 
daily basis or an extended stay” according to the Use Chart in the Code. 
In 2011, the applicant applied for a Low Impact Permit to amend the previously approved Conditional 
Use Permit. Blue Sky proposed to decrease the overall square footage by approximately half of what 
was previously approved so that a distillery/restaurant could be constructed. The 171 guest rooms 
previously approved were reduced to 60, and the 5 proposed restaurants were reduced to 3. On 
December 20, 2012, the Low Impact Permit was issued. 
On October 17, 2016, the Summit County Development Department approved a Low Impact Permit for 
amendments to the previously approved Low Impact Permit issued in 2012. Those amendments include 
a Conference and Event Arena, lodging for 46 keys, a Spa and Wellness building, and the existing 
Outdoor Riding area that was made into an Indoor Riding Arena.  
The additional facilities and amenities that are being requested include add itional hotel rooms, a 
restaurant, spa and yoga facility, an additional swimming pool, upgrades to the existing sewage 
treatment plant, and approval for a water reservoir for culinary and fire protection use. 
These facilities will be located on parcel NS-82 which is part of the 1007-acre Blue Sky Ranch, and they 
are about 1500’ north of the Lodge at Blue Sky Ranch. Parcels to the east, north and west are at least 
2400’ away from the proposed project.  
There are no wetlands at the site and none of the development will break the ridgeline. 
There are ample locations on the 614 acres for the storage of snow.  
The Proposal: 
There is a real need for additional hotel rooms as the demographic has shifted from what was originally 
thought it would to be to families. There are currently only 5 suites available that have two bedrooms 
and there is a significant demand for more of those, particularly from multi-generation families. The 16 
units proposed will contain 39 bedrooms. 10 of those units will be 2 bedrooms, and the  6 other units will 
be 3 bedrooms. 
The current spa and yoga facility at the Lodge is experiencing a much higher usage than originally 
planned and 7 additional rooms for the spa and 15 spots for yoga are proposed.
