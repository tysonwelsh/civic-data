                              RESOLUTION 22-27

A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL TO SIGN A FUNDING
AGREEMENT BETWEEN DRAPER CITY AND THE JORDAN VALLEY WATER
CONSERVANCY DISTRICT (JVWCD) FOR THE REIMBURSEMENT OF
FUNDS TO DRAPER CITY.

      WHEREAS, Draper City is a Member Agency of JVWCD; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City applied for a Member Agency Grant from JVWCD and
received notice of funding for $48,000; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City desires to construct a Conservation Garden to
demonstrate to residents how to use water-wise and water-conservation
techniques.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      1. Agreement. The Draper City Council approves the funding agreement
      between Draper City and the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District for
      reimbursement of funds to Draper City and authorizes Mayor Walker to sign
      the funding agreement. The funding agreement is attached as “EXHIBIT A.”

      2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
      passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE __________ DAY OF___________________, 2022.

           funding assistance, consistent with the terms of this Agreement.

                                     PROJECT DESCRIPTION AND SCHEDULE


Measure 1: Draper Parks & Recreation Conservation Garden

           Location: 12354 South 800 East, Draper UT 84020

           Description: The project consists of removing approximately 80% of the existing
           grass in the front, sides, and back yards of the Parks and Recreation Offices.
           The new plan includes paths, fencing, rock mulch, wood chips, boulders, dry
           stream bed, water rock bubbler feature, low water use plants, and signage. The
           project also includes upgrading the irrigation system with a low water use
           system, utilizing a new automatic filter system and controller.

           The city’s intention is to provide a conservation garden that meets the elements
           of Localscapes to demonstrate to residents what can qualify for a rebate in the
           Localscapes Reward Program, in hopes to encourage residents to make
           changes in their own yards.

           Funding Tier: Tier 3

           Schedule for Implementation:

           April 2022 – Finalize landscape designs, advertise for bid
           May 2022 – Award project, start construction
           July 2022 – Substantial completion

           Cost:

           JVWCD 40% - $48,000
           Draper 60% - $72,000
           Total Project Cost: $120,000
