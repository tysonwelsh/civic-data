                                  RESOLUTION NO. 2025-39

 A RESOLUTION OF THE LEHI CITY COUNCIL APPROVING A SALT STORAGE BUILDING
 CONSTRUCTION AGREEMENT WITH OUTBACK CONCRETE INC. dba OUTBACKMETAL
                                 BUILDINGS

       WHEREAS, Lehi City (“City”) desires to construct a salt storage building;

       WHEREAS, the City selected OutbackMetal Buildings to construct the building;

      WHEREAS, on June 10, 2025, the Lehi City Council held a duly-noticed public meeting to
ascertain the facts regarding this matter, which facts and comments are found in the meeting
record; and,

      WHEREAS, after considering the facts and comments presented to the City Council, the
Council finds: (i) that it should approve a Salt Storage Building Construction Agreement with
OutbackMetal Buildings; and (ii) such action furthers the health, safety and welfare of the
citizens of Lehi.

     NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Council of Lehi City, Utah, as follows:

      1.    The Agreement between Lehi City and OutbackMetal Buildings attached hereto as
Exhibit “A” is hereby approved and the Mayor is authorized to execute the Agreement and
take all other such action as is necessary to make it effective.

     2.     This resolution shall take effect immediately.

Approved and Adopted by the City Council of Lehi City, Utah this 10th day of June, 2025.
