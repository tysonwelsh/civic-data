Call to Order: 6:30 p.m. – Council Member Cotter

Approval of Minutes: Council Meeting, January 7, 2025
 MOTION: Mr. Hock moved to approve, and Mr. Pickett SECONDED the motion.
 Voice vote taken, all “Ayes.” Approved 5-0

Special Presentation:
  • State of the City Address – Mayor Hales delivered a speech on the State of the City.

Consent Agenda:
  • Confirmation of the Mayor’s appointment of Brian Miller to the Parks and Recreation Advisory
     Board for a term beginning December 30, 2024 through December 30, 2027. Mayor Hales introduced
     Mr. Miller expressing confidence that he would be a great addition to the Parks and Recreation
     Advisory Board.
     MOTION: Ms. Turner moved to approve the Consent Agenda. Mr. Hock SECONDED the motion.
         Council Roll Call Vote:
         Mr. Goodman        Aye
         Ms. Turner         Aye
         Mr. Hock           Aye
         Mr. Pickett        Aye
         Ms. Cotter         Aye
         Motion passed: 5-0

Public Hearing:
  • Consider an ordinance amending Section 17-12-070 of the Murray City Municipal Code relating to

     compensation for Planning commission Members. Planning Manager Zac Smallwood explained that
     the proposed request would raise the compensation for Murray planning commissioners from $40 to
     $50 per meeting. A cost comparison sheet was provided to show compensation for other city planning
     commissions in the Salt Lake Valley. Mr. Smallwood reviewed findings to explain why the Murray
     Planning Commission and City staff recommended approval to the Murray City Council.

     The public hearing was open for public comments. No comments were given, and the public hearing
     was closed.

        Mr. Goodman        Aye

        Mr. Pickett        Aye
        Ms. Cotter         Aye
        Motion passed: 5-0

Mayor’s Report and Questions: Mayor Hales announced upcoming events and activities held in the City and
reported that a recent small power outage was resolved quickly.
