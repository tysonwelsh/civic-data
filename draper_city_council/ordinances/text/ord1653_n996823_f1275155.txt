City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1653

On May 20, 2025, the Draper City Council Approved Ordinance #1653 amending Title
7-5 of the Draper City Municipal Code to align with Utah State Code Section 11-46-303
regarding the management and exemption of community cats. The complete
Ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at
https://www.draperutah.gov/

Published this 21ST day of May 2025
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder
