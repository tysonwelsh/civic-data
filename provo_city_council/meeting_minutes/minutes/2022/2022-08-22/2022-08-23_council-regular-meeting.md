Roll Call
THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS OF THE COUNCIL AND ADMINISTRATION WERE PRESENT:
     Councilor Shannon Ellsworth                Councilor Bill Fillmore

     Councilor Katrice MacKay                   Councilor Rachel Whipple

Prayer – Steve Kaufusi

Public Comment (14:18)

Chair Hoban asked Justin Harrison to read the public comment preamble. After which, he opened public
comment.

With none, Chair Hoban closed public comment.

Consent Agenda

1.          A resolution appointing an individual to the Civil Service Commission. (22-009) (16:38)

      Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-45, as currently constituted, has been
                   made by council rule.

Chair Hoban called for a vote on the implied motion.

      Vote:        The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                   MacKay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.

     Motion:      An implied motion to approve Ordinance 2022-34, as currently constituted, has been

Aaron Ardmore, City Planner, provided summary of what was presented in the work session.

Councilor MacKay shared her excitement for this project. She asked about the parking for the
