HR Director Rooker will present Resolutions #2025-1122 and #2025-1123.  
Resolution #2025-1122: Employment Policy Revision: (Sick Leave Section) 
• This resolution is to change our Sick Leave policy. Our current policy allows for sick leave to be used to care 
for a family member, leaving approval to the discretion of the supervisor. This has led to some inconsistent 
application across the agency. We (as a management team) feel it’s appropriate to change this to allow an 
employee to care for an immediate family member, providing documentation to support the request, and 
there is no inconsistency between departments or supervisors. The list of immediate family members is the 
same language used in other places in the Handbook. Commissioner Litvack asked who is verifying the sick 
leave. HR Director Rooker replied we don’t verify anything under three days; and the policy states we may 
require documentation beyond three days. CEO Kimball noted that, and we will clarify if necessary. CEO 
Kimball was asked to chair the meeting for a bit by Chair Bernal. 
o CEO Kimball asked for a motion. Commissioner Litvack motioned to approve Resolution #2025 -1122; 
Commissioner Johnson seconded the motion. All Board members present ( Vice-Chair Nguyen, 
Commissioners Johnson, Leonelli, Litvack, and Ramos ) voted In Favor of Resolution #2025 -1122; none 
opposed. Resolution #2025-1122 is approved and takes effect immediately.

Resolution #2025-1124: Housing Choice Voucher Department Increase of PBVs at Grace Mary Manor 
• CEO Kimball presented the resolution and HCV Director Brady will take questions. As you will remember, we 
lost our Continuum of Care (COC) Rent Assistance at Grace Mary Manor and Kelly Benson Apartments. Our 
primary concern is finding another revenue source for Grace Mary Manor. HCV Director Brady came back 
with the recommendation and vetted it through the regulations, and we are able to increase the vouchers 
by 30; and this means we have 80 out of 84 units covered with rent assistance at Grace Mary Manor. HCV 
Director Brady added, we feel it’s important for this property to continue to move forward and in reviewing 
our budget for this year, things are looking good, and we have put in the projection to add the 30 Project 
Based Vouchers (PBVs) at Grace Mary Manor. We support this. CEO Kimball  thanked HCV Director Brady for 
her leadership and thinking across departments. CEO Kimball turned the meeting back over to Chair Bernal. 
o Chair Bernal asked for any questions; there were none. He asked for a motion; Commissioner Johnson 
moved to approve Resolution #2025 -1124; Commissioner Ramos seconded the motion. All Board 
members present (Chair Bernal, Vice-Chair Nguyen, Commissioners Johnson, Leonelli, Litvack, and Ramos)

Resolution #2025- 1125: Housing Choice Voucher Department Seeking Approval for Moving on Initiative with 
Mainstream Vouchers to HOPWA Clients 
• CEO Kimball presented the resolution and HCV Director Brady will answer questions. This initiative was 
brought forward by HCV Director Brady; as you know, we received notice from the State this year that as of 
July, we are losing the funding the State provided for housing people with HIV or AIDS, as the State has lost 
eligibility for the funding. HCV Director Brady continued, we have availability in our mainstream vouchers, 
and because HOPWA does qualify under the disability status, we are able to, as a preference, move these 
clients over to a mainstream voucher, which is a tenant-based voucher; and the funding for these clients is 
in a more secure funding. Chair Bernal asked if we are talking about a one-time preference or a preference 
to our regular voucher program from now on? HCV Director Brady answered, it’s a little of both. If the 
funding ends, we would have the opportunity to switch a household to a more secure funding; the hope 
would be this is a rare occasion, but it is possible it could happen again. And this is for 15 additional 
vouchers to the five we’ve already designated. 
o Commissioner Litvack motioned to approve Resolution #2025 -1125; Vice -Chair Nguyen seconded the 
motion. Chair Bernal asked if there was any further discussion. All Board members present (Chair Bernal, 
Vice-Chair Nguyen, Commissioners Johnson, Leonelli, Litvack, and Ramos ) voted In Favor of Resolution 
#2025-1125; none opposed. Resolution #2025-1125 is approved and takes effect immediately.
