City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption - 
On April 19, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1537, amending the Official Zoning Map of Draper City and approving a Development Agreement known as the Jarvie Estates Phase 2 Zoning Map Amendment and Development Agreement. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder's Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 20th day of April 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
