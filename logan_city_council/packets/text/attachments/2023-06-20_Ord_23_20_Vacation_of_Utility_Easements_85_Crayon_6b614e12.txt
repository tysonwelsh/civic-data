CITY OF LOGAN
ORDINANCE NO. 23-20

AN ORDINANCE VACATING PUBLIC UTILITY EASEMENTS BETWEEEN

PARCELS 02-167-0043 and 02-071-0041

    WHEREAS, the City has received a request submitted by the property owners where the subject public
utility easements reside, requesting that the public utility easement be vacated;

    WHEREAS, the City has authority by State Law to vacate streets, rights of way, and public utility
easements, from use by the public;

    WHEREAS, the property owners will dedicate new public utility easements along a realigned boundary
between the above referenced parcels;

    WHEREAS, the Logan Municipal Council finds there is good cause for vacating the public utility easement
and finds such action shall not materially injure the public or any person; and,

    WHEREAS, the proposed vacation of public utility easement, as shown and described in EXHIBIT A, has
been reviewed by the City and the City Council, and all appropriate hearings, postings, and notifications have
been performed in accordance with Utah law to obtain public comment regarding the proposed determination.

    THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MUNICIPAL COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF LOGAN, STATE OF UTAH, AS
FOLLOWS:

    SECTION 1: Pursuant to Section 10-9a-609.5, Utah Code Annotated, 1953 the public utility easement
shown and described in EXHIBIT A are hereby abandoned and vacated.

    SECTION 2: Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective upon publication.

The City of Logan has received a petition to vacate a public utility easement on property
located at 85 Crayon Court and at 701 Southwest Street, Logan, Utah. You are being
notified per Utah State Law Code 10-9a-609.5 because you are listed as an owner of record
of a parcel of property that is adjacent to the public utility easements, within 300 feet of the
public utility easements, or are an operator of utilities, culinary water, or sanitary sewer
facilities entity that may provide public utilities in the area that is being petitioned to vacate.

Please see attached image, Exhibit A, of the area outlined in red that is being petitioned.

A City Council workshop on the issue will be held on:

                    Date: June 20, 2023
                    Time: 5:30 PM
                    Location: City Hall 290 North 100 West Logan, UT 84321

A Public Hearing and City Council meeting to take action on the issue is scheduled to be
held on:

                    Date July 18, 2023
                    Time: 5:30 PM
                    Location: City Hall 290 North 100 West Logan, UT 84321


If you support the vacation of the public utility easement, the petition will be available in the
Engineering office at City Hall, for signing until 5:00 PM on the date indicated for the
public hearing.

If you have any additional questions, please call Darren Farar, City of Logan City Engineer
at (435)716-9160 or Darren.farar@loganutah.org.

If you are opposed to the action, please email or mail a letter of opposition to Darren Farar,
City of Logan City Engineer at Darren.farar@loganutah.org or 290 N 100 W, Logan, Utah
84321 and / or present your opposition at the Public Hearing.
