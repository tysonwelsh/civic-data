John Miller said the County had given him preliminary numbers for the public works contract.
The proposal is $5.7 million, which is a 44% increase from last year. Miller said Holladay will
be going up 110% and Taylorsville will be going up 27%. Miller said the County has not

2.1 Discussion on ZT-18-015, Amendments to Code with Respect to Noticing
Requirements
Blaine Gehring said staff was proposing the following noticing requirements.
Where an application is within 600 feet of a community council district boundary,
Millcreek will notify and solicit input from both community councils. If staff
receives an application on a month that a community council does not hold a
meeting, staff may hold a neighborhood meeting in lieu of a community council
meeting. Staff will still solicit the community councils’ input, but it will be in
writing, and not at a meeting. Notice will be sent to the community council seven
days in advance inviting them to attend the neighborhood meeting. The practice
of holding neighborhood meetings before a formal application is submitted would
be added to code. The applicant would bear the burden of noticing and scheduling
the meeting and providing staff with proof of the notice. These meeting would be
held for future land use map amendments, rezones and development agreements,
conditional use applications requiring new construction or if the use is anticipated
to create traffic or parking demand at least 20 percent greater than the existing
use, PUDs, and multi-family developments in a residential zone. Gehring also
proposed to keep the 300-foot notice for conditional use permits, special
exceptions, subdivision amendments, and the like, and go to a 600-foot notice for
zone changes and general plan amendments. He said that staff felt the
amendments were needed to better clarify public hearings and public meetings
and how they are noticed. All of the community councils and the Planning
Commission made favorable recommendations to approve the ordinance.

Council Member Marchant asked about the mailing cost. Francis Lilly said the
applicant will pay for the mailing at cost. Mayor Silvestrini said the mailing cost
would roughly double for the applicants by increasing the noticing radius.

Mayor Silvestrini asked for public comment. There was none.
