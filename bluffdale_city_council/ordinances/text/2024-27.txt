                               CITY OF BLUFFDALE,UTAH

                                ORDINANCE NO.2024-27


AN ORDINANCE ENACTING SECTION 11.160.310 OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY CODE TO
PERMIT''ALLOWABLE FEATURES" WITHIN RESIDENTIAL REAR YARD SETBACKS TO
COMPLY WITH REQUIREMENTS IN UTAH STATE CODE ENACTED BY THE 2024
LEGISLATURE THROUGH HOUSE BILL 476.


WHEREAS the 2024 Utah State Legislature passed House Bill 476 which modified
subdivision review procedures and allowed specific features within required residential
rear setbacks;

WHEREAS the City proposes an amendment to Chapter 11.160 Supplementary ProvisionSy
enacting Section 11.160.310 to comply with the changes to Utah State Code permitting
"Allowable Features" within residential rear setbacks;

WHEREAS a notice of public hearing for the proposed amendments was provided pursuant
to the Bluffdale City Code and Utah State Code;

WHEREAS on November 6,2024,the Planning Commission conducted a public hearing and
unanimously forwarded a positive recommendation of the proposed Text Amendment to
the City Council.

NOW,THEREFORE,BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE,
UTAH AS FOLLOWS:


       Section 1.     Adoption. Ordinance 2024-27 is hereby adopted, enacting Section
11.160.310 of the Bluffdale City Code as shown in Exhibit A.

       Section 2. Amendment of Conflicting Ordinances. If any ordinances,
resolutions, policies, or zoning maps of the City heretofore adopted are inconsistent
herewith they are hereby amended to comply with the provisions hereof. If they cannot be
amended to comply with the provisions hereof,they are hereby repealed.

       Section 3. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid or unenforceable,such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and
independent provision, and such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance
shall be severable.
       Section 4.    Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting as required by law.

PASSED AND APPROVED: December 11,2024
