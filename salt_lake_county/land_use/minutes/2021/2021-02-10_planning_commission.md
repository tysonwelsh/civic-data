Commissioner Cohen asked about the lack of a shoreline. Mr. Young said there has been quite a 
change in the movement of water at the Great Salt Lake. Important part of plan to highlight what is 
happening. Ms. Peters asked what year there was a house resolution. Mr. Young said 2019.  
 
Commissioner Cohen  said masterplan community zoning exists and created to have a planned 
community. Mr. Young said there is a PC zone, but there is a proposed recommendation to go back 
and update the process to improve it. 
 
Ms. Peters  turned over to Mr. McMurty, Avenue s Consulting, who  provided a presentation 
regarding the Transportation Element.  
 
Commissioner Collard said on the planning maps, large segments of land are to be remediated; 
what is the plan on the pit ? Mr. Young said there are three levels of remediation.  S evere mining 
areas would be reclaimed and vegetated and fenced off and not open to the public; level two would 
be reclaimed and open to recreation and access, trails. Level three would be open to development 
after reclamation, including recreation and light industrial.  
 
Commissioner Cohen asked about timing moving forward. Mr. Young said they paused for new 
county council members, but are close to policy discussions, and will see written plans and chapters 
after March.
