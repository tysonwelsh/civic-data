City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On July 19, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1550, amending the
official Land Use Map of Draper City, otherwise known as the Fitzgerald Property Land
Use Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s
Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 20th day of July 2022. Laura
Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
