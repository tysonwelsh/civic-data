PRESENTATION: INTERMOUNTAIN HEALTH CARE

Intermountain Healthcare Governing Board Chair Cyndi Gilbert introduced Dr. Patrick Carrol and
CEO Mitch Cloward.

Mr. Cloward introduced Dr. Paul Krakovitz as the new President of the Intermountain Desert
Region. Dr. Krakovitz explained that Intermountain has grown and will be focusing on three (3)
regions throughout several of the mid-western states. Those regions will be known as the Canyons
Region, Desert Region, and Peaks Region.

Mr. Carrol went on the present how Intermountain Healthcare functions as a non-profit and how
it gives back to the community through charity care. In 2021, Intermountain provided $23,071,876
in charitable care over 19,666 cases.

PRESENTATION: WASHINGTON COUNTY LIBRARY SYSTEM

Washington County Library Director Joel Tucker gave a presentation regarding the statistics of
library visits, circulation, programs, internet usage, budget expenditures, and technology
purchases.

Mr. Tucker also mentioned that several precautions have been implemented for flood mitigation
after the flood that heavily damaged the St. George branch. Additional parking is also being created
in collaboration with St. George City.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2951, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AGREEMENT __WITH__SIMPSON_ COULTER STUDIO TO COMPLETE

ENGINEERING, SECURITY, AND_ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN SERVICES FOR A
PURGATORY __ CORRECTIONAL __ FACILITY MEDICAL _ UNIT, _ BOOKING
EXPANSION, AND SHORT-TERM HOUSING UNIT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that the Purgatory Correctional Facility is in need
of a medical unit, booking expansion and short term housing unit. Simpson Coulter Studio has
been recommended as the architectural design service to complete engineering, security, an
architectural design services.

Mr. Snow clarified that the project is being funding by the American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) as
it will directly correlate to better medical preparedness and more adequate housing in order to stop
the prevention of current and future illnesses.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2022-2951, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement with Simpson Coulter Studio to
Complete Engineering, Security, and Architectural Design Services for a
Purgatory Correctional Facility Medical Unit, Booking Expansion, and Short-
Term Housing Unit. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting
aye.
