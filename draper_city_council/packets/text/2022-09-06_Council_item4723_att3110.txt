                                 ORDINANCE NO. 1560

         AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AN ADJUSTMENT TO THE COMMON
         BOUNDARY BETWEEN DRAPER CITY AND LEHI CITY.

      WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-2-419 provides that legislative bodies of two or more
municipalities having common boundaries may adjust their common boundaries; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City and Lehi City are municipalities within the State of Utah and
share a common boundary; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City and Lehi City desire to adjust their municipal boundaries as
provided by state law; and

        WHEREAS, on June 21, 2022, the Draper City Council adopted Resolution 22-33
outlining its interest in reviewing a municipal boundary adjustment with Lehi City; and

        WHEREAS, the boundary adjustment would adjust the common boundary between
Draper City and Lehi City as depicted in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated herein
by this reference.

       WHEREAS, the boundary adjustment would relocate approximately 1,035.49 acres
(“1,035.49 Acres”) of property from Draper City into Lehi City; and

       WHEREAS, it is more efficient and economical for Lehi City to provide municipal
services to the 1,035.49 Acres once they are developed; and

        WHEREAS, the City has not received a written protest to the boundary adjustment by
any title holder of any state-owned land or the Utah State Developmental Center Board as
listed in State Code 10-2-419(5); and

      WHEREAS, the proposed Common Boundary Adjustment set forth herein has been
reviewed by the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been held in
accordance with state law to obtain public input regarding the proposed Boundary
Adjustment.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

      Section 1.      Boundary Adjustment. The City of Draper approves the Boundary
Adjustment of 1,035.49 acres of property as provided in Exhibit A, otherwise known as the
Draper City-Lehi City Common Boundary Adjustment.




Ordinance No. 1560                          1          Draper City- Lehi City Common Boundary Adjustment
        Section 2.   Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

      Section 3.   Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective when each
municipality has adopted an ordinance under Utah Code § 10-2-419(5) approving the
boundary adjustment.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON THE
6th DAY OF SEPTEMBER, 2022.
