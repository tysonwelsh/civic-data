Background
In January of 2025, Cache Valley Transit District (Connect) completed their new modern
and larger facility located in North Logan City. All operations and equipment from this
location at 754 West 600 North have moved to the new property, leaving this facility
vacant. The applicant is requesting a rezone from Public to Commercial Service with the
intention of subsequently selling the land. The 4.47-acre property currently contains an
office/administration building, shop building, and a large vehicle carport. Landscaping and
paved parking lots complete the site improvements, with fencing enclosing the parking and
shop areas. The property is currently accessed via three driveways approaching 600 North
and 800 West.

GENERAL PLAN
The Future Land Use Plan (FLUP) identifies this area as Public (PUB). The public
designation is reserved for public facilities such as government offices, safety services,
public schools, and similar public services. A change to the FLUP to recognize the change
from a public ownership and/or use to private ownership will be handled within the General
Plan update process.

LAND DEVELOPMENT CODE
The Land Development Code (LDC) describes the CS zone as a designation that fills the
need between industrial and commercial land uses. CS areas are intended for light
manufacturing, warehousing, wholesaling, construction support services, and limited
commercial. CS zoning is not intended for residential or heavy manufacturing.

EXISTING ZONING
The current PUB zoning designation would be appropriate if the property were to be
purchased and used by a public entity. The applicant has contacted several local
government entities, including the City of Logan, to see if there was interest in the property
for a public use. According to the applicant, there was no interest from public entities. The
surrounding area is currently a variety of land uses and zoning designations. To the east,
the 11-acre property contains commercial self-storage units. North of the site is currently
vacant and previously contained a landscaping business. To the south and west, the City of
Logan owns and operates the City's Service Center and Light and Power Department
building. 600 North acts as a larger boundary in the Bridger Neighborhood, with Industrial
zoning to the north and a mix of Commercial, Mixed Use, and Commercial Service zoning
to the south.

SUMMARY
With 600 North being a boundary between Industrial and Commercial zoning in the
Bridger Neighborhood, staff considers the proposal for CS appropriate. If this property is
purchased by a private entity (most likely buyer), the FLUP designation of PUB will be
inappropriate and will be evaluated during the next FLUP amendment cycle. The
surrounding area is a mixed bag, with storage units, service centers, warehousing, and
manufacturing. The CS zone permits or conditionally permits land uses that are in a similar
character with the current surrounding land uses and built environment. The CS zone

supply, wholesales, warehousing, gas stations, truck stops, lumberyards, and similar uses

RECOMMENDED FINDINGS FOR REZONE
The Planning Commission bases its decision on the following findings supported in the
administrative record for this project:
    1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as PUB. PUB is only appropriate if the
        property owner is a public entity.
    2. 600 North is a regional boundary between IP to the north and CS, COM, and MU
        to the south, and this application for CS is consistent with that pattern.
    3. The CS zone permits and conditional permits land uses that are similar or
        compatible with the current land uses and developments in the area.
    4. The design standards, height limitations, and setbacks in the Land Development
        Code will ensure compatibility for adjacent property use and enjoyment.

PUBLIC HEARING - Consideration of a proposed resolution approving the Wilson
Neighborhood Plan – Resolution 25-19 – Russ Holley (54:10)

At the May 6, 2025 Council meeting, Planner Russ Holley addressed the Council regarding
the proposed resolution. The neighborhoods are divided by older elementary school
boundaries. They have since evolved and are somewhat different now. The Wilson
Neighborhood has a long range of older structures. Generally, the further East, the newer
the neighborhood. The primary zones of the area are NR-4 & NR-6. The Cliffside area is
primarily owner-occupied, and the island is more non-owner occupied.

Wilson Neighborhood Recommendations:

   1. Future Logan City documents and planning efforts should recognize sub-
      neighborhood names Island, Cliffside, Canyon Road, & Sumac.
   2. Zoning Map amendments should consider changes inside and near downtown
      to better facilitate new affordable and obtainable infill housing.
   3. When developed, the large vacant areas south of Cliffside should include
      multiple street connections to for better traffic distribution and safety response.
   4. Future zoning along the edge of downtown should encourage higher density
      development, as that is the preferred location indicated by residents.
   5. Zoning Map amendments should change Public Zoning designations to
      Recreation for the large eastern areas of natural open space.
   6. Local programs should be established to help incentivize and retain more
      owner-occupied homes in the Island area.
   7. Local housing programs should be established to better inform and
      collectively harness buying power for potential home buyers.
   8. More efficient methods of construction and alternative building materials
      should be applied in appropriate locations for more affordable housing units.
