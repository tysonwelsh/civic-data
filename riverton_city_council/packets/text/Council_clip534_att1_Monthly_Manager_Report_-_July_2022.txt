                          Riverton Police Department Monthly Report

                                               July, 2022
   Total calls for service increased in June from the previous two months and total cases were at a
   high for the year during the month. This is something we typically see as we transition into the
   summer months. All types of crimes seem to increase with more activities which are not routine
   during the winter months or while school is in session.

   We observed a slight increase in the number of vehicle burglaries during the months of May and
   June. We generally see vehicle burglaries increase during the summer months and that is why
   we focus much of our patrol time in our neighborhoods as we try to identify suspicious behavior.

   Our officers spent many hours providing security during the Mountain View Village grand
   opening activities and it was a great opportunity to interact with our residents in that setting. We
   are so appreciative of the donated substation space which will be opening soon in the Mountain
   View Village office building. This space will provide our officers with a professional
   environment on the west side of town to meet with citizens, write reports, and otherwise engage
   the public. Additionally, we can provide a physical presence at what will surely be a popular
   destination for both Riverton residents and those from surrounding communities.

   Finally, I want to thank our officers who worked many long hours during the Town Days
   celebrations and sacrificed time with their families during the Independence Day holiday to
   ensure those who attended our events could enjoy the festivities and feel safe.
