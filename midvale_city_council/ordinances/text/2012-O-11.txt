                                   MID VALE CITY, UTAH

                                   ORDINANCE NO. 2012-11

               AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 2, ADMINISTRATIVE CODE,
                         OF THE MID VALE MUNICIPAL CODE

       WHEREAS, the City Council desires to amend Section 2.12.040 the Midvale Municipal
Code to reflect the current salaries of the Mayor and City Council as adopted in the FY 2013
budget; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council finds it in the City's best interest to amend Title 2,
Administrative Code, to comply with Utah State Code and provide a cohesive municipal code; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council of Midvale City, Utah, finds it appropriate to adopt such an
ordinance in an effort to provide comprehensive information to the citizens of Midvale.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Midvale City, Utah as
follows:

      Section 1.     The City Council desires to adopt Title 2, Section 2.12.040 of the Midvale
City Municipal Code, amending the current salaries of the mayor and city council as follows:

       Section 2.      This ordinance shall be effective upon date of first publication.
