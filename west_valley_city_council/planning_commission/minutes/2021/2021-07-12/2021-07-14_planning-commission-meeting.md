SV-2-2021
Alley Vacation between Warnock Avenue and Stratford Avenue
1685 West between 2525 and 2565 South


BACKGROUND

The West Valley City Engineering Division is requesting consideration to vacate the 16-foot alley located
between Warnock Avenue and Stratford Avenue. The location of the alley is approximately 1685 West
between 2525 and 2565 South.

The alley is located within the Chesterfield Plat. This plat was recorded with the County Recorder’s
Office in July 1914. The plat identifies a 16-foot alley along the east property line of those properties
fronting Redwood Road. It is presumed that this alley was to provide service capabilities for
properties that would be used for commercial purposes.

While the idea behind this was well intended, these alleys have not been utilized for that purpose.
To staffs knowledge, these alleys have never been formally improved. As a common practice, many
residents living adjacent to these alleys have fenced this area off. In some instances, residents have
used the alley as part of their yard and for parking. The vacation of these alleys generally allows for
property owners to take better care of these areas than has previously been done.

Per State Law, when a street or alley is vacated, each abutting owner will end up with half of the
vacated property. In this case, 8 feet of additional property would then belong to the property owners
on the east as well as the west side of the alley. If a property owner did not want their portion, they
could negotiate transferring their portion to the adjacent landowner.

In the case of this particular alley, there is an existing storm drain line. The City will request that an
easement be recorded that will accommodate the storm drain line. This will be done with the vacating
ordinance that ends up being recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office.

According to City Ordinance, streets and/or alley vacations shall be reviewed by the Planning
Commission with a recommendation to the City Council.

RECOMMENDATION

1.      Approve the vacation of the 16-foot alley located between Warnock Avenue and Stratford
        Avenue.

2.      Continue the application due to issues raised at the public hearing.

               property, therefore, they are requesting the vacation.

CA-5-2021
Superior Water and Air LLC
3536 S. 1950 W.
1.71 acres

Superior Water and Air LLC, represented by Jason Kelley, is requesting conditional use
amendment approval for outside storage at 3536 South 1950 West. The property is currently zoned
M (manufacturing) and the General Plan designation is light manufacturing.

The surrounding zones are C-2 with a portion of property to the southwest that is zoned M,
Manufacturing. The surrounding land uses include auto service, a Union Hall, barbershop and
Million Dollar Pawn to the north, auto service and auto/service/sales to the south. There is an
Advance Auto Parts to the east and Advanced Body and Paint to the west.

This property was recently rezoned from C-2 to M for the primary purpose of accommodating
outside storage. The approval was subject to a development agreement that allows for the current
C-2 uses to continue with the addition of only outside storage. Exhibit B has been included in
your packet for review.

Superior Water and Air LLC has been at this location for approximately 20 years (C-29-2000)
and their business model has evolved over time and they have grown as a company. They now
have equipment and vehicles that remain on the site for longer periods of time and have a need
for outside storage. They have also implemented a recycling program so there is occasionally
equipment and materials stored outside before they get recycled. Salt Lake Community College
will also temporarily be using the building on the southwest corner of the site for
classes/instruction so some of the storage within that building has been displaced and relocated
throughout the site.

All outside storage areas are paved with asphalt except for the section located directly along the
west side of the property. This area was originally landscaping and will be replaced with a gravel
surface to provide additional storage areas. Based off the M Zone standards of 5% landscaping
that would allow for some flexibility for them to reorganize their site. All other landscaping on
site will remain. The parking island to the south of the building no longer has trees and is not
required to be landscaping at this point but Mr. Kelley has indicated the lawn will remain in place
at this time. If it is modified in the future, it just needs to be maintained and improved based off
the use.

The site is currently secured with a 6’ tall chain link fence. Slats will be added to the east side of
this fence to screen the outside storage from the adjacent roadway, 1950 West. All storage
structures, sheds, cargo containers and dumpsters must comply with City Code and all applicable
building code requirements/permits.

C-15-21
Malone Powersports
Use Expansion and Landscape Exception
1379 West 3300 South
M Zone (5.16 Acres)

The applicant, Malone Powersports, is requesting a conditional use for expansion of a
recreational vehicle sales use (ATV/UTV) at 1379 West 3300 South, as well as an
exception to section 7-10 of the WVC Municipal Code, Standards for Landscaping Along
High Image Arterial Streets.

This property is zoned M, Manufacturing. The West Valley City General Plan designates
this area as General Commercial. The surrounding zone is C-2 to the north and east and M
to the west and south. The surrounding uses include Jones Heating to the north, Questar
Gas and GHID facilities to the east, Light Industrial/warehouse uses to the south, Dura
Crete to the west.

This property is also located within the Jordan River Overlay Zone (JROZ). The use is
considered “Motorized Outdoor Recreation Equipment” and is currently prohibited in
that overlay zone. However, per 7-14-203, existing businesses may continue and can
expand with approval by the Planning Commission.

Malone Powersports acquired Plaza Cycle in 2019. They have also now acquired the
Highland Golf site to the east, which was approved for an expansion in 2019 (C-15-19),
and the Volkswagen auto service center to the west. They will use the Highland Golf area
for service/offices, the existing Malone Powersports building for sales/parts and then the
auto service building does not have an intended use at this point and the current lease
expires this month. That area will be cleaned out and used for storage for the time being.

Now that the parcels are all consolidated Malone Powersports, in coordination with West
Valley City RDA and a Cares Act Grant, are looking to cover/abandon the canal to
provide better access and circulation internally on site. The final canal design and process
is being coordinated with the Canal Company. Then the back portion of the Highland Golf
property will also be improved and paved for additional storage and parking. This is the
use expansion portion of the application.

Since the site has frontage on 3500 South, which is a High Image Arterial, the
requirements set forth in 7-10 are triggered for this part of this project. The ordinance
requires a 15’ landscaped berm, a 10’ sidewalk and a 5’ strip of additional landscaping
and street lighting. Based off the existing site layout, access, and circulation the applicant
is requesting an exception to that streetscape. They would like a 10’ landscaped area and
5’ sidewalk in the center of the site to accommodate the existing parking and access.

 Then the remaining sections of the site they are requesting a 10’ setback, a 10’ sidewalk
 and then a 10’ display area.

 Staff has discussed alternative designs and circulation ideas with the applicant. These
 include internal circulation changes that would allow for more customer parking areas,
 more strategic consolidated display areas and would not require such a large exception to
the streetscape code.

UDOT has also indicated if an internal connection is provided that they may require an
access to be closed. This would impact the design of the site, so the applicant is
continuing to coordinate that review to ensure the design is completed as required.

As discussed in the Study Meeting if the exception to 7-10 is not granted at this time,
then the Highland Golf site would need to comply with the original approval from C-15-
19 which has the streetscape design as required by Ordinance with no exceptions. Then
the back portion of that site could be expanded as indicated on their plan and improved
with asphalt for outside storage and parking.

The remaining portions of the site would need to be maintained. Display would not be
permitted in the landscaped areas and all landscaping would need to be re-installed and
repaired as originally required. See C-59-86. The building façade is also in need of repair
and maintenance. There is currently a pole sign on the property for Plaza Cycle. The
applicant intends to keep this sign on site and is working on coordinating the Malone
Powersports name with Plaza Cycle, so the sign does not become off premise. Then the
Highland Golf pole sign will be removed.

Staff Alternatives:


Approval of the Use Expansion, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the
public hearing as well as the following:


           1. The existing business, Malone Powersports, may expand to the east on
              parcel 15-27-480-030 per the approved plans and requirements set forth in
              C-15-19 (Highland Golf).

           2. All existing buildings must be maintained and repaired.

           3. All existing landscaped areas west of Highland Golf must be repaired and
              replaced with live plant material per the original approvals (50%
              lawn/50% live plant material and one tree every 30’).

           4. Signage shall meet the standards set forth in Title 11 of the WVC
              Municipal Code.

           5. Must meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies.
              6. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Denial, of the request for an exception to the Standards for Landscaping Along High
Image Arterial Streets (7-10). The criteria in 7-10-104 have not been met and the site layout
should be examined further to provide better internal circulation, parking areas and designated
display areas.

                requirement is a fifteen-foot landscaped berm, a ten-foot sidewalk, and a five-foot
                buffer.

                they started to have enforcement issues with meeting code, and they were looking
                to move their business out of the City. Mr. Hicks said that he spoke to Jody Knapp
                and Jeff Jackson, and they felt the City and Malone could work together to keep
