                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-65

RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A FRANCHISE AGREEMENT
                   WITH GOOGLE FIBER UTAH
       WHEREAS, GOOGLE FIBER UTAH L.L.C. (“Google”) desires to install, maintain,
operate and/or control a fiber optic infrastructure network in Riverton City (“City”) owned right
of ways to provide internet service to City residents; and
       WHEREAS, it is both reasonable and necessary for City to allow the installation of
Google fiber optic infrastructure network in the City owned public right of ways within the City
and thereafter to maintain, operate and/or control said network.
       WHEREAS, the City finds and determines that the public interest in health, safety and
welfare will be enhanced by the provision of the proposed service; and
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
   1. The City Council concludes that entering into the above described agreement with
      Google will enhance the public health, safety and welfare and authorizes the City to enter
      into and sign the above described agreement.

   2. The Mayor is authorized to execute a Franchise Agreement with Google Fiber, Utah
      L.L.C. in substantially similar form to the Agreement attached as Exhibit A, once it is
      approved as to legal form by the City Attorney. Prior to execution, the Mayor is directed
      to arrange for the effective date of the Agreement to run consecutively to any recent
      amendments to the Riverton City Engineering Designs and Specifications Manual which
      address trenching methods for installation of subsurface fiber communication lines in
      City rights of way.

   3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 14th day of
December 2021 by the following vote:
