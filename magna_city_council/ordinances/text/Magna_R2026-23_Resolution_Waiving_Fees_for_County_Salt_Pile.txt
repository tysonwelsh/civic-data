MAGNA CITY COUNCIL
RESOLUTION #R2026-23 DATE: April 28 2026
A RESOLUTION WAIVING FEES AND BOND REQUIREMENTS FOR SALT
LAKE COUNTY IMPROVEMENTS ON 8000 WEST 4100 SOUTH, MAGNA CITY
WHEREAS, the Magna City Council (the “Council’”) may waive certain fees and
bond requirements pursuant to Magna City Code Section 2.24.080 (B)(1); and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Public Works Department (“SLCO”) is
constructing a fabric shed to enclose a salt pile on approximately 8000 West 4100 South,
in Magna City; and
WHEREAS, in connection with the construction of the shed, SLCO is installing
improvements including additional asphalt, curb, gutter, and sidewalk (together, the
“Improvements”); and
WHEREAS, SLCO requested that the Council waive the fees and bond
requirements for the Improvements; and
WHEREAS, because SLCO is a governmental entity, the Council desires to
waive the fees and bond requirements for the Improvements.
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MAGNA CITY COUNCIL
EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:
1. The Council hereby waives the fees and bond requirements for the
Improvements SLCO is installing on 8000 West 4100 South, in Magna City.
2. Staff is authorized to issue permits and approvals for the Improvements
without payment of the fees or posting of bonds.
