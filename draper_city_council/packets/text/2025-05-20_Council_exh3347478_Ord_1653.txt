                               ORDINANCE NO. 1653



      AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLE 7-5
      OF THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO ALIGN WITH UTAH STATE
      CODE SECTION 11-46-303, REGARDING THE MANAGEMENT AND
      EXEMPTION OF COMMUNITY CATS.

     WHEREAS, the City of Draper recognizes the desire of the community to
manage feral and free-roaming cat populations in a humane and effective manner;
and

       WHEREAS, Utah State Code Section 11-46-303, part of the Community Cat
Act, provides a framework for the release and management of community cats,
including exemptions from certain requirements and guidelines for their care; and

      WHEREAS, the City of Draper seeks to align its municipal code with state law
to support community cat programs that promote sterilization, vaccination, and
responsible oversight while ensuring public health and safety; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council finds that permitting community cats
under regulated conditions will benefit the community by reducing the population
of unsterilized feral cats and minimizing euthanasia rates in animal shelters;

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. PURPOSE. The purpose of this ordinance is to establish provisions
within the Draper City Municipal Code to permit and regulate community cats in
compliance with Utah State Code Section 11-46-303, fostering a community cat
program that supports humane population control and responsible management.


       Section 2. AMENDMENT TO DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE
       The Draper City Municipal Code is hereby amended to reflect the changes in
the attached Exhibit A.

       Section 3. CORRECTION OF EDITING ERRORS. The City Attorney is
authorized to correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis
errors prior to submitting the Ordinance to Sterling Codifiers.
       Section 4. SEVERABILITY. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance
is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 5. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Ordinance shall become effective
immediately upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever
is closer to the date of final passage.

7-5-010: DEFINITIONS:
The following terms, as used in this chapter, shall have the following meanings. The
examples contained herein are provided for illustrative purposes only and are not intended
to be mutually exclusive or inclusive of all possible animal species regulated by this Code.
ANIMAL AT LARGE: Any domestic animal, whether or not licensed, not under restraint, as
defined herein.
ANIMAL BREEDER: An establishment that breeds dogs, cats, and other household pets for
sale from the premises on which they were bred and reared.
ANIMAL SHELTER: A facility owned and/or operated by a governmental entity, by an
animal welfare organization incorporated within the State of Utah for the purpose of
preventing cruelty to animals, or by any properly authorized establishment used for the
care and custody of seized, stray, homeless, quarantined, abandoned or unwanted dogs,
cats or other small domestic animals.
ANIMAL UNDER RESTRAINT: Any animal under the control of its owner or person having
charge, care, custody or control, except a dog shall not be considered under the control of
the owner unless on a leash or lead, confined within a vehicle, or within the real property
limits of the owner.
BITE: Any actual puncture, tear or abrasion to person or property inflicted by the teeth of
an animal.
CAT: Any feline of the domesticated types.
CATTERY: An establishment having three (3) or more cats of any age for the purpose of
boarding, letting for hire, or training for profit.
COMMUNITY CAT: Any feral or free-roaming cat this is without visibly discernable or
microchip owner identification of any kind, and has been sterilized, vaccinated, and ear-
tipped.
COMMUNITY CAT CARETAKER: Any person other than an owner who provides food, water,
or shelter to a community cat or community cat colony.
COMMUNITY CAT COLONY: A group of cats that congregate together. Although not every
cat in a colony may be a community cat, any cats owned by individuals that congregate with
a colony are considered part of it.
COMMUNITY CAT PROGRAM: A program pursuant to which feral cats are sterilized,
vaccinated against rabies, ear-tipped, and returned to the location where they congregate.
DOG: Any Canis familiaris over four (4) months of age. Any Canis familiaris under four (4)
months of age is a puppy.
DOMESTIC ANIMAL: Animals or fowl domesticated by man and accustomed to living in or
about the habitation of man, such as dogs, cats, horses, and sheep.
EAR-TIPPING: Removing approximately a quarter-inch off the tip of a cat's left ear while
the cat is anesthetized for sterilization.
ENCLOSURE: A fence or structure of sufficient height and construction to enclose and
confine an animal.
FERAL: An animal that is normally domesticated but has reverted to the wild.
GROOMERY: An establishment for the grooming, bathing, clipping, and nonmedical care of
household pets. Such uses may also include the sale of grooming and care items, as well as
accessory items for the keeping of household pets. Such uses shall not include the sale,
breeding, training, or boarding of animals.
GUARD DOG: A working dog which must be kept in a fenced run or other suitable enclosure
during business hours, or on a leash or under absolute control while working, so it cannot
come into contact with the public.
HEALTH DEPARTMENT: The Salt Lake County Health Department.
IMPOUNDED: The taking of an animal into the custody of the Animal Control Division or
other law enforcement agency.
KENNEL: An establishment having three (3) or more dogs, of any age, for the purpose of
boarding, letting for hire, or training for profit.
LEASH OR LEAD: Any chain, rope or device used to restrain an animal.
PERSON: Any individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, trust, or other
legal entity however designated.
PET SALES: Any establishment wherein dogs, cats, small household pets such as birds, fish,
reptiles, or other household pets of any age are kept or displayed for the purpose of retail
sales. This definition does not include kennel; cattery; animals for sale by an animal
breeder; or the sale of small household pets that may be associated with another allowed
use. Pet sales does not include animal adoptions from a city or county animal shelter,
animal control agency, or a non-profit animal welfare organization incorporated within the
State of Utah for the purpose of preventing cruelty to animals, or by any properly
authorized establishment used for the care and custody of seized, stray, homeless,
quarantined, abandoned or unwanted dogs, cats or other small domestic animals.
PROVOKED: Any deliberate act by a person toward any animal done with the intent to
tease, torment, abuse, assault or otherwise cause a reaction by the animal. Any act by a
person toward an animal done with the intent to discourage or prevent an animal from
attacking shall not be considered to be a provocation.
QUARANTINE: The isolation of an animal in a substantial enclosure so that the animal is
not subject to contact with other animals or unauthorized persons.
SPONSOR: Any person or organization that traps feral cats, sterilizes, vaccinates against
rabies, and ear-tips them before returning them to the location where they were trapped. a
sponsor may be any animal humane society, non-profit organization, animal rescue,
adoption organization, or a designated community cat caretaker that also maintains
written records on community cats.

STRAY: Any "animal at large,", as defined herein.
WILD ANIMAL: Any animal of a species that in its natural life is wild, including nonhuman
primates, European ferrets, wild animal hybrids, alligators, crocodiles, venomous snakes,
lizards, or any animal that creates an immediate threat or danger to a human being or
public health, or any animal which cannot be vaccinated effectively for rabies, such as
bears, porcupines, raccoons, skunks and weasels. (Ord. 1397, 6-18-2019)


7-5-070: RABIES VACCINATION:
A. Required: Rabies vaccination is required for all dogs and cats, except community cats as
defined in Section 7-5-010. The owner or person having the charge, care, custody or control
of a cat or dog which is four (4) months of age or over, or any person permitting a cat or
dog which is four (4) months of age or over to habitually be on or remain, or lodged or fed
within such person's house, yard or premises, shall have said animal vaccinated within
thirty (30) days after it reaches such age. Unvaccinated dogs or cats over four (4) months of
age acquired by the owner or moved into the jurisdiction must be vaccinated within ten
(10) days of acquisition or arrival.
B. Procedure: Rabies vaccination shall be performed by a licensed veterinarian using a
modified virus rabies vaccine approved by the health department. All vaccines shall be
conducted in accordance with the standards contained in the "Compendium Of Animal
Rabies Control", as amended, published by the National Association Of State Public Health
Veterinarians, Inc. It shall be the duty of each veterinarian vaccinating any animal for rabies
to complete a standardized duplicate certificate of rabies vaccination requesting the name,
address and telephone number of the owner; the breed, sex, color, age and name of the
animal; and the date, type, tag number and a manufacturer's serial number of the vaccine
administered. It shall be the duty of each veterinarian to specify on the certificate the date
of vaccination, the type of vaccine used, and the date when revaccination is required. A
copy of the certificate shall be distributed to the owner and the original retained by the
issuing veterinarian. The veterinarian and the owner shall retain their copies of the
certificate of the interval between vaccinations specified in this section.
C. Tag: Upon vaccination and certification, the veterinarian shall provide the owner with a
metal or durable plastic serially numbered rabies vaccination tag. The owner shall attach
the tag to the collar or harness of the animal and see that the collar and tag are constantly
worn. Failure to attach the tag as provided shall be in violation of this chapter, except that
dogs which are kept for show purposes are exempt from wearing the collar and tag.
D. Revaccination: Every dog and cat, excluding community cats, shall be revaccinated prior
to the expiration of the previous vaccination and prior to licensing or renewal of license if
the previous vaccination will expire within the term of the new license.
E. Community Cat Exemption: Community cats are exempt from additional rabies
vaccination requirements once initially vaccinated as part of a community cat program.
Records of such vaccinations must be maintained for at least three years and be available
upon request.
F. Exceptions: This provision shall not apply to veterinarian or kennel operators
temporarily maintaining on their premises animals owned by others, municipal shelters or
animal welfare agencies incorporated in the state of Utah, or to any animal owned by a
person temporarily remaining within the jurisdiction for less than thirty (30) days;
provided, that such animal is kept under strict supervision of the owner. It shall be
unlawful to bring any animal into the jurisdiction which does not comply with city, state
and federal animal health and import laws.
FG. Impoundment: An animal without a valid rabies vaccination tag may be impounded by
the animal control officer. The owner of a vaccinated animal impounded hereunder may
reclaim its animal by paying all impound fees and furnishing proof of rabies vaccination.
The owner of an unvaccinated animal may reclaim its animal by paying all impound fees
and furnishing proof of rabies vaccination within seventy two (72) hours of release from
impoundment. Any animal not reclaimed within the minimum holding period under section
7-5-230 of this chapter may be disposed of pursuant to provisions provided therein. (Ord.
211, 10-1-1996)


7-5-130: ANIMALS RUNNING AT LARGE:
It shall be unlawful for the owner or person having charge, care, custody or control of any
animal to allow such animal at any time to run at large, except for community cats
participating in a recognized community cat program. The owner or person charged with
responsibility for an animal found running at large shall be strictly liable for a violation of
this section regardless of the precautions taken to prevent the escape of the animal and
regardless of whether or not he knows that the animal is running at large. (1994 Code)


7-5-160: HARBORING STRAY ANIMALS:
It shall be unlawful for any person, except an animal shelter, sponsor or community cat
caretaker, to harbor or keep any lost or strayed animal. Whenever any animal other than a
community cat shall be found which appears to be lost or strayed, it shall be the duty of the
finder to notify the Animal Control Division within twenty-four (24) hours, and the division
shall impound the animal as herein provided. A community cat may be released by an
animal shelter to a sponsor operating a community cat program prior to the five-day
holding period required under Utah Code Section 11-46-103. (1994 Code)
