BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Text Amendment to the DC zone (Exhibit B).
The applicant is requesting that a text amendment be approved to allow for ‘medical
service’ uses to be permitted in the DC zoning designation. The Destination Commercial
(DC) zone is generally located on the west side of Interstate 15 and north of Bangerter
Highway.

ANALYSIS
General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Destination
Commercial land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is
characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the DC zoning classification (Exhibit D). Draper City
Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020(C)(9) states: The purpose of the DC zone is to
“provide an area that is uniquely regional in nature, with lodging, food establishments,
retail, office, service uses, and entertainment as the only allowed uses.” The DC zoning
designation is identified by the General Plan as a preferred zoning classification for the
Destination Commercial land use designation. The DC zone is surrounded by a mix of
zoning designations including the following: RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square
foot minimum), RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot minimum), R3 (Single
Family Residential, 13,000 square foot minimum), RM1 (Multiple Family Residential), CSD-
SWF (Southwestern Furniture Commercial Special District), CSD-DRC (Dahle Retail Center
Commercial Special District), CSD-DPMU (Draper Pointe Mixed Use Commercial Special
District, TSD (Transit Station District), and M1 (Light Manufacturing).

Text Amendment. The applicant is amending Table 9-11-1 of the DCMC and proposes to
change ‘medical service’ from not permitted to permitted (Exhibit D). This modification


Elite Carpal Tunnel Text Amendment                               App. # TEXTMAP-166-2021

would allow for medical uses within the zoning designation. Medical service is defined in
DCMC Section 9-3-040 as follows:

        MEDICAL SERVICE: An establishment providing therapeutic, preventive, or corrective
        personal treatment services on an outpatient basis by physicians, dentists, and
        other practitioners of the medical or healing arts, as well as the provision of medical
        testing and analysis services.

The request is being made as there is a user who desires to locate within the DC zone and
cannot at this time. Should the change be approved, the user could open a new medical
service business.

Criteria For Approval. A text amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion
of the City Council and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a
recommendation to the City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the
following factors in Section 9-5-060(E) of the DCMC:

2.      Text Amendments:
        a.    Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and
              policies of the city's general plan;
        b.    Whether a proposed amendment furthers the specific purpose statements of
              the zoning ordinance;
        c.    Whether the proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the
              request and there is sufficient justification for a modification to the zoning
              ordinance;
        d.    The proposed amendment will not create a conflict with any other section or
              part of this title or the general plan;
        e.    Whether the potential effects of the proposed amendment have been
              evaluated and determined not to be detrimental to public health, safety, or
              welfare and represents an overall community benefit; and
        f.    The extent to which a proposed text amendment implements best current,
              professional practices of urban planning, design, and engineering practices.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request, receive public
comment, and make a decision based on the findings listed below and the criteria for
approval, or denial, as listed within the staff report.

The findings for approval as are follows:

1.       The proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the
         city's general plan;
2.       The proposed amendment furthers the specific purpose statements of the zoning
         ordinance;
3.       The proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the request and there
         is sufficient justification for a modification to the zoning ordinance;
4.       The proposed amendment will not create a conflict with any other section or part of
         this title or the general plan;
5.       The potential effects of the proposed amendment have been evaluated and
         determined not to be detrimental to public health, safety, or welfare and represents
         an overall community benefit; and
6.       The proposed text amendment implements best current, professional practices of
         urban planning, design, and engineering practices.

The findings for denial as are follows:

1.       The proposed amendment is not consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the
         city's general plan;
2.       The proposed amendment does not further the specific purpose statements of the
         zoning ordinance;
3.       The proposed amendment is not appropriate given the context of the request and
         there is not sufficient justification for a modification to the zoning ordinance;
4.       The proposed amendment will create a conflict with another section or part of this
         title or the general plan;
5.       The potential effects of the proposed amendment have been evaluated and
         determined to be detrimental to public health, safety, or welfare and represents an
         overall community benefit; and
6.       The proposed text amendment does not implements best current, professional
         practices of urban planning, design, and engineering practices.
