Nephi City Work Session Minutes
February 13, 2024
7:00 p.m.
21 E 100 N
Nephi, UT 84648

	•	City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed some proposed airport fees with the council. 
Because of the increased use at the airport more services are being demanded at the airport.

	•	 Recreation Director Craig Oswald presented the Council with a financial report for the Utah Super State Wrestling event and a draft of a Hive Manager job description.  Councilor Worwood suggested hiring a very capable part-time administrative assistant. Councilor Parady stated we need to hire the right person as a full-time Hive manager.   A discussion  ensued concerning  the lack of parking at the Hive.  Councilor Parady suggested maybe having a shuttle service for large events at the Hive.  Councilor Callaway suggested notifying the homeowners near the Hive when a big event is going to happen.  A suggestion was made to put signs up stating that cars would be towed if they are parked in front of driveways, mailboxes or fire hydrants. Mr. Atkinson suggested adding a parking mitigation fee to the rental fees that could help expand the parking areas.  

	•	Mr. Atkinson reviewed the Annexation Policy Plan and Annexation Area Map. The Council directed staff and the Planning Commission to take a look at updating the City’s  Annexation Area Map.  

	•	 Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council the annual Municipal Wastewater Planning Program Survey.

	•	Mayor Seely wanted to discuss where the staff and the Planning Commission was coming on drafting a short-term rental code.  Mr. Atkinson said that part of the problem with allowing short-term rentals is that it reduces the inventory of long-term rentals and adds to the problem of the housing shortage.  A discussion ensued about affordable housing issues. Councilor Parady expressed that he didn’t think that short-term rentals would affect the affordable housing issue because usually the short-term rentals are bigger family type homes.  Councilor Travis Worwood said that from his experience there is a real shortage for hotel rooms and short-term rentals.  The Council directed staff to have the Planning Commission look into adding code that governs short-term rentals. 

	•	Mayor Seely brought up the problem of semi trucks parking around the south exit and around Maverik that is causing a dangerous traffic situation.  Mr. Atkinson said that the issue is keeping the visual corridors open.  Councilor Cowan said that she knows that this is a problem even around Red Cliffs Elementary.  Councilor Travis Worwood said that we should discuss this issue with UDOT.  

	•	 Mr. Atkinson updated the Council about the Crimson Flats Development.  The preliminary plat should be finished very soon.  He reported that he hasn’t heard anything about the federal grant that the City was hoping to get.  He reported that he is working on a RFQ for a police station design.  

	•	 Mr. Atkinson presented an independent contractor agreement with Ken Tuttle for professional services to help the City write a policy for water rights/fee in lieu for annexations.  The Council directed staff to bring back the contract to the next regular meeting for official approval.  

	•	Mr. Atkinson updated the council about HB13- Infrastructure Districts.  He said the bill is looking like it will be adopted and could possibly affect us as new subdivisions are built. 

	•	Administratie and City Council Updates:
	•	Mr. Atkinson reminded the Council about the Clover
	•	 Creek substation tour available to the Council this next week. 
	•	Mr. Atkinson reported that Class C Road money has significantly increased because of a rural street funding that is new and also the County added an additional tax this year. 
	•	City Attorney Wright reported that he had attended a meeting with Brent Boswell with the Zero Fatalities people and that they are willing to help us do an education campaign dealing with ATV safety.  
	•	The Council directed staff to do another ATV enforcement blitz again this spring, 
	•	Councilor Cowan reported that the Planning Commission meeting has been rescheduled from the 14th to the 21st. 
	•	Councilor Cowan gave an update on the Miss Nephi Pageant that will be held Mar 16, 2024.
	•	Councilor Skip Worwood reported that there are many goat-head weeds along the side of the roads that need to be abated.
	•	Councilor Parady reported on the inland port project and said that the organizers would like to come in and visit the Council again about the inland port.  

	•	   Councilor Skip Worwood moved to convene into regular meeting and then convene into executive session to discuss the character and competence of an individual and litigation,  Councilor Cowan seconded the motion.  The motion passed unanimously. 

	•	Work session adjourned at 9:00 p.m.
