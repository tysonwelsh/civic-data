                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 22-13

  A RESOLUTION RATIFYING A PURCHASE ORDER CONTRACT PREVIOUSLY
  GIVEN TO PROBUILD CONSTRUCTION TO CLEAN OUT TWO WATER TANKS
                    AND TO REPAIR DRAIN VALVE

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like mixing systems to be put in the water
tanks; and

        WHEREAS, the water tanks will need to be cleaned after the mixing systems are
installed; and
       WHEREAS, a notice to proceed needed to be sent prior to council approval in order to
avoid draining the main tank twice and have the cleaning done while the main tank is emptied
and taken out of service for the Riverton Flex project.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council ratifies a Purchase Order contract previously given to Probuild
            Construction to clean out two water tanks and to repair drain valve.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
February 2022 by the following vote:
