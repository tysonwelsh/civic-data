                              MASTER LICENSE AGREEMENT

        This Master License Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into by and between Riverton
City, a political subdivision of the State of Utah (“City”) and New Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC,
a Delaware limited liability company (“Licensee”). City and Licensee may be referred to
herein individually as a “Party” or collectively as the “Parties.”

                                            RECITALS

      This Agreement is made with reference to the following Recitals, each of which is
deemed to be a material term and provision of this Agreement:

        A.     City is the owner of the public Right Of Way (hereinafter “ROW”) as defined
herein and has determined that the ability to occupy portions of the public ROW for the business
of providing Wireless Services is a valuable use of a unique public resource that has been
acquired and is maintained at great expense to the City and its taxpayers, and, therefore, the City
should receive fair and reasonable compensation for use of the public ROW.

      B.      Licensee is a duly organized entity existing under the laws of the State of
Delaware, and Licensee and its lawful successors, assigns, and transferees, are authorized to
conduct business in the State of Utah.

       C.      Licensee desires to construct, operate and maintain communication sites and, for
such purpose, to locate, place, attach, install, operate, control and maintain Wireless Facilities (as
defined in Riverton City Ordinance) consistent with small cell technology within the ROW.

        D.     City has enacted Chapter 12.30 of the Riverton City Municipal Code “Wireless
Communication Services” (hereinafter the “Ordinance”) which governs the application and
review process for Agreements and regulates providers of wireless services and systems that
require the use of City-owned ROW; and

        E.       City, in exercise of its management of public ROW, believes that it is in the best
interest of the public to provide Licensee a nonexclusive Agreement to operate a small cell
network in City.

       F.      Licensee is willing to compensate City in exchange for a grant and right to use
and physically occupy portions of the ROW as provided herein.

2.     Site License Granted and Terms.
        2.1.    Scope. City, acting in its proprietary capacity as the owner of ROW, grants to
Licensee a nonexclusive license to Collocate on existing Structures or new Structures as
identified in Exhibit 1 to each Site License. Each Site License entered into by the Parties is
subject to the terms, conditions and other provisions set forth in this Agreement; to applicable
provisions of the Ordinance; and all applicable Laws and reasonable regulations of any
regulatory agency having competent jurisdiction. The Parties recognize that pursuant to Utah
Code Annotated § 54-21-302(2)(b)(iii) City may not require the placement of a Wireless Facility
on a specific Utility Pole or category of Utility Pole. However, the Parties agree that insofar as is
possible Licensee will Collocate Wireless Facilities in the following order of preference:
existing third-party Utility Poles, replacement third-party Utility Poles, new Licensee-owned
Utility Poles, replacement City-owned Utility Poles, and existing City-owned Utility Poles.
Moreover, the parties recognize pursuant to Utah Code Annotated § 54-21-204 2(b) Licensee’s
collocation on City-owned Utility Poles cannot be done in a manner which interferes with City’s
use of City-owned Utility Poles. In the event City determines that it cannot use or modify the
use of a City-owned Utility Pole because Licensee’s Small Cell or related equipment are present
on a City-owned Utility Pole, City can require Licensee to remove Licensee’s Small Cell and
related equipment from the Utility Pole.


         2.2.     Use of City Property. The Site License issued pursuant to this Agreement allows
Licensee to access, occupy and use allocated available space on the City-owned Utility Poles, if any,
identified in Exhibit 1 to the Site License to attach, install, operate, maintain, upgrade, remove,
reattach, reinstall, relocate and replace the Wireless Facility, as identified in such Exhibit 1,
solely for the purpose of Licensee operating a Wireless Site. The Site License also allows the
installation, operation and maintenance of ground based, pad mounted Wireless Facilities and/or
power pedestals needed for the operation of a Wireless Facility, together with any related conduit,
cable or wiring, with the location of any such ground based Wireless Facility determined in
connection with the issuance of a Permit (if needed). Subject to any restrictions stated elsewhere in
this Agreement, Licensee shall have access to the City-owned Utility Poles upon which a Wireless
Facility is installed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

        2.3.    Limitations on Use. Except as otherwise expressly provided herein, Licensee shall
not:

                (a)    Occupy or use any poles, improvements or structures of any kind, whether
within or outside the ROW, other than the locations as shown in Exhibit 1 attached to a Site License;
and,

                 (b)     Subject to Section 2.5 below, enter upon public property and attach, install,
operate, maintain, upgrade, remove, reattach, reinstall, relocate, and/or replace any item of a Wireless
Facility in or on poles or other structures not owned by City even if located within the ROW.

         2.4     Alterations. If Licensee proposes to install Wireless Facilities which are different
in any material way from the then-existing and approved Wireless Facility, then Licensee shall
first obtain the written approval for the use and installation of the unauthorized Wireless Facility
from an authorized representative of the City as communicated to Licensee in writing pursuant to
the Agreement noticing provisions in paragraph 18, which approval shall not be unreasonably
withheld, conditioned or delayed. In addition to any other submittal requirements, and if
requested by City, Licensee shall provide “load” (structural) calculations for all City-owned
Utility Poles upon which it intends to modify Wireless Facilities in the ROW. Notwithstanding
the foregoing, Licensee may modify its Wireless Facility with like-kind or a similar Wireless
Facility without prior written approval of the City.

        2.5    Third Party Poles. Subject to obtaining the written permission of the owner(s) of
the affected property and any required Permits (and paying any standard fees), the City hereby
authorizes and permits Licensee to enter upon the ROW to attach, install, operate, maintain,
remove, reattach, reinstall, relocate, and replace a Wireless Facility in or on poles or other
structures lawfully owned and operated by public utility companies or other third parties located
within the ROW. Collocations on third-party-owned poles shall be subject to Site License
approval and the payment of fees as outlined in this Agreement. Licensee shall furnish to the
City documentation in a form reasonably acceptable to the City of the third party permission
from the individual utility or property owner responsible.

        2.6      No Title Conveyed. A license granted hereunder will not convey title, equitable
or legal, in the ROW. A license is only the right to occupy the ROW on a nonexclusive basis for
the limited purposes and for the limited period stated in the license; the right may not be
terminated, subdivided, assigned, or subleased except as may be expressly provided in this
Agreement.

        2.7     Subordination. Use of the ROW pursuant to a license granted hereunder, shall be
subject to the prior and continuing right of the City to use any and all parts of the ROW
exclusively or concurrently with any other person or entity consistent with this Agreement, and
shall be further subject to all deeds, easements, dedications, conditions, covenants, restrictions,
encumbrances, and claims of title of record which may affect the ROW prior to the City issuing a
Site License.

3.     Term of Licenses and Agreement; Cancellation; Termination; Removal or
       Abandonment at Expiration.

        3.1.   Agreement Term. This Agreement will be for an initial term of ten (10) years (the
“Term”) commencing on the date that this Agreement is fully executed (the “Execution Date”).
At the end of the Term, the Agreement will continue to apply to Site Licenses granted during the
Term. The term of the Agreement may be renewed if the Licensee is in compliance with the
terms of this Agreement and all applicable laws, rules, and regulations. The City will not renew
Site Licenses or grant new Site Licenses after the Term, until the Parties have executed a new
Master License Agreement with terms and conditions acceptable to both Parties and consistent
with applicable federal, state and local law.

       3.2.    License Term. Each Site License shall be in effect for a period of ten (10) years
commencing on the Commencement Date (as defined in the Site License), and expiring on the
day before the tenth (10th) anniversary of the Commencement Date unless sooner cancelled or
terminated as provided herein (the “License Term”). Provided that Licensee is not in default,
after written notice and the expiration of any applicable cure period, under the Site License, the
License Term will automatically be extended for a successive 5-year period (a “Renewal
Term”), with the five-year extension of the License Term commencing immediately upon the
expiration of the initial period of the License Term unless notice of non-renewal is provided to
City by Licensee prior to the commencement of the succeeding Renewal Term. All of the
provisions of this Agreement, whether or not the Term of the Agreement has expired, shall be in
effect during the License Term and any extension of the License Term. The expiration of the
Term of the Agreement shall not affect the validity and term of the Site License.

        3.3    Licensee Cancellation. Licensee may cancel this Agreement or any Site License
before the date of expiration by providing the City with ninety (90) days express written notice
of cancellation. Any prepaid fee or rate shall be retained by City. This Agreement and all Site
Licenses may only be cancelled or terminated as provided in this Agreement or any Site License.

        3.4.   Abandonment. If Licensee abandons or fails to utilize a Wireless Site for a period
of six (6) or more consecutive months, the Wireless Facilities for such Wireless Site shall be
removed by Licensee at the expense of Licensee. In the event Licensee is unable or does not
remove such Wireless Facility when requested by City, City may authorize removal and
Licensee shall be responsible for all actual and reasonable costs incurred for such removal.

necessary repair and charge Licensee the reasonable, documented, actual cost for the repair.

         7.3.     Public Emergency. In the event of an emergency or to protect the public health or
safety, prior to the City accessing or performing any work on a City-owned Utility Pole on which
Licensee has installed a Wireless Facility, City may require Licensee to deactivate such Wireless
Facility if any of City’s employees or agents must move closer to the Wireless Facility than the FCC
recommended minimum distance. In such case, City will contact Licensee at 800-832-6662 to
request immediate deactivation.

       7.4.    Pole Replacement.

                (a)      Subject to Sections 7.4(b) and (c), if a City-owned Utility Pole needs
replacement or repair due to a traffic accident or deterioration, Licensee shall have the right to

within thirty (30) days thereafter, and Licensee shall cooperate with City to temporarily relocate
its Wireless Facility, if necessary. Upon completion of the replacement, City shall notify
Licensee in order for Licensee to install its Wireless Facility.

               (b)    In the event City is responsible for replacing the City-owned Utility Pole
with a Replacement Pole, City shall only be responsible for the cost of a standard pole, and
Licensee shall be responsible for the cost of the Replacement Pole in excess of the cost of a
standard pole.

                (c)     Licensee shall have the right to temporarily use a City-owned Utility Pole
for its operation during the replacement period at a location reasonably acceptable to both
parties.

       7.5.    Removal and Relocation.

relocate one or more of its Wireless Facilities. Licensee shall at City’s direction and upon ninety
(90) days prior written notice to Licensee, relocate such Wireless Facilities at Licensee’s sole

the following purposes: (i) if required for the construction, modification, completion, repair,

Facilities is interfering with or adversely affecting proper operation of City-owned Poles, traffic

Wireless Facilities as requested by the City in accordance with the foregoing provision, City
shall be entitled to remove or relocate the Wireless Facilities at Licensee’s sole cost and expense,

expenses incurred by the City in performing any removal work and any storage of Licensee’s
property after removal within forty-five (45) days of the date of a written demand for this
payment from the City.

               (b)   In the event Licensee desires to relocate any Wireless Facilities from one
City-owned Utility Pole to another, Licensee shall so advise City. City will use reasonable
efforts to accommodate Licensee by making another reasonably equivalent City-owned Utility
Pole available for use in accordance with and subject to the terms and conditions of this
Agreement.

                (c)    In lieu of the relocation of Licensee’s Wireless Facilities in the case of an
abandonment or removal of a City-owned Utility Pole as provided in Section 7.5(a)(iii), unless
the City-owned Utility Pole is needed for a legitimate City purpose, Licensee shall have right to
purchase the City-owned Utility Pole, and continue to use the same pursuant to the then existing Site

       7.6.    Non-exclusiveness. Subject to Section 7.7(d), the rights and privileges granted to

                (b)     Existing Uses/Easements. Licensee shall not interfere in any manner with
the existing uses or easement of the ROW, including sanitary sewers, water mains, storm drains,

                (c)    City Communications. Licensee shall not interfere in any manner with
current or future City public safety communication.

                (d)     City Interference. City reserves the right, but not the obligation, to maintain
and operate its City-owned Utility Poles in such reasonable manner as will best enable City to fulfill
its own service requirements or obligations. However, City agrees that City and/or any other
tenants, licensees, or users of the ROW who currently have or in the future take possession of
space within the ROW will be permitted to install only such equipment that is of the type and
frequency which will not cause harmful Interference which is measurable in accordance with
then existing industry standards to the then existing Wireless Facility of Licensee.

               (f)     Remedies. Without limiting any other rights or remedies, if Interference
occurs and continues for a period in excess of 48 hours following notice to the interfering party
via telephone to Licensee’s Network Operations Center at 800-832-6662 or to City at
(_____________), the interfering party shall or shall require any other user to reduce power or
cease operations of the interfering equipment until the interference is cured. The Parties
acknowledge that there will not be an adequate remedy at law for noncompliance with the
provisions of this Section 7 and therefore the Parties shall have the right to equitable remedies
such as, without limitation, injunctive relief and specific performance.
