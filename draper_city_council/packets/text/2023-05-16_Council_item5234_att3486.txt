                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-23

            A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF THE CITY OF DRAPER TO
          APPOINT JENNIFER JASTREMSKY AS COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT DIRECTOR
                                  FOR DRAPER CITY

      WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint Jennifer Jastremsky as Community
Development Director of the City of Draper in accordance with the appointment
procedures provided by law and city code; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code 3-1-030 requires the Community
Development Director be appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City
Council; and

      WHEREAS, Jennifer Jastremsky has the credentials, experience and
professionalism necessary to be the Community Development Director.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council gives its advice and consent to
the appointment of Jennifer Jastremsky as Community Development Director and the
Mayor is hereby authorized to appoint Jennifer Jastremsky as Community Development
Director, in accordance with appointment procedures provided by law and city code.

        Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Resolution, and all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall
be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon
passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THIS 16TH DAY OF MAY 2023.
