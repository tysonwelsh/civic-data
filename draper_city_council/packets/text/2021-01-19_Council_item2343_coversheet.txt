Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Travis VanEkelenburg
CC:  Date: January 19, 2021
Re: Siempre Event Center - Development Agreement Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement for approximately 2.13 acres located on the north side of Mike Weir Dr., at approximately 1283 E Mike Weir Dr. The property is currently zoned C2 and RM. The applicant is requesting that an Agreement be approved to allow for:
1. A reduction in the number of spaces required in the 1990 Draper City Municipal Code; and
2.Twenty (20) of the proposed parking spaces to be constructed of geo-grass pavers to accommodate over flow parking for the site.
This item is scheduled for the January 14, 2021 Planning Commission meeting and an update of that meeting will be presented to the City Council on the January 19, 2021 meeting. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. The proposed development plans outlined in the Agreement meets the intent, goals, and objectives of the Draper City General Plan.
2. The application complies with the requirements of DCMC 9-5-200(C).
3. The proposed development plans will not be deleterious to the health, safety, and general welfare of the general public nor the residents of adjacent properties.
4. The public services in the area are adequate to support the subject development.
For denial:
1. The proposed Agreement does not meet the requirements and provisions of the Draper City Municipal Code.
2. The proposed Agreement does not conform to the general aesthetic and physical development of the area. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Ordinance 1472 1/14/2021 Cover Memo PC Staff Report 1/14/2021 Cover Memo
