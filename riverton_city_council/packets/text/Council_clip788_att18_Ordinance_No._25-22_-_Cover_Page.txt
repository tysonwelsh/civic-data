ITEM:
                          Amendments Proposed by Riverton City - Jason Lethbridge, Development
                          Services Director

SUMMARY:

In April of 2025 Riverton City made substantial changes to the City’s Code for Commercial Architectural
Standards. These changes in title 18 impacted eight (8) chapters and multiple sections of City code.
 With all the deletions and additions to code, Dumpster Closures were overlooked and mistakenly
removed from the code, without being replaced. This proposed code amendment seeks to restore
standards for commercial trash enclosures.
