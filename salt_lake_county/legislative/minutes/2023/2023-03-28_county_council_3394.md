2. PUBLIC COMMENT
3. REPORT OF ELECTED OFFICIALS:
3.1. County Council Members
Council Member Winder Newton  acknowledged the passing of former Council 
Member Max Burdick who had a fatal heart attack on Friday.  He and his wife lost 
a daughter two months prior, so it is a particularly tough time for his family.  She 
expressed condolences and heartfelt wishes to them as they go through this hard 
time.  
Council Member Stringham  recognized the work Max Burdick did at the 
County, and thanked his family for their sacrifice while he served the County.
3.2. County Mayor
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  expressed her sadness at Max Burdick ’s passing, stating 
she worked with him for several years and appreciated his approach and friendly 
demeanor, willingness to engage, and commitment to his district.  She also 
appreciated the journey he had with his family, with his daughter ’s health and her 
passing, and his love for them.  His focus was on his family.

3.3. Other Elected County Officials
Mr. Sim Gill , District Attorney, acknowledged Max Burdick's passing, and what a 
great person he was.  Some of his best qualities were generosity and kindness.  He 
also worked hard and cared about the County and everyone here.  Those who 
knew Max Burdick were lucky to have met him.  He had the privilege of becoming 
his friend and would miss him.
