                       COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

RESOLUTION NO. 2022-11-02                                              DATE: November 16, 2022

                 A RESOLUTION PROPOSING AMENDMENTS TO THE
                   COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP’S 2022 BUDGET

                                            RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, Copperton adopted its 2022 fiscal year budget on December 15, 2021; and

       WHEREAS, Copperton is considering amending the 2022 budget as set forth in the
attached and desires to hold a public hearing to receive public input on the same.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Copperton Metro Township Council:

        1.     The Copperton Council will hold a public hearing on December 21, 2022, at 6:30
p.m. at the Bingham Lion’s Club, 8725 W. Hillcrest Street, Copperton, Utah 84006, to receive
public comments on the proposed amendments to the 2022 Copperton budget.

       2.      Notice of the public hearing will be posted pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-
113.

       3.      A copy of the proposed amendments to the 2022 budget shall be made available
for public inspection for at least ten (10) days prior to the public hearing, pursuant to Utah Code
Ann. § 10-6-112.

       4.      This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption
