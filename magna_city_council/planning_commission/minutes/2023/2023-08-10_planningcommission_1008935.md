                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)

REZ2023-000852 – Joe Colosimo is requesting a recommendation of approval for a proposed rezone from
R-1-6 (Single-Family Residential, 6,000 SF Minimum) to the R-2-6.5 (Medium Density Residential) Zone.
Acreage: 2.84 acres. Location: 3045 South 8400 West. Planner: Jeff Miller (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jeff Miller provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding parcels, access, and density.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Joe Colosimo
Address: 11745 South Taitlynn Rose Lane
Comments: Mr. Colosimo said intent to do townhome 4-6 units. Four parking stalls per unit, 40% open
space. Promote diverse housing and PUD. Sent a technical letter. Approached to buy 3 lots, needed to do
some density and soil abatement.

Commissioners and applicant discussed soil abatement, number of units, and traffic off 8400 and 3100.

Commissioner Cripps motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Richards seconded that motion.

Speaker # 2: Citizen
Name: Jayne Pendleton
Address: 3019 South 8400 West
Comments: Ms. Pendleton said the project on Jayne Crest Circle took a long amount of time and should
finish up on any other projects he’s working on. Homeowners here to address. They have a wall destroyed
and would like to have taken care of and have a civil conversation with the applicant before anything moves
ahead with the next projects. Thank Mayor Peay for getting the Jayne Crest site cleaned up.

Speaker # 3: Citizen
Name: Shawna Berchtold
Address: 8364 West 3100 South
Comments: Ms. Berchtold said right now waiting 20 minutes to enter driveway, can’t get out of driveway
with school in session. She had to pull on to her lawn. Bought it 12 years ago because it was quiet, and it
won’t be quiet anymore. Light at 3100 south is all backed up and against this project.

Speaker # 4: Citizen
Name: Laura Burrows
Address: 3330 South 7200 West
Comments: Ms. Burrows said she is opposed to the density. Likes the Jayne Crest project and hopes this
development would be less dense. Recommends three to four homes or four to five homes per acre. Travels
3100 a lot and likes the road and speed limit. Seeing high density housing go up and is just a disaster and
opposed.
