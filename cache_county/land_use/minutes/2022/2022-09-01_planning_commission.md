#4 Public Hearing (5:40 PM): Cache County General Plan 
Watkins reviewed the staff report for the supplemental appendices to the General Plan: The Urban and 
Rural Area Assessment and the Cost of Services Plan. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed future growth trends, regional collaboration for development, how 
to work with landowners who want to develop, cluster development, proving availability of water for 
cluster subdivisions, septic tanks for development, and when do service districts can happen. 
06:44:00 
Lee motioned to open the public hearing; Daugs seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
Zan Summers asked if the information presented tonight is available to the public online. 
 
1 September 2022                       Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 3 of 3 
Watkins responded yes, under the media packet for the Planning Commission and through the 
Development Services website. 
06:46:00 
Spackman motioned to close the public hearing; Daugs seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed when different types of development studies, such as water or 
septic, need to be required for larger developments. 
Watterson motioned to continue the Cache County General Plan for up to 90 days; Daugs seconded;
