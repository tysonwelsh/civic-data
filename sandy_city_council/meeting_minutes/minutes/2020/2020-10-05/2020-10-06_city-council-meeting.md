Prayer / Pledge of Allegiance

                       Council Member Houseman offered the prayer.

                       Brody Gibbs, Eagle Scout, led the Pledge.

Non-voting Items

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                       Mike Applegarth, Executive Director, introduced Liz Theriault, Policy and
                       Communications Analyst. Ms. Theriault shared a video with the Council which provided
                       instruction regarding how the public can utilize the new ecomment tool. This new tool will
                       allow the public to submit public comments to the City Council regarding agenda items.

Council Member Business

                       Council Member Houseman gave an update on the Central Wasatch Commission and
                       encouraged the public to comment on the Mountain Transportation System Plan that the
                       CWC has released. She invited the public to fill out the survey on the CWC website and
                       provide their feedback.

                       Council Member Sharkey shared information from the recent Jordan River Commission
                       meeting regarding the decreasing population of the Monarch Butterfly, which is currently
                       being considered for the endangered species list. She also encouraged people to contact
                       the Facebook page, Monarchs of the Wasatch to get more information.

                       Council Member Robinson encouraged people to register for this evening's Night Out
                       Against Crime event sponsored by Sandy City.

Mayor's Report

                       Mayor Bradburn shared with the Council that Bob Thompson, the City Attorney, will be
                       leaving the City and has taken a new position with the University of Utah. He thanked Bob
                       for his service to the city.

                       Bob Thompson expressed his gratitude to the Mayor and thanked the Council for giving
                       him the opportunity to serve Sandy City.

CAO Report

                       Matt Huish, CAO, spoke about the City's new online training center which will kick off this
                       week. He recognized Melisse Stiglich, Justice Court Administrator, who was recently
                       awarded with the Justice Court of Utah Employee of the Year.

                       Ian Williams, Animal Services Director, provided information regarding the City's response
                       to an issue that was brought up by a resident at a recent City Council meeting relating to
                       the release of domestic bunnies in Dimple Dell park. He also showed a new post on the
                       city website which will help educate the public regarding the harmful impact of releasing
                       domestic animals in the wild.

                       Brian Kelley, Finance Director, gave an update on the CARES Funding guidelines and
                       provided some additional information regarding eligible expenses.

1.         20-341                 Council Member Zoltanski requesting that the Council receive a
                                  presentation on a recent Eagle Scout Project
                                  Council Member Zoltanski introduced Mr. Brody Gibbs, a Sandy resident, who recently
                                  received the rank of Eagle Scout. Mr. Gibbs shared with the Council the details of his
                                  Eagle Scout project. He collected and assembled hundreds of hygiene kits for villagers
                                  living in a remote area of Guayacana, Ecuador. The Council acknowledged his service
                                  and thanked him for his efforts.

3.         20-343                 Administration recommending the Council receive a presentation from
                                  Rocky Mountain Power (RMP) on the Wattsmart Communities Program.
                   Attachments:   Memorandum
                                  Presentation
                                  Park City Community Energy Plan

                                  Kim Bell, Deputy City Manager, introduced the item and provided information regarding
                                  the City's efforts to become a sustainability community and reducing our energy and
                                  carbon footprint. She spoke about the Sustainable Sandy 360 program and the city's
                                  partnership with Rocky Mountain Power. Kim Bell introduced Clay Monroe, Raenee
                                  Carlson, Brad Knoles and Becca Stock with Rocky Mountain Power who presented on
                                  the WattSmart Communities Program which is a designation that Sandy City received
                                  this year. They reviewed the process and goals of the program and how the data
                                  collected is used to identify the most effective strategies.

                                  Council comments and questions followed.
