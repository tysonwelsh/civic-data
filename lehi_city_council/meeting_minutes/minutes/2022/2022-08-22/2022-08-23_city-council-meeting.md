Pre Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present, except for
Councilor Southwick who is excused. Councilor Condie gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Discussion of a budget amendment for new job position
Mayor Johnson asked the Council to consider a budget amendment to accommodate a new job
position for a general contractor. He said that this was brought about due to the very high bids
that they received on small construction projects such as bathroom facilities and storage sheds.
He believes that this position would save money over time. The Council reached consensus that
they would be willing to consider a budget amendment.

3. Joint Planning Commission & City Council Discussion
3.1) Update on UTA’s Station Area Plan Study for the Thanksgiving Point Frontrunner Station.
Kim Struthers, Planning Director, introduced the consultants that have been working on the
project: The project area includes the 11-acre UTA parcel. They explained that they have done
an existing conditions analysis, and community engagement. They said that their goal is to
maintain the authenticity of Lehi, and provide a place for future generations. They want to
include a mix of uses that create a sense of place. There is a demand for smaller and more
affordable units. Right now, there is a significant population increase during the day for those

that work in the office space, so they want to encourage those people to become part of the
community.

Transit Oriented Development is an opportunity to promote active street life through an increase
in people living, shopping, recreating, and working at Thanksgiving Point.

The consultants presented the preferred concept with 200 units in 2 different buildings with
additional office and retail space.

Councilor Koivisto expressed concerns with the additional office space. She believes that there is
already too much office space in the area.

Jordan Swade, with UTA, explained that they would like their office space to be used for a
specific use such as academics. He explained that UTA plans to create a master development
agreement which would include a traffic analysis.

Mayor Johnson asked for the Planning Commissioners to provide their feedback.

Tyson Eyre stated that he would like to see the residential buildings taller. He hopes to see all the
pedestrian and bike connections on the final plan.

Heather Newall stated that she would like to see more of the allowed units allocated to this area.

Mr. Swade explained that the number of units in this area was decided based on a series of
negotiations with Stack Development.

Scott Carlson stated that he appreciates the effort to change the concept to avoid a free-standing
parking structure. He also felt that the area should have more residential units. He hopes they are
considering rooftop amenities. He also expressed concerns with parking and hopes that they will
provide enough space that the public will use it.

Emily Britton agreed with the previous comments and stated that she would also like to see more
residential units here. She also expressed concerns with the impact to the surrounding residents
that want to use Frontrunner.

14. Consideration of Ordinance #46-2022, approval of the Skye Area Plan amending the
    Micron Area Plan and including 2,436 equivalent residential units (ERUs), mixed-use
    space, a civic site, open space, trails and roadways. Located at approximately
    Timpanogos Highway and Center Street.
    Items #14- 17 were heard together.

    Scott with DR Horton was representing the applicant. He presented the current proposed plan
    and said that there were only a few minor changes from the original concept plan. He stated
    that the tech manufacture buffer was to be left as native open space, but TI is looking into
    installing solar panels and asked that they allow 60% of the buffer for possible solar panels,
    while still maintaining a 500 foot buffer between residential areas. He also explained that the
    dedicated open space next to Draper will be placed in a conservation easement.

    Blake Bowman was representing TI. He said that they need some flexibility with the buffer
    space as they may want to install solar panels and other storage facilities.
