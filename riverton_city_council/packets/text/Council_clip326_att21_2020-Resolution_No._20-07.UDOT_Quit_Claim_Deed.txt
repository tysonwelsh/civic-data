                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-07

   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE TRANSFER OF .353 ACRES OF REAL
  PROPERTY LOCATED IN THE SOUTH MEADOWS AND MERCED ESTATES
SUBDIVISIONS TO THE STATE OF UTAH, DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

       WHEREAS, in Riverton City holds a dedicated roadway known as 3800 West Street,
between approximately 12385 South and 12280 South, Riverton, Utah; and (the “Subject
Property”); and

        WHEREAS, due to increase traffic congestion and use of Bangerter Highway, the State
of Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) has a need for additional space to improve the
interchange at 12600 South and Bangerter Highway; and

       WHEREAS, UDOT desires to expand Bangerter Highway, in part by encroaching upon
the western edge of the Subject Property, and has offered to purchase a portion of said property
from Riverton; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that conveyance of said
right of way to UDOT will not interfere with the ordinary flow of traffic on 3800 West Street,
and will further advance the health and safety of Riverton City by improving safe traffic
circulation in the vicinity of Bangerter Highway and 12600 South.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council authorizes and directs the Mayor to transfer title to the
          Subject Property as descreibed in the Quit Claim Deed instrument attached to this
          Resolution, to the State of Utah, Department of Transportation.
       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 21st day of
January 2020 by the following vote:
