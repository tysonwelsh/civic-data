                              RESOLUTION NO. 23-30

      A RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING DOUG VAWDREY AND WALT
      ROSEMAN TO THE DRAPER CITY SPECIAL EVENT ARENA COMMITTEE

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has adopted Ordinances which provide
for the appointment of members to the Draper City Special Event Arena
Committee; and

      WHEREAS, members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee are
appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, terms of office are set to be four-year terms; and

     WHEREAS, the Mayor proposes to reappoint Doug Vawdrey and Walt
Roseman to serve as members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

     The City Council hereby provides its advice and consent to the Mayor’s
reappointment of Doug Vawdrey and Walt Roseman to serve as members of the
Draper City Special Event Arena Committee.

        Section 1. Appointment. The following individuals are hereby reappointed
to fill the term listed below as a member of the Draper City Special Event Arena
Committee according to the laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the
Draper City Special Event Arena Committee and its members for the term specified
herein.

             Four-year term:

             Doug Vawdrey              June 20, 2023, through June 30, 2027
             Walt Roseman              June 20, 2023, through June 30, 2027

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution
is held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.
      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS THE 20TH DAY OF JUNE 2023.
