2.1) Public hearing and recommendation of UTA and Lehi City’s request for review of the
Lehi Frontrunner Station Area Plan, 11-acres located at approximately 2800 West Executive
Pkwy in a Resort Community zone.
Katie Bussell Presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Carlson asked why Lehi City was listed as a co-applicant. Kim Struthers stated
the City and UTA were co-partners in design and participation. Ross Dinsdale stated the state
requires each city to participate in UTA transit plans where funding is granted. UTA owns the
property that is being developed.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

He acknowledged the public comment that was submitted through Engage Lehi by Brittney
Harris.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Carlson had many concerns with the UTA plan including lack of parking for
vehicles and bike stalls, walkability and connectivity, the housing unit configurations not being
specific enough, the phasing recommendation plan, courtyard and landscaping plans with
amenities for residents living in an active public area with requirements or encouragement from
the developer, rooftop amenities for residents and the commercial area for adding outdoor space
and viability without taking up green space, mixed uses being carefully thought out, street and
bike lane requirements. His concerns echoed the online comment that was submitted.

Commissioner Everett stated this eliminates parking and discourages others from using other
TOD in the area. Other people in northern Utah county will want to use this, but there is not
enough parking, and the accessibility is not ideal.

Commissioner Jackson said this will be a failure if it can't have the convenience of TOD if it is
easier to drive your vehicle and use I-15.

Commissioner Nielsen said larger garages would be a possible solution. Open space for bicycle
parking would be helpful also. Commuter parking makes more sense to be in the commercial
parking area.

Commissioner Newall asked for clarification on affordable housing.

Kim Struthers stated 80% AMI, based on Utah County median income. There have been studies
done with usage and savings where UTA does not want to preclude the community from using
TOD. Jordan Swain with UTA have done studies with that information. Kim stated the transit is
meant to interconnect.

Commissioner Bunker asked how this was planned where one development should accommodate
surrounding cities with the limited amount of parking and if the infrastructure is prepared to
handle the population of surrounding areas and Lehi citizens.

Ross Dinsdale wanted to clarify this was a general plan that gives UTA a guideline for what is
recommended to UTA.

Commissioner Eyre was concerned because there are many developments that get phased out
before making it to the next phases.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to send a positive recommendation of UTA and
        Lehi City’s request for review of the Lehi Frontrunner Station Area Plan, 11-acres
        located at approximately 2800 West Executive Pkwy in a Resort Community zone;
        including all DRC comments and the findings stated in the report. Also recommending
        the phasing of the project will maintain minimum of 600 parking spaces which are not
        restricted to public transit users from using those parking spaces until such a time as the
        proposed bus rapid transit system development, plan clarified residential mixed units that
        are not constructed on the first floor, commercial rooftop amenities are encouraged,
        residential courtyard amenities are required and residential rooftop amenities are
        encouraged, bike lanes are protected within the development from vehicle travel and the
        bike parking and storage areas are more clearly identified, move commercial to buffer the
        residential areas, define the AMI and median rental as a whole for cost comparison and
        clarify affordable housing and tax incentives.
        Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Bunker;
        yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes. The vote passed 6 – 1.
