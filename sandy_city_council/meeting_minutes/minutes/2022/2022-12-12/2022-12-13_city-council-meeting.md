3.         22-438                 The Central Wasatch Commission providing the Council with an update

                   Attachments:   Presentation.pptx

                                  Blake Perez, Chris Robinson, and Lindsey Nielsen with the Central Wasatch 
                                  Commission presented a brief overview of the organization and its mission. They reviewed 
                                  member jurisdiction benefits which focuses on a consensus approach to land and 
                                  watershed protection, consensus-based transportation approach, and short-term 
                                  improvements in the mountains. They also reviewed the visitor use study, environmental 
                                  dashboard, and regional stakeholder involvement. They presented an overview of the 
                                  Central Wasatch National Conservation and Recreation Area Act (CWNCRA) which would 
                                  create new wilderness, allow for the connection of the Bonneville Shoreline Trail, and 
                                  place permanent protections on land and water in the tri-canyon area. Council thanked 
                                  the CWC for their presentation.

4.         22-435                 Community Development Department presenting, for review by the City 
                                  Council and Mayor, a draft of a survey associated with the General Plan 
                                  update process.
                   Attachments:   Sandy City General Plan Survey 1-draft

                                  Jake Warner with Community Development provided an overview of the General Plan 
                                  Update process. A General Plan is a document which sets frameworks for the future 
                                  growth and development of an area by identifying a collective community vision. The 
                                  planning horizon for Sandy's General Plan update is 2045. Mr. Warner introduced Kyrene 
                                  Gibb with Y2 Analytics who reviewed the survey process with the Council and the 
                                  proposed questions included in the first of a total of three surveys which will be going out 
                                  to the residents to gather feedback for the General Plan. Council questions and 
                                  comments followed. More information about the General Plan Process can be found at 
                                  Sandypaceofprogress.org.

5.         22-439                 Public Works Department presenting information on Resolution #22-37, 
                                  which authorizes Administration to enter into a supplemental agreement 
                                  regarding residential solid waste collection service agreement with Waste 
                                  Management of Utah, Inc.
                   Attachments:   Resolution 22-37c Waste Management Rate Change 2022 v.4
                                  Supplemental Agreement to Residential Solid Waste Collection 
                                  Agreement - FINAL 12.2.22
                                  Mike Gladbach, Public Works Director presented to the Council Resolution #22-37C, 
                                  authorizing the Administration to enter into a supplemental agreement regarding 
                                  residential solid waste collection service agreement with Waste Management of Utah, 
                                  Inc. Due to unforeseeable and extraordinary market conditions, Waste Management of 
                                  Utah, Inc is requesting a temporary rate increase of 4.6% per service line through June of 
                                  2023. The requested increase in rate is based on a recalculation of the CPI. The costs 
                                  associated with the increase will be covered by the city's funds and will not impact the 
                                  current rate charged to residents of Sandy City. Per Lynn Pace, City Attorney, the City is 
                                  not under an obligation to cover the requested increase because the contract with Waste 
                                  Management is in place currently. Council questions and comments followed. Blake 
                                  Leonelli with Waste Management responded to questions and provided additional 
                                  information. The Council expressed concerns with making mid-year adjustments to costs 
                                  and with the request to backdate the increased fees to prior months.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Houseman spoke about a state-wide initiative sponsored by they state 
                    and partner organizations to gather feedback about the growth in Utah - Guiding Our 
                    Growth. She encouraged all to participate in the survey which can be found at 
                    guidingourgrowth.utah.gov.

                    Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledged the great work performed by the city's 
                    snow plow drivers and thanked them for doing such a good job. 

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa provided an update from the Sandy Youth Council. The 
                    Youth Council has 23 members, representing 11 different high schools across the valley. 
                    The Youth Council began in June 2022 and to date have performed over 240 hours of 
                    leadership and service to the City, helping at seven city-wide events. She invited the 
                    Council and Administration to join the Sandy Youth Council at any upcoming service 
                    event or meeting and on behalf of the SYC, gave the Council and Mayor a SYC tee shirt. 
                    She also mentioned that January 18, 2023 is the ULCT Annual Local Officials Day at the 
                    Capitol and that the SYC will be participating in this event.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey also thanked the snow plow drivers. She provided an 
                    update from the Utah League of Cities and Towns and reviewed some upcoming 
                    legislative issues. 

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski thanked the Human Resource Department for coordinating the recent 
                    employee luncheon. It was a great event at the Hale Centre Theatre and she 
                    acknowledged all who receive longevity awards, recognizing Council Member Scott Earl 
                    who has 35 years of service with the City. She invited the public to attend the Nutcracker 
                    Ballet at Mt. Jordan Middle School - a wonderful performance

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, updated the Council on the city's response to the recent snow 
                    storms. Mike Gladbach with Public Works spoke about his department's response and 
                    the process utilized by his department staff with respect to the plan they follow for snow 
                    plowing. He acknowledged and thanked his team for the great job they are doing for the 
                    city.


Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the Council meeting at 7:38 pm.
