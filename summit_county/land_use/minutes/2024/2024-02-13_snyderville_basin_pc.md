Public hearing and possible recommendation regarding a proposed change to the Snyderville Basin Development Code to include "dwelling unit, single family attached" (aka townhomes) to the list of conditional uses in the NMU-1 zone. Legislative review. Project # 23-152.
Madlyn McDonough and Laura Kuhrmeyer, County Planners.  (7:14 P.M.)

Madlyn McDonough,
County Planner
, presented a proposed change to the Snyderville Basin Development Code.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 7:18 p.m.
Tony Tyler was called to speak at 7:18 p.m.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 7:19 p.m.
Commissioners provided comments and asked follow-up questions of Planner McDonough and Director Putt.
Makena Hawley made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the Summit County Council to amend Chapter 2, Section 10-2-17B3 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code to include dwelling unit, single family attached to the allowed use table in the NMU-1 zone. Christopher Conabee seconded, and all voted in favor, (0-7).
Commissioner Cooke provided additional comments for discussion on the motion.
Makena Hawley made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the Summit County Council to amend Chapter 2, Section 10-2-17B3 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code to add dwelling unit, single family attached to the allowed uses of the zone and direct staff to return with additional research on the housing types potentially to be added to the allowed use table. DJ Hubler seconded, and the motion failed, (2-5).
Makena Hawley voted AYE
DJ Hubler voted AYE
Tyann Mooney voted NAY
John Kucera voted NAY
Bruce Carmichael voted NAY
Christopher Conabee voted NAY
Thomas Cooke voted NAY
Discussion was held on the motion.
Christopher Conabee made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the Summit County Council to amend Chapter 2, Section 10-2-17B3 of the Snyderville Basin Code pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Thomas Cooke seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
Tyann Mooney made a motion to staff, through the manager, to do a thorough analysis of why all housing is not included in the new NMU section not as a conditional use, as being part of the use, and add more definitions for clarification. Thomas Cooke seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
Planner McDonough asked clarifying questions about the motion.
