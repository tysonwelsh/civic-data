PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on December 12, 2018 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2018-46 declaring 1.243 acres of real property located at or near 11962 South Anthem Park Blvd. - Corner Parcel, as Surplus, and to establish a method to determine the highest and best economic return to the City shall be by competitive bid with a minimum bid to be set at $490,000. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
