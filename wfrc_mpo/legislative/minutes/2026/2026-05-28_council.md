 FRC Minutes 
W
 May 28, 2026 

 DRAFT 

 MINUTES 
 Wasatch Front Regional Council 
 May 28, 2026 
 WFRC Council meeting was held at WFRC Offices, 41 N Rio Grande Street, Salt Lake City and via Zoom. 
 1. Welcome & Consent Agenda (00:00:03) 
 At 1:33pm, Mayor Dawn Ramsey, Chair of WFRC Council, welcomed the group to the meeting. Introductions were 
 made around the room and online. (00:06:02) 
 1a. ACTION: Minutes of the WFRC meeting held March 26, 2026, current financial report, and financial 
 statements for February and March 2026 
 Mayor Ramsey brought attention to the minutes of the Council meeting held on March 26 and the financial 
 statements for February and March 2026 and entertained a motion to approve these items. Commissioner Bob 
 Stevenson made the motion and Commissioner Tyler Vincent seconded, and the affirmative vote was unanimous. 
 (00:06:50) Mayor Ramsey called for a motion to switch the order of the agenda, in order to have Public Comment 
 occur prior to the Chair Report. Commissioner Jim Harvey made the motion, seconded by Mayor Joy Petro, and 
 the motion was unanimous in the affirmative. 
 2. Chair Report (00:35:53) 
 Mayor Ramsey introduced Miranda Jones Cox, WFRC Government Affairs Manager, who gave a brief report 
 regarding the current federal transportation reauthorization bill. 
 (00:46:32) Mayor Ramsey noted the upcoming changes in Utah Transit Authority’s governance structure on July 1, 
 2026 and acknowledged the long-standing support of the three UTA Trustees that have worked with the Council for 
 a number of years: Carlton Christensen, Beth Holbrook, and Jeff Acerson. 
 3. Public Comment (00:07:43) 
 Mayor Ramsey opened the meeting up for public comment. There were several comments brought forth by 
 members of the public. (00:21:00) Mayor Ramsey then allowed time for other comments from members of the 
 Council. (00:27:57) Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director, and Carlos Braceras, UDOT Executive Director, 
 also provided comments. 
 4. Regional Growth Committee (RGC) (00:50:30) 
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Mayor Tami Tran, Chair of RCG for a report on the meeting from May 21, 2026. 
 Mayor Tran stated that it was an informative meeting where one of the topics of discussion was water use and how 
 it relates to urban growth. RGC will continue to explore water usage discussion in the future. 
 4a. ACTION: Station Area Plan Certification (00:51:20) 
 Mayor Tran then introduced Byron Head, Community Planner at WFRC, and shared that he will be presenting on a 
 Station Area Plan for certification. (00:55:29) ACTION: Mayor Ramsey called for a motion to certify the resolution 
 of impracticability for the Midvale portion of Historic Sandy TRAX station area. The motion was made by Mayor 
 Dustin Gettel and seconded by Commissioner Jim Harvey. The affirmative vote was unanimous. 
 5. Transportation Coordination Committee (Trans Com) (00:56:34) 
 Mayor Ramsey invited Commissioner Jim Harvey, Vice Chair of Trans Com, to provide the report in Trans Com 
 Chair Mayor Mark Shepherd’s stead. Commissioner Harvey introduced Ben Wuthrich, WFRC Transportation 
 Improvement Program Coordinator. 
 5a . Board Modifications to the 2026-2031 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Mr. Wuthrich stated 
 that since the last meeting of the Wasatch Front Regional Council (WFRC), requests were received to modify the 
 current 2026-2031 TIP. The modification required action from Trans Com (since WFRC was not meeting) and the 
 Utah Transportation Commission but did not require a new air quality conformity analysis or a 30-day public 
 comment period. The modification is attached to the approved resolution. 
 5b. ACTION: Board Modifications to the 2026-2031 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) (00:59:15) 
 Mr. Wuthrich continued the report by briefly describing the modification requests to the current 2026-2031 
 Transportation Improvement Program. [01:04:28] ACTION: Commissioner Harvey made a motion to approve the 
 modifications as discussed. Mayor Lorin Palmer seconded the motion and the affirmative vote was unanimous. 

 c . ACTION: Approve Surface Transportation Program (STP), Congestion Mitigation/Air Quality (CMAQ) 
5
 Program, Transportation Alternatives Program (TAP), and Carbon Reduction Program (CRP) projects for 
 the 2027-2032 TIP (01:06:50) Mr. Wuthrich continued, providing a brief overview of the process for developing 
 recommendations for adding new projects to the four federal funding programs: STP, CMAQ, TAP, and CRP. He 
 then gave an overview of the projects slated for inclusion in these programs for the 2027-2032 Transportation 
 Improvement Program (TIP). ACTION (01:20:30) Commissioner Harvey made a motion to approve the addition of 
 the new projects that have been reviewed and recommended by Trans Com in the four programs: STP, CMAQ, 
 TAP, and CRP. Carlton Christensen, UTA Trustee, seconded the motion. The affirmative motion passed 
 unanimously. 
 6. WFRC Budget Committee (01:24:24) 
 Mayor Ramsey introduced WFRC Budget Committee Chair, Councilmember Aimee Winder Newton, who reported 
 that the Budget Committee met earlier this month and is bringing one action item and one informational item before 
 the Council. 
 6a. Public Hearing and ACTION: Approve the FY27 WFRC Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work 
 Program (UPWP) 
 The FY27 WFRC goals, budget, and Unified Planning Work Program, outlining WFRC activities for fiscal year 
 2027, were provided in the packets. These materials were prepared by staff in coordination with the Budget 
 Committee, and were previously presented to the Council in March, authorizing their release for public and COG 
 review and comment. The Budget Committee reviewed them again on May 14. WFRC Chief Financial Officer, 
 Marian Florence, summarized the goals and budget, noting that there is one addition to funding since the March 
 review: WFRC received an award from the US Department of Transportation for the Regional Infrastructure 
 Accelerator (RIA) program. The UPWP summary has also been updated to include the RIA grant. Mayor Ramsey 
 opened a Public Hearing to discuss the WFRC FY27 goals, budget, and Unified Planning Work Program. There 
 were no public comments and the Public Hearing was closed. (01:29:08) ACTION: Councilmember Winder 
 Newton made a motion to approve the WFRC fiscal year 2027 Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work Program 
 (UPWP). The motion was seconded by Commissioner Bob Stevenson, and the affirmative vote was unanimous. 
 6b. Fraud Risk Assessment FY25 (01:29:45) 
 WFRC completed its annual fraud risk assessment as required by the Office of the Utah State Auditor. The Budget 
 Committee also reviewed this item at its last meeting. WFRC remains in the "very low risk" category due to robust 
 internal controls, separation of duties, trained staff, careful policies, and transparent structure and processes. The 
 assessment and accompanying memo were available in the packets for review prior to submission to the Utah 
 State Auditor. 
 7. Reports (01:30:47) 
 7a. Active Transportation Committee (ATC) update 
 Mayor Ramsey introduced Mayor Troy Walker who provided an update on ATC, by discussing the success of the 
 9th Annual Golden Spoke ride that was held on May 16, 2026 and letting the group know that there will be more 
 information forthcoming about legislation that was passed regarding the distinction between and the safety of 
 e-bicycles versus e-motorbikes. 
 7b. Executive Director’s Report (01:34:12) 
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director, who acknowledged Andrea Pearson, 
 Senior Executive Assistant, for 10 years of service at WFRC. 
 8. Other Business and Adjournment (01:37:16) 
 Mayor Ramsey noted that the next Council meeting will be held on Thursday, August 27, 2026. She asked if there 
 were other items of business and Beth Holbrook, UTA Trustee, mentioned the good work that Utah Urban Land 
 Institute is doing with students around the Wasatch Front. Mayor Dustin Gettel shared that Mayor Ramsey was 
 recognized by America 250 as an outstanding public servant. Mayor Ramsey then entertained a motion to adjourn 
 the meeting. Commissioner Bob Stevenson made the motion, which was seconded by Commissioner Tyler 
 Vincent, and the meeting adjourned at 3:16pm. 

 A recording of this meeting, as well as meeting packet materials, may be found on the WFRC website at www.wfrc.org
