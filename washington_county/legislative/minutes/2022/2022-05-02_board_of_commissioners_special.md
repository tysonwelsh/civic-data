WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
MAY 2, 2022

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on May 2, 2022, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner
Adam Snow, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, Emergency Services Director Jason
Whipple and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Others in attendance included: SSD Chair Roxanne Hansen and Chief Jim Banks

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:
DISCUSSION _OF NEW HARMONY VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE
— EEE ARMONY VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE

DISTRICT (NHVSSD)

Commissioner Iverson explained that there are challenges within the Special Service
Districts in regards to limited resources. There are ongoing discussions happening with the
other SSD’s throughout the County about how the County can best serve the communities,

Chief Jim Banks stated that he has been a member of the fire department in New Harmony
Valley for two (2) years. There are currently eighteen (18) volunteers on the NHVSSD
with approximately twelve (12) of those volunteers working towards becoming Fire 1 and
Fire 2 certified. Funding is expected to be received from the Community Impact Board
(CIB) as the District was identified as a priority in order to purchase a new fire apparatus.

The tax rate for the district is .0008%, however, only half is being collected. The total
budget for the district is $99,000.

Commissioner Iverson suggested that the SSD board could benefit from being an appointed
board rather than an elected board.

There was a discussion regarding the need for medical training within the SSD due to the
time is takes for Gold Cross Ambulance to arrive from Iron County. There is also a need
for a community area in order to hold meetings due to there not being a large enough space
currently.

A structured tier plan for payment of firefighters is being considered for implementation in
order to incentivize further training. The SSD would like to remain on its own rather than
continue the discussion of consolidation.

Jason Whipple invited the District representatives to attend the LEPC meetings.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
5:20 p.m.
