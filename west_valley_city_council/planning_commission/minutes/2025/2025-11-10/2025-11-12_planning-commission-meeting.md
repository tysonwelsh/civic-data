GPZ-5-2025 Petition by Grow Development LLC requesting a General Plan change from
Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre),
and a zone change from A-2 (Agriculture, min. lot size 2 acres) to R-1-8 (Single Unit
Dwelling Residential, min. lot size 8,000 sq. ft.) and A (Agriculture, min. lot size 1/2 acre).
The property is located at 3801 S 6400 W on 2.38 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-
963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Jake Hone with Grow Development, LLC has submitted a General Plan/zone change application
for a 2.4-acre parcel located at 3801 South 6400 West. The parcel is currently zoned A-2
(Agriculture, minimum lot size 2 acres) with a General Plan designation of Large Lot Residential
(2 to 3 units/acre). The proposed zone is R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot
size 8,000 square feet) for 1.87 acres and A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) for 0.54
acres. The proposed General Plan designation is Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre).

Surrounding zones include R-1-8 to the north, west, and a portion of the south and R-1-7
(Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 7,000 square feet) to the east and the
remaining portion of the south. The property is surrounded by single family homes. The
property includes two homes, two detached garages, and a barn. The larger home to the north
was built in 1943 and the smaller home to the south was built in 1928.

Development Proposal
The applicant is proposing to subdivide the property into a total of 8 lots. The northernmost lot
would be zoned A and retain the larger home. The 7 lots to the south would be zoned R-1-8
and one of these lots would retain the smaller home. The proposal also includes a 16.5-foot
wide, 0.08-acre parcel that is already zoned R-1-8. This parcel would be merged with the lot
directly to the south. The overall density of the development, including the existing homes, is
3.2 units/acre.

Development Agreement
Section 7-11-213 of the Zoning Ordinance states the following: “All new Subdivisions involving
a rezone of property, or a PUD, shall participate in a Development Agreement that addresses
housing size, quality, exterior finish materials, Streetscapes, Landscaping, etc.” To address this
requirement, the applicant has submitted a development agreement proposal. Below is a
summary of the items addressed in the proposal:

• 6 new homes will be built. The minimum area for the new homes will be 1,500 square feet
  for ramblers and 2,250 square feet for multi-levels.
• All new homes will include a basement.
• All new homes will include 3-car garages.
• Renderings of the proposed housing are included.
• The exterior materials will be brick, stone, stucco, fiber cement, or composite materials with
  at least 25% of the front being brick or stone except for the Farmhouse elevation which can
  be 100% fiber cement/composite siding.
• All homes will include a front porch of at least 50 square feet.
• The minimum roof pitch is 4:12.
• Variation is required between homes on adjoining lots or homes directly across the street
  from each other.
• Basement entrances will be prohibited before a certificate of occupancy has been issued.

3. Denial.

S-4-2025 Ana Po’oi is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Niu Haven
Meadows Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 1.38 acres. The
subdivision is located at 4404 South 5400 West and is zoned A. Staff-Steve Lehman at
801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov.

BACKGROUND
Ana Po’oi, is requesting minor subdivision approval for a two-lot subdivision. The subject
property is located at 4404 South 5400 West and is zoned Agriculture. An existing dwelling
occupies the front portion of the property and will remain as part of the subdivision.
Properties to the north and south are zoned R-1-10 while property to the west is zoned R-1-7.

STAFF/AGENCY CONCERNS:

Fire Department:
• The fire hydrant at the end of the existing stub street (5455 West) will suffice if an access
   gate is provided with a knox box.

• The required turnaround within the subdivision can be avoided if the applicant installs a
  gated access as noted above with a knox box. The width of the gate would need to be 3-4

• Follow recommendations of the WVC Building Official regarding basement depth. If the

Building Inspections:
• Based on the existing conditions of adjacent dwellings and according to the soils report of
  the Ashlee Commons Subdivision, the Building Official has determined that the basement
  depth shall be no greater than 2 feet from existing TBC in the Ashlee Commons Subdivision.
  If the applicant would like a basement depth greater than 2 feet from TBC, a soils report
  would be required from a geotechnical firm.

ISSUES:
The applicant is proposing a flag lot subdivision consisting of 2 lots. Lot 1 will be
approximately 33,454 square feet while lot 2 will be approximately 26,901 square feet. Lot 2 is
required to have a minimum square footage of 21,560 square feet which excludes the stem
portion of the lot. According to the subdivision plat, lot 2 will have an area of 21,730 square
feet excluding the stem. All frontage and area requirements relating to both lots will be
satisfied as part of the A zone.

While the subdivision is located between two stub streets, the access to the subdivision will be
gained from 5400 West. All public improvements including curb, gutter, sidewalk and parkstrip

are existing along said right-of-way. An existing drive approach for the new lot was installed
some time ago and appears to be situated in the correct location for access to the new lot. A
new water and sewer line will need to be installed for lot 2. The applicant and/or builder will
need to coordinate these improvements with GHID and City Engineering Division when these
lines are to be installed.

Given the depth of the new lot from 5400 West, a turnaround for emergency services would be
required as well as a new fire hydrant within the boundaries of lot 2 itself. However, after
discussions with the Fire Department, they are fine using the existing stub street and existing
fire hydrant to service this property. They do have a condition of approval that the existing
fence have a man gate for access. The gate would be locked from the outside and would be
required to have a knox box for the Fire Department. The applicant will need to work with the
Fire Department regarding this matter as the subdivision moves forward.

As with all new subdivision developments, there is a concern with the potential of ground
water impacts. In situations where only one new lot is being proposed, the City has allowed
the Building Official to do an on-site inspection as well as referencing old geotechnical reports
to determine the basement depth. In this case, a soils report was prepared by the Ashlee
Commons developer in 2015 which indicated a relatively shallow basement depth. Based on
that report and the on-site inspection, the Building Official has determined that the basement
depth can be 2 feet below existing top back of curb on the stub road.

CA-2-2025 Petition by Kilgore Properties LLC requesting a conditional use amendment
to extend the operating period of a batch plant. The property is located at 7057 West
2100 South on 8.0 acres, and is zoned M, Manufacturing. (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-
3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Kilgore Properties LLC and Altaview Concrete are requesting a conditional use amendment to
extend the operation period of a concrete batch plant at 7057 West 2100 South. This property
is zoned M, Manufacturing and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as Heavy
Manufacturing. The surrounding zones are M, Manufacturing and this parcel borders Salt Lake
City to the north and the zoning there is OS, Open Space.

The site is currently occupied by Kilgore Contracting and this batch plant was originally
approved as a temporary installation under CA-3-2021. Kilgore initially proposed to have this
plant in operation for approximately 6 months (June-November 2021) but have since decided
to extend that and would like to continue the operation of this facility.

The batch plant is approximately 11’ x 60’ and 30’ tall and is located in the existing yard area
on the south end of the site. As part of the initial review in 2021, the yard was also expanded to
the east to provide additional parking and storage area for Kilgore. All vehicular parking areas
are paved with a hard surface and all storage yard areas remain gravel/crushed asphalt.
Updated site, lighting and grading plans were reviewed for the initial expansion. This also
included approval of the curb cut and expanded landscaping along the north-east access. All
required improvements have been completed and the landscaping that was in need of
maintenance has been replaced.

If at some point in the future the plant operations cease, or another use is proposed for this
area, then a review will be conducted at that time to ensure all necessary requirements and/or
site restoration is completed.

Staff Alternatives:
   1. Approval, to extend the operation period of a concrete batch plant subject to the
      resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing and the following conditions:

         1) All vehicular parking areas must be paved with a hard surface and all yard areas
            may be gravel/crushed asphalt.

         2) Access along 2100 South shall be improved per the standards in Title 7 including
            curb cut width and landscaping.

         3) Must comply with all other requirements set forth from applicable departments
            and agencies.
   2. Continuance, to allow for the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing.

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented. The applicant, Lee Ware of Kilgore Properties, was present

Motion: Commissioner Porter motioned to approve CA-2-2025 subject to the three conditions
