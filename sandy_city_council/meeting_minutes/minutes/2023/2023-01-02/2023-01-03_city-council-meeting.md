7.         23-006                 Administration recommending further Council discussion and direction 
                                  surrounding the possible sale of naming rights for the Alta Canyon Sports 
                                  Center
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Naming Rights Policy Issues.docx

                                  Administration requested direction from the Council regarding the development of the 
                                  naming-rights policy for Alta Canyon Recreation Center. The discussion was led by Lynn 
                                  Pace, City Attorney, and Jeff Robinson, Deputy City Attorney. The attorneys presented 
                                  policy questions and recommended options for Council consideration. Council discussed 
                                  each option and provided direction to the legal staff.

                                  A summary of the discussion and direction to Administration is provided. Please access 
                                  the recording for a complete review of this discussion:

                                  Council recommended limiting the naming rights policy to Alta Canyon Recreation Center 
                                  at this point in time. The Special Service District will remain in place at this time. Council 
                                  has discretion over choosing naming-rights sponsors. Council also discussed limiting the 
                                  total number of potential sponsors to five and discussed varying amounts of funding 
                                  related to the different sponsorships deemed to be available. The majority of council 
                                  members agreed to a a maximum of 20 years for a naming rights sponsorship of the 
                                  recreation center.

                                  Legal and Administration thanked the Council for their direction and will move forward in 
                                  the development of a policy which will be brought back to Council for further discussion 
                                  and consideration.

                                  Council reconvened at 7:47 pm

8.         23-007                 Council considering whether to rebuild or remodel the Alta Canyon Sports 
                                  Center
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  November 8 2022 Memorandum
                                  Resolution 23-02C_Draft.docx

                                  Council moved to consider Option B and Option D presented in the Alta Canyon 
                                  Feasibility Analysis and discussed whether to rebuild or remodel the Alta Canyon 
                                  Recreation Center. The Council discussion focused on the following: the impact to Sandy 
                                  City residents, the Special Service District, the costs associated with each option, the 
                                  possible funding sources, capping the project at $40 million, and private sponsorships 
                                  and naming rights. Dan Medina, Parks and Recreation Director offered additional 
                                  information and responded to questions. Council emphasized that the Alta Canyon 
                                  Recreation Center would also include offices for the Parks & Recreation administrative 
                                  staff. A motion was made to adopt Resolution 23-02C selecting Option D, the rebuild of 
                                  Alta Canyon Recreation Center. A friendly amendment was offered and accepted to cap 
                                  the project costs at $40 million dollars.
                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Scott  Earl, to adopt 
                                  Resolution 23-02C, selecting Option D (rebuild option) of the Alta Canyon 
                                  Feasibility Analysis and directing Administration to move forward with the 
                                  architectural design process to rebuild the Alta Canyon Sports Center. The total 
                                  cost of the project shall not exceed $40,000,000 (Forty Million). The Resolution 
                                  shall become effective immediately....The motion carried by the following vote:

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Houseman thanked her colleagues for appointing her as the liaison to 
                    the Service Ambassadors Committee. She acknowledged and thanked two of our snow 
                    plow drivers (Tyler and Robert) who went above and beyond to help a resident by 
                    rebuilding a replica of her beloved mail box that had been damaged in the recent snow 
                    storm. Their efforts reflect the dedication of the employees city-wide.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud read comments from residents praising the city snow plow 
                    drivers for all their help and dedication in keeping our city streets safe and clear of snow.

                    Council Member Scott Earl thanked Mike Gladbach and the entire Public Works staff. As 
                    he is approaching one full year on the Council, he thanked his fellow council members for 
                    their support and confidence in him. 

                    Council Member Zach Robinson wished everyone a Happy New Year. He thanked the 
                    snow plow and parks staff for their dedication and efforts during the recent storms. He 
                    reminded residents to provide greater clearance when they see first responders on the 
                    roads as they respond to accidents - please drive slowly and carefully. He also reminded 
                    residents to remember to dig out any fire hydrants in their area or on their property.

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa also wished everyone a Happy New Year. She spoke 
                    about her first year serving as a City Council Member and appreciated the opportunity to 
                    serve. She mentioned that she was caught in the blizzard in Buffalo over the holidays, 
                    and just wanted to remind residents to be prepared for emergencies. She acknowledged 
                    the Sandy Communications staff for their efforts to keep the residents informed and all 
                    city employees for their commitment to helping our Sandy residents.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked the Public Works Crew member, Robert, who 
                    helped him dig out a car buried in the snow. And he thanked the public works and parks 
                    crews for their response and snow cleanup efforts following the recent storms. 

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey thanked the staff in Public Works and Parks - she 
                    appreciates all they do during the winter in response to the storms and for the bulk waste 
                    spring and fall clean up. She expressed her gratitude to all of these important employees.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the recent storm. The three day total was the largest 
                    snowfall in over 20 years. She reviewed how the Public Works department prioritizes the 
                    snow removal around the city and she acknowledged the Public Works and Parks staff 
                    for their amazing efforts in response to this storm. This Saturday is the Legislative 
                    breakfast from 9 am to 11 am in the Council Chambers. All are invited. She spoke about 
                    the swearing in ceremonies of newly elected officials. And she acknowledged the Council 
                    for their service to our residents.

CAO Report

                   Shane Pace, CAO, spoke about the snow clearing efforts and the city's response to the 
                   recent storm: 20 trucks were out, 891 lane miles were cleared, the city has gone through 
                   2,000 tons of salt. He mentioned the city will be requesting additional funds to replenish 
                   our salt reserves. He spoke about the challenges for the snow plow drivers when 
                   residents park their cars in the street. Mayor Zoltanski mentioned that the Public Works 
                   staff received 99 calls in six hours from residents during the snow storm. She asked the 
                   Council and residents to educate our residents and remind them to avoid parking in the 
                   streets during snow storms.


Adjournment
                   Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 8:56 pm.
