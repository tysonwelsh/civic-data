                                     RIVERTON, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-74

       APPROVING RIVERTON CITY'S RELEASE OF BOND TO GUARANTEE
              RESTORATION OF CR HAMILTON SPORTS PARK

       WHEREAS, the Lovell Development Group has engaged in the development of a
subdivision in the vicinity of 3854 West 13800 South, Riverton, Utah, known as the Hidden
Pines Subdivision; and

      WHEREAS, Riverton City (“City”) owns and operates a park known as the CR
Hamilton Sports Park adjacent to the Developer’s property; and

       WHEREAS, to facilitate the proper development of the Hidden Pines Subdivision,
Lovell Development Group needed to install an 8” sewer line through the southwest corner of the
CR Hamilton Sports Park; and

       WHEREAS, Installation of said sewer line required an easement be granted across the
park property affected by the sewer line; and

        WHEREAS, On June 18, 2020, the Riverton City Council adopted Resolution No. 1934,
which Authorized the City to execute an Easement Agreement with the Lovell Development
Group, whereby the City granted the Lovell Development Group a temporary construction
easement across the CR Hamilton Sports Park; and granted a second sewer easement to the South
Valley Sewer District once the Lovell Development Group completed installation of said sewer
line; and

        WHEREAS, to guarantee the CR Hamilton Sports Park would be restored following
installation of the sewer line needed to service the Hidden Pines Subdivision, the City executed a
separate Improvement Agreement, whereby the Lovell Development Group, dba Cazco
Enterprises, guaranteed the Park’s restoration by posting a cash bond; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines the Lovell Development
Group has completed restoration of the CR Hamilton Sports Park, and City staff has inspected
and approved the work conducted by the Lovell Development Group to restore the CR Hamilton
Sports Park; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the cash bond posted
by Cazco Enterprises should be released in fulfillment of the Improvement Agreement;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. The Mayor is authorized to release all funds held by Riverton City, and posted by
      Cazco Enterprises, in fulfillment of the City’s Improvement Agreement with Cazco
      Enterprises, upon verification that all aspects of said release are approved as to legal
      form by the Riverton City Attorney.

   2. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage and acceptance as provided
      herein.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 1st day of
