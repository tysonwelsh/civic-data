Ordinances, 2001 is hereby amended to read as follows:
9.04.030 - Membership
The Salt Lake County board of health shall consist of fifteen members 
residing in Salt Lake County, including: a member of the [county council ] 
Salt Lake County Council selected by the County Council  and fourteen other 
members, appointed by the [county mayor ] Salt Lake County Mayor  with 
advice and consent of the [c] County [c] Council.
A. Board members shall be appointed on a nonpartisan basis and include 
geographical representation from [both the incorporated and unincorporated 
areas of ] throughout the county.  Members should have knowledge of an 
interest in public health and environmental matters and shall include:
1. A physician or surgeon licensed in the state;
2. A dentist licensed in the state;
3. A health care professional licensed in the state;
4. A non-health care professional licensed in the state;
5. Two representatives of the public not representing or connected 
with the regulated business community;
6. A representative of the regulated business community;
7. A mayor representing a municipality in the county [;]
B. [Any potential conflict of interest of any member shall be disclosed in

Salt Lake City Annexation Resolution
RESOLUTION NO. 5995
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
AGREEING TO THE CREATION OF AN ISLAND RESULTING FROM 
THE PROPOSED SALT LAKE ANNEXATION BY SALT LAKE CITY
WHEREAS, Salt Lake City was served with a notice of intent to file a 
petition to annex what the petition sponsor terms the Salt Lake Annexation, 
as shown on the map attached as Exhibit A, which could lead to a portion of 
what is currently unincorporated Salt Lake County being annexed into Salt 
Page 6 of 11
County Council Meeting Minutes August 2, 2022
Lake City; and
WHEREAS, the proposed annexation is being pursued under and controlled 
by Utah State Code Sections 10-2-401, et seq.; and
WHEREAS, the proposed annexation would, in the opinion of the Salt Lake 
County Surveyor and the Salt Lake County District Attorney ’s Office, create 
an unincorporated island; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-2-402(1)(b)(iii)(B), if a 
proposed annexation will create an unincorporated island the County and 
annexing municipality must agree to such annexation;
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, THAT;
Salt Lake County agrees to allow Salt Lake City to create the unincorporated 
island described in Exhibit A that would result if Salt Lake City accepts, 
certifies, and approves, the proposed Salt Lake annexation and its otherwise 
able to meet the requirements of Utah Code Sections 10-2-401, et seq.
It is the hope of Salt Lake County that Salt Lake City will use its best efforts 
to also annex the unincorporated island that would be created by the 
proposed Salt Lake Annexation.
RESOLUTION NO. 5995 APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Salt Lake City, 
Salt Lake County, Utah this 2nd day of August, 2022.
