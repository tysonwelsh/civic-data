OATH OF OFFICE ADMINISTERED TO NEW COUNCIL MEMBERS:

  City Recorder Lisa E. Brough administered the Oath of Office to newly elected council members Shari
Cowan, Travis L. Worwood, and JD Parady. The press took pictures and the new council members
signed the official Oath of Office documents.

Mayor Justin Seely opened the regular council meeting and welcomed all present.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Parady moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting 12-19-23, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 1-2-24. Councilor
Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ANIMAL CONTROL OFFICER SHIRLENE BUCKLEY HONORED FOR HER RETIREMENT:

   Mayor Seely read a tribute for retiring Animal Control Officer Shirlene Buckley. Ms. Buckley started
working for Nephi City in September of 1988. She had been responsible for managing the animal
shelter, She was a big proponent of having a no-kill shelter. She was instrumental in getting donations
from sponsors for supplies and food for the animals at the shelter. Mayor Seely stated that Shirlene is
known for her kind and gentle way of handling animals and difficult situations. Shirlene has been an
exemplary employee for 35 years. Mayor Seely presented her with a plaque of recognition and a gift of
appreciation. Ms. Buckley thanked the City for her employment and said she will miss the people she
has worked with.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Cory Thompson stated that he did not want the city council to forget about making changes to the
vicious dog ordinance.

BLACK AND MCDONALD APPROVED FOR SUBSTATION CONSTRUCTION PROJECTS:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the design phase of the 138 Kv electric transmission project
had been completed and the City had requested bids for the construction of a substation on Nortonville
Road and construction of a new substation on Elevator Road and improvements to Substation 3. The
city received several bids and the low bidder was Black and McDonald for $2,558,224.00.
  Mr. Atkinson stated that Black and McDonald had received high reviews from other organizations that
had used them for projects. He also announced that the city had received notice of being awarded a
$600,000 Rural Communities Opportunity Grant. The grant is funded 90% up front and can be used for
the 138 KV electric transmission project.
   Councilor Callaway moved to approve the low bid by Black and McDonald. Councilor Parady
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Work Session 8:10 pm

   1. The Council discussed the possibility of hiring a full-time golf course superintendent.
      John Bale, the course's part-time superintendent, has recently retired. Mr. Atkinson reviewed
      the golf course number of rounds of golf played and revenue over the past several years. The
      revenues and rounds played have extremely increased. The council directed staff to move
      forward with the hiring of a full-time superintendent.

   2.    Mr. Atkinson presented a draft of a new On-Call Compensation Policy. The policy clarified the
        rate of pay for on-call status and offered some additional compensation time earned for being
        on-call. The council agreed with the on-call policy draft and directed staff to bring it back for
        formal adoption.
