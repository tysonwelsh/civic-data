COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER - STATED THAT SHE IS GRATEFUL FOR
THE WORK THAT HAS BEEN DONE AND THAT IT IS IMPERATIVE WE
UNDERSTAND WHERE OUR FOOD COMES FROM. SHE MENTIONS THE NEED
TO CONTINUE UTAH COUNTY’S RICH AGRICULTURAL HERITAGE.

DEAN MINOR, RETIRED USU EXTENSION - STATED HIS THANKS FOR THE
RECOGNITION. HE GAVE THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONER’S A FARM FIELD
DAY BOOKLET WRITTEN AND ILLUSTRATED BY SECOND GRADE
STUDENTS TELLING WHAT THEY LEARNED WHILE VISITING FARM FIELD
DAYS. HE POINTED OUT THE LARGE IMPACT FARM FIELD DAYS HAD ON
STUDENTS FROM THE INNER-CITY SCHOOLS AND THAT THEIR
UNDERSTANDING OF HOW THEIR FOOD IS PRODUCED WENT FROM 20% TO
80% AFTER ATTENDING.

CHRIS RILEY, VICE PRESIDENT UTAH COUNTY FARM BUREAU – STATED
ON BEHALF OF THE LEADERSHIP OF UTAH COUNTY FARM BUREAU, THE
DESIRE TO RECOGNISE THE COUNTY FARM BUREAU BOARD, AS WELL AS
THE FOOD GROWERS’ INDUSTRY. SPRING IS A DIFFICULT TIME FOR FOOD
PRODUCERS. MANY ARE UP ALL NIGHT PROTECTING CROPS FROM FROST.
OTHERS ARE IN THE MIDDLE OF LAMBING AND CALVING SEASON, YET

THEY BELIEVE IN WHAT IS BEING DONE DURING THESE FARM FIELD DAYS
AND ARE HAPPY TO VOLUNTEER THEIR TIME AND ENERGY TO THE
CAUSE. HE SPECIFICALLY RECOGNIZED DEAN MINOR AND BELVA PARR
FOR THE PASSION THEY HAVE FOR THE FARM FIELD DAYS PROJECT AND
THANKED THEM FOR THE MANY HOURS THEY HAVE SPENT OVER THE
YEARS. HE EXPRESSES GRATITUDE FOR BEING ABLE TO USE THE
AMERICAN FORK EQUESTRIAN CENTER DURING FALL FARM FIELD DAYS
AND THANKED HARWARD FARMS IN SPRINGVILLE FOR THE TIME,
ENERGY AND MONEY THEY HAVE SPENT OVER THE YEARS SETTING UP. IT
IS A BIG EFFORT FROM A LOT OF KEY PEOPLE WHO CONTRIBUTE, AND HE
EXPRESSED HIS GRATITUDE FOR THE RECOGNITION FROM THE BOARD
OF COMMISSIONERS.

BELVA PARR, CO-CHAIR OF FARM FIELD DAYS - STATED HER
APPRECIATION FOR BEING ABLE TO USE THE EQUESTRIAN CENTER AND
FOR THE SUPPORT UTAH COUNTY HAS GIVEN FARM FIELD DAYS,
THROUGH ITS COUNTY EXTENSION OFFICE AND THEIR AGENTS. THERE
WILL BE AN EVENT TO RECOGNIZE THE ORIGINAL GROUP WHO STARTED
THE PROJECT THIRTY YEARS AGO. THE COMMISSIONERS ARE INVITED TO
ATTEND. FARM FIELD DAYS IS A BIG UNDERTAKING WITH SOMEWHERE
AROUND FIFTY TO SIXTY VOLUNTEERS A DAY. IT COULD NOT BE DONE
WITHOUT THE SUPPORT FROM LOCAL SCHOOLS, FFA’S, AG TEACHERS
AND MANY OTHERS. SHE EXPRESSES HER GRATITUDE FOR BEING A PART
OF THIS FANTASTIC PROJECT. DEAN MINOR IS ONE OF THE FEW
ORIGINAL ORGANIZERS AND ACKNOWLEDGES THE WORK HE HAS DONE
OVER THE YEARS.

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER – STATED THAT THE OPPORTUNITY
GIVEN FOR MENTORSHIP OF THE FFA STUDENTS IS FANTASTIC.

CHRIS RILEY, VICE PRESIDENT UTAH COUNTY FARM BUREAU – STATED HE IS A
FRUIT GROWER IN GENOLA, AND THAT HE IS LOOKING FOR SOME FEEDBACK
FROM THE COMMISSIONERS REGARDING THE DIFFICULTY THEY ARE HAVING
SELLING FRUIT HERE IN UTAH. HE AND OTHER GROWERS IN SOTHERN UTAH
COUNTY FORMED A GROUP TO HELP MARKET THEIR LOCALLY GROWN FRUIT. A
GOOD PART OF THEIR FRUIT IS SHIPPED OUT OF STATE BECAUSE LOCAL FARMS
ARE SMALLER AND UNABLE TO MEET THE QUANTITY OF FRUIT NEEDED TO FILL
THE DISTRIBUTION CENTERS OF BIG RETAILERS.

THE GROUP DESIRES TO WORK CLOSELY WITH UTAH’S OWN, THEY ARE LOOKING
INTO GRANT OPPORTUNITIES AND EACH FARM WILL CONTRIBUTE BETWEEN
ONE AND THREE THOUSAND DOLLARS PER YEAR TO BE USED FOR MARKETING
THE VALUE OF UTAH FRUIT. THEY ARE LOOKING FOR SUPPORT AND
WONDERING, IF AT THE COUNTY LEVEL, THERE MIGHT BE OTHER AVENUES OF
INCOME TO HELP WITH THEIR EFFORTS. A MONTHLY MEETING IS BEING HELD BY
