              SUMMARY:     Chris Zarek, Cowboy Properties, is requesting a General Plan map amendment for
                           the property located at 1758 W. 5400 S. from Regional Commercial to High-Density
                           Mixed Use.


                           The Taylorsville General Plan is intended to guide land use and development
                           decisions that address the present and future needs of the City and to accomplish
                           coordinated, efficient, and harmonious growth and development within the City. The
                           City Council, at their discretion, may make revisions to the General Plan that it
                           considers appropriate and that are in the best interest of the City and its residents
                            per Section 13. 06. 030: General Plan Adoption and Amendments).


                           Page 3- 6 of the Taylorsville General Plan states the following," All amendments to
                           the Proposed Land Use Map should be required to consider and not be in conflict
                           with the following principles:
                                 Promote economic sustainability
                                   Promote efficient use of land and public infrastructure
                                   Provide community amenities and benefits
                                   Protect environmentally sensitive lands
                                   Support alternate modes of transportation including pedestrian
                                   Create safe attractive neighborhoods and protect residential quality of life
                                   Minimize non- compatible adjacent land uses


                           The applicant is seeking a General Plan map amendment with the intention to
                           develop approximately 182 apartment units. 4,675 square feet of commercial space,
                           and 1, 991 square feet for leasing and clubhouse space in a four-story mixed-use
                           structure. Exhibit E includes the most recent development concept illustrating




                                                 1
                                proposed development if this General Plan map amendment and a subsequent rezone
                                request were approved by the City Council.

                                The current zoning designation of the subject property is Community Commercial
                                 CC). The applicant has submitted a separate Zoning Map amendment application
                                 File # 1Z21) requesting a rezone to the Site- Specific Development ( SSD) zoning
                                district. This item has not yet been scheduled for consideration by the Planning
                                Commission. Instead, the applicant has requested that the General Plan map
                                amendment be considered by the City Council prior to scheduling a hearing for the
                                SSD request to weigh the merits of the larger development concept.


     STAFF RECOMMENDATION:     Staff recommends denial of Ordinance No. 21- 11 based on the following findings
                               related to the proposed development plan:

                                          It is not part of a comprehensive and cohesive master plan for the larger
                                          commercial shopping center. Such a plan should address site planning,
                                          general site design, architectural design and materials, landscaping,
                                          circulation, lighting, signage, and grading, drainage, and utilities( see Land
                                          Development Code Section 13. 23. 240);
                                          The three commercial centers on the intersection of 5400 South and
                                          Redwood Road are the key economic drivers in Taylorsville. The current
                                          adopted policy is to prioritize and promote retail and commercial uses in
                                          this area;

                                          Mixed-use structures should have significant nonresidential elements to
                                          activate the street level/ ground floor, this is a multi-family project with
                                          4, 675 square feet of retail space; and
                                          The project may negatively impact residential neighborhoods and property
                                          owners to the north and west due to building height, density, placement,
                                          setbacks, circulation, etc.

        Summary:
        Chris Zarek, representing Cowboy Properties, is requesting to change the Proposed Land
        Use Map designation in the City's General Plan for the property at 1758 W. 5400 S. from
         Regional Commercial" to" High- Density Mixed- Use." The applicant is seeking this General
        Plan Map amendment with the intention to develop a four-story multi- family residential
        building on a vacant site in the Legacy Plaza shopping center. Conceptual development
        plans are included in this report( Exhibit E) illustrating a proposed development scenario if
        the General Plan Map amendment and subsequent Site- Specific Development( SSD) rezone
        request are approved by the City Council.

        Site Description
        The subject property is 3. 83 acres on partially undeveloped land in the Legacy Plaza
        shopping center. The property has direct vehicle access to 5400 S. as well as Redwood Road
        via shared cross- access easement. The property has surface parking improvements on
        portions of the lot, while much of the lot is an undeveloped ( dirt) commercial pad.
        Surrounding zoning designations are as follows:

            North                     South                   East                      West
            Single- Family R- 1- 8    Regional                Community                 Community
                                      Commercial ( RC)        Commercial ( CC)          Commercial ( CC)


        General Plan Analysis
        The Taylorsville General Plan is intended to guide land use and development decisions that
        address the present and future needs of the City and to accomplish coordinated, efficient, and
        harmonious growth and development within the City. The Planning Commission reviews and
        makes recommendations to the City Council regarding adoption of or amendments to the
        General Plan. The City Council, at their discretion, may make revisions to the General Plan
        that it considers appropriate and that are in the best interest of the City and its residents
            Section 13. 06. 030).



        The subject property is currently designated as" Regional Commercial" on the Proposed
        Land Use Map and is within the Center Point Small Area Plan ( Exhibit F). The following
        statement describes the intent of the Regional Commercial designation ( pg 3- 4):

                    The Regional Commercial( RC) designation isfor those areas that contain large
                   shopping centers of regional significance, such as the Family Center at
                   Taylorsville located on Redwood Road. Properties that carry an RC classification
                   commonly provide a wider array of services such as major department stores,
                   specialty shops, and professional offices. Generally, RC districts serve an area
                   within a 5 to 15 mile radius, which usually extends beyond the boundaries of
                   Taylorsville. RC districts are usually located adjacent or near intersections of
                   major transportation corridors."



        The applicant' s proposal for a residential development is not consistent with the adopted
        policies and goals in the City's General Plan for this property; therefore, a General Plan map
        amendment is required to establish the necessary policy to support the applicant' s

                   High Density Residential ( HDR) districts are residential areas that contain

        Based on this information, staff does not support the applicant's request to amend the
        General Plan map from Regional Commercial to High- Density Mixed- Use. The following is a
        summary of staffs findings regarding the proposed concept development plan:

                  It is not consistent with the adopted General Plan or Center Point Small Area Plan;

                  It would create a high- density residential project in the middle of a vehicle- oriented
                  commercial center;

                  It is isolated, out of scale, and out of character with the area and the rest of the
                  shopping center;
                  The three commercial centers on the intersection of 5400 South and Redwood Road
                  are the key economic drivers in Taylorsville. The current adopted policy is to
                  prioritize and promote retail and commercial uses in this area;

                  The project may negatively impact residential neighborhoods and property owners
                  to the north and west due to building height, density, placement, setbacks,
                  circulation, etc.; and

                  Mixed-use structures should have significant nonresidential elements aside from
                  leasing offices and resident amenities to activate the street level/ ground floor.
