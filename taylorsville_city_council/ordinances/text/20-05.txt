 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING AMENDMENTS
  TO TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE CHAPTER 9. 61. 020: HOURS OF USE OF
                                                               OFF LEASH AREA.


          WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on
February 5, 2020, to consider, among other things, approving amendments to TAYLORSVILLE
MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 9. 61. 020: Hours of Use of Off Leash Area; and


        WHEREAS, TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 9. 61. 020 currently states the hours
of operation for off-leash areas of parks as 7: 00 a. m.—
                                                      8: 00 p.m. during designated winter months
and 7: 00 a.m. — 10: 00 p.m. during designated summer months; and




       WHEREAS, the City desires to amend the hours of operation for off-leash areas to allow
for more flexibility when determining health, safety, and welfare factors as they relate to citizen
use of off-leash areas; and


          WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 9. 61. 020 are hereby approved
and are designated by interlineating the words to be deleted and underlining the words to be added,
as follows:
