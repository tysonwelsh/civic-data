From: Tom Draper
CC:  Date: April 6, 2021
Re: Public Hearing: Approval of Ordinance #1483, Vacations of Wildrye Field Way Comments: This request is for the approval of Ordinance 1483, public right-of-way vacation of a portion of Wildrye Field Way. Wildrye Field Way was dedicated with the Big Willow Creek Phases 1A and 1B Subdivisions and recorded October 17, 2018. At the time, the Big Willow Creek Subdivision did not include several parcels that were later added to the overall project area in the first and second amendments to the development agreement, executed April 16, 2019, and May 5, 2020, respectively. Since that time, Ivory has been working on a comprehensive development plan for the additional property to be integrated into the Big Willow Creek Subdivision. This effort included a desire to relocate the subdivision access from 11400 South to a more central part of the subdivision. The findings are as follows: Neither the public interest nor any person will be materially injured by the vacations, the rights-of-way no longer serves a public purpose, and there is good cause for the vacations.
