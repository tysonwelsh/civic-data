Order of Business

Accomplishments/Progress on General Plan

             Kayla Mauldin, Long Range Senior Planner, Municipal Services District,
presented an update on what has been accomplished this year relating to the Kearns General
Plan.

    Two grants awarded this year as well as the Healthy Utah designation award.

    Coordinated with the Wasatch Front Regional Council and Wasatch Choice to make
     sure the land use centers, which are outlined in the general plan, made it onto the
     regional plan.

    Adjusted the zoning ordinance to allow for mixed used residential development in the
     Kearns Town Center.

    Transportation projects: study traffic calming on 5400 South, created guides to
     navigating the bus system, provided feedback on local and regional transportation
     efforts, and received a grant to study on street parking in Kearns. Working on active
     transportation project, which will be kicked off later this month.

    Economic Development: looking at best ways to showcase Kearns history, helped to
     improve first and last mile transit connections, preparing a plan to improve access to

       available in Kearns.

    Resilient and infrastructure held Kearns Arts Townhall to create a local arts agency in
     Kearns, shared lessons with other local agencies on what Kearns has to offer, digital
     equity working group to promote digital infrastructure in Kearns.

                Travis Hair, Planner, Municipal Services District, continued the presentation
highlighting the following:

    Updated zoning ordinances to allow for more permitted uses and reduced conditional
     uses, removed single family homes as permitted uses where commercial centers should

    Amended the Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) Ordinance.

    Amended Flood Damage Ordinance

    Building permits: there are currently 471 opened and 361 were closed this year. Most of
     which have been renovation permits.

Planning Commission

Application No. PAM2021-000364 -Shivam Shah (Brixton Kearns LLC)

                 Molly Gaughran, Planner, Municipal Services District, stated Brixton Partners
have purchased three lots of the Kinikini commercial subdivision located at 5198 West Liberator
Dr. in the Camp Kearns area and is requesting permission to amend the final plat to vacate a
public utility easement at the rear of the lots. Brixton Partners have received utility vacation
letters from Comcast and Dominion Energy and are working on obtaining a letter from
PacificCorp. There are no issues involved with this request. The preliminary plat has received
approval from all agencies as well as the Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission.

              Council Member Schaeffer, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
approve this application. The motion passed unanimously.

Application No. REZ2021-00430 – Rezone from R-1-6 (Single-Family Residential to C-2
(Commercial)

               Molly Gaughran, Planner, Municipal Services District, stated this 0.19 acre is a
corner lot that fronts 4015 West and 5500 South and is currently used as a single-family
residence with an active home business where the resident of the property provides services as
a barber. The proposed rezone would allow for the home business use to be the primary use,

Legal Updates/Information

Interlocal Agreement with Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)

               Mayor Kelly Bush stated there has been a couple of changes to the interlocal
agreement proposed by Salt Lake County, which has been accepted. However, there are a
couple of things that MSD is waiting on and not certain when it will be sent over. Since there is
a time constraint she asked if the Council would be okay to approving the agreement if the
changes are not substantial.

              Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated he expects the
changes will be minor tweaks and edits and not related to the amount of money Kearns would
get.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
approve interlocal agreement subject to the Mayor and Legal Counsel finalizing the agreement
with any non-substantial changes that may occur. The motion passed unanimously.

Food Vouchers

              Council Member Snow asked if the Council would like to use CARES Act funds
to purchase food vouchers again this year.

              Council Members all agreed that this would be a good idea.

              Mayor Bush asked how many and in what amount.

              Council Member Snow suggested the Council buy 1,000 vouchers in the
amount of $50.00 each.

            Council Member Peterson stated another idea would be to work with a utility
company and deduct $20 from all resident’s bills.

               Council Member Snow stated she was not sure what would be involved to do
something like this. The Council could go ahead with the food vouchers and then discuss other
ways to help residents.

              Council Member Schaeffer asked how these vouchers would be distributed.

                Council Member Snow stated there are several entities the Council could work
with to distribute the vouchers such as the food bank, Unified Police Department, Unified Fire
Department, and the senior center. Council Members could take 10 vouchers each and past
them out to residents within their districts that need of assistance.

             Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
approve using CARE funds to get 1,000 vouchers at $50 each. The motion passed
unanimously.
