Work Session
(4:30 P.M.)
1)
Work Session regarding a Master Planned Development application for the Latitudes project, a 5-building mixed use project located at 6417 N Pace Frontage Rd, Silver Summit, Summit County, UT; Parcel SCBP-10-2AM; Applicant: Clive Bridgwater representing Fisher Creek, LLC (Roger Arsht and Ron Sharp); Administrative Process. Project #25-001, 25-002, and 25-003. Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner.
(4:30 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Latitudes MPD-NMU Staff Report - Work Session - May 27, 2025.pdf
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner,
presented the Latitudes Master Planned Development application for an introductory work session.
Clive Bridgwater,
Applicant
, introduced his team and provided a brief history of the project. Mr. Bridgwater outlined the new vision and sought feedback on the mixed-use project. (4:32 P.M.)
Commissioner Young asked clarifying questions regarding the 55+ demographics, preferences for commercial space, building height, and setbacks. Mr. Bridgwater responded to Commissioner Young’s concerns. (4:45 P.M.)
Commissioner Hawley commented on the height of the project, setbacks, amenities, and affordable unit equivalents. (4:53 P.M.)
Commissioner Jeffrey asked a clarifying question about public transportation, affordable housing calculations, and building height. Mr. Bridgwater, Planner Caus, and Pierre Langue with Axis Architects responded to the concerns raised. (5:00 P.M.)
Commissioner Nagie asked for clarification regarding the number of units proposed and the breakdown of the units. Mr. Bridgwater responded. Commissioner Nagie asked for additional information about the 55+ programming and any council precedent for Master Planned Developments. Planner Caus, and Principal Planner, Jennifer Strader, provided additional information to the discussion. (5:08 P.M.)
Commissioner Peteroy commented on the need for a transit center to support a Neighborhood Mixed Use project. She asked about the potential for nightly rental units within the project and for additional information on community commercial options. Planners Caus and Strader provided information for the discussion. (5:26 P.M.)
Commissioner Sagerman provided additional comments on commercial spaces within a Neighborhood Mixed Use project. (5:38 P.M.)
Mr. Bridgwater thanked the Commission for their time and feedback. (5:48 P.M.)
-Break-

Laura Kuhrmeyer,
Planner
, introduced a two-part application to determine the historical significance of the Hi Ute barn as well as a Conditional Use Permit for the adaptive reuse of the structure for cultural purposes and activities.
Commissioners Sagerman and Nagie asked brief clarifying questions to which Wendy Fisher with Utah Open Lands and Planner Kuhrmeyer responded. (6:18 P.M.)
Commissioner Chair Mooney opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:21 p.m.
Chris Meeka was called to speak at 6:22 p.m.
Commission Chair Mooney closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:23 p.m.
Commissioners commented on the project and asked follow-up questions. Ms. Fisher and Joseph Geroux,
Applicant
, responded to their comments. (6:24 P.M.)
Makena Hawley made a motion to determine that the Hi Ute Barn is historically significant according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Spencer Young seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
Makena Hawley made a motion to approve the conditional use permit for the adaptive reuse of an historically significant structure according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Matthew Nagie seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
