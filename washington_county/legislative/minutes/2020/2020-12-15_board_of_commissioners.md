PUBLIC HEARING: TO OPEN THE 2020 WASHINGTON COUNTY BUDGET

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Open the Public Hearing for the
Opening of the 2020 Washington County Budget. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Almquist and Cox voting aye.

Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen explained that there were some minor changes to several of the
2020 accounts as adjustments were made throughout the year once actual figures were
known. The full year of Recorder’s Office fees have been implemented, and additions to
the .25% sales tax option were added. The Grants and Endowments fund was also adjusted
to compensate for the CARES Act funding that was received.

reflect that all public comments have been heard, and people have had the opportunity to
be heard. Comments were taken by many methods: in ZOOM using the “reaction” icon of
clapping or thumbs up or the chat feature, and by phone participation, and by in person
participation. All methods have been monitored by county staff. The Public Hearing was
opened and closed only after everyone had the opportunity to make public comment.

Seeing no one come forward, Chairman Iverson asked for a motion to close the public
hearing.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Close the Public Hearing for the
Opening of the 2020 Washington County Budget. Motion seconded by

Commissioners Iverson, Almquist and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2702, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUNDS GRANT DONATION
AGREEMENT __BETWEEN _WASHINGTON COUNTY AND CITY OF
ENTERPRISE

Enterprise City Councilman Jared Bollinger explained that the City is in need of power load
systems for their ambulances. They are requesting three (3) power lifts and three gumeys.
This system allows for fewer people required to lift occupants into the ambulances as well
as increase the social distancing between EMTs and patients.

Chairman Iverson clarified that Enterprise City provides ambulance services from
Enterprise City to Central as well as to the Pine Valley area.

Chief Moody explained that the majority of the EMT’s are volunteers and fall into the high
risk category due to their age. Having this equipment would reduce the exposure to the

Coronavirus.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2702, A
Resolution Approving a Coronavirus Relief Funds Grant Donation
Agreement in the Amount of $163,070.82 Between Washington County
and City of Enterprise. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist
and Cox voting aye.
