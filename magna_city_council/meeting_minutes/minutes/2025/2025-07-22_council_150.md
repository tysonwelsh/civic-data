Subject: Request for City Collaboration to Enhance Museum Visibility and Community
Impact

Dear Members of the Magna City Council,

On behalf of the Magna Museum--a local history museum proudly located in the heart of
downtown Magna-I am writing to explore a potential partnership with the City that would
enhance both our visibility and our shared impact on the community.

The Magna Museum is deeply committed to preserving and sharing the rich stories that have
shaped our community's identity. We believe that history is more than the past-it is a vital part
of our present and future. Our work supports civic pride, local education, and economic
development through tourism and engagement.

As we continue to grow and welcome more visitors, we are seeking the City's support in two
specific areas:

    a   Street Signage: Strategic directional signage near key intersections to help guide visitors
        to the museum.
    a   City Newsletter Inclusion: Periodic highlights or features in the City's newsletter to
        inform residents and visitors about current exhibits, events. and educational programs.

We believe that increased visibility for the Magna Museum will allow us to

   .    Attract more families, school groups, and tourists-generating increased foot traffrc for
        downtown businesses.
   .    Support the City's economic development goals through cultural tourism.
   .    Offer meaningful educational experiences for residents of all ages.
   .    Deepen community engagement and civic pride by celebrating our shared heritage.

This proposed collaboration is strongly aligned with the City's ongoing efforts to revitalize
downtown, support local businesses, and promote Magna's unique cultural assets. We are
excited by the potential to work together to achieve these shared goals.

We would welcome the opportunity to meet with you or your staff to discuss these ideas further
and explore how the City and the Magna Museum can collaborate more closely.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.
