        The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agendas for the Special City Council, the regular City Council,
and Special Redevelopment Agency meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.; discuss Recreation, Arts, Museum, and Parks
(RAMP) project priorities; discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board comments.

Agenda Review
         Chair Nadolski and members of Council staff briefly reviewed the items listed on the Special City Council, regular City
Council, and Special Redevelopment Agency meeting agendas.

Recreation, Arts, Museum, and Parks (RAMP) project priorities
          Council Policy Analyst Mabey explained each year, the City Council reviews the Administration's proposed Recreation,
Arts and Museum Projects (RAMP) funding requests and provides a prioritization of these to the RAMP Tax Advisory Board for
its consideration. City Administration is recommending six regular projects for RAMP funding in 2020. Three of these projects
are prospective CIP projects but have not yet been adopted and do not have an associated brief. Three requests align with the
Council's recommendations during the past two years, including: Arts, Culture and Events Programming, Dog Park on East Side
of Town, and Union Station programming. She distributed a list of the projects to the City Council and asked that each Council
member rank the projects in priority order; this infonnation will be used to formulate a resolution that can be adopted to
communicate Council priority rankings of Administration's proposed projects to the RAMP Board.

Council/Board Business
          Requestfor Proposals (RFP) for Auditing Firm Contract: Council Policy Analyst Symes reported the RFP for auditing
services will be published and posted this Sunday and the proposal period will close on February 7. Council staff is conducting
research regarding finns that should be targeted and invited to bid on the contract. He reviewed the process that has been
identified for reviewing the responses to the RFP, with action to award the contract scheduled for April 7. The first audit to be
perfonned by the selected finn would be for the current Fiscal Year (FY), which ends June 30, 2020. There was brief discussion
regarding the scope of work to be performed by the auditing finn chosen.
          Hof, Germany Visit: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith presented the design of a pin that will be fashioned to
commemorate the City's Sister-City relationship with Hof. The pin will be presented to Hofleaders during the Council's visit to
the city this summer. She then discussed updates to the itinerary for the visit.
          2020 Legislative Session: Council member White reported on her attendance at a recent Ogden Weber Chamber of
Commerce event during which there was discussion about legislation that will be considered in the upcoming 2020 Utah
Legislative Session. She has also been in contact with representatives of the Utah League of Cities and Towns (ULCT) about the
legislation they are closely following and how certain pieces oflegislation could impact Ogden City. This led to discussion among
the Council and staff regarding legislative priorities for the City, including infrastructure funding, statewide homeless policies, and
airport programming.

          Recognition Request: Council member Stephens reported he recently learned that Big D Construction Company is
celebrating its 50 th year in operation and he would like to recognize the corporation that originated in Ogden and now has a large
national presence. Council Executive Director Eller-Smith expressed her concern about recognizing one construction company
that the City has done work with in the past; this may send the wrong message to other construction companies with which the
City has worked. She stressed the need for consistency in ceremonial recognition items and indicated it may be best to send Big D
a letter communicating the City's recognition of their accomplishments. The Council discussed the appropriate form of
recognition and ultimately concluded to send a letter to Big D as suggested by Ms. Eller-Smith.


         The meeting adjourned at 4:59 p.m.
