Chairman Gaston at 1:30 p.m. on October 8, 2024, in the Washington County Commission Chambers
and via Zoom, at 111 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah. Commissioners in attendance were
Brad Gaston, Brian McMullin, Kevin Jones, Mark Owens, Keith Kelsch and Brandon Anderson. County
Staff in attendance were Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Victoria Hales, Deputy
Attorney; Todd Edwards, Public Works Director; Reid Walkenhorst, Planner; Emerson Rivera, Zoning

1.     MAJOR HOME OCCUPATION / CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT (Public Meeting –
       Administrative). Review and hold a public meeting for a Major Home Occupation and
       Conditional Use Permit for Red Desert Aviation in the Grassy Meadows Sky Ranch
       subdivision. The applicant is Derek Lewis.

       Mr. Messel provided a summary of the business and proposed use. The applicant has a hangar
       at his home, but is planning to only use his home for office space at this time.

       Ms. Hales added conditions 7 thru 11.

       Commissioner Owens added to condition 6, that fire inspections should only be required if
       repairs are made at the applicants home hangar.

          Mr. Lewis spoke in reference to his application, he indicated he has not approached the Grassy
          Meadows Sky Ranch HOA board regarding his application, but will do so when appropriate.

          Recommendation:

          Staff has reviewed the Major Home Occupation / Conditional Use application and
          recommends that the Planning Commission approve the Major Home Occupation based on
          the following findings and conditions:


     1.  An airplane maintenance repair is a major home occupation.
     2.  Major Home Occupation shall be secondary in nature to the primary residential use.
     3.  The home occupation shall maintain the residential character of the residential
         neighborhood.
     4. The business shall be registered with Utah State prior to receiving the County Business
         License.
     5. The home occupation shall not significantly impact traffic.
     6. The home occupation shall have a fire inspection by the local fire authority and as often
         as they deemed necessary. (but not unless he repairs planes onsite)
     7. No outside storage of parts, equipment, or planes being repaired.
     8. No employees except the owner/applicant.
     9. No tank storage of AV fuel.
     10. No noise from equipment or tools between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m.
     11. All work indoors.



          MOTION:       Commissioner Anderson made a motion to APPROVE the major home
          occupation / conditional use permit for Red Desert Aviation based on findings and
          conditions provided by Staff and added conditions from the public meeting.

          The motion was SECONDED by Commissioner Jones. The motion passed unanimously.

3.   SPECIAL USE PERMIT DISCUSSION (Public Meeting - Administrative). Review and
     discuss the potential of enacting a Special Use Permit to allow limited events on properly
     zoned agricultural and open space properties, i.e. dinner parties, receptions, weddings, or
     similar activities. The special use permit could also apply to agritourism activities and uses
     of a seasonal nature. The Special Use Permit would be more temporary (annual renewal)
     than a Conditional Use Permit and would not transfer with ownership of the property.

     Mr. Messel explained we are considering an option for residents in the county to apply for a
     Special Use Permit for specific activities or events.

     Mr. Walkenhorst explained some scenarios in which Short Term Rental operators wanted to
     host small dinner parties or weddings, but were unable to do so because we don’t allow
     events at Short Term Rentals.

     The commissioners provided input on how they would like to see a special use permit
     implemented and were also cautious about placing more regulation on existing business
     operators.

MOTION:     No Motion on this item.


4.   AGRITOURISM DISCUSSION (Public Meeting – Administrative): Review draft language
     and discuss ways to promote expanded use of agricultural properties as a source of revenue
     and promoting the agricultural heritage of Washington County.

     Mr. Messel shared examples of agricultural tourism opportunities the county would like to
     promote. This effort has educational value, economic value, recreational value, and helps
     preserve open space for agricultural purposes. The Community Development department is
     actively drafting language to support these types of activities in Washington County.
