 January 23, 2025 

 MINUTES 
 Wasatch Front Regional Council 
 January 23, 2025 
 WFRC Council meeting was held at WFRC Offices, 41 N Rio Grande Street, Salt Lake City and via Zoom, on 
 hursday, January 23, 2025. 
T
 1. W
 elcome & Consent Agenda [00:00:06]
 a. At 1:30pm, WFRC Council Chair, Mayor Dawn Ramsey, welcomed the Wasatch Front Regional Council
 members and guests. Chair Ramsey stated that prior to beginning with items on the agenda, the Council would
 have the opportunity to hear from Congressman Blake Moore [00:01:03] and Senator John Curtis [00:06:53] , who
 each provided an update on the happenings in Washington DC with the new administration.
 [00:18:05] Introductions were made around the table, and online. Members attending were asked to briefly
 introduce themselves. [00:26:25] ACTION : Mayor Ramsey brought attention to the first item on the agenda - the
 minutes of the October 24, 2024 meeting and Financial statements and check registers for September, October,
 and November 2024 and Budget/Expenditure Report to date. Mayor Tami Tran made a motion to approve all the
 documents as presented and Mayor Mark Shepherd seconded the motion. There was no discussion and the
 minutes and financial statements were approved unanimously.
 2. C
 hair Report [00:27:12]
 a. ACTION: Appointments to WFRC Council and Committees
 The WFRC Bylaws require that a Chair and Vice Chair be elected bi-annually at the first regular meeting in odd
 number years. At the October 2024 WFRC meeting, Council Chair Mayor Ramsey appointed a six-member
 Nominating Committee to propose a slate of officers for consideration. [00:28:07] Mayor Ramsey turned the time
 to the Nominating Committee Chair, Mayor Jeff Silvestrini, who stated that the Nominating Committee met in
 December 2024 and now presents recommendations for the following: South Jordan City Mayor Dawn Ramsey to
 remain as Council Chair and Commissioner Bob Stevenson to remain as Council Vice Chair for 2025-2026, as well
 as keeping the same leadership for the remaining committees.This will provide continuity and continue the strong
 and effective support structure that has been reflected for the past two years. [00:31:56] Mayor Ramsey and
 Commissioner Stevenson were willing to accept the Chair and Vice Chair roles for an additional term. [00:32:20]
 ACTION: A motion was made to accept the appointments of Council Chair and Vice Chair, Committee Chairs and
 Vice Chairs, appointed committee members, and endorse appointments made by the COGs and other entities, as
 presented, by Mayor Jeff Silvestrini and was seconded by Mayor Joy Petro. [00:32:40] Andrew Gruber, WFRC
 Executive Director, reminded the group that the Chair makes recommendations for some of the representatives to
 committees, and therefore recommends the 2025 committee memberships are accepted as noted in the meeting
 materials for the Regional Growth Committee (RGC), Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com), WFRC
 Budget Committee, Active Transportation Committee (ATC), and Joint Policy Advisory Committee (JPAC).
 Appointments to Council and the other Committees have also been made by the County Councils of Governments
 (COGs) and other appointing authorities that partner with WFRC. [00:34:40] Voting in the affirmative was
 unanimous. Updated WFRC Council and Committee membership lists were distributed at the meeting and will be
 posted on WFRC’s website. [00:36:35] Mayor Ramsey recognized the outgoing members of the Council: now
 Senator Cal Musselman, and Holladay Mayor Rob Dahle. Mayor Ramsey also gave a few moments to new Council
 members, Mayor Monica Zoltanski and Representative Ariel Defay, to introduce themselves.
 b. ACTION: State Legislative Session and Congressional preview [00:40:21]
 Mayor Ramsey introduced Miranda Jones Cox, WFRC Government Affairs Manager, who briefed the Council on
 several pertinent bills, focusing on key transportation, housing, and growth items, that are being tracked during this
 Legislative Session. [01:04:37] ACTION: Mayor Ramsey entertained a motion to endorse the recommended
 positions as discussed. Commissioner Jim Harvey made the motion and Commissioner Bob Stevenson seconded.
 The position approvals were accepted unanimously.
 3. P
 ublic Comment [01:05:25]
 Mayor Ramsey opened the meeting for public comment. There were no comments.

 4. Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com) [01:06:36] 
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Mayor Mark Shepherd, Trans Com Chair. Mayor Shepherd called on Ben 
 Wuthrich, WFRC Transportation Improvement Program Coordinator, to provide the Trans Com information. 
 a. Board Modifications to 2025-2030 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) 
 Mr. Wuthrich noted that since the last meeting of the Council, WFRC staff received requests to modify the current 
 2025-2030 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). The modification required action from Trans Com (since 
 WFRC was not meeting) and the Utah Transportation Commission, but did not require a new air quality conformity 
 analysis or a 30-day public comment period. The modifications are included in the meeting materials, and Mr. 
 Wuthrich stated that if there were any questions, he would be available to answer them. 
 b. ACTION: Board Modifications to 2025-2030 TIP [01:08:47] 
 Mr. Wuthrich continued, reporting that WFRC staff has again received several requests to modify the current 
 2025-2030 TIP and he briefly discussed each project on the list, also included in the meeting materials. [01:17:39] 
 ACTION: Mayor Mark Shepherd made a motion to approve the resolution to modify the 2025-2030 TIP as 
 requested. Mayor Monica Zoltanski seconded the motion and the voting was unanimous in the affirmative. 
 5. Regional Growth Committee (RGC) [01:18:38] 
 Mayor Ramsey requested that Mayor Bob Dandoy, RGC Chair, provide a report for the Regional Growth 
 Committee. 
 a. Station Area Plan Certification - South Jordan TRAX stations 
 Mayor Dandoy asked Meg Padjen, Community and Economic Development Director at WFRC, to provide an 
 overview of the Station Area Planning goals and requirements. Ms. Padjen then introduced the South Jordan 
 TRAX Station Area Plan, which includes four stations: the South Jordan portion of 5600 W Old Bingham Hwy, 
 South Jordan Parkway, South Jordan Central, and Daybreak Parkway. Mayor Ramsey and Steven Schaefermeyer, 
 South Jordan City Planning Director, discussed the significance for South Jordan City and Daybreak residents. 
 ACTION [01:38:43]: Mayor Brandon Stanger of Clinton City made a motion to certify the South Jordan TRAX 
 Station Area Plans. Commissioner Mike Newton seconded the motion. There was no discussion, and the motion 
 passed unanimously. 
 b. Wasatch Choice Vision status update [01:41:45] 
 Mayor Dandoy then turned the time to Jory Johner, Long Range Planning Director at WFRC, who reviewed the 
 progress of the Wasatch Choice Vision. Mr. Johner outlined the steps to develop the preferred scenario including 
 how the input from the Fall 2024 Wasatch Choice Workshops is being addressed. The preferred scenario will be 
 developed using an enhanced process designed to broadly explore transportation concepts. After this year and the 
 development of the preferred scenario, the projects within the scenario will then be “phased” or prioritized - using a 
 collaborative and rigorous process - prior to adoption of the Regional Transportation Plan by the WFRC Council, 
 anticipated in 2027. 
 6. Reports [01:59:02] 
 a. Wasatch Front Economic Development District (WFEDD) update 
 Mayor Ramsey called on Commissioner Gage Froerer, Chair of WFEDD, to provide an update. Commissioner 
 Froerer reported that Spencer Eccles, Chair of Utah Rising , the newly launched, statewide, business-led plan for 
 Utah’s economy, gave a presentation on the focus of this project. Commissioner Froerer also stated that the 
 WFEDD group is continuing to work on implementing tasks included in the current Comprehensive Economic 
 Development Strategy (CEDS) . 
 b. Executive Director’s report [02:01:48] 
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director, for his report. Mr. Gruber briefly 
 mentioned that WFRC was selected for two of the three federal grant applications for which we applied. Mr. Gruber 
 also acknowledged the exemplary service of two WFRC employees - Jory Johner with 25 years of service and Ben 
 Wuthrich, for 30 years of service. 
 7. Video: Overview of the Utah Open and Public Meeting Act [02:06:00] 
 Mayor Ramsey explained that, as part of WFRC’s compliance and annual training requirements, a video on the 
 Utah Public and Open Meetings Act was sent in the initial meeting email, and thanked those who have already 
 completed the training and asked that everyone else please do so to fulfill the board’s training requirements. She 
 explained that the training video will also be sent in a follow-up email for those who have not yet completed it. 
 8. Other Business and Adjournment [02:07:26] 
 Mayor Ramsey asked if there were any other business items to bring forward. Hearing none, she stated that the 
 next Council meeting will be held on Thursday, March 27 and adjourned the meeting at 3:37pm.
