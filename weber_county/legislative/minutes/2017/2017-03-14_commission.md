E. PRESENTATIONS:

   1. PRESENTATION OF MOTHERS OF THE YEAR
      Commissioner Gibson said that after spending time with these women there is a feeling of greatness and
      he highlighted some of their notable attributes. Cindy Allen was recognized as the 2017 Weber County
      Mother of the Year, Annette Spendlove as North Ogden Mother of the Year and Deanna Porter as Ogden
      City Mother of the Year. Ms. Porter championed motherhood, Ms. Spendlove said that the greatest thing
      she has done is being a mother and Ms. Allen spoke of service projects that her family does, including
      pulling dyers woad. The mothers were each presented with the book, “The Art of Motherhood.”

   2. PRESENTATION OF SEAL OF SERVICE AWARD TO ALISON COREY
      Rick Grover, County Planning Director, stated that Ms. Corey, of County GIS, is amazing and is a team
      player who helps other departments. She reached out to the Planning Division and has done many great
      things resulting in saving staff time, tax dollars and developers money. Ms. Corey said that she is happy
      to work with GIS, an innovative group. The commissioners expressed thanks for her professionalism and
      positive attitude and presented her with a plaque, a Seal of Service pin, and $100 for her exemplary work.

   3. PRESENTATION OF 2016 STORM WATER PROJECT OF THE YEAR AWARD (FROM APWA) FOR THE
      EMERGENCY WATERSHED LITTLE WEBER CUTOFF CHANNEL PROJECT
      Jared Andersen, County Engineer, referred to the Little Weber Overflow Channel in western Weber
      County. For 120+ years when high flooding has occurred in the area, the banks have overflowed creating
      many issues. After the last flood (2011), this large project received $20+ million from the State to help
      with mitigation, and many entities and county staff worked on it. He recognized two important
      individuals on this project—Craig Bagley with Bowen Collins & Associates and Michael Nebeker with
      Whitaker Construction. This is the fourth award received for this channel. Commissioner Gibson gave
      credit to Mr. Andersen, Lance Peterson, with County Emergency Management, the commissioners, and a
      large list of partners. He spoke of the importance of this critical infrastructure to our economic vitality
      and the commissioners expressed appreciation to these teams and their tremendous work.

   4. SECOND AMENDMENT OF C&D LANDFILL MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT - MOULDING INVESTMENTS

       Randy Moulding and Scott Moulding were present. Kevin McLeod, with County Operations, presented
       the contract amendment with Moulding, which is willing to install two large truck scales at the Landfill as
       contemplated in the underlying management agreement. In consideration of Moulding’s investment in
       the scales, the county is willing to extend this agreement for an additional five years. Mr. McLeod stated
       that the scales will equalize costs for all customers. He addressed Chair Ebert’s question stating that after
       the five years the county will not be responsible for any additional payoff.
       Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the Second Amendment of the C&D Landfill Management
       Agreement; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
       Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye

   5. RESOLUTION CALLING FOR SPECIAL ELECTION REGARDING A PETITION TO CREATE A SERVICE
       AREA FOR PARKS & RECREATION SERVICES IN THE TAYLOR, WARREN & WEST WEBER AREAS
       OF UNINCORPORATED COUNTY – RESOLUTION 12-2017

       Commissioner Gibson stated that a few years ago three individuals approached him, as their elected
       representative, indicating that West Weber School was being torn down, that the park around the school
       would not be reconstructed, that there was no other park in their neighborhood and asking how they could
       have parks and recreation for the children in their community. They asked the Commission recently to
       provide a park for them and a public hearing was held on 11/29/2016. The Commission declined because
       this is not a service that the county generally provides. This group has had various meetings, etc., and is
       now pursuing creating their own park district. It has collected over 500 signatures.

       Brent Fowers, of West Weber, said that as a committee, they have been concerned about the lack of
       public parks in their area. They started a petition to find out the people’s wishes, and he said that most in
       their area were positive with moving forward to vote on this issue.
       Commissioner Gibson moved to adopt Resolution 12-2017 calling for a special election regarding a
       petition to create a service area for parks and recreation services in the Taylor, Warren, and West Weber
       areas of unincorporated Weber County; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
       Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye

   7. FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION PRACTICES

       Scott Parke, County Comptroller, stated that this ordinance delegates the authority from the Commission
       to the Clerk/Auditor to act as the financial officer to approve routine expenditures once the internal
       control processes have occurred. In moving to electronic payments, this process is more efficient. Any
       single expenditure over $1 million still has to go before the Commission.
       Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the first reading of an ordinance establishing financial
       administration practices; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
       Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye
