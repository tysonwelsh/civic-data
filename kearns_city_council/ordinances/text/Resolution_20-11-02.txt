                            KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP RESOLTUION

    RESOLUTION NO.: 20-11-02                                                  DATE: November 19, 2020

    A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL REPEALING
    AND REVISING PARAGRAPH 4 OF RESOLUTION NO. 20-06-01 TO
    AUTHORIZE THE USE OF CARES ACT FUNDS FOR CARES ACT-QUALIFIED
    EXPENSES INCURRED BY LOCAL DISTRICTS THAT PROVIDE SERVICES TO
    THE PUBLIC IN KEARNS BUT ARE NOT CONTRACTED SERVICE PROVIDERS

                                                 RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.; and

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
governing body for Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501; and

    WHEREAS, Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to UCA Title 17:
COUNTIES; and

        WHEREAS, the CARES Act is a law passed by the Federal Government that provided for
an appropriation of funds to the States and certain Counties for certain governmental emergency
costs and economic relief efforts related to the COVID-19 pandemic; and

       WHEREAS, in May 2020, the State of Utah and Salt Lake County received CARES Act
appropriations from the Federal Government; and

        WHEREAS, on June 25, 2020, the Council adopted Resolution #20-06-01, authorizing
Kearns to enter into an agreement with the County by which Kearns would receive $1,015,500.82
in CARES Act funding from the County to assist with Kearns’ efforts in responding to COVID-
19; and

       WHEREAS, Paragraph 4 of Resolution #20-06-01 specified the uses for which the Council
will expend its CARES Act allocation, excluding expenses incurred by local districts that serve
the public within Kearns but are not contracted service providers; and

       WHEREAS, Kearns’ agreement with the County originally required it to expend its
CARES Act allocation by October 1, 2020, but Kearns requested and received an extension from
the County that allows Kearns to make all qualifying purchases and process all reimbursements by
November 29, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, on September 2, 2020, following the Council’s adoption of Resolution #20-
06-01, the U.S. Department of the Treasury issued updated guidance1 on the CARES Act that

support the use of Kearns’ CARES Act allocation to assist local districts that serve Kearns but are
not contracted service providers; and

        WHEREAS, the Council desires to amend paragraph 4 of Resolution #20-06-01 to: (1)
specify that all CARES-Act qualified expenses must be incurred by November 29, 2020; and (2)
to allow Kearns’ to use its CARES Act funds to assist local districts that serve Kearns and have
incurred CARES Act-qualified expenses but are not contracted service providers for Kearns.

                                         RESOLUTION

   NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township that effective
immediately Paragraph 4 of Resolution #20-06-01 is repealed and amended to state as follows:

       1. To the extent possible, the Mayor and Council will expend Kearns’ CARES Act funds
          as follows, provided that all CARES-Act qualified purchases and reimbursements must
          be approved by the Council and finalized by 11:59 pm on November 29, 2020:

               a. Reimbursement of CARES Act-qualified expenses incurred by the Unified Fire
                  Authority and Unified Police Department as Kearns’s first-responder agencies;
                  and

               b. Reimbursement of CARES Act-qualified expenses incurred by the Greater Salt
                  Lake Municipal Services District and its Contracted Service Providers as
                  Kearns’s municipal-type services provider; and

               c. Purchase or reimbursement of documented CARES Act-qualified expenses
                  requested by local districts that provide services to the public within Kearns;
                  and

               d. Reimbursement of any other Act-qualified expenses that Kearns incurs directly
                  responding to the COVID-19 pandemic.
