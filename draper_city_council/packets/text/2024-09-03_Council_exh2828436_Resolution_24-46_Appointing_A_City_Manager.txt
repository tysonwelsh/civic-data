                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-46

               A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL
                      APPOINTING A CITY MANAGER

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has created the position of City Manager to
administer the day-to-day operations of the City and its services according to the policies
and programs established by the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, Mike Barker has been appointed as and is currently serving as the
Acting City Manager; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Draper City Municipal Code 2-3-080, the Mayor desires
to appoint Mike Barker as the City Manager effective September 3, 2024, to exercise the
powers, duties, responsibilities, and authority assigned to the City Manager under statute,
ordinance, and policy; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council has determined it is in the best interest of the
health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Draper to appoint Mike Barker as City
Manager based on his abilities, tenure with the City, prior experience relating to the
duties of the office, and managerial capabilities.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council gives its advice and consent
to the Mayor’s selection of Mike Barker as City Manager, and Mike Barker is hereby
appointed Draper City Manager with all the powers, duties, responsibilities, and authority
specified by statute, ordinance, and policy.

        Section 2. Employment Agreement. A written employment contract has been
prepared and negotiated setting forth Mike Barker’s terms of employment. That contract
is hereby approved.

      Section 3. Status as Acting City Manager. Mike Barker’s status as Acting City
Manager is deemed terminated immediately upon the effective date of this Resolution.

       Section 4. Concurrent Duties. Mike Barker shall continue to exercise the
power, duties, responsibilities, and authority of his position as Draper City Attorney until
such time as a successor is appointed.

        Section 5. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution
shall be severable.
       Section 6. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 3rd DAY OF SEPTEMBER, 2024.
