                                 RESOLUTION 25-01

             A RESOLUTION APPOINTING MELISSA DAY AS A MEMBER OF
             THE DRAPER CITY HISTORIC PRESERVATION COMMISSION



        WHEREAS, the Draper City Council established the Draper City Historic
Preservation Commission for the purpose of preserving the historical heritage of the
city; and

      WHEREAS, the Council recognizes the importance of preserving the history of
the community for future residents; and

      WHEREAS, Bryn Heather Johnson began her term on the City Council in
January 2024 and her seat on the Historic Preservation Commission was not filled;
and

      WHEREAS, Melissa Day volunteered to serve as a member of the Historic
Preservation Commission and complete Bryn Heather Johnson’s term.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Appointment. Melissa Day is hereby appointed to serve as a
member of the Draper City Historic Preservation Commission according to the laws,
ordinances, and regulations governing the Commission and its members for the term
specified herein.

      Melissa Day                January 7, 2024, to January 31, 2027

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution
is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 7TH DAY OF JANUARY 2025.
