MEMO

Re: Public Hearing: Lone Peak Hospital Deviation Request (Administrative Action)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of Deviations from building and site design
standards for a future medical office building to be built on the approximately 35.87 acre
Lone Peak Hospital campus located at 11925 S. State Street. The property is currently
zoned CR. The applicant is specifically requesting deviations from facade articulation,
parapet and roofline variations, and the minimum percentage of primary materials, and
placement of the main building entrance.

Deviation from Facade Articulation

Findings for denial:
  1. A need for the deviation has not been established.
  2. The requested deviation is not consistent with the purpose and intent of the
     development standards of the applicable zoning district or land use category.
  3. A minimum of three (3) criteria listed in DCMC 9-22-030(B) have not been satisfied.

Deviation from Percentage of Primary Materials

Findings for approval:
  1. The requested deviation is consistent with the purpose and intent of the
     development standards of the applicable zoning district or land use category.
  2. A minimum of three (3) criteria listed in DCMC 9-22-030(B) have been satisfied.

Findings for denial:
  1. The requested deviation is not consistent with the purpose and intent of the
     development standards of the applicable zoning district or land use category.
  2. A minimum of three (3) criteria listed in DCMC 9-22-030(B) have not been satisfied.
