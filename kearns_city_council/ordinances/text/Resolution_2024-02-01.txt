 A RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING COUNCILMEMBER CHRYSTAL BUTTERFIELD
 AS THE REPRESENTATIVE APPOINTEE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP TO
    THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY AND THE
                      UNIFIED FIRE SERVICE AREA

     WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township is a member entity of the Unified Fire Authority (the
“UFA”) and the Unified Fire Service Area (“UFSA”); and

      WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) may appoint a
Councilmember to the Board of Directors of the UFA and UFSA; and

       WHEREAS, the Council desires to reappoint Chrystal Butterfield as Kearns’
representative to the UFA Board and UFSA Board.

    NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE KEARNS
METRO TOWNSHIP EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

      1. The Council reappoints Councilmember Chrystal Butterfield as the Kearns Metro
Township’s representative on the UFA Board and UFSA Board.

         2. Mayor Bush is authorized to provide UFA and UFSA with this resolution along with a copy
of a letter announcing Councilmember Butterfield’s reappointment and notifying UFA and UFSA of
Councilmember Butterfield’s contact information.
