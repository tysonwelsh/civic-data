WORK SESSION

1. WORK SESSION REGARDING THE 2022 UTAH COUNTY BUDGET FOR THE GENERAL

FUND AND OTHER BUDGETARY FUNDS OF UTAH COUNTY, UTAH
-Alice Black, Clerk/Auditor

(Timestamp: 1:23)

COMMISSIONER LEE HANDED OUT HIS BREAKDOWN TO ALL COMMISSIONERS
STATED THAT TOTAL EXPENDITURES AS $107,982,650.

COMMISSIONERS, RUDY LIVINGSTON (BUDGET MANAGER), JOSH DANIELS
(COUNTY CLERK/AUDITOR), DAVE SHAWCROFT (COUNTY DEPUTY ATTORNEY)
DISCUSSED AREAS THAT COMMISSIONERS WERE DIFFERENT IN AMOUNTS SUCH
AS INFLATIONARY FUND,

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH STATED THAT THE RECORDER’S OFFICE IS ASKING
FOR A YEAR’S WORTH OF EMERGENCY FUNDS SO THEY WON’T LOSE PERSONNEL
LIKE BACK IN 2008 SO ASKING FOR ROUGHLY $2 MILLION. JOSH DANIELS AND
COMMISSIONER LEE TALKED ABOUT RESTRICTED FUND BALANCE AVAILABILITY
OR APPROPRIATE FUNDS AS SUGGESTIONS.

ALSO DISCUSSED AREAS SUCH AS FUEL COSTS, RECORDER FEES AND
PERCENTAGES TO OTHER DEPARTMENTS, INTERNAL SERVICE FUNDS, HEALTH
DEPARTMENT FUND BALANCE AND INCREASE REQUEST, PROPERTY TAX, SALES
TAX, STAFFING INCREASES FOR SHERIFF'S OFFICE AND MARRIAGE
LICENSE/PASSPORTS AND JUSTICE CLERK AND CORRECTIONS. DISCUSSED
HEALTH DEPARTMENT STAFF INCREASES THAT ARE GRANT-FUNDED, CJC,
ASSESSOR'S OFFICE, I.T. STAFF INCREASES.

COMMISSIONER LEE STRESSED CONCERN OF MENTAL HEALTH ASSISTANCE.
WOULD PREFER TO NOT DO IT IN THE JAIL BUT WE DON’T HAVE A FACILITY
CURRENTLY. WOULD LIKE TO HAVE CONVERSATION WITH COMMISSION, SHERIFF
AND BEHAVIORAL HEALTH AND FIND SOMETHING INNOVATIVE AND NEW
BECAUSE THE OLD PATTERN IS NOT WORKING. COMMISSIONER GARDNER
AGREED AND DISCUSSED THE ASSISTANCE AVAILABLE THROUGH WASATCH
BEHAVIORAL HEALTH AND INCREASING ASSISTANCE.

GROUP CONTINUED DISCUSSION ON DIFFERENCES BETWEEN COMMISSIONER’S
BUDGETS INCLUDING MARRIAGE LICENSE STAFF INCREASE STATING THAT SO
FAR IN 2021 - THERE HAS BEEN A 40% INCREASE IN MARRIAGE LICENSES PLUS
THEY NOW CONDUCT MARRIAGES, SHERIFF'S OFFICE EQUIPMENT REQUESTS,
ELECTIONS STAFFING INCLUDING SECURITY, FUNDINGS SUCH AS FEDERAL,
STATE, COVID AND GRANT, WILDLAND FIRE FUNDING - SOME RECEIVED
THROUGH BILLING - 3 DIVISIONS, WILDFIRE EQUIPMENT, ETC.
