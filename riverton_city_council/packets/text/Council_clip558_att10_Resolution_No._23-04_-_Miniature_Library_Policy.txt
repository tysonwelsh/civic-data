                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 23-04

 A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL ADOPTING POLICIES FOR
     MANAGEMENT OF ITS DONATION “LIBRARY” ON CITY PROPERTY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, recently received a donation from the Utah Stem
Action Center, which provides a miniature “library box” where educational materials provided
by the Utah Stem Action Center may be dispensed; and

       WHEREAS, said miniature “library box” resembles and functions in similar fashion to
other boxes appearing in neighborhoods, commonly known as “Little Free Libraries” or “Take-a-
Book-Leave-a-Book” boxes; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that, owing to the
resemblance between the miniature library box provided by the Utah Stem Action Center and
other neighborhood library boxes, the public is treating the miniature library box as a repository
for donated books and as a place to retrieve books or materials; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Council finds and determines that use of the box in this
fashion calls for a uniform set of policies which will enable City staff to manage the materials
placed in the miniature library box in a nondiscriminatory manner, while reserving sufficient
space for the Utah Stem Action Center, the box donor, to deposit valuable educational materials;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

Section 1.     Riverton City Council hereby approves the Riverton City Miniature Library
               Policy, attached hereto as Exhibit A

Section 2.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 17th day of
January 2023 by the following vote:
