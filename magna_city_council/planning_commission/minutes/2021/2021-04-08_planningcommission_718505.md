OAM2021-000270 – The Magna Planning Commission will consider a recommendation to the Magna
Council regarding amendments to Chapter 15.14 of the Magna Municipal Code governing special
development standards in the Magna Overpressure Area. The amendments intend to clarify the boundaries
of the area, outline conditions required for building and developing within the Overpressure Area and moves
this section of code to Chapter 16.02. Planner: Travis Hair (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Jim McNulty and Counsel Jay Springer
provided an analysis of the regarded amendments to Chapter 15.14.

Commissioner Richards said there are two maps showing, are we going by the circles or the parcels. Mr.
Springer said going by the parcels.

Commissioners and Counsel had a brief discussion regarding the east west facing lots oriented, side yards
facing south, limit streets and houses built, and existing roads, consolidating overpressure areas, and
notifying buyers regarding the overpressure zone.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Weight seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

No one from the public present to speak
