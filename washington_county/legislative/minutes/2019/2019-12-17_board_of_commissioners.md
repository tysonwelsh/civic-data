PUBLIC HEARING: TO ADOPT THE FINAL 2020 WASHINGTON COUNTY
BUDGET

Chairman Iverson opened the public hearing for comments.

Mr. Hafen explained that a preliminary budget was passed in November. Several changes
have occurred since that time including: adding benefits for Public Safety regarding Tier
II retirement; Worker’s Comp lowered by $36,346; Violence Against Women Grant moved
from Fund 10 to Fund 28; additions to the Dixie Center for Audio Visual upgrade for
$150,000; addition of $1,000,000 for plans and demolition costs for the new
Administration Building; additions to the Greater Zion Tourism Office for potential
marketing agreement with Dixie State University (DSU); feasibility study on expansion of
the Dixie Center, and potential payout to Skywest for Dallas Fort Worth agreement.

CONSIDERATION _OF ORDINANCE NO. 2019-1152-O, AN ORDINANCE
ADOPTING THE _2019__ WASHINGTON _ COUNTY __ AFFORDABLE
"ATTAINABLE" HOUSING PLAN AS THE MODERATE INCOME HOUSING
PLAN ELEMENT OF THE GENERAL PLAN

Mr. Messel explained that the purpose of the housing plan is to ensure that Washington
County provides a reasonable opportunity for a variety of housing, including housing for
low and moderate income households, to meet the needs of the population desiring to live
in Washington County. It is required for each city and community to moderate the income
housing plan element of the State’s general plan. He also clarified that the term “attainable
housing” should be used in place of the term “affordable housing.”

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2019-1152-
O, An Ordinance Adopting the 2019 Washington County Affordable
“Attainable” Housing Plan as the Moderate Income Housing Plan
Element of the General Plan. Motion seconded by Chairman Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and
Almquist voting aye.

Deputy County Attorney Eric Clarke explained that as part of the original Habitat
