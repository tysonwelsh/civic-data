PRESENTATION: OPEN AND PUBLIC MEETINGS TRAINING

Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf conducted an annual training for the Commission
regarding the open and public meeting requirements as well as other statutory obligations
of the County Commission.

Mr. Graf explained that the purpose of the Open and Public Meetings Act (OPMA) is for
the State, its agencies, and its political subdivisions to take actions openly and conduct their
deliberations openly. Meetings are considered the convening of a public body for the
purpose of discussing, receiving comments from the public, or acting upon a matter over
which the public body has jurisdiction or advisory power.

CONSIDERATION _OF ORDINANCE _NO._2021-1173-O, AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING TITLE 9, CHAPTER 2A SECTION 1 OF THE WASHINGTON
COUNTY CODE REGARDING THE ENDANGERED SPECIES IMPACT FEE

Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan explained that there was a
reduction in fees collected for impact fees in regards to construction. The former fee in
place was $250 per acre; however, a point two percent (0.2%) fee on building permits will
still be collected.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2021-1173-
O, An Ordinance Amending Title 9, Chapter 2A Section 1 of the
Washington County Code Regarding the Endangered Species Impact
Fee. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and Iverson voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Commissioner Iverson reported on the success of the Washington County Fair as it was
moved to April this year. The boxing, MMA, and derby events all sold out.

Hurricane Valley Fire Chief Tom Kuhlman explained that the district responded to a large
number of fires and medical calls in 2020. He reminded citizens to be cautious with burning
during the open burn season, and to be mindful with campfires where they are allowed.
