       At the request of the Chair, all present stood and recited the Pledge of Allegiance led by Boy Scout Troop 174. Each

         A moment of silence was observed.

        Acknowledgements: Council member Choberka acknowledged an upcoming naturalization ceremony to be held at the
Union Station; she has heard that up to 120 individuals will receive their citizenship during the ceremony and it is a great
opportunity for celebration.

Public Comments
           Angel Castillo addressed a few topics discussed during tonight’s work session meeting; she is supportive of historic
preservation throughout the community and asked that the Council support initiatives that will help to facilitate preservation. She
also addressed the topic of painting brick and suggested that the City look into allowing specific masonry paint that does not
damage the brick and does last for decades. She noted that if the Council chooses to proceed with adoption of an ordinance
prohibiting brick painting, it will be necessary to communicate with the realtors association in the area in order for them to
communicate the law to potential home buyers in the Ogden community. She also encouraged the Council to provide tools to the
City’s Code Enforcement Division, such as a geolocation application, to help them do their job in a more efficient and effective
manner.
           Heath Satow also referenced items from tonight’s work session; one Council member had asked about the use of earned
interest on the Marshall White Center (MWC) bond and the answer provided by Administration was that it would be added to the
construction budget for the project. He asked if that means that the City is increasing the amount to be paid to the contractor or if
it will allow for a broader project scope.
           Chief Administrative Officer Brown indicated she will look into Mr. Satow’s question to ensure she provides an accurate
answer.
