PUBLIC HEARING

1. PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER THE DISPOSAL OF A SIGNIFICANT PARCEL OF
REAL PROPERTY CONSISTING OF APPROXIMATELY .9785 ACRES AND LOCATED AT
9826 N 4800 WEST NEAR THE INTERSECTION OF NORTH UTAH COUNTY BOULEVARD
AND HARVEY BOULEVARD, UTAH COUNTY, UTAH, TAX SERIAL NO. 14:003:0399.

(PUBLIC HEARING SET: APRIL 21, 2021 AND SCHEDULED FOR 3:00 P.M.)
-ROBERT J. MOORE, Attorney

ROB MOORE, UTAH COUNTY DEPUTY ATTORNEY - THIS IS A PUBLIC HEARING TO
CONSIDER THE DISPOSAL OF THE SIGNIFICANT PARCELS OF REAL PROPERTY AND
WHAT MAKES IT A SIGNIFICANT PARCEL OF REAL PROPERTY AND WHY THAT’S
NECESSARY IS UNDER BOTH OUR COUNTY CODE AND UTAH STATE STATUTE, IF A
PIECE OF PROPERTY IS CONSIDERED A SIGNIFICANT PARCEL OF REAL PROPERTY,
THEN YOU HAVE TO GO THROUGH A PUBLIC HEARING PROCESS AND THAT
SIGNIFICANT PARCEL OF REAL PROPERTY IS DEFINED UNDER OUR COUNTY CODE
AS EITHER 10 ACRES WHICH THIS IS NOT, OR THE VALUE OF MORE THAN
$500,000. JUST FOR INFORMATION, WE HAVE RECEIVED MULTIPLE OFFERS ON
THIS PROPERTY, ALL OF WHICH EXCEED $500,000 AND SO THAT’S WHY WE’RE
HERE TODAY TO HAVE A PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER DISPOSING OF THIS
PROPERTY.

TO BE ADVISED JUST QUICKLY, THIS PROPERTY WAS PURCHASED AS PART OF
THE NORTH COUNTY BOULEVARD EXPANSION WE DID BACK STARTING ABOUT
2007-2008, MAYBE EVEN MAYBE LATER. SORRY, I’M BEING CORRECTED BY
RICHARD NIELSON OUR PUBLIC WORKS DIRECTOR. THANK YOU, SO IT WAS
MORE RECENTLY. I KNOW WE DID THE EXPANSION. WE DID BUY SOME
PROPERTY FROM THIS PROPERTY OWNER AS WELL BUT THEN WE BOUGHT THE
ENTIRE PARCEL MORE RECENTLY.

THANK YOU BUT IT WAS STILL BOUGHT WITH A QUARTER CENT MONEY
(REFERRING TO RICHARD NIELSON)?

RICHARD NIELSON, PUBLIC WORKS DIRECTOR - YES, THIRD QUARTER.

ROB MOORE - SO THIS IS NOT GENERAL FUND MONEY SO WHEN WE DO SELL THIS
PARCEL, IT WOULD GO BACK INTO WHAT THE MONEY WE BOUGHT IT WITH AND
THEN THAT WILL BE USED FOR FUTURE PURCHASES.

PUBLIC COMMENTS
NO PUBLIC COMMENTS

COMMISSIONER GARDNER: MOTION TO CLOSE PUBLIC HEARING
COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH: SECOND

AYE: ALL IN FAVOR

PASSED: 2/0

PUBLIC HEARING CLOSED
