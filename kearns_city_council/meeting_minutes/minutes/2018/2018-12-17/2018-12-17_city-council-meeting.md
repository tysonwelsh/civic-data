              THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to hear public comment
on the 2019 recommended budget for the Kearns Metro Township

              Cathy Jensen, Chief Financial Officer, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (GSLMSD), reviewed the Kearns Metro Township 2019 budget.

                 Mayor Bush opened the public hearing.

Kelly open public hearing for the 2019 budget.

                 Paula Larsen, Kearns Community Council, stated she would like more money.

                 Mayor Bush closed the public hearing.

               Mayor Bush stated the budget reflects an allocation to the Kearns Community
Council for $58,000 under the contribution line item.

               Council Member Peterson asked if the Council would have a meeting to review
the budget with the Kearns Community Council.

            Mayor Bush stated the Council has reviewed the expenses with the Kearns
Community Council.

                 Ms. Larsen reviewed the Kearns Community Council budget.

                 Council Member Snow stated she would like to leave some money in
contributions.

               Mayor Bush suggested allocating $51,500 to the Kearns Community Council and
leaving the remainder of the $58,000 in the contribution line item.

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting a fee schedule for fees
and other assessments for services provided by the Kearns Metro Township:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-12-01                                              Date: December 17, 2018

       A RESOLUTION AMENDING AND ADOPTING A FEE SCHEDULE FOR FEES
       AND OTHER ASSESSMENTS FOR SERVICES PROVIDED BY THE KEARNS
       METRO TOWNSHIP

                                         RECITALS

   A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code Annotated
      (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq. and as such has to power to assess and collect fees for
      services and to recover the reasonable cost of regulating certain activity within the
      Township.

   B. The Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of
      Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Township provides services and regulates activity within the Township and in this
      regard has previously established or been the successor of Salt Lake County to previously
      established fees and assessments for these services and activities.

   D. The Council feels it is necessary to amend the previously adopted fee schedule to reflect
      increase costs and other changes in the Township activities.

                                       RESOLUTION

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township Council that the
attached Exhibit A hereto is hereby adopted as the Fee Schedule for the Kearns Metro Township
to be in effect from the 17th day of December 2018 until amended or repealed pursuant to law.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Kearns Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah, this 17th
day of December 2018.
