The meeting convened, and Jake Borenstein, U.S. Representative Curtis’s Deputy Chief of Staff, introduced himself and began the conversation.
Council Members and Deputy Chief Borenstein discussed High Valley Transit. (8:15 AM)
Council Member Robinson also raised the issue of the difficulty for rural communities in meeting the federal requirements to receive federal funding and asked for language that would permit waivers to such requirements. (8:28 AM)
Council Chair Stevens discussed law enforcement funding with Chief of Staff Borenstein. (8:18 AM)
Council Chair Stevens invited Council Member Robinson to discuss the background and needs of High Valley Transit. (8:11 AM)
Council Member Harte discussed investment in the biomass industry and the Weber River Restoration Fund. (8:20 AM)
The attendees discussed the issue of tourism’s impact on the public lands. (8:36 AM)
Other County priorities include child care, senior housing, and affordable housing. (8:41 AM)
Council Chair Stevens wrapped up the meeting. (8:53 AM)
