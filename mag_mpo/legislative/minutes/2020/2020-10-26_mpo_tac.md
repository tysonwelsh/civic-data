Provo City Corridor Preservation - Jim Price, MAG MPO Technical Advisory Committee October 26, 2020 Page 3 Provo City is requesting funds to purchase three properties. Both requests fall within the policies for the Corridor Preservation Fund. Funds available are approximately $6.5M 1. An existing residence at 741 E. 2200 N, located on 2230 N, #37 of the Approved Corridors Map. The property will be used for future planning after studies have been conducted to determine best use. Lot size .27 acres, appraised value: $560,000 Estimated acquisition costs: $10,000 Total Request: $570,000 Nestor Gallo moved that the Technical Advisory Committee recommend to the MPO Regional Planning Committee approval for the Provo City request for Corridor Preservation Funds of $570,000 for purchase of property located at 741 E 2200 N. Richard Nielsen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 2. An existing residence and adjacent vacant land both owned by the same entity at 836 N Geneva Road. These properties are located on 820 N, #38 on the Approved Corridors Map. The owner wishes to either sell both properties or develop them into new homes. The property will be used for future planning after studies have been conducted to determine best use. Eric Rasband clarified that the slides presented are identifying the area as the Provo 820 North Interchange, which is false because the environmental process is still underway. A specific location of the new interchange has not been determined and UDOT does not want to presuppose the outcome. The Provo North Interchange is evaluating multiple options between University Parkway and Provo Center Street for a new access point, including the no build alternative. Residence lot size: .29 acres, appraised value $230,000 Vacant lot size: 3.10 acres, appraised value $990,000 Estimated acquisition costs: $10,000 Total Request: $1,230,000 Brad Kenison moved that the Technical Advisory Committee recommend to the MPO Regional Planning Committee approval for the Provo City request for Corridor Preservation Funds of $1,230,000 for purchase of property located at 836 N Geneva Road for the 820 N Project. Marty Beaumont seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
