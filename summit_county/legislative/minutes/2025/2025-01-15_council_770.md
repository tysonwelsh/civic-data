Public Hearing 6:01 PM
1)
Public hearing and approval of a Special Exception to Ordinance 828 (2014 Tanger Outlets Development Agreement). The currently expired 2014 Tanger Outlets Development Agreement limited Restaurant uses to 2,000 sq. ft. Since the development agreement expired, the applicant is requesting a Special Exception to allow for a restaurant of up to 3,500 sq. ft. in the 2014 Expansion portion of Outlets Park City, Parcel FSE-1 (37.32 acres), located at 6699 N Landmark Dr, Kimball Junction, Summit County, UT; Applicant, Justin Keys of Hoggan Lee Hutchinson representing SRE Ontario LLC; Administrative Review. Project #24-191. Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner. (6:01 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Outlets Park City Special Exception (Restaurant Use Increase) Staff Report FINAL.pdf
Senior Planner Amir Caus introduced the request for the Special Exception. Council Members asked questions. Planner Caus responded.
Justin Keys of Hoggan Lee Hutchinson representing SRE Ontario LLC presented the requested special exception. Adam Greenbaum from SRE Ontario LLC assisted in presenting the request. Council Members asked questions. The presenters and Planner Caus responded. (6:05 PM)
Council Chair Hanson opened the public hearing. (6:10 PM)
No one appeared to comment.
Council Chair Hanson closed the public hearing.
Council asked further questions, and the presenters and Planner Caus responded. (6:11 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to approve a Special Exception to Ordinance 828 (2014 Tanger Outlets Development Agreement) to allow for a restaurant of up to 3,500 sq. ft. in the 2014 Expansion portion of Outlets Park City, Parcel FSE-1 (37.32 acres), located at 6699 N Landmark Dr, Kimball Junction, Summit County, UT in accordance with the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and find the request has met the requirements for a special exception as stated in the packet. (6:18 PM). Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
