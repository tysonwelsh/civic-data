3. Regular Agenda

3.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Uncommon Architecture’s request for review of
the Willow Park Church zone change, 10.92-acres located at approximately 1500 North 2600
West, changing the zoning from TH-5 to R-1-22
Chairman Jackson- opened the public hearing.

Lonnie Bates-Mr. Bates raised concerns about the proposed development plan, including the
construction of two churches and the placement of garbage facilities. He sought clarification on
whether his comments had been considered by the Commission and requested updates on the status
of the development plans. Mr. Bates emphasized the unique impact on his neighborhood and
expressed willingness to engage in the process to address his concerns.

Chairman Jackson- closed the public hearing.

Darren Mono, representing Uncommon Architects for the LDS Church, addressed the
commissioners. He clarified that only the first building is currently planned, with the second being
conceptual. They're not seeking site plan approval for the second building yet. There's flexibility
regarding the placement of trash facilities, pending coordination with the project manager. Darren
welcomed questions from the commission.

    Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to send a positive recommendation for Uncommon
    Architecture’s request for review of the Willow Park Church zone change, 10.92-acres located
    at approximately 1500 North 2600 West, changing the zoning from TH-5 (transitional holding)
    to R-1-22 (residential); including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in the staff
    report. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Eyre.

    Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.2.) Public hearing and consideration of Uncommon Architecture’s request for review of
the Willow Park Church concept, 10.92-acres located at approximately 1500 North 2600
West, requesting exceptions to the architectural and site orientation requirements of
Chapter 37, Nonresidential Design Standards.
Commissioners and staff discussed the construction scope of 2550 West and confirmed access
points on 1500 North and 2550 West. The density of churches in the area was noted, alongside
concerns about parking and seating capacity. Traffic and access issues were addressed, with
assurances provided. Phase two of the project was briefly mentioned without immediate concerns.

Chairman Jackson opened public hearing.

Lonnie Bates was representing the house on the corner between the two churches, expressed
concerns about the density of having two churches in close proximity. He noted that nowhere in
Lehi had a house with one church on each side, although he acknowledged the existence of parcels
with two churches. Mr. Bates found such arrangements odd from his perspective.

Chad Watkins (online comment) - criticized the oversized parking lot for the proposed LDS
churches, suggesting it could be better used for landscaping or to encourage walking accessibility.
He expressed concern that the large parking lot encourages driving and negatively impacts the
community and environment.

Lonnie Bates (Online Comment)- expressed concerns that the proposed plan for two LDS churches
does not align with Lehi city's general plan, citing issues with excessive parking, lighting, and
trash enclosures. He emphasized that the proposal would diminish the neighborhood's character,
value, and quality of life, and set a negative precedent for church and residential density in Lehi.

Chairman Jackson closed public hearing.

During the meeting, Mr. Mono from Uncommon Architects removed a second access point due to
proximity concerns, eliminating the need for an exception. Seating capacity was discussed,
requiring 225 parking stalls. Discussions covered glazing exceptions, dumpster locations, and
parking issues, prompting considerations for code standards and adjustments. Concerns about
neighboring properties led to discussions on landscaping buffers and road connectivity.

      Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Uncommon Architecture’s request for review
      of the Willow Park Church concept, 10.92-acres located at approximately 1500 North 2600
      West, requesting exceptions to the architectural and site orientation requirements of Chapter
      37, Nonresidential Design Standards in regards to the glazing on the front while denying the
      exception for the location that it still needs to adhere to the code and push the building to the
      corner; including the DRC comments that there needs to be a 12ft landscaping buffer for the
      neighboring properties and the parking facilities in general; including the findings listed in the
      staff report and recommendation to move the garbage location. Also, Commissioner Eyre
      recommended that the city council consider a requirement for both roads, 1400 North and 2550
      West, being completed. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Everett.

      Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
      Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
