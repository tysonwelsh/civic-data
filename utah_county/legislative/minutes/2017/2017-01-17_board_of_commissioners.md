7.      ADOPT (OR DENY) PROPOSED TEXT AMENDMENTS TO SECTIONS 5-11-B, 5-11-D AND 5-11-
        H OF THE UTAH COUNTY LAND USE ORDINANCE FLOODPLAIN OVERLAY ZONE
(Track A, 17:48)
Robert May of the Utah County Community Development Office clarified Bryce Armstrong is the County Zoning
Administrator. Mr. May talked about the County Engineer’s involvement in zoning, mentioning a pending
application for property in the floodplain. He noted the land mass has not changed. Bryce Armstrong came forward
to talk about floodplain overlays as designated by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), noting his
office is working with the state coordinator to update the entire county plan. The proposed text amendment will
directly serve the applicant in moving forward, but an overall comprehensive update is needed. Commissioner Ivie
advised the county maintain oversight and not rely on federal guidelines; Commissioner Graves questioned whether
the county should be more strict simply because it is allowed. Mr. Armstrong indicated the goal is balance.
Commissioner Lee requested Mr. Armstrong communicate with Sevier County regarding recent floodplain
insurance controversy, and provided wording suggestions to be implemented in the comprehensive update in the
upcoming months.

           Commissioner Graves made the motion to adopt the proposed text amendment
           to sections of the Utah County Land Use Ordinance Floodplain Overlay Zone as
           detailed in Regular Agenda Item No. 7. The motion was seconded by
           Commissioner Ivie and carried with the following vote:
                                                                         AYE: William C. Lee
