         The purpose of the joint work session was to discuss the proposed Ogden City Fiscal year (FY) 2024-2025 budget,
including discussion of the certified tax rate; discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board comments.

Council/Board Business
         Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reviewed the Council calendar for the remainder of June and for the month of
July. She also noted the City is currently performing its annual Fraud Risk Assessment and she asked that Council members
complete their annual fraud risk training to aid the City in completing their report.

Agenda Review
        Chair Richey and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council business
meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Proposed Ogden City Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Budget
          Certified Tax Rate: Management Services Director Stout reported the Mayor has proposed a property tax increase of
0.94 percent to generate approximately $185,000 to cover additional mental health services for first responders. Weber County
has provided the city’s certified tax rate—0.002127--and the average home value--$421,000. If a tax increase is contemplated, this
information is necessary to determine the percentage increase. She used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to compare the
proposed certified tax rate with historical rates dating back to 2016 and to illustrate the revenue associated with new growth. For a
copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Council Executive Director Eller-Smith noted that Council staff needs direction from the Council regarding the level of
tax increase they are comfortable with in order to proceed with accurate noticing of the possible action to be taken following the
August 6 Truth in Taxation hearing. The Council discussed the Mayor’s proposal and the anticipated use of the additional revenue
created by the tax increase and concluded they are comfortable proceeding with advertisement of the rate proposed by the Mayor.
Vice Chair White noted she would be comfortable considering a one-percent tax increase, rather than the proposed 0.94 percent
increase.
          General Budget Discussion: Council Deputy Director Symes used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to facilitate
general discussion of the Mayor’s proposed FY2024-2025 budget. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the
information packet for the meeting.
          Throughout the presentation, there was discussion among the Council and Council staff regarding staffing proposals for
the City; capital projects; facility programming; implementation of the downtown Ogden parking management system and plan;
grant writing efforts; use of fund balance and annual carryover amounts; fee amendments and the enterprise fund transfer; and
Council priorities such as asset preservation/restoration.
