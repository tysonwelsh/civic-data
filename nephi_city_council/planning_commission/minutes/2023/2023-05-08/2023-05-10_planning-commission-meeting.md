1.    Welcome to the Planning Commission Meeting
      a.   Prayer: offered by John Ford
           Ann Peterson welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2.    Deer Acres Plat C 6-lot Subdivision - Preliminary Plat - Jaren and Alyssa Lynn
      a.     Staff Presentation
      Shauna Talbot presented the Preliminary Plat and stated that the engineers have
      submitted updated plans since DRC. The biggest overall concern with the project has
      been having enough water pressure. She stated that Layne Allred, Nephi City Building
      Inspector, will be making sure the slopes are up to code.

      b.     Commission Discussion
      Ann Peterson stated that as long as the City is content that they’re going to hit the PSI, it
      sounds great.

      c.     Action Item - Motion
      Ann asked for a motion to accept the Preliminary Plat for the Deer Acres Plat C 6-lot
      Subdivision.
      Motion: Alan Hancock Second: Heather Roberston Outcome: Passed Unanimously

3.    Vantage Auto - Site Plan
      a.      Staff Presentation
      Shauna stated that the Vantage Auto site plan is for Lot 6 in the Nebo South Business
      Park. She said they are going to have a service center, an oil-water separator, and ample
      parking. Price Check Auto, currently located on North Main Street, will relocate to this
      site. Marty Barber, General Contractor, stated that there will be insulated panel, stone,
      and stucco on the exterior.

     b.       Commission Discussion
     Fran asked if there are any landscape requirements for this site. Shauna stated that there
     is, but it’s minimal. Mr. Barber stated that there will be some Xeriscaping because of the
     steep drop from Main Street. Heather asked if our current parking requirements would be
     affected by the used/new cars on the lot. It was clarified that the required stalls would be
     for customers.

     c.    Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to accept the site plan for Vantage Auto with the accompanying
     DRC notes.
     Motion: Cory Thomson Second: Karl Brough Outcome: Passed Unanimously


4.   Directrix Corp (Mad Dog Knives) - Site Plan
     a.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna mentioned that Directrix Corp currently conducts online sales, and would like to
     do some manufacturing. They are currently zoned Highway Commercial. The problem is
     that Highway Commercial does not currently allow for manufacturing. Shauna suggested
     making a change in the uses listed in the code.

     b.      Commission Discussion
     Skip Worwood asked why it couldn’t be classified as Light Industrial. Shauna said that is
     an option but since there are several other businesses conducting light manufacturing in
     the Highway Commercial zone, it would be nice to have this change made. Alan asked
     which change would take longer, to which Shauna replied that they would take the same
     amount of time. Skip suggested looking forward to ensure no future problems would arise
     because of this change. Fran asked what the Commission needed to decide tonight.
     Shauna replied that they are wanting their site plan approved. She also said that a zone
     change would not hold this up. Jake Sanchez, representing Mad Dog Knives, stated that
     there are no toxic fumes emitted as a result of their work. He explained a little about their
     business and that the only thing they send out for is chrome. Everything else is done in
     house. Cory asked about DRC notes mentioning a septic tank. Shauna answered that a
     septic tank is their only option for sewer. She also mentioned that a new 8” water line
     would be installed for stock watering, and there will be a water meter at this location.
     Alan suggested putting a hydrant in this area as well.

     c.    Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to accept the Site Plan for Directex Corp (Mad Dog Knives),
     occupancy contingent upon a zone change or an addition to the Highway Commercial
     zone.

     Motion: Cory Thomson Second: Alan Hancock Outcome: Passed Unanimously

5.   Title 10 Updates: Considering a new residential zone (R-4 zone) with a higher
     density equal to 16 units per net acres, and associated frontage, density, building
     height, setbacks, or other related additions to the zoning code for this zone.
     a.      Open Public Hearing
     This is a continuation of Title 10 Updates

     b.     Staff Presentation
     Shauna explained that we have already discussed this topic. We just need to close the
     hearing, as the Commission is not ready to consider a new R-4 zone.

     c.     Close Public Hearing
     Ann closed the hearing.

     d.    Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a vote to determine if the Commission was in favor of having an R-4 zone.
     All Commissioners voted against this proposition unanimously.
     Ann asked for a motion to deny the R-4 zone proposal at this time.
     Motion: Cory Thomson Second: Alan Hancock Outcome: Passed Unanimously

6.   Title 10 Updates: Updating required minimum lot size, frontage, density, building

     a.      Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the public hearing at 7:27pm.

7.   Title 10 Updates: Updating required minimum density, lot size and frontage in the
     R-3 residential zone when multi-family dwellings are built vertically.
     a.      Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the public hearing at 7:52pm.

     b.     Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented several plans that she had Kyle Marchant, Nephi City Public Works
     Director, draw up showing excess land that would result from building vertically. She
     showed the Commissioners various configurations and the land discount that would be
     applied in each situation. There was much discussion, several questions,
     advantages/disadvantages pointed out, and many concerns about this concept. Transcript
     and recording are available with full discussion.

     c.     Close Public Hearing
     Public Hearing was continued.

     d.     Action Item - Motion
     No action item needed at this time. A decision was made to hold a work session
     regarding this topic. This will be held on 5-17-23 at 6:00pm.

8.   Title 10 Updates: Considering an ordinance regulating IADUs (Internal Accessory
     Dwelling Units) within Nephi.
     a.      Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the Public Hearing at 8:32pm.

     b.     Staff Presentation
     Shauna pointed out that the Commission has already been through this once, and that it
     regards having an IADU as well as a short term rental in the same dwelling (ie Airbnb,
     VRBO, etc). She stated that, after consulting with staff, the decision was made to remove
     the short term rental terms from the code at this time.

     c.     Close Public Hearing
     Ann closed the public hearing at 8:37pm.

     d.    Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to accept Title 10 IADU into the Nephi City Code.

      Motion: Alan Hancock Second: Heather Robertson Outcome: Passed Unanimously

9.    Title 10 Updates: Considering an adjustment to the R1, R1-8, R2, R2-8, and R3
      zones, changing their uses and associated frontage, density, building height,
      setbacks, and other related additions to replace the previous zero-lot-line overlay
      zone specifically designed for twin homes or duplexes (dual-family) use.
      a.      Open Public Hearing
      Ann opened the Public Hearing at 8:38pm

      b.     Staff Presentation
      Shauna presented this adjustment as a way to create an area in town where dual family
      could exist as a buffer between single family and multi-family. She stated that taking out
      the overlay would simplify things. Any triplexes currently in this zone will be
      grandfathered in.

      c.     Close Public Hearing
      Ann closed the public hearing at 8:45pm.

      d.      Action Item - Motion
      Ann asked for a motion to consider an adjustment to the R1, R1-8, R2, R2-8, and R3
      zones, changing their uses and associated frontage, density, building height, setbacks, and
      other related additions to replace the previous zero-lot-line overlay zone specifically
      designed for twin homes or duplexes (dual-family) use.
      Motion: Heather Robertson Second: Cory Thomson Outcome: Passed Unanimously
