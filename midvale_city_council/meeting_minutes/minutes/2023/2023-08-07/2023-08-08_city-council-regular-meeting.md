       A.      REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION NO. 2023-R-33 ADOPTING
               THE INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN SALT
               LAKE COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT AND MIDVALE CITY FOR
              FUNDING THE CREATION OF A MIOVALE CITY COALITION AND THE
              HIRING OF A COALITION COORDINATOR
Nate Rockwood said the Salt Lake County Health Department has offered Midvale City
funding to create the Midvale Community Coalition (Coalition) and to hire a coalition
coordinator. The coalition will help build community capacity to address youth incidences
of violence through an integrated community, public health, and public safety approach.
Midvale and Salt Lake County have collaborated in the development of a proposed
lnterlocal Cooperation Agreement (lLA) that outlines the obligations of the parties.

ln the proposed lLA, Salt Lake County will provide a variety of services and resources,
including:

. Support for the training, reportrng, data collection, and technical needs of the coalition

. Annual funding for the coalition coordinator and coalition operations over five years. ln
years 1-3, the County will provide Midvale with $1 35,000 annually. ln years 4 and 5, the
funding will decline, with funding in year 5 being $33,750.

In the proposed lLA, in exchange for the resources provided by Salt Lake County,
Midvale, acting through the Coalition, will provide a variety of grant administration and
community service activities, including:

. Hiring a coalition coordinator that will work with the Coalition to fulfill its goals and
objectives. The coalition coordinator will be an employee of Midvale City.
