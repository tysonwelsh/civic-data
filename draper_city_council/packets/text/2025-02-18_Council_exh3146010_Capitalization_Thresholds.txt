                                                     Capitalization Thresholds

                                   Current standards establish more robust asset standard
                                   including breaking assets into specific categories with specific
                                   thresholds defined for each category.
                    DRAPER CIT Y
FI N ANCE DEPAR T MENT




                                   Finance reviewed all Draper City assets and determined City
                                   assets fell into eight categories:

                                   •   Office Equipment                      •   Vehicles
                                   •   Furniture and Equipment               •   Infrastructure
                                   •   Machinery and Equipment               •   Buildings (Including improvements)
                                   •   Improvements (Other than buildings)   •   Water Tanks
                                                           Draper City
                                                    Capitalization Thresholds

                                   Further review determined a standard dollar threshold and
                                   useful life for each category which we would propose to adopt
                                   as Draper City Capitalization Thresholds.

                                         Asset Classification                  Capitalization   Useful Life
                                                                                 Threshold       (In Years)
                                         Office Equipment                      $      10,000        3-7
                                         Furniture and Equipment               $      10,000       5 - 15
                                         Machinery and Equipment               $      10,000       5 - 15
                                         Improvements (Other than buildings)   $      10,000       7 - 40
                                         Vehicles                              $      20,000       5 - 15
                                         Infrastructure                        $      20,000       10 - 20
                                         Buildings (Including improvements)    $      20,000       7 - 40
                                         Water Tanks                           $      50,000        100
