PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on February 27, 2019 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2019-03 declaring approximately 88 acres of Mountain View Corridor, North 12600 South Street, East of the future alignment of Herriman Main Street, and South of Midas Creek Drainage in Herriman, Salt Lake County, as surplus; Establish that consideration for the surplus property shall be based on public policy factors identified. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
