BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.49
acres located on the south side of Hastingmeadow Lane, at approximately 12432 S.
Hastingmeadow Lane (Exhibit C & D). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is
requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to combine a 0.03 acre parcel to the
south,1777 E. Deer Canyon Lane(Exhibit F), with the Lot 9 of the Hastings Meadow
Subdivision.

Criteria For Approval.

Subdivision Plat Amendment
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing
        an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds
        that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
        way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the request based on the
findings listed below and the criteria for approval/denial, as listed within the staff report.
The Planning Commission should also review the request and hear from the applicant prior
to making a decision.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

    1. That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
       Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
       and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting. That all
       requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the development
       of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
    2. Section 17-9-070 of the Draper City Code specifies that the approval of a Plat
       Amendment expires one year from the date of approval if the plat has not been
       recorded or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not
       been commenced. The expiration of the approval. A maximum of one extension for
       a period of up to six months can be requested in writing to the Community
       Development Department prior to the date of expiration.
    3. Correct Engineering redlines before printing the final Mylar.

The findings for approval as are follows:

    1. There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
       way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
