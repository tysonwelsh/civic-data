                                        PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant is requesting to rezone property located at 2630 S. 7200 W., from A-20 (Agricultural zone) to M-2
(Manufacturing).
The subject property is approximately 4.85 acres of vacant and undeveloped land.


                           SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located at the northwest corner of the intersection of U201 and 7200 West.

The property to the North is zoned M-1 (Manufacturing -light industrial) and two parcels further North is zoned
M-2 (Manufacturing -heavy industrial).

The property across to the East is in West Valley City and is zoned “Manufacturing,” and a comparable zone to
Magna’s “M-2” zone (heavy industrial use).

The property to west, owned by Kennecott Utah Copper, is zoned A-20 (Agricultural) and is approximately 47
acres of wetlands.

Most of the properties further to the west have been rezoned to M-2 in the last few years.

                                      PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
The zoning request is compatible with the General Plan and the goals of the Magna Community for the area’s
growth to be notable in change, and to create a diversity of uses in this area off U201 Highway.

Staff recommends the Planning Commission review the list of Permitted and Conditional Uses currently allowed
under the Zoning Ordinance, to determine if other uses should be prohibited on this property. A copy of the uses
allowed by permitted use and conditional use are attached to this staff report. Staff will review this list of uses
during the Planning Commission meeting.

In order to set zoning conditions on this rezone, the following Ordinance is applicable:

19.90.060 Conditions to Zoning Map Amendment

    1. In order to provide more specific land use designations and land development suitability; to insure that
       proposed development is compatible with surrounding neighborhoods; and to provide notice to property
       owners of limitations and requirements for development of property, conditions may be attached to any
       zoning map amendment which limit or restrict the following:
            1. Uses;
            2. Dwelling unit density;
            3. Building square footage;
            4. Height of structures.
    2. A zoning map amendment attaching any conditions set forth in subsection A shall be designated ZC after
       the zoning classification on the zoning map and any such conditions shall be placed on record with the
       planning commission and recorded with the county recorder.
    3. In the event any zoning condition is declared invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, then the entire
       zoning map amendment shall be void. Any deletion in or change to zoning condition shall be considered
       an amendment to the zoning ordinance and shall be subject to the requirements of this chapter.
