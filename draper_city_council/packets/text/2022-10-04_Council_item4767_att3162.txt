                              RESOLUTION NO. 22-54

           A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING
   THE MISSIONSQUARE RETIREMENT GOVERNMENTAL MONEY PURCHASE PLAN
                  ADOPTION AMENDMENT TO AGREEMENT

WHEREAS, the City entered into an Agreement with MissionSquare, dated May 1992
for providing additional retirement savings for active employees through a
designated pension plan, 401a plan #109174, that ceased contributions from
Draper City participation March 2, 2012; and

WHEREAS, the Agreement addresses the reason for the termination of the plan;
and

WHEREAS, the City desires to terminate the Agreement in accordance with the
terms and conditions of the Adoption Agreement and Termination Letter, attached
hereto as Exhibit A; and

WHEREAS, approving the Termination of the Governmental Money Purchase Plan
Adoption Agreement will eliminate any recurring fees associated with the
maintenance of the plan for Draper City.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

Section 1. Amended Agreement. The Draper City Council approves terminating the
Draper City 401a Plan Adoption Agreement attached as Exhibit A.

Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
passage by the City Council.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, THIS
THE 4TH DAY OF OCTOBER, 2022.
