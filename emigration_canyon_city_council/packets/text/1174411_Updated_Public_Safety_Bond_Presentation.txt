Justice and Accountability Center
• Cost - effective alternative to jail for low - level offenders
• Balance accountability with resources/real solutions (behavioral     —mental health and substance
  use disorder, medical, job training, housing, etc.)
• Connected to criminal justice system
• Location: TBD (several county - owned parcels are available near the Adult Detention
  Center/jail)
Investment in Community                      and Public Safety
• Reduce crime
• Improve accountability
• Connect with resources/real solutions
• Break the cycle of repeat offenses
• County population has grown yet no jail beds have been added since 2001
• Cost effective/fiscally conservative and responsible
• Integral part of Salt Lake County’s Human Services, Homelessness, and Criminal Justice Reform
  Action Plan
• Bipartisan 8 - 1 Salt Lake County vote to move it to the ballot
• Supported by majority of community members surveyed
