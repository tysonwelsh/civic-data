2. 222 Business Park Final Subdivision Plat
      a. Staff Presentation

         used by the Johnsons to hold equipment, located on Sheep Lane at 618 West. The 7-

3.     Prime Honda Site Plan
       a. Staff Presentation
            Mrs. Talbot presented Lot 4 and 5 of the Nebo South Business Park Subdivision
          Amended. Due to recent changes through the State of Utah laws, a concurrent lot line
          adjustment between Lots 4 and 5 will need to occur in conjunction with this site plan
          application. The intended use is a Prime Honda dealership that will be relocating
          from its current 563 N Main location.
            Due to a lack of adjustments taking place on the exterior of the lot lines, the city by
          law is only required to have a survey conducted of both lots, new legal descriptions to
          be created, and two (2) new Warranty Deeds exchanging ownership be recorded in
          addition to city approval with old and new lot legal descriptions to proceed. There
          will be no crossover ownership easement with lot 3, but due to Prime Honda’s use of
          the parcels for a primary business building and parking, they will need the adjustment
          to assist with circulation and traffic control while meeting the city requirements of
          this project.
            Parking 2 ADA, 30 Normal Parking.
            The elevation of the building will be 28 ½’.

4. Public Hearing: Rezone Application Marty Barber Directrix Parcel #: XA00-2588-22
   located at approximately 825 W 2300 South (Sheep Lane) from ID3 (Industrial – Light
   3) to HC1 (highway Commercial 1).
       a. Open Public Hearing
                 TIME: 7:18 PM
          Mrs. Shauna Talbot presents the Staff Presentation.
          Mr. Marty Barber began that it was not his desire to own this particular property, but
          things had happened that had made it the case. Mr. Barber clarified that Vantage Auto
          decided that they had a need for additional space and will use that space for detailing
          primarily and not as a mechanical shop with 6-7 staff members.


       b. Staff Presentation
           Mrs. Shauna Talbot explained that the parcel was originally annexed as part of the
          Biglow Annexation and was zoned Highway Commercial 1 (HC-1) at
          annexation. The lot was subsequently rezoned to the ID-3 (Light Industrial) zone in

          June of 2023 allowing for the light industrial use needed by Mad Dog Knives. Since
          that time Mad Dog Knives has sold the property to Marty Barber (Barco
          Construction) who is assuming the building permit of the property now under
          construction, due to Mad Dog Knives going bankrupt.
            Mr. Barber is requesting that it be zoned back to Highway Commercial 1 (HC-1) as
          there is an interested party in buying it when it is finished for use as an auto-
          repair/maintenance shop. Nephi City has no objection to this parcel being re-rezoned
          back to its original zoning at annexation. The whole purpose of the city at the time of
          land sale and annexation was to be able to encourage sales tax, and job creation, and
          this zone would still accomplish that goal and will fit the overall zoning of adjacent
          properties that are also HC-1 zoning.

       b. Staff Presentation
           Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that in continuation of the last two public open

                  permission).

                  shelter (with landowner permission)
