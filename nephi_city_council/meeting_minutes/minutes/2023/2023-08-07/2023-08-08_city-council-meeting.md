                    NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                          Tuesday, August 8, 2023
                                 7:00 p.m.
                                21 E. 100 N.
                              Nephi, UT 84648

1. The Council discussed the cemetery issues with ruts being made from the backhoe
   and vault truck. Mr. Atkinson explained to the Council that cemetery crews are
   working on repairing any ruts in the cemetery. The council directed staff to look into
   purchasing a mini excavator and to build a new bathroom at the Vine Bluff Cemetery.

2. Mr. James Welburn addressed the council about his ongoing concerns of the
   flooding and lack of proper storm water drainage on his street. He presented
   pictures of his street after the latest severe rainstorm that had hit the City this past
   week. Mr.Chris O’Neil who lives along the same street echoed Mr. Welburn’s
   concerns. Tracy Welburn stated that there is a section of sidewalk that has all of the
   dirt under the sidewalk washed away.

3.   Mr. Atkinson reviewed some estimates for several public works projects.

4.   The council discussed the inland port idea. There were many questions about
     How the Inland Port Authority works. Mr. Atkinson said that he was going to look
into it more by talking with an economic development representative from Cedar City.
Councilor Parady said he would like to be part of the meeting with the man from Cedar
City. The Council was not interested in pursuing the inland port with the information they
have.

5. City Administrator Updates;
   ● Mr. Atkinson asked the council if they were interested in purchasing a portable
       speed sign that collects data.. Councilor Parady said he would prefer to set up
       more stop and yield signs. The council directed staff to purchase the data
       collection units and to act quickly on installing the additional signs
   ● Mr. Atkinson presented an idea of creating a document that would be shared with
       department heads and the Mayor and Council. The document would include
       items that the council wants follow up on and different project updates. Mr.
       Atkinson would add a link on every agenda just like the Financial Reports link
       that the Council could look at any time to see the progress on items that are
       concerning to the Council.
   ● The council asked Mr. Atkinson to follow up to the resident in Chapel View about
       the schedule of repairs to their road.
   ● City Attorney Kasey Wright reported to the Council about a basketball shooting
       camp that is going to be held at the Hive.
   ● Mayor Seely said that a Super State Wrestling event has been scheduled for the
       Hive in February.
           ●   Callaway asked about the annual ULCT conference,
           ●   Memmott asked if the staff had had any communication with Jeff Parkinson since
               he attended the work session on July 18, 2023. Mr. Wright reported that the
               development is complete and that the City hasn't heard anymore about the
               reimbursement agreement..
           ●   Mayor Seely reported that he had received a letter from Central Utah Water
               Conservancy District about a program they are promoting to incentivise

           ●   Mr Atkinson said that he has sent out the Arts Grant applications that are due by
               August 18, 2023.
           ●   Mr. Atkinson reported        about the progress of hiring a new lineworker.

       6. Councilor Parady moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character
and competency of an individual and acquisition of real property. Councilor Worwood seconded
the motion The motion passed unanimously.

      7. The work session meeting adjourned at 8:12 p.m.

       8. Mayor Seely called a regular meeting to order.

      9. Memmott moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion.
The motion passed unanimously.
