                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 26-04

        A RESOLUTION APPOINTING A CITY REPRESENTATIVE TO THE
                      JORDAN RIVER COMMISSION

       WHEREAS, Riverton City adopted Resolution No. 14-68 on November 11, 2014 which
approved the execution of an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with the Jordan River
Commission; and

     WHEREAS, the City is entitled to appoint a representative to serve on the Jordan River
Commission; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Councilmember
Alexander Johnson is willing to serve as the City’s representative on the Jordan River
Commission.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointment of
          Councilmember Alexander Johnson to serve as the City’s representative on the
          Jordan River Commission until a successor is appointed.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 20th day of
January 2026 by the following vote:
