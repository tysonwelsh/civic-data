Consideration of certifying the November 4, 2025, Municipal General
Election Results
          A memo from the City Recorder came before the Council to consider certifying the November 4, 2025, Municipal
General Election results. The memo stated Section 20A, Chapter 4 of the Utah State Code sets forth the election review process,
designates the legislative body – the City Council – and the Mayor as the Board of Canvassers and outlines the Board’s duties for
municipal elections. The City Recorder acts as the election official. The Board of Canvassers is responsible for certifying the
official election results of municipal elections. Canvassing of returns for a General Election must occur no sooner than seven (7)
days after the election and no later than fourteen (14) days after the election. After reviewing the returns, the Board must certify
the results of the election and file a report that includes the following information:
               1. The total number of votes cast in the Board’s jurisdiction;
               2. The names of each candidate whose name appeared on the ballot;
               3. The title of each ballot proposition that appeared on the ballot;
               4. Each office that appears on the ballot;
             5.    From each voting precinct:
                        a. The number of votes for each candidate;
                        b. The number of votes for and against each ballot proposition;
              6. The total number of votes given in the Board’s jurisdiction to each candidate;
              7. The number of ballots that were rejected; and
              8. A statement certifying that the information contained in the report is accurate.
          City Recorder Hansen summarized the memo and used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide the following
General Election statistics:
              • 33,000 active voters in Ogden across 41 precincts
              • 11,502 ballots counted citywide (34.55% turnout)
              • 34,000 ballots sent (including replacements and those for walk-in voters)
              • 1,158 ballots returned as undeliverable
              • 50 provisional ballots cast (44 counted, 6 not counted)
              • 556 people voted in person county-wide

the Canvass Report to the Lieutenant Governor's office. For a copy of Ms. Hansen’s presentation in its entirety, see the
information packet for the meeting.
          Chair White inquired as to election audits that are conducted by Weber County. Ms. Hansen explained multiple audits
were conducted before and after the election to ensure accuracy; she described pre-election audits where ballots with known
outcomes were run through machines to verify counts, and post-election audits where the State selects specific ballots to verify
they were recorded correctly.
