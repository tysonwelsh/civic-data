                                     RIVERTON, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 22-62

  APPROVING RIVERTON CITY'S ENTRY INTO A MEMBER AGENCY WATER
 CONSERVATION FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH THE JORDAN VALLEY WATER
                    CONSERVANCY DISTRICT

        WHEREAS, Riverton City ("Riverton") and the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy
District ("District") are government entities; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton obtains culinary water and distributes the same to its residents,
and said culinary water is primarily supplied from the District, pursuant to a Water Purchase
Agreement and Petition, effective January 1, 2015; and

       WHEREAS, Under the terms of the Water Purchase Agreement, the City is entitled to
purchase a minimum allotment of culinary water from the District, but may not purchase an
amount which exceeds the minimum allotment by a margin in excess of 20% of the minimum
allotment; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council ("Council") finds and determines that to manage
increase in demand for culinary water due to population growth, Riverton and the District must
cooperate to implement projects which enable Riverton to conserve water as it distributes the
same to Riverton Property or to Riverton residents; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton has identified a project which will save a significant amount of
water which is currently being used to irrigate sod at the Riverton “Spirit Corner” located at the
northeast corner of 12600 South Redwood Road; and

        WHEREAS, upon completion of the project, Riverton will convert approximately 75%
of existing turf at the Spirit Corner to xeriscape and drought-resistant plants which will conserve
a substantial amount of water; and

        WHEREAS, the District has reviewed this project, supports the same, and has expressed
a willingness to partially fund the project; and

        WHEREAS, the Council met in regular session on October 4, 2022, to consider, among
other things, a resolution approving a Member Agency Water Conservation Funding Agreement
(“Agreement”) with the District regarding the sharing of costs to improve landscaping found on
the Spirit Corner to reduce demand for water at this location; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton and District are authorized to enter into agreements with each
other for cooperative action; and

        WHEREAS, the Agreement sets forth the purpose thereof, the extent of participation of
the parties, and the rights, duties, and responsibilities of the parties; and
        WHEREAS, the Council finds that it is in the best interests of the inhabitants of Riverton
to enter into the Agreement.


       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. That the Mayor is authorized to sign an Member Agency Water Conservation Funding
      Agreement with Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District, in substantially similar form
      to the copy attached to this Resolution, upon verification that said agreement is approved
      by the Riverton City Attorney.

   2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage and acceptance as
      provided herein.


   PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4th day of
