                                            PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant (on behalf of Kennecott Utah Copper LLC, the current owner) is requesting approval to rezone approximately
9.10 acres of property from the current A1- z/c zone to the R-1-4 zone. The purpose of the rezone is to allow for
development of the property similar to that of the neighboring Gabler Grove Planned Development project. The applicant
will be seeking separate approval to include this subject property into the overall development plan for Gabler Grove.



                              SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The subject property lies South of the Riter Irrigation Canal and North of the Gabler Grove Planned Development project.
An existing Single-Family neighborhood is located to the West. The property currently consists of Agricultural land spread
across portions of multiple parcels.
         Request: Rezone                                                                File #: 30887




                                    GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The subject property is located in a mapped area of stability with limited change in zoning classification anticipated. The
subject property is immediately adjacent to the previously approved Gabler Grove Planned Development project and part
of the same parcels over which that development has been approved. Gabler Grove was approved by a Development
Agreement with the Magna Metro Township Council and multiple land owners. The Development agreement included a
complete development plan or Small Area Plan as part of the rezoning proposal at the time the development agreement
was entered into between the various owners and the Magna Metro Township, which is considered in practice as an
amendment to the General Plan. The applicant has indicated that they wish to develop the subject property in conjunction
with the Gabler Grove Development. The applicant has not submitted complete plans or an application for an amendment
to the development plans (Small Area Plan) for the Gabler Grove project, or for the subject property.

                           ISSUES OF CONCERN/PROPOSED MITIGATION
The property to be rezoned is anticipated to later be subject to a development agreement with the Magna Metro Township
Council. The R-1-4 zone requires that single-family project developments be approved through the Conditional Use process.
The Magna Metro Township Planning Commission should have an opportunity to review and approve the conditional use.
This could occur as part of the review of a future development agreement for the property, or as a separate conditional use
application.

                                         NEIGHBORHOOD RESPONSE
No neighborhood response has been received regarding this proposal at the writing of this report.



                                     REVIEWING AGENCIES RESPONSE
Due to the timing of the application submittal, no outside agencies have been consulted regarding the rezone proposal. The
rezone could ultimately impact traffic above the levels originally contemplated in the adjacent Gabler Grove Planned
Development project. However, if the reductions in overall unit count occur as has been verbally indicated by Ivory
Development, a lesser traffic increase than previously anticipated may result.



                                          PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
The rezone, while significantly increasing potential density for the property, would allow it to be developed similarly to the
adjacent property within the Gabler Grove Planned Development.

Additional staff analysis of the traffic, economic, and other impacts of the rezone may occur as time permits and may be
provided at the Magna Metro Township Council meeting.



                                 PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Planning Staff recommends that the Magna Metro Township Planning Commission forward a recommendation to the
Magna Metro Township Planning Commission that the 9.10 acres of subject property be rezoned to R-1-4. This
recommendation would be based upon the Planning Commission finding the following:
    1. That the resulting development pattern would be more consistent with that already approved for the adjoining
        planned development to the South of the subject property and South of the Riter canal.
    2. That the increased density would allow for more residential dwelling units to be built at a lower cost.
    3. That lower cost units are desirable for providing more affordable housing in the Magna Metro Township.
