   Mayor       Silvestrini      said the City          had been having              discussions            with       Google      Fiber.      John Brems
   said Google          had requested           a license,        to install,     maintain,         operate,          and/or     control       a fiber       optic
   infrastructure         network       in the public          right-of-way           for the purpose              of offering          communications
   services,      including         broadband         internet      access service,           but excluding              multichannel             video
   prograrnrning          services      that would         be subject           to a video      services          franchise,        to residents          and
   businesses       in the City.        The license          would        grant Google          permission              to encroach           on and occupy
   the public      right-of-way          for the purpose             of constructing,           installing,            repairing,        maintaining,           and
   operating      the network          within        the City.      He said it would            be a theoretical                pipe to houses without
   the content.         He then went           over the provisions               of the agreement.                Google         would     be required              to
   get a city excavation              permit     every time they cut into Millcreek's                              roads.       Google        would       use its
   best efforts        to install     or relocate        its network         underground.            If after utilizing             such best efforts

   damage       that poses an imminent                  threat to public            health     or safety,          (ii) 72 hours          from     City
   notifying      Google       of damage          that affects         roads or highways,                  or (iii)     14 days from             City
   notifying      Google       of all other types of damage.                      Google       would         not have a build              out
   requirement.         The City        would        consult      with     Google       regarding          the boundaries               of the City's
   recognized          neighborhood            associations         and City's          goals of equity               and inclusion.          Google         shall
   not deny service           or access, or otherwise                 discriminate           on the availability,                rates, terms,          or
   conditions       of broadband           internet       services        provided         to residential          subscribers           on the basis of
   race, color,        creed, religion,         ancestry,         national       origin,     gender,        sexual       orientation,         disability,
   age, familial        status, marital          status, or status with              regard     to public             assistance.
