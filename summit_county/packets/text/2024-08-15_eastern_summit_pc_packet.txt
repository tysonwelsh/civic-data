RECOMMENDATION 
Staff recommends the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission (ESCPC) review the 
proposed language amending specific sections of the Eastern Summit County Development 
Code (Code), conduct a public hearing, and vote to forward a positive recommendation to the 
Summit County Council.  
 
BACKGROUND 
Staff is proposing amendments to the Code to provide consistency with State Code and Eastern 
Summit County Development Code processes, fix outdated references, and make other 
necessary minor amendments. Staff determined these amendments are primarily 
administrative in nature, intended to clarify and clean up certain items. These amendments do 
not affect density or other substantive development standards. 
 
The proposed amendments are attached as Exhibit A and summarized below.  
 
Changes to Chapter 1: 
1. Remove section 11-1-4 (Cluster Bonus/Agriculture Preservation Incentive 
Subdivision) 
2. Remove section 11-1-5 (Community Preservation Incentive) 
Changes to Chapter 3: 
1. Modify the Section Title from “CHART OF ALLOWED AND PERMITTED USES” to 
“USE TABLE” 
2. Correct a typo (“Permit5ted Uses”) 
3. Remove “Agricultural Employee Dwelling Unit” from the Use Table 
4. Remove “Agricultural employee facility for the purpose of providing shelter for 
more than 1 family” from the Use Table 
5. Modify the term “Dwelling Unit, One-Family” to be “Dwelling Unit, Single-
Family” in the Use Table 
6. Modify the term “Open space, recreational (motorized)” to be “Recreation, 
Commercial/Public (Motorized)” in the Use Table

Changes to Chapter 6:

2. Correct all references to “Dwelling Unit, One-Family” to “Dwelling Unit, Single 
Family” 
Changes to Chapter 7:

ANALYSIS 
Section 11-5-3 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code establishes a process for 
amendments to the text of the Code, it states that whenever an amendment to the Code is 
initiated, it must be reviewed by the Planning Commission who will deliver a recommendation 
to the County Council. The County Council, after holding a public hearing, can approve, approve 
with modifications, or deny the amendment. There is no criterion.

CHAPTER 1 RURAL AGRICULTURE PROTECTION PROGRAM 
… 
11-1-4: CLUSTER BONUS/AGRICULTURE PRESERVATION INCENTIVE SUBDIVISION: 
The cluster bonus/agriculture preservation incentive subdivision is hereby created to promote 
the retention of agricultural land and operations in Eastern Summit County. It is specifically 
intended to assist those actively engaged in farming and ranching and to increase 
opportunities for residents and local workers to live in Eastern Summit County. A density 
bonus shall be awarded to property owners within the agriculture protection (AP) zone 
district only if it results in the preservation of actively used agricultural lands. The cluster 
bonus/agriculture preservation subdivision shall be applicable for legally created lots/parcels 
in the AP zone district, which constitute the heaviest concentration of agricultural enterprises 
and is located within the primary county infrastructure and service area. Chapter 4 of this 
title defines the procedures related to the cluster bonus/agriculture preservation subdivision. 
(Ord. 708, 12-10-2008) 
 
11-1-5: COMMUNITY PRESERVATION INCENTIVE: 
The minor subdivision of property and cluster bonus minor subdivision provisions are hereby 
created with the intent of increasing opportunities for residents and local workers to afford a 
place to live in Eastern Summit County. The minor subdivision of property and the cluster 
bonus minor subdivision provisions shall be applicable only in the AP and HC zone districts, 
which constitute the areas where the county's primary infrastructure and services are 
located. These provisions shall apply to any legally created lot/parcel. Chapter 4 of this title 
defines the procedures related to the minor subdivision of property and cluster bonus minor 
subdivision provisions. (Ord. 708, 12-10-2008) 
 
11-1-64: PRESERVING AND PROMOTING BUSINESS ENTERPRISES 
   A.   Existing Enterprises: There are many viable rural business enterprises in Eastern Summit 
County that are being encroached upon by new residential development. These business 
operations remain viable; some need to expand. These operations require protection from 
the effects of new residential development, in much the same way as agricultural lands and 
operations. Business enterprises that existed on the effective date hereof, so long as they 
were lawfully established under previous zoning regulations, shall hereunder be considered a 
"permitted use" within the zone district in which they are located. 
   B.   Expansion Of Existing Enterprises: The expansion of lawfully established business 
enterprises shall require a "conditional use" approval, as described in section 11-4-7 of this 
title. The intent of the conditional use approval shall be to ensure compatibility with 
surrounding uses to the extent practical and reasonable. They shall be allowed to undertake 
appropriate expansion when they reasonably mitigate potential impacts on nearby residential 
land uses. Provisions for mitigation are described in chapter 2 of this title. (Ord. 708, 12-10-
2008) 
   C.   Promoting New Enterprises: New business enterprises, and jobs within the tax base that 
will result, are crucial to the future of Eastern Summit County. It is difficult to identify 
locations for such activities without a specific proposal to consider . Therefore, a procedure 
has been incorporated in this title that allows the County the flexibility to consider these uses

Background 
 
This application was reviewed by the Planning Commission on July 18, and August 1, 2024. At 
those meetings, the Commission took public comment and discussed the proposed project. The 
Commission stated that a Nursery/Greenhouse would be appropriate for the site but was 
concerned about the other uses. They directed the applicant to clarify the proposal and return 
on August 15, 2024, for further review.  
 
On August 7, 2024, the applicant submitted a revised site plan along with proposed conditions 
of approval for each of the uses (attached as exhibit A).   
 
The proposed hours of operation for all uses are in line with construction work related hours as 
per the Summit County Noise Ordinance 7 am to 9 pm.

Section 11-3-16 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code requires that following uses 
be reviewed as a Conditional Use Permit in the AG-80 Zone; 
 
• Rock quarries, gravel pits, and associated surface mining uses, including, but not limited 
to, filtering, sifting, and processing of soil

Criteria for Approval 
 
Rock quarries, gravel pits, and associated surface mining uses, including, but not limited to, 
filtering, sifting, and processing of soil – Conditional Use Permit Required 
 
The applicant is proposing to accept material from construction sites. The applicant’s goal is to 
construct a platform which would be used as  a tree farm and an orchard. The proposed area is 
approximately 800 feet by 700 feet, with an anticipated fill that ranges from 0 to 25 feet 
(depending on slope). To create a usable planting area, the fill dirt will need to be processed with 
the dirt and rocks being separated, which will create excess gravel and material. The applicant 
has stated that these excess materials will be processed and sold. The reason he needs the quarry 
use is that he is proposing to filter, sift and process soil for sale. Were he only proposing to bring 
the soil in for the nursery, he would not need this use.  
 
He is proposing that this use be discontinued once he has enough soil on site to operate his 
nursery. He has proposed a condition of approval that would terminate the use at the end of 
2027.   
 
Recycling facility, Class II – Conditional Use Permit Required 
 
The applicant proposes to accept compostable material from various locations throughout the 
area (restaurants, resorts, contractors, farmers etc.). The material would be mulched, and either 
sold or used in the nursery. The CUP is necessary because he is proposing to accept material from 
off-site sources and sell it.  Conditions of approval have been proposed to limit the recycling of 
materials to green waste; no glass, metal, asphalt etc. would be allowed.  
 
Nursery/greenhouse – Conditional Use Permit Required 
 
They are proposing a tree farm with approximately 8,600 trees, a permanent apple orchard and 
a community garden. For sale trees would vary in size and variety. The applicant states that trees 
would be sold wholesale and that the use would not be a place designed to sell to individual 
members of the public. The applicant states that the reason for the immense amounts of fill is to 
create a flat area where they can plant the trees and care for them. Once enough soil is brought 
in to accommodate the trees, then the soil component of the operation would cease.   
 
The applicant has stated that the operation would have 6-10 employees, five (5) tractors, two 
(2) skid-steers, two (2) excavators, and two (2) pickup trucks with trailers. Additional 
attachments for equipment include a street sweeper, water tank, grinder, and compost turner. 
 
To water the trees, the applicant states that there is an existing well on site with one acre foot of 
water and another 2 shares worth one acre foot each  that he is working to  have transferred to
