                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 22-20

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
WITH ENTRYPOINT NETWORKS TO COMPLETE PHASE 2 OF THE CITY OWNED
                    FIBER NETWORK PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council required that the project must come back for
approval before proceeding; and

      WHEREAS, EntryPoint has completed Phase 1, the network design; and

      WHEREAS, the project is ready to be bid; and

      WHEREAS, phase 2 for EntryPoint is to provide bidding assistance.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with EntryPoint
            Networks to complete Phase 2 of the City Owned Fiber Network.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
March 2022 by the following vote:
