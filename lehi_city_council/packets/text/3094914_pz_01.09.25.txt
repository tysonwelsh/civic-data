125   3.5) Consideration of CIR Engineering’s request for approval of the Moto United Site Plan
126   located at 450 South Millpond Drive.
127
128   Jacob Curtis presented the item. Moto United is an indoor powersports and marine vehicle
129   retailer. The company is asking for approval of a concrete exterior, which is not typically
130   allowed in the design district. They also want an exception to the number of trees typically
131   required. The plan that was submitted to the city has about 30 trees per acre, rather than the usual
132   40 trees per acre.
133
134   Andrew was present on behalf of the applicant. He explained that a portion of the property is
135   protected wetland, which limits the space on the lot available to plant the required number of
136   trees. He also mentioned that the concrete exterior is to maintain congruity with other Moto
137   United locations, which all have similar looks.
138
139   Commissioner Everett asked if the required trees per acre would be reached if the wetland wasn’t
140   taken into consideration. The planning department explained that the undeveloped area is about
141   2.6 acres of the 8.4 acre lot. Commissioners Eyre and Jones both expressed comfort with
142   approving, based on this information.
143

      Lehi City Planning Commission                     3                                   January 9, 2025
144   Commissioner Roberts asked staff for clarification on the number of trees required. Mike West
145   pointed out that Moto United put in additional parking lot islands that were otherwise not strictly
146   necessary to increase the number of trees on the lot.
147
148                   Motion: Commissioner Jones moved to approve the plan with the inclusion of
149                   both exceptions, finding that the proposed use at the proposed location will not be
150                   detrimental or injurious to property or improvements in the vicinity, it will not be

164   3.6) Public hearing and recommendation of Ivory Development’s request for preliminary
165   subdivision review of Holbrook Place Phase 12, a 14-lot residential subdivision located at
166   approximately 3600 Turpin Lane.
167
168   Jacob Curtis presented the item. All lots meet the frontage and size requirements. Three lots fall
169   within the Jordan River overlay. The planning division has asked for a 50 foot overlay so that no
170   permanent structures are placed too close to the river.
171
172   Commissioner Roberts asked about the grading of the river bank, and where the 50 foot zone
173   would start. Mike West clarified that the zone begins where the land drops off. Vegetation and
174   small structures (such as sheds) will be allowed in the zone.
175
176   The applicant, Brad Mackey, was present. The lots have been configured so that they have as
177   little slope as possible. Access will still be available for future developers.
178
179   Commissioner Eyre asked where the subdivision is in relation to the proposed Clubhouse Drive.
180   The planning division showed that they are adjacent to each other.
181
182   Commissioner Everett opened public comment.
183
184   Laura Hardman represented the Hardman family and Jordan properties. She expressed
185   appreciation for the Joran River Overlay Zone, and suggested that the Commission visit the
186   property on foot. She had concerns about building too close to the river, especially considering
187   points where the ground is sandy and erodes easily in the rain. She expressed concern over
188   destruction of wildlife habitats along the river, and with storm runoff potentially going into the
189   river. Additionally, there is a piece of land owned by the federal government that she advised
190   obtaining permission to build on before starting the project. She mentioned that other than these
191   concerns, she thinks it is a great plan for the area.
      Lehi City Planning Commission                     4                                    January 9, 2025
192
193   Commissioner Everett closed public comment.
194
195   Mackey highlighted a historic discharge path and explained that the plan is to maintain it for a
196   small portion of the storm drainage. The rest will be going into the system on 3600 West. He also
197   mentioned that he had been in talks with representatives about acquiring the government
198   property she mentioned, but that they had led nowhere, and that the acquisition likely won’t
199   happen.
200
201   Commissioner Eyre asked about the grading plans for parcel A. Mackey explained that the only

329   3.10) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
330   Development Code amendment to Chapter 12, to update standards regarding barbed wire
331   fencing.
332
333   Jacob Curtis presented the item. The planning division wants to adjust the zones in the code that
334   are allowed to use barbed wire, removing industrial zones and adding public facility zones.

      Lehi City Planning Commission                    7                                    January 9, 2025
335   Additionally, they clarify that barbed wire is allowed for agricultural purposes, and that it may be
336   used temporarily in construction sites. Razor wire will be prohibited in all zones.
337
338   Commissioner Eyre asked if razor wire is considered a type of barbed wire. Mike West,
339   representing the city, clarified that it is not.
340
341   Commissioner Everett opened and closed public comment.
342
343   Commissioner Everett worried about the possibility of the city wanting to use razor wire in the
344   future and being unable to, such as in the use of water security. He mentioned his preference for
345   remaining silent in the code if possible. West stated that there are no plans to use razor wire for
346   water facilities. He also clarified that the code states that anything not directly outlined as
347   allowed is automatically not allowed, so in this case remaining silent would have the same effect
348   as the clause forbidding razor wire.
349
350                   Motion: Commissioner Kunze moved to give a positive recommendation to the
351                   city council, finding that the proposed development code amendment is in
352                   conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the general plan and its
353                   various elements. Commissioner Eyre seconded.
354
355                   Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes. Commissioner Eyre, yes. Commissioner
356                   Kunze, yes. Commissioner Jones, yes. Commissioner Roberts, yes.
357
358                   Motion passed unanimously.
