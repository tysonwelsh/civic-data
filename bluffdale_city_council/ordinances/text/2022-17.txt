                                  CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH
                                   ORDINANCE No. 2022-17

      AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 1 OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY CODE RELATED TO
 ADMINISTRATION AND DUTIES OF THE MAYOR RELATING TO SUPERVISION OF CITY EVENTS

        WHEREAS, the City of Bluffdale ("City") has adopted Administration, codified as Title 1
of the Bluffdale City Code; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Atm. § l 0-3b-303 and 10-3b-l 04, the City Council
desires to amend this title to modify and clarify the powers and duties of the Mayor; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor is responsible for external relations and events that are "outward
facing" and promotes the City and events are used for promotion of City initiatives; and

        WHEREAS, the Mayor is presently responsible for the City's town days that are currently
called Bluffdale Old West Days; and

      WHEREAS, it is proposed that the duties of the Mayor include all city events suppo1ied and
conducted by the City.

       Now, THEREFORE, THE BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL HEREBY ORDAINS AS FOLLOWS:

       SECTION 1. AMENDMENT OF BLUFFDALE CITY CODE. Title 1, Administration, of the
Bluffdale City Code is hereby amended as follows:


1.60 MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL



1.60.030: MAYOR:

A. Powers and Duties:

           1. The Mayor shall serve as a member of the six (6) member Council that governs
              the City.
           2. The Mayor shall serve as the Chairperson of the meetings of the City Council.
              (Ord. 2006-09, 10-17-2006)
           3. The Mayor, in conjunction with the City Council, shall appoint a City Manager,
              pursuant to Utah Code§ 10-3b-303(1)(b)(iii), to perform executive and
              administrative duties or functions that the Council by ordinance delegates to the
              City Manager.
           4. The Mayor shall represent the City in its external relationships. The Mayor may
              delegate authority to the City Manager to represent the City in select external
              relationships, with advice and consent of the City Council.


                                                1
         5. The Mayor may remit fines and forfeitures and may release any person
            imprisoned for violation of any City ordinance, and shall report such remittance
            or release to the City Council at its next regular meeting.
         6. The Mayor may at any reasonable time examine the books and/or the official
            papers of the City.
         7. The Mayor may recommend for City Council consideration any measure deemed
            to be in the best interest of the City.
          8. The Mayor may call, in time of emergency, on the residents of the City over
             twenty one (21) years to assist in enforcing the law of the State and ordinances of
             the City.
         9. The Mayor shall be responsible for the supervision and direction of all public city
            events. City employees working on these events will remain under the
            management of the City Manager in accordance with Bluffdale City Code Section
            1.70.020(A)(7).
          10. The Mayor shall be responsible for the supervision and administration of the
              Equestrian Council, the Historical Council and the Youth Council.
          11. The Mayor shall pe1fonn the legislative and judicial duties of the Office of Mayor
              as defined by State law. These legislative and judicial duties, as well as the Mayor's
              ex officio positions, may not be delegated by the Mayor or the City Council. (Ord.
              2017-14, 7-26-2017)

       SECTION 2. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Ordinance shall take effect upon publication or
posting, or thirty (30) days after passage, whichever occurs first.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED: September 28, 2022.
