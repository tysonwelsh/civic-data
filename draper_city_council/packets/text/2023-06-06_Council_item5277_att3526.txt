                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-26

          A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING
         THE FIRST AMENDMENT TO THE GROUND LEASE AGREEMENT
                    WITH THE LIVING PLANET, INC. d/b/a
                     THE LIVING PLANET AQUARIUM

WHEREAS, the City entered into a Ground Lease Agreement ("Lease") (Draper City Contract
Number 12-168) with The Living Planet, Inc., doing business as The Living Planet Aquarium
("Aquarium"), dated December 6, 2012, for development of the Aquarium property located at
approximately 12033 Lone Peak Parkway in Draper (the "Property"); and

WHEREAS, the Aquarium has constructed the improvements contemplated by the Lease; and

WHEREAS, Draper City has benefitted financially and in intangible ways from the success of
the Aquarium; and

WHEREAS, the Aquarium has received approval from Draper City to reconfigure the lots in
order to expand the attractions offered to the public; and

WHEREAS, the City and the Aquarium desire to amend the Lease in accordance with the terms
and conditions of the First Amendment to Ground Lease Agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit
A; and

WHEREAS, approving the First Amendment to Ground Lease Agreement promotes the health,
safety and welfare of the residents of Draper City.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

Section 1. Amended Agreement. The Draper City Council approves the First Amendment to
Grotmd Lease Agreement attached as Exhibit A and authorizes the Mayor to execute the same on
behalf of Draper City.

Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage by
the City Council.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 6 th DAY OF JUNE 2023.
