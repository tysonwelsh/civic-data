42   2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Boyd Brown's request for review of the
43   Exchange Business Park Zone Change on 1.19 acres located at approximately 1500 North

     Lehi City Planning Commission                    1                                    June 13, 2024
44   3600 West, changing the zoning from R-3 (high density residential) to Neighborhood
45   Commercial.
46   Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.
47
48   The applicant, Jake Hone, expressed the team's surprise at the need for rezoning to neighborhood
49   commercial and requested the commissioners' support to proceed with the site plan, and stated he
50   was available for questions.
51
52   Vice Commissioner Everett opened the public hearing.
53
54   Scott Larsen voiced concerns about increased traffic and the potential for people to cut through
55   their back alleyway if the zoning change to commercial was approved.
56
57   Marshall Gary, a leader of Strong Towns Lehi, was excited about nearby commercial
58   development but concerned it might become car-dependent. He advocated for pedestrian-friendly
59   design with parking behind or beside buildings to encourage foot and bicycle traffic, noting a
60   recent accident involving a teen in the area.
61
62   Cole Bolton expressed concerns about the impact of the commercial development on property
63   values, safety, and the operational hours of the businesses, particularly given its proximity to his
64   home and a nearby fire station.
65
66   Vice Commissioner Everett closed the public hearing.
67
68   Commissioner Eyre clarified that the commissioners were focusing solely on a zone change, not
69   the construction plans or concepts. The purpose of the meeting was to align the land use with the
70   general plan. Commissioner Eyre emphasized that the public's concerns had been heard and
71   would be considered in detail during the review of the final site plan.
72
73   Vice Commissioner Everett invited Mr. Hone back up to address any comments from the public.
74
75   Jake Hone stated the planning department previously shared the potential uses within the
76   neighborhood commercial zone, which generated excitement, particularly regarding dining
77   options, childcare, and small medical services. Feedback was incorporated into the site plan,
78   resulting in improvements such as better walking trails, a plaza area, extended bike trails with
79   green spaces, and additional walking connections to the nearby neighborhood. The goal was to
80   create a walkable area while ensuring the project's feasibility.
81
82   Luke Seegmiller clarified questions regarding a traffic study and potential traffic calming
83   measures, noting that while it wasn't directly related to the neighborhood commercial zone
84   change, it was connected to the overall concerns about the development.
85
86           Motion: Commissioner Roberts moved to recommend approval of Boyd Brown’s
87           request for review of the Exchange Business Park Zone Change on 1.19 acres
88           located at approximately 1500 North 3600 West, changing the zoning from R-3
89           (high density residential) to Neighborhood Commercial; including all DRC comments
90           and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Lockart seconded.

119   2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of an
120   amendment to the Lehi City Development Code Table 37.100, amending the requirements
121   for bicycle parking.
122   Jacob Curtis presented information on the item. He stated this amendment addressed specific
123   issues identified at the DRC level. There are concerns that arose regarding excessive bike
124   parking, which was often disproportionate to the size of buildings. Adjustments were proposed
125   for clubhouses, churches, and manufacturing establishments to better align parking requirements
126   with practical needs.
127
128   Vice Commissioner Everett opened public comment.
129
130   Marshall Gary was present to represent Strong Towns Lehi, which aimed to enhance Lehi's
131   appeal with outdoor amenities and transportation choices. Emphasizing a balanced approach, he
132   advocated for enjoyable driving, walking, biking, and public transit options. Mr. Gary expressed
133   pleasant surprise at initial plans for bike parking, noting his church currently lacked any bike
134   parking spots.
135
136   Vice Commissioner Everett closed the public comment.
