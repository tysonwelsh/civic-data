1. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH DAVIS COUNTY 4-H LIVESTOCK TO HOLD THEIR
LIVESTOCK SHOW AND SALE AT THE GOLDEN SPIKE EVENT CENTER.

Duncan Olsen (GSEC) explained Davis County 4-H could not hold their fair but had already started working
their animals. Offering discounts to help make this happen for them this year.

Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a contract with Davis County 4-H Livestock to hold their Livestock
Show and Sale at the Golden Spike Event Center; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye

2. APPROVAL OF THE FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING SOLID WASTE
FEES AND GOVERNING THEIR COLLECTION.

Sean Wilkinson (Director, Community Development) started collecting residential waste through contracts
with Republic Services and Waste Management for parts of unincorporated Weber County; they do the billing,
per contract, as County does not have system to collect utilities on broad scale. Ordinance ratifies existing fees
that are already part of existing contracts. Ordinance also allows the County option of collecting bills
delinquent greater than 90 days on their tax bill.

Commissioner Jenkins moved to first reading of an ordinance establishing solid waste fees and governing their
collection; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye

3. FINAL APPROVAL OF TERAKEE MEADOW SUBDIVISION, CONSISTING OF 12 LOTS. (LVT
100118).


Steve Burton (Planning) explained this is standard subdivision with 12 lots of 20k-80k square feet located near
900 S and 4300 W in Al agricultural zone. Western Weber Planning Commission recommended final approval
on January 8, 2019. Bond received of $879,432 for remaining improvements.

Commissioner Froerer moved for final approval of Terakee Meadow Subdivision, consisting of 12 lots. (LVT
100118); Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye
