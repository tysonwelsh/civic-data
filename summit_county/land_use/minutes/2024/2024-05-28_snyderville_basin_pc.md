3)
Public hearing and action regarding an amendment to a Conditional Use Permit to add an additional 560 sf to a previously approved 11,040 sf  "Major Accessory Building". 4200 N 250 East. Parcel SS-60-E (20.1 acres). Applicant: Stefan Brutsch, RM Koemans Construction. Owner: Mike and Kelly Perillo.  Administrative Process. Project #23-195.
Jennifer Leslie, County Planner. (6:04 P.M.)
Attachment:
23-195 Staff Report jl 5 28.pdf
Attachment:
Perillo CUP Amendment
Jennifer Leslie
, planner
, presented background information on the project and a proposal to amend the Conditional Use Permit to add 560 square feet to a Major Accessory Building.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:07 P.M.
Jason Travis was called to speak.
Stanna Frampton was called to speak.
Judy Bell was called to speak.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:13 P.M.
Commissioners commented on the proposal and asked clarifying questions to which Planner Leslie responded.
Commissioners addressed comments made during the public hearing. Patrick Putt,
Community Development Director
, provided additional information in response to the questions and concerns raised by members of the public and commissioners.
Planner Leslie provided the January 2023 conditions of approval for the original CUP for discussion.
The applicant, Stefan Brutsch, RM Koemans Construction, addressed the commission and responded to the questions and concerns.
Commissioners asked questions of the applicant.
Bruce Carmichael made a motion to approve the amendment to the Conditional Use Permit to add an additional 560 sf to the previously approved Major Accessory Building at 4200 North 250 East pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report along with the conditions found in the 2023 staff report which continue with special attention to condition of approval number 9 in previous staff report.
Discussion was held on the motion and Commissioner Carmichael amended his motion.
Bruce Carmichael made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit to add an additional 560 sf to a previously approved Major Accessory Building on parcel SS-60-E pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report along with the conditions found in Exhibit E of the staff report which continue with special attention to condition of approval number 9, in addition the condition of the road shall be assessed by the county engineer prior to the granting of the certificate of occupancy. The county engineer shall assess the difference between use and damage by the construction. Tyann Mooney seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
