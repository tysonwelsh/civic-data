                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-68

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING RIVERTON CITY TO EXECUTE A REAL ESTATE
PURCHASE CONTRACT WITH THE CHURCH OF JESUS CHRIST OF LATTER-DAY
SAINTS, A UTAH CORPORATION SOLE, F/K/A CORPORATION OF THE PRESIDING
BISHOP OF THE CHURCH OF JESUS CHRIST OF LATTER-DAY SAINTS TO ACQUIRE
REAL PROPERTY, LOCATED AT THE ADDRESS OF 4401 WEST 13400 SOUTH,
RIVERTON, UTAH (PARCEL NO. 33062000690000)

         WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-8-2, Riverton City may “purchase,
receive, hold, sell, lease, convey, and dispose of real and personal property for the benefit of the
municipality, whether the property is within or without the municipality’s corporate boundaries,
if the action is in the public interest and complies with other law;” and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Riverton City has a
need to purchase real property which will be used to increase the storage capacity of the
secondary water system used by Riverton City residents; and

       WHEREAS, the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints owns property at the
approximate address of 4401 West 13400 South, Riverton, Utah (Parcel No. 33062000690000)
(the Subject Property), which is also adjacent to existing secondary water facilities in need of
improvement to expand storage capacity; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that acquisition of the
Subject Property will enable the City to expand storage in the Riverton City secondary water
system in the near future; and

       WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that Riverton City staff has
negotiated an agreement with the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints to purchase the Subject
Property; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the purchase price for the
Subject Property is reasonable and of fair market value, based upon an appraisal of the same obtained
by City Staff;

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1.      The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute a Purchase Agreement with a properly
               authorized Agent for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints, A Utah
               Corporation Sole, F/K/A Corporation of The Presiding Bishop of The Church of
               Jesus Christ Of Latter-Day Saints to acquire property approximately 3.03 acres of
               real property located at 4401 West 13400 South, Riverton, Utah, Parcel Number
               33062000690000, once it is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney. In

                                                  1
             addition, the Mayor is authorized to execute any additional documents at closing if
             the same are necessary to facilitate closing, provided said documents do not
             culminate in a substantial alteration to the material terms of the Purchase Agreement,
             and said documents are approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

      2.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
