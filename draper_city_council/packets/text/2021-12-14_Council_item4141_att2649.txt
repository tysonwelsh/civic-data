                             RESOLUTION NO. 21-57

      A RESOLUTION APPROVING A LEASE AGREEMENT BETWEEN DRAPER CITY
      AND DISH WIRELESS, LLC FOR AN EQUIPMENT SHELTER AND
      INFRASTRUCTURE TO SUPPORT NEW ANTENNAS INSTALLED ON THE EXISTING
      WATER TANK CELL TOWER LOCATED AT SUNCREST WATER TANK #3, DRAPER,
      UTAH.

      WHEREAS, the city has received a request from DISH Wireless, LLC to lease
150 square feet of Draper City property for an equipment shelter and infrastructure
to support new antennas on the existing stealth cell tower due to a strong need for
wireless facilities; and

      WHEREAS, there will be a significant improvement in the wireless service with
the addition of the wireless telecommunication facility; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council previously entered into a lease agreement
with Verizon Wireless and T-Mobile for the water tank cell tower and
telecommunication equipment on city-owned property at the SunCrest water tank
#3; and

      WHEREAS, the wireless telecommunication facility is of sufficient stealth
design; and

      WHEREAS, the lease will provide for a continuous revenue stream; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has found the terms and conditions
thereof of the Lease Agreement (Exhibit “A”), acceptable to Draper City.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      1. It does hereby approve the attached Lease Agreement between Draper
         City and DISH Wireless, LLC.
      2. The Honorable Troy K. Walker, Mayor of Draper City, is hereby authorized
         to execute the agreement on behalf of Draper City and to act in
         accordance with its terms.
      3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.
PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 14th DAY OF DECEMBER, 2021.
