City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1659

On June 18, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1659 approving a
development agreement for 110 acres of property located at 3235 East 15780 South in
the RR-22 zone within Draper City, otherwise known as the Alpine Hollow development
agreement. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
