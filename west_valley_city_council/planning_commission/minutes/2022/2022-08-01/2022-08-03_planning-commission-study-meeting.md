ZONE TEXT APPLICATIONS
Z-9-2022  Petition by SHANE KEYVANI requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture,
          minimum lot size ½ acre) to RE (Residential Estate). The property is located at
          3940 S 5200 W on 10.6 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
          steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov) (Continued from the July 13, 2022 public hearing)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that this application was continued from the July 13,
              2022, public hearing. He reviewed the proposal for the Commission. Steve stated
              that the Commission decided to continue the application feeling the proposed
              housing standards fell short of other recently submitted applications.

              Steve presented a table outlining the standards proposed by the developer on July
              11, 2022, and July 27, 2022, as well as the previous housing standards. He then
              reviewed the newly proposed standards and how they compare to the previous
              housing standards.
              Chair Fuller asked Steve if the proposed housing standards are the same as the
              current standards. Steve explained that up until last year, the previous housing
              standards outlined in the table were part of our code. He stated that the legislature
              passed a bill limiting the housing standards for single-family homes. Steve said
              when anyone applies for a zone change, we can include housing standards via a
              development agreement, and that is what is being proposed with this application.

              Commissioner McEwen asked if the applicant had stated the proposed
              development would be similar to Newton Farms. Steve said that is correct. He
              asked if the homes in the Newton Farm development had two or three-car
              garages. Steve said the vast majority have a three-car garage. Commissioner
              McEwen would prefer the three-car garage instead of the twenty feet on the side
              of the proposed homes.

              Commissioner Lovato asked what thin brick material is and if it is durable.
              Commissioner Porter explained that it is brick but about half the width of a
              standard brick. Commissioner Woodruff said that it is usually glued on, and if not
              done correctly, it will not last.

              Commissioner Woodruff said he would like to eliminate the thin brick and
              increase the percentage of brick/stone from fifteen percent to twenty-five percent.
              He feels they should meet the previous housing standards for the design
              standards.


ZONE TEXT APPLICATIONS
SMI-6-2022 Petition by SHIVAM SHAH requesting preliminary approval for the Hot Rod
           Lane Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 5.8 acres. The
           subdivision is located at approximately 3703 West Parkway Boulevard and is
           zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
           steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Lehman explained that this application is to subdivide the subject property
              into two lots. He stated that the existing building would remain on one lot, and the
              vacant property would be on a separate lot. Steve noted that an additional two to
              three feet of right-of-way might need to be dedicated in anticipation of future road
              improvements. He explained that the width of the asphalt would not be affected.

              Steve said there are existing easements on the subdivision plat. He stated that the
              applicant proposes a new access on the southwest corner of the subject lot that
              will line up with Lake Erie Drive on the other side of Parkway Blvd.
