1. Plan Ogden Discussion

Representative Megan Moore with Logan Simpson presented the refined chapters in the
General Plan. The meeting focused on refining the city's general plan, emphasizing the need for
clear principles to guide housing and land use decisions. The plan includes updates to land use
maps, mixed-use models, and transportation improvements along Grant Avenue. The team
discussed the need for detailed housing goals, the distribution of human services, and the
potential for a public open house to present the final draft. Concerns about the legality of
specifying low-income housing locations and the need for updated housing data were also
raised.
