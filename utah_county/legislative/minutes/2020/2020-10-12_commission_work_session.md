Challenges

* COVID-19: State funding cut.

* Limited staff in building.

* No onsite treatment available.

HEATHER ALLEN, ASSISTANT DIRECTOR — OUR CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER
HAS BEEN AFFECTED BY THE PANDEMIC. OUR STATE FUNDING WAS RETRACTED
IN THE COVID-19 LEGISLATIVE SESSION. THAT WAS A BIG IMPACT FOR US. WE
WERE NOT THE ONLY ONES THAT HAD FUNDING CUT. THE DIVISION OF CHILD
AND FAMILY SERVICES ALSO HAD $600,000 CUT FROM THEIR FUNDING AND
THAT MEANT THAT THEY WOULD HAVE TO GET RID OF 13 POSITIONS. INORDER
TO SAVE THOSE 13 POSITIONS THERE WAS A CHANGE THAT WAS MADE TO THE
STATUTE — STATUTE 62A-48-409 WHICH SAYS THAT THE DIVISION IS NOT
REQUIRED TO CONDUCT AN INVESTIGATION UNDER SUBSECTION 1A IF THE
DIVISION DETERMINES THE PERSON RESPONSIBLE FOR THE CHILD’S CARE IS
NOT THE ALLEGED PERPETRATOR AND IS WILLING AND ABLE TO ENSURE THE
ALLEGED PERPETRATOR DOES NOT HAVE ACCESS TO THE CHILD.

THAT STATUTE CHANGE IMPACTED A LOT OF OUR CASES. IT’S UNCLEAR HOW
MANY EXACTLY YET. WE ESTIMATE ANYWHERE FROM 2,500-5,000 STATEWIDE
WHICH COULD BE 250-500 CASES FOR US. WHAT THIS MEANS IS THAT LAW
ENFORCEMENT TYPICALLY HAD THE ASSISTANCE OF THE DIVISION OF CHILD
AND FAMILY SERVICES. THEY HAD THE ASSISTANCE OF A CASE WORKER IN
COORDINATING WITH THE FAMILY AND COMPLETING THE INVESTIGATION AND
COMPLETING THE FORENSIC INTERVIEW. NOW THAT THEY WILL NOT BE
PANELING THESE NO ACCESS PERPETRATOR CASES, LAW ENFORCEMENT WIL1.
HAVE TO TAKE THOSE ON THEMSELVES AND SO WE’RE LOOKING AT HOW THE
CJC CAN STEP IN AND FILL IN FOR WHAT THE CASE WORKERS USED TO DO ON
THESE CASES. WE’RE TRYING TO FIND SOLUTIONS OF HOW WE CAN SUPPORT
OUR LAW ENFORCEMENT WHICH IS WHY WE ANTICIPATE WE WILL BE
WORKING A LOT MORE CLOSELY WITH THEM. AS WE RECEIVE THESE
REFERRALS, WE’LL BE FOLLOWING UP WITH LAW ENFORCEMENT ON WHICH
CASES THEY WILL BE INVESTIGATING. WE’LL BE ASSISTING IN SETTING UP
THOSE INTERVIEWS BECAUSE BEST PRACTICE FOR FORENSIC INTERVIEWS IS
TO HAVE ONE PERSON INTERVIEWING AND ONE OBSERVING. THEY USED TO,
WHEN THERE WAS A CASE WORKER, THEY WOULD BE THE ONES
INTERVIEWING THE DETECTIVES WOULD OBSERVE AND TAKE NOTES THAT
THEY NEED FOR THEIR INVESTIGATION. NOW THAT MAY NOT BE THE CASE AND
