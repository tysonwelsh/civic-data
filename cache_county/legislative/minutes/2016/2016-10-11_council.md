Chairman Merrill noted that because the Executive and Council members have received
many inquiries about the Bookmobile’s future, the Chair has the discretion of allowing public
comments.

Cache County Council
10-11-2016

Executive Buttars explained that his budget proposal does contain funding for the
Bookmobile through June 30, 2017. In the meantime, he would like to increase dialog and
collaboration with other libraries and the state library system to find better ways to meet the
needs of people in the county without a duplication of services. He would especially like to
increase the visibility of library opportunities online.

Chairman Merrill invited comments and asked that individuals limit their time to 3-4 minutes
each with a total time for all comments of 15 minutes.

Chad Hutchings — Logan — used the Bookmobile services growing up in Sandy, Utah when it was a
small farm community and urged the County to do all it can to maintain access to library services.

Donna Jones Morris, State Librarian, also wants to check out duplication of services and the
Bookmobile benefits to citizens. She wants to help the County Library in Providence and observed a
similar hearing on Bookmobile services was recently held in Utah County and the decision was to
retain the Bookmobile. The state pays 30% of Bookmobile costs and feels invested in the program.
Jones Morris asked citizens attending for this issue to stand and 13-15 people stood

Doug Clawson, River Heights City Council, supports the Bookmobile and asked when the decision
would be made to fund or defund the Bookmobile. Chairman Merrill said there would be public
hearings and the decision would be made by November 8, 2016.

Sean Damitz, Smithfield, and his family use the Bookmobile regularly and it is always busy. He
supports the Bookmobile as a great resource for the rural residents of the county.

Heidi Hyatt, Mendon, uses the Bookmobile and used it when she lived in Nibley. Mendon is getting
its own library, but she still intends on using the Bookmobile and the County Library. She does not
consider these as duplication of services; the collections are complementary, not competitive. Many
people don’t even know there is a County Library; it needs more promotion.

Chairman Merrill thanked all for their input.
