GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-1-2024 Petition by PAUL AND KAREEN SWENSON requesting a General Plan
           change from Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Light
           Manufacturing and a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½
           acre) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 4053 South 7200
           West on 2.96 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve explained the application lots size, with homes facing the lot on the east
              and north side of the property. On the west side along 7200 West borders Magna
              and a Salt Lake County Park. Property to the south is owned by the city and is
              the former animal shelter. The animal shelter property has been used for housing
              larger animals and storage of extra public works garbage cans. South of the
              animal shelter borders 4100 south. The property on the south of 4100 is owned
              by the Navy which was part of Northrop Grumman complex. Steve went over
              the property map, highlighting the area in red, which is the property in question,
              explaining the importance of the overpressure zone which the property is
              approximately 40% within the inner most circle located within the overpressure
              zone. Even though it is now zoned agricultural, that innermost ring does not
              allow for any residential use. Given that restriction, it would be difficult for this
              property, being so small, to have commercial industrial use in front and
              residential on the other.

              Steve went over the uses of this property, explaining different options and
              examples of developments that could work with the neighbors in the area and the
              overpressure zone. One example of a project on the corner of 5400 south 6055
              west, homes on one side, with a variance, a small building on the property line
              with taller buildings moving inward. Commissioner Winters asked if that
              building on the property line served as the wall for that project?
              Steve replied that was exactly what they had done.

              Steve explained that the other applications on the south side of this property, if
              the rezone occurs, then it will not affect this application and the setback
              requirement will be set at zero on the south side. The exception would apply on
              the east and north side stating the city has approved other developments like this.

              Steve presented some examples of ideas from the applicant of what the project
              might look like, looking at all sides of the property. Steve noted the right side of
              7200 west has no improvements and would be a requirement if the zoning is
              approved to install curb, gutter, and sidewalk. Steve summarized the general
              plan and rezone change of this application with the intended use of storage units.
              Steve stated staff feels with the overpressure zone and adjoining residential,
              storage units would be a good approach and noted the applicant is willing to
              exclude other industrial uses from the property that normally would be allowed
              in a manufacturing zone. Steve pointed out the proposal also shows the building
              adjacent to residential be single level, considering two story storage on A, B, and
              D on the map, which would be ok with the setbacks far enough from the
              residential. Steve asked for any questions. No questions were asked.

GPZ-2-2024 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a General Plan change from
           Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Light Manufacturing and a zone
           change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to M
           (Manufacturing). The property is located at 4063 South 7200 West on 2.0
           acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve stated this application is related to the first application after his
              conversation with the Swensen. If the commissioners and then the city council
              approve a rezoning of the previous application property, then this adjacent city
              owned two acer property used for large animals and garbage can storage, will
              need to be rezone. This application, if rezoned to manufacture, the storage of
              garbage cans and animals are allowed in a manufacturing zone as well as
              agriculture zone which allows either way for the use as it is not. Steve stated
              there is no proposal for development on this city property for the foreseeable
              future. City’s property is 50% is in the overpressure zone. Picture showing
              looking north, cell tower on the property, fence entrance into the property. North
              on 7200 and looking south on 7200 west. Idea would be both parcels be zoned
              manufacturing. Steve asked for any questions. No questions were asked.


ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-3-2024      Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zone change from A
              (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-10 (Single Unit Dwelling
              Residential, minimum lot size 10,000 square feet). The property is located at
              3575 South 5200 West on 0.38 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545
              or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve stated this is a city-initiated application to bring the property in question
              into compliance. Showing a map of three parcels in red, yellow, and blue,
              explain once the subdivision on the east side, in yellow, is recorded, the
              remainder parcel, in blue, will be less than half an acer and will not comply with
              the agriculture zone it is zoned now. Martel asked if any anticipated activity is
              there? Steve stated there is one home already there and with this rezone, the lot
              will then be in compliance with the requirements of the R-1-10 Single Unit
              Dwelling Residential. No further questions were asked.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-5-2024 Petition by PREMIER TRUCK GROUP requesting a conditional use for
         outside storage of heavy trucks, trailers and parts in conjunction with heavy
         truck service. The property is located at 5431 and 5477 West Lampert Lane
         on 4.81 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-
         963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody presented the conditional use application located in the Warrner Truck
              Center. She outlined the truck uses over the years for this site working with
              them over the years for a formal application for Truck storage, and east side of
              lot for outside storage for the truck repair business.

              Jody went over the map and explained the areas and proposed uses. The
              building to the north of this property is the service part of the business. The lot
              by this building will be used for dropping off the truck or trailer while the other
              is being serviced. The east side of the lot will be used for outside overflow
              storage of heavy truck or trailer parts like hoods, bumpers and fenders and will
              not be used for any tires, junk, damaged truck or trailer parts.

              Jody stated that chain link fencing and vinyl slats will be added to provide
              screening from the public view. This shall be located along the north side of the
              lot on Lampert Lane as well as along both sides of the property for 160 feet or
              until it meets with the Sprinter building on the adjacent east property.

              Jody explained that this area is basically one big lot which will be completely
              repaved with added storm drains and will go under plan review to make sure all
              the easements and drainage works for this project.

              Jody mentioned the landscaping will be completed along the frontage of Lampert
              Lane. There is a minimum of 5% landscaping for the site which they have
              currently with rocks. They will berm it up and add the 50% live plants and trees
              requirement.

              No further questions were asked.


C-7-2024      Petition by CASTLEWOOD DEVELOPMENT requesting conditional use
              approval for a 330-unit apartment development. The property is located at
              2940 West 3650 South on 3.02 acres and is zoned CC (City Center) and R-2-
              8 (Two Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet).
              (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).


Discussion:   Steve stated that this application has been submitted several times for approval
              since 2022. It was approved for apartment development west of the library. The
              developer had not started the project and requested for a renewal. It was
              approved in May of last year, and with the development not yet started, they are
              asking to renew their approval for another 12 months.

              Steve explained that everything is the same as the original application except for
              a small correction in the landscaping plan. Upon reviewing the site plan, it was
              noted the entrance to the parking structure of this building did not align with the
              landscaping plan. With this small correction, both plans are now in alignment.

              Steve went over several map views pointing out the different levels of the 7-story
              building with parking and units, which exceed the city unit minimum standard.

              Commissioner Fuller asked if the applicant has given a schedule of construction
              start time. Steve explained when the application was first submitted, the market
              changed, and they mentioned the challenges they have faced, with the biggest
              challenge being the rate environment and cost of construction.

              Commissioner Fuller mentioned there was a concern last time with Lancer Way
              and the construction that would be going. Steve explained when a project is
              being developed, the developer is required to put in the curb and gutter
              improvements. Since the City has scheduled to start road construction on Lancer
              Way, sometime end of 2024 or in 2025, with all new road improvements
              including curb and gutter. Since the applicant has not started the project, we
              cannot require them to put in the improvements and the City does not want to
              leave an uneven, unfinished section of the road.

              Commissioner Winters asked about the street south on 3650 Lancer Way, traffic
              pulling in and out of the project. Steve stated there will be a left turn lane put in
              for merging into traffic. Commissioner Winters went on to ask about the other
              entrance in the northwest corner of the property. Steve explained the developers
              were able to secure the easement from the owners of that parcel, which the city
              verified was in place.

              No further questions.



There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:20 p.m.
