                                  PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES
                                              Meeting of May 26, 2022
                            Logan City Council Chambers (290 N 100 W Logan, UT 84321) www.loganutah.org

Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session
Thursday, May 26, 2022, Commissioner Lucero, called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Roylan Croshaw, Jordy Guth, Ken Heare, David Lewis, Eldon Peterson,
Sara Doutre, Jessica Lucero

Commissioners Excused:

Staff Present: Russ Holley, Tanya Rice, Mike DeSimone, Mohammed Abdullahi

Commissioner Guth moved to approve the minutes from the May 12, 2022 meeting as submitted.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Doutre. The motion was approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING

 PC 22-022 Watermark Subdivision – Withdrawn [Design Review & Subdivision Permit]
 Joshua Low/David Nielsen, authorized agent/owner request a Design Review and Subdivision
 Permit to subdivide 11.13 acres into 65 lots to construct 59 flex building units that include 1,500

 PC 22-024 Annexation Policy Plan Review-2022 Draft – Continued from the April 28
 meeting. [Policy Plan Review] Mike DeSimone/Logan City are requesting the review and
 adoption of Logan City’s 2022 Annexation Policy Plan.


STAFF/PROPONENT: Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director, stated that this is the
second public hearing on the annexation policy plan. There have been no comments received
since the last hearing. Mr. DeSimone reviewed the proposed Annexation Policy Plan which is
required under Utah Municipal Code 10-2-401.5. All Utah municipalities are required to adopt an
Annexation Policy Plan that guides the City’s future growth and expansion through the annexation
of unincorporated lands adjacent to Logan City. The function of the Annexation Policy Plan is to
identify those areas outside the community that presents the greatest potential for inclusion into the
city with a logical and coordinated approach to new development and public service provision. M.
DeSimone reviewed the reasons why the City is adopting an updated Annexation Policy Plan, the
duties of the Planning Commission during this process, and the elements of the Plan.

M. DeSimone outlined the six different growth corridors identified in the plan and the future population
growth and general land use anticipated for each area. The amount of vacant land located within the
City was reviewed. M. DeSimone also discussed the Planning Commission’s review and
recommendation process as well as the next steps in the Commission’s process.


PUBLIC: Richard Oswald said that we are running out of water and that needs to be taken into
consideration when new residential developments are being considered.

Jotham Hatch asked what the process is when an annexation is proposed and What happens if
surrounding properties want to be annexed.

Matt Fuller asked if the north/south corridors are being discussed with the future annexation areas.
He also asked what Logan’s response would be if Benson decided to incorporate

Todd Ballard mentioned the property along Airport Road that was annexed into Logan in 2007 and
how there has not been any development there yet.

Elaine Ricks asked about Benson becoming incorporated.

Randy Udy stated that Logan has already created an island on the property they wanted to use for
a compost site.

Sue Olsen asked what the future zoning would be near 2400 West.

Jeff Ricks asked if utilities are included when properties are annexed into Logan

Heidi Spackman asked if there would be any effect on their property taxes

COMMISSION: Commissioner Doutre asked if there could be a quick overview of the annexation
process and the proposed policy plan and the public comments addresses.

M. DeSimone said that the annexation plan defines a boundary of growth that can be supported
with utilities and infrastructure so when there is an annexation request submitted there is a
guideline to follow. A property owner needs to initiate the petition to annex. The city does not
generally annex property unless it is requested by a property owner. State law does not allow the
creation of unincorporated islands as a result of an annexation petition.

M. DeSimone said that there has not been any development to the property annexed along airport
Road because there is no infrastructure in place to service that area. The Annexation Policy Plan
will create a better review process to follow, but ultimately it is a legislative decision and the
Municipal Council makes the final decision to annex a property or not.

Commissioner Guth asked if the General Plan would be updated following the Annexation Policy
Plan. M. DeSimone said that the Annexation Plan will inform the General Plan the same way each
specific water, sewer, and utility planning document will. Any issues are considered and
addressed within each specific plan.

M. DeSimone said that if Benson was to incorporate and they had the necessary services available
to service additional land and Logan did not, it would make sense to discuss that land being
annexed into Benson.

M. DeSimone stated that the property considered for the compost site is owned by Logan City, but
it is not within the city limits. It was not annexed because it is not adjacent to any Logan City land.


M. Desimone said that the zoning will be determined per each specific annexation depending on
the surrounding zones, the future land use plan, and the surrounding uses. The updated General
Plan will include a zoning overlay for the unincorporated areas that will also help to determine what
zone a property should be annexed in at.

MOTION: Commissioner Peterson moved to forward a recommendation of approval on the draft
2022 Annexation Policy Plan to the Municipal Council for PC 22-024 with the recommended findings
for approval as listed below. Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.

RECOMMENDED FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1.     Utah State Law requires that a local government adopt an Annexation Policy Plan.
2.     Utah State Law defines the specific role of the Planning Commission in preparing,
       reviewing and recommending a Policy Plan to the legislative body (Municipal Council).
3.     The proposed 2022 Annexation Policy Plan is consistent with the current Logan City
       General Plan.
4.     Any comments received from Affected Entities have been considered in the preparation of
       this Policy Plan.
5.     All minimum Affected Entities and public notification requirements established by Utah
       State Law have been met.

Moved: E. Peterson               Seconded: R. Croshaw                      Approved: 7-0

Yea: Lucero, Croshaw, Guth, Lewis, Heare, Doutre, Petersen              Nay:           Abstain:


 PC 22-023 Land Development Code Amendment-Annexations – Continued from the April
 28 meeting. [Code Amendment] Mike DeSimone/Logan City request an amendment to the Land
 Development Code, chapter 17.53 to provide general requirements for annexing property into
 Logan City. This amendment is in conjunction with the proposed Logan City 2022 Annexation
 Policy Plan also under consideration.


STAFF/PROPONENT: Mike DeSimone reviewed the proposed Land Development Code
Amendment to Chapter 17.53 which provides the general requirements for annexing property into
Logan City as well as delineates the review process the City will undertake in assessing
Annexation Petitions. Of importance is the clarification that requires annexation petitions to be
reviewed by the Planning Commission with the Commission making a recommendation to the
Council.

Commissioner Doutre stated that this policy and code amendment will create structure and allow
for a more detailed discussion to ensure better decisions are being made regarding annexations.

PUBLIC: Matt Fuller asked about defining the character of the land when an annexation proposal
is submitted and how the Planning Commission will determine compatibility.

Jotham Hatch asked about Benson becoming part of Mendon or Petersboro

COMMISSION: Commissioner Lucero asked M. DeSimone to address the public comments. M.
DeSimone said that each annexation site will be looked at to determine if it is compatible with the
potential future growth of Logan. A property owner has the right to choose what city to annex into
if their property borders more than one city.


MOTION: Commissioner Heare moved to forward a recommendation of approval on a Code
Amendment for PC 22-023 with the findings for approval as listed below. Commissioner Guth
seconded the motion.

RECOMMENDED FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1.     Utah State Law authorizes local Planning Commission to recommend ordinance changes to
       the legislative body (Municipal Council).
2.     The Code Amendments are done in conformance with the requirements of Title 17.51 of the
       Logan Municipal Code.
3.     The proposed Code Amendments are consistent with the Logan City General Plan.
4.     The proposed Code Amendments to establish the general requirements for submitting an
       annexation petition and the review procedures will help ensure the annexation process is
       transparent and thorough.

Moved: K. Heare                   Seconded: J. Guth                    Approved: 7-0

Yea: Lucero, Croshaw, Guth, Lewis, Heare, Doutre, Petersen          Nay:           Abstain:


Meeting adjourned at 6:54 p.m.
