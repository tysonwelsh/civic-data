Re: Rosee Florals Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for approximately 0.23 acres located on the west side of Snow Leopard Lane, at approximately 14636 S. Snow Leopard Lane. The property is currently zoned RM and is part of the Master Development Agreement for the Hidden Canyon Estates, Mercer Mountain Estates, And Lakeview Heights Properties Development Agreement recorded May 14, 2015 as entry number 12050023 with the Salt Lake County Recorder and as entry number 41579:2015 with the Utah County Recorder (the “Agreement”), and is subject to the provisions of that development agreement, as amended.  The agreement did not contain certain standards for Home Occupation Conditional Use Permits and accordingly the current City ordinances governing such are applicable to this property. The applicant is requesting that a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for the operation of floral arranging services from the home. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
For denial:
1. Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects, including traffic and parking. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type P.C. Staff Report 4/16/2021 Cover Memo
