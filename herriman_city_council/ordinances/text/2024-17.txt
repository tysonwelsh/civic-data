ORDINANCE NO. 2024-17

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH,
TO AMEND HERRIMAN CITY CODE § 4-6 (NOISE CONTROL).

WHEREAS, The City adopts the Salt Lake County’s health regulation no. 21,
entitled “Noise Control.” However, the County’s noise regulations do not address all
noise types.

WHEREAS, An issue recently presented itself to the City wherein the County’s
noise regulations were not sufficient to address the problem of construction noise.

WHEREAS, The proposed text amendment prohibits construction noise between
the hours of 10 p.m. and 7 a.m. The text amendment also makes other general updates
to this section of code.

WHEREAS, The City Council finds that passing the proposed text amendment is
in the best interest of public health, safety, and welfare.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

SECTION 1. Text Amendment. The City Council hereby adopts the text
amendments shown in Exhibit A.

SECTION 2. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon passage.

APPROVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH, ON
THIS 10% DAY OF JULY, 2024.

Exhibit A

4-6-1: Regulations Adopted By Reference

The provisions of the health department, health regulation no. 21, entitled “Noise Control",
as currently adopted by the board of health under authority of Utah Code Annotated title 26,
as amended, are hereby incorporated in their entirety by reference. One copy of the current
regulations shall be filed with and retained by the city recorder for examination by any
person. (Ord. 99-05 § 9.48.010, 5-26-1999, eff. 6-1-1999; amd. 2010 Code)

4-6-2: Interpretation

In interpreting and applying the provisions of this chapter, the requirements contained
herein are declared to be the minimum required for the purposes set forth. The City may
adopt more restrictive rules by ordinance. Notwithstanding the foregoing, whenever the
provisions of this chapter conflict with any other ordinances pertaining to the same subject,
the more restrictive provisions shall prevail. (Ord. 99-05 § 9.48.020, 5-26-199, eff. 6-1-1999)

4-6-3: Construction Noise

No person shall operate nor any person cause, allow, permit or fail to control the operation
of any mechanical construction equipment or conduct any construction or demolition
activities outside including loading and unloading equipment operating equipment outside
of a dedicated right-of-way with audible backup devices between the hours of 10 p.m. and
7 a.m. unless authorized in writing by the City. Snow removal and emergency response
operations are exempt from this section.

4-6-43: Penalty

Violation of any health regulation incorporated into this chapter shall constitute aeclass-B
misdemeanor,an infraction subject to penalty as provided in section 1-4-1 of this code. -Each
day such violation is committed or permitted to continue shall constitute a separate offense,
and shall be punishable as such. Additional violations within a 12 month period may
constitute a class B misdemeanor subject to the provisions of section 1-4-1 of this code, (Ord.
99-05 § 9.48.030, 5-26-1999, eff. 6-1-1999; amd. 2010 Code)
