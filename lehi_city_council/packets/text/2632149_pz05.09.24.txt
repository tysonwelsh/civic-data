54   2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Bill Elton’s request for review of the Triple B
55        Investment Group General Plan Amendment on 15-acres of property located at
56        approximately 1630 South 500 East, changing the land use designation from ESA
57        (environmentally sensitive area) to VLDRA (very low density residential agriculture).
58   Tony Trane was representing the applicant.
59
60   Kim Struthers’s, Community Development Director, explained that the property is no longer in
61   the floodplain due to elevation changes from infill. Mr. Struthers informed the Council that the
62   applicant was granted a grading permit in 2007 to import fill on the property. The amendment
63   would affect approximately 15 acres and any development would occur north of the future road.
64
65   Chairman Jackson opened the public hearing.
66
67   Wes Davey expressed concerns about the proposed development, citing environmental and
68   traffic concerns. He also asked the Commission to consider potential impacts to the surrounding
69   areas.
70
71   Theresa Mott inquired about the purpose and extent of the development. She expressed concerns
72   with traffic congestion and potential impacts.
73
74   Nathan Luthi stated that he lives near the proposed development. He was concerned with traffic
75   implications and adding additional density to the area.
76
77   Mr. Kolan stated that he is representing the Kolan family’s interests as they own property next to
78   the proposal. His concerns addressed changes to zoning and the implications of that.
79
80   Chairman Jackson closed the public hearing.
81
82   Mr. Trane answered questions and stated that this is Very Low Density Residential and they will
83   preserve the right of way for a future road and that development will take place on the north side
84   of the future road. There have been discussions to cluster the development creating more open
85   space near the future road.
86
87   Brad Kenison, Assistant City Engineer, informed the Commission of regional traffic plans for
88   the area which included widening and extending 1900 S (Pony Express Parkway), widening
89   Pioneer Crossing, and an offshore roadway.
90
91   Mr. Struthers explained that the ESA designation no longer applied due to the elevation change
92   and noted that the infill was done legally through a grading permit and confirmed that other
93   federal requirements were met.
94

 95   Commissioner Lockhart expressed frustration with the Commission’s limited scope of decision-
 96   making and emphasized the importance of public input on future ESA decisions.
 97
 98   Commissioner Jones noted that he is inclined to recommend approval because the infill has
 99   already been placed, so the ESA designation no longer applies and the need for housing.
100
101   Commissioner Jackson stated that he believes the infill component is an important piece. It helps
102   draw a sharp line in terms of setting precedent in the area.
103
104       Motion:     Commissioner Jones moved to recommend approval of the Triple B Investment
105                   Group General Plan Amendment on 15-acres of property located at approximately
106                   1630 South 500 East, changing the land use designation from ESA
107                   (environmentally sensitive area) to VLDRA (very low density residential
108                   agriculture); including the Development Review Committee comments; with the
109                   findings stated in the staff report, and also include the findings that the infill placed
110                   from an approved grading permit was heavily weighted in the decision; and noting
111                   that any future request for a grading permit would require any applicant to go
112                   through the appropriate approval process. Commissioner Lockhart seconded the
113                   motion.
114
115       Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Jackson, Yes; Commissioner Jones, Yes; Commissioner
116       Kunze, Yes; Commissioner Lockhart, Yes; and Commissioner Roberts, Yes. The motion
117       passed unanimously.

158   2.5) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of updates to the
159        Goals, Strategies, and Timeline for Moderate Income Housing.
160   Katie Bussell presented the item. She noted that this is a minor update to the strategies. None of
161   the goals changed.
162
163   Chairman Jackson opened the public hearing.
164
165   Marshall Gary discussed the ineffectiveness of high-density housing solutions for affordability,
166   citing market-rate sales and lack of affordable options. He expressed interest in focusing on
167   transportation, economic opportunities, and infrastructure development. He advocated for
168   people-oriented places and criticized sprawl. He argued that high-density housing can be cost-
169   effective if built near existing infrastructure.
170
171   Chairman Jackson closed the public hearing.
172
173       Motion:     Commissioner Kunze moved to recommend approval of updates to the Goals,
174                   Strategies, and Timeline for Moderate Income Housing to meeting the timeline for
175                   compliance as required by the State; with the findings that this will increase
176                   affordability and is in conformance with the purposes and intent of the General Plan
177                   and its various elements; and also including Development Review Committee
178                   comments. Commissioner Jones seconded the motion.
179
180       Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Jackson, Yes; Commissioner Jones, Yes; Commissioner
181       Kunze, Yes; Commissioner Lockhart, Yes; and Commissioner Roberts, Yes. The motion
182       passed unanimously.
