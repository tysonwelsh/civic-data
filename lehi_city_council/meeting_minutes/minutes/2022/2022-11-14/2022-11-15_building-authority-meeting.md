Minutes of the Regular Session of the Lehi Local Building Authority held Tuesday, November
15, 2022, at 7:30 p.m. at the Lehi City Administration Building, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah.

Members Present:        Mark Johnson, Chairman
                        Paige Albrecht, Member
                        Mike Southwick, Member
                        Chris Condie, Member
                        Katie Koivisto, Member

Excused:                Paul Hancock, Member


Others Present: Jason Walker, City Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney, Dean Lundell,
Finance Director; Teisha Wilson, City Recorder.

1. Welcome and Roll Call
   Chairman Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all members were present except for
   Mr. Hancock who is excused.

2. Regular Agenda
   2.a) Public Hearing to allow public input regarding (A) the issuance and sale by the Local
   Building Authority of Lehi City, Utah of not more than $29,000,000 aggregate principal
   amount of lease revenue bonds, series 2022; and (B) any potential economic impact that the
   project to be financed with the proceeds of the series 2022 bonds issued under the act may have
   on the private sector; and related matters.

   Mayor Johnson opened the public hearing.

   Michelle Stallings expressed concern that the city is spending millions of dollars at a time
   when the economy is suffering, there are supply and labor shortages, interest rates are high,
   and the new building is not needed.

3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the Local Building Authority at this time, Mr.
   Southwick moved to adjourn the meeting. Ms. Condie seconded the motion. The motion
   passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:40 p.m.
