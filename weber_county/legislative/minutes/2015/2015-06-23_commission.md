F. ACTION ITEMS:
    1. ACTION ON A PRESENTATION OF THE QUARTERLY INTERIM FINANCIAL REPORT
       Scott Parke, County Comptroller, presented the interim financial report as of March 31, 2015. He noted
       that expenditures and revenues are where expected. The county budgeted for 4% sales taxes but they may
       come in closer to 5%. He explained that $18 million for the corridor preservation funds were turned over
       by the State to the county to manage. He highlighted the new computer financial system noting that the
       current accounting system is outdated. The new system will greatly streamline the county’s processes.
       Commissioner Bell moved to approve this quarterly financial report; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert; Chair Gibson – aye

    2. APPROVE THE 2015 TAX RATES
       Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor, noted that Utah is a revenue neutral state and explained the State’s
       property tax system, how tax rates are set and truth in taxation requirements. For this year the county
       received the recommended tax rates from the State Tax Commission and the county has no intention to
       increase those tax rates.
       Commissioner Bell moved to accept the 2015 tax rates; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert; Chair Gibson – aye
    3. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE OGDEN VALLEY PLANNING COMMISSION –
       RESOLUTION 29-2015
       Sean Wilkinson, County Planning Division Director, noted that the Planning Division advertised for three
       vacancies on this Commission. Two terms expire this month and one other member, whose term expires
       next June is resigning early. Nine new applications were received and one for reappointment. These are
       4-year terms expiring 6/30/2019. The commissioners noted what a difficult decision this was, that these
       were extremely qualified applicants. They expressed thanks to all those who serve.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 29-2015 appointing A Stephen Waldrip to serve 1-year,
       Jami Denise Taylor to serve a 4-year term and the reappointment of Will Haymond to a 4-year term to the
       Ogden Valley Planning Commission; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert; Chair Gibson – aye
    4. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE WESTERN WEBER PLANNING COMMISSION –
       RESOLUTION 30-2015
       Sean Wilkinson, County Planning Division Director, stated that two terms were expiring. These are 4-
       year appointments expiring 6/30/2019. Commissioner Ebert expressed appreciation to Mr. Andrew
       Favero and Mr. Ryan Judkins for their great service in this Commission.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 30-2015 appointing Michael T. Slater and Lance C.
       Greenwell to the Western Weber Planning Commission; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert; Chair Gibson – aye
    5. REQUEST FOR CONTRACT WITH WASATCH CORRECTIONAL MEDICAL SERVICES TO PROVIDE
       MEDICAL SERVICES TO INMATES

       This item was held.
    6. RESOLUTION APPOINTING TWO MEMBERS TO THE WEBER COUNTY BOUNDARY COMMISSION –
       RESOLUTION 31-2015
       Chair Gibson noted that this is a seldom used Commission. The County Commission appoints two
       members, the mayors appoint two members and the final three are chosen by the four members appointed
       by the county and cities. He recommended appointing Commissioner Gibson and Commissioner Bell.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 31-2015 appointing Commissioner Gibson and
       Commissioner Bell to the Weber County Boundary Commission; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert; Chair Gibson – aye

    2. PUBLIC HEARING TO AMEND OPERATING & CAPITAL BUDGETS OF WEBER COUNTY FOR 2015 YEAR
         Scott Parke, County Comptroller, noted that the county received new grants and authorization is needed
         to spend those funds, the manner of accounting for Redevelopment Agency monies and for corridor
