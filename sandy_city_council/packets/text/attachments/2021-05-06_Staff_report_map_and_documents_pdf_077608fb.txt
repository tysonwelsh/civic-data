DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Joi Aoki, for the Aoki Event Garden, is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to
allow for a reception center in the Industrial Zone (ID) (See Exhibit #1: Application Material).

The lot is approximately 0.89 acres (38,768 square feet) and contains two buildings. On the west
side of the property there is a pavilion which is approximately 3,600 square feet and on the east
side of the property there is an approximately 1,500 square foot warehouse building. The
applicants are proposing to use the site to host outdoor events. The warehouse building is for the
owner’s use for the purpose of storage and office work and is not included as part of the event
center.

The applicant is proposing to have the event space be rentable with no employees and all events
supply their own catering. In addition to the pavilion and landscaped outdoor space the
applicants are proposing to provide three on-site trailers. One trailer would be a restroom trailer
while the other two trailers would be airstream trailers to be used as potential changing spaces

and photo opportunities. The proposed operating hours of the business are from 10:00 AM to
11:00 PM.

The subject property is located on west side of Monroe St north of 9000 S. and south of 8600 S.

properties to the east are single family homes zoned R-1-6.

the Planning Commission meeting. A neighborhood meeting was held on March 23, 2021. Five

   1. Insufficient onsite parking
   2. Increased traffic
   3. Street parking in the surrounding neighborhood

ANALYSIS
Under Section 21-23-19 of the Sandy City Land Development Code, any commercial uses
within 250 feet where such commercial uses desire to operate after 10:00 PM and/or before 6:00
AM shall require a separate Conditional Use approval from the Planning Commission.

The property is located in the Industrial Zone but abuts a residential zone (See Exhibit #2-
Zoning Map). The applicants are proposing that the operating hours of the business be from
10:00 A.M. to 11:00 P.M. The additional hour after 10:00 P.M. requires approval from the
Planning Commission.

Under Section 21-08-02(B) of the Sandy City Land Development Code the proposed outdoor
event center is classified as a “Social or Reception Center” and requires a conditional use in the
ID zone.

The applicant is proposing to have a social event center which would operate between April and
September of each year with approximately 100 guests per event.

ACCESS AND PARKING
Vehicular access to the site is on the west side of Monroe St. There is currently only one
vehicular ingress and egress on the site with no required cross access.

There is not a specific parking standard for a social or reception center. In this particular
instance where there are no fixed seats, and based on the amount of parking stalls that are
currently on-site, staff is recommending that a limited number of guests be allowed to attend
events at this location unless the applicant can arrange for alternate parking arrangements. There
are 34 dedicated parking spaces for the proposed event center. Based on approximately three
guests per stall, the recommended limit would be 100 guests. (See Exhibit #3 – Parking Plan)

TEMPORARY ON-SITE USES
The applicant currently has three on-site trailers. One of the trailers is a restroom and the other
two are airstream trailers to be used as changing spaces and for photo opportunities. The
applicant is proposing to place all three trailers underneath the existing pavilion. The
International Building Code requires that these trailers be used on a temporary basis of a period
of up to six months a year. Until such time that the applicant constructs permanent structures on
the property the use of the trailers as restroom and changing spaces will be restricted to the six-
month time period of April through September. (See Exhibit #4- Trailer Plan)

FUTURE SITE PLANS
The applicant is proposing to construct a permanent building underneath the existing
pavilion (See Exhibit #- Proposed Future Plans). This building would contain restrooms
storage space and kitchen. Once a permanent structure is constructed it may be used year-
round.

CONCERNS
Parking. The site abuts a residential zone and has no cross access to neighboring
properties. Staff is concerned that this may result in parking in the surrounding
neighborhood.

Trailers. Staff is concerned with the use of the proposed trailers as a temporary measure.
Due to the mobile nature of a trailer, it can be moved to different areas on site thus
making it difficult to regulate an approved site design. However, the applicants have
informed staff that they own a different lot outside of Sandy City that they will store the
trailers on when they are not in use.

Building and Safety. The applicant has redlines on the proposed trailers from Building
and Safety that need to be addressed in order to allow for the use of the trailers.


STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission grant the Conditional Use request to
allow extended hours to 11:00 PM and the Social Reception Center Use for the applicant
Joi Aoki at 8694 S. Monroe St. based on this staff report, one finding, and seven
conditions.

Findings:

       1. The proposed use, with the proposed conditions of approval, should have
          limited impacts on nearby uses.
       2. The applicant is willing to limit this use to six months until such time that a
          permanent structure is built for restrooms and changing rooms.

Conditions

   1. That there be no on street parking resulting from the event center.

   2. That events be restricted to no more than 100 persons.

   3. That the applicant addresses all building redlines for the proposed trailers.

   4. That an affidavit be signed by the applicant acknowledging the temporary nature
   of the trailers and the restrictions they impose.

   5. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.

   6. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City
   Development Code (or as approved by the Planning Commission), and all conditions
   of approval imposed by the Planning Commission.

   7. That this Conditional Use Permit be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.
