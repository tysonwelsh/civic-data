NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Mtg—April 10th @ 7pm.

       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          Council Chair Bynum read an email from South Salt Lake resident, Wendi Melling,
          that was sent in by email. Ms. Melling called for Council Member Sanchez’s
          resignation.

              Council Member Williams read an email from South Salt Lake resident, Attila Papp,
              that was sent in by email. Mr. Papp called for Council Member Sanchez’s
              resignation.

              South Salt Lake resident, James Brown, talked about the responsibility of elected
              representatives to ensure the safety, wellbeing, and prosperity of the residents of
              South Salt Lake. He called for the recommitment to manage the community better
              and to reflect on the goodness that resides within everyone.

              South Salt Lake resident, Susan Bowlden, talked about the behavior and actions of
              Council Member Sanchez and called for his resignation.

              Granite Library Branch Manager, Kim Welch, talked about some upcoming events at
              the library.

              South Salt Lake resident, Catherine Taylor, talked about the online petition she
              started to call for Council Member Sanchez’s resignation.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.

          Resolution’ that would be discussed under ‘New Business’ later in the meeting.
