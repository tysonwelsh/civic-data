REQUEST
Mr. Chris Bick, P.E., representing Kimley-Horn and Associates, is requesting preliminary
Commercial Site Plan Review and Conditional Use Approval for a new commercial
building with a dine in and a drive-up window restaurant use, on the property located
at 10986 S. State Street.  The site is currently occupied by the Larry H. Miller Gasoline
Service Station, which will be demolished for this new project. The property is located in
Auto Mall (AM) (Commercial)zoning district.  A Conditional Use Permit is required for the
drive-up window restaurant use by the AM (Commercial) zoning district.  The proposed
3,546 square foot, single story restaurant with a drive-up window use will occupy the
entire site, including the existing surface rights leased from the Jordan and Salt Lake Canal,
owned by the Salt Lake City Public Utilities Department.  The property will be purchased
from the LHM Ownership group by the Raising Cane's Restaurant entity, located in Plano,
Texas.  (Please see the companion staff report for the Site Plan Review on this same meeting
agenda, case SPR-02-21-5997) The Planning Commission is the land-use authority for these
requested actions.

BACKGROUND
The site consists of three parcels.  The west most portion is owned by LHM Group and is part of lot
25 of the Utah Auto Mall Subdivision.  Through the center of the site is property owned by Salt Lake
City Public Utilities Department, which is under lease for surface rights to LHM Group.  The third
parcel is owned by the LHM Group but is not in a subdivision.  This site has existed in this
configuration since 1996. All streetscape improvements are existing from the prior service station
development and a UDOT widening project along State Street.  The existing zoning, (AM)
Commercial, requires that Site Plan and Drive-up window restaurant use approvals be by the
Planning Commission.

Staff has been working with the Raising Cane's project team since November 2020 to arrive at the
current site plan.  The initial site plan proposal showed the drive-up window queuing lanes located
between the restaurant building and State Street.  Staff suggested that because the development
site is so large, that they look at putting the drive-up window queuing lanes behind the building and
place outside dining between the building and the street, which has been done.  After many
iterations of the site plan, the current plan was filed in February 2021 for review by the Planning
Commission

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting.  A neighborhood meeting was not held for this project
due to the lack of residential zoning in the area.


CONDITIONAL USE ANALYSIS
Conditional Use consideration for a Drive-up Window Restaurant Use in the Auto Mall
(Commercial) Zoning District.

Under the Sandy City Development Code, the Planning Commission, through the Conditional
Use process, can: approve Conditional Uses if no mitigation of reasonably anticipated
detrimental impacts is necessary; approve Conditional Uses, subject to reasonable conditions
designed to mitigate or eliminate reasonably anticipated detrimental impacts; or deny
Conditional Uses, if it is determined that reasonably anticipated detrimental impacts cannot be
adequately mitigated.  The burden of mitigating these impacts or effects is the responsibility of
the applicant and not of the municipality or adjacent property owners (Section 21-33-04).

Compliance with Section 21-33-04 Conditional Use Permit:
In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City may impose conditions
that address these standards:

1.     Size, configuration and location of the site and the proposed site plan layout.

The layout is properly designed and located.  The site is adequately sized for the proposed new
buildings.

2.     Proposed site ingress and egress to existing and proposed roads and streets.

The ingress and egress to and from the site will be adequate for use by these facilities.

3.     The adequacy, provision, relocation or protection of public facilities and amenities,
including roads and street, culinary water, secondary water, sanitary sewer, storm drainage, public

All these public facilities either presently exist at the site and are functioning or are proposed to

customer vehicle space designed into the drive-up restaurant queuing lanes.

The building design, materials, and colors meet the zoning requirements and the Sandy City
Architectural Design Standards for commercial buildings.

7.     The location and design of all site features, including proposed signage, lighting, and refuse
collection.

This standard will be met. Site signage is not included in this review but must be by separate
application.  A double dumpster enclosure is being installed on this site.


8.     The provision of useable open space, public features, and recreational amenities.

This standard is not applicable.

The site landscaping will be installed as part of this site plan approval as shown on the companion
Site Plan Review Staff Report.  This standard will be met.
