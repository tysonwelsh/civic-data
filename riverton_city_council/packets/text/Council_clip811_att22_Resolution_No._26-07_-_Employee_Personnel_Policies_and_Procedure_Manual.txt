                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-07

     A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO THE RIVERTON CITY
             PERSONNEL POLICIES AND PROCEDURES MANUAL

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual (the “Manual”)
is used to set standards for employment operations, and define employee benefits; and

      WHEREAS, the Manual is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in law or in
employment practices arise; and

       WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the manual and determined some terms need to be
updated to improve the standards in the Manual to clarify the appropriate date for the Juneteenth
Holiday, and to update policies which authorize donation of accrued leave to other employees; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment to the
Manual as proposed by City staff would achieve the purposes herein described;; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City and City
employees to adopt revisions to the Manual which clarify the appropriate date for the Juneteenth
Holiday and update the accrued leave donation policy;


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual, section 5.5 Holiday Leave
          policy, is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution.

       2. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual, section 5.15 Catastrophic
          Leave, is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit B of this Resolution.

       3. The City Recorder is hereby directed to incorporate the amendments approved by sections
          1. and 2. of this Resolution into the Riverton City Policies and Procedures Manual and
          republish the same.

       4. The Mayor is hereby directed to ensure that the Manual is accordingly revised and made
          available to all City employees within a reasonable time from adoption of this Resolution.

       5. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
