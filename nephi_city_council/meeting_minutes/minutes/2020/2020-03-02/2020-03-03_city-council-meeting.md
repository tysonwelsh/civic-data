PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

RECREATION VOLUNTEER RECOGNITION PLAQUES PRESENTED:

   Recreation Manager Craig Oswald presented Jodi Sipple and Heather Ballow with plaques to honor
them for their years of volunteer service to the recreation department. Ms. Sipple was instrumental in
starting the adaptive sports programs in Nephi. She state that she has worked seven years in the
program, and emphasized the need for more volunteers to help run the program. Ms. Ballow has
worked with Nephi Recreation as a coach and volunteer for the past eight years.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes of
the regular council meeting held on 2-18-20, work session minutes 2-18-20, work session minutes 2-25-
20, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 3-3-20, the sale of two burial rights to Roy and Eileen
Jenkins, four burial rights to Samuel and Margie Memmott, and two burial rights to Karl and Susan Pew.
Councilor Memmott moved to approve the consent agenda. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

   Councilor Jones reviewed four business license applications for the following businesses: Canyon
Pizza Co., WaspFit, Mountain West Logistics LLC, and Quantitive Solutions. Cameron Tooker owner of
the Canyon Pizza Co. addressed the council about how his business would offer digital ordering.
Councilor Jones moved to approve the business licenses for Canyon Pizza Co, WaspFit, and Quantitive
Solutions. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
   Councilor Jones moved to approve the business license for Mountain West Logistics LLC. Councilor
Memmott abstained from voting explaining that his brother-in-law was the owner of the business.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed with all other councilors voting in the
affirmative.

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson informed the council that the new owner of the North Ridge Estates
Subdivision property had consented to the development agreement. Councilor Memmott questioned
item 22 of the agreement and made sure that this only applied to phase 1 of the development.
Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the mayor to sign the agreement. Councilor Seely seconded
the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 03-03-2020 -RV PARK AMENDMENTS DECLINED:

  Mr. Atkinson reported to the council that the Planning Commission’s recommendation about the RV
park road surfacing issue was to remove the requirement for an RV Park to have hard surfaced road (i.e.
asphalt or concrete) and to adopt Ordinance 03-03-2020 that describes the requirements to maintain
the road system within a RV park. The ordinance deals more with the road condition than road type. A
discussion ensued about how Ordinance 03-03-2020 would leave the code too open to possible future
RV parks on what type of road they would be required to build. Councilor Worwood moved to adopt
Ordinance 03-03-2020: AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 10 CHAPTER 15 OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE.
Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion failed on the following vote:

                                Nay: Justin Seely, Nathan Memmott, Larry Ostler
                                Yes: Skip Worwood, Kent Jones

   After the vote the council agreed to have the Planning Commission revisit the subject of hard
surfaced road definition and to return to the council with a recommended list of road types that would
be acceptable as hard surfaced roads within a RV park.

C-PACE RESOLUTION 03-03-2020 AND AGREEMENT ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the request from the owners of the hotel development
project, to designate their property as an energy assessment area and part of a statewide district called
a Commercial Property Assessed Clean Energy (C-PACE). By designating the property as an energy
assessment area, the owner has access to private capital to invest in energy efficiency in commercial
structures.
   Mr. Atkinson went on to state that the obligations of the city are minor. The city would designate, by
resolution, a property as an energy assessment area and allow the property owner to levy a voluntary
energy assessment to pay back the upfront capital financing. The third-party lender can be designated
to in charge of collecting the assessment. This would allow the property owner to seek private funding
for energy efficient improvements.
   Councilor Ostler moved to adopt Resolution 03-03-2020: A RESOLUTION DESIGNATING PROPERTY
WITHIN THE CITY AS AN ERGY ASSESSMENT AREA. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion
passed on unanimous vote.
     Councilor Ostler moved to authorize the mayor to sign the C-SPACE Governing Body Participation
Agreement with the Governor’s Office of Energy Development. Councilor Jones seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
