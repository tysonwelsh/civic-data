#2 Hollow Ridge RV Campground Conditional Use Permit 
Watkins reviewed the staff report for the Hollow Ridge RV Campground Conditional Use Permit (CUP). 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the condition regarding responsiveness, the impacts of the shooting 
range, the water tank and water rights, the sewer truck, and the use of propane or wood for fires. 
Nate Whittaker as the applicant commented on the fence and what the intent is for the fence. 
Christensen commented that it was for helping contain pets, little kids, and trash. 
Mr. Whittaker commented on the fence as a safety issue for fire and on whether there needs to be a 
paved road vs. a treated gravel road. 
Sands asked about the fencing. 
Mr. Whittaker stated there is a 3 or 4 strand wire fence in place all ready around the entire property. 
Lee asked if the fencing was on the property. 
Mr. Whittaker stated it is along the property lines. 
Jenny Orme commented that Smithfield’s mayor and city council were against this proposal, there are 
ways around the maximum 30-day stay requirement, not wanting this in the County and if approved the 
precedent it sets, the fire risk the 65 fire rings present to the surrounding area, the traffic study is 
irrelevant because it’s the winter not summer, and concerns with having someone present during evening 
hours. 
Tom Johnson commented against the proposal and used examples from the County Ordinance and State 
law he felt supported that position.  
Muly Miller commented against the proposal with concerns regarding the proximity to the homes in the 
area and concerns regarding sex offenders staying at the site, concerns with the chemicals used to spray 
the road, and how the public access Hyde Park requested affects access. 
Darrell Benson commented against the proposal with concerns for sewer disposal and structural fire 
concerns. 
Diana Round asked that the rationale regarding consistency for the area be used for this application. 
Mr. Whittaker commented regarding the Smithfield City letter, the traffic study, the shooting range, and 
the dump pit for sewer not being needed as Honey Bucket can service each trailer directly. 
Commissioners discussed the water tank/fire suppression for a potential structural fire. 
Hammer commented on fire suppression for structural fires.

Spackman asked about fire spreading to other units. 
Hammer stated that usually in these situations fire doesn’t spread to other units. 
Sands asked about the necessity of a water or fire extinguisher at every site. 
Hammer commented that it depends on conditions. 
Daugs commented that usually in drier conditions in the area, restrictions are typically put in place. 
Hammer responded yes, in drought conditions fire restrictions are implemented. 
Christensen asked about the traffic study requirements. 
Lance Anderson commented that typically during traffic studies other people do some of the counts and 
he did the actual traffic study. 
Christensen asked about the shooting and hunting. 
Mr. Anderson commented that the 600 yards is the hunting laws and Ponds could come and do 
something where they own the land. 
Commissioners discussed the water, the road, fencing, and someone being onsite for response after 3 
pm. 
Watterson motioned to approve the Hollow Ridge RV Campground Conditional Use Permit with the 15 
conditions, 2 conclusions, and the addition to condition 2 regarding complaints for the campground rules 
and the addition to condition 7 regarding fencing and signage; Daugs seconded; Passed 6, 1 (Spackman 
voted nay).
