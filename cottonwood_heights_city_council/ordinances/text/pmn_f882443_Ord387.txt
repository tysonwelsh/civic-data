COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS
ORDINANCE NO. 387
AN ORDINANCE VACATING AND CONSOLIDATING WITH OTHER
CITY-OWNED REALTY A PARCEL OF PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAY
(3000 EaAST—PUBLIC WORKS YARD ADDITION)

WHEREAS, the city of Cottonwood Heights, Utah {the “City’) owns fee simple title to
two parcels of real property (the “Public Works Parcels”) located on the East side of 3000 East
street at approximately 6579 South, which together comprise the realty housing the City’s public
works facility and related improvement (the “Public Works Facility”), and

WHEREAS, the Public Works Parcels are particularly described on attached Exhibit “A”;
and

WHEREAS, the City’s city council (the “Councif’) met in regular session on 16 August
2022 to consider, among other things, (a) vacating a parcel of public-right-of-way consisting of a
portion (the “Vacated Parcel’) of the unpaved realty lying between the pavement of 3000 East
street (“3000 East’) and the Public Works Parcels, and (b) consolidating the Vacated Parcel with
the Public Works Parcels to create two surviving parcels (the “Consolidated Parcels”) consisting
of the current Public Works Parcels augmented by applicable portions of the Vacated Parcel; and

WHEREAS, the Vacated Parcel is shown and is particularly described on attached Exhibit
“B”; and

WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. §72-3-104(4) and related Utah law provides that the
governing body of a Utah municipality exercises sole jurisdiction over all Class “C” (city) roads
within such municipality; and

WHEREAS, 3000 East is a Class “C” (city) road under the Council’s sole jurisdiction,
with the Council therefore being empowered to vacate or narrow the 3000 East right-of-way and
to consolidate the vacated portion with adjoining realty owned by the City; and

WHEREAS, City ordinances authorize conveyance of City realty to City or any other
public entity without consideration if the Council determines that the transfer is for a public use
which benefits the City; and

WHEREAS, after due consideration, the Council has determined that good cause exists
for (a) the vacation as public right-of-way of the Vacated Parcel, and (b) consolidation of the
Vacated Parcel with the Public Works Parcels in order to create the Consolidated Parcels to
materially enhance the utility of the Consolidated Parcels for beneficial public use;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the city council of the city of Cottonwood
Heights as follows:

Section 1. Vacation. Any and all interests of the City in the Vacated Parcel as part of
the public right-of-way for 3000 East are hereby vacated, based on the Council’s findings that
good cause exists for such vacation; that neither the public interest nor any person will be
materially injured by such vacation; that vacation of the Vacated Parcel and subsequent
consolidation of the Vacated Parcel with the current Public Works Parcels to create the
Consolidated Parcels will materially enhance the utility of the Consolidated Parcels for beneficial
public use; and that such enhancement of public use provides adequate justification and
appropriate compensation for such vacation.

Section 2. Consolidation. Pursuant to UTAH CODE ANN. 10-9a-103(65)(c), the
Vacated Parcel is hereby consolidated with the Public Works Parcels to create the Consolidated
Parcels described on attached Exhibit “C”.

Section 3. Notices and Filings. The City promptly shall cause this ordinance to be
recorded in the official records of the Recorder of Salt Lake County, Utah as required by UTAH
CODE ANN. §10-2-609.5(4)(b).

Where the city’s interest in a street is transferred to another public entity for a public use which
benefits the city, the city council may determine that the benefit to the city from such use is
adequate compensation for the land

Section 4. Action of Officers. All actions of the officers, agents and employees of the
City that are in conformity with the purpose and intent of this Ordinance, whether taken before or
after the adoption hereof, are hereby ratified, confirmed and approved.

Section 5. Severability. All parts of this Ordinance are severable, and if any section,
paragraph, clause or provision of this Ordinance shall, for any reason, be held to be invalid or
unenforceable, the invalidity or unenforceability of any such section, paragraph, clause or
provision shall not affect the remaining sections, paragraphs, clauses or provisions of this
Ordinance.

Section 6. Repealer. All ordinances or parts thereof in conflict with this Ordinance
are, to the extent of such conflict, hereby repealed.

Section 7. Effective Date. This Ordinance, assigned no. 387, shall take immediate
effect as soon as it shall be published or posted as required by law and deposited and recorded in
the office of the City’s Recorder, or such later date as may be required by Utah statute.
