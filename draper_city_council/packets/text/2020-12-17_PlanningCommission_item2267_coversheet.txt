Re: Windsor Mill Townhomes – Final Subdivision Plat Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Final Subdivision Plat for approximately 4.789 acres located at approximately 820 W 11450 S. The property is currently zoned RM1, CN, and RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Final Subdivision Plat be approved to allow for the development of the currently vacant site into 30 townhome units and a 1 acre parcel for commercial use. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  The final plat is in conformity with the requirements of this title, other applicable ordinances, and reasonable conditions have been placed on the approval.
2.  The proposed development conforms to the general aesthetic and physical development of the area.
3.  The public services in the area are adequate to support the subject development. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 12/10/2020 Cover Memo
