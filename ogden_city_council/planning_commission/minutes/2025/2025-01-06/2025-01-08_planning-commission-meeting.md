2. Final Group Dwelling Amendment

Petitioner Selvoy Fillerup stated that they have 68 residential buildings, a commercial building
and a mixed-use building. The purchaser of the residential buildings had made some changes to their
exterior, so in making sure everything stays aesthetically cohesive, this petition is for an exterior
update to the commercial building.

Planning Director Braton Brierley noted that the project already has a group dwelling approval.
He acknowledged the request today was to update the exterior of the commercial building and
showed slides of what it looks like currently, stating the changes would have a lot of the same metal
elements, brick and glass on the store front, adding some lap siding and style change that is similar to
the changes made to the residential buildings and recommended approval of the proposed building
elevation Amendment.

COMMISSIONER SHALE MOVED TO APPROVE THE FINAL GROUP DWELLING AMENDMENT. MOTION
WAS SECONDED BY COMMISSIONER AABERG AND PASSED UPON BY THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL

3. Subdivision and Subdivision Amendment

a. Ogden Bend (3 lots) Subdivision, at approximately 1802 Wall and 1863 Lincoln

Petitioner Grant Wise was online with the Lotus Riverwalk Phase 3 site plan that was previously
approved and wanted to thank everyone for their consideration of the subdivision plat moving forward.

Planning Director Barton Brierley addressed the request as a two-part proposal for the same
property, one being for a 3-lot subdivision and two, for an amendment to that subdivision. Slides of the
vacant property were shown as well as the approved site plan. The subdivision would create 3 lots and
a parcel which would be the Ogden River. The Amendment would create the individual footprint parcels
for the townhomes to be individually sold which is consistent with the approval of the site plan. So,
staff is recommending approval with some conditions of, a geotechnical soil report, a Financial
Guarantee, and satisfying engineering and legal requirements.

4.Plan Ogden, review input from scenarios workshops

Consultant Christie Oostema Brown stated they as a group had made a commitment to
provide research and information to the public and have been working on more graphics behind the
scenes. They build their vision from public feedback through workshops, open houses and social
networking and ask questions that will lead to what is the best approach to downtown for Ogden.
Demographics are important and they are getting a good cross section of age and ethnicity with

more student housing, more land use, etc. and they consider all of this as to best enhance a vibrant
downtown. She then read a Vision/Principles draft that acknowledged Ogden as the heart of
welcoming people and highlighted our diverse culture with entertainment, businesses and residential
