SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SMI-12-2022 Petition by FRED COX requesting minor subdivision approval for the Moody
            Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 9 lots on 10 acres. The
            subdivision is located at 6780 west SR 201 North Frontage Road and is zoned
            M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
            steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:     Steve Lehman presented the application. He explained that this request is for a
                nine-lot subdivision. Steve stated that the existing building would be on lot 1 of
                the proposed development.

                He pointed out that the proposed drive is a dedicated right-of-way and would
                connect to North Frontage Road. Steve said that, as proposed, the drive
                connects to North Frontage Road at an angle and would not meet city
                engineering standards. He noted that the City’s Engineering and Public Works
                 Departments would discuss any allowances or provisions regarding this
                 connection.

                 Steve noted that the existing building sits along the boundary of the new right-
                 of-way, potentially challenging future development of the right-of-way.

                 Commissioner Winters asked if there were any proposed tenants for the
                 development. Steve said that he is not aware of any at this time. However, the
                 applicant can address that during next week’s public hearing.

SMI-13-2022      Petition by RYAN HALDER requesting minor subdivision approval for the
                 3500 South Krause Crossing Subdivision. The subdivision consists of 1 lot on
                 1.2 acres. The subdivision is located at 3500 South 6400 West and is zoned C-
                 1 (Neighborhood Commercial). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
                 steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Lehman presented the application. He explained that a few months ago,
                 Brock brought a conditional use before the commission regarding the subject
                 property. Steve said modifications and improvements would be added as part
                 of the conditional use. He noted that portions of the street would be dedicated
                 to UTA.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:38 p.m.
