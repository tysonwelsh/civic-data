                                       Meeting Minutes
                                      Work Session 4-14-20
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                        Nephi City Hall



        1. City Council members reviewed a document showing upcoming UDOT road projects in the
           area.

        2. Due to the COVID-19 situation, the City Council discussed the city’s response and the state
           of emergency ordinances they have enacted. The Council concluded that they would
           continue to look for additional information from the Governor and the state before changing
           their approach.

        3. Options for funding the Electric Substation III were considered. Direction was provided to
           staff to prepare a parameters resolution for consideration at a regular meeting.

        4. The Council was shown a plat map for an upcoming planned unit development that will be
           considered at the next regular meeting. After some discussion about the merits of the
           development, the Council instructed staff to prepare an ordinance which enacts a temporary
           land use regulation prohibiting new planned unit development applications. This ordinance
           would be considered at the next regular meeting.

        5. The staff presented an updated Anti-Drug Policy that would comply with federal pipeline
           regulations. This was in response to an inspection finding from federal and state regulators.

        6. The City Council held a brief executive session to discuss the character and competence of
           an individual.

        7. Meeting adjourned at 9:30 pm.

Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Nathan Memmott, Skip
Worwood, Kasey Wright, and Seth Atkinson.
