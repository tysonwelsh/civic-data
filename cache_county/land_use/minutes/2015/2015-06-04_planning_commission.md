#5 Crow Mountain Broadcast Facility Conditional Use Permit (Joe Chambers) 7 
 8 
Harrild reviewed Mr. Joe Chamber’s request for approval of a conditional use permit (CUP) to allow the 9 
modification of existing telecommunications towers located on Crow Mountain at approximately 7530 N 10 
Highway 91, north of Smithfield (Public Infrastructure (PI) Overlay Zone).  This request is to allow the 11 
modification of the tower height for the self supported lattice tower.  It was approved to a height of 140’ 12 
but was built to 160’.  This exhausts the allowable height expansion under the current interpretation of 13 
6409(a).  If you look at the letter of intent there are a number of modifications but most are minor and do 14 
not require the approval of the Commission.  However, any modification to height, such as this, requires a 15 
CUP.  The current FCC statute 6409(a) states that approval must be given if a tower has a height 16 
extension within 10% of their existing height or 20 feet, whichever is greater.  If the request is greater 17 
than that 10% or 20 feet the planning commission will hear but does not have to approve.  Those requests 18 
can be denied.  The guyed tower is 140’ tall as permitted.  There are also multiple antennas and a diesel 19 
generator that have been added without review or approval. These are minor modifications and will be 20 
reviewed through the administrative zoning clearance process once the CUP has been approved.  There 21 
are fault lines directly adjacent to this property, but the specific location is not known.  A geotechnical 22 
report shall be required as specified in §17.18.060.   23 
 24 
Staff and commission discussed waiving the geotechnical report.  Commissioners expressed concerns 25 
with waiving that requirement and felt it should stay.  The height of the tower was discussed.  It seems 26 
like the original condition for 140’ was approved and they deliberately built the tower to 160’.  Many 27 
commissioners do not like that the tower was built taller than what was approved.  Staff doesn’t disagree, 28 
but given that the tower and 160’ height are existing, this appeared to be the best approach to correct the 29 
violations.  There is an agreement between Mr. Davis and the current property owner that allows access 30 
across the property to the top where the towers are located.   31 
 32 
Smith motioned to approve the CUP based on the findings of fact and conditions of approval; Parker 33 
seconded; Passed 4, 0. 34
