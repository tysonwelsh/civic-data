REQUEST
Mr. Doug Meldrum of Maverik is requesting that the Planning Commission determine that
preliminary subdivision is complete for a commercial plat amending the Auto Mall Phase 1. This will
combine two existing lots into one large lot to accommodate a Maverik “C” Store.

BACKGROUND
The proposed subdivision is located within the Auto Mall Zone (AM). It is surrounded by other
commercial uses in the AM Zone to the South and to the East. To the West is the Interstate 15 Corridor,
and to the North is the South Towne Mall, including commercial pad sites in the Central Business
District (CBD) Zone.

NOTICE
Mailed notices were sent to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting. No neighborhood meeting has been held on this item as
the project is not located within 250 feet of residential uses.

ANALYSIS
The AM Zone has no minimum lot size requirement, with the stipulation that each lot can meet the
development requirements for parking, setbacks, landscaping, etc. These items will be evaluated
through the site plan review process. Buildings are limited to 40 feet in height, with an allowance of
one additional foot for each additional foot of setback beyond 30 feet, up to 70 feet tall. The proposed
Maverik will be approximately 29 feet tall.

There will be no direct access to 10600 S. from the Maverik parking lot. Instead, there will be two
points of access on the east that provide access to Holiday Park Dr. The new Maverik development
is on the agenda following this item (see site plan review item SPR-01-21-5979) so that they may
proceed through the site plan review process.

Staff has the following concern(s) with the plat:

    1. The plat does not appear to show the east property line being located behind the back of
       sidewalk. This will need to be modified to obtain final approval.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that preliminary review is complete for
the Utah Auto Mall Phase 1 Amended Subdivision located at approximately 10600 S. Holiday
Park Dr., subject to the following conditions:

1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments and redlines throughout the final
   review process and that all issues be resolved before the subdivision can be recorded.

2. That all City provisions, codes and ordinances are adhered to before final approval is given.

3. That the new development be required to proceed through the site plan review process as
   outlined in the site plan review procedures within the Sandy City Development Code.
