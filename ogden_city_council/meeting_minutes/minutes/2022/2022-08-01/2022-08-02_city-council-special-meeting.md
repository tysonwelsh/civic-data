Proposed Ordinance 2022-39 amending the zoning map to reclassify
property located at 171 Franklin Street and 162-170 25th Street from R-1-5
to CP-3 to expand the Young Ford auto dealership property
A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department regarding a proposed rezone at 171 Franklin
Street and 162-170 25" Street came before the Council for consideration. The memo stated Curtis Groft, representing the Young
Automotive Group, has filed a petition to rezone three properties to the east of the Young Ford dealership on Wall Avenue. The
properties, which include 171 Franklin Street and 162 and 170 35th Street, are currently owned by the Young Automotive Group
and are zoned R-1-5 (Low Density Residential). The petitioner is requesting these properties be rezoned to CP-3 (Regional
Commercial) to allow for the expansion of the dealership.
Chair Nadolski introduced in writing proposed Ordinance 2022-39, entitled:
“An ordinance of Ogden City, Utah, amending the zoning map of Ogden City, as adopted by Section 15-3-3 of
the Ogden Municipal Code, to reclassify as regional commercial zone (CP-3) property heretofore classified as
single-family residential zone (R-1-5), generally located at 171 Franklin Street and 162-170 35" Street, and
providing that this ordinance shall become effective immediately upon posting after final passage.”
A copy of the proposed ordinance was deposited with the City Recorder and ordered that the City Recorder have at least
one copy available for public inspection in her office during all business hours.
The proposed ordinance was then read by long title
VICE CHAIR LOPEZ MOVED TO TABLE CONSIDERATION AND ACTION ON PROPOSED ORDINANCE
2022-39 UNTIL AUGUST 9, 2022, DURING THE CITY COUNCIL MEETING TO BE HELD AT 6:00 P.M. MOTION
WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER HYER, ALL VOTING AYE.

Proposed Ordinance 2022-40 vacating a portion of Franklin Street between
Wall Avenue and Lincoln Avenue at approximately 171 Franklin Street
A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department regarding proposed vacation of a portion of
Franklin Street came before the Council for consideration. The memo stated Curtis Groft, representing the Young Automotive
Group, has filed a petition to vacate approximately 82 feet of Franklin Street west of Lincoln Avenue adjacent to 171 Franklin
Street. The petition to vacate the right-of-way is associated with a separate petition to rezone properties at 171 Franklin Street and
162 and 170 35" Street and the petitions are intended to allow for the expansion of the Young Ford dealership. The proposal will
address the following:
e Will provide a definite edge to development and buffering between types of uses by:
© restricting vehicle access on Franklin Street (as a requirements of the street vacation permit).
o installing the most intense screening and landscaping along the east property line.
© completing all development processes before expanding.
e Will reflect the prevailing character of the overall district by expanding the rezone to the vacated Franklin
Street right-of-way.
e Will not cut across lots or developments by completing the vacation of Franklin Street adjacent to the property
and combining all of the parcels associated with the car dealership.
e Is supported in the Jefferson Community Plan and the Wall Avenue Corridor Plan.
Chair Nadolski introduced in writing proposed Ordinance 2022-40, entitled:
“An ordinance of Ogden City, Utah vacating a portion of the Franklin Street right-of-way between Wall
Avenue and Lincoln Avenue along 171 East Franklin Street; quit claiming the property within the vacated
portion of said street to the abutting property owners as their interests may appear; reserving a right-of-way for
all utilities that may now exist in said portion of such street; and directing the City Recorder to record this
ordinance in the office of the County Recorder; and providing that this ordinance shall become effective
immediately upon posting after final passage.”
A copy of the proposed ordinance was deposited with the City Recorder and ordered that the City Recorder have at least
one copy available for public inspection in her office during all business hours.
The proposed ordinance was then read by long title
VICE CHAIR LOPEZ MOVED TO TABLE CONSIDERATION AND ACTION ON PROPOSED ORDINANCE
2022-40 UNTIL AUGUST 9, 2022, DURING THE CITY COUNCIL MEETING TO BE HELD AT 6:00 P.M. MOTION
WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER HYER, ALL VOTING AYE.

There being no further business to come before the Council, COUNCIL MEMBER HYER MOVED THE MEETING
ADJOURN AT 5:58 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER BLAIR, ALL VOTING AYE.
