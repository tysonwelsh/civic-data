     1. Stall of Fame: Scott Parke from Clerk/Auditor’s Office.
        Ricky Hatch (County Clerk/Auditor): I like the expression that budgets are priorities expressed in dollar
        terminology. When we talk about the priorities of the County, those are evident through how we spend
        our money. There is an extensive budgeting process we go through to obtain input from the public, the
        departments, and others so we have a budget that aligns with the strategic principles and values that we
        have in the County. Once a budget is done, 99% of the world forgets about it. But there are people in
        place that ensure the budget is complied with and that people are spending within the budget. That is not
        a popular position to be in most of the time because sometimes departments may try to spend outside of
        the approved budget. So in this month’s Stall of Fame, I want to thank ‘bad guy’ of the County, Scott
        Parke, the County Comptroller. There are several key contributions during his ten years with the
        County. He helped implement a new financial system which was a multi-year effort; he was the linchpin
        for that very complex process. He helped implement a new payroll, contract, and document management
        systems. One thing he did that will benefit the County and taxpayers for decades is proposed, refined,
