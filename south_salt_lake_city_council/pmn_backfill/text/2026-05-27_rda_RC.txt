New Business
   1. A Resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of South Salt Lake Authorizing the Execution
      of a Tax Increment Participation Agreement with Alpha Development Group, LLC, Relating to
      Development Within the Downtown SSL Housing and Transit Reinvestment Zone
      Community & Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, along with Cody
      Deeter and Bryce Baker, elected to present two projects simultaneously, the Utopia Apartments
      and aQui 2194, (New Business Items 1 & 2) explaining that the developments were always
      intended to be completed together despite differing land assembly timelines.

       Mr. Weidenhamer provided a comprehensive overview of the overarching downtown planning
       efforts, noting that the current progress represents the culmination of ten to fifteen years of
       planning. He explained that housing and transit reinvestment zone bonds issued in December
       2025 allocated $19.5 million for various development projects. He detailed that statutory
       guidelines permit the City or the Redevelopment Agency to repurpose up to eighty percent of
       the property tax increment over a fifteen-year period for qualifying projects. He further
       explained the criteria adopted by the Board in October 2024 for awarding these funds. To
       qualify for fifty percent of the tax increment, developers must provide twelve and a half percent
       affordable housing, activate twenty percent of the ground floor, and include three
       enhancements such as public art, transportation improvements, or upgraded design features.
       Developers can qualify for up to one hundred percent of the eligible eighty percent increment
       by providing additional enhancements.

       The presentation then focused on the specifics of the two proposed developments. Mr.
       Weidenhamer stated the Utopia project will feature 187 rental units, meeting the baseline
       requirement of twelve and a half percent affordable housing, along with 4,300 square feet of
       retail space and over 200 structured parking stalls.

       Director Weidenhamer concluded his presentation by discussing the project justification memos
       provided to the Mayor, Finance Director, and City Attorney. He emphasized that the assessed
       property value and subsequent tax revenues would experience a significant increase following
       the fifteen-year period. During the interim period, the projects are projected to generate
       ongoing revenues for the housing and transit reinvestment zone, the City, and the
       Redevelopment Agency.

       The Redevelopment Agency Directors had no further questions.
