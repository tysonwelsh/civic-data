Comments:

On January 12, 2023, the Planning Commission granted approval of for the Draper TOD
Fourth Amended Plat Subdivision, located at approximately 663 W. Vista Station Blvd.,
application number SUBD-86-2022. Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 17-9-070
states the following regarding the expiration of a Plat Amendment:

If the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date of approval or
construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced, such
approval shall be null and void. This time period may be extended by the Planning Commission
for up to one additional six (6) month period for good cause shown. The subdivider must
petition in writing for an extension prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year.

To date, the property owner has not recorded the subdivision plat and has not obtained a
land disturbance permit for construction of required infrastructure either. If the
extension is approved, all original conditions of approval from the January 12, 2023
approval will remain in effect. A copy of the written request for a 6 month extension, as
well as the approval letter and the staff report from January 3, 2023 are provided for
convenience. If approved, the Amended Subdivision Plat approval will be valid through
July 12, 2024.



Sample Motion for Approval – I move that we approve the extension of the Draper TOD
Fourth Amended Plat Subdivision, as requested by Connor Roberts, representing ICO
MultiFamily Holdings, LLC and Ivory Innovations, application SUBD-86-2022, based on the
finding below and the request by the applicant:

1. That the applicant has shown good cause to obtain an extension.

Sample Motion for Denial – I move that we deny the extension of the Draper TOD

MultiFamily Holdings, LLC and Ivory Innovations, application SUBD-86-2022, based on the
finding below and the request by the applicant:
1. That the applicant has not shown good cause to obtain an extension.
