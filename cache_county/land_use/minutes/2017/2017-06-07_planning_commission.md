#3 Rebound Unlimited Conditional Use Permit 1st Amendment 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for the Rebound Unlimited Conditional Use Permit (CUP) 1st 
Amendment. 
 
Staff  and Commission discussed the definition of small animals and pets. 
 
Karen Jenkins reviewed the definition of small animal pets, documents from the EPA, and the current 
operations and safety of the business. 
 
Watterson asked about the construction of the chamber. 
 
Ms. Jenkins stated the chamber is natural gas. 
 
Watterson asked about the length of time to cremate an animal. 
 
Ms. Jenkins stated that it is a 24 hour process and 5 to 7 animals a week are cremated. 
 
Gunnell asked about the time frame from when animal is received to when it is cremated. 
 
Ms. Jenkins stated that the longest an animal is in isolation is 24-48 hrs. If an onslaught of bigger 
animals was to be received, smaller animals would be placed in the freezer. 
 
Tom Bowman spoke for the surrounding neighbors about air quality, process of investigating air quality 
with information from someone other than the applicant, impacts to property values, water use and 
outflow, future planning and development for the business, spot zoning, increased traffic, impacts to the 
nearby school, and any detrimental effects for surrounding property owners. 
 
Watterson stated that the while the Planning Commission listens to concerns, it has to defer to the 
agencies for air quality and water. 
 
Ms. Jenkins responded to the concerns raised by Mr. Bowman. 
 
Mr. Bowman asked about noise impacts. 
 
Ms. Jenkins responded that there would be noise impacts outside of the building. 
 
Mark Cardell commented regarding the change in zoning, the pollutants coming from the crematorium 
and their effects, effects to property values. The neighbors have hired an attorney to help with fighting 
against the crematorium. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed air quality. 
 
Christensen asked if the crematorium would ever reach a level that would require a permit from the air 
quality control. 
 
Ms. Jenkins responded that she didn’t know. 
 
Commission discussed air quality and requirements for permits, hours of operations. 
 
Ms. Jenkins reviewed the hours of operations and how the chamber works.

Staff  and Commission discussed noticing, a condition for air quality permits, and smell. 
 
William Mackin commented that the current business is in an industrial area now and there is no smell 
emitted from the crematorium.   
 
Watterson asked about a stack. 
 
Mr. Mackin stated there will be a stack about 3 to 4 feet above the roof line and if there ever is a 
 problem it would be noticeable from that stack. 
 
Mr. Cardell commented regarding enforcement of regulations and response to problems. 
 
Tammy Pattinson asked about the process for receiving animals. 
 
Mr. Mackin responded that his staff picks up animals from veterinarians offices and occasionally 
walkins. 
 
Ms. Pattison commented regarding the noise from a crematorium burning 24 hrs, proximity of the 
school, traffic coming to and from the business and notification of residents in the city. 
 
Mr. Macking responded that there are no emissions or it would not be located at his home. 
 
Staff informed the Commission that the County is not regulating people coming to and from the business. 
 
Smith motioned to approve the Rebound Unlimited Conditional Use Permit 1
st Amendment with the 
additional condition, stated conditions, and conclusions; Gunnell seconded; Passed 4, 0.
