                                         RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                         RESOLUTION NO. 24-42

                  A RESOLUTION authorizing the issuance and sale by the City of
                  Riverton, Utah (the “City”) of its Hospital Revenue Refunding
                  Bonds, Series 2024 (Intermountain Health), in an aggregate
                  principal amount not to exceed $200,000,000 (the “Bonds”) and the
                  loan of the proceeds thereof to IHC Health Services, Inc., a Utah
                  nonprofit corporation (“Health Services”) for the purposes of (i)
                  currently refunding the outstanding City of Riverton, Utah Hospital
                  Revenue Refunding Bonds, Series 2012A (IHC Health Services,
                  Inc.), issued by the City in the original aggregate principal amount
                  of $200,000,000, and (ii) paying all or a portion of costs of issuing
                  the Bonds if deemed necessary or desirable by Health Services; said
                  Bonds and all obligations of the City in connection with such
                  transaction to be payable solely and exclusively from the revenues
                  arising from the pledge of a note of Health Services and certain of
                  its affiliates given as security for said Bonds and in no event to
                  constitute a general obligation or liability of the City or a charge
                  against its general credit or taxing powers; authorizing the execution
                  and delivery of a Loan Agreement, a Bond Trust Indenture, a Tax
                  Exemption Certificate and Agreement and a Bond Purchase
                  Agreement, in connection with such transaction; repealing all
                  ordinances, resolutions or portions thereof in conflict with the
                  provisions hereof; and related matters.



                              ***                     ***                ***


         WHEREAS, pursuant to the provisions of the Utah Industrial Facilities and Development

Act (Chapter 17 of Title 11, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended) (the “Act”), the City of

Riverton, Utah (the “City”) is authorized to issue revenue bonds and to loan the proceeds thereof

to IHC Health Services, Inc., a Utah nonprofit corporation engaged in health care services (“Health

Services”), in order to finance, refinance or provide reimbursement for the acquisition,

construction and equipping of health care facilities of Health Services located within the

jurisdiction of the City or within the jurisdiction of a city or county which has become a signatory

to an interlocal cooperation agreement with the City pursuant to the Interlocal Cooperation Act

(Chapter 13 of Title 11, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended) (the “Interlocal Cooperation

Act”); and

       WHEREAS, the City is also authorized under the Act and the Utah Refunding Bond Act,

Chapter 27 of Title 11, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended (the “Refunding Act”), to issue

revenue refunding bonds to refund revenue bonds previously issued under the Act; and

       WHEREAS, in order to achieve economies of scale and to reduce the costs of providing

health care services by Health Services, this City Council (this “Council”), at the request of Health

Services, approved at its meeting on March 20, 2007, by separate resolution, the execution and

delivery of the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement dated as of April 1, 2007 (the “Interlocal

Agreement”) with Box Elder County, Utah, Cache County, Utah, Davis County, Utah, Salt Lake

County, Utah, Summit County, Utah, Utah County, Utah, Washington County, Utah and Weber

County, Utah (collectively, the “Participants”) authorizing the City to issue its revenue bonds on

behalf of the Participants; and

       WHEREAS, on April 10, 2007, the City issued its Hospital Revenue Bonds, Series 2007A

(IHC Health Services, Inc.) (the “Series 2007A Bonds”) in an aggregate principal amount of

$200,000,000 for the purposes of financing and refinancing certain health care facilities of Health

Services; and

       WHEREAS, on May 15, 2012, the City issued its Hospital Revenue Refunding Bonds, Series

2012A (IHC Health Services, Inc.) (the “Series 2012A Bonds”) in an aggregate principal amount

of $200,000,000 for the purposes of currently refunding all outstanding Series 2007A Bonds; and

       WHEREAS, the City proposes to issue its Hospital Revenue Refunding Bonds, Series 2024

(Intermountain Health), in the aggregate principal amount not to exceed $200,000,000 (the

“Bonds”) for the purposes of (i) currently refunding all outstanding Series 2012A Bonds (including

paying any redemption premium on the Series 2012A Bonds if deemed necessary or desirable by



                                                -2-
Health Services), and (ii) paying all or a portion of costs of issuing the Bonds if deemed necessary

or desirable by Health Services (provided however, that Health Services shall be responsible for

all costs of issuing the Bonds whether or not paid from Bond proceeds); and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to the provisions of the Act, the Refunding Act and the Interlocal

Cooperation Act, the City proposes to enter into: (i) a Loan Agreement (the “Loan Agreement”)

between the City and Health Services, pursuant to which the City will loan the proceeds from the

sale of the Bonds to Health Services; (ii) a Bond Trust Indenture (the “Indenture”) between the

City and Zions Bancorporation, N.A., as trustee (the “Trustee”), pursuant to which the City will

issue the Bonds; (iii) a Tax Exemption Certificate and Agreement (the “Tax Exemption

Agreement”) among the City, the Trustee and Health Services; and (iv) a Bond Purchase

Agreement (the “Purchase Agreement”) among the City, Health Services and Wells Fargo Bank,

National Association (the “Purchaser”) providing for the sale of the Bonds by the City to the

Purchaser; and

       WHEREAS, the Indenture provides that the Bonds shall not constitute nor give rise to a

general obligation or liability of the City or be a charge against its general credit or taxing powers

and that the Bonds will be payable from and secured only by the revenues arising from the pledge

and assignment under the Indenture of a note (the “Note”) of Health Services and certain of its

affiliates issued pursuant to the provisions of the Master Trust Indenture (Amended and Restated)

dated as of November 15, 2003, as heretofore supplemented and amended among Health Services,

the other Obligated Group Members referred to therein and Zions Bancorporation, N.A., as

successor master trustee, and payable to the Trustee in the same principal amount as the aggregate

principal amount of the Bonds; and

       WHEREAS, this Council deems it necessary and advisable to authorize (i) the current

refunding of the Series 2012A Bonds (including paying any redemption premium on the Series



                                                -3-
2012A Bonds if deemed necessary or desirable by Health Services), (ii) paying all or a portion of

costs of issuing the Bonds if deemed necessary or desirable by Health Services, (iii) the issuance

and sale of the Bonds, and (iv) the execution and delivery of the Loan Agreement, the Indenture,

the Tax Exemption Agreement and the Purchase Agreement; and

       WHEREAS, there have been prepared and presented to, and are now before this Council, the

most recent drafts of the following documents:

       1.        the proposed Loan Agreement (presented to this meting in the form attached hereto

as Exhibit A);

       2.        the proposed Indenture (presented to this meeting in the form attached hereto as

Exhibit B); and

       3.        the proposed Purchase Agreement (presented to this meeting in the form attached

hereto as Exhibit C);

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RIVERTON,

UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1.      The City is authorized and directed to issue the Bonds in an aggregate

principal amount not to exceed $200,000,000 upon such terms and conditions as the Mayor of the

City or the Mayor Pro Tem (the “Mayor”) shall determine; provided, however, that the Bonds shall

initially bear interest at variable rates not exceeding a weighted average annual interest rate of

6.0% per annum subject to adjustment thereafter in accordance with the provisions of the

Indenture, but in no event exceeding the lesser of (x) 12% per annum or the maximum rate of

interest permitted by applicable law, other than Direct Purchase Bonds, Credit Facility Bonds and

Liquidity Facility Bonds (each term as defined in the Indenture), or (y) 25% per annum or the

maximum rate of interest permitted by applicable law, with respect to Direct Purchase Bonds,

Credit Facility Bonds and Liquidity Facility Bonds, shall mature not more than 35 years from their



                                                -4-
date of issue and shall be sold to the Purchaser at a discount from par not exceeding 2.0% of the

aggregate principal amount of the Bonds.

        Section 2.    The Bonds shall be issued as fully registered Bonds in denominations

authorized by the Indenture and otherwise substantially in the form thereof set forth in the

Indenture. The Bonds shall be callable prior to maturity and be subject to optional and mandatory

tender for purchase as provided in the Indenture, as executed and delivered.

        Section 3.    The Bonds shall be executed on behalf of the City with the facsimile or

manual signature of the Mayor and attested by the facsimile or manual signature of the City

Recorder of the City or authorized deputy thereof (the “City Recorder”) and shall have impressed

or imprinted thereon the official seal of the City or a facsimile thereof.

        Section 4.    The Bonds shall be secured solely and only by a pledge and assignment to

the Trustee of the Note of Health Services and the other Obligated Group Members and of all

rights of the City under the Loan Agreement (except certain rights of the City to indemnification

and payment of expenses). The Indenture specifically provides that no provision thereof or of the

Bonds shall constitute or give rise to a general obligation or liability of the City or a charge against

its general credit or taxing powers. Recourse on the Bonds shall be had only against the security

for the Bonds as provided therein and in the Loan Agreement and the Indenture. Health Services

shall be responsible for all costs of issuing the Bonds whether paid by Bond proceeds or not.

        Section 5.    The Loan Agreement shall provide for certain representations and warranties

by the City and Health Services, for certain conditions precedent to the purchase of the Bonds, for

certain affirmative and negative covenants, and for certain remedies in connection with the failure

to perform certain covenants thereunder all as approved by the Mayor as conclusively evidenced

by his execution thereof.

        Section 6.    The City hereby approves the Trustee as paying agent for the Bonds.



                                                 -5-
       Section 7.    The City is authorized and directed to execute and deliver the Loan

Agreement and the Indenture in substantially the same forms as the drafts thereof (Exhibits A and

B, hereto, respectively) which have been presented to this Council. Such instruments shall be

executed by the Mayor and attested (where required) by the City Recorder and shall be in

substantially the forms of the instruments now before this Council, with such changes therein as

may be necessary to reflect the terms of this transaction as presented to this Council and as the

Mayor shall approve, such approval to be conclusively evidenced by the Mayor’s execution of

such instruments.

       Section 8.    The sale of the Bonds to the Purchaser pursuant to the Purchase Agreement

is hereby authorized, subject to the limitations set forth in Section 1 of this Resolution, and the

Mayor is hereby authorized and directed to execute and the City Recorder is hereby authorized

and directed to countersign (if required) and deliver the Purchase Agreement on behalf of the City

in substantially the form of the draft thereof (Exhibit C hereto) which has been presented to this

Council, with such changes therein as may be necessary to reflect the terms of this transaction as

presented to this Council and as the Mayor shall approve, such approval to be conclusively

evidenced by the Mayor’s execution of the Purchase Agreement.

       Section 9.    In order to enable Bond Counsel, Chapman and Cutler LLP, Chicago, Illinois

(“Bond Counsel”), to render an opinion to the effect that the interest on the Bonds will be exempt

from federal income taxation, the City is authorized and directed to execute and deliver the Tax

Exemption Agreement to be dated the date of issuance and delivery of the Bonds. The Tax

Exemption Agreement shall be in the form prepared by Bond Counsel and shall be approved by

the Mayor executing the same, with such execution to constitute conclusive evidence of the

Mayor’s approval and of this Council’s approval of such Tax Exemption Agreement.




                                               -6-
       Section 10.   The Mayor, the City Recorder and all other officers and employees of the

City are hereby authorized, empowered and directed to do such further acts and execute and deliver

such further documents (including an escrow deposit agreement among the City, Health Services

and the trustee for the Series 2012A Bonds and any documentation necessary to facilitate the

subscription for United States Treasury Securities-State and Local Government Series, all in

connection with the current refunding of the Series 2012A Bonds) as may to them seem necessary

or desirable in order to effectuate the sale and issuance of the Bonds in accordance with this

Resolution.

      Section 11.    This Council hereby finds that, within the meaning of the Utah Municipal

Officers’ and Employees’ Ethics Act (Title 10, Chapter 3, Part 13, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as

amended), no “elected officer” or “appointed officer” of the City, or any member of this Council,

has a “substantial interest” in or is an officer, director, agent, employee, investor in, or owner of

Health Services, or has any direct or indirect pecuniary interest in, or will receive or has agreed to

receive any compensation with respect to, any contract, lease, purchase, sale, or employment made

or to be made in connection with the proposed transaction contemplated by the documents referred

to herein.

       Section 12.   It is hereby declared that all parts of this Resolution other than Section 4

hereof are severable and that if any section, paragraph, clause or provision of this Resolution other

than Section 4 hereof shall for any reason be held to be invalid or unenforceable, the invalidity or

unenforceability of any such section, paragraph, clause or provision shall not affect the remaining

provisions of this Resolution. The City intends that no general obligation or liability or charge

against the general credit or taxing power of the City shall occur by reason of the adoption of this

Resolution and the issuance of the Bonds, and the Loan Agreement shall provide for




                                                -7-
indemnification of the City against any and all liabilities incurred by the City without bad faith

arising from its capacity as issuer of the Bonds.

      Section 13.    All ordinances, resolutions and regulations or parts thereof heretofore

adopted or passed which are in conflict with any of the provisions of this Resolution are, to the

extent of such conflict, hereby repealed.

      Section 14.    This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its adoption by the

Council and its approval by the Mayor.
