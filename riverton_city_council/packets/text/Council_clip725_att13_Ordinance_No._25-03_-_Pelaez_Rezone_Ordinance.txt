                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 25-03

  AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN DESIGNATION AND ZONING FOR
    APPROXIMATELY 0.96-ACRES LOCATED AT 3516 WEST 12600 SOUTH FROM R-1
 RESIDENTIAL ZONE TO C-G (COMMERCIAL GATEWAY) ZONE. APPLICANT- DANIEL
                                PELAEZ

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to amend the Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map to Community Commercial and the Zoning Map
from RR-22 to Commercial Gateway (C-G) for the approximately 0.88 acre parcel located at 2284 West
12600 South.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map and Zoning Map
                              shall be, and hereby is, amended to reflect the described changes
                              as shown in Exhibit “A”

               Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 7th day of January
2025 by the following vote:
