1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Andrew Spencer’s request for review of the
Gerber Zone Change from A-1 to R-1-15 on 1.56 acres of land at 2295 North Center Street.
    The applicant, Andrew Spencer was present and confirmed that the general plan will conform
    to the zone change.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.


        Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to send a positive recommendation of Andrew
        Spencer’s request for review of the Gerber Zone Change from A-1 to R-1-15 on 1.56
        acres of land at 2295 North Center Street; including all DRC comments and findings
        stated in the report. Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Eyre, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

2.2) Consideration of Ross Lucas’s request for approval of the Westlake Emergency Center
site plan, a commercial development located at approximately 700 North Redwood Road.
    The applicant, Ross Lucas, was present. Commissioner Hemmert addressed a redline
    comment and whether or not it had been looked at. Ross Lucas stated that it had, and they are
    working with Engineering to fix the redline comment. Commissioner Nielsen asked that they
    consider dry landscape.

    Commissioner Nielsen noted that this item is not a public hearing item.

        Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to grant final approval of motioned the
        Westlake Emergency Center site plan, a commercial development located at
        approximately 700 North Redwood Road. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Eyre, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

2.3) Public hearing and consideration of Alpine School District’s request for conditional use
approval of a wall sign with and electronic message display for the Lehi Junior High School
located at 700 East Cedar Hollow Road.
    The applicant was not present. The Commission agreed the electronic sign needed to be
    turned off during the night as not to disturb the surrounding neighbors.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to send a positive recommendation of Alpine
        School District’s request for conditional use approval of a wall sign with and
        electronic message display for the Lehi Junior High School located at 700 East Cedar
        Hollow Road; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report, with the
        recommendation that sign is turned off during the night hours of 10:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m.
        Commissioner Hemmert seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Eyre, yes. The vote passed unanimously.


2.4) Consideration of Doug Meldrum’s request for approval of the Lehi Block Maverik site
plan located at 2211 North 1200 West in a Commercial zone.
    Commissioner Everett declared he works for the State Agency that regulates underground
    storage tanks, but no the location. The Commission did not feel this was a conflict for
    Commissioner Everett.

    The applicant, Doug Meldrum, was present. Commissioner Everett asked if the drive aisle
    was enough space for the trucks. Doug stated the truck turning is tight but works.

    Commissioner Nielsen noted that this item is not a public hearing item.

    The Commissioners discussed options for landscaping and suggested xeriscape as an option.

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to grant final approval of Doug Meldrum’s request
        for approval of the Lehi Block Maverik site plan located at 2211 North 1200 West in
        a Commercial zone; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report
        adding that the developer meet the xeriscape requirements in the code. Commissioner
        Petersen seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Eyre, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
