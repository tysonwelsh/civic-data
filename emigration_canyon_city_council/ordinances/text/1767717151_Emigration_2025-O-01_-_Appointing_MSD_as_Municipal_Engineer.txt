EMIGRATION CANYON
ORDINANCE NO. 2025-0-01 DATE: January 28, 2025

AN ORDINANCE APPOINTING THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES
DISTRICT AS MUNICIPAL ENGINEER FOR EMIGRATION CANYON

WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon (“Emigration”) is a city of the fifth class pursuant to
Utah Code § 10-2-301(2)(e); and

WHEREAS, Emigration is a member of the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(“MSD”), which provides administrative services to Emigration Canyon; and

WHEREAS, since its incorporation in 2017, the MSD has contracted with Salt Lake
County to provide engineering services to Emigration Canyon and the MSD’s other municipal
members; and

WHEREAS, the MSD has established an engineering department that includes many of
the engineers and staff from Salt Lake County’s engineering department that will provide
engineering services directly to the MSD’s municipal members, including Emigration Canyon;
and

WHEREAS, the MSD’s engineering department will become effective on January 1, 2024;
and

WHEREAS, under Section 10-3-917 of the Utah Code, third, fourth, or fifth class cities or
towns may by ordinance establish the office of the municipal engineer; and

WHEREAS, Section 17B-2a-1104(1)(b)(iv) of the Utah Code permits the MSD to provide
engineering services to its member entities; and

WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Council desires to appoint the MSD to serve as
Emigration Canyons’ municipal engineer.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:

1. Effective January 1, 2025, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-917 and Utah Code
§ 17B-2a-1104(1)(b)(iv), the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District is hereby
appointed as the municipal engineer for Emigration Canyon to fulfill all applicable duties
and responsibilities set forth in the Utah Code, Emigration Canyons’ ordinances, and all other
applicable laws and regulations.

2. The Mayor and Emigration Canyon staff are authorized to take such steps as
are necessary to implement this ordinance.
