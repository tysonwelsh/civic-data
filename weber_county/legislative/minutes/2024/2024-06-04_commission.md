D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: This is a great time of year. Everyone has their own
   perspective as to why it is great. For some it is the weather, for some it is the grass growing, for some it is taking
   care of their cows. As you know Utah acquired an NHL hockey team. 22k people applied for tickets the week it
   was announced. This week is very important as June 8th starts the Stanley Cup, the premier award for the best
   NHL team; the two teams playing are Edmonton Oilers and Florida Panthers. In 1892, Lord Stanley Preston
   came up with the idea while he was Governor General of Canada. You see the cup passed around and it has the
   names of players, coaches, and team staff. There are actually three cups: the original cup Lord Stanley presented
   to the Amateur Hockey League then to the NHL in 1926, the Presentation Cup that you’ll see given to the
   winning team, and the Replica Cup on display in the Hall of Fame.

H. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
     1. Stall of Fame: Camille Chamberlain, Clerk/Auditor’s Office
          Ricky Hatch (County Clerk/Auditor): Camille received a Bravo Award from Mona Wald for her work on
          Property Tax Relief. Weber County processes over 5k property tax relief applications through six different
          tax relief programs and they are all done by one person, Camille. She started at the front counter and her
          eye for detail led her to her current position. She treats people like she would want someone to treat her
          parents. She exemplifies world class customer service with a smile, has genuine care for others, and is
          continuously looking for ways to improve the process.
             Commissioner Froerer: The Clerk’s office does a great job serving our community. These are not easy
             programs to understand and explain to our elderly and disabled members of our community. I want to
             thank her for taking the time to understand property tax relief and for communicating in a way that is fair
             and equitable to a portion of the public that very much needs that help.
             Chair Harvey: You have always been friendly, focused, on time, and honest. Congratulations on being a
