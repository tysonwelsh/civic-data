A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
APPROVING AND ADOPTING AN AMENDMENT TO THE 2020
CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE TO ADD CEMETERY SERVICES FEES

RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (““UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body
for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. On November 26, 2019, pursuant to state law, the Council approved and adopted the
2020 Consolidated Fee Schedule for Magna.

D. On May 13, 2020 ownership and operational control of the Pleasant Green Cemetery
(the “Cemetery”’) was transferred to the municipality.

E. The Cemetery does have certain costs for services that require the payment of fees to
pay for those services, the municipality is required to adopt a fee schedule so fees can
be imposed and collected.

F. On June 9, 2020, the Council tentatively approved the Cemetery Fee Schedule
(Exhibit A) and set the public hearing to be held on June 23, 2020 at 7:00 PM to
accept input from the public regarding the Cemetery Fee Schedule.

1

G. On June 12, 2020, a notice of the public hearing and a copy of the proposed Cemetery
Fees were posted on the municipality’s website (www.magnametrotownship.org), the
Utah Public Meeting Notice website (www.utah.gov/pmn), and posted at the Webster
Center (8952 W Magna Main St Magna, UT, 84044) which fulfills the noticing
requirements under State Law.

H. The Cemetery Fees are necessary for the conduct of the business of the Cemetery.
