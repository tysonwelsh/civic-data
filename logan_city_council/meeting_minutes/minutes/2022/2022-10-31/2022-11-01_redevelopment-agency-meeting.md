WORKSHOP ITEM:

Budget Adjustment FY 2021-2022 appropriating: $500,000 additional funds for the
completion of the Center Block Project – Resolution 22-45 RDA – Kirk Jensen,
Economic Development Director

Economic Development Director Kirk Jensen addressed the Council regarding the
proposed budget adjustment for the Center Block project. Since March 2021 when we
appeared before the council and entered into a construction contract in June of 2022, we
tried to be conservative in our budget. However, with inflation, costs have increased by
roughly 33%. Tearing into old buildings, we have encountered unexpected issues
including requiring weatherproofing, structural support, totaling $500,000llars.

 Mayor Daines was able to approach potential donors and secured $2.2 million in private
donations to enhance the plaza. With the private donations, additional design features
were able to be added to improve the plaza including design fees, ($800,000). The good
news it that leaves $1.4 million dollars in excess that can help mitigate problems. Thanks
to the generous donations we are in a much better situation.

The plaza is in early construction phases, and crews are trying to ensure we are amply
covered. Mayor Daines has been working on finding additional donors. The appropriation
is $500,000 to reach the finish line.

Mayor Daines stated that she will provide additional information regarding donations at
the next council meeting.

Councilmember M. Anderson requested confirmation on the sale of the Emporium and
that the funds will go back to the RDA.

Mr. Jensen confirmed that is indeed the case.

The proposed resolution will be an action item and public hearing at the November 15,
2022, Council meeting.
