      C. Consider a request to change the General Plan from COM (Commercial) to HDR (High Density
         Residential) on approximately 8.7 acres located generally at 2400 S Pioneer Rd. The applicant is
         McGillis Investment Co & Wright Development Group and the representative is Thomas Hunt. The
         project will be known as St. George Pioneer Crossing. Case No. 2021-GPA-006 (Staff – Dan Boles)

         Dan Boles presented the following:

         Dan Boles – This property is next to Wal Mart. They are proposing to change it from commercial to
         HDR. The current zoning of the property is PD-C. We are only looking at a general plan change so
         is commercial appropriate or is high density residential. Staff has concerns, a lot of times when
         high density residential is behind commercial it can be forgotten. They would also be restricted on
         the number of units, only 50 units are allowed with one access. If they were able to secure a second
         access then they could have 132 units. The only way that they could get a second access is through
         Wal Mart. That would be hard with an already existing commercial site. Staff is not recommending
         approval.

         Thomas Hunt – We are a Utah company in northern Utah. Right now, we are doing a project in
         Washington. The University mall in the Provo area was not successful and they attributed that to not
         enough residential by the site. They redesigned the site and made it residential with commercial,
         now it is highly successful. We would leave the main frontage as commercial and then do some
         higher density residential. We know we need to meet the fire code for a second access. We are
         working with Wal Mart to get a second access. The second thing I want to note is that the projects
         we build we don’t sell. We have in house management; we don’t feel that it is a concern at all that it
         will be run down. Residential has a lot less traffic impact so we believe that by making it residential
         it will actually make less of an impact on traffic.

         Commissioner Kemp – How did you decide on the acreage?

         Thomas Hunt – We wanted enough residential, so it feels like a community, not just one building in
         the middle of commercial. We would like to do a flex use building in the front with the office space
         in front and then storage space in the rear.

         Chair Draper opened the public hearing.

         Chair Draper closed the public hearing.
