City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1650

On May 6, 2025, the Draper City Council Approved Ordinance #1650 amending the
official zoning map of Draper City for approximately 0.046 acres of property from RA2
(residential agricultural) to the RM2 (multiple family residential) zone and approving a
development agreement amendment for the property located at approximately 13040
South 1300 East within Draper City, otherwise known as the Lingo Zoning Map
Amendment and Development Agreement Amendment. The complete Ordinance is
on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at
https://www.draperutah.gov/.

Published this 5th day of May 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
