Nephi City Council Work Session 
July 26, 2022
7:00 p.m.
21 E 100 N
Nephi, UT 84648

	•	 The council reviewed a water agreement with the Ockey family 

	•	 The council reviewed the Concept Zoning Map.  Councilor Worwood pointed out that this is only a concept map and can be changed at any time.  Mr. Atkinson stated that this map can be used as a guideline for the council and the private sector when planning development.  The open market will be a big driver on how this map will be used and changed over time.

	•	 Atkinson reviewed the Raccoon Eye Annexation zoning request.  The petitioner requested the annexation be zoned industrial, but that zone doesn’t fit into the concept zoning map.  Mr. Atkinson suggested that the highway frontage area of the property be zoned Highway Commercial and the back part of the property be divided between Industrial zone 1 and light industrial zone 3. The Council were not in favor of zoning the property industrial but were favorable to light industrial in the back of the parcel.

	•	City Attorney Wright  updated the Council about the delay of the laying of the wood floors in the Hive.  The floors will not be finished until the end of September.  

	•	Mr.  Atkinson updated the council that the”no dogs allowed” signs have been installed in the cemetery.    

	•	 Mr. Atkinson updated the council about a meeting that council leadership and staff had attended with the  high power user company that is wanting to locate in the area.  They are proposing 300 employees initially and eventually reach 1000 employees. Ostler and Worwood would not like to keep pursuing the business locating here and Councilor Callaway would like to pursue it further.   Seely said that Councilor Memmott is not interested in pursuing the business and  Mayor Seely said that the level of growth that the business would bring is too fast and too much of an impact. 

	•	 The Council discussed revisiting the blight ordinance and adding more incentives to the ordinance.  

	•	 Ostler moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and competence of an individual.  Councilor Callaway seconded the motion.  The motion passed on a unanimous vote.  Meeting adjourned at  8:20 p.m.

Attendance:  Donald Ball, Corey Thomson, Mayor Justin Seely,  Councilor Skip Worwood, Councilor Larry Ostler, Councilor Jeremy Callaway, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough
Absent:  Councilor Nathan Memmott and Councilor JD Parady
