Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Hazel Dunsmore
CC:  Date: August 18, 2020
Re:  Comments: Utah Retirement Systems (URS) have requested that we update the language in our personnel policy regarding URS retirement benefits for appointed employees and elected officials.
Tier I appointed employees can exempt from retirement coverage.
Tier II appointed employees can exempt from the defined contribution vesting requirement.
Elected officials may exempt from participation in URS within 30 days of the start of their term of office.
Elected officials are exempt from the 4 year vesting requirement.
