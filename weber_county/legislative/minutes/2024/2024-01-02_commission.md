    3. APPROVE THE FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 2-15 OF THE
       WEBER COUNTY CODE.
         Lauren Shafer (Elections Director): We want to make a couple of changes to the County Campaign Finance
         ordinance. State statute currently requires four reports including the annual report, the primary report, the
         general report and the final report. We want to add two requirements and make clarifying changes to the
         Penalty section. Currently, voters do not see what candidates are spending until seven days prior to Primary
         or General election, after most voters have mailed in their ballots. We want to add a Convention report to be
         filed seven days before that candidate’s convention. We also want to add a pre-election report due seven
         days prior to ballots being mailed out for the primary election and/or general election. The clarifying
         changes in the penalty section are candidates can only be disqualified if they fail to file their primary or
         general report, which are the two that are required to be disqualified under state law, and that if they miss
         their 24-hour grace period. It also clarifies that candidates who fail to file reports cannot run for office again
         until their fine is paid with us or their statement is filed with our office.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the First Reading of an Ordinance amending Chapter 2-15 of the
         Weber County Code; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye
