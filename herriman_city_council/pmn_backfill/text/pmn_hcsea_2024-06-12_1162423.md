                                HERRIMAN CITY SAFETY
                              ENFORCEMENT AREA MINUTES
                                     Wednesday, June 12, 2024
                                     Approved August 27, 2024

The following are the minutes of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. The meeting was held on
Wednesday, June 12, 2024 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main
Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the
City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair Lorin Palmer

Trustees Present: Jared Henderson, Teddy Hodges, and Steve Shields

Trustees Excused: Sherrie Ohrn

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas, City Recorder
Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Kyle Maurer, Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette, City
Planner Michael Maloy, Unified Fire Authority Assistant Chief Anthony Widdison, Public Works Director
Justun Edwards, Community Development Director Blake Thomas, Deputy Chief of Police Cody
Stromberg, Police Chief Troy Carr, Public Utilities Engineering Manager Jonathan Bowers.

       1. Call to Order – 7:00 p.m. (or as soon as possible thereafter)
Chair Lorin Palmer called the meeting to order at 7:22 p.m.

       2. Approval of Minutes
                 2.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes
                      of June 12, 2024
Trustee Steven Shields moved to have the minutes of June 12, 2024 be prepared and distributed to each
member of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area Board. The Board will have ten days to review the
minutes and submit any changes to the secretary, if after ten days, there are no changes, the minutes will
stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes are made and the
board is in agreement. Trustee Teddy Hodges seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

       3. Public Hearings
                  3.1. Public hearing and consideration of a Resolution approving amendments to the
                       fiscal year 2024 Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area budget – Kyle Maurer,
                       Finance Director
Finance Director Kyle Maurer addressed the necessary budget amendments for the end of the fiscal year
2023-2024 Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. The primary reasoning behind the amendments was
due to the higher-than-expected tax increment revenue, which necessitated adjustments in various
agreements tied to the revenue.

Director Maurer highlighted the major amendments which included an increase in current property tax,
totalling $959,180. Of this amount, $889,200 was attributed to the property tax increase approved in August
2023 which the remainder resulted from tax receipts exceeding the Certified Tax Rate. The excess was
attributed to higher-than-expected collections by the County Assessor or increased property tax
prepayments.

Director Maurer highlighted the delinquent property tax collections. The interest income also saw an
increase which needed to be adjusted to capture actual collections. He turned the discussion to
expenditures and requested a transfer to the Capital Equipment Fund, which had been approved by the
Board during the Truth in Taxation meeting for the Herriman Police Department fleet reserve fund. Director
Maurer anticipated a fund balance in crease of $427,860 over the year, resulting from restricted fund
balance allocations as outlined in the Truth in Taxation hearing, interest earnings, and higher-than-
anticipated property tax collections.

Chair Palmer opened the Public Hearing.

There were no comments offered.

Trustee Henderson moved to close the public hearing. Trustee Shields seconded the motion, and all voted
aye.

Trustee Hodges moved to approve Resolution No R24-02 approving an amendment to the 2023-2024
Fiscal Year Budget. Trustee Henderson seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Trustee Jared Henderson                Aye
Trustee Teddy Hodges                   Aye
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn                   Absent
Trustee Steve Shields                  Aye
Chair Lorin Palmer                     Aye
The motion passed unanimously with Trustee Sherrie Ohrn being absent.

                   3.2. Public hearing and consideration of a Resolution to establish the property tax rate
                        and approve the fiscal year 2025-2026 budget for the Herriman City Safety
                        Enforcement Area – Kyle Maurer, Director of Finance and Administrative Services
Finance Director Maurer discussed the budget for police services and recommended several changes. For
fiscal year 2025, the transfer to the public safety fund was reduced by $83,440, which brought the public
safety fund balance to zero. In both fiscal years 2025 and 2026, $697,720 was to be transferred each year
to the Herriman City Capital Equipment Fund, designated for a fleet vehicle reserve program. Additionally,
for fiscal year 2026, the transfer to the public safety fund was increased by $159,240 to support both capital
and operating needs of the Herriman City Police Department. Director Maurer reported, as directed by the
City Council, new growth funds were to be restricted and tracked separately from other funds.

Director Maurer noted staff would need to revisit the fund balance and property tax projections for fiscal
year 2026, as these calculations would not be available until the following year. After previous discussions
with the Council, staff proposed that the armored vehicle originally included in the FY2026 budget would
not be funded in the final recommended budget. He planned to revisit this issue in the fall, once the final
fiscal year 2024 actuals became available. Director Maurer recommended adopting the Certified Tax Rate
as calculated by the Salt Lake County Auditor’s Office.

Chair Palmer opened the Public Hearing.

There were no comments offered.

Trustee Henderson moved to close the public hearing. Trustee Shields seconded the motion, and all voted
aye.

Trustee Henderson moved to approve Resolution No R24-03 adopting a final budget making
appropriations for the support of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area for the period beginning
July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2026, and determining the rate of tax and levying taxes upon all real and
personal property within the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. Trustee Hodges seconded the
motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Trustee Jared Henderson                Aye
Trustee Teddy Hodges                   Aye
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn                   Absent
Trustee Steve Shields                  Aye
Chair Lorin Palmer                     Aye
The motion passed unanimously with Trustee Sherrie Ohrn being absent.

      4. Adjournment
Trustee Henderson moved to adjourn the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area meeting at 7:28 p.m.
Trustee Hodges seconded the motion, and all present voted aye.
