F. PRESENTATIONS:
    1. PRESENTATION OF WEBER COUNTY HERO/HEROINE AWARDS TO BRANDON PETERSON & LESLIE MCNEELY
         Commissioner Ebert noted that as part of County Government month, the county is recognizing
         members of the community that through service and volunteerism can be considered heroes.
         Brandon Peterson was nominated by Brett Peterson, who read his nomination, and it noted that
         Brandon Peterson is the manager of two businesses that have long served the county and the attention
         and care they offer their customers are a reflection of their manager and owners’ commitment to
         honesty and ethics. Brandon Peterson has been serving on many local boards and charitable
         organizations volunteering/committing countless hours to serving Weber County. He is fair and kind
         to all. Brandon Peterson said that it has been an honor and a blessing to serve on the various boards.
         Annette Cummings read her nomination for Leslie McNeely stating that Ms. McNeely is not only
         committed to, but has a passion, for helping the youngest and most vulnerable citizens. She is an
         assistant teacher for a special education program. Last year she taught in a city/county south of
         Weber County but returned, taking a lower paying job, to teach in our county. She also teaches
         dance to underprivileged children at Marshall White Center. She is loved by the students and their
         parents, has made beautiful costumes for her dance students, pays out of her own pocket, and spends
         countless volunteer hours with students. Ms. McNeely expressed her love for our community.
         The commissioners expressed their gratitude and presented each of these heroes with a plaque.
    2. PRESENTATION FROM THE WEBER ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT PARTNERSHIP
         Douglas Larsen, Executive Director, gave a slide presentation on economic development in our
         community, noting that the goal is to increase business capacity and that a major component of that is
         transportation. He also noted that Utah is the only state to have a unified transportation plan.

    5. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO LITTLE MOUNTAIN SERVICE DISTRICT – RESOLUTION 26-2015
       Brooke Stewart, of the County Commission, stated that there were four vacancies, which were
       noticed and published according to State statute. Four applications were received. The District
       consists of seven members—four represent property owners in the service area, two represent the
       county and one represents Central Weber Sewer District. The District is recommending Kent
       Bradford, Douglas Larsen and Lance Wood for reappointment for four years and appointment of Bob
       Cunliffe to fill Chris Andersen’s vacancy.
       Mr. Bradford stated that this is an all volunteer member district. He has been on this board for a
       number of years, and it has historically been represented by Westinghouse employees, which is the
       primary user of this sewer plant and has historically covered the administrative expenses for
       operation of this sewer district, which is important to Westinghouse’s business.

       anticipated. An industrial park was established and the sewer district was a part of it, however, the
       business park did not develop much. There are only a couple of businesses that discharge into the

    6. CONTRACT WITH J-U-B ENGINEERS FOR ASSISTANCE WITH THE TECHNICAL REVISION UPDATE AND
       RE-WRITE OF THE WEBER COUNTY PORTION OF THE 2009 WASATCH FRONT REGIONAL
       MITIGATION PLAN AND CONVERT IT TO A WEBER COUNTY SPECIFIC PLAN AND SUBMIT IT TO UTAH
       STATE AND FEMA FOR APPROVAL – C2015-139

       Lance Peterson, with County Emergency Management Services, stated that in 2003 and in 2009 the
       State contractor with the Wasatch Front Regional Council wrote a Regional Mitigation Plan, which
       involved Salt Lake, Toole, Weber, Morgan and Davis counties amid protests. Now the individual
       counties are being extracted and this is for a Weber County-specific Pre-Disaster Mitigation Plan.
       The county received a grant to pay for this contract and there is an in-kind match. A Request for
       Proposals was issued and JUB was selected. The contract cost is $34,705. The county can continue
       to submit for grants through FEMA.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-139 with J-U-B Engineers for assistance
       with the technical revision update and re-write of the Weber County portion of the 2009 Wasatch
       Front Regional Mitigation Plan and convert it to a Weber County-specific plan and submit it to Utah
       State and FEMA for approval; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye
