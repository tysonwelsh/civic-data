    2. ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 2, CHAPTER 13, SECTION 7, OF THE WEBER COUNTY ORDINANCES
       RELATING TO COUNTY RECORDS ACCESS – ORDINANCE 2016-3

         David Wilson, Deputy County Attorney, presented the second reading of the ordinance noting that minor
         changes had been made for clarification.

         The two significant changes to the ordinance are the creation of the position of Chief Administrative
         Officer for Appeals and the creation of an Appeals Board.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Ordinance 2016-3 amending Title 2, Chapter 13-7, of the ordinances
         relating to the Government Records Access Management Act (GRAMA); Chair Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
G. PUBLIC COMMENTS:

    Alan Wheelwright, of Eden, is an avid cyclist and feels that the new signs (“Sharp curves, narrow road next
    four miles. Cyclists and pedestrians not recommended”) in the North Ogden Divide create a sense of
    animosity between drivers and cyclists. He said that “not recommended” to some people means the cyclists
    should not be there and angry motorists have shown animosity toward him. He asked what precedence the
    signs set, why they were put there (and not at other locations) and why no traffic study was conducted. He
    feels the signs are one-sided and recommends signs that say, “Narrow road with steep grades. Drivers,
    cyclists and pedestrians use caution. Be alert.”

    Commissioner Ebert explained that warning signs do not need a traffic study, that the particular sign was used
    because it is the current standard, and the Commission feels it is important to warn individuals of potential
    risks. The commissioners desire to make ours a bicycle-friendly community and are working on this in
    conjunction with Ogden City. He also stated that as our community becomes more attuned to active
    transportation, the overall feeling from the community should change.

    Chair Gibson concurred with Commissioner Ebert, stating that the commissioners are not trying to alienate
    any particular group, and they have a responsibility to warn citizens of potential risks. He said that in
    discussion yesterday with Chris Peterson, of UDOT, that UDOT plans to put similar road signs in the Ogden
    Canyon. He stated that these corridors are dangerous, narrow, etc.

    Mr. Wheelwright said that he had spoken with Mr. Peterson recently who had said that those signs were done
    20 years ago and that Mr. Peterson would not take them down now.
