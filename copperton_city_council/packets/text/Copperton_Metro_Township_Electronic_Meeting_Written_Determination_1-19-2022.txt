                          Electronic Meeting Written Determination
                                            of the
                                 Copperton Metro Township
                                              for
                                  Regular Council Meeting
                                        to be Held on
                                      January 19, 2022

Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-207(4), I, Mayor Sean Clayton, have determined that the
Copperton Metro Township Council will hold its regular Council meeting on January 19, 2022, at
6:30, without an anchor location due to the COVID19 pandemic and the risks it poses to the health
and safety of those persons who would be present at an anchor location. This written determination
shall expire 30 days from today.
Dated this 18th day of January 2022.


                                             _________________________________
                                             Sean Clayton, Mayor
