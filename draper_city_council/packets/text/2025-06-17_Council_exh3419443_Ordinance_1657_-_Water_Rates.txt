                                          ORDINANCE NO. 1657

           AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING UPDATED WATER RATES FOR CULINARY
                   WATER CUSTOMERS SERVICED BY DRAPER CITY.


        WHEREAS, Draper City has a water utility that services a portion of Draper City; and

        WHEREAS, there has been a significant increase in labor, supplies and water costs; and

         WHEREAS, Utah Code §10-8-22 (the “Statute”) establishes the process for fixing rates to be paid
for the use of water furnished by the City; and

        WHEREAS, the Statute states the City must establish by ordinance reasonable rates for the
services provided by the City to retails customers; and

        WHEREAS, the public has the right to participate in a public meeting addressing water rates; and

         WHEREAS, the City Council met on June 17, 2025, to discuss water rates and to hear from the
public; and

         WHEREAS, adjusting the water rates addresses the rising cost of providing water and benefits the
health, safety and welfare of Draper’s residents by ensuring the Culinary Water Fund is properly funded
and can address Draper City’s water needs for residents within its service area.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

         Section 1.       The City Council adopts the water rates as listed in Exhibit 1 attached hereto. The
rates are effective July 1, 2025.

         Section 2.        Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid, or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Ordinance, and
all sections, parts, and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

        Section 3.       Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final passage.
PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
