ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-3-2021 Petition by RUSSELL PLATT requesting a zone change from C-2 (General
         Commercial) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). The property is located at
         3995 S Redwood Rd. on 1.04 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
         steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained that the applicant is proposing an alley-loaded townhome
               development on the subject property. He said they would be 1444 sq. ft with a
               rooftop deck. In addition, Steve stated that the road along the north side of the
               property would be private and extend further than it needs for extra green space.

               Steve explained that the applicant could apply for RM zoning because the subject
               property has access and frontage off of Redwood Road. Steve stated the density for
               this type of project has typically been 12 units/ acre. However, the applicant is
               requesting a density of around 14 units/acre as the foundation still needs to be
               removed before they can build. Steve said staff feels categorizing the foundation as a
               structure is a stretch and recommends a density around 12 units/acre.

               Commissioner Woodruff asked Steve where a visitor would park and enter the unit.
               Steve said they would park in the driveway and walk to the entrance on the south
               side of the building.

               Commissioner Winters asked if the buildings that existed on the subject property
               were removed a while ago or just recently. Steve said he was not sure, but he will
               look into it. Commissioner Winters said he isn’t sure townhomes are a good fit in
               this location, but he also does not know what would fit better. He then stated that
               the color of the building is flat and asked if there is a requirement for color contrast.
               Steve said color contrast is not covered in the code. However, he can request the
               applicant add more of a contrast in color to the outside of the building. Steve

GPZ-8-2021 Petition by MOORE HOMES LC requesting a General Plan change from
           Community Use to Medium Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre) and a zone
           change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RM (Multiple Unit
           Dwelling Residential). The property is located at 7147 W Parkway Blvd. on 6.72
           acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)
           (Continued from April 28, 2021 public hearing)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained that the subject property is owned by the Church of Jesus
               Christ of Latter-day Saints, and they do not need to build a church here, so they are
               looking to sell the property. Steve said this proposal is for one, two, and four-unit
               condominiums. In addition, Steve told the Commission that there are homes that
               front Antelope Road, which runs along the south of the subject property. He then
               explained that there is a trail running along 7200 West and the staff recommends that
               with this proposal the trail be continued north.

               Steve presented two different concept plans. In the first plan, the subject property
               has access to Antelope Road, and the second does not. Steve explained a resident
               had concerns about overflow parking along Antelope Road, so the developer
               submitted the second concept plan. Steve said staff is in favor of the subject
               property having access to Antelope Road.

               Commissioner Lovato asked Steve the square footage of the proposed units and if
               there are one-bedroom units being offered. Steve said the size of the smallest unit is
               1400 square feet. He told Commissioner Lovato that he would find out the size of
               the larger units for the public hearing, and there are no one-bedroom units being
               proposed.

GPZ-9-2021 Petition by RUSSELL PLATT requesting a General Plan change from General
           Commercial to High Density Residential (12 to 20 units/acre) and a zone change
           from C-2 (General Commercial) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). The
           property is located at 2351 S Redwood Rd. on 1.81 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at
           801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained that this proposal is for a 24-unit, 2 to 3-story townhome
               development. He said there are three different unit types proposed. The applicant is
               again requesting a higher density where the buildings on the subject property were
               demolished. However, the foundation would still need to be removed to build. Staff
               recommends that the density is kept at 12 units/acre.

               Steve said one concern is with the setback requirements. He explained the buildings
               along Redwood Road are already at the minimum setback without factoring in the
               height difference. Steve said he is not concerned about this setback because these
               buildings are next to 160 feet of landscaping. The townhomes further back on the lot
               are 10 feet from the property line, so they are already deficient of the 20-foot
               minimum setback, and then you would need to include the height difference, which
               would make the minimum setback 25 to 30 feet. Steve said there is the ability to
               make some changes with the development agreement. He also passed along a
               thought that perhaps the neighboring properties to the north would like to be a part
               of this proposal.

               Commissioner Lovato is concerned with developments like these being able to keep
               the property looking nice. He asked Steve who would be responsible for
               maintaining this development. Steve explained the HOA would be responsible for
               maintaining the shared yard space.

               Commissioner Winters said he is also concerned with the 10-foot setback, and he
               asked Steve if the applicant is meeting the minimum setback without factoring in the
               height difference.
               Steve said no, they are still not meeting the requirement. He explained that this
               requirement is not usually discussed at the zone change. However, he wanted to
               mention it now because the proposal is so deficient. Steve said some revisions could
               be made to the proposal to comply with the setback requirements, and he will add
               different options in the staff report.

Discussion:    Jody Knapp explained that this subject property had come before the Commission in
               2019 for four large warehouse buildings. This proposal is for a tenant looking to
               rent the south end of building one. Jody said the applicant wants to put a commissary
               in a manufacturing zone, and they needed to come back to the Commission for a
               conditional use. Jody explained that Tovala is a company that prepares prepackaged
               food for incredibly busy people. They have a Wi-Fi smart oven that allows you to
               type in a QR code to tell the oven how to cook your prepackaged meal perfectly.

               Jody is not sure how much cooking will take place at the subject location. She said
               the kitchen area is a deferred submittal on the building permit, and she did not have
               that level of detail on the plans. Jody will provide that information next week at the
               public hearing. She said the cooking will prep for the meals. Jody said the main
               impact of the proposed commissary is potential smells. The cooking would be done
               inside the building, and the applicant will have the proper hood ventilation. Jody
               explained that the commissary would not need extra external ventilation because
               they will not be cooking with a lot of grease or oil, such as a chip manufacturing
               company.


CA-3-2021      Petition by KILGORE PROPERTIES, LLC requesting a conditional use amendment
               for a temporary batch plant. The property is located at 7057 West 2100 South on 1
               acre and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or
               jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:    Jody Knapp explained that Kilgore is looking to put in a temporary batch plant for
               approximately six months. Jody said that they are also looking to make changes to
               the east side of the site. She said this application is already a conditional use, and
               they are requesting an amendment of that conditional use.

               Jody explained the applicant would be relocating the fence along the east side of the
               property to sit on the property line. Jody explained that the frontage was not finished
               along the northeast edge of the property, and there were questions about access to
               the subject property. Currently, the approach is 170 feet, and the ordinance states it
               should be 40 ft., so they are communicating with Public Works to see how wide of
               an approach they will allow. Jody said the applicant is looking to have the batch
               plant installed in June and removed sometime in November. She explained the
               applicant did not mention what project the batch plant is for, and they need to have it
               on their property. Jody said public works would need to determine the size of the
               disturbed area. She also explained the applicant would need to add trees to the
               landscaping.
