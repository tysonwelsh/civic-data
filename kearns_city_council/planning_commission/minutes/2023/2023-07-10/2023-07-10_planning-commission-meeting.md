                                       LAND USE APPLICATION(S)
                                        Meeting began at – 5:33 p.m.

SUB2023-000806 – Kent Withers is requesting preliminary plat approval for the 7-Lot Camp Kearns
Subdivision. Acreage: 7.87 acres. Location: 5220 West Thunderbolt Drive. Zones: C-2 (Commercial), M-
1 & M-2 (Manufacturing) Zones. Planner: Jeff Miller (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jeff Miller provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant - Surveyor
Name: Kent Withers
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Withers said there are seven existing parcels, with various gaps and overlaps. Intent is to
correct that and Salt Lake County dissolve and give back portions and released back to the landowners.
Correct the right-of-way lines and fix the title errors in the block.

Commissioner Nelson motioned to open the public meeting, Commissioner Koester seconded that motion.

Speaker # 2: Citizen
Name: Patrick Lucerp
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Lucerp said he is assisting in putting together the lot lines. Kent has done a great job and
they are in favor.

Commissioner Koester motioned to close the public meeting, Commissioner Nelson seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Motion: To approve application #SUB2023-000806 Kent Withers is requesting preliminary plat approval
for the 7-Lot Camp Kearns Subdivision with staff recommendations.
        Motion by: Commissioner Koester
        2nd by: Commissioner Nelson
        Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)

Commissioners and staff discussed the three-hour training and availability.

Commissioner Nelson motioned to adjourn, Commissioner Koester seconded that motion.
