APPROVED                                            CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL
                                                               SPECIAL MEETING
                      October 15, 2022 at 8:00 a.m. - Herm’s Inn, 1435 Canyon Road, Logan, Utah 84321
 In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
appear and speak at a County Council meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinions or purported facts.
             The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


MEMBERS PRESENT: Chair Barbara Tidwell, Councilmember Gina Worthen, Councilmember Karl Ward
MEMEBERS EXCUSED: Vice Chair Paul Borup, Councilmember David Erickson, Councilmember Nolan Gunnell, Councilmember
Gordon Zilles
STAFF PRESENT: Cache County Executive David Zook, Devron Andersen County Recorder, Craig McAllister County Treasurer
Economic Development Director Shawn Milne, Karina Brown
OTHER ATTENDENCE: Dan Johnson, Scott Sandall, Casey Snider, Tom Peterson, Tim Marshall

Special Meeting

1. Call to Order –Chair Barbara Tidwell

2. DISCUSSION WITH COUNTY ELECTED OFFICERS AND LOCAL LEGISLATIVE REPRESENTATIVES
   Council and other county elected officials discussed with state local legislative representative about the needs of the county
   and upcoming legislation in the upcoming state legislative session.

  Executive Zook presented two lists of priorities titled “2023 Legislative Session Cache County Priorities,” and “Northern Utah
  Chamber Coalition 2023 Legislative Priorities (Version 10/12/22.)”

3. Adjourn – approximately 9:30 a.m.

                           Cache County Legislative Priorities




•   Justice court reform

•   Logan Canyon broadband for cell service, cameras, and electronic signs

•   Money for DMV - Cache County receives $260,000 per year to operate the OMV which
    is not adequate. Assessor Brett Robinson will provide more information on how much
    money is needed.

•   Infrastructure money for SR 101 realignment- also on NUCC priority list as South Valley
    East/West Connector, see attached

•   SR 30 full widening - also on NUCC pri.ority list, see attached
•   West Cache Corridor- also on NUCC list

•   Senior Center/Meals on Wheels

•   Public Works Needs
       1. Replace 100 year old bridge on 11400 North and Center Street on the border of
           Trenton and Lewiston. Cache County owns the bridge. Cost estimate is $7.5 to
           $9 million. These types of projects can take years. We can't wait for failure and
           then try to hurry and get it done. We need to work on funding the preliminary
           engineering/environmental work soon in hopes that in 5-10 years we can start
           construction.
       2. Funding for floodplain studies. Some areas have floodplain studies, others do
           not.
