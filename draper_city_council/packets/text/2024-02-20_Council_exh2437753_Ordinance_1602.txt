                                    ORDINANCE NO. 1602

         AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF DRAPER CITY
         FOR APPROXIMATELY 1.68 ACRES OF PROPERTY FROM CO2
         (PROFESSIONAL OFFICE) AND CR (REGIONAL COMMERCIAL) TO CR,
         LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 13673 SOUTH 100 EAST WITHIN DRAPER
         CITY, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE CHICK-FIL-A ZONING MAP
         AMENDMENT.

       WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a Zoning Ordinance and
Zoning Map to guide the orderly development and use of property within the City; and

      WHEREAS, from time to time it is necessary to review and amend the Zoning Map to keep
pace with development within the City and to ensure the provision of a variety of economic uses;
and

       WHEREAS, the proposed zone change set forth herein has been reviewed by the
Planning Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been held in
accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed revisions to the Zoning
Map; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to the
City Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Zoning Map of Draper City, and
the City Council has found the proposed zone change to be consistent with the City’s General
Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

       Section 1.     Zoning Map Amendment. The following described real property located
at approximately 13673 South 100 East within Draper City, Salt Lake County, State of Utah,
previously zoned C02 and CR as shown on the Draper City Zoning Map, as depicted in Exhibit
“A” hereto, are hereby changed and rezoned to CR:

Parcel 3406151071

Beginning at a point North 89°44’54” East 635.17 feet along the section line and North 815.36
feet from the West Quarter Corner of Section 6, Township 4 South, Range 1 East, Salt Lake Base
and Meridian and running;
        Thence North 0°09'32" East 33.54 feet;
        Thence North 89°50'28" West 16.30 feet;
        Thence North 19°09'11'' West 47.21 feet;
        Thence North 238.95 feet to the Southerly Right-of-Way Line of Bangerter Highway;
        Thence along said Southerly Right-of-Way Line the following two (2) courses; South
88"12'00" East 204.93 feet; thence South 82"56'00" East 56.89 feet;
        Thence South 251.05 feet;
        Thence West 147.83 feet;
        Thence South 97.87 feet to the Northerly Right-of-Way Line of 100 East Street:
        Thence Northwesterly 103.50 feet along the arc of a 66.75 foot radius curve to the left,
having a central angel of 88°50'24", having a long chord that bears North 61 °03'06" West 93.44
feet, along said Northerly Right-of-Way Line to the point of beginning.
