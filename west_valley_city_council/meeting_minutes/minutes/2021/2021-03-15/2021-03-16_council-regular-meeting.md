COMMENT PERIOD
A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
    Jim Vesock stated that he checked the number of parked cards on the street in his
    neighborhood. He indicated that there were 6 and if the new ordinance were passed,
    4 would be in violation Mr. Vesock stated that there needs to be more parking
    compliance officers in the City.

       Mike Rigdon stated that he agrees the parking compliance department needs more
       officers. He expressed concern about the amount of people parking on streets
       because of excess residents living in homes. Mr. Rigdon expressed concern about

       concerns and issues along this area. Mr. Rigdon indicated that this road is a hazard

RESOLUTION 21-37: APPROVE A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
("MOU") BETWEEN WEST VALLEY CITY AND THE FEDERAL BUREAU OF
INVESTIGATION (FBI) TO JOIN THE JOINT TERRORISM TASK FORCE
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 21-37 that would approve a Memorandum
of Understanding ("MOU") Between West Valley City and the Federal Bureau of Investigation
(FBI) to join the Joint Terrorism Task Force.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       In an effort to leverage the collective resources for the prevention, preemption,
       deterrence and investigation of terrorist acts that affect United States interests, and
       to disrupt and prevent terrorist acts and apprehend individuals. Law enforcement
       agencies of the greater Salt Lake Valley, State and Federal agencies have united
       efforts and resources to form the Joint Terrorism Taskforce (JTTF). The Police
    MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING MARCH 16, 2021

       Department seeks to assign one (1) investigator to this position. This agreement
       clarifies the responsibilities of participating agencies to establish the groundwork
       for the administration and operation of the taskforce.

       This task force is authorized to coordinate intelligence, investigative, and
       operational responses to various criminal, cyber and national security threats,
       attacks, and intrusions pursuant to various statutory and executive authorities.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City
Council, and he called for a motion.

D.     RESOLUTION 21-41: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO APPROVE A DELAY
       AGREEMENT WITH KILGORE COMPANIES, LLC TO DELAY
       CERTAIN IMPROVEMENTS AT 6831 WEST 5400 SOUTH
       Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 21-41 that would authorize the City
       to approve a Delay Agreement with Kilgore Companies, LLC to delay certain
       improvements at 6831 West 5400 South.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              Kilgore Companies, LLC has requested to delay the installation of off-site
              improvements at 6831 West 5400 South as required by ordinance for their
              proposed site improvements. Off-site improvements include the installation
              of curb, gutter, and sidewalk.

              Presently, there are no adjoining roadway improvements along this
              property. Postponing the construction of the required improvements would
              allow time for additional development to provide similar adjoining
              improvements and would eliminate the creation of drainage problems.

       Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of
