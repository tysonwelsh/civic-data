                                  Mr. Mike Wilcox presented information on the proposed amendments to Title 21, Chapter
                                  19, Section 34 SD (R-1-30A) Bell Canyon Acres, of the Sandy Municipal Code. He
                                  provided an overview of the history of the zoning of the area and the special district zoning
                                  created in 2009 to meet the unique needs of this area. Mr. Wilcox also reviewed the
                                  intent of the proposed amendments which apply only to this area. Council questions
                                  occurred throughout the presentation which included a discussion on requiring business
                                  licensing for those homeowners who board horses. Mr. Wilcox provided additional
                                  information regarding home occupation licensing, farm animal set aside area, and the
                                  simplification of calculations of number of animals permitted. Council Chair Robinson
                                  requested that any information sent out on this issue be sent to all the Council Members.
                                  Mr. Wilcox reviewed the next steps. The proposed amendments are scheduled to come
                                  back to the Council for consideration at the December 20, 2022 Council meeting.
