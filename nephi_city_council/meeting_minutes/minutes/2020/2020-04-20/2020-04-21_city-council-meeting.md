                                              NEPHI CITY

Guests: Myrna Trauntvein (press), Donald W. Ball, Jaida Stephenson, Teagen Christensen, JD Parady,
Justin Blackett

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City Recorder
Lisa E. Brough offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Miss Nephi Jaida Stephenson.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes of
the regular council meeting held on 4-7-20, work session minutes 4-7-20, work session minutes 4-14-
20, and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 4-21-20. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

CEDAR RIDGE TOWNHOMES PLANNED UNIT DEVELOPMENT PHASE I FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson presented to the council the final plat for Cedar Ridge Townhomes planned unit
development (PUD). Councilor Memmott moved to approve the final plat for Cedar Ridge Townhomes.
Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following vote.
                              Nay: Jones
                              Yes: Seely, Memmott, Ostler, Worwood

TEMPORARY LAND USE ORDINANCE 04-21-2020 ADOPTED.

  Mr. Atkinson presented to the council Ordinance 04-21-2020: TEMPORARY LAND USE ORDINANCE
PROHIBITING PLANNED UNIT DEVELOPMENT APPLICATIONS. The ordinance pursuant to Utah State
Code shall be in effect for a period not to exceed six months. The council agreed to have the planning
commission review the current code governing PUD and consider any revisions they might recommend.
Councilor Jones moved to adopt Ordinance 04-21-2020. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

KURTIS EVERITT RATIFIED AS SOLID WASTE COLLECTOR:

  Councilor Ostler moved to ratify the hiring of Kurtis Everitt as the new Solid Waste Collector.
Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

MAYOR NIELSON ARRIVED AT 7:35

SECOND AMENDED INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH CENTRAL UTAH 911 APPROVED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to approve the Second Amended Interlocal Agreement for Joint
Cooperative Action of Central Utah 911. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson discussed with the council the possibility of holding a regular council meeting next week
at the work session in order to take action on a parameter’s resolution for some electric revenue bonds.
He indicated the urgency in speeding up the process in order to capture the low interest bond market.
The council agreed to holding such a meeting.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

   Councilor Ostler mentioned some complaints he had received about the nuisance at Peach Tree
Trailer Park.
   Councilor Jones reported on watching the Electric Department work and he was very impressed with
their work and expertise.
  Councilor Worwood said that he contacted the Ute Stampede Committee and he said as for now the
celebration is still going to be held and has not yet been cancelled due to the pandemic.
  Mayor Nielson reported that the 4th of July Celebration at the park is still going forward and also the
Xtreme Racing event is still on for the end of June.
 Councilor Memmott reported on a new route for the high-voltage power lines that will be favorable to
Nephi City and to property owners.
Councilor Seely said that he had talked to some local businesses about how they have been affected by
the pandemic restrictions and encouraged everyone to shop locally.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character
and competence of an individual, real property acquisition and disposal. Councilor Ostler seconded the
motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 7:55 p.m.
