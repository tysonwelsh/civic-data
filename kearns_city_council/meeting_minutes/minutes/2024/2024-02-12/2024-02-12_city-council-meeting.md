Legal Updates/Information
Legislative Update

Mayor Bush asked to revisit the date of the special meeting, which was changed
to February 20, 2024 at 6:00 PM.

Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated he provided
training on the rules of order and procedure at the Planning Commission meeting last month
pursuant to Code. He will provide similar training to the Council next month on rules of order
procedure and ethics requirements. This will be helpful as it converts to a city. The Planning
Commission is discussing several land use decisions that Kearns will need to make in the coming
years.

There will be a new Interlocal Agreement draft between Kearns Metro Township and Unified
Police Department. Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA) will be removed
as a member entity. This is because the District Attorney’s Office is concerned SLVLESA’s
involvement could be misconstrued as requiring the County to make up any differences from the
General Fund.

He has been working with other metro townships on HB 35. Currently, the metro townships have
all the same obligations as a city or town but do not have the same funding authority. The bill has
passed out of the House. There was a committee hearing today, and it reported favorably. Magna
Metro Township is the only township to have expressed a desire to become a six-member form
of government, and the bill passed out of committee with the amendment to that effect. Mr.
Bracken did not anticipate this amendment would have any implications for Kearns Metro
Township. The township is expected to become a city on May 1, 2024.
