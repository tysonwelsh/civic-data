SUMMARY and BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 19.37
acres located on corner of Brookings Drive and Suncrest Drive, at 2142 East Brookings
Drive (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently zoned RR-22, RR-43, and RM. These
zoning designations correspond with the 1999 Draper City Municipal Code (1999 DCMC)
and the Development Agreement for Suncrest. The applicant is requesting to amend the
existing plat to consolidate the six parcels owned by the applicant into one large parcel. A
townhome project is proposed on the consolidated parcels.



Trailside Townhomes                                                 App. # 2023-4653-SUB
Plat Amendment                                 1
The property was originally part of the Suncrest development area and is recorded as Lots
1301-1306 of the Maple Hollow Phases 10, 11, and 13 at Suncrest plat. The Suncrest
development vested under the 1999 DCMC and properties in this area still are mapped
with zoning designations from the 1999 DCMC.

In 2015, the former property owner and Draper City entered into a Development
Agreement that removed the property from the Suncrest Development Agreement Area
and allowed the property to be developed with up to 160 townhomes. While the property
was not rezoned to a current Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) zoning designation, the
property is subject to the new Development Agreement and current zoning code
regulations.

On July 28, 2022, the Planning Commission approved a plat amendment to consolidate the
six properties into one large property. The plat was not recorded prior to the initial one
year expiration and a one time six month extension of the approval was granted by the
Planning Commission on May 11, 2023. The approval of that plat expired on January 29,
2024. The applicant has now submitted a new subdivision plat amendment application for
the essentially the same project.

        -       D103.6.1 Roads 20 to 26 feet in width. Fire lane signs as specified in Section
                D103.6 shall be posted on both sides of fire apparatus access roads that are 20
                to 26 feet wide.

              -      This section requires that parking be prohibited on both sides of
                     narrower fire apparatus access roads. Twenty feet is the appropriate
                     width needed for two average-size fire trucks to pass one another. If
                     that width is reduced by parking even on one side, it will be potentially
                     difficult for a fire department to undertake emergency operations in
                     that area.
     -      D103.6.2 Roads more than 26 feet in width. Fire lane signs as specified in Section
            D103.6 shall be posted on one side of fire apparatus access roads more than 26
            feet wide and less than 32 feet wide.

            -      Because this width is more than sufficient for maneuvering at least
                   two fire department vehicles by one another, parking would be
                   allowed on one side.
