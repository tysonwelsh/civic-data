                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-34

     A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL AUTHORIZING
     AMENDMENT TO A RIGHT-OF-WAY AGREEMENT WITH THE UTAH
 DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AND APPROVAL TO CONVEY BY QUIT
CLAIM, PERPETUAL EASEMENT, AND TEMPORARY EASEMENT THE VARIOUS
    PARCELS OWNED BY RIVERTON CITY ALONG THE REDWOOD ROAD
                            CORRIDOR.

       WHEREAS, In May of 2017, the Riverton City Council adopted Resolution No. 17-21,
authorizing execution of a Right of Way Agreement with UDOT, whereby the City would
convey interests in several parcels of property, by quit claim deed or easement; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to the Agreement approved by Resolution No. 17-21 the Utah
Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) agreed to install infrastructure improvements of benefit
to Riverton City along the Redwood Road Corridor in exchange for the additional right-of-way
provided by Riverton; and

      WHEREAS, UDOT has fully and satisfactorily performed its obligations under the Right
of Way Agreement; and

       WHEREAS, following execution of the Agreement, the parties identified a mistake in
understanding which parcels the City was entitled to convey to UDOT; and

       WHEREAS, through counsel, the parties have negotiated a means to resolve this
mistake, under the terms of an Addendum to the Right of Way Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council does hereby determine that it is in the best
interests of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to approve said
Addendum to Right of Way Agreement and execute the same.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

       1.      The Addendum to Right-of-Way Agreement between Riverton City and UDOT is
               hereby approved, and the Mayor is authorized to execute said agreement upon
               approval as to form by the Riverton City Attorney.

       2.      Upon execution of the Addendum to Right-of-Way Agreement, the Mayor is
               authorized to approve and the City Recorder is authorized to record the quit
               claims, perpetual easements, temporary easements and other documents necessary
               to fulfill said Agreement.
