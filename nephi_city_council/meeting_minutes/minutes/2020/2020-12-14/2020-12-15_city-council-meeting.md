                                                NEPHI CITY
                                             COUNCIL MEETING
                                             December 15, 2020

The Nephi City Council met in regular session in the council chambers of city hall located at 21 East 100
North at 7:00 p.m. Tuesday, December 15, 2020.
Those present were:

                Council Member . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Larry Ostler (Absent)

Guests: Myrna Trauntvein (Press), Donald W. Ball, Gerratt Bethers, Craig Oswald, JD Parady, Ali Painter,
Myrna Trauntvein, Jim McWilliam, Paul McPherson, David Phillips, Dominic Davidson, Boden Ballow,
Bodee Blackett, Mike Bowring, and Trey Lund.

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then Mayor
Nielson offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  Coach Mike Bowring and team captains from the State Champion Juab High School football team led
the pledge of allegiance.

PROCLAMATION - HONORING THE JUAB HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL TEAM:

  Mayor Nielson read a proclamation honoring the Juab High School football team who had recently
won the first ever state championship in the high school’s history. Each councilor congratulated the
team on this achievement. Coach Bowring thanked the council and the community for their support
over the years.

PROCLAMATION - ARBOR DAY

   Mayor Nielson read a proclamation proclaiming retroactively April 26, 2020, as Arbor Day in Nephi
City, and urged residents to celebrate Arbor Day every year and to support efforts to protect trees and
woodlands.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

   Councilor Memmott moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes for the regular council meeting held on 12-1-20, work session held on 12-1-20, sale of a burial
rights to Donald J. Eyre Jr. and Marcia M. Eyre and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 12-11-20
Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

ORDINANCE 12-15-2020 -PLANNED UNIT DEVELOPMENT CODE ADOPTED:

   Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 12-15-2020 : AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 10
CHAPTER 12 AND TITLE 10 CHAPTER 16 SECTION 8 (C) (2) OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE. Councilor Seely
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ANNEXATION AREA MAP REQUESTS:

 Two property owners, west of the city, requested to be included as a Level 3 property in the city’s
annexation area map. After council discussion, the Council decided to table the issue and seek further
                                                                                              Page 1 of 3
information from the Planning Commission and the staff. This item will be brought back before the
council on January 5th. Councilor Seely moved to table these requests until further study has been
done. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

FOUR MILE ANNEXATION PETITION:

  An annexation petition was presented to the Council for action. Councilor Jones moved to adopt the
Four Mile Annexation petition with Councilor Worwood seconding. After Council discussion regarding
which properties would be desirable to be annexed, Councilor Jones withdrew his motion. Councilor
Memmott made a motion to table the annexation for further study. Councilor Seely seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

COMMUNITY IMPACT BOARD GRANT:

   The staff recently submitted for a grant from the Community Impact Board (CIB)to update the city’s
General Plan. The CIB has granted the city funds for the General Plan update and requires the Mayor to
sign a contract for those funds. Councilor Seely moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the Community
Impact Board contract. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

ANNUAL MEETING SCHEDULE

The annual meeting scheduled for calendar year 2021 was presented to the council. Councilor
Worwood moved to adopt the annual meeting schedule for 2021. Councilor Jones seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION 12-15-2020 - SIX COUNTY HAZARD MITIGATION PLAN ADOPTION

   Councilor Memmott made a motion to adopt Resolution 12-15-2020 - Six County Hazard Mitigation
Plan. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

GARRETT LEASE AGREEMENT AMENDMENT

   Lease amendments were proposed to the City Council to an original lease agreement with Blake
Garrett. These amendments set a new term, identify an updated list of leased properties, and update
the terms for legal procedures. Councilor Memmott made the motion to accept the terms in the
amendment to the original agreement with Blake Garrett. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion.
Councilor Jones recused himself from the vote. Aside from Councilor Jones, the motion passed with a
unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION 12-15-2020-A - ENTERPRISE ZONE DESIGNATION

   Resolution 12-15-2020-A was presented to the council for approval. This resolution would designate
the property within the city limits as an enterprise zone. This would then allow businesses locating to
Nephi City to be eligible for state tax credits. Councilor Worwood made a motion to approve Resolution
12-15-2020-A. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed with a unanimous vote.

CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson mentioned that he would send out a program to the Council that was developed by the
airport engineering consultants for review. This program is for entities the federal government deems
disadvantaged or disadvantaged business enterprises (DBEs). The DBE program is required by the FAA
as part of their grant program.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Memmott moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session
to discuss the character and competency of an individual, litigation, and real property. Councilor Seely
seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 9:00 p.m.
