       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood shared some City highlights:

              South Salt Lake resident, Ann Marie, heard about the Adopt-a-Storm-Drain program
              and adopted 41 storm drains and is using that to educate her neighbors about
              keeping storms drains clean and free of debris.

              The City’s Promise Housing Navigator, Rebecca Yund, came across a couple of
              opportunities and wanted to share them with the Council. The first is a state grant
                                                   2
South Salt Lake – Regular Council Meeting                                            July 31, 2024


           called the ‘Utah Homeowners Assistance Fund’, that’s time limited and has limited
           funding. It's a program to assist homeowners that have been impacted by COVID-19
           and helps people that may be delinquent on their mortgage, property taxes,
           insurance premiums, HOA fees, etc. A person can receive up to $50,000 and the
           income cut off is 150% of the area median income (around $170,000 income for a
           family of four).

           The other program is a critical home repair program that covers up to $18,000 in
           home repairs such as leaking roofs, obsolete wiring, plumbing, window insulation,
           and helps people to stay in their homes and for them to be habitable. The income
           cut off is up to 100% of the area median income (around $115,000 income for a
           household of four). Residents can reach out the Ms. Yund, who works as the City’s
           Housing Navigator, to get guidance in applying for these programs and can also find
           more information at: homeownersassistance.utah.gov

           Additionally, the City has received a lot of calls regarding the Lead Pipe testing.
           Water Division Manager, Jason Taylor, talked about the recent changes to the Lead
           and Copper Rule that require the City to maintain an inventory of the service lines
           that are owned by property owners in the City. The Water Division teamed up with
           CRS Engineering to create an inventory. The process included evaluating aerial
           images and reviewing available construction plans. CRS Engineering identified 389
           services that needed to be identified. In April staff sent out letters to the property
           owners letting them know that the City needed to pothole their lines to identify
           these services.

       6. INFORMATION.

               a. Utility Assistance Program
                  Finance Director, Crystal Makin, along with Housing Navigator, Rebecca
                  Yund, and Promise Deputy Director, Edward Lopez, spoke about the City’s
                  newest program that will become available to residents who need help with
                  their utilities. The City is partnering with Utah Community Action, who will
                  administer the program, process the applications, and distribute the funds.
                  The program is available to homeowners and tenants who live in South Salt
                  Lake, meet the income qualification, or have suffered a life-changing event.
                  The assistance will be limited to $150 and one time per year. The program is
