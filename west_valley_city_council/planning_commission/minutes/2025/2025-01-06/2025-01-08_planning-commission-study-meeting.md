PRESENTATIONS
1) GPZ-8-2024: Petition by DAI requesting a General Plan change from
   Commercial/Light Manufacturing to Medium Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre)
   and a zone change from A (Agricultural, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RM (Multiple
   Unit Dwelling Residential). The property is located at 2996 South Redwood Road on
   2.48 acres. Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov

Steve Pastorik presented an application to amend the general plan and zoning for a 2.5-acre
property at 2996 South Redwood Road. The applicant, DAI, is the same developer who worked
on the H2O Lofts project. The property is located on the west side of Redwood Road, with the
Rocky Mountain Power transmission lines to the north, the American Heritage Mobile Home
Park to the south, and RV storage to the west. The proposal is to demolish the existing buildings
and redevelop the site, including incorporating part of the power corridor into the project. The
developer is working with Rocky Mountain Power to secure an easement for this purpose.

The project would consist of 49 rental townhomes, with 31 three-bedroom units and 18 two-
bedroom units all with unfinished basements. The three-bedroom units would have two-car
garages and driveways, while the two-bedroom units would have single-car garages and
driveways. This would be an exception to the Code, as most townhome projects have been three-
bedroom or larger units. The developer is asking for a reduction in parking on the two-bedroom
units. The buildings all face inward, except for one that faces Redwood. The developer might

parking, and landscaping. The developer secured additional parking in an office building for
those who choose to drive over to H2O Lofts. Finally, Steve reminded the Commission that both
the Commission and the Council had previously agreed that townhomes made sense for the
property in question.


2) PUD-3-2024: West Valley City Planning and Zoning Staff are requesting consideration
   to revise the conditions of approval for the Cottages at Pearce Farm PUD. This request
   is due to a new phasing plan proposed by the developer and the conditions related to the
   construction of 6800 West. The property is located at 6765 West 3500 South and is
   zoned RM. Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov.

Steve Lehman presented a Planned Unit Development (PUD) project that was approved in
September 2021. The project, which involves adding a couple of conditions of approval, is under
construction and has faced conflicts between the owner and developer. The project includes 65
units, with access from both 3500 South and 6800 West. The developer has committed to
installing the eastern portion of 6800 West including curb, gutter, and sidewalk, and the City will
be designing the west portion. The developer is now proposing to phase the project, with the first
phase including 11 units. The City engineer has proposed three conditions for this phasing, which
the developer has agreed to. The first condition is that building permits would be issued for 11
units-there would be no occupancy. The second condition is that there would be no building
permits in phase two. The third condition is that 6800 West would be built by June 15, 2025.


3) C-22-2024: Petition by DALE M LARSEN PROPERTIES (DMLP) requesting a

   total/.17 acres for storage and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-

vehicles associated with auto service businesses, and the primary tenant is United Fence. The

applicant has requested not to use masonry for screening, and Jody sought input on whether this

4) C-23-2024: Petition by AARON IVERSON requesting conditional use approval for
   outside storage. The property is located at 1774 W 2300 S on 1.86 acres and is zoned M
   (Manufacturing) Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-
   ut.gov

Brock Anderson presented conditions for allowing outside storage behind an auto repair
business, focusing on fencing requirements and preventing unauthorized expansion. The storage
area is set back from the street not requiring a masonry wall screening but is proposed to be
screened by a chain-link fence with black mesh material. The Commission expressed opposition
to the black mesh material. Brock agreed to suggest improvements to the screening.


5) C-24-2024: Petition by CDC MAINTENANCE LLC requesting a conditional use for
   outside storage associated with heavy truck repair. The property is located at 2256
   South 5700 West on 2.20 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). Staff – Jody Knapp at
   801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov

Jody Knapp presented a proposal for outside storage for heavy truck repair in a manufacturing
zone, which had previously been zoned light industrial. The property, located near the Mountain
View Corridor Trail, was subject to a development agreement that required updates to the
building and compliance with City Code. Jody mentioned that the applicant, Carlson
Distributing, had not proposed any screening but it would be reviewed. The discussion ended
with Jody mentioning that the formal plan review would provide more details.


6) C-25-2024: Petition by TOD WORKMAN requesting conditional use approval for a
   self-storage facility. The property is located at 2850 S Decker Lake Dr on 4.81 acres and
   is zoned M (Manufacturing) Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
   brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov

Brock Anderson presented a proposal to convert an existing 3-story office building into storage
units. He shared photos and a floor plan, noting that the site is adjacent to new apartments and a
County park. Brock also mentioned that he had sent the proposal to the fire department for
review. The team discussed the potential for repurposing office buildings in the Salt Lake area
due to high office vacancy rates. They suggested alternatives such as residential or industrial
uses. For this property storage units are allowed in the Code.
