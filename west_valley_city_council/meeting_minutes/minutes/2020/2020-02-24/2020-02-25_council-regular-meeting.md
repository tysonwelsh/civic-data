AWARDS, CEREMONIES, AND PROCLAMATIONS
A.  PGA PLAYER DEVELOPMENT AWARD- BRYANT BOSHARD, HEAD
    GOLF PROFESSIONAL AT THE RIDGE GOLF CLUB
    Bryant Boshard, Head Golf Professional at the Ridge Golf Club, previously
    received the PGA Player Development Award. He was not able to attend the
    meeting but the Council wanted to recognize his contribution and acknowledge the
    City’s excellent golf programming. This award is described as follows:

              Golf often has a stereotype of being hard to begin because of price, location
              and intimidation. For The Ridge Golf Club Head Professional Bryant
              Boshard, winner of the 2019 PGA Player Development Award, it’s exactly
              the opposite.

              “Everyone needs a chance to excel in something they enjoy doing,” Boshard
              said. “Each person that walks through our doors is another opportunity for
              us to share the great game. Our program selection makes it easy to cater to
              every type of golfer, from young to old and beginner to advanced. We want
              you at The Ridge and we are here to help develop your game and enhance
              your experience on and off the course.”

              Boshard has been in the golf business for 20 years, received his PGA
              Membership in 2014, has been at The Ridge for seven years and the head
              professional for two.

              Inclusion is a main focus for Boshard and his staff. Developing programs
              for juniors, seniors and veterans has shaped the day-to-day life at The Ridge.

              “I became a PGA professional because of my love and passion for the
              game,” Boshard explained. “Over the past few years working with juniors,
              seniors and veterans has shown me the value and joy it brings to not only
              their lives, but my own as well. My new reason for being a PGA
              professional is to continue to spread the game to as many people as possible
              and share the joy and love it brings to my life.”

              Because of this inclusion-centered belief, The Ridge has won several
              awards, including the Utah Parks & Recreation Outstanding Adaptive
              Program in 2018 because of their focus on veterans, and the Utah Best of
              State Golf Program for 2019.

              The Ridge offers leagues for men, women, juniors and veterans. Initially,
              The Ridge Veterans League was a three-month program. The following
              year, the program doubled and it has continued to grow.

              “I’m grateful and humbled that I have been selected for this prestigious
              award,” Boshard said. “This award would not have been possible without

     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular

    hear and consider public comments regarding application SA-8-2019, filed by West
    Valley City, requesting final plat approval for the Beagley Estates Subdivision
    Amended for property located at 2590 South 7050 West.

    Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
    information as follows:

           The West Valley City Engineering Division is requesting consideration to
           amend portions of the Beagley Estates Subdivision. More specifically, lots
           1, 6-9, 18 and Parcel B. Portions of Beagley Lane will be vacated and
           renamed Beagley Road with a new alignment.

           The Beagley Estates Subdivision was recorded with the Salt Lake County
           Recorder’s Office in 2006. In 2015 the City began processing the first phase
           of the ARA Industrial Center Subdivision at approximately 6755 West SR-
           201 South Frontage Road. Phase 2 of the ARA Industrial Center was
           reviewed and approved in 2016. Phase 2 is located immediately to the east
           of the Beagley Estates Subdivision.

           During the review process for phase 2 a recommendation was made that to
           provide for a more efficient access to the Industrial Park, a connection could
           be made with Beagley Lane. Beagley Lane intersects 7200 West at
           approximately 2590 South. The intersection of Beagley Lane and 7200
           West is better situated than the intersection of the SR-201 South Frontage
           Road for the vehicles coming out of the ARA Industrial Center. The
           distance from SR-201 and the South Frontage Road is insufficient for
           stacking and maneuverability for larger vehicles.

           Over the last few years, the City Engineering Division has been
           coordinating this effort. The home on lot 18 was purchased and demolished
           which allowed for the connection from ARA onto Beagley Lane. The
           Engineering Division redesigned the cross section of Beagley Lane by
           increasing the lot sizes of those properties directly accessing the street and
           increased the sidewalk width on the north side from 5 feet to 10 feet. With
           this new design, Parcel B will no longer be used for the cross-town trail.

           Parcel C is a remnant of the original lot 18. At the present time, the plan
           for this parcel is that it be landscaped and maintained by the ARA Industrial
           Park. A post and panel masonry wall would be constructed between this
           parcel and the residential lot to the north. Matters related to this parcel are
           still being negotiated, so changes could occur in the future.

           By virtue of the changes mentioned above, portions of the original Beagley
           Lane are going to be vacated and re-platted. The street will also have a new
           name which will be Beagley Road. The designation of “Road” better
           defines the type of street that this has become with a connection to the ARA
           Industrial Park.

           In summary, the request before the City Council is to amend lots 1, 6-9, 18
           and Parcel B of the Beagley Estates Subdivision. The subdivision plat will
           also vacate the original Beagley Lane and will re-plat the street with a new
           name of Beagley Road.

AGREEMENT BETWEEN WEST VALLEY CITY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY
FOR ACQUISITION OF THE SWIMMING POOL AT CENTENNIAL PARK
 MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING – FEBRUARY 25, 2020

Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-21 that would approve an Interlocal
Cooperation Agreement between West Valley City and Salt Lake County for acquisition
of the swimming pool at Centennial Park.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The Centennial Outdoor Swimming Pool has been owned and operated by Salt
       Lake County. Discussion has been initiated by West Valley City staff in regard to
       what implications may incur by acquiring and operating this amenity. After careful
       consideration, staff feel that this acquisition would have positive effects on the West
       Valley City community for several reasons.

       The Family Fitness Center, also located in Centennial Park, has full-time aquatics
       staff and a fully functioning division who could successfully operate and maintain
       this pool. The Family Fitness Center also has support staff to aid in the operation
       such as maintenance, concession and desk clerk staff. Adding this amenity to the
       operational structure of the Family Fitness Center would be a seamless endeavor.
       Acquiring this facility will assist with programming and management options that
       will be beneficial to West Valley City residents.

       A financial plan, based off of records obtained from Salt Lake County along with
       West Valley City staff calculations, has been designed. This shows that revenue
       produced (including admission fees, memberships, programming and events) will
       offset the expenses needed to operate this facility.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City
Council, and he called for a motion.

       Councilmember Christensen moved to approve Resolution 20-21.

RESOLUTION 20-22: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO PURCHASE FOUR OVERT
ACTIVITY SUPPRESSION POLE CAMERA KITS
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-22 that would authorize the City to
purchase four overt activity suppression pole camera kits.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The West Valley City Police Department is requesting to purchase four Overt
       Activity Suppression Pole Camera Kits at a cost of $43,940.00. These four cameras
       will be placed in permanent locations along the parkway trail system with West
       Valley City. The cameras will be used to alert the police department to crimes in
       progress, obtain evidence, and identify offenders. It will allow us to monitor issues
       such as drinking and gambling on the trail systems, gang loitering, and narcotic
       usage. The cameras will have alert notifications for afterhours violations and
       enable officers to speak to the person in violation of the law directly and increase
       both the safety of the public and the efficiency of public safety personnel in the
       area.

       Specialty Vehicle Concepts, Inc is a sole source manufacturer.

Mayor Bigelow discussed application SD-1-2019, filed by West Valley City, requesting
Final Plat Approval for the Beagley Road Dedication Plat for property located at 2540
South 6755-7000 West.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The West Valley City Engineering Division is requesting final plat approval for the
       dedication of Beagley Road from 6755 West to approximately 7000 West.

       In 2015 the City began processing the first phases of the ARA Industrial Center
       Subdivision at approximately 6755 West SR-201 South Frontage Road. Phase 2 of
       the ARA Industrial Center was reviewed and approved in 2016. Phase 2 is located
       immediately to the east of the Beagley Estates Subdivision and north of the Riter
       Canal.

       During the review process for phase 2 a recommendation was made that to provide
       for a more efficient access to the Industrial Park, a connection could be made with
       Beagley Lane. Beagley Lane intersects 7200 West at approximately 2590 South.
       The intersection of Beagley Lane and 7200 West is better situated than the
       intersection at the SR-201 South Frontage Road, especially for the vehicles coming
       out of the ARA Industrial Center. The distance from SR-201 and the South
       Frontage Road is insufficient for stacking and maneuverability for larger vehicles.

       Over the last few years, the City Engineering Division has been coordinating this
       effort. This plat completes the efforts to make a connection from 6755 West out to
       7200 West to better serve the ARA Industrial Park.

       The cross section of this right-of-way is 71 feet in width. Curb and gutter exist on
       both sides of the street, with a 10-foot sidewalk on the north side. The Cross-Town
       Trail was proposed to follow the north side of the Riter Canal. However, with the
       extension of Beagley Road out to 7200 West, a decision was made to locate the
       trail on the north side of this road, thus the 10-foot sidewalk. The wider sidewalk
       has been installed from 6755 West all the way to 7200 West.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City
Council, and he called for a motion.

A.   RESOLUTION 20-24: AWARD A CONTRACT TO KGB
     CONSTRUCTION, LLC FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A BRIDGE ON
     ANNA CAROLINE DRIVE
     Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-24 that would award a contract
     to KGB Construction, LLC for the construction of a bridge on Anna Caroline Drive.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              Bids were opened for the project on February 18, 2020. A total of three (3)
              bids were received. The lowest responsible bidder was KGB Construction,
              LLC.

              Anna Caroline Drive connects 2400 South to Parkway Blvd at
              approximately 5700 West. The road has been built in segments and is
              currently constructed from Parkway Blvd to the Riter Canal, UDOT
              recently built a segment of the road as part of the construction of the new
              maintenance facility at 5600 West and 2400 South. This project will build
              the Anna Caroline Drive bridge and roadway improvements through the
              Riter Canal. The remaining segment of the roadway between the UDOT
              maintenance facility and the Riter Canal is the frontage of the Cabco
              Subdivision, and construction for these roadway improvements was
              postponed by a delay agreement. Upon completion of the bridge, the city
              will call the delay agreement to construct the improvements along the Cabco
              subdivision.

              The bridge is partially funded by $250,000 in funds from a development
              agreement on the Sage Gate apartments. The remaining funds will be paid
              from Class C Road funds, and from the Transportation Sales Tax.

       Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of
       the City Council, and he called for a motion.
