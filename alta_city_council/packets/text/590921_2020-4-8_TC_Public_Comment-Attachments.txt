Mayor Sondak and Council Members :
We are homeowners in Town. We strongly urge you to explore funding sources for Fire and
EMS services which place the financial responsibility for those services upon those who most
directly benefit from or use them. As reflected in the notes from your last meeting, the vast
majority of usage is by visitors. In fact, a quick review shows, approximately 36 homes which
have no winter and limited summer access to these services. For us, if a fire or medical
emergency were to occur, the chance of a timely response is very small. As a result, it seems
that using property tax increases to fund service which property owners will never receive is
both imprecise and unfair . It would seem, a sound course of action would involve a thorough
accounting review, negotiation and the implementation of some combination of a transient
room tax and Town levied sales tax.
Thank you for your time,
Dan Colangelo and Keri Gardner
