Sunshine Terrace Proposal — Bryan Erickson thanked the county for this
opportunity to present his proposal. Erickson gave a brief history of the Sunshine
Terrace Foundation and quoted studies suggesting facility-based providers need to
examine alternative financing approaches to alleviate the reliance on public funding
for Medicare and Medicaid patients. Erickson proposes that Cache County
participate in the Upper Payment Limit (UPL) program. Sunshine Terrace
Foundation would be owned by Cache County, but operations would still be covered
by the Foundation. The UPL program allows higher federal matching funds which

contact the entities that have not used their funding to notify them the funding will be

pulled back unless they provide information at the March 08, 2016 Council meeting
justifying the delay in use of RAPZ funds.

their funds due to negotiations with the LDS Church History Museum. The

(Attachment 2)

e Discussion: Interns Working in the County — Executive Buttars remarked that
interns are handled in various ways in different counties. Basically, the larger
counties pay interns. The Fair Labor Standards Act requires that certain
requirements must be met in order for interns to serve unpaid. Buttars recommends
that, if departments choose to use interns, they should be paid positions.

Chairman Merrill supports the idea of using interns and recommends that the
information be given to departments and let them make the decision.

Director Runhaar observed his department has had three hires out of their intern
process.

Sheriff Jensen uses interns and cautioned that there should be a specific project in
mind for the intern(s) when hired.

COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS

Val Potter reminded Council members of the Republican Lincoln Day Dinner this Friday,
February 12, 2016 at 7:00 p.m. There will be a candidate reception prior to the dinner at
6:00 p.m.

David Erickson was impressed with the personal interest that UAC has in Cache County’s
Water Conservancy District promotion.

Council member Potter said that Representative Webb made a passionate plea at the
Saturday morning legislators’ meeting for Cache County to “get on the ball” with a water
conservancy district. Webb cited a meeting he attended this week where there is a big push
from the Wasatch Front to take any untapped sources of water, which is the Bear River. We
have to get it done (the water conservancy district) to protect water for Cache County.

Kathy Robison asked if she will be notified concerning the Public Relations Committee
meeting times and place. Executive Buttars responded he will let her know.

Jon White said the working group for the Water Conservancy District had its first meeting
and he feels it is a good group. The state legislature has said that if Cache County does not
have a Water Conservancy District, we will not be at the table on water matters. The county
needs to act now.
