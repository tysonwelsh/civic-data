PUBLIC HEARINGS
Hearings began at – 8:33 a.m.
Emigration Canyon Metro Township is considering adopting the proposed 2020 Emigration Canyon Metro
Township General Plan, which plans for the areas within Emigration Canyon Metro Township. The 2020
General Plan is Emigration Canyon’s first comprehensive plan since incorporation as a metro township in
2017. The Plan synthesizes data and public feedback to describe current conditions, envision Emigration

General Plan meets the required State laws for general planning, including elements on Land Use and

Development, Environment, Resilience and Infrastructure, and Community Work Program. Planner: Kate
Davies (Motion/Voting)
Please visit: bit.ly/lrp-ec to view a copy of the 2020 Emigration Canyon Metro Township General Plan.
The Planning Commission will act on the proposed general plan after taking comments from the public
during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s rules
of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three minutes
per person.
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Kate Davies provided an overview of
the General Plan.
Commissioner Karkut motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Wallace seconded that motion.
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED
Speaker # 1: Sustainability Alliance President
Name: Willy Stokman
Address: Not provided
Comments: Ms. Stokman said the best general plan ever had and inclusive. Reservation of emphasis on
environmental protection. Desires and needs of community is an excellent document. Pursue groundwater
study as a separate action item, need to get funding and support from the canyon. Reason this is supported,
reflected septic smart event, people are interested in the environment and improving and protecting. Raising
funds to purchase lands above Skycrest for Utah Open Lands and interest in protection of the lands, received
$43,000 in pledges and needs to be supported, need more protection of the land, policies protected of
development but not scenic life and streamflow.
Speaker # 2: Emigration Metro Township Council
Name: Catherine Harris
Address: Not provided
Comments: Ms. Harris said she would like to thank everyone and the steering committee. Community
owes you. Private citizen comment did not get the same sense of environmental protections. Hope utilize
resource councils to get word out of additional and wider participation in meetings.
Ms. Davies advised of the public process and has been robust. Provided an update to the survey. People
said similar things, clear feedback, like preservation of open space, clean air, safety. Had a workshop in
January on transportation open to the public.
Ms. Stokman looked through the comments received, saw three community council member comments,
agreed.
