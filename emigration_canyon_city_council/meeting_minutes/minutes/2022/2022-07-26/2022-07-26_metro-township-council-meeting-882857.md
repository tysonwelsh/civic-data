Pursuant to Utah Code 52-4-202 the undersigned, as Mayor of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township Council, hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the
electronic meetings of the Council will present a substantial risk to the health and safety of those
who may be present at the anchor location for the following reasons:
The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under a “High Level of
Transmission” alert stage for the COVID 19 pandemic; and
The regular meeting place for the Council (the Fire Station in the Canyon) does
not have sufficient space in the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing
to keep people safe from infection.

Reports from other Municipal Providers
Emigration Canyon Community Council (ECCC)
Bill Tobey stated annual brush chipping service was completed on July 10, 2022,
and the follow-up process to collect time and expense information for recertification of Firewise
has begun. An alternate project for this season’s fuel break project is being considered. The
ECCC has approached the Emigration Oaks HOA on extending the Acorn Hills fuel break. Jim
Woodward, Emergency Municipal Planner attended the ECCC meeting to discuss reinstating the
CERT team. Recruitment efforts are being made for a team manager. There will be a funding
request to the Metro Township Council for funding.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)
Chief Wayne Dial stated the marathon was held with no significant problems.
There was one complaint from a resident about noise. Detective Jake Elsasser will be using the
noise meter to enforce noise issues in the canyon. An additional dedicated officer has been
assigned to Emigration Canyon from May to October. There will be an increase in patrol during
the weekends.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Chief Bryan Case stated no official cause has been determined for the fire in Little
Dell. It is possible that the fire was caused by a power surge. He has contacted the fire crews to
see what it can do to get more involved in the community. He reviewed the calls for service in
Emigration Canyon. There will be an increase in staffing for July 4th and 24th to help suppress any
real issues.

Fire Restrictions
Mayor Smolka stated the state is implementing some fire restrictions and the
Council passed fire restrictions last year.
Polly McLean stated the Council adopted firework restrictions in March and there
are some fire restrictions already in the Emigration Canyon Metro Township code.
Council Member Harris stated due to the high number of fires, she would like to
adopt the same fire restrictions that were adopted last year.
Later in the meeting it was determined that the Council would wait to implement
additional fire restrictions until the State moved to a stage 2.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Implementation of a Franchise Fee
Mayor Smolka stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)
Board approached Senator Mayne about providing the metro townships the ability to enact a
franchise tax. It went through the legislative process and was approved. There is a need for
additional revenue for various areas like, Unified Fire Authority (UFA), Unified Police Department
(UPD), among others. He has asked Polly McLean to investigate enacting the franchise fee and
what the process is to move forward. The Council will be able to allocate funds wherever it feels
it is needed and the money can be split between several areas.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Appointment to the Utah League of Cities and Towns (ULCT)
Mayor Smolka stated the membership dues have been paid and Emigration
Canyon Metro Township is a member of the ULCT. The Council needs to appoint a Council
Member to the ULCT Board.
Mayor Smolka, seconded by Council Member Pinon, moved to appoint Deputy
Mayor Hawkes as the Emigration Canyon Metro Township representative to the Utah League of
Cities and Towns Board and Council Member Pinon as an alternate. The motion passed
unanimously.

Council Member Reports
Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)
Council Member Brems stated there were not UPD or SLVLESA Board meetings
held; however, he attended the SLVLESA workshop with Mayor Smolka. As SLVLESA looks into
the future, it will not have enough money to meet its obligations to procure UPD services. The
workshop had a lot of individuals attend that talked about taxation and the legislature. Discussions
continue at the legislature with the SLVLESA lobbyist. The legislation will not allow SLVLESA to
collect more taxes unless it is currently using all its available taxing options within its range. The
Board members agreed to go back to their councils to talk about the possibility of collecting
franchise taxes. If the Council approves a franchise tax, it can decide to offer all, part, or none of
those funds to SLVLESA. The projected amount the tax would yield is approximately $87,000 to
Emigration Canyon. In addition, UPD is asking if the Councils have extra ARPA funds that it is
willing to allocate to them.
