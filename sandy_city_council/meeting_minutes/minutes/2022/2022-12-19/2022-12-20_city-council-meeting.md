                                  amendment. 

                                  Council Member Stroud made a motion that the Council adopt Ordinance 22-18 amending 
                                  the effective date of the ordinance to be March 1, 2023, or upon the adoption of an 
                                  ordinance that creates a large animal hobby license, whichever comes first. The Council 
                                  further directs the Administration to prepare for council consideration, no later than 
                                  February 1, 2023, an ordinance that amends Chapter 12-2 of the Animal Services Code to

                                  where more than 4 large animals are kept. The motion was seconded by Mr. Earl.

                                  Council discussion on the motion followed. Ian Williams, Animal Services Director, Dustin 
                                  Fratto, Council Director, Jeff Robinson, Deputy City Attorney, and Tracy Cowdell, Council 
                                  Attorney, responded to Council questions related to the amendment and the motion on 
                                  the table.

                                  A motion was made by Alison Stroud, seconded by Scott  Earl, to adopt 
                                  Ordinance 22-18 amending the effective date of the ordinance to be March 1, 
                                  2023, or upon the adoption of an ordinance that creates a large animal hobby 
                                  license, whichever comes first. The Council further directs the Administration to
