41      •   Discussion of the sale, purchase, exchange or lease of real property including any
42          form of a water rights or water shares
43
44   At 8:41 PM, Board Member McDougal MOVED to convene in a Closed Session to discuss the
45   sale, purchase, exchange or lease of real property including any form of a water rights or water
46   shares. Board Member McDougal also moved to close the RDA meeting at the end of the Closed
47   Session. Board Member Haymond SECONDED the motion. Chair Staggs called for discussion
48   on the motion. There being none, he called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-
49   yes, Haymond-yes, McCay-yes, McDougal-yes, and Pierucci-yes. The motion passed
50   unanimously.
