Resolution No. 19-09-02

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting Amendment #1 to the
Master Interlocal Agreement clarifying the roles and relationships for the provision of certain
municipal administrative services for Magna:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-02                                              Date: September 10, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING
       AMENDMENT #1 TO THE MASTER INTERLOCAL COOPERATION
       AGREEMENT BETWEEN MAGNA, SALT LAKE COUNTY, THE GREATER SALT
       LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT, ET.AL., CLARIFYING THE ROLES
       AND RELATIONSHIPS FOR THE PROVISION OF CERTAIN MUNICIPAL
       ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES FOR MAGNA

                                          RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
      401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to Title 17: “COUNTIES.”

   D. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District that
      provides municipal-type services pursuant to Title 17B: “LOCAL DISTRICTS.”

   E. On January 28, 2018 Magna, for the municipality, approved and adopted a Master
      Interlocal Agreement between Magna, the County, the MSD, et.al, whereby Salt Lake
      County would provide certain municipal-type services on contract to the MSD and member
      municipalities, including Magna.

   F. During the General Session of the 2019 Utah Legislature, the legislature passed and the
      Governor signed SB124 which no longer required certain county offices to provide
      administrative services to a metro township/metro townships.




                                               3
DATE           TUESDAY                             SEPTEMBER                             24, 2019

   G. The attached document, also known as Amendment #1 to the Master Interlocal Agreement
      (Exhibit A) clarifies this change and the relationship process going forward for contracting
      with the County for those services.

                                         RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council hereby approves
and authorizes the Mayor to sign, the attached First Amendment to the Master Interlocal
Agreement, hereinafter to be known as Exhibit A, clarifying the change in the roles and
relationship between Magna and the county offices that choose to continue to provide certain
administrative services to Magna.

     APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Salt Lake County, Utah this 24th day of
SEPTEMBER, 2019.

RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-04                                                 Date: September 24, 2019

        A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
        ADOPTINGAN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN
        MAGNA AND SALT LAKE COUNTY, ET.AL., FOR ADDRESSING SERVICES

                                            RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
      401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to Title 17: “COUNTIES.”


   D. To provide for a uniform addressing system of real property, the County provides a service
      for assigning addresses (the “Service”) to real property.

   E. Uniform addressing allows for the effective and efficient response of emergency services,
      mail and package delivery, and has many more benefits for property owners.

   F. In Magna, the County has provided this service since the creation of addressing in Salt
      Lake County.

   G. Many municipalities contract with the County for the service.

   H. The attached agreement (Exhibit A) would formalize the continuation of the service.

   I.   The public health and safety benefits of the service to Magna justifies the continuation

                                          RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council hereby approves
and authorizes the Mayor to sign, the attached interlocal agreement with attachments Statement,
hereinafter to be known as Exhibit A for the provision of addressing services by Salt Lake County.

Resolution No. 19-09-06

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving the Bridge License
Agreements:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-06                                               Date: September 24, 2019

      A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
      THE 3500 SOUTH AND 9200 WEST BRIDGE LICENSE AGREEMENTS
      BETWEEN MAGNA AND THE UTAH-SALT LAKE CANAL COMPANY

                                          RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
      401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Utah-Salt Lake Canal Company (the “Canal Company”) is an irrigation water service
      provider that operates a canal within the municipality.

   D. Magna’s municipal roadways cross the canal in several locations within the municipality.

   E. In 2018, during routine inspection of roadway bridges in the Magna area, the Utah
      Department of Transportation determined the bridges that cross the canal at 3500 South
      and at 9200 West to be of the highest priority for replacement and appropriated funds to
      assist Magna in the costs of replacement.

   F. During the creation of their 2019 Budget, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
      also appropriated funding for the bridge replacement projects.

                                               8
DATE          TUESDAY                            SEPTEMBER                         24, 2019


   G. To conduct the replacement requires a license agreement between the Canal Company
      and Magna to perform the replacements.

   H. The attached license agreements, (Exhibits A and B) allow Magna to proceed with the
      bridge replacement projects over the wither to avoid disruption of irrigation service.

                                      RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council hereby approves,
and authorizes the Mayor to sign the attached license agreements, hereinafter to be known as
Exhibit A (3500 South) and Exhibit B (9200 West), between Magna and the Utah-Salt Lake Canal
Company, allowing Magna to replace the roadway bridges located at 3500 South and 9200 West.

     APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Salt Lake County, Utah this 24th day of
