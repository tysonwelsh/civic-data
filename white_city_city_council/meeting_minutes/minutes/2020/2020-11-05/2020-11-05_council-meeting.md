Reports from other Municipal Providers

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

               Captain Ken Aldridge stated UFA has asked its service area how UFA is doing
and what else it should or should not be doing. That information will be submitted to the UFA
divisions to use for its plans. The final plan should be adopted in January. Wildland fire season
has wrapped up. UFA responded to more than 100 fires locally and several fires in various states.
Sandy City Fire Department reported to 22 medical and five fire calls in October.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)

                 Officer Paula Stinson stated calls were up in October and there is a rise in vehicle
burglaries. Her time as White City’s liaison is expiring in March 2021 so, after 23 years of service,
she will be retiring and will be officially gone at the beginning of December.

               Chief Randy Thomas stated the murder in Midvale City was a domestic violence
situation. The mother was murdered in front of her 2 young kids. He pulled officers from White
City to help handle the situation and they did a great job.

                The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving the 2021 White City

      WHERAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.; and

         WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
legislative body for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501; and

Council Member Reports

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

              Council Member Perry stated last month UFA approved the updated policies.
There have been more retirements than anticipated. The Board discussed CARES Act funding
and if communities do not know where to spend the funds, UFA can benefit from additional funds.
UFSA conducted a fraud risk self-assessment and determined it is at a low risk of fraud. Several
months ago a hazardous mitigation grant was applied for to do seismic upgrades. Riverton and
Herriman City are continuing with the process of leaving the UFSA and contracting directly with
UFA.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

                Mayor Flint stated a committee was created to study what the outcome would be
if the Sheriff was removed as CEO of UPD. Salt Lake County contributes 20 percent and it owns
the Sheriff’s Office building and the jail. If the Sheriff was removed from UPD, it would have to
lease the Sheriff’s Office building. The cost of separation and changing them model would be
loss of service levels. There is no way that the high level of service could be retained. UPD is
putting in place an advisory committee for incident evaluations and assessments. SLVLESA
adopted a tentative budget for 2021.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                 Council Member Price stated by October, the seasonal employees were released
from their duties. There was a late hatching in the Davis county area, it has been horrendous for
the Salt Lake City areas and some issues for out district. There were only five West Nile pools,
and nobody contracted West Nile. The manager has been running things differently, a meeting is
held in the morning at altered start times. Three full-time season employees were cut, and more
service calls were made and some horse troughs. Pools and ponds were treated three times. A
fraud risk assessment was completed, and findings showed the district has a low fraud risk. A
fraud committee was created to change some of the by-laws and make it so everyone has to sign
a conflict of interest disclosure and behavioral statement.
