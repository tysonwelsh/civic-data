SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 0.83 acres located on the west side of Alan Point Drive, at approximately 873
W. Alan Point Drive (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicants
are requesting to vacate the recorded wetlands and no building zone designation at the
rear of the property.

The current house was built in the year 2000 and it part of the Alan Point Subdivision. The
Alan Point Subdivision is a 22-lot subdivision recorded in 1998 with lots between one half
acre to over an acre in size. The original subdivision plat includes a ‘wetlands and no

    waive all rights to appeal the permit, including its terms and conditions, and approved jurisdictional determinations

completing Section II of this form and sending the form to the division engineer (address on reverse). This form must be
