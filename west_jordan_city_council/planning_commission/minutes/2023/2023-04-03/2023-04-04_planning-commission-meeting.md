2.            Boulder Canyon Apartments Signs; 5517 West Slate Canyon Drive; Sign Development
              Plan; P- C Zone; Allied Electric Sign and                             Awning/ Tracy Barker( applicant) [# 29694;
              parcel 2036102001]


Ammon Allen noted that this application is a business item on the agenda.


Tracy Barker, Allied Sign, stated that the property owner is requesting that the existing smaller signs be
removed and replaced with 8' x 12' signs for better visibility and aesthetics.

Kent Shelton said that he visited the site and even with the weather conditions he thought that the
existing signs looked great and were very visible. His concern as a citizen was that we don' t need larger
signs in the city if the existing ones are adequate.

Tracy Barker said the property owner explained to her that they wanted larger signs for better visibility
from each road. The code allows for one sign of the larger size.


Megan Jensen showed the current sign locations. There are two on 5600 West and one on Ranches
Loop Road. The locations stay the same, they just want to replace them with larger signs. A 2- foot brick
base will match the apartment buildings. The overall height is eight feet, and the width is twelve feet.


Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the Boulder Canyon Apartments Sign Plan,
located at 5517 West Slate Canyon Drive in a P- C zone, with the Requirements of Approval listed.

Conditions and Requirements                              of Approval:
1.            All signage shall conform to the approved Boulder Canyon Apartments                                                    Sign Development                        Plan.


Ammon Allen             asked    what
                                                 specifically is causing this          application        to come to the               Planning Commission.

Megan Jensen said the sign standards allow for one large monument sign, but in order to have all three
at that size then it has to come to the Planning Commission for approval of a sign development plan.

3.
                         Wood Ranch Plat 1; 7400 South SR- 111; Preliminary Subdivision Plat( 37 lots on 15. 25
                         acres)            and         Sub- Area Plan; PCH Zone; Third Cadence/
                                                                                                Gary Langston ( applicant) [# 28624,
                         28674; parcels 20- 27- 101- 001- 4002, 4003; 20- 27- 100- 005; 20- 27- 176- 0011
Gary Langston, representative for Third Cadence, said this application constitutes the model home park
with the first 33 units and infrastructure. By locating the model homes in the proposed area the buyers
get a better perspective of what the living environment will be. North of the model homes will be the
base camp that includes amenities, their bistro, a community hall, and a pool. These features are in a
location that is important to the Wood family and is driving the model home park location. The roads in
this phase will provide emergency services access. The majority of the traffic will probably use 7400
South. The overall density of the Wood Ranch project is about 3, 000 units over 10 phases. This plan
has townhomes and a variety of single family lots. There will also be apartments along U- 111. They are
under contract with Jordan School District for an elementary school at approximately 7000 South U-
111 on ten acres of property, located in a different phase. Some park lots with manicured landscape are
included, and the TDOS parcels will be owned and maintained by the city after completion of the
improvements. Regarding utilities, he explained that the current water availability by West Jordan could
serve 900 units as approved in the master development agreement. They also have an agreement with
Kearns Improvement District for the northern two- thirds of the property for up to 2200 units for both
water and sewer.

the sub- area plan. He asked that condition# 7 be amended to allow the applicant to submit the final

Kent Shelton asked for an explanation of future city parks.

Ray McCandless explained that the TDOS areas will be steep or undevelopable areas, and in addition
the   PCH zone      also   requires   25% open space.


Duncan Murray said that the development of space is proportional as each phase comes in so that at the
completion of the development all of the open space will be completed.

Ammon Allen asked what the purpose of the HOA maintained but city-owned alleys was.

Duncan Murray said the city gets Class B and C road funds for city-owned roads. We are trying to
balance how to capture the tax revenue and who can functionally maintain it.
Ammon Allen said that in the overall plan, people living here will all want to use 7800 South. He asked
what the city' s future plans are to alleviate that east/west traffic.

Duncan Murray said there are also 7000 South and 7400 South as well as future connections to other
developments will allow for traffic flow.

4.       Text Amendment—                        Interchange Overlay Zone; Amend the 2009 West Jordan Municipal
         Code Section 13- 6K-4B Interchange Overlay Zone Table 1 Uses; City-wide applicability;
         City of West Jordan ( applicant)

Larry Gardner explained the history of the IOZ ordinance. In 2021 there were some amendments to the
map where certain areas were removed but the corresponding ordinance text was not corrected to reflect
the changes. The focus of the ordinance change tonight is to clarify the map amendment and to be more
poignant on the uses as discussed within the general plan. He reminded the Commission that this
ordinance is only the tool. Only one area has been zoned with the IOZ designation so far, which is at
the south end of Copper Rim.


Based on the analysis and findings contained in the Staff Report, staff recommended that the Planning
Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for this application.

Ammon Allen suggested that the wording " see building types section for standards" be added in the
box next to the section for single family housing.

Ammon Allen opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

MOTION:           Jay Thomas moved, based on the information and findings set forth in the staff
                  report and upon the evidence and explanations received today, to forward a
                  positive recommendation to the City Council for this application with the
                  correction of" see building types section for standards" in the wording for single
                  family housing. The motion was seconded by Trish Hatch and passed 6- 0 in favor.
