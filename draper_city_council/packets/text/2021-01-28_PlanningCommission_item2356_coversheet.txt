Re: Siempre at South Mountain - Site Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for approximately 8.99 acres located on the north side of Mike Weir Dr., at approximately 1247 E Mike Weir Dr. The property is currently zoned C2 and RM . The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved to allow for the development of an Event Center next to the existing South Mountain Golf Course Club House. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1.  The proposed development plans meet the requirements and provisions of the Draper 1990 City Municipal Code.
2.  The public services in the area are adequate to support the subject development.
For Denial:
1.  The proposed development plans do not meet the requirements and provisions of the 1990 Draper City Municipal Code.
2.  The public services in the area are not adequate to support the subject development. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 1/22/2021 Cover Memo
