MEETING MINUTES
APRIL 2, 2024

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on April 2, 2024, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah
Lloyd.

commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

for roadway right-of-way acquisition for the 1100 West corridor

Corridor Roundabout

allocated for the Apple Valley SR-9 to SR-59 Smithsonian Butte Corridor

COMPETITION EMCEE
Sarah Sun

Miss Utah 2023

Sarah Sun is from Salt Lake City, Utah, and

is a junior at Brigham Young University
studying music and political science. Previously,
she studied applied economics and management
at Cornell University, where she was elected
Freshman Representative on the Student
Assembly.

Sarah has an extensive musical background. She
has placed in three international piano
competitions, one of which led to the opportunity
to perform as a soloist at Carnegie Hall. As a
concert pianist, she has soloed with the Utah Symphony and with several
professional ensembles across the U.S. and China. As a graduate of the
International Baccalaureate Diploma Program, Sarah is an AP Scholar with
Distinction, scored in the 99th percentile on the ACT, and has placed in
international business case competitions through DECA and Future
Business Leaders of America.

Sarah was crowned Miss Utah 2023 on June 10, 2023 and has earned over
$13,000 in scholarship assistance through the Miss Utah Scholarship
Organization. Sarah’s community service initiative is entitled, “Changed
for Good: Reducing Recidivism, Strengthening Communities.” As Miss Utah,
she has formed a partnership with the Lt. Governor of Utah, the Utah
Department of Human Resource Management, and the Utah Department of
Corrections to spearhead a recidivism committee that meets monthly. She
has also introduced music education into the Utah State Correctional
Facility and has spoken to nearly 10,000 people across the state of Utah.
