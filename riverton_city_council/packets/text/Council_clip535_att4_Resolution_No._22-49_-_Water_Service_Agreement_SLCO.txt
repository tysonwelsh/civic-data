                                    RIVERTON, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-49

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN INTERLOCAL
   AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY TO SUPPLY CULINARY WATER

        WHEREAS, Riverton is the owner and operator of waterworks providing intake and
distribution of culinary grade water for distribution to residences, businesses, and community
centers; and

     WHEREAS, Salt Lake County owns and operates the Riverbend Golf Course and
community pool at the approximate location of 12800 South 1040 West; and

      WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has a continual need to obtain culinary grade water for
consumption, recreation, and irrigation purposes at the Riverbend Golf Course; and

       WHEREAS, under the terms of an existing interlocal agreement, Riverton City has
supplied Salt Lake County with water needed for the Riverbend Golf Course at discounted rates
to support the community need for recreation supplied by Salt Lake County; and

        WHEREAS, the existing interlocal agreement with Salt Lake County to supply culinary
water to the Riverbend Golf Course is set to expire in 2023; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the City is able to
continue to provide culinary water to Salt Lake County for the Riverbend Golf Course, provided
that it can diversify its culinary water supply by improving a culinary water well known as the
Green Well; and

         WHEREAS, said Green Well is in need of improvements in the form of a reverse osmosis
filtration system which will improve the culinary water quality to match water supplied by the
Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the Interlocal Agreement
with Salt Lake County, attached as Exhibit “A” updates the rate schedule for water supplied to the
County, and is subject to the City’s successful installation and operation of a reverse osmosis
system at the Green Well; and

        WHEREAS, Salt Lake County and Riverton are each authorized to enter into this
Interlocal Agreement to provide water service, pursuant to the Interlocal Cooperation Act of Utah,
Utah Code Ann. §§ 11-13-101 et seq.; and


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
1. Interlocal Agreement. The Riverton City Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to execute
   with Salt Lake County an Interlocal Water Service Agreement in substantially similar form
   to the Agreement attached as Exhibit A, after the same is approved as to form by the
   Riverton City Attorney.

2. Effective Date, Termination. This Resolution shall become effective immediately.


   PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 2nd day of
