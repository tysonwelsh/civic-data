 Background

This is a public hearing for a Conditional Use Permit, the applicant requests permission to create a
private lane that would serve two residential lots. The subject property is located at 13341 South
Trestle Lane and is a parcel which is about 1.25 acres. The west side of the parcel has an
existing home which currently accesses Trestle Lane, and there is a large area to the east of the
house where the applicant would like to create a new buildable lot. The lot is large enough that it
will not be necessary to rezone in order to subdivide the lot into two lots of at least ½-acre.



 Report by: TJP
The subject property is zoned RR-22 and all adjoining properties are also zoned RR-22. The
General Plan for the area is Estate Density Residential (minimum 21,000-sf lots).

Riverton City Code states a preference for single-family residential development to use public
streets. However, the code makes allowance and provisions so private lanes may be used on
properties where a public street may be impossible or impractical. The Planning Commission
determines if a private lane can be use in a particular setting. The Commission evaluates
applications for private lanes following the criteria in City Code sett out in 18.25.070 (for lots in
the RR-22 Zone).

Staff believes this property should be considered for a private lane for because:

    •    There are no city roads planned in this area on the Master Transportation Plan.
    •    No local streets are stubbed to this property or pointed towards this property.
    •    A private lane would not affect access by neighboring properties.

If the Commission approves this application, the owner would proceed to a Preliminary
Subdivision Plan which would return to the Commission to be heard in a public hearing.

Recommendations

Staff believes this meets the criteria for a private lane and is therefore recommending approval
with the conditions listed above.

Notices were sent to all property owners within 300 feet of this site and posted on the City’s web-
page.

 18.35.070 Lots located on private lanes.
 (1) Public Street Systems Encouraged. Public street systems shall be encouraged for access to all
 residential dwelling sites. However, the city recognizes that there are cases where it is impossible or
 impractical to develop the lot according to normal subdivision standards. In situations where insufficient
 land access exists for a public street system, a conditional use for a private lane or right-of-way may be
 approved by the planning commission.

 Subdivisions on private lanes or rights-of-way may be developed in any residential zone where at least two
 of the following conditions exist (subsections (1)(a) or (1)(b) and (1)(c) of this section):
           (a) A lot of record which is preexisting and has no frontage or adequate property to construct a

           (b) It can be demonstrated by the applicant that the property cannot be physically subdivided with
           public streets, and that development on a private lane would not impede the extension of any
           adjacent public right-of-way either now or in the foreseeable future; and

(2) Rights-of-way shall be of sufficient design to service the projected use of property and adjoining
