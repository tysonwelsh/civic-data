                                  RIVERTON REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
                                       RDA RESOLUTION NO. 23-01

       A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY, DISSOLVING THE
          “REDWOOD ROAD SOUTH NEIGHBORHOOD REDEVELOPMENT PROJECT AREA”

         WHEREAS, pursuant to the provisions of Title 17C of the Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”), the Agency
  adopted by resolution the Redwood Road South Neighborhood Redevelopment Plan (“Project Area Plan”) on
  September 2, 1997, thereby creating the Redwood Road South Neighborhood Redevelopment Project Area (the
  “Project Area”); and

          WHEREAS, the Agency has determined that the Project Area has expired per the terms and conditions of the
  Project Area Plan; and

          WHEREAS, the Agency has determined that there is no outstanding bonded indebtedness, other unpaid loans,
  indebtedness or advances, and no legally binding contractual obligations associated with the Project Area; and

         WHEREAS, the Agency, pursuant to UCA § 17C-1-702, desires to dissolve the Project Area.

  NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency as follows:

         1.    That the Agency hereby dissolves the Redwood Road South Neighborhood Redevelopment
               Agency Project Area, for which a map and legal description are attached to this resolution as
               Exhibit A.

         2.    That the Agency staff and legal counsel are hereby authorized and directed to take all such actions
               as necessary to effectuate the purposes and aims of this resolution.

         3.    This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency on this 4th day of April, 2023 by the
