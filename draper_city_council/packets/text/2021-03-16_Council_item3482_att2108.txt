                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-23

       A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPOINTING TWO
       MEMBERS OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD FOR THE
       TRAVERSE RIDGE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. (UCA) Section 17-D-1-301 et seq. Draper City
has, by Ordinance No. 1124 and Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 3-1-290, created
an administrative control board and delegated certain powers thereto to administer the
responsibilities of the Traverse Ridge Special Service District; and

        WHEREAS, DCMC Section 3-1-290(e) requires the Administrative Control Board be
comprised of five members appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City
Council, and that each member shall be a registered voter within the special service district or be
an officer or employee of Draper City; and

WHEREAS, the term for Paul Tonks, appointed October 16, 2018, and Simón Cantarero,
appointed February 7, 2017, as members of the Administrative Control Board, has expired; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to UCA Section 17B-1-304(2)(b) the City prepared a notice of
vacancy for these vacant board member positions, posted the notice in four public places within
the local district at least one month before the deadline for accepting nominees for appointment,
published the notice in a newspaper of general circulation, and otherwise complied with all
requirements of law for filling vacancies; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to UCA Section 17B-1-304 et seq. the City now desires to appoint
three members of the Administrative Control Board.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        The following registered voters of the Traverse Ridge Special Service District,
______________________, and ______________________, are hereby appointed to serve terms
of four years which shall expire on March 31, 2025.



PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 16TH DAY OF MARCH 2021.
