   1. GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT (GPA) (Public Hearing) Legislative

       Consider a request for a General Plan Amendment to change the land-use map from Open Space (OS) to
       Commercial (COM) on approximately 2.91 acres generally located on Bluff Street, just north of 676 S. Bluff
       Street. The applicant is McArthur Investment Co / Adams West LTD, and the representative is Jim McArther.
       The project will be known as Car Lot, Medical Office Condominium. Case No. 2022-GPA-007. (Staff – Carol
       Davidson)

       Carol Davidson presented the following:

       Carol Davidson – The acreage should be 2.45 but land use maps are bubbles. The zoning is currently open space.
       This property is in the hillside as you would expect. The applicant owns the entire piece of property, but they are
       only asking to change the front portion that has been disturbed, they are being sensitive to the hillside on this
       request. Staff does recommend approval.

       Commissioner Kemp – So is this technically all hillside? Have you looked at the access to this property off Bluff
       Street?

       Carl Davidson – Yes it is all located in the hillside. We have looked a little bit, there was talk of having access
       from the property to the south or if a new access is granted off of Bluff then we would line it up with the 600
       South. UDOT controls Bluff Street. Talking with the engineers they didn’t see that there would be a problem
       with them getting an access off of Bluff Street.

       Commissioner Chapman – What has the hillside ordinance said about the drainage and the location? I know it’s
       scarred but it is still sloped.

      Carol Davidson – They would have to go to the hillside before we do the zoning, if this is approved then they
      would need to get a slope analysis and apply to hillside before the zone change comes through.

      Consider a request for a Zone Change Amendment to the Atkinville Interchange Area Planned Development
      Commercial (PD-C) zone on approximately 7.43 acres to change the use from Mixed Use to Community
      Commercial with its associated permitted uses. This property is generally located on Pioneer Road, south of the
      extension of Havasu Drive and east of Arrowhead Canyon Drive. The applicant is Entitlement Holdings, LLC,
      and the representative is Heath Johnston. The project will be known as The View at Sun River. Case No. 2022-
      ZCA-031. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

      Carol Davidson presented the following:

      Carol Davidson – The general plan shows as mixed use, then the zoning is PD-C. Basically, commercial uses
      with residential option. Smiths did change the plan to have only commercial uses and that is what they would like
      to do. They are only asking to change to the C-2 use list, they don’t have anything specific at this time.

      Commissioner Fisher – So are we just changing the uses, it’s still PD-C?

      Carol Davidson – Yes, it will still be a PD-C but with commercial uses only.

      Heath Johnston – We are undecided, but we are leaning toward a hotel type structure that is high end.

      Chair Draper opened the public hearing.

      Douglas Brown – I am one of a number of individuals that have been monitoring and actively working on
      development that is happening within our area and as this commission knows, we have been making really good
      effort to try and make a good working relationship with the property owner and the developers that are coming
      along. We were a little surprised that the first time we heard anything about this was to see it on the agenda. So
      far we haven’t seen any plans. We have no opposition at this point and time. We are a little concerned that we
      would want the same lines of communication with the developer so we hope that they reach out to us so that we
      can address any concerns before it moves forward in the future.

      Richard Bender – As Doug Brown spoke, we have been active in gathering information. We are in favor so far,
      but there is nothing firm. The hotel that the gentleman spoke of would have some positive effects it would cut
      down on the traffic compared to any kind of residential high-rise structure. I think the occupancy tax and the
      property tax would be beneficial to the City than just a high-rise apartment. Speaking as a resident of SunRiver
      with limited information, I am a positive to go forward.

      Chair Draper closed the public hearing.

      MOTION: Commissioner Kemp made a motion to approve Item 2 on approximately 7.43 acres to
      change the use from Mixed Use to Community Commercial with its associated permitted uses as
      detailed by staff.
      SECOND: Commissioner Andrus
      ROLL CALL VOTE:
      AYES (6)
      Chairman Ray Draper
      Commissioner Steve Kemp
      Commissioner Emily Andrus
      Commissioner Nathan Fisher
      Commissioner Austin Anderson
      Commissioner Lori Chapman
      NAYS (0)
      Motion Carries unanimous recommend approval

   3. PRELIMINARY PLAT (PP) Administrative

      Consider a request for a sixteen (16) lot commercial subdivision known as Shakespeare Airport Subdivision
      located near the Airport Parkway. The property is .40 acres and is zoned ASBP. The applicant is Bush &
      Gudgell, representative Bob Hermandson. Case No. 2022-PP-021. (Staff – Wes Jenkins)

      Wes Jenkins presented the following:

      Wes Jenkins – We met with Washington City, this will be the first project in the area so there is some concern
      about how the roads would connect and the utilities. They are hoping they have a property owner that will
      develop this property in Washington soon. The applicant needs to loop the water line through, they will dedicate
      a 30 ft road and a cul-de-sac with the hope that Washington’s road will come through. Washington and said that
      the property developed there may be developed as one lot, but they are not sure. Hopefully we will know before
      this plats and develops. If the road doesn’t go through then we will vacate this and abandon the road. We will
      just have to see how this plays out. We are just trying to leave as many options as possible.

      Commissioner Andrus – I think it’s important to work with Washington.

      MOTION: Commissioner Fisher made a motion to recommend approval of Item 3 a 16-lot commercial
      subdivision known as Shakespeare Airport Subdivision with staff comments.
