Resolution Adopting Amendment #1 to the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting Amendment #1 to the
Interlocal Cooperation Agreement by and between White City Metro Township, the Greater Salt
Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD), and Salt Lake County:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-02                                        DATE: SEPTEMBER 23, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTING AMENDMENT #1 TO THE MASTER INTERLOCAL COOPERATION
       AGREEMENT BETWEEN WHITE CITY, SALT LAKE COUNTY, THE GREATER
       SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT, ET.AL., CLARIFYING THE
       ROLES AND RELATIONSHIPS FOR THE PROVISION OF CERTAIN
       MUNICIPAL ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES FOR WHITE CITY
       RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.




                                              1
DATE           MONDAY                           SEPTEMBER                               23, 2019

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to Title 17: “COUNTIES.”

   D. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District that
      provides municipal-type services pursuant to Title 17B: “LOCAL DISTRICTS.”

   E. On January 28, 2018 White City, for the municipality, approved and adopted a Master
      Interlocal Agreement between White City, the County, the MSD, et.al, whereby Salt Lake
      County would provide certain municipal-type services on contract to the MSD and member
      municipalities, including White City.

   F. During the General Session of the 2019 Utah Legislature, the legislature passed and the
      Governor signed SB124 which no longer required certain county offices to provide
      administrative services to a metro township/metro townships.

   G. The attached document, also known as Amendment #1 to the Master Interlocal Agreement
      (Exhibit A) clarifies this change and the relationship process going forward for contracting
      with the County for those services.

                                         RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council hereby
approves and authorizes the Mayor to sign, the attached First Amendment to the Master Interlocal
Agreement, hereinafter to be known as Exhibit A, clarifying the change in the roles and
relationship between White City and the county offices that choose to continue to provide certain
administrative services to White City.

     APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 23rd day of
SEPTEMBER, 2019.

/s/ SHERRIE SWENSEN

             Council Member Perry, seconded by Council Member Dickerson, moved to
approve Resolution No. 19-09-02. The motion passed unanimously.

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting Amendment #2 to the

RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-03                                             DATE: SEPTEMBER 23, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTING AMENDMENT #2 TO THE MASTER INTERLOCAL COOPERATION
       AGREEMENT BETWEEN WHITE CITY, SALT LAKE COUNTY, THE GREATER
       SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT, ET.AL., FORMALIZING THE
       TRANSITION OF PLANNING SERVICES FROM SALT LAKE COUNTY TO THE
       MSD

                                            RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to Title 17: “COUNTIES.”

   D. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District that
      provides municipal-type services pursuant to Title 17B: “LOCAL DISTRICTS.”

   E. On January 28, 2018 White City, for the municipality, approved and adopted a Master
      Interlocal Agreement between White City, the County, the MSD, et.al, whereby Salt Lake
      County would provide certain municipal-type services on contract to the MSD and member
      municipalities, including White City.

   F. Later in 2018, it was determined by the MSD Board of Trustees, a desire to provide
      Planning Services directly to the County and municipalities.

   G. The MSD is expected to commence the provision of Planning and Development Services
      on October 1, 2019.

   H. The attached document, also known as Amendment #2 to the Master Interlocal Agreement
      (Exhibit A) formalizes and clarifies this transition of services.

                                          RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council hereby
approves and authorizes the Mayor to sign, the attached Second Amendment to the Master
Interlocal Agreement, hereinafter to be known as Exhibit A, formalizing and clarifying the transition
of Planning and Development Services from provision by Salt Lake County to the MSD.

Resolution Adopting an Interlocal Agreement

            The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting an Interlocal Agreement
between White City Metro Township, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(GSLMSD), and Salt Lake County:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-04                                           DATE: SEPTEMBER 23, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTINGAN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN WHITE
       CITY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY, ET.AL., FOR ADDRESSING SERVICES
       RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to Title 17: “COUNTIES.”


   D. To provide for a uniform addressing system of real property, the County provides a service
      for assigning addresses (the “Service”) to real property.

   E. Uniform addressing allows for the effective and efficient response of emergency services,
      mail and package delivery, and has many more benefits for property owners.

   F. In White City, the County has provided this service since the creation of addressing in Salt
      Lake County.

   G. Many municipalities contract with the County for the service.

   H. The attached agreement (Exhibit A) would formalize the continuation of the service.

   I.   The public health and safety benefits of the service to White City justifies the continuation.

                                           RESOLUTION

      THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council hereby
approves and authorizes the Mayor to sign, the attached interlocal agreement with attachments
Statement, hereinafter to be known as Exhibit A for the provision of addressing services by Salt
Lake County.

     APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 23rd day of
