City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On June 21, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1545, prohibiting the
discharge of fireworks in specified areas. The complete ordinance is on file at the
Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 27th
day of June 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
