Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City
Administrator Seth Atkinson offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

    Fire Chief Kurtis Park and four other Juab County Fire District firemen lead the pledge of allegiance.
Chief Park then handed out a summary of the fire district’s personnel, equipment, call volume, and call
history.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting held on 2-2-21, work session held on 2-2-21, work session held on 2-9-
21, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 2-16-21, and the sale of two burial rights to Gail Hyde.
Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

  Councilor Kent Jones reviewed three business license applications. The applications presented were
Thalman Orthodontics, Family Foot and Ankle Specialists, LLC, and Fresh’s Homemade Eatery. Councilor
Jones moved to approve both license applications. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote. Dr. Brant Burnham, owner of Family Foot and Ankle Specialist, LLC,
reported on what kind of services he would be offering. Cameron Painter, owner of Fresh’s Homemade
Eatery gave a box of doughnuts to the council and reported on his future business plans.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

SAVAGE BLUFFS SUBDIVISION FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

   Councilor Worwood moved to approve the final plat for Savage Bluffs Subdivision with the caveat
that the final plat will show an irrigation easement on lot six of the final Mylar copy. Councilor Seely
seconded the motion and stated that he would like a requirement in our subdivision code that required
developers to provide more open space within their developments. The motion passed on the
following vote.

MAYOR NIELSON WELCOMED LARRY O’DONNAL TO THE LIBRARY BOARD:

  Mayor Nielson recognized and welcomed Larry O’Donnal, who was in attendance at the meeting, as a
new appointee to the Library Board.

MASTER FEE RESOLUTION 02-16-2021 ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson explained that each year the council typically reviews the fees charged for services.
Increases in wages and materials raise the cost of providing services over time and it is important to
maintain updated fees to keep up with these costs. Without fee updates, the costs to provide services
end up using tax revenues or fund balances, which erode the ability for the city to maintain other
services or provide for capital and equipment needs. The presented Master Fee Resolution 02-15-2021
changed golf course, second garbage can, gun range, and engineering fees. Councilor Ostler moved to
adopt Master Fee Resolution 02-16-2021. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on
a unanimous vote.

RECLYCLOPS AMENDMENT AGREEMENT APPROVED:

    Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that Recyclops, who provides recycling services for residents,
is raising their rates to 12.00/month and will begin collecting every other week instead of the first and
third Tuesday. An amendment agreement was needed to make these fee and service changes.
Councilor Ostler moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the Recyclops Amendment Agreement.
Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson reported that the General Plan Consultant Committee met and decided on
recommending Rural Utah Consultants as the firm to prepare an updated general plan. He also
suggested allowing one of the companies that submitted a proposal that uses University of Utah
students to do some of the demographic work for the plan.
  Mr. Atkinson reported that he had met with the Juab County Commissioners about the T-Shepherd
Annexation. The commissioners decided to table the issue until the city and county can agree about the
road issue.
  Councilor Seely reported on the Library Board meeting. He said that the library staff is looking into an
interlibrary loan program that the high school could utilize. He also reported that the library had
received a $4600 CLEF grant and plans are being made to implement a Teen Library Council.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual, and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed
on a unanimous vote.


   The meeting adjourned at 8:15 p.m.
