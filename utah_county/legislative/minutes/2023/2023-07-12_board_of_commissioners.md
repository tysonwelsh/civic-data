COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH - ANNOUNCED THE JULY 2023 UTAH COUNTY
EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH, KERI CHENEY (read nomination):

Keri Cheney has worked as a legal assistant in the Utah County Attorney’s Office for 12
years. In her current assignment with the Screening Division, Keri handles the intake
of cases from law enforcement, the organization of case records, the filing of charges in
District Court, and the assignment of cases for prosecution. In this role, Keri is
responsible for obtaining, organizing, filing, and initiating several thousand cases each
year. She coordinates not only with the attorneys in our office but with each of the
more than 20 law enforcement agencies the county and the staff of all nine courts in the
Fourth District and has developed an excellent working relationship with each one of
them. As such, Keri is an invaluable asset to the County Attorney’s Office. Her work
ethic, skill set, accuracy, professionalism, and collegiality are outstanding, and she is the
only truly indispensable employee in the Screening Division. Keri has mastered the art
of keeping the attorneys she works with focused and on task and is unfailingly pleasant
while doing so. She works long hours, is undeterred by distractions, and is an amazing
multi-tasker. Prior to her current assignment, Keri had assignments as a legal assistant
with the Juvenile Court team, the Justice Court team, multiple trial court teams, and
had responsibility for extraditions and Governor’s warrants. Keri has fulfilled each
assignment with the same outstanding level of performance. With each change in
assignment, the excitement of the attorneys in the new group to which she has been
assigned has been matched only by the disappointment of the attorneys in the group she
had to leave. Keri is well-liked and highly respected by all the attorneys in the office, as
well as by her fellow legal assistants, paralegals and other members of the support

staff. The Utah County Attorney’s Office is truly fortunate to have Keri Cheney as
part of our team.
