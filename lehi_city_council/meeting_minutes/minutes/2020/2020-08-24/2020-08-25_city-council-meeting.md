Pre-Council, 4:00 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Condie gave the invocation.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Presentation and Discussion of the Fluent Site Plan
McKay Christensen presented his development idea for the Fluent site. Mr. Struthers stated that
there are no approvals for this on the agenda. Mr. Christensen is just seeking feedback from the
Council at this point. Mr. Christensen stated that if it’s something the Council would consider then
he would proceed with the formal application process.

Councilor Koivisto expressed concerns with this being on the agenda. She would prefer that Mr.
Christensen just proceed with the application process. Mayor Johnson stated that there is no weight
to anything that is said here. He said that because this is so new, the developer would like to bounce
it off the Council because if it’s clear that the Council isn’t interested then there’s no reason to
waste everyone’s time going through the application process. He said that this request is made
from time to time by developers and can provide some direction to them before they file an
application.

Mr. Christensen wants to take the north side of Fluent and develop it as residential and change the
current Fluent space to mixed use. This location would have great proximity to I-15, Frontrunner,
light rail, and trails. He stated that he believes it will help take cars off the streets. It also has close
access to office space for employment. They are trying to appeal to the demographic of young
singles or newlyweds that are employed with high wages at the nearby companies. Mr.
Christensen said that they could mitigate the impact to the homes on the east side. He said this is
a different product then anything that’s been built in Lehi, with an urban feel and lots of amenities.

2.2. Discussion of the Lehi City Dump Pass Program for 2021
Marilyn Banasky, Information Center Manager, explained how the current dump pass program
operates. She explained that the cost to the city has increased over the years, but the cost to
residents remains the same. She is concerned that the garbage fund will not be able to support this
program at this rate without increasing the cost to residents. She is proposing a new program where
the residents would be mailed a post card that would become their dump pass. The post card would
be mailed twice a year and can only be used at a certain time. Councilor Condie suggested that the
punch card just have 3 punches instead of 5. Councilor Southwick was concerned that the City
could potential have to pay more if the cards are mailed because more people may use it. Mayor
Johnson liked the idea of reducing the amount of punches and having people still come into the
office to pick up the card. The Council reached consensus that residents would still need to come
into the office for a dump pass, but they should reduce the number to 3 punches, and residents
cannot purchase additional passes. Ms. Banasky stated that they will implement this in January.

4. Consideration of Resolution #2020-56, a notice of Withdrawal from the Carbon Free
   Power Project Power Sales Contract and JUMP allocation.
   Joel Eves, Power Director, presented on the Carbon Free Power Project and why it may be in
   the city’s best interest to withdraw from the agreement. There are concerns with risk and the
   lack of other subscribers at this point in the project. Logan City also recently withdrew from
   the project. There were also concerns with the ongoing contract negotiations. The Council
   expressed disappointment because they were excited about this project, but the risks are too
   concerning. Mr. Eves stated that there may be an opportunity for the city to participate at a
   later date with less risk involved.

    Motion:     Councilor Southwick moved to approve Resolution #2020-56, a notice of
                Withdrawal from the Carbon Free Power Project Power Sales Contract and JUMP
                allocation. Councilor Condie seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Southwick, Yes;
    Councilor Hancock, Yes; and Councilor Koivisto, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
