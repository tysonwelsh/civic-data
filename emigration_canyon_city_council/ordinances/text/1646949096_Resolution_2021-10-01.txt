                                   EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP

        RESOLUTION NO.: 21-10-01                                        DATE: October 26, 2021

                     A RESOLUTION OF EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP
                     COUNCIL APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN
                     INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH THE GREATER SALT LAKE
                     MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT, COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP,
                     MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP, KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP, TOWN
                     OF BRIGHTON, WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP, AND SALT LAKE
                     COUNTY REGUARDING THE ISSUANCE OF BONDS TO PAY FOR
                     INFRASTRUCTURE

               WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township ("Emigration Canyon") is a Municipality
        pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

              WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code
        Ann.§ 10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor, and


               WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon is authorized under the Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code
        Ann. § 11-13-101 et seq. (the “Interlocal Cooperation Act”), to enter into an interlocal agreement
        with the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the "District"), Copperton Metro Township,
        Emigration Canyon Metro Township, Kearns Metro Township, Town of Brighton, Magna Metro
        Township, and Salt Lake County Respecting the Issuance of Bonds to Pay for Infrastructure, and


               WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon, by and through its Council, has determined authorizing the
        District to issue bonds for infrastructure, as set forth in the attached Interlocal Agreement,
        Attachment "A", will benefit the residents of Emigration Canyon and will contribute to the
        prosperity, well-being, peace, and comfort of the community.


               NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council
        approves, and authorizes the Mayor to sign, the attached Interlocal Agreement ("Agreement") with the
        Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District, Copperton Metro Township, Emigration Canyon Metro
        Township, Kearns Metro Township, Town of Brighton, White City Metro Township, and Salt Lake
        County Respecting the Issuance of Bonds to Pay for Infrastructure (ATTACHMENT A).

                IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED, the Mayor and City Attorney may make non-material changes
        to the Agreement prior to signing if needed to respond to comments and suggestions of other
