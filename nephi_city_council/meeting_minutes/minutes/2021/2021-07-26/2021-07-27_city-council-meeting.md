                                        Nephi City
                                  Work Session Minutes
                                      July 27, 2021
                                         7:00 p.m.
                               Nephi City Council Chambers



  1. Drug force tasks officers Clete Carter, Mike Nielsen, Toby Kington, and Chief of
     Police Michael Morgan reported to the council the West Central Strike Force
     statistics for the period from April thru June 2021.

  2.    The council discussed with the RV park owners the issue of long-term tenants
       staying at the RV parks. Each owner and Code Enforcement Officer Andrea Holm
       expressed ideas, frustrations, and experience with long-term tenants as it relates
       to current city code. Mr. Ken Johansen addressed the council about his desire to
       change the city code to allow people that are building a home to be able to live in
       a RV during the construction of the new home.

  3. Adjournment at 8:35 convened into regular meeting.
