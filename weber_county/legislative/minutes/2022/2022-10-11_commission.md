G. ACTION ITEMS:
     1. APPROVAL OF A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH HILL AIR FORCE BASE TO
        PROVIDE GUIDELINES AND INSTRUCTION REGARDING CONFINEMENT AND HOUSING OF
        MILITARY MEMBERS AS WELL AS FINANCIAL OBLIGATIONS.
            Lt. Kyle Osiek (Watch Commander, Corrections): Memorandum with 75th Air Base Wing at Hill AFB and
            Weber County for the housing of military detainees, both pretrial detainees and post-trial inmates, inside the
            Weber County Correctional Facility; this is a continuation of a with Hill AFB. All costs of hospitalization,
            prescriptions, security, office calls, surgical procedures, dental care, and detainee transportation will be
            responsibility of Hill AFB. Detainees will be housed separately from other Hill detainees and those of Weber
            County. Hill AFB will pay $82/day for each inmate. Three year agreement will be effective one day after
            final signatures received. We average between two to three inmates from Hill AFB at any given time.
            Commissioner Harvey moved for approval of a Memorandum of Understanding with Hill Air Force Base to
            provide guidelines and instruction regarding confinement and housing of military members as well as financial
            obligations; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – N/A

     2. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH ANDERSON LAW TO REPRESENT INDIGENT PERSONS IN
        THE SECOND DISTRICT COURT WHEN APPOINTED BY THE COURT.
            James Retallick (Public Defender): Carl Anderson is taking over the complete Mental Health court contract
            to consolidate under one attorney to improve efficiency. Two attorneys split the duties before.
         Commissioner Froerer: What is view from outside of the county? Has it been beneficial to the county and to
         the public we serve?
         Mr. Retallick: A lot of counties looking to Weber County as example of how to approach indigent defense.
         Add one more attorney later this month with a goal of having three public defenders instead of two to lessen
         case load.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a contract with Anderson Law to represent indigent persons in the
         Second District Court when appointed by the court; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – N/A
