                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-33

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
 WITH MORGAN PAVEMENT TO COMPLETE THE 2023 SLURRY SEAL PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Purchasing bid out the 2023 Slurry Seal Project. Bid
schedules 1 & 2 are city projects. Bid schedule 3 is for the South Valley Sewer District; and

       WHEREAS, bid schedule 2 was intended to be done in Fiscal Year 2023 budget and bid
schedule 1 was intended to be done and is included in Fiscal Year 2024 budget; and

        WHEREAS, bid schedule 3 will be paid for by South Valley Sewer District and the
contractor for all bid schedules is Morgan Pavement who would like to complete the project all
at once; and

       WHEREAS, by approving this agreement, Council will need to approve $220,389.00 as
an expense and $128,827.00 as an incoming revenue in a future budget amendment and a
decrease by $91,561.00 in the Fiscal Year 2024 budget.
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Morgan
          Pavement to complete the 2023 Slurry Seal Project.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 18th day of
April 2023 by the following vote:
