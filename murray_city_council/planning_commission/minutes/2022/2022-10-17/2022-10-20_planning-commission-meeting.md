KIMBALL DEVELOPMENT & MANAGEMENT – 6045 South Fashion Blvd. - Project #22-150
Mr. Smallwood reviewed this application for a site plan review of an office building, located in
the GO zone; all new construction in this zone requires site plan approval by the planning
commission. He shared the site plan from the meeting packet. Regarding parking, for the
19,000 square foot building 64 spaces are provided, only 62 are required; there is also some
shared parking with the office building to the south. Staff ensured the office building to the south
still has enough parking with the current setup. There are two points of access to the buildings.
When a building is proposed next to residential zoning, the city requires a 10-foot landscape
buffer be provided along the perimeter. Mr. Smallwood noted that part of the plan needs to be

amended to show the full 10 feet of landscaping around the edge, the current measurement is
around 6-7 feet. He indicated in the picture's where existing landscaping is labeled as
“remaining,” and he wanted to make it clear that existing landscaping cannot remain. Regarding
fencing, the full details haven’t been provided yet but code requires a 6-foot masonry fence.
There is currently a partial existing masonry fence and a chain link fence. He noted in the staff
report that he requested the applicant work with the neighbor to the west to determine whether
or not they need the access, as that would determine whether the 6-foot masonry wall is needed
in that location. The 6-foot masonry fence also needs to be built at finished grade, so the current
masonry fence will need to be re-done to that specification. He discussed the dumpster
enclosure, and that will be addressed further at the building permit stage. He showed pictures
and details of the elevations, height and materials as included in the Staff Report. The code
allows for a parapet to extend into the height requirement, and this building has included that in
their design. Staff has no concerns with the architecture, elevation, height, etc. Staff is
recommending approval of the conditional use permit/site plan approval, subject to the 11
conditions.
Jayd Petersen, applicant, stated they are excited for this project. His father-in-law is Dave
Kimball, who has done many projects with Kimball Development in Murray and has had this
property for over 30 years. The group owned the Fashion Place Professional Building, and
when the group sold that they kept this piece in the hopes of developing something like this.
They have been in close contact with the surrounding neighbors to ensure parking is taken care
of, and they know the owners of the property with the fencing issue.
Mr. Lowry asked the applicant if he has reviewed the 11 conditions, and if he will be able to
comply with them.
Mr. Petersen responded that they have reviewed the conditions listed in the Staff Report and
will be able to comply with them.
Mr. Lowry noted there was an email comment that was received before the meeting from an
adjacent property owner who complained of light pollution from the parking lot, and Mr. Lowry
suggested that be passed on to Code Enforcement to look into this and discuss the issue. He
opened the hearing for public comment, there were no comments in person or electronically so
the hearing was closed. He asked for discussion regarding the light issues.
Ms. Milkavich asked if the lighting requirements are already part of the code.
Mr. Smallwood said it is part of the code, but it wasn’t addressed in the report. He suggested the
commission add something to the motion if they are concerned regarding following lighting
rules.
Ms. Milkavich asked if the lighting causing the issues was installed before the current code.
Mr. Smallwood said he didn’t know, but that based on the current code, this development would
not be adding to that light pollution.
Ms. Patterson moved to approve the conditional use permit to allow the construction of the
proposed office building on the properties addressed 6035 and 6045 South Fashion Blvd.,
subject to the following conditions:

MURRAY MANSION – 4872 South Poplar Street – Project #22-145
Mr. Smallwood gave a brief history of the Murray Mansion from the Staff Report and noted that
the Parks and Rec Department, along with Cultural Division have been working really hard on
this project and remodel. The building requires ADA upgrades including bathrooms and
accessibility. He reviewed the site plan with the proposed addition, including public
improvements of curb & gutter, sidewalks and landscaping. Fifth Avenue is a public street, so
the improvements will be done there as well. Poplar was vacated as a right-of-way by the city
and is mostly used as access; it will continue to be used as access to the north parking lot but
will not have the same street improvement requirements. At the entrance, the steps up go to the
entrance lobby area, where the custodial closet and ADA restrooms are located. The step down
goes into the basement area of the addition; there is also a lift station. They will be attempting to
match the front brick façade on the building with the current yellow brick, there will also be
bronze metal siding to match the rain gutters and other existing details. The MCCD Review
Committee reviewed these plans and forwarded a positive recommendation, and staff is
recommending approval with the three conditions listed.
Mr. Nay asked if landscaping will meet all current standards around the building.
Mr. Smallwood responded yes, that will be part of the improvements.
Mr. Lowry invited Mr. Sorensen, the Parks and Recreation Director, up and asked if he has
reviewed three conditions, and if they will be able to comply with them.
Kim Sorensen (applicant) stated that they have looked at the three conditions and will be able to
comply. He gave a brief history of the mansion. The previous owners used this as a wedding
reception center and for dancing. The city has a plan to move the museum to the mansion when
city hall is relocated. The artifacts will be displayed on the “second floor”, the public will not be
given access to any of the other floors; this is why they have focused on accessibility for the 2nd
floor.
Ms. Milkavich noted that her profession brings her in contact with lots of people who use things
like the lift being proposed. She thanked them for all the work that went into making sure this is
accessible for everyone. However, those lifts are noisy, the person has to find someone that has
a key to use it, and more times than not they’re broken; also, when they break the vendor has to
be sent out to fix it. She wishes that, since they were adding a new space, they could have
something better with maybe a ramp that allows everyone to move together. She has seen how
these lifts work, and there are much better ways. She understands ramps are hard, but if they

Mr. Richards asked why the rest of the mansion isn’t being used for the museum
Mr. Sorensen said it would be costly to retrofit the mansion for public space, as the halls are

redo it. He noted that they will be adding more modern heating as well, as the current
heating/HVAC system is the original.
