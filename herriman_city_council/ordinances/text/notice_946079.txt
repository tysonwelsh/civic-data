PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on October 9, 2024, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2024-25 amending the Herriman City Zoning Map by applying the Sports and Entertainment Mixed-Use Overlay Zone to 16.98 acres of property located at approximately 12225 South Herriman Main Street. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
