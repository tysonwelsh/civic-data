City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 4, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1565, amending
Chapter 9-18G of the Draper City Municipal Code pertaining to the Lone Peak
Commercial Special District. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 5th day of
October 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
