CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval of the purchase of a John Deere 160P Excavator for
        the Water Services Department.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This item is for the purchase of the John
          Deere excavator from RDO Equipment Company in the amount of $234,500
          through a cooperative contract with Sourcewell. This purchase replaces an
          existing unit in the fleet that has reached the end of its useful life. This item was
          included in the FY 2026 budget for replacement.

      b. Consider approval of the purchase of 2020 Freightliner 114 SD Manitex
         Crane for Energy Services.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This item is to purchase a 2020
          Freightliner 114 SD Manitex Crane from Crane Monsters Corporation in the total
          amount of $310,000. This purchase replaces the existing crane within the city's
          fleet which has reached the end of life. This item was included in the FY 2026
          budget. Staff recommends approval.

      c. Consider approval of a contract with Patriot Construction for the
         Construction of the Industrial Water Tank.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The existing Industrial Tank has been in
          operation for many decades. The tank was constructed as an earthen reservoir
          with a Hypalon liner and concrete lid. The tank has liner has failed and been
          repaired in the past, and the concrete lid lacks structural integrity. The Industrial
          Tank will be replaced with a reinforced concrete tank of similar size. Six bids
          were received for the Tank Replacement Project with the low bid from Patriot
          Construction. Bid amounts ranged from $2.5 to $3.2 Million. Staff recommends
          approval of the contract in the amount of $2,505,464.29.

      d. Consider approval to award bid to Interstate Rock Products, Inc. for the
         Desert Color New Costco (SR-7) Traffic Signal.

      m. Consider approval of a cooperative agreement between Spin and St.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Spin has operated in St. George under a

      n. Consider approval of a Water Right & Water Share Exchange Agreement
         with the Washington County Water Conservancy District.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City desires to enter into an
          agreement with the Washington County Water Conservancy District to exchange
          some groundwater rights that the City owns for shares in the St. George Clara
          Fields Canal Company. The City owns several groundwater rights in the airport
          vicinity. \The water quality in that area of the City is extremely poor. Advanced
          water treatment (reverse osmosis) is required to treat the groundwater to a
          quality that it could be for either drinking water or irrigation water. The WCWCD
          owns shares in the St. George Clara Fields Canal Company, whose source of
          water is the Santa Clara River and Gunlock Reservoir. Shares in this canal
          company are beneficial to the City because the quality of water is such that it can
          be used without additional treatment. In addition, the infrastructure to convey
          the water to city parks, cemeteries, schools, or residential areas already exists.
          The City would realize an immediate benefit from this exchange agreement.

PLAN/RESOLUTION:
     Public hearing and consideration of Resolution No. 2025-025R adopting a
     Joint Agency Regional Water Conservation.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Water Conservation Act requires each
      water conservancy district and public water system to submit a water conservation
      plan to the Utah Division of Water Resources and update the plan every five years.
      The City of St. George updated its water conservation plan in November 2023 and is
      not due for an update until Dec 2028. Since each regional municipality has enacted
      uniform water conservation ordinances, and are uniform in their water conservation
      efforts, the development and adoption of a regional water conservation plan will
