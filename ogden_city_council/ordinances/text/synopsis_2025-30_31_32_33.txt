                                              AFFIDAVIT OF POSTING
                                        SYNOPSIS OF OGDEN CITY ORDINANCE



         I, Jason Gould, the duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, in and for the City of Ogden, Weber County, State
of Utah, do hereby certify the Ogden City Council at its October 21, 2025, meeting adopted the following:

         ORDINANCE NO. 2025-30: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY, UTAH, AMENDING THE
         OGDEN CITY GENERAL PLAN BY AMENDING THE MOUNT LEWIS COMMUNITY PLAN TO
         PROVIDE THE ZONING OPTION OF R–2S FOR PROPERTIES GENERALLY LOCATED AT
         1514 NORTH WASHINGTON BOULEVARD.

         ORDINANCE NO. 2025–31: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY, UTAH, AMENDING THE
         ZONING MAP OF OGDEN CITY, AS ADOPTED BY SECTION 15–3–3 OF THE OGDEN
         MUNICIPAL CODE, TO RECLASSIFY AS TWO–FAMILY AND SMALL LOT RESIDENTIAL
         ZONE WITH CONDITIONAL OVERLAY (R–2S/CO) PROPERTY HERETOFORE CLASSIFIED
         AS TWO–FAMILY RESIDENTIAL ZONE (R–2), GENERALLY LOCATED AT 1514 NORTH
         WASHINGTON BOULEVARD.

         ORDINANCE NO. 2025–32: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY, UTAH, AMENDING THE
         OGDEN MUNICIPAL CODE BY AMENDING CHAPTER 4 OF TITLE 9 TO REMOVE
         BACKFLOW AND CROSS CONNECTION CONTROL; BY READOPTING CHAPTER 4 OF
         TITLE 9 TO ADOPT CROSS–CONNECTIONS, MAKE TECHNICAL CHANGES, AND TO
         APPROPRIATELY REVISE THE INDEX.

         ORDINANCE NO. 2025–33: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY APPROVING PUBLIC ART
         PROJECTS FOR OGDEN CITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2026.

         Copies of all ordinances in their entirety are available for review in the Ogden City Recorder’s Office located
on the second floor of the Municipal Building, 2549 Washington Boulevard # 210, during regular business hours.

         The undersigned, duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, does hereby certify that the above notice was
posted in accordance with Utah State law on this 30th day of October 2025.


                                                                         JASON GOULD
                                                                         DEPUTY CITY RECORDER
