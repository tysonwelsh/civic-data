Consistent with provisions of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act, Utah Code Ann. § 
54-4-207(4), the Redevelopment Agency Chair has issued written determinations supporting the 
Board's decision to convene electronic meetings of the Board without a physical anchor location . 
Due to the health and safety risks related to the ongoing COVID -19 pandemic and considering 
public health orders limiting in -person gatherings, members of the public should not attend Board 
meetings in person. However, members of the public are invited and encouraged to view and 
participate in the Board's electronic meetings as described below.

beginning of the “Public Comment” portion of the meeting. Comments will be limited to three 
minutes per individual unless otherwise approved by the Board. If an individual is unable to attend

at the appropriate time.
