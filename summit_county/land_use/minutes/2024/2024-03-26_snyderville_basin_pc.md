Work Session
(4:40 P.M.)
1)
Work Session regarding a Master Planned Development for the Marketplace at Silver Creek, a 10-building mixed use project located at 1500 Frontage Road, Silver Summit, Summit County, UT.  Parcel SCO-C-10-3AM (14.76 acres) Applicant: Henry Sigg, representing Marketplace at Silver Creek Center LLC; Administrative and Legislative Review. Amir Caus, Senior Planner, AICP and Ray Milliner, Principal Planner.
(4:43 P.M.)
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner
, provided an introduction for the MPD for the Marketplace at Silver Creek work session.
Applicants, Henry Sigg and Steve Coleman addressed the commission and presented the Master Plan for the Marketplace at Silver Creek Development.
Commissioner Chris Conabee joined the meeting at 5:42 p.m.
Commission Members asked questions and provided comments for discussion.

Senior Planner,

Christopher Conabee made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the Community Development Director for the Canyons Village Parking Garage LIP taking into account items 1 and 2 as listed in the letter from Brandon Brady to Tiffanie Robinson dated 3/19/2024 that reads examining a free right out of the Northwest exit onto Lower Village Road and a roundabout at Ozzy Way and Lower Village Road subject to the Conditions of Approval and Findings of Fact found in the staff report. Commission Items
(7:09 P.M)
Commission Chair Carmichael stated that interviews had been conducted for vacancies on the Commission and that the election of the Chair and Vice-Chair would occur after the notification of new members.
Director Items
(7:10 P.M.)
Community Development Director, Pat Putt addressed the Commission stating that the County Council would deliberate on the Commission interviews at the next SCC meeting. He acknowledged John Kucera’s final meeting as a Commission Member and thanked him for his service on the Commission. Director Putt provided a preview of agenda items for upcoming meetings.
Adjournment
(7:15 P.M.)
Tyann Mooney made a motion to adjourn. Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Thomas Cooke, DJ Hubler.
