              Stay WVC LLC, is requesting a plat amendment of lot 1 JMYL Subdivision.
              The JMYL Subdivision was recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder’s
              Office in January 2019. The original subdivision plat consisted of 7 lots on
              13.5 acres. This application will amend lot 1, to create an additional lot.

RESOLUTION 20-07: APPROVE THE PURCHASE OF FOUR FERNO INX
STRETCHERS AND RELATED ACCESSORIES FROM FERNO-WASHINGTON,
INC.
John Evans, Fire Chief, discussed proposed Resolution 20-07 that would approve the
purchase of four Ferno INX stretchers and related accessories from Ferno-Washington, Inc.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Purchase of (4) stretchers will replace (4) current Stryker stretchers that are past the
       manufacture recommended services life of 7 years. The stretchers to be replaced
       are each 13 or more years old.

       Purchase of accessories will provide EMS crews with the necessary storage, IV
       pole, and equipment hooks to provide patient care. It will also provide for a more
       comfortable stretcher for the patient while allowing larger patients to also be placed
       on the stretcher.

       Purchase of batteries and mounting plates will provide a continues and reliable
       power source to the stretcher. The mounting plate will also make the stretcher to
       ambulance connection SAE compliant.

       The Ferno INX stretcher utilizes a self-loading technology that allows the
       stretcher to be taken out of and placed into the ambulance with no lifting
       requirements of EMS personnel.

       West Valley City Fire Department has utilized the Ferno INX stretcher on two
       ambulances for the last year. The stretchers have shown to be reliable in their
       operation. West Valley City Fire Department would like to standardize our fleet of
       stretchers. West Valley City Fire Department has two individuals who are trained
       and certified to perform preventive maintenance and repairs on the Ferno INX
       stretcher. This saves the Fire Department cost associated with annual preventive
       maintenance and costly labor fees for repair.

COMMERCIAL SUBDIVISION LOCATED AT 3540 WEST TECHNOLOGY
PARK DRIVE
Steve Lehman, CED, presented application S-7-2019, filed by Legacy Properties LLC,
requesting Final Plat Approval for Technology Drive Commercial Subdivision located at
3540 West Technology Park Drive.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Legacy Properties LLC, is requesting final plat approval for the Technology Drive
       Commercial Subdivision. The subject property is occupied by an existing
       office/warehouse building and a large parking lot to the south. Surrounding
       properties to the north, east, south and west are zoned manufacturing.

       The proposed subdivision is being requested in order to divide the existing parcel
       into 2 lots. It is anticipated that future uses here will be light manufacturing, and
       office uses. Access will be gained from Technology Park Way and Technology
       Drive.

       Lot 1 will consist of 3.9 acres, while lot 2 will consist of 1.2 acres. Sufficient
       frontage will exist for each of the platted lots along Technology Park Way to
       work independent of each other. However, an existing access easement is already
       in place between the two lots if needed. A public utility easement will also be
       noted on the plat along each lot’s frontage.

       When asked what the purpose of the subdivision was, the applicant stated that the
       large parking area on the south is not needed for the existing building. The
       applicant stated that the division would allow this property to develop with a new
       land use which could better utilize this space.

Councilmember Buhler how big the smaller lot will be. Steve replied approximately 1.2
acres.

CONSIDER APPLICATION SMI-8-2019, FILED BY TOM BROWN,
REQUESTING FINAL PLAT APPROVAL FOR THE WEST VALLEY CITY
INDUSTRIAL PARK SUBDIVISION LOCATED AT 7301 WEST 5400 SOUTH
Steve Lehman, CED, presented application SMI-8-2019, filed by Tom Brown, requesting
Final Plat Approval for the West Valley City Industrial Park Subdivision located at 7301
West 5400 South.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Dominion Engineering, representing MTP WVC Industrial Park LLC, is requesting
       final plat approval for the West Valley City Industrial Park Subdivision. As part of
       the subdivision, a new road will be dedicated along the west boundary of the
       subdivision. This road will provide future access to the remainder of the subject
       property, as well as properties to the south and west. Staker & Parson Companies
       gravel operation is located to the east, Hexcel and Alliant Techsystems are located
       to the north with vacant ground to the south and west.

       The proposed subdivision is being requested in order to create a 1 lot subdivision
       with a new Right-of-Way. It is anticipated that future uses here will be light
       manufacturing. The property owner had expected to plat the entire subdivision, but
       after contemplating the uncertainty about lot sizes and future uses, decided to plat
       one large lot. Once future uses are determined, staff will process an amended
       subdivision plat.

       Lot 1 will consist of 22 acres and as mentioned previously, will establish a new
       dedicated right-of-way. The new street will be located along the western boundary
       of the subdivision. An access easement has been noted on the plat to ensure access
       to the western property.

       The property owner will dedicate a small 20-foot section along 5400 South in the
       northeast corner of the plat. The new road will be dedicated to a width of 66 feet.
       Standard improvements along this right-of-way will be installed.

Mayor Bigelow asked who will pay for the street. Steve replied the developer. Mayor
Bigelow asked if it would a dedicated right of way. Steve replied yes.

       Mayor Bigelow stated that he represents the City on ChamberWest. He stated that
