City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On August 6, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1617, amending
the text of the land use and development code of the Draper City Municipal Code
relating to the minimum lot size for detached accessory dwelling units in a specified
portion of the South Mountain Development. The complete ordinance is on file at the
Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 7th
day of August 2024. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
