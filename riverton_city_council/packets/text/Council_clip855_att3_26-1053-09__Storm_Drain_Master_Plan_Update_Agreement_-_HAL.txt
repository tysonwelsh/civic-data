Riverton City Storm Drain Master Plan
Riverton City needs to update their 2004 Storm Drain Master Plan. One primary goal is to create
an updated storm drain master plan using information from the 2004 plan, numerous master
plan amendments, completed small area drainage studies and any other pertinent regional
studies. Another primary goal is to identify capital projects and evaluate those projects for
impact fee eligibility.

Storm Drain Master Plan Suggested Scope of Work
The following is an outline for the tentative elements of the storm drain master plan.
Consultant can add or delete if they identify missing tasks/subtasks or, conversely,
identify redundant tasks/subtasks:

  1. Introduction
     o Provide an executive summary with the critical elements of the study
     o Define study purpose
     o Provide a background for the study
  2. Existing System Description
     o Using the Riverton City’s GIS mapping and past studies
              Identify the service area
              Identify stormwater originating outside of Riverton’s jurisdiction
     o Identify natural channels
     o Identify major creeks
     o Identify canals systems
     o Identify relevant past stormwater studies including, but not limited to the following:
              Salt Lake County Southwest Canal and Creek Study
              Butterfield Creek area Studies
              Foothills stormwater study
              Midas Creek area study
              Rose Creek study
              2010 Riverton City Storm Drain Master Plan Amendment
              Riverton 2004 Storm Drain Master Plan
              Other plans/studies listed on the Riverton City storm water website
  3. Riverton City Municipal Code Review
     o The City’s current code has not been updated to reflect recently enacted State laws.
     o Consultant to review current City codes and compare to State code requirements and
          other regulations.
     o Consultant to review the stormwater utility fees and recommend an update to the
          amount of the fees and an update to the fee structure (how the fees are charged)
     o Consultant to also review for best management practices.
     o Consultant to provide a list of suggested changes to bring Riverton’s code up to
          current requirements.
  4. Riverton City Standard Specifications and Plans Manual Review
     o The City’s Standard Plans Manual was updated in early 2023. The City’s Specifications
          were not updated in 2023
     o Consultant to review current manual and make recommendations to update the
          specifications and plans.
     o   Consultant to also review for development best management practices.
     o   Specific concerns:
               The City does not allow stone pore space to be used for underground storage.
                This concept needs to be reviewed to either stay the course or update,
               The City finds localized private surface detention/retention ponds have a
                tendency to disappear over time. Suggest best management practices to
                address this issue.
               Determine if the City’s standard storm event is appropriate.
               Review City’s requirement allowing only 15” dia. concrete pipe in the public
                ROW.
5.   Hydrology and Hydraulics Study
     o Riverton staff does not believe there is value in creating a full City hydrology and
         hydraulics model. Right now, the City may be interested only in area models
         associated with a specific area study. Examples follow:
               No name creek east of Lovers Lane – This creek has not been previously
                studied but with all the in-fill development in the area, it does need to be
                evaluated.
               Butterfield Creek study – A study was recently performed along the south
                reach of Butterfield Creek – The projects in this reach were evaluated for
                impact fee eligibility. Those that are eligible are to be included in the CIP plan.
                The other projects need to be discussed to determine recommended action.
               Review & provide recommendations on the City’s storm water restriction map
6.   Transfer of 12600 South
     o 12600 South between Bangerter and Mountain View Corridor is under the
         jurisdiction of Riverton City. In the near future, this road section will be transferred to
         the state and become part of the state road system.
     o Stormwater management needs to be addressed (City stormwater becomes State
         stormwater). UDOT is currently evaluating this concern through their own beneficial
         lens.
     o The goal of this task is to evaluate the impacts on stormwater caused by this
         jurisdictional transfer and provide recommendations for the City as the transfer
         process progresses (keep the water combined or separate State and City waters).
7.    Storm Drainage Analysis
     o Using discussions with City staff, available and past studies, citizens feedback
         of flooding issues, etc., determine areas with deficiencies.
               Rose Creek and Midas Creek both have deficiencies per alternative 3 of
                the Salt Lake County Southwest Canal and Creek Study. It is assumed
                these deficiencies would be addressed by the County but need to be
                included in the City’s capital improvement plan so they do not get lost.
               Analyze deficiencies in the City’s storm system to determine if a capital
                project is required.
     o If the City’s standard storm event is changed, determine if it creates additional
         deficiencies within the storm system.
     o Aged infrastructure – using the City’s GIS mapping and storm water division,
         identify storm water infrastructure that has reached or nearing its
         replacement age.
8.   Develop a Capital Improvement Plan
     o Provide an estimate of project cost and timing/prioritization of the projects
          broken into five (5) year increments
      o Provide updated CIP list including estimate of cost and estimate of project timing
      o Evaluate capital projects for impact fee eligibility
      o Riverton City may provide up to 10 known projects to be included in the CIP/Impact
          Fee analysis
   9. Public Engagement/Outreach
      o Provide presentation material for a presentation to Riverton’s Planning Commission.
          Consultant will not need to attend the Planning Commission meeting.
      o Provide presentation material for a presentation to Riverton’s City Council. Consultant
          will not need to attend the City Council meeting.
      o Provide public outreach to have the public identify flooding areas we are not aware
          of
                 Consultant will work with staff and our Director of Communications and
                    Public Affairs to develop the outreach methodology
      o Provide periodic public updates/information through emails, website, etc.

Fees

               explaining acronyms where appropriate
