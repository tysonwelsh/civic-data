                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 25-53

    A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF RIVERTON CITY, UTAH,
 AUTHORIZING THE RETIREMENT OF POLICE SERVICE DOG “TITAN” FROM
       ACTIVE DUTY WITH THE RIVERTON POLICE DEPARTMENT

        WHEREAS, the Riverton Police Department recognizes the valuable service rendered
by its K-9 unit and the important role such canine officers play in protecting the citizens of
Riverton and enhancing the safety of the community; and
     WHEREAS, Police Service Dog “Titan” has served faithfully and diligently as a
member of the Riverton Police Department’s K-9 unit for eight years; and
        WHEREAS, Titan, has received professional certifications as a Narcotics Dog and as a
Patrol Dog, which enabled him to perform duties while serving in roles related to patrol,
narcotics detection, suspect tracking, and public engagement; and
      WHEREAS, Titan is also an unanimously certified “Good-Boi” by the Riverton Police
Department officers and staff; and
        WHEREAS, Titan has played a material role in responding to innumerable calls for
service, with acts of performance ranging from drug-detection to criminal apprehension, and
giving bad guys the stink-eye and maybe a few nips to preserve the peace; and
       WHEREAS, after aforesaid dedicated service, it is now appropriate to retire Police
Service Dog Titan from active duty with the Riverton Police Department; and
        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council wishes to express their gratitude for Titan’s
service, and to ensure his ongoing well-being and care following retirement; and
         WHEREAS, it is deemed to be in the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of
the citizens of Riverton City that Titan remain in the custody and care of his handler upon
retirement; and
     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:
       1.      Police Service Dog “Titan” is hereby officially retired from active duty with the
               Riverton Police Department, effective as of the ___ day of ____________, 20__.
       2.      Titan shall be removed from the Department’s active roster and relieved of further
               service obligations as a police service dog.
       3.      Custody, care, and responsibility for Titan shall be transferred to his handler,
               __________________________ (“Handler”), at no cost to the City, effective
               upon the date of his retirement.
      4.     The Riverton City Council and the Riverton Police Department hereby express
             their deep gratitude to Titan for his loyal, courageous, and dedicated service to the
             community of Riverton.
      5.     This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.


     PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of
November, 2025 by the following vote:
