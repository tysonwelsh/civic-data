RICHARD NEILSON (Public Works Director) SAID THE PLAN RIGHT NOW IS TO DE-
COMMISSION THE OLD FUEL SITE AT THE PUBLIC WORKS COMPOND BEFORE
THEY MOVE TO A NEW PUBLIC WORKS FACILITY. IF THEY STAY AT THEIR
CURRENT LOCATION THIS WILL HAVE TO BE REBUILT. THERE IS MONEY SET
ASIDE FOR THE DECOMMISSIONING BUT NOT THE RECONSTRUCTION.

TANNER (Associate Public Works Director) STATED IT GETS TESTED EVERY YEAR. THE
TANKS ARE NOT LEAKING BUT THEY HAVE BEEN IN THE GROUND FOR OVER 40
YEARS AND THERE MIGHT BE SOME CONTAMINATION.

WASTE INSURANCE AS WELL AS EARTHQUAKE INSURANCE.
