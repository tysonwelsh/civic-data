1. Discussion, East Central Plan – public input strategy

    Mr. Spencer indicated Staff has been collecting information and is nearly ready to host an
    update for the East Central Community Plan. Because the Plan is relatively current, this
    update is not an overhaul of the existing plan but is to address three items which will or
    have affected the East Central Community. The first is the UTA Transit plan for Bus Rapid
    Transit which will travel through this community and may affect future development in the
    area. The second is an effort to include strategies of the 9-Rails Arts District Plan which
    was adopted a few years ago, and the third is to determine whether Accessory Dwelling
    Units should be allowed in the R-1 areas of the East Central Community where the existing
    plan has direction they should not be allowed. At the time the plan was adopted, these were
    allowed only on a petition basis, and have since been allowed in all residential zones but
    due to the language in the Plan, they were excluded from these areas. This discussion is an
    effort to determine whether the Commission is comfortable with pursuing a town meeting
    using an electronic/remote meeting rather than live meetings as well as how to inform area
    residents of the upcoming meeting. Because of the diversity of this community, there may
    be some language barriers. Staff has met with the City Diversity Committee to gain some
    insight to how to involve all citizens and was directed to OgdenCan, an interest group
    supporting this community. Staff is also exploring the NextDoor app and is working on an
    East Central Community website. He indicated Staff anticipates mailing postcards to both
    owners and residents of the community and will provide translators for the online meeting.
    He also has made contact with Sara Meiss who is working with Ogden Arts and the 9-Rails
    community in an effort to create an online presence for the plan amendment with their social
    media followings. He asked if the Commission has other ideas as to how to involve citizens
    of the community.

   Commissioner Stoker felt social media is a good option in that it is free and if the City could
   partner with some strategic groups of the community such as restaurants, the messages
   could be spread quickly. She also asked if there is a graphic designer the City might have
   access to in an effort to create simple yet effective content. Chair Graf felt while some may
   not participate in social media, he felt everyone responds to text messages if there is a way
   to obtain cell number information. Mr. Spencer indicated while the Planning Staff is limited
   to its expertise, the City does have a marketing division who may be willing to help
   participate and create simple and effective content for social media. Chair Graf expressed
   concern there may be limited internet access in this community. Commissioner Sandau
   stated each elementary student has been provided a Chrome Book by the School District,
   and that the District has committed to creating hot spots for internet access. Commissioner
   Boykin suggested flyers might be prepared and sent home with elementary school children.

   Mr. Spencer suggested the online open house be scheduled for October 22nd at 6pm via
   Zoom format. He stated Staff has put together some questions for an online survey to be
   completed before or during the Zoom meeting. He reviewed each of these questions and
   asked for feedback from the Commission.

   Commission asked that he provide a progress report and update at the regular October
   Commission meeting.

There being no additional business before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
6:30pm.
