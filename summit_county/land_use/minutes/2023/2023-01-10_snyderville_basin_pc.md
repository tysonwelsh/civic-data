DJ Hubler made a motion to approve
a Conditional Use Permit with the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval and with the additional conditions of approval as discussed which are 1) any road damage caused by construction of the building shall be mitigated or repaired at the cost of the applicant, 2) the building shall comply with code required on stream setbacks, 3) the height of the building shall comply with code when measured against elevations interpolated prior to unpermitted grading, and 4) any water runoff to neighboring lots shall be mitigated by the applicant
. Chris Conabee seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
