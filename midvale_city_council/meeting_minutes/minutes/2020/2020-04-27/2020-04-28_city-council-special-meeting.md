MIDVALE CITY
CITY COUNCIL MEETING
Minutes
Tuesday, April 28, 2020
													

MAYOR:			Robert Hale 

COUNCIL MEMBERS:	Council Member Quinn Sperry 
				Council Member Paul Glover 
				Council Member Heidi Robinson  
				Council Member Bryant Brown 
				Council Member Dustin Gettel

STAFF:	Kane Loader, City Manager; Matt Dahl, Assistant City Manager; Kyle Maurer, Administrative Services Director; Rori Andreason, H.R. Director/City Recorder; Lisa Garner, City Attorney; and Jake Shepherd, Network Administrator.

Mayor Robert Hale called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

I.	GENERAL BUSINESS
	A.	WELCOME AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

	B.	ROLL CALL - Council Members Heidi Robinson, Dustin Gettel, Bryant Brown, and Quinn Sperry, and Paul Glover were present at roll call. 

II.	CLOSED SESSION TO DISCUSS PENDING AND REASONABLY IMMINENT LITIGATION

MOTION:	Council Member Dustin Gettel MOVED to go into a closed session to discuss pending and reasonably imminent litigation. The motion was SECONDED by Council Member Bryant Brown.  Mayor Hale called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for roll call vote.  The voting was as follows:	
			Council Member Quinn Sperry		Aye						Council Member Paul Glover		Aye
			Council Member Heidi Robinson	Aye
			Council Member Bryant Brown		Aye
			Council Member Dustin Gettel		Aye

The Council went into closed session at 6:07 p.m.

MOTION:	Council Member Dustin Gettel MOVED to reconvene into open session. The motion was SECONDED by Council Member Quinn Sperry.  Mayor Hale called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for vote.  The motion passed unanimous.

The Council reconvene into open session at 7:39 p.m.

II.	ADJOURN

MOTION:	Council Member Paul Glover MOVED to reconvene into open session. The motion was SECONDED by Council Member Quinn Sperry.  Mayor Hale called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for vote.  The motion passed unanimous.

The meeting adjourned at 7:39 p.m.


					
Rori L. Andreason, MMC
CITY RECORDER

Approved this 5th day of May 2020
