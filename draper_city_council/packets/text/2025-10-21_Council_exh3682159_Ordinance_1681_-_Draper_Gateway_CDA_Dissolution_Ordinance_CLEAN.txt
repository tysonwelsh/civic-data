                                 ORDINANCE #1681

        AN ORDINANCE DISSOLVING THE DRAPER GATEWAY COMMUNITY
        DEVELOPMENT PROJECT AREA ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
                          DRAPER CITY, UTAH

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Community Reinvestment Agency (the “Agency”)
adopted a Project Area Plan (the “Project Area Plan”) for the Draper Gateway
Community Development Project Area (the “Project Area”) pursuant to the Limited
Purpose Local Government Entities - Community Reinvestment Agency Act, Utah
Code Ann. 17C-1-101 et seq. and any subsequent, replacement or amended law or
act (the “Act”); and

      WHEREAS, the Agency has adopted a resolution dissolving the Project Area;
and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code 17C-1-702(1)(b) of the Act states that the Project Area
remains in existence until the community legislative body adopts an ordinance
dissolving the Project Area; and

      WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the City Council of Draper City has
determined to dissolve the Project Area;

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

     Section 1. Dissolution of the Project Area. Draper City hereby dissolves the
Draper Gateway Community Development Project Area, the boundaries of which are
shown and described on the legal descriptions and map attached hereto as Exhibit
A.

       Section 2. Notice and Transmittals. Within 30 days after the adoption of this
Ordinance, City staff and consultants are hereby authorized and directed to transmit
copies of this Ordinance to each taxing entity that levies or imposes a tax on property
within the dissolved Project Area.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.
