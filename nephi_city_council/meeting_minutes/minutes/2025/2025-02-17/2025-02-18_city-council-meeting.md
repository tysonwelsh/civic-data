PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Blaine Malquist addressed the council and stated that he had been a resident of Nephi City for over 50
years. He raised concerns about the city purchasing a $60,000 sprayer for the golf course, citing health
risks from pesticides containing PFAs. Mr. Malquist referenced a letter from Utah Physicians for a
Healthy Environment. Mr. Malquist emphasized the importance of addressing the health threat posed
by these chemicals and requested a public response from the city.
   Malquist added how important the rabies vaccination is for animals. He said that he was once bit by a
dog and had to wait for months for the results of whether the animal was infected with rabies.

ORDINANCE 02-18-2025 ADOPTED-PUBLIC IMPROVEMENT REQUIREMENTS:

    City Administrator Seth Atkinson informed the council that in a recent analysis done by the state’s
Property Rights Ombudsman, it was determined that a city utility had the authority to determine its
service level requirements but that it would need to be clearly stated in the city code. To date Nephi
Power has, through its standards, required all residential, commercial, and industrial subdivisions to
provide redundant connection or looping as well as sufficient capacity to meet approved uses within a
zone. Based on the analysis offered by the Property Rights Ombudsman’s office this needs to be clearly
stated in the city’s code and not just in the standards.

   Mr. Atkinson stated that city staff had researched the code language from other cities who provide
power and had compiled language to update the Nephi City code for the Electric utility. Ordinance
02-18-2025 had been created to modify the existing code language regarding electric public utility
requirements. This proposed ordinance had been reviewed and recommended by the Planning
Commission. He said that similar ordinances will be forthcoming for the other city utilities.

GAS DEPARTMENT OUT-OF-STATE TRAVEL REQUESTS APPROVED:

  Gas Superintendent Zach Larsen addressed the council and requested that employees from the Gas
Department be allowed to travel to the following out-of-state trainings:

    1.​ MEA Evaluator Training in Bloomington, MN. Mr. Larsen stated that he had learned during the
        annual state inspection that the city is required to have a trained evaluator for the gas
        department’s operator qualifications. At least two of the department’s employees will need to
        attend.
    2.​ American Public Gas Association Annual Conference in Coeur d’ Alene, Idaho on July 20th-23rd.
        Mr. Larsen said that this training would be a good opportunity to network with other municipal
        gas companies. There will be classes on pipeline practices, new technologies, new Pipeline and
        Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA) regulations. Many of these conferences are
        held in the eastern United States where most public gas companies exist. This is a unique
        chance to attend a national conference held in a neighboring state.

Councilor Cowan moved to approve the out-of-state travel requests from the Gas Department. Councilor
Travis Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
