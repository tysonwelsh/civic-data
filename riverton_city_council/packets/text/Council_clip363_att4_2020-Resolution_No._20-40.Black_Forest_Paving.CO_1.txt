                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-40

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY TO APPROVE AND RATIFY CHANGE
ORDER #1 WITH BLACK FOREST PAVING TO COMPLETE THE OVERLAY OF
3600 W FROM 13050 S TO 12600 S

        WHEREAS, The City desires to overlay and reconstruct 3600 W from 13400 S to 12600
S prior to the closure of 12600 S for the Bangerter 3 Interchange Project; and

       WHEREAS, 3600 W will be used as the detour of all for all west bound traffic with the
closure of 12600 S; and

       WHEREAS, The City has entered into a contract with Black Forest Paving to overlay
3600 W from 13400 S to 13050 S as part of the 2019/2020 budget with plans of completing the
remainder of the overlay from 13050 S to 12600 S in the Spring of 2021 under a separate
contract which is included in the 2020/2021 budget; and

       WHEREAS, The City has recently been informed that UDOT has moved ahead of
schedule, the closure of 12600 S for the Bangerter 3 Interchange Project to January 2021; and

         WHEREAS, The City has concerns of finishing the project prior to the closure of 12600
S; and

       WHEREAS, The City received an exceptionally low bid from Black Forest Paving for
paving 3600 W from 13400 S to 13150 S; and

       WHEREAS, The Black Forest Paving has agreed to hold their prices and complete by
change order, the remainder of 3600 W; and

        WHEREAS, The City has prepared Change Order #1 in the Amount of $ 230,205.22 for
approval by the City Council outlining the items to be provided and included in the construction
of the Project; and

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Riverton City Council approves
and ratifies Change Order #1 with Black Forest Paving in the amount of $230,205.22 for the
overlay of 3600 W from 13050 S to 12600 S and the Mayor is hereby authorized to sign and
execute said Change Order #1.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 2nd day of
June 2020 by the following vote:
