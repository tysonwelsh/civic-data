Citizen Public Input

              Rob McDaniel, Principal, Cyprus High School, stated Tyler Garcia, former Cyprus
High Student Body Officer and football player, has been hired as the new football coach.

             Tyler Garcia stated he grew up in Magna and attended Cyprus High School. He
has been coaching football since he graduated high school. He is a big advocate of giving back
to the community and will be contacting the Council to find community projects.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
                 Donnie Sweazy stated he is a little broken hearted. He came to inform the Council
about the meeting with the District Attorney’s Office. More agencies are becoming involved. He
has been a member of the Magna community and donates to everything and to anyone who asks.
He owns a small business and pledges allegiance to the flag and joined the military. Code
enforcement needs to de-weaponized. Adult Protective Services wants to check on the lady who
lives in the home. He has dealt with Motor Vehicle Enforcement, Health Department, EPA,
building inspector, and code enforcement. Enforcement has been going on for years and hopes
it comes to a stop quick. Small businesses are being chased out of town and he is ready to pack
up and move. Magna is a neat community, he knows everyone and has helped everyone in
Magna. Everyone he knows is getting a code enforcement violation because they are car dealers
or repair cars. There is not anyone in Magna who has not had their car fixed or been helped. He
is owed over $400,000 in auto repairs that he has done for the community. He has donated tables,
T.V.’s, to movie in the park, and has cut locks anytime anyone has asked. Christmas night he
repaired a tire for the local mail carrier. This is the type of person that is being ran out of town and
he is appalled.

Magna Chamber of Commerce

                Sara Van Roosendaal stated the Chamber is having its Holiday Jingle Lunch on

Reports from other Municipal Services Providers

Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Detective Buhler stated the Stroll with Santa marathon was a success. Salt Lake
County Sheriff’s Office, Protective Services Bureau, and UPD did Shop with a Shield. Families
throughout Salt Lake County with five families being from Magna. The families were invited to
breakfast and then driven to Walmart to shop. The children received $200 gift cards to spend on
themselves and the parent received $200 to spend on household items and leave with a turkey
dinner. He thanked the Magna Metro Township Council and the Magna Community Council for
their donation to fund Sub for Santa families. On New Year’s Eve Saltair will host a rave. Saltair
management has pushed for more law enforcement to be present to prevent the problems its had
in the past. Planning for the Youth Academy will begin in January. The program is mainly for kids
ages 14-18.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

               Council Member Ferguson stated he attended a facilities meeting and informed
the board that Magna wants to keep the fire station on Main Street where it currently is.

               Council Member Prokopis stated no significant fires were reported in October
and calls for medical were typical. The UFA Board decided not to pursue litigation against the
former administration. Outside legal counsel was hired and based on the probability of winning
and recovering funds, its recommendation was that it would not be a prudent use of tax payer
money to pursue litigation. UFA is watching the Brighton annexation and what the effects of
incorporation will be. Thirty new fire fighters will start training on February 4. The December safety
message is to not overload electrical outlets, keep trees watered, and be careful using alternate
sources of heat.
