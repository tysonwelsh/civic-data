CITY OF EMIGRATION CANYON PUBLIC NOTICE

          Notice of Vacancy and Appointment as At-Large Council Member


Pursuant to Utah Code § 20A-1-510, Emigration Canyon hereby gives notice of a vacancy of At-
Large Council Member due to David Brems, a current At-Large Council Member, becoming the
Mayor-Elect. The remaining term will be from January 20, 2026 through December 31, 2027.

Individuals interested in being considered for appointment must meet the qualifications for office
and officially declare their candidacy (as outlined below) for consideration. Applicants must:

•   Be a United States citizen.
•   Be a registered voter of Emigration Canyon.
•   Be a resident of Emigration Canyon for at least 12 consecutive months prior to the appointment
    date of January 20, 2026.


The application includes:

    •   Declaration of Candidacy Form **DO NOT SIGN PRIOR TO APPOINTMENT**
    •   Resume (not to exceed 1 page)
    •   Conflict of Interest Form
    •   $50 Declaration of Candidacy Filing Fee (per current Emigration Canyon City Fee Schedule)

All applicants must make an appointment and declare their candidacy IN PERSON (BY
APPPOINTMENT ONLY) with the City Recorder, Diana Baun, at the MSD Offices in Taylorsville
between Monday, January 5, 2026 and Monday, January 19, 2026 at the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District Offices, 860 W. Levoy Dr., Suite 300, Taylorsville, UT 84123. Please
contact Diana Baun at the phone number/email below to schedule an appointment and receive a
copy of the application packet.


The Emigration Canyon City Council will interview candidates and select the appointee during the
City Council Meeting to be held on Tuesday, January 20, 2026, at 7:00p.m. at the Emigration
Canyon Fire Station, 5025 E. Emigration Canyon Rd., Salt Lake City, UT 84108.


For more information, contact the Emigration Canyon City Recorder at (385) 377-9466 or
dbaun@msd.utah.gov
