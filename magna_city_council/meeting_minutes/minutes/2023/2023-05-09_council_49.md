Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Paul Ashton stated the attorneys for the metro townships, along with the District
Attorney’s office, intend to have a conference call every Wednesday to discuss the Salt Lake
Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA). Additionally, the Unified Police Department
(UPD) will hold a second meeting at the beginning of the month to discuss reorganization.
Representatives Anthony Loubet and Jordan Teuscher have requested an interim study to
examine whether the metro townships should incorporate or annex. The study would also look
at possible funding sources to pay for law enforcement. Magna intends to incorporate and there
will be a resolution to that effect at the next meeting. Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns
Metro Township, has suggested legislation that would designate all metro townships as cities or
towns based on population, effective a certain date. The residents would then need to elect a
mayor and council members. He advised the members of the Council to continue to share their
thoughts and ideas with legal counsel. The metro township attorneys are trying to be collaborative
throughout this process.

Public Comments

                Mayor Dan Peay stated he had asked Clint Dilley, General Manager, Magna
Water District, to speak on the Flip Your Strip program administered by Utah Water Savers.

been pushing for adoption of water efficiency standards and landscape ordinances. Money has
been made available, but Utah Water Savers is telling grant applicants to ask their local legislators

Council what it would like Magna Water District to tell its customers regarding grant applications.

the Code, and it is going through the final steps of review by legal counsel.

adopted the standards.

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-05-01                                                    DATE: May 9, 2023

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL GRANTING
       AN EXCEPTION TO THE REQUIREMENT TO INSTALL PUBLIC
       IMPROVEMENTS ON 2820 SOUTH STREET FOR DAKOTA LANE ONE LOT
       SUBDIVISION

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

      WHEREAS, the Dakota Lane One Lot Subdivision Amended plat was approved by the
Magna Metro Township Planning Commission on October 14th, 2021; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to section 14.12.020 of the Magna Code, installation of street
improvements, consisting of curb, gutter, sidewalk, and asphalt tie-in for the property frontage
along 2820 South Street was required as a condition of subdivision approval; and

         WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Public Works Engineering Division has reported to the
Magna Metro Township Council that a federally funded street improvement project has been
approved and is under way for the improvements, consisting of curb, gutter, sidewalk, and asphalt
tie-in, on the north side of said 2820 South Street; and

        WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Public Works Engineering Division has further reported
that the installation of improvements on 2820 South Street along the frontage of the Dakota Lane
One Lot Subdivision would at this point impede rather than aid the aforementioned street
improvement project; and

      WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 14.12.150 of the Magna Code, the Magna Metro
Township Mayor (acting as chair of the Magna Metro Township Council) may grant exceptions to
the requirements of Chapter 14.12 after receiving recommendations from the planning
commission and the public works engineer; and

        WHEREAS, the planning commission and public works engineer have both forwarded
positive recommendations regarding the request for exception submitted by the subdivider, and

         WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council believes that installation of the
improvements along 2820 South Street in one, contiguous funded project would result in the
installation of better quality improvements than if done through multiple projects.

Discussion on U111 Skybridge and Intersection

               Council Member Hull stated Granite School District wants a skybridge near the
high school to the new subdivision. The Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) has been
amazing throughout the meeting process. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(MSD) and Salt Lake County Council Member Dave Alvord are involved. The sky bridge would
cost around $5 million dollars and there are concerns over how to obtain the land and whether
the high school students will use the skybridge. There are logistical issues to work through.
UDOT wants to enact a corridor agreement with the Council.

Discuss Township Meeting Procedures

             Rori Andreason asked if the Council was okay with her putting action items on
the workshop meeting.

              Council Member Hull stated she liked the model West Valley City used. They
would hold the workshop meeting and have that open to the public, have a break, and then hold
a regular meeting.

               Council Member Barney stated workshop meetings allow the Council to review
the item, ask questions, and fully understand the issue before making a decision. He does not
want to feel pressure to come to a decision without having a full understanding of what is being
