                                    RIVERTON CITY
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 25-46

    RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL APPROVING THE CASH
     RECEIPTS AND DEPOSIT POLICY OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES
                            DEPARTMENT
       WHEREAS, the Utah State Officer’s office is charged with the duty to “prescribe
uniform accounting and reporting procedures for cities, in conformity with “generally accepted
accounting principles,” pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-154 (1)(a); and

       WHEREAS, the Utah State Auditor’s office is further required to “prepare instructional
materials, conduct training programs and render other services deemed necessary to assist cities
in implementing the uniform accounting, budgeting and reporting procedures;” pursuant to Utah
Code § 10-6-154 (1)(d); and

        WHEREAS, in performance of these duties, the Utah State Auditor has published and
recommended the adoption of policies which aid in the appropriate administration of financial
transactions affecting municipalities; and

        WHEREAS, the Utah State Auditor has recommended the implementation of a formal
policy to govern the handling of cash receipts and deposits by municipalities; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Riverton City staff has
prepared a Cash Receipts and Deposits Policy which meets the recommendations of the Utah
State Auditor; and

        WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that adoption of this policy
will protect the health, safety, and welfare of the community by enhancing accountability of its
processes to manage cash payments tendered at Riverton City;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

   1. The Riverton City Council hereby approves and adopts the Cash Receipts and Deposits
      Policy as prepared by City staff and attached to this Resolution as Exhibit A.

   2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
September 2025 by the following vote:
