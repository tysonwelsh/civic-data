32   2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Amy Sylvester's request for conditional use
33   approval to board two horses on 0.59-acres at 196 West 2875 North in an R-1-15 zone.
34   Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.
35
36   Commissioner Lockhart asked why it was a conditional use. Jacob clarified this is a zone that it
37   is allowed use by State Law and is permitted.
38
39   The applicant, Nick Sylvester, was present. He stated he does not plan on using this for
40   commercial use. The horses will be boarded and will be exercised regularly at an equestrian
41   park. The main use is for therapy and mental and emotional support. He would also like his
42   children to get involved in 4H. The coral will be fenced in with livestock fence panels. They will
43   be building a loafing shed with durable materials. He addressed the smell of the horses and the

     Lehi City Planning Commission                   1                                   February 8, 2024
44   flies.
45
46   Commissioner Everett asked about the placement of the loafing shed and whether it meets the
47   code.
48
49   Commission Chair opened the public hearing.
50
51   Jana Rampton stated she was opposed to the item. She spoke with the applicant this morning and
52   asked them if they would consider moving the horses to another property. She stated nobody in
53   the neighborhood wanted the horses on the property. The neighborhood recently got rid of the
54   mink farms that brought in the flies. She was concerned with the 60-foot setback. She was
55   worried about the value of the homes.
56
57   Jared Rhyme stated he was opposed to the request. The mink farm that was near the homes
58   brought in a lot of flies. He went through a lot of work to get rid of that. He would like to make
59   sure the home value stays. He said he is concerned about not being able to resale his home,
60   although he bought his home with the purpose of staying. He appreciated the applicants are
61   looking at putting in a closed space.
62
63   Ted Rampton asked if he could request some conditions. He was wondering if Sylvester’s could
64   mitigate flies, rodents, animal noises, etc. He asked how they would be enforced. He stated they
65   wanted to remain cordial neighbors. He asked if the applicants would be willing to create a view
66   buffer. Mr. Rampton asked if the equestrian activities could be limited to 8:00 am to 10:00 pm.
67
68   Jacob Curtis read the online comment from Lauren Simper regarding where the horse trailer will
69   be parked and that it be prohibited from parking on the road keep the sidewalk cleared and the
70   drainage be contained to the owner's property.
71
72   Jennell Palmer stated she is concerned about the flies and the rodents. She stated the corrals are
73   always the furthest away from their own home, but always closest to the neighbors.
74
75   Commission Chair Jackson closed the public hearing.
76
77   Jacob Curtis wanted to clarify that noticing was done according to code and state requirements.
78
79   Commissioner Everett stated his neighbor has horses and he loves them and considers them his
80   best neighbors. He understands the concerns and the impacts, and he can only speak to his
81   experience, but he enjoys having this in his neighborhood. He is close to a mink farm, he likes to
82   think he can relate, and he appreciates the thoughts that have gone into the mitigation of the
83   request.
84
85   Commissioner Lockhart stated she understands the concerns, but they are bound to the code. She
86   stated the amount of care and concern the applicant brought to the meeting was thoughtful. The
87   60-foot requirement will need to be met if that is part of the code.
88
89   Commission Chair Jackson stated developments will impose on what your views are. He also
90   wanted to take the opportunity to remind the public to engage with state and local leaders
91   concerning the noticing requirements. He does not see how this conditional use can be granted if
     Lehi City Planning Commission                    2                                   February 8, 2024
 92   it needs to be measured from a certain setback.
 93
 94   Commissioner Roberts echoed Commissioner Jackson's sentiments regarding the setback.
 95
 96   Commissioner Everett stated that it would be the applicant's responsibility to meet the
 97   parameters of the code.
 98
 99   Commissioner Lockhart stated the time to say something was when there was a zone change,
100   because we have to respect property rights, and it is the applicant's responsibility to meet the
101   code.
102
103   Kim Struthers stated there was a reasonable condition to allow the request.
104
105           Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to grant the conditional use of Amy Sylvester’s
106           request for conditional use approval to board two horses on 0.59-acres at 196 West
107           2875 North in an R-1-15 zone; including all findings stated in the report and all DRC
108           comments, noting the property itself meets the requirements. Commissioner Roberts
109           seconded.
110
111           Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Roberts,
112           yes; Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
113           unanimously.

115   2.2) Public hearing and consideration of Brent Ventura's request for approval of the
116   updated Skyridge Subdivision Concept Plan, a 5-lot residential development located at
117   approximately 260 East 2750 North.).
118   Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.
119
120   The applicant, Brent Ventura, Reno Mahe, and Jason Mandaleras, were present. Skyridge High
121   School does not want access points to the school, and they will be working with the City. Reno
122   stated the exceptions they are asking for an integral sidewalk and to eliminate the park strip.
123   Commissioner Kunze asked about the extra lot. Reno stated he plans on building in the future.
124   Commissioner Everett asked about the design. The applicants stated they chose to have more
125   open space.
126
127   Commission Chair opened the public hearing.
128
129   Chad Watkins left an online comment stating he would like to keep the sidewalk, asserting they
130   increase walkability.
131
132   Commission Chair closed the public hearing.
