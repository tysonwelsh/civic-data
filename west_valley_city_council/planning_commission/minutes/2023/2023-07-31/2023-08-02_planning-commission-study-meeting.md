GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-7-2023  Petition by CHRISTOPHER CLIFFORD requesting a General Plan change from Mixed
            Use and Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Small Lot Residential (4 to 7
            units/acre) and a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RM
            (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). The property is located at 5143 West 3500 South
            on 4.34 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:       Steve Pastorik explained that the Commission looked at a proposal for this property last
                  year, and they approved it. However, it was denied by the City Council because they did
                  not favor the private streets. He presented the submitted concept plan and noted that the
                  size of the buildings is not to scale with the size of the roads. Steve stated that staff had
                  modified the concept for a more accurate reflection of the buildings and presented the
                  revised plan to the Commission. He noted that the applicant included the parcel to the
                  south in their project last year; however, they are not including it in this 21-unit proposal.
                  Steve said that the Public Works Department commented some turnaround would need to
                  be added to the south end of the access street. He explained that the roads are public and
                  noted that they are proposing attached units, which allows for a 44-foot right-of-way.
                  Steve showed a concept rendering and a floor plan for the proposed townhomes. He
                 stated that the developer would like to include basements. He reviewed other differences
                 between last year's proposal and today's proposal for the Commission.

                 Commissioner Lovato said he would be more comfortable if this were a 55+
                 development. He asked Steve if they had proposed to add a gate at the end of the cul-de-
                 sac last year. Steve stated that the prior concept showed the road terminating in a cul-de-
                 sac, and they would add a gate at the end. Steve noted that if the development was a 55+
                 community, at least 80% of the residents would need to be over 55.

                 Commissioner Winters asked if the proposed density would be similar to that of an R-1-8
                 zone. Steve said it would be approximately the same as an R-1-7 zone. He stated that a
                 PUD could have a density of up to 6.5 units/acre in the R-1-8 zone, but that is not what
                 the applicant is proposing.

                 Commissioner Winters asked if this development is required to offer amenities. Steve
                 explained that if this were a townhome development instead of a PUD, they would need
                 to meet the amenity requirements outlined in the zoning ordinance. Steve said he would
                 go into more detail in the staff report and during next week's presentation.

                 Commissioner Winters asked what the difference is between a duplex and a twin home.
                 Steve explained that with a duplex, both units would be under one ownership, whereas a
                 twin home units are under separate ownership.



SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SMI-1-2023   Petition by DAVE LLORET is requesting preliminary approval for the Advanced RV
             Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 4.81 acres. The subdivision is
             located at 4581 West 3500 South and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Steve
             Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Lehman pointed out that to the east of the subject property is the Lofts on 35th
                 development. He noted that the development, which is well under construction. Steve
                 explained that the applicant would like to divide off the property's southwest corner to
                 continue the existing use and will sell the larger parcel for a new business. He stated that
                 there is a secured gate in front of the new lot. Steve said they would have access from
                 3500 South via an access easement. Steve said the applicant had mentioned that the new
                 lot would be used as it has been used for the past 15 years. The applicant might provide
                 more information about the new use during the public hearing.

                 Commissioner Winters asked Steve if they would use the existing building or build a new
                 structure. Steve said he suspects it would be a new building; however, if they can modify
                 the existing building for the use, the current building could be an option to fit the
                 proposed use.



PUD-1-2023       Petition by RUSSELL PLATT is requesting preliminary approval for the Towns at
                 2351. This planned community will consist of 24 units on 1.84 acres. The subdivision is
                 located at 2351 South Redwood Road and is zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling
                 Residential). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)
Discussion:       Steve Lehman stated that the subject property might look familiar to the Commission
                  because it had been rezoned in 2021. He noted that this proposal is for a new townhome
                  development. Steve explained that the property is currently vacant, but a large mound of
                  dirt would need to be removed or relocated. Steve presented the site plan and explained
                  that the existing development agreement allows for 24 units, and the applicant proposed
                  three different building types. He stated that the proposed building units are large,
                  starting at 2059 square feet. Steve showed the preliminary renderings submitted by the
                  applicant. He explained that the staff made some suggestions, and there have been
                  modifications to the renderings, specifically the end units, which would provide more
                  design relief. He pointed out that there would be a fenced-in area in front of the bottom
                  units and rooftop decks. Steve stated that staff suggested an addition of a third amenity, a
                  walking path along the perimeter of the development. Steve pointed out that there would
                  be ten parking spaces for guests.

                  Commissioner Woodruff asked if we would consider this a private drive. Steve said it
                  would, but this project predates the City’s ordinance for private streets.

                  Commissioner Winters asked if the walking path would extend to the south property line.
                  Steve said he would speak with Mr. Platt to see if that was an option.

                  Commissioner Lovato asked if we could use a composite rather than a Vinyl fence. Steve
                  said that would be a good idea, mainly because it is heavily trafficked. He stated that he
                  would present the option to the applicant and noted that there is an anti-graffiti fence that
                  he would also suggest.

                  Commissioner Winters said that he agreed with Commissioner Lovato. He pointed out
                  that when a vinyl fence breaks, it is hard to repair.
