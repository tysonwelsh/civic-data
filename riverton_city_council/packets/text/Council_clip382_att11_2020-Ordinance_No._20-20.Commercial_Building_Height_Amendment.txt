                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 20-20

     AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 18.65, .70, .75, .80, .85, .95, AND .100
    SETTING MAXIMUM COMMERCIAL BUILDING HEIGHTS, PROPOSED BY
                         RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed amendment to the Riverton City Code; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to add to the Riverton City Code Sections 8.65, .70, .75, .80, .85, .95, AND .100, as
described in Exhibit “A”.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City Code shall be, and hereby is, amended as shown in Exhibit
                  “A”

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

Exhibit “A”



•   18.65.060 (2) Building Height. Buildings shall not exceed three stories or 50 feet in
    height, whichever is less.

•   18.70.060 (2) Building Height. Buildings shall not exceed two stories or 35 feet in
    height, whichever is less, except where adjacent to a regional transportation corridor
    where buildings may not exceed four stories or 80 feet in height. Where a zero lot line
    is utilized per RCC 18.70.040(4), building height adjacent to the property line shall not
    exceed one story.

•   18.75.050 (3) Building Height. Buildings shall not exceed two stories or 35 feet in
    height, whichever is less.

•   18.80.060 (2) Building Height. Buildings shall not exceed two stories or 35 feet in
    height, whichever is less, except where adjacent to a regional transportation corridor
    where buildings may not exceed four stories or 80 feet in height.

•   18.85.060 (2) Building Height. Buildings shall not exceed four stories or 80 feet in
    height, whichever is less.

•   18.95.040 Architectural Standards
          (1) Build Height. Buildings shall not exceed 50 feet in height.

•   18.100.060 (2) Building Height. Buildings shall not exceed four stories or 80 feet in
    height, whichever is less.
