                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     RESOLUTION NO. 25-60

            A RESOLUTION APPOINTING DANIEL J. MCDONALD TO SERVE AS A
            HEARING OFFICER FOR REVIEWING AND DECIDING REASONABLE
                   ACCOMMODATION REQUESTS UNDER CITY CODE

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Code section 18.165.010 requires that reasonable accommodation
requests filed by persons or entities seeking to obtain affordable housing be heard and decided by a
hearing officer with demonstrated experience as a hearing officer and knowledge of the Americans with
Disabilities Act or Fair Housing Act; and

        WHEREAS, said hearing officer is appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the
Riverton City Council; and

        WHEREAS, The Riverton City has identified a Daniel J. McDonald as a nationally recognized
expert in the laws governing the Fair Housing Act/Americans with Disabilities Act, who is also an
attorney licensed to practice in the state of Utah; and

         WHEREAS, Mr. McDonald is willing and able to serve as a hearing officer to hear and decide
fair housing-related requests for reasonable accommodation for Riverton City; and the Mayor accordingly
desires to appoint Mr. McDonald as Riverton City’s hearing officer;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

       1.      The Riverton City Council hereby provides its consent to the appointment of Daniel J.
               McDonald to serve as a hearing officer for Riverton city in considering and deciding
               requests for accommodation pursuant to the federal Fair Housing Act.

       2.      This resolution shall become effective upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 2nd day of
December, 2025 by the following vote:
