                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-77

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
           WITH RRJ CONSULTING FOR LOBBYING SERVICES

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, Purchasing issued a Request for Qualifications to prospective lobbyists to
provide lobbying services; and

       WHEREAS, from that process RRJ Consulting was selected.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with RRJ Consulting
          for Lobbying Services.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 6th day of
August 2024 by the following vote:
