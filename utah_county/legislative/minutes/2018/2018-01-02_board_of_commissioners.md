       BOUNDARY CHANGES TO SPECIFIED UTAH COUNTY VOTING PRECINCTS
(Track A, 05:00)
Utah County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson explained the rapid population growth has caused a need for two new
voter precincts in Saratoga Springs, and three new precincts in Lehi. This is to help balance some of the existing
precincts which were seeing as many as 2,000 active registered voters when the target figure is closer to 1,250. Mr.

                                             WORK SESSION
1.     UPDATE ON VOCA GRANT FUNDING BY VICTIM WITNESS COORDINATOR CHRIS
       BUCKLEY AS PER THE GRANT REGULATIONS (Continued from the December 19, 2017 Commission
       meeting)
(Track A, 22:40)
Chris Buckley of the Utah County Attorney’s Office mentioned the VOCA (Victims of Crime Act) grant has been re-
structured to span two years. She talked about the unfortunately high numbers of clientele to help between herself
and Katie Fox, the other Victim Witness Coordinator in her office who works 30 hours weekly. Referring to the
comments by County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson earlier in this session regarding population growth, Ms.
Buckley noted increase in city size usually correlates to increase in crime. She thanked the Board for approving an
additional Witness Coordinator position for the office in the upcoming year. Commissioner Lee mentioned the
focused efforts on awareness, and Ms. Buckley went on to talk about national media coverage of victims potentially
helping others to feel comfortable in stepping forward. She explained domestic violence is becoming less secretive
and there is no shame in being a victim.

                                            PUBLIC COMMENTS
(Track A, 27:35)
Provo resident Robert Stevens talked about the difference between water rights and water shares: rights are generally
for underground water below the surface, whereas shares arrive from rivers and reservoirs. Mr. Stevens hypothesized
it would cause many problems to require everyone in the county who purchases a vehicle to have annual requirements
or limitations for fuel use. He pointed to this as an example of government forcing a ‘one size fits all’ solution, without
taking variables into consideration. Mr. Stevens questioned the benefit in requiring subdivisions to meet specific
water requirements when there are many uses of land and even within similar crops the water use can vary greatly
throughout the year.
