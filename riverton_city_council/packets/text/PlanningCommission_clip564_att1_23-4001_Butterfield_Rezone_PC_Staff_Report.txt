This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are
based on reasonably debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a
recommendation to the City Council which has final decision authority for rezone applications.


Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.

rezoning 0.91-acres located at 4141 West 13400 South, to change the zoning to C-R

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.

Background
This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Damien Butterfield
for a single parcel of 0.91-acres located at 4141 West 13400 South. The requested zone is
Commercial Regional (C-R).

The subject property is currently zoned “R-1” which is a single-family residential zone with acre
lots and animal rights. However, the subject property is vacant, as is the next parcel to the
east. To the west there are three homes. Most of the area zoning is C-R. Going back to the
early 2000’s there were eight or nine large (1-acre or greater) residential lots facing 13400
South, north of Rosecreek. In 2007 several of the parcels rezoned to Regional Commercial,
but two property owners did not participate. This application will rezone one of the two
remaining properties and put all the similarly situated properties into the same zone.

The General Plan, which was adopted in August 2020, designates the area south of 13400
South as Professional Office. This designation has been on the properties since at least the
early 2000’s and was in place when the other properties were rezoned to Commercial
Regional. In 2007 the Planning Commission and City Council considered both C-PO and C-R
Zoning before deciding to rezone to C-R.
This rezone request to C-R is consistent with the other adjoining properties, places all similarly
situated properties in the same zone and doesn’t impact any residentially zoned properties.
Staff recommends this rezone be approved.
