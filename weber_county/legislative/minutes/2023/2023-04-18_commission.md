         2.       Public hearing for consideration of an ordinance and applicant driven text amendment to amend the
                  Ogden Valley Signs ordinance to allow a neighborhood identification sign to be placed on a retaining
                  wall - ZTA 2022-03.
                  Steve Burton (Planning): This is an applicant driven request by Wolf Creek Utah. Currently the
                  Ogden Valley sign code does not allow subdivision or neighborhood signs on retaining walls. The
                  Applicant’s proposed language is two sentences. Subdivision entry signs may be placed on a
                  retaining wall. Signage should not exceed a height of 4.5ft and width of 40ft. A 4-2 vote by the
                  Ogden Valley Planning Commission on February 7th, 2023 to recommend approving the applicant’s
                  Proposed language. Via the proposed Ordinance, staff recommends changing terms to
                  “neighborhood entry sign” in lieu of “subdivision entry sign”. Staff also recommends allowing
                  neighborhood entry signs installed on a monument sign or retaining wall. Staff also adjusted the sign
                  height limitation to 5ft while retaining 40ft width. The Ordinance also specifies allowance between
                  retaining wall or monument signs where only one can be implemented, not both.

         3.       Public Comments:
                  Ron Gleason, Eden, UT: I have no objection with signs on retaining walls. I ask that lighting either
                  not be allowed or capped at a specific number of lumens. There is no definition for material of
                  retaining walls, reflective or non-reflective. With the current sign ordinance and the number of
                  lumens that can be used. The proposed sign moves the sign into a category allowing 100 lumens.
                  This is the International Dark Sky Week.
                  Mr. Burton: The way the ordinance is written right now requires Dark Sky compliance for all signs.
