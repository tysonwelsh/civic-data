Section 10-6-128 of the Utah Code states that the budget for the City may be amended
by the Murray City Municipal Council following a duly noticed public hearing. Pursuant to
proper notice, the Murray City Municipal Council held a public hearing on February 6, 2024,
to consider proposed amendments to the Fiscal Year 2023-2024 budget. After considering
public comment, the Murray City Municipal Council wants to amend the Fiscal Year 2023-
2024 budget.
