6. CONTRACT WITH GSBS ARCHITECTS FOR A FEASIBILITY STUDY FOR THE EXPANSION OF THE MEDICAL
AND MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES AND REMOTE COURT ACCESS PROVIDED BY THE EXISTING WEBER
COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY

Chief Deputy Phillip Reese, of the County Sheriff's Office, proposed a feasibility study for expansion of the
medical and mental health services and remote court access provided by the existing Weber County
Correctional Facility. Feasibility Study deliverables include: review of existing facility to identify additional
building functions, develop four design solutions, analyze each based on needs and cost, provide recommended
course of action.

Commissioner Froerer stated there had been a substantial increase in mental health issues in the county in the
past several years. The number of related lawsuits had increased for counties/cities running their own jails.
Weber County’s prior decision to go with a third party provider had proven to be very successful and a good
move for our county in regards to cost and liability. He stated it is wise to plan for the future by conducting
this study.

Commissioner Jenkins asked how the study would be funded and Chief Reese responded that $20k was in last
year’s budget request for this study and the remainder would be pulled from building budget.

Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the contract with GSBS Architects for a feasibility study for the
expansion of the medical and mental health services and remote court access provided by the existing Weber
County Correctional Facility; Chair Harvey seconded.

Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye
