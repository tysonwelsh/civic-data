ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-4-2022   Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY to amend the text of the zoning
            ordinance by reducing the minimum area required for shopping centers
            in Section 7-7-124 from one acre to three quarters of one acre. (Staff –
            Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik explained that currently, the ordinance establishes a minimum
                 lot size for a shopping center to be one acre, and this provision was put into
                 place in March of 2017. Steve stated that at that time, the City saw several L-
                 shaped strip malls and wanted to see larger malls with more tenant options. He
                 noted that there had been several discussions at the Council level that one acre
                 may be too large of a minimum requirement and are proposing three-quarters
                 of an acre minimum.
                Commissioner McEwen asked what the zoning is on some of the newer
                projects. He gave an example of one building with large bay doors in the front
                of each unit leased to several tenants. Commissioner McEwen feels the
                structure resembles a strip mall. Steve said to be considered a mall, and there
                needs to be some retail use. He believes the project Commissioner McEwen
                refers to is an office/warehouse use. However, he needs to follow up on that.

                Commissioner McEwen asked if the Council had asked how many pieces of
                land the proposed amendment would affect. Steve said he has two maps, one
                with both developed and undeveloped land and another with just undeveloped
                property, that he will add to the Commission’s packets before next week’s
                meeting.


ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-9-2022   Petition by SHANE KEYVANI requesting a zone change from A
           (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RE (Residential Estate). The
           property is located at 3940 S 5200 W on 10.6 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik
           at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:     Steve Pastorik explained that the subject property is surrounded mainly by
                single-family homes and a church. He presented the concept plan. Steve
                pointed out that the proposed project shows several lots that do not meet the
                width requirement of one hundred feet, which is a requirement of the
                proposed zone. Steve feels if the lot boundaries are adjusted, it could
                minimize the number of lots below the required size to four. He explained that
                those four lots are located at a pinch point where there isn’t enough room to
                increase the lot size. Therefore, the applicant might request an exception for
                those four lots. Steve noted that the submitted plans are “comparable,” and
                staff suggested that they commit to specific plans and the applicant is aware of
                this needed change.

                Commissioner McEwen struggles with holding applicants to things such as
                the materials they can use but not to the plans submitted with a zone change
                request. Steve explained that the City has a requirement in the code requiring
                a concept plan to be submitted at this stage. However, the City can’t require
                engineering at this point, so we ask for a concept plan depicting a general
                layout of the site. Steve said when the final plans are submitted, they will
                include engineering, which will likely be different from the concept plan.

                Commissioner Wood said a previous proposal for the subject property had a
                concept plan with much larger lots, and they didn’t request as many
                exceptions. She feels asking for an exception for ten lots is too much to
                approve and turn over to the Council. Steve explained different possibilities of
                lot arrangements that would not need as many exceptions, and he will discuss
                these options with the applicant
SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SD-3-2022    Petition by JOE SALISBURY, representing DAI Utah is requesting a
             street dedication for a portion of Decker Lane. The property to be
             dedicated is part of the H20 development and is located at approximately
             2860 South 2050 West. (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
             steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:     Steve Lehman explained that this request is for a partial dedication of Decker
                Lane. He noted that while the engineering department was reviewing the site
                plan for the H2O development, approved earlier this year, they realized a
                portion of this street was not dedicated.

                Steve stated that this proposal would dedicate that portion to the City. Steve
                pointed out that there is an existing curb and gutter along the road; however,
                there are currently no sidewalks. He needs to confirm if the sidewalks were
                not added because there was not enough room or if they are planning to install
                them in the future.

                Chair Fuller asked if the proposed dedication would affect the development
                itself. Steve said that the development would not be affected.


SA-2-2022       Petition by BROOK HINTZE with Back Development is requesting a
                plat amendment for a portion of the 4100 Commons Office
                Condominiums. The purpose for the plat amendment is to adjust certain
                areas within the building from common to limited common space for
                ownership purposes and to adjust parking spaces to include limited
                commons stalls. The property is located at 3725 West 4100 South and is
                zoned RB (Residential Business). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311
                or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:     Steve Lehman explained that the Commission had reviewed a proposal last
                year that was approved. He said the applicant is returning to the Commission
                to amend the approved plat. Steve presented the site plan and reviewed the
                common areas that would be adjusted to limited common areas and the
                proposed parking stalls for those areas. He stated that the applicant would
                need to record a new plat that reflects the proposed changes.

                Chair Fuller asked if the proposal would only change the size of the common
                areas? Steve said yes, that is correct. Chair Fuller wondered if there has been
                interest from potential tenants. Steve said there had been interest, and one
                potential tenant is looking for a larger unit, which this proposal would
                provide.
SV-2-2022         Petition by KATHERINE PASKER, representing Northrop Grumman, is
                  requesting a street vacation for a dedicated but unimproved right-of-way
                  located at approximately 7000 West 5400 South. (Staff – Steve Lehman at
                  801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:       Steve Lehman explained that in 1932 there was a road dedication, not by plat
                  by word document, that dedicated the private road in question to Salt Lake
                  County. He stated that the applicant proposes to vacate this right-of-way to
                  avoid future issues regarding future development where this street runs
                  through their property.
