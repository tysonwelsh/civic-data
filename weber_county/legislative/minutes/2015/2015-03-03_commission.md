G. PUBLIC COMMENTS:
       Cory Combe, of Ogden, asked about the status of Skyline Drive and Jared Andersen, County
       Engineer, responded that it is 30% designed. Mr. Combe recommended putting in the sewer and
       water first so the road will not have to be dug up later. He asked if a holding strip would be left on
       each side of the road through the property that is being purchased for it so that future lot owners pay
       the fair share of the road.
       Doug Hansen, of West Weber, spoke of the current Little Weber cut-off flood control mitigation
       project. He received reports from two independent professional engineers, whom he said confirmed
       what he had stated at the last meeting--that the canal is overdesigned, that when full, it will carry
       2,500-2,600 cubic feet per second, which is 2½ times the stated capacity objective of the needed
       structure. He said this will impact his family’s farm operation and incur extra taxpayer costs that he
       is trying to save by presenting additional information. He requested that with this evidence the
       county reconsider the project’s design. He said that the two independent engineers requested
       additional information but he has received no response to his requests to the County Engineer. He
       feels the information he provides is not considered and asked if the county is refusing to provide
       information and to negotiate. He said that his last information may be turned over to the attorneys
       and his family will have to obtain an attorney, which creates more of an impact on their family. He
       is willing to work out an agreement with the county that protects his family’s liability and operation.
       Chair Gibson said it was unfortunate that Mr. Hansen continued to make unfounded accusations, that
       staff is more than happy, as they have in the past, to talk and work this out, but that Mr. Hansen will
       not give the name of the experts or what information they are using. Chair Gibson said that this item
       will be on an agenda very soon. Mr. Hansen said that the Chair was making a lot of accusations and
       his information was founded on professional engineers.
