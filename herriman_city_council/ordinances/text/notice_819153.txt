PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on March 8, 2023, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2022-06 annexing territory located at or near 11800 South 7200 West comprising of approximately 30 acres into Herriman City; establishing zoning for the annexed territory and amending the zoning map of the city; and related matters. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
