F. PRESENTATION FROM YOUR COMMUNITY CONNECTION (YCC).
   Julie Smith, YCC Director, stated that the YCC provides services for domestic violence, sexual assault victims
   and the homeless. Ms. Smith gave examples of families that come to the YCC and their very tragic
   circumstances. She noted that there were several cases of domestic violence in Weber County in the last month
   and that three people were dead as a result. Last year 45% of homicides in Utah were domestic violence-
   related, and out of those, four were 2 years old and younger, and she stated that they deserve a childhood and
   to be safe. A major cause of homelessness is domestic violence—40% of those who are homeless are at the
   YCC due to domestic violence. She expressed gratitude to the Commission, which provided the YCC last year
   with $50,000 to help house those in need, and they were able to help 92 households, along with 1,333 bus
   tokens and 150 $10 gas cards. She stated that these services are so vital. The YCC also helps connect their
   patrons with other resources. The commissioners thanked Ms. Smith, who is retiring, for what she has done
   for our community.

    3. CONTRACT WITH THE GOLDEN SPIKE GEM AND MINERAL SOCIETY FOR THE DONATION OF TABLES FROM
       THE GOLDEN MINERAL SOCIETY TO THE GOLDEN SPIKE EVENT CENTER (GSEC).

         Duncan Olsen, GSEC General Manager, stated that the Gem and Mineral Society had approached the
         GSEC and proposed to donate to them 100 new Lifetime tables in exchange for using them at no charge.
