                                RESOLUTION NO. 21-69

     A RESOLUTION APPROVING A SNOW REMOVAL SERVICES AGREEMENT WITH
                THE TRAVERSE RIDGE SPECIAL SERVICES DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, as a municipality, Draper City is authorized to perform services
related to snow removal within its city boundaries, pursuant to the broad authority
granted to it by the Utah Municipal Code, Title 10 of the Utah Code; and

       WHEREAS, The Traverse Ridge Special Services District (“TRSSD”) was created
as a special service district by Draper City in 1999 under the Utah Special Service
District Act, Utah Code Ann,, § 17A-2-1301, et seq. (1999) (now renumbered at Utah
Code Ann. § 17D-1-101, et seq.), for the purpose of providing snow removal in
portions of Draper City, and providing a means of funding the snow removal costs in
the area; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City and the TRSSD were involved in litigation in Third
District Court Case No. 170902106, which case was resolved by the entry of a final
declaratory judgement by the Court stating:

       1.     City of Draper must provide, or cause to be provided, standard
municipal services within the boundaries of Traverse Ridge Special Service District
equal to all other areas of the City, including snowplowing, street lighting, road repair
and maintenance, street sweeping, and disposal services;

       2.     If the costs of providing snowplowing, street lighting, road repair and
maintenance, street sweeping, and disposal services within the boundaries of
Traverse Ridge Special Service District are higher than the costs of providing these
same standard municipal services to other areas of the City, Traverse Ridge Special
Service District is responsible for payment of the additional differential costs; and

       3.     If the City provides, or causes to be provided, snowplowing, street
lighting, road repair and maintenance, street sweeping, and disposal services within
the boundaries of Traverse Ridge Special Service District at levels that exceed the
levels provided to all other areas of the City, Traverse Ridge Special Service District is
responsible for payment of the costs of the increased levels of service; and

    WHEREAS, the Parties desire to implement the Court’s judgment by
memorializing the way TRSSD will pay for snow removal services in the TRSSD area.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Approval. The Draper City Council approves the Snow Removal
Service Agreement (“Agreement”) with the TRSSD, attached hereto as Exhibit A and
incorporated herein by this reference.
      Section 2.    Execute Agreement. The Mayor is hereby authorized to execute
the Agreement.

       Section 3. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all provisions, clauses and words of this
Resolution shall be severable.

      Section 4.    Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 14TH DAY OF DECEMBER 2021.
