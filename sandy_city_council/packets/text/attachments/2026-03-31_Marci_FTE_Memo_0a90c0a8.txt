Date: March 31st, 2026
Subject: Amendment to Title 4 of the Sandy City Municipal Code


Utah State Code assigns the municipal legislative body the authority and responsibility to adopt
and control the City’s annual budget. As personnel costs represent a substantial portion of
budgetary expenditure, it is essential that the budget process reflects the authority to authorize
and limit municipal staffing levels. In order for the City Council to carry out its statutory duties,
the adopted budget should include a consolidated staffing schedule identifying all authorized
positions and establishing a total count of full-time benefited positions.
The total count of full-time benefited positions established in the staffing schedule shall be the
total number authorized for that fiscal year regardless of whether sufficient appropriations
exist. The proposed new language to our code establishes this limitation and provides a
mechanism to increase the staffing count if necessary. Any increase to the total count of full-
time benefited positions should require resolution from the council. A public hearing would
only be necessary if budget appropriations were increased. This change strengthens the
Council’s oversight and fiscal control consistent with State Code. The proposed language is
included as Exhibit A.
