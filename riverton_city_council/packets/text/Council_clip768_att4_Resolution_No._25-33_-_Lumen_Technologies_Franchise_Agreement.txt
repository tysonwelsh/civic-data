                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     RESOLUTION NO. 25-33

  RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A FRANCHISE AGREEMENT
       WITH QWEST CORPORATION, D/B CENTURY LINK QC (LUMEN
                         TECHNOLOGIES)

        WHEREAS, Qwest Corporation, D/B Century Link QC, through Lumen Technologies
(“Lumen”) desires to install, maintain, operate and/or control an telecommunications infrastructure
network in Riverton City’s (“City”) owned right of ways to provide telecommunications service to
City residents; and
        WHEREAS, it is both reasonable and necessary for City to allow the installation of Lumen’s
infrastructure network in the City owned public right of ways within the City and thereafter to maintain,
operate and/or control said network; and
        WHEREAS, the City finds and determines that the public interest in health, safety and welfare
will be enhanced by the provision of the proposed service; and
       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:
    1. The City Council concludes that entering into the above-described agreement with Lumen will
       enhance the public health, safety and welfare of the public, and authorizes the City to enter into
       and sign the same.
    2. The Mayor is authorized to execute a Franchise Agreement with Lumen in substantially similar
       form to the Agreement attached as Exhibit A.
    3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 3rd day of June,
2025 by the following vote:
