FINDINGS OF FACT 
1. In 2000 the Board of County Commission (BCC) approved the Eagles Dance 
Private Residence Club condominium plat on the Baker Parcel.  This 
consisted of twenty-four (24) 4,000 square foot log units and a 5,000 square 
foot clubhouse to be utilized as Hotel/Lodging. 
Snyderville Basin Planning Commission 
April 25, 2017 
Page 6 of 27 
 
 
2. On December 17, 2003 the Board of County Commissioners approved and 
adopted by Ordinance #476.  The plat was amended to be Aspen Creek 
Crossing subdivision.   
3. The Aspen Creek Crossing subdivision plat assigned maximum square 
footages for each lot within the plat that ranged from 3,500 to 6,000 square 
feet adding up to a total maximum of 87,000 square feet for the entire 
subdivision.   
4. Although a finding was made that the project was not consistent with the 
definition of Hotel/Lodging within the Canyons DA, a condition was made 
that the square footage shown were maximums and the homes are not to be 
used for primary residences.  No formal modification was made to the 
Canyons LUZ chart at that time. 
5. Between 2003 and 2009, the Canyons LUZ chart was amended twice for other 
development parcels and subsequently the chart classified this property as 
Detached Single Family Residential and also reflected the redaction in 
density on the Barker Parcel from 100,000 to 87,000 square feet.     
6. On March 5, 2005 then Principal Planner, Nora Shepard, issues a 
memorandum to the file and attached it to the plat to specify how square 
footage was calculated for dwellings within the Aspen Creek Crossing 
subdivision, consistent with their CC&R’s.  This memorandum exempted 
some garage and basement square footage from counting against the gross 
square footage allowed per lot.   
7. There are currently nine (9) single-family dwellings constructed within the 
Aspen Creek Crossing subdivision.  Four (4) of the dwellings meet the gross 
maximum square footage allotted to the associated parcel.  Five (5) of the 
dwellings exceed the gross maximum square footage allotted to the 
associated parcel.   
8. Public hearings were conducted on March 14, 2017 and April 18, 2017 to 
discuss the proposed Development Agreement Amendment language.   
Snyderville Basin Planning Commission 
April 25, 2017 
Page 7 of 27 
 
 
 
CONCLUSIONS OF LAW 
1. Aspen Creek Crossing should be listed in Section 3.7 of the Land Use and 
Zoning chart as a single-family detached development along with the other 
single-family detached developments within the Canyons DA.   
2. The Canyons DA clearly describes the square footage measurement 
requirement and any change to that calculation to exclude certain portions 
for the Aspen Creek Crossing project would result in an increase in density 
exceeding that which was authorized in the original approval and the Land 
Use and Zoning Chart.   
3. Although the Canyons DA describes the square footage measurement 
requirement for all single-family detached projects, amending that 
measurement requirement for the Aspen Creek Crossing project would result 
in a “deminimus” increase in density which would be reflected in the 
amended land use and Zoning Chart.   
4. Prior Community Development Director mistakenly used a square footage 
measurement standard found in CC&Rs rather than that found with the 
Canyons DA, and based upon an equitable doctrine of “fairness”, requiring 
future development to conform to the existing definitions within the Canyons 
DA would create disparate building stands within the Aspen Creek Crossing 
Development.
