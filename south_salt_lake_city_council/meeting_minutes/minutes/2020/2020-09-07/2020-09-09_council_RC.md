NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of
          upcoming events, meetings, activities, etc.

       2. CITIZEN’S COMMENTS. Susan Bowlden, 419 Burton Ave. She spoke
          about the situation at the South Parc apartments. She has been working with
          various people to help the residents of South Parc and gave some information
          about how people can get assistance with rent.

       3. MAYOR’S COMMENTS. Mayor Wood gave the Council an update on
          situation at South Parc. The City has been in contact with management of the
          apartments to see how the City can help. South Parc has recently been
          purchased and is under new management and has plans to renovate the
          apartments. These buildings are in need of repair to meet building
          requirements and fire code compliance. A couple of years ago the residents
          were put on month to month leases and at that time the City met with United
          Way to discuss concerns that management may go the direction they are
          taking now. The new management is choosing to upgrade these apartments in
          phases and not renew the month to month leases. Management is trying to be
          flexible with residents until they find another place to live. When renovations
          are complete the residents will have the option to reapply for a new lease.

          Council Member Siwik asked if all of these residents are under section eight
          housing and if that was transferable.

          Mayor Wood said the apartments are in such disrepair that section eight
          housing was removed a couple of years ago. The City is currently working
          with Utah Community Action and Refugee Services to help find housing for
          these residents.

          Council Member Pinkney asked if these residents will stay in South Salt Lake.

          Mayor Wood said the goal is to get these residents in livable housing and she
          is not sure if that will be in South Salt Lake.

          Council Member Pinkney has heard rumors that the developer plans to build
          luxury housing similar to the Zellerbach.

          Community Development Director, Alexandra White, said the developer plans
          to renovate the existing buildings.

          Mayor Wood added that Utah Community Action and Refugee Justice League
          are looking into the moratorium on evictions and have attorneys to help assist
          these residents.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS. None

PUBLIC HEARING

Ms. White said the Utah Food Bank and the Salt Lake County Transfer Station reached
out to the City asking for the consideration of business license fee waivers. These
businesses provide services to City residents.

The Public Hearing opened at 7:37 p.m. and no comments were made.

Council Member Siwiik said he had owned a non-profit organization in South Salt Lake
and has not been required to pay a business licensing fee and wondered if this something
that has recently changed in regards to non-profit organizations. He supports of waiving
these fees and thinks it should be policy to waive all business licensing fees for non-
profits in the City.

Ms. White said there was a change in the law recently regarding business licensing fees
for non-profits. The fees for these two businesses the fee is only a couple of hundred
dollars and they provide services to City residents. She also added that Salt Lake County
doesn’t waive business licensing fees for South Salt Lake so it is not the norm for
governmental agencies to waive fees for other entities and non-profits.

Council Member deWolfe questioned whether or not the City Council should wave the
fee for the Transfer Station because the County doesn’t waive fees for South Salt Lake
and they are a taxing entity.

City Engineer, Dennis Pay, reiterated that the County doesn’t waive fees for the City
when the City is doing work in the County. He also added that the Transfer Station has
cut back the hours in the City and is only open to the public limited hours.

Council Member Pinkney said she is in support of waiving the fees for the Food Bank but
not the Transfer Station. She also added that she may not be in support of waiving the
business license fee for all non-profits as some bring in quite a large sum of money
yearly.

Council Chair Bynum said she agrees about not waiving fees for all non-profits but there
should be a process in place for waiving fees. She is only in favor of waiving the fee for
