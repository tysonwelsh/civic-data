                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 ORDINANCE NO. 22-20

    AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE DEVELOPMENT
      AGREEMENT FOR PRESERVE AT LOVERS LANE LOCATED ON
 APPROXIMATELY 5.5 ACRES AT APPROXIMATELY 13265 S LOVERS LANE,
            BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND KEN OLSON

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that under Utah Code §10-9a-532, the City
Council may lawfully adopt development agreements that have different standards set forth in
existing land use regulations if it does so in accordance with the same procedures for enacting
a land use regulation, including review and recommendation from the Planning Commission
and a public hearing; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed development agreement; and,

      WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said development
agreement; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of
the public to adopt the Development Agreement for Preserve at Lovers Lane as described
herein and shown as Exhibit “A.”

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City,
Utah as follows:
              Section 1.   The Development Agreement for Preserve at Lovers
                           Lane, Exhibit “A”, is hereby approved and adopted.

               Section 2.            This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 16th day
of August, 2022 by the following vote:
