                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-63

 RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A MEMBER AGENCY
  WATER EFFICIENCY STANDARDS FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH JORDAN
              VALLEY WATER CONSERVANCY DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District (District) offers water
conservation grants to its member agencies to promote water conservation; and

        WHEREAS, the City of Riverton (City) purchases water from, and is a member agency
of the District; and

       WHEREAS, the City submitted an application for a Member Agency Water Efficiency
Standards Funding Grant; and

        WHEREAS, the District reviewed the application and awarded the City $54,000.00 to
aid the City water conservation efforts, provided the City execute a Water Efficiency Standards
Funding Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council does hereby determine that it is in the best
interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to execute said
Agreement.

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. That the Mayor is authorized to sign the member agreement from Jordan Valley Water
      Conservancy District as attached to this Resolution, upon verification that said agreement
      is approved as to legal form by the Riverton City Attorney.

   2. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage and acceptance as
      provided herein.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of
July 2023 by the following vote:
