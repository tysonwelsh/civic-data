       THIS FIRST AMENDMENT (‘Amendment’) is made and entered into as of the ______

day of _______________, 2020, to the above named Agreement dated April 17, 2018 by and

between SALT LAKE COUNTY (COUNTY), a body corporate and politic of the State of Utah,

and RIVERTON CITY (CITY), a Utah municipal corporation of the State of Utah. CITY and

COUNTY may be jointly referred to henceforth as the “Parties.”

                                       R E CI T A L S:

       WHEREAS, on April 17, 2018, the Parties executed an interlocal cooperation agreement

(the ‘Agreement’) whereby they agreed to cooperatively undertake to produce a UPDES media

campaign;

       WHEREAS, the Parties now desire to amend the Agreement to extend the term for an

additional two years and to adjust the annual budget and CITY’s financial contributions.

                                         A M E N D M E N T:

       NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises set forth herein, the

Parties agree to amend that certain Agreement, referenced above, as follows:

       1.      Paragraph 2, “Budget,” is amended to adjust the annual campaign budget to One

            Hundred Eighty-Eight Thousand dollars ($188,000.00).
                                               1
       2.       Paragraph 4, “City Responsibilities,” is amended to adjust the City’s monetary

            contribution from $5,000 per year to $5,957.25.

       3.      Paragraph 7, “Term,” is amended to extend the term of the Agreement two

            additional years to April 17, 2022.

       4.      All other terms, conditions and exhibits set forth in the original Agreement, not

            expressly addressed by this Amendment, shall remain in full force and effect.

       IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties execute this Amendment as of the date first set

forth above.
