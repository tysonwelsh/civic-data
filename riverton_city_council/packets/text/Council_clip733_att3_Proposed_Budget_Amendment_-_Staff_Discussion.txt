                                                        Proposed Budget Amendment




February 18, 2025



Honorable Mayor and Council,



The City’s Administrative Services department submits the following proposed amendments to the
2024-2025 budget for your consideration:



   1.   Fix December Budget Amendment – Water Van

        Reason - Move Water Van purchase expense portion to be paid by Secondary Water. In the
        December Budget Amendment, the City increased the budgeted expense line for culinary water
        (51131150-480502) to $57,730, this included $8,319 that should have been paid for with
        Secondary Water Funds. This amendment fixes that by decreasing the budgeted expenditure
        amount from culinary water and increasing the budgeted amount for secondary water.

        Proposed Amendment:

              51131150 - 480502 – Decrease budgeted expenditure by $8,319 (Culinary).
              53131150 - 480502 – Increase budgeted expenditure by $8,319 (Secondary).

2. Federal Grant Writer Amendment -

   Reason - The City went out to bid and has selected a professional federal grant writing
   consultant to assist the city in finding and applying for federal grants. This was discussed at our
   strategic planning meeting in January and the direction to staff was to select the top provider to
   help the city find grants for some of the larger needs of the city. The top provider’s bid was
   $14,250 for two grants, including pre and post award assistance. While the City does expect
   eventual revenue from receiving grants, not all grants allow the consultant expense to be paid
   for using grant funds. Therefore, staff is recommending to budget using the general fund
   balance. The proposed amendment includes the base amount, which includes 2 grants, plus one
   additional grant for a total of 3 grants.




   Proposed Amendment:

          10111012 - 420100 - Increase the budgeted expenditure by $19,000 to allow the City to
           enter into a contract with the winning consultant for grant assistance.
          General Fund - Use of Fund Balance - Increase the budgeted use of fund balance by
           $19,000 to allow the City to enter into a contract with the winning consultant for grant
           assistance.




3. Water Rate Study Amendment

   Reason - Staff anticipated next year to budget for a rate study for the Culinary and Secondary
   Water services. The city last changed its rate structure on September 1, 2017, and the last rate
   study was completed a few years prior to that. Staff recommends hiring a professional
   consultant who will examine current needs and help develop a rate structure that will pay for
   future needs while maintaining the lowest rates possible. In addition, staff will also explore
   purchasing software that will take the rate study and put it in a format that can be modified and
   updated with actual results or changes in assumptions, without requiring staff to pay the
   consultant for additional work.
