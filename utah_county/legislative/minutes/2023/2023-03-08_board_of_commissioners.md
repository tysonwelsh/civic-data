5. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE EXECUTION OF AN ADDENDUM TO WITH UTAH
COUNTY AGREEMENT NO. 2021-637 WITH MOTOROLA SOLUTIONS, INC. AND
WATCHGUARD, INC. FOR BODY CAMERAS AND CLOUD STORAGE FOR THE UTAH
COUNTY SHERIFF'S OFFICE
       -Ben VanNoy, Attorney

(Timestamp: 4:19)

JASON PHILLIPPI, UTAH COUNTY SHERIFF’S OFFICE – STATED THAT THE
SHERIFF’S OFFICE CURRENTLY HAS WATCHGUARD BODY CAMS AND DASH CAMS
IN THEIR SERVER BUT THE SERVER IS RUNNING OUT OF SPACE AND NOW WE
FIND OUT THAT THERE IS A NEW VERSION OF BODY CAMS THAT CAME OUT SO
WE NEED TO EITHER GET A NEW SERVER OR GO WITH THE CLOUD. HAVE ASKED
FOR QUOTES FOR BOTH AND NOW LEANING TOWARDS CLOUD ONLY BECAUSE OF
EXPANDABILITY IN THE FUTURE AND THAT WATCHGUARD TAKES A LOT OF
MEMORY. PLUS THE CLOUD COVERS ALL OF THE UPGRADES THAT COULD
HAPPEN IN THE FUTURE WITH NO ADDITIONAL COSTS.

ROD MANN, UTAH COUNTY AUDITOR – STATED THAT THERE IS ALSO A $10,500
UPFRONT COSTS FOR THE EQUIPMENT.

RUDY LIVINGSTON, BUDGET MANAGER – STATED THAT THIS CAME UP
UNEXPECTEDLY AND IF THE COUNTY WERE TO GO OUT AND BUY IT AND
CONTINUE LIKE WE ARE DOING THEN DOWN THE ROAD THERE WOULD BE
ADDITIONAL COSTS. GOING THIS ROUND WHICH WILL HAVE A LOWER INITIAL
COST. THE SHERIFF’S OFFICE DOESN’T HAVE ANY MONEY IN THE BUDGET FOR
