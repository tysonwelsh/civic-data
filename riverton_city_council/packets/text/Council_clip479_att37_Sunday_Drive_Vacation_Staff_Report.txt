                              MEMORANDUM
To:              Honorable Mayor and City Council

From:            Planning Department

Date:            November 16, 2021

Subject:         ORDINANCE 21-31, Vacating a portion of right-of-way located at approximately
                 4400 W Sunday Drive consisting of 0.015 acres (663 sf), and accepting dedication
                 of additional right-of-way along Sunday Drive


Proposed Motion:

I move the City Council ADOPT Ordinance #21-31, vacating .015 acre portion of public right-of-
way located at approximately 4400 W Sunday Drive, and accepting dedication of additional right-
of-way on Sunday Drive, as shown on Exhibits “A” and “B”.

Background

Rocky Mountain Power is developing a substation at the intersection of Sunday Drive and 12600
South, and the property involved extends several hundred feet to the south along the west side
of Sunday Drive. In the course of preparing plans for that site, the staff has identified the need
for minor adjustments to the right-of-way for Sunday Drive. The majority of the adjustment is
additional land being dedicated to the City, consisting of 7,165 sf along the west side of the right
of way. The current parcel line has an angled junction to the line, which is being adjusted to a
curve consistent with road geometry standards. This required a small area, 663 sf, of right-of-
way to be vacated. The property dedicated to the City comes from Rocky Mountain Power, and
the vacation of property will go to Rocky Mountain Power.

Both the vacation and dedication property descriptions have been reviewed by staff, and are
consistent with the required right-of-way configuration. Staff is recommending approval of the
vacation and acceptance of the dedicated ground.
