   2. Council Rules Discussion regarding Citizens Stating their address for public
      record. Mr. Burton called around to other cities and only some required an
      address to be given during a meeting but most did not. He also looked at the open
      meetings law and it states a name should be given but doesn’t say anything about
      an address. The South Salt Lake Council rules state that an address should be
      given during the meeting so the Council would need to update that rule.

       Council Chair Bynum said the only reason she is usually interested in hearing a
       person’s address it to know whether or not that person is a City resident. She
       would like the rule to state that an individual should say what City they live in.

       Mr. Burton suggested that the Council continue the use of the comment cards that
       are available when the Council holds in person meetings.

       A majority of the Council agreed with the rule change of an individual just stating
       their name and City of residency.

       Council Member Siwik made a motion to move this item to Unfinished Business
       for the March 10, 2021 Regular Meeting
