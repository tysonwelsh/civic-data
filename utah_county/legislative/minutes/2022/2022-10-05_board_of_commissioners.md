COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER - ANNOUNCED THE OCTOBER UTAH COUNTY
EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH, JAY CRITCHLOW. (Read nomination)

I would like to nominate Jay Critchlow for Utah County Employee of the
Month. Critch has been a valuable employee to not only our team but to everyone.
After Sgt. Jensen was moved to Booking and Deputy Martz went on FMLA, Critch
was placed into the position of "acting Sgt/DIC", he worked very hard at making
sure that everything ran as smoothly as possible. He would come in early and get
the shift briefing from the outgoing Sgt. He would assign shift positions to those
working OT and he was fair about it. He was the 11-11 deputy and would
consistently stay later than he needed too (approx 2 am most nights) and then
turn around and come back for more. I believe his blood at the time was 75%
monster energy drink.

Crich did all of this while keeping his quick wit and sense of humor. Very
rarely, if at all, did I hear him complain, he would just share some reference to a
1980s-1990s TV show, song, or movie and move on. He has always been a great
earpiece for bouncing ideas off him. Critchlow will be one of the first deputies
through the door on a backup call (if it is not too far down the hall, too much
young blood nowadays), but he will respond and give advice and help when
needed.

Critch is a straight shooter and isn't afraid to speak his mind. I have seen,
firsthand, the respect he gets from the inmates, even when he is reprimanding
them for a rule violation. I have seen him counsel inmates and how they can
better themselves. I have had inmates tell me "Critch is awesome", he gets respect
from inmates and gives it in return, even if they have not earned it.

Getting to know Critch over the past couple of years has been a great privilege
for me. He and I have had many talks, shared ideas, given each other advice and
criticism, and have been able to remain friends. Ask any deputy about Critch and
Iam sure you will get a half smile as they remember some story about Critchlow.
He has had an impact on a lot of people throughout his 17+ years in Corrections
and is still able to keep a smile on his face. He has helped the newer deputies
better themselves and has helped an old dog like me become a better Deputy. He
gives advice when needed and is always my go to guy when I need a second
opinion on a situation.

These are just a few reasons to nominate Jay Critchlow for deputy of the month.
