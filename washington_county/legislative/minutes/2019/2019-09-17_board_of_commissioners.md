SEPTEMBER 17, 2019

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Dean Cox at 4:00 p.m. on September 17, 2019, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were Commissioner Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner
Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle
Felshaw, and Deputy County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

PRESENTATION: _ALCOHOL _ FREE __ 2019-2020 _SCHOOL YEAR __-
WASHINGTON COUNTY PREVENTION COALITION

The Washington County Youth Coalition distributed a flyer for a free family dinner night
offered for all Washington County families on September 30, 2019. This event will be in
conjunction with the Hope Squad, which is a suicide prevention coalition. The group
presented that it is important for families to eat dinner together to encourage family
bonding and to prevent youth from making bad decisions in regards to alcohol use.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2514, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND
MCMILLEN JACOBS ASSOCIATES FOR AN ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT
STATEMENT _ON_THE WARNER DRAW SUPPLEMENTAL WATERSHED
PLAN FOR GOULDS WASH DRAINAGE

Washington County Public Works Director Ron Whitehead explained that Gould’s Wash
is located above Hurricane. As part of an agreement with Natural Resources Conservation
Service (NRCS) to assist with the Warner Draw Supplemental Watershed Plan project, a
Plan-Environmental Assessment is required to be completed. It is being recommended for
McMillen Jacobs Associated to complete the Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) on
the Goulds Wash portion of the project.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-
2514, A Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington
County and McMillen Jacobs Associates for an Environmental Impact
Statement on the Warner Draw Supplemental Watershed Plan for
Goulds Wash Drainage. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox, Iverson and
Almquist voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Chairman Cox recognized Nicholle Felshaw, mentioning that she has been promoted to the
position of County Administrator.

Ms. Felshaw explained that due to the Boulevard Building remodel, a parking lot
restructure was necessary. Forty-five (45) additional stalls have been created behind
Stapley Pharmacy, while a portion of the parking lot between the Boulevard and
Administration buildings is being resurfaced.

Commissioner Almquist stated that he and Commissioner Iverson attended meetings in
Washington DC last week, and provided updates regarding Payment in Lieu of Taxes
(PILT) funding.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:43 p.m.
