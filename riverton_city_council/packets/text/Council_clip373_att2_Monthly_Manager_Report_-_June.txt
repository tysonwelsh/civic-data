                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                             June 2020
We observed a slight increase in the number of vehicle burglaries in the city during the previous
month. This prompted us to initiate a program of reminding our citizens about the dangers of
leaving valuable items in their vehicle, specifically where potential vehicle burglars can see them.
We are targeting parking lots in our major retail areas and placing pamphlets on windshields of all
the cars in the lot with a message reminding people to secure their belongings in a safe place. Also,
we are partnering with some of our businesses to place our pamphlet in their business to remind
customers to lock their cars and hide their valuables.

We continued our efforts to keep our streets safe by focusing on DUI enforcement and that resulted
in 17 DUI arrests for drugs and alcohol, thus keeping our roadways more safe. Additionally, our
call volume seems to be increasing as we emerge from the pandemic and slowly work our way
toward more normalcy.

I would be remiss if I did not express my appreciation to the citizens of Riverton who have gone out
of their way to show their support for us by writing letters, personally stopping by and bringing
gifts, and offering words of encouragement. It has really helped our morale and boosted our spirits.
