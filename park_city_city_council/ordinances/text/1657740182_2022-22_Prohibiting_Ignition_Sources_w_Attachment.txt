            Ordinance No. 2022-22

            AN ORDINANCE CLARIFYING ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES IN PROHIBITING
            THE IGNITION OR USE OF IGNITION SOURCES, AMENDING SECTION 11-9-3 AND
            REPEALING SECTION 11-9-3.1 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF PARK CITY

            WHEREAS, the City Council of Park City, Utah, and the Park City Fire Code Official
            have a desire to reduce the threat of wildfires, which may cause extensive damage and
            economic hardship within the City; and

            WHEREAS, the Park City Fire Code Official and Park City Fire Marshal have deemed
            that Park City is subject to an elevated risk of wildfire because of its mountainous
            setting and extensive wildland interface; and

            WHEREAS, clarifying roles and responsibilities will ensure that decisions regarding the
            prohibition of ignition sources due to existing or historic hazardous environmental
            conditions can be made without undue delay;

            BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF PARK CITY, UTAH, THAT:

            Section 11-9-3 of the Municipal Code of Park City is amended and adopted to read as
            outlined in Exhibit A. Section 11-9-3.1 is repealed. This Ordinance shall become
            effective upon publication.

            PASSED AND ADOPTED this 23rd day of June, 2022.

            PROPOSED CHANGES
            11-9-3 Hazardous Environmental Conditions [Prohibiting Ignition or Use of Ignition Sources]
            If hazardous environmental conditions exist in the City, [When] the City F[f]ire C[c]ode
            O[o]fficial [determines that existing or historical hazardous environmental conditions
            necessitate controlled use of any ignition source, including fireworks, lighters, matches, sky
            lanterns, and smoking materials, City Council] may prohibit the ignition or use of an ignition
            source [in accordance with Utah Code § 15A-5-202.5.], including fireworks, lighters, matches,
            sky lanterns, and smoking materials as necessary to reduce the risk of wildfire.

            11-9-3.1 Delegation
            The City Council has delegated this authority to the City Fire Code Official and may elect to
            rescind such delegated authority at its discretion.

            CLEAN VERSION WITH PROPOSED CHANGES
            11-9-3 Prohibiting Ignition or Use of Ignition Sources
            When the City fire code official determines that existing or historical hazardous environmental
            conditions necessitate controlled use of any ignition source, including fireworks, lighters,
            matches, sky lanterns, and smoking materials, City Council may prohibit the ignition or use of
            an ignition source in accordance with Utah Code § 15A-5-202.5.
