                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                            August,2021


We observed a significant increase in the number of vehicle burglaries reported during the month of
August. We had 7 incidents in one night involving different victims and we believe one suspect
was responsible for all those incidents based on security video we recovered. This has prompted us
to focus our graveyard patrol assets on our neighborhoods as a majority of these cases have
occurred in residential areas and have targeted vehicles parked in driveways or on the roadway.

Recently, our specialty units have been extremely busy and have responded to several major
incidents. First, our SWAT Team deployed twice over the Labor Day weekend, both to assist UPD
with major incidents. In Millcreek, there was a double homicide/suicide which was believed to be a
hostage situation. That incident lasted for more than 24 hours and our SWAT Team provided
invaluable support to our partner agencies. The next day we responded to Kearns to assist with the
arrest of a wanted fugitive.

Our Major Accident Team investigated two fatal crashes which occurred on our roadways. The
first was on Bangerter Highway and resulted in the death of a 24-year-old female passenger and we
anticipate charges being filed against the driver due to evidence suggesting he was under the
influence of alcohol. The second was a motorcycle versus a car where the car turned in front of the
motorcycle on Redwood Road. Unfortunately, this collision resulted in the death of a young man
from Herriman.
