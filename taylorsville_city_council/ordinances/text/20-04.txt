              SUMMARY:     Consideration of Ordinance 20- 04 to amend the City of Taylorsville Zoning Map
                           from R- 1- 15( one residential unit per 15, 000 square feet) to R- 1- 10( one residential
                           unit per 10, 000 square feet) for the properties generally located 6581 South
                           Redwood Road, 6573 South Redwood Road, 6579 South Redwood Road, and 6577
                           South Redwood Road to facilitate the development of a nine- lot subdivision.

           BACKGROUND:     Lynn Rindlisbacher on behalf of Dave Wilson is requesting Zoning Map amendment
                           and subsequent General Plan amendment to facilitate the development of a nine- lot
                           subdivision for the parcels generally located at 6581 South Redwood Road( Parcel
                           ID 21224010140000), 6621 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010150000),
                           6581 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010080000), 6573 South Redwood
                           Road( Parcel ID 21222570160000), 6579 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID
                           21222570120000), 6579 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21222570100000), 6579
                           South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010060000), 6577 South Redwood Road
                           Parcel ID 21224010070000), and 6577 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID
                           21222570180000). The Zoning Map amendment will be requested in two different
                           ordinances( 20- 03 and 20- 04), as the parcels located within the subdivision have
                           different Zoning Map designations( see Exhibit A and B).

                           The property is owned by the Wilson family and they were granted Preliminary Plat
                           approval for a nine- lot subdivision by the Planning Commission on 12/ 10/ 19,
                           contingent upon the General Plan and Zoning Map amendments being approved by
                           the City Council. The Wilson families will remain in Lots 4, 6, and 8 of the
                           proposed subdivision and due to the placement of the existing homes a Zoning Map
                           amendment is required so the applicant can meet the requirements outlined in 13. 20
                           and   13. 21 of the Taylorsville Municipal Code.   The proposed subdivision will have
                           lots varying in size from 11, 754 square feet to 29,358 square feet, which is
                           congruent with the R- 1- 10 Zoning Map designation.

                           The proposed subdivision has nine parcels, with five different addresses.
                           Additionally, the parcels have two different General Plan and Zoning Map
                           designations. The two parcels that serve as the access to the property and are
                           adjacent to Redwood Road are designated as Limited Commercial( Ordinance 20-
                           03). The internal seven lots are designated as R- 1- 15( Ordinance 20- 04). The
                           requested Zoning Map amendment will provide continuity for all the parcels within
                           the subdivision by amending the designation for all nine parcels to R- 1- 10, this
                           designation is consistent with adjacent land uses.
                               In addition to Ordinance 20- 04, the applicant has requested a Zoning Map
                               amendment for two parcels from Limited Commercial to R- 1- 10( Ordinance 20- 03),
                               a General Plan amendment from Community Commercial to Low-Density
                               Residential( Ordinance 20- 01), and Medium-Density Residential to Low-Density
                               Residential( Ordinance 20- 02).


                               The parcels and addresses that will be amended by Ordinance 20- 04 are outlined
                               below:

                                         6579 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21222570120000)

                                         6579 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010060000)

                                         6577 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21224010070000)

                                         6577 South Redwood Road( Parcel ID 21222570180000)

        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on January 8,
2020, to consider, among other things, approving an amendment to THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE ZONING
MAP FROM R- 1- 15 TO R- 1- 10 FOR THE PROPERTIES OUTLINED IN EXHIBIT A; and


        WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 05. 030( A)(2), notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published in the Deseret News and the Salt
Lake Tribune on November 30, 2019; and


        WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was also
published on the State Public Notice Website on November 26, 2019; and


        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on the
proposed amendment on December 10, 2019, and the Planning Commission voted 7- 0 to recommend that
the City Council approve the proposed amendment; and

      WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy public
comment on the proposed amendment during its regularly scheduled meeting on January 8, 2020; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best interest
of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed amendment.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the proposed
amendment to TAYLORSVILLE ZONING MAP is hereby approved and is designated in Exhibit A.

        THIS ORDINANCE, assigned Ordinance No. 20- 04, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the city recorder, and
accepted as required herein.
