Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner
Carlson.

2. Consent Agenda

    2.1) Approval of minutes from the February 24, 2022 meeting.
    2.2) Approval of minutes from the April 14, 2022 meeting.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to approve the minutes from February 24, 2022
        and April 14, 2022 with the requested modifications. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.


3. Regular Agenda

3.1) Public hearing and consideration of Gardner Company’s request for approval of the
Cold Spring Business Park Concept located at approximately 4222 West Orinda Drive.
Sierra Pierson presented information on the agenda item. The applicant still needs to apply for a
conditional use if the item is approved.

The applicant was not present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to approve Gardner Company’s request for
        approval of the Cold Spring Business Park Concept located at approximately 4222
        West Orinda Drive; with the findings that the proposed concept is consistent with the
        Cold Spring Ranch Area Plan, meets the intent of the State Highway Design District, is
        consistent with the Lehi City Development Code and conforms to the goals and policies
        of the General Plan. Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Newall seconded the
        motion.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Arnaldo Denzeler’s request for review of the
Denzeler zone change on .38-acres of property located at 37 South 500 West, changing the
zoning from RA-1 (residential/agriculture) to Mixed Use.
Sierra Pierson presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant was present and did not wish to add anything.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation of Arnaldo
    Denzeler’s request for review of the Denzeler zone change on .38-acres of property
    located at 37 South 500 West, changing the zoning from RA-1 (residential/agriculture)
    to Mixed Use; with the findings that the proposed zone change is consistent with the Mixed
    Use designation of the General Plan and is consistent with the goals and policies of the
    General Plan. Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Everett seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner
    Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.3) Consideration of Jeff Knighton’s request for approval of the Osmond Design Site Plan,
a commercial development located at 3725 North Outlet Parkway in the Traverse
Mountain Planned Community.
Sierra Pierson presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Heather Osmond, was present and is excited to upgrade to a new location.

Not a public hearing item.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Osmond Design Site Plan, a commercial
    development located at 3725 North Outlet Parkway in the Traverse Mountain Planned
    Community; with the findings that the proposed site plan meets the requirements of the
    Traverse Mountain Area Plan, the proposed site plan meets the design requirements of the
    Urban Tech Mixed Use Design District, the use will be located and conducted in a manner in
    compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General Plan and the purposes of this
    Code. Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Ellis seconded the motion.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

4. City Business
   No City business was discussed.

5. Adjournment

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Bunker moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Newall seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 8:05 p.m.
