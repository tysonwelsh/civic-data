Cancellation of Meeting

              Council Member Little stated he wanted to talk about the cancellation of the
August 6, 2020, meeting.

               Paul Ashton, Legal Counsel, White City Metro Township, stated there were three
members of the White City Metro Township Council that were not available to meet on August 6,
2020. As per the Mayor’s instruction, the meeting was cancelled due to a lack of a quorum.

              Council Member Little stated the Council is the body that should have cancelled
the meeting, not the Mayor.

              Mr. Ashton stated the Mayor has the power to cancel a meeting due to the lack of
a quorum. A special meeting does not need to be called to cancel a meeting.

              Council Member Little stated the meeting scheduled was set by resolution of the
Council. The Council is the only body that can change a resolution.

              Mr. Ashton stated the Council can discuss this issue later.

Closed Session – Personnel Matter

              Paul Ashton, Legal Counsel, White City Metro Township, stated the Council
needed to go into closed session to discuss a personnel matter.

               Council Member Little stated it looks like the Council is working on a contract to
retain professional services and he was not sure that qualified as a personnel matter.

            Mr. Ashton stated he wanted to give the Council an opportunity to meet someone.
Council Member Little can oppose the motion if he would like.

               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to close the
White City Metro Township Council meeting to discuss a personnel matter. The motion passed
4 to 1 with Council Member Little voting in opposition.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
              A motion was made in closed session to reopen the White City Metro Township
Council meeting. The motion passed unanimously.

Municipal Administrator Services – Resolution

             Rori Andreason, Positive Impact Consulting, introduced herself. She stated that
she has worked in municipal government for 36 years. Currently she works for Midvale City as
an exempt employee with a flexible schedule. She also works for Holladay on a limited basis.



                                                2
 DATE           THURSDAY                                                    AUGUST 13, 2020

               Mayor Flint stated of the applicants interviewed Ms. Andreason had the most
experience and flexible time to meet the township needs. Arrangements have been made with
the Municipal Services District for a permanent cubicle for her to use.

                Council Member Little asked if Ms. Andreason would have the time needed to
devote to this job, if she is already working for two other municipalities.

              Ms. Andreason stated absolutely, she would have the time to do the necessary
work.

                Council Member Little stated the Council needed to follow the general
procurement provisions for professional services. The scope of work attached to the resolution
indicates this position is under the direction of the Mayor. It should be under the Council since
it is the governing body. The Mayor can only be given more authority by ordinance. Unless
someone can show him an ordinance that indicates that, then this position should be under the
direction of the Council.

                Mr. Ashton stated the Mayor acts as the chair for the Metro Township Council.
One of the first task that Ms. Andreason will be tasked with is to work on an ordinance outlining
the roles more specifically.

              Council Member Little moved to amend the language of the scope of work to what
was provided when it was send out. The motion died due to a lack of a second.

               Council Member Perry stated Ms. Andreason will be working as an administrator
helping with agendas and those types of things. It does not make sense for everything that she
does to come before the Council for direction.

              Council Member Little stated it does not mean that Ms. Andreason would not
work for the Mayor, only that she would be under the Council who has the authority to govern the
township. This is a metro form of government, not a mayor/council form of government.

               Council Member Perry, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to approve the
following resolution:

RESOLUTION NO.: 20-08-01                                           DATE: August 13, 2020

        A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
        APPROVING A CONTRACT BETWEEN WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP AND
        POSITIVE IMPACT CONSULTING LLC (RORI L ANDREASON) FOR
        MUNICIPAL ADMINISTRATOR SERVICES

                                            RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah Code
      § 10-2a-401 et seq.



                                               3
 DATE           THURSDAY                                                    AUGUST 13, 2020

   B. The White City Metro Township Council ("Council") is a Metro Township Council pursuant
      to Utah Code §10-3b-504, with the Mayor acting as Chair of the Council pursuant to Utah
      Code §10-3b-503.

   C. Commencing in 2020, the Municipal Administrator for White City, Government Consultants
      LLC (Gregory Schulz), could no longer provide Municipal Administrator Services for White
      City, due to his working full time for Magna Metro Township.

   D. Positive Impact Consulting LLC (Rori L Andreason) provides Municipal Administrator
      services to local governments.

   E.   The attached agreement was negotiated between Positive Impact Consulting LLC and
        White City to fulfill White City's needs for an Administrator.

                                          RESOLUTION

THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the Council
approves and adopts the attached contract for Municipal Administrator services from Positive
Impact Consulting LLC (Rori Andreason), marked Attachment "A" to this resolution, for the benefit
of White City, and authorizes the Mayor to sign the same.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this _13th day of August,
2020.
