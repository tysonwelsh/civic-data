A RESOLUTION OF KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP ADOPTING THE 2019 SALT LAKE
COUNTY HAZARD MITIGATION PLAN

WHEREAS Kearns Metro Township recognizes the threat that natural hazards pose to people
and property within Kearns Metro Township; and

WHEREAS the Kearns Metro Township has participated in the creation of a multi-hazard
mitigation plan, hereby known as the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan in
accordance with the Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000; and

WHEREAS the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan identifies mitigation goals and
actions to reduce or eliminate long-term risk to people and property in Kearns Metro
Township from the impacts of future hazards and disasters; and

WHEREAS adoption by Kearns Metro Township demonstrates their commitment to hazard
mitigation and achieving the goals outlined in the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation
Plan

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY Kearns Metro Township, UTAH, THAT:

Kearns Metro Township adopts the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan

This resolution shall be effective on the date it is adopted.
