Planning and Development Services

              Curtis Woodward, Planning and Development Services Division, Salt Lake
County, stated the street vacation by the new library will be coming before the Council again
because it has been decided that it would be best to go through the legal process to vacate the
street.

               Mr. Woodward stated the Camp Kearns plat will also be coming back. There is

Kearns Improvement District

               Pam Gill, Kearns Improvement District, updated the Council on the following:

    A new sewer waste line was installed in front of the daycare center at 5415 South. The
     road will be paved on July 13, 2019.

    Work will begin on July 22, 2019, to relocate the main water line and add service laterals
     to homes located on 5015 South and 4820 West.

    The floor was poured for the two-million-gallon tank on June 4th and 5th. Everything went
     well. Construction should be completed by February.

    The Cougar Pump Station is just about complete. It is expected to be done and running
     by the end of July.

    The Conservation Garden Event was held and well attended.

    The District participated with Oquirrh Park Fitness Center with its luncheon series. The
     District plans on participating two more times this summer.

    The District will have a booth at the Fire, Water, and Ice event and will be participating in
     the parade as well.

    The yellow or discolored water that was seen just north of the Kearns Improvement District
     building was a result of flushing out the lines. When the lines are flushed out it stirs up
     sediments, which can discolor the water and make it look yellow.

Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Chief Brian Lohrke stated the UPD board passed a good transparent budget for
2019/2020 year. Individuals involved in the shooting a couple of weeks ago have been charged
and federal charges are a possibility. A lot of accidents, domestic disturbances, and alcohol
related incidents occurred on July 4, 2019.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                Captain Wade Russell, Unified Fire Authority, introduced UFA members. The new
budget cycle has started. In June there were 232 calls in Kearns and only three minor incidents
in July so far. The UFA website, unifiedfire.org, has been redesigned with links to each
community. Fireworks can be sold from June 24 – July 25 and discharged two days before and
after the holiday from 11:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. The day of the holiday fireworks can be discharged
from 11:00 a.m. to midnight. The Fire, Water, and Ice parade is on August 10, 2019. The Kearns
Metro Township Council will ride in the parade on an antique engine and the Kearns Community
Council will ride on a ladder truck.

Council Business

Committee Reports

Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Council Member Peterson updates the Council on the following:

    A special meeting was held on June 4, 2019, where the Board voted to allow alternates to
     attend and vote at UPD meetings. Alternates can only be used no more than twice a year.
     The Kearns Metro Township Council needs to decide if it wants to appoint any alternates
     to this board.

    No separation agreement has been approved with Riverton and Herriman.

    Early termination of employment incentive was approved, as well as employee pay scale
     and budget for 2019/2020.

    Council Member Glover, Midvale City, asked for help from Kearns Metro Township Council
     in lobbying state representatives for additional funds for the homeless shelter. There are
     over 300 beds that are used year-round by people from various communities.
