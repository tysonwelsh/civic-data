                               ORDINANCE NO. 2012-01


    AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING TEMPORARY REGULATIONS
ALLOWING FOR ADDITIONAL TEMPORARY SIGNAGE FOR BUSINESSES
LOCATED ALONG STATE STREET WITHIN THE CORPORATE LIMITS OF
 MID VALE CITY DURING UDOT'S STATE STREET WIDENING PROJECT.


        WHEREAS, the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) has started
construction on the widening of State Street between 6400 South and 8000 South with
said road project including widening the street from five lanes to seven, upgrading the
drainage system, installing sidewalk and ADA ramps, and upgrading street lighting along
the roadway; and


        WHEREAS, the City Council recognizes the need for these improvements and
sees the finished project as an overall benefit for the community; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council also recognizes the road construction will create
impacts on the existing businesses along State Street during the period of construction;
and

        WHEREAS, the City Council desires to help the businesses along State Street
remain viable during this time by providing an opportunity for the businesses to utilize
additional temporary signage to promote greater visibility during the road construction
period; and

        WHEREAS, Section 10-9a-504 U.C.A. grants the City Council the authority to
establish by ordinance temporary land use regulations regarding zoning amendments,
conditional use permits, building permits, sign permits and other land use matters.

        NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the City Council of Midvale City, Utah
the following:

       (1)     This temporary ordinance shall apply to all properties and businesses with
frontages along State Street within Midvale City.

        (2)     This temporary ordinance shall allow these businesses to utilize
promotional signage, such as pennants, streamers, banners, balloon signs, flags, inflated
sign displays, etc., during the road construction activities. A temporary sign permit is not
required, however, said signs must be located on the private property of said business,
and shall be used in such a manner so as not to constitute a safety hazard. Promotional
signage shall not include spot light devices intended to direct a column of light from the
ground towards the sky.
        (3)     This temporary ordinance shall become effective upon adoption and
signature and shall be in effect until such time as UDOT's contractors have completed the
State Street road widening project.
