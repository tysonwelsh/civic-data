 6. REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. 2022 Annual Business Survey Report
          Council Member McConnehey left the meeting at 7:27 pm He rejoined via Zoom to listen to
          the remainder of proceedings but was unable to vote.

           Business Retention/Expansion Manager Marc Summers presented results of the 2022
           Annual Business Survey (second annual) and said results would be emailed to businesses.
           Gross sales were similar to 2021 for most businesses, growth and expansion projects
           were more moderate than in 2021 due to economic uncertainty, finding skilled labor at
           the right price was reported as the largest barrier to growth, and three of the top five
           areas of focus reported by West Jordan businesses were related to workforce
           development. West Jordan businesses gave the City a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 27%,
           which was considered a favorable NPS. Mr. Summers spoke of planned workforce
           development efforts and plans to aid businesses with expansion.

           Mr. Summers answered questions from the Council. Economic Development Director
           Chris Pengra said the survey would be shortened for the next year. He said consistent
           communication and intentional help when needs were identified would help to improve
           the City’s NPS. Vice Chair Green commented that the Veteran Business Resource Center
           at Salt Lake Community College offered help to veteran-owned businesses.

           Responding to a question from Council Member Bloom, Mr. Summers said 2022 was the
           first year the Net Promoter Score was included on the survey.

 8. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Ordinance No. 22-52 amending the Preliminary Master Development Plan for
          Copper Rim Town Center (IOZ) located at approximately 5950 West 7800 South, to
          revise approved fencing in the commercial area along the Mountain View Corridor;
          Matt Dean/CW Land (applicant)
          Matt Dean with CW Land Company, representing the Copper Rim Master Developer and
          Furniture Row USA, respectfully requested a change in wall and fence building material.
          The 2021 Master Development Plan for Copper Rim Town Center included an 8-foot solid
          concrete wall fronting both commercial and residential areas. Mr. Dean said commercial
          businesses along Mountain View Corridor highly valued visibility, and it was believed a
          concrete wall would be undesirable. The request was a modification to an 8-foot
          aluminum decorative fence fronting commercial development. Mr. Dean said sound
          attenuation was also a concern with an 8-foot concrete wall fronting commercial. He
          emphasized that some businesses may choose to locate elsewhere in the valley if a
          concrete wall were required. An 8-foot concrete wall would continue to be required for
          residential development at Copper Rim.

           MOTION: Vice Chair Green moved to APPROVE Ordinance No. 22-52 amending the
           Preliminary Master Development Plan for Copper Rim Town Center (IOZ) located
           at approximately 5950 West 7800 South, to revise approved fencing in the
           commercial area along the Mountain View Corridor. Council Member Bloom
           seconded the motion.

           The vote was recorded as follows:

           Yes: David Pack, Kelvin Green, Pamela Bloom, Kayleen Whitelock
