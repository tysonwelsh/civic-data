    2. Council Q & A: Budget for Fiscal Year 2026-2027
       Mayor Wood clarified that the proposed 33% increase pertained strictly to the Public
       Safety Service Fund, which translated to a 13% property tax increase for the City overall.

        The Mayor detailed $11.7 million in budget cuts, including a $1.4 million reduction in
        the General Fund, a $1.3 million reduction in the Public Safety Fund, and an $8.6 million
        reduction in Capital Improvements, noting no transfers were made between funds to
        achieve these reductions.

        Mayor Wood also answered questions regarding the rising costs of family health
        insurance plans and explained the police department restructuring, stating that the
        reorganization utilized a currently serving lieutenant position and only cost an additional
        $8,000.

grant entry for neighborhood improvements, an accounting transfer to recognize Public Works
bond interest moving to the Debt Service Fund, and a budget reduction in water and sewer
expenditures to ensure at least 5% of revenue went toward the unrestricted fund balance
