ORDINANCE NO. 26-11

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 12.24.175(B) AND ENACTING
SECTION 12.24.185 OF THE MURRAY CITY MUNICIPAL CODE
RELATED TO MURRAY ARMORY RENTAL FEES.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MURRAY CITY MUNICIPAL COUNCIL:

Section 1. Purpose. The purpose of this ordinance is to amend section
12.24.175(B) and enact section 12.24.185 of the Murray City Municipal Code to
establish Murray Armory rental fees.

Section 2. Amendment to section 12.24.175(B) of the Murray City Municipal
Code. Section 12.24.175 of the Murray City Municipal Code shall be amended to read
as follows:

12.24.175: PARKS AND RECREATION FEES:

B. With the exception of the Murray Park Center Membership fees, the Murray
Parkway Golf Course Green Fees and Rentals, the Murray Park Outdoor Swimming
Pool Fees, and the Murray Armory rental fees, the Mayor shall establish all Parks and
Recreation related fees.

Section 3. Enactment of section 12.24.185 of the Murray City Municipal Code.
Section 12.24.185 of the Murray City Municipal Code shall be enacted to read as
follows:

12.24.185: MURRAY ARMORY RENTAL FEES:

A. Purpose: The purpose of this section is to establish fees for the rental of the
Murray Armory.

B. The Murray Armory rental fees shall be as follows:

Available Hours Resident Fee Nonresident Fee

Rates Monday through Thursday:

Morning: 8am — 2pm $400 $800
Evening: 4pm — 10pm $450 $900
All Day: 8am — 10pm $750 $1500


Rates Friday through Sunday:

Morning: 8am — 2 pm $450 $900
Evening: 4pm — 10pm $500 $1000 |
All Day: 8am-— 10pm $800 $1600

1. The rental fee includes the use of tables, chairs, Audio/Visual (A/V) equipment,
firepit, refrigerator, food preparation area, and an attendant to provide general
assistance during rental time.

2. The Mayor shall set all other fees associated with the Murray Armory including,
without limiting, damage/cleaning deposit, wedding and reception fees, city
alcoholic beverage permit (where applicable), and when used, a food truck fee.

3. If alcohol is to be served, applicants shall be responsible for consulting with the
Department of Alcoholic Beverage Services and obtaining any required permits
(e.g., Single Event Permit, Temporary Beer Event Permit). All permit fees
assessed by the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Services shall be the sole
responsibility of the applicant.

Section 4. Effective date. This Ordinance shall take effect upon first publication.

PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Murray City Municipal Council on
this 19'" day of May 2026.
