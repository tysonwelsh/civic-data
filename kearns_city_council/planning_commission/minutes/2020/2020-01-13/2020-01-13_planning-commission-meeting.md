                                            PUBLIC HEARINGS
                                        Hearings began at – 5:43 p.m.

30865 – (Continued from 11/18/2019) - Bach Homes (Nick Mason) is requesting a Change of Site Plan
approval to add 47 additional units to the existing apartment complex. Acreage: 15.07 approx. Location:
5959 South Cougar Lane. Zone: R-M, R-M/zc & C-2. Planner: Diana Martinez

MSD Counsel Nathan Bracken provided a PowerPoint presentation and responded to questions and
concerns from the previous meeting.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Diana Martinez
provided an analysis of the staff report. Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding
elimination of buildings and asked if the two buildings C and F were eliminated, would they consider
approval. Ms. Martinez said they would have to talk. Discussed agencies reviews still waiting on for
drainage and, health. Additional information needed on traffic study. Density removing or just eliminating
buildings all together.
