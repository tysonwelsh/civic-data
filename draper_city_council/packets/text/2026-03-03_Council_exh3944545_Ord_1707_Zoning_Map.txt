                                  ORDINANCE NO. 1707

        AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF DRAPER CITY BY
        REMOVING FROM THE MAP APPROXIMATELY 0.25 ACRES OF PROPERTY
        LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 16199 SOUTH 3300 EAST WITHIN DRAPER CITY,
        OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE CITY INITIATED HIGHLAND BOUNDRY
        ADJUSTMENT ZONING MAP AMENDMENT.

      WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a Zoning Ordinance and
Zoning Map to guide the orderly development and use of property within the City; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City and Highland City are adjusting their common municipal
boundary line to remove approximately 0.25 acres of property located at approximately
16199 South 3300 East as described in Section 1 below (the “Property”) from Draper City’s
municipal boundary and place it in the municipal boundary of Highland City; and

         WHEREAS, it is necessary to remove the Property from the Draper City Zoning Map as
it will no longer be located within Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed zone change set forth herein has been reviewed by the
Planning Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been
held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed revisions to
the Zoning Map; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Zoning Map of Draper
City, and the City Council has found the proposed zone change to be consistent with the
City’s General Plan.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

       Section 1.     Zoning Map Amendment. The following described real property
located at approximately 16199 South 3300 East within Draper City, Salt Lake County, State
of Utah, previously zoned A5 and within the Hillside Sensitive Lands Overlay as shown on the
Draper City Zoning Map, as depicted in Exhibit “A” hereto, is hereby removed from the Zoning
Map:

      Section 2.   Correction of Errors. The City Administrator and City Attorney may
make non-substantive corrections to this ordinance for grammatical, typographical,
numbering, and consistency purposes in accordance with the expressed intent of the City
Council.

        Section 3.   Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 4.    Effective Date. This Ordinance shall take effect upon the effective date
of the boundary adjustment between Draper City and Highland City as governed by Utah
Code § 10-2-813, or immediately upon publication or posting of this ordinance, whichever is
later.
