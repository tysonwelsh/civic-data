Study Session

     Joint session with the Draper City Parks, Trails, and Recreation
     Committee to discuss goals and priorities
     Parks and Recreation Director Rhett Ogden reviewed a list of currently planned
     projects and Council directives, and presented recommendations from the
     staff and the Parks, Trails, and Recreation (PTR) Committee:
            • Upper Corner Canyon Road Evaluation
            • Lower Maple Hollow South Trail – Drainage Crossings
            • Canyon Hollow Trail – User-Conflict Evaluation/Measures
            • Corner Canyon LLC Property Acquisition & Trails
            • Connector Trail to Mayor’s Trail (Lehi City)

     The PTR Committee also recommended the following (to be covered by
     outside funding sources):
           • Lower Zooropa Bike Trail Reconstruction
           • Rattle n’ Hum Bike Trail Reconstruction and Extension
           • Achtung Baby Bike Trail Reconstruction
           • Carpe Diem Redesignation & Upper Reconstruction

     Mr. Ogden presented a prioritized list of the proposed projects and answered
     questions from the Council. The Council and staff discussed easement
     agreements with Lehi City and Salt Lake County. Geneva Woodmansee and
     Susanne Quick, members of the PTR Committee, answered questions from a

6.b   Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for an Off-Premises Beer
      Alcohol License for Demani Enterprise LLC dba Urban Smok es
      Business License Official Travis DeJong explained the applicant's business
      was classified as General Retail Tobacco, and said the applicant’s plan as he
      understood it was to sell tobacco, snacks, and alcohol products, similar to a
      gas station convenience store. Mr. DeJong said the application met City
      distance requirements. The City was allowed a total of 51 licenses, and
      Draper currently had 22 active off-premises alcohol licenses.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing and closed the public hearing
      seeing no one come forward.

      Councilmember T. Lowery asked if staff had more information regarding
      impacts such as traffic impact. Mr. DeJong said he did not have that
      information. Desh Kahn, the applicant, said traffic for the neighboring Swig
      location would not use the same route as traffic for Urban Smokes.
      Councilmember Green stated that staff advised the existing requirements
      for approval were met.

      Councilmember Green moved to approve the Local Consent for an Off-
      Premises Beer Alcohol License for Demani Enterprise LLC dba Urban
      Smokes. The motion failed for lack of a second.

      Councilmember F. Lowry moved to continue the issue to the next
      Council Meeting. Councilmember Roberts seconded the motion.

      Councilmember T. Lowery said she continued to have concerns regarding
      the impact on neighboring businesses. Councilmember Green suggested
      that treating the application differently from the previous application, when
      the two businesses were physically located in the same area, was unwise
      considering the report from staff, and would open the City up to possible
      litigation.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed four to one.

      Council/Manager Reports – Continued from Study Session
      Councilmember Roberts said the Parkstone HOA had questions regarding
      easement issues related to plans for a trail and requested staff follow-up.

      currently on hold.
