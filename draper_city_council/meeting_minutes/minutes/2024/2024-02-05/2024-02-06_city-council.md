Study Session

          Presentation: Parks Capital Improvement Projects
          Parks and Recreation Director Rhett Ogden provided an update on completed and
          current parks capital improvement projects, and answered questions from the
          Council.

          The Council received an update on preparations for Draper Days. Mayor Walker
          reported on plans for the Gold Star Memorial dedication.

1.        Call to Order by Mayor Troy K. Walker

2.        Pledge of Allegiance led by John Vuyk

3.        Public Comments
          Christine Keyser, Draper resident, provided a handout and requested Council help
          for the Willow Bend neighborhood. She said half the residents were over 80 years
          old, many living alone, and the neighborhood also had many children who played
          on the Porter Rockwell Trail bordering on the south side of Willow Bend. Ms. Keyser
          said homeless individuals had been staying along the Willow Bend fence line along
          Porter Rockwell Trail and entering the neighborhood, taking items, and creating a
          trespass problem. She repeated her request for help from the Council.

      Laurie Carroll, a Draper resident, said she lived in Willow Bend. She said she was
      scared to go to the RV parking area because of the homeless situation and
      explained health and safety concerns, as well as property value concerns.

      Rick Bradshaw, a Draper resident in Willow Bend, described a situation in which a
      community member was attacked by a homeless individual and expressed safety
      concerns.

      Fredrick Pack, a Draper resident in Willow Bend, said the Willow Bend
      neighborhood had a vulnerable population. He spoke of trespass issues and safety
      concerns and asked the City for help.

      June Andersen, a Draper resident in Willow Bend, said there were many trees at the
      east end of the neighborhood that needed to be cleaned out before the trees
      caused damage during a storm.

5.    Items for Council Consideration
5.a   Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for a Single Event Permit for MS
      Brands LLC.
      Business License Official Travis DeJong said the application for local consent for a
      single event met the City’s single requirement (limit to number of permits issued
      in a year) for such events.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing, and closed the public hearing seeing
      no one come forward.

      Councilmember T. Lowery said her concern was related to parking and noise from
      large events at the requested venue, based on previous experience. The Council
      and staff discussed Council discretion under Code. Mr. Dobbins asked that if the
      Council was hesitant, the Council continue the item to allow time for staff to provide
      information for a more solid decision basis.

      Councilmember F. Lowry moved to continue the Local Consent for a Single
      Event Permit for MS Brands LLC to the February 20, 2024, City Council meeting.
      Councilmember Roberts seconded the motion.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed four to one.
