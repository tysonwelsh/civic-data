Planning Commission Public Hearings – Nov. 9

October 29, 2021

The Planning Commission will hold three public hearings on Tuesday, Nov. 9 beginning at 6:00 pm.  The purpose of the hearing is to make a recommendation on proposed amendments to Appendix A Allowed Uses 13.100 regarding a consideration to allowed Commercial Preschools as a Permitted, Accessory land use in Public Zones.

The purpose of the second hearing is to consider an amendment to the Zone Map from RM zone to C2 for approximately .31 acres of property located at 4049 South Highland Dr.

The purpose of the third hearing is to consider an amendment to the Zone Map from RM zone to PO for approximately .5 acres of property located at 4681 – 4685 South Highland Dr.
