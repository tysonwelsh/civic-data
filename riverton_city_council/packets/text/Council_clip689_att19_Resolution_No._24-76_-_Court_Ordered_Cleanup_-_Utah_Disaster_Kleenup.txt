                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 24-76

A RESOLUTION APPROVING A PURCHASE ORDER CONTRACT BE ISSUED TO
UTAH DISASTER KLEENUP (UDK) TO CLEAN-UP TWO PROPERTIES THAT ARE
                   COURT ORDERED CLEANUPS

       WHEREAS, the properties located at 12419 South 2320 West and 12445 South 2320
West are owned by the same person; and

      WHEREAS, both properties have been court ordered to be cleaned up; and

      WHEREAS, the owner has been non-responsive to the court order; and

       WHEREAS, Now the city can have the properties cleaned up and then put a lien on the
property to recover the cleanup costs; and

     WHEREAS, if approved, Council will need to approve this in a future budget
amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
      1. The City Council approves a Purchase Order Contract be issued to Utah Disaster
         Kleenup (UDK) to clean-up two properties that are court ordered cleanups.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 6th day of
August, 2024 by the following vote:
