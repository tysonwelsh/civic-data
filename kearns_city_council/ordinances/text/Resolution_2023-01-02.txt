 RESOLUTION NO. 2023-01-02                                     January 9,                    , 2023


A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL CORRECTING A
    TECHNICAL ERROR IN KEARNS’ MODERATE INCOME HOUSING PLAN

                                           RECITALS

WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has the authority to pass all
ordinances and rules, and make all regulations, not repugnant to law, necessary for carrying into
effect or discharging all powers and duties pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-8-84; and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council timely approved amendments to its Moderate
Income Housing Plan (“MIH Plan”) prior to October 1, 2022, in compliance with House Bill 462
(2022); and

WHEREAS, on November 22, 2022, the Utah Division of Housing and Community
Development (“Division”) notified Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) that its MIH Plan was
compliant with House Bill 462, but that the Municipality did not qualify for priority funding
consideration from the State for TIF, TIFF, and COVID Local Assistance funds, because the
MIH Plan cited and summarized rather than quoting one of the statutory strategy options; and

WHEREAS, Kearns desires to qualify for priority funding consideration from the State, and the
Division has indicated that Kearns can remedy the identified deficiency by the Kearns Council
adopting a Resolution that corrects the technical error in the MIH Plan by including the relevant
statutory provision in its entirety in the MIH Plan.

                                          RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL, effective immediately, that Kearns’ Moderate Income Housing Plan shall be
corrected to include the following statutory language in its entirety:

      “Apply for or partner with an entity that applies for state or federal funds or tax incentives
      to promote the construction of moderate income housing, an entity that applies for
      programs offered by the Utah Housing Corporation within that agency's funding capacity,
      an entity that applies for affordable housing programs administered by the Department of
      Workforce Services, an entity that applies for affordable housing programs administered
      by an association of governments established by an interlocal agreement under Title 11,
      Chapter 13, Interlocal Cooperation Act, an entity that applies for services provided by a
      public housing authority to preserve and create moderate income housing, or any other
      entity that applies for programs or services that promote the construction or preservation
      of moderate income housing” (Utah Code 10-9a-403).
