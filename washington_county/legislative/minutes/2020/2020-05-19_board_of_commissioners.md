EMERGENCY SERVICES REPORT / SHERIFF CORY PULSIPHER

Sheriff Cory Pulsipher reported that there have been twenty (20) Search and Rescue (SAR)
incidents from April 21, 2020 to May 18, 2020 for a total of 593 man-hours.

Emergency Service Operations (EOC) Director Jason Whipple reported that a large fire
broke out near the Toquerville and Leeds area on March 18, 2020 and mentioned that it

has been mostly contained.

Mr. Whipple also explained that the EOC department set up a distribution site at the
Washington County Legacy Park on May 15, 2020 for all small and local businesses to
pick up requested Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) through Utah’s PPE Push Pack

resource.

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2604, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING TWO _INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENTS TO
PROVIDE SUPPORT TO WASHINGTON COUNTY _IN_ HOSTING FOR
IRONMAN EVENTS

Deputy Tourism Director Brittany McMichael explained that Washington County will be
holding several IRONMAN events over the next five (5) years. These agreements are with
Santa Clara and Ivins outlining services provided by those municipalities during the events.
Several more interlocal agreements are expected to be executed with other surrounding
municipalities in the coming months.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-
2604, A Resolution Approving Two Interlocal Cooperation Agreements
to Provide Support to Washington County in Hosting for IRONMAN
Events. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2605, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A NOTICE OF GRANT AND AGREEMENT AWARD FROM THE
US DEPARTMENT OF __ AGRICULTURE _ NATURAL _ RESOURCES
CONSERVATION SERVICE FOR THE SANTA CLARA WATERSHED
IMPROVEMENT PROJECT

Public Works Director Ron Whitehead explained that Washington County has agreed to
be the sponsor for the Santa Clara Watershed Plan Project. The Natural Resources
Conservation Service (NRCS) has agreed to provide technical and financial assistance to
the County for the planning phase and concept design of this project. The objective of the
project is to develop a watershed plan-environmental assessment (Plan EA) for the Santa
Clara Watershed for the purpose of flood control improvement, erosion control, and water

storage.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2605, A
Resolution Approving a Notice of Grant and Agreement Award from
the US Department of Agriculture Natural Resources Conservation
Service for the Santa Clara Watershed Improvement Project. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.
