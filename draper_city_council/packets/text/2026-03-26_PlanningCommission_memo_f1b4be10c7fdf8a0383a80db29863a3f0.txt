MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper, AICP
Date: 2026-03-26
Re: Public Hearing: Openshaw Corner Development Agreement Request (Legislative
Item)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement for approximately
1.44 acres located at the northeast corner of 13800 South and the Bangerter Highway, at
approximately 207 E. 13800 S. The subject properties are currently zoned either RA1
(Residential Agricultural) or OR (Office Residential). The City Council has approved a zone
change for the properties to the CR (Regional Commercial) zone subject to the passage of
a Development Agreement. The applicant is requesting that the Development Agreement
be approved to allow for the development of the overall site as a commercial center.

Findings for Approval:

  1. The Development Agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and (D).

Findings for Denial:

  1. The Development Agreement does not comply with all required elements of DCMC
     Section 9-5-200(C) or (D).
  2. The Development Agreement as proposed is not in the best interest of the city.
