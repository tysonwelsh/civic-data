STONE MOUNTAIN CASTINGS & DESIGN - 4084 South 300 West – Project #23-019

Mr. Smallwood presented the application for Stone Mountain Castings & Design to allow a
construction contractor’s business on the subject property. The subject property is in an
industrial area on Commerce Drive. The applicant recently purchased the property and would
like to operate their fireplace/mantel contractor’s business at the location. They design, cast, and
install mantels for homes. They also provide exterior concrete work for clients. The property is
accessed by Commerce Drive and has a single twenty-foot (20’) access. This access is already
existing and there doesn’t seem to be a way to expand it further as there are two existing
buildings that abut the access.

Kirk Olsen, the applicant, indicated that the parking is located on the property to the south, which
is actually in Millcreek City. He stated as part of the sale of the property they were guaranteed
thirteen spaces and has not had an issue with the current owner of the property in Millcreek City.
Staff does not see any concerns with the parking as shown, but would request that the applicant
provide planning staff a letter from the neighboring business acknowledging the thirteen spaces.
Staff does not have any concerns with the existing landscaping. Staff noted that the property is
very well landscaped and should be maintained, if changes do occur, they will need to continue
to conform with Chapter 17.68 of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.

Based on the analysis of the Conditional Use Permit application to allow a construction
contractor’s business, staff concludes the following:
1.      The proposed use for a construction contractor’s business (LU #6600), is allowed in the
M-G Manufacturing General Zoning District subject to Conditional Use Permit approval.
2.      With conditions as outlined in the staff report, the proposed use and property will comply
with the standards of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.
3.      The proposed use is not contrary to the goals and objectives of the Murray City General
Plan in this area.

Based on the information presented in this report, application materials submitted and a site
review, staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit to
allow a construction contractor’s business at the property addressed 4084 South Commerce
Drive, subject to the conditions listed in the staff report.

Ms. Patterson commented that this zone has a lot of allowed uses and that the reason this
application is before the commission is not because of the business itself, but because they are
contractors. She feels this should be looked at as some of the ordinances and where they are
allowed to have businesses.

Mr. Olsen indicated he is able to comply with the four conditions as outlined in the staff report.
He asked about the parking lot for the business. He was told the parking spots don’t belong to
him and are on a separate parcel.

Dan Thomas, the adjacent property owner, clarified that Mr. Olsen probably does own that
parking area. Mr. Hacker asked about the landscaping and what Mr. Olsen intends to do. Mr.
Olsen said that he already cut back a lot of it and improved the property.

Mandi Olsen, also associated with the property, stated that she has issues with drainage on her
property. She said that when she has spoken with Millcreek, they said talk to Murray. When she
talked to Murray, they said to talk to Millcreek. Ms. Olsen stated that she would like to put “No
Parking” signs out front.

Mr. Smallwood stated he was unaware of a separate parcel. He was under the assumption that
was part of their property. Mr. Pehrson stated the applicant should find out if there is a separate
parcel and make an agreement with the owner of the parcel. Mr. Smallwood stated that it
appears that Murray owns half of the right-of-way and Millcreek owns the other half at the
location of the business. Mr. Smallwood will follow up with Ms. Olsen. Mr. Smallwood said that
he’ll need to talk to a city engineer about putting “no parking” signs as requested by Mandi
Olsen.

Public comment was opened. Mr. Thomas said they’ve been talking to the City Streets
department for “No Overnight” parking. He told Ms. Olsen to keep in contact with the police. In
reference to Mr. Olsen’s business, he indicated that the fire escape has been blocked off by work
going on at Mr. Olsen’s business. He wanted to make sure that was addressed.

Public comment was closed. Mr. Smallwood said the fire department will make sure there is
access to the fire escape. Ms. Milkavich asked if there’s anything that can be done to help public
service staff understand city boundaries. Mr. Smallwood said it comes down to education of
staff.

Mr. Hacker made the motion to grant Stone Mountain Castings and Design a Conditional Use
Permit for contractor business at the property addressed 4084 South 300 West, subject to the
following conditions:
1.       The applicant shall obtain a Murray City Business License prior to beginning operations
at this location.
2.       The project shall comply with all applicable building and fire code standards.
3.       The applicant shall obtain permits for any new attached or detached signs proposed for
the business.
4.       The applicant shall provide Murray City staff documentation that the business has access
to the thirteen (13) parking spaces on the adjacent property.

DARK WOODLAND INK - 150 W 4800 S, # 30 – Project #23-0120

Mustafa Al Janabi presented the application for Nicole Waldeum requesting a conditional use
permit to operate a tattoo parlor with a single bed inside an already existing business. The
subject property is a multi-use industrial building on 150 West (private) and 4800 S. The
applicant proposes to occupy an office in an existing gym business located in unit #30. This
business would share space with Sweaty Pig Gym, that was approved in 2022. The applicant
would be using a single office within the unit. No changes to the interior of the unit are proposed.
The property has multiple points of access. One point is located at 150 West, on the south side
and is approximately (16’) wide. An additional access is located in the roundabout and is
approximately twenty-five feet (25’). Staff has no concerns with the points of access currently.
The applicant states that the space they are renting is approximately 390 square feet. This will
require approximately two (2) parking spaces. The existing multi-tenant building has sufficient
parking to accommodate the proposed use. The subject property has no direct frontage on any
public streets where landscaped setbacks and other improvements would be required by the
Land Use Ordinance. Staff does not recommend any changes or additional landscaping. Based
on the analysis of the Conditional Use Permit application to allow an auto sales business, staff
concludes the following findings:

1. The proposed use for a tattooing parlor (#6295), is allowed in the M-G Commercial
Development Zoning District subject to Conditional Use Permit approval.
2. With conditions as outlined in the staff report, the proposed use and property will comply with
the standards of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.
3. The proposed use is not contrary to the goals and objectives of the Murray City General Plan
in this area.

Based on the information presented in this report, application materials submitted and a site
review, staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit to
allow a tattooing business at the property addressed 150 W 4800 S, #30, subject to conditions.

Ms. Patterson asked if the hours for Dark Woodland Ink would need to be the same as those of
The Sweaty Pig. Mr. Al Janabi indicated that they would be.

Nicole Waldeum indicated that she can comply with the conditions. Mr. Hacker asked for
clarification on having to go through the Sweaty Pig gym to access her business. She said her
business is upstairs and separate.

Public comment was opened. No comments were made. Public comment was closed.
