C-3-2024
Moca Mountain View Business Park
6507 West 5400 South
M Zone (27.08 Acres)
The applicant, CIR Engineering representing MOCA, LLC, is requesting a conditional use for
an office/warehouse facility at 6507 West 5400 South. This property is zoned M,
Manufacturing, and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as Light
Manufacturing. The surrounding zone on all sides is Manufacturing (M). The surrounding uses
include the Altaview Concrete plant to the west, Hexcel and a Rocky Mountain Power
substation to the north, Mountain View corridor to the east and a Geneva gravel pit to the
south.

The following request is for approval of a 27.08 acre two-lot office/warehouse facility. Access
to this site will be from 5400 South along a private drive. There has been discussion of the
possibility of this being a public street, however, based off timing of development and grading
constraints the applicant has decided to proceed with the private drive design.

The development will consist of two (2) buildings. The sizes are proposed to be 142,851 SF for
building 1 and 147,791 SF for building 2. The general breakdown of the uses is proposed to
have approximately 20,000 SF of office in each building, which is between 13-14%. The
uses/tenant mix will be monitored as building permits and business licenses are submitted and
the parking will be evaluated periodically to ensure that the site is in compliance. No outside
storage has been proposed.

The buildings will be constructed of tilt up concrete panels that will be painted with white and
gray tones with brown ACM Metal Panel accents. The office side of the buildings are enhanced
with large windows and relief in the façade and roof line. These features will be repeated along
both ends of the two buildings. The office portions are oriented towards the east, Mountain
View Corridor, and the truck court portions are oriented towards the west, which is along the
private drive. This facility is not required to meet the Commercial Design Standards due to the
use being limited to a manufacturing zone.

The required landscaping for this site is a minimum of 5%. The plan currently indicates there is
approximately 15% per lot. The development also has frontage on two High Image Arterial
Streets, Mountain View Corridor and 5400 South. Both of these frontages are required to
comply with the standards set forth in 7-10. Landscaping will be provided along the west
frontage access drive, the perimeter of the buildings as well as enhanced areas by the office
portions and parking areas. All areas that are not developed as buildings or parking must be
landscaped per the City Code with 50% live plant material in addition to the required trees (7-
6-303) however, there will be a large gravel detention area on the east side of the site, that is
located outside of the required setback per Code. This detention area is not required to be
formally landscaped and is not considered in the overall landscaping percentage but must be
maintained free of weeds, debris and exposed dirt areas.

The details of signage have not been discussed at this stage in the development. All signage shall
comply with the requirements set forth in the Sign Ordinance and require a building permit prior
to installation. There are several dumpsters provided throughout the complex. These will all be
located in masonry enclosures designed to match the buildings.

Staff Alternatives:

BACKGROUND

Alpine Homes is requesting preliminary plat approval for the Kiowa Court Planned Unit
Development. The subject property is located at approximately 5143 West 3500 South. The
property is zoned RM and is bordered on the east by the Westward Terrace No. 8 Subdivision and
by agricultural properties to the south and west.

STAFF/AGENCY CONCERNS:

Fire Department:

             Fire hydrants to be installed in accordance with the Uniform Fire Code.

Granger Hunter Improvement District:

             Project will need to run availability for water, sewer and fire protection.
             Subject to design and review inspections.

Utility Agencies:

             Subject to all standard easement locations.

Public Works:
             Authorization required of ditch/water users for any abandonment, relocation,
              piping or any other modifications to existing ditches or irrigation structures.
              Concerns related to tail water exiting property to the south.

             Revisions to plat are required.

                            Evaluation of storm drain facilities and infrastructure needed.

                             Will need to re-work the south end cul-de-sac to provide for a more
              traditional stub               design.

                            UDOT approval required for access on 3500 South.
             Follow recommendations in the Soils Report.

Building Inspections:

             Follow recommendations outlined in the soils report.

ISSUES:

      Alpine Homes is requesting preliminary approval for the Kiowa Court Subdivision. The
       property for the subdivision was recently rezoned from the A zone to the RM Zone. While
       the rezone allowed for attached twin homes, the applicant would like to develop the
       subdivision with a detached product. The development agreement allowed for 21 attached
       units, and the proposal before the Planning Commission is also 21 units. The overall
       density for this project is 4.8 units per acre.

      The minimum square footage of finished, above‐ground, habitable floor space for homes
       will be at least 1,650 square feet for single story ramblers and 2,400 square feet for two
       story dwellings. Since the applicant is proposing to build detached units, it is anticipated
       that all dwellings will be two story. All dwellings will include a basement and a two car
       garage. Setbacks will be as follows: Front - 20 feet; rear – 20 feet; sides – 5 feet. There
       is one area at the southeast corner of the development where 20 feet might not be possible.
       Staff is willing to consider a 15-foot setback if needed for those two units.

      All new dwellings will require a minimum roof pitch of 6:12 and brick or stone shall be
       used on 20% of the exterior of all homes. The remainder of the exterior will be fiber cement
       siding. As mentioned earlier, the original concept for this property was to be twin homes.
       When the property was purchased by Alpine Homes, they proposed single-family detached
       homes. While staff likes this approach much better, we do have a few concerns related to
       the proposed architecture. Alpine Homes is working with staff to ensure that new
       elevations incorporate the various elements of the original twin home architecture. To date,
       we have one elevation that works, and per the development agreement, the project is to
       have another elevation. Alpine has submitted two additional elevations that are being
       reviewed by staff.

      Access to the subdivision will be gained from Kiowa Way an existing stub street in the
       Westward Terrace No. 8 Subdivision to the east and from 3500 South. The stub street has
       existed since the Westward Terrace No. 8 Subdivision was recorded in 1972. During the
       rezone, a lot of discussion centered on the extension of the stub street. While many did not
       want a connection, some thought it would help alleviate internal traffic by allowing a more
       direct connection to 3500 South than having residents wend their way through the existing
       subdivision as has been done for years. A transition with the old County 50-foot right-of-
       way to the City’s 54-foot right-of-way will need to be made.

      The new road will be a 54-foot dedicated right-of-way. While the development will be a
       planned unit community, private streets are no longer an option. The new road will be
       designed with curb, gutter, and sidewalk on both sides. Guest parking will be provided in
     two areas of the development so each unit will be parked at 4.75 spaces. In anticipation of
     future development to the south, the applicant is also providing a stub street at the south
     boundary.


    Access at 3500 South has been designed to align with Park Vista Drive on the north side
     of 3500 South. As has been done with all recent development on 3500 South, the developer
     will be dedicating an additional 17.5 feet for future widening of this right-of-way. The site
     can accommodate this widening, but it does reduce the setback to the adjacent dwelling
     known as lot 1. Staff will work with the applicant to provide a minimum setback of 15 feet
     and hopefully 20 feet for this dwelling. Existing improvements along 3500 South are likely
     to remain in place for a number of years. Therefore, the additional area of dedication will
     need to be improved with landscaping and maintained by the HOA until such time as full
     improvements are made by UDOT.

    The subject property has been farmed for many years, and has likely been irrigated. The
     developer will be required to ensure that any and all irrigation water is managed
     appropriately. This will be coordinated with the City’s Engineering Division and any water
     users in the area.

    The applicant has submitted a soils report that addresses ground water issues. No ground
     water was encountered in any test pit ranging from 8-13 feet deep. However, an artesian
     well was located behind the existing dwelling as well as other spring water. The
     geotechnical firm recommends that a qualified hydrologist design a well cap for the
     artesian well and a possible land drain system for the project. The soils report is being
     evaluated by the City’s Building Official and Engineering Division and recommendations
     will be forthcoming about their findings.

    Per the development agreement, perimeter fencing will be installed along the west, south,
     and east sides. Fencing in these locations shall be 6-feet tall and will be vinyl or composite
     materials. The perimeter fence along the north side adjacent to 3500 South shall be a 6-
     foot tall pre-cast concrete in the same color and pattern used by the City along 4100 South.
     Specifications for pattern and color will be provided to the applicant.

    The proposed development will consist of a number of amenities. These include the
     following:

        o An entry feature along 3500 South. The details of the entry feature will be
          coordinated as the subdivision moves through the application process.
        o A 5’ wide area of landscaping shall be included between the sidewalk and precast
          concrete wall along 3500 South. This area will be maintained by the HOA
        o Amenities shall include a pavilion placed in the common area, a playground, and a
          walkway around the perimeter of the property.

                       The existing landscaped areas will be cleaned up and weeds within the right-
                        of-way and beyond will be cleared away.
                       Improvements to the existing dwelling are to be made, i.e., painting etc.
                       Window mounted A/C will be replaced with central A/C

      The improvements to the existing dwelling and the addition of two new single-family

      basements, the Building Official has determined that the lowest floor slab shall be 4-feet
      from top back of curb. Should a future builder want a deeper basement, they can submit a
      soils report showing that one could be built.
