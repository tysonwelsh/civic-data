Emergency orders approved by Council at the start of the Covid-19 pandemic were reviewed. Council chose
to renew enforcement of temporary sign regulations beginning June 1, 2021. Council agreed to continue the
business license renewal fee waiver. The proposed Laguna Malibu development was presented during the work
session and Council requested more information. Development in the southern foothills area has been boosted
by the addition of water tanks. Council also requested exhibits and case studies regarding flag lots.

  1.2. Review of Agenda Items – Planning Staff
Commissioners stated no discussion was needed for tonight’s agenda item.

    1.3. Presentation of New Elementary School in the Hidden Oaks Development – Dave
         Rostrum, Jordan School District
Jordan School District Representative David Rostrum announced a new elementary school in the Hidden Oaks
Development. He stated the design would be 75,000 square feet and the same as Bastian and Ridge View
Elementary schools. City Planner Maloy provided the vicinity map, land use plan, site plan, and open space
summary. Rendering designs and the floor plan were displayed. No timeline had been determined for
construction of the adjacent middle school. A temporary cul-de-sac was to provide a turnaround for emergency
services vehicles. The groundbreaking was scheduled for May 13th, 2021, and it was projected to open for
students in autumn of 2022. Commissioner Jacobson asked if grading of nearby land would restrict residential
development. It was stated there was a 28-foot decent between the southwest and northeast corners, and the
slope would create a drop-off near the sports fields. The school district worked with staff and nearby home
builders to minimize impacts on road configuration and future residential development. A perimeter drain
system would be installed to limit runoff. Commissioner Jacobson asked if runoff would impact nearby
basements. The applicant replied they had no concerns with that potential issue. School opening was contingent
on the completion of roads and utilities.

    1.4. Training – Michael Maloy, City Planner
City Planner Maloy presented training videos. Commissioners discussed urban design, guiding principles,
neighborhood block plans, planned development sizes and equestrian areas.

The work meeting adjourned by consensus at 6:58 p.m.

    3.1. Request: An Approval of a Conditional Use and Site Plan for an indoor recreational use
         known as Sommer Sports Complex (Athletics Facility)
         Applicant: David Peterson (authorized agent)
         Address: 5693 Mirabella Drive
         Zone: C-2 Commercial
         Acres: 2.66
         File Number: C2021-015
Planner Sheldon Howa reviewed the request. The centrally located site was a permitted use in a C-2 zone, which
was consistent with the General Plan. Phase one proposed a 12,000 square feet indoor recreation facility It
would contain two indoor basketball courts to be rented as training facilities. Phase two would contain
Professional office space. The site plans and floor plans were displayed. The exterior building elevations met
the 60% brick or stone requirement. The 100 + ft. long building required three design elements such as texture,
color, building materials or architectural features. The landscape plan met City standards and water efficiency
requirements. They proposed 60 parking stalls, which exceeded the 51 spaces required for both phases, by the
trip study. Staff requested the addition of columns on the north/rear elevation to break up the facade, due to
the visibility from the right of way. The building would be for practice sessions only and no tournaments would
be held on site. Fifty-foot landscaping and a precast wall buffered the residential area. Staff expressed concern
with the dumpster location in the northwest corner. They thought it was too close to homes and recommended
relocation to the northeast corner of the lot. Discussions need to take place with a nearby resident regarding
replacement of a vinyl fence with a precast wall.

Gerald Sommer spoke on behalf of the applicant David Peterson. They requested the dumpster remain in the
proposed location due to traffic flow. Another dumpster would be added with phase two construction. The
facility would not have bleachers. It may be rented for athletic uses only, possibly volleyball or pickleball. It
would not be rented for parties of wedding receptions. City Planner Maloy stated staff initially expressed concern
with competitions or events taking place that would overwhelm the parking. The applicant assured staff it would
be for sports practice only. Commissioners discussed adding a recommendation requiring use for sports only
and limiting parking as a condition of approval. Commissioner Powell thought the trip study for phase two
looked low. He asked if staff had reviewed it. Engineer Josh Petersen responded staff had reviewed the study
and were comfortable with the numbers. The hours of operation were anticipated from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m.,
however they may be cut back dependent upon usage. Commissioner Bradford was concerned with traffic and
asked if another light was planned on Rosecrest Road. Engineer Petersen responded there were no plans for
another signal and the traffic study indicated no offsite actions were needed. Commissioners expressed concern
the trip generation would increase with phase two. Commissioners had no concerns with the dumpster location.
The applicant may add additional windows along the top around the building. Commissioner Ferguson noted
the dumpster was located the same distance from homes as the adjacent church dumpster.

Recommendations:
Staff recommends granting conditional use approval for Sommer Sports Complex with the following conditions:
    1. This approval is for Phase 1 of the development. Phase 2 of the development must come back to
         Planning Commission for approval.
    2. Columns or other architectural features to be added to north elevation to break-up long wall expanse.
    3. All lighting shall be shielded so as not to shine into surrounding residences.
    4. Provide amenities for onsite bicycle parking.
    5. Any rooftop mechanicals should be placed on the north slope of the roof, screened, and at a height
         below the ridgeline.
    6. No signs are approved with this request, separate approval will be required. No signs are allowed on
         public property. All signs must be located on private property.
    7. Move trash receptacle location to the northeast corner of site. Outdoor trash receptacle shall be
         screened to standards outlined by City Code 10-23-13.
    8. Receive and agree to the recommendation of other Administrative bodies, agencies and/or City
         departments.
    9. The facility shall be used for sports and recreational uses only and only at a capacity that does not
         exceed the facility’s on-site parking

Commissioner Lorin Palmer MOVED to approve item 3.1 file number C2021-015 Conditional Use and Site Plan for an
indoor recreational use known as Sommer Sports Complex (Athletic Facility) removing recommendation item #7 and adding #9
stating that it only be used for sports and recreation and not exceed the current parking. Commissioner Adam Jacobson
SECONDED the motion.

4. Chair and Commission Comment
City Planner Maloy explained most of the potential foothills development property was zoned Forestry
Recreation (FR) and in need of a text change to remove dwelling groups from the zone. Text changes may only
be requested by specific entities including the Commission, but not by staff. Commissioners agreed to direct
staff to initiate the text amendment to clarify the language. Chair Berbert asked Commissioners and staff to
look at the windows in the Herriman Town Center and confirm they were those requested by the Commission.
He also requested for staff to report the results back to the Commission.
