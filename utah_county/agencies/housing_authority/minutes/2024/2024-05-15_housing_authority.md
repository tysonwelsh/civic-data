V. Financial Statement Update 
 Accounting provided the financial update for April 2024. Everything is on track and moving forward

VI. PRESENTATION: 
 Auditors Presentation 2023 Annual Audit  
Third party contracted auditors presented the annual 2023 audit to the board. All information is 
reviewed as provided and is considered an independent audit. REAC has been submitted and is in 
review. The FDS is pending completion and will be submitted to HUD soon. HAUC received 
unmodified opinions on all reports. No audit findings and no prior year findings required comment 
or corrections. Auditors provide reasonable assurance the financial statements are present fairly. 
The auditors reviewed compliance with the new GASB 96 rules and find HAUC did not need to 
comply since there was nothing to report at this time. No concerns of fraud and no significant or 
unusual transactions. Overall management oversight is good with no findings, comments, or 
issues for management.

C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-05-03 Purchase Agreement Johnson Grove Purchase 
April made a motion to approve the Johnson Grove Purchase Agreement. Suzanne 
seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
Approval is sought to move forward with HAUC’s legal counsel to generate a purchase 
agreement. The purchase will consist of standard due diligence including building inspection, 
title and survey review by legal counsel, liability/hazard insurance, and closing at a title 
company determined by HAUC. HAUC will also work to prelease the units so as to generate 
revenue and provide affordable housing. HAUC desires to purchase 3 individual condo units 
located in Santaquin, UT. The units are new construction and will be stacked on above the 
other. Each unit consists of 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom and are approximately 970  sq.ft

G. Discuss/Approve Resolution 2024-05-07 HAUC Cell Phone Reimbursement 
Steve made a motion to approve the HAUC Cell Phone Reimbursement. April seconded 
the motion. Motion passed unanimously.  
HAUC is requesting for upper management to be reimbursed for cell phone usage. This will 
allow staff to be contacted when necessary, this can often be after hours or when  Director is 
out of the office or staff is out of town at training. This will create a policy and will be an 
automatic payment made to upper management staff only. The auditors were contacted and 
determined that the wording reimbursement is not a problem and no receipt is required from 
staff for reimbursement.
