        6. INFORMATION.
              a. Financial Literacy Month
              Mayor Wood read a proclamation declaring April as Financial Literacy Month in
              South Salt Lake. The proclamation emphasizes the City's commitment to its core
              promises: ensuring every child has the opportunity to graduate from college,
              every resident has a safe home, and everyone has the opportunity to be healthy
              and prosper. The proclamation highlights the importance of financial literacy in
              achieving these goals, recognizing its role in helping individuals make informed
              financial decisions and build a strong foundation for their future. It also
              acknowledges the work of Promise South Salt Lake, which offers financial
              literacy programs and services to support the community's financial well-being.
              Mayor Wood called on all residents, businesses, and institutions in South Salt
              Lake to commit to improving financial literacy and promoting smart money
              management.
