2.
Convene as the Board of Canvassers (4:40 PM)
Christopher Robinson made a motion to convene as the Board of Canvassers. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong, Tonja B Hanson.
1)
Certify the election results are true, accurate, and final, for the Primary Canvass; Eve Furse (4:40 PM)
Attachment: Summit County Primary Canvass 6 25 2024
Eve Furse, County Clerk , presented to Council the results of the 2024 June Primary Election for certification.
Council members asked follow-up questions, to which Clerk Furse responded.
Council Chair Stevens opened the meeting for public comment at 5:01 PM.
Karen Ballash addressed concerns to Council.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to Certify the Canvass for Summit County's Primary Election held on June 25, 2024 as presented Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong, Tonja B Hanson.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to dismiss as the Board of Canvassers. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong, Tonja B Hanson.
Attachment: Karen Ballash Statement
Attachment: Public Input Sign-In Sheet
3.
Adjournment (5:06 PM)
Christopher Robinson made a motion to adjourn. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong, Tonja B Hanson.
