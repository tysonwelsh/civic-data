PUBLIC HEARINGS
A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING THE WEST VALLEY CITY
     POLICE DEPARTMENT'S ACCEPTANCE OF A JUSTICE ASSISTANCE
     GRANT (JAG) THROUGH THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE
     PROGRAMS
     Mayor Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular Council
     Meeting scheduled February 6, 2024, in order for the City Council to hear and
     consider public comments regarding the West Valley City Police Department's
     Acceptance of a Justice Assistance Grant (JAG) through the U.S. Department of Justice
     Programs.

        Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
        information as follows:

               The U.S. Department of Justice Program is awarding the West Valley City
               Police Department the 2023 Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance
               Grant (J.A.G.). The allowable expenses of the grant include the purchase of
               equipment and the funding of training and programs they approve through
               the application process. The funds are allocated to West Valley City based
               on a formula that considers the population of West Valley City and our part
               one crime statistics. There is separate funding allocated to each state to
               ensure fairness. The J.A.G. Program allows states and local governments to
 MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING FEBRUARY 6, 2024

           implement otherwise unfunded programs designed to improve the criminal
           justice system.

           The West Valley City Police Department Investigations Units, Patrol
           Division, Special Operations Units, SWAT Team, Intelligence Unit, and
           Evidence Unit are in need of updated software and equipment. Department
           funds are not sufficient to cover the additional expense of equipping these
           units with the new or updated equipment or software and funds under this
           grant award will assist with this immensely. This equipment and software
           will increase the department’s ability to respond to, prepare for, and/or
           prevent crime or disorder in our community.

    Mayor Lang opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor or
    opposition, Mayor Lang closed the Public Hearing.

    ACTION: RESOLUTION NO. 24-16, AUTHORIZE WEST VALLEY CITY
    TO ACCEPT THE EDWARD BYRNE MEMORIAL JUSTICE ASSISTANCE

      THE EMPLOYEE DISCIPLINE HEARING OFFICER
      John Flores, Interim Assistant City Manager, presented proposed Resolution 24-22 that
      would ratify the Appointment of Clint Drake as the Employee Discipline Hearing Officer.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
      follows:

             Section 3-11-101 of the West Valley City Municipal Code requires the appointment
             of an Employee Discipline Hearing Officer to hear and decide certain employee
             appeals. Said appointment requires the advice and consent of the City Council.
             Mr. Drake has served as the Employee Discipline Hearing Officer in the past and
             is knowledgeable and experienced in the field of employment law. A contract for
             Mr. Drake’s services has been prepared for approval by the City Council.

      Councilmember Nordfelt moved to approve Resolution 24-22.

      Councilmember Christensen seconded the motion.
