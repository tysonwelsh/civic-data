 3.           Resolution 2022-02 supporting the environmental protections for Utah Lake. (21-122)
              (20:59)

        Motion:     An implied motion on Resolution 2022-02, as currently constituted, had been made by
                    council rule.

Councilor Handley provided background of research and encouraged council to pass resolution.

Councilor Ellsworth discussed the impact on the community that the resolution would provide if passed.

Vice-Chair Shipley opened public comment.

Craig Christensen of Conserve Utah Valley expressed his gratitude in the council looking into this and
disclosed his expectations going forward.

Angela Marrick of Provo and Conserve Utah Valley thanked council for taking leadership role and for
educating the public.

Adam Johnson of Provo thanked council for being in tune with residents’ desires to preserve the lake.

Anna Monson of Provo said she had lived in the Orem/Provo area for 19 years and had only been to the
lake twice due to misinformation and myths. She stated this initiative has provided her with more

Vice-Chair Shipley thanked Councilors Handley and Ellsworth for their work and expertise.

Councilor Fillmore shared that while serving on the Board of Directors for the Chamber of Commerce, he
thought the best thing for the lake would be to dredge it. He stated that after receiving more scientific
based education, he now understands how counterproductive to long term viability and health dredging
would be for the lake. He stated that he hopes the state legislature will address some of these issues.

Councilor Ellsworth thanked Councilor Fillmore for his open mindedness and said she hopes that it is
reflected in the community.

Councilor Handley also thanked Councilor Fillmore. He added that he would like to encourage the
celebration of the lake from the community.

Councilor Fillmore added that the Provo Marina is nothing to brag about. He proposed that the council
encourage legislators to provide more funding for the marina or for them to turn it over for Provo City to
maintain.

Mayor Kaufusi said she wanted to add her name to the resolution.

      Motion:           Councilor Ellsworth made a motion to change the resolution to a joint resolution.
                        Seconded by Councilor Handley.

Various councilmembers thanked Mayor Kaufusi for participation.

Vice-Chair Shipley called for vote on the motion.

      Vote:     The amended motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley,
                Hoban, Mackay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.

Vice-Chair Shipley called for a vote on the joint resolution.

 6.           The election of the Redevelopment Agency Chair and Vice-chair. (22-001) (51:21)
