Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Chief Del Craig, Unified Police Department, stated crimes and calls for service
are staying steady. UPD is down about 50 crimes from last year. Public Order calls are also
down and UPD hopes that continues. Unfortunately, gang activity has increased. UPD is in the
process of conducting and finalizing a youth gang assessment as part of a grant it received. It is
looking at risk and contributory factors. Gang activity tends to ebb and flow in Magna and can be
unpredictable. The main activity is on the east side of the city near the border of West Valley City.
He has met with the police Chief of West Valley to discuss how to collaborate and fight gang
activity. Most of the time with gang activity, the victims and the perpetrators know each other.

DATE           TUESDAY                             APRIL                                25, 2023

                 Council Member Hull stated UPD is implementing a youth court that will handle
and redirect the first time offenders. It has also received funds from Choose Gang Free (CGF) to
hire two more advocates. These advocates are very effective in getting kids to open up and talk.
It is also looking into parenting programs specific to youth involved in gangs. There are former
gang members on the task force who have real-world experience.

Consideration of Ordinance No. 2023-O-07, An Ordinance of the Magna Metro Township Council
Amending the Zoning Map to Change the Zoning of the Property at 8519 West 3100 South from
R-1-6 to C-1

                Curtis Woodward, Senior Planner, Municipal Services District (MSD), stated the
property, a limited-scale restaurant, has always been in a residential zone since it was built in the
1950s. The current owner, Michael Forsyth, is seeking to sell the property to a potential buyer
who intends to use the property as a catering business instead of a restaurant. The MSD does
not have the authority to approve such a change and encouraged Mr. Forsyth to apply for a
rezone. The property is small and converting it into a home would be impractical and costly. The
Planning Commission recommends approving the zoning amendment with the stipulation that the
property can be used for specific limited purposes.

               Michael Forsyth stated he has been trying to sell his properties at 8519 West and
3100 South but has lost potential buyers because of the zoning limitations. There are about 4 to
5 feet in between the buildings and other than that, there is just a driveway. This property has
been operating as a commercial property since 1957.

                                                 7
DATE           TUESDAY                              APRIL                                25, 2023

               Council Member Hull, seconded by Council Member Barney, moved to approve
the following Ordinance 2023-O-07, changing the zoning of the property at 8519 West 3100 South
from an R-1-6 to a C-1, subject to the conditions recommended by the Planning Commission.
The motion passed unanimously.
