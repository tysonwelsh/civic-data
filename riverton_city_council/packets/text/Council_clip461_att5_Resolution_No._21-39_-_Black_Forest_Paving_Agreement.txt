                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 21-39

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
 WITH BLACK FOREST PAVING TO COMPLETE THE 2021 OVERLAY PROJECT


       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, the Public Works Director deems this project is necessary to complete this
project.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council approves Resolution No. 21-39 authorizing the Mayor to execute
            an agreement with Black Forest Paving to complete the 2021 Overlay Project.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 20th day of
July 2021 by the following vote:
