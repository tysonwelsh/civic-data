WETLANDS AREA 
Commissioner Vernon asked where the 40-foot requirement came from.  Commissioner 
Clyde said it is the minimum setback accepted by the army core of engineers (ACE).  
When the State Code refers to wetlands, it is as defined by the ACE.    
 
Planner Milliner said when someone comes to the counter with a set of plans, the 
reviewer will look at the plans to see if there may be any wetlands on the property.  If 
wetlands may be indicated, the applicant is asked to provide a wetland delineation.  
Commissioner Vernon asked if the reviewer is basing this on the County's map.  Planner 
Milliner said it is used as a guide.  If an area is known to be generally wet, it requires 
further review.  Generally, the applicant will hire a consultant to delineate it.  The ACE 
will review it and either approve or deny the application.
