                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 24-69

     A RESOLUTION APPOINTING AN ALTERNATE CITY REPRESENTATIVE
                  TO THE JORDAN RIVER COMMISSION

       WHEREAS, Riverton City adopted Resolution No. 14-68 on November 11, 2014 which
approved the execution of an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with the Jordan River
Commission; and

       WHEREAS, the City is entitled to appoint an alternate representative to serve on the
Jordan River Commission; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Councilmember Tish
Buroker is willing to serve as the City’s alternate representative on the Jordan River
Commission.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointment of
          Councilmember Tish Buroker to serve as the City’s alternate representative on
          the Jordan River Commission until a successor is appointed.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
July 2024 by the following vote:
