City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On June 21, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1548, vacating a portion
of a Right-of-Way located south of 12300 South. The complete ordinance is on file at
the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this
27th day of June 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
