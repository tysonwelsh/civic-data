OPENING:
     Mayor Pro Tem Hughes welcomed all in attendance. He recognized member of the Youth
     City Council who were in the audience. The only item on tonight’s agenda is to interview
     applicants for the vacant Mayor position. He noted that he has had the honor of serving as
     the Mayor Pro Tem since Mayor Pike’s recent resignation as well as for the past several
     years. Mayor Pro Tem Hughes stated he will continue in this role until a new Mayor is
     sworn in. He explained that in preparation of filling the vacant Mayor seat, the City has
     followed the process as outlined in State code 20A-1-510.

       An invocation was offered by Councilmember McArthur and The Pledge of Allegiance to the
       Flag was offered by Councilmember Smethurst.

       Mayor Pro Tem Hughes asked City Manager Adam Lenhard to conduct the remainder of the
       meeting since he is a candidate.

       City Manager Adam Lenhard explained a list of the applicants and the order of the
       interviews was posted. The order was determined using the Lieutenant Governor’s
       random master ballot list for 2020-2021. Each applicant will be given three (3) minutes to
       speak. After each applicant has their time to present, the Council will proceed with voting.

Richard L Kirkwood
      Mr. Kirkwood thanked the Council for the opportunity to serve. He commented his goals
      are aligned with the Council: to work with the citizens to make St. George a better place.

      His goal is to serve with integrity. He asked for the Council’s support.

Robert Klarich

George F Whitehead:
     Mr. Whitehead commented he born and raised in St. George. The City has made a
     significant impact on his life. His hope that as Mayor, he can continue to have the same
     impact for those living here. He was educated here and has enjoyed many programs as a
     youth, learning to work hard. Following graduation from Dixie College, he attended and

Laura Bean
      Ms. Bean stated this is not a position she really desires. She recently attended a few
      meetings where they discussed how bad the nation is becoming, and the government in
      general. The groups discussed how the local area would need to be more firmly focused
      on the principals of the US Constitution and the Utah Constitution and adhere to actual
      law. While in these meetings, she felt a fire to step up. She hopes the right person is
      chosen for this position and hopes the City more fully embraces the heritage and values.
      She believes the City has strayed a bit and feels it is time to get back on the right track.
      Personally, she is quite transparent and has not hidden her feelings and beliefs; more of
      that is needed in government. As Mayor, she would honor the relationship with her
      constituents. Her only goal would be to serve them and support the Constitution. St.
      George needs someone in a leadership role who is willing to do what is right, no matter
      what. The proper role of a civil servant is to obey the will of the people, not the loud
      minority with strings. Many of her friends have given up on the US Constitution. She has
      met with many St. George citizens and has a good relationship with them.

Michele Randall:
      Ms. Randall explained she has sat on the Council for the past seven (7) years. While
      serving on the Council, she has learned so much from the current Council as well as
      previous Councils, Mayor Pike, City Manager, and staff. She has worked extremely hard
      for the citizens and employees of the City. She has been a champion for employees,
      making sure they have had the compensation they deserve as they are working each day
      to keep our city safe, clean, and functioning. Over the past seven (7) years, she has
      returned almost every email and phone call received from citizens as well as having met
      with citizens at City Hall, their home, and her home to find resolutions to problems they
      were having; being a conduit between the residents and City Hall. She currently sits on
      the Board of the Utah League of Cities and Towns – this gives St. George “a seat at the
      table” to have its issues in northern Utah. She has the experience to serve as Mayor, she
      is a leader, organized, a problem solver, proactive, has time management skills, and is a
      listener. She will seek out the Council’s opinions on the many important issues that come
      before them and will rely on the City Manager, City Attorney, and Department Heads for
      their advice and expertise. She noted that she has the time to put in full-time hours if
      necessary. Having lived in St. George since 1978, she has attended school here, raised
      her family here, and now her kids are raising their families here. She has witnessed St.
      George grow from a small town to the seventh largest city in the state. If appointed, she
      will be available for all residents and will continue to respond to all inquiries personally as
      much as possible. A goal she will always strive for is to continue the “Dixie spirit” as the
      City continues to grow.
