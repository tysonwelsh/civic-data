6:30 PM Business Meeting

Chair Andrew Adams called the meeting to order at 6:32 PM and welcomed those present.

1.     Items for Commission Consideration.

       A.      Public Hearing: GearBox IT, LLC Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit.
               (Administrative Action).
               On the request of Kendall Lewis representing GearBox IT, LLC, a Conditional Use
               Permit for a Home Occupation that Provides Out Call Tech and Security System
               Services and Installation on approximately 0.11 Acres, located at approximately
               11871 South Inauguration Road. Known as Application 2024-0222-USE. Staff
               Contact: Nick Whittaker, (801)576-6522, Nick.Whittaker@draperutah.gov.

City Planner, Nick Whittaker presented the Staff Report and stated that GearBox IT, LLC provides
out-call security services. The zoning is R-3 and the land use is Residential Medium Density. Site
plans were presented showing the external yard areas and internal office space to be used within
the home. The main reason for the conditional use is to provide parking for a work vehicle. Mr.
Whittaker stated that this property is unique in that the driveway is rear-loaded from a private alley.
The vehicle will not be visible from the public street. Parking the work vehicle on the property is
the impact. The proposed mitigation of that impact is that the work vehicle will not be parked on
the street. Installation of a designated parking stall for the business vehicle will be located in the
rear yard next to the existing driveway. A rendering of the front of the property was presented.
There is no driveway, which is true for the entire neighborhood. The subdivision was designed
that way. A rendering of the private alleyway was displayed. There are currently pavers in the

parking area. The applicant will construct a concrete pad for the parking location of the work
vehicle.

In response to a question raised by Chair Adams about one of the photos presented, Mr. Whittaker
clarified that a nearby shed is owned by the applicant, Kendall Lewis, and is not related to the
business. The only areas on the property being proposed for business use have been identified.

A question was raised as to whether the concrete pavers count as the surface required by Code,
which must be asphalt or concrete. Mr. Whittaker confirmed that they do not count and that hard
surface pavement is the standard the applicant will be held to.

In response to a question raised regarding other vehicles on the site as seen in the photograph that
were in the presentation, Mr. Whittaker explained that there is one work vehicle and the rest are
personal vehicles. The applicants will make sure that all personal vehicles are parked in the correct
locations, which is the driveway. Mr. Whittaker explained that there is a two-car garage in the
back and the applicants have adequate parking for the residential use. The property lines on the
rendering were clarified.

Mr. Lewis identified himself as the owner of GearBox IT which has been in business since 2017.
He explained that he leases additional storage space in a location outside the city where other
supplies and inventory are kept. Employees of the business work from the other location. He only
has an office space and the one work vehicle at the property.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public hearing
was closed.

Motion: Commissioner Fowler moved to APPROVE the Home Occupation Conditional Use
Permit as requested by Kendall Lewis representing GearBox IT, LLC, Application 2204-
0222-USE, based on the following:

Finding:

       1.      The proposal complies with the standards for approval found in DCMC
               Section 9- 5-080(E) and potential negative impacts are mitigated through the
               imposition of reasonable conditions.

Second: Commissioner Nixon seconded the motion.

Vote: A roll call vote was taken with the Commissioners voting 4-to-0 in favor of the motion.
Commissioners Fowler, Nixon, Fidler, and Shah voted “Yes”. The motion passed
unanimously.
