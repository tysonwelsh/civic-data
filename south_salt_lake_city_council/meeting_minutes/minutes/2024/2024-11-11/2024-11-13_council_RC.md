NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Council Meeting—December 11th @ 7pm.

       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          Richard Lane, South Salt Lake resident, expressed his agreement with the City’s
          direction on the subject of short-term rentals.

              Five South Salt Lake business owners, and one local attorney, expressed their
              disagreement with the proposed Auto-Related Ordinance.

              Granite Library Manager, Kim Welch, informed those at the meeting of upcoming
              events and activities at the library.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood congratulated everyone who ran for office during this election season
          and discussed the potential impact of Council Member Pinkney's departure to the
          Salt Lake County Council.

              A social media video was shared with those present at the meeting that highlighted
              this year’s ‘Spooky City Hall’ hosted by the City Hall’s departments for families with
              kids.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          City Attorney, Josh Collins, shared that the Judicial Nominating Commission recently
          had their first meeting to sort through applicants for the upcoming justice court
          judge vacancy. They were narrowed down to eight candidates who have been
          forwarded to the Mayor for review. The City Council will take final action on the
          Mayor’s proposed candidate in January.

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Sanchez thanked the business owners who have reached out to
          him regarding the proposed auto-related Ordinance.

           Council Member Pinkney addressed the pending Salt Lake County election results
           and her possible resignation from the City Council if she receives confirmation to
           move forward with the Salt Lake County Council At-Large seat.

           Council Member Bynum thanked the business owners who came to the meeting and
           emphasized that no final action would take place at tonight’s meeting.

           Council Member Huff thanked the business owners who came to the meeting.

           Council Member Mitchell thanked the business owners who came to the meeting.

           Council Member Thomas thanked the business owners who came to the meeting.

           Council Member Williams thanked the business owners who came to the meeting.
           She also highlighted November as American Indian Heritage Month and expressed
           the need to spread awareness about missing and murdered Indigenous women.

       6. INFORMATION.

               a. Just Serve Proclamation
                  Mayor Wood read the proclamation that highlights the City’s endeavor to
                  be a community of dedicated volunteers that will help to accomplish the
                  City’s goals of fostering a community where children, youth, and families
                  have the access to education, clean homes, safe neighborhoods, and the
                  chance to live, learns, work, play, and worship.

                   The City has been a partner with JustServe.org for several years and has
                   continued to deepen its partnership with the organization.

               b. Victim Advocate Report
                  Victim Services Supervisor, Jodi Fait, shared the data from her division’s
                  work over the July-September quarter. 693 clients were provided services
                  through the VOCA (Victims of Crime Act) grant, and 507 clients were served
                  through the VAWA (Violence Against Women Act) grant.
