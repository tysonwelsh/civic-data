RESOLUTION 21-01: AUTHORIZE THE AGENCY TO EXECUTE A
WATERLINE           EASEMENT          IN    FAVOR OF    GRANGER-HUNTER
IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT
Chair Lang presented proposed Resolution 21-01 that would approve a Business
Stabilization and Revitalization Grant Agreement.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Granger-Hunter Improvement District is replacing waterlines in 3500 South near
       1200 West. To facilitate its project Granger-Hunter Improvement District is
       requesting a Waterline Easement across property owned by the Redevelopment
       Agency of West Valley City and located at 3344 South 1200 West (parcel #15-26-
       352-016). The proposed easement is 15 feet wide by 20 feet long and is to be
       located at the northeast corner of the RDA property at the southwest corner of 3300
       South and 1200 West. Compensation to the Redevelopment Agency of West
       Valley City in the amount of $2,500.00 will be paid by Granger-Hunter
       Improvement District.

Upon inquiry by Chairperson Lang there were no further questions from members of the
Agency, and she called for a motion.

       Mr. Bigelow moved to approve Resolution 21-01.

       Mr. Christensen seconded the motion.

       A roll call vote was taken:

       Mr. Fitisemanu                Yes
       Mr. Buhler                    Yes
       Mr. Huynh                     Yes
       Mr. Bigelow                   Yes
       Mr. Nordfelt                  Yes
       Mr. Christensen               Yes
       Chairperson Lang              Yes

       Unanimous.

MOTION TO ADJOURN
Upon motion by Mr. Huynh, all voted in favor to adjourn.
