3)
Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for a ~2,700 sq. ft. Major Accessory Building, located at 7914 N Greenfield Dr, Silver Creek, Summit County, UT; Parcel SL-F-350 (2.57 acres). Applicant: Tressa Roberts, Beehive Buildings. Owner: Cyrus DeVere. Administrative review. Project #23-129. Jennifer Leslie, County Planner.
(6:23 P.M.)
Jennifer Leslie, Planner, presented a proposed CUP for a major accessory building.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions to which Planner Leslie and the applicant responded.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:29 p.m.
No public comments were offered.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:29 p.m.
Commissioner Cooke asked additional questions.
Thomas Cooke made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit for Project #23-129 based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in the staff report with the additional condition that the driveway extension will be pervious surface and not asphalt to be verified by county staff. DJ Hubler seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Makena Hawley.
