     2.          DISCUSSION ITEM: Discussion Concerning Amending Chapter 13.19 (Site Specific Development Districts)
                 of the Taylorsville City Code and the Proposed Chapter 13.41 SSD-IN. (Mark McGrath)


     2.1           7:00 PM Mark McGrath gave a Power Point presentation regarding this item which showed the proposed location and
            an example of what the building will look like. In the proposed zone classification - SSD-IN Temple the IN stands for
            Institutional. He advised the drawings at this point are still very basic and were submitted in order to give the Commissioners
            some idea of what is being proposed. He also advised that there is a Small Area Master Plan being worked on for the Bus
            Rapid Transit Corridor, which will have an impact on this particular site. That is important because this new use will generate a
            lot more traffic into this area. The question was asked if that would generate the need for a traffic study and Mr. McGrath said
            that would be the case, however, he was unsure of the time-line for completion of that study. He continued on to say that all
            four sides of the building will be equal architecturally. That this is a small parcel and half the parking will be underground in a
            parking garage (241 spaces underground and 231 spaces above ground). That the proposed landscaping design, would
            emphasize quality rather than quantity. Mr. McGrath concluded by saying that the proposal will come before the Commission
            in March 2020 and construction proposed to commence in August 2020.               7:32 PM
