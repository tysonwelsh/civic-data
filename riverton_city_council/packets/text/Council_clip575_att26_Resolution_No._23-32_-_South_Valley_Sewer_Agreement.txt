                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 23-32

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
     WITH SOUTH VALLEY SEWER DISTRICT FOR THE 2023 SLURRY SEAL
                            PROJECT

      WHEREAS, Riverton City Purchasing bid out the 2023 Slurry Seal Project; and

      WHEREAS, Bid Schedule 3 was for the South Valley Sewer District; and

      WHEREAS, the Sewer District agrees to reimburse the City for Bid Schedule 3; and

       WHEREAS, by approving this agreement, Council will need to approve $220,389.00 as
an expense and $128,827.00 as an incoming revenue in a future budget amendment.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

      1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with South Valley
         Sewer District for the 2023 Slurry Seal Project.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 18th day of
April 2023 by the following vote:
