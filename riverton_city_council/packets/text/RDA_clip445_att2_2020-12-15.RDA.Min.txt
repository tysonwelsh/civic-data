32          RDA Resolution No. 20-07 - Approving and Authorizing the Redevelopment
33          Agency Board Chair to Enter into a Restated Interlocal Agreement with Salt Lake
34          County for the Western Commercial Community Development Project Area
35
36   Chair Staggs explained that the RDA and Salt Lake County desire to approve and enter into an
37   Interlocal Agreement whereby the County, as a taxing entity, consents to the RDA's receipt of
38   certain property tax increment from the Western Commercial Community Development Project
39   Area attributable to the tax levy set by the County.
40
41   Board Member McCay MOVED the RDA Board approve RDA Resolution No. 20-07 -
42   Approving and Authorizing the Redevelopment Agency Board Chair to Enter into a
43   Restated Interlocal Agreement with Salt Lake County for the Western Commercial
44   Community Development Project Area as provided by Staff. Board Member Wells
45   SECONDED the motion. Chair Staggs called for discussion on the motion; there being none he
46   called for a Roll Call Vote and the vote was as follows: Board Member Buroker-Yes, McCay-
47   Yes, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
