CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2443, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING GRANT APPLICATION MATERIALS FOR A SHARED ADULT
FORENSIC INTERVIEWER PROGRAM

County Attorney Brock Belnap explained that a grant opportunity is available to provide
for an adult forensic interviewer program with the goals of minimizing trauma to victims
and requires immediate action in order to qualify to receive funds. The grant would provide
means to hire an employee to the Washington County Attorney’s Office which would
conduct a multidisciplinary team approach to forensic interviews based on trauma-
informed interview techniques. Interviews would be conducted in specified interview
rooms at both the St. George and Hurricane police stations.

Resources would also be extended to the County’s underserved populations, including
members of the Shivwits Band of Paiutes and the population of those living in or emerging
from communities based on polygamist relationships and religious fundamentalism.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution R-2019-2443, A
Resolution Approving Grant Application Materials for a Shared Adult
Forensic Interviewer Program, Subject to Grant Approval. Motion
Seconded by Commissioner Almquist and Carried by a Unanimous
Vote with Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and Almquist voting aye.
