RESULTS OF THE HARMONY HEIGHTS SPECIAL ASSESSMENT AREA PROTEST
PERIOD

Washington County Deputy Attorney Devin Snow explained that several residents within the
Harmony Height subdivision requested a Special Assessment Area for a paving project within
their subdivision. During the protest period, the County received twenty-one valid protests
accounting for forty point four percent (40.4%) of the residents protesting the creation of the
special assessment area. This was over the threshold needed to oppose the project and therefore,
the assessment area will not be created.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1210-O, AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING

AND_AMENDING VARIOUS TITLE 10 ZONING REGULATIONS, CREATING A
PILOT PROGRAM _ FOR _A_TEMPORARY_ HOUSING ACCOMMODATION _ AND

CHANGING VARIOUS PENALTY PROVISIONS

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that there are challenges with
affordable housing within Washington County. In order to combat some of those issues, a pilot
program for a temporary housing accommodations is being examined where family members can
temporarily utilize specified recreational vehicles as a housing accommodation if qualified to do

so.

The one requirement that will be included in the language is as follows: No one may live in an RV
on a lot or parcel without strict compliance with this chapter, including its license requirement.

Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

arrived back at the Incident Command at 1330 hours. The male was taken by private
vehicle to SGRH for treatment.
