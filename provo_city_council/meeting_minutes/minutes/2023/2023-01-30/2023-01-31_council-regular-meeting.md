5.         A resolution approving a dispatch services agreement, appropriating $1,642,862 in the
           newly created Emergency Response Fund, and transferring $676,520 of existing funds to
           accommodate the combining of Orem and Provo dispatch services. (23-009) (31:04)

      Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2023-4, as currently constituted, has been
                   made by council rule.

Wayne Parker, Chief Administrative Officer, shared his excitement for this resolution. He shared this
appropriation would account for six months of the dispatch operation. He added that next year’s budget
would include these funds.

Chair MacKay asked Chief Miguel to discuss how the transition has gone and if the employees are happy.

Chief Miguel shared the experience of the Provo dispatchers working in the Orem facility for several
weeks. He said the feedback that he has received is all positive. He discussed some of the difficulties
they have encountered with technology.

Chair MacKay opened public comment. With none, she closed public comment and brought the
discussion back to council.

John Borget mentioned some of the financial impacts the requested technology would have on the
budget.

Jeremy Headmen, Deputy Fire Chief, explained the technology that they are hoping to obtain going
forward. He said it would allow the dispatchers to use one system for both cities.

Chair MacKay asked what the cost would be for the new software.

Deputy Chief Headman said the technology would be $135,000.

Mr. Borget explained the appropriation includes the amount for the new software. He also explained
that amount would be shared with Orem, and they also plan to use some grant funds.

Mr. Jones shared what the resolution accomplishes based on how it is written. He said it approves the
joint dispatch agreement and the appropriation.

Councilor Hoban asked how it was determined what Orem would be paying.

Mr. Parker responded that it was based on population and calls received.

Chair MacKay called for a vote.

      Vote:     The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                Mackay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.

7.         A resolution appropriating $67,627 in the Economic Development division in the General
           Fund for a sales tax increment post performance payment to East Bay Shopping Center.
           (23-007) (51:29)

      Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2023-6, as currently constituted, has been
                   made by council rule.

Mr. Morey explained that this would be the third payment to the East Bay Shopping Center.

Chair MacKay asked if the payments are transferrable if the property was sold.

Mr. Morey said it depends on the agreement.

Vice-Chair Whipple asked how many more payments this agreement has.

Mr. Morey disclosed that this agreement is for the duration of ten years.

Vice-Chair Whipple asked if this agreement could be extended.

Mr. Morey responded that depending on legislation, creative alternatives might be required.

Chair MacKay called for a vote.

      Vote:     The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                Mackay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.
