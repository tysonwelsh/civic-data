            COMMISSIONER AINGE – ALL PRIVATE PROPERTY IS SUBJECT TO THAT RIGHT BUT IT IS NOT ANYTHING
            THAT EVER BEEN CONSIDERED FOR THE PROPERTY. ALSO, NOTED THAT THIS IS A PUBLIC HEARING -
            NOT REQUIRED TO TAKE ACTION.

            REED DIXON, RESIDENT AND SPOKESPERSON FOR HOA – GAVE DETAILS ON THE PROCESS THEY HAVE
            BEEN TAKING WITH THE RESIDENTS FOR INFORMATION AND INFORMAL/FORMAL VOTES. FINAL VOTE
            WAS 50/11 MAKING IT 88% IN FAVOR. HAVE 75 HOMEOWNERS.

            SHARED EXAMPLES OF PERSONAL PROPERTY BEING TRESPASSED ON (DECK AND BBQ BEING USED,
            FIREWOOD TAKEN OFF OF PROPERTY, STOLEN PROPERTY, TREES CUT DOWN, ETC.)

            ALREADY HAVE THEIR OWN WATER SYSTEM, STREETLIGHTS, TRASH REMOVAL, FIRE SUPPRESSION
            SYSTEM.

            THOSE THAT WERE AGAINST WERE CONCERNED ABOUT THE COST. IN ORDER TO KEEP THE COSTS
            DOWN, REQUESTING TO RECEIVE THE ROAD IN GOOD CONDITION. (HAD PICTURES OF CURRENT ROAD
            CONDITIONS) OR FUNDS TO BE ABLE TO HIRE OUT TO HAVE THE ROADS REPAIRED.

            VAL DUNN, RESIDENT OF SOUTHFORK ROAD AND TREASURER OF HOA – CONCERNED WITH COST AND
            LIABILITY. FEELS THAT A SEPARATE ENTITY SHOULD BE CREATED.

            KATHY WORTHEN, PROPERTY OWNER ON FAIRFAX – CONCERNED WITH LIABILITY.

            RON ASAY, FAIRFAX DRIVE RESIDENT – HAVE HAD TREES CUT OFF THEIR LAWN AND HAS SEEN AN
            INCREASE IN TRAFFIC. VERY MUCH IN FAVOR.

            NELSON DORNY, FAIRFAX DRIVE RESIDENT – AGREED WITH COMMENTS OF RON ASAY.

            REED DIXON – STATED THAT THEY ALREADY HAVE A LIABILITY POLICY FOR THEIR OPERATIONS. HAVE
            TALKED WITH THEIR CURRENT INSURANCE AGENT AND THERE WOULD BE NOT AN INCREASE IN
            PREMIUMS. THE SAME GENERAL LIABILITY POLICY WOULD ALSO COVER A ROAD.

            KATHY WORTHEN - MENTIONED THAT THERE WAS TALK ABOUT THE COUNTY PUTTING A TRAIL
            THROUGH FAIRFAX/VIVIAN PARK.

            COMMISSIONER LEE – THERE WAS AN EXCHANGE OF PROPERTY, FOR SAFETY PURPOSES, AT A TURN
            COMING DOWN SOUTH FORK FOR BIKERS MAKING THAT TURN. FAIRFAX WAS LOOKED AT AS A
            POSSIBILITY FOR THE BIKE TRAIL TO CONTINUE DOWN.

            RICHARD NIELSON, UTAH COUNTY PUBLIC WORKS DIRECTOR – SPOKE ABOUT SOME PROPERTY
            ABOVE THE HOA THAT WAS ACQUIRED TO MAKE A SEPARATED TRAIL AREA OFF OF SOUTH FORK
            ROAD. THE TRAIL GOING UP PROVO CANYON IS CURRENTLY BEING WORKED ON BY UDOT. THIS WILL
            GO FROM VIVIAN PARK UP TO DEER CREEK DAM.

            RON ASAY – FEELS THE SPEED ACQUIRED BY BIKERS COMING DOWN THESE ROADS IS VERY
            DANGEROUS.
