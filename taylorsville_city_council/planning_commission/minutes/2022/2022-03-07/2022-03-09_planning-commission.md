WORK SESSION — 6:00 P.M.
The work session was conducted by Mr. Jim Spung, Senior Planner. The subject of the work session was
Review of Installment 1— Administration & Procedures — Land Development Code.
Mr. Spung went over the agenda for this meeting as being:
1. Project overview.
2. Introduction of Installment 1 (draft)

a. Chapter 13.01: General Provisions.

b. Chapter 13.06: Administration & Procedures.

c. Chapter 13:07: Definitions (partial)

3. What the next steps will be.
4. Discussion: Mr. Spung opened his presentation as follows:

a. Project overview — He explained what the Land Development Code (LDC) is. That the LDC contains
the regulations for how land can be used and developed. He said by updating the LDC it improves
the user experience and increases staff efficiency. Also, that a comprehensive update tailored
specifically for Taylorsville has never been completed and the present version of the Code is dated
2012. When the City incorporated in 1996, the Code used was mostly the same as Salt Lake County
had been using. Then it was updated in 2004 and 2006 and in 2012 it was rewritten totally.

b. Now the goals are to streamline development review procedures and increase predictability of
land use decisions. Consider the needs of the City both now and in the future. Align the LDC with
the General Plan and Utah State Statutes. Enable great design and a diverse mix of land uses. Also,
to improve user-friendliness through logical structure, graphics, tables, and illustrations.

c. He explained the projected timeline of events to accomplish this rewrite. It started in the Fall of
2020 and has completed three steps in the order to prepare the draft LDC. Those being
Administration and Procedures; Zoning Districts and Uses; and Development and Design
Standards. The projected completion date for the consolidated draft is the Summer of 2022 with
the adoption draft completion scheduled for the Fall of 2022.

d. Mr. Spung explained the purpose of tonight’s meeting was to allow Staff to give their presentation
and then let the Commissioners conduct their review of the draft and furnish their comments or
concerns on their own. That there is a fillable form on-line for that purpose and Staff is requesting
inputs by May 1°.

e. Mr. Spung provided an in-depth presentation of items outlined on the agenda and answered
Commissioner’s questions regarding mostly the inclusion of many new ideas to make the process
easier and faster.

ADJOURNMENT: By motion of Commissioner Quigley and second by Commissioner Wilkey, the meeting was
adjourned at 7:00 p.m.
