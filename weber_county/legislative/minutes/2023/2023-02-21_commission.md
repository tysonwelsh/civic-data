APPROVAL OF A FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 42-4-2-1 OF THE
WEBER COUNTY CODE REGARDING PARKING ON COUNTY ROADS BETWEEN THE HOURS
OF 12:00 A.M. AND 7:00 A.M..

Joe Hadley (Roads Department): This request is to amend the Ordinance for parking amending the times for
enforcement. There has been a huge increase in parking along the roads this winter leading to problems
when snow plowing. This change falls into conformity with several other counties and cities.

Commissioner Harvey moved for approval of a First Reading of an Ordinance amending Section 42-4-2-1 of
the Weber County Code regarding parking on county roads between the hours of 12:00 a.m. and 7:00 a.m.;
Commissioner Bolos seconded.

APPROVE RESOLUTION 18-2023 AUTHORIZING THE USE OF FUNDS FOR PROJECTS ON
THE PRIORITY LIST APPROVED BY WACOG.

Brooke Stewart (Community Development): With this project, the County intends to widen and improve
2550 South from 3500 West to 4300 West. This project was recommended for funding by the Weber Area
Council of Governments (WACOG) and approved by the County Commission as part of the 2022
Transportation Priority list. With this resolution, the County Commission is authoring the use of funds that
were approved by WACOG, and resolves that those funds will be expended for the project pursuant to Local
Transportation Funds program policies and in accordance with the Utah State statute for the Sales Tax Funds.
The Sales Tax Funds for this project are programmed for 2025.

‘Commissioner Bolos moved to approve Resolution 18-2023 authorizing the use of funds for projects on the
priority list approved by WACOG; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Harvey — aye; Chair Jenkins — aye

6. APPROVE LOCAL TRANSPORTATION FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH PLEASANT VIEW
CITY TO ESTABLISH TERMS RELATED TO THE PLEASANT VIEW CITY 2727 & 2775 NORTH

CONNECTION PROJECT.

Brooke Stewart (Community Development): This project is a collaborative effort between Pleasant View
City and Farr West City, where they will be constructing roadway along 2725 & 2775 north connecting into
and improving 1900 West. The completion of this project will create safer access to 2700 North and provide
connectivity to the commercial and industrial developments that are in the area. The project was
recommended for funding by the Weber Area Council of Governments (WACOG) and approved by the
County Commission as part of the 2022 Transportation Priority list. This agreement outlines the terms
related to Sales Tax Funds that are programmed for calendar year 2025,

Commissioner Bolos moved to approve a Local Transportation Funding Agreement with Pleasant View City
to establish terms related to the Pleasant View City 2727 & 2775 North Connection Project; Commissioner

Harvey seconded.
Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Harvey — aye; Chair Jenkins — aye

7. APPROVAL OF A FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE “AIR POLLUTION
CONTROL FEE” AND MAKING OTHER TECHNICAL CHANGES (SEC. 28-1-3 POWERS AND

DUTIES).

Brian Cowan (Director, Weber-Morgan Health Department): There are two components to this Ordinance
revision. One is to increase the air pollution control fee from $1 to $2. The state statute requires $1
increments. The second part is housekeeping with text changes such as removing Paragraph C and revising
the name from ‘Air Pollution Control Fee’ to ‘Emissions Compliance Fee’.

Commissioner Harvey moved for approval of a First Reading of an Ordinance amending the “air pollution
control fee” and making other technical changes (Sec. 28-1-3 Powers and Duties); Commissioner Bolos

seconded.
Commissioner Harvey — aye; Commissioner Bolos — aye; Chair Froerer — aye
