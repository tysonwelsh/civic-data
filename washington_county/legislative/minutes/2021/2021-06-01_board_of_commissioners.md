CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2777, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_INTERLOCAL _AGREEMENT_BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND
MUNICIPALITIES TO SUPPORT TRANSIT SERVICES FROM ST. GEORGE TO
SPRINGDALE

Commissioner Cox explained that the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) was awarded
funding in the amount of $15,000,000 to use towards the establishment of a new St. George to
Springdale transit line contingent upon local support, governance, and long-term funding
commitments. The project will help to alleviate heavy congestion experienced as a result of
elevated tourism traffic from St. George to Springdale.

The County and the City of St. George entered into an agreement where the County pledged, for a
period of ten (10) years, to pay up to the amount Washington County collected of the 40% local
option sales tax towards the expenses of the transit line.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2780, A RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT
OF STATE FIRE RESTRICTION ORDER

Fire Warden Adam Heyder explained that Washington County is experiencing an exceptional
drought which has caused dry conditions and extremely dry vegetation. As a result the State
Forester in consultation with the County, has determined that measures must be taken to prevent
the ignition of forest and rangeland fires. Pursuant to the Fire Restriction Order the following acts
are prohibited in all unincorporated private and all state lands within Washington County:

1. No open fires of any kind except within established public facilities in improved
campgrounds, picnic areas. -OR- In permanently constructed fire pits at private homes
where running water is present.

2. Smoking, except within an enclosed vehicle, trailer or building, a developed recreation
site or while stopped in an area that is paved or free from dry vegetation.

3. Discharging or using any fireworks, tracer ammunition or other pyrotechnic devices
including exploding targets.

4. Cutting, welding or grinding metal in areas of dry vegetation.

5. Operating a motorcycle, chainsaw, ATV, or other small internal combustion engine
without an approved and working spark arrestor.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Resolution No. R-2021-2780, A Resolution in
Support of State Fire Restriction Order. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox
and Iverson voting aye.
