                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 26-22

RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A FRANCHISE AGREEMENT
                     WITH EMERY TELCOM

       WHEREAS, Emery Telcom desires to install, maintain, operate and/or control an
telecommunications infrastructure network in Riverton City’s (“City”) owned right of ways to
provide telecommunications service to City residents; and
        WHEREAS, it is both reasonable and necessary for City to allow the installation of Emery
Telcom’s infrastructure network in the City owned public right of ways within the City and
thereafter to maintain, operate and/or control said network; and
       WHEREAS, the City finds and determines that the public interest in health, safety and
welfare will be enhanced by the provision of the proposed service; and
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
   1. The City Council concludes that entering into the above-described agreement with Emery
      Telcom will enhance the public health, safety and welfare of the public, and authorizes the
      City to enter into and sign the same.
   2. The Mayor is authorized to execute a Franchise Agreement with Emery Telcom in
      substantially similar form to the Agreement attached as Exhibit A.
   3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 5th day of
May 2026 by the following vote:
