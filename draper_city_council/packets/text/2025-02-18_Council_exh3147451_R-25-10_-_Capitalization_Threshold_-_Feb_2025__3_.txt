                               RESOLUTION NO. 25-10

        A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE CAPITALIZATION THRESHOLD FOR
                             DRAPER CITY.

      WHEREAS, the City of Draper is required to capitalize tangible personal
property, land, capital improvements, and buildings; and

       WHEREAS, the previous valuation threshold for capital assets, set at an initial
individual cost of more than $5,000 and an estimated useful life of more than one
year, were established in 1999; and

      WHEREAS, the City desires to update the capitalization standards to reflect
current financial practices and guidelines; and

      WHEREAS, the City has conducted an analysis of the required capitalization
standards in accordance with the FY24 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report;
and

      WHEREAS, the proposed capitalization thresholds for various asset types have
been increased in 2025 as follows:

              WATER TANKS:                                      $50,000
              INFRASTRUCURE:                                    $20,000
              BUILDINGS AND IMPROVEMENTS:                       $20,000
              IMPROVEMENTS OTHER THAN BUILDINGS:                $10,000
              MACHINERY AND EQUIPMENT:                          $10,000
              VEHICLES:                                         $20,000
              FURNITURE & EQUIPMENT:                            $10,000
              OFFICE EQUIPMENT:                                 $10,000; and

      WHEREAS, the proposed standards comply with state law and generally
accepted accounting principles (GAAP): and

       WHEREAS, the City Council hereby finds that this action is in the best interest
of the public’s health, safety and general welfare.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:



       Section 1.      Approval.   The City Council hereby adopts the Capitalization
Resolution No. 25-10
Date 02/18/2025                    1    Adopting the Capitalization Thresholds Schedule
Standards of Draper City, including the capitalization thresholds as detailed above,
as set forth in Exhibit “A,” which is attached hereto and incorporated herein by
reference.

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held to be invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions shall
remain severable.

       Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon
its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 18TH DAY OF FEBRUARY 2025.
