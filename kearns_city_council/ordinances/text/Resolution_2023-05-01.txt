    A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL,
  GRANTING CONSENT TO THE COUNTY OF SALT LAKE TO FORM THE
 SALT LAKE COUNTY CONVENTION AND TOURISM ASSESSMENT AREA

                                       RECITALS

       WHEREAS, the Convention and Tourism Business Assessment Area, Act, Utah
Code Title 11, Chapter 42b, (the “Act”), Section 11-42b-103 authorizes the County to form
a convention and tourism assessment area for the purposes of promoting tourism; and

       WHEREAS, the County Council of the County of Salt Lake has requested consent
to form the Salt Lake County Convention and Tourism Assessment Area (the “SLCo
CTAA”) in Kearns Metro Township; and

        WHERAS, Kearns desires to benefit from the programs offered by a future SLCo
CTAA, particularly in promoting its assets and making investments to capture spending
dollars from visitors to the Olympic Oval, Oquirrh Park, and other Kearns amenities and
destinations.

                                     RESOLUTION

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY KEARNS METRO
TOWNSHIP COUNCIL that:

       Section 1: The above recitals are true and correct.

       Section 2: The County Council of the County of Salt Lake is hereby granted
consent to include Kearns Metro Township in the SLCo CTAA, for the formation of the
SLCo CTAA and future renewals.

        Section 3: The Kearns Metro Township Clerk is hereby directed to transmit a
certified copy of this Resolution to the Clerk of the Salt Lake County Council.

       Section 4: This Resolution is effective immediately upon its adoption.
