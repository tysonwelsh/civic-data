BUSINESS MEETING
Meeting began at – 8:35 a.m.
1) Other Business Items. (As Needed)
Commissioner Harpst provided thank you and participation for the commissioners and staff. Obtained
unified fire contact and the fire station has our 2025 planning commission meeting dates. He spoke to the
fire station access card person and confirmed who would have access to the building.
Commissioner Geroux asked for an update on applicant Ryan Lieke. Mr. Nakamura advised he has scaled
down the original proposal and will send the commissioners the plan. It is still in conceptual review due to
planned access isssues.
Commissioner Geroux asked for an update on Evan Glassman. Mr. Nakamura said staff have received
inquiries, as he has been trying to sell the property, but nothing has materialized. Glassman is working with
grading. Is there a risk of property sliding onto the road and contaminating water and access. Mr.
Nakamura will follow up and report back in March.

Commissioner Harpst inquired about house construction near Camp K. Doesn’t know if the building needs
to be tied in or have their own driveway and access or share with the other two. Mr. Nakamura said they
only wanted single access for the two lots.
Commissioner Geroux inquired about the garage being built and why it doesn’t follow current design
standards. It’s a plain box. Mr. Nakamura said design standards are loose in interpretation. Doesn’t think
a box is really in the meeting design standards as FCOZ is written and room for interpretation. Mr.
Nakamura suggested looking at ordinance and will figure out how to tighten up the language. Hard to
enforce how they’re written.
Mr. Starley confirmed we follow the 2006 Utah WUI code and 2021 IFC. Mr. Starley will reach out to UFA
regarding road width, length and load weight.
2) Approval of the January 9, 2025 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes. (Motion/Voting)
Motion: To approve January 9, 2025 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes with amendments.
Motion by: Commissioner Wallace
2nd by: Commissioner Berreth
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimously in favor
3) MSD Planning staff will continue the Title 19 Code Update Projects. Specific issues discussed will
be ecological protections and the Design Standards Checklist. Planner(s): Matt Starley and Brian
Tucker (Discussion)
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding 14.12 definitions and standards for roadways
and driveways consistency in setting forth requirements. Discussed vehicle tons and travel surface, and
minimum width. Mr. Starley will ask Shawn Peterson, UFA to attend the March 13th meeting. Remove word
“sidewalk” throughout the code, since there are not any sidewalks.
Discussed 19.38 trails and a master plan, and maintenance. Defer density bonus for trail development until
after the trails master plan completion. Trails approval body would be the director or designee. Limits of
disturbance, square footage, purpose statement, standards, and setbacks.
Discussed bonding, Mr. Starley will follow up with SWPPP and Alex.
Commissioner Geroux motioned to adjourn, Commissioner Karkut seconded that motion.
