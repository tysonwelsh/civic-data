Comments:

This application is a request for approval of an Amended Subdivision Plat for
approximately 0.86 acres located on the east side of Springdale Way, at approximately
13229 S Springdale Way. The property is currently zoned RA2 (Residential Agricultural).
The applicant is requesting that an Amended Subdivision Plat be approved to allow for a
reconfiguration of the property line between Lot 44 and Parcel D within the Steeplechase
Phase 2 Subdivision, inclusive of relocating associated easements, HOA open space, and
extending the buildable area for the currently platted Lot 44.



Findings for Approval:

  1. There is good cause for the vacation or amendment, and no public street or
     municipal utility easement has been vacated or amended.

Finding for Denial:

  1. Good cause does not exist for the vacation or amendment as proposed.
