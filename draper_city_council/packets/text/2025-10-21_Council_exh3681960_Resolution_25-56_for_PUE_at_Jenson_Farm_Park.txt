                                 RESOLUTION 25-56

          A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO GRANT A PUBLIC
       UTILITY EASEMENT ON REAL PROPERTY OWNED BY THE CITY FOR THE
                            JENSON FARM PARK

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has approved the development of the
Jenson Farm Park; and

      WHEREAS, the Real Property owned by Draper City for the Jenson Farm Park
does not presently have recorded Public Utility Easements; and

      WHEREAS, there is a present need for private utility installation on the City’s
Real Property to install, extend, lengthen, and support utility demand for the park
development; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City finds it beneficial to create Public Utility Easements
as described in the attachment for the use of said utilities.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Adoption. The City Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to
execute Public Utility Easements creation documents, as identified in Exhibit
A, and direct staff to record the easements at the Salt Lake County Recorder.

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.
