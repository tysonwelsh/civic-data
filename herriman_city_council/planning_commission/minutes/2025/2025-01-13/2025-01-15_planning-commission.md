The following are the minutes of the Herriman Planning Commission meeting held on Wednesday,
January 15, 2025, at 6:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main
Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City
Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Commission, media, and interested
citizens.

6:00 PM WORK MEETING (Fort Herriman Conference Room)

1.   Commission Business

     1.1. Review of City Council Decisions – Clint Spencer, Planning Manager
Planning Manager Spencer noted the City Council reappointed Terrah Anderson and Preston Oberg
as Planning Commission Alternates for another year. They also adopted the policy and procedure
for Planning Commission. The Teton Ranch Master Development Agreement (MDA) was approved.
     1.2. Review of Agenda Items – Planning Staff

Planner Hoadley presented the first four agenda items. She reminded the commission that for item
4.1, when opening the public hearing, they should remind attendees that comments should be for the
subdivision only. She noted that item 4.2 is for conditional use and does not require a public hearing,
but residents may want to speak about it.

Director of Public Works Edwards provided details about the fire station project, including
addressing concerns about fencing, landscaping, lighting, and setbacks. He mentioned they had
submitted a waiver of strict compliance for trees and were considering adding a monument sign in
the future.

The architect, Heber Slabbert from AJC Architects, presented color boards and explained the design
choices for the fire station. The building will feature dark brick, silver metals, and copper-like
panels. The design includes translucent panels for natural light in the apparatus bay and red glass
fire doors.

Commission members discussed the circulation of fire trucks, parking arrangements, and the overall
layout of the fire station. They also addressed concerns about noise and the station's impact on the
neighborhood.

Planning Manager Spencer noted item 4.5 is a road dedication required to be done by plat. It was
noted that this was primarily a procedural issue.
   1.3. Presentation and discussion of Land Use Development regulations, standards, policies, and
        procedures for Herriman City Planning & Zoning – Clint Spencer, Planning Manager


A training video was shown about parking minimums. A discussion followed involving what
Herriman’s future parking space could look like.

   4.1. Review and consider approval of the Fire Station Preliminary Subdivision Plat located
        approximately at 5616 W 12900 South in the A-1-43 Agricultural Zone. (Public Hearing)
        Applicant: Justun Edwards, Herriman City (authorized agent)
        Acres: ±20.34
        File No: S2024-106

        https://herrimancity-meeting-files-pc.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/1-15-

Chair Powell noted items 4.1 and 4.2 will be presented together with separate public hearing and
motions after.
Planner Hoadley presented the preliminary subdivision plat for Fire Station 103. She showed the
location of the approximately 20-acre parcel, with just over 1.5 acres being designated for the fire
station. The southern 30 feet of the parcel would be dedicated as roadway to the city.
Director of Public Works Edwards addressed the commission. He noted that they had tried to
address concerns from area residents by installing an 8-foot precast wall, increasing plantings
around existing residential areas, and adding enclosures around the dumpster and generator to
mitigate sound. They also planned to use city standard lights for the parking lot lighting on the
building to reduce light pollution.

Chair Powell opened the public hearing.

No comments were offered.

Commissioner Jacobson moved to close the public hearing, Seconded by Commissioner Fenn and all
voted Aye.

Commissioner Jacobson moved to approve item 4.1 Review and consider approval of the Fire
Station Preliminary Subdivision Plat located approximately at 5616 W 12900 South in the A-1-43
Agricultural Zone with staff’s recommendations: 1. The applicant continues working with staff
to resolve all conditions before final plat approval. 2. The applicant provides updated and
accurate information on lot sizes. 3. The Commission approves the requested Waiver of Strict
Compliance for street tree placement.
Commissioner Rypien seconded the motion.

    4.2. Review and consider approval of a Conditional Use Permit to construct Fire Station 103
         located at 5616 W 12900 South in the A-1-43 Agricultural Zone.
         Applicant: Justun Edwards, Herriman City (authorized agent)
         Acres: ±1.54
         File No: C2024-107

        https://herrimancity-meeting-files-pc.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/1-15-
        25/Item+4.2+C2024-107+Staff+Report.pdf
Planner Hoadley presented the conditional use permit application for Fire Station 103, providing a
detailed explanation of the proposed mitigation strategies aimed at minimizing the impact on the
surrounding area. She highlighted that the fire station's design includes trucks exiting with lights but
no sirens to minimize disruption to nearby residents. In addition, the station plans to incorporate
garage doors that open horizontally and silently, a feature that significantly reduces noise pollution
compared to traditional vertical, metal doors.
Commissioner Rypien moved to approve item 4.2 Review and consider approval of a Conditional
Use Permit to construct Fire Station 103 located at 5616 W 12900 South in the A-1-43
Agricultural Zone with staff’s recommendations: 1. Applicant shall acknowledge receipt of and
agree with all recommendations from City. Ex. Bike rack. 2. Applicant shall comply with City
Code 10-12-6.C.15.b regarding exterior metal material on the building. 3. Applicant shall provide
directional and shielded lighting to prevent light trespass onto residential property. 4.
Applicant shall work with City staff on the design and materials for the dumpster enclosure,
masonry wall, accessory structure, and generator enclosure. All finishing materials shall
match the main structure. 5. Applicant shall comply with the proposed terms of the Waiver of
Strict Compliance regarding park strip trees.
Commissioner Jacobson seconded the motion.

    4.4. Review and consider approval of a Conditional Use Permit to construct the Ridge View
         Church located at 14069 S Greenford Lane in the R-2-10 Residential Zone.
         Applicant: Chad Spencer (authorized agent)
         Acres: ±4.40
         File No: C2024-096

        https://herrimancity-meeting-files-pc.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/1-15-
        25/Item+4.4+C2024-096+Staff+Report.pdf
Planner Hoadley presented the conditional use permit application for the Ridge View Church. She
noted that the 32-foot-tall structure fits within the residential area height limits, with an exception
for the steeple. The parking meets requirements, and there is a walkway connecting to the
neighborhood. The landscape plan shows extensive planting, with larger grassy areas for activities.
Hoadley elaborated that the church, located in a multifamily residential zone, is well integrated into
the community with an elementary school across the road and residential homes surrounding the
area.
The designed connection point into the neighborhood provides easy access for residents, enhancing
community interaction. The planners and architects worked diligently to ensure that all
specifications, including the slopes for walkways, were compliant and user-friendly. The proposed
elevations highlighted different sides of the church, detailing the balance between aesthetic appeal
and functionality. Additional features include areas designated for outdoor activities, supported by
the landscape plan's well-distributed greenery and grassiness around the facility.
Commissioner Fenn moved to approve item 4.4 Review and consider approval of a Conditional
Use Permit to construct the Ridge View Church located at 14069 S Greenford Lane in the R-2-10
Residential Zone with staff’s recommendations: 1. The applicant shall address all recommended
conditions of approval made by the City and other agencies. 2. The applicant shall provide floor
plans to the City to verify compliance with adopted parking regulations and make necessary
adjustments to the final site or building plan before issuance of a building permit. 3. Where the
pedestrian path traverses the parking lot, the applicant shall comply with Herriman City Code
10-12-6(C)(3) and incorporate design elements to clearly identify the walking path and enhance
pedestrian safety. 4. The applicant shall coordinate the installation of the new perimeter fence
with adjacent property owners and avoid double fencing, per Herriman City Code 10-21-11.
Commissioner Jacobson seconded the motion.
