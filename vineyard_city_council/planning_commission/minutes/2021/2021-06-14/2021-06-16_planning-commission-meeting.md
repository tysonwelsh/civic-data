    4.2 Discussion and Action – 4Life Building Expansion Site Plan and Preliminary Plat

          Planner II Briam Amaya Perez introduced the applicant and project details. 4Life
    currently has a building on 1600 N. This site plan and preliminary plat would allow them to
    combine lots with the empty adjacent lot to build a new structure. The proposed building
    fulfills all of the Planning Departments requirements and staff recommended approval.

6. COMMISSION MEMBERS’ REPORTS AND EX PARTE DISCUSSION
     DISCLOSURE
            Mr. Brim updated the Commission on the staff process of selecting a consulting firm
      to redesign the waterfront area. There will be a vote in City Council on June 23, 2021 to
      confirm the staff’s recommendation. In two weeks, there will be an update about the active
      transportation plan.

      Vice-Chair Sullivan asked staff about the missing connection in the bike path near Sunset
      Beach. Mr. Brim asked if City Engineer Naseem Ghandour could have an update regarding
      that in the coming weeks.

      Commissioner Blackburn thanked the Commission for reading a letter he wrote regarding
      the Clegg Farm Development in the previous City council Meeting.
