                      A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY COUNCIL
                APPROVING THE CONTINUATION OF COUNCIL MEMBER
                  APPOINTMENTS TO SERVE ON LOCAL DISTRICTS AND
                       BOARDS THROUGH THE COUNCIL MEMBER’S

                                         ELECTION TERM


                                                  RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
       10-2a-401 et seq.
   B. White City has the right and legal responsibility to appoint a council member to local districts
       and boards to represent the residents of White City;
   C. By duly authorized resolutions, the White City Council, the legislative body of White City
       elected by the voters of White City, approved the following appointments:
            •   Mayor Paulina Flint – Greater Salt Lake Municipal Service District Board of Trustees
            •   Council Member Kay Dickerson – Mayor Pro-Tempore and Alternate for the Greater
                Salt Lake Municipal Service District Board of Trustees
            •   Council Member Allan Perry – Board of Trustees of the Unified Fire Authority and
                Unified Fire Service Area
            •   Council Member Kay Dickerson – Board of Trustees of the Wasatch Front Waste and
                Recycling District
            •   Council Member Linda Price – Board of Trustees of the South Salt Lake Valley
                Mosquito Abatement District
            •   Mayor Paulina Flint – Board of the Salt Lake County Council of Governments
            •   Mayor Paulina Flint – Board of Trustees of the Unified Police Department and Salt
                Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area
            •   Council Member D. Scott Little – Advisory Board for Salt Lake County Animal
                Services
   D. On this date, the White City Metro Council desires to continue the appointments to various
       local districts and boards through the election term of the appointed Council Member.
                                         RESOLUTION


       THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the
Council approves the continuation of the local district and board appointments through the
election term of the appointed Council Member, for the benefit of White City.

   APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this 7th day of January 2021.

                                                          PAULINA F. FLINT, MAYOR
