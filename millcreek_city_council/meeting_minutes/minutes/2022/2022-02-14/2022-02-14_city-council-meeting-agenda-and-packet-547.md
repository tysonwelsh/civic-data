Francis      Lilly      reported        that he had met with               each of the council                members.          He said every 10 years
the city needed to account                          for population       changes       following         the U.S. Census,            so council          districts
stayed roughly             proportionate.              The council        needed       to adopt a redistricting                 map by May 16, 2022.
He wanted            to solicit       feedback          from    the Council       before      taking       a map to the community                      for input.
Redistricting           is done using               U.S. Census       data, which         was not released              until    September             30, 2021,
due to the COVID-19                    pandemic.            Utah Code 10-3-205.5                  requires       the City       Council       to make
boundary        adjustments             where         necessary       to maintain         substantially          equal populations,             within        six

months       after the Legislature                    completes      its redistricting        process.         Utah     enacted      new congressional
districts     011 November              12, 2021. Gov. Spencer                  Cox signed             new state legislative               districts     for both
chambers        into law on November                       16, 2021.

Brian        Busch       said Millcreek           had grown            approximately            two percent           (1,241     people)          from       62,139
people        in 2010 to 63,380               in 2020.       Lilly     reviewed         Millcreek        Code 2.11.040              which         says:
        1.     Council       members            shall each be elected               to represent         a district     of the city.
        2.     The city is divided               into four (4) districts               of substantially         equal population,                 as shown          on
               the map and district               boundary           descriptions          on file in the city,         and which              are designated
               Districts       1, 2, 3, and 4.
        3.     Within       six months          after the Utah Legislature                    completes        its redistricting              process,       the
               council       shall make any adjustments                        in the boundaries            of the districts            aS    may be required
               to maintain         districts      of substantially             equal population.
        4.     Each elected           officer     of the city shall maintain                   a principal      place of residence                   within        the
               city during        the officer's          terin     of office      as required         by Utah         Code Ann.              §10-3-301.
        5.     A vacancy          shall occur if a Council                 member           shall die, resign,          terminate            legal domicile
               within      the corporate          limits     of the City         or be judicially           removed          from       office.


Busch         said staff tried to follow                 these standards            with      the redistricting         map proposals:
        *      The purpose           of redistricting            is to create an equal distribution                     of population               within     each
               council       district.
        *      When        drawing        districts,      the official       population          numbers        from       the 2020 census must be
               used.
        *      Districts       must be contiguous                 and reasonably              compact.
        *      Districts       must follow         natural        boundaries           such as streets and rivers.
        *      Districts       must be drawn              to create four districts.
        *      Districts       must be as nearly             equal as practical,               with   a deviation          no greater          than +/- 4.0
               percent       (percentage          from      other municipalities).
        *      Current       elected       officials       stay located         in their      designated       district.
Lilly        said the Utah Independent                    Redistricting          Commission            says that districts              should       be
contiguous,             be reasonably           compact,          preserve       communities           of interest,        follow        natural,        geographic,
or man-made                features,      boundaries,            or barriers,      preserve       cores of prior           districts,         and
prohibit        purposeful           or undue      favoring          of incumbents,             candidates,       or parties.


Busch         then reviewed              the existing       council      district       boundary         maps. Most          of the population                 growth
