2. Joint Work Session with the Planning Commission

    2.a. General Plan Update Discussion
    Lorin Powell, City Engineer, explained the history behind the current General Plan Land Use
    Update. He noted that the Council had indicated that they would prefer to see the density
    shifted to the TOD areas rather than spread out across the city. They also needed to conduct
    more analysis and research as to how ADU’s will affect the density.

    Kim Struthers, City Planner, stated that his staff tried to find some data that shows how much
    ADU’s are likely to be utilized. Some of their research found 8% of household are likely to
    have an ADU, but it’s could be higher than that. He doesn’t believe that the city would ever
    reach 20% utilization.

    Mr. Struthers also noted the areas that they changed. He said they removed density from Main
    Street and other non-TOD areas. They also increased the density in the Mill Pond area.
    The Planning Commission and Council discussed the ADU percentage. Most agreed that they
    would prefer that the city plan for a 50% utilization of ADUs.

    There was discussion about the proposal to increase density in Thanksgiving Point. The
    Planning Commission and Council discussed their concerns with the proposal and also shared
    ideas of how it could be beneficial to the city and its residents. There was discussion about
    implementing a mechanism or trigger and if it was reached, then the Council may allow for
    more density.

3. City Council Work Session

   Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
