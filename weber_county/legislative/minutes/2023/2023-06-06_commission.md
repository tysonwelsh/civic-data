    3. Public hearing and second reading for consideration of a county initiated text amendment to the Subdivision
       Ordinance to exempt lot adjustments from preliminary and final subdivision review, and to amend the fee
       schedule for the land use code application fees. (ZTA 2022-02)

         Steve Burton (Planning): This is second reading, required due to fees being involved. This Ordinance
         addresses lot line adjustments. Both Planning commissions last fall held public hearings and forwarded
         positive recommendations to you. Current code requires a subdivision plat amendment to address lot line
         changes; this drives reviews by Planning, Engineering department, and the Surveyors office. This could take
         several months and includes scheduling a public meeting. Proposed is a new section in the ordinance
         removing public meeting requirement for lot line adjustments within the same subdivision or if an owner
         wants to remove a restriction no longer applicable. The fees would be half of what is currently charged with
         the exception of the Surveyor’s office because they review the same amount of technical information the
         same for any plat. Current size fee charges are $30 plus 1000 square feet of building area and $30 plus 1000
         square feet of site area being modified. This seems a little high to projects like parking lots and warehouse
         that cover large areas but do not take a long time for review. Our proposed size fee change to our design
         review and conditional limited use permit applications will be $30 per $100K of project valuation.

    4. Public hearing for consideration on a request to vacate by ordinance the public utility easement within lot 29
       of the Silver Bell Estates Subdivision No. 1. (VAC 2023-01)

         Tammy Aydelotte (Planning): This lot is in a subdivision recorded in 1961. For some reason every lot in
         this subdivision has a utility easement running smack in the middle. The owner is seeking to vacate the 10
         foot wide easement that runs right down the middle of all properties in that subdivision. No objections from
