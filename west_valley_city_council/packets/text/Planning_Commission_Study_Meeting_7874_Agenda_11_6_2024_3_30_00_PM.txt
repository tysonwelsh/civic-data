SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SMI-4-2024   Alex Dahlstrom with Wasatch Commercial is requesting minor subdivision approval
             for the MIT Tech Park Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 24.5
             acres. The subdivision is located at 4334 West Lake Park Boulevard and is zoned
             B/RP. (Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

SA-3-2024         David Hamilton is requesting a plat amendment to lot 5 of the Westridge Commerce
                  Park Phase 2. The plat will amend and extend lot 5 to resolve adjacent boundary
                  issues with City property to the west and south. The plat will consist of 1 lot and 2
                  parcels. The property is located at 4700 South 5600 West and is zoned
                  Manufacturing. (Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

PUD-2-2024        Grey Garza is requesting final approval for the Sage Valley Townhomes PUD. This
                  application will amend lot 1 of the Sage Valley Subdivision and modify the
                  townhomes from rental units to owner-occupied units. The subdivision will consist of
                  18 units on 1.54 acres. The property is located at 1854 West 4100 South and is zoned
                  RM. (Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)
