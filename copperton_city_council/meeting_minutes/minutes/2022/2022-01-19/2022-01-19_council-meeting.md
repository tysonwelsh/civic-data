Annexation Plan

                 Council Member Stitzer stated she has been meeting with Nathan Bracken and
working with Kayla Mauldin, Planner, MSD, and Melissa Anderson, Planner, MSD, to develop a
policy plan and showed them the conceptual maps listing the priority areas, thoughts, and ideas
for Copperton. She also met with Rio Tinto, and they gave some very helpful feedback. They
said Copperton should ask for more land. They were happy with how the maps look together. It
will be a couple months before Copperton can adopt the map due to noticing requirements. Rio
Tinto is willing to put something in writing once the Council has adopted the plan. The current
draft is coming along nicely, and Ms. Mauldin is making good progress.

               Nathan Bracken stated this is one of the best annexation policy plans he has ever
seen. Approval will require an initial meeting with fourteen days’ notice, ten days of public
comments, a public hearing with fourteen days’ notice, and a second public hearing with fourteen
days’ notice. Ms. Mauldin will try to get what she can done.

               Council Member Stitzer stated there is more information she wants to include
and is hoping to have the draft ready for review by the end of the month.

West Bench Plan

               Mayor Clayton stated he read a letter in the Planning Commission meeting and
sent copies to the Salt Lake County Council and each planning commissioner. There is a lot of
opposition. The Planning Commission instructed the Salt Lake County Office of Regional
Development to go back and address the concerns stated in the letter.

                Council Member Stitzer stated she spoke with Rio Tinto earlier and they provided
a written statement in opposition to the West General Plan. There were parts they liked, but
ultimately, they oppose it and support Copperton.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
ARPA Update

                Mayor Clayton stated Copperton has only spent $168 of its allocated ARPA funds.
Another allocation is coming this summer and will be approximately $30,000. They will have until
2024 to use it. The final rule has been established on what the funds can be used for. He believes
the best use of the funds would be for stormwater.

Committee/Board Updates

    Legislative Research Committee

            Mayor Clayton stated the legislature started yesterday. There is a lot of talk about
mask mandates. He asked Mr. Bracken if there is anything they need to pay attention to.

                Nathan Bracken stated it is still early, but he has heard of some land use bills that
may be of interest. It gets moving quickly and he will do his best to keep the Council updated.
The rules dictate that Mayor Clayton is authorized to direct him if a decision needs to be made
before there is time to hold a meeting.

    Bingham Cemetery Board

               Nothing to report.

    Copperton Community Council

                Council Member Stitzer stated Community Council met tonight and approved one
of their budgets for activities. The administrative budget will be discussed next month. There is
a lot of mud on the walking path west of the barricades and this makes walking and hiking difficult.
She reached out to Rio Tinto and they will work on cleaning that up. They are planning to reopen
the visitor experience this spring.

Copperton Metro Township Council Member Reports

    Council Member Clayton

       Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)

               Mayor Clayton stated the Municipal Services District has been heavy in a few
items, mainly the bond. Marla Howard, General Manager, MSD, is doing a great job in her new
position. The job description for the MSD Chief Financial Officer position will be posted and the
Board will begin interviewing candidates.

    Council Member Olsen

       Unified Police Department (UPD)
       Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

                 Council Member Olsen reported on UPD budget amendments. The SLVLESA
meeting in December sought comments on the tax rate cape and truth in taxation. They voted in
favor of keeping the tax rate cap and Frank Pignanelli, Lobbyist, will put it on the back burner until
later this year.

    Council Member Bailey

       Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
       Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                 Council Member Bailey stated FEMA will not approve the construction of a fire
station in Millcreek City because the area is designated as a floodplain. UFA is working with FEMA
to re-evaluate the area. If the station is not built that portion of the bond will either be put into
escrow or sold. Chief Dan Peterson is retiring in March and there are three internal candidates
that are being reviewed as his replacement.
