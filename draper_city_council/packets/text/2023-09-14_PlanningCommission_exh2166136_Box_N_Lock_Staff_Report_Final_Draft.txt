BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for a new self-storage facility to be built
on approximately 3.88 acres. This property is located at approximately 15037 S. Minuteman
Drive (Exhibit B & C). The property is currently zoned M1.

The subject property is currently vacant. The applicant has proposed a new self-storage facility.

This application was made prior to the adoption of revised development standards relative to the
building design in ordinance. Deviations can be granted by the Planning commission under the
provisions in effect at the time the application was made. The planning commission would need
to find that the deviation is consistent with the purpose and intent of the policies and
development standards pertaining to the applicable zoning district or land use category, that the
variations are consistent with the purpose of DCMC Chapter 9-22 and any applicable master
plan or ordinance, and that at least two additional criteria from the criteria previously specified in
DCMC Subsection 9-22-030(B) are met. Staff can verify that the use meets the intent of the M1
zone as well as the CC Land use category of the general plan, and that the proposal does
comply with the criteria that the amount of primary materials used for a façade is at least ten
percent (10%) greater than the minimum required amount and that the buildings are designed in
a specific architectural styles which utilizes façade materials differently than prescribed in
subsection 9-22-040(F) and are designed by a licensed architect. Approval as proposed would
necessitate approval of the findings listed in the staff report for these variations to the building
materials and design.

Criteria For Approval.
Site Plan: The criteria for review and potential approval of a Site Plan request is found in
Sections 9-5-090(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such requests
as:

         E. Standards For Approval: The following standards shall apply to the approval of a site
             plan:
            1. The entire site shall be developed at one time unless a phased development plan
               is approved.
            2. A site plan shall conform to applicable standards set forth in this title, including but
               not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and
               building materials.
            3. The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the
               general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
            4. The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject
               development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
            5. The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in
               Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm water drainage,
               and utilities concerns.

For applications approved with significant conditions necessary for compliance with ordinance
DCMC Subsection 9-5-090(D)(5) also applies:

         “After the planning commission makes a decision, the zoning administrator shall give the
         applicant written notice of the decision. The applicant shall provide the city a copy of the
         approved site plan, including any required corrections or revisions. Once in final,
         approved form, the site plan shall be marked “Approved” by the city.”

       B. Deviation Criteria Threshold: Proposed developments requesting a deviation under
       this section shall meet at least three (3) of the following criteria.
       1. The proposed development includes additional amenities or pedestrian and bike
