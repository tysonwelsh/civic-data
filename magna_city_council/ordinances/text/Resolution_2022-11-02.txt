                            MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP


RESOLUTION NO.: 22-11-02                                 DATE: November 22, 2022


  A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
  TENTATIVELY APPROVING THE 2023 MAGNA CONSOLIDATED
  FEE SCHEDULE AND SETTING THE PUBLIC HEARING FOR PUBLIC
  COMMENT ON DECEMBER 13, 2022, AT 6:00 P.M. OR
  IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING THE PUBLIC HEARING FOR THE
  2023 MAGNA BUDGET
                                        RECITALS
       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to
Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and
       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the
municipal governing body for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501; and
       WHEREAS, on October 25, 2022 the Council reviewed the 2023 Consolidated
Fee Schedule proposed by the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Board of
Trustees, Salt Lake County, and the Pleasant Green Cemetery Advisory Board; and
       WHEREAS, Pursuant to State Law, the municipality is required to annually adopt
a Consolidated Fee Schedule so the municipality can impose certain use fees for some of
the municipal-type services being performed in Magna; and
       WHEREAS, the fee schedule attached to this resolution, hereinafter to be known
as Exhibit “A”, reflects the user fees to be collected on behalf of the municipality for
2023; and
       WHEREAS, pursuant to State Law, the proposed 2023 Consolidated Fee
Schedule, shall be made available for public inspection no later than ten (10) days prior to
the public hearing to consider and possible adopt the fee schedule; now therefore be it




                                              1
                                       RESOLUTION

       RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council hereby tentatively approves
the 2023 Magna Consolidated Fee Schedule, attached to this resolution as listed as
“Exhibit A” and sets the date, time, and place for the Public Hearing to be as follows:

       PLACE:        Webster Center
                     8952 West Magna Main Street
                     Magna, UT 84044
       DATE:         December 13, 2022

       TIME:         6:00 p.m. or Immediately following the Public Hearing regarding the
2023 Magna Tentative Budget; and be it further

       RESOLVED, that a notice of the Public Hearing for the 2023 Tentative Magna
Consolidated Fee Schedule shall be posted to the Utah Public Meeting Notice Website
and Magna Metro Township Website; and be it further

       RESOLVED, copies of the 2023 Tentative Magna Consolidated Fee Schedule be
available for public inspection at the Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office, located at 2001
South State St Suite N2-700 Salt Lake City, Utah 84114, attached to the Notice of Public
Hearing on the Utah Public Meeting Notice Website
https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html, and posted to the Magna Metro Township
Website www.magnametrotownship.org for public inspection..

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake
County, Utah this 22nd day of November, 2022.
