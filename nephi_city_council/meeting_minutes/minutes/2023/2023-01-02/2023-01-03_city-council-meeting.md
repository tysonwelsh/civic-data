Mayor Justin Seely opened the regular council meeting and welcomed all present.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Councilor JD Parady

MARTY PALMER RETIREMENT PLAQUE PRESENTED:

  Mayor Justin Seely presented Marty Palmer a plaque honoring him for his 33 1/3 years of service as a
Nephi City employee. Mr. Palmer began his employment in 1989 working as the solid waste collector
and then moved to the Water/Sewer department in 1999. He became the Lead Operator in the
Water/Sewer department in 2007. Councilor Ostler stated that he had enjoyed working with Mr. Palmer
both in city employment and community events. Mr. Palmer thanked the Mayor and Council and said
that it had been a privilege and honor to work for City. He mentioned that the Water Department had
some exceptional men working in the department and told the council to be good to them or they are
going to leave. He said that he would like to see the sick leave donation policy changed so that retiring
employees could leave their accrued sick leave to the sick leave bank.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Callaway moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting 12-20-22, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 1-3-23, and the
sale of burial right to Christopher and Susan Snijman. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

GENERAL PLAN OVERVIEW:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the final draft of the general plan. He
stated that Planning Commission Chairman Ann Peterson had helped write the history of the City
contained in the plan. Mr. Atkinson highlighted several areas of the plan and asked the council to read
through the draft and let him know if they wanted any revisions. The council directed staff to bring back
the general plan in the first meeting in February for adoption.

LANE ALLRED BUILDING INSPECTOR AND PLAN REVIEW SERVICES CONTRACT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council that The City had recently completed a Request for Proposals
process for plan review and building inspection services. The city had several respondents which
included companies and individual contractors. The selection committee was composed of staff
members as well as representation from the local building community. After reviewing the proposals,
the committee recommended Lane Allred to perform these services on behalf of Nephi City.
  A contract for these services has been drafted by the City Attorney and reviewed by Mr. Allred. The
basic outline of the contract is that plan review and building inspection services will be provided by Mr.
Allred and that he is responsible to provide the tools necessary to accomplish these services.
Automobile and general liability insurance will be provided and add Nephi City as additional insured.
 Councilor Ostler moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the contract with Lane Allred to provide
building inspector and plan review services. Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously.

CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATES:

  Councilor Parady reviewed one business license application for Dr. Simple.
  Mr. Atkinson reported on a meeting he attended with county and state officials concerning
developing an agribusiness district out by Mills. He stated that two engineering firms were working on
feasibility studies. The timeline for the project goes out to the year 2030.
  Donald Ball reported that 43% of the average precipitation for December was received in the last two
days of the month. 3.12 inches of precipitation was received with the average precipitation for
December being 1.54 inches.
  Councilor Parady expressed his concern about possible flooding in the spring with the spring runoff.
  Mayor Seely said that he had received many comments from residents about the water problems
around the City from the recent rain storms.
  Councilor Parady announced that the Chamber lunch will be held Thursday at the Holiday Inn Express.
  Mr. Atkinson said that the Planning Commission is planning on revamping the City sign code and they
would like feedback and suggestion from the Chamber. Councilor Parady said he would communicate
that with the Chamber members.
  The council expressed their appreciation to the Streets department for their snow removal efforts
during the recent storms.
  Councilor Ostler expressed his disappointment with the Recyclops service recently.

MEETING ADJOURNED INTO EXECUTIVE SESSION:

   Councilor Ostler noting that there was not a need for an executive session or work session, moved to
adjourn the meeting. Councilor Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

Regular meeting adjourned at 8:09 p.m.
