CONSIDERATION _OF _RESOLUTION NO. ___R-2023-3120, _A__ RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING REMAINING FUNDING RECEIVED UNDER THE AMERICAN
RESCUE PLAN ACT

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that Washington County received funds under
the American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA), which have been allocated to projects since March of
2021. This Resolution is to obligate the remaining funds, which will be used in construction of the
expansion of the Children’s Justice Center, the medical programming services at Purgatory
Correctional Facility, the Water Conservancy District and the Emergency Operations Center
(EOC) storage facility.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3120, A
Resolution Appropriating Remaining Funding Received Under the American

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3121, A RESOLUTION TO
APPROVE A MEMORANDUM _ OF UNDERSTANDING THAT ESTABLISHES
COOPERATING AGENCY STATUS FOR THE COUNTY AS PART OF A LAND
EXCHANGE UNDER THE DINGELL ACT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow reminded the Commissioners Washington County was
invited by the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) to participate as a cooperating agency in a land
exchange involving other Counties in the state. This Memorandum of Understanding would give
Washington County cooperating agency status.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3121, A
Resolution to Approve a Memorandum of Understanding that Establishes
Cooperating Agency Status for the County as Part of a Land Exchange Under
the Dingell Act. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3122, A RESOLUTION TO OPPOSE
OVER-REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES IN UTAH

Mr. Snow stated the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) issued a good neighbor plan that
requires significant reduction in emissions in several states, including Utah. These regulations
could increase costs. This Resolution would express opposition to the EPA’s rule.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3122, A
Resolution to Oppose Over-Regulation of Public Utilities in Utah. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3123, A RESOLUTION REGARDING
INSURANCE COVERAGE ON MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY PROPERTY

Ms. Felshaw stated the Municipal Building Authority (MBA) was born primarily to facilitate
transactions for special service districts where the County and the MBA owns various parcels of
real property. This property is utilized by special service districts and are required to insure those
parcels. This Resolution clarifies the insurance of these properties.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3123, a
Resolution Regarding Insurance Coverage on Municipal Building Authority
Property. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Snow and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF _RESOLUTION NO. _R-2023-3124, _A _RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING EXPENDITURES OF FUNDS COLLECTED AS WASHINGTON
COUNTY’S__§ RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS SALES TAX _ FOR
UNINCORPORATED WASHINGTON COUNTY

Ms. Felshaw explained the Resolution is in regards to the unincorporated Recreation, Arts and
Parks (RAP) tax that Washington County collects. The Silver Reef Foundation is a non-profit
corporation that operates the Silver Reef Museum in Leeds. It is recommended that $15,450 be
allocated to the Silver Reef Foundation to make necessary structural improvements for the
historical property.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3124, A
Resolution Appropriating Expenditures of Funds Collected as Washington
County’s Recreation, Arts and Parks Sales Tax for Unincorporated
Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Iverson voting aye.
