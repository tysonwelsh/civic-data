BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for approximately 3.37 acres located
at approximately 13697 South 200 West (Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned CSD-
136C. The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved to allow for the development
of the currently vacant site as a parking lot to serve adjacent office buildings.

The CSD was created in 2016, with the property plated shortly after that. There are two
office buildings and a hotel built within the CSD. A gas station obtained site plan approval
in 2021, but has not yet broken ground. The subject property is Lot 3 within the 136 Center
Office Plat.

The property has been assigned the CSD-136C zoning classification (Exhibit D). According to

compatible, master planned mix of day and evening uses consisting of office, light

to consist of well designed, architecturally integrated structures which are appropriately

DCMC Section 9-18L-010 provides the specific purpose for the CSD-136C zone as “the 136
Center Commercial Special District contains approximately 28.09 acres, located along 200 West
and 13800 South as shown in section 9-18L-100, exhibit A of this article. The size of the property
allows for a master-planned, mixed-use office park. The Draper City General Plan's goals and
objectives are reflected in the overall district elements such as land use, architectural standards,
signage, site design and landscape standards. The Commercial District of the master plan aims
to broaden the commercial tax base of the City while providing amenities to the City residents.”

Site Plan and Parking Layout. The proposed site plan will add a parking lot to serve the
adjacent office buildings located directly to the south and west of the property (Exhibit E).
The parking lot rows will connect to the existing drive aisles and will add an additional 376
parking stalls. The office buildings combined equal 305,640 square feet. With the additional
376 parking stalls, the overall office component of 136 Center will have 1,827 stalls. This
equals 5.97 stalls per 1,000 square feet. The CSD-136C zone has a min/max parking
requirement of 3.5- 8 stalls per 1,000 square feet of building floor area.

Landscaping and Lot Coverage. The plan shows landscape islands at the end of parking rows,
and a new corridor with extra landscaping that will connect the drive aisles that run in front
of the two buildings (Exhibit F). This will create an enhanced parking corridor between
building entrances. Perimeter landscaping is also provided.

Criteria For Approval. The criteria for review and potential approval of a Site Plan request is
found in Sections 9-5-090(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for
such requests as:

         E. Standards For Approval: The following standards shall apply to the approval of a
