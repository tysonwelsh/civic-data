2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Josh Baum’s request for review of the Baum
Zone Change, changing the zoning on 0.36 acres located at 1907 North Center Street from
TH-5 (transitional holding) to R-1-15 (residential).

Commissioner Everett asked about the driveway and curb and gutter and if that was an accurate
representation on what is being required. Ms. Harris stated because the home is existing the City
can't require it. Commissioner Everett asked when that would need to be completed. Brad Kenison
stated that would need to be discussed with the Streets Department, but it is one that is on the list.
Chairman Hemmert asked that it was ensured to be put on the list. Mr. Kenison responded that
safe schools tend to raise a higher priority, etc. Commissioner Bunker said he feels bad for the
citizens without any type of recourse. Commissioner Everett stated by allowing this to happen
some curb and gutter will be installed. Commission Eyre addressed the cost. Mr. Kenison stated
funding is an issue and grants that the City has will improve those developments. Chairman
Hemmert stated curb and gutter were points of concern for the citizen. Commissioner Eyre asked
about the blind driveway. Mr. Kenison stated they would have to meet site criteria and standards.

The applicant, Josh Baum, was present. He provided information about the driveway. He stated
the plan is to drop the driveway down so there is free line of site. Chairman Hemmert asked for
clarification on where the two driveways enter and exit the street.

Chairman Hemmert opened public comment.

Chairmen Hemmert closed public comment.

    Motion: Commissioner Nielsen moved to forward a positive recommendation of Josh
    Baum’s request for review of the Baum Zone Change, changing the zoning on 0.36 acres
    located at 1907 North Center Street from TH-5 (transitional holding) to R-1-15 (residential);
    Including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Everett
    seconded.


    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson; yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
    Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Hemmert, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3. City Business
Mr. Struthers said we have the Work Session next Thursday. We have the Governor’s Executive
Order allowing us to meet electronically, so cities can continue to meet electronically, but we
need to read a statement before the meetings. We’re trying to work out a way to have meetings in
person but for this work session we will continue to have it through teams. We’re trying to figure
out how we can set up the room for meetings. We’re going to try for the first meeting in
September.

Staff and the Commissioners discussed the draft for the General Plan and the amount of
amendments that have come through this year. Discussed the General Plan progress and if there
was a timeline for it to be adopted. Mr. Struthers said that we are looking at having a joint work
session in October. The Commissioners asked Staff how many amendments have been
approved. Commissioner Hemmert stated a lot of general plan amendments have come though
this year. Kim will look into that.

Brad Kenison discussed road projects. The bridge across Triumph is waiting to be funded
through MAG. This will hopefully happen next Spring. Discussed the Main Street widening
from 2300 West out to the bridge. He stated that the rail trail will be done and paved by the end
of September.

4. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Everett moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Hemmert seconded the motion. The
motion passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:06 p.m.
