MINUTES OF THE DRAPER CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY MEETING HELD
ON TUESDAY, JUNE 2, 2020, IN THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS, 1020
EAST PIONEER ROAD, DRAPER, UTAH

PRESENT:             Board Chair, Troy K. Walker, and Boardmembers Mike Green, Tasha
                     Lowery, Cal Roberts, and Marsha Vawdrey
EXCUSED:             Boardmember Fred Lowry

STAFF PRESENT:       David Dobbins, City Manager; Mike Barker, City Attorney; Laura
                     Oscarson, City Recorder; Scott Cooley, City Engineer; John Eining, Police
                     Chief; Russ Fox, Assistant City Manager; Rhett Ogden, Recreation
                     Director; Clint Smith, Fire Chief; and Bob Wylie, Finance Director


Business Meeting

1.0   Call to Order: Chair Troy K. Walker

2.0   Public Hearing: Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Tentative Budget, this is an opportunity for
      the public to comment on the Redevelopment Agency Fiscal Year 2020-2021
      Tentative Budget prior to its adoption.

2.1   Bob Wylie, Finance Director, said this was a similar budget as that proposed to the Council
      on May 19, 2020, for the Redevelopment Agency of Draper City. It was a balanced budget.
      Mr. Wylie explained that there were six projects within the RDA. The total budget
      proposed for fiscal year 2020-20201 was $11,295,550.

2.2   Board Chair Walker opened the public hearing. No one came forward so Board
      Chair Walker closed the public hearing.

2.3   Board Member Marsha Vawdrey moved to approve of the Fiscal Year 2020-2021
      Tentative Budget. Board Member Mike Green seconded the motion.

2.4   A roll call vote was taken with Board Members Green, Lowery, Roberts, Vawdrey,
      and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.0   Adjourn to a City Council Meeting

3.1   Board Member Tasha Lowery moved to adjourn to a City Council meeting. Board
      Member Mike Green seconded the motion.

3.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts,
      Vawdrey, and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.3   The meeting adjourned at 7:54 p.m.
