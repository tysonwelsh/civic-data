C-15-2021      Petition by MALONE POWERSPORTS requesting a conditional use for a site
               expansion as well as an exception to section 7-10 of the West Valley City
               Municipal Code, Standards for Landscaping along High Image Arterial Streets.
               The property is located at 1379 W. 3300 S. on 5.16 acres and is zoned M
               (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-
               ut.gov)

Discussion:    Jody Knapp explained that the applicant is requesting use expansion/lot
               consolidation that involves covering the canal and UDOT access review and a 7-
               10 exception to provide display areas and accommodate tight internal circulation
               and parking.

               Jody explained that in 2019 when Malone first moved to the subject location,
               there were enforcement discussions about the need to clean up the property. She
               said, or Economic Development Department became involved when the applicant
               decided to expand. Jody said this partnership would help clean up the site and
               reach the goals of Malone Powersports. Jody explained the applicant was the
               recipient of the Cares Act Grant to be put toward covering the canal.

               Jody explained that the applicant is requesting a ten-foot grass, ten-foot sidewalk,
               and a ten-foot rock area for display as the majority of the streetscape along 3500
               South. She said they are asking for a five-foot sidewalk along the middle portion
               of the landscape. Jody stated the required streetscape along a high-image arterial
               street is fifteen feet of berm with trees, a ten-foot sidewalk, and a five-foot
               landscaped area with light poles.

               She mentioned that to drive or walk through the subject location currently is
               unnerving. Jody said the heavy, high-speed traffic along 3500 South makes
               accessing Malone Powersports difficult. She explained visibility is diminished by
               having the utility poles on either side of the access. Jody stated that the property
               internally has its obstacles, with its displays and large trucks driving through to
               pick up their ATVs. She said she is concerned about the narrow sidewalk being
               requested as part of the streetscape. Jody said she would like her plans and the

DISCUSSION ITEM
Small Planned Unit Developments (PUDs)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik said this discussion item is addressing ways to maintain smaller
               PUDs with more minor HOAs long term. He presented different options, not
               requirements, to the Commission. Steve then pointed out some options the City
               can execute and others they can not. Steve discussed the various options the City
               could regulate. First, the minimum land area in acres. Second, the number of
               units the development will have. Third, use building materials with longevity.
               Fourth, set some specifications for private roads and have them inspected to keep
               the roads maintained and lessen the overall cost.

               Commissioner Winters asked if there is a reason that the developer would want a
               private street. Steve said yes, a private road does not need to be as comprehensive
               as a residential street.

               Commissioner McEwen asked if the City could require a public street for
               developments over a specific size or number of units. Steve said that is possible.
               However, some of the private streets that the smaller PUDs have been developing
               are narrow. Steve said if they are required to be public streets, the width of the
               road will either make the parcel tight for development or, in some cases, not allow
               enough room to develop at all.

               Commissioner Lovato feels that the Commission's view of maintenance for the
               smaller PUDs might be too stringent. He asked if there was a way to have a guide
               when assessing how they are being maintained. Steve said he would survey these
               smaller developments throughout the City to compare. He said most HOAs are
               functional but, the question is how well they are functioning.

               Commissioner Fuller asked if we were to turn all private roads into public roads,
               how many more miles of the street would the City be responsible for maintaining?
               A couple of years ago, Steve said that the Public Works Department located all of
               the private roads within the City and calculated the cost of repairing them to a
               level of being maintained. Steve said the number was huge, and he will gather that
               information to present to the Commission for a subsequent discussion.

               Commissioner Winters asked what the City is allowed to enforce with these
               smaller PUDs on private streets. He also asked if there is a way the City could set
               a minimum maintenance standard as part of the CCNR, allowing them to enforce
               those standards. Steve said that it is easier to enforce standards if one property
               owner or a functioning HOA. He explained if an HOA is defunked, enforcement
               becomes much more challenging. As part of a development agreement, Steve
               said that the City could state what they would like in the CCNR's. He said that
               once the project is complete, the HOA could vote to change what was added to
               the CCNR's via the development agreement without the City's knowledge.
