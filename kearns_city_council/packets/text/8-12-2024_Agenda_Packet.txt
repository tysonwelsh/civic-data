  I.    General Information

    A. Introduction
The City of Kearns (“Kearns”) located in Salt Lake County, Utah, is seeking proposals from
certified decontamination specialists for abatement of hazardous materials contractors
(“Vendor(s)”) interested in providing hazardous materials removal services to Kearns on a
contract basis. The work will be assigned on a rotating schedule and based on Vendor(s)
availability. Kearns reserves the right to award to more than one bidder.
    B. Background
Kearns occasionally requires a certified decontamination specialist for the removal of hazardous
materials for residential, commercial and industrial properties that have been closed to occupancy
by the Salt Lake County Health Department. Generally, the Abatement of these chemically
contaminated properties occurs after the landowner is provided with six months’ notice and fails
to take any action to demonstrate that the landowner will remove the hazardous materials. Such
abatements occur pursuant to and only after the requirements of Utah Code Section 10-11-1 et
seq..

                                           RECITALS

WHEREAS, the City of Kearns (“Kearns”) is a municipality and has the authority to pass all
ordinances and rules, and make all regulations, not repugnant to law, necessary for carrying into
effect or discharging all powers and duties pursuant to Utah Code § 10-8-84; and

WHEREAS, the Kearns, as a municipality, has the authority to leverage fees on development,
including impact fees, pursuant to the Impact Fees Act, Utah Code §§ 11-36a-101, et seq. (the
“Act”); and

WHEREAS, the Kearns City Council (“Council”) desires to study the feasibility of enacting
impact fees to cover some of the costs of new development on the public facilities that Kearns
provides; and

WHEREAS, before Kearns may enact an ordinance imposing an impact fee, the Impact Fees Act,
Utah Code § 11-36-101, et seq. requires Kearns to take certain steps, including the development
of an Impact Fee Facilities Plan and, depending on the results of the Impact Fee Facilities Plan, an
Impact Fee Analysis; and

WHEREAS, on             August 2, 2024        , pursuant to Utah Code § 11-36a-501, Kearns issued
notice of its intent to prepare an Impact Fee Facilities Plan covering the geographical area of its
service area; and

WHEREAS, the Council now wishes to memorialize and authorize its intent to prepare an Impact
Fee Facilities Plan by resolution.

WHEREAS, the Council further wishes to ensure that the development of the Impact Fee
Facilities Plan is consistent with the development of a broader Transportation Master Plan that the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (“MSD”) is developing for Kearns and its other
member entities.

                                         RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL, effective
immediately:

   1. The Council hereby authorizes and directs its Mayor, staff, and agents to perform all acts
      needed to prepare an Impact Fee Facilities Plan for the Kearns, including but not limited
      to publishing the attached request for proposals (“RFP”) to invite qualifies contractors to
      submit bids regarding the development of the Impact Fee Facilities Plan and, if necessary,
       the development of an Impact Fee Analysis in accordance with the Impact Fees Act, Utah
       Code § 11-36-101, et seq.

   2. The Council hereby authorizes and directs its Mayor, staff, and agents to review the bids
      submitted pursuant to the RFP and to recommend to the Council a contractor to develop
      the Impact Fee Facilities Plan and, if necessary, the Impact Fee Analysis, which
      recommendation the Council will consider at a future public meeting.

   3. The Council hereby authorizes and directs its Mayor, staff, and agents to coordinate with
      the MSD in the development of the Impact Fee Facilities Plan to ensure it is consistent with
      the Transportation Master Plan and vice versa.

   4. The Council authorizes and directs its Mayor, staff, and agents to perform all analyses,
      certifications, calculations, enactments, and other steps needed to enforce this Resolution.

   5. This ordinance shall go into effect upon its posting pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-711.

            A. Introduction

The City of Kearns (“City”) located in Salt Lake County, Utah, is seeking proposals from qualiﬁed
individuals or ﬁrms (Consultant) to develop a complete Impact Fee Facilities Plan (IFFP), and
depending on the results of the IFFP, a possible Impact Fee Study (Study). The Consultant will
evaluate all public facilities that Kearns provides, including stormwater, drainage, and ﬂood control
facilities; roadway facilities; parks, recreation facilities, open space, and trails; and any other public
facilities for which Kearns can impose impact fees under the Impact Fees Act, Title 11, Chapter 36a
of the Utah Code.

1.   Project Summary

     A. The Kearns Community Center Project consists of renovating the existing Oquirrh
        Hill Elementary School (located at 5241 S 4280 W Kearns, UT 84118) to convert it
        into a multi-use community center. The Project may necessitate renovation(s) of the
        existing educational facility.

        Oquirrh Hills Elementary School is owned by the Granite School District (the
        “District”). The District is working with Kearns to lease the building to the city for 20
        years with the option of two (2) additional ﬁve (5) year extensions, for a total lease of
        up to 30 years. The building has a square footage of approximately 43,000 square
        feet.

        Kearns as the tenant is permitted to use the school for government operations, and
        to sublease to other non-proﬁt community organizations, recreational programs and
        for other supplementary uses that support Keans’s primary operations, and which
        do not conﬂict with the mission of the School District.

        It is anticipated that this project be undertaken in two main phases. The ﬁrst phase
        (Phase I) will consist of an assessment of the existing building for Kearns to
        understand and budget for any required mechanical, structural, or code compliance
        improvements that may be required. The ﬁndings from Phase I will inform the
        development of a detailed scope of work and budget estimate for the subsequent
        renovation and upgrade projects.

        If the ﬁndings and improvements in Phase I are deemed workable, Phase II (the
        Construction Phase) will entail the implementation of the identiﬁed renovations and
        upgrades to enhance the building's functionality, safety, and overall quality. This
        may include structural repairs, system replacements or upgrades, accessibility
        improvements, interior redesign, and any other necessary measures to bring the
        building up to modern standards and address the needs of its occupants.

     B. The Project envisions a Community Center to house City Offices and other

Resolution 2024-06-01                                                              June 10, 2024

   A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL EXPRESSING
   APPRECIATION FOR KAYLA MAULDIN

WHEREAS, the community of Kearns (“Kearns”) incorporated as a metro township on January 1,
2017, and later became a city on May 1, 2024; and

WHEREAS, since its incorporation, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (“MSD”) has
provided the city’s administrative, planning and development, business licensing, and code
enforcement services; and

WHEREAS, Kayla Mauldin began working for the MSD as a long-range planner on May 26, 2020,
and subsequently became the MSD’s senior long-range planner with supervisory responsibilities
for other long-range planners within the MSD; and

WHEREAS, during her tenure with the MSD, Kayla has worked closely with Kearns to develop
and implement several instrumental projects that have significantly furthered Kearns’s interests
and prerogatives; and

WHEREAS, among other important projects, Kayla played a key role in facilitating the
development and implementation of the current Kearns General Plan, the restatement of Kearns’s
land use ordinances in Titles 18 and 19 of the Kearns Municipal Code, and numerous other land
use ordinances and actions that have significantly furthered the interests of Kearns and its
citizens.

NOW, THEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, effective immediately that:

1. The Kearns Council expresses its sincere appreciation for Kayla’s years of faithful service,
   valuable advice, and significant contributions.
