Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Brien Maxfield
CC:  Date: April 7, 2020
Re:  Comments: Draper City received notice that the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) has updated its Flood Insurance Study (FIS) and Flood Insurance Rate Maps (FIRMs) for areas within the city located in Utah County as part of the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP).  These updates are required to be adopted into the municipal code of Draper.
The modifications being considered in Title 12 include adopting the updated FIS and FIRMs, correcting language found in the current code, and updating Title 12 to comply with current standards. The findings are as follows: The City Council of Draper City finds the proposed amendment:
Is in compliance with Utah Code Annotated,
Is required by FEMA in order to comply with the NFIP, and
Is in the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper.
