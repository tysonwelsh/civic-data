                           RESOLUTION NO. 21-44

     A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL RETIRING POLICE
CANINE OFFICER JUDGE AND AUTHORIZING HIS SALE TO OFFICER ROBERT
BARLOW FOR A NOMINAL FEE



      WHEREAS, Canine Officer Judge (“K-9 Judge”) is an eight year old
Belgian Malinois; and

      WHEREAS, K-9 Judge has been partners with his handler Officer Robert
Barlow (“Officer Barlow”) for more than four years; and

      WHEREAS, K-9 Judge has been trained in drug detection and suspect
apprehension, the latter of which requires K-9 Judge to use force to apprehend
suspects; and

      WHEREAS, K-9 Judge and Officer Barlow have formed a unique and
inseparable bond through training and patrol activities, with Officer Barlow
providing K-9 Judge with daily care and support; and

      WHEREAS, K-9 Judge has served the City of Draper with distinction; and

       WHEREAS, Officer Robert Barlow will be leaving the K-9, therefore, K-9
Judge is going to be retired.

       WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code Section 3-3-150 (E) provides the
procedures to sell in a reasonable manner city personal property “so unique
as to be unmarketable”; and

      WHEREAS, because of his specialized training in suspect apprehension
and the close bond he’s formed with Officer Barlow, the Draper City Council
determines K-9 Judge is so unique as to be unmarketable; and

       WHEREAS, the humane disposition of K-9 Judge for both K-9 Judge and
Officer Barlow is to allow Officer Barlow to purchase K-9 Judge for the sum of
one dollar.
       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Declaration of Surplus Property. K-9 Judge is declared to be
surplus property.

      Section 2. Authorization to Sell for a Nominal Amount. K-9 Judge is so
unique as to be unmarketable. The City Manager is authorized to sell K-9
Judge to Officer Robert Barlow for the amount of one dollar, which the
Draper City Council finds is a reasonable amount.

      Section 3. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this
Resolution is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or
unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and all
sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, ON THE 17th DAY OF AUGUST, 2021.
