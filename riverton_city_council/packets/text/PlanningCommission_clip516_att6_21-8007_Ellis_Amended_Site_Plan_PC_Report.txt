Background

This is a public hearing for amending a commercial site plan located on the west side of
Redwood Road at 12694 South. Amended Site Plan applications are requests to make
changes to a previously approved site plan, or the architecture or appearance of a commercial
building.

Commercial Buildings are reviewed by the Planning Commission as part of commercial site
plans, and must conform to Riverton City Code. When a property owner desires to change an
existing commercial building they must apply for an amended commercial site plan.

The building was the Doctor Pugh Dentist Office for many years. Doctor Pugh has retired and
has sold the building. The subject property is located it the Commercial Downtown Zone (C-D).
The parcel is 0.24-acres with only about 55-ft of frontage. The existing structure is about
1300-sf. Most of the building is original from 1895, but there have been a number of additions,
some of those will remain.     The Riverton City Building Official and Planning Staff met several

This was noticed as a public hearing item, but no comments had been received when this report

Recommendations

The applicant is proposing remaining within the basic material palate and colors tones of the
building. The primary change to the building itself is the replacement of the existing horizontal
siding with vertical white fiber cement (‘hardi-board’) siding, with the siding color remaining the
same. The submitted proposed building elevation shows brick on the front (east) façade.
However, the applicant is maintaining the existing stucco on that façade, and the submitted
texture was not intended to indicate a change there. As the installation of ‘hardi-board’ in place
of the existing siding is a change to material type and orientation, it can be considered
consistent with the original materials. The addition of the ADA accessible sidewalk and removal
of the existing walkway and stars are improvements to the site to bring it more in line with
current requirements and codes, and also consistent with the existing look and feel of the site.
Staff has no objections to the proposed changes to the existing building and site.
