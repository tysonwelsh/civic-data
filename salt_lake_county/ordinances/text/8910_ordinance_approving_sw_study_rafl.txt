ORDINANCE NO.                                                            , 2023 
 
AN ORDINANCE APPROVING SOUTHWEST CANAL AND CREEK 
HYDROLOGY STUDY 
 
 The County legislative body of Salt Lake County, State of Utah, ordains as follows: 
 
 WHEREAS, due to the pace of new development throughout Salt Lake County it has become 
apparent that the studies that address storm drainage and flood control development need to be updated 
to reassess predevelopment stormwater release rates and how those rates will impact the County’s Flood 
Control Facilities;  
 WHEREAS, the Southwest Canal and Creek Hydrology Study has recently been completed;  
 WHEREAS, this study addresses a number of issues, including but not limited to, the maximum 
excess water release rates into storm drain systems that are allowed for new development projects in the 
unincorporated county, i.e., the amount of stormwater that a developer must retain onsite via stormwater 
facilities that a developer constructs; 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code section 17-27a-212, a public hearing is required before Salt 
Lake County can approve this study for the affected unincorporated portion of the County; 
 WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council held a public hearing for this matter on March 28, 
2023; and  
 NOW THEREFORE, the Salt Lake County Council hereby approves the Southwest Canal and 
Creek Hydrology Stud y.  A copy of this Study  is included in the QR Code inserted herein  and is 
incorporated as if fully set forth.   
 This ordinance shall take effect  fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon at least one 
publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in Salt Lake County, and if not 
so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall take effect immediately upon its first publication. 
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Salt Lake County Council has approved, passed and adopted this
