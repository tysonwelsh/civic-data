       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Mitchell thanked staff for answering all the questions he had sent
          over regarding the 2024-2025 budget discussion. He encouraged people to reach
          out to their Council Members so that they could vote accordingly on all subjects.

           Council Member Huff talked about ‘Contento Cafe’, a new Mexican restaurant that
           recently opened in the City and encouraged everyone to support the City’s
           restaurants.

           Council Member Pinkney wished everyone a happy Juneteenth and talked about
           some of the County resources that are available to seniors on a fixed income that
           may help address property tax increases.

           Council Member Bynum talked about the previous lack of action from older Councils
           that led to the situation that the City finds itself in now regarding property tax
           increases. She said that in more recent years, the current Council has decided that
           it's best to have smaller but more regular increases in taxes or fees so that decades
           don’t go by without addressing the increasing cost of what it takes to run a City and
           then have to propose a much higher increase.

Public Hearing – 7:00 p.m. (Or As Soon Thereafter As Possible)

    1. To receive public input with respect to (a) the issuance of not to exceed $50,000,000
       of Sales Tax Revenue Bonds and (b) the potential economic impact that the
       improvements to be financed with the proceeds of said bonds will have on the private
       sector.

        Community & Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, and Finance
        Director, Crystal Makin, reviewed the timeline of the sales tax revenue bond over the
        last few months. The bond is to assist the City in the rebuilding of the Public Works
        campus.
                                               8
South Salt Lake – Regular Council Meeting                                          June 12, 2024


       A copy of all the materials she reviewed are attached and incorporated by this
       reference.

       The public hearing opened at 7:02 p.m.

       South Salt Lake resident, Conrad Campos, asked a few questions. He wanted to know
       the amount, what exactly the funds would be used for, if the City already owned the
       land, how long the term would be for the bonds.

       Finance Director, Crystal Makin, answered Mr. Campos’ questions.

       She stated that the amount is for $50 million dollars intended for the rebuild of the
       building and its refurnishing’s. The building is currently in the planning stages with an
       architect and a general contractor. The City already owns the land, and the term of the
       loan is for 20 years.

       The public hearing closed at 7:04 p.m.

       There was no action needed as the purpose of the Public Hearing was just to receive any
       public input.
