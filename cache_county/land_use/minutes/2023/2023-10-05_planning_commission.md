         5 October 2023               Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 2 of 3 
Present:  Stephen Nelson, Tim Watkins, Chris Sands, Nate Daugs, Lane Parker, Jay Rigby, Brady 
Christensen, Kurt Bankhead, Jason Watterson, Taylor Sorenson 
Start Time:  
Daugs called the meeting to order and Sands gave the opening remarks. 
Agenda & Minutes 
Parker motioned to approve the Agenda and minutes from September 7, 2023 Rigby seconded; 7, 0. 
Consent Items 
#1 White Rock Subdivision 2nd Amendment 
Watterson motioned to approve the consent agenda; Sands seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
Regular Action Items 
#2 Geneva Rock – Newton Pit Conditional Use Permit 
Nelson reviewed access for the Geneva Rock – Newton Pit Conditional Use Permit and a possible 60 day 
extension. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed access and realignment of the road, possible approval without an 
updated site plan. 
Todd Wardell commented on access and realignment of the road. 
Christensen commented on a possible route through Newton rather than 48. 
Nelson commented on needing an updated site plan with the changes from UDOT. 
Mr. Wardell commented that they are waiting on approval from UDOT. 
Staff and Commissioners talked about phasing of the pit and reclamation and how the applicant has not 
gotten back to Staff on those questions. 
Craig Rawlins commented on accessing from the southwest vs. leaving the access where it is currently, 
and conditioning the public that uses that road,  
Rigby commented on not seeing far enough from the current stop north for big trucks. 
Mr. Rawlins commented on big trucks driving that road and making sure other drivers’ are conditioned 
for the road and traffic. 
Jeff Benson commented on the intersection being bad in general. 
Commissioners commented on approval being dependant on making sure the health and safety of the 
public is taken into consideration,  
 
 
         5 October 2023               Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 3 of 3 
Watterson motioned to continue the Geneva Rock – Newton Pit Conditional Use Permit for 90 days; 
Parker seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
#5 Public Hearing (5:55 pm) 
Smith reviewed the County Resource Management Plan updates. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the maps and how the plan only affects County land. A public open 
house will be held for the public to make comments and see the plan. 
Parker motioned to open the public hearing; Watterson seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
No Public Comment. 
Parker motioned to close the public hearing; Watterson seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
#3 Advanced Shoring 2023 Use Permit 2nd Amendment 
Nelson reviewed the staff report for the Advanced Shoring 2023 Use Permit 2nd Amendment. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed if a fence will be required. 
Parker motioned to approve the Advanced Shorting 2023 Conditional Use Permit 2nd Amendment with 
the 12 conditions and 2 conclusions; Rigby seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
#4 Amending Title 16 Subdivision Regulations 
Nelson reviewed the new updates to Title 16 Subdivision Regulations. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed how disputed boundaries can affect development. 
Nelson reviewed changes suggested by the Utah League of Cities, by the public works department, and 
needed changes to comply with state code. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the appeals process. 
Nelson continued reviewing the needs updates to comply with state code. 
Parker motioned to recommend to the County Council the amended Title 16 subdivision regulations; 
Watterson seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
Nelson reminded the Commissioners about the Cache Summit coming up that would also count towards 
the educations hours required. The County also received grant money to help with the Housing Crisis 
Task Force and publicity for that. 
Adjourned.
