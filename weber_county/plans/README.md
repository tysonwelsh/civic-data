## Moderate-Income Housing — NOT a standalone plan (honest note)

Weber County does **not** publish a standalone Moderate-Income Housing (MIH) plan/element
for its unincorporated areas. Per the county Planning FAQ, MIH is handled as **chapters
within the General Plans** for HB 462/463 statutory reporting. Verified in the extracted
text: both the **Ogden Valley GP** and the **Western Weber GP** contain substantial
"Moderate-Income Housing" content (dozens of references each), and the 2022 OV GP
Implementation Program carries MIH implementation items. There is therefore no separate
MIH row — search the GP text sidecars for MIH provisions.

## Jurisdiction note

These are the plans for **unincorporated** Weber County. With **Ogden Valley City**
incorporating (ballot passed 2024, council elected 2025 — see `../land_use/README.md`),
the Ogden Valley General Plan here is the **county's** pre-incorporation plan for that
area; the new city will adopt its own general plan over time (not part of this county
build).
