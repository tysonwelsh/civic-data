MAGNA
RESOLUTION 20-04-02
03-18-2020 MAGNA EARTHQUAKE STATE OF EMERGENCY EXTENSION
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL EXTENDING
THE STATE OF EMERGENCY DECLARATION FOR THE 03-18-2020 MAGNA
EARTHQUAKE UNTIL JUNE 30, 2020
WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to
Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and
WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council’”) is the municipal
governing body for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501; and
WHEREAS, At 7:09 am on March 18, 2020, an earthquake measuring 5.7 on the
Richter scale occurred in Magna; and
WHEREAS, Magna declared a "State of Emergency" based on the damages observed
from the effects of the earthquake; and
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County and the State also declared a "State of Emergency" for
the earthquake; and
WHEREAS, On the afternoon of March 18, 2020 Magna began collecting information
regarding the damages to property; and
WHEREAS, Due to COVID-19, the State of Utah requested a 30-day extension from
FEMA on March 20, 2020 to collect and assess the damages from the earthquake; and
WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code § 53-2A-208, a Declaration of a State of
Emergency can only be extended past 30 days by a resolution of the governing body; and
WHEREAS, Based on the compounding effects of COVID-19 extending the time
needed to collect and report data, extending the State of Emergency declaration would allow for
the continuation of the collection and assessment of damages to report to the County and State;
NOW THEREFORE, by the Magna Metro Township Council, the State of Emergency
declared by the Mayor on March 18, 2020, due to the earthquake, is extended until June 30,
2020.
