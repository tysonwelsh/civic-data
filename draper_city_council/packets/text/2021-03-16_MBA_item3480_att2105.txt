                                      RESOLUTION NO. MBA 21-01

        A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE ADOPTED BUDGET OF THE DRAPER
        CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2020-
        2021.


        WHEREAS, the Municipal Building Authority of Draper City has adopted Resolution No. MBA
20-02 which adopted the final budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2020 and ending June 30, 2021,
in accordance with the requirements of the Utah Code Annotated; and

       WHEREAS, the Municipal Building Agency of Draper City wishes to amend the fiscal year 2020-
2021 budget; and

         WHEREAS, a public hearing to consider the appropriations has been noticed and held and all
interested persons were heard, for or against the appropriations; and

         WHEREAS, the Municipal Building Authority of Draper City hereby finds this action in the best
interest of the public’s health, safety and general welfare.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MUNICIPAL BUILDING
AUTHORITY OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1.     Purpose. The purpose of this Resolution is to amend the budget of the Municipal
Building Authority of Draper City, as approved and finalized by Municipal Building Authority of Draper
City Resolution No. MBA 20-02.

        Section 2.      Adoption of Amendments. The budget amendments attached hereto and made a
part of this Resolution shall be, and the same hereby are adopted and incorporated into the budget of
Municipal Building Authority of Draper City, Utah for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2020 and ending
June 30, 2021, in accordance with the requirements of the Utah Code Annotated.

        Section 3.       Filing of copies of the Budget Amendments. The Budget Officer is authorized and
directed to certify and file a copy of said budget amendments in the office of the City Recorder which
amendments shall be available for public inspection.

         Section 4.        Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid, or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and
all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 5.        Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
passage.
