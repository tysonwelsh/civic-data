                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 23-64

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY TO ENTER INTO A CONTRACT WITH
  WASTE MANAGEMENT TO PROVIDE MUNICIPAL WASTE AND RECYCLING
                 HAULING AND DISPOSAL SERVICES

         WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code § 19-6-503 the Riverton City Council may, “(a)
supervise and regulate the collection, transportation, and disposition of solid waste generated
within its jurisdiction; (b) provide a solid waste management facility to adequately handle solid
waste generated or existing within or without its jurisdiction; and (c) assume, by agreement,
responsibility for the collection and disposition of solid waste whether generated within or without
its jurisdiction;” and

      WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any contract that exceeds
$30,000 in a public meeting;” and

       WHEREAS, a contractor is needed to haul and dispose of the residential municipal waste
and recycling; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that following receipt of
proposals as required by ordinance Waste Management has been determined to be the lowest
responsive, responsible bidder to provide residential solid waste and recycling services for the
residents of Riverton City;

     WHEREAS, by approving this agreement, Council will need to approve a future budget
amendment for can maintenance and overage and contamination penalties.


     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1.   Riverton City approves the execution of the contract with Waste Management to
            provide municipal waste and recycling hauling and disposal services.

       2.   This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

   PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 1st day of August
2023 by the following vote:
