                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-07


      A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL SUPPORTING THE
      IMPLEMENATION OF A CYBERSECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE AND
                          MANAGEMENT PLAN


        WHEREAS, stated generally, a municipality’s paramount corporate purpose is to protect
the health, safety, and welfare of the public through delivery of municipal services such as
emergency response, public safety, transportation, and utility services; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City relies on electronic information systems to provide each and
every municipal service it delivers to the public; and

        WHEREAS, maintaining Cybersecurity, therefore, is a top priority for Riverton City
officials, to protect its information systems from cyberattack; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to the Utah Governmental Records Management Act, Utah Code §
63G-2-305, records are protected from public disclosure if their release would jeopardize the
security of governmental property, governmental programs, or governmental recordkeeping
systems from damage, theft, or other appropriation or use contrary to law or public policy; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to the Utah Open Public Meetings Act, Utah Code § 52-4-205
issues related to the deployment of security personnel, devices, or systems are to be discussed in
closed sessions of the Riverton City Council; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to the Utah Open Public Meetings Act, Utah Code § 52-4-204, a
resolution may not be approved in a closed meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City council is advised that City staff, under the direction of
the Mayor and City Manager, has created and implemented a Cybersecurity Incident Response
and Management Plan; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has conducted a confidential review of said plan
and supports the same; and


       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:


Section 1.     Riverton City Council hereby expresses its support for the Cybersecurity Incident
               Response Management Plan created by City staff.

Section 2.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
