                                             Hearing began at – 6:03 p.m.

OAM2024-001257 – Proposed amendments to the White City Subdivision Ordinance, Title 18 of the White
City Municipal Code, to comply with 2024 Utah Legislative Mandates. Presenter: Jay Springer, Attorney;
Brian Tucker, Planning Manager (Discussion/Motion)


White City Planning Commission – September 26, 2024 – Meeting Summary                                                  Page 1 of 2
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Morgan Julian and Lisa Watts Baskin,
Counsel provided an analysis of Title 18 code amendments.

Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding preliminary administrative land use
authority, final land use authority, completed application timeline and checklist, pre-application meeting,
substantive review, public hearing at the final subdivision process, building permit hold and code
enforcement, state code reference

Commissioner Seiger-Webster motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Huntzinger seconded
that motion at 6:32pm.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

No one from the public is present to speak.

Commissioner Huntzinger motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Spagnuolo seconded that
motion at 6:33pm.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Commissioners and staff agreed to follow state code as much as possible.

Motion: To recommend file #OAM2024-001257 Proposed amendments to the White City Subdivision
Ordinance, Title 18 of the White City Municipal Code, to comply with 2024 Utah Legislative Mandates to
the White City Council for approval, subject to staff and legal counsel’s non-substantive and technical
changes.
      Motion by: Commissioner Frailey
      2nd by: Commissioner Spagnuolo
      Vote: Commissioners voted unanimously in favor (of commissioners present)
Commissioner Hunsaker motioned to adjourn.
Ms. Julian advised receiving a grant for traffic calming demonstrations on Larkspur through Galena
temporarily and creating devices to stay awhile.
