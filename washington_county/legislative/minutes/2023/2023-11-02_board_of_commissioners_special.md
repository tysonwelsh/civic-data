WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
NOVEMBER 2, 2023

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 2:01 p.m. on November 2, 2023, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Commissioner Adam
Snow, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, County
Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. __R-2023-3193, _A | RESOLUTION
AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A QUIT CLAIM DEED FROM WASHINGTON
COUNTY TO DEV3, LLC TO REMOVE PORTIONS OF TEMPORARY
CONSTRUCTION EASEMENTS FOR STORM DRAINAGE FACILITIES RELATED TO
DESERT HAVEN TOWNHOMES

Public Works Director Todd Edwards explained there is a current Natural Resources Conservation
Service (NRCS) project ongoing to realign a pipeline. The current easement goes into a townhome
construction project that is no longer needed. This Resolution would modify the temporary
easement to exclude certain areas, on condition that the county receives a permanent easement for

the pipeline realignment.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3193, A
Resolution Authorizing the Execution of a Quit Claim Deed From Washington
County to Deve3, LLC to Remove Portions of Temporary Construction
Easements for Storm Drainage Facilities Related to Desert Haven
Townhomes. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Iverson voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 2:05 p.m.
