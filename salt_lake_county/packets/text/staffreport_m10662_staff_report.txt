Requested Agenda Title:
Final Adoption of an Ordinance of General Revision, Amending Title 9, Entitled “Health and Safety” and Title
19, Entitled “Zoning” of the Salt Lake Code of Ordinances, 2001, to Provide Greater Consistency with the
Wildland Urban Interface Code.
Requested Agenda Item Description: The Salt Lake County Office of Regional Development staff raised the
need to update FCOZ to be more consistent with the Utah Wildland-Urban Interface code and its goals of
mitigating wildfire risk. Following initiation of the ordinance revision process at the County Council's May 7th
meeting, the ordinance update was reviewed by both the Salt Lake County and Mountainous Planning District
Planning Commissions. The ordinance is now ready for County Council review after receiving favorable
recommendations from both planning commissions.
