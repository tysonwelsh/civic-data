                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-10

  CUSTOMER REQUESTED GENERAL SERVICE CONTRACT BETWEEN ROCKY
   MOUNTAIN POWER AND RIVERTON CITY TO PROVIDE POWER FOR THE
  TRAFFIC SIGNAL AND STREET LIGHTS ON OLD LIBERTY WAY AT OR NEAR
                            13200 SOUTH

         WHEREAS, the Public Works Department has been working with Salt Lake County to
install a traffic signal at the intersection of Old Liberty Way (4150 W) and Majestic Rise Parkway
(13200 S); and

       WHEREAS, there is no power to energize the signal and street lights at the intersection;
and

       WHEREAS, the PW Department contacted RMP and asked them to provide a service
contract to the City to provide power for the signal and street lights; and

        WHEREAS, Rocky Mountain Power has replied and provided a Service Contract to
deliver said power; and

       WHEREAS, the contract has options for partial reimbursement to the City from adjacent
properties that may take advantage of the work performed by the City; and

       WHEREAS, the City chooses to take the 20% reimbursement option found in the Service
Contract; and

       WHEREAS, the agreement outlines the responsibilities of both parties; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council does hereby determine that it is in the best interests
of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to execute said General Service
Contract; and

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council that the
aforesaid Resolution authorizing the Mayor to execute a General Service Contract between
Rocky Mountain Power and Riverton City to provide power for the traffic signal and street lights
on Old Liberty Way at or near 13200 South as described above, copies of which are incorporated
herein by reference, be, and hereby is, approved, and the Mayor of Riverton is hereby authorized
to execute the same.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 2nd day of
February 2021 by the following vote:
