                             RESOLUTION NO. 25-62

        A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPOINTING
          MAYOR TROY WALKER AS DRAPER CITY’S DESIGNATED
      REPRESENTATIVE TO THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE JORDAN
                   BASIN IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, Draper City is a participating municipality within the Jordan
Basin Improvement District (“District”), a regional special service district that
delivers essential wastewater collection, treatment, and related infrastructure
services that directly benefit Draper City residents and support the City’s long-
term growth and sustainability; and

       WHEREAS, applicable Utah law authorizes Draper City to designate a
qualified representative to the District’s Board of Trustees to safeguard the
City’s priorities, protect ratepayer interests, and foster effective inter-
jurisdictional collaboration; and

        WHEREAS, Mayor Walker, through his tenure as Mayor and active
engagement on numerous regional boards, councils, and commissions, has
significant expertise in water resource management, infrastructure planning,
and intergovernmental coordination that advances the City’s objectives within
the District; and

        WHEREAS, the Draper City Council recognizes that Mayor Walker’s
continued service on regional bodies strengthens the City’s operations and
supports the City’s ability to deliver effective services to its residents, and finds
it in the best interest of the City to appoint a representative who can effectively
coordinate with the District while communicating matters of mutual concern
to the municipal legislative body.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Appointment. Mayor Troy Walker is hereby appointed to
serve as Draper City’s representative to the Jordan Basin Improvement District.
       Section 2. Term. The appointed representative shall serve until replaced
by further action of the City Council or until otherwise required by the bylaws
or governing documents of the district.
      Section 3. Authorization. The appointed representative is authorized
to attend meetings, participate in discussions, cast votes as permitted, and
perform all duties necessary to represent Draper City’s interests before the
Jordan Basin Improvement District.

       Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this
Resolution is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability
shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and
provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 5.     Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, THIS 18H DAY OF NOVEMBER, 2025.
