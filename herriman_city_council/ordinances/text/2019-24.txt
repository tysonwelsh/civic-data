HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2019-24

AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING A REPAYMENT REQUIREMENT FOR CITY
INSTALLED PROJECT IMPROVEMENTS BY ADDING SECTION 1-10-20 TO THE
HERRIMAN CITY CODE

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Council (the “Council”) met in regular session on
August 14, 2019, to consider, among other things, adopting an ordinance adopting a repayment
requirement for city installed project improvements by adding section 1-10-20 to the Herriman
city code; and

WHEREAS, on occasion Herriman will make project improvements that benefit
undeveloped or underdeveloped land of adjacent property owners and some adjacent property
owners participate and other refuse; and

WHEREAS, the Council has determined that it is fair and reasonable to impose a
repayment procedure that imposed a rationally equivalent obligation on all undeveloped or under
developed adjacent property owners (those that participate and those that do not participate) that
benefit or will benefit from the city installed project improvement; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the residents of Herriman to add section 1-10-20 to
the Herriman City Code.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council that the following be added
as section 1-10-20 of the Herriman City Code:

1-10-20 Repayment Obligation for City Installed Project Improvements:

If the City installs a project improvement that is adjacent to undeveloped or
underdeveloped land then the property owner or developer of the undeveloped or
underdeveloped land, shall, prior to receiving approval, or a permit for any development activity
on the land, pay for its share of the cost of the improvements installed. The determination of the
share of the cost of the improvement shall be determined by resolution of the City Council and a
notice of the same shall be recorded against non-participation adjacent property owners. Nothing
in this section, however, shall require the City to make project improvements unless the City has
determined, by the City Council, to do so.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 14" day of August, 2019.

HERRIMAN CITY COUNCIL
