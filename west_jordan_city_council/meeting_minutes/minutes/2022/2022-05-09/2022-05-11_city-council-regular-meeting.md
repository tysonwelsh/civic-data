  7.    PUBLIC HEARINGS
        b. Accessory Dwelling Units Text Amendment
City Planner/Zoning Administrator Larry Gardner explained a proposed text amendment regarding
accessory dwelling units (ADUs) to require a property owner to reside on the property if renting
out an internal ADU. Mr. Gardner said he had learned some cities had included property owner
occupancy as part of the definition of internal ADU, and said the proposed amendment would be in
line with State Code. Mr. Gardner stated the Planning Commission reviewed and recommended
approval of the proposed text amendment.

Responding to a question from Council Member Pack, Mr. Gardner said an internal ADU would not
be assigned a separate street address from a primary residence. Responding to a question from
Council Member McConnehey, Mr. Gardner explained that if the proposed amendment passed, a
property owner with a secondary unit would be able to rent out a primary residence and secondary
unit together as a single rental unit to another party without owner occupancy.

Acting Chair Green opened a public hearing at 8:41 pm

Comments:
None

Acting Chair Green closed the public hearing at 8:42 pm

Council Member Pack said he would like to see a provision included for a temporary property owner
absence, perhaps for a period of three years. Council Member Jacob said he understood the desire
to align with the State update, but said he liked where the City ordinance was. He pointed out the
City ordinance would still be at odds with the State in terms of which unit a property owner was
able to occupy, and said he would prefer to remain not in compliance with the current topic of
discussion as well.

Council Member McConnehey said he would prefer City Code to simply reference State Code rather
than spell requirements out, especially if State requirements were going to change over time. Mr.
Gardner said staff would be able to make changes to the proposed ordinance and bring it back to
the Council without further Planning Commission review.

MOTION:       Chair Whitelock moved to approve Ordinance No. 22-21 amending the 2009 West
              Jordan City Code Section 13-5B-8 Accessory Dwelling Units.
              Acting Chair Green seconded the motion.

Chair Whitelock believed the City needed to look out for all property owners, including those who
purchased a single-family property because they wanted to live in a single-family neighborhood.
Council Member Pack commented that it was difficult to incorporate affordable housing measures
into single-family residential. He said he agreed the proposed amendments could use additional
revision.

Council Member Worthen said she believed the biggest issue with rentals was the possibility that a
renter would not take care of a property. She asked if it would be possible to stipulate that a
landscape company must be hired to take care of a rental property yard. Council Member Jacob said
he would vote against the proposed amendment. He said he had rented the home he lived in for
twelve years, and the home next door had been owner occupied by five different families in those
twelve years. He said he disagreed with the blanket idea that renters were not invested in and did
not take care of properties, and added there were good renters and bad renters, and there were
good property owners and bad property owners. Council Member Bloom said she agreed with
Council Members McConnehey and Jacob and would vote against the proposed amendment. She
said she would want to include a provision to allow temporary property owner absence. Council
Member Pack said he knew the City had participated in the Good Landlord program in the past and
asked if the program was still available.

Council Member Worthen commented that owner occupancy was already required by the State,
which would override City Code, so it did not really matter if the Council passed the proposed
ordinance. City Attorney Rob Wall said the same discussion the Council participated in had taken
place at the State level and in many cities, and said it was a worthwhile discussion. Acting Chair
Green said he believed City Code should be consistent with State Code.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Chair Kayleen Whitelock               Yes

Council Member Pack                   No
Council Member Worthen                Yes
The motion failed 3-4
