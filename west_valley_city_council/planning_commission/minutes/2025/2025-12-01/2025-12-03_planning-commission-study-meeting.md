PRESENTATIONS:

GPZ-6-2025 Petition by Braxton Wehrli requesting a General Plan change from Large Lot
Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre) and a zone
change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling
Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). The property is located at 4301 West
4570 South on 1.83 acres. (Staff-Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-
ut.gov).

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented. The applicant seeks to rezone 1.83 acres from large lot
residential to low density residential, to build six homes with an average lot size of 11,000
square feet. The existing home to the west of this lot will remain and be accessible through the
new cul-de-sac going in to the west of that lot. Before the next meeting, Steve is hoping the
applicant will provide additional information about the material standards that will be in the
development agreement.
Z-7-2025 Petition by Roderick Enterprises requesting a zone change from LI (Light
Industrial) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 2444 and 2476 South Anna
Caroline Drive, on 7.39 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented a request to rezone 7 acres from light industrial to
manufacturing to allow overhead doors on the west side of the building facing Mountain View
Corridor. The building would be for a fireworks distribution business. Steve noted that while
the light industrial zone allows industrial buildings, the Manufacturing zone would restrict
overhead doors on major arterial streets, thus making the zone change necessary for the
proposed use.


Z-8-2025 Petition by Wadsworth Development Group requesting a zone change from LI
(Light Industrial) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 5750 West 2300 South
on 6.4 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented a rezone from Light Industrial to Manufacturing for a
vacant 6-acre property, with plans to develop industrial buildings. The applicant plans to develop
the property for King Bee, a van upfitting company, which requires a zone change due to
restrictions on auto service and outside storage in the current LI zone. The proposed
development includes a new concrete building for upfitting and storage of vans, along with
necessary screening and landscaping along Mountain View Corridor and 2400 South. The city
manager signed an application to allow the rezoning of certain city-owned parcels, with the
understanding that the applicant would need to acquire UDOT-owned land. The city would
vacate the unused road going through the property if the rezoning is approved.


Z-9-2025     Petition by Cal Johnson requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture,
minimum lot size ½ acre) to C-2 (General Commercial). The property is located at 5459,
5477, and 5491 W 4100 S on 5.71 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented an application requesting a C-2 zone, which included plans
for medical offices, fast food, and drive-up retail. The C-2 zone requires a 6-foot masonry wall
when commercial use is adjacent to Residential use. But there is language in the Code that states
when future commercial use in neighboring residential or agricultural property is recommended
in the General Plan a waiver or substitution for the wall requirement may be approved by the
Planning Commission. The Code also states that if a drive-up is next to residential, the speaker
or the order board needs to be at least 50 feet from a residential property boundary. Thus, the

PUD-8-2025 Sam Drown is requesting preliminary approval for the Haven Ridge
Townhomes PUD. The proposed development will consist of 24 new townhomes and 1
commercial lot on 3.80 acres. The property is located at 3230 South Lester Street and is
zoned RM and C-2. (Staff Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Steve Lehman presented an application for a new subdivision behind a commercial
lot off Redwood Road. The new street will be a public right-of-way off Lester Street, installed
with curb, gutter and sidewalk. The PUD will have perimeter fencing, guest parking, a tot-lot, and
each townhome will have a two-car garage.


S-5-2025 Chelsea Clark, representing Liberty Homes, is requesting preliminary approval
for the Ashton Grove Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 12 lots on 4.0 acres. The
subdivision is located at 5990 West 3500 South and is zoned R-1-8 and A. (Staff Steve
Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Steve Lehman presented an application for a new subdivision on 2 large lots that
were recently rezoned. The existing home on the southernmost lot will remain and be zoned
agriculture, and 11 new single-family homes will be built on the remaining lots. Timeron Drive
will be extended into the subdivision and end in a cul-de-sac. The owner of the existing home
on lot 12 did come to an agreement with their neighbor to the west that they would use a shared
driveway. This will help when the city approaches UDOT to install a light at the intersection of
6000 West and 3500 South. Steve noted that there are a few nuances of extra land that need to
be worked out with existing residents and Public Works.


CA-3-2025 Petition by IRVA Development requesting a conditional use amendment to
add an Instructional Facility. The property is located at 5679 South 6055 West on 2.63
acres, and is zoned M, Manufacturing. (Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Brock presented an application to add an instructional facility, specifically a soccer
training facility, on a 2.63-acre property zoned Manufacturing. Brock explained that the facility
would be used strictly for practices by small groups, without bleachers or seating for games.
Operating hours would be Monday through Friday from 5:30 PM to 10 PM, Saturday from 12 PM
to 8 PM, and Sunday from 9 AM to 8 PM. The facility would have 16 parking stalls, with the
potential to add more if needed, and Brock suggested adding a condition that the bay doors be
kept closed after 6 PM to prevent noise pollution to neighboring residential properties.

C-21-2025 Petition by Woodward and Moody LTD requesting conditional use approval
for Outside Storage for Lots 5 and 6 of the Moody Subdivision. The properties are located
at 6773 West Moody Circle and 2242 S. Moody Drive on 2.73 acres, and zoned M,
Manufacturing. (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Jody presented a conditional use application for outside material storage on lots 5
and 6 of the Moody subdivision, located off Highway 201. Lot 5 would be surfaced with crushed
asphalt and Lot 6 has existing concrete from the previous tenant. These surfaces will be reviewed
during the application process ensuring a hard surface for material storage and rubber-tired
equipment. On the south side of Lot 6 there would be stormwater retention that would be lined
with gravel. There aren’t storm drain facilities in the area so one of the lots in the development
is a retention basin. The lots would be screened with slatted chain-link fences around the entire
yards. Landscaping would be placed along the 10-foot setback area fronting Moody Circle. Jody
noted that the lots were platted out to the center of the road.
