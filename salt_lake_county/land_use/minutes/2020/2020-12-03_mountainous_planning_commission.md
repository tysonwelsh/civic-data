LAND USE APPLICATION(S) 
Meetings began at – 4:08 p.m. 
31186 – (Continued from 11/05/2020) - Robert Asay is seeking Conditional Use approval for a short-term 
rental. Parcel Area: 0.37 Acres.  Location: 11174 East Dunyon Canyon Drive. Zone: FR-0.5.  Planner:  
Shad Cook (Motion/Voting) 
 
The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting 
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.  
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Shad Cook provided an analysis of the Staff Report. 
 
Commissioner Cohen said a condition says parking must remain on site. Mr. Cook said there was talk to 
add a third bedroom, but applicant decided to make it into a storage room so there is ample parking. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Applicant 
Name: Bob Asay 
Address: Not provided 
Comments: Mr. Asay said he had a neighbor that gave property to the forest service because he did not 
want to pay taxes on 25 acres. Owned the property for 40 years.  
 
Commissioner Cohen asked if applicant has seen the conditions listed in the staff report. Mr. Asay said he 
does not have any problems. Commissioner Jones asked if last month talked about adding compliance of 
fire constrictions as a condition. Mr. Shaw said we did have a discussion and researched the conditions on 
that issue, which need to be tied to regulations by government agencies and drafted a condition in the staff 
report, condition 4G. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED 
Motion: To approve application #31186 from staff report with staff recommendations. 
Motion by: Commissioner Reid 
2nd by: Commissioner Derr 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
