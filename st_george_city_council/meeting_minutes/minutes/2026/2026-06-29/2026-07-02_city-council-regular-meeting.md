CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to TrueNorth Steel for the George
        Washington Pedestrian bridge.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This item is to award a bid to provide a
          95-foot span, 10-foot wide, prefabricated pedestrian bridge for the Virgin River
          South Trail crossing over the future George Washington Boulevard at
          approximately 900 East. The City requested bids and two bids were received
          back. TrueNorth Steel is the low bidder in the amount of $157,298. Staff
          recommends approval.

      b. Consider approval to award bid to JP Excavating, Inc. for the Roadway
         Improvements at various locations (3430 East, Ezra Drive).

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This item is for roadway improvements
          at multiple locations to improve safety and drainage. The city requested bids and
          a total of five bids were received back. Of the five bids received, three bids were
          considered non-responsive as required information in bid had not been
          submitted. The apparent low bid was from JP Excavating, Inc in the amount of
          $462,322.62. Staff recommends award to JP Excavating, Inc. in the amount of
          $462,322,62.

      c. Consider approval of award of bid to Caliber Contractor, LLC for
         construction of Lizard Wash Park.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This item is to award a bid to construct a
          12-acre community park in the Desert Color area of the City to Caliber
          Contractor, LLC in the amount of $3,212,532.75. The Lizard Wash Park will
          include trails, restrooms, pavilions, parking lot, pedestrian bridge, large
          playground, bike park features, pickleball courts, sand volleyball court, and an
          open turf area. Formal bids were requested and the City received six bids. The
          low bid was received from Caliber Contractor, LLC in the amount of
          $3,212,532.75 which includes the Base Bid plus two Alternate Bids. The Lizard
          Wash Park is included in the FY 2027 Business Plan, is a 2023 Trails, Parks and
          Recreation G.O. Bond project, and is on the City’s Master Plan. Staff recommends
          approval.

      d. Consider approval of purchasing two pavilions via state contract with
         PlaySpace Designs for Lizard Wash Park.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This proposed purchase is to provide two
          pavilions for Lizard Wash Park. This purchase will include materials and delivery.
          One of the proposed pavilions is 30X50 and the second is 30X30. Both will be
          entirely made of steel. Staff received a quote from PlaySpace Designs via state
          contract for this purchase in the amount of $153,792.50. The Lizard Wash Park
          is included in the FY 2027 Business Plan, is a 2023 Trails, Parks and Recreation
          G.O. Bond project, and is on the City’s Master Plan. Staff recommends approval.

         contract with PlaySpace Designs for Lizard Wash Park.

      f.   Consider approval of the purchase of a 2026 Freightliner 10-ton Boom
           Truck for the Water Services Department.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This item is for the purchase of a 2026
           Freightliner 10-ton Boom Truck for the Water Services Department. This is a
           replacement of an existing 1987 Digger/Crane Truck that is past its useful life.
           This equipment is used to remove, replace, and maintain various large pumps
           and motors in the water distribution, irrigation, and wastewater divisions of the
           department. This piece of equipment is available from Custom Truck One Source
           through state contract in the amount of $291,287. Staff recommends approval.

      g. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Alliance
         Consulting, LLC for construction phase services for Lizard Wash Park.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Alliance Consulting, LLC recently
           completed the civil design portion of the construction documents and bid phase
           for Lizard Wash Park and now the project is ready to move on to construction.
           This proposed PSA with Alliance Consulting, LLC is to provide construction phase
           services for Lizard Wash Park. The Lizard Wash Park is included in the FY 2027
           Business Plan, is a 2023 Trails, Parks and Recreation G.O. Bond project, and is on
           the City’s Master Plan. Staff recommends approval.

         Department of Transportation for the billboard located on the east side
         of I-15 at milepost 5.06560.

           city use of the billboard with a new lease. Staff recommends approval.

           Utah Commission on Criminal and Juvenile Justice (CCJJ).

           Juvenile Justice (CCJJ) has awarded the Washington County Drug Task Force

         Tournament sponsored by the Dove Center, to be held February 26-27,
         2027.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City of St. George has partnered
           with the DOVE Center for the last 30 years to enhance community safety and
           wellbeing to thousands of St. George residents. The annual Dink with DOVE
           Pickleball Tournament creates a fun, inclusive community event that raises
           awareness for survivors of domestic violence and sexual assault. Funds raised
           through this tournament directly sustain DOVE Center's essential services,
           including housing, counseling, advocacy and prevention education for more than
           1,400 individuals each year.
