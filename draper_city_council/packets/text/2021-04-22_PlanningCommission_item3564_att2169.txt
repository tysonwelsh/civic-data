BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 3.92
acres located at approximately 11507 S Wildrye Field Way (Exhibit B). The property is
currently zoned R3, R4, and RA1. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be
approved to allow for Wildrye Field Way to be converted into a cul-de-sac.

Big Willow Creek is a 70.65 acre development that will consist of 256 dwelling units when it
is completed. The development first obtained approval in January 2017 for a development
agreement for 115 dwelling units on 49.75 acres. Since that time, the applicant has
acquired an additional 20.9 acres and amended the development agreement to include the



Big Willow Creek Ph. 1A 1st Amend. and Ph. 1B 2nd Amend.               App. # SUBD-1033-2020
Plat Amendment Request                                     1
additional property and an additional 141 dwelling units. The amended development
agreements were approved in April 2019 and August 2020.

Preliminary plat approval for 115 lots was approved by the Planning Commission on July
18, 2017. Phases 1A and 1B obtained final plat approval on September 18, 2018, with an
amended plat approved for Phase 1B on November 21, 2019. The Planning Commission
approved the Phase 2 Final Subdivision plat on August 27, 2020.

The overall development is accessed from two roads, Wildrye Field Way on the north and
to the south by Rolling Hills Way within the Jenson Farms Subdivision. The northern access
road was approved as part of Phase 1A and 1B on September 18, 2018. With the additional
property added to the project in 2019 and 2020, the applicant is proposing to shift the
access road to the east, into the Phase 6 boundary. On April 6, 2021 the City Council
approved a street vacation for a portion of Wildrye Field Way. The Zoning Administrator
approved a Minor Subdivision Plat on April 7, 2021 which approved the new access road to
the development from 11400 South. With that street vacation and new access road, the
remaining portions of Wildrye Field Way can now be converted to a cul-de-sac.

Comments received from the public have been included as Exhibit F of this report.

         R3:
         “The purpose of the R3 zone is to permit medium density residential development without
         special mitigation requirements. This zone is intended to provide incentives to foster
         residential development with little impact on its surroundings and on municipal services,
         and to generally preserve the semirural character called for in the density element of the
         general plan.”

         R4:
         “The purpose of the R4 and R5 zones is to permit medium to medium-high density
         residential developments with single-family homes. These zones are intended to foster
         thriving and well maintained neighborhoods that allow upscale single-family homes on
         smaller lots that require less maintenance and provide a balanced lifestyle.”

         RA1:
         “The purpose of the RA1 and RA2 zones is to foster low density development with little
         impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of
         the city's semirural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot
         family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The
         predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings,
         protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.”

The proposed cul-de-sac will be 100-feet wide with the non-cul-de-sac portion of the street
will be 60-feet wide, per Draper City’s public street standards. The street will continue to be
a public street. The portion of the cul-de-sac which has not already been dedicated to the
City will be dedicated with this plat.

The vacated portion of Wildrye Field Way will include a water line easement and a sewer
line easement for the utilities that have already been installed with the original
construction of the roadway. This will ensure protection and access for maintenance of
those utilities.

There is one property adjacent to Wildrye Field Way which is not part of the Big Willow
Creek development. This property is privately owned and includes a single-family home. An
access easement, and paved driveway, will be provided to the property from the new
access road from 11400 South. This will preserve the historic access for this property. The
property was included in the noticing boundary for the street vacation that was approved
by the City Council on April 6, 2021.
