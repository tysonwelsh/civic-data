                                       DRAFT
                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 21-19


A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING DEVELOPMENT TO PROCEED ON PROPERTY
OWNED BY SUBURBAN LAND RESERVE WITHOUT COMPLETING A ROADWAY
          CONNECTION TO SUNDAY DRIVE, RIVERTON, UTAH.


     WHEREAS, on February 11, 2021, the Riverton City Planning Commission considered
a Community Scale Plan, submitted by Suburban Land Reserve, Inc.; and

       WHEREAS, said Community Scale Plan proposes 365 residential units to be developed
on approximately 41 acres, from the northeast corner of 13200 South Street and 4570 West,
Riverton, Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the north boundary of the Community Scale Plan lies adjacent to the
southernmost point of a City roadway known as Sunday Drive, located at approximately 4400
West Street; and

        WHEREAS, said Community Scale Plan is not designed to connect with Sunday Drive,
but routes all roadways located within the Community Scale Plan to connect with 4570 West
Street;

       WHEREAS, during its review of the Community Scale Plan on February 11, 2021, the
Planning Commission correctly noted that Suburban Land Reserve Inc.’s proposal to not connect
with Sunday Drive should be reviewed and approved by the City Council because of the
implications associated with not connecting to Sunday Drive; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has carefully considered whether to allow the
Community Scale Plan without a connection to Sunday Drive, after receiving information from
representatives of Suburban Land Reserve, Inc. and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that not connecting with
Sunday Drive is in the public’s best interest;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:


   1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the Community Scale Plan,
      inasmuch as the Plan, once constructed, will not connect with Sunday Drive.

   2. This Resolution shall not be construed to grant full and final approval to development of
      the Community Scale Plan. Said Plan is remanded to the Planning Commission for
      further review, in a manner consistent with this Resolution.
