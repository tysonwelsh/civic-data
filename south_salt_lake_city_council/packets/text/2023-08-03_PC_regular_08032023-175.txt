 SYNOPSIS:
 On June 29, 2023 Kesia Zarate applied for a business license for a Category II Home Occupation to run a
 nail salon out of her home. Her business will have customers coming to her home, but no more than one
 (1) customer at any given time.

 The property provides ample off-street parking for customers. In addition to two (2) enclosed parking
 spaces, the home has a driveway that stretches forty (40) feet by twenty three (23) feet. According to the
 parking dimensions outlined in Chapter 17.06.160 of the South Salt Lake municipal code, a driveway of
 these dimensions accounts for four (4) off-street parking stalls.

CONDITIONAL USE REVIEW STANDARD:
Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-507(2) sets forth the following standard of review for conditional uses by stating:

    (a)     (i)    A land use authority shall approve a conditional use if reasonable conditions are
                   proposed, or can be imposed, to mitigate the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects
                   of the proposed use in accordance with applicable standards.
            (ii)    The requirement described in Subsection (2)(a)(i) to reasonably mitigate anticipated
                    detrimental effects of the proposed conditional use does not require elimination of the
                    detrimental effects.




                                                      3
    (b)        If a land use authority proposes reasonable conditions on a proposed conditional use, the land
               use authority shall ensure that the conditions are stated on the record and reasonably relate to
               mitigating the anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use.
    (c)        If the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of a proposed conditional use cannot be
               substantially mitigated by the proposal or the imposition of reasonable conditions to achieve
               compliance with applicable standards, the land use authority may deny the conditional use.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION:


Staff recommends the Planning Commission move to approve the application submitted by Kesia Zarate for
a Category II Home Occupation at 3209 S 400 E with the proposed conditions of approval based on the
following finds of fact and conclusions of law:

Findings of Fact:

   1. On June 29, 2023, the applicant applied for a Conditional Use Permit for a Category II Home Occupation
      for the dwelling at 3209 S 400 E.

   2. The purpose of the application is to permit the lawful operation of a nail salon at the subject property.

   3. A home occupation that involves customers and clients visiting the dwelling would be classified as a
      Category II Home Occupation per Title 17’s Land Use Matrix.

   4. SSLC Code Section 17.01 defines a Home Occupation, Category II as “any business carried on entirely
                                                        7
       within a Dwelling by occupants of such Dwelling that is clearly incidental and secondary to the
       Residential Use of the Dwelling, has no outside impacts on the neighborhood in which it is located, and
       does not change the character of the Dwelling or the neighborhood in which it is located. Home
       Occupation, Category II includes clients/customers going to the Dwelling.”

  5. The previous use of the subject property was a single-family dwelling without a Home Occupation as a
     secondary use.

  6. Redevelopment of a property requires all current municipal codes to apply to the proposal, including
     development standards, use standards, and design standards.

  7. The subject property is in the R-1 land use district.

  8. SSLC Code Section 17.03, classifies a Home Occupation, Category II as a Conditional Use in the R-1
     land use district, triggering the need for a Conditional Use Permit.

  9. The Planning Commission is the land use authority over all Conditional Use Permits.

  10. The subject property is 0.24 acres in size.

  11. The subject property is a legally subdivided lot.

  12. SSLC Code Section 17.06.040 requires, prior to issuance of a Building Permit, that all new Development
      activity be on a legal Lot.

  13. The subject property has vehicular and pedestrian access along 400 E.

  14. The subject property has 83 feet of frontage along 400 E.

Conclusions of Law:

  1.    The use, as specifically conditioned below, is consistent with the South Salt Lake City General Plan.

  2.    The use, as specifically conditioned below, is compatible in use, scale, and design with allowed uses in
        the R-1 land use district.

  3.    The use, as specifically conditioned below, does not compromise the health, safety, or welfare of persons
        employed within or using the proposed development nor those residing or working in the vicinity of
        the proposed use; nor does the proposed compromise Development, Property, or improvements in
        the vicinity of the proposed use or development.

  4.    The use, as specifically conditioned below, does not impose disproportionate burdens on the citizens of
        the city.

Proposed Conditions of Approval:

  1. The applicant shall retain one (1) off-street parking stall for client use.

  2. Applicant will conduct all business activity indoors as to not create any nuisance for surrounding
     property owners.

  3. Clients will be served by appointment only and within the hours of 9:00 am to 6:00 pm.

  4. Applicant must obtain a sign permit for any sign related to business activities.


                                                          8
   5. Applicant may only have one sign on the property related to business activities. Sign may not
      exceed two (2) square feet in area. Sign must be located on or against the home.
