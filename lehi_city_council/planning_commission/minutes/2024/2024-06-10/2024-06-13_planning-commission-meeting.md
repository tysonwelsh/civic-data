1. Consent Agenda
Commission Vice Chair Everett welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commission
Chair Jackson from the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and recommendation of the Lehi City, Strata Networks, and Gardner
Plumb LC. Development Agreement on 51.45 acres located at approximately 2500 West
Main Street. The proposed Agreement includes a change in the land use designation from
Very Low Density Residential Agricultural (VLDRA) to Public Facilities and Very Low
Density Residential (VLDR). (withdrawn)

The item was withdrawn by the applicant.

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Boyd Brown's request for review of the
Exchange Business Park Zone Change on 1.19 acres located at approximately 1500 North

3600 West, changing the zoning from R-3 (high density residential) to Neighborhood
Commercial.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Jake Hone, expressed the team's surprise at the need for rezoning to neighborhood
commercial and requested the commissioners' support to proceed with the site plan, and stated he
was available for questions.

Vice Commissioner Everett opened the public hearing.

Scott Larsen voiced concerns about increased traffic and the potential for people to cut through
their back alleyway if the zoning change to commercial was approved.

Marshall Gary, a leader of Strong Towns Lehi, was excited about nearby commercial
development but concerned it might become car-dependent. He advocated for pedestrian-friendly
design with parking behind or beside buildings to encourage foot and bicycle traffic, noting a
recent accident involving a teen in the area.

Cole Bolton expressed concerns about the impact of the commercial development on property
values, safety, and the operational hours of the businesses, particularly given its proximity to his
home and a nearby fire station.

Vice Commissioner Everett closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Eyre clarified that the commissioners were focusing solely on a zone change, not
the construction plans or concepts. The purpose of the meeting was to align the land use with the
general plan. Commissioner Eyre emphasized that the public's concerns had been heard and
would be considered in detail during the review of the final site plan.

Vice Commissioner Everett invited Mr. Hone back up to address any comments from the public.

Jake Hone stated the planning department previously shared the potential uses within the
neighborhood commercial zone, which generated excitement, particularly regarding dining
options, childcare, and small medical services. Feedback was incorporated into the site plan,
resulting in improvements such as better walking trails, a plaza area, extended bike trails with
green spaces, and additional walking connections to the nearby neighborhood. The goal was to
create a walkable area while ensuring the project's feasibility.

Luke Seegmiller clarified questions regarding a traffic study and potential traffic calming
measures, noting that while it wasn't directly related to the neighborhood commercial zone
change, it was connected to the overall concerns about the development.

        Motion: Commissioner Roberts moved to recommend approval of Boyd Brown’s
        request for review of the Exchange Business Park Zone Change on 1.19 acres
        located at approximately 1500 North 3600 West, changing the zoning from R-3
        (high density residential) to Neighborhood Commercial; including all DRC comments
        and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Lockart seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Roberts, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of an
amendment to the Lehi City Development Code Table 37.100, amending the requirements
for bicycle parking.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the item. He stated this amendment addressed specific
issues identified at the DRC level. There are concerns that arose regarding excessive bike
parking, which was often disproportionate to the size of buildings. Adjustments were proposed
for clubhouses, churches, and manufacturing establishments to better align parking requirements
with practical needs.

Vice Commissioner Everett opened public comment.

Marshall Gary was present to represent Strong Towns Lehi, which aimed to enhance Lehi's
appeal with outdoor amenities and transportation choices. Emphasizing a balanced approach, he
advocated for enjoyable driving, walking, biking, and public transit options. Mr. Gary expressed
pleasant surprise at initial plans for bike parking, noting his church currently lacked any bike
parking spots.

Vice Commissioner Everett closed the public comment.


Vice Commissioner Everett opened the public comment.

        Motion: Commissioner Kunze moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request
        for review of an amendment to the Lehi City Development Code Table 37.100,
        amending the requirements for bicycle parking; including all DRC comments and
        findings stated in the report, with the suggestion to change “shall” on number two.
        Commissioner Roberts seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Roberts, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.
