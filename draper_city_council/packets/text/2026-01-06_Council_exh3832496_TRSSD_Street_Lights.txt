TRSSD Street Lights
                    Base Services
Draper City is responsible for the maintenance of:
• Below ground electrical to the street lights
• Electrical external to the pole assembly
• Ensuring power is supplied to the poles
• Providing underground repairs and fuse replacements.
           TRSSD Financed Services
The TRSSD will pay Draper City for:
• Maintenance of street lights, including the poles, foundations, and
  electrical components within the poles
• Proactive approach to determining replacement needs
• To build a place to store the TRSSD street light parts – poles, lights,
  etc.
               Proposed Approach
• Draper City Public Works will take over all street light maintenance
  in the TRSSD as if they were City street lights
• A GL will be created for the TRSSD to annually deposit an amount,
  up front, for Public Works to perform the work that will be financed
  by the TRSSD
• The amount will be determined annually; with the initial years being
  larger amounts to cover proactive approach and
  infrastructure(storage) needs.
• Public Works will track all work through the asset
  management/work order system in place so that the TRSSD can
  review expenses upon request
