Senior City Planner, Jennifer Robison, reported that the proposed draft removes the public hearing
portion for Preliminary Plat approvals. They are administrative actions where the law will be applied.
Citizens often attend meetings with the expectation of influencing the application when in fact it is
an administrative item. Staff recommended the removal of the hearing requirement to improve the
process timeframe.

In response to a question raised regarding Preliminary Plat procedures, Mr. Crowell stated that
construction drawings will be finalized, web stamps placed on the drawing, and the mylar and final
survey documents prepared. Staff will also verify addresses and contact the County Surveyor and
Plat Department. Staff will use a checklist to ensure and verify that all items are complete.
Mr. Crowell reported that before 2019, the State Legislature also elevated the adoption of Standards
that require approval by the Legislative body. Should anything discretionary regarding creating
standards cause a burden or additional cost on a subdivider. Staff would present the Standard change
to the City Council and possibly the Planning Commission. When considering a Special District or
Infill Overlay where policy is involved, a Concept Plan will be presented as a zoning action.

Commissioner Flynn asked what will be included in public meetings moving forward. Mr. Crowell
stated that Legislative Actions including Zoning, General Plan recommendations, and required
Standards will necessitate Planning Commission review. A new appeal authority will be implemented
ifthe appeal is fi*om subdivision improvement plans. There is a process where the City would create
a three-expert panel to evaluate whether the application complies with those specifications. This
would take place after the Final Review and in instances where the applicant and City have not come
to an agreement. Mr. Springer stated that the City Engineer will involve a subject matter expert in
appeals. The applicant's engineer will also select a subject matter expert with the two selected persons
choosing a third participant together.

Changes to language within the amendment were discussed. Mr. Springer stated that caution should
be exercised when using the terms "shall" and "may." The difficulty with the term "shall" was that
if misused it can be abused in interpretation. He explained that "may" is permissive,"may not" is
prohibitive,"shall" is mandatory,"must" is discouraged, and "will" is automatic. He confirmed that
"may not" should replace "shall."

Coimnunity and Economic Development Director, Grant Crowell, reported that the proposed text
amendments before the Planning Commission relate to the Bluffdale City Code, Land Use, and
Subdivision Ordinances in various sections of Title 11 and Title 12 and other related Administrative
provisions. He introduced Jay Springer from Smith Hartvigsen who has been consulting with the
City on the incorporation ofnecessary amendments to become compliant with SB-174.

Mr. Springer explained that changes to the Bluffdale City Code were minimized while focusing
specifically on the requirements. SB-174 went into effect in May of2023 after the State's Legislative
Session. He began consulting with Bluffdale City in November 2023 when Technical Assistance
Funding became available. The main effects ofSB 174 included the following:

     • Removal of the City Council from certain subdivision requirements, notably to apply for
       single-family, two-family, and townhomes not in an identified geologic hazard area.

     • Staff will implement the same process for other subdivisions as well to eliminate two
         divergent processes that do not mesh.

     • Along with the presented Code updates, there are requirements taking place administratively
         behind the scenes to ensure that website requirements are in place and to review checklists for
         Preliminary and Final Plat Applications.

     • With the removal ofthe City Council, the Planning Commission can only be involved at the
         time ofPreliminary Plat approval.
