MAGNA
RESOLUTION 20-04-01
COVID-19 STATE OF EMERGENCY EXTENSION
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL EXTENDING
THE STATE OF EMERGENCY DECLARATION FOR COVID-19 NOVEL
CORONAVIRUS UNTIL JUNE 30, 2020

WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to
Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
governing body for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501; and

WHEREAS, Following the actions taken by Federal, State, and County Officials
declaring a 30-Day “State of Emergency” due to the infectious disease COVID-19 Novel
Coronavirus pandemic, the Mayor signed Magna’s 30-Day State of Emergency declaration on
March 16, 2020; and

WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code § 53-2A-208, a Declaration of a State of
Emergency can only be extended past 30 days by a resolution of the governing body; and

WHEREAS, On March 12, 2020 the Utah State Legislature passed HJR 24 extending
the Governors Declaration of a State of Emergency for COVID-19 until June 30, 2020; and

WHEREAS, On April 7, 2020 the Salt Lake County Council extended the County
Mayor's Declaration until July, 6, 2020; and

WHEREAS, Following the State’s lead and extending Magna’s State of Emergency for
COVID-19 to June 30, 2020 will avoid possible disruptions in service and relief efforts.

NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved, by the Magna Metro Township Council, the State
of Emergency declared by the Mayor on March 16, 2020, due to the infectious disease COVID-
19 Novel Coronavirus, is extended until June 30, 2020
