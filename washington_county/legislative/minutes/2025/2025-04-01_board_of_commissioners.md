WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
                               MEETING MINUTES
                                  APRIL 1, 2025

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on April 1, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah
Lloyd.

        INVOCATION BY: Major Bob Schmig, Salvation Army

       PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Gunnersmate Chief Jerry Curtis, US Navy

CONSENT AGENDA:

The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

   •   Consideration of Auditor-Approved Claims for Payment for March 19, 2025, through April
       1, 2025
   •   Consideration of Regular Commission Meeting Minutes of March 18, 2025
   •   Consideration of Administrative Adjustments: Personal Property
   •   Consideration of Administrative Adjustments: Real Property
   •   Consideration of Proof of Publication of Ordinances No. 2025-1284-0, Ordinance No.
       2025- 1285-0 and Ordinance No. 2025-1286-0, published on Sunday, March 23, 2025

Children's Justice Center (CJC) Director Kristy Pike explained that the CJC collaborates with

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3492, A RESOLUTION
APPOINTING A BOARD MEMBER TO THE WASHINGTON COUNTY PLANNING
COMMISSION

Community Development Director Scott Messel stated that Bryan Simonson resigned his position
as a Planning Commissioner. It is recommended that Olivia Anderson be appointed to fill the
remainder of his term to expire on January 31, 2026.

MOTION:        Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3492, A
               Resolution Appointing a Board Member to the Washington County Planning
               Commission. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
               unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3493, A RESOLUTION
AUTHORIZING THE WASHINGTON COUNTY SURVEYOR TO ADOPT AND
IMPLEMENT CHANGES AND ADDITIONS TO THE FEE SCHEDULE FOR SERVICES

Recorder Gary Christensen explained this Resolution recommends changes and additions to the
fee schedule for the Recorder/Surveyor office. Chief Deputy Recorder Michael Draper added that
the costs incurred by the County and community have been large, and this will offset those costs.

MOTION:        Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3493, A
               Resolution Authorizing the Washington County Surveyor to Adopt and
               Implement Changes and Additions to the Fee Schedule for Services. Motion
               seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
               Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3494, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A CONSTRUCTION AGREEMENT FOR A SHADE COVER AT THE SHOOTING
SPORTS PARK

Legacy Park Director Susi Lafele stated this shade cover will come off of Barry's Pavilion at the
Southern Utah Practical Shooting (SUPS) bay. Practical Shooting will pay $10,000; with
Washington County paying the remainder. There is some question if the amount is $10,000 or 25%
of the overall cost. Commissioner Iverson stated he will discuss numbers with Practical Shooting
to see if SUPS can contribute more money to come closer to covering 25% of the total cost.

MOTION:       Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3494, A
              Resolution Approving a Construction Agreement for a Shade Cover at the
              Shooting Sports Park. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried
              by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting
              aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3496, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT FOR RAVEN EGG OILING IN AND
AROUND THE RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

Habitat Conservation Plan Director Cameron Rognan explained one protection for the Mohave
Desert Tortoise is raven mitigation. Ravens are protected as a migratory bird species, and
Washington County has recently been granted a Raven Depredation Control Permit. It is
recommended that OmiLogic conduct raven egg oiling in and around the Red Cliffs Desert
Reserve.

MOTION:       Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3496, A
              Resolution Approving a Professional Services Agreement for Raven Egg
              Oiling in and Around the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve. Motion seconded by
              Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
              Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.
