Public Hearings, with Planning Commission recommendation to City Council for final action
2. Mountain America Redevelopment; 6761 South Redwood Road; Future Land Use Map

Amendment from Professional Office to Community Commercial and Rezone 1.29 acres
from P-O (Professional Office) Zone to SC-2 (Community Shopping Center) Zone;
Evergreen Devco, Inc./Derek Lis (applicant) [426702, 27725; parcel 21-22-403-001]
Matt Nielson, manager for Evergreen Devco, said they are under contract to purchase and
redevelop the property. Mountain America Credit Union does not need as many branches
considering the popularity of online and mobile banking, so they are relocating and/or closing
many of them. He reviewed the site location. They are asking for the SC-2 zone, which matches
the surrounding properties on Redwood Road. Also, there aren’t as many options for developing
with professional office zoning. The branch is scheduled to close in February or March 2023 and
then redevelopment will occur. With traffic counts above 40,000 per day, this change will
expand the amount of community commercial in the area. A landscaping buffer will provide
separation from the neighboring residential uses.
McKenna Marchant asked if the current structure will be demolished.

Matt Nielson said the building will be demolished because it is expensive to retrofit. The sample
site plan is just a concept because they don’t have a tenant at this point. But it will be a single
story building.
Staff recommended that the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the
City Council to amend the Future Land Use Map from Professional Office to Community
Commercial, and Rezone 1.29 acres from P-O to SC-2 located at approximately 6761 South
Redwood Road.
McKenna Marchant asked if the code requires buildings along Redwood Road to be pulled
toward the road so that parking is behind the building, which is what she would prefer.
Larry Gardner said that the code has a provision for parking in the back of the building. The
Planning Commission will review the site plan for preliminary approval.
Scott Langford explained that if this project moves forward then another public hearing would be
noticed and held to review the specific building design, site layout, and could possibly require a
conditional use permit.
Kent Shelton referred to an email the Commission received from Ted Manwill, a concerned
citizen, that suggested there is an ordinance requiring a minimum acreage for the SC-2 zone,
which this project does not meet.
Lisa Elgin said this request for the SC-2 zone would be added to the acreage of the adjacent SC-2
zone to the west in order to meet the minimum requirement.
Jay Thomas opened the public hearing.
Brennen Esch, West Jordan resident, said the other SC-2 zoning in the area has become overrun
with homeless persons and people under the influence of narcotics, and other issues. School
children have to walk along Redwood Road. He pointed out that this property connects to a
neighborhood, where the other SC-2 properties do not. With the uncertainty of what this property
will be used for, they do not know who will be congregating right next to a school bus stop.
Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.
McKenna Marchant asked if this property is within an RDA and if there are any plans for
redevelopment of the area.
Scott Langford said the RDA is on the west side of Redwood Road and he doesn’t know of any
plans to redevelop.
Ammon Allen understood the safety and aesthetics concerns and looked forward to that

MOTION: Ammon Allen moved, based on the analysis and findings contained in this
staff report, and upon the evidence and explanations received today, to

and Rezone 1.29 acres from P-O to SC-2 located at approximately 6761

and passed 5-1 in favor with Kent Shelton casting the negative vote. Trish
Hatch was absent.

It was explained that the item will be decided by the City Council at a future meeting, which will

be noticed to the public.
