MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper, AICP
Date: 2026-03-26
Re: Public Hearing: Bangerter Crossroads Development Agreement Request (Legislative
Item)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement for approximately
17.88 acres located on the east side of Bangerter Highway, north of 13800 South, and
west of 300 East, at approximately 13782 S. 300 E. The property is currently zoned RA1
(Residential Agricultural). The applicant is requesting that the Development Agreement
be approved to allow for the development of the overall site as a commercial center.

Findings for Approval:

  1. The Development Agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and (D).

Findings for Denial:

  1. The Development Agreement does not comply with all required elements of DCMC
     Section 9-5-200(C) or (D).
  2. The Development Agreement as proposed is not in the best interest of the city.
