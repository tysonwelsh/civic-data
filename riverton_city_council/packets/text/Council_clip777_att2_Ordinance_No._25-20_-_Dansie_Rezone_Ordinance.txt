                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 25-20

     AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN TO LOW DENSITY
RESIDENTIAL AND REZONING APPROXIMATELY 2.32-ACRES TO THE R-3 ZONE
 FOR PROPERTY LOCATED NEAR 3100 WEST 13400 SOUTH. APPLICANT- ALAN
                      PRINCE OF MP&G LLC.

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made A recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment;
and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the General Plan designation to Low Density Residential and the Riverton City
Zoning Map from to R-3 for the approximately 2.32 acre parcels located at approximately 3100
West 13400 South.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
            Section 1.     The Riverton City General Plan and Zoning Map shall be,
                           and hereby is, amended to reflect the described changes as
                           shown in Exhibit “A”

              Section 2.             This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 15th day of
July, 2025 by the following vote:
