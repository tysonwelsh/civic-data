RESOLUTION NO. 20-02-01                                                    Date: February 6, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       RATIFYING THE APPOINTMENT OF PAULINA F. FLINT AS WHITE CITY’S
       MEMBER ON THE BOARDS OF TRUSTEES OF THE UNIFIED POLICE
       DEPARTMENT AND SALT LAKE VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE
       AREA

                                           RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §
      10-2a-401 et seq.



                                                3
DATE           THURSDAY                             FEBRUARY                               6, 2020

   B. White City is part of the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (“SLVLESA”), a
      local district, and the Unified Police Department (“UPD”), an interlocal entity consisting of
      various municipalities and Salt Lake County.

   C. White City has the right and legal responsibility to appoint a member to the Board of
      Trustees for SLVLESA and to the governing body of UPD.

   D. At its January 2, 2020 meeting the White City Metro Township appointed D. Scott Little as
      White City’s member of the Board of Trustees of SLVLESA and the governing body of
      UPD.

   E. Subsequently, it was determined Mr. Little could not accept the appointment and it became
      necessary to find a replacement.

   F. Paulina F. Flint, Mayor of White City Metro Township, accepted the appointment, and
      confirmed, through legal counsel polling the remainder of the White City Metro Council,
      that no one else on the Counsel was ready, willing and able to accept the appointment.

   G. The appointment was communicated to UPD and SLVLESA and Mayor Flint has already
      started the appointment.

   H. Based on the foregoing, the White City Metro Township Council, as the legislative body of
      White City elected by the voters of White City, believes it is in the best interest of White
      City to formally ratify by resolution the appointment of Paulina F. Flint as White City’s
      member of the Board of Trustees of SLVLESA and the governing body of UPD.

                                         RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the Council
formally ratifies the appointment of Mayor Paulina F. Flint to serve as White City’s member of the
Board of Trustees of the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area and governing body of
the Unified Police Department; and

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED the Council will provide a copy of this Resolution to the Salt
Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area and Unified Police Department to inform them of the
Council action.

         APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this 6th day of February, 2020.

Public Hearing – Title 5: “Business Licenses and Regulations”

               Paul Ashton reviewed the draft ordinance amending Title 5 – Business Licenses
and Regulations by adding public nuisance, crime-magnet, and general welfare justifications for
revoking a business license and providing greater due process for business license suspensions
and revocations. A public hearing will need to be set for March 5, 2020 at 7:00 p.m. time certain.
The notice of hearing will be published in the newspaper.

               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Dickerson, moved to set the
public hearing for March 5, 2020 at 7:00 pm. The motion passed unanimously.

South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                Council Member Price stated the process to hire seasonal employees has begun,
they will start in April and May. The pay will be $12.00 an hour after training is complete.
Employees will be cross trained to allow for more help. The Board is keeping the same officers.
Conferences are coming up and board members are encouraged to go; however, a limit was set
on who can go because it is expensive.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

                Mayor Flint stated the GSLMSD is fully staffed. The new Chief Financial Officer
will start this month. The Board is discussing the Olympia Hills development. The GSLMSD
planning staff have been working on the development and will continue to do so. The developer
will have to create a separate infrastructure for sewer and water. The development will take 20
years to be completed.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

               Council Member Dickerson stated the Board discussed utilization of its facilities.
Hiring and retaining employees has been difficult. There have been discussions about increasing
wages but there is not a lot that can be done. There may be a fee increase for start-up costs for
new homes, the cost would go from $50 to $100.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Salt Lake County Animal Services Advisory Board

                Council Member Little stated Animal Services is drafting an ordinance that it
would like the Council to adopt. He has concerns about the language giving Salt Lake County the
ability to change laws for the metro township.

                Paul Ashton stated the ordinance can be adopted as a template, but it would have
to notify the Council of any changes and the Council will determine if it will adopt the changes.

Council of Governments (COG)

Legislative Update

The League of City and Towns have agreed to work with the metro townships to get them full

as a full member.

              Mayor Flint stated the metro township services are being paid for by sales tax.
There is a concern with on-line sales for ski lift tickets and other items that are being sold without
charging sales tax. The legislature will need to look into this.
