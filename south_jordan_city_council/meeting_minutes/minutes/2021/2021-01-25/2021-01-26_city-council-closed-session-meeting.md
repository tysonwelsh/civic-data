                                     SOUTH JORDAN CITY
                      CITY COUNCIL CLOSED SESSION ELECTRONIC MEETING

                                              January 26, 2021

Present:      Mayor Dawn R. Ramsey, Council Member Patrick Harris, Council Member Brad Marlor,
              Council Member Jason McGuire, Council Member Don Shelton, Council Member Tamara
              Zander, CM Gary Whatcott, ACM Dustin Lewis, City Attorney Ryan Loose, CFO Sunil Naidu,
              IT Director Jon Day, City Recorder Anna Crookston

Others:       Attendance electronically via Zoom

5:30 PM
REGULAR MEETING

       A. Welcome, Roll Call and Introduction: By Mayor Dawn R. Ramsey

Mayor Ramsey welcomed everyone present. She introduced the electronic meeting.

       B. Invocation: By Council Member, Jason McGuire

Council Member Jason McGuire offered the invocation.

Council Member Marlor made a motion to recess the City Council meeting and go into a closed executive
session to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property. Council Member McGuire seconded
the motion. The vote was unanimous in favor.

CLOSED EXECUTIVE SESSION – Discussion of the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property.

Council Member McGuire made a motion to adjourn the closed session and return to the open City
Council meeting. Council Member Shelton seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous in favor.

The City Council reconvened in an open City Council meeting.

ADJOURNMENT

Council Member Zander made a motion to adjourn. Council Member Shelton seconded the motion. The vote
was unanimous in favor.

The January 26, 2021 City Council closed electronic meeting adjourned at 7:18 p.m.

This is a true and correct copy of the January 26, 2021 City Council Meeting Minutes, which were approved
on February 16, 2021.
