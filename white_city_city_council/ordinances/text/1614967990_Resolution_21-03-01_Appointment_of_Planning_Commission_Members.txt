RESOLUTIONNO.: 2 1-03-01 DATE: March 4, 2021
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO COUNCIL
APPROVING THE APPOINTMENT OF CHRISTOPHER
SPAGNUOLO AND CHRISTY SEIGER-WEBSTER AS PLANNING
COMMISSION MEMBERS FOR A THREE-YEAR TERM ENDING
FEBRUARY 28, 2024
RECITALS
A. The White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq.
B. Pursuant to Ordinance 17-06-02, Planning Commission Members are appointed by the Metro
Township Council to serve three-year terms;
C. On this date, the White City Metro Council desires to appoint Christopher Spagnuolo and Christy
Seiger-Webster as Planning Commission members with a term ending February 28, 2024.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the
Council approves the appointment of Christopher Spagnuolo and Christy Seiger-Webster as
Planning Commission Members for White City with the term ending February 28, 2024.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
4h day of March 2021.
