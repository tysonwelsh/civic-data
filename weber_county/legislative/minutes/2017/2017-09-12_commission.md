          Jared Andersen, County Engineer, stated that the design group had also performed a traffic analysis. As
          the county tried to implement that recommendation, there were a tremendous amount of complaints from
          residents and the county decided to obtain a second opinion from a traffic engineering firm. The
          procurement process was followed and Hales was the low bidder. Chair Ebert noted the importance of not
          leading/directing the firm to a specific finding due to potential liability reasons.
          Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Hales Engineering for the 1800 S. and 3500
          W. four-way stop study; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
          Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye
     3. CONTRACT WITH UTAH YOUTH RODEO ASSOCIATION FOR RODEO EVENTS AT THE GOLDEN SPIKE
        EVENT CENTER (GSEC) DURING THE 2017-2018 SEASON.
          Duncan Olsen, with the GSEC, presented this contract for the Golden Spike and the Outdoor Arena.
          Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Utah Youth Rodeo Association for rodeo
          events at the Golden Spike Event Center for the 2017-2018 season; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
          Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye

    7. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO WESTERN WEBER PARK DISTRICT. RESOLUTION 35-2017.
         Stacy Skeen, of the Commission Office, noted that the statutory publication/noticing process had been
         followed for this newly created district. There were 9 applicants. There are five positions for 4-year
         terms, with the exception of this first appointment—2 positions for two years (through 12/31/2019), 3 for
         four years (through 12/31/2021). Chair Ebert expressed thanks for the tremendous amount of work for a
         unified vision in those areas. Commissioner Gibson had spoken with each applicant and all happy to
         serve in whatever capacity needed. Christopher Crockett addressed Commissioner Gibson’s application
         to this board, and because he resides within the district and submitted an application he qualifies.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 35-2017 appointing Kerry Gibson (West Weber),
         Kathleen Vernieuw (West Weber), Heather Marriott (Warren), Jim Wayment (Warren) and Dwayne
         Hansen (Taylor) to the Western Weber Park District; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye
         After Commissioner Harvey drew the names of Jim Wayment and Heather Marriott he moved to appoint
         them to 2-year appointments; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye
    2. DISCUSSION TO VACATE THE RETENTION BASIN EASEMENT ON LOTS 4, 5, 6 AND 14 OF MALLARD
       SPRINGS SUBDIVISION AT APPROXIMATELY 2475 SOUTH 4000 WEST
         Chair Ebert noted that the public hearing for this item is set for next week but anyone present could make
         comments. Ronda Kippen, of County Planning, stated that when excavation commenced on the rear of
         the four lots for the retention basin they filled with water, were not draining and were a nuisance. There
         is a high ground water issue in this subdivision. County Engineering has been working with the applicant
         to relocate this storm water facility. It will be located within Cameron Cove Subdivision (not yet ready
         for recording). Because many of the lots have been sold, the vacation will be done by ordinance,
         however, prior to its recording, all of the applicable easements must be in place and recorded so that the
         storm water can be relocated to Cameron Cove, and the water will then be released at a gradual rate. The
         county had not wanted to move this application further until the new detention basin had been installed,
         which now has been done. This item was published, posted and notice mailed to residents within 500
         feet. Chair Ebert invited public comments and none were offered.
