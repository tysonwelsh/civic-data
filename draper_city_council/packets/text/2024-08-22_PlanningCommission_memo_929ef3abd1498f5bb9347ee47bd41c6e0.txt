MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Nick Whittaker
Date: 2024-08-22
Re: Public Hearing: Painted Pretty Nail Lounge LLC Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
Request (Administrative Action)



Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Conditional Use Permit for approximately 0.37 acres
located on the east side of Inauguration Rd at approximately 11568 S. Sweet Grass Ct.. The
property is currently zoned R3 (Residential, 13,000 ft2 min. lot size). The applicant is requesting a
conditional use permit to operate a personal care service home occupation that provides
manicures.


Finding for approval:

  1. The proposal complies with the standards for approval found in DCMC Section 9-5-080(E) and
      potential negative impacts are mitigated through the imposition of reasonable conditions.

Finding for denial:
   1. The proposal does not comply with all standards for approval found in DCMC Section 9-5-
      080(E) and potential negative impacts are not able to be sufficiently mitigated through the
      imposition of reasonable conditions.
