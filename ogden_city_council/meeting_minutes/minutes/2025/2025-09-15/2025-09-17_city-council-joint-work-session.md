          The purpose of the Joint Work Session was for the Council and Planning Commission to receive annual land use training
specific to Housing and Land Use in Utah: Requirements, Best Practices, and Current Trends; and to discuss items of joint
interest.

Annual Training – Housing and Land Use in Utah: Requirements, Best
Practices, and Current Trends
        Community and Economic Development Executive Director Johnson, Planning Director Brierley, Assistant City
Attorney Tanner, and Senior Policy Analyst Burton used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide training focused on the
Utah housing climate, including the following:
            • Fair Housing Laws and Best Practices.
            • Housing Affordability, Moderate Income Housing Requirements for cities, Station Area Plans.
            • Incentives like FHIZ (First Home Investment Zone), HTRZ (Housing and Transit Reinvestment Zone), and
                 HOPZ (Home Ownership Promotion Zone).
            • Statewide perspective, examples of relevant housing projects throughout the state.
        For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
