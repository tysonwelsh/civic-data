          Davidson)

          Carol Davidson – They already have 8 drive up stalls designated now for pick up. They will add 4
          spaces to that section and then 12 at the row above for a total of 24 spots. They will need to remove
          4 additional stalls and a tree to install this canopy. The new spaces will take out 16 spaces for the in-
          store shoppers. Carol showed pictures of the parking lot at about 11:00 am on a Tuesday, all the
          spaces were full. The staff has a concern with the location of the spaces. Carol showed pictures of
          Wal-Mart and Smith’s where the drive-up locations are located on the sides leaving the front spaces
          for the people who shop in store. In looking at this location this parking lot, staff does recommend

   2. LANDMARK DESIGNATION (HPC) Administrative

      Consider a request to designate the home at 295 South Main Street as a landmark site to be known as the
      Miner Heber Prisbrey Home Case No. 2022-HPC-002 (Staff – Carol Davidson)

      Carol Davidson presented the following:

      Carol Davidson – Carol showed a picture of what the house looks like right now. The house was built
      between 1913 and 1914. It previously was on our local Landmark Designation registry. In 2015 the
      current owners purchased the home, and they didn’t realize it was on the registry. They made some
      changes and then they came in to get a permit and found out it was on the registry. So, they did go to
      the Historic Preservation Commission. At that time, they had already changed the roof and the
      commission made a decision that some things needed to be changed, the things weren’t changed, so they
      were removed from the list. So now they are asking to be put back on. Carol showed pictures of the
      roof and the gabled dormer that replaced the hipped dormer. This wraparound porch was not on the
      home when this was presented to the Landmark Commission in 2015. They brought it before them to
      ask to put the porch on. Carol read the motion that was made in 2015, the motion is in the packet. The
      items didn’t get changed so the house was removed from the Landmark Designation. The applicants
      continued to make updates to the home to bring it up to code. So, they brought it back to see if the home
      could be put back on the Landmark registry. Carol described the criteria that must be met according to
      code. I do want to mention that the wrap around porch is not attached to the house, it stands separately
      from the house. That was brought up when the applicant brought this to the commission a few months
      ago. Carol continued to describe what must be met according to code. We did bring this to the Historic
      Preservation Commission their recommendation was denial stating that it does not meet the required
      criteria and intent of the local landmark requirements found in the St. George zoning regulations. I just
      want to point out that the last part of this sentence is what they felt it didn’t meet. They felt the
      additions which significantly detract or obscure the original form and appearance from the building or
      structure when viewed from the public right of way. I apologize I don’t have the minutes from that
      meeting they have not been approved yet.

      Commissioner Draper – Was it the wraparound porch that was not?

      Carol Davidson – They didn’t like the roof, they hadn’t put the wraparound porch yet, but they showed
      them what they wanted to do, and the previous landmark commissioners did not think that matched the
      historical character of the home.

      you would walk in it would creak.

      Mr. Potter read the entire document.

      Lynn Potter – A Landmark home will allow light commercial. Currently it has no commercial status.
