                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                                  May

Our total calls increased dramatically during the last couple of months and that was due to a change
recently implemented by the Valley Emergency Communications Center. For billing purposes, it
was determined some agencies were including traffic stops in their total calls number and others
were not. The Board of Trustees wanted to ensure consistency, so a decision was made to include
traffic stops for all agencies and that was something we were not doing prior to April. That is what
accounts for the change in total calls and we expect that number to be more consistently higher
moving forward.

All other statistical categories remained relatively constant. We did have our first entry in the
“Homicide” category, which may cause some alarm. This case was a domestic situation where a
former boyfriend of a female resident came to her home and attacked her new boyfriend and
stabbed him in the chest with a knife. The suspect then fled to California and we are in the process
of securing a warrant for his arrest and we will then extradite him back to Utah. He has been
charged with attempted murder, and thankfully, the victim has fully recovered from his injuries.
