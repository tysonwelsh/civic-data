MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 20-06-03 DATE: June 23, 2020
| A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
| COUNCIL ADOPTING THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL
SERVICES DISTRICT EMERGENCY OPERATIONS PLAN
(EOP) AS MAGNA’S EMERGENCY OPERATIONS PLAN
| WHEREAS, an Emergency Operations Plan (“EOP”) can reduce the
| vulnerability of citizens and community of the Magna Metro Township (“Magna” or
| “Municipality”), a member-entity of the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
| (“GSLMSD” or “MSD”), to loss of life, injury, damage and destruction of property
arising from natural, technological, or human-caused emergencies and disasters or
during hostile military or paramilitary actions; and
WHEREAS, the MSD has developed and adopted an EOP to address the need of
the member-entities of the District whereby the member-entities can adopt the MSD’s
EOP as their own entity’s EOP, together with annexes, if any; and
WHEREAS, an EOP will assist Magna prepare for prompt and efficient response,
and recovery from, emergencies and disasters, including using all systems, plans and
resources necessary to preserve the health, safety and welfare of persons affected by
an emergency; and
WHEREAS, an EOP will further help Magna with recovering from
emergencies and disasters by providing for the rapid and orderly restoration and
rehabilitation of persons and property affected by emergencies; and
WHEREAS, an EOP provides an emergency management system encompassing
all aspects of pre-emergency preparedness and post emergency response, recovery
and mitigation; and
WHEREAS, Magna desires to adopt the MSD’s EOP as the Magna EOP in the
interest of public health, safety and general welfare.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:
Section 1. Provision of emergency management services. The Greater Salt

Lake Municipal Services District, through an Interlocal contract with the Unified Fire
Authority, provides emergency management services to the member-entities of the
MSD, which includes Magna.
Section 2. Adoption of the National Incident Management System
(NIMS). The Magna Metro Township hereby adopts the National Incident Management
System (NIMS) as a framework to integrate and coordinate the emergency response and
recovery actions of all levels of government.

Section 3. Adoption of an Emergency Operations Plan. The Magna Metro
Township Council hereby adopts the Emergency Operations Plan previously
adopted by the MSD as the Magna EOP and pursuant to the EOP Plan and
documents received by the Council from the MSD.
