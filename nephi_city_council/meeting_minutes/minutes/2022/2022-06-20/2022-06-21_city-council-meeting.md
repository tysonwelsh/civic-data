NORTHRIDGE PHASE C DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson updated the council that the Planning Commission had held a public
hearing and recommended a zone change for the Northridge Phase C development. This portion of the
development would consist of mixed commercial and residential uses. The developer had requested a
Highway Commercial (HC) zoning adjacent to Main Street with an R-3 development for lots on the south
side of 1500 North and an R-3 zoning with a PUD overlay for the portion of the development east of the
commercial piece.
   The Planning Commission expressed some concern over the broad nature of the R-3 zoning and
would like to see the portion of the property east of the commercial section be a Planned Unit
Development. The Council also expressed their desire for the same thing. The developer agrees with
the Council on the proposed zoning. The attached development agreement would require a PUD on the
portion east of the commercial and allow for R-3 on the section south of 1500 North.

RESOLUTION 06-21-22-A -WASTEWATER PLANNING PROGRAM RESOLUTION ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council the annual wastewater planning self-assessment report. The
report helps the sewer department identify deficiencies and problem areas within the city. The City is
required to submit this annual report to the department of environmental quality (DEQ) and more
specifically a sub-group of DEQ known as the Water Quality Board each year for their review. As part of
that review DEQ requires that the City Council review the Municipal Wastewater Planning Program Self-
Assessment Report for 2021 and confirm that all appropriate actions have been taken to maintain
effluent requirements in accordance with the Utah Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (UPDES)
Permit.
  While completing the annual self-assessment report no deficiencies were identified for the Nephi City
Sewer System. Councilor Parady moved to adopt Resolution 06-21-22-A-Wastewater Planning Program.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
