MPO Regional Planning Committee March 5, 2020 Page 2 Transportation Improvement Program Modification Vineyard Rail Realignment UDOT Region 3 and the Town of Vineyard are requesting Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Modification for the Vineyard Rail Realignment. The funds to be used on the project are a Consolidated Rail Infrastructure and Safety Improvements (CRISI) Program federal grant funds and Vineyard City funds, neither of which are administered by UDOT or MAG. The project must be added to the TIP to allow UDOT to manage the project. The Vineyard Rail Consolidation Project will improve freight rail operations in Vineyard and Orem, by streamlining routes, consolidating switching, increasing safety by reducing the number of at-grade crossings, and improving overall freight operations through a fast- growing urban area. This project will abandon 2 miles of the Provo Industrial Lead track that currently runs along Geneva RD in Vineyard from approximately Orem Center ST to 1600 N and construct new leads from the Provo Subdivision tract that runs parallel to the Provo Industrial Lead to the west. Vineyard Local Funds $10,000,000 CRISI Federal Grant 6,839,292 Total Funds 16,839,292 Vineyard will be responsible for any cost overruns. The following RPC Members emailed their approval for this agenda item to Melanie Haws: Mayor Steve Leifson Mayor Brad Frost Mayor Richard Brunst Mayor Jenney Rees Mayor Wendy Pray Mayor Westmoreland moved that the TIP be modified to add The Vineyard Rail Consolidation Project at $16,839,292 to the TIP. Mayor Larson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO Regional Planning Committee March 5, 2020 Page 3 Updates Federal Highway Administration: None.
