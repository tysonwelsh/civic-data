PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on June 12, 2014 at 7:00 p.m. in the Community Center Council Chambers, 13011 South Pioneer Street (6000 West) Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 14-27 banning the use of fireworks and any open flame fires within certain areas of Herriman, Utah. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder’s Office during regular business hours. 
/s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
