3.9) Public hearing and recommendation of GWC Capitals’ request for preliminary
subdivision review of Vistas at the Point Phase 1, a 364-unit residential development
located at approximately 3000 West Traverse Mountain Boulevard in a TOD zone.
Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Jared Morgan with Geneva Rock, was present. He stated they feel they need to
continue to enhance the trail system.

Will need to listen to this item in depth.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Heather Muslusky stated she is a representative for the group of the hang gliders that were
present at the meeting. There were approximately 50 others that she was speaking for. She stated
she doesn't expect there to not be any development, but she is concerned about the landing zone.
This is the most active and consistent site that she has been to and improper planning could
impact the landing zone. She would like a wind study to be done. She stated there is a south site
and the LZ is north of the Flight Road. She stated this is a State Park. Commissioner Nielsen
asked what responsibility they have with it being a Stated Park. She stated the Flight Park road is
concerning where it meets the Frontage Road and asked if it was going to be paved.

Jared Morgan stated the State owns part of Flight Park road, Lehi City owns part of it, Geneva
Rock, Staker. There are four owners of Flight Park road. He stated the intent of the road is
utilization for water trucks but is planned for access of the development. They commissioned to
have a wind study done, taking it into account.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.
    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to send a positive recommendation of GWC
    Capitals’ request for preliminary subdivision review of Vistas at the Point Phase 1, a
    364-unit residential development located at approximately 3000 West Traverse
    Mountain Boulevard in a TOD zone; including the DRC comments and findings stated in
    the report, the request for adequate parking and to meet 10% open space, to complete the
    wind flow study that has been started, to meet traffic and wind calming measures, and to
    meet future TOD code for future growth and use. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
    Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
