                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 23-56

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
 WITH REDOUBT RESTORATION TO COMPLETE THE JORDAN RIVERBANK
ACCESS PROJECT AND AN AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE OF UTAH FOR THE
                UTAH OUTDOOR RECREATION GRANT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Purchasing bid out the Jordan River Access Project and the
bid came in over budget; and

       WHEREAS, the City received a Utah Outdoor Recreation Grant Agreement providing
$150,000.00 for the Jordan River Access Project; and

        WHEREAS, the grant money from the state will have to be received as revenue and then
transferred into the project’s budget through a budget adjustment; and

       WHEREAS, by approving this agreement, Council will need to approve $362,463.50 as
an expense and $150,000.00 as incoming revenue in a future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Redoubt
          Restoration to complete the Jordan Riverbank Access Project and an agreement with
          the State of Utah for the Utah Outdoor Recreation Grant.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 18th day of
July 2023 by the following vote:
