                           Riverton High School Varsity Soccer Day

WHEREAS, Riverton City prides itself on student success and excellence in education and
sports; and

WHEREAS, Riverton High School has a stellar winning tradition on and off the field; and

WHEREAS, The Riverton High School Girls Varsity Soccer team held a season winning record of
19-1 and brought home a prestigious 6-A state championship; and

WHEREAS, This special group of players will always be remembered for their athletic success
and outstanding representation of Riverton City; and

NOW, THEREFORE, I Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton, Utah, do hereby proclaim November 16,
2021, as Riverton High School Varsity Soccer Day and call upon all citizens of Riverton City to
congratulate Riverton High School’s Girls Varsity Soccer team on their state championship.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 16th day of November 2021.
