CONSIDERATION _ OF ORDINANCE NO. _2021-1170-O, AN ORDINANCE

ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY LAND USE REGULATION (MORATORIUM)

ON__ APPLICATIONS, APPROVALS OR PERMITTING OF RURAL
RECREATION GROUNDS AND FACILITIES IN ALL UNINCORPORATED

AREAS OF WASHINGTON COUNTY

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales explained that regulations need to be reevaluated
in regards to Rural Recreation Grounds and Facilities in all unincorporated areas of
Washington County. The ordinance will establish a temporary regulation and shall only
remain in effect for six months, until August 16, 2021, related to all Rural Recreation
Grounds and Facilities in all unincorporated areas of Washington County.

No new applicants for Planned Development Rural Recreation Grounds and Facilities shall
be processed in any unincorporated area in Washington County while this temporary
regulation is in effect.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2729, A RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING __ EXPENDITURES _OF | FUNDS _ COLLECTED __AS

WASHINGTON COUNTY'S SHARE OF THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND
PARKS (RAP) SALES TAX

RAP Tax Board Chairman Robert Schmidt explained that applications are collected in
September and reviewed by the RAP Tax Advisory Board over several months.
Recommendations of funding amounts are determined and recommended to the
Commissioners based upon qualifying criteria under the RAP Tax guidelines. The Board
is recommending a total of $584,000 for various projects submitted by twenty-nine

qualified (29) applicants.

(See attached funding spreadsheet)

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-
2729, A Resolution Appropriating Expenditures of Funds Collected as
Washington County's Share of the Recreation, Arts, and Parks Sales
Tax. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and Iverson

voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2021-2730, A RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING __ EXPENDITURES OF _ FUNDS _ COLLECTED _ AS

WASHINGTON COUNTY'S SHARE OF THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND
PARKS SALES TAX FOR UNINCORPORATED WASHINGTON COUNTY

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that the as part of the RAP Tax
collection established via ballot in 2014, Washington County receives a portion of funding
to be used in the unincorporated areas for recreation, arts, and parks. Applications are
collected at the end of each year, wherein, identified projects are prioritized by the
applicant. A total of $113,679.52 was recommended for various projects submitted by six
(6) qualified applicants.

Ms. Felshaw stated that a slight adjustment of $133.42 to the Diamond Valley award is
recommended in order to cover the cost of the project.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2730, A
Resolution Appropriating Expenditures of Funds Collected as
Washington County's Share of the Recreation, Arts, and Parks Sales
Tax for Unincorporated Washington County with the Amendment to
Diamond Valley Amount. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox,
and Iverson voting aye.
