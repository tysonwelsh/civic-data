                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-61

  A RESOLUTION TO APPROVING AN AGREEMENT WITH THE UNIFIED FIRE
 SERVICE AREA DETAILING THE TERMS OF RIVERTON CITY’S WITHDRAWAL
                      FROM SAID SERVICE AREA

        WHEREAS, Riverton City obtains fire protection, paramedic, and emergency response
services (known as “First Responder Services”) from Unified Fire Authority, under the terms of
an Interlocal Agreement executed by and between Riverton City, Salt Lake County, and several
municipalities within Salt Lake and Utah County; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City pays for the First Responder Services it receives from the
Unified Fire Authority through its membership in the Unified Fire Service Area (“UFSA”),
which it became a part of by UFSA’s adoption of Resolution No. 5 on December 30, 2005; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City residents and property owners pay the UFSA for the services
rendered by the Unified Fire Authority through the levy of ad valorem a property taxes upon
owners of property within the City, which is levied by UFSA; and

        WHEREAS, in June of 2016, the UFSA issued Lease Revenue and Refunding Bonds
Series 216A to refinance an unpaid portion lease revenue bonds issued in the year 2008 and
obtain additional funding to construct additional facilities owned by the UFSA, but not located
within the municipal territory of Riverton City; and

         WHEREAS, On February 18 of 2020, the Riverton City Council adopted a resolution to
initiate the City’s withdrawal from the UFSA pursuant to the procedures set for thin Utah Code
Ann. § 17B-1-505, however the Riverton City Council does not intend to terminate receipt of
First Responder Services from the Unified Fire Authority; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 17B-1-505 empowers a municipality to depart from a
service area which provides First Responder Services if the service area and the municipality
agree in writing to the terms of said withdrawal; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that City and UFSA
officials have negotiated in good faith and reduced to writing the terms and conditions of
Riverton City’s withdrawal from the UFSA;

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby authorizes and directs the Mayor to sign an
          agreement with the UFSA, titled, “Agreement Detailing Terms of Withdrawal of
          Riverton City from UFSA” and in substantially similar form to the Agreement
          attached hereto as Exhibit A. Said Agreement must be approved as to legal from by
          the City Attorney prior to execution of the same by the Mayor.

      2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

              PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th
day of September 2020 by the following vote:
