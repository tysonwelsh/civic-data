BACKGROUND:
Draper City has an existing lease agreement with Verizon Wireless for a cell tower/”water Tank” at the
water tank site #3 in Suncrest. The cell tower was designed to be co-locatable and DISH Wireless has
requested to co-locate on the tower and put an equipment shelter (150 square feet) on the property. The
lease amount is for $300.00 per month. The lease term is for five years with an automatic renewal four
times.
