ORDINANCE 06-18-2024 -PAINTER SMITH ANNEXATION ADOPTED:

   Councilor Travis Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 06-18-2024. This ordinance annexed 4.33
acres consisting of property owned by Russell Smith located approximately 1150 N 500 W, affecting
parcel XB00-2075-122R. The annexed property was classified in the ID-1 zone. Councilor Travis
Worwood asked if water rights were required with this annexation. Mr. Atkinson answered that at time
of annexation it is difficult to determine how much water will be used. At time of development is when
the water rights will be required.
   Councilor Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following vote:
       Yes: Jeramie Callaway, JD Parady, Shari Cowan, Travis Worwood
       Absent: Skip Worwood

ENCLOSED TRAILER PURCHASE APPROVED FOR HIVE/RECREATION/PARKS DEPARTMENTS:

   Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that there is a great need for storage for equipment used at the
Hive. Recreation Director Craig Oswald has proposed purchasing a enclosed trailer that could help store
and well as transport equipment to other facilities. Facilities Manager Clark Wood owns a trailer that he
is selling for $9,000. Mr. Oswald has researched other similar trailers for sell and has determined that
$9,000 is an excellent price for the trailer. The funds needed could come from the Recreation
Improvements line item in the Capital Projects Fund.
    The council discussed that the possible purchase of the Bishop’s Storehouse building located near
the Hive was going to be the solution for the storage problem, but the Church has not been willing to
sell the building Councilor Travis Worwood suggested that the City could reach out to the Stake
Presidents to have them help open doors and negotiate with purchasing department of the Church.
   Councilor Travis Worwood moved to approve the purchase of the enclosed trailer from Clark Wood
for $9,000. Councilor Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

RESOLUTION 06-18-2024 -NEPHI CITY PICKING UP THE EMPLOYEE CONTRIBUTION FOR PUBLIC SAFETY
TIER II EMPLOYEES:

   The Utah State legislators passed legislation requiring public safety tier II employees to pay an extra
2.59% for their contribution to the hybrid retirement system. Cities have the option to pick-up this
contribution for the employees. The Council had previously agreed to pick up this contribution for the
police officers. Resolution 06-18-2024 was presented for the council’s approval and states that
beginning July 1, 2024, Nephi City shall pick up and pay the required employee contributions for all
employees who are members of the New Public Safety Tier II Contributory Retirement System. The
