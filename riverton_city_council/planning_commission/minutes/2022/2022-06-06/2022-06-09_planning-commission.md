             LOCATED AT 12403 SOUTH REDWOOD ROAD. APPLICANT - DANIEL
             GILSTER

Mr. Prestwich presented the Staff Report and stated that the above request involves
consideration of a home occupation for DG Tree, LLC, a tree service to be located at 12403
South Redwood Road. The current zoning is R-22 Medium High-Density Residential with
five to eight dwelling units per acre with a Historic Preservation Overlay. The applicant
purchased the property in October 2021 and has utilized the rear yard for his tree service,
which includes four employees and three trucks with storage for tree remnants. In speaking
with Code Enforcement, an investigation was conducted in March 2022 and the applicant
was made aware of actions that were required and an application was submitted. A site
plan was displayed.

Mr. Prestwich reported that all home occupations must fit fixed standards. Home occupation
standards were described. He noted that any screened area or structures must be located
on the side or rear yard. After the application was submitted there was some question as to
whether the applicant resides in the subject property. Notices were sent out to all property
owners and residents within 300 feet. Staff recommended the matter be continued to allow
for additional discussion and information to be gathered.

Commissioner Rushton questioned the zoning allowance if this was a new business
application. Mr. Prestwich believed it would work well in the Industrial, CG, and CR Zones.
He added that the request would be an accessory use in a residential neighborhood.

Commissioner Park asked if fencing around the entire rear area would be required. He
believed it would also appear from photographs that the applicant is operating an RV
Storage business. Mr. Prestwich confirmed that any proposed outdoor areas be required to
be screened. RV Storage is on the prohibited list as a home occupation and it is the
responsibility of Code Enforcement to address the storage issue.

City Attorney, Ryan Carter stated that the proposed use would be allowed in the
Manufacturing District by Conditional Use and liken it to landscaping services, which can be
found in the Table of Commercial Uses. The use is not permitted in any Commercial Zone
except the CR Zone.

Commissioner Matheson asked if the applicant resides on the property and was concerned
that it is being considered a home occupation. Mr. Prestwich stated that the applicant
confirmed that he is a resident of the property and evidence to that effect would be
forthcoming. That was another reason he requested a continuance.

Mr. Carter explained that the general position being discussed was administrative approval
as the property is not currently in compliance with the Code. He believed it was reasonable
to delay any decision until the property owner can demonstrate compliance. If the
Commission feels that the existing uses on the property are minor, a condition could be
added to specify that this particular use must cease prior to approval being granted.

Item 5 which states that semi-trucks and trailers, construction equipment, and other heavy
equipment are not permitted. She asked if some of the existing equipment and trailer meet

       E.     CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT - SHORT-TERM RENTAL, LOCATED AT 2997
              WEST 11925 SOUTH. APPLICANT - CAITLIN WRIGHT

Mr. Prestwich presented the Staff Report and stated that the above matter involves
consideration of a Conditional Use Permit for a short-term rental located at 11925 South
2997 West. The subject property includes a .50-acre lot that is zoned RR-22. An aerial map
was displayed. Mr. Prestwich reported that notice was sent to all property owners within
300 feet of the subject property. All of the comments received were included in the Staff
Report. The concerns raised involved the condition of the property and yard maintenance.
He reported that the police responded to a call to the property and confirmed that all short-
term rental requirements and rules apply.

The issue of a home occupation existing on the property was discussed. Mr. Prestwich
explained that a long-term rental is not considered a home occupation and an Accessory
Dwelling Unit Permit would be required if there were four or more unrelated occupants. He
reiterated that the applicant is requesting a Short-Term Rental Permit where VRBO and
Airbnb would be acceptable.

Mr. Carter clarified that an ADU does not increase the number of individuals who are allowed
to live together on the property who are unrelated by blood or marriage. Prior to the Statute
being put in place, Riverton City’s Code specified that no more than two unrelated people
may live under the same roof. In Utah, the Legislature has determined that four unrelated
people are allowed to live in a single-family residential home. The Code specifies that with
regard to the family use restriction, the single-family residence must be structured in a way
that all occupants have equal access to all parts of the internal structure. The governing
standard is found in the base zoning for a single-family residence in the ADU Ordinance.

Commissioner Park expressed concern with a letter submitted regarding the property being
required to be maintained according to Riverton City Ordinance. Mr. Prestwich confirmed
that failure to maintain the property according to the Ordinance would result in revocation of
the Business License.

Commissioner Matheson stated that he has lived with an Airbnb adjacent to his residence
and asked for information regarding the enforcement process. Mr. Carter stated that
enforcement to this point has only involved complaints from property owners who are
suspected of operating rentals without the proper permits. When a complaint is received he
hoped that the Police Report would include information specifying whether the property is a
VRBO and forward it to Staff. If there are sufficient facts to show that the property owners

Commission for possible revocation of the permit.

Mr. Prestwich confirmed that without an ADU Permit, there would not be a separate living
area or permitted rental. The Short-Term Rental Code specifies that there may only be one
rental contract on the property at any given time and three or more violations within a 12-
month period will result in revocation of the Business License.

The applicant, Katelyn Wright, stated that she applied for a Short-Term Business License
and understood that Section 10 (4b) states that the owner or representative must physically
respond or arrive at the rental property within one hour after being called. She has a

Mr. Prestwich presented the Staff report and understood that the State of Utah is presently
discussing water efficiency standards and conservation options. The City Council directed
Staff to put forward efficiency standards based on the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy
District. The standards were carefully worded to specify that it is a recommendation for
developers at this point. This is the first step and is not intended to solve the problem but to
initiate the process and encourage different types of savings without imposing a mandate.

enacting conservation measures to be able to continue to be eligible to receive the water
they are getting from the Central Utah Project, which flows to Riverton City. They count the
consumption of the secondary water system when they show the Central Utah Project
whether conservation is happening. Riverton City’s conservation impacts Jordan Valley
Water Conservancy District’s rates that they are required to pay to Central Utah Projects.
