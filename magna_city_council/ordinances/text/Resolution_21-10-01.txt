MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
Resolution No. 21-10-01 Date: OCTOBER 26, 2021
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL SHOWING INTENT TO PARTICIPATE IN THE UTAH MAIN
STREET PROGRAM
RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Governor's Office of Economic Opportunity (Go Utah) is
creating and administering the Utah Main Street Program; and

WHEREAS, the new Utah Main Street Program will help Utah communities
revitalize their economy, appearance, and image of their downtown commercial districts
by providing a framework and resources to support a community’s downtown
revitalization efforts.

WHEREAS, Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is home to Historic Magna
Main Street, an iconic, charming community center that hearkens back to the main streets
of old America; and

WHEREAS, in early 2021, Magna Main Street was accepted into the National
Register of Historic Places, the official list of the Nation's historic places deemed worthy
of preservation by the National Park Service; and

WHEREAS, Historic Magna Main Street could greatly benefit from tools and
training that can help with revitalization, small business growth, and positioning the street
as a vibrant, historic destination which attracts residents and visitors.

1

Magna Resolution 21-10-01 Utah Main Street Program Participation

RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, it is resolved by the Magna Metro Township Council:

1. That Magna hereby consents to applying for membership in the Utah Main
Street Program under the "Tier J - Engaged Main Street" designation. This
designation would require Magna to agree to the following terms:

a. 1 to 3 year commitment to the Program, including costs related to
attending Utah Main Street Program events (see below).

b. Have a representative attend the following Utah Main Street Program
events: 1 Main Street workshop per year, the Utah Main Street
conference, and the “Main Street 101” Training.

c. Submit an annual report to Go Utah.

De That this Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its passage.

3. In the event of any conflict between this Resolution and any other enactment
of Magna, this Resolution shall control.
