Attachment:

Attachment:
Project 25-051 Offset Brewery CUP SBPC 5.13.25
Attachment:

Planner Kuhrmeyer introduced a brewery and taproom conditional use permit. She stated that staff has reviewed the application and recommended approval.
Commissioner Sagerman asked about the hours of operation. Planner Kuhrmeyer referred to the staff report for the proposed hours. He was concerned about parking and asked for clarification about the expected volume of patrons. (6:39 P.M.)
The owner of the brewery, Connor Brown, responded to the question about potential traffic to the building. (6:40 P.M.)
Commissioner Young asked about feedback from the other business owners in the area. Planner Kuhrmeyer provided information about the types of other businesses that may have clients that would benefit from the addition of the Brewery and Taproom. (6:42 P.M.)
Commissioner Peteroy asked for clarification about food offerings. Mr. Brown stated there would not be any food preparation, but they plan to offer snacks. (6:43 P.M.)
Rob Nielson,
Owner
, addressed the commission clarifying the number of the parking spaces and the availability of stalls. (6:45 P.M.)
Commissioner Hawley opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:48 p.m.
No one came forward to speak.
Commissioner Hawley closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:49 p.m.
Spencer Young made a motion to approve the applicant's request for a Conditional Use Permit according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Eric Sagerman seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Tyann Mooney.
