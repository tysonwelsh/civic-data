                                RESOLUTION NO. 23-06

       A RESOLUTION BY THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL UPDATING
       THE APPROVED CITY COUNCIL ASSIGNMENT LIST FOR 2023


      WHEREAS, the City Council reviews and makes changes to the Council
Assignment List every year; and

      WHEREAS, it is the desire of the City Council to update and approve the
Council Assignment List for 2023 to include assignments for Councilmember Green.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Approval. The Draper City Council hereby approves the updated
Council Assignment List for 2023 hereto attached as Exhibit A.

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 3. Effective Date.              This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, THE 10TH DAY OF JANUARY, 2023.
