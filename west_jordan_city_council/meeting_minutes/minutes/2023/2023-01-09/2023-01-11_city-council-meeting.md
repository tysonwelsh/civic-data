 6. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Resolution No. 23-001 certifying the 2023 election results for Council Chair and
          Vice-Chair
          Policy Analyst Cassidy Hansen explained the voting process to elect a Council Chair and
          Vice Chair for 2023. Council Members McConnehey and Bloom indicated willingness to
          be nominated for Council Chair. The Council voted, and staff reported five votes for
          Council Member McConnehey and two votes for Council Member Bloom.

           Council Members Green, Bloom, and Pack indicated willingness to be nominated for Vice
           Chair. Council Member Green withdrew. The Council voted, and staff reported four votes
           for Council Member Bloom and three votes for Council Member Pack.
           MOTION:       Council Member Green moved to place Christopher McConnehey as
                        Chair and Pamela Bloom as Vice Chair on Resolution No. 23-001. Council
                        Member Jacob seconded the motion, which passed by unanimous voice
                        vote (7-0).
