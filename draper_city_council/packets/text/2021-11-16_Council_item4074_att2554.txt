                            RESOLUTION NO. 21-61
       A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL ADOPTING VOTER
       PARTICIPATION AREAS.


        WHEREAS, state law requires each city of the third class to adopt voter participation areas for the
purposes of ballot initiatives and referenda every ten years after receiving updated data from the United
States Census; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City has received the census data and divided the city in accordance with state
law; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed map meets all the requirements of state law; and

         WHEREAS, The City Council finds this map to meet the needs of the health, safety, and welfare of
the citizens of Draper City.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1.      Adoption. Draper City hereby adopts the attached map of voter participation areas
for the next ten years. See Exhibit A.

         Section 2.     Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and
all sections, parts and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

       Section 4.      Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.



    PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, THIS THE ___ DAY OF NOVEMBER, 2021.
