On March 13, 2025, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this item. No
public comment was offered, and a record of motion is included below. The Planning
Commission voted to recommend the following motion to approve this item:

I move the City Council adopt Resolution 25-16, authorizing the Mayor to execute the 2nd
Amendment to the Mountain View Place Development Agreement increasing the number of
allowed units to 288.

Background

This item is a amendment to the MVV Development Agreement to facilitate a minor change to
the previously approved Village Lofts Apartments located in the Mountain View Village
Development.

The property is zoned SLR as are all the surrounding properties. It is about 5-acres and is
located at the northeast corner of the Mountain View Village Master Site Plan. It is bounded on
the north by 13200 South (a public street), the Welby Jacob Canal on the east and Mountain
View Village to the west and south. A bridge across the canal is planned south of this project
and will be installed separately.

This project was approved as shown, with 7 stories and 282 residential units. The original site
plan included 16,900-sf of commercial space on the ground floor. That site plan was
subsequently amended to remove most of that commercial and add additional units into that
space, with a small retail corner space remaining. As the development group has been
finalizing plans for construction, the decision was made in consultation with the Master
Development group for Mountain View Village to eliminate the remaining proposed commercial
space from the building, converting that space into six (6) additional residential units. As the
development agreement currently limits the total residential units to 282, the developer has
proposed an amendment to that agreement to raise the number to 288 to accommodate the
additional six units. The footprint of the building remains the same, and no changes are required
to the civil design package for the project. The parking requirements also do no increase, as
the commercial space required a larger number of stalls than the resulting residential square
footage. No additional parking is required beyond what is proposed.

The elimination of the retail commercial space does require minor changes to the building
exterior where the commercial space was planned. This actually results in a more consistent
and cohesive building façade, as it no longer requires storefront space and entries. The
removal of the remaining commercial space also both simplifies the parking arrangement and
allows for safer vehicle and pedestrian flow, as all commercial parking and traffic will now be
specific to the Mountain View Village commercial development.

With the additional units and minor amendments to the exterior, the project remains consistent
with the adopted Master Site Plan and the Mountain View Village development agreement. The
Master Developer, CenterCal, has reviewed and consented to the proposed amendments.

The Planning Commission recommended approval of the amendment to the development
agreement, and staff concurs with that recommendation.
