Special Recognition


2.         22-324                 Sandy Youth Council Installation and Special Recognition

                                  Council Member Brooke D'Sousa welcomed the Sandy Youth Council and introduced 
                                  Program Advisors, Christine Edwards and Liz Theriault. The Sandy Youth Council 
                                  members were called up to the front of the dais and officially sworn in as the 2022-23 
                                  Sandy Youth Council by the Honorable Judge Paul Farr. 

                                  Following the installation, the Sandy Youth Council presented a Special Recognition 
                                  award to Marsha Millet acknowledging her 24 years of service coordinating the Sandy 
                                  Youth City Council. 

                                  Council convened a recess at approximately 5:45 pm.
                                  Council reconvened the council meeting at approximately 6:00 pm.

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                                  Dustin Fratto reviewed upcoming agenda items with the council. Mr. Fratto thanked 
                                  Christine Edwards and Liz Theriault for their efforts on behalf of the Sandy Youth Council.

Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Brooke D'Sousa thanked Chris Edwards and Liz Theriault for their role 
                                  with the official start of the new Sandy Youth Council program.

                                  Council Member Zach Robinson echoed Council Member D'Sousa's comments.

                                  Council Member Scott Earl acknowledged Chris Kirk and his crew for their hard work and 
                                  commendable efforts on behalf of the Parks Department.

                                  Council Member Marci Houseman acknowledged the Public Utilities staff for the recent 
                                  water tour. She provided an update from PUAB and spoke about water conservation 
                                  efforts and a new program for kids in grades 1st through 5th sponsored by the Sandy City 
                                  Arts Guild.

                                  Council Member Alison Stroud thanked the Public Utilities staff for coordinating the 
                                  recent tour. She acknowledged the Sandy City Police and Fire departments for their 
                                  efforts during a recent call. The community appreciates you!
