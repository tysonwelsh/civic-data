WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
NOVEMBER 18, 2025

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 1:30 p.m. on November 18, 2025, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow,
County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

PUBLIC HEARING: EMPLOYEE COMPENSATION INCREASES FOR 2025

The Clerk/Auditor explained that county employees may be eligible to receive up to $1,000 in
additional compensation depending on factors including service, employment classification,
standing and total hours worked. Pursuant to S.B. 91 any compensation increase awarded to an
executive county officer must be publicly disclosed and subject to a public hearing.
Commissioner Iverson opened the public hearing.

Seeing no one come forward, Commissioner Iverson closed the public hearing.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 1:34 p.m.
