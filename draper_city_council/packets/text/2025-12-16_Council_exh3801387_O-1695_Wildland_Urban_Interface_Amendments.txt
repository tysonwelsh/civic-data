                               ORDINANCE NO. 1695



AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLE 7 OF THE DRAPER
CITY MUNICIPAL CODE ESTABLISHING THE WILDLAND URBAN INTERFACE AREA.



       WHEREAS, Title 7 of the Draper City Municipal Code establishes standards for
fire prevention; and

     WHEREAS, Utah Code 65A-8-203 establishes a process for cooperative
agreements and funding with the Utah Division of Forestry Fire and State Lands;
and

       WHEREAS, Draper City has previously adopted the Utah Wildland Urban
Interface Code as part of the Draper City Fire Code; and

       WHEREAS, The Draper City Fire Code as currently adopted allows the
legislative body to establish wildland-urban interface areas; and

     WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code 2-4-010 requires exercise of legislative
powers through ordinance unless otherwise specifically provided; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the proposed text amendment and
finds good cause to revise the terms and provisions of Title 7.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following
findings to amend Title 7 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) The proposed
amendments comply with the Utah Code Annotated. 2) The proposed amendments
serve the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper.

     Section 2. Adoption of Map. The map found in the attached Exhibit B is
hereby declared as the wildland-urban interface area of Draper City
       Section 3. Revision. Title 7 of the Draper City Municipal Code are hereby
amended to read as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein
by reference.

      Section 4. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to
correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis errors prior to
submitting the Ordinance to Sterling Codifiers.

       Section 5. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

      Section 6. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to
the date of final passage.




PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 16h DAY OF DECEMBER, 2025.

   International Code Council as adopted by the State of Utah is hereby
   adopted as the Fire Code of Draper City. All appendices contained within the
   International Fire Code, excluding Appendix A, are also hereby adopted as
   the Fire Code of Draper City.

B. The most recent version of the Utah Wildland Urban Interface Code, issued
   by the International Code Council and adopted by the State of Utah, with the
   alternatives or amendment approved by the Utah Division of Forestry, is
   hereby adopted as the Wildland Urban Interface Code of Draper City.

   Wildland Urban Interface Area Code

      1. The most recent version of the Utah Wildland Urban Interface Code,
         issued by the International Code Council and adopted by the State of
         Utah, with the alternatives or amendment approved by the Utah
         Division of Forestry, is hereby adopted as the Wildland Urban
         Interface Code of Draper City.

      2. Maps for wildland-urban interface areas can be found on the Draper
         City Map Portal.

      3. The city council may amend maps for wildland-urban interface areas
         by resolution as frequently as once each calendar year with a
         recommendation from the Draper Fire Department.

      4. Structures built in wildland-urban interface areas must comply with
         the Wildland Urban Interface Code.

C. Subject to the provisions of subsection D of this section, automatic fire
    sprinkler systems are required for structures built under the provisions of
    the International Residential Code (IRC) under any of the following
    conditions:

      1. The structure:

             a. is located in an urban-wildland wildland-urban interface area as
                provided in the Utah Wildland Urban Interface Code adopted as
                a construction code under the State Construction Code; and

             b. does not meet the requirements described in Utah Code,
                    Subsection 65A-8-208(4)(f) and Utah Administrative Code R652-
                    122-1300, Minimum Standards for County Wildland Fire
                    Ordinance;

          2. The structure is in an area where a public water distribution system
             with fire hydrants does not exist as required in Utah Administrative
             Code, R309-550-5, Water Main Design;

          3. The only fire apparatus access road has a grade of greater than
             10% for more than 500 continual feet;

          4. The total floor area of all floor levels within the exterior walls of the
             dwelling unit exceeds 10,000 square feet; or

          5. The total floor area of all floor levels within the exterior walls of the
             dwelling unit is double the average of the total floor area of all floor
             levels of unsprinkled homes in the subdivision that are no larger than
             10,000 square feet.
D. A single-family dwelling described in subsection C of this section does not
   require a fire sprinkler system if the dwelling:

      1. is located outside of the wildland-urban interface;

      2. is built in a one-lot subdivision; and
  has 50 feet of defensible space on all sides that limits the propensity of fire
  spreading from the dwelling to another property.

Memorandum of Findings to Support the Wildland-Urban Interface Areas Map
                      MEMORANDUM

TO:     Draper City Council
FROM:   Don Buckley, Fire Marshal
DATE:   12/4/2025
RE:     Designation of Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) Zone in Accordance with
        the 2024 IWUIC



Purpose
This memorandum documents the methodology used by the Draper City Fire
Department to identify and map the Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) areas.

Code Basis
Under the International Wildland-Urban Interface Code (IWUIC), local
jurisdictions may designate WUI areas where structures and other human
development are located adjacent to or intermixed with wildland fuels. WUI
designations must account for vegetation hazards, topography, and fire-
protection capabilities.

Method of Identification
On August 4, 2025 the I met with Utah Forestry, Fire and State Lands (FFSL) to
discuss a hazard identification process using the following criteria:

   1. Vegetation and Fuel Loading
      Areas with moderate to high wildland fuel continuity were identified using:

          o   GIS vegetation data from Utah DNR’s Utah Wildfire Risk Assessment
              Portal (UWRAP) which gives a good overview of Wildfire Hazards.

          o   Fuel models based on data from Utah DNR’s Utah Wildfire Risk
              Assessment Portal (UWRAP) which gives a good overview of Flame
              Front Characteristics, and Ember Characteristics.

   2. Topographic Influence
      Slopes exceeding 30% were incorporated due to increased fire spread
      potential.

   3. Proximity of Structures to Fuels
      Areas where buildings are located:
                                                                                 .../2
         o   Within approximately 0–1.5 miles of wildland fuels

         o   Intermixed within continuous fuels

         o   In isolated pockets within urban settings adjacent to fuels

   4. Historical and Operational Fire Risk
      Local wildfire history (2008, 800 Acre Wildfire in Corner Canyon, 2025, 1.5
      Acre Wildfire behind Draper Heights) and response limitations such as
      access, water supply, and evacuation routes were evaluated.

   5. GIS Mapping and Boundary Determination
      Layers from FFSL and UWRAP were merged with parcel information in our
      GIS system to generate the final WUI boundary.
      Mapping performed by: Karen Burnett GIS Manager
      Software: ArcGIS

      Date completed: November 5, 2025

Resulting WUI Designation
The analysis resulted in a designated WUI zone that starts at Highland Drive as
the base line. On the West end of Highland then South to the city boundary.
Then from Highland Drive South and East over the mountain to the city
boundary. On the North end of Highland Drive East up the mountain to the city
boundary. The zone is classified as the Draper Wildland Urban Interface (WUI)

This is mapped and shown in Exhibit B (attached map).

Implementation
All new construction, significant remodels, and applicable fuel-modification
requirements will apply to properties within the designated WUI zone.
