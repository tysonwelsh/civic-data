ZT-5-2023
West Valley City
A zone text change to the Zoning (Title 7) and Condominium (Title 9) Ordinances to
comply with State code changes from HB 406 and SB 174.

City staff is proposing an ordinance change to amend the Zoning (Title 7) and
Condominium (Title 9) Ordinances to comply with State code changes from HB 406 and
SB 174. Both HB 406 and SB 174 were passed by the Utah Legislature in the 2023 General
Session of the Utah Legislature and later signed by the governor. Copies of these bills are
included with this report. Among other changes, the bills prohibit cities from requiring an
applicant to post an improvement completion bond for landscaping on private property (HB
406) and prohibit the legislative body of a municipality from reviewing subdivision
applications for single-family dwellings, two-family dwellings, or townhomes (SB 174).

Instead of having a separate subdivision process for subdivision applications for single-
family dwellings, two-family dwellings, or townhomes, staff is proposing that the
subdivision review process be amended to not require the approval of the City Council for
any subdivision, especially since these decisions are administrative.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. The ordinance should be approved as proposed by staff to comply with
      State law.

   2. Continuance.

C-25-2023
Sage Valley Townhomes 1854
W 4100 S
RM Zone (1.54 acres)


Grey Garza is requesting conditional use approval to construct townhomes at 1854 W
4100 S. The property is zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) and is
designated as very high density residential in the City’s General Plan. The project will
include 18 units.


Background
The Planning Commission reviewed and approved this request in September of last year
(C-20-2022). The approval has lapsed, so the applicant is looking to renew the approval.
In April of 2018 the site was rezoned to RM, which included a development agreement for
the overall project. The townhomes will be located on the west side of the Sage Valley
Apartments. The apartment project received conditional use approval in November of
2019 and is mostly complete. Some existing buildings were demolished on the south end
of the subject project area, but a majority of the site has remained undeveloped since K-
Mart originally developed the property.

Project Review
The proposal includes 18, 3-story townhome units, divided between four buildings. There
will be two 5-unit buildings and two four-unit buildings. Access to the property will be
from the apartment project parking lot on the north and south ends of the parcel. On the
west side of the property is a commercial property as well as single unit dwellings.
Existing multi-unit dwelling residential borders the north edge.

Buildings
The units are required to be at least 2,400 sq. ft. of finished floor area per the
Development Agreement. The building’s exterior includes brick, stucco and metal siding.
The building elevations need to comply with all materials, building relief, building design,
window treatments, and roof treatments outlined in 7-11-400 of the City Code.
The building currently lacks one window treatment on the Primary Façade. The Planning
Commission in the previous approval allowed the windows to remain as proposed.

All units will include individual patio and balcony spaces on all three floors. The
applicant is proposing to include roof-top decks on the end units of each building. Due to
concerns for privacy from residential property, staff recommends that the building in the
north-west corner not have roof-top patios.

Landscaping
There will be 5’ of landscaping along the west and north edge of the property. A 6’
masonry wall is also required along the north and west property lines, per the
development agreement, and has mostly been installed. All other areas of the site around
the buildings will be landscaped. Landscaped areas need to include at least 50% live plant
material.

Amenities
The townhomes will share amenities with the apartment project and be part of the HOA
for the community. The amenity area is located adjacent to the townhomes on the east
edge of the parcel. Amenities will include a swimming pool, hot tub, basketball court,
and a tot lot.

Parking
Each unit will have a 20’x20’ garage. At the time the property was rezoned, townhomes
only required two parking spaces per unit. The apartment project provided 8 more parking
stalls than were required in their conditional use proposal, so some of those stalls could be
shared with the townhomes. The applicant has also added 10 additional parking stalls on
the townhome parcel.


Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as
the following:

     1. The applicant shall meet all provisions in the development agreement.
     2. Roof-top decks shall not be allowed on the building in the north-west corner of
        the site.
     3. The building elevations shall comply with the following requirements:
            a. All materials, building relief, building design, and roof treatments outlined
                 in 7-11-400 of the City Code shall be met.
            b. The window treatments as proposed on the primary façade are sufficient.
     4. All landscaping shall include at least 50% live plant material.
     5. All requirements of any affected department or agency must be met, including but
        not limited to Granger Hunter Improvement District, WVC Public Works, WVC
        Building Inspections and WVC Fire Department.

Continuance, for reasons determined during the public hearing.
