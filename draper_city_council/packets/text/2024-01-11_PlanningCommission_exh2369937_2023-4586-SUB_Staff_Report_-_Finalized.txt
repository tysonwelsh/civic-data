BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 1.36
acres located on the west side of 1700 E., at 12540-12544 S. 1700 E. (Exhibits B & C). The
property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be
approved to allow for the two (2) lots within the subdivision to be combined into one (1).

A home was built on the subject property in 1987. When the Duke Subdivision Plat was
recorded in 2008, it divided the property into two (2) lots, and the existing home became
part of Lot 2. Lot 1 provided access to Lot 2 through a 20 ft private driveway access
easement. Lot 1 is not developed.

services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas; and to promote and

animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family

Subdivision Layout. The applicant is proposing to combine the two (2) lots within the
subdivision into one (1) lot as part of the Duke Subdivision Amended (Exhibit F). Several
internal private access and public utility easements of the original Duke Subdivision (Exhibit
G) are no longer needed and are proposed to be vacated with the recording of this plat.
These include the 20 ft private access easement and the 7 ft public utility easement along
the southern property line and the 7 ft and 10 ft public utility easements that were placed
between the two lots.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request, receive public
comment, and make a decision based on the findings listed below and the criteria for
approval, or denial, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

        1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                 Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the
                 site and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
        2.       That if the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date
                 of approval, or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure
                 has not commenced, such approval shall be null and void. This time period
                 may be extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6)
                 month period for good cause shown. The subdivider must petition in writing
                 for an extension prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year.

The findings for approval as are follows:

        1.       That there is good cause for the amendment; and no public street, right-of-
