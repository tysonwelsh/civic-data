                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 25-10

  AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE TITLE 6, CHAPTER 5 TO
     REVISE ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS OF THE ANIMAL CONTROL
                           ORDINANCE

       WHEREAS, Title 6, Chapter 5 of the Riverton City Code governs the ownership of
animals as pets within the territorial jurisdiction of Riverton City (hereinafter referred to as the
“Animal Control Ordinance”); and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City staff has undertaken a comprehensive review to the Animal
Control Ordinance to streamline processes related to investigation and prosecution of cases
where domesticated animals attack other persons or animals; and

       WHEREAS, under direction, staff has also created an amendment to the Animal Control
Ordinance which eliminates the need for obtaining an annual license to own a domesticated
animal, while preserving sections of the code which require maintaining current rabies
vaccinations; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, finds and determines that Riverton City Code
Title 6, Chapter 5 is in need of amendments as provided by City staff;

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

      Section 1.       Title 6, Chapter 5, of the Riverton City Code shall be, and hereby is,
                       amended to reflect the following changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

      Section 2.       This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 1st day of
