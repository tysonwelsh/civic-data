                                           Nephi City
                                     Work Session Minutes
                                         April 13, 2021
                                            7:00 p.m.
                                  Nephi City Council Chambers

   1.   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the results of the Utah State
        University Well-Being Survey.

   2. The council reviewed and discussed a draft of a revision of the residency requirement
      policy for Nephi City employees and directed staff to add a radius of area or a mileage
      distance requirement to the draft.

   3.   Mr. Atkinson reviewed contracts with Rural Utah Consultants and PDS for the General
        Plan update.

   4.   The council reviewed the updated Financial Reports.

   5.   Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the city’s vehicle leasing arrangements.

   6.    Mr. Atkinson talked to the council about putting earnest money down to help secure the
        property that could be used for a telecommuter center that the city is waiting to see if
        they will receive a grant for.

   7. Mayor Nielson reported on the Six County meeting he had attended.

   8. Adjournment at 8:16 p.m.




Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielsen, Councilor Larry Ostler, Councilor Justin Seely, City Attorney
Kasey Wright, City Administrator Seth Atkinson, City Recorder Lisa Brough, Jeremy Lynn, J.D.
Parady.

Absent: Councilor Kent Jones, Councilor Skip Worwood, and Councilor Nathan Memmott
