                       Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                      March 14, 2023
                                         7:00 p.m.
                                        21 E 100 N
                                      Nephi, UT 84648

1.    Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council the Nephi City Code 11.3.2 dealing with
     subdivision exemptions. Chet and Hailee Ingram requested from the council an
     exception of putting in curb and gutter around their existing home on a lot that they are
     wanting to divide into two lots so they can build a new home. Mr. Atkinson reminded
     the council that there was a recent situation (Warburton Agreement) involving a parcel
     dividing into three parcels on 400 East and 300 North. While the parcel was in the
     original platted block system of Nephi, it did not have curb, gutter, or sidewalk, asphalt,
     or sewer infrastructure. The city entered into an agreement with the property owner to
     exempt the parcel from the subdivision process if these improvements were installed on
     each lot, including the lot with the existing home. The city also agreed to install the
     asphalt in front of the parcel that had an existing home, consistent with the policy where
     other homeowners install curb, gutter, and sidewalk in front of their homes. In this way,
     the city creates the network of paved roads, sidewalks and stormwater infrastructure
     over time and they eventually all connect. By offering to asphalt on homes that make
     the choice to install stormwater infrastructure, an incentive is created to help complete
     this network that benefits all residents.
     After much discussion and some examples within the city that had not been required to
     install curb and gutter when dividing a lot into two, the council directed staff to change
     the current code that stated that when a lot is divided in the platteed part of Nephi and
     the existing home does not have curb and gutter then it would not be a requirement of
     the landowner to install curb gutter around the existing home. Mr. Atkinson reminded
     the changes that the council were recommending would be land use code changes that
     would require planning commission approval and a public hearing.

2.    The council reviewed the RV Park agreements. Attorney Kasey Wright explained the
     finer parts of the agreements. High Country RV’s agreement is different because of the
     grandfathered 10 permanent spaces. Kallie Bowers owner of Rollin’ Home thanked the
     council for their work on the RV ordinance.and invited the council out to their opening of
     the new cafe on March 17th.

3. The council discussed the Jenson Agreement amendment request. The council directed
   staff to not change the current agreement with Jenson Refrigeration.

4.    Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the state legislature has passed a new law
     requiring supervision of a person18 years or older to any one under 18 driving an ATV
     who doesn’t have a driver's license. The Nephi code would need to be revised to
     comply with state law.
   5.    Mr. Atkinson informed the council what the planning commission had recommended to
        keep the R-3 zone as is. The recommendations would be addressed at a future meeting

   6. Councilor Parady asked if any decisions have been made on Hive operations. Mr.
      Atkinson said that the staff had been discussing the issue but he had not met with the
      school district about the issue.

   7.    Councilor Ostler said he had a request for a four-way stop on 700 North and 4th East.
        Mr. Atkinson said that that issue had been studied in the past and the recommendation
        was that a 4-way stop would actually cause more traffic issues. He stated that the new
        road connection 1250 North to 700 North was helping with traffic congestion on 400
        East.

   8.    Councilor Worwood moved to convene into executive session to discuss litigation and
        character and competence of an individual. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
        motion passed on a unanimous vote.

   9.   Meeting adjourned at 8:10 p.m.

   10. Executive session adjourned at 8:40 p..m.
