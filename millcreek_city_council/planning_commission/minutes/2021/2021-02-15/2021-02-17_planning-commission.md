    1.3        Consideration                 of ZT-21-001,                 Amendments                  to the Mfflcreek                Historic        Preservation
        Ordinance             Staff:        Blaine         Gehring,           Legislative          Analyst
        Blaine         Gehring         said the chapter               in the Millcreek              zoning          ordinance          related      to historic
        preservation            fell     short      of what         was needed            to preserve          the historic           features       of Millcreek.
        Currently         the chapter's              purpose          is to "preserve            sites with          historical        architectural,           or aesthetic
        value      which        are unique            and irreplaceable                assets."        The problem             is it only         identifies       four
        structures         of historic           value       and then         discusses         a conditional             use permit        process           when       making
        any modifications                   to a historic           site or structure.            The      code fails         to identify          other      historic
        structures         and districts             and outline            a process        for identifying              and preserving             them.       The
        proposed          code         amendments             would          create    a Historic          Preservation              Commission,              create       a list of
        historic        sites and districts,                designate          historic      sites and districts              on a historic           register,        provide
        standards         for rehabilitation                 of historic          structures,          and retain         the existing           conditional           use
    process.            The Historic               Preservation             Commission             would           have    seven       members,         one from             each
        City     Council         district        and three          at-large      chosen        by the Mayor               with      consent        of the City
        Council.         Each      of the members                   must      be a bona         fide      resident        of Millcreek            and at least two
    members               must         have professional               background             and knowledge                  related      to history          preservation.
        The Commission                    would           issue    certificates        of appropriateness,                   establish      a repository             for
        important         documents,               artifacts,        and items         of historical            significance,           provide        a written           history
Millcreek        Planning         Commission               Meeting       Minutes                        17 February              2021          Page 7 of 10



    of Millcreek,           put on historical          events to promote                education,       and designate            entries       to the list of
    sites for state and national                  registries.
