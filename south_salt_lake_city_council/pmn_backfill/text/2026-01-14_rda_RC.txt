No Action Comments.
       1. Report of the Executive Director. Executive Director Wood gave an update on the recent
           closing of the Downtown bonds for the Housing & Transit Reinvestment Zone. The agency
           had a successful response from investors and closed on a 6.25% interest rate. Due to factors
           like the Agency’s good creditworthiness and solid financial planning and representation by
           its underwriter, DA Davidson, the Agency received ten times the amount of orders from
           investors than bonds which lead to drop in the final interest rate and an increase in project
           funds of nearly a million dollars.
