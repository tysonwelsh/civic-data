         A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF RIVERTON CITY,
         UTAH (THE “CITY”), FOR THE PURPOSE OF APPROVING A
         SUPPLEMENTAL INDENTURE OF TRUST; AND OTHER
         DOCUMENTS REQUIRED IN CONNECTION THEREWITH;
         AUTHORIZING THE TAKING OF ALL OTHER ACTIONS
         NECESSARY TO THE CONSUMMATION OF THE TRANSACTIONS
         CONTEMPLATED BY THIS RESOLUTION; AND RELATED
         MATTERS.

       WHEREAS, Riverton City, Utah (the “City”) previously issued its Water Revenue
Bonds, Series 2020A in the total principal amount of $5,491,000 (the “Series 2020A
Bonds”) pursuant to a General Indenture of Trust dated as of February 1, 2000 (the
“General Indenture”), as supplemented by an Eighth Supplemental Indenture of Trust dated
as of January 1, 2020, by and between the City and Zions Bancorporation, National
Association, as trustee (the “Trustee”); and

        WHEREAS, the City previously issued its Water Revenue Bonds, Series 2020B in
the total principal amount of $11,985,000 (the “Series 2020B Bonds”) pursuant to the
General Indenture, as supplemented by a Ninth Supplemental Indenture of Trust dated as
of April 1, 2020, by and between the City and the Trustee; and

       WHEREAS, the Series 2020A Bonds and Series 2020B Bonds were both issued to
fund the same project consisting of improvements to the City’s water system; and

        WHEREAS, the City desires to reallocate funds which have been drawn between
the Series 2020A Bonds and Series 2020B Bonds and after such reallocation, to provide
for the redemption of the outstanding Series 2020B Bonds using legally available funds of
the City. To accomplish such reallocation and redemption, the City desires to enter into a
Supplemental Indenture of Trust (the “Supplemental Indenture”) in in substantially the
form presented to the meeting at which this Resolution was adopted and which is attached
hereto as Exhibit B; and

        WHEREAS, the Council desires to grant to any one of the Mayor, City Manager or
Administrative Services Director (the “Designated Officer”) the authority to approve the
Supplemental Indenture and any changes with respect thereto from those terms which were
before the Council at the time of adoption of this Resolution;

       NOW, THEREFORE, it is hereby resolved by the City Council of Riverton City,
Utah as follows:

        Section 1.   For the purposes of reallocating funds which have been drawn
between the Series 2020A Bonds and Series 2020B Bonds and providing funds to payoff
and redeem the outstanding Series 2020B Bonds, the City hereby authorizes the entering
into of the Supplemental Indenture and the taking of all actions necessary or prudent in
accomplishing the same.

        Section 2.    The Supplemental Indenture, in substantially the form presented to
this meeting and attached hereto as Exhibit B, is hereby authorized, approved, and
confirmed. The Mayor or Mayor Pro Tem and the City Recorder are hereby authorized to
execute and deliver the Supplemental Indenture for and on behalf of the City, with final
terms as may be established by the Designated Officer, and with such alterations, changes
or additions as may be necessary or as may be authorized by Section 3 hereof.

        Section 3.      The Designated Officer or other appropriate officials of the City are
authorized to make any alterations, changes or additions to the Supplemental Indenture or
any other document herein authorized and approved which may be necessary to conform
the same to the final terms agreed with the City and the Trustee, to conform to any
applicable requirement, to correct errors or omissions therein, to complete the same, to
remove ambiguities therefrom, or to conform the same to other provisions of said
instruments, to the provisions of this Resolution or any resolution adopted by the Council
or the provisions of the laws of the State of Utah or the United States.

        Section 4.     The execution of the Supplemental Indenture by the Mayor or
Mayor Pro Tem and City Recorder on behalf of the City shall conclusively establish such
necessity, appropriateness, and approval with respect to all such additions, modifications,
deletions, and changes incorporated therein.

        Section 5.     The Designated Officer and other appropriate officials of the City,
and each of them, are hereby authorized and directed to execute and deliver for and on
behalf of the City any or all additional certificates, documents and other papers (including,
without limitation, any escrow agreement and tax compliance procedures and other
documents) and to perform all other acts they may deem necessary or appropriate in order
to implement and carry out the matters authorized in this Resolution and the documents
authorized and approved herein.

       Section 6.     All resolutions or parts thereof in conflict herewith are, to the extent
of such conflict, hereby repealed and this Resolution shall be in full force and effect
immediately upon its approval and adoption.
