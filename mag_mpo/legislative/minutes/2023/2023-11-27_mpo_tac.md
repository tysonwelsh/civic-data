Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Modification Bob Allen, MAG Senior Transportation Planner and Program Manager a.   Lindon Heritage Trail Phase 5 (2:40) In 2018, Lindon was awarded $1,589,850 in federal funds to complete the final phase of the Lindon Heritage Trail from Murdock Canal Trail to the Lindon Boat Harbor. This phase of the project includes 2 railroad crossings and a corridor through an industrial area. The project is now ready to go out to bid. Due to permitting delays with the railroad and corridor acquisition as well as construction inflation the project is now estimated at $2,677,101. Lindon is requesting additional funds to complete the project.   Original funds $1,589,580 Additional funds $1,087,251 New MAG total $2,677,101 Remaining Contingency Funds $4,696,216. MPO Technical Advisory Committee November 27, 2023 Page 3 Brad Kenison moved to recommend the addition of $1,087,251 be added to the Lindon Heritage Trail Phase 5. Aaron Wilson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. b.  Payson Main Street Bus Pullout (12:22) In 2020, Payson was awarded $292,000 in federal funds to shift and improve a bus pullout near the intersection of Main Street and SR198.  Buses using the existing stop impede traﬃc and create problems at an already congested intersection. The project is now ready to go out to bid. Due to construction inflation the project is now estimated at $532,138.  UDOT has already added $119,768 in TSP funds to the project.  Payson is requesting $140,138 MAG funds be added to complete the project.   Original funds $292,000 Additional funds $140,138 New MAG total $432,138 UDOT funds $119,768 New project totals $532,138 Remaining Contingency Funds $4,556,078. Aaron Wilson moved to recommend the addition of $140,138 to the Payson Bus Pullout project. Richard Nielson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
