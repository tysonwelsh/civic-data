BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 22.58
acres located on the south side of Highland Dr., at approximately 166 East Highland Dr.
(Exhibit B & C). The property is currently zoned CR and OS. The applicant is requesting that
a Plat Amendment be approved to subdivide the property into two lots.

The property currently includes two parcels. One parcel is 17.28 acres in size and vacant.
The second parcel is 5.29 acres in size and is currently developed as The Seasons mixed
use building. Combined the properties are Lot 1 of the South Pointe Commerce Center
Subdivision Plat. In 2007 Lot 1 was divided into two parcels outside of the City approval
process. This plat will formalize the subdivision of the two parcels, creating Lots 4 and 5 of
the South Pointe Commerce Center plat. The plat will also add a 0.40 acre strip of land
located along the east side of the The Seasons parcel into the subdivision, included in Lot 4.
This strip of land is owned by The Seasons and part of their development site. It ranges
from 34-feet wide to 10-feet wide.

Engineering and Public Works Divisions Review.
       1.     All subdivision improvements, specifically public improvements including
              frontage improvements, utilities, etc., are required to be installed as part of
              the subdivision approval, see DCMC 17-1-070. Proposed Lot 5 does not have
              sidewalk or park strip. Curb and Gutter is existing along public rights-of-way.

              All public frontage improvements are required for site plan(s) approval per
              DCMC 9-27-100. Public improvements will be required when each lot is submitted
              for site plan approval due to the unknown usage and public impact. With the
              current plat amendment application, the use and public impact of future
              commercial lot(s) are not known. This may require additional public right-of-way
              dedication for turning lanes or public street widening at the time of site plan
              approval.

       2.     Provide a traffic impact study for the subdivision per DCMC 17-3-030(G). Final
              version of the TIS for the development agreement and zone change is
              acceptable for this application. Plat amendment application does not include any
              land use or site plan for Lot 5. Without having proposed uses quantified, public
              impact cannot be assessed. TIS will be required with site plan for Lot 5 to determine
              impacts to public infrastructure.
       3.     Once final plat has been approved, a Land Disturbance Permit is required prior
              to construction activities onsite. Permit is obtained through the Engineering
              Division. Informational comment.
       4.     Any work in a public right-of-way will require an Encroachment Permit
              obtained through the Engineering Division. Informational comment.
       5.     Dedication of public right-of-way required per DCMC 9-27-100. Provide
              dedication for park strip and sidewalk on Traverse Ridge Road. Public right-of-
              way dedication will be required when each lot is submitted for site plan approval
              due to the unknown usage and public impact. With the current plat amendment
              application, the use and public impact of future commercial lot(s) are not known.
              This may require additional public right-of-way dedication for turning lanes or
              public street widening at the time of site plan approval.
       6.     All grading and drainage design will be required for site plan approval.
       7.     All utilities and streetlights will be required for site plan approval.
       8.     Public right-of-way improvements and mitigation to public infrastructure
              impacts due to site use to be addressed with site plan approval.
