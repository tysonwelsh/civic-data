3.   Public hearing and possible action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for a Wind 
Power Generation Facility located off the Wahsatch Road Exit from Interstate 80; 
Echo Wind Divide LLC, Applicant – Ray Milliner, Principal Planner 
 
Planner Milliner shared the background for this application and noted that since there is 
not a quorum for this meeting, this item is not a Public Hearing. The application is for up to 
forty wind turbines greater than forty-five feet in height near the Utah-Wyoming border. 
There was a Work Session not long ago where a proposal was shown to the Planning

Commission. The applicant was asked to have simulations of the turbines without a snowy 
landscape. Those simulations were shown. 
Chair Clyde stated his concern is for the shadow flicker for the residents in Wyoming, 
about one mile from the proposed turbine site. Spencer Martin, representing the 
applicant, stated there was a shadow flicker study done showing that the shadow flicker 
would only be in effect, at worst, 28 hours per year, or less than three minutes per day, 
which is below industry standards. The best way to eliminate shadow flicker is through 
landscaping. Greg Probst, also representing the applicant, stated that the applicant will 
work with the landowners to address any concerns and price landscaping. These are 
solvable issues. 
Chair Clyde asked if this project would affect the property values for empty lot owners in 
the area. Mr. Martin answered that all property owners within a three-mile radius have 
been notified and no concerns have been raised. 
Planner Milliner has contacted the Uinta County, Wyoming Planner, who is aware of this 
project but had no comments for the Staff Report. There was no call back from the Rich 
County Planner. 
Public Comment was taken. 
Kent Williams is the Uinta County, Wyoming Planner and has a history working with wind 
farms, with a project to commence soon. Uinta County, Wyoming has had a fairly favorable 
relationship with wind energy in general. Many turbines have a flashing red light that 
identifies the turbine for air traffic. It is helpful if there is a ground blinder for the red light 
to not be so visible from nearby homes. There have not been any complaints to Uinta

County, Wyoming about shadow flicker. Uinta County, Wyoming is supportive of these 
types of projects. This project would be on private property, and Uinta County takes the 
rights of private property owners seriously. There have not been any negative comments 
from Uinta County, Wyoming residents. 
Chair Clyde stated that this project is so radically different from anything that the Planning 
Commission has dealt with and asked if Uinta County has had to hire outside engineers for

County has not hired outside engineers. Uinta County has used a Conditional Use Permit

the County. Uinta County would be happy to email copies of permits for Summit County to 
reference. 
Planner Milliner stated that reviews should not be a problem. There are consultants on 
retainer for these types of projects. The turbines are pre-fabricated. The base will be built 
on-site and the turbine will be tilted onto the structure. Power lines and roads will be run 
to the turbines. All permits will be required before construction begins. 
Chair Clyde stated the Planning Department and Planning Commission will look at the 
existing Uinta County, Wyoming permits and will schedule a Public Hearing and re-notice 
for this application.

Work Session  
1. Discussion regarding possible creation of a Recreation Commercial (RC) Zoning 
District. – Ray Milliner, Principal Planner 
 
Planner Milliner shared the background for this discussion. The Recreation Commercial 
(RC) Zone was discussed by the Planning Commission during the time of the amendments 
to Chapters 3 and 4 of the Development Code. The RC Zone was holding up the process, so 
it was pulled from the recommendation, to be discussed later. There was a member of the 
public that asked for the RC Zone to be discussed again. Planner Milliner has not made 
any changes to the language of the RC Zone since the last discussion. The most recent copy 
of the language is found in the Staff Report. The language was discussed by the Planning 
Commission. Agricultural Tourism is included in the RC Zone, but would require its own 
Section as an expansion of the RC Zone. 
Chair Clyde stated that the intent of the RC Zone is to have facilities to support commercial 
entities at the reservoirs and ski resorts. 
Commissioner Sonntag stated that the Planning Commission needs to be careful to not 
separate the Zoning Act, which is Legislative, from the Master Planning. Planner Milliner 
stated that rezoning eligibility is included with a Master Plan Development.

Chair Clyde stated that size limitations could be a problem. Overnight lodging is a stretch 
in the reservoir context, but essential in the ski resort context. There should not be a height 
limit, but the height should be determined through review by the Planning Commission, 
with the limit being no less than required by the existing zones. There are certain setbacks 
that must conform to existing zoning requirements, at a minimum. RC uses are impactful 
uses and require strict setbacks. 
Planner Milliner stated the density would be determined through a floor area ratio, with a 
generous 2.0 floor area ratio. Planner Milliner will work to make the height, floor area 
ratio, and lot coverage mesh better. Performance standards are taken from the Commercial 
Zone for Eastern Summit County. Parking requirements listed are the standard. Chair 
Clyde stated that having parking in the back is not always the best option. Proper 
infrastructure will be required for all facilities.  
Planner Milliner stated there is a Use List for consideration in the Staff Report. Chair 
Clyde suggested some additional uses could be a boat or jet ski rental, a water park, a 
fueling station for boats, a convenience store, and a bike shop. It may be a good idea to 
designate separate processes for a ski resort, water recreation, and agricultural tourism. 
Planner Milliner will start adding the suggested uses to the Use List and consider 
separating the ordinances for the different types of recreation. Chair Clyde stated it needs 
to be clear that proper infrastructure will be required. The State Parks should be asked 
about possibilities and jurisdiction and work in tandem. 
A member of the public asked about a potential gas line that might be put in Marion on Rob 
Young Lane. Planner Milliner will meet with her about her concerns.
