2. Public hearing and possible action regarding a Low Impact Permit to construct a 
single-family residence that will project into the horizon line as viewed from a 
designated roadway; 6395 N. Silver Sage Dr.; HE-B-270, Michael Sapp, applicant – 
Amir Caus, County Planner 
 
County Planner Amir Caus presented the staff report and explained that the plat for this 
lot was recorded in 1964.  The applicant proposes a 4,800-square-foot single-family 
residence on a 2.55-acre parcel.  The Development Code requires any structure projecting 
into the horizon from a designated roadway to be subject to a Low Impact Permit (LIP) 
review.  Due to concerns about a previous project in the same neighborhood, Staff chose 
to bring this to a public hearing to see if there are any concerns about the project.  The 
mitigation includes having only 11,000 square feet of disturbance instead of the 15,000 
square feet allowed and limiting the height to 26 feet instead of the 32-foot surrounding 
structures.  He explained that there is no way to build a house on this lot without 
projecting into the horizon, and the house will be built in the middle of the lot to decrease 
visibility from other vantage points on I-80.  Staff recommended that the Planning 
Commission forward a positive recommendation to the Community Development 
Director for this LIP based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of 
approval shown in the staff report.

There was no public comment.
