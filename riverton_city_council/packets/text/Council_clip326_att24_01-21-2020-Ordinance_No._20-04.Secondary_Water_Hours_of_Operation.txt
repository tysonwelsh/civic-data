                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   ORDINANCE NO. 20-04

   AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 13, CHAPTER 10 RIVERTON CITY CODE
          PROHIBITING USE OF SECONDARY WATER IRRIGATION
                    BETWEEN 10:00 AM AND 6:00 PM.

       WHEREAS, Title 13, of the Riverton City Code establishes and governs the secondary
water utility for Riverton City; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council is improving our secondary water system by
installing water meters for the purpose of educating the public about water conservation and
encouraging a reduction in excess consumption; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the City can receive
funding from the state of Utah to help finance the cost of installing secondary water meters by
providing low interest lending; and

       WHEREAS, as a condition of receiving said funding, the State of Utah, Division of
Water resources requires that we follow state administrative rules adopted to encourage
conservation; and

       WHEREAS, State Administrative Rule R 653-2-4 requires municipalities to adopt an
ordinance prohibiting municipal irrigation of landscapes between the hours of 10:00 am. and
6:00 pm.; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that prohibiting municipal
irrigation of landscapes between the hours of 10:00 am and 6:00 pm will advance the interests of
Riverton City to conserve secondary water by delivering the same during hours of the day which
reduce evapotranspiration.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
RIVERTON CITY, UTAH as follows:

       Section 1.     Title 13 of Riverton City Code is hereby amended as described in the
                      attached Exhibit “A” incorporated herein by reference.
       Section 2.     This Ordinance shall take effect upon publication or upon posting as
                      required by law.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 21st day of

13.10.320 Schedule of use.

(1) The water director may schedule the use of the secondary water, in the event it is determined
that the use needs to be scheduled in order to meet the demands of the system.

(2) Subject to subsection (1) of this section, sprinkler irrigation of public and private
landscapes is prohibited between the hours of 10:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. The provisions of this
section shall apply to all landscapes within the City with the following exceptions:
       (a) New lawns or other sensitive landscaping that require frequent irrigation for
       establishment purposes within 90 days of planting.

       (b) Short cycles required for testing, inspecting and maintaining irrigation systems.

       (c) Where pressure problems exist in the water infrastructure requiring an alternate
       watering time as approved by the Department.
(3) Any person who continues to violate this schedule after written notice may be subject to
an infraction.
