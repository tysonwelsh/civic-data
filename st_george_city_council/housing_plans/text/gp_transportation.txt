Transportation
St. George City will increase access across the city by enhancing the diversity of transportation options available, expanding transit ridership, increasing active transportation, and reducing the need to drive across the city for daily services. These strategies will enhance accessibility for everyone.
4.1 ENHANCE TRANSPORTATION OPTIONS
Enhance transportation options through the continuation and expansion of a comprehensive trail network to support recreation and active transportation within St. George.
a. Integrate the trail system into the urban fabric through improved connections between trail networks and urban streets.
i. Implement improvements noted in the Downtown Area Plan to enhance the urban trail network in St. George.
b. Provide clear wayfinding signage throughout the city, including at trailheads, along trails, and within the urban fabric, to improve connectivity and access within St. George.
i. Consider the implementation of a wayfinding app for residents and visitors
4.2 . ENCOURAGE COMPLETE STREETS AND WALKABLE NEIGHBORHOODS THROUGHOUT ST. GEORGE
a. Prioritize pedestrian/bicycle improvements on roadways with no current pedestrian/bicycle infrastructure.
b. Strategically implement Complete Streets improvements across the city.
i. ii. For each Neighborhood Area, identify streets to be prioritized for Complete Street improvements. Implement Downtown Area Plan recommendations for Complete Streets.
c. Ensure all new roadways and all redevelopment or road upgrades meet the Complete Streets Policy.
d. Align transportation networks with growth to the south and east of the city.
e. Implement improvements identified within the current Transportation Master Plan.
i. Prioritize east/west and north/south connections to improve traffic and flow through the city.
ii. iii. Re-establish grid street network as physically feasible to improve connectivity throughout the city. Support the development of commercial centers nodes at crucial intersections or destinations to support neighborhoods and reduce traffic congestion for daily services. Align with goals identified in Responsible Growth.
4.3  EXPAND TRANSIT
a. Implement on-demand transit services and integrate this service with the Suntran system to support current and future needs within St. George.
b. Continually gather data on existing transit ridership, route and stop use, and time of use trends to monitor transit system and inform future transit improvements.
c. Create a new transit master plan to serve the City.
i. Identify opportunities to expand the network through traditional and innovative approaches, increasing accessibility to transit for all.
ii. iii. iv. v. vi. vii. viii. Prioritize key routes that connect commercial centers to workers. Align with the vision for Neighborhood Areas and Neighborhood Commercial Centers as presented in this plan. Provide strategies to address the community’s first/last mile needs (e.g., active transportation connections to transit). Identify routes to increase access between the workforce and their jobs. Identify key routes within the city for transit optimization. Identify future bus rapid transit (BRT) routes. Focus on investments that improve transit travel time and reliability.
d. Implement transit improvements and investment between the St. George Regional Hospital and Tech Ridge, with improved access to Utah Tech University and the Downtown Arts District.
e. Conduct a public outreach program to encourage transit use and support positive transit experiences.
i. Consider providing free transit days and free or discounted passes for high school students and young adults.
4.4
UPDATE PARKING STANDARDS ACROSS ZONE DISTRICTS TO ALIGN WITH THE VISION FOR THE CITY
a. Align the parking standards with the vision for pedestrian- oriented and walkable centers.
b. Consider updating parking standards in mixed-use districts to accommodate shared parking where complementary uses exist.
c. Consider parking maximums in key zone districts where shared parking is available and viable.
d. Improve access to parking through improved signage and wayfinding to public parking in the downtown and commercial centers.
