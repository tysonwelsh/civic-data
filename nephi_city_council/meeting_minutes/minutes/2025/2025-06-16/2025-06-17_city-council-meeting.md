ANNEXATION AREA MAP CHANGE
      Seth Atkinson presented the planning commission’s recommendation to add an area to the
      annexation area map as a Level 3 for potential annexation. Recent developments have occurred
      to require Nephi City to consider a change to the annexation area map. This area is fairly
      extensive and exists as an island to the west of the current Nephi City boundaries. The
      developments being considered are the Agri-Park project that Juab County and the R6 Regional
      Council are spearheading, and also developers for data center projects.
      Some of the reasons that Nephi City would support these projects are the tax and utility revenue
      from the developments proposed. In addition, the data center projects are not likely to yield a
        large influx of jobs or population and thus not have large impacts on the city’s housing market or
        school system. The Agri-Park may have an impact on jobs and population growth depending on
        the types of businesses which locate within the park. However, it may have a very positive effect
        on the farmland surrounding the current cities in Juab County.
        Nephi City is in a unique position to be one of the only entities who could provide the necessary
        infrastructure to these projects which is why it is necessary to have these areas be annexed into
        the city limits. State law prevents the city from providing electricity to areas outside of the city
        limits.
        Councilor Jeramie Callaway made the motion to add the areas to the annexation map as a level
        3. Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

ANNEXATION AREA MAP LEVEL CHANGE REQUEST
      Seth Atkinson presented the area from 5th south to 8th south on 4th west to move from a level 2
      to a level 1 on the city’s annexation map. The conclusions of the Level 2 analysis are that the
      area would mainly fit with the city’s general plan and growth pattern. The properties in the
      annexation area all have city utilities and so annexing into the city would also provide property
      taxes to the city for general services. The properties are also contiguous with the city’s current
      boundary. It is recommended that the petitioner also submit a concept plan for any
      development proposed.
      Councilor Skip Worwood motioned to approve the area as a Level 1 on the city’s annexation
      map. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

MONA CITY NATURAL GAS SYSTEM AGREEMENT
      Seth Atkinson explained that Mona has an employee who handles these services for them and
      that employee is retiring at the beginning of the fiscal year.
      Nephi City has been under agreement to provide on-call and backup services to Mona City for

BUDGET
   a. FINAL FY 2026-2027 BIENNIAL BUDGET
       Seth Atkinson detailed the final FY 2026-2027 Biennial Budget. Nephi City employs a two-
       year, or biennial budget, to make a financial plan for city departments. The fiscal biennium
       would begin on July 1, 2025 and go through June 30, 2027. Through this process the Nephi City
       Council, with recommendations from city departments, sets their priorities for the future. City
       staff use the resources available to carry out these priorities. The priorities of the Nephi City
       Council for the next two fiscal years can be viewed on the city's website. They are divided
       into General Services (services mainly paid for with taxes) and Utility Services (services for
       infrastructure provided through fees).
       Councilor Shari Cowan moved to approve the FY 2026-2027 Biennial Budget. Councilor Skip
       Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
