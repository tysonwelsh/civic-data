Recommendation 
Staff recommends that the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission (ESCPC) review the 
proposed Plat Amendment, conduct a public hearing, and vote to approve the Hidden Lake 
Ranch Subdivision Lots 62 & 64 Amended Plat that proposes to combine three lots and vacate a 
private road, pursuant to the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and Conditions of Approval 
found in this Staff Report. Staff have reviewed the application for compliance with all standards 
in the Eastern Summit County Development Code and have found that it meets the 
requirements for approval.  
 
Project Description 
Project Name:   Hidden Lake Ranch Subdivision, Lots 62& 64 Amended Plat 
Applicant(s):   Langley Kushman 
Property Owner(s):  Kathryn Scherer 
Location:   8163 Aspen Loop 
Zone District:   AG-80 
Parcel Number and Size: HL-62 (0.29 acres), HL-64 (0.24 acres), and HL-Roads-62 (0.01 acres) 
Type of Process:   Administrative 
Final Land Use Authority: Eastern Summit County Planning Commission  
 
Proposal 
The applicant, on behalf of the property owner, is requesting a plat amendment to combine Lot 
HL-62, Lot HL-64 and Parcel HL-Roads-62 to create one lot totaling 0.55 acres. They also petition 
to vacate the portion of the platted private road within parcel HL-Roads-62. These proposed 
changes are depicted in Exhibit A.  
 
  
3
                                             2 
 
Vicinity Map 
 
 
 
Background 
Hidden Lake Ranch Subdivision was recorded May 5, 1962 (Exhibit B). It consisted of 162 lots. In 
2007, a portion of the unbuilt but platted road adjacent to Lot HL-62 was transferred by the 
Hidden Lake Association to the owners of Lot HL-62. The owner of HL-62 now wants to 
combine the unbuilt road parcel known as HL-Roads-62 with Lot HL-62 and Lot HL-64 to 
create one lot. This amendment includes vacating the portion of private subdivision road 
known as parcel HL-Roads-62. The private subdivision road, Aspen Loop, was not built on 
the HL-Roads-62 parcel.  
 
Analysis and Findings 
Section 11-4-5(E.10) of the Eastern Summit County Development Code sets the following 
grounds for vacating or amending a subdivision plat:  
 
1. If the Land Use Authority is satisfied that the public interest will not be materially 
injured by the proposed vacation, alteration or amendment, and there is good cause for 
the vacation, alteration or amendment, the Land Use Authority, may vacate, alter or 
amend a public or private road or any portion of the final subdivision plat.  
  
Analysis: Lot HL-62 and Lot HL-64 and parcel HL-Roads-62 are owned by Kathryn 
Scherer. Because some sections of the platted road were not built, the HOA transferred 
those sections, including HL-Roads-62 to the adjacent lot owners. This plat amendment 
would legally combine the road parcel and the two lots into one lot and vacate the 
private road.  COMPLIES 
 
2. No plat amendment shall be approved which results in an increase in density unless the 
requirements listed in subsection E6 are met. 
 
4
                                             3 
 
 Analysis: Density is defined in the Code as, “The number of dwelling units per acre with 
respect to residential land uses.” The amendment will not result in an additional 
dwelling unit. COMPLIES 
 
Public Notice, Meetings and Comments  
Public notice was published as a public hearing and possible action in The Park Record and 
individual notices were mailed to property owners within 1,000’ from the boundaries of Lot HL-
62, Lot HL-64 and parcel HL-Roads-62. As of the date of this report, no public comment has 
been received. 
 
Findings of Fact: 
1. Lot HL-62, Lot HL-64 and parcel HL-Roads-62 are located within the AG-80 (Agriculture 
80) zoning district in the Hidden Lake Ranch Subdivision. The address for HL-62 is 8163 
Aspen Loop.  
2. On May 7, 1962 the Hidden Lake Ranch Subdivision (Exhibit B) was recorded in Summit 
County, Entry Number 95105.  
3. Lot HL-62 consists of 0.29 acres, Lot HL-64 consists of 0.24 acres, and parcel HL-Roads-
62 is 0.01 acres. 
4. Kathryn Scherer owns Lot HL-62, Lot HL-64 and parcel HL-Roads-62. 
5. On March 4, 2026 the applicant, Langley Kushman, on behalf of Kathryn Scherer, 
submitted a complete plat amendment application to combine the three parcels.  
6. Service Providers, including the County Recorder’s Office and County Engineer’s Office 
reviewed the proposed plat amendment and gave comments. 
7. Prior to recording the plat, the applicant will update the plat to comply with the 
comments of the Service Providers.  
8. Density is defined in the Code as, “The number of dwelling units per acre with respect to 
residential land uses.”  
9. The amendment will not result in an additional residential unit. No new lots, tracts, or 
parcels are created through the plat amendment.  
 
Conclusions of Law:  
1. Public interest will not be materially injured by the proposed amendment and there is 
good cause for the amendment.  
2. The amendment would not result in increased density. 
 
Conditions of Approval:

amendment. 
2. Prior to issuance of a building permit for any structure on the new Lot HL-62, the

Proposal 
The applicant is requesting to allocate eight (8) units of Incentive Density and one (1) Unit of 
Estate Base Density in the Promontory Specially Planned Area, located off Golf Club Link Drive 
(South of the Promontory Links Edge Subdivision). 
 
 
 
  
13

Background 
The Promontory Development Agreement was approved on January 2, 2001. It provided for 
885 estate lots as part of a base density, 716 incentive density lots, two hotels/300 resort units, 
equestrian facility, up to five golf club houses, and other accessory uses on approximately 6500 
acres with no less than 3900 acres remaining as passive open space. Of the 1,901 total units in 
the density pool, 1,535 units have been approved.  
 
Analysis and Findings 
The applicant is using eight (8) Units of Incentive Density and one (1) Unit of Estate Base 
Density from the existing pool of density. Staff and the applicant verified that the proposed 
subdivision is not located on a designated ridgeline per Exhibit B of the Development 
Agreement. 
 
Service Providers 
Staff has not received any negative service provider comments which would hinder the 
proposal.  
14
Promontory Conservancy

Development Agreement Requirements 
The Development Agreement delegates decision making powers to the Board of County 
Commissioners for all Final Plats. With the change of government that took place, the former 
Board of County Commissioner’s administrative duties diverted to the County Manager and the

Type of Item:  General Plan Amendment – Work Session 
Process:   Legislative  
 
 
Proposal 
The purpose of this work session is to obtain feedback and direction from the Eastern Summit 
County Planning Commission regarding the proposed Future Land Use Map (FLUM) for the 
Eastern Summit County General Plan update. Staff is seeking input on the land use designations, 
map clarity, and whether the proposed future land use pattern reflects Eastern Summit County 
goals and policy direction established throughout the draft General Plan.  
 
Background 
During the February 19 work session, the Planning Commission reviewed Chapter 1 and 2 of the 
Eastern Summit County  General Plan  draft. This work session continues that review with a 
discussion of the proposed Future Land Use Map. The Future Land Use Map provides a long -
range framework for future growth, development, and land use decisions within Eastern Summit 
County.  
To facilitate the Commissioner’s review, staff has developed an interactive web map that allows 
Commissioners to provide comments and identify suggested revisions directly on the draft Future 
Land Use Map. Commissioners may add comments, mark locations for potential changes. Link to 
Interactive Map 
 
Recommendation 
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the proposed Future Land Use Map of 
the draft Eastern Summit County General Plan and provide staff with direction on the future 
land use designations, including the appropriateness of the proposed future land use 
designations and any revisions the Commission believes should be considered.
