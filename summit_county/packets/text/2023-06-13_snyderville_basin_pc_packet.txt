Background 
Parcel SMT-A-100 is a 0.15-acre platted l ot within the Silver Summit Community Phase A  
subdivision, which was recorded in December of 1983 . The lot is within the Rural Residential 
(RR) zone, which requires a minimum side setback of twelve feet (12’)  and a minimum front 
setback of thirty feet (30’). The existing single-family dwelling is located approximately four feet 
(4’) feet from the north side property line  at the closest point and approximately twenty-three 
feet two inches (23’-2”) from the front property line.   
 
The applicant proposes to co nstruct an addition that expands the footprint of the existing 
garage, located on the north side of the property.  The proposed addition would extend the 
existing garage toward the front property line  and toward the north side property line to meet 
the exi sting structure.  Also, the addition would expand the second story to extend over this 
proposed new footprint.  None of the proposed additions extend further into the side or front 
setbacks than the existing structure.   
 
Including a modified ridge to acco mmodate the proposed addition, the maximum height of the 
structure would be twenty-four feet three and three-quarters inches (24’ 3 ¾”).

Section 10-8-1(I)(3)(d) of the Development Code states that an expansion of a nonconforming 
structure’s wall is proposed to be higher than the existing wall and is not set back by at least 
twenty-four feet from the closest neighboring primary dwelling structure wall or meeting the 
zone required setback, a Conditional Use Permit shall be required.  The existing structure is 
approximately fourteen feet (14’) from the closest neighboring primary dwelling structure.   
 
Review from all service providers stated that the prop osal meets the minimum requirements 
for approval.

Findings of Fact: 
1. The applicant, Stefan Brutsch, on behalf of the homeowner, is seeking conditional use 
approval to expand a nonconforming structure located on parcel SMT -A-100 at 5672 N 
Kingsford Ave.  
2. Christopher Lee is the property owner of parcel SMT-A-100. 
3. Parcel Number SMT-A-100 is 0.15 acres in size. 
4. The property is located in the Rural Residential (RR) zone district. 
5. The RR zone requires a minimum sid e setback of twelve feet (1 2’) and a minimum front 
setback of thirty feet (30’).   
6. Per Development Code 10 -8-1(I)(3)(a), at lea st fifty percent (50%) of the existing 
structure walls to be expanded, from which the setback is measured, must be 
nonconforming. 
7. Per Development Code 10 -8-1(I)(3)(b), additions to nonconforming structures may 
extend to the existing nonconforming setback line, but not enc roach farther into the 
setback.   
8. Per Development Code 10 -8-1(I)(3)(c), in no case shall the addition be closer than fifty 
percent (50%) of the zone required setback. 
9.  Per Development Code 10 -8-1(I)(3)(d), the existing non- conforming wall to be 
expanded shall not be increased in height, unless it is set back at least twenty-four feet 
(24') from closest neighboring primary dwelling structure wall or meeting the zone 
required setback. If the expansion of the non- conforming wall is proposed to be higher 
than the existing wall, is not set back at least twenty-four feet (24') from the closest 
neighboring primary dwelling structure wall or meeting the zone required setback, a 
conditional use permit shall be required. In no case can the expansion be higher than 
the maximum height requirement allowed in section 10-4-22. 
10. At least 50% of the walls to be expanded are non-conforming. 
11. The proposed addition is located on the northern  portion of the existing structure, 
extending the garage forw ard to what is the existing  front wall and side setback lines 
and expanding the second story above this proposed new footprint. 
9
7 
 
12. The portion of the existing structure that will be added to is currently only four (4’) from 
the no rthern side property line.  The current structure is approximately twenty -three 
(23’) feet from the front property line. 
13. Development Code 10 -8-1(I)(3)(c) standard requires that in no case can the expansion 
be closer fifty percent (50%) of the zone required twelve (12’) foot setback. The addition 
has been pushed back two (2’) feet approximately (6’) feet from the north side property 
line and twenty -three feet, two inches  (23’-2”) from the front property line . The 
proposed second story  addition will go directly above the e xisting wall and thus will 
have the same setback. 
14. In the RR zone, structures can be a maximum of thirty -two feet (32’) in height per 
regulations in section 10-4-22 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code.   
15. The second story addition will increas e the height to  twenty-four feet three and three-
quarters inches (24’ 3 ¾”).   
16. The application was routed to affected county departments, state agencies and service 
providers. No issues were raised.
