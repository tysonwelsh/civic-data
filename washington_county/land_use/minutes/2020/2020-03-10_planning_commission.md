Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                                March 10, 2020
The Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting was held on Tuesday, March 10, 2020 in the
Council Chambers of the Washington County Administration Building at 197 E. Tabernacle, St.
George, UT. Chairman Kevin Jones opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of Allegiance
and then explained meeting protocol.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Shayne Durrant, Kevin Jones, Slade Hughes, Mark
Owens, Reed Scow (Alternate acting as a voting member), and Jon Walter.

Excused: Dan Briggs (Alternate), Doug Solstad.

Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Brian Graf, Deputy County Attorney;
Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Kurt Gardner, Building Official; Todd Edwards,
County Engineer; Gil Almquist, County Commissioner; Kim Hafen, County Clerk.


1.     PUBLIC HEARING FOR AMENDMENTS TO THE RESOURCE MANAGEMENT
PLAN ELEMENT OF THE GENERAL PLAN. Review and discussion of the amendments to the
Resource Management Plan Element to the Washington County General Plan.

Brian Graf explained the implementation of the Resource Management Plan as required by State Law.
Washington County adopted their Resource Management Plan into the General Plan in 2017. Anytime
there are changes to the General Plan, a public hearing is required. We recently made some revisions
and updates to the plan and so we are here asking the commission to review the changes and to hold
the public hearing. He asked if there were any questions.

The planning commission and staff discussed and clarified the changes within the plan. The
commission had a few minor questions, which Mr. Graf and Mr. Messel answered. Commissioner
Owens suggested the commission review the documents and send any comments they had to Mr.
Messel or Mr. Graf.

Chairman Jones opened the floor to the public hearing.

No one was in attendance who wished to speak on the item.

Commissioner Hughes commented that this resource is helpful and something that the county uses.

Chairman Jones closed the floor to the public hearing.

Chairman Jones asked if the commission would like to take action on the item or wait. Commissioner
Walter answered he concurred with Commissioner Owens to review the documents and submit
comments back to staff.

MOTION:        Commissioner Durrant MOVED to table approval of the changes to the

2.     FINAL PLAT APPROVAL FOR PINE VALLEY REC SUBDIVISION. Review and
approve the final plat of the Pine Valley Rec Subdivision located at approximately 1000 W. Cottom
Ranch Road in Veyo. The parcel number is 7139-G-NW. The applicants are John Leavitt and Slade
Hughes.

Mr. Messel explained that back in February 2019, the applicants requested a conditional use permit
approval for a Rural Recreational Ground and Facility project on Parcel 7139-G-NW, which is
approximately 19.93 acres in size. The proposed project was for a RV park with cabins and tent pads.
The County Commission denied the project at that time due to several issues. In the fall of 2019, the
property owners applied to rezone the property from the OST-20 (Open Space Transition 20 acre
minimum lot size) zone to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone and subdivide the parcel
into two lots. The proposed Lot 1 would be 10 acres in size and Lot 2 would be 9.41 acres in size. The
zone change and preliminary plat were approved in December of 2019. Staff reviewed and approved
the construction drawings and the property owners have now submitted application for final plat
approval. The proposed subdivision is located at approximately 1000 N. Cottom Ranch Road in Veyo.
The applicants have submitted a will serve letter for the water to each lot. Septic is also available.
Access to each lot will be off Cottam Ranch Road.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the final plat and recommends that the Planning Commission approve the final plat
based on the following findings and conditions:

 1.    The subdivision would allow the applicants a means to utilize their property in a beneficial way.
 2.    The subdivision is consistent with the General Plan.
 3.    The subdivision meets the applicable Washington County Land Use Ordinances.
 4.    The proposed final plat is consistent with the approved preliminary plat.

The applicant had no comments.

Todd Edwards, County Engineer stated the plat is straightforward. It is a little odd because the road
runs through the lots. The construction drawings have been approved. They show the road to be asphalt.
However, the rest of the road is not. They could go to the county commission to see if they would
waive the asphalt requirement.

Mr. Messel explained why the road ran through the lots.

A discussion regarding affordable housing ensued between staff, the planning commission, and Karl
Rasmussen, Pro-Value Engineering and the applicant’s engineer.

3.      CONCEPT PLAN DEVELOPMENT PRESENTATION. Presentation and review of a
possible development in the Grassy Flats area near Pine Valley. The applicant is James Lear.

Mr. Messel introduced Mr. James Lear who provided a presentation on the proposed “The Gathering
Place at Grassy Flat” project.

Mr. Lear thanked staff and the commission for their time. He provided handouts of the concept plan
and explained that the project is being designed for family reunions. It may also be utilized for
corporate retreats. He explained the location and indicated the two privately owned, approximately
300-acre parcels zoned OST-20 (Open Space Transition 20 acre minimum lot size) are currently used
as Ag land with some grazing. They would consider creating a conservation easement on the property
to maintain the grazing of the land. He provided conceptual drawings of the proposed buildings, the
floor layout, and the construction plans. The buildings would be approximately 12,000 sq.ft, two-story
units and some 1,500 sq.ft, two-story single-family dwelling units around the buildings. He noted the
total number of occupants would be a maximum of eighty. When the family is not using the units, they
would be rented out in totality for family reunions or corporate gatherings. He stated there is plenty of
water to serve the project and explained the design standards. He also addressed the septic/sewer
requirements and design, the roads and other standards. There are still some challenges and items to
address before the project design is completed and they could move forward.

The project was further discussed where staff and the commission provided feedback to Mr. Lear.
Their concerns were with traffic, emergency access, fire, number of people that would be in each
building, the project layout and amenities, and the impact on the Pine Valley area.

County Ordinances: Title 10, Chapter 4-1: Definitions; and Chapters 6A and 6B: Open Space Zones.

The commission and staff discussed the various minor changes and updates. A lengthy discussion
regarding recreational lands, campgrounds, and RV Parks, ensued.

The commission indicated that they would review the changes and provide feedback to staff.
