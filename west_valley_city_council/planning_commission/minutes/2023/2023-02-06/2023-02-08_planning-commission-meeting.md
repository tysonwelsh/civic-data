 C-1-2023
 Northrop Grumman Finishing Building
 8 5000 South U-111 Hwy
 M Zone (11.32 acres)


 Eric Malmberg with AWA is requesting conditional use approval for a new manufacturing
 building at the Northrop Grumman facility located at 5000 S U-111 Hwy. The property is
 zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley City General Plan designates the area as
 Heavy Manufacturing. The adjacent area around the future building is zoned M also.

 The proposed development will impact 11.32 acres of Northrop Grumman’s overall larger site.
 A new 46,844 sf building is proposed and will be used for processing rocket motors. The
 building does not need to meet the Commercial Design Standards, due to it being for a use that
 is only allowed in the M Zone. It will be internal to the site, with no frontage on a public
 street. Access to the building will be from existing private roads. The parking requirement for
 manufacturing uses is 1 stall per 800 sf. The proposed parking area is required to have 58
 stalls.

 The landscaping requirement in the M zone is 5%. Historically, formal landscaping at
 Northrop Grumman has not been required for projects more than 300 feet from the public
 right-of-way. Given this precedent, no landscaping improvements are required at this
 time.

 No signage is being proposed. Any future signage on the property or building will need to
 comply with Title 11.


 Staff Alternatives:

 Approval, subject to the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing and
 the following conditions.

    1.   The building and site shall be constructed per the approved plans.
    2.   Parking shall meet the requirements of 7-9 of the West Valley City Code.
    3.   Future signage shall comply with Title 11 of the West Valley City Code.
    4.   All requirements of effected departments and agencies shall be met.
    5.   Subject to review upon valid complaint.

 Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.


Discussion:    The applicant, Eric Malmberg, noted that he is with AWA Engineering. He said
               he is the project's civil engineer and is proposing the building to the Commission;
               however, he isn't fully aware of what they are planning to do inside the structure.
              Steve Pastorik pointed out that sometime last year, Northrup Grumman amended
              the Overpressure Zones that apply to the Northrup Grumman facility. He asked
              Mr. Malmberg if the structure proposed in this application, or the following
              application would impact those Overpressure Zones. Mr. Malmberg said that he is
              unaware of any impact.


      Motion:         Commissioner Winters motioned to approve C-1-2023 subject to the five

                      Commissioner McEwen seconded the motion.

                      Unanimously – C-1-2023 – Approved



C-2-2023
Northrop Grumman Shipping Building 6
5000 South U-111 Hwy
M Zone (13.5 acres)


Eric Malmberg with AWA is requesting conditional use approval for a new warehouse
building at the Northrop Grumman facility located at 5000 S U-111 Hwy. The property is
zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley City General Plan designates the area as Heavy
Manufacturing. The adjacent area around the future building is zoned M also.

The proposed development will impact 13.5 acres of Northrop Grumman’s overall larger site.
A new 58,576 sf building is proposed and will be used for the storage and distribution of
finished rocket motors. The building does not need to meet the Commercial Design Standards,
due to it being for a use that is only allowed in the M Zone. It will be internal to the site, with
no frontage on a public street. Access to the building will be from existing private roads. The
parking requirement for warehousing uses is 1 stall per 2,000 sf. The proposed parking area is
required to have 29 parking stalls.

The landscaping requirement in the M zone is 5%. Historically, formal landscaping at
Northrop Grumman has not been required for projects more than 300 feet from the public
right-of-way. Given this precedent, no landscaping improvements are required at this time.
 No signage is being proposed. Any future signage on the property or building will need to
 comply with Title 11.


 Staff Alternatives:

 Approval, subject to the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing and the
 following conditions.

    1.    The building and site shall be constructed per the approved plans.
    2.    Parking shall meet the requirements of 7-9 of the West Valley City Code.
    3.    Future signage shall comply with Title 11 of the West Valley City Code.
    4.    All requirements of effected departments and agencies shall be met.
    5.    Subject to review upon valid complaint.

 Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.


 Discussion: Eric Malmberg, the applicant, stated that this building is similar to the one
