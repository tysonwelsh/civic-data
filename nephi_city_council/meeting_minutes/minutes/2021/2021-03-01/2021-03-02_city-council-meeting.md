Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City
Recorder Lisa Brough offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  Heather Williams-Young, representing the Juab Children’s Justice Center led the Pledge of Allegiance
and then explained the services provided by her office.

LIONS CLUB/AMERICAN LEGION SEPTEMBER 11TH EVENT PRESENTATION:

   Robert Painter representing the Lions Club and Blair Painter representing the American Legion
explained to the council their plans for a September 11th commemorating event they are planning. They
asked for the council’s support for the event. The event will begin at 6:45 a.m. and will include a
moment of silence, the posting of the American flag as well as flags of each branch of the military, a
breakfast, 5K race, bake sale, patriotic program, and speeches, etc. They also asked if the city could
remove a tree at the Vine Bluff Cemetery that was damaging the flag posted at the veteran’s memorial
monument. The council expressed their appreciation and agreed to waive the fee for use of the city
park and will notify the cemetery sexton about the tree.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes for the regular council meeting held on 2-16-21, work session held on 2-16-21, work session
held on 2-23-21, sale of two burial rights to Kerry and Jill Gee, and a list of claims on the warrant register
dated 3-2-21. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

  Councilor Kent Jones reviewed two business license applications. The first one was Forthright
Massages and the second one was for Chrysalis. Councilor Jones moved to approve both license
applications with a contingency on approval of the Chrysalis application based on whether it complies
with the city ordinance that states how close in proximity the residential facility is to another residential
facility. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

NO PLANNING ITEMS

RATIFICATION OF EMPLOYMENT FOR SOLID WASTE COLLECTOR AND LIBRARY WORKER

   Councilor Memmott moved to ratify the hiring of Cody Hudson as the Solid Waste Collector and Amy
Zirbes as a part-time library worker. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote. Cody and Amy both gave a brief history of their work and life experience to the
council.

RESOLUTION 03-02-2021- WASTWATER PLANNING PROGRAM RESOLUTION ADOPTED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the annual wastewater planning self-
assessment report. The report helps the sewer department identify deficiencies and problem areas
within the city. The city is required to submit the annual report and to the Water Quality Board each
year for review and confirm that all appropriate actions have been taken to maintain effluent
requirements in accordance with the Utah Pollutant Discharge Elimination System permit. Councilor
Jones moved to adopt Resolution 03-02-2021. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 03-02-2021 TWO YEAR BUDGET ADOPTED:

  Councilor Kent Jones moved to adopt Ordinance 03-02-2021 that empowers the city to adopt a two-
year biennial budget. Mr. Atkinson explained that by adopting this ordinance, staff time and costs
spent on budget preparation will be reduced long-term planning and efficiencies will be promoted.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CHIEF OF POLICE OUT-OF-STATE TRAVEL APPROVED:

   Chief of Police Michael Morgan requested through a written staff report permission from the council
to attend the National FBINAA Conference to be held in Orlando, Florida July 6-11, 2021. The report
stated that the FBINAA would pay for the hotel, conference registration and per diem. The police
department budget would cover the airfare and rental car. Councilor Jones suggested that the cost be
taken out of a different budget and that the Chief report back what he learned from the conference to
other law enforcement officers. Councilor Jones then moved to approve the out-of-state travel for
Chief Morgan. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual, and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed
on a unanimous vote.


   The meeting adjourned at 8:30 p.m.
