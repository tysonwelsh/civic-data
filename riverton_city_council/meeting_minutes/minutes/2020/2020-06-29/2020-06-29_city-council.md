   3.a     Resolution No. 20-46 – A Resolution Approving and Adopting a Litigation
           Settlement Agreement between Riverton City and the Defendant in a Civil
           Action Filed by Riverton City and Herriman City Against the Unified Police
           Department in the Third Judicial District Court, State of Utah, Case No.
           190904012

Ryan Carter, City Attorney, explained the terms of the settlement agreement with UPD,
including a $150,000 cash settlement, a Faro Laser, and one Harley Davidson motorcycle. He
said that Riverton City maintained that there was no modified debt owed as a result of the years
UPD Officers served in Riverton. He also explained that the main ongoing discussion with UPD
had been regarding URS and future retirement funds.

Council Member Stewart said that UFA went through a similar exercise as they were preparing
a new interlocal agreement.

Mr. Carter stated that Riverton City had thoroughly considered the settlement agreement in light
of the continued expense of ongoing litigation against an entity with a questionable future.

Following brief discussion, Council Member Stewart MOVED the Riverton City Council
approve Resolution No. 20-46 approving and adopting a litigation settlement agreement
between Riverton City and the defendant in a civil action filed by Riverton City and
Herriman City against the Unified Police Department in the Third Judicial District Court,
State of Utah, Case No. 190904012. Council Member McDougal SECONDED the motion.

Ryan Carter proposed a friendly amendment that stated that Robert Hale was the new Board
Chairman of UPD, which was accepted by Council Members Stewart and McDougal. Mayor
Staggs called for discussion on the motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and
the vote was as follows: Council Members Buroker-Absent, McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes,
Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Absent. The motion passed.

4. Adjournment

call vote and the vote was as follows: Council Members Buroker-Absent, McCay-Yes,
McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Absent. The motion passed and the meeting adjourned
at 8:12 a.m.
