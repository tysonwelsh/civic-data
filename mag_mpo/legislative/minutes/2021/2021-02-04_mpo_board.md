Election of Officers for the Regional Planning Committee (RPC) – Shawn Seager, MAG Each year in February a Chair and Vice Chair are elected. Article II, Section 4 (1), in the MPO Bylaws states: The Mountainland Regional Planning Committee, in consultation with MPO staff, shall nominate from its membership for the positions of Chair and Vice Chair. Both Chair and Vice Chair shall be elected officials from jurisdictions within the MPO. Ratification of nomination shall require a simple majority vote. The position of Chair and Vice Chair shall be for one-year terms. Successive terms are not limited. Individuals may request to be replaced at any time. The position requires meeting time in Salt Lake City and Utah County. Both Mayor Leifson and Mayor Miller expressed interest in continuing to serve on the MPO Regional Planning Committee. No other nominations were given. Mayor Pray moved to nominate Mayor Leifson for Chair, and Mayor Miller for Vice Chair of the Mountainland MPO Regional Planning Committee. Mayor Johnson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Shawn Seager reported that there are slots open to serve on WFRC committees. Those who are interested or would like to nominate someone should contact him directly. TranPlan50 Amendment 1-2021 – Shawn Eliot, MAG Every four years, MAG is required to prepare and adopt a Regional Transportation Plan. The current plan was approved in June 2019. An amendment process allows for changes to be made. Amendment 1-21 proposes two changes: widening Pioneer Crossing and reconfiguring the I- 15/Santaquin Main ST interchange. MPO Regional Planning Committee February 4, 2021 Page 3 Proposed changes: #20 | Pioneer Crossing; Widen to 6-lanes Over 40,000 trips a day travel this corridor (Bangerter HWY levels of traffic). The current 4-lane cross section needs to be widened to a 6-lanes to handle current volumes. Phase one of the RTP has widening this corridor to 6 lanes between Redwood RD and Lehi 2300 W. This amendment would extend these limits to the east ending at Lehi 300 E. (east of 300 E to I-15 currently has 6 lanes). The amendment includes: • Change limits of project to Redwood RD to Lehi 300 E. • Change cost from $5.9m to $13.6m. • RTP has enough funding to cover the new cost. • Modeling shows air quality conformity is met. #81 | I-15/Santaquin Main ST Interchange Traffic on Santaquin Main ST, under I-15, is nearing failure. With many new businesses and housing proposed around the interchange, additional capacity is needed. The RTP proposes to reconstruct the interchange in phase two. UDOT has concluded that a retrofit of the current interchange is all that is needed in the next 30 years. It is proposed to remove the phase two reconstruction project and place a add a reconfiguration project in phase one. UDOT will shore up the current sloped embankments under I-15 to create two more travel lanes under the freeway. Main ST will also be widened around the interchange. The amendment includes: • Move project from phase 2 to phase 1 in the RTP. • Change the description from reconstruction to reconfiguration. • Change the cost from $40m to $25m. • RTP has enough funding to cover the new cost. Modeling shows air quality conformity is met. The proposed amendments allow congested corridors to be addressed by UDOT in the near term. Staff recommends approval of the proposed amendment 1-21 to TransPlan50 and the associated Air Quality Conformity Determination recommended at the January 25, 2021 MPO TAC meeting. Mayor Larson moved that MPO Regional Planning Committee approve the TransPlan50 amendment #1-21 and the associated Air Quality Conformity Determination to allow for UDOT address congestion along Pioneer Crossing and the I-15/Santaquin Main ST Interchange, barring no significant public comment is received. Mayor Staheli seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO Regional Planning Committee February 4, 2021 Page 4 Utah Transit Authority 5 Year Service Plan – Laura Hanson and Trustee Jeff Acerson Trustee Acerson and Laura Hanson gave a brief introduction to UTA’s Five-Year Service Plan. It is a vision for the future to guide potential service planning changes. The following potential changes are part of the Five-Year Service Plan vision for Utah County: 1. Open Vineyard FrontRunner Station. 2. Adjust local bus service to serve Vineyard FrontRunner Station. 3. Improve all-day service and overall hours of service on many routes. 4. Consider innovative mobility solutions in west Provo and Thanksgiving Point to add better coverage and replace routes with few riders. UTA staff is working to strengthen their relationships with local governments. They will be visiting city planning directors and staff for input and feedback as they refine service changes.
