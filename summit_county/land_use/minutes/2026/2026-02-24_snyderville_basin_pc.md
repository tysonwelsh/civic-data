3)
Discussion and Action for a proposed Rezone and Master Planned Development for the Junction Commons, a 19-building mixed use redevelopment project at Junction Commons (formerly Outlets Park City), located at 6699 N Landmark Dr, Kimball Junction, Summit County, UT; Parcel FSE-1; Applicant: Elliott Workgroup representing SRE Ontario LLC; Legislative Process. Project #24-087.
Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner. (6:28 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Junction Commons Master Planned Development Staff Report - February 24, 2026 Possible Action.pdf
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner
, reintroduced the Rezone and Master Planned Development for the Junction Commons for discussion and action. (6:28 P.M.)
Adam Greenbaum,
SRE Ontario LLC
, briefly addressed the Commission. Mr. Greenbaum thanked the Commission for the opportunity to present to them again and respectfully asked for the Commission's support in advancing the project to the County Council. (6:31 P.M.)
A robust discussion followed wherein the Commissioners asked detailed questions regarding items such as amenities, recreational parks, outdoor/open space, playground equipment, affordable housing, traffic mitigation, building phases, building heights, walkability, and numerous community benefits. Planner Caus, Mr. Greenbaum, Craig Elliott,
Elliott Workgroup
, and Justin Keys,
Hoggen Lee Hutchinson,
responded to their comments and concerns. (6:34 P.M.)
Commissioners provided conditions and hurdles to moving the project forward. (7:50 P.M.)
Eric Sagerman made a motion to approve the proposed rezone and master planned development according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in the staff report with the following added conditions: there be traffic mitigation plan for each phase and overall build out to include UOP and DPRE considerations as allowable, three townhomes provided as attainable but Council will determine final integration of affordable housing, and the exterior of the affordable unit buildings will look the same as the exterior of the market rate. Spencer Young seconded, and the motion carried, (6-1).
Heather Peteroy voted AYE
Makena Hawley voted AYE
Eric Sagerman voted AYE
Spencer Young voted AYE
Matthew Nagie voted AYE
Tyann Mooney voted AYE
Tim Jeffrey voted NAY
