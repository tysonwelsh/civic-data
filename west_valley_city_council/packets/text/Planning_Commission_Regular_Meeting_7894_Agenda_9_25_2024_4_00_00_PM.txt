 ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
 ZT-10-2024  Petition by West Valley City requesting an amendment to the zoning ordinance by modifying
             Section 7-6-301, adding Sections 7-10-400P through 7-10-402, and removing Sections 7-14-
             100P through 7-14-104. These changes would remove the Decker Lake Station Overlay Zone
             and add development standards for properties along Decker Lake Drive between 3100 South
             and 3500 South. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

 ZT-11-2024          Petition by West Valley City requesting an amendment to the zoning ordinance by modifying
                     Section 7-6-301 to allow tattoo establishments as a permitted use in the BRP, MXD, CC, C-1,
                     C-2, C-3, and LI zones and to remove the prohibition on tattoo establishments in all overlay
                     zones. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)
