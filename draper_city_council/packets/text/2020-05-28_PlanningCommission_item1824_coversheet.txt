Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: May 28, 2020
Re: East Point CSD – Land Use Map, Zoning Text and Zoning Map Amendment Requests Comments: This application is a request for approval of a land use map amendment, zoning text amendment and zoning map amendment for approximately 17.03 acres.  The applicant is requesting to rezone the property to a newly created commercial special district called the East Point Commercial Special District (CSD-EP).  The proposed CSD-EP zone will be located in Chapter 9-18 (Commercial Special Districts) in the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC).
The subject property is vacant and has never been developed.  The proposed zoning designation would allow the site to be developed with commercial and retail development.  The findings are as follows: Positive recommendation:
That DCMC Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the text of the DCMC.
That this map amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
That this text amendment does not compromise any of the six factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
That the proposed text change is consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the City’s General Plan.
That the proposed land use map amendment complies with DCMC Section 9-2-020(F).
Negative recommendation:
That this map amendment compromises one or more of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
That this text amendment compromises one or more of the six factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
That the proposed text change is not consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the City’s General Plan.
