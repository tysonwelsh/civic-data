5.    Text Amendment - Amend the 2009 City Code Planned Development Zones Sections 13-
      5C-5.C.2.a. and b. Garages in the P-C zones; City-wide applicability; City of West Jordan
      (applicant) [#21528]

Larry Gardner said the City Council suggested a change in the P-C code due to the large master
planned developments that have many housing types. Current code requires the garage to be behind the
front of the home five feet or even with the porch. This amendment will allow for the developer to
submit house plans in the master development plan and get approval from the city rather than the need
to make sure that each house plan is set back five feet. This will make for a more diverse design in the
large developments.

Kent Shelton asked if parking in front of the house will be impacted by the rule.

Larry Gardner said it won’t change the parking requirement, and if someone is going to park in front of
the house then the distance will remain 22 feet.

Ammon Allen began a discussion regarding if we need to keep the regulation for a five-foot recess for
garages if this amendment will allow an exception. It was pointed out that keeping it will allow
flexibility in the master development plan for the builder to provide a very good product that has a
garage slightly forward but remaining nice architecturally. The master development plan requires the
developer to show what they are going to build. It also makes it easier for staff to review. The
advantage of the proposed wording is that there is a default requirement in case the submitted plan
doesn’t show a standard.

Trish Hatch asked if all communities in West Jordan require garages.

Larry Gardner said all new construction requires a two-car garage.

Trish Hatch said the City needs to discuss options for affordable housing. Garages increase the cost of
a home and could be prohibitive to home ownership. Parking needs to be provided, and covered or
garage parking is preferred, but if it prohibits someone from owning a home then it is a factor to be
aware of.

Larry Gardner said although the two-car garage requirement is in the current code, options are being
discussed. The larger planned communities require 5% moderate income housing to be dispersed
throughout the development. One way of making it affordable is to leave off the garage. If any changes
are made to the code in the future, then this portion will be amended at that time.

Matt Quinney opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

MOTION:        Kent Shelton moved to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to
               Amend the 2009 City Code Planned Development Zones Sections 13-5C-5.C.2.a.
               and b. Garages in the P-C zones; City of West Jordan (applicant). The motion was
               seconded by Corbin England and passed 6-0 in favor.
