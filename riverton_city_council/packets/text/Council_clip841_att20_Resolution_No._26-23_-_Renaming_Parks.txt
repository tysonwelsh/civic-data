                                        RIVERTON, UTAH
                                      RESOLUTION NO. 26-23

             APPROVING THE RENAMING OF FOUR RIVERTON CITY PARKS

      WHEREAS, Riverton City (“City”) owns and maintains various public parks for the use and
enjoyment of its residents; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that updating park names can better reflect community
identity, improve wayfinding, and enhance the character of City amenities; and

       WHEREAS, the City desires to rename four parks as identified below; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds and determines that renaming these parks will promote the
health, safety, and welfare of Riverton residents by providing clear and meaningful park identification;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

       1. The following park names are hereby approved to be changed as follows:
             A. Western Springs Central Park shall be renamed Western Hills Park.
             B. Western Springs Tot Lot shall be renamed Diamondback Park.
             C. Canyonview Mini Park shall be renamed Harvest Springs Park.
             D. Western Springs Mini Park shall be renamed Geronimo Park.

       2. The City staff is authorized and directed to update all official records, signage, maps, and
          related materials to reflect these name changes.

       3. This Resolution, assigned No. 26-23, shall take effect immediately upon passage and
          acceptance as provided herein.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 5th day of
May 2026 by the following vote:
