Opening Statement
Council Chair Bynum began the Special Meeting by stating that as part of the allegations of the Rules of
Procedure and Code of Conduct violations that were sent to Council Member Sanchez, the following
statement was included from the Council Chair: “Should the Council vote to go into Closed Session, in
compliance with South Salt Lake City Council Rules of Procedure/Code of Conduct Rule 12(2)(e)(v) at my
discretion, you will be excluded from that Closed Door Meeting.”

She then opened the floor for a motion for a Closed Door Meeting.

Motion for Closed Meeting
  Closed meeting held pursuant to Utah Code Annotated, 1953, as amended, Sec. 5-4-204, Sec. 52-4-
  205 (1) (a), et. seq. for the following purposes: Discussion of the character, professional competence,
  or physical or mental health of an individual.

Items for Discussion and Vote:

    1. Discussion and possible action relating to alleged violations of South Salt Lake City Council
       Rules of Procedure.
       After the Council Members returned from the Closed Door Meeting, Council Chair Bynum
       announced that the Council Members had determined that the alleged violations were
       sustained.

        As a result, Council Member Sanchez’s actions have created fear and have distracted the Council
        from City business.

        The following sanctions will be issued:

             a. Council Member Sanchez is officially censured as of this meeting.

             b. Council Member Sanchez will be suspended from writing the ‘Council Corner’ message
                in the City’s newsletter.

             c. Council Member Sanchez will be suspended from placing items on the Council agenda.

             d. Council Member Sanchez will be suspended from conducting formal meetings.

             e. Council Member Sanchez will be suspended from traveling as related to the City or
                Council.

             f.   Council Member Sanchez will not be allowed to serve in leadership or on committees.

             g. Council Member Sanchez will be required to participate remotely via Zoom for all
                Council Meetings.

             h. The Council Members are requesting Council Member Sanchez’s resignation.
