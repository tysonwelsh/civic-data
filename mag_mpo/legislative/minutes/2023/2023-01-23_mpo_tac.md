Approve October 24, 2022 Meeting Minutes (3:56) Richard Nielson moved to approve the October 24, 2022 TAC meeting minutes. Andy Spencer seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO TAC Officers Chair and Vice Chair Rotation Shawn Eliot, MAG The Mountainland MPO Bylaws govern MPO TAC officers. A chair and vice chair are selected every two years. The current 2-year cycle has concluded. The current vice chair is Jill Spencer, Payson. She is willing to assume the role of chair for the next 2-year period. This is an automatic rotation as stated in the Bylaws. A new vice-chair needs to be nominated and accepted to serve a 2-year term with the understanding that in 2-years they will serve as the chair. Andy Spencer nominated Jared Johnson, Spanish Fork to serve as MPO TAC Vice Chair Andy Spencer moved that Jared Johnson be approved to serve as the MPO TAC vice chair for a period of 2-years. Gordon Haight seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Jill Spencer and the committee thanked Brad Kenison for his service on the committee. RTP Project Lists Needs and Fiscal Constraint Shawn Eliot, MAG (9:15) Shawn Eliot presented the MAG Transplan50 Story Map stating that MAG is to the point in the process that the list needs to be adopted. The transportation portion of the map was discussed in detail. The committee discussed various projects and expressed their concerns. The committee requested additional information before making a decision. MPO Technical Advisory Committee January 23, 2023 Page 3 Shawn Eliot explained that delaying the approval doesn’t leave enough time for public comment and approval by the MPO Board in June. If the plan is not approved in June, MAG will be in nonconformity and the projects will be stopped. Jill Spencer recommended that MPO TAC move forward to approve the RTP Projects List Needs and Fiscal Constraints as proposed. Mary DeLaMare-Schaefer recommended that MPO TAC wait to discuss the transit portion of the fiscally constrained list next week due to time and transit complexities. (1:39:18) Brad Kenison moved to approve the RTP project list and fiscal constraints submitted by MAG staff provided that project #117 be moved from a vision project to an unfunded phase 3. No second was provided. Eric Rasband reiterated that the motion doesn't follow the standard MAG process. Andy Spencer recommended a substitute motion and moved to recommend that MAG move forward with the plan as proposed and give Lehi City the ability to work with MAG staff and other agencies, such as UDOT, to get additional information on how this project could be prioritized in relation to other projects that are currently in phase one or phase two of the RTP, and bring back to the group to possibly amend it. This would allow the RTP to move forward and maintain the timelines. Jared Johnson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. TIP Modifications Bob Allen, MAG (1:51:49) Pleasant Grove 2600 North Additional Funding Pleasant Grove was awarded two projects to widen 2600 North to three lanes, including shoulders and a trail. The two projects were combined for design purposes and are ready to go out to bid. Project Selected Funds Type Scope Phase I 2018 $2,693,450 Exchange Widening, shoulders and trail Phase II 2020 $1,732,300 Exchange Widening, shoulders and trail Estimates show that an additional $3,113,174 is needed to complete the project due to unforeseen slope mitigation and construction and ROW inflation. Pleasant Grove requests the following funding modification. Original Funds $4,425,750 10% Contingency $442,575 Additional Funds $2,670,599 New project Total $7,538,924 MPO Technical Advisory Committee January 23, 2023 Page 4 MAG Staff recommends that the additional funds be awarded to the project. Jeremy Lapin moved to recommend that $2,670,599 be added to the combined Pleasant Grove 2600 North widening project. Gordon Haight seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Spanish Fork Center Street Scope Modification Spanish Fork was awarded $6.5mto widen Center St to 5 lanes from US6 to 900 East. Several properties were acquired to build the project and the remnants are ready to be sold with an estimated value of $131,000. Spanish Fork would like to use the proceeds of the sale to construct a trail to the school in this corridor. The trail was originally to be built by a different project that was removed. The estimated cost would be $116,160. MAG Staff recommends that the trail be added to the scope of the project and be built with the proceeds from the sale of unused properties. Jeremy Lapin moved to recommend that a trail be added to the scope of the Spanish Fork Center Street project. Aaron Wilson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. UDOT 800 North Trail; 400 West to 900 West The MPO Bylaws were changed last year and now states that if UDOT is going to make a TIP modification using solely their funds, the TIP Manager can approve that addition into the TIP and will bring it back to TAC as an information item only. $375,000 UDOT funds were added to the project. SAP/TAG Program Update Dan Wayne, MAG (1:59:20) Dan Wayne reported that MAG is in the contracting process with the consultants for 6 Station Area Plan (SAP) projects. The Technical Assistance to Governments (TAG) program received 12 applications that are currently being evaluated. Announcement of awards will be coordinated WFRC and UDOT in March. Context Sensitivity in Utah’s Local and Regional Planning Shawn Eliot, MAG (2:00:29) Mountainland MPO is partnering with the Governor’s Office of Planning and Budget, WFRC, UDOT and UTA to study what makes great streets great. The plan will look at the context of land use, transportation modes, and community and regional needs within Wasatch Choice Center areas and balance predictability of regional transportation facilities design, work through tradeoffs, shape better projects, and support better communities. MPO Technical Advisory Committee January 23, 2023 Page 5 The Wasatch Choice Great Streets study is in the beginning stages. MPO staff would like to offer local staff the opportunity to review the study framework and typology as the study moves forward. Information will be sent encouraging local participation.
