Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
Council Meeting scheduled July 6, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear and
consider public comments regarding application GPZ-8-2021, filed by Moore
Homes, LC, requesting a General Plan Change from Community Use to Medium
Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre) and a Zone Change from A (Agriculture,
Minimum Lot Size ½ acre) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) for property
located at 7147 West Parkway Blvd.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
information as follows:

       The applicant is proposing a 43-unit, single level, senior condo
       development. The proposed density of the project is 6.4 units/acre. Each
       unit would have a two-car garage and a driveway large enough to park
       four vehicles in since the driveways are over 40’ deep. The proposed
       minimum unit size is 1,520 sq. ft. The attached concept plan and
       application materials from the applicant provide details on and support for
       the proposed project. For comparison, the table below describes four
       existing senior condo developments and one recently approved project
       within the City:

        Project Name          Address           Built   Acreage     Units   Density    Zoning

        Reunion                                2009-

        Bingham                                2015-
        Point            3151 S 7200 W         2019         12.9      81        6.3 R-1-8

       While the property is eligible for RM zoning, the City may still deny
       requests for RM zoning. An argument could be made for the RE

B.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION ZT-2-2021,
     FILED BY WEST VALLEY CITY, REQUESTING A ZONE TEXT
     CHANGE TO ADD REGULATIONS FOR INTERNAL ACCESSORY
     DWELLING UNITS
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled July 6, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear and
     consider public comments regarding application ZT-2-2021, filed by West Valley
     City, requesting a Zone Text Change to add regulations for internal accessory dwelling
     units.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            City staff is proposing an ordinance amendment to add regulations for
            internal accessory dwelling units. HB 82 was passed by the Utah Legislature
            in the 2021 General Session of the Utah Legislature and later signed by the
            governor. A copy of this bill is included with this issue paper. Among other
            changes, the bill “requires municipalities and counties to classify certain
            accessory dwelling units as a permitted land use” and “prohibits
            municipalities and counties from establishing restrictions or requirements
MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING JULY 6, 2021
                     -9-

       for certain accessory dwelling units with limited exceptions”. In summary,
       cities must now allow internal accessory dwelling units within single family
       dwellings and the State now limits the extent of local regulation of internal
       accessory dwelling units.

       The proposed ordinance incorporates all the internal accessory dwelling
       unit regulations allowed under HB 82. If this proposed ordinance or one like
       it is not adopted, the City would be required to allow internal accessory
       dwellings units without regulations on things like lot size or parking.

Mayor Bigelow opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
or opposition, Mayor Bigelow closed the Public Hearing.

C.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION ZT-4-2021,
     FILED BY WEST VALLEY CITY, REQUESTING A ZONE TEXT
     CHANGE TO AMEND STANDARDS FOR SINGLE UNIT DWELLINGS
     AND TWO UNIT DWELLINGS
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled July 6, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear and
     consider public comments regarding application ZT-4-2021, filed by West Valley
     City, requesting a Zone Text Change to amend standards for single unit dwellings
     and two unit dwellings

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

        HB 1003 was passed by the Utah Legislature in the 2021 1st Special Session of
        the Utah Legislature and later signed by the governor. A copy of this bill is
        included with this issue paper. Among other changes, the bill prohibits cities
        from imposing building design elements on a one to two family dwelling.
        Exceptions to this prohibition include:

               Building design elements agreed to under a development agreement
               A land use regulation, including a planned unit development or
                overlay zone, that a property owner requests:
                    o the municipality to apply to the owner’s property and
                    o in exchange for an increase in density or other benefit not
                       otherwise available as a permitted use in the zoning area or
                       district.

        The vast majority of remaining vacant ground planned for single unit dwellings
        either already has a development agreement or would need to be rezoned and
        would therefore become subject to a development agreement. The City can still
        require building design elements in these instances through a development
        agreement.

        The following is a list of the building design elements from HB 1003 that are
        prohibited: exterior color; type or style of exterior cladding material; style,
        dimensions, or materials of a roof structure, roof pitch, or porch; exterior
        nonstructural architectural ornamentation; location, design, placement, or
        architectural styling of a window or door; location, design, placement or
        architectural styling of a garage door, not including a rear-loading garage door;
        number or type of rooms; interior layout of a room; minimum square footage
        over 1,000 square feet, not including a garage; rear yard landscaping
    MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING JULY 6, 2021
                         -11-

       requirements; minimum building dimensions; and a requirement to install front
       yard fencing.

       The proposed ordinance change removes from the City’s Zoning Ordinance any
       reference to building design elements as defined by State law.

    Mayor Bigelow opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
    or opposition, Mayor Bigelow closed the Public Hearing.
