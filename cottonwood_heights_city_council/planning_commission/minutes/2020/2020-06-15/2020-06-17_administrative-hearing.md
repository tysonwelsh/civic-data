                              COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY
                              DEPARTMENT OF COMMUNITY AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

                              ADMINISTRATIVE HEARING MINUTES
                              June 17, 2020

 Cottonwood Heights Community and Economic Development Director, Michael Johnson held an
Administrative Hearing Meeting on Wednesday, June 17, 2020 at 12:00 p.m. In view of the current
COVID-19 pandemic, this meeting was held electronically, without a physical location, as authorized by
the Governor’s Executive Order 2020-05 dated 18 March 2020. The public was able to remotely hear
the open portions of the meeting through live broadcast by connecting to http://mixlr.com/chmeetings

Staff Present: Deputy City Recorder Heather Sundquist, Community and Economic Development
Director Michael Johnson, City Planner Andrew Hulka, Assistant City Planner Samantha DeSeelhorst

Others Present: Applicant Paul Harmon

Community and Economic Development Director Michael Johnson called the meeting to order and
welcomed those attending the meeting via Zoom.

1.0 Business Items


       1.1 (Project CUP-20-011)
                      Request from Paul Harmon for a wall height extension at 3219 E Danish Way


 Assistant Planner Samantha DeSeelhorst shared a PowerPoint presentation and gave an overview of the
project. Samantha stated that the height extension is being requested in increase privacy between the
two properties and to mitigate visual and sensory nuisances. There is also a grade difference between
the two properties which makes a 6 foot fence insufficient. Samantha explained that City Code requires
neighbor consent for a wall height extension exceeding 8 feet and stated that neighbor consent was
received and submitted to staff.
Staff received two neighbor comments in support of Mr. Harmon’s application. Those comments
agreed with the need for added privacy, and with the unique nuisances originating from the adjacent
property. The two comments received were from Gene Bosley & Nicholas Enterline, they were read into
record by Samantha DeSeelhorst and are available for view in the Administrative Hearing Packet posted
on the City website. Staff received no comments in opposition of this application.

Staff Recommendation on this request is to Approve with the following conditions;
       A building permit and all necessary inspections shall be obtained and completed.

CED Director Michael Johnson ask the applicant Paul Harmon if there was anything else he wanted to
add or address. Mr. Harmon reiterated the need for privacy and stated that the property has been very
problematic.
Michael Johnson moved to approve the request for wall height extension pursuant to the requirement
specified in the staff report and directed Mr. Harmon to follow up with Samantha DeSeelhorst to
complete the building permit process.
