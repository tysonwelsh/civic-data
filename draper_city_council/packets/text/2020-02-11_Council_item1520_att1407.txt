                                     RESOLUTION NO. 20-08

        RESOLUTION OF THE LEGISLATIVE BODY OF DRAPER CITY REMOVING
        ALAN SUMMERHAYS AS A SERVING DELEGATE TO THE TRANS JORDAN
        CITIES BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND APPOINTING CAL ROBERTS TO
        SERVE AS A DELEGATE TO THE TRANS JORDAN CITIES BOARD OF
        DIRECTORS

        WHEREAS, Draper City is a member community of the Trans Jordan Cities Landfill, an
entity created pursuant to interlocal agreement; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City is required to designate a principal and alternate delegates to the
Board of Directors of Trans Jordan Cities; and

        WHEREAS, Alan Summerhays is no longer a member of the Draper City Council and is
to be removed as one of the alternate delegates to the Trans Jordan Cities Board of Directors.

       WHEREAS, Cal Roberts is to be appointed as one of the alternate delegates to serve
alongside the already designated principal and one other alternate delegates.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Removal. Alan Summerhays is hereby removed as an alternate delegate to
represent Draper City at any and all meetings of the Trans Jordan Cities Landfill Board.

       Section 2. Appointment. Cal Roberts is hereby appointed as an alternate delegate to
represent Draper City at any and all meetings of the Trans Jordan Cities Landfill Board.

        Section 3. Severability. If any section, part or provision of the Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution,
and all sections, parts and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

       Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
passage.


     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, ON THIS THE 11TH DAY OF FEBUARY, 2020.
