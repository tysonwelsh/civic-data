Hearing – Report of Costs Expended to Demolish a Dangerous Building at 5176 South 4580 West

                Clayton Preece, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township Council, stated the
Council will be sitting as a quasi-jurisdictional body for this hearing. After the hearing the decision
will need to be made to adopt the order or make changes to the order and then adopt it. The
order will then need to be read into the record.

On August 2, 2018, the Salt Lake County Health Department closed the property located at 5176
South 4580 West due to chemical contamination. On March 28, 2019, a fire occurred within the
house, the Kearns Public Works Director determined that the structure was so damaged as to
cause sufficient danger to the public. Notice was provided to the estate that the dangerous
conditions needed to be abated. This was not done. An appeal was filed by the spouse of the
owner of record and after numerous hearings, in which the appellant failed to appear the appeal
was dismissed. Kearns was then given permission to take whatever action needed to abate the
dangerous conditions. Kearns engaged various companies to clean up the property and demolish
the house at a total cost of $33,070. The Kearns Council can assess this amount to the owner of
record or against the property. Legal staff is recommending that the assessment be placed on
the property itself. The property is under foreclosure proceedings and within two weeks it should
be sold.

               Mayor Bush stated she wanted it on record that the Council received numerous
bids for work done on this property.

               Mayor Bush opened the public hearing.

               No one appeared for the public hearing.

               Mayor Bush closed the public hearing.

               All Council Members voiced support of the recommendation from staff to assess
this property with the full charges.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to approve
the following order as read into the minutes:

            ORDER OF THE METRO TOWNSHIP OF KEARNS CHARGING COSTS

               Pursuant to Section 902 of the Uniform Code for the Abatement of Dangerous
Buildings, which is adopted by reference pursuant to Section 15.16.010 of the Kearns Metro

Township (“Kearns”) Code, a hearing was held before the Kearns Council at 5658 South Cougar
Lane, Kearns, Utah on the 12th day of November, 2019, at the hour of 6:30 p.m. regarding a report
on the costs expended to demolish and abate a dangerous building at 5176 South 4580 West,
Kearns, UT 84118 (the “Property”). After reviewing the Report of Costs and conducting a hearing,
the Kearns Council makes the following findings of fact:

   1. On or about August 2, 2018, the Salt Lake County Health Department closed the dwelling
      on the Property due to chemical contamination.

   2. On or about March 28, 2019, a fire occurred at the Property inside the vacated dwelling,
      resulting in substantial damage to the dwelling, including structural damage.

   3. The Kearns Public Works Director determined that the dwelling was so damaged by fire
      as to pose a threat to public health and safety.

   4. After providing notice and ordering that the dangerous conditions on the Property be
      abated pursuant to the Uniform Code for the Abatement of Dangerous Buildings, neither
      the Property owner, nor anyone else, abated the dangerous conditions on the Property.

   5. Carlida Montoya appealed the Notice and Order to Abate to Kearns’ Administrative Law
      Judge.

   6. After holding multiple hearings on Ms. Montoya’s appeal, the Administrative Law Judge
      dismissed the appeal on August 9, 2019.

   7. The Administrative Law Judge ordered that Kearns could take whatever actions necessary
      to eliminate, eradicate, and remove the dangerous conditions that existed on the Property.

   8. Kearns engaged Evans & Sons Automotive & Towing, Envirospec, LLC, and Thermal
      West Industrial, Inc. to abate the dangerous conditions on the property, including but
      limited to demolishing the swelling, removing asbestos in the dwelling, and removing a
      vehicle from the Property.

   9. On October 29, 2019, Salt Lake County Public Works, which is under contract to provide
      public works services to Kearns, issued a Report for the Recovery of Costs detailing the
      actions taken by Evans & Sons Automotive & Towing, Envirospec, LLC, and Thermal West
      Industrial, Inc. to abate the dangerous conditions on the Property.

   10. The cost to abate the dangerous conditions on the Property were as follows:

                      Junk Car Removal:                       $290.00
                      Asbestos Inspection:                    $645.00
                      Demolition:                          $32,135.00

                      Total                                $33,070.00

                           ORDER AND ASSESSMENT OF COSTS

              Based on the foregoing findings of fact, it is hereby ordered that the cost to abate
the dangerous conditions on the Property in the amount of $33,070.00 be assessed against the
Property and placed on the Salt Lake County Tax Assessment Roll in the form of a special
assessment and lien against the Property. This order constitutes Kearns’ final and conclusive
order and judgement.

                                               APPEAL

                 In accordance with Kearns Municipal Code Section 1.16.130 and the Section 906
of the Uniform Code for the Abatement of Dangerous Buildings, this Order may be appealed by
filing a petition for review with the Third District Court of the State of Utah. A petition for review
shall be barred unless it is filed within thirty days of the date of this Order. The filing of a petition
with the Third District Court does not stay execution of this order.

RESOLUTION NO. 19-11-01                                             Date: November 12, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL DECLARING
       KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP’S INTENT TO PARTICIPATE IN THE UTAH
       COMMUNITY RENEWABLE ENERGY ACT

                                         RECITALS

       WHEREAS, the 2019 Utah legislature passed, and Governor Gary Herbert signed, H.B.
411, Community Renewable Energy Act, Utah Code Ann. §54-17-901, et seq., (the “Act”);

       WHEREAS, the Act authorizes the Utah Public Service Commission to establish a
community renewable energy program whereby metro townships, acting on behalf of their
residents, may cooperate with qualified utilities to provide electric energy for participating
customers entirely from renewable energy resources;

       WHEREAS, the Act requires participating metro townships to adopt a resolution no later
than December 31, 2019, that states a goal of achieving an amount equivalent to 100% of the
annual electric energy supply for participating customers from a renewable energy resource by
2030; and

        WHEREAS, it is in the interest of the Kearns Metro Township and its residents to
participate in the Act, and to state and affirm such goal.

        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Kearns Metro Township Council
that effective immediately:

   1. It is the Kearns Metro Township’s goal to achieve an amount equivalent to 100% of the
      annual electric energy supply for participating customers from a renewable energy
      resource by 2030 pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §54-17-903(2)(a).

   2. Pending program approval and development by the Utah Public Service Commission, the
      Kearns Metro Township shall begin the process of fulfilling the Act’s requirements as
      described in Utah Code Ann. §54-17-903, including entering into an agreement with a
      qualified utility and adopting an ordinance approving the program.
