Pursuant to Utah Code 52-4-202 the undersigned, as Mayor of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township Council, hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the
electronic meetings of the Council will present a substantial risk to the health and safety of those
who may be present at the anchor location for the following reasons:
The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under an “Yellow” alert
stage for the COVID 19 pandemic; and
The regular meeting place for the Council (the Fire Station in the Canyon) does
not have sufficient space in the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing
to keep people safe from infection.

Emigration Canyon Community Council Report (ECCC)
Bill Tobey stated the cost for the Acorn Hills fire break was a little over $9,900.
The invoice has been mailed to the Metro Township. The Sustainability Alliance requested that
the ECCC fund a portion of the Watershed Celebration on September 18. Before the ECCC could
convene a meeting to discuss funding, the Emigration Canyon Improvement District (EID) Board
agreed to fund the Watershed Celebration in full. The ECCC is now going to help make the
celebration successful, they will work with UFA to provide information on wildfire preparedness.
The Council is going to try and hold the Firewise Open House this fall; however, with the rise in
COVID cases, it does not seem likely that it will be held before May 2022. The next issue of the
electronic newsletter is anticipated to be sent out on September 1.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)
Officer Jake Elsasser reviewed calls for service for July, there were 108 calls with
18 initial reports taken. The archery hunt started on Saturday, August 21. Several UPD units were
dispatched to Lambs Canyon to assist with the road closures caused by the fire up Parley’s
Canyon. There are continuing parking issues at Killyons Canyon, the cars are being issued
citations. Once the paving is done in that area, the gate will be locked to help prevent parking
issues.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Captain Michael Conn stated the fire in Parleys Canyon is official contained. It
was determined that it was human causes as is 55 percent of the fires this year. UFA decided
that they would open stations and being tours; however, with the current surge in COVID cases
the tours have been discontinued. Community events will be allowed as long as there are face
masks and social distancing is maintained. Fire crews have been deployed to Oregon,
Washington, Montana, and California among others to help with fires in those states. UFA
continues to get EMAC requests, especially from California. Each EMAC request is evaluated
individually and balanced so that services are not inhibited. Members of the Utah Task Force
teams have been deployed throughout the United States to help with the tropical storms. The 4th
and 24th of July saw an increase in calls and community complaints for fireworks. A couple
members retired in the last month and there have been promotions. UFA is trying to get Fire
School 101 started again and made available to the Council Members.

Resolution No. 2021-08-01 – 2021 General Election Cancellation
The Council reviewed the following Resolution cancelling the Emigration Canyon
Metro Township’s November 2, 2021 election:
RESOLUTION NO. 2021-08-01

Date: August 24, 2021

A RESOLUTION, PURSUANT TO UTAH CODE 20A-1-206, CANCELLING
EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP’S NOVEMBER 2, 2021 LOCAL
ELECTION AND DETERMINING THE UNOPPOSED DECLARED CANDIDATES
TO BE ELECTED TO THE TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township (hereafter “ECMT”) is a metro
township under Utah Code Ann. § 10-2a-401 et seq., and
WHEREAS, ECMT is governed by a five-member elected Council, each of whom is
elected at-large under Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-205.5, and
and

WHEREAS, the terms of two members of the Council are set to expire at the end of 2021,

WHEREAS, because of the expiration of terms, an election was called for November 2,
2021, pursuant to applicable sections of the Utah Code, to fill those two positions on the Council,
and
WHEREAS, notice of the election was duly noticed to the public, and
WHEREAS, only two candidates filed declarations of candidacy to run for the two open
positions on the Council, said candidates being ROBERT PINON and JOSEPH SMOLKA, and
WHEREAS, as of August 24, 2021, no other individual gave notice of intent to run as a
write-in candidate, pursuant to Utah Code §20A-9-601, and
WHEREAS, the deadline for write-in candidates to declare is August 30, 2021, and
WHEREAS, due to ballot printing deadline requirements for mail in ballots the
determination of whether to print ballots is needed prior to September 17, 2021, and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 20A-1-206, municipal legislative body can
choose to cancel an election where the number of declared candidates, including declared write
in candidates, do not exceed the number of open council positions subject to election, and may
certify the unopposed declared candidates to be elected to the council and
WHEREAS, a significant amount of money will be saved by ECMT by not holding a formal
election as allowed by the Utah Code, and

WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Council wishes to inform the clerk of its
intention to cancel the election upon the expiration of the deadlines for others to declare for
candidacy, so ballots could be finalized, and committed to ratify the same at its next regularly
scheduled meeting.
NOW THEREFORE, IT WAS HEREBY RESOLVED, APPROVED AND CERTIFIED, by the
Council of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township that:
1.
The scheduled election for members of the Council, set for November 2, 2021 (the
“Election”) shall be cancelled if no write in candidates are submitted by August 30, 2021, and due
to the herein certified fact that in such case only two declared candidates are running, pursuant
to statutory requirements, for the two open positions on the Council that were to be chosen by the
Election.
2.
Effective January 3, 2022, the two unopposed candidates for the two open positions on
the Council, are hereby deemed to be elected to those positions, which unopposed candidates
are: ROBERT PINON and JOSEPH SMOLKA.
3.
Council’s clerk is instructed to take whatever action is required to give notice of the
cancellation of the election pursuant to Utah Code Section 20A-1-206 after August 30, 2021 if it
is determined that there are no write in candidates.

The Council reviewed the following Resolution to approve the decision by the Salt
Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA) Board of Trustees to increase the
property tax revenues:
RESOLUTION NO. 2021-08-02

Date: August 24, 2021

A RESOLUTION OF THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL APPROVING A DECISION, IF MADE BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES
OF THE SALT LAKE VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA
(SLVLESA) FOLLOWING PROCESSES REQUIRED BY UTAH LAW, TO

INCREASE SLVLESA BUDGETED PROPERTY TAX REVENUES BY 16.5%
FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2022 WHICH WOULD RESULT IN THE LEVY OF A
PROPERTY TAX RATE IN EXCESS OF THE CERTIFIED TAX RATE
PURSUANT TO SECTION 17B-2a-903 OF THE UTAH CODE.
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (“SLVLESA”) was
created by Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council dated August 18, 2009; and
WHEREAS, the area of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township is included in the
boundaries of SLVLESA for the funding and provision of law enforcement services to the areas
within the boundaries of SLVLESA; and
WHEREAS, SLVLESA contracts with the Unified Police Department (the “UPD”) to
provide law enforcement services within its boundaries; and
WHEREAS, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees, by a duly adopted Resolution dated August
19, 2021, stated its intent to increase its budgeted property tax revenues from prior year budgeted
property tax revenues by 16.5% for calendar year 2022 which would result in the levy of a property
tax rate in excess of the certified rate as defined in section 59-2-924 of the Utah Code; and
WHEREAS, a report dated August 19, 2021 (the “Report”), a copy of which is attached,
was submitted to the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council (the “Council”) pursuant to
section 17B-1-1003 of the Utah Code, providing the purposes for the increase in SLVLESA
budgeted property tax revenues; and
WHEREAS, the purposes for the increase in SLVLESA budgeted property tax revenues
by 16.5% for calendar year 2022 as stated in the Report include the need to maintain the viability
of SLVLESA, increase compensation to be able to recruit and retain police officers, increase
staffing to meet rising caseloads, pay for the increased costs of cars, equipment and supplies, to
ensure that the target reserves for SLVLESA are met, to hedge against future loss of revenues
and unexpected expenditures and generally to meet the costs of law enforcement services for
SLVLESA members; and
WHEREAS, the Council allowed time during the duly noticed meeting on August 24, 2021
for comment on the proposed tax increase from members of the Council and the public; and
WHEREAS, section 17B-2a-903 of the Utah Code requires that before the SLVLESA
Board of Trustees can adopt a property tax levy for calendar year 2022 in excess of the certified
tax rate, it must have prior approval of the property tax increase from the legislative bodies of any
municipality and county whose territory is located within SLVLESA or approval of a majority of the
legislative bodies of the municipalities and two-thirds (2/3) vote of the county legislative body; and
WHEREAS, before the SLVLESA Board of Trustees considers levying a property tax rate
that exceeds the certified tax rate, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees shall hold a public hearing on
December 16, 2021, to provide to all interested persons the opportunity to be heard regarding the
proposed property tax increase; and
WHEREAS, SLVLESA shall provide notices of the public hearing as required by section
59-2-919 of the Utah Code; and

WHEREAS, after considering all public input from the public hearing and all other
information available to them, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees will vote on whether or not to

the levy of a property tax rate that exceeds the certified tax rate.
NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council as
follows:
1. The Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council hereby reaffirms its authorization of the
SLVLESA Board of Trustees to impose property taxes on the areas within the boundaries
of SLVLESA.
2. Pursuant to section 17B-2a-903 of the Utah Code, the Emigration Canyon Metro

Trustees following the processes required by Utah law, to increase SLVLESA budgeted
property tax revenues for calendar year 2022 in excess of prior year property tax revenues

increase in its budgeted property tax revenues as defined in section 59-2-924 of the Utah
Code.
