     3. Public Hearing
         3.1. Public hearing and consideration of a Resolution amending the fiscal year 2025
              budget – Kyle Maurer, Director of Finance and Administrative Services
     Finance Director Kyle Maurer presented a resolution to amend the fiscal year 2025 budget
     for the Community Development and Renewal Agency (CDRA). The amendments primarily
     aligned the budget with the actual tax increment revenues received and the corresponding
     participation agreements. In the Town Center CDA, the tax increment came in lower than
     projected at approximately $3.7 million, leading to reduced payments to HTC Communities.
     A new participation agreement with Elevated Acquisitions was also added. Additionally,
     $57,300 in non-tax-related expenditures were included for utility removals and the relocation
     of the City’s message board on 13400 South. In the Business Center CRA, which includes the
     REAL Stadium area, tax revenues were about $55,000 lower than expected, resulting in
     decreased payments to participating entities such as Sorenson Legacy and others.
     Conversely, the Anthem CRA received about $50,000 more than anticipated, which required
     increased incentive payments to Arbor Commercial and higher rebates to Salt Lake County
     and the school district. Lastly, the Innovation CRA, where Bullfrog Manufacturing is located,
     saw $63,000 more in tax increment than forecasted, necessitating an increased mitigation
     payment to Jordan School District and Salt Lake County.

     Chair palmer opened the public hearing.

     No comments were offered.

     Director Ohrn moved to close the public hearing. Director Hodges seconded the motion and
     all voted aye.
