Mayor Bigelow stated that due to the recent COVID-19 outbreak, the City is practicing
recommendations and guidelines suggested by the Federal Government, CDC, and the
State of Utah. He indicated that every person present should stay at least 6 feet from one
another and the meeting is also being displayed and conducted electronically via Zoom. He
asked that members of the public participating be patient while the Council and staff
navigate this new technology.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF STUDY MEETING HELD MARCH 10, 2020
The Council considered the Minutes of the Study Meeting held March 10, 2020. There
were no changes, corrections or deletions.

Councilmember Fitisemanu moved to approve the Minutes of the Study Meeting held
March 10, 2020. Councilmember Christensen seconded the motion.

A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

RESOLUTION 20-33: APPROVE THE METRO FIRE AGENCY INTERLOCAL
AGREEMENT
John Evans, Fire Chief, presented proposed Resolution 20-23 that would approve the Metro
Fire Agency Interlocal Agreement.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The Metro Fire Agency was formed in order for valley departments to come
       together to share resources, training, purchasing and response to incidents. For
       years the USAR TaskForce 5 has been operational as a state resource and this new
       agreements puts this into the Interlocal Agreement.

The City Council will consider Resolution 20-33 at the Regular Council Meeting scheduled
April 7, 2020 at 6:30 P.M.

RESOLUTION 20-34: AUTHORIZE THE EXECUTION OF A COST SHARING
INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND MAGNA WATER
DISTRICT FOR THE 2540 SOUTH ROADWAY PROJECT
Russ Willardson, Public Works, presented proposed Resolution 20-34 that would authorize
the execution of a Cost Sharing Interlocal Agreement between the City and Magna Water
District for the 2540 South Roadway Project.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as

       The Public Works Department recently completed construction on the 2540 South

       The agreement reimburses West Valley City for the construction of various

NEW BUSINESS
A.   POTENTIAL FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS
     Council Suggestions
     Councilmember Nordfelt requested a detailed report on how the City is handling
     the Coronovirus (COVID-19) pandemic. He stated that he would like to know how
     the Council can help facilitate assistance. He also indicated that he would like a
     follow-up on how public safety is functioning and what precautions are being taken.

       Mayor Bigelow stated that he would like an update on the Council Chamber
       remodel as that begins to occur in the next couple weeks. He also noted that City
       Council meetings may need to be entirely electronic if the situation doesn’t improve
       before the next scheduled Council Meeting on April 7, 2020.
