Continued Public Hearing on the FY 2024-25 Budget:
Council Members introduced proposed amendments to the FY 25 Budget for Council
consideration.
Council Member Stroud proposed the appropriation of $30,000 to fund a Municipal
Buildings Study which will focus and assess whether the city office space is meeting the
needs of the staff and effectively utilizing the space in City Hall, Justice Court Building,
and the Parks and Recreation Building. The study should provide insight and guidance to
the Council and Administration regarding future growth and needs of city departments
with respect to office space. The proposed funding source would come from the Capital
Project line item. Council questions followed. Council Members expressed support of
aspects of the proposal and provided feedback to Council Member Stroud. Shane Pace,
CAO, also expressed support of the municipal buildings study and provided feedback.
Council Member Sharkey proposed an amendment to the FY 2025 budget to maintain the
$575,000 subsidy from the General Fund for the Bulk Waste Program. The bulk waste
program is an appreciated program that is highly valued by our residents. The program
has always been subsidized by the General Fund. The continuation of the subsidy would
eliminate the need to charge the residents a program fee for the bulk waste program. A
lengthy Council discussion followed. Brian Kelley, Administrative Services Director,
responded to many Council questions. Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the FY25 proposed
budget. She spoke about the goal of providing and maintaining the highest level of
services for our residents while balancing increasing costs to provide these services.
Costs have increased significantly which necessitates the need to impose a fee to fund
the bulk waste program. The proposed fees relate directly to the expense of the bulk
waste service.
Public Hearing comment period opened:
Mr. Steve Van Maren thinks the city is very well run. The fee increase to cover rising
costs of the bulk waste program is appropriate and he accepts the need for a fee.
The Council moved to General Citizen Comment.
Following General Citizen Comment, the Council continued the discussion on the
Sharkey proposal.

6. 24-219 Administration presenting a resolution for Council consideration
encouraging Salt Lake County to impose the optional sales and use tax to
enhance public transportation
Attachments: Resolution 24-18C
Kim Bell, Deputy CAO, presented the Resolution 24-18C for Council consideration and
action. The resolution expresses support to Salt Lake County to impose the optional
0.2% sales and use tax to enhance transportation funding. The local tax was specifically
designed to fund improvements to highways and public transit systems which are vital for
managing the growing transportation demands. Ryan Kump, Public Works, provided
additional information regarding infrastructure projects funded through this tax. He spoke
about funding for transportation to the canyons and a pedestrian bridge over 115, providing
for an east-west connection from the Trax station in Sandy to the South Jordan front
runner station. Mr. Kump spoke about the need to establish the east-west connection in
order to qualify for Housing and Transit Reinvestment Zones (HTRZ) funding. He spoke
about future area projects including the National Hockey League practice and training
facility and other capital projects proposed for the Cairns development area. Council
questions, comments and discussion continued.
A motion was made to adopt Resolution 24-18C.
Public comment opened.
Public comment closed.
Council members continued their discussion and expressed whether they were supportive
of the resoluton. Council Members requested that a voting record be attached to the
resolution.

City Council Meeting Minutes June 11, 2024
Following the vote on the motions, Council moved to Standing Reports.

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report
Dustin Fratto, Council Director, mentioned the PCG consultant will finalize their report
next week. The consultants will present their findings and recommendations to the
Council at the July 9th meeting. The public hearing on the FY25 budget will continue next
week. He updated the Council on upcoming agenda items.

Council Member Business
Council Member Marci Houseman provided an update from the Jordan River Commission
meeting. The commission accomplishes many things with a small budget. She also
spoke about a presentation by Don Willie with the Point Development. She noted that he
spoke about the economic boon that is being predicted for Utah in the next 25 years and
the impact of the Point Development on neighboring cities.
Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer spoke about the recent town hall meeting held by the
Administration on annexation. The event was very well attended and very informative for
the residents of existing county islands.
Council Member Ryan Mecham said he was looking forward to the Historic Sandy
Community Barbecue and thanked all who were coordinating the event.
Council Member Stroud mentioned that she and Council Member Sharkey distributed
flyers in Historic Sandy inviting the residents to the barbecue. The town hall meeting was
a great event and well attended. She also mentioned that an area crosswalk had been
only temporarily removed, but was being put back in.
Council Member D'Sousa spoke about last year's Autism Community Barbecue. She was
very proud to be the sponsor of the initial event. It was a very successful and important
gathering and she looks forward to the continuation of the community event this fall and
expressed support to the Police Department for their coordination of this year's barbecue.
She looks forward to hearing more details about it.
Council Member Stroud also mentioned that this Friday there was an active shooting
training and she encouraged the Council to attend if they were available.

Mayor's Report
Mayor Zoltanski is looking forward to the Historic Sandy Barbecue and she thanked the
Council Members and Dawn Sidwell for their help in distributing flyers and invitations to
the residents. The town hall was a great event with over 150 residents of county islands in
attendance. She thanked all who attended. This Friday night is our first Movie in the Park
event at America First Field and will feature the Barbie movie. All are invited. The Sandy
Farmer's Market is this Saturday at Amphitheater Park from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm.
