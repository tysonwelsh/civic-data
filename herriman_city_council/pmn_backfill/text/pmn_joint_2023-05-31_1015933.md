2.    Discussion Items
      2.1.    Discussion regarding a proposed amendment to the Wasatch-South Hills
              Master Development Agreement- Blake Thomas, Community Development
              Director
Community Development Director Thomas disclosed the draft amendment changes
proposed for the Wasatch-South Hills MDA. Staff wanted the group to discuss how to
proceed with this further.

Councilmember Ohrn preferred to do work meetings for more discussion. Community
Development Director Thomas noted PID and land use issues would be a big subject for
discussions in this project. Councilmember Ohrn affirmed some things in this MDA are very
unbeneficial to the city, but it was already approved. City Planner Maloy detailed his concern
with the MDA not aligning with the new general east Herriman general plan. Despite this, the
MDA and the zoning ordinance need to align. Councilmember Shields aired the agreement
made in 2009 doesn’t correlate with our current general plan for that area. This wasn’t a
simple discussion; we need to open the door for a bigger discussion.

Commissioner Jacobson alluded these changes are more beneficial to the developer than any
of the city’s current voices. He inquired about letting the old MDA end and creating a brand
new one, or at least reviewing if we started brand new with today’s general plan then
compare it to what was already approved. Councilmember Shields agreed it benefited the
developer but isn’t in harmony with the current general plan. Mayor Palmer agreed to set up
work meetings for further discussion. Councilmember Shields disclosed he wanted the
clause removed that adds land to MDA and moved the housing to other pods. Community
Development Director Thomas detailed there was no new commercial, the commercial for
the MDA had already started construction but nothing new has been added to the area.
Councilmember Ohrn expressed if the MDA was open for discussion, she wanted the legal
team’s position on what was beneficial to the city and their advice on what could be amended.

       2.2.   Discussion regarding a planned development by Development Associates,
              Inc-Blake Thomas, Community Development Director
Community Development Director Thomas discussed proposed changes to the planned
development by Development Associates. Commercial property was being cut which would
require the general plan to be amended. Commissioner Rypien wondered why the developer
kept cutting commercial property and would like to hear from the developer.

Legal Counsel for the developer Chase Andrizzi addressed the group and stated currently,
the new plan didn’t add any new units and iterated how they were laid out. Part of the
proposal would be to amend the Rosecrest MDA and present the possibility of creating a new
MDA for removed sections. Developer Mike Bradshaw conveyed there always had been
residential in the commercial section that was removed per the MDA. The value of residential
and commercial was not viewed the same between the parties. Commercial wasn’t viable in
some areas due to topography issues. Community Development Director Thomas mentioned
the CDA contemplated commercial in the area, but also mentioned flexibility in uses.
Commissioner Sickles asked if there was a way to get both residential and commercial,
possibly mixed use or even vertical mixed use. Councilmember Ohrn claimed commercial
developers are wanting land in Herriman.

Community Development Director Thomas showed areas that would be removed from
Rosecrest and Wasatch MDA and moved to a new MDA. Business center CDA also wanted
to amend the units. Councilmember Shields asked for verification on the commercial success
has been in this area, Community Development Director Thomas responded 98% of the
commercial property was filled.

Community Development Director Thomas informed the group of the environmental aspects
needed to be considered with hillside overlay, drainage path, vegetation, and minimal grading.
The staff asked if they should start with working groups to set up discussions.

Commissioner Powell suggested walking/bike trails that would connect all the way to Utah
County along the corridor. He specified making trails wide enough to accommodate bikes
and walking but not connected to the corridor.

Mr. Andrizzi assured them they have worked with landowners and have a lot of details with
engineered trail connection and want to present in a work meeting. Mr. Bradshaw
emphasized they understand that not everyone was happy with the old plan. This project

was started 25 years ago, and they want to have a great plan for finishing this out.
Councilmember Shields showed appreciation for them laying out what they are proposed for
this last bit, but the MDA was in conflict of what the city is wanting now. Councilmember

to connect to the existing trail in the Rose Crest area. Commissioner Sickles asked for

Commissioner Oberg scrutinized where are the kids going to play as the lots were small, and
suggested the kids would end up playing in the street when the proposed open space is not
actually usable with the slopes.

Councilmember Ohrn implied this should come to a work meeting and if needed break out to
committees. Councilmember Hodges agreed the committees should be working on
assignment under the council’s advice.

Councilmember Shields disclosed a lot of the changes that are being proposed are from
listening to past council meetings. He thanked them for making those changes that align with
previous meetings, but a few more things need to be ironed out with it. Commissioner Rypien
pointed out the new units don’t have a central gathering place, small park, or place to play.
