CALL TO ORDER
Chair McConnehey called the MBA meeting to order at 7:09 p.m.

MINUTES REVIEW AND APPROVAL
The minutes of the December 4, 2019 MBA meeting were reviewed.

MOTION:        Board Member Green moved to approve the minutes of the December
               4, 2019 MBA meeting. Board Member Whitelock seconded the motion.

A roll call vote was taken

Board Member Whitelock       Yes
Chair McConnehey             Yes
Board Member Lamb            Yes
Board Member Worthen         Yes
Board Member Jacob           Yes
Board Member Green           Yes
Board Member Pack            Yes

The motion passed 7-0.
