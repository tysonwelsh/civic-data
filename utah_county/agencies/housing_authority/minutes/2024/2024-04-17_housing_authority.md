B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-02-02 Fraud Assessment 2023 State of Utah  
April made a motion to approve the submission of the Fraud Assessment 2023 State of 
Utah. Amelia seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
Each year HAUC is tasked with completing the Fraud Assessment for the State of Utah. This 
is a self-questionnaire to determine risk. The questions were scored and currently HAUC is 
scoring 375 which is the lowest risk category. Commissioner Gardner asked about question 9 
regarding a fraud committee. HAUC believe using BDO would be considered part of  a fraud 
committee for the agency. Commissioner Gardner requested that we double check on that 
definition and offered to reach out to the State Auditor’s office. The resolution was approved 
pending feedback from the State Auditor regarding question 9. HAUC will either submit or 
adjust as necessary and then submit, per feedback from the State Auditor.  
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-02-03 Modify Resolution 2024-01-04 RE Purchase Agmt 
Suzanne made a motion to approve the modification of Resolution 2024-01-04 Purchase 
Agmt. April seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
Staff is recommending that the prior resolution 2024-02-03 be amended to included looking for 
real estate outside of the Spanish Fork and Springville jurisdictions only. Originally the MAG 
grant submission was going to include an element of real estate purchase. As part of the grant 
the purchase would need to be in either Spanish Fork or Springville. After thorough research 
from HAUC staff it was determined the grant submission would not include a purchase 
element. Therefore, at this time it will be wise to expand the search area for real estate 
purchase in order to expand the affordable units owned by HAUC. 
 
 
D. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-02-04 Inspection Agreement 
Amelia made a motion to approve the Inspection Agreement. Sara seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
HAUC was approved by Community Action Services (CAS) to provide inspection services for 
their agency. Community Action was awarded a grant for rental assistance that requires each 
unit be inspected for HUD HQS deficiencies. Community Action does not have the capacity to 
hire an inspector at this time. CAS would like to have HAUC certified HQS inspectors 
complete the inspections for the tenant based rental assistance they will be providing to 
clients. HAUC will be paid a base rate for the inspection and mileage, a lower base rate will be 
paid for any re-inspections that will occur. HAUC currently has several certified staff members 
who will be willing to complete the inspections for CAS. The contract is a 1-year contract that 
will be reassessed at the end of 12 months before renewal.
