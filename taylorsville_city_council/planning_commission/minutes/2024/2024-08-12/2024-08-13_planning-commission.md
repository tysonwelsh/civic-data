Chair Wilkey called the meeting to order at 6: 02 p. m. and turned the time over to Senior

Chair Wilkey opened the public hearing.

Meredith Harker explained that Summit Vista was in her district, and the residents were
great neighbors. She felt it was a beautiful development and was excited about their
continued       development.


Andy Triplett' s house was located adjacent to the property. He disagreed that they were
good neighbors, saying they had failed to be responsive to the concerns of those that
lived just outside the development. He explained that they were pleasant enough on the
phone, but never actually did anything to help. Water suppression had been horrible, and
this aggravated           the asthma that he and his wife suffered from. He also expressed concern

that storm drains were not remaining clear and accessible.

There was no one else who expressed a desire to speak, so Chair Wilkey closed the
public hearing.

Commissioner Quigly was grateful that Mr. Triplett had taken the time to attend the
meeting and outline his concerns. He encouraged Summit Vista to sit down with Mr.
Triplett and his neighbors to discuss their concerns and come up with some solutions.

Chair Wilkey acknowledged the nod and " thumbs up" she received from the Summit Vista
representatives, that they were willing to work with the concerned neighbors.

MOTION:          Commissioner McElreath moved to approve File# 20C24 for a conditional
                 use permit for Phase 2 of the Summit Vista Master Planned Community,
                 subject to the findings and conditions outlined in the staff report. The
                 motion was seconded by Commissioner Willardson.
