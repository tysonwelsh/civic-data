1.

General Plan, Water Element, Quality Open Lands, Livable Neighborhoods
Senior Planner Brandon Rypien presented three chapters: Quality Open Lands, Livable

Neighborhoods, and Water Element. Maps and slides were shown based on the vision. Key points
included the need to add 60 acres of open space by 2050 to maintain per capita acreage, the
importance of infill housing, and the goal to increase owner-occupied housing to 70%. The water
element emphasized the need for infrastructure updates and water conservation measures,
including hydro zones and gray water use. The Plan focused on key elements of what Ogden
provides the community throughout the city and what it needs to provide in the future.
