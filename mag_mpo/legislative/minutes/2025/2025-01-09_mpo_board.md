Public Comment (00:03:06) Chair Mayor Bill Wright opened the meeting to the public. There were no public comments. Minutes - Action (00:03:34) Mayor Daniel Olson moved to approve the minutes from November 7, 2024. Mayor Julie Fullmer seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Corridor Preservation, Provo 820 North (00:04:12) Calvin Clark stated that the City of Provo was approached by the property owner for the purchase of the single family parcel that backs up to corridor H42. Mayor Julie Fullmer moved to approve the City of Provo's request for $675,575 from the Corridor Preservation Fund, finding that it adheres to the fund's requirements. Mayor Matt Packard seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. UTA Update (00:09:11) Trustee Acerson highlighted the efforts of the northwest county mayors, and Jay Fox presented a revenue report, explaining the geographic economic model and the importance of a regional system. Jay Fox also discussed the challenges of the labor crunch and the sustainable service delivery system, emphasizing the need for good working hours for operators. Jay Fox then outlined the additional service planned for Utah County in April 2026, including new routes and on-demand zones. A question was asked about the criteria for adding new routes, and Jay Fox explained the systemic-based analysis and the importance of ridership propensity. The Board expressed the importance of being involved in the planning process to ensure equitable service distribution. Congestion Management Process Adoption (00:50:00) Calvin Clark explained the Congestion Management Process (CMP) and its federal requirements, including analyzing travel demand reduction strategies. Calvin Clark explained the approach taken to meet the federal requirements and how the CMP will be integrated with existing processes like the TIP and RTP. Mayor Brad Frost moved to approve the proposed Congestion Management Process. Page 2 of 4 DRAFT Mayor Jim Miller seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. RTP Amendment 2/ Air Quality Conformity Determination (01:03:32) Shawn Eliot highlighted the four changes adding 10 RTP Projects. There is one Level 1 amendment and four Level 3 amendments Level 1 Amendment: ● A88 Highline Canal this is moving from phase 2 to phase 1 fiscally constrained Level 3 Amendments: ● H31 Cory Wride FWY (west) proposes a new route alignment, change to fiscally constrained phase from 2 to 1, the cost of the project, and construction time. Cory Wride FWY & Cedar Valley FWY propose a new route alignment, the RTP phasing, and the cost of the project. ● H114 Geneva RD Widening proposes adding the project to the RTP. ● H115 I-15 FWY Passing Lane proposes adding the project to the RTP. Mayor Mark Johnson moved to approve TransPlan50 Amendment 2 and the associated Air Quality Conformity Determination Report, barring any regionally significant comments during the public comment period. Mayor Daniel Olson seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. TIP Modifications (01:14:03) Bob Allen explained that with the adoption of the previous RTP Amendment 2 item, three of the projects need to be added to the TIP. Mayor Fullmer moved to approve that the Cory Wride FWY, I-15 Passing Lanes, and Geneva RD projects be added to the TIP as presented with the finding that these projects come from phase one of the fiscally constrained, air quality conforming Regional Transportation Plan. Mayor Denise Andersen seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Wasatch Choice Vision Goals and Technical Process (01:15:31) Dan Wayne presented on the Wasatch Choice Vision (WCV) and the process for updating it, including assembling local general plans and transportation master plans and scheduling individual meetings to extend plans to the necessary planning horizon of 2055. Mayor Denise Andersen moved to approve the WCV Update technical approach (Attachment A) with WCV Workgroup refinements and carry over existing WCV goals and strategies (Attachment B) for the WCV update. Mayor Julie Fullmer seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Page 3 of 4 DRAFT RTP Process and 2025 Work (01:23:22) Shawn Eliot presented an overview of the upcoming year's work for the Regional Transportation Plan, emphasizing the need for data-supported projects and the detailed steps in the 2025 RTP process. Other Business and Adjournment (01:30:19) Chair Mayor Bill Wright stated the next MPO Board meeting is scheduled for February 13, 2025. Mayor Frost moved to adjourn the meeting.
