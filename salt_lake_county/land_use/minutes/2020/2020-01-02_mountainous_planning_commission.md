6) Short Term Rentals ordinance update discussion – Laynee Jones 
 
Commissioner Jones said they were approving a short-term rental/Airbnb to require owner of home 
renters have four-wheel drive and snow tires and Mr. Shaw was concerned didn’t have backup. Mr. 
Shaw said he took the discussion and drafted what’s in the packet draft ordinance, allows planning 
commission when approving a conditional use short term rental to impose a condition that the rental 
agreement requires snow tires or four-wheel drive when snow packed surfaces. Tailored with date 
range, snow tires and four-wheel drive. Researched the issue, one big problem is they ’re rentals 
and a requirement in ordinance not realistic or feasible, researched if the airport rents out, found 
that most car rental companies do not rent four-wheel drive that are reliable. Found one company 
that does provide four wheel drive rentals. Checked on the website for tires, and indicate all season, 
not snow tires. Don’t allow chains on the tires. Commissioner Palmer said UPD has been working 
and calling it all wheel drive , mud and snow, and four-wheel drive snow tires or chains. They say 
traction laws aren’t in effect, and now you have a non -compliant vehicle in the canyon. Mr. Shaw 
said goes into ordinance it must be four-wheel drive and snow tires. People get to  the airport and 
setting it up, doesn’t know how to enforce it. Commissioner Palmer said instead of turning on and 
off and make it a requirement for the whole winter. 
 
Commissioners and counsel had a brief discussion regarding UD OT, rental car companies, 
compliance issues, imposing conditions, applying 24/7, bus service/ski uber. Commissioner Jones 
said need to choose the time frame and is there a way to match UDOT traction requirements. Mr. 
Shaw said he will update the language and bring it back to February 6 th. County Council likes to 
make a green light and then to Community Council and then to the planning commission. Restriction
