RECITALS
A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
401 et seq.
B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.
C. The Magna Long-Range Strategic Planning Committee (the “Committee”) is an Advisory
Comumnittee created by the Council to provide advice to the Council regarding issues
involving the Long-Range Strategic Plans of Magna.
D. To create a basic framework from which to work forward on strategic planning issues, the
Committee created the attached Magna Vision and Mission Statements to guide future
discussions and work.
E. The Committee recommends the Council formally adopt the Magna Vision and Mission
Statements.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council hereby approves
and adopts for use, the Magna Vision and Mission Statements, hereinafter to be known as
Exhibit A. .
