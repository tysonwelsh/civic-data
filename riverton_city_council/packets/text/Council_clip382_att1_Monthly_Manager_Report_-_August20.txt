                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                                August 2020
August was highlighted by the return of our children to school and the associated responsibilities
this brings to our department. We spent time during the summer to solidify our emergency
response plans at our two middle schools and Riverton High School. Our School Resource Officers
have a year under their belt and are excited to be in the halls protecting the students. Additionally,
they have devised some innovative ways to teach our DARE curriculum while being conscientious
about the spread of COVID-19.

We are still seeing vehicle burglaries in our city at a level which makes me uncomfortable. This is
not an issue specific to Riverton as surrounding communities have seen an even more dramatic
increase in these types of crimes. That said, we have coordinated with our Communications
Department and engaged in a public service campaign on our social media platforms to inform the
public about the risk of leaving garage doors open at night, cars unlocked, and valuable items in
vehicles.

DUI arrests were again outstanding during August as our graveyard crew continues to focus on
identifying drivers who may be impaired on our roadways to ensure the safety of our residents. Our
traffic units also continued to ramp up our speed enforcement efforts as we see the threat of the
spread of COVID-19 reduce with time, which resulted in an increase in traffic violations.
