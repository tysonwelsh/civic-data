Re: Summit Commerce Center Subdivision Plat Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for approximately 26.937 acres located on the west side of Pony Express Road, at approximately 12965 S. Wheatfield Way. The property is currently zoned M1 . The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat Amendment be approved to allow for consolidation of the existing lots in the Greenfield Farms Subdivision into a single lot to facilitate future development of the site with larger commercial and manufacturing buildings. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1.   That there is good cause for the vacation or amendment.
2.   No public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended by the plat.
For Denial:
1.   Good cause does not exist for the vacation or amendment.
