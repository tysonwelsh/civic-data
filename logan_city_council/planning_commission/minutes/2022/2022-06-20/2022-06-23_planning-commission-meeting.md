 PC 22-032 Hansen Street Airbnb-Short Term Rental [Conditional Use Permit] John Steven &
 Jlene Hansen TRS are requesting a Conditional Use Permit to rent out a portion of their home as
 a short-term rental located at 800 East Center Street in the Suburban Neighborhood Residential
 (NR-4) zone; TIN 06-088-0045 (Wilson Neighborhood).


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a short-term rental at 800 East Center Street. The short-
term rental portion of the home is a separate two (2) bedroom living space on the east side of the
home above the garage with access via the deck on the south side of the home. The rest of the
home will remain the primary residence for the owners. As proposed, the project meets the parking
requirement with two designated parking stalls for the short-term rental and two for the residence.
The maximum occupancy for the short-term rental is six individuals.


    Commissioner Croshaw asked about the length of the driveway meeting requirements for fire truck
    access. R. Holley said that homes that are 150 feet or more from the street are required to have a
    fire truck turn around. This home does have a loop turnaround, and this would be addressed at
    licensing. Commissioner Doutre asked about the proposed parking stall measurements being less
    than the required size, and if the stalls have to be marked. R. Holley said that the parking stall
    measurements have to be met to obtain the license, and the stalls do not have to be marked.

    PROPONENT: John Hansen, the property owner, stated in response to the presentation slide
    showing they owned additional property below a neighbor who had sent in comments, that they do
    not own the narrow arm of the property that extends to the east, connecting to Mountain Road. It is
    owned by the Ballams.

    PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

    COMMISSION: There was no discussion by the Commission.

    MOTION: Commissioner Heare moved to conditionally approve a Conditional Use Permit for PC
    22-032 with the conditions of approval and findings for approval as listed below. Commissioner
    Peterson seconded the motion.

    CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1  All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
   available in the Community Development Department.
2 This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a two (2) bedroom short-term rental occupying a portion
   of the single-family home.
3 This Conditional Use Permit authorizes two (2) people per bedroom with two (2) additional
   individuals for a maximum of six (6) occupants within the STR.
4 Occupancy for the single-family home portion of the structure shall comply with LDC regulations.
5 A minimum of two (2) parking stalls are required for the STR and two parking stalls (2) are
   required for the residence. All parking areas shall be located on-site, off-street, and outside of
   the front yard 25’ setback. All parking stalls shall be paved, shall measure at least 9’x18’, and
   the site shall maintain a minimum 12-foot-wide driveway for access. Parking shall be fully
   installed and approved prior to the issuance of the business license.
6 Parking of recreational vehicles, trailers, boats, ATVs, or similar vehicles associated with the STR
   shall be legally parked on-site and are not permitted to be parked on street.
7 Rental contracts for the STR shall be for 30 days or less.
8 Rental contracts shall not be offered to the same individual, group, or party for more than two
   consecutive rental contracts in a six (6) month period. The owner shall maintain rental records
   available for review and inspection by Logan City.
9 Quiet times shall comply with the City noise ordinance of 10 pm to 8 am.
10 A Logan City building/fire inspection is required which may result in building remodeling and
   building permits prior to the issuance of a business license.
11 A Logan City business license is required before a rental contract can be issued.
12 The STR shall comply with local and state Building, Health, Fire, Safety, Maintenance, and
   Nuisance Codes prior to operating.
13 Exterior lighting shall be concealed source, down-cast, and reviewed and approved prior to the
   issuance of a building permit and shall comply with current LDC regulations.
14 No signs are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All signage shall be approved and
   permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.
15 No fences are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All fences shall be approved and
   permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.


16 Prior to issuance of a Building Permit, the Director of Community Development shall receive a
   written memorandum from each of the following departments or agencies indicating that their
   requirements have been satisfied:
         a. Water/Cross Connection - contact 716-9627
             • All points of use of water downstream of this property's water meter should meet Utah’s
                current IPC plumbing code and amendments. This would help minimize the risk of
                contamination of the water for the home's occupants and Logan Cities' water system. This
                also would help minimize liabilities to the homeowner.
         b. Business Licensing - contact 716-9230
             • Business license required.

 1. The proposed project conforms to the requirements of the Land Development Code and is listed
    as a conditional use in the Use Table.
 2. This STR meets the City’s STR density and location parameters as specified in Chapter
    17.37.130.
 3. As conditioned the use is compatible with the surrounding land uses and will not interfere with
    the use and enjoyment of adjoining or area properties.
 4. The streets providing access and other infrastructure to the subject property have adequate
    capacities and level of service for the conditional use.
 5. The Planning Commission has substantiated the findings for a Conditional Use Permit in Chapter
    17.42.050.

 PC 22-036 Ville 364 Rezone [Zone Change] Mike DeSimone/Logan City are requesting a zone
 change of two parcels totaling 1.32 acres from Commercial (COM) to Town Center 1 (TC-1) to
 allow for the refurbishment of the existing motel into 64 residential units located at 364 South
 Main Street; TIN 02-053-0038; -0014 (Wilson Neighborhood).


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a zone change from Commercial to Town Center One
(TC-1) at 364 South Main Street where the existing two-story motel is converting to long-term
housing units on both the ground floor and upper floor. A zone change is needed due to the number
of housing units exceeding the residential density limit in the commercial zone, and the commercial
zone not allowing residential units on the ground floor. The TC-1 zone requires commercial in the
first 50 feet on the ground floor. The South portion of the hotel already contains office space, they
will need to create a commercial space in the first 50 feet of the north building. The owner has
partnered with state and federal programs to offer affordable housing to people who may not
otherwise qualify for rentals.

Commissioner Doutre asked if any other zones would allow for housing units on the main floor. R.
Holley said that the Town Center One zone is the only zone that will allow a density of 70 units per
acre.

Commissioner Lucero asked if a property can be rezoned just for a specific project, or use. R. Holley
said no, once a property is rezoned, any permitted use in that specific zone would be allowed and it
remains that zone until a zone change is requested and approved.

Commissioner Croshaw asked if additional landscaping could be required. R. Holley said that you
cannot do conditional zoning. Because the TC-1 zone is an urban-type of zone, there are no
landscaping requirements except for the front setback.

PROPONENT: Keith Warburton, owner of the property said they have been renting the units on the
upper floor as long-term rentals and short-term rentals on the main floor to help get people off the
street and become stable. We are a housing first organization, if we can get people housed, we can
then get them the other services they need to help stabilize their housing.

They work closely with BRAG (Bear River Association of Governments) and other service providers
in the area, and there are a lot of people that receive funding for housing for a year, but they do not
qualify for rentals. It would be nice to see people get to use the funding and stay at one place for a
year and not have to bounce from hotel room to hotel room. With our zero barrier entry program,


everyone is eligible as long as the rules are followed, their past does not prevent them from getting
housing.

There have been some small upgrades to the units, adding cabinets, cooktops, and fridges. Most
of the units are studio units while the back building does have some larger units which will be
remodeled into one-bedroom units.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Heare said this is what should be done to keep the property
maintained and provide affordable housing. Commissioner Doutre said this type of zero barrier
housing is needed in Logan and maybe there should be a zone created for future development of
this type of housing. Commissioner Lucero said that nothing is going to change with the property
externally. Commissioner Croshaw asked if the zone change will affect the parking. R. Holley said
that the use of the property from motel to long-term residential will change the required parking from
1 to 1.5.

MOTION: Commissioner Heare moved to forward a recommendation of approval for PC 22-036
to the Municipal Council on a Zone Change for PC 22-036 with the findings for approval as listed
below. Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as COM.
2. This area is positioned along Highway 89/91/Main Street with commercial uses to the north and
   south and adequate utilities present.
3. Infrastructure and access are located nearby and have the ability to adequately serve the future
   development of this property.
4. Design and use are compatible with surrounding areas and can be mitigated through Design
   Review processes.
