I would like to nominate Detective Carson Holm for employee of the month.

Carson is assigned as the detective for the contract city of Vineyard. Carson takes care of Vineyard
cases with professionalism and dedication to the city he is in charge of. Over the past couple of

Vineyard was having a rash of vehicle burglaries and vehicle thefts. The suspects were targeting
Hyundai vehicles due to a manufacturing problem with the keys making the vehicles easy to

Vineyard Substation; other substations in the state had been recently hit also. Carson was able to
