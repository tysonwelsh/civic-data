Study Session

1.0   Presentation: Utah Wellbeing Project

1.1   Dr. Courtney Flint with Utah State University shared the results of the 2022
      Utah Wellbeing survey conducted earlier in the year, compared with results in
      previous years, and answered questions from the Council.

2.0   Update: Traffic

2.1   Police Chief John Eining reported September traffic enforcement statistics.

      The Mayor and Council expressed appreciation to Bob Wylie, Finance Director
      who is retiring from City employment.

3.0   Council/Manager Reports

3.1   Fire Chief Clint Smith showed a short video highlighting work done by the Fuels
      Mitigation crew during the summer.

9.0   Public Hearing: Ordinances #1555 and #1556, an Ordinance approving the
      Avery Townhomes Development Agreement and amending the official Zoning
      Map of Draper City for approximately 2.44 acres of property from the OR
      (Office/Residential) zone to the RM2 (Multi Family Residential, up to 12 du/ac)
      zone, and an Ordinance amending the official Land Use Map of Draper City
      from Neighborhood Commercial to Residential High Density. The property is
      located at 638 East 12225 South, otherwise known as the Avery Townhomes
      Development Agreement, Zoning Map Amendment, and Land Use Map
      Amendment.

9.1   Planning Manager Jennifer Jastremsky explained the proposed Development
      Agreement and rezone request. The Development Agreement would limit the
      number of allowed units on the site to 21 and would lock in a specific concept
      plan. The applicant was approved to build 17 units. She showed a concept site
      plan with 21 units and said the Planning Commission forwarded a positive
      recommendation.
