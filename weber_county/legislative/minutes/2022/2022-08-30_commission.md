     2. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 41-2022 CONFIRMING THEIR DIRECTION TO FILE CROSS-
        APPEALS FOR 2022 CENTRALLY ASSESSED PROPERTIES.
            Courtlan Erickson (Attorneys Office): Every year certain properties within the state of Utah are assessed for
            their valuation by the state Tax Commission’s Property Tax division. Typically these are mining properties,
            railroads, utilities, very large companies that by statute the county Assessor does not assess their business
            property, it’s the state that does that. If the taxpayers disagree with the value assigned to their property, they
            have the right to appeal to the state Tax Commission. Counties’ tax revenues are potentially impacted. Each
            year we determine if we want to participate in these appeals as a responding party. The question before you
            today is whether to approve filing of cross appeals for Pacific Corp, Lumen Technologies (formerly Century
            Link, Utah Railway Company, and Compass Minerals; three were filed under your prior direction and Compass
            Minerals will be filed if you approve this. We recommend the county files as directed in the past.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 41-2022 County confirming their direction to file cross-
            appeals for 2022 centrally assessed properties; Commissioner Freorer seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
