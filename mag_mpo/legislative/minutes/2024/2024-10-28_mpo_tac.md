Public Engagement Plan Adoption (00:02:58) Kendall Willardson reminded the Committee that the Public Engagement Plan is a Federal requirement the MPO must have. He gave a brief overview of the plan explaining this is a great document to help with public engagement for the RTP and TIP. Jason Bond moved to recommend that the MPO Board approve the Public Engagement Plan as presented, finding that it adheres to federal regulations and addresses the issues raised in the 2023 Federal Planning Certification Review of the MAG MPO. Chris Trusty seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Corridor Preservation, Provo 800 North (00:05:30) Calvin Clark addressed the Committee stating this is for Provo along 820 North, Corridor H42. The property owner is a willing seller and approached the city for the purchase. Any unused portion of the property that is not needed for the project will be sold and the funds from the sale will go back into the Corridor Preservation Fund. Page 2 of 4 Rob Hunter moved to recommend that the MPO Board approve the city of Provo's request for $460,875 from the Corridor Preservation Fund, finding that it adheres to the fund's requirements. Jason Bond seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Congestion Management Process (CMP) Overview (00:14:05) Calvin Clark stated this is a federal management process. A CMP is required and must provide an approach for managing congestion, assesses alternative strategies for congestion, analysis of reasonable travel demand reduction and operational management strategies, and integral part of a larger overall planning process. It is important to monitor and evaluate effectiveness to better measure what MAG is doing. Calvin Clark explained what MAG has been doing to develop a document detailing the CMP. It is important this process provides tools that marginally congested roadways can utilize to avoid capacity projects. TIP Amendment Policy Update (00:27:44) Bob Allen stated in meetings with the FHWA it was advised to update the TIP Amendment Policy. This is federally required and there were two concerns raised related to Scope Modifications and Air Quality Consultation (ITC). The proposed changes are not major but rather minor tweaks to help clearly spelling out process and expectations. This item is for information only at this time and as the document is refined it will come to the Committee for a recommendation of approval to the MPO Board. The proposed process presented will be taken to ITC and UDOT for their approval. Draft MAG MPO Environmental Guidance Update (00:37:20) Bob Allen explained that he frequently gets questions about what environmental work needs to be completed with the RTP projects. MAG hired an environmental firm to spell out what permitting and work is needed based on the scope and scale of the project. This is a guidance sheet for locally funded projects. There will be multiple questions that guide the reader to what permits are needed based on the response to the questions. This is not a new process, just a tool to guide Cities through the permitting process for environmental work. The guide will also include best practices when involving the public. The final form should be presented the first of next year to the MPO TAC and MPO Board. There was discussion of the proposed amendment related to H66, H67 and H89. Data Modeling Process (01:06:04) Tim Hereth stated this item is informational for the Committee. The RTP is updated every four years and there is a base year for the data. The data uses building stock, household and population estimates, job estimates, travel demand model inputs, travel demand model calibration, land use capacity, building stock plus, and socioeconomic forecasting. Tim Hereth went into depth for each data set outlining when the information is collected and analyzed through the four year process. There was discussion of utilizing ADU data from the cities and other concerns related to accurate assessment of the housing in each city. 2025 MPO TAC Meeting Schedule (01:32:55) Shawn Eliot presented the staff report with the proposed MPO TAC dates on the fourth Monday of each month.
