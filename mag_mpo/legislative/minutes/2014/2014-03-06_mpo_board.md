InterPlan, Connetics Transportation Group and HDR are consultants for the study. Study area includes: Eagle Mountain, Saratoga Springs, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Lindon, Orem, and Vineyard. Three areas of focus for the study: examine potential north/south LRT extension from Draper into Utah County, working with UTA to plan circulator bus service from Thanksgiving Point FrontRunner station, and east/west transit connections between Eagle Mountain, Saratoga Springs, and Lehi. Currently we are developing a draft circulation network from FrontRunner station and transit competitiveness. Study should be completed summer 2014. MPO Congestion Management/Project Selection Process - Shawn Eliot The Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) is a 4 year program of transportation projects and programs of all federally funded projects and all significant state and local projects. Changes are addressed with modifications and amendments. The Congestion Management/Project Selection Process is use to select and place new projects into TIP. The MPO Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) and the MPO Regional Planning Committee (RPC) are the approving bodies for this process. Every two years the MPO selects new projects to be funded with MPO funds and county funds. TAC committee develops projects ideas to be considered, TAC then chooses which project ideas should be submit with a detailed concept report. TAC will then recommend the final list to Regional Planning for approval. Special Study requested from the cities will also be reviewed and scored for placement in the 2014-2015 Unified Planning Work Program. A Concept Report for Special Study is not required.
