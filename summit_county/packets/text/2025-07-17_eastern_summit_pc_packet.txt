Proposal 
The Applicant is requesting an amendment to the Kamas East Plat “A” Subdivision that involves 
vacating Parcel KE-A-21 from the Subdivision. As part of the vacation, a portion of N Redwing 
Lane, which is platted as a Private Road, would also need to be vacated from the Subdivision. 
The Planning Commission is the Land Use Authority for subdivision plat amendments involving 
the alteration or vacation of a private road. If the vacation is approved, the Applicant proposes 
to combine Parcel KE-A-21 with Parcel CD-450-D. The resulting combined Parcel would be 15.62 
acres. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
9
Vicinity Map 
  
 
Background 
On August 11, 1972, the Kamas East Plat “A” Subdivision (Exhibit C) was recorded in Summit 
County (Entry Number 116600). In the original Subdivision, Lot 21 contained 2.04 acres. Prior to 
May 6, 1996, several of the original Lots in the Kamas East Plat “A” Subdivision, including Lot 
21, were split into two parcels. Staff has included the Working Copy of the original Subdivision,  
Exhibit C, to show that Lot 21 was divided into two parcels. This specific division was completed 
prior to the Development Code being in effect and therefore both Lots (KE-A-21 and KE-A-21-A) 
are considered Grandfathered Parcels. On April 4, 2025, the Community Development 
Department received the subject Plat Amendment application. 
 
Analysis and Findings 
The Planning Commission may approve, approve with conditions, or deny an Amendment to a 
Recorded Subdivision Plat based upon written findings of fact according to each of the 
following standards. It is the responsibility of the applicant to provide written and graphic 
evidence demonstrating compliance. 
  
Section 11-4-5 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code sets the following grounds for 
vacating or changing a plat: 
 
10

or amendment, and the public interest or any person shall not be materially injured by 
the proposed vacation, alteration or amendment, the Land Use Authority may vacate, 
alter or amend a public or private road within a final subdivision plat. 
 
Analysis: Both Parcels KE-A-21 and CD-450-D are both owned by Mark Monsen. There is 
currently an existing barn on Parcel CD-450-D that does not meet the required setbacks 
from the property line along the southwest side of KE-A-21. The property owner would 
like to add an addition to the existing barn but would not be able to do so without 
encroaching on Parcel KE-A-21. The proposed amendment would bring the existing barn 
into compliance with setbacks as well as allow the applicant to move forward with the 
proposed addition to the barn. Staff finds that there is good cause for the proposed 
amendment. COMPLIES 
 
2. No subdivision plat amendment that vacates, alters or amends a public or private road 
shall be approved which results in an increase in density except as provided in 
subsection E10 of this section. 
 
Analysis: The proposal will not result in an increase in density. COMPLIES

boundary along Lot 10.

STAFF REPORT 
 
To:    Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
From:    Jennifer Leslie, County Planner

Type of Item: Eastern Summit County Development Code Amendments –

Final Land Use Authority:  Summit County Council 
Process:   Legislative 
Project Number:  25-016 
 
 
PROPOSAL 
Staff proposes to remove the requirements for Final Site Plans and the entirety of Section 11-4-
6 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code. The proposed amendments would remove 
duplicitous requirements for Conditional Use Permit applicants.   
 
UPDATE SINCE MARCH 6, 2025 
This item was first brought to the ESCPC on March 6, 2025. A public hearing was held and 
closed and there was no public comment. During the meeting, the ESCPC expressed concerns 
related to public access to approved conditional uses. The ESCPC requested that Staff explore 
ways to combine the Final Site Plan requirements with the site plan requirements for 
conditional use permits. Prior to exploring those options, Staff would like to better understand 
the intentions of the ESCPC and clarify the how documents in the public record can be 
accessed. 
 
BACKGROUND 
Staff is proposing amendments to the Code to remove Section 11-4-6, which describes 
requirements for submitting Final Site Plans. The intention of the Final Site Plan language is to 
ensure that a document is recorded against the property to depict the site plan for an approved 
development. Final Site Plans are required to have signature blocks and include the meets and 
bounds of a property with the proposed development shown. The Final Site Plan section of the 
code is only required for Conditional Use Permits (CUP), which have their own site plan and 
survey requirements. CUPs also run with the land and impose conditions for development, 
which effectively achieves the same result as recordation of a Final Site Plan.  
 
As written, the current language requires that a separate land use application is filed with any

ANALYSIS 
Section 11-5-3 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code establishes a process for 
amendments to the text of the Code. It states that whenever an amendment to the Code is 
initiated, it must be reviewed by the Planning Commission who will deliver a recommendation 
to the County Council. The County Council, after holding a public hearing, can approve, approve 
with modifications, or deny the amendment. There is no criterion. 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
Staff recommends the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission review the proposed 
language amending specific sections of the Eastern Summit County Development Code, conduct 
a public hearing, and vote to forward a positive recommendation to the Summit County 
Council.  
 
 
Findings of Fact: 
1. Staff is proposing amendments to the Eastern Summit County Development Code 
(Code) to remove Section 11-4-6: Final Site Plans and all references to final site plans. 
Any references to the Code sections following 11-4-6 within Chapter 4 would have 
updated references.  
2. Staff determined these amendments are primarily administrative in nature, intended to 
clarify and reduce the submittal requirements for Development Permit applicants.  
3. These amendments do not affect density or other substantive development standards. 
4. The amendments apply to the entire Eastern Summit County Planning District. 
 
Conclusions of Law: 
1. The amendments are consistent with the goals, objectives, and policies of the General 
Plan. 
2. The amendments are consistent with the requirements established in chapter 6 of the 
Eastern Summit County Development Code.  
3. The proposed amendments are not detrimental to public health, safety, and welfare.
