CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL MEETING
June 09, 2015

The Cache County Council convened in a regular session on June 09, 2015 at
5:00 p.m. in the Cache County Council Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.

ATTENDANCE:

Chairman: Kathy Robison

Vice Chairman: G. Gregory Merrill

Council Members: David Erickson, Val Potter, Jon White, Cory Yeates
& Gordon Zilles.

County Executive: Craig “W” Buttars

County Clerk/Auditor: Jill N. Zollinger

County Attorney: James Swink

The following individuals were also in attendance: Janeen Allen, Jay Downs, Reed Ernstrom, Jim
Gass, Chief Rod Hammer, Griffin Hazard, Alton Hebdon, Sharon L. Hoth, Bartt F. Nelson, Dave Nielsen,
Chief Jeff Peterson, Tim Rawlings, Director Josh Runhaar, Media: Kelly Cannon (Herald Journal), Jennie
Christensen (KVNU).

OPENING REMARKS AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Council member Gordon Zilles gave the opening remarks and led those present in the Pledge
of Allegiance.

REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF AGENDA

ACTION: Motion by Council member Yeates to approve the agenda with Item 12b —
Penalty and Interest Abatement Requests — deleted. Potter seconded the motion. The vote
was unanimous, 7-0.

REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF MINUTES

ACTION: Motion by Council member Yeates to approve the minutes of the May 26, 2015
Council meeting as corrected. Erickson seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous,
7-0.

REPORT OF THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE: CRAIG “W” BUTTARS

APPOINTMENTS: _ Griffin K. Hazard Cache County Deputy Attorney

ACTION: Motion by Council member Potter to approve the appointment. Yeates
seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 7-0.

At the invitation of Executive Buttars Hazard introduced himself to the Council.

WARRANTS: Warrants for the period 05-30-2015 to 06-05-2015 were given to the Clerk for
filing.

ITEMS OF SPECIAL INTEREST

> Bear River Mental Health (BRMH) Fiscal Year 2016 Area Plan - Reed Ernstrom
reviewed the 2016 Area Plan including mandated services, budget, funding and the Justice

Reinvestment Initiative. The current Mental Health and Drug Courts coalition will be utilized
to track whether the Justice Reinvestment Initiative is effective in reducing recidivism.
Ernstrom stressed the importance of the county funding which qualifies BRMH to receive the
federal match for Medicaid.

-funding

-required licenses

-call volume

-time on scene

-calls by city

-equipment replacement

-new ambulance station in Hyrum

COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS

Jon White told Director Runhaar that the conservation districts have much of the information he
will need for the County Resource Management Planning (HB323).

Greg Merrill has met with the external auditors and the general consensus is that the county
has made significant progress in addressing challenges identified earlier. A final report should
be ready for the Council by the June 23, 2015 Council meeting.

Council member Potter left the meeting at 6:26 p.m.

David Erickson stated visits to the various volunteer Fire Departments are almost completed
and he is impressed with the commitment of those involved. At the Fire District Board meeting it
was reported that all the bids for the new training facility are in and they are working with Logan
City about the location.


Cache County Council
06-09-2015

Executive Buttars reported that building permits for May 2014 were $3.8 million and for May
2015 they are over $16 million. Year-to-date for 2014 was $9.6 million and year-to-date 2015 is
$37 million.

Kathy Robison asked for a discussion on the RU2 and RUS zones in the near future and asked

Director Runhaar how soon that can happen. Runhaar reiterated that a General Plan is urgently
needed to address these zones.

Council member Merrill said he recalled Runhaar seeking funding for the General Plan last year.
Attorney Swink interjected the county needs an update to the current out-of-date general plan.
The current one puts the county at risk and a new one should be a high priority for the Council.
Executive Buttars asked if it is possible to dovetail the Resource Management Plan with the
General Plan. Runhaar replied he would like to do that and said he understands the state will
contribute $50,000.00 per county to help with the Resource Management Plan but there will be
no match from CIB.

A full-time person is needed to work on the General Plan and, if done on a consulting basis,
consultant work needs to be supervised very carefully.

Vice Chair Merrill suggested that Director Runhaar put together something specific for the
General Plan for next year’s budget so the Council will know what it has to come up with.

Clerk/Auditor Zollinger left the meeting at 6:41 p.m.
Runhaar said he will put something together for the first meeting in July.

Cory Yeates asked if the Compensation Committee has a report from their last meeting.
Executive Buttars said he will bring their report to the next Council meeting.

ADJOURNMENT

The Council meeting adjourned at 6:43 p.m.
