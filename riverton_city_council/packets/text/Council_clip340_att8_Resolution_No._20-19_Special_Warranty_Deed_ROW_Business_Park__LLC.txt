                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-19

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY TO ACCEPT A SPECIAL WARRANTY
DEED FROM RIVERTON BUSINESS PARK, LLC FOR RIGHT OF WAY TO
CONSTRUCT OLD LIBERTY WAY FROM MAJESTIC RISE PARKWAY (13200 S) TO
12600 S

   WHEREAS, The City desires to construction Old Liberty Way (the Project) from Majestic
Rise Parkway (13200 S) to 12600 S; and

   WHEREAS, During the design process, it was discovered that there exists a gap in the right
of way for the road that resulted from an error in survey that happened during land transactions
between Jordan School District and the property owners; and

   WHEREAS, The current owner of the property is Riverton Business Park, LLC; and

   WHEREAS, Riverton Business Park LLC desires to have the Project completed in order to
develop their property; and

    WHEREAS, Riverton Business Park LLC has agreed to dedicate the property which equals
1,822 sq. ft. in exchange for water services and access to their property from the Project; and

   WHEREAS, The City of Riverton feels the exchange of services for right of way is fair and
equitable to both parties; and

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Riverton City Council accepts and
executes the Special Warranty Deed from Riverton Business Park, LLC for 1,822 sq. ft. of right
of way in exchange for water service and access to their property from the Project.

    PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 3rd day of
March, 2020 by the following vote:
                                             YES    NO
