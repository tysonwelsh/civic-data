                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-25

  A RESOLUTION APPOINTING VOLUNTEER MEMBER, JEFF EATON, TO THE
             RIVERTON CITY EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS
                AND SPECIAL PROJECTS COMMITTEE


        WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance No. 18-07 and Resolution No.
18-34, the Mayor shall appoint with advice and consent of the City Council, residents of
Riverton City to serve on the Emergency Preparedness and Special Projects Committee; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the following volunteer
appointment of Jeff Eaton;

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the appointee is a
resident of Riverton City and is willing and capable of serving on the Emergency Preparedness
Committee; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the appointment of the Mayor's
nomination to the Emergency Preparedness Committee;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the appointment of Jeff Eaton to
          the Riverton City Emergency Preparedness and Special Projects Committee,
          which term shall end _____________________.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 6th day of
