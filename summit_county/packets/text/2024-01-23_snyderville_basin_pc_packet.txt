RECOMMENDATION:  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the proposal to 
amend Chapter 10-2-10, Use Table in the Snyderville Basin Development Code, conduct a public 
hearing and forward a positive recommendation to the County Council per the findings of fact, 
conclusions of law and conditions of approval in this staff report.   
 
Proposal 
The applicant, Maverick Inc., is requesting an amendment to the Snyderville Basin Development 
Code Chapter 10-2-10: Use Table that would make “ Gasoline Service Station with Convenience 
Store” a conditional use in the Service Commercial (SC) zone. If the amendment is approved, the 
applicant would then return with a CUP application for the use in the SC zone.  
 
Background 
 
The SC zone is a 141-acre area located northeast of Quinn’s Junction off Highway 40. There are 
no other areas within Summit County zoned SC. Access to the zone is from the Highway 40 
frontage road between Highway 248 and Silver Summit. Among other things, there is a 
concrete plant, towing business, gun range, auto repair shop, offices, commercial retail, art 
studio and live work development inside the zone.  
 
The applicant would like to build a Gasoline Service Station with a Convenience Store on a 
parcel on the southern end of the zone near the Quinn’s Junction interchange. To build what is 
desired the applicant will need to make the use permitted in the SC zone.  
7
Code Amendments     
 
Figure 1 SC Zone Area 
The applicant is requesting an amendment to the language in the SC zone. No changes to other 
zones are proposed.  
 
Analysis 
Section 10-7-3 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code states that whenever there is 
initiated an amendment to the Code, it must be reviewed by the Planning Commission who will 
deliver a recommendation to the County Council. The County Council, after holding a public 
hearing, shall approve, approve with modifications or deny the amendment according to the 
following criteria. 
 
Criteria 1: The amendment shall be consistent with the goals, objectives, and policies of the 
general plan. COMPLIES 
 
Analysis: The SC zone is in the Quinn’s Junction neighborhood area of the Snyderville 
Basin General Plan. The Neighborhood Description in that section States: 
 
“The types of uses in this area support a viable economy in Summit County. 
Recognizing this, the continued use of and the expansion of additional service 
commercial and industrial uses should be considered, especially near the existing 
development located in the northeast quadrant of SR-248 and US-40. 
Appropriate uses may include general offices, business parks, manufacturing, 
and other service related and industrial uses.”   
 
The proposed amendment to the SC zone is consistent with the General Plan statement 
that service-related uses should be expanded within the zone.  
 
8
Code Amendments     
Criteria 2: The amendment shall not permit the use of land that is not consistent with the uses 
of properties nearby. COMPLIES 
 
Analysis: Some of the uses that are permitted in the zone are Auto Repair, Service and 
Detailing, Auto Impoundment Yard and Towing Services, Commercial Parking Lots, 
Wholesale Retail Sales, Storage, RV or boat, Storage, Vehicle, Vehicle and equipment 
sales or rentals.  
 
Some of the uses surrounding the proposed use are a storage facility for Deer Valley 
vehicles, a cement processing plant, an impound yard for towed vehicles, and an 
electrical substation.  
 
A service station in this area would be consistent with the other auto-related uses 
nearby. The impacts of the use would be comparable to the impacts of other uses that 
are permitted in the zone. Staff finds that the proposed Service Station with a 
Convenience Store use is consistent with the uses and properties in the SC zone.  
 
Criteria 3: The amendment will not permit suitability of the properties affected by the proposed 
amendment for the uses to which they have been restricted. COMPLIES 
 
Analysis:  The amendment will not permit suitability of the properties affected by the 
proposed amendment to the uses to which they have been restricted.  
 
Criteria 4: The amendment will not permit the removal of the then existing restrictions which 
will unduly affect nearby property. COMPLIES 
 
Analysis: The proposed Code language does not remove any existing restrictions that 
would unduly affect nearby property owners. It would add to the existing list of 
permitted uses.   
 
Criteria 5:  The amendment will not grant special favors or circumstances solely for one 
property owner or developer. COMPLIES 
 
Analysis: Although the use was requested by a single property owner, the use would 
apply to the entire SC zone. Any property owner would be allowed to apply for the use.   
 
Criteria 6: The amendment will promote the public health, safety and welfare better than the 
existing regulations for which the amendment is intended to change. COMPLIES 
 
Analysis: There are no service stations on HWY 248 between Kamas and Park City. This 
means that any resident living along that corridor (including Brown’s Canyon) must drive 
to Kamas or Park City to get gas. Allowing a service station use in the Quinn’s Junction 
9
Code Amendments     
area could reduce vehicle trips into Park City or Kamas by providing an alternative gas 
station for those residents.

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the proposal to amend Chapter 10-2-
10, Use Table in the Snyderville Basin Development Code, conduct a public hearing and forward 
a positive recommendation to the County Council per the findings of fact, conclusions of law
