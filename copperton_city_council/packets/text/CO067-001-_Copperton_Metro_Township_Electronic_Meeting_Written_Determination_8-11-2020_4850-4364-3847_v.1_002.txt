                          Electronic Meeting Written Determination
                                            of the
                                 Copperton Metro Township

Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-207(4), I, Mayor Sean Clayton, have determined that the
Copperton Metro Township Council will hold its regular Council meeting and a public hearing on
the proposed 2020 fee schedule on August 19, 2020 at 6:30, without an anchor location due to the
COVID19 pandemic and the risks it poses to the health and safety. This written determination shall
expire 30 days from today.
Dated this ___th day of August, 2020.
