Agenda Item 3.7 is a request by Gardner & Associates, Inc. for the Planning Commission to forward a recommendation to the City Council to amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by enacting Article 22-11-65 and Appendix ZZ of the Orem City code and by rezoning the property located generally at 1350 East 740 North from the R12 zone to the PD-52 zone (approximately 6 acres). 

Staff Presentation:  In 1984, the City Council granted a conditional use permit for the subject property which allowed the property to be used for hospital services. The original intent was that the property would be used “for psychiatric and addictive disease rehabilitation.” The original conditional use permit limited the hospital use to 80 beds. In 2001, the City Council amended the original conditional use permit to allow a total of 127 beds and also approved the use of a secondary school on the property for the use of juveniles who reside at the facility. The applicant would now like to construct a building addition on the property that would house an additional 24 beds. 

Because a hospital use is no longer a conditional use in the R12 zone, in order to allow the construction of the additional 24-bed building, the property would need to be rezoned to allow this use. The applicant therefore proposes the creation of a new PD-52 zone that is designed to allow the existing hospital use and the additional building addition that the applicant would like to construct on the property. Under the provisions of the proposed PD-52 zone, no other uses would be allowed on the property other than hospital services and secondary school uses. The PD-52 zone would also require that the property be developed and maintained in conformance with the concept plan. Any deviation from the site layout and building elevations shown in the concept plan would have to go through a rezoning process before it would be allowed. 

A rezone of the property to PD-52 would effectively supersede the original conditional use permit and would make hospital services a permitted use instead of a conditional use as it has been since 1984. This would have the effect of eliminating the conditions that were made part of the original 1984 conditional use permit. One of the conditions included as part of the 1984 conditional use permit was that “no person (1) who has criminal charges pending against him or (2) who is involuntarily committed for treatment as a result of conviction for or arising from criminal activity shall be treated or housed at the hospital.” If the subject property is rezoned to the PD-52 zone, this condition will have no further effect. 

The facility operated by the applicant on the property is a secure “lockdown” facility and therefore the applicant believes that these restrictions are no longer necessary. Furthermore, the applicant believes that these original restrictions are impractical as hospitals do not generally check to see if a person has criminal charges pending before admitting them and individuals in need of the services of a psychiatric hospital will sometimes have pending charges arising from a criminal incident such as disorderly conduct or assault. 

If the property is rezoned to PD-52, the applicant will need to obtain site plan approval for the new building addition. In response to comments at the neighborhood meeting, the applicant shifted the location of the addition on the concept plan to be further away from the neighboring residential properties.

Height: The maximum height of any new structure in the PD-52 zone is 15 feet from slab to top of parapet, this will apply to the new addition.  

Setbacks: The addition will be about 80 feet from the south property line. 

Architecture: The façade of the addition will be of similar masonry materials as is found on the current building. 

Parking:  The existing portion of the building was granted a parking reduction of 47% by the Planning Commission on July 11, 2001. The new portion of the building will be required to comply with a parking standard of 1 stall per 2 beds as shown in the proposed text. This standard requires 12 new stalls be provided along with the addition, 19 new stalls are proposed per the concept plan. A minimum total of 139 stalls are required.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a recommendation to the City Council to amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by enacting Article 22-11-65 and Appendix ZZ of the Orem City code and by rezoning the property located generally at 1350 East 740 North from the R12 zone to the PD-52 zone (approximately 6 acres)

Agenda Item 3.8 is a request by Jay Liechty for the Planning Commission to amend the Lakeview Area Street Connection Master Plan to eliminate the street connections between 1430 South/400 West and 360 West/1600 South.

 Staff Presentation:  The applicant is proposing to eliminate two street connections between 1430 South and 1600 South which are in the Lakeview Area Street Connection Master Plan. One connection would provide connectivity between 1430 South and 400 West. The second connection to be eliminated is between 360 West and 1600 South.

The street connections need to be in place to prevent parcels in the area from becoming landlocked. The identified street connections also complete a grid network to mitigate intersection congestion at Sandhill Road and Main Street. The street connections enhance emergency response time and opportunities for water and sewer utility connections. Currently there are 10 separate parcels that are on a septic tank system that could connect into a City sewer system with the opportunity of a street connection. With the elimination of street connections, traffic isn’t evenly distributed on the street network resulting in reduced levels of service and the need for signalized intersections.  Staff supports street connections and the completion of a grid network.

If this elimination of required street connections is approved, the applicant could proceed with the proposed concept plan, however, if not approved the street connection from 1430 South and 400 West would be required for street connectivity as described above.
