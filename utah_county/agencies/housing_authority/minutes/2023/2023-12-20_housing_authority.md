A. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2023-12-01 Minutes of Regular Board Meeting held 11-15-2023 
April made a motion to approve minutes from regular board meeting. Suzanne 
seconded the request. Motion passed unanimously. 
 
B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2023-12-02 Approve Employee Benefits 
April made a motion to approve the employee benefits. Suzanne seconded the motion. 
Motion passed unanimously. 
HAUC requested the benefits broker seek bid for ongoing medical, dental and vision benefits. 
After researching is appears that staying with PEHP medical and also switching to PEHP 
dental is in the best interest for both HAUC and the employee. Switching the dental coverage 
at this time will provide more coverage for employees and is a lower rate for HAUC. It is the 
staff recommendation that PEHP be the full service medical and dental provider for HAUC.  
Vision benefits will remain the same. 
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2023-12-03 Approve 2023 Revised Budgets (All Programs) 
Dave made a motion to approve 2023 Revised Budgets (All Programs). April seconded 
the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
Revised budgets were presented to the board for the 2023 calendar year. Administrative 
expenses changed due to the approved allocation plan. This allows all programs to cover their 
share of actual expenses. Revised budgets include the additional amounts earned for interest 
in all of the PTIF accounts. It was noted that the Section 8 accounts were a bit lower in 
balances due to the restricted nature of the funds. Office rent was adjusted in all programs due 
to GASB 87 requirements. Over all every program is performing and the NOI is positive.  
 
D. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2023-12-04 Approve 2024 Proposed Budgets (All Programs) 
April made a motion to approve the 2024 Proposed Budgets (All Programs). Suzanne 
seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
The 2024 proposed budgets are presented using the 2023 revised budgets. In the 2024 
budgets adjustments were made for a 6% utility increase and a 10% employee cost increase. 
It was noted the CoC program no longer has the rent revenue and the 2024 budget reflects 
this change. Section 8 has an increase in HAP dollars which is reflected in the budget. 
Management fees are higher in business activities due to the increase rent revenue. 
Bridgeway 2024 budget was presented for the first time. There was not a revised budge t from 
2023 the 2024 budget was and they are not part of the approved allocation.  
 
E. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2023-12-05 Approve Owned Housing and HCV Write Offs 
Dave made a motion to approve the Owned Housing and HCV Write offs. Suzanne 
seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously.  
Each year through normal business operations of the Housing Choice Voucher and Owned 
Housing programs, uncollectable accounts are created. Generally, this is due to incorrect 
income reported, unpaid rent, damages upon move out. The amounts represent funds owing 
either because a client entered into a repayment agreement and stopped making payments or 
never made an attempt to make payments toward the balance owed. The uncollectable 
accounts are presented to the board to be written off. These accounts have been or will be 
turned over to a collection agency for further collection activity. 
 
F. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2023-12-06 Approve 2023 Inventory Disposal List 
April made a motion to approve the 2023 Inventory Disposal List. Dave seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously.   
Each year assets, electronic equipment and other items necessary for daily operations 
become broken, outdated or otherwise no longer used in operations. Items from 2023 are 
being presented for removal from inventory. The equipment is donated to charity when 
feasible, however broken or unsafe items are discarded.
