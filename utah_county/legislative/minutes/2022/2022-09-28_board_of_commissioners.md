GOING TO INTRODUCE YOU TODAY FITS THE MOLD OF US CONTINUING THE

FOR OUR REGION AND LOCALLY AS SPANISH FORK CITY.

DAVE ANDERSON, SPANISH FORK ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT DIRECTOR - I'VE
HAD THE UNIQUE OPPORTUNITY TO WORK WITH A NUMBER OF DIFFERENT
DEVELOPERS FOR WELL OVER 15 YEARS TO TRY TO FIGURE OUT HOW TO DO
SOMETHING SIGNIFICANT IN A PART OF OUR COMMUNITY. WE ARE HERE IN THE
HOPE THAT YOU WILL HELP US UNDERSTAND HOW WE CAN WORK WITH THE
COUNTY TO PROCEED TO POTENTIALLY CREATE A NEW REDEVELOPMENT AREA

COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AREA WITH A CRA. THE MARKET FOR WHAT THEY
WOULD LIKE TO DO IN OUR COMMUNITY IS STRONG. IN SPITE OF HAVING

FRUITION FOR 10-20 YEARS, BUT WE THINK IT’S VERY PRUDENT AND ESSENTIAL

THAT WOULD LIKE TO START CONSTRUCTION ON REALLY SIGNIFICANT
FACILITIES UP TO HUNDREDS AND HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS OF SQUARE FEET
OF NON-RESIDENTIAL SPACE IN AREAS WHERE WE DON’T HAVE ROADS AND
NEED LARGE QUANTITIES OF POWER.
