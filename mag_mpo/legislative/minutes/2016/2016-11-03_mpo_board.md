Public Comment No comments Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Modification - Bob Allen UDOT Region 3 and American Fork City have requested to transfer approximately $6.8 million from SR- 74: Main Street to 740 North American Fork to US-89/State Street: 220 South Pleasant Grove to 500 East American Fork. American Fork City decided to wait on the widening of SR-74 and have the money transferred to the choke point project on US-89. The original scope of on US-89/State Street did not include improvements at the 500 East intersection. With the additional funds it gives the project enough funding to do recommended improvements. Mayor Mike Daniels moved that the Transportation Improvement Program be modified to transfer 6.8 million from SR-74 project to the US-89/State Street project as requested by UDOT Region 3 and American Fork. Commissioner Greg Graves seconded the motion and the motion carried unanimously. Local Corridor Preservation Fee and Program Follow up - Jim Price The Corridor Preservation list was approved December 2015. Since 2007 23 property have been purchased and 14 of those have been used on I-15 CORE Project, North County Blvd, and Pioneer Crossing. Six property in Highland have been held for Murdock Connector Road, Provo has one property to widen the airport road, Springville has properties held for a future interchange, UDOT property on 1200 West Orem, and Eagle Mountain is in negotiation for property on SR-73. Contact Jim for more details.
