Capitalization of Projects
Mayor Smolka stated Cathy Jensen, Financial Officer, Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District (GSLMSD), recommended adopting an asset capitalization policy. The
policy would set an asset value of $5,000 from which point certain assets may be considered for
capitalization.
Council Member Paine, seconded by Council Member Brems, move to adopt the
capitalization policy. The motion passed unanimously.

Council Member Reports
Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)
Council Member Brems stated there has been a lot of arguing back and forth on
how to split assets with Herriman and Riverton. One of the big issues is the retirement fund. A
public hearing will be held to receive public input on police officers carrying body cameras. UPD
has enough cameras to equip about a third of the officers. Data storage and cameras is the
biggest expense.
