City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1682

On November 18, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1682 amending
titles 7 and 10 of the draper city municipal code regarding the requirements for fire
sprinklers under the draper city fire and construction codes. The complete ordinance
is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at
https://www.draperutah.gov/
