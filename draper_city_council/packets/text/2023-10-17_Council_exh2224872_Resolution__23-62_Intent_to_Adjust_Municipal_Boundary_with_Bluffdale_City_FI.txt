                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-62

A RESOLUTION INDICATING THE INTENT OF DRAPER CITY TO ADJUST THE
   COMMON BOUNDARY BETWEEN DRAPER CITY AND BLUFFDALE CITY
      AFFECTING PARCEL NUMBER 33-12-100-017-0000 WHICH IS
APPROXIMATELY 0.889 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED WITHIN IN
                          DRAPER CITY.


     WHEREAS, The City Council hereby determines that it is interested in reviewing a
 municipal boundary adjustment with Bluffdale City; and

       WHEREAS, the boundary adjustment would adjust the common boundary between
 Draper City and Bluffdale City as depicted in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated
 herein by this reference; and

      WHEREAS, the boundary adjustment would relocate approximately 0.889 acres
 (“0.889 Acres”) of property from Draper City into Bluffdale City; and

     WHEREAS, it is more efficient and economical for Bluffdale City to provide
 municipal services to the 0.889 Acres once they are developed; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City intends to hold a public hearing to review the boundary
 adjustment no less than 60 days after adopting this Resolution; and

      WHEREAS, this Resolution complies with Utah Code Ann. §10-2-419.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL,
STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Intent to Adjust a Common Boundary. The City of Draper approves this
Resolution 23-62 indicating the intent to adjust the common municipal boundary with
Bluffdale City as provided in Exhibit A, otherwise known as the Draper City – Bluffdale
City Common Boundary Adjustment.

        Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of the Resolution and all provisions, clauses, and words of this Resolution shall be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 17th DAY OF OCTOBER, 2023.
