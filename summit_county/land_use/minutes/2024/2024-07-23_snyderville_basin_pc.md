Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for upgrades to the aboveground utility facilities (power poles) within the Rocky Mountain Power utility easements in the greater Summit Park area. Attachment:
Staff Report
County Planner, Jennifer Leslie, presented a Conditional Use Permit proposal for upgrades to the aboveground Rocky Mountain Power utility facilities for public hearing and action.
Richard Bardauskas, from Rocky Mountain Power, introduced himself to the Commission.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:06 p.m.
Steve Horvat was called to speak.
Betsy Nazare was called to speak.
Dave Birkbeck was called to speak.
Mike Quinones was called to speak.
Chris Price was called to speak.
Westerfield Wendelin was called to speak.
Jim Pelletier was called to speak.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:25 p.m.
Commission Chair Conabee reiterated issues and questions raised during the public hearing. Planner Leslie and Mr. Bardauskas, responded to those questions and concerns.
Commissioners asked additional questions and provided comments regarding the proposed project to which Mr. Bardauskas and Attorney Viti responded.
Tyann Mooney made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the Community Development Director for the proposed Conditional Use Permit for the Rocky Mountain Power distribution upgrades in the Summit Park and Timberline area based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report as well as an additional requirement to hold an open house with notice given to the public of anyone who is affected by the new poles. Thomas Cooke seconded.
Discussion was held on the motion.
Community Development Director, Pat Putt addressed the Commission and suggested as part of the Engineering Construction Mitigation Plan, the applicant would commit to doing a minimum of the following: Create a Q&A fact sheet to include a map, description of the work to be done, revegetation standard, construction timeframe, and contact information for someone at Rocky Mountain Power.
Commissioner Mooney amended her motion.
Tyann Mooney made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit for the Rocky Mountain Power distribution upgrades in Summit Park and Timberline area based on the finds of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval as found in the staff report. In addition, as part of the construction mitigation permit, the applicant will commit to do a minimum to create and distribute a Q&A fact sheet including a map, description of the scope of work, revegetation standards, timeframes, and contact information for the contractor and project supervisor and county staff will add it to the county website the residents to easily access. Thomas Cooke seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Bruce Carmichael.
