                                                                          Public Works Department



David Brickey, City Manager

Dear David,

I respectfully request the opportunity to extend the 2020 Overlay Project by change order to finish 3600 W from
13050 S to 12600 S. This project extension is budgeted in the 2020/2021 budget in Fund 21 under the
Maintenance – Asphalt line. This extension will require an approval of a change order and budget amendment
from the City Council. The budget amendment would transfer money from the current Fund 21 fund balance
which would result in a reduction of the 2020/2021 budget allocation to match the transfer. The amount of the
change order and budget amendment request is $230,205.

Currently we are under contract with Black Forest Paving to reconstruct and overlay 4000 W from 12600 S to
11800 S and 3600 W from 13400 S to 13050 S. The total contract cost for the project is $550,073. Due to budget
constraints, we were only able to construct the south portion of 3600 W which is included in the current year
budget with the plan of completing through a new contract, the northern portion of the road with the 2020/2021
budget.

After the award of the bid to Black Forest Paving, we learned that UDOT will be placing the 6 month closure of
12600 S earlier than expected as part of the Bangerter 3 Project. This shut down will occur at the end of January
2021 and will detour all west bound traffic to 3600 W. We feel that the current condition of 3600 W from 12600 S
to 13050 S cannot handle the projected traffic of the detour without a structural overlay. With this in mind we
looked for opportunities to complete the project before the shutdown of 12600 S.

Traditionally June, July, and August are not the best times to bid projects. Contractors have already scheduled
their summer and fall projects and bids are at a premium price. Having said that, we received an exceptional bid
from Black Forest Paving with our current project and asked them if they would be willing hold their prices and add
this portion of 3600 W to their current project. They agreed to do so and submitted a proposal to complete the
work. We feel that the proposal to extend the existing project will save the City tens of thousands of dollars over
rebidding the project. This work would be completed before July of 2020 and would not interfere with the heavy
school traffic generated by Rose Creek Elementary when school goes back into session sometime in the fall.

I appreciate your willingness to consider this request and I hope this helps you understand the reasoning of our
desire to complete this project. Please let me know if you have any questions or need additional information.

Sincerely,



G. Trace Robinson
PW Director/City Engineer
