                       COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

                                  RESOLUTION #2020-11-05

                                      November 18, 2020

         A RESOLUTION DESIGNATING TESSA STITZER AS THE DEPUTY MAYOR OF
                       THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP

         WHEREAS, Councilmember Apollo Pazell resigned from the Copperton Metro
   Township Council on October 20, 2020 (the “Resignation”); and

          WHEREAS, Councilmember Pazell served as Copperton’s Deputy Mayor; and

          WHEREAS, because of the Resignation, the Council desires to elect a new Deputy
    Mayor to serve out the remainder of Councilmember Pazell’s term.

         NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE COPPERTON
   METRO TOWNSHIP EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY that Tessa Stitzer will serve as Deputy
   Mayor for the remainder of Councilmember’s Pazell’s terms, which expires on December 31,
   2021.

                                               COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
