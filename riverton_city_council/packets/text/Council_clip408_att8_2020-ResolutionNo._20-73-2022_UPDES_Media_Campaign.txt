                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-73

           A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE EXECUTION OF THE FIRST
          AMENDMENT TO THE NTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT
       BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR UPDES MEDIA
                        CAMPAIGN COST SHARING

       WHEREAS, the City is obligated to the Jordan Valley Municipalities Utah Pollutant
Discharge Elimination Systems (UPDES) Permit; and

       WHEREAS, the Jordan Valley Municipalities UPDES Permit requires Riverton City to
implement public education and outreach programs for residents, institutions and businesses for
the purpose of preventing and reducing pollution affecting the Jordan River and its tributaries;
and

       WHEREAS, under the UPDES Permit, Riverton City is required to develop multimedia
outreach programs for its citizens; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City is a member of the Salt County Stormwater Coalition, which
creates and implements multimedia outreach programs; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County administers contracts for consultants and contractors
needed to facilitate functioning multimedia outreach programs; and

      WHEREAS, Salt Lake County requested Riverton City renew the Interlocal Cooperation
Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake County for cost sharing of the UPDES Media
Campaign; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has provided Riverton a document to execute titled The
First Amendment to the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake
County for UPDES Media Campaign Cost Sharing; and

        WHEREAS, said document defines the services rendered and required costs to
participate in the multimedia program; and

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

        1.      The First Amendment to the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Riverton
City and Salt Lake County of the UPDES Media Campaign Cost Sharing is hereby approved,
and the Mayor is hereby authorized to sign the Agreement. Prior to signing the agreement, the
City Attorney is authorized to make any minor revisions deemed necessary to accomplish the
intent of the agreement but do not result in a material alteration to the terms of the Agreement.
        2.      The First Amendment to the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement is made effective
on the date the Agreement is signed by the Mayor of Riverton City and Salt Lake County Mayor.
