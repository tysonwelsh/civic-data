COMMENCE 
 
The Housing Connect Special Board of Commissioners Meeting was held on Thursday, March 28, 2024, and commenced at 2:17 p.m.  Chair Spencer 
Moffat facilitated and led the meeting. 
 
1. GUESTS/PUBLIC COMMENT 
 
There were no guests or members of the public in attendance. 
 
2. CLOSED MEETING 
 
At 2:17 p.m., Commissioner Nguyen motioned for the Board to enter Closed Meeting to discuss real estate matters, and Commissi oner 
Johnson seconded the motion, and a ll Board members present ( Chair Moffat, Vice Chair Bernal and Commissioners Johnson, Litvack, 
Nguyen, and White) voted in support, and the Board entered Closed Meeting. 
 
At 2:46 p.m., Commissioner Johnson made a motion for the Board to exit closed session and to return to regular (open) session , with 
Commissioner Nguyen seconding the motion.  All Board members present ( Chair Moffat, Vice Chair Bernal and Commissioners Johnson, 
Litvack, Nguyen, and White) voted favorably, and the Board returned to regular session. 
 
3. ACTION ITEMS 
 
The following action items were voted on by the Board: 
 
- Resolution #1102 – Approval of the development budget change for New City Plaza:  Commissioner Johnson proposed a motion to approve 
the resolution.  Commissioner White seconded the motion, with all Board members present ( Chair Moffat, Vice Chair Bernal, and 
Commissioners Johnson, Litvack, Nguyen, and White) voting in support.  The motion passed, and Resolution #1102 was approved. 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
- Resolution #1103 – Reserve funds allocation:  Housing Connect will use funds from the following sources:  
 
- Unrestricted cash:  $2,500,000 
- Restricted cash – Public Housing proceeds from Sale/Transfer of Units:  $757, 259 
- Restricted cash:  Public Housing Operating resereve:  $2,447,741 
- Funding awarded – Salt Lake County ARPA funds:  $1,400,000 
- Funding awarded – Salt Lake City Corp. RDA funds:  $895,000 
 
TOTAL:  $8,000,000 
 
Commissioner Johnson motioned that the Board approve the reserve funds allocation, and Commissioner Mguyen seconded the motio n, 
and all Board members present ( Chair Moffat,  and Commissioners Johnson, Litvack, Nguyen, and White) voted in support.  The motion 
passed, and Resolution #1103 was approved. 
 
4. OTHER 
 
CEO Kimball said that she would be meeting with the architect to tighten up some of the turnaround and to ensure that the revised drawings 
are provided in the next couple of weeks. 
 
Chair Moffat expressed his thanks to CEO Kimball and noted that she is wearing multiple hats while running the agency.  He ex pressed his 
feelings that the next month or two would be critical for the project.  He remarked that getting some of the issues back on track and pushing 
some of the things through or get them moving again will have a big impact on the project’s success.  He requested that CEO Kimball inform 
the Board of how they can be of support and said that the Commissioners would be happy to help out. 
 
He gave credit to the agency for being in a position to have reserves, adding that it is a testament to where the agency is, as a whole. 
 
Commissioner Litvack asked CEO Kimball to inform the Board on the approval dates, saying that she would like to know once CEO  Kimball 
hears. 
 
ADJOURN 
 
At 2:53 p.m., Chair Moffat adjourned the meeting without objection. 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Dan Pincock
