                                Wreaths Across America Day

WHEREAS, Wreaths Across America is a non-profit organization that began in 2006 as an
extension of the Arlington National Cemetery Wreath Project; and

WHEREAS, The project originally began in 1992, when wreaths donated by the Worcester
Wreath Company were placed on the headstones of our nations fallen heroes during the
holidays; and

WHEREAS, Riverton’s Historic Preservation Commission, the Daughters of the American
Revolution, and the American Legion have partnered with Wreaths Across America on their
mission to remember the fallen, honor those who have served, and teach children the value of
freedom; and

WHEREAS, To date, 338 brave Veterans have found their final resting place in the Riverton City
Cemetery and we must always remember the sacrifices they made in order to protect the
freedoms we hold so dear; and

WHEREAS, On December 18, 2021, a Wreaths Across America ceremony will be held at the
Riverton City Cemetery as a tribute to our Riverton Veterans; and

NOW, THEREFORE, I Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton, Utah, do hereby proclaim December 18,
2021, as Wreaths Across America Day in Riverton and ask the residents of our community to
support this special project and take a moment this holiday season to remember our fallen
heroes.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 9th day of December 2021.
