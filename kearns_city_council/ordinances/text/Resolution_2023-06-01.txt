RESOLUTION NO. _________________                  DATE _June 12th, 2023___

 A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL COMMITTING
   TO FUTURE IMPACT FEE WAIVERS FOR MODERATE INCOME HOUSING.

                                          RECITALS

WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has the authority to pass all ordinances
and rules, and make all regulations, not repugnant to law, necessary for carrying into effect or
discharging all powers and duties pursuant to Utah Code § 10-8-84; and

WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township, as a municipality, has the authority to leverage fees on
development, including impact fees, pursuant to the Impact Fees Act, Utah Code § 11-36a-101, et
seq. (the “Act”); and

WHERAS, Kearns Metro Township is currently undergoing a fee audit, which may lead to
changes in permitting fee structures and the eventual adoption of impact fees on new development;
and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council timely approved amendments to its Moderate
Income Housing Plan (“MIH Plan”), in compliance with House Bill 462 (2022); and

WHEREAS, the adopted MIH Plan selects Strategy L from Utah Code § 10-9a-403, which reads,
“Reduce, waive, or eliminate impact fees related to moderate income housing”; and

WHEREAS, the preservation and construction of Moderate Income Housing is a priority to
Kearns Metro Township, and the municipality is committed to implementing the recommendations
of its adopted MIH Plan, which requires that Kearns Metro Township proactively commits to
waiving future impact fees on moderate income housing; and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council finds that the benefits of subsidizing future
moderate income housing outweigh any potential costs to the municipality’s general fund and is
in the best interest of the community.

                                        RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL, effective immediately:

   1. The Kearns Metro Township commits to waiving all future impact fees on development
      projects where at least 15% of residential dwelling units are made affordable to moderate-
      income households (those earning 50% to 80% of AMI) and are held affordable for a period
      not less than twenty years from the date of occupancy (the “Commitment”).
   2. The Kearns Metro Township directs its staff and agents to honor the Commitment when
      preparing impact fee facilities plans, analyses and certifications, calculations, enactments,
      and taking other steps needed to impose an impact fee under the Act.

   3. This ordinance shall go into effect upon its posting pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-711.

PASSED and ADOPTED this 12th day of June, 2023.
