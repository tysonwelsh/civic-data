                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 22-79

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
WITH NEWMAN CONSTRUCTION FOR THE COMPLETION OF THE SECONDARY
                 WATER METER PROJECT, PHASE 2

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to proceed with the phase 2 of the
Secondary Meter Project; and
     WHEREAS, by approving this resolution, Council will need to approve a future budget
amendment for this project.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an Agreement with Newman
            Construction for the completion of the Secondary Meter Project, Phase 2.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 13th day of
December 2022 by the following vote:
