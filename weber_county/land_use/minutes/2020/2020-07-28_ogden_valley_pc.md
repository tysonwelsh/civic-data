                  1. CUP 2020-09 Request for approval of a conditional use permit for expansion of restaurant services
                     and installation of equipment rental services on the site of the Alaskan Inn, Ogden Canyon Rd,
                     Ogden, UT.

                               Ms. Aydelotte explained the CUP in the Staff Report to the Planning Commissioners.

                               Commissioner Howell asked if they could use the grassy area in the front of the Alaskan Inn.
                               Staff stated no because on the approval that the received in 1997 excluded that area.

                               The applicant, Roy Gabbay said that they would screen the trash, they would have enough
                               parking and a sidewalk.

                               Chair Lewis asked where would you store jet skis? Mr. Gabbay said that they would move
                               the Propane tank and but up screening between the sheds.

                             Commissioner Francis wanted to see designated storage, actual loading area not in the
                             roadway. She also asked if the propane tanks were currently ready to be moved?

                             Mr. Gabby stated that when we move the tanks that will be the storage for the
                             Paddleboards. There is plenty of parking at the anglers parking, which some of that is our
                             property.

                             Maura Hatch, Travis, Jessi, Jay Heinz and Parker Litchfield. All of these residents had the
                             same concerns about jet skis, paddleboards, parking issues, storage of gas for the jet skis.

                             Mr. Burton said that if the applicant can’t meet all the conditions in the staff report and the
                             findings the County could revoke the Conditional Use Permit.

                             Director Grover stated that the applicant has to satisfy all conditions.

                             Commissioner Francis made a motion to approve CUP 2020-09 Request for approval of a
                             conditional use permit for expansion of restaurant services and installation of equipment
                             rental services on the site of the Alaskan Inn, Ogden Canyon Rd, Ogden, UT subject to staff
                             recommendations and findings. Also that there will be no parking in the right of way and no
                             blocking of the right of way when loading recreational equipment and no fueling of vehicles
                             on site.
                             Commissioner Hogge seconded the motion. Chair Lewis, Commissioners Howell, Hogge, Vice
                             Chair Francis, Burton, Lackey all voted aye, motion carried.
