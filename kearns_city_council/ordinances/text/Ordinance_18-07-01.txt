AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 19, ENTITLED "ZONING", OF THE

KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP CODE OF ORDINANCES, 2018, BY

RECLASSIFYING CERTAIN PROPERTY LOCATED IN KEARNS METRO

TOWNSHIP FROM THE R-1-43 (SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL) ZONE TO THE

R-1-5 (SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL) ZONE.

The Kearns Metro Township legislative body of Kearns Metro Township, State of Utah, ordains
as follows:

Section 1: Section, 19.06.020, Zoning Maps of Kearns Metro Township Code of Ordinances
2018, is hereby amended, as follows:

The property described in Application #30647 filed by Randy Moore located at 5944 South
Cougar Lane within the Kearns Metro Township (the “Property”), is hereby reclassified from the R-1-
43 (Single-Family Residential) zone to the R-1-5 (Single-Family Residential) zone.

The Property is more particularly described as follows:
PARCEL # 20-13-426-069-0000
BEG S 0°32' E 981.74 FT & N 89°57'41" W 33 FT FR E 1/4 COR SEC 13, T2S, R2W, SLM; N
89°57'41" W 924.30 FT; N 0°01'16" E 338.25 FT; S 89°57'41" E 699.63 FT; S 02°13'50" E 288.46 FT;
S 89°57'41" E 212.86 FT; S 0°32' E 50.02 FT TO BEG. 5.73 ACM ORL.

Section 2: The map showing such change shall be filed with the Kearns Metro Township
Planning Commission in accordance with Section 19.06.020 of the Kearns Metro Township, Code of
Ordinances, 2017.

Section 3: This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon at least
one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in Salt Lake County, and if
not so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall take effect immediately upon its first publication.
