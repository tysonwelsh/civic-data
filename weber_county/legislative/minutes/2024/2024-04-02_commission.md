D.        THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: Something that's been on my mind for quite a few weeks
now that I wanted to talk to you just basically share with you that I thought was really kind of insightful within our
little family unit. My son years ago met a Brazilian gal and they were married and six years ago she started the
process and of becoming a US citizen. I thought I'd ask you some of the questions in they have in the naturalization
test. She took the oath called the Oath of Allegiance during the naturalization ceremony last Thursday at the Union
Station. They had 126 people from 38 different countries that have been preparing for years. After they took their
Oath of Allegiance, a couple of them got up and talked about their stories. Some of the ones that really got to me
were the ones who came from different places where they never knew what it was to have freedom. They were under
oppression, they were under tyranny or a dictator. They had no freedoms and for whatever reason and however the
process was they came here and some at the peril of different family members. I remember years ago teaching a
family and the father had left with his eight children and his oldest son. As they were leaving the shores of Laos to
swim across the river, his oldest son was shot and there was bullet fire all around him just because they wanted to
leave to get here. So my daughter-in-law had to take a test and some of the questions I thought were interesting; I
thought how many of us could answer these questions today. Let me read you a couple of them I thought were really
good.
       Why did the colonists fight the British from the 13 original colonies?
              o High taxes,
              o The British army stayed in their houses,
              o The colonies didn't have any self-government
       We all know who wrote the Declaration of Independence but they asked them to name some of the people on
          the Declaration of Independence and when it was signed
       Name some of the original 13 colonies
              o Are there some of you that can't name one let alone three or five as the test would ask?
       What happened at the Constitutional Convention?
              o The answer is at the Constitutional Convention the Constitution was written by the founding fathers
       When was the Constitution written?
              o 1787
       The Federalist Papers supported the passage of the US Constitution. Name one of the writers.
              o James Madison, Alexander Hamilton, and John Jay under the pen name of Publius.
       What is one thing Benjamin Franklin is famous for?
              o He was a US Diplomat
              o He was the oldest member of the Constitutional Convention
              o He was the first Postmaster General in the United States
              o He was the writer of Poor Richard's Almanac
              o He started the first free libraries

I thought this is great stuff and it goes on and on. Of course they talk about who is our Congressman, who's our
Senator, name one of the two longest rivers in the United States, name one of the four states that border Mexico,
name one state that borders Canada, name a US Territory (Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands, American Samoa,
Northern Mariana Islands, and Guam). There were over a hundred questions in this book and she had to answer with
certain accuracy in order to pass that test. So, to Iris Marcia Firmo Dos Santos Harvey, my daughter-in-law, I want
you to know how proud we are of you for investing in yourself, for doing things the right and legal way, and for
pouring your heart and soul into that accomplishment. I appreciate you allowing me to have that heartwarming
experience and seeing others from so many different countries do the same.
