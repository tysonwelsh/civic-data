Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order
    Commission Vice Chair Jackson welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2) Discussion Item

    2.1) Discussion of first book reading “A Time to Build”
        The Commissioners and Staff discussed the assigned reading from the book "A Time to
        Build". The Commissioners discussed how to manage expectations, community education
        and trust, what accomplishments are at hand, life cycle housing and mixed use
        neighborhoods, and forward thinking ideas.

        The assignment for next Work Session will be chapters three and four.

                Commission Chair Nielsen and Commissioner Everett joined the meeting
                during discussion item 2.1

    2.2) Discussion of full Micron Area Plan
        Scott Bishop with DR Horton gave a presentation of the full Micron Area Plan update. He
        stated they would be referring to it as the "Sky Area Plan" moving forward. He provided
        an update based on feedback from the Commissioners comments and welcomed any
        comments.

    2.3) Discussion of Sign Ordinance
        Brittney Harris introduced information on the sign ordinance and asked for direction on
        the amendment with the height on the street frontage, pylon signs, and wall signs. Jacob
        Curtis stated every city has their own code as far as sign ordinances. Commissioner Ellis
        stated repetitive signs weren't necessary with multiple signage. Staff was looking for
        direction on what the Commission would like to see with code in regard to signage.
        Commissioner Jackson asked if there was proof that more signage was bringing in more
        services. Commissioner Bunker agreed with Commissioner Jackson and asked if there
        were any studies done that were impacting service and business. Commissioner Newall
        was curious if there was code regarding viewable signage from the inside. Commissioner
        Nielsen stated he would like to know more about code in regard to lighting that was
        visible from the inside.

3) Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Ellis moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:35 p.m.
