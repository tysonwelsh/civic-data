       AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 19, ENTITLED "ZONING", OF THE
       KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP CODE OF ORDINANCES,              2017, BY
       RECLASSIFYING CERTAIN PROPERTY LOCATED IN KEARNS METRO
       TOWNSHIP FROM THE R-1-43 (SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL) ZONE TO THE
       R-1-6 (RESIDENTIAL SINGLE-FAMILY) ZONE.

        The Kearns Metro Township legislative body of Kearns Metro Township, State of Utah, ordains
as follows:

       Section 1: Section, 19.06.020, Zoning Maps of Kearns Metro Township Code of Ordinances

2017, is hereby amended, as follows:

       The property described in Application #30551 filed by Patti Hansen located at 5928 South Park

Wood Drive within the Kearns Metro Township (the "Property"), is hereby reclassified from the R-1-43

(Single-Family Residential) zone to the R-1-6 (Single-Family Residential) zone.
