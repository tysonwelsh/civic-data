BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 2.81 acres located on the west side of 700 E., at approximately 11630 S. 700
E. (Exhibits B and C). The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat Amendment be
approved to allow for the consolidation of a Parcel and an existing Subdivision Lot into a
single Lot.

The subject property is comprised of lot 82 of the Wyngate Commons Plat A and an
adjacent parcel to the south. The Rock Church was constructed on the subject property in
2014. The owner would like to add an accessory structure for storage on the parcel, but as
the principal church structure is located on the Lot to the north, consolidation of the
properties into a single lot is needed so that both principal and accessory structures are
located on the same lot.

The property has been assigned the RA1, RM2, and CN zoning classifications (Exhibit E),
supporting approximately one dwelling unit per acre in the RA1 zone, and twelve (12)
dwelling units per acre in the RM2 zone. According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)
Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA1 zone is to “foster low density development with little
impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the
city's semirural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life,
including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these
zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by
commercial and industrial uses.” The purpose of the RM2 zone is to “permit well designed
apartments, townhomes, twin homes, and condominiums at relatively high densities that are
appropriately buffered from and compatible with surrounding land uses.“ The purpose of the
CN zone is to “provide areas where convenience buying outlets, having small trade areas, may
be established to serve surrounding residential neighborhoods. This zone is intended to promote
a combination of retail and service facilities which, in character and scale, meet day to day
needs of nearby residents.“ Typical development standards pertaining to subdivisions in the

Criteria For Approval.
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

    The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing an
    amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds that there
    is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-way, or
    easement has been vacated or amended.
