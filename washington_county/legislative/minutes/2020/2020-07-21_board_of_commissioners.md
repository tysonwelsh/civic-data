PRESENTATION: RECOGNITION OF K9 VINNIE'S RETIREMENT

Commissioner Almquist recognized K9 Vinnie for his six years of service in the Patrol
Division of the Washington County Sheriff's Office. K9s are a valuable tool for law

enforcement to utilize in tracking, narcotics detection, and criminal apprehensions. K9
Vinnie has been deployed over six hundred times in the field. He has been instrumental in

been involved in tracking and apprehending twenty-three wanted fugitives.

Solid Waste Special Service District No. 1 Attorney Fay Reber explained that the current
ten (10) year contract with Republic Waste is ending, which will subsequently end the ten
(10) year contract with Washington County for billing and collecting. A third contract for
recycling originally entered into with Dixie Waste and assigned to Republic Waste will

also be ending.

It is recommended that it is in the best interest to extend the contracts with Republic Waste
for an additional ten (10) years as the rates are the most competitive in the State and as well
as the extensive experience and knowledge that Republic Waste has in regards to collecting
waste within Washington County. Each municipality within the District will have the
opportunity to approve the contract renewal and enter into their own billing and collections
agreement with the District.

The recycling agreement will be included with the waste collection agreement to provide
the opportunity to citizens who desire to recycle. The new contract will not allow for
Washington County Commission

manual pickups as has been allowed previously. Manual pickups include any garbage not
contained into the provided waste bins.

Commissioner Iverson mentioned that it would be more beneficial for recycling to be
optional for citizens as this would likely reduce the amount of contamination from
improperly recycled material. Additionally, it would provide citizens the opportunity not
to pay the additional recycling fee. However, he encouraged those that desire to recycle to
continue to do so.

Mr. Reber clarified that those who wish to opt out of the recycling program have the option
to do so, and those that have already opted out from the original contract will remain opted

out.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Hurricane Valley Fire Chief Tom Kuhlman cautioned citizens regarding the use of
fireworks for the upcoming holiday and urged that fireworks only be used where they are
designated. The largest fire within the County in 2020 was likely caused by a firework. It

06/17/2020 @ 1030, Injured Hiker — Aspiration Trail - Man Hours — 12

SAR responded to a 38 year old female that broke her ankle while hiking. SAR and St.
George Fire hiked in. The female was located and her ankle stabilized. She was taken
out on a UTV. She refused transport by ambulance.

06/17/2020 @ 1730, Missing Person — St George - Man Hours — 56

SAR responded to assist SGPD on a 60 year old missing man. He had some medical issues
and hadn't been seen for several hours. SAR searched up Turkey Farm Road Area and
flew 2 drones in areas he is known to frequent. He was located by Washington City PD on
the Hell Hole Trail.

06/20/2020 @ 1915, Over Due, Tubing, Virgin River — Man Hours — 22

SAR was called to search for 4 over due people tubing the Virgin River. Two 29 year olds,
a 9 year old and 6 year old. Deputies and SAR searched the Confluence, Babylon, and
Grandpa's Pond Areas. The subject were located just east of Babylon. They were picked
up by deputies and reunited with their family.
06/22/2020 @ 1930 — Injured Hiker — Mill Canyon Trail, Pine Valley - Man Hours — 78

SAR responded to a hiker that had been bitten by a Rattle Snake. The 29 year old male
had gotten off trail and up a cliff. SAR, Enterprise EMS, PV Fire, and Mercy Air
responded. The subject was located a little over 2 mile up the canyon. He was up a rock
scree slope. SAR and EMS stabilized him. SAR found a way down the slope and assisted
him to the bottom with EMS. He was carried to an ATV and driven back to the
helicopter. He was flown to DRMC for treatment.

06/26/2020 @ 1315 — Injured Mountain Biker — Wire Mesa Trail, — Man Hours - 6

SAR responded to a 14 year old male that had a compound fracture of his arm. The male
was near the Wire Mesa Trail. SAR and Hurricane EMS located the subject on Cry Baby
Hill. He was stabilized and placed in the ambulance.

06/27/2020 @ 1200 — UTV Accident — Sand Mountain —- Man Hours — 7

SAR responded to a UTV accident on Sand Mountain. As they arrived on scene, they
were notified by Utah State Parks there were no injuries. SAR and EMS canceled their

response.

06/27/2020 @ 1445 — Lost Hikers — Hurricane Canal Trail —- Man Hours - 20

SAR responded to locate 2 lost hikers. The hikers stated they were not from the area and
were too exhausted to hike out. The gave a generalized description of their location. SAR
went in and located them on the Hurricane Canal Trail. They were hiked out through
Pah Tempe Hot Springs. EMS checked them and released them.

06/29/2020 @ 1045 — Stranded Subject — West of Gunlock — Man Hours — 4

SAR and Deputies responded to a male that said he was out with his friends looking at the
stars the night before. There vehicle got stuck on a rock and his two friend left to get
help. They had been gone for several hours and he said he was worried and out of water.
Deputies and SAR located the subjects in their vehicle driving out. Someone had picked
up the two hiking out and got them unstuck. They were checked and released. SAR was
canceled just as they arrived on scene.
06/30/2020 @2030 ~ Injured Mountain Biker — Bear-Claw Poppy — Man Hours — 33

SAR responded to a 43 year old male that wrecked on his bike. The subject hit his head
and was unable to walk out. Life Flight was called out. They located him as SAR was on
their way in. The male refused the offer to fly out. He was picked up by SAR and driven
to the trail head. He was checked by EMS and refused transport.

07/01/2020 @ 0200 — Lost Subject, Tobin Wash Area — Man Hours - 28

SAR and deputies responded to a missing person in the Tobin Wash area. The daughter
of an 83 year old male called in and stated her father called and said he had been
