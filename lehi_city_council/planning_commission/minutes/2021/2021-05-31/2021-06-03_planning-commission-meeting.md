Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Discussion Items

    2.1 Discussion of Development Code Chapter 26, Accessory Uses, clarifying maximum
    building heights for accessory dwellings.

    Mike West introduced the discussion items for the meeting.

    Sierra Pierson presented on current code on detached accessory dwelling units and garages
    and what the potential changes could include. Sierra asked about what the Commission
    would like to see with permitted uses, setbacks, and height limits. The City Council
    requested the permit be site specific review for conditional use. The Commissioners
    suggested the Planning Department look at windows, permitted use, lights that aren't on

    fence line and existing homes, possibly garage or something more aesthetically pleasing,
    following setbacks and height per current code.

    2.2 Discussion of Lehi City’s accessory dwelling regulations and new State law

    Brittney presented information of the State's new accessory dwelling regulations and new
    state law.

    HB-82 Single Housing Family housing modification requirements include IADUs permitted
    in all primary residential zones, can't prohibit minimum size, or frontage, HOAs can't prohibit
    IADUs, etc. Mike outlined commercial and residential zones and overall land use of the City
    with different types of housing options. The Commission agrees that traffic is a concern. The
    Planning Department will continue to evaluate.

    2.3 Discussion of Bike and Pedestrian Master Plan update

    Mike West gave a brief presentation on Bike and Pedestrian Master Plan update. He talked
    about traffic, growth of Utah and the City, and the solution to traffic. Mike discussed increased
    capacity of roadways and how the research shows that is not a solution. He talked about TOD,
    biking and walking to help alleviate traffic issues. He hopes to implement a safe and connected
    network of biking facilities and would like to accommodate all use of alternative land use
    transportation. Mike discussed the "Centers Approach" and how it will help with the transition
    phase. The Commissioners and Planners discussed the East and West Corridors of Lehi that
    are frequented at an exceptional rate by traffic. Mike would love to see connections circulate
    around, not through the City. He would like to see TOD's work and use them as a viable
    alternative. Transit opportunities and future traffic studies were also discussed.

3. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Nielsen moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
