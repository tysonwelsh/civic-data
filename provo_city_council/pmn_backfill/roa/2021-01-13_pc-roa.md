CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
 Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning
 Commission. Key issues raised in written comments received subsequent to the Staff Report or public comment during
 the public hearing included the following:
 • The item was opened for public comment and no comments were received.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
 Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
 • The homes will be built by a builder that the applicant has selected.
 • A neighborhood meeting was held and some people wanted this subdivision to be a cul-de-sac. Ultimately, the
    decision was made to have it be a through street.
 • Some wanted to see duplexes, but that was not something the applicant wanted.
 • A neighbor had a concern about his fence being removed. The applicant will not remove the fence and will be
    installing their own next to the existing fence.
 • There is a dried-up well on the property where the street will be located. This well will be capped and inspected by
    the state and city engineers.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
 Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
 • The street width being narrower than streets have been is a nice thing for the neighborhood.
 • Happy to see this type of development occurring as infill in the area.
 • Capping the well in an approved manor will help ensure that there are not issues in the future with water related
    issues coming to surface.
