RESOLUTION NO. _20-04-01____________                                               April_6_, 2020

  A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL CONSENTING TO
   AND AUTHORIZING EXTENSION OF THE DECLARTION OF A PUBLIC HEALTH
       EMERGENCY FOR THE CORONAVIRUS DISEASE OF 2019 (COVID-19)

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) declared a public health emergency
for the coronavirus disease of 2019 (“COVID-19”) on March 14, 2020 (the “Declaration”)
pursuant to Chapter 2.86 of the Kearns Metro Township Code (the “Code”); and

       WHEREAS, consistent with Chapter 2.86 of the Code and consistent with its own terms,
the Declaration is set to expire on April 13, 2020, thirty days after its adoption; and

       WHEREAS, the Declaration may be continued or renewed with consent the Kearns Metro
Township Council (the “Council”) pursuant to Chapter 2.86 of the Code and Utah Code Section
53-2a-208 of Utah’s Disaster Response and Recovery Act; and

      WHEREAS, guidance from the President of the United States received on March 29, 2020
recommended that states continue stay home orders until at least April 30, 2020; and

     WHEREAS, the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation expects the peak of the
COVID-19 pandemic in Utah to be April 24; and

        WHEREAS, the Council finds that the health, wellness, and safety of Kearns and its
citizens are served by continuing the Declaration for at least thirty additional days beyond its
current term; and

        WHEREAS, these conditions continue to constitute a “State of Emergency” within the
intent of the Disaster Response and Recovery Act found in Title 53, Chapter 2a of the Utah Code
Annotated 1953, as amended; and necessitate that Kearns continue the proclamation of a local
public health emergency.

       NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, effective immediately by the Kearns
Metro Township Council that:

       SECTION 1: The Council consents to and hereby extend the Declaration by thirty days
       from its current expiration, and sets the new expiration of the Declaration’s term as May 13,
       2020; and

       SECTION 2: The Mayor of Kearns may amend the Declaration consistent with Utah’s
       Disaster Response and Recovery Act and Chapter 2.86 of the Code as needed to address
       the coronavirus public health emergency as it evolves; and

       SECTION 3: This resolution shall be filed with the office of the Salt Lake County Clerk
       pursuant to Utah Code Section 53-2a-208(c); and

       SECTION 4: This resolution shall take effect immediately upon its publication pursuant
       to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-711(1) and Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-712.
