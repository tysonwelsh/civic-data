                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO.22-81

       A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE EXECUTION OF INTERLOCAL
   COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND SALT LAKE
   COUNTY FOR COST SHARING OF THE 2022-2028 UPDES MEDIA CAMPAIGN.

       WHEREAS, the City is obligated to the Jordan Valley Municipalities Utah Pollutant
Discharge Elimination Systems (UPDES) Permit; and

       WHEREAS, the Jordan Valley Municipalities UPDES Permit requires Riverton City to
implement public education and outreach programs for residents, institutions and businesses
including industrial properties for the purpose of preventing and reducing pollution affecting the
Jordan River and its tributaries; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is a member of the Salt County Stormwater Coalition, of
which coalition created and implemented multimedia programs; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County holds the contracts for the consultants and contractors
needed to facilitate the functioning multimedia programs; and

      WHEREAS, Salt Lake County requested Riverton City renew the Interlocal Cooperation
Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake County for cost sharing of the 2022-2028
UPDES Media Campaign; and

       WHEREAS, the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake
County for cost sharing of the 2022-2028 UPDES Media Campaign defines the services and
maintains the funding source; and

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake County for
          cost sharing of the 2022-2028 UPDES Media Campaign is hereby approved, and the
          Mayor is hereby authorized to sign the Agreement. Prior to signing the agreement,
          the City Attorney is authorized to make any minor revisions deemed necessary to
          accomplish the intent of the agreement but do not result in a material alteration to the
          terms of the Agreement.

       2. The Interlocal Cooperation Agreement is made effective on the date the Agreement is
          signed by the Mayor of Riverton City and Salt Lake County Mayor.
