OAM2023-000938 - The Municipal Services District (MSD) planning staff is recommending an ordinance
change to be made to the Magna Metro Township Municipal Code, section 19.42.030 (B)(2). This is a
proposed amendment to the lot area standards of detached accessory dwelling units. Presenter: Morgan
Julian (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Morgan Julian provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding 25% coverage, on-site parking and meeting all
standards, and size of lots requested.

Commissioner Cripps motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Richards seconded that motion.
