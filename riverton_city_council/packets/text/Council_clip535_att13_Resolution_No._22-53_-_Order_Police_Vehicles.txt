                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-53

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING PURCHASING TO ORDER THE FY 23/24 POLICE
                        VEHICLES NOW

        WHEREAS, the Police Chief would like to order the FY 23/24 police vehicles now in
order to get them in July 2023; and

       WHEREAS, the payment for the vehicles will be in July, out of the FY 23/24 budget;
and

       WHEREAS, the lease contract with Zion's Bank will go to council in or after July 2023
and the first year's payment will not exceed $107,000.00 and will be made after the contract is
executed; and

       WHEREAS, by approving the ordering of these vehicles now, Council will need to
approve $315,000.00 in the FY 23/24 Police Budget for the total lease vehicle budget.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council approves Resolution No. 21-53 authorizing Purchasing to
          order the FY 23/24 police vehicles now.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 2nd day of
