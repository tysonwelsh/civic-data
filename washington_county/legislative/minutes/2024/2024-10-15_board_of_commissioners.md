CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3371, A RESOLUTION ADOPTING
THE WASHINGTON COUNTY _ POLICY FOR ESTABLISHING A _ PUBLIC
INFRASTRUCTURE DISTRICT

Deputy County Victoria Hales stated that we have seen an increase in development pressure within
the County, and public infrastructure districts are being proposed by developers. Outside counsel
and the civil team prepared a proposed policy for handling requests to form a public infrastructure
district. This resolution would adopt the policy.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3371, A
Resolution Adopting the Washington County Policy for Establishing a Public
Infrastructure District. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting
aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3372, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT FOR RECOVERY COURT LAW ENFORCEMENT
SERVICES WITH ST. GEORGE CITY

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated the Sheriff has oversight over probationers, and St.
George City Police is able and willing to assist the Sheriff’s office in these matters. This agreement
is proposed for approval subject to legal review as the attorneys coordinate with the St. George
City attorneys to finalize minor details in the document.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3372, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Agreement for Recovery Court Law
Enforcement Services with St. George City Subject to Attorney Review.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3373, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE WASHINGTON COUNTY FAIR DIRECTOR TO EXECUTE AGREEMENTS FOR
THE FAIR ON BEHALF OF WASHINGTON COUNTY

Mr. Snow explained that this is a Resolution similar to 2023, where the fair director can enter in
smaller agreements regarding the fair. All contracts would be reviewed by the County Attorney’s
Office, and would be subject to purchasing and requisition rules of the Clerk/Auditor’s office.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3373, A
Resolution Approving the Washington County Fair Director to Execute
Agreements for the Fair on Behalf of Washington County. Motion seconded
by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3374, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AGREEMENT WITH VARIETY ATTRACTIONS, INC. FOR TALENT
ACQUISITION FOR THE 2025 WASHINGTON COUNTY FAIR

Mr. Snow stated that Variety Attraction has agreed to talent broker that will assist the Fair Director
in talent acquisition for the big-ticket events for the 2025 fair. This agreement would be
conditionally approved, subject to legal review.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3374, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement with Variety Attractions, Inc. for Talent
Acquisition for the 2025 Washington County Fair. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

OF WASHINGTON COUNTY’S RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS SALES TAX FOR
A CULTURAL DISPLAY

Commissioner Iverson explained that over a year ago the County was asked to display a George
Washington Stand Your Ground sculpture until Liberty Village is ready to display it. It is
recommended that the statue be duplicated and this Resolution would allow $240,000 to be

appropriated to recreate that statue.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3375, A
Resolution Appropriating Funds Collected through Unincorporated Portion
of Washington County’s Recreation, Arts, and Parks Sales Tax for a Cultural
Display. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.
