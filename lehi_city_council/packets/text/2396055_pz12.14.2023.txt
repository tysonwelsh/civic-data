160   3.7) Public hearing and consideration of Jeff Southard’s request for conditional use
161   approval of the Bellaview Commercial site plan with drive-thru's located at 1200 West
162   3600 North.
163   Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.
164
165   Commissioner Everett asked for clarification on the drive aisle and the exceptions.
166   Commissioner Newall asked about the exceptions. Craig Chambers stated an exception will
167   always have to be granted. Kim Struthers recommended the phases of the fencing should be
168   brought up when they needed to be installed.
169
170   The applicant, Jeff Southard, was present. He stated the three drive thru's are part of phase one.
171   All the drive thru's are conditional uses and the code states they "shall" be approved. He stated
172   this type of layout has been approved numerous times and the applicant has worked with staff to
173   ensure they have met safety standards and walkability. Commissioner Everett asked if there were
174   alternative layouts. Mr. Southard stated the drive aisles were critical to the plan. He stated they
175   will do the fencing in phase one. They will install a 6-foot opaque fence. He does not know the
176   hours of operation.
177
178   Commissioner Newall opened the public hearing.
179
180   Scott Williams – He wanted to recognize development is inevitable. The speed is out of control,
181   and he has concerns about this and other developments in the area. Hours of operation is a
182   concern, kids crossing the street to attend school is a concern. He hasn't seen an effort to mitigate
183   congestion. He doesn't recognize this is an area to develop but would like to see if something can
184   be done.
