                                 Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                          June 23, 2020
                                            7:00 p.m.




   1.    The council discussed the possibility of creating a policy for high-cost impact fees for
        commercial developments.

   2.    City Administrator Seth Atkinson asked the council if they would like to present a letter
        of support to the city’s police department because of recent events across the nation.

   3. The council discussed the need of creating a gas system policy addressing individuals
      tapping into our system and doing the work themselves instead of contacting the city’s
      gas department.

   4. The council reviewed the latest financial reports.

   5.   Adjournment 7:55 pm.




Present: Mayor Nielson, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Justin
Seely, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, JD Parady, and Kalli Bowers
