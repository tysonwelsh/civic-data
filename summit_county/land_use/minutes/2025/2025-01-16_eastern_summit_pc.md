Madlyn McDonough,
Planner
, presented an application to amend the plat notes in the Country Haven Subdivision.
Commissioners asked brief clarifying questions of Planner McDonough. Planner McDonough responded.
Lauren Bolger, attorney for the property owner, addressed the Commission and provided additional information for discussion. Ms. Bolger shared a presentation with the Commissioners.
Commission Chair Peterson opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:19 p.m.
Megan Sargent was called to speak.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:21 p.m.
Commissioner Sargent responded to the questions raised during public comment.
Planner McDonough and Ms. Bolger added additional information to the discussion.
Helen Strachan,
Civil Deputy Attorney,
asked Planner McDonough a question about the recommendation.
Don Sargent made a motion to approve the plat note amendment as discussed based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval as listed in the staff report with the modification to replace "a structure" with "any structure" but keeping the drafted language regarding an exception for utility infrastructure. Clinton Benson seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).

Madlyn McDonough,
Planner
, presented a proposal to amend the Eastern Summit County Development Code by adding a use to the Chart of Allowed and Permitted Uses in 11-3-16, adding a definition of that use to the Definitions section of the Code and adding an exception to the Lighting Regulations section of the Code in 11-6-20.
Planner McDonough provided background information on the application.
Commissioners provided comments and asked clarifying questions of Planner McDonough. Planner McDonough responded to their concerns.
Commission Chair Peterson opened the meeting for public comment at 6:47 p.m.
Jan Perkins was called to speak.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:51 p.m.
Daniel McCay,
Applicant
, addressed the Commission and gave a brief presentation. Discussion followed the presentation.
Peter Barnes,
Community Development Director
, addressed the Commission and responded to comments raised by the Commission Members.
Mr. McCay responded to the Commission concerns and asked for direction to move forward with the application. The discussion continued.
Don Sargent made a motion to table the matter and direct staff to work with the applicant to propose language to address Commission concerns. Marion Wheaton seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
