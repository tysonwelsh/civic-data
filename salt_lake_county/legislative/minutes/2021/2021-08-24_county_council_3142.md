Mr. Lawrence Horman  stated he would encourage the Council to look into the 
authorization of a campground for homeless people.  He also stated that residents should 
love everyone regardless of what opinion they may have relating to COVID -19 and the 
mask mandate.  Truth is better than lies.
The following citizens spoke in opposition to a mask /vaccine mandate because science 
does not support the wearing of masks, its a citizen's constitutional right to decide what 
they put in their body, parents are responsible to choose what is right for their children, 
there are other preventive options available, the government is requiring a shot for 
something that doesn ’t exist anymore, and a person is 14 times more likely to die from 
being alive than from COVID.

3. REPORT OF ELECTED OFFICIALS:
3.1. Council Members
Council Member Snelgrove stated the events taking place in Afghanistan will be 
felt around the world.  People have to get out of Afghanistan, or they face certain 
death.  The residents of Salt Lake County can help by welcoming refugees into the 
community and donating to a worldwide charity.
Council Member Alvord  shared additional information with the Council regarding 
his research into COVID and the wearing of masks.  He stated a lot of European 
countries are not requiring the wearing of masks for children under 12.  There is not 
sufficient evidence to require the wearing of masks.
