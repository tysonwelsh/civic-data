  AN ORDINANCE RATIFYING ACTION OF SENDING A LETTER TO THE GREATER
     SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT, PRIOR TO INCORPORATION,
    ALLOWING GSLMSD TO COLLECT ALL FINES, FEES, CHARGES, LEVIES, AND
           OTHER PAYMENTS IMPOSED BY THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP.

BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Magna Metro Township Council met in a
regular public meeting on January 3, 2017

                                              WITNESS

        WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township may impose and collect fines, fees, charges,
levies, and other payments, per State Law; and

        WHEREAS, prior to the creation of the Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County
imposed and collected these funds as the municipal government of Magna; and

        WHEREAS, to insure the effective and efficient transition of the revenues created from
these charges, the Council was required to send a letter to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal
Services District (GSLMSD) to direct the District to collect the fines, fees, charges, levies, and
other payments imposed by the Magna Metro Township prior to January I, 2017; and

        WHEREAS, this work had to be performed prior to the Council's official terms of office
commencing; and

       WHEREAS, the elected members of the Magna Metro Township Council approved the
creation and sending of the letter and authorized the chair to sign the letter; and

       WHEREAS, the required documents were delivered to the CEO of the GSLMSD on
December 22, 2016.
                                        RESOLUTION

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council does
hereby ratify and approve the letter attached to this ordinance, authorizing the GSLMSD to
collect all fines, fees, charges, levies, and other payments imposed by the Magna Metro
Township.
