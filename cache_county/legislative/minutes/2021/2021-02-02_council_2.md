MINUTES
CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL
February 2, 2021 at 5:00 p.m. - Cache County Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.

In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons
who appear and speak at a County Council meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or
purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

MEMBERS PRESENT: Chair Gina H. Worthen; Vice Chair Barbara Tidwell; Council Members Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup,
David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles

MEMBERS EXCUSED:

STAFF PRESENT: County Clerk Jess Bradfield

OTHER ATTENDANCE:

1. Call to Order — Gina H. Worthen

2. Opening Remarks and Pledge of Allegiance — Barbara Tidwell

3. Accept and Swearing in of Cache County Executive - David Zook
Action: Motion to accept and approve David Zook as the Cache County Executive. Seconded by David Erickson.
Motion Passes.
Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
Nay: 0
Discussion: David Zook was sworn in as the Cache County Executive by Jess Bradfield, Clerk/Auditor

Adjourn - 5:15 p.m.

ATTEST: Jess W. Bradfield APPROVAL: Gina H. Worthen
County Clerk/Auditor Chair

ATTACHMENT 1

Official Statement
February 1, 2021

Cache County Republicans,

This past Saturday the County Delegates and members of the County Central Committee that
you chose at your Caucuses in 2018 turned out in a tremendous effort to represent you, their
neighbors. Almost 80% of those eligible voted in the special election to fill the County Executive
vacancy created by Governor Cox's appointment of Craig Buttars to be the Commissioner of
Agriculture and Food for Utah. What an incredible show of support for the Party and the four
candidates! A huge thank you to our Executive Committee for your efforts to get everyone there!

| appreciate the four candidates, David Zook, Dave Erickson, Marc Ensign, and Ladd
Kennington for running to fill the vacancy. The Cache County Council has scheduled a special
meeting on Tuesday, February 2, 2021 to appoint David Zook as your next County Executive.
On behalf of the Party, | want to congratulate David Zook for being chosen as your next County
Executive!
