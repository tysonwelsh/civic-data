3.         25-091                 City Council receiving a 2025 Economic Outlook Presentation

                   Attachments:   ZB Economic Update

                                  Robert Spendlove, Senior Economist with Zions Bank, presented an Economic Overview
                                  for 2025. He spoke about the current and future tarriffs and the financial impact. He also
                                  spoke about soft indicators such as market volatility, consumer confidence, consumer
                                  sentiment, small business optimism, unemployment claims, consumer loans, personal
                                  savings rate, and credit card delinquency rate. He also spoke about the impact of the
                                  GDP, job growth, and inflation. Mr. Spendlove spoke about Utah's economic outlook
                                  which is strong. Most industries grew over the past year and the employment growth is
                                  strong. Utah home prices are above the national average. Utah has the lowest poverty
                                  rate in the country. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

4.         25-094                 Fire Department presenting the Council with its 2024 Annual Report

                   Attachments:   Annual Report 2024

                                  Fire Chief Ryan McConaghie and Deputy Fire Chief Mike Bullock provided an overview of
                                  the 2024 Fire Department Annual Report. He thanked his team for their help in compiling
                                  the report and expressed his appreciation to the Council and Administration for their
                                  support. He also invited all to attend the Station 31 Topping Off ceremony and beam
                                  signing. Chief McConaghie reviewed the Fire Department's mission, vision, and values.
                                  He reviewed the organizational chart and staffing which reflected two deputy chiefs, as
                                  recommended by the management consultant. The organization also reflected the
                                  addition of the emergency manager to the fire department. He reviewed the facilities and
                                  current fire apparatus. Deputy Chief Bullock reviewed the departments calls and
                                  responses for the 2024 calendar year which totaled 9,430. 77% of those were medical
                                  related with 23% were fire related. They showed the emergency and fire call volume by
                                  month and provided a breakdown of the transport responses. They spoke about their
                                  departments community outreach and educational programs and provided statistics.
                                  Deputy Chief Chris Aston reviewed the operations of the Sandy Fire Department and
                                  trainings. Council questions and comments followed. Council thanked the Fire
                                  Department for their service and dedication to our community.

                                  Council Member Marci Houseman was excused from the meeting at 6:30 pm.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson thanked the Sandy Fire Department for their help with a
                    recent incident at Jordan High School. He also mentioned that the Lone Peak Park
                    Pavilion is a wonderful gathering space and he recently held an event at the facility.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham mentioned that the One Sandy Awards program is a
                    great opportunity for the City to recognize residents for their impact on our community.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update on the Salt Lake Facilities Advisory
                    Board meeting. The Salt Palace had its best year ever with $21 million in sales. The
                    venue is fully booked for the next two plus years. The Mountain America Expo Center's
                    revenue is up $6.7 million in revenue with an 80% rate of repeat business. There is
                    currently a feasibility study underway on the Salt Palace and she spoke about potential
                    changes to the facility in the future. The advisory board members had training on Open
                    and Public Meetings Act. She mentioned that officials should not text each other during a
                    public meeting regarding any item that is being discussed at the meeting. She
                    acknowledged Charlie Millard for his service to Sandy City and wished him well in
                    retirement. The Canyons Gala was a wonderful event. The Arts Guild production of Beetle
                    Juice did very well. She spoke about the ribbon cutting she attended for Bedrock Quartz,
                    a local family owned and operated business.


Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the Wasatch Front Regional Council. She acknowledged
                    Community Development for receiving a significant grant funding. She recently visited with
                    Jordan High School seniors and the Lone Peak Elementary School art exhibit. The
                    Canyons Gala was a great event. She recently had a new baby horse that was born over
                    the weekend.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, invited the Council to the April 8th top out ceremony for Fire station
                    31.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 7:44 pm.
