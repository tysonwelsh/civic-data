Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: April 12, 2022
Re:  Comments: This application is a request for approval of a text amendment to DCMC Section 9-6-080 “Nonconformities Created by Public Action”.   The code currently only addresses nonconformities as to area and setback created by public action.  This proposed text amendment addresses other nonconformities  created when a government entity takes property or purchases property for a public use, such as road construction.
The proposed amendment would permit a property owner to continue to use the parcel or structure as long as the nonconformity is not increased.  The property owner could amend the site without bringing the nonconformity into compliance with the current code so long as the amendment does not increase the degree of nonconformity.
