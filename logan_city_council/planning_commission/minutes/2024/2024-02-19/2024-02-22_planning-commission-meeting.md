[Conditional Use Permit] Troy Benson-Verizon/Logan City is requesting a design review permit for a
new Verizon Wireless Communication Tower located near the West Willow Park batting cages at
565 West 700 South in the Recreation (REC) zone; TIN 02-058-0043 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the proposal for a new Verizon cell tower to be located at West Willow
Park in the Recreation (REC) zone. Cell towers are allowed in the Recreation zone with a Conditional
Use Permit. This is the third presentation on the proposed tower to the Planning Commission, The
first presentation included the tower located next to the batting cages and playground. The second
presentation included an updated more stealth design at the same location, and they are now
proposing three alternate locations, location A is in the center of the parking lot between the batting
cages and the ball field, location B is on the north edge of the parking lot between the batting cages
and the ball field, and location C just to the north of the parking lot on the south side of the batting
cages, which is the proponents preferred option because it will be easier to landscape a buffer area
around the enclosure, it is less impactful on the existing parking lot, it will not create blind spots for
the traffic in the parking lot, and it will be better concealed with the existing trees.

Commissioner Doutre asked Mohammed Abdullahi, Logan City Attorney to review the commission's
role in approving Conditional Use Permits.

M. Abdullahi explained that all conditional uses are approved uses and a Conditional Use Permit has
to be approved unless the commission determines that any detrimental impact cannot be mitigated
and the Commission articulates the reasons why.

where the tower should be located. The existing tower at the fairgrounds is not structurally capable
of accommodating Verizon's equipment and there is not enough space on that property to put up a
new tower. The Armory site is outside of the area they are trying to service and is also a secured

PC 24-008 LDC Amendment-Subdivision Update [Code Amendment] Mike DeSimone/Logan City
is requesting an amendment to Chapter 17.40 Subdivisions, and 17.41 Boundary Line Adjustments,
Subdivision Plat Amendments or Vacations or Vacations of Rights of Way, or Easements to update
application requirements, processing requirements, review procedures, and appeals procedures.

STAFF/PROPENENT: Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director reviewed the proposed
code amendments to the Land Development Code Chapters 17.40 and 17.41, to bring the City's
land regulations into compliance with Senate Bill 174 and UCA Title 10, Chapter 9a, Part 5 and Part
6 adopted by Utah State Legislature during the 2023 legislative session.

The changes required by SB174 are centered around four general areas:

Procedural
   • Establish an Administrative Land Use Authority for each type of land division.
   • Cities cannot mandate Conceptual Plan Review for plats.
   • Cities shall define a complete application.
   • Plats are administrative decisions and are not legislative decisions.

Timing of Review Process
   • The city will determine within 5 business days of receiving a preliminary plat application
       whether the application is complete.
   • Initial review of a complete preliminary plat application shall be completed within 15 business
       days. The City will tie this with the current pre-development process.
   • Final plat review completed within 20 business days of certifying that an application for a final
       plat is complete.
   • The final plat review process cannot be no more than 4 review cycles.
   • Applicants are required to respond to changes in writing.
   • Lot line adjustments are not required to record a plat amendment.

Engineering Standards
   • Cities are required to have codified Engineering Standards. (Road standards already
      codified, water/sewer standards in process).
   • Can't require bonding for landscaping on private property.
   • Residential streets can be no wider than 32 feet.

Appeals Process
