Mayor Worell opened the meeting and welcomed attendees. She reminded people about the General Election and provided information about in-person Early Voting, Election Day Voting and ballot drop box locations.
Regional Housing Authority update; continuation of the policy discussions regarding the potential to create a Regional Housing Authority (9:04 A.M.)
Committee Co-Chairs, Tana Toly and Canice Harte gave brief remarks and opened the continued policy discussions regarding the potential to create a regional housing authority.
Jason Gliddden, Park City Housing Development Manager , provided background information as well as presented questions surrounding the potential creation of a regional housing authority.
What problem are we trying to solve?
What goals are we trying to achieve?
What are each council willing/not willing to contribute to reach these goals?
Which mechanisms best align with the goals given with the commitment levels of each council?
Are the Councils ready to direct staff to return to both Councils with draft recommendations?
Council Members asked clarifying questions and provided feedback.
Jeremy Rubell left the meeting at 9:44 A.M.
