    9.3 DISCUSSION AND ACTION – Community Garden
        Communications Manager Kathryn Newman and Water and Irrigation Manager Sullivan Love
        will present recommendations for expansion of the community garden. The mayor and city
        council will take appropriate action.

     Ms. Newman presented a year-in-review on the Community Garden, the new members to
the Community Garden committee, and the plans for 2022. Mayor Fullmer explained that the
city had been working with this project for several years.

    Mr. Love reviewed the expansion plans for the Community Garden boxes. He said that they
were requesting about $1,400 and 40-50 additional man hours. There was a discussion about
funds for the expansion.

     Councilmember Sifuentes asked about options for vines. Mr. Love replied that he was
working on a solution. There was a discussion about how they could solve the issue with the
vines. Councilmember Sifuentes asked how they communicated with the residents about how the
boxes worked. There was a discussion about the lottery process and how the vegetable stands
worked.

    Mr. Love proposed adding a park bench to the garden area. Mayor Fullmer mentioned that a
youth councilmember was working on a bench program for the city, and this was a suggested
location.

    Mayor Fullmer recapped the Community Garden discussion.

    Councilmember Rasmussen felt that she was leaving the garden in excellent hands.

    Mayor Fullmer called for a motion.

   Motion: COUNCILMEMBER FLAKE MOVED TO ADOPT THE PLAN FROM THE
COMMUNITY GARDEN COMMITTEE AND TO CONTINUE TO PURSUE DONATIONS
AS THEY BECOME AVAILABLE AND THE CITY WOULD BE HAPPY TO STEP IN IF
NEEDED AND TO ALLOW FOR THE EXPANSION. COUNCILMEMBER SIFUENTES
SECONDED THE MOTION. MAYOR FULLMER, COUNCILMEMBERS FLAKE,
RASMUSSEN, AND SIFUENTES VOTED AYE. COUNCILMEMBER WELSH WAS
ABSENT. THE MOTION CARRIED WITH ONE ABSENT.
