April6,2021 and tabled pending a development agreement. The applicants have worked

is ready to proceed to Council; likewise, the rezone request.

The development agreement outlines and stipulates parameters of development for the
proposed 8-unit townhome project. Final site plan submission and review is contingent
on the Council's approval of the rezone. Such site plan will be reviewed and approved (if
all parameters of the underlying zone and development agreement are met)
ad m inistratively.

Staff requests one item be clarified in the development agreement prior to signatures (if
approved). Section 1.2 states that "vehicle and pedestrian access will be provided from
900 East, with garbage serviced from Casa Blanca Dr as shown on Exhibit 8". Exhibit B,
however, shows no such layout for garbage collection off Casa Blanca Dr. Further, it was
previously stipulated at the April 6, 2021 Council meeting that there be no access to the
south; no gate, sidewalk or road access be allowed.

This request for clarification is identified as condition of approval for the development
agreement. lncluded in the materials is the staff report from the AWil 6,2021 Council
meeting (as further background information) and associated ordinance of rezone.

Public notice has been sent to property owners within 500 feet of the subject parcel. No
objections have been received in writing as of the writing of this report. One phone call
was received in opposition to the original rezone request. Staff forwarded public
comments received between the time the report was prepared and the Council meeting
on October 18th.
