MARCH 19, 2024

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on March 19, 2024, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2024-1252-O, AN ORDINANCE ADOPTION
AND IMPLEMENTING REVISED REGULATIONS FOR LANDSCAPING AND WATER
CONSERVATION FOR DEVELOPING LAND IN WASHINGTON COUNTY, UTAH

Community Development Planner Reid Walkenhorst explained this Ordinance contains small
language changes from the Ordinance that passed in December 2023. Doug Bennett with the
Washington County Water Conservancy District (WCWCD) stated the goal of this Ordinance to
ensure the County and its residents have reliable, resilient and sustainable water supplies.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2024-1252-O, An
Ordinance Adopting and Implementing Revised Regulations for Landscaping
and Water Conservation for Developing Land in Washington County, Utah.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3275, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A _ PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT FOR RAVEN EGG OILING IN AND
AROUND THE RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

Habitat Conservation Plan Director Cameron Rognan explained one protection for the Mohave
Desert Tortoise is raven mitigation. Ravens are protected as a migratory bird species, and
Washington County has recently been granted a Raven Depredation Control Permit. It is
recommended that Hardshell Labs, Inc. conduct raven egg oiling in and around the Red Cliffs
Desert Reserve.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3275, A
Resolution Approving a Professional Services Agreement for Raven Egg
Oiling in and Around the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3276, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE WASHINGTON COUNTY COUNCIL ON AGING FOOD PREPARATION
AGREEMENT, AMENDMENT V, WITH TRIO COMMUNITY MEALS

Council on Aging (COA) Director Jacob Browning explained due to inflationary pressures, there
needed to be a 4% price increase to the current contract with TRIO Community Meals to the
current Senior Centers that provide Meals on Wheels and other services. The COA is currently
working with Five County Association of Governments to receive grants to offset these costs.

02/23/2024 @ 1832, Stranded Motorcyclist — Toquerville Falls - Man Hours — 38

SAR responded to a 42 year old male motorcyclist that crashed his bike in LaVerkin
Creek. He stated he had no supplies to make it through the night. SAR went in and
located him. They brought him out and he was checked by EMS. He refused medical and
said he would come back and get his motorcycle in daylight.

02/27/2024 @ 1788, Stranded Motorist, LaVerkin Creek — Man Hours — 32

SAR responded to a 50 year old male that got his Toyota Tacoma stuck in LaVerkin
Creek. SAR located the male and assist him out. He was taken back to the IC at the
LaVerkin Shooting Range. He denied EMS and was released.
03/12/2024 @ 2024, Stranded Motorist, Smith Mesa — Man Hours — 28

SAR responded to locate four subjects, 2 parents and 2 children, that were stranded on
Smith Mesa. Their vehicle broke down and they started walking to get out. The subjects
got lost and had no supplies. SAR went in and located the family. They were brought out
to the IC and released. ASAR member gave them a ride to their hotel.

03/13/2024 @ 2027, Overdue Climbers — Water Canyon — Man Hours — 12

SAR responded to five overdue climbers in Water Canyon. Another hiker stated they
yelled at her to get help. SAR was paged along with the DPS Helicopter. The climbers
made their way out prior to SAR arrival. All responding units were canceled.

03/14/2024 @ 1242, UTV Roll Over — Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 16

SAR responded to a UTV roll over on Sand Mountain. SAR and Utah State Park
Rangers located the UTV. The 3 subjects involved were unhurt. They were brought out to
the OHV Staging area and released. No EMS was needed.

03/15/2025 @ 1629, Injured Mountain Biker — Bear Claw Poppy — Man Hours — 30

SAR responded to a 21 year old female that crashed on a Mountain Bike. The female had
a possible shoulder injury. SAR went in and located the injured female. They stabilized
her shoulder and brought her out by the Water Tanks. She was turned over to EMS and
transported to SGRH for treatment.

03/16/2025 @ 1600, Telematic Alarm — Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 14

SAR responded to a crash alarm on Sand Mountain. They located a rolled UTV with 4
subjects. All of the individuals were not hurt. State Parks took the accident scene and the
subjects were brought back to Sand Hollow State Park and released.

03/17/2024 @ 1638, In Reach Alarm — Red Cliffs Recreation Area — Man Hours — 42

SAR responded to an In Reach signal asking for help. The GPS showed over a mile north
on the Red Reef Trail. SAR unit started hiking in. A Drone was launched and they
located two subjects near the creek. Another signal came in stated someone had a broken
ankle. DPS Star 8 also responded. SAR located the subjects. The 53 year old female and
55 year old male were brought out. She refused medical care and they were released.
