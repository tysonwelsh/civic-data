                                       Meeting Minutes
                                      Work Session 5-12-20
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                        Nephi City Hall


       1.    Miss Nephi Pageant Director Jamie John discussed with the council the idea of cancelling
            the pageant this year because of the pandemic restrictions on large gatherings. She said
            that the Miss Nephi royalty 2019-20 said they would be in favor of continuing their reign
            until the pageant could be held in the spring of 2021. The council agreed to give the
            continuing royalty half of the scholarship amounts that would have been awarded at the
            2020 pageant.

       2. The council discussed with Chief of Police Michael Morgan and Code Enforcement Officer
          Andrea Holm the draft of a nuisance and abatement ordinance that would change the
          current code’s definition of a nuisance and change the way to enforce violations of the code.
          The council wanted some changes to the ordinance draft and agreed to email City Attorney
          Kasey Wright with their suggestions.

       3. The council discussed the idea of expanding the growth area map of the city in order to
          accommodate some annexation requests. The council directed staff to provide cost
          estimates of bringing utilities to the potential annexed areas to those people who are
          requesting annexation.

       4. The council discussed a long-term plan for electric rates.

       5. Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council a statewide utility license agreement with the Utah
          Department of Transportation.

       6. Mr. Atkinson reviewed the sales tax estimates that the state recently provided to the city.
          The numbers were much better than were anticipated because of the pandemic restrictions
          that were in place during the month of March.

       7. Mr. Atkinson and Planning Administrator Glenn Greenhalgh reviewed several issues that
          Planning Commission would be addressing at their meeting on May 13, 2020.

       8. City Councilor members gave various updates.

       9. Councilor Memmott moved to convene into executive session to discuss real property and
          competence of an individual. Councilor Seely seconded the motion, which passed on a
          unanimous vote.

       10. Meeting adjourned at 9:40 pm.

Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Nathan Memmott, Skip
Worwood, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Michael Morgan, Andrea Holm, Glenn Greenhalgh
(arrived at 8:45 p.m.), and Jamie John.
