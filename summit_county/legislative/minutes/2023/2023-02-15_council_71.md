Consideration of Approval
a.
Advice and consent of the Deputy County Manager's recommendation to appoint a member to serve on the Summit County Board of Health 4:50 PM
Janna Young, Deputy County Manager , recommended the Council appoint Alissa Van Wie to serve the unexpired term of Doug Evans on the Summit County Board of Health. The term will start on March 1, 2023, and expire on December 31,2026.
Chris Robinson made a motion to consent to the Deputy County Manager's recommendation to appoint Alissa Van Wie to serve the unexpired term of Doug Evans on the Summit County Board of Health; term to start on March 1, 2023, and expire December 31, 2026. Tonja Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong.
Attachment: Appointment to the Board of Health

Advice and consent of the Deputy County Manager's recommendation to appoint a member to serve on the Summit County Weed Control Board 4:52 PM
Janna Young, Deputy County Manager , recommended the Council reappoint Sara Jo Dickens to serve on the Summit County Weed Control Board. Term of service to expire November 30, 2026.
Tonja Hanson made a motion to consent to the Deputy County Manager's recommendation to reappoint Sara Jo Dickens to serve on the Summit County Weed Control Board; term of service to expire November 30, 2026. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong.
Attachment: Appointment to Weed Control Board
c.
Appoint members to serve on the Summit County Restaurant Tax Advisory Board 4:54 PM
Council Members reappointed the following five members to serve on the Summit County Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee:
1) Jodie Rogers to serve for another term of three years expiring in 2025.
2) Maureen Dunn to serve for another term of three years expiring in 2025.
3) Billy Demong to serve for another term of three years expiring in 2025.
4) Re appoint Heleena Sideris as a member representing Park City Chambers. Term expiring in 2025.
5) Appoint Beth Rossi as a member representing the Park City Restaurant Association. Term expiring in 2025.
Chris Robinson made a motion to approve the reappointment of Jodie Rogers, Maureen Dunn, Billy Demong, Heleena Sideris and appoint for the first time, Beth Rossi, to the Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee for terms to expire July 31, 2025 . Tonja Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong.
Attachment: Appointment to Restaurant Tax Advisory Board

Appoint member to serve on the High Valley Transit District Board of Trustees 4:58 PM
Council Members reappointed Kim Carson to serve on the High Valley Transit District Board of Trustees. Term of Service to expire December 31, 2027.
Chris Robinson made a motion to approve the reappointment of Kim Carson to the Board of Trustees for High Valley Transit District; term of service to expire December 31, 2027. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong.
Attachment: Appointment to HVTD Board of Trustees

Consideration and approval of Resolution 2023-01, Resolution of Intent to Annex Territory to Summit County Service Area #6, Summit County, Utah, to wit: Discovery Ridge (Phases 1-3), Spring Creek Commercial Plaza, Sun Meadow Subdivision (Silver Summit, Phase 5), FJ Gillmor Subdivision, Silver Creek Village, Park City Business Center Subdivision, Shadow Mountain Subdivision, Trout Creek Townhomes PUD, Crestview Condos (Phases 1-2), Park City Tech Center (Liberty Peak), Park East II Business Community Condos (Amended), Synergy Commons Condos (Amended), Silver Creek Business Park, Business Commons III Condo, Silver Creek Center Condos, Silver Creek Commerce Center, and the Market Place at Silver Creek Subdivision; John Angell and Michael Kendell 4:59 PM
John Angell, Public Works Director , and Michael Kendell, Engineer , presented for consideration and approval of Resolution 2023-01. Director Angell provided a history and overview of the subdivision that they would like to annex in to Summit County Service Area #6.
Council Members asked clarifying questions to which Director Angell, Mr. Kendell and Mr. Thomas responded.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve Resolution 2023-01, Resolution of Intent to Annex Territory to Summit County Service Area #6, Summit County, Utah, to wit: Discovery Ridge (Phases 1-3), Spring Creek Commercial Plaza, Sun Meadow Subdivision (Silver Summit, Phase 5), FJ Gillmor Subdivision, Silver Creek Village, Park City Business Center Subdivision, Shadow Mountain Subdivision, Trout Creek Townhomes PUD, Crestview Condos (Phases 1-2), Park City Tech Center (Liberty Peak), Park East II Business Community Condos (Amended), Synergy Commons Condos (Amended), Silver Creek Business Park, Business Commons III Condo, Silver Creek Center Condos, Silver Creek Commerce Center, and the Market Place at Silver Creek Subdivision . Chris Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong.
