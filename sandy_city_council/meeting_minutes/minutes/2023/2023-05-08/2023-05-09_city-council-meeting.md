2.         23-176                 Administration, City Council Office, Justice Court, City Attorney's 
                                  Office,Community Development Department, and Non-Departmental 
                                  presenting an overview of their FY 2023-24 tentative department budgets.
                   Attachments:   Department Budget Presentations May 9th.pdf

                                  Administration and Department Heads presented to the Council an overview of their Fiscal 
                                  Year 2023-24 tentative department budget.

                                  Jared Gerber, Deputy CAO, presented the proposed FY 23-24 Budget requests for the 
                                  Mayor's Office, Administration, and Non-Departmental. Budget requests include the 
                                  following: inflationary adjustments, a part-time administrative staff person, and an 
                                  increase to professional services line item. Council questions followed. 

                                  Eric Richards, Communications Director, reviewed highlights from his department during 
                                  the prior year and cost savings due to the utilization of the SandyNow! Chatbot. He 
                                  provided an overview of the city's social media engagement, new features, and additional 
                                  communication needs associated with the emergency communication joint information 
                                  center.

                                  Elyse George with Community Arts provided an overview and some department 
                                  highlights. Community Arts was recently awarded Best in State in Community Theater. 
                                  The Sandy Amphitheater impacted over 83,000 people with a 73% increase in 
                                  Amphitheater attendance. The TRCC grant funding will be used for Amphitheater 
                                  improvements. Community Arts budget requests include funding for an additional 
                                  part-time FTE and amphitheater improvements. Council questions followed.

                                  Jared Gerber, Deputy CAO, presented the budget requests for Emergency Management 
                                  which includes the addition of a coordinator position and funds to cover  increasing 
                                  programming needs for Emergency Management.

                                  Jared Gerber presented budget requests for the Community Events department which 
                                  included funding to meet the needs for larger community events and equipment needs. 
                                  Council questions followed. 

                                  Lynn Pace, City Attorney, reviewed his department's budget requests which include 
                                  funding the compensation plan and reclassifications of personnel. The City Recorder's 
                                  Office budget request include increased funding to cover the 2023 election costs with Salt 
                                  Lake County. Risk Management has increased costs relative to the city's insurance 
                                  costs. Chase Parker provided additional information associated with claims and 
                                  insurances cost increases. Council questions followed. 

                                  Following the City Attorney's presentation, Council moved to General Citizen Comments - 
                                  Item 1 on the Agenda.

3.         23-177                 First Reading: Department Management Studies Proposal

                   Attachments:   5-9-23 Memorandum
                                  Draft Management Studies Policy

                                  Council Member Marci Houseman acknowledged the Council staff for their help putting 
                                  together this proposal. As a Council, it is our responsibility to continually strive to make 
                                  improvements and pursue best practices. To help achieve this Council goal, Ms. 
                                  Houseman proposed the Council hire a third-party subject matter expert to conduct a 
                                  thorough management study to ensure that all city departments have adequate 
                                  resources, organization structure, and staffing levels to perform their duties. In speaking 
                                  with associates at Ogden City, their staff is very positive and supportive of conducting 
                                  department audits. Shane Pace, CAO, spoke in support of management studies. He 
                                  provided a history of how Sandy City has used management studies in the past to 
                                  improve department efficiencies. Mr. Pace spoke of the collaborative nature and 
                                  effectiveness of conducting management studies. This is a Council initiative and the 
                                  council office would head the RFP process and the hiring of a consultant. Dustin Fratto, 
                                  Council Director, stressed the importance of collaboration to the success of this process. 
                                  Council questions and feedback followed.

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto, Council Director: The Council meeting on May 16, 2023 is cancelled. The 
                      Annual Police Banquet is next week. Open Enrollment is underway for FY 2023-24.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Marci Housemean is attending the National Chamber Conference next 
                      week and will be representing Sandy City at the conference.

                      Council Member Brooke D'Sousa gave out Sandy Beautification tee shirts from the Sandy 
                      Youth Council. The Sandy Youth Council will join the Council for this service project on 
                      May 20th.

                      Council Member Ryan Mecham spoke about the history of city halls and council 
                      chambers. He is honored to sit in the "people's home" and spoke about the duty to serve. 
                      He reminded all of the importance to treat everyone with value and dignity, which includes 
                      listening to each others' comments. When we meet in the people's home, we should 
                      aspire to treat everyone with dignity. He does not believe the Mayor should have been 
                      interrupted at last week's council meeting and wanted to share this with the Mayor.

                      Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledged the Community Arts staff for winning Best 
                      in State. He thanked Chase Parker, Risk Manager, for his many years of service with 
                      Sandy City and wished him well in retirement. He thanked staff and Administration for 
                      their efforts in coordinating the town hall event at Alta Canyon recreation center and he 
                      thanked all the residents who came out to the town hall last night. 

                      Council Member Scott Earl thanked Council Member Robinson for his efforts conducting 
                      the ACRC town hall last night. Administration, Council, and residents came together and 
                      offered their perspective in an informed and respectful manner.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey expressed appreciation to the Public Utilities crews for 
                      all their efforts and preparations to mitigate flood issues around the city. She provided an 
                      update on the RFP and naming rights process of ACRC. 

                      Council Member Alison Stroud attended the ribbon cutting for the new Hale Centre 
                      Theatre Beehive Educational Center - an exciting addition to the HCT which will open in 
                      approximately 18 months. She thanked the Sandy Service Ambassadors for their help at 
                      last night's town hall event.

Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the city's budget priorities for the 2023-24 fiscal year. On 
                      the city website, video presentations have been posted that provide detailed information 
                      regarding the budget priorities for the upcoming fiscal year and she encouraged residents 
                      to watch these videos and get educated on the city's priorities. She thanked the 
                      Communications team for their help to produce and post the videos. She spoke about the 
                      city's flood preparation efforts. You will find up to date information on the city's website. 
                      Mayor Z expressed her gratitude to Chase Parker for his years of service to Sandy City 
                      and his efforts to keep our residents safe. She thanked Council Members Robinson and 
                      Earl and Parks Director, Dan Medina for participating and organizing last night's ACRC 
                      Town Hall event. And she thanked Council Member Mecham for his remarks.
