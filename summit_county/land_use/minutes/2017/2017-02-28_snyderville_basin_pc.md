Principal Planner Ray Milliner said the purpose of this meeting is to re-introduce the 
Commission to the application and go through the history of the project up to this point.  
Another objective is to give an overview of what the project is.  The third objective is for 
the Commission to understand the reasons why the Community Development Director 
decided how this application should be processed.        
 
Planner Milliner said the parcel is 126 acres in size.  It is located off I-80 at the Jeremy 
Ranch Exit.  A Development Agreement was granted on July 26, 1999.  It was partially 
built with the tubing runs.  The parking lot was put into place.  The ponds supplying the 
water for snowmaking were put in.   
 
On July 26, 2009, the Development Agreement expired.  The uses that were developed 
were vested and are allowed to continue.  On June 6, 2016 a conditional use application 
was submitted for an accessory building over 10,000 square feet.  That application was 
reviewed by the Planning Commission on July 26, 2016 in a work session.   
 
Planner Milliner explained two applications were required.  First was a special 
exception for additional height for a building.  The applicants were requesting a 45.5 
foot building height.  This was granted by the County Council on December 7, 2016.  The 
second application was a conditional use, which is what the Commission is reviewing at 
this meeting.   
 
Planner Milliner explained the application for the special exception went before the 
County Council first because the Planning Commission is not authorized to grant that 
type of approval.  They would have been required to have denied the application.

Planner Milliner listed the vested uses and the uses the applicant has requested.  The 
vested uses were outlined in the Development Agreement.  The most significant use 
being proposed is a 52,000 square-foot training facility.  This will be described further 
in the meeting by Mr. Barille.   
 
Planner Milliner listed the other requested uses.  He said that each of the described 
uses, within the Development Agreement, fit within what is being proposed as part of 
the conditional use.  Because the Development Agreement has expired, neither it nor 
the SPA can be modified.   
 
Planner Milliner said the project is located in the HS zone.  Applications for a SPA are no 
longer allowed within the zone.  The property now reverts back to the HS zone.  The 
uses allowed within the zone, along with the vested uses, are the permitted uses for this 
application.   
 
Planner Milliner said in the HS zone, an accessory building greater than 10,000 square 
feet is a conditional use.  The Community Development Director made the finding that 
the principal use is an outdoor recreational facility.  The training facility is an accessory 
to the principal use.  The activities within this building are accessory to the outdoor 
recreational use.   
 
Planner Milliner said the final issue is in regard to the process.  The Community 
Development Director reviewed this application and made the determination that this 
is the process that should be followed.  Planner Milliner said other processing methods, 
such as rezoning, were looked at.  Each of these methods had problems that would have 
the potential to negatively impact the surrounding neighborhood.  This process will 
allow the Commission to review all of the issues that are necessary to mitigate the 
project without the risk of unintended consequences.
