                             Snow Removal Services Agreement



        This Agreement to provide snow removal services (the “Agreement”) is made and
entered into as of the _____ day of ______________, 2021, by and between DRAPER CITY
(“Draper”), a municipality and political subdivision of the State of Utah; and TRAVERSE
RIDGE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT (“TRSSD”), a quasi-municipal corporation and
political subdivision of the State of Utah. Draper and TRSSD may hereinafter be referred to
individually as “Party” and collectively as “Parties”.

      WHEREAS, as a municipality, Draper is authorized to perform services related to snow
removal within its city boundaries, pursuant to the broad authority granted to it by the Utah
Municipal Code, Title 10 of the Utah Code; and

        WHEREAS, TRSSD was created as a special service district by Draper in 1999 under the
Utah Special Service District Act, Utah Code Ann,, § 17A-2-1301, et seq. (1999) (now
renumbered at Utah Code Ann. § 17D-1-101, et seq.), for the purpose of providing snow removal
in portions of Draper City (the “TRSSD Area” Exhibit A), and providing a means of funding the
snow removal costs in the area; and

         WHEREAS, the Parties were involved in litigation in Third District Court Case No.
170902106, which case was resolved by the entry of a final declaratory judgement by the Court
stating:

       1.      City of Draper must provide, or cause to be provided, standard municipal services
       within the boundaries of Traverse Ridge Special Service District equal to all other areas
       of the City, including snowplowing, street lighting, road repair and maintenance, street
       sweeping, and disposal services;

       2.     If the costs of providing snowplowing, street lighting, road repair and
       maintenance, street sweeping, and disposal services within the boundaries of Traverse
       Ridge Special Service District are higher than the costs of providing these same standard
       municipal services to other areas of the City, Traverse Ridge Special Service District is
       responsible for payment of the additional differential costs; and

       3.      If the City provides, or causes to be provided, snowplowing, street lighting, road
       repair and maintenance, street sweeping, and disposal services within the boundaries of
       Traverse Ridge Special Service District at levels that exceed the levels provided to all
       other areas of the City, Traverse Ridge Special Service District is responsible for
       payment of the costs of the increased levels of service.; and

      WHEREAS, the Parties desire to implement the Court’s judgment by memorializing the
way TRSSD will pay for snow removal services in the TRSSD area.

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements of the
Parties contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties hereby agree as follows:

        1.     Purpose. The purpose of this Agreement is for Draper to provide the services
listed below for the benefit of TRSSD and the residents residing in the TRSSD Area in return for
TRSSD’s payment.

       2.      Services. The respective Parties shall provide the following services:


               A. Snow Removal. Draper shall provide all snow removal on all publicly
                  dedicated roads in the TRSSD Area and TRSSD shall pay to Draper for snow
                  removal services performed in the TRSSD Area in the manner provided in
                  Section 3 herein.

       3.       Payment for Services. The Parties acknowledge the Court judgment requires
Draper to provide standard municipal services, such as snow removal, equal to all other areas of
the City and TRSSD to pay additional differential costs if the costs to provide standard municipal
services, such as snow removal, are higher in the TRSSD Area. In order to simplify the
accounting for and payment of additional differential costs, the Parties agree on the following
payment method and agree this method will result in TRSSD paying the additional differential
cost of the City providing snow removal services in the TRSSD Area as ordered by the Court.

               A. The TRSSD shall pay for the cost, as defined in Exhibit B, to provide the
                  services described in Section 2, including the cost of labor, supplies used in
                  operations, and equipment charges based on FEMA rates and, generally all
                  expenses, exclusive of depreciation and other non-cash items which under
                  generally accepted accounting practices are properly allocable to the cost of
                  the services.
               B. Draper shall pay the TRSSD for the base level of services as defined in
                  Exhibit B.
               C. TRSSD shall pay Draper an annual administrative fee equal to 10% of
                  Draper’s expenses related to snow removal services provided to the TRSSD
                  Area pursuant to this Agreement minus the credit given to the TRSSD for the
                  snow removal base The Administrative Fee is payable within 30 days from
                  receipt of annual invoice.

       4.      Equipment. Except as otherwise stated herein or as agreed to by the Parties,
Draper shall use equipment owned by Draper and/or TRSSD in performing its obligations under
this Agreement. Attached as Exhibit “D” is a schedule of fixed assets owned by TRSSD. Draper
may use these assets in its performance of this Agreement. TRSSD shall pay for lease space to
store the 2009 Larue Snow Blower in the SunCrest Fire Station. The yearly lease is $15,000.
This shall be paid by July 30th each year for the current fiscal year.

                                                2
       5.       Effective Date/Term. This Agreement shall take effect on the date the last
authorized signature is obtained and shall terminate on June 30, 2022, which is the end of the
fiscal year of both Parties. This Agreement may, however, be renewed by the Parties for future
fiscal years by signing an addendum to extend this Agreement.

       6.      Termination. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, in whole or in part, at
any time upon written notice to the other Party at least sixty (60) days prior to the anticipated
termination date. Obligations and amounts owed by either party accrued prior to the date of
termination shall survive past termination.

       7.     Negligent Acts. The Parties hereto are governmental entities as set forth in the
Governmental Immunity Act of Utah, Title 63G, Chapter 7 of the Utah Code (the “Immunity
Act”). Consistent with the Immunity Act, each Party is responsible and liable for its own
wrongful and negligent acts.

        8.      Contracts. Draper may enter into contracts and agreements with private
organizations, the State of Utah, counties, municipal corporations, and/or any other
governmental agency for any purpose necessary or desirable for providing the services which are
the subject of this Agreement.

       9.       Certified Audit. Draper shall provide for a certified audit of the accounts and
records of its operations in performing the Services referenced herein, which audit shall conform
to generally accepted auditing standards. Such audit shall be open for inspection by each Party’s
representative at all reasonable times.

        10.     Severability Clause. In the event that any provision, clause, or other part of this
Agreement should be held invalid or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, such
invalidity or unenforceability will not affect the validity or enforceability with respect to other
clauses, applications, or occurrences, and this Agreement is expressly declared to be severable.

        11.     Complete Agreement. The foregoing constitutes the full and complete agreement
of the Parties. There are no oral understandings or agreements not set forth in writing herein.

       12.     Amendment. This Agreement may be amended at any time by the written
approval of the Parties with the same formality as required to enter into this Agreement.

        13.    Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed according to the laws of the
State of Utah.

       14.      Captions. The captions to the various Sections of this Agreement are for
convenience and ease of reference only and do not define, limit, augment, or describe the scope,
content, or intent of this Agreement.

        15.   Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which
shall be deemed an original, but all of which shall constitute one and the same instrument.

agreement insurance against claims for injuries to persons or damages to property, which may
arise from or in connection with the services provided or with related use of TRSSD equipment.
TRSSD shall provide Draper with proof of insurance by August 1 of each year.

by and through their respective duly authorized representatives as of the day and year first above

A.     Definitions.

        i.     Base Level of Services: The Base Level of Services for the TRSSD are calculated
and provided to the TRSSD based upon the cost per lane mile for Draper to provide snow
removal services to the non-TRSSD areas within Draper as calculated by Lewis Young
Robertson & Burningham or other third party consultant. A more detailed explanation of this
calculation may be found in the annual report (the “Lewis Young Report”). The report is
attached hereto as Exhibit “C” and is incorporated herein by reference.


        ii.    Cost. The total cost incurred by Draper to provide snow plow services to the
TRSSD Area, not including private roads, or roads maintained by another jurisdiction. Costs
includes equipment usage time as used in FEMA calculations, labor, fuel and other maintenance
related supplies for TRSSD owned equipment, salt and brine used, audits hereinafter required,
and, generally all expenses, exclusive of depreciation and other non-cash items which under
generally accepted accounting practices are properly allocable to operation and maintenance.
Only such cost as are ordinary and necessary to the proper and efficient operation of equipment
to plow and remove snow within the TRSSD boundaries shall be included. Subject to Section 4
of this Agreement, each Party agrees to charge for its equipment used outside its boundaries
based upon the rates per hour as stated in FEMA’s Schedule of Equipment Rates dated August
2019.

SNOW REMOVAL BASE SERVICE COST ANALYSIS
Lewis Young Robertson & Burningham, Inc. (“LYRB”) was historically retained by Draper City (the “City”) to analyze
the cost of base services provided to the Traverse Ridge Special Service District (the “TRSSD”) specifically related to
snow removal services and activities in the TRSSD. The methodology used to update the base service cost was
consistent from fiscal year (“FY”) 2014-2015 through FY 2018-2019 and was intended to ensure that citizens living in
the TRSSD were not being double charged for certain services. This report provides an updated analysis based on the
Amended Agreement to Provide Transportation Services, entered into June 11, 2019, which defines and details the
base service cost for snow removal services and TRSSD payment for disproportionate snow removal cost within the
TRSSD. It is also important to note that, unlike our FY 2019-2020 analysis, B&C expenditures were not used to
calculate the cost of base services for the TRSSD.

BASE ASSUMPTIONS
Table 1 below details the base data assumptions used in this analysis. The TRSSD includes 53.10 lane miles and
Draper proper includes 387.85 lane miles for a total of 440.95 lane miles within the incorporated boundaries of the City.
Actual snow removal expenditures from FY 2020-2021 (year ending June 30, 2021) were utilized and are clearly
identified later in the analysis.

BASE COST ANALYSIS
Table 2 below details the cost analysis for base level snow removal services (“Base Snow Removal Services”). The
allocation methodology considers the total cost of snow removal services for Draper City for FY 2020-2021. The actual
cost to provide snow removal services to the TRSSD is subtracted from the total City snow removal expense. The
remaining City proper costs are divided by the 387.85 lane miles identified within Draper City, which exclude the lane
miles within the TRSSD. In order to obtain the appropriate cost allocation, the snow removal expenditures are assigned
specifically to the City proper lane miles, which produces a unit cost allocation related to base level of service. The unit
cost is then multiplied by 53.10 TRSSD lane miles to achieve the total Base Snow Removal Services cost for the
TRSSD.

The City spent approximately $672 per lane mile to provide snow removal services across 387.85 lanes miles. Applying
the unit cost of $672 per lane mile to the 53.10 lane miles contained within the TRSSD equates to $35,678 for Base
Snow Removal Services.
