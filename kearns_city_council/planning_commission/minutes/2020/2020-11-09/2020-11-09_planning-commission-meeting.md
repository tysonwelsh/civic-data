                                          LAND USE APPLICATION(S)
                                           Hearings began at – 5:50 p.m.

31082- Paul Kinikini is requesting a Conditional Use approval for a Office/Warehouse Use for Lot 2 of the
Kinikini Subdivision. Acreage: Approximately 3.23 acres. Location: 4725 South Camp Kearns Road.
Zone: M-2. Planner: Julia Friedman. (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Julia Friedman provided
an analysis of the staff report.

Commissioner Hatch asked where they want to have the trucks come in and off of. Commissioner Wellman
said ultimately off of 4700 south and turn on to 5170 west. Commissioner Wellman asked if there would
have Sunday operations. Ms. Friedman will defer to the applicant. Commissioner Hatch asked the nature
of the business and commodity of trucks hauling. Ms. Friedman said only knows it will be a warehouse, and
applicant tends to lease and interested party.
