                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-05

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE APPOINTMENT OF COMMISSION MEMBERS
            TO THE RIVERTON CITY PLANNING COMMISSION

       WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance 2-95-020, the Mayor shall
appoint with advice and consent of the City Council residents of Riverton City to serve on the
Planning Commission; and

       WHEREAS, due to vacancies of Commission Members that have occurred on the
Planning Commission, it has become necessary to appoint a new Commission Member; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for appointment of Keith
Breinholt-District 1, Shelly Cluff-District 2, and Natalia Brown-At large; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the appointments of the Mayor's
nominations to the Planning Commission.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Mayor's appointment of Keith Breinholt-District 1, Shelly Cluff-District
          2, and Natalia Brown-At large be made with the advice and consent of the
          City Council to serve as Planning Commissioners, which shall expire January
          31, 2024.
       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 19th day of
January 2021 by the following vote:
