MEMO
To: City Council
From: Mike Barker, City Manager
Date: 2025-01-07
Re: Approval of Resolution #25-02


Comments:

Draper City entered into an interlocal agreement with Lehi City, Highland City, Alpine City,
Cedar Hills City and American Fork City to cooperate in obtaining voter approval for the
creation of a new school district. A majority of voters approved the creation of a new
district. This amendment gives the interlocal board authority to take any action legal
under Utah Code 53G-3-301.4 in preparation for the operation of the new district.
