      RESOLUTION 24-12: UTHORIZE THE AGENCY TO EXECUTE A PURCHASE
      AND SALE AGREEMENT WITH ABILITY INCLUSION SERVICES
      Chairman Nordfelt presented proposed Resolution 24-12 to authorize the Agency to Execute
      a Purchase and Sale Agreement With Ability Inclusion Services.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
      follows:

             Ability Inclusion Services has submitted a signed agreement for the purchase of
             three parcels of land owned by the WVC Redevelopment Agency. The purchase
             amount is $350,000. The purpose of the land will be for the development of an
             independent living facility for persons with disabilities. The facility will be owned
             and operated by Ability Inclusion Services.

             Ability Inclusion Services (AIS) is a non-profit organization located in West Valley
             City that serves persons with disabilities providing community opportunities for
             independence and individual growth. AIS wishes to purchase this property for the
             construction of a new independent living facility for adult persons with disabilities.
             The facility will be owned and operated by AIS.

      Upon inquiry by Chairman Nordfelt there were no further questions from members of the
      Agency, and he called for a motion.

             Mr. Huynh moved to approve Resolution 24-12.

             Mr. Fitisemanu seconded the motion.

             A roll call vote was taken:
