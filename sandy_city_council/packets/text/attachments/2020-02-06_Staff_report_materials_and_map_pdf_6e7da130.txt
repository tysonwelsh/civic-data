DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Matthew Marz, representing Enterprise Rent-A-Car, is requesting that the
Planning Commission review and approve a commercial site plan for a site conversion of an
existing one-story soda and treat shop to a rental car agency, located at 10710 S. State St.
(Exhibit 1- Site Plan) This conversion also requires Conditional Use review. An associated staff
report addresses the Conditional Use application for the rental car land use. The Planning
Commission is the land use approval body for this action, as designated in the Sandy City Land
Development Code.

BACKGROUND
The building is located on a 0.76 acre lot on the west side of State Street between Motor Park
Ave. and 10600 S. The property is currently owned by Harmon Management Group and the
previous soda shop tenants have already vacated the site. Currently the building contains 2973
square feet and the proposed conversion will decrease the size of the building to 2769 square
feet by removing the cold storage area which was necessary for the prior food use. The
conversion will involve creating a manual car wash bay, a reception area, an office, a break
room, a restroom, and a storage area. This conversion will not increase the building footprint
and will not require additional parking.

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING
No neighborhood meeting was required for this project.

ANALYSIS

Access and Parking.  Access to the site will be from an existing driveway on the east side of the
property. Customer vehicle parking will be on north (front) of the building while rental cars will
be stored on the west (rear) side of the building. There will be approximately 8-20 rental cars on
the lot at any given time. The car wash will be directed from the driveway, west across the front
of the building, then south to the end of the site and then loop back to the east through the wash
tunnel building, before exiting the car wash.

Signage
Any signs will be required to be permitted under separate applications and meet Section 21-26 of
the Sandy City Land Development Code.

Building Siting and Massing.  There will be no change in the location of the building.

Architectural Design, Materials and Colors.  The applicant is proposing to leave the existing
gray stucco and repair on an as needed basis. The applicant will be adding 12 foot garage doors
for the car wash on both the east and west sides of the building. They are also proposing to add
two new 36" x 84" doors on the west and south sides of the building. In addition the applicant is
proposing to add awnings on the east and north side of the building per staff suggestion. (Exhibit
2- Plans)

Landscaping.  Existing landscaping to remain. The conversion does not require additional
improvements.

Land Use.  The proposed use of the new building is classified by the Sandy City Development Code
as "Auto, light trucks, RV rental and leasing agencies" which is listed as a Conditional Use in the
AM Commercial zoning district.  The Conditional Use is addressed in a separate staff report for an
additional action by the Planning Commission. (Also on this agenda.)

City and Agency Reviews.  All of the regular reviewing agencies and City Departments have
reviewed and approved this project, subject to their normal conditions, as per the site plan and
site civil design drawings.

STAFF ISSUES

Reciprocal Driveway Connections.  Staff has raised the issue of reciprocal access on the
property. The Public Works Department recommends that as a condition of approval, the
property owner by required to sign the reciprocal access ("Grant of Easement for Vehicular and
Pedestrian Access") and the temporary construction access easement ("Grant of Easement-
Temporary for Installation of Reciprocal-Access Improvements") prior to site plan approval by
the City Engineer. The applicant has been made aware of this requirement and is currently
working with city staff to reach an agreement on language to be utilized in the agreement.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that preliminary site plan review is
complete, based on this staff report, and the two findings listed below and subject to the
following nine conditions:

FINDINGS:

    1. That the various City Departments and Divisions, including the City Engineer,
    Transportation Engineer, Fire Marshal, and the Public Utilities Department Chief Engineer
    have all preliminarily approved the proposed site plan.
    2. That the proposed driveway access, internal site circulation and off-street vehicle parking
    will be adequate for the proposed use of the building and the site.

CONDITIONS:

    1.  That the developer proceed through the final site plan review process with staff prior to the
    start of any construction (including payment of development fees and posting of an
    appropriate bond to guarantee completion of all required improvements on and off the site),
    according to the Site Plan Review Procedures Handout. The final site plan shall be in
    compliance with all Development Code requirements and those modifications determined
    by the Planning Commission.

    2.  That the developer sign a reciprocal access easement prior to final site plan approval.

    3.  That the development comply with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety Codes
    applicable to this type of use.

    4.  All utility boxes shall be screened from view to the extent possible (i.e. transformers,
    switch gear, telephone, cable TV, etc.) and shall be shown on the site plan and shall be
    placed underground or moved behind the front setback (minimum of 30 feet from the front
    property line) and screened from view. Each box shall be shown in its exact location and
    shall be noted with its exact height, width and length and approved by staff.

    5.  That the architectural design, colors and materials proposed for this development will
    comply with the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards and the details shall be
    finalized with staff prior to the issuance of a building permit.

    6.  That the developer be responsible for the placement of a temporary 6 foot high chain link
    fence around the perimeter of the project during the construction phase of the project for
    security. Said fence shall also be required to include fabric to prohibit blowing dust
    problems, if it becomes necessary or if it is required by the Community Development
    Department during Site Plan Review.

    7.  That the applicant comply with the Sandy City Noise Ordinance, including working hours
    during construction.
