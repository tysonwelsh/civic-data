                     COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

                             NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN Pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-113 that the Copperton Metro
Township Council will hold a public hearings on December 21, 2022, at 6:30 PM to receive public
comment on: (1) an amendment to Copperton Metro Township’s fiscal year 2022 budget (“2022
Budget Amendment”); (2) the Copperton Metro Township’s proposed budget for 2023 (“2023
Tentative Budget”); and (3) the proposed 2023 Copperton Metro Township’s fee schedule (“2023
Fee Schedule”). The meeting will be held at the Bingham Lion’s Club, 8725 W. Hillcrest Street,
Copperton, Utah 84006.
The Council will consider the adoption of the Amended 2022 Budget, the 2023 Tentative Budget,
and the 2023 Fee Schedule after taking comments from the public during the hearing. The public
is also invited to review and inspect the proposed budget on the Utah Public Notice Website
(https://www.utah.gov/pmn/) and on the Copperton Metro Township’s official webpage
(https://coppertonutah.org/).
You may also obtain copies of proposed budget and submit written comments prior to the public
hearing by contacting Nichole Watt at nwatt@slco.org or (385) 468-7400. Individuals needing
special accommodations (including ancillary communicative aids and services) during this public
hearing should contact Ms. Watt three (3) days before the public hearing.
