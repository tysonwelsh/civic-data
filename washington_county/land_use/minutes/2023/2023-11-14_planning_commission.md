2. Zone CHANGE (Public Hearing). The applicant is proposing a zone change to the zoning of parcel
    BKE-1-42-NW in the Brookside Estates Subdivision. The lot is currently zoned RE-40 (Residential
    Estate 40,000 sq ft minimum lot size) but is under the minimum lot size requirement. The applicant is
    proposing to rezone the parcel to RE-20 (Residential Estate 20,000 sq ft minimum lot size). The
    applicant is working toward using the property for Short Term Rental and requires a building permit
    to build a structure. There are concerns with this parcel related to setback and structures / parking in
    the roadway ROW. The applicant is Amanda Figueredo.

    Mr. Walkenhorst explained the location of the parcel and showed the concerns with existing structures
    and setback requirements. The property also drops off dramatically behind the existing home as it
    slopes down the Santa Clara river.

    Staff has reviewed the requested zone change application for completeness and recommends that the
    Planning Commission hold the required public hearing and send a positive recommendation to the
    County Commission to approve the zone change request from the RE-40 (Residential Estate 40000 sq
    ft minimum lot size) zone to the RE-20 (Residential Estate 20000 sq ft minimum lot size) zone for
    Parcel BKE-1-42-NW located in Brookside.

         1.   The public hearing for the zone change request was properly noticed.
         2.   The public hearing was held on November 14, 2023, in the regularly scheduled Planning
              Commission Meeting.

              compliance.

3. Preliminary Plat and Final Plat (Public Meeting). Review an application for Preliminary/Final Plat
    for the Pleasant Meadows Subdivision located in Veyo, encompassing parcels 7143-A-1-B-1-NW and
