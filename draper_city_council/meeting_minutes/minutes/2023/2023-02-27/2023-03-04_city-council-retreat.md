THE MINUTES OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL MEETING RETREAT HELD ON
SATURDAY, MARCH 4, 2023, AT THE MARRIOTT TOWNPLACE SUITES, 13690
SOUTH 100 EAST, DRAPER, UTAH.

PRESENT:         Mayor Walker, and Councilmembers Mike Green, Tasha Lowery,
                 Fred Lowry, Cal Roberts, and Marsha Vawdrey

                 Mayor Walker left the meeting from 11:45 a.m. - 12:45 p.m.
                 leaving Mayor Pro Tempore Roberts to run the meeting in his
                 absence.

STAFF PRESENT:   David Dobbins, City Manager; Kellie Challburg, Chief of Staff;
                 Bret Millburn, Assistant City Manager; and Laura Oscarson, City
                 Recorder

Call to Order by Mayor Walker at 10:17 a.m.

  1. Solid Waste pickup on private lanes: A map was displayed highlighting the
     private roads in Draper. Concerns were listed and possible solutions were
     discussed.
  2. Uses for property recently acquired by Draper City: The council discussed
     what the vision and future use of the city-owned property east of City Hall
     could be. Suggestions ranged from an arts/cultural center, expansion of City
     Hall, conference area or auditorium and recreational facilities.
  3. The roundabout at 1300 East and Pioneer Road: Council discussed the timing
     of the repairs to the roundabout.
  4. A second Draper City cemetery: Discussion included how to fund a second
     cemetery.
  5. Trails Master Plan: A Trail Approval process was discussed. Using volunteers
     to build and groom trails was also discussed.
  6. Glass recycling at IKEA: The program has been successful and there was
     discussion around whether a dumpster was an option.
  7. Bellvue Trail Connection: The council discussed the need for the trail
     connection. Materials, resident’s feed-back and timing were also discussed.
  8. Private pickle ball courts: The council considered whether there was a need
     to change the Draper City Municipal Code regarding pickle ball courts to
     mitigate the effects of sound and lighting from private courts.
  9. South Mountain Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) Enforcement: The council
     acknowledged that illegal ADU’s are a concern in the South Mountain area.
    Areas of concern were parking and emergency vehicle access. Other
    concerns were identification of non-compliant ADUs, cost of enforcing city
    code, and whether existing detached ADUs could be “grandfathered” in.
10. Fire station: The council agreed there was a need for an additional fire
    station west of I-15. Options discussed were renting in an industrial park, or
    building a station on land currently owned by Draper City.
11. Lone Peak Parkway widening: City manager, Mr. Dobbins updated the council
    on the discussions with Utah Department of Transportations (UDOT) to
    install a traffic signal on Lone Peak Parkway, and stated the road needed
    widening to handle the traffic in the area. The council also indicated a need
    for a safe way to cross Galena Boulevard at Galena Park.
12. Sponsorship for Draper Days and the Gold Star Monument: Funds are being
    raised for both items.
13. Draper Days: A map of Draper Park was displayed to help the council
    visualize where various activities would be. Volunteers are still needed.
    Councilmembers wanted the event to be only advertised locally. The parade
    float design was discussed. The council liked being on a trailer together.
    Parade safety was a concern and several ideas were explored. Candidates for
    Grand Marshall and Volunteer of the Year were also discussed.
14. Draper Community Foundation’s responsibilities were reviewed.
        a. Miss Draper program is working on a payment method for the
            application fee.
        b. Miss Rodeo needs to be advertised more in Draper.
        c. Historical Society is working with the city to get updated computers.
15. Special Events Committee upcoming events:
        a. Farmer’s Market – Mid-June through August, Saturday’s from 9 am to 1
            pm.
        b. Food Truck Nights will resume from mid-May through Labor Day on
            Monday nights.
        c. The Rodeo will be held on July 7-8.
        d. The Horse pull will be July 10.
        e. A Monster Truck event will be held on August 4-5.
        f. A Bull’s Only event will be held on August 29.
16. The possibility of a heavy spring run-off was discussed. Ideas for preparing
    residents were exchanged. The possible areas to be affected included
    around Cherry Creek, Willow Creek and Corner Canyon Creek.
  17. Councilmembers asked how best to show their appreciation to the Public
      Works employees. They recognized all the hard work, time away from family,
      and long hours that were required for plowing roads this winter.
  18. Councilmembers stated they would like to communication monthly about
      how their respective committees are functioning.
  19. A council member asked if there was a mechanism for a race to be held on
      city property if it was a non-profit, for example, to raise money for an
      individual to pay medical bills.
  20. Mayor Walker gave an update on legislation that had passed. Some of the
      topics he covered were gravel pits, homelessness, affordable housing, the
      Development Infrastructure District Act. He also discussed his role with the
      League of Cities and Towns, the Point of the Mountain project and previous
      litigation.

The meeting was adjourned at 2:16 pm.
