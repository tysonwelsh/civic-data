In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final
decision authority for rezone applications.

Example Motions:

     I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of amending Section 18.90.010,
     Table of Commercial Uses, with amendments as described herein.

     OR

     I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of the ordinance amendment, thus
     leaving the Table of Commercial Uses as currently constituted.


Background


Riverton City code includes a Table of Commercial Uses in Section 18.90.010. The Table of Uses
lists many Land Uses and lists whether each use is Permitted (P), Not Permitted (NP), or Conditional
(C) in each of the six commercial zones. From time to time the Table of Commercial Uses is
updated to meet the current needs of the City.

This amendment affects four (4) Land Uses in the Table of Commercial Uses.

     1. Adding a Land Use for “Assisted Living facilities, Nursing homes and Residential Care
        Facilities”. This amendment adds clarity to the existing table, and completing the City’s
        action to remove Assisted Living from the Elderly Housing Overlay.         The proposed
        amendment replaces the Land Use category of “Nursing/care facility, assisted living center”
        so that the new category appears listed under “Assisted Living” and adds the land use of
        “Residential Care” It also changes the use to Conditional (C) in the C-PO zone, which is in
        harmony with the existing built facilities and the previous applications of the EHOV.

     2. Adding a Land Use for “Building Trades, sales and services including Electricians, HVAC,
        Plumbers.” This land use is not listed in our current table, thus creating confusion about
        what these uses are most comparable to in the current table. This change will make it
        easier for the public and staff to help people desiring to locate Building Trade Businesses in
        the City.

     3. Bus Passenger Terminal is amended to include the clarifying phrase “as a building or site,
        not to include Public Bus Stops”. This addresses the question about whether a bus stop for
        a transit authority (ie UTA) would be considered a Bus Passenger Terminal and therefore
        restricted in some zones. With this clarification a bus terminal would be a regulated land
        use, while public bus stops would clearly be excluded. Public Bus Stops would continue to
        be handled administratively in consultation with City Council and the Transit Authority.

     4. Adding a Land Use for “Dance Studios, gymnastic studios, martial arts studios”. This land
        use is frequently asked about but is not listed in our current table. Consequently, staff must
        find similar and related uses in the table in order to discuss possible projects. This
        proposed addition will add clarity and convivence.
