FROM:          Trace Robinson, Public Works Director

ITEM:          Resolution to enter into an agreement with Riverton Business Park, LLC for Right
               of Way dedication to Build Old Liberty Way in exchange for water utility stubs,
               sleeves, and access opening from Old Liberty Way.

SUMMARY:

As we started to prepare plans to construct Old Liberty Way from Majestic Rise Parkway (13200
S) to 12600 S, we discovered a gap of ownership in the right of way. This small parcel of property
was caused by a mistake resulting from an old survey used in land transactions by Jordan School
District and the surrounding property owners. Riverton Business Park LLC has agreed to dedicate
the land associated with this gap to the City in exchange for access and utility improvements
which will be constructed with the road project. The area of dedication is 1,822 sq. ft.

The Agreement outlines the location and type of improvements the City will install with the project.


FISCAL IMPACT: $ 20,000

FUNDING SOURCE: 54-60-300, 52-60-300

ATTACHMENTS:

   1. Agreement
   2. Resolution
   3. Location map of improvements


RECOMMENDED MOTION OR ACTION:

I move the City council approve Resolution # ??? to enter into an agreement with Riverton
Business Park, LLC for Right of Way dedication to Build Old Liberty Way in exchange for water
utility stubs, sleeves, and access opening from Old Liberty Way.
