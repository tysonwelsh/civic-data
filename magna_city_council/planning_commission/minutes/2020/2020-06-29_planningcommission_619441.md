31173- (Continued from 06/11/2020) - Scott Braithwaite is requesting a roadway improvements waiver

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed waiver after taking comments from the public during
the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s rules of
conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three minutes
per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Crystal Hulbert with Salt Lake County Engineering advised they have a draft storm drain master plan south
of U-201 and finalizing and master transportation plan. She provided a map of Magna and advised there
are storm drains in the area and advised of public and private storm drains. Development south of U-201
is requiring curb and gutter and not encouraging delay agreements. North of U-201 is special with high
ground water and water tables and no storm drain owned by Magna. Issues they find a lot when curb,
gutter, and sidewalk, is being very expensive, land acquisition, grading, right of way, and potential
damages. If they deny the delay agreement, her 100% second recommendation would be to not have a delay
agreement, but not require curb and gutter at this time. If they require storm drain to be installed, it is
millions of dollars to get it that far. Commissioner Cripps said idea of grading and prepping. Commissioner
Elieson said he knows the inland port has discussed running 7200 west to I-80, if in fact what they decide,
what happens when they come in and UDOT needs to take 7200 west and turn into a major arterial, with
all the work they’re during. Commissioner Elieson said they have had a lot of deals not enforced with a
small property and isolated. Ms. Hulbert said people try to get out of it everywhere. She recommends below
they install curb, gutter, and sidewalk, here is less sidewalk to nowhere, at some point a development comes
in and needs to be installed. That is not why she is recommending no curb, gutter, and sidewalk. If there
was a place for drainage, she would be for it. Ms. Hulbert said they will do the calculations at the end of
the right of way, elevation will match what would need to be there for curb, gutter, and sidewalk when they
come in to pour, wont have to go back and redo it all. Will cause drainage issue more expensive for Magna
to fix, than if we forgo the curb, gutter, and sidewalk, to tell them to pipe it way high up is quite a burden.
Commissioner Elieson said concern with delay agreement what is in there to enforce. Ms. Hulbert said can
talk with Magna’s attorney and her request is to not require a delay agreement and not have them put it in.
Commissioner Cripps said he likes having the prep and installing would cost more headache and solve but
would like a starting point at some time and the attorneys look at the delay agreement, but ten years is a
lot. Ms. Hulbert said changing the time period can be done but need a storm drain to change something.
Commissioner Lockwood said sounds like staff recommends denying and engineering recommends we do
not deny. Ms. Hulbert said engineering does not recommend causing a drainage issue and would deny the
curb, gutter, and sidewalk. Ms. Martinez said MSD planning just provides a recommendation based on
ordinance, exception should be denied.
