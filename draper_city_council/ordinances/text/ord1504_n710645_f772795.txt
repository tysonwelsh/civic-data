City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 19, 2021, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1504, amending
Title 12 of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding Flood Damage Prevention. The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 20th day of October 2021. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
