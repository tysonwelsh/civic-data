PC 23-019 Jenny Sanchez Daycare [Conditional Use Permit] Jenny Sanchez/Jose Marroquin,
authorized agent/owner is requesting a conditional use permit to operate an in-home daycare for up
to 16 children for up to 24 hours a day located at 30 Teal Loop in the Traditional Neighborhood
Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 02-167-0081 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

STAFF: Tanya Rice, City Planner reviewed the request for a conditional use permit to operate an
in-home daycare for up to 16 children at 30 Teal Loop in the Woodruff Neighborhood. The garage
of the home was converted to a living space to house the daycare that will operate from 5:30 am to
5:30 pm with an occasional overnight. The drop-off and pick-ups are staggered, and the applicant
does not anticipate more than three vehicles there at the same time.

Commissioner asked about the daycare operating 24 hours a day. T. Rice said that the applicant
said she has a couple of friends whom she may watch their kids overnight if they need on occasion
but currently, she does not have any daycare children enrolled for overnight.

PROPONENT: No comments were made by the proponent.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

PC_ 23-022 West Meadows [Subdivision Permit] Clark Rigby/Northwest 17 LLC, authorized
agent/owner is requesting a subdivision permit to develop 57 single-family building lots located on
17.57- acres at approximately 1800 North 500 West in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential
(NR-6) zone; TIN 04-079-0019; -0016; -0012 (Bridger Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley, Senior Planner, reviewed the request for a subdivision permit to develop a 57
single-family lot subdivision on 17.57 acres at approximately 1800 North 500 West. This area is part
of the original 203-lot, 97-acre Quayle Meadows subdivision approved in 2016. The overall project
expired and has now been sold off to separate developers. The street alignments for the original
subdivision will continue as utilities and infrastructure alignments have set a precedence. There is
an existing home in the northwest corner that will need to be included in the final plat. The proposed
subdivision includes two street connections and four stubs to meet the minimum number of
connections required in the Land Development Code.

Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director said a perimeter trail was part of an original RDA
agreement between the City and the Quayle Meadows Subdivision. It should be verified that this
new proposal does not violate that agreement.

Darren Farar, City Engineer read the following stormwater condition added to the engineering conditions
under utilities, “For a number of reasons related to the depth of the existing system relative to the elevation
of the areas to be served, the proposed sewer system is not expected to be able to function as laid out.
An updated design will be required to maintain a gravity system as was originally planned for the
development’. D. Farar also spoke about how the perimeter trail agreement included a sewer corridor
easement to bring sewer into Logan City from North Logan.


PROPONENT: Clark Rigby said they are proposing a larger sidewalk in front of the homes and
eliminating the perimeter walking trail along the back of the lots. This way there is only one sidewalk
that needs to be maintained and it keeps the backyards larger and more appealing to homeowners.
R. Holley said that the original agreement will need to be looked at and either the project possibly
amended, or the original agreement amended.

PUBLIC: Dean Quayle said they will not be developing any housing further to the north. He spoke
about the irrigation ditch that they depend on to water their property to the Northwest and if it could
be piped. He also stated that there needs to be a fence installed along the north side of the
development to separate the housing and the agricultural land to the north.

COMMISSION: J. Guth asked if the project should be continued so the commission can view the
changes to address the sewer and trail system.

Commissioner Lucero asked if there would be changes made to the connectivity or lot layouts in
relation to the sewer issue.

Mr. Rigby spoke about the new North Logan sewer line that is being installed to connect to Logan
City. They have stubbed a line into lot two in the Southwest corner of the subdivision. Their plan is
to connect phase two to this new sewer line. They have a meeting scheduled for next week with
Logan City Engineers and North Logan City to figure out the sewer connections. Mr. Rigby also
spoke about the riverine (wetland) and how that area is being proposed as open space so the
delineation should not change the proposed layout.

The Commission discussed adding the new engineering stormwater condition under utilities that
Darren presented previously and adding a condition that the original agreement associated with the
Quayle Meadows Subdivision regarding trails, parks, and open space, with be reviewed for
compliance and amended if needed.
