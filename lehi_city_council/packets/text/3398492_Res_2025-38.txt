                                  RESOLUTION NO. 2025-38


    A RESOLUTION OF THE LEHI CITY COUNCIL ACTING AS THE LOCAL
  HIGHWAY AUTHORITY CLOSING VEHICULAR TRAFFIC AT 1870 NORTH AT
                          STATE STREET.


        WHEREAS, the Lehi City Council acting as the Local Highway Authority (“Authority”)
has determined that it is necessary to mitigate unsafe conditions at the intersection of 1870 North
at State Street in Lehi City; and

      WHEREAS, the City is in the process of improving other intersections in the area to create
safer and more efficient traffic flow; and

      WHEREAS, after considering the facts and comments presented to the Authority, the
Authority finds: (i) there are unsafe conditions at the intersection of 1870 North at State Street;
and (ii) to mitigate the unsafe conditions, the intersection should be closed for an indefinite
period of time pursuant to Utah State Code 72-5-105.

      NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Council of Lehi City, Utah, acting as the
Local Highway Authority, as follows:

     1.    To mitigate unsafe conditions at 1870 North at State Street, the access to State Street
from 1870 North shall be closed to vehicular travel.

      2.    This resolution shall take effect immediately.

Approved and Adopted by the City Council of Lehi City, Utah this ______ day of _______, 2025.
