 APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 29.020. Criteria for Entering into a Development Agreement.
The city may enter into a development agreement only if the development agreement:

    A. Has been duly adopted by the City Council in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.

    B. The proposed development to which the development agreement pertains is in conformity with the gen-
       eral plan, zoning district regulations, and other applicable requirements of the City.

    C. The proposed development subject to the agreement contains outstanding features that advance the pol-
       icies, goals and objectives of Lehi City, or the property owner agrees to provide capital improvements
       that exceed the development's proportionate share of the costs of the facilities needed to service the
       development, which hereby advance the provision of facilities to serve Lehi City.

  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests review and recommendation of the Whitmore Development Agreement. The applicant previ-
ously requested a General Plan Amendment from Commercial to Light Industrial. The City Council directed staff to
draft a Development Agreement to allow some uses for the next three years so that the applicant can use his property
prior to utilities being available in the area.

A Development Agreement allows for uses other than those normally allowed in the zone. This is a legislative item,
initiated by the City Council. Recent State Legislation requires that these agreements have a public hearing with the
Planning Commission prior to their approval by the City Council.

This Development Agreement specifically would allow vehicle and equipment storage, sod transfer, and green waste
facilities as temporary permitted uses for three years. After that time the Development Agreement would need to be
amended or the applicant switch to an allowed use under the existing zoning.

The DRC did not review the proposed Development Agreement.


  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development Agreements are legislative items at the full discretion of the City Council. The Planning Commission
reviews the Development Agreement and can recommend any changes to the City Council.

The public can also speak to this item and express any concerns or support for the development agreement. The public
can also suggest changes to the language of the agreement.


  POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS

The Planning Commission may provide a positive or negative recommendation on the proposed Whitmore De-
velopment Agreement. Any motion should include findings. Possible findings include:

    1.   The proposed Development Agreement will allow the applicant to utilize their property now before utilities
         are provided to the site.
    2.   The proposed Development Code amendment is/is not in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provi-
         sions of the General Plan and its various elements.
    3.   Additional findings based upon information presented during the public hearing.
