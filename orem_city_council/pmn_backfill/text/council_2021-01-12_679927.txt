37        DISCUSSION – Utility Master Plan Update
38
39   Mr. Tschirki stated the Public Works Department is working closely with Bowen, Collins, and
40   Associates to finalize the 2021 Water, Sewer, and Storm Water Master Plans. The finished product
41   will include updated capital facility plans which will assist in the development of impact fee
42   facility plans and impact fees. Orem's Financial Advisor, Lewis, Young, Robertson, and
43   Burningham, will develop financial models, funding options, and utility rate solutions that support
44   the implementation of the approved master plans. These financial models include a revenue bond
45   to accelerate construction, eliminate inflationary construction forces, and save millions of dollars.
