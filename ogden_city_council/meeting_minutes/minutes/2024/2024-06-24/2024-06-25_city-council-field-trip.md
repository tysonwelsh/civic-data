         The purpose of the field trip is to tour the Marshall White Center and WonderBlock sites to observe construction progress
on each project.

WonderBlock Site
         Council members and staff gathered at the WonderBlock site and received a safety briefing, walked through the parking
garage, and engaged in discussion with representatives of companies associated with the project.

Marshall White Center
         Council members and staff arrived at the Marshall White Center. The group received a safety briefing, walked around
the construction site, and discussed the building with representatives of companies associated with the project.


         The field trip ended at 6:00 p.m.
