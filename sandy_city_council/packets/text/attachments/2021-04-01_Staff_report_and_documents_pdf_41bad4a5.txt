REQUEST
Mr. Kelly Lund, owner of K. Lund Mechanical is requesting preliminary Commercial Site Plan
Review and Conditional Use for a new Warehouse building addition to a "Legal, Non-
Conforming Use" Heavy Commercial Use (Contractor's Office and Equipment Yard),
located in the Research and Development (RD) Zoning District. The property is located 415
W. 9800 S. Street and is the current location of K. Lund Mechanical Contractors. A 4,913

BACKGROUND

The use of the subject property as a "Heavy Commercial" land use began in 1997 when Allstate
Construction received approval from the City to build a small office and storage/warehouse
building. The subject property was subsequently re-zoned by the City as a Research and
Development (RD) zoning district located along the west side of the Interstate Highway I-15.
This action of re-zoning made the Allstate Construction operation a "Legal, Non-Conforming"
land use. As such, "Legal, Non-Conforming uses are allowed to continue, but not expand. The
applicant in this matter purchased the property in 2016 for his mechanical contractor business,
thereby continuing the non-conforming use of the property. In 2020, the applicant approached
the City for a building permit to add a warehouse addition to the existing buildings, which was
denied due to the fact that it would result in an expansion of the non-conforming use. Staff then
worked with Mr. Lund to amend the zoning ordinance to allow the "Heavy Commercial" land use
as a Conditional Use in the RD zone, which was approved by the City. This was all in preparation
for this site plan and conditional use review now before the Planning Commission.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting. A neighborhood meeting was not held, nor was it
required for this project, due to the project's low visiblity location in a non-residential area.

ANALYSIS
Vehicle Access and On-site Circulation. 9800 South Street is a cul-de-sac teminated public
street which extend west from 300 West Street (Freeway frontage road) to the elevated UTA
TRAX line railroad tracks. Two existing driveway approaches to this property extend south from
9800 S. Street near the cul-de-sac bulb to access the property. Curb and gutter is in place from the
prior development, as is the parking strip landscaping. No sidewalk exist anywhere on this public
street. The Salt Lake Community College campus parking lot is also accessed from 9800 S. Street,
north of this property. On-site circulation is existing and will be interupted from circling the
existing building by the construction of the new warehouse addition. Access to the rear (south)
outside equipment area and the rear of the new warehouse will be from the east driveway. Vehicle
access and on-site circulation will be sufficient for the propoesed development.

Zoning Review. The project will meet all of the requirements of the RD zoning district and the
Sandy City Development code. Parking for this project will meet the City requirements on site.
All required City Departments and Divisions have reviewed and recommend approval, subject to
their usual requirements and conditions. The referenced zoning code amendment in 2020 enables
the expansion of this prior, non-conforming use to become a legal, conforming use with requesite
Conditional Use approval. The 2020 amendment to the zoning code, included a location restriction

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan review is
complete and that the building's architectural design, materials and colors meet the Sandy City
Architectural Design requirements, for the proposed K. Lund Mechanical Warehouse Addition
project, based upon the following two findings and the following seven conditions:

FINDINGS.
    A.  That the proposed project meets or will meet all the applicable requirements of the
        Sandy City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as
        to building architectural design, materials and colors.
    B.  That the elimination of this Legal, Non-Conforming "Heavy Commercial" land use
        benefits the overall planning and development goals of the City, and allows
        expansion of the existing business use to better meet its needs as a growing local
        small business.

CONDITIONS.

    1.  That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior to
        the start of any construction. The final site plan shall comply with all Development Code
        requirements and those modifications required or allowed by the Planning Commission.

    2.  That the development complies with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety Codes
        applicable to this type of use.

    3.  That the developer be responsible to meet all provisions of the RD zoning district, and
        the Sandy City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design
        Standards with the details finalized with staff during final site plan review.

    4.  All utility boxes (i.e. transformers, switchgear, telephone, cable TV, etc.) shall be shown
        on the site plan and shall be placed underground or moved behind the front setback of the
        buildings and screened from view, preferably located along the proposed driveway. Each
        box shall be shown in its exact location and shall be noted with its exact height, width
        and length. Building utility meters shall be mounted to the side or rear elevation or
        screened.
