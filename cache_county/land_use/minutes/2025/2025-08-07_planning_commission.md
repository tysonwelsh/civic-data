#2 Public Hearing (5:35 PM or soon thereafter) – Lowers Foods – South Road Extension Rezone 
Smith reviewed the staff report for the Lowers Foods – South Road Extension Rezone. 
05:40:00 
 
07 August 2025                       Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 3 of 5 
 
Daugs motioned to open the public hearing for the Lowers Foods – South Road Extension Rezone; Rigby 
seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
Ayes: Val Jay Rigby, Brady Christensen, Nate Daugs, Kurt Bankhead, Jason Watterson, Chris Sands 
Nays: 0 
Bankhead asked about annexation into Richmond. 
Jake Leatham commented that Lowers would like to keep the property in the County. 
Allen Lowers commented that the road is for staging trucks and to put a truck scale in. The scale will be 
in Richmond but they would like this parcel to remain in the county. They are also currently working on a 
new well on this parcel as well, 
Christensen asked how many families Lowers feeds in the valley. 
Mr. Lowers commented that they employee 300 people, they try to hire local contractors for work, and 
they try to help the local community as much as possible. 
Daugs asked about working with UDOT. 
Mr. Leatham responded that everything has been submitted and they are waiting on a decision from 
them. 
Daugs asked if there would be long term parking. 
Mr. Lowers responded that they might park some of Lowers trailers there but other than that no. 
Kurt Ander commented on concerns with safety for the road with increased traffic. 
Mr. Lowers responded that there should be no through street to State Street. 
Mr. Ander asked about connections to through to State Street and concerns with an LLC owning the 
extension. 
Mr. Leatham commented that due to the well being drilled, a road would have to go around the well 
house so there are no plans for a road and commented on safety of removing the trucks off the highway. 
05:53:00 
Daugs motioned to close the public hearing; Watterson seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
Ayes: Val Jay Rigby, Brady Christensen, Nate Daugs, Kurt Bankhead, Jason Watterson, Chris Sands  
Nays: 0 
Commissioners discussed the safety impacts of the road extension, and impacts to the county road 
master plan. 
Daugs motioned to recommend approval to the County Council for the Lowers Foods – South Road 
Extension Rezone with the 4 conditions; Christensen seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
Ayes: Val Jay Rigby, Brady Christensen, Nate Daugs, Kurt Bankhead, Jason Watterson, Chris Sands
