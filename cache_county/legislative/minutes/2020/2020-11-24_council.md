PENDING ACTION

O Ordinance No. 2020-11 - BGTS Rezone — Request to rezone 5.91 acres from the
Agricultural (A10) Zone to the Commercial (C) Zone, located 550 West 8700 South, near

Paradise - Vice Chair Worthen asked if any response had been received from Paradise.
Paradise Mayor Little (who was in attendance) replied he supports the work Avalon Hills
does as well as their request for additional beds, but he is not in support of the rezone to
Commercial. It will create a spot zone for uses not compatible with surrounding
properties. It is not in Paradise’s future annexation area and must be served by a
suitable road. Mt.Pisgah Road is not suitable. ~ Little also cited septic concerns.

Council member Borup asked if any other zoning was possible. Harrild said no. Vice
Chair Worthen asked if the County Code reflects the state code. Harrild responded yes.
Council member Erickson remarked he worries about a commercial zone in that area.
Mayor Little agreed noting that if the property is sold, Paradise would have no control.

Applicant Bryon Bostrum said he understands that improvements on the Mt. Pisgah
Road are “on the radar.”

Vice Chair Worthen agreed with Council member Erickson’s remarks. Avalon Hills
performs a needed service, but Worthen worries about putting a commercial district in
that area.

(Attachment 2)

ACTION: Motion by Vice Chair Worthen to deny Ordinance No. 2020-11-BGTS Properties
Rezone. Erickson seconded the motion. The motion passed 4 aye — Erickson, Tidwell,
Worthen & Zilles and 2 nay — Borup & Ward. White absent.

e Discussion and Adoption — Trails Cache Committee Bylaws — Trails Coordinator

Carly Lansch presented the bylaws. Vice Chair Worthen and Council members Tidwell
and Erickson questioned the selection of committee members. Council member Borup
asked if it is correct that there are only two Logan seats rather than three as on the
County Council. Lansch said it is correct. Worthen suggested language similar to: “The
advisory membership which elects the committee cannot choose a person from their own advisory
organization.” Lansch will rework the bylaws and return for future Council approval.
Worthen thanked her for her hard work on the bylaws.

e Discussion — Torgersen/Zollinger Potential Annexation — Tayler Jensen indicated

the parcel in question is looking to annex into River Heights. The parcel is currently an
island and annexing creates three islands. It is within the River Heights annexation plan,
but only a small portion of it is contiguous to River Heights. Chairman Ward asked why
those properties in the three islands that would be created are not interested in
annexation, Dallas Torgersen said there was an attempt by one of the parcels to annex
to Providence, but there were issues and it did not go forward. Providence City owns
one of the parcels and the owner of the other property is not interested in annexation.
Council member Erickson asked who will maintain 1000 East. There is no agreement on
road maintenance yet. Ron Zollinger said there have been conversations with River
Heights which has intentions of taking responsibility for the road. Council member Zilles
stated information is needed about who will take responsibility for the road before a

Ordinance No. 2020-11
Cache County, Utah

BGTS Properties Rezone

An ordinance request to amend the County Zoning Map by rezoning 5.91 acres of property
from the Agricultural (A10) Zone to the Commercial (C) Zone

Whereas, the “County Land Use Development and Management Act,” Utah Code Ann.
§17-27a-101 et seq., as amended (the “Act”), provides that each county may enact a land
use ordinance and a zoning map establishing regulations for land use and development; and

Whereas, pursuant to the Act, the County’s Planning Commission (the “Planning
Commission”) shall prepare and recommend to the county's legislative body, following a
public hearing, a proposed land use ordinance and a zoning map, or amendments thereto,
that represent the Planning Commission’s recommendations for zoning the area within the

: county; and

Whereas, the Planning Commission caused notice of a public hearing for the rezone to be
advertised at least ten (10) days before the date of the public hearing in The Herald Journal,
a newspaper of general circulation in Cache County; and

Whereas, on October 1, 2020, the Planning Commission held a public hearing, accepted
all comments, and recommended the approval of the proposed amendments to the County
Council for final action; and

Whereas, the Act also provides certain procedures for the county legislative body to
adopt or reject amendments to the land use ordinance and zoning map for the county; and

Whereas, following proper notice, the County Council held a public hearing on October
27, 2020, to consider any comments regarding the proposed rezone. The County Council
accepted all comments; and

Whereas, the Cache County Council has determined that it is both necessary and
appropriate for the County to amend and implement this ordinance.

Now, therefore, the County Legislative Body of Cache County ordains as follows:
1. Statutory Authority
The statutory authority for enacting this ordinance is Utah Code Annotated Sections 17-
27a Part 1 and Part 3, and 17-53 part 2(1953, as amended to date).
2. Adoption of amended Zoning Map
The County Council hereby amends the County’s Zoning Map to reflect the rezone of the
property affected by this ordinance and hereby adopts the amended Zoning Map with

the amendment identified as Exhibit B, of which a detailed digital or paper copy is
- available in the Development Services Department.
( 3. Conclusions
A. The location of the subject property is compatible with the purpose of the
Commercial (C) Zone as identified under §17.08.030[E] of the Cache County Code
as it: ,
i. Allows for commercial development in an area with minimal residential
development, which may limit conflict or deleterious effects upon
surrounding properties.

| 4. Prior ordinances, resolutions, policies, and actions superseded
This ordinance amends and supersedes the Zoning Map of Cache County, and all prior
ordinances, resolutions, policies, and actions of the Cache County Council to the extent
that the provisions of such prior ordinances, resolutions, policies, or actions are in
conflict with this ordinance. In all other respects, such prior ordinances, resolutions,
policies, and actions shall remain in full force and effect.

5. Exhibits

A. Exhibit A: Rezone summary and information
B. Exhibit B: Zoning Map of Cache County showing affected portion.

, 6. Effective date
This ordinance takes effect on 2020. Following its passage
but prior to the effective date, a copy of the ordinance shall be deposjted with the
County Clerk and a short summary of the ordinance shall be published in a newspaper of
general circulation within the County as required by law.

C) Approved and adopted , 2020.
