Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) for Farm Stand for Plant and Agricultural Sales 1285 W. Old Ranch Road. Parcel PP-119 and CBE-2-AM; Applicant: Kristi Cumming. Owner: JDC Family Investment. Administrative Process. Project #25-072. Ray Milliner, Principal Planner.

Attorney for the Owner
, briefly addressed the Commission. (6:08 P.M.)

Debbie Scoggin was called to speak again at 6:27 p.m.
Commission Chair Mooney closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:28 p.m.
Commission Chair Mooney and Commissioner Nagie provided additional comments to the discussion. (6:28 P.M.)
Matthew Nagie made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit for the Farm Stand for Plant and Agricultural Sales at 1285 West Old Ranch Road subject to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Spencer Young seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
3)
Public hearing and recommendation to the County Council for amendments to the Snyderville Basin Development Code Chapters 10-3-2: General Provisions, 10-3-6: Zoning Variances, 10-9-10: Decision Making and Administrative Bodies, 10-9-22: Appeal Procedures, and 10-11-1 Terms Defined. The purpose of the amendments is to replace the Board of Adjustment with an Administrative Law Judge and to bring the process by which Variances are reviewed into compliance with Utah State Law. Legislative Process. Project #25-098. Ray Milliner, Principal Planner.
(6:30 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Staff Report June 10, 2025.pdf
Commissioner Young recused himself from the item discussion.
Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner,
presented changes to the Code to replace the Board of Adjustment with an Administrative Law Judge.
Commissioners asked detailed questions of Planner Milliner, Deputy Civil Attorney, Lynda Viti, and Community Development Director, Peter Barnes. (6:37 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney opened the meeting for public hearing at 7:22 p.m.
No one came forward to speak.
Commission Chair Mooney closed the meeting for public hearing at 7:22 p.m.
Matthew Nagie made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council for the amendments to the Snyderville Basin Development Code based on the findings of fact and conclusions of law found in the staff report. Tim Jeffrey seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0).  Recuse: Spencer Young.
