MEMO
To: City Council
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2025-05-06
Re: Approval of Resolution #25-19


Comments:

The Utah State Legislature passed Senate Bill 179 (SB 179) in the 2025 legislative session,
with the bill becoming effective on May 7, 2025. This bill requires cities to adopt
ordinances establishing a process for reviewing and approving land use classification
requests to determine if a proposed business use aligns with existing land uses or
constitutes a new or unlisted use. It also requires City Council review of uses that are
found to be a new or unlisted use and the designation of appropriate zones for the use.

Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-050(B) authorizes the Zoning
Administrator to resolve ambiguities in use classifications and determine if a proposed
use is substantially similar to permitted uses. It does not set a process for City Council
review of uses if they are found to be a new or unlisted use.

Resolution #25-19 sets an interim policy for how the City will process use classification
requests until an ordinance amending DCMC Section 9-8-050(B) can be adopted in
compliance with SB 179. Due to public noticing and hearing standards found in Utah
Code Ann. Section 10-9a-205, it is not feasible for the City to adopt a policy before the
May 7, 2025 effective date of SB 179. The City Council should expect to see a text
amendment within the next couple months amending DCMC Section 9-8-050(B) and
bringing the City into full compliance with SB 179.
