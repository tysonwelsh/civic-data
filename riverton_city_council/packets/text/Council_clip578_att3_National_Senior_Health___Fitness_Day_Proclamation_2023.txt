                       National Physical Fitness and Sports Month

WHEREAS, May is designated as Older Americans Month and National Physical Fitness and
Sports Month; and

WHEREAS, it is appropriate to honor our mature citizens for their many contributions to the
vitality and strength of our community; and

WHEREAS, the United State Surgeon General has determined that regular physical activity
results in significant health benefits and improved quality of life for older adults; and

WHEREAS, all older adults can participate in activities that improve and maintain their health;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton City, do hereby proclaim May 31st, 2023,
as National Senior Health & Fitness Day in Riverton City, Utah and urge all our citizens to
support the efforts of local organizations that encourage older adults to enhance their lives
through regular physical activity.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 16th day of May 2023.
