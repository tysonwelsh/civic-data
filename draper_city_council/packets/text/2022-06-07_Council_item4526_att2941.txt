                              RESOLUTION NO. 22-31

    A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING THE FIFTH
AMENDMENT TO THE BIG WILLOW DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

WHEREAS, the City entered into a development agreement with Ivory Development,
L.L.C., dated February 13, 2017, (the Development Agreement”) for the
development of property located at approximately 425 West 11400 South State
Street (the “Property”); and

WHEREAS, the Development Agreement addresses the development of the
Property as a residential development known as Big Willow (the “Project”); and

WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has approved four amendments to the
Development Agreement to address various aspects of the Project; and

WHEREAS, the City and Ivory Development, L.L.C., desire to amend the Agreement
in accordance with the terms and conditions of the Fifth Amendment to the Big
Willow Development Agreement (the “Fifth Amendment”), attached hereto as
Exhibit A, to address access to 11400 South and to the adjacent project known as
Jenson Farms; and

WHEREAS, approving the Fifth Amendment promotes the health, safety and welfare
of the residents of Draper City

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

Section 1. Amended Agreement. The Draper City Council approves the Fifth
Amendment to the Big Willow Development Agreement attached as Exhibit A and
authorizes the Mayor to execute the same on behalf of Draper City.

Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
passage by the City Council.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 7TH DAY OF JUNE 2022.
