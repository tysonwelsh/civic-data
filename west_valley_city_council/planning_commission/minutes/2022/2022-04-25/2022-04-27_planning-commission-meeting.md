BACKGROUND

Mr. Cameron Scott, representing David Weekley Homes is requesting preliminary approval for a
residential development to be known as the Westview Townhomes. The subject property was
rezoned in October 2020 to the RM zone. A development agreement was approved at that time
allowing for both a townhome development and Hotel adjacent to 5600 West. The proposed
development is bordered on the north by the Balmoral Townhomes, the east by a future Hotel and
the south by existing commercial development and storage units. Mountain View Corridor borders
this property to the west.

ISSUES:

Project Overview:

       The Westview Townhomes is a proposed planned unit development consisting of 64 units
       on 5.5 acres. This equates to an overall density of 11.6 units per acre. Proposed townhomes
       will be two-story units with a combination of 3 and 4 plex units. There will be one 5 plex
       unit at the far west end of the development. Per the development agreement, the minimum
       square footage will be 1,500 square feet. The proposed units will be 1530 to 1740 square
       feet. Basements are not proposed here due to a high water table. Per the development
       agreement, all units will have a two-car garage accessed from a drive court with each unit
       having a full driveway. The project site will also have 12 guest parking spaces for a parking
       ratio of 4.2 spaces per unit. This project is vested under older standards and exceeds the
       parking ratio of past developments.

       The architecture for this project is traditional. The materials will be brick, stone, stucco
       and fiber cement siding. The developer is proposing to use 2 different color styles and a
       mix of building materials to vary one building from another. The colored variations will
       need to be substantial enough so that one color does not dominate the project.

       The unit type here is an alley loaded product. The front door is located on a common green.
       Each unit will have a courtyard that will be fenced for an element of limited privacy. Staff
       will work with the applicant on the courtyard designs as the project moves forward.

Other matters related to this project:

Access:

       Access to the subdivision will be gained from 5655 West. Said street is a public access
       easement that serves both the proposed townhomes and hotel to the east. Plans show a
       gated entrance, but the developer is still debating that. An entrance feature will be required
       and will be evaluated during final plat review.
       All streets and drive courts within the development will be private. The main access into
       the development will have 26 feet of asphalt, curb and gutter and sidewalk on both sides
       of the right-of-way. The drive courts extending north and south from the main access road
      will be 20 feet per the Fire Code. Guest parking will be located on the south side of the
      main road.

Grading:

      The site is relatively flat, so staff does not anticipate grading problems. The engineering
      division will review a grading and drainage plan that ensures that water runoff will not be
      a problem for adjacent properties. Staff understands that some elevation change is to be
      expected for drainage and sewer requirements, but staff’s expectation is that the site will
      have a similar grade as it does today when finished. Measures shall be taken to eliminate
      significant grade differences at any property line and fill shall be limited to help with this
      potential problem. Although basements are not planned, a soils report will be required for
      construction purposes.

Building Setbacks:

      Setbacks for this project are illustrated on the site plan. Staff is comfortable with the
      setbacks as illustrated. Setbacks along the south side of the development is 20 feet and the
      north side adjacent to the Balmoral townhomes is 15 feet. Buildings on the north side are
      approximately 20 feet from the right-of-way and approximately 7 feet on the south side.
      Staff would recommend that the southern buildings be shifted south a few feet to have a
      minimum setback of 10 feet to the right-of-way. Setbacks between buildings where the
      courtyards are located will be 36 feet.

Landscaping:

      While landscaping is generally addressed at the final plat stage, staff would like to mention
      a few things at this point in the review process. The overall open space requirement is
      50%. According to the development agreement, this application is allowed to proceed with
      45% landscaping.

      The project site contains a large open space at the west end of the development and one at
      the eastern boundary. Both of these spaces are visible and at the forefront when residents
      and/or guests arrive at this project. The site has good spacing along the perimeter and drive
      courts will have landscaping between driveways. The space between driveways measures
      between 6 and 8 feet. Usually, these spaces are smaller and landscaping is more difficult
      to maintain. Having a wider space is much more desirable.

Site Amenities:

      The developer is required to provide 4 amenities per the development agreement.
      However, the developer is providing 5 amenities for this project. Amenities proposed for
      this development are courtyards for each dwelling, a tot lot, sports court, walking path
      around the entire perimeter and a pavilion near the tot lot. The site has good connectivity
      from each of the units to the trail system which is a great amenity for the community.
Fencing:

      Fencing along the east boundary and at the south boundary adjacent to the car wash will
      be a 6-foot masonry wall. A 6-foot fence which matches the material and style of the fence
      along the north property line will be installed along the west boundary and along the south
      boundary west of the storage units.

Entry Feature:

      This project will require an entry monument. There are a number of locations at the
      entrance that would work for said monument. The developer is working on this item but
      is still in the planning stages. Staff will work with the developer as this project moves
      forward. The entry feature can be shown to the Planning Commission during a future study
      session and/or at such time that the final plat is submitted.

Lighting:

      The project site will street lighting along the main street as well as internal pedestrian
      lighting for the open space areas. Units will have garage lights that are on a photo cell to
      better light the drive courts. The developer is still deciding on a light type, and staff will
      continue working with the developer on this matter. A lighting plan will be required to
      ensure that appropriate spacing and locations work.

Site Construction:

      To ensure that the private streets are constructed in accordance with standards proposed by
      the developer, staff will require a 3rd party inspection to ensure that these rights-of-way are
      constructed appropriately. In addition, upon the completion of all on site improvements,
      an independent engineering firm will certify that all improvements are completed as shown
      on the approved plans.

Project Overview:

       same property as the Sonrise Baptist Church. The overall project will consist of 33 units
       on a total of 5 acres, with 3.5 acres comprising the townhome portion itself. This equates

       combination 3, 4 and 6 plex units. The square footage of units is rather large at 2,538
       square feet with 760 square feet on the main level, 1025 square feet on the second level
       and 750 square feet in the basement. All units will have a two-car garage with a full
       driveway.

       The architecture for this project is being proposed as a traditional farm style architecture.
       The materials will be brick, stone, and fiber cement siding and stucco. The developer is
       proposing to use two color schemes with white and gray as the predominant colors. Accent
       colors of black will be added for windows, doors and garage doors.

       As mentioned previously, all townhome units will have a two-car garage for parking. In
       addition to the garage, each unit will have a driveway for additional parking. The project
       will include an additional 24 spaces for guest parking, bringing the total parking ratio to
       4.5 spaces per unit.

       The project is being proposed as a front load product. The back of the units (which actually
       look like the front of the units) will face on to the common green with a patio space adding
       to the feel of the community.

Godfrey West Valley Phase
II 2466 S 5900 W
M Zone (16.54 acres)


Sansone Group is requesting conditional use approval for a warehouse building at 2466 S
5900 W. This property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley City General Plan
designates the area as Light Manufacturing. The zoning is also M for adjacent parcels to
the north, east and west. The zoning to the south is Agriculture. The surrounding area is
undeveloped to the north and south. There is a Rocky Mountain Power Corridor to the
east, and warehouse uses to the west.

The proposed development is the second phase of a larger Sansone project. Phase I was
approved in February of last year. That building is currently under construction. The Phase II
proposal includes a 289,100 sq. ft. warehouse building, which will accommodate up to four
tenants. The building will be constructed primarily of tilt up concrete. This facility is not
required to meet the Commercial Design Standards due to the use being limited to a
manufacturing zone, however, there are three design-related requirements outlined in the
development agreement for the property (see attached), which the proposed building meets.

There are two new roads currently under construction adjacent to the site. Beagley Road is
being extended to the east and 5900 West is being constructed on the east side of the site.
There will be an access to the site from Beagley Road, which was approved on Phase I of
the project. The applicant is proposing one additional entrance in the north-east corner of
the property on 5900 West, however, the Public Works Department has indicated that the
proximity of the access is too close to 2400 South. The applicant will need to work with
Public Works for the location of a second access.

The proposal places customer and employee parking on the east side of the building and the
truck and trailer loading and parking on the west side of the building, which is a mirror image
of Phase I. There is a drive aisle proposed on the south side of the building that connects the
Phase I access from Beagley Road to the customer and employee parking on the east side of
the building. The applicant is proposing 192 customer and employee parking stalls on the
site. The exact number of parking stalls required is based on the proposed uses and square
footages in the building, which will be monitored as Tenant Improvement permits are
reviewed. The Lee Creek drainage channel runs along the east edge of the site.

The landscaping requirement in the M zone is 5%. The landscaped area between Beagley
Road and the drive aisle needs to incorporate a 3’ berm for screening. The area south of the
truck court shall be screened with similar plant material as provided in Phase I. The code
requires 20’ of landscaping, which incorporates a 3’ berm, between 5900 West and the
proposed parking area. The Lee Creek Channel running along the east edge of the property
will have
native vegetation, and then the applicant is proposing a 10’ formal landscaped area between
that and the parking. At the narrowest point, there is about a 50’ setback from the property
line to the parking. Staff suggests that in lieu of the berm requirement and 20’ of formal
landscaping, the applicant provides shrubs that will grow 3’ tall and that are spaced closely
enough to create a hedge row to screen the parking area. Street trees shall be provided along
the entire frontage, with one tree provided every 30’.

No signage is being proposed at this time. Any future signage on the property or building will
need to comply with Title 11.


Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing and
the following conditions.

    1.  The building and site shall be constructed per the approved plans.
    2.  All requirements of the Development Agreement on the property shall be met.

    7. All other landscaping shall be completed in accordance with applicable standards in

Brad Jones Outside
Storage 2181 S 7200 W
M Zone (2.0 acres)

Brad Jones is requesting conditional use approval for outside storage for a landscaping
products business. The property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley City
General Plan designates this area as heavy manufacturing. The surrounding zoning is also M.
Adjacent land uses include other outside storage and light industrial businesses. The
properties located on the west side of 7200 West are located in Salt Lake City.

The applicant operates a landscape products yard where he processes and recycles materials to
re-sell. This includes things like gravel, decorative rock, sand, topsoil, and compost. The
landscape products are processed and then stored in large material bays. Per 7-7-119, outside
storage areas are to be screened from public view. There is existing chain link fencing along

Surfacing for outside storage uses is also evaluated by the Planning Commission. The
applicant is proposing to pave the access and all parking and maneuvering areas. A large area
in the north- east corner of the property will remain unimproved for materials processing. The
applicant has indicated this area needs to remain as it is because of the tract equipment that is
used for sifting and processing the different material.

There are two existing buildings on the property. The applicant would like to use one building
as an office/retail space and the other as a storage building. The applicant will need to work
with the Building Inspections Division to ensure the buildings are properly permitted and meet
all applicable building codes. The applicant has also noted that he may want to have some
retail sales out of the building. All parking areas on the property need to be paved, and the
number of parking spaces required for the site will be based on the square footages of the
buildings and the proposed uses in the buildings.

The property is required to have 5% landscaping on the site. The Public Works Department
has indicated that additional right-of-way dedication is required along 7200 West. The
