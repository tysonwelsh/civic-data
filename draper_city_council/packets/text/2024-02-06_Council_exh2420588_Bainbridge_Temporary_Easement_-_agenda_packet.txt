                              RESOLUTION NO. 24-08

          A RESOLUTION RECORDING A TEMPORARY EASEMENT ON DRAPER
          CITY PROPERTY LOCATED AT 12460 S 1130 E FOR THE
          CONSTRUCTION OF A PUBLIC TURNAROUND TO SERVICE
          BAINBRIDGE SUBDIVISION PHASE 1.

      WHEREAS, Ivory Development, LLC (“Ivory”) intends to develop Bainbridge
Subdivision (“Bainbridge”); and

      WHEREAS, 1060 E in Bainbridge Phase 1 dead ends at vacant property, parcel
NO. 28-29-452-029-0000, owned by Draper City (“City”); and

       WHEREAS, a development cannot dead end without proper turnaround
capacity for the provision of city services, such as, but not limited to, emergency
services, sanitation, and public right-of-way; and

      WHEREAS, Ivory has requested to the City record a temporary turnaround
easement (“ Temporary Easement”) on City property as depicted in Exhibit A attached
hereto; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City will receive consideration in the amount $4,095.20 for
the Temporary Easement.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

      Section 1. Temporary Easement. The City of Draper grants a Temporary
Easement on its property as provided in Exhibit A. Final survey quality control may
make required technical adjustments to the exhibits prior to construction of the
turnaround.

     Section 2. Agreement. The Mayor is authorized to sign any agreement or
document necessary to effectuate the grant of and payment for the Temporary
Easement.

       Section 3. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution and all provisions, clauses and words of this


Resolution No. 24-08                       1   Temporary Easement for Bainbridge Subdivision Phase 1 turnaround
Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 3. Effective Date.      This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THE 6th DAY OF FEBRUARY, 2024.

          TEMPORARY TURNAROUND AGREEMENT AND EASEMENT

       THIS TEMPORARY TURNAROUND AGREEMENT AND EASEMENT
("Agreement") is made this _____ day of _________, 2024, by and between Draper City, a
Utah municipal corporation located at 1020 East Pioneer Road, Draper, Utah (“Draper”) and
Ivory Development, LLC, a Utah limited liability company located at 978 Woodoak Lane,
Salt Lake City, Utah 84117 ("Ivory").

       A.      Ivory is developing the Bainbridge subdivision located at approximately 1085
East 12650 South, Draper, Utah. To comply with Section 503.2.5 of the International Fire
Code regarding dead-end streets exceeding 150’ in length, a temporary turnaround needs to
be constructed at the north end of what has been designated as 1060 East on the approved
Bainbridge Phase 1 final plat.

        B.     Draper owns property adjacent to the northern terminus of 1060 East and is
willing to grant itself a temporary easement on its property for the construction of a public
temporary turnaround pursuant to the terms stated in the easement attached hereto as Exhibit
1.

       NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy
of which is hereby acknowledged, Grantor and Grantee agree as follows:

      1.      Grant of Easement. Draper hereby grants, transfers, and conveys to itself a
temporary easement located on its property as stated in the easement attached as Exhibit 1.

       2.       Construction and Removal of Turnaround. Ivory agrees to install, maintain,
operate, repair, inspect, protect, remove and replace the turnaround at its own expense. The
turnaround shall meet fire code standards.

        3.      Payment. Ivory agrees to pay Draper upon execution hereof the sum of four
thousand ninety-five dollars and 20 cents ($4,095.20) for the approximately 3,643.52 square
feet (0.084 acres) required for the easement, which was calculated at $11.24 per square foot
representing 10% of the appraised land value per Salt Lake County Assessors records at
$489,600.00 per acre. The 10% figure is the same as has been paid and received by Draper
City in other temporary easement agreements.

        IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the
date first appearing above.
