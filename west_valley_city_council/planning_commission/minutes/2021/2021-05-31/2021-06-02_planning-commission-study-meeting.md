ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-4-2021  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zone text change to amend
          standards for single unit dwellings and two-unit dwellings. (Staff – Steve
          Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that a recent bill passed, HB1003, which prohibits Cities
              from having design standards for one to two-family dwellings. He said the design
              standards for prior developments in the City are covered by either having a
              development agreement or would require the property to be rezoned, and a
              development agreement can be added at that time.

              Commissioner Winters said that he fails to see the benefit of restricting
              development standards. Steve said the staff was not supportive of this
              amendment. However, they are glad that the standards still apply to townhomes.
              Steve explained that the thought was the cost of housing is already high, and the
              design standards were adding to the price.

GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-11-2021 Petition by VAN DAELE HOMES OF UTAH INC. requesting a General Plan
            change from General Commercial to Medium Density Residential (7 to 12
            units/acre) and a zone change from C-2 (General Commercial) to RM (Multiple
            Unit Dwelling Residential). The property is located at 3688 South Redwood Road
            on 24.9 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-
            ut.gov)

               Discussion: Steve Pastorik stated that the property in question is the old
               Redwood Drive-in. He said the applicant is proposing to leave approximately an
               acre of land on the northeast corner of the property zoned commercial, and the
               balance would be residential. Steve explained that a swap meet is currently
               running on the subject property. He then showed photographs of the subject
               location followed by the proposed concept plan.
               Commissioner McEwen asked if there is only one access off of Redwood Road.
               Steve pointed out that there are two proposed entrances.

               Steve said the applicant is proposing a multi-family development with two
               hundred and ninety-nine units at a density of twelve units/acre. Steve started a
               traffic study has been done. However, it was based on the applicant's first concept
               plan that depicted four hundred and eighty-five units at twenty units/acre.
               Therefore, the traffic study is showing higher numbers than the proposed concept
               plan.

               Steve discussed the three different housing plans proposed, townhomes,
               bungalows, and twin homes. While describing the designs of the various housing
               types, Steve mentioned that adjustments are needing to be made with things such
               as roof pitch and window size to meet code. Steve said the townhomes would be
               two and three stories with 5,6, or 7 units. Every unit will have a two-car garage.
               There is an overlap between the first and second floors and three central units. He
               explained the bungalows would consist of a pod of three units, two units in the
               front and one over the garage in the back. These units will have a fenced-in
               courtyard between the garage and the two units in the front. Lastly, Steve said the
               townhomes do not have shared walls in the living space. The one shared wall is
               part of the garage.

               Commissioner McEwen asked what the benefit would be for a twin home. Steve
               said the size of the townhomes are larger, and the area around the twin homes is a
               shared space maintained by the HOA. He said there is a private fenced-in
               backyard that the occupant would maintain. Commissioner McEwen said he is
               glad the HOA will be maintaining the property. He feels most of the twin homes
               throughout the City are not well kept. Steve explained another reason could be a

SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SA-4-2021 Petition by Petition by MERIDIAN ENGINEERING, representing the Utah
          Department of Transportation, requesting approval for the Hunter Cove
          Mountain View Corridor Subdivision. This plat will amend lots 1, 2, 21 & 22
          and a portion of Rushford Court, and will vacate lots 3-8 & 26-35, and Timber
          Oaks Lane within the Hunter Cove Subdivision Phase 1. This plat also amends
          Parcels B & C of Hunter Cove Phase 2 Amended. The application is being
          requested to resolve various modifications to the original subdivisions resulting

          approximately 5920 West 4165 South and is zoned R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling
          Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-
          963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

               lots, vacate various rights-of-way, and dedicate numerous rights-of-way,

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-11-2021 Petition by PREMIER TRUCK GROUP OF UTAH requesting a conditional use
          for truck sales storage. The property is located at 2377 S. 5370 W. (Lot 9) on 2.0
          acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497
          or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

               Discussion: Jody Knapp explained that the subject property is undeveloped, and
               the applicant proposes developing the property for outside truck storage. She
               stated that the overall truck center was approved in the mid-’90s, and
               improvements were different. She explained the priority was a broad road to
               accommodate large trucks, so sidewalks were not required. Jody explained this
               difference would place the sidewalk on the subject property. She stated that they
               propose putting up a six-foot chain-link fence with barbed wire on the top.

C-12-2021      Petition by ANDERSON WHALEN & ASSOCIATES, on behalf of Ken Graff
               Automotive Group, requesting conditional use approval for a truck processing
               center. The property is located at 4770 S 5600 W on 36.53 acres and is zoned M
               (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
               brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

               Discussion: Brock Anderson explained that the applicant proposes using the
               subject location to process, outfit, and complete trucks for their factory. He
               stated that they are planning to use the property as-is for now. However, they
               might look to expand, and that proposal would come back to the Commission
               for approval. Brock stated the applicant had not proposed changes to the outside
               of the building. He said that they would be removing some of the landscaping
               behind the building.

C-13-2021      Petition by TISHA MORENO, requesting conditional use approval for a
               construction office and outside storage yard. The property is located at
               7041/7055 W Gates Ave on 0.88 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff –
               Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

               Discussion: Brock Anderson explained that the subject property was recently
               rezoned from A to M. He stated an enforcement case had initiated the proposal
               to rezone the property. Brock noted the subject property is surrounded on three
               sides by residential uses. He explained that the business is looking to use the
               existing home as an office and the remainder of the property for a
               construction/staging area, and there will be four to six employees on site. Brock
               displayed the concept plans to the Commission and explained that they need to
               be updated. The subject property needs to comply with the standard for the M
               zone and the development agreement that was part of the rezoning. Brock noted
               that the applicant is proposing a 10-foot setback, and it will need to be 20 feet to
               comply with the standards. He stated that they are required to have 20 feet of
               landscaping between the subject property and the surrounding residential areas
               and a masonry wall. Brock explained the landscaping and wall would need to be
               added to their plans. He told the Commission that if the updated plans are not
               submitted before the public hearing next week, they can continue this
               application to acquire updated plans and answers for how they are going to
