         The purpose of the Joint Work Session was to review the agenda for the City Council meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00
p.m.; discuss proposed Ogden Municipal Code Title 8 amendments; receive a Redevelopment Agency Administrative update;
discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board comments.

Agenda Review
        Chair White and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Proposed Ogden Municipal Code Title 8 Amendments
          Community and Economic Development Executive Director Johnson, Airport Director Condie, and Ryan Leick of
Ascension Group Partners introduced phase two of the proposed Title 8 amendments related to airport lease policies. The policies
currently reside in Title 8, Chapters 3 and 5 with Chapter 3 primarily addressing commercial activity and Chapter 5 addressing
rates and charges. The current version of Chapter 3, Commercial Activities, was updated in 2021 and primarily addressed leases.
Changes to Chapters 1 and 5 were included in the 2021 amendments as well. As with the minimum standards proposal from the
first phase of the changes, the primary request from Administration is to move large sections of Title 8, Chapters 3 and 5 of the
City’s Code of Ordinances (legislative code) to Title 11 of the Administrative Code. If moved, the Administrative Code, acting as
Primary Management Documents, would set forth the lease policies of the City. Mr. Condie used the aid of a PowerPoint
presentation to expound on the shared responsibilities of Administration, the Mayor, and City Council if the proposed
amendments are approved. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Council discussion of the information provided by Mr. Condie centered on the financial implications – if any – of the
proposed adjustments to lease policies; lease terms; aligning any current or future leases with the guidelines established by the
Federal Aviation Authority (FAA); the manner in which lease rates will be determined and the frequency with which they will be
reviewed; including minimum maintenance standards in new hangar leases for the Airport, as well as enforcement of those
standards; and the Council’s desired amendments to a proposed ordinance that would adjust Title 8.

Redevelopment Agency Administrative Update
       Community and Economic Development Executive Director Johnson used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to
provide an update regarding recent Redevelopment Agency activities, including:
               • Updating the Redevelopment Agency Mission, Goals, and Values;
               • Preparing the Redevelopment Agency annual summary (to be presented to the Board on September 16);
               • Conducting a request for proposals (RFP) process for development of a Housing Fund Policy;
               • Staffing adjustments.
          Recent priority projects for the Redevelopment Agency include the Alpha Malakite Development in the Adams
Community Reinvestment Area (CRA); the Williams International expansion and reviewing potential new companies with site
selectors in the Airport CRA; development activities on the Ogden River and Trackline CRAs, and development of new CRAs or
Housing and Transit Reinvestment Zones (HTRZs) in the community.
          Discussion between the Board and Mr. Johnson centered on specific development projects in the various project areas he
referenced.
