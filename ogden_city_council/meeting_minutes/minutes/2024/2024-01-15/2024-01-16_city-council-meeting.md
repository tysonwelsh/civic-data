Celebrating the 70th Anniversary of Ogden’s Sister City Relationship with
Hof, Germany; and
Joint Resolution recognizing the 70th Anniversary of Ogden’s Sister City
Relationship with Hof, Germany
          A Joint Resolution regarding the 70th Anniversary of Ogden’s Sister City relationship with Hof, Germany came before
the Council for consideration.
          Chair Choberka and Mayor Nadolski provided comments welcoming Hof guests and elected officials, after which the
Council heard from Hof Lord Mayor Eva Döhla.
          Council members spoke of their appreciation for the continued relationship with Hof, Germany and of past experiences
related to the sister City relationship.
          Hof, Germany Council members also introduced themselves and discussed the impact the sister City relationship has had
on them as well.

       Council member Blair read the joint resolution, after which COUNCIL MEMBER BLAIR MOVED TO APPROVE
THE JOINT RESOLUTION. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY VICE CHAIR RICHEY, ALL VOTING AYE.
      Chair Choberka then presented the Joint Resolution to the officials from Hof, Germany.

Public Comments
           Linda Sanchez stated she was impressed by the information provided by the Harvard students during tonight’s work
session. She then offered a suggestion to the Council regarding their public hearing procedures; she suggested that an action not be
taken on an item on the same night that the public hearing is held, but instead that multiple public hearings be held or that action
be scheduled for a later date following a public hearing. She then reported the water coming from the water fountain outside of
Council Chambers is white and milky.
           Adrienne Zubiller stated she was excited to hear from the visitors from Hof, Germany as her family is from Bavaria. She
then echoed Ms. Sanchez’s recommendation regarding public hearings; residents are disappointed when the Council does not
listen to the input provided during a public hearing. They are insulted when the Council takes action on a matter immediately after
a public hearing is closed and the action is contrary to the input provided by the public. She stated she worked for the federal
government and would be happy to consult the Council on their policies surrounding public hearings.
           Angel Castillo thanked the Council for hearing from Wynter Storm at the beginning of the meeting. She thanked Mayor
Nadolski for attending the Martin Luther King, Jr. Day celebration events and noted that City Administration has not been
represented in those events in the past. She offered a ‘shout out’ to the City’s Public Works Department due to their response to
the recent winter storm events; they have cleared the City’s streets of snow in a quick and efficient manner, but she suggested the
City look into purchasing equipment to be added to plow blades that will prevent snow from being stacked in driveways. She
noted she would be interested in learning if there were any challenges in enforcing the recently adopted ordinance regarding on-
street parking during winter months. She would also like an update on the status of the lawsuit dealing with Ogden Airport
hangars and the extension of the contract with the Northern Utah Wildlife Rehabilitation Center.
           Council member Hyer addressed the comments regarding the Council’s public hearing procedures. He stated it is his
hope that people do not wait until a public hearing to provide their thoughts; the Council accepts public comments at every
meeting or via email at any time. A public hearing is a statutory requirement, but it is not the first opportunity for public input. He
added, however, that he is not opposed to a policy change that would delay action on an item until a later date following a public
hearing. Council Executive Director Eller-Smith noted that change has been made for Council actions required on the annual
budget. Chair Choberka added that there have been instances where the Council has delayed action on an item due to the input
provided during a public hearing.

Mayor Comments
          Mayor Nadolski stated that he will be meeting with all Department Directors later this week as part of a three-day budget
retreat and he will ask for an update regarding enforcement of the winter parking ordinance at that time. He shared that he had a
chance to meet with the employees of the Public Services Department and he was also sure to thank the snowplow drivers who are
exhausted, but also preparing for forecasted storms. He advised those that have the opportunity to pull their vehicles into their
driveways to do so before snow starts again. He added he is not prepared to provide information regarding the Airport hangar
lawsuit tonight.

Council member Comments
         Council members provided additional comments regarding their support for continuing the sister City relationship with
Hof, Germany and thanked Council staff for their efforts to coordinate the many events that will be held in the coming week to
celebrate the 70th Anniversary of the relationship. They also commended Public Services employees for their quick response to
snowstorms; but they engaged in discussion with Administration regarding the party that is responsible for removing snow from
sidewalks and at intersections to ensure that they can be navigated by pedestrians and those in wheelchairs.
