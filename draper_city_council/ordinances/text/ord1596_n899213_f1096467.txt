City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On March 12, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1596, amending
the Official Zoning Map of Draper City, otherwise known as the Tunbridge Zoning Map
Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office
and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 13th day of March 2024. Laura
Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
