Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: June 6, 2023
Re: Resolution 23-27 Disposing of Lost or Mislaid Property Comments: The Police Department possesses unclaimed property in the form of bikes that are lost or mislaid property. Utah Code Sections 77-11d-104 and 105 outline the procedure for disposing of lost or mislaid personal property.  The Police Department has followed the procedures to try and indentify the owners of the property but have not been able to do so.
The Police Department wishes to apply the property described herein to a public interest use. The Police Department has identified the WARD Foundation as a public interest use. The WARD Foundation is a 501(c)(3) charity that provides aid, relief and development opportunities for refugees living in Draper and along the Wasatch Front.
