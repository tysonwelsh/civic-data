     APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING HELD NOVEMBER 19, 2019

ELECT CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FOR 2020
Chair Fitisemanu stated election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson for 2020 had been
scheduled for consideration by the Board. He advised a rotation schedule had been used
in the past to elect a Chair and Vice Chair, however this was an option for the Board and
not mandatory.

Chairman Fitisemanu called for a motion.

Mr. Buhler moved to elect Don Christensen as Chair of the Redevelopment Agency for
2020 and Karen Lang as Vice Chair of the Redevelopment Agency for 2020. Ms. Lang
seconded the motion.

A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.


MOTION TO ADJOURN
Upon motion by Mr. Huynh all voted in favor to adjourn.


THERE BEING NO FURTHER BUSINESS OF THE WEST VALLEY
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY THE ANNUAL MEETING OF TUESDAY, JANUARY
7, 2020, WAS ADJOURNED AT 6:48 P.M. BY CHAIRMAN FITISEMANU.
