WORK SESSION
Ms. Steck presented budget amendments recommended to reflect new or revised activity in FY 2020.

       Revenues
   •   Tax Increment: The Agency has received its final distribution of tax increment for tax year 2019
       in the amount of $4,287,121. Reduce the budget by ($74,648) to correspond to actual receipts.
   •   Interest earnings: As a best practice, the Agency does not include a budget for interest earnings
       until actual receipt. The Agency estimates receipt of $186,594 of interest earnings by year end.
       Increase the budget by $186,594.
   •   Transfer from the General Fund: Economic Development Area #1 (Dannon) has expired with
       an outstanding balance of ($86,989). There is no revenue source to make up this deficit, therefore,
       the General Fund will need to transfer funds to the RDA Fund to bring the balance to zero and
       close the area. Increase the budget by $86,989.

       Expenditures
   •   Professional & Technical Services: The Agency does not estimate any professional & technical
       services will be used by year end. Reduce the budget by ($90,000).
   •   Participation Agreement: Increase the budget by $1,337,037.
           o On August 14, 2019, the RDA Board adopted Resolution 218 approving a participation
               agreement with Amazon. Increase the budget by $1,575,000.
           o The budget for the participation agreement with Aligned Energy and Paypal was in excess
               of the eligible amount for tax year 2019. Reduce the budget by ($245,850).
           o The budget for the participation agreement for Jordan Station is based on a reimbursement
               of taxes paid. This eligible reimbursement for tax year 2019 was greater than the budget.
               Increase the budget by $7,887.
   •   Low-Income Housing: The Agency does not estimate any low-income housing projects in
       excess of the current YTD expenses. Reduce the budget by ($291,477).
   •   Area Improvements: The Agency does not estimate any area improvements for this fiscal year.
       Reduce the budget by ($2,385,985).
   •   Recruitment & Marketing: The Agency does not estimate any expenses in this account for this
       fiscal year. Reduce the budget by ($2,500).
   •   Property Acquisition: On July 17, 2019, the RDA Board adopted Resolution 217 approving the
       purchase of 30.1 acres of vacant real property for purposes of development. Increase the budget
       by $7,000,500.
   •   Interest: The Purchase agreement for the property referenced above had terms over 18 months
       with an annual interest rate of 2.5%. Increase the budget by $120,000.

The total change in the budget was an increase of revenue in the amount of $111,946, an increase of
expense in the amount of $5,687,575, and a transfer in the amount of $86,389 for a net use of reserves
