3. REPORT OF ELECTED OFFICIALS:
3.1. Council Members
Council Member Stringham  invited Pam Roberts, Director, Wasatch Front 
Waste and Recycling District to give a report of what the district is doing.
Ms. Pam Roberts , Director, Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District 
(WFWRD), stated WFWRD ’s Administrative Control Board of Directors has 
tentatively adopted its tentative 2023 budget, which includes a residential fee 
increase of $ 2.50 per home per month to the valley residents, and $ 1.25 per home 
per month for the town of Brighton and Big Cottonwood.  The difference between 
the two fees is WFWRD does not provide curbside service in Big Cottonwood; it 
only provides front load -front load dumpsters and trash compacters there.  The 
board will be holding a public hearing next Monday, November 14th, at 6:00 PM, at 
its administrative office, located in the Salt Lake County Public Works Building at 
604 West 6960 South.  A Webex link will be available on the Public Notice 
Website.
