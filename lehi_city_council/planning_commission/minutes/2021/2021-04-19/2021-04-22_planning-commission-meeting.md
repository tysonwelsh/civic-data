3. Regular Agenda

3.1. Public hearing and recommendation of Cole Peck’s request for review of the Lakeview
Land and Rock General Plan Amendment at approximately 600 East 600 South, changing
the designation from LDR (low density residential) to LI (light industrial) on
approximately 12.81 acres.

    Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

    The applicant, Clay Peck, was present and answered questions from the Commissioners.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment. He acknowledged the
    comments that were submitted through ecomment and email.

    Benjamin Lee stated he borders the property and will be negatively affected by the zone
    change. He feels the Pecks have good intentions but feels when a company is given leeway to
    build what they want you must assume the worst. He stated the majority of the homes along
    the westside do not support the rezoning on the property. Some of the negative effects
    include losing the small town feel in the neighborhood that is built with people, not
    businesses. Most of the comments he has read are based on LDR. One of the biggest
    problems is that there is a housing crisis right now. He stated he would be at a loss because of
    the housing crisis right now with the rezoning. Pollution, noise, large buildings, and the
    concrete wall are all things that he does not want in his back yard.

    Wade Lott owns the parcel to the north. He has a parcel of property that borders the change
    and is not planning on doing anything with his property right now but knows it will affect his
    property. He also expressed his concern about the traffic and cars near Broadmoor Park.

    Jeremy Thomas is a local business owner and recently met the Pecks after trying to find a
    place to park his truck. He is here to support the light industrial as part of being a small
    business owner, knowing his neighbors wouldn’t want him to talk to park in his
    neighborhood. As a person with a need, he can’t find anywhere to park and would hope that
    he would have the support for businesses and to be economically where we need people who
    are in this type of business to build a develop.
    Melissa Peck stated the property was acquired by her dad and uncle. She is also representing
    Jerry and Nikki Hunter who can’t be here tonight. They have been surrounded by light
    industrial for thirty years and have lived in Lehi her entire life. She understands that Lehi is a
    small town and people want to be here for the small-town life, but she is fine with the light
    industrial. She is concerned with water and stated with more homes comes more water usage.
    She stated when Broadmoor was put in the water pressure went down and is concerned if
    more housing is put in that it will be even worse. She wanted to second Wade Lotts comment
    about the traffic and parking on the road to Broadmoor being too congested. She is 100%
    against housing and residential if it is annexed into Lehi.

    Jay Bodine stated his mother in law is closer to Pecks and she is in favor of Pecks zoning
    light industrial. He stated Broadmoor has great people, but it has created a traffic and parking
    issues. He stated the plan for the annexation is good for the general wellbeing of the City.

    Bryan Max is a small business owner and does flooring. He has been looking for a light
    industrial place to rent to park is trailers and hasn’t been able to find a place. When he heard
    there may be a place to rent, he was glad.

    Megan Butler stated she owns property near the Pecks. She does not want a lot of homes in
    the area because they would not have the correct slopes for sewage. She said appraisals of
    homes aren’t really a factor because homes are selling quickly. She does want it to look nice
    as her family prides themselves in that. They had the idea to make Lehi High School a Jr
    High and rebuild the High school. She is in favor of the zone change.

    Bart Anthony does understand the need for light industrial and the two people that talked
    about the need to generate revenue. He expressed his approval for the Peck’s business and for
    the zone change.

    Alexa Jarrett stated her property touches the Pecks. She also wanted to note that a lot of
    mom’s couldn’t be here tonight because they have young families. She is concerned that a
    blank check without having a plan and saying have at it. She is concerned that their
    livelihood will be affected for the worse. She is also concerned about the traffic and noise
    from the light industrial zone change.

    Liz Anderson stated she understands the concern of the community, but she knows the need
    of light industrial and the need for the businesses. She stated the west side of the property is
    the only side that is being affected.

    Jennifer stated her property borders the Pecks and feels that there are a lot of people now that
    will border the Peck property, existing and future. Her point is that a lot of homes and
    homeowners are going to be affected. She feels that the applicant has used scare tactics. She
    talked about Broadmoor park not effecting the new plan and would like homes built. She is
    not in favor of the light industrial and annexation.


    Darren Jones stated he is in favor of the light industrial. He understands that residential is
    important but also knows that light industrial is important.

    Alaina Allred is in support of the Peck’s. She stated Broadmoor’s is an issue and building
    more homes will create a lot more traffic. She stated the semi’s go slow and feels that having
    more homes will create more congestion. Pecks Trucking is right across the road and so it
    makes sense to have the light industrial across the road. She is in support of the light
    industrial.

    Cole Peck is one of the applicants. He never wanted to have LDR or HDR and bought the
    property to have an expansion of Thomas J Peck. They are trying to find a solution and feels
    that the light industrial is a great buffer. Their machines mostly live on the job and live there
    until the job is finishes unless they need maintenance or are not in compliance.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Hemmert stated there will be appreciable impact on the adjacent properties.
    Commissioner Bunker stated he can see both sides of the homeowner and the applicant. He
    has concerned about changing the zone but likes that the property sits lower and would
    recommend a buffer. Commissioner Bunker stated there is a need for light industrial.
    Commissioner Peterson stated the subdivision to the north has been approved, but to the east
    there is light industrial properties. He stated there is demand for both zones and there could
    be solutions with some sort of a buffer that would include light, sound, and stated he thinks
    something more than a wall would need to do that. Commissioner Peterson stated the
    connectivity through the subdivision should end there and not continue through. He stated a
    lot of that can be worked out through a site plan and an agreement. He would be opposed to
    just a flat zone change without buffering and a plan. Commissioner Everett asked Jacob if the
    east property was City or County? Jacob responded it would be Light Industrial that would
    only be allowed. Commissioner Everett stated he agreed the Broadmoor to the north being
    low density compounds the issue, and agrees if there were a site concept it makes it easier to
    decided, but without that it is harder to decide. Commissioner Eyre stated if 675 South was
    connected, he would feel better about deciding. He understands the concerns the neighbors
    have and is leaning more towards that with the buffers. Commissioner Hemmert reiterated
    they are looking at a general plan amendment, not a site plan. There are seven elements that
    should be considered. Commissioner Peterson does not disagree but believes in some cases it
    makes sense. Commissioner Jackson liked the idea of light industrial and sees this as a
    positive overall. He heard some of the important things as a non-factor for the property
    owner. He sees value to it and if it is in line and feels that it would be good for Lehi.
    Commissioner Hereth agreed with Commissioner Hemmert and felt he would be inclined to
    deny it until there is a better solution. Commissioner Nielsen stated there has been a lot said
    that he agrees and disagree with. He wanted to understand why planning put this on the
    agenda with a negative recommendation. He wanted to reiterate that it was because of
    buffers, which was confirmed. Commissioner Nielsen didn’t know if he agreed with either of
    those statements. He stated it would take and enormous berm to buffer. He also discussed the
    seven factors when reviewing the general plan amendment. He stated that code reads we
    “may” not “shall” take the seven factors into consideration. He discussed negative and
    positive impacts of light industrial and LDR. Commissioner Jackson stated whatever happens
    with the Light industrial the Commission would need to see the plan. He believes he falls in
    favor, even though he does not have a vote tonight. Commissioner Peterson wanted to add
    that he is happy to have the public be able to participate in person again. Commissioner
    Peterson asked Commissioner Nielsen how tall of a wall is needed to be an effective buffer.
    Commissioner Nielsen stated it depends on what is surrounding that area and what is trying
    to be protected and that the buffer is trying to transition the buffer and compatibility. Ross
    Dinsdale pointed out that if it is changed, they would need to meet the landscaping and
    buffering requirement. He stated the 600 east lends itself as a natural buffer right now. Tony
    Trane, Civil Engineer, stated we will come back with a concept with the annexation. They
    are just looking for direction before they come forward with the concept plan and then will
    come forward to City Council.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to send a positive recommendation of Cole
        Peck’s request for review of the Lakeview Land and Rock General Plan
        Amendment at approximately 600 East 600 South, changing the designation from
        LDR (low density residential) to LI (light industrial) on approximately 12.81 acres,
        not including DRC comments and findings. Peterson seconded, adding that we
        recommend some sort of development agreement be pursued to determine site plan.
        Commissioner Bunker agreed with the amendment. Commissioner Peterson second still
        stands.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        no; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, no;
        Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed 5 – 2.

3.4. Public hearing and recommendation of Dale Jones request for preliminary subdivision
review of Jones Legacy Estates, a 2-lot residential subdivision located at approximately 1330
East 600 North in an RA-1 (residential/agriculture) zone.

    The Applicant, Darren Jones, was present. There are two lots that are being annexed into the
    City from the property. There was a policy change that narrowed their lot size down a little
    under half acre. They would like to keep the original variance and will not plant trees in the
    mow strip if they can keep the lot size. They will be able to keep animal rights if they can keep
    the half acre lot size. The new City policy dramatically effects the applicant.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.


    Bart Anthony stated implementing wider park strip and wider sidewalk on a single lot makes
    the most sense and asked that logic prevail.

    Jacob stated there was a public comment on the new engagement platform.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert asked about the timing of the application. Ross Dinsdale stated a
    recommendation can be given by the City Council. Commissioner Peterson stated it was a
    catch .22 and the lot size was lost along with the animal rights. Ross also stated that lot two
    will tie into existing sidewalk, but lot one will need to maintain the new standard, unless
    decided otherwise by Council.

        Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to send a positive recommendation of Dale
        Jones request for preliminary subdivision review of Jones Legacy Estates, a 2-lot
        residential subdivision located at approximately 1330 East 600 North in an RA-1
        (residential/agriculture) zone, including all DRC comments and findings stated in the
        report; recommending an exception for road width and sidewalk width that are consistent
        along 1300 East. Commissioner Hemmert seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth,
        yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
