1.         26-022                 City Council Holding a Council Budget Priorities Workshop

                   Attachments:   Matt Dixon Bio
                                  Budget Workshop Summary
                                  Approved Council Budget Priorities FY25-26

                                  The Council held a Budget Priorities Workshop to review and update the Council's
                                  existing budget priorities leading into the FY2027 budget and beyond. These budget
                                  priorities will be used to assist the Council in evaluating various budget requests.
                                  Evaluations will take place using a simple evaluation tool that will be developed based on
                                  the Council's weighted priorities.

                                  Council Chair Cyndi Sharkey invited Shane Pace, CAO, to provide a brief preview of the
                                  Administration's budget priorities. Mr. Pace mentioned the Mayor was meeting with
                                  community members at the intersection by Alta High School and apologized for not being
                                  present at this evening's City Council meeting.

                                  Mr. Pace said this upcoming budget for FY 2027 will be a hard budget. Public Safety will
                                  be our top priority for the Administration. It also was a top priority for the Council and our
                                  residents. Compensation will also be a top priority for Administration. Compensation has
                                  been very effective in retaining experienced city employees and our first responders in the
                                  Police and Fire Department. We have an aging workforce with over 58 employees who
                                  have reached 30 years. We cannot afford to loose the experienced city staff that work for
                                  them, which means keeping the city's compensation competitive was very important. Mr.
                                  Pace went on to discuss capital projects. While Phase I and II of the Public Works
                                  capital projects have been completed, we still need to complete Phase III. The new
                                  recreation center will open during FY2027. We will need to include the needs of that new
                                  facility in the budget. Additionally, the Administration has asked all departments to cut
                                  their budget by 2% to 4%. Mr. Pace thanked the Council for the opportunity to provide an
                                  overview of the upcoming fiscal budget.

                                  Dustin Fratto, Council Director, introduced the workshop facilitator, Matt Dixon and
                                  provided his background to the Council. Mr. Dixon facilitated our budget workshop last
                                  year and we have invited him back to lead the Council through the workshop this evening.
                                  Mr. Dixon will review with you the budget priorities the Council created last year and
                                  through this evening's workshop, will help you to evaluate, modify and update those
                                  priorities for the upcoming fiscal year.

                                  The workshop began with introductions and a review of the Citys Mission and Vision
                                  statement. Mr. Dixon then reviewed the Council Budget Priorities developed at the prior
                                  year's workshop: Economic Development, Employee Satisfaction, Community Safety,
                                  Quality of Life, and Fiscal Health. Mr.Dixon then walked the Council through the process
                                  to develop a strategic plan to accomplish the Council's initiatives. He reviewed why
                                  priorty-based budgeting matters.

                                  He then moved the Council into a SWOT analysis of the city's budget: Strengths,
                                  Weaknesses (Internal), Threats (External) and Opportunities. The Council presented their
                                  analysis to the group.
