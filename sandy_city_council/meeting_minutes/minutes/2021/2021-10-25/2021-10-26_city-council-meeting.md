3.         21-413                 City Council receiving an update on the Alta Canyon Sports Center
                                  Feasibility Analysis
                   Attachments:   Alta Canyon Feasibility Analysis Presentation.pdf

                                  Brian Parker and Brandon Liebst with AECOM Economics presented their feasibility
                                  study and overview of the redesign of the Alta Canyon Recreation Center. They presented
                                  various options and associated costs. Council questions and comments followed. The
                                  Council and City Attorneys and Jason Burningham with Lewis, Young spoke about the
                                  bonding process for the city.

                                  Council convened a brief recess at 8:37 pm.
                                  Council reconvened at 8:46 pm.

                                  Council moved to Item 4 on the Agenda.

4.         21-408                 Administrative Services Department reporting on a request from the
                                  Waterford School for Conduit Debt Issuance
                   Attachments:   Term Sheet and Conduit Process.pdf

                                  Mr. Bret Neuman introduced Mr. Jonathon Ward with Zions Bank who provided a high
                                  level summary regarding conduit debt issuance associated with Waterford School. Mr.
                                  Ward discussed the process related to conduit financing. Council consideration and
                                  action would be needed to allow for this type of financing. Council questions and
                                  comments followed. Jared Gerber offered additional information. Council is supportive of
                                  Administration moving forward with the process.




5.         21-412                 Council Office Staff Providing a One Year Update on City Council Social
                                  Media
                   Attachments:   Social Media One Year Update

                                  Liz Theriault, Policy and Communications Analyst with the Council Office, provided a one
                                  year update to the Council on the social media. She gave an overview of the initial
                                  objectives of creating a social media presence for the Council Office and provided
                                  statistics on community reach and feedback. Ms. Theriault also spoke about next steps
                                  and goals moving forward. Council questions and comments followed. Council thanked
                                  Ms. Theriault for all her work this past year.

7.         21-414                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Council Chair Stroud invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comments.

                                  Mr. Brian Hanson recommended the city consider adding a skate park to the Alta
                                  Canyon Recreation Center.

                                  Ms. Jessica Hanson spoke about the theraputic benefits of skating and advocated a new
                                  skate park at the Alta Canyon Recreation Center.

                                  Mr. Dan Hughes and his wife, June, supports the addition of a new skate park in Sandy.
                                  The current skate park is their favorite and they would like to see another one built at Alta
                                  Canyon Recreation Center.

                                  Ms. Megan Johnston asked the Council to consider a new skate park into the plans of
                                  the Alta Canyon Recreation Center.

                                  Ms. Jameson Johnston asked the Council to build a new skate park in Sandy City. The
                                  park would help kids get outside and off video games. Sandy City needs another skate
                                  park.

                                  Mr. Rob Johnston advocated for a new skate park. He has been skating for a long time
                                  and would like for the City to look into adding a park.

                                  Ms. Kathy Walker spoke about the fifty foot easement located at Bell Canyon Acres and
                                  the associated law suit. She feels the neighborhood issues have been ignored by the
                                  City.

                                  Ms. Bonnie Stout is a resident in Bell Canyon Acres and expressed concerns with the
                                  trail and easement access and associated legal and neighborhood issues regarding the
                                  easement. She asked for help from the city to resolve the issues associated with the
                                  trail/easement.

                                  Ms. Bee Jenson advocated for indoor tennis courts to be built in Sandy City at the Alta
                                  Canyon Recreation Center.

                                  Ms. Amelia Hohl presented a visual aid and spoke about the need for indoor tennis courts
                                  in the area and asked the Council to consider adding indoor courts to the redesign of the
                                  recreation center. She spoke about the potential revenue from pickleball and tennis
                                  tournaments that indoor courts could bring in.

                                  Ms. Lori Sperry, coach of the Alta tennis team, supports adding indoor tennis courts to
                                  the Alta Canyon Recreation Center. She spoke about the positive impact of tennis and
                                  sports on the kids. We do not have enough tennis clubs in the area.

                                  Mr. Rod Horton has been involved with tennis for over fifty-one years. We need indoor
                                  courts in the area. All the tennis clubs are full.

                                  Mr. Matt Brown advocated for indoor tennis courts. His whole family plays tennis and
                                  they struggle to find indoor courts in the winter time.

                                  Ms. Raquel Passey plays tennis for Alta High School. She spoke about the distance,
