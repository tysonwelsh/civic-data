Findings of Fact:  
1. The proposed project is located on Parcel #SS-67-7 on High View Road.  
2. The property is in the Agriculture - 80 (AG-80) zone.  
3. Parcel #SS-67-7 is approximately 40 acres in size.  
4. A Contractor’s Yard is a conditional use in the AG-80 zone.  
5. Access to the property is from High View Road.  
6. The applicant is proposing to use the yard to store and stage excavation equipment and 
materials as well as processing aggregate materials and topsoil for use with his construction 
business.  
7. The uses in the area are all large lot development with a mix of contractor yard/gravel/rock 
quarry uses, agricultural and residential.  
8. Directly adjacent to the proposed use are contractor and agricultural uses.  
9. The use is proposed on a parcel that has traditionally been used for agriculture. However, it 
has not been used actively for agriculture for a while now.  
10. No sewage or water uses are proposed on site.  
11. There are steep slopes in the area, but none of the proposed development will be located on a 
slope greater than 30%.  
12. None of the development will break the ridgeline.  
13. The applicant is proposing to use 8.6 acres of the 40 for the use, the remaining lot area will 
remain as is.  
14. There are no wetlands on the site.

Eastern Summit County Development Code.  
4. The use is not detrimental to public health, safety and welfare, as the roads and public services 
in the area are sufficient to accommodate the increase in intensity of the use.  
5. The effects of any differences in use or scale have been mitigated through careful planning.

6.  **Continued** Public Hearing and possible recommendation to the County Council 
for an amendment to Chapter 11-4-12 Master Planned Developments. The 
amendment would create language requiring a Development Agreement be part of 
any approval of a rezone. – Ray Milliner, Principal Planner 
 
Planner Milliner shared the background for this application. The recommendation is that 
language be added to the Code that would require a Development Agreement for any 
Master Planned Development (MPD) application that includes a rezone.  
Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
August 5, 2021 
Page 15 of 17 
 
Commissioner Sargent asked about the potential MPDs that are long-term phased 
projects and suggested that there be an option to have a Development Agreement where 
Staff believes it would be appropriate and on any phased development to provide 
accountability. Planner Milliner suggested that language be included that the landowner 
or the applicant may request a Development Agreement. 
 
The Public hearing was opened. No Public Comment was made, and the Public Hearing was 
closed. 
 Findings of Fact:  
1. The goal of Chapter 2 of the Eastern Summit County General Plan is to develop land use 
codes which balance the diversity of desires of Eastern Summit County residents, including 
private property rights.  
2. In furtherance of this goal, §11-1-1 of the Eastern Summit County Code provides that “The 
eastern Summit County general plan was developed to ensure that the rural, agricultural, and 
small-town character of the eastern portion of the county shall remain, even in the presence of 
growth and change. The intention of the county is to assure the managed, proper and sensitive 
development of land to protect and enhance these desired qualities and the lifestyle that exists.”  
3. On June 14, 2017, the County Council adopted Ordinance 868 creating a Master Planned 
Development process of the Eastern Summit County Development Code.  
4. On June 14, 2017, the County Council adopted Ordinance 868 which removed the Specially 
Planned Area process from the Eastern Summit County Development Code.  
5. Summit County has chosen development agreements as the mechanism for approval of MPDs.  
6. There is no specific requirement that an MPD approval have an associated development 
agreement in the Code.  
7. The language in Section 11-6-9 of the Code regarding Development Agreements, still refers to 
specially planned areas.  
 
Conclusions of Law:  
1. The amendment is consistent with the goals, objectives, and policies of the General Plan.  
2. The amendment is consistent with the requirements established in chapter 5 of the Eastern 
Summit County Development Code.  
3. The proposed amendment is not detrimental to public health, safety, and welfare.
