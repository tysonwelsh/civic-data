3. Regular Agenda

3.1 Public hearing and consideration of Mark Bennett’s request for approval of the
Innovation Pointe Building 4 Concept Plan located at 1355 West Innovation Way in a
Commercial zone.

    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    The applicant, Ken Glenn, was present. He discussed the DRC comments and answered
    questions regarding the access and exception requested for grading.

    Opened the public hearing.

    Closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Peterson motioned to grant final approval of the Innovation
        Pointe Building 4 Concept Plan located at 1355 West Innovation Way in a
        Commercial zone, including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Fred Clark’s request for preliminary subdivision
review of Mendenhall Place, an 11-lot residential subdivision located at 230 West 400 South.

    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item

    The applicant, Fred Clark, was present and commented that the lot was below the required 80
    feet and had been corrected.

    Opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Peterson read the public comment via Granicus into the record.

    Closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker motioned to recommend approval of Fred Clark’s
        request for preliminary subdivision review of Mendenhall Place, an 11-lot residential
        subdivision located at 230 West 400 South; including all DRC comments and findings
        stated in the report. Commissioner Peterson seconded.


        Vote: Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

The meeting was passed to Commissioner Nielsen.

3.5 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code amendment to Chapter 37, updating connectivity standards, including a trail
connectivity option, and updating the connectivity indices.
    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item to update
    connectivity standards, add definitions, changing lock length requirements, and adjusting
    index scoring.

    Mike West presented for Staff and Commission as to why the changes are being proposed.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Peterson motioned to recommend approval of Lehi City’s
        request for review of a Development Code amendment to Chapter 37, updating
        connectivity standards, including a trail connectivity option, and updating the
        connectivity indices; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
