 APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 29.020. Criteria for Entering into a Development Agreement.
The city may enter into a development agreement only if the development agreement:

    A. Has been duly adopted by the City Council in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.

    B. The proposed development to which the development agreement pertains is in conformity with the gen-
       eral plan, zoning district regulations, and other applicable requirements of the City.

    C. The proposed development subject to the agreement contains outstanding features that advance the pol-
       icies, goals and objectives of Lehi City, or the property owner agrees to provide capital improvements
       that exceed the development's proportionate share of the costs of the facilities needed to service the
       development, which hereby advance the provision of facilities to serve Lehi City.

  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests review and recommendation of the Hammond Development Agreement. The applicant previ-
ously requested to do a zoning map amendment to their property to bring it into conformance with the Heavy Com-
mercial designation on the General Plan. However, the City Council directed staff to draft a Development Agreement
to allow the zone to stay as Mixed Use and put some guidelines into effect to help with the buffering of the develop-
ment from nearby residential.

A Development Agreement allows for uses other than those normally allowed in the zone. This is a legislative item,
initiated by the City Council. Recent State Legislation requires that these agreements have a public hearing with the
Planning Commission prior to their approval by the City Council.

The DRC reviewed the proposed Development Agreement and had three comments, including the following:

    1.   Building placement encroaches the required PUE space, no permanent structures in PUE. (Need to update
         with the site plan submittal)
    2.   Do things like noise, hours or operation, odors, lighting, building height, etc... need to also be included and
         be more restrictive than the current code?
    3.   The agreement as originally drafted by staff required some landscaping adjacent to the residential to the
         north. However, this was removed at the request of Mr. Hammond because the layout of the property did
         not allow space. The applicant is attempting to preserve the existing trees with this agreement, which would
         provide some landscape buffer.


Please consider all DRC comments in the motion.


  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development Agreements are legislative items at the full discretion of the City Council. The Planning Commission
reviews the Development Agreement and can recommend any changes to the City Council.

The public can also speak to this item and express any concerns or support for the development agreement. The public
can also suggest changes to the language of the agreement.

        A.      City, acting pursuant to its authority under Municipal Land Use, Development, and

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises, covenants, and provisions
set forth herein, the receipt and adequacy of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as
follows:

                                           ARTICLE 1

       1.1     The City agrees, and this Agreement confirms, that Light Office/Warehouse uses
pursuant to the Lehi City Development Code Table of Uses shall be allowed as a permitted use
on the Property (“Approved Use”) as of the Effective Date of this Agreement, subject to the
terms of this Agreement. Under this Agreement the Approved Uses do not include any outside
storage of materials or goods.

        1.2    Developer agrees to install a 6 foot Rhino Rock wall where the Property is adjacent
  to a residential use. The fence will be installed off of the property line (on the Property side) in
  order to help preserve the majority of the existing trees on the adjacent residential use.

       1.3     Developer agrees to construct the building on the western portion of the Property
  (State Street side), with the parking area adjacent to the residential use, to minimize impacts of
  the building (see exhibit B).

       1.4    Developer shall obtain site plan approval through the City’s development process
  for the Approved Use and meet all other applicable requirements of the Development Code and
  Design Standards and Public Improvements Specifications Manual.
