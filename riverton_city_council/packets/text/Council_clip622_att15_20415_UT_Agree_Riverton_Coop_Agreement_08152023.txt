                                                    RECITALS

WHEREAS, UDOT has awarded a design-build contract for the highway project identified as Project Number
S-0154(250)4, Bangerter Highway South Interchanges in Salt Lake County, Utah, (“Project”).

WHEREAS, The Parties agree that the existing Rose Creek crossing of Bangerter and a portion of its
alignment south of 13400 South and 2700 West of Bangerter will be realigned, as part of the Project as shown
in Exhibit A which is incorporated by reference.


NOW THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

    1.   Drainage
             a. UDOT will coordinate Project drainage plans with the City prior to construction.
             b. UDOT will provide drainage facilities to meet the City’s or county’s design standards for City
                roads and/or cross streets within Construction Limits and where Project provides roadway
                improvement to City facilities.
             c. UDOT will not connect to or impact non-UDOT facilities except as allowed by permit, or an
                agreement.
             d. UDOT agrees to obtain floodplain permits required by City Floodplain Administrator.
             e. for 13400 South Interchange Drainage UDOT will:
                       i. Relocate existing Riverton City stormwater trunkline located on the west side of
                          Bangerter Highway and maintain conveyance into Rose Creek. Coordinate with
                          Riverton City and Suburban Land Reserve (SLR) on the SLR Regional Detention
                          Facility Project.
                      ii. Relocate existing privately owned detention facility (located in the southwest
                          quadrant of the Bangerter/13400 South intersection) outside of UDOT ROW and
                          discharge into Rose Creek. Demonstrate relocated facility meets or exceeds
                          existing detention volume and water quality treatment functionality. Provide a
                          relocated and functioning collection, conveyance, and detention system before
                          altering the existing detention facility.
