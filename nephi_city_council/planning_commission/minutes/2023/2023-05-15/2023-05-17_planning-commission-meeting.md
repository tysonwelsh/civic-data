NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION
                          WORK SESSION MINUTES
                           MAY 17, 2023 @ 6:00 p.m.
                         21 E. 100 N. Nephi, UT 84648

Attendance:
Staff: Shauna Talbot
City Council Member: Skip Worwood (7-8p)
Commissioners: John Ford, Fran Petersen (6:30-8p), Ann Peterson, Cory Thomson, Karl
Brough, Heather Roberson, Alan Hancock
Scribe: Shari Cowan
Public: Donald Ball

This is a work meeting to discuss items that were continued at last week’s regular meeting.
Prayer was offered by Ann Peterson.

Shauna started the meeting by discussing land discount percentages and figures to the
Commission. She explained that land discounts would apply to the second and third levels.

Alan asked about parking requirements. Shauna stated that there would still be a parking
requirement per unit. Heather expressed concern over a potential increase in density per acre,
and Shauna agreed that density would increase. She asked if the leftover land would be large
enough to do something with. Shauna stated that it depends on the size of the discount.
Heather also asked if we can require landscaping for the leftover land. Shauna stated that the
city can’t require landscaping on private property. The city can require setbacks, but not
landscaping. Shauna pointed out that Fran brought up the issue of minimum frontage. Alan
stated that Fran had sent him a message expressing that she doesn’t have an issue with
reduced frontage on 8 units per acre.

Shauna says the idea of discounting is to not have excessive open space where ground costs
so much, and to hopefully get to a place where we help with affordable housing. Ann asked the
Commissioners if they were in favor of the idea of a discount. Karl, Fran, and Alan all expressed
interest in the idea. Ann and Heather both expressed concern with increased density, as
compared with the general plan. Alan pointed out that if these areas are scattered throughout
the town, the increase in density won’t be as noticeable. Heather pointed out that the
Commission has already had a conversation about this subject. Cory stated that if there is no
discount, developers will never build vertically.

Shauna showed the Commission some photos that she and Kyle Marchant took of a few
properties with extra land that is not being taken care of. She pointed out that developers are
currently having to piece together land in order to have enough square footage to build on, even
if it leaves extra unusable land.

Ann read a text she received from Skip Worwood that stated, “We (meaning the City Council)
discussed density at length, especially in regards to the general plan. The consensus was to
lower density, not increase it.” Alan expressed concern about pursuing this subject and wasting
time if it is ultimately going to be rejected by the City Council. Karl said that if there is something
the Commission feels strongly about, there’s no reason they can’t go to the City Council with
what they feel. Fran stated that she has gone both ways on this issue, and says she would
rather see us give a discount on a Max 8 unit than to go to 16 units. She also stated that if you
are paying rent & sharing a space with multiple families, you deserve a green space. Alan
pointed out that since an apartment complex makes money from rent, it should be considered a
business and should have landscaping requirements. John asked if apartment house owners
are required to have a business license. Alan confirmed that they do and they also have to have
separate insurance, which is commercial.

Fran brought up the fact that affordable housing is a problem. She feels instead of going 16
units per acre, the compromise may be to give a land discount for building vertically. John feels
that there’s no such thing as affordable housing, to which Alan agreed. Shauna said she will
check into legality for landscaping requirements in the R-3 zone. Heather stated that she is in
favor of a 25% discount, only if there are some landscaping requirements.

Skip expressed that in order to be efficient, the Commission should take one thing at a time. He
feels that Shauna needs to check into the legal requirements for landscaping before any land
discounts are decided upon. Fran stated that she is not concerned with where the State is
heading and feels that the Commission should proceed forward until they are told otherwise.
Ann asked everyone’s opinion regarding the different discount ratio options to get an idea of
which direction to head at the next regular meeting. It was decided that the 50%, 55%, and 60%
options are the most appealing. Alan asked Shauna to have Kyle draw up some schematics of
the different discount ratios to provide a visual for the Commission. Shauna said the required
frontage would be determined based on what is decided for the discount.

Ann asked for a motion to close the work session.
Motion: Cory Thomson Second: Heather Robertson Outcome: Passed unanimously
