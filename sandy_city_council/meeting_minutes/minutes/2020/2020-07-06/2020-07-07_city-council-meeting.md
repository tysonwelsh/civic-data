Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Houseman asked for clarity on the Mayor's budget veto. She noted that
                                  she declined the cost-of-living wage increase.

                                  Council Member Zoltanski asked if there would be a fireworks report, especially about the
                                  fire in Dimple Dell. Mayor Bradburn noted he would request an update from the Fire
                                  Department.

                                  There was a discussion about the implications of the Mayor's veto as it pertains to
                                  elected compensation.

                                  Council Member Nicholl recommended additional fireworks related signs above 1300
                                  East.

Mayor's Report

                                  Mayor Bradburn noted the City would receive $2.86 million in CARES Act funding through
                                  the County.

CAO Report

                                  Deputy Fire Chief Ryan McConaghie reported on a fire in Dimple Dell Park, and other
                                  fireworks related calls. He reported on the effort to install signs in the fireworks prohibition
                                  area.

6.         20-226                 Council to receive public comment on Administration's proposed changes
                                  to the weekly recycling program.
                   Attachments:   2020 Sandy EOW Recycle Proposal
                                  Social Media Plan
                                  2020 EOW Sandy Customer Svc Talking Points
                                  2020 Sandy City EOW Postcard
                                  Sandy City Proposal Fact Sheet
                                  2020 Sandy City Resident FAQ
                                  2020 Sandy City EOW Email

                                  Mike Gladbach introduced the item and reintroduced Blake Leoneli from Waste
                                  Management who gave the presentation.

                                  Dustin Fratto provided instruction about how to comment on this item.

                                  Steve Van Maren expressed concern about increasing the cost of service and thought the
                                  savings should be returned to residents.

                                  Cathy Spuck supported moving to recycling every other week.

                                  Jim Edwards was interested in how the savings calculation was obtained. He
                                  recommended that residents be allowed to opt-out of recycling, and that savings should
                                  be returned to residents. The education campaign should be focused on the can itself.

                                  Mike Applegarth read comments provided to the Council Office in advance.

                                  A detailed Council discussion ensued. There was no action on this item.

7.         CODE-04-20             Jolley Pharmacy - Proposed Amendments to the SD(Carnation) Zone
           -5844_CC               Amend Title 21, Chapter 19 - Special Development (SD) Districts, Section
                                  20 - SD(Carnation), of the Sandy Municipal Code
                   Attachments:   Staff Report.pdf
                                  Exhibit A.pdf
                                  Exhibit B.pdf
                                  Applicants Letter.pdf
                                  Ord 20-06 LDC-Jolley Pharmacy 21-19-20.pdf
                                  PC Minutes 06.04.2020.pdf

                                  Zoning Administrator Mike Wilcox presented the code amendment to the City Council.
                                  Adam Nash, property owner discussed the need for a pharmacy to have a drive-up
                                  window. The Planning Commission recommended in favor of staff's proposal which does
                                  not include the requested parking reduction. Council questions followed.

                                  Dustin Fratto provided instruction on how to comment on this item. There was no public
                                  comment.
                                  A motion was made by Brooke Christensen, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey, to adopt
                                  the proposed ordinance #20-06, which is an amendment to the Land
                                  Development Code and shown in Exhibit "B", for the following reasons:

                                  1.           The proposal complies with the Purpose of the Land Development
                                  Code as stated in section 21-1-03.

                                  2.            Compliance with the Goals and Policies of the General Plan by
                                  establishing appropriate development standards for all uses and zoning
                                  categories within Sandy City... The motion carried by the following vote:
