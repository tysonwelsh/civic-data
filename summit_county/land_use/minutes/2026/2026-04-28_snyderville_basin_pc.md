Planner Curtis introduced a Conditional Use Permit for a Major Accessory Building located on Old Ranch Road and provided background information on the proposed project. (6:04 PM)

architect for the applicant
, addressed the commission and responded to questions and comments raised during the public hearing. (6:19 PM)
Commissioners asked additional questions and provided final comments to which Planner Curtis and Mr. Vance responded. (6:21 PM)
Spencer Young made a motion to approve the applicants request for a Conditional Use Permit according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Tyann Mooney seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0).
