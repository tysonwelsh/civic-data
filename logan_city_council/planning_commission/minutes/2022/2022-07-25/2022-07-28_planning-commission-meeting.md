 PC 22-040 Homestead Commercial Building Revision [Design Review Permit] Klair Fullmer/200
 North Land Holdings LLC, authorized agent/owner are requesting a Design Review Permit to
 construct a new 7,800 square foot commercial building. This application is a revision to the
 commercial building approved under Design Review Permit #21-035 and is located at
 approximately 650 West 400 North in the Mixed Use (MU) zone; TIN 05-127-0003 (Ellis
 Neighborhood)


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a Design Review Permit for a revision to the previously
approved homestead 7,900 square foot commercial building located at 650 West 400 North in the
Mixed Use (MU) zone. This proposal includes changing the building from two stories to one as well
as adjustments to the footprint and parking layout. The proposed building is 7,800 square feet and
is designed to accommodate one single commercial tenant or up to seven commercial tenants at
approximately 1,100 square feet for each partition.

Commissioner Peterson asked about the east access not being approved. R. Holley stated that the
east access can be approved in the future when 400 north is developed. That access is not required


with the proposed parking lot. Commissioner Guth asked if they are meeting the required percent
of commercial with this smaller building. R. Holley stated that the minimum commercial required is
7,500 square feet, they are over that with the 7,800 square foot commercial building.

PROPONENT: Bracken Anderson said that they originally intended to occupy the commercial space
with a daycare facility. Throughout the construction process over the past three years, they have
had no luck filling the space. They decided to change the commercial building to a spec project,
envisioning the future possibilities for the spaces once 400 north connects. This is a better project
that is more appealing to the neighborhood and will be a great opportunity for commercial tenants.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Guth stated that this is a much better design than the original, which
does present itself better for commercial uses.

040 with the conditions of approval and findings for approval as listed below. Commissioner Doutre
seconded the motion.

STAFF: T. Rice reviewed the request for a Design Review Permit for two new industrial buildings

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Guth stated that they should be able to shift building two to the east
by eliminating some of the parking spaces on the east side of the building. Commissioner Peterson
asked about eliminating the greenspace along the west side of building two to be added to the
required 15-foot setback buffer. Commissioner Lucero said that the code requires a 3-foot green
space around the building. M. DeSimone said that the 15’ setback is required because of the side
parking. However, the commission can approve a reduction of up to 10% or reduce the 15’ setback
to 13.5’.

The Commission determined that Hyclone Road would be added to condition number 4b, and a
condition number 11 added that states that all of the man doors will be covered.

 PC 22-042 Cache Cottage – Short Term Rental [Conditional Use Permit] Darren & Diane
 Roundy, owners are requesting to rent their five-bedroom home as a short-term rental located at
 295 North 500 East in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 06-074-0012
 (Adams Neighborhood).


STAFF: T. Rice reviewed the request for a Conditional Use Permit for a short-term rental of a five-
bedroom home located at 295 North 500 East in the (NR-6) zone. There are two parking stalls in
the garage and 3 along the west side of the home totaling the five required parking stalls. This home
is not within 500 feet of another approved short-term rental.

Commissioner Croshaw asked if there was enough space on the side of the home for the extended
parking. T. Rice said that there is enough room for three 9x18 parking stalls along the side of the
home.

There was discussion regarding the public comments that were received on this project, one
comment was in favor of the short-term rental, and one was against short-term rentals in general.


PROPONENT: Diane & Darren Roundy, proponents, asked the commission if they had any
questions.

PUBLIC: Lora Petersen, neighbor to the west, said that the yard is looking better than it has in a
long time. She is concerned with the little space between her property and the driveway. She
mentioned that the owner may want to get additional garbage cans to accommodate the increase in
garbage. She asked if the rental would change the value of her home

Mark Howland, Admas Neighborhood resident, said he is in full support of short-term rentals
considering the substantial housing crisis we’re in right now. The next step will be to allow ADU’s in
the Adams Neighborhood.

COMMISSION: R. Croshaw said that he likes the idea of a short-term rental in the entire home
opposed to a family living upstairs and renting the downstairs.

Commissioner Doutre asked if they can rent out fewer bedrooms until they get the additional parking
added. T. Rice said that the parking will be verified when the business license is obtained.

MOTION: Commissioner Peterson moved to conditionally approve a Conditional Use Permit for
PC 22-042 with the conditions of approval and findings for approval as listed below. Commissioner
Croshaw seconded the motion.

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
    available in the Community Development Department.
2. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a five (5) bedroom short term rental occupying the full
    single-family home.

    individuals for a maximum of twelve (12) occupants in the STR.

around it so any stormwater that collects naturally will be retained. He also said that the hatched

PC 22-044 Campbell Scientific Building 05 Addition [Design Review Permit] Mike
Stettler/Northern Front Land & Livestock LLC, authorized agent/owner are requesting a Design
Review Permit to construct a new addition directly to the north of their existing campus. The new
addition will house additional manufacturing, machine shop space, and office space. A new drive
will be completed around the entire building, and additional parking will be installed north and east
of the addition. This project is located at 815 West 1800 North in the Industrial Park (IP) zone;
TIN 04-076-0016 (Bridger Neighborhood).


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a Design Review Permit for a new 60,000 square foot
addition to the north side of the Campbell Scientific campus located at 815 West 1800 North. The
new building addition will house manufacturing, machine shop and approximately 12,000 SF of office
space. In addition, a new 260 stall parking lot, loading dock and landscaping areas are proposed
with this application. With a proposed 260 total stalls, the project will likely exceed minimum parking
requirements. If parking exceeds requirements by more than 125% additional landscaping will be
required in and around the parking lot. They can choose to remove some of the parking area to
avoid the extra landscaping requirements.
