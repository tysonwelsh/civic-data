WORK SESSION

Place:            Orem City Council Chambers

At 4:33 p.m.      Chair Komen called the Work Session to order.

Those present: Mike Carpenter, Gerald Crismon, Rod Erickson, James Hawkes, Madeline Komen and Haysam
               Sakar, and Britton Runolfson Planning Commission members; Gary McGinn, Assistant
               Development Services Director/Legal Counsel; Jared Hall, Planning Division Manager; Rebecca
               Gourley, Associate Planner; Grant Allen and Matt Taylor, Senior Planners; Taggart Bowen, City
               Engineer; John Dorny Transportation Engineer; David Spencer, City Council Liaison.

Those excused: Haysam Sakar

    1.   Final Discussion of Planning Commission Bylaws: Commissioners advised they were prepared to
         approve the final draft of the Planning Commission Bylaws, subject to the removal of Section 9.
    2.   Public Notice Requirements Discussion: Staff Member Jared Hall provided a presentation of Orem City’s
         public noticing requirements. Differences between City and State requirements were discussed. Several
         questions were asked by Commissioners regarding information accessibility, discrepancies in former staff
         members’ policies, desired future policies, and other items. Click here to view the recording.
    3.   Zone Density Calculations: Staff Member Matt Taylor provided a presentation on the two common ways
         density is calculated throughout the City’s zones. The differences between gross and net density were
         discussed; For example, gross density includes the area of a street, whereas net density only calculates the
         area of the lots in a subdivision. The item was continued for further discussion after the Consent Agenda
         items. Commissioners asked Staff clarifying questions both during the initial and the follow-up discussion.
         Click here to view the recording.
