order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on June 16, 2020, in the Washington County

Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

CONSIDERATION OF THE 2020 CERTIFIED TAX RATES FOR WASHINGTON
COUNTY

Mr. Hafen recommended that the tax rates remain the same for the General Operations at
0.000548; Interest and Sinking Fund/Bond at 0.000068; and the library at 0.00189 for a
total budgeted revenue of $15,968,774.

Mr. Hafen explained that it is being recommended for the tax rate to decrease for the
County Assessing and Collecting levy from 0.000361 to 0.000349 for a total budgeted
revenue of $7,022,326. This is due to the County collecting sufficient funds to operate the
assessing and collecting departments.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve the 2020 Certified Tax
Rates for Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson,

Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2618, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING GRANT APPLICATIONS FOR TOURISM MARKETING

Washington County Marketing Director Sara Otto explained that the Utah Office of
Tourism administers cooperative grant funding for tourism-related marketing. The
Tourism Office plans to submit multiple applications requesting funds to be used for
marketing the area and to assist with the economic recovery from the impacts of the
COVID-19 pandemic.

Commissioner Cox mentioned that it will be imperative to ensure that visitors can still
come to the Washington County area and feel safe while recreating here.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Approve Resolution No. R-2020-2618,
A Resolution Approving Grant Applications for tourism Marketing.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox

voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Hurricane Valley Fire Chief Tom Kuhlmann explained that there have been several large
fires in the Washington County area. Mr. Kuhlmann cautioned citizens regarding the use
of fireworks in the upcoming weeks.

Commissioner Cox expressed gratitude to the fire crews for their hard work.

Commissioner Almquist mentioned the success of the Utah High School Rodeo that was
held at the Washington County Legacy Park from June 10th through the 15". The Junior
High School Rodeo is currently taking place at the Legacy Park.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:55 p.m.
