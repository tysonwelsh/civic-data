From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: March 24, 2022
Re: Sax Spy - Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit Request Comments: This application is a request for 0.16 acres located on the west side of Dreamfield Drive.  The property is currently zoned R3.  The applicant is requesting approval for private music lessons and cottage business for online sales of photographs and creating arts and crafts for online sales.   The applicant is requesting deviations to the standards outlined in Section 9-34-040 of the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC).  Section 9-34-060 allows the Planning Commission to consider the requested use and make deviations to the standards.
The home was originally built in 1998 and is Lot 70 of The Fields at Draper Phase I residential subdivision.  The subdivision was developed as RM (Multiple Residential) zoning and therefore has smaller lot sizes than what would be allowed today with R3 zoning. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.   Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
For denial:
1.   Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
