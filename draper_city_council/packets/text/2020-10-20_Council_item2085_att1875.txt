                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-47

               A RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS AND ALTERNATE
               MEMBERS TO THE DRAPER CITY DIVERSITY AND
               INCLUSION ADVISORY BOARD


        WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has established the Draper City Diversity and
Inclusion Advisory Board to identify and advise on issues faced by diverse groups and to create
opportunities for an inclusive community within the City; and

        WHEREAS, the Council recognizes the importance of diversity and inclusion within the
City; and

     WHEREAS, the Mayor has recommended persons to serve as members and alternate
members on the board.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1.     Appointment. Members and alternate members listed in Exhibit A are
hereby appointed to serve on the Draper City Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Board according
to the laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the Board for the terms listed in Exhibit A.

        Section 2.     Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3.     Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective upon with the
effective date of Ordinance 1461.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 20TH DAY OF OCTOBER, 2020.
