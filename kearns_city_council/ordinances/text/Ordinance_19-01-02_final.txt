Conditions of approval:
1. The structure is made of durable, permanent materials, (vinyl, canvas, nylon,
and other cloth “roofing” materials are not allowed).
2. Acarport up to 600 square feet may be free-standing or attached to the dwelling, A
deck or patio cover allowed under this provision shall be permanently attached to the
dwelling,
3. The structure shall be approved by the building official prior to construction,
4. The applicant shall submit the written approval of the property owner immediately
abutting the property line toward which the exception is requested,
5. A carport or patio cover allowed under this provision shall have no exterior walls
or panels. A deck allowed under this provision may have walls built up to the
height of the railing, but shall not be enclosed with a roof and walls, and,
6. Nomore than 50% of the available (unoccupied) portion of the lot may be
covered with structures allowed under this section,
