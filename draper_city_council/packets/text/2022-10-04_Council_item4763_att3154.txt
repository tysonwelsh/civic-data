                                 RESOLUTION NO. 22-53

         A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL TERMINATING
          THE UNILATERAL OPERATIONAL PROCEDURES AGREEMENT
                       WITH SGR, LLC d/b/a SEABIRD

WHEREAS, the City entered into a Unilateral Operational Procedures Agreement with SGR,
LLC d/b/a/ Seabird (“Seabird”) dated November 1, 2018 – Draper City Contract 18-257 (the
“Agreement”); and

WHEREAS, the Agreement addresses certain operational procedures for the operation of the
Seabird business; and

WHEREAS, Seabird has complied with the operational procedures outlined in the Agreement;
and

WHEREAS, Seabird would like to expand its hours of operation to align with the operational
requirements of the Utah Alcoholic Beverage Control Act governing bar establishments; and

WHEREAS, terminating the Agreement promotes the health, safety and welfare of the residents
of Draper City.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

Section 1. Terminate Agreement. The Draper City Council hereby terminates the Unilateral
Operational Procedures Agreement (Draper City Contract 18-257).

Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage by
the City Council.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 4th DAY OF OCTOBER 2022.
