                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                                February

The most significant impact to police operations during the month of February was the closure of
12600 South at Bangerter Highway, which dramatically changed our traffic patterns and resulted in
a significant increase in traffic accidents during the month, a 34% increase from January to
February. Additionally, a series of several snowstorms impacted our community and added to the
number of collisions on our roadways.

It appears our efforts to focus our patrol resources on areas where we have had a high number of
vehicle burglaries is having an impact and we have seen a dramatic reduction from 26 vehicle
burglaries in January to 13 during February. Also, we have continued to focus traffic enforcement
efforts on identifying impaired drivers in our community. Attention to this issue resulted in 15
Driving Under the Influence of Drugs or Alcohol arrests during the month, a significant increase
from the previous month and more than double the number of arrests in February, 2020.

We are very much looking forward to participating in more community events as pandemic
restrictions lessen during the coming months, which will give us an opportunity to continue to
develop relationships between our members and the citizens they serve.
