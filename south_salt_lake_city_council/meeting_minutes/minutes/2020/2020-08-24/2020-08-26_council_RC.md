NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of
          upcoming events, meetings, activities, etc.

       2. CITIZEN’S COMMENTS. Jason Keffer, 524 Farmer Alumni Dr. He has
          commented before about the importance of having a Citizen Review Board.
          He also added how important he feels it is to have plan in place if something
          goes wrong in the police department. He had a couple of questions from
          tonight’s work meeting about the Citizen Review Board.

          Council Chair Bynum said there will be more discussion about a Citizen
          Review and police policies at future meetings.

          Mekeli Wesley, 3149 South State Street. He purchased a business in South
          Salt Lake and has been trying to obtain a business license and has run into
          issues with the business licensing department. He wonders what steps he can
          take next and who to speak to about the issues he is having with that
          department.

          Council Chair Bynum recommended that Mr. Wesley reach out to the Council
          via email regarding his concerns and in the meantime she will try to find out
          more information.

       3. MAYOR’S COMMENTS. Mayor Wood stressed the importance of taking
          the Census and asked that the Council continue to spread the word about the
          Census. She also added that there is a lot of information regarding Mr.
          Wesley’s business and would like to talk to the Council about this further in
          the future.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS. None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS. Council Member Pinkney had an email
          from a resident regarding the police department’s involvement with the
          Cottonwood Heights protests. A copy is attached and incorporated by this
          reference. She also added that she agrees with Mr. Keffer’s comments that
          were made about the Citizen Review Board and she believes by just reviewing
          police policy the Council isn’t making systemic changes. She said that she has
          had a lot of businesses reach out to her regarding their frustrations with the
          business licensing department and wondered if the Council could have further
          discussion regarding this.

          Council Chair Bynum said the Council will meet to address business licensing
          concerns in the future.

          Council Member deWolfe said some residents in the City voiced frustration
          with the Council’s decision on the temporary business ordinance that was
          passed by Council at the last meeting. He gave some clarification as to why
          the Council made that decision.

       6. INFORMATION

              a. City’s Tiered Opening Plan Update. Mayor Wood gave a brief
                 update on the City’s tiered opening plan. Unified Command decided to
                 push back the tier 2 opening until there is adequate supplies and seeing
                 what happens with schools reopening.

              b. COVID-19 Update. Fire Chief, Terry Addison, gave the Council a
                 brief update on the COVID-19 pandemic.

ACTION ITEMS

NEW BUSINESS

   1. City’s Street Lighting Update. City Engineer, Dennis Pay, gave a brief overview
      of the City’s current street lighting project.

   2. Discussion of Resolution against the Privatization of the United States Postal
      Service. Council Member Pinkney spoke about why the Council should support a
      resolution that disfavors the privatization of the United States Postal Serives and
      commented on all the good that the Postal Service does for communities.

       Council Member deWolfe made a motion to bring a resolution back at the next
       Council meeting.
