GENERAL SESSION

Chair Tippetts called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. She
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

The state law is written to allow a city to exempt a residential zone or area from allowing accessory

recommend that the City Council exclude a specific neighborhood. We did not have data or a logical

4.     Wasatch Residential Group requests an amendment to Chapter 17-7-10.11 (Jordan
       Bluffs Subarea 4) of the Midvale City Municipal Code. (TXT-21-12; Wendelin
       Kobloch)

Mr. Kobloch stated that the applicant, Wasatch Residential Group, requests an amendment to
Chapter 17-7-10.11 (Jordan Bluffs Subarea 4) of the Municipal Code. The requested amendment
would reduce the required landscape setback area/build-to line along Seghini Drive from thirty
feet (30’) to twenty feet (20’). The applicant requests this change as they propose a residential
product with front porch entrances along Seghini Drive. A 20’ setback, they feel, will create a
better streetscape by bringing the structures closer to the road. The applicant has an adjacent
development (ParcView), along the northeast portion of Seghini Drive, with a 20’ landscape
setback. Per the Code, this area consisting of ParcView (Area A in the Code) is exempt from the
30’ landscape setback requirement (see map in Exhibit A). However, another adjacent
development, directly east across Seghini Drive (Reserve at View 78) has adhered to the 30’
setback as it falls outside of the exempt “Area A” as identified in the Code.

                                                ***
APPLICANT:

Mr. Adam Lankford stated that his group is before the Planning Commission with the final
product of Phase 3 for this development. He added that this is the first step in the approval
process and that he will be back with a large-scale master plan and a site plan. The product
presented will look like a large house, with the front elevation facing Seghini road. He stated that
by providing a smaller set back, Wasatch Residential Group is hoping to provide a more
residential feel.

                                                ***

PUBLIC HEARING:

Chair Tippetts opened the public hearing.
