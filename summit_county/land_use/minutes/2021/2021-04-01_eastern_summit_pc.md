Findings of Fact:  
1. The Pinnacle at Promontory Phase 1 Subdivision was recorded in November of 2019 as part of 
the overall Promontory Specially Planned Area.  
2. Promontory Development LLC (Francis Najafi) is the owner of record of Parcel PINNP-1- 15 
(2.09 acres), located at 3822 Pinnacle Sky Loop, Promontory, Summit County, UT.  
3. The applicant is requesting to amend the size and location of the designated building pad on 
Lot 15 of the Pinnacle at Promontory Phase 1 Subdivision as outlined in Exhibit A of this Staff 
Report.  
4. Note 3 of the Pinnacle at Promontory Phase 1 Subdivision states; The maximum height limit 
of any residence proposed for construction in PINNACLE is 32' from existing grade. However, 
any residence that breaks the natural ridgeline when viewed from identified locations on US 
Highway 40, Interstate 80, Highway 248 or the Brown's Canyon Road shall be limited to a 
maximum height of 28' from existing grade. The County or the Design Reviewer may require 
special studies to assure that any proposed structure either does not break the ridgeline or does 
not exceed the 28' height. Based on studies by the Developer, a home on Lots 2, 3, 4, 15, 16, 17, 
and 18, ore deemed to break the ridgeline and are limited to 28 feet in height. It is the sole 
responsibility of each lot owner to ensure that a residence on the owner's lot is in compliance 
with this Subsection.  
5. The proposed amendment would reduce the building pad from 46,427 sq. ft. to 36,073, 
resulting in 22% decrease in potential disturbance area.  
6. As designed, the only portions of the proposed home that would be outside of the original pad 
would be the driveway and garage areas (16 ft. height).  
7. The downhill massing of the home would block the driveway and garage areas as seen from 
the designated vantage points.  
8. The applicant has provided a letter from Promontory supporting the proposed amendment.  
9. Public notice of the public hearing was published in the Summit County News.  
10. Postcard notices announcing the public hearing were mailed to property owners within 1,000 
feet of the subject parcels.  
 
Conclusions of Law:  
1. There is good cause for this Plat Amendment.  
2. The proposed Plat Amendment will not result in additional density.

1. All service provider requirements shall be met.  
2. In order to ensure that the public interest will not be materially injured by the proposed

3.  Discussion and possible recommendation of amendments to Sections 11-6 
General Regulations, 11-3-15 Chart of Allowed and Permitted Uses, and Appendix A 
Definitions of the Eastern Summit County Development Code to create regulations 
for the use of Private Campgrounds, and the Seasonal use of Recreational Vehicles. – 
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner 
 
Planner Milliner shared the background for this application. The Planning Commission 
has discussed recreational campgrounds in previous meetings. There are some impacts, the 
majority from improper disposal of sewage, ATV use, and litter. There are some suggested 
regulations to limit the impact of uses. Regulations for people who want to use their lot as a 
campground would put limits on what can be done with RVs on the lot. There have been 
several Public Hearings and Work Sessions to discuss this issue. The Planning Commission 
is being asked to review the suggested changes made to the Code and consider forwarding 
a positive recommendation to the County Council. 
Commissioner Wilde suggested limiting the number of trailers allowed on a lot to six per 
acre and not allowing the trailers to be occupied longer than fourteen days at a time.

Planner Milliner clarified that current uses are grandfathered according to existing Code

Findings of Fact:  
1. The goal of Chapter 2 of the Eastern Summit County General Plan is to develop land use 
codes which balance the diversity of desires of Eastern Summit County residents, including 
private property rights.  
2. In furtherance of this goal, §11-1-1 of the Eastern Summit County Code provides that “The 
eastern Summit County general plan was developed to ensure that the rural, agricultural, and 
small-town character of the eastern portion of the county shall remain, even in the presence of 
growth and change. The intention of the county is to assure the managed, proper and sensitive 
development of land to protect and enhance these desired qualities and the lifestyle that exists.”  
3. On January 21, 2021, the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission conducted a public 
hearing and reviewed potential amendments to the Development Code regulating private and 
commercial campgrounds as well as the seasonal use of recreational vehicles.  
4. On February 4, 2021, the Commission reviewed the language and directed staff to make 
changes and return with an ordinance prepared for a recommendation to the County Council.  
5. The proposed regulations are response to complaints about property owners using single 
family lots as campgrounds or parking multiple RVs on a property to be occupied intermittently 
throughout the summer.  
6. RV users have stated that parking an RV on a lot is a viable alternative for individuals who 
would like to use their land but cannot afford to build a cabin or home on it.  
7. This language is designed to allow persons who want to park/camp RVs on their land to do so.  
8. The language does not address issues such as noise or impacts from ATV use because those 
issues are not within the jurisdiction of the Development Code.
