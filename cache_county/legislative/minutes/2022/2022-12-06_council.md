                              RESOLUTION 2022-28
            A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING COUNTY POLICY ON SUPPORTING
                        LOCAL NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS



WHEREAS, the Cache County Council recognizes the significant and important contribution of
local nonprofit and volunteer organizations that provide social services in Cache County; and

WHEREAS, because these services are not provided by government, these organizations save
taxpayers money; and

WHEREAS, the contributions of these organizations strengthen the community; and

WHEREAS, local social services nonprofits specialize in providing for specific and complex
needs of residents, and some organizations have served the community for more than 20 years;
and

WHEREAS, these organizations provide services based on relationships of trust with clients,
and many clients will not seek help from government affiliated agencies; and

WHEREAS, nonprofit organizations have formalized and well-established systems in place to
coordinate with each other, with government agencies, and with service providers;

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Cache County Council establishes the following
County policy:

                          County Support of Nonprofit Organizations

   1. Cache County shall not establish programs or services that compete with non-profit
      organizations which are designated by the Internal Revenue Service to be tax-exempt
      under section 501 (c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

   2. Cache County shall keep an ongoing dialogue with social services nonprofits to
      understand community needs and available services, even if there is no forthcoming
      funding.

   3.    No remuneration or privileges of any kind shall be expected or given to any county
        officer, employee, or county elected official due to donations given to non-profit or
        volunteer organizations.
This resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption.

Resolved this 6th day of December, 2022

      AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 2.08 OF THE CACHE COUNTY CODE

(A)     WHEREAS, Cache County has an executive-council form of government, as described in
        Utah Code 17-52a-203, wherein the County Executive is the chief executive officer, and
        the County Council is the county legislative body; and

(B)     WHEREAS, the County Council has the powers, duties, and functions of a legislative
        body and the county executive has the powers, duties, and functions of an executive; and

(C)     WHEREAS, the power to appoint and to remove officers has been generally deemed an
        executive power; and

(D)     WHEREAS, The County Council respects the legitimate enumerated powers of the
        executive; and

(E)     WHEREAS, the County Council may pass all ordinances and rules and make all
        regulations, not repugnant to law, necessary for carrying into effect or discharging its
        powers and duties; and

(F)     WHEREAS, the County Council has an interest in maintaining long term institutional

      A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING A COUNCIL SPECIAL AUDIT COMMITTEE


         WHEREAS, County Code enables the County Council to "establish and define the duties and
functions of appointed boards and commissions deemed appropriate to expedite and facilitate the duties
of the council" (Cache County Code 2.12.120H); and

        WHEREAS, State Code empowers the County Council to "examine and audit the accounts of all
county officers having the care, management, collection, or distribution of money belonging to the
county, appropriated to the county, or otherwise available for the county's use and benefit" (Title 17,
Chapter 53, Section 106(2)(a)(iii), Utah Code Annotated, 1953, as amended); and

        WHEREAS, State Code also empowers the County Council to "investigate any matter pertaining
to a county officer or to the county or its business or affairs" (Title 17, Chapter 53, Section 106(2)(a)(iv),
Utah Code Annotated, 1953, as amended); and

         WHEREAS, the County Council provides for an annual audit but may also provide for a special
audit of accounts to be made (County Code 2.12.120G); and

        WHEREAS, the County Council has deemed it necessary to conduct a special audit; and

        WHEREAS, assigning a committee to coordinate and oversee the audit will allow the Council to
focus on its regular duties, provide for continuity, and ensure the proper completion of the audit;

        NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved that the Cache County Council establishes the following:



               CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL SPECIAL AUDIT COMMITTEE

1. DUTIES
The Special Audit Committee shall work closely with the external auditors, including reviewing
auditing approaches, coordinating the audit, and directing the extermal auditors. Committee
members shall have access to County information as requested. The committee shall maintain
communication with and report to the Council Chair. When the audit is complete, the committee
will review the audit and help the County Council interpret and understand the report.


2. MEMBERSHIP
The committee shall consist of two to three members appointed by the County Council. Term of
office shall be two years. The term may be extended by the County Council. Members shall be
removed for cause, by a majority vote of the Council.

3. MEETING SCHEDULE
The committee meets as needed to fulfill its responsibilities, but shall meet at least quarterly. The
committee shall meet privately in order to discuss matters that should not be disclosed as part of
the audit effort.

4. COMPENSATION
This is a volunteer committee without compensation.
