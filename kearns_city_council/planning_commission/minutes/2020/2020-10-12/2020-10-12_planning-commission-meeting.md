                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)
                                       Hearings began at – 5:36 p.m.

Kearns Metro Township is considering adopting the proposed 2020 Kearns Metro Township General Plan,
which plans for the areas within Kearns Metro Township. The 2020 General Plan is Kearns’s first
comprehensive plan since Kearns incorporated as a metro township in 2017. The Plan synthesizes data and
public feedback to describe current conditions, envision Kearns’s future, and guide Kearns from present
conditions toward that desired vision. This General Plan meets the required State laws for general planning,
including elements on Transportation and Land Use (the Moderate-Income Housing Plan was adopted
previously). It also includes the following supplemental Chapters: Introduction to the Planning
Process/Methodology, and Economic Development. Planner: Kayla Mauldin (Motion/Voting)

Please visit: https://bit.ly/lrp-kearns to view a copy of the 2020 Kearns General Plan and Kearns
Expanded Appendix.

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed general plan after taking comments from the public
during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s rules
of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three minutes
per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Kayla Mauldin provided an analysis of
the general plan up for recommendation to the council for adoption.

Commissioner Nelson motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Hatch seconded that motion.
Commissioners voted unanimous in favor.
