1. Commission Business

   1.1. Review of City Council Decisions – Michael Maloy, Planning Director
Planning Director Maloy advised the Council had some big items last week. The 5th
amendment of the South Hills Master Development Agreement (MDA) was approved
provisionally with subsequent amendments to the general plan and the zoning map to align
with their land use plan. The overall density is less than their original master plan. The
July 17, 2024 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes                                Page 2 of 18


location and types of uses have shifted enough to not line up with the vested general plan.
The council requested they bring the plan up to the current general plan with a subsequent
6th amendment. The amendment will include some land exchanges with other landowners.
The 6th amendment will be an amended and restated document to limit looking back at older
documents.
The agricultural amendments to the A-1-10 zone were approved by the Council. The density
bonus option was removed. Also, the subdivision plat amendment was approved for 1 year
with an extension of only 1 year from the previous 6 years.


   1.2. Review of Agenda Items – Planning Staff
Item 4.1- Planner Howa pointed out they are short one parking space. The applicant is also
the owner of the adjacent property with a cross-parking agreement. The event center will
typically operate after regular business hours. Commissioner Garcia sought if they could
obtain an alcohol license? Planner Howa advised the State would need to investigate since
they are very close to the high school.
Item 4.2- Planning Manager Spencer briefed they will have excessive parking, almost twice
as much as required. Part of the rear parking will be used for Slackwater Pizza. They will
add a pedestrian crossing back to Slackwater’s building. They are asking for a waiver of
strict compliance for landscaping. They are requesting the use of potted plants instead.
The Commission might want to consider a pot size restriction, possibly 30 inches tall.
Commissioner Anderson sought why they are requesting the exception? Planning Manager
Spencer clarified it has to do with flexibility with their tenants. Commissioner Ferguson
expounded in their letter it explained they are unsure where the doors for the tenants will
be. The pots give them the ability to move them around.
Item 5.1- Planner Howa conveyed the applicant has been in negotiations with a property
owner adjacent to theirs. They purchased .81 acres and want to create phase 2 of their
subdivision. They are asking for a rezone to submit the subdivision plats.
Item 5.2- Planning Manager Spencer aired the requests for Mountain View Plaza MDA
amendments. They are Introducing some uses they had not anticipated, submitting a
redesign of the corner plaza and requested a sign master plan. The uses are similar to
what is already approved. Staff are recommending restrictions on the animal care unit. The
corner plaza was intended for entertainment and food. Staff are not sure how a veterinary
clinic would fit into those uses. The Commission could add a provision not allowing the
clinic to be adjacent to the corner plaza or possibly any of the plazas. Planning Director
Maloy expressed he understands the veterinary wants a center location. Commissioner
Fenn aired he is not opposed to animal services, but the corner area was meant for specific
uses. Commissioner Sickles checked if they put it on another courtyard, would it prohibit
the other buildings in that courtyard’s uses? Planner Howa relayed the health department
has restrictions to where the animals can be as far as food services. Commissioner Sickles
expressed his worry about the plaza being used as a dog potty yard.

Planning Manager Spencer conveyed office use is already permitted, and they are now
asking for medical use, bar services, and animal services in this amendment. They have a
base requirement of 35% entertainment use. In talking to economic development, it would
be interesting to see a study of the benefits of vet clinic verses a restaurant. No public
comments have been received regarding the public hearing. Commissioner Anderson
sought if the animal services could be a restricted use. Planning Manager Spencer
counseled since this is an MDA the Planning Commission could restrict it. In a normal
commercial space, the commission could not restrict the use.
A major redesign of the corner plaza was proposed. The redesign is taking away the water
feature. In lieu they are doing a performance stage with fire features and concrete pavers.
They are taking out the game tables and replacing it with a mulched playground. Planning
Director Maloy communicated if the Commission are not comfortable with the changes
they don’t have to approve the item.
Item 5.3 and 5.4- Planning Manager Director Spencer informed Hidden Oaks was asking for
interrelated amendments. They don’t feel the market is sufficient to produce pods 15 & 17 as
senior restricted housing. The proposal was to have the same number of units but doing
simple subdivision fee owner lots. In changing the development, it would change the open
space within the development. They are anticipating they will be below the 20% minimum
requirement. There is a provision in the code in lieu of providing open space allowing them
to provide open space land outside of their development or they pay a fee in lieu to the city
for use as parks and openspace. They want to exercise the opportunity. They city council
has not had conversations with them about these alternatives. They provided a land use
table, but it did not include a concept plan. The city did receive one email inquiry for the
public hearing.
Commissioner Fenn pressed what is behind the decrease of the right of way. Planning
Director Maloy detailed the city had planned to have 7900 West be a capital improvement
project. The transportation plan has changed the capital improvement project to 7300 west
allowing 7900 west to become a local street.
Commissioner Garcia queried if the commission could say anything about the open space
not being satisfied. Planning Director Maloy outlined this is an MDA so the commission can
tell them we don’t want to satisfy the open space with a cash payment, ask for open space
outside of Hidden Oaks, or recommend maintaining the 20% requirement. The city has not
had a development ask for the lowered open space before. They do have a trail network
and recreation within this area. Commissioner Ferguson disclosed the fee in lieu is because
of the senior restriction change, it doesn’t change the physical look. The yards don’t count
as contiguous land open space as single family housing.
Item 5.4- Planning Director Maloy outlined there have been some recent issues with
specialty permits concerning how many dogs are allowed. Through extensive
conversations with council, it was decided to move forward with removing some permits.
They could be re-introduced in title 5 if the council requested. These changes are being
made to make it consistent with the previous changes in title 5. The legal department has
July 17, 2024 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes                                 Page 4 of 18


requested to take out animal establishments in the zoning codes. Exotic permits are
currently in Title 10, but animal services would rather have a code stating what permits are
allowed. The city can’t supersede the County, State, and Federal laws. Legal wants to
remove them from Title 10 and have council amend Title 5 to include them. Assistant City
Attorney Brooks conveyed Title 5 has been shortened by at least half. It’s cleaner to have it
all in one section. Planning Manager Maloy clarified residents are still allowed to breed and

move the litter out of their home.
