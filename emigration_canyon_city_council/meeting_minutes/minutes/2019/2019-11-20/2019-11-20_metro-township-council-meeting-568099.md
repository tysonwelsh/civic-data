PUBLIC MEETING
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Council Member Bowen stated UFA would like the Council to approve a change
to the interlocal agreement that pertain to its creation, rules of governance, and to clarify the voting
process on certain budgetary issues.
David Church stated he recommends the Council passes the updated interlocal
agreement. If the Council does not sign it then it will not have a seat on the board.

Chief Dan Peterson stated the UFA Board started the process of amending the
interlocal agreement when the metro townships were enacted to allow them a seat on the board.
As discussions continued the larger cities shared concerns that their vote was being marginalized
and eventually left UFA. It was determined that UFA did not have good leave policies in place and
the interlocal needed to be updated. The larger cities acknowledged that they did not want the
smaller communities to be marginalized. He helps coach and drive the agenda so that nobody
feels cornered. Issues are talked through so the board understands and a unanimous vote can
be taken. The feeling on the board is the organization is better with the small communities involved
and the larger cities have a safe guard on budget issues.
Council Member Brems stated Emigration Canyon is getting more benefits than
what it is paying for. Eventually the member cities of UFA will want Emigration Canyon to pay
what it costs to protect the canyon. If a fire occurred, there will be a lot of property loss and
possibly loss of life. Emigration Canyon needs to have a significant voice in the budget process.
Council Member Hook, seconded by Deputy Mayor Hawkes, moved to approve
the resolution and direct Mayor Smolka to sign the Interlocal Agreement. The motion passed
unanimously.

Public Hearing
2020 Emigration Canyon Metro Township Budget
THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to consider the 2020
Budget for Emigration Canyon Metro Township.
Council Member Bowen, seconded by Council Member Hook, moved to open the
public hearing to hear comments on the 2020 Emigration Canyon Metro Township budget. The
motion passed unanimously.
No one spoke in favor of or in opposition to the proposed 2020 Emigration Canyon
Metro Township.
The Public Comment period was closed.
The Council reviewed the 2020 Emigration Canyon Metro Township budget.
The Council decided to table the decision on the budget until the December
meeting.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Captain Michael Conn stated the strike team and task force went to California for
2 weeks to help with fires. UFA is short staffed and is trying to balance helping neighboring states
but also providing services in Utah. Riley Pilgrim has been promoted to Assistant Chief to take
over Chief Watson’s position over Support Services upon his retirement. Training for

UFA for behavioral health and wellness for fire fighters.
