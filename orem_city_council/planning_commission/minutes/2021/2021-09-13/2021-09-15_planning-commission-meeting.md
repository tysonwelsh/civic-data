                              PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES
                                         September 15, 2021

The following items are discussed in these minutes:
    • PRELIMINARY PLAT – Approving the overall preliminary plat of Fields at Lakeview, including Fields at
         Lakeview, Plat F and Fields at Lakeview, Plat H located generally at 1980 South Geneva Road – Continued
         to November 3, 2021.
    • PRELIMINARY PLAT - Approving the preliminary plat for Hernandez Plat A, located generally at 534 N
         600 West - Approved

STUDY SESSION

Place:           City Council Conference Room

At 4:00 p.m.     Chair Cochran called the Study Session to order.

Those present: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Shauna Mecham, Barry Roberts and Ross Spencer, Planning
               Commission members; Ryan L. Clark, Development Services Director; Jason W. Bench, Planning
               Division Manager; Cheryl Vargas and Rachel Garey, Associate Planners; Grant Allen, Long Range
               Planner; Sam Kelly, City Engineer; Steve Earl, Legal Counsel and Terry Peterson, City Council
               Liaison

Those excused: Haysam Sakar, Planning Commission member; Matt Taylor, Long Range Planner

The Commission and staff briefly reviewed agenda items and minutes from September 1, 2021 meeting and
adjourned at 4:25 p.m. to the City Council Chambers for the regular meeting.

REGULAR MEETING

Place:           Orem City Council Chambers

At 4:30 p.m.     Chair Cochran called the Planning Commission meeting to order and offered the invocation.

Those present: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Shauna Mecham, Barry Roberts and Ross Spencer, Planning
               Commission members; Ryan L. Clark, Development Services Director; Jason W. Bench, Planning
               Division Manager; Cheryl Vargas and Rachel Garey, Associate Planners; Grant Allen, Long Range
               Planner; Sam Kelly, City Engineer; Steve Earl, Legal Counsel and Terry Peterson, City Council
               Liaison

Those excused: Haysam Sakar, Planning Commission member; Matt Taylor, Long Range Planner

Chair Cochran asked Ms. Garey to introduce Agenda Item 3.1:

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Jeff Mansell, Boardwalk Industries, LLC for the Planning Commission to approve
the overall preliminary plat of Fields at Lakeview, including Fields at Lakeview, Plat F and Fields at Lakeview, Plat
H located generally at 1980 South Geneva Road in the PD43 zone.

                                           Recommendation: Due to the concept plan for this project being
                                           continued at City Council until October 26, 2021, Staff recommends the
                                           Planning Commission to continue the overall preliminary plat of Fields at
                                           Lakeview, including Fields at Lakeview, Plat F and Fields at Lakeview,
                                           Plat H located generally at 1980 South Geneva Road in the PD43 zone to
                                           November 3, 2021.

                             Planning Commission minutes for September 15, 2021


Planning Commission Action: Ms. Bentley moved to continue the overall preliminary plat of Fields at Lakeview,
including Fields at Lakeview, Plat F and Fields at Lakeview, Plat H located generally at 1980 South Geneva Road in
the PD43 zone until November 3, 2021. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Marisa Bentley,
Mickey Cochran, Shauna Mecham, Barry Roberts and Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Cochran asked Ms. Garey to introduce Agenda Item 3.2:

Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Ramon and Rosalia Hernandez for the Planning Commission to approve the
preliminary plat for Hernandez Plat A, located generally at 534 N 600 West in the R7.5 zone.

                                       Staff Presentation: The applicant desires to subdivide a larger lot into two
                                       lots in an existing neighborhood in the R7.5 zone. The smaller lot, contains
                                       7,510 square feet, has an existing home which will remain. The larger lot,
                                       contains 18,615 square feet, and shall have improvements installed. Both of
                                       the lots will front on 600 West.

                                       Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this
request complies with the Orem City Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the preliminary
plat for Hernandez Plat A, located generally at 534 N 600 West in the R7.5 zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Garey.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to come forward. Rosalia Hernandez introduced herself and had nothing to add to
the presentation.

Chair Cochran called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Roberts moved to approve the preliminary plat for Hernandez Plat A, located
generally at 534 N 600 West in the R7.5 zone. Ms. Mecham seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Marisa
Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Shauna Mecham, Barry Roberts and Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.

Minutes: The Planning Commission reviewed the minutes from the previous meeting. Chair Cochran then moved to
approve the meeting minutes for September 1, 2021. Mr. Roberts seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Marisa
Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Shauna Mecham, Barry Roberts and Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.

Adjourn: Chair Cochran moved to adjourn. Ms. Bentley seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Marisa Bentley,
Mickey Cochran, Shauna Mecham, Barry Roberts and Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.

Adjourn: 4:34 p.m.



                                                     Jason Bench
                                                     Planning Commission Secretary
