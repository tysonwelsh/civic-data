OPENING:
     Mayor Pike called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
     invocation was offered by Reverend Alex Wilkie and the Pledge of Allegiance to the
     Flag was led by Mayor Pike.

       Marianne Hamilton with Art Around the Corner presented a PowerPoint presentation
       covering the following topics: Ubuntu/Nathan Brimhall; At Play on the Prairie/Morgan
       Clements; Love the Earth/Cheryl Collins; Get Your Mind Rolling/Deveren Farley; The
       Fool/Kimber Fiebiger; Pelican Ahoy!/Jim Green; Little Fawn/Fritz Hoppe; Golden
       Desert/Myles Howell; The First Snow/Michael Howey; Winter Leaves/Mark Hyde;
       Faith/Victor Issa; Male/Female Figure/Franz Johansen; Think Green/Richard Prazen;
       Bad to the Bone/Richard Prazen; Amelia Earhart/Gary Lee Price; Heavy
       Laden/Warren Price; Emissaries of Light/Marcia Robinson-Rouse; Rectangles/Charles
       Sturrock; Good Morning Sunshine/Reven Marie Swanson; Chilone Garden
       Orb/Christopher Thomson; Visioning #5/Christopher Thomson; Sun Seekers/Dan
       Toone; Cacti/Matt Clark; Great Blue/Mike Dwyer; Studio Salvage/Nathan Johansen;
       Forward/Josh Toone; Resonate/Doug, Dianne & Ryan Adams; Rhapsody/Karen
       Coburn; Anika/Angela De La Vega; Learning to Fly/Jason Millward; Reaching/Richard
       Pakratz; and Daydream/Dan Toone.

       Mayor Pike mentioned the Governor and LT. Governor held a press conference
       regarding the COVID-19 Coronavirus. Staff is encouraging citizens not to gather in
       groups of 100 or more. The LDS Church has cancelled all meetings effective
       immediately. Effective immediately, there is no business travel for City staff.
       Communications and Marketing Director David Cordero and Battalion Chief Darren
       Imlay are point people for the City. He doesn’t want panic but wants to be
       proactive. The Arts Festival will be cancelled.

DISCUSSION REGARDING THE MASTER PLANS FOR ENERGY SERVICES, WATER
SERVICES, AND WASTEWATER SERVICES:
     Energy Services Director Laurie Mangum provided a PowerPoint presentation
     covering the following topics: Aerial map; Community changes; Revenues; Meter
     Counts; Map of existing and future substations sites; Photos of Millcreek Substation;
     and Things to note.

      Water Services Director Scott Taylor presented a PowerPoint presentation covering
      the following topics: Culinary Water, Secondary Irrigation & Sewer Master Plan
      updates; Existing water and sewer system evaluation, Growth/population projection,
      Hydraulic modeling; Growth/population projection; Where will growth occur in the
      next 10 years?; Existing level of service; Recommended conveyance system
      improvements; Recommended storage construction plan through buildout; City of St.
      George 10-year water system Capital Facilities Plan - Water; Sewer Master Plan
      update; Map of projects; Recommended sewer collection system improvements
      through buildout; City of St. George Wastewater Treatment Facility Plan; Wastewater
      Collections projects – 10 year; Current secondary irrigation use and reuse plant;
      Existing irrigation system facilities; Extent of amended secondary irrigation
      expansion; Estimated number of ERU’s added to secondary irrigation system;
      Secondary irrigation storage projects; Secondary irrigation conveyance projects; and
      Secondary Irrigation 10-year project.

      Mari with SUHBA thanked the City for including them in this process; not too much
      concern was expressed. She inquired about impact fee discussions.

      Budget and Financial Planning Manager Deanna Brklacich (via video) replied the
      impact fee discussions will take place on March 26th.

REPORTS FROM MAYOR, COUNCILMEMBERS, AND CITY MANAGER:
     Councilmember Hughes reported on the St. George Housing Authority board
     meeting. He also mentioned the crossover facility; Bed N Biscuits; and the recent
     Flood Control Authority meeting.

      Mayor Pike mentioned an allocation committee to review grants to be submitted by
      hornless shelter providers. He mentioned a homelessness bills being discussed at
      the Legislative Session.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman outlined one of the bills mentioned by Mayor Pike as
      well as one regarding unisex restrooms.

      Councilmember Smethurst spoke about the recent trip to Washington, DC.
      Councilmembers Randall, Larkin and McArthur, Mayor Pike, City Manager Adam
      Lenhard and City Attorney Shawn Guzman also attended.

The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:00 pm
