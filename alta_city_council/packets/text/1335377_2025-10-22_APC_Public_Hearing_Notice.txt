                                             PUBLIC NOTICE
                              PUBLIC HEARING – ALTA PLANNING COMMISSION
                                      WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 22, 2025
                                                3:00 PM
                                        ALTA COMMUNITY CENTER
                                            10351 E HWY 210
                                               ALTA, UTAH

            The Town of Alta Planning Commission will hold a public hearing to receive input on proposed
   amendments to Town of Alta Code, including: adoption of the 2006 Utah Wildland Urban Interface (WUI)
  Code; designation of a local WUI boundary; and amendments to Titles 8 and 9 to adopt the Wildland Urban
                                Interface Code and allow for defensible space.
The Town of Alta Planning Commission will hold a public hearing to receive input on proposed amendments to
 Town of Alta Code, including: Section 10-1-8 “Amendments” and Section 10-1-9 “Hearing and Publication of
                                        Notice Before Amendment.”
 A draft ordinance is available for review on the Town’s website, www.townofalta.utah.gov and in the Town
Office at 10201 East Highway 210 during normal business hours. This notice shall formally initiate proceedings
                                to amend the Town of Alta land use regulations.
Following the public hearing, the planning commission will hold a regular business meeting where it may take
action to vote to recommend the amendments to the Town Code to the Alta Town Council for final adoption.
                          Questions regarding the draft ordinance may be directed to:
                        Chris Cawley, Town Manager, P.O. Box 8016, Alta, Utah 84092,
                     by calling 801-742-6010 or by email to Ccawley@townofalta.utah.gov
 Individuals making public comment may speak for up to three minutes during the public hearing. To make a
  comment during the public hearing virtually please contact (molly@townofalta.utah.gov) in advance of the
                                                  meeting.
If you are interested in watching the meeting virtually without making public comment, it will be broadcast on
                                  the Town of Alta Live Stream available here:
                                  https://www.townofalta.utah.gov/live-stream/

        Written public input can be submitted here: https://www.townofalta.utah.gov/public-comment-form/
