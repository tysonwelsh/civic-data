 A.    ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2019-
       2020 BUDGET
       Chairman Nordfelt informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
       Municipal Building Authority Meeting scheduled June 2, 2020, in order for the
       Authority to hear and consider public comments regarding re-opening the FY
       2019-2020 Budget.

       Written documentation previously provided to the Authority included information
       as follows:

              State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
              allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The
              West Valley City Municipal Building Authority holds, as needed on a
              quarterly basis, public hearings to amend the annual budget.

              A Public Notice was posted May 21, 2020 in (the Salt Lake Tribune and
              the Deseret Morning News) general circulation to the general public.
              Notice was given that a public hearing is to be held June 2, 2020 at 6:30
              p.m., West Valley City Hall, 3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley City,
              Utah.

       Chairman Nordfelt opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
       or opposition, Chairman Nordfelt closed the Public Hearing.

       ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 20-03, AMEND THE BUDGET OF
       WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY FOR THE
       FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2019 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2020
       TO REFLECT CHANGES IN THE BUDGET FROM INCREASED
       REVENUES AND AUTHORIZE THE DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
       The Municipal Building Authority previously held a public hearing regarding
       proposed Resolution 20-03 that would amend the budget of West Valley City
       Municipal Building Authority for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2019 and ending
       June 30, 2020 to reflect changes in the budget from increased revenues and
       authorize the disbursement of funds.

       Upon inquiry by Chairman Nordfelt there were no further questions from members
       of the Authority, and he called for a motion.

       Ms. Lang moved to approve Resolution 20-03.

       Mr. Buhler seconded the motion.

       A roll call vote was taken:

 B.    ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING THE FY 2020-2021 BUDGET
       Chairman Nordfelt informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
       Municipal Building Authority Meeting scheduled June 2, 2020, in order for the
       Board to hear and consider public comments regarding the FY 2020-2021 Budget.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              This resolution adopts the final budget for the Municipal Building Authority
              of West Valley City for the 2020-2021 fiscal year after receiving public
              comment at a hearing on June 2, 2020.

              The Municipal Building Authority adopted a tentative budget that was made
              available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s
              Recorder’s office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment,
              before adopting this final budget for FY 2020-2021.

       Chairman Nordfelt opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
       or opposition, Chairman Nordfelt closed the Public Hearing.

       ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 20-04, ADOPT THE ANNUAL
       BUDGET OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING
       AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1, 2020
       AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2021
       The Municipal Building Authority previously held a public hearing regarding
       proposed Resolution 20-04 that would adopt the annual budget of the West Valley
       City Municipal Building Authority for the fiscal year commencing July 1, 2020 and
       ending June 30, 2021.

       Upon inquiry by Chairman Nordfelt there were no further questions from members
       of the Authority, and he called for a motion.
