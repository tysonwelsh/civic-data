                                                                   MINUTES
                                                       WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                       Tuesday, March 26, 2019 - 10:00 a.m.
                                             Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who appear and speak at a
County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy
or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Scott K. Jenkins, James “Jim” H. Harvey, and Gage Froerer.
OTHER STAFF PRESENT: Ricky D. Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney; and
Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME – Chair Jenkins
B. INVOCATION – Scott Parke
C. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Stacy Skeen
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY – Chair Jenkins
E. PUBLIC COMMENTS:
F. CONSENT ITEMS:
   1. Warrants #1736-1741 and #437726-438006 in the amount of $1,607,844.31.
   2. Purchase orders in the amount of $117,756.27.

      Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the consent items; Commissioner Harvey seconded.

G. ACTION ITEMS:
      1. INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH NORTH OGDEN CITY TO PARTICIPATE IN FUNDING THE GOLDEN SPIKE
         STORM WATER COALITION & PROVIDING FUNDS TO HIRE A PROFESSIONAL MANAGER FOR COALITION –
         RESOLUTION 21-2019.
            Jared Andersen, County Engineer, stated that this is the ninth of these contracts.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 21-2019 approving the Interlocal Agreement with North Ogden
            City to participate in funding the Golden Spike Storm Water Coalition and providing funds to hire a professional
            manager for the Coalition; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
      2. RIGHT-OF-WAY CONTRACT WITH BENCHLAND INVESTMENTS FOR THE LITTLE WEBER CUTOFF CHANNEL.
            Jared Andersen, County Engineer, stated that this is for the upper portion for this Channel, between 5500 W. and
            the river, and the county had been working with these property owners for quite some time. Recently, Chair
            Jenkins had spoken with Randy Marriott who had told him that the county had not dug that channel to the Lake
            and if the county used the channel, the county would flood his property. Chair Jenkins asked Mr. Andersen to
            contact Mr. Marriott this week and report back to him. Mr. Andersen believes that the majority had been cleaned
            out and that it was ready for the water to flow out.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the right-of-way contract with Benchland Investments for the Little
            Weber Cutoff Channel; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
      3. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE WEBER COUNTY FAIR BOARD – RESOLUTION 22-2019.
            Ashton Wilson, County Fair Director, presented the names for the four appointments.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 22-2019 reappointing Glenda Lee and Susan Clonts through
            12/31/2019 and appointing Teddy Andersen and Connie Robinson through 12/31/2022 to the Weber County Fair
            Board; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
      4. APPROVAL OF WEBER COUNTY CONFINED SPACE ENTRY POLICY AND PROCEDURES.
            Sarah Swan, Human Resources Director, stated that this policy is simply to follow OSHA’s guidelines.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the Weber County Confined Space Entry Policy and Procedures;
            Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
      5. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE WEBER AREA DISPATCH 911 & EMERGENCY SERVICES
         DISTRICT – RESOLUTION 23-2019.
            Tina Mathieu, Weber Area Dispatch 911 & Emergency Services Executive Director, presented the two names for
            reappointment/appointment.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 23-2019 reappointing Mayor Jim Truett and appointing
         Commissioner Gage Froerer, both through 12/31/2022, to the Weber Area Dispatch 911 & Emergency Services
         District; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    6. CONTRACT WITH THE CHINESE RAILROAD WORKERS DESCENDANTS ASSOCIATION.
         Marty Smith, Ogden Eccles Conference Center General Manager, stated that this agreement is in partnership with
         Zions Bank, Spike 150 and the county. It waives the building rental for the Gold Mountain musical.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the contract with the Chinese Railroad Workers Descendants
         Association; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    7. CONTRACT WITH SPIKER’S HIGH SCHOOL RODEO CLUB TO HOLD A SPIKER’S HIGH SCHOOL RODEO AT
       THE GOLDEN SPIKE EVENT CENTER (GSEC).
         Duncan Olsen, GSEC General Manager, presented this contract.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with the Spiker’s High School Rodeo Club to hold a
         Spiker’s High School Rodeo at the Golden Spike Event Center; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    8. CONTRACT WITH COMMANDER INDUSTRIAL GROUP FOR DISMANTLING A SPRUNG STRUCTURE (TENT).
         Bill Ross, County Community Development Assistant Director, stated that Commander was the low bidder and
         their service will include dismantling, marking and cataloging the substructure parts for future reassembly.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the contract with the Commander Industrial Group for dismantling a
         sprung structure; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
H. PUBLIC HEARING
   1.
      Commissioner Harvey moved to adjourn the public meeting and convene the public hearing; Commissioner
      Froerer seconded.
      Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    2. PUBLIC HEARING TO DISCUSS AND/OR TAKE ACTION ON AMENDMENTS TO THE OPERATING AND CAPITAL
       BUDGET OF WEBER COUNTY FOR THE 2019 CALENDAR YEAR.
       Scott Parke, County Comptroller, gave highlights which included that new grants were received for the Sheriff’s
       Office, that some expenditures for the monument preservation fund needed to be added to the 2019 budget, that
       revenue from some sale of fixed assets would be used for building improvements, and that the bulk of the request
       is a rollover from prior years for approved projects that did not get completed. Mr. Parke addressed Commissioner
       Froerer’s question on the Blue Stakes Reviewing Services referring to the current contract and stating that the
       money will come out of the Monument Preservation Fund. Commissioner Harvey spoke of the importance of
       monument preservation. Chair Jenkins asked that the format for these presentations be changed starting with next
       quarter to more easily identify the proposed changes.
    3. Public comments: None.
    4.
       Commissioner Harvey moved to adjourn public hearing and reconvene public meeting; Commissioner Froerer

      Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
