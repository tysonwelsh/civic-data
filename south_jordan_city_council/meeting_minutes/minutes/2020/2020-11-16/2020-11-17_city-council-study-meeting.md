STUDY MEETING

   A. Welcome, Roll Call and Introduction: By Mayor Dawn R. Ramsey

Mayor Ramsey welcomed everyone present. She excused Council Member Marlor, who will be
arriving shortly. She introduced the electronic meeting.

   B. Invocation: By Chief Technology Director, Jon Day

Chief Technology Director Day offered the invocation.

   C. Mayor and Council Coordination

Council Member Zander said staff did a good job on the Light the Night ceremony. Mayor
Ramsey concurred.

Council Member Shelton reported on the southwest Salt Lake County group that includes
WFRC, UDOT, and UTA. They are doing an analysis of our area. We want the input of that
study as part of the ask of the legislature for transportation funding. He said they will be talking
about it in West Growth Leadership tomorrow. They will be bringing a Resolution forward
supporting the Western Growth Coalition’s Resolution. They will likely bring that forward in
January. He also expressed thanks for Melinda Seager, who does a great job.

Council Member Marlor arrived at this time.

Mayor Ramsey said there are 73 bill files already open regarding policing. There is a lot that they
will be working on and watching during the Legislative Session.

    D. Discussion/Review of Regular Council Meeting

Mayor Ramsey said they will be having an ADU discussion tonight. She said South Jordan has
allowed ADU’s for a long time. She said they may want to wait on a decision to change ADU’s
in the city until they see what Legislation is passed on that this year.

Council Member Zander asked about action item J. City Attorney Loose said it is the same
action they took last year at this time. He said staff will be working through environmental and
planning issues with the developer over the next year. If they don’t extend the put option, it will
trigger the buy back of the Glenmoor golf course by the city.

City Attorney Loose said Mr. Young does not want to give the course back to the city. He has
put millions into it. He is happy with how the course is going. Mr. Young wants to make sure
they aren’t up against a deadline on the water reclamation piece of property.

City Attorney Loose asked that item J. be moved up on the agenda.

Council Member Marlor made a motion to go into an executive closed session to discuss the
purchase, exchange, or lease of real property. Council Member Shelton seconded the
motion. The vote was unanimous in favor.

    E. Executive Closed Session: Discussion of the purchase, exchange, or lease of real
       property (By City Attorney, Ryan Loose)

Council Member Shelton made a motion to adjourn the closed meeting. Council Member
McGuire seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous in favor.

ADJOURNMENT

Council Member Marlor made a motion to adjourn. Council Member Zander seconded the
motion. The vote was unanimous in favor.

The November 17, 2020 City Council meeting adjourned at 6:37 p.m.
