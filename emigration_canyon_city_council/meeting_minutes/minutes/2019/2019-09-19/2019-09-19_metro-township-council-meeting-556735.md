Council Business
Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission Appointment
Council Member Brems stated Tim Harpst has agreed to serve on the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission. Mr. Harpst is a professional traffic engineer and
will bring valuable experience to the commission.
Council Member Brems, seconded by Council Member Hawkes, moved to appoint
Tim Harpst to the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission. The motion passed
unanimously.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission
Robert Pinon, Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission,
updated the Council on the revision of the code enforcement policy. He also made a
recommendation to approve a moratorium on new development in the canyon. The moratorium
will stop new development and the issuance of permits until canyon infrastructure can be
addressed and the general plan can be updated.

Agreement Amendments between Salt Lake County, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (GSLMSD), and the Metro Townships
David Church reviewed the following resolutions approving the first and second
amendments to the Master Interlocal Agreements and an agreement with Addressing:
First Amendment Resolution
RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-01

Date: September 19, 2019

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNCIL OF EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
TOWNSHIP AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF THE TOWNSHIP TO SIGN FIRST
AMENDMENT TO THE MASTER INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN
GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISRICT, SALT LAKE COUNTY,
COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP, EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
TOWNSHIP, KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP, AND WHITE CITY METRO
TOWNSHIP FOR MUNICIPAL, ADMINISTRTATIVE, AND OPERATIONAL
SERVICES.
RECITALS
A. Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council is committed to participate with the Greater
Salt Lake Municipal Services District for its municipal services.
B. An Interlocal Agreement is needed to define the services and set out the various rights
and duties of all the participants in the municipal services District and has previously
been adopted by all parties.
C. An amendment to the Interlocal Agreement has been negotiated between the County,
the District and the Townships within the County to changing certain the terms of getting
services from the District and from Salt Lake County.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Council of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township:
1. The Mayor of the Township is hereby authorized to sign the First Amendment to the
Master Interlocal Agreement for municipal and other services in the form attached as
Exhibit A hereto subject only to the other participating entities approving the same form
of agreement.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Emigration Canyon Metro Township, Salt Lake County,
Utah, this 19th day of September, 2019.

Addressing Services Resolution
RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-03

Date: September 19, 2019

A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNCIL OF EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
TOWNSHIP AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF THE TOWNSHIP TO SIGN AN
INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL
SERVICES DISRICT, SALT LAKE COUNTY, COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP,
EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP, KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP,
AND WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP FOR ADRESSING SERVICES TO BE
PROVIDED BY THE COUNTY.
RECITALS
A. Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council is committed to participate with the Greater
Salt Lake Municipal Services District for its municipal services.
B. One of the services required is “Addressing” which is the process of assigning new
addresses to property, correcting errors in addressing and coordinating the naming of
streets.
C. Addressing services have been performed by the County for the Township in the past
and now an Interlocal Agreement is needed to define the services and set out the
