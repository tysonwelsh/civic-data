24   Call to Order
25
26   Chair Staggs called the Riverton Redevelopment Agency Board Meeting to order at 8:47 PM
27   and conducted a roll call. Board members Buroker, Haymond, McCay, McDougal, and Pierucci
28   were present.
29
30   Discussion/Action – N/A
31
32   Closed Session
33
34      Discussion of the sale, purchase, exchange or lease of real property, including any
35      form of a water rights or water share.
36
37   At 8:47 PM, Board Member McDougal MOVED to suspend the Redevelopment Agency Meeting
38   and convene in a Closed Session to discuss the sale, purchase, exchange or lease of real
39   property, including any form of a water rights or water share. Board Member McCay SECONDED
40   the motion. Chair Staggs called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for a
41   roll-call vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-yes, Haymond-yes, McCay-yes, McDougal-yes,
42   and Pierucci-yes. The motion passed unanimously.
43
44   The Redevelopment Agency meeting resumed in a Closed Session at 8:55 PM. Minutes for the
45   Closed Session were taken and recorded and are now on file as a Protected Record.
46
47   Board Member Haymond MOVED to adjourn the Closed Session at 9:27 PM. Board Member
48   McDougal SECONDED the motion. Chair Staggs called for discussion on the motion. Chair
49   Staggs called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-yes, Haymond-yes, McCay-
50   yes, McDougal-yes, and Pierucci-yes. The motion passed unanimously.
