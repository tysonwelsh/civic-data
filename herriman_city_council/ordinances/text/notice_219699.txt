PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on June 12, 2014 at 7:00 p.m. in the Community Center Council Chambers, 13011 South Pioneer Street (6000 West) Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 14-25 to adopt a final budget, and to determine the rate of tax and levying taxes in Herriman, Utah. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder’s Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
