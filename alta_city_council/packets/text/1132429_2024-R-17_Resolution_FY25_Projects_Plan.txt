                                  TOWN OF ALTA

                           RESOLUTION NO. 2024-R-17

      A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE TOWN OF ALTA FISCAL YEAR
                      2025 PROJECTS PLAN

        WHEREAS, the Town developed a Fiscal Year 2025 Projects Plan, which
identifies projects to be implemented during current and future fiscal years;

        WHEREAS, this Projects Plan includes the Capital Projects Plan; and

        WHEREAS, recent events have indicated the need to update the plan:

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE ALTA TOWN COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. The Amended Town of Alta Fiscal Year 2025 Capital Projects Plan is
attached as Exhibit A.

    ADOPTED THIS 20th day of June, 2024.
