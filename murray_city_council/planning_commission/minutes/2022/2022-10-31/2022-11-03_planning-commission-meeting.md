TRIANGLE AUTO SALES – 4195 South 500 West, Unit #3

Ms. Nixon presented the application for requesting Conditional Use Permit approval to
allow the operation of an auto sales business at the subject property within the M-G Zone.
The applicant proposes to operate an auto sales business from unit #3 at the subject
property, this is a typical 900 ft2 unit within the industrial park. The applicant would like to
operate an auto sales business inside the warehouse unit and any auto repair would be
conducted at another location. Parking on this property is extremely limited, and City

Staff has expressed concerns about the maintenance of clear access through the
property as well as overflow parking from business activity on the site onto 500 West.
Unit #3 is an open shop with a very small office area indicated on the plan and a small
restroom. Required parking for warehouses and open shops is calculated at a rate of four
(4) spaces for every 1,000 square feet of office and one (1) space for every 750 square
feet of warehouse space. Based on the floor plan provided by the applicant the proposed
use requires one parking space. The parking that is indicated on the site plan shows two
(2) exterior parking spaces in front of the unit. Parking code regulations do not allow for
parking in front of overhead doors to be considered as a space. Staff has determined that
the two spaces are adequate for any customer there may be. The sales vehicles will be
inside the unit. On July 19, 2018, the Planning Commission granted conditional use
permit approval for Master Scrapers (project #18-86) operating in the adjacent unit #2. At
that time one of the conditions of approval was to stripe one ADA parking stall. Upon a
site visit on October 25, 2022, it was found that there is no ADA parking stall within a
couple hundred feet of units #2 & 3. It is recommended that one ADA stall be provided
for the units near #36. The applicant will be required to have their vehicles for sale
located entirely within the subject unit. All parking associated with this unit will need to be
used for customers. Staff is recommending approval with conditions. Applicant does
have sufficient room for ADA stall and should be as close to the entrance as possible.

The applicant was not present to represent this request. The commissioners agreed to
proceed with the application even though the applicant was not present. They expressed
concern with the applicant not being in attendance. The agenda item was opened for
public comment. No public comments were made and the open comment was closed for
this item.

A question was asked about the integrity of the parking lot. Ms. Nixon indicated that the
parking lot needs repair. It’s uneven and broken up, making it difficult for ADA access.
The zoning enforcement officer will not sign off on business licenses until conditions are
met to add striping to the parking lot. The application applies to the building and
unfortunately it is difficult to enforce improving the parking lot when it is private property.
That’s on the owner to repair. The city has been trying to work with the property owner
for years with very little results. The city has tried to entice owners to cooperate by
making the process easier for rental applicants if the owners with repave the parking lots.
Repaving a couple of spots at a time is not a reasonable request. It was questioned if this
motion should be approved without the applicant present to answer questions. It was
decided that the questions should be directed at property owner, not the applicant. Mr.
Lowry expressed concern that the applicant should still be present.

Ms. Patterson made the motion to approve the conditional use permit of Triangle Auto
Sales subject to the following conditions:

   1. The project shall meet all applicable Building and Fire Code requirements.
   2. The applicant shall obtain Murray City Building Permits for any construction.

DISCUSSSION ITEM

Discussion on Section 17.56 related to the planning commission authorizing community
development staff to administratively review conditional use permit applications, led by
Susan Nixon. Section 17.56.03 states: "Planning commission may authorize members of
community development department to grant or deny conditional use permits". the
planning staff would like input from the commissioners. Chair Lowry commented that they
would not hold a public hearing if staff reviews the CUP's. He commented that it could
Planning Commission

be viewed as not being as transparent, but could streamline longer meetings on
redundant items.

Commissioner Patterson was stated that she cannot remember if the commission has
ever denied applications and on occasion they have added conditions to help mitigate
impacts. She stated that she is comfortable that the planning staff can do the same and
add conditions if necessary to help mitigate impacts. Lisa Milkavich felt it is important to
allow the opportunity for public input. Chair Lowry stated that it is an important part of
participating in a republic for conditional use permits and the public to engage in this
process. It was discussed that often the commission doesn’t add meaningful input and
that staff could handle these items. Benefits are that the meetings would be streamlined
and save staff time, potentially hours, per applications. One negative is some staff could
potentially not engage adequately in doing the leg work needed. The discussion
continued at length as to the pros and cons of conditional use permits being administered
by community development staff. One take-away is the larger concern for the operation
of small car sales businesses throughout the valley and the need to improve the
ordinances that regulate them. More research would need to be done to determine the
failure rate of these businesses, as well as the impact they have on the community. Also,
another take-away is what is the benefit of changing the process of conditional use
permits. There needs to be a tangible benefit and more data needs to be gathered.
