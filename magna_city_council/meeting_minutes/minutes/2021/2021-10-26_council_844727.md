Magna Water District

                Clint Dailey, General Manager, Magna Water District, stated the district declared
level 2 water restrictions in its service area in response to the drought. Water customers reduced
water usage by 50 percent and 200 million gallons were kept in the aquafers. The district was not
heavy handed in enforcing water reduction so, only seven warning letters were issued. Jordan
Valley Conservancy District are strongly encouraging agencies with planning authority, to adopt
a more conservative-minded landscaping ordinance for future development. Over the summer
there were issues in the response to leaks, because of this, an operational response protocol has
been developed. The State has a Low-Income Household Water Assistant Program (LIHWAP) to
help those that qualify, pay their water bills, this information will be included in the customer mailer.
He updated the Council on the progress of various projects.

Utah Main Street Program – Resolution No. 21-10-01

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution showing intent to participate in the
Utah Main Street Program:

RESOLUTION NO. 21-10-01                                                        Date: October 26, 2021

        A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL SHOWING
        INTENT TO PARTICIPATE IN THE UTAH MAIN STREET PROGRAM

                                              RECITALS

       WHEREAS, the Governor’s Office of Economic Opportunity (Go Utah) is creating and
administering the Utah Main Street Program; and

      WHEREAS, the new Utah Main Street Program will help Utah communities revitalize their
economy, appearance, and image of their downtown commercial districts by providing a
framework and resources to support a community’s downtown revitalization efforts.

        WHEREAS, Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is home to Historic Magna Main Street, an
iconic, charming community center that hearkens back to the main street of old America; and

        WHEREAS, in early 2021, Magna Main Street was accepted into the National Register of
Historic Places, the official list of the Nation’s historic places deemed worthy of preservation by
the National Park Service; and
                                                    2
DATE            TUESDAY                                OCTOBER                              26, 2021


       WHEREAS; Historic Magna Main Street could greatly benefit from tools and training that
can help with revitalization, small business growth, and positioning the street as a vibrant, historic
destination which attracts residents and visitors.

                                           RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, it is resolved by the Magna Metro Township Council:

   1. That Magna hereby consents to applying for membership in the Utah Main Street
      Program under the “Tier 1 – Engaged Main Street” designation. This designation would
      require Magna to agree to the following terms:

           a. 1 to 3 year commitment to the Program, including costs related to attending Utah
              Main Street Program events (see below).

           b. Have a representative attend the following Utah Main Street Program events: 1
              Main Street workshop per year, the Utah Main Street conference, and the “Main
              Street 101” Training.

           c. Submit an annual report to Go Utah.

   2. That this Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its passage.

   3. In the event of any conflict between this Resolution and any other enactment of Magna,
      this Resolution shall control.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
26th day of October, 2021.

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution authorizing the execution of an
Interlocal Agreement regarding the issuance of bonds:

        A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING AND
        AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH
        THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT, COPPERTON
        METRO TOWNSHIP, EMIGRAITON CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP, KEARNS
        METRO TOWNSHIP, TOWN OF BRIGHTON, WHITE CITY METRO
        TOWNSHIP, AND SALT LAKE COUNTY REGARDING THE ISSUANCE OF
        BONDS TO PAY FOR INFRASTRUCTURE

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a Municipality pursuant to Utah Code
§§10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, Magna is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code Ann.
§ 10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor, and

       WHEREAS, Magna is authorized under the Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Ann. §
11-13-101 et seq. (the “Interlocal Cooperation Act”), to enter into an interlocal agreement with the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “District”), Copperton Metro Township,
Emigration Canyon Metro Township, Kearns Metro Township, Town of Brighton, White City Metro
Township, and Salt Lake County Respecting the Issuance of Bonds to Pay for Infrastructure, and

        WHEREAS, Magna, by and through its Council, has determined authorizing the District to
issue bonds for infrastructure, as set forth in the attached Interlocal Agreement, Attachment “A”,
will benefit the residents of Magna and will contribute to the prosperity, well-being, peace, and
comfort of the community.

       NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council approves, and
authorizes the Mayor to sign, the attached Interlocal Agreement (“Agreement”) with the Greater
Salt Lake Municipal Services District, Copperton Metro Township, Emigration Canyon Metro
Township, Kearns Metro Township, Town of Brighton, White City Metro Township, and Salt Lake
County Respecting the Issuance of Bonds to Pay for Infrastructure (ATTACHEMENT A).

        IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED, the Mayor and City Attorney may make non-material
changes to the Agreement prior to signing if needed to respond to comments and suggestions of
other parties to the Agreement, it being understood and agreed that each party to the Agreement
is represented by legal counsel that may have input to the document.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED this 26th day of October, 2021 by the Magna Metro Township
Council in Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah.
