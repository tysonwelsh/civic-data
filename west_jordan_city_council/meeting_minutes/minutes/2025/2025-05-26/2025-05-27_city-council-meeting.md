 4. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. Ordinance No. 25-18, a Rezone to R-1-9 for Bingham Heights Phase 5 (20.96 acres at
          5891 West 7000 South)
          Lynsi Neve with Perry Homes oriented the Council with the request to rezone
          approximately 21 acres to R-1-9 for a minimum of 9,000 square foot lots. She said she
          believed the request to be consistent with Bingham Heights development on the other
          side of Mountain View Corridor, and consistent with the goals and objectives of the
          General Plan.

           Chair Lamb said he remembered approval of the first phase, and said he was excited for
           the next phase of development. Responding to a question from Vice Chair Whitelock, Ms.
           Neve said the homes were intended to be sold to individual homeowners. Council
           Member Jacob asked if there were anything in place that would prevent someone not
           intending to be an owner occupant from purchasing the homes. Ms. Neve said she was not
           aware of any such measure.

           Associate Planner Mark Forsythe said the request to rezone stemmed from previous
           Council direction that holdover zones from the West Side Planning Area (WSPA) would
           need to be rezoned to zones more conducive to the General Plan. He stated R-1-9 was in
           line with surrounding zones.

           Council Member Bloom asked why R-1-9 was chosen instead of R-1-10 or R-1-12. Mr.
           Forsythe said he believed R-1-9 was chosen to optimize the maximum number of lots. Mr.
           Forsythe said the plan was to provide a trail connection between Phase 5 and Phase 4. He
           said storm water and detention details would be worked out in the preliminary
           subdivision process. Council Member Green suggested the Council consider eliminating
           the outdated zones.

           Chair Lamb opened a public hearing at 7:25 pm.
           Comments:
           None
           Chair Lamb closed the public hearing at 7:25 pm.

           Council Member Green said it was not often a developer wanted to use low density
           designation, and thanked Perry Homes for recognizing that low-density areas were still
           needed.

           MOTION:      Council Member Shelton moved to APPROVE Ordinance No. 25-18 a
                        Rezone to R-1-9 for Bingham Heights Phase 5 (20.96 acres at 5891
                        West 7000 South).
                        Vice Chair Whitelock seconded the motion.
