RECOMMENDATION:
Approve Resolution 25-11 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule of Draper City as
proposed.


BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule lists fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and
administrative service costs.

The proposed changes will update the fees schedule to reflect current Draper City
operation and costs associated with allowed fees. The changes will also update the
transportation impact fees to match City ordinance 1482.
