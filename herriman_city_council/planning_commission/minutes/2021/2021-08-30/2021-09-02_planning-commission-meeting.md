   1.2. Review of Agenda Items – Planning Staff
The Commissioners requested no further information and had no questions.

    1.3. Discussion of Text Amendment to Move Planning Commission Meetings to Wednesdays
         in 2022 – Clinton Spencer, Planning Manager
City Planner asked the Commission if they would be interested in moving meetings to Wednesday evenings
for 2022. This would also eliminate the possibility of having more than one meeting per week. Commissioners
Rypien and Fenn were in favor of moving them to Wednesday. Commissioner Jacobson recommended
standardization of all meetings to Wednesday nights and consistency was ideal. Commissioners Berbert and
Palmer had conflicts with Wednesday evenings and were not in favor of changing them. City Planner Maloy
discussed the possibility of switching meeting scheduling from a Title Ten regulation to Title One regulation
for more administrative control.

   1.4. Goodboro Development Discussion – Michael Maloy, Planning Director
The Goodboro discussion has been rescheduled to the next meeting in two weeks.

     1.5. Accessory Building Ordinance Amendment Discussion – Clinton Spencer Planning
          Manager
Planning Manager Spencer requested direction from the Commission regarding accessory buildings.
Commissioners recommended increased code enforcement, as many residents violated the rules without
realizing. The guidelines should be made easier to understand. The Commissioners thought anything above
200 square feet should require a building permit, and lot size was an important factor. Commissioners
recommended focusing on matching color and quality to the main structure. They also agreed larger lots should
have more freedom with proper protections for neighboring homes. Smaller lot options should be more
limited. Placement of small, preconstructed sheds should be allowed with no regulations. Simplify
measurement of the yard sizes and allow them in back yards. The construction of large accessory buildings on
larger lots may be allowable on a percentage of the lot size. Commissioners debated the best measurement
tools and allowable sizes. They requested staff to come back with more specific guidelines broken down by
small, medium, and large lots with a variety of building sizes, to help them identify appropriate standards.

The work meeting was adjourned by consensus at 7:01 p.m.

    3.1. Request: Approval for the Copper Fields Site Plan at Creek Ridge in the R-2-10 Zone
         Located Generally at 6400 W Herriman Parkway
         Acres: 42.41
         Applicant: Brandon Watson w/ Edge Homes (representative)
         File Number: C2020-024
City Planner Michael Maloy reviewed the request. He reported the applicant has worked with landscape architect
Heidi Shegrud on design, city standards compliance, and improvements consistent with the MDA. He identified
the target area location, a commercial portion would be on a forthcoming plat. A Church site was planned on
the Northwest corner. Asphalt trail connections were identified. The perimeter road was a wide service lane
along the drainage channel to be paved, consistent with the trail master plan. The landscape plan incorporated
a mix of vegetative materials and was consistent with drought tolerant landscaping. Design guidelines and
building elevations were presented. Materials have been reviewed by Suburban Land Reserve. Staff
recommended approval of the site plan and elevations with conditions of approval.

Applicant Brandon Watson with Edge Homes approached the podium and offered to answer questions. The
park had been moved to the east side, it would be private and maintained by the HOA.

Commissioner Bradford asked about the Kate, Jaden and Farmhouse elevations which had no stone or brick
unless the homebuilder chose to add it. Applicant Watson stated if stone or brick wainscoting was required,
they would be added to comply with the City standards and/or MDA. Commissioner Rypien asked about
pedestrian connections to trails. City Planner Maloy explained staff recommendation #1 included this as a
condition of approval.

Recommendation
   1. The applicant shall design and construct an asphalt paved pathway (8′-0″ minimum width) through Parcel
      A in compliance with Herriman City Standards and Trail Master Plan.
   2. The applicant shall design and construct a pedestrian pathway from Road I to Parcel C, which provides
      both stormwater detention and recreational space for the development.
   3. Prior to final subdivision plat approval, the applicant shall submit to the City Attorney for review and
      approval a copy of the proposed Codes, Covenants, and Restrictions (CC&Rs) to ensure the proper
      creation of a Homeowners Association, which shall be responsible for maintenance of all privately
      owned common space including private streets within the subdivision.
   4. Following approval by the City Attorney, the applicant shall concurrently record the approved CC&Rs
      with the subdivision plat.
   5. Home elevations must comply with MDA regarding stone and brick.
   6. The building elevations shall comply with Herriman City Code or as specified in the Creek Ridge Master
      Development Agreement.

    4.1     Request: Approval to Amend Title 10 to Update City Ordinances to Reflect Recent
            Changes in State Legislation Pertaining to Single Family Design Standards (Public
            Hearing)
            Applicant: Herriman City
            File Number: Z2021-079
Planner Talia Wright clarified House Bill 1003 which only applied to single family homes and would not apply
to homes in a PUD. Planner Wright reviewed the request and text amendment changes. Minimum floor area
was reduced to 1,000 square feet, excluding the garage. Roof pitch may no longer be regulated. Commissioner
Jacobson asked about the wording of the text change regarding allowing single-family or two-family dwellings
in the PUD. He thought the wording was confusing. Planner Maloy clarified the city would retain the rights for
establishing standards as conditions of approval for the PUD as part of the review process. Commissioner
Jacobson was concerned existing PUDs would want to change to the current plan. City Planner Maloy
recommended the addition of a condition of approval, with the motion, requesting for staff to clarify the
wording to exclude single-family PUDs. Planner Talia Wright identified an error on the presentation for draft
amendment item D and clarified the wording. It should say dwelling shall include a minimum two car garage
(24 by 22 feet) or equivalent approved by Commission. Commissioner Rypien clarified the inclusion of item G,
which proposed that building elevations must vary every three homes adjacent to and across. It was explained
the HOA and Builder may additionally have their own requirements.

Chair Berbert opened the public hearing.

No comments were offered.

Chair Berbert closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Adam Jacobson MOVED to refer to City Council item 4.2 File # Z2021-079 Approval to Amend Title 10 to
Update City Ordinances to Reflect Recent Changes in State Legislation Pertaining to Single Family Design Standards with two
adjustments, #1 item G should say building elevations shall vary so that the same house was not built within three lots, or across
the street of the same elevation, and #2 have planning staff address the language for PUDs on the except for detached single family
or two family dwellings. Commissioner Brody Rypien SECONDED the motion.
