today, he sent two letters to the County.  One was to the Mayor regarding the new liaison

improve communication.  The other letter is a recommendation for a representative on the 
Municipal Services District (MSD).  However, Council Member Burdick is willing to keep 
those MSD responsibilities as he chairs the County Council.

PROCLAMATION
WHEREAS, in 1972, France Davis came to Salt Lake City with his wife 
Willene and their family to fulfill a one -year teaching fellowship at the 
University of Utah; and
WHEREAS, upon their arrival, the Davises joined Salt Lake Calvary Baptist 
Church, where France would become pastor in 1974 and would serve in that 
position for the next 46 years; and
WHEREAS, in those 46 years, Reverend France Davis has employed his 
academic and spiritual scholarship, his duty to faith, his dedication to 
education and civil rights, and his love for others to improve and enhance the 
quality of life not only for his congregation, but also for residents throughout 
Salt Lake County and the State of Utah; and
Page 2 of 9

WHEREAS, born in rural Georgia in 1946, Reverend Davis attended and 
graduated from the segregated Waynesboro High & Industrial School, joined 
the United States Air Force where he served as an aircraft mechanic, and 
went on to earn a B .A. degree in rhetoric from the University of California at 
Berkeley, B .S. degree in religion from Westminster College, M .A. degree in 
mass communication from the University of Utah, and Master of Ministry 
degree from Northwest Nazarene University; and
WHEREAS, Reverend Davis participated in the civil rights campaigns of Dr . 
Martin Luther King Jr ., including the March on Washington for Jobs and 
Freedom in 1963, and the Selma -to-Montgomery Voting Rights Marches in 
1965; and
WHEREAS, Reverend Davis has modeled a “servant attitude,” asking, 
“What can I do for somebody else?” rather than “What’s in it for me?” and, 
believing that “Our lives begin to end the very day we become silent about 
what matters to other people,” Reverend Davis has engaged other 
denominations and government and civic leaders in his work to improve 
education, provide scholarships, build housing for veterans, meet the needs 
of underserved populations, and strengthen our communities.
WHEREAS, Reverend Davis has built a strong foundation of trust, stability 
and faith at Calvary Missionary Baptist Church and in the community and is 
now entrusting Calvary ’s leadership to the able stewardship, hands, and heart 
of The Reverend Dr. Oscar T. Moses.
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Jenny Wilson, Mayor of Salt Lake County, in 
recognition  of and appreciation for the remarkable, positive impact of 
France Davis on the lives and institutions around us, and in anticipation of his 
continued influence in our community through his writing, teaching, 
mentoring and friendship, do hereby proclaim Tuesday, February 4, 2020 as
Reverend France Davis Day in Salt Lake County
Adopted this 4th day of February, 2020.
By /s/ Jennifer Wilson

RESOLUTION NO. 5691
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO GRANT A 
PERPETUAL EASEMENT TO MAGNA WATER DISTRICT
RECITALS
A. Salt Lake County (the “County”) acquired title to a parcel of real property 
located at approximately 4042 South 7200 West in Magna, Utah, Parcel No . 
Page 5 of 9
County Council Meeting Minutes February 4, 2020
14-33-406-002 (the “Property”) in 2013.
B. The prior owner granted an easement with a twenty -year renewable term 
to Magna Water District, formerly known as Magna Water Company 
(“Magna Water ”) to install and maintain an underground waterline on the 
Property (“Prior Easement”), but the Prior Easement was never recorded.
C. Based upon the unrecorded Prior Easement, Magna Water installed and 
has maintained an underground waterline and related improvements 
(“Waterline”) on the Property.
D. The County is in the process of developing the Property into a regional 
park and discovered the existence of the Waterline.
E. To clean up the record and acknowledge the existence of the Waterline, 
Magna Water would like to obtain and record a perpetual easement on the 
Property to allow for ongoing operation, repair and maintenance of the 
Waterline.
F. As considering for this perpetual easement, Magna Water agrees to 
release the Prior Easement, which the Salt Lake County Real Estate Section 
has determined to constitute fair and adequate consideration in exchange for 
this easement.
G. The County and Magna Water have prepared an Easement Purchase 
Agreement (”Purchase Agreement ”) attached as Exhibit A hereto, and a 
related Perpetual Easement (“Easement”), wherein the County grants a 
perpetual waterline easement across the Property to the Magna Water.
H. It has been determined that the best interests of the County and the 
general public will be served by granting the Easement to Magna Water.  The 
terms and conditions of the Purchase Agreement and the Easement are in 
compliance with all applicable state statutes and county ordinances.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the Purchase Agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit A 
and by this reference made a part of this Resolution, is hereby approved; and 
the Mayor is hereby authorized to execute said Purchase Agreement.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED by the Salt Lake County that the Mayor and
