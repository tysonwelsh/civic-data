IN CONSIDERATION of the foregoing hereinafter set forth, it is mutually agreed by the parties as follows:

1. Riverton City will be purchasing ten feet of land along the street frontage of 1300 West (5,158 sf) to be included
   into the City’s Right-of-Way (ROW). Riverton City will pay the grantor $43,843.1
2. The grantor agrees to grant Riverton City a 20-feet wide Temporary Construction Easement (TCE) (4,320 sf)
   across the 1300 West Street frontage of the grantor’s property for construction and installation of
   improvements. The TCE will expire on December 31, 2021. Riverton City will pay the grantor $2,938. 1
3. The grantor agrees to grant Riverton City a 10-feet wide Temporary Construction Easement (TCE) (4,320 sf)
   across the 1300 West Street frontage of the grantor’s property for construction and installation of
   improvements. The TCE will expire on December 31, 2021. Riverton City will pay the grantor $1,040.1
4. Riverton City will be compensating the grantor for existing improvements on said property. Improvements
   include the following: 1,490 square feet privacy barrier. Riverton City will pay the grantor $1,751.1
5. City may remove existing improvements in the existing Right of Way. If improvements within said Right of Way
   belong to the grantor, and the grantor desires to retain them, they must be removed by the grantor before
   construction begins.
6. Riverton City will install curb & gutter, and MSE retaining wall along the grantor’s street frontage.
7. Property within the temporary construction easement shall be returned in as good or similar manner as was
   first entered in by the Contractor as part of the construction project, except as otherwise noted in this
   agreement.
8. All work done under this agreement shall conform to all applicable building, fire, and sanitary laws, ordinances,
   specifications and regulations relating to such work, and shall be done in a good and workman like manner.
9. This Agreement is a complete agreement and final understanding between the parties. Other prior discussions
   representations, agreements or other considerations expressed between the parties but not made a part of this
   Agreement are not binding upon any party to this Agreement. Grantor understands this agreement is an option
   until approved by Riverton City.
