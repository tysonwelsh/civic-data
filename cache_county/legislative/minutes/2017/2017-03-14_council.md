PUBLIC HEARING: MARCH 14, 2017-5:40 P.M.-ORDINANCE NO. 2017-02 — AMENDING
SECTION 3.76.010 REGARDING THE TRANSIENT ROOM TAX AND REGULATION OF TAX
— Executive Buttars stated the issue is a 1.25% Transient Room Tax (TRT) increase which
would raise the rate from the current 3% to 4.25%. TRT funds can be used for construction of
recreational facilities and tourism promotion and the county proposes using the funds for the
proposed Fairgrounds Events Center.

Chairman Merrill opened the public hearing and invited public comment.

Todd Johnson, Cache Valley Lodging Association, opposes the increase and understands that the
County intends to use a portion of the TRT funds towards paying off the bond for the Events Center.
Johnson said the 1.25% increase will raise about $226,000.00 and suggested that, if the increase is
approved, only $176,000.00 be used for the Events Center and use the other $50,000.00 to increase
promotions through the Cache Valley Visitors Bureau. Johnson recommended the other funds come from
RAPZ/Restaurant Tax funds. Johnson also recommended forming a Tax Advisory Board and hiring of an
individual to oversee and promote the proposed Events Center. Council member Worthen asked
Johnson what his main concern is with the 1.25% increase and he responded the increase will take away
a little of his competitive edge on lodging.

Dave Nielsen proposed there is an opportunity for some local entrepreneur to build a luxury resort or
guest ranch to generate high lodging dollars in Cache County.

Julie Hollist-Terrill said the Hotel Association suggests a cap at $176,000.00 to be used for the
Fairgrounds Events Center. Hollist asked hoteliers present to raise hands for Council to see the number
present.

Shirlene Davis, Springhill Suites Marriott, said a few dollars a room makes a big difference to conferences
who are booking 100 rooms for a 3-day conference.

Kip Gilley, Old Rock Church, opposes the increase because they are a very small operation and every
penny counts.

Bill Bower said he worked at a resort, Mount Rushmore, many years ago and even with a conference
center, they still have many empty rooms in the winter.

There was no other public comment.

ACTION: Motion by Council member Ward to close the Public Hearing, Ordinance No.
2017-02-Amending Section 3.76.010 regarding the Transient Room Tax and Regulation of
Tax. Zilles seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 7-0.

purposes; and

of four and one quarter percent (4.25%) three-pereent-3%) of the rent for every occupancy of a

policies, or actions are in conflict with this ordinance. In all other respects, such prior
