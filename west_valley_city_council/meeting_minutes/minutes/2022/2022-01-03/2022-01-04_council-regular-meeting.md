COMMENT PERIOD
A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
    Mike Markham provided a kudos to the Public Works department for great work in
    snow removal over the holidays. He indicated that there was a house in West Valley
    that would put on a large holiday show and the resident was advised by City staff
    to not do this given new on-street parking requirements. He indicated that this is
    bad press coming from the City. Mr. Markham stated that he is no longer going to
    speak up. He stated that City employees should be doing their jobs and citizens
    should not be required to report violations. Mr. Markham stated that there should
    be job descriptions for every position in the City.

       Jim Vesock stated that a lot of promises were made during the newly elected
       officials campaigns. He stated that employees need to be held accountable to do
       their jobs and this starts at the top. He indicated that Department Heads need to be
       held accountable by Mr. Pyle and they need to hold their employees accountable as
       well. Mr. Vesock stated that he submitted GRAMA requests from Code
       Enforcement and will offer the Council a report with his findings. He expressed
       concern that the men’s locker rooms at the Family Fitness Center are not clean and
       residents have food and drinks in the gym even though this is prohibited. He
       indicated that management does not care.

       Randy Lucero stated that he is concerned about loud vehicles and street racing. He
       indicated that this is has been going on too long and no one is able to sleep at night.
       He said this is a problem everywhere and West Valley City needs to deal with it.

       Darrell Curtis expressed concern about the $3 bill increase related to street lighting.
       He stated that this is a tax increase in his mind and he wants to know how the funds
       will be used. Mr. Curtis said that some areas of the City have a lot of street lights
       while others don’t. He added that sidewalks are important.

B.     CITY MANAGER COMMENTS
       Wayne Pyle, City Manager, had no comments.

C.     CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS
       Councilmember Huynh stated that he would like to revisit the parking ordinance
       that prevented on-street parking 5 feet from the driveway. He also stated that he
       would like to find a solution to the street racing issue.

PUBLIC HEARINGS
A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2021-2022
     BUDGET
     Mayor Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular Council
     Meeting scheduled January 4, 2022, in order for the City Council to hear and
     consider public comments regarding Re-Opening the FY 2021-2022 Budget

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
              allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The
              West Valley City holds public hearings on budget amendments on a
              quarterly basis each fiscal year.

              A Public Notice was posted December 9, 2021 in public view at City Hall,
              the Family Fitness Center and UCCC; also, posted on the Public Notice
              Website and West Valley City’s Website. Notice was given that a public
              hearing is to be held January 4, 2022 at 6:30 p.m., West Valley City Hall,
              3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley City, Utah.

       Mayor Lang opened the Public Hearing.

       Jim Vesock stated the Finance Department has put the information together well.

       Mayor Lang closed the Public Hearing.

       ACTION: ORDINANCE NO. 21-68, AMEND THE BUDGET OF WEST
       VALLEY CITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2021 AND
       ENDING JUNE 30, 2022 TO REFLECT CHANGES IN THE BUDGET
       FROM         INCREASED         REVENUES           AND        AUTHORIZE          THE
       DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
       The City Council previously held a public hearing regarding proposed Ordinance
       21-68 that would amend the Budget of West Valley City for the Fiscal Year Beginning
       July 1, 2021 and Ending June 30, 2022 to Reflect Changes in the Budget from Increased
       Revenues and Authorize the Disbursement of Funds.

       Upon inquiry by Mayor Lang there were no further questions from members of the
       City Council, and she called for a motion.

              Councilmember Nordfelt moved to approve Ordinance 21-68.

CONSENT AGENDA
A.  RESOLUTION 21-215: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO ENTER INTO A
    RIGHT OF WAY PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND ACCEPT A
    WARRANTY DEED AND A TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION
    EASEMENT WITH AND FROM XIUMENG WU AND JIANAN XIAO FOR
    PROPERTY LOCATED AT 2554 SOUTH LAKE CREST DRIVE
    Mayor Lang discussed proposed Resolution 21-215 that would authorize the City
    to enter into a Right Of Way Purchase Agreement and Accept a Warranty Deed and a
    Temporary Construction Easement with and from Xiumeng Wu and Jianan Xiao for
    Property Located at 2554 South Lake Crest Drive

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:

             The Xiumeng Wu and Jianan Xiao property is one of several properties
             affected by the required removal and replacement of pedestrian ramps to
             meet current ADA standards as part of the 2022 Pedestrian Ramp Project.
             As part of this project pedestrian ramps will be constructed at various
             locations throughout the city in locations where they do not currently exist
             and existing pedestrian ramps which do not comply with current ADA
             standards will be removed and replaced.

             The Warranty Deed and the Temporary Construction Easement will
             facilitate construction of the pedestrian ramps. Compensation in the amount
             of $425.00 was negotiated based upon land values for properties in the area.

      Upon inquiry by Mayor Lang there were no further questions from members of the
      City Council, and she called for a motion.
