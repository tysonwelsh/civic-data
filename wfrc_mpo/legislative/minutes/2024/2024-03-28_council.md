2. Chair Report [00:06:22]
a. 2024 Utah Legislative Session Overview Commissioner Stevenson introduced Representative
Calvin Musselman, who shared his thoughts on the Session. [00:18:19] Senator Wayne Harper also
provided remarks about some legislative outcomes. [00:21:18] The Council also heard from Andrew
Gruber and Miranda Jones Cox, both with WFRC, who explained the legislation that is pertinent to the

3. Public Comment [00:44:27]
Commissioner Stevenson opened the meeting for public comment. There were no public comments.
4. Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com) [00:44:56]
a. Board Modifications to the 2024-2029 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Mayor Mark
Shepherd, Trans Com Chair, stated that since the last meeting of the Wasatch Front Regional Council
(WFRC), requests were received to modify the current 2024-2029 TIP. The modification required
action from Trans Com (since WFRC was not meeting) and the Utah Transportation Commission but
did not require a new air quality conformity analysis or a 30-day public comment period. The
modification is attached to the approved resolution.
b. ACTION: Board Modifications to the 2024-2029 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP)
[00:45:10] Mayor Shepherd then turned the time to Ben Wuthrich, WFRC. Mr. Wuthrich continued the
report by briefly describing the modification requests to the current 2024-2029 Transportation
Improvement Program (TIP). [00:55:10] ACTION: Mayor Shepherd made a motion to approve the
modifications as discussed. Commissioner Jim Harvey seconded the motion and the affirmative vote
was unanimous in the affirmative.
c. ACTION: Roadway Safety Resolution [00:57:45] Mayor Shepherd continued the Trans Com report
by asking Kip Billings, WFRC, to explain the collaborative effort of developing a Comprehensive
Safety Action Plan for all roadway users in the WFRC region. An analysis of safety needs was
completed last fall, which included crash history, roadway risk factors, and input from communities
throughout Weber, Davis, Salt Lake, Tooele, Morgan, and southern Box Elder counties. Strategies
and project types to address these safety needs have recently been shared and discussed with
communities at a series of 12 geographic focus area meetings. The needs analysis and
recommended strategies, along with other elements of this planning effort are being documented in
the draft Comprehensive Safety Action Plan. As part of the Action Plan, a resolution has been
prepared to establish a goal to work towards zero roadway fatalities and serious injuries. This
resolution will affirm WFRC’s commitment to improving roadway safety. Trans Com reviewed and
unanimously recommended the adoption of this safety resolution at their February 15 meeting.
[01:13:48] ACTION: Mayor Shepherd made a motion to adopt the resolution to work towards zero
roadway fatalities and serious injuries, and delegate to Trans Com the authority to adopt the
Comprehensive Safety Action Plan. Commissioner Lee Perry seconded the motion and the
affirmative vote was unanimous in the affirmative.
d. Report on Obligations of federal transportation funds during FY23 [01:14:15] Mayor Shepherd
turned the time back to Ben Wuthrich, WFRC. Mr. Wuthrich presented information on the annual
obligations of federal transportation funds during FY23.

6. Budget Committee [01:39:20]
Commissioner Stevenson then brought the group’s attention to Councilmember Aimee Winder Newton,
WFRC Budget Committee Chair, for the WFRC Budget Committee report.
a. ACTION: Release the Draft FY25 WFRC Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work Program
(UPWP) for public comment Councilmember Winder Newton gave a brief introduction and then
turned the time over to Andrew Gruber, WFRC, who provided an overview on the process for the
FY25 Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP). Mr. Gruber provided a high-level
summary of WFRC’s FY25 Goals and the UPWP. [01:45:14] Marian Florence, WFRC, presented the
FY25 Draft WFRC Budget, discussing the two major changes to the budget: consulting, and staffing.
[01:50:50] ACTION: Councilmember Winder Newton made a motion to release the draft FY25 WFRC
Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP) for public comment. Mayor Mark
Shepherd seconded the motion. With no further discussion, releasing the three FY25 documents for
public comment was approved unanimously.

Economic Development Strategy tasks and has internally prioritized four key anchors for 2024:

Capital Development, and Leveraging a Diverse Business Environment.
