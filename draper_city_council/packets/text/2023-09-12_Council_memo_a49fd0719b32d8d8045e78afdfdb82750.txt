MEMO
To: City Council
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2023-09-12
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1586.



Comments:


This application is a request for approval of an Amended Development Agreement for
approximately 22.39 acres located on the north side of 12650 South, at approximately 1085 East
12650 South. The property is currently zoned R5. The applicant is requesting that an Amended
Development Agreement be approved to eliminate the connection to 12600 South, adjust setbacks
for Accessory Dwelling Units, and create detention basin design standards.



The Planning Commission reviewed this item at their August 24, 2023 meeting and forwarded a
positive recommendation on a vote of 3-1 with the changes to the agreement provided to staff on
the day of the meeting. The updated agreement is included in the Ordinance 1586. An edited
version of the agreement has also been included outlining the changes that have been made since
the original Planning Commission staff report was posted.

One item that is still needs to be resolved is the width of the turnaround cul-de-sac on 12600 South.
The applicant is proposing a 75-foot turnaround with 2.5-feet of curb. City staff is recommending
an 80-foot wide cul-de-sac. Cross sections for both have been attached to this memo. The City
Council will need to decide which turnaround design they want to approve and modify the language
of the agreement to reference the standards and add that cross section as an exhibit.

The findings for approval are as follows:




  1. The proposed amended development agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and
      (D).
  2. The proposed conditions and changes to the agreement will protect the health, safety, and
      welfare of properties along 12600 South by providing a turnaround design which meets the
      design criteria of the Fire Department, City Engineer, and the Public Works Director per DCMC
      Section 17-5-030(F).



The findings for denial are as follows:
  1. The proposed amended development agreement does not address the potential health,
     safety, and welfare impacts to properties along 12600 South that could result if a cul-de-sac
     turnaround is not provided. Such turnaround is needed per DCMC Section 17-5-030(F).
