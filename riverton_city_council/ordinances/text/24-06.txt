18. 215. 030( 2)

       12) Commercial Exterior Lighting

      1.   Unless specifically exempted by this section, all outdoor commercial lighting shall include fully
           shielded fixtures and shall be installed so light is directed downward with no direct light emitted

           above a horizontal plane extending out from the bottom of any light fixture. Lighting must not be
           placed at a location, angle, or height that makes the lighting source visible outside the property
           boundaries where the light fixtures are located.


      2.   In order to qualify as a " fully shielded" fixture, a light fixture must meet all of the criteria below:
               a.
                   The top and sides must be made of completely opaque material, such that light only
                       escapes through the bottom of the fixture. Fixtures with translucent    or transparent   sides, or

                       sides with perforations or slits, do not qualify as fully shielded. The light source must be
                       entirely contained inside the opaque sides of the fixture housing.
                  b.   If mounted on or attached to a building or structure other than a parking lot light pole or a
                       sign, the bottom of the light source ( including all lenses, diffusers, reflectors, or similar
                       elements) must be at least two inches higher than the bottom of the fixture housing.
                  c.   Surrounding structures such as eave overhangs, canopies, or patio covers are not to be
                       considered in determining if a fixture is a fully shielded fixture. The fixture must be a fully
                       shielded fixture in and of itself.
                  d.   If mounted on or attached to a building or structure other than a parking lot light pole, the
                       light source of a fixture must not be visible from any point not on the property where the
                       fixture is located.
                  e.   Exceptions as noted in Section 4 below.

     Outdoor Commercial Building Lighting Adjacent to Residential Properties
        a.
             Except as noted in Section 4 below, exterior lighting the building side facing or oriented
             towards residential property on commercial structures within 100 feet of residential
             property shall be turned off at 10: 00 pm or the close of business, whichever is earlier.

4.   Exceptions to Sections 2 and 3:
        a.   A fixture that has a total light output less than 1, 000 lumens provided the fixture has sides
             that completely cover the light source and are made of opaque or semi opaque material.
             Completely transparent materials, such as clear glass, are not allowed. The light source
             must not be visible from any point outside the property on which the fixture is located.
        b.   Lighting for security purposes, provided:
                  i. The lighting is placed and directed such that no direct illumination falls outside the

                     property boundaries where the security lighting is placed, or
                 ii. The lighting is activated by motion sensors and does not stay on for more than 10
                       ten) minutes at a time, or

                iii.   As approved during Commercial Site Plan approval.
        c.
             Public Event Spaces lighting: Riverton City sponsored public events may use outdoor
             illumination as necessary to light the event space( s) and for the safety of the public.
             Lighting as necessary for public events may be also utilized for before and after the event
             for staging, preparation, rehearsals, clean- up, etc. Reasonable efforts will be made to
             mitigate impacts to surrounding areas.
        d.   Parking Lot Pole Lighting: Lighting specific to illuminating a parking area and mounted
             on a pole shall meet the requirements for shielding as described in Sections 2. a, b, and c
             above, and shall make reasonable efforts to minimize visibility of the light source from
             adjacent properties.

5.   Specialized Outdoor Lighting
        a.
              Gas station canopies: Gas station canopies may be illuminated provided all light fixtures
              are mounted on the undersurface of the canopy and all light fixtures are fully shielded.
              Except for directed beam lighting, merely placing the fixtures on the underside of the
              canopy does not qualify as fully shielding the light fixture. Directed beam lighting
              mounted under the canopy is allowed, provided the light source cannot be seen from
              outside the property boundaries.
        b.    Lighting of Flags: U. S. Code states that: " It is the universal custom to display the flag only
             from sunrise to sunset on buildings andflagstaffs in the open. However, when a patriotic
              effect is desired, the flag may be displayed twenty-four hours a day ifproperly illuminated
              during the hours ofdarkness." The lighting of Federal or State flags shall be permitted
              provided that the light is a narrow beam spotlight rather than a floodlight, with the beam
              directed away from any public right-of-way and as feasible from adjacent residential
             properties.
        c.   Commercial Holiday Lighting:
