 UNIFIED FIRE SERVICE AREA


 Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA) is a taxing entity that
 uses collected taxes to go directly towards fire service
 costs. UFSA is a proud member of Unified Fire Authority
 (UFA). UFA is a governmental entity that provides
 emergency services on behalf of its member entities.
