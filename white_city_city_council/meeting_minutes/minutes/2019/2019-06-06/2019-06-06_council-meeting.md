                                           Staff Meeting

Discussion/Clarification of Agenda Items

              The Council reviewed the following agenda and future agenda items:

    Update on Unified Fire Authority (UFA)/Unified Fire Service Area and Sandy contract
    Contracted city letters sent to Unified Police Department (UPD) and possible impacts to
     services being provided to White City
    General Plan and Planning Department update
    Sandy Canal Trail update
    Creation of Title 12 – Code Enforcement and Community Enhancement Program
    Proposed stormwater maintenance agreement with Salt Lake County

               Council Member Dickerson, seconded by Council Member Cutler, moved to close
the staff meeting. The motion passed unanimously.

White City Community Council Report

             Council Member Dickerson stated the community council is trying to recruit more
members and is considering meeting quarterly instead of monthly.

             Council Member Price stated the community council will need to meet every
month from May to August then quarterly after that.

             Paul Ashton asked if the White City Metro Township Council will have a booth at
Southeast Township Days.

              Mayor Flint stated it will have an informational booth; however, there will not be
anyone there to oversee the booth.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

               Captain Marc West stated fireworks should not be much of threat this year. He
likes the idea of having Sandy City Fire report to UFA, so he can report back to the White City
Metro Township Council.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)

                 Officer Paula Stinson stated last months calls for service look great. The speed
trailer is going to be out in the community and the house on Violet has been issued a permit to
start decontaminating it.

             Chief Randy Thomas stated the speed sign has been installed and shows a
message that says you are going too fast; it has slowed traffic down.

White City Metro Township Council Member Reports

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

               Council Member Cutler stated an agreement was reached with Riverton City to
transfer vehicles and fire arms to its police department. Copperton Improvement District has a
lease agreement to house a substation for UPD. The UPD budget will be adopted on June 20,
2019 and is more transparent than it has been in previous years.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

               Council Member Perry stated the budget was approved with a substantial
increase in pay which will put employees on a more competitive playing field. UFA is working on
interlocal agreements with participating entities. Mayor Jennifer Wilson will be joining the board
instead of her Deputy Mayor. UFSA is working on interlocal agreements and policies to clarify
what the process would be for withdrawing or joining UFSA.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                Council Member Price stated the district was given the Accountability Award.
Seasonal employees have been hired and trained. The number of mosquitos this year is
anticipated to be high because of all the water the state has seen.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

               Mayor Flint stated the GSLMSD is heavily involved in the transition of Salt Lake
County Planning and Development Services employees. Employees will receive a Reduction in
Force letter on August 31, 2019. Not all Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services
employees will be qualified for positions being offered by the GSLMSD.

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

              Council Member Dickerson stated the District is looking into what it can do to
decrease employee turnover. Several homes have left the district and are using Sandy City
services. Recycling and processing costs are being reviewed to see how they can be lowered.

Staff or Committee Reports

Legislative Update

               Paul Ashton stated the Council will want to start thinking about a cleanup bill and
what bills the metro townships want to carry for the 2020 legislative session. A few ideas have
come up, one is to change the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) board
member for the unincorporated area. Currently a member of the Salt Lake County Council
represents the unincorporated areas and it can be confusing to determine if they are representing
the entire county or just the unincorporated areas.
