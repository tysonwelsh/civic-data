WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
JULY 16, 2019

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Cox at 2:00 p.m. on July 16, 2019, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil
Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, Attorney’s Office Intern James Haws,
Community Development Director Scott Messel, Tourism Director Kevin Lewis, Public
Works Director Ron Whitehead, Sheriff Cory Pulsipher, Undersheriff James Standley,
Noxious Weed Director Dale Gubler, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION __AND__ PERIODIC _ REVIEW__OF_ _COUNTY _RESOURCE
MANAGEMENT PLAN (RMP)

The sections that were reviewed included: Land Use, Land Access, Recreation and
Tourism, Law Enforcement, Noxious Weeds, and Economics.

Minor wording suggestions were made from those present to enhance the sections
discussed including changing the word ‘residents’ to ‘all individuals within Washington

County’.

Updates will be brought before the County Commission for final approval.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
3:15 p.m.
