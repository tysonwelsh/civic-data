MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 6, 2024

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on August 6, 2024, in the Washington County Commission

Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.

commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that this Ordinance will consolidate the two
offices. Under State law, the County Surveyor is an office whose duties overlap with the
Recorder’s office. The County Recorder will carry out the duties of the County Surveyor.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2024-1267-O, An
Ordinance Consolidating the Office of the County Surveyor with the Office of
the County Recorder. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting
aye.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2024-1268-0, AN ORDINANCE CREATING
THE WASHINGTON COUNTY FAIR ADVISORY BOARD — TITLE 2 CHAPTER 11

Mr. Snow stated that this Ordinance will formalize a fair board. The board members would be
advisory to the Fair Director, Susi Lafaele. Mr. Snow suggested a change to remove the number
of board members from seven to a decision made later, and a shift in how board members are

appointed.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2024-1268-O, An
Ordinance Creating the Washington County Fair Advisory Board — Title 2
Chapter 11 with the Changes as Outlined by Devin Snow. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION __OF__ RESOLUTION _NO.__R-2024-3335,_ A | RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING FUNDS COLLECTED UNDER THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND

PARKS SALES TAX FOR THE GUNLOCK RODEO PAVILION

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that in 2014 Washington County approved a
county option sales tax to fund Recreation, Arts, and Parks. Earlier this year, Gunlock Rodeo was
approved for RAP funding. The citizens of Gunlock have recently reached out for further funding
to finish the Gunlock Rodeo Pavilion, and it is suggested that such funds be appropriated to finish

the project.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3335, A
Resolution Appropriating Funds Collected Under the Recreation, Arts and
Parks Sales Tax for the Gunlock Rodeo Pavilion. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION __OF_ _ RESOLUTION __NO.___R-2024-3336, A | RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING RAP TAX FUNDS — DAUGHTERS OF UTAH PIONEERS

Mr. Snow stated that the Daughters of Utah Pioneers met the deadlines to be considered for the
2024 RAP. Still, The Daughters of the Utah Pioneers were omitted from consideration due to a
clerical error. This Resolution would allocate $4,000 to The Daughters of the Utah Pioneers.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3336, A
Resolution Appropriating RAP Tax Funds — Daughters of Utah Pioneers.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.
