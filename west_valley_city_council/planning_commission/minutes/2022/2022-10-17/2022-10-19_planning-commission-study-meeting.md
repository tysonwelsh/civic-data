GENERAL PLAN CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GP-2-2022 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY to amend the City’s General Plan by updating the
          Major Street Plan to: 1) extend Lester Street (5700 West) as a 66’ right-of-way north of
          2400 South, 2) add 7200 West as an 80’ right-of-way between 2100 South and SR-201,
          and 3) extend 5900 West as a 66’ right-of-way between Beagley Road (2540 South) and
          Parkway Blvd. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)
          Continued from the October 12, 2022 public hearing

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik stated that this agenda item was continued from the previous public
               hearing. He explained that David Drake, an attorney representing a current business
               located on Lester Street, had addressed the Commission during that meeting. Steve
               pointed out that Mr. Drake’s major concern was regarding the widening of Lester Street
               and how it would affect the business. Steve explained that the Major Street Plan calls for
               a sixty-six foot right-of-way and the City already owns sixty-five feet of the right-of-way
               for Lester Street. He stated that the most the City could widen the street would be six
               inches on either side. Steve said that he believes the attorneys concern was the need to
               add improvements in the future. He explained that adding improvements such as, curb,
              gutter, and sidewalk, could be done within the sixty-six feet of right-of-way. Steve noted
              that the City is proposing to lengthen Lester Street and that would not impact any
              businesses.

              Chair Fuller said that the question would then be, has the property owner built his
              business on City property? Steve said he is not sure if that is the case. He stated that the
              attorney has his contact information, and he has not reached out. Steve stated that perhaps
              this proposal would not be as impactful as Mr. Drake originally thought.

              Commissioner Wood said she had heard the property owner state they would need to pay
              for half of the future improvements. She asked Steve if that is true. Steve explained that
              there is a provision in our code regarding someone who owns a property located along a
              street that does not have improvements. He explained that if that homeowner were to do
              construction on their site costing fifty thousand or more, then they would be required to
              put in improvements.

              Commissioner Wood asked if the City adds the improvements would the property owner
              need to pay a portion of that? Steve said the only situation where a property owner would
              need to pay for improvements would be if there is a delay agreement in place.

              Chair Fuller asked if the City or the property owner would be responsible for the park
              strip. Steve said if the City is doing a project, we would usually put in a concrete park
              strip. However, if the property owner puts in the park strip, it could contain gravel,
              stamped concrete or other forms of landscaping and they would be responsible for
              maintaining it.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SMI-9-2022 Petition by BRENT MALILI requesting preliminary approval for the Carpe Diem
           Auctum Phase 2 Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 2acres. The
           subdivision is located at approximately 5520 South 5600 West and is zoned C-2 (General
           Commercial). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Lehman explained that this proposal is regarding the second phase of the Carpe
              Diem subdivision. He pointed out the gap separating this parcel from the one to the north
              and stated it is owned by UTA. Steve said the developers have been working with UTA
              and have submitted an access plan to the City. He noted that upon review of the access
              plan, UDOT agreed to one access for both lots. Steve explained that a multi-tenant
              building is proposed for lot one and a restaurant for lot two.

              Commissioner Winters said he thought there was a previous application for the subject
              property. Steve said there hasn’t been any recent proposals.


SA-4-2022     Petition by PAUL SNYDER is requesting a plat amendment for lots 5 and 6 of the JMYL
              Subdivision. The proposed consolidation of these lots will create a larger lot for a future
              restaurant. The property is located at approximately 1814 West 4100 South and is zoned
              C-2. (General Commercial). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
              steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Lehman explained that this application is to consolidate two lots for a new
               restaurant drive-thru. He said that this type of application previously would not come
               before the Commission. However, due to changes in state law the consolidation is part of
               a plat amendment and before the Commission today.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-21-2022 Petition by GOLDEN SPIKE RESTAURANTS, LLC requesting a conditional use for a
          Taco Bell fast food restaurant with a drive-thru. The property is located at 1814-1838
          West 4100 South on 1.01 acres and is zoned C2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody
          Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Jody Knapp explained that this conditional use is for a Taco Bell that would sit on
               the subject property of the previous application, SA-4-2022. She said the
               proposed drive-thru would wrap around the front of the building. Jody stated that
               they would need to meet the commercial design standards, and there is a standing
               development agreement stating that their landscaping design needs to match the
               adjacent CVS. She has sent some redline comments to the applicant and is waiting
               for their reply. Jody pointed out that the applicant would need to add some relief
               treatment to the building. She mentioned that a sidewalk was added on the side
               where the medical center sits. Jody explained that she needs to talk to the
               applicant about what they are proposing for a pedestrian connection and that it is
               the same as the one running along CVS. She pointed out how the trees have
               matured and are starting to buckle the sidewalk. Jody explained that this issue
               would be addressed as improvements were added. Jody explained that the north-
               south sidewalk connections have already been established, and we must ensure
               they match the applicant's plans.


C-22-2022      Petition by J&J TIRES requesting a conditional use amendment for auto and tire sales.
               The property is located at 2875 S. Redwood Road on 0.55 acres and is zoned C2 (General
               Commercial). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Jody Knapp explained that in 2012 there were issues with the property being used as a
               salvage yard with a large amount of outside storage. She said the property's condition led
               to the conditional use being revoked. Jody stated that when J&J Tires took over, their
               original conditions of approval stated they could do auto servicing; however, no sales
               would be permitted. She explained that J&J Tires has been running their business from
               this location for approximately ten years, making significant improvements to the site and
               running a good company. Jody stated that the applicant would like to amend the
               conditional use to allow tire and auto sales. She reviewed where they propose to display
               their vehicles and tires for sale. Jody noted that she needs to discuss if they intend to keep
               the fenced portion of the property open during the review. She pointed out an
               unmaintained area containing only dirt where the landscaping should be. Jody stated that
               the applicant is in the process of landscaping the area. Jody noted that the applicant
               would need to add one tree every sixty feet to comply with landscaping requirements.
               She presented an image of the existing masonry wall and explained how it would need to
               be modified to meet the code. Jody pointed out that the current structure needs
               maintenance. She noted that they have started constructing the dumpster enclosure;
               however, it needs to be finished. Jody explained that she has excellent communication
              with the applicant and doesn't foresee any problems working with them through this
              process.



There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:57 p.m.
