BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

The applicant is requesting approval of a Sidewalk Deviation to allow for a reduction in the amount of
sidewalk installed from approximately 3,905 lineal feet along each side of Suncrest Drive to
approximately 490 lineal feet on the east side and 100 lineal feet on the west side of the road. The
property is approximately 235.33 acres in size and is bisected by Suncrest Drive. The approximate
address is 16377 South Suncrest Drive (Exhibit C). The property is currently zoned A5 (Agricultural, 5
acre min. lot size).

The applicant currently has applied for a building permit for a single family dwelling on the subject
property. That permit is under review. An initial site plan was reviewed and accepted by staff in April of
this year as part of the building permit. Due to changes to the plans (Exhibit G) and the request for the
deviation from sidewalk requirements (Exhibits F and H), review and approval from the Planning
Commission is now required. The proposed home is located on the eastern portion of the property and is
accessed by a drive of approximately 3,450 feet in length that connects to Suncrest Drive.

designation for the subject property (Exhibit D). This category is characterized as follows:

The property has been assigned the A5 zoning classification, supporting approximately one dwelling unit
per 5 acres (Exhibit E). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose
of the A5 zone is to “is to maintain the status of large tracts of agricultural land by allowing most
commercial agricultural uses. Typical uses include farming, dairy, and cattle production”. Agricultural
zones are further described as being established “to preserve and protect agricultural lands and related
activities, to permit activities normally and necessarily related to agricultural production, and to prohibit
land uses that may undermine continued agricultural activity”. The A5 zoning abuts the subject property
on the west and east. Also located to the east is the Utah County CE-1 (Critical Environment) zoning and
Utah County RA-5 (Residential Agriculture 5) zoning. Abutting the property to the North is the RR-22
(Rural Residential) and RR-43 (Rural Residential) zones. Highland City PD-1 (Planned Development)
zoning and Utah County RA-5 (Residential Agriculture 5) zoning abuts the subject property on the South.

Site Plan Layout.
The overall site plan provided as part of the building permit application for the construction of the home
(Exhibit G) shows the home located on the far eastern portion of the property, approximately 605-feet
east of a bend in Suncrest Drive. The footprint of the home and related accessory structures is
approximately 33,400 square feet and the access drive to the home is approximately 3,450 feet in length.
Due in part to the large size of the property, the location of the home significantly exceeds required
minimum setbacks from property lines. Suncrest Drive bisects the property and there are approximately
3,905 feet of frontage along each side of the street. The applicant is not subdividing the parcel and meets
the minimum five acre requirement to construct a single family dwelling. The site plan provided for the
Sidewalk Deviation request shows a smaller portion of the overall property where sidewalk is proposed to
be installed (Exhibit F). A majority of the sidewalk proposed would be on the east side of the street along
the southernmost 490-feet of Suncrest Drive between the Draper City limits and the proposed access drive
entrance. Also included in the proposal is the installation of 100-lineal feet of sidewalk on the west side of
the street opposite the proposed access drive.

Application for building permit DCMC Section 10-3-020
       C. Construction On Existing Parcels: Building permit applications for residential and non-

            reviewed by the Community Development Department to determine the requirements for
            building permit issuance. The Community Development Department shall route applicable
            plans to the Engineering Division to determine frontage improvement and right- of-way
            dedication provisions. The Engineering Division shall determine the presence of adequate
            frontage improvements and right-of-way dedication in accordance with sections 9-27-
            100 and 9-27-110 of this Code. The Engineering Division shall inform the Community
            Development Department of the requirement and method by which frontage improvements
            shall be provided by the applicant. The Community Development Department shall inform the
            applicant of the Engineering Division's determination.

There are some minor conflicts between the provisions of DCMC 9-27-100(B) and DCMC 10-3-020(C)
as they relate to the determination of and construction of frontage improvements on existing parcels.
While the Engineering Division determines if existing improvements are adequate to meet ordinance or if
additional improvements are required, a deviation from the installation of full improvements requires a
Planning Commission action. The property is located in the A5 zone and the minimum lot width for the
zone is 100-feet, per DCMC Table 9-9-2. The applicant’s proposal to install a cumulative total of 590-
lineal feet of sidewalk is greater than the permissible minimum. The Engineering Division supports the
proposed installation of the approximately 490-feet of sidewalk on the east side of Suncrest Drive. The
Engineering division further supports delayed installation of improvements along the remainder of the
east side of the road until such time that additional areas of the property may be developed. The
Engineering Division does not support installation of the proposed 100-feet of sidewalk on the west side
of Suncrest Drive, as the proposed sidewalk in that location would be non-contiguous. The Engineering
Division supports delayed installation of improvements along the west side of the road until such time
that additional area of the property may be developed.

Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review and potential approval of a Sidewalk Deviation request is found in Section 9-27-
100 of the Draper City Municipal Code. This section depicts the standard of review for such requests as:

Frontage Improvements DCMC Section 9-27-100
       B. Extent Of Improvements: When the size of a lot or parcel exceeds minimum zone
           requirements, the planning commission may determine the extent of the required
           improvements if the frontage adjoining a public street is, in its judgment, excessive based on
           cost calculations reviewed by the city engineer. However, frontage improvements shall be
           provided for no less than the minimum lot width required by the zone in which the lot or
           parcel is located.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request, receive public comment, and make a
decision based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, or denial, as listed within the
staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include the following
conditions of approval:

          1.        That approval of the Sidewalk Deviation does not alleviate the applicant from compliance
                    with all other ordinances and standard practices applicable to the review and approval of
                    the building permit or future land use applications.
          2.        That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building, Planning,
                    and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the construction of all buildings on the site,
                    including permitting.
          3.        That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the construction
                    of all buildings on the site.
          4.        That a deferral for sidewalk installation be given until such time additional development
                    beyond the proposed single family dwelling may occur on the property.
          5.        That approximately 490 linear feet of sidewalk be installed on the east side of Suncrest
                    Drive in the location proposed on the site plan.
          6.        That no sidewalk be installed on the west side of Suncrest Drive until such time that
                    additional development of the property on the west side of Suncrest Drive may occur.

The findings for approval as are follows:

          1.        That the size of the parcel exceeds the minimum requirements of the A5 Zone.
          2.        That the required improvements are excessive based on a review by the city engineer.
          3.        Proposed frontage improvements will be provided for a length that meets or exceeds the
                    minimum lot width required by the zone.
          4.        That approval for the deviation from sidewalk standards along Suncrest Drive in the
                    proposed locations is appropriate to the situation.

The findings for denial as are follows:

          1.        That the required improvements are not excessive.
          2.        That approval for the deviation from sidewalk standards along Suncrest Drive is not
                    appropriate to the situation.
