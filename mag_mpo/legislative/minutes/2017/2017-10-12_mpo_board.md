University of Utah Gardner Policy Institute Population & Job Forecast to 2065 – Shawn Eliot, MAG Staff The Governor’s Office of Planning and Budget (GOMB) prepared population, housing, and employment projections for the state and each county in the state up until 2012. The state has moved this task to the newly formed Gardner Policy Institute (GPI) at the University of Utah. Mountainland staff have worked collaboratively with GPI to develop the final 2017 projection series. GPI projections are out to 2065. The next set of estimates should occur in 2023 after the next decennial census in 2020. The data projections are used for planning purposes only and are not used to distribute any federal, state, local, or sales tax monies. This data will be used by the MPO for transportation modeling and air quality conformity. Federal, state, and local governments as well as the private sector use this data for their planning activities. This fall, the MPO will also work with the municipalities to develop new projections for each area using this data as a county control total. The data is available at www.mountainland.org MAG TIP Congestion Management Program/Project Selection Policy and Process Approval – Shawn Eliot, MAG Staff The Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) lists all funded regional transportation projects (MPO, UDOT, UTA, locals). The MPO selects projects for State Exchange Funds, MPO Federal Funds, and County Transportation Sales Tax Funds. The Congestion Management/TIP Selection Mountainland MPO Regional Planning Committee October 12, 2017 Page 3 Process document outlines the policies and procedures in selecting MPO programmed projects. The selection process has the following milestones: • Kickoff - Explain process and calendar to committees • Project Idea Report - Proposed projects reviewed by TAC • Concept Report - Detailed report of technical need, benefits, costs, and air quality • MPO Staff Technical Report - Scoring of all Concept Report projects • MPO TAC Project Priority List - Score projects, place in ranking list • MPO RPC Priority List Approval - Approve final list • TIP Approval - Match funding with projects, finalize TIP document In November 2017, the process selection will be reviewed by the MPO Regional Planning Committee to prepare for the new funding cycle that will be made available in 2021-2022. Funding is used for highway projects, transit, intelligent transportation systems, active transportation and air quality reduction projects. Every two years funding is allocated for selected projects. During the selection process in 2014, a lot of changes were made, but not codified. The update presented adds those changes with some clarifications. Document changes include: • Corrections, clarifications, and updates • Expanded timeline to allow longer RPC review of Project Priority List • Joint Project Management Process added • Policy updates It was asked how often the TIP is reviewed throughout the year. Shawn Eliot stated once every two years, however there is an amendment process in the policy that would allow new projects to be reviewed and weighed by TAC, then approved by MPO Regional Planning Committee if there is funding available. Mayor Hadfield moved that the MPO Regional Planning Committee approve that the Congestion Management Program/Project Selection Process be changed as shown in the markup document. Mayor Thompson seconded the motion. Commissioner Graves opposed. The motion carried unanimously.
