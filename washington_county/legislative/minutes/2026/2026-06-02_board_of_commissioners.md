CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3769, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH UTAH TECH UNIVERSITY FOR
THE RED CLIFFS CONSERVATION EDUCATION PARTNERSHIP

Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan stated this item is a recommendation
from the Habitat Conservation Advisory Committee (HCAC). This is for collaboration regarding
conservation, public outreach, exhibit development and community science initiatives not to
exceed $10,000.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3769, A
Resolution Approving a Memorandum of Understanding with Utah Tech
University for the Red Cliffs Conservation Education Partnership. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3770, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A MINOR BOUNDARY ADJUSTMENT TO THE RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

Mr. Rognan explained this item was also recommended by the HCAC. There is a road in
development next to Zone 6 with utilities needing to move outside of the reserve. 3.5 acres will be
moved out of the reserve, with 3.5 acres being moved in to the reserve. Fish and Wildlife Service
would also have to approve the boundary adjustment.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3770, A
Resolution Approving a Minor Boundary Adjustment to the Red Cliffs Desert
Reserve. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3771, A RESOLUTION OF THE
COUNTY COMMISSION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY, UTAH. CLARIFYING THAT
NO SEPARATE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT IS REQUIRED IN CONNECTION
WITH THE CREATION OF THE SOLARA PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE DISTRICTS

Washington County Commission
Meeting Minutes of June 2, 2026

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales stated the Solara Public Infrastructure (PID) was approved
in March with an amended governing document approved in April. There was some confusion in
the PID policy if there are separate development agreements are necessary for the PID, and this
Resolution would clarify the record. Ms. Hales specified this is only for the Solara PID and would
not affect any future PID’s.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3771, A
Resolution of the County Commission of Washington County, Utah, clarifying
that no Separate Development Agreement is Required in Connection with the
Creation of the Solara Public Infrastructure Districts. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3772, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AMENDMENT _NO. 1 TO THE PROFESSIONAL SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH
JONES & DEMILLE ENGINEERING, INC. FOR THE FORSYTH FIRE PROJECT

Public Works Director Todd Edwards explained his amendment would allow Jones & DeMille to
provide construction management once this project begins that cannot be provided in house.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3772,
A Resolution Approving Amendment No. 1 to the Professional Service
Agreement with Jones & DeMille Engineering, Inc. for the Forsyth Fire
Project. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3773, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO THE PROFESSIONAL SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH
JONES & DEMILLE ENGINEERING, INC. FOR THE FORSYTH FIRE PROJECT

Mr. Edwards stated this Resolution is for additional funding to expand the scope of work on the
Forsyth Fire Project that will be reimbursed from NRCS. Mr. Snow clarified that the previous
Resolution addressed the NRCS agreement with this Resolution being for the project management
services.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3773, A
Resolution Approving Amendment No. 2 to the Professional Service
Agreement with Jones & DeMille Engineering, Inc. for the Forsyth Fire
Project. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
