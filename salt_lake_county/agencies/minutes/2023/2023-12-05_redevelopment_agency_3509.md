RECITALS
WHEREAS, Utah Code § 17C-1-601.5 requires the governing board of the 
Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County (the “Agency”) to adopt an 
annual budget of revenue and expenditures for the Agency before December 
15th of each year; and 
WHEREAS, the Agency staff, with the assistance of the County Mayor ’s 
Offices, has caused to be prepared proposed and final budgets for the 
Agency for fiscal year 2023, which have been reviewed and discussed in 
public meetings, and which have met all the requirements imposed by Utah 
law; and
WHEREAS, the Agency held a public hearing on its proposed annual budget 
for fiscal year 2023 on December 6, 2022, at 6:00 p.m., and provided notice 
of the public hearing as required by law; and
WHEREAS, the adoption of this resolution has been duly noticed in 
accordance with the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act, and Utah Code § 
17C-1-601.5(4).
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, the Governing Board of the Redevelopment Agency 
of Salt Lake County, Utah, resolves as follows: 
A. The Agency hereby adopts an annual budget for fiscal year 2023, as more 
specifically described in Attachment “A” to this Resolution.
B. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its approval and

RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County (the “RDA”) 
and Salt Lake County (the “County”) are “public agencies” as defined by the 
Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, UTAH CODE §§ 11-13-101 to -608, and as 
such, are authorized to enter into agreements to act jointly and cooperatively 
in a manner that will enable them to make the most efficient use of their 
resources and powers;
WHEREAS, the RDA and the County desire to enter into an agreement 
whereby the County will provide administrative services to the RDA to 
enable to RDA to coordinate redevelopment activities within its jurisdiction;
AND WHEREAS, the agreement will provide for the RDA to share its 
revenue with the benefit of the County.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Board of 
Directors of the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County: 
1. That the Interlocal Agreement between the Redevelopment Agency of Salt 
Lake County and Salt Lake County is approved, in substantially the form 
attached hereto as ATTACHMENT “A”, and that the Chairperson of the 
Board is authorized to execute the same.
2. That the Interlocal Agreement will become effective as stated therein. 
APPROVED AND ADOPTED this 5th day of December, 2023.
