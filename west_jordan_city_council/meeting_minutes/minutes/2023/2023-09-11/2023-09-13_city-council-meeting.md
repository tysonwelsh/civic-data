 4. PUBLIC COMMENT
Chair McConnehey opened the public comment period at 6:07 pm
Citizen Comments:
Ryan Wilkinson commented that some municipal airports used moratoriums, curfews, and other
restrictions to protect the public. He asked if the City had liability for allowing known dangerous
conditions to persist in the community. Mr. Wilkinson said he would appreciate a response.
Chair McConnehey closed public comments at 6:12 pm


 5. REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. City Council Reports
          Council Member Green said it was a great honor to attend the recent Utah National Guard
          Governor’s Day. He reported on a recent Association of Municipal Councils meeting, and
          spoke of West Jordan’s participation in a cooperative agreement for wildfire mitigation.
          He also expressed appreciation for the opportunity to meet with the CEO of Holy Cross
          Jordan Valley Hospital at a recent event.

           Council Member Whitelock was grateful to have been able to attend the recent Utah
           League of Cities and Towns (ULCT) annual convention. She emphasized the importance
           of sharing with state legislator’s that West Jordan’s entitled housing units are 8,933 and
           mentioned a significant number of bills being prepared for the next upcoming legislative
           session.

           Council Member Jacob provided an update on the Jordan Valley Water Conservation
           District and commented that conservation habits developed in the last few years had
           made a difference.

           Chair McConnehey commented that with changes in requirements for new construction
           landscaping approved by the current Council, the almost 9,000 units entitled in West
           Jordan would be built with water-wise landscaping. He commented that West Jordan had
           a fantastic Fire Department.

       b. Council Office Report
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson introduced new Policy Analyst Warren Hallmark.
          Mr. Hallmark spoke of his previous professional experience and was excited to work with
          West Jordan City.

           Mr. Anderson provided an update on preparations for the West Jordan Youth Council. He
           demonstrated language features included with PrimeGov agenda software, and said staff
           were working on a demonstration video to add to YouTube. Mr. Anderson updated the
           Council on the City Hall renovation project and reported on a recent water tank tour.

       c. Mayor’s Report
          Mayor Dirk Burton reported on the recent Demolition Derby, a recent Salt Lake City
          International Airport event, Brazilian SWAT Team training with the West Jordan Police
          Department, and local high school homecoming events. He spoke of recent and upcoming
          local community events, and reported on a meeting with Holy Cross Jordan Valley
          Hospital with CEO Christine Sweeney.

       d. City Administrator’s Report
          Public Works Director Brian Clegg gave an update on 7800 South sewer line and widening
          projects, and reported on green waste collection. Utilities Manager Greg Davenport
          updated the Council regarding a water line replacement project along Leland Drive. Chair
          McConnehey suggested staff alert Google Fiber that road work would be occurring in case
          fiber could be laid down while the road was open.


 6. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. Ordinance No. 23-26 amending City Code Sections 13-5C-1 and 13-5C-5 regarding
          Planned Development (PRD/PC) Zones and Section 13-5b-3 regarding residential
          bulk standards
          Community Development Director Scott Langford said the proposed PRD/PC Zone text
          amendment would provide consistency to the Code. He explained the proposed text and
          reported of a positive recommendation made by the Planning Commission.

           Chair McConnehey opened a public hearing at 6:53 pm
           Comments:
           None
           Chair McConnehey closed the public hearing at 6:54 pm

           Council Member Green thanked staff for the hard work on the amendment, and said he
           believed it was a good start. He was interested in having further conversation about
           setbacks and lot width/depth. Chair McConnehey agreed that the amendment was a good
           start, and also expressed interest in further conversation about setbacks.

           MOTION: Council Member Green moved to APPROVE Ordinance No. 23-26

 7. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Follow-up Discussion on Sanitary Sewer Lift Station Code Amendments (Title 9-4-4)
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson explained that staff were tasked earlier in the year
          with making sure a proposed new section of Code prohibiting sewer lift stations
          conformed with adopted engineering standards. Mr. Anderson explained changes to the
          proposed section since the language was given to staff. Council Member Green said he
          reviewed the proposed changes and believed the language accomplished what was
          desired. All Council Members indicated a desire to move forward.
