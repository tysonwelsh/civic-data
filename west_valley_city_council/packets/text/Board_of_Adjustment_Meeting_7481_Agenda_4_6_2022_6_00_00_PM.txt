 NON CONFORMING USES
 B-1-2022   BRIAN PITCHER has filed a petition with the West Valley City Board of Adjustment
            seeking a non-conforming use determination for existing storage units and RV parking. The
            request is to review and grant non-conforming use approval for those existing uses which
            according to the applicant have been in existence since 1977. The property is currently zoned
            R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) and is located at
            4270 South 4060 West. (Staff - Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov


 VARIANCES
 B-2-2022                     MIKE BRODSKY is requesting a variance from Section 7-6-202 of the West Valley City
                              Code. This section requires that the minimum side yard setback adjacent to a single family
                              dwelling be 10 feet in the RE (Residential Estate) Zone. The applicant is requesting a variance
                              of 4.3 feet in order to enclose an existing carport. The dwelling in question will be part of a
                              new subdivision located at approximately 3716 South 5450 West. (Staff - Steve Lehman at
                              801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)
