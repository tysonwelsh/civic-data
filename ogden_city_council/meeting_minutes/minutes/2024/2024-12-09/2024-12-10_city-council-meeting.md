Community & Economic Development Citizen Advisory Committee
Appointments/Reappointments
          A letter from Mayor Nadolski requesting consideration of the reappointment of Rick Southwick, Mitchel Garsz, Amanda
Lessard, and Benjamin Hughes and the appointment of MJ Munger and Flor Lopez to the Community and Economic
Development Citizens Advisory Committee came before the Council for consideration.
          Vice Chair White stated that she has nothing against any person listed in the Mayor’s letter but noted that at least one of
them is already serving on another committee in the City and she wondered if there may be others desirous of being appointed to
the position. She is not objecting to voting on the appointments tonight but felt it appropriate to state her concern.
          COUNCIL MEMBER HYER MOVED TO APPROVE THE REAPPOINTMENT OF RICK SOUTHWISK,
MITCHEL GARSZ, AMANDA LESSARD, AND BENJAMIN HUGHES AND THE APPOINTMENT OF MJ MUNGER
AND FLOR LOPEZ TO THE COMMUNITY AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT CITIZENS ADVISORY
COMMITTEE. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER BLAIR, ALL VOTING AYE.

Closed Session
         At this time, the Council gave consideration of adjourning into a Closed Session pursuant to one or more of the
provisions of Section 52-4-205(1) of the Open and Public Meetings Law:
         a. Discussion of the character, professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual
         b. Strategy session to discuss collective bargaining
         c. Strategy session to discuss pending or reasonably imminent litigation
         d. Strategy session to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property
         e. Strategy session to discuss the sale of real property
         f. Discussion regarding deployment of security personnel, devices, or systems
         g. Investigative proceedings regarding allegations of criminal misconduct
         COUNCIL MEMBER HYER MOVED THE COUNCIL ADJOURN INTO A CLOSED SESSION PURSUANT
TO THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION 52-4-205(1) OF THE OPEN AND PUBLIC MEETINGS LAW. MOTION WAS
SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER BLAIR, WITH THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE –
COUNCIL MEMBERS BLAIR, CHOBERKA, GRAF, HYER, MYERS, VICE CHAIR WHITE, AND CHAIR RICHEY.
VOTING NO - NONE.

         The meeting adjourned into Closed Session at 8:13 p.m.
         The meeting reconvened at 8:50 p.m.

ADJOURN AT 8:51 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER BLAIR, ALL VOTING AYE.
