ITEM 1
PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT- SUN RIVER COMMONS SIGNS:
      Consider a request for a Planned Development (PD) amendment to the Sun
      River Commons Master Sign Plan. Proposed changes include adding two 30
      ft. tall pylon signs, increasing monument sign heights, and allowing all signs

              A motion was made by Planning Commission Member Fisher to recommend
              approval to City Council, item #4, the zone change as it’s presented with
              recommendations by staff with one restriction, as far as single family
              residences that are located on the North, that adjoin the neighborhood that is
              on the North side of this proposed development where the elevation is
              significant as it is, and concern and the risk, for safety purposes the ability to
              see down into people’s property and the height differences and what could be
              a safety risk as far as items thrown or discarded into their property, that the
              homes be limited to a single story at street level, which would allow for a
              basement beneath without any kind of height restriction for lots 11-21. And
              that the applicant be allowed, in the event that staff approves, that they are
              not required and can remove the additional parking on the road that encircles
              on the South side.

           The motion was seconded by Planning Commission Member Casey
