WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
Ordinance No. 2023-0-01 Date: June 1, 2023
AN ORDINANCE OF WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL TO
REPEAL AND REPLACE TITLES 18 & 19 OF MUNICIPAL CODE,
PROVISIONS ON ZONING AND SUBDIVISIONS
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District provides services to the
five Metro Townships in the Salt Lake Valley, unincorporated areas, and the Town of
Brighton; and
WHEREAS, White City Metro Township adopted each county ordinance in effect at the
time of its incorporation pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-2a-414(3) and has
authority to amend or repeal the county ordinance when it determines it is necessary; and
WHEREAS, White City Metro Township is a municipality and has authority to regulate
zoning and subdivisions in general pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-3c-103 (2);
and
WHEREAS, White City Metro Township has authority to adopt zoning and subdivision
regulations pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-501 in accordance with the Municipal
Land Use, Development, and Management Act, (““MLUDMA”), Title 10, Section 9a, Utah
Code, to protect public health, safety, and welfare; and
WHEREAS, the White City Council “Council” deems it necessary to amend its
ordinances in order to ensure compliance with State Statute, encourage conformity with
the White City General Plan, promote ease of use for residents and staff, and preserve the
unique character of the White City community; and for the protection and preservation of
the public health, safety and general welfare.
WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann § 10-9a-502 provides the planning commission shall provide
notice as required by Subsection 10-9a-205(1)(a) and, if applicable, Subsection 10-9a-
205(4) and hold a public hearing on the proposed land use ordinances; and
WHEREAS, on April 4, 2023, the required notice was published; and
WHEREAS, on April 27, 2023, the proposed amendment was submitted to the planning
commission for its recommendation; and

WHEREAS, an April 27, 2023, the planning commission held the required public hearing
with respect to repealing and replacing Title 18 Subdivisions & Title 19 Zoning of the
White City Metro Township Municipal Code of Ordinances; and
WHEREAS, on May 25, 2023, the Council caused the required notice to be given; and
WHEREAS, on June 1, 2023, the Council considered amending Title 18 & Title 19 of the
White City Metro Township Code of Ordinances by repealing Title 18 Subdivisions &
Title 19 Zoning of the municipal code and enacting a revised version of Title 18
Subdivisions and Title 19 Zoning.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL as follows:
1. Chapter 19.60, Section 14.12.050, and Section 14.12.060 (Subsection E) of White
City Municipal Code are repealed.
2. Title 18 Subdivisions is repealed and replaced to read as follows:
Subdivision Ordinance: The following Title 18 Subdivisions is hereby repealed and
replaced in its entirety with the revised Title 18 attached hereto as Attachment 1.
3. Title 19 Zoning is repealed and replaced to read as follows:
Zoning Ordinance: The following Title 19 Zoning is hereby repealed and replaced in its
entirety with the revised Title 19 attached hereto as Attachment 2.
3. Severability. Ifa court of competent jurisdiction determines that any part of these
Ordinances is unconstitutional or invalid, then such portion(s) of these Ordinances, or
specific application of these Ordinances, shall be severed from the remainder, which shall
continue in full force and effect.
4, Effective Date. This Ordinance will take effect immediately upon posting and
publication as required by law.
PASSED AND ADOPTED this Ist day of June 2023.

WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

SUMMARY OF
WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
ORDINANCE NO. 2023-0-01
On June 1, 2023 the White City Metro Township Council enacted Ordinance No.
2023-O-01 that adopts new and updated versions of Titles 18 and 19 (Subdivisions and
Zoning) of the White City Code (“WCC”) and repeals and replaces in their entirety
the prior versions of Titles 18 and 19 as well as repeals Chapter 9.60 (weeds), Section
14.12.050 (trees and landscaping that overhang street pavement), and Subsection E of
Section 14.12.060 (vegetation that obstructs sidewalks).
A complete copy of Ordinance No. 2023-O-01 is available in the office of the White City
Metro Township Clerk, 2001 South State Street, N2-700, Salt Lake City, Utah.
