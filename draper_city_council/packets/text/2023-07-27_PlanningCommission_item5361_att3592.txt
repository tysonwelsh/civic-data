BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 3.57
acres located on the west side of 1700 E, at approximately 1661 E 12435 S (Exhibits B and
C). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment
be approved to amend and extend three (3) existing subdivided lots in the Godfrey
Property Subdivision in order to create five (5) lots.

The Godfrey Property Subdivision was originally recorded as a four (4) lot subdivision in
2012, and the owners of lots 2, 3 and 4 now desire to modify the lot design to add an
additional two (2) lots and to include a small amount of additional property into the
subdivision from a neighboring parcel. Prior approval for a Subdivision Plat amendment to
add one (1) additional lot was given in 2021, however that plat was never recorded. Lot 1 is
not included in the proposed amendment and no changes to lot 1 are proposed.

The amended subdivision plat complies with all applicable subdivision design requirements
with the exception of Public Utility Easements (PUE’s) along the rear lot lines. PUE’s were
not required along the rear lot lines as part of the existing subdivision plat (Exhibit F), and
adding them to an amended plat is unnecessary. Private drainage easements ten feet (10’)
or greater in size are located along the rear and some side lot lines for all lots except Lot 5,
and serve necessary drainage needs. There is an existing single-family dwelling on Lot 5
that is non-conforming to current setbacks in the rear and on the east side that will remain.
The westernmost lot line of lot 5 will be located at least twelve feet (12’) from the existing
single-family dwelling so as to comply with setback requirements and will not increase the
existing degree of non-conformity. On some of the proposed subdivision plans (Exhibit H)
this Single-Family Dwelling is called out as a General Office/Garage use. These uses are not
permitted in the zone, and are not legal uses of the property, and staff is requiring that this
inaccurate label be corrected on revised plans submitted to the City before the amended
subdivision plat is signed.

The cul-de-sac was installed as part of an earlier proposed subdivision plat amendment
that was not recorded. This proposed amendment vacates a small portions of the
previously approved and recorded hammerhead turn around, private street, and related
PUE easements along the street and across the newly proposed Lot 8. A new sewer line
easement has also been added to the plat to reflect the actual installation of the sewer line
across the subject properties and adjacent properties.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application
based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff
report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

    1. That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
       Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
       and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
    2. That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
       development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
