CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2021-1196-O. AN ORDINANCE AMENDING
THE WASHINGTON COUNTY CODE MODIFYING VARIOUS TITLE 10 ZONING
REGULATIONS. DEFINITIONS. AND FURTHER DETAILING RULES AND
GUIDELINES FOR ACCESSORY DWELLING UNITS (ADUS) AND SHORT TERM
RENTALS (STRS). AND DETERMINING WHEN A LICENSE FOR A STR IS
ALLOWED. AND DELETING “TOURIST HOMES” AND “PLANNED DEVELOPMENT
RURAL RECREATION GROUNDS AND FACILITIES” FROM THE ORDINANCES

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the County had previously adopted
the term Tourist Home to allow people operating these homes to get a business license and pay
Transient Room Tax (TRT). Since that time, complaints to the County have gone up steadily
regarding Tourist Homes and Short Term Rentals (STRs). Mr. Messel presented a slideshow
regarding these various STRs.

(See attached report.)

County Deputy Attorney Victoria Hales stated that there are 5,552 approved STRs in Washington
County that would be eligible for a business license. She explained many more homes currently
being offered as STRs are unapproved or not platted and would be ineligible for a business license.

Mr. Messel went over concerns by various departments within the County, including Tourism and
Public Works. Tourism expressed that between all approved STRs and hotels, there is plenty of
accommodations for all tourism and event needs. He stated Public Works had concerns regarding
Washington County Commission

infrastructure, fire flow, sewer and wastewater, on-site parking and access. Mr. Messel explained
that a STR is any home rented for twenty-nine (29) days or less. He stated that ninety-nine percent
(99%) of the complaints Community Development receives regarding STRs are non-owner
occupied. Planning Commission discussed the implementation of a good neighbor policy, a
Washington County STR use license. They further recommend the TRT must be remitted, to allow
existing non-conforming STRs to remain and stay licensed and levying penalties against the owner
and the local property manager due to complaints. Staff recommends approving Option 1 as written
based on the following findings:
e Current ordinances are inadequate
© Property rights are not balanced, and non-owner occupied short term rentals have negative
impacts on residential neighborhoods
e Failure to enact changes gives too much deference to commercial use in residential
neighborhoods, and such use has led to noise, traffic, parking and safety complaints
e More detail for licensing and taxing, and more clarity for enforcing the code are needed

Commissioner Almquist read the definition of Tourist Homes from the previous ordinance, which
include Tourist Homes being properly managed and defines good neighbor practices including
being courteous to neighbors.

Commissioner Iverson stated the lack of infrastructure regarding STRs has caused problems that
are now requiring the County to step in. It is unfortunate that the government has to step in because
absent owners are running hotels in residential neighborhoods, and they have not been good
neighbors.

Commissioner Snow noted that private property rights are not an argument for commercial activity
in residential areas. He stated he is supportive of people using their private property as they see fit
until it affects your neighbors, as they have private property rights as well.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2021-1196-O
(Option 1), An Ordinance Amending the Washington County Code Modifying
Various Title 10 Zoning Regulations, Definitions, and Further Detailing Rules
and Guidelines for Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and Short Term Rentals
(STRs), and Determining When a License for a STR is Allowed, and Deleting
“Tourist Homes” and “Planned Development Rural Recreation Grounds and
Facilities” from the Ordinances. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and Snow
voting aye.
