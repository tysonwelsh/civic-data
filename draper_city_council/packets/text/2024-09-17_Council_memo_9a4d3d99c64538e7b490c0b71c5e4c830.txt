MEMO
To: City Council
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
Date: 2024-09-17
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinances #1621 and #1622


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Land Use and Zoning Map Amendments for
approximately 0.75 acres located on the north side of 13200 South, at approximately 1201 E. 13200
South. The property is currently zoned RA2. The residence on the property was built in 1963 and
the property is not located within a platted subdivision.

The Planning Commission forwarded a unanimous positive recommendation on both items at their
meeting on August 22, 2024.

Land Use Map Amendment
Findings for Approval:
1. The proposed land use map amendment is consistent with goals, objectives, and policies of the
city's general plan.
2. The proposed land use map amendment is harmonious with the overall character of existing
development in the vicinity of the subject property.
3. The proposed land use map amendment will not adversely affect adjacent property.
4. There are adequate facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including, but
not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools,
stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and wastewater and refuse collection.
5. The proposed land use map amendment complies with DCMC Section 9-2-020(F).
6. The legislative body may adopt any amendment to the general plan land use map that the
legislative body considers appropriate.

Findings for Denial:
1. The proposed land use map amendment is not consistent with goals, objectives, and policies of
the city's general plan.
2. The proposed land use map amendment is not harmonious with the overall character of existing
development in the vicinity of the subject property.
3. The proposed land use map amendment will adversely affect adjacent property.
4. There are not adequate facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including,
but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools,
stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and wastewater and refuse collection.
5. The legislative body may reject any amendment to the general plan land use map that the
legislative body considers inappropriate.
