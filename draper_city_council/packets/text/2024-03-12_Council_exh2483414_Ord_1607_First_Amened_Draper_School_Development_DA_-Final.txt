                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1607

         AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE FIRST AMENDED DRAPER SCHOOL
         DEVELOPMENT (DSD) DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR APPROXIMATELY
         0.13 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 352 EAST
         MISTILAKE LN WITHIN DRAPER CITY.

       WHEREAS, the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code
has been established to provide regulations concerning general developments within the
City Boundaries; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council approved the Draper School Development (DSD)
Development Agreement (“Development Agreement”) for the Rasmussen Farms Subdivision
on June 1, 2021 modifying the development standards, including minimum setback
standards; and

      WHEREAS, Lot 20 of the Rasmussen Farms Subdivision has an odd and narrow shape
making it difficult to determine how the setbacks specified in the Development Agreement
should apply to the property ; and

        WHEREAS, the proposed First Amended Draper School Development (DSD)
Development Agreement (“First Amended Development Agreement”) will provide
clarification as to the how the Development Agreement specified setbacks shall apply to Lot
20; and

      WHEREAS, no development standards are being amended by this First Amended
Development Agreement; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it will be in the best interest of the
City to formalize setback locations on Lot 20 in accordance with the First Amended
Development Agreement; and

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

     Section 1. Development Agreement. The City of Draper approves the First
Amended Development Agreement as provided in Exhibit A attached hereto.

       Section 2.    Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

                           Amendment To Development Agreement
                               (Draper School Development)

       This Amendment (the “Amendment”) to the Development Agreement (Draper School

Development), dated ________ is made by and between Draper School Development, LLC, a Utah

limited Liability company (the “Developer”) and the City of Draper, a municipal corporation of

the state of Utah (the “City”). Developer and City may be referred to collectively as the “Parties”.

                                           RECITALS

       A.      Parties entered into the Development Agreement for the Draper School

Development on or around June 7, 2021 (the “Development Agreement”).

       B.      The Development Agreement addressed and controlled certain aspects of the

development of certain real property, located and described in Exhibit A.

       C.      Among other restrictions, the Development Agreement contained the following

setback requirements:

       a.      Front Yard – 20’
       b.      Side Yard Interior – 5’
       c.      Side Yard Collector Street – 15’
       d.      Side Yard Private Street – 10’
       e.      Rear Yard – 15’

       D.      The Development Agreement does not specify for the property described in Exhibit

B which areas of the property correspond with each yard area setback.

       E.      The Development Agreement does not mandate that the front yard setback be

associated with the location of the front door for a prospective house.
       F.       Alpine Homes, LLC has submitted a site plan drawing for a home design to be

located at Lot 20 for the Draper School Development (the “Lot 20 Design”), attached hereto as

Exhibit B.

       G.       The purpose of the Amendment is to clarify the location of the different setbacks

on Lot 20.

                                  TERMS AND CONDITIONS

       NOW THEREFORE, for valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are

hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

       1.       The Lot 20 Design abides by the Development Agreement, including the setback

requirements, as follows:

             a. The Lot 20 Design maintains a 20-foot front yard setback to the east.

             b. The Lot 20 Design maintains a 10-foot side yard private street setback to the north.

             c. The Lot 20 Design maintains a 15-foot rear yard setback to the west.

             d. The Lot 20 Design maintains a 15-foot side yard collector street setback to the

                south.

       2.       The Development Agreement is hereby amended to include the Lot 20 Design

found in Exhibit B as part of the development.

       3.       Except to the extent expressly amended hereby, the Development Agreement shall

remain in full force and effect without impairment or modification. From and after the execution

and delivery of this Amendment by each of the City and Developer, all references to the

Development Agreement shall be deemed to constitute references to the Development Agreement

as amended hereby.
       4.      This Amendment may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which

shall be deemed to be an original, but all of which shall constitute one and the same instrument.

This Amendment shall be binding upon, and insure to the benefit of, the parties hereto and their

respective heirs, administrators, executors, assigns, and successors in interest. The provisions of

this Amendment may not be amended or altered except by written instrument duly executed by

each of the Parties hereto.

       5.      Each of the Parties shall execute such other and further documents and do such

further acts as may be reasonably required to effectuate the intent of the parties and carry out the

terms of this Amendment.
