EMIGRATION CANYON
ORDINANCE NO. 2025-0-12 DATE: September 23, 2025
AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING TITLE 3 CHAPTER 13, MUNICIPAL ENERGY SALES
AND USE TAX AND A CORRESPONDING RATE

WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon has limited sources of revenue it is sound accounting to
have more diverse funding sources; and

WHEREAS, the Utah State Legislature authorized the Municipal Energy Sales and Use
Tax pursuant to Utah Code 10-1-3; and

WHEREAS, on September 23, 2025, Emigration Canyon held a public hearing on a
proposed enactment of the Municipal Telecommunications License tax; and

WHEREAS, the Council finds at this time that it is necessary to implement a Municipal
Energy Sales and Use tax in Emigration Canyon to help with the burden and rising costs that are
now associated with operating a municipality; and

WHEREAS, it is necessary for the Council to determine the appropriate Municipal Energy
Sales and Use tax rate pursuant to Utah Code 10-1-305 in accordance with Utah Code 10-1-304;
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:
Section 1: Municipality hereby adopts and enforces the following Title 3, Chapter 13, as outlined
in Exhibit A setting the energy use tax rate to 6%.
Section 2: This Ordinance’s Effective Date shall be its date of publication.

ADOPTED AND APPROVED at a duly called meeting of the Emigration Canyon Council on this
23rd day of September 2025.
