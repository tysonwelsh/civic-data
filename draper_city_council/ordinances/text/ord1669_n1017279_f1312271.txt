City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1669

On August 19, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1669 amending
Title 19-1-110 of the Draper City Municipal Code to allow employment of relatives of
city officers under certain conditions. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper
City Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
