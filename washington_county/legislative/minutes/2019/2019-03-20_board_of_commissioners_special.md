WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
MARCH 20, 2019

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Cox at 2:00 p.m. on March 20, 2019, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commission Gil
Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Eric Clarke, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf,
Commission Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, Deputy Tourism Director Brittany
McMichael, Tourism Office Staff: Jacqueline Grena and Doug Boyer, Legacy Park
Director Gerry Brown, Fair Director Susi Lafaele, Chamber Representative Pam Palermo.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION REGARDING PERMITTING ALCOHOL AT THE WASHINGTON
COUNTY LEGACY PARK

Commissioner Cox called the meeting to order at 2:00 pm on March 20, 2019. There was
a broad discussion with feedback from Chief Brooksby that as long as reasonable measures
are put in place to limit the amount and location of the beer, then law enforcement feels
permitting beer at events is doable.

Pam, Suzi, and Brittany spoke as to how beer has been permitted at other events in the
county. Generally bracelets and stamps are used to allow up to two drinks and the drinking
is only permitted in a certain area, i.e. beer garden.

Gerry Brown raised some concerns, but doesn't feel like they can't be overcome.
Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf was assigned to reach out to Hurricane City Attorney

Faye Reber about whether permitting beer at events in the Washington County Legacy
Park is permissible under Hurricane City ordinance and regulations.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
2:45 p.m.

EAN COX
DEPUTY CLERK CHAIRMAN
