            15. APPROVE AND ADOPT A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING UTAH COUNTY PICK UP OF PUBLIC SAFETY AND
            FIREFIGHTER EMPLOYEE RETIREMENT CONTRIBUTIONS.
            -Ralf Barnes

            RALF BARNES, DIRECTOR OF HUMAN RESOURCES – GAVE A BRIEF SYNOPSIS OF SENATE BILL 56. WAS
            PASSED TO ENHANCE TIER 2 PUBLIC SAFETY BENEFITS FOR URS. IT INCREASES THE MULTIPLIER FROM
            1.5 TO 2.0 AND ENHANCES THE DEATH BENEFIT FOR PUBLIC SAFETY OFFICERS IN TIER 2 TO 30% OF
            THEIR FINAL AVERAGE SALARY. THIS WAS BROUGHT TO THE LEGISLATURE IN HOPES OF IMPROVING
            THE ATTRACTION AND RETENTION OF LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS. THE STATE WILL PICK UP THE
            EXTRA COST FOR THE STATE EMPLOYEES BUT ARE ASKING THE COUNTIES AND LOCAL GOVERNMENTS
            TO PICK UP THE EXTRA COST FOR THEIR AREAS. SPOKE ABOUT DIFFERENT OPTIONS AS TO HOW TO
            COVER THE COSTS.

            COMMISSIONER AINGE COMMENTED ON THE STRUGGLES RIGHT NOW OF ATTRACTING NEW
            OFFICERS. DISCUSSED OPTIONS OF WHAT WOULD MAKE THE JOB MORE ENTICING – WOULD IT BE
            BETTER TO APPLY TOWARDS WAGES OR BENEFITS ENHANCEMENTS?

            RALF BARNES – LOOKING AT BENEFITS PLAN, THE COUNTY IS TYPICALLY RICHER THAN WHAT YOU
            FIND IN THE PRIVATE SECTOR. FEELS THAT PUTTING THE INCREASE INTO WAGES IS RISKY BECAUSE
            THAT WOULD BEGIN AN EXPECTATION THAT YOU HAVE TO CONTINUE TO DO SO JUST TO STAY UP
            WITH INFLATION.

            COMMISSIONER LEE – IN SUPPORT. FEELS IT GET US GOING IN THE RIGHT DIRECTION.

            SHERIFF MIKE SMITH – IN AGREEMENT WITH COMMISSIONER LEE. FEELS THE PROBLEM IS WE ARE
            NOT ONLY COMPETING WITH THE COMMERCIAL MARKET BUT ARE ALSO COMPETING WITH
            OURSELVES. THERE ARE SO MANY PRESSURES GOING ON RIGHT NOW. STRONGLY FEELS THAT THIS
            NEEDS TO BE DONE TO STAY SOMEWHAT COMPETITIVE AND TO KEEP GOOD PEOPLE.

            COMMISSIONER LEE: MOTION TO APPROVE AND ADOPT
            COMMISSIONER IVE: SECOND
            AYE: ALL IN FAVOR
            PASSED: 3/0
