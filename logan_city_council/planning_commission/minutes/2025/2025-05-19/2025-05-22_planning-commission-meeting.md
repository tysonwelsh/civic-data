Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session on
Thursday, May 22, 2025. Commissioner Peterson called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Eldon Peterson, David Lewis, Ken Heare, Sarah McNamara, Jennifer
Duncan

Commissioners Excused: Jessica Lucero, Sara Doutre

Staff Present: Russ Holley, Aimee Egbert, Mike DeSimone, Mohamed Abdullahi, Amanda Pearce,
Darren Farar

Approval of the Minutes: Commissioner Heare made a motion to approve the minutes from the
April 24, 2025 meeting. Commissioner Duncan seconded the motion. The motion was approved
unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING:

PC 25-023 Chase Bank -Requesting continuance to the July 10" meeting. [Design Review
Permit] Matt Anderson/Logan Main and 4" Pads, Steven Usdan, authorized agent/owner, is
requesting a design review permit for a new 3,350 square foot Chase Bank located at the southeast
corner of 400 North and Main Street in the Town Center One (TC-1) zone; TIN 06-043-0017; -0015
(Adams Neighborhood).

MOTION: Commissioner Heare made a motion to continue a Design Review Permit for PC 25-023
to the July 10, 2025 meeting as requested. Commissioner McNamara seconded the motion. The
motion was approved unanimously.

PC 25-031 Young Automotive Sales and Service -Requesting continuance to the June 12"
meeting. [Design Review and Conditional Use Permit] Kenneth Stuart/YPPVRE LLC, authorized
agent/owner, is requesting a design review and conditional use permit for a new auto dealership and
service department located at approximately 1900 South 800 West in the Commercial (COM) zone;
TIN 03-006-0003; -0004; -0005; -0006; -0007; -0013; -0033 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

MOTION: Commissioner Heare made a motion to continue a Design Review and Conditional Use
Permit for PC 25-031 to the June 12, 2025 meeting as requested. Commissioner McNamara
seconded the motion. The motion was approved unanimously.

PC 25-030 Verizon Wireless Communication Tower-UT1 Don Reese [Design Review &
Conditional Use Permit] Troy Benson/Blue Spring Building 1 LLC, authorized agent/owner, is
requesting a design review and conditional use permit for a new 77-foot tall Monopine Wireless
Communication Tower located at 1148 North 600 West in the Industrial (IP) zone; TIN 05-130-8001
(Bridger Neighborhood).


Staff: Aimee Egbert, Logan City Planner, reviewed the request for a new 70-foot-tall wireless
telecommunication tower designed as a stealth pine tree with artificial branches extending seven feet
above the pole, bringing the total height to approximately 77 feet. A 40 x 40 square foot area at the
base of the tower will be enclosed by a 6-foot woodland brown composite fence to house the
accessory ground equipment.

The Land Development Code allows monopoles to be 70 feet tall in the Industrial zone with antennas
mounted to the side of the pole not extending beyond three feet from the outer edge of the pole. The
applicant is asking for the antennas to extend 6.3 feet from the pole to better conform with the
branched below that extend out 8-feet from the pole.

The proposed access road sits next to a storm water detention area for the neighboring building. If
the access road affects the storm water basin capacity, the portion affected will have to be replaced
to ensure the capacity is not changed. There are also existing shrubs and trees within the proposed
access road that will need to be replaced, and a storm drain grate that will have to be moved out of
the access road or replaced with a traffic rated grate.

Proponent: Troy Benson said they have enough room for the access road and should not have to
impede into the storm water basin.

Commissioner Heare asked if there is a maintenance plan for the tower for weathering. Mr. Benson
stated that a Verizon technician will visit the site monthly to inspect the tower.

Public: No public comment was made.

Commission: The Commission discussed amending condition 4 allowing the antennas to extend
6.3 feet from the pole, adding a condition that any removed landscaping shall be replaced at a 1 to
1 ratio, and adding a condition under Engineering that the existing storm drain box shall be located
outside of the access drive or constructed as traffic rated.

MOTION: Commissioner Heare made a motion to conditionally approve a Design Review and
Conditional Use Permit for PC 25-030 with the amended conditions of approval and findings for
approval as listed below. Commissioner Duncan seconded the motion. The motion was approved
unanimously.

Any landscaping removed shall be replaced at a 1 to 1 ratio.

The tower shall adhere to FFA and Logan Regional Airport regulations and standards.
