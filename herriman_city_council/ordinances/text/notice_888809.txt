PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on January 24, 2024, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2024-02 Amending the Herriman City Land Development Code to comply with updated subdivision review and public notice requirements in the 2023 Utah State Municipal Land Use, Development, and Management Act. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
