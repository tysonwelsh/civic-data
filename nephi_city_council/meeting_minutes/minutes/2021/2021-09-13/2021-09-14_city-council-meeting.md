                                        Nephi City
                                  Work Session Minutes
                                  September 14, 2021
                                         7:00 p.m.
                               Nephi City Council Chambers




1. Chief of Police Michael Morgan addressed the council about recent complaints of illegal
   use of ATVS/OHVs and golf carts on public roads by underage drivers. Chief Morgan
   indicated that one of the difficulties of enforcement is that stops of these types of
   violations can take a lot of time to contact parents and have the parents come and get
   their child and other more important calls may come in while dealing with the violation
   and there is only one patrol officer on duty. here is typically only one officer on at a time
   and call volumes have increased over the past several years while the number of patrol
   officers has stayed the same.

   Chief Morgan proposed having a special detail and have an off-duty officer wok an
   overtime shift specifically policing for ATVs/OHVS/golf cart violations. This special detail
   would only be worked on specific times and days where most of the violations may be
   occurring. These overtime patrol shifts would only be seasonal in the fall and the spring.


   Mayor suggested taking the children home and leaving the ATV on the road. Chief said
   that is not possible because of leaving the ATV on the side of the road and the liability
   the city would incur with something like this. Chief suggested advertising the blitz
   enforcement so that the public is aware of the patrolling. Chief invited the council to
   come and ride along with the officers. Councilor Memmott asked about the VIPS being
   utilized for this patrol or about the reserve officers. Ostler said that the biggest problem
   is the parents allowing the children to operate the ATV’s and golf carts illegally. Kasey
   Wright reviewed the penalties of the violations per Nephi City Code. Councilor Jones said
   that the entire council along with the Chief should sign the article in the paper. Councilor
   Jones said we need another patrol officer.

   The council agreed to the special detail and the overtime shifts.


2. City Administrator Seth Atkinson updated the council about an individual who
   complained about the humming and the lights at the north substation. Mayor Nielson
   had met with the individual and offered some different solutions to help mitigate the
   annoyance. The individual said a water connection would help. Mayor Nielson said that
   he went down in the evening to examine the situation and he said that their lives have
   been changed for the worse from the substation. All council members were worried
   about the precedent it would set if they allowed a water connection outside city limits
      and didn’t understand how a water connection would help solve the problem of the
      substation annoyance.

  3. Mr. Atkinson informed that council about a property owner that is considering
     petitioning for annexation to develop a commercial subdivision. Half of the property is a
     Level 1 and the other half of his property is Level 3 on the annexation area map. Mr.
     Atkinson requested feedback from the council on changing the property in question to a
     Level 2. The council directed staff to put the level change on the next regular meeting
     agenda as an action item.

  4. Mr. Atkinson reviewed several land use code changes that the Planning Commission had
     recommended. The council directed staff to bring back the revisions in ordinance form
     next week for consideration.

  5. Mr. Atkinson reviewed a zone change request from North Ridge Estates Plat B. There
     are Rocky Mountain power poles running through this phase. The city would like to draft
     an addendum to the original development agreement that would require the poles
     moved before the development is completed.

  6. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the ATV Nebo site plan had been approved by the
     Planning and Zoning Commission and that construction would begin soon.

  7. Mr. Atkinson informed the council about a zone change request for the Mangelson
     property approximately at 200 West 700 North. The request is for a zero-lot overlay.
     The zone change needs a public hearing that will be held at the next Planning and Zoning
     Commission meeting in October.

  8. The council discussed the water service connection project in Nebo Heights.

  9. City Attorney Kasey Wright informed the council that the businesses involve in a business
     license revocation hearing that was to be held on September 21st have requested that
     the hearing be moved to October 5th. The council agreed to grant the courtesy.

  10. Mr. Atkinson and the City Council gave various updates.

  11. Mayor Nielson called to order a regular council meeting at 8:55 p.m.
