BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 0.99 acres located on the north side of 12000 South, at approximately 685 E.
12000 S. (Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a
Subdivision Plat Amendment be approved to allow for the creation of a flag lot behind the
existing home on the subject property.

The existing home on the property was originally built in 1939. The current Denney
Subdivision is a two (2) lot subdivision which amended Lots 2, 3, and 4 of Block 34 of the
Draperville Subdivision Plat recorded in 1873. This proposed amendment would create a
flag lot to the rear of the existing home on Lot 1 of the Denney Subdivision and split the lot
into two (2) lots.

ANALYSIS
General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
Low/Medium Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This
category is characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately
two (2) dwelling units per acre (Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)
Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little
impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the
City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large-lot family life,
including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these
zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by
commercial and industrial uses”. The RA2 zoning abuts the subject property on the south,
west, and north. The RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 ft2 min. lot size) zone also abuts
the property on the north. The CN (Neighborhood Commercial) zone abuts on the east.

Subdivision Layout. The proposed subdivision layout (Exhibit E) shows how the flag lot will
be accessed from the west side of the current lot by means of a twenty feet (20’) wide
driveway with an easement for emergency vehicle access to Lot 3 (rear lot). A turn around
near the terminus of the driveway for the use of emergency vehicles is also provided. A
small portion of this easement and turn around will be provided across lot 4 (front lot). An
easement for the adjacent canal encumbers a sizable portion of the eastern and
northeastern area of the property. The detention pond easement previously near the front
of original lot 1 (Exhibit F) is proposed to be vacated with this plat and new individual
easements for retention established on each of the two new proposed lots. As this
easement is private and not a public easement, the alteration will not violate ordinance
provisions pertaining to the approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment.

The flag lot measures 22,579 ft2 in size, and the flag portion of the lot is set at exactly
20,000 ft2 in order be able to utilize setbacks of twenty-five feet (25’) from the property lines
rather than the thirty feet (30’) that would be required of larger flag lots. The proposed Lot
4, or the front lot, measures 20,651 ft2 in size. The proposed subdivision design
incorporates much of the land area encumbered by the canal easement along the east
within the proposed Lot 4 as a method of increasing the potentially available area on Lot 3
to construct a single-family dwelling in the future. The subdivision design complies with the
flag lot requirements found in DCMC Section 9-27-090. The required frontage
improvements are already in existence.

The plans indicate that the existing home is to remain, and the home will comply with
setback requirements to the property lines. Access to the driveway for Lot 3 by Lot 4 will
not be permitted as doing so would have the effect of creating a private roadway, which
would not meet minimum width requirements of ordinance and from which the existing
home would not meet the minimum setbacks required of a corner lot. There is a small
existing shed on the property in the area of the proposed Lot 3. This shed must be
removed prior to final signatures and recording of the plat so as to not create a zoning
violation at the time the subdivision is recorded.

Landscaping and Lot Coverage. The landscape plan (Exhibit D) shows the existing street trees
in the park strip and proposes to add an additional tree. The maintenance of these trees
will continue to be the responsibility of the adjacent property owner. An existing spray type
irrigation system will continue to provide the necessary irrigation.

Criteria For Approval.
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing
        an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds
        that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
        way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request, receive public
comment, and make a decision based on the findings listed below and the criteria for
approval, or denial, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

        1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                 Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the
                 site and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
        2.       That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
                 development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
        3.       That the amended plat includes all requirements outlined in Chapters 17-3,
                 17-4, and 17-9 of the DCMC.
        4.       That the existing shed on the property in the area of the proposed Lot 3 be
                 removed prior to obtaining final signatures and recording of the plat.
        5.       That no vehicular access to Lot 4 is permitted from the proposed driveway
                 and emergency vehicle turn-around providing access to Lot 3, and that a
                 note to this effect be included on the Plat.
        6.       Add plat note indicating requirement of lot retention basin, volume, and
                 maintenance responsibility per DCMC 17-5-050(E).

The findings for approval as are follows:

        1.       There is good cause for the amendment
        2.       No public street, right-of-way, or easement will be vacated or amended.

The findings for denial as are follows:

        1.       That there is not good cause to amend the plat.
