                                        SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL
                                              MINUTES
                                          Thursday, October 8, 2020
                                         Approved November 18, 2020

The following are the minutes of the Special City Council Electronic Meeting of the Herriman City Council. The
meeting was held on Thursday, October 8, 2020 at 6:15 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Council Chambers, 5355
West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in
the City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Mayor Pro Tempore Clint Smith

Councilmembers Present: Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields and Mayor David Watts

Staff Present: Interim City Manager Wendy Thomas, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Communications
Manager Jonathan LaFollette, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, City Attorney Chase Andrizzi, and Finance Director
Alan Rae.

Councilmembers Excused: Jared Henderson

6:15 PM - SPECIAL MEETING: (Or as soon thereafter as reasonably feasible)
1. Call to Order
Councilmember Steven Shields moved to nominate Councilmember Clint Smith to serve as the Mayor Pro Tempore. Councilmember
Sherrie Ohrn seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

Mayor Pro Tempore Clint Smith called the meeting to order at 6:17 p.m. and excused Councilmember Jared
Henderson.

2. Discussion and Action Items
    2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution to initiate the process by which the Herriman City
         Council may consider the creation of the Herriman City Fire Service Area to fund Fire and EMS
         Services within Herriman City – Chase Andrizzi, City Attorney
City Attorney Chase Andrizzi informed the Council staff had recently commenced withdrawal negotiations with the
Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA). He iterated the UFSA was the entity which collected property taxes to distribute
 October 8, 2020 – Special City Council Minutes                                                         Page 2 of 2
funds to the Unified Fire Authority (UFA). Attorney Andrizzi offered a brief overview of the statutory requirements
and timeline to initiate the creation of the Herriman City Fire Service Area (HCFSA). The new entity would be set up
specifically for the collection and distribution of property taxes for fire and EMS services for Herriman City residents.

Councilmember Shields asked what the next step in the process would entail. Attorney Andrizzi responded a notice
of public hearing would be published. It was noted the public hearing would be conducted on November 5, 2020 at
5:30 p.m. followed by a protest period. He explained the protest period would conclude on January 4, 2021.

Attorney Andrizzi specified the tax rate set by the HCFSA would be exactly the same as currently being assessed by
the UFSA, so there would not be any amount change to the residents.

Mayor Pro Tempore Smith clarified this District would be set up to collect the property tax and not the collection of
Impact Fees as approved in the previous meeting.

Councilmember Steve Shields moved to approve Resolution No. R35-2020 to initiate the process by which the Herriman City Council
may consider the creation of the Herriman City Fire Service Area to fund Fire/EMS services within Herriman City. Mayor Watts
seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Mayor Watts                  Aye
Councilmember Smith          Aye
Councilmember Shields        Aye
Councilmember Ohrn           Aye
Councilmember Henderson Absent

The motion passed unanimously with Councilmember Henderson being absent.

3. Adjournment
Councilmember Shields moved to adjourn the special meeting at 6:28 p.m. Councilmember Ohrn seconded the motion and all voted aye.

I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true,
accurate and complete record of the meeting held on October 8, 2020. This document constitutes the official
minutes for the Special Herriman City Council Meeting.
