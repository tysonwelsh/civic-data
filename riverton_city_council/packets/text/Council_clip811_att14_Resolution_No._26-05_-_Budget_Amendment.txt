                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-05

             A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL
      APPROVING AMENDMENTS TO THE 2025-2026 FISCAL YEAR BUDGET

       WHEREAS, the Budget for the 2025-2026 Fiscal Year was adopted on June 17, 2025; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration it has been determined by the Administration that
several revenue line items will likely require adjustments to more accurately reflect actual
collections and newly projected trends; and

        WHEREAS, the Administration has also determined that several expenditure lines in the
budget corresponding to the changes in revenue should also be adjusted reflecting operational and
capital needs.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

      1.   The Riverton City Budget for the 2025-2026 Fiscal Year be amended according to the
           attached detail identified as “Exhibit A”.

      2.   This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 20th day of
January 2026 by the following vote:
