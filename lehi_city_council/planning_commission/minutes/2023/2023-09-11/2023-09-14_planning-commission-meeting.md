2.5) Public hearing and recommendation of Consideration of Brianna Nichols’s request for
approval of the Gurney Manor Estates Plat B 2-lot subdivision with an existing home located
at 1018 North 700 East in the RA-1 zone.

Applicant Brianna Nichols was present for questions.

The discussion from the Commissioners focused on stormwater requirements for a flag lot.
Concerns were raised about the ambiguity of "all stormwater" and the need for specificity
regarding storm conditions. The standard proposed was retention for the 100-year storm. The
conversation also touched on grading challenges and the potential for a cleaner-looking plat with
clear stormwater guidelines.

Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Brianna Nichols’s request for approval of the
Gurney Manor Estates Plat B 2-lot subdivision with an existing home located at 1018 North 700
East in the RA-1 zone; including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in the staff report.
With a change to the wording that the water retention is to reflect the 100 year storm. The motion
was seconded by Commissioner Everett.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.6) Public hearing and recommendation of JDH Development’s request for preliminary
subdivision review of Vivian Estates Phase 4, a 22-lot residential development located at 600
East 2000 North.

Applicant Josh Moffit for JDH discussed ongoing work on a trail south of Dry Creek. The
challenge is creating a connection from the neighborhood across Dry Creek due to grading and
stream alteration concerns. While a connection exists at 600 East, internal neighborhood
connections are being explored. The trail is crucial per the development agreement, and efforts are
underway to address connectivity challenges. The discussion also touched on the possibility of
steps for smoother trail connections.

Chairperson Newall opened the public hearing.
Chairperson Newall closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved recommend approval for JDH Development’s request for
preliminary subdivision review of Vivian Estates Phase 4, a 22-lot residential development located
at 600 East 2000 North; including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in the staff report
and the recommendation that we do everything possible to make that connection with the trail. The
motion was seconded by Commissioner Eyre.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.7) Public hearing and recommendation of JDH Development’s request for review of a
grading permit for Vivian Estates phases 3 & 4 on approximately 43-acres located at
approximately 600 East 2300 North.

Applicant Josh Moffit for JDH was available for questions.

Chairperson Newall opened the public hearing.
Chairperson Newall closed the public hearing.

There was one public comment online through Engage Lehi.

Commissioner Carlson questioned the frequency of oversight and inspections for adequate

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.8) Recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development Code Amendment
to Chapter 26, Accessory Uses, changing the lot size requirement for detached ADUs from
1/2 to 1/3 acre. (tabled from the August 10, 2023 meeting)

Applicant Gary Clark was present for questions.

Chairperson Newall opened the public hearing.

There were two new comments that had been sent to commissioners on this item in support of this
project through Engage Lehi.

Chairperson Newall closed the public hearing.

During the past discussion on windows for detached ADUs, concerns were raised about potential
loopholes, leading to a debate on the advantages and disadvantages of having windows close to
property lines. The conversation also touched on setbacks, the overall impact of ADUs on
neighborhoods, rental properties, and impact fees. Ultimately, they agreed to limit the number of
small windows to two and make them opaque.

Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to approve Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Chapter 26, Accessory Uses, changing the lot size requirement
for detached ADUs from 1/2 to 1/3 acre.; including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed
in the staff report. With the exception that paragraph two read that it can be within five feet on
either side of this, either the side or rear yard. If that wall has no windows, or up to two small
windows with opaque glass smaller than two square feet each in size. And if the walls have
Windows larger than two square feet in size, minimum setback of 10 feet is required and so forth
as continued in that paragraph. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Bunker.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
