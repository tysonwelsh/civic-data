# meeting_minutes/ — Holladay City Council (+ in-session RDA/LBA) vote pipeline

Turns **152 council minutes** (2020-01-09 → 2026-04-16) into structured motions + votes.
Source spine: **Utah Public Notice (PMN), public body 388** — every doc is a born-digital,
`pdftotext -layout`-clean minutes PDF pulled from `utah.gov/pmn/files/<id>.pdf` (the
`<id>` is the trailing token in each filename and the `source_url` in `minutes_index.csv`).

## Cardinal Holladay fact — the MAYOR VOTES (max council roll = 6)
Council–Manager form: 5 district members **+ a voting Mayor** (the City Manager is the
executive). A full named roll tops out at **6** (`... Mayor Dahle-Aye`). Roster turned over
at the Jan-2026 seating (Mayor Dahle → **Fotheringham**; D1 Brewer → **Sundwall**; D3
Fotheringham → **Bradley**), so who-voted analysis must be date-aware. `roster.csv` is
**OBSERVED** (regenerated by `validate_votes.py` from the actual vote rows, never
hand-authored); a member is flagged `mayor_rows_seen=yes` when the source printed them as
`Mayor <Name>` in a roll.

## What's here
- `minutes/<year>/<week-monday>/<date>_<slug>_<pmnFileId>.md` — 152 files (Council meets
  **Thursday**, ~twice monthly, schedule varies; folder keyed on that week's Monday). A
  handful of dates carry **two docs** (e.g. a 6:00 Regular meeting + a Legislative
  Breakfast/Meeting) — both retained, distinguished by `<pmnFileId>`.
- `raw/` — the retained source PDFs (never modified).
- `minutes_index.csv` — `date,year,title,slug,path,source(=pmn),source_url,format(=pdf-text),body`.
- `minutes_unrecovered.csv` — **25 honest gaps**: meeting noticed on PMN but no Meeting-Minutes
  attachment published there (annual planning retreats that produce no minutes, a few work
  meetings, and recent 2026 meetings whose minutes are pending approval). Never stubbed.
- `extract_votes.py` — PURE deterministic (no LLM/network), resumable → `votes/<year>/<week>/*.json`
  + `all_votes.csv`.
- `validate_votes.py` → `votes/_validation_report.txt` + regenerates `roster.csv`. **PASS.**
- `screen_corpus.py` — mandatory corpus-screen gate (stub<200B + per-year anomaly). **CLEAN**
  (0 stubs, 0 low-alpha; born-digital throughout — no OCR needed).

## Vote grammar (two eras, both named per-member rolls)
- **Modern (2022+):** `Vote on Motion: Council Member Gray-Aye; ...; Mayor Dahle-Aye.`
  (Council mostly prints `-Aye/-Nay`; some `-Yes/-No`. Dissent observed: `-Nay`, `-Recuse`.)
- **Legacy (2020–2021):** prose roll — `The Council roll call vote was as follows: Council
  Members Gibbons, Quinn, Fotheringham, Durham, Petersen and Mayor Dahle in favor [with
  Council Member X opposed].` Favor→Yes, opposed→No, abstained→Abstain (parsed structurally,
  no roster hard-coding, so a member named only in the prose list is never dropped).
- **Consent/procedural:** `passed with the unanimous consent of the Council` → `names_recorded:false`,
  blank member/vote (tally-only, per the "honest gaps are data" rule).
- **In-session RDA / LBA:** the council recesses & reconvenes as the Redevelopment Agency /
  Local Building Authority; those motions are re-tagged `body=RDA`/`body=LBA` via a
  section-walk. Standalone RDA **Board of Directors** meeting docs (own "Board Member/Chair"
  roster) are routed wholly to `body=RDA` by slug. Total: 60 RDA + 12 LBA vote rows.

## Known SOURCE error — the stale closed-session roll (2024; verified 2026-07-29)
The clerk's **closed-session paragraph is a template**, and on four 2024 dates it was carried forward
with the **2023 slate** still in it. On **2024-02-15, 2024-03-21, 2024-04-25 and 2024-12-12** the
motion *"moved to adjourn the Closed Session"* prints a roll naming **Gibbons** — e.g. 2024-12-12:
*"The Council roll call vote was as follows: Council Members Durham, Fotheringham, Quinn, Gibbons,
Brewer and Mayor Dahle in favor."* **Daniel Gibbons left D5 at the 2024-01-04 seating** (Emily Gray
won the seat, uncontested); Gray voted the full slate on those same meeting nights. The same stale
name also appears as a **`seconder`** on **2024-06-13**. The clerk *did* update the template to Gray
on other 2024 dates (2024-09-19, -10-03, -10-24) and throughout 2025.

**These rows are NOT extraction artifacts** — the extractor reproduced the printed roll exactly, and
**no motion in this city exceeds a named roll of 6** (verified across `all_votes.csv`). An earlier
`roster/` note that called them "EXTRACTION ARTIFACTS (a 7th name over the roll of 6)" was wrong: the
7th name is a per-**meeting** distinct-name count (6 real voters + the stale template name), never a
7-name roll call. Per cardinal rule 2 the rows are **retained verbatim**; no override is applied
(`db/vote_overrides.csv` resolves *contradictory* duplicate pairs and add-member rows, and these are
neither). **When attributing votes to seated members, exclude these 4 `Gibbons` rows** — the
`roster/` tenure clamp already does this, ending his window at 2024-01-04.

`all_votes.csv` 13-col: `date,year,title,body,motion_no,motion,motion_type,result,mover,
seconder,member,vote,source`. Only `result` is city-faithful/verbatim; `motion_type`
is a derived convenience label, and **`vote` is NORMALIZED to the controlled vocabulary**
(SCHEMA_SPEC §4: Aye/Nay/Abstain/Recuse/Absent). The 2022+ minutes print some rolls as
`-Yes/-No`; those tokens are mapped to **Aye/Nay** at extraction (the PC already prints
Aye/Nay natively). So `all_votes.csv` carries **zero** `Yes`/`No` values by design — do not
expect the raw source token in the `vote` column. Members keyed by surname as printed (source prints surnames
only); OCR/spelling variants folded via `SURNAME_ALIASES` (Peterson→Petersen, Gibbon→Gibbons).
702 motions, 2,475 vote rows (2,173 named), 6 contested motions, 365 mayor vote rows.
