B. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-12-02 Budget Revisions 2025 
Sara made a motion to Resolution 2025-12-02 Budget Revisions 2025. Suzanne 
seconded the request. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
 HAUC prepares annual budgets before the beginning of a new calendar year. The annual 
budgets are presented and approved by the HAUC Board of Commissioners in November 
or December. During the 4th quarter, HAUC reviews the annual budgets and adjusts 
based on market conditions, current trends, and anticipated needs.  
The following budget revisions for 2025, were presented for approval:  
Pacific Drive, Bridgeway, Section 8, Business Activities, and Cedar View

D. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-12-04 Owned Housing Hail Damage Repair 
April made a motion to approve the Resolution 2025-12-04 Owned Housing Hail 
Damage Repair. Suzanne seconded the request. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
 TN031-34 units sustained damage to the roof, rain gutters, siding, and shutters. The selected 
bid to replace the damaged elements equals the insurance's actual cash value, and when the 
work is completed, HAUC will receive a depreciation amount from the insurance provider, and 
when combined with the actual cash value, equals the full bid amount. Work is anticipated to 
be completed this year.  
 
 
 
E. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-12-05 Carillon Court Concrete 
April made a motion to approve the Resolution 2025-12-05 Carillon Court Concrete. 
Suzanne seconded the request. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
 
HAUC received 3 bids from local contractors ranging from $62K to $66K. The selected 
vendor is the lowest bidder, has provided quality concrete work in the past for HAUC. The 
selected vendor has experience with ADA requirements and is recommended by HAUC staff 
to be engaged to complete the work.  
 
 
F. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-12-06 Purchase Agreement Addendum #3 
Suzanne made a motion to approve the Resolution 2025-12-06 Purchase Agreement 
Addendum #3. April seconded the request. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
  
The Housing Authority of Utah County Board of Commissioners approved a purchase 
 
agreement for the acquisition of a property located in Orem, Utah. HAUC staff continue to work 
on due diligence items, including the property assessment report, property survey, and title 
report. HAUC continues to work to address issues with boundary lines identified on the survey. 
Addendum #3 extends the due diligence period and the closing date to January 30, 2026. 
Additional time is needed to work with the necessary entities to address the boundary line and 
survey issues.  
 
 
G. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-12-07 Holiday Schedule 
April made a motion to approve the Resolution 2025-12-07 Holiday Schedule. Sara 
seconded the request. The motion passed unanimously.  
  
  
The proposed holiday list contains 11 Federal holidays and 1 State holiday for a total of 12 
holidays. Out of these 12 holidays, HAUC’s offices will be closed for 11 of them. Due to 
the Martin Luther King Holiday (MLK) being so close to Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New 
Year's holidays, MLK will be a floating holiday for staff to utilize during the same year, 
while HAUC offices remain open for normal business.
