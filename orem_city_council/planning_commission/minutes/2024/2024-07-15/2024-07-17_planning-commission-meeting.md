                               16      Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Logan Johnson for the Planning Commission to
                               17      approve the site plan for Orem Art Lofts Located generally at 841 South State Street
                               18      in the C2 zone. Mr. Crimson recused himself from the agenda item.
                               19
                               20      Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing the site plan of the Orem Art Lofts.
                               21      There were two homes on the southeast corner of the property and have demolition
                               22      plans in place or may have already been demolished.
                               23      The residential units are comprised of a combination of Studio, One Bedroom, Two
24   Bedroom and Three-bedroom apartments. There is a total of 656 Parking stalls that will be in a 4 story Parking
25   Structure, there is 2.09 stalls per unit. There will be a variety of amenities on the roof of the property that include
26   yoga lawns, pickleball courts, dog park among other things.

28   The site plan is in accordance with the settlement agreement. The requirements were placed according to the
