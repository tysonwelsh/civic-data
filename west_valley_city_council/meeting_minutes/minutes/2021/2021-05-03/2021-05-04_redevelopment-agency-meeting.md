RESOLUTION 21-02: ADOPT A TENTATIVE BUDGET AND SET FORTH
PROPOSED          APPROPRIATIONS             FOR     THE   SUPPORT       OF     THE
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY
1, 2021 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2022; SET JUNE 1, 2021 AS THE DATE FOR THE
PUBLIC HEARING
Wayne Pyle, Chief Executive Officer, presented proposed Resolution 20-02 that would
adopt a tentative budget and set forth proposed appropriations for the support of the
Redevelopment Agency for the Fiscal Year commencing July 1, 2021 and ending June 30,
2022; set June 1, 2021 as the date for the Public Hearing.

Written documentation previously provided to the Redevelopment Agency included
information as follows:

       This resolution adopts the tentative budget for the Redevelopment Agency of West
       Valley City for the 2021-2022 fiscal year and sets a date for public comment at a
       hearing on June 1, 2021.

       The Redevelopment Agency will adopt a tentative budget that will be made
       available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s Recorder’s
       office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment, before the final
       adoption of this tentative budget for FY 2021-2022.

Upon inquiry by Chairperson Lang there were no further questions from members of the
Agency, and she called for a motion.

       Mr. Bigelow moved to approve Resolution 21-02.

       Mr. Christensen seconded the motion.

       A roll call vote was taken:


       Mr. Fitisemanu                Yes
       Mr. Buhler                    Yes
       Mr. Huynh                     Absent
       Mr. Bigelow                   Yes
       Mr. Christensen               Yes
       Mr. Nordfelt                  Yes
       Chair Lang                    Yes

       Unanimous.
