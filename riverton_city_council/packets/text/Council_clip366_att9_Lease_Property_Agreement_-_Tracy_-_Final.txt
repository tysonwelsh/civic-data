                                 TRACY AVIARY LEASE AGREEMENT



This LEASE AGREEMENT (‘Lease”) is made and entered into as of the 16 day of June 2020 by and
between Riverton City (the “CITY”) and FRIENDS OF TRACY AVIARY, a Utah non‐profit corporation
("FRIENDS").

                                              RECITALS

   A. CITY owns certain real property located at: Beginning at a point on the eastern line of Lot 143,
      River View Estates Phase 1, Subdivision, said point being South 00°06'50” West along the section
      line 673.15 feet and S 89°53'10" E 2,059.01 feet from the West Quarter Corner of Section 26,
      Township 3 South, Range 1 West, Salt Lake Base and Meridian, and running thence along said
      subdivision the following seven (7) courses:
         (1) N 17°31'54" W 67.00 feet;
         (2) N 27°00'14" W 118.15 feet;
         (3) N 18°42'36" E 98.05 feet;
         (4) N 00°35'06" E 75.42 feet;
         (5) N 08°56'24" W 95.20 feet;
         (6) N 21°14'24" W 104.05 feet;
         (7) N 37°38'34" W 230.90 feet;
      thence N 61°25'25" E 43.22 feet; thence N 76°59'51" E 154.02 feet; thence S 17°45'04" E 89.99
      feet; to a point on a non‐tangent 721.98‐foot radius curve to the left; thence along the arc of
      said curve 274.73 feet (chord bears S 40°44'53" E, with a chord length of 273.07 feet);thence S
      57°12'58" E 101.74 feet to a point on a 319.91‐foot radius curve to the right; thence along the
      arc of said curve 209.91 feet (chord bears S 38°25'08" E with a chord length of 206.17 feet);
      thence S 22°44'21" E 49.02 feet; thence S 90°00'00" W 127.13 feet; thence S 00°00'00" E 212.22
      feet; thence S 90°00'00" W 264.06 feet; to the point of beginning. Containing 4.83 acres, or
      about 210,522 square feet.
   B. FRIENDS would like to lease the identified property (the “Leased premises”) for the purpose of
      running a nature center to educate and connect visitors with the natural environment (the
      “Nature Center”). This small Nature Center is intended to be an interim use in anticipation of the
      development of a larger nature center.
   C. CITY desires to lease to FRIENDS and FRIENDS desires to lease from the CITY the Leased Premises
      to the terms and conditions of this Lease Agreement.

                                            AGREEMENT

IN CONSIDERATION of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the CITY and FRIENDS agree as follows:

   1. PREMISES. The CITY hereby leases to FRIENDS and FRIENDS herby leases from the CITY the
      Leased Premises that is further described and illustrated in Exhibit “A”

   2. TERM. The term of this Lease shall be for a period of five (5) years commencing June 22, 2020
      and continuing in full effect until June 21, 2025 or upon termination as provided herein. the
     CITY shall have the option to renew this Lease Agreement on the terms and conditions provided
     herein for one (1) additional period of five (5) years by notifying FRIENDS in writing of CITY’s
     intention to renew at least ninety (90) but not more than one hundred twenty (120) days before
     the expiration of the then current term of this Lease Agreement. FRIENDS must accept, in
     writing, the renewal after the City’s notification in order for the Lease to renew.

3. RENT. FRIENDS shall pay CITY $1.00 per year as rent for the Leased Premises, payable in
   advance for each term of this Lease. In addition, FRIENDS shall maintain the Leased Premises
   during the term of the Lease in a clean and orderly manner, including watering, weeding,
   mowing, and removal of litter, junk, garbage, trash and any debris that may appear on the
   Leased Premises.

4.    PURPOSE. The Premises are to be used as specified in the Management Agreement and for no
     other purpose without written consent of the CITY. FRIENDS shall not use the Premises in a
     manner that conflicts with any law, statute, ordinance, or governmental rule or regulation now
     in force or that may hereafter be enacted or promulgated nor shall FRIENDS cause, maintain, or
     permit any nuisance in or about the Premises. FRIENDS shall not commit or suffer to be
     committed any waste in or about the Premises except as provided in the Management
     Agreement.

5. IMPROVEMENTS. FRIENDS may place or install on the Leased Premises the following structures:
   a. a visitor education center; b. a shade or picnic pavilion; and c. a container structure for bicycle
   share/rental. FRIENDS understand and agree that any structure contemplated to be installed on
   the Lease Premises will require formal review and approval by the CITY and may require one or
   more applications to be submitted for approval by City building officials and the Riverton City
   Planning Commission. FRIENDS agree to cooperate with the CITY in the filing of said applications
   and will furnish any information required for review by the CITY to determine compliance with
   applicable laws or ordinances. The CITY, as an inducement to development of a Nature Center
   by FRIENDS, will pay the sum of Twenty‐Eight Thousand Seven Hundred Fifty Dollars
   ($28,750.00) toward the cost of architectural designs for said structures to be constructed at the
   Nature Center. CITY and FRIENDS may cooperate in future years to obtain additional funding for
   the development and/or operation of the Nature Center, but said cooperation and supply of
   funding, if any, shall be determined by separate agreements between FRIENDS and the CITY and
   shall not be included as consideration under this Lease. FRIENDS shall install a fence around the
   perimeter of the Leased Premises that complies with all applicable laws and ordinances.
   FRIENDS may also install such fixtures and equipment as it deems necessary for the conduct of
   the Nature Center that are also compliant with applicable laws, ordinances or regulations.
   Except as provided above, FRIENDS agree not to disturb the surface or construct any structures,
   roads, trails, fences, or improvements of any kind upon the Leased Premises without the prior
   express written consent of CITY. Upon vacating the Leased Premises, FRIENDS will leave the
   Leased Premises in as good a condition as when FRIENDS took possession of it.

6. OWNERSHIP OF IMPROVEMENTS. The ownership of the structures that FRIENDS may install on
   the Leased Premises pursuant to Section 5, shall remain with FRIENDS at the expiration or
   termination of this Lease. Friends agrees to remove such structures and repair any damage to
   the Leased Premises resulting from such removal upon the expiration or termination of this
    Lease. The ownership of any other improvements and repairs which FRIENDS shall make or
    install in or on the Leased Premises shall remain with the Leased Premises at the expiration of
    this Lease. Should City request Friends to remove any such improvements made by FRIENDS,
    FRIENDS shall do so prior to the termination of this Lease.

7. MAINTENANCE AND UTILITIES. FRIENDS shall pay all utilities costs associated with the Leased
   Premises. FRIENDS shall, at its sole expense, keep the Premises in reasonably good order,
   condition, and repair, including but not limited to all structural and nonstructural components;
   plumbing, heating, and electrical and lighting facilities and equipment within the Premises. If
   FRIENDS does not maintain the Premises and make the repairs and replacements required
   herein promptly and adequately (in the CITY's reasonable judgment), the CITY may, after
   providing FRIENDS twenty (20) days written notice (unless exigent circumstances exist and make
   a shorter notice period necessary), make such repairs and replacements and FRIENDS shall,
   upon demand, pay the CITY for its reasonable costs for such repairs and replacements. City will
   control the water to the wetland areas; however, the CITY and FRIENDS are to coordinate on
   what is needed. Nothing in this paragraph precludes, restricts or limits the CITY from providing
   budgetary funding to FRIENDS.

8. PARKING. During the term of this Lease, FRIENDS’ staff, volunteers, and guests may use the Dr.
   O. Roi Hardy Park parking lot, which is adjacent to the Leased Premises.

9. RE‐ENTRY. The CITY reserves the right, at any time, to enter the Premises to inspect the same.
   Said access shall be afforded at reasonable times. The CITY reserves the right, at any time, to
   enter the premises to make necessary repairs to city owned utilities. The CITY will notify and
   coordinate access with FRIENDS to make the repairs; however, FRIENDS will provide the CITY a
   key to the perimeter fencing gates in order to provide the CITY access in case of an emergency.
   When any repairs are made, the CITY shall make any landscaping and/or surface restoration, to
   as good as the existing conditions.

10. AS IS CONDITION. FRIENDS accepts the Leased Premises in its present condition and CITY
    makes no warranties as to the present or future condition of the Leased Premises or and portion
    thereof, and it is expressly understood and agreed that CITY is under no obligation at any time
    during the term of this Lease to repair, maintain or improve the Leased Premises.

11. INDEMNIFICATION. FRIENDS shall indemnify, hold harmless, and defend the CITY from and
    against all claims, damages, expenses, liabilities, and judgments on account of injury to persons,
    loss of life, or damage to property occurring in the Premises caused by the negligence or willful
    misconduct of FRIENDS, its agents, officers, or employees. The CITY shall indemnify, hold
    harmless, and defend FRIENDS from and against all claims, damages, expenses, liabilities, and
    judgments on account of injury to persons, loss of life, or damages to property caused by the
    negligence or willful misconduct of the CITY, its agents, officers, or employees. FRIENDS' and
    CITY's obligations with respect to indemnification hereunder shall remain effective,
    notwithstanding the expiration or termination of this Lease Agreement, as to claims accruing
    prior to the expiration or termination of this Lease Agreement.
12. INSURANCE. Riverton City requires the insurance provider to be rated an A‐ or better or a
    financial category of VII (7) or Higher as rated by AM Best Ratings.

    Workers Compensation insurance, sufficient to cover all employees in the employ during the
    term of the contract including any renewal thereof must be maintained. Coverage amount
    should be amount required by Utah Workmen’s Compensation Laws. All subcontractors are
    required to also carry workers compensation insurance as specified above. The CITY will require
    employees from companies who choose to not carry workers compensation insurance, (three or
    less employees) to obtain a Workers Compensation Waiver Form from the State of Utah.

    Commercial General Liability insurance in the amount of $1,000,000.00 per occurrence with an
    aggregate of $2,000,000.00.

    Commercial Auto insurance in the amount of $1,000,000.00 per occurrence. The insurance
    must apply to hired, not owned and owned vehicles.

    Insurance needs to protect CITY and FRIENDS from claims for damages for personal injury,
    including accidental death, and from claims for property damage that may arise from FRIENDS’
    use of the Leased Premises. Insurance must name as the certificate holder; Riverton City, its
    elected officials, appointed officials, officers, employees and volunteers.

    Riverton City will assume no liabilities for FRIENDS including any of FRIENDS representatives,
    employees or properties. FRIENDS agree to, at all times, protect, indemnify, defend and hold
    harmless the CITY and city employees from any and all claims

    Insurance provided is required to be primary and non‐contributory.

    Proof of coverage must be provided at the beginning of the Leased Premises and FRIENDS
    agrees to provide proof of insurance upon demand by the CITY throughout the terms of the
    agreement. The insurance policy must be written to have a 30‐day written cancelation notice.
    If any of the required insurance coverage is cancelled or lapse, the CITY may at the CITY's
    discretion, obtain substitute coverage at reasonable rates and recover the costs of said
    insurance from FRIENDS.

13. LIENS AND ENCUMBRANCES. FRIENDS shall not take any actions or make any representations
    in connection either with the Leased Premises or its activities on the Leased Premises which
    shall have the effect of, or result in the attachment of, any lien or other encumbrance to the
    Lease Premises or otherwise interfere with CITY’s title to the Leased Premises.

14. ASSIGNMENT FRIENDS shall not assign this Lease or sublet the Leased Premises or any part
    thereof.

15. TERMINATION. This Lease Agreement may be terminated by either party upon giving six (6)
    months prior written notice to the other party. In the event FRIENDS provides a termination
    notice, any funding provided to FRIENDS by the CITY within one year prior to receipt of said
    notice shall be returned to the CITY.

16. INTERPRETATION. This Lease Agreement shall be construed according to and be governed by
    the laws of the State of Utah.

17. NON‐FUNDING. It is understood and agreed by the CITY and FRIENDS that this Lease
    Agreement shall not bind future City Councils and Mayors. This Lease Agreement shall be
    subject to the terms and conditions of the Management Agreement which is incorporated
    herein by reference. Any obligation on the CITY to provide funds shall be subject to the
    availability and appropriation of funds by the City Council.

18. DISPUTES. It is the intent of the CITY and FRIENDS to communicate to each other about the
    performance of this Lease Agreement and to try to work together in good faith to resolve any
    disputes that may arise under this Lease Agreement. Therefore, if either party under this Lease
    Agreement intends to take any action based on an alleged breach of this Lease Agreement (or
    any other agreement between the parties), that party shall provide the other party at least
    thirty (30) days advance written notice (unless exigent circumstances exist and make a shorter
    time period necessary) prior to taking the intended action.

19. INTEGRATION. It is understood that there are no oral agreements between the parties affecting
    this Lease Agreement and this Lease Agreement supersedes and cancels any and all previous
    leases; verbal understandings, negotiations, arrangement, agreements, representations, and
    undertakings between the parties relating to the leasing of the Premises.

20. REPRESENTATION REGARDING ETHICAL STANDARDS FOR CITY OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES
    AND FORMER CITY OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES. FRIENDS represents that it has not: (1) provided
    an illegal gift or payoff to a City officer or employee or former City officer or employee, or his or
    her relative or business entity; (2) retained any person to solicit or secure this Agreement upon
    an agreement or understanding for a commission, percentage, or brokerage or contingent fee,
    other than bona fide employees or bona fide commercial selling agencies for the purpose of
    securing business; (3) knowingly breached any of the ethical standards set forth in the City's
    conflict of interest ordinance, Chapter 2.120.070, Riverton City Code;. or (4) knowingly
    influenced, and hereby promises that it will not knowingly influence, a City officer or employee
    or former City officer or employee to breach any of the ethical standards set forth in the City's
    conflict of interest ordinance, Chapter 2.120.070, Riverton City Code.
