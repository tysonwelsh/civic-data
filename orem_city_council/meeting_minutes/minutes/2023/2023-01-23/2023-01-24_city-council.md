PRESENTATION - City Hall Project Update

A representative from Method Studios gave a Virtual Reality Tour of the New City
Center layout. They also had some virtual reality goggles for the Mayor & Council to try on to be
able to walk through the new building with.

He started by showing the outside exterior of the building on the screen and informed the
council members that the presentation was not an exact replica of the landscape but is to give an
idea of what could possibly happen. Mayor Young stated that he likes the clock in the virtual
design of the building. The representative displayed all different angles and answered orientation
and structure questions. General discussion of basement space usage, location of 311 help center,
how leighton construction is helping with the project, materials of the building, city council
chambers occupancy limits, escape route members of the council, ceiling height, location of
training rooms, earthquake standards and budget occurred.

e Conserve the public’s water resources by establishing water conservation standards for
outdoor landscaping.
She stated that this should apply to all new developer/contractor installed residential,
commercial, institutional, and industrial construction. As well as all new landscaping
construction installed by homeowners.
She explained that a lawn should not be installed on slopes greater than 25% or 4:1 grade and
areas less than 8 feet wide as its narrowest point. Small residential lots, which have no back
yards, which the total landscaped area is less than 250 square feet, and which the front yard
dimensions cannot accommodate the minimum 8 feet wide lawn area requirement of the
landscaping requirements, are exempt from the 8 feet minimum width lawn area requirement.
She stated that in Commercial, industrial, institutional, and multi- family, common area
landscapes shall not exceed 20% of the total landscaped area outside of active recreation areas.
