                                 ORDINANCE NO. 2021 - 13

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SOUTH JORDAN,
UTAH, AMENDING CHAPTER 6.04 AND CHAPTER 6.12 OF THE SOUTH JORDAN
CITY MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO ANIMAL RESTRAINTS.

      WHEREAS, the City may pass ordinances necessary to discharge its duties pursuant to
Utah Code §10-8-84; and

        WHEREAS, the City regulates animals to preserve the health and to protect both animals
and citizens of the City; and

       WHEREAS, the South Jordan City Code is unclear as to whether a dog has to be
physically restrained in the front yard of its owners home when the owner is outside with the
dog; and

        WHEREAS, the South Jordan City Code did not clarify that a dog in a vehicle had to be
restrained in a way the dog did not leave the vehicle as there have been instances where dogs
have left vehicle; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds it in the best interest of South Jordan citizens to
amend City Code so that it is clear that a dog may be on the owner’s property without physical
restraint if the dog is with the owner.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
SOUTH JORDAN, UTAH:

       SECTION 1. Amendment. South Jordan City Municipal Code sections 6.04.010 and
6.12.040 are amended as follows:

6.04.010: DEFINITIONS:
  ANIMAL UNDER RESTRAINT: Animals shall at all times be under restraint. An animal shall
only be deemed under restraint if controlled or confined by a leash, cage or pen, inside a
stationary vehicle such that the animal is prevented from leaving the vehicle, inside a residence,
confined within a secure fence structure, or secured by a restraint such that the animal cannot get
loose, or if in the case of a dog the dog is within the real property limits of the owner and the
owner is physically present with the dog.


6.12.040: RESTRAINTS:
The owner of or person responsible for a dog shall restrain said dog either by a) a leash or lead
not to exceed six feet (6') in length, b) other physical enclosure, or c) within the real property
limits of the owner if the owner is physically present with the dog leash, rope, impenetrable wall,
fence or other barrier, or by physical control, such that the animal is prevented from leaving the
owner's or responsible person's property or supervision. Violation of this section is a class B
misdemeanor, and the owner of an animal not restrained as provided in this section shall be
strictly liable for any damage caused by such animal.
