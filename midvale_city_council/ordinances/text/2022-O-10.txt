       AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PORTION OF CENTER STREET AT
            APPROXIMATELY IOO W CENTER, MIDVALE, UTAH.

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Annotated Section l0-g-g and Chapter 12.16
of the Midvale City Municipal code, the city has authority ro vacate and dispoie of city-
owned rights-of-way; and

           WHEREAS, a request has been made to vacate a portion of the Center Street right-
otway at approximately 100 West described in Exhibit A; and

        WHEREAS, each City Department has reviewed the request to determine impact
to the City and made a recommendation to approve the request to vacate; and

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission of Midvale City, Utah held a public hearing
on April 27,2022, which meeting was preceded by notice ofpublication in the Salt Lake
Tribune, to review the request to vacate, and, after considering all the information received,
made a recommendation to approve the vacation request to the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council of Midvale City, Utah held a public hearing on May
17,2022, which meeting was preceded by notice of publication in the Salt Lake Tribune;
and

        WHEREAS, after taking into consideration citizen testimony and the
recommendations from City Staff and the Planning Commission, the City Council finds
that the vacation of that portion of Center Street will allow for better use of the adjacent
property and the public interest will not be materially injured by the proposed vacations.

     NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
MIDVALE CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

           Section l.    The foregoing recitals are true and correct;

           Section 2.    The portion of Center Street described on the attached Exhibit A is
hereby vacated;

       Section 3.    Ownership of the rights-of-way being vacated shall revert to the
adjacent property owner(s) on the north side ofthe Center Street right-of-way;

       Section 4.   The Midvale City Council does hereby authorize the Mayor to sign
any documents needed to complete this vacation; and

           Section 5.    This ordinance shall be effective upon a publication of a summary
thereof.
