                                Small Business Saturday 2021

WHEREAS, The importance of small businesses should not be taken for granted as they make
essential contributions to our local economy and community: and

WHEREAS, According to the United States Small Business Administration, there are 31.7
million small businesses in the United States, they represent 99.7% of all firms with paid
employees and they are responsible for 65.1% of net new jobs created from 2000 to 2019; and

WHEREAS, Statistically, 96% of consumers who shopped on Small Business Saturday agree that
shopping at small, independently owned businesses supports their commitment to making
purchases that have a positive social, economic, and environmental impact; and

WHEREAS, Riverton’s economy is supported by a vast number of small businesses that are key
in supporting the economic viability of our great city; and

WHEREAS, In support of local small businesses, Riverton City has spent the last year promoting
its “Local First” campaign to incentivize local spending and to help businesses rebound from
the lingering impacts of COVID-19; and

NOW, THEREFORE, I Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton, Utah, do hereby proclaim November 27,
2021, as Small Business Saturday in Riverton and ask the residents of our community do their
part to support small businesses and merchants located in Riverton on Small Business Saturday
and throughout the year.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 16th day of November 2021.
