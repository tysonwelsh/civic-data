OPENING:
     Mayor Randall welcomed all in attendance. The invocation was led by Police Chief
     Kyle Whitehead and The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Councilmember Smethurst.

      Mayor Randall noted the only item on the agenda is to interview applicants for the
      vacant City Council seat. The term will expire in January, 2022. In preparation for
      filling the vacancy, the City has followed the process as outlined in State code 20A-1-
      510. The City Recorder has prepared the applicant master list in accordance with the
      Lt. Governor’s random master ballot list. Each applicant will be given three (3)
      minutes to make a presentation to the Council.

Kelly Parker
       Mr. Parker explained he came to St. George many years ago. He attended Dixie
       College and served on the Executive Council. Years ago, he worked as an
       administrator at the Dixie Eye Center and has since served in the Rotary Club and
       Exchange Club. He noted the Rotary Club was instrumental in starting the St.
       George Marathon. He also noted that he has served as a volunteer for many years
       and has worked with Parks and Recreation in supporting their projects. He considers
       St. George his home and looks forward to the opportunity to serve this community.

      Mayor Randall commented that the Council has received all of the applications and
      statements as to why applicants want to serve on the Council. The Council has
      reviewed each of the submissions thoroughly.

M. Rick Erickson
      Mr. Erickson commented the Council is familiar with the details of his resume. The
      work he has done in the community is a matter of record. He would like to serve on
      the Council to be able to work with a Council of diverse backgrounds. Although the
      Council has different opinions but reach a common consensus for the overall good of
      the community. He would like to preserve the legacy, heritage, and traditions of
      those that have come before us as well as the beauty of the area. He believes he is
      the right person because it is not about how commissions or boards one sits on, but
      about the integrity of character.

Gregory Brooks
