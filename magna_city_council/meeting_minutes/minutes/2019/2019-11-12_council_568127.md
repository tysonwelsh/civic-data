Reports from Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department

                Chief Jason Ackerman reported on an incident at the Matheson Junior High
School this morning, where some parents reported a male entering the school wearing a raid type
jacket with a firearm. The school was put on lockdown and multiple agencies responded.
Ultimately, the male was a cafeteria worker wearing a vape vest, so everything was okay.

He also reviewed the quarterly report, which has a few additions in it. Juvenile statistics and
statistics for the Violent Crime and Sex Crime Units are in there. This year, in July, August, and
September the Special Victims Unit handled 42 cases related to adult sexual assaults, and
physical or sexual child abuse, specifically in Magna. In that same time last year, it handled 294
cases for all of the UPD, and this year, it handled 343 cases in two cities. Caseloads in those
units are really high. Some of the reason for that is increased reporting. The reports are a living
document, so if the Council sees something that needs changing, he asked that it let him know.

               Council Member Hull asked if the tax increase would allow the UPD to hire more
detectives to help with that.

              Chief Ackerman stated he has not heard a proposal to add detectives to those
units. The Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area’s (SLVLESA) tax increase is to fund
the budget that is already approved for this year. Adding detectives would have to occur in the
July 2020 budget.

               Council Member Prokopis stated the current tax increase will pay for one new
officer for Magna, but not a new detective.

Planning and Zoning

              Paul Ashton reviewed the following agenda item:

Application #30930 – Bryon Prince representing Ivory Development LLC and property
owners Ivory Land Corporation and Kennecott Utah Copper LLC requesting modifications to
the previously approved preliminary plat for the Gabler Grove Subdivision, pursuant to the Master
Development Agreement for property located at 7824 West 2820 South, 7792 West 2820 South,
7762 West 2820 South, 7758 West 2820 South, 7744 West 2820 South, 7746 West 2820 South,
2665 S Oshea Lane, 7834 West 2820 South, 7774 West 2820 South , 2851 South 8000 West,
7701 West U0201 Highway.


The Council approved the preliminary plat a few years ago when Fieldstone Homes was the
developer. The modifications to the preliminary plat comply with the terms of the master
development agreement as well as the zoning ordinances. Earlier this year, the Council approved
rezoning the northern nine acres next to the Ritter Canal, and this preliminary plat was designed
around that. The bottom line is it is less dense than the original design by Fieldstone Homes –
more of a single-family detached product. However, the master development agreement was not
clear whether the trail along the Ritter Canal could be counted as open space. The preliminary
plat was reviewed by the County and the Magna Planning Commission reviewed, which approved
it subject to a few conditions, and those are in the packet. He recommended the Council follow
the planning commission’s recommendation.

              Council Member Hull asked how many homes there were going to be now.


                                               8
DATE           TUESDAY                             NOVEMBER                             12, 2019

                Mr. Byron Prince, representative, Ivory Development LLC, stated 462 houses will
be built, although the development allows up to 776 units total. Fieldstone Homes purchased the
property, but never closed on it, and then sold it in pieces to separate developers. Ivory
Development LLC purchased the western portion and is proposing to develop about 50 percent
single family homes and 50 percent townhomes. The development agreement requires 40
percent open space, but all of the private yards, driveways, common area, and open space next
to the Ritter Canal all account for that. Ivory Homes presented its plan to the staff and County
and it complied with that 40 percent open space requirement. Over the course of this year, Ivory
Homes will purchase the additional nine acres next to the Ritter Canal from Kennecott, as well as
the property on 2820, known as the Belliston. Those properties were rezoned independently, but
the Magna Planning Commission placed a condition on those separate parcels that they be
developed in accordance with the terms of the development agreement too.

             Council Member Pierce asked if this development was the one the citizen
complained about earlier.

               Mr. Ashton stated yes.

               Council Member Pierce asked what the developer had to say about those
concerns.

                Mr. Prince stated none of the land uses contemplated in the development
agreement have changed. All of the homes backing that area are single family detached homes.
The townhomes will be located in the northern and eastern corner of the subdivision. With regard
to an easement, there is a 20-foot flood control easement on the south side of the Ritter Canal.
That existing easement runs probably 50 feet from that western subdivision to the east and will
be extended along the entire length of the Ritter Canal. In addition to that, a 10-foot wide
pedestrian trail will be installed as part of the amenity package.

               Aubree Baer stated she was talking about a 6-foot line between the residents’
properties and the Ivory Homes property, which residents were using as their own.

                Mr. Prince stated Ivory Homes designed off of the fence adjacent to the property
owners. However, there is another fence put up by farmers that will be coming down. The County
Surveyor verified the boundaries and that fence is in conflict with the boundaries. The Surveyor
will review the plat and confirm those boundaries in relation to the adjacent deeds.

               Council Member Hull asked who would be taking care of the upkeep on the Ritter
Canal trail.

            Mr. Prince stated the pedestrian trail will be owned and maintained by the
Homeowner’s Association.

              Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to approve
Application #30930, with the conditions set by the Magna Planning Commission. The motion
passed unanimously.
