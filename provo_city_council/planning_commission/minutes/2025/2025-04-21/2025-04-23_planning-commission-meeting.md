OVERVIEW
The applicant is requesting permission from the Planning Commission to rezone the area from
R1.8 to R1.6 to be able to create 19 additional building lots at basically the same elevation as
Canyon Road. Staff believe this is a reasonable option for the development of this land. As the
property exists now, for any additional lots to be created on the hilltop, utilities would have to
extend from the east. The properties to the east are platted lots with homes that make new
utility easements for additional homes difficult. By reducing the elevation of the hillside and
creating these new lots, utilities can be extended from the parcels to the west, and across
Canyon Road, which is the preferrable option of the Public Works Department.

The fire department has noted that emergency vehicle access up the existing driveway, which is
steep and narrow, is problematic and that grading the hillside would allow better access for their
equipment.

Provo needs more single-family lots. Staff believe that these new lots in this neighborhood
would be a benefit to the area. The proposed subdivision would have direct access onto
Canyon Road, a city collector road so new trips generated by this subdivision would not directly
impact an existing neighborhood.

The zones surrounding the property are R2PD zone to the north, Campus Residential (CR) to
the south, R1.6 to the west and R1.8 to the east. The additional single-family lots would add to
the neighborhood feel that is a desire of the area.

There are additional conditions that Staff would recommend as required before permits are
issued.

These include:
   •   Address all CRC comments.
   •   Obtain a geotechnical report.
   •   Record the final plat map.
   •   Supply required bonding.
   •   Revegetate the area as required.

the Airport Industrial (AI) Zone for the property at 3185 West 550 South. This rezone

flex/warehouse space, tying into the property to the south and east.

divided into five leasable/sellable spaces, with associated parking and landscaping

OVERVIEW
Joel Pilling is requesting concept plan approval for new flex/warehouse space, subject
to approval of a zone map amendment from the Agricultural (A1.5) Zone to the Airport
Industrial (AI) Zone for the property at 3185 West 550 South. This proposal would link a
new warehouse (phase two) to the existing warehouses (phase one) at 652 S Lakeview
Parkway.

The concept plan for the property shows one new 31,753 square-foot building, divided
into five leasable/sellable spaces, with associated parking and landscaping around the
building. Access would be shared with the existing flex spaces (phase one) to the east,
developed by Mr. Pilling. New parking for this building would be built on the south, east,
and north edges of the proposed building, totaling 34 new stalls. While the new building
would require a total of 53 stalls based on the use and square footage, the entire site
(both phases) shares parking and is currently overparked by 59 spaces (requirement of
179 with 238 provided), so they have the 19 to spare that would be required for this new
building.

The architecture, materials, and colors will be identical to the existing buildings at 652 S
Lakeview Parkway and meet all relevant design standards. There are no transition
standards to apply as there are no residential zones adjacent to the subject property,
and setbacks have been met with the provided concept layout.

Additional details will be reviewed in future Project Plan and Final Subdivision
applications that staff will review and approve administratively, subject to the zone map
amendment to the AI Zone being approved by the City Council.

FINDINGS OF FACT
   1. The property is in the A1.5 Zone (Provo City Code 14.08).
   2. The proposed zone is AI (Provo City Code 14.17D).
   3. The overall proposed parking for the concept combined with the existing phase is 272
      stalls.
   4. The overall parking required for both phases is 213 stalls (Provo City Code 14.37.060).
   5. The new phase will require 13 additional trees (Provo City Code 15.20.080).

CONCLUSIONS
Staff have worked with the applicant on the initial phase of this development and are
happy with the results. This new extension of flex space into the subject property will
help this area to grow in ways that the city and General Plan anticipated. Staff
recommend that the concept plan is approved.

                              MEMORANDUM

TO:           Provo City Planning Commission
FROM:         Bill Peperone, Development Services
SUBJECT:      Item 4 – Development Services Ordinance Text Amendment (PLOTA20250182)
DATE:         April 14, 2025



Last month, the Planning Commission considered an Ordinance Text Amendment to allow data
centers and power generation in the Airport Industrial Zone. Since that time, staff have
continued to discuss and analyze data centers within the city. During this analysis, it was
realized that data centers are currently allowed in 17 zones. Those zones comprise 2,896
acres. Additionally, many of the zones have better access to infrastructure than the Airport
Industrial Zone.

Based on these factors, it was the consensus of Power, Public Works, and Development Services
not to support a code amendment to allow data centers in the AI Zone when this request went
before the Municipal Council. The Council denied the request to amend the AI zone and
directed staff to bring back to the Council a policy for evaluating data center requests and
possible ordinance text amendments. This agenda item is intended to initiate pending
legislation to allow staff time to fulfill the Council's request.

A Planning Commission motion to continue indefinitely would be appropriate. It is the
intention of staff to bring forward suggested policy and text amendments within 90 days.

Rachel Breen | April 17, 2025
Neighborhood District 1 Meeting Feedback

Planning Commission, Planning Secretary, and City Planners,
Neighborhood District 1 held their meeting last night where they discussed two planning items:
   • Rezone at 2000 N Canyon Rd in the Pleasant View Neighborhood
   • Rezone and Ordinance Text Amendment at 3433 N 100 E in the Riverside Neighborhood



PLRZ20250097 - Proposed Zone change from R1.8 to R1.6 to subdivide 3.8 acres into 6,000 sq ft
lots at 2000 N Canyon Road in the Pleasant View Neighborhood

City Planner Nancy Robison presented the proposed zone change from R1.8 to R1.6 for a 3.8-acre
property at 2000 N Canyon Road. The change would allow for 6,000 sq ft lots instead of 8,000 sq ft
lots. Residents expressed concerns about hillside stability and retaining walls. Nancy clarified that
detailed engineering studies would be required before any development could proceed. Residents
were frustrated that they could only see a flat plat map, not a drawing of the elevations before and
after taking away the hillside and how that affects neighboring properties.

The item will go to the Planning Commission on April 23rd and City Council on May 20th. Residents
were encouraged to attend those meetings or email comments to the provided addresses.



PLRZ20250082 & PLOTA20250083 - Proposed Zone change from PO to LDR and SSC to develop
a 2.35-acre mixed-use development with six townhomes, a retail center, and an indoor self-
storage facility at 3433 N 100 E in the Riverside Neighborhood

City Planner Mary Barnes presented the proposed zone change and ordinance text amendment for
a 2.35-acre mixed-use development at 3433 N 100 E. The project would include retail units, an
indoor storage facility, and six townhomes. The developer, Douglas Nielsen, was present to answer
questions.
