   1. McDonalds at River Crossing Planned Development Amendment – PUBLIC HEARING: Darrin
      Perkes, representing Dominion Engineering is requesting approval for an amendment to the River
      Crossing Planned Development (PD). The application is a request to develop a 5,230 square foot restaurant
      on 0.86 acres. This project is located on the southeast corner of River Road and George Washington Pkwy.
      Case No. 2023-PDA-011 (Staff – Carol Winner)

       Carol Winner presented the following:

       Carol Winner – This is right on George Washington Parkway. The General Plan is commercial, it is a PD-
       C zone that used to be called Boulder Creek Commons. Drive Thru restaurants are allowed but they must
       follow the standards. There was a conceptual site plan that was approved last year or the year before. The
       entrance will be on the east side. The drive thru splits into 2, then you may leave the drive thru if finished
       or move on to the second window if the order is large or you have an issue with your order. They are
       meeting the parking requirements.

       Chair Kemp – Do they have cross access, cross parking here?

       Carol Winner – Yes, I believe that they do have that in here. When they got rid of the number of drive
       thrus on the use list they added the standards for drive thrus, Carol went through the required standards
       that are listed in the packet. They provided a photometric plan that doesn’t meet our code currently but
       those will be fixed through the site plan process. Carol showed elevations of the building. In review of
       their packet staff feels at this point they are meeting the requirements and recommends approval.

   2. Desert Color Substation Preliminary Plat – Craig Coats, representing Alliance Consulting is requesting
      the approval of a one (1) lot with three parcels at the extreme south end of Desert Color in Sage Haven to
      serve as a future site for a power substation. The property is approximately 4.63 acres and is zoned PD-R
      (Planned Development Residential). Case No. 2023-PP-025 (Staff – Wes Jenkins)

      Wes Jenkins presented the following:

      Wes Jenkins – They are also creating a private drive, that is not in the description of the item. You will
      have the 3 parcels with the one lot and a private drive the HOA will own it. The 3 parcels will be future
      civic spaces.

      Chair Kemp – Why is the private drive gravel?

      Wes Jenkins – Because that is how Dixie Power wants it for their substation.

      Commissioner Chapman – What about that open space, will the public have access to that?

      Craig Coats – It will be part of the HOA, anyone can walk around it but there is a 15 ft elevation change,
      that is why we are calling it open space. The right side of the substation is at the toe of the hill. They are
      going to build the block wall, establish where it is because it takes about 2 years to build a substation and
      then homeowners will know that is what is here before they buy homes here.


       MOTION: Commissioner Rogers made a motion to recommend approval of item number 2 a
       preliminary plat for the Desert Color Substation.
