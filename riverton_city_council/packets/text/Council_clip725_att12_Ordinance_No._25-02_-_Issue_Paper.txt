                                     City Council Meeting Agenda
                                            January 7, 2025


PRESENTER:                Jason Lethbridge

                          Ordinance No. 25-02 - Sycamore Glen South Rezone, Rezone of
                          approximately 1.04-acres located at 11851 South Redwood Road from RR-22
ITEM:
                          (Residential 1/2 acre) to RM-14-SD. Applicant- RD Development Holding,
                          LLC. - Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director

SUMMARY:

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, RD Development Holdings, for
about 1.04-acres located at 11851 South Redwood Road. The property is currently zoned RR-22 which
is a single-family residential zone with minimum lot size of 1/2-acre lots, and the General Plan
designation for this property is Professional Office.
The applicants are requesting a rezone to allow two (2) 5-plex townhomes for a total of ten (10)
townhomes, to match the zoning on the property to the north.
The property to the north is a project known as “Sycamore Glen Townhomes” and was zoned for
townhomes in 2020 by Riverton City Council Ordinance# 20-13. The property was a portion of a larger
rezone which set aside an area for a city storm drain pond, several single-family lots ranging from 6,000
to 8,000-sf and about 1-acre which due to its size and narrow shape was designated for townhomes.
The Sycamore Glen Townhomes Site Plan was approved by the Planning Commission at the May 11,
2023, PC meeting.

FISCAL IMPACT:            N/A

FUNDING SOURCE:           N/A

RECOMMENDED MOTION OR ACTION:

On December 12, 2024, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this application.
 Minutes from that meeting are included below. The Planning Commission voted to recommend
approval, with the following recommended motion:
I move the City Council ADOPT Ordinance 25-02, “Sycamore Glen South Rezone,” by rezoning a 1.04-
acre parcel located at 11851 South Redwood Road to RM-14 SD with the SD adjustments of:
1. Minimum lot size of 1-acre.
2. Up to 10 units total, no more than 5 contiguous units.
3. Other zoning regulations to be consistent with the standards applied to the Sycamore Glen
Townhomes.
