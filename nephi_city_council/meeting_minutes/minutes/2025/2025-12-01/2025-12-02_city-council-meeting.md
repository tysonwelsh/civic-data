PLANNING ITEMS
      A. ORDINANCE 12-02-2025 – CREATION OF THE ID-4 ZONE
             Nephi City has been approached by several data centers with the purpose of creating an
             area where data centers may be located. To be proactive with the parameters and
             conditions on which the city would entertain a use of data centers, staff has prepared a
             draft working-copy ordinance. The new zone will have specific uses, setbacks, height
             restrictions, and landscaping requirements, including dark sky lighting ordinances.

               In previous meetings, the Nephi City Council has examined the pros and cons of allowing
               a data center within the city limits. They have recently considered a Level 2 analysis for
               an area of land that may request annexation for the purpose of data center construction.
               The City Council has elected to be involved in the development of potential data centers
               so that there can be some benefits that will accrue to the city.

               The Planning Commission has reviewed the code addition and is recommending it to the
               City Council. There was a concern by a Planning Commission member that the zone
               does not seem to conform to the City Council’s definition of rural found in the city’s
               General Plan. However, the majority voted in favor of the new zone. In addition, the
               Planning Commission wanted specific language restricting evaporative cooling. It also
               requires electric power plants to provide water rights and supply for their needs.

               The council expressed some concerns over the maximum building height of 100 ft
               allowed in the zone and the impact of the tall buildings on the rural feel of the area. Fire
               suppression requirements and cooling system requirements as well as water usage were
               also mentioned in the discussion.

               Councilor Travis Worwood moved to approve Ordinance 12-02-2025 – Creation of the
               ID-4 Zone. Councilor JD Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed on the
               following role call vote:
                       Jeramie Callaway: Yes
                       Shari Cowan: Yes
                       JD Parady: Yes
                       Travis Worwood: Yes
                       Skip Worwood: Absent
        B. ORDINANCE 12-02-2025-A – MODIFYING THE ID-1 ZONE TO INCLUDE MODULAR DATA
           CENTERS
              Nephi City has been approached recently by a developer wishing to bring “modular”
              data centers into Nephi. A modular data center is a facility or group of facilities
              designed for the housing and operation of computer servers, networking equipment,
              data storage systems, and related infrastructure, where the primary structures are
              prefabricated or modular units capable of being transported and assembled on
              site. There is generally an industry limitation on the size of these modular units,
              approximately 20 megawatts (MW) of total electrical load capacity, and generally there
              is associated accessory equipment such as power generation equipment, battery
              storage, uninterruptible power supply (UPS) systems, backup generators, transformers,
              cooling and ventilation systems, and security facilities that may be remotely
              manned. Large-scale, mega “permanent” data centers would not be included.

                The use of the modular data centers would be allowed in the ID-1 zone. The Planning
                Commission has reviewed the addition of this use to the ID-1 zone and is recommending
                this addition to the City Council. The same concern for water that was expressed in
                relation to the ID-4 was reiterated. Within the definition of the modular data center, a
                restriction on water usage can be found.

                The council considered the potential impact the modular centers would have on the
                electrical load factor and the need for utility availability.

                Councilor JD Parady moved to approve Ordinance 12-02-2025-A – Modifying the ID-1
                Zone to include Modular Data Centers. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The
                motion passed on the following role call vote:
                        Jeramie Callaway: Yes
                        Shari Cowan: Yes
                        JD Parady: Yes
                        Travis Worwood: Yes
                        Skip Worwood: Absent

      Kevin Garlick, formerly with UMPA has offered his knowledge as a consultant while the city is
      negotiating with various data center developers. He has agreed to provide technical knowledge
      regarding the proposed agreements, compliance with UMPA policies, fee proposals, and meeting
      with developers. The fee for his services will be added to the fees charged to the developers as

AMERICA 250 AGREMENTS
      The Council passed a resolution in June to participate in the America 250 program.
      Representatives from the state have prepared some logos for the city to use in their celebration
      efforts for the America 250 event next year. To use these logos and receive the $1,500 in
      program funding, the city will need to sign the agreements for licensing for the logos and a
      memorandum of understanding setting the terms for the use of the program funding. The
      program funding needs to be used with the celebration ideas generated from the local America
      250 committee. Ruth Bonzo will be the contact person for this committee. Councilor Shari
      Cowan and Mayor Seely also participate on the committee.
      Councilor JD Parady moved to approve the mayor to sign the America 250 licensing agreement
      and Memorandum of Understanding. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The
      motion passed unanimously.
