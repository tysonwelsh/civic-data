                                         PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant Michael Margetts, on behalf of Planetree Housing is requesting a Plat Amendment to the existing
Colony Farms PUD Plat in order to divide Lot 55 into 4 separate parcels. The existing lot has 4 townhouse units in
the process of being built on it as a part of the Colony Farms development. The owner would like to divide these
into separate lots to sell them individually.



                           SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located at numbers 2612, 2614, 2616 and 2618 South Fernhill Lane in the Colony Farms
subdivision. The property is zoned R-M and the development has been approved and recorded in 2018.
                                                                       File #: 31181




                                 GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The property is located along the 7200 West Corridor and specific General Plan Projects include improvements
to the corridor to increase capacity as well as Gateway Beautification elements at this location. Adaptable and
flexible changes are anticipated along this corridor. This specific project will have no additional impact or
changes to those uses found in the general plan as the density and uses remain the same, the only difference
this change would create is the ownership of the units being built.



                        ISSUES OF CONCERN/PROPOSED MITIGATION
The only known concerns are related to ensuring that the units change in ownership would not impact the
development or the limited common area that is outlined around the units. Due to the unique nature of the
Colony Farms development and that groups of units were intended to be owned and then rented out; each unit
already pays into the HOA which maintains the common areas. The division of the lot/unit would not change
that in any way and so long as the units continue to pay those fees either as a group, or individually there would
be no change to the development or common area associated with the units.

                                     PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
Utah State Code 10-9a-609 allows a land use authority to approve plat amendments so long as:

    (1)     The land use authority may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing an amended
            plat showing the vacation or amendment if the land use authority finds that:
            (a)     there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and
            (b)     no public street or municipal utility easement has been vacated or amended

Staff believes that good cause exists as this allows for residents to own units rather than solely for rental uses
and does not have a negative impact on the surrounding area. The proposed amendment also does not impact a
public street or utility easement as the units are on the same approved footprint as approved with the original
subdivision.

Additionally, according to the building department this type of division was already contemplated with the
approval of the Colony Farms Subdivision as it was assumed that at least some owners of the units may want to
sell the units individually. Fire breaks, separate entrances and fire sprinklers have already been mandated as a
part of the approved plans.

Staff has determined that the only impact of this division would be to create the separate ownership with no
other impacts anticipated.

                                     OVERPRESSURE ZONE

                AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
                REVISING SUBDIVISION RESTRICTIONS FOR CERTAIN AREAS IN
                               THE OVERPRESSURE ZONE

WHEREAS, Sections 19.76.270, 18.20.060, 15.14.010, 15.14.030, 15.14.040, and 15.14.050 of
the Magna Code of Ordinances, without limitation, impose various restrictions on development
within the overpressure zone; and

WHEREAS, Section 18.20.060 has previously restricted all subdivision of parcels within the 0.5
psi overpressure area; and

WHEREAS, the restriction on subdivisions is separate and distinct from the use, health and
safety, and other restrictions in place to protect Magna Metro Township and its residents and
workers from the overpressure zone; and

WHEREAS, subdivision of parcels within the 0.5 psi overpressure area will allow appropriate
development subject to the use, zoning, and development restrictions affecting all
development within the overpressure zone and the 0.5 overpressure area; and

WHEREAS, on July 9, 2020, the Magna Planning Commission reviewed and recommended the
adoption of the amendment contained in this ordinance, which staff and attorney have
reviewed; and

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council does hereby determine that it is in the best
interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Magna to amend the Magna Code
of Ordinances as stated herein.

NOW THEREFORE, be it hereby ordained by the legislative body of the Magna Metro Township:

   1. Amendment to Section 18.20.060 of the Magna Code of Ordinances 2017.

   The third sentence of Section 18.20.060 of the Magna Code of Ordinances 2017, “New
   subdivisions shall not be approved in the 0.5 psi overpressure area.”, is hereby deleted and
   replaced with the following:

              Subdivision approvals in the 0.5 psi overpressure area shall not alter or
              modify any existing restrictions on the lot existing prior to subdivision,
              including without limitation restricted uses, zoning designation, and
              prohibitions identified in Section 19.76.270 of the Magna Code of
              Ordinances 2017, as amended.

    2. This ordinance will take effect 15 days after its passage and upon at least one
       publication in a newspaper publishes in and having general circulation in Magna Metro
       Township of Salt Lake County, and if not so published within 15 days then it will take
       effect immediately upon its first publication.
