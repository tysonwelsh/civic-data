                             EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL
                               NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING


 Pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3-818, notice is hereby given that a Public Hearing will be
 held in the Emigration Canyon Fire Station located at 5025 E. Emigration Canyon Road, Salt
Lake City, UT 84108 during the City Council Meeting on Monday, December 15, 2025, at 7:00
                                          pm MST.
 The purpose of the hearing is to discuss possible adjustments to the Fiscal Year 2026 Budget
beginning July 1, 2025, and ending June 30, 2026, including possible changes to compensation
                                      for elective officers
 At the date and time fixed for the public hearing all who appear will be given an opportunity to
express their views on the topics above. Those who wish to attend and speak at the hearing may
do so. Public comment may also be submitted before the scheduled meeting for the City Council
         to review by sending comments via email to the City Recorder, Diana Baun at
                                      dbaun@msd.utah.gov.
  In accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, the City of Emigration Canyon will
 make reasonable accommodations to participate in the hearing. Requests for assistance can be
           made by calling 385-377-9466 within three working days of the meeting.
                            Dated this 8th day of December, 2025
