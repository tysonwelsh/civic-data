STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
and recommendations.

CITY DEPARTMENTAL ISSUES
 The Coordinators Review Committee (CRC) has reviewed the application and given their approval.

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING DATE
A neighborhood meeting is not required for this item.

NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
Sam Oman, resident of the Maeser Neighborhood, called this an “ideal M-1 development.” He commended the applicants
for putting up masonry walls because they are a good benefit to neighbors. The applicants would gain some space if they
had a one-way driveway and angled parking. The parking is a little tight.

 APPLICANT RESPONSE
 Key points addressed in the applicants’ presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:


                                                        Page 1 of 2
   •   The applicants have a good relationship with the townhome complex to the east. They expressed a willingness to
       work with them on issues of trees and walls. The eastern vinyl fence (separating the applicants’ property with the
       adjacent townhome complex) will be replaced by a masonry wall that the applicants will construct).
   •   The applicants explained they worked with staff to resolve conflicts with parking requirements. Keeping the
       parking area large will be important to accommodate potential construction company owner tenants.
   •   Increasing the number of tree diamonds is a concern to the applicants because it would complicate the parking lot
       for future tenants.
   •   Citing desire to keep cash-flows up, the applicants were resistant to reducing the size of the east and west,
       proposed, flex space units—to open more land up for parking and landscaping on the east of the building.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   • The Planning Commission expressed that the proposed use is a good one for the subject property.
   • Commissioner Gonzalez applauded the applicants for the aesthetically pleasing design of the project, especially
       considering proximity to residential zones.
   • There was concern expressed about the lack of landscaping, especially trees, in the parking lot and on the eastern
       boundary of the project.
   • Support for a one-way driveway was voiced by Commissioner Jensen.
   • Commissioner Jensen moved that the Planning Commission approve the proposed project plan with the following
       condition:
            o That the applicants replace the smaller tree with a larger tree—on the more central portion of the eastern
                boundary, or add one additional smaller tree along the eastern boundary. The motion passed, 6-0.

The Planning Commission also continued Item 3, the related, proposed Knuttgen rezone, at the August 25, 2021 hearing.

STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
and recommendations.

CITY DEPARTMENTAL ISSUES
 The Coordinators Review Committee (CRC) has reviewed the application and given their approval.

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING DATE
A neighborhood meeting was held on August 23, 2021.

NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
Sam Oman, resident of the Maeser Neighborhood, expressed concern with existing parking that is occurring along 500
South, and near the proposed development. He also expressed concern with where trash cans would be located on the
project. He suggested that no reasonable place is provided for the storage of the trash cans within the development. There
was a mistake made by Development Services on noticing for the development—the notice was for six (6) units (four units
are proposed). The subject property is located in a historic neighborhood and RC (Residential Conservation) zoning was
                                                        Page 1 of 3
intended to keep existing development the way it is.

 APPLICANT RESPONSE
 Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
   • The applicant noted struggles with remodeling the existing home. He explained that it is old, and the foundation is
       not sound. The home is “scabbed” together.
   • He expressed an intent to rent the proposed dwelling units.
   • The applicant fielded many questions from the Planning Commission relative to site layout, landscaping, owner
       verses renter occupancy, etc. The applicant demonstrated a sincere willingness to work with the Planning
       Commission to address their concerns.
   • He noted that he had been responsive to the neighborhood’s concerns about making the development attractive for
       larger, long-term families by increasing the number of bedrooms in the units.

 PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
 Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
    • The Planning Commission expressed concern with many issues related to the proposed development, including
        safety (pedestrian and lighting), lack of open space and outdoor living space, plainness of proposed architecture,
        crammed feel of the development.
    • Commissioner Jones expressed concerns with the functionality of the proposed development, including issues
        with trash removal, navigating the proposed driveway, and the large open parking area to the south that could
        invite unwanted visitors.
    • Commissioner Jensen suggested varied color and material treatments between the proposed dwellings to add
        variety.
    • The Planning Commission asked the applicant to get with staff and work on a revised layout plan with a more
        open feel, and perhaps include detached units to increase livability and safety.
    • Commissioner Jones made the following motion, which was seconded by Commissioner Urquiaga: Continue this
        proposal to a date uncertain, so the applicant could consult with staff and address the following issues:
             o Development lighting
             o Variation of exterior building materials
             o Architectural style that blends with neighborhood
             o Add tall vegetation near the eastern elevation of the dwellings to break up long walls
             o Add sidewalks to driveway
             o Move dwellings to north to facilitate addressing parking lot congestion in the southeast corner of the
                 development
             o Porches
        The motion passed, 7-0.
