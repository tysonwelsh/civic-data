A RESOLUTION APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE FY2017 KEARNS METRO
TOWNSHIP BUDGET
RECITALS

A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 ef seq.

B. The Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-S01.

C. Asa part of the budget process, pursuant to UCA §§ 10-6-111 ef al, Kearns was required to
prepare an annual tentative budget for consideration, possible amendment, and tentative
adoption.

D. On March 22, 2017 the Council adopted the tentative budget and set the date for the public
hearing for consideration and possible final adoption of the budget for April 5, 2017 at
7:00pm at the Kearns Library located at 5350 South 4220 West Kearns, Utah.

E. On March 28, 2017 the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District adopted their amended
FY 2017 Budget, making the funding available to Kearns to fund their FY2017 Budget.

F. A public hearing to consider the budget (Exhibit “A” to this resolution) was duly noticed
under Utah Code §10-6-113 and was properly held on April 4, 2017, with final consideration
of the budget deferred to April 19, 2017 at the time of the Council’s regularly scheduled
meeting. Adoption of the FY2017 Budget will allow Kearns to fund the governance work of
the new Metro Township.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves and adopts the FY 2017

Proposed Kearns Budget, Exhibit “A,” and establishes it as the framework for funding Kearns’

governance work.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Kearns, Salt Lake County, Utah this Bila day of April,
2017.
Steven A. Perry, Council Chair
