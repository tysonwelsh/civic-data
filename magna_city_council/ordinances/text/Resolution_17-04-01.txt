A RESOLUTION APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE FY2017 MAGNA METRO
TOWNSHIP BUDGET
RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 ef seq.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. As a part of the budget process, pursuant to UCA §§ 10-6-111 e¢ al, Magna was required to
prepare an annual tentative budget for consideration, possible amendment, and tentative
adoption.

D. On March 21, 2017 the Council adopted the tentative budget and set the date for the public
hearing for consideration and possible final adoption of the budget for April 4, 2017 at
7:00pm at the Webster Center located at 8952 West Magna Main Street Magna, Utah.

E. On March 28, 2017 the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District adopted their amended
FY 2017 Budget, making the funding available to Magna to fund their FY2017 Budget.

F, The noticing for the public hearing and copies of the budget (Exhibit “A” to this resolution)
were made available for public consideration at least seven (7) days prior to the date of the
public hearing to formally adopt the budget pursuant to UCA §§ 10-6-113 ef al.

G. Adoption of the FY2017 Budget will allow Magna to fund the governance work of the new
Metro Township.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves and adopts the FY 2017

Proposed Magna Budget, Exhibit “A,” and establishes it as the framework for funding Magna’s

governance work.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Salt Lake County, Utah this AL. _. day of April,
2017.
