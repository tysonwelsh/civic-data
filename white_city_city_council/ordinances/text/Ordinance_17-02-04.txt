BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the White City Metro Township Council met in
a regular public meeting on January 5, 2017

WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township may impose and collect fines, fees, charges,
levies, and other payments, per State Law; and

WHEREAS, prior to the creation of the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County
imposed and collected these funds as the municipal government of White City; and

WHEREAS, to insure the effective and efficient transition of the revenues created from
these charges, the Council was required to send a letter to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal
Services District (GSLMSD) to direct the District to collect the fines, fees, charges, levies, and
other payments imposed by the White City Metro Township prior to January 1, 2017; and

WHEREAS, this work had to be performed prior to the Council’s official terms of office
commencing; and

WHEREAS, the elected members of the White City Metro Township Council approved
the creation and sending of the letter and authorized the chair to sign the letier; and

WHEREAS, the required documents were delivered to the CEO of the GSLMSD on
December 22, 2016.

RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE BE TT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council does
hereby ratify and approve the letter attached to this ordinance, authorizing the GSLMSD to
collect all fines, fees, charges, levies, and other payments imposed by the Magna Metro
Township.
