                          Herriman City
                 Safety Enforcement Area Minutes

                           Wednesday, December 11, 2024
                            Approved January 22, 2025

The following are the minutes of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. The meeting
was held on Wednesday, December 11, 2024 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council
Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this
meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to
members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Trustees Present: Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, and Steve Shields

Trustees Excused: Teddy Hodges

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Deputy City Recorder Angela Hansen,
Finance Director Kyle Maurer, City Attorney Todd Sheeran, Communications Manager
Jonathan LaFollette, City Planner Michael Maloy, Unified Fire Authority Assistant Chief
Anthony Widdison, Building Official Cathryn Nelson, Public Works Director Justun Edwards,
Community Development Director Blake Thomas, Operations Director Monte Johnson,

Wendy Thomas

    1.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes
         of December 11, 2024
Trustee Ohrn moved to have the minutes of December 11, 2024, be prepared and distributed
to each member of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area Board. The Board will have
ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary, if after ten days
there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process
will be followed until all changes are made and the board is in agreement. Trustee Shields
seconded the motion, and all present voted aye.

2. Public Hearing
   2.1. Public hearing and consideration of a Resolution amending the fiscal year 2025
        budget– Kyle Maurer, Finance Director
Finance Director Kyle Maurer presented the budget amendment for the Herriman City Safety
Enforcement Area (HCSEA). He explained that the HCSEA was a taxing district that uses tax
funds to contract with Herriman City for police services. The amendment requested an
additional $365,670 to increase operating expenses for the police department, mainly for
personnel costs related to hiring two new officers. It also included $165,100 to purchase two
vehicles for those officers. Director Maurer noted this would leave $124,000 of restricted
new growth money available in the HCSEA fund.

Trustee Jared Henderson noted this was the final step to authorize the transfer of funds and
hiring of officers after several months of preparation. Trustee Steve Shields added that this
came out of planning related to the tax increase voted on last year, with funds reserved
specifically for this purpose.

Chair Palmer opened the Public Hearing.

There were no comments offered.

Trustee Henderson moved to close the public hearing. Trustee Shields seconded the motion,
and all voted aye.

Trustee Henderson shared there were many conversations in other meetings, this is the final
step to hiring the officers. Trustee Shields affirmed they have made sure the funds were
reserved for officers, and this is the outcome of all that work,

Trustee Henderson moved to approve Resolution R24-04 approving an amendment to the
2024-2025 fiscal year budget. Trustee Ohrn seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
