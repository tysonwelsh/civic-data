City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1631

On January 7, 2025, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1631 approving the
10th Amendment to the Hidden Canyon Estates-Mercer Mountain Estates
Development agreement. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 9th day of
January 2025. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
