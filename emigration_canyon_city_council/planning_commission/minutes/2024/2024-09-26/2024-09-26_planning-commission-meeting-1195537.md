PUBLIC HEARING(S)
Hearing began at – 8:38 a.m.
OAM2024-001247 – Public Hearing by the Emigration Canyon Planning Commission on the proposal to
repeal and replace Emigration Canyon’s Title 18 Subdivisions. The draft ordinance has been drafted to
comply with SB 174 and other legislative mandates; Replace the existing subdivision ordinance which was
a copy of Salt Lake County’s ordinance; Promote ease of use by the staff and public through the use of clear
and concise language and organization; and Meet the unique needs and preserve the character of Emigration
Canyon. This ordinance is on the agenda for formal recommendation by the planning commission to the
Emigration Canyon Council. Presenters: Jay Springer, Attorney; Brian Tucker, Planning Manager
(Discussion/Motion)
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Brian Tucker and Jay Springer, Attorney
provided an analysis of the Title 18 Amendments.
Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding difference between a concept plan and
preapplication meeting with staff, subdivision improvement plan, conceptually compliant to planning
commission or staff approval, development review committee identified as agencies, DRC 15-day review
and comment period, definitions from 18.06.040 put in Title 19.04 but could be moved ahead of 18.06.010.
Applicant needs to respond and comply to staff and DRC comments within 6 months, the application
expires. Discussed septic and soils, recording the plat, legislative action, Administrative Land Use
Authority refers to as the commission, any variation or exception from the required engineering standards,
wildlife migrating areas, 18.12.050, paragraph b zoning conformity perk test implied, septic field and
buildable area of the lot, foothill and canyon design standards to be possibly renamed to Emigration
Canyon design standards, 18.10.030B, paragraph 6 include existing sewers, existing septic tank, and drain
field. Napkin drawings appropriate for preliminary meeting. Council directed to get rid of FCOZ and
combine with site development standards.
Commissioner Wallace opened the public hearing.
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED
No one from the public was present to speak.
Commissioner Harpst motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Karkut seconded that motion.
