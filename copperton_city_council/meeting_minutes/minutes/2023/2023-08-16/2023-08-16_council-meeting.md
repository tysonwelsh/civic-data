Community Input

Recognize Visiting Officials

               There were no visiting officials.

Citizen Comments

               Clair Alder asked about the status of the stormwater easement vacation.

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, stated he would
prepare an ordinance vacating the easement pursuant to 10-9a-609.5, that allows for the
recording of a new plat. This would remove the easement later. He stated he would get this done
before the meeting on September 20th, 2023. It will require a 10-day notice, so the ordinance will
need to be prepared by September 1st.

              Ed Walden, UFA, reported that it has been a good wildland season with hardly

resulting in 11 cases.

Council Business

Approval of Minutes

               Council Member Stitzer, seconded by Council Member Bailey, moved to approve
the minutes of the Copperton Metro Township Council meetings, held July 19, 2023, and August
1, 2023. The motion passed unanimously.

                                   −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Fiscal Items

                 Mayor Clayton reviewed the attorney bill for $6,525.00 for legal services for the
month of July.

               Council Member Stitzer, seconded by Council Member Bailey, moved to approve
the bill. The motion passed unanimously.

                                   −−−−−−−−−−−−−−

Law Enforcement Discussion

                     Mayor Clayton stated a meeting was scheduled for September 27th so that
he, Nathan Bracken, and Dave Olsen could meet with the mayors of Millcreek City, Holladay City,
and Midvale City. He asked Nathan Bracken what the possibility was of getting on the interim
agenda in September.

                        Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, stated he
still had not heard from the drafting attorney.

                      Mayor Clayton stated he does not yet have the estimates from Unified
Police Department (UPD) and the Sheriff, nor does he anticipate having those estimates until the
beginning of next year. He questioned whether continued monthly work sessions are necessary.
Without new information, the Council seemed to be at a standstill.

                       The Council agreed to hold a final work session on September 5th to
discuss criteria to help with the decision. Mayor Clayton asked everybody to come with ideas.
The Council will schedule additional work sessions as needed.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
HB 374

                         Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, explained
he is waiting for Bob Reese, the drafting attorney with the Office of Legislative Research, and
General Counsel, to convert the conceptual language the metro townships developed into the
Legislature’s form and to fill in the strikethrough and amendment portions of the bill. He hopes
there will be sufficient time before the September interim committee meeting.

Cemetery

                       Council Member Stitzer stated there is a cleanup scheduled for
September 9th as part of the National Day of Service. There will be a lot of volunteers, who will
be painting the fence around the cemetery, pulling weeds, and laying down gravel. The cemetery
board will be arranging to repair the flagpole, which is leaning.

Committee Assignments

    Legislative Research Committee

               Nothing additional to report.

    Bingham Cemetery Board

               Nothing additional to report.

    Copperton Community Council

.              Council Member Stitzer stated Rio Tinto recently reported it has done some
grading on the roads. It has also performed flood and weed mitigation throughout town, including
behind the fire station. Some dead trees still need to be removed. The conveyor belt has been
completely refurbished, which will cut down on noise and dust. Town Days will be on August 25th
and 26th. She has requested additional police patrol since the park lights will be off during the
Movie in the Park event. There are currently vendor spots available for the 26th. There will be a
car show, door prizes, face painting, etc. and the Municipal Services District (MSD) booth will be
handing out magnets. The Halloween decorating contest is coming up. Bingo is happening at
the Lion’s Club.

    Planning Commission

               Nothing to report.

Copperton Metro Township Council Member Reports

    Mayor Clayton

       Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)
       Council of Governments (COG)

               Mayor Clayton stated the MSD met and reviewed expenditures. The Board
approved those. The MSD is a little behind in billings from Parks and Recreation and has asked
the finance director to remind Parks and Recreation that staying on schedule is important for the
budgetary session. Brian Hartsell at the MSD will attend the next meeting and report on financial
issues.

COG’s most recent meeting was postponed.

    Council Member Olsen

       Unified Police Department (UPD)
       Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

                Council Member Olsen reported that UPD is working on an interlocal agreement
that will receive another round of edits. He will forward the revised version once it is available.

SLVLESA is reviewing and approving the contract for its new treasurer and the current clerk. It
is also continuing to discuss tax increases for 2024 that will be necessary to survive through the
year afterward. SLVLESA’s projections show it will be running out of money in 2025 or 2026 if it
does not receive an increase in revenue. There will be a vote tomorrow on whether to pursue a
tax increase.

    Council Member Bailey

       Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
       Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                Council Member Bailey stated UFA is possibly going to go through Truth in
Taxation. It has not raised taxes since 2018 and the current inflation has hit UFA hard. It is
keeping a 15 percent Rainy Day Fund that it has been utilizing, but because the organization has
so much staff, an increase may be necessary.

    Council Member Stitzer

       Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

               Council Member Stitzer reported that Copperton citizens have been complaining
about the dumpster container reservation program, which was paid for by Copperton Metro
Township. They would like to go back to the old system where a dumpster would be delivered on
a specific day. WFWRD is trying to come up with a solution that works for everybody.

    Council Member Severson

       Salt Lake County Animal Services

               Council Member Severson stated Animal Services has asked for a microchip
report from the various municipalities. Some municipalities like the idea of having scanners to
scan stray animals and others see it as a liability more than anything. Animal Services recently
received a grant in the amount of $50,000 to assist in trapping feral cats. The SPAYghetti & No
Balls event, the Last CarniBALL, is scheduled for Friday, August 25th at 6:00 PM. Tickets are
$100 a piece, and the goal is to raise $100,000. Petapalooza will be held Saturday, September
23rd from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. This event will help adoptable pets find permanent homes.
Copperton had one dog and three cats taken in by Animal Services this last quarter.
