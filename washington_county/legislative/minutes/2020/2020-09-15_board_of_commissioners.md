CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2649, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGRICULTURE LEASE AGREEMENT TO RESTORE
AGRICULTURE AT THE CONFLUENCE PARK

Chairman Iverson explained that there are three (3) fields totaling 344 acres located
between La Verkin, Hurricane, and Toquerville in need of historical agricultural
restoration. This would help to reduce the threat of wildfires as well as establish pasture
for grass for rotational grazing or harvesting crops. The County advertised an RFP to lease
the land for agriculture purposes, and in response to the RFP, The Confluence Heritage
Foundation LLC submitted a proposal to restore the Property to its original agriculture use.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2649, A
Resolution Approving an Agriculture Lease Agreement to Restore
Agriculture at the Confluence Park. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2650, A RESOLUTION

AUTHORIZING THE FILING OF CROSS-APPEAL FOR 2020 CENTRALLY

ASSESSED PROPERTIES

Commissioner Almquist stated that there are entities within multiple boundaries of the state
including Washington County that have filed an appeal to the Utah State Tax Commission
contesting their 2020 assessment. Those entities include: CenturyLink, Intermountain
Power Agency, AT&T, and Union Pacific Railroad Company. Washington County will
file a cross appeal to fully participate in the proceedings initiated by the owner with the
purpose of protecting the County’s financial interests and equality of the tax burdens of
taxpayers within its jurisdiction.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-
2650, A Resolution Authorizing the Filing of Cross-Appeal for 2020
Centrally Assessed Properties. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson,
Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION _NO._R-2020-2653, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A FEASIBILITY STUDY REGARDING EMERGENCY SERVICES

Commissioner Cox explained that it would be beneficial to have a feasibility study
conducted to evaluate the potential of consolidating emergency services on the West side
of the County. The study will include the financial feasibility and should consider revenue
resources, operating costs, capital costs, and potential for shared facilities/equipment
particularly within the current Special Service Districts in the West side of the County.

voting aye.

08/18/2020 @ 1830, Lost Hikers — Gardner Peak, Pine Valley - Man Hours — 30

SAR responded to four lost hikers on the Gardner Peak Trail in Pine Valley. The hikers
said they were not prepared to stay the night. SAR hiked the trail and located them 3
miles up. They were led off back to the IC. ‘Later, they admitted they started a fire to
stay warm. They said they put it out, but it flared up and by morning it was burning.
Fire resources and The U.S. Forest Service had to put out the fire. It took several days.

08/18/2020 @ 2230, Over Due Hunters — Enterprise Reservoir Area — Man Hours — 20

SAR, Deputies, and US Forest Personnel responded to two over due hunters in the
Enterprise Reservoir area. Other family members called dispatch and said they received
a call from their parent. The parents advised they were lost and stuck somewhere on the
west side of the county. Deputies, SAR, and Forest Service personnel searched for several
hours without located them. The next day, SAR put up a plane to try and locate them.
The subjects called their family and said they were located by other hunters and had
returned to their camp. The search was called off.
