                              WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING

A Study Meeting of the West Valley City Planning Commission will be held on Wednesday, October 1, 2025, at 3:30 PM,
 in West Valley City Hall, 3600 Constitution Blvd., Suite 220, West Valley City, Utah. Members of the press and public
      are invited to attend in person or view this meeting live on Zoom at https://us06web.zoom.us/j/83896786911.

 Preliminary application information will be available by 6:00 PM on Tuesday, September 30, 2025 at https://www.wvc-
 ut.gov/669/Agendas-Minutes. Staff analyses, as well as any final application materials, will be updated by 12:00 PM on
                                               Monday, October 6, 2025.

    Testimonies for or against the proposed applications can be provided to the Planning Commission during the public
hearing or in writing prior to the hearing. If you wish to submit written comments, be advised that this should be provided
to the Planning Staff member assigned to the application at least seven (7) days prior to the meeting to enable the Planning
                                       Commission to properly consider the comments.
