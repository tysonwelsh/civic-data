                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-15


A RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING COUNCILMEMBER TISH BUROKER TO SERVE
   AS A BOARD TRUSTEE FOR THE SOUTH SALT LAKE VALLEY MOSQUITO
                       ABATEMENT DISTRICT.


       WHEREAS, the South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District (the "District") is
governed by a Board of Trustees, whose members represent the area in which the District
provides mosquito abatement service; and

        WHEREAS, members of the Board of Trustees are appointed to serve in office for a
fixed term of four years; and

       WHEREAS, on January 7, 2020, the Riverton City Council approved Resolution 20-01,
thereby appointing Councilmember Tish Buroker to serve as a board member for the District;
and

       WHEREAS, Councilmember Buroker has faithfully served Riverton City in her capacity
as a Board Trustee during her first full term in office; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Councilmember
Buroker’s experience acquired from years of faithful service on the District’s Board of Trustees
benefits the residents who receive mosquito abatement, and therefore Councilmember Buroker
should be reappointed to the Board of Trustees.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1.     The Riverton City Council hereby reappoints Councilmember Buroker to
                      serve on the South Valley Sewer District’s Board of Trustees for a four-
                      year term commencing January 1, 2024 until January 1, 2028.

       Section 2.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
