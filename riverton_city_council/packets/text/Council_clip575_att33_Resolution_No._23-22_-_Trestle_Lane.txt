                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-22


   A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL HALTING FURTHER
IMPROVEMENTS TO TRESTLE LANE, TO CONNECT THE SAME WITH MEADOW
              WAY, DURING THE 2022-2023 FISCAL YEAR


       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, adopted the Riverton City 2022-2023 Fiscal
Year Budget (“Budget”) by adoption of Resolution No. 22-44, following public hearings to
consider the same on the dates of June 7, 2022 and June 21, 2022 and in compliance with Utah
law; and

        WHEREAS, the Budget, included, among other things, funding to pay for a number of
projects to improve or maintain roadways, utility systems, parks, and properties owned by the
City and operated for the benefit of the public; and

       WHEREAS, among the projects approved under the Budget, a line item bearing account
No. 47-69-766 allocated funding for Trestle Lane, to “connect” the same with “2305 West,” also
known as Meadow Way; and

        WHEREAS, in February of 2023, Riverton City staff publicly issued a Request for
Proposals in compliance with City ordinance, to invite contractor bids for making improvements
to Trestle Lane, as described in the Budget; and

      WHEREAS, during a regularly held meeting of the Riverton City Council on the date of
March 21, 2023, the Riverton City Council received comments from a number of residents
making inquiries, and expressing disapproval of connecting Trestle Lane to Meadow Way; and

        WHEREAS, in light of the public comments received on the date of March 21, 2023, the
Riverton City Council finds and determines that further study is needed to determine whether
Trestle Lane should be connected to Meadow Way; and further engagement with area residents
should take place both during the study process, and following any determinations made
pursuant to study; and


       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:


Section 1.    Riverton City Council hereby requests the Riverton City Manager take all legal
              steps necessary to halt further action necessary to implement any roadway
              improvements to Trestle Lane, which would connect the same to Meadow Way,
              for fiscal year 2022-2023.

Section 2.    The Riverton City Council further directs staff to take all actions necessary to
              reevaluate whether Trestle Lane should remain to be shown on the Riverton City
              Master Transportation Plan as connecting with Meadow Way, by undertaking the
              process to amend the Master Transportation Plan, in compliance with state law.
Section 2.   This Resolution shall take effect immediately.


      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 18th day of April
2023 by the following vote:
