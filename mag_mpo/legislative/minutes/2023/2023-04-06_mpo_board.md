MAG Small Area Population Projections – Shawn Eliot, MAG (24:45) The population forecast is updated alongside the update of the Regional Transportation Plan (RTP). This forecast uses the year 2019 as a base to start. The projections go to 2050 to align with the RTP and inform the RTP, the traffic forecasts, and air quality conformity. The draft population forecast was presented to the MPO TAC last fall for feedback and comments. 7 communities gave valuable feedback that was incorporated into the inputs of the Real Estate Market Model. The forecast showed that the persons per household drop as a county from 3.51 to 2.92. To view the forecast online, go to: mountainland.org/se-forecast-viewer Mayor Lundberg moved to approve the small area population projections as presented. Mayor Packard seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 2023 TransPlan50 Regional Transportation Plan – Shawn Eliot, MAG RTP Financial Plan (32:24) Shawn Eliot reported that the financial planning for the RTP is a collaborative effort between UDOT, the transit districts, and the four Utah MPOs (MAG, WFRC, Dixie MPO, and Cache MPO). An overview of the TransPlan50 revenues, costs and deficits was given. Approve RTP Highway, Transit, and Active Transportation Maps (51:00) The RTP Highway, transit and active transportation maps were presented. MPO TAC met on March 24, 2023 and recommended approval of the lists presented. An overview of the next steps and upcoming dates were provided. See Maps and Lists for more details. Mayor Frost recommended the Meadows Crossing project be added to the TransPlan50 due to the increased growth in American Fork. No action was taken. Mayor Andersen moved to approve the highway, transit, and draft active transportation lists as presented. Commissioner Sakievich seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO Board April 6, 2023 Page 4 Approve Air Quality Conformity Determination (1:10:30) The Clean Air Act allows EPA to create health-based air quality standards. EPA can intervene if standards are violated. To be approved, the RTP must document that it supports/conforms to the through emissions modeling and gain FHWA/FTA concurrence. Mayor Packard moved to approve the draft Conformity Determination Report for the 2023 TransPlan50-Regional transportation Plan, noting that the plan conforms to all federal air quality regulations. Mayor Christensen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Modifications – Bob Allen, MAG (1:18:19) Approve Dye Art Trail; American Fork Modification 2018, American Fork was awarded $637,000 STBG (Federal) funds to complete the last section of the Art Dye trail from the American Fork River to the Murdock Canal Trail. Due to potential conflicts with park visitors, the trail was moved to the other side of the road. The project is ready to bid and needs $631,300 in additional funds. The TIP Manager has already approved the 10% contingency funds.
