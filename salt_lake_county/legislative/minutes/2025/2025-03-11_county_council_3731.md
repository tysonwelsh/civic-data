Mayor’s Office on a successful legislative session.  A lot of bills were considered

and advocacies.

team who worked hard on behalf of the County.

3.2. County Mayor
Mayor Jennifer Wilson made the following announcements:
• The 2025 National Association of Counties (NACo) Legislative Conference was 
productive this year.  The County had great participation from the Council, and she 
enjoyed spending time with Council Members.  She also enjoyed working with the 
NACo team to better understand opportunities for County residents and meeting 
with her delegation, and  she appreciated Council Members for meeting with their 
delegation.   
• The Health Department is preparing for a measles outbreak, as there have been 
recent cases, after having been relatively dormant for decades.  Measles is highly 
transmissive and has an impact on people who get the disease, so it made sense for 
the Health Department to prioritize immunizations.  During the late 50s and early 
60s, the vaccine was not as effective as it is now, so people who were vaccinated 
during that time period, should probably get a booster vaccine.  She commended 
Page 2 of 21

the Health Department for always being one step ahead of the health issues.
• A lot is going on at County facilities for youth.  The My County Rec Pass offers 
free access for youth to recreation facilities.  It is also time to sign up for spring and 
summer programs.  In addition, summer camps will be available at Wheeler Historic 
Farm, and there will be programs at the Clark Planetarium and County libraries.    
• The Arts and Culture Division ’s Westside CultureFest will be held August 15th 
and 16th, at the Mid-Valley Performing Arts Center in Taylorsville.  
• The Clark Planetarium is continuing its Science Utah Lecture Series, which is 
held the second Thursday of every month.  The next lecture will be about 
butterflies, beetles, and bugs, on March 13th.  
• The 2025 Lens of Magna Photography Contest is a community effort that the 
County is involved with.  The contest encourages photographers of all levels to 
capture the beauty of Magna Main Street.  Information about the contest is available 
at lensofmagna.com.  
• Saturday, the County Library Division is hosting its annual Teen Formal Wear 
Swap.  Buying formalwear can be a challenge for people with limited income, so 
this is a great way to get a prom dress or suit for less money.  Recycling and 
reusing is also great to do.  
• On March 15th, the Parks and Recreation Division will be conducting a free 
Youth Soccer Clinic at the Northwest Recreation Center and Try Hockey for Free 
at the Salt Lake County Ice Center.
• On Sundays, Wheeler Historic Farm holds its Winter Sunday Market.

Council Member Stringham  asked if the people from the District 
Attorney’s Office receiving incentive plans were doing things outside their 
normal duties or filling in for someone who was not there anymore.
Mr. Ralph Chamness , Chief Deputy District Attorney, stated the incentive 
requests were for employees doing additional work outside their normal 
duties.  He reviewed what each person was being asked to do in addition to 
their regular duties, all of which were critical functions.  
Council Member Stringham  asked if the incentives were temporary or 
ongoing incentives.
Mr. Chamness stated the incentives were ongoing.  The District Attorney ’s 
Office has scarce resources, and this was the only tool it had to get the work 
done.  It either needed additional employees or a way to incentivize 
employees and recognize the extra work they were doing.

Council Member Winder Newton read the proclamation:
Salt Lake County Council Proclamation Recognizing International Women ’s 
Day March 8, 2025 
Whereas, International Women ’s Day is celebrated globally on March 8th 
each year to honor the achievements and contributions of women throughout 
history; and 
Whereas, women in Salt Lake County have played an invaluable role in 
shaping our community as pioneering leaders, educators, business owners, 
healthcare workers, caregivers, mothers, sisters, friends, and more; and 
Whereas, we honor the legacy of trailblazing women in Utah ’s history 
including Martha Hughes Cannon, the first female state senator in the United 
States; Reva Beck Bosone, Utah ’s first female member of the U .S. House of 
Representatives; Seraph Young, the first woman to legally vote in the United 
States, marking a pivotal moment for women's rights, and Alice Kasai, a 
tireless advocate for the rights of Japanese Americans; as well as countless 
other women, past and present, whose courage and leadership continues to 
inspire future generations; and 
Whereas, we celebrate women of all ages, races, and ethnicities who inspire 
us to be better, encourage growth, provide nurturing support, and contribute 
to the strength and vitality of our community; and 
Whereas, International Women ’s Day 2025 serves as an opportunity to 
recognize, support, and uplift women and foster a community where all 
women can thrive; 
Now, Therefore, Be It Resolved, that the Salt Lake County Council does 
hereby proclaim March 8, 2025, as International Women ’s Day in Salt Lake 
County and encourages all residents to celebrate the achievements of the 
women and girls in their lives. 
Be It Further Resolved, that Salt Lake County recognizes the valuable 
contributions of women and girls to our community and remains committed 
to supporting their continued success and growth.
