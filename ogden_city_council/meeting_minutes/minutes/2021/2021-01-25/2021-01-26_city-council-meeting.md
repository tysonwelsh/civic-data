Public Comments
          David Timmerman thanked the Council for their service. He then stated that at the previous meeting he spoke about the
appointment of a new police chief and what the ramifications would be if a majority of the Council chose not to support the
Mayor's recommended appointee. He stated he would still like an answer to that question.
          Heath Satow addressed comments made by the Mayor at the conclusion of the last Council meeting and noted he fmds it
sad that the Mayor deems actions taken to hold him accountable every four years as 'bullying'; the people of Ogden have recently
asked many questions and the Mayor has spent just four seconds speaking to those questions. The Mayor is being asked to answer
to a community that he was sworn to serve. Mr. Satow indicated he will not be shamed into being quiet and he will be present
every week to ask these questions; when the Mayor refuses to answer those questions, it looks as ifhe is hiding something or just
woefully uninformed about those doing the work beneath them. He then stated that he would like to hear answers from those who
know the answers on the following topics: recycling and homeless encampments. In terms of recycling, the citizens were lied to          )
and much information was hidden. Even the Sustainability Committee did not know what was happening and the Administration's
actions make it clear that Committee was created for appearances only, similarly to the Planning Commission and Diversity
Commission. The Mayor has made one thing clear, if you step out the bounds ofbeing more than a rubber stamp to him, you will
be punished by being kicked off that committee. The recycling issue was hidden from the public until exposed by a newspaper
article and recycling was dumped in the landfill for the better part of a year; someone needs to answer for this negligence since the
Mayor refuses to. Relative to the removal of an encampment at the Lantern House, he would like to know what City
Administration is doing to prevent another similar crisis in the future. Last year, home prices increased a record 9.1 percent and
the rate was even higher in Ogden; homelessness will not go away if it is ignored, as the Administration seems to be doing, and it
is not an option to sweep human beings under the rug using the police force as a maid service. He concluded he knows that being
a Council member is a part time job and that is why it is up to citizens to look into these issues and bring them to the Council.
          Chair Blair asked that someone address Mr. Timmerman's question. Council Executive Director Eller-Smith indicated
she will research that matter and report back.

Mayor Comments
          Mayor Caldwell stated he will also look into Mr. Timmerman's question, though he is disappointed that some chose to
turn the appointment of a police chief into a political issue. He then addressed Mr. Satow and indicated that he will not respond to
his comments as he chooses not to participate in recreational outrage. He stated that Mr. Satow has the right to spend his three
minutes in public comment how he chooses, but he will not engage and become emotionally involved by responding to him.
