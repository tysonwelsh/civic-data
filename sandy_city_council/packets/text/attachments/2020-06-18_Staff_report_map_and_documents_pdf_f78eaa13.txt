DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Sand R Holdings, LLC, is requesting that the Planning Commission determine
that preliminary subdivision review is complete for a two-lot commercial subdivision. The
proposal will split the existing 7.94 acre Sandy Rehabilitation lot into two. One lot will be 6.1
acres and will contain the existing rehabilitation building and its parking. The other will be 1.2
acres and will be vacant for the time being. Both lots will front onto 9000 S. The applicant is not
requesting any exceptions or overlays in association with this application. All provisions of the
base zone will be adhered to through the review and approval process.

BACKGROUND
The proposed subdivision is in the Institutional Care District (IC), The property is bordered on
the north by 9000 S with single family homes in the R-1-7.5(HS) zone across the street. To the
west and south is the Regional Commercial District (RC), including various commercial
businesses that front State Street. To the east is an existing canal with various buildings located
on the other side in the Industrial Development District (ID).

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500 foot radius of the subject parcels as per Sandy
City Land Development Code requirements, to notify of the Planning Commission meeting.

SUBDIVISION ANALYSIS
City staff and departments have reviewed and recommend preliminary subdivision review is
complete, subject to their normal requirements and specifications. The subdivision meets all
zoning requirements. The subdivision is being done to create a lot for a potential future use, which
has not been determined at this time. A shared access easement will be recorded between the two
lots. This will ensure that residents and customers can access both lots from the existing access
point. All buildings on both lots will comply with the setback requirements of the zone. Finally,
no new building construction is being proposed at this time. The public utilities department is
requiring water line improvements to bring the existing building into compliance for water
pressure. Any further future site improvements or modifications will have to be done through a
site plan application and review process.

The applicant will be required to dedicate all property necessary to ensure that 33 feet of property
west of the centerline of the canal is owned by the canal company. This property will be used in
the future for a trail system constructed by Sandy City.

CONCERNS
Staff has two concerns regarding the proposed development. First, there is a fence along the
western part of the canal that may or may not indicate the actual property line between the proposed
subdivision and the canal. The applicant has agreed to verify the actual property line and deed all
land necessary to the canal company to ensure all 33 feet west of the center line along the canal is
owned by the canal company. There is no specific lot size requirement in the IC Zone, provided
there is enough room to comply with all development requirements.

Second, there is a potential concern about any proposed uses that will be allowed on the newly
created lot. Potential uses may be limited depending on the size of the building needed and the
number of parking stalls available to support the use. Furthermore, there are significantly fewer
uses allowed in the Institutional Care District than in a typical commercial zone. The applicant
should show a concept plan showing a use that can feasibly be placed on this new lot meeting all
of the required development standards outlined in the Development Code. Staff has recommended
this as a condition of approval.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine preliminary subdivision review is
complete for the Sandy Rehabilitation two-lot commercial subdivision based on the following
findings and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:

   1. That the various City departments and divisions have preliminarily approved the
      proposed subdivision plat.
   Sandy Rehabilitation Subdivision – SUB-05-20-5853                                       Page - 3 -


       2. That the proposed subdivision will be finalized with City staff, through recording with
          the County Recorder.

   Conditions:

       1. That there be a shared access easement recorded on the plat to serve the two lots.

       2. That the applicant ensure 33 feet west of the canal centerline is properly deeded to and
          owned by the canal company.

       3. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments, redlines and requirements
          throughout the final review process and that all issues be resolved before the subdivision
          can be recorded.

       4. That the applicant provide a concept plan with an acceptable proposed use that meets all
          zoning, setback and parking requirements as part of the final review with staff.
