CUP2021-000394 - Bach Homes is requesting a Conditional Use Permit for a revised site plan expanding
the existing Carrington Square fitness center and pool area. Acreage: 15.02 acres. Location: 6015 South
Thorncrest Way. Zone: R-M/zc. Planner: Travis Hair (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Travis Hair provided
an analysis of the staff report.

Mr. Bracken said similar issue with pool property on the northern part that didn’t require a condition use
that didn’t change site plan or footprint of the building. Commissioner Taylor said looking at the
construction with additional footprint, what does that do to green space percentage. Mr. Hair said amenity
buildings fits in to that and doesn’t significantly cut into the green space. Concern with previous work, but
not on this proposal doesn’t impact. Commissioner Nelson asked if it effects the drivability of the complex.
Mr. Hair said doesn’t impact the parking or drivability at all.

Commissioner Wellman confirmed this is on the council agenda and asked if this is a recommendation. Mr.
Bracken said could recommend to them, the planning commission is the land use authority, but the Council
has interest in this item.

                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)
                                       Hearings began at – 5:51 p.m.

OAM2021-000271 - A proposal to amend Chapter 19.15 of the Kearns Municipal Code which allows for
and regulates Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) to conform to requirements regarding Internal Accessory
Dwelling Units mandated by state legislation. Planner: Travis Hair (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed Land Use Ordinance changes after taking comments
from the public during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning
commission’s rules of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be
limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Travis Hair provided
an analysis of the staff report.

Commissioner Hatch noticed detached ADU online allowed 12000 square feet or greater or 6000 square
feet in PC zone and how many homes in Kearns would fall in the 12000 square foot realm. Mr. Hair said
next to none, most lots are 8000 square feet and changed in the original at 6000. If wants to update could
be on board with the change. Majority lots are R-1-6, compliant with zone, how big of an ADU can fit.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Citizen
Name: Roger Snow
Address: 5977 South Parkwood Drive
Comments: Mr. Snow asked about the lot sizes fit Kearns, more size of the lots.

Mr. Hair said when making a motion amend that section, remove PC zone. Compliant with zoning and be
at least 6000 square feet. Mr. Bracken said delete sentence and change to 6000 square feet and take care
of it. Commissioner Wellman said remove paragraph B of 19.15.070.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend approval of file #OAM2021-000271 - A proposal to amend Chapter 19.15 of the
Kearns Municipal Code which allows for and regulates Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) to conform to
