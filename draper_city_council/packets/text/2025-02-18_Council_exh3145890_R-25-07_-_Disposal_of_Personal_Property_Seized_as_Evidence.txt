                               RESOLUTION NO. 25-07

       A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL DISPOSING OF PROPERTY
 SEIZED AS EVIDENCE IN ACCORDANCE WITH UTAH CODE SECTION 77-11a-402

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Police Department (“Police Department”) seizes
personal property as evidence in criminal investigations; and

      WHEREAS, the Police Department possesses personal property seized as
evidence but is unable to determine the rightful owner; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Section 77-11a-402 outlines the procedure for
disposing of personal property held as evidence once the prosecution of a criminal
case is concluded; and

       WHEREAS, the Police Department has complied with the process specified in
Utah Code Section 77-11a-402, including: (1) the prosecuting attorney has
determined that the seized property no longer required for court proceedings and
has notified the Police Department that the property may be returned to the
rightful owner if that owner is legally permitted to possess it, or it may be disposed
of or destroyed; (2) the Police Department has exercised due diligence in
attempting to notify the rightful owner of the property regarding its return; and (3)
the Police Department is requesting the City Council’s permission to allocate the
property or its proceeds for public interest use within the Police Department and
Draper City.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

      Section 1. Disposition of Property. The Draper City Council hereby authorizes
the Police Department to dispose of personal property no longer needed as
evidence, as listed in Exhibit A attached hereto, by:

      i. applying the property to a public interest use;
      ii. selling the property at public auction and applying the proceeds from the
      sale to a public interest use; or
      iii. destroying the property if it is deemed unfit for public interest use or sale.
       Section 2. Public Interest Use. Pursuant to Utah Code Section 77-11a-
101(20), “public interest use” refers to a use by a government agency as determined
by the Draper City Council. The Draper City Council determines that the seized
property or proceeds from its sale may be utilized by the Police Department or any
other city department, as determined by the City Manager, to further the
operational or administrative needs of Draper City, thereby benefitting the health,
safety and welfare of Draper City residents and businesses.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon
passage by the Draper City Council.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 18TH DAY OF FEBRUARY 2025.
