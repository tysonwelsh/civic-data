A RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE KEARNS METRO
TOWNSHIP COUNCIL DECLARING A NEED FOR THE HOUSING
AUTHORITY OF THE COUNTY OF SALT LAKE (THE “AUTHORITY”) TO
EXERCISE ITS POWERS WITHIN THE BOUNDARIES OF KEARNS.

WHEREAS, the Housing Authority of the County of Salt Lake (the “Authority”) was
created pursuant to the provisions of Title 35A, Chapter 8, Part 4, Utah Code Annotated
(“UCA”) 1953, as amended (the “Act”); and

WHEREAS, the Act authorizes the Authority to prepare, carry out, and operate projects
and provide for the acquisition, construction, reconstruction, rehabilitation, improvement,
extension, alteration or repair of any project within its area of operation; and

WHEREAS, the Act provides that the Authority’s area of operation shall include the
municipality only if a resolution has been adopted by the governing body of the municipality
declaring that there is need for the Authority to exercise its powers within the boundaries of the
municipality; and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township is a municipality pursuant to UCA Title 10,
Chapter 2A, Part 4; and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council is the local legislative body for Kearns
pursuant to UCA §§10-3b-501; and

WHEREAS, it is to the mutual benefit of Kearns and the Authority to enter into an
agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Words used in the foregoing recitals shall have the same meanings when
used in the body of this resolution.

Section 2. Kearns hereby finds, determines and declares that there is a need for the
Authority to exercise its powers within the boundaries of Kearns in order that the Authority may
provide rental assistance; prepare, carry out, and operate projects; provide for the acquisition,
construction, reconstruction, rehabilitation, improvement, extension, alteration or repair of any
project within the boundaries of Kearns; and exercise all or any part or combination of the
powers granted it under the Act.

Section 3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon its adoption.
