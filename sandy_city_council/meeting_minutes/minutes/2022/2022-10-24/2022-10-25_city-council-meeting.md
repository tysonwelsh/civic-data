2.         22-381                 Special Recognition of the Sandy City Explorers

Special Recognition

                                  Mayor Zoltanski acknowledged and thanked the Sandy City Police Law Enforcement 
                                  Explorer Post 9550 for all their volunteer work on behalf of Sandy City. The Explorer 
                                  Program is a very intensive professional law enforcement career orientation program. The 
                                  intent of the program is to educate and involve youth in police operations, to interest them 
                                  in possible law enforcement careers, and to build a mutual understanding between the 
                                  police and the youth in the community. The Mayor presented a video showcasing the 
                                  Explorer Post 9550. The Explorers volunteer approximately 3,700 hours for Sandy City, 
                                  which translates into a savings of over $50,000. Mayor Zoltanski thanked the Explorers 
                                  and presented each one with a gift. The Council also thanked the Explorers for all their 
                                  efforts on behalf of the residents of Sandy City and presented each member with a flag 
                                  that was flown at the Sandy City Healing Fields.

                                  Council moved to Item 3 on the Agenda.

5.         22-380                 Sandy City Fire Department presenting information on the ISO results, 
                                  Strategic Plan, and a fire staffing and compensation update
                   Attachments:   City Council Executive Summary 10-25-22.pdf
                                  ISO-strategic plan.pptx
                                  Strategic Plan 2022.pdf
                                  Public Protection Classification Summary Report.pdf

                                  Deputy Chief Ryan McConaghie presented the ISO/Strategic Plan Overview and 
                                  introduced Brian Larson with Insurance Services Office, Inc (ISO). The ISO provides a 
                                  Public Protection Classification (PPC) which is determined through Fire Suppression 
                                  Rating Schedule (FSRS). Sandy City has recently improved to a Class 2 City 
                                  designation. Deputy Chief McConaghie reviewed the ISO Standards and the Distribution 
                                  of PPC Grades. The goal for the Sandy City Fire Department is to attain a Class 1 City 
                                  designation. The Fire Department is already implementing changes to reach this goal. He 
                                  reviewed the approximate savings to our residents as a result of changing to a Class 2 
                                  city which translates to approximately a 3% to 5% savings per home and commercial 
                                  building on their insurance costs. 

                                  Deputy Chief McConaghie also presented the Strategic Plan and reviewed the strategic 
                                  planning process and timeline. He reviewed the SWOC assessment with the Council: 
                                  strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, challenges. 

                                  Council questions and comments followed. 

                                  Chief Bruce Cline thanked the Fire Department staff and crews for their efforts to bring 
                                  Sandy City up to a Class 2 City designation. Chief Cline spoke about the Fire 
                                  Department staff compensation survey and provided an update on the current staffing 
                                  levels. Currently there are a total of 8 positions open and he spoke about the process to 
                                  fill those positions. He anticipates filling 4 of those positions very soon. He anticipates 
                                  filling the remaining 4 vacancies by May of 2023. Council thanked Chief Cline and his 
                                  department.
