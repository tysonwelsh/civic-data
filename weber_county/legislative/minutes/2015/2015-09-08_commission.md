G. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. SECOND CHANGING THE FEE ORDINANCE TO ALLOW THE CLERK/AUDITOR’S OFFICE TO CHARGE A
       FEE TO RECEIVE PAYMENT FROM PASSPORT APPLICANTS, THEN CREATE CHECKS TO BE SENT WITH
       PASSPORT APPLICATIONS – ORDINANCE 2015-16

         This item was heard last week. Lynn Taylor, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, stated that a check to the U.S.
         Department of State must be mailed with the passport application. People frequently do not bring the
         check and they have to go back and obtain one. Today’s action gives people another option to make that
         payment with a credit card or cash and the county generates the check to the U.S. Department of State. In
         speaking with other counties, this is a first in innovation to be offered in the State.
         Commissioner Bell moved to adopt Ordinance 2015-16 amending the Fee Ordinance relating to the
         Clerk/Auditor’s Office; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    2. CHANGE ORDER #1 ON A CONTRACT WITH GRANITE CONSTRUCTION FOR THE ADDITIONAL 24” RCP
       STORM DRAIN PIPE ON THE 3500 WEST RECONSTRUCTION PROJECT – CONTRACT C2015-341

         Jared Andersen, County Engineer, stated that there was some 24” reinforced concrete pipe that was not in
         good condition as had been anticipated. Commissioner Ebert noticed that the pipe went from 24” to 18”
         and Mr. Andersen believes it is a typographical error and will check on it.
         Commissioner Bell moved to approve Contract 2015-341, Change Order #1 on a contract with Granite
         Construction for the additional 24” RCP storm drain pipe on the 3500 W. reconstruction project;
         Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
    3. RIGHT-OF-WAY CONTRACT WITH SCOTT E. DAY FOR THE 3500 WEST EXTENSION PROJECT- PARCEL

    4. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE LIBERTY CEMETERY BOARD – RESOLUTION 37-2015

       Brooke Stewart, of the County Commission Office, stated that there were five vacancies on this board; all
       five members’ terms expired. Five applications were received—four members reapplied. The new terms
       will be staggered with three members serving four years and two serving two years.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 37-2015 making the following appointments to the
       Liberty Cemetery Board: Ronnie Chambers, Jennefer Hilburn and James Hadlock to serve 4-year terms
       and Tym Dickon and Tessa Bailey to serve 2-year terms; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
