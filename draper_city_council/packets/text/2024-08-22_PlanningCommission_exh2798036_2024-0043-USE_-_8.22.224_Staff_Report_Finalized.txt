BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application was continued from the June 13, 2024 Planning Commission meeting.
This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for
approximately 0.66 acres located on the east side of 1300 East, at approximately 13575 S.
1300 E. (Exhibit B & C). The property is currently zoned RA1. The applicant is requesting
that a Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for a combination cottage business and
personal instruction service home occupation that produces artwork for sale and teaches
art classes.

The single-family home on the property was constructed in 1986. The proposed business
is located inside an attached four car garage. Access to the property is unique in that it is
acquired through an easement on City owned property. It appears to be the remnants of
an older historic access to property further to the east, which was otherwise abandoned

The home-based business consists of two allowed business types. In the cottage business
portion, the applicants make jewelry and ceramics for sale online. In the personal
instruction portion of the business the applicants hold small class groups of up to six (6)
individuals at a time for art instruction. Proposed hours for classes would be between 6:00
pm and 9:00 pm on weekdays and between 12:00 pm and 8:00 pm on Saturdays, except
during the summer when the proposed hours would be between 12:00 pm and 8:00 pm

Monday through Saturday. Up to three class sessions per day may be held except for
weekdays during the school year which would be limited to a single class. During the
School year the applicants also anticipate that classes would only be held on Monday,
Wednesday, Friday and Saturday. The applicant’s explanation for the change for summer
hours is that they are both educators and are otherwise busy teaching school during the
schoolyear. Additional details regarding the operation are contained in the applicants’
statements provided in Exhibit H. Staff has included as a suggested condition of approval a
similar but simplified schedule that will allow for more flexibility to adapt to changes
without requiring a modification to the Conditional Use Permit.

Staff noted in their review the potential for negative impacts from smoke, odor, or gas
emissions from the kiln. The kiln is not very large and the exhaust is located towards the
southern portion of the property. Staff is unsure if any additional mitigation beyond the
location would be necessary.

The other identified impact would be the customers coming to the home and vehicular
traffic. Given the small group sizes, location of the property, and unique access to the
home and business, staff sees this limited increase as a very limited impact, and that the
existing available parking on site as shown in Exhibit I is sufficient to accommodate the
increase. Staff suggests as an additional mitigating condition that the driveway and parking
area for customers be kept clear and free of other personal storage.
