MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 20-12-01 DATE: December 8, 2020
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
ADOPTING THE 2021 MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP TENTATIVE BUDGET
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 ef seq.; and

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
legislative body for Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501: and

WHEREAS, as a part of the budget process, pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-6-111 et al,
Magna was required to prepare an annual tentative budget for consideration, possible
amendment, and tentative adoption; and

WHEREAS, on November 24, 2020 the Council adopted the 2021 tentative budget and
set the date for the public hearing for consideration and possible final adoption of the budget for
December 8, 2020 at 7:00 pm meeting to be held electronically without an anchor location
pursuant to UCA § 52-4-207(4) of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act:

WHEREAS, the noticing for the public hearing and copies of the budget (Exhibits “A”
and “B” to this resolution) were made available for public consideration at least seven (7) days
prior to the date of the public hearing to formally adopt the budget pursuant to UCA §§ 10-6-113
et al; and

WHEREAS, Adoption of the 2021 Budget will allow Magna to fund the governance
work of Magna; now therefore be it

RESOLUTION
ea ecco I

RESOLVED, the Council hereby adopts the 2021 Magna Budget, which includes the
2021 Cemetery Budget, hereinafter known as Exhibit “B,” and establishes it as the framework
for funding Magna’s governance work for 2021.
