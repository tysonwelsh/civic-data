   2. PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER AND TAKE ACTION ON A REQUEST TO VACATE A PORTION OF THE
      OLD SNOWBASIN ROAD, A DEDICATED PUBLIC THOROUGHFARE LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 5923
      SNOWBASIN ROAD

         Ronda Kippen, of the County Planning Division, showed an area map. The Old Snowbasin Road
         was dedicated in 1979 and there were outcroppings along the road (some due to steep slope, for snow
         storage, etc.). The subject area is adjacent to the applicant, Roger Dutson’s current residence. He
         currently gains access to his lot across a section of the subject property to be vacated, and the
         remainder is very steep embankment. Mr. Dutson has willingly committed to dedicate a public right-
         of-way necessary for roadway maintenance purposes along his lot that runs along Old Snowbasin
         Road through a plat amendment. He will proceed with an application to amend all of lot 1 in the
         Dutson Subdivision and the amendment will combine the 1.6069 acre parcel that will be vacated with
         the applicant’s existing 6.9 acre lot. Based on this commitment, the County Engineer gave a positive
         recommendation for this vacation. At the 12/6/2016 public hearing the Ogden Valley Planning
         Commission unanimously recommended approval. Ms. Kippen said that this vacation conforms to
         the Ogden Valley General Plan.
         This approval is subject to all review agency requirements and upon the following conditions:
         1) The applicant will submit an application for a plat amendment for all of Lot 1 in the Dutson
            Subdivision to combine the 1.6069 acre parcel with the existing 6.9 acre lot and
         2) The ordinance to vacate the subject property will be recorded with the Weber County Recorder’s
            Office in conjunction with the Dutson Subdivision plat amendment.

         Commissioner Harvey asked if this item had impact on trails and Ms Kippen replied it did not.
