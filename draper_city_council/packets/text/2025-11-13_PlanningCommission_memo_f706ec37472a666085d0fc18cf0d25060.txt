Re: Action Item: 2026 Draper City Planning Commission Meeting Schedule
Recommendation to the Draper City Council (Administrative Action)


Comments:

Staff has attached a proposed meeting schedule for the Planning Commission in 2026. All
dates are either second or fourth Thursdays of each month with the following proposed
exceptions:

     Meeting on the fourth Thursday in July (July 23) moved back to the fifth Thursday
     (July 30) due to the Pioneer Day Holiday.
     Meeting on the fourth Thursday in November (November 26) moved up to the third
     Thursday (November 19) due to the Thanksgiving Holiday.
     Meeting on the fourth Thursday in December (December 24) moved up to the third
     Thursday (December 17) due to the Christmas Holiday.

Another potential alternative to the proposed meeting dates for November and
December would be to move both meetings in those months to the 1st and 3rd
Thursdays (not included in proposal).
