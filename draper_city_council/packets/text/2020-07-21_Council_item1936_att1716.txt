BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Land Use Map Amendment and Zoning Map Amendment
for approximately 3.57 acres located on the east side of 700 East, at approximately 11743 South (Exhibit
C). The property is currently zoned RA1 (Residential Agriculture, 40,000 square foot lot minimums). The
applicant is requesting that a Land Use Map Amendment be approved to change the designation from
Community Commercial to the Neighborhood Commercial designation, and a rezone be approved from
the RA1 zone to the CN (Neighborhood Commercial) zoning district in order to allow for the
redevelopment of the property into an office development.

The application encompasses three parcels, all of which contain homes that pre-date city incorporation in
1978 (Exhibit B). The parcels are as follows:

      1. 28-20-353-002, 1.19 acres
      2. 28-20-353-003, 1.19 acres
      3. 28-20-353-037, 1.19 acres

ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Community Commercial
land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit D). This category is characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the RA1 zoning classification, supporting approximately one dwelling
unit per acre (Exhibit E). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the
purpose of the RA1 zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and
municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote
and preserve conditions favorable to large-lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of
animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings,
protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.” The RA1 zoning abuts the subject
property on the north and east sides. The R3 (Single-family Residential, 13,000 square foot lot minimum)
zone abuts on the south side, and the CC (Community Commercial) and CN zones abut on the west.

Land Use Map Amendment. The applicant is requesting to amend the land use map from the Community
Commercial designation to the Neighborhood Commercial designation. The intent of the Neighborhood
Commercial designation is to have small-scale retail and office uses that serve nearby residential
neighborhoods with limited impact on adjacent properties. The specific characteristics of the designation
are as follows:

Zoning Map Amendment. The applicant is requesting to rezone the property from RA1 to CN. The CN
zone’s purpose, per DCMC Section 9-8-020, is to “provide areas where convenience buying outlets,
having small trade areas, may be established to serve surrounding residential neighborhoods. This zone is
intended to promote a combination of retail and service facilities which, in character and scale, meet day
to day needs of nearby residents.”

The CN zone has several important distinctions from the CC zone contemplated in the Land Use Map.
The CN zone limits building height to 35-feet, the same as allowed within the adjacent residential
neighborhoods. The CC zone allows for 45-foot tall buildings, which allows for a 3-story office building,
and additional height then found on adjacent properties. The CN zone also restricts the maximum lot
coverage (that area that can be covered by a building) to 30%. The CC zone allows for a 50% overall
coverage, which can create a more intense feel for a development. There is also a limited number of uses
in the CN Zone, compared to the CC zone. Exhibit F shows the list of uses allowed in the CN zone.

Planning Division Review.

       1.      The CN zone can act as an excellent buffer zone to residential uses given the building
               height is the same as residential zones and it is the most restrictive of the commercial
               zones in the types of uses allowed.

Engineering and Public Works Divisions Review.

       1.      The increase in density from one residential unit per acre zoning to community commercial
               zoning constitutes significant traffic increase to and from the subject area. One single family
               residential structure constitutes up to nine daily trips and two peak hour trips, based on industry
               standard vehicle trip tables. Based on use tables, there are high traffic uses permitted in the zone.
               This increase in traffic would be limited to 700 East, an atrial street designed to accommodate
               the greatest volume of traffic in the city. Traffic issues in the area are at peak travel times, as
               well as school beginning and ending times in the area. Expansion of 700 East in the area is a
               future Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) project.
       2.      Connectivity with this lot to a public right-of-way is to 700 East, an arterial street owned by
               UDOT. Specific access locations will be evaluated with any site plan or subdivision submittal.
               Access locations will be required to meet Draper City Standards as outlined in our Master
               Transportation Plan and according to UDOT standards. A Traffic Impact Study may be required
               with a site plan or subdivision application based on the proposed use and projected traffic
               volume.
       3.      There are existing storm drainage facilities available fronting the subject area, but due to
               geographic limitations, may not be feasible to connect. Provisions for onsite storm drainage will
               need to be addressed with any subdivision or site plan application, and shall comply with the
               provisions of the development requirements within the Draper City Municipal Code.
       4.      Sanitary sewer facilities will be provided by South Valley Sewer District. Any subdivision
               application will require a commitment to serve from the Sewer District that facilities are
               adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.
       5.      Drinking water facilities will be provided by WaterPro. Any subdivision application will require
               a commitment to serve from the WaterPro that facilities are adequate to provide service for the
               proposed uses.

Fire Division Review.

       1.      The Draper City Fire Department has no issues at this time.
