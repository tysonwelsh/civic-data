PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meet-ing held on February 11, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2026-03 amending Her-riman City's Zoning Map for 1.06 acres of property located at 7411 West Hi Country Road from A-1-43 (Agricultural Single Family) to A-1-10 (Agricultural Single Family) with a Zoning Condition that would limit development to two dwelling units per acre. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
