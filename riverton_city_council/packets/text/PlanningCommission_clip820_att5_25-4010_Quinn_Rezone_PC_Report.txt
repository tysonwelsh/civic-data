This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final decision
authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-25-4010, “Quinn
Rezone,” by changing the General Plan Designation to Medium Density Residential (4
du/acre) and Rezoning the subject properties to R-4 (single-family residential, 4 du/acre).

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-25-4010, “Quinn
Rezone,” for changing the General Plan Designation and Rezoning thus keeping the current
General Plan Designations and current Zoning of R-3 and R-4.

Background

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Dallis Quinn for a
single parcel at 1980 West 13400 South. The subject parcel is 0.97-acres with an existing
home.

The current zoning for the property is both R-3 (3 du/acre, 14,000 sf-lots), and R-4 (4 du/acre,
10,000 sf lots). This request would zone the entire parcel R-4.

The current General Plan for the property is Estate Density Residential which corresponds with
lot sizes greater than 21,780-sf (half-acre). The applicant is requesting the General Plan
Designation be changed to Medium Density Residential (min 10,000-sf lots) which is consistent
with R-4 zoning.

The General Plan designation and zoning in this area are sometimes at odds. For example, to
the west and north are newer homes on lots Zoned R-3 but the General Plan designation for these
lots is Estate Density Residential. On the other hand, a few parcels to the east are a few parcels
zoned RR-22 but they have the General Plan designation of Medium Density Residential.
Overall, the area has a mix of zoning with R-4, R-3 and RR-22. All of the properties touching on
the subject property are either R-3 or R-4 so the requested rezone to R-4 does not create an
incompatible uses.
