GEORGE AND JUDY ANN PHILLIPS NAMED AS GRAND MARSHALLS OF THE 2022 MAMMOTH PARADE:

naming them as the 2022 Mammoth Parade Grand Marshalls. Councilor Callaway read a life history of

events throughout the years. Mr. Phillips expressed his appreciation for the recognition and said he
loved growing up and raising his family in Nephi. Pictures were taken of the Phillips and their family
members in attendance.

SUBDIVISION EXCEPTION ORDINANCE 07-05-2022 ADOPTED:

    Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council Ordinance 07-05-202: AN ORDINANCE OF NEPHI CITY
AMENDING TITLE 11 CHAPTER 3 SECTION 2 OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE. He stated that in the old platted
block system of Nephi, the city code had allowed an exemption from the subdivision ordinance if a
single residential lot was created. This worked in most cases, but it could be a challenge on a corner lot.
In some cases, two lots can be created which required the developer or property owner to go through
the laborious subdivision process. The proposed code changes rectified this situation and allowed for
the creation of up to 3 lots in the platted block system for residential purposes.
   It also required that no easements or public infrastructure (roads, utility improvements, etc.) be part
of the development. The new proposed code also simplified the language by combining the language in
item (1) and (3) of the code into the first paragraph. He stated that the Planning Commission had also
reviewed the changes and had passed their recommendations on to the Council.
   Councilor Memmott moved to adopt Ordinance 07-05-2022. Councilor Parady seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

AIRPORT LEASE AGREEMENT WITH SKYLER CHAMBERLAIN APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed a hangar lease agreement with Skyler Chamberlain for a newly constructed
hangar at the airport. The hangar is two spaces to the north of the city’s Snow Removal Equipment
Building and is 50’X50’.
 Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the Airport Hangar Lease Agreement with
Skyler Chamberlain. Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR AND CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Mr. Atkinson reported that the fireworks display on the 4th of July was a success.
  City Recorder Lisa Brough reported that the water department has hired two high school aged interns,
the police department has hired an intern to work at the animal shelter and that the office staff has
hired back the two interns they had working for them last summer.
  Councilor Callaway mentioned how well the parks looked for the 4th of July celebration. He also
reported on the JRDA meeting. He said that the JRDA would begin advertising soon the new fee
schedule for the weigh station at the landfill.
    Councilor Parady reported that the Chamber will be having a meeting on Thursday to swear in the
new officers and that Economic Director Brent Boswell will be presenting at the meeting.
   Councilor Ostler expressed his concern about the high price of natural gas and the inability of the gas
department to get parts they needed in a timely matter.
  Mayor Seely reported that 60+ baseball teams would
 be in town this next weekend for a tournament.

WORK SESSION CONVENED AT 8:12 p.m.

    1.    The council reviewed a draft of a master fee resolution that will be brought back for final
         approval at the next regular council meeting. The council agreed to change the fee for gun
         range use for out-of-county residents from $500 to $300 annually.

    2. The council discussed the lack of landscaping and fencing at the Cedar Ridge Development. Mr.
       Atkinson said that it should be completed when the other phases of the development are
       completed. Councilor Memmott suggested that high density zones that are next to single
       family residential zones should be required to fence their developments.
