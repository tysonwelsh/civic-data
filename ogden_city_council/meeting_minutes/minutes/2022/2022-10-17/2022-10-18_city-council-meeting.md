Proposed Resolution 2022-21 modifying the Ogden City Youth Council
Bylaws to expand the size of the Youth Council and remove the Leadership
Academy

A memo from Council staff came before the Council to consider a resolution modifying Ogden Youth Council bylaws.
The memo stated after considering the input of current participants and evaluating the programs of other cities (specifically
Cottonwood Heights and their Youth City Council), staff proposes an overhaul to the recently implemented youth
council/leadership academy.

Council Policy Analyst Hallmark summarized the memo and stated keeping the initial purposes and objectives of this
initiative intact, general revisions to the Ogden Youth City Council program can be summarized as follows:

e Youth Council and Youth Leadership Academy are consolidated into a single membership group called the
Ogden Youth City Council (OYCC).

e From the OYCC, seven members will be elected to leadership positions, collectively serving as part of the

Ogden Youth Executive Council (OYEC).
o Youth are elected to leadership positions for one term (one school year = one term) and positions
include:
= Chairperson
= Vice-Chairperson
* — Recorder
= Treasurer
= Public Relations Officer
«Social Officer
= Service Officer

e No limits on how many youth can be accepted to the OYCC; previously only nine were permitted to youth
council, and an unlimited number permitted to the academy.

e Noterm limits (one school year = one term); if a student wants to participate in OYCC for the duration of their
high school experience for example, they’re no longer restricted to only two years.

e Allmembers of the OYCC, which are not elected/appointed to executive positions, will be assigned to various
committees and/or appointed to deputy positions, as approved by Chair, Vice Chair, and/or program Advisor(s)
and Supervisor.

e OYCC members will have the opportunity to serve as liaisons to various boards and commissions, as
appropriately coordinated.

Chair Nadolski introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2022-21, entitled:

“A resolution of the Ogden City Council modifying the Ogden City Youth Council to provide a youth advisory
group to advise the City Council on issues of particular importance to youth in the community and to provide
leadership opportunities and training that will encourage civic engagement and interest in future City
government service.”

COUNCIL MEMBER RICHEY MOVED THE RESOLUTION BE ADOPTED, AND THE CHAIR BE
AUTHORIZED TO SIGN THE SAME. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER HYER, WITH THE
FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE—COUNCIL MEMBERS BLAIR, CHOBERKA, HYER, RICHEY,
WHITE, VICE CHAIR LOPEZ, AND CHAIR NADOLSKI. VOTING NO — NONE.

Public Comments

Teresa Bramwell stated that she was in attendance last week to talk about her property on 25" Street and Monroe
Boulevard and the extreme amount of crime that has occurred in that area as well as two fires that have occurred at her home and
inside Topper Bakery. When the Fire Department responded to the Topper Bakery fire, they damaged the back of her home, which
was not on fire. Today she wished to let the Council know where the crime is coming from; over the course of the 25 years that
she has owned the property, there has never been this much crime. There were previously two single-family homes located next to
her property that were demolished in order for a Family Dollar store to be constructed. The Family Dollar is 60 feet from her
property line and their dumpsters are right outside the bedroom window of her home. The area behind the dumpsters is an open
area with grass and garbage and the garbage is never cleaned up; it is taken out of the dumpsters by homeless people nearly every
day. There is a vast amount of garbage and drug needles in the area. About half of the crime is caused by homeless people
attracted to the area because of the dumpsters that are not secured. The other half of the crime is related to the nearby apartments
on 25" Street and Monroe Boulevard; recently some people from the apartments have been arrested for crimes that have been
committed on her property. She then presented a document she found on her door today; it is an affidavit for an administrative
search warrant by Ogden City’s Code Enforcement Division. On the back page of the document, it states the City has spoken with
her and she has withheld consent for the inspection of the property. She stated this is entirely false; she has never been asked for
consent to open her property for anyone to see it. She asserted that City staff has lied about trying to obtain consent to enter her
property.

Angel Castillo thanked Council staff for making Council meetings open and accessible to the public who need to
participate via electronic means. She then referenced a few topics that were discussed in tonight’s work session; she believes the
Council is truly invested in making sure the Neighbor Up Grant Program is effective at bringing residents together and she
suggested allowing bigger parties and allowing the party coordinator to work with the Ogden City Police Department to ensure
safety of party attendees and the general public in the surrounding areas. She then stated she could not be happier about the
proposed East Central Community Plan amendment; she has been talking about the need for smaller homes, especially along
public transit lines, and she is very excited about the Council’s consideration of this model that has been successful in other areas
and other communities. With regard to the proposed rezone on 7" Street, she is adamantly in agreement with Council members
Hyer and Richey that the City should not be rezoning property without an understanding of how it will be developed. The City
does not have specific form-based code that would require new construction to be compatible with its surroundings and the City
also does not have a specific palate of colors for development. It is important to preserve the quality of residential neighborhoods
by ensuring continuity and appropriate construction of new development. Until that can be assured, the rezone should be denied.
