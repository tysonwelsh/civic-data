WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
NOVEMBER 5, 2019

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Dean Cox at 4:00 p.m. on November 5, 2019, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist,
Deputy County Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy
County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2019-2525, A RESOLUTION IN
SUPPORT OF A PROCLAMATION EXTENDING THE CLOSED FIRE SEASON
THROUGH NOVEMBER 15

Hurricane Fire Chief Tom Kuhlmann explained that due to the dry conditions over the
summer in Washington County, the State Forester is requesting to extend the closed fire
season through November 15, 2019. If the dry conditions persist throughout the next few
weeks, the season will extend until the end of November.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-2525,
A Resolution in Support of a Proclamation Extending the Closed Fire
Season Through November 15. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox,

Iverson and Almquist voting aye.
