| would like to take this opportunity to request that Gayla Quittner be considered for employee
of the month. Gayla is currently assigned to the UCAP Program as the Senior Office Specialist.

Gayla is responsible for the clerical operations for the UCAP Program. This is a responsibility
that she has taken very seriously and become very proficient at. During the Covid- 19 epidemic
the numbers in the UCAP program participants reduced significantly due to the closure of the
courts. This presented Gayla with time that she used to assist with revamping the program.
This consisted of rewriting and updating all forms, procedures as well as receiving training to
ensure that the program was consistent with best practices.

The program is now back to operating numbers; however, it operates much more efficiently
and is far more professional. Gayla’s skill, professionalism and hard work was critical in making
this process happen.

The pandemic also presented an opportunity for Gayla to use her knowledge and skills to
assist with the federal CARES grant. Gayla worked tirelessly to assist with the grant and
quickly became a critical part of the grant management team.

Gayla has shown her versatility and dedication to both the UCAP program and the grant
management team. Gayla puts forth high quality work and does it with a great attitude.

Please consider Gayla Quittner for the Employee of the Month.
Thanks, Sergeant Robert C. Weidlein II - Utah County Alternative Probation (UCAP)
