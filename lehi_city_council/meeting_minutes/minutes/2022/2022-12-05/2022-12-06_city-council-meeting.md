Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council held Tuesday, December 6, 2022, at Lehi
City Hall, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at approximately 3:45 PM.

Present:     Mark Johnson, Mayor
             Paige Albrecht, Council Member
             Chris Condie, Council Member
             Paul Hancock, Council Member
             Katie Koivisto, Council Member
             Mike Southwick, Council Member

Others Present:      Jason Walker, City Administrator; Cameron Boyle, Assistant City
Administrator; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Andy Gowans, Management Analyst; and Teisha
Wilson, City Recorder

1. Consideration of Adjourning into a Closed Executive Session to discuss the character,
   professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual.

   Motion: Councilor Koivisto moved to adjourn into a Closed Session to discuss the character,
   professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual. Councilor Albrecht
   seconded the motion.

   Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
           Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed
           unanimously.

2. Welcome and Opening Comment
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that Council Hancock had to leave the meeting
   and is excused.

3. Discussion Items
   3.1 Alpine School District Update- Stacy Bateman, ASD School Board
   Stacy Bateman gave on update on Alpine School District and distributed the district’s annual
   report. There was discussion of the bond not passing and Ms. Bateman noting that the district
   is looking at options which includes a district wide boundary study. There was also discussion
   of the district’s financial status and how FTE’s are calculated in schools.

   3.2 Discussion of revisions to the Gardner Plumb Development Agreement, transferring
   residential units from the Hardman property to property along west Main Street (approximately
   2500 W Main)
   Kim Struthers, Community Development Director, informed the Council that Gardner Plumb
   is requesting a density transfer, but that would require the Council’s approval with a

   3.3 Tree Removal Discussion- City Council
   Steve Marchbanks, Parks Manager, and BJ Benson, Street Manager, discussed the steps the city
   went through with the tree removal project near Olympic Park. They felt that it was the city’s
   responsibility to deal with the nuisance and create a safer environment for pedestrians. There
   was discussion about what the Council could do with the funds if some residents choose not to
   replant a tree. The Council would like to see a plan for any remaining funds and would like that
   money to be spend in the neighborhood.

4. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time the meeting adjourned
   at approximately 6:00 p.m.
