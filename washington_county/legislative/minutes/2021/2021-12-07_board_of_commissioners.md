PUBLIC HEARING: CREATION OF THE MOUNTAIN FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE
DISTRICT

Chairman Almquist outlined the guidelines for those that wished to speak and asked for a motion
to open the public hearing.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Open the Public Hearing Regarding the
Creation of the Mountain Fire Special Service District. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

Commissioner Snow explained the purpose of the hearing and the County’s reasoning behind the
potential creation of the new district.

Seth Stinson expressed that Central is in the middle of the proposed Special Service District area
and stated that he felt that Central made the most sense for the main hub of the district.

Jason Ranoa stated that they have formed a local 501¢3 for a neighborhood watch in Pine Valley
and stated that he is in support of the proposed coverage for the area.

Gordon Poppit stated that Central residents were not aware of the newly proposed District and
stated that Central has been primarily comprised of volunteers.

Stewart Bowler from Veyo stated that clarification needs to be given to the residents and current
fire fighters in the area.

Commissioner Iverson stated that the goal would be to have a chief and a captain of each area and
consolidate training in order to provide a better level of emergency service for all of the areas.

Seeing no one else come forward, Chairman Almquist asked for a motion to close the public
hearing.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Close the Public Hearing Regarding the
Creation of the Mountain Fire Special Service District and Open the Western
Fire Special Service District Public Hearing. Motion seconded by

PUBLIC HEARING: CREATION OF THE WESTERN FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE
DISTRICT

Patrick Johnson stated that the Commissioners should conduct an audit of the area to determine
the equipment and resources that are available in the area. The collaboration of the district may
result in a catastrophic event as many residents are not in favor of the district.

Joyce Gaufin is a resident of Dammeron Valley spoke in favor of the transitional plan for the
Districts and suggested allowing additional time to allow for the advances that Dammeron Valley

has made.

Rex Burton of Dammeron Valley stated that the response time is very slow for the area and
expressed a desire for a professional District to increase the response time for emergencies.

Joel Burnham of Dammeron Valley expressed concern over the lack of certification of the fire
fighters in the area and slow response time when he moved to the area. He stated that Dammeron
Valley has had great success in the last year in obtaining certified structure fire fighters and
increasing response time.

Richard Downer of Dammeron Valley stated that unified training is needed for all of the
responders in the area and spoke in favor of the District consolidation.

Ryan Anderson of Dammeron Valley spoke regarding the success of the transition of Dammeron
Valley’s EMS team.

John Cazier stated that a collaborative effort should be represented by a member of each of the
communities along the SR-18 corridor.

Charles Tandy of Brookside stated that Central should not be placed within the Enterprise District
and expressed concern with the potential impact on property taxes and fees imposed on the district
as a result of the collaboration.

Mack Sorenson of Dammeron Valley spoke in favor of the collaboration of the Districts and would
like to work together with all of the communities.

Seeing no one else come forward, Chairman Almquist asked for a motion to close the public
hearing.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Close the Public Hearing Regarding the
Creation of the Western Fire Special Service District. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.
