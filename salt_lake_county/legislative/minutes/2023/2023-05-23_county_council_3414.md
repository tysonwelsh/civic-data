RESOLUTION NO. 6105
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL CONCERNING THE 
CONTINUING FUNDING OF PUBLIC SAFETY SERVICES WITHIN A 
MUNICIPALITY THAT IS LOCATED IN A DESIGNATED RECREATION AREA
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council, acting as the legislative body for Salt Lake 
County, has the authority to provide county general funds for fire, paramedic, and police 
protection services within a municipality that is located in the area designated as a 
recreational area pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 17-34-1(5)(d), provided that the Council 
makes a finding that a disproportionate share of public safety service needs within the 
municipality are generated by residents of the county who live both inside and outside the 
limits of cities and towns; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council previously approved Resolution No. 5644 on 
October, 8, 2019, which resolution, in part, designated the Town of Brighton as a 
municipality within the recreation area that is defined and designated for budgetary 
purposes in accordance with the requirements Utah Code Ann. § 17-34-1(5)(d); and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council, in recent budget years, and including the first 
six months of fiscal year 2023, has provided county general funds to the Unified Fire 
Authority for the purpose of mitigating public safety services within the Town of 
Brighton; and 
WHEREAS, a disproportionate share of public safety service needs within the Town of 
Brighton continue to be generated by residents of the county who live both inside and 
outside the limits of cities and towns; and 
WHEREAS, as part of its 2023 budget deliberations and subsequent public meetings, the 
Salt Lake County Council has discussed various proposals related to the ongoing 
provision of county funding for eligible public safety service needs within the Town of 
Brighton; and 
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council now finds it expedient to communicate policy 
direction to stakeholders and taxing entities about the continuing provision of county 
funding for eligible public safety service needs within the Town of Brighton, and 
particularly the status of funding for the final six months of fiscal year 2023;
Page 3 of 12
County Council Meeting Minutes May 23, 2023
NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Salt Lake County Council, acting as the 
legislative body for Salt Lake County, that the following is the sense of the Council:
1) The Council hereby provides notice that it intends to continue providing county general 
funds to the Unified Fire Authority for the purpose of mitigating public safety service 
needs within the Town of Brighton in a manner consistent with Utah Code Ann. § 
17-34-1(5)(d). The Council intends to provide such funding for the remainder of Salt Lake 
County’s 2023 fiscal year, as well as for Salt Lake County ’s 2024 fiscal year, but may 
cease providing such funds after December 31, 2024. The Council reiterates the following 
finding that it made in Resolution No. 5644: “The decision as to fund and the exact 
amount of funding shall be determined by the County Council annually during the County 
budget process, based on budget presentations and requests provided to the County 
Council by the appropriate Public Safety service providers.” 
2) The Council desires to see the Unified Fire Authority and the Unified Fire Service Area, 
along with their members, including the Town of Brighton, take action to reduce existing 
funding gaps for public safety needs in the Town of Brighton, potentially including but not 
limited to the following: redrawing municipal boundaries to the extent allowed by existing 
law or legislative change; maximizing currently available revenue streams such as resort 
sales and use tax, municipal energy sales and use tax, and general property tax; and 
seeking new revenue streams from the Utah State Legislature in the 2024 legislative 
session. The Council will cooperate in offering support for this effort.
3) The Council commits to work with the Salt Lake County Mayor to better assess Salt 
Lake County ’s approach to public safety funding in Salt Lake County ’s recreational areas 
in the 2024 budget, potentially including but not limited to the following: assessing whether 
Salt Lake County should update the recreational areas that it has defined and designated 
for budgetary purposes; determining the ongoing status and funding eligibility of 
municipalities that are located in the designated recreational areas; identifying appropriate 
Salt Lake County funding sources and amounts for public safety needs in the designated 
recreational areas that are generated by residents of the county who live both inside and 
outside the limits of cities and towns; developing policy criteria for making future funding 
decisions that best represent the interests of all Salt Lake County taxpayers, including 
whether to cease providing funds to designated recreational areas.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 23rd day of May, 2023.

B. The County ’s plan includes the design and construction of a trail running alongside the 
Utah and Salt Lake Canal from approximately 3570 South 8000 West to 2600 South 8990

Cooperation Agreement with Magna Metro Township for the transfer of $ 162,000.00 
from Magna Metro Township to Salt Lake County for use by Salt Lake County to 
complete the Project is approved and the Salt Lake County Mayor is hereby authorized to 
execute the same.

RESOLUTION NO. 6107
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL APPROVING AND 
AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO GRANT A PERPETUAL EASEMENT TO 
ROCKY MOUNTAIN POWER IN SOUTH JORDAN
RECITALS
A.  Salt Lake County (the “County”) owns a parcel of real property located in South 
Jordan, Utah adjacent to the South Jordan City Park, specifically identified as Parcel No . 
27-15-376-024 (the “Property”).
B.  Rocky Mountain Power, an unincorporated division of PacificCorp (“RMP”), will be 
providing electrical power service to the Property, and an easement is necessary across 
the Property and across portions of the South Jordan City Park (the “Park”) to allow 
RMP to access, service and maintain the main electrical power facilities on the Property.
C.  The County, South Jordan City (the “City”), and RMP have prepared an 
Underground Right of Way Easement (“Easement”) attached hereto as Exhibit A, wherein 
the County grants the powerline easement across the Property to RMP, and the City 
grants the powerline easement across the portions of the Park.
D.  In exchange for the Easement attached hereto as Exhibit A, RMP will provide 
electrical services to the Property.
E.  It has been determined that the best interests of the County and the general public will 
be served by granting the Easement to RMP. The terms and conditions of the Easement 
are in compliance with all applicable state statues and county ordinances.
NOW THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake County Council that 
the Easement, attached hereto as Exhibit 1 and by this reference made a part of this 
Resolution, is hereby approved; and the Mayor and County Clerk are hereby authorized 
to execute said Easement and to cause the Easement to be delivered to RMP.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 23rd day of May, 2023.
