                               RESOLUTION NO. 21-26

             A RESOLUTION APPOINTING HAN KIM, ERIN SEIBEL, AND GARY
             THOMPSON TO THE DRAPER CITY DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION
             ADVISORY COMMITTEE

      WHEREAS, the City is authorized to establish subordinate bodies with the city
as deemed appropriate by the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has established the Draper City Diversity
and Inclusion Advisory Committee to identify and advise on issues faced by diverse
groups and to create opportunities for an inclusive community within the City; and

       WHEREAS, the Council recognizes the importance of diversity and inclusion
within the City; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint and the Council hereby consents to
the appointment of Han Kim, Erin Seibel, and Gary Thompson to serve as regular
members on the Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Committee.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Appointment. The following individuals are hereby appointed to
serve on the Draper City Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Committee according to the
laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the committee.

             Han Kim             June 15, 2021 through October 31, 2021
             Erin Seibel         June 15, 2021 through October 31, 2021
             Gary Thompson       June 15, 2021 through October 31, 2021

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.

       Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective upon the
effective date of Ordinance #1493.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THIS 15TH DAY OF JUNE, 2020.
