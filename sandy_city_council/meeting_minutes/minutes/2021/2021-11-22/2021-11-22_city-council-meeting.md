NOTICE OF SPECIAL MEETING
It is hereby ordered that the Sandy City Council hold a special meeting on Monday November
22, 2021, at 5:30 PM. The meeting will be held both at Sandy City Hall (10000 South Centennial
Parkway) in the City Council Chambers and online via Zoom Webinar. During this special
meeting the City Council, acting as the Sandy Board of Municipal Canvassers, will re-canvass
certain returns for the 2021 Election.

City Council Meeting Minutes November 22, 2021
1. 21-462 The Sandy City Council, acting as the Sandy Board of Municipal
Canvassers, meeting to re-canvass certain returns for the 2021 General
Election following the required recount of the Mayoral election.
Attachments: Click here to eComment on this item
Wendy Downs, City Recorder presented the mayoral election results tablulated and
provided to the Council on November 16, 2021 as well as November 17, 2021.
Council Member Houseman made a motion to certify the election results. The motion
was seconded by Council Member Christensen.
The vote was as follows:
Houseman: yes
Christensen: yes
Sharkey: yes
Stroud: yes
Nicholl: yes
Zoltanski: yes
Bradburn: yes
