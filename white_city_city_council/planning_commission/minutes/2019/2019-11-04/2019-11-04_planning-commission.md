                                            Hearing began at – 6:04 p.m.
31051 – (Continued from 10/22/2019 meeting) - MSD Planning Staff is seeking public input as well as
discussion and/or a recommendation regarding the White City Housing Element, which will become a
component of the White City Metro Township General Plan. Planner: Lupita McClenning

Ms. McClenning stated here to formalize the SB 34 Housing Element.

Commissioner Millen motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Wilson seconded that motion
and addressed citizen comments received.
