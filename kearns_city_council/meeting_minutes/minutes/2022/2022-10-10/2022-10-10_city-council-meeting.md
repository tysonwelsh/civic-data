Legal Updates/Information

Presentation of Title 12

                Nathan Bracken stated Title 12 of the Kearns Metro Township Code addresses
the process of a code enforcement offense. The resident will be noticed and given a timeframe to
correct the offense. If the offense is not corrected within the timeframe, the Council can correct it
themselves at the owner’s expense. The notification process is also being cleaned up. The draft
language has been sent to the MSD for their comments.

Resolution – Unified Police Department

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving an interlocal agreement
with the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA) regarding the funding of a
new Sergeant position for the Unified Police Department’s (UPD) Kearns precinct.

RESOLUTION NO. 2022-10-01                                                   Date: October 10, 2022

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH THE SALT LAKE VALLEY LAW
       ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA REGARDING THE FUNDING OF A NEW
       SERGEANT POSITION FOR THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT’S KEARNS
       PRECINCT

                                           RECITALS


        WHEREAS, Utah Code § 11-13-202 authorizes two or more public agencies to execute
interlocal agreements to carry out joint or cooperative actions, including the provision of law
enforcement services; and

       WHEREAS, the Unified Police Department’s (“UPD”) Kearns precinct currently has one
sergeant who supervises twelve-and-a-half officers, which has limited the sergeant’s ability to
mentor and supervise the precinct’s four school resource officers (“SROs”).

        WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) Council and UPD desire to reclassify
one of the precinct’s detectives as a sergeant (the “New Sergeant”), thereby allowing the precinct
to have two sergeants who can divide the precinct’s supervision and mentoring responsibilities;

       WHEREAS, the New Sergeant will supervise six officers, including all four of the SROs,
which will improve the effectiveness of the SROs by increasing their opportunities to communicate
with and receive mentorship from the New Sergeant;

      WHEREAS, reclassifying the detective as the New Sergeant will require an additional
$100,000 over the next three years in increased compensation and benefits;

        WHEREAS, the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (“SLVLESA”) levies
property taxes for the law enforcement services Kearns receives from UPD and is responsible for
providing the funding used to pay the compensation and benefits of the UPD officers assigned to
Kearns;

        WHEREAS, Kearns and SLVLESA are both public entities and desire to execute an
interlocal agreement pursuant to their authority under Utah Code § 11-13-202 by which Kearns
will provide SLVLESA with the $100,000 needed to fund the New Sergeant’s re-classification over
the next three years.

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Kearns Metro Township Council that
effective immediately:

   1. The attached interlocal agreement is approved pursuant to Utah Code §§ 11-13-202 and
      11-13-202.5;

   2. The Mayor and Kearns’ staff are authorized to take all steps needed to implement the
      interlocal agreement, including the transfer of the $100,000 to SLVLESA in a manner that
      is mutually agreeable to the Mayor and SLVLESA; and

Resolution – Conveyance of Public Parks to Kearns Metro Township

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving an interlocal agreement
with Salt Lake County authorizing the conveyance of public parks to Kearns Metro Township:

RESOLUTION NO. 2022-10-02                                               DATE: October 10, 2022

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY AUTHORIZING
       THE CONVEYANCE OF PUBLIC PARKS TO KEARNS

                                          RECITALS

        WHEREAS, Utah Code § 11-13-202 authorizes two or more public agencies to execute
interlocal agreements to carry out joint or cooperative actions; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code § 11-13-214 provides that any public agency may convey property
to or acquire property from any other public agencies for consideration as may be agreed upon;
and

         WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) and Salt Lake County (“County”) are
public agencies as contemplated in the above referenced sections of the Utah Code, which are
part of the Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code § 11-13-101, et seq.; and

        WHEREAS, following Kearns’ incorporation in 2017, the County retained ownership of
certain parks located within Kearns’ municipal boundaries; namely, Bruce Field Park, David

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

                Pam Roberts, General Manager, WFWRD, introduced Paul Korth, Finance
Director, WFWRD. She reviewed the 2023 budget and priorities the District has. A fee increase
of $2.50 is being proposed to the board. She reviewed the various programs that WFWRD offers
residents. WFWRD continues to deal with staffing issues. In 2018, WFWRD began having trouble
hiring enough CDL drivers to keep the bulk waste program the same. In 2020 it was short full-
time and part-time driver positions and that created the need to change the bulk waste pick up
program to the Seasonal Container Reservation Program (SCRP). She continues to look for
improvements and ways to absorb increases in costs.

               Paul Korth, Finance Director, WFWRD, reviewed cost increases since the last fee
increase in 2018 and WFWRD’s cash projections for 2022-2026.

Kearns Improvement District (KID)

               Greg Anderson stated conservation landscape classes were recently held and
additional classes will be held in the spring. KID participated in a Halloween event and handed
out conservation promotional items. He is working with Mayor Bush and Jordan Valley
Conservation District to relandscape the 911 park. Various water providers met in Vernal and
discussed water issues. The drought will continue in a very serious manner over the next year.
Water providers will work together to handle water supplies and make sure KID has what it needs
to live day by day. A public hearing will be held on October 18, 2022, for the Capital Facilities
Plan.
