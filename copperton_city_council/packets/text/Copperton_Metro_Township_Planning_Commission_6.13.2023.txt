                                     STAFF ANALYSIS
• The NMU Zone is a residential zone that allows a small-scale, horizontal or vertical mixture of neighborhood
  commercial uses to serve the daily needs of area residents.

• This zone is intended only for small-scale, low-impact uses, to promote a better mixture of uses close to home
  and within a walkable environment.

• If rezoned to NMU, the subject property could be seen as a transitional property between the residential uses to
  the immediate west, and the commercial properties to the immediate east.
