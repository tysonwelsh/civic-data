MEMO
To: City Council
From: Todd A. Draper, AICP
Date: 2026-04-07
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinances #1713 and #1714


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Land Use Map Amendment and Zoning Map
Amendment for approximately 0.45 acres located on the southeastern boundary of the
City at approximately 16001 S. 3500 E. The property is currently zoned RR-22 and is
located in the Hillside Sensitive Lands Overlay Zone.

Draper City and Alpine City are mutually adjusting their municipal boundary lines in the
subject area to incorporate a small piece of land into Alpine City. The property is located
between properties in Alpine and the Alpine Hollow development in Draper City. The land
appears to be a gap between deeds, and is intended to be absorbed by the neighboring
properties in Alpine City after the adjustment to the city boundaries has taken place.
Draper City is seeking to amend the Draper City Land Use and Zoning Maps to remove
the zoning and land use designations from the property that will become part of Alpine
City.

At the March 12, 2026 Planning Commission meeting the Planning Commission
forwarded positive recommendation for both applications on a unanimous 5-0 vote.

Findings for Approval:
  1. The proposed amendments are consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the
     city's general plan.
  2. The proposed amendments are harmonious with the overall character of existing
     development in the vicinity of the subject property.
  3. The proposed amendments will not affect the standards of any applicable overlay
     zone.
  4. The proposed amendments will not adversely affect adjacent property.
  5. There will be adequate facilities and services to serve the subject property within
     Alpine City.
  6. The proposed Land Use Map amendments comply with DCMC Section 9-2-020(F).
