Minutes of the Planning Commission meeting held on Thursday, August 17, 2023, at 6:30 p.m. in
the Murray City Municipal Council Chambers, 10 East 4800 South, Council Chambers, Murray,
Utah.

The public was able to view the meeting via the live stream at http://www.murraycitylive.com or
https://www.facebook.com/Murraycityutah/. Anyone who wanted to make a comment on an
agenda item may submit comments via email at planningcommission@murray.utah.gov.

The Staff Review meeting was held from 6:00 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. The Planning Commission
members briefly reviewed the applications on the agenda. An audio recording is available at the
Murray City Community and Economic Development Department Office.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES

Commissioner Nay made a motion to approve the minutes from the June 1, 2023, and June 15,
2023, Planning Commission meetings, with minor changes noted.

Seconded by Commissioner Hacker. A voice vote was made, motion passed 5-0.

CONFLICT OF INTEREST

There were no conflicts of interest identified.

APPROVAL OF FINDINGS OF FACT

Commissioner Hacker made a motion to approve the findings of fact and conclusions for the
Magnolia Rouge Day Spa conditional use permit and for TD Sports West conditional use permit.

Seconded by Commissioner Richards. A voice vote was made, motion passed 5-0.

SUBDIVISION REVIEW(S)

Clover Crest Subdivision Amendment - 5101 S. Wintergreen Circle & 5102 S. Glendon Street –
Project #23-088

Mr. Smallwood presented the application for Daniel Snarr to amend the lot line between lots five
and ten of the Clover Crest Subdivision. The property is in the R-1-8 zone, where it’s required to
have a minimum of 8,000 square feet per lot. David Snarr has 20,847 square feet and Daniel
Snarr has 9,800 square feet. The proposal would amend the lots so that Mr. David Snarr would

have 14,400 square feet and Mr. Daniel Snarr would get 16,000 square feet. Staff recommends
approval subject to conditions.

Mr. Daniel Snarr said he read and can comply with the conditions listed. He said he’s concerned
that the application reads that lot 5 becomes 5A because there is already a lot 5A.

Chair Pehrson said that can be addressed.

Mr. Smallwood said the application would go through engineering and then the county. If they
see an issue with the lot naming, they will fix it.

Public comment was opened and closed, with no comments being made.

Commissioner Lowry made the motion to approve the amendment for lots five and ten of the
Clover Crest subdivision for the properties addressed 5101 S. Wintergreen Circle & 5102 S.
Glendon Street, subject to the six conditions:

1. Meet the requirements of the City Engineer, including the following:
   a. Meet City Subdivision amendment requirements and plat format standards.
   b. Provide public utility easements (PUEs) on proposed lots.
   c. Provide PUEs for any existing utilities that are located along previous lot lines.
2. Meet the Power, Water, Sewer and Fire Department requirements.
3. Meet all requirements of Section 17.100 of the Murray Land Use Ordinance for the R-1-8
   Zone.
4. The project shall meet all requirements of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.
5. The applicant shall prepare the Subdivision plat which complies with all requirements of Title
   16, Murray City Subdivision Ordinance
6. The subdivision plat shall be recorded within one year of the final approval by the Planning
   Commission or the final plat shall be null and void.

Seconded by Commissioner Hacker. Roll Call Vote:

 A     Pehrson
 A     Nay
 A     Hacker
 A     Lowry
 A     Milkavich

Motion passed 5-0

CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT(S)

Pine River Roofing and Construction – 4627 South Cherry Street – Project #23-087

The applicant was not present to represent application. The commissioners and staff held a
discussion regarding a motion and issues with the application.

Commissioner Nay said they should table the application at this time, due to two major issues
that need to be publicly addressed. The issues involve the restriping and refinishing of the
parking lot, as well as the dumpster enclosures. The onus to resolve these is on the property
owner, and they need to present to speak to it.

Chair Pehrson said it would not be prudent to move forward without them here.

Vice Chair Milkavich asked if staff had talked to the applicant.

Commissioner Nay said they should have received letters and a phone call.

Mr. Al Janabi said he tried to call them about attending the meeting.

Commissioner Lowry made a motion to table the application by Pine River Roofing and
Construction, until the applicant can be present.

Seconded by Commissioner Milkavich. Roll Call Vote:

 A     Pehrson
 A     Nay
 A     Hacker
 A     Lowry
 A     Milkavich

Motion passed 5-0
