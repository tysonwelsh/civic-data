WHEREAS, Article Two of the Optional Plan for Salt Lake County 
Government (hereinafter “Plan”) provides that six (6) members of the Salt 
Lake County Council are elected by geographical district; and WHEREAS, 
Section 2.03 of the Plan requires that for the purpose of electing district 
councilmembers, the County shall be divided into six (6) geographical 
districts designated Districts 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6; and 
WHEREAS, Section 2.04 of the Plan provides that “Council districts shall 
have substantially equal populations and shall be reapportioned by the 
Council within one year after each decennial census report. Upon 
reapportionment, the Council districts should, to the extent practical, remain 
consistent with their original geographical configuration and representation, 
allowing continuity and ease of contact between residents and district 
Council members;” and 
WHEREAS, on or about April 26, 2021, the United States Census Bureau 
published the most recent decennial census report; and 
WHEREAS, Chapter 2.71 of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 
2001, provides for the establishment of a County redistricting commission to 
act in an advisory capacity to the Salt Lake County Council for the purpose 
of reapportioning Council districts; and 
WHEREAS, on September 15, 2020, the Council appointed seven members 
of the Salt Lake County Redistricting Commission to serve a term 
commencing on January 1, 2020, and terminating on December 31, 2028;  
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Redistricting Commission has met in 
open and public meetings throughout 2021, and has faithfully discharged its 
duties under Section 2.71.040 of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances; 
and 
WHEREAS, on November 2, 2021, the Salt Lake County Redistricting 
Commission recommended three written redistricting plans for Council 
districts in ranked order, and provided copies of those plans along with 
maps of boundary changes to the Council for its consideration; and 
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council, acting in its capacity as county 
legislative body for Salt Lake County, now intends to adopt a plan that 
reapportions its six geographical Council districts as required by and in a 
Page 3 of 6
County Council Meeting Minutes November 16, 2021
manner consistent with the applicable legal requirements of federal and state 
law, the Optional Plan for Salt Lake County Government, and Salt Lake 
County Ordinance; 
NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Salt Lake County Council that: 
1) The Salt Lake County Council hereby adopts a reapportionment plan for 
Salt Lake County ’s six geographical Council districts as further provided for, 
described, and incorporated by reference in Exhibit A, which exhibit includes 
a map of the reapportioned Council district boundaries and their respective 
voting precincts. 
2) The Salt Lake County Council certifies that this reapportionment plan 
achieves substantial equality of population among the six Council districts as 
reflected in the decennial census report. 
3) The Salt Lake County Council certifies that this reapportionment plan 
complies with all applicable federal, state, and local legal requirements, 
including the Plan ’s requirement that “upon reapportionment, the Council 
districts should, to the extent practical, remain consistent with their original 
geographical configuration and representation, allowing continuity and ease 
of contact between residents and district Council members.” 
4) The Salt Lake County Council commends the Salt Lake County 
Redistricting Commission, the Salt Lake County Clerk, and the Salt Lake 
County Surveyor, for their professionalism, expertise, and assistance 
throughout the reapportionment process.
