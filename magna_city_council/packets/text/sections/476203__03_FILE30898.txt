                                       PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
The facts as understood by staff pertaining to the proposed changes are as follows:
    • Ivory Development intends to become a Developer within the “Gabler Grove” project.
    • Ivory Development desires to develop an additional 9.10 acres that are North of the approved Gabler
        Grove Planned Development.
    • Ivory Development desires that the additional 9.10 acres be rezoned and developed to the same or
        similar requirements that are applicable to the previously approved Gabler Grove Planned
        Development.
    • Ivory Development desires multiple amendments to the text of the original agreement. The proposed
        amendments include:
             o Inclusion of the additional 9.10 acres within the agreement
             o Removal of sections of the agreement pertaining to other portions of the development property
                 included in the original agreement but not under the potential future control of Ivory
                 Development.
             o Specific addition of Ivory Development by name as a developer in the agreement
             o Specific rezone of the additional 9.10 acres and re-distribution of zoning per a conceptual zoning
                 plan yet to.
             o Rezoning of a portion of property owned by Troy L and Corinna M. Belliston. This property
                 owner was never part of the original agreement. Applicant has not indicated if the intent is to
                 add the property or property owner into a Development Agreement or not.
             o Elimination of the intent to provide opens space and amenities in conjunction with an approved
                 preliminary plat (exhibit C)
             o Change to the definition of Open Space within the Project to remove exclusions to open space,
                 remove balcony allowances for RM zoned property, remove open space percentage
                 requirements for the project, remove discretionary elements originally afforded the developer
                 of the RM zoned parcel.
             o Removal of Magna’s review and approval authority pertaining to required recreational
                 amenities.
             o Removal of Magna’s ability to require bonds of the developer for required recreational
                 amenities which are deferred to later phases of the project.
             o Addition of a section regarding the Subordination of Conflicting Terms.
    • The current development agreement allows for reconfiguration of the subdivision plat and lots
        regardless of the underlying zoning. This would not change with the proposed changes.
    • The current development allows for re-assignment of development rights to future developers without
        need to amend the agreement. This would not change with the proposed changes.
    • No amended preliminary plat has been submitted (change to exhibit C) that matches with the
        conceptual zoning plan submitted.
    • Changes to identify the affected parcel numbers, acreage amounts, and other elements have not yet
        been identified in the proposed amendment.


                               PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Planning Staff recommends that the Magna Metro Township Planning Commission continue the decision on a
recommendation to the Magna Metro Township Planning Commission for a period of up to 3 months to allow the
Developer the opportunity to submit a complete application and obtain the required written permission from all owners of
land subject to the proposed development agreement amendment that they consent to the submittal of an application to
amend the agreement. Also, to allow the applicant the opportunity to submit complete plans detailing the resulting
amendments and changes to the General Plan, Zoning, and preliminary Subdivision Plats.
