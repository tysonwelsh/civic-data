39            allow signage incorporated into certain types of fencing to qualify as monument
40            signs, amendment proposed by Riverton City
41
42   Mr. Lethbridge said this is a proposed amendment to Riverton City Sign Ordinance, specifically
43   to the section regulating monument signage and is proposed by Riverton City. A request was
44   made by a business in the City who had enclosed their site in a precast masonry fence to

48   allow for signage attached to or cast into a fence. In reviewing potential amendments to sign
49   ordinance, the Council indicated their willingness to consider allowing use of a fence panel in a
50   precast fence as a monument sign, and instructed staff to prepare an amendment for

16            Ordinance No. 21-28 – Amending Section 18.45, RM-6, amending the RM-6 zone to
17            individual lot development only, amendments proposed by Riverton City
18
19   Mr. Lethbridge said that Riverton City currently has in its code the RM-6 Zone, which allows for
20   multifamily development at a maximum density of six units per acre. The code allows for both
21   attached units within commonly held open space, as is more typical with multi-family, or
22   subdivision of property into individual lots and that in its application of this Zone, Riverton City
23   has repeatedly only approved it with a Specific Development designation limiting development
24   to individual lots. Staff is proposing the zone itself be amended to only allow development
25   utilizing individual lots. This would still allow for both attached and detached units, as allowed
26   individual lots are specified for both and the standards for individual dwelling lots are already
27   contained in this section, and so the amendment is primarily deletion of the sections for
28   common open space developments.
29
30   Councilmember Stewart asked for clarification on this ordinance. He said he thought this
31   ordinance would make RM-6 a single-family zone.
32
33   Mr. Lethbridge explained that the language in place in the RM-6 zone is focused on individual
34   lots. He said that the property on 3600 West 12800 South which was recently rezoned to RM-6
35   utilized the individual lot standards and still allowed for detached and duplex units. He
36   explained that the ordinance did not adequately address how duplex units could be utilized on
37   individual lots. He said from the staff’s perspective they wanted to remove all the language that
38   expanded beyond the individual lot designation.
39
40   Councilmember McDougal said that several projects had a special designation put on them for
41   single family lots but with the new zoning they could have duplex units throughout. He asked if
42   the projects already approved could be changed. Mr. Lethbridge said that because there was
43   an SD designation put on those projects, they cannot be changed.
44
45   Councilmember McDougal asked if the Council wanted to approve an RM-6 zone and not allow
46   for duplexes then they would need to add an SD designation to accomplish that. Mr. Lethbridge
47   explained that because there are some current developments under the RM-6 that include
48   duplex units then this language would be appropriate to keep those projects moving forward.
49
50   Councilmember McDougal stated that his position is that the City should be leaning toward
51   single family detached units and the exception should be duplexes. Mr. Lethbridge asked if they
     Page | 9 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     October 5, 2021




 1   would want the code to state that duplexes could only be allowed under an SD designation.
 2   Councilmember McDougal said he would like RM-6 zoning to be single family detached units
 3   and have a separate zoning for duplexes.
 4
 5   Councilmember Stewart said that he recognizes that there are developments in the process,
 6   and he asked if they could set a date for the new code to take effect so that these developments
 7   are not affected. Ryan Carter said that his recommendation would be for staff to research
 8   developments in the pipeline and gage an effective date. He explained there is no way to
 9   protect a pending application from being modified but the City can strategically make an
10   effective date and he recommends tabling this to the next meeting.
11
12   Mr. Lethbridge asked if the Council would support the language of splitting the lots between
13   duplexes. Councilmember McDougal said that he is okay with that.
14
15   Mayor Staggs asked how this language affects current projects. He said he thought that most
16   projects had a SD designation. Mr. Lethbridge said that the current language doesn’t allow
17   property to be split between duplexes and that is what will affect current projects from moving
18   forward. He said this language affects the Ivory Development specifically.

20   Councilmember Wells said he agrees with Councilmember McDougal and Stewart. He said that
