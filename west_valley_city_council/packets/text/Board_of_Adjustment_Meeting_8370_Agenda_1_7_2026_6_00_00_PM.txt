 VARIANCES
 B-3-2025  Shane Dougal is requesting a variance from two provisions in Section 7-6-202 of the West
           Valley City Code. This section requires that any detached structure have a minimum setback
           of 3 feet behind the primary dwelling and that the maximum building coverage of all structures
           in the rear yard not exceed 25%. The applicant is asking for a setback variance of 13.5 inches
           and an overall increase of the 25% coverage by 10 feet. The property is located at 3759 South
           5200 West and is zoned R-1-8. Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-
           ut.gov.
