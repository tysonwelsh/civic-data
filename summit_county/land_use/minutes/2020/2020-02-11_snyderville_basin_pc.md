2. Public hearing and possible action regarding proposed amendments to the Silver 
Creek Village Center Development Agreement; located on the southeast corner of 
I-80 and US 40: Matt Lowe, Applicant– Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner     
 
Planner Strader said the applicants are requesting to amend the Silver Creek 
Development Agreement (DA).  The amendments would apply only to Lot 80 of the Lot 
2 Subdivision (Parcel 22.1).  The lot is located at the eastern boundary of the Silver 
Creek Village Center and is 4½ acres in size.  A multi-family development is being

proposed to be located on the lot.  The applicant’s team consists of: Wade Budge, Matt 
Lowe, Eric Langvardt, and Scott Loomis.   
  
Planner Strader said the Commission held a work session about this application on 
February 12, 2019 and a public hearing was held on October 22, 2019, but no action 
was taken.  A slide was shown that listed the requested amendments: 
1. To measure the height from a modified grade. 
2. To permit an increase in height from thirty-two (32) feet to forty-five (45) feet. 
3. To add language to the Development Agreement allowing Parcel 22.1 to “have a 
unit mix between market rate and affordable housing units of sixty-five percent 
(65%) affordable units.”  
4. To remove the maximum lot size.   
 
1. MEASURE THE BUILDING’S HEIGHT FROM A MODIFIED GRADE 
Planner Strader said the applicants would like to add fill dirt to cover existing sewer 
ponds.  They request to measure the building’s height from the top of the fill.  The grade 
will be raised between 8 and 14 feet.  A diagram showed the current method of 
measuring a structure’s height and what is being proposed.  The result of the request is 
essentially granting a height exception.   
 
Planner Strader said Staff believes the development can take place without the need to 
fill the site.  Most of the lot is a large flat area.  The road may need to be located above 
the lot, but there are many areas in the Snyderville Basin where that is the case.      
 
2. ALLOW AN INCREASE IN HEIGHT  
If the Commission believes that fill is required to develop the lot, Staff recommends the 
Commission deny the request to increase the height to 45’.  With the added fill and the 
additional height increase, the height would range from 53’ to 59’.  Planner Strader 
added that the exhibits in the DA indicate that taller buildings will be located in the 
Snyderville Basin Planning Commission 
February 11, 2010 
Page 3 of 38 
 
center of the project and that the density will feather out from the center.  That 
configuration makes the edges of the development transition into the adjacent open 
areas.  A map of the project was shown with this lot being highlighted.   
 
3. ALLOW A MIX OF AFFORDABLE AND MARKET RATE HOUSING  
Planner Strader said the applicant is requesting a mix of 65% affordable housing to 
35% market rate.  Staff asked the applicant to explain why this amendment is being 
requested.  How will the request impact the integration of affordable housing and 
market rate units throughout the rest of the project? 
 
Planner Strader said the DA states that  35% of the first 271 units (95 units) shall be 
affordable housing units.  After that point, no less than 25% of each development shall 
be affordable housing until the total obligation is satisfied.   
 
The DA also states that a variety of unit types and styles will be provided.  There will be 
no more than 60% of one type throughout the project.  She said concentrating the units 
in a limited number of parcels is less likely to result in meeting the requirement to have 
a variety of unit types and styles.  She said the intent of the DA wanted to ensure there 
are a variety of units with a variation in pricing.      
 
4. AN AMENDMENT TO REMOVE THE MAXIMUM SIZE 
Planner Strader said the DA restricts the maximum lot sizes created on this 
parcel to 4,000 square feet.  The applicant states this restriction will not allow 
any type of apartment, condo, or assisted living project.  Only townhomes and 
single family lots would fit.  The applicant thinks this was an error on the 
standards sheet; however, Staff does not believe the maximum lot size was an 
oversight, but was intentional to aide in the feathering of the development.

FINDINGS OF FACT 
1. The property is located at 4275 Quarry Mountain Road within the Rural 
Residential (RR) zoning district.    
2. The property is lot 1 of the Quarry Mountain Ranch amended subdivision and 
consists of 2.31 acres.  There is a platted building envelope of 31,198 square feet 
(0.76 acres) on Lot one.

amended subdivision (Parcel ID QMR-1-AM).

(SPA) Development Agreement (DA) and the Quarry Mountain Ranch SPA Plan 
book of Exhibits, approved by the Eastern Summit County Commissioners on 
August 24, 1998.

recorded on September 15, 2003.
