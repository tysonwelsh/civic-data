                            EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL
                              NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING


  Pursuant to Utah Code Title 17, Chapter 41, Section 302, notice is hereby given that a Public
   Hearing will be held in the Emigration Canyon Fire Station located at 5025 E. Emigration
     Canyon Road, Salt Lake City, UT 84108 during the City Council Meeting on Monday,
                             November 17, 2025, at 7:00 pm MST.
              The purpose of the hearing is to hear public comment on and review:
   -   The proposal to create the agricultural protection area;
   -   The recommendations of the Salt Lake County Advisory Committee and Emigration
       Canyon Planning Commission;
   -   Any requests for modification of the proposal and any objections to the proposal.
 At the date and time fixed for the public hearing all who appear will be given an opportunity to
express their views on subject above. Please note that the City Council has had the opportunity to
      review the previously submitted public comments that were submitted to the Planning
                       Commission, and those comments have been heard.
   PARKING REMINDERS – We encourage those attending to park along Emigration Oaks
   Road, just above the Fire Station. If parking on the main canyon road, your vehicle must fit
                           COMPLETELY within the white fog lines.
  In accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, the City of Emigration Canyon will
 make reasonable accommodations to participate in the hearing. Requests for assistance can be
           made by calling 385-377-9466 within three working days of the meeting.
                             Dated this 4th day of November, 2025
