RESOLUTION NO. __20-04-01_________                    ______________April 15__________, 2020

 A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL CONSENTING
 TO AND AUTHORIZING EXTENSION OF THE DECLARTION OF A PUBLIC HEALTH
      EMERGENCY FOR THE CORONAVIRUS DISEASE OF 2019 (COVID-19)

      WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) declared a public health
emergency for the coronavirus disease of 2019 (“COVID-19”) on March 13, 2020 (the
“Declaration”) pursuant to Chapter 2.86 of the Copperton Metro Township Code (the “Code”);
and

       WHEREAS, consistent with Chapter 2.86 of the Code and consistent with its own terms,
the Declaration expired on April 12, 2020, thirty days after its adoption; and

       WHEREAS, the Declaration may be renewed with consent the Copperton Metro
Township Council (the “Council”) pursuant to Chapter 2.86 of the Code and Utah Code Section
53-2a-208 of Utah’s Disaster Response and Recovery Act; and

      WHEREAS, guidance from the President of the United States received on March 29, 2020
recommended that states continue stay home orders until at least April 30, 2020; and

     WHEREAS, the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation expects the peak of the
COVID-19 pandemic in Utah to be around April 24; and

        WHEREAS, the Council finds that the health, wellness, and safety of Copperton and its
citizens are served by reinstating and continuing the Declaration for at least thirty additional days
beyond its current term; and

        WHEREAS, these conditions continue to constitute a “State of Emergency” within the
intent of the Disaster Response and Recovery Act found in Title 53, Chapter 2a of the Utah Code
Annotated 1953, as amended; and necessitate that Copperton continue the proclamation of a local
public health emergency.

      NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, effective immediately by the
Copperton Metro Township Council that:

       SECTION 1: The Council consents to and hereby reinstates the Declaration and sets the
       new expiration of the Declaration’s term as May 15, 2020; and

       SECTION 2: The Mayor of Copperton may amend the Declaration consistent with Utah’s
       Disaster Response and Recovery Act and Chapter 2.86 of the Code as needed to address
       the coronavirus public health emergency as it evolves; and

       SECTION 3: This resolution shall be filed with the office of the Salt Lake County Clerk
       pursuant to Utah Code Section 53-2a-208(c); and

       SECTION 4: This resolution shall take effect immediately upon its publication pursuant
       to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-711(1) and Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-712.
