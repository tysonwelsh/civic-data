WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 13, 2025

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on August 13, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, County
Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Jason Whipple

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION _OF__RESOLUTION NO. ___R-2025-3575, A _ RESOLUTION
APPOINTING A COUNTY ATTORNEY

Commissioner Iverson thanked everyone in attendance and thanked the candidates for putting their
names forward. Commissioner Snow recommended appointing Jerry Jaeger as the Washington
County Attorney.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to appoint Jerry Jaeger and Approve
Resolution No. R-2025-3575, A Resolution Appointing a County Attorney.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

SWEARING IN OF WASHINGTON COUNTY ATTORNEY

Clerk/Auditor Ryan Sullivan swore in Mr. Jaeger.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:18 p.m.
