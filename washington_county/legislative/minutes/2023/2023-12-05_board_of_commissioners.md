WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
DECEMBER 5, 2023

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 4:00 p.m. on December 5, 2023, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow,
County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

Commissioner Victor Iverson was excused.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3209, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AMENDMENT NUMBER ONE TO THE INTERLOCAL COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT
BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND THE CITY OF ST. GEORGE THAT
CREATED THE WASHINGTON COUNTY INTERLOCAL AGENCY

Mr. Snow explained this amendment relates to an Interlocal agreement that was signed in 1999.
Since that time, the Washington County Interlocal Agency (WCIA) has operated the Dixie Center.
This amendment would modify the Interlocal agreement to make it clear that WCIA would be
dissolved as of December 31, 2023.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3209,
A Resolution Approving Amendment Number One to the Interlocal
Cooperative Agreement between Washington County and the City of St.
George that Created the Washington County Interlocal Agency Subject to
Legal Review. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Almquist voting aye.
