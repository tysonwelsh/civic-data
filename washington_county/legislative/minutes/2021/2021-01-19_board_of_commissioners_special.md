WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
JANUARY 19, 2021

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Almquist at 2:30 p.m. on January 19, 2021, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner
Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Others in attendance included: Zachary Renstrom, Tyce Palmer, Ron Whitehead, and Cory
Cram

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION __AND_ PERIODIC _REVIEW__OF_ _COUNTY _ RESOURCE
MANAGEMENT PLAN (RMP)

The sections that were reviewed included: Ditches and Canals, Fisheries, Floodplains &
River Terraces, Riparian, Water Rights, Wetlands, Wild and Scenic Rivers, Water Quality
and Hydrology.

Each section was reviewed line by line and small revisions regarding wording and
grammatical corrections were suggested.

Updates to the RMP will be brought before the Planning Commission and then to the
County Commission for final approval.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
3:29 pam

CHEYWNNE BENTLEY
DEPUTY CLERK
