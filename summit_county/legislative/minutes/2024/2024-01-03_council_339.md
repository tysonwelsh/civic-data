James Vanderwest told Council why he was making the request. (5:45 PM) Lora Alexander is the property manager for Mr. Vanderwest and supported his request with her personal observations.
Council Members asked questions and commented. (5:52 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to grant a Discretionary Tax Abatement for James Vanderwest, Parcel IRH-FS-F-1 for $1,693.52 Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
