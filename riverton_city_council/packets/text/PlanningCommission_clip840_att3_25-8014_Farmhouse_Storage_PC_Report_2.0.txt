This application is a public hearing and administrative action item.
In reviewing the application, the Planning Commission is serving an administrative function,
decisions are based on substantial evidence.

Example Motions
I move the Planning Commission approve PLZ-26-8014 final commercial site plan for property
located at 12538 South Rhetski Lane with the following conditions:

    1. Approval is conditional upon City Council executing the development agreement and
        conveying property to the developer.
    2. An amended subdivision plat must be recorded prior to issuing building permits for the
        site.
    3. Site Improvements and the Building, including the exterior finish, dumpster enclosures,
        and landscaping, and all other improvements shall be consistent with the plans
        presented to the Planning Commission except where otherwise noted.
    4. Applicant shall correct or resolve the remaining staff redlines.
    5. Building and Site lighting shall be installed and screened according to Riverton City
        Standards.
    6. Roof top mechanical shall be screened from view.
    7. Wall mounted service panels shall be painted to match the building.
    8. The site and structures shall comply with any and all applicable Riverton City Standards
        and ordinances, including the International Building and Fire Codes, and the
        Development Agreement executed between the Riverton City and the Developer.
    9. Storm drainage systems and accommodations shall comply with Riverton City Standards
        and ordinances, and with the recommendations of the Riverton City Engineering
        Division.
    10. The Applicant must create and agree to follow a Long-Term Storm Water Management
        Plan according to Riverton City standards and ordinances prior to receiving building
        occupancy.
    11. The applicant must obtain final approval from utility providers.

Background

This item is the Final Site plan for a proposed storage unit facility which is being developed
under a development agreement for approximately 5.63 acres located at 12270 South 4000
West. The property has been the subject of numerous public hearings before the Planning
Commission and the City Council. The Development Agreement came to the Planning
Commission April 24, 2025 (see attached minutes), and before the City Council on May 20,
2025 (see attached Ordinance No 25-15).

Developing the site required a number of steps including zoning, executing a development
agreement, city disposing property, amending the subdivision plat, securing access to the
neighbors parcel, and approving this Final Site Plan. This Final Site Plan reflects the approvals
and requirements of City Ordinances, standards, and provisions of the Development Agreement
and previously approved preliminary site plan.

City Staff from Engineering, Stormwater, Water, Fire, and Planning have reviewed these plans
and are recommending approval.

This item was noticed to neighbors within 300-ft. No comments were received at the time of
this reports. Staff did receive phone calls questioning why another public hearing was required
and to ask if anything was changing from the preliminary site plan the public saw last spring.

the width of 4000 West. They have to consider the City as a whole and determine where
they can best solve problems. The City’s goal is access management so residents can
travel where they need to without 12600 South being the only option to do so.

                  18. 85. 090 Check cashing regulations.


               1) The Riverton City council finds that the regulation of title lending businesses and check
              cashing businesses is a serious matter which affects the prosperity and welfare of the
              residents of Riverton City. The council further finds that the regulation of title lending
              businesses and check cashing businesses is appropriately the responsibility of the
              governments of the United States and of the state of Utah. The council also finds that current
              regulation and control over title lending businesses and check cashing businesses by the
              federal and state governments is inadequate to protect the welfare of the citizens of Riverton
              City, in that state and federal regulations do not place limitations on the locations wherein
              title lending businesses and check cashing businesses can be found, and further do not limit
              the number of eligible title lending businesses and check cashing businesses within a
              community.
