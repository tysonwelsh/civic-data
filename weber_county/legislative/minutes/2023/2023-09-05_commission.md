G. ACTION ITEMS:

     1. APPROVE AN AGREEMENT WITH WEBER COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT FOR LAW
        ENFORCEMENT SERVICES.
            Chief Aaron Perry (Sheriff’s Department): This a three year agreement with Weber County School District
            for SRO services. This does a good job of clearing up the responsibilities and training required by the SROs
            and things that are not the responsibility of the SROs. Establishes good guidelines for both sides in this
            agreement. This is our standard SRO and does not include recent legislative changes as those are being
            worked out still.

            Commissioner Bolos moved to approve an agreement Weber County School District for law enforcement
            services; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
     2. APPROVE A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BY AND BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY
        AND OGDEN CITY FOR THE PURPOSE OF DISTRIBUTING JAG GRANT FUNDS.
            Chief Aaron Perry (Sheriff’s Department): We are a subrecipient on a Federal JAG grant with Ogden City
            for $17,298. Approximately $5k of that will be sent back to Ogden City for maintaining their simulator at
            the shooting complex with remaining funds going toward offsetting equipment costs.
         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve a Memorandum of Understanding by and between Weber County and
         Ogden City for the purpose of distributing JAG Grant Funds; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    3. APPROVE AGREEMENT WITH UTAH YOUTH RODEO ASSOCIATION TO PROVIDE A
       LOCATION FOR THEIR SEASON ENDING BANQUET.
         Duncan Olsen (GSEC Director): This is a contract ratification for an event that has already happened. Our
         fee ordinance does not cover our hourly structure for an event like this in the exhibit hall.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve an agreement with Utah Youth Rodeo Association to provide a
         location for their season ending banquet; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    4. APPROVE AGREEMENT WITH UTAH YOUTH RODEO ASSOCIATION TO PROVIDE A
       LOCATION FOR THE 2023-2024 SEASON RODEOS.
         Duncan Olsen (GSEC Director): This is for their season next year. This is the same sponsorship from previous
         years.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve an agreement with Utah Youth Rodeo Association to provide a
         location for the 2023-2024 season rodeos; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    5. APPROVE AGREEMENT WITH MAJOR BRENT TAYLOR FOUNDATION TO PROVIDE A
       LOCATION TO HOLD THEIR WEBER REMEMBERS EVENT.
         Duncan Olsen (GSEC Director): We are sponsoring tables and chairs and receiving recognition as one of their

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve an agreement with Major Brent Taylor Foundation to provide a
