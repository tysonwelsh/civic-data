3.     7-Eleven Site Plan for large truck fueling station expansion located at
       approximately 892 E 100 N (Hwy 132), parcel number XA00-0975-11BN2 (Lot 2 of
       the Barbarosa Nephi subdivision)
       a.      Staff Presentation
       Shauna explained that the City received a Site Plan application from 7-Eleven in March
       for an expansion that would include a large vehicle fueling station, located on the lot just
       east of the existing 7-Eleven. The biggest concern to the DRC committee is the potential
       increase of traffic on the existing access roads. There are no current expansion plans for
       the existing building, but there are plans for an exterior upgrade.

       b.     Commission Discussion

        John Ford asked how the flow of traffic will go. Shauna showed the Commission a few
        different options being considered for flow. Alan Hancock said he is concerned about
        the narrowness of the property behind 7-Eleven. The regional director for Dimension
        group explained that there will be some interior remodeling, and also confirmed the
        anticipated flow of traffic. He also confirmed that the existing drive-thru window will not
        be utilized. John Ford asked if this project will facilitate a stop light at the intersection.
        Seth Atkinson said that UDOT will determine that. Cory Thomson said that he doesn’t
        think a fuel station for truck purposes fits in the HC-2 zone. He is also concerned that
        there is an ATV route that runs right along this property. Alan said that his biggest
        concern is that 800 East will not be able to handle that much traffic if it’s not taken care
        of before the project starts. Shauna pointed out that there is already truck traffic in this
        area because of the parking.

        c.     Action Item - Motion
        Ann asked for a motion to accept the 7-Eleven site plan, conditional on them meeting
        any remaining DRC notes including fee in lieu of covering any additional asphalt. Ann
        would also like to add additional direction to staff to bring this up with UDOT.

        Motion: Alan Hancock Second: John Ford Nay: Cory Thomson
        *Roll call was taken.
        Thomson: Nay, Peterson: Aye, Hancock: Aye, Ford: Aye
        Outcome: Motion Carries

4.      CR Circle 3 (CR Circle 2 and Winn Ridge Estates subdivisions amendment),
        including three lots located at approximately 1146 N (previously Lot 60 Winn
Ridge
        XA3A-0630-112WR60) and 1138 N (previously Lot 61 Winn Ridge
        XA3A-0630-112WR61), and 1012 N (Lot 2 CR Circle 2 XA2A-0010-CR22) on 700 E.
        a.      Staff Presentation
        Shauna explained that this is in essence a boundary line adjustment. According to Utah
        State Code, an amendment is required because the boundary is between two
        subdivisions. It helps the County Recorder’s office to trace it, and also helps for future
        research of these areas. Daryl Penrod has been helping with the surveying of this
        property. The plan is to vacate Lots 1 & 2 of the CR Circle 2 Subdivision and Lots 60 &
        61 of the Winn Ridge Subdivision. A new plat would be recorded with a new subdivision
        and a new name. This will most likely be the CR Circle 3 Subdivision and CR Circle 2
        would be abandoned.

        b.     Commission Discussion
        Ann stated that it is very straightforward. There were no other comments or questions
        from the Commission at this time.

        c.    Action Item - Motion
        Ann asked for a motion to vacate Lots 60 & 61 of Winn Ridge, and vacate Lots 1 & 2 of

      CR Circle 2 and give an approval for a final proposed plat for CR Circle 3 or whatever it
      is appropriately named.

      Motion: Cory Thomson         Second: John Ford        Outcome: Motion Carries

5.    Arrow Builders Apartments Site Plan for One (1) Four-Plex located at
      approximately 421 N Main St. (Hwy 28) parcel # XA00-0670-2 with approximately
      .67 acres, recently rezoned and located in the CU-2 zone.
      a.     Staff Presentation
      Shauna stated that this was tabled at the previous meeting due to public comments
      regarding the sewer system. Kyle Marchant submitted a written letter to the Commission
      explaining that there is enough capacity in the 6” line to handle the additional 4 units,
      and more. Shauna stated that there have been some lateral line problems and that is
      never the city’s responsibility. The City is responsible for the main line, and has never
      had a problem with it. She also stated that the City has been working with FEMA to
      make sure there won’t be any flooding problems. A Geotech will be required for this site
      and the structure will be built slab on grade to avoid any basement issues.

      b.     Commission Discussion
      John asked if the drainage would be going towards the hollow. Alan said that it would
      have to be maintained inside the sump pump located on the southwest corner.

      c.      Action Item - Motion
      Ann asked for a motion to accept the Arrow Builders Site Plan, contingent on meeting
      any DRC notes & working with the city to comply with all FEMA requirements during this
      building process.

      Motion: Cory Thomson Second: Ann Peterson Outcome: Motion Carries

6.    Public Hearing - Rezone request for Mt. Peak Development (Terry Messersmith)
for
      property located at approximately Main Street to 100 E and 1475 N to 1500 N from
      HC1 (Highway Commercial 1) zone to a R2-8 (Residential 2-8) zone for the purpose
      of eight (8) units in four (4) residential twinhomes. (This would require a change to
      North Ridge Plat E at a future meeting)
      a.     Open Public Hearing
      Ann opened the public hearing at 7:37pm.

      b.       Staff Presentation
      Shauna went through the zoning history of this property, which is currently zoned as
      HC-1. Terry Messersmith has indicated that he’s been unable to sell this property for a
      commercial purpose and is requesting a rezone to residential. Shauna stated staff would
      like to recommend that the Commission does not recommend this rezone to the CIty
      Council. Staff is concerned about heavy traffic in that area and would like to stick to their

     vision for commercial development in this area. Additionally, this rezone would also
     require 16 cuts to the brand new road for sewer and water laterals. According to Public
     Works, the developer would be required to then rip up and replace the new road.
     Heather Saez asked if the proposed twinhomes are supposed to be the same as the
     ones that are to the north. She expressed concern that the existing units are being used
     as a four-plex instead of twinhomes. Shauna said that the property is zoned for
     twinhomes, and it’s an enforcement issue if they are being used otherwise.

     c.     Close Public Hearing
     Ann closed the public hearing at 7:53pm.

     d.     Commission Discussion
     Ann asked if anyone was leaning towards approving the rezone. Alan stated that his
     opinion has not changed since the last rezone request. Brett Ludlow reminded the
     Commision that zoning is interchangeable. Cory pointed out that this is a new project,
     and Alan stated that the Commission doesn’t have an issue with a rezone if it will benefit
     the community. Jan Horton asked for confirmation on what the city is recommending.

     e.    Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to not accept the zone change application from Mt. Peak
     Development.

     Motion: Alan Hancock      Second: John Ford       Outcome: Motion Carries

7.   Public Hearing - Rezone Request for Desert Stream (Stony Brooks) for property
     located at approximately 25 S 1159 W Parcel # XA00-2282-BW2 of approximately
     4.65 acres from HC1 (Highway Commercial 1) to ID3 (Industrial 3) for the purpose
     of a food-grade manufacturing facility.
     a.     Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the public hearing at 7:56pm.

     b.       Staff Presentation
     Shauna explained that the proposed site plan is for food grade production. This business
     needs more space to do their production, and would be light industrial use. The
     assembly and production they want to do is not approved in the Highway Commercial
     zone. An industrial rezone is necessary for Desert Stream to produce some items. ID-3
     is our lightest manufacturing zone. Greg Johnson explained that they really don’t
     manufacture anything and are actually known as a co-packer.

     c.     Close Public Hearing
     Ann closed the public hearing at 8:07pm.

     d.     Commission Discussion

      Alan asked if the staff has any issues with the request. Shauna stated that there aren’t
      any concerns. The main question was if there would be any output to the sewage
      system. She said that there will actually be a vacuum system that cleans their facility.
      Cory expressed his concerns about this zone change since the Commission had a
      discussion a few months ago, deciding to include light industrial in the Highway
      Commercial zone. He is wondering why a zone change is even necessary. Shauna
      stated that it would be good to have more flexibility since we don’t know what will come
      in the future. Seth also explained that Highway Commercial doesn’t allow for outside
      storage, and ID-3 does.

      e.     Action Item - Motion
      Ann asked for a motion to accept the rezone request for Desert Stream’s 4.6 acres from
      HC-1 to an ID-3.

      Motion: Cory Thomson      Second: Alan Hancock      Outcome: Motion Carries
