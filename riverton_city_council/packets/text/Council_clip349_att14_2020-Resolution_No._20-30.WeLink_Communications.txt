                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-30

A RESOLUTION TO APPROVE NEGOTIATIONS OF A CONTRACT WITH WELINK
COMMUNICATIONS TO DESIGN AND CONSTRUCT A CITY OWNED NETWORK

        WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council has adopted a strategic plan which features a set
of priorities to improve municipal services from the years 2018 to 2022; and

       WHEREAS, the priorities approved by the Council include a description of objectives
and goals which will advance the Councils priorities over time; and

        WHEREAS, Strategic Priority No. 2 is to “Promote a thriving business climate that
supports the needs of Riverton residents,” is supplemented by objective 2.1 to “) Retain, expand,
and attract a mix of high-quality employers and commercial businesses that contribute to the
community’s economic sustainability,” and sets a goal to “explore solutions for a municipally
backed fiber optic network;” and

       WHEREAS, Strategic Priority No. 5 is to “Create a well-connected community with
properly maintained utilities and infrastructure,” is supplemented by objective 5.3 to “Identify
innovative solutions in a continuous effort to reduce cost or provide enhanced services/quality to
residents,” and sets as a goal to, “explore possibility of a municipally owned fiber optic
network;” and

        WHEREAS, Strategic Priority No. 6 is to “Actively engage and inform residents,” is
supplemented by objective 6.1 to “provide opportunities to engage, educate, involve and inform
the community,” and sets as a goal to, “identify viability for online self-service for all city
service offerings opportunities;” and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that by connecting our city
assets, the City will pay less for telecommunications services, get faster speeds, and will be able
to build innovative new methods of delivering services to residents; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that City staff has worked
to fulfill the aforementioned goals by soliciting proposals to assist in the design of a
telecommunications network which directly connects critical City-owned infrastructure; and

       WHEREAS, an evaluation committee consisting of elected officials and employees have
reviewed responses issued under the RFP and recommended an award of the RFP to WeLink
Communications;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council authorizes the City Purchasing Department to negotiate a
          contract with WeLink Communications to design and construct a city owned network.
       2. The City Council will include the funding of this project in next year’s budget.
       3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 21st day of
