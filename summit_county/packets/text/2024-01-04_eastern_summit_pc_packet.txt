Amendment. The Planning Commission is the Final Land Use Authority for all Subdivision Plat

Project Description 
Project Name: Little Dipper Lot 10 Amended 
Applicant(s):   Jack Robert York; Owen and Janet Wood

Location:   7561 Escalante Loop; N/A 
Zone District:   Agricultural 80 (AG-80)

Final Land Use Authority: Planning Commission 
 
Proposal 
The applicant is requesting to adjust the Subdivision boundary along Lot 10. The purpose of the 
proposed amendment is to incorporate a portion of parcel CD-672-A-1 into Lot 10. There is an 
existing Single-Family Dwelling on Parcel CD-672-A-1 that is owned by the property owner of 
Lot 10.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
5
Little Dipper Lot 10 Amended, Page 2 
 
Vicinity Map 
 
  
 
Background 
The Little Dipper Subdivision was recorded on November 25, 1970, Entry Number 112216. 
Currently, Lot 10 is approximately 0.83 acres. With the proposed amendment, Lot 10 would 
increase to approximately 1.35 acres. 
 
The owner of Lot LD-10 owns the existing Single-Family Dwelling (SFD) on Parcel CD-672-A-1. 
According to the County Assessor’s office, the existing Single-Family Dwelling was constructed 
in approximately 1993. There was a building permit (92127) issued for Lot LD-10, but it seems 
the house was constructed across the property line and on parcel CD-672-A-1 instead.  
 
On August 10, 2023, the Planning Department received a Plat Amendment application for the 
above-mentioned parcels. The Applicant proposes to remove approximately 0.52 acres from 
parcel CD-672-A-1 and add that acreage to Parcel LD-10.  
 
Analysis and Findings 
The Planning Commission may approve, approve with conditions, or deny the proposed Plat 
Amendment based upon written findings of fact according to each of the following standards 
(see 11-4-5). It is the responsibility of the applicant to provide written and graphic evidence 
demonstrating compliance. 
 
Standard 1: The Land Use Authority is satisfied that the public interest will not be materially 
injured by the proposed vacation, alteration or amendment, and there is good cause for the 
vacation, alteration or amendment, the Land Use Authority, may vacate, alter or amend the 
final subdivision plat, or any portion of the final subdivision plat; COMPLIES

Little Dipper Lot 10 Amended, Page 3 
 
Analysis: There is good cause for the amendment as the proposed plat amendment 
would bring an existing house into the parcel boundaries of the homeowner’s land. The 
adjustment does not result in an increase in density. 
 
Standard 2: No subdivision plat amendment shall be approved which results in an increase in 
density unless the requirements set forth in subsection E6 of this section for a re-subdivision 
have been satisfied; COMPLIES  
 
Analysis: The proposed amendment does not create new density. COMPLIES

Background 
 
The Promontory Development Agreement was approved on January 2, 2001. It provided for 
885 estate lots as part of a base density, 716 incentive density lots, two hotels/300 resort units, 
equestrian facility, up to five golf club houses, and other accessory uses on approximately 6500 
acres with no less than 3900 acres remaining as passive open space. Of the 1901 total units in 
the density pool, 1,462 units have been approved. In addition to the approved units, the 
Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation for the 2-lot Mountain Crest 
Subdivision on November 16, 2023. The development parameters for this project are 
specifically set forth in the Development Agreement. 
 
Analysis and Findings 
 
The applicant is using Resort Unit density from the existing pool of density. The development is 
not located on critical lands.  
 
Service Providers 
 
Staff has not received any negative service provider comments which would hinder the 
proposal.
