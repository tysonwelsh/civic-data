Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: July 28, 2022
Re: Scalar New Office Building Site Plan Amendment and Conditional Use Permit Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plant Amendment and Conditional Use Permit for approximately 1.32 acres located on the south side of Pioneer Road, at approximately 874 E. Pioneer Road. The property is currently zoned TC. The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan Amendment be approved to allow for the development of the site with a new office building. As part of the Amended Site Plan the applicant is also requesting approval of a Conditional Use Permit to install fencing in excess of six feet (6’), up to a height of ten feet (10’), around a proposed basketball and sports court. The findings are as follows: Parking Stall Deviation
For Approval:
1. Adequate parking will be provided.
2. The total number of spaces that would otherwise be required is overly burdensome for the site specific factors of the application.
3. Trade-offs between businesses which are open when others are closed will not overburden the parking provided.
4. There is adequate availability of approved on street or shared parking for all associated uses.
5. Site and or use specific conditions or factors do not provide for compliance with the parking requirements outlined in DCMC section 9-25-100, Table 9-25-1.
6. Transportation alternatives, including proximity to transit stations will provide adequate transportation alternatives to offset any reduction in parking
7. Any potential future expansion or addition to the development will have or will provide adequate parking for that expansion or addition.
8. ADA compliant parking requirements are not proposed for adjustment.
For Denial:
1. Adequate parking will not be provided.
2. The total number of spaces that would otherwise be required is not overly burdensome for the site specific factors of the application.
3. Trade-offs between businesses which are open when others are closed will overburden the parking provided.
4. There is not adequate availability of approved on street or shared parking for all associated uses.
5. Specific Conditions of the site or use do not preclude compliance with the parking requirements outlined in DCMC section 9-25-100, Table 9-25-.
6. Transportation alternatives, including proximity to transit stations will not provide adequate transportation alternatives to offset any reduction in parking
7. Any potential future expansion or addition to the development will be unable to provide adequate parking for that expansion or addition.
Parking Diamonds
For Approval:
1. The site requires a deviation from strict compliance to reduce parking.
2. The site would require a deviation from strict compliance to reduce parking in order to implement such landscaping.
3. Constraints of the site would necessitate the elimination of required parking spaces or reduction of site boundary landscaping buffers in order to facilitate such landscaping.
For Denial:
1. The site does not require a deviation from strict compliance to reduce parking.
2. The site would not require a deviation from strict compliance to reduce parking in order to implement such landscaping.
3. Site constraints would not necessitate the elimination of required parking spaces or reduction of site boundary landscaping buffers in order to facilitate such landscaping.
Roofline and Parapet Deviation
For Approval:
1. The deviation is consistent with the purpose and intent of the policies and development standards pertaining to the applicable zoning district or land use category.
2. The deviation is in the benefit of the health, safety and welfare of the city and the project.
3. The proposed development meets a minimum of 3 criteria from the list in DCMC Subsection 9-23-030(B).
For Denial:
1. The deviation is not consistent with the purpose and intent of the policies and development standards pertaining to the applicable zoning district or land use category.
2. The deviation is not in the benefit of the health, safety and welfare of the city and the project.
Significant Horizontal Façade Variation Deviation
For Approval:
1. The deviation is consistent with the purpose and intent of the policies and development standards pertaining to the applicable zoning district or land use category.
2. The deviation is in the benefit of the health, safety and welfare of the city and the project.
3. The proposed development meets a minimum of 3 criteria from the list in DCMC Subsection 9-23-030(B).
For Denial:
1. The deviation is not consistent with the purpose and intent of the policies and development standards pertaining to the applicable zoning district or land use category.

Conditional Use
For Approval:
1. Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
For Denial:
1. Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
Site Plan Amendment
For Approval:
1. The proposal represents development of the entire site at one time.
2. The proposed plans conform to applicable standards set forth in DCMC Title 9, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3. The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
4. The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
5. The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of the DCMC, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns.
For Denial:
1. The proposed plans do not conform to applicable standards set forth in DCMC Title 9, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
2. The proposed development plans do not meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
3. The public facilities and services in the area are not adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
4. The proposed development plans do not comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of the DCMC, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns.
