STUDY SESSION

Place:           Canceled

REGULAR MEETING

NOTICE OF ALL-ELECTRONIC MEETING
To minimize the potential spread of COVID-19, and as authorized by Utah Governor Gary Herbert’s March 18,
2020 Executive Order, the Orem City Planning Commission meeting on August 19, 2020 will be an all-electronic
meeting. There will be no public access to the Orem City Council chambers. Those desiring to view or participate in
the meeting must do so electronically. You can view the meeting live at orem.org/meetings. If you want to make a
comment, please use: orem.org/comments or PlanningCommissionPublicComments@orem.org

Comments will be accepted before and during the meeting. All comments received will be read into the record
during the live meeting. Once an item has been voted on, that item will be closed to additional comments. Thank
you for your cooperation during these challenging times.

Place:           Orem City Council Chambers - Electronic

At 4:30 p.m.     Chair Cochran called the Planning Commission meeting to order and asked Ms. Jensen, Planning
                 Commission member, to offer the invocation.

Those present: Mickey Cochran, Planning Commission Chair; Ryan L. Clark, Development Services Director;
               Jason W. Bench, Planning Division Manager; Cheryl Vargas and Jake Harding, Associate Planners
               and Steve Earl, Legal Counsel

                 Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer and Mike Staker,
                 Planning Commission members and Terry Peterson, City Council Liaison (via telephone)

Those excused: Sam Kelly, City Engineer; Brady Hale, Traffic Engineer; Grant Allen and Matthew Taylor Long
               Range Planners

Chair Cochran asked Mr. Harding to introduce Agenda Item 3.1:

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Learning Dynamics Preschool for the Planning Commission to approve the site
plan of Learning Dynamics Preschool located at 1196 W 950 North in the PD22 zone.

                                             Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to develop the site
                                             with a pre-school. As part of the improvements they will install a new
                                             sidewalk to the south of the project and will also install a sidewalk
                                             north of the preschool to the property line in preparing for future
                                             development.

                                             Architecture: The proposed one-story structure will be 5,297 square
                                             feet and have a maximum height of 23’6”. The roof will be typical
                                             non-asphalt roof tiles consistent with the PD22 zone requirements.
                                             The siding will be stucco and ledge stone.

Parking: They have met all the required parking requirements. Twenty-seven spaces were required, and they have
forty-two existing plus fourteen new places for a total of fifty-six parking spaces.

Dumpster: The dumpster is in the northwest corner of the project and enclosed as required by code.

Fencing: There is no external fence for this project as the children are not allowed outside and must remain inside at
all times.

Landscaping: The overall landscape plan complies with applicable City and PD22 codes.

Transportation/Engineering: No Concerns.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the site plan of Learning Dynamics Preschool located at
1196 West 950 North in the PD22 zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Harding.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to speak via telephone. John and Cheryl Lant introduced themselves. They have
had the preschool in Orem for thirty years and this is just a relocation of the preschool. They feel they will add to the
area. Mr. Spencer said it is nice to have this weedy lot turned into something nice. The site plan shows grass in the
back of the property, but it slopes down. Mrs. Lant said it will be grass on a hill. Mr. Harding said no children will be
outside playing at the preschool, they will be inside only.

Ms. Jensen asked if the preschool will be the same size or are they expanding in terms of size of the building compared
to what they have now. Ms. Lant said they have three hundred eighty-four children currently enrolled in the school
and they will run that same program, but this will be a much better location. The children are not all there at the same
time.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did,
he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Spencer moved to approve the site plan of Learning Dynamics Preschool
located at 1196 West 950 North in the PD22 zone. Mr. Cook seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Mickey
Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer and Mike Staker. The motion
passed unanimously.

Minutes: The Planning Commission reviewed the minutes from the previous meeting. Chair Cochran then called for
a motion and moved to approve the meeting minutes for August 5, 2020. Ms. Jensen seconded the motion. Those
voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer and Mike
Staker. The motion passed unanimously.

Mr. Clark said the day after Labor Day the new swimming pool at the fitness center will be opening. He would like
to add a time to the agenda when the Planning Commission members can all come on a tour of the fitness center.
Thank you for your service on the Planning Commission. Mr. Cochran asked when the new library auditorium will
open. Mr. Clark said the library hall is scheduled for an October 20, 2020 grand opening.

Adjourn: Chair Cochran called for a motion and moved to adjourn. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting
aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer and Mike Staker.
The motion passed unanimously.

Adjourn: 4:47 p.m.
