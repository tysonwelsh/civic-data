IX. DISCUSSION ITEM
    A. DISCUSS PROPOSED FY2O21 BUDGET AMENDMENTS TO THE
             GENERAL FUND AND OTHER FUNDS AS NECESSARY
Kyle Maurer said Staff has identified a number of budget amendments that are needed
to ensure good budgeting practices are followed and appropriate financial controls are
maintained. The amendments fall into the following categories:

1.       Salary Adjustments - These adjustments are made to align projected salary costs
         with budget. These variances typically stem from terminated employee vacation
         payouts, insurance changes, and new employees' starting salary higher or lower
         than budget. Savings from various personnel line items will cover the line items
         projected to be over budget.

2        lnsurance Proceeds     -
                                The City has received approximately $95,000 from
         insurance companies to cover damages and other costs (such as litigation). A
         budget amendment is proposed to allocate budget to cover these expenses.

3        Harvest Days Funding - Adjustments to fund the 2021 Harvest Days celebration;
         to be funded with greater than anticipated sales tax revenue ($56,000).
