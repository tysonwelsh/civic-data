FROM:          Trace Robinson, Public Works Director/City Engineer

ITEM:          Approval of a Resolution for the addition of Exhibit B, entitled “Riverton City
               Aesthetic and Landscape Betterments” to the Master Utility Agreement between
               the Utah Department of Transportation and Riverton City associated with the
               12600 S, Bangerter interchange project.

SUMMARY:

Included with the funding of the Bangerter/12600 S interchange project is $100,000 for the City
to use as Betterments for the enhancement of the aesthetics of the intersection. UDOT’s
consultant has submitted a schedule of Aesthetic and Landscape Betterments they would like the
City to approve and add to the Master Utility Agreement. These improvements are as follows:
     1. Street name on bridge parapets.
     2. City logo on two ramps
     3. Black powder coat all galvanized railings.
     4. Black powder coated signal poles, mast arms, and lights.
     5. Black powder coated sign poles with decorative slip bases, and dome caps.
If approved an exhibit will be added to the Master Utility Agreement as a Betterment to the project.
At the City’s request, additional items can be added to the list. The cost of Betterments will be
subtracted from the $100,000 allocation until the money has been spent. At that time the City will
be responsible for all cost of additional Aesthetic and Landscape Betterments.




FISCAL IMPACT: Anticipated to be $0.00

FUNDING SOURCE: NA

ATTACHMENTS:
   1. Exhibit B, Riverton City Aesthetic and Landscape Betterments
   2. Copy of Original Master Utility Agreement

approval; and

        WHEREAS, the City feels these Betterments will enhance the appearance of the
interchange and aid in the branding of the City; and

      WHEREAS, Riverton City Council finds that the attached exhibit establishes municipal
improvements which Riverton City desires UDOT to construct.

       1.     The Riverton City Council hereby approves the addition of Exhibit “B” entitled
              the “Riverton City Aesthetic and Landscape Betterments” and allow UDOT to
              include said exhibit with the Master Utility Agreement attached hereto.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
