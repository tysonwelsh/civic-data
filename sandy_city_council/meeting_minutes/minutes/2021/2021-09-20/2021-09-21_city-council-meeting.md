2.         21-358                 Presentations from Save Our Canyons, Save Not Pave, and Wasatch 
                                  Backcountry Alliance regarding transportation options for Little Cottonwood 
                                  Canyon
                   Attachments:   SOC Sandy City

                                  Sandy City Council Presentation
                                  WBA LCC Transportation presented to Sandy City

                                  Council Member Zoltanski introduced the following presenters: Carl Fisher with Save our 
                                  Canyon. Micki Harris with Save not Pave, and Brad Rutledge with Wasatch Back Country 
                                  Alliance.

                                  Mr. Fisher with Save Our Canyons is a local non-profit dedicated to protecting the 
                                  wildness and beauty of the Wasatch Mountains. Mr. Fisher provided historical data, 
                                  visitation stats and detailed recreation activities in the canyons. He reviewed the 
                                  concerns and issues with the development in the canyons.  

                                  Ms. Harris with Save not Pave spoke about her organizations concerns with the gondola 
                                  transportation option and the plans for Wasatch Blvd. 

                                  Mr. Brad Rutledge with Wasatch Backcountry Alliance spoke about the transportation in 
                                  Little Cottonwood Canyon, the risks and offered common sense solutions.

                                  Council moved to Time Certain Items, Public Hearing and heard comments for Item 6: 
                                  Amendments Related to Accessory Apartments.

5.         21-360                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Council Chair Stroud invited the public to participate in general citizen comments.

                                  Mr. Craig Johnson thinks the city has done an excellent job with the installation of 
                                  pickleball courts around the City. He offered suggestions to improve the access and 
                                  safety of the pickleball courts.

                                  Mr. Frank Prater spoke about the canyon visitors. He supports the gondola transportation 
                                  option. 

                                  Mr. Brent Lange is in favor of the gondola option, citing the safety features the gondola 
                                  option offers.

                                  Mr. Bruce Ward thinks the buses will create more pollution to the area. We have at 
                                  times, the worst pollution in the nation. 

                                  Mr. Brandon Sheranian thinks if we build the gondola, more people will come. He thinks 
                                  people will continue to drive up the canyon road even with the gondola. The entire canyon 
                                  is the destination.

                                  Mr. Sam Clark enjoys skiing in Snowbird and thinks access to the canyon is a good 
                                  thing. He is in support of the gondola option. 

                                  Mr. Patrick Egbert is opposed to adding busses and is in favor of the gondola option 
                                  because of safety and access. 

                                  Mr. Robert Douglass supports a scalable option. It is important for the Council to take a 
                                  stand on this.

                                  Ms. Ellen Birrell thinks the City has an impact on UDOT's decision. She spoke about the 
                                  citizen impact on decisions made by other cities. She is opposed to the gondola option.

                                  Mr. Jeff Mansell wants the canyons to stay beautiful. He is in support of the gondola 
                                  option.

                                  Mr. Jason Stowell thinks everyone cares about the canyons and encouraged the 
                                  community to be open to transportation options.

                                  Mr. Matt Ireland spends a lot of time in the canyon. He spoke about the growth in the 
                                  valley. He is in support of the gondola.

                                  Mr. Shawn Marquardt offered to provide information on the gondola option. He thinks the 
                                  gondola would help solve the transportation issues and help preserve Little Cottonwood 
                                  Canyon.

                                  Mr. Colby Rollins is in support of the gondola option which he believes is a cost effective 
                                  option.

                                  Mr. Brent Christasen thinks the busses will increase congestion. He is in support of the 
                                  gondola.
