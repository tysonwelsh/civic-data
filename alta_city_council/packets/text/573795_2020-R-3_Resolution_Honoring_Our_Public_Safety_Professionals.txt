                      TOWN OF ALTA RESOLUTION 2020-R-3


        A RESOLUTION HONORING OUR PUBLIC SAFETY
     PROFESSIONALS AND THOSE WHO ASSISTED DURING THE
    UNPRECEDENTED STORM OF FEBRUARY 5-8, 2020 IN LITTLE
                  COTTONWOOD CANYON

        WHEREAS, Little Cottonwood Canyon including the Town of Alta experienced
sustained snowfall, gusting and sustained winds, and severe avalanche conditions from
Wednesday, February 5 through Saturday, February 8, 2020;

        WHEREAS, the 41” of snow that fell during this period, 6.79” of snow-water
equivalent1, the longest period of sustained westerly winds in the history of the Alta Ski
Area records, and the storm conditions caused numerous avalanches which snapped trees,
buried the road numerous times, and hit a residential cabin;

       WHEREAS, 48 avalanches occurrences were recorded by Utah Department of
Transportation (UDOT) Avalanche Safety over State Route 210 (SR-210), 21 of which
hit SR-210, 15 of which were destructive force 3 or larger avalanches, and 4 natural
avalanches crossed the closed highway including at Tanners Gulch, White Pine, and
Cottonwood Draw;

        WHEREAS, SR-210 which terminates in Alta and which is Alta’s only route of
access to the Salt Lake Valley was closed due to avalanche hazard for 54 hours, with
approximately 4,850' of road impacted by avalanche debris as deep as 15’;

        WHEREAS, interlodge travel restrictions were enforced for almost 50 hours, the
longest such period in recent memory, requiring numerous visitors, residents and staff to
stay indoors during this extended period;

         WHEREAS, Town of Alta Marshal Mike Morey, Deputy Marshals, Dispatchers,
Town of Alta Staff, Utah Department of Transportation Avalanche Forecasters and
Mitigation Crews, Alta Ski Lifts Management, Alta Ski Lift Snow Safety Personnel, Alta
Ski Lifts and independent snow removal staff, the Lodges, Property Managers,
Firefighters and Alta Clinic staff provided a coordinated response and worked together
tirelessly over this period to clear the road, support those interlodged, prioritize the health
and safety of those in the Town, give updates to the public and respond to the needs of
the people in the Town of Alta and the contributions and sacrifices made by those
responders;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Town Council acknowledges the
work and sacrifice of the Town of Alta Marshal Mike Morey, Deputy Marshals,
Dispatchers, Town of Alta Staff, Utah Department of Transportation Avalanche
Forecasters and Mitigation Crews, Alta Ski Lift Snow Safety Personnel, Alta Ski Lifts
and independent snow removal staff, the Lodges, Property Managers, Firefighters and
Alta Clinic staff who ensured the safety of the guests, workers and residents interlodged
in the Town of Alta;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Town Council acknowledges those who
responded to the extraordinary event caused by the avalanches which hit and threatened
those in the Town and worked tirelessly to clear the roads;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Town Council gratefully honors and thanks
those who carried out the critical role of protecting and ensuring public safety during this
period.

   PASSED AND APPROVED THIS 12th DAY OF FEBRUARY, 2020.
