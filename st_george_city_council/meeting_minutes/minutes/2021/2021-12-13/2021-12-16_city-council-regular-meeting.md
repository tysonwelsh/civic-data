PRESENTATION OF THE CAFR REPORT FOR FY2021:
     Presentation of the audited Comprehensive Annual Financial Report (CAFR)
     for Fiscal Year 2021.

      Administrative Services Director Trevor Coombs thanked staff members involved
      with the audit for their many hours spent on working with the auditors. He then
      invited Steve Palmer with Hinton Burdick CPA's & Advisors who outlined the findings
      from the audit. The City was given an unmodified opinion, and no items of any
      significance of items needed to be reported on compliance. One minor item found
      was that the CDA fund had a deficit fund balance; this is a result of the St. George
      Inn and alleyway project. On the federal single audit, there was one finding
      regarding the FTA grant timing.

      After being asked by Mayor Randall, Mr. Coombs explained due to Covid, the FTA
      grant was extended; however, staff should have requested an extension in writing.
      At that time, there was a change in management at Suntran and this letter was
      missed. Staff has since requested an extension.

COMPLETE STREETS POLICY:
     Consider approval of a proposed Complete Streets Policy which provides
     additional oversight of planning and designing of City streets.

      Public Works Director Cameron Cutler presented the ordinance amendment to adopt
      the Complete Streets Policy and to create the Complete Streets Committee. He
      showed the proposed language for the ordinance amendment. He also showed and
      outlined the proposed Complete Streets Policy. After being asked by Councilmember
      Hughes, Mr. Cutler explained most of the elements in the proposed policy are already
      being utilized; the policy simply formalizes the elements. He explained the “shalls”
      have been removed and noted there is an option to receive input from the committee
      for proposed projects as needed. The policy balances out modes of transportation.

      Councilmember Larkin commented that the policy brings back the balance with
      regards to modes of transportation and coordination. Historically, streets were able
      to be used by all forms of transportation; however, during the 50’s and 60s’s,
      streets have become auto centric. This removed community spaces where residents
      could walk or use other modes of transportation.

      Councilmember Hughes agreed with Councilmember Larkin; however, he believes
      the City needs to be cautious when “dieting down” streets due to the safety factor.

      Councilmember Larkin noted data has shown streets are safer when “dieted” as
      drivers tend to decrease their speed. The goal is not to get drivers from point A to
      point B as quickly as they can; the goal is to get drivers there safely.

      Councilmember McArthur commented the City is a recreation destination. He likes
      the proposed policy.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Larkin to approve the
                    ordinance amending Title 7, Chapter 1, Section 1 of City code
                    pertaining to Supervision of Streets to incorporate and adopt a
                    Complete Streets Policy.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember McArthur.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a roll call vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.
