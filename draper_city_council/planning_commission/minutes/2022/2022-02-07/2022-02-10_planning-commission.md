   2.      Public Hearing: MD Aesthetics Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
           (Administrative Item)

           On the request of Debi Babinski representing MD Aesthetics LLC, a request for a
           Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit to operate a personal care service for a home
           based business, 11815 S Cottage View Ln., known as application USE-0180-2021,
           Staff Contact: Todd A. Draper (801) 576-6335, todd.draper@draperutah.gov.

Todd Draper, Planner III presented the staff report. He mentioned this is a request for a home-
based business to provide personal care services to clients for Botox and other services by means
of injection. He presented an aerial view of the property and mentioned it is located in the Cottages
Development. The property is designated as Medium High Density in the General Plan Land Use
Map and zoned R-3. He showed the proposed site plan and indicated two parking spaces will be
provided for clients in front of the garage. The clinic room is located adjacent to the front entrance.
He presented the floor plan and showed the Botox room.

Commissioner Hawker asked about the HOA rules and regulations and what is required to obtain
the conditional use permit.

Mr. Draper responded that HOA rules are not enforced by the city and cannot be taken into account
in the Planning Commission decision.

Commissioner Hawker mentioned the applicant would have to go to the HOA separately.

Commissioner Fowler directed Todd to the home occupation code subsection M which states no
customers are allowed and asked for clarification.

Mr. Draper responded the conditional use permit allows applicants to deviate from the standards
of the code. Customers would only be allowed through an appointment, one client per hour, and
will not exceed seven per week.

Commissioner Fowler indicated the code is confusing.

Commissioner Squire mentioned this is a normal request if customers are coming to the home.

Mr. Draper stated the home occupations the Planning Commission sees are conditional use permit
requests that are seeking a deviation from the code.

Commissioner Fowler stated that subsection J states that traffic and parking shall not be in excess
of what is typically allowed in the zone. She stated this is a limited access neighborhood with a
gate and code and believes there is overlap with HOA rules.

Chair Adams invited the applicant to speak.

Debi Babinski, applicant stated she is not trying to run a busy business. She stated she typically
goes to people’s homes and there would be limited clients coming to her home. She indicated she
spoke with Mr. Harrison regarding concerns with safety and traffic. She noted the gate code would
not be provided to clients and she is planning to buzz them in. There is parking near the club house
where clients could park.

Chair Adams commented the driveway is shared and asked how long a home visit takes.

Ms. Babinski stated the appointments are about 20 minutes and at a maximum there would be two
clients per day. She mentioned the HOA president provided consent for the business.

Commissioner Hawker asked if multiple people show up in one car.

Ms. Babinski stated there is usually one person at a time.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing.

in the year and did not have issues with people parking in the driveway. She stated she has now
been receiving calls from neighbors that there are problems with parking. She indicated their
CC&Rs are for private use only and not for businesses. She noted the situation has not been

3.     Public Hearing: Airborne-Alcatraz Re-zone (Legislative Item)

       On the request by Stew Knight of KREA, representing the property owner to rezone the
       property from CR to CG (General Commercial). No new development or change of users
       is proposed at this time. The property is located at approximately 12674 S. Pony Express
       Road. Application TEXTMAP-200-2021. Staff Contact: Maryann Pickering at (801) 576-
       6391 or maryann.pickering@draperutah.gov.

Maryann Pickering, Planner, presented the staff report. She indicated the zoning for the property
is CR. The land use designation is Community Commercial. The property is located on Pony
Express Road. The applicant is requesting to rezone the property from CR to CG. There are no
development proposals at this time. They are looking for uses that would work for the future is
Airborne and Alcatraz go away.

Commissioner Hawker asked if they received public comment.

Ms. Pickering stated they have not received any.

Commissioner Squire asked if the rezone would increase traffic to the property.

Chair Adams invited the applicant to speak.

Applicant, Stew Knight asked if they had any questions for him. He mentioned that this is a
preemptive move to assist them in their planning. The current tenants plan to be in the area for a
while.

Chairman Adams opened the public hearing. There were no comments. He closed the public
hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Fowler motioned to forward a positive recommendation to the city
council for the Airborne-Alcatraz zoning map request, as requested by Stew Knight of
KREA, Application TEXTMAP-200-2021, based on the findings and subject to the
conditions listed in the staff report, dated February 1, 2022.

Second: Commissioner Hawker

Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 5-0 in favor of the motion.
Commissioners Fowler, Squire, Hawker, Bingham and Van Hoff voted “Aye”.

4.     Planning Commission Bylaws and Rules of Procedure Amendment (Administrative
       Action)

       On the request of Draper City for an amendment to the Planning Commission Bylaws and
       Rules of Procedure. Staff Contact is Mike Barker

Mike Barker, City Attorney commented that they have been working on this project for a while.
He reviewed the redlines with the Planning Commission. The purpose of going through the by-
laws is to make them easier to read and conform to what they Planning Commission is doing in
practice. He mentioned the strike through language is proposed for removal. He indicated that by-
laws are advisory but they try to stick to them. He mentioned the appointment process for the chair
and vice-chair positions had several steps so they simplified these.

Commissioner Squire mentioned that if there are no term limits, and asked if there are rules beside
from these that they need to abide by.

Chair Adams stated the chair and vice-chair positions are for one year. The Planning Commission
is appointed to a four-year term. He asked if there was anything regarding closing the meeting.

Mike Baker stated they removed items that are redundant. He clarified the procedures for tie
breaking and absence of the chair. He mentioned if they do not have a chair they can still operate
until they appoint a new chair. He stated that the per-diem will only be provided for those in
attendance.

He continued that he has cleaned up the conflict-of-interest issue. He mentioned the by-laws have
been written to shield the Planning Commission from being lobbied.

Chair Adams stated that he directs people to the planner over projects.

Commissioner Squire asked if they receive a phone call and explain the process should they be
disqualified from participating in the project.

Mike Baker mentioned they should disclose ex parte discussions and may explain the process to
on how to provide comments.

Commissioner Hawker asked about past meetings he has had that may impact current decisions
and explained that is why he has chosen to miss some meetings.

Mike Barker suggested that commissioners contact him prior to the meeting.

Chair Adams asked regarding Council members that contact the Planning Commission regarding
their reasons for recommending a certain issue.

Mike Barker stated that this is fine since the Planning Commission is no longer reviewing the
application.

Chair Adams asked regarding commissioners that move out of the city.

Mike Barker stated moving out of the city means the commissioner has resigned. He mentioned
that alternate members only participate if they are sitting. If they are not sitting, they should be
silent observers.

Chair Adams stated they have been advised in the past to not make comments to the city council.

Mike Barker stated if a commissioner declares a conflict of interest their seat stays vacant.

Chair Adams asked what happens if this vacancy results in a lack of quorum.

Mike Barker responded they would have to continue that item to a later date. It would be helpful
to know this prior to the meeting. He discussed protocols for public hearings and indicated it is
difficult to know if someone is speaking for a group receiving five minutes of speaking time. He
is proposing everyone receives three minutes.

Commissioner Squire asked if an applicant can request a continuance.

Jennifer Jastremsky stated the applicant can have one continuance but the Planning Commission
would need to vote to continue an item a second time.

Commissioner Squire asked if the postponement is a continuance and if they would have to make
a motion.

Mike Barker commented the commission would have to make a motion if they choose to continue
the item. He indicated they could add timeframes for the developer to request a continuance. He
stated he can clarify this in the bulk text amendment number three.

The commission discussed procedural issues dealing with removing someone from a meeting, the
public and commissioners. Mike Barker explained when re-noticing is required for projects. It was
requested that adjournment be required once agenda items are completed. They discussed adding
the ability to abstain into the bylaws and discussed situations where a commissioner would rather
not vote even if there was not a conflict of interest.

Mike Barker indicated roll call votes would be required. He commented if an item is continued,
they should strive to have the same members hear the same item again.

Commissioner Fowler asked if there was an alternate on the board during the continuance would
that person sit again.

Mike Barker indicated the rules don’t contemplate this. There are times when an alternate is sitting
and the regular member is not in attendance at the beginning, but when they show up, the alternate
will be removed after the item is finished.
