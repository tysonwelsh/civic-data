This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on
reasonably debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City
Council which has final decision authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motions
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-22-4008 Rezone of
.45-acres of land located at 1420 West 12600 South, thus applying the Pedestrian-Oriented
Overlay to the C-PO (Professional Office Commercial) zoning.

I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-22-4008 Rezone of .45-
acres of land located at 1420 West 12600 South, thus denying the Pedestrian-Oriented Overlay
to the C-PO (Professional Office Commercial) zoning.


Background

This is a public hearing for an application to apply a zoning overlay to .45-acres of land located
at 1420 West 12600 South. The property has frontage on the north side of 12600 South. The
rezone includes all, or portions of, parcels 27-27-481-005, & -006 as shown on the attached
exhibit. The property is rectangular in shape and is roughly 132-ft wide and 108-ft deep. The
applicant wishes to add this overlay in order to expand and add to their building.

The property is currently zoned C-PO (Professional Office Commercial). Most of the
surrounding properties are zoned Community Commercial or Medium High Density Residential.
The current General Plan Designation for the property and area is “Medium High Density
Residential” and “Community Commercial” which was set with the August 2020 General Plan
Amendment.

This application was noticed on the city web-page and public noticing websites. Direct mailers
were sent to property owners within 1,000 feet. At the time of this report no written comments
had been submitted.
