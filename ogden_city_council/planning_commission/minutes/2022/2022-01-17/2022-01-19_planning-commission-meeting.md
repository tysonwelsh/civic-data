1.   Request for Encroachment Permit to allow transformer and screening fence to extend into the public right-
     of-way at 2955 Washington Boulevard. (Attachment 1)

     Mr. Bill Knowlton stated he is in the process of building a new senior living apartment building on
     Washington Boulevard and due to regulations of Rocky Mountain Power, the only location for the power
     transformer is in front of the building. It is proposed to be located on the northeast corner of the building,,
     and will be 4’ from the sidewalk, but will extend 5’ beyond the property line into the public right-of-way. It
     will then be screened from the public view. It will not affect the use of the sidewalk, as it would be 4’ from
     the walkway. He also showed other locations in the downtown where railings, utility boxes and furniture
     extend into the right-of-way.

     Commissioner Southwick asked if the vault is underground, Mr. Knowlton responded while the vault is
     underground, the transformer is above ground and visible, but will be screened as required by the City’s
     ordinances.

     Mr. Montgomery stated the City Ordinances requires all utilities to be screened from the public view. The
     enclosure and gate would encroach into the public right-of-way. The underground vault will be 20’x35’ and
     the location proposed will satisfy the requirements of Rocky Mountain Power. He described the
     connections and appearance proposed. He indicated the Commission is to determine there is a good
     cause for the use of the public right-of-way and will not interfere with the use of the right-of-way by either
     pedestrians or vehicles, or with existing utilities. He stated there had been other locations considered, but
     this was the only option approved by the power company. The encroachment is 4’5” into the public right-
     of-way and will not interfere with the use of the sidewalk or street by either pedestrians or vehicles. Staff
     recommendation is for approval. Staff recommendation is for approval.

     Commission asked about the connections and whether any wiring would be visible. It was indicated the
     wiring into the transformer will be serviced in the vault. It also was indicated the location proposed does
     not affect the security of the building.
MOTION:        A motion was made by Commissioner Blaisdell to recommend approval of the
               encroachment permit as requested based on the findings the construction of the building
               and alternating proposals from Rocky Mountain Power created a situation without other
               options becomes a good cause for the encroachment, its location will not interfere with
               the use of the public way by either pedestrian or vehicular travel, and the encroachment
               for utilities is an appropriate use of the public right-of-way. Motion was seconded by
               Commissioner Southwick and passed unanimously with Commissioners Blaisdell,
               Sandau, Schade, Southwick and Graf voting aye.

There being no further business before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at 6:00 p.m.
