CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL MEETING
JANUARY 28, 2020

The Cache County Council convened in a regular session on January 28, 2020 at 5:00 p.m.
in the Cache County Council Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.

ATTENDANCE:

Chairman: Karl B. Ward

Vice Chair: Gina H. Worthen

Council Members: Paul R. Borup, Barbara Tidwell, & Gordon A. Zilles. David L.
Erickson & Jon White absent.

County Executive: Craig “W” Buttars

County Clerk: Jill N. Zollinger

County Attorney: James Swink

The following individuals were also in attendance: Janeen Allen, Shaun Bushman, Mayor John Drew,
Randy Eck, Cathy Harper, Mike Harper, Interim Development Services Director Chris Harrild, Sharon L.
Hoth, Angela Martin, Jon Mock, Chief Deputy County Executive Bryce Mumford, IT Director Bartt Nelson,
Media: none

OPENING REMARKS AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

Council member Barbara Tidwell gave the opening remarks and led those present in the Pledge
of Allegiance.

REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF AGENDA

ACTION: Motion by Council member Zilles to approve the agenda. Borup seconded the
motion. The vote was unanimous, 5-0. Erickson & White absent.

REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF MINUTES

ACTION: Motion by Vice Chair Worthen to approve the minutes of the January 14, 2020
Council meeting as written. Tidwell seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 5-0.
Erickson & White absent.

MINUTES FOLLOW-UP: Vice Chair Worthen asked when the CCCOG recommendations will
be discussed. Executive Buttars said it will probably be on the February 25, 2020 Council
meeting agenda.

REPORT OF THE COUNTY EXECUTIVE: CRAIG “W” BUTTARS

APPOINTMENTS: Bradley Shaun Bushman CVTD Board

ACTION: Motion by Vice Chair Worthen to approve the recommended appointment.
Tidwell seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 5-0. Erickson & White absent.

OTHER ITEMS:

Financial Fund Summary Report for the 12 months ending December 31, 2019 were
given to the Council Chair and Clerk/Auditor. Chairman Ward questioned the numbers
of the tax collections vs. the budget. Executive Buttars believes it is due to late
payments still coming in. He will find out.

(Attachment 1)

© County Public Works Director and Community Development Director Positions —
Executive Buttars said Human Resources Director Adams is developing the job

descriptions for the two positions. Executive Buttars is working with Chief Deputy County
Executive Mumford to update county ordinances to reflect the positions.

O Legislative Update — Executive Buttars indicated the legislature is in session and has
repealed the tax bill. Buttars received an email from the Public Lands Committee
advising him that a water banking bill will be in committee tomorrow afternoon.

INITIAL PROPOSAL FOR CONSIDERATION OF ACTION

« Resolution No. 2020-02 — Multi-Jurisdictional Emergency Access to County Road
1000 East - Interim Director Harrild explained this relates to a development in

Providence City which proposes an emergency access on the north end of the
subdivision. The long-term plan is to develop the rest of the property and annex the
property on the north. The access will feed onto a county road which is maintained by
Providence City. A review by the County Engineer and staff raised no concerns with the
request.

Council member Zilles asked Providence Mayor Drew if he had anything to say about
the request. Mayor Drew stated he had no concerns.

(Attachment 2)

ACTION: Motion by Council member Zilles to waive the rules and approve Resolution
No. 2020-02 — Multi-Jurisdictional Emergency Access to County Road 1000 East.
Worthen seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 5-0. Erickson & White absent.

Y Local Legislators Meetings — 2020 Legislative Session — Saturdays from 7:30 a.m. to
9:00 a.m. — January 25, 2020 through March 7, 2020 — County Historic Courthouse
Council Chambers, 199 N Main — Chairman Ward remarked the procedure of having

the Legislators to better understand citizens’ questions.

Council Member Reports

Barbara Tidwell attended strategic planning meetings for the homeless. Affordable housing is
the biggest problem facing the homeless.

Friday, March 13, 2020 will be the Celebration of the Women’s Right to Vote at the Logan
Tabernacle at 6:00 p.m.

Gordon Zilles asked the status of Logan City and CCEMS. Council member Borup responded
Logan is pulling out and has applied for paramedic licensure through the state for some areas in
the valley, however, Executive Buttars commented there has been no discussion with those
entities. Another update should be forthcoming by April 28, 2020. Executive Buttars is drafting
a letter to Logan Mayor Daines requesting at least a six-month extension on Logan City’s
withdrawal.

Zilles reported no brucellosis was discovered from the testing done on the elk at Hardware
Ranch.

Executive Buttars will be in Salt Lake for UAC and the annual Silicone Slopes Conference.
The Conference will be participating in a service project from noon to 6:00 p.m. packing one
million meals for children. The UAC meeting begins at 8:30 a.m.

ADJOURNMENT

The Council meeting adjourned at 5:30 p.m.

Memorandum 24 January 2020

To: County Council
Subject: Multi-Jurisdictional Access Request

A. Description
1. This memorandum is issued in response to a multi-jurisdictional access request made by Randy
Eck of Visionary Homes to allow temporary emergency and construction access to County
Road 1000 East for a new 40 plus lot subdivision within the Providence City limits (Providence
City roadways 500 North & 300 East). The proposed subdivision, Vineyard Subdivision (parcel
#02-004-0003), would construct a 20’ wide gravel road within the unincorporated county on
parcels 02-004-001 & 02-004-0002 that abuts the proposed subdivision and would be used as
a temporary emergency access to 1000 East. Staff has determined that the request for a
temporary construction access is no longer needed as this section of road has been annexed
into Providence City. The main access to the subdivision is from 500 North, which is a
Providence City road. County review for the requested access will no longer be needed when
future development and annexation occurs.
B. Multi-Jurisdictional Access Requirements
1, The Cache County Manual of Roadway Design and Construction Standards (Road Manual) §5.6
Access Requirements for Multi-Jurisdictional Development specifies that:
“Municipal Development Accessing County Roadways: No municipal development
shall be permitted to access a County roadway without the express written
approval from the Cache County Council. It'is the policy of Cache County that no
service shall be provided to municipal developments from County roadways
unless extreme circumstances provide no alternative and annexation or
acceptance of ownership of the roadway by the municipality is not possible.”
C. Proponent Request
1. “The access being requested is for emergency access use only, and will have little to no impact
on the County Roadways”.
2. “There is no other alternative option for emergency access for these phases of Vineyard
subdivision other than the County roadway 1000 East.”
3. “It is our intent to further annex and develop the parcels immediately to the north of phase
1,2, and 3 of Vineyard Subdivision, along 1000/300 East.”
D. Staff Recommendation and Conclusions
Staff recommends that the Council approve this request for multi-jurisdictional access, as:
1. The access is for temporary emergency access as is required by Fire Code for subdivisions over
30 lots, and will not be used for the main access or regular residential use.
2. County Road 1000 East is currently maintained by Providence City.
3. It is the developer's intent to further annex and develop the parcels to the north that include
the access.
