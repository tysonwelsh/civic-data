             Ordinance No. 2026-08

             AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 2-3-11 REVIEW OF DISPOSALS OF
             SIGNIFICANT PARCELS OF REAL PROPERTY

             WHEREAS, the City desires to align its Code with Utah State Code Sections 10-8-2 and
             11-1-201 to -205, as those sections were amended by House Bill 535 of the 2026
             General Session of the Utah Legislature.

             WHEREAS, the amendments clarify the requirements for notification and disposal of
             significant property.

             WHEREAS, lengthening the public notification period leads to a more informed public
             before disposing of significant property.

             BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF PARK CITY, UTAH, THAT:

             Section 2-3-11 of the Park City Code is amended and adopted to read as outlined in
             Exhibit B. This Ordinance shall become effective upon publication.

             PASSED AND ADOPTED this 7th day of May 2026.

             2-3-11 Review oOf Disposals oOf Significant Parcels oOf Real Property
                A. The City Council shall review all proposedals for dispositionsals of significant
                    parcels of real property. Prior to the proposed disposition, the City shall comply with
                    the notice and publication requirements in the Utah Code. Council shall provide at
                    least fourteen (14) days reasonable notice before the opportunity for public comment
                    on the proposed disposition. After such reasonable notice, publication and public
                    comment, the City Council may take action on the proposed disposition.

                   1.     “Dispose of” shall mean to transfer control of City-owned property to
                   another by any means, including sale, lease, or other type of conveyance of such
                   property.
             2. “Reasonable notice” shall mean posting on or about the property in a conspicuous
                location and publication in a newspaper having general circulation in Park City.
             A. B.

             B. “Significant parcel of real property” shall mean a parcel of real property owned by the
                City with an appraised value equal to or greater than Two Hundred Fifty Thousand
                Dollars ($50250,000) or a lease valued at equal to or greater than Fifty Thousand Dollars
                ($50,000) per yearannum.

                 3. Affordable or Attainable Housing Parcels subject to the City's Affordable Housing
                    Resolution and/or deed restrictions, including all units and properties included in or
                    funded by the City's Affordable Housing Budget, are not Significant Parcels subject
                    to this section, regardless of value or sales price.
                 4. Temporary Special Event or Master Festival leases of public property are not
                    Significant Parcels subject to this section, regardless of lease value.
