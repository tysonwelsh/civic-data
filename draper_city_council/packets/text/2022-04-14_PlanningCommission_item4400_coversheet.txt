Re: Jordon Maddocks I-ADU Deviation Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Deviation for approximately 0.82 acres located on the north side of Bluff Point Drive, at approximately 1515 E. Bluff Point Dr. The property is currently zoned RR-43 and is within the Suncrest area . The applicant is requesting that a Deviation be approved to allow for the creation of an Internal Accessory Dwelling Unit (I-ADU) within the Primary Dwelling on the subject property. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least twenty-thousand square feet (20,000 ft²);
2. No D-ADU exists on the property;
3. The proposed I-ADU meets all of the requirements of sections 9-31-030 and 9-31-045, including other applicable requirements of this chapter; and
4. At least two (2) parking stalls meeting the requirements of 9-31-045(B)(2) are provided for use by occupants of the I-ADU. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type P.C. Staff Report 4/8/2022 Cover Memo
