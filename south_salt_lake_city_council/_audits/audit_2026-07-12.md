## 4. The agenda-packet gaps are GENUINE (spot-checked at source)

The audit re-fetched a logged "gap" date to confirm it is truly an agenda, not a dropped
minutes file. **PMN file `963367`, labelled `2023.02.22RC Minutes.pdf`** — the very kind of
"Minutes"-labelled file that could look like a dropped record — downloads (4.6 MB) as text
beginning **`"South Salt Lake City Council / AMENDED REGULAR MEETING AGENDA … Wednesday,
February 22, 2023"`** with **0** roll-call-grammar hits. It is an agenda; the content-detector
correctly logged 2023-02-22 as `agenda-only/minutes-not-posted`. The size manifests
(`.harvest/*_min_sizes.json`) show the same date's WM file (963365) is likewise a 2.0 MB agenda.
This validates the honest-gap mechanism for the **253 council** (+ 48 RDA + 19 PC) logged gaps.
