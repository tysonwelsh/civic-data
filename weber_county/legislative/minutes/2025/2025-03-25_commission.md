                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVAL OF FINAL READING FOR ORDINANCE 2025-05 AMENDING THE NAME OF THE
      REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF WEBER COUNTY.
            Stephanie Russell (Community Development Director): No change from First Reading (approved March 18th,
            2025). Resolution 37-2017 changed the name of RDA of Weber County to Community Redevelopment
            Agency of Weber County, matching changes in state statute. This is an established agency and are only
            aligning its name with state statute.

            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a First Reading of an ordinance amending the name of the
            Redevelopment Agency of Weber County; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
H. PUBLIC HEARING:
   1. Request for a motion to adjourn public meeting and convene public hearings.
      Commissioner Harvey moved to adjourn the public meeting and convene public hearing, 10:07 a.m.;
      Commissioner Harvey seconded.
      Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

     2. Public Hearing to consider and take action on a proposal to vacate a portion of Nordic Valley Way, located at
        approximately 2800 Nordic Valley Way, Eden, UT, 84310. File No. VAC 2025-04

            Tammy Aydelotte (Planning): This is a request from Nordic Village Venture, owner of Nordic Ski Resort, to
            vacate a portion of Nordic Valley Way adjacent only to Nordic Village. Applicant has assured no traffic
            interruption during construction. Staff recommends approval.
    3. Public Comments:
          a. Peggy Dooling-Baker, Nordic Valley, UT: Land owner has the right to apply to move the road up to
               200 feet. Can you give me the reference for that? There is a home at the end of that road on the
               corner before Viking Drive. Is that affected and how will it be addressed?

    4. Request for a motion to adjourn public hearings and reconvene public meeting.
       Commissioner Harvey moved to adjourn the public hearings and reconvene public meeting, 10:12 am;
       Commissioner Froerer seconded.
       Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

    5. Action on public hearing:

         H2: Approval of Ordinance 2025-06 to vacate a portion of Nordic Valley Way, located at approximately 2800
         Nordic Valley Way, Eden, UT, 84310. File No. VAC 2025-04.

         Tammy Aydelotte, (in response to public comment questions): The Weber County Land Use Code reference
         is 104-22-8. The portion of affected road does not touch the parcel in question. No interruption to access of
         that parcel is expected.
         Commissioner Froerer: This went before the Planning Commission?
         Ms. Aydelotte: No, this did not go before the Planning Commission. The vacation of this road is not part of
         the Development Agreement. This came out of the Street Regulating Plan. The roundabout is part of the
         Development Agreement.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Ordinance 2025-06 to vacate a portion of Nordic Valley Way, located
         at approximately 2800 Nordic Valley Way, Eden, UT, 84310. File No. VAC 2025-04; Commissioner Harvey
         seconded with comment: We have heard from residents for a long time concerns about traffic and parking in
         that area. This begins to address those concerns.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
I. ADJOURN

      Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
