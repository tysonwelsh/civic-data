1.     Public Meeting – A Petition for a Conditional Use Permit for a Public Utility Facility,
       which includes the Installation of Piping as well as Construction and Operation of a
       Regulator Station.

Action Item

Applicant:     Kevin Mulvey, on Behalf of Dominion Energy
Address:       334 West Archard Avenue

Ms. Ungricht presented the Staff Report and stated that the request is similar to other Dominion
Energy requests made recently but is in a different location. The vacant .15-acre property is located
off 3300 South and 300 West behind Motel 6. The surrounding uses include motel as well as
commercial and residential uses. The purpose of the application is to install and construct a natural
gas regulator station. There is an existing small station on or near the Motel 6 property. A larger
station is needed and Dominion Energy would like to own property rather than place it in an
easement. The property is in the Transit-Oriented Development (“TOD”) District. A single-family
home was located on the property at one time but it is now vacant.

Ms. Ungricht reported that the request will help encourage development and provide services by
serving people throughout South Salt Lake City and the Greater Salt Lake Valley. It will also
enhance the streetscape as the property will be landscaped. The Site Plan includes one fence and
curb cut. The main pipes remain in place with one drive approach onto the property. The applicants
will be on site once per month to perform routine maintenance and the station will be fenced for
security purposes. The request was determined to meet all Building and Fire Code standards and
some of the proposed conditions are intended to meet those standards as well. Staff recommended
approval of a Conditional Use Permit (“CUP”) to establish a Public Utility Facility that includes the
construction and operation of a utility regulator station for Dominion Energy at 334 West Archard
Avenue subject to the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval
enumerated in the Staff Report.

  CONDITIONAL USE REVIEW STANDARD:


Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-507(2) sets forth the following standard of review for conditional uses by stating:

    (a)
              (i)    A land use authority shall approve a conditional use if reasonable conditions are proposed,
                     or can be imposed, to mitigate the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the
                     proposed use in accordance with applicable standards.

              (ii)    The requirement described in Subsection (2)(a)(i) to reasonably mitigate anticipated
                      detrimental effects of the proposed conditional use does not require elimination of the
                      detrimental effects.

    (b)       If a land use authority proposes reasonable conditions on a proposed conditional use, the land use
              authority shall ensure that the conditions are stated on the record and reasonably relate to
              mitigating the anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use.

    (c)       If the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of a proposed conditional use cannot be
              substantially mitigated by the proposal or the imposition of reasonable conditions to achieve
              compliance with applicable standards, the land use authority may deny the conditional use.

establish a Public Utility Facility, which includes the installation,
construction, and operation of a regulator station by Dominion Energy at
334 West Archard Avenue. Staff’s recommendation is based upon its
analysis and findings included in the staff report.
