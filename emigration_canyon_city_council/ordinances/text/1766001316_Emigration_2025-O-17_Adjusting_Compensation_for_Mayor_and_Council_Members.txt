EMIGRATION CANYON
ORDINANCE # 2025-O-17
AN ORDINANCE SETTING THE COMPENSATION OF
THE MAYOR AND COUNCIL MEMBERS
WHEREAS, Utah law 10-3-818 provides that elective and statutory officers in
municipalities may only receive compensation for services pursuant to ordinance enacted by the
legislative body following a public hearing;
WHEREAS, compensation for work performed for Emigration Canyon should be
sufficient to retain and attract qualified candidates and officials to serve in these elected positions;
WHEREAS, the compensation of the Mayor and Councilmembers has remained constant
since 2016, with inflation increasing approximately 35% since 2016, and Emigration Canyon has
undergone significant changes, including converting to a city, that require significant work and time
for elected officials to manage Emigration Canyon’s business;
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Emigration Canyon Council of
Emigration Canyon, Utah that:
Section I: The compensation for mayor and council members is:
Mayor —$ 2,000.00 per mont
