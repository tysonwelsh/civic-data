    3. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH L.A. ROSER FOR REPLACEMENT OF AGING MECHANICAL
       SYSTEM FOR ICE SHEET FUNDED THROUGH STATE GRANT.

         Todd Ferrario (Culture, Parks, and Recreation Director): This will contract will replace about 80% of the
         infrastructure in the equipment room at the Ice Sheet; some of which is pushing 30 years old. Funds are from
         the $3.8M state Olympic grant.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a contract with L.A. Roser for replacement of aging mechanical
         system for Ice Sheet funded through state grant; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye

    4. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH MASTER’S TOUCH PRINTING SERVICES FOR PRINTING,
       MAILING, AND EMAILING VALUATION NOTICES AND TAX NOTICES.

         Lynelle Jensen (County Treasurer): This effects the Clerk/Auditors office as well as us for sending valuation
         and tax notices. 105k notices are sent out twice a year. We have saved a lot of money since we contracted
         with them.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a contract with Master’s Touch Printing Services for printing, mailing,
         and emailing valuation notices and tax notices; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye
    5. APPROVAL OF AGREEMENT WITH VITALCORE HEALTH STRATEGIES, LLC. FOR INMATE
       HEALTHCARE SERVICES.
         Joshua Marigoni (Sheriff’s Department - Corrections): This is a renewal of a contract providing inmate health
         services at the jail. This contract was started in 2020 as Covid pandemic hit.

         Commissioner Froerer: Originally we pursued this contract to reduce liability. Even though these fees have
         gone up over the last 5 years, if you look at lawsuits in neighboring counties, this pays for itself. I appreciate
         the work you do.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve with VitalCore Health Strategies, LLC. for Inmate Healthcare
         Services; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
    6. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH GARRETT DENTAL SERVICES, PLLC. FOR INMATE DENTAL
       SERVICES.
         Joshua Marigoni (Sheriff’s Department - Corrections): We selected this dental service through competitive
         bid.
         Chair Bolos: Number 2 in the contract says inmates have access to dental services 1 day per week for four
         hours. Is that different than previous contracts?
         Lt. Marigoni: Scheduled services are the same with slight expansion on emergency services.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a contract with Garrett Dental Services, PLLC. for Inmate Dental
         Services; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye
