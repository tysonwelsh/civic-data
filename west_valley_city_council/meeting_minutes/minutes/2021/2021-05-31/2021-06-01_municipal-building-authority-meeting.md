       PUBLIC HEARINGS
       A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING THE FY 2021-2022 BUDGET
            Chairman Fitisemanu informed a public hearing had been advertised for the
            Regular Municipal Building Authority Meeting scheduled June 1, 2021, in order
            for the Board to hear and consider public comments regarding the FY 2021-2022
            Budget.

              Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
              information as follows:

                     This resolution adopts the final budget for the Municipal Building Authority
                     of West Valley City for the 2021-2022 fiscal year after receiving public
                     comment at a hearing on June 1, 2021.

                     The Municipal Building Authority adopted a tentative budget that was made
                     available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s
                     Recorder’s office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment,
                     before adopting this final budget for FY 2021-2022.

              Chairman Fitisemanu opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in
              favor or opposition, Chairman Fitisemanu closed the Public Hearing.

              ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 21-02, ADOPT THE ANNUAL
              BUDGET OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING
              AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1, 2021
              AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2022
              The Municipal Building Authority previously held a public hearing regarding
              proposed Resolution 21-02 that would adopt the annual budget of the West Valley
              City Municipal Building Authority for the fiscal year commencing July 1, 2021 and
              ending June 30, 2022.

              Upon inquiry by Chairman Fitisemanu there were no further questions from
              members of the Authority, and he called for a motion.

              Mr. Bigelow moved to approve Resolution 21-02.

              Mr. Buhler seconded the motion.

              A roll call vote was taken:
