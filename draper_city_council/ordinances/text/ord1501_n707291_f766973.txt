City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 5, 2021, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1501, amending
Titles 9, 11, 17, and 18 of the Draper City Municipal Code. The complete ordinance is
on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov.
Published this 6th day of October 2021. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
