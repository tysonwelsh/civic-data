D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: Why was Labor Day invented? It started in the 1830s when
   workers worked 70 hour weeks; unions stepped in to reduce those hours; now we are about 40 hours per week. It
   became a national law signed by President Grover in 1892. Businesses decided this is such a good idea because
   now employees have more time to spend money. This contributed to the change from a working class in the late
   1800s, early 1900s to today what is called the consuming class. It’s good to see the progress we have made with
   our workers.
