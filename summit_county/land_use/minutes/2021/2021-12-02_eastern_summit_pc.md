Canyon Road parcel KS-1. Dallin Donaldson, Applicant. – Ray Milliner, Principal 
Planner. 
 
Planner Milliner shared the background for this application. The applicant lives on Spring 
Canyon Drive and is the owner of the lot. The applicant wants the building for a reception 
area for their dogsledding business. There will not be any dogs or sledding on the property. 
The building will be used to greet guests and there will be parking for the guests who will 
then be shuttled up the canyon for dogsledding. The lot has been in use for quite some time. 
Approval of the Conditional Use Permit would make the business properly approved. Staff

recommends holding a Public Hearing and consider approval based on the Findings of Fact, 
Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval found in the Staff Report. 
Chair Sonntag clarified that the Conditional Use Permit is for a building for reception as a 
continued use, not based on dogsledding. There will not be any dogs on the property. 
Commissioner Peterson asked about lighting for parking. Planner Milliner answered 
there will not be any outside lighting for parking, but there will be lighting on the building, 
which will be reviewed during the Building Permit process. Any parking area needs to be 
constructed to Engineering Standards at the Building Permit stage.  
 
The Public Hearing was opened. No public comment was made, and the Public Hearing was 
closed. 
Findings of Fact:  
1. Dallin Donaldson is proposing an open space recreation, nonmotorized use at 395 E. Spring 
Canyon Road.  
2. The property is zoned Agriculture – 5 (AG-5).  
3. Open Space Recreation, Nonmotorized is a conditional use in the AG-5 zone.  
4. The base of the operation will be at 395 E. Spring Canyon Road, where clients will meet and 
park.  
5. Applicant proposes a 3,600 square foot building to accommodate guests and use as a shop.  
6. The 3,600 square foot building is similar in size and design to other accessory and agricultural 
buildings in the area.  
7. Clients will be shuttled from the base area to an area farther up Spring Canyon Road where 
they will meet the dogs and guides.  
8. Dogs are housed in an area farther up the canyon away from residential areas (private 
boarding of dogs is an allowed use).  
9. Clients are taken on a tour up an unimproved section of Spring Canyon Road.  
10. There are 4 time slots each day, with 5 to 7 teams taking 15 – 20 clients on a 1- or 2- hour 
tour.  
11. Access to the property is from Spring Canyon Road.  
12. The base of the proposed use is in an area that is residential with larger lots (most lots in the 
area are larger than 2 acres).  
13. Parking for the site is adequate for what is proposed (there are no specific parking 
requirements in the Eastern Summit County Development Code).

slope greater than 30%.

1. The proposed Conditional Use as conditioned complies with all requirements of the Eastern
