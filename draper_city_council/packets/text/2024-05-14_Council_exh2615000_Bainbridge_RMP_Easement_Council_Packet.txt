BACKGROUND: Rocky Mountain Power (RMP) is the power provider for the Bainbridge
Subdivision, developed by Ivory Development, LLC (Ivory). To meet power requirements for the
subdivision, Ivory must access the RMP main transmission line off of Pioneer Road, which necessitates
an easement through Draper City property, specifically parcel 28-29-452-029-0000.

                                              RESOLUTION NO. 24-19

          A RESOLUTION GRANTING A PERPETUAL EASEMENT TO ROCKY
          MOUNTAIN POWER FOR THE INSTALLATION OF AN ELECTRICAL
          POWER TRANSMISSION LINE ON DRAPER CITY PROPERTY LOCATED
          AT 12460 S 1130 E TO SERVICE THE BAINBRIDGE SUBDIVISION.

      WHEREAS, Ivory Development, LLC (“Ivory”) intends to develop Bainbridge
Subdivision (“Bainbridge”); and

          WHEREAS, Ivory must provide electricity (“Power”) to Bainbridge; and

      WHEREAS, Rocky Mountain Power (“RMP”) is the power provider for
Bainbridge; and

      WHEREAS, to provide the required power for Bainbridge, Ivory must access
the RMP main transmission line off of Pioneer Road; and

      WHEREAS, the only way for Ivory to access 12460 S from Pioneer Road is
through Draper City property; specifically, parcel number 28-29-452-029-0000; and

      WHEREAS, Ivory is requesting the Draper City Council grant a power
transmission line easement (“Easement”) to RMP as depicted in Exhibit A attached
hereto; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City will receive consideration in the amount $22,238.92 for
the Easement.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

      Section 1. Perpetual Easement. The City of Draper grants a Perpetual
Easement to RMP as provided in Exhibit A. Final survey quality control may make
required technical adjustments to the exhibits prior to construction of the power
transmission line.

     Section 2. Agreement. The Mayor is authorized to sign any agreement or
document necessary to effectuate the grant of and payment for the Easement.

 BAINBRIDGE POWER TRANSMISSION LINE AGREEMENT AND EASEMENT

       THIS BAINBRIDGE POWER TRANSMISSION LINE AGREEMENT AND
EASEMENT ("Agreement") is made this _____ day of _________, 2024, by and between
Draper City, a Utah municipal corporation located at 1020 East Pioneer Road, Draper, Utah
(“Grantor”) and Rocky Mountain Power, an unincorporated division of PacifiCorp, located at
1407 West North Temple Ste. 110, Salt Lake City, Utah 84116 ("Grantee").

       A.     Ivory Development, LLC is developing the Bainbridge subdivision located at
approximately 1085 East 12650 South, Draper, Utah. The Bainbridge subdivision is located
within Grantee’s service area.

       B.      In order to provide electrical power to the Bainbridge subdivision, Grantee
needs to run a power transmission line south from Pioneer Road to 1030 East.

        C.      Grantor owns property through which the power transmission line must be run
and is willing to grant an easement across its property to Grantee for the construction of the
waterline pursuant to the terms stated in the easement attached hereto as Exhibit 1.

       NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy
of which is hereby acknowledged, Grantor and Grantee agree as follows:

       1.     Grant of Easement. Grantor hereby grants, transfers, and conveys to Grantee
an easement located on its property as stated in the Easement attached as Exhibit 1.

        2.     Payment. Grantee agrees to pay Grantors upon execution hereof the sum of
eleven thousand one hundred nineteen dollars and 46 cents ($11,119.46) for the
approximately 3,957.322 square feet of Grantor’s property required for the easement, which
was calculated at $11.24 per square foot representing 25% of the appraised land value per
Salt Lake County Assessor records at $489,600.00 per acre. The 25% figure is the same as
has been paid and received by Draper City in other easement agreements.

        IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the
date first appearing above.
