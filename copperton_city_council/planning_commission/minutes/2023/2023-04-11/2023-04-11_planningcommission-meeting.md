proposal to repeal and replace Copperton’s land use ordinances, Title 18 Subdivisions, and Title 19 Zoning.

with revisions having been made as a result of input received from various reviewing agencies. These 
ordinances are on the agenda for formal recommendation by the planning commission to the Copperton 
Metro Township Council. Planners: Curtis Woodward and Kayla Mauldin (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Supervisor Curtis Woodward provided an analysis

Mr. Woodward provided the additional changes to Title 18 and 19 not previously provided.  
 
Commissioners, Counsel, and staff had a brief discussion regarding 19.5.050 landscape setback,

commercial development, HB 406, zoning maps and character areas, pigeons. Use consistent language of

food production prohibited date after adoption date.  
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED 
No one from the public was present to speak. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED 
Motion: To recommend approval to the Copperton Council of file # OAM2022-000737 to repeal and 
replace Copperton’s land use ordinances, Title 18 Subdivisions, and Title 19 Zoning to the Copperton 
Council with recommended changes and amendments as provided below. 
1. Remove the larger animals, cows, sheep, and goats, from the definition of “Family Food                 
Production.”  
2. Amend Table 19.28.020 - Uses Allowed in Single Family Residential Zones to clarify the “Animals and 
Fowl for family food production established after May 1, 2023” is not allowed (making existing properties 
with those listed animals nonconforming uses).  
Motion by: Commissioner Breinholt 
2nd by: Commissioner Taylor 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor
