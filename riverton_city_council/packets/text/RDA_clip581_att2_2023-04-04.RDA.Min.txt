25   Call to Order
26
27   Vice Chair McDougal called the Riverton Redevelopment Agency Board Meeting to order at
28   7:06 PM and conducted a roll call. Board members McCay, McDougal, Pierucci, and Wells were
29   present. Mayor Staggs and Councilmember Buroker were excused.
30
31   Discussion/Action Items
32
33          RDA Resolution No. 23-01 – Dissolving the “Redwood Road South Neighborhood
34          Redevelopment Project Area”
35
36   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that the Redwood Road South
37   Neighborhood Redevelopment Project Area had expired in 2017. However, it has remained on
38   the books of Salt Lake County. This resolution will dissolve the project area.
39
40   Board Member McCay MOVED that the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency approve
41   RDA Resolution No. 23-01 – Dissolving the “Redwood Road South Neighborhood
42   Redevelopment Project Area.” Board Member Pierucci SECONDED the motion. Vice Chair
43   McDougal called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for a roll-call vote.
44   The vote was as follows: Board Members McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes, Pierucci-Yes, and
45   Wells-Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
