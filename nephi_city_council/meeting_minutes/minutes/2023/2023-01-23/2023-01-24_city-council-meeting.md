                            Nephi City Council Work Session Minutes
                                        January 24, 2023
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                          21 E. 100 N.
                                        Nephi, UT 84648


    1. Nephi Great Chamber of Commerce Executive Director Abby Ivory presented to the
       council a summary of the accomplishments, goals, and vision for the Chamber. She
       introduced the idea a bringing an innovation center to the City through a possible grant
       that would bring high-speed internet, 3D printing and other resources that City
       businesses could access. Ms. Ivory requested funding from the City to help pay for
       increased hours for the executive director and for some conferences that she could
       attend. The council members agreed with helping to fund the Chamber.

    2. The following department heads presented budget requests and department overviews:

            a.   Kyle Marchant-Public Works and Streets
            b.   Gale Bryan – Treasurer
            c.   Michael Morgan -Police Department
            d.   Lisa Brough – Finance and Justice Court
            e.   Justin Blackett- Water/Sewer Department
            f.   Drew McPherson – Natural Gas Department
            g.   Rust Finlinson – Electric Department

    3. The council agreed to have the budget priority setting work session on the Tuesday,
       January 31, 2023.

    4.   Work Session ended at 9:00 p.m.

    5. Mayor Seely called to order a regular council meeting. (See CM minutes 1-24-23)
