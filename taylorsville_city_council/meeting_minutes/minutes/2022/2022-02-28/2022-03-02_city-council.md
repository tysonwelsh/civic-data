         1. 2    Mayor' s Report


 El Mayor Kristie Overson reported that Sen. Mayne' s and ChamberWest' s bill involving the
ongoing problem of catalytic converter theft had been passed by Utah' s legislature and would now
be forwarded to Governor Cox for his signature.


HB440 ( Homeless Services amendments) and HB462 ( Housing Affordability amendments) were
concerning, and city administration was keeping a close eye on both bills. It had been interesting
to watch them move through the legislative process.            She expressed great appreciation to John
Hiskey for his tireless efforts keeping city leaders apprised of rapidly changing legislative
activities.




The Chairs of all city volunteer committees recently met with City Attorney Tracy Cowdell and
Jay Ziolkowski ( Community Engagement) regarding their roles and responsibilities. The Mayor
thanked Councilmember Burgess for attending the meeting as well.

Some new streetlights had been installed along 2700 West both north and south of the 6200 South
intersection near Bennion Jr. High. She encouraged the councilmembers to check them out and
provide any feedback.

Chair Barbieri inquired if other street lights were anticipated. Mayor Overson responded that the
intersection at 6200 South 2700 West had the most immediate need but if the council liked the
increased illumination there, she hoped they would approve funding for others.

Councilmember Harker asked           acquiring flashing crosswalk lights.

Mayor Overson explained that the city had hosted a Covid vaccine clinic recently, during which
19 vaccinations were provided. She thanked Mr. Ziolkowski for arranging the event with the Salt
Lake County Health Department.

The Mayor explained that each councilmember should have received a draft of the updated Land
Development Code. She pointed out how fortunate the city was to have Senior Planner Jim Spung
on staff, and had taken on this monumental task.

Mr. Cowdell had presented annual harassment training for the entire city staff the previous week.
Mayor Overson pointed out what a great job he had done presenting some very important
information.


GIS Planner Karyn Kerdolff, and her husband had recently climbed Mt. Kilimanjaro which was
the tallest free- standing mountain in the world. She offered her congratulations and stated that the
city was thrilled to support staff,whether it was for attaining their work-related goals or the pursuit
of personal endeavors.




A letter of appreciation had recently been submitted by a South Jordan resident whose tire had
been changed recently by Deputy Chief Gray while outside of Taylorsville. Mayor Overson then
invited Chief Cottam to step to the podium and give the council some news.

         Chief Cottam mentioned how proud he was of his team for acts of kindness as had just been
described and also for their skills and investigative abilities. He explained that TVPD' s Violent
Crimes/Persons Crimes Unit had been working tirelessly for several days to locate and arrest an
armed robbery suspect. The various robberies had been escalating in the sense that the suspect had
started by passing a note to the victim, progressed to brandishing a knife and ultimately committed
robberies at gunpoint throughout the valley. The suspect had been taken into custody earlier that
day after hours and hours of investigation from TVPD personnel. Chief Cottam also expressed
appreciation to the FBI and other outside agencies for their assistance.
