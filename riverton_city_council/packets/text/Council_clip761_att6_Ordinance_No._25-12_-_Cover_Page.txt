SUMMARY:

In response to feedback received at the previous meeting, staff has incorporated amendments into the
proposed language. Additional information will be presented at the hearing.


Riverton City has initiated a significant update to its commercial architectural standards for the exterior of
commercial buildings within the City. The process of updating the standards has involved multiple
discussions with Planning Commission and City Council. This public hearing is to review and
recommend amendments to the Commercial architectural standards.



These standards address the exterior materials and visual elements of commercial buildings, and guide
the development, review and approval process for proposed commercial projects in the City.



Staff received feedback from the Commission and direction from the City Council on the proposed
amendments before preparing the final draft. The intent of the update is to provide broader standards in
a simplified code text while preserving the existing character of Riverton City’s commercial areas.
 Generally speaking, the proposed updated language features fewer rigid “checkbox” type standards,
with more discretion given to the Land Use Authority (typically the Planning Commission) to allow for
variation in architectural styles and materials with requirements for consistency to nearby existing
commercial buildings.



The proposed amendments would cover all six commercial zones, the manufacturing zone, and
“Churches, religious, cultural, public and quasi-public uses” developed in Residential Zones under City
Code 18.215.030, and the architectural standards would be the same for all these zones.
