                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-09

A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE REAPPOINTMENT OF COMMISSION MEMBER
           TO THE RIVERTON CITY PLANNING COMMISSION

       WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance 2-95-020, the Mayor shall
appoint with advice and consent of the City Council residents of Riverton City to serve on the
Planning Commission; and

       WHEREAS, due to vacancies of Commission Members that have occurred on the
Planning Commission, it has become necessary to reappoint a Commission Member; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for reappointment of Troy
Rushton; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the reappointments of the Mayor's
nomination to the Planning Commission.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Mayor's reappointment of Troy Rushton be made with the advice and
          consent of the City Council to serve as Planning Commissioners, which shall
          expire January 31, 2024.
       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 2nd day of
