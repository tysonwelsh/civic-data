Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: April 27, 2023
Re: Summerhays Landscaping Office – Plat Amendment Request Comments: On April 28, 2022, the Planning Commission granted approval of a plat amendment for Summerhays Landscaping Office, located at approximately 134 East 12300 South.  The application number is SUBD-12-2022.  Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 17-9-070 states:
If the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date of approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced, such approval shall be null and void.  This time period may be extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6) month period for good cause shown.  The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year.
To date, the property owner has not been able to record the plat due to a delay in getting an easement from the shopping center owner and South Valley Sewer Districts signature on the mylar.  As such, the applicant is seeking a six-month extension to finalize the process and get the plat recorded.  Attached to this memo is the applicant’s written request for approval extension, the original approval letter, and Planning Commission staff report.  Staff received the request for extension on March 22, 2023, prior to the expiration of the plat amendment.
If approved, the site plan approval will be valid through October 28, 2023.
The findings are as follows: Sample Motion for Approval – I move that we approve the plat amendment extension, as requested by Ryan Summerhays representing Summerhays Landscaping, for the Summerhays Landscaping Office Plat Amendment Request, application SUBD-12-2022, based on the findings listed in the memo dated April 27, 2023.
1. That the applicant has shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has occurred towards acquiring the signatures to record the mylar.
Sample Motion for Denial – I move that we deny the plat amendment extension, as requested by Ryan Summerhays representing Summerhays Landscaping, for the Summerhays Landscaping Office Plat Amendment Request, application SUBD-12-2022, based on the findings listed in the memo dated April 27, 2023.
1. That the applicant has not shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has not occurred towards acquiring the signatures to record the mylar.
