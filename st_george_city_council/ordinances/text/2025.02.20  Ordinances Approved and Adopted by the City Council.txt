Please be advised that on February 20, 2025, the City Council of the City of St
George approved the following ordinance(s):


Ordinance No. 2025-012 approving the vacation of a public utility and drainage easement as
described in Doc. No. 20170025075.

Ordinance No. 2025-013 approving the vacation of a portion of an existing public cross

and 7 of The Fields at Mall Drive Phase 2 Partial Amendment A.

easement located between Lots 58 and 59 of Cottam Cove Phase 5.

Ordinance No. 2025-015 amending an approved PD-C (Planned Development Commercial)
zone on approximately 0.43 acres located east of River Road and south of 900 South.

Ordinance No. 2025-016 re-approving the Flats at Grand View PD-R (Planned Development
Residential) zone on approximately 8.93 acres to amend the approved Flats at Grand View
development with conceptual site plan and elevations, located generally at 1600 West and
Skyrocket Road.

Ordinance No. 2025-017 amending the existing Rillisante Villas at Divario PD-R (Planned
Development Residential) zone on approximately 24.10 acres to amend the approved
Rillisante Villas development with conceptual site plan and elevations located generally on
the west side of the intersection of Canyon View Drive and Gap Canyon Parkway.
