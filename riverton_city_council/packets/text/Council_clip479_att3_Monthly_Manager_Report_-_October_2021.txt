                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                             October, 2021


The statistics during the last couple of months have remained consistent and we are not seeing
any concerning trends in any statistical category. In fact, vehicle burglaries, shoplifting cases,
and stolen vehicles have remained quite low relative to other months.

We have been busy with our first annual Citizen’s Academy and we are scheduled to graduate
our first class on November 18th, 2021. The reviews from the participants seem to be very
positive and we look forward to making this a tradition in our community for years to come.

Also, we had an attempted homicide case in August and the suspect fled the state after the
incident. We leveraged our relationship with the U.S. Marshal’s Service and enlisted their help
to track our suspect. The suspect initially went to California, and then to Houston, Texas, where
he was arrested working at a TJ Maxx retail outlet. Eventually, two of our detectives flew to
Texas and transported the suspect back to the Salt Lake County Jail to face attempted homicide
charges in our court system. This case was very complex and emphasized the importance of
maintaining relationships with federal partners and the state criminal justice system to give us the
capability to bring this case to a successful resolution.
