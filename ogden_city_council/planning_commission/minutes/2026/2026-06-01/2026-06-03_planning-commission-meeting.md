vision of the East Central Community Plan and that staff recommends approval with conditions,

5. Preliminary Group Dwelling, “Ponderosa Drive”, 5 units, 1041 16" St
Petitioner Jason Dimmick wants to build two duplex units with the goal of having them for
individual ownership.
Assistant Planning Director Joseph Simpson stated the project was reviewed as a preliminary
group dwelling, with a conditional use for the group dwelling. He said staff determined the
project complied with the group dwelling standards and zoning requirements, with conditions
such as a solid, white six-foot fence and water-wise shrubs.
Local resident Kristen Basset stated via Zoom that she was concerned about parking options
with the limit of on-street parking, that the exterior of the new development should match the
exterior of her townhomes so to blend in, and that landscaping might include fast growing trees.

COMISSIONER ROSS MOVED TO APPROVE THE PRELIMINARY GROUP DWELLING
SUBJECT TO STAFFS RECOMMENDED CONDITIONS. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY

8. Review of Correspondence, resident concerns on code enforcement for ICE facility
The commission reviewed concerns and opened up public comment about the local ICE facility,
noting alleged violations of its conditional use permit, including overnight detentions and public
viewings. They agreed to request an administrative report and revisit the issue next month and
opened up public comment.

COMMISSIONER SOUTHWICK MADE A MOTION TO TAKE PUBLIC COMMENT ABOUT
THE ICE FACILITY. COMMISSIONER AKHMEDOV SECONDED THE MOTION, ALL VOTING AYE.

Kim Hasian shared concerns with treatment and conditions of those being held at the facility
and statistics from the government.

Sarah Fischer stated there had been a complaint filed with the Weber Morgan Health
Department of concerns about overnight containment and the conditions they were held in.
Dave Belnap is the leader of Community Ice Response and said he continues to see actions that
raise serious questions about safety, accountability, and transparency.

A local resident stated his concerns with the breakage of the Conditional Use Permit agreement
that was in place for the facility.

Cameron Shirts spoke about human rights and showing courage to enact oversight on the ICE
facility.

COMMISSIONER ROSS MADE A MOTION TO CLOSE THE PUBLIC COMMENT ABOUT
THE ICE FACILITY. COMMISSIONER WILLIAMS SECONDED THE MOTION, ALL VOTING AYE.
