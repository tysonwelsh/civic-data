A.  EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH AWARD, JANUARY 2020- COREY
    SHERWIN, LEGAL DEPARTMENT
    Councilmember Buhler offered to read the nomination of Corey Sherwin, Legal
    Department, to receive Employee of the Month Award for January 2020. The award
    reads as follows:

              Over the past four months Corey Sherwin’s prepared for nine jury trials.
              Four of them have actually happened while the others resolved the morning
              of trial which means Corey was prepared for nine jury trials. One of the jury
              trials was an evidence based domestic violence trial, meaning he
              successfully presented evidence to the jury of a domestic violence assault
              without the victim testifying or participating in the case. Additionally,
              Corey prepared and argued before the Court of Appeals on a case and
              recently became a Constitutional scholar preparing and arguing a complex
              constitutional issue. The very next day he was prepared for another jury trial
              (that got continued the morning of trial). Corey has a strong legal mind and
              a quick wit that makes working with him a pleasure. He is always available
              for anyone, attorneys and law enforcement officers, alike, to run through
              facts of a case and figure out a legal strategy. He is well-respected in Court
              and is always steadfast in his prosecution of cases. West Valley City is
              extremely lucky to have Mr. Sherwin represent the City in such a competent
              and passionate manner.

RESOLUTION 20-01: APPROVE AN AMENDMENT TO THE INTERLOCAL
COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN WEST VALLEY CITY AND SALT
LAKE COUNTY FOR COUNTY LIBRARY SECURITY
Colleen Jacobs, Chief of Police, presented proposed Resolution 20-01 that would approve
an amendment to the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between West Valley City and Salt
Lake County for County Library Security.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Salt Lake County wishes to renew the Interlocal Agreement for an additional one-
       year period extending the expiration date from December 31, 2019 to December
       31, 2020.

       In February 2018, Salt Lake County and West Valley City entered into an Interlocal
       Agreement for Salt Lake County to hire off-duty West Valley City Police Officers
       to provide law enforcement services at the West Valley City Library located at 2880
       West 3650 South.

Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if the relationship is going well. Wayne replied yes and
indicated that it allows the City to keep a closer eye on the area, including the promenade.

The City Council will consider Resolution 20-01 at the Regular Council Meeting scheduled
January 14, 2020 at 6:30 P.M.

      FROM PACIFIC RESOURCES ASSOCIATES, LLC FOR PROPERTY

      Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-03 that would authorize the City
      to enter into a Right-of-Way Purchase Agreement and accept a Special Warranty
      Deed and Temporary Construction Easement from Pacific Resources Associates,
      LLC for property located at 3765 South Constitution Boulevard (2700 West).

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:


             This portion of the Pacific Resources Associates, LLC parcel located at
             3765 S. Constitution Boulevard is being acquired as part of the 2700 West
             Improvement Project, scheduled to be constructed in 2019. This project
             will include construction of sidewalk, textured colored concrete parkstrip,
             landscaping, and decorative street lighting along the west side of
             Constitution Boulevard (2700 West) from 4100 South to 3671 South. The
             project will also include an eight-foot tall decorative concrete post and panel
             wall along those properties with backyards facing Constitution Boulevard.
             The traffic signal at the intersection of 3800 South and Constitution
             Boulevard will also be upgraded as part of this project. The acquisition
             from Pacific Resources Associates, LLC includes a Special Warranty Deed
             for 232 square feet of additional right-of-way, along with a Grant of
             Temporary Construction Easement. Compensation in the amount of
             $3,500.00 is based upon the appraisal report prepared by Integra Realty
             Resources.

       - Justice Courts
              o A major accomplishment for the court this past year has been the
                   Online Dispute Resolution pilot program which we
                   implemented for handling Small Claims cases. Essentially how
                   it works is instead of going to trial at the justice court at a
                   specific date and time, the person and the defendant work with
                   a third party or facilitator to reach a solution using an online
                   resolution system. If parties cannot come to an agreement, they
                   still have a right to go to trial. Initially we implemented this for
                   the Administrative Office of the Courts have refined this
                   process. Judge McCullagh and a couple of our clerks have
                   helped to iron out bugs and problems since day one and it has
                   been very successful. Other courts throughout Utah and other
                   states, as well as courts from other countries have reached out to
                   us for information so they can model a program like ours.
              o Our Mental Health Court run by Judge Gilmore was established
                   to improve the response to individuals with Medium-Risk and
                   High-Risk mental illnesses. Under this program offenders are
                   linked to local behavioral health service providers where
                   individual treatment plans are developed that serve the offender.
                   The collaborating partners the court works with in this effort are
                   still very committed to making this effort succeed.
              o The court continues to streamline our efforts so that we can
                   handle the workload the court is given. Over the past 8 years we
                   have reduced the number of clerks we have from 22 to 16 and
                   we are doing all we can to not only manage the existing level of
                   work, but we are able to manage occasional increases of work
                   due to efforts of law enforcement.
              o To provide better service to the patrons of the court, the court
                   has moved our fingerprint machine so we can accommodate
                   court patrons every day and hour the court is open for business.

      statistics. Chief Jacobs replied some of them. Councilmember Buhler asked how
      many homicides there were in the previous year. Chief Jacobs replied 11.
      Councilmember Buhler asked how this compares to previous years. Chief Jacobs
      replied it is higher and the average is about 6-9. Councilmember Buhler asked how

      this compares to other Cities in Utah. Chief Jacobs replied it is high but noted that
      West Valley City does have a higher population. She briefly discussed types of
      homicide and in response to Councilmember Huynh, she discussed mental illness.
      Mayor Bigelow asked what time frame parking compliance officers work in. Chief
      Jacobs stated one officer works the Monday-Thursday day shift, one works

      have been a handful. Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if there is anything that may
