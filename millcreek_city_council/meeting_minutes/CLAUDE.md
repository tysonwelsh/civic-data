# meeting_minutes/ — Millcreek City Council + CRA vote pipeline

## What's here
- `minutes/<year>/<meeting-date>/<date>_<slug>.md` — **372** minutes files, 2016-12 → 2026-06,
  covering **both** the City Council **and** the Community Reinvestment Agency (CRA). The
  subdir is the **meeting date** (not the week-Monday). Indexed in `minutes_index.csv` (8-col
  standard); `source` = `civicplus` (CivicPlus AgendaCenter `ViewFile/Minutes/_<MMDDYYYY>-<id>`)
  for all but **one** file — the 2017-11-21 Board of Canvassers minutes carries `source=pmn`
  (Utah Public Notice, promoted from `pmn_backfill/` 2026-07-20; see that folder's CLAUDE.md);
  `format` = `text` (242) or `scanned` (132). **Council + 58 CRA** files.
- `raw/<year>/<date>_<Body>_<docid>.pdf` — the retained source PDFs (never modified). **376 raw
  PDFs**; reconciles to 372 indexed as **376 − 3 duplicate content-hashes − 1 unrecovered = 372**.
- `minutes_unrecovered.csv` — the **1** meeting that exists but has no usable minutes
  (2018-03-20 budget work meeting: the published file is a budget spreadsheet, no narrative/votes).
- `extract_votes.py` — the PURE deterministic parser (no LLM, no network; resumable — skips
  meetings whose JSON exists unless `--force`). Run it to regenerate everything below.
- `votes/<year>/<meeting-date>/<file>.json` — one structured JSON per meeting (the resumable
  intermediate; `all_votes.csv` is rebuilt from these).
- `votes/_validation_report.txt` — integrity checks, per-year roster, mayor-vote count,
  tally-mismatch + contested-vote lists (regenerated by `validate_votes.py`).
- `all_votes.csv` — long format, **one row per member-vote** (or one placeholder row per
  tally-only motion), 13-col standard:
  `date,year,title,body,motion_no,motion,motion_type,result,mover,seconder,member,vote,source`.
- `roster.csv` — the 7 observed members with first/last-seen + meeting counts.

## Run
```
python3 extract_votes.py     # writes votes/*.json then rebuilds all_votes.csv + roster.csv
python3 validate_votes.py    # writes votes/_validation_report.txt
```
`all_votes.csv` is valid RFC-4180 (motion text contains commas/quotes — read it with a real
CSV parser, NOT `awk -F,`).

## The OCR caveat — even the `text` PDFs are OCR-derived
The AgendaCenter minutes are **scanned / bad-text-layer** PDFs, so `pdftotext -layout` output
carries systematic garble regardless of whether the format column says `text` or `scanned`:
`Councn Member`, `TTipi voterl yes`, `Coinmission`, `01son`, `snd Maynr.Tsirkqnn vnt`. **`format`
here means "had a usable text layer" (`text`) vs "needed image OCR" (`scanned`) — NOT
"born-digital clean."** The parser therefore **fuzzy-matches** each garbled surname against the
fixed 7-name roster (`difflib`), and where a name is unrecoverable it leaves the member **BLANK**
rather than guessing. The corpus screener + independent verification confirmed the garble did
**not** propagate into fabricated data: distinct named voters == exactly the roster (Uipi,
C. Jackson, Marchant, Silvestrini, Handy, Catten, DeSirant), zero OCR-invented names.

## The Mayor VOTES — max tally 5 (the single most important structural fact)
Millcreek's five-member council-mayor form makes the **Mayor a full voting member**. Named
council roll calls include the mayor ("… **and Mayor Silvestrini voted yes**"), so a complete
tally tops out at **5** (4 districts + mayor), never 4. `validate_votes.py` confirms **no
roll-call exceeds 5** (sizes observed: 3, 4, 5). Both mayors appear as voters — **Silvestrini**
(695 named votes) and **Cheri Jackson** (897 named — she cast votes first as D3 councilmember,
then as Mayor from Nov 2025). Treat a 5-vote tally as complete; the mayor is **not** excluded
from the voting roster (unlike South Jordan).

## Named roll-call: 2017 (recovered) + 2022→present; 2018–2021 is TALLY-ONLY by source
This is a **source-format change, not a parser miss** — with one recovered exception. Most
early minutes record the outcome collectively — *"…called for the vote. **All Council Members
voted yes.** The motion passed unanimously."* (CRA: *"All Board Members voted yes"*) — naming
**no** individual voters. Individual named prose rolls (*"Council Member Uipi voted yes, …and
Mayor Silvestrini voted yes"*) begin ~2022.

**2017 is DIFFERENT and was RECOVERED (2026-07-19).** In 2017 the city DID name every voter,
but in a **tabular en-dash roll call printed AFTER the outcome word** — *"Motion passed
unanimously by roll call vote with members voting as follows:"* then one member per line
*"Councilmember Uipi – Aye / Councilmember Catten – Aye / Mayor Silvestrini – Aye / …"*. Because
the prose parser reads the span BEFORE the outcome, these had been recorded as tally-only. The
`parse_endash_votes` grammar (safe-direction: fires only on the explicit `<role> <name> <dash>
<vote>` form, which occurs **nowhere outside 2017**) recovered **362 named Aye rows across 77
motions** — every roll all-Aye/unanimous, only the five seated members, and correctly OMITTING
excused members (e.g. Marchant excused → 4-member rolls, Uipi injured 2017-04-10 → 4-member
roll) with no fabrication. **2018–2021 remains genuinely tally-only** (the city dropped the
tabular roll after 2017 and did not resume named voting until the ~2022 prose form).

The parser records the remaining collective form as **tally-only** (`names_recorded:false`,
blank member, one placeholder row) — it never fabricates individual votes. Named-vs-tally-only
motions by year: **2017 [77/97] · 2018 [0/259] · 2019 [4/235] · 2020 [5/253] · 2021 [6/255] ·
2022 [70/158] · 2023 [222/0] · 2024 [236/1] · 2025 [253/0] · 2026 [139/0]**. **Member-level
vote analysis is meaningful for 2017 and 2022→present**; 2018–2021 blank-member motions are an
honest source seam, not missing extraction. (Marchant now carries 72 named 2017 votes on top of
his tally-era service.)

### Vote grammar the parser handles
- **Named prose (dominant 2022+):** `"…Council Member DeSirant voted yes, Council Member Jackson
  voted yes, Councn Member Uipi voted yes, and Mayor Silvestrini voted yes. The motion passed
  unanimously."` → named Aye rows incl. the mayor.
- **Named dissent / grouped:** `"Council Members Marchant, Jackson, and Catten voted yes.
  Council Member Uipi voted no. Mayor Silvestrini abstained (counted as a no vote). The motion
  passed."` → the named Nay/Abstain rows; majority named where named.
- **Absent-from-vote:** `"Council Member DeSirant was not present for the vote."` → **Absent**
  (never a Nay).
- **Unanimous shorthand (no names):** `"…All Council Members voted yes. The motion passed
  unanimously."` → tally-only, `names_recorded:false`, empty member list.
- **2017 en-dash TABULAR roll call (recovered 2026-07-19):** `"…Motion passed unanimously by
  roll call vote with members voting as follows: Councilmember Uipi – Aye  Councilmember Catten
  – Aye  Mayor Silvestrini – Aye  Councilmember Jackson – Aye"` → named Aye rows incl. the mayor
  (block sits AFTER the outcome word; `parse_endash_votes`).
- **Early tally-only (2016 + procedural 2017):** `"MOTION was made by Councilmember Catten,
  seconded by Councilmember Marchant… Motion passed unanimously."` (no tabular block) →
  tally-only.
- **Died / lack of second** (`"failed for lack of a second"`) → recorded with **no** members
  (never came to a vote).

`motion_type` uses the fixed 12-category taxonomy (Procedural/Administrative, Public Hearing
Action, Ordinance, Land-Use/Zoning, Resolution, Other, Interlocal, Budget Amendment, Appointment,
Contract/Purchase, Ceremonial, Grant-Funding); `result` and the numeric tally are **verbatim as
printed**. Normalized outcome/tallies/type live **alongside** in `motions_std.csv` (per the
cardinal rules — verbatim is never overwritten).

## `body` column — Council vs CRA (Board Member / Chair synonyms)
Default `Council`. The Council convenes **in-session** as the **Millcreek Community Reinvestment
Agency (CRA)** (Utah 17C — the RDA-equivalent redevelopment body): project-area plans/budgets,
participation agreements, inter-fund loans. Each file's governing body is carried in the markdown
front matter (`**Body:** Council|CRA`, harvested at acquisition and authoritative), and
`extract_votes.py` tags every motion `body ∈ {Council, CRA}` — **314 Council files (2,011
motions) · 58 CRA files (246 motions)**. In CRA capacity the minutes say **"Board Member <Name>"**
(= the councilmember) and **"Chair <Name>"** (= the mayor) — the parser maps both to the **same
five people**, so CRA rolls also cap at 5 (Chair = mayor counted). There are **no separate CRA
portal files** to acquire.

## Decoy-trap avoidance (mis-uploaded / duplicate source docs)
Cities routinely attach the wrong or a duplicate PDF to a meeting. The acquisition + screening
caught these here:
- **Duplicate content-hashes:** **3** raw PDFs are byte/text duplicates of another meeting's
  doc (same file served under two meeting slots) — deduped to **372** indexed files (376 raw −
  3 dup − 1 unrecovered). The corpus screener reports **0 duplicate_bodies** in the *indexed*
  set (no mis-upload propagated into the corpus).
- **Date-in-body check:** each converted file carries `**In-body date match:**` in its front
  matter — the meeting date is confirmed to appear in the document text, guarding against a
  wrong-date/wrong-doc upload being silently indexed.
- **The 1 unrecovered meeting** (2018-03-20) is logged in `minutes_unrecovered.csv` with reason,
  its raw PDF retained — **never** stubbed or filled with a wrong doc.
