SUMMARY:

In order to get the police vehicles here and upfitted by September, which is the time we have to turn the
leased vehicles back in, they need to be ordered now. The order will be for 13 vehicles. The payment
for the vehicles will be out the FY 25/26 budget, and will not exceed $676,000.00.

By approving the ordering of these vehicles now, Council will need to approve $676,000.00 in the FY
25/26 Budget for the payment of these vehicles.
