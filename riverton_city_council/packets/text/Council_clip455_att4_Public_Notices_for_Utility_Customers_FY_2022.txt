                          Public Notices for Utility Customers
                                                       Riverton City
                                                 Notice of Public Hearing
              The Riverton City Council has determined it is necessary to transfer $905,000 from the Culinary
              Water fund to the Secondary Water fund in order to write-off a debt between them. This debt was
              created by a loan from fiscal year 2020, used to pay debt service for the water revenue bonds.
              Utah Code 10-6-135 requires that the city notify all utility customers of this transfer. A public
              hearing will be held on June 15, 2021, at 7 p.m. for the 2022 fiscal year. The public hearing will
              be held at Riverton City Hall at 12830 S Redwood Road, Riverton, Utah, to receive public
              comment on this action.



                                                         Riverton City
                                                   Notice of Public Hearing
              The Riverton City Council has determined that it will not charge itself for any Riverton-provided
              utility services (Culinary Water, Secondary Water and Sanitation) used for municipal purposes.
              Utah Code 10-6-135 requires that the city notify all utility customers of its intent to not charge city
              departments for these utility services. A public hearing will be held on June 15, 2021, at 7 p.m.
              for the 2022 fiscal year. The public hearing will be held at Riverton City Hall at 12830 S Redwood
              Road, Riverton, Utah, to receive public comment on this action.

              This practice will result in the following non-cash transfers (amounts are approximated) for FY
              2022:

                      $145,000 from the Culinary Water enterprise fund to the General Fund
                      $20,000 from the Secondary Water enterprise fund to the General Fund
                      $62,000 from the Sanitation enterprise fund to the General Fund
