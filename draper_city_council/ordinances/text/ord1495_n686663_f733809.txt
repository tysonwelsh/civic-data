City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On June 23, 2021, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1495, prohibiting the
discharge of fireworks in specified areas. The complete ordinance is on file at the
Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 24th
day of June 2021. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
