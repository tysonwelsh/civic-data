                                       City Council Meeting Agenda
                                               June 2, 2026



PRESENTER:             Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director


ITEM:
Ordinance No. 26-11 - General Plan Amendment and Rezone of approximately 0.49-acre located at the
NW corner of Trestle Lane and 13400 South. Changing the General Plan Designation to Medium Density
Residential and the zoning to R-4. Applicant-David King - Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director
On May 14, 2026, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this item. Minutes from that
meeting are included below. The Planning Commission voted to recommend approval of this proposed
rezone, with the following recommended motion:
“I move the City Council adopt Ordinance 26-11, amending the General Plan Designation to Medium Density
Residential and rezoning to R-4 the .49 acre parcels located at the northwest corner of Trestle Lane and
13400 South as shown on Exhibit “A”.”


SUMMARY:


This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, David King for multiple parcels,
and/or portions of parcels at Trestle Lane and 13400 South. The subject parcels add up to 0.49-acres with
an existing home.
The parcels are located at 13382, 13392, and 13400 S. Trestle Lane, and 2426 West 13400 South. The
largest parcel is 0.36 acres is owned by David King. The remaining property is made of parts of overall
parcels 27-33-476-045 and 27-33-476-044 which are both owned by Riverton City. The property is part of
the previous road way, which was realigned to its current location several years ago. David King is seeking
this rezone as a first step towards a proposed purchase of property which would, combined with his existing
adjacent property, allow for potential development of an additional residential lot. City officials have
discussed the possible sale of the City property, but have not committed to selling the property, nor has the
City endorsed nor guaranteed the rezone
