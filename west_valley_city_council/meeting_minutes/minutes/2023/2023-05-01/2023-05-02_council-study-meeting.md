A.   POTENTIAL FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS
     Wayne Pyle, City Manager, stated that there was a presentation last week for the
     CTAA. He indicated that staff’s only concern would be that a board is typically
     designated to oversee funding of the money and allocates that funding as they see
     fit. He stated that staff would rather see money come back to the City directly and
     to have direct say in what it’s spent on. Councilmember Harmon asked if the setup
     of the board structure can be changed. Councilmember Whetstone replied it is set
     for 5 years. Councilmember Christensen stated that Kevin Bruder at the Maverik
     Center is confident in the proposal and the transparency of the organization. Mayor
     Lang stated that she is concerned that there is no guarantee that the money
     generated in West Valley City would be spent in West Valley City. She also
     expressed concern about their plan to program out ball fields when they are already
     fully booked. Wayne replied that the City would still have ultimate say in the use
     of the ball fields but this proposal could definitely apply pressure to specific
     programs. Councilmember Whetstone stated that he would like more information
     on how the funds would be controlled and allocated. He indicated that his
     understanding is that the hoteliers seem to have the greatest control.
     Councilmember Fitisemanu agreed and stated that the program is compelling but
     he would like a little more information. The Mayor and Council agreed to invite a
     representative to a Council meeting to discuss the issue further.
