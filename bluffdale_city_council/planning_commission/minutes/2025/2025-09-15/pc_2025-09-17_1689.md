Planning Manager, Caitlyn Tubbs, presented the Staff Report and stated that the request was for a
new Development Agreement on approximately 3.83 acres on 2950 West. The property is adjacent
to Bangerter Highway, 2950 West, and 13900 South in the GC-1 Zone. The existing Tri-City Center
Development Agreement for the subject property is entitled for a professional office building.
However, that building was not constructed and the land was for sale. Because the Applicant
proposed additional land uses not currently permitted in the zone, a new Development Agreement
was required.
Ms. Tubbs reviewed the Concept Plan, which specifies approximately 55,000 square feet of microflex
units. The Applicant proposed 46 units in three buildings, one interior building, one abutting the Utah
Department of Transportation ("UDOT") easement and Bangerter Highway, and one retail building
at the corner of 13900 South and 2950 West. The microflex units were intended for third-party sale
and range in size from approximately 1,100 to 1,500 square feet. Renderings were displayed,
reflecting the architectural style and development pattern. Building 2 will have a retail focus and
include additional pedestrian amenities and awnings.
The Applicant proposed the following deviations from existing GC-1 Zone requirements:

e Corner lot setbacks: 10 feet (GC-1 Zone requires 20 feet).
This deviation was requested to better fit the retail use and be more pedestrian-friendly.

proposed on the eastern facade of Building 1, and Staff suggested that the Planning Commission make
a recommendation to require glazing on that facade. Renderings of the proposed reduced glazing

City Planner, Ellen Oakman, presented the Staff Report and reviewed the Zoning Map. The subject
property is located on South Loumis Parkway across from Vintage Park and is currently zoned R-1-
43. Most surrounding properties are in the R-1-43 Cluster Residential Overlay (“CRO”) Zone, with
parcels to the north in the R-1-10 Infill Overlay (“IO”) Zone as proposed by the Applicant.
The property’s Land Use designation is Low Density Residential, which allows for one to four
dwelling units per acre and, as such, supports the rezone. Approximately 26 lots were proposed for
the 7.26-acre property, which would equate to 3.6 dwelling units per acre. Ms. Oakman reported that
Sage Estates Phase 3, north of the subject property, is approximately 3.14 dwelling units per acre.

standards, but Staff believed more information was required to clarify the proposal. She then provided
a summary of considerations.

proposal.
e A Development Agreement requiring Rose Creek Trail connections was recommended.
e Road connections to the south and north were recommended.
e The southern sliver should be included in the project proposal to allow south access.
e Recommended home attributes:
o Home sizes of 3,500 to 4,900 square feet
o Three-car garage

The IO Zone allows the City to assess a unique site with Code standards that may differ from the
underlying R-1-10 Zone. Because it is intended to facilitate compatible infill development, additional
information is required per Bluffdale City Code (“BCC”) 11.110.030.50 to demonstrate how the
proposed development would fit in with the surrounding area. The following additional information
was needed:

e Proposals for special requirements of the zone, including architectural controls, materials,
uses, massing, or other similar ideas.

e A detailed explanation of how the proposed use will be compatible and consistent with the
existing neighborhood.

e Proposed setback, width, frontage, parcel size, and other requirements of the zone.

The best way to demonstrate the IO Zone and lot standards would be through a Concept Plan that
identifies access to other neighborhoods and a Development Agreement specifying home attributes.
Ms. Oakman reported that County records show a small separate parcel south of the subject property.
Staff requested clarification on this parcel. If it exists, the road stub from Sage Estates Phase 2E could
not be connected. This needed to be clarified prior to rezoning approval. The relevant Sage Estates
Subdivision Plats and photographs of the existing cul-de-sac were displayed, indicating the intended
connections.

The Staff’s findings were as follows:

e Negotiations regarding Code standards must be conducted during this phase. Per State law,
they cannot be conducted after the property is rezoned.

e Road access must be clarified. It is intended to connect north to south. Per BCC
12.80.030.1A, Streets shall be located within a subdivision so they connect with existing
streets.
