Study Session

      Discussion: Town Center Station Area Plan
      Community Development Director Jennifer Jastremsky presented design
      concepts for the Town Center Station Area Plan (SAP). Councilmember Green
      said he would prefer to focus housing on the Utah Transit Authority (UTA) side
      and try to alleviate traffic on Pioneer Road, with Pioneer Road designed as
      more of a gathering place. Councilmember Green commented that the
      community was not used to anything higher than two stories along that
      corridor, and said he did not think the community would be supportive of
      putting taller buildings on City property. Ms. Jastremsky said throughout the
      process UTA had indicated five stories was the minimum for them.
      Councilmember T. Lowery encouraged staggered heights to avoid the
      appearance of a big block of building. The Council and staff discussed parking
      options.

      Ms. Jastremsky explained the next steps in the SAP process, including a
      meeting with the stakeholder committee, and a public open house.

4.   Presentation: Upcoming 2025 Utah State Legislative Session
     Utah House Representative Cal Roberts said he was grateful to serve in his
     new capacity on the House of Representatives and encouraged residents to
     reach out to him. Representative Roberts said he believed key issues during
     the 2025 Legislative Session would be: higher education and aligning higher
     education with workforce development; energy production; and housing
     affordability.
