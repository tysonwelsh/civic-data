  Conducting:     Chair George Handley

 Prayer – Melanie McCoard

 Pledge of Allegiance – Councilor Shannon Ellsworth

 Public Comment - 09:30

Melanie McCoard spoke on the work session issues. She stated that the zoning department needs
funding to help with enforcement. She also spoke on the need for a housing audit.

Semisi Moli Ngatuvai of Provo proposed to build a road next to the mountainside trail.

 Presentations, Proclamations, and Awards

 1.           Presentation of the Utah Chiefs of Police Association's Accreditation of the Provo Police
              Department. (22-044) 15:35

Troy Beebe, Acting Chief of Police presented.

Val Shupe, Utah Chiefs of Police presented the Provo Police Department with the Accreditation.

Mayor Kaufusi presented. She has recommended that David Hertado be appointed to this committee.

With no public comment or council discussion, Chair Handley called for a vote.

        Vote:         The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,

 3.           A resolution appointing an individual to the Civil Service Commission. (22-009) 21:30

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-59, as currently constituted, has been
                      made by council rule.

Gary Millward, Assistant City Attorney presented. He has recommended that Hollie Fluhman be
appointed to this committee.

With no public comment or council discussion, Chair Handley called for a vote.

        Vote:         The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                      MacKay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.

 4.           A resolution appropriating $15,050 in the General Fund for an Information Technology (IT)
              assessment. (22-080) 23:05

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-60, as currently constituted, has been
                      made by council rule.

Kelsey Zarbock presented.

Councilor MacKay stated she is glad they are doing this study.

With no public comment or council discussion, Chair Handley called for a vote.

        Vote:         The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                      MacKay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.

 5.           *CONTINUED* An ordinance amending the zone map classification of approx. .22 acres of
              property at 1220 E 960 S, from One Family Residential (R1.8) & Residential Conservation (RC)
              to Low Density Residential (LDR). Provost South (PLRZ20220244)

 Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned by unanimous consent at approximately 5:50 PM.
