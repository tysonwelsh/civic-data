Ms. Holly Yocom , Director, Community Services Department, stated 
she wanted to take this opportunity to recognize Council Member 
Burdick for his time and work as Chair of the Utah Performing Arts

Mayor Jennifer Wilson stated she wanted to be part of this 
presentation in order to thank Council Member Burdick for his 
ongoing commitment to the arts, especially his help in getting the 
Eccles Theater planned and built.   Council Member Burdick has 
impacted downtown Salt Lake in a big way.
Ms. Erin Litvack , Deputy Mayor of County Services, thanked 
Council Member Burdick for all the hours and time he has put in on 
the UPACA Board.  His contribution will be part of his legacy to the 
community.
Council Member Newton thanked Council Member Burdick for his 
work done on this board.  He has done a great job in representing the 
Council on the UPACA Board.  It has made a big difference.
Council Member Burdick stated it has been a great opportunity to 
serve on this board for the past seven years. This was a once in a 
lifetime opportunity, which he appreciated.   
Mr. Justin Belliveau , Chair, UPACA Board, read the following letter 
into the record:
Council Member Burdick:

I write today on behalf of the entire Board of Directors of the Utah 
Performing Arts Center to thank you for your leadership on the 
UPACA Board over the past seven years.  During your involvement 
with the Eccles Theater you have profoundly and positively impacted 
this glorious community asset in numerous ways.  As an 
organization, we have especially benefited over the term you served 
as board chair.  From early on in the planning you were a steady 
and consistent champion at every stage of the development of the 
theatre, from concept to construction and through opening, you 
played a most crucial role in securing both support and funding 
from Salt Lake County in this venture. It is indeed true that the 
Eccles Theatre simply would not exist today but for your diligence 
and care in evaluating and fully exploring the positive impact that 
this investment would have on our community
What has impressed us most in your tenure is the care and 
thoughtfulness that you have brought to every issue that we have 
encountered together.  The challenges that we have faced are many, 
but you have been relentless in your thorough analysis of the details 
of every issue always bringing the clearest understanding of us all of 
the founding documents and prioritizing the best interest of the 
Eccles Theatre in guiding us to resolution.  
The operational success of the Eccles Theatre is a testament to the 
value of your dedication to improving everything that you touch and 
the partnership that was forge along the way is a lasting legacy to 
your leadership.  We heartily share our congratulations to you in 
acknowledging a stellar tenure as board chair and look forward to 
brighter days ahead with you for the Eccles Theater. 
Thank you Max

THE FINAL 2021 BUDGET ON DECEMBER 1, 2020, AND PROPOSING

RECOMMENDED ADJUSTMENTS TO THE 2020 BUDGET FOR 
COUNCIL CONSIDERATION ON THE SAME DATE
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council, as required by the Uniform 
Fiscal Procedures Act for Counties, Utah Code Ann. §17-36-1, et seq. and 
by Salt Lake County ’s Budget Process Ordinance, Salt Lake County Code 
of Ordinances Chapter 2.93, is required to adopt by resolution an annual 
budget and compensation plan for 2021 on or before December 10, 2020; 
and 
WHEREAS, as a part of the required process for adopting the 2021 budget, 
the County Council must hold a public hearing on the budget, where all 
interested persons shall have an opportunity to be heard for or against the 
estimates of revenue and expenditures and performance data or any item in 
any fund; and
WHEREAS, the County Mayor has previously recommended, and the 
County Council has previously set, the date of December 1, 2020, as the 
appropriate date to hold a public hearing on the 2021 Salt Lake County 
budget; and
WHEREAS, as a part of the required process for adopting the 2021 budget, 
the County Council must propose a recommended budget including all 
supportive schedules and data, and shall make said recommended budget 
available as a public record available for inspection during business hours at 
the offices of the County Mayor, County Clerk, and County Auditor for at 
least 10 days prior to the public hearing on the adoption of a final budget; 
and
WHEREAS, the County Council is required by Utah law to publish and post 
certain public notice concerning the date of the public hearing on the 
adoption of a final budget; and
WHEREAS, the County Council also desires to make certain adjustments to 
the 2020 budget, which adjustments shall be proposed for ratification at the 
December 1, 2020, hearing:
NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Salt Lake County Council that:
1) The County Council hereby proposes a recommended budget for fiscal

year 2021, as more specifically described in Attachment A and any 
applicable schedules.
2) The County Council hereby sets the time and place of the public hearing 
on the final budget on December 1, 2020, at the hour of 6:00 pm, in the 
County Council Chambers, 2001 S. State St. N1-100, Salt Lake City, Utah, 
84114.  Council staff shall publish applicable public notice regarding the 
public hearing as required by Utah Code Ann. § 17-36-12.  Due to the 
ongoing COVID -19 pandemic, Council staff shall make preparations to 
conduct the public hearing electronically consistent with the provisions of the 
Utah Open and Public Meetings Act, and shall communicate to the public the 
methods(s) by which public comments may be received in writing and 
offered electronically during the hearing.  Council staff shall also advise the 
County Mayor, County Treasurer, and County Auditor of the public hearing 
prior to December 1, 2020, as required by County Ordinance.
3) The County Council hereby designates that copies of the recommended 
budget for fiscal year 2021, as more specifically described in Attachment A 
and any applicable schedules, shall be a public record made available for 
inspection during business hours at the offices of the County Council, 
County Mayor, County Clerk and County Auditor for at least 10 days prior 
to the public hearing on December 1, 2020.  Due to the ongoing COVID -19 
pandemic, members of the public should be advised that the Salt Lake 
County Government center is currently open by appointment only .  
Electronic copies of the recommended budget shall also be made available 
on the County Council’s website, https//slco.org/council/.
4) The County Council hereby proposes recommended year-end 
adjustments to the 2020 budget, which are intended to be effective for the 
2020 fiscal year, as more specifically described in Attachment B and any 
applicable schedules, and which may be considered for ratification at the 
public hearing on December 1, 2020.
