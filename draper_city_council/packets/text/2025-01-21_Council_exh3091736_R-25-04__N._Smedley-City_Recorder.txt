                                RESOLUTION NO. 25-04

      A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF THE CITY OF DRAPER
      TO APPOINT NICOLE SMEDLEY AS CITY RECORDER FOR THE CITY OF
      DRAPER

       WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint Nicole Smedley as City Recorder of the
City of Draper in accordance with the appointment procedures provided by law and
City Ordinance; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code 2-5-040 requires that the City Recorder
be appointed by a majority vote of the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code 2-5-020 requires the advice and consent
of the City Council in order for the Mayor to fill a vacancy in an appointed office; and

      WHEREAS, Nicole Smedley has the credentials, experience, and professionalism
necessary to be the City Recorder.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Appointment. The Mayor is hereby authorized to appoint Nicole
Smedley as City Recorder, in accordance with appointment procedures provided by law
and City Code.

       Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Resolution, and all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall
be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect on February 18, 2025.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 21ST DAY OF JANUARY 2025.
