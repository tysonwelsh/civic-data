COMMUNITY LISTENING SESSION FOR THE LITTLE COTTONWOOD CANYON ENVIRONMENTAL
               IMPACT STATEMENT FOR ALTA COMMUNITY MEMBERS
                    5:00-7:00 P.M. - Wednesday, July 28, 2021

                     This will be an electronic meeting without an anchor location.
                  A majority of the town council members are expected to be present.
                               No Town business will occur at this meeting.

Listening Session Purpose: To provide an opportunity for Alta community members to share
their thoughts related to the Little Cottonwood Canyon Environmental Impact Statement (LCC
EIS) with the Alta Town Council members. It is also a designated time where community
members can listen to one another. The Town is offering this event to provide a safe and
respectful opportunity for dialogue to strengthen the community’s understanding of the
project. Comments shared at this meeting will not be submitted as comment to the LCC EIS
project. The Town encourages community members to submit official comments on the EIS to
UDOT either in person at an official UDOT public comment event or through the project’s
website by September 3, 2021.

For information about the LCC EIS visit: https://littlecottonwoodeis.udot.utah.gov/
