Citizen Input
Robert Macfarlane stated when the sign indicating violators of the noise
ordinance would be sited, the noise level went down for a bit; however, the noise level has
increased again. He thinks that the public may have a sense that the noise violations are not
being aggressively enforced and that is why the noise levels are up. There is an equipment
violation that is easily identifiable and can be easily managed, it is not impossible to enforce.
Probable cause is obvious when vehicles are driving loudly.
Council Member Bowen thanked Mayor Smolka and the employees at the MSD
for talking about ditch work that is being done to help keep water off the road. There is a
tremendous asset to this work, and he hopes that it will be done on an annual basis. It is not just
water but when they widen the road, there was a massive amount of gravel and rocks in the bike
lanes. Instead of doing a weekly sweep in the canyon, he recommends that the cleaning and
widening the ditch and keeping the ditch a place where rocks can come down is done on an
annual basis. He also thanked Wayne Dial and Jake Elsasser from UPD for the benefits that the
community has received from enforcing the noise ordinance. An individual that does not live in
the canyon is also benefiting from the noise ordinance because of the sign that has been placed
at the bottom of the canyon. He hopes the sign will remain to help continue reducing noise.

Ordinance No. 21-09-01 – Internal Accessory Dwelling Units
ORDINANCE NO. 2021-09-01

Date: September 28, 2021

AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING TITLE 19, CHAPTER 15 TO REGULATE
INTERNAL ACCESSORY DWELLING UNITS
WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township recognizes the Internal Accessory
Dwelling Units in single-family residential zones can be an important tool in the overall housing
plan for Emigration Canyon Metro Township.
WHEREAS, State of Utah legislation allows for Internal Accessory Dwelling Units
generally and requires municipalities to adopt an ordinance if they wish to regulate certain
requirements of the dwellings;
WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township wishes to ensure that building permits or
licenses are not issued for an IADU until the applicant has provided written confirmation from the
applicable water supplier that the IADU complies with all applicable water service requirements.
WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township wishes to allow opportunities for property
owners to provide social or personal support for family members were independent living is
desirable and to provide for affordable housing opportunities;

METRO CODE AND ENACTING A NEW CHAPTER 19.72 TO ESTABLISH
FLOODPLAIN DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS AND FLOOD DAMAGE
PREVENTION
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township (“Emigration Canyon”) is a
municipality pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §10-2a-401, et seq; and
WHEREAS, one of the natural disaster risks that exist in Emigration Canyon is the risk of
flooding; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §§10-3-701 and 10-9a-101 et seq. the Utah
Legislature delegated the responsibility of local governmental units to adopt regulations designed
to minimize flood losses; and
WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon inherited and formally adopted Chapter 19.74 – Flood
Damage Prevention Ordinance – of the Salt Lake County Code in Chapter 19.74 of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Code (“ECMC”); and
WHEREAS; most of the Emigration Canyon area is designated by the Utah Geological
Survey as a high risk flood hazard area; and
WHEREAS, the Federal Emergency Management Agency (“FEMA”) mandates the
municipality adopt a flood control development ordinance that adequately covers and addresses
the flood control for risks that exist in Emigration Canyon to be a member entity of the National
Flood Insurance Program (“NFIP”) and
WHEREAS, the current ordinance that was inherited by Emigration Canyon, namely
Chapter 19.74 of the ECMC incorporating Chapter 19.74 of the Salt Lake County Code, has been
deemed unsatisfactory by the State and FEMA for covering and addressing Emigration Canyon’s
flood damage prevention measures due to development; and
WHEREAS, adopting a new ordinance to conform with the regulations of FEMA and the
State will ensure Emigration Canyon ability to maintain its membership in the National Flood
Insurance Program;
NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED: by the Emigration Canyon Metro Township
Council the following ordinance amendments, repeals, and additions to the Emigration Canyon
Municipal Code of Ordinances:
A. Chapter 19.74 is repealed in its entirety; and
B. Chapter 19.74 is adopted in the form attached hereto as Exhibit A
C. This ordinance shall become effective fifteen days after its passage and upon
publication of the ordinance, or a summary thereof on the Utah state notice
website.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Emigration Canyon, Salt Lake County, Utah this 28th day
of September, 2021.

Enforcement of the Noise Ordinance
Cameron Platt, Legal Counsel, stated Polly McLean and himself met with UPD,
the District Attorney’s Office and Salt Lake City Police administration to see how other agencies
have dealt with noise ordinances. Through those meetings we have gone through a workable way
to enforce the noise ordinance. The officers would use probable cause or would use their body
camera to identify extreme or unusual vehicles to make the stop. The Health Department will
come out with sound monitoring devices to identify vehicles that are exceeding the noise levels.
Chief Wayne Dial, UPD, stated working with the Health Department will help make
the prosecution of the violation more successful. He is going to try to get a grant from Highway
Safety to fund overtime patrol. The sign has been a tremendous help and he would like to get
another sign board to put at the top of the canyon. He is also looking at a legislature fix to bring
back vehicle inspections to help prevent the loud mufflers upfront.
Mayor Smolka asked about the effectiveness of the decibel meters in the Canyon.
Chief Dial stated that is why the officers will work with the Health Department so
there are experts that can testify to the equipment.
