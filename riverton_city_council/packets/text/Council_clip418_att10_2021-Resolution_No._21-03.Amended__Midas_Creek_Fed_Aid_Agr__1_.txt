                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 21-03

   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY TO AMEND THE FEDERAL AID
  AGREEMENT WITH THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION FOR
 PROJECT PIN NUMBER 14945 MIDAS CREEK TRAIL; 4000 WEST TO 4510 WEST
                             PROJECT

              WHEREAS, Riverton City has secured $182,285.00 federal funding through the
Transportation Alternative Program (TAP) for the construction of a portion of the Midas Creek
Trail from 4000 W to 4510 W; and

       WHEREAS, the Utah Department of Transportation has the responsibilities to
administer and spend federal funds awarded to cities through the Transportation Alternative
Program (TAP); and

       WHEREAS, the Federal Aid Agreement designates Trace Robinson as the City’s
representative and has asked him to sign the agreement as engineer for the City; and

      WHEREAS, a Federal Aid Agreement is required for The Utah Department of
Transportation to authorize the City to proceed with the project; and

       WHEREAS, the design is now complete, all Federal requirements have been met, and
the Project is ready for bid; and

        WHEREAS, the Federal process requires an Engineers estimate prior to bidding the
Project; and

        WHEREAS, the Engineers estimate exceeds the Project amount shown in the executed
Federal Aid Agreement by $102,200.00; and

        WHEREAS, the executed Federal Aid Agreement obligates the City to pay Project costs
that exceed the amount identified in the Agreement; and

       WHEREAS, UDOT has provide an Amended Federal Aid Agreement for the City to
execute that includes the $102,200.00 as part of the City match for the Project; and

        WHEREAS, UDOT requires the City to approve the Amended Federal Aid Agreement
prior to advertising and bidding the Project; and

      WHEREAS, the Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the health, safety
and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to approve the amended Federal Aid Agreement in order
complete the construction of the Midas Creek Trail; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council does approve of the Amended Federal Aid
Agreement and cooperative action contemplated by said agreement.
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

          1. Aforesaid amendment to the Federal Aid Agreement for the Midas Creek Trail;
             4000 West to 4510 West, copy of which is incorporated herein by reference, be,
             and hereby is, approved.

          2. Riverton City Council authorizes Trace Robinson to execute the same as the
             City’s representative and Project Manager for the Project.

          3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 19th day of
January 2021 by the following vote:
