MINUTES OF THE SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL MEETING
 FRIDAY, JANUARY 17, 2020
 The City Council of Salt Lake City, Utah, met in Special Formal Session
 on Friday, January 17, 2020 in Room 326, Committee Room, City County
 Building, 451 South State .
 The following Council Members were present :
 James Rogers Chris Wharton Analia Valdemoros
 Amy Fowler Andrew Johnston Daniel Dugan
 Cindy Gust-Jenson, Council Executive Director; Erin Mendenhall,
 Mayor; Rachel Otto, Mayor' s Chief of Staff; Katherine Lewis, City
 Attorney; and Scott Crandall, Deputy City Recorder; were present .
 Councilmember Wharton presided at and conducted the meeting.
 The meeting was called to order at 5 : 09 p.m.
 NEW BUSINESS
 #1. 5:09:02 PM Adopting an ordinance enacting a temporary land use
 regulation to allow temporary overflow homeless shelter at approximately
 2234 South Highland Drive . View Attachment
 Councilmember Rogers moved and Councilmember Johnston seconded to
 adopt Ordinance 1 of 2020, enacting temporary zoning regulations
 authorizing overflow homeless shelter use at approximately 2234 South
 Highland Drive, which motion carried, all members voted aye.
 Councilmember Wharton thanked everyone who worked on the proposal
 and provided support (Mayor/Staff, County, State, service providers,
 community organizations, etc . ) . He said he echoed comments/concerns made
 during the prior meeting and encouraged the public/others who were
 interested in donating, volunteering, or searching for information to
 visit the City' s website at : www. slc.gov/temporary-shelter.
 (0 20-2)
 The meeting adjourned at 5 : 10 p.m.
