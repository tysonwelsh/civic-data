GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
GPZ-5-2020 Petition by James Graham requesting a General Plan change from Rural
           Residential (1 to 2 units/acre) to Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre) and a
           zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RS (Residential
           Sustainability, minimum lot size 8,000 sq. ft.). The property is located at 1580 W
           Whitlock Ave. on 3.05 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545)

Discussion:     Steve explained how this applicant has made several applications to the City starting in
                2017 that were not approved. Steve explained in reviewing the past applications it
                appeared that the Planning Commission and City Council were trying to retain the A
                zone in the subject area. Chairperson Meaders asked Steve if there is a way to put
                agricultural rights within a development agreement. Steve stated if they rezone the
                agricultural rights would no longer apply due to the lot size being too small for the A
                zone. Commissioner Lovato asked if the applicant could propose to change the General
                Plan and not the zone. Steve explained they could change just the General Plan;
                however, the proposed zone would still be the only zone that would fit for this subject
                property.
                Commissioner Winters stated remembering the Planning Commission denying a past
                application because the development would be much larger than the surrounding
                properties and would not be a good fit. Commissioner Fuller feels that the neighbors
                would not approve of this proposal. Chairperson Meaders explained with the prior
                proposals the neighbors were concerned about new residents having complaints about
                the agriculture zone. Steve agreed with Chairperson Meaders. He stated there was talk
                that the Chesterfield Overlay Zone would be amended to retain the agriculture zone.
                This would enable the area to keep the same look, no curb, and gutters, lot size, etc.
                Commissioner Winters asked Steve if City Council stated when they are looking to
                review the overlay zone and if the applicant is aware of the review. Steve explained he
                is not aware when the review will happen. Steve did explain this to the applicant a
                while back and when the process was put on hold, they gave up on that process.

                Commissioner Fuller stated his feeling is the City is trying to build these larger
                developments in areas like the subject property thinking the neighborhood would then
                change.

                Chairperson Meaders asked Steve if he has received any calls from concerned
                neighbors. Steve stated he hasn’t had any calls thus far.



There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:55 p.m.
