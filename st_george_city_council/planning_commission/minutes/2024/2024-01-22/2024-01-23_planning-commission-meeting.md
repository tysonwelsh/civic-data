   1. White Sage Terrace Planned Development Amendment and Preliminary Plat – Ryan McDougal,
      representing McHyve LLC, is requesting approval of a Planned Development Amendment and a
      Preliminary Plat to develop 51 townhome units on 5.92 acres located at approximately 2000 S Mesa Palms
      Way. The applicant is McHyve LLC. (Staff – Mike Hadley)

       Mike Hadley presented the following:

       Mike Hadley – This is general planned MDR which allows 9 units per acre. They built the units with the
       design features to face the residential neighbors and the garages to face the road. So, the garages face the
       interior of the project, and the architectural features face the existing residential.

       Commissioner Draper – The site isn’t level; can you speak to how that will affect the project?

       Ryan McDougal – We have some significant elevation change from north to south. A couple of things we
       are designing into the neighborhood is that we have patios built into the units to take advantage of the
       views to the golf course. Some of the units that are facing south will back up into the hill and the ones that
       are facing north will have exposed basements. The elevation on the road itself is not extreme, the middle
       is a flatter area. As Mike said this area was approved for 53 units, economically it didn’t make sense to
       put in that many units because of the elevations on the site. We took out some of the units because of the
       expense of the retaining walls it would take to build them.

       Commissioner Draper – Will you have any retaining walls?

      Ryan McDougal – We will have some, but we want to do it more organically.

      Commissioner Rogers – How long since you purchased the property from the original owner did it take
      to put the road in?

      Ryan McDougal – I’m not sure of all the history or what we will find when we get over there, I know
      some of the water is put in, but I don’t know all of the history.

      Commissioner Rogers – I know it created significant drainage issues with Beehive homes, I can see a
      small retaining pond on your property, what else have you done for the drainage issues?

      Ryan McDougal – You can see one of our amenities is a pickleball court, that will mitigate any storm,
      that will be any water that flows on the street will go into those. We have this as well as part of our storm.

      Vice Chair Chapman – The only retention that you have on this project, it looks like there are some black
      lines scattered throughout, there is a really big drop off by the Beehive homes. I was out there today, and
      it drops substantially.

      Ryan McDougal – Beehive already has a large retaining wall here, we won’t be messing with the slope
      here, our units will have walk out basements.

      Vice Chair Chapman – On the upper side, how much of the slope will be taken away?

      Ryan McDougal – I’m not an engineer, but the units themselves will back into the hill and be part of that
      retaining. Then you see these walls here that will be to City standards. The units themselves and then we
      will have retaining walls. It is sloped substantially now; any changes we make we will mitigate it.

      Commissioner Rogers – I’m comfortable with Ryan’s explanation.

      Commissioner B. Anderson – Are you going to over ex or put it on piers?

      Ryan McDougal – We will put it on piers.

      Vice Chair Chapman – How large is Mesa Palms Drive?

      Wes Jenkins – Like he indicated there was a bond on this road years ago, the which is a big production.
      As someone indicated, they built their portion of the road, and it will have to come in on the west side
      based on development.

      Vice Chair Chapman – Are we confident that the traffic on the road with this addition is not going to be
      an issue?

      Wes Jenkins – Yes. It’s something the City will have to watch and monitor, their development by itself
      would not have to finish the development of the road.
