                                   RIVERTON, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 25-27

APPROVING RIVERTON CITY'S ENTRY INTO AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT
     FOR THE MUTUAL AID OF MUNICIPALITIES IN THE EVENT OF
                        CYBERATTACK

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council ("Council") met in regular meeting on May 6,
2025 to consider, among other things, approving Riverton's participation in mutual aid
among participating municipalities and Central Utah 911 to address the need for mutual aid
in the event of a cyberattack; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that mutual aid among
municipalities is a crucial component for each participating municipalities to provide a
sufficient defense against cyberattack; and

        WHEREAS, the Utah Local Cooperative Act (UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-101, et
seq.) provides that two local governmental entities are authorized to enter agreements with
each other, upon a resolution to do so by their respective governing bodies, to do what each
agency is authorized by law to perform; and

        WHEREAS, all the Parties, as defined in the Interlocal Agreement attached to this
resolution are governmental entities with IT and public safety resources which would be of
value for use in response to a cyberattack; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton has determined that it is best interests of the inhabitants of
Riverton to enter into the Interlocal Agreement to provide for mutual aid, a copy of which is
attached hereto.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as
follows:

   1. That the Mayor is authorized to sign an interlocal agreement to provide for mutual aid
      in the event of a cyberattack, in substantially similar form to the copy attached to this
      Resolution, upon verification that said agreement is approved as to legal form by the
      Riverton City Attorney.

   2. This resolution, assigned No. 25-27, shall take effect immediately upon
      passage and acceptance as provided herein.
