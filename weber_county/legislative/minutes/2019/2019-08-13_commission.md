      2. MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH UTAH RECREATION COMPANY FOR THE COLLECTION OF
         VARIOUS FEES AT PINEVIEW RESERVOIR.
            Chief Brandon Roundy, of the County Sheriff's Office, presented this 5-year contract with Utah Recreation, the
            concessionaire contracted with the Forest Service for the coordination of the campgrounds, recreational
            operations, etc. Utah Recreation will collect various fees including boat launch and anchor permit fees to
            provide revenue for law enforcement, operation costs and equipment by the Sheriff’s Office on Pineview. They
            will collect a fee per boat launch and will pay the Sheriff’s Office $1/boat launch. They will also pay
            $8,000/year towards services with a $25,000 cap. The anchor permit fee is charged for overnight watercraft
            usage and the county has agreed that they collect 16% of that overall fee and provide 84% to the Sheriff’s
            Office. This contract started in May.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the Memorandum of Understanding with Utah Recreation Company
            for the collection of various fees at Pineview Reservoir; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

     6. RATIFY A CONTRACT WITH THE TOWN OF HUNTSVILLE FOR COLLECTION AND REIMBURSEMENT OF
        FUNDS COLLECTED BY BASECAMP.
         Holin Wilbanks, of County Economic Development, stated that the county collects a $3.00 fee for the boat
         launches at Cemetery Ramp, Anderson Cove and Port Ramp, which go directly into the restricted account for
         the county to make improvements to the Pineview and Causey areas. The county will collect the $3 on behalf
         of the Huntsville Town at Cemetery Point and reimburse them back to the Town. She addressed Commissioner
         Froerer’s question stating that there is a 16% fee that the vendor collects that will come off overall and the
         county will then disburse the funds to the Town. The contract is just for this season and will be up for future
         renegotiations.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to ratify the contract with the Town of Huntsville for collection and
         reimbursement of funds collected by Basecamp; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
     7. TOWER LEASE AGREEMENT WITH VERIZON WIRELESS.
         Sean Wilkinson, County Community Development Director, stated that Verizon approached the county about
         leasing some tower space as well as a small area of ground for their equipment. The county owns this area in
         Eden where the Ogden Valley Translator District has its equipment and the Sheriff’s Office has some of its
         communication equipment. Verizon will pay the county $18,000 annually. In the past the county agreed with
         the former landowner, Eden Heights, LLC, and they will receive 35% of the revenue that the county obtains
         from Verizon. The county received the property from Eden Heights a few years ago but in exchange for the
         property it was required to give them that 35% cut of any revenue the county receives from the tower for 25
         years. Two years have already expired. The initial agreement with Verizon runs for five years with two
         additional 5-year terms that Verizon can opt out of but the county cannot, with a total of up to four additional
         5-year terms.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the Tower Lease Agreement with Verizon Wireless; Commissioner
         Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
