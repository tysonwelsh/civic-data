1)
Work Session to discuss and receive direction on proposed amendments to Chapter 3: Housing and Chapter 4: Transportation and Connectivity of the Snyderville Basin General Plan. This item is for discussion and direction only; no recommendation or action will be taken by the Planning Commission.
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner, Mustapha Osman County Planner. (4:39 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Work Session Report March 10, 2026.pdf
Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner
, introduced the work session focused on proposed amendments to Chapter 3: Housing of the General Plan. Planner Milliner presented the goals and objectives outlined in the plan. (4:40 P.M.)
Commissioners asked detailed questions and provided comments. Planner Milliner and Community Development Director Peter Barnes responded to their concerns. Items discussed included a Moderate-Income Housing Assessment, 55+ living development possibilities, additional housing unit direction from the planning department, seasonal employee housing, setting income standard definitions, HTRZ coordination, NMU improvements, housing unit size and specificity, and locations for building housing in the Snyderville area, as well as what is appropriate within those locations. Commissioners requested additional clarity on income levels, and levels of diversity, with a particular emphasis on home ownership within a high-cost community. (4:49 P.M.)
Planner Milliner transitioned the work session conversation to Chapter 4: Transportation of the General Plan. Planner Milliner briefly discussed the transportation documents and items that can be addressed within the planning areas, with the goal of promoting safe, efficient and connected transportation systems through a variety of transportation options. (5:33 P.M.)
Commissioners directed the transportation discussion to active transportation and bicycle-first infrastructure, seeking the delineation between various recreational users and bicycle users. Comments and questions were heard regarding accessibility and integration, and smart transportation infrastructure implementation. It was stated that a piece of resolving the transportation problem would need to start by looking at traffic origination such as commuters, visitors and workers coming in from Salt Lake City. Discussion continued with the suggestion to go beyond buses and think about other options, such as trams or a train, with more of a focus on the big picture. (5:36 P.M.)

Public Hearing and Action regarding a Snyderville Basin Development Code Amendment for Section 10-8-2 (Sign Regulations) and associated Definitions; Applicant: Summit County Staff; Legislative Process. Project #26-029.

Attachment:

Attachment:
Scenic Utah Recommendations
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner,
presented code amendments to Section 10-8-2 (Sign Regulations) and associated definitions for public hearing and action. Planner Caus provided recommendations to amend lumens with nits and dimming controls, shut off hours for dark sky compliance, and a requirement for digital sign certifications from the sign manufacturer. He introduced options for additional static language and considerations for shielding, direction and low-profile monument signage. Lastly, Planner Caus suggested a sign removal amendment to enhance code enforcement with respect to signage in right-of-ways.
(6:13 P.M.)
Commissioners provided feedback and asked clarifying questions about the amendments presented. Planner Caus responded to their concerns. (6:23 P.M.)
Scott Buchanan, Code Enforcement Officer, responded to a question regarding code enforcement. (6:39 P.M.)
Commissioners discussed and considered static language options and language for shielding and low-profile signage.
(6:44 P.M.)
Commissioner Young opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:47 p.m.
Francisca Blanc with Scenic Utah was called to speak at 6:48 p.m.
Commissioner Young closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:52 p.m.
Commissioners offered follow-up questions. (6:52 P.M.)
Eric Sagerman made a motion to send a positive recommendation to the County Council to approve the Code Amendments for Section 10-8-2 Sign Regulations subject to the additional conditions of adding 24-hour static language discussed, fewer restrictions for the resorts - specifically exempt traffic and related restrictions for static, no audio on any signage with the exception of drive through signs for ordering purposes, and the addition of the word "both" to shielding, and the code enforcement language subject to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval stated. Matthew Nagie seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
