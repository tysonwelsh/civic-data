II.   COUNCIL MEMBER CANDIDATE INTERVIEWS
Matt Dahl said on Tuesday, December 10, 2024, Council Member Dustin Gettel was
appointed as the Mayor of Midvale City. This appointment created a vacancy for City
Council District 5, which must be filled within 30 days of the date of Mayor Gettel’s
resignation as a City Council Member.

The City Council set the schedule for filling the vacancy, and the vacancy notice and
application were published. Applications were accepted from qualified candidates until
Monday, December 30, 2024 at 5:00 p.m.

1. Candidate Interviews
The City Council will interview the candidates and evaluate the submitted applications
and letters of interest to determine their qualifications and suitability for the position.

       • Each qualified candidate will be interviewed by the City Council.

       • Candidates will speak in the order their applications were submitted.

       • Each candidate will have up to 3 minutes to speak.

       • Up to an additional 5 minutes per candidate is allotted for questions from the
       Council.

2. Voting Process for Council District 5 Selection

       • A candidate must be approved by the majority of the City Council to be selected.

       • Voting will be conducted by ballot. In each round, council members will receive a
       personalized ballot to select their chosen candidate. The City Recorder and City
       Attorney will collect and count the ballots, announce the results, and place the
       ballots in an envelope labeled with the voting round. This process ensures the vote
       count is verified by two officials and the ballots from each round are kept separate
       for future reference.
       • If the first round of voting does not result in a candidate receiving the majority
       vote of the Council, a second round of voting will be conducted between the two
       candidates with the most votes in the first round of voting.

       • As provided for in State law, the City Recorder will conduct a coin toss to break
       a tie in the event of a tie between candidates to determine which candidate(s) will
       advance to the second round of voting.

3. Resolution of Appointment
After selecting a candidate to fill the City Council District 5 vacancy, the City Council will
proceed with the formal approval of a resolution appointing the selected candidate to
serve as Council Member for District 5 in Midvale City for the remainder of the current
term, commencing January 7, 2025, and concluding January 5, 2026.

4. Swearing-In Ceremony
The City Recorder will administer the oath of office to the newly appointed Council
Member for District 5 immediately following the resolution’s approval.

LUKE MAYNES

       A. VOTING PROCESS AND SELECTION OF COUNCIL MEMBER FOR
           DISTRICT 5.
Mayor Gettel explained the voting process. In the first round 4 council members will vote.
To win in the first round a candidate must have 3 or 4 votes. Voting will be conducted by
a ballot. Iin each round the council will receive a personalized ballot to select their
candidate. The City Recorder and City Attorney will collect and count the ballots. The
results will be handed to Mayor Gettel, and he will announce the results. The ballots will
be placed in an envelope and the process will be verified by the City Recorder and the
City Attorney.

Any ballots from a second round will be kept separate from the ballots from the first round.
If the first round does not result in a candidate receiving a majority vote, a second round
will be conducted immediately. The second round will be conducted between the two
candidates with the most votes in the first round. In the event of a tie, the City Recorder
will conduct a coin toss as provided by State law.

First ballot results:
       Denece Mikolash – 2 votes
       Jeanette Hernandez Heltman – 1 vote
       Wayne Sharp – 1 vote

Mayor Gettel announced that there would be a coin toss between Jeanette Hernandez
Heltman and Wayne Sharp.

Matt Dahl explained that the City Recorder will conduct the toss. She will be using the
same coin that was used during the mayoral selection process. The Liberty side is heads
and the Eagle side is tails. The assignment of heads and tails to the candidates is based
on the order they submitted their application resulting in Jeanette Hernandez Heltman as
heads and Wayne Sharp as tails.

The coin is flipped, and the toss came up as tails, Wayne Sharp will proceed to the second
round of voting.

Second ballot results:
