28   2. Presentations and Reports
29
30   Chief Craft reported on the residential fire in Lehi that occurred earlier today.
31
32   2.1 Park Concept Plan Discussion (park is located at approximately 197 W 2875 N)
33   Steve Marchbanks, Parks Manager, presented some concept ideas for the park which included
34   large open space, minimal turf, walking paths, and shade structure. Councilor Koivisto expressed
35   concern with these types of things being on the agenda when it may be ready for the public to
36   view. She stated that she’s generally concerned when a plan is presenting in early planning
37   phases that may not come to fruition and that can be frustrating for residents. Mayor Johnson
38   stated that he believes the Council should look at these things and that most will know that it’s
39   only a concept and that it may not be the final product. The other Councilmembers present
40   agreed that it was okay for this to be an agenda item.
41
42   3. Agenda Questions
43   Ryan Wood, City Attorney, stated that he found that State Code won’t allow for a change to the
44   financial disclosures in the middle of the elections. He said the Council could still pass the
45   ordinance, but it would not take affect until the next election.

93   5. Consideration of Ordinance #54-2021, amending the Lehi City Municipal Code Section
94      1-9-4, campaign finance disclosure requirements.
95      There was discussion regarding an additional financial disclosure date. Councilor Hancock
96      stated that because we use vote by mail, it’s possible for voters to submit their ballot before
     Lehi City Council Meeting                        2                                September 28, 2021

 98       future elections. There was also discussion of “soft money” and whether that should be part
 99       of the disclosures and how it would be enforced.

103       Aaron Bullen had some concerns with enforcement of disclosing soft money.

105       Jesse Riddle stated that his biggest soft with soft money is when a developer is involved.
