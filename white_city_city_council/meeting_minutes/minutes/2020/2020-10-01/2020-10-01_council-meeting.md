Community Stakeholders

White City Community Council

                    Council Member Price stated the plant swap event was held on September 26.
There were approximately 100 people that brought seeds and plants to swap. Everyone in
attendance wore masks and social distanced. For those that did not have a mask, one was
provided to them. There was a sign in sheet that at least one person from each family was required
to fill out, so if there was an exposure, contact tracing could be done.

Reports from Municipal Providers

Unified Fire Authority

                Chief Bruce Cline, Sandy City Fire Department, stated there were 31 calls for
service in September. Make sure that smoke detector and carbon monoxide detectors are
working. There was one car fire and a couple of small brush fires due to the windstorm and wires
being down. Salt Lake City and VECC are on the same computer aided dispatch (CAD) system.
Calls are now being dispatched to the closest unit using automatic vehicle locator (AVL) system.
Sandy Fire Department has rolled out a new drug disposal program. Individuals can pick up a
packet of powder that needs to be mixed with the water and the prescription drug. This will destroy
the drug and then it can be disposed of. Red Ribbon weeks is coming up for Canyons School
District and the fire department will be educating families on proper disposal of prescription drugs.

                Captain Ken Aldridge stated in September Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
responded to emergencies in various states with 55 personnel deployed. The deployment did not
affect the staffing at the fire stations. New captain bootcamp will have 50 potential candidates
attend. Eventually a ranked list will be created and remain in use for 18 months. Recruit camp
starts in February 2021, the top 90 individuals will move on to oral interviews. Light the Night week
was kicked off to honor fallen firefighters. The CAD system and no boarder response to calls is
working well.

Council Business

Resolution No. 20-10-01

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving and authorizing the
execution of an interlocal cooperation agreement with the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling
District (WFWRD) and to pay WFWRD $1,240.69 from White City’s CARES Act funding for
WFWRD’s COVID-related expenses:

       A RESOLUTION OF WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AND AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION
       AGREEMENT WITH THE WASATCH FRONT WASTE AND RECYCLING
       DISTRICT AND TO PAY WFWRD $1,240.69 FROM WHITE CITY’S CARES ACT
       FUNDING FOR WFWRD’S COVID-RELATED EXPENSES

RESOLUTION NO. 20-10-01                                                      Date: October 1, 2020

       WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City” or the “Municipality”) recognizes
our need for municipal-types service; and

         WHEREAS, the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (“WFWRD”) is a Local
District that provides trash collection and recycling services to White City; and

        WHEREAS, Salt Lake County is a County pursuant to Utah Code Title 17: COUNTIES
and is the County where White City is located; and

        WHEREAS, due to the impacts to state and local governments nationwide, the Unified
States Government passed the CARES Act which, through Salt Lake County provided White City
with certain funding that could be used to cover unbudgeted COVID-related costs for the delivery
of municipal-type services; and

       WHEREAS, as White City’s municipal services are provided by local districts and interlocal
agencies, it is prudent for White City to assist them with their unbudgeted COVID-related costs;
and

      WHEREAS, WFWRD has presented White City with an interlocal agreement (subgrant
agreement) with an invoice and purchase information to request $1,240.69 from White City’s


                                                 3
DATE           THURSDAY                                                      OCTOBER 1, 2020

CARES Act funds to cover WFWRD’s unbudgeted COVID-related costs, hereinafter known as
Attachment A; and

      WHEREAS, providing this financial assistance benefits White City and softens the impacts
caused to a service provider due to COVID-19; there be it

        RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council approves, and authorizes the Mayor
to sign, the attached interlocal cooperation agreement with the Wasatch Front Waste and
Recycling District (ATTACHMENT A) and authorizes the payment of $1,240.69 to WFWRD to
assist them in covering their unbudgeted COVID-related costs.

      APPROVED AND ADOPTED THIS 1st DAY OF OCTOBER, 2020 in White City Metro
Township, Salt Lake County, Utah.

Council Member Reports

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                Council Member Perry stated UFA has been busy with deployments but also
making sure stations are fully staffed. Chief Peterson said the area on fire in Oregon is in a similar
residential landscape as Utah. He is working on creating a more streamlined process to request
aid if an emergency happens in the future. The automatic vehicle location (AVL) response system
will be reviewed after six months. UFSA has formed a finance committee, it is preparing and
analyzing new stations.

                                   −−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

                Mayor Flint stated the tentative budget was adopted. There is not any new
information relating to COVID-19. UPD is trying to get new recruits and discussing merit increases
and cost of living. The Kearns precinct is not adequate for the officers, so the board is looking at
updating the building. Chief Levi Hughes is the new chief for Kearns.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

               Council Member Price stated there were four pools that tested for West Nile virus.
There was one virus case in the entire state and no issues with Zika. The amount of acreage that
was treated is down and test results are coming back quicker with the new lab. All conferences

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

              Mayor Flint stated GSLMSD celebrated its five-year anniversary. The GSLMSD

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

                   Pam Roberts, General Manager, Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District,
stated staff proposed the 2021 budget with a 1.5 percent cost of living and a 2 percent merit
increase. The modified clean-up will stay the same for 2021. There are two driver vacancies that
it is hiring for. The tentative 2021 budget will be adopted in October. A small fee increase is being
considered for green waste and non-residential services.
