                                     TAYLORSVILLE,                   UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE                NO. 25- 10


AN ORDINANCE          OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE                              AMENDING     TAYLORSVILLE
                  MUNICIPAL         CODE CHAPTER 2. 13: JUDICIAL CODE


       WHEREAS,        the   Taylorsville     City Council ( the " Council")            met during its regularly
scheduled meeting     on     September      17,   2025,       to   consider,    among   other   things,   amending
TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL        CODE Chapter 2. 13: Judicial Code; and


       WHEREAS, the City is in the process of moving the location of its justice court to better
and more efficiently serve the needs of the City and the public; and

       WHEREAS, the location of the Taylorsville Justice Court is designated by city ordinance;
and


       WHEREAS, the proposed amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 2. 13:
Judicial Code as detailed herein below, designate the new location of the Taylorsville Justice
Court; and


       WHEREAS,       after careful consideration,            the Council has determined        that it would be in
the best interest of the City and the public to amend TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter
2: 13: Judicial Code as proposed.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 2. 13: Judicial Code are hereby
approved and are designated by interlineating the words to be deleted and underlining the words
to be added, as follows:


                                 TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE
                                    Chapter 2. 13: Judicial Code


2. 13. 050: TRIAL FACILITY AND HOURS OF BUSINESS


The Justice Court shall be located       at 2600 West Taylorsville Blvd., Taylorsville,              Utah 8040 S
Redwood Rd, West Jordan, Utah. All official court business shall be conducted at that location
unless otherwise determined by the Mayor in the case of a declared emergency. The Justice Court
shall be open and judicial business shall be transacted five ( 5) days a week except for holidays.
Regular operating hours shall be as determined from time to time by the City Council. The hours
of operation for small claims matters shall be Thursday evening, as needed, and at such times as
defined from time to time by the Justice Court. The hours ofthe court shall be posted conspicuously
at the Justice Court and in any local public building. In addition to other duties, the Judges and
court staff shall attend be present at the court at regular scheduled times. All calendared court
events shall commence promptly as scheduled.
