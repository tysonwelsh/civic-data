      ORDINANCE 09-16-25 – ZONE CHANGE AMENDMENT
             Mtn. Peak Development LLC requested a zone change for the North Ridge Estates Plats
             F, G, and H from the R1-H zone to the R1-8. The R1-8 zone has a minimum of 8000
             square feet and the R1-H minimum is .25 acres. If the zone is changed to R1-8, the
             minimum will be .22 or higher because of the development agreement just signed.
             Shauna Talbot explained that the Planning commission had a split vote concerning the
             change. She explained that zone was decided on annexation, and a lot has happened
             since then (Covid, increase in housing market, increased interest rates). Ms. Talbot
             stated that the .22 lots are consistent with other developments in the area and allow
             people to get attainable housing.
             Councilor Parady inquired about plans for green space in the development. The
             developer mentioned the possibility of using unbuildable lots with power lines for
             community use. Mr. Atkinson discussed the parks impact fee and the potential for using
             it to purchase and develop green spaces.
             Councilor Parady made the motion to approve Ordinance 09-16-25 – Zone Change
               Amendment. Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on the
               following roll call vote:
                      YES: Shari Cowan, Travis Worwood, Skip Worwood, JD Parady, Jeramie
                      Callaway
                      NO: None

STREET VACATION—ANDERSON RANCHES
      When the Anderson Ranch Subdivision was rezoned, Nephi City asked Mr. Warburton to
      clean up any boundary line disputes, including overlaps and gaps before a rezone would
      be issued (a rezone policy of the city). Mr. Warburton has previously cleaned up all
      boundaries except for certain small slivers of land owned by Nephi City. These sliver
      pieces are not beneficial to the city’s purposes at this location, and due to the power
      lines and poles owned by both Nephi City, and Rocky Mountain Power on both the East
      and West sides of 300 W, the road right-of-way is permanently established already and
       cannot expand. Relinquishing the sliver properties will not have any noticeable impact
       on any current or future traffic needs or access. Mr. Warburton is having Juab Title
       create a quit claim deed from Nephi City to Mr. Warburton that would be recorded
       directly before recording the subdivision that will finalize all boundary cleanup.
       Councilor Shari Cowan made a motion to approve Nephi City vacating the property at
       300 W. Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on
       unanimous vote.
