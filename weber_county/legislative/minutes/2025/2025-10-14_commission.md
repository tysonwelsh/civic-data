G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH LACIE DOMAN, MOUNTAIN BREW COFFEE TO OPERATE A
      CAFÉ/COFFEE SHOP IN THE WEBER COUNTY LIBRARY SOUTH WEST BRANCH.
            Lynnda Wangsgard (Library Director): Growing up in the Ogden Valley we had our first spaces which was
            our homes, second places such as school and work; and we had some wonderful third places like our local
            grocery store, post office, and markets where people would discuss things going on in the community, their
            families, (etc). Times have changed and now those third places are gone, not just in Huntsville but across
            America. Research shows there is a areal disconnectedness and loneliness across all age groups in our
            communities. In response, libraries across the nation have evolved into modern-day third places. Public
            libraries are hosting crafting meetings, art exhibits, makerspaces, job training, cooking, and exercise classes.
            By year end, your five public library buildings will have hosted 3,900 programs attended by 29,500 people
            making connections. They will have hosted nearly 5,800 community meetings attended by 185,000 people.
            No matter how many programs are offered, few things bring people together better than food and drink.
            Sharing a cup of coffee or a sandwich breaks down barriers and turns strangers into acquaintances and
            acquaintances into friends. A café in a library doesn’t serve coffee, it serves conversation, comfort, and
            community. Each week of the year, enough people visit our libraries to fill the Dee Event Center 1.5 times.
            Café proposals were sent out via Bonfire, social media, emails, flyers, and the local newspaper. The proposals
            were evaluated by a diverse group of committee members, board members, staff members, and friends of the
         library. Today there are four contracts being proposed covering four libraries including North Branch, Main
         Library, Pleasant Valley, and Southwest with three different local vendors. Each contract is for five years with
         options for two renewals. Target opening date is November 15th. Each contract includes an automatic rent
         increase indexed to the consumer price index and a clause stipulating when they are really, really successful,
         we will receive a small portion of their income.

         Chair Bolos: I see Item G3 title only covers the Main Branch. It was supposed to also cover the Pleasant View
         Branch.
         Chris Crockett: The Pleasant View Branch contract can be added to next week’s Consent Items agenda.
