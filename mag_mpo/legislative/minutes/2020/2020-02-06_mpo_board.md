Election of Officers for the Regional Planning Committee – Shawn Seager, MAG Each year in February a Chair and Vice Chair are elected. Article II, Section 4 (1), in the MPO Bylaws states: The Mountainland Regional Planning Committee, in consultation with MPO staff, shall nominate from its membership for the positions of Chair and Vice Chair. Both Chair and Vice Chair shall be elected officials from jurisdictions within the MPO. Ratification of nomination shall require a simple majority vote. The position of Chair and Vice Chair shall be for one-year terms. Successive terms are not limited. Individuals may request to be replaced at any time. The position requires meeting time in Salt Lake City and Utah County. Both Mayor Leifson and Mayor Miller expressed interest in continuing to serve on the MPO Regional Planning Committee. No other nominations were given. Mayor Pray nominated Mayor Steve Leifson for Chair, and Mayor Jim Miller as Vice- Chair of the MPO Regional Planning Committee. Mayor Stout seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Springville Corridor Preservation Application – Jim Price, MAG Springville is attempting to acquire undeveloped ground from Utah Power and Light (UP&L) for sections of the 1200 W project. Because portions of the parcel contain ROW for both a funded and an unfunded project, the city has asked UP&L to divide the parcel so that Corridor Preservation Funds can be used to purchase the appropriate portion, and project funds the remaining when those are available. UP&L is refusing to divide the parcel and is willing to sell only as a whole. Project funds will be available in 2021, but the city feels it necessary to purchase the property now and is asking TAC to allow the Corridor Preservation Fund to pay for the portion of the parcel that lies outside the funded project. The portion inside the funded project will be paid for by the city, to be reimbursed later by the project. Jim stated that the numbers have changed since the staff report was sent out. The parcel is 5.86 acres, with the portion eligible for CP funds at approximately 3.5 acres (60% of the total area). The appraised value is $104,095 per acre (updated cost); therefore, the city is requesting $364,332 (updated cost) for the land and 60% of the estimated closing costs of $5000, or $3000, for a total of $367,332. Mayor Miller moved that the Regional Planning Committee approve the Springville City request for $367,332 in Corridor Preservation Funds, with approval to lapse one year from today if the property has not yet been acquired. Mayor Mann seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. UVU Pedestrian Bridge Project Update – Eric Rasband, UDOT Construction of the pedestrian bridge over I-15 between the FrontRunner Station and UVU has begun and is scheduled to be completed fall 2020. The project design and construction were made possible with a partnership between UDOT, UVU, MAG, Orem City, and UTA. The bridge cost approximately $30m and will be one-quarter mile long and sixteen feet wide. In addition, the bridge will have an elevator on each side, bike track, and will be heated and covered to keep snow and ice off the walkway. Utah Transit Authority: Kent Millington reported that in November 2019 a Micro Transit operation was initiated in the south western portion of Salt Lake County. December 2019 had an average of 224 rides a day, the highest being 285. The most frequent stops going to the FrontRunner and Trax stations. January 2020 had an average of 334 a day, the highest being 415, in addition to 87 wheelchair trips. The ride is free for those who have a FrontRunner or Trax pass and has proven to be successful in completing the first mile, last mile for many people.
