WORK SESSION

3. Work Session Items

3.1) Bike/Ped Plan Update – How to Make a Successful Active Transportation Network
Mike West gave a presentation on the Bike and Pedestrian Plan Update and “How to Make a
Successful Active Transportation Network”. Mike would like to follow-up with a mapping session
later. The general principles are safety and convenience, cohesion, directness, comfort, and
attractiveness. The Commissioners discussed goals and standards they would like to see in the
future. Mike stated they would like to create a prioritization plan.

3.2) Discussion of Mixed-Use Code Amendments
Kim Struthers deferred the last item and asked the Commissioners to review.

4. City Business
No City business was discussed.

5. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Eyre moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Jackson seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:45 p.m.
