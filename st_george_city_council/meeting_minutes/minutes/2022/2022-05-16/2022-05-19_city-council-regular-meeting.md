CONSENT CALENDAR:
  a. Consider approval of the execution of a Quit Claim Deed to release old
     reservations of rights from a 1954 Deed.

       BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Black Ridge KD-DAI LLC is the owner of real
       property that was deeded to a prior owner from the City in 1954. Certain
       reservations set forth in the 1954 deed are no longer applicable and the new owner
       wishes to clear title to the property. The Legal Department made corresponding
       changes to the proposed Quit Claim Deed and now presents it to the Council for
       approval. Staff recommends approval.

   b. Consider award of bid to Offen Petroleum for Bulk Fuel Supply Agreement.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Offen Petroleum was the only respondent to
      the RFP for bulk fuel purchases. Offen has been providing the City with bulk fuel (red
      diesel and 87 octane unleaded) for the past 15 years and continues to deliver fuel at
      the lowest bulk rate possible. Fuel pricing is based on the weekly OPIS rate. This
      agreement will begin immediately and terminate in 2027. Bulk fuel deliveries are
      received at the City fuel island and Energy department. The City typically uses
      240,000 gallons of unleaded and 240,000 gallons of red diesel annually. Energy
      Services is also included in this agreement for an additional 300,000 gallons of diesel
      annually for power generation. The fuel island on Red Hills Parkway is utilized by all
      City departments to fuel diesel and unleaded products. Per this Agreement, Offen
      Petroleum agrees to provide the City with priority fuel supply in emergency
      situations. Staff recommends approval to award the bid.

AMEND GENERAL PLAN/ORDINANCE:
    Consider approval of an ordinance changing the general plan future land-
    use map from Low Density Residential (LDR) and Medium Density
    Residential (MDR) to Commercial (COM), Professional Office (PO), and Open
    Space (OS) on 7.36 acres located at the southeast quadrant of River Road
    and 1450 South for a project to be known as River Crossing. Case No. 2022-
    GPA-006

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This project has gone through this process
      several times with the latest case on February 3, 2022. The proposal was to change
      the land-use map to commercial which was denied. On April 12, 2022, the Planning
      Commission held a public hearing. There were eight public comments. Seven of the
      comments were in opposition to this proposal. Based on the public comments and
      the comments from the Planning Commissioners, the applicant requested this item to
      be continued to the April 26, 2022 Planning Commission meeting. The applicant
      updated their plans by rearranging the location of the land use designations to better
      address the concerns. In response to the changes, a public letter was sent in favor of
      the proposed changes. At their meeting held on April 26, 2022, the Planning
      Commission recommended approval of the updated plans.

HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a Hillside Development Permit to determine the ridge
     line on lot 120 of Foremaster Ridge for the construction of a residential
     home on approximately .28 acres approximately located at 445 S. Five
     Sisters Drive to be known as SG-FORR-120. Case No. 2022-HS-011

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: On February 23, 2022, the applicants
      appeared before the Hillside Review Board (HRB) to have the location of the ridgeline
      determined on Lot 120. The HRB recommended approval of the location where the
      home would be placed taking into account the applicant moves the house forward 5'
      using the 5’ reduction in the front-yard setback (10-13A-6). On March 8, 2022, the
      Planning Commission recommended approval. The applicants felt the HRB was okay
      with the applicants moving their house forward only 3'; therefore, they decided to
      return to the HRB, which they did on April 27, 2022. The HRB recommended
      approval of moving the house forward 3'. On May 10, 2022, the Planning
      Commission had a public meeting and recommended approval with conditions.
