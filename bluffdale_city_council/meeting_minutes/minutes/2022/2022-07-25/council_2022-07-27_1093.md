the meeting. Emailed comments will be submitted to the City Council but will not be read at the meeting. Notice is further
given that access to this meeting by the City Council may be by electronic means.

the right to immediately remove the individual(s) from the meeting and, if needed, end virtual access to the meeting.

offensive pictures, remarks, or making offensive statements, disrespectful statements or actions, and any other action
deemed inappropriate.

         5.1    Up on Recreation Proeram (StaffPresenter,Shane Paddock),

Public Works Director, Shane Paddock provided a brief background about the position held by
Recreation Coordinator, Cami Wilson. He stated that it has been about one year since Ms. Wilson
was hired and a lot has happened during that time. A significant amount of work was done behind
the scenes to prepare the City, protect the City, and protect the players. Ms. Wilson presented an
update about the Recreation Program. When she was hired, there was a desire to start the program,
so she met with other cities and determined what needed to be accomplished. To start the Recreation
Program in Bluffdale, the following had been done:

     •   Create a Recreation Page on the City website;
     •   Find and purchase recreation software for registrations;
     •   Create liability waivers, code ofconduct policies, and a concussion policy;
     •   Research labor laws for minors and hired site supervisors and referees;
     •   Create training for coaches and referees;
     •   Bring background checks in-house for volunteers;
     •   Purchase equipment and jerseys; and
     •   Open registration, create teams and schedules and organize equipment bags.

During the spring season, there were 472 registrations. Ms. Wilson presented a breakdown of the
registrations by age group. 437 registrants were Bluffdale residents, which meant that only 35 were
non-residents. There were 57 volunteer coaches and seven Staff Members,including herself. A map
was displayed to show how the fields are organized. Images of the uniforms were shared. She
explained that the uniform is reversible and included as part ofregistration.

46 non-resident registrants. Ms. Wilson reported that the number of volunteer coaches decreased for
the fall season to 51. Based on feedback fi-om the spring season, the yoimger children want an
