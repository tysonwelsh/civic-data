2. CITIZEN PUBLIC INPUT
Mr. Rhett Wimmer , Board Member, Utah Chapter of the Foundation Against 
Intolerance and Racism (FAIR), stated he is concerned that the Sandy Library is 
aggressively promoting certain ideological views over others in the display space of its 
children’s section.   Of the eighty -five books in the display, 16 percent focus on race, 11 
percent focus on the climate crisis, 9 percent focus on critical social justice, and 5 percent 
are biographies of Democratic politicians.  There were no biographies of conservative or 
Libertarian politicians, white or Latin Americans, founding fathers, or mathematicians .   
FAIR opposes any banning or limiting of books from public libraries and promotes 
neutrality.

A. To fund the acquisition, construction, renovation, and equipping of 
parks and recreation facilities and related improvements, Salt Lake 
County (“County”) sought approval by voters in the November 8, 
2016 general election to issue general obligation bonds in an amount 
not to exceed Ninety Million Dollars ($90,000,000) (the “Recreation 
Bonds”).
B. Salt Lake City (“City”) submitted a Recreation Bonds Application 
proposing to replace an accessory building at its recreation facility 
known as Oak Hills Tennis Center with a new clubhouse (“Facility”) 
for which it will receive a contribution of County Recreation Bonds 
funds in the amount of $1,750,000.00
C. County and City entered into an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement, 
Salt Lake County Contract No. 0000001949 ( “the Agreement ”) 
whereby the County contributed the Recreation Bonds funds for City 
to complete the desired improvements to its Facility.
D. The adoption of the Agreement was made pursuant to the Utah 
Interlocal Cooperation Act, U.C.A. § 11-13-101 et seq. (the “Act”).
E. The Agreement requires the City to construct an improvement to real 
property thereby requiring approval of the County ’s legislative body 
pursuant to the Act.
F. The Agreement expired on December 31, 2002, but the work is not to 
be complete.
G. The City and the County wish to amend the Agreement to extend the 
termination date.
H. The amendment of the Agreement is in the best interests of the parties 
and in furtherance of the public interest.

WHEREAS, Salt Lake County (the “County”) and West Valley City (the 
“City”) are “public agencies” as defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation 
Act, Utah Code § 11-13-101 to -608, and are therefore authorized to enter 
into an Agreement to act jointly and cooperatively in a manner that will enable 
them to make the most efficient use of their resources and powers; and 
WHEREAS, during the 2018 General Session, the Utah State Legislature 
enacted Senate Bill 136, which allowed for a one percent local option sales 
and use tax, which is used by the County in its Regional Transportation 
Choice Fund (4th Quarter ), which provides for on -going transportation 
funding in Salt Lake County (hereinafter “Transportation Funds”); and 
WHEREAS, the County has appropriated Transportation Funds to further 
regional transportation by financing all or a portion of the costs of 
transportation projects throughout the County in accordance with Utah Code 
§ 59-12-2219 and all other applicable federal, state, and local laws, rules and 
regulations; and
WHEREAS, among these transportation projects is the Cross Towne Trail 
project; and

has sought the participation of the City to complete the Project.  The Parties 
now desire to enter into this Agreement providing for the transfer of up to 
One Million Three Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($1,350,000) of 
Transportation Funds to the City to reimburse the City for certain costs 
incurred by the City to complete the Project; and
WHEREAS, the County now desires to enter into an interlocal cooperation 
agreement with the City, which agreement is attached hereto as 
ATTACHMENT A  (the “Interlocal Agreement ”), to provide for the transfer 
of County Transportation Funds to the City on a reimbursement basis for 
certain transportation project, as more fully described in the Interlocal 
Agreement;
