#4 Holyoak Airport Conditional Use Permit Revocation Appeal Clarification 
Luthy reviewed the information for the Holyoak Airport Conditional Use Permit (CUP) Revocation 
Appeal clarification. 
Dan Dygert stated an objection to the report before the Board from the County Attorney’s office and for 
not being given the opportunity to speak. 
Commission and Staff discussed the findings of facts in the staff report and the reason for those findings 
of facts. Substantial work in the first 12 months needs to be based on what was submitted to the County 
for the revised runway plan. 
Mr. Dygert again stated an objection to the report from the Cache County Attorney’s office. 
Commission and Staff discussed if the findings of fact summarized the information from June 2019’s 
meeting to uphold the decision to not revoke the CUP.

#5 Public Hearing (5:45 p.m.): Amendment to Title 17.07 & 17.09 
Zetterquist reviewed the proposed amendments to Title 17.07 & 17.09 regarding Kennels. 
06:17:00 
Parker motioned to open the public hearing for Amendment to Title 17.07 & 17.09; Sands seconded; 
Passed 6, 0. 
Jed Packer asked if the rural kennel would allow for a kennel on rural agricultural land. 
Watterson responded yes. 
Joe Chambers thanked the commission for their time and commented on why a conditional use 
permit is required for dogs in an agricultural area where it is recognized by the Department of 
Agriculture as husbandry. 
Christensen asked Mr. Chambers if he would want a kennel to move in next to him. 
Mr. Chambers stated there has to be a balance for the interests involved. 
Christensen stated the ordinance changes are a step to help those who would like to run a kennel. 
Sands responded that the point Mr. Chambers is making is that he can do it anyway. 
Mr. Chambers responded cases are based on the facts and circumstances to each individual case. 
Judges apply life experience to their decisions. 
Commission and Staff discussed what makes a CUP a CUP. If an application has subjectivity to it, it 
makes it a CUP. 
Mr. Chambers stated in a revocation of a CUP it becomes a quasi judicial item. 
Matt Phillips introduced himself as the County engineer. 
06:30:00 
Watterson motioned to close the public hearing for Amendments to Title 17.07 & 17.09; Parker 
seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
Sands motioned to recommend approval to the County Council for the amendments to Title 17.07 and 
17.09; Watterson seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
06:31:00
