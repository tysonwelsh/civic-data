Affordable Housing and Home Ownership Discussion

The City Council is interested in ascertaining whether there are additional policies or programs that the City could
undertake to facilitate home ownership. The Council has invited developers, experts in commercial lending, non-profits, and
investment groups to share their expertise with the Council. The Council hopes that these discussions will lead to new
opportunities for home ownership in Ogden. Further discussion will focus on how non-profit and for-profit organizations can
work together to create housing for underserved populations.

Chris Parker, representative of the Giv Group, discussed their mission, which is to foster vibrant, sustainable, and
balanced communities throughout the Intermountain West. They discussed their belief that land use can define communities and
that healthy food, safe housing, clean air, and meaningful connections should be available to everyone regardless of background or
economic status. Giv pairs the resources of nonprofit and socially driven for-profit organizations together to meet their goals.
Through historic preservation, catalytic development, area planning, operational support, and community partner facilitation, Giv
strives to help neighborhoods turn their best ideas into places to connect with and call home.

Discussion among the Council and Mr. Parker centered on topics such as projects he has worked on in the Ogden
community; appropriate housing densities; the Giv Group’s profit-sharing/shared ownership model for rental properties; access to
Low Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC) for affordable housing rental projects; investors who participate in shared ownership
projects; the liability associated with a condominium projects; and the level of demand for Class A office space.

Proposed Amendments to the Airport Committee Ordinance

Community and Economic Development Deputy Director Johnson explained Administration is proposing a change to the
Airport Advisory Committee ordinance to remove the sunset provision. Under the current ordinance, last amended in 2018, the
sunset date was set for June 30, 2023. The Administration believes it is in the best interest of the City and Airport operations to
have the advisory committee operate perpetually. This will ensure that any vacancies can be filled in a timely manner.

Council Business
Proposed Amendments to Council Rules and Norms: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith explained the proposed
revisions include moving the election of Council leadership from the first week in January to the first week in February and
providing that intergovernmental appointments will occur in December instead of January. The proposed revisions also remove a
reference to a leadership interest form that should have been removed with the August 2023 amendments, and makes incidental ,
corrections to grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
Flash Vote Survey Results Snow Removal and Parking: Administration has proposed changes to the City’s snowplow
ordinance to limit on-street parking from November 1 to March 30. Some Council members wanted to understand how the
proposed restrictions might impact residents before considering the proposal. A FlashVote survey was conducted.
Chair Choberka facilitated the Council’s review of the interactive survey results; the survey was available to local
residents only and 326 people participated, which is a 55 percent response rate based on the fact that 457 people were invited to
participate. Fifty-one percent indicated that they never, or almost never park one or more cars on their street overnight; 67 percent
indicated they would like to have better snow removal on their street, and this was more important to them than being able to park ;
acar on their street during the winter months. She summarized the responses to the additional questions, after which she noted that
the answers can be filtered based upon age of the respondent, the area the respondents live in, gender of the respondent, and what
is most important to them. She concluded by inviting the Council to review and consider the results on their own in preparation for
continued discussion of Administration’s proposal.
