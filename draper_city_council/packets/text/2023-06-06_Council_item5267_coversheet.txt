From: Travis DeJong

Re: Providing Local Consent for an On-Premises Beer Retailer alcohol license for KGM Restaurants LLC Comments: KGM Restaurants LLC located at 1247 E Mike Weir Dr, is seeking an on-premises beer retailer license. They have provided the following:
- A floor plan showing where the alcohol is to be stored, served, and consumed.
- A bond payable to the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Services.
- The results of a background check performed on business owner.
- Copy of sales tax license.
- Liquor liability insurance.
KGM Restaurants LLC has applied for and is currently in the process of obtaining a Draper City business license for accommodation and food services. The findings are as follows: 6-4-040 of the Draper City Municipal Code states the following:
a.   An on-premises banquet license, reception center, on- premises beer retailer license, or bar establishment license shall not be granted to a business located within six hundred feet (600') of any community location as measured from the nearest patron entrance of the proposed outlet by following the shortest route of ordinary pedestrian travel to the property boundary of the community location or within two hundred feet (200') of the proposed outlet measured in a straight line from the nearest patron entrance of the proposed outlet to the nearest property boundary of the community location.
KGM Restaurants LLC has met both the 600ft and 200ft distance requirements.
Per Draper City Municipal Code 6-04-050:  The total number of on-premises beer retailer licenses in the City shall not exceed one per each four thousand (4,000) population of the City based on the latest population estimate provided by the State of Utah Department of Workforce Services.
As of 2020, the Department of Workforce Services estimates Draper City's population to be 51,017. This permits 12 on-premises beer retailer licenses to be issued. Draper City currently has one on-premises beer retailer license issued.
