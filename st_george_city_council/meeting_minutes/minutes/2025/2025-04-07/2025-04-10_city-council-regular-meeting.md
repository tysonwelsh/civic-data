CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval of a Reimbursement Agreement with Quality
        Excavation for upsizing Water Lines in Desert Talus Phase 1 and Flowers
        Roadway.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The master plan for the area includes
          transmission lines that are larger than the minimum sizes the developer would be
          required to install. To save the costs of constructing the lines later after the
          roadways have been improved the contractor is planning to upsize the line sized
          from 8" to 24" and 8" to 18". Contractor is constructing Desert Talus Phase 1, an
          approved subdivision in Desert Canyons. The contractor is also constructing a
          portion of Flowers Way. Staff recommends approval.

      b. Consider approval to purchase a replacement vacuum truck in the
         Streets Division.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This proposed purchase is for a new
          VACTOR 2100i combination single engine sewer cleaner with dual stage fan
          system to be mounted on a 2026 heavy-duty chassis. The purchase request is
          submitted for the Streets Division. This purchase will replace an existing Vaccon
          storm water truck. The asset will be used to clean storm water drains, curbs,
          gutters, and other city infrastructure associated with the City drainage system.
          This item would be purchased using a State Contract with a trade-in option
          through Owen Equipment for purchase amount of $501,386.94 (trade-in value of
          $70,000 will reduce the purchase amount). Staff recommends approval.

      c. Consider approval of a Reimbursement Agreement with Quality
         Development for 3000 East Improvements.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The developer is providing frontage
          improvements along 3000 East for a future church. City desires adjacent north
          parcel frontage improvements to be constructed which includes an additional 2.5-
          foot width of pavement, gutter, curb, and sidewalk on 3000 East to provide
          complete roadway sections to serve the public. Staff recommends approval
          contingent upon review by the City's Attorney's office.

      d. Consider approval of a Reimbursement Agreement with Lexington
         Properties LLC for 2800 South Improvements along Hacienda Farms
         Development.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The developer is planning to design and
          install improvements on 2800 South along Hacienda Farms Development. The
          developer agrees to donate a 50 foot wide drainage/canal along the north side of
          the Developer's property, from Coyote Springs west boundary to River Road, to
          own and maintain as part of the city's overall drainage facilities in exchange for
          reimbursement of the 2800 South roadway improvements. Staff recommends
          approval contingent upon review by the City's Attorney's office.
