Presentations, Proclamations, and Awards

1.          A ceremony for the pinning of badges for newly sworn police officers. (25-007) 00:02:41

Chief Beebe expressed appreciation to the Council for the opportunity to present the police
department’s newest peace officers. He explained that the badge pinning ceremony is a proud law
enforcement tradition symbolizing a major milestone for those who have chosen to serve and protect
the community. The badge, he said, represents dedication, integrity, and the public’s sacred trust. Chief
Beebe emphasized that this ceremony also honors the families of the officers, acknowledging their love,
patience, and unseen support that make this calling possible. He concluded by introducing the newest
members of Provo’s law enforcement family and sharing their stories and accomplishments as they
received their badges. The officers recognized were Hannah Teeples, Stephen Johnstun, Crystal Coates,
Eric Tryon, Jake Fisher, and David Hallam.

Deputy Chief Geyerman introduced and recognized the following: Joshua Webster, David Strebe,
Johnathan Blodgett, Eric Littlefield, Alvaro Nicolia, and Katie Tillman.

Heidi Allman, City Recorder, administered the oath of office to the group.

Public Comment

Chair Garrett read the public comment preamble and opened the public comment period.

Aaron Gallegos, representing the Kingdom Hall of Jehovah’s Witnesses at 491 North 1280 West, shared
a safety concern about street parking along 500 North between 1375 West and 1280 West. He explained
that parked cars in this area block visibility for drivers entering and exiting the church parking lot,
creating a hazard. Following recent on-site construction, several near misses occurred, prompting the
request for the city to review the area and consider designating it as a no-parking zone to improve
safety.

Chair Garrett closed the public comment period.

Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned by unanimous consent at approximately 5:51 PM.
