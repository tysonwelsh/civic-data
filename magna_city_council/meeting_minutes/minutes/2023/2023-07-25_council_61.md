Public Comments

                Becky Colonna stated she received a letter from the Greater Salt Lake Municipal
Services District (MSD) to remove Donnie Sweazey’s trailers by July 27th. Due to unforeseen
circumstances, she would like to request an extension until August 10th.

             David Brickey stated since the MSD holds the authority over this matter, he
suggested Ms. Colonna negotiate directly with them for the extension.

               Lawrence Wheeler stated he recently submitted a traffic calming request to the
Council, which was signed by other residents, requesting a light at an intersection. This
longstanding issue has been under discussion and proposals for years. The proposed
interchange light would cover the areas of 2700 South and 2820 South, addressing traffic flow,
and pedestrian crossing. This location has a history of accidents and safety hazards, making
resolution essential. He understands the need to address this matter with the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District (MSD), but he also wanted to present this matter to the Council for its
consideration.

              Will Griggs stated he supports the letter submitted by Mr. Wheeler and
neighboring residents. Since the new development, he has been unable to make improvements
to his house due to concerns about potential land acquisitions. He is concerned about road
conditions and deterioration due to construction. He encourages the Council to be vigilant in
ensuring construction companies deliver quality work when roads are repaired.

              Mick Sudbury stated concerns regarding the lack of response to resident input.
He suggested the Council consider providing written feedback to individuals who address them
during meetings to improve communication and address community issues effectively. He
proposed the use of an electronic sign or other forms of communication to inform the community
about Council meetings, crime prevention initiatives, and community events to enhance
communication in the town. He raised concerns about the delayed resolution of storm drain
problems on Magna Main Street.

that residents want a carnival. The 4th of July committee is attending community events to try and

Pleasant Green Cemetery

                Sharon Nichols stated three residential plots were sold, there were two adult
burials and three headstone placements. A family ordered headstones for two family members
and wanted to install them themselves. She could not locate the burial plots and needed the family
to identify the plots. Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) located the plots earlier, and they were
identified with survey markers. She requested approval of funding for new software to map the
data that was gathered with the GPR.

                Council Member Barney stated this matter would be formally discussed at the
next meeting.

               Ms. Nichols stated the cost for all the upgrades would be $6,290, the online
subscription fee would be $550 annually, and the web software subscription fee would be $800
annually.

Ordinance No. 2023-O-08

                 Justin Smith, Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD),
reviewed the following ordinance rezoning property located at 2198 South 7400 West from A-20
(Agricultural) to M-1 (Manufacturing):

ORDINANCE NO. 2023-O-08                                                        Date: July 25, 2023

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AMENDING
       THE ZONING MAP TO CHANGE THE ZONE OF THE PROPERTY LOCATED AT
       2198 S 7400 W FROM A-20 (AGRICULTURAL) TO M-2 (MANUFACTURING)

                                            RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District provides services to the five Metro
Townships in the Salt Lake Valley, unincorporated areas, and the Town of Brighton; and

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township is a municipality and has authority to regulate zoning in
general pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-3c-103 (2);

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township has authority to adopt zoning ordinances, including a
zoning map pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-501 in accordance with the Municipal Lane
Use, Development, and Management Act, (“MLUDMA”), Title 10, Section 9a, Utah Code, to
establish zones within the metro township; and

WHEREAS, the Magna Planning Commission held a public hearing on July 13, 2023 in regard
to the proposed rezone request and recommended approval; and

WHEREAS, the Council deems it appropriate to amend its zoning map in order to accommodate
the use of the land for industrial purposes, which is adjacent to another lot owned by the applicant
and currently zoned M-2; and for the protection and preservation of the public health, safety and
general welfare.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL as follows:

1.      Section 19.06.020, The Zoning Map of the Magna Metro Township hereby amended as
follows:

at 2198 S 7400 W within the MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP, is hereby reclassified from A-20
(Agricultural) to M-2 (Manufacturing), said property being described as follows:

             Paul Ashton reviewed the following resolution relating to the transfer and
conveyance of Lamplight Village Park.

                                  MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-07-01                                                    DATE: July 25, 2023

       A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AUTHORIZING
       THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT
       BETWEEN MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP AND SALT LAKE COUNTY RELATING
       TO THE TRANSFER AND CONVEYANCE OF THE LAMPLIGHT VILLAGE PARK
       LOCATED AT 3645 SOUTH ELK POINT DRIVE, MAGNA UTAH TO THE MANGA
       METRO TOWNSHIP

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a Municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq; and

WHEREAS, Magna is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code Ann § 10-3-
205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor, and

WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-202 provides that any two or more public agencies may
enter into an agreement with one another for joint or cooperative actions; and

WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-214 provides that any public agency may convey
property to or acquire property from any other public agencies for consideration as may be agreed
upon; and

WHEREAS, Magna and the County are public agencies as contemplated in the above referenced
sections of the Utah Code (more specifically referred to as UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-101, et
seq. Interlocal Cooperation Act); and

WHEREAS, the conveyance of property provided herein is an interest in real property as
contemplated in the Interlocal Cooperation Act; and

WHEREAS, the County owns a parcel of real property known as Lamplight Village Park (the
“Park”), located at approximately 3645 South Elk Point Dr., Magna, Salt Lake County, State of
Utah 84044 (Parcel No. 14-32-104-001-000, and as further described in the Quit Claim Deed
attached hereto as Exhibit A), which is real property in the public use as a public park; and

WHEREAS, the County desires to formally transfer and convey the Park to Magna, and Magna
desires to formally take and receive the Park from the County, for the purposes and on the terms
and conditions set forth in the Agreement,

NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, by the Magna Metro Township Council, Magna, Utah:

Section 1. The Magna Metro Township Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to enter into an
Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Magna Metro Township and Salt Lake County relating
to transfer and conveyance of the Lamplight Village Park located at 3645 South Elk Point Drive,
Magna, Utah as set forth in Attachment A.

Section 2. This resolution shall take effect immediately.

Unified Police Department (UPD)

                 Council Member Prokopis stated work continues on the reorganization of the
Sheriff’s Office and UPD 2.0. The Sheriff and Undersheriff are working on separating and
reestablishing the law enforcement arm within the Sheriff’s Office to fulfill its statutory
responsibilities. Sheriff Rivera is awaiting data and salary survey results. This process involves
determining the scope, personnel implications, and funding needs. He has informed the board
that Magna is committed to UPD 2.0 moving forward.
