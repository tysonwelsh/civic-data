BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 1.71
acres located on the west side of Jerema Court, at approximately 12194 S. Jerema Court,
and on the north side of Stephens View Way, at approximately 828 W. Stephens View Way
(Exhibit B). The properties are currently zoned R3 (Jerema Court) and RA2 (Stephens View
Way). The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to allow for the
subdivision of the larger lot in the River Park Hill subdivision and to adjust the lot line with



River Park Hill 2nd and River Park Estates Ph 2 Plat Amendment          App. # SUB-232-2018
Plat Amendment Request                                        1
Lot 223 in the River Park Estates Phase 2 subdivision. The lot proposed to be subdivided in
the River Park Hill subdivision is approximately 1.4 acres or 60,984 square feet.

The River Park Hill subdivision was initially approved as a four lot subdivision and the plat
was recorded on January 8, 2013 (Exhibit E). The plat consisted of one larger lot and three
smaller lots.

The first amendment to the River Park Hill subdivision (Exhibit F) was recorded later in 2013
and reconfigured the three smaller lots into two lots. Those two lots are on the west side
of the subdivision and have frontage on Joseph View Lane.

The River Park Estates Phase 2 subdivision was recorded in May, 1996 (Exhibit G). This is
the first amendment to that subdivision plat.


ANALYSIS
General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
Medium Density and Sensitive River Overlay land use designations for both properties
(Exhibit C). These categories are characterized as follows:

The properties has been assigned the R3 and RA2 zoning classifications. The R3
designation supports approximately three dwelling units per acre. The purpose of the R3
zone is to “permit medium density residential development without special mitigation
requirements. This zone is intended to provide incentives to foster residential
development with little impact on its surroundings and on municipal services, and to
generally preserve the semirural character called for in the density element of the general
plan.” The RA2 zoning classification supports approximately two dwelling units per acre.
The purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its
surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's
semirural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life,
including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in
these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected from
encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.” The existing subdivisions are
completely surrounded by the RA2 zoning designation.

Subdivision Layout. Although this is a single Plat Amendment application request, there
are two plats that will be amended as part of this request (Exhibit H). Therefore, each of

River Park Hill
The existing subdivision consists of three lots. This request will divide the largest lot and
result in a four lot subdivision. The exterior boundary will be slightly modified so the
adjustment can be made between the two plats. A total of 1,125 square feet will be
removed from River Park Hill and moved to River Park Estates Phase 2. Below is a
summary of the existing and proposed lots.

       Current Lot                   Current Acreage       Proposed Lot        Proposed Acreage
           101                            0.46                 101                   0.46
           102                            0.54                 102                   0.54
           103                            1.40                 103                   0.91
            -                               -                  104                   0.45

All of the current and proposed lots will meet the standards for the minimum lot size and
dimensions for the R3 zoning district.

The access for the proposed two lots on the west side of the subdivision will continue to be
from Joseph View Lane and the two proposed on the east side will be accessed from
Jerema Court. The overall boundaries of the subdivision will not be modified as a result of
this application.

River Park Estates Phase 2
The existing River Park Estates Phase 2 consists of 33 lots. The modification to this plat will
add approximately 1,125 square feet to Lot 233. This is being done as the owner of Lot 233
has improvements on the adjoining lot to the north that have been there for several years.
This will put those improvements on the lot of the owner that utilizes those improvements.

       Current Lot                   Current Acreage       Proposed Lot        Proposed Acreage
           233                            0.30                 233                   0.33

Although the lot will be made slightly larger, it will still fall short of the minimum 20,000 for
the RA2 zoning district. However, when this area was developed it was done as a planned
unit development that resulted from a settlement agreement. The developer was allowed
to develop lots with a minimum of 8,000 square feet since the developer donated land to
the city for various improvements and trails. Therefore, the lot will continue to meet the
minimum standards based on the approved planned unit development agreement.
