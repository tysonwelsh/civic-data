                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-76

      A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING ANNUAL MEETING SCHEDULES FOR
                 RIVERTON CITY FOR THE YEAR OF 2021

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 52-4-202(2) Utah Code Annotated, a public body which
holds regular meetings that are scheduled in advance over the course of a year shall give public
notice at least once each year of its annual meeting schedule, specifying the date, time, and place of
the scheduled meetings; and

       WHEREAS, all City Meetings are open to the public unless closed, pursuant to the
applicable provisions of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act; and

      WHEREAS, the City Office is an accessible facility; individuals needing special
accommodations or assistance during this meeting shall notify the City Recorder’s Office at 801-
208-3126, at least 24 hours in advance of the meeting.

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, State of
Utah, as follows:

       1. That Riverton City adopts its 2021 Annual Meeting Schedules, as attached hereto.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
