                                NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARINGS

The Ogden City Planning Commission will hold a public hearing on Wednesday, April 1, 2026,
at 5:00 p.m. in the City Council Chambers, 3rd Floor, Ogden Municipal Building, 2549
Washington Boulevard, Ogden, Utah, to consider the following items:

   •   Zone change from R-3EC (Multiple-family Residential East Central) to C-9 (Commercial

       at 3324 Parker Drive
