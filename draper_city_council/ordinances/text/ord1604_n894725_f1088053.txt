City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On February 20, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1604, amending
the Official Land Use Map of Draper City, otherwise known as the City-initiated
Draper-Lehi Zoning Map Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper
City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 21st day of
February 2024. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
