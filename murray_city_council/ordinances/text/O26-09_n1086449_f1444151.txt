ORDINANCE NO. 26-09

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE STANDARD LAND USE CODE
(ORIGINALLY ADOPTED BY MURRAY CITY ON APRIL 7, 1987) TO
CREATE AND ADD LAND USE CLASSIFICATION NUMBER 8230 FOR
“VETERINARY DENTISTRY’.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE MURRAY CITY MUNICIPAL COUNCIL:

Section 1. Purpose. The purpose of this ordinance is to amend the Standard
Land Use Code, originally adopted by Murray City on April 7, 1987 (the “Standard Land
Use Code”), to create and add Land Use Classification Number 8230 “Veterinary
Dentistry” under the 8220 Animal Husbandry Services Classification Category.

Section 2. Amend Classification Category 8220 Animal Husbandry Services of
the Standard Land Use Code. Classification Category 8220 Animal Husbandry
Services of the Standard Land Use Category shall be amended to read as follows:

8220 Animal Husbandry Services

8221 Veterinary Services.

8222 Animal Hospital Services.

8223 Poultry hatchery services on a contract or fee basis (Poultry raising is
assigned to 8151 and 8152).

8224 Animal kennels, animal boarding, breeding, training, and grooming
(excluding large animals).

8229 Other animal husbandry services, NEC. (Includes pedigree record
services, worm raising, etc.).

8230 Veterinary Dentistry (providing operation is completely enclosed within an
air-conditioned building, no outdoor kennels or pens, no overnight
boarding, no grooming).

Section 3. Effective date. This Ordinance shall take effect upon first publication.

PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Murray City Municipal Council on
this 19" day of May 2026.
