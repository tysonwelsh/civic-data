            2. APPROVE PROPERTY TAX ACTIONS CONTAINED IN RECOMMENDATION LETTER 2019-10
            - Paul Jones, Deputy Attorney -

            COMMISSIONER LEE – LETTER CONCERNING HARDMAN PROPERY

            PAUL JONES/DEPUTY ATTORNEY – HARDMANS WERE TAKEN OFF OF GREEN BELT FOR LACK
            OF PRODUCTION. LOOKED THROUGH THE RESOLUTION THAT WAS PASSED BY THE BOARD
            OF COMMISSIONERS CONCERNING PROPERTIES THAT ARE WITHDRAWN FROM GREENBELT
            AND THIS PROPERTY DOES NOT QUALIFY UNDER THE RESOLUTION. PARCEL WAS
            WITHDRAWN DUE TO LACK OF PRODUCTION. THE RESOLUTION THAT WAS PASSED LAST
            YEAR DEALS WITH PROPERTIES WITHDRAWN DUE TO TECHNICALITY (EX: NAME CHANGE,
            FENCE LINE, ETC.). THIS PARCEL DOES NOT FIT INTO THAT, BUT IT WAS WITHDRAWN DUE TO
            LACK OF PRODUCTION.

            MRS. HARDMAN - THE PROPERTY IS PASTURELAND SO THERE WILL BE NO PRODUCTION ON
            IT. DURING THE BOARD OF EQUALIZATION, A RECOMMENDATION WAS MADE TO REQUEST
            AN OPPORTUNITY FOR PROBATION. DURING THE LAST MEETING HARDMANS REQUESTED
            INFO ON THE GREENBELT TO KNOW THE PARAMETERS TO BE SURE TO DO WHAT WAS
            EXPECTED. STILL HAS NOT RECEIVED ANYTHING (INFO/GUIDELINES) TO PROCEED
            CORRECTLY. REQUESTING A PROBATION TO HAVE TIME TO GET THE INFORMATION.

            DIANE GARCIA/ASSESSOR’S OFFICE – THERE IS NO AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTION ON THE
            TWO PARCELS. AERIAL PHOTO AND ONSITE INSPECTIONS DETERMINED THIS. A NEW HOME
            HAS BEEN BUILT ON THE PROPERTY. THERE ARE TWO HOMES ON THE PROPERTY, BUT MS.
            HARDMAN IS SAYING THAT ONE IS NOT HERS. IT IS ON SOMEONE ELSE’S PROPERTY. PRIOR
            TO THE HOMES BUILT ON THE PROPERTY, IT WAS JUST BUSHES, NOT CULTIVATED AND S NO
            COMPLETE FENCING. CURRENTLY THERE ARE SMALL CORRALS AND HOUSING AREAS
            BUILT. THE PARCEL THAT WAS NEXT TO IT, HAS SOME ALFALFA PRODUCTION ON IT, BUT
            THE ARIAL PHOTOS SHOW THAT THE 2 PARCELS DO NOT HAVE ANY OF THE HAYFIELD
            PRODUCTION. THE REST OF THE FARM PRODUCTION HAS BEEN IN FOR PROCESS OF BEING
            CONVERTED TO DEVELOPMENT OVER THE PAST COUPLE OF YEARS.

            MRS. HARDMAN – STATED THAT SOME INFORMATION IS INCORRECT. THE PASTURE FIELD
            WHICH HAS BEEN REZONED IS ON THAT SOUTH SIDE, HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH THE
            HOUSES. IT IS STILL IN THE FAMILY TRUST, UNDER HER NAME. THE HOUSES THAT WERE
            BUILT THERE, WERE BUILT BECAUSE UDOT BROUGHT THE ROAD THROUGH REDWOOD
            THROUGH HER BROTHER’S HOUSE. HE BUILT THERE BECAUSE HE WAS FORCED TO MOVE
            OFF OF REDWOOD ROAD. HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH THE HAYFIELD OR THE PASTURES IN
            QUESTION. THE PROPERTY THAT WAS DEVELOPED IS SEPARATE FARM AREA ON 2100
            NORTH WHICH IS SOUTH OF HOLLBOOK FARMS.

            PAUL HARDMAN – FAMILY HAS OWNED THE PROPERTY SINCE THE 1890’S. THE AREA HAS
            BEEN IN A SEVERE DROUGHT FOR THE LAST 5 YEARS. THOSE PIECES OF GROUND ARE
            GRAZING GROUND. NO WAY TO GET WATER TO THEM PLUS THE GROUND IS SAND. THE
            ENTIRE FARM IS ONE OPERATION.

            COMMISSIONER LEE - PROPERTY HAS A LIEN ON IT DUE TO GREENBELT. IF THERE HASN’T
            BEEN ANY PRODUCTION IN THE PAST, THERE IS NO WAY TO PREDICT PRODUCTION IN THE
            FUTURE. ASKED IF THERE ARE ANY KEPT RECORDS.

            PAUL HARDMAN – NO RECORDS. NOTED THAT HE HAS BEEN HONEST IN WHAT HE HAS DONE
            ON THE FARM HIS WHOLE LIFE. NOW HIS INTEGRITY IS BEING QUESTIONED.
