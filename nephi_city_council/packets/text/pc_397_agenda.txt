    2.​ Public Hearing - accept public comment on changes to Title 10 & 11 to:
           a.​ Create a new Industrial Zone in Title 10 the: “Industrial 4 (ID4) Zone with uses primarily
                pertaining to large Data Centers, and their support structures.
           b.​ Modify Title 11 to only require prescribed landscaping buffering of an industrial use from
                other adjoining uses in certain circumstances.
           c.​ Modify Title 10 & 11 to allow for xeriscape landscaping, and a certain percentage of native
                landscaping if well maintained.
           d.​ Modify Title 11 to require certain “dark sky” lighting requirements in the ID4 zone.
           e.​ Modify other areas to Title 10 & 11 as they concern data center uses, including the definition
                section of Title 10.
