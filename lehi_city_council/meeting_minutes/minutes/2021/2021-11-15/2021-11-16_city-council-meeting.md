27   2. Presentations and Reports
28   2.1 Glass Recycling Discussion
29   Jason Utgaard, with Momentum Recycling explained the glass recycling program to the Council
30   and introduced different options. He said that they would like to paint a mural on the dumpster
31   size containers for beatification and to attract attention. He said that the city could also supply
32   smaller pods in key locations. This allows for a smaller footprint with more flexibility for service
33   hours and location. He stated that they also have a program where residents can pay to
34   participate with their own container.
35
36   2.2 Update on Recycling Technology Pilot Project.
37   This was postposed to the next Council meeting.
38
39   2.3 Fire Department ISO Rating
40   Chief Craft reported that the Fire Department improved their rating which may benefit residents
41   and businesses with a lower insurance rate.
42
43   2.4 PARC Tax Update
44   Dean Lundell, Finance Director, reported that the Council will need to adopt an Ordinance that
45   needs to be sent to the Utah Tax Commission. They will be able to start collecting the new tax in
46   April. He also stated that the city will maintain the same tax rate for residents on their utility bill,
47   so the PARC tax will not affect utilities. However, the city will need to make up that different
48   with the Tax Commission.
