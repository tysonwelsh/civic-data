                                                                MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                  Tuesday, October 18th, 2022 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Gage Froerer and James “Jim” H. Harvey. Scott Jenkins is excused.
STAFF PRESENT: Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Liam Keogh, of the County Attorney’s office; and Craig
D. Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME: Commissioner Froerer
B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Steve Burton
C. INVOCATION: Commissioner Froerer
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Jenkins: We are approaching election time. It’s time for us to stand up
and be counted. Go to the polls. This is how it happens in our country. We have a great democratic republic. It’s our
job to keep this. I am really proud of Ricky Hatch and our Elections team. I have total trust in what is happening in
Weber County. I want to leave the message and impression today to get out and vote. You can complain all you want
but if you don’t vote your complaint is hollow.

H. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Commissioner Harvey: I’ve grown up watching teams of horses pulling wagons. I’ve watched as many as ten
      horse hitches. It’s a thing of beauty to watch the wagon. I can’t imagine what would happen if a team of
      horses were hitched to each end of a wagon; they would likely pull the wagon apart. I have never seem a team
      of horses like the ones in this county. I am grateful for our teams here that are pulling together.
