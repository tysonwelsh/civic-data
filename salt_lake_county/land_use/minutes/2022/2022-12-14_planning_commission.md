ADMINISTRATIVE LAND USE APPLICATION(S) 
Meeting began at – 8:37 a.m. 
PER2022-000749 – McKay Hooker (Smartlink Group on behalf of AT&T Mobility) is requesting an 
exception to allow for an 80’ monopole wireless telecommunication facility. Acreage: 3.02 acres. 
Location: 7181 West 11800 South. Zone: M-2 (Manufacturing Zone). Planner: Jeff Miller 
(Motion/Voting) 
 
The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting 
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.  
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jeff Miller provided an analysis of the staff report. 
 
Commissioner Cohen said the monopole isn’t just a pole. Mr. Miller said antennas are at the top and 
structure is not a stealth monopole. It is the only monopole in the immediate vicinity. Commissioner Cohen 
confirmed with applicant they know the conditions and do not have a problem with them. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING PORTION OF MEETING 
Speaker # 1: Smartlink on behalf of AT&T 
Name: McKay Hooker 
Address: 64 East Ambernest Lane, 84005 
Comments: Mr. Hooker said they would paint the pole standard color, antennas would match the pole. 
 
Speaker # 2: Smartlink on behalf of AT&T 
Name: Jason Hadley 
Address: Not provided 
Comments: Mr. Hadley said AT&T wants additional height for better coverage . Nothing else to add and 
hopes this is an easy one out of the way and provides better coverage for additional homes going up. 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion. 
 
No one from the public present to speak. 
 
Commissioner Elieson motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Vance seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED 
Motion: To approve application #PER2022-000749 McKay Hooker (Smartlink Group on behalf of AT&T 
Mobility) is requesting an exception to allow for an 80’ monopole wireless telecommunication facility with 
staff recommendations. 
Motion by: Commissioner Watkins 
2nd by: Commissioner Vance 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present) 
MEETING ADJOURNED
