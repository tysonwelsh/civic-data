        AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAGNA CITY COUNCIL (*THE COUNCIL")
         REPEALING AND REPLACING MAGNA CITY CODB CHAPTER 2.39
               ADMINISTRATIVE ADVISORY COMMITTEES ANI)
                            ORGANIZATIONS

                                           RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Chapter 2.39 of the Magna City Code sets forth outdated provisions for
Voluntary Services Advisory Boards that are inapplicable to Magna City; and

      WHEREAS, the Council desires to repeal and replace Chapter 2.39 of the Magna City
Code with the enactment of updated and concise requirements for Administrative Advisory
Committees and Organizations; and

       WHEREAS, the Council has determined that the requirements for Administrative
Advisory Committees and Organizations set forth in Attachment A will serve the best interests of
Magna City and the general public.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MAGNA CITY COUNCIL AS
follows:

l.      Repeal/Enact. Chapter 2.39 of the Magna City Code is hereby repealed. Chapter 2.39 is
hereby enacted in substantially the form set forth in Attachment A of this Ordinance, subject
only to administrative, numbering, or grammatical corrections.

2.      Severability. If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any part of this
Ordinance is unconstitutional or invalid, then such portion of this Ordinance, or specific
application of this Ordinance, shall be severed from the remainder, which shall continue in full
force and effect.

3.      Direction to Staff. Staff is authorized and directed to: (i) Correct any administrative,
numbering, or grammatical errors within this Ordinance and Attachment A; and (ii) Codifu and
publish this Ordinance and Attachment A pursuant to all governing law, including, but not
limited to, Utah Code Annotated $ l0-3-71l.

4.    Effective Date. This Ordinance will take effect immediately upon posting pursuant to
Utah Code Annotated $ l0-3-712.


PASSED AND ADOPTED this 9th day of June,2026
