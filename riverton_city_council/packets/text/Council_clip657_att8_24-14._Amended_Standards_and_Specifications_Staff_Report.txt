                               MEMORANDUM
To:              Honorable Mayor and City Council
From:            Matthew Cassel, City Engineer
Date:            April 16, 2024
Subject:         24-14: Updates to the Riverton City Standard Specifications and Plans Manual


The Planning Commission held a public hearing on this item, and minutes from that meeting are
included below. The Planning Commission recommended approval, with the following
recommended motion:

I move the City Council adopt Ordinance 24-04, approval of the amendments to the Riverton City
Standard Specifications and Plans Manual as shown in Exhibit “A”.

Background

This is a public hearing for updates proposed by Riverton City to the Riverton City Standard Specifications
and Plans Manual. Proposed updates only pertain to the Standard Plans section of the manual.

The proposed updates have been reviewed by the Public Works Department, the Development Services
Department, and the City Attorney, and were previously presented to the City Council in a work session.
All comments received were addressed.

Staff is recommending approval of the updates, with any requested changes or additional items from the
Commission.

      2. Updated Riverton City Standard Plans.
