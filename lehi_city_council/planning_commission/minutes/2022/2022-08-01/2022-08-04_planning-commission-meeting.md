2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and recommendation of DR Horton’s request for review of a General
Plan Amendment for the Skye-Lehi Addition (boundary adjustment between Lehi City &
Draper), approximately 1,035.49-acres of property with the General Plan Designations of
Environmentally Sensitive Area (ESA), High Density Residential (HDR), Low Density
Residential (LDR), Medium Density Residential (MDR), Open Space (OS), and Very Low
Density Residential (VLDR).

Brittney presented information on the agenda item 2.1


General Plan Designation on the whole area. Proposed land use follows the topography of the
site. DRC had a total of 2 comments for review, including low density area and approval needs
to be subject to the approval of concept and boundary line adjustment.

    Motion: Commissioner Ellis motioned to hear 2.1 and 2.2 simultaneously. Commissioner
    Everett seconded. All Commissioners were in favor.

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of DR Horton’s request for review of the Skye
Area Plan Second Amendment Concept Plan including 985 additional residential units in
the Draper-Lehi Municipal Boundary Line Adjustment Area.

Brittney presented information on agenda item 2.2

DRC had a total of 15 comments for review. All would be tied together through a development
agreement. They are legislative items with a high level of discretion.

Kim Struthers, Planning Director, explained the trade with the open space and the units. Kim
clarified the footprint of the units and preserves open space. Ross Dinsdale, City Engineer,
clarified the 300 units can be accommodated with utilities within the existing system. The
Micron Area Plan analysis included the 300 units in Lehi. TSSD had also planned for units in
Draper and Highland.

The applicant, Jeff Stephenson with DR Horton, presented information on the items. Draper is
requesting the conservation easement. DR Horton has worked with Draper and Lehi for trail
connection and open space. The applicant stated he was not concerned with abiding with the
DRC comments. Ben Tuckett with LEI Engineer provided information on the grade. They have
looked at the existing topography and will be able to meet the requirements. The area to the East
has development opportunities if the 300 units are not approved, however, the preference would
be the 300 units.

Luke Seegmiller, City Traffic Engineer, is confident with the traffic layout if the 300 units are
granted.

Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing for item 2.1

2 comments online for 2.1; Commissioner Nielsen clarified it was concerning pressurized and
irrigation lines. Ross Dinsdale stated he was aware of the irrigation lines and there are not
concerned for those lines.

Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing for 2.1

Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing for 2.2

Brandon Ethington, Lehi resident, was opposed. The elementary schools are at capacity as it is,
and the development has already doubled the numbers. He would like to know if work is being
done to account for an elementary school to accommodate for more units.

Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing for 2.2
Kim Struthers spoke to Mr. Ethington's comment and stated the City coordinates with Alpine
School District and if there are additional sites needed, that information will be passed along.
Ross Dinsdale also stated Alpine school District has the General Plan map and may have an
estimate of the total number within their plans.

Mr. Stephenson stated they have worked with Alpine School District with the future site.
Currently, there isn't a current school site with this plan, but there is a school site in the overall
plan.

Commissioner Nielsen questioned whether or not it was a fair trade with the City since it is not
usable land but feels the City has done their research. Commissioner Eyre felt it was not a fair
trade but once it has been explained understands that it is useable. Commissioner Ellis stated
Eagle Mountain made a similar trade and likes the concept. Commissioner Newall would like to
see a plan for the density and would like to see the research and consistency and keep the density
down the mountainside as much as possible. Ross Dinsdale stated the Traverse Mountain Trails
Association has already partnered with DR Horton to work with their trail connectivity.
Commissioner Bunker expressed there is a lot of opportunity for grants for parks and trails.
Commissioner Carlson would like to see a master plan of trails with how the funding will be
provided, need to see effort with ASD and communication, community impact is being taken
care of by the developer.

        Motion 2.1: Commissioner Eyre moved to send a positive recommendation DR
        Horton’s request for review of a General Plan Amendment for the Skye-Lehi
        Addition (boundary adjustment between Lehi City & Draper), approximately
        1,035.49-acres of property with the General Plan Designations of Environmentally
        Sensitive Area (ESA), High Density Residential (HDR), Low Density Residential
        (LDR), Medium Density Residential (MDR), Open Space (OS), and Very Low
        Density Residential (VLDR); including all DRC comments. With the findings the
        proposed general plan land use designations fit with the surrounding areas, the land use
        designations benefit Lehi City, is not detrimental to the public health, safety, and welfare
        of Lehi City, is in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the General
        Plan and its various elements, is consistent with the land uses and zoning of nearby and
        adjoining properties, will not have a substantial adverse effect on surrounding property, is
        physically suitable in terms of design, location, shape, size, operating characteristics, and
        the provision of public services to accommodate any potential future uses, including all
        landscaping, loading areas, open spaces, parking areas, setbacks, buffering/screening,
        walls, fences, yards, and other required features. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

Commissioner Nielsen asked about the block length and how they plan on addressing it. Ross
Dinsdale stated the block length standard has to be met and in theory if the code can't be met,
they can't develop. Cost Benefit Analysis and if there was a dollar amount for gain vs loss. Kim
Struthers stated there has not been an analysis done.

        Motion 2.2: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation of DR
        Horton’s request for review of the Skye Area Plan Second Amendment Concept
        Plan including 985 additional residential units in the Draper-Lehi Municipal
        Boundary Line Adjustment Area; including all DRC comments. With the request that
        Staff and the developer research trail and trailheads for open space pending an equitable
        solution between Staff and the developer, also requesting modifications for block lengths.
        With the findings that the proposed concept is a good layout for the topography of the
        site, the concept plan provides the benefit of 500 acres of open space dedicated to Lehi
        City, is consistent with the Lehi City Development Code, conforms to the goals and
        policies of the General Plan. Commissioner Bunker seconded. Amendment: With a
        recommendation to the City Council that the land required for the City designation to this
        development will be extremely difficult to parcel or sell independently. Second still
        stands.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.3) Public hearing and recommendation of DR Horton’s request for review of the Skye Area
Plan, amending the Micron Area Plan and including 2,436 equivalent residential units
(ERUs), mixed-use space, a civic site, open space, trails and roadways, located at
approximately Timpanogos Highway and Center Street.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

Luke Seegmiller provided information on the traffic study. He stated he feels confident that the
study is sufficient and will work.

The applicant, Jeff Stephenson with DR Horton, was present. He clarified the parking issue and
the recommendation from the City to eliminate tandem parking to help alleviate parking
problems is something they will do, but certainly does not agree with.

Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing for 2.3

There were 3 online comments.

Brent Frampton, Lehi resident, would like to know what will be built in the medium density area.
He would also like to know what is being built near Center Street.

Brent Ethington, Lehi resident, appreciated that there was a traffic study, but noted that it was
outdated and that it needed to be redone. He would like to see it updated. Traverse Terrace is
already a nightmare.

Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing.

Luke Seegmiller stated the Commuter Lane off ramp onto Center Street won’t connect to Center

to head North. The road above Viewpoint won't be redone and there are some existing issues that
will be fixed once Center Street is a through street and connectivity is finished. The existing
situation in being managed and Luke does not see it being unmanageable in the foreseeable
future.

Jeff Stephenson clarified the questions and concerns from the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to recommend approval of DR Horton’s request
        for review of the Skye Area Plan, amending the Micron Area Plan and including
        2,436 equivalent residential units (ERUs), mixed-use space, a civic site, open space,
        trails and roadways, located at approximately Timpanogos Highway and Center
        Street; including all DRC comments. Noting the modifications that the current traffic
        study be updated to meet current patterns and conditions, the developer will amend the
        current area plan to meet city parking requirements, the architectural review committee
        will include at least one non-employee resident and City would review prior to granting a
        permit, the number of rear loaded townhomes are increased to at least 40%, the list of
        uses that are not compatible be discussed with Staff, the plan is reviewed and language
        modified so references “should” are changed to “shall”, and that the reviewing
        departments will approve all site plans that do not request an exception or conditional use
        and all DRC comments are incorporated in the area plan. With the findings that the
        proposed area plan will provide a quality community in Lehi City, contains regulations
        that will provide quality development, is consistent with the approved concept plan,
        meets the requirements of the Lehi City Development Code in regard to area plan
        approval. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes. The motion passed 6 - 1.

                Motion: Commissioner Eyre to take a five minute break. Commissioner Ellis
                seconded.

                Vote: Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner
                Bunker, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner
                Newall, yes; Commissioner Carlson, yes. The motion passed 6 - 1.

2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Lee and Mary Kelly’s request for review of the
Kelly zone change, from RA-1 (residential agriculture) to NC (neighborhood commercial)
on 1.08-acres located at 3055 North 1200 West.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicants, Lee and Mary Kelly, were present and had nothing to add.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Troy Scheffield, Lehi resident, was opposed to the item. He was concerned about the property

being changed to Neighborhood Commercial and a plan not being submitted.

Derek Owens, Lehi resident, was opposed to the item. He stated all the neighbors are on the
same page. He isn't against development and spoke of the medium density that is near them.
There concerns are minimal and would like to have a collaborative discussion.

Mr. Johnson, Lehi resident, was opposed to the development. He thinks they deserve to leverage
their property since they have a substantial investment but would like to see it looked at very
carefully.

Sharon Scheffield, Lehi resident, stated code was set for a reason and would like to follow it so it
doesn't have a negative effect on surrounding neighbors.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to recommend approval of Lee and Mary
        Kelly’s request for review of the Kelly zone change, from RA-1 (residential
        agriculture) to NC (neighborhood commercial) on 1.08-acres located at 3055 North
        1200 West; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner
        Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
