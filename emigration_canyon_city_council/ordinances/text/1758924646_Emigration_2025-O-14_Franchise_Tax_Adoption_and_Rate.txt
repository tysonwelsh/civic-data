ORDINANCE NO. 2025-O-14 DATE: September @, 2025
AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING TITLE 3 CHAPTER 14, MUNICIPAL
TELECOMMUNICATIONS LICENSE TAX AND A CORRESPONDING RATE

WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon has limited sources of revenue it is sound accounting to
have more diverse funding sources; and

WHEREAS, the Utah State Legislature authorized the Municipal Telecommunications
License tax pursuant to Utah Code 10-1-4; and

WHEREAS, on April 22, 2022, Emigration Canyon held a public hearing on a proposed
enactment of the Municipal Telecommunications License tax; and

WHEREAS, the Council finds at this time that it is necessary to implement a Municipal
Telecommunications License tax in Emigration Canyon to help with the burden and rising costs
that are now associated with operating a municipality; and

WHEREAS, it is necessary for the Council to determine the appropriate
Telecommunications License tax rate pursuant to Utah Code 10-1-404 in accordance with Utah
Code 10-1-407;
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:
Section 1: Municipality hereby enacts and adopts the following Title 3, Chapter 14, as outlined in
Exhibit A setting the telecommunications tax rate to 3.5%.
Section 2: This Ordinance’s Effective Date shall be its date of publication.
