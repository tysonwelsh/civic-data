RESOLUTION NO. 2021-02-02                                                 DATE: February 8, 2021


  A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING A
    PROCESS TO GOVERN KEARNS’ PARTICIPATION IN THE LEGISLATIVE
                PROCESS WITH THE UTAH LEGISLATURE

                                            RECITALS

       WHEREAS, the Utah Legislature considers numerous legislation each year that could

impact the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) and its residents, public safety, and Kearns’ ability

to regulate, govern, or oversee important issues within its jurisdiction, including but not limited to

Kearns’ land use authority, police powers, and other local government issues; and

       WHEREAS, due to the potential ramifications that such legislation may have on Kearns’

and its ability to govern its own affairs, Kearns’ needs to be prepared to take prompt action to

either support or oppose legislation; and

       WHEREAS, due to the speedy nature of the legislative process, the Mayor and Kearns’

legal counsel are often asked to support, oppose, or otherwise opine on legislation on behalf of

Kearns before the Kearns Metro Township Council (“Council”) can meet to take a position on

pending legislation; and

        WHEREAS, Section 2.04.160(B) of the Kearns Metro Township Code permits the Council

to adopt a resolution “for policy declarations and proposals” that “may be used to exercise Council

authority in matters of statements of policy and communication;” and

       WHEREAS, to protect Kearns’ interests, the Council desires to provide the Mayor and

Kearns’ legal counsel with principles and instruction to guide them in responding, reviewing, and

acting on legislation.

       NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Council that when the Utah Legislature is in

session, including but not limited to general, special, and interim sessions, the following provisions

will apply effective immediately:
1.   Position of Concern. The Council takes a general position of concern on any

     legislation that would: (A) adversely impact public safety within Kearns; or (B)

     adversely impact Kearns’ ability to regulate, govern, exercise its jurisdiction, or

     that would otherwise negatively impact local control, including but not limited to

     legislation affecting Kearns’ land use authority, police powers, and autonomy.

2.   Legislative Communications. Where there is insufficient time to coordinate with

     the Council, the Council authorizes the Mayor, and Kearns’ legal counsel acting

     under the direction of the Mayor, to act on behalf of the Council in discussing,

     responding to questions or comments about, and engaging with legislators,

     governmental entities, and others regarding legislation in accordance with

     principles set forth in Section 1 above.

3.   Official Legislative Positions. For legislation that is not subject to the principles

     set forth in Section 1 above, the Mayor will propose a position on the legislation

     and any related recommended actions to the Council. If possible, the Council will

     meet as soon as is reasonably practicable to consider the Mayor’s recommendations

     and take action as needed. Provided, however, that the Council recognizes that it

     may not be able to meet before a response from Kearns is required on certain

     legislation, in which case the Mayor will notify each Councilmember via email or,

     if possible, by telephone as soon as is reasonably practicable of: (A) the legislation

     at issue and how it affects Kearns; (B) the Mayor’s proposed course of action; (C)

     why a response from Kearns is needed before the Council is needed; and (D) a

     reasonable deadline given the circumstances for Councilmembers to express any

     objections to the Mayor’s proposed course of action. If no objections are received

     by the requested deadline, the Mayor and legal counsel, acting under the direction

     of the Mayor, may execute the Mayor’s proposed course of action. If a timely

             and Kearns will be deemed to be neutral on the legislation until such time as the

      4.     The Mayor or legal counsel will provide a report on any actions they may take

             regarding any legislation at the next possible Council meeting, and the Council will

             ratify any positions taken by the Mayor pursuant to Section 3 above or otherwise

             address objections raised pursuant to Section 3.

       APPROVED and ADOPTED this 8th day of February, 2021.
