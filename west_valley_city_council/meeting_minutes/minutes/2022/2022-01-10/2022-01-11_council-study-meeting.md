A.  PUBLIC INTERVIEW OF QUALIFIED CANDIDATES WHO HAVE
    APPLIED FOR THE OPEN COUNCILMEMBER DISTRICT 3 SEAT ON
    THE WEST VALLEY CITY COUNCIL
    Wayne Pyle informed a midterm vacancy has occurred in District 3 with the
    election of Karen Lang as Mayor. He indicated that public notice was given through
    the month of December 2021 and qualified candidates submitted their applications
    January 3, 2022- January 7, 2022.

       Mr. Pyle noted that the Mayor and Council have received a binder that includes a
       declaration of candidacy, a cover letter/letter of intent, a resume, and answers to the
       following questions:

              1. What do you think about taxes and special fees?
              2. Suppose the City received a $1 million grant. What would you do with
                 the money and why?
              3. Please tell us about any activities, specialized skills, knowledge, or
                 professional experience that would contribute to your effectiveness in
                 serving on the City Council.
              4. What goal would you like to accomplish in your 2-year term and why
                 do you think it’s important to accomplish this goal? Will you use your
                 own decision-making process or will you seek other opinions?
              5. What are the three most important issues facing the City and why are
                 they important? What are your recommended solutions?
              6. West Valley City has grown exponentially in terms of population and
                 diversity. What do you think City Government should be doing to
                 engage all of our City’s residents?

       Mr. Pyle stated that a single question was provided for this questionnaire by each
       member of the Council.

  Mayor Lang asked how long it would take crews to get through the entire City if
  there was one large snowstorm. Dan replied approximately 2 days. He noted that
  cul-de-sac’s are the most difficult. Mayor Lang stated that some of the newer signs
  have deteriorated from the sun and asked if this issue has been resolved. Dan replied
  yes. Councilmember Huynh stated that some of the older green signs need
  replacing. Dan agreed and noted that there is not enough funding to replace them
  all at once but some are being replaced each year. Councilmember Harmon asked
  how many street sweepers the City owns. Dan replied 4. Councilmember
  Christensen asked what operation positions are needed. Dan replied
  operators/laborers and noted that it is difficult to find employees for this type of
  work during these times. Councilmember Christensen asked if the 3 brine trucks
  are enough to pre-treat the roads. Dan replied yes and noted that these are used for
  the main roads. Councilmember Huynh asked if Crystal Avenue sidewalk repair
  will occur in the coming year. Dan replied the process will begin this year with
  construction occurring in 2023.

  Wayne asked Dan to discuss Bangerter. Dan replied the City would prefer the
  underpass option. He stated that it is aesthetically more pleasing but is much more
  expensive. Mayor Lang asked if it would make sense to require underpasses all
  along Bangerter in West Valley City. Dan replied this would probably be ideal but
  would be very expensive. He indicated that overpasses are the less expensive option
  but staff felt it was important to argue the point with UDOT and Taylorsville for
  the sake of the community. He indicated that there are pros and cons to both options.
  Councilmember Nordfelt ask if either option is easier regarding maintenance. Dan
  replied not for the City. Councilmember Christensen stated that he travels
  Bangerter frequently and he feels the overpasses are more dangerous during
  snowstorms. He indicated that he would like the City to push for the underpass
  option. Mayor Lang asked if the aqueduct would be more impacted by an underpass
  option. Dan replied yes most likely. Mayor Lang asked if the City has time to make
     MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – JANUARY 11, 2022

       the decision regarding funding for the other intersections on Bangerter. Wayne
       replied there would not be time because UDOT will need an answer on 4700 South

C.     COUNCIL CALENDAR
       Mayor Lang referenced a Memorandum previously received from the City Manager
       that outlined upcoming meetings and events.

       Wayne asked if the Council would like to hold the Strategic Plan meeting in person
       or if they’d look to move it to a virtual platform given the COVID-19 outbreak.
       Councilmember Christensen stated that he is fine to hold the meeting in person if
       there is enough social distancing and spacing available. The Mayor and Council
       agreed. Mayor Lang stated that she would like Executive Staff present at the
       meeting. Councilmember Christensen disagreed and stated that they are given
       assignments and are given details of the conversation. Councilmember Fitisemanu
       agreed and stated that there is a better use for their time. He noted that he would
       like them to be available if specific questions need to be asked but they don’t
       necessarily have to be present. He also stated that the Strategic Plan is a
       brainstorming session for the Council. Councilmember Huynh stated that he
       believes it’s important for them to be present. Councilmember Harmon stated that
       he is still learning names and faces so it may be helpful to him. Councilmember
       Nordfelt stated that he often speaks to Department Heads about issues discussed at
       the Strategic Plan and they have all read the minutes or listened to the recording
       and have a good understanding of what transpired. He noted that the Council’s only
       employee is Wayne. The Mayor and Council agreed to have a light breakfast at the
       meeting and have lunch brought in.

       Members of the City Council had no further questions regarding the Council
       Update.

A.   POTENTIAL FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS
     Councilmember Huynh expressed concern about noise from custom built cars that
     has been brought up by a resident in Council Meeting. Wayne indicated that the
     Police Department did an op over the weekend and cited multiple vehicles. He
     MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – JANUARY 11, 2022

       indicated that this issue needs to be addressed at the Legislature. Nicole Cottle,
       Assistant City Manager, explained what conversations are being held about the
       issue at this time. Councilmember Huynh asked if the vehicles belong and are being
       built in West Valley City. Wayne replied it’s about 60/40. Councilmember
       Christensen stated that he hears them at night as well and it is a widespread and
       common problem. He noted that the City could look into developing a raceway
       where drivers can do this activity safely and away from residential homes.

       Wayne replied that we are asking employees to be vaccinated but will not require
       that until everything plays out in the Courts. Councilmember Huynh asked if the
       City needs to require masks. Wayne replied that the County has issued a mask
       mandate and West Valley City falls under that jurisdiction.

       Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if Mayor Lang would still like to continue
       adding issues to the agenda that are brought up during Council meetings. Mayor
       Lang replied yes unless it’s something that has been brought up repeatedly.
