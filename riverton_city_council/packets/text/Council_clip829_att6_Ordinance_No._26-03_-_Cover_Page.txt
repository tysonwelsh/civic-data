"I move to adopt/deny Ordinance No. 26-03 - “Quinn Rezone,” General Plan Amendment and Rezone of
approximately 0.97-acre located at 1980 West 13400 South. Changing the General Plan Designation to
Medium Density Residential and the zoning to R-4, a single-family residential zone with up to 4 dwelling
units per acre.”


SUMMARY:
This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Dallis Quinn for a single parcel at
1980 West 13400 South. The subject parcel is 0.97-acres with an existing home.
The current zoning for the property is both R-3 (3 du/acre, 14,000 sf-lots), and R-4 (4 du/acre, 10,000 sf
lots). This request would zone the entire parcel R-4.
The current General Plan for the property is Estate Density Residential which corresponds with lot sizes
greater than 21,780-sf (half-acre). The applicant is requesting the General Plan Designation be changed to
Medium Density Residential (min 10,000-sf lots) which is consistent with R-4 zoning.
The General Plan designation and zoning in this area are sometimes at odds. For example, to the west and
north are newer homes on lots Zoned R-3 but the General Plan designation for these lots is Estate Density
Residential. On the other hand, a few parcels to the east are a few parcels zoned RR-22 but they have the
General Plan designation of Medium Density Residential. Overall, the area has a mix of zoning with R-4, R-
3 and RR-22. All of the properties touching on the subject property are either R-3 or R-4 so the requested
rezone to R-4 does not create an incompatible uses.
