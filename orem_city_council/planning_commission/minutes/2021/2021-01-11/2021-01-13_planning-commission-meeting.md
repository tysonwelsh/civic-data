Chair Cochran asked Mr. Harding to introduce Agenda Item 3.2:

Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Scott Johnson for the Planning Commission to vacate Lot 1 Smith’s Estates, Plat C
and approve the preliminary plat of Johnson’s Deer Haven with eight lots located generally at 1852 North 400 West
in the R8 zone.

                                 Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing an eight lot subdivision.

                                 He originally proposed a four lot subdivision. However, because of some property
                                 line issues, the City required him to include several surrounding lots to correct them.

                               Transportation/Engineering:
                               Normally, the minimum width of a local road would be 28 feet. However, the City
                               asked the applicant to match the width of the existing segments of 350 West that they
would be connecting to. That width of 32 feet is being shown on the plat.

Non-Buffered Sidewalks:
Typically, buffered sidewalks are required on new lots in the R8 zone. However, the applicant is requesting that the
Planning Commission waive the buffered sidewalk requirement. As stated in Orem City Code 17-7-1(B), the
Commission has the authority to waive the buffered sidewalk requirement in the event that they “find that compliance
with the requirement would be functionally or aesthetically inconsistent with existing development located near or
adjacent to the property.” As the existing portions of 350 West do not have buffered sidewalks, the applicant and staff
believe it would make more sense to match the sidewalk alignment that is already existing on the completed segments
of the street.

Zoning:
This subdivision is in the R8 zone. Each proposed lot complies with the zone’s minimum frontage requirement of 80
feet and its minimum size requirement of 8,000 square feet.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Lot 1 Smith’s Estates, Plat C and approve the preliminary
plat of Johnson’s Deer Haven with eight lots located generally at 1852 North 400 West in the R8 zone.

Chair Cochran asked Mr. Harding to introduce Agenda Item 3.3:

Agenda Item 3.3 is a request by TriCo Construction for the Planning Commission to vacate Golden Pond at Sleepy
Ridge Phase 7 and recommend the City Council approve the site plan which includes the final plat of Springwater
Pointe at Sleepy Ridge located generally at 1900 West Golden Pond Way in the PD15 zone.

                                 Staff Presentation: On May 22, 2019, the site plan for Golden Pond at Sleepy Ridge
                                 Phase 7 was approved with eight twin home units. Units 1-6 were approved as two-
                                 story twin homes (33’3” high) and Units 7-8 were approved at one story units (24’8”
                                 high)

                                The applicant now desires to amend the existing plat in order to create three additional
                                units, Units 9-11, to the south of the site. Units 9-11 will be two-story units (33’3”
                                high). Access to the development will no longer be from Golden Pond Way which
                                required the approval of the Homeowners Association (HOA), but from a new access
at Sleepy Ridge Drive. They will not be a part of the existing HOA, but will create their own HOA, Springwater Pointe
at Sleepy Ridge.

Architecture:
The proposed buildings will be stucco and brick/rock front. The roof will be 30 year architectural shingles and standing
seam metal roof accent. At 33’ 3”, the height is under the maximum height of 35’ allowed in the PD15 zone.

Parking:
Each unit will have the required two spaces and there will be seven visitor parking spaces (one-half space is required
for each dwelling). No parking will be allowed in the emergency vehicle turn around area.

Landscaping:
The proposed landscaping and overall plan comply with the requirements of the PD15 zone for deciduous trees,
evergreen trees, shrubs and grasses.

Transportation/Engineering:
Access to the development has been changed from Golden Pond Way to an existing access to the south. The new street
will be private and will be called Springwater Lane. There will be no access from Golden Pond Way.

The City Council is the final approving authority for the site plan.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Golden Pond at Sleepy Ridge Phase 7 and make a
recommendation to the City Council to approve the site plan which includes the final plat of Springwater Pointe at
Sleepy Ridge located generally at 1900 West Golden Pond Way in the PD15 zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Harding. Mr. Spencer clarified that access
to all eleven units is from this new road. Mr. Harding said that is correct. Mr. Cook noted that this item has to go
through the City Council for approval and asked what the reason is, or if there is a problem or concern. Mr. Harding
said there is no problem or concern but that this zone, the PD15 zone, requires any site plan to go to City Council for
approval.

Mr. Clark commented on the new access to this project and said with the improvements there, it will work out well.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to introduce himself. Stephen Tripp introduced himself. Mr. Tripp said this has
been an interesting project. Originally, there were hard feelings with the neighborhood but now everyone is thrilled
with the new access and separate Homeowners Association.

Chair Cochran then opened the public hearing and invited those who wanted to make a comment on this item to send
in their comments via chat.

Fred Cook: see comments attached to these minutes. Mr. Cook commented on the storm drain issues in the area. Mr.
Bench said the City is going to redo some of the storm drains in this area so if there are any concerns they will be
taken care of at that time.

Chair Cochran closed the public hearing and asked if the Planning Commission had any more questions for the
applicant or staff. When none did, he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Ms. Bentley moved to vacate Golden Pond at Sleepy Ridge Phase 7 and make a
positive recommendation to the City Council to approve the site plan which includes the final plat of Springwater
Pointe at Sleepy Ridge located generally at 1900 West Golden Pond Way in the PD15 zone. Mr. Cochran seconded
the motion. Those voting aye: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam
Sakar and Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.

Minutes: The Planning Commission reviewed the minutes from the previous meeting. Chair Cochran then called for
a motion and moved to approve the meeting minutes for December 2, 2020. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion.
Those voting aye: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar and
Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.

Mr. Clark said he would like to welcome Ms. Bentley to the Planning Commission. The City recently purchased the
Hillcrest Elementary property and in an upcoming City Council work session there will be some discussion as to
how that property can be utilized. The library auditiorium will be completed soon. The Fitness Center is looking at
March 9th as the date the City will take the project over.

Adjourn: Chair Cochran called for a motion to adjourn. Mr. Spencer moved to adjourn. Mr. Roberts seconded the
motion. Those voting aye: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam
Sakar and Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.
