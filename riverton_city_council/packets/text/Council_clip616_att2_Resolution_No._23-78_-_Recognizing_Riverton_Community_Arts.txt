                                     RIVERTON CITY
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 23-78

    A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL RECOGNIZING THE
   RIVERTON COMMUNITY ARTS, A UTAH NONPROFIT CORPORATION, AS A
  PARTNER IN PROVIDING ARTS PROGRAMS TO RIVERTON CITY RESIDENTS
        WHEREAS, Riverton City has supported the arts by providing public venues and
funding to organizations such as the Riverton Arts Council to facilitate the development of
theater productions and other arts programs benefitting Riverton residents; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has also reinvested in its facilities such as the Sandra N.
Lloyd Community Center (“Center”) to provide venues for theater productions and other
programs related to the arts; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City plans to continue its support of the arts by completing its
investments in the Center and plaza area in the vicinity of the Center to increase the opportunities
for participation in arts programs; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the Riverton Arts
Council has shifted its focus away from supporting a variety of arts programs in favor of solely
providing theater productions; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council further finds and determines that the Riverton
Arts Council is no longer an entity with a registered business address within Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, the organization known as Riverton Community Arts is registered as a
nonprofit entity, established in Riverton City, with a corporate purpose to support a variety of
public arts programs in addition to theater productions; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City staff has interacted with the Riverton Community Arts and
recommends that it be allowed to be recognized as a strategic partner who may cooperate with
Riverton City in providing arts programs hosted on City Property, and with City taxpayer
support.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:


   1. The Riverton City Council hereby discontinues its recognition of the Riverton Arts
      Council as a partner in providing taxpayer funded arts programs in Riverton City.

   2. The Riverton City Council hereby recognizes Riverton Community Arts as a partner in
      providing taxpayer funded arts programs in Riverton City.

   3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
