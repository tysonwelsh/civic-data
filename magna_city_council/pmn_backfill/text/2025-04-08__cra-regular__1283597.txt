B. Salt Lake County Redevelopment Agency Transfer Update — Board Chair Eric
Barney

They are working on getting all the paperwork through the appropriate jurisdictions. They are

still aiming for a June transfer date in totality, moving over will be all of the assets to include

the properties currently held by the RDA, as well as the funds held by the RDA.

Council Member Prokopis asked if all properties were current with income taxes.

Chair Barney responded that they should be.

David Brickey additionally updated that Arbor Park and the issues revolving around the 8400

improvement under the sidewalk appear to have been completed and the water will turn on

April 15.

Council Member Hull added that UFSA approved transferring to Magna’s RDA in March, as did

SLVLESA per Council Member Prokopis.

Chair Barney noted everyone in Salt Lake County is on board for the transfer, the last update

was a short meeting confirming everything was on track.

Mr. Brickey noted that he and Dan Torres will work on begin attending the Salt Lake County

RDA meetings should there be any questions or need for clarification.
