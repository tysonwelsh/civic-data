D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: I need to tell how grateful I am to be amongst people who care
   about doing the right thing and doing good at every turn. Over the last couple of weeks, I have run into people
   that were somehow through tender mercy were placed there; they were kind and helpful. Not everything goes
   the way you hope; things happen, people die or get sick. But there are kind people along the way that help you
   through those circumstances and make things a little easier. We are surrounded by those kind of people here at
   Weber County. I felt privileged to marvel at the beauty of cemeteries yesterday and pay tribute to both those
   who have paid the ultimate price and their families left behind. My thought today is on gratitude and on those
   who chose the better way, the kinder way.
