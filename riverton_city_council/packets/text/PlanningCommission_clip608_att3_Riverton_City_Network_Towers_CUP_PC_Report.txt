Background

This item differs somewhat from what is typically seen by the Planning Commission. Riverton
City has contracted with a company, Tuvas, to update the City’s network connectivity between
municipal facilities. The primary locations are City Hall, the Public Works Building, and the Water
Shops. Underground fiber-optic connections have been installed to these facilities as part of this
project, providing both network connection between the facilities as well as connection to the
internet. Tuvas has proposed in their design that three eighty (80) foot towers be installed at the
primary locations with two purposes to the towers. The first is to provide a backup wireless
connection between facilities should the fiber-optic connection fail, and second to provide
wireless connection through receiver antennas to be installed at secondary locations including
the Main City Park and the C.R. Hamilton Sports Park. Final detail has not been provided on the
secondary location antenna size and placement. This hearing is to review the Conditional Use
Permit for the three proposed towers. Public notices were sent to the surrounding notification
area for each site individually, but for the purposes of this meeting the staff report covers all three
towers.
Towers similar to these in height have generally in Riverton City been associated with mobile

18.195.040 Determination.

         be substantially mitigated by the proposal or the imposition of reasonable conditions to
         achieve compliance with the standards contained in this chapter, the conditional use may
         be denied.
Consideration may be made by the Commission regarding anticipated effects/impacts of
proposed towers, and potential conditions that may mitigate such concerns. This item was
noticed for a public hearing and decision by the Planning Commission, and was previously
discussed with the Commission at the July 27 meeting. Additional information will be presented
at the hearing.


Technical Information
The towers are proposed to be located at three sites in the City, all of which are owned by
Riverton City. The sites are as follows, and as shown on the included maps:
         •       12830 South Redwood Road, Riverton City Hall Campus, directly west of the Sandra
                 Lloyd Community Center.
         •       12526 South 4150 West, Riverton City Public Works Building, adjacent to the north
                 side of the building.
         •       13680 South 3160 West, Riverton City Water Shops, adjacent to the east side of the
                 building.
The Public Works and Water Shop sites have secured fencing and gates
surrounding the property. The Riverton City Hall campus site does not. The
towers are each eighty (80) feet in height, and are proposed as lattice
structures, consisting of a braced metal framework, as shown in Figure 1.
The engineering provided with the towers show that given the height and
proposed load, “guy wires”, or cables extending at angles from the towers to
stabilize the tower, are not required. Panels would be placed on the bottom
sections of the towers to prevent climbing. The antenna equipment mounted
on the towers is shown in the included information and will consist of
relatively small dish-type antennas. The towers will be linked by line-of-sight
wireless connection.

Since the previous discussion with the Planning Commission, Tuvas has
provided the City with additional information about the proposed location of
the towers and the towers themselves. The height is specific at eighty (80)
feet. Staff has included in the material below the information provided by
Tuvas for each location. To help visually convey the height of the tower,
