       5.5.        Public Hearing: Holdaway Fields Development Agreement Amendment

           Motion: COMMISSIONER CHRISTENSEN MOTIONED TO OPEN INTO A
       PUBLIC HEARING AT 6:11PM. COMMISSIONER HUNTINGTON SECONDED.
       ALL IN FAVOR VOTED YES: HUNTINGTON, PEARCE, CHRISTENSEN, AND
       OSTLER. THE VOTE CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.

              Senior Planner Hancey discussed Daria’s questions on ADUs.

       City Engineer and Public Works Directo Naseem Ghandour noted he had answers to her
       other questions for the end of the meeting.

            Planner Anthony Fletcher presented a development summary. He provided
       locational context, site context, developer obligations, and the segments of developments.
       Planner Fletcher clarified the senior housing.

             Planner Fletcher discussed the requested amendment to revise the phasing plan and
       park completion timing. He noted staff discussions about impact fees. He noted the
       recommendation to allow for 1 year for the City Council to decide on structures for the
       East Park. Planner Fletcher presented the original phasing plan, the currently approved
       phasing plan, and the proposed phasing plan.

               Chair Tempore Pearce clarified that there were changes on each side.

            Planner Fletcher highlighted the next steps. He further discussed the impact fees
       and provided a breakdown.

              Chair Tempore Pearce clarified that the entire structure is being changed.

       Planner Fletcher continued to discuss the impact fee suggested changes.

              Commissioner Ostler asked about the progress on the church in this area.

       Senior Planner Hancey noted he will conversate off line.

            Ryan Bybee with Cadence Homes noted that the agreement came before many staff
       with the City. He provided history on the development and impact fees. He provided the
       progression of agreements within the development agreement from his experience. Mr.
       Bybee discussed the proposed amendments to phasing and amenities. He noted that the
       developer would not to go through with the amendments if the impact fees are required to

       change as an additional agreement. Mr. Bybee noted that there was zero obligation on the
       developer to cooperate with Heritage Park.

           Chair Tempore Pearce noted that he was impressed by his presentation. He noted that
       having impact fees locked in that early is fairly impressive. He asked if there has been
       any compromise on the amount staff has presented.

           Mr. Bybee noted that there has not been any open discuss on impact fees. It was
       brought up from staff.

           Director Ghandour noted he had information on water and history to share.

       Chair Tempore Pearce asked if the developer withdrew their requested amendments.

       Senior Planner Hancey noted that staff did not care about phasing.

          Director Ghandour noted his understanding and experience with the water rights and
       impact fees. He discussed the fee schedule, water types, and park phasing.

       Chair Tempore Pearce noted that changing in phasing has been seen many times. He
       noted that impact fees has not been brought to this body regarding this development.

           Resident Ms. Evans noted concerns for the TSSD fees and Orem water.

       Director Ghandour noted that we have disconnected from Orem water.

           Resident Ms. Evans noted concerns for a 300 W connection through this
       community. She voiced that the City shouldn’t bare this.

          Motion: COMMISSIONER CHRISTENSEN MOTIONED TO CLOSE THE
       PUBLIC HEARING AT 7:02PM. COMMISSIONER OSTLER SECONDED. ALL IN
       FAVOR VOTED YES: HUNTINGTON, PEARCE, CHRISTENSEN, AND OSTLER.
       THE VOTE CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.

             Planner Fletcher noted that the commissioner opportunity to recommend,
       recommend with conditions, further compromise, or recommended denial. He noted that
       the applicant may pull their application and the amendments would not be further
       considered.

            Commissioner Christensen noted comments on land use is not contributing the
       City’s financial well being.

            Mr. Bybee discussed the impact that the development brought.

       Commissioner Christensen discussed his point and discussed competing land uses.

            Mr. Bybee noted that if the impact fees are needed from the city, they would want
       to implement higher density through new zoning.

       Discussion ensued.

           Resident John Pettingill discussed his experience and his preference on density.

       Commissioner Christensen noted that it is in the City’s best interest to adjust the impact
       fees.

             Mr. Bybee noted that the agreement needs to be approved from both parties. He
       further commented on the disagreement with the perspectives of impact fees.

       Mr. Pettingill clarified who is responsible for what in the development.

       Discussion ensued.

            Chair Tempore Pearce noted that he supports the rephasing and feels that that
       should be separate from the impact fees.

           Commissioner Christensen noted he’d make a motion to recommend that impact fees
       should be handled separately.

            Planner Fletcher clarified the recommendation.

           Director Ghandour noted that Planning Commission’s recommendation is separate
       from staff’s recommendation.

       Discussion ensued.

          Motion: COMMISSIONER CHRISTENSEN MOTIONED TO RECOMMEND
       THE APPROVAL OF THE CHANGES FOR PHASING AND STRUCTURES WITHIN

       HUNTINGTON SECONDED. ROLL CALL WENT AS FOLLOWS: OSTLER,
