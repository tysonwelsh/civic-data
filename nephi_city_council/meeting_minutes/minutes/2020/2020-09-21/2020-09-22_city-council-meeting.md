                                         Meeting Minutes
                                       Work Session 9-22-20
                                             7:00 p.m.
                                    Nephi City Council Chambers


1.    Real estate developers Jeanne Lewis and Carol Holthaus met with the council to discuss the
     possibility of vacating a street easement between 1st and 2nd West and 800 North. They explained
     the advantages to their planned development if 800 North was never extended to 2nd west in the
     future. They offered ideas to the council on how the development could work and how traffic could
     flow. The council asked Ms. Lewis and Ms. Holthaus to bring in a detailed drawing of their
     development plans.

2.   The council discussed changing the Nephi City code that deals with beer licenses. City Attorney
     Kasey Wright suggested to draft two different ordinances, one that allows beer sales at special
     events and one that doesn’t allow beer sales at special events. The council and public could review
     and give input on each ordinance and then the council could vote on what ordinance to adopt.
     Councilor Memmott would like the definition of premises to be changed to the Google definition or
     one similar to American Fork City’s code definition.

3. The council discussed and reviewed the latest draft of the PUD ordinance that the planning
   commission and staff have been working on. Councilor Memmott offered several suggestions of
   revision to the draft. Mr. Atkinson said that the council may need to extend the temporary land use
   ordinance deadline, if the ordinance is not ready for adoption on October 20, 2020. Attorney Kasey
   Wright will check on the law to see if an extension is possible.

4. The council discussed the possibility of broadcasting/streaming city council meetings. The council
   agreed that they are not in favor of broadcasting the council meetings because of the expense of
   purchasing the proper equipment to make the broadcast effective.

5. Mr. Atkinson asked the council to review the newly updated financial reports. He indicated that the
   sales tax numbers are impressive.

6.    Mr. Atkinson presented a revised Biglow Annexation agreement draft. Power Superintendent Rust
     Finlinson expressed his opinion on how the Biglow Annexation and proposed development could be
     beneficial in help paying for the south substation. The council directed staff to make changes to the
     requirements of the property owners in the agreement.

7. The council agreed to meet on Tuesday, September 29, 2020 at 6:30 p.m. to put up Halloween
   decorations at city hall.

8. Adjournment 9:15 p.m.


Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Kent
Jones, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Donald Ball, Jeanne Lewis, Carol Holthous and Rust
Finlinson.
