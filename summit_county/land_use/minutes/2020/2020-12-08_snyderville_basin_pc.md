2. Public hearing and possible action regarding the Utah Olympic Park Ski Run and 
Lift Phase 2 Conditional Use Permit; located at 3419 Olympic Parkway, Parcel PP-
63-A; Jamie Kimball, Applicant- Amir Caus, AICP, County Planner 
 
Planner Caus said before the Commission is a Conditional Use Permit application for 
Phase 2 of the Utah Olympic Park Ski Run and Ski Lift.  The Phase 2 area is 
approximately 27 acres.  The applicants request to add two ski runs and a ski lift to 
portions of the western slope of the Utah Olympic Park hillside which is near the 
bobsled track.  The ski lift will be 3,416' long.  It will include 19 towers, including 
terminals.  The towers will average 33' in height.

Planner Caus reviewed the background of the Utah Olympic Park (UOP).  He said that

Snyderville Basin Planning Commission; however, Staff is seeking Commission 
discussion about the height of the light poles.  Assuming that the pole height is 
approved, Staff recommends approval as outlined in the Staff Report following a public 
hearing.  He noted 31 public comments were received.  All were in favor.

Mr. Kimball described the proposed ski runs.  There will be slalom and mogul runs.

Snow making

moved around to different locations.  The advantage of this system is that it will only 
make snow when it is cold enough to do so.  No water will be wasted.  They are working 
with the water companies.  The location of the detention pond was pointed out.

INDINGS OF FACT: 
1. Utah Athletic Foundation is the owner of record of Parcel PP-63-A.  
2. Parcel PP-63-A is located in the Resort Center (RC) Zone and is governed by the Utah 
Olympic Park Specially Planned Area and Development Agreement.  
3. The applicant is requesting to improve portions of the Utah Olympic Park hillside 
(Parcel PP-63-A), adjacent to the Olympic jumps into a ski run and ski lift to serve the 
run.

December 8, 2020

4. The proposed improvements are for Phase 2 only which include improvements of the 
two ski runs adjacent to the bobsled track and a 3,416-foot-long ski lift which includes 
19 towers (including terminals).  
5. Utah Olympic Park is one of the designated Snyderville Basin Neighborhood areas.  
According to the General Plan; The goal of this planning area is to ensure that 
training opportunities and support services are available and can be sustained for 
athletes, as well as ensuring the Park provides a unique opportunity and experience to 
the surrounding community, and the general public.  New development should be 
located in the most appropriate areas on the site taking into consideration topography 
and views from the Kimball Junction area. 
6. The proposed ski run and its location are part of the anticipated improvements to 
further the Utah Olympic Park training programs.  
7. According to the Utah Olympic Park Development Agreement Land Use & Zoning 
Chart, New Ski Runs and Ski Lifts are a Conditional Use.  
8. The Utah Olympic Park Ski Run Phase 2 area is ~27 acres.  
9. Most of the work performed for the Phase 2 ski run improvement will take place by 
grubbing the vegetation.  
10. Most of the grading earthwork for Phase 2 will take place for the construction of the 2- 
million-gallon water storage pond for snowmaking.  
11. The total cut/fill generated for Phase 2 is ~16,000 cubic yards. 
12. The Phase 2 ski run grading does not require fill material to be imported to the site.  
13. Any material generated from the Phase 2 ski run grading is anticipated to be placed as 
fill within the ski run and will not be stockpiled.  
14. Phase 2 of the proposed improvements will result in the removal of 22 mature 
evergreen trees; however, the construction plans specify the planting of new trees 
outside of the ski run boundary.  
15. The remainder of the Phase 2 vegetation is generally scrub oak and aspens above the 
pump house up to the top of the ski run.  
16. All areas of disturbance will be required to be revegetated.  
17. The top terminal of the proposed lift is located in the Ridgeline Overlay Zone.
