                                      Special Workshop

Magna Metro Township Title 18 & 19

            Brian Tucker, Planning Manager, MSD, with the Council reviewed Title 19 -
Zoning amendments in the following chapters:

   -   Chapter 19.29 Residential Mobile Home Park Zone
   -   Chapter 19.30 Medium and High Density Residential Zones
   -   Chapter 19.32 Commercial Zone
   -   Chapter 19.34 Manufacturing Zones
   -   Chapter 19.36 Mixed-Use zone

He continued with phase 2 of amendments, reviewing the following items:

   -   Planning Commission appointments and term limits
   -   Land use hearing officer
   -   Conditional uses
   -   Variances, exceptions and appeals
   -   Specific use
   -   Density bonus
   -   PUD
   -   Short term rentals
