                                             Elected Officer Annual Conflict of Interest Disclosure
                                                             (Effective May 1, 2024)

Park City Municipal Corporation elected officers must submit a written disclosure statement to the City
Recorder no sooner than January 1 and no later than January 31 of each year in office. This written disclosure,
required by Utah Code section 10-3-1313, must be posted on the city's website while the officer is in office. If
an elected officer fails to timely submit the written disclosure or to complete all the requirements below, the
City Recorder will notify the officer. Failure to provide the disclosure or amend the disclosure within 7 days of
receiving notice is a class B misdemeanor, and it must be reported to the attorney general and requires the
imposition of a civil fine of $100 against the officer.
