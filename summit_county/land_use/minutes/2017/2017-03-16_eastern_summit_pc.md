3. Public hearing and possible action for a proposed Plat Amendment and Conditional 
Use Permit to provide a culinary water tank to serve residential homes in the area; 130 
S. Snyders Lane, Coalville; HAMILTON-2-AM; Cluff Ward Water Company, applicant. –
 Sean Lewis, County Planner  
 
Planner Lewis shared the background and proposal. In January the applicant went 
before the County Council for a special exception that would allow them to develop in 
areas with a greater than thirty percent slope. A special exception was granted. The 
other two portions of the application are what the Planning Commission is here for 
today. There are two actions that have to happen for this application. The first is a plat 
amendment which creates a little more than one acre section that carves out of 
Hamilton Subdivision 2. This would allow the applicant to put the tank on this new 
section and own that land. This is not considered to be development as far as density is 
concerned.  It is not an increase in density as per the standards of the plat amendment 
because there is no structure that someone could live in. This is just a structure being 
buried under the ground for utility. It is not a density structure, which allows the 
applicant to carve that section out of the plat. The other process that is needed is a 
Conditional Use Permit for the use of the utility structure underground and the 
associated piping that moves the water to and from the water tank. This is a 100,000 
gallon water storage tank and associated distribution and supply lines for the culinary 
water company. This is located up Chalk Creek in the Hamilton Subdivision just at the 
edge of the Highway Corridor Zone District on the south side of the road. The intent is

future residences in the Cluff Ward area of Chalk Creek.

the findings and conclusions address both actions.

the plan, how they got to the structure of the plan and that type of thing. There was the 
House Bill 323 that was passed in 2015 requiring all the counties in Utah to draw up a

management of resources on federal lands. One of the important things when Logan-
Simpson was brought into the project was to make sure they were really writing a plan that

natural resources, land access and that type of thing. That is how they were addressed in 
the plan, kind of grouped into the larger headings because a lot of them interrelate with 
each other. Even outside of those headings, wildlife can interrelate with water quality and 
hydrology.
