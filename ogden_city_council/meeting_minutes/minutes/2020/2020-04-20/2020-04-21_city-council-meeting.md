    Fiscal Year 2021-2025 Consolidated Plan & Fiscal Year 2021 Annual Action
    Plan
             With the passage of the federal CARES Act, entitlement cities like Ogden are expected to receive additional CDBG
    funding for fmancial assistance resulting from the COVID-19 pandemic. This notice came after the City's Administration
    developed and forwarded the proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2021- 2025 Consolidated Plan and Fiscal Year 2021 Annual Action Plan
    to the Council. As a result, the City's Community and Economic Development Department is proposing to amend the initial
    Annual Action Plan budget for Fiscal Year 2021.
             Community Development Senior Project Coordinator Fuentes used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize
    Administration's proposed FY202 l-2025 Consolidated Plan (ConPlan) and FY21 Annual Action Plan (AAP). She indicated the
    ConPlan is the City's mechanism for receiving Housing and Urban Development (HUD) funding from the federal govermnent.
    The City receives annual Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) and HOME grant funding and if an activity is not
    included in the ConPlan, HUD funds cannot be used to fund it. Administration is recommending an amendment to the ConPlan
    and AAP to adopt the HUD urgent need certification; this will allow the City to relax documentation requirements for providing
    emergency loans to businesses impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic. Proposed AAP budget changes include the following:
                  •   Increase the budget $778,188 to recognize new program income and updated PI estimates.
                  • Re-allocate $250,000 CDBG from Target Area Public Improvements and $100,000 CDBG from Infill Housing
                      to Small Business Loan Program.
                  • $156,635 increase to Special Econ Dev Projects to budget PI from business loan payoffs.

Proposed Amendments to Own in Ogden Guidelines
         Community Development Senior Project Coordinator Fuentes used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize
the proposed amendments to the Own in Ogden down-payment assistance loan program as follows:
              •   Loans up to $20,000 for sworn Ogden City police officers and fire fighters;
              • Loans to sworn police officers and fire fighters forgivable after 10 years owner-occupancy and continued
                  employment with Ogden City;
              •   Subject to availability of non-federal funds; and
              •   Income restrictions waived for sworn Ogden City police officers and fire fighters.
         Discussion among the Council and Administration staff centered on the terms of individual Own in Ogden loan
agreements and, more specifically, how long a police officer or fire fighter must work for the City in order to be eligible for loan
forgiveness. The Council indicated they are comfortable with the proposed amendments and Council Deputy Director Symes
indicated an action item will be included on the May 12, 2020 meeting agenda to allow the Council to act on the proposal.

Fiscal Year 2020 Budget Amendment - Strategic Plan Directives
          During the FY2020 budget process, the City Council set aside project funding in anticipation of completing the City's
new Strategic Plan. Through a collaborative process with residents, the Strategic Plan has now been finalized and the Council
desires to fund recommended projects.
          Council Policy Analyst Mabey stated that the Council's last review of this matter took place on March 31, 2020; given
the anticipated budgetary challenges relative to the COVID-19, there was a request to review and determine which projects are not
yet underway and might be carried forward for later consideration. Council staff has reviewed the original funding plan for
Strategic Plan projects; $628,925 was set aside in the FY20 budget, but that amount could be reduced by $280,000 if projects not
currently underway are delayed until future budget years. This includes General Fund projects relating to translation services, the
annual citizen satisfaction survey, and the Ballet Folklorica events and arts grant and Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) Fund
projects relating to splashpad design and additional phases of the wayfinding signage project.
          The Council engaged in discussion regarding the potential unlmown economic impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic;
several Council members expressed concern about delaying funding of projects that they feel are vital. Chief Administrative
Officer Johnson indicated that the youth recreation pilot program with the Ogden School District could be put on hold at this time
as it may not be possible to pursue extracurricular activities in schools in the coming school year. The funding that was allocated
for that project ($25,000) could be shifted to the translation services project; however, Administration is concerned about
providing ongoing funding for the translation services project after the FY21 budget year. The Council discussed the idea
mentioned by Mr. Johnson and ultimately reached consensus to support the removal of funding for the pilot recreation program,
but further evaluate the scope of the translation services project and determine if the project should be funded in the FY21 budget.
Ms. Mabey noted that a public hearing regarding the budget amendments relating to Strategic Plan directives will be scheduled for
May 12.

Fiscal Years 2021-2025 Capital Improvement Plan - Marshall White Center
Design Analysis (RG003)
         The Council has been asked to consider a Capital Improvement Plan ( CIP) amendment to incorporate project RG003 for
Marshall White Center design and activity analysis.
         Council Policy Analyst Mabey explained this project anticipates the use of$300,000 of funding in FY 2021, and a 24-
month time line for project completion. The project scope includes involving stakeholders in reviewing and recommending current
and future uses of the Marshall White Center facility. This also includes hiring architectural and engineering professionals to
analyze facility conditions and provide more detailed cost information on design ideas and improvements. Ms. Mabey concluded a
public hearing has been scheduled for April 28 for the consideration of the total FY2021-2025 CIP; the Council can determine at
that time whether to include the proposed project in the Plan.
