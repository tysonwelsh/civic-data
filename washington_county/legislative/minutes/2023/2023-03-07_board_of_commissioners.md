CONSIDERATION OF APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION NO. __R-2023-3093, A
RESOLUTION APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH
THE SHIVWITS BAND OF PAIUTES FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICES

stated the Shivwits will be undergoing the process to find a prosecuting attorney and a courthouse,

CONSIDERATION OF APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION NO. __R-2023-3100, A
RESOLUTION APPROPRIATING FUNDS FOR THE DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION
OF THE “GREEN DRAGON TAVERN” IN LIBERTY VILLAGE

Commissioner Iverson related that the goal of United We Pledge is not only education, but also
replicas of important locations in American history. Dennis Leavitt further explained Liberty
Village will be a forty (40) acre campus duplicating replicas of twenty (20) or more historical
buildings from Americas founding. Liberty Village will be open for students to visit by September
17, 2024 to be educated on the three (3) charter documents of the United States. The Green Dragon
Tavern was crucial to the founding of the United States and the planning of the Revolutionary
War, and there is currently no Green Dragon Tavern replica anywhere in the world. This
Resolution would appropriate TRT funding to design and build the Green Dragon Tavern.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3100, A
Resolution Appropriating Funds for the Design and Construction of the
“Green Dragon Tavern” in Liberty Village. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

PUBLIC WORKS REPORT

Public Works Director Ron Whitehead reported that many rural roads that are not improved; and
with the recent weather maintenance of these roads has been difficult. Equipment can be damaged
if used on unimproved roads, and therefore these roads are not scheduled for maintenance at this
time. Mr. Whitehead reminded residents to use caution when driving under these conditions.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Accept the Public Works Report. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson, with Commissioner Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting
