NARRATIVE

  Due to budge constaints, the current project only completed the 3600 W overlay from 13400 S to 13050 S. The plan included
finishing the overlay in the 20/ 21 budget year. With the contractor already mobilized we approaced him and requested a qote to
    finish 3600 W from 13050 S to 12600 S. The contractor agreed to hold his prices and agreed to complete the project while
   mobilized. The funding will be added to the project by a budget amendment by transfere from the 20/ 21 budget from which it
was planned. Advantages of completing the project: School is out for the summer, UDOT will be using this section of road as a
                                        major detour during the shutdown of 12600 S for the Bangerter project. ( Jan 2021)
