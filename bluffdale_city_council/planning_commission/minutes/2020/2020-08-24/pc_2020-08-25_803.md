3.     PUBLIC HEARING, CONSIDERATION, AND RECOMMENDATION on Ordinance
       2020-12 Repealing Title 11, Chapter 27 (Residential Facilities for Elderly or Disabled
       Persons), and Enacting Title 14 (Residential Facilities for the Handicapped). Staff
       Presenter, Todd Sheeran.

Mr. Sheeran reported that the intent of the proposal under consideration was to remove the Residential
Facilities Regulations from Title 11 , which is the Land U se Section, make major changes, and give
the section its own Title. The proposed Title is necessary because it includes a variety of elements
beyond land use, such as business licensing and other regulations that would need to be administered .
Having a separate Title would preclude the land use entitlement arguments that would occur if it was
left in the Land Use Section. The goal was to eli minate barriers by providing housing flexibility and
equal opportunities for the handicapped. The proposed Title includes parameters and guidelines for
reasonable accommod ations .

Mr. Sheeran noted that with a treatment facility or any type of group living facility, there are urban
problems such as increased occupancy, noise, traffic, parking, etc. The goal is to protect the
residential neighborhoods. He began by looking at the owner occupancy housing rate in B luffdal e .
