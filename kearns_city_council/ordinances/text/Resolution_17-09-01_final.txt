RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE WAIVING OF BONDS REQUIRED FOR THE
INSTALLATION OF REQUIRED IMPROVEMENTS FOR CONSTRUCTION OF THE
KEARNS OQUIRRH PARK FITNESS CENTER AND OLYMPIC OVAL CONNECTOR

BUILDING (Project #30338 and Building Permit #170812)
WHEREAS, the Connector Building is a joint project of Salt Lake County, the Kearns Oquirrh
Park Fitness Center, and the Olympic Legacy Foundation and;
WHEREAS, the purpose of bonding is to guarantee that all required improvements of projects
will be installed, and;
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has the potentially conflicting responsibility to approve the
completion of the improvements it has required of itself and;
WHEREAS, the required improvements for this project shall be completed before any
certificate of occupancy shall be granted;
NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved that in the interest of the most efficient use of limited
funds, the Kearns Metro Township Council has determined that the bonds required for the
installation of required improvements for this project shall be waived.

RE: Waiving bond requirements for the Kearns Oquirrh Park Fitness Center and Olympic Oval Connector
building (Project #30338, and Building Permit #170812).
Dear Mr. Yoshinaga:
The Kearns Oquirrh Park Fitness Center and Olympic Oval Connector building is jointly funded and
managed by Salt Lake County, the Utah Olympic Legacy Foundation and the Kearns Oquirrh Park Fitness
Center.
Since this project is on behalf of the Kearns Metro Township, Salt Lake County has the responsibility to
both establish bonds for the completion of the public improvements as well as the responsibility to
approve such improvements.
Under the authority granted it by the Kearns Metro Township, ordinance §15.28.070, the Metro
Township Council may amend or waive the bonding requirements. Therefore, to preserve and protect
the efficient use of scarce public funds for this project, the Metro Township Council waives the
requirement to bond for the installation of improvements associated with the construction of the
Kearns Oquirrh Park Fitness Center and the Olympic Oval Connector building project.
