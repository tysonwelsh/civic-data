        4.         5Z20 – Recommendation to the City Council for a Zoning Text Amendment to the Taylorsville
                   Municipal Code, Chapter 13.19.010 D adding Part 7, SSD-IN (Institutional). Mark McGrath/
                   Community Development


     4.1              Mr. McGrath presented this item. This ordinance amendment is a City-sponsored modification
                      intended to facilitate the proposed Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints Temple to be located
                      at 2603 West 4700 South. Based on multiple factors, the City and applicant have decided to pursue a
                      Site-Specific Development (SSD) District zoning classification to entitle the proposed temple. Given
                      that the current Code doesn’t anticipate Institutional, Public, or Quasi-public uses as an SSD use, City
                      Staff is proposing to amend the Code to permit the creation of an SSD-Institutional classification that
                      will incorporate these uses. He advised the proposed amendment is a simple one-line addition to
                      Section 13.19.110 Part D. The new line will add the suffix “IN” for Institutional, public, and quasi-
                      public uses as Number Seven on the list of underlying uses. Although this amendment is intended to
                      help facilitate the Temple development, this adjustment is a City-wide amendment that could
                      potentially be utilized elsewhere in the community. Given that the City has wide discretion in how
                      properties are zoned, Staff sees no unintended negative consequences to the amendment.

     4.2              FINDINGS:
                      4.2.1  Current City Code does not identify institutional, public, or quasi-public uses as an
                             underlying land use in the Site-Specific Development (SSD) zoning classification (Chapter
                             13.19 of Taylorsville City Code).
                      4.2.2  It is in the best interest of the community to incorporate more flexibility into the SSD
                             classification by permitting institutional, public, or quasi-public uses.

     4.3              STAFF RECOMMENDATION: Staff recommends approval of the proposed ordinance amendment.
                      Commissioner Quigley added that this is very straight-forward and involves just creating a new line
                      item in the SSD Zoning, so he saw no problem with it.

     4.4              SPEAKING: Commissioner McElreath opened the public hearing and asked if anyone wished to
                      speak in favor or opposition. Since no one came forward he closed the public hearing and opened
                      the meeting for discussion by the Planning Commission or a Motion.
     4.5              MOTION: Commissioner Wendel –              I move that we send a positive recommendation to the
                      City Council for a zoning text amendment to the Taylorsville Municipal Code, Chapter 13.19.010 D,
                      adding Part 7, the SSD-IN (Institutional), File 5Z20.
                      SECOND: Commissioner Quigley

     7.1              Ms. Kerdolff presented this item. The City of Taylorsville is proposing a text amendment to the
                      Taylorsville Municipal Code Section 13.08.020(E) Permitted Land Use Matrix by the Commercial,
                      Office, Industrial, Mixed Use, Transit Corridor, and Research and Development Districts and Chapter
                      13.36 Definitions. The proposed text amendment was initiated in order to become compliant with
                      the Medical Cannabis Act (HB3001, 2019) within the Utah Health Code (Title 26-61a) and the
                      Cannabis Production Establishments Code within the Utah Agricultural Code (Title 4-41a). The
                      proposed text amendment is City-wide. She gave a brief summary of the proposed content of this
                      change. She said the proposed text amendment was reviewed by the City Attorney’s Office and
                      found to be compliant with the Taylorsville Municipal Code and Utah State Code.

     7.2              FINDINGS:
                      7.2.1  This application was initiated by the City of Taylorsville.
                      7.2.2  The purpose of this application is to become compliant with the provisions in Title 26-61a
                             and Title 4-41a of Utah State Code.
                      7.2.3  The proposed text amendment to Section 13.08.020(E ) Permitted Land Use Matrix by the
                             Commercial, Office, Industrial, Mixed Use, Transit Corridor, and Research and Development
                             Districts, permits Cannabis Production Establishments within all Industrial Districts (ID) and
                             Medical Cannabis Pharmacies in any zone that is not defined as primarily residential.
                      7.2.4  The proposed text amendment to Chapter 13.36 Definitions, provides ten new definitions as
                             they relate to Cannabis Production Establishments and Medical Cannabis Pharmacies.
                      7.2.5  Cannabis Production Establishments and Medical Cannabis Pharmacies within Taylorsville
                             must adhere to all applicable regulations as outlined in Utah State Code.
                      7.2.6  Cannabis Production Establishments and Medical Cannabis Pharmacies will not be granted a
                             Conditional Use Permit to extend their hours of operation and Medical Cannabis Pharmacies
                             may only be open to the public from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
                      7.2.7  A text amendment must be approved or denied by the City Council.

     7.3              STAFF RECOMMENDATION: Staff recommends the Planning Commission sends a positive
