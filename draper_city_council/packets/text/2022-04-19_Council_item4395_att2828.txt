                     REVOCATION AND ABANDONMENT OF EASEMENT

          WHEREAS, Draper City, hereinafter referred to as the "City," has heretofore been granted and
presently holds an easement for the maintenance, operation and repair of a ditch and storm drainage
facilities pursuant to an easement recorded November 25, 1996 as Entry No. 6513716 in Book 7542 at
Page 1006 the official records of the Salt Lake County Recorder (the “Easement”); and

        WHEREAS, the City no longer needs the Easement; and

        WHEREAS, The City has been requested to vacate the Easement and is willing to do so;

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises set forth herein, and other good
and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged,

the City hereby vacates and abandons the Easement to the owner or owners of the dominant tenement, as
their interests may appear. A legal description of the real property that was subject to the Easement is
included attached as Exhibit A and the legal description of the Easement is attached as Exhibit B (which
are attached as Exhibits A and B hereto).

        IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the City has authorized this document to be executed by its duly
authorized representatives as of the _____ day of April, 2022.
