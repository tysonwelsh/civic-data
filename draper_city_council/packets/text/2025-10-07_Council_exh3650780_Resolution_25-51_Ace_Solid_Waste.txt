                               RESOLUTION NO. 25-51

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN AGREEMENT WITH ACE RECYCLING AND DISPOSAL
   INC. FOR SOLID WASTE AND RECYCLABLE MATERIAL COLLECTION SERVICES ON
                       PRIVATE STREETS IN DRAPER CITY

       WHEREAS, Draper City provides solid waste and recyclable material collection
services (“Collection Services”) to promote public health, safety, and welfare; and

      WHEREAS, providing such services on private streets increases liability and
maintenance costs due to the city’s lack of control over private road conditions; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City has determined that it’s in the City’s best interests to
contract for a third-party provider to offer Collection Services; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City issued a Request For Proposal (RFP 25-13) in
compliance with applicable procurement laws to secure a qualified contractor for the
curbside collection, transportation, and disposal of solid waste and the collection and
processing of recyclable materials for subdivisions on private streets; and

       WHEREAS, following the review of proposals and negotiations, Draper City
selected Ace Recycling and Disposal Inc. as a city-approved service provider for said
services; and

     WHEREAS, Draper City and Ace Recycling and Disposal Inc. have negotiated an
Agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit A, setting forth the terms, scope of work,
compensation, and liability protections for such services; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council entering into the Agreement with Ace Recycling and
Disposal Inc. are in the best interest of Draper City and its residents to ensure cost-
effective, safe, and reliable waste management services;

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER AS
FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Approval of Agreement. The Agreement between Draper City and
Ace Recycling and Disposal Inc., attached hereto as Exhibit A, is hereby approved,
and the Mayor is authorized to execute the same on behalf of Draper City.

       Section 2. De Minimus Changes. The City Manager is authorized to attach the
final exhibits to this Agreement and correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting,
clerical, or de minimus errors or ommisions, provided such changes do not alter the
substantive terms of the Agreement as approved by the City Council.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon
passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THE 7TH DAY OF OCTOBER, 2025.
