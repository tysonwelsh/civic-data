                                          WORKSHOP

               The Council reviewed the following agenda items:

    Google Fiber License agreement.
    White City Metro Township General Plan meeting schedule and public hearing.
    Engineering update.

               Erin O’Kelley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(MSD), stated the Walk White City plan does a full inventory of sidewalks and trails, reviews safe
routes to school, and proposed connectivity connections and improvements. There will be several
meetings between now and June and she would like 10-20 volunteers to join a committee to
review and discuss the plan. She is looking for at least one Council Member to participate,
residents, and individuals from the school.

             Mayor Flint asked the Council if they would be interested in volunteering and
suggested Council Member Cardenaz.

               Council Member Perry stated he would participate in the Walk White City plan
meetings.

             Paul Ashton stated only two members of the Council will be able to attend to avoid
a quorum. He suggested that the Council forward any recommendations to Ms. O’Kelley.

Resolution No. 21-11-01 – White City Metro Township Tentative 2022 Budget

             The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving the 2022 White City
Metro Township tentative budget:

RESOLUTION NO. 21-11-01                                               Date: November 18, 2021

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING THE 2022 WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP TENTATIAVE
DATE            THURSDAY                                NOVEMBER                           18, 2021

       BUDGET AND SETTING THE PUBLIC HEARING FOR PUBLIC COMMENT AND
       A POSSIBLE FINAL ADOPTION OF THE BUDGET

                                            RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Municipality pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.; and

         WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
legislative body for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501; and

      WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-6-111 et al, White City is required to prepare
an annual tentative budget for consideration, possible amendment, and tentative adoption; and

        WHEREAS, the budget and related supporting documents shall be made available for
public inspection no later than ten (10) days prior to the public hearing for the budget pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-6-112 et al; now therefore be it

                                          RESOLUTION

         RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the 2022 White City Tentative Budget, attached
to this resolution as listed as “Attachment-Budget” and sets the date, time, and place for the Public
Hearing to be as follows:

      PLACE:         White City Water Improvement District Offices – Board Room 999 East
Galena Drive, White City, UT 84094

       DATE:           December 2, 2021

       TIME:           7:30 PM; and be further

       RESOLVED, that a notice of the Public Hearing for the 2022 White City Tentative Budget
be printed a newspaper of general circulation and posted to the Utah Public Meeting Notice
Website http://www.utah.gov/pmn/ no later than November 22, 2021; and be it further

       RESOLVED, copies of the 2022 White City Tentative Budget be available for public
inspection no later than November 22, 2021 at the Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office, located at
2001 South State St Suite N2-700 Salt Lake City, Utah 84114, attached to the Notice of Public
Hearing on the Utah Public Meeting Notice Website https://www.utah.gov/pmn/ and posted to the
White City Metro Township Website www.whitecity-ut.org for public inspection.

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this 18th day of November 2021.

Resolution No. 21-11-02 – White City Metro Township Continuity of Operations Plan

              Jim Woodward, Unified Fire Authority, Emergency Management, reviewed the
following Resolution adopting a Continuity of Operations Plan (COOP):

RESOLUTION NO. 21-11-02                                             Date: November 18, 2021

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTING A CONTINUITY OF OPERATIONS PLAN (COOP)

       WHEREAS, it is the mission of the White City Metro Township, ensure, its operation are
performed efficiently with minimal disruption, especially during an emergency;
DATE           THURSDAY                               NOVEMBER                          18, 2021

       WHEREAS, this document provides planning and program guidance for implementing the
Continuity of Operations Plan (COOP) to ensure the White City Metro Township is capable of
conducting its daily operations under all threats and conditions;

       WHEREAS, this CONTINUITY PLAN (COOP) has been developed in accordance with
guidance in Executive Order 12656, Assignment of Emergency Preparedness Responsibilities;
National Security Presidential Directive 51/Homeland Security Presidential Directive 20, National
Continuity Policy; Homeland Security Council, National Continuity Policy Implementation Plan;
Federal Continuity Directive 1, Federal Executive Branch National Continuity Program and
Requirements, October 2012;

        WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township desires to adopt a COOP in the interest of
public health, safety and welfare.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Adoption of a Continuity of Operations Plan. The White City Metro Township
Council hereby adopts the Continuity of Operations Plan attached hereto as Exhibit A.

      Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
passage.

Resolution No. 21-11-04 – Interlocal Agreement for the Issuance of Bonds

                The Council reviewed the following Resolution considering the issuance of bonds
to pay for infrastructure within the metro township and unincorporated areas:

RESOLUTION NO. 21-11-04                                              Date: November 18, 2021
DATE            THURSDAY                                 NOVEMBER                            18, 2021

       A RSOLUTION OF WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AND AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT
       WITH THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT,
       COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP, EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
       TOWNSHIP, KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP, TOWN OF BRIGHTON, MAGNA
       METRO TOWNSHIP, AND SALT LAKE COUNTY REGARDING THE ISSUANCE
       OF BONDS TO PAY FOR INFRASTRUCTURE

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Municipality pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, White City is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code
Ann. § 10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor; and

       WHEREAS, White City is authorized under the Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Ann.
§ 11-13-101 et seq. (the “Interlocal Cooperation Act”), to enter into an interlocal agreement with
the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “District”), Copperton Metro Township,
Emigration Canyon Metro Township, Kearns Metro Township, Town of Brighton, Magna Metro
Township, and Salt Lake County Respecting the Issuance of Bonds to Pay for Infrastructure, and

         WHEREAS, White City, by and through its Council, has determined authorizing the District
to issue bonds for infrastructure, as set forth in the attached Interlocal Agreement, Attachment
“A”, will benefit the residents of White City and will contribute to the prosperity, well-being, peace,
and comfort of the community.

       NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council approves,
and authorizes the Mayor to sign, the attached Interlocal Agreement (“Agreement”) with the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District, Copperton Metro Township, Emigration Canyon
Metro Township, Kearns Metro Township, Town of Brighton, Magna Metro Township, and Salt
Lake County Respecting the Issuance of Bonds to Pay for Infrastructure (ATTACHEMENT A).

        IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED, the Mayor and City Attorney may make non-material
changes to the Agreement prior to signing if needed to respond to comments and suggestions of
other parties to the Agreement, it being understood and agreed that each party to the Agreement
is represented by legal counsel that may have input to the document.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this 18th
day of November 2021.

Council Reports

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                Council Member Perry stated Captain Bone passed away from COVID-19 and
his death is considered an in the line of duty death. Construction continues at the various fire
stations. A grant was issued to fund the retrofit of some of the fire stations. Chief Peterson will be
retiring in 2022 and a committee was created to begin hiring for his position. Consideration of a
five percent member fee increase for next year is being discussed.

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

               Mayor Flint stated she included documents on SLVLESA funding and reviewed

where the funding is going to come from.
