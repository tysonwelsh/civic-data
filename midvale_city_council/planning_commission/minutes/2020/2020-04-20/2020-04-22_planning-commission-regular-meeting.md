2. JORDAN BLUFFS POD “A”; 8172 S MAIN ST; CONCEPT PLAN FOR RSL
ACADEMY TRAINING FACILITY; JB & CI ZONES; ADAM
LANKFORD/WASATCH
Mr. Alex Murphy presented that the development review process under the Jordan Bluffs Subareas
1-3 Ordinance requires the Planning Commission have a work session with the developer for each
Pod before any formal development applications are submitted. This is intended to provide the
Commission an opportunity to discuss proposals and provide feedback to the developer on their
proposed concept plan before more detailed plans are prepared and submitted for formal review.
No action is required by the Planning Commission as part of this workshop review.
Adam Lankford, representing Wasatch, is proposing to construct a soccer training facility for the
Real Salt Lake Academy at 8172 South Main Street. Mr. Lankford has had initial meetings with
staff, including an Initial Contact Meeting and a Development Review Committee Meeting
regarding the development proposal, and now seeks the Planning Commission’s input.
The proposal would construct a soccer training facility overlapping Pod A of the Jordan Bluffs
Subareas 1-3 zone. The project is approximately 7 acres in size and is located on the southwest
corner of the Main Street and Ivy Drive intersection. Access would be provided from a new drive
approach on Ivy Drive at the intersection of Ivy Drive and Seghini Drive. The proposed
development includes a 48’ tall building with indoor soccer field and office/team space, an outdoor
field with lighting for nighttime use, on- and off-street parking, and landscaping with public art
installations and plaza space.
As part of their discussion, the Planning Commission may wish to consider/discuss the following:
e A rezone will be required to add the property at 8172 S. Main St. to the Jordan Bluffs
Subareas 1-3 zone. Rezoning will allow for larger buildings closer to Main Street than
currently allowed by the Clean Industrial zone.
e The zoning ordinance was not written with a project of this type in mind. As a result, the
proposal will require the following changes to the zoning ordinance:
o Update to the pod map to include additional property within the Jordan Bluffs
project area;
o Reduction of the landscape setback along Ivy Drive from 30’ to 25’;
o Increase of the allowable athletic fencing height from 14° to 20’; and
o Addition of athletic facility lighting standards for the outdoor field.
e The property includes a portion of the existing Midvale City Public Works facility. City
staff has been discussing requirements to transfer the property to RSL as part of this project.
The Planning Commission will need to make a recommendation to the City Council
regarding its disposal. Should the transfer proceed, a subdivision plat will be required to
redefine the property boundaries.
e The zoning ordinance allows Staff to grant a number of exceptions to its requirements. The
following exceptions are being considered:

o A modification of the parking calculation to use a parking study provided by the
applicant instead of the calculation for a Commercial Recreation Facility and
include on-street parking in the calculation. The change would reduce the required
number of parking stalls from 529 to 125.

o Allowing the outdoor field to count towards the 10% overall landscaping
requirement. Without the field included, landscape coverage is approximately 5%.

o A reduction in the opening requirement along Main Street from 25% to 17% and
along Ivy Drive from 25% to 23%. This end of the building is where the indoor
field is located, which makes it challenging to add any additional windows, and
improved landscaping has been proposed along the street frontages to mitigate the
impact of the large walls.

e Integration of the project into the existing neighborhood to the east.

As proposed, the application will require the following process after the workshop discussion:

1. Planning Commission: Public Hearing for Text Amendment, Recommendation for
Disposal of City-Owned Property, Rezone, Large Scale Master Plan, and Preliminary
Subdivision Plat.

2. City Council: Public Hearing for Text Amendment, Decision regarding Disposal of City-
Owned Property, Rezone, Development Agreement, and Final Subdivision Plat.

3. Staff: Preliminary and Final Site Plans.

Staff has been working with Mr. Lankford to establish an overall schedule for the project and
anticipates the Planning Commission will review the project in May and June and the City Council
will review the project in June and July.
