MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 20-05-01 DATE: May 12, 2020
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
REQUESTING THE OPENING OF A CEMETERY FUND ACCOUNT BY
THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT TO
MANAGE THE FINANCES OF THE PLEASANT GREEN CEMETERY
RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body
for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District
that provides certain municipal-type services to Magna which include accounting and
financial management of Magna’s accounts.

D. The Pleasant Green Cemetery Preservation and Development Association is a Utah
nonprofit organization (the “Nonprofit”)

E. In October 2019, the nonprofit reached out to Magna regarding the municipality’s
possible interest in taking over the ownership, operation, and management of the
Pleasant Green Cemetery (the “Cemetery”).

]

F. Pursuant to discussions between the nonprofit and Magna, it was decided the
Cemetery ownership, operation and management would be transitioned to the
municipality.

G. Transitioning the ownership, operation, and management of the cemetery to the
municipality insures the long term viability of the cemetery as a locally operated
entity.

H. Pursuant to commencing operation of the cemetery, Magna requires the MSD open a
fund account for the cemetery to manage the revenues and expenses of the cemetery.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township, the Mayor and

Council hereby requests the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District open an

account for the Pleasant Green Cemetery to accept revenues and to pay from those

revenues the expenses of the Cemetery necessary for its operation and management.
