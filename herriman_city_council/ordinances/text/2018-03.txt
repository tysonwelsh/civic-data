HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2018-03

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 1 CHAPTER 7 OF THE HERRIMAN CODE OF
ORDINANCES REGARDING APPOINTMENT OF THE CITY RECORDER AND CITY
TREASURER

WHEREAS, the City Council (the “Council”) met in regular meeting on January 24,
2018, to consider, among other things, an ordinance amending title 1 chapter 7 of the Herriman
Code of Ordinances regarding appointment of the city recorder and city treasurer; and

WHEREAS, prior to 2017 state statute required that on or before the first Monday in
February following a city election, the mayor, with the advice and consent of the city council,
shall appoint a recorder and city treasurer; and

WHEREAS, the Herriman Code of Ordinances contained the same requirements; and

WHEREAS, in 2017 the Utah legislature amended the state statue to eliminate the
requirement to appoint a city recorder and city treasurer following a city election; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council finds that it is in the best interest of
the health, safety, and welfare of the inhabitants of Herriman to amend title 1 chapter 7 of the
Herriman Code of Ordinances to make it consistent with state statute.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council that title 1 chapter 7 of the
Herriman Code of Ordinances be amended, designated by interlineating the words to be deleted
and underlining the words to be added, as follows:

1-7-3: CITY RECORDER AND CITY TREASURER:

A. Appointment: On-or-beforethe-first Monday-in February folowing a city election-The mayor,

with the advice and consent of the city council, shall appoint a qualified person to each of the
offices of city recorder and city treasurer.

B. Ex Officio Auditor: The city recorder is ex officio the city auditor and shall perform the duties
of that office.

This Ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 2018-03, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 24" day of January, 2018.

HERRIMAN

Mavid Watts, Mayor
