                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-27


        A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING RIVERTON CITY TO EXECUTE A
           RIGHT OF WAY PURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH JOSEPH E.
       KINGSTON TO ACQUIRE APPROXIMATELY 3,330 SQUARE FEET OF
              FRONTAGE PROPERTY AT 4311 WEST 12600 SOUTH,
                           RIVERTON, UTAH

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has a need to purchase real property which will be used to
provide sidewalk improvements located at 4311 West 12600 South, Riverton, Utah; and

        WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that an agent for Riverton City
has negotiated an agreement with the above described property owners to purchase approximately
3,330 square feet of property which fronts the above described location, and said purchase will
enable installation of sidewalk and related improvements; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the purchase price for the
Subject Property is reasonable and of fair market value, based upon comparable prices of similar
properties recently purchased and sold within the general vicinity of the Subject Property;


     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute a Right of Way Purchase Agreement with
          Joseph E. Kingston to acquire approximately 3,330 square feet of real property from
          Joseph E Kingston located by Parcel Number 27-31-201-017, at the address of 4311
          West 12600 South Riverton, Utah, once it is approved as to legal form by the City
          Attorney, at a purchase price of $36,630 at closing. In addition, the Mayor is authorized
          to execute any additional documents at closing if the same are necessary to facilitate
          closing, do not culminate in a substantial alteration to the material terms of the Right of
          Way Purchase Agreement, and are approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
