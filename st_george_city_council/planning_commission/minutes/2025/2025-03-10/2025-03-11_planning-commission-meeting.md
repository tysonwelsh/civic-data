MOTION:

Link to motion by Commission Member Rogers to recommend positive
recommendation for item number 1 for modifying the zone from R-10 to PD-R at
Chandler Heights with staff recommendations. On the North and East lots 11-21,
limiting those lots to single level as measured per City Zoning to 16 ft height per
code, single level with a walk out basement. Additionally, storage garages would not
be allowed in our approval. The submitted elevations for the townhomes are not
approved and do not meet City standards. They would need to re-submit to the
council modified elevations that meet the requirements. We would make the
findings that roofs are pitched on those same lots; no flat roofs with staff findings as
recommended. The reasonings for the single level is to not encroach on the
neighbor’s privacy, peace and serenity in their own backyard or home or private
residences.

SECOND:
     The motion was seconded by Planning Commission Member Casey.

      VOTE:
              Commission Chair Anderson called for a vote, as follows:

              Planning Commission Chair Anderson – aye
              Planning Commission Member Anderson –aye
              Planning Commission Member Fisher – nay
              Planning Commission Member Casey – aye
              Planning Commission Member Chapman – nay
              Planning Commission Member Rogers –aye
              Planning Commission Member Draper – absent

              The motion carries 4-2
