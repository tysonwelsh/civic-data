                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    ORDINANCE NO. 24-03

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE TITLE 13, CHAPTERS 5 AND
               10 [CULINARY AND SECONDARY WATER]

         WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-7-14(2) authorizes Riverton City to “enact ordinances, rules
and regulations for the management and conduct of the waterworks system owned or controlled
by it;” and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-7-14(4), Riverton City shall “provide
service to all retail customers in a manner consistent with principles of equal protection;” and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds that updates to the current Riverton City
Code are needed to (1) ensure efficient, safe, and secure culinary water and secondary water
service; (2) further protect the equal rights of all water customers; and (3) align the code with the
needs of the city, and;

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council further finds and determines that these actions are
in the best interest of the City’s health, safety and general welfare.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

Section 1.     Repealer. The following Code Sections are hereby repealed:

               Title 13, Chapter 5, Section 40; and Title 13, Chapter 10, Section 207.

Section 2.     Adoption. Title 13. Chapters 5 and 10, are hereby reenacted and readopted
               as provided in the Attached Exhibit A.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 16th day of April,
