THE BREAK BAR & GRILL – 4760 South 900 East – Project #22-111
Mr. Hall presented this item, a request to amend an existing permit that was granted in 2012 for
this same property. Under city code, conditional use permits run with the property, so it is still in
force, but the conditions are specific and relate to offsite parking on a specific property. The
request is to provide offsite parking on a different property under different conditions. The
property in question has been many different restaurants in the past, and the 2012 conditional
use permit required they maintain a lease for parking offsite on property owned by Salt Lake
City. The property no longer has that lease and The Break Sports Bar and Grill wants to move in
and needs to replace those parking spots offsite in another location. This is in the C-D zone,
current uses are allowed and the zoning hasn’t changed. Based on the aerial map from the
meeting packet, Mr. Hall showed that offsite parking can be provided within 200 feet of the
property on adjacent parcels; the bubbles on the map show what is considered adjacent
property within 200 feet. The current owners have different offsite parking agreements for other
parking to supplement their existing parking. While looking at the area on the map, there
appears to be plenty of parking nearby, however many of those stalls are tied up in easements
for another building nearby and are not always available. Currently, the required amount of
spaces is 67, on site they never have that amount of spaces. With previous leases they have
been able to exceed those 67 spaces, except for before 6:00 p.m. on weekdays. During those
times they only have 58 spaces available, which is a deficit of nine spaces. They have secured

more stalls for nights and weekends, and on the Ivy Space property they have 29 spaces
available under an agreement. These are the most likely to be used, and there are about 75
spaces in that area. Staff recommends approval of this amendment; the other conditions are the

report.

HOGGAN HAVEN SUBDIVISION REVIEW – 17 East 6150 South – Project #22-072
Susan Nixon presented this item. This application is for a flag lot subdivision located at the
address above. The property is on the north side of 6150 South, north of the Murray City Senior
Center and adjacent to the ballpark parking lot. This property is in the R-1.8 residential zoning
district. The request is for preliminary and final subdivision approval. She reviewed information
in the staff report and meeting packet. She shared photos of the home and property from
tonight’s packet and explained what was currently located there. If approved, the existing
structure will have to be removed prior to recording the plat. In the R-1-8 the flag lot regulations
require the minimum lot size to be 8000 square feet, but the flag or back lot must be at least
125% of the underlying zone. In this case, the front lot needs to be at least 8000 square feet
and the back or flag lot must be at least 10,000 square feet. She reviewed site plans from the
staff report. The new flag lot would need to have 25-foot setbacks in the front and rear, and a
minimum of eight feet on one side for a total of 20 on each side. This plan works as far as the
setbacks and public utility easement. In June of 2022, the applicant applied for a variance for
the require four-foot landscaping on each strip. For a flag lot, the drive access must be a width
of 20 feet hard surface, with four feet of landscape on each side, totaling 28 feet. In the
southwest corner of the property there is an existing fire hydrant that has been there for many
years. The fire hydrant is just over five feet from the west boundary, which lies just outside the
four-foot landscape strip. They applied for a variance to shift the driveway a few feet so the
hydrant is in the landscape strip and eliminate the four feet on the east side. To the eye, you will
never notice the difference because of the existing mature landscaping. By shifting the driveway
to the east, they would meet the 20 feet hard surface requirement, and still meet the intent of
