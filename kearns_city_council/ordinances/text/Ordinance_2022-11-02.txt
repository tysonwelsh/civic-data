    Ordinance No.: 2022-11-02                                Date: November 28, 2022

    AN ORDINANCE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
  AMENDING SETBACK REQUIREMENTS FOR DETACHED ACCESSORY
                      DWELLING UNITS


                                       RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District provides services to the
five Metro Townships in the Salt Lake Valley, unincorporated areas, and the Town of
Brighton; and
WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) adopted each Salt Lake County
ordinance in effect at the time of its incorporation pursuant to Utah Code § 10-2a-414(3)
and has authority to amend or repeal the county ordinance when it determines it is
necessary; and
WHEREAS, Kearns is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3c-103 (2) and has
authority to regulate detached accessory dwelling units under Utah Code § 10-9a-102; and
WHEREAS, Kearns has authority to amend land use ordinances pursuant to Utah Code §
10-9a-501 in accordance with the Municipal Land Use, Development, and Management
Act, Title 10, Section 9a, Utah Code, to regulate the construction and location of detached
accessory dwelling units; and
WHEREAS, the Kearns Council deems it necessary to amend its ordinances in order to
allow for the conversion of previously built accessory buildings to accessory dwelling units
and to allow Kearns residents a reasonable opportunity to build accessory dwelling units,
and for the protection and preservation of the public health, safety, and general welfare.
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL as follows:
1.     Amendment: Section 19.15.050 of the Kearns Municipal Code is hereby repealed
and replaced in its entirety with the revised Section 19.15.050, including Figure 1 and
Figure 2, attached hereto as Exhibit A.
2.      Severability. If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any part of this
Ordinance is unconstitutional or invalid, then such portion of this Ordinance, or specific
application of this Ordinance, shall be severed from the remainder, which shall continue in
full force and effect.
3.     Effective Date. This Ordinance will take effect immediately upon posting and
publication as required by law.
PASSED AND ADOPTED this 28th day of November 2022.

                                         EXHIBIT A



19.15.50 Setbacks

  A. Internal Accessory Dwelling Units. Setback standards for internal ADUs shall be
     consistent with setback standards for a single-family dwelling in the zone.
  B. New Detached Accessory Dwelling Units. Side yard setbacks on detached ADUs
     shall be a minimum of five (5) feet. Rear yard setbacks shall be a minimum of five
     (5) feet. Detached ADUs shall also be a minimum of six (6) feet from the main
     dwelling. If existing public utility easements are greater than the required setback,
     the minimum setback shall be the public utility easement boundary.
  C. Existing Accessory Structures Built Prior to November 28, 2022. Side yard and rear
     yard setbacks on existing detached structures to be converted into an ADU shall
     comply with the accessory building setback standards at the time the structure was
     erected.
