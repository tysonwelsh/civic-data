                                   RESOLUTION NO. 23-71


            A RESOLUTION REQUESTING THE RECERTIFICATION OF THE
                        DRAPER CITY JUSTICE COURT

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Annotated 78A-7-103 requires justice courts be certified at the end

of each four-year term; and

       WHEREAS, the term of the Draper City Justice Court expires on the 31st day of January,

2024; and

       WHEREAS, the members of the Draper City Council have received an opinion letter from

Mike Barker, City Attorney, which sets forth the requirements for the operation of justice courts and

the feasibility of continuing to maintain the same; and

       WHEREAS, the members of the Draper City Council have determined that it is in the best

interests of Draper City to continue to provide for the justice court.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

       1. Draper City, through its duly authorized representatives, hereby requests recertification of

the Draper City Justice Court by the Board of Justice Court Judges and the Utah Judicial Council.

       2. The Draper City Council hereby affirms its willingness to continue to meet all

requirements set forth by the Judicial Council for continued operation of the Draper City Justice

Court for the next four-year term of court, except as to any requirements that may be waived by the

Utah Judicial Council.

       3. This Resolution shall be effective upon passage.
