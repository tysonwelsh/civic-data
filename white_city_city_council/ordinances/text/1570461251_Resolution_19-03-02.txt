WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 19-03-02 DATED: April 4, 2019
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL APPROVING A PIPELINE LICENSING
AGREEMENT WITH SANDY CITY
RECITALS
A. The White City Metro Township (the “Metro Township”) is a Metro Township
pursuant to Utah Code Annotated §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.
B. Sandy City is a municipality that provides culinary water service to its residents,
and a small section of White City.
C. Sandy desires to install a pipeline and requires approval of the licensing
agreement, hereinafter known as Attachment “A” to this resolution.
D. White City, as the grantor of such agreements also desires Sandy to perform the
work described for the license.
E. White City and Sandy view the completion of the pipeline as beneficial to meeting
the public needs of the people of White City.
RESOLUTION

THEREFORE, IT IS RESOLVED the White City Metro Township Council
hereby approves and authorizes the Mayor to sign the attached pipeline license agreement
with Sandy City, hereinafter known as Exhibit A, subject to approval as to form by White
City legal staff.

LICENSE FOR PIPELINES

THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered into this day of

.20__, by and between the WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP, a municipal
corporation of the State of Utah, hereinafter called WHITE CITY, or GRANTOR, and
Sandy City Corporation, a political subdivision of the State of Utah, hereinafter called
GRANTEE.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the GRANTEE is desirous of obtaining from GRANTOR a license to
construct, and thereafter maintain and operate pipelines (PROJECT) within the right-of-
way limits of GRANTOR’S roads and highways as described in Exhibit A, attached
hereto and incorporated by reference , for the purpose of providing drinking water; and,

WHEREAS, it is in the mutual interest of the parties the GRANTEE can access
infrastructure within a roadway for the purpose of maintenance, repair, and/or
replacement; and

WHEREAS, GRANTOR is willing to grant said license under the terms and
conditions hereinafter set forth.

NOW, THEREFORE, In exchange for valuable consideration, including the
mutual covenants contained herein, it is agreed by and between the parties hereto as
follows:

1. LOCATION OF PIPELINES

The GRANTOR hereby grants a license to GRANTEE to construct, maintain, and
operate pipelines as provided by the terms of this Agreement. The pipelines shall be
located within the boundaries of the GRANTOR, as represented in EXHIBIT A of this
agreement. The location of the pipeline within the roads and highways, as shown in
EXHIBIT A, shall be in accordance with the plans, specifications and maps attached
hereto as EXHIBIT A, and on file in the offices of the parties.

. The plans, specifications and maps and any revisions thereto shall be approved by
GRANTOR or GRANTOR’S agent prior to construction. GRANTOR’S agent is the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Service District, by and through Public Works Engineering,
or any other authorized representative of the GRANTOR.

Pipeline location is subject to such changes or variations therefrom as may be
required by GRANTEE and approved by the GRANTOR at the time of construction.
Following completion of construction GRANTEE shall provide documents, plans or GIS
data, reasonably acceptable to GRANTOR showing distance from right-of-way line to
pipeline center lines on all GRANTOR roads and highways where said pipelines are
installed. All such documents, plans or related GIS data information shall be provided to
the GRANTOR and on file in the offices of the parties, for a time period defined by the
individual party, both parties agreeing that such information is protected under the Utah
Records Access Management Act. Utah Code 63G-2-100 et seq.
2. APPROVAL OF CONSTRUCTION

The excavation of the trench for the PROJECT shall not be commenced by the
GRANTEE until and after notice has been given by the GRANTEE to GRANTOR, and a
road-cut permit has been issued by GRANTOR. GRANTEE or its contractor shall
provide the GRANTOR a completed application for road-cut permit noting the location,
length of cut (feet), name and address of contractor performing the work, and name and
address of the licensed engineer or surveyor, if so required by Drinking Water regulation.
If required by GRANTOR, an application for road-cut permit shall be accompanied by
engineer stamped plans for the project. Approval of and issuance of a road-cut permit
shall be performed by GRANTOR or GRANTOR’S agent.
3. TRAFFIC CONTROL DURING CONSTRUCTION

The GRANTEE shall require traffic control in accordance with the standards set
forth in the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (“MUTCD”) with respect to
handling of traffic, and shall require watchmen, barricades, lights or other measures
pursuant to the standards set forth in the MUTCD during construction of the PROJECT.
4. COMPACTION OF BACKFILL

The backfilling of any trench within the paved poltion of the highway, the
shoulders thereof, or the portion under or intersecting street or highway shall be
compacted in accordance with GRANTOR’s standards and specifications and shall be
subject to review by the GRANTOR or GRANTOR's agent. The GRANTEE, or
GRANTEE’s contractor shall be liable for any damage which may result to the pavement
due to failure to properly compact the backfill.
5. RESTORATION OF EXISTING PAVEMENT

The GRANTEE shall replace, at its expense, any pavement removed or damaged
in accordance with GRANTOR’s standards and specifications. This pavement shall be
constructed in conformity with the standard specifications and shall be subject to the
inspection and reasonable approval of the GRANTOR or GRANTOR’s agent. If weather
conditions do not permit immediate placing of permanent pavement, a temporary
pavement shall be placed until such time as weather conditions are favorable, at which
time the temporary pavement shall be removed and replaced with a permanent pavement.
No cleated or metal crawler type equipment shall be permitted to operate on any
GRANTOR hard surfaced street. The repairs to pavement or surface shall include
pavements which have been damaged with construction equipment.

6. DISPOSAL OF SURPLUS MATERIAL IN CLEANING UP HIGHWAY

Upon completion of the work, all surplus material shall be removed from within
the limits of GRANTOR’s highway.

7. MAINTENANCE OF PIPELINES BY GRANTEE

The PROJECT shall at all times be maintained, repaired, reviewed and operated
by and at the expense of the GRANTEE.

8. MOVING OF PIPELINE IN CONSTRUCTION OR MAINTENANCE OF
GRANTOR INFRASTRUCTURE.

It is expressly understood and agreed by the parties hereto and as part of the
consideration for this agreement that the GRANTOR or GRANTOR’s agent shall have
the right to require relocation of said pipeline at any time necessary in the future
construction and maintenance of GRANTOR infrastructure, including but not limited to
highway expansion or reconstruction, storm drain installation or maintenance, and curb,
gutter, and sidewalk installation or maintenance, provided that the GRANTOR or
GRANTOR's agent gives ninety (90) days’ notice before construction is to commence.
The GRANTOR shall assume and pay all costs incident to relocation of the pipeline.

9. PRESERVATION OF SURVEY MONUMENTATION

Pursuant to Utah State Code Section 17-23-14, and Chapter 14.17 of the Salt Lake
County Code of Ordinances, if the Grantee finds it necessary to disturb a survey
monument for any reason, Grantee shall notify the Salt Lake County Surveyor and obtain
a Monument Preservation Permit at least five business days prior to the disturbing,
damaging, moving, removing, covering, resurfacing, roto-milling, or destroying of any

existing public survey monument(s). New survey monuments, or replacement survey
monuments, shall be constructed in accordance with the Monument Preservation Permit

10. LIABILITY

Any supervision or control exercised by the GRANTOR, or on its behalf, shall in
no way relieve the GRANTEE of any duty or responsibility to the general public, nor
relieve said GRANTEE from any liability for loss, damage or injury to persons or
property sustained by reason of the installation, maintenance, repair or removal of the
pipeline and its appurtenances, nor of said GRANTEE 's liability for damage to the
highway. The GRANTEE shall protect and indemnify and save harmless the GRANTOR
and GRANTOR’s agent from any and all damages, claims or injuries that may occur by
reason of the construction, maintenance, repair or removal of the PROJECT by the
GRANTEE provided, however, that this agreement shall not constitute an admission of
any liability as to any third party or give to any third party any greater or further right of
cause of action, it being understood and agreed that neither the GRANTOR nor the
GRANTEE recognized any liability for any acts of negligence whether of omission or
commission of any of its agents, servants or employees.
11. AGREEMENT NOT TO BE ASSIGNED

The GRANTEE shall not assign this agreement or any interest therein without the
written consent of the GRANTOR. |
12. SUCCESSORS AND ASSIGNS

All covenants and agreements herein contained shall be binding upon the parties
hereto, their successors and assigns.
13. NOTICE

Any notice required or permitted to be given hereunder shall be deemed sufficient
if given by a communication in writing, and shall be deemed to have been received (a)
upon personal delivery or actual receipt thereof, or (b) within three days after such notice
