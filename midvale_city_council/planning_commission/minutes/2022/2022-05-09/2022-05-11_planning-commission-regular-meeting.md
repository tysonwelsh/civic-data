GENERAL SESSION

Chair Snow called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:03 p.m. He
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS

     1. Gary Schow and Steve Bryner request Conditional Use Permit approval for a duplex to
        be constructed at 7169 S. 150 E. in the SF-1 (DO) zone.

Mr. Olsen stated that the applicants request conditional use permit for a duplex to be located at
7169 S 150 E in the SF-1 (DO) zone. If approved, a duplex structure will be constructed on an
existing flag lot. It will remain under one ownership. Public notice has been sent to property
owners within 500 feet of the subject parcel. No objections have been received as of the writing
of this report.

Mr. Olsen explains that there is commercial use to the South and East, with residential use to the
North and West. The Midvale City Code outlines requirements for the conditional use permit
(CUP) and the plans meet all required criteria. The Planning and Zoning Department
recommends approval of the CUP. The duplex would be placed towards the center of the lot. All
set back requirements follow code of both the main structure and the accessory garage building.
The Unified Fire Department has seen and approved this proposed plan.

DISCUSSION:

   1. Discussion on Bylaws regarding annual review of such bylaws (8.B) and election of
      officers (4.1).

      The bylaws are discussed in this meeting and will be voted on in a future meeting. The City
       Council does not need to vote on these. Under the Planning Commission bylaws, Section
       4.1 states that the Chair and Vice Chair are selected at the first meeting in January. In the
       City Municipal Code, Section 17.4.2 states that the first term meeting is supposed to be
       held in the first week of February.

      It is being suggested that the Commission changes their bylaws to match the Municipal
       Code. It is more logical to have the new members present when Chair and Vice Chair are
       elected. It would be fairer for current members of Planning Commission to elect their
       Chair and Vice Chair.

      Mr. Liedtke is concerned that the Commissioners would not yet know each other by the
      first meeting of February.

      The Commissioners would like to hold their election in March after working together for
       two months and having experience with two prior meetings.

      Mr. Olsen and Mr. Wilcox agree that this aligns with the Municipal Code while allowing
      the members to get to know each other prior to the election of Chair and Vice Chair.
