GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-2-2021 Petition by MATTHEW IDEMA requesting a General Plan change from Large
           Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre)
           and a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RS
           (Residential Sustainability, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). The property is
           located at 3724 S 3200 W on 4.3 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545
           or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

              Discussion: Steve Pastorik explained back in 2019, the RS zone was created.
              When the council passed the ordinance, they placed a cap on the size, under 10
              acres. If this application is approved, Steve stated this would be the first RS zone
              in the City. Steve said the subject property has duplexes to the north and single-
              family homes to the south.

               Commissioner Winters asked if the adjacent homes are zoned R-1-8. Steve said
               they are zoned R-1-8. Steve presented the proposed concept plan for a 15 lot
               subdivision to be built on the subject location. He reminded the Commission that
               the RS zone requires energy and water efficiency, and sustainability. Steve said
               the applicant meets the code requirements.

               Steve stated a proposal in 2015 for a townhome development on the subject
               property was denied.

               Commissioner Woodruff asked if the detention area is going to be something
               other than a weed patch. Steve said there had been communication with the
               applicant and Public Works about this. He explained the Federal Government
               requires Cities to handle stormwater differently. He explained there are two ways
               to handle the detention area for this application. We could have an association be
               responsible for taking care of the detention area. He explained one concern would
               be if the tenants would continue to pay for this service.

               Another option would be placing the detention area on a lot. So, there would be
               an easement on someone’s lot, and the owner would be responsible for
               maintaining the detention area. Steve explained there would be further discussion
               on this topic.

               Steve explained typically we don’t have all of the engineering figured out at this
               point in the process. The applicant is just looking to have the zone change
               approved. So, they are aware of what they need to deal with regarding the
               detention area, the just don’t have everything worked out yet.

               Commissioner Winters said that it sounds like the applicant meets the
               requirements to apply for the RS zone, is that correct? Steve said that was
               correct, and the question for the Commission is if this proposal will fit in this
               location.

               Commissioner Winters asked if the alternative zone change would be from A to
               RE. Steve said that is correct. Commissioner Winters feels the RS zone would fit
               better than the RE zone in this location.

SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SA-3-2021 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY ENGINEERING DIVISION is requesting a
          plat amendment for the Anna Caroline Drive Subdivision. The amended plat will
          affect lot 1, parcel A and a portion of Anna Caroline Drive. The purpose of the
          amended subdivision is to correct the alignment of Anna Caroline Drive. The
          subdivision is located at approximately 2450 South Anna Caroline Drive and is
          zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
          steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman explained how this proposal is a fix for Anna Caroline Drive.
               UDOT had built their portion of the road in the wrong location. Steve presented a
               slide of Anna Caroline Drive road and explained what portion of the road will be
               vacated and which part will be dedicated to place the right-of-way in the correct
               location.

               Steve noted the applicant’s proposal is 5 to 6 feet shy of landscaping on either

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
CA-1-2021 Petition by DLM DEVELOPMENT requesting conditional use approval for 201
          Commerce Center buildings 1-4, an office/warehouse facility. The property is
          located at 4387 West 2100 South on 45.11 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing).
          (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Jody Knapp explained this proposal is for a conditional use for the same property
               addressed in the PUD-1-2021 application. She said they would meet the
               landscaping requirement with the addition of the park. Jody then presented both
               cross-sections showing the footage for the park strip, sidewalk, and landscaping.
               Commissioner Winters feels the park is a good tradeoff for the landscaping.

               Commissioner Lovato asked if there will be a curb, gutter, and sidewalk added to
               the subject location. Jody explained there had been more discussion about this
               since the study meeting. She pointed out that Godfrey is quite a ways west of the
               subject property, and they have a curb and gutter along the Frontage Road to 7200
               South. On the north side, the first phase of 201, there is no sidewalk. The
               sidewalk was not a requirement at that time. She explained that The ordinance
               has since been updated to say at least one side of the road is required to have a
               sidewalk. Jody explained there would be a further conversation about what side
               of the road will add the sidewalk.
