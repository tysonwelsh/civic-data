Mr. Smallwood prepared a presentation regarding House Bill 462 and asked if the commission
wanted to see graphs presented to the council before or after the presentation. He brought up
the graphs that had been updated, specifically he updated the graphs to reflect the 2022 AMI
values and noted that the 80% amount is $81,920. He also broke down what each adult in the
family would have to make per hour, working full-time, to get to that annual amount; each parent
or adult would have to make $19.69 an hour in order to afford 80% of the AMI, $12.31 an hour
to reach the 50% of the AMI, and $7.38 an hour to reach the 30% of the AMI. Moderate income
is considered 80% of the AMI, low income is considered 50% of the AMI, and very low income is
30% of the AMI. He discussed monthly housing allowances, and for the calculations, the utilities
were based on $250 a month. Including utilities, the monthly housing allowance for an income of
80% of the AMI is $2048, 50% is 1280, and 30% is $768; those amounts are calculated based
on a family of four. He also discussed the monthly rental amounts at Birkhill, Metro at Fireclay
and Lost Creek.
