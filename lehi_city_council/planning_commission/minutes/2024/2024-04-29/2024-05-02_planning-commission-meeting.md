Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order

Commission Chair Jackson welcomed everyone to the meeting after the initial downtown bike
tour took place.

2) Downtown bike tour

3) Discussion of Bike and Pedestrian Plan Update

Mike West welcomed the Planning Commission back to the Council Chambers to discuss the
bike tour they had completed downtown.

Brittney Harris shared public comments regarding transportation and presented the results of a
walking survey that gathered information from the Lehi community on walkability.

Mike West then presented on trail systems, marketing and outreach efforts, as well as
coordination and planning initiatives within the community.

4) Adjournment

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Everett moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Roberts seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:05 p.m.
