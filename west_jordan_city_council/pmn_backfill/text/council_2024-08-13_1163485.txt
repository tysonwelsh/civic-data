 2. PUBLIC HEARINGS
        a. Truth in Taxation Public Hearing
           Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck gave a brief history of the Fairway Estates
           Special Service Recreation District (FESSRD). She reminded the Board of the resident
           survey and their support to use reserves with no tax increase in FY2024 and
           implementation of a larger increase in FY2025. Ms. Steck said the FESSRD budget was
           prepared based on that direction and the proposed budget included both a tax increase
           and continued use of reserves. She outlined the five-year plan.

           Ms. Steck showed the proposed FESSRD property tax rate for FY2024 of .000810 for
           $11,146 revenue. While state law did not require public action for a budget with a
           revenue stream less than $20,000, the City chose to hold a public hearing for
           transparency. The Board was required to adopt the tax rate and budget before September
           1, 2024.

           Chair Jacob opened a public hearing at 7:10 pm.
           Comments:
           None
           Chair Jacob closed the public hearing at 7:10 pm.

           Responding to a question from Board Member Shelton, Ms. Steck explained that needed
           repairs had reduced FESSRD reserves, and staff advised increasing the tax rate to build
