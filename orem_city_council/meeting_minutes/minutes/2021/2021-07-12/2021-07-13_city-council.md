       PRESENTATION - Utah Valley Drug Prevention Coalition

Ms. Brittney and Ms. Wendy introduced themselves and informed the council on what the Utah
Valley Drug Prevention Coalition does. Ms. Brittney informed the council on youth drug use
statistics. Ms. Wendy spoke to the council about safe drug disposal. She then informed the

to the council at a later date. Mayor Brunst asked the coalition representatives to send local drug

       UPDATE - Hillcrest Park Update

Mr. Bybee started off his presentation by introducing the proposed master design for the Hillcrest
Park property. Ms. Lisa from Landmark Design briefly ran through the different features of the
proposed park plan, including a demonstration park, twelve new Pickle-Ball courts, a splash pad,
and large open-lawn flexible play spaces.

General discussion ensued regarding basketball and tennis court locations, as well as shaded
areas within the park.

Ms. Lisa from Landmark Design then introduced different possible phases the park could be built
in, but recommended that building it all in one phase would be the most cost effective method.
Mr. Davidson expressed that the City could most likely pay for the entire project in one phase,
instead of having to split the project into multiple.

General discussion ensued regarding the cost of various features, other miscellaneous cost items,
water conservation within the park, homeless population around the park, and how the city can
make Hillcrest Park more accessible to the rest home directly to the west of the property.

The council all expressed their agreement on the direction the park design is heading.

       PUBLIC HEARING / RESOLUTION - CDBG Projected funds

Mr. Ransom stated in response to the Coronavirus Pandemic (COVID-19), the U.S. Department
of Housing and Urban Development Community Development Block Grant provided the City of
Orem with a second CDBG allocation of $287,940 to be used to prevent, prepare for, and
respond to COVID-19. The CDBG Advisory Commission recommends that the Orem City
Council allocates funding from the previously allocated in second round CDBG-CV funding for
public service projects. Mr. Ransom showed the council a summary of CDBG funding and the
amount left to be allocated. Mr. Ransom asked the council to approve $20,000 worth of funds to
be given to the Orem Senior Friendship Center to fix current plumbing issues that they have.

Mayor Brunst opened the public hearing at 6:10 PM. Hearing no comments, Mayor Brunst
closed the public hearing and brought the item back to the City Council for discussion.

Mayor Brunst moved to approve the enterprise fund transfer. Mr. Macdonald seconded the
motion. Those voting aye: Richard F. Brunst, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson,
Debby Lauret, and David Spencer. The motion passed.

       Accessory Apartments

Mr. Bench stated the State Legislature recently changed the law pertaining to accessory
apartments throughout the State. Due to these changes, the City has found a need to change some
of the language in the Accessory Apartments ordinance.
The City will no longer be allowed to restrict the size of the accessory apartment, the number of
allowed bedrooms or require interior connectivity. The accessory apartment will be required to
be located within the footprint of the single-family dwelling. Accessory apartments will be
subject to the rental dwelling license requirements set forth in the Orem City Code.

Mayor Brunst opened the public hearing at 6:15 PM. Hearing no comments, Mayor Brunst
closed the public hearing and brought the item back to the City Council for discussion.

Mayor Brunst moved to approve by Ordinance the amendments as listed. Mr. Macdonald
seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Richard F. Brunst, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald, Terry
Peterson, Debby Lauret, and David Spencer. The motion passed.


       PUBLIC HEARING / ORDINANCE - Amending Appendix DD of the Orem City Code
       by adding the concept plan and elevations; and amending Section 22-5-3(A) and the
       zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone of approximately 14.7 acres from
       the Highway Services (HS) zone to the PD36 zone at approximately 1430 North Geneva
       Road

Mr. Bench stated the applicant requests the rezone and map amendment from the Highway
Services zone to the PD36 zone to build three buildings similar to the buildings at Orem
Technology Campus directly to the south of his property. Building 1 will be 95,760 square feet,
building 2 will be 99,900 square feet and the third building will be 8,400 square feet. Appendix
DD will also be amended by adding the concept plan and proposed elevations.
One of the biggest reasons for the rezone is the parking. The HS zone requires one parking stall
for every 250 square feet of gross leasable area. That would be approximately 783 required
parking stalls for the three buildings. The PD36 zone requires one parking stall per 750 square
feet for the first 50,000 square feet of building area and one parking stall per 1,000 square feet
for the second 50,000 square feet of building area. That would be approximately 250 required
parking stalls for the three buildings. These buildings will have an office/warehouse use similar
to the Orem Technology Campus.
The PD36 zone boundary is currently allowed per ordinance between 1200 North and 1600
North and between the I-15 freeway and Geneva Road.
Mr. Bench stated it was staff's recommendation to approve the proposed rezone.

General discussion ensued regarding building height and public road access.

       PUBLIC HEARING / ORDINANCE - FY 2021-2022 Budget Amendments

Mr. Nelson stated the City contracted with a third-party firm to evaluate and analyze the City's
compensation and benefits programs to determine if the City needed to make changes to better
align itself with the market. This budget amendment is the culmination of the results of the study
recommendations.
Mr. Nelson explained the documents received during this meeting’s work session pertaining to
new changes in employee pay programs and hybrid step programs are part of the budget and
therefore need to be voted upon.

Mayor Brunst opened the public hearing at 6:30 PM. Hearing no comments, Mayor Brunst
closed the public hearing and brought the item back to the City Council for discussion.

Mayor Brunst moved to approve by Ordinance the amendments to the City of Orem Fiscal Year
2021-2022 Budget. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Richard F. Brunst, Jeff
Lambson, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson, Debby Lauret, and David Spencer. The motion
passed.
