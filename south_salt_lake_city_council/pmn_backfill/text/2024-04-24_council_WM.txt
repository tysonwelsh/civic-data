       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood made some informational statements about City related programs and
          then highlighted some upcoming City events.

              Regarding the adopt-a-storm-drain program that were raised at the last meeting.
              The start-up fee for this year is $1,548, and the annual cost for the program is
              $2,288. This program enables the City to meet permit requirements, as the City
              holds their permit under Salt Lake County. The program also helps the City to
              effectively manage the stormwater and brings attention to storm drains by letting
              people know that they can remove debris from the storm drain surface. Residents
              can sign up at: ut.adopt-a-drain.org

              Mayor Wood then informed the Council that they are continuing to be a part of the
              ‘Healthy Utah Community’ program. The City was initially awarded this in 2021 and
              received redesignation last week. The City continues to ensure that residents have
              access to mental health, physical activity, healthy eating opportunities and
              resources.

              May 3rd - ‘Family Sports Fun Night’ will be hosted by the Recreation Department at
              the Central Park Community Center from 6-8:30 p.m. Pre-registration is required
              and can be done at: sslc.gov

              May 4th – ‘Mural Fest’ will showcase 14 murals that will be across several locations
              in the City. Residents can download a map and event guide at: themuralfest.com

Unfinished Business


    1. A Resolution of the South Salt Lake City Council Authorizing the South Salt Lake City
       Police Department to Dispose of Unclaimed or Confiscated Firearms in the South Salt
       Lake Police Department Possession.

        Police Evidence Technician, Tiffany Kalinowski, gave a review of the matter. The police
        department has two sets of guns that need to either be donated to the Utah State Crime
        Lab and to send the rest to an FFL (Federal Firearms License) approved organization that
        will destroy the guns and provide a certificate of destruction for them.

        This item was discussed in the Work Meeting that preceded.

        A copy of the Resolution is attached and incorporated by this reference.
