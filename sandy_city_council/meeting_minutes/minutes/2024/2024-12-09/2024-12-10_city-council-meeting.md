General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                                  6:00 pm - General Citizen Comment

                                  Council Chair Zach Robinson invited the public to participate in General Citizen
                                  Comment.

                                  Ms. Susan Hardman expressed concerns with the proposed development and rezone of
                                  the parcel by 7800 South that is being considered by the Council this evening (Item 11).
                                  She had concerns about parking, traffic, and safety.

                                  Ms. Rebekkah Allen with Salt Lake County spoke about the upcoming Point in Time
                                  Count of unsheltered people in the community and invited the community to volunteer for
                                  the effort.

                                  Ms. Vitalia Sun, Sandy Youth Council member, spoke about alternative transportation
                                  options for Sandy and prioritizing the best options for Sandy City.

                                  Public comment closed.

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      8:40 pm
                      Dustin Fratto, Council Director, updated the Council on upcoming agenda items. There
                      will be interviews for committee appointments. Alta Canyon design team will present to
                      the Council. The Council meetings for the 24th and 31st are cancelled.

Council Member Business

                      8:42 pm

                      Council Member Marci Houseman provided an update from the Jordan River Commission.
                      She spoke about the Great Salt Lake Basin Integrated Plan. More information can be
                      found on their website.

                      Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked the Council staff and Administration for holding
                      the Holiday Dinner. He also thanked the Administration for receiving his 5 year pin.

                      Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer thanked Jake Warner for meeting with him. He is
                      planning on bringing to the Council a mobility amendment to the general plan. He also
                      suggested options for tours for the Council for 2025: Waste Management MRF Center
                      and the UDOT Transportation Center.

                      Council Member Zach Robinson thanked staff for organizing the Holiday Dinner. He
                      appreciated taking part in the longevity awards for employees at the recent luncheon. It
                      was a great event.

                      Council Member Alison Stroud congratulated all the employees for receiving longevity
                      awards. She enjoyed helping at the Senior Center Thanksgiving luncheon event. She
                      acknowledged Jake Warner's work on the CDBG committee and Mike Bullock with
                      Emergency Managment. She enjoyed the Light Up the Cairns event and also suggested
                      a tour of the MVP Shelter for the Council in 2025.

Mayor's Report

                      8:45 pm
                      Mayor Zoltanski expressed thanks to everyone in leadership roles in the city. The Light
                      Up the Cairns event was a wonderful community event. The addition of the holiday market
                      was very positive. The employee luncheon was a wonderful event and she acknowledged
                      Public Utilities for winning the decorated tree contest. She also acknowledged the VIPS
                      and thanked them for their many hours of service to the city. She congratulated all
                      employees who received longevity awards and noted that Leslie Casaril was recognized
                      for 40 years with the city. She also announced that the City will receive $700,000 in
                      TRCC funding for improvements to Bicentennial Park which will include the addition of
                      pickleball courts, new playground, and a hammock park.

CAO Report

                      9:07 pm
                      Shane Pace, CAO, thanked the Council for approving the increased fire department
                      staffing of the 4th ambulance. He acknowledged the Police Department response to an
                      incident and spoke about the use of drones by the police department in their response.
