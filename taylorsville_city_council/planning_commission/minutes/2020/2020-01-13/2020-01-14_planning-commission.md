                                             ZONING TEXT AMENDMENT


  4.       Recommendation to the City Council for a Zoning Text Amendment to the Taylorsville Municipal Code,
  Section 13.08.020 ( E ) Permitted Land Use Matrix by the Commercial, Office, Industrial, Mixed Use, Transit
  Corridor, and Research and Development Districts, and Chapter 13.36 Definitions. (Angela Price/Senior Planner)


4.1     Ms. Price presented this item. Staff is cleaning up the recent over-haul of the Taylorsville Municipal Code
        regarding land uses. (6:35:14) The City Staff is requesting a text amendment to Section 13.08.020 (E)
        Permitted Land Use Matrix by the Commercial, Office, Industrial, Mixed Use, Transit Corridor, and Research
        and Development Districts, and Chapter 13.36 Definitions. The intent is to clean up some inconsistencies
        among zoning districts and to ensure that land uses that are found within the Land Use Table have proper
        definitions and correct footnotes. She highlighted Staff’s recommendations, such as changes to the auto light
        trucks, RV and all terrain’s dealership both with use of indoor and outdoor storage. Another change was bed
        and breakfast facilities to allow that use in the Community Commercial Zone as a non-administrative
        conditional use. Commercial retail sales and services would be allowed in all zoning districts. Allowing hotels
        in all the zoning districts. Mixed use structures, same thing. Just making sure those land uses are allowed
        within all zoning districts and footnotes added appropriate for future development. She continued on to say
        they have proposed changes to some of the footnotes, i.e., Footnote #2 – changing the distance between a
        building and a residential zoning boundary from 500’ to 150’ for a drive through, because there is probably no
        single development within the City that would need the 500’ distance. In a few of the footnotes, she had
        struck a few of the agricultural and residential uses because there are no longer agricultural zones in the City
        and to assure consistent language throughout the Code.              With Footnote #4 – striking out language;
        Footnote #8 is tied to the indoor storage of RV, auto sales and equipment. Adding clarifying language to the
        Mixed-Use Structure Section regarding allowed densities. With the definitions, there was one added for
        indoor storage of auto, truck, recreational vehicles and cleaned up language regarding automotive sales,
        service station and automotive service station. Basically, they had been the same thing but tied to different
        land uses in the Land Use Tale, which was confusing during the processing of applications. Also adding a
        definition of equipment sales and services because it previously had not been defined in the Land Use Table.

4.2     She listed her specific changes as follows:

        4.2.1    Amend the Land Use Table (13.08.020 (E) to ensure land uses are congruent between districts, and to
                 facilitate the potential for future development;
        4.2.2    Amend the footnote section to be consistent with current code, and to provide requirements specific
                 to Auto, Truck, RV, Indoor Storage;
        4.2.3    Add a definition for Auto, Truck, RV, Indoor Storage, and Equipment Sales and Rentals;
        4.2.4    Amend the definition for Automotive Self-Service Station and Automotive Service Station.

4.3     FINDINGS:
        4.3.1  This application was initiated by the City of Taylorsville.
        4.3.2  The City is requesting a text amendment to the Taylorsville Municipal Code Section 13.08.020 (E)
               Permitted Land Use Matrix by the Commercial, Office, Industrial, Mixed Use, Transit Corridor, and
               Research and Development Districts to ensure consistency between zoning districts and with current
               code.
        4.3.3  A propose amendment to 13.36, Definitions includes a definition for Auto, Truck RV, Indoor Storage
               and Equipment Sales and Rentals.

      5.      12S19 – Consideration of a Preliminary Plat for an Eight-Lot Subdivision at 6573 South 3200 West.
      (Karyn Kerdolff/GIS Planner). 7:22:13

 5.1        Ms. Kerdolff presented this item. The applicant, Peter Gamvroulas, on behalf of Ivory Homes, is requesting
            consideration of a preliminary plat for an eight-lot subdivision at 6573 South 3200 West for approximately
            2.89 acres. The property is zoned R-1-8 with a minimum 8,000 square foot lot size. The proposed subdivision
            will have eight lots. Lots will range in size from 11,933 to 16,038 square feet. All lots meet the 8,000 square
            foot requirement. To meet the Code requirements outlined in Chapter 13.20.030, the minimum lot width
            must be at least 70 feet. The proposed subdivision meets the setback requirements for the R-1-8 zone. The
            lots will be accessed from Himalaya Way and meet all applicable lot standards. Currently there is no sidewalk
            or ADA access along the western portion of the property on 3200 West that connects the current Ivory
            Highlands walking path and a sidewalk at the southwest corner of the property. She showed the site plan for
            the preliminary plat.

5.2         FINDINGS:
            5.2.1  This application was initiated by Peter Gamvroulas.
            5.2.2  The property is located at 6573 South 3200 West.

            5.2.3    The property is zoned R-1-8 (Residential one dwelling per 8,000 square feet)
            5.2.4    The applicant is requesting preliminary plat approval for an eight-lot subdivision.
            5.2.5    The proposed plat meets the required square footage and setbacks for the R-1-8 zone.
            5.2.6    All required documentation has been submitted by Peter Gamvroulas or his team.

5.3         STAFF RECOMMENDATION: Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approves the Preliminary Plat
            for an eight-lot subdivision at 6573 South 3200 West.
