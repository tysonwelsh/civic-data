       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood thanked everyone who voiced their comments and shared a
          statement that addressed the findings of the audit, the challenges when there’s a
          change in leadership, the challenges when unraveling years’ worth of operational
          inefficiencies, and the importance of the values that residents and City leadership
          have expressed in what they want to see modeled in the City’s policing approach. A
          detailed action plan will be made within the next couple of months to address the
          next steps in the City’s approach to the matter. Mayor Wood emphasized that
          public safety remains one of the City’s top priorities.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None.

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Mitchell noted that he would like more clarity on the comments
          made about community policing in the investigation. He thanked those that shared
          their thoughts and concerns during the public comments section.

           Council Member Williams stated that she would reserve from making comments
           until she was able to read the report in its entirety.

           Council Member Bynum thanked those that shared their thoughts and concerns
           during the public comments section. She also said that clarity on what community
           policing means when its brought up and what the City’s definition of it is may need
           to be further expanded on.

           Council Member Thomas thanked those that shared their thoughts and concerns
           during the public comments section.

           Council Member Huff thanked those that shared their thoughts and concerns during
           the public comments section.

           Council Member deWolfe thanked those that shared their thoughts and concerns
           during the public comments section. He stated that he was committed to finding a
           solution and providing honest feedback.
