         Council Rules and Norms relating to Council Work Sessions and Meetings
\
                   Council Executive Director Eller-Smith provided a presentation regarding Council staff's recommended adjustments to
    ,1   the Council's Rules and Norms in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. The goal of the adjusted meeting format and procedures
/
         is to limit the number of people in attendance at meetings while still providing ample opportunity for public input and ensuring
         legal requirements are met. She reviewed the typical meeting schedule and asked if the Council is intent on maintaining that
         schedule or holding fewer meetings each month. The Council indicated they are comfmtable holding virtual meetings and
         maintaining their typical schedule of meeting once each week. Ms. Eller-Smith stated the original intent was to open a space at the
         Municipal Building to allow for public or staff to attend a meeting physically; however, as public orders are updated, it may not be
         possible to keep a space available. Chair Choberka indicated that in discussing the issue with staff, leadership was concerned with
         disenfranchising certain members of the population based on their inability to participate in a virtual meeting because they do not
         have a computer, smart phone, or internet access. The Council discussed this concern, but indicated it is necessary for the City
         Council to lead by example and stress the importance of social distancing to minimize exposure to the illness. Ms. Eller-Smith
         stated that meetings will still be recorded and posted in a public location so they can be accessed after the meeting has concluded.
         The Council communicated their desire to advertise options for the public to communicate their feelings about any agenda item to
         the entire Council prior to discussion or voting. Ms. Eller-Smith stated Council staff can advertise accordingly.
                   Ms. Eller-Smith moved to the section of the Rules and Nonns document dealing with action items on City Council
         agendas. There was a focus on the way the Council will accept public input regarding action items and whether it is possible to
         accept public comments in real-time. The Council concluded to support the language encouraging residents to provide comments
         through the Council's website, via a dedicated phone line, or email. All public comments will be made available to the public as
         part of the materials for a given meeting. The Council expressed their desire for constant evaluation of the functionality of the
         Zoom platfonn to detennine appropriate adjustments to the virtual meeting fonnatto ensure transparency and public accessibility.
                   There was brief discussion regarding the way the Council will handle recognition items during virtual meetings. The
         Council indicated they do not want to stop recognizing important events or people in the community. Ms. Eller-Smith stated that
         Council staff can develop protocol for continuing with recognition items during vhtual meetings and recognition plaques or
         documentation can be sent to recipients after the meeth1g during which they are recognized.
                   Ms. Eller-Smith stated that Council staff will develop a resolution for Council consideration that will fonnalize the
         Council's intent to adjust Rules and Nonns to allow for virtual meetings for the duration of the public health pandemic.

         Council Business
                  Housing issues impacted by COVID-19: Chair Choberka stated that she received an email from the housmg committee
         associated with the Ogden Community Action Network (Ogden CAN) requesth1g that the City Council and Administration
         consider a joint resolution that would supp mt action by the State of Utah to enact a 60-day moratorium on evictions. Ms. Eller-
         Smith emphasized that the City Council and Mayor do not have the authority to prohibit evictions or mandate adjusted policies for

lenders seeking to collect mortgage payments during this time. However, the Council can adopt a resolution intended to
communicate a message to State leaders. She facilitated discussion among the Council regarding the concept of the proposed
resolution and the group reached the conclusion to review the draft language included in the proposed resolution provided by
Ogden CAN. Council members Blair and Hyer expressed concern that placing a moratorium on evictions and providing tenants
the oppmtunity to skip or miss rent payments will only shift the financial burden of this crisis to landlords/property owners. Chair
Choberka stated there are some federal programs intended to provide support for landlords. Council member Nadolski added that
if 'shelter in place' orders are issued, the intent of the moratorium would be to ensure that renters are not at risk oflosing their
residence, which would make it harder for them to self-quarantine. Council member White agreed and stated it is importantto take
actions from a public health standpoint now. Council member Lopez said he is supportive of adopting the resolution to
recommend the moratorium to State leaders. The group ultimately concluded to advise staff to evaluate the proposed resolution
and take the steps necessary to add an action item to a future business meeting agenda for consideration of adoption.
          Annual audit contract: Ms. Eller-Smith reported that an internal working committee has evaluated proposals received in
response to the City's request for proposals for the City's annual audit. The committee has developed a recommendation for
awarding the audit contract and she asked if the Council would like to personally interview the auditing finn before considering
the recommendation. The Council expressed their confidence in Council and Administration staff that participated in the
evaluation process and indicated they are comfortable considering their recommendation without a Council interview.
          Miscellaneous Council business: Council member Hyer thanked Mayor Caldwell and members of City Administration
for providing regular updates regarding adjustments to City operations in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. Chief
Administrative Officer Johnson provided the Council with a summary of the manner in which the City is conducting business; this
included a report of networking with local community partners and health care providers who are providing the City with advice
regarding appropriate response protocol and procedures. Mayor Caldwell added that City Administration is participating in daily
conference calls with the Utah League of Cities and Towns and the State of Utah to gather as much information as possible about
the appropriate response to what is an unprecedented situation. He will continue to update the public and City employees
regarding any changes to City operations.
          Chair Choberka encouraged all Council members and residents to support local businesses during this difficult time.
Council member Stephens stated he would be willing to consider additional adjustments to the budget to allocate funding for local
businesses and others in the community who may be suffering because of this health crisis. Council member White said some
cities are anticipating a reduction in their sales tax fund source and she would advise that the City prepare for something similar
rather than look for ways to spend money.
          Emergency loan program: Mr. Johnson provided the Council with a report of the loan application process and the
criteria an applicant must meet to qualify for a loan. The Business Information Center has received 30 inquiries and 16
applications to date. Community and Economic Development Director Christopulos noted that businesses located in the Central
Business District Zone will have priority for receiving loans and Administration is seeking additional funds that could be spread to
businesses throughout the entire City.
          Council calendar: Ms. Eller-Smith briefly reviewed the Council's calendar for the coming weeks and reported on agenda
items that are slated for upcoming meetings. There was brief focus on imminent actions related to the Marshall White Center.


         The meeting adjourned at 5:48 p.m.
