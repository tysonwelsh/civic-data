throughout the project.  In the fall of 2017, Mountainland Community Housing Trust

the Planning Commission to look at the plan and determine if it complied with the 
integration provision of the Code.

applicant.  These were: 
1. Amend the language of the Development Agreement regarding integration.

for 1,290 residential units and 50,000 SF of commercial use through the Specially 
Planned Area (SPA) provisions of the 1998 Code and General Plan.

SPA was recorded.
