CONSENT ITEMS

   ● APPROVAL OF MEETING MINUTES
       ○ Minutes of the SSLD Meeting - May 11, 2021

Chair Brunst moved to approve the Consent Agenda as listed. Mr. Spencer seconded the
motion. Those voting aye: Richard F. Brunst, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson,
David Spencer, and Brent Sumner. The motion passed.


SCHEDULED ITEMS

       PUBLIC HEARING / RESOLUTION - Adopting Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Special
       Service Lighting District of Orem (SSLD) Budget

Mr. Nelson stated on May 11, 2021, the Board of Directors received the SSLD Tentative Budget
for Fiscal Year 2021-2022. The purpose of this hearing is to receive input from the citizens,
make any changes, approve, and adopt the Budget for Fiscal Year 2021-2022.



      Special Service Lighting District - June 15, 2021 (pg.1)          A complete video of the meeting can be found at

General discussion ensued regarding various SSLD budget details and light post ownership.

Chairman Brunst opened the public hearing at 7:23 pm. Hearing no comments, Chairman Brunst
closed the public hearing and brought the item back to the City Council for further discussion.

Chair Brunst moved to approve by Resolution, the adoption of the Fiscal Year 2021-2022
Special Service Lighting District of Orem (SSLD) Budget. Mr. Peterson seconded the motion.
Those voting aye: Richard F. Brunst, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson, David
Spencer, and Brent Sumner. The motion passed.


ADJOURNMENT
Chair Brunst moved to adjourn. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Richard
F. Brunst, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson, David Spencer, and Brent Sumner.
The motion passed.




The meeting adjourned at 7:24 PM.
