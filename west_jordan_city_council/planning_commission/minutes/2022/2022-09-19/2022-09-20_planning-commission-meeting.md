Jadon Wagner, applicant and owner of Truck Ranch, said the site is approximately 20 acres. Sixteen
acres will be used for an auto mall and the remaining property will be used as flex retail space. Truck
Ranch has multiple locations and sells primarily trucks. The proposed location will be their
headquarters and will also have a large service department for all types of vehicles. They feel that there
is an opportunity for an underserved demographic in this area that has little to no dealerships. The RDA
study found that only 8% of West Jordan residents are purchasing vehicles within West Jordan. He said
that the location on the freeway has a good opportunity for a successful business. The flex retail will
have some food/ beverage uses and other types of retail, which will be on lot 2.


Larry Gardner noted that Paul Coates, who represents the West Jordan Redevelopment Agency, was
also in attendance. The West Jordan RDA owns the subject property. He reviewed the surrounding land
uses. The 5- lot subdivision plat includes an area along 5600 West that requires dedication to the city.

4.
         Comprehensive General Plan— Discuss and provide input regarding the 2022 West Jordan
         Comprehensive General Plan Update; City-wide applicability; City of West Jordan
         applicant)



Ray McCandless shared the history and process this document had gone through with the General Plan
Committee. They met more than 20 times in regular meetings beginning in 2020. Three subcommittees
were established in early 2021 assigned to review certain elements of the plan. Initially staff made
recommendations to the General Plan Committee on the overall plan. As the General Plan Committee
suggested changes, including the subcommittees, those changed were made on the document. The draft
that is before the Planning Commission   tonight is a culmination of all of the recommendations.   A lot of

 time and work had been spent on each ofthe chapters within the plan. It is now up to the Planning
 Commission to decide how they want to review the document. Staff recommended that they could
 review a few of the easier chapters tonight and take other chapters at future meetings. Staff appreciated
 all of the time and work that was expended by the General Plan Committee in bringing this document
 forward.


Jay Thomas agreed that there had been hours and hours of hard work by the Committee and staff, and
he appreciated all who participated.

Ray McCandless said the old plan was divided into goals, policies, and implementation measures. The
General Plan Committee wanted those things to be consolidated and more manageable. They took out
the goals that had already been attained by the city as well as any that were duplicated. They then
created new goals. ` Goals' will be renamed to `Guiding Principles' as recommended by the Planning
Commission. They are working on placing the goals into a hierarchy with subsequent related goals
falling under that.

Ammon Allen liked how the goals are set up at the end of each chapter, he stated that he had some
corrections that will be given to staff, which are mostly grammatical and would not change the overall
content of the document.

Introduction

This section was reworked to be clearer. It also talks about the survey and open houses. The 2012 Plan
only mentions the capital facilities plan and the electrical plan. However, many other plans that support
the General Plan are now referenced in order to tie them all together. The Committee wanted to keep
this document a high-level, visionary document, so specifics are left within each supporting plan
document.



There were no changes from the Planning Commission.

Population and Demographics
This section was updated with information from the 2020 Census and 2019 American Communities
Survey. West Jordan is now the 3`d largest city in Utah. Ethnic distribution has changed somewhat with
the largest growth being the Hispanic population. Median family income is a little higher than Salt Lake
County, but per capita income is a little less because West Jordan has a larger family size.
There were no changes from the Planning Commission.

Housing
Updates to this chapter include numbers from the 2020 Census, American Community Survey, and city
building permit records. The introduction addresses challenges we have seen in housing affordability
and availability. Housing inventory with single and multi- family numbers were updated and show a
steady increase from the first half of the past decade. The Balanced Housing ordinance most likely
influenced the trends shown with multi-family permits. A new section for Accessory Dwelling Units
describes both internal and external units. When the report was originally composed, there were not
many ADU applications, but more and more walkout basement permits are coming in, which trigger the
ADU permits. However, it is difficult to always know when    an ADU    is in place.

A rental license is required in order for an ADU to be approved. West Jordan will have to start
reporting ADU numbers next year to the state relating to moderate- income housing issues. However,
there is a large percentage that are not licensed and tracked.

There was a discussion regarding enforcement of illegal ADU' s. A 2- week courtesy notice asks the
property owner to come into compliance by obtaining a rental license. If that does not occur, then an
official notice of violation is sent with another two weeks to comply. After that, the issue proceeds to
the Administrative Law Judge.


Mark Forsythe said survey results from 2021 relevant to housing and multi- family, density, small lot
subdivisions, and preferred type of housing were added to this section. There were no questions related
to housing affordability on the survey, but respondents were asked to rank priorities from which
affordable housing came in at 3 or 4.

McKenna Marchant said at the time of the survey her focus was on increased home values, but now that
she has grown in her profession and experience, and based on the current market, more people are
focused on housing affordability. She felt that prioritizing multi-family and small lot subdivisions is
important in an effort to provide affordable housing.

Mark Forsythe said home prices grew steadily in the decade but then began an upward trend in 2015
when home prices nearly doubled by 2020. They also looked at trends by zip code. The east side had
the highest price increases, but the lowest starting prices. The west side had the lowest rise in costs but
