City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1696

On December 16, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1696 amending
the text of Titles 9, 10, 17, and 18 of the Draper City Municipal Code related to the
Geologic Hazards Ordinance. Known as the City Initiated Geologic Hazards Ordinance
Text Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office
and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
