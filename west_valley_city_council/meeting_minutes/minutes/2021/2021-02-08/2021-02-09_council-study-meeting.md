       Mayor Bigelow thanked staff and the consultant team for all the information and
       work that has been done in this process. He stated that he feels objectives have been
       met and is satisfied with the results. Councilmember Buhler stated that this study
       was conceived over a year ago to minimize impacts to neighborhoods. He indicated
       that he has confidence that UDOT will increase mobility and speed on Bangerter
       Highway but his concern is more the impact that residents and commercial
       properties will feel. Councilmember Buhler stated that he feels the City has wasted
       funds on this study because it will only accomplish what UDOT will do in their
       study. He stated that he feels the study did not find a balance between impact to
       resident’s and mobility on Bangerter. Mayor Bigelow indicated that a line has to be
       drawn between impact and functionality. He stated that the least amount of impact
       would be no exits or access to Bangerter but this would create no access to the City.
       Mayor Bigelow stated that balance is different in everyone’s mind and it’s difficult
       to determine what is right. Councilmember Lang asked what the next steps are.
       Russ replied that the City feels confident in talking to UDOT with the information
       it has gained. Councilmember Christensen stated that impact to residents can
       always be stressed to UDOT. Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if UDOT has a
       process that they go through in determining a balance between impacts and need.
       Ivan Hooper, Avenue Consultants, replied yes and stated that UDOT will evaluate
       a purpose and need as well as environmental impact and a community need. Mr.
       Hooper stated that the City will be able to provide valuable feedback in this process.
       Mayor Bigelow stated that UDOT will have conversations with the City but will
       ultimately decide what they want to happen that meets their own goals. He indicated
       that the study, the discussions, and the conversations the Council has had will be
       very beneficial for staff as they move forward in working on this project with
       UDOT. Councilmember Lang suggested leaving Bangerter north of 3500 South the
       same. Councilmember Buher stated he is more concerned about residents south of
       3500 South. Mayor Bigelow stated that UDOT will push for overpasses and the
       removal of lights. Russ agreed and noted that things will not be left the same.
