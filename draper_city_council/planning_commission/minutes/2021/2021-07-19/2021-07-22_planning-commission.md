1.     Public Hearing: K9 Lifeline Conditional Use Permit Request (Administrative Action)

On the request of Heather Beck, representing K9 Lifeline, for a Conditional Use Permit to
modify the existing Conditional Use Permits on 1.29 acres of property located at 986 East Draper
Parkway for a kennel use. Application USE-100-2021. Staff contact: Jennifer Jastremsky,
801.576.6328, jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, provided an overview of the item indicating that K9
Lifeline desired to modify their existing conditional use permit. She indicated that the first building
on their site received the conditional use permit on June 23, 2011, and the second building on May
9, 2019. She noted that denial of tonight’s application would not invalidate the existing permits.
She pointed out that the property was located near the frontrunner line and within the town center
land use designation and zoning designation. She explained that the proposed permit looked at
reorienting the onsite outdoor dog potty area and parking. She shared the size of the outdoor areas
and that the outdoor areas were fenced with six-foot vinyl and chain-link fencing, any feces are
immediately removed, and the outdoor area was sprayed down once a day. Because parking had
been removed from the property, she remarked that they have recommend a condition of approval
to cap the number of dogs allowed at the site to match the reduction in parking to 34 on-site parking
spaces.

Commissioner Susan Nixon asked if they were required to have ADA parking stalls.

Commissioner Nixon inquired if the dumpsters needed to be enclosed.

building.

2.     Public Hearing: TriView Apartments Phase II Site Plan Request (Administrative
       Action)

On the request of Cara Hicks, representing Triago Ventures, for a Site Plan on 8.27 acres of
property located in the CR Zone, approximately at 12308 South Pony Express Road. The Site
Plan is for three apartment and mixed-use buildings. Application SPR-022-2021. Staff contact:
Jennifer Jastremsky, 801.576.6328, jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

Ms. Jastremsky presented an overview of the item noting that it was within the community
commercial land use designation and the regional commercial zoning designation. She noted there
was a development agreement that allowed for several mixed-use buildings and up to 835 dwelling
units and required up to 75,000 square feet of commercial space. She pointed out that phase one
of the project was currently under construction. She explained that the proposal was for three
buildings. Building A was a mixed-use building that was six stories tall consisting of both
commercial and residential units. Building B was also six stories with both commercial and
residential use. Building C was mixed use with commercial and residential some of which would
be short term rental spaces with amenities.

Ms. Jastremsky remarked that parking on all three buildings was at the ground floor with the units
built above. She stated that the proposed access on Lone Peak Parkway would be limited to a right-
turn in and out and a left turn in only. If the traffic design fails it would have to be modified to a
right-turn in only into the development. She announced that the property complied with city code
including landscaping standards, and material standards. She noted the proposal complied with the
development agreement and exceeded the parking requirements. She explained that there were a
few different third parties that the applicant needed to finalize approvals with as well as continued
coordination with the City for the design of the road. She noted that the recommended motion was
very specific and contained two types of conditions some that had to be met prior to the effective
date of the approval and general conditions that had to meet during the general process of
developing the property.

Commissioner Van Hoff asked if there was enough parking around each individual building.

must be brought back to the Planning Commission for additional review and approval.
