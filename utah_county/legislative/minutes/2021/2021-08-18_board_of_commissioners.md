29. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE SIGNING OF AMENDMENT 2 TO 2020-10 WITH

UTAH DEPARTENT OF HEALTH FOR THE ASTHMA HOME VISITING PROGRAM.
-Julie Dey, Health

COMMISSIONER GARDNER: CAN YOU TELL ME A LITTLE BIT ABOUT THIS
PROGRAM?

ERIC EDWARDS, UTAH COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT DIRECTOR - WE WERE
ONE OF THE FIRST COUNTIES IN THE STATE TO HAVE THIS PROGRAM. YEARS AGO
TO START, WE WERE GIVEN SIG MONEY TO DO ASTHMA PREVENTION AND
CONTROL. ASTHMA IS A CONCERN ALL OVER THIS NATION. OFTEN THERE ARE
TRIGGERS IN THE HOME THAT PARENTS DON’T EVEN KNOW ABOUT. ONE OF THE
LEADING CAUSES OF VISITS TO EMERGENCY ROOMS ARE BREATHING
EMERGENCIES FOR CHILDREN AND MOST OF THOSE COULD HAVE BEEN
PREVENTED HAD THE PARENT KNOWN OF SIMPLE THINGS THAT THEY COULD
DO TO REMOVE THOSE TRIGGERS SUCH AS MOLD, PET DANDER, ETC. THE STATE
ASTHMA PROGRAM IS FANTASTIC AT THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH. WE
HAVE CONSISTENTLY HAD AN ASTHMA CONTRACT - PROBABLY LONGER THAN
ANY HEALTH DISTRICT IN THE STATE OF UTAH. DURING COVID, WE WERE NO
LONGER ABLE TO DO HOME VISITS AND THROUGH OUR HEALTH EDUCATORS WE
FIGURED OUT HOW TO DO HOME VISITS VIRTUALLY. THIS PROGRAM WORKS
WITH PHYSICIANS, HUD, AFFORDABLE HOUSING, HEAT, ETC.

BEN VAN NOY, UTAH COUNTY ATTORNEY, THE PERSON WANTING TO BE IN THE
PROGRAM IF WORKING THROUGH A REFERRAL, PER HIPPA REGULATIONS, HAS
TO OPT INTO THE PROGRAM.

ERIC EDWARDS - ALL OF THIS FUNDING IS CDC TO THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF
HEALTH. THEY TAKE THE LARGER PORTION OF THAT AND THEY DIVVY IT UP TO
THOSE HEALTH DISTRICTS THAT APPLY FOR IT AND WE’VE BEEN AWARDED
FUNDING ON A REPETITIVE 3 OR 5-YEAR CYCLE. IT IS AGRANT THAT WE APPLY
FOR.
