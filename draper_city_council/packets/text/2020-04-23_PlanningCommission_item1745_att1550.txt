BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of an Amended Site Plan for approximately 3.07 acres located
on the north side of Pioneer Rd, at approximately 1393 East Pioneer Rd. An aerial of the property can be
found as Exhibit B of this report. The applicant is requesting that an Amended Site Plan be approved to
allow for the modification of the sidewalk and parkstrip configuration.

The Harmony Hills Assisted Living facility obtained Site Plan approval from the Planning Commission
on October 13, 2016. The project is currently under construction.

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential High Density
land use designation for the subject property. This category is characterized as follows:

The property has been assigned the IC zoning classification. The purpose of the IC zone is to “provide a
residential environment within Draper City for institutional care developments such as Alzheimer’s
facilities, Assisted living facilities, Congregate housing facilities, Hospices, Medical services, limited,
Nursing care facilities, Transitional care development, or other similar land uses that are constructed and
used primarily for long term or permanent residence by the elderly and persons with a disability.” The
land use map and zoning map are included as Exhibit C and Exhibit D of this report, respectfully.

The property abuts the TC (Town Center) zone to the west, the CC (Community Commercial) zone to the
north, and the RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot minimum lot) to the east. To the south
are the RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot minimum lot) and R3 (Single-family
Residential, 13,000 square foot lot minimum) zones.

Site Plan Layout. During construction of the assisted living facility, the applicant has struggled to create a
driveway that complies with the 12% maximum grade allowed by the Draper City Municipal Code
(DCMC) Section 9-27-160. As a way to help resolve the issue, the applicant is proposing a modification
to the sidewalk and parkstrip configuration along Pioneer Road. This modification would push the
sidewalk 5-feet into the right-of-way, allowing for an additional 5-foot that can be utilized in the slope for
the driveway. The changes will not impact asphalt, curb, or gutter design, and will not affect drivable
surfaces along Pioneer Road.

          E. Standards For Approval: The following standards shall apply to the approval of a site plan:

                approved.

                materials.

             4. The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject
                development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
             5. The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7,
                8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities
                concerns.
