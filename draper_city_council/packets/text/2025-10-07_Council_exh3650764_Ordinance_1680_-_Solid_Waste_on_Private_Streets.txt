                                  ORDINANCE 1680

 AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO DRAPER CITY CODE SECTIONS 7-
   11-010 AND 7-11-030 REGARDING SOLID WASTE AND RECYCLABLE MATERIAL
           COLLECTION SERVICES ON PRIVATE STREETS IN DRAPER CITY

       WHEREAS, Draper City provides solid waste and recyclable material collection
services (“Collection Services”) to promote public health, safety, and welfare; and

      WHEREAS, providing such services on private streets increases liability and
maintenance costs due to the city’s lack of control over private road conditions; and

        WHEREAS, Draper City seeks to allocate limited resources efficiently by
amending Draper City Code Section 7-11-10 (Definitions) and Section 7-11-030 to
clarify the City will not directly provide Collection Services to residences on private
streets but will contract with a qualified third-party provider to provide Collection
Services to residences currently serviced by the City; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed amendments would allow residents on private
streets currently serviced by the City to either use a city-contracted third-party
provider or contract independently for solid waste and recyclable material collection
services;

      WHEREAS, the City Council finds that amending Draper City Municipal Code
Sections 7-11-010 and 7-11-030 is in the best interest of Draper City and its residents
to ensure cost-effective, safe, and reliable waste management services;

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER AS
FOLLOWS:

     Section 1. Approval of Code Amendment. The amendment to Draper City
Code Sections 7-11-010 and 7-11-030, attached hereto as Exhibit A, is hereby
approved and adopted as presented.

      Section 2. De Minimus Changes. The City Manager is authorized to correct any
punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimus errors or omissions.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the
date of final passage.
