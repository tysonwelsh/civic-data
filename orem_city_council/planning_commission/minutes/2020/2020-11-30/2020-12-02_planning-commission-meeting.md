Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Matt Hawkins for the City to approve the conditional use permit for an accessory
structure, basketball court and garage, located at 1391 South 1140 East in the PD18 zone.

Staff Presentation​: The applicant is adding an accessory structure containing a basketball court and garage. The lot
contains a total area of 63,701 square feet or 1.46 acres. The accessory building contains a total building area of
1,735 square feet.

The ordinance requires a conditional use permit for accessory structures in the PD18 zone which are 1,000 square
feet or greater. Accessory structures cannot be more than 10% of the area of the parcel. This structure is 3 percent of
the lot at 1,735 square feet. The structure is also within the height requirement of the ordinance (which is 35 feet for
accessory buildings/structures) at 31 feet 2-7/16 inches.

The setback requirements in the PD18 zone for accessory buildings are:
    ● Front – Thirty (30) feet from back of curb.
    ● Rear and Side – Five (5) feet from the property lines.

Proposed garage/basketball court:
    ● Front - One hundred twenty-nine feet seven inches (129’7”) from the back of curb
    ● South side - Ten feet six inches (10’6”)
    ● North side - Eight feet five inches (8’5”) from the house (it is required to be a minimum of six (6) feet from
        the house)
    ● Rear - One hundred one feet (101’)

It is well within the required setbacks.

The proposed elevations consist of stone and other finishes matching the existing residence. The proposed
landscaping and overall plan comply with the requirements of the PD18 zone.

The applicant will also build a pool house and swimming pool. Because the pool house is only 597 square feet, it
does not require a conditional use permit in the PD18 zone. The swimming pool also does not require a conditional
use permit. They will, however, need to meet the zoning requirements as part of the building permit process.

Recommendation​: ​Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City
Council to approve the conditional use permit for an accessory structure, basketball court and garage, located at
1391 South 1140 East in the PD18 zone.

There was no discussion on the item.

Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce ​Agenda Item 3.3​:

Agenda Item 3.3 is a request by Gene Robison for the City to amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the
City of Orem by changing the zone on approximately 1.96 acres from the R20 zone to the R12 zone at 925 South
Carterville Road.

Staff Presentation​: The existing property is approximately 85,231 square feet (1.96 acres). It currently has two
lots, both owned by the applicant.

The applicant would like to divide the two lots into three lots to facilitate the development of two lots along
Carterville Road. The existing home will remain on Lot 1 which will have 54,851 square feet (1.26 acres). Lot 2 will
have 18,177 square feet and Lot 3 will have 16,853 square feet.

The current R20 zone requires a lot size minimum of 20,000 square feet meaning that the applicant’s proposed lots
will not meet current zoning requirements. The proposed R12 zone would allow for the proposed two lots up front
on Carterville Road and their sizes would meet the minimum size requirement of 12,000 square feet. The General
Plan designation of Low Density Residential (LDR) supports the rezone to R12.

A neighborhood meeting was held and several neighbors were in attendance; however, no one had any objections to
the rezone.

Recommendation: . Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City
Council to amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone on approximately
1.96 acres from the R20 zone to the R12 zone at 925 South Carterville Road.

There was discussion on the item. Some of the key issues that were discussed were:
    ● It was noted by the applicant that even though the surrounding lots are currently zoned R20, they were
        previously zoned R12. After some discussion, staff confirmed it and noted that the general plan designation
        of LDR (Low Density Residential) allows for R12 zones.
    ● Mr. Staker asked what the sidewalk requirements were in this area? Staff explained that Carterville Road is
        the only road in the City designated as a rural road. As such, no sidewalk is required here.
    ● The northern access shown on the concept plan would remain in order for the applicant to maintain access
        to his barn.

    ●   The applicant explained that the current access off of Carterville Road is seldom used, and would therefore
