Work Session Items

2. 22-106 Council Emergency Management Training: G 0402 NIMS Overview for
Senior Officials
Council Chair Houseman introduced this evening's training: ICS Overview for
Executives/Senior Officials.
Amy DeNeff, Sandy City Emergency Manager, introduced Clint Mecham, with Unified
Fire who led this evening's training. He provided an overview of the Incident Command
System (ICS) which is a scalable, integrated organizational structure which responds to
and matches the complexities and demands of single or multiple incidents without being
hindered by jurisdictional boundaries.
Council convened a short recess at 6:43 pm.
Council reconvened at 6:55 pm and continued the training.
Council and Staff thanked Mr. Mecham for this evening's training and presentation.
Council moved to Standing Reports.

City Council Meeting Minutes April 12, 2022
Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report
Dustin Fratto, Executive Director, updated the Council on upcoming agenda items and
the redistricting process.
Council Member Business
Council Member Brooke D'Sousa thanked the Public Works department for the recent
tour.
Mayor's Report
Mayor Monica Zoltanski spoke about a grant recently received, a Child Care Stabilization
grant.
CAO Report
Cliff Strachan, CAO, spoke about the data switch issue experienced by the City
yesterday and how the IT department resolved the issue. The first movie night is
scheduled for June 10th at Rio Tinto Stadium. The Fire Department Town Hall event is on
April 25th. Our employee luncheon is on April 27th. The police department has a new
canine officer.
Adjournment
Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 8:07 pm.
