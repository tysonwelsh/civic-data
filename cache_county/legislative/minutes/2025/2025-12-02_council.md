     C. County Recreation Center Study- Whitney Ward, VCBO Architecture 55:40 Whitney Ward presented the study findings of
         the recreation center with fact for the need of the center. Nathan Leavett talked about options of districts to strategize
         plans and implement the project. Councilmember Keegan Garrity asked Wendy what her response to comments that
         rec centers are a money drain were and if she knew of examples the county was managed by a district. Wendy
         responded the answers in the survey for desire of close access to the center might put some challenges.
         Councilmember Keegan Garrity asked about staffing needs and costs. Wendy and Nathan answered those details would
         come in the next step of the study. 1:12:20 Vice Chair Kathryn Beus asked what the next steps were. Whitney explained
         that would depend on the decision for district or county oversight. Councilmember Nolan Gunnell asked what amount
         the taxpayer would be willing to pay. Whitney answered $15/month. Council discussed. Executive Daines asked for
         costs to be broken out, and examples with data of other successful rec centers the percentage of actual public use.
         Andrew Erickson asked how the formation of decision makers would be decided. Whitney answered cities elect their
         board members. Council and Whitney discussed the formation structure. Councilmember Barbara Tidwell asked why if
         the recreation center in Logan city failed why this would be different. Whitney answered the Logan City recreation
         center was in the high school and there was a conflict of use. Councilmember Barbara Tidwell brought up the cost for
         land purchase. Councilmember David Erickson brought up what answering no would look like. Whitney responded the
         response from the survey wanted public recreation. 1:29:03 Councilmember Keegan Garrity said this issue was following
         process of voters choice and market demand. Vice Chair Kathryn Beus clarified how the districts would be formed.
         Chair Sandi Goodlander added it would be up to the voters to decide.
