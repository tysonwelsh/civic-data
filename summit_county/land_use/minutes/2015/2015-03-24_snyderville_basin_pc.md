3. Public hearing and possible action regarding a plat amendment to realign Oakridge 
Drive; 1682 East Oakridge Drive; Parcel SL-A-31 – Ray Milliner, Senior Planner 
 
Planner Milliner presented the staff report and explained that this is a proposal to realign 
the County road on Lots 30, 31, and 33 of Silver Creek Plat A.  He presented a map 
showing the property lines and the existing road running through the three lots.  The road 
was originally platted along the property lines, but the existing road cuts through these 
three properties.  The purpose of the plat amendment is to be consistent with the road as it 
currently exists, and a portion of the remainder parcel will be added into Lot 31.  Two 
plats will be recorded, one for realignment of the right-of-way, and another for the 
property that will be absorbed into Lot 31.  Staff recommended that the Planning 
Commission approve the plat amendment with the findings of fact, conclusions of law, 
and conditions of approval shown in the staff report.  
 
Commissioner Barnes asked if there is a reason the road was not cut along the property 
line as it was originally platted.  Planner Milliner replied that the applicant has stated that 
there was a fire about 40 years ago, and perhaps this is where a fire break was cut for that 
fire.  
 
Commissioner Klingenstein asked who will correct this.  Planner Milliner clarified that 
the road exists, and they are amending the plat to show what exists on the ground.

Commissioner Lawson asked about the other remainder parcel that will not be absorbed 
into Lot 1.  Planner Milliner explained that the owner of Lot 17 was not involved in this 
plat amendment, so the owner of Lot 30 will continue to maintain that small remainder 
piece on the other side of the road.  Commissioner Lawson asked how the property 
would be transferred to Lot 31.  Deputy County Attorney Jami Brackin explained that 
will be done through quit claim deeds.  She verified that the road correction must be done 
through the plat amendment process.   
 
Chair DeFord asked why they cannot get the remainder added to Lot 17 while they are 
fixing everything else.  Planner Milliner stated that he understands the owner of Lot 17 
did not want to participate in the plat amendment.  Jensen Poor, owner of Lot 31, stated 
that the owner of Lot 17 is aware of the plat amendment and has no issues with it.  Mr. 
Poor stated that he is purchasing the remainder parcel from the Kelsch family, but the 
owner of Lot 17 did not seem to care, as his house already exists.  He explained that the 
utilities are in the road, so it did not make sense to push the road back to the platted right-
of-way, and all the neighbors felt they should leave the road in place.  Ms. Brackin 
confirmed that this is the easiest way to resolve this situation.

Findings of Fact: 
1. The applicant is the owner of Lot 31 of the Silver Creek Estates Unit 
A subdivision. 
2. The owners of Lots 33 and 30 of the Silver Creek Estates Unit A 
subdivision have consented to this application. 
3. The lots are located within the Hillside Stewardship (HS) zone. 
4. The applicant is requesting a plat amendment and right-of-way 
realignment. 
5. The existing Oakridge Road was built outside of the platted right-of-
way (ROW). 
6. The right-of-way realignment would make the Oakridge Road platted 
right-of-way consistent with the existing built Oakridge Road right-of-
way. 
7. The plat amendment would convey land on the east side of the right-
of-way from Lots 33 and 30 to Lot 31. 
8. There is no evidence that the proposed plat amendment will 
materially harm the public or any individual person. 
9. Staff’s review of the proposal; including review from applicable 
service providers, did not raise any issues or concerns that would 
warrant special conditions of approval or a denial of the application. 
10. Access to each lot is from existing Oakridge Road. 
11. There will be no increase in density as a result of this plat amendment. 
12. No changes to the access are proposed as part of this application. 
13. All necessary public facilities are available on site, including water, 
septic, and roads. 
Conclusions of Law: 
1. The proposed Plat Amendment as conditioned complies with all 
requirements of the Development Code.
