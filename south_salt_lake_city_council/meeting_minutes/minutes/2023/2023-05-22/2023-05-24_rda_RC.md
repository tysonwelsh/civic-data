Unfinished Business

    1. A Resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of South Salt Lake Authorizing the Dissolution of
       the Granite High Community Development Project Area.
       Community & Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, gave a brief overview
       of this item that has been discussed at recent RDA meetings. There is also a draft Ordinance for
       the City Council to consider for the same item. The dissolution is for a 2012 project pursuing a
       redevelopment project for the Granite High property. The RDA created a CDA, but the project
       never came to fruition. The project has remained on the books with the County and so he is now
       asking the RDA Board to dissolve it.

        Director Siwik made a motion to approve the RDA Resolution.

MOTION: Shane Siwik
SECOND: Corey Thomas

    2. A Resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of South Salt Lake Authorizing the Loan of Project
       Area Funds from the Market Station Project Area to the 3900 South Community Development
       Area.
       Community & Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, gave a brief overview
       of this item that has been discussed at recent RDA meetings. This request is for a project loan
       from the Market Station Project Area to the River Front CDA. It was believed that the increment
       would start being given to the developer this year but has not been triggered yet. Mr.
       Weidenhamer explained that a minor administrative adjustment is needed to fix this. With the
       help of the Finance Director and the Mayor they found that it would be easy to borrow money
       from the Market Station CDA and loan it for the payment of the 3900 South CDA.

        Director Siwik made a motion to approve the RDA Resolution.

MOTION: Shane Siwik
SECOND: Natalie Pinkney
