9. Consideration of Ordinance #19-2021, approval of the Lakeview Land and Rock General
   Plan Amendment at approximately 600 East 600 South, changing the designation from
   LDR (low density residential) to LI (light industrial) on approximately 12.81 acres.
   Cole Peck was representing the applicant. He stated that they would like to annex this property
   and use it as a place to park their trucks and equipment. He stated that they want to be good
   neighbors and are willing to install a fence and create a buffer to mitigate some concerns. He
   noted that even low density would put more demand on traffic, water, and sewer. He said that
   they are here for suggestions also and hopes to find a good compromise. He noted that they
   may allow other companies to park their equipment there.

    Mayor Johnson asked for public comments.

    Wade Lott stated that he was in favor of the amendment because there would be less traffic.

    Melissa Peck was in favor of the amendment because there would be less traffic compared to
    a residential development. She stated that it’s important for this type of business to stay in
    Lehi.

    Jeremy Thomas was in favor of the amendment and stated that this type of use was needed in
    the City.

    Lance Moore was against the amendment and was concerned what other types of businesses
    might use the property. He would prefer residential.

    Leslie Cuppet was against the amendment and stated that a re-zone would open the door to a
    lot of other possible businesses that would be concerning.

    Darrell Forman was in favor of the amendment and stated that it was not detrimental.

    Stephanie Moore was against the amendment due to safety concerns.

    Grace Beck stated that she would prefer residential

    Ms. Nelson was in favor of the amendment.

    Kathy Maquinna was against the amendment and was concerned about what other types of
    businesses could come in under light industrial.

    Scott Gerber was in support of the amendment. He stated that this was a good location for this
    type of business.

    Benjamin Lee was opposed to the amendment.

    Clay Peck, the applicant, stated that they have property rights and should be able to use their
    property, but they also want to be good neighbors and hopes to work through concerns.

    Meagan Butler was in favor of light industrial.

    Brook Lee was against the amendment and was concerned with vehicles traveling in and out
    of the complex. She was also concerned about the potential of other businesses allowed in the
    light industrial zone.

    Liz Anderson was in favor of the amendment because it is needed.

    Mayor Johnson closed the comments from the public.

    Councilor Koivisto stated that she doesn’t feel that this merits an amendment. There was
    discussion regarding concerns about the other types of uses that would be allowed in the light
    industrial zone and how a different zone might be more appropriate for the type of use they are
    proposing. Mayor Johnson also suggested that a development agreement could be applied to
    this situation.

    Motion:     Councilor Koivisto moved to deny this item. Councilor Albrecht seconded the
                motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Southwick, Yes;
    and Councilor Koivisto, Yes. The motion passed Unanimously.

    Mr. Struthers explained that the applicant could apply for a different zone or submit a different
    proposal.
