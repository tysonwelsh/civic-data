Unfinished Business

   1. Budget Discussion Regarding Proposed 2024-2025 Redevelopment Agency Budget
      Community & Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, reviewed and
      answered the questions that were submitted by the RDA Directors after the RDA’s budget
      presentation at the last meeting.

              a. What is legislative consulting? (Line item 71-70-310-02 in the RDA Admin Fund) The
                 annual contract with political lobbyist, Capstone Strategies.
                                                    1
City of South Salt Lake - Redevelopment Agency Meeting                          May 22, 2024


              b. What is the economic development contract? (Line item 71-70-310-03 in the RDA Admin
                 Fund) The annual contract with Rob Sant, consultant and son of former Economic
                 Development Director, Randy Sant. He worked closely with his father on almost every
                 project and provides continuity due to his knowledge of past projects and remains a
                 primary contributor on current projects like the HTRZ, 2280 South State Street-Blaser
                 Ventures, and Central 15.

              c. What is project professional/technical? (Line item 71-70-310-05 in the RDA Admin Fund)
                 An allowance for the consulting work, studies, and professional services that the RDA
                 needs to implement the City’s redevelopment and economic goals. Examples from the
                 past include: the HTRZ application, real estate broker/consulting services, financial
                 advisor, Bond Council, Real Estate legal support, environmental legal support.
