                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 26-16

     AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN TO HIGH DENSITY
  RESIDENTIAL AND REZONING APPROXIMATELY 3.14 ACRES LOCATED AT
  13172 SOUTH REDWOOD ROAD FROM RR-22 AND C-NTO RM-14, MAJESTIC
                       HOMES- APPLICANT.

  WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
   recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment;
and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City General Plan to High Density Residential and amending the
Zoning Map to RM-14 for the approximately 3.14-acre parcel located at 13172 South Redwood
Road.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
            Section 1.     The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is,
                           amended to reflect the described changes as shown in
                           Exhibit “A”

              Section 2.             This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 16th day of
June 2026 by the following vote:
