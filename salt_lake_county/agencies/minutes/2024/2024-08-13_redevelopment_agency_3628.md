PARK URBAN RENEWAL PROJECT AREA
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County (the “RDA”) 
and the Magna Water District (the “District”) are “public agencies” as 
defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, UTAH CODE §§11-13-101 
TO -608, and as such, are authorized to enter into agreements to act jointly 
and cooperatively in a manner that will enable them to make the most efficient 
use of their resources and powers; 
WHEREAS, on June 16, 2009, the RDA adopted a project area plan known 
as the Magna /Arbor Park Project Area Urban Renewal Plan.  The plan 
contemplated the use of tax increment to help finance the development of the 
project area including, among other things, the installation of public 
infrastructure such as roads, curb, gutter and sidewalk, water lines, sewer 
lines and storm water facilities. 
WHEREAS, on October 9, 2012, the RDA entered into a tax increment 
reimbursement agreement with Arbor Park Associates, L .C. ( the 
“Developer”), whereby the RDA agreed to reimburse the Developer for 
certain of its costs in developing the project area.  The RDA and the 
Developer also agreed to share the costs, up to $ 294,030, to reimburse the 
District for replacing 1,725 linear feet of water lines in the project area.
WHEREAS,  the District is the local authority for supplying water and 
wastewater treatment in the project area. Accordingly, the District has 
installed the necessary water lines, a portion of which costs shall be borne by 
the RDA and the Developer as set forth in the tax increment reimbursement 
agreement.
WHEREAS,  the Parties now desire to provide for the payment to the 
District for the water line replacement costs as set forth herein.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE Board of 
Directors of the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County:
