ORDINANCE NO. 2024-08

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH, AMENDING
BLUFFDALE CITY CODE 11.100.010.080 IN THE LIGHT INDUSTRIAL ZONE (1-1) TO AMEND THE
BUILDING HEIGHT FROM THIRTY-FIVE FEET (35’) TO FORTY-FIVE FEET (45’) AND OTHER
APPLICABLE LANGUAGE AMENDMENTS

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-9a-102 grants the Bluffdale City Council (the “City Council”) authority
to enact or amend ordinances that it considers necessary or appropriate for the use and
development of land in the City of Bluffdale (the “City”); and

WHEREAS, the text amendment modifies the Light Industrial Zone (|-1) chapter of the Bluffdale
City Code 11.100.010.080 regarding the building height and other language is consistent with
adjacent commercial zones; and

WHEREAS, the Bluffdale Planning Commission has held a public hearing and provided a
recommendation to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing and reviewed the subject text amendments
and finds the text amendments are consistent with the Bluffdale City General Plan to provide
flexibility for economic and commercial development.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH:

Section 1. Text Amendments, The City Council hereby amends BCC 11.100.010.080 as
indicated in Exhibit A hereto.

SECTION 1. Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid
or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this
Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

SECTION 2. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting as required by law.
