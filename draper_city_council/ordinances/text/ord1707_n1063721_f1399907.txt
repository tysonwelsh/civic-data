Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1707

          On March 3, 2026, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1707
          Amending the official Zoning Map of Draper City by removing from the
          map approximately 0.25 acres of property located at approximately 16199
          South 3300 East Within Draper City, known as the City initiated Highland
          Boundary Adjustment Zoning Map Amendment. The complete ordinance
          is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at
          https://www.draperutah.gov/
