68   3.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Mark Skousen’s request for review of the JD XIII
69   zone change on 1.01-acres located at approximately 1070 West State Street changing the
70   zoning from Mixed Use to C-H (Heavy Commercial).
71
72   Commissioner Kunze had a question about the parcel to the south and if it was an existing resident.
73   The staff confirmed that it is a house, and it would be right next to them. The area is zoned mixed
74   use but planned to change to commercial.
75
76   The applicant owns all the parcels that are subject to the zone change tonight.
77
78   Staff and Commissioners discussed the buffer and other concerns about changing the use of the
79   homes to the south that may be negatively impacted.
80
81   Josh Moffitt was there on behalf of the applicant and was available for questions.
82
83   Chairperson Jackson opened the public hearing.
84   Chairperson Jackson closed the public hearing.
85
86   There was discussion about a 4th smaller parcel that had been left off and if this was its own parcel
87   and why it was left off. It seems that there was no main reason for it being left off, it was just a
88   small parcel. The commissioners decided to include the small parcel in the zone change to not
89   create any islands or inconsistencies in the area.
90
91       Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to send a positive recommendation for Mark Skousen’s
92       request for review of the JD XIII zone change on 1.01-acres located at approximately 1070
93       West State Street changing the zoning from Mixed Use to C-H (Heavy Commercial). Parcel
94       120018-19 will also be included in this zone change to not create any islands in zoning;
95       including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in the staff report. The motion was
96       seconded by Commissioner Kunze.
