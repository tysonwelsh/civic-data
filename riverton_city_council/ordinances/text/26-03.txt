                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 26- 03


 AN ORDINANCE GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT AND REZONE OF A PORTION
 OF THE APPROXIMATELY 0. 97- ACRE LOCATED AT 1980 WEST 13400 SOUTH.
    CHANGING THE GENERAL PLAN DESIGNATION TO MEDIUM DENSITY
   RESIDENTIAL AND THE ZONING TO R-45 A SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL
ZONE WITH UP TO 4 DWELLING UNITS PER ACRE. APPLICANT- DALLIS QUINN.

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment;
and,


        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map to Medium Density Residential
and the Riverton City Zoning Map to R-4 for a portion of the approximately . 97- acre parcel
located at approximately 1980 West 13400 South.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
               Section 1.              The Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map and Zoning
                                       Map shall be, and hereby is, amended to reflect the
                                       described   changes as shown in Exhibit " A"


               Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.
