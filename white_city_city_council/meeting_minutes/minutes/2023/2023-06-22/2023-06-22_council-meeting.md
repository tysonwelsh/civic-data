Community Development Block Grant

             The Council reviewed the following Resolution entering into an interlocal
agreement with Salt Lake County relating to the Community Development Block Grant Program,
Emergency Solutions Grant Program, and the Home Investment Partnership Program:

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-06-02                                              Date: June 22, 2023

      A RESOLUTION OF WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
      AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT
      BETWEEN WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP AND SALT LAKE COUNTY
      RELATING TO THE CONDUCT OF THE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK
      GRANT PROGRAM, EMERGENCY SOLUTIONS GRANT PROGRAM AND THE
      HOME INVESTMENT PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Municipality pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, White City is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code
Ann. § 10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor, and

        WHEREAS, on May 25, 2023 the White City Council made a motion to approve the
Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between White City Metro Township and Salt Lake County
relating to the Community Development Block Grant Program, Emergency Solutions Grant
Program, and the Home Investment Partnership Program; and



                                            1
DATE           THURSDAY                             JUNE                               22, 2023

       WHEREAS, the Department of Housing and Urban Development (“HUD”) has informed
the White City Metro Township that a resolution authorizing the Mayor to sign the agreement is
required; and

       WHEREAS, the White City Council finds it is in the best interest of the citizens of White
City Metro Township to enter into an Interlocal Agreement between White City Metro Township
and Salt Lake County relating to the conduct of the Community Development Block Grant
Program, Emergency Solutions Grant Program, and the Home Investment Partnership Program,

       NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, by the White City Metro Township Council, White
City Utah:

        SECTION 1. The White City Metro Township Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to
enter into an Interlocal Agreement between White City Metro Township and Salt Lake County
relating to the conduct of the Community Development Block Grant Program, Emergency
Solutions Grant Program and the Home Investment Partnership Program as set forth in
Attachment A.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the White City Metro Township Council, in White City,
Salt Lake County, Utah this 22nd day of June 2023.

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Municipality pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Ordinance 17-06-02, Planning Commission Members are
appointed by the Metro Township Council to serve three-year terms;

      Printing/Publications/Advertising

               Mayor Flint stated the printing, publications, and advertising line item should be
increased to $5,000 to fund one mailer per quarter. The mailers are very beneficial in notifying
residents of pertinent information.

    Office expenses and supplies
    Attorney – civil

              Rori Andreason recommended increasing the budget to $6,000 to cover
conferences for the Utah League of Cities and Towns and Utah Association of Special Districts.

    Web page development/maintenance

               Ms. Andreason stated there are problems with White City’s website. She
recommends putting out an RFP for a website developer. She recommended increasing the line
item budget to $13,000.

    Software/Streaming
    Payroll processing fees
    Communications

              Mr. Ashton stated the communications line item funds the newsletter.

              Mayor Flint recommended increasing the line item budget to $4,000.

    Contributions/Special Events
    Credit Card and bank expenses
    Insurance

              Mayor Flint recommended increasing the line item budget to $10,000.

              Mr. Ashton suggested increasing the line item budget to $15,000.

    Workers comp insurance
    Postage

               Council Member Shelton stated nearly $2,000 was spent on postage.

               Mr. Ashton suggested increasing the line item budget to $2,000.

    Professional and technical

              Mr. Sanderson stated this line item funds the administrator, lobbyist, and
financial manager.

             Mayor Flint stated she has asked Ms. Andreason to take over the White City
Metro Township website and would like to increase her salary.

                Mr. Ashton suggested raising Mr. Andreason’s salary to $4,000 a month and the
lobbyist’s to $10,000.

             Mayor Flint suggested increasing the lobbyist’s line item to $20,000 in the event
of an emergency.

month.

    UFA emergency services

               Council Member Perry suggested increasing the line item budget to $13,000.

    SL (Client) county support

               Mr. Ashton suggested leaving the election line item at $15,000.

    Justice court remediation

                  Mr. Ashton stated if the Council wants Ms. Andreason to negotiate for justice court
services, it will need to increase its budget. He suggests increasing the line item budget to $5,000.

    Rent

            Council Member Perry stated the Council has paid $600 more than the previous
year. How much does it cost to rent the school for meetings?

               Mayor Flint stated she does not pay anything for the rental of the school.

                Council Member Perry stated the current budgeted amount is okay if there are
no rental fees for the school.

                Mr. Ashton stated he thinks the line item budget needs to be raised to cover the
costs of additional meetings.
