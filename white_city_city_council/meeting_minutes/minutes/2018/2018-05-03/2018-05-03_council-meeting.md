Unified Fire Authority

                Clint Mecham, Emergency Manager, Unified Fire Authority (UFA), stated the UFA
is about done with its budget process and has presented the budget to the finance committee.
The entire budget, as recommended by the finance committee, will be presented to the full board
for tentative approval, with final approval in June. The UFA held testing for individuals wanting to
be captain; 29 out of 80 individuals finished and will be placed on a list that is good for two years.
As captains are needed, the UFA will pick from the list. Recruitment school will start in May; 12
individuals have signed up and will go through a 16-week training. At the end of the 16 weeks
those individuals will be probationary fire fighters. The UFA is preparing for a more active wildland
fire season.

Emergency Operations Plan and Directing

            The Council reviewed the following resolution creating the White City Metro
Township Emergency Operation Plan:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-05-01                                                           Date: May 3, 2018

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CREATION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO
       TOWNSHIP EMERGENCY OPERATION PLAN AND DIRECTS STAFF TO
       WORK WITH THE UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY, UNIFIED POLICE
       DEPARTMENT, AND GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT
       TO DEVELOP WHITE CITY'S PLAN

                                             RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township ("White City") is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated ("UCA") §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the "Council") is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Unified Police Department ("UPD") is a Law Enforcement Agency created through an
      Interlocal Cooperation Agreement pursuant to UCA §§ 11-13-202 et al that provides law
      enforcement services to White City.

   D. The Unified Fire Authority ("UFA") is a Fire Agency created through an Interlocal
      Cooperation Agreement pursuant to UCA §§ 11-13-202 et al that provides Fire and EMT
      Services to White City.


                                                  2
DATE            THURSDAY                            MAY                                 3, 2018

   E. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District ("MSD") is a Local District pursuant to
      UCA §§ l 7B-2a-1103 et seq that provides certain municipal-type services to White City,
      specifically Public Works Operations services.

   F. Salt Lake County (the "County") is a County pursuant to UCA Title 17 - Counties, and
      operates the Salt Lake County Emergency Operations Center.

   G. The Salt Lake County District Attorney's Office (the "DA") is the Attorney for the County.

   H. Prior to the incorporation of White City, the County wrote and managed the Emergency
      Operations Plan ("EOP") for White City.

   I.   On April 2, 2018 the DA's Office rendered an opinion that Salt Lake County cannot prepare
        Emergency Operation Plans for the Metro Townships.

   J. FEMA requires municipalities to have an Emergency Operations Plan to be able to receive
      disaster funding assistance during disaster events.

   K. Since White City receives its Law Enforcement, Fire/ EMT, and Public Works Operation
      Services via UPD, UFA, and the MSD, the White City EOP will require collaboration with
      these agencies to be an effective plan.

   L. White City desires to be in compliance with the FEMA directives in the event of a disaster
      to avoid adding municipal fiscal hardships to the disaster recovery needs.

                                         RESOLUTION

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council approves
and authorizes the creation of the White City Emergency Operations Plan; and,

        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the Council directs staff to coordinate the creation of the
Emergency Operations Plan with the Unified Fire Authority, Unified Police Department, and
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District to insure the EOP can provide for the necessary
effective actions necessary in the event of a disaster.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 3rd day of May, 2018.

Local Option Sales Tax

                The Council reviewed the following resolution supporting Salt Lake County’s
imposition of the .25 percent local option sales tax dedicated to transportation:

RESOLUTION NO: 18-05-02                                                          Date: May 3, 2018

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       SUPPORTING SALT LAKE COUNTY'S IMPOSITION OF THE .25 PERCENT
       LOCAL OPTION SALES TAX DEDICATED TO TRANSPORTATION PER SB 136
       (2018).

                                            RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township ("White City) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated ("UCA") §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the "Council") is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. Salt Lake County (the "County") is a county pursuant to UCA Title 17 - COUNTIES.

   D. In 2018, the State Legislature passed SB136 authorizing counties to adopt a .25 percent
      local option sales tax dedicated to transportation.

   E. On April 24, 2018 Salt Lake County imposed the .25 percent local option sales tax
      dedicated to transportation, subject to the requirement municipalities and their councils,
      representing 67 percent of the County's population, pass resolutions of support for
      imposing the tax before June 30, 2018.

   F. Given the benefits to White City for possible funding to build the Sandy Canal Trail,
      improving transit operations, and performing timely road repairs, maintenance, and
      construction, it is in the best interest of the residents of White City to pass a resolution of
      support for Salt Lake County imposing the .25 percent local option sales tax prior to the
      June 30 deadline.

                                          RESOLUTION

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council approves
this resolution of support for Salt Lake County's imposing of the .25 percent local option sales tax
dedicated to transportation.

Fiscal Procedures – Policy #4

              The Council reviewed the following resolution adopting Policy #4 – White City
Fiscal Procedures:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-05-05                                                     Date: May 3, 2018

       A RESOLUTION APPROVING AND ADOPTING POLICY #4 - WHITE CITY
       FISCAL PROCEDURES SUBJECT TO USE WHEN THE COUNCIL
       ESTABLISHES INDEPENDENT BANKING AND MONEY HANDLING FROM THE
       MSD AND SALT LAKE COUNTY

                                         RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township ("White City") is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated ("UCA") §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the "Council") is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District ("MSD") is a Local District pursuant to
      UCA §§ 17B-2a-1103 et seq that provides certain municipal-type services to White City,
      specifically code enforcement services.

   D. Salt Lake County (the "County") is a County pursuant to UCA Title 17 - COUNTIES.
                                              7

   E. Currently, all financial work for White City is being performed by the County/MSD.

   F. Based on White City's desires to better comply with UCA Title 10, Chapter 6- UNIFORM

and authorizes the use, subject to such time as White City decides to open their own bank account

Unified Police Department Interlocal Agreement

            The Council reviewed the following resolution adopting the fourth and fifth
amendments to the interlocal agreement creating the Unified Police Department:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-05-06                                                       Date: May 3, 2018

       A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING AND ADOPTING THE FOURTH AND FIFTH
       AMENDMENTS TO THE INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT

                                               8
DATE        THURSDAY                           MAY                                 3, 2018

      CREATING THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT AND AUTHORIZES THE
      MAYOR TO SIGN THE AMENDMENTS ON BEHALF OF WHITE CITY

                                       RECITALS

 A. The White City Metro Township ("White City) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
    Annotated ("UCA") §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

 B. The White City Metro Township Council (the "Council") is the municipal legislative body
    for White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

 C. The Unified Police Department ("UPD") is a Law Enforcement Agency created through an
    Interlocal Cooperation Agreement ("Interlocal Agreement") pursuant to UCA §§ 11-13-202
    et al.

 D. The Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area ("SLVLESA") is a Special Service
    District created to provide for extended police protection pursuant to UCA § 17D-1-
    201(15).

 E. The Unified Police Department was created in 2009 and began officially began operation
    on January 1, 2010.

 F. The Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area was also created in 2009 and
    immediately signed a contract with UPD to provide police services to the Service Area
    effective January 1, 2010.

 G. SLVLESA continues to contract with UPD for the provision of police services.

 H. The unincorporated township of White City was wholly contained in the service area of
    SLVLESA.

 I. On November 3, 2015, White City residents voted to incorporate as a new form of
    municipality- a Metro Township.
 J. On November 8, 2016, White City voters elected the White City Metro Township Council.

 K. On January 1, 2017, the White City Metro Township began to conduct business as an
    incorporated governmental entity.

 L. Pursuant to SLVLESA's Bylaws, White City as a newly incorporated municipality, became
    a member entity of SLVLESA.

 M. Councilmember Cody Cutler was chosen to serve by the Council as White City's first-ever
    trustee to the SLVLESA Board of Trustees.




                                           9
DATE           THURSDAY                           MAY                                      3, 2018

   N. To accommodate White City and the other Metro Township's desires for membership on
      the UPD Board of Trustees, a Third Amendment to the lnterlocal Agreement ("Third
      Amendment") was presented for approval making them voting members of UPD.

   O. On June 1, 2017, the Council approved the Third Amendment.

   P. On November 16, 2017, UPD presented a Fourth Amendment to the Interlocal Agreement
      ("Fourth Amendment") accepting the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area, Riverton
      Law Enforcement Service Area, and Millcreek City as members of UPD.

   Q. On the January 18, 2018, UPD presented a Fifth Amendment to the Interlocal Agreement
      ("Fifth Amendment") clarifying the Sheriff shall retain all executive authority in the hiring,
      firing, transfer, discipline and termination of all UPD employees.

   R. Approving the Fourth Amendment allows UPD to retain members and preserve its service
      footprint benefitting White City and the other member entities through economies of scale.

   S. Approving the Fifth Amendment makes clear the role of the Sheriff as the CEO of UPD
      and formalizes the relationship, and is consistent with the understanding White City has
      with UPD's operation and governance.

                                         RESOLUTION

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council approves
the fourth and fifth amendments to the interlocal agreement, hereinafter known as EXHIBIT A and
EXHIBIT B, and authorizes the Mayor to sign the amendments on White City's behalf.
