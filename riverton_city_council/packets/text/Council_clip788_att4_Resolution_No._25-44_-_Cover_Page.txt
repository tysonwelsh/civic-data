PRESENTER:                 Jason Lethbridge

                           Resolution No. 25-44 - Declaring the portion of the Draper Station Area Plan
ITEM:                      within Riverton City Impracticable Due to Existing Conditions - Jason
                           Lethbridge, Development Services Director

SUMMARY:

Utah State Code Section 10-9a-403.1 requires every City with property within 1/2 mile of a fixed
guideway public transit station to approve a "Station Area Plan" for that property. The Station Area
surrounding the Draper Frontrunner Station includes a small sliver of property in Riverton City adjacent
to the Jordan River on the west side. As it technically lies within the 1/2 mile Station Area, Riverton City
is required to either develop a Station Area Plan or submit a resolution to Wasatch Front Regional
Council declaring that development of the property within the City to be 'impracticable'. As the sliver of
property is within the Jordan River channel, and the adjacent property is part of the Riverbend Golf
Course with no available access, this resolution declares the property impracticable and determines that
the requirements for station area planning are satisfied. This has been prepared in consultation with
Wasatch Front Regional Council, and WFRC has reviewed this resolution and included documentation.
