7. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE COMMISSION SIGNATURE ON THIRD AMENDMENT TO
AGREEMENT NO. 2020-1089 BETWEEN UTAH COUNTY AND THE UTAH COUNTY
PUBLIC DEFENDER ASSOCIATION FOR THE PROVISION OF LEGAL DEFENSE FOR THE
YEAR 2023.
        -Zachary Zundel, Attorney

(Timestamp: 22:12)

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH – STATED THAT SALT LAKE AND WEBER COUNTIES
ARE BEGINNING TO MOVE TOWARD PARITY AND DAVIS COUNTY IS GOING TO
MOVE THIS WAY AS WELL. THE REASONING BEHIND IT IS IF WE HAVE A LOWER
PAID ATTORNEY DEFENDING SOMEONE ACCUSED OF A CERTAIN CRIME, THAT
ATTORNEY MAY NOT HAVE THE SKILL SET NEEDED SO THERE IS A NEED TO FIND
THE FUNDS NECESSARY TO INCREASE PAY IN ORDER TO RETAIN PUBLIC
DEFENDERS. ALSO STATED THAT THERE IS APPROXIMATELY $400,000 LEFT
OVER FROM THE ARPA (AMERICAN RESCUE PLAN ACT) THAT WE USED FOR
FUNDING FOR VOCA (VICTIMS OF CRIMES ACT) FOR THE CHILDREN’S JUSTICE
CENTER FOR POSITIONS FOR 1 YEAR. GIVEN THE FACT THAT THE FEDERAL
GOVERNMENT HAS NOT COME TO TERMS TO REPLENISH THE VOCA GRANT, WE
EXTENDED IT TO THE END OF 2026. SUGGESTED THAT MAYBE THERE IS A WAY
TO DO A MIXTURE WITH THE CJC (CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER) AND THE PUBLIC
DEFENDERS OFFICE FOR FUNDING THESE POSITIONS AND UPGRADING THE
PUBLIC DEFENDER’S OFFICE STAFF.

COMMISSIONER GORDON – STATED THAT HUMAN RESOURCES DOES MARKET
STUDIES TO KEEP THE COUNTY POSITIONS SALARY IN LINE WITH THE TREND
AND SUGGESTED THAT THEY DO A MORE COMPREHENSIVE STUDY WITH
COMPARABLES FOR THESE POSITIONS.

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER – STATED THAT THE INCREASE IN PAY THAT
THE ATTORNEY’S OFFICE DID CAME FROM FUNDS WITHIN THEIR BUDGET. THEY
MOVED THINGS AROUND AND REDUCED OVERHEAD, ETC. WHERE WITH THE
PUBLIC DEFENDERS OFFICE, THERE IS NO MONEY IN THE BUDGET FOR AN
INCREASE. SHE ALSO STATED THAT WEBER COUNTY’S TAX RATE IS 0.87%, DAVIS
COUNTY’S IS 0.72% AND SALT LAKE COUNTY’S IS 0.75% AND UTAH COUNTY’S IS
0.59%. IF WE WERE NEAR TO EVEN ONE OF THOSE, WE’D HAVE A 13% INCREASE

MUCH TRAINING AND EFFORT IN TO.

VERY HELPFUL. THE MANDATE FOR PUBLIC DEFENSE IF FROM THE
CONSTITUTION WHICH IS SUPPOSED TO BE FUNDED BY THE STATE, BUT UTAH
