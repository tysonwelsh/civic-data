CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2024-1249-O, AN ORDINANCE REVISING
THE ENVIRONMENTAL MITIGATION IMPACT FEE FOR THE WASHINGTON
COUNTY HABITAT CONSERVATION PLAN

Washington County Commission

Deputy Washington County Attorney Devin Snow explained that in 1990, Congress designated
the Mohave Desert Tortoise as an endangered species, which brought all development to a stop.
The Habitat Conservation Plan was created to allow development to continue and established the
Red Cliffs Desert Reserve. To fund the HCP, the County and several municipalities established an
impact fee to fund the Reserve. The purpose of this Ordinance is to update and make it uniform to
the other cities and municipalities, including establishing fees, sunset dates and periodic updates.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2024-1249-O, An
Ordinance Revising the Environmental Mitigation Impact Fee for the
Washington County Habitat Conservation Plan. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF _RESOLUTION NO. _R-2024-3252, _A RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING EXPENDITURES OF FUNDS COLLECTED AS WASHINGTON
COUNTY’S SHARE OF THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS SALES TAX

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that fifteen percent of the funds generated by
the 1/10" of one percent sales and use tax has been designated for support of cultural activities.
Washington County established a Recreation, Arts and Parks (RAP) Board to oversee the process.
There were 40 applications that were submitted for the 2024 funding year, with the RAP Board
providing recommendations to the Commission for review.

Commissioner Almquist allowed David Watson, a member of the RAP Board to speak. He thanked
the Commissioners for their support and reminded them that the RAP tax is on the ballot again this

year.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3253, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_ COMPLETE REDUCTION OF PAST PROPERTY TAXES FOR A PARCEL
LOCATED IN THE RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

Mr. Snow stated His Family Matters, LLC acquired property located in the Red Cliffs Desert
Reserve several years ago and has allowed the property to remain in the Reserve while waiting for
the federal government to acquire the land. The property owner has requested the property taxes
be reduced to zero while waiting for the acquisition to happen.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3253, A
Resolution Approving a Complete Reduction of Past Property Taxes for a
Parcel Located in the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve. Motion seconded by

Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.
