RESOLUTION NO. 149
RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BOARD OF THE 
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY TO 
EXPLORE AND NEGOTIATE A TRANSFER OF PROJECT AREAS TO 
THE COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AGENCY OF MAGNA
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County (the “RDA”) 
received a request from the Community Reinvestment Agency of Magna (the 
“CRA of Magna ”) asking for the following RDA project areas to be 
transferred to the CRA of Magna:
•  Magna/Packard Bell Redevelopment Area
•  Magna/Arbor Park Urban Renewal Area
•  Magna West Main Street Neighborhood Development Area
•  Magna Main Street Community Development Area
WHEREAS, the Magna West Main Street Neighborhood Development 
Project Area was dissolved on February 6, 2024. See RDA Board Resolution 
No. 143.
WHEREAS, the Magna /Packard Bell Redevelopment Area is inactive and is 
ready to be dissolved.
WHEREAS, a transfer of project areas from one community reinvestment 
agency to another is permitted under the circumstances described in Utah 
Code 17C-1-205.
WHEREAS, such a transfer would require an interlocal cooperation 
Page 5 of 7
Redevelopment Agency Meeting Minutes December 10, 2024
agreement between the RDA and the CRA of Magna that authorizes the RDA 
to transfer or assign to the CRA of Magna its real property, rights, 
indebtedness, obligations, tax increment, or other assets and liabilities 
resulting from the relocated project areas.
WHEREAS, the RDA wishes to explore the option of transferring its active 
project areas located in Magna City to the CRA of Magna and initiate the 
transfer process.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Board of 
Directors of the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County that:
A. The RDA ’s staff is directed to prepare for the dissolution of the 
Magna/Packard Bell Redevelopment Area;
B. The RDA ’s staff is directed to create a plan to wind down its 
management of the two active project areas located in Magna City, and to 
identify all its real property, rights, indebtedness, obligations, tax increment, 
or other assets and liabilities that would be involved in, or otherwise affected 
by, such a transfer; and
C. The RDA ’s staff is directed to begin negotiating a transfer interlocal 
cooperative agreement with the CRA of Magna.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 10th day of December, 2024.
