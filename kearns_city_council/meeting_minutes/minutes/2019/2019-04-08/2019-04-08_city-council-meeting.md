Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services Division

               Application No. 30882 – Adoption of a new chapter to the Kearns Metro Zoning
Ordinance to create two “public facilities” zones, the PF and PI zones, as zoning in which public
uses like parks, schools, recreation centers, civic buildings, churches, utility buildings, and similar
uses are allowed.

                Curtis Woodward, Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services
Division, stated this application was discussed during the March 11, 2019, Council meeting. The
Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission tabled this request at its last meeting due to
questions relating to height of buildings and lot coverage. As a result, a couple of changes have
been made to these zones. One change allowed for higher buildings if the setback was increased,
with a cap at 65 feet. The other change dealt with the 40 percent maximum lot coverage. The
adjustment took into consideration the entire campus acreage and not just the size of a lot. As a
result of these changes, the Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission is recommending
approval of these two zones.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to approve
the following ordinance. The motion passed unanimously.

The Kearns Metro Township Council hereby ordains as follows:

       Section 1.     The creation of public facilities zones is important to provide a zoning map
of Kearns that more clearly indicates land uses.

      Section 2.   Chapter 19.20 “Public Facility Zones” of the Uniform Zoning Ordinance of
Kearns Metro Township, Utah is hereby enacted as follows:

Chapter 19.20 PUBLIC FACILITY ZONES

19.20.010: PURPOSE:

Public facility zones are established to provide public or quasi-public uses.

   A. The purpose of the PF zone is to provide areas for facilities owned by public and quasi-
      public entities and which utilize relatively large areas of land. This zone is intended to
      provide immediate recognition of such areas on the official zoning map.

   B. The purpose of the PI zone is to provide areas for educational institutions, municipal uses,
      and athletic facilities, whether publicly or privately owned, that host organized team sports.
      These uses would utilize relatively large areas of land and would be established for the
      health and betterment of the community at large. This zone is intended to provide
      immediate recognition of such areas on the official zoning map.

19.20.020: SCOPE:

The provisions of this chapter shall apply to any real property located in a public facility zone as
shown on the official zoning map. No building, structure or real property shall be used, and no
building or structure shall be hereafter erected, structurally or substantially altered, or enlarged
except as set forth in this chapter. Such requirements shall not be construed to prohibit or limit
other applicable provisions of this title, this code, or other laws.

19.20.030: USES ALLOWED:

   A. Permitted And Conditional Uses: Permitted and conditional uses allowed within public
      facility zones shall be as set forth in section 19.20.070, table 19.20.1 of this chapter.
      Permitted and conditional uses are indicated by a “P” or “C”, respectively, in the
      appropriate column. Uses not permitted are indicated by “NP”. Any permitted or

SECTION III. Section 19.04 of the Uniform Zoning Ordinance of Kearns Metro Township, Utah is
hereby amended to add the following definitions:

19.04.153 Cultural Service

“Cultural Service” means a library, museum or similar public or registered nonprofit organizational
use displaying, preserving, and exhibiting objects of community and cultural interest in one or
more of the arts and sciences.

19.04.438 Protective Service

“Protective Service” means a facility for public safety and emergency services, including fire and
police protection services and emergency medical and ambulance services.

Beer Tax Funding

                The Council reviewed the following Resolution authorizing the ongoing beer tax.
This tax will be used in the Kearns Metro Township for alcohol suppression.

RESOLUTION NO. 19-04-02                                                       Date: April 8, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AUTHORIZING THE
       TOWNSHIP’S PORTION OF THE BEER TAX BE GIVEN TO THE UNIFIED
       POLICE DEPARTMENT/KEARNS PRECINCT FOR USE WITH KEARNS METRO
       TOWNSHIP FOR PURPOSES CONSISTENT WITH THE TAX

                                          RECITALS

       A.     The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) As a Utah municipality, may pursuant to
              Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-8-2, appropriate money of the municipality
              for any purpose that in the judgement of the Metro Township Council provides for
              the safety, health, prosperity, moral well-being, peace, order, comfort or
              convenience of the inhabitants of the Township.

       B.     The Kearns Metro Township receives an allocation of money from what is
              commonly called the beer tax that can be used for alcohol and or substance abuse
              related services within the categories of prevention, treatment, law enforcement,
              prosecution and incarceration.

       C.     The Unified Police Department provides police services to the Township.

       D.     The Township anticipates receiving approximately $17,245.78 in beer tax funds in
              the next fiscal year.

       E.     The Unified Police Department has committed that if those funds are provided to
              the Department that it will use the funds in the Township for alcohol enforcement.

       F.     The Township is willing to dedicate its portion of the beer tax for the next fiscal
              year to the Unified Police Department/Kearns precinct on the terms and conditions
              contained in this resolution.


                                              10
DATE             MONDAY                                 APRIL                                  8, 2019


                                            RESOLUTION

         NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township Council as follows:

      1. The Kearns Metro Township’s allocation of beer tax for the 2018-2019 fiscal year in the
         amount of $17,245.78 shall be given to the Unified Police Department/Kearns Precinct on
         condition that it be used for officer overtime shifts for DUI enforcement; direct enforcement,
         saturation patrols, DUI checkpoints and the purchase of portable breath test devises and
         cameras. All these services are to be provided within the Kearns Metro Township area by
         the Kearns Precinct.

      2. This resolution is conditioned on the Unified Police Department entering into a
         Memorandum of Understanding with the Township that includes at a minimum the Unified
         Police Departments covenant to use the funds provided only in the Kearns Metro
         Township for the purposes state herein and the Unified Police Department will submit to
         the Township Council a plan on how they intent to spend the money and provide
         documentation the Township Council showing proof that the allocated money is spent as
         indicated in this resolution and the Memorandum of Understanding.

      3. This resolution shall take effect upon passage.
