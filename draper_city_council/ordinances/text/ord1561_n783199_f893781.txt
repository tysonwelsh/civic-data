City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On September 20, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1561,
amending Chapter 4 of the Draper City General Plan regarding Housing and
the Moderate Income Housing Plan. The complete ordinance is on file at the
Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published
this 22nd day of September 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
