Attachments: Click here to eComment on this item
10-17-23 RDA Agenda
Resolution RD 23-05

Resolution RD 23-06
82223 Minutes

A motion was made by Ryan Mecham, seconded by Zach Robinson to convene a
board meeting of the Redevelopment Agency of Sandy City at approximately
5:20 pm...The motion carried by a unanimous voice vote.

RDA Board meeting commenced:
The Council opened the Public Hearing comment period.

Mr. Steve Van Maren spoke on the agreement with the consultants. He expressed
concern that the consultants may have been paid to participate in the golf tournament
with Canyons School District. He also inquired about all the adjustments to the carryover
funds.

Public Hearing comment period closed.

Council asked Mr. Dunlavy to respond to the questions asked during the comment
period. The consultants were not paid to participate in the golf tournament.

Please refer to the minutes of the Redevelopment Agency Board.

Council reconvened the City Council meeting at 5:36 pm and moved to Standing Reports.

Standing Reports
Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

Dustin Fratto, Council Director updated the Council on upcoming agenda items and
reviewed the timeline and process for the selection of the Managment Consultant.

Council Member Business

Council Member Zach Robinson is working with council staff on a second reading of a No
Solicitation resolution for Council consideration and action. He requested that next
week's Council Meeting be designated as a Business Session. Council supported Mr.
Robinson's request.

Council Member Marci Houseman thanked the Council, Administration and Community
for expressing their condolences to her and her family in the loss of her father. She
thanked all for their compassion.

City Council Meeting Minutes October 17, 2023

Mayor's Report

Mayor Zoltanski was excused from this evening's meeting.

CAO Report

Shane Pace, CAO, mentioned that Sandy City was participating in a gently used blanket,
coat, and towel drive beginning next week. He provided an update on the Copper Creek
Reception facility tear down. This Saturday is the Chili Cookoff event at America First
Stadium. All are welcome. He spoke about the recent thefts of copper wiring in the city
and the city's response and preventative efforts.

Council convened a recess at 5:44 pm.

Council reconvened at 6 pm and moved to Item 1 on the Agenda: General Citizen
Comment.

Adjournment

Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:04 pm.
