G. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. RECOMMENDATION FROM THE WEBER COUNTY TAX REVIEW COMMITTEE TO REFUND TAXES ON
       PARCEL #22-131-0004 (KENT CHARLES FOX) DUE TO A COUNTY ERROR IN SQUARE FOOTAGE OF
        LIVABLE AREA AND GARAGE

        Joe Olsen, of the Assessor’s Office, on behalf of the Tax Review Committee, said that through the 2016
        Board of Equalization, the Assessor’s office was made aware of this error; which had now been corrected.
        The Tax Review Committee recommended refunding four years prior to 2016 (back to 2012), based on
        county policy, in the amount of $476.41. Courtlan Erickson, Deputy County Attorney, stated that the 4-
        year period for refunding taxes is based on State statute.
        Kent Fox, of Eden, stated that the Assessor’s Office had been very helpful, and that he understood the
        four year reach-back statute. He had looked back to 2002 and square footage had not been listed on the
        tax notices until last year, and he had not been aware of the error previously. He overpaid for 400+
        square feet since 2002 and requested that the Commission reconsider a refund from 2002-2011.
    Based on today’s discussion, Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the Weber County Tax Review
    Committee’s recommendation to refund taxes for four years ($476.41) on Parcel #22-131-0004 (Kent
    Fox) due to a county error in square footage of livable area and garage; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
    Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye
