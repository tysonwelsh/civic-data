City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On May 14, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1612, amending the
text of the land use and development code of the Draper City Municipal Code relating
to the allowed primary materials in the Southwestern Furniture Commercial Special
District. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 15th day of May 2024. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
