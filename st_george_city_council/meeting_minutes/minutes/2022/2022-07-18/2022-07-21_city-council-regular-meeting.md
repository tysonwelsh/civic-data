         Agreement with Jviation dated September 23, 2021, for the
         reconstruction of taxi lane 56.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Taxi lane 56 was damaged when an

         taxi lane 56 at the St. George Regional Airport.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: One bid was received and opened on
           July 12, 2022, for the above referenced project. The project Public Notice was
           posted on the City website and at five locations in St. George City as required by
           City Purchasing. There were four prime contractors at the mandatory pre-bid
           meeting. Staff recommends awarding the bid toe Sunroc Corporation in the
           amount of $262,685.

      e. Consider approval to award bid to Peak Asphalt for the chip seal oil
         supply.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This award is for 900 tons of chip seal oil
           for the yearly chip seal project. This was a formal bid and one (1) bid was
           received. The price per ton increased 70% from last year. The price increase is
           fairly consistent with the UDOT asphalt binder index which in June of 2021 (date
           of previous bid) was $479/ton and July 2022 was $747/ton (56% increase).
           Staff recommends awarding the bid to Peak Asphalt in the amount of $690,300.

HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a hillside development permit to allow disturbance of
     areas in the 20-30%, 30-40% and 40% and above slope areas on
     approximately 33.52 acres known as Divario PA-4, Lugano Landing,
     generally located along the future extension of Divario Canyon Drive at the
     boundary line between St. George City and Washington County.
St. George City Council Minutes
July 21, 2022
Page Twelve

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Divario development went through the
      hillside process in 2005. The approval of the hillside development permit in 2005
      required certain planning areas to come back through the hillside process in the
      future if areas shown on the approved hillside permit were proposed to be disturbed
      within these certain planning areas. There is an existing wash located within PA-4
      that triggered a hillside development permit if the wash were to be disturbed in the
      future with development of PA-4. The developer is proposing to disturb the westerly
      portion of this existing wash. This item went to the hillside committee twice. The
      first time the hillside committee agreed that the portion proposed to be disturbed by
      the developer did not have much aesthetic value. However, the hillside committee
      requested that before a recommendation of approval could be given, the rear lot
      lines of the proposed lots along that portion of the wash to remain open be staked so
      that the hillside committee could review the location of these lots relative to the
      existing wash to determine if the lot lines would need to be adjusted to protect the
      wash. The developer staked the rear property line of these lots, and the hillside
      committee did a second visit to the site and some of the lot lines were adjusted to
      reduce the impacts on the existing wash to remain. At their meeting held on June
      14, 2022, the Planning Commission recommended approval.
