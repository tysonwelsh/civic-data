Jake Hone with Grow Development, LLC has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for
two parcels totaling 6.68 acres located at 3814 and 3876 South 6000 West. Both parcels are currently
zoned A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) with a General Plan designation of Large Lot
Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) on the south parcel and Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre) on
the south parcel. The proposed zone is R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size
8,000 square feet) with a planned unit development (PUD) designation and the proposed General
Plan designation is Small Lot Residential (4 to 7 units/acre).

the remaining east portion. Surrounding land uses include a church to the south, Hunter Junior High
School to the west, and single-family homes to the north and east. The north parcel includes 13
homes and 2 duplexes (4 units) and the south parcel includes 2 homes. All 17 units on the north
parcel were built in the 1950s, are 624 square feet in area, have 2 bedrooms, and have a 1-car garage.
On the south parcel, the house to the north is 1,296 square feet and was built in 1936 and the house

The County Assessor has an overall condition that is assigned to every structure. The condition
options include poor, fair, average, good, very good, and excellent. In this case, all of the units on
the north parcel are rated as fair, meaning maintenance, rehabilitation, and replacement are needed
on many items. On the south parcel, the home to the north is rated fair and the home to the south is
rated poor.

Eligibility for R-1-8 Zoning

Up until recently, a request for R-1-8 zoning could only be made on properties under 2 acres.
However, in July of this year, the Council adopted Ordinance 24-28 which allowed zone change
applications on properties 2 acres and larger that meet the following conditions:
   1. The property is developed as residential with a density of at least two dwelling units per acre.
   2. The property has access and frontage on a street with a planned right-of-way width of at least
       66 feet as indicated on the Major Street Plan.
   3. The property does not have the off-site improvements of curb, gutter, and sidewalk along the
       street frontage.
The two subject properties meet these conditions as described below:
   1. The property is developed as residential with a density of 2.8 units/acre.
   2. The property has access and frontage on 6000 West, which has a planned right-of-way width
       of 66 feet on the Major Street Plan.
   3. There is no curb, gutter and sidewalk on the property’s street frontage along 6000 West or
       along the internal private street.

Development Proposal

The applicant is proposing to demolish all the existing buildings and develop a planned unit
development (PUD) of single unit dwellings. The applicant’s original proposal included 42 units,
yielding a density of 6.3 units/acre. However, after incorporating feedback from staff, the proposal
now has 41 units, resulting in a density of 6.1 units/acre. The streets would be public and utilize the
typical 54’ residential right-of-way. A total of just below one acre of open space is proposed in two
different locations. Included with this report is a memo from the applicant that provides support for
this application. Also included is a concept plan for the proposed improvements on the open space.

The applicant has also included a development agreement proposal covering a variety of topics. The
latest version of the development agreement proposal includes revisions made at the request of staff.
The following is a summary of items addressed in the proposal:
     The minimum lot size is 4,000 square feet and the minimum lot width is 45’.
     The minimum setbacks are 20’ in the front, 15’ in the rear, 5’ for both sides on lots that are

SMI-3-2024
Rushton Square Subdivision – Amending a portion of Lots 1 and 2 of the Floyd L Rushton
Subdivision
5529 West 4100 South
C-1 Zone 1 Lot

BACKGROUND:

Shane Sanders representing Mountain America Federal Credit Union is requesting minor subdivision
approval for the Rushton Square Subdivision. The Subdivision will also amend lots 1 and 2 of the
Floyd L. Rushton Subdivision. The property was recently rezoned from the A zone to the C-1 Zone.

Surrounding land uses include single unit dwellings to the north and south, a CVS store to the west,
and agricultural property to the east. The subject property was previously used for single family
dwellings. However, those were demolished around the time UDOT widened this portion of 4100
South as part of the Mountain View Corridor project.

ISSUES:

The proposed subdivision will consolidate lots 1 and 2 of the Floyd L Rushton Subdivision to create
1 new lot. That subdivision was recorded in 1993 and consisted of 2 lots on 1 acre. As mentioned
earlier, the property was recently rezoned to the C-1 zone to accommodate a new Mountain America
Credit Union.

Access to the subdivision is a little different than what is normally proposed. There will be no access
drives specific to this lot along the frontage of the proposed subdivision. This is due in part to spacing
requirements resulting from the Mountain View Corridor and improvements to the existing rights-of-
way at 5600 West 4100 South. Access is proposed to be gained from an existing approach at the
northeast corner of the CVS store to the west and from an existing approach on the adjacent property
to the east.

The applicant has been negotiating with both CVS and the east property owner to secure an access
easement through those properties for and on behalf of the Credit Union. The access agreement will
need to be resolved and in place prior to the plat being recorded. Improvements across the frontage
of the subdivision were previously installed with the widening of 4100 South and 5600 West.

C-15-2024
AWA Engineering (Maverik Inc.)
2384 South 5600 West
LI zone, 3.99 acres

The applicant, AWA Engineering representing Maverik, Inc., is requesting conditional use approval
for a convenience store and gas station at 2384 South 5600 West. The zoning for this property is
LI, Light Industrial and the General Plan designation is Light Manufacturing. This property is also
located within the 5600 West Overlay Zone. The surrounding zone is LI, Light Industrial on three
sides and the zoning to the east across 5600 West is M, Manufacturing. The surrounding uses
include salvage yard facilities, a UDOT maintenance facility to the south and Warner Truck Center
to the east.

The site is a redevelopment of the GVI auto repair and salvage yard property and also includes
assembly of property from UDOT and Masic Holdings, which was Butcher Auto Wrecking. The
proposal is to construct a 5,982 SF building. This location will operate 24 hours a day and includes 8
fueling stations along the east side and an additional 4 diesel/truck high flow dispenser stations on
the west side. There will also be an RV dumping station.

The facade of the building will be constructed of a variety of products including fiberboard siding

and cultured stone and must comply with the Commercial Design Standards. The gas canopy
supports also need to have materials that match the primary building so a cultured stone wainscot
must be added. The dumpster enclosure is located on the northwest corner of this building that will
be enclosed behind a masonry screen wall that matches the building per 7-2- 118.

For landscaping, the LI zone requires a minimum of 10% and the plan is currently showing 22.7%

CA-5-2024
ARK Plaza Auto Service Amendment
1927 West 3600 South
C-2 Zone (1.03 Acres)

The applicant, Rasoul Gassmepour representing ARK Plaza, is requesting a conditional use
amendment to add auto service uses to the existing multi-tenant commercial/warehouse building
located at 1927 West 3600 South. This property is zoned C-2, General Commercial, and the West
Valley City General Plan designates this area as General Commercial. Surrounding zones include A
(agricultural) and C-2 to the north, A (agricultural) to the west, C-2 to the east, and RM to the south.
Surrounding uses include residential and medical office (Planned Parenthood) to the north, multi-
family residential to the west and south (Homestead Farms) and office/warehouse to the east
(Interwest Brokerage).

This site is Lot 8 of the Plaza 3600 Business Park which was approved by the Planning Commission
in 1998 (C-26-98). Access to the site is gained off of 3600 South, which is a private road. The
original approval specified that each development must be reviewed as a separate conditional use
before the Planning Commission since the tenants were not known at the time of the overall
approval.
                     West Valley City Planning Commission Public Hearing Minutes
                                          September 11, 2024
                                             Page 8 of 11
This site development was approved by the Planning Commission on March 23, 2022 under C-5-
2022. The building that was constructed contains 11 units. Now that the construction of the project is
complete Mr. Gassmepour is ready for tenants to occupy the building and is requesting to expand the
type uses allowed to include Auto Service. That modification requires an amendment to the original
approval.

The primary concern at this time is parking. When the building was constructed, the plans outlined
that 49% would be warehouse and 51% would be retail. That configuration required 30 parking
spaces and 31 were provided. The current proposed tenant mix now contains two neighborhood
grocery uses, a three-chair hair salon, two Zumba fitness centers, car sales as well as three auto
service uses. Based off those parking requirements the site cannot accommodate all of those tenants.
There are also some of the tenants that are not adhering to the guidelines that Mr. Gassmepour has
indicated. The auto service uses show as one service bay (3 stalls/1 bay = 3) however during a site
inspection they have 3-5 cars being serviced (3 stalls/3-5 bays = 9-15 stalls). Also, the auto sales
business has been putting up to 7 vehicles in the required parking stalls for display. This is not only
creating parking problems within the site but also along the adjacent street.

Staff is working with Mr. Gassmepour, as well as with Building and Fire, to make sure all the
parking requirements are being met and that the building square footage and occupancy are verified.
Regardless of the request to expand the uses to include auto service all of these requirements must be
complete and in compliance prior to the issuance of any business licenses for any use. At this time
no business licenses have been issued.

Staff Alternatives

Approval, subject to the following conditions:

1) All of the prior conditions from C-5-2022 are applicable including but not limited to the building
design, landscaping and parking.

2) Auto service uses are approved for this location with the following restrictions:

       a) Auto service hours of operation limited to 9:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.

       b) Overhead doors must remain closed during auto service hours

       c) No outside storage

Continuance, to allow time for the applicant to address the current parking requirements as well as
any other reasons determined during the public hearing.
