                   the water fund.

7.         20-187                 Council Member Christensen introducing proposed amendments to the FY
                                  2020-21 Tentative Budget.
                   Attachments:   FY2020 21 Budget Proposal Memo
                                  FY2020 21 Budget Proposal Brooke

                                  Council Member Christensen reviewed the summary memo on the proposal. Council
                                  questions followed.

                                  Dustin Fratto gave direction for how to participate in public comment on this item.

                                  Sandra Haak recommended the Council use some kind of benchmarking. She complete
                                  support the retention of the compensation package, and filling vacant positions.

                                  Steve Van Maren spoke in favor of funding the Sandy Boys and Girls Club and funding the
                                  step-in-grade for police and fire.

                                  Shalom Orton, Executive Director the Sandy Club spoke about the benefit of maintaining
                                  City funding for the Club.

                                  Animal Services.

                                  considered and that public safety compensation should be retained.

                                  Sandy Club should be held to the same standard as other nonprofits.

                                  Robinson. She recommended additional financial transparency with the Sandy Club
                                  donation. Police and Fire personnel will leave for other cities.

                                  employee compensation.
