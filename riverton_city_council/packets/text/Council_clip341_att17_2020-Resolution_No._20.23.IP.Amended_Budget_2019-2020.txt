FROM:          Kevin Hicks, Administrative Services Director

ITEM:          Proposed Resolution No. 20-23 – Approving Amendments to the budget for fiscal
               year 2019-2020

SUMMARY:

Budgets are estimates. Therefore, there are times it may be necessary to amend the City’s
budget for unforeseen items.

The attached amendment includes the following changes to the budget:
    Funding to cover the contract with Cavortex approved by the Council in the March 3,
       2020 City Council meeting. This contract was for the feasibility study of City wells and
       the potential of re-opening the wells.
    An adjustment to the compensation structure for elected officials to create a more
       uniform treatment of salaries that will better cover the time devoted to the office as well
       as time devoted to the various boards that councilmembers attend.
           o The Mayor’s salary will be set at 25% of the midpoint of grade 20
           o The City Council salary will be set at 15% of the midpoint of grade 20
