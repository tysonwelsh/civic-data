                                   REIMBURSEMENT AGREEMENT
                                  [Strom Drain/Transportation – Sycamore Glen]

         This Reimbursement Agreement (“Agreement”) is made as of this ___ day of , 2022 (the
“Effective Date”), by and between RIVERTON CITY, a Utah municipality (“City”), and TOLL
SOUTHWEST LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Developer”) (each a “Party” and
collectively, the “Parties”).

                                               RECITALS:

         A.      Developer intends to develop a single-family residential development project known as
“Sycamore Glen” consisting of approximately 36 lots (the “Project”) and that is located at approximately
the intersection of 11800 South and Redwood Road in Riverton City, Salt Lake County, Utah, and adjacent
to the City Parcel [defined later].

         B.      City has acquired certain real property more particularly described on Exhibit A attached
hereto, and located in the vicinity of or adjacent to the Project (the “City Parcel”) for the future construction
and development of certain storm drain, road, park, and culinary waterline improvements, including,
without limitation, a regional storm drain detention basin (the “Detention Basin”).

        C.       As part of Developer’s development of the Project, Developer intends hereafter to cause to
be installed system improvements to Project and the City Parcel, including, without limitation, roadway
surfacing, curb, gutter, and street lights, storm drain upsizing within the Project, offsite city water system
improvements, and other improvements, as more particularly shown on the approved plans for said
improvements attached or to be attached as Exhibit B (collectively, the “Water and Roadway
Improvements”). The City Parcel and the Water and Roadway Improvements are depicted on Exhibit C
attached hereto.

          D.     City intends to reimburse Developer for the cost of the Water and Roadway Improvements..

        E.      City intends and will agree that the Detention Basin will accept storm drain water from the
Project’s storm drain system.

                                               AGREEMENT:

         NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises, mutual covenants, and undertakings, the
Parties hereby agree as follows:

         Section 1.      Developer’s Obligations. Developer will cause any design, installation and
construction of the Water and Roadway Improvements (the “Work”) to be completed at Developer’s sole
cost and expense by qualified licensed contractors, and in accordance with all plans and specifications for
the Water and Roadway Improvements approved by City and any and all other applicable governmental
and quasi-governmental entities and agencies with jurisdiction, and all necessary utility providers
(collectively, “Governmental Authorities”), and all required applicable Governmental Authorities’
approvals and permits for the work of construction of the Water and Roadway Improvements (collectively,
“Plans and Permits”). Upon City’s and any other Governmental Authority’s required approvals, the Plans
and Permits shall be added to this Agreement as Exhibit B. Prior to City’s acceptance of ownership of the
Water and Roadway Improvements, Developer shall provide evidence satisfactory to the City that all labor,
materials, equipment, rental, and other costs incurred in performing the Work have been paid in full and
that the City will receive the Water and Roadway Improvements free and clear of all liens, claims, and
encumbrances, substantially in accordance with the Plans and Permits. After City’s acceptance of

          Section 2.      City’s Obligations.

                  (a)     Construction Easement.

                        (i)       Declaration of Easement. City hereby declares, conveys, and grants for
                  the benefit of Developer and all or a portion of the Project, a temporary, non-exclusive
                  construction easement (the “Temporary Easement”), over, upon, and across the City
                  Parcel and that portion of City’s land underlying the Water and Roadway Improvements
                  (the “Easement Area”). Without limiting the enforceability of the easements granted
                  under this Section, the memorandum of this Agreement contemplated by Section 5(m)
                  below may include provisions evidencing such easements, or any Party may require the
                  other Party to execute a customary and commercially reasonable supplemental easement
                  form to be recorded in Salt Lake County records as needed to aid such Parties in exercising
                  the easement rights granted under this Section.

                        (ii)       Duration and Use. The Temporary Easement declared hereby shall
                  continue until City accepts the Water and Roadway Improvements in accordance with
                  Section 1 above. The Temporary Easement declared herein may be used for (a) the
                  installation, construction, maintenance, use, repair, replacement, and removal of
                  infrastructure improvements, together with a right of access to the Easement Area,
                  including the surface or any portion of the Easement Area, to exercise the foregoing rights;
                  and (b) vehicular and pedestrian ingress and egress to and from the Project and public
                  streets adjoining the Easement Area.

                  (b)     Reimbursement.

                          (i)      Generally.    The Water and Roadway Improvements are system
                  improvements as that term is defined by the City and Utah Code Ann. § 11-36-101, et seq.
                  and are subject to reimbursement. As used in this Agreement, the “actual costs of design
                  and construction” are limited to the work items and costs described in Exhibit D.
                  Changes or additions to the Work required by the City shall be requested by the City and
                  approved by execution of a change order approved in writing by the City prior to
                  commencement of work associated with said change order. Cost of any change orders
                  reasonably required in the performance of the Work shall include: sales tax, contractor’s
                  so-called “general conditions,” and a reasonable construction manager’s fee.

                          (ii)      Conditions Precedent to any Reimbursement. Prior to any reimbursement,
                  Developer shall post applicable performance and warranty bonds in accordance with City
                  ordinances to guarantee the installation and workmanship of the Water and Roadway
                  Improvements and to ensure that the Water and Roadway Improvements remain in good
                  condition and free from defects for a period of one (1) year, in accordance with City
                  ordinances, regulations, and standards. Prior to any reimbursement, City must approve the
                  Water and Roadway Improvements in writing in connection with the standard inspections
                  conducted by City to ensure that the Water and Roadway Improvements are constructed
                  per City standards. Prior to any reimbursement, Developer shall deliver a certified set of
                  as-built plans (in both paper and electronic format) along with the verified actual costs of
                  construction of the Water and Roadway Improvements.

                         (iii)     Reimbursement. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the approved
                  schedule of values (“Water and Roadway Improvements Budget”) for the costs to
                  complete the Water and Roadway Improvements is attached hereto as, and noted as
                  “Reimbursable Amount” in yellow highlighting on, Exhibit D. Developer shall use diligent

                  Improvements. In addition, in no case shall the City be obligated to reimburse Developer

                  Improvements Budget unless the additional cost was approved by change order. City shall
                  pay one hundred percent (100%) of the Water and Roadway Improvement Costs within
                  thirty (30) days of receipt of such notice from Developer. Developer shall not be entitled
                  to reimbursement from City for any costs in excess of the Water and Roadway
                  Improvements attached hereto as, and noted as “Reimbursable Amount” in yellow
                  highlighting on, Exhibit D.

                         (iv)      Invoices. Developer will regularly forward all invoices it receives for the
                  actual costs of design and construction of the Water and Roadway Improvements to City
                  for review and approval.

                 (c)     Storm Drain Connection. City acknowledges and consents to the Project’s storm
drain infrastructure connection to the City Parcel or the Detention Basin, and that the City Parcel or the
Detention Basin will accept storm drain water from the Project’s storm drain system, including as described
and depicted in Developer’s current plat application currently pending with the City. This provision shall
survive termination of this Agreement.

and City agrees that the right to reimbursements remains with Developer despite a transfer of the underlying
land to a third party.
