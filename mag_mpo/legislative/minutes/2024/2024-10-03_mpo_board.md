Minutes - Action (00:04:06) Mayor Denise Andersen moved to approve the minutes from August 29, 2024. Mayor Caroyn Lundberg seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Congressman Burgess Owens (00:04:57) Congressman Owens said MAG is doing remarkable things by being collaborative and thoughtful in the best way to move forward. He spoke to how the state is collaborative, fiscally aware, and creating a place for families to stay. MAGs collaboration and power to figure out what is best for all is important. Congressman Ownes stated he believes that the inland port will make Utah the distribution hub of the western United States. As the state grows we have to be smart in planning. Congressman Owens spoke to traﬃc and said with the Olympics coming in 2034 it is important to be innovative. He shared his moto he has with his team; we can not control the scoreboard but we can control the hustle. He expressed his gratitude to be invited to this meeting and invited the Board Members to reach out to his oﬃce if he can assist with any legislation to help propel progress. ULCT Check-In with President and Draper City Mayor Troy Walker (00:18:51) Cameron Deihl started by thanking Mayor Kaufusi for her service as the ULCT President. He introduced Mayor Troy Walker who is the new ULCT President. Mayor Troy Walker thanked Mayor Kaufusi for her work as the ULCT President. He took a minute to introduce himself and his background related to transit. He talked about the former prison site and the development occurring there. He spoke to local leaders dealing with all the crises in the City, and at times there has not been forward thinking related to housing. It is important that cities do what they can to aid the housing development by creating the areas for the different housing types from high density to low density. MPO Organizational Assessment update (00:45:20) LaNiece Davenport addressed the Board stating A/R360 was hired to look at the MPO Organization to understand strengths, weaknesses and opportunities. She introduced Fred Abousleman and Lindsey Riley with A/R360. Fred Abousleman introduced his team working on this project, their background, and their love of regional coordination. Having a strong partnership with the MPO to address overwhelming issues related to high growth Page 2 of 4 is important. There have been four focus groups completed and about 20 in depth one on one interviews. Fred Abousleman thanked those that have participated in those. He went into depth with what his team is doing in researching best policies, procedures and the best processes for the MPO. He concluded that a final report should be delivered to the MPO by the end of the year. Corridor Preservation - Provo 2230 North (00:53:33) Calvin Clark stated this is a request for corridor H41 for the purchase of a single family home. The property owner approached the city and is a willing seller. He gave a breakdown of the costs for the purchase of the property. Mayor Denise Andersen moved to approve the 2230 North Provo City Corridor Preservation Fund request for $630,875. Mayor Tom Westmorland seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. TIP Modification - Utah Lakeshore Trail - Saratoga Springs Hot Pots (00:57:10) Bob Allen stated this is a project awarded in 2016. This project fills in two segments of the trail along the west side of the lake; the Hot Pots and Amanda Lane. The project was started and encountered issues with the contracting company, as well as settlement issues. At this time Saratoga Springs is asking to separate the two segments and would work with UDOT and MAG to complete the Hot Pots trail section. The Amanda Lane trail section will be funded through a different avenue and the City is not asking for MAG funding. Mayor Tom Westmorland moved to add $1,233,000 to the Hot Pots Trail project and that the scope modification to construct the project through stabilization be approved. Mayor Denise Andersen seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Title VI Plan Adoption (01:03:46) Kendall Willardson stated that this is the final step with the Title VI plan. Title VI is a Civil Rights Act and prohibits discrimination of race, religion, color, national origin, sex, or age. This document spells out should there be an issue, how the MPO will address it and also recognizes where some of these different Title VI populations live within the MPO. Mayor Mark Johnson moved to approve the Title VI, Limited English Proficiency, and ADA Accessibility Plans contingent on there being no significant comments that alter the plans. Mayor Bill Wright seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Wasatch Choice Vision Utah County (01:06:33) (01:08:35) Commissioner Brandon Gordon moved to continue the Wasatch Choice Vision Utah County update. Mayor Kurt Ostler seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor.
