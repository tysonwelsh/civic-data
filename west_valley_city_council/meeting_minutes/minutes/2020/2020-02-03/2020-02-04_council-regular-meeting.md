A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION Z-3-2019, FILED
     BY KYLE MCMULLIN, REQUESTING A ZONE CHANGE FROM 'A'
     (AGRICULTURE, MINIMUM LOT SIZE 1/2 ACRE) TO 'M'
     (MANUFACTURING) ON PROPERTY LOCATED AT 6941 WEST SR-201
     NORTH FRONTAGE ROAD
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled February 4, 2020, in order for the City Council to
     hear and consider public comments regarding application Z-3-2019, filed by Kyle
     McMullin, requesting a Zone Change from 'A' (agriculture, minimum lot size 1/2
     acre) to 'M' (manufacturing) on property located at 6941 West SR-201 North
     Frontage Road.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:
MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING – FEBRUARY 4, 2020

          All surrounding property is zoned M. Neighboring uses include the ARA
          Industrial Park to the south, an auto salvage business to the west, an asphalt
          products business to the east and a recently approved storage lot to the north.
          The subject property is designated as light manufacturing in the West Valley
          City General Plan.

          The applicant has submitted the attached concept plan, which shows a
          proposed building of 310,800 square feet. Also attached is a letter from the
          applicant describing the proposal. The intended use is “office/warehouse or
          distribution”. Given the size of the proposal (over 10 acres), a conditional
          use permit would be required for the use approval assuming the zone change
          is approved.

          Up until the end 2015, the City’s General Plan showed heavy manufacturing
          uses for the area located north of SR-201 between 5600 West and 7200
          West. However, with inclusion of the ARA Industrial Park and other large,
          light industrial projects along SR-201, the General Plan was updated to
          show light industrial uses for those properties with frontage along the SR-
          201 north frontage road. For this reason, the Planning Commission
          recommended a development agreement to establish design standards and
          use restrictions.

   Mayor Bigelow opened the Public Hearing.

   Kyle McMullin stated that he is the applicant and stated that he has proposed a
   resolution to some of the Council concerns expressed during the Study Meeting the
   previous week. He indicated that he would propose that no storage be permitted in
   the front 250 feet of the property. Mr. McMullin also indicated that he would
   request a modification to the Development Agreement to allow two single loaded
   buildings with glass windows on one corner and not two. Councilmember
   Fitisemanu stated that this is consistent with what was proposed in the previous
   meeting earlier tonight. Mayor Bigelow stated that he would like the other corner
   of the single loaded buildings to have some sort of architectural feature. Mr.
   McMullin agreed. He stated that this is a completive market and inviting
   architecture is important.

   Mayor Bigelow closed the Public Hearing.

   Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included

          Logan Johnson with Wright Development has requested a zone change for
          two parcels totaling 1.5 acres at 5712 and 5724 West 3500 South from A
          (agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to C-2 (general commercial). The
          subject property is designated as commercial/office in the Hunter Town

          Mountain View Corridor. The west parcel will remain in UDOT ownership

RESOLUTION 20-11: AWARD A CONTRACT TO BROKEN ARROW
CONSTRUCTION CORP. FOR THE REPLACEMENT OF THE ROOF FOR FIRE
STATION 74
Mayor Pro-Tem Buhler discussed proposed Resolution 20-11 that would award a Contract
to Broken Arrow Construction Corp. for the replacement of the roof for Fire Station 74.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The City has budgeted $75,000 for the roof replacement of Fire Station #74. Bids
       were obtained from three reputable contractors. This procurement is below the bid
       limit set forth by state code 11-39-101, as referenced in City Code 5-3-115.

       The roof of Fire Station #74 has reached its life expectancy under normal usage,
       wear, and tear. After 15 years, maintenance on the roof is now become reactive,
       repairing many leaks and other issues. It is necessary to replace the roof with a new
       membrane. Broken Arrow submitted the lowest bid and proven work history with
       the City.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Pro-Tem Buhler there were no further questions from members of
the City Council, and he called for a motion.

       Councilmember Lang moved to approve Resolution 20-11.

RESOLUTION 20-13: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO PURCHASE A PRISONER
TRANSPORT VAN
Mayor Pro-Tem Buhler discussed proposed Resolution 20-13 that would authorize the City
to purchase a prisoner transport van.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Frequently, we have several officers at the jail in South Salt Lake at the same time
       waiting to book prisoners. A prisoner transport vehicle would allow one officer to
       safely transport more than one prisoner. This would free up the other officers to
       stay in WVC and respond on calls for service or patrol the city.

       The Utah Beer Tax Grant provides funding for equipment used in alcohol and drug
       related investigations and enforcement. A large percentage of the prisoners we
       transport are under the influence of alcohol or drugs. The grant funding to purchase
       the prisoner transport vehicle has been approved by the USAAV+ Council.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Pro-Tem Buhler there were no further questions from members of
the City Council, and he called for a motion.

       Councilmember Christensen moved to approve Resolution 20-13.

       Councilmember Lang seconded the motion.
