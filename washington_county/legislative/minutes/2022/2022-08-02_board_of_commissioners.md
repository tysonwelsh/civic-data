CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1215-O, AN ORDINANCE AMENDING

THE WASHINGTON COUNTY CODE, TITLE 10 ZONING REGULATIONS, MAKING
ADDITIONS AND REVISIONS, ADDING THE AGRICULTURE ESTATE OVERLAY

ZONE, UPDATING USE TABLES, SIGNAGE AND MINING PROVISIONS, AND
OTHER VARIOUS CHANGES

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales explained that the county is adding and revising provisions
in its Title 10 zoning regulations in order to address growth and changes within the County. The
County has received several owner driven requests to update agriculture zones on parcels 20 acres
or larger in order to provide for additional building options such as multiple dwellings. This is an
owner-requested overlay zone. Use tables are also being updated including the commercial,
manufacturing, industrial and mining and gravel use tables. Performance standards for the
manufacturing and industrial zones are clarified within the code. Other various Title 10 changes
are being made, including off premises signage and removing some CUPs in favor of new
Permitted with Standards provisions.

The Planning Commission reviewed the changes and unanimously recommended the amendments
to Title 10.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2022-1215-O, An
Ordinance Amending the Washington County Code, Title 10 Zoning
Regulations, Making Additions and Revisions, Adding the Agriculture Estate

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2982, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
TRT FUNDING FOR THE SPONSORSHIPS OF VARIOUS EVENTS TO BE HELD IN

WASHINGTON COUNTY

Deputy Tourism Director Brittany McMichael explained that the Tourism Tax Advisory Board
(TAB) made recommendations for several upcoming events in the County as follows:
(1) $10,500 to Santa Clara BMX for the sponsorship of the BMX State Finals;
(2) $25,000 to TopNotch Management for the sponsorship of the WTA Event; and
(3) $55,000 to Huntsman World Senior Games for event sponsorship and $20,000 toward
Greater Zion branded track and field equipment to be used at Utah Tech University for
the Huntsman Senior Games and other UTU-hosted events, a total of $75,000 in

funding;

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2982, A
Resolution Approving TRT Funding for the Sponsorships of Various Events
to be Held in Washington County as Outlined. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2983, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE TOWN OF VIRGIN AND WASHINGTON COUNTY
FOR PATROL LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICES

Sheriff Nate Brooksby explained that the Sheriffs Office provide patrol services to unincorporated
areas as well as incorporated areas that do not have their own law enforcement agencies. The Town
of Virgin is in need of patrol services and the County will provide approximately 320 hours of
patrol time per year for the amount of $14,400.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2983, A
Resolution Approving Agreement Between the Town of Virgin and
Washington County for Patrol Law Enforcement Services. Motion seconded
by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

ENGAGEMENT OF NEW BENEFITS CONSULTANT

Human Resources Director Johnathan Liddle explained that Washington County posted a Request
for Proposal (RFP) for employee benefits brokerage consulting services. There were three (3)
applicants that provided an in person presentation of its service offerings and how each would best
assist the County with a re-design of its benefits package as well as improvements to the employee
experience. It is recommended for NFP to become the new benefits consultant for the County.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2986, A
Resolution Approving Engagement of New Benefits Consultant. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.
