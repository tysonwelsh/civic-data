HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2018-29

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 6 CHAPTER 1 OF THE HERRIMAN CODE OF
ORDINANCES BY ADDING SECTIONS REGARDING INTERFERENCE WITH
CONTROL OF VEHICLE PROHIBITED, REASONABLE AND PROPER LOOKOUT
REQUIRED, AND NEGLIGENT COLLISION

WHEREAS, the City Council (the “Council”) met in regular meeting on September __,
2018, to consider, among other things, an ordinance amending title 6 chapter 1 of the Herriman
Code of Ordinances by adding sections regarding interference with control of vehicle prohibited,
reasonable and proper lookout required, and negligent collision; and

WHEREAS, the Council finds that maintaining safe control of a vehicle is critical to the
safety of the inhabitants of Herriman; and

WHEREAS, the Council has been informed that police officers need provision dealing
with interference with control of vehicle prohibited, reasonable and proper lookout required, and
negligent collision; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council finds that it is in the best interest of
the health, safety, and welfare of the inhabitants of Herriman to amend title 6 chapter 1 of the
Herriman Code of Ordinances by adding sections regarding interference with control of vehicle
prohibited, reasonable and proper lookout required, and negligent collision.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council that Herriman Code of
Ordinances be amended, designated by interlineating the words to be deleted and underlining the
words to be added, as follows

6.1.3 - Interference with control of vehicle prohibited.

No driver shall engage in any activity that interferes with the safe control and operation of
his vehicle while the same is in motion.

6.1.4 - Reasonable and proper lookout required.

It is unlawful for any person to drive a vehicle on the streets of the city without keeping a
reasonable and proper lookout for other traffic, persons or objects.

6.1.5 - Negligent collision.

It is unlawful to operate a vehicle with such lack of due care as to cause the same to
collide with any vehicle, person or object.

This Ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 2018-29 shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 12" day of September, 2018.
