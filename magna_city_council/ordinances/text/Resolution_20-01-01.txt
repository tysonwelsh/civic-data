| RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (““UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. The Utah Local Governments Trust (“ULGT”) is a Nonprofit Interlocal Corporation
that provides insurance coverage to Local Governments pursuant to UCA 11 Chapter
13a — Governmental Nonprofit Corporations Act.

D. ULGT provides General Liability and Workers Compensation Insurance to Magna.

E. ULGT provided Magna with a Bid for Insurance in December 2019 for the year 2020.

F. Magna is required to accept the bid prior to payment of the insurance premium.
| RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves this resolution,
accepts the Bid for Insurance from the Utah Local Governments Trust, hereinafter known
as Attachment “A,” and approves the payment of the insurance premium for Magna upon
receipt of the invoice.
