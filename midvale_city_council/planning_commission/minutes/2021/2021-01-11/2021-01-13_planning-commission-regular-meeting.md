4.       CUP/SUB-22-19-328-072; VUNDER AMENDED SUBDIVISION; 287/293 E 6790 S;
         CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT AND PRELIMINARY SUBDIVISION PLAT FOR
         SUBDIVISION WITH TWIN HOMES ON 4 RESIDENTIAL LOTS; SF-1 ZONE
         WITH DUPLEX OVERLAY (SF-1/DO); BRANDON FRY/FRY DEVELOPMENT
         (APPLICANT); JASON BINKS, SENIOR PLANNER (STAFF).

Mr. Jason Binks stated that the applicant, Mr. Brandon Fry, requests approval of a conditional
use permit and preliminary subdivision plat to reconfigure two adjacent parcels at 287 & 293
East 6790 South into a 4-lot subdivision w ith two twin homes. The twin homes, labeled building
#1 on the west and building #2 on the east, will be on either side of a single driveway entrance
on 6790 South that accesses all 4 lots.

The two existing parcels are undeveloped and have a total of 0.42 acres with 146 feet of frontage
along 6790 Sand 128 feet of frontage along 300 E. The parcels meet lot standards and,
according to Title 17-7-1.14, can be subdivided to allow for individual ownership of each side of
a single family attached structure. The resultant 4 lots will be between 0.07 - 0.14 acres.

The subject property is zoned Single Family Residential with Duplex Overlay (SF-1 /DO). All
sunounding properties are in the same zoning district and developed with single family
residential uses. There is an existing twin home to the south across the street. All new
development is required to comply with the standards of the SF-1/DO zone.

ORDINANCE CONSIDERATIONS:

Conditional Use Permit for Twin Homes

The SF-1/DO zone allows twin homes with a conditional use pe1mit, provided the Planning
Commission concludes that the application fully mitigates all identified adverse impacts and
complies with the following general standards:

     1. The application complies with all applicable provisions of this title, State and Federal
        Law;
     2. The structures associated with the use are compatible with surrounding structures in
        terms of use, scale, mass and circulation;
     3. The use is not detrimental to the public health, safety and welfare;
     4. The use is consistent with the Midvale City General Plan, as amended;
     5. Traffic conditions are not adversely affected by the proposed use including the existence
        of or need for dedicated tum lanes, pedestrian access, and capacity of the existing streets;
     6. Sufficient utility capacity;
     7. Sufficient emergency vehicle access;
     8. Location and design of off-street parking as well as compliance with off-street parking
        standards provided for in § 17-7-1.8;
     9. Fencing, screening, and landscaping to separate the use from adjoining uses and mitigate
        the potential for conflict in uses;

    10. Compatibility of the proposed mass, bulk, design, orientation, and location of the
        structures on the site; including compatibility with buildings on adjoining lots and to the
        street;
    11. Exterior lighting that complies with the lighting standards of the zone and is designed to
        minimize conflict and light trespass with surrounding uses; and
    12. Within and adjoining the site, impacts on the acquirer, slope retention, and flood
        potential have been fully mitigated and the proposed structure is appropriate to the
       topography of the site.

Staff has not identified any conflicts with applicable provisions of the zoning ordinance, the
Midvale City General Plan, or state and federal law. The Midvale City General Plan notes that "a
diversified housing stock is integral to any city's sustainability [and that] housing choices must
be available for all life stages, income levels, and lifestyles." Twin homes in this location offers a
price point and variety that may be more affordable for all life stages than other housing
opportunities in the immediate neighborhood. Staff finds that the proposed twin home structures
fulfill the goals of the General Plan.

The proposed twin homes, building # 1 and building #2, are not detrimental to public health,
safety or welfare. The proposed structures comply with the required SF-1/DO development
standards, including setbacks, build-to line, height, parking, and utilities for standard lots. The
twin homes do not conflict with adjacent residential structures in terms of use, scale, mass,
circulation, design, or location. The traditional front fa9ade of building #2 is oriented toward the
shared driveway, but the applicant is providing an entrance, eave and concrete patio along 300
East to provide a design aesthetic along this street frontage that is compatible with adjacent
homes in the neighborhood.

The lot and dimensional building plans were reviewed only as proposed. Staff recommends a
condition for the conditional use pe1mit that the buildings must be constructed as shown on the
plans submitted for this conditional use pe1mit. Staff recommends a note be added to the final
plat that only twin homes can be constructed unless the plat is officially amended.

Per Section 17-7.1.7, landscaping is required in the yard and setback areas visible from street
access and shall include the treatment of ground surfaces with live materials. The proposed
development does not identify a specific landscaping plan. Landscaping shall be installed within
6 months of building occupancy. Staff recommends a condition to this effect.

No traffic issues have been identified in the single-family residential neighborhood. Each twin
home has the required 2 off-street parking spaces per dwelling unit. Sufficient utility capacity
will be verified and guaranteed through provision of the water and sewer duty to serve letters
required by the final subdivision process. Emergency vehicle access is not a concern. There are
no lighting requirements for this use.

The City Engineer found that the proposed structures are appropriate for the topography and
there are no development impacts on the aquifer, slope retention, or flood potential.

Subdivision

The proposed Vunder Amended Subdivision includes 4 lots with a common 20' driveway shared
by all residential units.

With the exceptions listed below, the proposed subdivision complies with the design standard
requirements of Title 16 Subdivisions. The lots conform with the SF-1/DO zone requirements.
No new streets are proposed. Curb, gutter, and sidewalk exist along 300 East, but need to be
installed along 6790 South.

Section 16.04.040(A)(3) requires that 2"-caliper deciduous street trees be planted with any new
subdivision at a rate of one tree for every 30 feet of frontage. Therefore, two (2) deciduous street
trees are required on both Lots 1 and 4. Four (4) deciduous street trees are required along 300
East. Trees must be planted before issuance of a Certificate of Occupancy or guaranteed by a
cash bond. Staff recommends a note be added to the final plat to this effect.

The City Engineer approved the preliminary plat on Dec 14, 2020, with minor technical revisions
addressed on the final plat. The Fire Marshall and Public Works approved the preliminary plat
with no conunents.

Notice of the proposed conditional use and preliminary subdivision plat was mailed to 41
property owners within 300 feet of the project, posted to the city and state websites, and
advertised in the Salt Lake Tribune and Desert News.

Based on compliance with requirements of Title 16 and Chapter 17-7-1 of the Midvale City
Municipal Code, the analysis above, and by the inclusion of conditions of approval, Staff

the applicant meets all the requirements of the statute, then the Planning Commission is required
to approve this application. If there are issues, then the Planning Commission is required to seek
mitigation of those issues. Only if issues presented can't be mitigated can the Planning
Commission deny the application.

Mr. Binks stated that it is not. It is a residential with a duplex overlay. A duplex is allowed by right
on this comer.
