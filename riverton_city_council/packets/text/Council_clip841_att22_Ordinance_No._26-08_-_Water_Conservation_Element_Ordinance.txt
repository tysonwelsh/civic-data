                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 26-08

      AN ORDINANCE CORRECTING THE PREVIOUSLY ADOPTED WATER
     CONSERVATION AND EFFICIENCY STANDARD TO BE INCLUDED AS AN
      ELEMENT OF THE GENERAL PLAN. PROPOSED BY RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission and City Council have held a public hearing to
consider said the adoption of this item; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to adopt as an element of the Riverton City General Plan the Water Conservation and
Efficiency Element, as described in Exhibit “A”.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. The Water Conservation and Efficiency Element hereby is adopted as an
                  element of the Riverton City General Plan, as shown in Exhibit “A.”.

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 5th day of
