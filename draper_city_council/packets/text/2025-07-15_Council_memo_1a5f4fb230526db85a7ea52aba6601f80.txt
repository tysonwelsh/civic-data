MEMO
To: City Council
From: Travis DeJong
Date: 2025-07-15
Re: Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for a Hospitality Amenity License for Draper
Hotel Holdings LLC dba Homewood Suites.


Comments:

Draper Hotel Holdings LLC dba Homewood Suites located at 473 W 13490 S is seeking
Local Consent for a Hospitality Amenity license. They have provided the following:

- Liquor liability insurance.
- Sales tax license.
- A background check conducted by the Bureau of Criminal Identification.

Draper Hotel Holdings LLC dba Homewood Suites currently holds a commercial business
license numbered CBL-0316-2024.

The findings for Local Consent approval are as follows:

DCMC 6-4-040 requires that any establishment holding a hospitality amenity license be at
least six hundred feet (600') away from any community location (schools, churches, public
libraries, public playgrounds, and public parks) measured by the shortest pedestrian
route taken from the nearest patron entrance of the outlet to the property boundary of
the community location.

Draper Hotel Holdings LLC dba Homewood Suites meets this requirement.

DCMC 6-4-040 requires that any establishment holding a hospitality amenity license be at
least two hundred feet (200') away from any community location (schools, churches,
public libraries, public playgrounds, and public parks) measured in a straight line from
the nearest patron entrance of the outlet to the property boundary of the community
location.

Draper Hotel Holdings LLC dba Homewood Suites meets this requirement.

DCMC 6-4-050 does not put any limit on the number of hospitality amenity licenses
granted by Draper City.
