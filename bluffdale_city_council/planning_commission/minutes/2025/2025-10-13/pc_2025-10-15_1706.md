4 Ms. Tubbs reported that the Text Amendment was in response to legislative updates. HB 368 was
5 passed by the State Legislature in the 2025 General Session and intended to facilitate the
6 production of housing statewide. Changes were made to annexation code, buildings, bonding,
7 public improvements, subdivisions, etc., but only limited modifications were needed to bring the
8  Bluffdale City Code into compliance. Ms. Tubbs then reviewed the required changes.
9
10 Building Permits
11
12 e Identical Plans must be allowed. They are defined by State code and qualify builders for
13 expedited review and reduced plan review fees. The lesser of actual costs or 30% of the
14 standard review fees can be charged.
15 e Land Disturbance Permits must be issued or waived by the City before building
16 commences.
17
18 Public Notice
19
20 e HB 368 prohibits public input on appeals of land use decisions, including site plans,
21 subdivision plats, and ADU permits, so all mention of public hearings was removed from
22 the noticing table.
23 e Updated Annexation notice section to refer to the correct State Code section.
24 e Updated Development Agreement notices to clarify that notice is provided for the first
25 public hearing. Public notice is only required if the agreement differs from what is already
26 permitted in the zone.
27
28 Non-Conformities
29
30 e Definitions of “Noncomplying Structures” and “Nonconforming Use” were updated to
31 match State-adopted definitions.
32 e Non-complying Structure: a structure that:
33 a) legally existed before the structure’s current land use designation; and

2 to the setback, height restrictions, or other regulations, excluding those regulations

Commission on Wednesday, October 15, 2025, at Bluffdale City Hall, 2222 W 14400 S, which will begin

corridors;

- Chapter 11.190 (“Annexation”) of the Land Use Ordinance to reflect changes required by State
Code;
