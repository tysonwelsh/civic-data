-) Public Services Director Lowder summarized the National League of Cities (NLC) Service Line Warranty Program,
yo offered by Utility Service Partners, a HomeServe Company. The program serves to educate property owners about their service E

Chair Choberka spoke to her personal experience with costly utility line repairs; many in her neighborhood have had to

Mr. Lowder then introduced Dennis Lyon of HomeServe, who reported on the goals of the program, as follows:
e Providing homeowners affordable protection against significant and unexpected costs to remedy
leaking/broken/clogged water lines, sewer lines, and in-home plumbing lines. :
e Ensuring the delivery of timely, high-quality repair services in adherence to all applicable codes. ;
e Providing exemplary service that reflects positively on Ogden City.
e The program generates an ongoing, sustainable source of revenue for partner municipalities and stimulates the
local economy by using fully vetted local contractors to complete the repairs.
¢ Ogden will receive a royalty of 10% per product per month for the duration of the program (or a reduction in
price for homeowners should Ogden opt out of the royalty).
Mr. Lyon summarized coverage provided by the warranty program, after which he noted that if the program is approved,
the NLC will utilize Ogden’s logo to brand the materials used to educate city residents/customers about our repair service plans.
Program marketing literature clearly discloses that the Program and Ogden are separate entities and that the program is voluntary
for residents. The NLC Service Line Warranty Program will create all marketing materials with input from Ogden staff and will
submit all marketing/communications materials to the staff for final approval before distribution.
Mr. Lowder concluded Administration is seeking approval to enter into the non-royalty marketing agreement with Utility
Service Partners, Inc. (USP) for an initial term of three (3) years, subject to City Attorney review with an additional (1) year
renewal.

Union Station - Community and Economic Development Update
Community and Economic Development Director Cooper used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to discuss plans for
the future development and redevelopment of the Union Station campus. He referred to information provided in past meetings,
including a historical timeline of the City’s involvement in management of the Union Station. He discussed land planning studies
that have been conducted for the campus and surrounding areas of downtown Ogden, noting the findings of those studies have
informed decisions regarding pre-development activities that have occurred for the campus. He then summarized the terms of the
Union Pacific Purchase agreement and discussed the joint announcement of a developer for the campus development in February
of 2023; a Union Station Campus Advisory Committee will be created to guide and inform the creation of a final implementation
development plan for the Union Station Campus. He summarized the makeup of the Committee, noting there is one seat open for a
City Council member as well as representatives from the Union Station Foundation and other community partners. The target date
for implementation of this group is April 2023. There will also be a Museum Planning Group to provide technical guidance and
information related to the creation of a final implementation development/business plan for the Union Station Museum;
membership will include representatives from events, collections/exhibits, architects, image/branding, and Union Station staff.
The target date for implementation of this group is July 2023. Finally, a fundraising group will be created to coordinate, sponsor,
and facilitate philanthropic fund raising to support the capital needs of the final Union Station Campus Plan and the Union Station
Museum Plan. Membership will be comprised of Union Station Foundation members and Ogden City representatives. The target
date for implementation of a fundraising group is July 2023. These three groups will work with the Utah Transit Authority, the
Redevelopment Agency and the developer as all parties work to implement the MAKE Ogden plan.
Council members thanked Mr. Cooper for his efforts to create the different groups to provide the public and volunteers at
. Union Station with the ability to be involved in the Union Station campus project.
