RECOMMENDATION:
Approve Ordinance 1658 amending compensation for Statutory Officers for fiscal year ending June
30, 2026 as proposed.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The proposed budget includes funding for cost increases for all City employees including statutory
officers. In accordance with Utah Code 10-3-818, this ordinance adopts the compensation schedule
for Statutory Officers following the public hearing which was held on May 20,2025.
