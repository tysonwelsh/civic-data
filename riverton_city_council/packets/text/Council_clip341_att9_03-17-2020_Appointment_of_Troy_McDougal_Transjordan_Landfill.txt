                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-25



    A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF RIVERTON CITY GIVING ITS
   ADVICE AND CONSENT TO THE APPOINTMENT OF TROY MCDOUGAL TO
    SERVE AS BOARD MEMBER TO THE TRANSJORDAN LANDFILL BOARD


WHEREAS, Riverton City is a member of an interlocal entity known as the Transjordan
Landfill under an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with seven member cities including Draper
City, Midvale City, Murray City, Sandy, City, South Jordan City, and West Jordan City; and

WHEREAS, the Trans-Jordan Cities Interlocal Agency is governed by a board of
representatives of the seven member-cities; and

WHEREAS, the representative of each City is appointed by the governing body of such city to
serve as a member of the Board; and

WHEREAS, an alternate representative is also appointed to serve as a member of the Board in
the absence or disability of the representative with the same voting privileges; and

WHEREAS, the City Council desires to appoint Trace Robinson, Public Works Director, as an
alternate representative for the City of Riverton on the Trans-Jordan Landfill Board.


NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as follows:


   1. The City Council approves the appointment of Councilmember Troy McDougal to serve
      as the City’s representative to the Transjordan Landfill Board.

   2. The City Council approves the appointment of Trace Robinson to serve as the City’s
      alternate representative to the Transjordan Landfill Board.

   3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.


PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 17th day of March,
