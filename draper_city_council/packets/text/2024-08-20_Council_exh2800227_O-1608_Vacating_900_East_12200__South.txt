                               ORDINANCE NO. 1608

AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PORTION OF CITY RIGHT-OF-WAY LOCATED AT
APPROXIMATELY 900 EAST 12200 SOUTH

      WHEREAS, Draper City received the right-of-way at 900 East 12200 South as a
land grant; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City no longer needs the portion of 900 East north of
12200 South as right-of-way; and

       WHEREAS, nothing herein should be construed to impair any right-of-way or
easement of any parcel or lot owner; the rights of any public utility; the rights of any
public utility; or the rights of a culinary water authority or sanitary sewer authority;
and

     WHEREAS, to best serve the interests of Draper residents the Draper City
Council desires to vacate a portion of the right-of-way.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Findings. The Draper City Council finds (1) good cause exists for
the vacation; and (2) neither the public interest or any person will be materially
injured by the proposed vacation.

     Section 2. Vacation. The right-of-way depicted in color in Exhibit A and
more particularly described in Exhibit B is vacated.

       Section 3. Severability Clause. If any section, part, or provision of this
Ordinance is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall
affect any other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of
this Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 4. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 20TH DAY OF AUGUST, 2024.
