City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1656

On June 18, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1656 amending the
official zoning map of draper city for approximately 20 acres of property from RA1
(Residential Agricultural) and RA2 (Residential Agricultural) to RM2 (Multi-family
Residential), together with a development agreement located at approximately 600
East Kimballs Lane within Draper City otherwise known as the Kimballs Junction Zoning
Map Amendment and Development Agreement. The complete ordinance is on file at
the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
