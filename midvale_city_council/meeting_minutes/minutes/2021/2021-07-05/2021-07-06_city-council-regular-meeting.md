Rori Andreason said Juneteenth is the oldest nationally celebrated commemoration of the
ending of slavery in the United States.

From its Galveston, Texas origin in 1865, the observance of June 19th as the African
American Emancipation Day has spread across the United States and beyond.

Today Juneteenth commemorates African American freedom and emphasizes education
and achievement. lt is a day, a week, and in some areas a month marked with
celebrations, guest speakers, picnics, and family gatherings. lt is a time for reflection and
rejoicing. lt is a time for assessment, self-improvement and for planning the future. lts
growing popularity signifies a level of maturity and dignity in America long overdue. ln
cities across the country, people of all races, nationalities and religions are joining hands
to truthfully acknowledge a period on our history that shaped and continue to influence
our society today.

ln June 2016, Utah became the 44th state to recognize Juneteenth as a commemorative
period. The bill, H.B. 338, was signed during the 2016 legislative session. lt states that
"Juneteenth Freedom Day, [shall be held] on the third Saturday in June, in honor of Union
General Gordon Granger proclaiming the freedom on all slaves on June 19, 1865, in
Galveston, Texas." However, Juneteenth is not a state holiday in Utah. That would require
approval from the state legislature.

ln June 2021, President Joe Biden and Vice President Kamala Harris signed into law
legislation to make Juneteenth a federal holiday. Some cities celebrated Juneteenth this
year, other cities are waiting to see what action the state legislature takes towards making
Juneteenth a state holiday in Utah.

Cunently, Midvale City recognizes the following holidays:
. New Year's Day January 1st
. Martin Luther King Day 3rd Monday in January
. Presidents' Day 3rd Monday in February
. Memorial Day Last Monday in May
. lndependence Day July 4th
. Pioneer Day July 24th
. Labor Day 1st Monday in September
. Veteran's Day November 11th
. Thanksgiving Day 4th Thursday in November
. Thanksgiving Holiday 4th Friday in November
. Christmas Day December 25th
. Christmas Holiday December 26th

As the 2016 legislation recognized Juneteenth as a commemorative period on the third
Saturday in June, that would mean if the council adopted Juneteenth as a city holiday,
the employees would take the Friday prior to that Saturday as holiday. Employees on the
10-hour work schedule would split the holiday between the prior Thursday and the next
Monday in order to keep city hall open on those days.

DETECTIVE MARK UTLEY
Detective Mark Utley is a valuable member of the Midvale Shelter Resource Unit and
member of the Mental Health Unit. As part of these assignments, Detective Utley became
acutely aware of two individuals who have been staples in the Midvale community for
over a decade. Both are homeless and suffer from severe mental disorders. Midvale
officers have been attempting to help these individuals for many years. However, they
have been resistant to resources and, invariably, ended up back on the streets in Midvale.
Detective Utley was able to develop a rapport with these individuals and created a plan
that meets their specific needs. He facilitated a meeting between the two individuals and
a doctor to help address mental health issues and then secured them long term housing.
These individuals have remained in that housing for many months.

Detective Utley was able to assist these two and eliminate a persistent concern in the
Midvale area. Businesses and citizens alike benefitted from his passion and dedication.
For this, Detective Utley is to be commended.

On October 28, 2020 Midvale Officers responded on report of an assault in progress
involving a knife. The complainant stated a juvenile female (7 years of age) came to their
door with a cut on her hand and blood on her clothes. The juvenile informed the
complainant her father was fighting with her mother. The young girl said her mother was
"down in the master bedroom" and her father was still inside with a knife. Lt. Barker was
on scene and took incident command. Based on the information, it was decided an
emergency entry would be made with the intent to save the life of the mother. Sgt. Lord
organized an entry team and entered the residence. Unfortunately, the mother was found
deceased on the master bedroom floor. She was found with multiple stab wounds and
lacerations. The father was lying on the bed in the same room. He had similar wounds to
his arms and neck, but he was alive and speaking with officers who immediately rendered
aid. Their care likely saved his life.

Victim Advocates Carmen Contreras, Norma Hood, and John Pfeiffer responded and took
amazing care of the girl and her 11-year-old brother. Both were present in the home
during the altercation. The response to this tragic event was well coordinated. The
professionalism of all on scene was noticed and appreciated by Commanders of the
Unified Police Department. The same professionalism was also felt by members of the
community as they watched the response.

    A. CONSIDER RESOLUTION NO. 2021-R-30 APPOINTING MARIAH HILL
              AS THE ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES DIRECTOR FOR MIDVALE CITY
Kyle Maurer said he recommended the appointment of Mariah Hill for the position of
Administrative Services Director. He said he was seeking the advice and consent of the
City Council for Mariah's appointment.

The City received 8 applications for the position of Administrative Services Director - 6
were qualified, 2 did not meet minimum qualifications, and one candidate dropped out
due to another job offer. The applications were reviewed by the Assistant City Manager,
and all candidates meeting minimum qualifications (five) were selected for the first round
of interviews. The first round of interviews included the Assistant City Manager,
Administrative Services staff (Finance/lT/Justice Court), HR, and the Communications
Director (7 staff members total). After interviewing these candidates, a "top two" was
unanimously agreed upon by the interviewing committee, and an additional interview with
the City Manager and Assistant City Manager were performed. After I interviewed their
top candidate, lagreed with the interview panel, the City Manager, and Assistant City
Manager, and offered the position of Administrative Services Director to Mariah Hill.

Mariah's appointment is based on her high level of education, extensive background in
government finance, and strong technical skills. She received a Master of Public
Administration (MPA) from the University of Washington and has worked in municipal
government for six years. ln particular, the last five and a half years were at the City of
Vineyard, where she had extensive involvement in the City's budget, financials, and
Redevelopment Agency. ln fact, she performed many of the functions required of the
Finance Director (who is currently also the City Manager), with the only finance functions
not performed by Mariah being those that require a separation of duties. The City of
Vineyard has also received the Government Finance Officers Association's Distinguished
Budget Award.

The interview committee, City Manager, Assistant City Manager, and myself feel Mariah
is a proven leader and would be a great fit for the Administrative Services Department
and will be a valuable addition to the City and the community.
Mariah will be available at the City Council meeting to answer any questions as part of
the advice and consent process.
