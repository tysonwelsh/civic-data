    NOTICE OF OGDEN PLANNING COMMISSION PUBLIC HEARING

The Ogden City Planning Commission will hold public hearings on Wednesday,
November 6, 2024, at 6:00 pm in the City Council Chambers, 3rd Floor, Ogden Municipal
Building, 2549 Washington Boulevard, in Ogden, Utah to consider the following:

      Zone change at 2869 Grant Ave. from CP-3 (Regional Commercial) to R-4 /CO
       (Multiple-family residential with conditional overlay)
      Zone change at 150 32nd St. from CP-3 (Regional Commercial) to R-4 /CO
       (Multiple-family residential with conditional overlay)
      Zone change at 397 & 399 Harrisville Rd. from R-2 (Two-family residential) to R-2S
       R-2S (Two-family and Small Lot Residential)
The proposals may be viewed at the Ogden Municipal Building, 2549 Washington
Boulevard, during regular business hours, or on the city’s website
planningnotices.ogdencity.com. Anyone wishing to comment on the proposals may speak
at the hearing. This meeting will also be held electronically.
Any person or persons interested in these items may participate in person or electronically
by following the instructions at:
