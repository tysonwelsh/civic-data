SLVLESA Proposed Tax Increase Presentation

                Rachel Anderson, Legal Counsel, Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service
Area (SLVLESA), stated the Board of SLVLESA voted to pursue a tax increase of approximately
7 percent. This would put the district at the statutory limit. Utah Code requires SLVLESA to report
to all the municipalities, as well as the County. Due to the passage of HB374, SLVLESA will likely
be spending a portion of its income to contract with the Sheriff for law enforcement services. It
will be losing some economies of scale due to the split and will have to fund two separate
administrations. The increase is a result of this. Due to inflation, costs have increased across
the board. Government entities have less flexibility to respond to inflation due to Truth in Taxation.
To implement the tax increase, all the municipalities will need to vote in favor. Alternatively, the
Salt Lake County Council can vote in favor by a simple majority.

ORDINANCE NO. 2023-11-01                                              DATE: November 15, 2023

   A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP’S TENTATIVE
   2024 BUDGET.

WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality and political subdivision
of the state of Utah; and

WHEREAS, as a metro township, Utah Code § 10-3c-202 requires Copperton to comply with the
Uniform Fiscal Procedures Act of Utah Cities as set forth in Title 10, Chapter 6 of the Utah Code.

WHEREAS, Copperton prepared a budget for the upcoming 2024 fiscal year and desires to adopt
it as its tentative 2024 budget and to schedule a public hearing to take public comment on the
same.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Copperton Metro Township Council:

1) The attached budget is hereby adopted as Copperton’s tentative budget for the 2024 fiscal
   year.

2) The Copperton Council will hold a public hearing on December 5, 2023, at 6:30 p.m. at the
   Bingham Lion’s Club, 8725 W. Hillcrest Street, Copperton, Utah 84006, to receive public
   comments on the tentative 2024 Copperton budget.

3) Notice of the public hearing will be posted pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-113.

4) A copy of this tentative budget shall be made available for public inspection for a period of at
   least ten days prior to the public hearing, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-112.

5) This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 15th day of November, 2023.
