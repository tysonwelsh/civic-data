                             KEARNS CITY COUNCIL

      RESOLUTION NO. R2026-02                            DATE: January 12, 2026

       A RESOLUTION APPOINTING PATRICK SCHAEFFER AS THE
 REPRESENTATIVE APPOINTEE OF THE CITY OF KEARNS TO THE BOARD
   OF DIRECTORS OF THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT OF GREATER
    SALT LAKE AND TO THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE SALT LAKE
  VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA, AND APPOINTING JESSE
   VALDEZ AS THE ALTERNATE REPRESENTATIVE APPOINTEE TO THE
     BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT

       WHEREAS, the City of Kearns is a member entity of the Unified Police Department
of Greater Salt Lake and the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area; and

     WHEREAS, the Kearns City Council (the “Council”) may appoint a Council
Member to the Board of Directors of UPD (“UPD Board”) and the Board of Trustees of
SLVLESA (the “SLVLESA Board”); and

       WHEREAS, former Council Member Al Peterson, whose term on the Council
ended on December 31, 2025, represented Kearns on the UPD Board of Directors; and

       WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint Patrick Schaeffer to replace Council
Member Peterson as Kearns’ representative on the UPD Board and the SLVLESA
Board. The Council also desires to appoint Jesse Valdez as the alternate Kearns’
representative on the UPD Board.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

       1. The Council appoints Patrick Schaeffer as the Kearns representative on the UPD
Board and the SLVLESA Board, and appoints Jesse Valdez as the alternate Kearns
representative to the UPD Board.

       2. Patrick Schaeffer, Jesse Valdez and Kearns staff are authorized to provide UPD and
SLVLESA with a copy of this resolution and to complete and send the attached letter
announcing Patrick Schaeffer and Jesse Valdez’s appointments and notifying UPD and
SLVLESA of their contact information.

       3. The Council thanks Council Member Peterson for his dedicated service on the UPD
Board and SLVLESA Board.
