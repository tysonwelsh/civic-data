                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-33

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN INTERLOCAL
   AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY TO RECEIVE TRCC FUNDING TO
             PLAN A NATURE CENTER AT ROI HARDY PARK

       WHEREAS, Riverton is the owner of park space generally located in close vicinity of the
western bank of the Jordan River, and known as the Roi Hardy Park; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County receives funds pursuant to the Tourism, Recreation,
Cultural, Convention, and Airport Facilities Tax Act, Utah Code Ann §§ 59-12-601 et seq.
(“TRCC Funds”); and

       WHEREAS, Riverton staff has requested and obtained approval to receive TRCC Funds
from Salt Lake County toward the planning of a nature center at Roi Hardy Park that will provide
education, awareness, and stewardship of the Jordan River ecosystem; and

       WHREAS, Salt Lake County has approved the distribution of TRCC Funds to Riverton
under the terms of an Interlocal Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that acceptance of the TRCC
Funds, under the terms of the Interlocal Agreement offered by Salt Lake County, will enhance the
quality of life for Riverton Residents by promoting appreciation through education and awareness
of the Jordan River ecosystem.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

1. Interlocal Agreement. The Riverton City Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to execute with Salt
   Lake County an Interlocal Agreement in substantially similar form to the Agreement attached as
   Exhibit A, after the same is approved as to form by the Riverton City Attorney.

2. Effective Date, Termination. This Resolution shall become effective immediately.

        PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah this 21st day of April
2020 by the following vote:
