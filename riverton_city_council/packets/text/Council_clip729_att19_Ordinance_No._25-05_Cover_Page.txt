                          Ordinance No. 25-05 - Joseph White Text Amendment, Proposed
                          Amendment to Section 18.90.010, Table of Commercial Uses to allow for a
ITEM:
                          Reception Center as a Conditional Use in the C-N Zone. Applicant- Joseph
                          White - Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director

SUMMARY:

This is a public hearing for a requested Zoning Text Change to Chapter 18.90 of the Riverton City Code.
  This application was brought by a property owner who owns property in Riverton City which is zoned C-
N (Commercial Neighborhood). The applicant is requesting a change to the City’s Table of Commercial
Uses found in Chapter 18.90 of Riverton City Code. The table lists several commercial uses and
designates these uses as P- Permitted, C- Conditional, or NP- Not Permitted. One of the uses listed is
“Reception Center" which is currently not a permitted use in the C-N zone. The applicant is a requesting
the Table of Commercial Uses be modified to make “Reception Center” a Conditional Use in the C-N
Zone.

RECOMMENDED MOTION OR ACTION:

On January 9, 2025, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to consider this text amendment
application. The Commission at that hearing voted to recommend denial of the proposed text
amendment. The Commission recommended the following motion:

I move the City Council DENY Ordinance 25-05, Joseph White Text Amendment” proposed amendment
to the Riverton City Table of Commercial Uses such that the category “Reception Center” becomes
Conditional in the Commercial Neighborhood (C-N) Zone.
