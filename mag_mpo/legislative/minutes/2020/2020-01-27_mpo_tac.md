Springville Corridor Preservation Request – Jim Price, MAG Springville is attempting to acquire undeveloped ground from Utah Power and Light (UP&L) for sections of the 1200 W project. Because portions of the parcel contain ROW for both a funded and an unfunded project, the city has asked UP&L to divide the parcel so that Corridor Preservation Funds can be used to purchase the appropriate portion, and project funds the remaining when those are available. UP&L is refusing to divide the parcel and is willing to sell only as a whole. Project funds will be available in 2021, but the city feels it necessary to purchase the property now and is asking TAC to allow the Corridor Preservation Fund to pay for the portion of the parcel that lies outside the funded project. The portion inside the funded project will be paid for by the city, to be reimbursed later by the project. Jim stated that the numbers have changed since the staff report was sent out. The parcel is 5.86 acres, with the portion eligible for CP funds at approximately 3.5 acres (60% of the total area). The appraised value is $104,095 per acre (updated cost); therefore, the city is requesting $364,332 (updated cost) for the land and 60% of the estimated closing costs of $5000, or $3000, for a total of $367,332 (updated cost). Ken Anson moved to recommend to the Regional Planning Committee approval of this request from Springville City for a total of $367,332 from the Corridor Preservation Fund. Richard Nielson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. UVU Area Presentation – Tim Hereth, MAG The area around UVU was recently studied and a prioritized transportation plan was compiled to provide feasible multi-modal transportation solutions. An overview of the process and outcome was given. Realignment and improvement projects were discussed to be recommended to add to the TIP. The study can be found online at uvuareaplan.com TransPlan50 RTP Amendments – Shawn Eliot, MAG Every four years, MAG is required to prepare and adopt a Regional Transportation Plan. The current plan was approved in June 2019. The Utah Valley University (UVU) Area Plan MPO Technical Advisory Committee January 27, 2020 Page 4 was completed soon after proposing numerous regional projects. UDOT has requested that the following changes be amended into TransPlan50: Phase 1 | 2019-2030 • Remove project #31 | I-15/Orem 800 S | New Interchange • Add I-15/Orem Center ST | Ramp & Lane Improvements | $3m • Add I-15 Orem 800 S/Campus DR; Geneva RD to UVU | New 5 Lane Road/I-15 Bridge | $80m • Add I-15/University PKWY | Grade Separated Off-ramp | $24m Phase 3 | 2041-2050 • Add University PKWY/Sandhill RD | New Interchange | $60m The removal of project 31 coupled with a surplus of planned funding in the plan, allow for all the proposed projects to be funded in the phases proposed. UVU Pedestrian Bridge Project Update – Eric Rasband, UDOT Construction of the pedestrian bridge over I-15 between the FrontRunner Station and UVU has begun and is scheduled to be completed fall 2020. The project design and construction were made possible with a partnership between UDOT, UVU, MAG, Orem City, and UTA. The bridge cost approximately $30m and will be one-quarter mile long and sixteen feet wide. In addition, the bridge will have an elevator on each side, bike track, and will be heated and covered to keep snow and ice off the walkway.
