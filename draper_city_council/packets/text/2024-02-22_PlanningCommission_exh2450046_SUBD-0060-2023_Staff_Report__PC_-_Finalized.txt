BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for a
commercial condominium on approximately 0.78 acres located between State Street and
150 E. and South of Kimballs Ln., at approximately 96 E. Kimballs Ln. (Exhibits B and C). The
property is currently zoned CR. The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat
Amendment be approved to allow for first floor condominium unit boundaries to
incorporate adjacent internal common area into the condominium unit.

The Lone Peak Medical Center Subdivision was recorded January 11, 2016 subdividing the
property on which the Lone Peak Hospital sits into 3 lots. Lots 2 and 3 of that plat do not

Lone Peak Medical Center Subdivision plat contains the Lone Peak Specialty Center building
which is a four-floor medical office building. Lot 2 of the Lone Peak Medical Center
Subdivision was then amended March 13, 2016 to create the Lone Peak Specialty Centre
Condominium Plat. The Lone Peak Specialty Centre Condominium Plat was then amended
on August 10, 2018 to divide unit 406 into two units, unit 409, and unit 410, and again in
June 2021 to divide unit 308 into units 309 and 310. This application represents the third
amendment to the Lone Peak Specialty Centre Condominium Plat.
