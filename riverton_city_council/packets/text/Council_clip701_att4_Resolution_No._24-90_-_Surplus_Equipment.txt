                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 24-90

  A RESOLUTION TO DECLARE THE IDENTIFIED EQUIPMENT AS SURPLUS,
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO DISPOSE OF THIS ITEM AS HE DEEMS
                         APPROPRIATE

       WHEREAS, the Ford transit van was involved in an accident and has been totaled by the
insurance company; and

       WHEREAS, the Ford F-150 was a vehicle we received from UPD and is no longer
needed; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets as surplus in
a public meeting before discarding.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council declares the following equipment as surplus, authorizing
          the City Manager to dispose of them as he deems appropriate

          RW2698 - 2018 Ford Transit Van, Vin #1FTYE1ZM8JKA32698
          RPD8113 - 2018 Ford F-150, Vin #1FTEW1EGXJKC58113

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 1st day of
October, 2024 by the following vote:
