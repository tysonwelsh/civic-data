Request
 The applicant, Michelle Martin of Ford & Associates
 Architects, with permission from the property owners,
 Clark & Orfalea Investments, LLC, requests approval of a
 conditional use permit and a modified site plan for a
 property located at 10291 S. State St. The conditional use
 permit request is to allow for 24-hour operation of a
 veterinary office. The modified site plan request is for an
 outdoor pet relief area at the rear (east side) of the building
 in the Central Business District (CBD) zone (See Exhibit
 A and B).

Background
 The subject project is located to the east of State Street, a
 major arterial road. The building is an 11,000 square foot
 single story multi-tenant building that was originally
 constructed in 1994. There are three tenants occupying the
 building, including MedVet Urgent Care veterinary office.
 MedVet Urgent Care uses 3,549 square feet of space
 within the building.

 Property directly north, west, and south are commercial
 buildings zoned CBD. Properties to the east are single
 family homes zoned R-1-20A.

Analysis
 Business Operation
 The business currently operates as a veterinary urgent care facility and is proposing to
 extend their veterinary services to emergency care. The emergency care would expand
 to include overnight kenneling of animals. They are proposing to operate 24 hours a
 day, seven days a week. Sec. 21-23-19 of the Sandy City Land Development Code
 states that any commercial use located within 250 feet of a residential district where such commercial use desires to
 operate after 10:00 p.m. and/or before 6:00 a.m. shall require a separate conditional use approval from the Planning
 Commission. It is important to note that this request would apply to the entire building and not just this specific space
 within the building.

 Modified Site Plan
 The applicant is proposing exterior modifications to create a designated outdoor pet relief area on the building’s east side.
 The proposed pet relief area, intended for use by individual patients and under supervision, will cover 92 square feet. It
 will be enclosed by a fence and feature artificial turf as ground covering. A canopy will provide overhead coverage. Waste
 disposal will be directly connected to the sewer line.

 Parking
 There are three businesses that use the building and there are 52 shared parking spaces. On the east side of the building
 there are four parking spaces and a loading zone. The pet relief area extends out into the loading zone space. The
 applicant is proposing to adjust the loading
 zone and parking spaces around the pet relief
 area and maintain those four parking spaces
 (See exhibit B). Existing and revised vehicle
 parking will be adequate for the expanded use.

     Staff Concerns
     Staff does not have any concerns with the proposed project.

   Recommendations
#1 Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional use Permit for 24 hour operation for MedVet
   Emergency Hospital located at 10291 S. State St. based on the following findings and subject to the following conditions:

   Findings:
      1. The proposed use meets the intent of the CBD zone.

       2. The proposed use should have minimal to no impact on the surrounding uses due to the required mitigation
          measures.

   Conditions:
      1. That no outside activity between 10 p.m. and 6 a.m. that would create a large amount of noise be allowed (including
          but not limited to lot sweeping, trash collection, and store deliveries). That each tenant shall restrict employee
          activity behind the building adjacent to residential property during those hours as well.

       2. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City Development Code, and all
          conditions of approval imposed by the Planning Commission.

       3. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.

       4. That this Conditional Use Permit be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.

#2 Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine a modified site plan review is complete for MedVet
   Emergency Hospital located at 10291 S. State St. based on the following findings and subject to the following conditions:

   Findings:
      1. The proposed project is consistent with previous approvals for the site.

       2. An outdoor pet relief area is properly located on the site and mitigated by the proposed and conditioned
          requirements.
