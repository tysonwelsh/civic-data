                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 24-57

   A RESOLUTION APPROVING A BUSINESS SERVICES AGREEMENT WITH THE
                SOUTH VALLEY CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

        WHEREAS, the South Valley Chamber of Commerce is a non-profit organization created to
assist businesses in building their business through networking opportunities, marketing, education,
and assisting with government entities; and

     WHEREAS, the City needs the services of a person or agency to assist the City in
communicating with and supporting the Riverton business community; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton Council finds and determines that the Chamber has the skills and
resources to provide an interface between the City and the Riverton business community; and

         WHEREAS, the City, having duly considered the matter, has determined that the welfare of
the citizens of the City as well as the growth, development and continued progress of the City would
be enhanced if the Chamber were to promote the Riverton City businesses, and economic development
in the City, as well as City programs and events as provided herein,

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

       1. The Business Services Agreement attached to this Resolution as Exhibit A is hereby
          approved. The Mayor is hereby authorized to sign the Agreement upon verification that
          the same has been approved as to form by the Riverton City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of June,
2024 by the following vote:
