ALDERATIONS LLC – 388 EAST 4800 SOUTH - MAJOR HOME OCCUPATION

Zachary Smallwood presented the application for requesting Major Home Occupation approval
at 388 East 4800 South located within the R-M-10 Zone. Homebased businesses are allowed in
the zone. Major Home Occupations are usually handled administratively. They can get
approval from planning commission, if unable to obtain the needed signatures. One vehicle and
one trailer may be parked on a cement pad, to support home and business. The applicant has
indicated he will not have any employees. Hours of operation are Monday through Friday 8
a.m. to 6 p.m. No clients or subcontractors will be coming to the home business. Neighborhood
impact – one work truck and one 6' x 10' trailer.

Mr. Pehrson asked Mr. Alder if he read the conditions of the permit. Matthew Alder stated he
has read the conditions of approval and will comply with the conditions.

MOFFAT FARMS - 6190, 6270, 6288, 6294, 6304, and 6334 South Moffat Farm Lane -
Preliminary & Final Subdivision Approval

Mr. Smallwood presented the application for preliminary and final subdivision approval. The
existing properties are located immediately east of Wheeler Farm, along a private street (Moffat
Farm Lane) that is accessed off of Vine Street. The properties are owned by Claudia Luttrel and
her family. Ms. Luttrel would like to do some clean-up of these property lines and make it more
streamlined. Current property lines cut through homes/lots.

The proposal is a single-family residential subdivision with a total of seven (7) lots along a
private street. The current area is comprised of nine (9) parcels that vary in size between 0.03
acres to 5.42 acres. The applicant would like to clean up the property lines that they own and
would consolidate the properties. The new seven (7) lot subdivision is purely a clean-up project;
no new development is proposed with the changes. The new lots will conform with the
requirements of the A-1 zoning district where the existing lots did not.

The application before the Planning Commission is for Preliminary & Final Subdivision approval.
If the Planning Commission approves the subdivision the applicant will work with the
Engineering Division to prepare a Final Plat. Once the Final Plat has been accepted by the City
Engineer, it will be circulated for signatures of approval which will allow the recordation of the
plat at the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office.

Both the Engineering and Planning Divisions have determined that because there is no possible
way to provide a public street or an ability to develop additional property at this location, we feel
confident in recommending that the exception be applied to this application. These properties
are not in a platted subdivision currently and could have been addressed as a boundary line
adjustment; however, because of the complexity of the changes to be cleaned up staff
determined it would be better and cleaner to take the changes through the subdivision process
by applying this exception. This will allow for the properties to come into conformance with
existing codes and clean up property lines that have been subdivided prior to incorporation into
Murray City.

It is important to note that this exception is not lightly invoked and applied by staff in this or any
other case. With this collection of existing homes, no public road can be created for access and
there are additional constraints to the property that would exclude further development. We are
confident that this exception could and would not be applied in any other manner on this
property.

The applicant approached the podium and was asked if she read and could comply with
conditions. She stated that she could.

LAND USE ORDINANCE TEXT AMENDMENT - Section 17.78.050.J Detached ADU's

Mr. Smallwood presented (on behalf of Susan Nixon). Peggy Imai is requesting an update to
Chapter 17.78.050(J), changing the minimum lot area of 12, 000 square feet (.275 acre) to
10,000 square feet (.23 acre) required for Detached Accessory Dwelling Units. Section
17.78.050(J) states: "The minimum lot size required for construction of a detached ADU in all
single-family residential zones shall be twelve thousand (12,000) square feet." The request is to
reduce the minimum area required from 12,000 square feet to 10,000 square feet. Staff has
been considering other amendments for detached ADUs such as setbacks and maximum
square footage for a detached ADU but has not fully researched these other changes and any
potential impacts. Therefore, staff is not recommending other changes at this time.

The proposed request will potentially allow more property owners the ability to have detached
ADU's. The Planning Division does not anticipate adverse impacts to the community as a result
of the proposed change. The proposed amended ordinance will continue to regulate accessory
dwelling units and potentially allow additional properties the ability to have a detached dwelling
unit.

Based on the background, staff review, and the findings in this report, Staff recommends that
the Planning Commission forward a recommendation of approval to the City Council for the
request to amend the text of Chapter 17.78.050(J), Detached Accessory Dwelling Units, to
state: The minimum lot size required for construction of a detached ADU in all single-family
residential zones shall be ten thousand (10,000) square feet.

Ms. Imai stated she recently purchased the property at 451 East Vine Street and would like to
build a single-family dwelling with detached accessory dwelling unit. Her property is just shy of
the 12,000 sq.ft. minimum area requirement.

Mr. Pehrson opened for public comment. None came forward. The public comment for this
item was closed.

Ms. Patterson made the motion to send a positive recommendation to the city council for the
Land Use Text Amendment to Section 17.78.050.J. reducing the minimum area requirement for
detached accessory dwelling units from 12,000 sq.ft. to 10,000 sq.ft. Seconded by Mr.
Richards.
