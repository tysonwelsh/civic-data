Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District

                Pam Roberts, General Manager, Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District
(WFWRD), stated due to shortages in CDL drivers, the area cleanup program was changed to
the Seasonal Container Reservation Program (SCRP). Residents can reserve a container and
have it delivered to their home in the driveway. At this point, it does not appear that WFWRD will
                                                 1
DATE            TUESDAY                                  JUNE                             14, 2022

be able to go back to the previous cleanup program. There are other programs, in addition to
SCRP, that residents can use to have bulk waste picked up, including, renting a trailer and dump
vouchers. The SCRP was available to Magna residents from May 2 -16.

She has heard a lot of conversations about code enforcement. WFWRD can provide additional
cleanup options for Magna. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) will need to
designate funds to have a trailer or container delivered to the home with the violations.

Webster Community Center Ownership and Lease Issues

                Council Member Barney stated the Council is working on a draft lease agreement
between the Metro Township and the Webster Community Center. He is concerned that there is
not a written lease agreement with specific terms. There are questions regarding access to the
center and if the Council leaves the center what will happen with equipment that it has brought in.
He would like an agreement that has clear boundaries and expectations.

               Brint Peel, Trustee, Webster Community Center, stated it tries to accommodate
every request. There is a process in place to schedule regular meetings where the Council would
not have to schedule it every month. He will take the Councils concerns to the board and discuss
them.

4th of July Celebration

               Kari Duckworth stated currently there are 53 entries for the 4th of July
celebrations. She has posted information about the need for volunteers as there are only 10
currently. T-shirts have been ordered for those that volunteer. Food trucks, food vendors, and
non-food vendors have been arranged. There will be a rock band, all ages talent show, DJ,
obstacle course and other entertainment. She is waiting to hear back on the details for the firework
show. Costs are staying within the budget. June 21 is the regular meeting as well as a volunteer
meeting.

Unified Police Department Report

               Chief Del Craig stated he is four officers short; however, two individuals have
recently graduated from the academy, and one officer is a lateral hire that will be on the street
soon. In conjunction with Unified Fire Authority (UFA), UPD has been participating in active school
shooting training. While school is out UPD will be doing infrastructure assessments. Granite
School District has a policy where all officers have keys to the school; however, as a back-up
plan, UPD is trying to get keys for its officers.

Citizen Public Input

                Kerri Bauer stated she is concerned with a property that has had prior code
enforcement issues. At one point she was able to enjoy being outdoors, but an individual has
moved back in, and the animals were brought back, there are 8-foot-tall weeds, bug infestation,
and the smell has come back. The individual refuses to do anything about it and refuses to provide
the landlords contact information. She has not received a response from code enforcement, and
they are in the same situation as before.

She is also concerned with the lack of police enforcement for parking issues in the neighborhood.
There are vehicles parked in the driveways and streets. She is concerned that these issues are
not being addressed and wants to make sure there is follow through and consistency. She hopes
that there is a recourse for repeat offenders in the planning for Magna Metro Township.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
               Mark Elieson stated he spoke to a code enforcement officer that was getting
information about various problems in the neighborhood. Currently there is only one officer for all
of Magna. There are a lot of code enforcement cases in Magna and one officer is not enough.
What needs to be done to get the MSD to hire additional officers that will cite problem areas.
There are three houses within a block of his home that are disastrous. The code enforcement
officer said that the property owner had been contacted and were going to take care of the
property but that was two months ago and there has not been anything done. He has tried
contacting the code enforcement supervisor and there has been no action.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
                Mick Sudbury stated since there is only one code enforcement officer, could
Magna Metro Township contract with West Valley City for code enforcement services. Residents
pay for this service, if they are not going to receive service, then the money needs to be given
back. He has tried contacting code enforcement regarding issues in his neighborhood and does
not ever get a call back. He would like the Council to look into hiring its own code enforcement
officer or contract it out.

Public Hearings

Ordinance No. 2022-O-04

            THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to receive public
comment regarding the following request:

Ordinance 2022-O-04 repealing of Title 19, Chapter 55 of the municipal code and replacing it with
a new downtown historic mixed-use zoning district.

               Council Member Hull, seconded by Council Member Prokopis, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

              No one appeared in favor or opposition.

              Council Member Barney, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to approve
the following Ordinance with amendments. The motion passed 4 to 1, showing that Council
Member Pierce voted in opposition.

ORIDNANCE NO. 2022-O-04                                                     Date: June 14, 2022

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL REPEALING
       TITLE 19, CHAPTER 55 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE AND REPLACING IT WITH
       A NEW DOWNTOWN HISTORIC MIXED-USE ZONING DISTRICT

                                          RECITALS

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-2a-414(3) provides that a Salt Lake County ordinance
will remain in effect as the ordinance of the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) “until the metro
township council amend or repeals the ordinance;” and

        WHEREAS, Title 19 of the Magna Metro Township Code (the “Code”) governs zoning
within Magna; and



                                               4
DATE           TUESDAY                                 JUNE                             14, 2022

        WHEREAS, because Salt Lake County originally adopted Title 19 prior to the incorporation
of the Magna Metro Township, Title 19 as currently constituted is not specific to Magna and does
not account for the metro township of municipal government; and

        WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council directed the Magna Metro Township
Planning Commission and staff to start working on “proposed amendments to the zoning and
code for properties on Magna Main Street to bring it into alignment with the long term goals of
that historic district” raised in an email on February 17th, 2022; and

       WHEREAS, the Magna planning and development staff discussed the issues with the
Magna Metro Township Planning Commission and Council at a Workshop on April 26th, 2022,
and created a draft ordnance; and

        WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Planning Commission held a public hearing on
the draft ordinance at their meeting on June 9, 2022; and

       WHEREAS, after holding a public hearing pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-90-502, the
Magna Metro Township Planning Commission recommended that the Council adopt the draft
ordinance prepared by the planning and development staff with revisions as incorporated; and

        WHEREAS, the Council has reviewed the ordinance and determined that it is in the best
interest of Magna and its citizens.

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
that the previous Title 19, Chapter 55 is repealed and the attached ordinance is adopted and will
become effective upon publication pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-711(1) and Utah Code
Ann. § 10-3-712.

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 14th day of June 2022.
