                                    RIVERTON, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 25-22

    A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE THE FIRST
  AMENDMENT TO AGREEMENT BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND SALT LAKE
       COUNTY FOR WATER SERVICE AT RIVERBEND GOLF COURSE

        WHEREAS, Riverton is the owner and operator of waterworks providing intake and
distribution of culinary grade water for distribution to residences, businesses, and community
centers; and

     WHEREAS, Salt Lake County owns and operates the Riverbend Golf Course and
community pool at the approximate location of 12800 South 1040 West; and

      WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has a continual need to obtain culinary grade water for
consumption, recreation, and irrigation purposes at the Riverbend Golf Course; and

       WHEREAS, under the terms of an existing interlocal agreement, Riverton City has
supplied Salt Lake County with water needed for the Riverbend Golf Course at discounted rates
to support the community need for recreation supplied by Salt Lake County; and

       WHEREAS, the existing agreement with Salt Lake County to supply culinary water to the
Riverbend Golf Course needs to be amended due to delays in the improvement of the Green Well;
and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the City remains
committed to providing culinary water to Salt Lake County for the Riverbend Golf Course, and
recognizes the necessity of updating the yearly water fee rates to account for the delays in
improving the Green Well to ensure adequate water supply diversification; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the First Amendment to
Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake County for Water Service for Riverbend Golf
Course updates the rate schedule for water supplied to the County; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County and Riverton are each authorized to enter into this
Agreement to provide water service, pursuant to the Interlocal Cooperation Act of Utah, Utah Code
Ann. §§ 11-13-101 et seq.; and

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
1. The Riverton City Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to execute with Salt Lake County a
   First Amendment to Water Service Agreement at Riverbend Golf Course in substantially
   similar form to the Agreement attached hereto, after the same is approved as to form by the
   Riverton City Attorney.

2. Effective Date, Termination. This Resolution shall become effective immediately.
