                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 23-19

      A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL OBJECTING TO
    IMPLEMENTATION OF “ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL AND GOVERNANCE”
                  POLICIES IN THE FINANCE SECTOR

      WHEREAS, the Utah Legislature has introduced Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 9
(“SCR 9”) On February 21, 2023; and

       WHEREAS, SCR 9 states, among other things, that “environmental, social, and
governance (“ESG”) investing is an investment approach that considers ESG factors in the
investment decision-making process; and

       WHEREAS, ESG investing encourages investment managers to invest in companies that
meet certain ESG standards and priorities; and

        WHEREAS, ESG investing priorities are subjective and change substantially based on
political agendas; and

        WHEREAS, ESG investing outlines societal goals without appropriately considering the
costs associated with ESG's coercive nature; and

         WHEREAS, ESG investing pursues political objectives without the appropriate
checks, balances, and accountability present in the legislative bodies created to address these
issues; and

         WHEREAS, ESG investing attempts to use the allocation of capital to punish or reward
entities to enforce compliance with and commitment to the social issues promoted by ESG; and

        WHEREAS, investment managers have a fiduciary obligation to focus solely on the
financial interests of their clients; and

       WHEREAS, ESG investing promotes social issues and political agendas even
though such investing may result in less favorable financial outcomes; and

       WHEREAS, statements or commitments to promote ESG goals are evidence of motive
to promote non-financial purposes; and

        WHEREAS, ESG investing may compromise an investment manager's fiduciary duty
by placing social issues above the financial interests of their clients; and
        WHEREAS, many individuals do not have the ability to decide where to invest
their savings for retirement; and

        WHEREAS, the state attorney general, the state treasurer, and the state auditor have
stated publicly that ESG investing is putting individual's retirement savings at risk;” and

        WHEREAS, John Skjervem Chief Investment Officer of the Utah Retirement Systems -
which funds the retirement pensions of City employees- has argued ESG-driven investment
policies would interfere with earning returns which support retirement programs of its members;
and

       WHEREAS, Mr. Skjervem has publicly stated, “From a place of humility, not ideology,
we are deliberately allocating across a mosaic of energy investments because we don’t know
exactly how the transition will evolve and play out;” and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that agrees with the
philosophical approach to ESG policies, as expressed by Mr. Skjervem and SCR 9;

        WHEREAS, Riverton City should avoid doing business with financial advisors who are
committed to advance, or market themselves as advancing, social or environmental goals with
client assets;

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Riverton City Council,
categorically objects to any ESG ratings, ESG credit indicators, or any other ESG scoring system
that calls out ESG factors separate from, in addition to, or apart from traditional credit ratings.

        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that Riverton City take immediate action to direct its
financial advisor(s) to work with investment managers which are committed to focus solely on
financial interests.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 7th day of
March 2023 by the following vote:
