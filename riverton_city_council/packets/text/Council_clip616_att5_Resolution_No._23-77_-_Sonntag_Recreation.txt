                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-77

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A PURCHASE ORDER BE ISSUED TO SONNTAG
 RECREATION TO PURCHASE A PAVILION FOR THE CITY HALL BACK PLAZA
                            PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, City staff intended to purchase a pavilion direct form the vendor; and

       WHEREAS, in order to have the pavilion here in time for the completion of the project it
needs to be ordered now; and

        WHEREAS, if approved, Council will need to approve $50,000.00 as an added expense
in a future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council authorizes a Purchase Order be issued to Sonntag Recreation to
          purchase a Pavilion for the City Hall Back Plaza Project.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 3rd day of
October 2023 by the following vote:
