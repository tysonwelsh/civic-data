                                      CITY OF LOGAN, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 26-12

    AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING A TEMPORARY LAND USE REGULATION
 PROHIBITING THE DEVELOPMENT AND CONSTRUCTION OF DATA CENTERS
                  AND DATA CENTER POWER PLANTS

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-20-504 authorizes municipalities, without prior
consideration or recommendation from the planning commission, to enact an ordinance
establishing a temporary land use regulation for any part or all of the area within the municipality;

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that data centers and data center power plants are
currently unregulated in Logan City’s ordinances;

        WHEREAS, the City Council desires to protect the public health, safety and welfare of
Logan City by preventing potentially incompatible or inadequately regulated development during
the period of study and ordinance preparation; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the adoption of a temporary land use ordinance is
a reasonable and necessary measure to preserve the status quo and allow the City sufficient time
to evaluate and prepare appropriate standards and regulations applicable to data centers and data
center power plants.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MUNICIPAL COUNCIL OF THE
CITY OF LOGAN, UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       SECTION 1. Definitions. As used in this ordinance, the following words, terms and
phrases shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this section:

        “Application” means any request for City approval, permit, authorization, or entitlement related
to land use or Construction, including but not limited to zoning approvals, conditional use permits, site plan
approvals, subdivision approvals, building permits, grading permits, and any similar approvals required by
the City.

       “Construction” includes any site disturbance, grading, excavation, building,
reconstruction, erection, placement, installation, or assembly of structures or improvements, and
any substantial physical alteration of land or structures.

        “Data Center” means a facility, campus, structure, building, or portion of a building or
structure that is primarily used to house computer systems and associated components, including
servers, data storage systems, network equipment and supporting infrastructure such as power
distribution equipment, backup generators, battery energy storage systems, cooling systems, and
related mechanical and electrical equipment, where the principal purpose is the processing,
storage, transmission or hosting of digital data.

        “Data Center Power Plant” means a utility-scale commercial facility that produces electric
power for consumption by a Data Center, which may store, produce, transmit and/or distribute
electric power by any combination of energy storage, solar energy, thermal energy or other power
conversion technology, including from hydrocarbon-based fuels, geothermal wells, wind energy
systems, or nuclear fuel systems, for the primary purpose of supplying power to the Data Center.

        “Development” includes Construction and any change in the use of land or structures to
establish, expand, intensify, or operate a Data Center, whether as a principal use or accessory use.

 SECTION 2. Temporary Land Use Regulation (Moratorium) Established. The City hereby
imposes a temporary land use regulation (moratorium) for a period of one hundred eighty (180)
days from the effective date of this ordinance. Logan City shall not accept, process, approve, or
issue any application, permit or approval that would authorize the Construction or Development
of a Data Center or Data Center Power Plant. No person shall commence the Construction or
Development of a Data Center or Data Center Power Plant within the City.

SECTION 3. Severability. If any provision of this ordinance, or its application to any person or
circumstance, is declared invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect
the validity of the remainder of this ordinance, which shall remain in full force and effect.

SECTION 4: Effective Date and Expiration. This ordinance shall become effective
immediately upon passage and shall automatically expire at the end of the 180-day term unless
terminated earlier by the City Council by ordinance.
