Action / Discussion Items

             WVR2023-000924 – Ben Hansen, Ivory Homes, is requesting an exception from
development standards for the Gabler’s Grove Phase 5 Subdivision for 2.71 acres located at
7688 West Blawn Wash Lane.

                                                 2
DATE            TUESDAY                                AUGUST                              22, 2023

               Jeff Miller, Planner, Municipal Services District, stated this is a request from Ivory
Homes Development. The request pertains to an exception for Gabler’s Grove Phase 5
Subdivision. The current design includes an 18-foot-wide driveway to accommodate two vehicles,
deviating from the typical 10-foot spacing between driveways. This design choice was intended
to address the narrower townhomes, resulting in approximately 4.5 feet of space between
driveways.

This request was reviewed by the Planning Commission on August 10th which recommended
approval for the special exception. Magna Metro Township’s planning staff has approved the
exception for driveways, subject to the condition that the applicant collaborates with the Municipal
Services District’s (MSD) staff and external review agencies to ensure compliance with the final
procedures associated with the development.

              Ben Hansen, Ivory Homes Representative, stated this request exception is to align
with what has been previously approved for other townhome phases within the Gabler’s Grove
community.

              Council Member Barney asked if these previous exceptions were requested and
approved by the Council or is it part of the development agreement.

                Mr. Hansen stated the exceptions were not included in the development
agreement and may not have been addressed by the Magna Metro Township Council. He
inherited the project from Brian Prince, who had previously attended Council meetings and will
return to discuss a similar exception for another townhome phase. The development agreement
prohibits parking on driveways with a depth of less than 5 feet, but additional parking stalls were
added to alleviate the parking issues.

               Council Member Hull asked if Gabler’s Grove had a Homeowners Association
(HOA).

               Mr. Hansen stated it does have an HOA and will communicate with the HOA group
to address the parking issues to prevent further parking-related problems.

               Council Member Barney stated the trend in new developments is to have
enforcement responsibility shift to the HOA, but if the HOA fails to address the issues, then it
would ultimately become the Council’s responsibility. He suggested postponing further
deliberation on this matter until the next meeting.

                Council Member Barney, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to postpone
a decision until September 26, 2023. The motion passed unanimously.

               EXP2023-000937 – Ben Hansen, Ivory Homes is requesting a special exception
for the roadway standard for the Mahogany Ridge Phase 1 Planned Unit Development (PUD)
Subdivision for 4.26 acres, located at 8230 West 4100 South.

                                                 3
DATE           TUESDAY                                AUGUST                             22, 2023

               Jeff Miller, Planner, Municipal Services District (MSD), stated this special
exception request is for Phase 1 of the Mahogany Ridge Planned Unit Development (PUD). This
78.48-acre community is in a Planned Community (PC) Zone. Ivory Homes is seeking the Magna
Metro Township Council’s approval for roadway standards and additional street parking. The
proposal involves reducing the park strip width from the standard 5 feet to 4.5 feet, aligning with
Unified Fire Authority’s (UFA) requirement for a 32-foot paved width on curves to accommodate
parking on both sides. This adjustment is consistent with the existing development agreement
and approved by the Salt Lake County’s Public Works Engineering Division. The MSD planning
staff recommended approval of the proposed special exception for roadway standards.

                Nick Mango stated during the previous meeting in March or April, concerns were
raised regarding the narrow road width and shallow driveways in Gabler’s Grove, which could
lead to larger vehicles encroaching on sidewalks, potentially causing conflicts between cars and
pedestrians. Upon investigation, it was determined that the drivable road surface measures 28
feet, with an additional 2-foot gutter on each side. In total, the 32-foot width meets the UFA
requirement for parking on both sides of the street. It was reviewed and approved by the UFA and
Salt Lake County’s Public Works Engineering Division.

              Council Member Barney asked about the purpose of the one-foot space behind
the sidewalk between properties.

                Mr. Miller stated the one-foot space is the homeowner’s property for later
maintenance accessibility. He suggested reducing the parking strip by 5 feet to accommodate on-
street parking, which the Magna Metro Township Council had deliberated on.

               Council Member Hull, seconded by Council Member Pierce, moved to approve the
special exception for the Mahogany Ridge Subdivision. The motion passed unanimously.

               PUD2022-000659 – Ben Hansen, Ivory Homes is requesting an amendment to the
PC Plan for the Mahogany Ridge Planned Community. The development is 78.48 acres, located
at 8230 West 4100 South.

                 Jeff Miller, Planner, Municipal Services District (MSD), stated this amendment
aims to facilitate the future intersection of Cordero Drive and the truck ramp in coordination with
the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT). Mayor Peay was supportive of the removal of
the truck ramp to improve traffic flow and safety. The proposal also includes wider streets,
additional parking, and changes in housing types. The MSD’s planning staff recommends
approval by the Magna Metro Township Council, with the following conditions: 1) the applicant
collaborate with the MSD staff, UDOT, and the Magna Metro Township Council to proceed with
the future line of Cordero Drive to move the truck ramp; 2) any future amendments must be
presented before the Magna Metro Township Council.

               Paul Ashton stated new legislation was passed this year and he would like to
defer the request until he can review the legislation and request.

Resolution 2023-08-01

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting the amended 2023 fee
schedule:

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-08-01                                                 DATE: August 22, 2023

               A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
               ADOPTING THE AMENDED 2023 FEE SCHEDULE

                                           RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township ("Magna") inherited the version of Salt Lake
County’s Code that was in existence on the date of Magna’s incorporation in 2017 pursuant to
Utah Code § 10-2a-414(3); and

        WHEREAS, as a result, Section 19.94.070 of the Magna Metro Township Code (the
“Code”) set forth the civil penalties for zoning violations (the Civil Penalties”) within the Code
rather than within the Magna Fee Schedule; and

      WHEREAS, as part of Magna’s recodification of its Code, the Magna Council (the
“Council”) desires to move the Civil Penalties from Title 19 of the Code to the Magna Fee
Schedule; and

       WHEREAS, when the Council adopted the 2023 Magna Fee Schedule on December 21,
2022, the revisions to Title 19 were still in process and, as a result, the Council adopted the fee
schedule subject to the civil penalties remaining in Title 19 until such time as Title 19 could be
restated to remove the Civil Penalties; and

                                                5
DATE           TUESDAY                             AUGUST                             22, 2023

       WHEREAS, Magna has adopted a final version of Title 19 that does not include the Civil
Penalties; and

       WHEREAS, because Title 19 no longer includes the Civil Penalties, the Council desires
to amend the 2023 Fee Schedule to include the Civil Penalties that were previously set forth in
Section 19.94.070; and

       WHEREAS, the Council also desires to revise language in Section 5.02.060 of the Code
regarding the fees Magna applies to business license withdrawal fees to coincide with the 2023
Fee Schedule.

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
that effectively immediately:

       1. The attached amended 2023 fee schedule is adopted: and

       2. Section 5.02.060.B of the Code is amended to read as follows:

       If, prior to a license being granted or issued, the application for a license is
       withdrawn by the applicant for any reason, the license official shall retain a portion
       of the application fee if authorized to do so in the metro township fee schedule.
       However, the mayor may, in their discretion, authorize the license official to refund
       the entire application fee if they determine a refund is appropriate under the
       circumstances.

       3.    Magna staff are instructed to file this resolution and the attached amended
fee schedule with the Magna Metro Township Clerk; and

      4.     Staff are instructed to post this resolution and the attached amended 2023
fee schedule to the Magna Metro Township website and to the “Municipal Resolutions
Book” within Magna’s Municode page; and

        5.     Pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-719, the amended 2023 fee schedule will go
into effect on the date this resolution is approved and adopted.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED this 22nd day of August, 2023.
