Study Session


         Report: Draper Days 2023
         Kellie Challburg and David Wilks reported on Draper Days 2023 and
         answered questions from the Council. The Council discussed possible
         scheduling changes for future Draper Days celebrations.

         Discussion: Trails and Open Space Approval Process
         Parks and Recreation Director Rhett Ogden expressed a desire to keep the
         Trails Committee invested and make sure each trail project had a purpose.
         He proposed a process for trails and open space project approval. Following
         discussion, a majority of the Council indicated support for the proposed
         process.

         Council/Manager Reports
         Councilmember F. Lowry said he continued to receive questions from
         residents regarding the enforcement of ADU parking issues.



         Draper City Council DRAFT Meeting Minutes – November 7, 2023

5.    Items for Council Consideration
5.a   Public Hearing: Ordinances #1593 and #1594, Ordinances of Draper City
      amending a Master Area Plan design guidelines and amending the text
      of the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal
      Code Section 9-14-110, regarding the Vista 600 West Master Area Plan
      located at approximately 496 West 13490 South


      Draper City Council DRAFT Meeting Minutes – November 7, 2023
     Planner Maryann Pickering explained the request to amend the Master Area
     Plan design guidelines and text of the Draper Land Use and Development
     Code for the Vista 600 West development. The proposed changes would
     eliminate office use, to be replaced with a hotel on the southern boundary,
     retail, and up to 75 residential units. She said the proposed changes to
     design guidelines and Code text were minor and intended to clarify design
     guidelines overall. Ms. Pickering answered questions from the Council.

     Adam Lankford with Wasatch Residential Group, applicant, said the
     additional 70 residential units would not change the overall residential
     maximum for the development.

     Mayor Walker opened a public hearing and closed the public hearing
     seeing no one come forward.

     Councilmember Green moved to approve Ordinances #1593 and #1594.
     Councilmember Vawdrey seconded the motion.

     A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
