REQUEST
Mr. Chris Bick, P.E., representing Kimley-Horn and Associates, is requesting preliminary
Commercial Site Plan Review and Conditional Use Approval for a new commercial
building with a dine in and a drive-up window restaurant use, on the property located
at 10986 S. State Street. The site is currently occupied by the Larry H. Miller Gasoline
Service Station, which will be demolished for this new project. The property is located in
Auto Mall (AM) (Commercial) zoning district. A Conditional Use Permit is required for the
drive-up window restaurant use by the AM (Commercial) zoning district. The proposed
3,546 square foot, single story restaurant with a drive-up window use will occupy the
entire site, including the existing surface rights leased from the Jordan and Salt Lake Canal,
owned by the Salt Lake City Public Utilities Department. The property will be purchased
from the LHM Ownership group by the Raising Cane's Restaurant entity, located in Plano,
Texas. (Please see the companion staff report for the Conditional Use on this same meeting
agenda, case CUP-02-21-5998.) The Planning Commission is the land-use authority for these
requested actions.

BACKGROUND
The site consists of three parcels. The west most portion is owned by LHM Group and is part of lot
25 of the Utah Auto Mall Subdivision. Through the center of the site is property owned by Salt Lake
City Public Utilities Department, which is under lease for surface rights to LHM Group. The third
parcel is owned by the LHM Group but is not in a subdivision. This site has existed in this
configuration since 1996. All streetscape improvements are existing from the prior service station
development and a UDOT widening project along State Street. The existing zoning, (AM)
Commercial, requires that Site Plan and Drive-up window restaurant use approvals be by the
Planning Commission.

Staff has been working with the Raising Cane's project team since November 2020 to arrive at the
current site plan. The initial site plan proposal showed the drive-up window queuing lanes located
between the restaurant building and State Street. Staff suggested that because the development site
is so large, that they look at putting the drive-up window queuing lanes behind the building and
place outside dining between the building and the street, which has been done. After many iterations
of the site plan, the current plan was filed in February 2021 for review by the Planning Commission.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting. A neighborhood meeting was not held for this project
due to the lack of residential zoning in the area.

ANALYSIS
Pedestrian and Vehicle Access and On-site Circulation. Customer vehicles will enter the site
from existing driveway approaches located on State Street and 11000 South Streets. Almost
everything else on the existing site will be removed and redone with this proposal. On-site
circulation will change drastically from the prior use. This business experiences a very high
drive-up window percentage of their business. Because of their experience in other markets and
locations, they are proposing as "super sized", double lane order queuing driveway. This will be
operated similar to how Chic-fil-A has their employee's out in the driveway lanes taking orders
from customers while they are in their cars waiting in the queue. This business model is
manditory for this restaurant chain. This layout of vehicle on-site access and circulation has been
reviewed by the City Engineers and will be adequate for the proposed development. The
required queuing space in the drive-up window driveway is designed to exceed City
requirements.

Pedestrian access will be greatly improved when compared to the existing development. ADA
compliant pedestrian sidewalks will connect through the dine-in customer parking in two
dedicated and buffered sidewalks and will connect to the east side of the restaurant by well
delineated crossings through the drive-up window queuing lanes. ADA sidewalks will also
connect to State Street and 11000 South Street. Pedestrian access will be adequate for the
development.

Zoning Review. The project will meet all of the requirements of the AM (Commercial) zoning
district and the Sandy City Development code. Parking for this project will meet the City
requirements on site. All required City Departments and Divisions have reviewed and
recommend approval, subject to their usual requirements and conditions.

Architectural Design Review. The building's design, materials and colors were reviewed by
the Sandy Planning Staff as to compliance with the Sandy City Architectural Design
Requirements. This "Branded" building fully meets the Sandy City Architectural Design
Requirements and has a variety of high quality building materials and compatible colors. There
is less than 7 percent of the entired building exterior surface that is proposed as stucco/EIFS.
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the building architectural design,
materials and colors as now proposed by the applicant.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan review is
complete for the proposed Raising Cane's Restaurant Building project, based upon the
following two findings and the following seven conditions:

FINDINGS.
     A.  That the proposed project meets or will meet all the applicable requirements of the
         Sandy City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as
         to building architectural design, materials and colors.
     B.  That re-development of this property is needed at this south State Street
         neighborhood and will add additional food variety to both dine-in and drive-up
         restaurant customers.

CONDITIONS.

     1.  That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior
         to the start of any construction. The final site plan shall comply with all Development
         Code requirements and any modifications required or allowed by the Planning
         Commission.

     2.  That the development will comply with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety
         Codes applicable to this type of use.

     3.  That the developer be responsible to meet all provisions of the AM (Commercial)
         zoning district and the Sandy City Architectural Design Requirements with the
         details finalized with staff during final site plan review.

     4.  All utility boxes (i.e. transformers, switchgear, telephone, cable TV, etc.) shall be shown
         on the site plan and shall be placed underground or moved behind the front setback of
         the buildings and screened from view, preferably located along the proposed driveway.
         Each box shall be shown in its exact location and shall be noted with its exact height,
         width and length. Building utility meters shall be mounted to the side or rear elevation
         or screened.

     5.  That the developer be responsible for the placement of a temporary 6 foot high chain
         link fence around the perimeter of the project during the construction phase of the
         project for security. Said fence shall also be required to include fabric to prohibit
         blowing dust problems, if it becomes necessary or if it is required by the Community
         Development Department during Site Plan Review.
