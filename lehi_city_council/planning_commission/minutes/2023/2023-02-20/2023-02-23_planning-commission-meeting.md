3. Regular Agenda

3.1) Consideration of Kris Sorensen’s request for approval of the Precision Air Management
Shop site plan located at 157 West State Street.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

Kim Struthers stated the item had been pre-approved a couple of years ago with the same exceptions.

The applicant, Lester VanAusdal and Lester Sorenson, was present. They would like the same item to
be approved with the same exceptions that were approved as last time. Commissioner Bunker asked
about the islands that were left as buffers. They would lose the handicap parking if they were required
to install landscape buffers. Ross Dinsdale stated this is a flex zone.

Not a public hearing item.

Motion: Commissioner Everett approved Kris Sorensen’s request for approval of the Precision Air
Management Shop site plan located at 157 West State Street; including all DRC comments and
findings stated in the report. Noting the exception for the landscaping allowance for the bookends on
the project. Commissioner Britton seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.2) Consideration of Fox Brothers’ request for approval of the Fox Shops 2 site plan located at
391 North 500 West.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Bryan Fox, was present. He stated he is willing to comply with the code for landscaping,
as well as providing the tree requirements.

Not a public hearing item.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Fox Brothers’ request for approval of the
        Fox Shops 2 site plan located at 391 North 500 West; including all DRC comments and
        findings stated in the report. Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes;
        Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Britani & John Underwood’s request for review of the
Marwood zone change, 0.84-acres of property located at 301 East State, changing the zoning from
Mixed Use to TOD (transit oriented development).

3.4) Recommendation of Britani & John Underwood’s request for concept approval of the
Marwood Group land use plan, a 0.84-acre transit-oriented development located at 301 East State
Street.
Agenda items 3.3 and 3.4 were heard simultaneously.

The GPA designation is TOD. The development code states they must include a land use plan. There
will be on-site and on-street parking with 1.31 stalls per unit. There will be commercial and retail space.
DRC had 26 redline comments, as well as a traffic impact study and parking study.

Commissioner Eyre clarified they are asking for 14%. Staff clarified the applicants are asking for a
reduction on landscaping, but this is just a concept right now.

Commissioner Everett asked if a market study has been done. A market study has not been conducted.
Craig Chambers stated a market study can be issued as part of the motion.

Commissioner Britton asked why this couldn't stay a mixed use. Kim Struthers stated this would have
to be a TOD because of the type of project this is and would be a better use for what is trying to be
accomplished.

Commissioner Bunker liked the TOD, especially in this location. He is concerned about parking and
why they are asking for a reduction but would like to see a traffic and parking study.

Commissioner Everett asked what the requirement is for parking stalls per unit.

The applicant, Curtis Miner, was present. He presented on the TOD zone first. The designation is
something that is new to the area and the transit is character to what the City has planned. As far as the
project, the trax line does not exist, but the design will transform to the building. The parking structure
will result in a lower parking structure. The retail portion will result in a market study to see what can
be supported. One of the issues that isn't well defined is the frontage along 750 North. He and the
Commissioners discussed parking at length.

Commissioner Eyre opened the public hearing for 3.3

Trevor (inaudible) was opposed to the TOD and the number of units based on the amount of traffic it
would bring. It is already a dangerous area where there are railroad tracks. He is also concerned about
where there is a sewer easement that serves the building to Transwest Auto. If there is a TOD, he is
opposed to spot zoning.

Commissioner Eyre closed 3.3

Ross stated UTA will bring transit when TOD is available.

        Motion for 3.3: Commissioner Bunker recommended approval of Britani & John
        Underwood’s request for review of the Marwood zone change, 0.84-acres of property
        located at 301 East State, changing the zoning from Mixed Use to TOD (transit oriented
        development); including DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Eyre
        seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, no. The motion passed 3-1.


        Motion for 3.4: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Britani & John Underwood’s
        request for concept approval of the Marwood Group land use plan, a 0.84-acre transit-
        oriented development located at 301 East State Street; including all DRC comments and
        findings stated in the report. Contingent on the parking study, market study, landscape and tree
        requirements are met, that the parking provided is adequate for the number of units the developer
        is asking for. Also including a design recommendation for any future retail and a traffic study if
        improvements need to be made. Contingent on City Council and UTA's input on this specific
        project. Commissioner Britton seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, no. The motion passed 3-1.
