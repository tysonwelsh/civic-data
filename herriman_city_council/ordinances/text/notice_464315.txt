PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

NOTICE OF DISPOSITION OF A PARCEL OF REAL PROPERTY

Notice is hereby given that Herriman City Council has declared the Herriman Community Center located at 13011 South Pioneer Street (6000 West) as surplus, established a minimum bid, and established that the method for determining the highest and best economic return to the City shall be by disposing of the property. Any comments regarding such disposition will be accepted until May 28, 2018 at 3:00 p.m. Submit comments to the attention of Jackie Nostrom, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, UT 84096 /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
