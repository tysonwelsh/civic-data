Commissioner Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Natalie Nelson, County Administrator
Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

CONSIDERATION OF PROCLAMATION NO. 2020-14, A PROCLAMATION
DECLARING AUGUST _6, 2020, GREATEST GENERATION DAY_IN
WASHINGTON COUNTY

Washington County Fair Director Susi LaFaele explained that it is the 75" anniversary of
World War II and the Fair Board would like to honor those veterans still living in Southern
Utah.

Commissioner Iverson read aloud the Proclamation.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Proclamation No. 2020-
14, A Proclamation Declaring August 6, 2020, Greatest Generation Day
in Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and

and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2632, A RESOLUTION IN
SUPPORT OF THE DEDICATION OF LEGACY PARK ROAD AS A PUBLIC

RIGHT OF WAY

Chairman Iverson explained that 5500 West which runs adjacent to the Legacy Park
complex has never officially been dedicated. In working with Hurricane City, it was
determined that the road should be dedicated as a public-right-of way in order to help
mitigate traffic needs in the area.

Hurricane City Engineer Arthur LeBaron explained that 5500 West will connect into 5300
West. The connecting intersection will be reconfigured along with the future expansion of
Purgatory Road (5300 West).

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2632, A
Resolution in Support of the Dedication of Legacy Park Road as a
Public Right of Way. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist,

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2633, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN

HURRICANE VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT (HVF SSD) AND

WASHINGTON COUNTY

Hurricane Valley Fire Chief Tom Kuhlmann explained that several local fire districts
provide emergency and fire protection services within unincorporated areas of the County.
Many of the small fire protection districts are run by volunteers and occasionally become
overburdened by various responsibilities. HVFSSD is the largest special service district
responsible for providing emergency and fire services to the public within its boundaries.
However, the district is in need of a protection specialist that would be available to perform
fire-related duties for HVFSSD and for other unincorporated areas outside of the HVFSSD
boundaries.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2633, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement Between
Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District (HVF SSD) and
Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist,
and Cox voting aye.
