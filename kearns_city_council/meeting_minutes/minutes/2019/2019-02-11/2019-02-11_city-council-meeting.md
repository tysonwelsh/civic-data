Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Chief Brian Lohrke updated the Council on the following:

    Introduced Lieutenant Chambers, a new lieutenant for the Kearns Precinct.

    UPD will be asking for a budget adjustment at the upcoming Salt Lake Valley Law
     Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA) meeting to cover the cost for a new school
     resource officer (SRO) at the junior high. Currently, one officer is covering two junior
     highs.

    Employee surveys were sent to officers within the Kearns Precinct to receive input on what
     is needed. The results from the survey indicate that employees are concerned about
     wages, retirement benefits, and the need for a new precinct building.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

              Captain Wade Russell, Unified Fire Authority, updated the Council on the
following:

     210 calls were made from the Kearns fire station including ambulance and hazardous
      materials.

     Bill passed through committee today at the State Legislation relating to Tier 2 retirement.

     UFA is watching HB 5 relating to the ongoing appropriation of $12 million and $17 million
      in one-time money to the Utah Retirement System specifically for the firefighters.

     Asked Council to encourage residents of Kearns to clear snow away from hydrants so
      firefighters have easy access to them.

     CERT class is scheduled to start on April 3, 2019. It is a six-week class.

     The CPA class for Kearns Royalty was held.

     There was a significant fire on Brookshade Lane. No one was injured, but it did result in
      $200,000 to $250,000 in damages.

                Captain Jenn Bevin stated her team has been working with a three-year old who
has cancer. His grandma had invited them over to his birthday party to surprise him because he
loves firetrucks. The crew presented him with his own turnouts. The little boy has been signed
up for Mascot Miracles where he can participate in activities year-round.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) Payroll

               Mayor Bush stated the GSLMSD would like to move payroll from Salt Lake County
to the GSLMSD. If this happens then all of payroll would fall under the GSLMSD identification
number. She was concerned about this because of transparency and auditing purposes.
Currently payroll is with National Payroll and she thought it should stay that way until employees
could use their own identification numbers. She asked the Council what it would like to do.

              Council Member Brown stated for auditing purposes and transparency it should
remain the way it is.

               Council Member Bush stated it will remain the way it is, unless there are any
objections.

Oquirrh Hills Elementary School Contribution

               Mayor Bush stated the Kearns Metro Township Community Council requested the
Kearns Metro Township Council to partner with them for an end of the year celebration for the
children that attend Oquirrh Hills Elementary School, which will be closing after this year. The
Council was asked to contribute 500 superhero capes. A bid has been received totaling $2,275
for these capes. This is the best deal that she was able to find.

                Council Member Snow stated the funds for this contribution will come out of the
contribution line item within the budget.

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to
approve this contribution. The motion passed unanimously.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Lapel Pins for Kearns Unified Police Department (UPD) and Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

               Mayor Bush stated the UPD and UFA requested lapel pins to wear on their
uniforms that represent Kearns Metro Township. She asked if the Council would like her to
investigate the price for these.

               The Council Members all agreed that this should be done.

               Council Member Brown asked that Mayor Bush investigate getting name badges

               Mayor Bush stated once she gets the information she would bring it back to the
Council for approval. She suggested the Kearns Metro Township logo be used minus the cannon
because people would not know what it was due to the size of the pin.

Website Update

               Council Member Brown stated initially the website was supposed to be up and
running by the end of January, but it keeps getting push back. Mitch Hansen was informed that
the Council needed a launch date, it has waited long enough. She was told the website should
be ready by the end of February.

the website is working.
