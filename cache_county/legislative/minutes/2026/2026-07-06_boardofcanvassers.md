# Cache County Council — BoardOfCanvassers — 2026-07-06

> Format: text

CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL
                                                               BOARD OF CANVASSERS
                         JULY 6, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. - Cache County Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.
      In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
     appear and speak at a County Council meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinions or purported facts.
                  The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


MEMBERS PRESENT: Vice Chair Kathryn Beus, Councilmember JoAnn Bennett, Councilmember Keegan Garrity, Councilmember
Nolan Gunnell, Councilmember Mark Hurd.
MEMBERS EXCUSED: Chair Sandi Goodlander, Councilmember David Erickson.
STAFF PRESENT: Andrew Erickson, Bryson Behm, Matt Funk, Michelle Stanger, Kyla Doyle, Carter Chatterton.

1.     Call to Order 5:00 p.m. – 0:00

2.     Opening Remarks and Pledge of Allegiance – 0:06 Vice Chair Kathryn Beus noted Chair Sandi Goodlander and
       Councilmember David Erickson were absent. Vice Chair Kathryn Beus expressed gratitude for the opportunity to live in a
       nation with free and fair elections and thanked election staff for their service. She led the council and attendees in reciting
       the Pledge of Allegiance. 1:21 Councilmember Nolan Gunnell moved to approve the agenda with Councilmember JoAnn
       Bennett seconding the motion.

       Action: Motion by Councilmember Nolan Gunnell to approve agenda; seconded by Councilmember JoAnn Bennett.
       Motion passes.
       Aye: 5 Kathryn Beus, JoAnn Bennett, Keegan Garrity, Nolan Gunnell, Mark Hurd
       No: 0
       Absent: 2 Sandi Goodlander, David Erickson

3.     Certification of the 2026 Cache County Primary Election – 1:36 County Clerk Bryson Behm presented the general canvass for
       the 2026 Republican Primary Election. He shared that 14,636 ballots were counted, resulting in a 36.2% voter turnout rate.
       Clerk Behm reported no discrepancies in the vote counts or audit. 7:33 Clerk Behm shared the results of the two races on the
       ballot:

       Republican for U.S. House District 2:
          Blake Moore: 8,650
          Karianne Lisonbee: 5,807

       Republican for County Attorney:
          J. Brett Chambers: 8,381
          Dane Murray: 6,035

       Action: Motion by Councilmember Nolan Gunnell to certify the 2026 Republican Primary Election; seconded by
       Councilmember JoAnn Bennett.
       Motion passes.
       Aye: 5 Kathryn Beus, JoAnn Bennett, Keegan Garrity, Nolan Gunnell, Mark Hurd
       No: 0
       Absent: 2 Sandi Goodlander, David Erickson

4.     Adjourn: 5:15 p.m. – 9:39

       Action: Motion by Councilmember Nolan Gunnell to adjourn; seconded by Councilmember Mark Hurd.
Motion passes.
Aye: 5 Kathryn Beus, JoAnn Bennett, Keegan Garrity, Nolan Gunnell, Mark Hurd
No: 0
Absent: 2 Sandi Goodlander, David Erickson
