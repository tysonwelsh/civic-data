                                              AFFIDAVIT OF POSTING
                                        SYNOPSIS OF OGDEN CITY ORDINANCE



         I, Jason Gould, the duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, in and for the City of Ogden, Weber County, State
of Utah, do hereby certify the Ogden City Council at its February 18, 2025, meeting adopted the following:

         ORDINANCE NO. 2025-7: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY, UTAH, AMENDING THE
         OGDEN MUNICIPAL CODE BY AMENDING SECTION 7–6–1 TO REVISE PROVISIONS
         REGARDING ENGINEERING REGULATIONS, DESIGN STANDARDS, CONSTRUCTION
         SPECIFICATIONS AND TRAFFIC CONTROL REGULATIONS, AND VARIANCES; BY MAKING
         TECHNICAL CHANGES; BY AMENDING THE CODE INDEX ACCORDINGLY; AND BY
         PROVIDING THAT THIS ORDINANCE SHALL BECOME EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY UPON
         POSTING AFTER FINAL PASSAGE.

         Copies of all ordinances in their entirety are available for review in the Ogden City Recorder’s Office located
on the second floor of the Municipal Building, 2549 Washington Boulevard #210 during regular business hours.

         The undersigned, duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, does hereby certify that the above notice was
posted in accordance with Utah State law on this 24th day of February 2025.
