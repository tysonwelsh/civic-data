1.         25-299                 Visit Salt Lake Updating the Council Regarding the Convention and
                                  Tourism Assessment Area (CTAA)
                   Attachments:   CTAA x Sandy City

                                  Martin Jensen, Deputy CAO, introduced the item and the staff from Visit Salt Lake and
                                  the Convention & Tourism Assessment Area South Committee who provided an overview
                                  of the Convention & Tourism Assessment Area (CTAA) and the economic impact of the
                                  CTAA on Sandy City.

                                  Sandy City is a critical hub for Salt Lake's visitor economy. Sandy has 8 hotels and
                                  1,1014 hotel rooms with an average occupancy rate of 70%. Last year, Sandy hotels
                                  generated approximately $25 million in room revenue. Visitors are responsible for 27% of
                                  all spending in Sandy City (food, beverage, retail, etc.). The CTAA is a hotel-led initiative
                                  approved by Sandy City in 2023. A 2% assessment on hotel room revenue is reinvested
                                  into tourism development and was designed to increase awareness and demand for room
                                  night sales at participating hotels. Sandy City is part of the South District. CTAA funds
                                  are invested to drive near-term hotel bookings while strengthening the region's long-term
                                  brand and destination appeal. 10% of CTAA funds are generated in the South District
                                  (Sandy & Draper). They reviewed key districtwide activities and initiatives. Visit Salt Lake
                                  team has won 85% of the events it has bid on using the CTAA funds and was expecting
                                  to bring 22 events to the South District that could have an economic impact of
                                  approximately $107 million. They reviewed CTAA hotel performance compared to other
                                  cities and reviewed a recent Expedia campaign launched in February-March of 2025 and
                                  the impact of the 2025 Development Program National Championships for USA
                                  Gymnastics. Other CTAA-supported events include the Triple Crown Volleyball 2025
                                  Western Invitational and the National Horseshoe Pitchers Association. They also
                                  reviewed the 2026 CTAA budget and future goals. The upcoming renovation of the Salt
                                  Palace has resulted in the cancellation of many events. They are working to secure those
                                  events for the Mountain America Expo Center. Council questions and comments
                                  followed. Council thanked Visit Salt Lake and the Convention & Tourism Committee for
                                  their presentation and partnership.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledged the Sandy Ambassadors. He attended
                    their last meeting and had a robust discussion on Alta Canyon Recreation Center. It was
                    a fantastic meeting. He also attended the Sandy Farmer's Market. It was a very well
                    attended event and well organized.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked all staff involved with coordinating the Heritage
                    Festival. It was a great event. He enjoyed judging the cars in the show.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud attended the Heritage Festival. It was a wonderful event
                    and she thanked all staff involved with the event for their outstanding work.

                    Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer attended the ribbon cutting for the Utah Mammoth
                    Training Facility. It was a great event. He spoke about current research topics being
                    studied by the Utah Foundation Organization.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the Salt Lake Convention &
                    Visitors Board. The demolition date for the Salt Palace has been scheduled for February
                    15, 2027. Convention Halls 1-3 will continue to be open and utilized for convention events.
                    Halls 4 & 5 are scheduled for demolition. The Board is in the process of rebooking
                    convention events. 32 events have been rescheduled. 6 events have chosen to cancel
                    their event and looking for an alternative location.

                    Council Member Marci Houseman praised Pam Roberts, Director of the WFWRD for her
                    service to Sandy. She shared a picture of Ms. Roberts from her retirement farewell event.
                    Ms. Roberts and her successor, Evan Tyrrel will join the Council for a meeting in
                    November.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski acknowldeged the One Kind Act resolution passed. She thanked Chief
                    Larson, Sandy's Emergency Manager. The Heritage Festival was a great community
                    event and she thanked the festival sponsors. The Visual Arts Show is occuring at the
                    Senior Center. All are welcome. She recognized Community Development staff for
                    receiving an award for the Sandy General Plan. The City received notice of the Housing
                    and Transit Reinvestment Zone (HTRZ) designation. This is a big win for our City.
