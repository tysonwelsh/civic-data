                  SUMMARY:
                                 The City of Taylorsville is requesting a text amendment to the Taylorsville
                                 Municipal Code Sections 13. 21. 070: Drainage Systems; and Section
                                 13. 21. 080: High Water Table Areas.
                                 The proposed amendment will make the following changes to the
                                 Taylorsville Municipal Code:
                                              Section 13.21. 070: Replaces specific language regarding
                                 stormwater runoff with a general reference to Title 17: Storm Drainage and
                                 Flood Control Development.

                                            Section 13.21. 080: Rewords and clarifies the responsible party
                                 for stormwater facilities located on private property.
               BACKGROUND:
                                 Every three to five years the City receives a new storm drain permit from the
                                 State of Utah. Changes to storm water requirements often accompany the
                                 new permit. The requested change to Title 13 is to streamline our municipal
                                 code so that all storm water provisions are included in one place, Title 17.
                                 This change will also minimize the possibilities of conflicting ordinances by
                                 only having the storm water criteria in one place. An update to the storm
                                 water regulations in Title 17 will be presented in a future meeting.
     STAFF RECOMMENDATION:       Staff recommends a positive recommendation of Ordinance No. 20- 19 based on the
                                 findings stated in the staff report.

amendments.
