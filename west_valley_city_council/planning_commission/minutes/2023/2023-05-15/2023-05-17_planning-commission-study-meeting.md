GENERAL PLAN CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GP-1-2023 Petition by RUSSELL PLATT requesting a General Plan amendment from Mixed
          Use with High Density Residential to Mixed Use with Very High Density
          Residential. The property is located at 1251 West 3300 South on 13 acres. (Staff –
          Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik explained that this proposal would amend the subject property's
            General Plan and development agreement. He stated that the existing
            development is called Village @ River's Edge and pointed out the four parcels
            included in this proposal. Steve noted that the condo building has different
            ownership and is not part of this application. He pointed out the four buildings
            with commercial space on the ground floor and explained that the applicant
            proposes converting that space into thirty apartment units. Steve said the applicant
            mentioned that the existing commercial space retail had increased the number of
            police calls, and tenant turnover was high. Therefore, they feel residential space
would benefit them and the City. Steve displayed an image depicting where they
propose adding doors and patio areas for those apartments. He noted that the
wetland area would be filled in to accommodate the proposed townhomes amenity
space.

Steve said there are two hundred and forty-four apartments on the subject
property, and this proposal would add thirty apartments and seventeen
townhomes. He said they would repair and paint the stucco on the existing
structures and offered to do the same for the condo development. Also, they
would fix the larger landscaped areas and add some xeriscape. He said the
applicant is requesting a General Plan change because this project would have a
density of 22 units per acre. Steve noted that this project exceeds the parking
requirement.

Steve stated that when the homeless shelter near the wetland area opened, the
applicant started having issues with transients in and around the wetlands, and
they are hoping once it is developed, it will alleviate those issues. Steve pointed
out that to fill in the wetlands, the applicant would need approval from the US
Army Core of Engineers, and they have been in contact with them. He said some
drainage from the site goes into wetlands, and they would work with the Public
Works Department to address that issue.

Commissioner Woodruff asked if the townhomes would be rentals. Steve said
they would, and they would have the same owner as the apartments.

Commissioner Fuller asked if there has been any thriving mixed-use in West
Valley City. Steve explained that there are two types of mixed-use - horizontal
and vertical. He said horizontal mixed-use is more successful in the City.
However, vertical mixing has proven to be successful in areas with very high
density, like New York City.

Commissioner Winters asked if there was a way to combine the old-style
architecture with the modern style so that a development could look more
uniform. He gave an example of Park City and said they must have standards that
keep the architecture uniform throughout the city. Steve said Park City has the
most stringent standards in the state. He noted that the applicant had submitted
different renderings before those shown today. Steve felt they could have been
more aesthetically pleasing. However, he can bring this to the applicant's
attention.

Commissioner Lovato asked if West Valley City could initiate something to
enhance the overall look of our apartment and townhome developments. Steve
pointed out that the applicant has included management standards in the
development agreement as part of this proposal. He noted that this could be one
way to address his concern. Steve stated that staff works closely with the
applicants to find ways to spruce up the developments before it is presented to the
              Planning Commission. However, Commissioner Lovato brings up a good point;
              maybe we can do more.

              Commissioner Lovato asked if there has been any impact on residential
              developments near the homeless shelter. Steve said that the applicant had
              mentioned a significant increase in security concerns following the opening of the
              homeless shelter. He said the applicant should be at next week's meeting and can
              speak more about this. Steve noted that the Police Department is keeping a record
              of the calls they receive that are transient-related. Also, the applicant is proposing
              extra lighting as an additional security measure.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
S-3-2023  Petition by SCOTT GODFREY requesting approval for the Godfrey Industrial
          Subdivision No. 3. The subdivision will also amend lots 1 and 2 of the Godfrey
          West Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 4 lots on 108 acres. The
          subdivision is located at 5900 West 2400 South and is zoned M.
          (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-
          ut.gov).

Discussion: Steve Lehman explained that this request is to approve a subdivision on 108 acres
            owned by Scott Godfrey. He explained that the applicant intends to extend 2400
            South to the west as part of this subdivision. Therefore, two lots that are part of
            the Godfrey West Subdivision are included in this application. Steve presented the
            site plan and pointed out the extension of 2400 South, which would provide
            access to the subject property and the two lots of the Godfrey West Subdivision.

              Steve said he is unsure how Mr. Godfrey plans to develop this property. However,
              he believes it would be used for a warehouse or office/flex space like his
              surrounding property. Steve pointed out the areas on the subject property with a
              large amount of drainage and stated that the applicant intends to leave that area in
              its natural state.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-9-2023 Petition by HUNT ELECTRIC requesting conditional use for outside storage. The
         property is located at 1784 W. 2300 S. on 1.02 acres and is zoned M
         (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-
         ut.gov).

Discussion: Jody Knapp explained that the subject property is a satellite office for Hunt
            Electric. She stated they lease one unit of a multitenant condo development as
            extra warehouse space and is requesting a conditional use for outside storage.

              Jody presented the most detailed site plan she could find and noted the subject
              property was developed in the early 70s. Jody pointed out where the applicant
              wanted to house their outside storage. She explained that typically a business is
              required to have a site inspection as part of the business license process. However,
              in this case, Hunt Electric is leasing a unit and does not own the subject property,
              so the City would not hold their business license for general site issues.

              Jody reviewed the current landscaping and mentioned that some of the
              landscaping had either been removed and not replaced or needed maintenance.
              She pointed out trees that, over time, have died and are now lying on the ground.
              Jody noted that as part of the landscaping for the parking lot, there should be one
              tree every 30 feet. Jody noted that the areas that should have grass are now
              primarily weeds, and the areas with only rock previously contained live plant
              material. She stated that this is an excellent opportunity to look at the landscaping
              for the overall site. If the Commission wants specific landscaping added, it could
              be part of this conditional use.

              Commissioner Winters asked Jody if the best way for the Commission to address
              these issues would be requiring the applicant to meet the typical landscaping
              requirements. Jody said that would be fine. She said she would also like their
              thoughts regarding adding live plant material in the area that is just rock.


C-10-2023     Petition by CASTLEWOOD DEVELOPMENT requesting conditional use
              approval for a 330-unit apartment development. The property is located at 2940
              W 3650 S on 3.02 acres and is zoned CC (City Center) and R-2-8 (Two Unit
              Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). (Staff – Steve
              Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik noted that the Planning Commission approved this application on
              June 8th of last year. He pointed out that around the same time, interest rates were
              climbing, and the applicant ran into financing issues. Steve explained that our
              ordinance states approval from the Planning Commission expires after a year if
              there hasn’t been significant progress, like obtaining a building permit and
              beginning construction. He noted that the applicant believes they are ready to
              proceed with this project and are returning to the Commission for approval before
              the original approval expires.

              Steve reviewed this proposal. He presented the site plan and mentioned that there
              had been some slight adjustments. Steve stated there would be seven floors, and
              floors four through seven would sit back from the street. Steve pointed out area
              15, the loading area, and explained that someone could park temporarily to load
              or unload.

              Chair Fuller asked what would separate the subject property from the neighboring
              library. Steve said there is currently five feet of landscaping, and as part of this
              project, there would be an additional five feet of landscaping and a sidewalk
              between the two properties. Chair Fuller asked if a fence would exist between the
              subject property and the library. Steve stated that the proposed walking path
               would connect to the library and the park, and neither connection would have a
               fence.

               Chair Fuller pointed out that the City has approved the widening of 3650 South
               and asked how that would impact the proposed development. Steve explained that
               they would need to work with the Public Works Department to figure out who
               would be responsible for adding the improvements along 3650 South.

               Commissioner Winters asked if the City Council had approved this proposal last
               year. Steve said this application would not need approval from the Council
               because the zoning is already in place and is a conditional use request.

               Commissioner Winters asked if the City Center zone is developing as anticipated
               or if there have been any complications. Steve stated that there had been mention
               of one issue with residents bringing their dogs to the park to relieve themselves
               and damaging the grass. Steve said they are looking into providing a specific area
               with artificial turf for dogs to use instead of the park, which would keep waste
               contained and help maintain the grass.

               Commissioner Winters asked if we intend to keep R-1-8 zoning south of this
               property. Steve said yes, that is why we have the restriction of no more than three
               floors close to the road; it helps transition from residential uses to the City Center
               zone.

               Commissioner Winters asked if there had been any concerns from the residents of
               the adjacent properties regarding this project. Steve mentioned one gentleman
               who opposed this proposal because of the high density. He stated that if approved,
               he would prefer a connection to the north so all the traffic would not go to Lancer
               Way. Subsequently, we were able to work out that connection.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:43 p.m.
