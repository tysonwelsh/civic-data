6. 23-469 Community Development Department presenting an update of the General
Plan update process for review and discussion in a combined work
session with the City Council, Mayor, Administration, and Planning
Commission.
Attachments: Sandy General Plan Survey Phase II Summary Report
Sandy General Plan Framework Presentation
Jake Warner with Community Development, thanked the Council and Planning
Commissioners for attending this evening's joint meeting and presentation of the Sandy
City General Plan Update. He introduced Kyrene Gibb with Y2 Analytics and Christine
Richman with GSBS.
Ms. Gibb provided an overview of the results form the summer 2023 Public Opinion
Research her company conducted. She reviewed the 5 Key findings, the survey
methodology, the SWOT Analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats), and
converging priorities. She also reviewed the residents’ priorities based on the survey
results. Safety remains a top priority across the districts. Residents are concerned about
the affordability of living in Sandy. Housing and Neighborhood Character, recreation, open
space, and the economy are important priorities for our city's residents.
Mayor Zoltanski arrived at approximately 6:20 pm.
At 6:23 pm, Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda: General Citizen Comment.

City Council Meeting Minutes December 6, 2023
Following General Citizen Comments, Ms. Gibb continued with her presentation. Council
questions and discussion followed.

Planning Commissioner Jamie Tsandes arrived at 6:44 pm.

Jake Warner discussed the goals for the second survey - to build on the first survey and
dig a little deeper. The survey gathered information about the "why" behind the feedback
we were hearing. He introduced Christine Richman who provided an overview of Sandy
General Plan framework approach, summary of the Y2 Analytics Survey #2, and a review
and discussion of the draft values, key strategies, and vision. She also discussed the
Next Steps in the General Plan process.

A lengthy discussion with the Council Members and Planning Commissioners occurred
throughout the presentation. Staff and consultants responded to questions and feedback.
Following the discussion, Jake Warner thanked the Council and Planning Commissioners
for their feedback and time. Please visit the Council website for the recording of the full
presentation and discussion of the Sandy City General Plan Update. Planning
Commissioners were excused from the meeting.

Council moved to Standing Reports.
