1.         25-045                 Administrative Services Department presenting the Fiscal Year 2024
                                  Annual Comprehensive Financial Report and the Government Finance
                                  Officers Association Awards
                   Attachments:   Sandy City AA 2024 Auditor's Report - Goverment Basic with RSI SI
                                  and Other Information
                                  Sandy City AA 2024 Auditor's Report - Goverment GAGAS IC and
                                  Compliance
                                  Sandy City AA 2024 Report on State Compliance and Schedule of
                                  Findings
                                  Brian Kelley, Administrative Services Director, introduced our city's auditors, David
                                  Peaden with Eide, Bailly. Mr. Peaden presented the Independent Financial Audit Report.
                                  The auditor issued a clean opinion for FY 2024.He provided an overview of the reports and
                                  findings and provided a reasonable assurance that there are no material misstatements.
                                  Based on their evaluation, they determined the assumptions that went into determining
                                  the pension liability were reasonable and appropriate. They noted a small item related to
                                  depreciation expense that was a continuation of an item from the prior year. Mr. Peaden
                                  also reviewed with the council the letter on internal control and the letter on internal
                                  control related to state compliance. Mr. Peaden commended the finance and accounting
                                  team for their responsiveness and dedication throughout the audit process. Council
                                  thanked Mr. Peaden for his presentation.

                                  Nick Hales with the Utah Government Finance Office Association presented awards to
                                  the city finance and accounting team. He presented the Accounting team with the
                                  Certificate of Achievement for Excellence in Financial Reporting for the year ending in
                                  June of 2023. This is the 37th consecutive year the team has received this award. Mr.
                                  Hales awarded the Finance team the Distinguished Budget Presentation Award. This is
                                  the 21st consecutive year the team has received this award. The Council congratulated
                                  the Finance and Accounting team and acknowledged them with a congratulatory card
                                  and council coin.

6.         25-043                 City Council Holding a City Council Budget Priorities Workshop

                   Attachments:   Draft_Staff Proposed Priorities Narrative

                                  Council participated in a follow-up Budget Priorities Workshop which convened at 6:30
                                  pm in the Multi Purpose Room. Matt Dixon facilitated the follow-up Budget Priorities
                                  Workshop. He reviewed the 5 areas of focus that was developed at the initial workshop by
                                  the Council:
                                     1. Economic Development
                                     2. Employee Satisfaction
                                     3. Public Safety
                                     4. Quality of Life
                                     5. Fiscal Health

                                  Council reviewed and discussed each of their areas of focus developed at the January
                                  workshop. Led by the workshop facilitator, the Council expanded and more clearly
                                  defined their commitment related to each area of focus. Council discussion, comments
                                  and questions occurred throughout the workshop. Council will work with staff to further
                                  define the budget narrative document based on this evening's discussion. Mr. Dixon
                                  thanked the Council for allowing him to facilitate the two workshops.

Adjournment
                                  Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 8:23 pm.
