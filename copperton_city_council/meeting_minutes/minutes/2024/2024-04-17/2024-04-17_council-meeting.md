Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA) and Unified Police Department
(UPD) Update

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, stated Sheriff Rosie
Rivera has submitted a request to UPD for $5.8 million in Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement
Service Area (SLVLESA) funds. This request has been endorsed by the Salt Lake County
Council. SLVLESA met the next day and the Board put together a committee to consider the
request. There is a question of whether the Sheriff’s request would amount to double taxation.
Some of the other metro townships have asked for a legislative audit of some of the things the
Sheriff has requested. The audit committee will decide tomorrow.

               Council Member Olsen stated contracting out these services has been brought
up to Sheriff Rivera multiple times, but she has been resistant to the idea. A legislative audit
would help determine if contracting those services out would make more sense financially.

               Mayor Clayton asked what Rio Tinto’s stance was.

Copperton Logo

               Mayor Clayton reviewed the options for the logo. He preferred the options that
incorporated the arch in the park.

               Council Member Stitzer stated she liked the arch but preferred green trees over
brown.

               Mayor Clayton suggested removing the trees entirely.

              Council Member Stitzer asked what would need to be done to correct the issue
of Copperton addresses coming up as Bingham Canyon addresses with the United States Postal
Service (USPS).

               Mayor Clayton stated he would reach out to the regional representative.

               The Council decided to go with logos #8 and #11, but with the images of the trees
removed.

Economic Growth, Land Use, and Annexation

               Mayor Clayton stated this would be an ongoing agenda item.

                Council Member Stitzer stated the last meeting was casual. She, Ryan Perry,
Rio Tinto, and Laura Ingersoll, Rio Tinto, sat and outlined some thoughts and ideas. She reviewed
Copperton’s annexation plan and the high priority areas highlighted in red. She noted timelines
can be difficult to discuss and annexation can take years. She met yesterday with Dan Torres,
Economic Development Manager, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Service District (MSD). He also
understands what Copperton’s top priority area is. They want all appropriate parties to look at
the plan.
