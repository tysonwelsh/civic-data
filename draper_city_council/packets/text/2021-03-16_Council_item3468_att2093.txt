RECOMMENDATION:

Staff recommends the City Council approve resolution No. 21-11 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule
regarding the fees charged for Master Area Plan or Amendments and Zoning Verification Letter Fee.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule shows fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and administrative
service costs. The fees being proposed for the Master Area Plan or Amendments and the Zoning Verification
Fee is a new user fee that is being adopted. Only the items or sections that are proposed for changes are listed
in Exhibit A. Items highlighted in blue are the new proposed fees or description. The suspension of the Storm
Water Impact Fee was approved previously by Ordinance 1480.
