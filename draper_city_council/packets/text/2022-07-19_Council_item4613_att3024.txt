                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1549

         AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE FIRST AMENDMENT TO THE MASTER
         DEVELOPMENT   AGREEMENT    FOR    EPG  PONY  EXPRESS  FOR
         APPROXIMATELY   26.93  ACRES   OF   PROPERTY  LOCATED  AT
         APPROXIMATELY 13004 SOUTH PONY EXPRESS ROAD WITHIN DRAPER
         CITY.

       WHEREAS, the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code
has been established to provide regulations concerning general developments within the
City Boundaries; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it will be in the best interest of the
City to allow development of the subject property in accordance with the Proposed
Amendments to the Development Agreement; and

      WHEREAS, the Amendment to the Development Agreement will allow for a use that
would otherwise be allowed in the M1 (Light Manufacturing) zone; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed Amendment to the Development Agreement set forth
herein has been reviewed by the Planning Commission and the City Council, and all
appropriate public hearings have been held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public
input regarding the proposed Amendments to the Development Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed Amendment to the Development Agreement, and
the City Council has found the proposed Development Agreement to be consistent with the
City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

       Section 1.  Development Agreement.       The City of Draper approves the
Amendments to the Development Agreement provided in Exhibit A, otherwise known as The
First Amendment to The Master Development Agreement for EPG Pony Express.

       Section 2.    Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

        Section 3.   Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of
final passage.

       This Amendment to Master Development Agreement for EPG Pony Express (“First
Amendment”) is entered into this _______ day of July, 2022 (“Effective Date”), by and between
DRAPER CITY, a municipality of the State of Utah, (“City”), and EXETER DRAPER PSA, LLC, a
Delaware limited liability company (“Master Developer”), sometimes referred to jointly as

                                             RECITALS:

       WHEREAS The Parties previously entered into that certain MASTER DEVELOPMENT
AGREEMENT FOR EPG PONY EXPRESS (“MDA”) dated on or about April 7, 2020 with respect to
real property located in Draper City, Salt Lake County, State of Utah (“Property”);

      WHEREAS Master Developer and the City have cooperated in the preparation of this First
Amendment and desire to enter into this First Amendment to specify the rights and
responsibilities of Master Developer to develop the Property as expressed in this First
Amendment and the rights and responsibilities of the City to allow and regulate such
development pursuant to the requirements of this First Amendment.

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises, covenants, representations and
warranties hereinafter set forth, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

              1. Definitions. All capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined herein shall
       have the same meanings assigned such terms in the MDA.

              2. Exhibits. Exhibits A and D to the MDA shall be deleted and replaced with the
              Exhibits A and D attached hereto.

              3. Interpretation. In the event of a conflict in the terms and conditions of this First
       Amendment with the terms and conditions of the MDA, the terms and conditions of this
       First Amendment shall be binding and govern the conduct of the Parties.
