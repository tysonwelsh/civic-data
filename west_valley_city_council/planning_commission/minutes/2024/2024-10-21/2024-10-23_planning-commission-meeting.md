37.53 acres located at 5892 and 5998 W Parkway Blvd. The property is currently zoned A-1

(2 to 3 units/acre) for the portion of the property that is west of Stegner Way with the balance being
Medium Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre). The proposed zone is M (Manufacturing) and the

approved, the applicant would need to apply for a conditional use permit to pursue the proposed
industrial development.

Issues to Consider

Since the adoption of the City’s first General Plan in the 80’s, the Riter Canal has been the dividing
line between industrial uses to the north and residential uses to the south in the northwest part of the
City. However, much has changed in the northwest part of the City since the 80’s. The following list
of current conditions may warrant consideration of M zoning on the subject property:

    •   The Northwest Economic Development Area (EDA), which was created in 2012, includes
        the subject property. The Northwest EDA covers approximately 1,000 acres in the northwest
        part of the City and makes available incentives for commercial development.
    •   As indicated on the City’s Major Street Plan, the City plans to eventually extend 5900 West
        between Beagley Road and Parkway Blvd. on the Rocky Mountain Power property directly
        east of the subject parcel. This road extension will enable industrial traffic in the area north
        of Beagley Road to access Mountain View Corridor without using Parkway Blvd. In other
        words, the subject property would be next to two roads with significant industrial traffic –
        Beagley Road and 5900 West.
    •   The charter school and Magna Water property to the south could be seen as a buffer between
        the residential property on the south side of Parkway Blvd. and the potential industrial
        development on the subject property.
    •   The zoning needed to develop the property as Medium Density Residential, the current
        General Plan designation on the east part of the property, is RM. However, to be eligible for
        RM zoning, the current zoning ordinance requires a property of this size to have access and
        frontage on a street with a planned right-of-way of at least 100 feet as indicated on the Major
        Street Plan. Currently, the only streets the subject property has access and frontage on are
        Stegner Way and Beagley Road, which are not roads with a planned right-of-way width of at
        least 100 feet. This means that this property is not eligible for RM zoning.
    •   The City is currently widening Parkway Blvd. to 5 lanes between Mountain View Corridor
        and 6400 West. This widening project is anticipated to be completed next year.

Access

If the Commission believes M zoning is appropriate here, the primary access would be off of
Beagley Road. A future driveway connection to 5900 West may be possible at such time this road is
extended south and if the owner is able to secure permission to construct an access across Rocky
Mountain Power’s property. Access to Stegner Way is also possible; however, the applicant has
proposed prohibiting truck traffic on Stegner Way in the development agreement.

The property only has direct access and frontage on Beagley Road on the east side of the property.
To the west, two small slivers of property are located between Beagley Road and the subject
property. One of these slivers is owned by the City and the other is owned by Godfrey West
Investors, LLC. The applicant has proposed language in the development agreement regarding these
two slivers of property to facilitate access to Beagley Road. This language is under review by the
City’s legal department.

Kristofor Kvarfordt with Arete Collective is requesting a change to the General Plan from Low
Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre) to Small Lot Residential (4 to 7 units/acre) on 12.85 acres of
property located at 5332 W Highbury Parkway. This application also includes a request to replace a
development agreement for this property that was entered into back in 2017. The subject property is
currently zoned R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet).
Surrounding zoning includes C-2 (General Commercial) to north, RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling
Residential) to the east and west, and R-1-8 to the south. Surrounding uses include a waterway and
InstaCare facility to the north, apartments to the east, townhomes and a water feature to the west, and
single family detached homes to the south.

Development Proposal

The applicant is proposing a planned unit development (PUD) with 67 lots for single family
detached homes, yielding a density of 5.2 units/acre. The proposed average lot size is 5,526 square
feet with a range from 4,997 to 9,604 square feet. City Code affords PUD’s an increase in density
from what is permitted in the underlying zone when the Planning Commission finds that the increase
in density is compensated by increased amenities and improved design that are proportional to the
density increase. The proposed amenities and design features are covered later in this report.

Existing Development Agreement

As mentioned earlier, a development agreement exists on this property. At the time the zone change
on the Pinnacle at Highbury Apartments (the apartments directly east of the subject property) was
being considered by the City, the City Council required that the subject property include a
development agreement to ensure that it be developed as single family detached homes as part of the
approval of the zone change on the apartments. The existing development agreement only includes
the following two provisions:

   •   The property shall be developed as single family detached homes.
   •   All lots shall be at least 8,000 square feet in area.

At the time this agreement was finalized in 2017, City Code included significant housing standards
for minimum house sizes, exterior materials, and design features. However, since that time, the City
was required by the State to reduce or eliminate many of these standards. At this point, any homes
built on the property under the existing development agreement would only need to follow the
standards in the City’s current ordinance, which are found in Sections 7-11-203 through 7-11-211.

Proposed Development Agreement

To support this General Plan change application, the applicant has submitted a development
agreement proposal. The high points from this agreement are summarized below:

   •   Minimum House Size: The proposed minimum square footage of finished, above-ground,

       proposed.
   •   Home Design Features: All homes will include a front porch. Homes will surpass Energy
       Star standards. Design variation is required for homes on adjoining lots.
   •   Minimum Setbacks: Proposed setbacks are 25’ in the front for garages, 15’ in the front for
       porches, 5’ on sides, 15’ in the rear for rear porches and homes next to open space (20’ in the
       rear in all other cases).

coverages. The lack of standards was intentional to allow for design flexibility for applicants who
could essentially propose their own standards through a development agreement. Another unique

detailed development agreements for developments within the MXD zone essential in staff’s view.
