     3. Discussion and Action Items
        3.1. Consideration of an Amendment to the Herriman Business Center Community
             Development Project Area Plan and Budget – Blake Thomas, Community
             Development Director
     Community Development Director Blake Thomas explained that the CDA was established to
     generate additional revenue through tax increment financing (TIF) for infrastructure and
     commercial development. Interlocal agreements with entities govern the use of the funds.
     Director Thomas noted an amendment to the interlocal agreement with Salt Lake County,
     approved in September, capped the number of housing units within the project area. The
     county requested that the project area plan be updated before approving the amendment.

     Director Thomas turned the time over to Zions Bank Vice President Susie Becker to present
     changes made to the project area plan. Vice President Becker explained that while the
     boundaries of the CDA had not changed, the amendment involved reallocating some non-
     residential acres to residential use due to market shifts. The overall impacts of the changes
     would result in more rapid absorption and increased taxable value, which would benefit the
     taxing entities. However, the revenue to the agency would not significantly increase due to
     the agreed-upon caps.

     Director Ohrn moved to approve Resolution R2025-01 adopting the amended project area
     plan and budget for the Herriman Business Center Community Development Project Area
     dated January 2025; Director Hodges seconded the motion.

     The vote is recorded as follows:

     Chair Lorin Palmer           Aye
