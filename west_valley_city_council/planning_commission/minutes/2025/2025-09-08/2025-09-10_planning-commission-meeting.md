SMI-7-2025
Mann Way Subdivision
4030 West Mann Way/4 Lots/.50 Acres
R-1-4 Zone

BACKGROUND
Jake Hone is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Mann Way Subdivision. The
subdivision will consist of 4 lots on .50 acres and is zoned R-1-4. The proposed subdivision is
bordered on the north, south and west sides by existing residential housing and the east by 4000
West.

STAFF/AGENCY CONCERNS:
Fire Department:
• Existing Fire Hydrant on Mann Way will suffice for this project.

Granger Hunter Improvement District:
• Project will need to run availability for water, sewer and fire protection.
• Subject to design and review inspections.
Utility Agencies:
• Subject to all standard easement locations.

Public Works:
• Authorization required of ditch/water users for any abandonment, relocation, piping or any
other modifications to existing ditches or irrigation structures.

• Will need to replace damaged sidewalk along the south side of Mann Way and will need to
install standard City improvements, i.e., parkstrip and sidewalk. If there is existing damage to
the curb and gutter, that will need to be replaced as well.

• Revisions to the plat required.

• Will need to coordinate subdivision name with County Auditor's Office.

• Follow recommendations outlined in the soils report.

• On site retention of storm water required if it cannot be directed to the right-of-way.

• Coordination of irrigation ditch with canal company required.

• Will need to evaluate whether the existing drive approach on 4000 West needs to be removed.

Building Inspections:
• Follow recommendations outlined in the soils report.

ISSUES:
• The developer is proposing a minor subdivision to be known as the Mann Way Subdivision.
The subdivision will consist of 4 lots on .50 acres. Although zoned R-1-4 which allows a
minimum lot area of 4,000 square feet, the smallest lot size is 5,225 square feet with an average
lot size of 5,485 square feet. All lot area and frontage requirements of the R-1-4 zone have been
met.

• Access to the subdivision will be gained from Mann Way. Although access does exist along
4000 West it is anticipated that all new dwellings will front Mann Way. Curb, gutter and
sidewalk are existing along both Mann Way and 4000 West. The sidewalk along Mann Way is
lifting in several places due to the large trees. With all new subdivision development, the City
Engineering Division is recommending that parkstrip be added and that a new sidewalk be
installed per City standards which will replace the damaged walk along the frontage.

• A soils report has been submitted. Ground water was not encountered to a depth of 8 feet.
However, should ground water be present during excavation of new dwellings, an updated report
would be required to determine appropriate basement elevations.

• The corner lot has access on both Mann Way and 4000 West. A drive approach for the existing
single-family dwelling is currently shared with the single-family dwelling on the south side.

PUD-7-2025
Heather on the Parkway Amended
2875 South 6105 West
RM Zone

BACKGROUND
Ken Milne is requesting a plat amendment for the first phase of the Heather on the Parkway PUD
to adjust square footage of certain units within the PUD and increase the percentage of open space
within the community.

STAFF/AGENCY COMMENTS:
Staff and agency comments related to the construction of this project were provided during the
preliminary review process and are still applicable for this development.

ISSUES:
 The Heather on the Parkway is a residential community for senior living. The overall project
consists of 84 units on 13.8 acres. The first phase of this project was recorded in September 2023
and consists of 38 units.

Residential units are planned to be one story in height. All units will have a loft option that would
add approximately 900 square feet to each dwelling if selected by future buyers. Unit sizes
originally planned for this community were 2731, 2037, 1903, 1966 square feet.

 As development of the project began, it became apparent to the developer that while there was
interest in the larger units, there was even greater interest in units that would be more in line with
what was approved in the development agreement. That square footage number is approximately
1500 square feet, which is the same square footage that the development agreement requires.

 Over the last few months, the developer has been formulating plans to construct a smaller
bungalow at 1500 square feet. They have worked with staff to ensure that the architectural
integrity of building elevations are similar to what was originally approved. Building materials
will be consistent with what has already been constructed, and additional colors will be used
throughout to provide variety.

 The developer informed staff that while phase 2 has yet to be submitted, he is planning for both
the large and smaller units depending upon demand.

There are two benefits that come about regarding the requested change in square footage.

       1. Individual dwelling units will just be connected at the garage wall. Meaning there won’t
       be any living spaces adjacent to each other. This will likely provide for a quieter
       environment inside of each dwelling.

       2. The overall open space will be increased. Having smaller units will increase the open
       space by approximately 14,000 square feet within the first phase.

Godfrey Industrial Park No. 3
2349 South 5900 West
M Zone (13.5 Acres)

The applicant, Scott Godfrey representing Godfrey Industrial Park No. 3, is requesting
conditional approval for a flex industrial, office/warehouse, heavy truck and trailer service and
outside storage at 2349 South 5900 West. This property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and the
West Valley City General Plan designates this area as Light Manufacturing. The surrounding
zone on all sides is Manufacturing (M). The surrounding uses include large office/warehouse
complexes and similar trucking facilities.

The overall development will consist of seven (7) buildings. There are also two wetland areas
that are being coordinated with the Army Corps of Engineers. These areas will most likely
remain undeveloped with natural vegetation and will be required to be maintained free of
weeds and debris.

The southern portion of the development can potentially have 12 units that are 3,500 SF in size.
These units are laid out to have an office, restroom and warehouse or light industrial area with
overhead doors on the front and rear of the buildings for pass through access. Each tenant space
has been allocated three parking stalls so the uses/tenant mix will be monitored as building
permits and business licenses are submitted and the parking will be evaluated periodically to
ensure that the site is in compliance. These buildings will be required to meet the Commercial
Design Standards along the front primary facades.

These south units will also have outside storage yards of approximately .2 acres with one larger
yard of .40 acres for Unit F-1. The exterior access to the yards will all be gated and paved. As

surfaced unless the tenant proposes to store tracked equipment or material storage that does not

The north development is for heavy truck and trailer service. There will be a 5,200 SF

   •   Option 2: The area outlined in RED on the site plan must be paved and the remaining

two street frontages so there will be a 20’ wide landscaped setback, measured from the property
line. These areas will contain a minimum of 1 tree for every 30’ of frontage and 50% plant
material coverage not including tree canopies. This is required along the entire length of both
frontages and detention areas must also be outside of this setback.

The perimeter of the development will be screened with a block masonry wall, which also
screens the outside storage. The specific details of the wall have not been provided at this time
and gray unfished concrete block is prohibited. Staff also recommends that the wall be extended
along the north frontage, approximately 250’ in length along the developed edge of the adjacent
north property boundary, to screen that section of the development from public view

The details of signage have not been discussed at this stage in the development. All signage shall
comply with the requirements set forth in the Sign Ordinance and require a building permit prior
to installation. There are several dumpsters provided throughout the complex. These will all be

Right Side Up RV Display of Merchandise
6028 West 4700 South
M Zone (2.8 Acres)

The applicant, Right Side Up RV, is requesting conditional use approval for display of
merchandise for a new RV sales and service facility at 6028 West 4700 South. The zoning for
this area is M, manufacturing. The West Valley City General Plan designates this area as light
manufacturing. The surrounding zone is manufacturing on all sides with R-1-4 to the north
across the railroad tracks. The surrounding uses include office/warehouse and other light
industrial or freight/trucking uses and residential homes to the north.

Right Side Up RV is currently under review as a Permitted Use (PR-19-25) for RV Sales and
Service. At this time, the applicant is requesting to construct an RV display area for the
southeast corner of the site. Per §7-7-118 of the West Valley City Municipal Code the Planning
Commission may permit display of merchandise within the setback area if it maintains a 10’
setback to the right-of-way.

The display area included in the reduced setback does have frontage along a High Image
Arterial (4700 South) and would be incorporated into the landscape design and maintain a 10’
setback from the right-of-way. The landscaping includes the required plant material in 7-10
with additional landscaping at the entrance to enhance this area.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing, as well as the
following conditions:

   1. The display area shall be located to the east of the main entrance as outlined on the
      approved site plan.
   2. The display area must maintain a 10’ setback from the right-of-way along 4700
      South.
   3. The display area shall be incorporated into the landscaping per the approved plan
      including landscaping outlined in section 7-10 of the West Valley City Municipal Code.
   4. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

at 2255 S. Technology Park Way. This property is zoned M, manufacturing and the West Valley

manufacturing.

that time. At this location they primarily work with HVAC, security, and fire alarm systems and
are now in need of some additional space. This includes a 2,760 SF warehouse addition and .21
acre storage yard.

Storage for the facility will primarily be located within trailers and Conex cargo containers. The
gravel area in the yard is for positioning the trailers. The asphalt path shown is for forklifts to
transfer materials between the yard and the warehouse.

onto the roadway.
This storage area is set back off the road and behind the parking area and building and will be
secured with 6’ chain link fence. Slats will be added along the access area for screening. The

The site is very well maintained with mature landscaping and no other site changes are proposed.

C-15-2025
Aisza Wilde – Commissary with Outside Storage
1802 W Alexander St.
M Zone (0.71 Acres)

The applicant, Aisza Wilde, is requesting conditional use approval for a commissary kitchen
with outside storage at 1802 W Alexander St. This property is zoned M, manufacturing and the
West Valley City General Plan designates this area as Light Manufacturing. The surrounding
zone is Manufacturing on all sides. The surrounding uses include warehouse, office and light
manufacturing.

The proposed use for the building is a commissary kitchen, which is a commercially licensed
food preparation and storage facility. It will be used by multiple food business operators
including caterers, food truck operators, meal prep services, or small food producers. The
commissary kitchen will be equipped to meet local health and safety codes, and include shared-
use cooking equipment, cold and dry storage, dishwashing stations, and waste disposal.

The site will be designed to accommodate approximately 19 mobile units including trucks and
trailers. These will all be stored on a hard surface behind a secured and screen fenced area. These
units will primarily park on site overnight or when not in active use for vending as required by
the Salt Lake Valley Health Department.

The facility will be open 24 hours a day to accommodate various schedules and provide the
required services necessary for these vendors to operate. Peak activity is anticipated during
normal business hours (e.g., 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM), with lower volumes during early morning
