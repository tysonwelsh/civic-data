PUBLIC NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Magna Metro Township Council will hold a business
meeting on the 9th day of April 2024 at the Webster Center, 8952 West Magna Main Street Magna,
Utah as follows:

** Portions of the meetings may be closed for reasons allowed by statute. Motions relating to any of
the items listed below, including final action, may be taken.

6:00 PM – PUBLIC MEETING
 1. CALL TO ORDER
 2. Determine Quorum
 3. Pledge of Allegiance

4. PUBLIC COMMENTS (Limited to 3 minutes per person)
   Any person wishing to comment on any item not otherwise scheduled for a public hearing on the
   agenda may address the Council at this point by stepping to the microphone and giving their name
   for the record. The Town Council is interested in hearing directly from residents. In an effort to be
   both transparent and responsive, the Town Council previously adopted rules to help govern public
   meetings. As such, Councilmembers cannot respond directly to comments during ‘public comment.’
   However, Magna staff will be responsible for responding directly to citizens who request a response.
   Comments should be limited to not more than three (3) minutes unless additional time is
   authorized by the Governing Body.

5. Unified Police Department Report [Chief Del Craig]
