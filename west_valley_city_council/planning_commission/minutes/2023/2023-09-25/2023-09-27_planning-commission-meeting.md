James Warner
An amendment to Section 7-14-403 of the zoning ordinance to allow non-masonry materials
on the street facing exteriors of buildings within the 5600 West Gateway Overlay Zone

James Warner with Truckpro, LC has requested an amendment to Section 7-14-403 of the zoning
ordinance to allow non-masonry materials on the street facing exteriors of buildings within the
5600 West Gateway Overlay Zone. The ordinance currently states: “All Buildings shall be
constructed using one hundred percent masonry on any Street facing Façade”. The applicant is
requesting that the City allow “architectural metal siding as well as ceramic and wood textures
that are being used extensively in most cities on higher-end buildings”.

The applicant’s justification for this application is as follows:

       “The types of textures used in the past 5-10 years on higher-end buildings are mainly
       ACM (Architectural Metal), ceramic tiles and wood siding. The facility that we are
       considering building has specific requirements to qualify it to be one of 22 INEOS
       Grenadier dealerships in the U.S. and the only dealership in Utah. The buildings require
       high-end ACM and possibly wood or simulated wood look on ceramic or metal. By
       approving these materials, we believe that the city will attract higher-end buildings and
       businesses as most of them are using these premium materials. With the current standards
       and requirements, businesses that the city might otherwise want may not even consider
       West Valley City as if they understood the current code and did not want to go through
       the text amendment process and cost.”

The 5600 West Gateway Overlay Zone, which was adopted in April of 2004 (Ordinance 03-82),
extends from the 2100 South Expressway (Highway 201) south to 3100 South, and 250 feet on
either side of the Right-of-way line for 5600 West. Ordinance 03-82 included the following
whereas statement: “the City would like to encourage development in this area that is
aesthetically pleasing, complimentary to adjacent uses, and appropriate for an important gateway
to the City”. After reviewing the supporting materials for Ordinance 03-82, it is apparent to staff
that the City did not want to perpetuate the type of metal buildings that existed within the
Overlay Zone.

The architectural metal siding proposed by the applicant has been used in the City on
commercial buildings such as Embassy Suites, Granger Medical, SpringHill Suites, Fairbourne
Office Tower, and Mountain America Credit Union. Tile has been used on the SpringHill Suites
as well as the Lofts on 35th Townhomes. A faux wood composite material was used on the
Embassy Suites.

Staff is supportive of allowing more flexibility in materials within the Overlay Zone; however,
we believe the ordinance language should be specific enough to prohibit the lower quality
materials the ordinance was originally intended to exclude. As such staff suggests the following
language for the Overlay Zone:

“Only the following exterior materials shall be allowed on all Street facing Façades of Buildings:
      masonry which shall include brick; stone; colored, painted, or textured concrete block;
       stucco or EIFS, fiber cement siding, and architectural concrete;
      architectural metal siding that is used only as a veneer and not as structural sheeting;
      ceramic tile;
      faux wood composites; and
      other materials that, at the discretion of the Planning Commission, are comparable to the
       above listed materials.”

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. This application should be approved subject to the language recommended by
      staff.

   2. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the
      public hearing.

   3. Denial. The 5600 West Overlay Zone should remain as it is.

C-18-2023
CR England Expansion
4527 West SR-201 S. Frontage Rd. M
Zone (40.96 Acres)
The applicant, CR England, is requesting conditional use approval for expansion of their truck
center and trailer storage located at 4527 West SR-201 S. Frontage Rd. The zoning for this area
is M, manufacturing. The West Valley City General Plan designates this area as light
manufacturing. The surrounding zone is manufacturing on all sides. The surrounding uses
include similar freight/trucking uses with Wheeler Machinery to the west and Asahi Refining
located to the east.

The applicant is requesting approval for various portions of the CR England campus that have
been improved over the last several years and are occupied by trailer storage areas that are
leased as well as truck parking. The first area is a crushed asphalt lot on the northeast section of
the site that is u-shaped around Asahi Refining. This area is used for storage of leased trailers.
There is no direct access to a public right-of-way and the traffic in and out is intermittent and
not daily. The west edge of this u-shaped area has frontage along the 2100 South Frontage Rd
and is set back about 150’ behind a landscaped recreation area. This section of fence is chain
link and slats will be added to provide screening.

There are 2 lots to the south east that are paved and have been used by Amazon for van storage
as well as for FedEx trucks. The last area is to the south west. This is crushed asphalt and is for
overflow trailer storage. The remaining areas of CR England have been part of past approvals
and are all paved. These areas are used for employee, truck and cab parking as well as the
maintenance facilities and offices etc.

The entire north frontage along the 2100 S Frontage Road will be required to comply with the
7-10 landscaping standards as well as the 7-2-117 right-of-way standards for curb, gutter
sidewalk and street lights. The width of the section of landscaping along the building frontage
will remain as it is currently, however trees must be added as well as the required right-of-way
improvements.

The 4800 West landscaping improvements will also be completed as part of this project. That
will include trees planted 1 every 30’ with additional plant material coverage of 50%
minimum. Sidewalk will be located on the west side of 4800 West due to the location of the
right-of-way and sidewalk on 2400 South, so that is not part of this application.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following conditions:

        1. The properties discussed in this application shall be improved per the approved site
           plan which includes crushed asphalt trailer parking and paved vehicle/van parking.
           There shall be no junk storage or salvage uses at this site.

        2. The site surfacing shall be maintained and free of weeds.

        3. The section of trailer parking east of 4605 West that has frontage on the 2100 S
           Frontage Road shall be setback a minimum of 150’ and must be screened from
              public view. The screening may be chain link with slats.

        4. All parking and maneuvering areas must have the required lighting as outlined in 7-
           9-113.

        5. The overall minimum required landscaping for the site is 5%. The north frontage
           shall be designed in accordance with the Landscaping Standards for High Image
           Arterials (7-10). The width of the section of landscaping along the building
           frontage will remain as it is currently, however the required trees must be added
           per the Code.

        6. The 4800 West frontage shall include 20’ of landscaping, measured from the
           curb, and one tree every 30’ with additional plant material coverage of 50%
           minimum.

        7. All requirements of affected departments and agencies must be met, including but
           not limited to WVC Public Works and section 7-2-117.

        8. This use is subject to review upon a valid complaint.

C-23-2023
Squared Away Properties, LLC (Outside Storage)
2601 South 2700 West
M Zone (1.53 acres)

The applicant, Jim Arbuckle with Squared Away Properties, LLC, is requesting conditional use
approval for outside storage at 2601 South 2700 West. This property is zoned M, manufacturing
and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as Light Manufacturing. The
surrounding zone is Manufacturing on all sides and the surrounding uses include warehouse/light
industrial with Ready Made Concrete to the south and Roger Knight Construction to the north.

The current owner of this property has been in the process of updating the site and has requested
a conditional use for outside storage. The storage area would be located on the south side of the
building. As part of this use review the site layout and parking requirements must be evaluated.
Therefore, it is important to understand a little bit of history with this property.

This building was originally built in the mid-1970’s as a light industrial warehouse (C- 305-
1973). At that time 2590 South was a private street/access. Therefore, historically there have not
been improvements in that area along the north frontage. Parking has also not ever been
designated in this area. In 2021, an application was submitted for a Church. That change of use
triggered the installation of improvements to that north frontage that included landscaping, curb,
gutter and streetlights. Due to the narrow depth of the lot and the increased parking need on that
north side the applicant requested a variance from the Board of Adjustment (B-5-2021) The
Board granted the variance for a reduced setback that would include 10’ of landscaping from the
property line and 10’ of landscaping in the right-of-way for a total of 20’.

That use ended up not coming to the site however, that process established a parking and site
layout. The property owner has since re-paved and repaired the parking lot but chose a different
layout from that plan. The concern now is the parking stalls are located out into the right-of-way,
which creates an unsafe situation and the outside storage use is eliminating a lot of parking from
the south side of the building that does need to be relocated to a conforming location on site.
Therefore, Staff recommends that the parking layout be completed on the site in accordance with
the variance approval and setback reduction. This can be achieved by repainting the stalls and
creating a painted buffer and installing parking curb stops. That plan was also not reviewed or
approved by Public Works. They will have to review the access spacing, even for just painted
stalls and access. This ensures that when the physical improvements to the ROW and
landscaping are triggered in the future there is no confusion or discrepancies. The actual parking
calcs are also still being reviewed and evaluated and so that final required number will be part of
that formal site approval process.

The outside storage will be located on an existing asphalt surface. The items that will be stored
in this area will be cars from the two automotive tenants, and finished metal products from the
powder coating company and machine shop. The storage will not exceed 6 feet in height. The
area is directly adjacent to 2700 West which is a High Image Arterial so the masonry screening
will be required along that frontage. That masonry screening will also be extended along the
south property line for approximately 95’ on the shared property line with Ready Made
Concrete (RMC), starting at the 20’ setback and running east to the edge of the RMC building.
There will be an access gates with slats and a fire lane access must also be maintained through
this area. The east end of the lot will also be gated but slats are not required there due to the
parking and storage location.

Site maintenance issues were previously addressed with the property owner through
business license reviews and an enforcement case so the landscaping on site has been
repaired and installed in accordance with the 7-10 standards.

Staff Alternatives:
Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:

              1. Outside storage is only permitted on the south side of the site and must be
                 located on the improved hard surface area.

              2. The outside storage area must be screened in accordance with 7-7-119
                 including masonry along the 2700 West frontage as outlined in the report.

              3. All landscaped areas need to be maintained free of weeds. The 2700 West
                 frontage shall contain the required landscaping per the 7-10 standards in the
                 City Code. The south frontage shall be landscaped with trees at a ratio of 1 for
                 every 30’ of frontage as well as 50% live plant material coverage.

              4. A parking plan shall be approved by West Valley City that complies with the
                 standards in 7-9 of the City Code and the variance approval from B-5- 2021.
                 Parking stalls shall not be located within the public right-of-way.

              5. Must meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies.

              6. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.

C-24-2023
Rocky Mountain Power Lay Down Yard 5875
West 4700 South
M Zone (6.90 Acres)

The applicant, Rocky Mountain Power (RMP), is requesting conditional use approval for a
laydown storage yard as part of their public utility installation facility. This property is zoned
M, Manufacturing and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as Utility. The
surrounding zone is M, Manufacturing on all sides and the surrounding uses include Ken Garff
(NAC) to the east and south and an existing RMP substation to the west.

This property is located adjacent to an existing RMP substation and will be utilized as a storage
yard for utility infrastructure equipment and materials. Due to the nature of the yard the area
will be surfaced with gravel, which is typical for the utility yards and stations. The area will
also be screened. Since this is adjacent to 4700 South and is a high image arterial the north
section of wall and approximately 150’ of the east side will be masonry to comply with City
Code section 7-7-119. The landscaping along that frontage will also comply with the 7-10
standards of the City Code.

RMP is currently reviewing the grading and their security fencing needs along the east side
but their intent is to have the screen wall on the east property line so there is not additional
landscaping to maintain and interfere with their security protocols. If the screening wall is
moved off the property line they will maintain that area as landscaping with 50% live plant
material coverage and 1 tree for every 60’ of frontage. That exact design will be determined
during the formal plan review process.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing, as well as the
following conditions:

        1. The site shall be improved and landscaped per the approved plans including
           landscaping along that frontage to comply with the 7-10 standards of the WVC
           Municipal Code.
        2. The north frontage and first 150’ of the east side of the site shall be screened with
           a masonry wall to comply with section 7-7- 119 of the WVC Municipal Code.

        3. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing or to allow
for a revised site/landscape plans to be submitted.
