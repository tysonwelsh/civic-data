2. PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER AND APPROVE THE REORGANIZATION OF THE

SOLDIER SUMMIT SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT AS A LOCAL DISTRICT.
-TIM TAYLOR, Attorney

TIM TAYLOR, COUNTY DEPUTY ATTORNEY - STATED THAT IN 1984 THE TOWN
SOLDIER SUMMIT WAS DISSOLVED THROUGH A STIPULATED COURT
AGREEMENT AND THROUGH A RESOLUTION UTAH COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
CREATED THE SOLDIER SUMMIT SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT AND PART OF
THAT RESOLUTION WAS THAT IT WAS TO BE GOVERNED BY A TRIAD OF
OFFICERS AND THIS WOULD BE ONE COMMISSIONER FROM UTAH COUNTY,
ONE COMMISSIONER FROM WASATCH COUNTY AND A RESIDENT OR PROPERTY
OWNER FROM THE SOLDIER SUMMIT SERVICE DISTRICT. IT ALSO APPOINTED
THE UTAH COUNTY AUDITOR TO HANDLE ALL THE FINANCIAL AFFAIRS
ASSOCIATED WITH THE DISTRICT AND THE COUNTY ATTORNEY’S OFFICE HAS
BEEN THE LEGAL ADVISOR. IN 2013, THE STATE LEGISLATURE PASSED
LEGISLATION THAT ALLOWED A SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT TO REORGANIZE
INTO A LOCAL DISTRICT. THIS DOES NOT AFFECT THE WATER SERVICE,
OPERATIONS NOR THE NEW WELL. ONCE THIS RESOLUTION IS
APPROVED/SIGNED, THE COUNTY WOULD PASS IT ON TO THE LIEUTENANT
GOVERNOR’S OFFICE WHO WOULD THEN CERTIFY IT TO MAKE IT A LOCAL
DISTRICT.

COMMISSIONERS, TIM TAYLOR, JOSH DANIELS (COUNTY CLERK/AUDITOR)
DISCUSS REVENUE, CURRENT FEES, HANDOFF AND TRAINING, ETC.
