Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council held Tuesday, May 3, 2022, at Lehi City
Hall, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at approximately 4:00 PM.

Present:     Mark Johnson, Mayor
             Paige Albrecht, Council Member
             Chris Condie, Council Member
             Paul Hancock, Council Member
             Katie Koivisto, Council Member
             Mike Southwick, Council Member

Others Present:     Jason Walker, City Administrator; Cameron Boyle, Assistant City
Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney; Kim Struthers, Community Development Director;
Lorin Powell, City Engineer; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Steve Marchbanks, Parks
Superintendent; Beau Thomas, Assistant to the City Administrator; Shaye Ruitenbeek,
Management Analyst; and Teisha Wilson, City Recorder;

1. Welcome and Opening Comment
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
   Albrecht attended virtually. Councilor Koivisto offered the opening comment.


2. Discussion Items
   2.a. Review and Discussion of Budget Request Items- All City Departments.
   Every City Department was available to present their budget requests to the Council. The
   Council asked clarifying questions and made suggestions to be included in the final budget.

The Mayor asked for a Closed Session to discus the character, professional competence, or
physical or mental health of an individual.

    Motion: Councilor Hancock moved to adjourn into a Closed Executive Session to discuss
    the character, professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual.
    Councilor Southwick seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

The meeting adjourned for the Closed Session at approximately 6:45 p.m.
The meeting reconvened at approximately 7:00 p.m.
