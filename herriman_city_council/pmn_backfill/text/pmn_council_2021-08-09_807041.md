                               SPECIAL HERRIMAN CITY
                           COUNCIL WORK MEETING MINUTES
                                        Monday, August 9, 2021
                                      Approved January 26, 2022

The following are the minutes of the City Council Meeting of the Herriman City Council. The meeting was held on
Monday, August 9, 2021 at 5:30 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main
Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City Hall, on the
City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Mayor Pro Tempore Jared Henderson

Councilmembers Present: Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith.

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas, Assistant City Manager
Tami Moody, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Alan Rae, City Attorney Chase Andrizzi,
Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette, City Engineer Jonathan Bowers, Community Development
Director Blake Thomas, Public Works Director Justun Edwards, Planning Manager Clint Spencer, and Police Chief
Troy Carr.

Councilmembers Excused: Mayor David Watts

conduct the meeting. Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

been determined to be in compliance with the planned community. Section 2.2: the approved maximum residential

last statement in the section read that the developer would have to comply with design guidelines in the Master
Development Agreement. It clarified the developer could build up to those many units and still be in compliance.

Section 2.2.2 identified specific housing types. At least 30-percent must be single family dwelling units detached and
at least 33-percent must be individually platted dwelling units, which was defined as single family homes,
condominiums and town homes. The remaining 37-percent could be apartments. He offered some numbers as an
example of those percentages reporting these numbers are used to project the impacts of traffic. A Traffic impact
study had been conducted and was currently being updated. Councilmember Henderson asked about Public

other project. The Council asked for further explanation on what this would entail. Director of Public Works Justun

resolution procedure in section 2.4.3 if needed. Mayor Watts asked what the timeframe would be to start the process.
