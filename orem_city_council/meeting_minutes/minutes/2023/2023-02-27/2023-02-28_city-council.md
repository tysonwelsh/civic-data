PERSONAL APPEARANCES

Linnea Pugmire stated that she is a citizen of Orem and has been involved in the Orem City
programs as well as schools in Orem. She stated some topics that were discussed at the city
council 2023 retreat. She stated some things that she would like council members to clarify on
the council portal. She spoke about the library director appointment and qualifications associated
with it.

Megan Loyd is a resident of Orem she stated that she is a frequent visitor at the Orem library and
her children participate in library activities. In recent months she has noticed differences in the
library polity and she is disappointed with them. She discussed grant funding, digital collections,
resources, and state code. She gave the council some advice on keeping transparency clear within
Orem.

Cissy Rassmussen is an Orem citizen and she participates in the library activities. She would like
to see transparency within the library and she spoke about the newly appointed library director.
She spoke about voting, past elections, what Orem residents really want, and managing the
library.

Rita Christensen spoke about her past experiences with working for the Orem library. She spoke
about her background and cultural identity. She spoke about book display discussions that have
happened at the library with staff, activities that she has participated in within the library, the
library directory position, library board, state grants, and statements made in the daily herald.

Wes Parish stated that he has been a long time resident of Orem, he expressed that he is
concerned with the library content and materials. He also stated that he has read the statutes and
believes that 30% of some local libraries are out of compliance. He gave some advice to the
council in regards to the content in the library.

Jennifer Jensen spoke about an experience that she had with walking in an area near parkside
elementary school. She spoke about an incident that happened within the area and she expressed
her concern with gun safety, children safety and public awareness. She spoke about another
example when she believes that the Orem police department should have handled better.

Mayor Young & Mr. Bybee let Mrs. Jensen know that she may help resolve the issue with Chief
Josh Adams after the meeting.

Greg Carlile spoke about his concerns with the Orem Library and the issues that have been
brought up in the media and in the organization. He advised the public to approach the situation
with open minds.

Laura Cole stated that she is concerned with the lack of transparency, no paper trail, and library
concerms. She would like to have more of an idea of what is going on and the why behind the
actions of the City Council.

Amy Carr spoke about her involvement with the library, and she spoke about past meetings
statements. She spoke about state statutes, grant money, library board, library policy, advisory
board, funds awarded, and library director.

Timo Christensen spoke about his current employment within Orem City. He spoke about him
traveling to work in a bike lane, he spoke about an experience where there was ice chunks and
snow in the bike lane.

Karen Adamson expressed her gratitude to the Mayor and City Council. She believes that by
