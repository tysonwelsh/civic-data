PUBLIC HEARING:
 Mayor Justin D. Seely opened a public hearing to accept public comment on an interfund loan. The
funds for the loan would come from the Gas Department fund balance and pay for the purchase of a
piece of real estate located at 158 N 100 E Nephi. The general fund will pay back the loan of $429,.000
over 20 years. The proposed purpose of the purchase of the property is to have the Fire District
purchase the property within five years for potentially building a new Nephi City fire station. When the
Fire District purchases the property, the interfund loan would be paid back.
There was no public comment given.

RESOLUTION 01-21-2025 - INTERFUND LOAN FROM THE GAS DEPARTMENT TO THE GENERAL FUND
ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson explained that recently the council elected to purchase a property that would eventually
be acquired by the Fire District for the construction of a Nephi Fire Station. That purchase temporarily
was funded by one of the utility departments, but it would be best funded by the city’s General Fund. In
order to accomplish this, an interfund loan would need to be approved by the city council from the
Natural Gas Fund to the General Fund.
 The resolution to create the interfund loan with the payment schedule was reviewed. If the Fire
District agrees to the terms presented to them in their last board meeting, it is likely that they will
purchase the property within the next 5 years. At that point, the interfund loan can be paid off and the
loan would dissolve. According to the draft agreement with the Fire District, the City will absorb all
interest costs associated with the loan and just the principal would be paid off by the Fire District.
   Councilor Travis Worwood moved to adopt Resolution 01-21-2025. Councilor Skip Worwood
seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

CIVIL SCIENCE PRESENTED UPDATED PARK DESIGNS:

   Jeff Peay, Landscape Architect/Project Manager for Civil Science presented a revised Red Cliff’s Park
design. Councilor Travis Worwood asked about the irrigation water line that runs through the park. Mr.
Peay said they are aware of the line and have designed the park to alleviate any problems in the future
with the water line. Mr. Atkinson pointed out that one of the reasons to have a park design is so that
the city would know where to stub out utilities on 10th South. Mr. Peay stated that rubberized tiles
would be used under the playground. The tiles are cost effective, easily repaired, and make the
playground ADA accessible. Councilor Travis Worwood asked if the open field area would be lighted.
Mr. Peay answered that there are currently no plans for lighting the field, but lighting would be installed
for the pickleball courts, parking lot, and pavilions.
    Mr. Peay next presented the Crimson Flats Baseball Complex design. He stated that Recreation
Director Craig Oswald had requested that the fields be designed so that they would feature a view of
Mt. Nebo. The design showed a rental space area, a referee changing area, 400 space parking lot, a
small playground area, a ticket booth and fencing to control entrance, batting cages, maintenance area,
and a warm-up area. Councilor Parady stated that shade covers would be needed. The council agreed
with the designs and directed staff to move forward in the next steps of the project.
   Mayor Seely asked if some skate elements could be implemented in the park. A discussion ensued
about the planned pump park and liability at these types of parks. The importance of proper signage to
use the park at one's own risk was discussed.

OUT-OF-STATE POLICY UPDATE CONSIDERED:

   Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that from time to time, city employees will travel out of the
state of Utah for training, conferences, certification courses, or other similar purposes. In order to keep
the city council notified on how taxes and fees are being spent, the Nephi City Council will monitor when
out of state travel occurs.

    1. City departments are required to present their request to travel out of the state of Utah for
        training, conferences, certification courses, or other purposes to the City Council at a public
        regular or work session meeting.
    2. Funding for any travel out of the state of Utah will be approved through the budget process.
        Any travel out of the state of Utah not approved through the budget process must be approved
        through an amended budget.
    3. Due to the number of events that occur in Mesquite, Nevada for city departments, this location
        will be assumed to be within the state of Utah for the purposes of this policy.
Mayor Seely wanted to know how employees and vehicles are insured when traveling out of state.
Councilor Travis Worwood stated that he wanted the administration to always be aware when
employees are traveling out of state for city business. The council agreed to consider the changes in the
out-of-state policy when his questions about insurance have been answered.
