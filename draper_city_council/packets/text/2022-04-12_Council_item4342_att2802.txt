                                 ORDINANCE NO. 1530

    AN ORDINANCE AMENDING DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE 2-4-060
                 “CONSTITUTIONAL TAKING ISSUES”

WHEREAS, Utah Code 63L-4-101, et seq. is known as the “Constitutional Takings
Issues Act (the “Act”); and

WHEREAS, the Act requires Draper City to enact an ordinance establishing guidelines
to assist the City in identifying actions involving the physical taking or exaction of
private real property that may have constitutional taking issues; and

WHEREAS, Draper City enacted Draper City Municipal Code (“DCMC”) 2-4-060 to
comply with the Act; and

WHEREAS, the Draper City Council wishes to amend DCMC 2-4-060 to clarify the
factors staff should follow in analyzing a potential constitutional taking issue, to clarify
appeals and designate the city’s Appeals and Variance Hearing Officer as the individual
to hear appeals of constitutional taking issues in accordance with the Act, and to make
other minor edits to the text; and

WHEREAS, the proposed amendments comply with state law and benefit the health,
safety, and welfare of Draper residents.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

Section 1. Amendment. Draper City Municipal Code 2-4-060 is hereby amended as
shown in Exhibit A attached hereto.

Section 2. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to correct
any punctuation, spelling, formatting, or similar errors prior to submitting this
Ordinance to Sterling Codifiers.

Section 3. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be
severable.

Section 4. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of
final passage.
