         The purpose of the work session was to review the agenda for the City Council meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.,
discuss proposed amendments to Youth Council bylaws, and discuss Council business.

Agenda Review
        Chair Richey and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Council Business
         Proposed amendments to Youth Council bylaws: Communications Director Garside used the aid of a PowerPoint
presentation to summarize proposed changes to the Youth Council bylaws as follows:
              • Rename the organization from Ogden Youth City Council to Ogden City Youth Council (OCYC) for
                   consistency with Ogden City branding guidelines and replace all references of the abbreviations OYCC to
                   OCYC accordingly.
              • Rename the Ogden Youth Executive Council to Youth Council Executive Committee and replace all
                   references of the abbreviations OYEC to YCEC accordingly.
              • Allow exceptions to residency requirements on a case-by-case basis, approved by a majority vote of the
                   OCYC.
              • Change the meeting date from the first and third Wednesday of each month to the first and third Monday
                   and allowing flexibility in the schedule depending on activities and availability of the OCYC.
              • Requiring that in order to be eligible to serve as Chair, an OCYC member must have one full school year of
                   experience serving on the OCYC.
              • Change attendance policy to apply only to those elected to the Youth Council Executive Committee,
                   including the potential loss of a YCEC position, which would then be filled through a special election
                   within the OCYC.
         For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
         Discussion among the Council and Mr. Garside centered on opportunities for improving recruitment of students
from local schools to participate with the OCYC and terms of members appointed to the OCYC.
         OCYC Chair Ellie Gomez then provided the Council with a report of recent accomplishments of the Youth Council
and the events/training opportunities they have participated in over the past year.


         The meeting adjourned at 5:59 p.m.
