WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 5, 2020

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 10:00 a.m. on August 5, 2020, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Natalie Nelson, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, Community
Development Director Scott Messel (via Zoom), and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Commissioner Cox was excused from the meeting.
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Public Works Director Ron Whitehead

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. _2020-1160-O, AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING TITLE 10 OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY CODE TO CLARIFY

TERMS AND ADDRESS ACCESSORY DWELLINGS AND PAVILIONS

Deputy County Attorney Natalie Nelson explained that the current county code allows
individuals to have one accessory dwelling on parcels that are less than five (5) acres in
size. Additionally, the County desires to allow pavilions to be constructed prior to a
primary dwelling in certain zones. Campgrounds have also been stricken from certain
residential zones.

Commissioner Almquist suggested setting a maximum square footage of 1,400 square feet
and stated that clarification for shipping container definitions should be easier to find

within the code.

The Planning Commission previously expressed preference that regulations appear in the
supplemental information and that only definitions appear in the definition section.

Commissioner Almquist requested that shipping containers are described as accessory
buildings and if a shipping container is to be used as a living space, the applicant must
follow all applicable building codes. Clarification was suggested in regards to a business
wherein shipping containers are sold and it was determined that the property must be zoned
properly to do so. Agricultural and Ag-Residential can obtain approval for more shipping

containers.

Chairman Iverson stated that it should be clarified that only one primary dwelling and one
accessory dwelling should be allowed per parcel.

Commissioner Almquist expressed concern with converting garage space to living space
and suggested adding in a section clarifying that adequate parking shall be provided in the
event that the garage is fully converted. Any residential parcels above one acre can only
have two (2) shipping containers maximum.

Commissioner Almquist stated that there should be no camping within residential, open
space, and agricultural zones unless a zone change were to take place.

Community Development Director Scott Messel clarified that currently, camping is
allowed under a conditional use permit.

MOTION:

Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2020-1160-
O, An Ordinance Amending Title 10 of the Washington County Code
to Clarify Terms and Address Accessory Dwellings, Pavilions,
Campgrounds, and Storage Containers with the Recommended
Changes which include: Adding the Maximum Square Footage
Allowance of 1,400 Square Feet for Pavilions; Adding Language
Clarifying that if Shipping Containers are to be used for Living Space,
the Applicant Must Follow all Applicable Building Codes; Shipping
Container will be Defined as an Accessory Building; Regulations
regarding shipping containers will be moved to the Supplementary
Section of the Code, and only the Definition will be in the Definition
Section; One Container per Acre May be Permitted with a Max of Two
Containers Per Parcel Above One Acre; Ag and Ag-Residential can
Obtain Approval for Additional Shipping Containers; Accessory
Dwelling, Temporary Use, and Nonconforming Use will be Removed as
they are Already Covered or not Enforceable; Sales of shipping
containers are exempted from Shipping Container regulations but
must still be appropriately zoned; Remove language stating that there
cannot be a nonconforming use; Only One Accessory Dwelling Unit Per
Lot, Removing Language “Of Less than Five Acres”; Campgrounds
are being Removed from Seasonal Forest Residential and All
Residential zones; and Accessory Dwellings must Provide for Parking
if Garage Space Becomes Unavailable. Motion seconded by Chairman
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson
and Almquist voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Chairman Iverson reported that the kick-off fair parade was successful in Washington City.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at

11:05 a.m.
