                               ORDINANCE NO. 1542

      AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL ADOPTING
      SUBSECTION 15-1-075 OF THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE
      REGARDING REGULATIONS FOR PUBLIC PROPERTY ADJACENT TO PARADE
      ROUTES.


     WHEREAS, Chapter 15-1 “Special Events” of the Draper City Municipal Code
(“DCMC”) regulates special events in the City; and

      WHEREAS, parades on City streets are a category of “Entertainment Event” and
are subject to regulation under DCMC Chapter 15-1; and

       WHEREAS, parades in Draper are traditionally well attended and spectators
claim their viewing spots along the parade routes days, if not, weeks, in advance by
a variety of methods, including placing chairs and roping off areas in the right-of-way
and parking motorhomes and similar large vehicles along the parade route; and,

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council wishes to adopt regulations regarding when
and how spectators can occupy viewing areas on public property along a parade
route; and

       WHEREAS, the regulations proposed herein balance the public interest in
observing a parade, the property rights of homeowners along the parade route and
the City’s authority to regulate city-owned rights-of way; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council finds good cause to adopt DCMC 15-1-075
and further finds adopting DCMC 15-1-075 promotes the health, safety and welfare
of Draper residents.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Adoption. Draper City Municipal Code Subsection 15-1-075 is
hereby adopted to read as shown in Exhibit “A”, which is attached hereto and
incorporated herein by reference.

      Section 2. Penalty for Violations. Violations of Subsection 15-1-075 shall be
punished for a first violation as a Class C misdemeanor punishable by a term of
imprisonment not to exceed 90 days and/or a fine not to exceed $750.00; and for
subsequent violations as a Class B misdemeanor punishable by a term of
imprisonment not to exceed 364 days and/or a fine not to exceed $1000.00. The City
is required under Utah Code Section 78B-22-301 to provide for indigent defense
services as that term is defined in Utah Code Section 78B-22-102.

       Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to
correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, or similar errors prior to submitting
this Ordinance to Sterling Codifiers.

       Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable..

       Section 5. Effective Date.     This Ordinance shall become effective
immediately upon publication or posting or 30 days after final passage, whichever is
closer to the date of final passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THE 7TH DAY OF JUNE 2022.
