1. Discussion regarding Silver Creek Village Development Agreement – Jennifer 
Strader, County Planner 
 
County Planner Jennifer Strader presented the staff report and explained that a SPA designation 
was approved for Silver Creek Village in 2011.  The process of approving a development 
agreement for a SPA requires a public hearing by the Planning Commission, which then 
forwards a recommendation to the County Council for a final determination.  The development 
agreement is to be approved within 24 months of the SPA approval, and the applicants have 
requested and received an extension to March 31, 2015.  The Planning Commission appointed a 
subcommittee, which has met numerous times with Staff, the applicant, and the Snyderville 
Basin Special Recreation District representatives, and all the parties agree with the proposed 
development agreement language.  Because of the limited amount of time for the development 
agreement to be approved, Staff is requesting comments from the Planning Commissioners in 
this work session in preparation for a public hearing on February 24. 
 
Commissioner Harte commented that the spirit of the subcommittee meetings was very 
positive, and the Recreation District was also involved in the discussions.  He believed there are 
still some issues regarding sign regulations and proposed ghost signs. 
 
Commissioner Franklin believed the language of the development was well thought out, but a 
few things need to be addressed in the appendices regarding signage, setbacks, snow removal, 
etc.  He explained that the developer proposes moving snow to one side of the road and over the 
top of the sidewalks, and he expressed concern about who would be responsible for the sidewalk,

Commissioner Klingenstein asked how the development agreement would deal with dispute

Commissioner Klingenstein asked if the goal of the project is to be a community of full-time

regulate them.  He encouraged the applicant to make this a community of people rather than

addressed nightly rentals, but there is a significant amount of affordable housing in the SPA, and 
that does not run as great a risk of nightly rentals.  They have also made sure to not allow a 
situation where someone could buy a major parcel and try to combine lots to create less density, 
which could lead to fewer actual residents.  He stated that people who might purchase a mega-lot 
are more inclined to not live here all the time.  He believed not allowing that would help insure 
that the type of product developed will be used by people who live and work in the community

County Planner Tiffanie Northrup-Robinson presented the staff report and explained that

That includes the base terminal, mid-station terminal, pinecone ski run, evacuation and 
maintenance trail, tree removal and smoothing of ski runs, and minor support structures.
