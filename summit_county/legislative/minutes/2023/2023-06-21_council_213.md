Attachment: Recorder Email 6-16-23
Christopher Robinson made a motion to adopt Ordinance No. 961 Enacting a Summit County Recorder Appeal Authority modifying (d)(1) to say the then-current chair and vice chair of the County Council will serve as the chair and vice of the review board, and change (e) to a written demand for a final decision and final approval from Council Chair. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens. Abstain: Canice Harte.
