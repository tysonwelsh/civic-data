South Jordan City                                                                                    2
City Council Meeting
October 6, 2020

Council Member McGuire said the city’s art show is currently going on at the community center.
He said he attended a meeting regarding snow event parking hosted by the public works
department. There was not a great turnout, but they did get some feedback from the residents.

Council Member Zander said they did not have good attendance, but they did get good feedback
from the residents regarding the snow event ordinance. She noted the recent bike tour of
Daybreak as part of the ULCT conference. She noted a story of a fire fighter, who stopped at a
hospital and helped a lady in need. She shared the story on social media. The story was well
received. She said the wife of one of the fire fighters in the city contacted her and elaborated how
highly they think of Chief Dawson. She thanked Fire Chief Dawson for his efforts. She also
noted a police officer who recently helped her find her cell phone.

Council Member Harris said many businesses in the city are weathering the Covid crisis well.
Everyone is coming together including the residents, Mayor, the City Council and the City
Manager to make sure that the city stays strong.

Council Member Marlor said he met with the architectural review committee. He said it seems
like they are seeing less buildings going up. He met with a real estate developer, and he met with
the compensation committee where they discussed attracting and keeping their great employees.

Mayor Ramsey noted that the city received the Utah Ethical Leadership Award for government.
She noted the recent ULCT virtual conference. She noted that she has been elected president
elect of Utah League of Cities and Towns. She said she attended an EDCUtah meeting. She has
been named the public sector vice chair of EDCUtah. She noted that she attended the
gubernatorial debate. She said she filmed the city’s economic development video. She said she
attended the Jordan District Education Foundation and noted that Tracey Miller received the
Friend of Children award from the Utah PTA. She said they held a press conference for the Point
of the Mountain State Land Authority. She said they also gave the annual corporate citizen award
to the U of U clinic. She noted that the VA clinic is open.

         F. Public Comment:

None.

         G. Action Item: Resolution 2020-24, adopt a 5-year City Water Conservation Plan (By
            Public Works Director, Jason Rasmussen)

Jordan Allen, Water Conservation Manager, presented the water conservation plan (Attachment
A).

Council Member Harris asked in general, how are the reservoirs in the State of Utah? Mr. Allen
said overall, it’s looking pretty good. Deer Creek is at 88 percent capacity, but if they get some
snow, it should be up to full capacity. Deer Creek is a water source for the city.
South Jordan City                                                                                     3

Council Member Zander asked if residents in Daybreak are allowed to flip their strip? Mr. Allen
said it depends. He said the changes are allowed, but they have to get certain approvals from the
HOA managers.

Council Member Zander asked about the city’s goal to make city owned properties be more
water wise. Are they flipping those park strips? Public Works Director Rasmussen said they are
at the start of that project. Currently, they are identifying areas in park properties that do not need
turf grass. The next step is to see what is feasible with the park department to replace the turf
with water wise plants.

Mayor Ramsey said South Jordan has done more flip your strips than the rest of Jordan Valley
Water Conservancy District combined. She said JVWCD also gets water from Jordanelle and the
Provo river. She said Deer Creek is an important source.

Council Member Marlor made a motion to approve Resolution R2020-24. Council Member
McGuire seconded the motion. The vote was 4-0 in favor.

         H. Staff Reports and Calendaring items

CM Whatcott indicated that he would not be present at the next City Council meeting.

City Attorney Loose said there will not be a City Council meeting the 1st Tuesday in November
because of the election.

ADJOURNMENT

Council Member Zander made a motion to adjourn. Council Member Harris seconded the
motion. The vote was 4-0 in favor.

The October 6, 2020 City Council meeting adjourned at 7:45 p.m.
