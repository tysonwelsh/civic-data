APPROVAL OF CONSENT AGENDA
      Councilor Skip Worwood made a motion to approve the consent agenda including Council
      meeting minutes 10-14-25, Claims dated 10-20-25, and Burial Rights for Mac and Diane
      Traasdahl (2 spaces). Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed
      unanimously.

PUBLIC COMMENT
       Mission at Nephi, a skilled nursing facility (1100 N 400 E Nephi), wanted the support of the City
       Council to do a Sub for Santa for Seniors. The Council discussed bundling it with the Sub for
       Santa for families. Councilor Parady will reach out to both programs to discuss this opportunity
       with them. Lisa Brough also mentioned putting a flyer with the utility bill to inform citizens.

NO PLANNING ITEMS

CITY RECORDER POSITION—OATH OF OFFICE
       Mayor Seely discussed the upcoming retirement of Lisa Brough and the recommendation to
       appoint Leslee Anderson as the new City Recorder. Mayor Seely praised Leslee for her work as
       Deputy Recorder and mentioned her value as a team member and quick adaptation to the job.
       Councilor Shari Cowan made the motion to appoint Leslee Anderson as the City Recorder.
       Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.
       Lisa Brough administered the oath of office to Leslee Anderson.

400 W ANNEXATION
       This annexation was approved in the 10-14-25 City Council meeting. Following that meeting a
       few individuals on the petition withdrew. Derrik and Makenna Jacobson were present to discuss
       the changes and steps moving forward. The Council agreed to still proceed with the annexation
       of the smaller parcel, as it is contiguous. A new map will need to be certified and the County
       Recorder will need to be notified of the change and then the city and Jacobsons will move
       forward with the next steps.

LETTER OF INTENT—DCPC
       Jon Jensen, Bob Patty, and Mike Denton representing DCPC (Design & Construction Performance
       Collaboration, LLC) attended the meeting and presented a letter of intent to study the natural
       gas transmission line and determine a plan for necessary upgrades to Nephi’s gas infrastructure.
       Councilor Jeramie Callaway made a motion to authorize the mayor to sign the letter of intent
       from DCPC. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

ADMINISTRATON AND COUNCIL UPDATES
   • Lisa Brough excused Seth Atkinson from the meeting. He was at a national conference.
   • Ms. Brough announced a change in time for the Chamber of Commerce Trick or Treat on Main
      from 1:30 to 3:30 instead of 3 to 5 so people could attend Juab football’s playoff game at 4
      o’clock. The Chamber of Commerce is coordinating the time change with the police department.
   • Ms. Brough mentioned the transition of Sara Samuelson as the new finance director. She started
      today. She will be working part time until she can complete work with Mona City (current
      employer).
   • Choice Hardware is not quite ready for their open house. Councilor Cowan will keep the council
      updated on that event.
   • Councilor Callaway announced a meeting this week for the Christmas village. He will provide
      updates on information at the next council meeting.
Work Session 7:19 pm
   1. Real Estate RFP
           a. The city owns property in the Crimson Flats area.
           b. Mayor and Seth Atkinson have talked about putting that out to bid to real estate agents
               in the area to get a fair assessment and the maximum amount for those three acres.
           c. An RFP will be sent out to real estate agents and try to find the best use for that land.
   2. Christmas Party Details
           a. Ms. Brough announced the tentative date as Wednesday, December 3.
           b. Venue location was discussed including the steak house and Cinder Creek.
           c. After discussion, the council agreed to have it at Cinder Creek but have the steak house
               cater the meal.
   3. Fraud Risk Assessment
           a. Lisa Brough, Nephi City Finance Director, presented the fraud risk assessment, scoring a
               low risk for fraud and identified areas for improvement.
           b. The council discussed the need for a written policy on reporting fraud and abuse.
