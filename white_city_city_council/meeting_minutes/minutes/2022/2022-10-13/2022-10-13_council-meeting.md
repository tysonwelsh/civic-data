                                    Special Workshop Meeting

Upgrade and Construction of Sego Lily Drive

               Ryan Nuesmeyer, Project Manager, Wall Consultant Group (WCG), presented a
PowerPoint presentation regarding the Sego Lily transportation improvements. He reviewed the
progress of the project. The survey and ROW research, survey control and high resolution aerial
imagery, and safety and operations review has been completed. Utility mapping and pavement
design and report are in progress and the roadway design is ready.

                 Mayor Flint asked if connecting roads were going to tie into Sego Lily so that the
road is not different heights. If there is a problem once the road is graded to where it needs to be,
who is the contact if there is a problem.

              Mr. Nuesmeyer stated if there are known trouble spots, let him know, he will then
weigh out what options there are to fix the road, depending on how much money is available.

               The following recommendations were recommended and discussed:

    Sidewalks

                  Mr. Nuesmeyer stated Sego Lily east of Flint have existing sidewalks. Sidewalks
will be the first to be installed.

    Consolidated crosswalks
    Raised crosswalks

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated crosswalks at Antimony Lane, Hosta Lane, and 1280 East
will be removed. If there are too many crosswalks next to each other, people will begin to disregard
them. The four remaining crosswalks will be located at Galena Drive, Trail Crossing with a raised
crosswalk, Flint Drive with a Rectangular rapid-flashing beacon (RRFB), and Marble Street. The
raised crosswalk will help with visibility, and it slows traffic.

                 Council Member Price asked if the coloring will be different.

                 Mr. Nuesmeyer stated yes, crosswalk lines will be added.

    RRFB

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated an RRFB will be added at the trail crossing, another one
will be added at the Flint Drive crosswalk.

                 Mayor Flint stated the old solar powered signs do not work.

            Mr. Nuesmeyer stated the RRFB’s can be hard wired. He will work with Rocky
Mountain Power to get lighting.

    Bulb-outs

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated bulb-outs narrow the road and slows drivers down. Each
crosswalk location will have a bulb-out. Drivers cannot park next to a bulb-out, they will have to
park further down. That will create better site distance for pedestrians and drivers.

    Bike lanes

                 Mr. Nuesmeyer stated there have been five bike accidents in five years which is
a lot of auto/bike accidents.

                Mayor Flint stated residents want parking on the road, if bike lanes are added, the
street is narrowed even more.

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated adding bike lanes will slow traffic by 13 percent.
Connectivity is a popular option right now.

                Mayor Flint stated the MSD has received $40 million in grants because
municipalities comply with walkability, bike, and trail plans.

             Shane Ellis, Transportation Engineering Manager, Salt Lake County Engineering
and Flood Control, stated it is easier to get grant money, if you add active transportation to the
community.

                Council Member Perry stated electric bikes, bicycles, and scooters are being
used more. Sego Lily takes riders to the Trax station, library, and schools. Designing a road to
last up to 50 years, it would be foolish not offer this option.

                 Mr. Nuesmeyer stated the benefit of bike lanes for parked cars is the buffer a bike
lane provides.

               Council Member Little stated his concerns are funding the bike lanes. The project
is already going to be over budget and there are added costs to create bike lanes. Even if a
funding source is located, the road will still need to be widen and trees will need to be removed.
Citizens are going to be upset.

              Mr. Nuesmeyer stated he is aware of the trees and is mindful of property. There
are a few reasons to consider bike lanes; it will improve the corridor by making it consistent and
encourage slower speeds.

    Green paint

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated he encourages the Council to consider 6-feet wide
sidewalks that will allow pedestrians to stay a foot off the curb.

               Mayor Flint stated there are rights-of-way that were put in place 100 years ago
that need to be protected. There are agreements made in perpetuity.

              Mr. Nuesmeyer stated as stewards of the public rights-of-way, the Council needs
to determine what it wants that space to look like.

             Mayor Flint stated White City’s image will be enhanced. With traffic being funneled
through Sego Lily, the Council needs to show a forward pace into the future and make it more
livable.

               Mr. Ellis stated if construction is inside the existing gutters or moving into the
yards; every tree, fence, or bush will be affected. If those areas are affected regardless, then the
project should be done right.

               Council Member Price asked if the homes would lose on-street parking.

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated no, on-street parking will be available everywhere there
are not bulb-outs.

               Council Member Price asked if lighting has been included in the project.

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated the plan is to include lighting.

               Mr. Ellis stated lighting will be included from 700 East to 1300 East.

               Council Member Cardenaz asked what the spacing of the lights will be and how
many lights are included.

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated the lighting design has not been completed.

               Mr. Ashton asked if the Council would like to hold a public meeting to present the
project plan. Mr. Nuesmeyer cannot move forward until he gets direction from the Council to do
so.

              Council Member Little stated this project is looking at what is wanted versus what
there is money for. Road maintenance for other roads cannot be ignored.

               Mayor Flint stated the Sego Lily project has been bonded for, there is $500,000
in grant funds available and the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) will fund the
balance for any shortfall.

               Mr. Ashton stated once the design is determined, Mr. Nuesmeyer can provide a
cost.

              Council Member Little stated residents will want to make sure their roads are
being maintained and fixed.

                Mayor Flint stated road maintenance comes from another budget. The Sego Lily
project has its own set of funding. She would like to showcase the project and have various boards
set up around the room for residents to view.

               Mr. Nuesmeyer stated WCG has an outreach department and has addresses for
residents along Sego Lily.

               Mr. Ashton asked if the Council feels good about the design that was presented
today.

               Mayor Flint, Council Members Perry, and Cardenaz support the current design.

                 Mr. Nuesmeyer recommended getting the design to 30 percent completion before
it presents it to the public. During the public meeting he can roll out a map where everyone can
view it.

               Mayor Flint stated she would like an open house type public meeting.

               Council Member Little stated he would like the plan presented to the residents
with a question period after.

              Mr. Nuesmeyer stated it will take him approximately 30-45 days to get to a 30
percent design phase.

               Council Member Little stated he supports the design but has concerns about the
bike lanes.

               Council Member Price stated she supports the design but also has concerns
about the bike lanes. She asked when the project will go to bid.

               Mr. Ellis stated after the design phase is complete, the bid process will begin.

               Mr. Ashton recommended holding the public meeting on January 12, 2023.

             Rori Andreason stated she will notice the meeting for January 12, 2023, from 6:00
pm – 8:00 pm with the presentation beginning at 6:30 pm.
