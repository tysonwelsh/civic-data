1. Zone Ogden, Outstanding questions
Presenter John Janson with Logan Simpson went over some questions of possible change

and sought feedback to some final topics in the Plan such as application expirations, ADU’s,
procedure definitions, development standards, land divisions, property and boundary changes, etc.

2. Attendance discussion

Planning Director Barton Brierley touched on a reminder on Planning Commission

attendance with the Commissioners of not missing more than 3 meetings and communicating
when attendance is not possible. He also expressed appreciation for their efforts and time they
contribute to the city in their roles as Commissioners.
