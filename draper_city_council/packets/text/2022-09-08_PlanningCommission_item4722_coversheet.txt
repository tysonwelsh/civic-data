Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: September 8, 2022
Re: Price Real Estate Lot 10 Map Amendments Comments: This application is a request for approval of Land Use and Zoning Map Amendments for approximately 0.61 acres located on the south side of 13560 South and to the east of 150 East.  The property is currently zoned CBP.   The applicant is requesting a Land Use Map Amendment from Community Commercial to Commercial Special District and a Zoning Map Amendment from CBP to CSD-LP.  The subject site is currently vacant. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  The proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the city’s general plan.
2.  The proposed amendment is harmonious with the overall character of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property.
3.  The proposed amendment is consistent with the standards of any applicable overlay zone.
4.  The proposed amendment will not adversely affect adjacent property.
5.  The facilities and services intended to serve the subject property are adequate, including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and wastewater and refuse collection.
6.  The land use map amendment complies with standards found in DCMC Section 9-2-020(F), DCMC Section 9-5-060(D), and Utah Code Annotated 10-9a-404.
For denial:
1.  The proposed amendment is not consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the city's general plan.
2.  The proposed amendment is not harmonious with the overall character of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property.
3.  The proposed amendment is not consistent with the standards of any applicable overlay zone.
4.  The proposed amendment will adversely affect adjacent property.
5.  The facilities and services intended to serve the subject property are not adequate, including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and wastewater and refuse collection.
