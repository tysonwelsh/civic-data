Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: July 8, 2021
Re: Summit Commerce Center Site Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for approximately 26.937 acres located on the east side of Pony Express Rd. and south of Golden Harvest Rd., at approximately 77 W. Golden Harvest Rd. / 13004 S. Pony Express Rd. The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved to allow for the development of the currently vacant site with an industrial building. The findings are as follows: Deviation Request from DCMC Subsection 9-22-040(C)(1) -  Horizontal and Vertical Façade Articulation.
For approval:
1. The project contains amenities such as pedestrian and bike connectivity in excess of what is required in the development code and site plan review.
2. The overall percentage of dedicated public or private landscape and open space area exceeds the required amount by more than ten percent (10%).
3. The variations are consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.
4. The amount of primary materials used for a facade is at least ten percent (10%) greater than the minimum required amount.
For denial:
1. The project does not contain amenities such as pedestrian and bike connectivity in excess of what is required in the development code and site plan review.
2. The variations are not consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.

Site Plan
For approval:
1. The site plan application represents a complete development plan for the overall site.
2. The proposed site plan conforms to applicable standards set forth in this title, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3. The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the M1 zoning district in which the site is located.
4. The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
5. The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns.
For denial:
1. The proposed site plan does not conform to all applicable standards set forth in this title, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
2. The proposed development plans do not meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the M1 zoning district in which the site is located.
3. The public facilities and services in the area are not adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
