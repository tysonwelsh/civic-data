                               RESOLUTION NO. _____________



   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO A MASTER
LICENSE AGREEMENT WITH AT&T DBA NEW CINGULAR WIRELESS PCS, LLC
FOR USE OF PUBLIC RIGHTS OF WAY TO LOCATE 5G TELECOMMUNICATION
                             SERVICES.


       WHEREAS, AT&T, doing business as New Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC, (“Singular”)
provides telecommunications services residents in Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, Singular delivers telecommunication services by use of an array of fiber,
cellular tower and now 5G telecommunication systems; and

         WHEREAS, In order to operate a 5G telecommunication system within the city public rights
of way, Riverton City must require Singular to abide by certain terms and conditions of locating,
installing, and maintaining 5G networks in city public rights of way, and further require Singular to
pay Riverton City certain fees to compensate the public for the use of its rights of way for Singular’s
commercial purposes; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Attorney has negotiated an agreement with Singular to
establish the terms and conditions in which Singular may operate a cable television network within
City public rights of way, and has approved as to legal form this agreement,


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the governing body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The attached Master License Agreement with Singular for use of public rights of way to
          locate 5G infrastructure is hereby approved.

       2. The Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, on behalf of the City, the proposed Agreement
          with the Singular, upon verification that the Agreement has been approved as to legal
          form by the City Attorney.
               PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 20th
day of July 2021 by the following vote:
