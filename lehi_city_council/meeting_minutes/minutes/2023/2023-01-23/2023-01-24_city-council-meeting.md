Pre Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present, except for
Councilor Albrecht who is excused. Councilor Condie gave the opening comment.

2. Select a Mayor Pro Tempore
Councilor Koivisto and Councilor Hancock were nominated for Mayor Pro Tempore. Mayor
Johnson asked for a secret ballot vote and participated in the voting. Council Hancock received 3
votes; Council Koivisto received 2 votes. Councilor Hancock was elected to Mayor Pro Tempore.

3. Presentations and Reports
3.1 Presentation and Update from Texas Instruments
Virginia Martinez Schaefer was there of behalf of Texas Instruments. She stated that Lehi is
being considered as a location to construct their next manufacture facility. It would be an 11-
billion-dollar capital investment which would be the largest capital investment in Utah history. If
Lehi is selected, it would create over 800 new jobs and they would continue to invest in the
current facility that was formally Micron. She stated that they intend to break ground on a
location by the end of the year.

3.2 Presentation and Discussion of the John Haws Presidential Campaigns Collection
Jannette Haws presented her late husband’s presidential campaigns collection. She stated that
these items teach a history of the Presidents of our country. She said that it was John’s dream to
have this collection donated to the city of Lehi, a city that he served all his life. The City plans to
partner with the Lehi Archives to display the collection.

The Environmental Sustainability Committee Update was heard next.

3.4 Environmental Sustainability Committee Update and Report- Todd Munger.
Todd Munger and Susan Wilson gave the Council and update on what they’ve done in the past
year. Mr. Munger stated that they have an extensive network of committees and coalitions that
they work with to understand policy and grant money that may be available. They received a
$25,000 grant to mitigate phragmites around the Jordan River. They continue to work with
schools on their no-idling campaign to educated students and parents. They also organized an
electronic waste collection drive for recycling. They also partnered with the Hutchings Museum
on the monarch butterfly restoration project. The Committee is conducting a survey to better
understand what residents expect of the committee and learn ways to help educate. They also
promoted the Jordan River and participated in community events and activities.

3.3 Presentation of the Audit Report- Finance Department
Dean Lundell stated that they recently completed an outside audit of the City’s financial
statements. Ian Robbins presented the audit report. He stated that they have a clean audit report
with no findings.

5. Consideration of Ordinance #09-2023 amending Title 3 Chapter 2, Planning
   Commission, of the Lehi City Municipal Code.
   Kim Struthers stated that they discussed this at a previous meeting. He said that this reduces
   the number of Commissioners. If there’s a reason to remove a member, then it would be done
   with the consent of the City Council. Mr. Struthers stated that the alternates would no longer
   sit on the dais, but they would still be paid for being there. The Council agreed that the
   expectation is that the alternates attend all the Planning Commission meetings.

    Mayor Johnson asked for public comment.

    Michelle Stallings was concerned with reducing the number of commissioners and disagreed
    with being able to arbitrarily dismiss a Commissioner without cause.

    Councilor Koivisto and Condie expressed concerns with being able to dismiss members
    without cause. There was discussion about amending the verbiage to exclude “without cause”,
    so it would state that the Mayor may remove a member of the Commission with the advice and
    consent of the City Council.

    Councilor Koivisto stated that she still has the same concerns.

    It was suggested that the Planning Commissioners be required to take an Oath of Office.
