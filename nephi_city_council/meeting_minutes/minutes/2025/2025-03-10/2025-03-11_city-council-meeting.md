                                      Nephi City
                                 Work Session Minutes
                                    March 11, 2025
                                       7:00 p.m.
                                      21 E 100 N
                                      Nephi, UT

1.    Animal Control Officer Thomas Talbert presented to the council some proposed
     changes to the Nephi City code governing kennel licenses, dog registration, and rabies
     vaccination requirements. Officer Talbert proposed capping kennel licenses at five dogs
     and requiring physical fencing. The council discussed the need for hobby licenses for
     more than two dogs and the enforcement of nuisance complaints. The council also
     discussed the city’s no-kill shelter status and the challenges of managing a growing
     number of dogs.The council discussed the definition of a service animal. The council
     discussed the lack of a dog shelter in Mona. Officer Talbert said he gets around two
     dogs a month from Mona. The council agreed to study the proposed changes in the
     code that Officer Talbert had suggested. The council expressed their appreciation to
     Officer Talbert for the fine work he is doing.

2. City Administrator Seth Atkinson discussed the CIB projects options that the council
   would like to put on the comprehensive list of possible projects that could benefit from
   CIB funding.The council agreed with the following three projects:
       a. Police Station
       b. Bridge Improvements
       c. Sewer System Improvements

3. The council discussed forming a 2026 Independence Week Celebration task force. Mr.
   Atkinson stated that Library Director Ruth Bonzo had indicated at the management
   meeting that she would like to be on the task force. Mayor Seely stated that he would
   also like to be a part of the task force. Donald Ball indicated that he would be interested
   in helping because he has an interest in history. City Administrator Seth Atkinson
   suggested looking into adding a drone light show in with the fireworks display for part of
   the celebration.

4. The council discussed opening up the Nephi City sesquicentennial time capsule that is
   buried in the flower bed on the west side of the Old Gym. The capsule was buried in
   September of 2001 and is due to be opened up.

5.   Administrative Updates:
       a. Councilor Cowan and Councilor Parady volunteered to be on the hiring
           committee for the new patrol officer.
       b. Mr. Atkinson reminded the members of the Airport Advisory Committee to review
           and provide feedback on the RFPs for the master plan update and engineering
           services.
           c. The council discussed the potential use of Placer AI software for tracking visitor
              behavior at events and the need for collaboration on purchasing the software
              with the Chamber.
           d. Councilor JD Parady stated that he will be attending the Chamber board meeting
              on March 27.
           e. Councilor Parady mentioned the need for repairs and improvements to the Old
              Gym parking lot staircase and railings.

   6.   The meeting adjourned at 7:50 p.m.
