2.10 A Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council Approving an 
Interlocal Agreement that Provides Notice of Salt Lake 
County’s Withdrawal from the Central Wasatch Commission 
and Provides Funding to the Central Wasatch Commission
21-1384
Attachments: Staff Report
2021 CWC ILA_final_AATF
Resolution Approving ILA_1Dec21_AATF
Presenters: Deputy Mayor Catherine Kanter (Approx. 20 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Council Member Bradshaw stated he is struggling to understand how the 
$200,000 is tied to the withdrawal of the County from the Central Wasatch 
Commission (CWC).  It was his understanding that the $ 200,000 was for 
fiscal year 2020/2021, but has not been paid.  
Mr. David Delquadro , Chief Financial Manager, Council Office, stated that 
was correct, the money has been appropriated, but it has not been paid.
Ms. Catherine Kanter , Deputy Mayor over County Services, stated the 
interlocal agreement (ILA) tied to this $ 200,000 expired in June 2021.  
However, the master interlocal agreement that the Council adopted when the 
CWC was created has a payment associated with it, so there are differing 
opinions on whether or not a new ILA is needed.  She felt the County needed 
Page 6 of 10
Council Work Session Meeting Minutes December 7, 2021
to honor the appropriation in its 2021 budget.
Mayor Jennifer Wilson stated the County should make its withdrawal from 
the CWC the right way and live up to its expectation. 
Council Member Stringham asked, for the record, what the County got 
from being a member of the CWC.
Ms. Kanter stated as a member of the CWC, the County accomplished a lot 
of work, which included but was not limited to the environmental dashboard, 
short-term projects, work done on accessing transportation options in Little 
Cottonwood Canyon, and federal legislation that is still moving forward .  
This brings a lot of value to taxpayers.  
Council Member Stringham, seconded by Council Member Snelgrove, 
moved to table this request for one week so additional conversations can be 
held.
Council Member Newton, seconded by Council Member DeBry, made a 
substitute motion to fund the CWC for $ 100,000, put another $ 100,000 in the 
Open Space Fund, and defer this for one week so Mitchell Park, Legal 
Counsel, Council Office, can prepare an interlocal agreement that outlines the 
County’s departure and includes the funding piece. 
Mr. Mitchell Park , Legal Counsel, Council Office, stated the Council does 
not enact ILAs.  It can request the terms for the agreement, but the Mayor ’s 
Office does the ILA.  The Council will need to adopt a resolution supporting 
the ILA.  
Ms. Kanter stated the CWC held a meeting last night and in anticipation of 
this changed, authorized the ILA to be amended to the payment of $ 100,000. 
This enables the Council to make the necessary changes to the ILA today.  
A substitute motion was made by Council Member Winder Newton, seconded 
by Chair DeBry, to fund the CWC for $ 100,000,  approve the resolution, and 
forward this item to the 4:00 PM Council meeting for ratification.   The motion 
carried by the following vote
