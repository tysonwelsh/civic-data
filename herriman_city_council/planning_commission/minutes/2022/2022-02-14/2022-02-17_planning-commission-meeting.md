    1.3. Presentation and discussion of a proposed amendment to The Cove Planned Unit Development
         located at approximately at 6813 W Vista Spring Drive in the FR-1 Forestry Recreation Zone
City Planner Maloy offered a brief background of the Cove planned development history and background.
The southern hillside portion lacked the water infrastructure and tank for project completion. Toll Brothers
had the property under contract and would be requesting an amendment to make changes to the original
planned development. Planner Maloy stated this item was for presentation only and an opportunity for
the developer to receive feedback from the Commissioners. No decisions would be made this evening.

Toll Brothers representatives Jay Cone and Alex Marsh presented an overview of the proposed
development. They provided a potential timeline, road and lot layouts, open space, and trailhead locations.
The original approval contained 645 lots, of which 222 were still to be developed. They emphasized the
developer was not seeking additional lots. Their goal was to maintain the hillside landscape, provide a less
impactful layout design and maintain the natural drainage channel. To achieve this, they would request to
decrease the size of some lots. Two different lot sizes with separate price points and product types were
proposed. 119 smaller nine thousand square feet minimum lots would include at least 75-foot frontage,
setbacks of twenty-foot front and rear, and five feet on each side. The 103 larger lots would include 12,000
minimum square feet, at least 100 feet of frontage, with twenty-foot front and rear setbacks, and eight-
foot on each side. All homes would be luxury modern, farmhouse, or prairie style homes. The development
timeline would be dependent upon water infrastructure installation.

Commissioners requested more details regarding the trails and parks, and debated ideal lot size transitions
near the original development. Commissioners were reluctant to place small homes next to large homes
and expressed concerns that five-foot setbacks would not be large enough to accommodate basement
window wells. The applicant stated reduction of the depth of the small lots was less noticeable, and all lots
contained relatively wide frontage. The applicant proposed the updated layout was a more efficient use of
the property and provided more open space. The original pool and clubhouse were originally proposed for
the entire community, including the unfinished section. New facilities were not planned. Commissioners
emphasized the importance of road connectivity and safety, for potential fire evacuation, and said the
trailhead must be large enough to accommodate emergency vehicles.

The Commission adjourned the work meeting by consensus at 6:47 p.m.

      3.1. Request for approval of a Conditional Use of a site plan for a dental office located generally
      at 11962 S Anthem Park Boulevard in the C-2 Commercial Zone
      Applicant: Herriman Dental Partners, LLC/Jenny Taumoepeau (agent)
      Acres: ± 1.24
      File Number: C2021-020

Planner II Sheldon Howa presented the dental office conditional use request in the C-2 commercial zone.
He identified the parcel location, site overlay, access easement, parcel boundary and detention basin. The
elevation, floor plan, and landscaping all met city standards. Additional office space would be available for
lease. The applicant requested a deviation of the road section to offset the previously installed curb and
gutter, which was installed five feet north of the easement line. This had already been approved by City
Council. Landscape requirements named in the conditions of approval have already been provided by the
applicant. The applicant received a variance which removed the six foot precast wall requirement, as the
fifty foot public access space was adequate. The recommendations of approval were reviewed.

Applicant Stephen Selu offered to answer questions.

Commissioners asked if parking spaces could be removed to increase setbacks. Applicant Representative
Selu replied the number of parking spaces was consistent with those utilized at other valley locations.
Leases were available for the additional professional office space, the uses in those offices was yet to be
determined.

Recommendations:
   1.   Receive and agree to all recommendations from other agencies.

   2.     Work with City Engineer to ensure public access easement for adjacent properties is adequate
          for the required road cross-section. This should be done by separate deed or by plat and shall
          be complete prior to issuance of building permit.
   3.     All trash enclosures to be constructed with materials and colors of the primary building as
          outlined in 10-23-13 of City Code.

   6.     Submit a drainage study for review that addresses flood control and the “80th percentile”

   7.     Provide additional landscape island(s) in parking stalls along the east edge of the site. (No more

   8.     Detention Pond shall be landscaped to meet Herriman City’s Water Efficiency Standards.
   9.     All landscaping must comply with Herriman City’s Water Efficiency Standards.
   10.    Parking lot landscaping to be a minimum of 5% of the parking area.
   11.    No signs are approved with this request.

for a dental office located generally at 11962 S Anthem Park Boulevard in the C-2 Commercial Zone with
