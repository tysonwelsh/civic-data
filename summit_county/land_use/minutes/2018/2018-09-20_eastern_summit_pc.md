subdivision; 4450 N 1000 E; CANR-1-2; Timothy & Melissa Evans, applicants – Sean

Planner Lewis shared the background. There was a noticing issue at the last meeting for 
this item. This application is proposing two units of density for Lot 2 in the subdivision. 
This is a plat amendment of an existing subdivision but was noticed as a new subdivision. 
There are questions that have come up from the public that need to be addressed. What is

Findings of Fact 
1. Timothy and Melissa Evans are listed as the fee title owner of record for Parcel CANR-1-2.  
2. Parcel CANR-1-2 is 20.0 acres in size.  
3. Parcel CANR-1-2 is located near 4450 North 1000 E.  
4. Parcel CANR-1-2 is considered a conforming parcel for development purposes.  
5. Parcel CANR-1-2 is currently zoned Agriculture 10 (AG-10)  
6. Parcel CANR-1-2 is not located within a previously approved subdivision plat.  
7. The name of the proposed subdivision is: Evans Family Ranch Subdivision.  
8. Evans Family Ranch is not a duplicate name of any previously recorded plat.  
9. The proposed Evans Family Ranch Subdivision would be comprised of three (3) lots, two each 
1.01 acres and the other 17.98 acres in size, utilizing the entire 20.0 acres of Parcel CANR-1-2. 
No remnant parcels are proposed.  
10. Access to all lots of the Evans Family Ranch subdivision is proposed via 1000 East, a County 
road.  
11. Staff review of the application indicates that it meets the minimum requirements for approval 
in the Eastern Summit County Development Code.  
12. A portion of Parcel CANR-1-2 is located within 100’ of a canal that has provided notice per 
Utah Code Annotated 17-27a-211. Notice was properly provided.  
13. The Eastern Summit County Water Conservation Special Service District recommended 
approval of a traditional septic wastewater system to the Summit County Manager on June 28, 
2018. The Manager is expected to provide final approval prior to the August 16 meeting of the 
Planning Commission.  
14. Staff has verified that property taxes on Parcel CANR-1-2 are current.  
15. Staff is unaware of any current or potential violations of Summit County regulations as a 
result of this proposal.  
 
Conclusions of Law 
1. Based upon the findings of fact listed above, Parcel CANR-1-2 is eligible for subdivision as 
the 3 lot Evans Family Ranch Subdivision.  
 
 
Commissioner Sonntag made a motion to send a positive recommendation for the

4.  Public hearing and possible action on a Conditional Use Permit to install a 104-
foot-tall cellular tower and associated equipment; 491 Lower River Road; CD-2197-
A; Liz Walker, applicant. -Amir Caus, County Planner 
 
Commissioner Wheaton recused herself from this item because she is an adjoining 
property owner. Chair Clyde stated for the record that he is not directly affected by this 
application but does live in the general neighborhood. 
Director Putt stated the applicant has requested a continuation to October 4, 2018. This 
item had notices sent out on September 4, 2018 for this public hearing. Staff recommends 
that this item be moved to the October 4, 2018 meeting. Public comment may be made 
tonight if the Planning Commission would like. 
 
The public hearing was opened.  
 
Bob Wheaton is an adjoining landowner and has written a letter to Staff concerning this 
application. In the process of the last year, this project and application have been 
misrepresented and inaccurate. Several other adjacent landowners understand this public 
hearing will be extended to next month. Mr. Wheaton appreciates the Planning Commission 
taking his comments under consideration. 
Chair Clyde asked Staff to make sure that Bob Wheaton’s letter makes it into the record for 
this application. The property in question fronts along Lower River Road, with access 
coming along the bench. The applicant has been negotiating with Mr. Wheaton for access, 
but the access has not been granted. Mr. Wheaton has let the applicant know that access 
across his property would not be appropriate and will not be granted.

Chair Clyde stated he cannot imagine a more visible location for a cell phone tower in this 
area. With an area as mountainous as this there ought to be a way to stick a cell tower in a 
rock on one of the surrounding mountains that would be not visible. It would sure be nice 
to have cell service in the area, which does not exist for the most part now. 
Martina Nelson is a land surveyor and stated cell towers can be hidden in forests of trees 
to mitigate cell towers and their appearance. Ms. Nelson saw a cell tower just the other day 
that blended in with other trees and was not visible.
