City Attorney, Fred Donaldson expressed appreciation for the citizen comments. He reported that
Mark Reid will be retiring effective July 1,2025, and the Resolution will approve the appointment
of a New City Manager and the terms of their employment contract. Approximately 80
applications were received for the position. The City Council interviewed the most qualified
candidates and discussed the applicants in a closed meeting. They selected Assistant City
Manager, Bruce Kartchner as the new City Manager. Mr. Kartchner has served as Assistant City
Manager since 2024. He has over 40 years of professional experience and is a Certified Public
Accountant who has been an integral part of Bluffdale for 19 years.

Mr. Donaldson reported that the contract terms were negotiated between the City Council and
Mr. Kartchner and they are similar to the current City Manager's contract. Some changes were
made as a result ofthose negotiations and agreed to by all parties. The contract is legally acceptable
and includes all relevant terms. An important aspect of an employee contract is to provide the
employee with a sense of security that they will not be arbitrarily retaliated against. It defines
being terminated for cause or not for cause and allows for terms related to resignation, death, and
disability. It also discusses what would happen if the employee was sued while serving in a
leadership capacity. The terms are similar to those found in city manager contracts in other
jurisdictions.


Mr.Donaldson clarified that the contract does not tie the hands ofany future City Council. Rather,
it imposes requirements and potential consequences for any action. He also believes that Utah
State Code prohibits the firing of a city manager after an election.

Arthur Brooks, and wanted to share his thoughts regarding the process. A keen responsibility falls
on the shoulders ofthe City Council. He does not have contempt or animosity towards anyone but
he wanted to express his opinion on the process. The City Council is not experienced in selecting
a City Manager and he did not know what to expect from the process. After thinking about it for
a few weeks, he believes they rushed the process and may have done the City a disservice by doing
