Study Session

1.0   Discussion: Convention Tourism Assessment Area

1.1   Tyson Lybbert, Vice President of Sales for Visit Salt Lake, and Scott George,
      representing Woodbury Hotels, described the timeline for inclusion in the
      planned Convention Tourism Assessment Area (CTAA) and gave examples of
      how CTAA funds might be spent (to be determined by a board of primarily
      hotel representatives). He expressed the opinion that the CTAA would boost
      the economy in the area and explained that city council approval via resolution
      was required for hotels in a city to be eligible to participate. A decision/vote
      was needed by June 6, 2023.

1.2   Councilmember T.f Lowery said she was not necessarily opposed to the CTAA,
      but felt if city councils were asked to vote for city involvement, cities should
      have some representation in determining how funds were spent.

5.0   Council/Manager Reports from Study Session

5.1   Councilmember T. Lowery commented on the increasing number of Surron
      bikes on City trails and said they looked like e-bikes but were really
      motorcycles, and were tearing up the trails. She said it was her understanding
      that City Code would need to be changed to prohibit the use of Surron bikes
      on City trails.

5.2   Councilmember F. Lowry said it was his understanding that throttled e-bikes
      were already illegal on the trails. The Council discussed the difficulty of
      enforcement. Staff indicated they would take a look at the current Code
      compared to what was out there and bring back recommendations.

5.3   Councilmember F. Lowry asked what the Council could do to help address
      graffiti throughout the City. He suggested the City perhaps provide supplies to
      clean the graffiti. Staff said they would look into options.
