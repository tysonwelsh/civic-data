PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on November 8, 2017 at 7:00 p.m. in the Community Center Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2017-46 authorizing a text change to the Land Use Ordinance to add a chapter regarding Planned Development Overlay Zone. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
