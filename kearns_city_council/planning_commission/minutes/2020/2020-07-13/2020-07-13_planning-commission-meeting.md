                                          LAND USE APPLICATION(S)
                                        Hearings began at – 5:42 p.m.

31138- Kent Woodruff is requesting approval for a Conditional Use for Motor Vehicle repairing and sales,
and a Used Car Lot. Acreage: 1.18 acres. Location: 4768 South 5325 West. Zone: M-1 (Manufacturing).
Planner: Diana Martinez. (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Diana Martinez
provided an analysis of the staff report.

Commissioner Wellman had a question on staff recommendation #4, asked if this was addition or
modification of building. Ms. Martinez said a new building but will be attached and will be addition to the
existing building. Commissioner Wellman asked #2 business license. Ms. Martinez confirmed MSD business
license. Mr. Bracken indicated he already obtained the state license and needs business license through the
MSD. Commissioner Wellman asked if okay to add existing buildings or new buildings on the lot. Mr.
Bracken said he is fine. Commissioner Nelson said #8 should be one, not once. Commissioner Hatch said
geared towards not becoming a salvage lot or storage, cars up for sale traded in may not be currently
licensed and avoid an issue for the property owner. Ms. Martinez said could put a 90-day check for code
enforcement. Mr. Bracken said the language is clear and not sure how long it takes, this is the code
violation, be clear in business license and a lot of the vehicles were not registered. More comfortable with
all vehicles must be licensed and registered in accordance with applicable laws. Commissioner Hatch asked
about #2. Mr. Bracken said would be taken care of through the MSD.

                                            PUBLIC HEARING(S)

30614 – Subdivision Ordinance as remanded to the planning commission from the Metro Council for
recommendation regarding: (1) updating Title 18 of the Metro Code to account for the metro township form
of government; (2) designating the Metro Council as the land use authority for subdivisions; and (3)
authorizing the planning commission to make recommendations regarding subdivisions to the Metro
Council. Presenter: MSD Counsel. (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed ordinance amendment after taking comments from the
public during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s
rules of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three
minutes per person.

Counsel Mr. Bracken provided an analysis of the ordinance.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Kearns Community Council
Name: Roger Snow
Address: 5977 South Parkwood Drive
