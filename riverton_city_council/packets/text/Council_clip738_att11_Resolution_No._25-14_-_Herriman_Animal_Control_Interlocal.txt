                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO 25-14

  A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT
             BETWEEN HERRIMAN CITY AND RIVERTON CITY FOR
                    ANIMAL CONTROL SERVICES

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has identified a need to provide animal control sheltering
services by contract as it transitions toward constructing an animal control shelter; and

      WHEREAS, Herriman City has expressed a willingness to provide Riverton City
temporary sheltering services needed by Riverton City; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Section 1-13-202.5 requires an agreement of this nature be
approved in a public meeting and by Resolution of the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, the parties desire to contract with each other for the purpose of Animal
Control services set out herein, and

       NOW THEREFORE RE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

    1.         The Riverton City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an lnterlocal
               Cooperation Agreement with Herriman City for animal control sheltering and
               related Services, upon approval as to legal form by the City Attorney.

  2.           This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
