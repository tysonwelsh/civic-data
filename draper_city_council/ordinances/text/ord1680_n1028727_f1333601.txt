City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1680

On October 7, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1680 approving an
amendment to Draper City Code Sections 7-11-010 and 7-11-030 regarding solid
waste and recyclable material collection services on private streets in Draper City. The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online
at https://www.draperutah.gov/
