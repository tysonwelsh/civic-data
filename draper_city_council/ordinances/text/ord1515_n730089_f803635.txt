City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On January 18, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1515 amending the Draper
City Municipal Code, Title 15, Chapter 4, Special Events. The complete ordinance is on
file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published
