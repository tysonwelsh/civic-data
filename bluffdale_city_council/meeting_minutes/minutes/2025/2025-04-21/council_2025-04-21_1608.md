BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL REGULAR BUSINESS MEETING


1.       Call to Order.


Mayor Natalie Hall called the meeting order at 1:00 p.m.

All members ofthe City Council were present.


2.       Closed Meeting.

Mayor Hall requested a closed session to discuss the character, professional competence, or
physical or mental health of individuals applying for City Manager position.

Council Member Aston MOVED to approve moving into a Closed Session. The motion was
SECONDED by Council Member Austin. Vote on motion: Council Member Austin-Yes,
Council Member Aston-Yes, Council Member Wilding-Yes, Council Member Crockett-Yes,
Council Member Lord-Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

The City Council was in Closed Session from 1:01 PM to 2:29 PM.

3.       Adiournment.


Council Member Aston moved to ADJOURN. The motion was SECONDED by Council
Member Wilding. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Council.

The City Council Meeting adjourned at 2:30 PM.

                           CLOSED MEETING CERTIFICATE

Council Member Wendy Aston MOVED to meet in Closed Session pursuant to Utah Code §52-
4-205(1) to discuss the character, professional competence, or physical or mental health of
applicants for the position of Bluffdale City Manager at 1:01 p.m. Council Member Steve
Austin SECONDED the motion. Mayor Hall called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows:
Steve Austin-Aye, Wendy Aston-Aye, Greg Wilding-Aye, Traci Crockett-Aye and Alan Lord-Aye.
All members of the City Council present voted and the motion carried by a unanimous vote.
Those others present at the Closed Session were as follows: Mayor Hall

CLOSED SESSION


I, Natalie C. Hall, Mayor of the City of Bluffdale, do hereby certify that a meeting of the
City Council held on April 21, 2025, was closed to discuss the character, professional
competence, or physical or mental health of applicants for City Manager, wherein no
other items were discussed.

Council Member Wendy Aston MOVED to adjourn the Closed Session at 2:29 p.m. Council
Member Greg Wilding SECONDED the motion. The motion passed with the unanimous
consent of the Council.
