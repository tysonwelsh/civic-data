RESOLUTION NO. 20-05-01                                                  Date: April 15, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       CONSENTING TO AND AUTHORIZING EXTENSION OF THE DECLARTION OF
       A PUBLIC HEALTH EMERGENCY FOR THE CORONAVIRUS DISEASE OF
       2019 (COVID-19)




                                              2
DATE            WEDNESDAY                                APRIL                             15, 2020

       WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) declared a public health
emergency for the coronavirus disease of 2019 (“COVID-19”) on March 13, 2020 (the
“Declaration”) pursuant to Chapter 2.86 of the Copperton Metro Township Code (the “Code”); and

       WHEREAS, consistent with Chapter 2.86 of the Code and consistent with its own terms,
the Declaration expired on April 12, 2020, thirty days after its adoption; and

       WHEREAS, the Declaration may be renewed with consent the Copperton Metro Township
Council (the “Council”) pursuant to Chapter 2.86 of the Code and Utah Code Section 53-2a-208
of Utah’s Disaster Response and Recovery Act; and

      WHEREAS, guidance from the President of the United States received on March 29, 2020
recommended that states continue stay home orders until at least April 30, 2020; and

     WHEREAS, the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation expects the peak of the
COVID-19 pandemic in Utah to be around April 24; and

        WHEREAS, the Council finds that the health, wellness, and safety of Copperton and its
citizens are served by reinstating and continuing the Declaration for at least thirty additional days
beyond its current term; and

        WHEREAS, these conditions continue to constitute a “State of Emergency” within the
intent of the Disaster Response and Recovery Act found in Title 53, Chapter 2a of the Utah Code
Annotated 1953, as amended; and necessitate that Copperton continue the proclamation of a
local public health emergency.

       NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, effective immediately by the Copperton
Metro Township Council that:

       SECTION 1: The Council consents to and hereby reinstates the Declaration and sets the
       new expiration of the Declaration’s term as May 15, 2020; and

       SECTION 2: The Mayor of Copperton may amend the Declaration consistent with Utah’s
       Disaster Response and Recovery Act and Chapter 2.86 of the Code as needed to address
       the coronavirus public health emergency as it evolves; and

       SECTION 3: This resolution shall be filed with the office of the Salt Lake County Clerk
       pursuant to Utah Code Section 53-2a-208(c); and

       SECTION 4: This resolution shall take effect immediately upon its publication pursuant to
       Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-711(1) and Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-712.

       APPROVED and ADOPTED this 15th day of April, 2020.

Arborist RFP

              Council Member Bailey stated she has read Nathan Bracken’s proposed RFP
and did not see anything that needed changing.

                 Nathan Bracken stated the Council discussed hiring a consulting arborist. That
classification is given by one specific organization and there are only 700 in the country. A master
arborist has similar qualifications to a consulting arborist so that was included in the RFP. The
RFP requires candidates to describe their experience on similar projects, and the expertise of
their staff to complete the components included in the scope of the plan. It should be open for a
fair amount of time because the Council is looking for a rare set of skills. This will be someone
who can plan the park.

              Mayor Clayton stated maybe the wording could say the candidate would provide
an example of a project they have done.

              Mr. Bracken made that change to the RFP. He also recommended including
dates for when proposals are due, presentations will be done, and final work product should be
completed.

               Council Member Bailey stated sometimes there is a meeting with the applicant
before the proposal is submitted so the applicant can ask questions before they submit a proposal.

             Mr. Bracken suggested having that meeting before the May Council meeting. The
Council and Mr. Bracken established the following timeline:

   •   RFP advertised by April 30, 2020
   •   Questions due by May 15, 2020 at 11:59 pm
   •   Proposals due June 12, 2020 at 11:59 pm
   •   Presentations July 15, 2020 at 6:30
   •   Evaluation and Selection by Council on or before July 22, 2020
   •   Final Copperton Park Master Plan due August 30, 2020

               Mr. Bracken stated this falls under the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (MSD) contract. They should be responsible for publishing the RFP.

Social Media Policy

              Council Member Bailey stated there are limitations on what can be removed
from a public social media site. The proposed policy does not affect personal social media
accounts, only the Council’s page.

               Nathan Bracken stated the County has three separate policies. They were quite
good, but they would be better for Copperton if they were consolidated into one. Copperton must
have a social media policy because it is dealing with free speech rights. The Council can regulate
comments not germane to the conduct and good governance of Copperton. Currently, the
Council has its own website and a Facebook page. When an Administrative Assistant is hired,
that person can moderate the Facebook page. If the Council wants public social media accounts,
it must have a policy in place.

               Council Member Pazell stated he would like to take another month to review the
policy.

               Mayor Clayton tabled the matter until the May meeting.
