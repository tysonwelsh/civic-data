BAR W ANNEXATION – ORDINANCE 09-21-2021 ADOPTED:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council that Nephi City received the BAR W
Annexation petition in July of 2021. At that time, the City Council approved the continuation of the
annexation process. Since that time, the annexation has been certified by the City Recorder, the 30-day
protest period had elapsed, and a public hearing was held on September 14th, 2021. No comment was
received at the public hearing, but two letters were received from residents of the county near the area
of the annexation. The concerns listed in the letter were mainly regarding a slaughterhouse. The zoning
proposed on this annexation, Highway Commercial (HC), would not allow a slaughterhouse to be
constructed. In addition, this annexation would be the city’s first island annexation, which means it is
not contiguous with the city’s current limits. Juab County needed to grant permission for an island
annexation, and they approved the annexation in their 8-2-2021 commission meeting.
   Councilor Ostler moved to adopt Ordinance 09-21-2021 Bar W Annexation. Councilor Seely seconded
the motion. The motion passed on the following vote:

                                 Nay: Councilor Kent Jones
                                 Yes: Councilor Larry Ostler
                                      Councilor Nathan Memmott
                                      Councilor Skip Worwood
                                      Councilor Justin Seely

LAND USE CODE REVISIONS-ORIDINANCE 09-21-2021-A:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council that In the 2021 Utah state legislative session, a bill required cities
to allow interior accessory dwelling units or Interior ADUs. Examples of an interior ADU would be a
basement apartment or renting out a bedroom within a primary dwelling unit. The state removed most
of the ability for a city to regulate these units. The items that can be regulated by cities must be in place
by October 1st of 2021. Mr. Atkinson explained that the proposed ordinance mainly changes land use
code regarding interior ADUs but also proposes clarification to zoning districts regarding Planned Unit
Developments (PUDs), changes to noticing requirements, and a small change to the Central Commercial
Zone list of uses.
   Councilor Ostler moved to adopt Ordinance 09-21-21-A. Councilor Jones seconded the motion the
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

  Mr. Atkinson informed the council that The City had received a request from property owner Joe
Humphrey to change the annexation area level on a portion of his property from a Level 3 to a Level 2.
The front portion of this property, adjacent to the highway, is already zoned Level 1. Mr. Humphrey had
stated that he would like to develop this property into commercial/industrial pads. The property is
across from the county road shed on Highway 132 which is in the city so the annexation would be
contiguous. Councilor Worwood moved to change the area identified from a Level 3 to a Level 2 on the
Annexation Area Map. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following
vote:

                                      Councilor Nathan Memmott
                                      Councilor Skip Worwood

POLICE DEPARTMENT OUT-OF-STATE TRAVEL APPROVED:

   Chief Michael Morgan had put in a written request for Captain Bert Wright and Sergeant John
Deeben to travel to Redmond, Washington to receive top rated training on human trafficking and sexual
abuse of children investigations. Operation Underground Railroad would be paying for the airfare,
hotel, and registration. The Nephi City Police budget would pay for the rental car and meals. The
conference is being held on October 6-8th. Councilor Ostler moved to approve the out-of-state travel
request. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. Councilor Jones requested that Chief Morgan have
the officers report on the conference. The motion passed with a unanimous vote.
