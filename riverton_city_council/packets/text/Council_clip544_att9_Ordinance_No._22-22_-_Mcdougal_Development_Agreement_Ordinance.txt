                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 22-22

AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING “THE MASTER DEVELOPMENT
AGREEMENT FOR RIVERTON RANCH ESTATES” FOR DEVELOPMENT OF 3.2 ACRES
AT APPROXIMATELY 13040 S REDWOOD ROAD, BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND
RYAN MCDOUGAL

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that under Utah Code §10-9a-532, the City Council may
lawfully adopt development agreements that have different standards set forth in existing land use
regulations if it does so in accordance with the same procedures for enacting a land use regulation,
including review and recommendation from the Planning Commission and a public hearing; and,

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed development agreement; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said development
agreement; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to adopt the Master Development Agreement for Riverton Ranch Estates, located at 13040 S
Redwood Road as described herein and shown as Exhibit “A.”

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Master Development Agreement for Riverton Ranch
                              Estates, Exhibit “A”, and all associated design documents as
                              approved, is hereby approved and adopted. The approval
                              includes the following conditions:
                                        a.   “Redline” corrections and issues be addressed prior to recordation
                                             of the subdivision plat.
                                        b.   Solid fencing, consistent with Riverton City’s ordinance
                                             requirements, be installed on the subdivision boundaries, including
                                             solid masonry fencing on Redwood Road.


                Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4th day of
October, 2022 by the following vote:
