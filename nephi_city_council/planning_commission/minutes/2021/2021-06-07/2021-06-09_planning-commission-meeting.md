Nephi City Planning Commission Meeting
                                    June 9, 2021
Chairman Greenhalgh welcomed those in attendance and opened the meeting at 7:00 p.m.

Opening prayer offered by Karl Brough.

Minutes of May 12th regular meeting and public hearings were reviewed by Commission. Karl Brough motioned the
approval of all May 12th minutes. Motion was seconded by Ann Peterson. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Brent Bowles has requested zone change from R1-H to R1-8-Z involving 10 acres southwest of current Gardner
Village development. If this area is developed it would require the expansion of 200 West to 900 North. Cory
Thomson motioned for public hearing to be held at next month’s meeting. Motion was seconded by Ann Peterson.
Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Commission reviewed Bryce’s Landing final plat along with DRC notes. New plat has been presented and Rick
Carlton is asking for conditional approval while they are waiting for UDOT to issue their permit. Karl Brough asked
if phase 2 will have access to the road by the hospital. Rick Carlton said the hospital has no plans to develop in that
area but there will be spots they can tie into. Geotechnical report has been submitted to the City. Storm drainage
will be contained on this property alone. Kent Heap motioned conditional approval (upon UDOT permit) of Bryce’s
Landing final plat along with recommendations from DRC notes. Motion seconded by Karl Brough. Motion passed
with a unanimous vote.

Winn Ridge has submitted new plat drawings. All items from DRC notes have been taken care of. Commission
decided second meeting in June will be held on 23rd to review new plat. 3 lots were deeded to Clint Robertson.
City is asking utilities be stubbed into them.

General Land Use Code updates were reviewed by Commission. Karl Brough and Kent Heap asked why ‘Steep
Slope Lot’ has percentage in the definition instead of degrees. Glenn will look into it. Updates will be given to
Commission as Glenn continues work on this.

Karl Brough updated Commission on their meeting with City Council. Blight ordinance was discussed and a few
council members were concerned with the subject.

Chairman Greenhalgh informed Commission that legislation has passed training requirements for Planning
Commission members.

Other items of business discussed include high-density plans, traffic studies, and commercial areas.

Chairman Greenhalgh adjourned the meeting at 8:23pm.
