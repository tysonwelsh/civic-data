                                        City Council Meeting Agenda
                                              January 20, 2026



PRESENTER:              Mayor Tish Buroker


ITEM:


Resolution No. 26-06 – Approving the Creation of a Skate Park Subcommittee and appointing three City
Councilmembers to serve on a subcommittee to evaluate skateboarding as a use of City park facilities.


"I move to adopt Resolution No. 26-06 – Approving the Creation of a Skate Park Subcommittee and
appointing City Councilmembers _________, ____________, and _____________ to serve on the
subcommittee which considers what, if any, level of skateboarding services shall be provided by Riverton
City.




SUMMARY:


Riverton City has recently removed the Riverton City Skate Park from the City’s Main Park, owing to safety
concerns associated with the Skate Park. The City needs to decide which direction to take regarding the
level of service the City should provide for skateboarding and related activities. To this end, the City Council
needs to deliberate and appoint three members of the Council to create a sub-committee for evaluating the
City’s options related to the future of skateboarding in Riverton.
