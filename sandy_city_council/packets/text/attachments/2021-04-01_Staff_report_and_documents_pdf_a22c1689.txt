REQUEST
Mr. Brandon Lundeen, representing Diversified Design Services and Engineering, is
requesting preliminary Commercial Site Plan Review and Conditional Use Approval for
a new commercial building with a drive-up window restaurant use, on the property
located at 9492 S. Highland Dr.  The site is currently a vacant, pad site within the existing
Little Cottonwood Shopping Center project. The property is located in the Special
Development (Magna) (CC) Zoning district.  A Conditional Use Permit is required for the
drive-up window restaurant use, due to the location of residential zoning within 250 feet
(East) of the subject property. The proposed 3,160 square foot, single story commercial
building is known as the "Sandy Lease Restaurant" building which is being built by the

BACKGROUND
The site is lot 5, of the Little Cottonwood Center, Amended Lot 1 Subdivision, and is vacant.  All
streetscape improvements are existing from the overall development of this portion of the shopping
center.  The existing zoning, SD (Magna) (CC), requires that Site Plan Approval be by the Planning
Commission.  This is the last lot in the subdivision to be developed.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting.  A neighborhood meeting was held for this project
due to the project's location relative to to existing Residenital Zoning accross Highland Drive to
the East, which is the Sandy City Fire Station. The meeting was a virtual meeting hosted on Zoom
and no one from the public attended.  The meeting was held on February 11, 2021.

ANALYSIS
Pedestrian and Vehicle Access and On-site Circulation.  All street improvements, sidewalks,
landscaped parking strips and internal driveway connections are in place from prior
development of the shopping center, and will be adequate for the proposed development.  This
is a shell building for future leasing to probable restaurant users.  The north portion of the
building is being designed for a drive-up window restaurant use.  As such, the required queuing
space in the drive-up window driveway is designed to meet City requirements.

Zoning Review.  The project will meet all of the requirements of the SD (Magna) (CC) zoning
district and the Sandy City Development code.  Parking for this project will meet the City
requirements on site, but also have shared parking rights to all other parking stalls in the
shopping center development.  All required City Departments and Divisions have reviewed and
recommend approval, subject to their usual requirements and conditions.  A drive-up window
restaurant is a Conditional Use in the SD (Magna) (CC) zoning district when in proximity to
existing residential zoning.  As mentioned above, the Sandy City Fire Station is zoned residential.
The Conditional Use review is being addressed in a subsequent staff report and agenda item on
this same meeting agenda. (See separate Conditional Use application #CUP-03-21-6010.

Architectural Design Review.  The building's design, materials and colors were reviewed by
the Sandy Planning Staff as to compliance with the Sandy City Architectural Design
Requirements.  Staff made some recommendations to the developer on building articulation and
fenestration by adding more depth to the building projections and adding some spandrel glass
to blank building faces, which has been done.  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission
approves the building architectural design, materials and colors as now proposed by the
applicant.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan review is
complete for the proposed Sandy Lease Restaurant Building project, based upon the following
two findings and the following seven conditions:

FINDINGS.

        A.  That the proposed project meets or will meet all the applicable requirements of the
            Sandy City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as
            to building architectural design, materials and colors.
        B.  That the elimination of this vacant pad site will complete the buildings in the Little
            Cottonwood Center commercial shopping center and add needed community scale
            retail capacity to the neighborhood.

CONDITIONS.

    1.  That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior
        to the start of any construction. The final site plan shall comply with all Development
        Code requirements and those modifications required or allowed by the Planning
        Commission.

    2.  That the development complies with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety
        Codes applicable to this type of use.

    3.  That the developer be responsible to meet all provisions of the SD (Magna) (CC) and
        the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards with the details finalized with staff
        during final site plan review.

    4.  All utility boxes (i.e. transformers, switchgear, telephone, cable TV, etc.) shall be shown
        on the site plan and shall be placed underground or moved behind the front setback of
        the buildings and screened from view, preferably located along the proposed driveway.
        Each box shall be shown in its exact location and shall be noted with its exact height,
        width and length. Building utility meters shall be mounted to the side or rear elevation
        or screened.

    5.  That the developer be responsible for the placement of a temporary 6 foot high chain
        link fence around the perimeter of the project during the construction phase of the
        project for security. Said fence shall also be required to include fabric to prohibit
        blowing dust problems, if it becomes necessary or if it is required by the Community
        Development Department during Site Plan Review.

    6.  That the applicant complies with all department requirements as noted in all
        Preliminary Review letters prior to submittal for final site plan review with staff.

    7.  That all signage be reviewed and approved under separate permit and be in
        conformance with City Code.
