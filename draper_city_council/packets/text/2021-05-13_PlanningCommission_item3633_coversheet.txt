Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: May 13, 2021
Re: NCS Residential Development Land Use and Zoning Map Amendment Requests Comments: This application is a request for approval of a zoning map amendment for approximately 9.81 acres located at approximately 12197 S. 300 East.  The site has a land use designation of Neighborhood Commercial and is currently zoned A5.  The applicant is requesting to change the land use designation to Residential High Density and rezone the entire property from A5 to RM2.
The subject property has historically been utilized as agricultural or farming land since at least 1978 when the City incorporated.  There have been previous applications to rezone the property and the most recent one was in the fall of 2020.  The fall 2020 application was for a commercial zoning, and there were concerns expressed about that request from the public.  The applicant has submitted this application for residential zoning based on those concerns raised. The findings are as follows: For a positive recommendation:
1.   That DCMC Section 9-5-060(E) provides the approval standards for amending Draper City’s zoning map.
2.   That this map amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
3.   This land use map amendment proposes a land use designation that has less intensive traffic uses and therefore would result in a reduced traffic impact to the surrounding area than the current commercial land use designation.
4.   The proposed residential land use and zoning category will provide a buffer from the existing commercial uses to the south and the existing single-family residential to the north.
For a negative recommendation:
1.   That this map amendment compromises one or more of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
