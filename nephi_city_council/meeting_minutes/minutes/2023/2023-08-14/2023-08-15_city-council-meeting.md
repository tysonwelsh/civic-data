Mayor Justin Seely opened the regular council meeting and welcomed all present.

PRAYER: Council Member Nathan Memmott

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Nephi North Stake and Nephi Stake Relief Society presidency members led the
pledge of allegiance. Betty Jo Morgan, Nephi Utah Stake Relief Society President, and DeAnn
Sutherland, North Nephi Stake Relief Society President talked of their plans for the Community Day of
Service on September 11th. The project they have chosen is to raise money to pay the unpaid school
lunch bills for students in the Juab School District in addition they will be doing a food drive. The Day of
Service will begin on September 9 with a Fun Run, flag ceremony, cornhole and spike ball competitions.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ost.er moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting 8-1-23, work session minutes 8-8-23, a list of claims on the warrant
register dated 8-15-23, the sale of burial rights to Jamie Coombs and Debra and Rodney Painter.
Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

RATIFICATION OF PRESTON LUNT AS A WATER/WASTEWATER UTILITY TECHNICIAN:

  Councilor Memmott moved to ratify the hiring of Preston Lunt as the new water/wastewater
technician. Mr. Lunt is replacing Brandon Aagard who has taken other employment. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 08-15-2023 REZONE PARCEL #XA00-0670-2 (431 N. MAIN) FROM CU-1 TO CU-2 ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that Arrow Builders have requested to rezone parcel # XA00-
0670-2 at approximately 431 N Main St from the CU-1 zone (primarily commercial, with limited
residential) to the CU-2 zone (primarily residential with limited commercial). This rezone is running
concurrently with a project site plan. They wish to put a horizontally built four-plex on this lot which is
not an allowed use in their current CU-1 zone but is an allowed use in the proposed CU-2 zone.
    Councilor Parady moved to adopt Ordinance 08-15-2023 with the caveat that to the best of the City’s
knowledge there is not any boundary line dispute on the parcel that is being rezoned from CU-1 to CU-2.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

AIRPORT HANGAR GROUND LEASE WITH TREVOR AND CHANTEL BENNETT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that Trevor and Chantel Bennett would like to lease hangar
number 16 at the airport. The lease would be a 20-year lease at the standard ground lease rate.
Councilor Parady moved to approve the lease agreement with the Bennetts, and to authorize the Mayor
to sign the lease. Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously with
Councilor Memmott abstaining from voting due to being closely related to the Bennetts.
   Mr. Atkinson stated that the Airport Advisory Board may request from the council permission in the
future to adopt additional use fees at the airport, including tie-down fees, glider parking fees, and water
connection usage fees.


CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATES:

         Mr. Atkinson reported that he had been in contact with the residents of Chapel View Estates
          that have had problems with sunken roads and water drainage and have informed them that
          the City has contracted with a company to help fix the damage and the work should be started
          between September 11-15.
         Mr. Atkinson reported that he had the opportunity to observe the intersection at 600 E and
          200 South. He stated that yield signs will be installed on this intersection.
         Councilor Memmott complimented the water department on their excellent work completing
          a new waterline connection at his home.
         Mayor Seely said that the Police Department will provide security at the Day of Service event.
         Councilor Parady expressed his appreciation to the Chamber of Commerce for hosting the
          Meet the Candidates Night.
         Councilor Ostler reported that he had attended the information meeting about how the
          ballots are counted and how the election process works presented by the Juab County Clerk
          Tanielle Callaway.
         The council agreed to cancel the work session next week because of no pressing business.

ADJOURN INTO EXECUTIVE SESSION:

      Councilor Memmott moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual. Councilor Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.


Work Session 8:02 pm

    1.    Developer Vic Arnold addressed the council and requested that a piece of property located at
         400 N 300 W be declared “blight” and was interested in the waiver of landfill fees. He said he
         plans on demolishing the building and cleaning up the entire lot. Mr. Arnold would like to build
         a new home on the property. Mr. Arnold suggested that the City could add to their blight
         ordinance the provision that the amount of waived landfill fees could be required to be put into
         the property improvements. Councilor Ostler said he would like an estimate of the landfill fees
         that are being requested to be waived. Councilor Memmott expressed his concern that this
         piece of property was not one that the council had originally thought about being a blight area.
         The council directed staff to put this issue on the September 5th meeting agenda.

    2. Councilor Memmott moved to adjourn. Councilor Parady seconded the motion. The motion
       passed on a unanimous vote. The meeting adjourned at 8:15 pm.
