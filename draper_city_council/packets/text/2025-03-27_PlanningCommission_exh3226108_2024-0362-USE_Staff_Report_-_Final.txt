BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a Conditional Use permit for approximately
5.77 acres located on the east side of Vista Blvd, at approximately 12383 S. Vista Station
Blvd. (Exhibit C & D). The property is currently zoned OS and RA1. The applicant is
requesting that a Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for construction of a new
wireless telecommunications facility (monopole).

The Municipal Building Authority of Draper City owns the property and the property is
primarily used as parking for Galena Hills Park (East), and as a materials storage area for
the Parks Department. The City Council, acting as the Municipal Building Authority,
approved a lease of the subject area to Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC on January 10, 2023. A
Conditional Use Permit for a facility with a similar layout to the one proposed with this

A Conditional Use Permit is necessary for this project as proposed because:
   1. The monopole will not be set back at least one hundred feet (100') from all adjacent
      public rights-of-way.
   2. No landscaping is proposed with the facility installation.
   3. The height of the proposed monopole exceeds sixty feet (60’).
   4. The proposed communication and power lines between the accessory equipment
      building and antenna structures are not located underground.

Per DCMC Subsection 9-41-070(C), “Specific fencing and screening plans for each facility shall
be submitted to the city in conjunction with any conditional use permit application. Higher grade
fencing, more aesthetic fence designs, and landscaping may be required for the purposes of
visual screening, architectural compatibility, site compatibility, and other impact mitigation.”

The proposed fencing (Exhibit G) indicates the use of standard chain link fencing topped
with barbed wire. Per the proposed fencing elevations, the fence will exceed six feet (6’) in




AT&T Galena Grove                                                           App. # «JS_File_Number»
Conditions Use Permit Request                        6
height. Fencing in excess of 6’ in height and up to a maximum of ten feet (10’) in height
requires Planning Commission approval as part of a Conditional Use Permit. The Planning
Commission will need to determine if the fencing as proposed is acceptable. If the Planning
Commission determines that higher grade fencing is required, a specific condition should
be added to this Conditional Use Permit stating what the requirements and mitigations
that will be required of the applicant in revised drawings.

Where the actual location of the adjacent parking and assumed access right-of-way is not
clearly dimensioned on the site plans, staff is unable to determine an exact setback
distance, however staff has approximated the nearest wheel stop and parking area is
located between fifty feet (50’) and sixty feet (60’) from the nearest corner of the leased
area. The rail line right-of-way is also approximately fifty-four feet (54’) from the lease area.
Although these are short of the ordinance standards, the Planning Commission can alter or
waive the requirements through the Conditional Use Permit process. Staff does not believe
that any negative impacts are created by the proposed locations for the monopole,
accessory structures, or other equipment, for which mitigation would be needed with
respects to the proposed setbacks from property lines, other features of the site, or
adjacent properties.

Per DCMC Subsection 9-41-070(E)(2), “No monopole with antennas and antenna support
structures shall exceed a height of one hundred feet (100').”

The proposed elevation drawings (Exhibit H) show the antennas and antenna support
structures on the monopole at one-hundred feet (100’) height. This complies with DCMC
Subsection 9-41-070(E)(2) and will potentially provide additional co-location space on the
pole for other carriers. Future requests for co-location on the pole should be handled
administratively as Permitted Use Permit requests.

An Ice bridge is proposed to connect the power and communication lines between the
monopole and the equipment building. This bridge will be approximately ten feet (10’)
above the ground rather be underground as per the standards in ordinance. The Planning
Commission may approve of the proposed location of the ice bridge or require
modifications to the location to mitigate negative impacts to visibility or aesthetic concerns
should the Planning Commission find that mitigation of such impacts is needed.

Architecture. The proposed elevations drawings for the monopole and the equipment
cabinets and structures are included as Exhibit H. As the equipment is screened by the
fencing, staff believes that any negative impacts that may exist are sufficiently mitigated.

Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review and potential approval of a conditional use permit request is found
in Subsection 9-5-080(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

         E. Approval Standards: The standards of this subsection shall apply to the issuance of a
             conditional use permit:
          1. A conditional use permit may be issued for a use to be located within a zone where
             the particular conditional use is allowed by the use regulations of the zone.
          2. Reasonable conditions may be imposed as necessary to substantially mitigate
             reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use. These conditions may
             include conditions concerning use, construction, character, location, landscaping,
             screening, parking, hours of operation, and other matters relating to the purposes
             and objectives of this section. Such conditions shall be expressly set forth in the
             motion authorizing the conditional use permit.
          3. If the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of a proposed conditional use
             cannot be substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions to
             achieve compliance with applicable standards, the conditional use may be denied.

Additionally, DCMC Subsection 9-41-070(H) contains additional criteria for review and
potential approval of a Conditional Use Permit for a Wireless Telecommunications Facility.
These additional factors include:
       1. Compatibility of the proposed structure with the height and mass of existing buildings
           and utility structures.
       2. For new sites, whether location of the antenna on other existing structures in the
           same vicinity such as other towers, buildings, water towers, utility poles, athletic field
           lights, parking lot lights, etc., is possible without significantly impacting antenna
           transmission or reception.
       3. Antenna location in relation to existing vegetation, topography and buildings to
           obtain the best visual screening.
       4. Whether spacing between monopoles creates quantifiable detrimental impacts to
           adjoining properties.

The applicant’s responses to the criteria necessary for approval of a Conditional Use Permit
are included as Exhibit I. The applicant acknowledged the visual impacts that a monopole
tower creates and suggests that the impacts are mitigated through the placement of the
pole in a commercial area that is almost 500 yards from the nearest residential property.
Furthermore, the city building in front of the lease area helps to screen the ground
equipment as does the proposed fencing. Small HVAC units create minimal noise and the
use does not generate objectionable odors. The applicant also indicates in their narrative

The applicant indicated that they believed the City’s General Plan to support the

development though the approval of the lease agreement with AT&T by the City Council.

Project Overview: In an effort to improve cellular coverage in the area AT&T is seeking
approval for a new cell tower. In an effort to balance the visual effects from a cell
tower with the needed coverage, a great deal of time I spent to identify viable
locations that meet the needed coverage objectives while minimizing impact and
meeting city code. While it is impossible to completely eliminate visual impact, one
of the most affective options is the location where the tower is placed. Being in a
commercial area behind a city building adjacent to the train tracks and away from any
residential structures, the proposed location is one of the more ideal place for a tower
to be placed and is in compliance with the requirements of the city code.

1. A conditional use permit may be issued for a use to be located within a zone where the
particular conditional use is allowed by the use regulations of the zone. Code does allow
towers in this zone with a CUP.
2. Reasonable conditions may be imposed as necessary to substantially mitigate
reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use. These conditions may
include conditions concerning use, construction, character, location, landscaping,
screening, parking, hours of operation, and other matters relating to the purposes and
objectives of this section. Such conditions shall be expressly set forth in the motion
authorizing the conditional use permit. Most of the impact from a cell tower is created
from visual appearance. In an effort to mitigate this the tower is was placed in a
commercial area with the nearest residential property being almost 500 yards away. It
is also set back on the property near the railroad tracks. The city building in front of
the lease area helps to screen the ground equipment. The tower is a monopole design
to minimize its cross section and is proposed to be non-reflective galvanized steel to
again minimize its visual appearance. Once constructed the tower operates 24/7 but
is unmanned and requires no parking and has no effect on traffic in the area. The site
creates minimal noise created by small HVAC units, and generates no odors. The
location of the tower allows for plenty of room for staging and stacking of the tower
during construction. As the area is used for mainly commercial uses it is more
appropriate for a cell tower them other more intrusive locations.
