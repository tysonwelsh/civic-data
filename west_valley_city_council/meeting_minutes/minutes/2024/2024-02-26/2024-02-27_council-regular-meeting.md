     APALOOSA DRIVE
     Mayor Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular Council
     Meeting scheduled February 20, 2024, in order for the City Council to hear and
     consider public comments regarding application GPZ-8-2023, Filed by Lieu Tran,
     Requesting a General Plan Change from Parks and Open Space Existing to Light
     Manufacturing and a Zone Change from A-1 (Agriculture, Minimum Lot Size 1 acre)
     to M (Manufacturing) for Property Located at 7007 West Apaloosa Drive.

   Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
   information as follows:

          The intended development for this property is 83,000 square feet of
          warehouse space and 4,000 square feet of office. The applicant’s original
          proposal included self-storage and industrial flex space. However, after
          Magna Water expressed concerns about these uses, the applicant modified
          the proposal. Access would be gained primarily from Beagley Road by
          crossing the Riter Canal. Apaloosa Drive would provide secondary access.

          The following list of current conditions may warrant consideration of M
          zoning on the subject property:
               The Northwest Economic Development Area (EDA), which was
                  created in 2012, includes the subject property. The Northwest
                  EDA covers approximately 1,000 acres in the northwest part of
                  the City and makes available incentives for commercial
                  development.
               The Magna Water property to the south and east includes wells
                  and a water treatment facility. These uses will likely remain in
                  perpetuity with no chance of having connecting roads to the
                  subject parcel.
               The Western Mobile Estates mobile home park to the west has
                  only one access, Apaloosa Drive, to 7200 West. Given that there
                  are already 144 mobile homes with only one access, additional
                  homes on the subject parcel would not be allowed without another
                  access.
               There are large, distribution warehouses that are part of the ARA
                  Industrial Park on the north side of Beagley Road.

   Mayor Lang opened the Public Hearing.

   Lieu Tran stated that she owns the property and has dreamed of having the
   opportunity to succeed as an immigrant woman. She asked the Council for their
   support and approval of the application.

   Mayor Lang closed the Public Hearing.

   ACTION: RESOLUTION NO. 24-31, AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO ENTER
   INTO A DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT WITH 7007 SERIES OF CHINITA,
   LLC FOR APPROXIMATELY 5.91 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED AT
   APPROXIMATELY 7007 WEST APALOOSA DRIVE
   John Flores, Interim Assistant City Manager, discussed proposed Resolution 24-31
   that would authorize the City to Enter Into a Development Agreement With 7007
   Series of Chinita, LLC for Approximately 5.91 Acres of Property Located at
   Approximately 7007 West Apaloosa Drive

   Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
   information as follows:

          Lieu Tran has submitted a General Plan/zone change application (GPZ-8-
          2023) on 5.91 acres to change the General Plan from Parks and Open Space
          Existing to Light Manufacturing and a zone change from A-1 (Agriculture,
          minimum lot size 1 acre) to M (Manufacturing).

          Magna Water District expressed concerns about the proposal given its
          proximity to their wells and water treatment plant. In response to Magna
          Water’s concerns, the applicant submitted a modified proposal for
          warehousing with a development agreement proposal to limit the allowed
          uses on the property. After reviewing the applicant’s modified proposal,
          Trevor Andra with Magna Water provided a letter to the applicant, which is
          attached, wherein he states: “While we still do have concerns over the
          potential contaminants and site security, they have been reduced. MWD
          supports the proposed exclusions and the potential future development of
          the property.”

          The proposed development agreement incorporates the prohibited uses that
          the applicant proposed and Magna Water reviewed.

   Upon inquiry by Mayor Lang there were no further questions from members of the
   City Council, and she called for a motion.

RESOLUTION 24-37: APPOINT IFO PILI AS CITY MANAGER
John Flores, Interim Assistant City Manager, presented proposed Resolution 24-37 that
would appoint Ifo Pili as City Manager.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The interactive process to hire a new City Manager began in July 2023. After
       evaluating several highly professional public sector executive search firms, Affion
       Public was selected to support the recruitment effort in finding the best candidate
       for the position. City Council has interviewed multiple finalists and come to the
       decision to offer the job to and approve the appointment of Ifo Pili to the position
       of City Manager.

       Previous City Manager, Wayne Pyle, retired in January 2024. During his last six
       months, City Council participated in the interactive recruitment process to
       identify qualified candidates and fill his vacancy.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Lang there were no further questions from members of the City
Council, and she called for a motion.
