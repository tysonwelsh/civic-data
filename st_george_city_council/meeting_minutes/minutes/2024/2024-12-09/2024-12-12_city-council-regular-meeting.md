APPROVAL OF AGREEMENT:
     Consider approval of the renewal of the City's Google Workspace
     Agreement.

       BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Google Workspace (previously known as G
       Suite) is the City's productivity suite and cloud storage that employees use to create
       and store documents and emails. The current agreement for the City's Google
       Workspace licenses expires in January, 2025. This renewal extends the agreement
       for 36 months at a fixed cost of $182,970 per year; with the total cost being
       $548,910 for the entire 36 months. Staff recommends approval.

       Link to presentation from City Manager John Willis: 00:01:43

AMEND INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT/RESOLUTION:
    Consider Approval of Resolution No. 2024-037R to amend the Interlocal
    Agreement with Utah Tech University, Ivins City, Hurricane City, Santa Clara
    City, Washington City, and Washington County to dissolve the Community
    Education Channel and distribute its assets.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City of St. George entered into an
      Interlocal Agreement with Utah Tech University (formerly known as Dixie College), Ivins
      City, Hurricane City, Santa Clara City, Washington City, and Washington County to
      create the Community Education Channel. It was amended in 1998, and 2007. Staff
      recommends approval.

      Link to introduction from City Manager John Willis and presentation from City Attorney
      Ryan Dooley, including discussion between the City Council, Mayor Randall, City
      Manager John Willis, Communications and Marketing Director David Cordero,
      Operations Director Marc Mortensen, and Mr. Dooley: 00:02:30

      Agenda Packet [Page 7]

      Link to motion: 00:05:51

            2024-037R to amend the Interlocal Agreement with Utah Tech University, Ivins
            City, Hurricane City, Santa Clara City, Washington City, and Washington County
            to dissolve the Community Education Channel and distribute its assets.
      SECOND:
