                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-71

      A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A PURCHASE ORDER BE ISSUED TO
    DAKTRONICS TO PURCHASE SIX (6) SCOREBOARDS FOR CR HAMILTON
                        SPORTS COMPLEX

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to replace all six scoreboards at the
same time instead of replacing them one at a time as the go out in order to keep them all the
same; and

       WHEREAS, by approving this agreement, Council will need to approve $34,644.00 as
an expense in a future budget amendment.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council authorizes purchase order be issued to Daktronics to purchase six
          (6) Scoreboards for CR Hamilton Sports Complex.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 5th day of
September 2023 by the following vote:
