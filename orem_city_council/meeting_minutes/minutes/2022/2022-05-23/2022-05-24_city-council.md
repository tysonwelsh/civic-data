       DISCUSSION – City Center Park Cell Tower

Mr. Earl began his presentation and stated the city has received a request from T-Mobile for
construction of a cell tower in the City Center Park. He then showed the Council the proposed
location for this tower.

Mayor Young asked if T-Mobile would be paying the city more for this new tower location than
their current one. Mr. Earl responded T-Mobile would be paying the same amount they currently
do.

Mayor Young inquired on why this tower was being added, and asked if it was being built for
5G, or just added cell coverage. Mr. Earl responded this tower would just be added for coverage,
and would have no 5G purpose.

Mr. Davidson asked how long this kind of use agreement is typically signed for. Mr. Earl
responded they are generally signed for a 25-year period.

Mr. Macdonald inquired about escalators in the agreement, wondering if payment to the city
would gradually be increased. Mr. Earl answered there is typically a 4 percent per year escalator.

Mrs. Millett asked how much space this new structure will take up. Mr. Bench responded that
because there is no building being constructed, the cell tower will take up a very minimal amount
of ground space.

Mr. Peterson expressed he wished to go on record that he was worried about the safety of the
towers. He expressed it could be similar to powerlines, where you can see grass and other plants
don’t grow as well under them.

Mr. Lambson indicated there are a few Orem residents the Council has heard from who have
expressed these same health and safety concerns about cell towers. He asked Mr. Earl if the city
could verify with T-Mobile that they are going to use the safest technologies and standards when
building this tower.

       UPDATE – Hillcrest Park

Mr. Gray introduced the item and expressed that the design team had received Council
suggestions and have incorporated those into this new design

Representatives from Blu Line Designs, the company which the city has contracted with to
design Hillcrest Park presented the Council with an updated design for Hillcrest Park, and went
through new updates. These updates included expanding walking paths and adjusting feature
placements.

Mrs. Lauret asked if this park will have family dressing/restrooms near the splash pad. The Blu
Line team responded they currently have planned for two large family-style rest/changing rooms.
Mr. Gray further expanded on this stating they have 14 gender-neutral floor-to-ceiling walled
stalls, two of which are large family rooms.

Mrs. Millet asked if there was an exterior entrance to these restrooms. Mr. Downs responded it
was like a half-bath in a house.

Mr. Peterson asked why they couldn’t just have separate men’s and women’s rest and changing
rooms. Mr. Davidson responded the city wanted to accommodate families while at the same time
having private stalls.

Mrs. Millett expressed she would prefer not to share restrooms with men. She stated how she
would like some restrooms that were just for women, and if a woman wanted to wait to use a
women’s only restroom, they could.

Mr. Spencer asked for separate men’s and women’s restrooms.

Mrs. Millett asked if there could be two men’s only, and two women’s only restrooms, then have
the rest be gender-neutral.

The Blu Line team continued to update the Council on the planned splash pad features and
technology. They then showed the Council a planned outdoor plaza, and stated the team wanted

gender-neutral, with one large room for men and women with 14 stalls. Seven on each side, and
a large family room on each end. They also stated there is a shared wash/sink area in the large

       MOTION / CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT – Approval of a T-Mobile stealth location of
       cell antennas and equipment located on top of Alpine Credit Union at 1510 North State
       Street in the C2 zone.

Mr. Bench introduced Matt Shucher, a representative from Rage Development. He stated Rage
Development, who is contracted with T-Mobile is applying to be able to build a stealth cell
antenna on top of the Alpine Credit Union at 1510 North State Street. He expressed to the
Council that T-Mobile/Rage Development already has the right to build a cell antenna on top of
the building, and they are coming before the Council today to ask for approval to build a “stealth
enclosure” around the antenna.

Mr. Peterson asked Mr. Shucher about the safety & possible health effects of cell towers. Mr.
Shucher responded he feels they are safe, and federal law prohibits the denial of building cell
towers due to health or safety concerns.

Mrs. Lauret asked why they want to enclose this antenna. Mr. Shucher responded they want to

CITY MANAGER INFORMATION ITEMS

Mr. Davidson updated the Council on the progress of the new City Hall. He stated the city has
decided to work with Method Studios and Layton Construction to design the new building before
sending it out for bid. He then stated they plan to start construction of the new building in March
of 2023. Mr. Davidson also updated the Council on the water tank being built at 400 South and
400 West, and detailed some constructability concerns.

Mr. Spencer asked for updates on fencing for the Farley property and a Wiggy Wash location.
Mr. Davidson responded they are still working through the fence pricing stage, and Wiggy Wash
is looking at installing quieter blowers.

Mr. Macdonald stated he assumes the city will need to do ground test and other surveys before
construction can start on the new City Hall building, and asked who would be managing this
process. Mr. Davidson responded that Lane Gray, the Capital Improvement Project Manager will
be leading this process, and informed the Council that an asbestos review of the current building
has already been initiated.
