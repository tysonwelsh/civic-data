                                RESOLUTION #2024– 05 – 04
                    A RESOLUTION DESIGNATING OFFICIAL NAME
                     OF MUNICIPALITY TO EMIGRATION CANYON

       WHEREAS, previously, Emigration Canyon was a metro township under Utah Code Ann.
§ 10-3c et seq; and

       WHEREAS, during the 2024 legislative session, the Utah Legislature passed H.B. 35,
which converted metro townships into cities and towns based on their populations effective May
1, 2024; and

       WHEREAS, now that Emigration Canyon is classified as a city under Utah Code
Ann. § 10-2-301; and

      WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon desires to designate its name as a municipality to
“Emigration Canyon” pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-1-118.

    NOW, THEREFORE, THE FOLLOWING IS RESOLVED BY THE EMIGRATION
CANYON CITY COUNCIL EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

       Section I: that its municipal name is Emigration Canyon; and

       Section II: that this Resolution be filed with the Utah Lieutenant Governor’s Office
with a Notice of Impending Name Change to receive a Certificate of Name Change; and

       Section III: that a certified copy of this Resolution approving the name change, the
Notice of Impending Name Change, and the Certificate of Name change be submitted to the
Salt Lake County Recorder.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the Emigration Canyon City Council, Utah, this 28th day
of May in the year 2024.
                                            EMIGRATION CANYON




                                            Joe Smolka, Mayor
