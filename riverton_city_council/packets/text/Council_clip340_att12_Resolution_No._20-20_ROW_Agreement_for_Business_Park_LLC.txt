                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-20

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
WITH RIVERTON BUSINESS PARK, LLC FOR RIGHT OF WAY DEDICATION TO
BUILD OLD LIBERTY WAY IN EXCHANGE FOR WATER UTILITY STUBS,
SLEEVES AND STREET ACCESS OPENINGS TO THEIR PROPERTY

       WHEREAS, The City desires to construction Old Liberty Way (the Project) from
Majestic Rise Parkway (13200 S) to 12600 S; and

        WHEREAS, During the design process, it was discovered that there exists a gap in the
right of way for the road that resulted from an error in survey that happened during land
transactions between Jordan School District and the property owners; and

   WHEREAS, The current owner of the property is Riverton Business Park, LLC; and

   WHEREAS, Riverton Business Park LLC desires to have the Project completed in order to
develop their property; and

    WHEREAS, Riverton Business Park LLC has agreed to dedicate the property which equals
1,822 sq. ft. in exchange for water service stubs, sleeves and access openings to their property
from the Project; and

   WHEREAS, The City of Riverton feels the exchange of services for right of way is fair and
equitable to both parties; and

     WHEREAS, The City has created an agreement outlining the items to be provided and
included in the construction of the Project; and



    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Riverton City Council accepts and
executes said agreement with Riverton Business Park, LLC which will dedicate 1,822 sq. ft. of
right of way to construct Old Liberty Way in exchange for water service stubs, sleeves and access
openings to their property from the Project.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 3rd day of
March, 2020 by the following vote:
