1.       Roll Call.

All members of the City Council were present.

2.       PUBLIC HEARING - Consideration and Vote on Resolution No. 2021-32, Adopting
         the Proposed Tax Rate for Salt Lake County for the Purpose of Levying Taxes within
         the City of Bluffdale for the Fiscal Year 2021-2022, Staff Presenters, Bruce Kartchner
         and Stephanie Thayer.

Mayor Timothy stated that Items 2 and 3 would be presented together but votes and adoption would
be separate.

Admini strative Services Director, Bruce Kartchner, indicated that the C ity received the needed
information from the State. Chief Accountant/Human Resources Manager, Stephanie Thayer,
reported that the tax rate for Salt Lake County from the Auditor's Ce11ified Tax Rate was set at
0.001519. It was expected to generate $1 ,657,017 in prope11y tax revenue. The same tax rate was
applied to Utah County which was expected to generate $837. The comment was made that $800
was not much revenue based on the effort.

Mayor Timothy opened the public hearing for agenda items two and three. There were no members
of the public online and no e-mails received. The public hearing was closed.

Jeff Gaston moved to approve Resolution No. 2021-32. Mark Hales seconded the motion.
Vote on motion: Traci Crockett-Aye, Dave Kallas-Aye, Mark Hales-Aye, Wendy Aston-Aye,
Jeff Gaston-Aye. The motion passed unanimously.

3.     PUBLIC HEARING - Consideration and Vote on Resolution No. 2021-33, Adopting
       the Proposed Tax Rate for Utah County for the Purpose of Levying Taxes within the
       City of Bluffdale for the Fiscal Year 2021-2022, Staff Presenters, Bruce Kartchner
       and Stephanie Thayer.

Jeff Gaston moved to approve Resolution No. 2021-33. Dave Kallas seconded the motion.
Vote on motion: Traci Crockett-Aye, Dave Kallas-Aye, Mark Hales-Aye, Wendy Aston-Aye,
Jeff Gaston-Aye. The motion passed unanimously.

4.     PUBLIC HEARING - Consideration and Vote on Resolution No. 2021-34, Adopting
       a Tentative Operating or Final Budget for the 2021-2022 Fiscal Year Budget, Staff
       Presenters, Bruce Kartchner and Stephanie Thayer.

Mr. Kartchner repo11ed that the budget was adjusted to reflect the new tax rate. Other adjustments
were made to the budget previously. Ms. Thayer repo11ed that there were two other items besides
the property tax revenue change consisting of the speed mitigation of $20,000, and consulting
services in Capital Projects.

Mayor Timothy opened the public hearing. There were no online public comments or e-mails.
Mayor Timothy closed the public hearing.

Jeff Gaston moved to approve Resolution No. 2021-34. Mark Hales seconded the motion.
Vote on motion: Jeff Gaston-Aye, Traci Crockett-Aye, Dave Kallas-Aye, Mark Hales-Aye,
Wendy Aston-Aye. The motion passed unanimously.
