E. PRESENTATION OF THE NATIONAL ELECTIONS ADMINISTRATION BEST PRACTICES AWARD FOR WEBER
   COUNTY ELECTIONS OFFICE
   Ricky Hatch, Weber County Clerk/Auditor, stated that he has found, through his involvement with
   election officials across the country, that a nation-wide challenge is the recruiting, training, maintaining,
   etc., of poll workers because the elections team swells immensely on election day, and that team has to
   be trained, function perfectly, and handle complex issues on that one day. He noted that Ryan Cowley,
   Weber County Elections Director, came up with an idea a couple of years ago that streamlined the
   communication efforts with poll workers using Google Voice. The creative use of this great tool, at no
   cost to the taxpayer, was recently featured in ELECTricity, a nationwide newsletter, and subsequently
   Weber County received telephone calls from various states on using this tool. Weber County was the
   only one to receive a best practice award in elections this year and it received a plaque, which Mr. Hatch
   presented to the elections team for their great work.

    1. RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE WEBER AREA DISPATCH 911 AND EMERGENCY SERVICES
       DISTRICT, UTAH TO CREATE A LOCAL BUILDING AUTHORITY; AUTHORIZING INCIDENTAL ACTION;
       AND RELATED MATTERS – RESOLUTION 25-2016

       David Wilson, Deputy County Attorney, noted that years ago the County Commission created this
       special service district and granted to an administrative control board all the powers to run the day-
       to-day operations. By statute the county retains the power to issue bonds and to increase taxes. He
       said that Ogden City has agreed to give the District the property interest in the building that it has
       been leasing as well as the property on which the new building will be constructed. The resolution
       authorizes the District to create the Building Authority and to issue bonds up to $6 million for a new
       2-story building and to remodel the current space that the District has been leasing.
       Tina Mathieu, District Executive Director, stated that the District has been leasing about 3,100 square
       feet from Ogden City for about 15 years (since the building was constructed). The City is about to
       pay off its bond on the building and will allow the District to own the portion it currently leases and
       will also give a piece of property south of the building where the District can add an approximately
       9,000 sq. ft. building. The second floor will be expanded, giving the dispatchers about 4,000 sq. ft.
       This area allows for expansion over the next 20-25 years. The District hired a consultant in 2008 and
       the study stated that at that time the District needed more space. Several offices have 3-4 employees
       and the dispatch floor has significant background and cross-over bleed from call takers onto radio
       channels because everyone is sitting so close together. Commissioner Ebert said that the study
       recommended up to 25,000 sq. ft., that the approach has been financially responsible, and partnering
       with Ogden has been a tremendous asset. Ms. Mathieu said that since the District’s creation Morgan
       County, which has a significant relationship with Weber County (through the Sheriff’s Office, Jail,
       Health Department, Strike Force, SWAT, gang unit, CSI, etc.), is renewing its contract. For a long
       time, Morgan County has been significantly underpaying for its contract; it has been paying
       $83,000/year. It will be charged at a similar tax rate as Weber County residents and effective 1/2017
       the charge will be $273,000 plus the 911 surcharge ($70,000-$75,000).
       Pleasant View City Mayor Toby Mileski and District chair noted that in addition to the increased
       Morgan revenues, the District will no longer be making the lease payment (about $90,000/yr.). He
       said that this plan gives the District space to expand for 20-30 years.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 25-2016 authorizing the Weber Area Dispatch 911
       and Emergency Services District to create a Local Building Authority; authorizing incidental action;
       and related matters; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
