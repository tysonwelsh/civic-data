Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chair Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner Britton.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearings and recommendation of Jamie Lamb’s request for R-1-22 zoning on the
Lamb Annexation, 5.601-acres located at approximately 1900 South Center Street.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Jamie Lamb, was present.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.


Luke Seegmiller clarified information on the traffic regulations that were brought up on the on
crossing. Luke will continue to collect data and continue to make the crossing safer by adding
signage.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Jamie Lamb’s request
        for R-1-22 zoning on the Lamb Annexation, 5.601-acres located at approximately
        1900 South Center Street, including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.2) Consideration of Kimley-Horn's request for approval of the Ken Garff Porsche
Parking Expansion Site Plan expanding a legal nonconforming use at 3425 North Digital
Drive in a Commercial zone.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Chris Vick, the applicant, was present.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Kimley-Horn's request for approval of
        the Ken Garff Porsche Parking Expansion Site Plan expanding a legal nonconforming
        use at 3425 North Digital Drive in a Commercial zone, including all DRC comments
        and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.3) Consideration of Gardner Company’s request for approval of the Cold Spring Storage

Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item

Howard Cook, with the Gardner Company, was present to represent the item and is aware of the

        approval of the Cold Spring Storage Phase 2 concept plan, 1.73 acres located at 4193

        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.6) Public hearing and consideration of Adam Sheridan’s request for approval of the
Floor & Décor Outlets of America concept plan, including an exception for the orientation
of the building to Mill Pond Drive, located at 405 South Mill Pond Drive.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item

Jonathan Eversworth was present to represent the applicant.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Adam Sheridan’s request for approval
        of the Floor & Décor Outlets of America concept plan, including an exception for
        the orientation of the building to Mill Pond Drive, located at 405 South Mill Pond
        Drive; not including the site plan, based on the information that has been received the
        wetlands will be filled to some degree, including the exception for parking on the south
        side of Mill Pond Drive. Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.7) Recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development Code
Amendment to Chapter 35, adding standards for water efficient landscape requirements.
(Tabled from the January 12th meeting)
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Staff and the Planning Commission discussed the item.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to recommended approval of Lehi City’s request
        for review of a Development Code Amendment to Chapter 35, adding standards for
        water efficient landscape requirements; including all DRC comments and findings
        stated in the reports. Noting the City’s ability to access more water and recommending
        funding for trees in parking strips throughout the city. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes. The vote passed 4 – 1.

2.8) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code amendment to Chapter 3 and a Municipal Code Amendment, removing
redundant language and changing the number of Planning Commissioners from seven to
five.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request
        for review of a Development Code amendment to Chapter 3 and a Municipal Code
        Amendment, removing redundant language and changing the number of Planning
        Commissioners from seven to five; including all DRC comments and findings stated in
        the report. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3. City Business

Work Session with City Council and Planning Commission. March 2, 2023 at 5:00pm.

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Everett moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Eyre seconded the motion. The motion
