Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order
Commission Chair Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting and noted that Commissioner
Jackson will be in attendance but is running late.

2) Discussion Item

2.1) Discussion of proposed revisions to the animal rights regulations of the Development
Code.
Brittney Harris presented information on different property zoning districts and property
requirements regarding allowed animal rights.

2.2) Discussion of parking minimums.
Kim Struthers presented a lot of analytical research and showed that over 60 % of Lehi was
parking area. He questioned if there was a more efficient way to utilize land. He stated parking
structures can be costly and require maintenance. The commission and staff discussed parking
and the contingency based on walkability. Kim stated they will look at regulations and bring new
information back in November.

3) City Business
Kim Struthers reviewed the meeting schedule for November and December.

4) Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Eyre moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Everett seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:05 p.m.
