23   1. Call to Order

25   Chair Staggs called the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Meeting to order and
26   conducted a Roll Call. Board Members Buroker, McCay, McDougal, Stewart, and Wells were
27   present.
28
29   2. Public Hearing(s)
30
31      a. RDA Resolution No. 20-04 - Amending the Riverton Redevelopment Agency
32         Budget for Fiscal Year 2020-2021
33
34   Kevin Hicks, Administrative Services Director, explained that Budgets were estimates, and that
35   it was necessary periodically and at fiscal year-end, based on year-to-date budget to actual
36   comparisons, to open and amend the fiscal budget as needed.
37
38   Mayor Staggs opened a Public Hearing and called for public comment; there being none, he
39   declared the Public Hearing closed.
40
41   Board Member Stewart MOVED to approve RDA Resolution No. 20-04 - Amending a
42   Riverton Redevelopment Agency Final Amended Budget for Fiscal Year 2020-2021. Board
43   Member Buroker SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the motion;
44   there being none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as follows: Board Members
45   Buroker-Yes, McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The motion passed
46   unanimously.
