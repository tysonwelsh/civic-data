The Town of Alta Capital Projects Account

Capital projects involve the purchase, maintenance, or improvement of Town of Alta
assets including equipment, facilities, and infrastructure. The Town maintains a capital
projects account with the Utah Public Treasurer’s Investment Fund where it holds a
balance of funds dedicated to such projects.

The balance available in the Town of Alta Capital Projects Account as of the December
31st, 2020 account statement is $160,584.29, and the attached plan is based on this
amount. The Amended Fiscal Year 2021 Budget the council will consider in the January
13th, 2020 meeting includes a transfer of $10,120.00 to the capital projects account
from the general fund, which would bring the balance of the capital projects account to
$170,704.29.

The Plan

The Alta Town Council must dedicate the balance of the capital projects account to
specific projects by approving a capital projects plan. The plan can be amended as the
need for additional projects arises, or as financial circumstances dictate the amount of
funds available for capital projects. The council has not yet approved a capital projects
plan in Fiscal Year 2021. This is partially due to financial uncertainty related to the
COVID-19 Pandemic..

The attached plan lists project descriptions, estimated costs for each project, and a
projected timeline for completion. The cost estimates are based on a combination of
research into likely project costs and formal estimates from vendors and contractors.
The project completion timeline for each project is subject to change due to financial
circumstances and availability of staff time to execute each project.

The plan also includes a list of future capital projects, and an updated copy of the
approved Fiscal Year 2020 Capital Projects Plan. Each of the future projects is important
for the maintenance of Town of Alta assets but were determined by the capital projects
committee to be lower priority. The updated FY 2020 Plan describes the status of each
of the projects included in last year’s plan.

Request for Council Action
The Town of Alta Capital Projects Committee requests that the Town Council pass a
motion to approve the FY 2021 Proposed Capital Projects Plan during it’s January 13th,
2020 meeting.
