2.
Project #23-015: Administrative Process: Address: ~1662 S West Hoytsville Rd., Wanship, Summit County, UT; Parcels NS-125-A, S-415, and NS-124; Public hearing and action regarding Rees's Gravel Pit Powerline Extension - Conditional Use Permit Amendment; Applicant: Robert Wadman
Amir Caus, Senior Planner
(6:03 P.M.)
Commissioner Wilde recused himself from this hearing and vote.
Amir Caus,
Senior Planner
, provided information for a Conditional Use Permit Amendment for a Powerline Extension located at Rees's Gravel Pit, Parcels NS-125-A, S-415 and NS-124.
Commissioners asked questions and provided comments. Mr. Caus responded.
Chair Sonntag opened the meeting for public input.
(6:07 P.M.)
Larry Dean Vernon commented. (6:08 P.M.)
Attachment:
L. Vernon Public Input
Chair Sonntag asked questions. Mr. L. Vernon responded.
Bradley Ray Vernon commented. (6:31 P.M.)
Attachment:
B. Vernon Public Input
Chair Sonntag closed the meeting for public input.
(6:36 P.M.)
Commissioners asked questions. Helen Strachan,
Civil Deputy Attorney
, and Mr. Caus responded.
Bill Wilde spoke to the Commission on behalf of the applicant.
Commissioners responded to Mr. Wilde with questions and comments. Mr. Wilde and Ms. Strachan responded.
Rich Sonntag made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit Amendment for Rees's Gravel Pit Powerline Extension according to the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and Conditions of Approval found in the Staff Report with the additional condition that the powerline be moved to the middle of the easement or buried within the easement. Marion Wheaton seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Clinton Benson, Don Sargent. Abstain: Bill Wilde.
