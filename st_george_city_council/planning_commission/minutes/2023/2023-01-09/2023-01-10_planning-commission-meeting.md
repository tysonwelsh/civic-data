   2. PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT (PD-A) (Public Hearing) Legislative

      A. Consider a request to amend the PD-C (Planned Development Commercial) zone for the purpose of
         redesigning the layout and elevations of an approved but not yet developed storage rental unit
         facility which is generally located at approximately 3425 S. River Road. Please see map on back.
         The applicant is Devin Sullivan – Pioneer Boys LLC, and the representative is Adam Allen. The
         project name will be known as STG Storage Facility. Case No. 2022-ZCA-052. (Staff – Carol
         Davidson)

         Carol Davidson presented the following:

         Carol Davidson – Back in 2018 this site had an approval under a temporary ordinance. They were
         required to put in the taller building to shield the other portion. They are proposing to remove that
         building. They have changed the sizes, shapes of the units. They will have a 1 story retail office
         building instead of the two story that was approved in 2018. They are proposing a 60 ft ROW, that
         road will continue on at some point. This is on a hill, there is quite a difference between this and the
         Fossil Hills Development. They are going to do the same grading that was approved in 2018, they
         will take off the top of that hill, but the elevation will end up above the townhomes. They will be
         required to do a buffer on the north and east side. The west side has a 90 ft ROW, so no buffer is
         required there. It is required that they put the street trees 5 feet on center, there will be enhanced
         vegetation. A tree or a shrub. The applicant is asking to use the back of their building as the 6’4”
         privacy wall where feasible. This was asked for by Pioneer Storage, they were granted that. The
         applicant here is asking the same.

         Commissioner Chapman – Will that meet the setbacks?

         Carol Davidson – Yes, it would. There will be a 10 ft buffer. With a ROW we typically ask for
         landscaping on the other side of the fence, not the fence on the property line.

         Commissioner Fisher – Didn’t we want them to landscape the slope as well so that it would look
         better for the Fossil Hills subdivision?

         Carol Davidson – It was discussed, our parks division went out and decided that the slope would not
         be able to handle the landscape. They decided it should only have the 10 ft of landscape.

   3. HILLSIDE PERMIT (HS) Administrative

      Consider a request for a hillside development permit to determine the ridgeline on lot 29 of South Rim at
      Formaster Ridge for the construction of an infinity pool. The property is currently zoned R-1-10 (Single
      Family Residential, minimum lot size 10,000sf). The site is located at 1682 E. Howard Lane. The
      applicant is Ryan Andrews and Tonya Shepard. Case No. 2022-HS-019. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

      Wes Jenkins – I think this is the last lot that we will look at for this. The engineer at the time called the
      property lines the ridgeline. They made the ridgeline setback 30 ft from the property line, not the actual
      ridgeline. You can see that the ridgeline is not at the property line. We took it to hillside. Their
      recommendation was to adjust the property line to match the ridgeline and that they match the materials
      to the surrounding landscape. Normally we walk the entire ridgeline, but we didn’t with this one.

      Commissioner Anderson – I don’t see any issues with it at all, as long as they match the surrounding
      hillside.

      Commissioner Rogers – Did the Hillside recommend that they replace that rock?

      Wes Jenkins – No, just that they match the coloring of the surrounding rock.

      Chair Kemp – To Austin’s point, the ridgeline isn’t straight.

      MOTION: Commissioner Andrus made a motion to recommend approval of Item 3 a Hillside
      development permit to adjust the ridgeline on lot 29 with the condition that Hillside recommended that
      any of the construction or disturbed soil be mitigated with natural colors to blend in with the hillside.
