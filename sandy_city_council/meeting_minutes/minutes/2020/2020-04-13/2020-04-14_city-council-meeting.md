1.         20-108                 Public Works Department recommending the Council receive an
                                  informational presentation on traffic signals
                   Attachments:   Signal Timing Info Sheet
                                  Presentation
                                  Sandy City Signal Coordination Report

                                  City Transportation Engineer Britney Ward gave a presentation on the Sandy City Signal
                                  Synchronization Project. Council questions and comments followed which were
                                  addressed by Ms. Ward and Blake Unguren from Avenue Consultants.

2.         20-111                 City Council Office requesting update on current parks usage and
                                  conditions.
                                  City Parks and Recreation Director Scott Earl provided an update on the status of the
                                  Department including personnel, parks and trails usage, facilities status, and enterprise
                                  funds impacts due to closures. Council questions and comments followed.

3.         20-110                 Administration briefing on City staffing during COVID-19 restrictions.

                   Attachments:   City-wide Employee Status COVID-19 Update

                                  Chief Administrative Officer Matt Huish discussed the status of employees and
                                  telecommuting during the current stay-at-home order. Additional data will be available
                                  later this week. Council questions and comments followed. Deputy CAO Kim Bell also
                                  provided an update on emergency management coordination including the precautionary
                                  measures of city first responders.

4.         20-112                 Economic Development Director providing an overview of the
                                  Redevelopment Agency.
                   Attachments:   Economic Development RDA Presentation CC 4.14.20

                                  Economic Development Director Nick Duerksen provided an overview of economic
                                  development methods in Sandy City including the goals and mechanics of the
                                  Redevelopment Agency and its project areas.

6:00 Time Certain Items

Citizen Comment

                                  Council Senior Analyst Dustin Fratto provided instruction on how to utilize the public
                                  comment portion of the meeting.

                                  Steve Van Maren asked about the status of the budget. Mayor Bradburn noted that the
                                  Tentative Budget would be delivered on Tuesday, May 5.

Adjournment
                                  The meeting unanimously in adjourned at 7:48 PM.
