                                           Nephi City
                                     Work Session Minutes
                                         July 13, 2021
                                            7:00 p.m.
                                  Nephi City Council Chambers

   1. The council discussed the Education Assistance Personnel Policy and offered staff
      direction for revising the policy. The majority of the councilors present agreed that the
      current request for education assistance should come from the requestor’s department
      budget.

   2.    City Administrator Seth Atkinson presented two draft ordinances revising the part of the
        city code that addresses beer licensing at special events. The first ordinance would allow
        for beer sales with conditions, and the second would prohibit beer sales at events. The
        council agreed to introduce both ordinances at the next regular council meeting and to
        seek public input on the ordinances.

   3. The council reviewed a development agreement involving the Canyon Mines property.

   4. The council discussed the idea of contracting out services for a masterplan for
      some city owned property on the south end of the city. Mr. Atkinson explained
      that it would be helpful to create a plan which includes roads, any city facilities,
      infrastructure and how the property could all connect with neighboring private
      development. The council agreed to the idea of creating a masterplan for the
      area.

   5. Mr. Atkinson reported that he would be filling in at Planning Commission meeting for
      Planning Administrator Glenn Greenhalgh who had recently passed away.

   6. City Councilors gave various updates.

   7. Work Session adjourned at 8:45 p.m.

   8. Mayor Nielson called to order a regular meeting and Councilor Seely moved to convene
      into executive session to discuss the character and competence of an individual.
      Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.




Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielsen, Councilor Larry Ostler, Councilor Justin Seely, Councilor
Kent Jones (Absent), Councilor Skip Worwood, and Councilor Nathan Memmott, City Attorney
Kasey Wright, City Administrator Seth Atkinson, City Recorder Lisa Brough, Jeremy Lynn,
Peyton Lynn, and Craig Oswald.
