                    WATER REUSE SERVICES AGREEMENT:
                SOUTH VALLEY COALITION REUSE PROJECT


       This Agreement is entered into this ____ day of ____________, 2021, by and between

U.C.A. Section 16-6a-101, et seq. (“DIC”) which is operating under the regulatory authority of

the Utah Public Service Commission and Riverton City, a Utah municipality organized and

existing pursuant to U.C.A. Section 10-3-101, et seq. (“Riverton”) for the joint and cooperative

efforts to study, design, construct, fund and operate a wastewater reclamation and reuse facility in

the south end of Salt Lake Valley (DIC and Riverton are the “Parties” to this Agreement).

                                           RECITALS

       WHEREAS, DIC is authorized to provide culinary and secondary water service within and

around its service area in the south end of Salt Lake Valley; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton is authorized to provide culinary and secondary water service within

and around its service area in the south end of Salt Lake Valley; and

       WHEREAS, water is a precious and limited resource in Utah and should be put to its most

beneficial and economical use; and

       WHEREAS, the Wastewater Reuse Act, U.C.A. Section 73-3c-1 et seq., as amended

(“Reuse Act”), provides for the beneficial reuse of water reclaimed from sewage effluent; and

       WHERAS, the Reuse Act authorizes public agencies, including Riverton, to use or contract

for the use of reuse water as defined by the Reuse Act; and

       WHEREAS, the South Valley Sewer District (“SVSD”) has constructed a regional sewage

treatment facility, known as the Jordan Basin Water Reclamation Facility (“JBWRF”), within

Riverton’s municipal boundaries; and
       WHEREAS, DIC, Riverton, Bluffdale City, Draper City, Herriman City, South Jordan

City, certain unincorporated areas of Salt Lake County, Central Utah Water Conservancy District,

and the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District (collectively “Coalition Members”) previously

determined to pursue a joint reuse project, which is designated as the “South Valley Coalition

Reuse Project” or “SVCRP,” although after some work was done to drill a test well for the

SVCRP, Riverton has since decided that it no longer wants to be part of the SVCRP;” and

       WHEREAS, DIC has incurred actual expenses in advancing the SVCRP with the

expectation of Riverton (and the other participants in the SVCRP) providing pro-rata

reimbursement for appropriate expenses; and

       WHEREAS, the governing boards of DIC and Riverton have determined that it is in their

respective best interests to promote water conservation and the beneficial reuse of reclaimed water

within their respective services areas and to cooperate in the SVCRP and are now desirous of

formalizing an agreement to provide for the reimbursement of construction costs for a test well on

the JBWRF; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton is agreeing to pay its pro rata portion of the costs incurred for the

construction of the test well and the Parties agree that DIC will not invoice Riverton for any costs

in addition to the costs incurred for the construction of the test well; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton agrees that if it decides to rejoin the SVCRP in the future, that it will

reimburse DIC and the other Coalition Members for the pro rata costs that would have been paid

by Riverton if Riverton would have remained a participant in further development of any water

reuse project(s) which make use of the test well.

       NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants contained

herein, the Parties agree as follows:

     1.    RIVERTON’S PRO RATA SHARE. The Parties agree that Riverton’s pro rata share

           for the cost to install a reuse test well at the JBWRF is described on “Exhibit A,”

           incorporated herein by reference, which amounts to $33,268.06 (the “Reimbursement

           Amount”) as shown on the invoices attached as “Exhibit A.” This Reimbursement

           Amount is currently due and owing by Riverton to DIC within 30 days from the date

           this Agreement is executed by the parties (the “Due Date”). Failure to pay the

           Reimbursement Amount by the Due Date will result in a $150.00 late fee and the

           Reimbursement Amount and late fee will accrue interest at 25% per annum.

           a. The Parties agree that the Reimbursement Amount is calculated properly according

               to Riverton’s pro rata share of 20.70% of the total costs to install the reuse test well

               as provided in Section 1.

           b. The Parties agree that the Reimbursement Amount is for proper and qualifying

               installation relating to the reuse test well.

           c. The Parties further agree that Riverton will not have any further participation in the

               SVCRP and will accordingly not be billed for any future expenses beyond those

               expenses identified in Section 1.

           d. If Riverton decides to rejoin the SVCRP at a later date, Riverton must reimburse

               DIC and each of the Coalition Members in an amount equal to the amount Riverton

               would have paid for its pro rata share of the expenses further development of any

               water reuse project project(s) which make use of the test well.
