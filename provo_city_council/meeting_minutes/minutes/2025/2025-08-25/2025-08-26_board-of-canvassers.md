Action Agenda

1.          A resolution of the Board of Canvassers accepting the election returns and declaring and
            certifying the results of the vote for the Primary Election held on August 12, 2025 (25-085)
            (15:40)

Heidi Allman, Provo City Recorder, presented the Primary Election canvass report and resolution. The
report indicated the following election results and statistics:

On August 12, 2025, Provo City held a Primary Election for the offices of Mayor, Council Member District
2, Council Member District 5, and Council Member City-Wide I. Provo City contracted with Utah County
to conduct a vote-by-mail election.

Provo City had a total of 43,871 active registered voters. Of this number, 23,417 voters are classified as
private registrations, while 14,462 are withheld pursuant to state voter registration confidentiality
provisions. During the election, a total of 11,821 ballots were counted, resulting in a voter turnout of
26.9%.

There were 278 ballots that were not accepted. Of the ballots that were not accepted, 12 ballots were
returned unsigned, 5 signatures did not match the voter’s record and could not be verified, 256 ballots
were returned after the deadline, and 5 ballots were rejected for other non-curable reasons.

Board Member Garrett confirmed that each envelope may contain only one ballot, as the envelope must
bear a single voter’s signature.

Ms. Allman stated that the county makes every effort to contact voters if there is an issue with their
ballot.

Board Member Garrett expressed regret that 256 ballots were received after the deadline and therefore
could not be counted. He noted his hope that residents will be more aware of the deadline and the new
legislation for the General Election.

Board Member Whipple asked if the rejected ballots that were received after the deadline are informed
that their ballots were not counted due to being received late.

Ms. Allman said she did not know for certain but would follow up with the county to confirm.

With no other comments or questions, Chair Garrett called for a vote.

      Vote:              The resolution was approved 6:0 with Mayor Kaufusi, Board Members Bogdin,
