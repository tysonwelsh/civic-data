Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: October 14, 2021
Re: Summit Commerce Center Site Plan Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan Amendment for an approximately 26.937 acre site located on the east side of Pony Express Rd. and south of Golden Harvest Rd., at approximately 13004 S. Pony Express Rd. The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan Amendment be approved to allow for the additional development of a commercial building on the site. The site was previously approved for the construction of an industrial building which is currently under construction on the site.
Some aspects of the plans proposed by the applicant do not comply with Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) requirements. Planning staff is recommending that if the Planning Commission approve the application that specific conditions be included requiring that revised plans comply with ordinance requirements and the specific conditions as listed in the staff report, and that final approval will only be issued by the Zoning Administrator upon completion of the specific conditions required by the Planning Commission and as listed in the staff report.  The findings are as follows: Deviation Request from DCMC Subsection 9-22-040(C)(1) – Horizontal and Vertical Façade Articulation.
For approval:
1.  The project contains amenities such as pedestrian and bike connectivity in excess of what is required in the development code and site plan review.
2.  The variations are consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.
3.  The amount of primary materials used for a facade is at least ten percent (10%) greater than the minimum required amount.
For denial:
1.  The project does not contain amenities such as pedestrian and bike connectivity in excess of what is required in the development code and site plan review.
2.  The variations are not consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.

Site Plan
For approval:
1.  The applicant acknowledges it has not met the specific conditions of approval.
2.  The application will meet the requirements and provisions of the Draper City Municipal Code, including Section 9-5-090(E), once the specific conditions of approval have been met.
3.  The Planning Commission has agreed to approve the application with conditions (as outlined above) subject to the applicant complying with the specific conditions of approval.
4.  The final effective date of the Planning Commission’s Site Plan Amendment approval will be when the Zoning Administrator confirms the applicant has complied with the specific conditions of approval.
5.  The applicant will comply with the general conditions of approval throughout the construction of the project.
6.  The proposed use is consistent with uses permitted on the site;
7.  Existing uses were permitted when the site plan was approved, or have received a conditional use permit.
8.  The site will accommodate any change in the number of people on the site or any change in impact on surrounding infrastructure.
For denial:
1.  The proposed site plan does not requirements and provisions of the Draper City Municipal Code, including Section 9-5-090(E).
