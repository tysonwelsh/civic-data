CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1204-O, ANORDINANCE REZONING A
CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY
1700 EAST ON THE SOUTH SIDE OF SR 144 NEAR NEW HARMONY FROM THE A-

20 ZONE (AGRICULTURAL 20 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE), TO THE RA-5 ZONE
(RESIDENTIAL AGRICULTURAL 5 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) HEREINAFTER

FULLY DESCRIBED IN THIS ORDINANCE

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the applicant is proposing a zone
change and subsequent subdivision on two parcels located near New Harmony. Initially, the
applicants requested RA-1 zoning, but the most recent proposal from the applicant contemplates
RA 2.5. The Planning Commission recommended not approving the RA-1 zone requested by the
applicant; but instead approve the Planning Commissioner’s recommendation to rezone the parcels

to RA-S.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2022-1204-O, An
Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County Located at
Approximately 1700 East on the South Side of SR 144 Near New Harmony
from the A-20 Zone (Agricultural 20 Acre Minimum Lot Size), to the RA-5
Zone (Residential Agricultural 5 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Hereinafter fully
Described in this Ordinance. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist

voting aye.

CONSIDERATION __OF_ _RESOLUTION NO. ___R-2022-2896, A | RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING EXPENDITURES OF FUNDS COLLECTED AS WASHINGTON

COUNTY’S SHARE OF THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS SALES TAX

Recreation, Arts, and Parks (RAP) Tax Board Chair Dimon McFerson explained that the Board
recently accepted and reviewed thirty-nine (39) applications for Washington County’s portion of
RAP Tax funds set aside specifically for cultural arts. The board is recommending allocation of
funding to thirty-four (34) of the applicants that met the criteria for a total amount of $610,000.
Mr. McFerson reminded the Commissioners that the tax will go before the public on a ballot for
consideration at the end of 2024.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2897, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A LAND TRADE AGREEMENT IN CONFLUENCE PARK

Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan explained that this agreement is in
support of a potential land trade agreement with JB Holding LLC who is an owner of real property
adjacent to Confluence Park. The property being traded would provide public legal access to the
Easement property and continue to provide protection and enhance the natural wildlife habitat,
scenic, cultural, historical, and open space values of Confluence Park.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2897, A
Resolution Approving a Land Trade Agreement in Confluence Park. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2898, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE RENAMING OF A PORTION OF ROAD FROM HOLMSTEAD RANCH ROAD TO
TONY HSIEH WAY

Mr. Messel explained that the applicant has requested to rename a portion of road in Washington
County from Holmstead Ranch Road to Tony Hsieh Way. The road provides access to private
property formerly known as Holmstead Ranch.

Commissioner Snow clarified that the road was paid for and maintained by the private property
owner and all the costs of the signage change will be borne by the applicant.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2898, A
Resolution Approving the Renaming of a Portion of Road from Holmstead
Ranch Road to Tony Hsieh Way. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and
Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2902, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_AWARD_OF BID AND EXECUTION OF A CONTRACT WITH SUNROC
CORPORATION FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A PASSING LANE ON A PORTION
OF STATE ROAD 18

Mr. Edwards explained that the County sought bids from qualified contractors to construct a
passing lane on a portion of State Road 18 in preparation for the 2022 Ironman event as well as
for general safety concerns in the area. The lowest responsible bidder was Sunroc Corporation for
$2,022,210.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2902, A
Resolution Approving an Award of Bid and Execution of a Contract with
Sunroc Corporation for the Construction of a Passing Lane on a Portion of
State Road 18. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2903, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A MINOR BOUNDARY ADJUSTMENT IN THE RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

Mr. Rognan explained that the State of Utah School & Institutional Trust Lands Administration
(SITLA) has proposed a Reserve boundary adjustment neat the existing Grapevine trailhead area
to install a needed power sub-station and developed trailhead. The boundary adjustment would
remove approximately five (5) acres from the Reserve and add six (6) acres.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2903, A
Resolution Approving a Minor Boundary Adjustment in the Red Cliffs Desert
Reserve. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.
