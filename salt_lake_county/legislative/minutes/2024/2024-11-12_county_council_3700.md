Veterans Day Proclamation
Council Member Stringham read the proclamation.  
Salt Lake County
A Proclamation of the Salt Lake County Council Commemorating Veterans 
Day and Honoring Veterans of the United States Armed Forces
WHEREAS, Veterans Day, formerly known as Armistice Day, has been 
commemorated in the United States since 1919, and has been formally 
established as a federal holiday since 1954; and
WHEREAS, Veterans Day is also an official holiday of the State of Utah and 
Salt Lake County; and
WHEREAS, Veterans Day originally commemorated the end of major 
hostilities at the conclusion of World War I, signaling a cease fire on the 
eleventh hour of the eleventh day of the eleventh month; and
WHEREAS, as a matter of law, Veterans Day is observed on November 
11th of each year, including by Salt Lake County government; and
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County, its municipalities, and its residents 
commemorated Veterans Day throughout the past preceding weekend and on

yesterday, November 11, 2024, with solemn events and remembrances 
intended to honor our county ’s veterans, living and dead, as well as our 
active duty service members; and
WHEREAS, according to the United States Department of Veterans Affairs, 
approximately 42,000 Salt Lake County residents are veterans or active 
service members of the United States Armed Forces; and
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County ’s veterans are celebrated for their courage, 
resilience, and valor in defending our great nation, its Constitution and 
republican form of government, and the safety and security of the American 
people; and
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County ’s veterans are further celebrated for their 
contributions to civilian life, which enriches and improves our governments, 
industries, universities and nonprofits, and churches and civic organizations 
through their continued service and proud example; and
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County honors the sacrifices of our veterans, and 
recognizes our disabled veterans, including those living with physical and 
mental health conditions attributable to their service; and
WHEREAS, President Dwight D. Eisenhower noted when signing the bill 
establishing Veterans Day as a federal holiday, “On that day let us solemnly 
remember the sacrifices of all those who fought so valiantly, on the seas, in 
the air, and on foreign shores, to preserve our heritage of freedom, and let us 
reconsecrate ourselves to the task of promoting an enduring peace so that 
their efforts shall not have been in vain”;
NOW, THEREFORE, the undersigned do hereby resolve that the records of 
the County Council show that the County Mayor and County Council do 
hereby commemorate yesterday, November 11, 2024, as Veterans Day in 
Salt Lake County, and do solemnly pledge our unyielding gratitude to the 
brave women and men of the United States Armed Forces who have 
supported and defended the Constitution of the United States against all 
enemies, foreign and domestic, and that have borne true faith and allegiance 
to the same.
Proclamation
