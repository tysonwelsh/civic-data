      B. Consideration of Resolution R2025-05, Designating a Survey Area for Potential
         Community Reinvestment Project Area Creation.

Dan Torres gave a brief background of the process for designating a new survey area and
what is involved in the creation process, as outlined in the supporting documents published
prior to tonight’s meeting.

Board Member Hull moved to approve Resolution R2025-05, Designating a Survey Area
for Potential Community Reinvestment Project Area Creation. Board Member Prokopis
seconded the motion; vote was 4-0, unanimous in favor, with Board Chair Barney
absent from the vote.

      C. Lens of Magna Main

David Brickey asked the council to confirm their support of giving $2,875.00 from the CRA
Budget to compensate individuals awarded specific prizes in the Lens of Magna Main contest
described in detail during the previous CRA Meeting. It was suggested in a previous meeting
that the council move forward with funding part of the contest this year, and re-address the
support before the next year’s contest.

Board Member Hull moved to approve the funds described above for sponsoring the
Lens of Magna Main Contest for one year. Board Member Prokopis seconded the
motion; vote was 4-0, unanimous in favor, with Board Chair Barney absent from the
vote.

      D. Discussion Regarding Recent Updates

David Brickey noted that taxing entities are beginning to inquire regarding their payments with
the upcoming tax season. He will work with Daniel Torres to figure out those details pursuant
to the agreements entered into in the past and get that information to the involved entities.
