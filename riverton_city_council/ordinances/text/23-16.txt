    6. 05. 425 Feeding Wild Birds / Fowl

    It shall be unlawful for any person to deposit, place, or distribute feed of any kind
    intended for or attracting to wild birds or fowl on the ground or other surface, or in
   feeders or other receptacles or structures placed on the ground or other surface,
   except as provided herein. Feed intended for or attracting to wild birds or fowl may
    be placed in a container located on private property a minimum of ten ( 10) feet from
    any property line. If placed in the front yard area containers must be located a
    minimum of five ( 5) feet off the ground, and such feeders may not exceed 300 cubic
    inches in capacity. This language shall not be construed to prohibit feeding of legally
    maintained chickens, ducks, or other fowl where allowed by zoning, nor to prohibit
    the occasional recreational feeding of ducks or birds along waterways.
