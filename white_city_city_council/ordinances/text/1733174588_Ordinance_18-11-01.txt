ORDINANCE NO.: 18-11-01 DATE: 11-01-2018

AN ORDINANCE OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL CREATING
CHAPTER 2.85: "ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES," AUTHORIZING AND REGULATIING
THE USE OF ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES FOR THE EFFECTIVE AND EFFICIENT
PROCESSING OF CERTAIN DOCUMENTS FOR THE METRO TOWNSHIP

The White City Metro Township Council does hereby ORDAIN as follows:
RECITALS

1. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq.

2. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of

White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

3. Utah Code § 46-4-201 allows for the use of electronic signatures on legally binding
documents and contracts, unless otherwise disallowed.

4. The Utah State Legislature uses electronic signatures for the effective and efficient
processing of legislation and other legal work of the legislature.

5. Municipal Code Online is a Corporation that hosts the White City Code of Ordinances, and
among the features of its software can allow for the secure use of electronic signatures in the
approval of Ordinances pertaining to changes, additions, and/or repeals of White City’s
Code.

6. Certain legal and business documents processed by the administrative agents of White City
require the efficiency and effectiveness provided by using electronic signatures.

7. It would be a benefit to White City and its residents to use this regulate and use feature.
RESOLUTION
BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council does hereby adopt .
the following ordinance, to be added to the White City Metro Township Code of Ordinances:
CHAPTER 2.85 — ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES
2.85.010 — Purpose
|

The ordinance codified in this chapter regulates the use of electronic signatures by elected
officials and other parties in approving legislation, contracts, and/or other legally-binding
documents of White City.
2.85.020 — Uses of Electronic Signatures Allowed
In any document accepted by White City in which a signature is required or used, White City
may authorize the use of an electronic signature so long as it complies with the requirements of
the most currently adopted version of Utah Code § 46-4: Uniform Electronic Transactions Act.
2.85.030 — Rules Regarding Force and Effect of Electronic Signatures
The use of an electronic signature shall have the same force and effect as the use of a “wet” or
manual signature if:

A. The signature is capable of verification;

B. The signature is under the sole control of the person using it; and

C. The signature is linked to the data in such a manner that it is readily ascertainable if the

data is changed after the signature is applied.

2.85.040 — Determination of Approved Technologies, Vendors, and Uses of Electronic
Signatures

Upon the recommendations of Staff, the Council shall determine and approve acceptable
technologies and vendors under this section consistent with industry best practices to ensure the
security and integrity of the data and the signature. The Administrator shall further determine
and recommend to the Council the documents for which White City will accept electronic
signatures.
2.85.050 — Enactment Date
This Ordinance shall be effective on the 31* day after the date of its adoption by the Council.
