1. Preliminary Condominium Plat Approval, Wonder Block Condo Plat to garage from building
   spaces at approximately 2557-2565 Grant Avenue

  Mr. Simpson expressed appreciation to the Commission for allowing a special meeting for
  this agenda item as it is time-sensitive.

  Mr. Simpson explained while the Commission has previously reviewed both the project plan
  and the subdivision for the Wonder Block project this is a request to create condominium
  space to allow separate ownership of the parking garage which will be owned by Ogden City
  and provide public parking for the downtown area. The mixed-use portion will then be
  owned by the developer of the project. He reviewed the previously approved subdivision
  and outlined where the three phases would exist, indicating Phase 1 is the parking garage
  facing Grant Avenue which will be in the interior of the block with the mixed-use buildings
  next to the street. He stated some details have yet to be completed to allow the subdivision
  plat to be recorded such identifying easements and access for the State as property
  currently owned by the State for the use of the courts building is being conveyed to Ogden
  City in exchange for parking space provided within the parking structure. He indicated the
  subdivision plat is required to be recorded before the condominium plat can proceed. He
  stated the site plan approval process has been completed, and these details can easily be
  worked out. He reviewed the condominium process, stating as there are no existing
  tenants, no notification was required. The project is in compliance with the Coondominium

Act and City requirements for creating a condominium. He stated this is the only process
whereby a separate ownership of the parking garage can be provided. Staff
recommendation is for approval subject to the Wonderblock Subdivision plat being recorded
prior to the condominium plat, and all staff and private utility comments be satisfied,
including:
1. Ownership transactions be completed for the State-owned parcels;
2. Vacating easements where structures are to be built;
3. Correcting plat language and satisfying outstanding deeds on title; and
4. Obtaining and satisfying legal and engineering staff comments.

Commissioner Safsten asked about the area to be owned by Ogden City. Mr. Simpson
responded the City would own the air space within the parking structure as well as
easements allowing access to the structure. Chair Sandau asked if there are financial
benefits to the City by obtaining ownership of the parking structure and whether there
would be a fee for parking. Mr. Simpson indicated the intent of separate ownership is to
provide parking for both tenants within the development, and public parking for visitors to
the downtown. He stated the Make Ogden Plan had recommended centralized parking
structures be provided for public parking rather than each use providing its own parking or
create a sea of separate surface parking within the downtown. He indicated while he is
unaware of the financial impact of the parking structure, it is beyond the purview of the
Commission as the item being discussed is only the creation of the proposed condominium
plat. It is felt this project could become a catalyst for other development in the downtown
area in accordance with the Make Ogden Plan. Commissioner Graf noted the
recommendation of Staff refers to Attachment 9 and asked what it includes. Mr. Simpson
stated there were some symbols and typos on the plat that need to be corrected and many
of these items have since been corrected. He stated his intent is to assure all professional
staff review comments are satisfied so both the subdivision plat and the condominium plat
can be recorded. He stated these details are minor, but do need to be corrected.

Mr. Al Jones, 2500 Tyler Avenue, asked who is responsible for the cost and maintenance of
the parking structure.

Ms. Amelia Jones, 241 25th Street, stated the State-owned parking lot has become an
eyesore where trash has accumulated. She asked if there is a plan for its maintenance
during the time of construction.

Mr. Brandon Cooper, CED Director, stated the parking lot is now owned by the State who
should be maintaining it. Once the subdivision is recorded, it will become part of the
project, and as the development occurs, it may be a staging area for construction
equipment. He stated the City is in the process of developing a paid parking system for the
downtown area, and the parking structure will be available to the public, but will require a
fee. He stated the intent is to provide predictable and reliable parking options in the
downtown which will be managed by the Redevelopment Agency and Municipal Building
Authority. Revenue received for parking can then be used to maintain both parking areas

  MOTION:     A motion was made by Commissioner Southwick to recommend approval of the
              proposed condominium plat subject to recording of the Wonderblock
              Subdivision plat prior to recording the Wonderblock Condominium plat and
              obtaining and satisfying all staff and private utility requirements that are
              partially outlined in Staff Report Attachment 9 and includes the following:
              1. Ownership transactions be completed for the State owned parcels;
              2. Vacating easements where structures are to be built;
              3. Correcting plat language and satisfying outstanding deeds on title; and
              4. Obtaining and satisfying legal and engineering staff comments.
              Motion was seconded by Commissioner Graf and passed unanimously with
              Commissioners Aaberg, Blaisdell, Graf, Safsten, Shale, Shinoda, Southwick,
              Williams and Sandau voting aye.

There being no additional business before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
