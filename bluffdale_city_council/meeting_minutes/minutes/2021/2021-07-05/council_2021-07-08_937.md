2.       PUBLIC HEARING-Consideration and Vote on Ordinance No. 2021-12, Amending
         Table 11.350.020 of the Bluffdale City Code to Allow Personal Services
         Establishments as a Permitted Use in the 1-1 Light Industrial Zone, Staff Presenter,
         Ellen Oakman.

Associate City Plaimer, Ellen Oakman, presented the staff repo1t and stated that the City Council
has been asked to consider a text amendment to add Personal Serv ice Establishments as a permitted
use in the I-1 Zone. The use is currently allowed in other similar commercial zones but is not
allowed in the I-1 Zone. The applicant is seeking to open a nail salon, which is classified as a
Personal Service Establishment use. A map of the area was displayed. Ms. Oakman explained
that personal service establishments provide non-medical services to individuals as a primary use.
Examples include barbershops, beauty salons, day/health spas, hair salons, nail salons, shoe repair
shops, tanning salons, tailors, and similar businesses not including tattoo establislunents.

Mayor Timothy opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public hearing
was closed.

Mark Hales moved to approve Ordinance No. 2021-12, amending Table 11.350.020 of the
Bluffdale City Code to allow Personal Service Establishments as a permitted use in the 1-1
Light Industrial Zone. Wendy Aston seconded the motion. Vote on motion: Mark Hales-
Aye, Wendy Aston-Aye, Jeff Gaston-Aye, Traci Crockett-Aye, Dave Kallas-Aye. The motion
passed unanimously.

3.       Adjournment.
