KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE FY 2017 KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP TENTATIVE
BUDGET AND SETTING THE PUBLIC HEARING FOR PUBLIC COMMENT AND A
POSSIBLE FINAL ADOPTION OF THE BUDGET
RECITALS

A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 ef seq.

B. The Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. Pursuant to UCA §§ 10-6-] I 1 ef al, Kearns is required to prepare an annual tentative budget
for consideration, possible amendment, and tentative adoption.

D. Upon tentative adoption of the budget by the Council, a public hearing date for the budget
shall be held no earlier than seven (7) days prior to the date from which the public hearing to
formally adopt the budget shall be held pursuant to UCA §§ 10-6-113 ef al.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the FY 2017 Kearns

Tentative Budget, attached to this resolution as listed as “Attachment-Bud get” and sets the date,

time and place for the Public Hearing to be as follows:

PLACE: Kearns Library located at 5350 South 4220 West Kearns, Utah 84118
DATE: April 5, 2017
TIME: 7:00PM

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that a notice of the Public Hearing for the FY 2017 Kearns
Tentative Budget be printed a newspaper of general circulation and posted to the Utah Public
Meeting Notice Website no later than March 29, 2017.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, copies of the FY2017 Kearns Tentative Budget be available
for public inspection no later than March 29, 2017 at the Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office,
located at 2001 South State St Suite N2-700 Salt Lake City, Utah 84114, and attached to the
Notice of Public Hearing on the Utah Public Meeting Notice Website for public inspection.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Kearns, Salt Lake County, Utah this 72)’ day of March,
2017.

Steven A. Perry, Council Chair
