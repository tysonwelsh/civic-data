AS THE TIMPANOGOS SANITARY SEWER DISTRICT.

-Skyler Beltran, Commission

Dale Eyre (Utah County Deputy Attorney) explained that this public hearing is required
by statue to approve a reorganization of a special service district. The
Timpanogos Special Service District, in his opinion, is the best run, most effective
special service district we have in the county. This is the logical progression for a
special service district to become a special district, independent of the county. It
allows them to pursue their appointments, boundary changes, taxes, etc. This has
been three years in the works.

No public comment.

RESOLUTION OF THE UTAH COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS COMMEMORATING THE
SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA AND RECOGNIZING THE AMERICA 250
CELEBRATION

WHEREAS, July 4, 2026 marks the 250th anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of Independence and
the founding of the United States of America, a milestone known nationally as the Semiquincentennial and
commemorated through the America250 initiative; and

WHEREAS, on July 4, 1776, our nation’s founders declared that all men are created equal and endowed by
their Creator with certain unalienable rights, establishing the enduring principles of liberty, self-government,
individual responsibility, and the rule of law that continue to define the American experiment; and

WHEREAS, for two and a half centuries, the United States of America has stood as a beacon of freedom,
opportunity, innovation, and representative government, inspiring generations to pursue lives of purpose,
prosperity, and service; and

WHEREAS, Utah County recognizes the profound sacrifices made by the men and women throughout our
nation’s history who have defended the liberties secured at our founding, including members of the Armed
Forces, first responders, civic leaders, pioneers, entrepreneurs, educators, and countless citizens who have
strengthened our communities through service and sacrifice; and

WHEREAS, Utah County holds a unique place in the American story through the legacy of pioneer settlement,
religious liberty, entrepreneurship, constitutional governance, and community-building that helped shape
both the State of Utah and the nation; and

WHEREAS, the Semiquincentennial provides an opportunity to reflect upon our shared history, honor the
principles that unite us as Americans, educate future generations about the responsibilities of citizenship,
and recommit ourselves to preserving liberty and opportunity for generations yet to come; and

WHEREAS, America250 celebrations across the nation are intended not only to commemorate the past, but
to inspire a renewed spirit of civic engagement, volunteerism, innovation, patriotism, and national unity; and

WHEREAS, Utah County has long embraced a spirit of leadership, resilience, innovation, and service that
reflects the highest ideals of the American experiment and continues to position the county as a place where
families, businesses, and communities can live, work and play;

NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved that the Utah County Commission hereby recognizes and commemorates
the 250th anniversary of the founding of the United States of America and proudly joins the America250
celebration honoring our nation’s enduring legacy of freedom and self-government; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Utah County Commission encourages residents, schools, civic
organizations, businesses, faith communities, and local governments throughout Utah County to participate
in educational, historical, cultural, and service-oriented activities that celebrate America’s history and
inspire future generations to preserve and strengthen the blessings of liberty; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Utah County Commission expresses gratitude for the generations of
Americans whose courage, sacrifice, faith, and determination built this nation and whose example reminds
us that freedom must continually be protected, strengthened, and passed forward.
