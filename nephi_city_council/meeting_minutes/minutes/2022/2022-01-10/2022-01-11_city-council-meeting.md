                                         Nephi City
                                    Work Session Minutes
                                      January 11, 2021
                                           6:00 p.m.
                                    Senior Citizens Center
                                         160 N Main
                                       Nephi, UT 84648



1. The following twelve applicants for the vacant city council seat spoke for seven minutes on
   growth and annexation, what a rural community means to them, what they think is the
   appropriate role of city government, and if they would be able to attend meetings almost
   every Tuesday evening:
              1. Shari Banks
              2. Carolyn Ford
              3. Robert Painter
              4. Shirl Nichols
              5. Cory Thomson
              6. Brent Arns
              7. Chad Nielson
              8. John Bradley
              9. Terry Cook
              10. Britney Ford
              11. Jeanne Lewis
              12. JD Parady

 The Mayor and council thanked the participants for their time and effort of completing the
application and preparing their comments for the public.

2. The council agreed to vote on the city council appointment at the regular meeting on
   January 18, 2022, at 7:00 p.m.

3. The meeting adjourned at 8:00 p.m.
