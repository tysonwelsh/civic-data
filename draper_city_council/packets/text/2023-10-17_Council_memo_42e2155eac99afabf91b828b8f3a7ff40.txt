MEMO
To: City Council
From: Travis DeJong
Date: 2023-10-17
Re: Approval of Ordinance #1587


Comments:

Title 6 Chapter 9 of the Draper City Municipal code has not been substantially revised since 2016.
Community Development in conjunction with Legal has reviewed Chapter 9 to bring the chapter up-to-
date. The following is a list of the most significant revisions:

1. Scope of Special Event Business License

DCMC is currently unclear on if vendors are required to obtain their own business licenses when
participating in special events.

This ordinance makes it clear that the Special Event business license issued to the event organizer
authorizes both the event organizer to hold the event and all vendors making taxable sales to participate
in the event.

2. Fees for City-Sponsored Events

This ordinance exempt city-sponsored events from paying any licensing fees.

3. Requirements for Issuance

This ordinance states that the Special Event business license will only be issued upon the submission of
a complete application, payment of applicable license fees, the issuance of a special event permit to the
event organizer as applicable, and the issuance of temporary sales tax licenses by the Utah State Tax
Commission to vendors, as applicable.

The proposed amendments comply with state law and promote the health, safety, and welfare of Draper
residents.
