   2. Public Hearing: Considering a rezone request for property owned by Nikki Hansen,
      represented by Priority Homes for parcel number: XA00-0775- located at
      approximately 200 W from approximately 700 to 800 N, with approximately .53
      acres to go from the current R1-8 to the R3-8 zone.
         a. Open Public Hearing
                     TIME: 7:02 PM

          b. Staff Presentation
                     Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that the property is located on the northeast
             corner of the intersection of 200 W and 700 N. The property is currently owned
             by Nikki Hansen, who has signed authorization to allow Priority Homes
             (Ludlow’s) to represent the interests of her property. Priority Homes is looking to
             build two more four-plex’s along 200 W similar to the two they have already,
             south of Loveless Estates Plat A subdivision. In order to have the opportunity to
             do so they are proposing the rezone of parcel XA00-0775- (.53 acres) from R1-8
             to R3-8 to allow for multi-family housing, A partial street vacation on 200 W
             from approximately 700 to 760 N, abandoning the right-of-way to 60 feet. The
             excess property would be placed in surplus by the city, and could potentially be
             obtained by Priority Homes, a vicinity plat for the Loveless Apartments, and an
             Amendment to the Common Area Lot of the Loveless Estates Plat A Subdivision,
             Subdivision includes two existing 4-plexes on parcel XA00-0776-11 (1.03 acres),
             and, An amendment to Loveless Estates Plat A Common Area Lot (XA00-0822-
             12-COMM). Staff suggests that the planning commission recommends the rezone
             and street vacation to the city council, approve the vicinity plan for the amended

              this is contiguous area, and not spot zoning.

              currently. Mrs. Shauna Talbot responded that the right of way is a 90 foot and if
              the city abandons a little bit, then they will have the amount that they need.

              Travis Ludlow, representative for the Ludlow Group, explained to the Planning
              Commission what the project was, and how they relate to the Ray’s project. They
              will be a slab on grade for the rental, designed for handicap accessibility, larger
              turning radius in the halls and rooms, walk in showers, etc. Parking for the units

   3. Public Hearing: Considering a street vacation request from Priority Homes for
      portions of 200 W from approximately 700 to 760 N along both sides of the newly
      installed right-of-way, reducing the width to only 60 feet R.O.W.
          a. Open Public Hearing
                     TIME: 7:19 PM

          b. Staff Presentation
             Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that this is in relation to the previous item. Priority
             Homes is looking to build two more four-plex’s along 200 W similar to the two
             they have already, south of Loveless Estates Plat A subdivision. In order to have
             the opportunity to do so they are proposing the rezone of parcel XA00-0775- (.53
             acres) from R1-8 to R3-8 to allow for multi-family housing, A partial street
             vacation on 200 W from approximately 700 to 760 N, abandoning the right-of-
             way to 60 feet. The excess property would be placed in surplus by the city, and
             could potentially be obtained by Priority Homes, a vicinity plat for the Loveless
             Apartments, and an Amendment to the Common Area Lot of the Loveless Estates
             Plat A Subdivision, Subdivision includes two existing 4-plexes on parcel XA00-
             0776-11 (1.03 acres), and, An amendment to Loveless Estates Plat A Common
             Area Lot (XA00-0822-12-COMM). Staff suggests that the planning commission
             recommends the rezone and street vacation to the city council, approve the
             vicinity plan for the amended subdivision contingent on the rezone and street
             vacation being approved by the Nephi City Council.

          c. Public Comment

              Commissioner Fran Peterson asked about the vacate area in conjunction with the
              numbers needed for the rezoned project. Mrs. Shauna Talbot answered that the
              area has the option to vacate both sides, however the Ludlow group only need the
              one side currently to meet the zoning area requirements and that is all the city
              staff is recommending.

              Alternate Commissioner John Ford asked about the length of the property to
              vacate if it extended the length or just the small area. Mrs. Shauna Talbot
              responded that in 1880 the area was needed, but the laws and space have been
              adjusted. There is the option to do both sides, however the staff are not
              recommending that option. Staff recommends that only the one side is done at this
              time.

              Area Lot (XA00-0822-12-COMM). Staff suggests that the planning commission
              recommends the rezone and street vacation to the city council, approve the
              vicinity plan for the amended subdivision contingent on the rezone and street
              vacation being approved by the Nephi City Council.

          b. Commission Discussion
             Commissioner Fran Peterson asked if they are recommending three separate
             subdivisions? Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that it is a subdivision with an
             amendment essentially. The fence has been installed for the Loveless Plat A, and
             so the adjustment will put the current fence in the wrong property line. To correct
             this there are two options: 1. A blight ordinance to remove the current home and it
             will go forward to the City Council. 2. The Ludlow’s’ do a development
             agreement with them. Though the second option is not what the staff and city
             would like to see done, and our city attorney is strongly opposed to it.

              Mr. Brett Ludlow, representative for the Ludlow Group, added additionally that
              the second option came up as Nephi City didn’t see the property serving a need,
              and it was agreed upon that the city would assist in the road construction.

              Commissioner Fran Peterson asked if it was necessary for it to be a subdivision?
              Mrs. Shauna Talbot responded it was not necessary and that the staff recommends
              that they are separate for ownership’s sake.

              Mr. Brett Ludlow stated that one day they will die they will need to sale it and it
              will be easier if it is subdivided which is why they are requesting it be done.
              Another benefit is the separate utilities, and how the units are rented out.

              Acting Chairman Cory Thomson raised a concern that if they are done like this
              and you separate them then they won’t have the density needed for that specified
              zoning. Mrs. Shauna Talbot responded if it comes forward then we have the
              option to say they don’t have enough area and space, this is currently just a site
              plan.

              Chairman Cory Thomson again raised concern that this item is borrowing from
              one area to the next, when eventually this area comes for sale the lines will be
              very blurred and difficult to survey. Mrs. Shauna Talbot again responded that the
              city would make sure that they have what they needed, she additionally gave an
              example of another builder that is in a similar situation to this one and so far it has
              worked out.

              Mr. Travis Ludlow, representative for Ludlow Group, added comment that
              Ludlow’s will follow the ordinance, it would be ideal to have the eight units but if
              they have to will settle for seven if things do not work and aren’t approved.
