BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a site plan amendment and deviations for the
construction of an accessory building. The overall property is approximately 10.56 acres in
size and is located on the east side of Minuteman Drive, at 72 E. Sivogah Court (Exhibit B &
C). The property is currently zoned M1 and is developed with several buildings housing
operations for the City’s Public Works Department.

The accessory buildings is proposed to be placed in an existing parking and landscaping
area directly to the west of the main Public Works building. Other areas of the site are
currently utilized for equipment and material storage. The proposed orientation does not
meet the requirements of DCMC 9-22-050(A) which call for buildings to be located with the
main facade and main entrance facing the street. While the accessory function of the
building and the site constraints led to this orientation, the applicant is requesting a
deviation from this requirement.

The accessory building is of modular construction with simple, flat sides and a single
exterior material comprised of engineered wood siding. Engineered wood siding is

considered a wood fiber composite siding. The modular building was chosen for its ability
to quickly provide temporary office space on the site while Public Works determines
whether to expand or replace its existing facility. The proposed exterior materials and
colors do not meet the requirements of DCMC 9-22-040(F) which call for a minimum of
three (3) colors, two (2) primary materials on each facade. The accessory building is painted
in two (2) colors and the exterior is constructed of only one (1) primary material,
engineered wood siding. Therefore, the applicant is requesting a deviation from these
requirements.

Site and Architectural Design Standards Modification. DCMC Section 9-22-030 states that the
Planning Commission may approve site plans and architectural plan that deviate from the
requirements of the design standards within Chapter 22 of the DCMC based on making
certain required findings in whole. Per DCMC Section 9-22-030(b), the Planning Commission
shall find an application complies with at least three of the following criteria:

         1. The proposed development includes additional amenities or pedestrian and bike
            connectivity in excess of what is otherwise required.
         2. The overall landscape and open space area provided within the proposed
            development exceeds required amounts by more than ten percent (10%).
         3. The proposed development is a transit oriented development (TOD) and provides
            direct connectivity for active transportation methods to a transportation facility.
         4. The traffic impacts of the proposed development will be mitigated through creative
            design, traffic calming measures, companywide carpool or transit pass programs, or
            other similar measures.
         5. The proposed development is designed to meet LEED certification standards.
         6. The proposed development involves redeveloped buildings, additions to existing
            buildings, minor structures being added to a site, or is a new building within a
            previously approved phased development and the deviation is necessary to create a
            consistency with other existing buildings.
         7. Vegetative landscaping (excluding lawn, turf, and groundcovers) exceeds the amounts
            required in this Title by at least ten percent (10%).
         8. The building is designed by a licensed architect in a specific architectural style, that
            utilizes facade materials differently than prescribed in subsection 9-22-040(F) of this
            chapter.
         9. The amount of primary materials used for each building facade exceeds the required
            amount by more than ten percent (10%).

The applicant has indicated in their Deviation Request Letter (Exhibit I) how they meet at
least three (3) of the criteria as outlined below:

    •    Criteria 2. The overall site has a large open space area which is approximately 55%
         of the site. DCMC Section 9-23-050 requires 20% of the site be landscaped or in
         natural open space. Staff agrees this criteria has been met.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the requests, receive public
comment, and make a decision based on the findings listed below and the criteria for
approval, or denial, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the Site Plan Amendment request, staff
recommends they include the following conditions of approval:

    1.        That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
              Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
              and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
    2.        That the applicant submit a revised landscaping plan identifying all specifications
              for tree type and shrub size in compliance with DCMC Section 9-23 for review
              and approval by staff.
    3.        That the applicant submit a lighting plan in compliance with DCMC Section 9-20
              for review and approval by staff.

RE:    Planning Applications 2023-4627-SP and 2023-4631-VAR
       Request for Deviation from Design Standards

The city is adding an accessory building to the overcrowded public works operation
facility to provide temporary office space while the city is activing searching for site
to expand or replace the existing facility. Because city code does not allow for this
type of temporary structure within the city, the city is required to apply for approval
of a site plan amendment, including deviation requests for the following design
changes: exterior colors, materials, and building orientation. The specific code
sections from which the city is requesting a deviation are found in DCMC 9-22-
040(F)(1) Minimum Colors, DCMC 9-22-040(F)(3) Primary Materials, and DCMS 9-22-
050(A) Building Placement. The city is proposing two colors for all four building
sides rather than three, one primary material rather than two, and to orienting the
building to face the existing building rather than to the street.

To qualify for approval of these deviations, the city must demonstrate that the
project meets three of the nine deviation criteria listed in DCMC 9-22-030(B), the
city believes that they meet at least four of the criteria as outlined below:

DCMC 9-22-030(B)(2): The existing site exceeds the minimum landscaping and open
space requirements by more than 10%. The entire site is approximately 459,994
square feet (10.56 acres) of which approximately 255,855 square feet are
landscaped or naturalized open space. This calculates out to a 55% landscaping
percentage.

DCMC 9-22-030(B)(6): The accessory building is a minor structure is being added to
an existing site.
