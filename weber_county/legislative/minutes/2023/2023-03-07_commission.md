                                                                MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                    Tuesday, March 7th, 2023 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Gage Froerer, James “Jim” H. Harvey, and Sharon Bolos (attended via Zoom)
STAFF PRESENT: Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney; and Craig
D. Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.

A. WELCOME: Chair Froerer
B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Brian Cowan
C. INVOCATION: Shelly Halacy
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: A song written by Billy Joel Shaver says “I’m just a lump o’
coal but I’m gonna be diamond someday; I’m gonna grow and grow until I’m so blue perfect, I’m gonna put a smile
on everybody’s face. I’m gonna kneel and pray everyday lest I should become vain along the way. I’m gonna learn
the best way to walk. I’m gonna learn the best way to talk. I’m gonna spit and polish my old edgy self.” A diamond
is one of the most precious things we have and one of the hardest things we have. A diamond is merely a lump of coal
that did really well under pressure.

E. PUBLIC COMMENT:
     1. BAB FROM OHIO, LIVES IN OGDEN: It’s really awesome to live here. I was hoping to hear some words from
        the newly elected Commissioner. I enjoyed the new issue of Weber Connections. I think it would be a service
        to the community to better distribute Weber Connections. I think Utah and Ogden are really great and I thank
        the Commissioners and encourage you to keep it up.
F. CONSENT ITEMS:
   1. Warrants #7431-7485, #474867-474979 and #247-249 in the amount of $1,591,245.34.
   2. Purchase orders in the amount of $197,463.14.
   3. Summary of Warrants and Purchase Orders.
   4. Minutes for the meeting held on February 28, 2023.
   5. Local Transportation Funding Agreement with Ogden City to establish terms related to the Ogden City D
      Ave. Project.
      Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the consent items; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
      Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
G. ACTION ITEMS:

     1. APPROVE RESOLUTION 21-2023 APPOINTING BOARD MEMBERS TO THE OGDEN VALLEY
        PARK SERVICE AREA.
            Brian Cowan (Director, Weber-Morgan Health Department): Propose reappointing Angela Choberka.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 21-2023 reappointing Angela Choberka to the Weber-
            Morgan Board of Health; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
     2. APPROVE THE FINAL READING OF ORDINANCE 2023-05 OF WEBER COUNTY AMENDING
        SECTION 28-1-3 OF THE WEBER COUNTY CODE.
            Brian Cowan (Director, Weber-Morgan Health Department): No changes from First Reading (see Commission
            Meeting minutes from February 21st, 2023).
            Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the Final Reading of Ordinance 2023-05 of Weber County amending
            Section 28-1-3 of the Weber County Code; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
     3. APPROVAL OF A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH THE STATE OF UTAH FOR
        THE FIRST RESPONDERS MENTAL HEALTH FUND.
            Chief Phillip Reese (Sheriff’s Department, Corrections): Thank you to Mrs. Halacy for helping this agenda
            item work out. Thank you all those individuals that made this MoU happen. The grant is for $60k covering
            all expenses for employees in 2023. The contract is with Comprehensive Psychological Service who will
            provide voluntary mental health services, critical incident debriefs, and wellness training events. I have been
         involved in critical incident debriefs and I cannot give enough credence to how beneficial they are to our
         staff.

         Commissioner Harvey moved for approval of a Memorandum of Understanding with the State of Utah for the
         First Responders Mental Health Fund; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye

    4. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH THE COMPREHENSIVE PSYCHOLOGICAL SERVICE INC.
       FOR WEBER COUNTY SHERIFF’S OFFICE EMPLOYEE MENTAL WELLNESS.
         Chief Phillip Reese (Sheriff’s Department, Corrections): See Item G3 above.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a contract with the Comprehensive Psychological Service Inc. for
         Weber County Sheriff’s Office employee mental wellness; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

H. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Stall of Fame: Kelly Janis. She achieved her Master’s Degree in Public Administration and recognized by the
