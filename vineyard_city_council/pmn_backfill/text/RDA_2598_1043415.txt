            3.1 DISCUSSION AND ACTION – Tentative Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Budget

                and set a public hearing for June 14, 2023.

              Chair Fullmer turned the time over to City Manager Ezra Nair.

             Mr. Nair gave a brief background on the creation of the RDA. He then reviewed the tentative
         budget.
