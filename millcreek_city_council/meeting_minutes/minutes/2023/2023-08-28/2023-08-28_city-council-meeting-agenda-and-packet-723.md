2. Discussion            on Updates            to the City's       Ethics     Code;      Lisa     Dudley,       HR-Finance             Director
Lisa Dudley           said this was a first reading                for the council's           consideration.          Millcreek        would
continue       to seek out and apply                for various       types of grants.          It was important             for Millcreek's
conflict      of interest       policy     and procedures           to address key elements                 required         by the awarding
federal      agencies.        Many,      if not all, professional            associations         for government              professionals         also
require      formal        training     in ethics    for their members.            She said suggestions                had been added to the
draft policy         to address requirements                 for the expenditures              of federal     grant        funding,    but the draft
policy      also required           elected      and appointed        officials       and all employees             to sign an annual             ethics
pledge       form.    The annual          ethics pledge        form      was a requirement              of the State Auditor's              Office.        She
briefly     reviewed         the summary           of changes       which      included        compliance           with     the State Auditor's
Office      Fraud     Risk Assessment               to include      an annual         ethics pledge         to be signed         by all elected           and
appointed        officials      and all employees             of Millcreek,        mandatory            ethics training         for all city elected
officials      and employees,             and periodic        training      for federally        funded      projects/activities            as required
by the federal           agency/funding            source,     and updated        definitions        necessary         to comply          with    federal
grant requirements               and for a conflict          of interest      policy.


Mayor        Silvestrini       brought        up the Government             in the Sunshine          section        and asked if it applied                to
all of the city's          public      bodies.     He wanted        every     city public        body     to be subject          to it. Brems
confirmed         they would.
