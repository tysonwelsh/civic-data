         ro




 When reading the specific language in the Building Height exception section of the code,

   p




 3(F), which states: “Parking Spaces must be at least nine feet (9’) wide by eighteen feet

Ap




 dimensions.” While Section 15-3-4(A)(1) provides minimum dimensions for Single
 Garages, a defined term in the LMC, the HR-1 Building Height exception regulations

 understands this requirement as being for a Parking Space, as dimensioned in Section
 15-3-4. While the LMC may be somewhat unclear regarding the required depth for a
 garage with a Building Height exception, Staff understands the requirement for a more
 compact garage is intended to mitigate the impact of the building that exceeds the height.
