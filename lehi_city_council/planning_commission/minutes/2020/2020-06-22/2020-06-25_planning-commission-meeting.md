Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Hemmert welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Consent Agenda

2.1 The Commission reviewed the minutes from May 14, 2020 and May 28, 2020. Commissioner
Everett moved to approve the consent agenda consisting of the May 14, 2020 and May 28, 2020
minutes. Commission Bunker seconded. The motion passed unanimously.

3. Regular Agenda

3.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Dan Ford’s request for review of the Pine
Springs Concept Plan, a 37-unit residential development located at 225 South 600 East.
    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item. She stated there
    were some amenities were missing in order to meet the development code.

    Commissioner Hemmert asked for clarification from Staff for the exceptions on the concept
    plan.

    The applicant, Dan Ford, was present. He clarified that they are installing a pickle ball court
    in hopes that it would suffice the top lot and green space requirements. He also stated that
    each unit is built with a two car garage in hopes to eliminate on road parking.

    Commissioner Ellis asked what the new all bordering the existing residential property would
    look like. The applicant stated there are no design plans yet, but they would work with the
    resident and engineering to make sure that line of sight is protected as well as making sure
    the resident is happy. Commissioner Everett asked about the setback reduction. The applicant
    stated they chose to incorporate a streetscape.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker forwarded a positive recommendation of Dan Ford’s request for
review of the Pine Springs Concept Plan, a 37-unit residential development located at 225 South
600 East; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Nielsen
seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed 6 – 1.

3.2) Public hearing and consideration of Creative Signs’ request for approval of a
conditional use permit for a state-owned public-school pylon sign with EMD (electronic
message display) for Willow Creek Middle School located at 2275 West 300 North.
    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    The applicant was not present.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Nielsen moved to approve Creative Signs’ request for approval of a
conditional use permit for a state-owned public-school pylon sign with EMD (electronic message
display) for Willow Creek Middle School located at 2275 West 300 North; including all DRC
comments from June 10, 2020 and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Peterson seconded.

    Amended Motion: Commissioner Nielsen amended the motion to include that operating hours
    should stop from 10:00 pm to 6:00 am. Commissioner Peterson seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code amendment to Chapter 35, clarifying regulations for Community
Forestry.
    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Jessica Smith, Urban Forester, Trails, and Open Space Supervisor for Lehi City, stated the
    biggest issue is street clearance. The amendment would allow Lehi to make it required to
    have a permit to plant a tree to help with street clearance, urban forestry, parks, sidewalks,
    etc. The goal is to educate and plan for future development and urban canopies. The
    Commissioners had questions on who would enforce the code. Commissioners and Staff
    discussed the recommended item.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code amendment to Chapter 35, clarifying regulations for Community Forestry.
Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Chapter 37, adding single-family residential design
standards for (PC) Planned Communities, (PRD) Planned Residential Development, and
(PUD) Planned Unit Development.
    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Everett inquired about the cost increase that would impact new homeowners
    when affordable housing was the goal. He stated the new requirements would increase the
    cost for new home builders. Brittney stated that was a concern. Staff stated exterior surfaces
    that are added to meet code increase the cost for the homeowner significantly.

Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to table agenda item 3.4 pursuant to the applicant’s
request. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

4. City Business

There will not be a Work Session on July 2, 2020

Mayor Johnson and City Council voted unanimously that they will not approve any new TOD’s

until they receive a cohesive update regarding the Point of the Mountain Transit Study and
Central Transit Study. UTA is a party to both.

5. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Ellis moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Nielsen seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 8:05 p.m.
