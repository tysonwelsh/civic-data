CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to Caliber Contractor the 700 East
        Improvements Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The 700 East Improvements Project
          consists of the installation of new intersection bulb-outs at 600 South, 300 South,
          and Tabernacle Street; an urban trail on the east side of 700 East; a landscaped
          median between the vehicle travel lane and cycle track; pavement milling, micro-
          surfacing, and surface sealing of 700 East and Tabernacle Street; angle parking
          on Tabernacle Street; and new striping and stenciling on the roadway. UDOT

           costs. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Caliber Contractor in the amount of

      b. Consider approval to award bid to Feller Enterprises for the South
         Industrial Park Debris Basin and Middleton Floodwall Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The project was advertised and four bids
           were received. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Feller Enterprises in the
           amount of $349,287.89.

      c. Consider approval to award bid to Caliber Contractors for the Desert
         Color Parkway and Black Mountain Drive Traffic Signal.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This traffic signal project was a formal
           bid and five bids were received. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Caliber
           Contractors in the amount of $508,714.35.

      d. Consider approval to award bid to Sletten Construction for the Clarifier
         1-4 Improvement Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: There are 8 clarifiers at the St. George
           Regional Water Reclamation Facility (SGRWRF). Two of the clarifiers were
           recently constructed with the plant expansion project. Clarifiers 1-4 are the
           original clarifiers for the WWTP that was constructed in 1989. While the concrete
           basins are still in good shape, the mechanical components of the clarifiers are at
           the end of their useful life and needs replacement. Clarifiers 5-6 were
           constructed in approximately 1998 and are still in relatively good condition. This
           project will replace the mechanical components of Clarifiers 1-4 and rehabilitate
           the mechanical components of clarifiers 5-6. Staff recommends awarding the bid
           to Sletten Construction in the amount of $5,906,000.

      e. Consider approval to purchase and install playground equipment via
         state contract with Big T Recreation for Las Colinas Park.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This proposed purchase is to provide
           new playground equipment for Las Colinas Park and is for materials, surfacing,
           delivery, and installation. The proposed playground equipment includes towers
           with multiple slides, swings, spinners, zipline, and shades. Surfacing will be a
           mix of engineered wood fiber (wood chips) and rubber tiles. The rubber tiles will
           be placed under the tower structures. Staff recommends purchasing the
           equipment from Big T Recreation in the amount of $754,245.

      f.   Consider approval for a Construction Management Contract with Alpha
           Engineering for the Little Valley Pump Station Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Little Valley Pump Station is being
           constructed by Feller Enterprises and was designed by Alpha Engineering. This is

      g. Consider approval of a Contract Agreement for Construction Manager at
         Risk (CMAR) Design Services with Big D for the Terminal Expansion
         Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Construction Manager at Risk
          (CMAR) process allows the City staff, architect and contractor to collaboratively
          develop the project scope, optimize design, improve quality, manage costs and
          share risks. This enables greater owner control, collaboration, innovation, better
          risk management, time and cost savings. The CMAR process comprises two
          phases: design (pre-construction) and construction. Approval of this agreement
          only commits the City to pay for the pre-construction phase of the agreement in
          the amount of $105,025. Staff recommends approval of the contract agreement
          in the amount of $105,025.

APPROVAL OF NON-COMMERCIAL HANGAR PAD LICENSING POLICY:
     Consider approval of the Non-Commercial Hangar Pad Licensing Policy.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: When the demand for airport hangars for
      general aviation became greater than the availability of hangars it became clear that
      policies were needed to inform the use of airport land by the general aviation
      community. Several work meetings were held to discuss the need and proposed
      policies, and a public hearing was held before the Council to gather input from the
      general aviation community. Staff recommends approval.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: State law requires the recommended budget
      to be tentatively adopted and the public hearing dates to be set during a regular or
      special City Council meeting prior to considering adoption of the final budget in June.
      The final budget is required to be adopted before June 30th each fiscal year. The FY
      2025-2026 City Manager Recommended Budget was presented to the City Council
      and made publicly available during the Thursday May 1, 2025 City Council Meeting.
      A copy of the FY 2026 budget is available on the city's website at
      www.sgcityutah.gov/citybudget and through the City Recorder's Office. Tonight's
      item is to consider tentatively adopting the FY 2026 budget and to set the dates for
      this year's public hearings on the FY 2026 budget as Thursday June 5, 2025 and
      Thursday June 19, 2025 during the regularly scheduled City Council Meetings.
      Adoption of the Final FY 2026 budget is planned for Thursday June 19, 2025
      following the public hearing. Staff recommends approval.
