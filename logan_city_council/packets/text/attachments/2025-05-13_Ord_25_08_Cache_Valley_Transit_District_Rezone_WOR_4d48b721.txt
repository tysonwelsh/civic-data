RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission recommend approval to the Municipal Council for a rezone of approximately 4.47 acres of property located at approximately 754 West 600 North (TIN# 05-061-0004) from Public (PUB) to Commercial Services (CS).

Land use adjoining the subject property
North:  IP: Industrial Uses          East:  COM: Commercial Uses
South:  PUB: Public Uses             West:  PUB & CS: Public & Commercial Uses

Background
In January of 2025, Cache Valley Transit District (Connect) completed their new modern and larger facility located in North Logan City. All operations and equipment from this location at 754 West 600 North have moved to the new property, leaving this facility vacant. The applicant is requesting a rezone from Public to Commercial Service with the intention of subsequently selling the land. The 4.47-acre property currently contains an office/administration building, shop building, and a large vehicle carport. Landscaping and paved parking lots complete the site improvements, with fencing enclosing the parking and shop areas. The property is currently accessed via three driveway approaches onto 600 North and 800 West.

GENERAL PLAN
The Future Land Use Plan (FLUP) identifies this area as Public (PUB). The public designation is reserved for public facilities such as government offices, safety services, public schools, and similar public services. A change to the FLUP to recognize the change from a public ownership and/or use to private ownership will be handled within the General Plan update process.

LAND DEVELOPMENT CODE
The Land Development Code (LDC) describes the CS zone as a designation that fills the need between industrial and commercial land uses. CS areas are intended for light manufacturing, warehousing, wholesaling, construction support services, and limited commercial. CS zoning is not intended for residential or heavy manufacturing.

Figure 2 shows the 4.47-acre property.
[embedded aerial photo — labels 600 N, 750, 680, 530]

EXISTING ZONING
The current PUB zoning designation would be appropriate if the property were to be purchased and used by a public entity. The applicant has contacted several local government entities, including the City of Logan, to see if there was interest in the property for a public use. According to the applicant, there was no interest from public entities. The surrounding area is currently a variety of land uses and zoning designations. To the east, the 11-acre property contains commercial self-storage units. North of the site is currently vacant and previously contained a landscaping business. To the south and west, the City of Logan owns and operates the City's Service Center and Light and Power Department building. 600 North acts as larger boundary in the Bridger Neighborhood, with Industrial zoning to the north and a mix of Commercial, Mixed Use, and Commercial Service zoning to the south.

SUMMARY
With 600 North being a boundary between Industrial and Commercial zoning in the Bridger Neighborhood, staff considers the proposal for CS appropriate. If this property is purchased by a private entity (most likely buyer), the FLUP designation of PUB will be inappropriate and will be evaluated during the next FLUP amendment cycle. The surrounding area is a mixed bag, with storage units, service centers, warehousing, and manufacturing. The CS zone permits, or conditionally permits land uses that are in a similar character with the current surrounding land uses and built environment. The CS zone permits office, automobile service and repair, heavy truck service and repair, contractor supply, wholesales, warehousing, gas stations, truck stops, lumberyards, and similar uses further specified in the LDC 17.11.030. Staff recommends the Planning Commission send a favorable recommendation on to City Council.

RECOMMENDED FINDINGS FOR REZONE
The Planning Commission bases its decision on the following findings supported in the administrative record for this project:
   1.  The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as PUB. PUB is only appropriate if the property owner is a public entity.
   2.  600 North is a regional boundary between IP to the north and CS, COM, and MU to the south, and this application for CS is consistent with that pattern.
   3.  The CS zone permits and conditional permits land uses that are similar or compatible with the current land uses and developments in the area.
   4.  The design standards, height limitations, and setbacks in the Land Development Code will ensure compatibility for adjacent property use and enjoyment.

This staff report is an analysis of the application based on adopted city documents, standard city development practices, and available information. The report is to be used to review and consider the merits of the application prior to and during the course of the Planning Commission meeting. Additional information may be revealed by participants at the Planning Commission meeting which may modify the staff report and become the Certificate of Decision.
