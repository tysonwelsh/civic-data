      1. APPROVAL OF INAUGURATION/APPOINTMENT OF NEW COMMISSIONER, SEAT B – RESOLUTION 22-2018.
            Lynda Pipkin, Weber County Republican Party Chair, stated that last night the Weber County Republican
            Party’s Central Committee held a special election to elect an interim commissioner to fill the unexpired term of
            former Commissioner K. Gibson, who resigned. There were two candidates, Scott Jenkins and Nathan Hale,
            and the Weber County Republican Party declared Mr. Jenkins the winner. Chair Harvey read the resolution.
            Mr. Wilkinson moved to adopt Resolution 22-2018 appointing Scott Jenkins as the interim Weber County
            Commissioner (Seat B); Commissioner Ebert seconded.
            Commissioner Ebert – aye; Mr. Wilkinson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
            Scott Jenkins was then sworn in and he introduced his family. As State Senator for 16 years he held the same
            position that his uncle had held. He is excited about this opportunity to serve. Ms. Pipkin said that
            Commissioner Jenkins has always served with integrity. Nathan Hale, of Roy City, expressed thanks. He was
            the other candidate and had submitted a letter of intent last week for the position.
      2. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE WEBER-MORGAN BOARD OF HEALTH. (WACOG SEAT WILL
         BE FILLED AFTER AUGUST 6, 2018 WACOG MEETING) – RESOLUTION 23-2018.
            Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, noted that an amended interlocal agreement was approved by
            the Morgan County Council on 5/15/2018 and by the Weber County Commission on 5/23/2018. The
            amendment included restructuring of the board, which supersedes the former appointments, and the time has
            come to make appointments—11 will be made by Weber County and the other two by Morgan County which
            will consist of the following: 1 Weber County commissioner; 2 members representing municipal interests (1
            recommended by Ogden City, 1 by WACOG); 1 member recommended by the Weber Medical Society; 1
            member on a rotating basis between the three School Districts; 1 recommended by Weber State University
            (WSU - Health Administration or Public Health); 1 by Weber Human Services (WHS), 1 on a rotating basis by
            Ogden Regional or McKay Dee Hospitals; 1 by Midtown Community Health Center; and 2 at-large positions
            (representing both local environmental and business interests).          Chair Harvey read the received
            recommendations from the following: Weber Medical Society-Dr. Frank Brown; WSU-Dr. Ken Johnson;
            Ogden City-Neil Garner; WHS-Director Kevin Eastman; Midtown Community Health-Director Alicia
            Martinez; environmental representative- Jennifer Bodine or Joe DeCaria; business representative-Cole M.
            Brinkerhoff; McKee Dee Hospital-Donna Chapman, and Ogden Regional Hospital would provide a
            representative if they were on first rotation; Ogden School District-Karen Harrop or Weber School District-
            Lori Jo Rasmussen. The names for the Morgan County School District and for WACOG had not yet been
         submitted and a Weber County commissioner needed to be determined. Mr. Crockett stated that the initial
         board is to be staggered so that approximately 1/3 of the terms will expire each year and the rotating positions
         for a hospital or school district need to be given full 3-year terms. Commissioner Ebert recommended initially
         selecting the Ogden School District and McKay Dee Hospital due to their current relevant programs. Brian
         Bennion, Health Department Director, addressed Commissioner Jenkins’ questions stating that the
         recommendations for environmental interests had been interviewed and that both were excellent candidates.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt resolution 23-2018 appointing the following representatives to the Weber-
         Morgan Board of Health: Weber Medical Society-Dr. Frank Brown; Weber State University-Dr. Ken Johnson;
         Ogden City-Neil Garner; Weber Human Services-Director Kevin Eastman; Midtown Community Health
         Center-Director Alicia Martinez; environmental representative-Jennifer Bodine; business representative-Cole
         M. Brinkerhoff; McKay Dee Hospital-Donna Chapman, then rotate to Ogden Regional; and Ogden City School
         District-Karen Harrop, then rotate to Weber School District. Chair Harvey recommended that Morgan County
         School District follow Weber School District and noted that WACOG’s representative will be named at a
         subsequent meeting. After discussion, the consensus was to select a Weber County commissioner at a later
         date; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
         Mr. Crockett said that the Attorney’s Office and the Health Department gave recommendations to the Health
         Board on term expirations with an effort to get them as close as possible to those of members who were currently
         serving, and also to give the rotating entities full 3-year terms.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the following term expirations for the Weber-Morgan Board of Health:
         Weber County Commission in 2020, Morgan County Council in 2020, Morgan County at large in 2021, Ogden
         City in 2019, WACOG in 2019, Weber Medical Society in 2021, Ogden School District in 2021, Weber State
         University in 2019, Weber Human Services in 2019, McKay Dee Hospital in 2021, Midtown Community
         Health Center in 2021 and the at large environmental and business interests in 2020; Chair Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    3. APPROVAL OF THE 2018 TAX RATES.
         Scott Parke, County Comptroller, noted that the final budget is set in the previous year but at that time final
         property values are not known and the tax rolls are not finalized. The latter occurs at the end of May. There
         was no tax increase in 2018. This is an administrative action to finalize those rates discussed in a public hearing.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the 2018 tax rates; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    4. FINAL READING OF ORDINANCE ON AMENDMENTS TO DEVELOPMENT SERVICE FEES – ORDINANCE 2018-7.
         Steve Burton, of the County Planning Division, stated that as noted last week the three categories—Building
         Parcel Designation ($75 fee), Zoning Development Agreement without a rezone ($600 + $10/acre), and Zoning
         Development Agreement amendment ($350 + $20/page or $150/page, whichever is less)—were being proposed
         and there were no fee increases. He addressed the commissioners’ questions, explained that some of these
         services take a lot of time and effort by the Planning Division to ensure the best final product, and reiterated
         that the Planning Division has been providing these services for free, that other counties charge for them and
         these fees fall in line at the median level.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to adopt Ordinance 2018-7 amending the Development Service Fees;
         Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
