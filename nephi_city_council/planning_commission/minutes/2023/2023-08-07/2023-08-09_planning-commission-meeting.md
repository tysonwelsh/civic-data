2.     Rezone Request - Arrow Builders (Ben & Louis Allred) to rezone their property
       parcel #XA00-0670-2 located at approximately 431 N. Main Street from the CU-1
       zone to the CU-2 zone. (Tabled/Continued from previous month’s meeting)
       a.     Open Public Hearing
              Cory Thomson opened the public hearing at 7:07 pm.

       b.     Staff Presentation
              Shauna stated that this ¾ acre property is currently in the CU-1 zone. She talked
              a little bit about the history of the zoning of this property, and the difference
              between CU-1 and CU-2 zones. She explained that the developers are wanting
              to use the land for multi-family housing. Shauna went on to say that changing this
              zone to CU-2 would actually be less intensive to the surrounding area.
              Alan Hancock asked if this had been presented to the City Council yet. Skip
              confirmed and stated that the Council generally agreed that the change would be
              less intensive to the area. Rod Steele asked about the plumbing and stated that
              he just spent $10,000 to fix the sewer in his son’s backyard. He is concerned that
              4 more units would amplify the problem, and wondered if the City can do
              anything to help this situation. Shauna explained that a sewer manhole would be
              placed and would provide two ways to dredge the line. She also stated that the
              sewer will have to be included in the site plan and will be taken under
              consideration by city staff when it comes in. Rod asked who is responsible for the
              curb and gutter. Shauna replied that at this time it’s the resident’s responsibility to

            replace it if there’s a problem because it’s a UDOT right-of-way. Alan expressed
            concern over the purpose of zoning, if we’re just going to honor every rezone
            request that comes in.

     c.     Close Public Hearing
            Cory closed the public hearing at 7:23pm.

     d.     Commission Discussion
            Cory stated that he’s been thinking about this topic for a few months. He said that
            the Commission made some changes to the CU-1 zone, and that if those
            changes wouldn’t have been made there wouldn’t be a need to request a rezone.
            Fran asked the neighbors to this property that were in attendance what they
            would prefer. Rod Steele and Louise McPherson both stated that they would
            rather have a business than residential. Shauna reminded everyone of the height
            allowances in each zone.

     e.     Action Item - Motion
            Cory asked for a motion to accept the rezone request.

            Motion: Fran Petersen Second: John Ford Opposed: Alan Hancock
            Roll Call- Hancock: Nay, Thomson: Yea, Fran: Yea, Ford: Yea
            Outcome: Motion Passes

3.   Arrow Builders Apartments Site Plan for One (1) Four-Plex
     a.    Staff Presentation
           Shauna stated that approval of this Site Plan will be contingent upon the City
           Council approving the previous rezone request. She said that the applicant’s
           engineer has already reached out to UDOT for a 30 ft access and has tentative
           approval for that. Shauna said that the City has a flood zoning ordinance, and
           that the site plan will be carefully reviewed to make sure it is in compliance. She
           pointed out that there are only two areas in town that are actually on the FEMA
           map. Shauna also stated that a Geotech report will be required. Debbie Zirbes
           pointed out that the Hollow is under the Army Corp of Engineers, so they will
           need approval from them.

     b.     Commission Discussion
            Fran Petersen expressed concern if the developer doesn’t have flood insurance.
            Kasey Wright assured her that if the developer is aware of the risk and feels they
            can build safely, then the City would not be held liable. Fran asked if they needed
            to update the floodplain policy before this project is approved. Shauna assured
            her that there is a policy in place and even if there were changes, this project
            would not be affected. Cory asked if there was going to be an issue with a fence
            and the existing property line. Shauna stated that property line disputes are not
            something the City gets involved with. Skip wondered if a property dispute needs

            to be resolved before approving a site plan. Seth replied that it only applies to
            annexation. Shauna told Skip she knew which policy he was talking about and
            that she would double check to make sure it doesn’t also include rezones.

     c.     Action Item - Motion
            Cory asked for a motion to table this issue until the next meeting.

            Motion: Alan Hancock Second: Fran Petersen Outcome: Passed Unanimously

4.   Title 10: Clean up code language in all zones to include the verbiage, “whichever
     is smaller” when referencing the maximum height and minimum allowed stories in
     each zone’s maximum building height for better clarity.
     a.      Open Public Hearing
             Cory opened the public hearing at 7:52pm.

     b.     Staff Presentation
            Shauna stated that while visiting with the City Attorney, he suggested that the
            verbiage is still a little open to interpretation. She said that cleaning up the
            the code will make it clearer for applicants and for the City.

     c.     Public Comment/Close Public Hearing
            Seeing no public comment, Cory closed the hearing at 7:53pm.

     d.     Action Item - Motion
            Cory asked for a motion to Clean up code language in all zones to include the
            verbiage, “whichever is smaller” when referencing the maximum height and
            minimum allowed stories in each zone’s maximum building height for better
            clarity.

            Motion: John Ford Second: Alan Hancock Outcome: Passed Unanimously

5.   Land Use Training presented by the Office of Property Rights Ombudsman (An
     office in the Utah Department of Commerce) Presenters: Jordan Cullimore and
     Rob Terry.

     Special training is being provided for the Commission, City Council, & City Council
     candidates by the Utah Office of the Property Rights Ombudsman. Presenters are
     Jordan Cullimore (Riverton), Lead Attorney and Rob Terry (South Jordan), Statewide
     Land Use Training Director. The Ombudsman’s office is independent and neutral. The
     entire charge behind the office is to protect the property rights of the citizens and act as
     a resource for citizens. The office is government funded, but doesn’t represent the
     government. The areas that the Ombudsman’s office covers is land use and planning,
     and eminent domain. Jordan and Rob explained the importance, and the duties of, a
     Planning Commission. They also explained the difference between legislative and

     administrative decisions. The remainder of the time was used for a question and answer
     period, where those in attendance were able to pick from a variety of topics. Those
     discussed were impact fees, property line disputes, roads by public dedication, the
     “illegal” subdivision of lots, and public input vs clamor.
