Elected officers of the City of West Jordan (“City”) are required to comply with the provisions and requirements outlined
in West Jordan Code § 1-11-1, ef seg. (including Article A), Utah Code §§ 10-3-1301, et seg. (Municipal Officers’ and
Employees’ Ethics Act), and Utah Code §§ 67-16-1, et seg. (Utah Public Officers’ and Employees’ Ethics Act). Elected
officials are required to complete this form and submit it to the City Recorder between January 1-31 each calendar
year. Pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-1313(5), any elected official who fails to complete and submit this form
within seven calendar days after receiving notice of their failure to do so is guilty of a class B misdemeanor,
will be reported to the attorney general, and is subject to a $100 fine.
