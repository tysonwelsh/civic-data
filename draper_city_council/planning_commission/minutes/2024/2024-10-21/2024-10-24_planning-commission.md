         A.     Public Meeting: Lone Peak Canyon Subdivision Amended Plat Request
                (Administrative Action)
                On the request of Pratt Dimond, representing Lance and Erica Stewart, a request
                for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment to Consolidate Lots for
                Approximately 1.07 Acres located at 14691 South Canyon Peak Drive. Known as
                Application 2024-0075-SUB. Staff Contact: Todd Taylor, 801-576-6510,
                todd.taylor@draperutah.gov.

City Planner, Todd Taylor presented the Staff Report and displayed vicinity and aerial maps of the
subject property. The current Land Use designation is Medium-Density Residential and the zoning
is Multiple Dwelling Units Residential. The proposed plat would combine Lots 40 and 41 into a
new Lot 73. The existing Internal public utility easements would be vacated, but all other
easements would remain.

In addition to the standard Conditions of Approval, Staff recommended the following three
additional conditions:

     •   That a wrought iron fence be installed along the back of the entire lot, per the Development
         Agreement, prior to recordation of the plat.

       B.      Public Hearing: Joco Jewelry Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
               Request. (Administrative Action)
               On behalf of Joely Larson, Representing Eric Larson and Alyson Larson, a request
               for a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit to operate a Home Occupation that
               creates custom jewelry, on approximately 0.47 acres, located at 12983 South Fox
               Crossing Lane. Known as Application 2024-0338-USE, Staff Contact: Nick
               Whittaker, (801) 576-6522, Nick.Whittaker @Draperutah.gov.

City Planner, Nick Whittaker presented the Staff Report and stated that because the business will
not have customers coming to the home, no additional parking is required. The Land Use is
Residential Low-Medium Density and the zoning is Residential Agricultural. Site and floor plans
were reviewed. The business makes custom jewelry that is sold online. The workspace is
approximately 80 square feet in the kitchen area of the home. Negative impacts were extremely
unlikely given the scale of the business operation but the proposed mitigation was to maintain the
current scale of operations by requiring that customers not come to the property and that business-
related production of jewelry be confined to the designated workspace as illustrated in the floor
plans found in the Staff Report.

In response to a question raised by Chair Adams, the applicant Joely Larson confirmed that orders
are taken online or off-site for shipment and no bulk deliveries are received.

Commissioner Shah supported those who do things the right way and congratulated Ms. Larson
on her business.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. No public comments were received. The public hearing
was closed.

Motion: Commissioner Nixon moved to APPROVE the Home Occupation Conditional Use
Permit as requested by Joely Larson, based on the following Findings and subject to the
Conditions listed in the Staff Report dated October 11, 2024.

Findings for Approval:

       1.      The proposal complies with the standards for approval found in DCMC
               Section 9-5-080(E) and potential negative impacts are mitigated through the
               imposition of reasonable conditions.

Second: Commissioner Squire seconded the motion.

Vote on motion: 5-0 in favor.

       C.      Public Hearing: Jason Smith Fence Conditional Use Permit Request.
               (Administrative Action)
               On behalf of Jason Smith, representing Jason Smith and Misty Smith, a Conditional
               Use Permit to allow for a fence taller than six feet (6’) in height, on approximately
               0.459 acres, located at 934 East Crosswind Way. Known as Application 2024-
               0316-USE,       Staff    contact:      Nick      Whittaker,      (801)      576-6522,
               Nick.Whittaker@Draperutah.gov.

Commissioner Shah recused himself from the above item as he has a business relationship with
the applicant.

Mr. Whittaker presented the Staff Report and stated that the applicant is requesting a fence height
of up to eight feet. Vicinity and aerial maps of the property were displayed. The Land Use
designation is Residential Low-Medium Density, and the property is in the RA-2 zone.
Mr. Whittaker reviewed the Site Plan, which shows an eight-foot fence along the east and west
sides of the property and seven feet on the other sides. There are abrupt grade changes along the
property line that cause the fence to appear lower at certain points.

The identified potential negative impact is the visual impact of the fence height exceeding six feet
and the proposed mitigation is to require that the fencing not exceed eight feet in height and require
that the additional height only be constructed in the locations indicated in the Site Plan included
in the Staff Report.

In response to a question from Chair Adams, Mr. Whittaker reported that the current vinyl fencing
will be replaced with Trex fencing. Where grading permits, the fencing will remain at the six-foot
height.

The applicant, Jason Smith thanked the Planning Commission for considering his request and
stated that, although he loves his neighbors, he also loves his privacy. He hoped to install a high-
quality fence that also benefits the neighbors. The property slopes both from east to west and north
to south. The slope is gradual. In response to a question raised by Chair Adams, Mr. Smith verified
that a section at the rear of the property would retain the existing vinyl fencing. Currently, the
fencing along the east and west sides is different from the fencing on the south side.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing.
