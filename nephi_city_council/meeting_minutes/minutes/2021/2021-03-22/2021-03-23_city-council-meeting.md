                                             Meeting Minutes
                                          Work Session 03-23-21
                                                 7:00 p.m.
                                        Nephi City Council Chambers

    1. Leon Roullard, owner of Skydive the Wasatch, reported on his involvement with the airport. He said
       that he has been working with City Administrator Seth Atkinson and City Attorney Kasey Wright on a
       lease agreement for the old Blake Garrett home. He is currently occupying the home. He reported
       that his business is growing and doing well. He expressed his appreciation to the council for working
       with him and for getting a water connection down at the airport.
    2. Mr. Atkinson gave an update on the recreation center project. Mr. Atkinson reported that the center
       would give the recreation department some office space plus our own gym space for city recreation
       programs. Juab School District Superintendent Kodey Hughes reported that the proposed recreation
       center project will add seven classrooms, a weight room and gym space. He said there is a real need
       for the building for the school district.
         The private donors are donating a fully enclosed shell of the building. The project cost estimates far
       exceeded what had originally been estimated because of the large increase in the cost of building
       materials over the past few months. Steve Carter who is working as the project liaison offered some
       suggestions of ways to cut costs.
         Additional funding sources such as grants, COVID relief funds, Community Impact Board funding,
       tournament revenue, recreation impact fees, and other donations were discussed as possible ways to
       make up the deficit in funding.
         All councilors agreed to direct staff to move forward with the project. Superintendent Hughes said
       that the original timetable would have to be revised and the project would move slower as additional
       funding if obtained.
    3. Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council a discussion he has had with the developer of the Red Cliffs
       Estates subdivision to enter into an agreement to trade of the developer paving a parking lot and a
       paying for a water connection in exchange of the city vacating a portion of the road easement for a
       road that would never be needed. The council directed staff to move forward with drafting an
       agreement.
    4. The council directed staff to move forward on the parks, recreation, and natural gas impact fees
       analysis.
    5. The council and administration gave the following updates:
            a. Councilor Seely would like to provide different sights that were interesting within the city
                where tourists could take selfies.
            b. Councilor Memmott would like the airport advertised on the digital billboard.
            c. Councilor Ostler thanked the council for their help with the Miss Nephi Pageant.
            d. Councilor Jones reported that the bell from Pioneer Park had been moved to Town Square
                Park and that the old cabin at Pioneer Park is being restored by the Daughters of the Utah
                Pioneers.
            e. Mr. Atkinson reported on a positive meeting with UDOT Region III officials.

    6.   Councilor Ostler moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and competence of
         an individual. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

    7. The meeting adjourned at 8:30 p.m.




Present: Mayor Glade Nielson, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Justin Seely, Kasey
Wright, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Craig Oswald, Leon Roullard, Kodey Hughes, Steve Carter, Jeremy Lynn,
Lynsey Cuff, Randy Cuff.
