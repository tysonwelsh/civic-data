  APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 04.060. Criteria for Approval.
No amendment to this Code, or the Zoning District Map(s), may be recommended for approval by the Commission
nor approved by the City Council unless such amendment or conditions thereto are consistent with the General Plan.
In considering a Code amendment, or Zoning District Map(s) amendment, the applicant shall identify, and the City
Staff, DRC, Commission, and the City Council may consider the following factors, among others:

    1.   The effect of the proposed amendment on the character of the surrounding area;
    2.   Consistency with the goals and policies of the General Plan.
    3.   Consistency and Compatibility of the proposed zone with the General Plan land uses of nearby and adjoining
         properties.
    4.   The suitability of the properties for the uses requested and their suitability for the existing uses identified by
         the General Plan;
    5.   Whether a change in the uses allowed for the affected properties will unduly affect the uses, or proposed uses
         for nearby and adjoining properties;
    6.   The gain to the public health, safety and welfare from the existing classification to the proposed amendment;
         and
    7.   The overall community benefit of the proposed amendment.


  REPORT ANALYSIS

Staff recommends amending Chapter 11 and Figure 15 of the Development Code to adjust and clarify the approval process
for two-lot subdivisions with an existing home. The proposed code amendment would remove the requirement that they
require approval by the Planning Commission and make it a review and approval by the Reviewing Departments. However,
if the project were a flag lot it would still need conditional use approval. Oftentimes the two applications (two-lot
subdivision with an existing home and conditional use for a flag lot) have been heard together with separate motions for
each item at Planning Commission. This code amendment will still give the public the opportunity to comment on any
potential negative impacts during a public hearing for the conditional use permit.

Additionally, the update to Figure 15 is just to clearly show the timeline of approval.

During an internal staff meeting, Engineering staff requested that an addition be made to another section of Chapter 11 to
clarify that bonds may be accepted from banks outside of Utah.

  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development Code Amendments are legislative items and also public hearings. The public can share their opinions
on the amendments and recommend any changes. Ultimately, they would need the Planning Commission to include
the proposed changes in their motion.


  POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS

The Planning Commission may move to recommend approval or denial of the proposed amendments to the Lehi
City Development Code. Any motion should include findings. Possible findings include:

    1.   The proposed Development Code amendment is/is not in conformance with the purposes, intent, and
         provisions of the General Plan and its various elements.
    2.   Additional findings based upon information presented during the public hearing.

Narrative –Chapter 11 & Figure 15 Development Code Amendment

The Planning Division is proposing a Development Code Amendment to Chapter 11 and Figure
15 of the Development Code. Specifically, we are proposing an adjustment to the Two-Lot
Subdivision with an Existing Home standards to remove the requirement that they require
approval by the Planning Commission. However, flag lots would still be required to go through
the standard process for a Conditional Use approval, including a Public Hearing at Planning
Commission.

This change would clarify and simplify the process. For two-lot subdivisions that are not flag
lots, the process would just require DRC approval based on plans meeting the Development
Code. For a flag lot, the public would still have the opportunity to bring up any potential
negative impacts during the Public Hearing. In the past, these were two separate items with a flag
lot conditional use and a two-lot subdivision with an existing home. They have often been heard
together at Planning Commission with separate motions for each item.

Please contact the Planning Division if you have any questions.
