                                                Public Comment Procedure
This is an open meeting, which the public may attend. However due to restrictions related to COVID-19, public attendance to
this meeting may be limited in number. The staff report will be available for public review through the City website prior to the
meeting. Audio and video of the meeting will be broadcast live. Audio and written minutes will be available following the
meeting. Public comment for this item may be submitted via email to planning@rivertonutah.gov or by phone to 801-208-3138.
All comments submitted before 5:00 pm on the date of the hearing will be relayed to the Planning Commission.

Riverton City Meeting information: https://www.rivertonutah.gov/meetings/index.php. This link will take you to the applicable
meeting date and to the meeting packet, where you can review all items scheduled for review by the Planning Commission.
