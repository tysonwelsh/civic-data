PUBLIC HEARING/VACATE SEGMENT OF ROADWAY/ORDINANCE:
     Public hearing and consideration of Ordinance No. 2024-074 to vacate a
     segment of the existing roadway on Five Sisters Drive, located on the south
     side of its intersection with Howard Lane (at approximately 1840 East and
     1030 South).

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This roadway was dedicated as part of the
      Foremaster Ridge Subdivision, which was approved by City Council on the 18th day
      of August, 1994 and recorded in the Office of the Washington County Recorder on
      the 16th day of March, 1995. It has been determined that this roadway will not be
      constructed due to the steep slope of the hillside from Foremaster Ridge to Riverside
      Drive. As such, it is proposed that this roadway stub out be vacated (south leg of the
      intersection at Five Sisters Drive and Howard Lane). The Joint Utility Commission
      recommended approval.

PD AMENDMENT/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of Ordinance 2024-076 amending the city zoning map by
     amending the existing Desert Color Planned Development Commercial (PD-
     C) zone on approximately 1.31 acres located at the terminus of Black
     Mountain Drive on the north side of the road. (Case No. 2024-PDA-023 -
     Panda Express at Desert Color).

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: There have been several approvals for
      commercial development within Desert Color. The purpose of the amendment is to
      approve a new building in the Desert Color development with conceptual site plan,
      landscape plans, materials, and elevations, generally located at the terminus of Black
      Mountain Drive on the north side of the road, to be known as Panda Express at
      Desert Color. At their meeting held on November 12, 2024, the Planning
      Commission held a public hearing and voted unanimously to recommend approval of
      the application, with conditions.

      Link to introduction from City Manager John Willis and presentation from Planner Dan
      Boles: 01:15:46
