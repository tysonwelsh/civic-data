Chair Jacob called the meeting to order at 8:44 pm and noted that Council Member Bloom was
participating via Zoom.


 2. DISCUSSION TOPICS
       a. Discussion of amendments to Title 2 – Executive Branch Committees
          The Council last discussed amendments to Title 2 and gave direction to staff on February
          21, 2024. Council Office Director Alan Anderson outlined changes made. Council
          Member Whitelock requested that “Council Office Director” change to “Council Office” on
          line 439. The Council discussed committee/commission terms of service. There
          appeared to be consensus regarding two terms of any length, moving 10-3-3 to Title 2,
          and moving the reference of $25 per diem for the Board of Adjustment to the salary
          schedule. Mr. Anderson said the changes would be included on a future Council meeting
          agenda for approval.

       b. Discussion of amendments to Title 1 Chapter 8 – Administrative
          Mr. Anderson said the Council last gave direction regarding Title 1 Chapter 8 on February
          21, 2024. He outlined changes made. Council Member Green expressed the opinion that
          the word “shall” had been used too much, suggested formatting should be parallel, and
          wanted to work with staff to clean up grammar throughout the language. No Council
          Members voiced opposition.

          The Council discussed language on line 584 regarding special functions. Council Member
          Green asked that staff clean up the wording for clarity. He suggested adding “parks and
          recreation” to the list of special functions, to enable funding in the future without changes
          to the code. City Administrator Korban Lee believed adding “recreation” to the list of
          special functions that could be funded in the future was a good idea.

        c. Discussion regarding Changing or Removing the Senior Housing Overlay
           District (SHO)
           Mr. Anderson outlined proposed changes to the code regarding Senior Housing Overlay
           Districts (SHO). Council Members Whitelock and Green did not like the proposed
           threshold. The Council discussed resale of properties within an SHO. Council Member
           Green suggested line numbering should be added, and references to the Utah Code should
           be standardized. Mr. Anderson said language would be brought back to the Committee of
           the Whole for further discussion.
