                                 TOWN OF ALTA

                             RESOLUTION 2022-R-3

     A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING INDIVIDUALS TO SIGN CHECKS
           FOR THE TOWN OF ALTA ZION BANK ACCOUNTS

WHEREAS, the Town Council finds it necessary to name four authorized signers to
make changes and access Zions Bank checking accounts,

WHEREAS, the Town Council finds that the following individuals are both trusted and
conveniently available to sign checks when necessary, and

WHEREAS, the Town Council desires to certify the following two staff and two council
members as authorized individuals.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Alta Town Council hereby certifies
that the following four individuals may sign checks, authorize changes to the Town’s
sewer and water accounts, open or close accounts and execute any necessary forms in
connection with such changes on behalf of the Town of Alta.

Roger Bourke         Mayor
Elise Morgan         Council member
John Guldner         Treasurer/Town Administrator
Chris Cawley         Assistant Town Administrator




ADOPTED THIS 26th day of January, 2022.



                                             ____________________________
                                             Roger Bourke, Mayor
