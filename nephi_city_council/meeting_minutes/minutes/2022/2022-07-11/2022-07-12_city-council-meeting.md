                             Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                       July 12, 2022
                                         7:00 p.m.
                                 21 E 100 N, Nephi, UT

1. City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed a development agreement between the City
   and The Wright Direction LLC. The agreement deals with the development of what is
   commonly known as the Greenhalgh Property. Councilor Memmott questioned why we
   would spend money to construct the infrastructure for the ball fields lot and the police
   department lot long before we can afford to finish those projects. The council directed
   staff to make some revisions to the agreement addressing the issue of constructing the
   infrastructure to those two lots.

2.    The council discussed the DWR land swap draft agreement. The agreement requires
     two closing dates in order to accommodate the federal agencies involved in the land
     swap. The Bureau of Land Management has a reversionary clause on the property that
     requires the property return to BLM ownership if transferred. Staff is working to keep the
     reversionary clause on the property but allow the swap to take place. Change 10E
     agreement section 13 should say section 12.

3.   The council discussed the Water Right Services Agreement with Sunrise Engineering.
     The council directed staff to bring back the agreement for approval at the next regular
     meeting.

4.    Mr. Atkinson and Power Superintendent Rust Finlinson updated the council about a
     business that had approached the City about locating here and connecting to our 138kv
     power line and would be an extremely large power consumer.

5.    Mr. Atkinson said that the 138 kv powerline project may not be completed before the
     demand for power exceeds what we can provide with our current system.. Therefore,
     there may be a need to have a temporary power source to cover the peak demand
     periods. Power Superintendent Rust Finlinson explained that he is examining using
     generators on the south end of the city powered by natural gas that would require
     installing a south natural gas gate station.

6. The council discussed the idea of purchasing security cameras for the parks and utility
   yards.

7.    Councilor Memmott asked who is responsible for maintaining retention ponds that turn
     into weed patches. Mr. Atkinson answered that the retention ponds are deeded to the
     City and are owned by the City and the City should maintain the ponds.

8.    Councilor Ostler asked about a log home on 100 North between 400 and 500 East that
     he thought was not to be occupied and should only have been used as a model home.
        Council directed staff to research the agreements file to see if there were some
        restrictions on the property.

   9.    Work Session adjourned at 9:52 p.m. Mayor Seely opened a regular council meeting.
        Councilor Worwood moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character
        and competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Parady seconded the
        motion, which passed unanimously.

   10. At 9:25 p.m. the council reconvened into regular session. Councilor Parady moved to
       adjourn. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.
