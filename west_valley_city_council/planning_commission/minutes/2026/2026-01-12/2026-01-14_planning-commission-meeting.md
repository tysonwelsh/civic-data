ZT-6-2025
West Valley City
A zone text change to update the Jordan River Overlay Zone to be consistent with the
rest of the Zoning Ordinance

Staff is proposing a zoning ordinance amendment to amend Sections 7-14-201 through 7-14-
206 to update the Jordan River Overlay Zone to be consistent with the rest of the Zoning
Ordinance. The Jordan River Overlay Zone was first adopted in April of 1998 as Ordinance #98-
35.

In 2017, the City adopted a comprehensive update to the Zoning Ordinance (Title 7 of City
Code). Part of the intent of this update was to eliminate vague or subjective terms, remove
duplication, and to standardize appeal processes. At the time of the update, the City was
involved in litigation on property within the Jordan River Overlay Zone. Due to this litigation,
the City chose not to amend the Jordan River Overlay Zone at that time. However, the litigation
is now over, and staff recommends that the Jordan River Overlay Zone be updated to be
consistent with the changes made to the Zoning Ordinance in 2017.

Staff Alternatives:
   1. Approval. The ordinance change should be approved.

   2. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the
      public hearing.
   3.

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented. He addressed Commissioner Winters Study Meeting
question about the building site standards in other overlay zones, stating that similar language
is used in other overlay zones on Bangerter and 5600 West.



Motion: Commissioner Winters motioned to approve ZT-6-2025. Commissioner Durfee
seconded. A voice vote was taken, and all were in favor of the motion.



                             UNANIMOUS—ZT-6-2025—APPROVED

Utah First Credit Union Amphitheater Pole Sign
5219 S Upper Ridge Rd
M Zone (36.22 Acres)

The applicant, YESCO LLC, is requesting conditional use approval for a pole sign which will
be located within one of the parking lot areas for the Utah First Credit Union

Amphitheater. The parcel where the sign will be placed is 36.22 acres and is zoned M,
Manufacturing. The West Valley City General Plan designates this area as light
manufacturing. The surrounding zoning is M. The amphitheater concert area, other
overflow parking lots, the Ridge Golf Course, and the Mountain View Corridor are all
adjacent to this parcel.

The applicant, on behalf of the West Valley City Redevelopment Agency (RDA), is
proposing to install a pole sign in the center of an existing drop-off area located near the
main entrance to the concert venue. West Valley City owns the parking lot area where the
sign will be going. The RDA is proposing a sign to promote the City, to create a gateway
for the concert venue, and to communicate community events and information to the
public.

The property is over 10 acres, which means that pole signage is allowed. There is currently
no permanent ground signage on the parcel. The sign will be 20’ tall and include
approximately 200 sq. ft. of sign area. The proposed sign includes a 120 sq. ft. electronic
message center (EMC). The proposed sign will be within the code allowance for pole signs,
which is 200 sq. ft. and 35’ in height. The sign code typically restricts electronic messaging
on a pole sign to 50% of the overall sign area, but Section 11-4-109(4), Criteria for Bonus
Sign Area, does allow for the Planning Commission to approve an alternative sign plan or
design for unique and special circumstances.

The unique circumstance with the proposed sign is the advertisement for community
events and other City information. That type of content will always be changing and can be
more easily delivered using electronic messaging. The EMC shall not display any off-
premises advertising (11-1-104(16)).

Title 11 requires that all pole signs be incorporated in a landscaped area that is at least
equal to twice the size of the sign. The area around the existing sign is over 4,000 sq. ft. The
RDA plans to re-do the landscaping around the new signage, which includes a new circular
entry wall feature and new rock mulch. They are requesting that no live plant material be
required around the sign as it is a very large area and it would be difficult to get water to it
for irrigation.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing, as well as
the following conditions:
1. One pole sign is allowed on the property and shall be constructed per the approved
   sign plan.

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented the application. He clarified that the sign could be used
for both amphitheater events and City-related messaging and would only be turned on during
events. Brock also explained the sign's dimensions and electronic panel specifications.

Commissioner Winters asked Brock if the pillars are part of the sign area calculation. Brock
replied that it depends on how the sign is measured, but that they could be included in the
sign area. The overall sign can be up to 200 square feet, and the EMC panel is 120 square feet,
so the EMC is 20 square feet more than the code allows, but the Planning Commission could
allow this based on the unique circumstance of the sign.

The Economic Development Director for the City, Jonathan Springmeyer, said that the
amphitheater gets between 600,000 and 750,000 visitors annually and many of those attendees
have no idea that they're in West Valley City. He wants to make sure that visitors know that the
good time they had happened in West Valley City.

Commissioner Durfee asked Jonathan if the sign would face due west or a little bit south,
because most of the traffic comes in from the south. Jonathan answered that the sign would
face north-south as that's a walking path and entrance into the venue. Ideally, if RDA wanted to
spend twice as much money, they would probably do two signs to capture the west, towards
the 6400 West road. Commissioner Durfee then asked Jonathan if they intended to add some
color to the rocks and mulch because it’s mostly grey tones. Jonathan said that that would be
their intent.

Motion: Commissioner Durfee motioned to approve C-22-2025. Commissioner Woodruff
seconded. A voice vote was taken all were in favor of the motion.

C-23-2025
A.D Constructors (Outside Storage)
2258 S. Moody Drive
M Zone (.67 acres)

The applicant, A.D Constructors, is requesting conditional use approval for outside storage for
a warehouse/office located at 2258 S. Moody Drive, on lot 4 of the Moody Subdivision (ZSMI-
12-2022). This property is zoned M, Manufacturing and the West Valley City General Plan
designates this area as Light Manufacturing. The surrounding zone is Manufacturing on all
sides. The surrounding uses include outside storage, warehouse, office and light
manufacturing.

This lot is located on a private street that serves this development. The site will be occupied by
A.D Constructors, which is a general building contractor. There will be a building with an office
(2600 SF) and warehouse (5400 SF) as well as outside storage.

The outside storage area is set back off the private roadway behind the parking area and
adjacent to the building. The front of the storage area will be screened with a 6’ masonry wall
to match the building elements. The sides and rear will be chain-link, and the south side will
need slats to screen the yard from public view. The storage area will be hard surfaced with
asphalt and will at times be used for small utility and flatbed trailers, scissor lifts, job site
storage/toolboxes, small amounts of scaffolding. In general, the types of things a contractor
would store or park in a secure area.

The building exterior is constructed of architectural ribbed metal panels with a CMU block
wainscot. There are steel columns and horizontal elements provided in the elevations for some
building and roof line relief. The exterior elevations will comply with the Commercial Design
Standards based off the tenant use for this site. As discussed in the Study Meeting, the north
elevation should have the design elements concentrated towards the top of the building since
the adjacent outside storage use is enclosed with a 6’ screen fence.

There is a 10’ utility easement along the frontage of this private road which also serves as the
landscape setback for this development. There are also additional landscaped areas adjacent to
the building. A minimum of 5% landscaping is required for each site, and the landscaping must
have a minimum of 1 tree every 30’ of frontage and at least 50% coverage of live plant material
not including tree canopies.
