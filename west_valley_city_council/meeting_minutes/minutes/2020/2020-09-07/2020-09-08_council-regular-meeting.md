RESOLUTION 20-145: APPROVE A PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
WITH AVENUE CONSULTANTS, INC. FOR A STUDY OF TRAFFIC PATTERNS
AND FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS ON BANGERTER HIGHWAY
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-145 that would approve a Professional
Services Agreement with Avenue Consultants, Inc. for a study of traffic patterns and future
improvements on Bangerter Highway.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       UDOT is converting all at-grade interchanges on Bangerter Highway to freeway
       style grade-separated interchanges. Please see the attached memo for additional
       information regarding UDOT’s Bangerter Highway schedule.

       Concerns have been expressed about the effects on the City’s transportation system
       and adjacent properties, including: effects on east-west traffic, possible closure of
       some current Bangerter Highway accesses, possible construction of one-way
       frontage roads, impacts on businesses and neighborhoods, loss of landscaping
       adjacent to the corridor, impact to Stonebridge Golf Course, coordination with
       active transportation plans crossing the corridor, and planning for new growth in
       Lake Park Business Center.

       These are all legitimate concerns which normally would be evaluated during the
       UDOT environmental process, at UDOT’s expense. Past experience on the
       Mountain View Corridor project has shown that City staff and officials would have
       numerous opportunities during the environmental process to express concerns and
       work with UDOT to find acceptable solutions. The City’s study would be at least
       partially redundant, since UDOT will conduct its own current traffic data collection
       and analysis and develop concept designs.

       The advantage for the City in conducting its own high-level study, would be to get
       a head start on the process, and enable the City to predetermine preferences and
       recommend solutions to UDOT.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City
Council, and he called for a motion.

       Councilmember Buhler moved to approve Resolution 20-145.

       Councilmember Huynh seconded the motion.

       A roll call vote was taken:

       Mr. Fitisemanu                Yes
       Mr. Buhler                    Yes
       Mr. Huynh                     Yes
       Mr. Christensen               Yes
       Mr. Nordfelt                  Yes
       Mayor Bigelow                 Yes

       Unanimous.

RESOLUTION 20-146: APPROVE A COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT BETWEEN
THE CITY AND THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION FOR
FUNDING FOR THE COMPLETION OF 2550 SOUTH
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-146 that would approve a Cooperative
Agreement between the City and the Utah Department of Transportation for funding for
the completion of 2550 South.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Under the 2018 Senate Bill 234 (Utah Inland Port Authority Bill), it was directed
       that the Department of Transportation shall fund, from money designated in the
 MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING – SEPTEMBER 8, 2020

       Transportation Investment Fund for that purpose, the completion of 2550 South
       from 5600 West to 8000 West, with matching funds from the county in which the
       road is located.

       Total funding was in the amount of $4,000,000, with funding coming from UDOT
       and Salt Lake County in equal shares.

       This agreement provides for the final $500,000 from UDOT.

       The Public Works Department will use the funds for the design and construction of
       2550 South. The first segment was completed in 2019. The next phase east of 6000
       West to 2400 South over Mountain View Corridor, is anticipated to be completed
       in 2021.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City

       Mr. Huynh                     Yes
