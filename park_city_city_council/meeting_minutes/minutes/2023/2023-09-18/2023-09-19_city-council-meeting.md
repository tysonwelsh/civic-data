1. Consideration to Adopt Resolution 17-2023, a Resolution of the Board of Canvassers
Certifying the Official Canvassers’ Report from the September 5, 2023, Municipal

Council Member Gerber moved to adopt Resolution 17-2023, a resolution of the Board of
Canvassers certifying the official Canvassers’ Report from the September 5, 2023, Municipal
Primary Election for Park City, Utah. Council Member Toly seconded the motion.
