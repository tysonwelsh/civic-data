CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval of amending the Construction Management/General
        Contractor (CMGC) contract with Big-D Construction for the Parking
        Structure Site work construction phase.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The CMGC process consists of two
          phases: design (pre-construction) and construction. Approval of this agreement
          commits the City to pay for the construction phase of the agreement of the
          Parking Structure Site work construction phase. Staff recommends approval of
          amending the contract.

      b. Consider approval of amending the Construction Management/General
         Contractor (CMGC) contract with Big-D Construction for the City Hall
         Site work construction phase.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The CMGC process consists of two
          phases: design (pre-construction) and construction. Approval of this agreement
          commits the City to pay for the construction phase of the agreement of the City
          Hall Site work construction phase. Staff recommends approval of amending the
          contract.

      c. Consider approval to purchase a Webex Calling subscription in the
         amount of $58,200 to replace the Call Manager enterprise phone system.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The proposal is to replace our current

          Cisco Call Manager phone system and Primary Rate Interface (PRI) phone
          services through CenturyLink/Lumen with Webex Calling and Session Initiation
          Protocol (SIP) phone service through Cisco. WebEx Calling offers the flexibility
          and features we need that are not available from Call Manager without the
          purchase of additional software and upgrades to our current server platform. The
          annual cost of moving to WebEx Calling and Cisco SIP phone service is $50,700.
          This amount is based on the current number of phone accounts and may increase
          as additional employees are added (approximately $124 per user/yr). To
          implement the project, an additional $7,500 is required for installation and
          consulting to be paid to Cache Valley Electric (CVE). The current Cisco Call
          Manager system has been in use over 15 years. Staff recommends approval.
