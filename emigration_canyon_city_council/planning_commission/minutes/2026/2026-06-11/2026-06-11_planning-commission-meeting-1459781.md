ADMINISTRATIVE LAND USE
Meeting began at – 8:31 a.m.
CUP2025-001542 - Gregory Palis is applying for a dwelling group. Location: 6771 East Emigration Cyn
Road. Zone: FR-0.5. Acres: 1.08. Planner: Justin Smith (Discussion, Action)
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Justin Smith provided an analysis of the application.
Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Gregory Palis
Address: 6771 East Emigration Canyon Road
Comments: Mr. Palis said there are no utilities going to the shed and the shed is for storage. The garage is
still in the process of being completed. The approval was 2 ½ years ago. The upstairs is empty and approved
for storage. Fitting storm water and wastewater on two separate lots, more plans for a system down canyon.
Health department has looked at it but has not given consensual. Last time here, quite clearly this was the
intention for the structure. Long term intention is to rebuild the original dwelling. Need all appropriate
approvals from outside agencies.
