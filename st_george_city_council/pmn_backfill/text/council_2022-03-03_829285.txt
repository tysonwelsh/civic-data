CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to Holbrook Asphalt, Inc. for HA5 seal coat
        for various trails and parking lots within the City.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This is a sole source award. The HA5
          product is used to put a seal over newer asphalt trails and parking lots to slow the
          damage from oxidation. The treatment is used to extend the life of the asphalt
          surfaces. It has been used throughout the City with excellent results. Staff
          recommends awarding the bid to Holbrook Asphalt, Inc in the amount of
          $183,769.11.

      b. Consider approval to award bid to Feller Enterprises for the construction
         of the Virgin River Emergency Watershed 2021 Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This project includes construction of
          riprap erosion control and stream restoration in an area along the northerly bank
          of the Virgin River upstream of Man of War Bridge and downstream of I-15. Funds
          for this project come from the Natural Resource Conservation Service (NRCS),
          75%, and the Washington County Flood Control Authority, 25%. Staff
          recommends awarding the bid to Feller Enterprises in the amount of
          $1,539,053.62.

      c. Consider approval of a reimbursement agreement with the Washington
         County Flood Control Authority for the purchase of flood control easement
         to construct the Abberley Farms Detention Basin.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This agreement is for a flood control and
          drainage easement acquisition near 3210 East and Seegmiller Dr in the Little
          Valley area to construct a stormwater detention basin. The construction of the
          basin will be completed eventually with funding from the NRCS under the Warner
          Draw Watershed project. However, the city is responsible for acquiring the
          underlying property. Staff recommends approval of the agreement.

      d. Consider approval of a reimbursement agreement with the Washington
         County Flood Control Authority for the purchase of property to construct
         the Arbors Detention Basin.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This agreement is for property acquisition
          near 3000 East and Seegmiller Drive in the Little Valley area to construct a
          stormwater detention basin. The construction of the basin will be completed
          eventually with funding from the NRCS under the Warner Draw Watershed project.
          However, the city is responsible for acquiring the underlying property. Staff
          recommends approval of the agreement.

      e. Consider approval of an Agreement to Purchase Real Property and
         Easements from Abberley Farm, LLC, and reimburse Abberley Farm, LLC
         for installation of improvements.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City desires to acquire property for a
          roadway and storm water detention basin including the installation of roadway
          improvements including pavement, curbing, sidewalk, drainage facilities, and other
          applicable roadway improvements. The cost of the property and easements
          amount to $291,500 and the cost of improvements amounts to $137,816.71, for a
          total of $429,316.71. Staff recommends approval.

ORDINANCE/AMEND FEES FOR REUSE CENTER:
     Consider approval of an ordinance amending fees for services rendered at the
     Reuse Center.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The WCSW District has increased their
      "tipping" fees at the landfill to residential and commercial users, including
      municipalities. This proposed ordinance to adjust the Reuse Center fees is to match
      corresponding fees at the landfill and to encourage commercial users to use the landfill
      rather than the Reuse Center. Due to limited space/land at the Reuse Center,
      operations become inundated with commercial users, and when this occurs, City crews
      are unable to keep up with the amount of green waste dumped at the Reuse Center.
      Staff recommends approval of the ordinance amending the fees for the Reuse Center.

      Link to item presentation from Public Works Director Cameron Cutler: 00:52:40
