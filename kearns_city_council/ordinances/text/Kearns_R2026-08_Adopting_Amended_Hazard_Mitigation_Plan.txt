                                  KEARNS CITY COUNCIL

        RESOLUTION NO. R2026-08                               DATE: February 9,2026

  A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL ADOPTING THE AMENDED
       SALT LAKE COUNTY MULTI.JURISDICTIONAL HAZARD PLAN

         WHEREAS, the City of Keams is exposed to a variety of natural hazards, including but
not limited to earthquakes, fires, and severe weather events, all of which pose signihcant risks to
life, property, and critical infrastructure within Keams; and

         WHEREAS, proactivehazard mitigation planning enables Kearns to identiff and assess
the risks associated with these hazards and to implement measures to reduce their potential impacts
to protect its citizens and to build long-term resilience; and

       WHEREAS, the 2025 Salt Lake County Multi-Jurisdictional Hazard Mitigation Plan (the
"PIan") was adopted on September 8, 2025 via Resolution R2026-10; and

         WHEREAS, the Plan has been amended to continue meeting federal requirements and
reflects Kearns's commitment to hazard risk reduction; and

        WHEREAS, the amended Plan provides a blueprint assessing vulnerability, prioritizing
mitigation actions, and identifring local, state, and federal funding sources to implement those
actions; and

        WHEREAS, the Keams City Council has finds that it is in the best interests of the health,
safety, and welfare of the citizens of Kearns to adopt the amended Plan.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL:

       1.      The amended Plan attached to this Resolution is adopted as the official hazard.
mitigation plan of the City of Kearns.

        2. Any and all other resolutions or policies, excluding ordinances, that are in conflict
with the Plan are repealed, including but not limited to Resolution R2025-10.

       3.       The provisions of the amended Plan are severable and, if any section, subsection,
table, clause, or phrase of the amended Plan is declared invalid for any reason, the remaining
portions of the amended Plan shall remain in full force and effect.

       4.      This Resolution and the amended Plan shall take effect immediately.
