ELECT CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FOR 2024
Chairman Nordfelt stated election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson for 2025 had
been scheduled for consideration by the Board. He advised a rotation schedule had been
used in the past to elect a Chair and Vice Chair, however this was an option for the Board
and not mandatory.

Chairman Nordfelt called for a motion.

Mr. Harmon moved to elect the District 4 appointee as Chair of the Redevelopment Agency
for 2025 and Don Christensen as Vice Chair of the Redevelopment Agency for 2025. Mr.
Whetstone seconded the motion.

A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.


MOTION TO ADJOURN
Upon motion by Mr. Huynh all voted in favor to adjourn.


THERE BEING NO FURTHER BUSINESS OF THE WEST VALLEY
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY THE ANNUAL MEETING OF TUESDAY, JANUARY
14, 2025, WAS ADJOURNED AT 6:59 P.M. BY CHAIRMAN NORDFELT.
