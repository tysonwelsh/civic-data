2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Toby Cordova’s request for review of a zone
change for the Bushman Property, 7.16-acres located at 1610 West 700 South changing the
zoning from RA-1 (residential/agriculture) to R-1-Flex (residential).

2.5) Public hearing and recommendation of Toby Cordova’s request for review of the
Bushman Property Concept, a 21-lot residential development located at 1610 West 700
South.
2.4 and 2.5 were heard simultaneously.

Brittney presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for 2.4

Karl Kamper stated he was here to represent the community. He read an affidavit that was
opposed to the zone change. He stated the zoning is not in compliance or compatible with the
City Code Policy. He offered another recommendation on the zone and offered a copy of the
affidavit. He also stated he wanted to represent himself. He read an affidavit that stated he was
opposed to the zone change that resulted in high density and stated it would have consequences
for the community. He wanted to express that recommendation of approval will not demonstrate
outstanding citizenship of Lehi and he hopes that those that express their concern will be taken
into consideration.

Kevin Clegg stated his backyard will border the new development that is being proposed. When
he bought his property, it was with a farm field behind him. His other concern is destroying the
lifestyle here in Lehi and the rural community. He expressed his concern about the drought,
traffic, and the rapid development. He asked the Commissioners to take the community into
consideration. He would like to see the City limit building permits and submitted
recommendations on how to do that.

Collin Peterson stated he is also opposed to the zone change. He is also concerned about this year
being a drought year and would like to see the City be an example for water conservation. He
stated by allowing flex zoning the developers are being allowed to have a break with utilities.

Gary Smith stated he is also opposed. His concern is what is developed will have an impact on
his neighborhood.

Paul Allred stated he is concerned about water restrictions and would like to see the structure fit
in with existing homes. He is also concerned about traffic and congestion.


Kaysie Christensen stated the surrounding community should benefit from the project. She is
concerned about the area and wondered if the park that is near her home will be utilized by the
new development.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for 2.4

Commissioner Nielsen asked Ross Dinsdale to clarify water rights and the efforts of City
conservation and zone appropriations. Commissioner Everett suggested hearing 2.5
simultaneously.

Brittney presented information on agenda item 2.5

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing 2.5

Jeff Ray stated he is fine with the development but wanted to know what "flex" meant in the
standard code. He would like to keep the development in the character of the neighborhood.

Kevin Clegg stated he strongly disagrees that expansion is controlling the revenue for Lehi and
he rejects that notion.

Karl Kamper stated he would like to see the development fall within the general plan for low
density residential. He also stated when a flex zone is put in a foreign zone is being designated to
the community. He expressed his concern for water usage. He was also concerned about the type
of structure was going to be built.

Paul Allred would like clarification on some of Felt's property.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for 2.5

Ross stated the existing home will have to meet setbacks. The developer decided to keep the
existing home by shifting the road and said the developer would be able to clarify. Toby stated
the large trees would be maintained, along with the Felt home.

2.9) Public hearing and recommendation of Salt Development’s request for review of a zone
change for Sanctuary, changing 9.4 acres from Commercial to TOD located at 2601 West

2.10) Public hearing and recommendation of Salt Development’s request for review of the
Sanctuary Concept Plan including retail and 527 apartment units located at 2601 West
Slipstream Avenue in a proposed TOD zone.
2.9 and 2.10 were heard simultaneously

Brittney presented information on the agenda item.

Ross stated the parking was not ideal because there could be conflicting traffic patterns. Kim
Struthers stated there were two studies from Lehi North and Lehi South. There is an
environmental study that will take approximately 4 to 5 years to complete and fund.
Commissioner Nielsen asked about the resolution. Brittney clarified that for this site the
resolution was amended so TOD development could be entertained. Staff is working with UTA
for active transportation. Ross stated a traffic study has been completed and it included this
development. It included connectivity from Triumph Blvd to the Frontage Road.

The applicant, Ethan Bedingfield, was present. He stated the project was started as a traffic issue
and continued to grow from there. He presented on parking, the commercial adjoining grocer,
tenant space, transit, and connectivity.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing on 2.9

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing on 2.9

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing on 2.10

Thomas Bay, who partners with Salt Development, spoke on the item. He stated they have
worked to create a place for people to go to and relax and have a sanctuary once they get home.
There is a dog park and a sports park. He stated they have a good track record that has a positive
impact and hope to have the concept improved. The goal is to make Compass Point a popular
place to go and hang out.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing on 2.10
