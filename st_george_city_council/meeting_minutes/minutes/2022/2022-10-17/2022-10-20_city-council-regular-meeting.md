          current fencing and gates surrounding the airfield at the airport. An equipment

      b. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Alpha
         Engineering for the design and construction management of the Ft.
         Pierce Sewer Main Replacement Projects- Reach 4.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Sewer Master Plan, which was
          prepared in 2019 identified the need of upsizing the Ft. Pierce sewer outfall line
          from an 18" diameter line to a 36" diameter line to meet the demands of the
          anticipated growth in the Desert Canyons and Little Valley areas. Last year, one
          section of the line was upsized. This project is a continuation of the upsizing.
          This project will include the replacement and upsizing of 8,300 feet of sewer
          outfall line. Staff recommends approval.

      c. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Bowen
         Collins & Associates for the design of the Washington Fields Drainage
         Project - Phase 3.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: With the current development in the
          area, there is a need for this project to be completed. Phases 1 and 2 are
          complete. This project will complete the facility for this region, connecting the
          existing recently installed drain at 1140 South to Merrill Road. Staff recommends
          approval.

         Valley View Drive along the Santa Clara River Tax ID SG-2-26-31181.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This parcel, approximately four (4)
          acres along the Santa Clara River at Valley View will assist the City in connecting
          the Santa Clara River Trail at Cottonwood Cove Park to Mathis Park. Staff
          recommends approval.

APPROVE ST. GEORGE REGIONAL AIRPORT MASTER PLAN:
     Consider approval of the St. George Regional Airport Master Plan.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City approved the hire of McFarland
      Johnson, Inc. to develop the airport master plan on 8/13/2020. This plan has
      evaluated the airport facility needs and requirements for meeting continued growth
      in passenger air service, general aviation and the potential for new/expanded cargo
      and aircraft maintenance operations. The master plan features a scenario-based
      planning approach that will allow the airport to make more informed decisions using
      the data from otherwise unplanned alternatives to create a series of what-if
      scenarios. The additional data use and analysis will provide a greater level of utility
      and shelf-life to a traditional static master plan document. Staff recommends
      approval.

AMEND TITLE 10 OF CITY CODE/ORDINANCE:
    Consider approval of an ordinance amending Title 10 of City code to add
    hobby garage units as a permitted with standards (PS) use in the Planned
    Development Commercial (PD-C) zone and to add the specific standards for
    this use. Case No. 2022-ZRA-003

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This request is to add hobby garages as a
      permitted with standards use to the zoning regulations. At this time, our adopted
      zoning regulations do not address hobby garages as a use. However, we have
      approved hobby garages on two separate occasions in the past. In 2007, case 2007-
      ZC-026, was approved to allow hobby garages to go in at 2180 West 1800 North. In
      2012, case 2012-ZC-019, was approved to allow hobby garages to go in the Sun
      River development off of Blue Grass Way. In both cases the approval was done
      through a planned development commercial zone change. A public hearing for this
      item was held on September 13, 2022. No comments were made. The Planning
      Commission recommended approval on September 27, 2022.
