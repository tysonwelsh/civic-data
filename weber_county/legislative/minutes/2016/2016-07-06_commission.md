                                            MINUTES
                                     WORK SESSION OF THE
                          BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER COUNTY
                                Wednesday, July 6, 2016 - 8:00 a.m.
                      Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah


COMMISSIONERS: Kerry W. Gibson, Matthew G Bell and James Ebert.
OTHERS PRESENT: Courtlan Erickson, Deputy County Attorney; Charles Ewert, of the County Planning
Division; and Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office.


DISCUSSION REGARDING THE PROGRESS OF THE OGDEN VALLEY GENERAL PLAN:

The County Commission held a work session to get updated on the Ogden Valley General Plan. Charles
Ewert, of the County Planning Division, presented this item. The Ogden Valley Planning Commission has
been working on the plan for the last two years. It is anticipated that the County Commission will be hearing
the plan in a public hearing in the next couple of months. Staff wants to make sure the Commission is well
versed in the plan, and acknowledges there may be a need for several work sessions to provide the final
County Commission draft of the plan.

The Commissioners discussed several key points of the plan and asked staff for better clarification.
Discussion revolved around historic preservation efforts; land use programs, specifically TDR’s and the lack
of public support for bonuses as a tool to incentivize TDR’s; transportation, public services, and parks and
recreation.

Of specific concern were some of the strong language directives throughout the plan. The Commission is
interested in providing for the objectives of the plan, but is concerned about balancing all of the various
directives specific to Ogden Valley against competing interests that are county-wide. Mr. Ewert agreed to
review all of the directives of the plan and provide alternative language to soften certain ones. He suggested
reviewing those changes in a second work session and the commissioners agreed.

Chair Gibson invited public comments and none were offered.
