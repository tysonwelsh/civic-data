  APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 11.340. Vacating, Altering, or Amending a Recorded Subdivision Plat. (Amended 01/23/24)

A. The Planning Commission, following review by the Reviewing Departments, shall be the Land Use Authority
for approving any proposed vacation, alteration, or amendment of a subdivision plat, any portion of a subdivision
plat, or any road or lot contained in a subdivision plat. The Planning Commission shall follow all requirements and
procedures identified in Utah State Code Section 10-9a-608.

  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant is requesting approval of the New Star Commercial Subdivision Plat Amendment. The proposed plat
amendment would consolidate the existing lots two and three. These lots are presently vacant and are designated
currently and under the General Plan as commercial.

This amendment is appearing before the council concurrently with the Moto United Site Plan which outlines the
development plan for the two lots. The resulting lot will be 8.458 acres. The lots will have a two-story building that
includes a sales office, service area, and warehouse area, open parking space, and a landscaped display area. 2.618
acres bordering Mill Pond will remain undeveloped.

There were five technical comments from the DRC, including:
    1. Adjust easement along Mill Pond Drive. PUE&RCE required beginning at the back of walk. 10’ PUE
         behind the walk.
    2.   Clarify the interior easements & locations.

Please consider all DRC comments in your motion.
