RESOLUTION NO.: 19-01-01 DATE: January 3, 2019
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP APPOINTING
ALLAN PERRY AS DEPUTY TREASURER OF THE WHITE CITY METRO
TOWNSHIP AND DIRECTING THE DEPUTY TREASURER, MAYOR AND
STAFF TO ESTABLISH THE WHITE CITY BANK ACCOUNT AND TAKE
OTHER ACTIONS NECESSARY TO MANAGE WHITE CITY’S
ADMINISTRATIVE FINANCES
RECITALS

A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The White City Metro Township Council (“Council”) is a Metro Township Council pursuant
to Utah Code §§ 10-3b-501 et al.

C. Pursuant to White City Policy #4, adopted on May, 3, 2018, the Council has decided to
commence management of its own administrative finances independent of the Greater Salt
Lake Municipal Service District and Salt Lake County commencing in 2019.

D. To fulfill one of the requirements of Policy #4, the Council has requested Councilmember
Allan Perry to serve as the Deputy Treasurer of White City.

E. Councilmember Perry has accepted the role of Deputy Treasurer.

F. Pursuant to the federal banking requirements for the establishment of corporate accounts, a
resolution of the Council appointing the Treasurer and others to set up the necessary accounts
is needed.

RESOLUTION

THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the
Council formally appoints Councilmember Allan Perry to serve as the Deputy Treasurer of the
| White City Metro Township; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED the Council hereby directs the Deputy Treasurer, Mayor
and Staff to establish a bank account, PTIF account, and other accounts necessary to manage
White City’s administrative finances; and to take other actions necessary to complete this
financial transition.
