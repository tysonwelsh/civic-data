City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption - 
On November 15, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1568, amending the amending the text of the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code otherwise known as the Lone Peak Marketplace Freeway Sign Text Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder's Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 16th day of November 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
