                                          Nephi City
                                    Work Session Minutes
                                     November 23, 2021
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                 Nephi City Council Chambers




1. President of the Chamber of Commerce Abby Ivory updated the city council about the
   reorganization efforts of the Chamber of Commerce. The new Chamber’s name will be
   “Greater Nephi Chamber of Commerce” and will be set up as a non-profit 501c3. Staff
   recommended the idea of the city giving the chamber a one-time grant of $10,000, if the
   chamber meets certain benchmarks. The funds could be used to hire an executive director.
2.
3. Juab County Tourism Director Shannon Memmott addressed the council and asked for a
   letter of support on a trail project she is working on. The project would be an off-highway
   vehicle regional trail system collaboration with Juab, Millard, and Beaver Counties.

4. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that he had not received information back from the
   city’s financial advisors about the different options for the Hive financing. The agenda item
   was postponed for discussion at a later date.

5. Mr. Atkinson reminded the council about the Illuminate the Night event scheduled for
   November 27th from 5-8 p.m. He also reminded the council about the employee Christmas
   party, Thursday, December 2nd beginning at 6:30 p.m.

6. The council reviewed the financial reports.

7. Councilor Worwood moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
   competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
   motion passed on a unanimous vote.

8. Work Session adjourned at 8:04 pm.

Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielsen, Councilor Larry Ostler, Councilor Justin Seely, Councilor Skip
Worwood, Councilor Nathan Memmott, City Administrator Seth Atkinson, City Attorney Kasey
Wright,, Lisa Brough (Absent), JD Parady, Cory Thomson, Travis Worwood, Clinton Painter,
