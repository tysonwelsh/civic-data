2.    Loveless Apartments Site Plan for Two (2) Four-Plexes
      a.     Staff Presentation
      Shauna Talbot presented the site plan for Loveless Apartments. She stated it’s in
      the R-3 zone and the minimum lot size requirements, frontage requirements, and all
      DRC requirements.Shauna pointed out that some of the recent DRC notes have been
      exhaustive on site plans. She anticipates that when the electronic Zip Flow system is
      incorporated, the notes won’t be as extensive. There will be some forthcoming changes
      as the State is starting to enforce certain site plan requirements for single family homes
      and subdivisions.

      b.      Commission Discussion
      Ann suggested planting several trees on this property. She asked Brett Ludlow when he
      is planning on starting this project; he said it would be within the month. He also stated
      that he hopes to have asphalt by the end of the month.

      Ann asked for a motion to approve the Site Plan for Loveless Apartments Two (2)
      Four-Plexes.

      Motion: Alan Hancock       Second: John Ford      Outcome: Passed Unanimously

3.    Title 10 Updates: 10.7B.8 CU-2 Zone “Height Limitations” accepting public
      comment on the reduction in maximum height allowed in the CU-2 Zone (primary
      residential, with some allowed neighborhood commercial businesses) from 75 feet
      to a proposed 45 feet (3 stories).

     a.    Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the continuation of the public hearing on Title 10 Updates regarding the
     maximum height allowance in the CU-2 zone at 7:10pm.

     b.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna explained that she reviewed the minutes and talked to Seth Atkinson about
     some discussions that were had about this topic. She explained that, at one time, the
     maximum height allowance was 75 feet for both the CU-1 and the CU-2 zones. She also
     reminded the Commission of when they talked about a “candy stripe” approach to zoning
     between 700 South and 900 North.
     Alan pointed out that they were focused on the CU-1 zone at the time. Heather asked if
     the maximum height for CU-2 was missed, to which Shauna replied it must have been
     overlooked. She said there was not much discussion about the CU-2 zone when the
     height allowance for CU-1 was being discussed. Skip asked Shauna if she could tell who
     in City Council had approved the 75 feet by reviewing the minutes from that time period,
     and she said that it doesn’t look like it was really discussed. He agrees that this was
     most likely overlooked and that the topic needs to be addressed. He also remembers
     Council Member Larry Ostler expressing concern over the fire district’s lack of sufficient
     equipment for a building of that height. Joel Cowan, a member of Nephi Fire
     Department, stated that the existing ladder truck is “limping along” and it would be about
     $1.5-$2 million dollars to replace it. Mr. Cowan also stated that the direction the
     Commission goes depends on what they want the city skyline to look like. Skip feels that
     dropping the height to 45 feet reflects the general plan.

     c.     Close Public Hearing
     Ann closed the Public Hearing at 7:18pm.

     d.     Commission Discussion
     Ann asked the Commission if they had any other discussion. Seeing none, she asked for
     a motion to reduce the maximum height allowed in the CU-2 zone from 75 feet to 45
     feet.

     e.    Action Item
     Motion: Heather Robertson Second: John Ford Outcome: Passed Unanimously

4.   Rezone Request - Arrow Builders (Ben & Louis Allred) to rezone their property
     parcel #XA00-0670-2 located at approximately 431 N. Main Street from the CU-1
     zone to the CU-2 zone. (Tabled/Continued from previous month’s meeting)
     a.     Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the Public Hearing at 7:20pm.

     b.    Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented the rezone request from Arrow Builders to rezone their property from

     CU-1 to CU-2. Heather asked if this rezone request could be decided upon before the
     City Council approves the change in maximum height in the CU-2 zone. Shauna
     confirmed that they could reach a decision, as long as it’s contingent upon the City
     Council approving the height change. Heather also asked if the trees on the property are
     protected. Skip & Shauna explained that the Army Corps of Engineers has control over
     the waterway easement. Shauna stated that if the property owners proceed with their
     development, a Geotech study will be required and will need to be followed to the letter.
     Alan expressed confusion over this subject, as they had already decided as a
     Commission how to zone that area of town. Skip asked why the property owners desire
     to rezone to CU-2. Shauna replied that it would enable them to build their duplexes
     horizontally. She stated that from a planning standpoint, this is a good location for this.
     Alan spoke against the change and said that this particular lot is right in downtown
     Nephi. He stated that the Commission had already decided against having multi-family
     or medium density right in the middle of town. Brett Ludlow pointed out that there is a
     multi-family dwelling right across the road. He also pointed out that this property is
     unique because of the Hollow that runs through it. He stated that you will never be able
     to put a commercial building there. If this four-plex isn’t allowed, then all you can do is a
     single family dwelling. Alan expressed concerns over traffic, and Brett said that the
     proposed plan would actually be a better alternative as far as traffic goes. John Ford
     walked the property and stated that his concerns are about the sewer and the traffic. He
     feels there is a solution to the sewer issue, but that would be up to the builder. Much
     discussion was had concerning the pros and cons of the proposed change. Shauna
     suggested that the Commissioners attend the City Council work session next week to
     hear what the Council has to say, and voice any concerns about this proposal.
     Transcript and recording is available for full conversation.

     c.     Close Public Hearing
     Continued.

     d.    Commission Discussion
     See Staff Presentation above.
