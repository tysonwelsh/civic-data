D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Commissioner Bolos: Last week I attended the re-entry simulation out at the complex on 12th Street and it
      was put together by some members of the Local Homeless Council. It was a reality game where you jump
      into the shoes of someone who's just leaving incarceration and you get handed a profile. My profile left jail
      with no identification, no job, rent to pay, and luckily no children to support. You go around to the different
      stations and do things like get a driver's license, buy your food, and apply for a job. When you get a job, you
      start out each session, which represents a week, you start out sitting at that station for two minutes. You're
      spending two minutes of your time working instead of going around to do the other various activities. It was
      a real eye opener and really beneficial to help understand and gain compassion for those individuals who are
      leaving incarceration and trying to get back into the real world. The simulation was put on by our Re-entry
      team. And as you know, we had an appropriations bill at the legislature that was passed that will continue
      that program and add additional case managers who can help those individuals and help us know how to help
      them. It was really insightful and they'll probably be doing it every six months.

     2. Commissioner Harvey: There was a show called Beretta back in the early 70s. The first thing I remember
        out of that song is, “Keep your eye on the sparrow. Don't do the crime if you can't do the time.” Second, I
        was taught as a youth it's a lot easier to stay out of trouble than it is to get out of trouble. We need to re-
        emphasize that because once you're out of trouble you need to be able to get all that stuff and get your life on
        track. It's so it's important for us as leaders to continue working together and getting all the agencies working
        together so people can truly have a good (opportunity to start over). I am absolutely 100% for second
        chances. But I would love to make it a place where people could stay out of trouble meaning that they
        wouldn't make those decisions in the first place but I realize there's a lot of stress.

     3. Chair Froerer: I appreciate Commissioner Bolos bringing up about the funding we have for that program.
        We have potentially a couple of bills going through that will give us additional opportunities to make sure
        that Re-entry program expands and gives a lot of opportunity for those people not to get in the revolving
        door and go back. I'm very optimistic. We've got great support at the state. That's a benefit to everybody in
        the society. Everybody in our community benefits by not putting those people back into the jail system.

    2. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 10-2026 APPROVING A THIRD AMENDMENT TO THE
       GOVERNING DOCUMENT OF PROMONTORY COMMERCE CENTER PUBLIC
       INFRASTRUCTURE DISTRICT NOS. 1, 2, AND 3 ( THE “DISTRICTS”) TO ALLOW THE
       BOUNDARY OF DISTRICT NO. 1 TO OVERLAP DISTRICT NOS. 2 AND 3; AND RELATED
       MATTERS.
         Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): When we originally did our public infrastructure
         district for this area, the governing document allowed for this modification with written permission from the
         Commission. We were approached by Promontory Commerce Center PID in December. They are in the
         process of getting ready to go to the bond market with this first tranche of bonds going directly to the current
         sewer project in that corridor. It's part of the match money required for that project. They are asking for an
         overlap. Normally, we would not allow this and I'll explain why the Economic Development Finance
         Committee is recommending this. They looking at creating PID 1 as the whole entire PID area and it would
         be the first that goes out to the bond market for assessment bonds. They're not the traditional millage bonds
         which would cause a problem if we did an overlap. So the proposal on the table is that we allow this overlap
         with the condition that it is PID 1 can only do assessment bonds. PIDs 2 and 3 will be created in the in the
         later part of this project. PID 1 will carry the burden of the bonds because all the upfront infrastructure is
         being paid for out of that PID. This proposal comes with recommendation from the Economic Development
         Finance Committee and the language in the governing document that will be amended is very tight and will
         last in perpetuity of the Public Infrastructure District. This change will help prevent someone from building
         a home in different PIDs and getting double taxed.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve Resolution 10-2026 approving a third amendment to the governing
         document of Promontory Commerce Center Public Infrastructure District Nos. 1, 2, and 3 ( The “Districts”) to
         allow the boundary of District No. 1 to overlap district Nos. 2 and 3; and related matters; Commissioner Harvey
         seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye
