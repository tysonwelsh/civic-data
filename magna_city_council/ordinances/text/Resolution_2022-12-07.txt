                                MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

RESOLUTION NO.: 22-12-07                                       DATE: December 13, 2022


            A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
            APPROVING THE NINTH AMENDMENT TO THE
            COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT BY PUBLIC AGENCIES TO
            CREATE A NEW ENTITY, TO CONSOLIDATE LAW
            ENFORCEMENT SERVICES, AND PROVIDE RULES OF
            GOVERNANCE

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, Magna is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code Ann.§
10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor, and

       WHEREAS, the Unified Police Department Board of Directors is comprised of elected officials
from each member entity; and

       WHEREAS, Magna, by and through its Council, has determined approving the Ninth
Amendment to the Cooperative Agreement by Public Agencies to Create a New Entity, to Consolidate
Law Enforcement Service, and Provide Rules of Governance, as set forth in the attached Cooperative
Agreement, Attachment "A", will benefit the residents of Magna and will contribute to the prosperity,
well-being, peace, and comfort of the community.


      NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, by the Magna Metro Township Council,
Magna, Utah:
       Section 1.  The Magna Metro Township Council hereby approves the Ninth
Amendment to the Cooperative Agreement by Public Agencies to Create a New Entity, to
Consolidate Law Enforcement Service, and Provide Rules of Governance as set forth in
Attachment A.

      APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Magna Metro Township Council, in Magna, Salt
Lake County, Utah this 13th day of December 2022.
