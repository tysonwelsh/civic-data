H.B. 35 – Metro Township Modifications
Cameron Platt stated the H.B. 35 – Metro Township Modifications bill had its first
and second reading at the Legislature. The bill has had positive recommendations and is moving
forward. If the bill is passed, May 1, 2024, would be the day that the metro townships would
convert to a city or town.
Council Member Pinon stated the bill tracker indicates there is no fiscal impact.
Mr. Platt stated there are no fiscal impacts for the state itself; however, the
sponsors were asked about some type of funding. He will look into where that ended up.
Council Member Brems asked how much it costs to brand the city.
Deputy Mayor Hawkes stated it was $5,000 to brand the metro township and
create a website.

Mayor Smolka stated the bill does not include the costs of having an attorney
amend resolutions and ordinances to reflect Emigration Canyon as a city.
Mr. Platt stated he does not know how much work needs to be done; however,
reviewing title sections every year and updating those should only take between 10–15 hours. If
the entire code needs to be rewritten it could take 50-100 hours and that should be done by a law
clerk. The urgency of amending the code is low.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Emigration Canyon Name and Logo Conversion
Mayor Smolka stated with the conversion to a city, the Council will need to choose
a new name and redo the logo. Does the Council want to determine the new name and logo, or
should a survey be given to the residents?
Deputy Mayor Hawkes stated she liked the idea of the community having a buyin. She can put a survey on the website.
Council Member Brems stated a lot of information needs to be disseminated to
the community regarding the conversion of Emigration Canyon into a city, including reasons for
the conversion and what needs to be changed. Once the bill is passed, a mailer or email blast
should be prepared and distributed. Suggestions for a city name should be considered at that
stage.
Deputy Mayor Hawkes stated there will need to be an election held.
Mr. Platt stated after the conversion there will be a five-member council with an
elected mayor. At the next municipal election, the mayor seat and a vacant council member seat
will need to be voted on.
