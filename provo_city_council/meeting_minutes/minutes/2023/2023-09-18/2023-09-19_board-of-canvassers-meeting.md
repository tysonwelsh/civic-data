 1.       A resolution of the Board of Canvassers accepting the election returns and
          declaring and certifying the results of the vote for the Primary Election held on
          September 5, 2023.

           Heidi Allman, Provo City Recorder Presenting. She read the prepared resolution that
           stated the participation numbers and results of the City Wide II Council Position that
           qualified for a primary election.

           Board Member Rachel Whipple asked if the information for how many ballots were
           called on to confirm and then accepted was available. Recorder Allman didn’t have
           those numbers but said she would follow-up with the Council once she had gotten
           them.

           Motion was made by Mayor Kaufusi to accept the resolution 2023 canvassing on
           behalf of the canvassing board of Provo. Seconded by Councilor Travis Hoban.
           Summary of Action, Passed 8-0
