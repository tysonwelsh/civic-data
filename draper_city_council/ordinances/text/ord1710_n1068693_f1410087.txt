Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1710

          On March 24, 2026, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1710
          Amending the Official Zoning Map of Draper City for approximately 2.03
          acres of property from RA2 (Residential Agricultural) to RM2 (Multiple-
          Family Residential), located at approximately 791 East 12200 South within
          Draper City, otherwise known as Fratto Farms Zoning Map Amendment.
          The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
          available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
