Disclaimer: The State required Annual Moderate-Income Housing Reporting Form directs the City to
report the calculations and data provided by the State Division of Workforce Services Five Year housing
Projection Calculator. A copy of the calculator is available at:
https://jobs.utah.gov/housing/affordable/moderate/.

The City has discovered and the State Division of Workforce Services staff has acknowledged that the
data and formulas used in the calculator have not been updated and therefore do not provide credible
results for the 2021 calendar year. This exhibit contains the data and calculations produced by that
calculator for the 2021 calendar year. The City is reporting the results as-is, as directed, as a part of the
required annual moderate-income housing report.
