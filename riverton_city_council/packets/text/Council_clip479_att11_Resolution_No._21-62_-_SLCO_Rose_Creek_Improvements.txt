                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-62

  RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN INTERLOCAL
COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN SALT LAKE COUNTY AND RIVERTON
 CITY FOR IMPROVEMENTS TO ROSE CREEK CHANNEL WITHIN RIVERTON
                       CITY BOUNDARIES

      WHEREAS, Rose Creek is a natural channel which crosses property owned by Suburban
Land Reserve (SLR) between Mountain View and 4250 W; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton entered into a Development Agreement with SLR wherein the
City agreed to relocate the Rose Creek Channel to a location agreed upon by both parties; and

        WHEREAS, to obtain a permit to relocate said channel, the Army Corps of Engineers is
requiring the City to improve the Rose Creek channel to carry the 100 year storm event; and

       WHEREAS, Rose Creek is a Salt Lake County stormwater facility; and

        WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has agreed to partially fund improvements included in the
relocation project by providing $225,000 from their 2020 budget; and, $200,000 from their 2022
budget; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has prepared an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement for
execution by the City to obtain these funds; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council does hereby determine that it is in the best interests
of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to execute said Agreement.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Mayor is authorized to execute an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement to receive
          from Salt Lake County the sum of $225,000 from their 2020 budget and $200,000
          from their 2022 budget for improvements to the Rose Creek channel within Riverton
          boundaries, copies of which are incorporated herein by reference.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

   PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
November 2021 by the following vote:
