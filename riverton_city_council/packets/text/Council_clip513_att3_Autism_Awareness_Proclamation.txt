                              Autism Awareness and Acceptance Month

WHEREAS, Autism is the fastest growing developmental disorder. Autism spectrum disorder (ASD)
is a developmental disability that can cause significant social, communication and behavioral
challenges. The learning, thinking, and problem-solving abilities of people with ASD can range from
gifted to severely challenged; and

WHEREAS, About 1 in 68 children has been identified with autism spectrum disorder (ASD)
according to estimates and ASD is about 4.5 times more common among boys (1 in 42) than among
girls (1 in 189). Millions of Americans live with an autism spectrum disorder; and

WHEREAS, People with ASD often have problems with social, emotional, and communication skills.
Many people with ASD also have different ways of learning, paying attention, or reacting to things.
Signs of ASD begin during early childhood and typically last throughout a person's life; however,
research shows that early intervention treatment services can improve a child's development; and

WHEREAS, ASD can sometimes be detected at 18 months or younger; however, many children do
not receive a final diagnosis until they are much older. This delay means that children with ASD
might not get the early help they need; and

WHEREAS, during the month of April, we strive to promote autism awareness, inclusion, and self-
determination for all, and assure that each person with ASD is provided an opportunity to achieve
the highest possible quality of life.

NOW, THEREFORE, I Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton, Utah, do hereby proclaim the month of April as
Autism Awareness and Acceptance month and urge our community to help in creating awareness and
acceptance for this disorder.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 5th day of April 2022.
