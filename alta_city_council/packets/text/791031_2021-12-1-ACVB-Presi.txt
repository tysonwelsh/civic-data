Strategic Transition Recap
 ACVB continues to operate under a new strategic
 plan:

  • Updated Mission Statement:


  To promote the Town of Alta and Little Cottonwood Canyon as
  world-class, year-around, mountain resort destinations; to
  enhance the Alta experience for local residents, local businesses
  and destination visitors; to function as a partnership between the
  Town of Alta and the Alta business community; to encourage a
  strong business alliance that will strengthen these businesses and
  be an advocate for the business community.
