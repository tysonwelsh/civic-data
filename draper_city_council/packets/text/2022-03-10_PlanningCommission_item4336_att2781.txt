BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for approximately 2.3 acres located
on the south side of 12300 South, at approximately 137 West 12300 South (Exhibit B). The
property is currently zoned CR. The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved to
allow for the development of a vacant piece of property into a gasoline service station and
convenience store.

The area subject to the request is part of a larger 3.65 acre parcel. The remaining portion of
the property would be developed at a later date. The property has been historically used to
house animals, most recently horses.

The noticing that went out for the public hearing errantly listed the acreage for the site as
1.4 acres. The address and parcel map included with the listing was correct.

ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Community
Commercial land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is
characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the CR zoning classification (Exhibit D). According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the CR zone is to
“provide areas where a combination of destination oriented business, retail commercial,
entertainment, and related uses may be established, maintained and protected to serve both
residents and nonresidents of the city. Typical uses in this zone include large scale, master
planned commercial centers with outlying commercial pads, big box stores, and offices.” The
property abuts the CI (Interchange Commercial) zoning to the east, the CR zones to the
south and west, and the CBP (Business/ Manufacturing Park) zone to the north.

Site Plan Layout. The applicant is proposing a single convenience store building, along with
gasoline service pumps and canopy (Exhibit E). The property will have one ingress and
egress point from Lone Peak Parkway, but is designed to accommodate a second access

point through the undeveloped portion of the property. The access point planned for
development under this application will also serve the remaining undeveloped portion of
property. The convenience store will be located along the east property line, with parking
located to the west and south of the building. The gasoline service pumps and canopy will
be located west of the building and parking.

The applicant has provided a Traffic Impact Study (TIS). The TIS executive summary has
been included in Exhibit H of this report. The study reviewed the proposed development
against current conditions and a proposed widening of Lone Peak Parkway by Draper City.

The site access has been designed with a full-movement access based on the TIS analysis
deviating from the city’s standard access spacing requirements. This access may be
modified based on traffic accident data or other safety issues once the site is constructed
and operational. The adjacent intersection of 12300 South and Lone Peak Parkway,
controlled by the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT), is being analyzed for
potential future widening to alleviate traffic congestion in the area. The TIS provided by the
applicant indicates the site access will improve once the potential intersection
improvements are completed.

The TIS found the following conclusions and recommendations.

    Conclusions

    •    Under existing traffic conditions, the signalized intersections within the study area
         currently operate at overall acceptable levels of service (LOS) “E” or better during the
         weekday AM and PM peak hours.
    •    Under background future 2023 traffic conditions, without the development of the
         subject site, delays would increase slightly at study intersections due to regional
         traffic growth. The signalized intersections would continue to operate at LOS “E” or
         better.
    •    The proposed site development would generate, upon completion and full
         occupancy, 57 new weekday AM and 74 new weekday PM peak hour vehicle trips as
         well as 1,084 new weekday daily trips.
    •    Under 2023 total future traffic conditions, all study intersections, including proposed
         site access would operate at an overall level of service consistent with background
         conditions.

    Recommendations

    •    It is recommended that the proposed development provide access consistent with
         the attached plan.
    •    It is recommended that Lone Peak Parkway go through with the expansion.
    •    It is recommended that one of the northbound left turn lanes along Lone Peak

Landscaping. The applicant is proposing to install 24,848 square feet of landscaping, or 36%
of the subject property (Exhibit F). The DCMC Section 9-22-050(D) requires a minimum of
20% overall landscaping. The majority of the landscaping will be located along the
perimeter of the site. There will be substantial landscaping area along the street frontages
and a detention pond along the south property line. Additional landscaping will be placed
along the building and at the ends of parking rows. Landscaping will be a mix of trees,
planting beds, and sod.

Parking. The proposed building is 5,620 square feet in size (Exhibit E). According to DCMC
Table 9-25-1 a convenience store with gasoline sales requires a minimum of 4 parking
spaces per 1,000 square feet of gross building floor area plus queuing space for the
gasoline pumps. The DCMC also allows for a 10% plus or minus that base number. The site
is required to have 24 parking spaces, or a min/max range of 21-27 stalls. The applicant is
proposing 25 stalls, plus the queuing space around the gasoline service pumps.

Architecture. The proposed convenience store will be 21-feet tall (Exhibit G). It will feature
windows and a metal awning along the west, north, and south of the building. The main
entrance will face west towards the parking lot and gasoline pumps. This building will
feature 100% primary materials, and more specifically fiber cement panels, stone, and
aluminum composite material.

The canopy over the gasoline service pumps will also be 21-feet tall. The materials will
match the convenience store building and include fiber cement panels and aluminum
composite material.

Lighting. The lighting and photometric plans show 18.5-foot tall light poles within the
parking lot, wall packs along the building, and lighting underneath the canopy. The
proposed foot candle levels comply with the standards within DCMC 9-20-070 and the
lights will be full cut-off fixtures to prevent light pollution.

Executive Summary
Site Location and Study Area
The property that comprises the application area for the proposed development is approximately 1.4 acres
in size and is currently vacant. It is located south of W 12300 S and east of Lone Peak Parkway. The site
is further identified as Salt Lake County Parcel Number 27254760140000. It is zoned Community
Commercial (C-2).

The study area, as discussed with the City of Draper (Staff) is generally bounded by the site boundaries to
east and south, as well as W 12300 S to the north, and Lone Peak Parkway to the west. The study area for
the project includes those intersections identified that could be affected by the proposed development:

    •   W 12300 S/Lone Peak Parkway
    •   W 12650 S/Lone Peak Parkway
    •   Proposed Site Accesses

Description of Proposed Development
The Applicant seeks to develop the property with a gas station and convenience store use. Site access is
proposed via two full movements on Lone Peak Parkway.


Conclusions and Recommendations
Conclusions
Based on the results of this traffic impact study, the following may be concluded:

    •   Under existing traffic conditions, the signalized intersections within the study area currently operate
        at overall acceptable levels of service (LOS) “E” or better during the weekday AM and PM peak
        hours.

    •   Under background future 2023 traffic conditions, without the development of the subject site, delays
        would increase slightly at study intersections due to regional traffic growth. The signalized
        intersections would continue to operate at LOS “E” or better.

    •   The proposed site development would generate, upon completion and full occupancy, 57 new
        weekday AM and 74 new weekday PM peak hour vehicle trips as well as 1,084 new weekday daily
        trips.

    •   Under 2023 total future traffic conditions, all study intersections, including proposed site access
        would operate at an overall level of service consistent with background conditions.


Recommendations
    •   It is recommended that the proposed development provide access consistent with the attached
        plan.

    •   It is recommended that Lone Peak Parkway go through with the expansion.
