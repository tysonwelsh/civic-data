  REPORT ANALYSIS

As part of the Lehi City annexation procedures, all annexation requests are required to be reviewed by the Planning
Commission. The Planning Commission’s responsibility is to review the proposed annexation and determine whether
or not the requested zoning is appropriate and complies with the General Plan. The Planning Commission’s recom-
mendation will be forwarded to the City Council for their review upon final consideration of the annexation.

The General Plan designates this 5.08 acre property located at 7730 N 8730 W (approximately 1500 S 1100 W) as
VLDRA, which corresponds with the proposed R-1-22 zoning. This annexation is being done by resolution because
the property is part of an existing county island.

The DRC reviewed the proposed annexation and had no critical issue concerns. Some of their comments include:

    1.   Clarify the proposed zoning will be R-1-22 on the annexation plat
    2.   As a condition of recording the annexation plat, the applicant will need to provide water dedication per Chap-
         ter 27 of the Development Code. The rate for the R-1-22 Zone is .79 acre feet per acre of culinary water and
         2.9 acre feet per acre of irrigation water.

These comments were informative for the applicant.
