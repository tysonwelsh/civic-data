                                        Meeting Minutes
                                      Work Session 7-14-20
                                            7:00 p.m.
                                   Nephi City Council Chambers



        1. Ken Tuttle and John Iverson from Sunrise Engineering discussed with the council their work
           on Nephi City’s 40-year water right plan. This plan is something being worked on as part of
           the culinary water project. The current draft of the 40-year plan shows a surplus of water
           rights. The council had expressed the possible need of requiring that additional water rights
           be included with annexation property. Councilor Memmott expressed his concern about the
           difference between having water rights and actually having wet water. CUP is currently
           studying the amount of wet water available. The council agreed that the city can continually
           update their 40-year plan and maintain the water needed by a growing city.

        2.    Mr. Atkinson presented to the council the details of a proposed partnership between the
             city, the school district, and community donors for the building of a Recreation
             Center/Fieldhouse. It had been requested that the City contribute $1,500,000 toward the
             cost of the facility construction. Mr. Atkinson presented several funding options. The
             Mayor asked each councilor to comment. All councilors and mayor commented in favor of
             the project but wanted to make sure the city could make the annual payments. The Mayor
             asked for comments from the audience about the project. The following people made
             comments all in favor of the project going forward: Randy Cuff, Joel Holman, JD Parady,
             Dale Whitlock, Kodey Hughes, Gerratt Bethers, Craig Oswald, James Rosenbeck, Lynsey Cuff,
             and Mike Bowring.
             Mayor Nielson directed staff to continue pursuing the project and to move forward with the
             Parks & Recreation Masterplan and then to begin the process of implementing parks and
             recreation impact fees.

        3.    Mr. Atkinson reported on the Planning Commission’s recommendations to increase Nephi
             City’s annexation growth area. He said that three property owners north of the city had
             approached the Planning Commission about their desire for annexation and access to city
             services.

        4. Council members gave various updates.

        5. Meeting adjourned at 8:55 p.m.
