Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commissioner Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner and
Commissioner Jackson Britton.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and consideration of BLJ Construction’s request for approval of
Homestead Estates, a 2-lot subdivision with an existing home located at approximately
1400 North 2020 West.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Brandon Jones with BLJ Construction, was present. Ross Dinsdale clarified the
road would need to be a public frontage to meet city code.

Commissioner Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve BLJ Construction’s request for
        approval of Homestead Estates, a 2-lot subdivision with an existing home located at

        approximately 1400 North 2020 West; including all DRC comments and findings
        stated in the report. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.2) Public hearing and consideration of Natalie Crump’s request for approval of a
conditional use permit for an 8-foot fence located at 1383 South 1960 West.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant was present but did not wish to add anything.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to recommend approval of Natalie Crump’s
        request for approval of a conditional use permit for an 8-foot fence located at 1383
        South 1960 West; including all DRC comments, not including comment number four,
        and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.5) Public hearing and recommendation of Gardner Plumb’s request for review of the
Hardman Heights Zone Change on 14.7 acres located at approximately 4551 West 2100
North, changing the zoning from Commercial to Heavy Commercial.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item

The applicant, Howard Cook, was present to speak to the item.

Commissioner Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Gardner Plumb’s
        request for review of the Hardman Heights Zone Change on 14.7 acres located at
        approximately 4551 West 2100 North, changing the zoning from Commercial to
        Heavy Commercial; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, no; Commissioner Jackson, no; Commissioner Everett, no;
        Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, no. The motion failed.

2.6) Public hearing and consideration of Gardner Plumb’s request for approval of the
Hardman Heights Concept, a 2-lot commercial development on 14.7 acres located at 4551
West 2100 North.
Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Howard Cook, was present to speak to the item.

Commissioner Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Gardner Plumb’s
        request for review of the Hardman Heights Zone Change on 14.7 acres located at
        approximately 4551 West 2100 North, changing the zoning from Commercial to
        Heavy Commercial; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report,
        noting the inclusion of connectivity and the concept could be implemented in heavy
        commercial or commercial zoning. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, no; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed 5 – 1.

2.7) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of an
amendment to the Bike and Pedestrian Master Plan, adding trail connections between Dry
Creek Elementary and the Dry Creek Trail.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.
Commissioner Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Lehi City’s request for review of an
        amendment to the Bike and Pedestrian Master Plan, adding trail connections
        between Dry Creek Elementary and the Dry Creek Trail; including all DRC
        comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.8) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of an
amendment to the Design Standards and Public Improvements Specifications Manual
adding standards and requirements for traffic studies.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Lehi City’s request for review of an
        amendment to the Design Standards and Public Improvements Specifications
        Manual adding standards and requirements for traffic studies; including all DRC
        comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.9) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Chapter 37, Multi-Family Design Standards, to include
an exceptions clause.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request
        for review of a Development Code Amendment to Chapter 37, Multi-Family Design
        Standards, to include an exceptions clause; noting the language must be clear,
        convincing, and knowledgeable as presented to the planning commission to modify
        language. Also include the exceptions to the design standard, as approved by the
        Planning Department to allow flexibility to the applicants with clear proof to the
        commission. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

3. City Business
Jacob Curtis reviewed the November and December Planning Commission schedule for the
remainder of 2023.

4. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Bunker moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Jackson seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 8:18 p.m.
