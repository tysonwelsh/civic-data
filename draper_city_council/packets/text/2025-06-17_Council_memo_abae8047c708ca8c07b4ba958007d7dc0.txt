MEMO
To: City Council
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2025-06-17
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1659


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement for approximately
110 acres located to the east of Suncrest Dr., at approximately 3235 East 15780 South.
The property is currently zoned RR-22. The applicant is requesting that a Development
Agreement be approved to allow for a 50-lot single-family development.


The Planning Commission reviewed the Development Agreement and the Preliminary
Subdivision Plat application at their June 12, 2025 meeting. An update on their
recommendation will be provided to the City Council at the meeting.



The findings are as follows:


For Approval:
  1. The proposed Development Agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and
     (D).
  2. The addition roadway width is needed for the safety of future residents living in the
     proposed development.


For denial:

  1. The proposed Development Agreement does not comply with DCMC Section 9-5-
     200(C) and (D).
