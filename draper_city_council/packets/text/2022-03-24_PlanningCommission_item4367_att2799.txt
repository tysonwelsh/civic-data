BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a text amendment to DCMC Section 9-6-080
“Nonconformities Created by Public Action”. The code currently only addresses
nonconformities as to area and setback created by public action. This proposed text
amendment addresses other nonconformities created when a government entity takes
property or purchases property for a public use, such as road construction.

The proposed amendment would permit a property owner to continue to use the parcel or
structure as long as the nonconformity is not increased. The property owner could amend
the site without bringing the nonconformity into compliance with the current code so long
as the amendment does not increase the degree of nonconformity.

ANALYSIS

DCMC Section 9-6-080 currently states:

    When area or setbacks of a legally created lot or parcel are reduced as the result of
    conveyance to a federal, state or local government for a public purpose and the remaining
    area or setback is at least seventy five percent (75%) of the required minimum in the zone
    where it is located, the lot or parcel shall be deemed to be in compliance with the minimum
    lot size and setback standards of this title without any need for a variance.

The proposal would designate the current text as subsection A and add a subsection B:

    B. When a lot or parcel is determined to be nonconforming, other than as to lot area or
    setbacks, as a result of the conveyance of property to a federal, state, or local government
    for a public purpose, the owner may continue the existing use of the lot, parcel, or structure
    on the lot or parcel. Any subsequent alterations to the lot or parcel, other than the portion
    of the lot or parcel rendered nonconforming by public action, shall comply with this title and
    shall not increase the degree of nonconformity caused by the public action.

The final text amendment would read:

9-6-080: NONCONFORMITIES CREATED BY PUBLIC ACTION:

    A. When area or setbacks of a legally created lot or parcel are reduced as the result of
    conveyance to a federal, state or local government for a public purpose and the remaining
    area or setback is at least seventy five percent (75%) of the required minimum in the zone
    where it is located, the lot or parcel shall be deemed to be in compliance with the minimum
    lot size and setback standards of this title without any need for a variance.

    B. When a lot or parcel is determined to be nonconforming, other than as to lot area or
    setbacks, as a result of the conveyance of property to a federal, state, or local government
    for a public purpose, the owner may continue the existing use of the lot, parcel, or structure
    on the lot or parcel. Any subsequent alterations to the lot or parcel, other than the portion
    of the lot or parcel rendered nonconforming by public action, shall comply with this title and
    shall not increase the degree of nonconformity caused by the public action.
