IN APPRECIATION FOR OUTSTANDING PUBLIC SERVICE BY 
KERRY STEADMAN
WHEREAS, Kerry Steadman began public service in 1976 and served the 
residents of Salt Lake County until December of 2020, providing County 
residents with exceptional service in the area of Human Services leadership, 
management, and best practices.
WHEREAS, Kerry Steadman served on numerous boards and commissions, 
most notably the Housing Connect Board, the Road Home Board, and the 
Hogle Zoo Board, all related to his work within Salt Lake County where he 
brought his unquestionable leadership, mentorship, and positive outlook to

WHEREAS, Kerry Steadman has brought respect, honor, and integrity to 
Salt Lake County, the Office of Regional Development, the Division of 
Housing & Community Development, and the Division of Human Services, 
by leading by example, exhibiting the highest level of ethics and maintaining a 
superior moral character.
WHEREAS, Kerry Steadman volunteered numerous hours, incurred personal

8.1 A Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council Approving 
and Authorizing the Mayor to Grant a Perpetual Easement to 
Mt. Olympus Improvement District
20-1275
Attachments: Staff Report
Resolution for Easement to Mt Olympus Improvement 
District at Hillview Basin (12-22-20) - AATF
MOID Hillview Basin Easement
RESOLUTION NO. 5827
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO GRANT A 
PERPETUAL EASEMENT TO MT OLYMPUS IMPROVEMENT 
DISTRICT
RECITALS
A. Salt Lake County (the “County”) owns multiple parcels of real 
property located at approximately 4191 South 900 East and 4189 South 900 
East in Millcreek, Utah, identified as Parcel Nos. 22-05-201-005, 22-05-128-
020, 22-05-128-036, 22-05-128-032, 22-05-128-034, and 16-32-378-011, also 
known as Hillview Basin (the “County Property”).
Page 6 of 10
County Council Meeting Minutes January 5, 2021
B. Mt. Olympus Improvement District (the “District”) owns and operates 
a major sewer trunk line and other appurtenances (the “Sewer Line ”) under 
the County Property.
C. The County granted an easement to the District ’s predecessor in 
interest to install and maintain the Sewer Line (“Prior Easement ”), but the 
Prior Easement was previously terminated and a new easement was 
apparently never recorded.
D. Based upon the Prior Easement, the District ’s predecessor in interest 
installed and has maintained the Sewer Line in the County Property.
E. To clean up the record and acknowledge the existence of the Sewer 
Line, the District and the County would like to record a perpetual easement 
to allow for ongoing operation, repair and maintenance of the Sewer Line.
F. Because the Sewer Line was installed pursuant to the Prior Easement, 
no consideration is necessary for the new perpetual easement, which is 
intended to officially document on the record the location of the Sewer Line 
and set forth the rights and obligations of the parties.
G. The County and the District have prepared a Sewer Line Easement 
Agreement (“Easement”) attached hereto as Exhibit 1, wherein the County 
grants a perpetual sewer line easement across and under the County Property 
to the District.
H. It has been determined that the best interests of the County and the 
general public will be served by granting the Easement to the District.  The 
terms and conditions of the Easement are in compliance with all applicable 
state statutes and county ordinances.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the Mayor and County Clerk are hereby authorized to 
execute the Easement, attached hereto as Exhibit A and by this reference 
made a part of this Resolution, and to deliver the fully executed document to 
the County Real Estate Section for delivery to the District.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 5th day of January, 2021.
