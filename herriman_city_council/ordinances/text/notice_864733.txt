PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on October 11, 2023, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2023-18 amending Title 7 of the Herriman City Code adding additional language for encroachments on City Property, adding violations and enforcement proceedings. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
