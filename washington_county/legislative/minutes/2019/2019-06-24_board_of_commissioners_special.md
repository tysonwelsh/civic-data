WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
JUNE 24, 2019

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Dean Cox at 4:05 p.m. on June 24, 2019, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Chairman Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf,
Commission Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Commissioner Almquist was excused as he was out of town.
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf
REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION OF SETTLEMENT OF CENTRALLY ASSESSED TAX
APPEAL FOR THE 2018 AT&T

Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf explained that an agreement has been reached as to a
valuation of centrally assessed property owned by AT&T Inc.

Mr. Graf recommends accepting the proposed settlement agreement in the amount of
$45,886 due to overpayment in taxes under the original valuation.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution R-2019-2488, A
Resolution Approving a Stipulated Settlement in the Matter of AT&T
Inc. Vs. Property Tax Division of the Utah State Tax Commission.
Motion Seconded by Chairman Cox and Carried by a Unanimous Vote
with Commissioners Cox and Iverson voting aye.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
4:11 p.m.
