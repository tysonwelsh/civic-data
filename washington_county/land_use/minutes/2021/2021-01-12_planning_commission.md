Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                               January 12, 2021

The regular meeting of the Washington County Planning Commission was held on Tuesday,
January 12, 2021 in the Commission Chambers in the Washington County Administration
Building, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Also conducted via electronic means through
Zoom. Chairman Jones opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of Allegiance and then
explained how the meeting would be conducted.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Shayne Durrant, Kevin Jones, Slade Hughes, Mark
Owens, Reed Scow (Alternate), Doug Solstad, and Jon Walter.

Excused: Dan Briggs (Alternate).

Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director via Zoom; Victoria Hales, Deputy
County Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Kurt Gardner, Building Official;
Todd Edwards, County Engineer.


1.   DALTON WASH ROAD DEDICATION. Review of a proposed road dedication of
Dalton Wash Road to Washington County. Parcels 1145-B-HV, 1145-A-HV, 1145-D-HV, 1145-
D-2-HV, 1145-A-5-HV, 1145-C-8-HV, 1145-C-2-A-HV, 1145-C-2-B-HV, and 1145-C-7-HV, in
Kolob. The applicant is Washington County.

Mr. Messel provided a power point presentation with a map of the area and a copy of the proposed
realignment of Dalton Wash Road. He stated Todd Edwards would be reviewing the project and
turned the meeting over to him.

Mr. Edwards explained the background of the road and changes that occurred overtime.
Washington County has been maintaining Dalton Wash Road for several years. Currently, the road
runs through several private properties. The property owners have requested to move the road to
the property line boundaries in order better utilize their properties. The county is asking the
planning commission to recommend that the County Commission accept the newly reconfigured
Dalton Wash Road.

The commission and Mr. Edwards briefly discussed the realignment for informational purposes.

MOTION:       Commissioner Solstad MOVED to send a positive recommendation to the County
              Commission to accept the road dedication for the newly reconfigured Dalton Wash
              Road located in Section: 18 & 19; T:41S; R:11W; SLB&M, on Parcels 1145-B-
              HV, 1145-A-HV, 1145-D-1-HV, 1145-D-2-HV, 1145-A-5-HV, 1145-C-8-HV,
              1145-C-2-A-HV, 1145-C-2-B-HV, 1145-C-7-HV. The applicant is Washington
              County, based on the following findings:

                1. The road dedication request was properly noticed.
                2. The project will allow for the property owners a way to use their property
                   better.
                3. Public access goes through private lands.

2.     PUBLIC HEARING/ZONE CHANGE. Review and hold a public hearing for a zone
change of Parcel 7205-M-DVF from the OST-20 (Open Space Transition 40,000 sq.ft. minimum
lot size) to RE-40 (Residential Estate 40,000 sq.ft. minimum lot size) zone. The parcel is located
at approximately 180 N. Pinion Hills Dr. in Dammeron Valley. The applicant is Val Schulthies/
Kelly Schmutz, agent.

Mr. Messel provided a map of the area and a power point presentation of the project. He reported
the applicants are requesting a zone change for Parcel 7205-M-DVF from the OST-20 (Open Space
Transition 20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the RE-40 (Residential Estate 40,000 sq.ft. minimum
lot size) zone. The intent of the change is to realign the property to add a portion to their existing
lot in the Pinion Hills subdivision and create a new lot. Currently, the new lot would not be
buildable as there is no water or septic available to it. Once all of the issues are addressed, the
applicant will come in for a plat amendment to the existing Pinion Hills Phase 6 Subdivision. The
parcel is located at approximately 180 N. Pinion Hills Dr. in Dammeron Valley. The applicant is
Val Schulthies/Kelly Schmutz, agent.

Recommendation:
Staff reviewed the zone change request and would recommend that the Planning Commission hold
the public hearing then, if they are comfortable with the change, send a positive recommendation
to the County Commission to approve the requested zone change from the OST-20 (Open Space
Transition 20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the RE-40 (Residential Estates 40,000 sq.ft.
minimum lot size) zone based on the following findings:

 1. The zone change and public hearing were properly noticed.
 2. The public hearing was held on January 12, 2021 in the regularly scheduled Planning
    Commission Meeting.
 3. The zone change will bring the property into zoning compliance to allow the applicant a
    means to utilize their property in a beneficial way.
 4. The proposal is consistent with the General Plan.

Commissioner Durrant asked what the motivation for the zone change was.

3.    PUBLIC HEARING/ ZONE CHANGE. Review and hold a public hearing for a zone
change request for Parcel 6199-D-PVF from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size)
zone to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone and the FR-13.5 (Forest Residential
13,500 sq.ft. minimum lot size) zone. The parcel is located at 1200 E. Main St. in Pine Valley. The
applicants are Nathan and Brian Thiriot and Ben Willets.

Mr. Messel provided a power point presentation, the map of the area, and layout of the possible
future subdivision. He pointed out that the applicant appeared before the planning commission
several months ago for a preliminary proposal of Parcel 6199-D-1-PVF, located at approximately
1200 E. Main Street in Pine Valley as a PD (Planned Development) zone. The applicant has since
decided to move in a different direction and subdivide the parcel for individual ownership for
family members. In order to do so, the applicants are requesting to change the zoning designation,
from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size) zone into two different zones; the A-5

(Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone and FR-13.5 (Forest Residential 13.5 sq.ft. minimum
lot size) zone. The subdivision request will come back to the planning commission in the near
future.

Recommendation:
Staff reviewed the zone change request and recommends that the Planning Commission hold the
public hearing then, if they are comfortable with the change, send a positive recommendation to
the County Commission to approve the zone change request of Parcel 6199-D-1-PVF from the A-
20 (Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size) to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone
and a portion to the FR-13.5 (Forest Residential 13.5 sq.ft. minimum lot size) zone, based on the
following findings:

 1. The zone change and public hearing were properly noticed.
 2. The public hearing was held on January 12, 2021 in the regularly scheduled Planning
    Commission Meeting.
 3. The zone change will bring the property into zoning compliance to allow the applicants a
    means to utilize their property in a beneficial way.
 4. The proposal is consistent with the General Plan.

Commissioner Owens inquired if there were other parcels within the county that had multiple
zones on them and if that was something the county can approve. Mr. Hughes responded by stating
yes and reported that his property has two different zoning designations.

Mr. Messel stated yes there are other parcels within the county and other areas outside of the
county with multiple zones attached to them.

Chairman Jones confirmed that the Residential Forest zone was being requested due to the lot size
requirement. Mr. Messel indicated it was.

Commissioner Solstad had some questions on the original application and timing. Mr. Messel
responded that this is considered a new application and any fees associated with the prior zone
change request would be applied to this new application. However, the applicant needs to update
his application with the county indicating the request for the different zone change request.

A discussion occurred regarding the proposed project that included the Pine Valley area zoning. It
was reported there are several areas zoned Forest Residential. The Forest Residential zone would
fit into this area and would be an appropriate zone. Some clarification in regards to the application
form was briefly discussed.

Chairman Jones confirmed that the zone change request currently before the commission for Parcel
6199-D-1-PVF from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the A-5
(Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) and FR-13.5 (Forest Residential 13.5 sq.ft. minimum lot
size) zone was properly noticed. Mr. Messel stated it was.

A discussion regarding the FR-13.5 (Forest Residential 13.5 sq.ft. minimum lot size) zone
occurred. The question was raised regarding the proposed subdivision cul-de-sac and if it would
be a public road. Mr. Messel stated he understood it would be.

Brian Thiriot addressed the commission. He introduced his brother Nathan Thiriot and his son
Breckin Thiriot. He reported there are 6 family members who would take the 6 future lots for their
own use.

Commissioner Owens asked if the project would be done in phases. Mr. Thiriot answered they
would be and that they are working toward finalizing the subdivision process.

The future subdivision and development of the property was discussed and clarification was made
regarding the different zones.

Mr. Thiriot explained that the A-5 zone section would be placed in a conservation easement in
order to leave the portion in open space for preservation of that area. The portion of the property
being zoned to the FR-13.5 zone is for the 6-lot subdivision.

Questions and answers regarding zoning requirements and the zone change information took place.

Chairman Jones opened the public hearing.

Maxine Davie via Zoom commented that the plan is for 6 homes to use one septic system. If there
are 6 single family dwellings, how does that work? Even if they are for family members now, how
will that be addressed in the future? Commissioner Hughes answered that it would be an advanced
treatment system and would be required to comply with the local Health Department and State
guidelines for approval and adherence.

Chairman Jones asked who would own the treatment plant and would be responsible for it and
how it would work for future homeowners.

Todd Edwards answered that once the system is in place, a body politic would be put in place,
whether that be the County, Water Conservancy District, etc., to take care of the system and ensure
it is maintained properly.

Mary Esther Putnam via Zoom asked if she could get a zone change on her 2-1/2 acre parcel
located in Pine Valley for 13.5 sq.ft. lots. She was advised she would need to go through the
application process and that it was irrelevant to this project.

Greg Whitehead, reported that his sister is Crystal Thiriot, was married to the late Jason Thiriot
who was one of the brothers. He stated they have going to the property for years and looks forward
to being able to go up as a family and enjoy the area. He believed the Thiriot’s were good stewards
of the land and will ensure that it will be a nice addition to the area.

Mary Esther Putnam via Zoom questioned the proposed 5-lot subdivision for 13,500 sq.ft. lot sizes.
She indicated she has a property that is 2-1/2 acres zoned residential. She was told that in order to

build a home, it could not be on less than 3acres. She asked if she could subdivide that 2-1/2 acres

Mr. Messel stated the sixth lot will be in the agricultural zone. Again, this is for the zone change
and not the subdivision. The subdivision will need to come in for approval when the applicants are
ready for it to move forward. He noted that any property owner has the right to come in and request
to subdivide their property but it doesn’t mean it will happen. He pointed out that in the Pine Valley
area there are grandfathered in lots smaller than some of the other areas, so the FR-13.5 (Forest
Residential 13,000 sq.ft. minimum lot size) zone designation was put in place to allow those
properties to be in compliance.

Duane Krohm stated he had property in Pine Valley and request a stipulation be in place that the
units could not be used for short term rentals i.e. Air B&B or that type of business.

Commissioner Hughes stated the commission would not commit to that request. We are in the
public hearing for the zone change and not the subdivision plat.

Ms. Hales stated that the subdivision is not legally before the commission, only the zone change.
She encouraged the people making those types of requests to address them with their neighbor and
not in this public hearing.

Commissioner Owens asked Mr. Thiriot if he had put any thought into moving the lot on the west

into the area.
