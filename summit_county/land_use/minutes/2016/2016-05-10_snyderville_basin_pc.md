The other is for a ~18,500 square-foot riding arena.  In addition, an existing 3,600

The proposed floor plan for the basketball gym was shown.  It will have a lounge, an

Planner Lewis requested the Commission discuss if the basketball area and horse stalls

Ivan Broman introduced himself.  This is a second home for the property owner.  Their

be a court for his three boys to play on.  It will not be open to the public.  In addition to 
the court, the building will have a fitness center and a play area.  Upstairs will be the 
three offices.   
 
Mr. Broman said the arena backs a neighboring arena.  This means, there will be two 
arenas on the back property line.  It also means the back-yard neighbors will not be 
affected.  The neighboring arena was shown in an aerial photograph.   
 
Mr. Broman said the existing barn on the property will also be renovated.  Currently, it 
is a pre-manufactured building.  It will be converted to a garage with storage space.  
This action will only require a building permit.  They are hoping they can start 
construction this summer.

2. Discussion regarding the  Colby School site Conditional Use Permit; 3770 North 
Highway 224; PP-102-A-3 –Ray Milliner, Principal Planner  
 
Julie McBroom, Emma Worsley, and Brook Hontz introduced themselves.  They are 
representing the applicant, Hoffvest LLC.  Planner Ray Milliner said the application is 
Snyderville Basin Planning Commission

for a conditional use for a property located on 3379 North Highway 224 in the Rural 
Residential (RR) zone.  A vicinity map was shown of the area.  A correction in the Staff 
Report was noted; TGC LLC is not part of the application.   
Planner Milliner began with a background of the property.  In 1985, Snowed Inn was 
approved as a class II development.  At that time, a class II development allowed a 
number of things including a bed and breakfast, hotel, meeting hall, and a reception 
center.  Later the property changed hands and was converted to the Colby School.   
 
Planner Milliner said that County Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas issued a memorandum 
that this application is conforming and vested under the class II permit.  He determined 
that class II permits run with the land.  Planner Milliner said the memorandum states 
the original vested uses are applicable through the low impact process, but this process 
has since been upgraded to the conditional use process.  Most of Staff’s findings have 
been based on the memorandum of Attorney Thomas.    
 
Planner Milliner said on December 8, 2015, a public hearing was held.  The applicant 
proposed 15 hotel rooms.  They also proposed several cabins that would be located on 
two Brookside Estate lots.  Other activities included in the proposal were: a venue 
function center, a restaurant/bar, a yoga and palates’ studio, café, reception center, and 
a bath house.  The location of each of these was pointed out on the plat map.             
 
At the public hearing a lot of the discussion centered on if the use could be allowed to 
expand to the Brookside Estate lots.  Following that meeting, Attorney Thomas was 
asked to clarify if this is allowed.  A second memorandum issued by Attorney Thomas, 
stated the vested uses cannot be extended to the Brookside Estate parcels.  Planner 
Milliner said that as a result, the applicant went back and redesigned the project.  The 
development is now proposed to be placed entirely on the Colby School property.   
 
Planner Milliner said the current proposal is for a total of 33 rooms, 15 in the old Colby 
School and 18 rooms in an addition.  There are three cabin-style rooms proposed, and a
