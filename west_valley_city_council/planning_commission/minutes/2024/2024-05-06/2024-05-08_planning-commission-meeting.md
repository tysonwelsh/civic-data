Application #: GPZ-1-2024
Applicant:     Paul and Kareen Swenson
Address:       4053 South 7200 West Request: General plan change from Large Lot
               Residential to Light Manufacturing and zone change from A to M
               Property Size: 2.96 acres

This application was continued during the Planning Commission meeting on April 24th.

Paul and Kareen Swenson have submitted a General Plan/zone change application for a
2.96-acre parcel located at 4053 South 7200 West. The property is currently zoned A
(Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) and the current General Plan designation is Large
Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre). The proposed zone is M (Manufacturing) and the
proposed General Plan designation is Light Manufacturing.

Surrounding zones include A to the south, R-1-10 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential,
minimum lot size 10,000 square feet) to the east, R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential,
minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) to the north, and R-1-7 to the west in Magna.
Surrounding land uses include a City-owned storage lot to the south, single unit dwellings
to the north and east, and a Salt Lake County park to the west. The property includes two
homes – one built in 1978 and the other built in 1982.

Approximately the west 40% of the property falls within Overpressure Zone A with the
remainder of the property being within Overpressure Zone B. Overpressure Zone A is the
innermost circle (closest to Northrop Grumman) and prohibits residential use.

Development Proposal

If this application is approved, the applicants intend to develop a self-storage facility on
the subject property. Included with this report is a letter from the applicants that describe
how they arrived at the proposed use. In summary, the applicants indicated that self-
storage was the preferred use because residential isn’t viable primarily due to
Overpressure Zone A, commercial uses like retail, office, or a restaurant are unlikely in
that there are no other similar businesses nearby, and most industrial uses would be
problematic given the adjoining residential. In the applicants’ letter they also mention that
they will not pursue other uses allowed in the M zone.

The M zone requires a 20’ building setback from a residential zone, agricultural zone,
or residential use. The applicant is requesting that, through a development agreement, this
20’ setback be reduced to 0’ to eliminate a 20’ space that is not visible to the public and
could create a security concern for the neighboring residents. There are several self-
storage facilities in the City that adjoin residential where the City’s Board of Adjustment
has approved variances to waive the 20’ setback requirement due to security concerns or
the self-storage facility was constructed before the residential. A development agreement
can be used to accomplish the same outcome.

A recent variance example is the self-storage development at 5443 South 6055 West. The
buildings along the east side of the property adjacent to residential were built on the
property line and are 10’ tall.

The City has also submitted a General Plan/zone change application on the City-owned
parcel to the south for a General Plan designation of Light Manufacturing and M zoning.
If the City’s application is approved, the 20’ setback would not apply along the south
property line.

Staff received a phone call and visit from a resident in opposition to this application. This
resident lives on the street east of the subject property and is concerned about the potential
impact of the proposed project on property values and views. Staff also received an email
from another nearby resident in opposition to industrial uses on the site. When staff
explained that only self-storage was proposed, the resident indicated that if the City can
guarantee the self-storage use, he would not oppose the project as much.

Staff Alternatives:

    1. Approval. This application should be approved subject to a development
       agreement that includes the following standards:
           a. The use of the property shall be limited to a self-storage facility.
           b. The buildings along the north and east sides of the property may be placed
              at the
              property line. The height of buildings placed along the north and east
              property
              lines shall be limited to 10 feet.
           c. The Developer shall dedicate 7 feet of right-of-way to the City along the
               Property’s entire frontage on 7200 West.
           d. In lieu of installing the off-site improvements required in Section 7-2-117 of
              West Valley City Code, the Developer shall make a cash payment to the
              City equal to
              the estimated cost of said improvements as determined by the City
              Engineer.
           e. Rock or bark mulch shall be installed within the 20’ setback area along
              7200 West prior to the issuance of a business license. The installation of the
              irrigation and live-plant material landscaping required in Section 7-6-303 of
              the West Valley City Code may be delayed until the reconstruction of 7200
              West is complete, or until September 30, 2030, whichever first occurs.
           f. The Developer shall grant a temporary construction easement to the City for
              the reconstruction of 7200 West. The easement shall apply to the westerly
              20 feet of the Property along the entire 7200 West frontage after the
              dedication described in item 3 above. The temporary construction easement
              shall commence upon the beginning of actual 7200 West construction on the
              Property and shall continue only until 7200 West project construction on the
              Property is complete, or for three (3) years, whichever first occurs.

    2. Approval. This application should be approved subject to a development
       agreement that includes standards a, b, c, d, and f from approval option 1 above.

    3. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during
       the public hearing.

    4. Denial.

   Application #: GPZ-2-2024
   Applicant:     West Valley City
   Address:       4063 South 7200 West
   Request:       General Plan change from Large Lot Residential to Light
                  Manufacturing and zone change from A to M
   Property Size: 2.0 acres

   This application was continued during the Planning Commission meeting on April
   24th.

   City staff has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for a 2.0-acre parcel
   located at 4063 South 7200 West. The property is currently zoned A (Agriculture,
   minimum lot size ½ acre) and the General Plan designation is Large Lot Residential (2
   to 3 units/acre). The proposed zone is M (Manufacturing) and the proposed General
   Plan designation is Light Manufacturing.

   Surrounding zones include M to the south, R-1-10 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential,
   minimum lot size 10,000 square feet) to the east, A to the north, and R-1-7 to the west
   in Magna. Surrounding land uses include single family homes to the north and east, a
   Salt Lake County park to the west and railroad tracks and industrial property to the
   south. The property is owned by West Valley City, used by Public Works to store
   garbage cans, and used by Animal Control to house larger animals.

   Approximately the west 50% of the property falls within Overpressure Zone A with
   the remainder of the property being within Overpressure Zone B. Overpressure Zone
   A is the innermost circle (closest to Northrop Grumman) and prohibits residential use.

   Staff is proposing these changes for several reasons. First, the outside storage of
   garbage cans is not an allowed use in the A zone. Second, the housing of animals is
   still allowed in the M zone. Third, the overpressure zone does not allow residential,
   which is allowed in the A zone, on roughly 50% of this property. Fourth, a General
   Plan/zone change application was also submitted on the property directly north for a
   General Plan designation of Light Manufacturing and M zoning. If the application on
   the adjoining property is approved, the applicant intends to develop storage units on
   the property. If the zoning on the subject property remains A, the storage units will be
   required to meet a 20’ setback from the A zone and a 20’ height limitation at the
   minimum setback. These requirements would not apply if the subject property is zoned
   M.

   Staff Alternatives:

       1. Approval. This application should be approved.

       2. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined
          during the public hearing.

       3. Denial.
