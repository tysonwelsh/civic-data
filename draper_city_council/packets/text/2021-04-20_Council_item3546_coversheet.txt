Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: April 20, 2021
Re: Big Willow Creek 3rd Amendment– Development Agreement Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a 3 rd Amendment to an existing Development Agreement, for approximately 41.33 acres located on the south side of 11400 South, at approximately 491 West 11400 South.
The Agreement seeks to do two things, as outlined below.
1. Phase 3 Approval Process
Allows plat approval and infrastructure construction prior to completion and acceptance of second access.
Prohibits plat recordation or building permit issuance until second access is constructed and accepted by City.
2. Modification of Private Roadway Widths and Driveway Depth Standards
Allows developer to utilize code provisions in effect prior to May 19, 2020 and November 10, 2020 text amendments.
The Planning Commission reviewed this item at their April 8, 2021 meeting and forwarded a split recommendation. The Planning Commission recommended approval of the first item to modify the Phase 3 approval process on a 3-0 vote. The Planning Commission recommended denial of the second item to modify the private roadway width and driveway depth standards on a 3-0 vote.
Staff has included two ordinances to this memo. The first, Ordinance 1487 , represents the application in its entirety and is what the Applicant is requesting. The second, Planning Commission Recommendation Ordinance , represents the recommended vote by the Planning Commission at their April 8, 2021 meeting.  The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  The developer began master planning the Big Willow Creek Phase 6 area prior to the City amending the private street width and driveway standards. It is reasonable to allow development under the old standards rather than require a redesign of the Phase 6 area.
2.  The application complies with the requirements of DCMC 9-5-200(C).
For denial:
1.  The provision within Section 2 of the 3 rd Amendment would lead to inadequate facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, specifically roadways and police and fire emergency access.
2.  While the developer began master planning the Big Willow Creek Phase 6 area prior to the City amending the private street width and driveway standards, the standards were modified to ensure future development provides adequate access, snow removal area, and parking spaces and it is not reasonable to allow development under the old standards.
