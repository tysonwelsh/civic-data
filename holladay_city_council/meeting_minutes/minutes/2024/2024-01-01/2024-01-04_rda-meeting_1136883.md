I.    Call to Order – Vice Chairman Dahle
Vice Chairman Dahle called the RDA Meeting to order.

II.    Consideration of Resolution RDA 2024-01 –Appointment of Chair and Vice Chair
Board Member Fotheringham moved to APPROVE Resolution RDA 2024-01 –Appointing Ty
Brewer as Chair and Drew Quinn as Vice Chair of the RDA Board. Board Member Durham
seconded the motion. Vote on Motion: Board Member Durham-Aye; Board Member
Fotheringham-Aye; Board Member Dahle-Aye; Board Member Quinn-Aye; Board Member Gray-
Aye, Board Member Brewer-Aye. Resolution RDA 2024-01 was passed by a unanimous vote..

III.   Approval of Minutes of June 1 & 15, 2023
Board Member Brewer moved to approve the minutes. Board Member Fotheringham seconded
the motion. The Board voted in the affirmative and the minutes were approved.

IV. Other Business
There was no additional business to discuss.

V.     Adjourn RDA Meeting
II.Board Member Fotheringham moved to ADJOURN the RDA Meeting. Board Member Durham
seconded the motion. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Board.

The RDA meeting adjourned at 8:12 pm.
