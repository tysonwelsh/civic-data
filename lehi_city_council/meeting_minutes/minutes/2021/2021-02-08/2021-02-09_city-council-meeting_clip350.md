7. Consideration of Preliminary subdivision approval for Hidden Canyon Plat D, a 100-lot
   single-family development located approximately north of Fox Canyon Road.
   Matthew Loveland, with Fieldstone Homes, was representing the applicant. He noted that he
   was aware of the concerns with the trail location, but he believes that the location complies
   with the Area Plan. He also stated that the difference between the trail and sidewalk width is 3
   feet. A sidewalk would have 5 feet and the trail is 8 feet. He also noted that trails have been
   placed in front of homes in other areas of Traverse Mountain.

    The Council noted that they have received emails and phone calls regarding the safety concerns
    with a trail being located in front of a home. There was discussion of how to mitigate those
    concerns. Council Condie stated that there’s a safety concern with a standard sidewalk too.

    Karl Karren was representing the Homeowners Association. He stated that they would be
    responsible to maintain the trail, but he has concerns with that because the trail would cross
    the driveways. He said that he HOA does not want to take responsibility for an asphalt trail
    that will be driven on by homeowners. He also expressed concern with the easements being
    proposed.

    Parker Dixon stated that he is a recent homeowner and was concerned with the density being
    allowed in the area and was also concerned about the school capacity.

    Rob Ludlow expressed concern with the design. He stated that this is a significant safety issue
    because the trail will have a lot of traffic on it and it will be cutting across 16 driveways. He
    wants to see a safer design plan that doesn’t put children at risk. He also expressed concern
    with the easements and setbacks.

    Mr. Struthers noted that the developer has the right to max out his allowed density as long as
    they can engineer it and it meets all the design standards, which staff has determined that this
    proposal does that. He also stated that the setbacks and right of way comply with the Area
    Plan.
