                               ORDINANCE NO. 1519

       AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE BIG WILLOW CREEK 4TH AMENDED
                       DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it will be in the best interest of
the City to
allow development of the subject property in accordance with the Development
Agreement; and

WHEREAS, the Development Agreement addresses the development activities
permissible in Phase 2 of the project pending completion of the Jenson Access as
defined in the Development Agreement; and

WHEREAS, the Development Agreement will allow Ivory Homes to pull building
permits for ten (10) homes in Phase 2; and

WHEREAS, the Development Agreement will allow for Ivory Homes to receive a
temporary certificate of occupancy for a Parade of Homes home subject to the
limitations listed within the Development Agreement; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

Section 1. Development Agreement. The City of Draper approves the Development
Agreement provided in Exhibit A, otherwise known as the Big Willow Creek 4th
Amended Development Agreement.

Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be
severable.

Section 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date
of final passage.
PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 14th DAY OF DECEMBER 2021.
