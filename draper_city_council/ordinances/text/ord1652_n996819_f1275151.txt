City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1652

On May 20, 2025, the Draper City Council Approved Ordinance #1652 amending the
official Zoning Map of Draper City for approximately 0.85 acres of property from CBP
(business/ manufacturing park) to M1(light manufacturing) located at approximately
13488 S. 200 W. within Draper City otherwise known as the Granite Construction
Zoning Map Amendment. The complete Ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/

Published this 21ST day of May 2025
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder
