                                    RIVERTON, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-69

 APPROVING RIVERTON CITY'S ENTRY INTO A PROJECT AGREEMENT WITH
      TIM MAY AND CONTRACTOR TO BE SELECTED BY THE CITY

      WHEREAS, Riverton City (“City”) owns and operates a park known as Margaret Park at
12322 South Lampton View Drive; and

       WHEREAS, Mr. May owns property adjacent to Margaret Park; and

        WHEREAS, the City and Mr. May have experienced longstanding difficulties in
management of groundwater which percolates from Mr. May’s property, which in turn affects
City stormwater management systems; and

        WHEREAS, historically, the parties have disputed the cause of percolating groundwater
in the vicinity of Mr. May’s property; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that regardless of the
percolating groundwater’s cause, the parties have a shared interest in managing the same in a
manner which does not create a nuisance for area residents; and

        WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that City staff and Mr.
May have negotiated the terms of a suitable Project Agreement, whereby the City will share the
cost with Mr. May to make underground drainage improvements which will help prevent
groundwater from continually entering City gutters before draining from the area; and

       WHEREAS, the City and Mr.May desire to enter into Project Agreement with an
appropriate contractor attached hereto as Exhibit A, to provide for the construction of a
subsurface drain system which will connect with existing stormwater management systems.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. That the Mayor is authorized to sign Project Agreement with Mr. Tim May and a suitable
      contractor, in substantially similar form to the copy attached to this Resolution, upon
      verification that said agreement is approved by the Riverton City Attorney.

   2. This resolution, shall take effect immediately upon passage and acceptance as
      provided herein.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 18th day of
October, 2022 by the following vote:
