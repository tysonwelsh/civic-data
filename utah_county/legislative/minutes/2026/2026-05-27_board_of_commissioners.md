2. APPROVE AN AIRCRAFT PURCHASE AND SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH AIRMAX
PATROL LLC AND RAPID IMAGING SOLUTIONS LLC, AN ESCROW AGREEMENT WITH
AIRMAX PATROL LLC AND ZIONS BANK, AUTHORIZE COUNTY OFFICIALS TO EXECUTE
SUBSEQUENT CONTRACTUAL DOCUMENTATION, AND APPROVE THE EARLY RELEASE

OF WIRE TRANSFER.
-Shaun Bufton, Sheriff

(Timestamp: 6:27)

Commissioner Gordon expressed support for this project. The current plane the
county is using is from 1972. He thanked all who donated and helped get funds.

Commissioner Powers Gardner said this is a great example of how the community
can rally and give our law enforcement the tools that they need. We have a local
company giving us a huge discount, a local citizen giving us a donation, our
federal delegation is giving us a donation, and we have found ways to put this
over two budget years.

Commissioner Beltran also expressed thanks and said we can’t have our officers
in a 50-year-old airplane for liability, for safety and for efficiency.

Ben Van Noy (Utah County Deputy Attorney) said Zions Bank proposed some edits to the
escrow agreement and the documents have been uploaded to the Agenda
Program. He wanted to make sure that the bank edits are approved with the
understanding that the Sheriff's office is authorized to sign subsequent
documentation in accordance with the contract.
