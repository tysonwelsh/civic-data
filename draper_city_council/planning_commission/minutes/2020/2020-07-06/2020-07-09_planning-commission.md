I, Andrew Adams, Chair of the Draper City Planning Commission, do hereby determine
conducting an electronic meeting of the Draper City Planning Commission with an anchor location
presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be present at the anchor
location and hereby authorize the Draper City Planning Commission to conduct electronic
meetings without an anchor location.

The foregoing determination is based on the following facts:

      Federal, state and local leaders, including the Draper City Mayor and City Council, have
       all recognized a global pandemic caused by the spread of the COVID-19 virus.
      COVID-19 cases in Salt Lake County and Draper continue to increase at rates which pose
       a risk of overburdening the local health care system.
       It is difficult, if not impossible, to predict the number of attendees at any meeting and to
       manage issues regarding social distancing in order to comply with State Phased Guidelines.
       COVID-19 poses a continuing and immediate threat to the health, safety, and welfare of
       Draper City residents.
      The City has the technological capability to provide means by which the public may hear,
       or view and hear, the open portions of the meeting and to participate in public hearings.

The original of this written determination is on file in the City Recorder’s Office.


2. Public Hearing: Draper Cove Phase 2 Site Plan Request (Administrative Action)
       On the request of Adam Orme, representing Gould Plus Architects for a site plan on 0.42
       acres of property located at 12391 South 800 East. Application SPR-957-2020. Staff
       contact Jennifer Jastremsky, 801-576-6328, jennifer.jastremsky@draper.ut.us.

       Jennifer Jastremsky gave an overview of the item. This property was on the corner of 8 th
       East and Pioneer Road. Phase one was under construction and this application was a site
       plan request for phase two. This property had Town center land use designation and TC
       Town Center zoning designation. The applicant was requesting an extension of what was
       approved for phase one. This would be a two story building that housed retail office type
       uses. There would be five tenant spaces. It would front Pioneer Road and parking would
       be located in the back. They were required to have 21 to 26 spaces, and the applicant was
       proposing 22 onsite parking spaces. They were also providing 15 parking spaces on the
       street. The applicant was meeting landscaping code with 15% landscaping. They planned
       on having brick pavers and landscaping. The TC zone required fencing when ajacent to
       residential uses and required a decorative masonry wall, but the applicant wished to deviate
       from code due to a special circumstance. The house to the North was on the property line
       and already had fencing, so the applicant wanted to match the rest of the property fencing
       to existing fencing and not building an additional fence along the north of the property.
       The criteria for the deviation from this fencing code was to have an adequate buffer
       between the house and the property. The application met these criteria by planning to have
       double the amount of landscaping needed at the northside of the property. The building
       would be 30 feet tall with mostly brick and met code.

      He agreed it was important the homeowner had access to fix their home if needed.

      problem that the Planning Commission did not have the authority to solve. The deviation
      status might allow them to have this authority to protect the homeowner.

      Mr. Adams stated they could have an easement that would allow the homeowner to step
      onto the other property if they needed to fix something on the house.

      property if maintenance was needed.

4.Public Hearing: Bangerter Crossing 2 Office (Administrative Action)

      On the request of Elizabeth Cole representing Wadsworth Development Group for a site
      plan for an office building on 1.67 Acres of property located at 13673 S 100 E, Application
      SPR-951-2020.          Staff contact is Travis Van Ekelenburg, 801-576-6522,
      travis.vanekelenburg@draper.ut.us.

      Travis Van Ekelenburg gave an overview of the application. The developers were planning
      on building an office building on approximately 1.67 acres of property at 13673 S 100 E.
      Bangerter Crossing had been developed since 2006, and this office building would be the
      final development in the area. There were several properties involved in this proposal, and
      the applicant would be completing an amended plat application in conjunction with the
      request as well as an adjustment of the boundary lines. This property had a CR commercial
      regional zoning designation and offices were typical for this type of zone. The proposed
      office building was four stories with first floor covered parking. The development would
      meet the 20% landscaping requirement. It would also have walkways for pedestrians. The
      applicant was requesting a landscaping deviation from City Code section 09-23-030(b). A
      ten foot landscape buffer was required, but there was already a Chase bank with a 9.5 foot
      buffer. The applicant suggested adding 3 additional feet to the additional buffer. The
      development was required to have 145-177 parking spaces, but the site plan only had 134
