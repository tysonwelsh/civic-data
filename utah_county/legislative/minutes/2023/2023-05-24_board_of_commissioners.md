STUDY HISTORY — WHY IS THE PROVO RIVER
BEING STUDIED?

- Flood risk is never static and changes over time

- FEMA, Utah DEM and local communities periodically update their
flood maps and risk information to take into account changes in
topography, development, weather patterns and modeling
technology to inform current and future land use decisions

+ The current flood risk information being used by Provo City is from
1988. In paper format until 2020.

PROVO RIVER PROJECT

- Three jurisdictions (Utah County, Orem City, and Provo City)

- Current mapping is from the previous 1988 Flood Insurance Study; shows
levees as providing protection from 1%-annual-chance flood (or are
accredited on the map as being “certified”)

+ Provo River, Airport, and Lakewood Subdivision Levees are not certified
- This effort is NOT to certify the levee

- Uncertified levees cannot be shown on the new FIRM as providing
protection from flooding. 44 CFR 65.10

- Even brand new and perfectly maintained existing levee systems include
residual risk for those areas shown as protected in the event of a levee
failure or overtopping

(100.year) flood by levee, dike, oF other structures subject
