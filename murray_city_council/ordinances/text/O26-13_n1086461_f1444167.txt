ORDINANCE NO. 26-13

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 2.06.010, 2.06.020, AND
2.06.030 OF THE MURRAY CITY MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO
CITY OFFICERS GENERALLY

BE IT ENACTED BY THE MURRAY CITY MUNICIPAL COUNCIL:

Section 1. Purpose. The purpose of this ordinance is to amend sections
2.06.010, 2.06.020, and 2.06.030 of the Murray City Municipal Code relating to city
officers and coverage under a crime insurance policy. ,

Section 2. Amend sections 2.06.010, 2.06.020 and 2.06.030. Sections 2.06.010,
2.06.020, and 2.06.030 of the Murray City Municipal Code shall be amended to read as
follows:

2.06.010: REQUIREMENTS:

A. All statutory officers of the City, whether elected or appointed, shall, before they
enter upon the duties of their respective offices, take and subscribe the constitutional

i oath of office.
B. The City shall add each elected City officer and the City Treasurer to the City’s
crime insurance policy before an elected officer or City Treasuer may discharge the
duties of the officer's office. (Ord. 17-38)

2.06.020: PROOF OF CRIME INSURANCE:
officers shall be The City Recorder shall maintain proof of crime insurance acquired
under section 2.06.010. (Ord. 16-17)

2.06.030: CRIME INSURANCE COVERAGE:

The minimum amounts for which each of the respective officers of the City shall be
covered under the City’s crime insurance policy, except as otherwise provided by law,
are as follows:

A. Mayor: Ten thousand dollars ($10,000.00).
B. City Council members: Ten thousand dollars ($10,000.00).

C. City Treasurer: The amount set by the State Money Management Council. (Ord.
17-38)
