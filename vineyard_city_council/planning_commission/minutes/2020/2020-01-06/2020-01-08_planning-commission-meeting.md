6:32 PM          WORK SESSION

1.     CALL TO ORDER
Mayor Fullmer opened the meeting at 6:32 PM.

2.        WORK SESSION
     Anderson Geneva will present a concept plan of the following four Town Center Open
     Spaces:
     2.1 Lake Promenade/Station Plaza
     2.2 North Greenway
     2.3 Lakefront
     2.4 Geneva Park

Mayor Fullmer turned the time over to Anderson Geneva.

Bronson Tatton with Anderson Geneva reviewed where the Town Center was located. He also
reviewed their current plan and what they were working on. He mentioned that they would only
be looking at three of the park plans and introduced the open space areas they would be
discussing tonight. He also included their goals and objectives.

Mr. Tatton reviewed the Town Center map. He mentioned that DPZ, their master planning
consultants, would be transferring the plans into AutoCad, that there would be some code text
amendments, and then they would be working on the construction drawings. He explained that

they were currently working on the utility master drawings. They hoped to start moving dirt in
March or April.

The three areas for discussion tonight were:

The Promenade – He reviewed the Promenade map. This area would be more urban than the
other two parks. It was divided into six blocks: The Transit Plaza, Market Street (shared), a
square-about, a civic space, an urban park, and a water front park. He showed examples of what
the spaces could be like.

Mr. Tatton explained that the Transit Plaza would have six- and seven-story buildings, down to
the water front with three-story buildings. The area was also confined by streets. It also had to
accommodate the UTA and FrontRunner needs. Mr. Love asked about parking in the area. Mr.
Tatton replied that there would be on-street parking. He mentioned that they were hoping to
make changes to the Vineyard Connector located in blocks 5 and 6 on the west end of the
Promenade.

Waterfront Area – This was on the southwest end of the Vineyard Connector going north to 1600
North. Mr. Tatton showed examples of what could be built in the area. He explained that they
had to take into consideration that most of the area was State lands. He asked them to come up
with great ideas and then they would see what they could make happen. He mentioned that there
was a retention basin in the area that could be cleaned up and possibly used as a marina. He felt
that with the reputation of the lake the point of the shoreline was not a destination. They wanted
to clean up the reputation on the Vineyard end and make it a destination point.

Geneva Park - Mr. Tatton explained that this park had the most constraints because of the
contaminates. He said that they had commitments with the Utah Department of Environmental
Quality (DEQ) with what the area was currently planned for. He said that it was a long-term
holding site for impacted materials, which limited what they could put on the site. He said that it
would most likely have native landscaping. He explained what the area would look like. He
added that they needed to keep the existed intersection on the plan and add a road to it.

There was a discussion about the goals for the groups. He gave tips for their discussions. They
then divided into three groups at 6:52 PM.

3.        DESIGN CHARRETTE
     3.1 Town Center Open Spaces
         Those in attendance will be divided into three to four groups as part of the design
         charrette to discuss the plans and obtain comments from participants. There will be no
         action taken during this session. The design charrettes will not be recorded and no
         minutes will be taken.

Starting at 7:57 PM, each group gave a brief presentation about the things they had discussed.

Mr. Tatton reviewed the next steps. They would be compiling the ideas presented and then move
into conceptual designs. He proposed that the possible framework could be a public open house
and then finalize the master plan. He said that the Promenade might be the first phase, then the
waterfront, and then Geneva park.

4.     ADJOURNMENT
The meeting adjourned at 8:08 PM.
