#6 Workshop/Discussion – Powder Mountain Master Plan 68 
Brooke Hontz presented on the Powder Mountain Master Plan and gave an overview of the project and 69 
with a projected timeline for the project development. 70 
Christensen asked if everything that has been presented tonight follows the code. 71 
Ms. Hontz responded that there is a request with the CUP to rezone the land that is currently FR-40 to 72 
the Resort/Recreation Zone. 73 
Sands asked about the fiscal analysis and the revenue for the different taxing municipalities. 74 
Ms. Hontz responded that the revenues and expenses are both included in the report. 75 
Christensen asked about access from Cache County. 76 
Ms. Hontz responded there are no proposed changes for access from Cache County. 77 
Christensen asked about access for construction. 78 
Ms. Hontz responded that there are historic dirt road/ATV access but all access for the resort will 79 
continue as it currently is. 80 
Christensen asked about the tax revenue and how it is generated. 81 
Ms. Hontz explained how the tax revenue estimates were determined. 82 
Christensen asked about inspections. 83 
Ms. Hontz responded that the inspections that Cache County did, they inspectors drove around and 84 
through Weber. 85 
Sands asked about communication with Weber regarding the project. 86
