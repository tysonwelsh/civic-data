REQUEST
Utah Development Group (Applicant) has submitted an application for a zone change of a portion (approximately 0.55 acres) of a 1.02 acre parcel located at approximately 1368 E. Copper Creek Road from the CN (Planned Center-Neighborhood District) to the R-1-8 Zone (Single Family Residential District).

BACKGROUND
The parcel is located on the corner of Copper Creek Road and 1380 E. An existing building on the property has most recently been used as a reception center.

The parcel has two zoning designations, the west side is zoned CN and the east side is zoned R-1-8. The zoning designation (and existing land use) of the surrounding properties are as follows:

North:  R-1-8 (Copper Creek Road, church building)

[map/plat page 2 — no text; aerial zoning map, labels: R-1-8, Copper Creek Rd, CN, 1380 E, subject parcel outlined in red. Title block: REZ04192021-6030 :: Coppercreek (R-1-8), 1368 E. Coppercreek Road. Sandy logo. Scale bar 0 15 25 40 50 100.]

Two previous rezone applications affecting the property have been submitted over the past year. An application submitted in 2020 requested a zone change of the entire parcel to the RM(12) Zone. Another application submitted in January 2021 requested that the majority of the property be changed to the RM(10) Zone. Both applications were denied by the City Council.

The Applicant submitted a concept plan with the application. The concept plan depicts five residential lots. The final layout is ultimately subject to compliance with the Development Code upon a full review of a complete set of plans by City staff and approval by the Planning Commission as part of a future application for a subdivision/site plan.

The Application was presented in a neighborhood meeting held by Zoom Webinar on May 5, 2021. Notes of the meeting are attached.

FACTS AND FINDINGS
•  The Property is adjacent to both Copper Creek Road and 1380 E. Both streets are City streets.
•  The R-1-8 Zone (Development Code §21-20-02) includes, among others, the following requirements:
   o  Setbacks (regular lot):
      ▪  Front to living area: 30'
      ▪  Side: 8'
      ▪  Rear: 20'
   o  Height: Buildings shall be no higher than 35'.
•  The Sandy City Development Code does not allow for the submittal of the same zoning "amendment" application, after having previously denied, for a period of one year from the denial by the City Council. (Development Code §21-5-4)
•  The Sandy City General Plan contains applicable goals and policies, including the following:

CONCULSIONS
•  The Planning Commission makes recommendation to the City Council regarding zoning amendments to "regulate the use and development of land within all or any part of the area of the City." (Development Code of Sandy City 2008, 21-5-3(a)(1))
•  As with all new development, particularly infill development, any development of the Property will impact and be impacted by surrounding properties.
•  As both the zone being requested and the proposed zone boundary of this application are different from the zoning applications processed in 2020 and January 2021, City Staff considers this application to be requesting a different amendment.
•  The proposed land use of the subject property, being that the property is located between commercial uses and single family detached residential, could assist in transitioning and buffering between the different uses of the adjacent properties.
