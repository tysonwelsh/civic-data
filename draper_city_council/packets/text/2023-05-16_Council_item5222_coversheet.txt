Re: Ordinance 1578 Amending DCMC 3-5-100(E) regarding Appointments to the TRSSD Board Comments: The Draper City Council has created the Traverse Ridge Special Service District Administrative Control Board (“TRSSD Board”). Section 3-5-100(E) of the Draper City Municipal Code (“DCMC”) addresses appointments to the TRSSD Board.
DCMC Section 3-5-100(E) must comply with state law. Ordinance 1578 amends DCMC 3-5-100(E) to bring the TRSSD Board appointment process in line with state code. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Ordinance 1578 5/8/2023 Cover Memo
