                                      RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       ORDINANCE NO. 20-09

 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE TITLE 2, CHAPTER 105 TO
                     ADJUST COMPENSATION OF ELECTED OFFICIALS


         WHEREAS, Title 2, Chapter 105 of the Riverton City Code governs the salaries of elected
officials; and

        WHEREAS, the City Human Resources Department has conducted a salary survey of the City’s
elected officials and found their compensation to be well below market rates for paying elected officials
in municipalities located in Salt Lake and Utah Counties; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, following a public hearing required by law has
determined to amend Title 2, Chapter 105 of Riverton Code to base the compensation of elected officials
on a percentage of the mid-range for a grade 20 City employee;


NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as follows:


       Section 1.       Title 2, Chapter 105, of the Riverton City Code shall be, and hereby is, amended
                        to reflect the following changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

       Section 2.       This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.
