New Business

   1. Potential Property Acquisition and Disposition Policy
      Community & Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, talked about the
      incentive tools they have for this project that’s related to the potential purchase and sale
      agreement for the acquisition of the property located at 2280 South State Street that’s located
      in the Market Station Urban Renewal Area that was created in 2010. The URA Statute allows up
      to 20% of the property tax to be repurposed back to the City for affordable housing. He also
      went over the purchase and sale details as outlined in the presentation.

        Brandon Blaser of Blaser Ventures then talked about his vision for the project and reviewed the
       site plan and conceptual designs with the Board.

       Board Members Sanchez, Pinkney, Williams, and Bynum shared some comments they had
       regarding the project and thanked Mr. Blaser for his presentation.

       There will be updates and agenda items regarding this project at future meetings.

       A copy of the presentations from Mr. Weidenhamer and Mr. Blaser are attached and
       incorporated by this reference.
