CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to American Pavement Preservation for
        the Slurry Seal Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This was a formal bid. Five (5) bids
          were received. Staff recommends awarding the bid to American Pavement
          Preservation in the amount of $320,205.

      b. Consider approval to award bid to Geneva Rock Products for the Micro-
         Surface Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This was a formal bid. Two (2) bids
          were received. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Geneva Rock Products in
          the amount of $336,336.

      c. Consider approval to award bid to Holbrook Asphalt for the FY 2023
         Crack Seal Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: total of eight (8) bids were received.
          The low bid was from Superior Asphalt in the amount of $311,325; however, due
          to the local preference provision of our purchasing ordinance, Holbrook Asphalt, a
          local contractor, was able to match their bid. Staff recommends awarding the bid
          to Holbrook Asphalt in the amount of $311,325.

      d. Consider approval of a Memorandum of Agreement between The State of
         Utah Division of State Parks and the City of St. George for maintenance
         of the Red Hills Detention Basin.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: A floodwater detention basin will be
          constructed on the site. The property is within the Desert Reserve and Red Cliffs
          State Park. This agreement allows the City to construct and maintain the basin.
          Staff recommends approval.

      e. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Desert
         Edge Architecture for the design and construction management of the
         SunTran Maintenance and Administration Facility.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This project will be funded by a grant
           from UDOT. The facility will facilitate the buses, maintenance, and overnight
           parking of buses used for the proposed Springdale route. Staff recommends
           approval.

      f.   Consider approval of an Agreement for Purchase of Water Rights from
           the Dyer Family Trust.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: At times, water rights within the City of
           St. George become available for purchase. The City generally is interested in
           purchasing these water rights for future development of a water source to
           expand capacity in existing water sources. Staff recommends approval.

      g. Consider approval of a Real Property Purchase Agreement with Wells
         Fargo.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City has a need to purchase land on
           which to site a new city hall to meet the needs of the city and to allow expansion
           for the Police Department into the current city hall. This agreement is to
           purchase approximately 2.5 acres on Main Street just south of the Wells Fargo
           bank building. Staff recommends approval of the agreement, allowing the Legal
           Department to make final adjustments prior to signature.

      h. Consider approval of minutes from the meetings held on September 1,
         2022; September 22, 2022 (Regular Meeting); September 22, 2022
         (Work Meeting); September 22, 2022 (Joint Planning Commission Work
         Meeting); September 29, 2022 (Regular Meeting); September 29, 2022
         (Work Meeting); October 13, 2022; October 20, 2022; and October 27,
         2022.

ADOPT THE DOWNTON PLANNING AREA/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance adopting the Downtown Area Plan.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City has been undergoing the process
      of updating our General Plan, and in conjunction with this update, the City would like
      to present our Downtown Area Plan. This vision document focuses in on future
      planning within the downtown area. With the large size and many distinct
      neighborhoods in St. George, the City will benefit with several neighborhood-area
      plans. The first area plan to be presented for adoption is the Downtown Area Plan.
