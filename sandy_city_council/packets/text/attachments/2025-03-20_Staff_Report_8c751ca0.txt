Request
  The applicant, Ramin Nasrabadi, P.E., representing Rasht Properties,
 LLC, is requesting Planning Commission review of a commercial site
 plan and conditional use permit, for the property located at 8475 S. State
 St. The project proposal consists of three new single-story commercial
 buildings arranged on the site. Two buildings will front onto State St.,
 with a driveway between them that extends east to access the third
 building and the vehicle parking area. The buildings will contain retail
 space, flex warehousing, and light industrial units with accessory office
 space for small companies or businesses. Please see the attached
 applicant’s letter (Exhibit A) and the attached plans and application
 materials (Exhibit B).

Background
 The proposed site consists of two properties. The site is mostly vacant,
 but there is a restaurant on the west side of the north parcel, which will
 be demolished to make way for this redevelopment project. The north
 property is zoned Regional Commercial (RC), and the smaller south
 property is zoned Commercial Neighborhood, Historic Sandy
 Neighborhood (CN(HSN). The north parcel is approximately 376 feet
 deep east to west and has approximately 112 feet of frontage on

Public Notice and Outreach
 A notice for a neighborhood meeting was prepared and
 notices sent to surrounding property owners within 500 feet
 of the subject property on August 9, 2024, for a
 neighborhood meeting held on August 20, 2024. A notice
 sign was posted on the property on March 3, 2025, and a
 mailed notice was sent on that day and an amended notice
 sent on March 11, 2024 to include the Conditional Use Case
 and correct the notice map exhibit. Notices for the Planning
 Commission meeting were posted in required locations and
 on required public notice web sites.

Neighborhood Meeting
 The neighborhood meeting was hosted on Zoom and city
 staff and the applicant explained the project to the attendees
 who participated. Several property owners from the residential area to the south and east of the proposed site participated in
 the presentation and asked questions about the nature of the proposed development and the types of business contemplated
 to be in it. Concerns were expressed about operating hours for the businesses, light spill into the neighborhood and possible
 noise that might be generated by the businesses.

Analysis
 This staff report includes two action items for consideration by the Planning Commission. The site plan review will be
 analyzed first, followed by an analysis of the conditional use request. Following the analysis of the two requests, staff will
 present two separate suggested motions, each to be considered and voted upon by the Planning Commission in sequence.
 Commercial Site Plan Review
 The applicant has obtained approval from UDOT to install a driveway approach and curb and gutter along the approximate
 167-foot frontage. This approval will be finalized through the UDOT permitting process and deeds of dedication, as needed
 to set the curb and gutter, park strip and sidewalk at the proper distances from the centerline of State Street.

 Internal Site Circulation
 The proposed internal site circulation of vehicles will be entirely from a new State St. driveway approach and extended east
 thru the property and ending short of the east property line. A hammerhead style turn-around for trucks will be located
 between the two buildings on the north side of the driveway as required by city standards for both delivery vehicles, garbage
 collection, and fire department equipment vehicles. Customer and employee vehicles will maneuver within the driveway
 width to exit the property back to State St. Driveways have been stubbed to the property lines, to the north in two places
 and to the south in one place for possible future cross-access connection opportunities, when future commercial development
 occurs. These stubbed driveways will be blocked with an 8-foot-high masonry wall between the zero side yard setback
 buildings, located along the north and south sides of the project. Internal site vehicle circulation will be adequate for the
 development.
 Pedestrian access will be extended via a sidewalk from the State St. frontage to the east end of the buildings. A new park
 strip and sidewalk will be installed on the State St. frontage.

 On-site Customer and Employee Vehicle Parking
 Required on-site customer and employee vehicle parking has been calculated using the mix of the three land use types
 proposed for the project. There are 38 parking stalls required, and this is the number provided. Off-street vehicle parking
 will be adequate for the development.

 As noted in the background portion of this report, the southern parcel of this proposed project is in Historic Sandy. The
 Sandy Historic Sandy Neighborhood Master Plan contains guidelines for commercial development within the district
 (including predominantly brick or masonry façade, historically compatible window arrangements, etc.). These guidelines
 need to be reviewed by the Historic Committee with a view to incorporating the historic character of the district into the
 new development to aid in compatibility. This means that a review and recommendation on the south building design and
 materials is needed by that committee. Staff is proposing a condition of approval that requires this site plan to be reviewed
 by the committee and to hear the committee’s input as to building architectural details, materials and colors for the south
 property parcel portion only. It is the intention of staff to follow up on this detail with the historic committee and during
 finalization of the site plan review, incorporate the committee’s recommendations as reasonably possible.
 Building colors and materials meet the requirements of the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards. Building colors are
 as shown on the included building elevation drawings and perspective renderings.
 Landscaping and Irrigation
 Wherever commercial development abuts residential zoning, an 8-foot-high masonry wall and 10-foot-wide landscaped
 buffer strip is required and has been provided. The proposed water-wise landscaping and irrigation plans meet the city
 requirements as specified in Sandy City Development Code section 21-25.

 Commercial Signage
 No signage approval is included in this site plan review. Signage is regulated by separate permits and review after business
 license issuance and will meet City requirements.

 Fencing
 This project will consist of zero lot line setbacks for the buildings and surrounding 8-foot-high masonry walls on the north,
 east and south sides of the projects.
 Staff Concerns
 Staff is concerned about the north property line of the project, where it abuts the existing mobile home park. There is an
 existing fence for the mobile home park that encroaches nearly two feet onto this property at a point located approximately
 in the middle of the north property line and then tapers back to property line as the fence extends east and west. This fence
 encroachment must be removed so that the proposed buildings can be located at the zero-lot line setback. A condition of
 approval to this effect is suggested by planning staff.

 Staff is also suggesting that the applicant combine the two existing property parcels through the City’s administrative parcel
 consolidation process, to unify the project into one property parcel and parcel identification number, tax noticing and
 building code and zoning administration.
 As noted above, the architecture of the south building needs further refinement to accommodate the design guidelines of
 the Historic Sandy area. Another architectural related concern is about the compliance with the 4 sided architecture
 requirements on the zero property line building sides. Currently they are indicated as a blank wall with no defined finish
 material.

Recommendations:
Site Plan Review
  Motion #1. Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine preliminary site plan review is complete for the
  proposed Rasht Development Commercial Site Plan, located at 8475 S. State St., based on the following findings and subject
  to the following conditions:
Findings:
   1. That the proposed project will meet all the applicable requirements of the Sandy City Development Code and the
       Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as to building architectural design, materials and colors.
    2. That the proposed new buildings and site improvements to this property will improve the visual appearance of the
       streetscape on this portion of State St. and hopefully be the beginning of more commercial redevelopment and
       improvement within the existing RC and CN(HSN) zoning districts.

Conditions:
  1. That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior to the start of any construction.
      The final site plan shall comply with all Development Code requirements and those modifications required or allowed
      by the Planning Commission.

 warehouse/wholesale and light industrial uses within 250 feet distance of existing residential zoning, for the proposed Rasht
 Development project, based upon the following finding and subject to the following conditions:

  Findings:
    1. Reasonably anticipated negative impacts associated with the proposed development will be adequately mitigated with
        site improvements and building locational and structural improvements.

  Conditions:
   1. That the operational hours be limited to the normal Sandy City business hours of 7:00 A.M. to 10:00 P.M. weekly.
