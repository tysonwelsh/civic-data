WESTERN WEBER PLANNING COMMISSION
3/7/2023

  Minutes for Western Weber Planning Commission Work Session meeting of March 7, 2023, held in the Weber County Commission
  Chamber-Break-Out Room, 2380 Washington Blvd. Floor 1 Ogden UT at 5:00 pm.

  Pledge of Allegiance
   Roll Call

  Members Present: Andrew Favero, Jed McCormick, Casey Neville, Wayne Andreotti, Sarah Wichern. Bren Edwards, Cami Clontz
  Pledge of Allegiance

  Staff Present: Rick Grover, Director; Charlie Ewert, Principal Planner; Bill Cobabe, Planner; Felix Lleverino, Planner; Liam Keogh,
  Attorney; June Nelson, Secretary

   Work Session
   WS1 A discussion on a proposal to re-zone property at 7100 W 550 S from A-2 to RE-15. The development is also known as Vaquero
   Village phase 2. A total of 109 lots is depicted on the concept plan. Presenter: Pat Burns

   Mr Burns presented information on Vaquero Village, Phase 2.

   WS2: Review and discussion regarding proposed ordinances to implement the Western Weber General Plan. The proposal also
   affects ordinances that govern development in the Ogden Valley Planning Area. The proposal is pertaining to lot area, design
   standards, dark sky lighting, block lengths, pathway and street connectivity, and related amendments. Planner: Charlie Ewert

   WS3: Review and discussion regarding the proposed amendments to the Form-Based zone, and related amendments. Planner:
   Charlie Ewert
   WS3-WS3
  Principal Planner Charlie Ewert discussed proposed ordinances pertaining to dark sky and signs. There was also review and discussion
  regarding the proposed amendments to the Form-Based zone.

   Adjourn at 8:22

   Respectfully Submitted,
  June Nelson
  Lead Office Specialist
