                                      RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       ORDINANCE NO. 24-19

  AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN TO PROFESSIONAL OFFICE AND
  REZONING TO C-PO THE .56-ACRE PARCEL LOCATED AT 13083 SOUTH REDWOOD
                   ROAD, COURTNEY JENSEN, APPLICANT

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

        WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and,

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to amend the Riverton City General Plan to Professional Office and the Zoning Map from RR-22 to C-PO
for the approximately .56 acre parcel located at 13083 South Redwood Road.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is, amended
                              to reflect the described changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

                Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 1st day of October
