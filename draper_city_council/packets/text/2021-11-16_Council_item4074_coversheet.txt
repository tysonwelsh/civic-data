From:
CC:  Date: November 16, 2021
Re:  Comments: Under Utah Code 20A-7-401.3, municipalities are required to adopt voter participation areas ("VPA") every ten years after U.S. census data has been disbursed for the purposes of ballot initiatives and referenda. Staff has received the census data and created the maps for the council's consideration. The statute requires VPAs meet the following criteria: 1) compact, 2) contiguous, and 3) roughly proportionate in total population. Cities of the third class are required to adopt four VPAs.
The two VPA maps for the council's consideration meet this criteria. Although neither map has VPAs with exactly 25% of the city's population, each map's VPAs come within approximately 2% of that target. Both maps meet the criteria of compact an contiguous.   The findings are as follows: For Option 1
The VPA map complies with state law;
The VPA map sufficiently gives voters an opportunity to exercise the co-extensive legislative authority granted by the Utah Constitution through the initiative and referenda process;
The VPA map serves the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper.
For Option 2
The VPA map complies with state law;
The VPA map sufficiently gives voters an opportunity to exercise the co-extensive legislative authority granted by the Utah Constitution through the initiative and referenda process;
The VPA map serves the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper.
