Highlights

05/19/2026 @ 1917, Lost Hikers, Red Reef Trail, Red Cliffs Desert Reserve — Man Hours—
108 (Non Certified) 4 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Red Reef Trail on a report of two hikers who were lost
near Red Reef Trail. The two individuals were instructed to stay where they made the call from. A
drone was utilized to confirm the location of the hikers and SAR members responded to their GPS
coordinates. They were provided fluids to re hydrate. SAR members then utilized a series of lifting and
lowering rope systems to assist the hikers safely back to Incident Command. They declined medical
evaluation and were returned to their vehicle.
05/28/2026 @ 1223, Drowning, Sand Hollow Reservoir — Man Hours — 45.5 (Non
Certified) 13 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Sand Hollow State Park on a report of a 22 year old
male who had gone under water while swimming to the swim dock with a group of friends. The victim
was located deceased by SAR divers approximately five and a half hours after the call was dispatched
at a depth of 19 feet. Evidence was gathered and the deceased male was retrieved and placed in the care
of the mortuary.

05/29/2026 @1418, Missing Person, General Steam Rd — Man Hours — 170 (Non Certified)
19 (Certified)

On 05/29/2026 Deputies responded to General Steam Rd area after a vehicle belonging to a
missing male out of Santa Clara area had been located. The male had been missing for approximately
five days when the vehicle was located. There did not appear to be any recent signs of activity around
the vehicle. SAR K9 Scout was deployed in an attempt to locate him. STAR9 was also requested to fly
the area. Law enforcement units searched for approximately four hours without locating the missing
male. A plan was made at that point to return with Search and Rescue (SAR) at first light and resume
the search.

At approximately 0730 the following morning, deputies, SAR units, STAR9, and BLM rangers
returned to the location of the vehicle. They began an extensive search of approximately two square
miles around the vehicle location utilizing hiking teams, drones, the helicopter, and K9 Scout. SAR
members searched the area for approximately 10 hours. However, despite the search parties extensive
efforts, they were unable to locate the male. At that point, it was determined to call off the search due to
a lack of new evidence showing that the male was still in the area.

On 05/31/2026, Deputies returned to the area and again attempted to locate the missing male
using K9 scout. They were unable to locate any further sign that the missing male was still in the area.

Additional Calls

05/24/2026 @1219, ATV Rollover, Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 10.5 (Non Certified) 3
(Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Sand Mountain on a report of an ATV rollover. The
female patient reportedly had a hand and wrist injury. SAR units located the patient and she was
transported back to Incident Command. She was then loaded in an ambulance and transported to the

hospital.

05/24/2026 @ 2123, Stranded Climbers, Island in the Sky, Snow Canyon — Man Hours —
34 (Non Certified) 2 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Island in the Sky in Snow Canyon State Park on a
report of a group of three who were part way down a rappelling route and unable to find the next
anchor. The group was also out of water and food. SAR High Angle Team responded to the location of
the stranded group. They were provided fluids to re hydrate. They were then safely guided down the
rest of the rappelling route and back to Incident Command. All three individuals declined medical
evaluation.
05/31/2026 @ 1426, Injured Hiker, Water Canyon — Man Hours — 45 (Non Certified) 4
(Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Water Canyon after an SOS beacon was activated by a
male and female who were on a hike. The male in hiker reportedly sustained an ankle injury as a result
of falling and was unable to walk. SAR members arrived and began hiking to the patient location. Due
to the extreme terrain, UHP Helicopter STAR9 was requested to assist. STAR9 arrived at the patient
location and both individuals were hoisted from their location to Maxwell Park in Hildale. The male
was transported to the hospital via ambulance and the female was returned to her vehicle. All SAR
members were accounted for as they returned to Incident Command.

06/09/2026 @1327, Side by Side Rollover, Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 10 (Non
Certified) 2 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Sand Mountain on a report of a side by side rollover
involving two occupants. One had a reported hand injury and the other a shoulder injury. SAR
members, DNR Rangers, and Hurricane Valley Fire medics responded to the scene of the accident.
They were loaded in a side by side and transported back to Incident Command. The two patients were
then evaluated in the ambulance and declined medical transport.

06/13/2026 @ 1342, Injured Climber, The Subway, Zion National Park — Man Hours —
27.5 (Non Certified) 2.5 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to the Subway on a report of a male who had a reported
ankle injury. SAR members responded to the location of the patient and he was stabilized. STAR9 was
requested to assist due to the location. The patient was hoisted from the area and back to Incident
Command. He was then loaded in an ambulance and transported to the hospital.

06/13/2026 @ 1646, Boating Incident, Quail Creek — Man Hours — 24 (Non Certified) 4.5
(Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) Dive Team responded to Quail Lake on a report of a boat that had
sunk near the boat ramp. Dive team members utilized lift bags to recover the boat. It was then removed
from the water. A large quantity of fuel spilled near the boat ramp as a result of the crash. A fuel spill
kit was deployed to remove the fuel from the water.
