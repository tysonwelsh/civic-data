                                         HERRIMAN CITY SAFETY
                                       ENFORCEMENT AREA MINUTES
                                                  Wednesday, June 9, 2021
                                                   Approved July 6, 2021

The following are the minutes of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. The meeting was held on Wednesday,
June 9, 2021 at 6:30 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman,
Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered
to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Vice Chair David Watts

Trustees Present:          Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith

Staff Present: Interim City Manager Wendy Thomas, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Community
               Development Director Blake Thomas, Police Chief Troy Carr, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom,
               Finance Director Alan Rae, Director of Operations Monte Johnson, Public Works Director Justun
               Edwards, City Attorney Chase Andrizzi, Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette, Deputy
               Director of Parks, Recreation and Events Anthony Teuscher, City Planner Michael Maloy,
               Economic Development Manager Heather Upshaw, City Engineer Jonathan Bowers, Building
               Official Cathryn Nelson, Unified Fire Authority Division Chief Anthony Widdison, Deputy Chief
               of Police Cody Stromberg, and Sergeant Brent Adamson.

6:30 PM - SPECIAL MEETING:
       1. Call to Order
Vice Chair David Watts called the meeting to order at 6:44 p.m.

                  1.1.    Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes of
                          June 9, 2021
Trustee Shields moved to have the minutes of June 9, 2021 be prepared and distributed to each member of the Herriman City Safety
Enforcement Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary, if after ten days, there
are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes are made and the board
is in agreement. Trustee Smith seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

         2. Consent Agenda
              2.1. Approval of the May 27, 2020 HCSEA Minutes

       3. Discussion and Action Items
               3.1. Discussion and Consideration of a Resolution Adopting a Final Budget for the
                    Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area; Making Appropriations for Support of the
                    Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area for the Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2021
                    and Ending June 30, 2022, and Determining the Rate of Tax and Levying Taxes Upon
                    All Real and Personal Property within Herriman City – Alan Rae, Finance Director
Finance Director Alan Rae explained this Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area budget needed to be approved by
the Board of Trustees as depicted in the Herriman City Budget.

Trustee Clint Smith moved to approve Resolution R21-01 adopting a final budget and making appropriations for the support of the
Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area for the period beginning July 1, 2021 and ending June 30, 2022, and determining the rate of
tax to be set at .001728 for levying taxes upon all real and personal property within the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area.
Trustee Shields seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Trustee Jared Henderson                             Aye
Trustee Steven Shields                              Aye
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn                                Aye
Trustee Clint Smith                                 Aye
Vice Chair David Watts                              Aye
The motion passed unanimously.

         4. Adjournment
Trustee Ohrn moved to adjourn the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area meeting at 6:57 p.m. Trustee Shields seconded the motion,
and all present voted aye.
