2.
Project #19-111. Public Hearing and Discussion, Cedar Crest Village Overlay application, located in eastern Hoytsville area, containing approximately 1,085 acres. Applicant: Cedar Crest Village Overlay Committee with representatives from Ivory Development and Larry H. Miller.
County Staff: Patrick Putt, Community Development Director; Peter Barnes, Planning Director; and Laura Kuhrmeyer, County Planner
(6:01 P.M.)
Scott Lalli,
Larry H. Miller Real Estate
, provided a background on the Cedar Crest Village Overlay. Mr. Lalli and his team presented an overview for discussion and public hearing. The team includes Wade Budge, Kort Utley, Ryan Hales, Greg Flint and Skyler Tolbert.
Laura Kuhrmeyer,
Planner
, briefly reviewed the Village Overlay process. (6:40 P.M.)
