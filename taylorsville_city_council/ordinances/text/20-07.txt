  TO TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE CHAPTER 5. 06: BUSINESS LICENSE
                                               FEES.


       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on July
1, 2020, to consider, among other things, approving amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL
CODE Chapter 5. 06: Business License Fees; and


       WHEREAS, TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 5. 06.040 currently lists fees for
 alcohol and beer" sales and for class A and class B private clubs, which no longer exist under the
Alcoholic Beverage Control Act; and


       WHEREAS, the City desires to amend the business license fee schedule to more
accurately reflect the types of licenses available and the fee for each; and

       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 5. 06: Business License Fees
are hereby approved and are designated by interlineating the words to be deleted and underlining
the words to be added, as follows:


                                         CHAPTER 5. 06
                                  BUSINESS LICENSE FEES

monitoring records, preparing business reports, and verifications.
