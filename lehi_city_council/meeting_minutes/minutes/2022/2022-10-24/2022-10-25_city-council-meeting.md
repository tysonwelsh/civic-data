Pre Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present, Councilor
Koivisto gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Reports
Utah Wellbeing Project Update- Dr. Courtney Flint
Bailey Holdaway presented in place of Dr. Courtney Flint. She said that the most recent survey
showed that personal wellbeing in Lehi is above average for the State. She also noted that survey
respondents believed that the population and economic growth was too fast. She said that they
are planning to skip 2023, but they will conduct the survey again in 2024.

2.2 IPP Power Plant Renewal Project Update
Cameron Cowan, the General Manager at Intermountain Power Agency, updated the Council on
the IPP Power Plant Renewal Project and explained the benefits to Lehi City. He stated that they
have been able to supply energy with no state tax or economic development incentives. They
have invested in renewable energy with opportunities to develop advanced clean energy storage
for hydrogen production.

3. Agenda Questions
None.

4. Administrative Report
BJ Benson updated the Council on the tree removal in Olympic Park. They plan to begin on
November 7. He said some residents understand the need to remove the trees for safety and
others are still in opposition.
