Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce Agenda Item 3.1:

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Susan Harding to approve the preliminary plat of Victoria Park, Plat G located at 726 North 600 East in the R8 zone

Staff Presentation:  Victoria Park, Plat G is a one-lot subdivision on 600 East. The proposed lot is currently two lots of record with the smaller one being an unbuildable lot due to being too small for the R8 zone. This small lot is 5,663 square feet and in the R8 zone, the minimum lot size allowed is 8,000 square feet. 

These two lots are currently lots of record, so in order to combine them a preliminary plat needs to be approved and a final plat will be recorded at the County. The new lot will be 14,375 square feet which more than meets the minimum requirement of 8,000 square feet. 



Recommendation: Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the preliminary plat of Victoria Park, Plat G located at 726 North 600 East in the R8 zone.

Mr. Cook noted that the house to the east seemed to be really close to the property line. Ms. Vargas clarified that that structure was an accessory structure. 

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Roberts moved to approve the preliminary plat of Victoria Park, Plat G located at 726 North 600 East in the R8 zone. Ms. Bentley seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer, and Marisa Bentley. The motion passed unanimously. 

Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce Agenda Item 3.2:

Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Corbin Church to vacate Riverside Estates, Plat A and approve Riverside Estates, Plat A Amended located generally at 1089 South Carterville Road in the R20 zone.

Staff Presentation:  The applicant is seeking to revise the final plat for Riverside Estates in order to move the detention basin from the south area of Lot 3 to the northeast corner of Lot 3. This will allow them to put a future indoor tennis court in the southern end of the lot. 
At the same time, the neighbor to the west of Lot 3 at 1123 South Carterville Road, is currently on septic. The sewer main line is to the east of Lot 3. A Public Utility/Sewer Easement in favor of this lot has been created so the sewer line can be run from the main line, through Lot 3 and into 1123 South Carterville Road.

Recommendation: Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Riverside Estates, Plat A and approve Riverside Estates, Plat A Amended located generally at 1089 South Carterville Road in the R20 zone.

Mr. Spencer asked how much excavation is involved in moving a detention basin from one place to another. 

Mr. Dudley explained that detention basins are landscape swales and that they are designed to run with the land. It would be a very easy process to move a detention basin. 

Mr. Cochran agreed that it would be easy. 

Mr. Dudley added that the other request involved in this plat amendment is to put a gate on the front of the private drive that will make this a gated community and would be similar to the one at the Berkshires subdivision right now.  

Chair Cochran opened the public hearing. There was no public comment. Chair Cochran closed the public hearing.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Cook moved to vacate Riverside Estates, Plat A and approve Riverside Estates, Plat A Amended located generally at 1089 South Carterville Road in the R20 zone. Chair Cochran seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Barry Roberts, Ross Spencer, Haysam Sakar, and Marisa Bentley. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce Agenda Item 3.3:

Agenda Item 3.3 is a request by Adam Larson to amend Appendix A of the Orem City Code by enacting Standard Land Use Code 0303 Shave Ice Shack; and amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone of approximately 0.84 acres from the R12 zone to the C1 zone at approximately 1323 East 800 North. 

Staff Presentation:  The applicant is proposing this rezone and text amendment so his business can be accommodated at the mini golf course located generally at 1323 East 800 North. He applied for a temporary site plan in early 2020 and was denied because businesses are not allowed in residential zones. The existing mini golf course is a legal nonconforming use in the R12 zone. His business could not be absorbed into the mini golf course business because that would expand its non-conforming use, which is not allowed. After working with the staff for a few months to come up with a solution that would permit his business in that location, the applicant is now ready to propose this rezone and text amendment. 

The applicant originally hoped to rezone the property to C2, but staff informed him that even though the general plan designation of CC (Community Commercial) would allow for a C2 rezone, staff pointed out that while a shave ice shack might make sense in that location, other C2 uses probably wouldn’t. We expressed that a C1 rezone would probably make more sense for this parcel as there is a C1 zone adjacent to this property. 

The applicant is proposing first to create a new SLU code for shave ice shacks. Currently, there is no SLU code specifically for these businesses; they fall under fast food restaurants 5811 which are not allowed in C1 zones. This new SLU code would allow shave ice shacks in all zones that fast food restaurants are permitted and would also allow them in the C1 zone. 

The applicant is proposing to rezone the property from R12 to C1. The R12 zone is a residential zone that provides single-family-detached housing with a minimum lot size of 12,000 square feet. The existing mini golf course is a legal, non-conforming use in this zone which dates back to the Cascade Golf Course that has since been removed. The C1 zone provides light commercial uses, including financial uses, office uses, and medical uses. Currently, no restaurant or other related land uses are permitted in the C1 zone. 

This proposed text amendment would allow shave ice shacks in all C1 zones within the City. Currently, there are three general areas within the City that host C1 zones. The first is this area the applicant is proposing the amendment at, near the mouth of Provo Canyon along Canyon Parkway. The second is located generally along Center Street between I-15 and 300 West. The third is located near the University Mall at the edges of commercial development in that area before coming to residential land uses. It is evident from the locations of each of these C1 zones that the purposes of the C1 zone are currently being fulfilled as outlined in the Orem City Code 22-8-3, “The C1 zone is established to promote non-retail commercial uses, such as offices and financial institutions, as the primary use and to encourage development in such a manner so as to be compatible with adjacent residential uses.” 

Each of the C1 zones borders at least one residential zone and serves to buffer these areas from more intense commercial land uses. If this request were granted, it would allow shave ice shacks to be much closer to residential zones. As all C1 zones host only office, medical, or financial uses. Typically shave ice shacks are temporary in nature, however, could be permanent provided they meet all applicable ordinance requirements.

Recommendation: Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to amend Appendix A of the Orem City Code by enacting Standard Land Use Code 0303 Shave Ice Shacks; and amending Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone of approximately 0.84 acres from the R12 zone to the C1 zone at approximately 1323 East 800 North.

Mr. Spencer asked if the City anticipated that the ski shop site to the west of this site would be eventually included in the C1 zone?

Ms. Vargas said that the City has not heard anything from the ski shop.

Chair Cochran asked if it would cause any heart burn if shave ice shops were allowed in all C1 zones?

Mr. Sakar did not think so. He indicated his family lived in the area and would not be opposed to the change. 

Chair Cochran asked if this change would detract from the business aspect of the C1 zone. He invited the applicant to speak. 

Mr. Larson indicated that he did not think his business would. He also noted that he had had recent conversations with the owner of the ski shop. He had expressed to Mr. Larson the possibility of doing a restaurant eventually in the far future but had no intentions to do so now. 

Chair Cochran opened the public hearing. There was no public comment. Chair Cochran closed the public hearing. 

Planning Commission Action: Ms. Bentley moved to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to amend Appendix A of the Orem City Code by enacting Standard Land Use Code 0303 Shave Ice Shack; and amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone of approximately 0.84 acres from the R12 zone to the C1 zone at approximately 1323 East 800 North. Chair Cochran seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer, and Marisa Bentley. The motion passed unanimously.
