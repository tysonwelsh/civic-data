 VARIANCES
 B-6-2022                     MATTHEW OBORN, representing Wasatch Commercial Builders, is requesting two
                              variances from the West Valley City Code. Both variance requests are from Section 7-6-
                              303(3)(a). This section requires that the minimum setback adjacent to a street be permanently
                              landscaped and that fences above 3 feet in height be prohibited within the minimum setback
                              adjacent to a street. The applicant is asking that both requirements be waived due in part to the
                              existing right-of-way being a dead-end street and that the required landscaping and fencing
                              location is not visible to the general public using Printers Row (2300 South). The property is
                              located at 1820 West Printers Row and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at
                              801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)
