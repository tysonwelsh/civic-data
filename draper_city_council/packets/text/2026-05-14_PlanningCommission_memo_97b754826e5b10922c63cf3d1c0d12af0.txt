Re: Public Meeting: The Cove at Bear Canyon Phase III Plat Amendment Request
(Administrative Item)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 1.00 acre located on the west side of Grizzly Hollow Cove, at approximately
12222 S. Grizzly Hollow Cove. The property is currently zoned RA2 (Residential
Agricultural, 20,000 ft² min. lot size) and is within the Sensitive Hillside Overlay. The
applicant is requesting to split one existing lot into two lots of approximate equal size.



The Cove at Bear Canyon Phase III subdivision was originally recorded in March 1994. This
application is the first amendment to the recorded plat. The lot affected by this
amendment is Lot 301 of the original plat.


Finding for approval:
  1. There is good cause for the amendment, and no public street or municipal utility
     easement will be vacated or amended.
