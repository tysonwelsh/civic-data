AN ORDINANCE OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL DISSOLVING 
THE MAGNA WEST MAIN STREET NEIGHBORHOOD DEVELOPMENT 
PROJECT AREA 
The County Legislative Body of Salt Lake County ordains as follows: 
SECTION I.  The community reinvestment project area created by the Redevelopment 
Agency of Salt Lake County and known as the “Magna West Main Street Neighborhood 
Development Project Area” is hereby dissolved.

description of the Project Area:
