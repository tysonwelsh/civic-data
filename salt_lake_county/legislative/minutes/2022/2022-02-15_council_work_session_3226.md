22-0136
Attachments: Staff Report
Resolution for Seventeenth Extension of Public Health 
Emergency (April 15 2022)(2-9-22)(AATF)
Sponsor: Chair Laurie Stringham (Approx. 10 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Council Member Stringham  stated this resolution would extend the public 
health emergency to April 15th, after which the eligible Federal Emergency 
Management Act (FEMA) reimbursable percentage will drop from 100 
percent to 75 percent.  The resolution includes intent that the emergency end 
on April 16th.     
Council Member DeBry, seconded by Council Member Winder Newton, 
motioned to approve the resolution as stated.  
Council Member Theodore  asked if the County would still have 30 days to 
be reimbursed by FEMA in the event the Council voted not to extend the 
order any further.
Mr. Darrin Casper , Deputy Mayor of Finance and Administration, stated 
expenditures will be eligible for reimbursement for 30 days past the end of 
the emergency, but the time it takes to compile expenses is likely to be longer 
than 30 days.  If the Council chooses not to extend the emergency, the 
30-day period for eligibility will start today.
Council Member Theodore made a substitute motion not to extend the public

3.8 Legislative Update 22-0137
Attachments: Staff Report
Presenter: Kara Trevino, Legislative Director, Council (Approx. 60 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Ms. Kara Trevino , Intergovernmental Relations Manager, Council Office, 
reviewed the following legislative bills:
- 1st Sub SB 162 County Governance Amendments
Ms. Trevino stated the County had previously decided to hold off on supporting this bill .  
The language has since been changed to reflect the status quo on how Salt Lake County 
runs its budget process. This bill passed out of committee and is now on the Senate floor.  
Council Member Newton stated this language gives the Council options to change 
definitions by ordinance.  It provides for more separation of powers.  
Mr. Darrin Casper , Deputy Mayor of Finance and Administration, stated he likes the 
modifications to this bill and appreciates the effort.  
Council Member Stringham asked if the County Auditor supported this bill.
Mr. Christopher Harding, Salt Lake County Auditor, stated he does. 
A motion was made by Council Member Winder Newton, seconded by Council Member 
Snelgrove, to support SB 162. The motion carried by a unanimous vote.
- SB 200 Revisions to Property Tax
Ms. Trevino stated this is a bill the Assessor ’s group has been working on through the 
Utah Association of Counties (UAC).  
Page 7 of 12
Council Work Session Meeting Minutes February 15, 2022
Mr. Chris Stavros , Salt Lake County Assessor, stated he would like to monitor this bill . 
The ultimate goal would have been to combine the North American Industry Classification 
System (NAICS) codes with the filing requirement because HB 199 reduces the filing 
requirement on personal property from five years to one.  He feels there is no point in 
collecting the data not to utilize it.  He would prefer that the Council monitor for now. 
Council Member Snelgrove  stated he agrees and cannot see why NAICs codes are 
required.  It could be misinterpreted by different people.  
Council Member Bradley asked if the other county assessors agreed with Mr. Stavros.  
Mr. Stavros stated they do.
The Council decided to continue to monitor the status of SB 200.
- HB 199 Tangible Personal Property Tax Amendments
Ms. Trevino stated HB 199 is moving quickly through the process.  It has a lot of 
support.  UAC and the assessors oppose it, but the sponsor is resistant to backing off .  
This bill will lead to more audits.
A motion was made by Council Member Winder Newton, seconded by Council Member 
Snelgrove, that the Council change its status of HB 199 to oppose. The motion carried by a 
unanimous vote.
- SB 20 Property Tax Amendments
Mr. Stavros stated SB 20 is a telecommunications bill sponsored by Senator Bramble .  
This bill would change how telecoms are valued; it would go from being centrally to 
locally assessed.  The passage is inevitable, so he is working with the sponsor to add a 
funding component to hire the experts needed to properly value those properties.  His 
recommendation is that the Council continue to monitor.
- HB 362 Taxing Entity Amendments
Ms. Trevino stated this bill would allow Community Redevelopment Agencies (CRAs) 
to force the County’s increment into a deal if other entities opted in.  
Page 8 of 12
Council Work Session Meeting Minutes February 15, 2022
Mr. Jevon Gibb , Director, Regional Development, stated this would severely limit the 
County’s ability to say no.  He understood the motivation because there is frustration 
when certain taxing entities do not participate, but not all entities should participate in all 
deals.  He asked the Council to oppose.
A motion was made by Council Member DeBry, seconded by Council Member Winder 
Newton, to oppose HB 362.  The motion carried by a unanimous vote.
- SB 66 Electric Assisted Bicycle Use
Ms. Trevino stated this bill already passed through the Senate.  There is some concern 
from the Parks and Recreation Division over it.
Ms. Erin Litvack , Deputy Mayor of County Services, stated the Mayor ’s Office is very 
supportive of providing access to trails for citizens.  The concern is the conservation 
easements the County has on some of these properties within its trails.  The easements do 
not allow for motorized vehicles.  The Mayor's Office is working through American With 
Disabilities Act (ADA) language for ways to provide flexibility to keep from violating state 
law and the County’s easement.  
Ms. Darcy Goddard , Deputy District Attorney,  stated the bill, as it is currently drafted, 
states any trail open to bicycles must allow all bikes. The bill ’s sponsor is open to ideas .  
She is hoping he will accept changes that will include ADA language that would allow for 
more nuanced decisions based on the characteristics of the trail and area.  The County 
does not want to lose access to those properties.  
Council Member DeBry  stated he understood the spirit of this bill, but thought these 
issues needed to be worked through.  He would suggest monitoring and working with the 
sponsor for now. 
A motion was made by Council Member DeBry, seconded by Council Member Winder 
Newton, to monitor SB 66  and work with the sponsor. The motion carried by a unanimous 
vote.
- HB 239 Governmental Entity Budget Transparency
Ms. Trevino stated the Council opposed this bill last week.  The sponsor has since come 
up with a first substitute and used Salt Lake County’s budget as an example.
Page 9 of 12
Council Work Session Meeting Minutes February 15, 2022
Mr. Casper stated from what he understands, the bill has substantially improved and is 
very agreeable.    
Council Member Newton  asked if it will be cumbersome for other counties or if it 
makes sense for transparency’s sake.
Mr. Casper stated understanding population growth and Consumer Price Index (CPI) 
growth is very important.  Taxes do not grow with inflation.  Overall, it is not a burden to 
Salt Lake County, nor should it be a burden to smaller counties.
A motion was made by Council Member Winder Newton, seconded by Council Member 
Snelgrove, to support HB 239. The motion carried by a unanimous vote.
- SB 25 Property Tax Deferral Amendments
Ms. Trevino stated Wayne Cushing, Salt Lake County Treasurer, has been following this 
bill.  It passed the House Committee and is now in the House Rules Committee because it 
has a fiscal note.  The County ’s position is already to support it, but to make sure it gets 
funded.
