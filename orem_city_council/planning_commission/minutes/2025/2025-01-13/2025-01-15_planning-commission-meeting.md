4.2      PUBLIC HEARING – Ken Garff Rezone – Rezoning the properties located generally at 134 East 1200
         South and 110 East 1200 South from Single Family Residential (R8) to Commercial (C2).

Chair Komen requested the developer to speak.

Ken Garff representative Carlos Iglesias
Representative

Public Comments:
5:56 p.m.: Lucy Ramerez - She lives across the street from the proposed plot. She has concerns with access to 1200
S. A lof of accidents have happened on this street. Traffic is usually going 40 mph instead of 25 mph. Concerned if
the project is approved that the traffic will get worse.There was a fatality resently on the road and no charges were
filed against the person speeding. It is already difficult getting out of the driveway because there is so much traffic.

5:59 p.m.: Stuart Merrill – He is across the street. Been there for 44 years. The area used to be orchards. 1200 South
is busy. Cars are going fast. There used to be a cop to help with traffic. Now there is no cop.Garff hasn’t talked to
him. It is difficult to get out on the street. The trucks dropping off vehicles for Ken Garff are there parked for a long
time. Originally Garff said there would not be an entrance there, and then suddenly changed his mind.

6:03 p.m.: Susan Parkinson – Owns the property that will be surrounded by the parking lot. Originally Garff said
there would be a wall around the new parking. She has residential and business on here property and wants to
protect them. Had water right of way on her property and the plan could ruin her right of way. 1200 south is a busy
street.

6:06 p.m.: Jared Hall: The difference between anticipated proposal and this proposal from Garff is that the C2 zone
would allow access to 1200 South.

DRC Action: Rod moved to continue the project until February 19 th meeting. Those voting Yes: The motion passed
unanimously.


Regular Session Adjournment: Britton motioned to adjourn; seconded the motion.
Those voting Yes: Mike Carpenter, Gerald Crismon, Rod Erickson, James Hawkes, Madeline Komen, and Britton
Runolfson
Those voting No: None
The motion passed unanimously, and the Regular Session adjourned at 6:30PM.
