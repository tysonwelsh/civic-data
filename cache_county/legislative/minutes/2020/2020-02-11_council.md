American Mothers 5" Grade Statewide Essay Contest Winner: Jaeda Coon — Vice
Chair Worthen introduced Jaeda, who won the state essay contest and she read her

Bear River Health Department Annual Report — Holly Budge reported on the
following: Board members, Health Department history, budget, services, health issues,

INITIAL PROPOSAL FOR CONSIDERATION OF ACTION

e Ordinance No. 2020-01 —- Amending Title 2, Chapters 36 and 40 of the Cache

County Code regarding the Public Works and Development Services Departments
(NO ACTION TAKEN) — Chief Deputy County Executive Bryce Mumford indicated the

code changes were structured for possible future departments being included without a
code change. Divisions were listed with their functions and department director duties
within each department. The term “the land use authority” has been changed to “a land
use authority” and the term “activities” changed to “actions” in 2.40.030(A)(3).

* Consideration of 2019 CCCOG Funding Recommendations — Mayor Jeff Young
gave a brief explanation of CCCOG and noted CCCOG’s recommendations must be

approved by the County Council and that body can make modifications to the
recommendations. White and Erickson both expressed concern because Item 4 is still
under litigation. Young assured them that other projects have been approved when
there was pending litigation. Council member Zilles said several citizens have concerns
about Item 4 and have asked Council members’ assistance in being heard. After much
discussion, the Council agreed that entities considering applying for CCCOG funds need
to have public hearings regarding their proposed projects before applying.

(Attachment 3)

ACTION: Motion by Vice Chair Worthen that from 2020 forward entities applying for
CCCOG funding must include a public hearing and vote from the Councils of the city or
county submitting the project(s). Borup seconded the motion. The vote was
unanimous, 7-0. :

ACTION: Motion by Council member Erickson to approve the 2019 CCCOG
recommendations — Items 1, 2 and 3 — and send back projects 4 thru 10 for
reconsideration at a later date. Worthen seconded the motion. The vote was
unanimous, 7-0.
