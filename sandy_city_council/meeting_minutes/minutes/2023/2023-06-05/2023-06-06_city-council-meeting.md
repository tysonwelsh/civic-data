Citizen Comment Procedure

23-213      General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM


                           Council Chair Stroud invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment.

                           Public Comment opened.

                           Mr. Steve Van Maren spoke about the revenues from a GO bond and expressed concerns 
                           about the possibility of any excess funds being rolled into the general budget.

                           Mr. David Jack spoke about D Day which occurred over 70 years ago on June 6th, when 
                           the Allied forces invaded Normandy Beach in France. He spoke about visiting the site and 
                           reflected on the sacrifices of the many who fought that day and died or were injured for 
                           the well-being of future generations. He spoke about the importance of following the rule 
                           of law and by being a good citizen as a way to honor those who fought for us those many 
                           years ago.

                           Ms. Pat Jones spoke about how all elected officials are also sacrificing in order to serve 
                           their community and she acknowledged their service and time. She thanked the Mayor 
                           for her work and knows it is not easy to ask for a tax increase and appreciates the 
                           Council looking for ways to decrease the proposed tax impact to residents. She is also 
                           on a fixed income, but she understands the needs of the city and departments. She 
                           thanked the Council for the great discussion on the budget. She went to Alta Canyon 
                           Recreation Center and does think the center is in need of repairs. She spoke about the 
                           importance of getting city-wide buy in for the Alta Canyon project.

                           Ms. May does not think rebuilding Alta Canyon Recreation Center is the best use of 
                           taxpayer money right now. She expressed support for the Fire Department funding. We 
                           need to show our support for the Fire Department staff. Our first responders are the 
                           backbone of our city and we need to support them. Sandy has many well maintained 
                           parks and she is not supportive of the Alta Canyon rebuild project. 

                           Public Comment closed.

                           Council moved to Standing Reports.

2.         23-216                 City Recorder presenting amendments to Utah Code as related to 
                                  candidate financial disclosure deadlines
                   Attachments:   Financial Disclosures Presentation.pdf
                                  Memo.pdf

                                  Wendy Downs, City Recorder, presented on a change to the Utah State Code 
                                  10-3-208(4b) & 5, effective 5/3/2023. The amendment now includes an additional state 
                                  required financial disclosure for those candidates who are not eliminated at the primary 
                                  election, and are in the General Election race. The Sandy City Code follows state code 
                                  for financial disclosure submittals. Ms. Downs reviewed the recommended amendments 
                                  to the Sandy City Code to align with the Utah State Code. Council questions followed. 
                                  Council members expressed support for amending the Sandy City Code to align with the 
                                  Utah State Code mandates for required candidate financial disclosures due dates.

4.         23-214                 Discussion of Council Members' recommended amendments to the FY 
                                  2023-2024 Tentative Budget.
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Robinson Budget Proposal 6.6.23.pdf

                                  Council Member Robinson presented proposed amendments to the FY 2024 Budget. He 
                                  provided an overview of the proposed amendments which include the following: fully 
                                  funding the employee compensation plan, fully funding the Fire Department FTE's and 
                                  pay plan, and the issuance of bonds to fund building Fire Station 31 and Alta Canyon 
                                  Recreation Center. Council discussion and comments followed. The discussion and 
                                  feedback provided included adjustments to the employee compensation plan, addressing 
                                  one capital project at a time, the importance of funding core services, the funding of the 
                                  proposed Fire FTE's, and the utilization of funds from a GO Bond for funding capital 
                                  projects. Discussion also included the costs to maintain the high level of service to our 
                                  residents and support for the needs of the fire department. Sandy City currently has one 
                                  of the lowest property tax rates among other cities of a comparable size. Council Member 
                                  Robinson thanked the Council and Administration for their feedback.

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto, Council Director: The Sandy Youth Council will be joining the Council for 
                      dinner next week and will be recognized at the June 13th City Council meeting. The 
                      Council will hear budget proposals for FY 2024.


Council Member Business

                      Council Member Zach Robinson spoke about the Bell Canyon trailhead and upgrades, 
                      and he reminded the public to be safe around the water.

                      Council Member Marci Houseman thanked Dr. Jack for his comments honoring the 
                      military and those that stormed the beaches of Normandy to protect our freedoms. She 
                      thanked all those who have served in the military. Ms. Houseman also acknowledged the 
                      first responders for their quick and lifesaving response involving a recent incident.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey encouraged all to nominate people for the Healthy Sandy 
                      Champion award. She recently attended the Spavia Grand Opening - it is a beautiful 
                      place. Ms. Sharkey provided an update from the General Plan Transportation 
                      Subcommittee meeting. It was an informative and interesting discussion at the meeting.

                      Council Member Scott Earl thanked Dr. Jack for his earlier comments. Mr. Earl 
                      mentioned that is currently meeting with the city's transportation engineer to review the 
                      speeding issues along Wasatch Blvd. 

Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Zoltanski thanked Dr. Jack for his comments. She spoke about honoring those in 
                      the military. She invited residents to come to the food truck nights at the Sandy 
                      Amphitheater and to City Hall to view the art work from local artists which is sponsored 
                      by the Sandy Arts Guild. Mayor Z spoke about the proposed tax increases and the 
                      importance of supporting the fire department. She spoke about the recent article 
                      regarding the proposed tax increase and thought the article was misleading to residents. 
                      The residents she spoke to were supportive of funding the needs of the fire department. 
                      She also encouraged residents who are on a fixed income to go to the Sandy City 
                      website to find information on financial assistance programs that can offer help to those 
                      on fixed incomes.

CAO Report

                      Shane Pace, CAO, also spoke about the upgrades and the Bell Canyon Trailhead which 
                      included the addition of a helipad. That was used this weekend by first responders who 
                      helped an injured hiker on the trail. He also mentioned to check the website to find drop 
                      off locations for sandbags.

Adjournment
                      Council unanimously adjourn the meeting at 7:38 pm.
