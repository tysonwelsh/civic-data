KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
Ordinance No. 2023-05-01 Date: May 8, 2023
AN ORDINANCE OF KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL RESCINDING
| ORDINANCE 2023-04-01 (‘AN ORDINANCE OF KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL AMENDING TITLES 18 & 19 OF MUNICIPAL CODE, PROVISIONS ON
ZONING AND SUBDIVISIONS”)

WHEREAS, on April 18, 2023, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) Council (“Council”)
adopted Ordinance 2023-04-01 to adopt recodified versions of Titles 18 and 19 of the Kearns Metro
Township Code regarding subdivisions and zoning; and

WHEREAS, in adopting Ordinance 2023-04-01, the Council directed Kearns staff to review
the recodified versions of Titles 18 and 19 to make any formatting, grammatical, or other non-
substantive changes that may be needed; and

WHEREAS, the Council also directed that Ordinance 2023-04-01 would not become effective
until after Kearns staff had finalized the recodified versions of Titles 18 and 19 for publication to
Municide and posted a summary of the ordinance to the Utah Public Notice Website; and

WHEREAS, in conducting their review of the recodified versions of Titles 18 and 19, Kearns
staff identified potential issues regarding setbacks, lot sizes, and planned unit development standards
(collectively, the “Changes”) that while minor, constitute substantive changes that require review and
approval by the Council; and

WHEREAS, because the recodified versions of Titles 18 and 19 require substantive changes,
the staff did not publish the summary of Ordinance 2023-04-01 to the Utah Public Notice Website; and

WHEREAS, Ordinance 2023-04-01 has not gone into effect because: (1) the summary of
Ordinance 2023-4-01 was not published as required by the Council for the ordinance to go into effect;
and (2) Utah Code § 10-3-711(1) states than an ordinance may not take effect until a summary has
been posted on the Utah Public Notice Website; and

WHEREAS, the Council desires to rescind Ordinance 2023-04-01 and to direct the staff to
revise the recodified versions of Titles 18 and 19 to include the Changes and to present the Changes to
the Kearns Planning Commission.

BE IT ORDAINED BY KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL as follows:

1. Ordinance 2023-04-03 is repealed and rescinded effective upon the date the attached
summary is published to the Utah Public Notice Website.

2. The Kearns Planning Commission is directed to: (1) hold a public hearing in
accordance with Utah Code §§ 10-9a-205 and 10-9a-502 on the Changes; and (2) provide a
corresponding recommendation to the Council regarding the same.

PASSED AND ADOPTED this 8" day of May 2023.

rescind Ordinance 2023-04-01, which adopted new and updated versions of Titles 18 and 19

(Subdivisions and Zoning) of the Kearns Municipal Code (“KMC”) but has not gone into effect.

Ordinance 2023-05-01 also instructs the Kearns Planning Commission to revise additional
| revisions to Titles 18 and 19 regarding setbacks, lot sizes, and planned unit development standards
