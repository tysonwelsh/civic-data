                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

     1. PRESENTATION OF VISIT OGDEN’S 2021 ANNUAL REPORT AND REQUEST FOR APPROVAL

            Commissioner Jenkins moved approve to the 2022 Strategic Plan; Commissioner Froerer seconded.

     2. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 49-2021 OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER
        COUNTY APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE ROY WATER CONSERVANCY DISTRICT.
            Stacy Skeen (Commissioners Office) discussed two applicants for one position expiring at the end of the year.
            Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve Chris Davis; no second.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 49-2021 of the County Commissioners of Weber County
            appointing members to the Roy Water Conservancy District and appoint Jon Ritchie for a new term beginning
            January 1, 2022; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – no; Chair Harvey – aye
     3. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 50-2021 OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER
        COUNTY APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE OGDEN MUSICAL THEATRE ADVISORY BOARD.

            Jolene Zitto, Sasha Clark, and Karen Bristol.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved for approval of Resolution 50-2021 of the County Commissioners of Weber
         County appointing current members (Annette DeFries, Jolene Zitto, Sasha Clark, and Karen Bristol) to a second
         term on the Ogden Musical Theatre Advisory Board; Chairman Harvey seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    4. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH THE UTAH INDIGENT DEFENSE COMMISSION
       ALLOWING WEBER COUNTY TO APPLY FOR FEDERAL FUNDS THROUGH THE UTAH
       INDIGENT DEFENSE COMMISSION FOR REIMBURSEMENT OF UP TO 50% OF THE
       AMOUNT SPENT ON LEGAL REPRESENTATION FOR THE PARENTS OF CHILDREN WHO
       ARE CANDIDATES FOR TITLE IV-E FOSTER CARE.
         Bryan Baron (Attorney) federal program providing money for admin costs. Attorney fees now covered under
         that program. Expected reimbursements up to $250k per year.
         Commissioner Froerer moved for approval of contract with the Utah Indigent Defense Commission allowing
         Weber County to apply for federal funds through the Utah Indigent Defense Commission for reimbursement of
         up to 50% of the amount spent on legal representation for the parents of children who are candidates for Title
         IV-E foster care; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

    5. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH PUBLISHING CORPORATION TO PROVIDE PUBLIC
       DEFENDER ATTORNEYS WITH ACCESS TO WESTLAW, A LEGAL RESEARCH DATABASE.
         Bryan Baron (Attorney) prior agreement expires end of this year. New agreement for 36 months.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve of a contract with West Publishing Corporation to provide public
         defender attorneys with access to Westlaw, a legal research database; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye


G. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS: None.
