                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21- 16

        A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING RIVERTON CITY TO EXECUTE A
       WATER REUSE SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH DRAPER IRRIGATION
                             COMPANY

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Wastewater Reuse Act, Section 73-3c-201 et seq., a public
entity which owns a water right or has the right to use a water right, may reuse any domestic
waste water created by lawful use of said right; and

        WHEREAS, the Draper Irrigation Company, in affiliation with the South Valley Sewer
District (the “District”), has explored ways in which treated wastewater may be reused by
member entities of the District; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has participated in the first phase of a project to blend waste
water with ground water produced from a test well, to determine whether the project is viable at a
larger scale; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the City needs to pay
for costs associated with this project under the terms and conditions of a Water Reuse Service
Agreement with the Draper Irrigation Company;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1.      The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute a Water Service Reuse Agreement with
               Draper Irrigation Company, in substantially similar form to the Agreement attached
               as Exhibit A, once it is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

       2.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
February 2021 by the following vote:
