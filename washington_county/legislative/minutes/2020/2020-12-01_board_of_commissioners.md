commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are
placed at the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a
motion to approve the items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

Chairman Iverson explained that there are several county employees being recognized for
longevity awards.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _ R-2020-2691, A RESOLUTION
TRANSFERRING 22.73 ACRES TO THE UTAH DIVISION OF WILDLIFE

RESOURCES

Commissioner Cox stated that the Congress passed and fully funded the Land and Water
Conservation Fund (LWCF). Those funds are used to acquire properties for
environmentally sound reasons. Twenty-two point seven three (22.73) acres have been
purchased by Washington County and is proposed to be transferred to the DWR as the
State of Utah is the only entity that can make an application for LWCF funds. A twenty-
five percent (25%) match is required from the local entity as a pre-requisite for that
exchange.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2691, A
Resolution Transferring 22.73 Acres to the Utah Division of Wildlife
Resources. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox

voting aye.
