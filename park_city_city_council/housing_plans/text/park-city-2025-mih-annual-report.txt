3. Describe each action taken between August 1, 2024 and July 31, 2025 to implement the

A
Clark Ranch P3: In November of 2024, an Exclusive Negotiation Agreement was signed with
the Alexandar Company. The Alexandar Company includes local partners familiar with the
affordable housing community such as Stereotomic Architecture, Talisman Civil Consultants
and NewStar Contractors. The first large task was to determine a primary entrance to the
development site that didn’t travel through the adjacent neighborhood. The City Engineer’s
office is taking the lead on a feasibility study and design for a complete rebuild of an existing
UDOT frontage road which will become the primary access for the development site. The
Alexandar Company is also preparing an application for rezoning to present to the City Planning
Commission in the Fall of 2025.Bonanza 5-Acre Parcel: The process for selecting a private
partner for this project was an extensive process prioritizing alignment with community input,
Council goals, and experience in delivering mixed-use projects. The process took place
throughout the fall of 2024 and into spring of 2025. In June, Brinshore Development was
selected. Brinshore will rezone the site to deliver a mixed-use neighborhood, complete with
cultural amenities, affordable housing, transit connectivity, underground parking, and
neighborhood-scale retail.

C
• Bids were accepted on the construction drawings in September 2025. Bids came in, but at the
advice of all three bidding contractors, the actual completion of contract and start of construction
was rescheduled to Spring of 2025. Contractors advised that weather would cause delays and
an increase in costs since it was a project involving partial demolition.•Contractors updated their
bids in March of 2025.•The lowest bidder was unable to supply sufficient bonding for
Performance and Payment, so th City had to go with the second highest bid, Bailey
Builders.•Construction began on May 1, 2025 and continues at a good pace. •The projected
completion date is by end of Calendar year 2025.

L

stages of site planning, and a fee waiver hasn’t been requested yet.•On April 24, 2025, $1.9M in
fee waivers were granted to the Studio Crossing project that will produce 208 units of affordable
rental housing at or below 80% of AMI. Crandall Capital is building Studio Crossing, a mix of
market-rate and affordable residential as well as commercial space and other amenities. The
development agreement resulted in a requirement that 65 units of affordable housing be
included in the project to mitigate the development’s impact on the workforce’s housing needs.
The developer is providing significantly more affordable units than required. •On April 24, 2025,
