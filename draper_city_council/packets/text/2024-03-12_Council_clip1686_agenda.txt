3.     Public Comments
       To be considerate of everyone attending the meeting, public comments will be
       restricted to items that are not listed on this or a future agenda and limited to three
       minutes per person. Comments which cannot be made within these limits should be
       submitted in writing to the City Recorder prior to noon the day before the meeting.
       Comments pertaining to an item on the agenda should not be given at this time but
       should be held until that item is called.

     5.a Public Hearing: Ordinance #1596
         An Ordinance amending the Official Zoning Map of Draper City for
         approximately 0.48 acres of property from RA2 (Residential Agricultural,
         20,000 square foot minimum lot size) to CC (Community Commercial)
         located at approximately 11853 South 700 East, otherwise known as the
         Tunbridge Zoning Map Amendment. Staff report by Tevai Haunga.

         This item was continued from the December 6, 2023 City Council meeting
         agenda.

     5.b Public Hearing: Ordinances #1605 and #1606
         An Ordinance amending Official Land Use Map and Zoning Map of Draper

         square foot minimum lot size) to CN (Neighborhood Commercial), located at
         approximately 11743 South 700 East within Draper City, otherwise known at
         the Federal Heights Holdings LLC Zoning and Land Use Amendments. Staff
         report by Maryann Pickering.

     5.c Action Item: Ordinance # 1607
         An Ordinance approving the first amended Draper School Development

         report by Jennifer Jastremsky.

     5.d Action Item: Resolution #24-02
