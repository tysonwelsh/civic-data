Public Comment No Comments Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Modification - Shawn Eliot Orem is requesting to cancel the Orem Utah Lakeshore Trail project, funded in 2014 for $433,000, and transfer that funding to the 1600 North 400 West intersection widening project, this will cancel the Trail Project. The original estimate for the 1600 North project didn’t account for property acquisition from Texaco, Rite Aid and a parcel east of parcel 4. The additional funds are needed to complete the construction for the 1600 North widening project. A large amount of commercial zoned land will remain after the road improvements. The city plans to sell the land and use the proceeds toward another federally funded transportation project in Orem. TAC recommends that proceeds from the property sale go back into the MPO federal TIP funds to fund other MPO projects. Mayor Richard Brunst moved the MPO Regional Planning Committee approve the recommendation of the MPO TAC Committee by canceling the Orem Utah Lakeshore Project and moving its funding of $433,000 to the Orem 1600 North project to keep this project whole, as proposed by the projects sponsor. Any proceeds from the sale of property acquired by this project will go toward other approved MPO federally funded project. Mayor Mike Daniels seconded the motion. The motion carried unanimously. Annual Report of Funded Projects - Shawn Eliot Annually the MPO issues a Year End Obligation Report for the TIP of all federal funds used in the MPO. Twenty-five projects, have obligated federal funds and have either started, in design, under construction, or completed. There were seven projects funded with Utah County sales tax funds. See the 2015 MAG Funded Project list on page 6. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning March 5, 2015 Page 3 The TIP is four year program that uses federal, state, and county money, and every two years new projects are selected. Funding for projects come from various sources: MPO federal funds:  Congestion Mitigation/Air Quality (CMAQ): project must show air quality benefits, can’t widen roads.  Surface Transportation Program (STP): can be used on road improvements, transit, trails, pedestrian, and studies Utah County Sales Tax: Projects using Utah County funds must be approved by the County Commission. UDOT: State and federal funds UTA: Funds include local sales tax and federal grants Eligible Projects: Air Quality Spot improvements Pedestrian/Trail Bridge Safety ITS Transit Park & Ride Transportation Studies North County Transit Study Results - Chad Eccles The Study started in 2014 to analysis the potential transit options to accommodate the growth in north Utah County in partnership with UTA. The Study is looking at a circulator bus service connecting to Lehi commuter rail station; evaluated north/south light rail transit including locations for future stations; East/West transit “visioning” connecting Saratoga Spring, Eagle Mountain, Lehi and other western areas, and develop a transit market analysis for the north county. The final draft will be available in April.
