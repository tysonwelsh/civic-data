RESOLUTION 22-39: AWARD A CONTRACT TO WALTON CONCRETE
PROTECTION AND RESTORATION, INC. FOR THE REPAIR OF THE
FAIRBOURNE STATION PARKING GARAGE
Nicole Cottle, Acting City Manager, presented proposed Resolution 22-39 that would
award a Contract to Walton Concrete Protection and Restoration, Inc. for the repair of the
Fairbourne Station Parking Garage.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       On March 18, 2020, the City experienced a rather impactful earthquake based in
       Magna, UT. Reaveley Engineers and EDA Architects inspected the Fairbourne
       Station Parking Structure in response to visual damages.

       The report from Reaveley states good news in that “the primary structure capacity
       remains intact without any loss to its ability to support original design gravity and
       lateral loads.” It also records superficial damages limited to cracks in concrete
       frame beams and columns, drainpipe damages, and aesthetic damages, such as
       cracked windowpanes and displaced stone veneers “due to damage to CMU walls
       that act as masonry veneer backup support.”

       FEMA also inspected the structure and prepared a damage report approving
       75%/25% project reimbursement for the repairs.

       The Facilities Department received three proposals to repair the superficial
       damages. Of the three, Walton Concrete Protection and Restoration, Inc was the
       lowest responsible bid. The City has worked with Walton on other projects in
       response to earthquake damages, as well as aesthetic improvements with City Hall
       landscaping.

             of their driveway to match the new sidewalk elevations.
