UNDERSTANDING LEAD EXPOSURE

 • Individuals do not know what levels of
   lead they are actually being exposed to in
   their drinking water.
 • Lead in drinking water is primarily from
   materials and components associated
   with service lines, premise plumbing,
   fountains and faucets
 • Exposure to lead can cause serious health
   problems, particularly for young children
LEAD IN DRINKING WATER IS REGULATED, Right?

  Yes…
  •   Lead and Copper Rule requires 5 to 50 samples at least every 3 years
  •   Regulatory Action Level, 90th percentile below 15 ug/L
  •   Customer notification required

  …and No
  •   No health-based limit
  •   Violation requires study on corrosion control – not exposure reduction
  •   Not representative sampling locations

  Considerations
  • Public expectations are different than actual laws
  • Analytical limitations, cannot confirm Zero
     •     only non-detect
  • Non-detected levels accumulate on pipes and then
     •     release in higher concentrations

LEAD & COPPER RULE REVISIONS

 • New “Trigger Level” for Lead at 10 ppb

 • Keeps the Action Level of 15 ppb

 • Lead Service Line (LSL) Inventories

 • LSL inventory must be made public

 • 90th Percentile exceeds AL = Notify ALL connections with 24      LSL Replacement in Madison, WI
   hours

 • After initial inventory, unknowns are considered lead

 • Water systems required to sample all elementary schools and
   licensed childcare facilities within five years (20% per year)

 • Must sample secondary schools upon request

SERVICE LINE INVENTORIES – Due October 2024


    • Must inventory all lines in your system – including privately owned lines
       • Connectors not considered part of service line
    • Service Line Categories
       • Lead or partial lead
       • Non-lead, specify
       • Galvanized
       • Unknown
    • Only need location identifier
    • Submit to state and publish publicly
       • If you are a large (>50,000 served) system, you must publish electronically

FIND AND FIX

     Applies to individual homes with Lead > Action Level of 15ppb

     • Notify resident

     • Follow up tap sampling

     • Strive to identify source of lead​

     • Review water quality parameter samples to
       determine CCT optimization

     • Develop mitigation strategy
        - Adjust CCT
        - Flush portions of the system
        - Remove lead source
