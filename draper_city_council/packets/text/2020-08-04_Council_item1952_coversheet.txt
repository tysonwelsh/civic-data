Re: City Initiated Commercial Buffer Zone – Zoning Text Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a  text amendment that would apply citywide.  The application has been initiated by Draper City.  The proposed zone is called the Commercial Buffer Zone (CBU) and would have a CBU designation on the zoning maps.   The proposed CBU zone will be located in Chapter 9-11 (Commercial Uses) in the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC).
On July 23, 2020, the Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation with a vote of 4-1. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.   That Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the text of the DCMC.
2.   That this text amendment does not compromise any of the six factors listed in Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
3.   That the proposed text change is consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the City’s General Plan.
For denial:
1.   That this text amendment compromises one or more of the six factors listed in Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
