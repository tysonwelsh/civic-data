Item 1: Presentation Regarding FY25 Tentative Budget Amendments 00:03:21

Presenter: Kelsey Zarbock, Budget Officer

Kelsey Zarbock, Budget Officer, presented an overview of proposed amendments to the FY25
tentative budget:

Revenue Corrections

       Adjustments were made to the general fund, golf course, and ice sheet budgets related to
        Parks and Recreation due to double-counting of sales tax impacts.
       The general fund required a $96,000 offset to account for changes in the recreation center
        net income projection.

New Information

       General Services added $138,000 in property tax revenue based on the certified tax rate
        from the county.
       Miscellaneous revenue was reduced by $41,000 to align with FY24 trends.

Item 2: Discussion Regarding the FY25 Budget and Property Tax 00:26:20

Presenter: Justin Harrison, Council Executive Director

Justin Harrison, Council Executive Director, provided an overview of the city's property tax rates
and budget considerations:

Library Tax Rate

       The library's tax rate is currently at approximately 40% of its statutory cap.
       A proposed $500,000 increase would equate to approximately $12.40 per year for an
        average household.
       This represents a 12.5% increase to the library tax rate, but only a 2.7% increase to the
        overall city tax rate.

General Fund Revenues

       Sales tax constitutes 33% of general fund revenue, with transfers in accounting for 19%.
       Property tax contributes 7% to the general fund revenue.
       The general operations property tax rate is at about 8% of its statutory cap.

Potential Increases

       A 3% increase to the general operations tax rate would generate approximately $170,000,
        translating to $4.20 per year for an average household.

Council members discussed the following topics:

       Funding needs and governance structure for the library.
       Potential for increased fees or fundraising initiatives.

          o Councilor George Handley moved to instruct staff to prepare a truth in taxation

    2. General Fund Tax Increase:
          o Councilor George Handley moved to request staff prepare for next Tuesday a
             truth in taxation proposal for the city budget property tax equivalent to a 3%

The Council also discussed other budget considerations, including vehicle replacements, staffing
