                                             PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant is proposing to rezone the property from R-1-6 to RM to allow for an office use of the property.



                              SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located on the 3500 South corridor and near the intersection with 8400 West. Surrounding land use to the
West and North is single family residential. To the East and South are commercially zoned properties. Directly to the East is
an office building in an RM zone.

                                    GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The property is located on a mapped General Plan corridor which encourages adaptable and flexible changes to zoning for
properties that have direct frontage onto the corridor.
