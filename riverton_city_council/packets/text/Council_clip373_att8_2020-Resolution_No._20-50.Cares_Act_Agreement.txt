                                    RIVERTON CITY
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-50

      A RESOLUTION APPROVING CARES ACT FUNDING AGREEMENT FOR
             GOVERNMENTAL ENTITIES (SALT LAKE COUNTY)
        WHEREAS, In the year 2020 Congress of the United States enacted, and the President
of the United States signed the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act, known as
the CARES Act; and

        WHEREAS, the CARES Act provided, among other things, a substantial compensation
package to reimburse local governments for expenses incurred which arose from their response
to the COVID-19 pandemic; and

        WHEREAS, the compensation provided by the United States was distributed to counties
in the state of Utah, which will then distribute funding to cities within the territories of each
county; and

      WHEREAS, Riverton City has been and continues to be committed to combating the
COVID-19 epidemic to reduce the loss of life and property, and alleviate human suffering and
economic disruption; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has incurred expenses in combating the COVID-19 pandemic
and therefore ought to be compensated with funds available under management of Salt Lake
County; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County is ready to distribute CARES Act funding but first
requires cities execute a CARES Act Funding Agreement; and

      WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that the CARES Act
Funding Agreement is consistent with the requirements of the CARES Act;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

   1. The CARES Act Funding Agreement for Governmental Entities, attached to this
      Resolution as Exhibit A is hereby approved. The Mayor is authorized and directed to
      sign a copy of the Agreement, once the same has been approved by the Riverton City
      Attorney.

   2. This resolution shall take effect immediately.

    PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 21st day of July 2020
