 2. Public Hearings
        a. Public Hearing to Receive Comment on the West Jordan Municipal Building Authority Fiscal
           Year 2027 Budget
           Budget Analyst Becky Condie reviewed the budget and public hearing process. She explained
           that the Municipal Building Authority collects lease payments from the General Fund and
           Utility Funds for use of the Public Works building, with those revenues used solely to pay
           debt service on the 2016 Lease Revenue Bond. She noted reserve funds would be held until
           the final bond payment, currently scheduled for 2039. The budget timeline was reviewed,
           noting the tentative budget was adopted on May 12, the public hearing was held that
           evening, and final budget adoption was anticipated on June 9, 2026.

           Chairperson Bedore opened the public hearing at 8:21 pm
