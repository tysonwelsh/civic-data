NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
October 27, 2021
21 E. 100 N. 
Nephi, UT 84648
7:00 p.m.

Newly appointed Planning Commission Chair Ann Peterson called the meeting to order and introduced the new appointed commissioners Alan Hancock, Heather Robertson (alternate), and Fran Peterson (alternate).

PRAYER: Commissioner Karl Brough

BRYCE’S LANDING PHASE B SUBDIVISION FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

    Mr. Atkinson reviewed the final plat for Bryce’s Landing Phase B Subdivision.  He said issues with storm water drainage and side yards had been worked out.  He showed an area for parking on the plat and reviewed the code’s definitions of a “parking area” versus a “parking lot”.   Parking lots cannot back out onto a city street.  A discussion ensued about the parking issue.  Commissioner Thomson moved to designate the parking spots as a “parking area”.  Commissioner Robertson seconded the motion.   The motion passed on a unanimous vote.   
    Commissioner Brough moved to approve Bryce’s Landing Phase B Subdivision final plat contingent on the completion of the Development Review Committee’s (DRC) recommendations.   Commissioner Thomson seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

MAVERICK SITE PLAN APPROVED:

    Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the commission the areas of concern with the Maverick site plan,  including the sign height and some utility issues.  He reported that the issues had been resolved and Public Works Director Kyle Marchant was recommending the site plan be approved.  Commissioner Thomson had some issues with the entrance on the south side of the development.  A discussion ensued about the access and roads.  Commissioner Thomson moved to approve the Maverick site plan.  Commissioner Hancock seconded the motion.  The motion passed on a unanimous vote. 

ROLES FOR NEW COMMISSIONERS AND ALTERNATES DISCUSSED:

    Mr. Atkinson reported on the Land Use Institute Conference he had attended.  He passed on information about training for planning commissions.  The commission discussed the role of the planning commission and the alternates.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

Commissioner Peterson moved to adjourn the meeting.  
Commissioner  seconded the motion.   The motion passed on a unanimous vote. 
Meeting adjourned at 8:33 p.m.
