REQUEST
James Derby, AIA, Architect, representing St. Anna Greek Orthodox Church, is requesting
preliminary commercial Site Plan Review for a proposed 1,600 square foot accessory
storage building and bell tower additions to the existing church site, located in the
Neighborhood Commercial (CN) zoning district.  Building architectural design review
approval is also required.  The Planning Commission is the land-use authority for these
requested actions.


BACKGROUND
The 3 acre property was approved for a change of use from a retail plant nursery and
wedding reception venue to a church use in 2019 by a Conditional Use Permit approved by
the Planning Commission.  Residential zoning is present abutting the site on the east and
partially on the north.  The Neighborhood Commercial zoning is a strip extending along the
east side of 1300 East Street.  Zoning in this area has existed as currently mapped for many
years.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to
notify them of the Planning Commission meeting.  A neighborhood meeting was held for

ANALYSIS
Pedestrian and Vehicle Access and On-site Circulation.  All street improvements and
utilities are existing from the prior site development.  There is an existing public sidewalk
along the frontage of this property.  The driveway access from 1300 East St. will not change.

Zoning Review.  The project will meet all of the requirements of the (CN) zoning district
and the Sandy City Development code.  Accessory storage buildings are allowed for
Church uses.  The bell tower addition to the front of the existing building is proposed to
be approximately 48 feet high to the top of the roof with a cross symbol rising another
approximate 5 feet above that.  Steeples and bell towers are permitted as exceptions to
the building height restrictions of the zone, which is otherwise 40 feet to the roof ridge.
The addition of the bell tower (or steeples) will help identify this former commercial
building as a place of worship and is encouraged on all churches.  Parking for this project
will meet the City requirements on site.  All required City Departments and Divisions have
reviewed and recommend approval, subject to their usual requirements and conditions.

Noise issues.  The  comments from the neighborhood meeting were concerning the
operation of the real bells in the bell tower.  Noise levels and schedules were discussed
in the context of the neighborhood meeting.  The church desires the bells and intends to
use them on Sundays and for special occasions, like Easter Midnight Mass.  The bells need
to be loud enough that they can be heard inside the building, but will otherwise meet the
City and County regulations.  A noise permit will be needed for any use after 10:00 P.M.
or before 7:00 A.M.  The Church does not intend for the bells to chime the passing of hours
or half hours or otherwise be objectionable to the neighbors.  Planning staff explained the
noise regulations of Sandy City and the Salt Lake County Health Department.

Architectural Design Review.  The building's design, materials and colors were
reviewed by the Sandy Planning Staff as to compliance with the Sandy City Architectural
Design Standards.  This project includes a major renovation and remodeling of the
interior of the existing building to better meet the religious requirements of the
congregation and the Greek Orthodox Church's doctorine and religious practices.

The existing building, being a prior commercial building, has a unique roof design as a
series of three parrallel pitched roofs with the gabel ends facing west and east.  The walls
are made of tilt-up concrete panels, which have been finished with paint to ressemble a
stucco wall.  The internal renovations being made to this building require that some of
the existing window areas be removed and infilled with solid wall material.  This is most
easily done with a stucco/EIFS material and finshed to be indistinguishable from the
painted concrete. The walls of the bell tower are also proposed to be a stucco/EIFS wall
system with a stone wains cott to match the existing buidling.  The Sandy City
Architectural Design Standards limit the use of stucco/EIFS material to 20 percent per
each building face.  The new stucco/EIFS materials for the bell tower and the infills to
replace some windows will meet this limitation.

The detached accessory storage building is proposed to be built at the southeast corner
of the existing building, but spaced 6.0 feet south of the existing building wall and in line
with the existing rear yard setback of the main building, which is 14 feet 8 inches west of
the east property line.  The building wall material for the 1600 square foot accessory

Staff recommends that the  Planning Commission approve the building architectural

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan
review is complete for the proposed St. Anna Church Accessory Storage Building and
Bell Tower Additions project, and approve the building's architectural design, materials
and colors as proposed, based upon the following two findings and the following six
conditions:

FINDINGS.
    A. That the proposed project meets or will meet all the applicable requirements
       of the Sandy City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design
       Standards as to building architectural design, materials and colors.
    B. That addition of the bell tower to the existing building will help to identify
       this former commercial building as a Church.

CONDITIONS.
    1. That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff
       prior to the start of any construction. The final site plan shall comply with all
       Development Code requirements and any modifications required or allowed by
       the Planning Commission.
    2. That the development will comply with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life
       Safety Codes applicable to this type of use.
    3. That the developer be responsible to meet all provisions of the (CN) zoning
       district and the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards with the details
       finalized with staff during final site plan review.
    4. That the developer be responsible for the placement of a temporary 6 foot high
       chain link fence around the perimeter of the project during the construction
       phase of the project for security. Said fence shall also be required to include
       fabric to prohibit blowing dust problems if it becomes necessary or if it is
       required by the Community Development Department during Site Plan Review.
    5. That the applicant complies with all department requirements as noted in all
       Preliminary Review letters prior to submittal for final site plan review with staff.
    6. That all signage be reviewed and approved under separate permit and be in
       conformance with City Code.
