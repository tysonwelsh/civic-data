7. ADOPT AND SIGN A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING A PROPERTY TAX LEVY FOR THE

FUNDING OF THE HEALTH DEPARTMENT.
-Juli Van Ginkel, Health

(Timestamp: 4:33)

Commissioner Beltran asked about the timeline on the tax levy. He is concerned
about some unintended consequences; a bad sales tax year or underestimating
the growth.

Commissioner Powers Gardner talked about how this tax levy would increase
transparency with the public and it would put some responsibility onto the Board
of Health to balance the budget. She said out of the 10 largest counties in Utah we
are the only county which does not do a tax levy.

Eric Edwards (Executive Director - Health Department): He spoke in support of the tax levy
and the efficiency of the Health Department.

Commissioner Beltran, Rod Mann (Utah County Auditor) Gina Tanner (Budget Manager)
discussion on the rate, base amount, new development growth.

19. APPROVE A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE 2026 BUDGET FOR THE GENERAL FUND
AND OTHER BUDGETARY FUNDS OF UTAH COUNTY WITH ITS APPLICABLE
SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION.

-Aileen Conder, Commission
(Timestamp: 29:00)

Commissioner Beltran thanked all of those involved. He stated that not everyone
got what they wanted. They puta million dollars into the sheriff's office and
wishes it could have been more. They also gave a million dollars to the attorney's
office. He said we made good investments in the Assessor’s office and IT. Those
were the four he wanted to pinpoint.

Commissioner Power Gardner: She said this was the best year we have had with
the budget. There was good commissioner engagement and good support from
the auditor staff. She talked about the additional $600,000 spent on jail food
because of the 24% more inmates in the jail. She also spoke about the need to
have more victims and witness coordinators. She also mentioned that she wanted
a Director of Government Efficiency and Innovation (DOGE) position, but it was
not approved this year.

Commissioner Gordon talked about the process of doing the budget. Getting
everyone together in the same room and helping the different departments
understand each other’s needs and projections. He congratulated Rod Mann and
his team. He thanked Ezra and his team.
