Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular council meeting to order.

ORDINANCE 05-11-2021 TEMPORARY LAND USE ORDINANCE ADOPTED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to adopt Ordinance 05-11-2021: TEMPORARY LAND USE ORDINANCE
PROHIBITING SUBDIVISION DEVELOPMENT APPLCATIONS. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

STAKER PARSON AWARDED SOUTH AIRPORT APRON PROJECT BID:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council that three bids had been received for the south airport apron
project. Staker Parson’s bid was the low bid but was almost $200,000 over the engineering estimate.
He stated that the FAA would cover 100% of the project. Councilor Worwood moved to award the bid
for the airport apron extension Project to Staker Parson. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

   Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual, and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed on
a unanimous vote.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

   The meeting adjourned at 8:50 p.m.
