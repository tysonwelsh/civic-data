CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to Sunroc for asphalt replacement
        project on City trails.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This project is for the annual repair and
          maintenance of various sections of trails throughout the City's trail network. This
          maintenance included repairs on various segments of the following trails: Virgin
          River Trail, Sunriver Trail, Santa Clara River Trail, and the Virgin River North
          Trail. This maintenance will repair sections of these trails have deteriorated.
          Three bids were received. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Sunroc in the
          amount of $137,904.

      b. Consider approval to award bid to Caliber Contractor, LLC for the
         Redwood Tree Park Improvements.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The purpose of the bid is to add some
          improvements to Redwood Tree Park. The Bid includes the curb, gutter, asphalt
          around the whole perimeter of the park on Redwood Tree Circle, a new basketball
          court and standards, removal of the old basketball court, landscape and irrigation
          improvements, a concrete pathway around the park, site lighting, lighting in the
          pavilion, and electrical outlets. The park improvements were one of the identified
          projects for the G.O. Bond. This project will be an enhancement to the
          playground equipment and surfacing were upgraded in 2023 with RAP Tax funds.
          Staff recommends awarding the bid to Caliber Contractor, LLC in the amount of
          $473,392.

      c. Consider approval to purchase a gazebo and bandshell from Smith
         Steelworks via state contract for Vernon Worthen Park.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This proposed purchase is to provide a
          replacement gazebo and a new bandshell for Vernon Worthen Park and is for
          materials and delivery only. It is anticipated that the Parks Division will install
          the structures. The proposed gazebo is a 32-foot diameter hexagonal pavilion
          and the proposed trapezoidal bandshell is 30 feet wide in front, 25 feet in rear,
          and 25 feet deep. The gazebo and bandshell will be entirely made of steel.
          Approval contingent upon adoption of the budget amendment on tonight's
          agenda. Staff recommends purchasing the gazebo and bandshell from Smith
          Steelworks in the amount of $215,900.

      d. Consider approval to purchase playground equipment from Landscape
         Structures, Inc. via state contract for Sycamore Park.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This proposed purchase is to provide
          new playground equipment for Sycamore Park and is for materials, surfacing,
          delivery, and installation. The proposed playground equipment includes a Hedra,
          Curva Spinner, swings, interactive musical equipment, and a shade structure.
          Surfacing will be artificial turf for the entire playground area even though the
          exhibits show wood chips under the swings. Staff recommends purchasing the
          equipment from Landscape Structures, Inc in the amount of $236,522.

     the southwest corner of 1470 East and 170 South, with conditions from the
     Planning Commission. (Case No. 2025-ZC-005 - Holiday Inn Express - Rim
     Rock Commercial lot 9)

PD AMENDMENT/ZONE CHANGE:
     Consider approval of Ordinance No. 2025-021 amending the Atkinville
     Interchange Area Zone Plan PD on approximately 5.91 acres generally
     located north of Pioneer Road and south of Sand Piper Drive between
     Bluegrass Way and Nighthawk Drive. (Case No. 2025-PDA-008 - Encompass
     Health)

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This proposal seeks to amend the Atkinville
      Interchange Area Zone Plan Commercial PD (Planned Development) to allow for the
      construction of a 56,095-square-foot inpatient rehabilitation hospital and to add
      “Medical/Hospital” as an approved use to the Community Commercial area in the
      Atkinville PD-C zone. The first phase would include 50 beds. A future expansion, if
      pursued, would add 4,168 square feet of building space, 11 additional parking
      spaces, and increase the facility’s capacity to 60 beds. The project site covers
      approximately 5.91 acres. Community Commercial uses in the Atkinville Plan share
      the same permitted uses as the C-2 Highway Commercial Zone. However, a
      rehabilitation hospital is not an approved use in the C-2 zone. Since the project site
      is within a PD zone, the applicant is requesting “medical/hospital” as an additional
      permitted use. At the March 11, 2025 Planning Commission meeting, with a 5-0
      vote, the Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation with
      conditions.

AMEND CITY CODE/ORDINANCE:
    Consider approval of Ordinance No. 2025-022 amending a portion of Title 9-
    13-4 of the City Code to prohibit off-premises signs along SR-7 (State Route
    7), also known as Southern Parkway.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: On January 16, 2025, the City Council
      approved Ordinance No. 2025-006, establishing a temporary six-month prohibition
      on off-premises signs along SR-7. This item is a request to amend a portion of the
      City Code, Title 9-13-4, to prohibit off-premises signs along SR-7. The existing sign
      code doesn't allow off-premises signs on SR-7 but this proposed code amendment
      would make it expressly clear that they are prohibited. The St. George General Plan
      includes a goal to “preserve the natural beauty and features that provide a healthy
      environment and give St. George its unique identity.” Prohibiting off-premises signs
      along SR-7 aligns with this goal by preserving the area’s scenic character and
      mitigating potential negative impacts on public health and safety. The Planning
      Commission held a public hearing on February 25, 2025, and thereafter forwarded a
      recommendation for approval of the requested code amendment to the City Council
      with a 5-0 vote.
