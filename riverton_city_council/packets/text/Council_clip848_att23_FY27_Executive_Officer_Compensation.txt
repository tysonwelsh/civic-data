                                                                 Ordinance No. 26-15
                                                           Adopting Compensation for
                                                          Executive Officers for FY 26-27



Under State Code 10-3-818, before a governing body may adopt a final budget or a final amended
budget that includes a compensation increase for executive municipal officers and the City Attorney,
the body must hold a public hearing. For the purposes of this code section, "executive municipal
officer" means all department heads and their chief assistants or deputies. As a fulfillment of the
requirements of this code, Riverton City will be giving all executive municipal officers and the City
Attorney the same percentage increase as all other civilian employees, namely a 3% cost-of-living
increase (COLA).
