What Works
 • 2x annual ACVB member events are well attended business
   community gatherings convened by a non-profit entity
 • ACVB Support for Alta Resort/Town Shuttle Programs
  • Working with Visit Salt Lake to receive night shuttle contributions
  • Applying to Utah Office Tourism Grant to support shuttle (unsuccessful)
  • Facilitating contributions by local partners
  • Making one-time contributions to close funding gaps
 • ACVB Relationship with A.C.E.
  • Provides ACVB initiatives local credibility and visibility
  • Enhances funding available for A.C.E. programs
What Doesn’t Work
 Persistent ACVB challenges of past and present:
 • Board and member recruitment is challenging, static
 • ACVB budget and program planning continues to
   underachieve
  • Routinely underspend budget, growing balance of funds on-hand
 • Low return on investment for destination marketing initiatives
 • Recent application to UOT grant for shuttle funding
  • Very time consuming, not successful
 • Is ACVB a good use of public funds, member contributions?
Dissolution or Dormancy?
Board of Directors is exploring the possibility of dissolving
ACVB
 • Dormancy would require continued board activity, some overhead
   expenses
   • Will ACVB ever have enough resources to retain an employee?
 • ACVB maintains $84,000 checking account balance
   • Bylaws require assets to transferred to another 501(c)6, but…
   • ACVB has not collected member dues or TOA contribution in FY 23
 • Can we ensure successful elements live on?
   • Community gatherings, A.C.E. support
 • Dissolution requires written consent of 2/3 of active ACVB members
   • Will discuss with membership at 4/18 member event
