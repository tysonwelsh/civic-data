BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation for approximately 0.46
acres located at approximately 1935 E. Stag Hill Circle (Exhibit B & C). The property is zoned
RA2. The applicants are requesting that a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit be
approved to allow for a limited assembly and repair home based business with online sales
and three (3) non-residential, non- family employees.

The property is part of the Hunter Ridge At Draper subdivision and the home was built in
2005. This home based business application was originally filed in June of 2023 as the
result of City code enforcement actions, where the applicant was cited for operating
without a business license. Resolution of the citation regarding the lack of a business
license is currently pending in a separate court case. The business is operating outside of
the permitted use standards for home occupations, and may not receive a business license
in order to continue operating without first obtaining a conditional use permit.

Home Occupation.
According to DCMC Section 9-5-080, an application for a conditional use permit for a home
occupation is required at minimum to submit the following:
    a. The name, address and telephone number of the applicant and the applicant's
       agent, if any.
    b. The address and parcel identification of the subject property.
    c. The zone, zone boundaries and present use of the subject property.
    d. A complete description of the proposed conditional use.
    e. A plot plan showing the following:
       (1) Applicant's name;
       (2) Site address;
       (3) Property boundaries and dimensions;
       (4) Layout of existing and proposed buildings, parking, landscaping, and
           utilities; and
       (5) Adjoining property lines and uses within one hundred feet (100') of the
           subject property.
    f. Traffic impact analysis, if required by the city engineer or the planning
       commission.
    g. A statement by the applicant demonstrating how the conditional use permit
       request meets the approval standards of subsection E of this section.
    h. A statement indicating whether the applicant will require a variance in connection
       with the proposed conditional use.
    i. Such other and further information or documentation as the zoning administrator
       may deem necessary for a full and proper consideration and disposition of a
       particular application.

Furthermore the application checklist requests from the applicant a statement by the
applicant demonstrating how the home occupation conditional use permit request meets
the standards of DCMC 9-5-080(E), and a separate written narrative describing the nature
of the home occupation conditional use including information about the proposed use
(describing the proposed use, including information such as required parking, hours of
operation, expected patrons and/or employees, proposed site improvements, etc.). Also,
the statement was to include details of reasonably anticipated detrimental effects to
adjacent properties and the neighborhood (such as traffic or noise), and any mitigation
measures being proposed to alleviate those effects.

The applicant is running a limited assembly and repair home based business. The business
repackages bulk and wholesale shipments of clothing and other articles they receive into
individual packages for sale and shipment to online customers. The applicants state there
are occasional deliveries to the home and shipments being picked up from the home, but
did not provide more specific details of the size or frequency of the deliveries or shipments
or the type of delivery vehicles that are making the deliveries to the home or picking up
shipments from the home. The applicants have proposed to limit deliveries and shipments
to mid-day to minimize noise disruptions to the neighborhood. The listed hours of
operation are Monday – Friday 9:00 am – 7:00 pm. The applicants have stated they have a
home office in the home and that they store inventory in the garage and the home. The
applicants declined planning staffs request to provide dimensioned or scaled floor plans
for the home but instead provided general sketches and statements that they exceed the
maximums listed in the permitted use standards of ordinance (Exhibits F and G).

Staff has included several suggested conditions in the recommended conditions section of
this report. These are intended to mitigate the following existing and anticipated negative
impacts to adjacent properties and the neighborhood created by the business operation as
identified by staff:
    • Increases in traffic and parking due to the presence of non-resident employees.
    • Noise from inventory deliveries and shipments.
    • Impacts to the neighborhood if deliveries were made by large commercial vehicles
        such as box trucks or semi-trucks.
    • Non-compliance with required residential parking inside the garage and
        displacement of vehicles onto the driveway or street.
    • Overall scale of the operation overtaking and displacing the primary residential use
        of the home.
    • Potential nuisances created by the operation of packaging equipment.
    • The additional trash and recycling generated by shipping and receiving business and
        the increased burden placed on utility pick up services and potential for overflow to
        spill into yard areas or the street.

Planning Division Review.

    1. Staff comments and concerns have primarily been included in the body of the
       report. Statements and submittal documents provided to staff by the applicants are
       very inadequate to determine specifics as to the extend of current operations or the
       full extent of what is being requested. The limited assembly and repair use is
       intended to be for small scale production of items produced one at a time in small
       quantities or on a made to order basis and not for mass production. Staff is mainly
       concerned with the existing detrimental impacts to the neighborhood such as noise,
       increased business and delivery traffic, increased demands on utility services, and
       the like. These impacts are principally being created by the large magnitude of the
