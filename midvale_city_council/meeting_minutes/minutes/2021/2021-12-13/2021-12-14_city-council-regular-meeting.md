        A.      CONSIDER RESOLUTION NO. 2021-R4O A RESOLUTION
                AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO REGISTER MIDVALE CITY AND
             PARTICIPATE IN THE NATIONAL OPIOID SETTLEMENT
Lisa Garner thanked Mayor Hale and for the tremendous service he has provided. She
hopes when he reflects back that he remembers all of the words that have been said and
know that they are very genuine. She said Mayor Hale brings a positive energy that has
been very helpful to staff.

Lisa Garner said in July of this year, a nationwide settlement agreement was reached
between several states, including Utah, against the three largest pharmaceutical
distributors: McKesson, Cardinal Health and AmerisourceBergen and manufacturer
Janssen Pharmaceuticals, lnc., and its parent company Johnson & Johnson. These
settlements could potentially provide substantial funds to states and subdivisions for
abatement of the Opioid epidemic across the country and will impose transformative
changes in the way the defendants conduct their business. Utah has agreed to participate
in these settlements and has also pursued claims in bankruptcy proceedings for Purdue
Pharma and Mallinckrodt. ln the settlement agreements, the state of Utah and the political
subdivisions all receive more funds if more subdivisions join. lf Utah enters both
                                             10
Proceeding of City Council Meeting
December 14, 2021

settlement agreements with no subdivision participation, it will recover approximately
$151 million dollars over 18 years. lf Utah enters both settlement agreements with full
subdivision participation, Utah and the political subdivisions will recover approximately
$270 million over'18 years. Utah should also recover about $72 million over 9 years for
the bankruptcy cases of Purdue and Mallinckrodt. Most, if not all, of the money will be
required to be used to address the opioid epidemic and its impact on Midvale.

The terms of the settlement agreement require that states and political subdivisions
register with the national settlements website in order to participate in the opioid
settlements and be eligible for possible funds. After registration, the City would receive
additional information and documentation for participation if eligible. lt is still unknown the
amount of funds the state may receive or the extent of participation by the counties and
other cities in the proposed settlement.

Mayor Hale asked what the cost is for the city.

Ms. Garner said there is no cost to the city and no guarantee for any fund compensation

        B.     CONSIDER RESOLUTION NO, 2021.R41 REAPPOINTING PAUL
               GLOVER TO SERVE AS MIDVALE CITY'S REPRESENTATIVE ON THE
               MOSQUITO ABATEMENT BOARD
Matt Dahl said the South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District (District) was
created in 1952 and serves most of Salt Lake County. The District has an 18 member
Board of Trustees comprised of representatives from each municipality within the District
and an at-large member representing the county. The mission of the District is to
safeguard the health, welfare, and comfort of the public by controlling mosquitos and
black flies, with an emphasis on safety, high standards, environmental awareness, cost
efficiency, responsibility, and leadership.

Councilmember Paul Glover has been Midvale City's representative on the District's
Board of Trustees since January 1, 2018. His term on the Board of Trustees expires
                         .

Councilmember Glover be reappointed to the District's Board of Trustees. Mr. Hougaard
