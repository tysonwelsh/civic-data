ORDINANCE 05-16-2023 – AMENDING TITLE 10 DEFINITIONS ADOPTED:
   Mr. Atkinson explained to the Council that recent changes to the state code have made it necessary
to change some of the definitions to the city’s code and also add in certain definitions governing Internal
Accessory Dwelling Units (IADU). The state legislature passed a code last year requiring every

detached home with other conditions for the purpose of increasing the available inventory of affordable

ORDINANCE 05-16-2023-A- INTERNAL ACCESSORY DWELLING UNITS ADOPTED:

    Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the Council that Ordinance 05-16-2023 specifies a new code governing
IADU within a primary residence which will be also occupied by the primary owner. IADUs will be
allowed in the single-family residential zones on parcels that are 6,000 square feet or larger. The goal
from the state is to provide long-term rental space for the affordable housing market. A permit will be
required and will accompany a business license. Separate utility meters will be prohibited, and one off-
street parking space is required for the user of the IADU. The IADU will also have a separate address for
public safety and mailing purposes.
   Councilor Callaway moved to adopt Ordinance 05-16-2023-A. Councilor Worwood seconded the
motion. The motion passed unanimously.

ORDINANCE 05-16-2023-B – OHV/ATV CODE CHANGES ADOPTED:

    Mr. Atkinson updated the Council that recent legislation by the State of Utah has made it necessary
to modify the city’s code regarding OHV/ATV rules. State law requires that anyone under 18 years of
age must be under direct supervision of a person who is at least 18 years of age unless they possess a
valid driver’s license. The person who is directly supervising someone who is under 18 years of age must
be no more than 300 feet away, must have visual contact, and must be able to give and receive advice
and assistance.
   These rules apply for any off-highway vehicle on public land, trail, street, or highway of the
state. "Off-highway vehicle" means any snowmobile, all-terrain type I vehicle, all-terrain type II vehicle,
all-terrain type III vehicle, or motorcycle. Mr. Atkinson said that although these changes differ from the
original intent of the city’s code, Nephi City needed to modify the OHV/ATV code to comply with these
state requirements. He stated that concerns over these changes can be conveyed to our local state
legislators.
    Councilor Ostler moved to adopt Ordinance 05-16-2023-B AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 6.2
OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE, PROVIDING FOR THE REGULATION OF OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES WITHIN NEPHI
CITY. Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

TOMPKINS AGREEMENT AMENDMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson presented an amendment to an earlier agreement with Mr. Taylor Warburton because
recently the City Council made the decision to modify the City Code to not require existing homes to
install curb, gutter, and sidewalk when falling under the subdivision exemption law. Mr. Warburton
previously had an agreement with the City to install curb, gutter, and sidewalk on a 3-lot subdivision
that was exempt from the subdivision code. He has requested that the agreement be modified to
remove the requirement to install curb, gutter, and sidewalk on an existing home in that subdivision.
   Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the amendment to the Tompkin
Agreement. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
