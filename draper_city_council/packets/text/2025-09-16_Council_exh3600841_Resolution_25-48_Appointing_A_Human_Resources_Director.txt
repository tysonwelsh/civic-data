                                RESOLUTION NO. 25-48

               A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL
               APPOINTING A HUMAN RESOURCES DIRECTOR

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has created the position of Human
Resources Director to administer the day-to-day human resources functions of the City;
and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Draper City Municipal Code the Human Resources
Director shall be appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City
Council; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint Derek Orth as the Human Resources
Director to exercise the powers, duties, responsibilities, and authority assigned to the
Human Resources Director under statute, ordinance, and policy; and

         WHEREAS, the City Council has determined it is in the best interest of the
health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Draper to provide its advice and consent to
the appointment of Derek Orth as Draper City Human Resources Director based on his
abilities and prior experience relating to the duties of the office.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council gives its advice and consent
to the Mayor’s appointment of Derek Orth as Draper City Human Resources Director,
and Derek Orth is hereby appointed Draper City Human Resources Director with all the
powers, duties, responsibilities, and authority specified by statute, ordinance, and policy.

       Section 2. Employment Agreement. A written employment contract has been
prepared and negotiated setting forth Derek Orth’s terms of employment. That contract is
hereby approved and the City Manager is authorized to execute the same.

        Section 3. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution
shall be severable.

       Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.
     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 16th DAY OF SEPTEMBER 2025.
