HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2018-28

AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING A NEW HERRIMAN FLAG

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Council (the “Council”) met in regular session on
August 22, 2018, to consider, among other things, an ordinance adopting a new Herriman flag;
and

WHEREAS, on or about June 1, 2000, the Council adopted Ordinance 00-30 that
adopted a Herriman Flag; and

WHEREAS, the Council desires to adopt a new Herriman Flag to replace the old
Herriman Flag; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the residents of Herriman to adopt this ordinance.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council that the flag currently
depicted in Herriman Code of Ordinances 1-5-2 be deleted and the following be inserted in its
place.
