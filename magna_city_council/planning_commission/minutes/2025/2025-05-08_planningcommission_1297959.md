                                          PUBLIC HEARING(S)

                                       Hearings began at – 6:38 p.m.

REZ2025-001360 - James Schouten requested a rezone from C-3 to C-1. Acres: 0.35. Location: 2734
South 8750 West. Planner: Justin Smith (Motion/Voting)

The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Justin Smith provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding paving requirements, restricted locations, auto
dismantling and major repairs, limiting auto sales and small dealerships.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Cripps seconded that motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

No one from the public is present to speak.

Commissioner Richards motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Cripps seconded that motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend application # REZ2025-001360 James Schouten requested a rezone from C-3 to
C-1 to the Magna Council for approval with the condition prohibiting auto sales and small dealerships.
        Motion by: Commissioner Cripps
        2nd by: Commissioner Richards
        Vote: Commissioners voted unanimously in favor (of commissioners present)


OAM2025-001372 – Consideration of an ordinance adopting Magna Engineering Standard Drawings and
Specifications. The proposed ordinance includes the adoption by reference of the latest revision of
AASHTO “A Policy on Geometric Design of Highways and Streets” (Green Book), the Utah Manual on
Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD), and APWA Manual of Standard Plans and Manual of Standard
Specifications, with some exceptions noted in the document. Presenter: MSD Engineering
(Discussion/Recommendation)

The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Engineering Manager Tamaran Woodland provided an
analysis of the Standard Drawings and Specifications.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding mill and overlay, AASHTO requirements, and
street lighting.

No one from the public is present to speak.
