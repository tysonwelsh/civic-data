Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From:
CC:  Date: March 16, 2021
Re:  Comments: Council Member Mike Green has been called to active, full-time duty in the armed forces in a status other than in accordance with Title 10, U.S.C.A.   His leave is governed by the provisions of Utah Code Ann. 20A-1-513.   He may elect to carry out his elected duties by confirming the same in writing within 10 days of arriving at the place of his deployment.
The Draper City Council thanks Council Member Green for his service and looks forward to his safe return.
