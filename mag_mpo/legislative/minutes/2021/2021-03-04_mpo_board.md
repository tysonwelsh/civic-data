Approve February 4, 2021 Meeting Minutes Mayor Mann moved to approve the February 4, 2021 meeting minutes. Mayor Fullmer seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Public Comment None. Legislative Update Carlos Braceras, UDOT Executive Director and Carolyn Gonot, UTA Executive Director gave a summary of HB433, in addition to the process and timeline moving forward. HB433 authorizes $1.1B in investments in roads, public transportation, and active transportation, through a combination of the authorization of new bonding and the appropriation of available one-time revenues. The Transportation Investment Fund (TIF) will receive $705M for the cost of right-of-way acquisition, construction, reconstruction, or renovation of or improvement to various projects. The Transportation Commission will have the responsibility to assign a dollar value to each project. The Transit Transportation Investment Fund (TTIF) will receive $101.6M to double track strategic sections of the FrontRunner commuter rail system, in addition to paying for a rail station in Vineyard. Active Transportation will receive $35M that will go into the TIF. Funding is for paved pedestrian or paved nonmotorized transportation projects. The bill does not specify projects. Andrew Jackson thanked UDOT and UTA for all their hard work during the legislature. Other bills of interest were discussed. Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Amendment-UTA Vineyard Commuter Rail Station and Double Tracking – Bob Allen, MAG In 2018 the State Legislature awarded $4M UDOT Transportation Investment Funds (TIF) to build the Vineyard Commuter Rail Station. MPO Regional Planning Committee March 4, 2021 Page 3 Additionally, UTA is following a federal mandate to implement Positive Train Control to prevent train collisions. A study determined two miles of double tracking north of the station would be required to implement the new safety measures and $10M UTA funds were programmed to construct them. Design on the combined project is now complete and an additional $1.68M is needed to complete the station and an additional $6.94M is needed to complete the double tracking. UTA has committed to providing the additional $8.62M with their funds. MAG does not control any of these funds or select how they are used. However, for them to be added to the project and the Statewide Transportation Improvement Program (STIP), they must be amended into the TIP which MAG does control. MAG Staff and TAC recommend the TIP be modified to add the UTA funds to this project. Mayor Johnson moved that $8.62M UTA Funds be added to the Vineyard Commuter Rail Station and Double Tracking project. Mayor Westmoreland seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
