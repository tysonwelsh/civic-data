PRESENTATION: VICTIMS OF CRIME ACT (VOCA) GRANT: CHILDREN’S

JUSTICE CENTER

Children’s Justice Center Director Kristy Pike explained that the Children’s Justice Center
(CJC) collaborates with multidisciplinary partners to protect each child, advance justice,
promote healing, and educate the community. The Victims of Crime Act (VOCA) grant is
a huge funding source for the Children’s Justice Center. There were 502 primary victims
served in 2020.

Victim Advocate Kenzie Bradshaw explained that the CJC assists with victim reparation
applications, child protective orders, providing information on victim’s rights, and contract
with school-based therapists.

Megan Bigwood explained that direct individual therapy for primary and secondary victims
is provided by the therapists.

The Commissioners expressed appreciation for the CJC employees for the work that they
do in the community.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2740, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON
COUNTY AND THE CITY OF ST. GEORGE FOR SUPPORT OF THE PUBLIC
TRANSIT SERVICE TO AND FROM ZION NATIONAL PARK

Commissioner Cox explained that the County imposed a .25% County Option Sale and
Use Tax for highways and public transit on all goods and services within Washington
County to fund transportation facilities and public transit. Washington County collects 40%
for municipal public transit or to regional public transit for the County and the
municipalities within the County that do not have their own public transit system.

The Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) awarded fund towards the establishment
of a new St. George to Zion National Park transit line contingent upon local support,
governance and long term funding commitments. The City of St. George currently operates
a public transit services that serves the areas in St. George and has negotiated an agreement
with UDOT for the implementation of Transit Services. This will help to alleviate traffic
congestion on state and local roadways as well as provide transportation for Washington
County residents and tourists visiting Zion National Park. Washington County supports
this agreement for the transit line and desires to see its success.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2740, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Agreement Between Washington
County and the City of St. George for Support of the Public Transit
Service to and From Zion National Park. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and Iverson voting aye.
