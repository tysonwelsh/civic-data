                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-50

  A RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE HISTORIC PRESERVATION
                           COMMISSION

        WHEREAS, on June 14, 2016, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No. 16-11
which provides for the preservation of historic structures and other assets located within Riverton
City; and

       WHEREAS, Administration of Ordinance No. 16-11 requires appointment of a Historic
Preservation Commission; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the following volunteer
appointments of Kim Rindlesbach, Jeff Trimble, and Perry Newman; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Kim Rindlesbach, Jeff
Trimble, and Perry Newman are residents of Riverton City, and are willing and capable of
serving on the Historic Preservation Commission;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointments of Kim
          Rindlesbach, Jeff Trimble, and Perry Newman to serve on the Historic Preservation
          Commission, which term shall end on January 31, 2028.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 4th day of
June 2024 by the following vote:
