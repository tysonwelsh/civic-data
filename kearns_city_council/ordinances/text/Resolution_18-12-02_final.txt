A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING THE ANNUAL
BUDGET FOR THE YEAR 2019 FOR THE OPERATION OF THE TOWNSHIP.
WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council has previously adopted a tentative budget for the
2018 budget year; and
WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township has made the tentative budget available to the public for
review and consideration as required by law; and
WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council has held a public hearing on the proposed 2019
annual budget for the Township;
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE KEARNS METRO
TOWNSHIP:
1. That the attached exhibit A is hereby adopted as the 2018 annual budget for the operation of the
Kearns Metro Township for the 2019 calendar year or until such time as it may be amended.
2. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.
