2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of DR Horton’s request for review of the Micron
Area Plan Concept changing the Land Use Map of the existing Micron Area Plan.

    Brittney presented information on the agenda item. The proposed plan shows mixed use with
    a trail network.

    Commissioner Ellis asked that the ESA was based on the original plan and that the City has
    not designated it to the general area. The City does not have an open designation. The
    development code lists areas that are included for open space.

    The applicant, Adam Loser representing DR Horton, was present. He presented the original
    area plan from 2012. DR Horton met with surrounding City's and decided that it was not a
    great use for Lehi. The same amount of ERU's are still accounted for. The dominant feature
    will be a trail head corridor system.

    Commissioner Everett asked about the surrounding City's being annexed and if it is includes
    other ERU's. The applicant stated it does include the ERU's and they are just being spread out
    across the land.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment. He acknowledged that there
    were 24 comments on the Engage Lehi website.

    Joyce Pritchard is a resident of Highland. She stated the annexed property has an original
    agreement that will be breached by the amount of ERU's per acre. She stated it will impact
    wildlife and their migratory path. She is concerned about the traffic flow, where there has
    already been a fatality. She asked about the retention pond and asked if it was going to be a
    water easement.

    Lindsay Gayman stated as a homeowner she doesn't see how this is an amendment as it is
    drastically different as what was approved by Lehi City. The land in the proposal has shrunk

    green space is questionable at best and is not safe or useable. She stated DR Horton does not
    follow best practices that Lehi City has laid out. She stated it is not sustainable for the long
    term and would encourage the City to look at the long term goal.

    John Spencer stated his field is used by many because there is no place else to use. They will
    be located near the 55 and plus open area and thought the development would be a park or
    open space and he does no see that in the ordinance. He feels that the Planning Commission
    needs to be addressed as part of the planning process.

    eliminating key elements. They are proposing "Fern Gully" and are eliminating open space
    and parks. He stated the open space is essentially in the detention basin near SR-92. He stated
    Belmont was counted as open space, detention basin was counted as open space, designation
    of restricted space was counted as open space and that is not in the code. Open space requires
    some level of access. They are offering the hill side and counting that as open space. They
    have missed out and have put units in the path of a fire. He encouraged them to read the code
    and apply it to usable open space that benefits private and public open space.
