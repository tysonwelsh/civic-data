BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of an Amended Site Plan for approximately 0.65
acres located on the east side of 700 East, at approximately 11681 South (Exhibit B). The
property is currently zoned CC. The applicant is requesting that an Amended Site Plan be
approved to allow for modification to the buffer fence standards along the south property
line.

The subject property is part of a larger two parcel development encompassing 1.08 acres.
The Planning Commission approved a site plan in September 2009 for a single office
building on the south portion of the larger development. The applicant then obtained a site
plan amendment in 2018 to add one additional office building, located on the north side of
the larger development and on the subject property.

On February 2, 2021 the City Council approved Ordinance 1474, which amended the

ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Community
Commercial land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is
characterized as follows:

The property has been assigned the CC zoning classification (Exhibit D). According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the CC zone is to
“provide areas where commercial uses may be established which are generally oriented toward
local residents rather than out of town patrons. Uses typical of this zone include planned retail
and office development and limited medium to high density residential uses that can be
harmoniously mixed with commercial development.” The property abuts the CC and RA1
(Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot lot minimum) zones on the north, the CC zone
on the east and west, and the CC and R3 (Single-family Residential, 13,000 square foot lot
minimum) zones on the south.

Site Plan Layout. The applicant has submitted a modified site plan showing a revision to the
south landscape buffer (Exhibit E). The property abuts four residential properties along its
south property line. DCMC Section 9-23-110(A) requires a 20-foot wide landscape buffer
with a 6-foot tall fence for office buildings adjacent to residential zone. The Zoning
Administrator is authorized to approve a deviation allowing for a 10-foot wide landscape
buffer with an 8-foot tall fence per DCMC Section 9-23-110(C)(1). The site plan amendment
approved in 2018 included a 10-foot south landscape buffer and a condition of approval
that the south landscape buffer contain an 8-foot tall fence. The code at that time allowed
the 8-foot tall fence to be one of four types of fencing: masonry, precast concrete,

lightweight precast fiber reinforced concrete, or lightweight steel reinforced polyethylene
plastic wall.

The applicant is requesting to eliminate the fence requirement and instead provide a
hedgerow. DCMC Section 9-23-110(C)(2) allows the Planning Commission to approve a
deviation granting additional reductions to the buffer standards. The minimum landscape
buffer allowed under such a deviation has to be at least 5-feet in width and contain an 8-
foot tall wall or a hedgerow of at least five feet tall at maturity. The code requires the
Planning Commission to make the four findings found in DCMC Section 9-23-110(C)(2):

     9-23-110: LAND USE BUFFERS:
     C. Reductions:
            1. The zoning administrator or designee may reduce land use buffer requirements
        based on the character of specific uses, building orientations, topographic features, or
        other unique characteristics of adjoining uses. A reduced buffer shall consist of a
        minimum ten foot (10') wide landscape buffer and an eight foot (8') tall masonry, precast
        concrete, lightweight precast fiber reinforced concrete, or lightweight steel reinforced
        polyethylene plastic wall.
            2. The planning commission may approve additional deviations to landscape buffer
        requirements if the following criteria are met:
              a. Physical site features preclude strict compliance. In this case an alternative
        landscaping scheme shall be provided which meets or exceeds the intent of the code.
              b. Additional landscaping shall be added to the overall site to compensate for any
        approved reduction in buffer area widths, up to the amount of trees required for the
        original buffer width.
              c. An eight foot (8') tall wall or a hedgerow of at least five feet (5') at maturity has
        been provided.
              d. In no case shall a landscape buffer be less than five feet (5') in width.

The residential properties along the south property line have fenced in back yards (Exhibit
F). The applicant is proposing to maintain the majority of the fencing as is, with one
exception. One residential property will have a 6-foot tall vinyl fence replaced with a 7-foot
tall vinyl fence. This will be consistent with the neighboring property to the west. Other
types of fences along the south property line include a 7-foot tall masonry block fence and
a 6-foot tall trex or wood fence.

The applicant has planted fruitless plum bushes along the existing fence line. At full
maturity they can reach up to 6.5-feet in height and will produce a hedge. The
requirements found in DCMC Section 9-23-110(C)(2)(b) requires additional landscaping be
placed on the overall site, up to the amount of trees required for the original buffer width.
In this case the applicant will be required to provide an additional 7 trees on the property.
These trees can be provided within the buffer area, or somewhere else on the site. A
condition of approval has been included requiring the trees.

standard of review for such requests as:

               E. Standards For Approval: The following standards shall apply to the approval of a
                   site plan:
                1. The entire site shall be developed at one time unless a phased development plan
                   is approved.
                2. A site plan shall conform to applicable standards set forth in this title, including
                   but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping,
                   and building materials.
                3. The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the
                   general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
                4. The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject
                   development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
                   5. The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found
                   in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm water
                   drainage, and utilities concerns.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request, receive public
comment, and make a decision based on the findings listed below and the criteria for
approval, or denial, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

          1.        That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                    Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the

          2.        That additional landscaping be added to the site in conformance with DCMC
                    Section 9-23-110(C)(2), including seven (7) additional trees.
          3.        That all previous conditions of site plan approval are adhered to.
          4.        That no signage is reviewed or approved through this site plan amendment
                    process.

The findings for approval as are follows:

          1.        The proposed amendment complies with DCMC Section 9-23-110(C).
