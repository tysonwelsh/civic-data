| RECITALS
|
| A. Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council is committed to participate with the
| Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District for its municipal services.
|
|
| B. One of the services required is “Addressing” which is the process of assigning
| new addresses to property, correcting errors in addressing and coordinating the naming of streets.
c. Addressing services have been performed by the County for the Township in the
past and now an Interlocal Agreement is needed to define the services and set out the various
rights and duties of all the participants in the municipal services District regarding such services
in the future.
Cc. A Interlocal Agreement has been negotiated between the County, the District and
the Townships within the County to setting out the terms of getting Addressing services from the
District and from Salt Lake County.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Council of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township:
1. The Mayor of the Township is hereby authorized to sign the Interlocal Agreement for
Addressing services in the form attached as Exhibit A hereto subject only to the other
participating entities approving the same form of agreement.
