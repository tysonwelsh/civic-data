      Planner Genna Goodwin presented the request for a Conditional Use Permit to
      construct an 85’ tall unmanned telecommunications facility on Washington County
      School District property located at approximately 301 North 2200 East. She
      provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics: 10-7B-1 Uses;
      Zoning map; Site plans; Proposed location; MonoPINE design/height; Monopole
      design/height; Site design fencing; Current coverage; Potential coverage; and
      Photos of similar poles. She mentioned that staff received several letters and emails
      from citizens in opposition.

      Councilmember Randall commented that she does not like the pine tree look. Other
      Councilmembers agreed.

      Applicant Daniel Thurgood explained Verizon Wireless is the anchor tenant for this
      pole; there is room available for two (2) other carriers. Mr. Thurgood explained why
      the pole needs to be 85’ – to allow for three (3) different carriers to place their
      equipment on the tower and 60’-65’ is the lowest height a carrier will be interested
      in; both of which will lessen the number of towers and puts them in one place. They
      have looked at other locations for the pole; the nearest open space is the park on
      2450 East which is 100’ lower in elevation than the proposed location. Other towers
      in the area are either too far away or lower in elevation so they cannot add
      additional tenants. He understands this area is a concern for neighbors, but this is
      where people are using their phones. This pole will be on Washington County School
      District property, they are favorable of installing the tower. He commented this is
      not a 5g tower; 3g and 4g is what is being installed; however, 5g can be installed in
      the future.

      Councilmember Hughes commented if 5g comes, the tower does not need to be very
      high, just need to be closer in multiple areas. Some can be as low as 4’ which would
      mean they would not need Council approval. The reason this item is before the
      Council is due to the height of the proposed tower.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman explained there are only limited times the City restrict
      5g, such as on a power pole. With this tower, the Council has the ability to lower the
      height, but doing so would not restrict 5g. Approving the 85’ tower does not
      guarantee that there will be co-location; other companies can come in for a similar
      request.

      Councilmember Larkin asked Mr. Thurgood if he has the ability to share the
      information they provided to the residents. Mr. Thurgood explained he cannot unless
      he provides it to the Council and it becomes part of the record. Mayor Pike noted the
      City can provide it to the public. Councilmember Larkin noted the number of
      letters/emails the City received voicing concerns.

      The Council discussed the articles they read while researching 5g technology and cell
      phone usage.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman reminded the Council the item is before them tonight
      due to the height of the proposed tower. They have the ability to set the height. He
St. George City Council Minutes

      further commented that the applicant has stated that 85’ would be the optimum
      height for co-location; however, that height does not guarantee another carrier
      would want to co-locate on the tower. They may come in to request their own
      tower. The federal and state governments have passed laws that take away the
      ability for cities to regulate these towers in most areas. For example, if 5g were
      planned, it is very limited on what the City can deny and where they can locate the
      poles. The City has a handshake agreement with the School District as they would
      like the towers located on their property to generate a bit of income from the leasing
      fees.

      Councilmember Randall noted she is aware of the need for adequate cell phone
      coverage; however, she has an issue with the pole being 85’ in this location.

      location, if a 65’ tower is approved, there may be several towers. Councilmember
