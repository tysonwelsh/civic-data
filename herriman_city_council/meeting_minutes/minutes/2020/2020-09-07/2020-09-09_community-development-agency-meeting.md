                            SPECIAL COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND
                             RENEWAL AGENCY OF HERRIMAN CITY
                                         MINUTES
                                              Wednesday, September 9, 2020
                                              Approved September 28, 2020

The following are the minutes of the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City. The
meeting was held on Wednesday, September 9, 2020 at 6:30 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355
West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in
City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair David Watts

Directors Present:          Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith

Staff Present: City Manager Brett geo. Wood, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Assistant City Manager Wendy
               Thomas, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, City Attorney Chase Andrizzi, Finance Director Alan Rae,
               Community Development Director Blake Thomas, Chief of Police Troy Carr, City Planner Michael
               Maloy, and Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette.

6:30 PM: (OR AS SOON AS POSSIBLE THEREAFTER)
   1. Call to Order
Chair Watts called the meeting to order at 7:45 p.m.

                  1.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes of
                        September 9, 2020
Director Ohrn moved to have the minutes of September 9, 2020 be prepared and distributed to each member of the Community
Development and Renewal Agency Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary,
if after ten days, there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes
are made and the board is in agreement. Director Shields seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

    2. Discussion and Action Items
            2.1. Discussion pertaining to a Participation Agreement with Herriman 73 Partners, LLC for the
                 designation of the Automall as a project area and the sales tax incentive for the project–
                 Chase Andrizzi, City Attorney
City Attorney Chase Andrizzi presented a Participation Agreement for the Herriman North Community
Reinvestment Area for the installation of infrastructure to help support the development of the Auto Mall. Under
this agreement, certain sales tax increment could be used to reimburse the developer for infrastructure as well as for
the acquisition or participant incentives for the project. The agreement would allow for 100-percent of the sales tax
rebate after the City’s obligations had been met. He noted this item would come back to the Board to consider the
participation agreement.

    3. Adjournment
Director Ohrn moved to adjourn the Community and Development Renewal Agency meeting at 7:28 p.m. Director Smith seconded the
motion, and all voted aye.


I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true, accurate
and complete record of the meeting held on September 9, 2020. This document constitutes the official minutes for the
Community Development and Renewal Agency Meeting.
