MPO Technical Advisory Committee MEETING MINUTES May 28, 2024 | 1:30 pm ATTENDEES ABSENCES Ryan Robinson, Alpine Brandon Larsen, Eagle Mountain Andy Spencer, Highland Brad Kenison, Lehi Rob Hunter, Mapleton Taggart Bowen, Orem John Dorny, Orem Jill Spencer, Payson Vern Keeslar, Provo Jason Bond, Santaquin Dillon Muirbrook, Spanish Fork Jered Johnson, Spanish Fork Alma Haskell, UTA Dede Murray, UTA Nick Wilcox, UTA Eric Ellis, Vineyard Richard Nielson, Utah County DISCUSSION & AGENDA ITEMS Public Comment Chair Jill Spencer opened the meeting at 1:30 pm Action: 2025 Unified Planning Work Program Shawn Eliot stated the UPWP describes the transportation planning work undertaken by the MPO, UDOT and UTA within the MPO area. The work program has three purposes related to supporting the MPO which are, demonstrate how federal planning funds will be used within the MPO area, fund staﬃng, and fund studies and contracts. There are 10 planning factors that must be considered by the MPO as transportation plans and programs are developed that include the following: ● support the economic vitality● increase the safety● increase the security● protect and enhance the environment, promote energy conservations, and improve the quality of life● enhance the integration and connectivity● promote eﬃcient system management and operation● emphasize the preservation● improve resiliency and reliability● increase the accessibility and mobility● enhance travel and tourism There are five main focus areas over the next work cycle that are incorporated into the UPWP. The UPWP is broken into task areas taken from the FTA Metropolitan Planning Program grant categories. Each of these areas are designated within the document under different colors.● Program Support & Administration● General Development & Comprehensive Planning● Long-Range Transportation Planning● Short-Range Transportation Planning● Transportation Improvement Program There is a chart within the UPWP that shows the revenue and expenditures of funds. It shows within the planned revenue the anticipated federal, state and local funding. The planned expenditures were broken out into the five task areas. Shawn Eliot showed a chart that breaks down the planned revenue into the state, local and federal funding sources. Shawn Eliot stated there are about $3.1 million for transportation studies and contracts led by MAG. Those include three new studies and contracts, one adjusted study and contract, five annual studies and contracts, and nine carryover studies and contracts. UDOT is leading one new study and contract with 11 carryover studies and contract totalling approximately $30 million. With approximately $10 million UTA is leading six new studies and contracts and one carryover study and contract. Brad Kenison moved to recommend that the MPO Board approve the 2025 Unified Planning Work Program, contingent that no significant public comments are received that would require an update to the program. Richard Nielson seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Page | 2 Conclusion and Adjournment Chair Jill Spencer reminded the Committee the next meeting will be held on July 29, 2024. Chris Trusty moved to adjourn the meeting. Brad Kenison seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Page | 3
