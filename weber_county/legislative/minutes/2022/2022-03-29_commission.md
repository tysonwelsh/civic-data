         2.       Public Hearing to discuss and and/or take action on a county initiated ordinance to amend various
                  sections of the Weber County Land Use Code to adjust and clarify permitted and conditional uses
                  and site development standards in the manufacturing zones (MV-1, M-1, M-2, & M-3), and to
                  include general administrative and clerical amendments.
                  Scott Perkes (Planning): County was approached in the past year by a food manufacturing business
                  looking to locate out west in manufacturing zones. While looking at manufacturing zone ordinances
                  and land use codes, the business brought forward some concerns for food manufacturing. Text
                  amendment initiated by staff to address these potential issues. Changes to indicate all land uses are
                  to be indoor only unless specifically stated otherwise as “outdoor” or “yard”; process review
                  required. Zoning/rezoning process looked at as well, including parcel size limitations. Design
                  Review process adjusted to waive the design review if a larger scale development agreement was
                  reviewed through legislative process with Planning and County Commissions. While this business
                  may not come to Weber County, this effort improves our land use regulations and will help to attract
