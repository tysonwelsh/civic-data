The following are the minutes of the Herriman Planning Commission meeting held on Wednesday,
September 04, 2024, at 6:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman
Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the
City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Commission, media, and interested
citizens.

6:00 PM WORK MEETING (Fort Herriman Conference Room)
Chair Andy Powell called the meeting to order at 6:05 p.m.

1.   Commission Business

     1.1. Review of City Council Decisions – Michael Maloy, Planning Director
Planning Manager Spencer conveyed the City Council approved the Alan Prince rezone and the
Master Development Agreement (MDA) amendments for the Mountain View Plaza. The Council
supported not allowing the animal service at any plaza. The corner plaza draft changes were shown.
The stage was moved to the corner with fire features and trees with seating around it. The wall at
the back of the stage is 70” inches. Commissioner Fenn disclosed the iron fence was approved.
Planning Manager Spencer briefed during the work session they talked about accessory vehicles.

Their final requests for the ordinance include any cosmetic/aesthetic language to be removed,
screening requirements are removed unless they are requesting to exceed the amount of approved
vehicles, RVs utilized during home construction can hook up to electric, water and sewer, the
breakdown of acreage and number of RV’s will be updated. This will go back to the council for

their work meeting. Planning Director Maloy detailed the meeting will discuss the Rose Crest,

finalized. The presentation would be advantageous for the commission to join. Planning Manager

   1.3. Discussion of Herriman City Planning Commission policies and land use regulations –
        Michael Maloy, Planning Director
Planning Director Maloy explained the Planning Commission has three policies. The policy on how
to manage the meetings was amended and approved in March of 2023. The Ethics Policy was
reviewed, and a little bit of language was changed. The Referral Policy has been worked on slowly
due to the changes in the animal ordinances. The animal permits have been removed from the
ordinance and will now be removed from the referral policy. The council wants to create a
formalized policy for appointing or reappointing commission members. This would be a council
policy for directing the administration.
A draft Referral Policy was shown. The policy will clarify we want discussion among all members
including the alternates. Once a motion has been made a non-voting member would not be allowed
to further discuss the item. Commissioner Sickles desired to clarify after a motion to close a public
hearing the commission could still discuss the matter. Planning Director Maloy informed the draft
changes “acting member” of the commission to “voting member” for consistency. The council
wants to assign a threshold for attendance of less than 50% would be a cause for removal.
Staff want to change the utility cabinets of a certain size be administratively approved. When the
code is changed to allow for staff approval the council is in favor of the change.
A draft of the Appointment Policy was distributed. Planning Director Maloy suggested having the
Mayor be a liaison for representing the new candidates. This formalizes the process to bring a new
appointment consideration to the council. For a reappointment, they would like an evaluation on the
member including contributions, attendance, deminer, respect for each other and the applicants,
representing the city, possibly inviting feedback from the public or an applicant but these can be

subjective. He pointed out we have had a great commission, so this hasn’t been an issue. He asked

   4.1. Review and consider a Preliminary Subdivision Plat Amendment for Academy Village
        POD 39 located generally at the intersection of Real Vista Drive and Mountain View
        Corridor in the C-2 Commercial Zone. (Public Hearing)
        Applicant: Alex Dahlstrom (authorized agent)
        Acres: ±23.63
        File No: S2024-100
https://www.herriman.org/uploads/files/4857/Item-4.1-Staff-Report.pdf
Planning Manager Spencer explained the previous request for approval expired March 2024. The
property map was shown. The originally proposal was for 17 lots, this is now reduced to 11 lots.
They will vacate the existing subdivision. The current plat was recorded in 2018. The proposal
includes adding a buffering for the residential area to the east. Compliance and zoning have no
issues. Staff recommends approval with conditions. Commissioner Bradford questioned the
reason for the reduction in the number of lots. Planning Manager Spencer noted the applicant is
online to answer the question.

Alex Dahlstrom-Applicant- disclosed they are phasing pod 39 into 2 phases. The road needs to be
installed before they can record the lots without requiring bonding. These 11 lots will be phase 1.
The road will connect over to Real Vista Dr. Until it is installed the sewer district won’t let them
record it as individual lots. Once the road is complete, they will bring an adjustment for the larger
lot.

Chair Powell opened the public hearing.

No comments were offered.

Commissioner Garcia moved to close the public hearing, Seconded by Commissioner Sickles and all
voted Aye.
Commissioner Rypien relayed it makes sense why they are reducing the lots. Commissioner Fenn
clarified all the roads are private roads and not public.
Commissioner Garcia moved to approve item 4.1 Review and consider a Preliminary Subdivision
Plat Amendment for Academy Village POD 39 located generally at the intersection of Real Vista
Drive and Mountain View Corridor in the C-2 Commercial Zone with staff’s recommendations 1.
Satisfy staff comments. 2. Vacate existing subdivision. 3. Show water easement. 4. Apply
buffered setbacks as proposed by staff.
Commissioner Rypien seconded the motion.
