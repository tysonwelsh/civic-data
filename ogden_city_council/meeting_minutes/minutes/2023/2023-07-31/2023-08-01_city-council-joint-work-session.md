Agenda Review
Vice Chair Richey and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.
Community and Economic Development Update
Community and Economic Development Director Cooper used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide the
Council with an update on the Capitol Square project; this included a presentation of conceptual plans for the project,
environmental work that is being completed at the site currently, parking accommodations, and cost of the units. He then provided
a link to the new page on the City’s website that is now live regarding the developer selection process for Redevelopment Agency
(RDA) projects. He navigated through the website and highlighted the buttons that visitors can click on to fill out a form to
include them in the City’s registry for developers or service providers. The City will provide a press release regarding the registry
and staff is working to identify other opportunities for publicly advertising the new website and the registry.
Natural Resources and Sustainability Stewardship Committee Annual Report
Jonathan Melton, Acting Chair of the Natural Resources and Sustainability Stewardship Committee, used the aid of a
PowerPoint presentation to provide the Committee’s annual report; he touched on the creation and staffing of the Sustainability
Coordinator position in January 2023, the Committee’s participation in many sustainability-geared events in the community
throughout the year, development of recommendations for electric vehicle (EV) charging stations in downtown Ogden zones, and
creation of marketing materials for outreach events. For Fiscal Year (FY) 2024, the Committee’s strategic action plan includes a
focus on implementation of the Energy Wise Ogden Strategic Implementation Plan, continued public outreach, and acting as a
resource for the City Council and Mayor on any topics that arise throughout the year. ( )
There was brief discussion among the Council and Mr. Melton regarding certain environmental topics of public interest,
such as the changes to the City’s recycling program and hiring a new service provider.

j Creation of Elected Officials Compensation Commission or Committee: Council Director Eller-Smith explained on July
/ 18, 2023, during a discussion about possible changes to the City’s Mayoral compensation ordinance, Council members indicated i
they would like to consider creating a compensation committee. The committee would be tasked with making recommendations :
for total compensation for Ogden’s elected officials. Provo and Salt Lake City use citizen committees for this purpose as do many
cities across the U.S. The State of Utah has also established the State Elected Official and Judicial Compensation Commission.
Council staff have been working to develop a draft ordinance for review by the Council. Prior to completing the draft ordinance,
staff is seeking direction on certain topics, including the following:
1. Should the group be referred to as a committee or commission?
2. Should there be any specific qualifications for appointees (¢.g., experience in compensation packages,
representation from each municipal district)? .
3. Should the mayor and council have equal number of appointees on the committee?
4. Should there be additional appointees selected by those appointed by the elected officials? Ifso, how many?
5. What process should be used to identify potential appointees?
6. Should the committee meet every year?
Ms. Eller-Smith facilitated discussion among the Council regarding the six questions listed above and indicated Council
staff will use the feedback provided by the Council to formulate a draft ordinance for Council consideration. There was discussion
about the appropriate number of members of the Committee; the number that should be selected by the Council and the number :
that should be selected by the Mayor; the role of the Human Resources Division in the process; and the public advertising methods
that will be used to recruit members, and the application/selection process for members.
Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Budget Amendment — Junction Parking, Trackline
Property Sale, and Capital Square Property Sale
Comptroller Stout explained Administration is proposing several amendments to the Fiscal Year 2024 Redevelopment
Agency (RDA) budget. These amendments include recognizing bond proceeds for improvements to the Junction parking
structures, recognizing proceeds from the sale of property within the Trackline development, and recognizing revenue from the
sale of property associated with the Capital Square project. The total proposed increase to the RDA budget is $3,638,900.
. Council member Hyer inquired as to the scope of work at the Junction parking structure. Ms. Stout stated she does not
’ have that information but will ask the City’s Facilities Manager to provide a response to the Council regarding that matter.
Council member Hyer stated he would like that information before voting on the budget amendment. There was then a brief
discussion about the improvements to be completed at the Trackline development, including landscaping and general site
improvements.

Proposed Amendments to Joint Resolution on the Acquisition and Conveyance of Real Property: Board Administrator
Eller-Smith reviewed background on this matter; in 2003, the Board adopted Resolution 2003-17, dated September 9, 2003, to
establish a policy for the acquisition and conveyance of Agency real property. This policy was amended in 2007 by Resolution
2007-4, dated March 6. 2007. In 2008, the Utah State Legislature adopted S.B. 294 which amended Utah State Code §17C,
Limited Purpose Local Government Entities - Community Development and Renewal Agencies Act. The amendment added
language requiring the mayor of a municipality operating under a council-mayor form of government to serve as the executive
director of the Redevelopment Agency and exercise the executive powers of the Agency. The city attorney indicated that the
change in the statutory language gave the executive director of the Agency the same powers and authority for acquisition and
conveyance of property held by the mayor. Then Mayor Mathew Godfrey, acting in his capacity as the Ogden Redevelopment
Agency Executive Director, verbally agreed to abide by existing RDA property policies adopted by the Board. In 2012, the
newly elected mayor, Mike Caldwell, agreed to formalize the agreement related to acquisition and conveyance of Agency
property. Joint Resolution 2013-1, adopted January 15, 2013, was a complete restatement of the Agency’s policies and procedures
for acquisition and conveyance of RDA-owned real property. In 2023, the Board adopted a Developer Selection Policy. The Board
Administrator suggested that this new policy, the joint resolution relating to Agency property, Agency by-laws, and future RDA
policies be placed on the website maintained by the City’s codifier, American Legal Publishing. This would make the policies
easily available to the public and ensure future consistency in the application of Agency policies. Board staff are recommending

Council/Board Business

Ceremonial Recognitions: Chair Choberka stated Council leadership receives many requests for ceremonial recognitions
for individuals or groups in the community and it may be helpful to have a Council discussion about the group’s ideas for the
types of recognitions that should be considered and how often. The Council engaged in discussion of the matter, specifically the
frequency with which the Compass Award, a Pioneer Days horse, or the key to the City should be given. Council Executive
Director Eller-Smith invited Council members to provide their feedback to the Council staff, anonymously if they prefer,
regarding the frequency of giving significant awards to individuals.

Fireworks Restrictions: Council member Hyer stated he would like to push back against the State Legislature in some
way regarding the State laws regarding fireworks. Council Executive Director Eller-Smith stated she can reach out to the Utah
League of Cities and Towns (ULCT) to see if other cities have expressed similar concerns and if there may be interest in lobbying
the Legislature regarding the matter.

Pioneer Days Rodeo: Vice Chair Richey stated that next year if the 90" anniversary of the Pioneer Days Rodeo and he
and Council member Blair have been talking about a special way to commemorate the event. Council member Blair stated he
would also like to recognize the clown and announcer, who have been part of the Pioneer Days celebration for several years.

Council member Hyer stated it would be great for the Council to attend the rodeo and give the clown and announcer an award,
rather than requiring them to attend a Council meeting. The Council concurred.

United States 250" Anniversary: Council member Lopez stated he has been appointed to a commission that is charged
with planning State celebrations of the 250" anniversary of the founding of the United States. He invited Council members to send
him any suggestions for things they think should be incorporated into the celebration. He looks forward to representing Ogden in
this endeavor.
