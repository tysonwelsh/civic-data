City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 4, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1564, amending the
Official Land Use Map of Draper City, otherwise known as the Price Real Estate Lot 10
Land Use Map Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 5th day of
October 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
