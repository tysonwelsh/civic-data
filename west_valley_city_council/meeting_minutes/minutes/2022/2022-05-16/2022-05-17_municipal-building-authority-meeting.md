 Councilmember Lars Nordfelt moved to add a public comment period to the agenda with the same

        COMMENT PERIOD
        A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
            Alan Naumann stated that he is in support of the Community Renewable Energy
            Program. He indicated that he believes the City will save money in the long run and
            he believes in the program. Mr. Naumann discussed the goals that the program aims
            to achieve and noted that he believes this program is the only affordable option for
            residents.

               David Garbett stated that he also supports HB 411. He indicated that he owns a
               company that focuses on environmental efforts and believes this is a good program.
               Mr. Garbett stated the program isn’t perfect and it’s difficult to know what the costs
               will be but there are many compelling reasons to participate.

               Jim Vesock stated that he would encourage the Council to study the issue and
               remember the goals that the City has already achieved and put in place.

        B.     CITY MANAGER COMMENTS
               Wayne Pyle, City Manager, had no comments.

        C.     CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS
               Upon inquiry, members of the Authority had no comments.

             Wayne Pyle, City Manager, informed a public hearing had been advertised for the
             Special Building Authority Meeting scheduled May 17, 2022, in order for the
             Authority to hear and consider public comments regarding (A) The Issuance and
             Sale of Not More Than $6,500,000 Aggregate Principal Amount of Lease Revenue
             Bonds, Series 2022, and (B) Any Potential Economic Impact that the Project
             Described Herein to be Financed With the Proceeds of the Bonds Issued Under the
             Act May Have on the Private Sector, and Related Matters.

             Chairman Christensen opened the Public Hearing.

             Laura Lewis, Bond Counsel, detailed the process and timeline of this bond.

             Chairman Christensen closed the Public Hearing.
