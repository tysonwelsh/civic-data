                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-17

               A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY
     COUNCIL APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN
   INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE OF UTAH
 DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION, FOR MUTUAL ACCESS TO FIBER OPTIC
                         CABLE SYSTEMS

        WHEREAS, The State of Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) has
constructed a fiber optic cable system to provide high speed broadband network community for
internet and telecommunications communications (the “UDOT Network”); and

        WHEREAS, the UDOT network is generally located within public rights of way created
for state highway systems, some of which course through Riverton City, such as Bangerter
Highway; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to improve its network and telecommunications
capabilities by installing fiber optic systems connecting key City-owned facilities; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that in order to connect a
City-owned fiber network to backbone infrastructure which supports general
internet/telecommunications services, it must work with a strategic partner such as UDOT to
provide “middle mile” connectivity; and

        WHEREAS, under the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Annotated, 11-13-
101 et seq., any two or more public agencies may enter into agreements with one another for
joint or cooperative action and may also contract with each other to perform any governmental
service activity which each public agency entering into the contract is authorized by law to
perform; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that obtaining middle mile
connectivity by executing an Interlocal Agreement with UDOT will advance the City’s
objectives to improve its network and telecommunications capabilities, and the City’s obligation
to UDOT under the Agreement to provide connectivity through the system to be constructed by
the City, is both a fair and reasonable consideration; and

        WHEREAS, an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement (“Agreement”) has been prepared for
approval and execution by and between UDOT and Riverton City, which states the purposes
thereof, and the extent of the required participation of the parties and the rights, duties,
responsibilities, and obligations of the parties described in this Resolution.
        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

      1. An Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Riverton City and the State of Utah,
         Department of Transportation, in substantially similar form to the attached Exhibit A
         to this Resolution is hereby approved by the Council and the Mayor is hereby
         authorized to execute the same on behalf of Riverton City, once the same is approved
         as to legal form by the Riverton City Attorney.


       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 15th day of
