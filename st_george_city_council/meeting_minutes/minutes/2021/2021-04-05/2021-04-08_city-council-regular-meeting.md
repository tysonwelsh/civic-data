TOUR OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER:
     The Council toured the Washington County Children’s Justice Center. A discussion
     took place regarding the need for them to expand.

      The Council returned to the City offices for the remainder of the meeting.

      Mayor Randall called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
      invocation was offered by Pastor Joe Doherty and The Pledge of Allegiance to the
      Flag was led by Councilmember McArthur.

      Mayor Randall read a proclamation proclaiming April, 2021 as Fair Housing Month.

DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT:
     Consider approval of a development agreement with 3000 East Holdings,
     LLC.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard explained this agreement is to reimburse 3000 East
      Holdings, LLC for pavement installed with the Arbors subdivision. With past
      developments along 3000 East, the City has agreed to install the center 30’ of
      asphalt pavement along 3000 East. The City is reimbursing the developer for 15’ of
      asphalt pavement along the frontage of their property for approximately 1,000 linear
      feet as the developer only improved the west half of 3000 East.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Larkin to approve the
                    development agreement with 3000 East Holdings, LLC.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Smethurst.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.

PRESENTATION FROM SANDSTONE FIXED BASED OPERATIONS (FBO):
     Airport Manager Rich Stehmeier introduced representatives from Sandstone FBO and
     explained what an FBO does. He then outlined the proposal and stated this FBO is
     needed.

       Bob Fahnestock with Sandstone Aviation LLC presented a PowerPoint covering the
       following topics: Our objective; Versatile staff; Services; Fuel storage; Military
       aircraft; Reliable supply; Proposed 12-acre area to lease; Proposed front elevation;
       and Preliminary costs. Employee numbers will be based on demand. Mr. Fahnestock
       spoke about their operation in Boulder City.

       Councilmember Curtis commented there were two (2) FBO operations at the old
       airport.

       City Attorney Shawn Guzman noted there were several complaints regarding some of
       the FBO operations at the old airport. Since the new airport opened and Justin
       Hansen with Above View has been providing the services, things have been much
       better. Staff knew there would be a day where additional services are needed.

       City Manager Adam Lenhard explained staff will review the proposal and return to
       Council with a recommendation.

       Councilmember McArthur commented that before any decisions are made, he would
       like the Council to hear from Justin Hansen with Above View.

REVIEW OF SUMMER WATER AND ENERGY UTILITY EFFICIENCY PROGRAM:
     Water and Energy Conservationist Rene Fleming provided a handout regarding
     energy efficiency. Her proposal is to have a Green, Orange, and Red day, each of
     which have different conservation efforts based on different factors. Staff will
     advertise using social media and other avenues. She then spoke about irrigating
     efficiency and will be coming to the Council soon for the daytime water restrictions.
     She spoke about ways to reduce water use and commented that The Water
     Conservancy District can evaluate and give residents suggestions to conserve water.
     She explained that City and School District facilities use irrigation quality water, not
     culinary water; they water during the day because the City’s irrigation capacity does
     not have the storage or production to provide enough water in the 12-hour block
     suggested for those using culinary water.

     Example of plat; Grand Heights preliminary plat; Rockfall hazard area; Example of

     residential; and Geologic hazard area.

DISCUSSION REGARDING COMPLETE STREETS POLICY:
     Support Services Director Marc Mortensen introduced Tom Millar who is attending via
     Zoom. Mr. Millar was hired to put together a draft policy for complete Streets. He
     provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics: Overview;
     Interactive exercise #1; The why of complete streets; Interactive exercise #2;
     Interactive exercise #3; and What is in the Complete Streets Policy?.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman advised the policy is a guideline that the City uses
      when considering projects. Staff will look at the policy to determine if some of the
      recommendations should be included in design standards for streets.

      Councilmember Larkin left the meeting.

      After being asked by Councilmember McArthur, Public Works Director Cameron
      Cutler explained a complete streets policy tries to balance the use of the street. He
      likes the ideas in a complete streets policy; there is a balance.

      The Council discussed the proposed policy.
