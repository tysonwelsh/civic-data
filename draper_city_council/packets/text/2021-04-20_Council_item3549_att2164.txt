                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-15

       A RESOLUTION APPOINTING PIMMIE LOPEZ AS A REGULAR MEMBER OF THE
       DRAPER CITY ARENA ADVISORY BOARD

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has adopted Ordinances which provide for the
appointment of members to the Draper City Arena Advisory Board; and

      WHEREAS, members of the Draper City Arena Advisory Board are appointed by the
Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, Karen Larsen is no longer able to serve as a regular member of the
Draper City Arena Advisory Board and Pimmie Lopez is currently serving as an alternate
member of the Draper City Arena Advisory Board; and

       WHEREAS, Pimmie Lopez has expressed a willingness to serve as a regular member
of the Draper City Arena Advisory Board; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor proposes to appoint Pimmie Lopez to serve as a member of
the Draper City Arena Advisory Board.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      The City Council hereby provides its advice and consent to the Mayor’s appointment
of Pimmie Lopez to serve as a member of the Draper City Arena Advisory Board.

      Section 1. Appointment. Pimmie Lopez is hereby appointed to fill the term listed
below as a member of the Draper City Arena Advisory Board according to the laws,
ordinances, and regulations governing the Draper City Arena Advisory Board and its
members for the term specified herein.

       Regular Member:
       Pimmie Lopez                               April 20, 2021 through April 30, 2022

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part of provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts and provisions of this Resolution shall be
severable.

        Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
its passage.
      PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THIS THE 20TH DAY OF APRIL 2021.
