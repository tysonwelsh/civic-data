                                Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                          May 26, 2020
                                           7:00 p.m.




   1.   The council discussed a request for a concert in the park by two local bands.

   2.    City Administrator Seth Atkinson updated the council about new law for Police
        retirement tier II funding.

   3.    The council discussed drafting a new ordinance for golf carts use of city streets, and the
        enforcement of ATV use on city streets. The council instructed City Attorney Kasey
        Wright to draw up a golf cart ordinance to include the council’s recommendations.
        The council directed staff to communicate with the residents about enforcement of the
        ATV laws. The areas of concern were the age of the drivers, helmets, speed, the
        number of riders, and young drivers being accompanied by an adult.

   4.    Proposed Annexation Level Change Request-- Mr. Atkinson explained the different
        sewer options of bringing the utility to the proposed annexation area.
        The council directed staff to change the annexation policy plan to include the
        requirement that you cannot skip levels when requesting a level change.
        Councilor Seely suggested that we ask Spanish Fork how they planned for their different
        zones within their city. Kyle Marchant suggested that we work on a sewer master plan
        sooner rather than later.

   5.    City Administrator Updates: Mr. Atkinson shared a letter from the victims advocate
        program requesting continued support of their program.

   6.   The City Councilors gave various updates.

   7.   Adjournment 9:12




Present: Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Justin Seely, Kasey Wright, Seth
Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Kyle Marchant. Absent: Mayor Glade Nielson, Councilor Kent Jones.
