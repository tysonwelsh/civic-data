                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-59

     A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO THE RIVERTON CITY
             PERSONNEL POLICIES AND PROCEDURES MANUAL

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual (the “Manual”)
is used to set acceptable parameters for employment operations, and define employee benefits; and

      WHEREAS, the Manual is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in law or in
employment practices arise; and

       WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the manual and determined some terms need to be
updated to improve benefits for employees who choose to serve in the Utah National Guard or in
the Armed Services as a reservist; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment to the
Manual as proposed by City staff would encourage servicemen and women to work for Riverton
City by providing enhanced benefits when employees are on military leave; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City and City
employees to adopt revisions to the Manual which describe the terms and conditions by which the
City’s employees may receive benefits described in this Resolution.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual, Section 5.11, Military
          Leave, attached to this Resolution is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit A of this
          Resolution. The Mayor is hereby directed to ensure that the Manual is accordingly
          revised and made available to all City employees within a reasonable time from adoption
          of this Resolution.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

5.11 MILITARY LEAVE

Riverton City will enforce the USERRA regulations for employees enlisted in the military. For further
reference employees are directed to visit the following website:
http://www.dol.gov/vets/programs/userra/

An employee shall be granted leave with compensation for the difference in salary for active duty, for
service in the Utah National Guard or in the Armed Forces Reserves. Differential pay shall not be
granted on holidays. Employees shall receive full holiday leave pay while on active duty. Leave must be
pre-approved by presentation of orders to serve active duty, and by providing any information required
by the Human Resources Department to establish the employee’s compensation in the Utah National
Guard or Armed Forces Reserves, as the case may be. Orders and compensation information must be
provided no less than fourteen (14) calendar days prior to reporting for active duty. Leave with
compensation will not be granted for activities undertaken by the employee with the employee’s
military service unless ordered to do so. Discretionary training events, public ceremonial events,
preparation and post service activities, and the like shall not be granted compensation under this policy.
In determining the difference in salary for active duty, the City shall not take into consideration the
monetary value of, medical benefits, retirement benefits, or other non-monetary benefits offered by
Riverton City or the military service of which the employee is a member.
