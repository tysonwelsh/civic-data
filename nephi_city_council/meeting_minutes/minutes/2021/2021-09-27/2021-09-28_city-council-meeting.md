                                          Nephi City
                                    Work Session Minutes
                                    September 28, 2021
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                 Nephi City Council Chambers



1. Fed Philpot from Lewis Young Robertson & Burningham presented via zoom a draft analysis
   of the parks and recreation impact fees. Mr. Philpot fielded questions from the council
   about the analysis. The council directed Mr. Philpot to remove the gun range property out
   of the analysis. The council set a public hearing date for October 19, 2021, to accept public
   comment on the parks and recreation impact fee analysis.

2. Mr. Atkinson presented to the council information about the Nephi City’s water rights and
   system. He showed how much water is used by agriculture land versus municipal water. He
   described the increase in capacity that the culinary water project provided.

3. Power Superintendent Rust Finlinson reviewed the finalized draft of the city’s electrical
   master plan. He talked about future needs with current projects and potential new and
   expanding industrial customers.

4. Mr. Finlinson discussed a Mutual Aid Agreement draft with UAMPs , RMP, UMPA, and
   URECA that all parties are interested in signing that would provide each agency assistance
   during emergencies.

5. Mr. Atkinson requested feedback from the council on the Well-Being Survey questions. Mr.
   Atkinson will be part of a panel discussion about the survey at the upcoming Utah League of
   Cities and Towns conference.

6. Mr. Atkinson reported that there are American Rescue Plan funds available for the airport.
   The council directed him to pursue getting the funds available and possibly using the funds
   to update the credit card machines at the fueling station and possibly for bringing
   broadband service to the airport.

7. Mr. Atkinson reported that the airport apron project was completed.

8. Mayor Nielson reported on a meeting with the BLM and DWR and state and federal
   legislative representative about the land swap for the back nine holes at the golf course. He
   stated that it may take nine months to a year for the swap to be complete.
9. Councilor Ostler reported that the Lion’s Club will be sponsoring a Meet the Candidates
   Night on Thursday, October 7th at the Senior Citizen Center at 7:00 p.m.

10. Mayor Nielson called to order a regular meeting and Councilor Worwood moved to convene
    into executive session to discuss the character and competence of an individual and the
    acquisition and disposition of real property. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The
    motion carried on a unanimous vote.

11. Work Session adjourned at 9:00 p.m.
