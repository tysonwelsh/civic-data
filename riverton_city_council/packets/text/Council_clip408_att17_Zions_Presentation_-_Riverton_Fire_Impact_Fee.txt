                                                        WHAT IS AN(Utah
                                       BASIC DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS  IMPACT    FEE?
                                                                        Code 11‐36a)



                • One‐time fee charged to new development to offset the
                  capital costs associated with new development

                • Can only be collected for the costs of system improvements
                   • Fire stations and training facilities
                   • Fire vehicles costing > $500,000

                • Must have an Impact Fee Facilities Plan (IFFP) and an Impact
                  Fee Analysis (IFA)
