Chair Cragun asked if the same rules would apply to both residential and industrial areas. Ms. Tubbs

permitted, one container would be allowed per 8,000 square feet of lot area, provided that it complies
with all applicable standards.
Not including shared driveways or private rights-of-way, residential lots that are less than 20,000
square feet in size may have one container up to 40 feet in length. Lots between 20,000 square feet
and one acre in size would be allowed one container per 5,000 square feet of lot area. One acre or
larger lots may have one container per 5,000 square feet, not to exceed six total storage containers.

Commercial, Regional Commercial, Heavy Industrial, and Light Industrial, and prohibited in General

Commercial, Neighborhood Commercial, Sand and Gravel, and Civic Institutional, which typically

abut residential zones. Staff wanted to buffer those areas from the anticipated noise impacts of sirens.

e Limited ability to identify and track STRs.

e Define STRs and permitted locations.
e Establish basic requirements.

allow short-term rentals, in accordance with BCC 3.100, as a permitted use.
