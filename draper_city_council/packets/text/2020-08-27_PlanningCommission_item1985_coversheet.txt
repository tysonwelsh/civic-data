Re: Quinn Court Office – Amended Site Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of an Amended Site Plan for approximately 1.93 acres located on the west side of 700 East, at approximately 11980 South 700 East. The property is currently zoned CN (Neighborhood Commercial) . The applicant is requesting that an Amended Site Plan be approved to allow for a deviation to the buffer wall standards. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. The proposed development plans meet the requirements and provisions of the DCMC Section 9-5-060(E).
2. The proposed deviation complies with the standards of DCMC Section  9-23-110(C).
For denial:
1. The proposed development plans do not meet the requirements and provisions of the DCMC Section 9-5-060(E).
2. The proposed deviation does not comply with the standards of DCMC Section  9-23-110(C).  ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 8/20/2020 Cover Memo
