Background

This is a public hearing to amend the River Haven Estates Phase 2 subdivision by changing the
property line between Lots 45 and 46. River Haven Estates Phase Subdivision was recorded in
January 1984. The subdivision, and all surrounding subdivisions are in the R-4 Zone (Single-
family, detached with 10,000-sf lots).

The Tolleys and Fenns share a sideyard property line that is fenced. Sometime in the past the
fence was misplaced from the property line. This appears to be a common event within this
subdivision. The applicants desire to amend their two common boundaries to a line agreed
upon.

The proposed adjustment involves lots in a subdivision, therefore a Plat Amendment is required.
The city evaluates proposed plat amendments to determine:
        1- No new lots are created.
        2- The resulting lots meet the size and dimension requirements of the zone.
        3- A proposed property line does not create an illegal setback for existing structures.
        4- The resulting lots meet all other city ordinances.
