BACKGROUND
On April 7, 2005 the Planning Commission approved the Architectural Design, Colors and
Materials for this particular Wal-Mart. As part of that approval, there were approved elevations
and a list of approved signs. This request is to modify the signs that currently exist on this
building as well as modify the color of the EIFS (Stucco). Condition number 4 attached to the
approved elevations required a specific EIFS color for this building. It says:

4. That the main EIFS colors be Kiln Beige and Divine White.

DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Heather Maxwell, for BRR architecture is proposing a sign theme amendment for
the Walmart located at 9151 S. Quarry Bend (See Exhibit #1 – Sign Theme Renderings). Walmart
is creating a new gray toned color scheme. This color scheme includes the following colors dark
gray, light gray, medium gray, and black gray (See Exhibit #2- Color Legend) The proposed color
changes will provide the background for several of the proposed signs as well as for the awnings.

Sign #1: Single-Tenant Primary Wall Sign Front Elevation Store Logo
The proposed Walmart logo sign is to be a single tenant illuminated channel letter wall sign. It
contains two parts the yellow Walmart spark (8’8” x 7’9”) and the lettering underneath that will be
Walmart Sign Theme: SIGN-11-15-26584                                     Page 2


done in white (1’7” x 9’3”). The letters will be illuminated with white L.E.D. while the spark will
be illuminated with yellow L.E.D lighting. There are no proposed changes to the wall background
utilized for this sign.

Sign #2: Single Tenant Primary Wall Sign Front Elevation : Pickup
The applicant is proposing to change the pick-up area from the north side of the building to the
south side of the building requiring a change in signage. The proposed pickup sign is a white
(12’1” x 2’5”) illuminated channel letter sign with an arrow pointing to the south side of the
building. In addition to this signage the background of the sign will be painted a medium gray.
Section 21-26-5(b) of the Sandy City Land Development Code states that sign copy mounted or
painted on a background panel or area distinctly textured or constructed as a background for the
sign copy shall be measured as that area contained within the outside dimensions of the
background panel or surface. The background area of the sign is approximately 311 square feet.

Sign #3: Single Tenant Primary Wall Sign Front Elevation: Home and Pharmacy
The applicant is proposing to change the Home and Pharmacy wall sign to a (22’4” x 2’7”) white
non-illuminated wall sign with a dark gray background. The background is (25’6” x 7’1”) or
181.76 square feet and is counted towards the square footage of the sign per section 21-26-5(b) of
the Sandy City Land Development Code.

Sign #4: Single Tenant Primary Wall Sign Front Elevation: Market
The applicant is proposing to change the Market wall sign to a (8’8” x 2) wall sign on a dark gray
(25’6” x 7’1”) 181.76 square feet background. The background of the sign is counted towards the
overall the square footage of the sign per section 21-26-5(b) of the Sandy City Land Development
Code. The proposed sign is a non-illuminated white channel letter sign.

Sign #5: Single Tenant Primary Wall Sign Front Elevation: Outdoor
The applicant is proposing to change the Market wall sign to a (8’8” x 2) wall sign on a dark gray
(25’6” x 7’1”) 181.76 square feet background. The background of the sign is counted towards the
overall the square footage of the sign per section 21-26-5(b) of the Sandy City Land Development
Code. The proposed sign is a non-illuminated white channel letter sign.

Sign #6: Single Tenant Primary Wall Sign Front Elevation: Vision
The applicant is proposing to add a Vision (3’5” x 1’) wall sign with a dark gray (17’ x 3’7”)
background. The sign is white and not proposed to be illuminated.

Sign #7: Single Tenant Primary Wall Sign Front Elevation: Auto Care
The applicant is proposing to replace the Auto Care sign with a white (10’8” x 1’5”) non
illuminated channel letter wall sign. In addition, the applicant is proposing to paint the background
wall a medium gray (41’ x 8’9”).

Awnings
In addition to the seven signs proposed on the front of the building the applicant is proposing to
change the color of the two current metal awnings. The current awnings are green and the
proposed awnings are constructed of a dark gray heat resistant metal.

Sign #8: Single Tenant Secondary Wall Sign Side Elevation: Auto Care

property with a (12’3” x 2’) white non-illuminated sign on a (23’7 x 11’5”) medium gray

Sign#9,10,11,12,13 Single Tenant Secondary Wall Signs Side Elevations: Numerals
The auto care center has five bays for cars. The applicant is proposing to place five (2’6” x 1’6”)

Sign #14,15,16 Single Tenant Secondary Wall Sign Side Elevation: Pharmacy Drive Thru
The applicant is proposing three signs at the pharmacy drive thru. Two of the signs are white non-
illuminated channel letter directional signs that area approximately (3) square feet for the entrance
sign and two (2) square feet for the exit sign. The pharmacy sign itself is (26’ x 1’6”) white non-
illuminated channel letter sign. The background the signs are placed on is proposed to be a light
gray (43’ x 8’8”) wall.

NOTICE
The Wal-Mart shopping center is located in Community #7, Quarry Bend. Due to the nature of the
request and the limited impact to surrounding properties, a neighborhood meeting was not
required. However, notices were sent to surrounding property owners within 500 feet of the
subject property. At the time of writing this staff report, no comments have been received by staff.

ANALYSIS
Section 21-26-10 of the Sandy City Land Development Code states that developments of seven or
more acres and having more than 300 linear feet of frontage may vary from the regulations set
forth in the Sign Ordinance with Planning Commission approval under the following conditions:

1.     The proposed sign exceptions are not in conflict with the purpose and intent of this chapter,
2.     The proposed signs are in architectural harmony with the development,
3.     The proposed signs appropriately utilize those elements listed in the design
       criteria of this chapter, as follows:

Sign Design. Each sign submitted for approval shall incorporate the following elements:

1.     Architectural compatibility
2.     Size, scale, proportion (balance)
3.     Illumination
4.     Color and style
5.     Location
6.     Landscaping

This particular site has over 26 acres and approximately 700 feet of street frontage along Quarry
Bend Dr. The proposed sign theme incorporates the sign design elements as follows:

Architecturally, the proposed signs seek to integrate into the updated branding for the overall look
of the Walmart site. Illumination is not excessive. The applicant is proposing internally
illuminated display on two of the sixteen proposed signs.

STAFF CONCERNS
Staff has no concerns. We feel that the proposed alterations are still in keeping with the
architectural theme for this master planned development.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the modified sign theme for 9151 S.
Quarry Bend Drive to allow the installation of the proposed signs as shown in the applicant’s
attached plans, as well as to allow the proposed color theme changes to shades of gray as shown in
the attached color legend, subject to the following conditions:

Findings

1. Staff finds that the proposed sign theme meets the intent of Section 21-26-10 of the Sandy City
Development Code regarding approval by the Planning Commission for developments of seven or
more acres and having more than 300 linear feet of frontage.
2. The proposed sign design meets the sign design elements regarding architectural compatibility,
size, scale, proportion, illumination, color and style, and location as approved by the Planning
Commission.

Conditions

If the Planning Commission does approve the sign theme amendment, staff recommends the
following conditions:

1. That installed signage match the location and design of the submitted renderings.
2. A sign permit be obtained from the Community Development Department for the proposed
signs, with any future amendments to be reviewed by the Planning Commission.
