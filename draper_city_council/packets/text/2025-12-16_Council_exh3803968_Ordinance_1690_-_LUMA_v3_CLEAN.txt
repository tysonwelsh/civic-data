                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1690

        AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL LAND USE MAP OF DRAPER CITY
        FOR APPROXIMATELY 1.44 ACRES LOCATED AT 231 EAST 13800 SOUTH FROM
        OFFICE SERVICE TO REGIONAL COMMERCIAL LAND USE DESIGNATION,
        OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE OPENSHAW DRAPER 138 LAND USE MAP
        AMENDMENT.

      WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a General Plan and Land
Use Map to guide future growth and development within the City; and

       WHEREAS, from time to time it is necessary to review and amend the Land Use Map
to keep pace with development within the City and to ensure the provision of a variety of
economic uses; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that redesignation of the subject 1.44-acre parcel to
Regional Commercial is consistent with the General Plan only if the owner dedicates
necessary additional right-of-way along 13800 South and/or Bangerter Highway to allow the
City to widen and improve those roadways in conjunction with the Bangerter Crossroads
project; and

       WHEREAS, the applicant seeks to avoid the need for eminent domain proceedings
and the City desires to secure voluntary dedications through a development agreement; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed Land Use Map change set forth herein has been reviewed
by the Planning Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have
been held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed
revisions to the Land Use Map; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Land Use Map of Draper
City, and the City Council has found the proposed land use change, subject to the
contingency and effectiveness provisions herein, to be in the best interest of the health,
safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper City and consistent with the City’s General Plan.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

Section 1.     Findings and Contingent Approval. The City Council finds that the
amendment of the official Land Use Map as described in Section 2 below is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper City and is consistent
with the City’s General Plan, provided that appropriate infrastructure improvements, traffic
mitigation measures, voluntary dedication of all rights-of-way determined by the City

      Section 3.     Contingent Effectiveness; Expiration if Conditions Not Met. This
ordinance shall not take effect unless and until a development agreement between Draper
City and the applicant that adequately addresses traffic mitigation, roadway improvements,
voluntary dedication of all rights-of-way determined by the City Engineer to be necessary for
the widening and improvement of 13800 South and Bangerter Highway, and other
conditions deemed necessary by the City Council has been fully executed by all parties and
recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder. The City Recorder is hereby instructed not to
publish this ordinance until the development agreement has been fully executed and
recorded. Publication shall occur within ten (10) calendar days after recordation.

       All activities undertaken by the applicant prior to the effective date of this ordinance

becomes effective. If the development agreement has not been executed by all parties and

      Section 4.      Requirement of Development Agreement.          This land use map
amendment is granted in express reliance upon the applicant’s commitment to enter into a
development agreement with Draper City that includes, at a minimum, the voluntary
dedication of all rights-of-way determined by the City Engineer to be necessary for the
widening and improvement of 13800 South and Bangerter Highway, together with any
additional mitigation or conditions deemed appropriate. The applicant is expected to submit
a complete development agreement application no later than December 29, 2025, to allow
processing and City Council consideration on or before May 7, 2026.

       Section 5.    Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.
