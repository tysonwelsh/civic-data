Pre-Council, 4:00 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present.
   Councilor Southwick gave the invocation.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Towing Ordinance History and Potential Revision Discussion
    Councilor Koivisto stated that there were some concerns from residents that were brought to
    her attention regarding the possible need for a code revision. In an HOA there must be a
    designated person that’s contacted before the vehicle can be towed which creates an issue when
    that person is difficult to reach because of odd hours. Billy Loveridge, Lehi Police Department,
    explained the history of the Ordinance and discussed possible options. It was determined that
    Officer Loveridge would reach out to the HOA that had concerns and work with them on
    solutions that wouldn’t require a change to the City Code.

3. Agenda Questions
   None.

4. Administrative Report
   Mayor Johnson asked about the tour from last week to the Saratoga Springs North Marina. The
   Council expressed interest in the project and agreed that they would like to move forward with
   a cost analysis.

5. Mayor and Council Reports
   Mayor Johnson stated that they will be working with MAG (Mountainland Association of
   Governments) and Utah County on the CARES Act distribution of grant money to local
   businesses and nonprofit organizations.

6. Consideration of Concept plan approval for Pine Springs, a 37-unit residential
   development located at 225 South 600 East.
   Dan Ford was representing the applicant. There was discussion about the applicant providing
   more parking within the development. Mr. Struthers noted that although the public street
   parking does not count toward the required amount of parking stalls within the development,
   it can still be considered.

    Motion:     Councilor Koivisto moved to approve the Concept plan for Pine Springs, a 37-unit
                residential development located at 225 South 600 East; subject to the completion
                of all Development Review Committee and Planning Commission comments; with
                the recommendation that the applicant include additional parking within the
                development. Councilor Southwick seconded the motion.

    Councilor Hancock suggested adding to the motion that they would like to see how many
    parking stalls would be available on the public street when the item comes back for Preliminary
    approval.

    Amended Motion: Councilor Koivisto amended her motion to include Councilor Hancock’s
