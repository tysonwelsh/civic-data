                                       TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE NO. 24- 05


   AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING
      TEXT AMENDMENT TO SEVERAL SECTIONS OF THE TAYLORSVILLE
 MUNICIPAL CODE, TO ALIGN CITY STANDARDS WITH UTAH SENATE BILL 13
    RELATED TO CHARTER SCHOOLS, HOME-BASED MICROSCHOOLS, AND
      MICRO-EDUCATION ENTITIES AND CORRESPONDING DEFINITIONS.


          WHEREAS, the Taylorsville   City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on
August 21, 2024, to consider, among other things, approving a zoning text amendment to several
sections of the Taylorsville Municipal Code, to align city standards with Utah Senate Bill 13
related    to   charter   schools,   home-based   microschools,   and   micro- education   entities   and

corresponding definitions.; and

          WHEREAS, pursuant to Taylorsville Municipal Code Section 13. 35. 050, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published on the city' s official website
on June 27, 2024; and


          WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the Utah Public Notice Website on June 27, 2024; and


        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on July 9, 2024, after which the Planning Commission continued the
item to August 13, 2024 and voted 7- 0 to forward a positive recommend to the Council regarding
the proposed text amendment; and



       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on August
21, 2024; and


          WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed text amendment to the Taylorsville Municipal Code, related to charter schools, home-
based microschools, and micro-education entities and corresponding definitions, as articulated in
Exhibit" A."


          This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 24- 05, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited, and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.

13   13. 11. 040: HOME OCCUPATIONS:

14   A.   Purpose:
15        1.   Provide an opportunity for home occupations as an accessory use when they are compatible with
16             the neighborhoods in which they are located.
17        2.   Provide an opportunity for a home occupation to engage in the business of childcare and other
18             group child activities and encourage this type of home occupation to draw clients/ customers from
19             their immediate neighborhood.
20        3.   Guide business activities which are not compatible with neighborhoods to appropriate
21             nonresidential zoning districts.
22        4.   Safeguard peace, quiet, and domestic tranquility within all residential neighborhoods. Protect
23             residents from the adverse effects of business uses being conducted in residential areas from
24             noise, nuisance, traffic, fire hazards, and other possible business uses that create significant
25             impacts on a neighborhood.
26        5.   Provide a means to enforce and regulate the businesses that are licensable through the authority of
27
               the business license regulations of the applicable ordinances, and if necessary, terminate home
28
               occupations if violations of the ordinances regulating home occupations occur.
29   B.   Review Procedure.
30        1.   The Director is the approval authority for a home occupation; however, if the Director determines
31
               the application is unusually complex, requires greater scrutiny or public involvement, or raises
32
               potentially unique or serious impacts on the City or the surrounding development and/ or
33
               neighborhood( s), the Director may refer the application to the Planning Commission for decision
34             in accordance with the nonadministrative conditional use procedure established in Chapter 13. 33.
35        2.
               If the Director refers the application to the Planning Commission. the Director shall notify the
36
               applicant in writing and include an explanation of why the application has been referred to the
37             Planning Commission.
38        3.
               If the Director refers the application to the Planning Commission, a notice for a public hearing
39             shall be provided in accordance with Section 13. 35. 090.
40   C.
          General Standards. All Home occupations shall operate in accordance with the following
41        requirements:

42        1.   Licensing and Number.
43             a.
                    Home occupations shall only operate after receiving a home occupation business license from
44                  the City, in accordance with Title 5: Business Licenses and Regulations.
45             b.   No more than two home occupations shall be allowed per dwelling unit.
46             c.
                    No more than one home- based microschool, residential child day care, residential preschool,
47
                    high- impact residential instruction, or home occupation including a business vehicle or
48                  trailer shall be allowed per lot or parcel.
49             d.   Home occupations shall remain compliant with all applicable statutes, rules and regulations.
50             e.   Home occupations shall not be licensed, approved, or conducted without the written consent
51
                    of the property owner.
52             f.   A home occupation license granted by the City shall not be assignable or transferable to
53                  another person or another location.
54
               g.   Home occupations are subject to review upon complaint. If a complaint is received by the
55                  City, the home occupation will be reviewed for compliance with applicable ordinances and
56                  conditions of approval.

57        2.
               Location and Proximity. Home- based microschools, Rresidential child day cares, residential
58
               preschools, and high- impact residential instruction home occupations are subject to the following
59             standards:

60             a.   Shall only be allowed in an attached or detached single- family dwelling that:
61                         1)   Has a privately fenced rear yard area for the sole and exclusive use of the
62                            residents of the lot or parcel on which the home occupation is situated; and
63                         2) Fronts on a public or private street with a minimum pavement width of 25 feet
64                              that has curb, gutter, and sidewalk; and
65                         3) The minimum 25- foot pavement width and curb, gutter, and sidewalk extends
66                              from the home occupation lot or parcel to the nearest through street with those
67                              same improvements.

68         b.   Shall not be permitted on a flag lot.
69         c.   Shall not be permitted on a lot or parcel with an accessory dwelling unit( ADU).
70         d.   Shall not be located within 300 feet of another actively licensed:
71               1)   Home- based microschool;
72              0-)(2)          Residential child day care;
73               43)            Residential preschool; or
74                    4)        High- impact residential instruction.

75              This distance shall be measured in a straight line from the nearest property line. This standard
76              does not apply if the properties are bisected by a freeway or principal arterial street, as
77              identified in the Taylorsville General Plan.
78    3.   Operational.

79         a. Home occupation activity shall be clearly incidental to the residential dwelling. No more than
80            50 percent of the total floor area of any dwelling unit shall be used for the home occupation.
81         b. The outside appearance and architecture of the dwelling shall not be modified to
82              accommodate or promote a home occupation.
83         c.   Home occupations shall be conducted entirely within the dwelling. Garages and accessory
84              buildings shall not be used for home occupations except for the parking of approved business
85              vehicles or trailers.

 86        d.   No storage of merchandise, equipment, supplies, or materials shall occur outside of the home
 87             and shall not be visible from outside of the dwelling. Explosive or combustible materials shall
 88             not be stored or used in association with a home occupation.
 89        e.   Delivery of commodities used in connection with the home occupation shall be provided by
 90             parcel or letter carrier service only.
 91        f. Activities associated with the home occupation shall not produce traffic, light, glare, noise,
 92             fumes, odor, or vibration that will diminish the residential character of the neighborhood.
 93        g.   Unless otherwise authorized by the decision- making authority, home occupations shall not be
 94             conducted after 08: 00 P.M. or before 6: 00 A.M.
 95        h.   Employees that do not reside on- site are prohibited, except for residential child day cares
 96             which may have one nonresident employee. Off-site employees may visit the residence in
 97             accordance with §       13. 11. 040( C)( 4): On- Site Visits.
 98        i.   Property address numbers a minimum of four inches in height that are a contrasting color
 99             from the primary dwelling shall be maintained on the dwelling unit.
100   4.   Vehicles and Trailers.

101        a.
                Only one vehicle may be used in association with a home occupation.
102        b.   Vehicles and trailers used in association with a home occupation must be parked in an
103             approved off-street parking location and shall not encroach or overhang on any sidewalk or
104             pedestrian way.
105        c.   The number of off-street parking spaces must be sufficient for both the residents of the home
106             and the vehicle and/ or trailer used      in association with a home occupation.
107         d.   The size of a vehicle used in association with a home occupation shall not exceed a 10, 000-
1108             pound gross vehicle weight rating and have no more than two axles.
 109        e.   Tow trucks, tanker trucks, box vans, delivery vans, and similar vehicles are prohibited and
 110             shall not be parked on- site. Such vehicles may be located off-site in an approved storage
 111             location.
 112        f.   Only one trailer with no more than two axles may be used in association with a home
 113             occupation.

 114
            g.   The maximum body length of an enclosed trailer is 20 feet. The maximum body length of an
 115             open trailer is 16 feet. For purposes of this standard, trailer length does not include the trailer
 116             tongue.

 117        h.   Auto transport trailers designed to transport more than one vehicle or that exceed the body
 118             length requirements are prohibited and shall not be parked on- site. Such trailers may be
 119             located off-site in an approved storage location.
 120   5.   Modified Vehicle and Trailer Standards
 121        a.
                 Vehicles and trailers used in association with a home occupation that do not satisfy the
 122             standards in paragraph( 4) above may be approved when the following criteria are satisfied:
 123              1)   The home occupation associated with the vehicle( s) and/ or trailer(s) receives an
 124                   administrative conditional use permit approval;
 125             2) The lot or parcel with the home occupation is in an R- 1- 20 or R- 1- 40 zoning district and
 126                   is one acre or larger in area;
 127             3) The primary means of ingress and egress to the lot or parcel with the home occupation is
 128                   via a collector or arterial street, as identified in the General Plan;
 129             4) All vehicles and trailers related to the home occupation are parked behind the primary
 130                dwelling and screened from public view;
 131             5) The parking and maneuvering area for vehicles and trailers is limited to 35 percent of the
 132                   rear yard area;

 133             6) The designated parking and maneuvering area is located a minimum of 50 feet from the
 134                nearest dwelling;
 135             7) No more than five vehicles and 10 trailers may be parked or stored within the approved
 136                   on- site parking and maneuvering area at any given time;
 137             8) The vehicles and trailers related to the home occupation must be registered to the home
 138                   occupation business or the current resident; and
 139             9) The outdoor storage of equipment, materials, or other items related to the home
 140                   occupation is prohibited;

 141        b.   Gravel may be used for parking and maneuvering areas for vehicles and trailers associated
 142             with a home occupation when:
 143              1)   The criteria in paragraph( 5)( a) have been satisfied;
 144             2) The parking and maneuvering area has been designed and constructed to support the
 145
                       weight of the vehicles and trailers using the area;
 146             3) The gravel used is clean, a minimum of two inches deep, compacted, free of dust, grass,
 147                   and weeds, and contained with durable borders;
 148             4) A minimum 30- foot paved track-out pad is provided between the public right-of-way and
 149
                    the gravel surface. Gravel surfacing is not permitted within 30 feet of the public right-of-
 150                   way; and
 151
                  5) Gravel parking and maneuvering areas are maintained to remain compliant with these
 152                   standards.

                       Except for home- based microschools, residential child day care, residential preschools, and

 164
                       residential instruction, drop- off and pick-up shall not occur in the public right- of-way.

 180    F.   Residential Preschools. Residential preschools may be offered as a home occupation in accordance
 181
             with the standards in this Section 13. 11. 040, and the following:
 182         1.  A residential preschool is a home occupation providing care and instruction, whether or not for
 183
                 compensation, of 12 or fewer children including the caregiver' s own children under the age of six
 184
                 and not in full day school, prior to entrance into kindergarten.
 185         2.   A maximum of 12 children per session is allowed. A single session shall not exceed four
 186              consecutive hours per day and individuals shall not attend more than one session per day.
 187         3.   Instructor licensing and curriculum requirements shall comply with the requirements of the State
 188              of Utah, as applicable.

 189    I.   Residential Instruction. Residential instruction may be offered as a home occupation in accordance

                  include residential child day care or residential preschools as defined in this Title.
