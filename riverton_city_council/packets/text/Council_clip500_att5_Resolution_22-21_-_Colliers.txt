                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 22-21

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
WITH COLLIERS INTERNATIONAL TO PROVIDE COMMERCIAL REAL-ESTATE
                   BROKER SERVICES, AS NEEDED

      WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any contract that exceeds
$30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the fee paid to a real-estate broker for purchasing and/or selling property as
Council directs may exceed $30,000; and

        WHEREAS, the City may need a commercial real-estate broker to represent the City to acquire
and/or sell property.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Colliers
            International to provide Commercial Real-Estate Broker Services, as needed.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
March 2022 by the following vote:
