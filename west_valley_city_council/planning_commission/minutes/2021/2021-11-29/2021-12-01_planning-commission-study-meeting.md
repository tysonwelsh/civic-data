SUBDIVISON APPLICATIONS
SA-6-2021   Petition by BRETT CARTER requesting preliminary approval for the
            Carmen’s Place Subdivision. This application will amend lot 8 of the Belmont
            Downs Subdivision and will create 1 new building lot. The subdivision is
            located at 6256 West 2900 South, consists of 2 lots on 2.15 acres and is zoned
            A-1 (Agricultural, minimum lot size one acre). (Staff – Steve Lehman at
            801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve explained, in 1996, part of the subject location was designated as a
                 wetland by the Army Core of Engineers. Over the years, the definition and
                 designation of wetland have changed, and Mr. Carter received approval from
                 the Core that the subject property is not considered a wetland. Therefore,
                 building a home in this location would not be a problem. In speaking with
                 engineering, Steve stated that there should not be any issues. Steve received
                 an anonymous call from an adjacent property owner. This owner said that
                 building a home in the proposed location will cause his house to flood. Steve
                 spoke with engineering and the applicant about this phone call and expressed
                 that postponing this application might be the best option. This will give them
                 time to look at the property and implement the proper flood precautions.

                 Commissioner Winters asked if there is a grade to the subject property that
                 would increase the risk of flooding, and Steve said the terrain is very flat.


SA-7-2021        Petition by RIK DUNHAM requesting preliminary approval for the CR
                 England Lot 4 Amended Subdivision. The subdivision plat will amend lot 4 to
                 create a new lot for the Expansion of an existing Dominion Energy
                 facility. The subdivision is located at 4527 West 2100 South, consists of 2
                 lots on 9.6 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at
                 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Lehman explained that the subject property is in the northeast corner of
                 lot 4 in the CR England Subdivision. He said that a Dominion Substation is
                 located on that corner, and they would like to expand the substation. Steve
                 noted that this is a platted subdivision, so the applicant is proposing an
                 amendment to the subdivision for the expansion.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-21-2021   Petition by WESTERN STORAGE requesting a conditional use for outside
            storage. The property is located at 6551 W. 2100 S. on 6.0 acres and is zoned
            M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497or
            jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Jody Knapp explained that a towing company is currently located to the south
                 of the subject property with access off of the Frontage Road. She said the
                 applicant is looking to expand to the north adding approximately six acres for
                 outside storage. Jody said that the definition of outside storge is very broad
                 because the type of surfacing and fencing is determined by what will be stored
                 there. She noted that the applicant has submitted a letter of intent that lists
                 many possibilities of what will be stored on the subject property because there
                 isn’t a tenant at this time. Jody explained that this could pose a problem for
                 the fire department and public works with plan development. The decision
                 was to move forward with the proposed plan knowing that changes may need
                 to be made once reviewed by the fire department and public works.

                 Jody stated that the existing chain link fence will be moved out of the setback
                 and vinyl slats will be added. She also mentioned that the applicant could be
                 required to add a restroom.

                 Jody stated that in two to four years the City will be rebuilding the Frontage
                 Road. Therefore, public works is not requiring any improvements or formal
                 landscaping to that frontage of the subject property along that road at this
                 time. However, there will be a delay agreement addressing those
                requirements. The landscaping and roadway improvements for 2100 South
                will be required at this time along with streetlights. Public works will pursue a
                delay agreement for curb, gutter and sidewalk along that frontage.

                Jody said an important part of this application is the Planning Commission
                approving this proposal to address screening and surfacing with such a broad
                definition of what will be stored outside.

MISCELLANEOUS APPLICATIONS
M-2-2021   Petition by NEIL NIXON requesting an exception to the requirements of 7-
           10-103 due to landscaping impacts from right-of-way widening at 3550/3556
           South 5600 West. (Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
           brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:     Brock Anderson explained that the subsequent three applications are
                pertaining to properties impacted by the widening of 3500 South and 5600
                West. He said that the code states an exception to the City’s high image
                arterial landscaping standards can be requested if one can't meet these
                requirements due to the location of existing buildings or other site
                improvements on the property. Brock noted that this proposal incorporates
                three commercial buildings along a high-image arterial street. They are
                requesting an exception to the 20-foot landscape requirement. Brock said that
                UDOT had completed widening the road, and the subject property was left
                with a narrow frontage, down to four feet in some spots.

                Brock presented the landscape plan, submitted by the applicant, to the
                Planning Commission. He noted one concern of staff: the majority of the live
                plant material is Red Leaf Japanese Barberry. This shrub has many thorns, and
                it is proposed to be located along a walkway. Brock noted the thorns would
                keep any trash or debris stuck in the shrub and would need to be removed
                often. Unfortunately, Brock said, the applicant has installed landscaping
                before receiving approval, and what was planted does not match what is on
                the plan. Brock suggested that the Commission add requirements to the
                landscaping, such as three or more varieties of plants, as part of this
                exception.

                Chair Meaders asked Brock if the Commission could require a specific size of
                plants or shrubs. Brock said that is a possibility, especially in areas that
                accommodate taller plants.

                Commissioner Winters agrees with the staff's concerns. He said we usually
                just look to see that they meet the fifty percent requirement, right? Brock said
                that is correct, but where this is a unique circumstance, the Commission could
                add more specifications to the type of landscaping.

                Commissioner Fuller asked if this site was irrigated, and Brock said it was.
              Commissioner Fuller said they could probably add plants into that existing
              irrigation system. Brock said he will add some specifications to his staff report
              for the public hearing.

M-3-2021      Petition by ROGER KEHL requesting an exception to the requirements of 7-
              10-103 due to landscaping impacts from right-of-way widening at 5550 W
              3500 South. (Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
              brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that a Subway is located on the subject property.
              He said the drive-thru now sits where the twenty-foot landscape requirement
              would be. They are asking for an exception of seven feet next to the drive-
              thru. Brock stated they would meet the fifty percent lawn and 50% live plant
              material requirement. Brock pointed out that the applicant is not asking for an
              exception west of the drive approach. Staff suggested removing one parking
              spot to add the required amount of landscaping where they have four parking
              spaces over what is needed.

M-4-2021      Petition by NIEL HARMON requesting an exception to the requirements of 7-
              10-103 due to landscaping impacts from right-of-way widening at 5670 W
              3500 South. (Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
              brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson said that when UDOT widened 3500 South, they added an
              unusual V-shaped sidewalk in front of the subject property. He explained that
              the east end of the subject property is shy two feet of meeting the landscaping
              requirement, and staff feels that leaving this as is would be a better option
              than removing a parking stall and redoing the landscaping to gain the two feet.
              Brock stated that there are currently twenty-three parking stalls at the subject
              location, and the business is required to have twelve stalls. He said that the
              applicant is requesting an exception to the landscaping on the west side and
              proposing to redo the landscaping to the west. Brock explained that the green
              space to the west belongs to a different parcel. Therefore, the staff
              recommends removing a parking spot and adding the required twenty feet of
              landscaping.

              Chair Meaders asked Brock if he could explain the reason for the V-shaped
              sidewalk. Brock said that UDOT added it. However, he isn’t sure why they
              configured the sidewalk in that manner.

              Commissioner Winters said he isn’t concerned about meeting the twenty feet
              landscape requirement next to the parking area. He feels the landscaping
              would not blend in well with the rest of the street.

              Commissioner McEwen asked if there was a reason that UDOT didn’t finish
              the landscaping. Brock said the property owners were given money to replace
                 it based on the removed landscaping.

                 Commissioner Woodruff feels that the landscaping would enhance the look of
                 the project. Commissioner Fuller said the only way to make this look right
                 would be to change the location of the curb, gutter, and sidewalk they just put
                 in. Commissioner Lovato feels it is challenging to know the best fit given the
                 location of that sidewalk.

                 Commissioner Winters said another concern is that the property owners were
                 paid to replace the landscaping; however, they probably did not receive any
                 money to tear out concrete to add additional landscaping. So, that would be
                 brand new money out of their pocket.




There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:24 p.m.
