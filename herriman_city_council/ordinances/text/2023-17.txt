ORDINANCE NO. 2023-17

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH,
ENACTING HERRIMAN CITY CODE § 7-14 (DEDICATION OF PUBLIC PROPERTY)

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-9a-102 grants the Hertiman City (the “City”) authority to enact
ordinances that the Herriman City Council (the “City Council”) considers necessary or appropriate;
and

WHEREAS, the subject text amendment prohibits a person from dedicating public property
unless certain conditions are met and the City has accepted the property; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public meeting and reviewed the subject text
amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the subject text amendment will enhance public
health, safety, and welfare.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

SECTION 1. Text Amendment. The Council hereby enacts City Code § 7-14 as shown in
Exhibit A.

SECTION 2. Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid
or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this
Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

SECTION 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting as required by law.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN,
UTAH, ON THIS 11° DAY OF OCTOBER, 2023.

CHAPTER 7-14 DEDICATION OF PUBLIC PROPERTY

7-14-1 Dedication Regulations

Any dedication of interest in real property to the City for any purpose shall be governed by
the following:

A.

Evidence of Intent to Dedicate: The owner shall evidence its intent to dedicate by
presenting to the City a final plat or other instrument of conveyance for recordation
showing the dedication.

Title Report: The intention to dedicate shall be accompanied by a cutrent-title report
identifying every petson and/or entity whose consent is necessaty to give clear title to
the real property interest to be dedicated.

Acceptance of Dedication: The acceptance of the dedication by the City for public
use shall be evidenced by the approval of the final plat ot other instrument of
conveyance for recording.

. Free and Clear of Liens and Encumbrances: Any teal property interest dedicated to

the City shall be conveyed free and clear of all liens and encumbrances of any kind,
including tax liens. The final plat or other instrument conveying the dedicated real
property interest to the City shall contain a warranty by the owner that the dedicated
teal property interest is free and clear of all liens and encumbrances as requited under
this subsection.

Tax Clearance: At the time of dedication, the owner shall provide the City with tax
clearance showing that all taxes, interest and penalties have been paid, including
greenbelt or rollback tax assessments and accrued property taxes, ot acknowledge in
writing the obligation to assume and pay all taxes, interest and penalties, including
gteenbelt or rollback tax assessments and accrued property taxes by January 1 of year
following dedication.

Withholding of Final Plat Approval: The City may withhold final plat approval until
the owner provides the City with tax clearance requited under subsection E of this
section.

7-14-2 Enforcement

The Community Services Director, or designee, is hereby authorized and empowered to
enforce this chapter.

7-14-3 Penalty

The City has sole discretion in deciding whether to file a civil or ctiminal case for the

violation of this chapter. The City may choose to file both, ot one ot the other. A criminal
violation shall be a Class B misdemeanor and subject to penalty.
