 VARIANCES
 B-4-2022                     MARK GIBBONS, representing the property owner, is requesting a variance from Section 7-
                              6-202 of the West Valley City Code. This section requires that a side yard setback adjacent to

                              build a two-car garage. The remaining distance to the right-of-way will be 10.2 feet. The
