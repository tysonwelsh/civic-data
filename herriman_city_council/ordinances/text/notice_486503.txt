PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on September 12, 2018 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2018-29 amending Title 6, Chapter 1 of the Herriman Code of Ordinances by adding sections regarding Interference with Control of Vehicle Prohibited, Reasonable and Proper Lookout Required, and Negligent Collision. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
