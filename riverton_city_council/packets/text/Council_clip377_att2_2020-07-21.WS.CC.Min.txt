21       b. Redevelopment Agency (RDA) and Community Reinvestment Area (CDR) Update
22
23   Randy Sant, via video conferencing, presented information regarding the Riverton City
24   Redevelopment Agency: Downtown East Community Reinvestment Project Area. He said that
25   on October 15, 2019, a resolution was adopted creating the Riverton Downtown East
26   Community Reinvestment Project Area, which included expansion of the remnant parcels from
27   12600 south to Bangerter Highway to align to the east with properties in a north south formation
28   to Nicholas Lane from Redwood Road.
29
30   Mr. Sant reported that, in working with Advisors Consulting Services for the development of the
31   City’s Downtown Economic Development Plan, their scope of work was to determine where they
32   were, where they wanted to go, and how they were going to get there, what would get in the
33   way of getting there and how do we determine success in regards to a Community
34   Reinvestment Project Area (CRA). He said they had reviewed the demographics and conditions,
35   as well as existing studies that had been done. He said they had reviewed the real estate
36   market in significant detail to determine development opportunities in the CRA. He said they had
37   also worked to determine strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and challenges, which would
38   be part of putting together an Economic Development Tool Box. He said a significant part would
39   be looking at the availability of creating Community Reinvestment Areas within the RDA Study
40   Area. He then asked how many areas should be created; one large project area, or several
41   smaller project areas. He said that a budget needed to be prepared, which included the affected
42   taxing entities that would seem right for their participation. He said that they had tried to identify
43   the areas that they thought would develop first and recommended project areas, i.e. parcels
44   along 12600 South, parcels along Redwood Road, the rodeo grounds, as well as other areas.
45   He said there were 28 steps to creating a CRA, which could take three to seven months. He
46   said that real estate developers have indicated that there were development opportunities within
47   the corridor.
48
49   Mr. Sant then addressed questions from the Mayor and Council Members and explained that
50   the ability exists, under the RDA law, to transfer increment from one project area to another. He
51   then said that to begin with, it would be better to create one CRA; however, everything could be

20   Council. He said that some of the things that the Council was concerned about happening in

22
23   City Attorney Ryan Carter referred to RCC 18.225.080 Accessory Dwelling Units, and explained
24   that they were limited to all single-family zones, provided the lot under consideration for an ADU
25   could meet all technical requirements and applicable building/fire codes. He further explained
26   that an ADU may be created through converting an existing living area within a single-family
27   dwelling, as an addition to an existing single-family dwelling, or within a single-family dwelling
28   created as new construction; converting an existing detached accessory building, as an
29   addition to an existing accessory building; or as a newly constructed accessory building.
30
31   Mr. Carter further explained that the City could only permit an ADU when an owner occupant
32   lived on the property within either the principal or ADU, and the total number of residents
33   residing in an ADU may not exceed the number allowed for a “family” for the lot on which the
34   ADU was located. He then explained that a “family” means one person living alone or a group of
35   individuals who share ties of blood, marriage, or adoption; a group residing together and
36   consisting of parents, children, and other relatives by blood or marriage; a group of individuals
37   residing together who have consented to an arrangement similar to ties of blood or marriage; or
38   a group not to exceed four unrelated persons living together as a single household unit.
