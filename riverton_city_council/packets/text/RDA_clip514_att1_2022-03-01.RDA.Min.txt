45          Downtown Redwood Road Community Reinvestment Project Area
46
47   Chair Staggs said that communication for the Community Reinvestment Area did not go very
48   well and he takes ownership of that. He explained that the Community Reinvestment Area
49   process defines a boundary and the baseline property tax level in this boundary. Development
50   or improvements in this boundary can raise the property tax value and this is called increment.
51   This money can be used by the RDA board for development. This tool has been used
     Page | 2 Minutes – Redevlopment Agency Meeting
     March 1, 2022


 1   throughout the City to entice developers to develop land in Riverton. Riverton City goes out to
 2   all of the taxing entities that are listed on property tax statements and enters into an interlocal
 3   agreement which defines how the increment will be divided, 75% to Riverton City and 25% to
 4   the taxing entities.
 5
 6   Chair Staggs said that one of the strategic plans of the City Council is to develop the “Town
 7   Center,” which includes both the Redwood Road CRA and the 12600 South CRA. Development
 8   on 12600 South has been attempted for decades and it has been hard to get developers in this
 9   area. The Redwood Road CRA includes property across from City Hall and land that fronts
10   1650 West. By creating the CRA, the City is identifying a boundary so that anything that is
11   developed or improved in this area will receive property tax increments. There are potential
12   developers that would like to develop this property, however it is very expensive and this tax
13   increment will help with future costs. Chair Staggs asked Jason Lethbridge and Randy Sant to
14   speak.
15
16   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, said he would give the majority of the time to
17   Randy Sant who is the City’s consultant for the CRA. Mr. Lethbridge explained that the CRA is
18   not a development plan. The CRA plan provides the legal and financial basis that the area will
19   be established. The CRA plan also shows a budget and shows what tax increments may be
20   over time.
21
22   Randy Sant, Consultant, explained that he is an expert in the formation of these kind of areas.
23   He said there are procedural things that need to be placed on the record. There was a 30-day
24   notice sent to the property owners and taxing entities. In this notice, he was required to give the
25   date and time of the public hearing, include a summary of the plan and budget and provide a
26   place for written comments to be sent. This notice was sent to the property address owners on
27   file with Salt Lake County.
28
29   Mr. Lethbridge said that he received several phone calls and questions. Most of the property
30   owners were concerned with imminent domain and whether their homes were going to be taken.
31
32   Mr. Sant stated that the RDA does not have the power of imminent domain. The state and city
33   have the power of imminent domain. He has not received any plans from developers and no tax
34   increments are collected until development is completed and the taxing entities agree. The
35   RDA does not have zoning powers. If a developer wants to rezone the area, they have to go
36   through the planning commission and city council. The base year for the RDA will be January
37   2022. The boundaries were established by the RDA board through a survey area resolution.
38   After public comments are heard, the RDA Board can increase or decrease the area that will be
39   included in the CRA.
40
41   Board Member McDougal asked if the public hearing tonight was only for the Redwood Road
42   CRA Area. Mr. Sant said yes.
43
44   Board Member Wells stated that both of the CRAs the City is forming are in his district and he
45   asked that anyone contact him that has questions or concerns.
