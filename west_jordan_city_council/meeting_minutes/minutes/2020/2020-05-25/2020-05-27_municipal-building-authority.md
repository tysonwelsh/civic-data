CALL TO ORDER
Chair McConnehey called the MBA meeting to order at 7:09 p.m.

PUBLIC HEARING – TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2020-2021
Danyce Steck presented the MBA FY 2021 Tentative Budget, and explained debt service
for the Public Works building. When the bond is paid, the Public Works building will
become property of the City, and the MBA will become dormant.

Chair McConnehey opened a public hearing at 7:11 p.m., and closed the public hearing
seeing that no one electronically indicated a desire to comment.

MOTION:        Board Member Lamb moved to approve Resolution No. 103, adopting
               the Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year 2020-2021 for the Municipal
               Building Authority. Board Member Jacob seconded the motion.

A roll call vote was taken
