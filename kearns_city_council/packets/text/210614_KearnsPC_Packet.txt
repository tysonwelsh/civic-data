             Rules of Conduct for Planning Commission Meetings
PROCEDURE FOR PUBLIC COMMENT

1.   Any person or entity may appear in person or be represented by an authorized agent at any meeting of the
     Commission.

2.   Unless altered by the Chair, the order of the procedure on an application shall be:

     a. The supporting agency staff will introduce the application, including staff’s recommendations and a
        summary of pertinent written comments and reports concerning the application

     b. The applicant will be allowed up to 15 minutes to make their presentation.

     c. The Community Council representative can present their comments as applicable.

     d. Where applicable, persons in favor of, or not opposed to, the application will be invited to speak.

     e. Where applicable, persons opposing the application, in whole or in part will be invited to speak.

     f. Where applicable, the applicant will be allowed 5 minutes to provide concluding statements.

     g. Surrebutals may be allowed at the discretion of the Chair.

CONDUCT FOR APPLICANTS AND THE PUBLIC

1.   Speakers will be called to the podium by the Chair.

2.   Each speaker, before talking, shall give his or her name and address.

3.   All comments should be directed to the Commissioners, not to the staff or to members of the audience.

4.   For items where there are several people wishing to speak, the Chair may impose a time limit, usually 3
     minutes per person, or 5 minutes for a group spokesperson. If a time limit is imposed on any member or
     spokesperson of the public, then the same time limit is imposed on other members or spokespersons of the
     public, respectively.

5.   Unless otherwise allowed by the Chair, no questions shall be asked by the speaker or Commission Members.

6.   Only one speaker is permitted before the Commission at a time.

7.   The discussion must be confined to essential points stated in the application bearing on the desirability or
     undesirability of the application.

8.   The Chair may cease any presentation or information that has already been presented and acknowledge that it
     has been noted in the public record.

9.   No personal attacks shall be indulged in by either side, and such action shall be sufficient cause for stopping the
     speaker from proceeding.

10. No applause or public outbursts shall be permitted.

11. The Chair or supporting agency staff may request police support to remove offending individuals who refuse to
    abide by these rules.

12. After the public comment portion of a meeting or hearing has concluded, the discussion will be limited to the
    Planning Commission and Staff.

Re:    OAM2021- 000342 – 19.62 and 19.02.050 Amendment - C-2 Zone Use Changes


MSD Staff is currently in the process of revising the permitted and conditional uses for all zoning
districts in Kearns. However, a recent concern over conversion of commercial space to
residential uses requires an update now for the C-2 zone.
The proposed revision of Chapters 19.62 and 19.02.050 of the Kearns Metro Code is attached.
This revision replaces the current Chapter 19.62 in its entirety to remove obsolete uses and
remove many existing conditional uses in the C-2 zone. Conditional uses have been moved to
the permitted use list if they were deemed appropriate for the zone. It also changes the
residential uses allowed in the zone. Ground floor residential is now prohibited, but a mixed-use
product would be allowed with units above ground floor commercial. These uses, definitions,
and zones will be further refined as the code is updated in the coming months.
The proposed revision to 19.02.050 adds text stating that uses not listed in each zone’s use
tables are prohibited. This text is standard in most codes but was not in the text inherited from
Salt Lake County.
A detailed summary of the changes has been provided by Nathan Bracken of Smith Hartvigsen
and is attached along with the proposed code text for your review and consideration.
The MSD staff recommends that the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation
for the proposed ordinance amendment to the Kearns Council for their consideration and
approval.

  Date:        June 3, 2021
  Re:          Public Hearing to Consider Amendments to Chapter 19.62 of the Kearns Metro Code

Background

        Chapter 19.62 of the Kearns Metro Code (the “Code”) governs land use within the C-2
(community commercial) zone, including conditional uses. These proposed amendments are intended
to update the C-2 Zone to conform to the new general plan the Council adopted on October 22, 2020,
and the Kearns Town Center Master Plan the Council adopted on March 28, 2019. These proposed
edits seek to ensure that permitted uses within the C-2 commercial zone are consistent with the general
and master plans, while also removing incompatible conditional uses (e.g., cemeteries, mobile home
parks, golf courses, etc.) inherited from Salt Lake County pursuant to Utah Code § 10-2a-414(3).

Key Changes

        Section 19.62.020, which Kearns inherited from Salt Lake County, currently requires
conditional use permits for all commercial developments over 1 acre in size in the C-2 zone regardless
of the type of commercial development. The proposed draft removes this requirement because of the
Council’s stated goal, as set forth in the general plan, to encourage commercial development in Kearns’
existing C-2 zones. Relatedly, it appears this requirement necessitates significant staff time and
resources and adds extra costs and time to commercial projects without providing corresponding
benefits to Kearns or commercial land use applicants.

        Section 19.62.030 (Conditional Uses) is “reserved.” The uses identified in that list were either
moved to “permitted” (and added to Section 19.62.020) or, if they were determined to be incompatible
with the C-2 zone, were removed entirely from the list of possible uses in the C-2 zone.

        One use that was previously permitted has been removed: ground floor housing. The intent is
to encourage mixed-use developments where a retail or commercial use is on the first floor and housing
is permitted on higher floors. Because Kearns has limited existing commercial development and
limited available land for new commercial development, it needs to preserve the commercial nature
and mixed-use features of its C-2 zone to implement the general the master plans. These edits seek to
further this goal by allowing for both commercial and residential development but prohibiting
residential development that comes at the expense of Kearns’ limited commercial development.

        Lastly, a new Section 19.02.050 clarifies that uses not identified as permitted or conditional
within Title 19 of the Code are prohibited. This language is common in most municipal land use codes
but was missing from the Salt Lake County Code that Kearns inherited.
