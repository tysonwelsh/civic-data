                                 KEARNS CITY COUNCIL

           RESOLUTION NO. 2024-5-1                               DATE: May 13, 2024

A RESOLUTION APPOINTING MAYOR KELLY BUSH AS KEARNS’
REPRESENTATIVE TO THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES
DISTRICT BOARD OF TRUSTEES

                                          RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the City of Kearns (“Kearns”) is a member entity of the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District (the “MSD”); and

      WHEREAS, during the 2024 legislative session, the Utah Legislature passed H.B. 35,
which converted the metro townships of Copperton, Emigration Canyon, Kearns, Magna, and
White City into cities and towns based on their populations effective May 1, 2024; and

        WHEREAS, previously, when Kearns was a metro township, Section 17B-2a-1106
(2)(b)(ii) of the Utah Code automatically designated the mayor of each metro township to serve as
their municipality’s designated representative to the MSD Board of Trustees; and

      WHEREAS, effective May 1, 2024, H.B. 35 repealed section 17B-2a-1106 (2)(b)(ii) of the
Utah Code; and

      WHEREAS, now that Kearns is a city, Section 17B-2a-1106 (2)(b)(i) of the Utah
Code requires the Kearns City Council (the “Council”) to appoint a representative to the
MSD Board of Trustees; and

       WHEREAS, the Council desires to reappoint Mayor Kelly Bush to serve as Kearns’
representative to the MSD Board of Trustees.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, that the Council reappoints Mayor Kelly Bush as Kearns’s
representative on the MSD Board of Trustees.
