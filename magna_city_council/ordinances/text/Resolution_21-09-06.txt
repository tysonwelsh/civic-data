WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a Metro Township pursuant
to Utah Code Section 10-2a-401 et. seq.

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council (“Council”) is a Metro Township
Council pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3b-504, with the Mayor acting as Chair of the
Council pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3b-503.

WHEREAS, DS Accounting Services, LLC provides financial management
services to local governments.

WHEREAS, the attached agreement was negotiated between DS Accounting
Services, LLC and Magna to fulfill Magna’s needs for a financial manager for the
Pleasant Green Cemetery.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township Council, the
Council approves and adopts the attached contract for financial management services for
the Pleasant Green Cemetery from DS Accounting Services, LLC (David Sanderson),
marked Attachment “A” to this resolution, for the benefit of Magna, and authorizes the
Mayor to sign the same.

CONTRACT
WITNESS THIS CONTRACT (hereinafter called Contract) made the day of
, 2021 by and between DS Accounting Services LLC, the (“Contractor”) and
Pleasant Green Cemetery, the (“PGC”):
I. PURPOSE
The Contractor shall provide accounting, and any other related services to the PGC
according to directions or projects made by the Executive Director. The Contractor
retains the right to perform services required by the specifications by any reasonable
means and in any reasonable manner it sees fit.
SPECIFIC SERVICES
Daily — Provide assistance on texts, emails and telephone conversations. Prepare
accounts payable checks with proper invoices to be signed by appropriate signatures.
Monthly - Bank, PTIF reconciliation’s, bonds, monthly financial reports . Global review
of finances and provide recommendations based on current financial information.
Quarterly - Processing of quarters revenues/expenses, per quarter transparency web site.
Semi-Annual — Complete money management report and submit to the Money
Management Council.
Yearly - Audit preparation and PGC budget along with uploads to State Auditor for
yearly budget, impact fee reports, and financial reports.
Il. TERM
The term of the Contract shall commence October 1, 2021 and continue until either party
decides to terminate contract. Said Contract shall continue in full force and effect unless
and until either party gives thirty (30) days written notice of its intent to terminate.
Receipt of a termination notice by either party shall automatically terminate this Contract
upon the expiration of the applicable notice period, unless both parties agree to reinstate
this Contract. Access to banking, bond and investment electronic reports will be granted.
Ill. COMPENSATION

The PGC agrees to pay the Contractor $550 per month. Reimburse for miscellaneous
out-of-pocket expensed such as postage and travel expenses. “SPECIFIC SERVICES”
not detailed above will be billed in quarter hour increments at an $85.00 hourly rate.
This amount is to be paid monthly and the Contractor will invoice the PGC at the end of
each month and the terms of the payment will be net 15. Any delinquent amounts will be
assessed | 2% interest per month.
