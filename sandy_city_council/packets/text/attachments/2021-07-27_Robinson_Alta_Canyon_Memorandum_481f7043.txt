                                             MEMORANDUM

                                               July 27, 2021

To:              City Council Members

CC:              Mike Applegarth, Council Executive Director

From:            Zach Robinson, Council Member At-large

Subject:         Creating a timeline and implementing incremental decision making as related to
                 Alta Canyon Sports Center




Throughout my tenure on the Council, we have repeatedly held markedly similar conversations about
Alta Canyon Sports Center with very little movement towards making any real decisions. The future of
Alta Canyon is a complex and complicated question that we cannot expect to answer all at once.
Instead, the Council needs to take a step back, stop trying to solve the problem in one fell swoop, and
focus on smaller and more incremental decisions. Tonight, I would like the Council to begin this process
by implementing additional structure, committing to dates, and outlining incremental decisions that we
can take to help us more efficiently solve this very important issue.

To that end, I propose the following process and outline for our future discussions on Alta Canyon
(Exhibit “A”). I am open to additional discussion items and/or minor re-arrangements to my proposal. I
would like to ask that during tonight’s discussion we do not get into the details or fall back into the trap
of discussing the future of the Center in its entirety. If we do that, I believe strongly that we will not
make any headway. Instead let us focus only on planning out the process, content, dates, and logistics
of future discussions and decisions.
