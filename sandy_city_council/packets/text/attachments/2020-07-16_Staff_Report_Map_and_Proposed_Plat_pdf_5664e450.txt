DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicants, Tyson and Chelsea Wakley, are requesting preliminary subdivision review for a
two-lot single-family subdivision. The .41-acre property is proposed to be split so that the north
lot will be approximately 9,559 sq. ft. and front 9000 S. The south lot will be 8,000 sq. ft. and front
onto Tolman Farms Cir. The applicants are not requesting any exceptions or overlays in association
with this application. All provisions of the base zone will be adhered to through the review and
approval process.

BACKGROUND & SITE CONDITIONS
The subject property borders 9000 South to the north, the Tolman Farms 2 subdivision to
the west, and the original Tolman Farms subdivision to the south. All of the properties
surrounding the subject property to the west, east, and south are single-family homes in the
R-1-8 zone. The property to the north and across 9000 South, is zoned RM(10).

Tolman Farms Circle was mostly completed with the development of Tolman Farms 2 to the
west. However, the cul-de-sac is currently incomplete, with asphalt surrounding the
southwest portion of the proposed new lot on the south.

   NOTICE
   Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel as per Sandy
   City Land Development Code requirements, to notify of the Planning Commission meeting.
   Because this is only creating one additional lot, and the existing house is to remain, a neighborhood
   meeting was not deemed necessary.

   SUBDIVISION ANALYSIS
   The R-1-8 zone is a traditional single-family zone. All standard requirements (setbacks, building
   height, lot size, etc.) of the zone are typical when compared to other similar zones. The Tolman
   Farms 2 subdivision project, adjacent to the west of this property, finished the majority of the cul-
   de-sac for Tolman Farms Cir. Part of this project will be to dedicate right-of-way and complete
   construction of the cul-de-sac, as well as install a 5’ park strip and 5’ sidewalk.

   The existing home on the site will remain, so only the lot created to the south will become a newly-
   buildable lot. All City departments have given preliminary approval of the project.

   STAFF CONCERNS
   Staff’s primary concern is the building envelope of the lot created to the south. The applicants
   intend to build and occupy the home on the new lot. Because of the irregular shape of the lot and
   the fact that it will be on the lowest end of the zone’s allowable square footage (8,000 sq. ft.), staff
   recommends that a concept plan be provided showing that the planned home can meet all required
   setbacks.

   STAFF RECOMMENDATION
   Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine Preliminary Subdivision review is
   complete for the Tolman Farms 3 Subdivision located at 372 East 9000 South, subject to the
   following three conditions:

   1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments and redlines throughout the
      final review process and that all issues be resolved before the subdivision can be recorded.

   2. That all City provisions, codes and ordinances are adhered to during the review, construction
      and operations process of this project.

   3. That a conceptual site plan showing compliance with all setback requirements be provided by
      the applicants prior to final approval and recording of the subdivision.
