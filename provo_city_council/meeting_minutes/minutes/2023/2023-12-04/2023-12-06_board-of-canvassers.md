 Agenda

 1.           A resolution of the Board of Canvassers accepting the election returns and declaring and
              certifying the results of the votes for the General Election held on November 21, 2023. (23-
              038)

Heidi Allman, City Recorder and Election Officer, presented. She reviewed the Canvass Report and
accompanying Resolution with the council. She discussed the statistics of each district’s race and
announced the elected Council Members as follows:
         Council Member District 1: Craig Christensen
         Council Member District 3: Becky Bogdin
         Council Member District 4: Travis Hoban
         Council Member City-Wide II: Gary Garrett

Board Member Hoban thanked Ms. Allman for her communication during the election. He asked how
the voter turnout percentage was compared to previous elections.

Ms. Allman responded that the voter turnout percentage of this year’s election was comparable to other
elections that did not have a Mayoral race or bond.

With no other questions, Board Member MacKay opened public comment. With none, she invited
discussion from the council. With no discussion, she called for a motion to approve the Resolution.

        Motion:       Board Member Fillmore made a motion to approve the Resolution as written. Board
                      Member Handley seconded.

With no discussion on the motion, Board Member MacKay called for a vote.

        Vote:         The motion was approved 7:0 with Board Members Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley,
                      Hoban, Kaufusi, MacKay, and Whipple in favor. Board Member Shipley excused.
