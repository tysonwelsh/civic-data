                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1553

         AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE OPEN SPACE EASEMENT REDUCTION FOR
         APPROXIMATELY 0.63 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY
         2019 EAST GRAYSTONE CT. WITHIN DRAPER CITY.

       WHEREAS, the Land Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code has been
established to provide regulations concerning subdivisions within the City Boundaries; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it will be in the best interest of the
City to allow an easement modification of the subject property in accordance with the Open
Space Easement Reduction request; and

      WHEREAS, the Open Space Easement Reduction will allow a more gradual grade
change from the build home on the property to the drainage channel located to the north;
and

       WHEREAS, the Open Space Easement Reduction will not have a negative impact on
the function of the drainage channel to the north; and

      WHEREAS, the proposed Open Space Easement Reduction set forth herein has been
reviewed by the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been held in
accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed modifications to the
easement; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has found the proposed Open Space Easement Reduction
to be consistent with the City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

       Section 1.     Open Space Easement Reduction. The City of Draper approves the
Open Space Easement Reduction from 75-feet to 40-feet as depicted in in Exhibit A, subject
to Section 2 of this ordinance, otherwise known as the Pheasant Hills Plat 4 Lot 405 Open
Space Easement Reduction.

        Section 2.     Condition of Approval. The approval shall be subject to the condition
listed in Exhibit B.

        Section 3.   Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.
