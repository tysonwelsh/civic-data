From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: April 13, 2023
Re: Galena Grove AT&T Conditional Use Permit Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Conditional Use Permit for approximately 5.77 acres located on the east side of Vista Station Blvd., at approximately 483 W. 12300 S. The property is currently zoned OS and RA1, and the portion of the property subject to this application is located in the OS zoned area. The applicant is requesting that a Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for the development of a currently vacant portion of the site as a new wireless telecommunications tower and facility for AT&T. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1. Reasonable conditions are included as part of the approval that substantially mitigate reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use.
2. There are no reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed conditional use that cannot be substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions to achieve compliance with applicable standards.
For Denial:
1. Reasonable conditions are not able to be included that will substantially mitigate reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use.
2. There are reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed conditional use that cannot be substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions to achieve compliance with applicable standards. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type P.C. Staff Report 4/4/2023 Cover Memo
