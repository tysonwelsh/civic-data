         1. REQUEST FOR APPROVAL OF A CHIEF DEPUTY POSITION, PURSUANT TO UTAH CODE 17-33-16 TO
            SUPERVISE THE CIVIL/SUPPORT DIVISION

                County Sheriff Ryan Arbon referred to the high demands being put on the Sheriff’s Office in recent
                years and noted that there used to be three office managers. One left some time ago and the position
                remained open in hopes of converting it to a chief deputy.
                The position duties include being over evidence, over all of the support staff, over records and other
                reports with corrections, training, etc. Fiscally, this position will use the current vacant position and
                combine with cost salary savings by some others that have left and there will not be additional costs.
                The commissioners agreed that this position is needed and support it.
                Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the Chief Deputy position, pursuant to Utah Code 17-33-16
                to supervise the Civil/Support Division; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
                Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
         2. AGREEMENT TO INCREASE THE GRANT AWARD FROM THE INDIGENT DEFENSE COMMISSION TO
            WEBER COUNTY FOR THE PROVISION OF INDIGENT DEFENSE SERVICES IN THE 2020-21 FISCAL YEAR
                Mike Bouwhuis, Indigent Defense Coordinator, noted that the legislature cut funding earlier in the year
                due to the pandemic’s economic fallout, which impacted the State Indigent Defense Commission and
                which consequently impacted grants for the county’s indigent defense services. They were able to
                recover a good portion later this summer and this modification reflects that for specialty contracts to
                handle homicides, serious sex offenses, etc., and also for the Administrative Assistant to receive a
                salary increase commensurate with the average for the position in county government.
                Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the agreement to increase the grant award from the Indigent
                Defense Commission to Weber County for the provision of indigent defense services in the 2020-21
                fiscal year; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
                Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    3. RESOLUTION APPOINTING A MEMBER TO THE WEBER COUNTY BOUNDARY COMMISSION –
       RESOLUTION 35-2020
         Matt Wilson, Deputy County Attorney, gave a brief overview of the Boundary Commission’s duties
         involving filed protests regarding annexations. He stated that the county appoints two county elected
         officials to the Boundary Commission. Commissioner Harvey is currently on that commission and the
         other position was vacated by former commissioner Gibson. A protest relating to the proposed West
         Weber incorporation is anticipated. Commissioner Harvey spoke to the other two commissioners’
         expertise and suggested that they serve as the two members on the Boundary Commission.
         Commissioner Harvey’s term expires at the end of 2020. Commissioner Jenkins said that his only
         concern is a conflict of interest because he represents the county in that area and also lives in the city
         where the conflict exists. Commissioner Jenkins had publicly said that he felt that West Weber should
         be a city but has not been involved in any way. Chair Froerer stated that as a Commission they had
         not taken any position of support or non-support on the proposed incorporation; the commissioners
         had decided that it was up to the citizens to make that decision.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 35-2020 appointing Commissioner Gage Froerer to
         the Weber County Boundary Commission expiring 12/31/2020; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
