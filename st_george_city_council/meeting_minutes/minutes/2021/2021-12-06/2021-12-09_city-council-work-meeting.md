     Councilmember Jimmie Hughes

OPENING:
     Mayor Randall called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
     invocation was provided by Councilmember Curtis and the Pledge of Allegiance to the
     Flag was led by Councilmember Larkin.

DISCUSSION REGARDING THE PROPOSED SIXTH AMENEMENT TO THE LOAN
AGREEMENT BETWEEN FORT PIERCE BUSINESS PARK, L.C., THE CITY OF ST.
GEORGE, AND DIXIE-RURAL ELECTRIC ASSOCIATION, INC.:
     Economic Vitality and Housing Director Shirlayne Quayle provided a history regarding
     the loan agreement between the City, Fort Pierce Business Park, L.C. and Dixie-Rural
     Association, Inc. She provided a handout outlining the following topics: Loan
     agreement timeline; Loan agreement terms – Amendment 6 (Phase 8); Map of
     boundaries; and Map of coverage of improvements.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard added all of the previous loans have been repaid as
      contemplated in the agreement.

      Steve Jennings provided an update on the project at the Fort Pierce Business Park.
      After being asked by Councilmember Curtis, Mr. Jennings explained commercial real
      estate is busy, there is a lot going on. Their outlook is that fund would be paid back
      fairly quickly.

UPDATE FROM STAFF REGARDING THE PROPOSED TECH RIDGE DEVELOPMENT
AGREEMENT AND ZONE PLAN:
     Community Development Director John Willis presented a PowerPoint presentation
     covering the following topics: Tech Ridge Development Agreement: PD Zone Plan
     Map; and 5. Purchase of Property; 5.2 Grant of Options; 5.3 Term of Option; and 5.4
     Purchase price under each option exercise. He read a portion of section 5.2 as
     follows: “An approved Site Plan and Final Plat shall be approved by City prior to any
     takedown of property by Developer. If no building permit has been obtained within
     thirty months of property takedown, City shall have first right of refusal to
     repurchase the property at the same price at which the land was sold plus cost of
     any improvements made to property”. A brief discussion took place regarding the
     purchase price if the City were to purchase the property in this instance.

      Community Development Director John Willis continued with the PowerPoint
      presentation covering the following topics: 5.10 Land Use Applications; 5.15
      Permitted Uses; 6.1 Project Developers; 6.2 Vested Rights; 6.3 Residential Unit
      Restrictions; 6.3.1 Maximum Residential Units and Uses; 6.3.2 Maximum Residential
      Ratio; Chart Depicting Resident to Commercial Ratio; 6.5 Mixed Use; 6.6 Height
      Limits; 6.7 Development Timing; Ridgeline Building Height; Planning area 1.1 – 1.3
      Building Heights Section; 6.8 Park Improvements; and 6.8.1 Park Improvements on
      Project Land.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard commented that he thought 6.8.1 had been stricken
      from the latest draft. There have been discussions regarding recreation projects on
      the west side. Not knowing what those projects were specifically and not knowing
      what the Council would be comfortable with, the language was stricken for now.
      Isaac Barlow with Tech Ridge agreed to the language being stricken at this time.

      Community Development Director John Willis continued with the PowerPoint
      presentation covering the following topics: 6.8.2 Minimum Park Improvement
      Acreage; 6.8.3 Park Improvement Plan; 6.8.4 Minimum Park Improvement
      Expenditure; 6.8.6 Park Impact Fees; 6.9 Ownership and Maintenance of Park
      Improvements; 6.9.1 Construction and Dedication; 6.9.2 City Maintenance; 6.9.4
      Town Square and Park Categories; 6.9.5 Alteration of Park Improvements; 6.9.6
      Pavilion; 6.10 System Improvements; 6.11 Restrictive Covenants; 6.12 Architectural
      Design Standards; 6.13 Signage Standards; 6.14 Landscape Standards; 7.1 Changes
      to the PD-MU Zoning Plan; 7.2 Changes to Permitted Land Use; 8.1 Community
      Reinvestment Areas/Tax Increment Revenues; 9.1 Conditions of Approval; 9.2
      Impact Fees; 9.3 Dedication of Project Improvements; 9.4 Pre Approval of
      Construction Activities; 9.5 Construction of West Access Road; 9.6 Reconfiguration of
      Tech Ridge Parkway; 9.7 Agreement Contingent Upon Vehicular Access; 9.8 Buyers
      Inspections and Due Diligence; 9.9 Title Matters; 10.1 Ordinances of General
      Applicability and Reserved Legislative Powers; 10.2 Provision of System
      Improvements and Other Infrastructure; 11.1 Water Infrastructure; 11.1.1 Water
      Availability Not Guaranteed; 11.2 Transportation and Traffic Mitigation; 11.2.1
      Developer Obligations; 11.2.1.1 Road Improvements; 11.2.1.2 Dedication of Roads;
      11.2.1.3 Roadway Configuration; 11.2.1.4 Streetscapes – Construction and
      Maintenance; 11.2.2 City Obligations; 11.2.2.1 Streets Rights of Way; 11.2.2.2
      Street Design and Streetscape; 11.3 Hillside Areas; and 13.2 Successors and
      Assigns.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard explained the idea with section 11.2 provides the City
      the ability to approve the successor or assignee of the project if Mr. Barlow were to
      divest himself of the project or Tech Ridge LLC. This section gives the City a more
      interested position on disposition of the land and who it may be sold to.

      Mayor Randall thanked everyone who has worked on the development agreement.
      After reading through the agreement, it appears to protect the City.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard noted staff still has to finalize some of the language.
      The agreement will be on the agenda for the December 16th meeting for the Council
      to approve.

      Councilmember Smethurst mentioned that at a recent Council meeting, the integrity
      of some of the Councilmembers was questioned. He has spent a lot of time with Mr.
      Barlow and has been in favor of this project the entire time. He wanted everyone to
      know that his honor and his integrity is something he has and will keep; he cannot
      be bought off. He is voting on this because it is the right thing to do, not because
      someone paid him.

      Mayor Randall commented that she likes that language added regarding water
      resources. This language will need to be on everything that comes forward for
      approval.

      Councilmember Larkin commented she is excited for the Tech Ridge development.
      As this project is an extension of the downtown area, she would like to see a
      connection between the two.

ADJOURN:
     MOTION:
         A motion was made by Councilmember Larkin.
     SECOND:
         The motion was seconded by Councilmember Smethurst.
     VOTE:
         Mayor Pro Tem Hughes called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.
