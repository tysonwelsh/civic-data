, Cameron Platt explained the hierarchy of laws and regulations to which the Town is subject, and ways
the Town, implements laws, regulations, and policies. At the pinnacle are state statutes, succeeded by |
county ordinances, Town code, Town resolutions, and policies and procedures. Resolutions, such as !
| the one outlining the discussed procedures, function as the Town's mechanism for enacting policies.
Platt noted that resolutions hold the lowest level of binding authority and retain a malleable nature, |
subject to change by the Town at its discretion. Platt underscored that these resolutions, specifically
the outlined procedures, serve as preferred practices without legal repercussions on a :
Councilmember's elected status. The primary goal is to afford staff ample time for reaction,
scheduling, and document compilation. The flexibility of these procedures allows the Council leeway in |
the event of unforeseen challenges, offering adaptability beyond the constraints of ordinances or
statutes. While serving as a framework for optimal operation, the procedures do not curtail the
Council's authority to address matters falling outside these established guidelines.
