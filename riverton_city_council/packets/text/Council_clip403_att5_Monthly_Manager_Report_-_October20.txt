                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                             October 2020

October brought the first cold temperatures of the year and a slight increase in the number of stolen
vehicles (11) in our city. We believe we had an active ring of car thieves working in our
community as we were able to collect surveillance video and it showed the same group of suspects
involved in several of our vehicle theft cases. We were able to identify one of the suspects and as
we were trying to determine where the suspect was living, he was arrested by another police agency
driving a stolen vehicle. The suspect is an ex-convict and was on parole from Idaho, so we are
working with the District Attorney to ensure he is sent back to Idaho to account for his criminal
actions.

We conducted active impaired driving enforcement which yielded 17 arrests for impaired driving,
involving both drugs and alcohol. We also manned a booth at the annual Halloween Bash, which
provided us with an opportunity to interact with the community in a fun and relaxed atmosphere.
Although the COVID pandemic required us to implement safety protocols and limit some of our
activities, it was a great event and we were honored to be a part of it.
