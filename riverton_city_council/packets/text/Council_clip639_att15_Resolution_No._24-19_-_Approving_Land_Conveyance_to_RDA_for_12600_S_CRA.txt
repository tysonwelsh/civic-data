                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 24-19

    A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CONVEYANCE OF LAND OWNED BY
       RIVERTON CITY TO THE RIVERTON REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency (the “Agency”) created the
12600 South Community Reinvestment Project Area (the “Project Area”) to promote the
purposes as set forth in the adopted Project Area Plan (the “Plan”), including the creation of jobs
and stimulating economic development; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §17C-1-207 authorizes Riverton City, Utah (the “City”) to
assist and cooperate in the planning, undertaking, construction, or operation of the Project Area;
and

       WHEREAS, among other things, the City may assist the Agency by conveying real
property owned by the City within the Project Area to the Agency for reduced or no
compensation; and

         WHEREAS, the City owns five acres of land located within the Project Area, described
in the legal description attached hereto as Exhibit A (the “Parcel”); and

        WHEREAS, the City desires to convey the Parcel to the Agency for the purposes of
assisting the Agency in achieving the goals of the Agency in the Project Area and Plan; and

        WHEREAS, the Agency and City have caused the notice required for the City to provide
assistance to the Agency under UCA 17C-1-207 to be properly given; and

         WHEREAS, the Agency desires to accept the conveyance of the Parcel by the City to
assist the Agency in achieving the goals of the Agency in the Project Area and Plan.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

       1.      The City approves and authorizes the conveyance by the City of the Parcel to the
               Agency via a Quit Claim Deed in substantially the same form as the attached
               Exhibit B, which will be duly recorded with the office of the Salt Lake County
               Recorder.
       2.      The Riverton City Council finds the conveyance of the Parcel to the Agency will
               advance and further the purposes of the Project Area and Plan and aid the Agency
               in fulfilling the goals of the Plan.
      3.     City officers and employees are hereby authorized and directed to take such
             actions as necessary or convenient to affect the purposes of this Resolution as set
             forth herein.
      4.     This Resolution shall take effect upon adoption.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 6th day of
February, 2024 by the following vote:
