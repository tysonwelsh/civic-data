STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
and recommendations.
    • The proposed Airport Zones was collaboratively created with the Airport Director.
    • Staff worked with the Ombudsman on the airport properties.
    • All 8 parcels are proposed to be rezone to the Airport (A) zone are owned by Provo City.
    • The proposed rezone furthers the goals of the General Plan and Airport Master Plan
