PC 25-001 DworQ Office Building -Continued from the January 23" meeting. [Design Review
Permit] James Johnson/DworQ LLC, authorized agent/owner is requesting a design review permit
for a new 44,178 square foot, three-story office building located at 1099 West 400 North in the
Commercial Service (CS) zone; TIN 05-093-0003, 05-062-0026 (Ellis Neighborhood).

Staff: Russ Holley reviewed the request for a design review permit for a new Office Building that the
Planning Commission continued from their January 23, 2025, meeting. R. Holley reviewed the Jan
23" meeting where it was stated by staff, that the applicant would need to obtain a permit from the
Army Corp of Engineers before the City could issue a Design Review Permit. After closer review of
the Land Development Code by the Logan City Attorney, it was determined that the “permitted
activity” referred to in Chapter 17.24.110 F is a Building Permit and not a Design Review Permit.

Mohamed Abdullahi, Logan City Attorney, clarified that the Army Corp Permit needs to be obtained
and provided to the City before a Building Permit can be issued, not a Design Review Permit.

M. Abdullahi read Land Development Code Chapter 17.24.110 F which states, Wetland Disturbance.
No activity will be permitted which disturbs, fills, dredges, clears, destroys, or alters any water, soils
and vegetation within delineated wetlands as set forth in this Section, unless approved in writing by
the U.S. Army Corp of Engineers (Corp Permit). A copy of the USACE permit approval shall be
provided to Logan City.


R. Holley reviewed the site, the proposed building and parking area, and the existing wetlands on the
property, and stated that the applicant has submitted their delineated wetlands to the Army Corp of
Engineers for review. The proposed parking lot is 50 feet from the delineated wetland, which meets
the Land Development Code setback requirements for wetlands. There are two areas of proposed
wetland disturbance, one at the parking lot entrance and one where the stormwater retention pond
pipe runs. The Army Corp will have to review and approve mitigation for both wetland disturbance
areas.

Building heights are limited to 38 feet in the Commercial Service zone. The applicant is requesting
an additional 3 feet of building height to 41 feet 8 inches. The Planning Commission has the authority
to adjust building heights up to 10% if the need is justified.

Commissioner Lucero asked about the large parking lot. R. Holley said the proposed building will be
used as a call center with 300 employees, requiring at least 300 parking stalls.

Proponent: Courtney Wallace, the Architect on the project, explained the extra height is needed to
fit the building structure and the duct work between the floors. Travis Johnson with Lundahl Building
said that having a third floor adds 100 additional jobs and spoke about the function of the call center
being mainly tech positions that serve Cities.

Commissioner Lewis asked if the building was designed with the 10% height allowance. Travis said
yes, it was designed with the extra 10% height.

Public: Garen Perrett, Owner of the property and business, said that the call center employs tech,
developer, and sales positions. G. Perrett also mentioned that he has three separate locations that
they are trying to combine into one, and if he can’t get three stories on this building, they won't be
able to complete the project. He asked the commission to approve the proposed project because
he would like the business to operate in Logan.

Commission: Commissioner Heare said the additional 3-foot height is not a big concern.
Commissioner Guth agreed and said the extra height in this area would not be impactful.
Commissioner McNamara thanked the applicant for explaining why the extra height is needed.
Commissioner Lucero spoke about the height having a minimal impact compared to the benefit the
building will bring to the community.

Commissioner Heare stated that the maximum height requirement in the CS zone may need to be
updated to better allow for three-story buildings.
