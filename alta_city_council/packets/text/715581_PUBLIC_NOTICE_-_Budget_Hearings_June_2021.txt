                                  PUBLIC NOTICE


 The Alta Town Council will hold a public hearing to receive comments and questions
 regarding the TENTATIVE 2021-2022 Fiscal Budgets (General Fund and Sewer and
      Water Enterprise Funds) and the Certified Tax Rate for the Town of Alta.

Following the public hearing on the proposed 2021-2022 Fiscal Budgets, the Alta Town
  Council will hold a public hearing to receive comments and questions regarding the
                proposed amendments to the 2020-2021 Fiscal Budgets.

Both hearings will be held virtually on Wednesday, June 16, 2021 at 4:00 P.M. with live
   viewing details to be announced on the Town’s website at www.townofalta,com

Questions regarding the proposed 2021-2022 Fiscal Budgets or the proposed amendments
 to the 2020-2021 Fiscal Budgets may be directed to Piper Lever, Town Clerk, P.O. Box
            8016, Alta, Utah 84092, by calling 385-449-1887 or by email to
 plever@townofalta.com. The deadline for submittal of written comments is 3:00 pm on
                               Tuesday, June 15, 2021.
