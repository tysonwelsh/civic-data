                                      Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who appear and speak at a
County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy
or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

G. ACTION ITEMS:

      1. MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY, DIVISION
         OF EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT FOR USE OF COUNTY RESOURCES.

            Lance Peterson, with County Emergency Management, stated that this new agreement allows the State
            to use county facilities as necessary following a disaster, if available. It addresses reimbursement to
            the county if any costs are incurred. The term is five years.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the Memorandum of Understanding with the Utah
            Department of Public Safety, Division of Emergency Management for use of county resources;
            Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
            Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

      2. AMENDMENTS TO THE CONTRACTS FOR THE FOLLOWING INDIGENT DEFENSE ATTORNEYS:
          AMMON NELSON; JENNIFER CLARK; JONATHAN PACE; MARY ANNE ELLIS; RICHARD WILLIAMS.

            Bryan Baron, Deputy County Attorney, noted that indigent defense attorneys are paid a contractual
            base amount, and they are paid $50/hour to cover additional assignments at the court. Some of those
            amounts have changed and are reflected in these contracts as well as adding $1,200 for indigent defense
            for juvenile court representation.
            Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve amendments to the contracts for these indigent defense
            attorneys: Ammon Nelson; Jennifer Clark; Jonathan Pace; Mary Anne Ellis; and Richard Williams;
            Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    3. CONTRACT WITH ROUGH DRAFTS POLKA BAND, LLC, FOR ENTERTAINMENT AT 2019 HOF
       GERMANFEST.

         Duncan Olsen, GSEC General Manager, presented this contract.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the contract with the Rough Drafts Polka Band, LLC, for
         entertainment at the 2019 Hof Germanfest; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

    4. APPOINTING A CHAIR AND VICE CHAIR FOR THE WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION.

         Chair Harvey feels it is appropriate to rotate the chair of this body and give another commissioner that
         opportunity. Commissioner Froerer expressed appreciation to Commissioner Harvey for an excellent
         job and Commissioner Jenkins echoed those sentiments.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to appoint Commissioner Jenkins as chair for the Weber County
         Commission for 2019; Chair Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

         Chair Harvey moved to appoint Commissioner Froerer as vice chair for the Weber County Commission
         for 2019; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

H. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS: Commissioner Harvey referred to the great accolades by the current
   commissioners to former Commissioner Ebert for his genuine interest for the county’s wellbeing. He
   stated that he is a better person for having worked with Commissioner Ebert.

      Commissioner Harvey moved to adjourn; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
