 4              1. 2   Mayor' s Report
 5

 6   ®         Mayor Kristie Overson mentioned that the annual tree- lighting ceremony had taken place
 7   two nights before. She appreciated everyone' s hard work to make it an enjoyable event for the
 8   community.
 9
10   Unified Fire recently received a clean audit. It was a very well- run organization, and she
11   appreciated the opportunity to sit on the Board of Directors.
12
13   TVPD, along with other agencies, spearheaded a retail blitz to send a message to criminals in the
14   city that law enforcement and retailers were working together to stave off retail theft this Christmas
15   season.

16
17   The Mayor had observed some SWAT training recently. She was very impressed with Brett
18   Miller' s leadership and the entire team' s abilities.
19
20   The family who owned local business Speed Kleen had recently donated 8 baskets full of
21   Thanksgiving food and asked TVPD to distribute it to families in need. This was something the
22   family did each year, and the police department appreciated the opportunity to participate.
23
24   Mayor Overson had participated in a phone call between Congressman Burgess Owens and various
25   local leaders. She appreciated his willingness to collaborate with various municipalities including
26   Taylorsville and she pointed out that this relationship with him would be key as a new congress
27   began their new session next month.
28
29   The Taylorsville High School madrigals had sung at the Christmas tree lighting event. The school
30   was currently holding a fundraiser in order to provide their auditorium with much- needed
31   upgrades. Organizers hoped to raise $ 500, 000 for this great school which did a lot for the
32   community. She wished them well in their endeavors.
33
34   The Youth Council had decorated trees in city hall, served cookies and hot cocoa at the tree-
35   lighting event, decorated the museum and would help out with Saturday with Santa. They, along
36   with their advisor Kris Heineman never hesitated to jump in and help whenever needed, and she
37   greatly appreciated their efforts.
38
39   A code enforcement officer was recently hired, and the city was fully staffed. The Mayor then
40   shared a note from a resident about a recently replaced streetlight.
