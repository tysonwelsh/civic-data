MEMO
To: City Council
From: Todd Draper, AICP
Date: 2026-03-03
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinances #1706 and 1707


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Land Use Map Amendment and Zoning Map
Amendments for approximately 0.25 acres located on the southeastern boundary of the
City at approximately 16199 S. 3300 E. The property is currently zoned A5 and is located
in the Hillside Sensitive Overlay Zone.

Draper City and Highland City have recently approved a municipal boundary line
adjustment that would remove the subject property from Draper City’s jurisdiction and
place it in the jurisdiction of Highland City. Draper City is now seeking to amend the Land
Use and Zoning Maps to remove the land use and zoning designations for the property.
Once the boundary adjustment is final Highland City will be responsible to set land use
and zoning designations for the property.

The Planning Commission will hear this item at their February 26, 2026 meeting and their
recommendation to the City Council will be presented at the Council meeting.
