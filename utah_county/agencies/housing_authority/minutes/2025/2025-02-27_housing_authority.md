C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-02-03 Project Selection 
April made a motion to approve the Project Selection for Development in Salem, UT . 
Sara seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
The Executive Director presented two independent projects proposed by out-of-state 
development groups. Each of the group desired HAUC to participate in their project, have 
small ownership equity, and provide the property management once completed. The 
particulars and ownership structure for each of the developments was presented to the board. 
Both projects had many similarities, however, the ownership structures were different.  
percentages varied between projects. One project provided nearly ½ the ownership 
percentage and exiting the ownership at year 15.  The second project provided higher and 
continuous ownership.  Discussion ensured by the board with all questions being answered 
satisfactorily.  At the end of the discussion, HAUC staff and board believed the higher 
ownership project better met the mission of HAUC. 
 
D. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-02-04 Purchase Agreement Modification 
Sara Hubbs made a motion to approve the Purchase Agreement Modification. April 
Smith seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC has been under contract to purchase a property located in central Orem, Utah. Several 
items were discovered from the various reports and surveys obtained with respect to the 
property.  These discoveries require more time to pursue and better determine the viability of 
the property. These discoveries include asbestos, the impacts of overhead power lines, and 
boundary issues. The Executive Director is working with city staff to regarding various options 
for the property. The due date is the beginning of March. HAUC is recommending working with 
real estate agents and the owner of the property to extend the date until June 2025, this will 
allow HAUC to properly research and bid the cost of all remediation, relocation, and other 
items before finalizing the purchase of the property.
