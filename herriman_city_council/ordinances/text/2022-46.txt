ORDINANCE NO. 2022-46

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH,
AMENDING HERRIMAN CITY CODE § 10-11-6(C)(7) AND § 10-15B-6(B), WHICH RELATE
TO TRAILS ON STEEP SLOPES.

Background

A. Herriman City is approximately 23 square miles, and the City Council and
Herriman residents value its ample open space and trails.

B. Currently, Herriman has over 43 miles of natural and paved trails, which are
used for walking, running, hiking, and biking.

C. Along with the Governor’s Office of Economic Opportunity, Herriman seeks to
inspire and facilitate quality trails, community connections, and outdoor recreation.

D. Utah Code § 10-9a-102 grants the Herriman City authority to enact ordinances
that the Herriman City Council considers necessary or appropriate for the use and
development of land within the City.

E. Currently, in City Code § 10-11-6(C)(7), the City has a restriction in recreational
zones on “clearing, excavating and grading ... where the slope exceeds thirty percent.” This
restriction is also in all residential zones, but the regulation specifically excludes “roads and
trails.”

F. And currently, in City Code § 10-15B-6(B), the City has a regulation in the
hillside overlay zone that should include trails when considering an exception to “... cross
slopes greater than 30% ...”

G. The subject text amendment clarifies that trails in the recreational zones are
exempt from the thirty percent slope restriction and that trails in the hillside overlay zone
may cross slopes greater than 30%.

H. The subject text amendment clarifies that trails in the recreational zones are
exempt from the thirty percent slope restriction.

l. The Planning Commission held a public hearing, reviewed the subject text
amendment, and gave its recommendations to the City Council.

J. The City Council held a public hearing and reviewed the subject text
amendment.

K. The City Council finds that the subject text amendment will enhance public
health, safety, and welfare.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

Herriman City Code § 10-11-6(C)

7.

Steep Slopes:

a. No development, including clearing, excavation and grading, is permitted
where the slope exceeds thirty percent (30%), except trails. All areas with
slopes greater than thirty percent (40%) shall remain in natural private or
public open space, except as expressly allowed in this chapter.

Herriman City Code § 10-15B-6(B)

1.

No grades greater than or equal to thirty percent (40%) slope may be modified to be
less than thirty percent (40%) slope to create a new or additional developable area.

Roads, trails, and other vehicular routes shall not cross slopes greater than thirty
percent (30%) unless, after review by the Planning Commission, it is determined
that:

a. Appropriate engineering measures can be taken to minimize the impact of the
cuts and fills, consistent with the purpose of this article, and

b. The environment and aesthetics of the area will not be significantly affected.

Retaining structures shall be required on all approved cuts or fills on thirty percent
(50%) slopes to minimize disturbing of sloped areas.
