                               RESOLUTION NO. 23-40

          A RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING CAL ROBERTS AS A MEMBER OF
          THE DRAPER CITY AUDIT COMMITTEE

      WHEREAS, the City is authorized to establish subordinate bodies within the city
as deemed appropriate by the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, the City has created an Audit Committee within the city for the
purpose of providing a framework to advise the City Council on the annual audit of
the City’s financial statements; and

       WHEREAS, the duties of this Committee are to recommend and engage an
auditor to audit the city’s annual financial statement; provide oversight of the city’s
financial statements and follow up on auditor findings and management letter
comments provided by independent auditors; and

      WHEREAS, the terms of one of the members of the Audit Committee will expire
on August 18, 2023; and

    WHEREAS, the City Council recognizes the need to fill this position on the Audit
Committee; and

    WHEREAS, the Mayor has identified one Council Member to serve on the Audit
Committee; and

      WHEREAS, one member of the City Council has agreed to serve as per DCMC
3-5-110(C)(1); and

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1.     Appointment.       The City Council consents to the Mayor’s
appointment of the following individual to serve on the Audit Committee:

      Council Member Cal Roberts is appointed for the term of August 18, 2023,
through August 18, 2025; and

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
