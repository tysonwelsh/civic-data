CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL MEETING
January 05, 2015

The Cache County Council convened in a special session on January 05, 2015 at
12:30 p.m. in the Cache County Council Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.

ATTENDANCE:

Chairman: Kathy Robison

Vice Chairman: G. Gregory Merrill

Council Members: Val Potter, Jon White, Cory Yeates & Gordon Zilles.
County Executive: Craig “W” Buttars

County Clerk/Auditor: Jill N. Zollinger

County Attorney: James Swink

The following individuals were also in attendance: Janeen Allen, LaDawn Allen, Danny Brownell,
Shara Buttars, Callie Erickson, David L. Erickson, Ila Erickson, Noble Erickson, Assessor Kathleen
Howell, Verlo Howell, Clark Israelsen, Treasurer Craig McAllister, Joan Merrill, Nancy Potter, Sid
Zollinger, David Zook.

° OPENING REMARKS AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Council member Yeates led those present in the Pledge of Allegiance.
REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF AGENDA

ACTION: Motion by Council member Potter to approve the agenda as written. Yeates
seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 6-0.

INITIAL PROPOSAL FOR CONSIDERATION

* Appointment of Interim County Council Member - Chairman Robison invited Cache
County Republican Party Chair Danny Brownell to speak. Brownell presented the name of
David L. Erickson as the choice of the Cache County Republican Party to be appointed an
interim County Council member to fill the County Council North District seat recently
vacated by Craig “W” Buttars.

David Erickson said he is honored to serve and hopes to help keep Cache Valley the
wonderful place to live that it presently is.

ACTION: Motion by Council member Merrill to appoint David L. Erickson as the interim
County Council member for the County Council North District. Yeates seconded the
motion. The vote was unanimous, 6-0.

* Swearing-In of New County Council Member - Chairman Robison asked Clerk/Auditor
Jill Zollinger to perform the Oath of Office for Erickson. Zollinger administered the oath.
Erickson was invited to take his seat on the stand.


Cache County Council Special Session
01-05-2015

Chairman Robison invited those present to a reception in the Council Conference Room
following the meeting.

ADJOURNMENT

ACTION: Motion by Council member Yeates to adjourn from the special session of the
Cache County Council. Zilles seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 7-0.

The Council meeting adjourned at 12:40 p.m.
