                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-07

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF THE CITY OF DRAPER TO
       APPOINT MALENA MURRAY AS HUMAN RESOURCES DIRECTOR FOR DRAPER

       WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint Malena Murray as Human Resources
Director of the City of Draper in accordance with the appointment procedures provided
by law and city code; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code 3-1-030 requires the Human Resources
Director be appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, Malena Murray has the credentials, experience and professionalism
necessary to be the Human Resources Director.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council gives its advice and consent to the
appointment of Malena Murray as Human Resources Director and the Mayor is hereby
authorized to appoint Malena Murray as Human Resources Director, in accordance with
appointment procedures provided by law and city code.

        Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Resolution, and all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall
be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon
passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON THIS
1ST DAY OF FEBRUARY 2022.
