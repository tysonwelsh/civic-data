3) Review and acceptance of Planning Commission meeting schedule for 2025

Noreen Edwards presented the suggested meeting schedule, and noted several dates that may
need to be cancelled due to holidays, graduations, and other conflicts. Commissioner Everett
suggested that the work session for April be cancelled due to overlap with spring break.
Commissioner Lockhart suggested changing July meeting dates so that they could avoid meeting
on July 24. The commission agreed that July 31made the most sense as a replacement date.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre motioned to approve the 2025 meeting schedule with the
        modifications to remove the April 3 and July 24 meetings, and to add a meeting on July
        31. Commissioner Lockhart seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Roberts, yes; Commissioner Jones, yes.

        Motion passed unanimously.

4) Review and acceptance of the Planning Commission Bylaws

Kim Struthers presented the Planning Commission Bylaws using a Jeopardy-style game, which
Commissioner Everett won.

Struthers expressed interest in doing a training on the Municipal Officers and Employees Ethics
Act, similar to other trainings that have taken place in the past.

The Commission asked for clarification as to what counts as bias when seeking information prior
to a public hearing. Noreen Edwards explained that discussing aspects of the development code
in relation to projects is allowed, but personal feelings and opinions are discouraged.

Commissioner Lockhart asked where amendments to the bylaws originate. Struthers explained
that they primarily come from the Planning Commission itself, but that they may also happen if
there are changes to State law that affect the bylaws.

Struthers took the opportunity to bring up a recent appeal that was approved due to wording in
the bylaws that allowed applicants 30 days after minutes were approved and posted. This had
caused confusion during the appeal decision. The Commission discussed the possibility of basing
appeals off the date of the meeting in which the decision was made, rather than the approval of
the minutes. This is a possibility due to instant access to the video feed of the meetings, and is
fairer to citizens, as everyone would have an equal amount of time, rather than waiting on
minutes approval, which may vary depending on the meeting. Commissioner Lockhart suggested
taking out the sentence about the minutes entirely.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre motioned to accept the bylaws with the included
        modification of removing the sentence about posted minutes in section 8.2.
        Commissioner Jones seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Roberts, yes; Commissioner Jones, yes.
        Motion passed unanimously.

5) Discussion of the mission, vision, and core values of the Planning Commission

Kim Struthers presented the item. He stated that the planning division felt it would be out of line
to assign core values and mission statements without input from the Commission.

Commissioner Lockhart asked if adding a mission statement would open the Commission up for
allegations and litigation from citizens. Commissioner Everett shared her concerns and
mentioned that the mission of the Planning Commission is outlined in the municipal code
already.

Commissioner Jones suggested that if anything were to change, it should be an addition to the
bylaws that identify the duties of the Planning Commission, as he could not find anything of the
sort in the current bylaws. Noreen Edwards suggested a referral to the duties outlined in the
municipal code instead.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre motioned to amend his previous motion and include an
        amendment to the “Purpose” section of the bylaws that refers to the duties of the
        Planning Commission as outlined in the municipal code. Commissioner Kunze seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Roberts, yes; Commissioner Jones, yes.

        Motion passed unanimously.

6) Review of 2023 Meeting Statistics

Jacob Curtis presented the item. He stated that nothing in the statistics indicated that any major
changes needed to be made.

7) Discussion of meeting preparation importance

Kim Struthers presented the item. He emphasized that meeting preparation improved decision
making, increased time efficiency, and ensured clear communication. He suggested reviewing
agendas and materials, researching background information, and preparing questions and talking
points ahead of each meeting.

Noreen Edwards gave a brief overview of how public noticing functions. She stated that she tries
to get all notices out ahead of the required time. She also explained that noticing happens based
on county records, which are imperfect, and may not have been updated, which is why there may
be complaints from citizens when it comes to receiving notices (or lack thereof).

Commissioner Everett expressed thanks to the planning division for their help in keeping things
running smoothly. He also expressed gratitude for being able to serve the public in his capacity
as a Commissioner. The rest of the Commission expressed agreement.

8)Adjournment

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Lockhart moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Jones seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:55 p.m.
