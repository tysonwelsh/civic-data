Campbell Scientific Weather Station Donation to Logan River Golf Course – Mayor
Daines

Mayor Daines stated that on August 25, 20222 the following Press Release went out
regarding a donation from Campbell Scientific.

Representatives from Campbell Scientific, Inc., were instrumental in donating a fully
integrated weather station valued at $12,000 to Logan River Golf Course during the
month of July 2022. The timing of the donation and subsequent installation of the Toro
T107 model Weather Station allows Randy Oldham, Course Superintendent, and his staff
to monitor and adjust in real time watering conditions for the golf course. As an
integrated system, the weather station communicates with the Golf Course’s recently
upgraded irrigation system and these technological improvements equate to water and
power conservation and improved turf conditions.

The City of Logan, along with Logan River Golf Course is grateful for Campbell
Scientific’s gracious donation and recognizes its representatives for this project
including Bruce Smith, Senior Technical Support Engineer, Dana Worley, Manager and
who initially approved the donation idea, and Larry Shirk, Vice President of Client
Services for Campbell Scientific, Inc.

Campbell Scientific, Inc., is a Logan-based company dedicated to delivering state-of-the-
art measurement and control systems to customers all over the world.

Electric Surcharge – Mark Montgomery, Light & Power Director

Light & Power Director Mark Montgomery addressed the Council regarding an electric
surcharge. The department held off from enacting an electric surcharge, however, it can
no longer be put off for the month of July surcharge. The budgeted power for the month
of July surcharge came up short $709,451.82. The base residential rate is $0.09/KWH
and at present will be bumped by an additional $0.0180/KWH (Kilowatt per hour).

Today, the electric grid was short for many hours and electricity had to be purchased at
the market rate of $963 per MWH (Megawatt per hour equals 1,000 kilowatts of
electricity per hour). In perspective, the kilowatt is being purchased at .90 cents and being
sold to the citizens for 0.09 cents. The kilowatt is being purchased at ten times more than
we sell it to Logan City rate payers under the current rate.

officers and fire department personnel to live away from the City. Recruitment and
retention affect this notion today. Part of the drive is the competitive nature of trying to
reach out and acquire staff and retain employees. One of the factors to consider is what
other communities are doing to address similar situations and be mindful of competitors
as well.

At present, there is a 17-mile radius of residency for Police and Fire. The radius for
Police will be moved out three miles to create a 20-mile radius of recruitment. This will
open recruitment options and give housing options to potential employees as well.

The change to a 20-mile radius will include new areas of Cache County but also slightly
extend outside areas of Cache County barely into Idaho. The Mayor and Chief Jensen
were not comfortable extending the radius through Sardine Canyon toward Brigham
City. The map is slightly skewed North as a result.

Mayor Daines reminded he Council that the requirement for firefighters is not changing.
The Fire department will remain within the 17-mile radius of residency. Part of the
reason is that Police officers are able to utilize sirens and have a take home vehicle to
move about, whereas Firefighters do not have that same capability.

Councilmember A. Anderson asked where the Idaho line starts and stops.

Chief Jensen answered the dip just past the Idaho border outside of Franklin before
Preston is roughly the extent of the radius.
