                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 21-07

            AN ORDINANCE APPROVING A NEW RIVERTON CITY SEAL

       WHEREAS; pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-1-202 municipalities may sue and be
sued, enter into contracts and by ordinance adopt a municipal name and seal which may be
changed from time to time; and

       WHEREAS; the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the time is right for
Riverton City to refresh its existing seal; and

        WHEREAS; the Riverton City Council finds and determines that a new Riverton City
Seal, enclosed as Exhibit A, is an appropriate improvement to the City’s existing Seal;


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The new Riverton City Seal, attached as Exhibit A is hereby adopted as the
          official Seal of Riverton City.

       2. The City’s adoption of a new City Seal terminates further use of the City Seal
          in effect before passage of this Ordinance. The City Manager is directed to
          inform staff to conduct future City business in a manner consistent with this
          Ordinance.

       3. This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 2nd day of
March 2021 by the following vote:
