Lt. Nelson stated they are looking to utilize technology and resources available to gather data
collected via reports and contacts with individuals to help utilize more efficient police work.
Other cities have moved to having analysts like this, including Salt Lake which pays an out-of-
state company for those services. There is an opportunity for a part-time person here to utilize
the current systems to provide this service, allowing the department to better focus their efforts
in the right place at the right times to prevent crime, as well as be able to properly identify
those committing crimes with the technology. This would not incur an increase in costs, as it is
using money currently available for a part-time position. This position would only be funded
