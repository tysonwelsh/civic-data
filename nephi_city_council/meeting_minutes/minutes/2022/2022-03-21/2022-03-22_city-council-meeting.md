                                       Nephi City
                                 Work Session Minutes
                                    March 22, 2022
                                        6:00 p.m.
                              Nephi City Council Chambers

1. The City Council toured the Hive recreational facility. The Juab School Board joined the
   council on the tour.

2. The council discussed the current policy of frontage improvement requirements for new
   construction and whether they were interested in changing the policy. After some
   discussion the council agreed to keep with the current policy but to define and codify
   what major renovation means.

3. City Administrator Seth Atkinson gave a presentation on how property taxes are
   calculated in Utah and collected for Nephi City. The council discussed some budget
   priorities and directed staff to not pursue a property tax increase and to fund the budget
   priorities, which included a drug task force officer and CPI wage increases, with excess
   sales tax revenue.

4.    The council reviewed and discussed the draft of the ID-2 Meat Packing Development
     Agreement. Jeremy Lynn representing the developer answered questions about the
     discharge and how the wastewater would be removed. He said that at most, 40 animals
     would be killed per day. Mayor Seely said that Rural Water was very impressed with
     the design and wanted to use it as a model for other communities.

5. The council reviewed the financial reports.

6. Mayor Seely opened the meeting for public comment. The following is a summary of the
   comments given:
      a. Michael P. Morgan spoke against the meat packing plant. He was concerned
         about the water consumption, the waste contaminates, the low-income jobs it
         would provide, and many other environmental issues.
      b. Terry Cook thanked the council for not agreeing to a property tax increase and
         said he supports the meat packing plant.
      c. Joel Cowan stated that he had a friend whose life was ruined because of drug
         use. He appreciated the council funding a drug task force officer and he would be
         willing to pay for it with a tax increase.
      d. Jeremy Lynn said that the meat packing plant would provide career type jobs and
         that Snow College was having a course related to butchering meat. He said that
         at Planning Commission meeting a local farmer had said how important a
         slaughterhouse could be for our community.
      e. Donald Ball asked if the water would be culinary or secondary water. The council
         answered that it would use culinary water.

7.   Mayor and council gave various updates.

8. Mayor Seely called to order a regular meeting. Councilor Ostler moved to convene into
     executive session to discuss real property and the character and competence of an
       individual. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
       unanimous vote.

   9. Meeting adjourned at 8:53 p.m.
