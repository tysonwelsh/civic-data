16. ADOPT A RESOLUTION FORMALLY ESTABLISHING AN AUDIT COMMITTEE
-Jeremy Walker, Clerk/Auditor

JEREMY WALKER, DIRECTOR OF FINANCIAL SERVICES - TO GIVE YOU A LITTLE
BIT OF BACKGROUND AS A DEFAULT, THE GOVERNING BOARD OF A LOCAL
GOVERNMENT AGENCY IS THE AUDIT COMMITTEE. SO THE COMMISSION THAT
CHOSE NOT TO DO AN AUDIT COMMITTEE STILL RETAINS THE RESPONSIBILITY
TO OVERSEE EXTERNAL AUDITS OR ANNUAL AUDITS BY AN INDEPENDENT CPA
FIRM AND BY STATE CODE, THE COMMISSIONERS ALSO HAVE RESPONSIBILITY TO
APPROVE THE SCOPE OF ANY PERFORMANCE AUDITS DONE IN THE COUNTY.
THAT SAID, THE ELECTED COUNTY AUDITOR BY STATUTE HAS A RESPONSIBILITY
TO OVERSEE AND PERFORM INTERNAL AUDITS. SO WHAT WE HAVE HERE IS KIND
OF THIS PARSING OUT OF RESPONSIBILITY SO WE HAVE EXTERNAL AUDIT AND
WE HAVE TWO PIECES OF AN INTERNAL AUDIT, PERFORMANCE AUDITING AND
FINANCIAL INTERNAL AUDITING AND MY EXPERIENCE IN THE STATE AUDITOR’S
OFFICE FOR 6 YEARS, OVERSEEING LOCAL GOVERNMENTS IS IN THIS
RESPONSIBILITY OF AUDIT COMMITTEES. THE DEFAULT VERSION OF IT, 99% OF
THE TIME BY ROUGHLY 1000 LOCAL GOVERNMENTS IN THE STATE IS NOT
REALLY WELL UNDERSTOOD AND OFTEN THOSE RESPONSIBILITIES JUST SIMPLY
GET DONE AT THE LAST SECOND AND OFTEN JUST NOT DONE AT ALL. WHAT THIS
AUDIT COMMITTEE DOES IS IT ARTICULATES SPECIFIC PEOPLE, SPECIFIC TIMING
AND SPECIFIC DUTIES THAT WILL HELP THE COMMISSION AND THE ELECTED
AUDITOR TO SHARE POWER IF YOU WILL, TO BRING UNDER ONE UMBRELLA
THOSE INTERNAL AND EXTERNAL AUDIT RESPONSIBILITIES. IN ADDITION TO
SHARING THOSE POWERS, BY GETTING REPRESENTATIVE FROM THE
COMMISSION, THE COUNTY TREASURER AND THE COUNTY AUDITOR, TO SIT ON
THIS BOARD SO EVERYONE GETS BUY-IN, TO ALSO COMPLY WITH ANOTHER
PART OF THE STATE CODE THAT REQUIRES THE COUNTY COMMISSION TO
INSTITUTE AN INTERNAL CONTROL PROCESS - THAT’S A VERY BROAD TERM BUT
AT THE STATE AUDITOR’S OFFICE IT’S BEEN INTERPRETED THAT MEANS
THERE’S A RELATIONSHIP WITH THAT AUDIT COMMITTEE AND ALSO WITH
INTERNAL AUDIT BUT BECAUSE AT THE COUNTY LEVEL, YOU HAVE THIS
STATUTORY INDEPENDENCE OF THE ELECTED AUDITOR, YOU HAVE THIS
DIVISION AND SO IT CREATES THE POTENTIAL FOR, WELL CERTAINLY IN OTHER
COUNTIES THERE HAVE BEEN STANDOFFS BETWEEN THOSE OFFICES ON
INTERNAL AUDIT ISSUES BUT THIS AUDIT COMMITTEE AND WHAT'S THE BEST
PRACTICE RECOMMENDED BY THE STATE AUDITOR’S OFFICE, IS TO BRING THOSE
PARTIES ALL TOGETHER TO HELP EVERYONE ACCOMPLISH THEIR STATE
STATUTORY RESPONSIBILITIES TO COORDINATE AND COOPERATE IN THIS
EFFORT. IT ISN’T A REQUIREMENT, BUT IT IS ARECOMMENDED BEST PRACTICE. I
RECOMMEND IT.
