                                RESOLUTION NO. CRA 23-02

       A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING THE COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT
       AGENCY OF DRAPER CITY TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL
       YEAR ENDING JUNE 30, 2024.

       WHEREAS, the Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City (“CRA”) is required
to adopt a tentative operating budget for each fiscal year; and

        WHEREAS, the approved tentative budget will be available for review by the public in
the City Recorders Office; and

        WHEREAS, the CRA Board of Directors will hold a public hearing to take public
comment regarding the tentative budget on May 16, 2023 where all persons will be heard, for or
against the budget; and

       WHEREAS, the CRA Board of Directors hereby finds this action to be in the best interest
of public’s health, safety and general welfare.


     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMUNITY
REINVESTMENT AGENCY OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1.    The CRA Board of Directors hereby adopts the Tentative Budget for Fiscal
Year ending June 30, 2024. The details of this budget are more specifically outlined in the
document entitled “Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City Tentative Budget For Fiscal
Year 2024” as incorporated herein, and established as the Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Tentative Budget.

        Section 2.     Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3.     Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
its passage.
