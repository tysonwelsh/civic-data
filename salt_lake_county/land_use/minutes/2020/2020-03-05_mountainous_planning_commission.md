(Continued from 02/06/2020) - Salt Lake County (SLCo) is considering adopting the proposed Wasatch 
Canyons General Plan (WCGP/Plan). The WCGP is a 20  to 30 year long-range plan for the areas within 
Parleys Canyon, Mill Creek Canyon, Big Cottonwood Canyon, Little Cottonwood Canyon, and adjacent 
unincorporated foothills. The Plan meets the required State laws for General Plans including the elements 
of Housing, Transportation and Land Use, along with additionally important topics of Recreation, 
Environment and Economy. Planner: Jake Young
