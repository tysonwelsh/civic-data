                             KEARNS CITY COUNCIL

     RESOLUTION NO. R2026-03                             DATE: January 26, 2026

    A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE ALTERNATE REPRESENTATIVE
 APPOINTEE OF THE CITY OF KEARNS TO THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF
     THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT OF GREATER SALT LAKE

       WHEREAS, the City of Kearns is a member entity of the Unified Police Department
of Greater Salt Lake and the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area; and

     WHEREAS, the Kearns City Council (the “Council”) may appoint a Council
Member to the Board of Directors of UPD (“UPD Board”); and

       WHEREAS, Jesse Valdez was appointed as the alternate board member on
January 12, 2026, with Patrick Schaeffer as the primary representative of Kearns on the
UPD Board of Directors; and

       WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint Lyndsay Longtin to replace Jesse
Valdez as Kearns’ alternate representative on the UPD Board.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

      1. The Council appoints Lyndsay Longtin as the alternate Kearns representative on
the UPD Board.

       2. Lyndsay Longtin and Kearns staff are authorized to provide UPD with a copy of
this resolution and to complete and send the attached letter announcing Lyndsay Longtin’s
appointment and notifying UPD of their contact information.
