                                 TOWN OF ALTA

                           RESOLUTION NO. 2021-R-18

     A RESOLUTION REPEALING AND REPLACING THE TOWN OF ALTA
     FEE SCHEDULE TO INCLUDE FEES FOR PERMITS TO USE TOWN-
                       MANAGED PARKING

       WHEREAS, the Town of Alta manages public parking in Alta, including
parking in areas designated for overnight use; and

        WHEREAS, the Town Council of the Town of Alta recently adopted Town of
Alta Ordinance 2021-O-7 Enacting Title 6 Chapter 3 Permit Parking, which
established a requirement to obtain a permit for use of Town-managed parking areas
and granted authority to establish parking permit fees to the Town Council, by
resolution; and

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. The Town of Alta Fee Schedule is hereby repealed and replaced with
Exhibit A attached.

       Section 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

APPROVED by the Town Council on the 13th day of October, 2021.

                                           By:


                                           MAYOR HARRIS SONDAK
ATTEST:



PIPER LEVER, TOWN CLERK
