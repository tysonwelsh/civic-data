                                            NEPHI CITY

North at 7:00 p.m. Tuesday, August 3, 2021.
Those present were:

                Council Member . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. Kent Jones (ABSENT)
                Council Member . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. Nathan Memmott
                Council Member . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Larry Ostler
                Council Member . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Justin D. Seely

Guests: Rebecca Dopp (Press), Donald W. Ball, Kurt Lainhart, L. Nyle Robinson, JD Parady, Jeff Hansen,
Jeremy Lynn, Jeramie Callaway, and Steven Jones.

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then offered a
prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

 The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Councilor Skip Worwood

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting held on 7-20-21, work session held on 7-20-21, work session held on 7-
27-21, regular council meeting held on 7-27-21, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 8-3-21.
Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

   City Recorder Lisa E. Brough reviewed the business license application for Mtn. Pass. Councilor
Worwood moved to approve the license. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.
   A business license application for Beal Rentals was reviewed and the council discussed that the
business address was in a residential zone but the equipment being rented would be stored at a farm in
the county and only book work would be done at the residence. Councilor Memmott moved to approve
 the business license application for Beal Rentals. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Kurt Lainhart addressed the council about his opposition to the sale of alcohol at public events. He
stated that he chose to live in Nephi because of its good family values.
  Steven Jones expressed his opposition also to alcohol sales at public events. He stated that he is a
newcomer to Nephi. He implored the leaders to protect our community values and if they allow the sale
of beer at special events, it is like “the camel’s nose in the tent”. He said it is fine for people that like to
drink beer to buy their beer a the grocery store and go home and drink.
   Jeff Hanse addressed the council about the problem of speeding on 1250 North. He stated that lines
needed to be painted on the road, a four-way stop installed on 200 East and maybe speed bumps
added.
   Nyle Robinson informed the council that Todd Robison had recently painted the old golf sign that had
been installed in 1983. He said the sign looks fantastic and now people from the freeway can see it.

ADDENDUM NO. 4 TO JENSON AGREEMENT EXTENDING DUE DILIGENCE PERIOD APPROVED AND FIRST
AMENDMENT TO PURCHASE AND SALE AND DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENTS TABLED:

  Travis Worwood, filling in for City Administrator Seth Atkinson, informed the council that upon review
of the closing documents for the Jenson sale, there were two LLCs listed: SES 103 and JLC. An
amendment to both the Jenson Purchase & Sale Agreement and the Jenson Development Agreement
was necessary to add those parties to the agreements. The amendment to the Development Agreement
adds SES as a party but does not release JLC from the agreement. The amendment to the Purchase &
Sale Agreement releases Jenson as a party and adds JLC and SES as parties.

  The agreement amendments were straightforward but the manager of the third party would like time
for his legal counsel to review them, therefore another extension was necessary. Councilor Memmott
moved to accept the Addendum No 4 an extension of 30 days. Councilor Worwood seconded the
motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.
   Councilor Memmott moved to table the approval of the first amendment to the agreements until
after the review. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

GENERAL PLAN UPDATE SURVEY DISCUSSED:

  Mr. Worwood reviewed with the council the survey for the general plan update that will be mailed
out with August’s utility bill and that is available to the public on the Nephi City website.

BRYCE’S LANDING FINAL PLAT APPROVAL REMOVED FROM AGENDA:

 Mayor Nielson removed the Bryce’s Landing final plat approval from the agenda.

MASTER FEE RESOLUTION 8-3-2021 BUILDING PERMIT FEE CHANGES APPROVED:

  Mr. Worwood updated the council that after reviewing Utah Code 110-9a-510-Limit on fees, staff
determined that the amount being charged for the plan review fee with the new building inspector
exceeded the reasonable cost of performing a plan check review. Staff researched other cities and
analyzed the actual time spent in plan review and administrative services and determined the proposed
fee schedule to be more in alignment with Utah Code.
   Councilor Seely moved to Adopt Resolution 08-04-21 with Exhibit A - a revised Master Fee Schedule.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR AND CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Mr. Worwood informed the council that the County Commissioners had approved the Bar W island
annexation and that now the annexation process can move forward.
  Mr. Worwood stated that the billboard redesign had cost $1000 less than originally quoted and that
Custom Signworks had done a wonderful job.
   Councilor Seely reported that the state legislature had combined the Department of Human
Resources and the Department of Health. He said that representatives from this new organization were
having a meeting on August 16. 2021. He extended an invitation to all the people in the room to attend
this unique opportunity to express our community’s needs.
   Mayor Nielson reported on a meeting with the Senate Confirmation Committee, where he was
appointed to the Community Impact Board. He will be attending the first meeting on Thursday. He said
that now two members from the Six County Association of Governments will be on the board.
   Mayor Nielson asked the council to be thinking about some meaningful service projects to help
support the September 11, 2021, event commemorating the twentieth anniversary of September 11
attacks.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to adjourn into executive session to acquisition and disposition of real
property and water shares. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 7:45 p.m.
