REQUEST
Jake Hill, with EDA Architects and Cary Dunn, Owner's Representative and Construction
Manager, are requesting that the Planning Commission consider a request for preliminary
Commercial Site Plan Review for a New Science Center Building and a Conditional Use
approval for a "School, Private or Quasi-Public" in the Professional Office (PO) zoning
district.  The new building also needs review of the proposed building architectural design,
materials and colors as required by the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards.  The
subject property is approximately 6.00 acres of the existing school campus located at 1480
E. 9400 S. in Sandy.   The Planning Commission is the land-use authority for these

BACKGROUND
The building site within the larger school campus, is currently occupied by landscaping,
sidewalk and an asphalt parking lot and is located in the southeast portion of the existing
Waterford School Campus.  The entire campus is in the Professional Office (PO) zoning
district and has been present at this location for many years.  Site plan review is necessary
to make a building expansion on the existing campus.  A Conditional Use Permit is required
for the expansion of a School, private or quasi-public in a PO Zone.  A Conditional Use Permit
also triggers the requirement for the City to hold a neighborhood meeting to gather
comments and input from the neighbors, prior to scheduling the matter for a decision by the
Planning Commission. The conditional use will be considered in a separate staff report on
this same agenda.

ANALYSIS

Zoning Review.  The site plan has been reviewed by all of the Sandy City Departments
and Divsions involved in development review and changes have been made to meet the
requirements of the various City Departments, particularly relative to fire truck access
and waterline relocations and extensions and stormwater detention requirements.  The
revised site plan and other site related drawings are now ready for preliminary approval
by the Planning Commission.

The use of the property is subject to Planning Commission approval of a Conditional Use
Permit, due to designation of such a school being a conditional use in the PO zoning
district.

Architectural Design Review.  The new 38,488 square foot, 32 foot high two story
science center building design has also been revised to better meet the requirements of
the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as to;  the screening of the roof top
mechanical units (RTU's) and vents; enclosing the outdoor stairway to be within the
building; and changes to the porportions of the building materials.  It has been deemed
to be impractical to raise the building parapet high enough to completely screen the RTUs
and vents. The architects are now proposing to corral the proposed units into a common
roof top screen wall enclosure.  This sceen wall will be seven (7) feet above the building
roof level and be made of white painted metal with a verigated surface.  The screen wall
will be set back from the building edge on all sides, which will help to reduce its visability
from ground level. (Please see the attached building elevation drawings and renderings
attached to this report.)

Requested exception.  Because this building is a science education center, there will be
laboratories within the building, some of which will require vent hoods and ducting to a
specified height above the roof top for safety.   There are three (3) vent stacks that will
protrude above the roof top screening wall by about four (4) feet.  These vent stacks will
be enclosed within the screen wall at their base, but will be higher than the screen wall.
Because these are necessary for safety, and are located in the approximate middle of the
roof, staff believes that they will be of minor visual impact from off-site.  Staff therefore
recommends that the Planning Commission grant this requested exception to allow the
extra height of the vent stacks above the screen wall.

The building's design, materials and colors have been reviewed by staff and will meet the
City's Architectural Design Standards for commercial development, with the above
requested exception being granted.

Vehicle Access.  Access to the site will remain the same as it currently is.  Even though
this new building is being added to the campus, enrollment will not increase due to its
construction.  Vehicle access, parking and on-site circulation will be adequate for this
development.

Pedestrian Access.  Pedestrian access to the campus will remain the same as it is now.
Pedestrian access arround the new building will be expanded with a system of walkways
and plazas as is shown on the site plan.  Pedestrian access will be improved and will be
adequate for this development.

City Departmental Reviews.  All of the reviewing City departments and agencies have
reviewed the proposed new site plan development.  All departments and agency redlines
and conditions will be incorporated into the Final site plan for approval by City staff.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan
review is complete for the proposed Waterford School Science Center Building project
and approve the requested exception for the three (3) vent stacks to protrude
approximately four (4) feet above the top of the proposed roof top equipment screen
wall, for the project located at 1480 E. 9400 S., based upon the following two findings
and the following six conditions:

FINDINGS.
        A.  That the proposed project will meet all the applicable requirements of the
            Sandy City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design
            Standards as to building architectural design, materials and colors.
        B.  That the requested exception to the Sandy City Architectural Design
            Standards for the extra height of the laboratory vent stacks is appropriate
            and meets the intent of the regulation by minimizing off-site visibility.

CONDITIONS.
    1.  That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff
        prior to the start of any construction. The final site plan shall comply with all
        Development Code requirements and those modifications required or allowed
        by the Planning Commission.

    2.  That the development complies with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life
        Safety Codes applicable to this type of use.

    3.  All utility boxes (i.e. transformers, switchgear, telephone, cable TV, etc.) shall
        be shown on the site plan and shall be placed underground or moved behind the
        front setback of the buildings and screened from view.  Each box shall be shown
        in its exact location and shall be noted with its exact height, width and length.
        Building utility meters shall be mounted to the side or rear elevation or
        screened.

    4.  That the developer be responsible for the placement of a temporary 6 foot high
        chain link fence around the perimeter of the project during the construction
        phase of the project for security. Said fence shall also be required to include
