                                      MINUTES OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
                                           REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY

                                      Wednesday, January 26, 2022 - 8:55 pm
                                           Waiting Formal Approval

                  West Jordan City Council Chambers • 8000 S Redwood Road • West Jordan, UT 84088


COUNCIL:      Chair Kayleen Whitelock, Vice-Chair Kelvin Green, Pamela Bloom (via Zoom), Zach Jacob,
              Christopher McConnehey, and David Pack (via Zoom)

STAFF:        Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson, Mayor Dirk Burton, Policy Analyst & Public Liaison
              Cassidy Hansen, City Administrator Korban Lee, Economic Development Director Chris
              Pengra, Council Office Clerk Cindy Quick, City Attorney Robert Wall


1. CALL TO ORDER
Acting Chair Whitelock called the meeting to order at 8:55 pm and read a Notice of Declaration
regarding meetings held electronically due to COVID-19, signed January 7, 2022 and valid for 30
days.

2. ELECTION OF CHAIR AND VICE CHAIR
     a. Consider Resolution No. 230 appointing agency officers

MOTION: Board Member Jacob moved to approve Resolution No. 230 appointing Kayleen
        Whitelock to serve as Chair and Kelvin Green to serve as Vice Chairperson of the
        Redevelopment Agency Board.
        Council Member McConnehey seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Board Chair Kayleen Whitelock               Yes
Vice Chairperson Kelvin Green               Yes
Board Member Pamela Bloom                   Yes
Board Member Zach Jacob                     Yes
Board Member Chris McConnehey               Yes
Board Member David Pack                     Yes
Board Member Melissa Worthen                absent
The motion passed 6-0

3. CLOSED MEETING
     a.    The Redevelopment Agency Board may temporarily recess the meeting to convene in a
           closed session to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property as provided
           by Utah Code Annotated §52-4-205
At 9:01 pm, Board Member McConnehey moved to convene in a closed session to discuss the
purchase, exchange, or lease of real property as provided by Utah Code Annotated §52-4-205
and adjourn from there.
Board Member Green seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
