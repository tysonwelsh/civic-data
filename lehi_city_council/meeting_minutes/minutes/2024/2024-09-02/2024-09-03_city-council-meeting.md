4:00 PM Work Session Meeting
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
   Stallings offered the opening comment.

3. Discussion Items

   2.a. Downtown Revitalization Plan Update and Survey Results- Community Development
Sam Taylor and Mike West reported that progress had been made by gathering analysis and
using the data to engage various groups. At that point, they were mid-project, with the next step
being to determine a preferred direction for the path. Y2K Analytics had conducted the survey
and gathered the results. Sam presented the key takeaways, noting that the area had historic
significance, served as a primary place to run errands, was family-friendly, and could benefit
from missing amenities. Concerns about traffic and parking were also raised, particularly
because the 100 East transit station was not viable in its current state. Principles of guidance and
alternative concept plans, such as a baseline, transportation solutions, distributed centers, and a
unified downtown concept, were presented. The questionnaire also provided public feedback.

Council Member Hancock clarified that there was no reason a hybrid of the concepts could not
be presented and inquired about parking and the walkability of the plans. Mayor Johnson asked
about connectivity from Center Street and possible adjustments for side and collector streets.
Paige Albrecht suggested waiting to see what ASD would do or letting the situation evolve.
Heather Newall expressed that it was crucial to bring more people into the area, especially with
the ASD property on Center Street. She also emphasized the importance of keeping residents
involved while pursuing commercial development in the downtown area.

   2.b. Discussion of Legislative Directive on Housing Affordability
Mayor Johnson emphasized the importance of legislative mandates affecting Lehi. He noted that
he had been vocal about prioritizing infrastructure before allowing building and growth,
specifically mentioning transportation, power, and water. The Mayor presented an audit
summary that included target housing needs, local land use regulations, statewide housing goals,
and the incentives and penalties for non-compliance, as well as the requirements and timelines
that concerned cities. He also addressed the issues created by HB 82 and discussed housing
approaches and financing tools that could be used.

Mike West gave a presentation on Moderate Housing Goals. He expressed these goals are
mandated reporting with the State and they must be altered if the goal is not being met.

Sierra Pierson presented on upcoming housing legislation and recent bills that had been passed.
She also touched on potential future legislation and bills that were already in place.

  2.c. Review of City Council and Planning Commission Roles- Legal Department
Ryan Wood led the discussion on the roles of the City Council and Planning Commission,
emphasizing the importance of each to act as two separate, independent bodies of government.
Ryan talked about best practices for providing comments and attending meetings, as well as what
was appropriate to discuss with the public and other Council Members and Commissioners. Mr.
Wood answered questions and listened to concerns from the City Council and Planning
Commission members.

    2.d. Dry Creek Reservoir Update and Discussion (City Council Only)
Cameron Boyle presented information on an updated plan for the Dry Creek Reservoir. Some
of these updates included:

• The entryway to the dam was removed and reconstructed.
• The bridge over the dam needed to be installed.
• Fish habitat became a primary focus for the reservoir.
• A fish cleaning station, restrooms, and handwashing stations were installed.
• A three-foot beach area with a trail was built and maintained.
