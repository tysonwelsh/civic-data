                          Nephi City Council Work Session Minutes
                                      October 25, 2022
                                         7:00 p.m.
                                        21 E. 100 N.
                                      Nephi, UT 84648


1. The Juab County Commissioners were unable to attend the meeting to discuss the
   Public Safety Complex, Travis Kenison from the Juab County Sheriff’s Office addressed
   the council and stated that there will be a meeting at the Jail on Wednesday, October
   25th with the architect that is creating the feasibility study for the Public Safety Complex.
   Chief Morgan, Lisa Brough and Councilor Nathan Memmott will attend the meeting to
   communicate the city’s interests for the feasibility study.

2.    Josie Bell addressed the council about her idea of bringing back roller skating to the
     city’s recreation programming. She said that she has fond memories of skating at the
     Old Gym in her childhood and wished that her children could have a similar experience.
     She had thought of several possible venues and thought maybe that the Juab County
     Multi-purpose building at the fairgrounds. Mayor Seely suggested that she talk to the
     county about using the building and maybe Nephi Recreation could help run the
     program.

3. City Administrator Seth Atkinsonreviewed with the council the draft of the Natural Gas
   Master Plan. T/he council directed staff to bring the plan back to the next regular council
   meeting for adoption.

4. Mr. Atkinson updated the council about a Rural Community Grant program that the state
   has recently introduced. The deadline for the grant applications is November 18th. The
   council decided to form a committee to review the applications.

5.    Mr. Atkinson talked to the council about entering into an agreement with Riley Park so
     that he can begin building before the amended plat for Cedar Ridge Phase B has been
     approved.

6.    Mr. Atkinson reported on the Land Use Institute conference that him, Shauna Talbot
     and Kasey Wright had attended earlier in the day. He said that most of the discussion
     had been about marrying land-use issues with water conservation.

7.   The City Council have the following updates:
       a. Hive Grand Opening scheduled for November 2, 2022.
       b. The City has been awarded a safety grant
       c. Mayor Seely expressed his appreciation to Lisa Oswald for her helping purchase
           the Halloween decorations.
       d. Councilor Worwood thanked Craig Oswald for the “no dogs allowed” signs in the
           cemetery.
       e. Councilor Parady reminded the council about the HalloweenTown trick or
           treating on Monday, Oct 31, 2022.
       f. Maverick ribbon cutting ceremony will be Thursday.
            g. Councilor Ostler reported on the Fire District Board meeting and said they are
               looking to purchase a new ladder truck.
            h. Councilor Ostler reported that Fire Chief Curtis Park is inspecting commercial
               properties for fire standards.
            i. The council agreed to help put up the Halloween decorations on Wednesday..
            j. The council agreed to add Gym Membership Fees to the wellness program
               benefit beginning next fiscal year.
