Arbor Day Celebration and Tree Planting

In attendance: Mayor, City Council and Executive Staff

Mayor Young spoke about gathering to celebrate Arbor Day and to plant three trees in the
City Center park. Orem City has had the Tree City USA distinction for the last 31 years. There
are four standards by the Arbor Day Foundation to qualify as a Tree City which are 1) maintain a
tree board or department, 2) have a community tree ordinance, 3) spend at least $2 per capita on
urban forestry - in Orem that is $200,000 and 4) celebrate Arbor Day. Orem City has more than
7000+ city owned trees. He thanked Josh Story, the Urban Forester, for all of his hard work.

Budget: CIP Presenter: Brandon Nelson, CPA, Finance Director and Taggart Bowen, Acting

Mr. Nelson introduced the Capital Improvement Projects (CIP) Budget. Mr. Bowen gave
a capital/CIP summary. The CIP fund $4,018,000, water fund $1,944,588, water reclamation
fund $4,165,650, storm water fund $2,040,645 and streets $4,970,378. The total is $17,139,261.
The CIP fund is made up of LWCF grant reimbursement ($412,000), CARE tax ($1,468,000)
and Lakeview Parkway funds (Fund 24, $500,000). The CIP funds will be used for the following
park improvement projects: cabin restoration (Heritage Park) $200,000, Center Street and 800
North landscaping (year 1 of 3) $450,000, Windsor park improvements $1,750,000, parks fiber
& security project (year 1 of 4) $50,000, bike skills trails park phase 3 $220,000, and parks
sidewalk leveling (year 1 of 4) $50,000. This totals $2,720,000.

Mr. Bowen explained the transportation projects of the CIP are the ongoing annual
transportation improvements $200,000, 1200 West/Center Street Intersection -MAG (year 3 of 3)
$50,000, Lakeview Parkway - MAG (year 3 of 3) $500,000, trail improvement fund $50,000,
and bike plan update (active transportation plan) $50,000. This total is $850,000. The last part of
the CIP is a library project to remodel the administrative area of the Orem Library for $448,000.

Mr. Bowen spoke about the building maintenance projects. These projects include bike
racks at public parks and facilities $5,000, recreation center diamond plate installation $7,000,
Biosolids building improvements $37,000, Orem senior center freezer condenser replacement
$13,000, new city center security cameras and locks $110,000 and fire station bay doors $70,000.
This total is $242,000. For ongoing funded projects in building maintenance department include
the recreation center snow melt system (year 1 Of 3) $20,000, SCERA pool locker room boilers
(year 1 of 3) $44,000, SCERA concrete deck replacement (year 1 of 3) $37,800, painting all
buildings (year 1 of 3) $35,000, public works roof replacement $138,850, and public works
rooftop HVAC unit replacement (year 4 of 4) $90,225. This totals $365,875 for ongoing funded
projects. That brings the total for all building maintenance projects to $607,875.

Mr. Bowen continued to water projects. These projects include the masterplan projects of
$1,894,588 and Center Street / 800 North landscaping (year | of 3) $50,000. For a total of
$1,944,588. Water reclamation projects include maintenance projects for $500,000 and master
plan projects for $3,665,650. This total is $4,165,650. Stormwater projects are for the master
plan stormwater projects for a total of $2,040,645, Street projects will include asphalt overlay
and reconstruction for $1,860,378, slurry seal $600,000, crack seal $375,000, microsurface
$500,000, trip hazard mitigation $250,000, misc street construction $640,000, street striping
$220,000 and concrete and ADA ramp upgrades $525.000. The total for street projects is
$4,970,378. Mr. Nelson explained how there are three revenue sources that pay for the roads,
BNC roads tax, sales tax and a new public transportation tax. He reminded the council that he
will be back in June to speak about the transfer of unused funds.

Public Hearing- Ordinance - City Manager Contract Presenter: Keri Rugg, Management

Services Director

Ms. Rugg is bringing this ordinance before the City Council for consideration. The
ordinance is to sign an employee agreement with the current City Manager, Brenn Bybee. She
gave a brief overview of his career here in the City of Orem. She spoke highly of Mr. Bybee as
the current City Manager. She highlighted some parts of the employee agreement. This
agreement does not change the appointed designation that is applied by the state code for all
executive leaders in the city. The City Manager shall serve at the pleasure of the City Council.
The City manager will be paid an initial salary of $200,000. City Council will determine all
adjustments to the salary. Benefits to the City Manager include a city vehicle, additional

PUBLIC HEARING — ORDINANCE -Amending Article 22-11-41 of the Orem City Code
to — an oplonal use of interior climate-centrolled storage units as a permitted use;

property located generally at 1385 West 1600 North from the HS zone to the PD28 zone;
and amending Appendix L by adding the concept plans and building elevations for the
Citadel Storage development as a permitted use in the PD-41 zone. Presenter: Jason Bench,

Assistant Development Service Director

Mr. Bench is representing the applicant for this zone change. This zone change will only
affect the area that is presented tonight, not other zones in the city. The amendment will allow
climate controlled storage units. There are two different concept plans for consideration. This
proposal is for indoor storage and the top floor will have office space. The building will appear
as an office building. The traffic will be light for the area. The proposal will include both concept
plans. The concept plan will depend on the easement with UDOT. The planning commission
recommended the City Council approve the ordinance for a zone change.

PUBLIC HEARING OPENS 7:00 PM

Mr. Norman Avery is the developer for this project. He confirmed that there will be landscaping
and they will use the office space on the top floor for the company's offices. He is excited to
improve that area. He worked with the city staff to come up with the concept plan. He explained
the business plan for the project.

PUBLIC HEARING CLOSED 7:07 PM

Ms. Spencer moved to approve by ordinance to Amending Article 22-11-41 of the Orem City
Code to include an optional use of interior climate-controlled storage units as a permitted use;
amending Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by rezoning the property
located generally at 1385 West 1600 North from the HS zone to the PD28 zone; and amending
Appendix L by adding the concept plans and building elevations for the Citadel Storage
development as a permitted use in the PD-41 zone. Seconded by Mr. Lambson. Those voting
yes: David Young, LaNae Millett, David Spencer, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald, Chris
Killpack, and Jenn Gale. The motion passed.
