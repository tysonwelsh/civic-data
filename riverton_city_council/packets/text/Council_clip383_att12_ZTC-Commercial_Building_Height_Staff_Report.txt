Subject:        Zone Text Change- Amendments to Title 18 Regarding Maximum Building
                Heights in Commercial and Industrial Zone



On August 13, 2020, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to consider this proposed
text change. A record of motion from that meeting is included below. The Planning Commission
recommended the following motion:

I move the City Council ADOPT Ordinance #20-20, amending Section 18.65, .70, .75, .80, .85,
.95, and .100 setting maximum commercial building heights as shown in Exhibit “A”, with the
stipulation that building heights adjacent to residential zones or uses be limited to a maximum of
thirty-five (35) feet in all commercial zones.


Background

This is a public hearing for a Zoning Text Change proposed by Riverton City. Riverton has
different maximum height requirements for each of the six commercial zones, and no maximum
height in the M-1 Zone.      Some of these maximum heights are listed with exceptions that allow
the building to be higher if next to a Regional Transportation Corridor. Some of the zones also
limits building heights by the number of building stories. The attached table shows each zone
with the Code section and current maximum height.

This proposed Zone text change would eliminate the bonus height allowed next to regional
transportation corridors, remove the ambiguous limitations of building stories, and add a
maximum building height for the M-1 Zone. No changes were proposed to the maximum heights
as they are currently defined in each chapter. However, the Planning Commission included in
their recommendation that commercial building heights adjacent to residential zones or uses be
limited to 35 feet in all zones. This would only affect the Commercial Downtown, Commercial
Regional, Planned Commercial Center, and M1 Zones, as all others have a maximum of 35 feet.
Staff would like feedback from the Council on the Commission’s recommendation. Additional
language may be necessary to refine that requirement, should the Council concur with the
Commission’s recommendation.
