MINUTES OF THE SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL WORK SESSION MEETING
 TUESDAY, MAY 19, 2020
 The City Council met in Work Session on Tuesday May 19, 2020, in
 an Electronic Meeting, pursuant to Salt Lake City Emergency
 Proclamation No. 2 of 2020 (2) (b) .
 In Attendance: Council Members Andrew Johnston, Amy Fowler, Chris
 Wharton, Daniel Dugan, Darin Mano, James Rogers, and Analia
 Valdemoros .

 Development Deputy Director; David Salazar, Human Resources

 Manager; Jodi Langford, Human Resources Acting Director; Allison
 Rowland, Council Public Policy Analyst; Jaysen Oldroyd, Senior
 City Attorney; Jonathan Pappasideris, Senior City Attorney; Lehua

 Tarbet, Council Senior Public Policy Analyst; Samantha Slark,
 Senior City Attorney; Lani Eggertsen-Goff, Housing & Neighborhood
 Development Director; Marcia White, Community & Neighborhoods
 Director; Brent Beck, Community & Neighborhoods Financial &
 Administrative Services Director; Jonathan Larsen, Transportation
 Director; Mike Brown, Police Chief; Tim Doubt, Assistant Police
 Chief; Shellie Dietrich, Police Financial Manager; Scott Mourtgos,
 Police Lieutenant; Michelle Hoon, Housing & Neighborhood
 Development Homeless Services Project Manager; Lisa Shaffer,
 Mayor' s Chief Administrative Officer; Amanda Lau, Council Public
 Engagement & Communications Specialist; Benjamin Luedtke, Council
 Policy Analyst; Katherine Lewis, City Attorney; Scott Crandall,
 Deputy City Recorder; and DeeDee Robinson, Deputy City Recorder.
 Councilmember Wharton presided at and conducted the meeting.

 AGENDA ITEMS
 #1 . 3:17:32PM UPDATE FROM THE ADMINISTRATION REGARDING THE
 MAYOR' S EMERGENCY DECLARATIONS RELATING TO COVID-19 (CORONAVIRUS)
 AND THE MARCH 18TH EARTHQUAKE IN THE SALT LAKE VALLEY. As part of
 the update, the Council may discuss public health and other public
 safety, policy and budget issues stemming from the emergency
 declarations. The Council may also receive information or updates

 from organizations or experts related to the emergency responses
 and coordination, including but not limited to earthquake damage
 to City, the functioning of the Emergency Operations Center (EOC) ,
 City response and aid, and the status of City buildings. View

 Mayor Mendenhall, Marcia White, Jonathan Larsen, Ben
 Kolendar, and Rachel Otto briefed the Council regarding Salt Lake
 City' s "Orange" COVID-19 status (limited to 20 persons in a group,
 schools remaining closed, limited out-of-state travel, etc. ) vs .
 County' s "Yellow" COVID-19 status (limited to 50 persons in a
 group, schools allowed to be open, team sports allowed, etc. ) ,
 street closures/reopening information, updates from the Salt Lake
 County Health Department, local businesses facing challenges
 regarding: customers wearing/not wearing masks, getting the
 correct information regarding current COVID-19 guidelines, and
 concerns with lower foot traffic possibly inviting more crime.
 #2 . 3:34:33PM BRIEFING REGARDING THE PROPOSED COMMUNITY AND
 NEIGHBORHOODS (CAN) DEPARTMENT BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2020-2021 .
 View Attachments
 Mayor Mendenhall, Allison Rowland, Marcia White, Brent Beck,
 Jonathan Larsen, Michelle Hoon, Lani Eggertsen-Goff, Rachel Otto,
 and Lisa Shaffer briefed the Council with attachments . Discussion
 was held regarding budgetary changes for FY 2021, overview of
 divisions in CAN, service changes regarding Advantage Services
 (supporting bio-cleanup on private property) , and Open Space
 Contract (trash pick-up in parks/Health Department abatements) ,
 Quarter-Cent availability for transportation/transit projects,
 concerns regarding policy issues surrounding revolving loan fund
 dollars, coordination with various partners for future shelter
 needs (for the winter) , funding for the Inn Between, and future
 follow-up on the following items : existing/new transportation
 investments, policy discussions regarding new Housing and
 Neighborhood Department (HAND) programs, updates on metrics for
 building fire plan for Homeless Resource Center (HRC) host
 neighborhoods, tracking of revolving loan program funds, holding
 accounts, traffic calming efforts, expectations for camp clean-
 ups, and funds moved from Funding Our Future to other programs .

 #4 . 5:32:57 PM BRIEFING REGARDING A GRANT APPLICATION REQUEST
 THAT WOULD FUND THE SALARY AND BENEFITS OF TEN (10) NEW POLICE
 OFFICER POSITIONS. The new officers would be assigned to the Patrol
 Division, and the Police Department would assign ten experienced
 officers to newly created, intelligence-led policing squads which
 would focus on addressing emerging violent crime issues and repeat
 violent crime offenders in Salt Lake City. View Attachments
 Benjamin Luedtke said the grant would be tracked/included in
 the expected mid-year budget amendment, the grant period starting
 in October, and hiring of officers could be delayed up to a year
 (flexibility built into the grant) .

 Lake Police Association compensation (merit increase eligible) ,
 Local 81 Firefighters (step increase range 6% to 23 . 5%, with
 paramedic incentive bonus plan) , and updated compensation
 philosophy.
