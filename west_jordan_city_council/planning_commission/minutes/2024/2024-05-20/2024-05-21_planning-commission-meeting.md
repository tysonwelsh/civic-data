2. Terraine West Bench Plat 4; 7400 South SR-111; Preliminary Subdivision Plat (65 lots on
18.402 acres) and Sub-area Plan; PCH/MDA Zone; Third Cadence/Gary Langston
(applicant) [#32176; parcel 20-28-200-009; 20-27-101-001-4002; 20-27-176-001]

Gary Langston, Third Cadence, stated that the proposed plat conforms to and is consistent with all the
adopted goals, objectives and policies of the PCH Zone and complies with the terms and provisions of
the Master Development Agreement and Master Development Plan. Plat 4 is a continuation of the
previously approved plats, which have adequate access from two connections to U-111 at 7400 South
and 7200 South. All areas have at least two accesses for fire response. Regarding public facilities,
water and sewer are provided by Kearns Improvement District for Plat 4. Storm drain, roads, and
streetlights are provided by West Jordan. Parks and open space that are labeled as P-lots are
maintained by the HOA, and those labeled TDOS (transfer development open space) are maintained by
West Jordan. In order to demonstrate they are compliant with the details in the different zoning areas,
they provided a Sub-area plan that outlines lot types and setbacks.
Ammon Allen asked how many plats there will be and where they are in the process of selling lots.
Gary Langston said the project is allowed about 3,000 units, which will be in about 25 plats. Plat 1 is
almost finished with paving, and this week they begin selling the first lots for model homes. Lots will
probably become available to sell to the public in September with the first homeowner occupying in
December or January. He explained the phasing, which is designed to provide inventory for their
homebuilders that follows an absorption rate of about two per month.
Ray McCandless said this plat contains 65 lots within Terraine West Bench that meets all code
requirements as explained.

Ammon Allen asked about the timing for when new water tanks will be needed for developing the

areas that will be serviced by West Jordan.

Gary Langston said the imminent plats 1 through 11 or 12 will be serviced by Kearns Improvement

District, which has water that is immediately available. It isn’t until they have about 1,000 units that

the homes will be serviced by West Jordan. When they get to that point, they will have to see how

many ERUs are available.

George Winn opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

George Winn said it appeared that all applicable criteria had been met.

Gonzalez and passed 6-0 in favor. Trish Hatch was absent.
