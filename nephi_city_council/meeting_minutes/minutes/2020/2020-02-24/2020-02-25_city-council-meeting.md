Meeting Minutes
Work Session 2-25-20
7:00 p.m.
Nephi City Council Chambers


	•	The City Council discussed some options for the gun range.  They provided direction to staff to begin the annexation process for that property and to bring back a fee schedule for consideration in a regular meeting.  

	•	 The City Attorney, Kasey Wright, proposed an alternative to approving business licenses and burial rights in a city council meeting.  The Council directed staff to bring back policies on both of those items for consideration in a regular meeting.  

	•	Mr. Atkinson requested feedback on a proposed policy for sick leave donations.

	•	Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council a state program called C-PACE that promotes energy efficient improvements in commercial buildings.  The Council directed staff to bring back a resolution and an agreement in a regular meeting for consideration.

	•	Mr. Atkinson presented a planning commission recommendation regarding road surfacing on RV Parks.  Staff was directed to bring an ordinance to Council for consideration.

	•	Staff and Council had updates on various city and code enforcement issues.

	•	Meeting adjourned at 9:30 pm.  




Present:  Mayor Pro Tem Justin Seely,  Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Nathan Memmott, Skip Worwood, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson
