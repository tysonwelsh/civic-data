Recognize Visiting Officials

Citizen Comments

             Mayor Clayton stated he met with Reece Stein of At Your Leisure, who wanted to
do a segment on Copperton Metro Township. The meeting was a little over a week ago. Scott
Crump, Copperton’s “de facto” historian, joined in the interview.

                                  ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦

Council Worksession

Copperton Law Enforcement Services

              Council Member Stitzer stated Gabby Saunders, who runs a survey business,
has agreed to do a door-to-door, sliding scale survey. Council Member Stitzer relayed the five
questions the Council decided to use. The final cost will be well within Copperton’s budget-
probably around $1,500. Ms. Saunders will be sending over a contract shortly.

Citizen Comment

                Mayor Clayton stated he recently had a lunch meeting with Representative
Burgess Owens to discuss the infrastructure project. Representative Owens has a budget for
projects in his area and the preference is that the funds go to rural areas.

Closed Session

             Council Member Stitzer, seconded by Council Member Severson, moved to close
the open session of the Copperton Metro Township Council meeting. The motion passed
unanimously.
