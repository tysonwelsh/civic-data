In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

   1. Chair Froerer: Today started our fourth edition of our Weed Board Training. There is a lot of learning

      engineered to eradicate yellow star thistles.

    7. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH MEDICI FOR AI RECORDING INTEGRATION AND SMART
       SEARCH.
         Bahy Rahimzadegan (County Recorder/Surveyor): This is a document AI system that will allow us to skip
         multiple steps that require multiple employees to do. The first time the employees see a document that
         comes through for recording, all the documents going to be filled out, all the text is going to be extracted. At
         that point there's going to be a level of confidence the AI has pulled the correct stuff. We're also getting an
         additional search tool online where you can do AI searches. People that are doing genealogy, they don't
         know how to search our system based on legal descriptions, parcel numbers, or anything like that. They can
         type in their grandma's name and, if it's been scanned through this program, anything can be searchable. It'll
         be like Google searching all your abstract data. We also have another agreement to create a system where
         each homeowner will be able to log in and get all their documents for free. This is a three-year frontloaded
         contract right now so we're not stuck with them if we ever want to pull away from them with our system that
         we created.
