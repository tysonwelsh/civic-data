City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1663

On July 1, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1663 amending the
official zoning map of Draper City for approximately 0.93 acres of property from RA1
(residential agricultural) to RA2 (residential agricultural) located at approximately
13232 S. 300 E., otherwise known as the Whittington Zoning Map Amendment. The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online
at https://www.draperutah.gov/
