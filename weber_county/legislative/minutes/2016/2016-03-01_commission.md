         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the contract with Wardell Brothers for the construction of the

        WESTINGHOUSE ELECTRIC COMPANY, PARCEL 1; DARIO & MARY COSTESSO, PARCEL 172
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the right-of-way contracts with Westinghouse Electric Company,

         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye
    5. APPROVAL OF PARTIAL RELEASE OF PROTECTIVE COVENANTS – WEBER INDUSTRIAL PARK
         Douglas Larsen, Weber County Economic Development Partnership Director, stated that a property
         owner wishes to develop the property, and in the process it was recently discovered that the boundaries
         and protective covenants also included properties beyond the Park’s boundaries. The following parcels
         should not be included within the Park’s protective covenants: 19-041-0046, 19-342-0001, 19-041-0024,
         19-041-0019, 19-041-0085, 19-041-0086, and 19-041-0021 (aka 19-045-0094).
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the partial release of protective covenants for parcels 19-041-
         0046, 19-342-0001, 19-041-0024, 19-041-0019, 19-041-0085 and 19-041-0086 and 19-041-0021;
         Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye
    6. CONTRACT WITH RECYCLING WASTE SOLUTIONS, LLC, (RWS) FOR TRANSFER STATION RECYCLING
         Sean Wilkinson, County Community and Economic Development Director, noted that the major change
         from the previous contract related to pricing. The provision for RWS to pay not less than $10 per ton for
         single-stream material was removed so that the County does not build a deficit if the price for the material
         drops below that amount. The price is now determined using the market rate as described in the contract.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the amendment to the contract with Recycling Waste Solutions,
         LLC, for transfer station recycling; Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye
    7. RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE WEBER COUNTY PRE-DISASTER MITIGATION PLAN – RESOLUTION 6-2016
         Lance Peterson, with County Emergency Management Services, noted that last year he had presented the
         draft plan, there was a 30-day public comment period for all cities and special taxing districts and no
         comments were received. The plan was sent to FEMA, which approved it, pending county approval.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 6-2016 adopting the Weber County Pre-Disaster
         Mitigation Plan; Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye
    8.   RESOLUTION APPOINTING NEW MEMBER TO WEBER COUNTY BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT – RESOLUTION 7-2016

         Rick Grover, County Planning Department Director, noted that three names had been recommended and
         he gave an overview of those members’ professional experience.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 7-2016 appointing Bryce Froerer as a fulltime member
         (term expiring 6/30/2020) and Neal Barker as an alternate member (term expiring 6/30/2017) to the
         Weber County Board of Adjustment; Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye
