45   3.2 Update on the Point Project- Alan Matheson
46   Alan Matheson, Executive Director at for the Point Project, gave an update on the development of
47   the old prison site. He said that they are proposing 740,000 housing units on 600 acres. They want
48   to create a 15 minute city where everyone can get to where there need to go by walking 15 minutes

49   or less. They are also planning for open space and trail connections. They are in the process of
50   conducting 8 or 9 supplemental studies to look at the key elements needed in this type of
51   community and understand the costs associated with that. He said that the transportation piece will
52   minimize the need and use of vehicles. They are also emphasizing sustainability with water, low
53   pollution, and energy, and how it's critical for quality of life in Utah.

85   7. Consideration of Ordinance #03-2021, the Myers’ Zone Change on 0.97-acres of property
86      located at 2269 North 600 West changing the zoning from R-1-12 to R-1-8.
87      Mrs. Myers stated that she would like to have a similar zone to the surrounding properties. She
88      stated that they originally pursued a Detached Accessory Dwelling Unit (DADU) to save
89      money, but they want to drop the DADU for now and pursue a flag lot. Mr. Myers stated that
90      they do not want to do both a flag lot and a DADU.
91
92       Mayor Pro Temp Southwick asked for public comments.
93
94       Kaelynn Osbourne was opposed to the zone change. She said that traffic will increase along
95       her property line and was concerned with safety. She stated that they shouldn’t be allowed to

 96       have a DADU and a flag lot. Ms. Osbourne also read a letter from James Cox stating that he
 97       was against the zone change.
 98
 99       The Council expressed concerns with the zone change, specifically noting that the DADU is
100       already approved.
101
102       Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to deny the zone change request with the findings that it
103                   doesn’t meet the goals or policies of the General Plan, and that the property isn’t
104                   conducive to a zone change. Councilor Hancock seconded the motion.
105
106                   Councilor Hancock suggested amending the motion to include that a DADU has
107                   been approved at this location.
