3. Everbrook Academy; 5301 West 7800 South; Preliminary Site Plan and Administrative
Conditional Use Permit for Daycare, general; P-C Zone; NLD Ranches, LLC/Allison
Morgan (applicant) [#29336, 29337; parcel 20-36-134-003]
Allison Morgan, applicant representing Learning Care Group, said this daycare is the premier daycare
concept with emphasis on STEAM curriculum. Classrooms and play areas are divided into age groups.
They have been working with staff to refine the site plan that has good circulation, parking, alignment
with the access to the residential road, and there is a small retaining wall for a smooth transition of
grade. Materials and elevations have visual interest. The play area is at the rear of the building and large
boulders behind the fence provide protection from vehicles. Typical hours are from 6:00 a.m. to 6:00
p.m. Monday through Friday for infants through school aged children.
Kent Shelton asked if there was a plan in place to prevent vehicles from stacking up and blocking
traffic on Ranches Loop Road.

4, Lacier Lash Rezone; 1897 West 7800 South; Rezone .22 acres from R-1-8 (Single-family
Residential 8,000 square foot lots) Zone to P-O (Professional Office) Zone; Lacier
Lash/Nhung Cao (applicant) [#29555; parcel 21-34-126-010]

Andrew Rojas and Nhung Cao represented the item. Andrew Rojas said that Ms. Cao currently has

another location for an eyelash extension salon. The subject property is in a commercial corridor along

7800 South and is next to other properties that are zoned for professional office uses. This property is

the last one in the commercial corridor to change zoning to be in line with business uses. He showed

photos of the existing structure, which was built as a residential home. He reviewed the concept site
plan that shows with minimal modifications the site will be well-suited for this type of business. There
is a wide driveway, but there is not a fire turnaround, which they hoped could be worked out with an
exception in a grandfathered situation. He pointed out the existing landscaped areas and noted a space
to the west that could be used to satisfy water retention requirements. There are examples of insurance
offices in the area that were formerly houses that were converted to commercial uses. He explained how
the property will be modified to meet accessibility requirements both inside and outside of the structure.

Converting this property will help to prevent activities that can occur in an isolated home.

Mr. Rojas presented photographs of a similar property in South Salt Lake that they converted from a

home to a salon, which they are using as an example for this property. In that location they were

allowed to use the existing driveway, but they upgraded the site and interior. At the pre-application
meeting, staff suggested that they work with the Ortega property next door for proper circulation. Mr.

Rojas thought could be problematic should that business leave, and they would be responsible for all of

the improvements. However, they were not totally against the idea. They would just like to have their

site stand alone at first. He stated that the request is in line with the general plan and will help to
beautify the city. He noted that a salon is a conditional use within the P-O zone, and he asked the

Commission if they could identify anything at this time that would keep them from getting that permit.

Ammon Allen explained the process that this zoning request will take.

Mark Forsythe said the property to the east was rezoned to P-O last year and at the same time the future

land use map was changed to professional office for this property. The zoning pattern in the area to the

north and east is P-O, and the properties to the north have been converted from homes into office uses.

The request is conducive to the area, and this is the last remaining single-family home along this stretch

of 7800 South. Impacts with utilities, if any, are minimal because there is no increase in density or

usage.
Staff recommended that the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City

Council for the proposed rezone.

Jay Thomas was concerned with emergency access and asked the fire marshal to comment.

Paul Brockbank, Fire Marshal, said his comments on the concept design were to show the exact

distances on the property. Once those are shown when the site plan is submitted, he will be able to

comment further. The fire code says dead ends may not be longer than 150 feet, and this property is
close to that mark. Emergency crews can reach the house itself from 7800 South, but the distance
applies to the rear yard as well because they may have to respond to vehicles behind the building. They
had talked to the applicant about connecting to the Ortega property to help solve circulation problems,
but the city cannot require it. He did not think that a grandfathered situation applies in this case because
they are asking for a change from single-family to professional office.

Duncan Murray agreed that any time there is an application that changes the zoning or requires a

conditional use permit or site plan then the requirement is to meet the code as of the date that the

application was filed.

Matt Quinney said that the application before them tonight is a rezoning request, and the site questions

will be addressed if the zoning is approved and when the site plan application is made.

Duncan Murray agreed that the issues dealing with the site plan are not relevant to the zone change

application.

Ammon Allen opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

Andrew Rojas said he is willing to work out the dead-end issue.

MOTION: _ Trish Hatch moved, based on the information and findings set forth in the staff
report and upon the evidence and explanations received today, to forward a
positive recommendation to the City Council to rezone 0.22 acres of property
located at 1897 West 7800 South from an R-1-8A zone to P-O zone. The motion was
seconded by Matt Quinney and passed 6-0 in favor. George Winn was absent.

Prior to the second, Matt Quinney clarified that they did address the criteria in the packet and found that

there are no issues with the criteria.
