   million,      totaling      $10.7 million            in the fund. The other part of the bill                       deals with         how      Salt Lake
   County       deals with           overflow     temporary          shelters.      Every        year the Conference                 of Mayors           must
   come up with           temporary           shelter     locations        otherwise       the State will         do it. The current              shelters
   will   flex. Mayor          Silvestrini       said they negotiated               some semblance                of local     control.       If the
   overflow       is well      managed,         then it will        not be a problem              for that city.       Council         Member            Jackson
   asked what happened                  if a facility      was identified.              Mayor     Silvestrini        said the payment               and
   maintenance           of it would          be taken care of by the State but other                        service       cost increases,           like law
   enforcement,          would        be borne     by the city. A city could                    not be forced         to have a temporary
   shelter     more than once every                four years.
