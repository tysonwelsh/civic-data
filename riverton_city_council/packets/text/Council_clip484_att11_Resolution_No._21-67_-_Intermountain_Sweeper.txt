                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 21-67

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE PURCHASE OF A NEW STREET SWEEPER
             FROM INTERMOUNTAIN SWEEPER COMPANY

      WHEREAS, Riverton City’s street sweeper will be up for replacement in Fiscal Year
2022/2023; and

      WHEREAS, by purchasing a new street sweeper now, the city would save between
$20,000 to $30,000 thousand dollars; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, by approving this purchase, Council will need to approve $241,852.00 in a
future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council approves the purchase of a new street sweeper from
          Intermountain Sweeper Company.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 14th day of
