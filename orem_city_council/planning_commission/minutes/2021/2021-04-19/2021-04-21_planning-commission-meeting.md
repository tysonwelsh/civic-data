Those present: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts and Ross Spencer,

Chair Cochran read the following: To minimize the potential spread of COVID-19, and as authorized by Utah
Governor Gary Herbert’s March 18, 2020 Executive Order, NOW THEREFORE, I Mickey Cochran, as Chairman
of the Orem City Planning Commission, make the following determinations:
       a. Under current conditions, conducting an Orem City Planning Commission meeting with an anchor
           location presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be present at the anchor
           location.
       b. This determination is based on the facts that 1) COVID-19 is a worldwide pandemic threatening the
           health and safety of the residents of Orem; 2) the State of Utah has determined that people should
           maintain social distancing of at least six feet to help mitigate the spread of COVID-19; and 3) the City of
           Orem cannot maintain appropriate social distancing if Planning Commission members and/or the public
           convene at the usual anchor location – the Orem City council Chambers.
       c. Pursuant to U.C.A. 52-4-207(5), this determination expires 30 days after the date that it is signed.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City Code.
Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Timp Retail Plaza Subdivision, and final plat approval of Timp
Retail Plaza 1st Supplemental located at 1400 South State Street in the HD-C zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Harding.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to introduce himself. Roger Dudley, Dudley & Associates, Inc. introduced himself
and had nothing more to add.

Chair Cochran then opened the public hearing and invited those who wanted to make a comment on this item to send
in their comments via chat.

When there were no comments/chats, Chair Cochran closed the public hearing and asked if the Planning Commission
had any more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did, he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Spencer moved to approve vacate Timp Retail Plaza Subdivision, and final plat
approval of Timp Retail Plaza 1st Supplemental located at 1400 South State Street in the HD-C zone. Ms. Bentley
seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts
and Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Cochran asked Mr. Harding to introduce Agenda Item 3.2:

Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Habitat for Humanity for the Planning Commission to approve the preliminary plat
of Habitat Restore Plat A with 2 lots located generally at 340 South Orem Boulevard in the C2 zone.

                                       Staff Presentation: The applicant is requesting this plat to adjust the property
                                       line between the two existing lots of record. Because the lots had not previously
                                       been platted, the City required a preliminary plat.

                                       For years, the property owner to the south has been neighborly and has allowed
                                       employees of the Habitat Restore to park near the border of the two properties
                                       on his side of the line. This property line adjustment is to transfer the ownership
                                       of that portion of the property that is already in use by Habitat employees to be
                                       owned by Habitat for Humanity.

Parking:
The Artist Corner is required to provide 9 stalls. They are providing 27 stalls. This number does not include the area
that will be sold to Habitat for Humanity. The Habitat Restore is providing 23 stalls. As the current City Code stands,
the Restore would normally be required to provide 29 stalls. However, the current number of stalls they are providing
is legal, non-conforming because the building was originally approved with 23 stalls. Since there is no addition to the
existing building proposed as part of this application, this property line change does not require the parking lot to
come up to current standards.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City Code.
Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the preliminary plat of Habitat Restore Plat A with 2 lots located
generally at 340 South Orem Boulevard in the C2 zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Harding.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to introduce himself. The applicant was not in attendance.

Chair Cochran closed the public meeting and asked if the Planning Commission had any more questions for the
applicant or staff. He then said at this location they are currently parking on gravel and asked if they would be required
to pave that parking lot. Mr. Harding said no, if it were required parking they would, but this is not required parking.
Mr. Cochran said the issue here is they do not have to put in the additional parking, and they will stay legal non-
conforming. Ms. Jensen asked if they do not expand the building, will they keep using the storage to the north. Will

Minutes: The Planning Commission reviewed the minutes from the previous meeting. Chair Cochran then called for
a motion and moved to approve the meeting minutes for April 7, 2021. Mr. Roberts seconded the motion. Those
voting aye: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts and Ross Spencer. The
motion passed unanimously.

Mr. Clark: It is with sadness that Staff and the Planning Commission wish Ms. Jensen good luck, as she will be
leaving the Commission. Ms. Jensen said this has been a wonderful experience, but she is moving out of the City
