In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: We are honored to have our Congressional representative
   Blake Moore with us today. You are welcome to join us on the dais. Nelson Mandela said, “It always seems
   impossible until it’s done.” I had an acquaintance that said, “The difficult takes a while, the impossible takes just
   a little longer.” In our lives we have a myriad of tasks whether they are home tasks, work tasks, church tasks,
   and personal fitness tasks. In our society, we are told you have to win, you have to win the contest, you have to
   be first. One of the great speed skaters of our time, Bonnie Blair said, “Winning doesn’t always mean you’re
   first. Winning means you’re doing better than you’ve done before.”
E. PUBLIC COMMENT:
   1. Leslie Stock, 24th Street, Ogden, UT: I bought land in Huntsville. I just watched a YouTube thing where
      developers are not being able to get water. The earth gives us a lot of snow and water every year. I heard 10
      years ago about a sustainable method called Earthships. It uses water from the sky to garden inside, instead of
      outside. I had challenges with my outside garden due to grasshoppers; chemicals and organic methods were
      not working. I am a big advocate for the sustainable living offered from Earthships. It makes sense to start
      building more this way. I recommend looking into Earthships.
F. PRESENTATION:
   1. Congressional Update.
      Congressman Blake Moore: It’s not easy to be in any type of political role so thank you for running things so
      well in Weber County. It’s truly an honor to serve where I grew up. It’s my fourth year in this seat where I
      have been elected to a leadership position in the House Republican Conference and I sit on the House Ways
      and Means Committee. One of the most specific things our team does that directly impacts the things we do
      here is the support of Hill AFB. We just passed in the House and sent to the Senate the 2025 National Defense
      Authorization Act with funding for the F-35 and Sentinel nuclear missile program. For the last 50 years we
      have been the nation’s central point for nuclear deterrence. We have a strong landing gear industry here and
      we are working to keep and bolster that operation. We were able to get an additional $30M for construction
      of hangars for F-35 sustainment. We need to continue with the A-10 divestiture. If we continue to bicker and
      not find our way forward, there will be a $4.3T tax increase. The reason I wanted to be on the Ways and Means
      Committee is because that is where we deal with this particular issue. I believe we need to focus on making
      sure we are not taxed into oblivion. We have seen positive growth for lower and middle income Americans
      with much needed relief coming from child tax credit and doubling the standard deductions. All of this is up
      for renegotiation next year. One of the three tax teams I am on in International Competiveness; that team
      focuses on not wanting to be pay taxes to foreign governments when those taxes should be paid here. I
      appreciate all the support and involvement.
