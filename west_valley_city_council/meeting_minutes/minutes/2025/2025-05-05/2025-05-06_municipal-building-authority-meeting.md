    A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

    ACCEPT PUBLIC COMMENT REGARDING THE TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR
    THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1, 2025 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2026
    Chairman Huynh stated a public hearing had been scheduled regarding the tentative budget
    for the Fiscal Year Commencing July 1, 2025 and Ending June 30, 2026.

    Chairman Huynh opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor or
    opposition, Chairman Huynh closed the Public Hearing.

    RESOLUTION 25-03: ADOPT A TENTATIVE BUDGET AND SET FORTH
    PROPOSED APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE SUPPORT OF THE MUNICIPAL
    BUILDING AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1,
    2025 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2026 AND SET JUNE 10, 2025 AS THE DATE FOR
    THE PUBLIC HEARING
    Chairman Huynh presented proposed Resolution 25-03 that would adopt a Tentative
    Budget and Set Forth Proposed Appropriations for the Support of the Municipal Building
    Authority for the Fiscal Year Commencing July 1, 2025 and Ending June 30, 2026 and Set
    June 10, 2025 as the Date for the Public Hearing.

    Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
    follows:

           The Municipal Building Authority will adopt a tentative budget that will be made
           available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s Recorder’s
           office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment, before the final
           adoption of this tentative budget for FY 2025-2026.

    Upon inquiry by Chairman Huynh there were no further questions from members of the
    Authority, and he called for a motion.

           Ms. Lang moved to approve Resolution 25-03.

           Mr. Nordfelt seconded the motion.

           A roll call vote was taken:

           Ms. Wood                      Yes
           Mr. Whetstone                 Yes
           Mr. Harmon                    Yes
