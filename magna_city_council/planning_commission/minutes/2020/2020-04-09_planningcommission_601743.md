31132 - Yusef Basmenji is requesting a Rezone Approval from A-20 (Agriculture) to M-2
(Manufacturing). Acreage: approx. 4.92 acres. Location: 2360 South 7200 West. Planner: Diana
Martinez

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Services Planner Diana
Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioner Elieson asked if estimated or projected use in the rezone. Ms. Martinez said material
storage for landscaping, they changed gears and made an application for rezone. Applicant is the
property owner and do not know if the tenant changed.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant Representative
Name: Bijan Fakhrieh
Address: 8704 Sugarloaf Drive, Cottonwood Heights
Comments: Mr. Fakhrieh said he is a potential tenant, letter of intent, all prior to Covid 19, cannot speak
to what the outcome is. Wanted for landscaping material, strange situation in West Jordan and growing
out and needed larger area. They don’t have a plan, based with the surrounding and rezoned for M-1 and
M-2.

Commissioner Elieson went through the list of projected prohibited, borderline issues on some prohibited.
Commissioners went over the lists Ms. Martinez provided. Concerned with stockyards, like livestock and
feed lot. Commissioner Richards asked if new development would need fencing. Ms. Martinez said would
need to do fencing and landscaping and conditional use before the board. Animal hospital could be
concerned, large animal would be in corals, separated by 201 and a school.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Elieson seconded that
motion.

No one from the public present to speak.

Commissioner Elieson motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Weight seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend approval on application #31132 to the Magna Metro Council as presented with
Staff Recommendations.
       Motion by: Commissioner Richards
       2nd by: Commissioner Elieson
       Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)

31131 - LCG Truck, LLC (Loriann Gabrielsen) is requesting a Conditional Use approval for a
Contractor’s Equipment Storage (Vehicles only). Acreage: approx. 2.16 acres. Location: 2167 South
Jenkins Park Lane. Zone: M-2. Planner: Diana Martinez

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Services Planner Diana
Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioner Richards said add condition if truck storage, trucks registered and legal. Commissioner
Weight said #8 condition to remove existing junk. Condition #9 would have to be relicensed and
registered. Far enough into the subdivision you can’t see it from the road, okay without fencing. At
Lyndon Jones, Lot 1 was required to add fencing. Commissioner Elieson said would prefer to see fencing,
and backup for security for equipment. Commissioner Cripps is worried about equipment moving beyond
the parameters of the lot. Commissioner Cripps would allow chain link or barbed wire. Commissioner
Richards said under regulations needs to be a solid, visual barrier. So far off the road, could go with
chain link.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: LCG Truck, LLC
Name: Loriann Gabrielsen
Address: 679 Draper Heights Way, Draper
Comments: Ms. Gabrielsen said she owns trucking company and parking her registered trucks there. She
said she has invested more than $100,000 and work put in. Purpose of a bond and if not going forward,
feels bad pin on her, others have not completed bonds, but should not be put on her.

Commissioner Lockwood asked what type of trucks. Ms. Gabrielsen said she has semi and trailers and
parking in the evening. Commissioner Cripps asked if difficulty with chain link fence. Ms. Gabrielsen said
yes, she has access roads for turnarounds, after this piece, apply for conditional use on the rest of the
property and park her trucks. Costly chain link like to incorporate the other piece. Commissioner Cripps
said would require at least the perimeter. Could postpone the center section barrier but require around
the property.

Speaker # 2: Property Owner
Name: Ben Mitchell
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Mitchell said he owns 10 acres. Purchased a year ago, property had four times garbage
then it does now. Met applicant and have spent a large sum of money cleaning 12 acres. Rollback taxes
are costly cutting in to industrial. Dealing with the acres, garbage is everywhere. Hard to put fencing in
and tear out $8,000 when combing the 10 and 2 acres, would fence east and west sides with visual side
fencing. Maybe bond or do not have to throw in that type of money to tear out.

Commissioner Elieson asked if 10 acres is fenced. Mr. Mitchell said west side is a pond and inaccessible,
barb wires down north and south sides, not fenced half of the 10 acres. Fence between Lyndon Jones.
Almost all is fenced now, except for the east side and most vulnerable spot. Commissioner Cripps asked if
possible to create a condition or bond and a time period. Ms. Martinez said could in the time period.
Commissioner Richards asked condition at the time to the other property is included for the fencing and
whole area is fenced. Mr. Mitchell said the plan is to fence across the road, far east side, everything is
virtually fenced. Back 10 acres is agricultural right now, goal once cleaned up over the next year.
Commissioner Richards said he thought the whole 10 acres was zoned M-2.

31103 – Ty Vranes requests approval of the proposed 6 Lot Centennial Place Subdivision Preliminary
Plat. Acreage: .77. Location: 7382 West 3500. Zone: R-2-6.5 (Medium Density Residential). Planner:
Todd A. Draper, AICP

Friedman provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Name: Ty Vranes
