public comment on the proposed Nephi South Commercial Park Annexation of approximately 73 acres
owned by Nephi City. Donald Ball questioned what was planned for the property being annexed. Mayor
Nielson said that good things, but he was not at liberty to say at this time.

The hearing closed at 7:05 p.m.

ANNEXATION AREA MAP CHANGE REQUESTS TABLED:

 Two property owners, west of the city, requested to be included as a Level 3 property in the city’s
annexation area map. The council had requested at the last meeting to have representatives from the
Planning Commission come and explain why they had recommended changing the Annexation Area
Map. Planning Commission Vice Chairman Karl Brough told the council that he thought changing the
Annexation Area Map provided a great opportunity for the council to study the issues of residential

growth. A lengthy discussion ensued with Planning Administrator Glenn Greenhalgh, Planning
Commission member Kent Heap, and Public Works Director Kyle Marchant all adding input of how
growth is coming and how we need to plan for orderly growth and to look at other growing communities
and how they have dealt with growth.
   City Administrator Seth Atkinson provided a detailed staff report stating the pros and cons of adding
property to our Annexation Area Map. Councilor Worwood and Councilor Ostler expressed their
approval of adding the property to the map. Councilor Jones expressed his opposition to adding the
property. Councilor Memmott thought that we should have a meeting with the County Commissioners

county. The council agreed to table the issue until the council could meet with the County.

TWO ANNEXATION PETITIONS APPROVED TO MOVE FORWARD:

   City Recorder Lisa Brough presented two annexation petitions to the council. The first petition was
for 156.18 acres of land located one mile south of the Four Mile/Hwy. 28 intersection near the south
water tank. The landowners were Craig Dansie and James Phillips. Councilor Worwood moved to allow
the Four Mile Annexation petition to move forward in the annexation process. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
   The second petition was for 57 acres located approximately 1300 South Main the fields to the west of
Hwy 28. The landowner is Nephi City. Councilor Memmott moved to allow Red Cliffs Commercial
annexation petition to move forward in the annexation process. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ANNEXATION AREA MAP LEVEL CHANGE REQUEST:

   Last year the Planning Commission recommended some additions to the city’s Annexation Area Map
near the north and south ends of the city. The City Council included those recommendations at the
time. On the north end, near the freeway exit, two property owners have requested a change in their
Level designation from a Level 3 to a Level 2. These property owners are industrial businesses that
would like to receive city services, specifically, water and power.
    Mr. Atkinson explained that the staff needed to look at these requests in depth to evaluate the
properties and make some initial projections on utility cost extensions. He also stated that there may be
one more property owner interested in a level change and he though it would give additional time to
see if that property owner would want to join in on the request. The council agreed to allow the staff to
study the issue further and to bring the analysis for discussion at the work session on January 19, 2021.

AWOS MAINTENANCE SERVICES AGREEEMENT APPROVED:
   Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that for the past three years, the city has used a company
 called DBT Transportation to monitor, test, and repair the municipal airport AWOS system. The AWOS
 system is the Automated Weather Observing System and provides pilots critical weather information.
 City Hall also has a link to view the information that is being sent to pilots. Equipment at the airport
 collects this weather information and compiles it for pilot use. DBT checks this equipment annually to
 ensure that it is in proper working order.
     Mr. Atkinson stated that the time had come to renew the agreement with DBT Transportation for
 these services. There has been a good working relationship with DBT, and staff does not see the need
 at this point to look for services elsewhere. The City Attorney had reviewed the agreement and his
 recommended changes were in the agreement. Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the mayor
 to sign the AWOS agreement with DBT Transportation. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
 motion passed on a unanimous vote.

 PARKS AND RECREATION MASTER PLAN ADOPTED:
