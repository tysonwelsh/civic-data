Unified Police Department (UPD)

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Chief Bryan Case stated there were four calls last month in the canyon. Fire
School will begin on April 14, 2023. Carbon monoxide calls are still coming in at residential
properties. With the amount of snowfall this year, there is a possibility of flooding. UFA crews are
getting information together for crews and the community regarding flood safety and high waters.

Salt Lake County Animal Services
Gary Bowen, Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council Representative, stated
legislation that would bring Animal Services under the control of the State Department of
Agriculture passed with an amendment to exclude Animal Services. The purpose of the bill is to
enforce procedures for euthanizing animals.

FEMA Flood Map
Steve Kuhlmeier, Project Manager, Public Works Engineering, delivered a
PowerPoint presentation on the Emigration Creek FEMA flood map revisions. He reviewed survey
and data collection, hydrologic and hydraulic analysis, and submission of an application for a letter
of map revision for the study area. The FEMA review process is anticipated to take 6 -12 months
with approval coming in 2024.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Flood Report
Mayor Smolka stated piles of sand will be placed at Ruth’s Diner and sandbags
will be delivered by April 5, 2023. A group of youth will help fill and stack sandbags for residents
to take. Residents are welcome to fill their own sandbags and take what they need. Flood control
is prioritizing areas and Emigration Canyon is low on the list. He encourages residents to clear
debris from the creek in their areas before the runoff begins. Dump vouchers are available for
residents that need to take debris to the landfill. There are also dumpsters located in the canyon.

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)
Council Member Brems stated there is a lot of discussions around police
services. An agreement is being drafted stating what direction the metro townships want to go in,
it is subject to a lot of input. The greatest concern is the uncertainty of the UPD officers.
Chief Wayne Dial stated Sheriff Rivera and the UPD Board want to move this
process along quickly with the transition being finished by July 2024.
Council Member Brems stated Sheriff Rivera will withdraw as CEO of UPD. The
metro township mayors will meet to discuss the situation further. Midvale, Millcreek, and Holladay
are working together to figure out what they will do moving forward.

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)
Council Member Pinon stated the Board continues to work on improving the
Seasonal Container Reservation Program (SCRP). For now, it will run SCRP like it has in the
past. Emigration Canyon is unable to participate in SCRP like other communities. There may be
a way to provide a dumpster at a central location that residents can use. WFWRD will be testing
an electric truck; however, they are expensive and probably will not be purchased at this time.
WFWRD updates its website with information on weather related pickup delays.
