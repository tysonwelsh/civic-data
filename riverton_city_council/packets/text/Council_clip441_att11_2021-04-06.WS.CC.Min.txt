 3             Ranked Choice Voting Discussion - David Brickey, City Manager
 4
 5   Mayor Staggs explained that the council needed to determine whether they wanted to
 6   continue to move forward with Ranked Choice Voting. Mayor said that he would like to see
 7   citizens weigh in on this issue and would rather wait so that they could get a response from
 8   citizens.
 9
10   Councilmember Wells said that we do not know what the software cost would be because the
11   $25,000 cost is spread out among all of the cities. He said that his number one concern was
12   whether Salt Lake County would be ready to do ranked choice voting. He said that now that
13   they are ready, he is okay with choosing ranked choice voting. He said that he is hesitant as
14   to whether they would be able to educate the public because people don’t understand ranked
15   choice voting. He asked what the plan was going to be to educate citizens.
16
17   Councilmember Buroker said that cost of the software would be split among all cities
18   participating in ranked choice voting. She believes that the $200,000 that has been set aside
19   by the Lieutenant Governor’s Office would be a significant media campaign to educate
20   citizens.
21
22   Councilmember McDougal said that he believes that the longer time frame there is to educate
23   the public the better.
24
25   Councilmember Stewart said that the one thing he feels strongly about is changing the way
26   someone has the right to vote. He thinks this decision should be made by the citizens and not
27   the city council. He said that he would like to see a path that would allow the citizens to make
28   this decision and to possibly put this on the ballot in the fall.
29
30   Councilmember McCay said that she supports ranked choice voting and feels the state can
31   educate the voters.
32
33   Councilmember Wells said he believes that the cities choosing ranked choice voting could
34   work together to put out the information.
35
36   Mayor Staggs said that he thinks the decision to do ranked choice voting will place more
37   responsibility on staff but would like staff to know as soon as possible if the council would like to
38   continue moving forward with ranked choice voting.
39
40   Councilmember Buroker said she doesn’t want residents to get worried because it is a pilot
41   program. She said that this method of voting is not set in stone for future elections. She said
42   that after the election was over, residents would be surveyed and would then determine if they
43   wanted to continue using ranked choice voting. Councilmember Buroker absolutely supports
44   using Salt Lake County to run the elections.
