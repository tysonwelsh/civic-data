      RESOLUTION 20-105: AUTHORIZE THE EXECUTION OF AN AMENDMENT
      TO A SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH THE COMMUNITY EDUCATION
      PARTNERSHIP OF WEST VALLEY CITY, INC.
      Wayne Pyle, City Manager, and Councilmember Buhler presented proposed Resolution
      20-105 that would authorize the execution of an Amendment to a Service Agreement with the
      Community Education Partnership of West Valley City, Inc.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
      follows:

             This amendment provides a total funding commitment of $90,000 to CEP in 2021
             through 2025.

             CEP provides a wide variety of afterschool and out-of-school programming for
             school aged children in West Valley City. These programs have been a positive
             force in the community and have reached numerous families throughout the City.
             The City has supported these efforts financially for several years and CEP has been
             able to leverage funding provided to secure matching grants and additional revenue
             sources. To continue to receive these funds, CEP requires additional assistance.

             The amendment provides for a maximum annual payment of $90,000 for 2021
             through 2025, with previously allocated funds counting as the 2021 payment. This
             replaces a commitment of $35,000 annually that was approved by the Council under
             the original agreement.

Councilmember Huynh arrived at 4:33 PM.

      The City Council will consider Resolution 20-105 at the Regular Council Meeting
      scheduled September 22, 2020 at 6:30 P.M.

COMMUNICATIONS
A.  OPEN SPACE REQUIREMENTS IN RESIDENTIAL AREAS
    Steve Pastorik, CED, presented a PowerPoint presentation summarized as follows:
           - Multi-Unit Residential
                   o “At least 15% of a multi-unit residential Development’s total
                      area shall be useable open space.”
                   o “Useable open space shall mean recreation areas including
                      basketball, tennis or racquetball courts; baseball, softball or
                      soccer fields; trails; picnic areas; plazas; dedicated or private
                      park sites or any other use deemed acceptable by the Planning
                      Commission. Useable open space shall not include parking lots,
                      all buildings except clubhouses, setback areas less than 20', and
                      narrow landscaped strips.”
           - Planned Unit Developments
                   o “Open space means landscaped areas that are not occupied by
                      buildings, structures, parking areas, streets or alleys and are
                      devoted to recreation use or preservation of natural features.”
