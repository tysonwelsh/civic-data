CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION
C-10-2020 Petition by Mele Tile & Natural Stone Co., LLC requesting conditional use
          approval for outside storage. The property is located at 3650 W 2150 S on .43
          acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497)

Discussion:     Jody explained the subject property had a new business license review that included a
                site inspection. This review showed that the subject property needed outside clean-up,
                improved landscaping, and issues with the building that needs to be fixed. Jody then
                explained the applicant’s plans to improve the building and landscaping have been
                delayed due to current events. She explained the applicant is looking to put the outside
                storage in the back of the building and paving that area, taking down the sign on the
                front of the business, maintaining the façade of the building, and relocating the
                dumpster to behind the subject property.

                Jody explained the City did not start an enforcement case on the subject property, they
                are processing the applicant’s business license and will work with the applicant on
                current violations as part of this application.
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:47 p.m.
