                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    ORDINANCE NO. 23-12

     AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE SECTIONS 3.10.020
                     [SALE OF CITY PROPERTY]

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-8-2 (1)(a)(iii), the Riverton City Council
may “purchase, receive, hold, sell, lease, convey, and dispose of real and personal property for the
benefit of the municipality, whether the property is within or without the municipality’s corporate
boundaries, if the action is in the public interest and complies with other law.”

      WHEREAS, pursuant to statute, the Riverton City Council has adopted Riverton
Municipal Code Title 3, Chapter 10, to set governing standards by which parcels of real property
may be purchased or sold by Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, existing Riverton Municipal Code Section 3.10.020 contains standards for
notifying the public and allowing for public comment prior to the sale of real property; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that in the 2023 session of
the Utah Legislature, said Legislature adopted Senate Bill 43 which was signed into law by
Governor Cox; and

      WHEREAS, Senate Bill 43 updated noticing requirements for purchasing property by
municipalities, thereby creating inconsistent standards when SB 43 is compared to Riverton City
Code Section 3.10.020; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that updating Riverton City
Code to match new noticing requirements found in SB 43 will enable efficient governance and
avoid public confusion during the course of selling real property;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

Section 1.     Riverton City Code Section 3.10.020 is hereby amended to read as
               follows:

               3.10.020 Sale of real property - Surplus property list

               (1) There is hereby created a list to be known as the surplus real property list, the
               same to be maintained by the mayor or designee, upon which are specifically or
               categorically described items of real property which the city council, by
               resolution, has approved for sale, trade, encumbrance or other action divesting
               Riverton City of an ownership interest.

               (2) Before a significant parcel of real property is placed on the surplus property
               list, reasonable notice of the proposed disposition shall be provided at least 14
               days in advance to allow an opportunity for public comment on the proposed
               disposition. For purposes of this subsection:
                                                 1

Section 2.   This ordinance shall take effect immediately.

           the opportunity for public comment.
