       FUTURE LAND USE MAP TO REDESIGNATE PARKS/RECREATION AREA TO MEDIUM-TO-
       LARGE RESIDENTIAL RELATED TO THE WINSTON PARK DEVELOPMENT.
         Charlie Ewert (Planning): Resolution to change the General Plan to change Park and Open Sopace
         designation to large residential ... And there is a Development Agreement for Winston Park that includes
         $7500 per lot and a 12 foot wide trial easement to ... There is an Ordinance to rezone from A-1 to R1-15
         zone. ...

         Wade Rumsey, Idaho Falls, ID: As we explained with the park donation, we prefer the $5k per lot. We hope
         you would take that under consideration. We ... think we can make that land easement happen for... Thank
         you.

         Commissioner Bolos: I asked about outreach to the property owners bordering what should be the park.

         Mr. Ewert: The applicant has reached out to some of the owners. The CCRs for Phase 1 contained language
         that adjacent properties in future phases would be developed.

         Commissioner Bolos: I am not going to agree until all property owners are notified.

         Commissioner Froerer: I know the developers had an agreement with the Park District for the $5k per lot.
         Any ... ?

         Mr. Ewert: One caviat to the $7500 per lot is if they build less than 1k square feet...

         Chair Harvey: Trail access but not a road?

         Mr. Ewert: Yes, a road

         Chair Harvey: I need three things for approval. All neighbors notified. %7500 per lot. And road access to
         the west.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 39-2024 amending the Western Weber General Plan's
         Future Land Use Map to redesignate Parks/Recreation area to Medium-to-Large Residential related to the
         Winston Park Development; no second.
         Commissioner Bolos motion to table until time uncertain based on three items neighbors satisfied, road access
         to the west, and $7500 per lot for parks; seconded
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - no
