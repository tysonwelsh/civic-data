A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION ZT-10-2020,
     FILED BY WEST VALLEY CITY, REQUESTING A ZONE TEXT
     CHANGE TO ALLOW OUTDOOR EVENT CENTERS IN CONJUNCTION
     WITH A REGIONAL SHOPPING MALL AS A CONDITIONAL USE IN
     THE C-2 ZONE
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled February 9, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear
     and consider public comments regarding Application ZT-10-2020, filed by West
     Valley City, requesting a Zone Text Change to allow outdoor event centers in
     conjunction with a regional shopping mall as a Conditional Use in the C-2 Zone.

        Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
        information as follows:

           The Valley Fair Mall has held or is planning on holding outdoor events that fall
           into one of the two following categories:

                  Third Party Vendor Events - these events are primarily hosted,
                   organized, and operated by third party vendors. Examples of these
                   events include car shows, breast cancer awareness, Bubble Bash, and
                   friends for sight.
MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING FEBRUARY 9, 2021
                       -3-

             Valley Fair Mall Sponsored Community Events - these events are
              concert series, outdoor movie nights, festivals, fairs, and multiple
              vendor booths. These are typically between 200-500 people.

      The mall is in the process of remodeling the outdoor plaza west of the main
      mall building. In addition to making the plaza a more inviting place for patrons
      to linger, the remodel would also facilitate small, outdoor events.

      The proposed code change would allow the mall to apply for a conditional use
      permit to receive approval to hold the outdoor, sponsored, community events
      like concerts. Through the conditional use process, the Planning Commission
      can impose conditions on items like hours of operation and the place of sound
      emitting equipment to minimize impacts on adjoining properties.

   Mayor Bigelow opened the Public Hearing.

   Casey Bulkley stated that there are several events planned that the mall and
   community would benefit from significantly. He noted that he will work with the
   City and the intent is not to make a concert hall of Valley Fair Mall.

   Jim Vesock stated that he is in favor of this proposal as long as it doesn’t grow large
   enough to effect the neighborhood.

   Mayor Bigelow closed the Public Hearing.

B.      ACCEPT PUBLIC COMMENT REGARDING APPLICATION GPZ-8-2020,
        FILED BY WEST VALLEY CITY, REQUESTING A GENERAL PLAN
        CHANGE FROM VERY HIGH DENSITY RESIDENTIAL (OVER 20
        UNITS/ACRE) TO HIGH DENSITY RESIDENTIAL (12 TO 20
        UNITS/ACRE) AND A ZONE CHANGE FROM CC (CITY CENTER) TO
        RM (MULTIPLE UNIT DWELLING RESIDENTIAL) AND R-2-8 (TWO
        UNIT DWELLING RESIDENTIAL, MINIMUM LOT SIZE 8,000 SQUARE
        FEET) FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT 3060 WEST 3650 SOUTH ON 6.73
        ACRES
        Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
        Council Meeting scheduled February 9, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear
        and consider public comments regarding application GPZ-8-2020, Filed by West
        Valley City, Requesting a General Plan Change from Very High Density Residential
        (Over 20 Units/Acre) to High Density Residential (12 to 20 Units/Acre) and a Zone
        Change from CC (City Center) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) and R-2-
        8 (Two Unit Dwelling Residential, Minimum Lot Size 8,000 Square Feet) for Property
        Located at 3060 West 3650 South on 6.73 Acres.

        Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
        information as follows:

           The City initiated the General Plan/zone change application (GPZ-1-2016) that
           was approved back in 2016 for the CC zone and the very high density residential
           General Plan designation. At the time, the County Housing Authority was
           proposing to redevelop the Valley Fair Apartments along with the duplexes to
           the north assuming the properties could be acquired by the City similar to other
           properties within Fairbourne Station. The General Plan/zone change application
           approval was granted subject to a development agreement. While the City
           Council approved the development agreement, it was not signed by the County
           Housing Authority. At the time, the County Housing Authority was unsure that
           project financing could be secured. For the information of the City Council,
           staff no longer takes a development agreement to the City Council without the
           appropriate signatures.
MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING FEBRUARY 9, 2021
                       -5-

      While Housing Connect is still interested in redeveloping the Valley Fair
      Apartments given the age of the units and Housing Connect’s desire to provide
      more units, the design will be different from the concept forwarded in the 2016
      development agreement. This is due in part to the duplex properties no longer
      being considered as part of the project. Since the current zoning and General
      Plan designation were put in place subject to a development agreement that is
      not valid, City staff is recommending that the General Plan and zoning be
      changed back to what they were before the change in 2016.

   Mayor Bigelow opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
   or opposition, Mayor Bigelow closed the Public Hearing.

Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 21-25 that would ratify the City Manager's
appointment of Coby Wilson as City Engineer.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Coby has been employed with West Valley City since 2012. His current position
       is Assistant City Engineer over project design and construction.

       Coby is a licensed Professional Engineer in the State of Utah and meets all
       qualifications and requirements of this position. His previous experience as a
       consulting engineer and land developer combined with his City experience have
       prepared him well for this new position. He is imminently qualified and able to
       perform all the duties of the City Engineer.

       admirably served as City Engineer since 2007. Dan’s new position will be
