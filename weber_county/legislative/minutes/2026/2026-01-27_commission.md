    2. Public hearing to take input on the compensation increases of certain executive county officers included in
       the 2026 Operating and Capital Budgets.

         Steffani Ebert (County Comptroller): The 2026 approved budget included funding of 1.5% of each
                 department’s personnel budget to be allocated to employees in 2026 as part of the County’s pay for
                 performance program. We are required by state law to hold a public hearing prior to the adoption of
                 our budget if the proposed budget includes compensation increases for executive county offices,
                 which would be elected officials, department heads, chief deputies, division directors, and assistant
                 directors. We held this hearing in November 2025 and presented the salary of each of these
                 executive county officers with a pay for performance increase of 1.5%.

                  In the month of January, each of the elected officials and department heads and managers are tasked
                  with evaluating their staff and allocating the budgeted funds to the employees, based on the
                  employees’ performance ratings. Because the public hearing we are required to have took place
                  before these performance ratings were completed, there is a potential that these executive county
                  officers may exceed the 1.5% that was estimated during the budget process.

                  We did have a couple of our departments award two executive county officers with proposed
                  increases greater increase than 1.5%. Our personal property division administrator in the County
                  Assessor’s Office and our assistant human resources director were set to receive annual pay-for-
                  performance increases of $1716 and $1683 and they are proposed to receive increases of $2475
                  (2.15%) and $1789 (1.60%).
                  USC 17-66-401 states that: Before a county legislative body may adopt a final budget or a final
                  amended budget that includes a compensation increase for an executive county officer, the county
                  legislative body shall:
                   (i) hold a public hearing on the compensation increase;
                  Each of these employees’ proposed increases are within the budget set for these departments, so no
                  budget amendment is necessary. However, to make sure we are complying with state law, we are
                  holding this public hearing due to the compensation increase for these two county officers being
                  greater than what was previously presented.

                  In consultation with our County Attorney’s Office, because we are not amending the budget, I don’t
                  believe any action is required on the part of the commission.

         Jared Preisler (County Assessor): One of the employees is my employee. I am very much appreciative of
         this process. I wanted to remind the Commissioners in this particular role as our personal property director
         she actually functions in two areas. She represents us with MCAT and is responsible for the valuation of all
         the telecom in the state, not just in Weber County; Weber County gets compensated for that service. So part
         of her increase came from us evaluating her role and the revenue that is brought in directly from that MCAT
         contract. I just wanted to say thank you for the opportunity for following the process. We really appreciate
         her and her professionalism and representing the county and helping the entire state with the telecom
         valuations as well.
