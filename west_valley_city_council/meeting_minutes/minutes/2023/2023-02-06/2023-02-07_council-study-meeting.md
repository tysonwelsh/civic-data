      B.     FORM OF GOVERNMENT PRESENTATION
             Eric Bunderson, City Attorney, explained the process for changing the Form of
             Government. He indicated that a resolution must be passed by the Council before
             August 23, 2023 and one of the 4 Forms of Government must be selected:
                    - Council-Mayor/ 5 Council Members
                    - Council- Mayor/ 7 Council Members

          - 6 Member Council
          - 5 Member Council
  Eric stated that 2 public hearings must be held within 45 days of the passing of the
  resolution and the resolution must pass at least 75 days before the election of
  November 7, 2023. Eric detailed how each Council term would be impacted
  depending on what Form of Government was selected. He noted that if the proposed
  change of government is not approved by the voters, it cannot be placed on an
  agenda again until 2027.

  Councilmember Harmon asked what information would be included in the
  resolution. Eric replied the type of government, the positions and dates, etc.
  Councilmember Huynh stated that if the 7-person form of government were
  approved, the current Council terms would not be interrupted and an additional
  Councilmember could be appointed at large. Councilmember Huynh verified the
  current terms of office.

  Wayne Pyle, City Manager, presented a PowerPoint presentation summarized as
  follows:
         - The various forms of government have subtle advantages and
           disadvantages, but the main differences are seen between council
           manager and mayor council forms. These are described in Utah State
           code as mayor-council form or simply council form (with 5 or 6
           members). For simplicity, we’ll limit pros and cons to the general
           characteristics of the two forms.
         - Forms
               o Council-Manager Form
                        Council/Mayor- Set all policy together as a City Council
                        City Manager- Administers Council Policy via
                           Operations
               o Council- Mayor Form
                        Council- Sets policy legislatively via budget and
                           ordinance
                        Mayor- Sets policy administratively and implements
                           operations
                                CAO
         - Council Manager Form
               o Advantages
                        Effectiveness – appointed managers are professionally
                           trained and experienced as a CEO/CAO. Staffs working
                           for the manager are typically longer-tenured and also
                           professionally trained and experienced. Council policy,
MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – FEBRUARY 7, 2023

                        vision and initiatives are better executed in this
                        environment.
                     Stability – council and mayor are united
                        organizationally by statute and therefore more able to
                        formulate effective policy directive togther. This
                        stability influences the “personality” of the city and
                        carries across generations of administrations as long-
                        term policy needs are then executed.
                     Politically Representative – council and mayor as equal
                        members represent the electorate together. Once a
                        decision has been made by the council legislatively, the
                        manager complements this dynamic by recognizing that
                        the decision made by City Council is representative of
                        the whole.
                     Cost – this form naturally limits staff as the mayor and
                        council are one legislative entity and direct the
                        management staff by policy including budget. When the
                        council and mayor are separated into two entities, there
                        is a natural tendency to duplicate functions for both.
                        Administrative, legal and advisory positions multiply.
             o Disadvantages
                     Confusion– Citizens, outside elected officials and other
                        external organizations may be confused on the subject of
                        who to work with appropriately for given needs.
                        Traditionally mayors are seen as the executive branch
                        separate from the council legislative branch, and council-
                        manager form has to be repeatedly educated to those we
                        work with.
                     Perception – A common suggested disadvantage is that
                        a city manager is too powerful in this form. This is an
                        unfounded fear based on the actual structure of the form
                        wherein the elected officials and the city manager have
                        checks and balances that limit such possibilities.
       -   Mayor Council Form
             o Advantages
                     Reverse – of Council Manager disadvantages.
                     Traditional – the citizenry expects a more direct
                        relationship to their elected mayor.
                     Regional policymaking – peer-to-peer relationships are
                        facilitated by mayor representing city in external
                        organizations.

                 o Disadvantages
                       Reverse – of Council Manager advantages.
                       Policy Accountability – when a mayor leads the
                         executive branch of a municipality and is not part of the
                         legislative branch, the mayor can potentially become the
                         singular focus of policy discussion. The council then has
                         difficulty participating in or steering the policy
                         discussion. A manager can be terminated by the council.
                         A mayor only leaves by changes in election years.

  Wayne stated that a third document was shared with the Council that is
  informational only and is an article titled “Taking Stock of the Council-Manager
  Form at 100” (attached to minutes).

  Councilmember Harmon thanked staff for the presentation. He noted that form of
  government is in the top 10 comments that he receives from citizens and in the top
  2 as far as passion and importance. He indicated that he sees the benefits to both
  types.

  Councilmember Fitisemanu asked why the City Manager/Council form of
  government was removed from State Code. Wayne replied that it happened 15-20
  years ago and was a particular issue to one jurisdiction at that time. Nicole Cottle
  agreed and noted that it wasn’t just that form of government that was eliminated
  from State Code and noted that she would provide a history on the bill.
  Councilmember Nordfelt noted that not many City’s in Utah currently have the
  Council/Manager form of government but nationally more than half of City’s do
  and that number continues to grow.

  Councilmember Harmon asked if the citizens have voted on a form of government
  change in the history of the City. Wayne replied no. Councilmember Harmon stated
  that he sees a benefit to allowing citizens that option periodically.

  Councilmember Huynh thanked Wayne for the honest presentation. He noted that
  he grew up in Vietnam and was witness to a corrupt government system so he is
  appreciative of the checks and balances that government has in the United States.
  Councilmember Huynh indicated that he agrees with Councilmember Harmon in
  that citizens should have the option to decide on the form of government. He stated
  he believes the Mayor should be full time. Councilmember Huynh discussed City
  Attorney roles in the City and contracted attorney options utilized by other City’s.

A.   POTENTIAL FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS
     Mayor Lang stated that she received complaints about officers ticketing in the area
     of 5480 W 2400 S. She indicated that people are receiving tickets for rolling
     through stop signs and they are concerned it’s a targeted revenue source. Wayne
     indicated that staff looked into the concern and the reason police were watching
     this particular area was because there were concerns expressed by businesses in the
     area that people were going right through the stop sign. He indicated that he went
     to the location personally and witnessed the problem firsthand. Chief Jacobs
     indicated that have been there 5 different times officers have been in the area over
     the last 3 months and 75 vehicles have been cited or warned. She indicated it’s a
     large intersection with a lot of semi-truck and vehicle traffic and drivers are not
     being safe and taking the time to come to a complete stop. Mayor Lang asked what
     the accident rate is. Chief Jacobs replied that she could provide this number. Mayor
     Lang asked the Council if they were interested in looking into an alternate option
     here that isn’t a stop sign. The Council indicated that drivers should be more
     cautious and aware of the stop sign.
