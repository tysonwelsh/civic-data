BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Text Amendment to DCMC Section 9-18N-
020. The CSD-IOP zone covers 21.68 acres of property located at approximately 344 West
13800 South (Exhibit C). The property is currently zoned CSD-IOP. The applicant is
requesting that a Text Amendment be approved to allow Medical or Dental Laboratory as a
permitted use.

The CSD-IOP zone was approved on October 2, 2018. The CSD is an office based CSD that
allows for up to three office buildings. Currently there is one office building on the
property.

the CBP (Business Manufacturing Park) zone.

Text Amendment. The applicant is proposing to add the Medical or Dental Laboratory use as a
permitted uses in the CSD-IOP zone (Exhibit B). This use is defined in DCMC Section 9-3-040
as:

        DCMC 9-3-040
        MEDICAL OR DENTAL LABORATORY: An establishment that conducts basic medical or
        dental research and analysis. This term does not include a facility providing any type of
        in-house patient services typically provided by hospitals and clinics.

The CSD-IOP zone allows for Medical Services, which is designed for doctor’s offices as
defined below, but does not allow for the research component of medical uses. The CSD-
IOP zone is designed to create an office park environment. Medical research is a typical use
found within office parks.

        DCMC 9-3-040
        MEDICAL SERVICE: An establishment providing therapeutic, preventive, or corrective
        personal treatment services on an outpatient basis by physicians, dentists, and other
        practitioners of the medical or healing arts, as well as the provision of medical testing
        and analysis services.

Criteria For Approval.
A Text and Map Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City
Council and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation
to the City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in
Section 9-5-060(E) of the DCMC:

        2. Text Amendments:
             a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies
                of the city's general plan;
             b. Whether a proposed amendment furthers the specific purpose statements of the
                zoning ordinance;
             c. Whether the proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the request
                and there is sufficient justification for a modification to the zoning ordinance;
             d. The proposed amendment will not create a conflict with any other section or part
                of this title or the general plan;
             e. Whether the potential effects of the proposed amendment have been evaluated
                and determined not to be detrimental to public health, safety, or welfare and
                represents an overall community benefit; and
