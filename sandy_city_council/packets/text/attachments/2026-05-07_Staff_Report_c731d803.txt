Request

 Monty Eyink, is requesting approval of a conditional use

Background

 R-1-20(A) zone. The subject property is located in the
 Dimple Dell Heights subdivision. Properties to the south,
 east, and west are single family residential zoned R-1-20(A).
 To the north is a cemetery zoned R-1-20(A).

Analysis
 The applicant is requesting to build a 32’ x 50’ (1,600 square feet) accessory structure that is 16.5 feet tall in the northwest
 rear yard of the property. The proposed structure would be 10 feet from the north rear property line, 14 feet from the
 west side property line, and 46 feet from the east side property line. The accessory structure would consume
 approximately 13% of the rear yard. The applicant has an existing driveway that will be used to access the structure. There
 is an existing 575 square feet detached garage in the front yard of the property that was constructed at the same time as
 the house.

 Building Size
 The allowed size for accessory structures for lots of 20,000 square feet or larger is 1,500 square feet. The subject property
 is in the R-1-20(A) zone and is approximately 22,000 square feet. Section 21-11-2(a)(2)(d) of the Sandy City Development
 Code states that any property over 40,000 square feet, or with an "A" designation, the total maximum square footage of
 all accessory buildings on the property may be increased up to 50 percent larger than the permitted size through a
 conditional use permit. A single accessory building over 1,500 square feet also requires conditional use permit review.
 The Planning Commission shall consider the scale of the buildings in relation to the immediate surroundings, the
 nature of the zone and land uses in the immediate vicinity, architectural design, landscaping, access, proposed use,
 impact upon adjacent properties, in addition to other criteria normally considered during the conditional use permit
 process.

 The applicants proposed accessory structure is 1,600 square feet and the existing detached garage is 575 square feet. The
 total request of 2,175 square feet is approximately 45% larger than the allowed maximum of 1,500 square feet without a
 conditional use permit. A list of accessory structures and their size in the nearby vicinity is included.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for increased square footage and as
  described in the staff report for the property located at 1779 E. 10980 S. based on the following findings and subject to
  the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. There is an existing 575 square foot detached garage located in the front yard.

    2. The proposed structure is consistent with rear yard area coverage for accessory structures.

    3. Staff finds that the proposed structure meets the intent of Section 21-11-2 provided the applicant complies with the
       following conditions:

Conditions:
   1. That the structure be located as per the enclosed site plan and be built to 1,600 square feet and 16.5 feet in height
       from finished grade to peak of roof. The structure will be sited 10 feet from the rear property line, 14 feet from the
       west side property line, and 46 feet from the east side property line.

    2. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.

    3. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City Development Code, and all
       conditions of approval imposed by the Planning Commission.

    4. That this Conditional Use Permit be reviewed upon a legitimate complaint.
