EMIGRATION CANYON
ORDINANCE NO. 2024-07-01
ORDINANCE PROHIBITING FIRE IGNITION SOURCES

WHEREAS, due to current and expected weather conditions and the drying of vegetation in

northern Utah, including Emigration Canyon;

WHEREAS, Utah fire managers are implementing Stage 1 Fire Restrictions beginning July 1,

2024, at 12:01 a.m. on all unincorporated Private and State lands in Box Elder County, Cache

County, Rich County, Weber County, Tooele County, Utah County, Salt Lake County, Davis

County, Morgan County;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Emigration Canyon City Council as

follows:

SECTION 1. Due to the above considerations, the following are prohibited until November 15,

2024, unless earlier terminated by action of the Council.

1. No cutting, welding or grinding metal in areas of dry vegetation unless the areas where the
work is being done is thoroughly wetted down and the areas are kept wet throughout the
process.

2. No operating a motorcycle, chainsaw, ATV, other small internal combustion engine without
an approved and working spark arrestor.

3. Smoking, except within an enclosed vehicle, trailer, or building, a developed recreation site,
or while stopped in an area that is paved or free from dry vegetation.

4. No open fires of any kind except within established public facilities in improved
campgrounds, picnic areas, or in permanently constructed fire pits at private homes where
running water is present-(compressed or liquid gas grills, stoves, and pellet fed smokers
allowed with proper clearance of flammable materials are allowed).

SECTION 2. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall go into effect upon posting pursuant to Utah

Code Ann.§ 10-3-711(1)(b)(ii) and Utah Code Ann.§ 10-3-712.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 30th Day of July 2024.
