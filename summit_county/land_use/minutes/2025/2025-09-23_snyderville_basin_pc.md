Public hearing and possible action regarding a Master Planned Development for the Junction Commons, a 19-building mixed use redevelopment project at Junction Commons (formerly Outlets Park City), located at 6699 N Landmark Dr, Kimball Junction, Summit County, UT; Parcel FSE-1 (37.32 acres); Applicant: Elliott Workgroup representing SRE Ontario LLC; Legislative Process. Amir Caus,
AICP Senior Planner,
presented a brief introduction for the mixed-use redevelopment project at Junction Commons (6:08 P.M.)
Adam Greenebaum,
Senior Vice President, Asset Management for Singerman Real Estate
, addressed the commissioners and supported the Master Planned Development introduced by Senior Planner Caus. (6:13 P.M.)
Craig Elliott,
Principal with the Elliott Workgroup
, provided a short history of the project and highlighted specific items within the project for discussion. (6:15 P.M.)
Commissioner Nagie asked for more detail regarding affordable housing. Justin Keys,
Hoggan Lee Hutchinson
, addressed his concerns. Mr. Elliott reviewed the housing numbers phase by phase. (6:31 P.M.)
Commissioner Young asked follow-up questions on affordable housing, community engagement, and green space. The applicants responded to his concerns. (6:37 P.M.)
Commissioner Peteroy thanked the applicants for their presentation and asked questions about the mix of affordable housing and traffic. (6:47 P.M.)
Commissioner Jeffrey requested elaboration on the height and roof of the parking structure as well as traffic. Applicants responded. (6:51 P.M.)
Commissioner Peteroy commented on the traffic study and service levels. (7:03 P.M.)
Commissioner Sagerman asked for clarification regarding the timing of phasing, affordable housing, and population. Mr. Keys and Mr. Elliot provided additional information. Director Barnes responded to Commissioner Sagerman’s concern about population. (7:07 P.M.)
Commissioner Nagie had a follow-up question about ownership within the affordable housing. Mr. Keys addressed the question. (7:21 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney expressed concerns about the 60-foot height issue. Mr. Elliott responded. (7:23 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney opened the meeting for public hearing at 7:25 p.m.
Chris Mega was called to speak at 7:26 p.m.
Marian Crosby was called to speak at 7:30 p.m.
Commissioner Chair Mooney closed the meeting for public hearing at 7:34 p.m.
The applicants addressed the questions that were raised during the public hearing. (7:34 P.M.)
Carl Miller,
Regional Transportation Planning Director
, addressed the Commissioners' concerns surrounding traffic issues. (7:44 P.M.)
Commissioners asked additional questions and provided comments on the project. Discussion continued. (7:57 P.M.)
Commissioners, staff, and the applicants weighed in on the possibility of an open house or site visit for members of the public to attend. Discussion continued. (8:07 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney summed up the discussion and suggested the next steps involve an open house and site visit. (8:25 P.M.)
The item was continued to a date certain of October 28th. (8:33 P.M.)
