Findings of Fact  
1. The Colony at White Pine Canyon Phase I Subdivision was originally recorded in 1998 
and has been amended three times.  
2. The Colony at White Pine Canyon Phase I Subdivision consists of 30 lots and has a 
~150- foot wide Open Space and Utility Easement that runs through Lots 2 and 3 along 
White Pine Canyon Road. 
3. The Colony at White Pine Canyon Phase I Subdivision Lot 2 (Parcel CWPC-2-3AM) 
also identifies a historic grave site easement. 
4. Parcel CWPC-2-3 AM is developed with a ~2,000 sq. ft. single-family dwelling 
constructed in 1985 and a 6,362 sq. ft. Accessory Dwelling constructed in 2018. 
5. Mary and Greg Pearson are the owners of record of Parcel CWPC-2-3AM (40.71 
acres), located at 1 White Pine Canyon Road, Summit County, Utah.  
6. The applicant is requesting to amend and relocate a grave site easement on Lot 2 of the 
Colony at White Pine Canyon Phase 1 Subdivision Plat. 
7. The applicant’s primary goal for the amendment is to allow for a preferred driveway 
connection from the Northeastern property line via the existing Accessory Dwelling 
Unit access. 
8. The proposed amendment includes the removal of an existing asphalt driveway and 
parking area which is used as the primary access for the existing single-family 
residence and for a historic gravesite easement. 
9. The historic gravesite access would be reduced and improved with a pervious surface. 
10.  Both areas are located within an Open Space and Utility Easement identified on the 
recorded Colony at White Pine Canyon Phase I Third Amendment subdivision plat. 
11. The removal and relocation of the access would result in the removal of a 5,817 sq. ft. 
asphalt area and an overall removal of 9,016 sq. ft. of hard surface area. 
Snyderville Basin Planning Commission 
October 27, 2020 
Page 8 of 41 
 
12. The historic gravesite easement area will result in a reduction from 2,533 sq. ft. to 
1,620 sq. ft.; it will be replaced by pervious material, and will be used for the historic 
gravesite access only. 
13. The primary access for the single-family residence would be from the Northeastern 
property line via the existing Accessory Dwelling Unit access. 
14. There is no evidence that any property owners will be materially injured by the 
proposed amendment as evidenced by applying for the amendment. 
15. The applicant has stated that the owner will be maintaining the access year-round for 
the gravesite easement.  Staff has included a condition of approval to include a plat 
note to reaffirm the maintenance of the easement.  
16. Postcard notices announcing the public hearing were mailed to property owners within 
1,000 feet of the subject parcels. 
17. Public notice of the public hearing was published in The Park Record.  
18. Service providers have reviewed the plats for compliance with applicable standards. 
19.  Staff has reviewed the plats for compliance with applicable Development Code 
standards. 
 
Conclusions of Law 
1. The proposed amendment would result in a decrease of an overall disturbed area 
within the Open Space and Utility Easement and there is good cause for the 
amendment.  
2. This amendment will not increase lot density in the Colony at White Pine Canyon. 
 
Conditions of Approval 
1. All service provider requirements shall be met.  
2. A Plat Note shall be placed to reaffirm that the property owner is responsible for 
maintaining year-round access for the gravesite easement.  
3. The approval of this plat amendment does not imply approval of any future 
development permits.
