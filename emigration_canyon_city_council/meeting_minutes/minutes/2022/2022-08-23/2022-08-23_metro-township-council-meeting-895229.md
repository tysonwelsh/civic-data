Pursuant to Utah Code 52-4-202 the undersigned, as Mayor of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township Council, hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the
electronic meetings of the Council will present a substantial risk to the health and safety of those
who may be present at the anchor location for the following reasons:
The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under a “Medium Level of
Transmission” alert stage for the COVID 19 pandemic; and
The regular meeting place for the Council (the Fire Station in the Canyon) does
not have sufficient space in the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing
to keep people safe from infection.

Council Business
Pinecrest Restroom Restoration Update
David Richardson, Capitol Hill Construction, stated the area around the restroom
was cleared, a fire break was created, and the slope was stabilized. Salt Lake County has
specified a seed mix for Emigration Canyon that was worked into the topsoil. Next week the
abstract masonry will begin on the front of the building. Cedar shingles are relatively fire resistant
and will be used on the structure.
Council Member Pinon stated he reached out to Mr. Richardson’s team with
some concerns about the structure being in compliance with building codes to avoid any potential
liabilities. Since the building is considered a historic building, certain code compliance is not
required. He has asked Lisa Miller, Capitol Hill Construction, to put that information into writing.
Mr. Richardson stated the project will conform to the International Existing
Building Code (IEBC).
Matt Starley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District,
stated the Council can pass a historic model preservation ordinance to establish Emigration
Canyon Metro Township as a Certified Local Government (CLG). Having the CLG designation
would qualify the Metro Township to receive matching grants from the Utah State Historic
Preservation Office (SHPO). In addition to passing an ordinance, a historic preservation
commission would need to be created and be made up of five members.
