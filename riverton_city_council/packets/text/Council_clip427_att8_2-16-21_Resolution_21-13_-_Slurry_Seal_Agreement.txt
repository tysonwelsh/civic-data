                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 21-13

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
   WITH M&M ASPHALT SERVICES TO COMPLETE THE 2021 SLURRY SEAL
                            PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the City Engineer has determined that this project is needed.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with M&M Asphalt
            Services to complete the 2021 Slurry Seal Project.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
February 2021 by the following vote:
