1.         22-367                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Council Chair Zach Robinson invited the public to participate in General Citizen 
                                  Comments.

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren spoke about carryover budget and the furniture needs at the Sandy 
                                  Senior Center. He suggested that the Council consider audio recordings of the meetings 
                                  that involve bus tours.

                                  Ms. Cathy Stark expressed a concern about trucks parking on south side of 90th South. 
                                  This is an ongoing issue and she would liek to see a resolution to the problem. 

                                  Mr. Aaron DeKeyser expressed concern regarding the letter sent by the Sandy City 
                                  Council to the Salt Lake County Council. He thinks the data supports a phased approach 
                                  to the transportation needs of Little Cottonwood Canyon.

                                  Mr. Dave Fields, President and GM of Snowbird, supports the gondola transporation 
                                  option. He updated the Council on the current status of the UTA ski bus. UTA is short 
                                  sixty to eighty bus drivers and he spoke about the impact on skiers this winter., 

                                  Dave Summers, Risk Manager with Utah School Boards, expressed concerns about the 
                                  semi trucks parked along 90th South. The Canyons Technical School is on this road and 
                                  he does not believe the area is wide enough to handle the truck parking. He hopes the 
                                  Council will find a resolution to this issue.

                                  Mr. Michael Nebeker supports the gondola transporation option and applauds UDOT for 
                                  choosing this option. He spoke about the use of gondolas in Switzerland and thinks it is 
                                  the right thing to do.

                                  Ms. Beverly Aston is opposed to the gondola option. She thinks there are bigger 
                                  problems than how to get 3% of people who ski get up the canyon. This is a ten day a 
                                  year problem. We have other issues like the water issue with the great Salt Lake. She 
                                  found the letter to the County Council offensive.

                                  Ms. Kathy Higgs is opposed to the gondola. LCC is such a delicate canyon and adding 
                                  the towers and more people to the canyon is not a good idea.

                                  Ms. Anna Marie Knider feels blessed to live in LCC. She is opposed to the gondola. It is 
                                  too expensive and Utahns will not take the gondola because it is too expensive.

                                  Ms. Nancy Peterson lives in LCC. She is disappointed that the City Council sent a  letter 
                                  to the County Council. She expressed her concerns with the gondola option and is 
                                  opposed to the option.

                                  Mr. Fred Burton lives in LCC and is opposed to the gondola. He expressed his concerns. 
                                  There were no Olympic competitions held in LCC because a study conducted on the 
                                  canyon. The canyon was too fragile. We do not have the infrastructure to accommodate 
                                  all the visitors. 

                                  Mr. Joe Taylor is opposed to the gondola and thinks more buses should be added.

                                  Senator Riebe thanked the Council for their service. She thinks we need to solve the
