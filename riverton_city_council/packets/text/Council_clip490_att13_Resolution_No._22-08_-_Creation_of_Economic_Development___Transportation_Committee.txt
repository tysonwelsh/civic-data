                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 22-08

   A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL CONSOLIDATING THE
    TRANSPORTATION COMMITTEE AND THE COMMITTEE ON ECONOMIC
    DEVELOPMENT INTO A SINGLE COMMITTEE NAMED THE ECONOMIC
           DEVELOPMENT AND TRANSPORTATION COMMITTEE

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, has formulated strategic objectives to give the
City direction as it provides municipal services for Riverton residents; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has adopted Ordinance No. 18-07 authorizing the
creation of committees to assist the Mayor in formulating strategies to implement strategic
objectives and provide other counsel; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the Mayor has
requested the formulation a transportation management committee to assist the Mayor in
advancement of strategic objectives which relate to traffic management within Riverton City and
the southwest region of Salt Lake County.

        WHEREAS, in the year 2018, the Riverton City Council adopted resolutions creating: 1)
a transportation committee to aid in management of traffic through Riverton City; and 2), a
Committee on Economic Development, to assist with projects which advance economic
development within Riverton City; and

       WHEREAS, since the formulation of these separate, independent committees, the
Mayor’s office has come to recognize that the issues of managing traffic and promoting
economic development are integrally related to each other, to the degree that the objectives of
both committees should be addressed by a single, combined committee; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines creating an economic
development and transportation committee will support implementation of the City’s strategic
objectives;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:


Section 1.     The Riverton City Council hereby provides its consent to the creation of the
               hereby named Economic Development and Transportation Committee. The
               Economic Development and Transportation Committee shall adhere to the
               following objectives:
               a. To assist in the creation of a traffic management strategy for the City.
               b. To assist in creating transportation concept plans for key areas of interest,
                   such as (and without limitation) facilities which enhance east-west
                   transportation facilities within Riverton City and in the vicinity of the City.
               c. To assist in the creation of an economic development strategy for the City.
               d. To assist in creating concept plans for key areas of interest, such as (and
                   without limitation) revitalizing the downtown area of Riverton City and to
                   assist the Mayor with other projects which advance economic development
                   such as mobilization of public support for community projects.
Section 2.   The Committee shall consist of up to nine members. Approval of said committee
             members shall be made in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance No. 18-07.
