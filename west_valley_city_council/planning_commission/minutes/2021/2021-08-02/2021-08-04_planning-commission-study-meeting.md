ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-5-2021 Petition by BRENT BEUTLER requesting a zone change from R-2-8 (Two Unit
         Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) to R-1-8 (Single Unit
         Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). The property is
         located at 3532 South 5450 West on 0.43 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-
         963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Lehman presented the application for Steve Pastorik on Z-5-2021. Steve
              Lehman explained that the applicant had talked to him about subdividing the
              subject property. At that time, Steve said to Mr. Beutler that the current zone
              would allow for a two-family dwelling, such as a duplex or a twin home.
              However, the existing home on the subject property would need to be demolished.
              Steve explained that staff suggested the applicant rezone the property from R-2-8
              to R-1-8 to develop a new single-family home instead of a multifamily dwelling.
              Steve stated that Mr. Beutler decided to pursue the rezone. Steve explained that
              the parcel is large enough for the existing house and a new single-family home.
              However, the frontage would not meet the code requirements. Steve said that Mr.
              Beutler is willing to enter into a development agreement that will outline the
              housing standards and the frontage exception.

              Commissioner Winter asked what the requirements are for the size of the
              frontage, is it 80 feet? Steve explained that in an R-1-8 zone, the frontage must be
              80 feet, and there is a 40-foot frontage requirement for each side of the duplex or
              twin home within an R-2-8 zone.

              Commissioner Winters stated that there would essentially be a trade, single-
              family homes on deep lots for a frontage exception. Steve said that is correct. He
              pointed out that both houses would meet the setback requirements.

              Clover asked if there was any feedback from the neighbors. Steve stated that he
              had spoken with Steve Pastorik yesterday, and he did not mention that he had
              received any calls or emails from neighbors regarding this application.

              Steve explained that public works assessed 5450 West to see if they should put in
              curb, gutter, and sidewalk, and they will not be adding improvements. Therefore,
              there will be a delay agreement in place if they add improvements in the future.



SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
S-3-2021  Petition by SHAUN JOHNSON requesting a preliminary approval for 2nd phase
          of the Weston Fields Subdivision. The subdivision consists of 38 lots on
          approximately 13 acres, which equates to a density of 2.9 dwelling units per
          acre. The subdivision is located at approximately 6840 West 4100 South and is
          zoned R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square
          feet) and RE (Residential Estate). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
          steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Lehman presented the application for preliminary approval of Phase 2 of
              the Western Fields Development. Steve pointed out what portion of the subject
              property will be included in the second phase. He explained the portion of the
              property to the west is zoned RE and will meet the requirements for that zone, and
              the part to the east is zoned R-1-8 and will meet those requirements. Steve stated
              that there would be improvements and a masonry wall along 4100 South.

              Commissioner Winters asked Steve to remind him what the minimum square
              footage in the RE zone is. Steve said the minimum square footage in the RE zone
              is 12,000 and the average is 15,000 with a frontage requirement of 100 feet.
              Steve stated that he would provide the development agreement in the staff report
              to remind the Planning Commission of what is outlined within the agreement.
              Commissioner Winters asked if there was a plan for a walking connection along
              the perimeter of the subject property and the abutting Brookfield Subdivision.
              Steve said that there was nothing platted for a walking connection.

PUD-2-2021 Petition by KEN MILNE is requesting preliminary approval for the Heather
           Villas PUD. The subdivision will consist of 84 senior units on approximately
           13.8 acres. The subdivision is located at approximately 6087 West Parkway
           Boulevard and is Zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff – Steve
           Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:   Steve Lehman explained that the subject property was rezoned from A to RM
              three to four months ago. Steve stated that he has been working with the
              developer and City Engineer on extending 5990 West from just south of the
              subject location north to Parkway Blvd. He said this extension would create land
              that the applicant would like to purchase and add to the proposed project. Steve
              explained the access to the subject location would be a shared access, 6100 West.
              He stated that the proposal is for pods of 4 units totaling 84 units. Each unit will
              have a driveway and a two-car garage. The applicant is proposing a trail system
              that will go throughout the development. There will also be a clubhouse
              containing a kitchen, exercise room, theater, and mail delivery room.

              Commissioner Fuller asked Steve if the applicant were to purchase the extra land
              and add it to this property, what width would he gain? Steve said they would gain
              approximately 90-100 feet of depth.

              Commissioner Fuller thought there had been discussion about this project being a
              tight fit in this location. Steve said the subject property has 25 to 30 feet setbacks
              on all sides, so the proposed subdivision will have enough room for development.

              Commissioner Winters asked what options the applicant would have for the use of
              the acquired land. Steve explained that if the applicant were to purchase the land,
              he would first need to come back to the Commission to rezone it to RM, then
              calculate the density of the property to include the purchased land.

              Commissioner Lovato asked if they would need to start the entire process over
              again. Steve said they might need to do a revised preliminary plat. However, this
              plan would not need to be reviewed again.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-16-2021 Petition by GMC SPECIALTY SERVICES, LLC requesting a conditional use for
          outside storage. The property is located at 2160 S. 3270 W. on .95 acres and is
          zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497or
          jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)
Discussion:   Jody explained that this request is for outside storage for a construction,
              demolition, and dumpster rental business. She stated that this lot is entirely paved
              and well maintained. The outside storage area in the back of the building will be
              enclosed with a chain-link fence with slats. Jody said the outside storage would
              be mainly a staging area, and they are not HAZMAT certified, so they will not be
              storing any materials covered by that certification. She explained that a dumpster
              with material in it could come back to the subject location. However, it would
              never come off the truck and be brought to the dump the next business day.

              She said that the applicant did apply for a business license and the licensing
              officer had a question regarding trees. Jody said that where this property was built
              in the mid 70’s they could not find a landscaping plan place, so the rule is that
              they must replace what was existing. She stated there were several trees along the
              frontage of the building and one side removed, with only two trees being
              replaced.

              Commissioner Winters asked what the tree requirement is for the subject location.
              Jody explained the current requirement for the manufacturing zone is 5%
              landscaping. She said there would be trees required along the street east side of
              the building. However, there would not be a tree requirement for the south side of
              the building.

              Commissioner Winters feels the applicant should put trees in along the frontage or
              street-side; however, he does not feel they would need trees along the side. Jody
              stated that the sidewalk changes from narrow to wide, and there are low hanging
              powerlines over the sidewalk. She will look more into the tree requirement where
              this location presents a few challenges and will provide options for the
              Commission in her staff report.

C-17-2021     Petition by R5 DEVELOPMENT, LLC requesting a conditional use for a light
              industrial multi-tenant building. The property is located at 3076 S Decker Lake
              Dr. on 1.0 acre and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-
              963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that the proposed structure would allow four tenants. The
              applicant does not have any tenants in mind at this time, so they wanted to leave
              some flexibility in their plans. She explained that there would be overhead doors
              in the front and the rear of the building. Jody explained that the applicant did not
              intend to use the overhead doors in the front of the building initially for vehicle
              access but if a tenant chose to do so they would modify the site and landscape
              plan to accommodate that.

              Jody stated that the subject property is adjacent to a hotel and the parking for the
              subject property abuts the hotel parking. She explained that the applicant is
              proposing to remove the 5-foot landscape edge of their parking lot to flow into the
             hotel parking. Jody pointed out that this setup is different where the hotel has
             standard ninety-degree parking stalls where the subject property is proposing
             parallel stalls. She said the applicant is structuring the parking to allow room for
             vehicles to access the overhead doors. They will meet the parking requirements;
             however, the setup is unique.

             Jody pointed out a slope from 3100 into the subject property that is under
             UDOT’s jurisdiction and has been left untouched. She spoke with the applicants
             about possibly improving the park strip along that section to finish the edge. Jody
             said they are still discussing what will be put up along the east side of the
             property, next to residential.

             Commissioner Winters asked Jody if they are meeting the parking requirements
             but using a different layout. Jody said that is correct, and if they need to remove
             some landscaping to make their plans work, they can. She explained they are
             required to have five percent landscaping and currently have fifteen to sixteen
             percent.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:18 p.m.
