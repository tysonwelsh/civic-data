       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          Granite Library Manager, Kim Welch, gave an update on activities at the Granite
          Library. Haunted House contest with categories for every age group. Participants
          can come pick up a kit to decorate and bring back. Over 30 submissions. There will
          also be a gingerbread house contest starting in late November.

      3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
         Mayor Wood highlighted some events that are coming up:

          October 26th – Promise South Salt Lake Trunk or Treat at the Central Park
          Community Center from 6-7:30 p.m.

          October 27th – Promise South Salt Lake Healthy Halloween at the Historic Scott
          School from 4:30-7:00 p.m.

          October 28th – Animal Services will host the ‘Howl at the Moon’ pet adoption event
          from 11-4 p.m. There are also free cat adoptions throughout the month of October.

          October 31st - Spooky City Hall will be throughout the City Hall from 3-4:45 p.m.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Mila noted all the events that are happening throughout the City
          and thanked the City staff who made the events happen.

          Council Chair Bynum acknowledged the City staff’s behind-the-scenes work to
          create the events and thanked them for their efforts.

          Council Member Pinkney encouraged residents to adopt a cat through the South
          Salt Lake Animal Shelter. She also shared comments on the pending discussion
          regarding the In-N-Out project on 2100 South.

          Council Member Williams talked about the City’s upcoming events and encouraged
          residents to attend.

      6. INFORMATION.

              a. Annual Stormwater Report
                 Stormwater Division Manager, Corby Talbot, went over the annual report, a
                 copy of which is attached and incorporated by this reference, and
                 encouraged residents to keep their gutters clean.

    3. Selection to Fill Vacant Council District 5 Seat.

        The Council conducted the interview that was passed over from the Work Meeting.
        Candidate Paul Sanchez answered the questions that were asked by the Council in the
        following order:

        1. Tell us about yourself and your level of involvement in South Salt Lake
        2. How would you prepare for a controversial agenda item prior to a meeting?
        3. What is your long-term vision for South Salt Lake?

        Since Mr. Sanchez was the only individual to apply to fill the vacancy, and as the sole
        candidate for District 5, Council Chair Bynum congratulated him on his new position as
        the Council Member for District 5.

        A copy of Mr. Sanchez’s resume is attached and incorporated by this reference.
