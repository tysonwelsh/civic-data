                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-82

    A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO THE INTERLOCAL
 AGREEMENT BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR THE
           OPERATION OF THE RIVERTON SENIOR CENTER.

        WHEREAS, Title 11, Chapter 13, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended, entitled
"Interlocal Cooperation Act" provides that any one or more public agencies may contract with
any one or more other public agencies to perform any governmental service, activity, or
undertaking which each public agency is authorized by law to perform; and

       WHEREAS, an Interlocal Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake County was
previously approved under Resolution No. 09-2 for the construction and operation of the Senior
Center located at 12890 South Redwood Road in Riverton City; and

       WHEREAS, there is a need to amend said agreement in order to modify the duties and
responsibilities of Riverton City and Salt Lake County; and

        WHEREAS, the Parties executed an amendment to the Interlocal Agreement on our
about December 12, 2011, which modified Sections 9(A) and 10(A) of the Agreement as to the
duties and responsibilities of the parties for maintenance of the Senior Center; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the work tasks shared
by Salt Lake County and Riverton City should be adjusted to enable a more efficient
maintenance of the Riverton Senior Center; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that adjusting work tasks
requires the execution of an amendment to the Agreement, hereinafter referred to as Amendment
No. 2 which is now before the Council for consideration; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to approve
Amendment No. 2 to the Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake County;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, on behalf of the City, Amendment No. 2 to the
          Agreement with Salt Lake County in form substantially similar to that enclosed with
          this Resolution, and upon verification that said Amendment No. 2 and any documents
          related thereto have been approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
