                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-55


         A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING RIVERTON CITY TO EXECUTE A
         REAL ESTATE PURCHASE CONTRACT WITH BLAINE AND HILEA
           WALKER TO ACQUIRE REAL PROPERTY, LOCATED AT THE
         ADDRESS OF 11845 SOUTH REDWOOD ROAD, RIVERTON, UTAH.


         WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-8-2, Riverton City may “purchase,
receive, hold, sell, lease, convey, and dispose of real and personal property for the benefit of the
municipality, whether the property is within or without the municipality’s corporate boundaries,
if the action is in the public interest and complies with other law;” and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Riverton City has a
need to purchase real property which will be used to expand the capacity of the Riverton City
stormwater management system; and

     WHEREAS, Property held by the Blaine and Hilea Walker address of 11845 South
Redwood Road is in a suitable location to construct a stormwater detention facility; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that acquisition property
held by Mr. and Mrs. Walker will enable the City to plan for better management of regional
stormwater discharge, now and in the future; and

       WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that Riverton City staff has
negotiated an agreement with the Walker family to purchase their property at the address of 11845
South Redwood Road; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the purchase price for the
Walker Property is reasonable and of fair market value, based upon competing appraisals of the
same;


     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute a Real Estate Purchase Contract with Blaine
          and Hilea Walker, to acquire approximately 2.68 acres of unimproved real property
          located at 11845 South Redwood Road, once it is approved as to legal form by the City
          Attorney, at a purchase price of $850,000, plus associated closing costs in amounts
          described in the enclosed Real Estate Purchase Agreement. In addition, the Mayor is
          authorized to execute any additional documents at closing if the same are necessary to
          facilitate closing, provided said documents do not culminate in a substantial alteration to
          the material terms of the Real Estate Purchase Agreement, and said documents are
          approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

      2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.


      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 21st day of
September, 2021 by the following vote:
