   1. APPROVE RESOLUTION 42-2025 DESIGNATING THE WEBER COUNTY CLERK/AUDITOR AS
      WEBER COUNTY’S CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER FOR DATA PRIVACY.
         Chris Crockett (Chief Civil Deputy Attorney): We are commencing a very long project that will install changes
         and oversight on processes on personal information collected by the County. The first step is to appoint
         someone to oversee this effort. The Clerk/Auditor office is recommended to be the Chief Administrative
         Officer over Data Privacy.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 42-2025 designating the Weber County Clerk/Auditor as
         Weber County’s Chief Administrative Officer for Data Privacy; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye
    2. APPROVE FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE TO COMBINE THE TWO PLANNING
       COMMISSIONS INTO A SINGLE PLANNING COMMISSION COVERING ALL OF
       UNINCORPORATED WEBER COUNTY.

         Courtlan Erickson (Civil Attorney): With the pending incorporation in Ogden Valley, there will not be a lot
         of concentrated development in what’s left of unincorporated areas in Ogden Valley. One of the options laid
         before you is to dissolve both Planning Commissions and create one Planning Commission for unincorporated
         Weber County. The proposed Ordinance updates County Code as well as a cleanup of Rules of Order to reflect
         the one Commission. Those current Ogden Valley Planning Commissioners residing in the new incorporated
         city could not reapply for the new Weber County Planning Commission. Applications can begin to be accepted
         before final adoption of the Ordinance. Appointing members on December 2nd allows for an effective date of
         December 3rd which aligns perfectly with the State requirement to wait 15 days after Ordinance approval to
         enact those changes.
         Commissioner Harvey: I do think it is good to have representation from the unincorporated areas of Ogden
         Valley.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve first reading of an ordinance to combine the two planning
         commissions into a single planning commission covering all of unincorporated Weber County; Commissioner
         Harvey seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye
