MINUTES
WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
Tuesday, September 14, 2021 — 10:01 a.m.
Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT

In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “‘in brief’
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

Craig Brandt, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.

ACTION ITEMS:

1.

APPROVAL OF A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH THE WEBER COUNTY
CENTER OF EXCELLENCE AND OGDEN SCHOOL DISTRICT OUTLINING WORKING
RELATIONSHIP.

Melissa Freigang (Weber Prosperity Center of Excellence): Data sharing MoU with Ogden School District
enabling sharing of data, reporting and monitoring of a number of educational indicators on each of the children
in the Integrated Community Action Now (ICAN) program such as regular school attendance, enrollment in
after school programs, literacy and numeracy gains, etc.

Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the Memorandum of Understanding with the Weber County Center
of Excellence and Ogden School District outlining working relationship; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye

APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 35-2021 APPROVING AN INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT WITH
BOX ELDER COUNTY FOR CERTAIN SURVEYING WORK AND REQUEST FOR APPROVAL
OF AN INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY AND BOX ELDER
COUNTY ALLOWING WEBER COUNTY TO CONSTRUCT FIVE SECTION MONUMENTS
WITHIN BOX ELDER COUNTY BOUNDARIES.

Steve Collier (Weber County Surveyor): Box Elder lost surveying resources. They need help with property
just on their side of the county line; two of the five monuments do control some of Weber County property.
Box Elder County will pay Weber County for this work via this agreement.

Commissioner Jenkins: Background on issue. Box Elder to contribute $500. Helps solve some issues on
Weber County side of the line.

Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve resolution 35-2021 approving an inter-local with Box Elder County
for certain surveying work and request for approval of an inter-local with Box Elder County allowing Weber
County to construct five section monuments within Box Elder County boundaries; Commissioner Jenkins
seconded.

Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye
