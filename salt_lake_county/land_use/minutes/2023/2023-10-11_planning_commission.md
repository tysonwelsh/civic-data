Speaker # 3: Citizen 
Name: Ed Marshall 
Address: 6451 East Millcreek Canyon Road 
Comments: Mr. Marshall said he submitted a letter and asked if the staff has no opposition to the revision 
they requested. Ask to adopt the amendments 19.12.030.g.1. 
 
Speaker # 4: Citizen 
Name: Michael Cole 
Address: 7973 West Country View Lane 
Comments: Mr. Cole said he doesn’t own horses but is a member of the community and was sought out to 
be involved in discussions with the county. He was invited to listen and express concerns. It was an invite 
meeting and HOA members expressed concerns. Meeting wasn’t lengthy, members asked questions, and 
County came back with a document. Ordinance seems to come after an easy target --owning horses. Other 
reasons E. coli could be in the stream could be hikers and swimmers before horses, cow and deer use as a 
water source. Seems a little bit of going after the easy without pinpointing the difference in E coli risk; no 
factual statements pointing to the horses. Dozens of horses using Yellow Fork Trail. Are there facilities for 
the hikers?  Ordinance does not address recreational uses. 
 
Speaker # 5: Citizen 
Name: Kathy Fuller 
Address: 15467 Rose Canyon Road 
Comments: Ms. Fuller said how frustrating this process has been. She was on the recent phone call s with 
SLCO staff. She did not represent as a board member or HOA at the meeting, that was not her intent, but 
she gave her personal opinion. She understands and appreciates the fine and understands the nervousness 
of compliance. Even if enacted today, ordinance would not change the e. coli count by February 2024. She 
reviewed FR/FA zoning when she started house hunting in 2015, HOA rules in Hi-Country I wouldn’t allow 
anything other than recreational animals. Their area is not protected watershed and requirements should be 
different from east canyons because Rose Creek not supplying drinking water. Setbacks are 75 feet away 
from the stream and she doesn’t have that space. She has done research and there are lots of different e. coli 
and the one they need to worry about is e. coli 0157. It is most often carried by cows, sheep, and deer. She 
will hand in articles that horses do not carry e. coli 0157.
