MAGNA CITY COUNCIL
RESOLUTION #R2026-22 DATE: April 14, 2026
A RESOLUTION APPOINTING TERRY GEORGE AS THE REPRESENTATIVE
APPOINTEE OF MAGNA CITY TO THE WASATCH FRONT WASTE AND
RECYCLING DISTRICT BOARD OF TRUSTEES

WHEREAS, Magna City is a member entity of the Wasatch Front Waste and
Recycling District (the “District”); and

WHEREAS, the Magna City Council (the “Council”) may appoint a
Councilmember to the District’s Board of Trustees (the “District Board”) pursuant to
Title 17B, Chapter 3, Part 3 of the Utah Code; and

WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint Council Member Terry George to

represent Magna City on the District Board.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MAGNA CITY COUNCIL
EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

1. The Council appoints Council Member Terry George as its appointed
representative on the District Board.

2. The Magna City Recorder is authorized to provide the District with this
resolution, a copy of Council Member George’s oath of office, and their contact
information.

ADOPTED AND APPROVED at a duly called meeting of the Magna City Council on
this 14° day of April 2026.
