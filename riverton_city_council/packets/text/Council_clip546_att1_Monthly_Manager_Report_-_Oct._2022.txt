                           Riverton Police Department Monthly Report

                                               October, 2022
     An analysis of the crime reporting statistics for the months immediately preceding October
     reveal a consistent pattern of types and volume of criminal activity. We are encouraged the
     vehicle burglaries which numbered more than 20 for two consecutive months are trending
     downward with 13 reported during September.

     Traffic citations also trended up as our traffic unit returned to full capacity after one of our
     officers healed from injuries sustained during training in the summer. Also, every year we put an
     added emphasis on conducting speed enforcement operations in the city as children return to
     school to remind our citizens of the changing dynamic of children crossing streets. 208 citations
     and 21 warnings were issued during the month as a result of these increased efforts and a focus
     on our roadways.

     We are excited to host our 2nd Annual Citizen’s Academy this month and look forward to
     providing instruction and hands-on activities for our attendees. We will be meeting for six
     consecutive Thursday evenings and the class will culminate with a graduation ceremony on
     November 10th.
