                          The Council stood in recess until 6 PM. Senior Analyst Dustin Fratto provided

Citizen Comment

                          Benjamin Barron from Stone Gate Circle in Sandy discussed a technology infrastructure
                          issue in his neighborhood. They lack high speed internet.

                          Mark Furlong from Brighton Drive also spoke about lack of internet access in Sandy. He
                          recommended the City explore UTOPIA.

                          Jeff Gardner, also from Stone Gate Circle emphasized Mr. Barron's points about lack of
                          reliable internet service.

                          Tom Ward, Public Utilities Director spoke about his lack of internet access as a resident.
