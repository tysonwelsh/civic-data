Nephi City
 Work Session Minutes
November 9, 2021
 9:00 p.m.
Nephi City Council Chambers
	


	•	 The council discussed the recreation center (the Hive) construction progress and financing.   Mr. Atkinson presented an analysis of costs to complete the project and possible funding sources.  The council directed staff to pursue funding to finish the project by possibly fall of 2022.  

	•	 The council reviewed an annexation petition and letter of certification of the petition for W. Wright Industrial Annexation.  

	•	 The council reviewed the most current financial statements. 

	•	Work session adjourned at 8:04 p.m.  The council convened into executive session to discuss the character and competence of an individual and real property. 

Present:  Mayor Glade R. Nielsen, Councilor Larry Ostler, Councilor Justin Seely,  Councilor Skip Worwood,  Councilor Nathan Memmott, City Administrator Seth Atkinson,  City Attorney Kasey Wright,, Lisa Brough (Absent), JD Parady, Cory Thomson, Vanessa Goodes, Kodey Hughes, Terry Cook, Craig Oswald, and Travis Worwood.
