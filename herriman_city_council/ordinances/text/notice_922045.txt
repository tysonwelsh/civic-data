PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on June 12, 2024, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2024-13 amending Herriman City Code 10-12-16 to allow architectural metal panels, exceptions to secondary exterior building finish materials, clarifying requirements for pedestrian access, and updating site amenities on projects that encompass five or more acres. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
