Call to Order: 6:30 p.m. – Council Member Cotter

Approval of Minutes: Council Meeting April 7, 2026.
 MOTION: Mr. Hock moved to approve. Mr. Bullen seconded the motion. All in favor 3-0.

Citizen Comments:
  Catherine Burton – Murray Resident
  Senior Center Advisory Board member Ms. Burton stated that the senior center was making an effort to be more
  cognizant of visitors attending in order to better adjust to their needs. She explained that a new lunch system
  was in place and that new classes were being offered.

  Sam Marks – Midvale City Resident
  Mr. Marks with NeighborWorks of Salt Lake explained functions of the non-profit organization. He spoke about
  NeighborWorks Week, a homeownership workshop associated with the Nationwide NeighborWorks America
  that would be held at the Murray location, on June 3, 2026.

Special Recognition:
  1. Consider a Joint Proclamation of the Mayor and Municipal Council of Murray City for Emergency Medical
      Services Week. Medical Director Dr. Balls shared about his 20 years of experience as an emergency medical
      physician and praised Murray Fire and EMS (Emergency Medical Services) crews for delivering high-quality,
      compassionate care under high-pressure conditions. Dr. Balls stated that Murray crews are leaders in the
      Salt Lake Valley as well as the backbone of Murray City’s public safety. He expressed appreciation to the
      Mayor and City Council for their continued support, which included budget approvals that provide funding
      for equipment, training, and tools that support EMS work. Assistant Fire Chief Travis Bodtcher explained
      how EMS played a vital role in the City and read the Joint Proclamation.

 4. Consider a resolution providing advice and consent to the Mayor’s appointment of Ryan Madsen as the
    City’s Information Technology Department Director. If approved, the swearing-in ceremony will be
    conducted by Brooke Smith, City Recorder. Mayor Hales introduced IT Supervisor Ryan Madsen, stating
    that with the recent retirement of Robert White, Mr. Madsen should be promoted to the IT Director
    position. Mayor Hales commended Mr. Madsen for his 24 years of experience in the IT Department and his
    dedication as a hard working employee.
      MOTION: Mr. Hock moved to adopt the resolution. Mr. Bullen SECONDED the motion.
          Council Roll Call Vote:
          Mr. Bullen        Aye
          Mr. Hock          Aye
          Ms. Cotter        Aye
          Motion Passed: 3-0

     Mr. Madsen was sworn in by City Recorder Brooke Smith.

Mayor’s Report and Questions: Mayor Hales reported that the Miss Murray pageant was well attended at the
Murray Theater, where Sarah Christensen was crowned the new 2026 Miss Murray. He noted that Free Access
was underway at the Murray Theater, allowing anyone to attend all events for free. Mayor Hales also mentioned
that several concerts and other events are scheduled throughout the summer.
