     invocation was offered by Carmella Fitzpatrick with the Unity Church of Positive
     Living and the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag was led by Councilmember Hughes.

      item has already been resolved.

CHANGE LAND USE DESIGNATIONS/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing a portion of FP (Flood Plain)
     land use designations to COM (Commercial) on property generally located
     on the north side of 1450 South at approximately 1600 East and is
     approximately 12 acres.

      Planner Dan Boles presented the request to change a portion of FP (Flood Plain) land
      use designations to COM (Commercial) on property generally located on the north
      side of 1450 South at approximately 1600 East and is approximately 12 acres.
      He provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics: General plan
      and zoning maps; and photos of the property. The Planning Commission
      recommended approval. The property was removed out of the floodway by FEMA;
      but it is still in the floodplain. It will have to be developed according to the
      guidelines set forth by FEMA.

      Councilmember Randall commented she would rather see this property zone
      commercial.

      Mr. Boles noted there was one citizen who spoke in opposition of the proposal on the
      property.

      Applicant Nick McKinley explained he and the other owners have spent quite a bit of
      time working with City staff and Rosenberg Associates. It is currently zoned R-1-10;
      City staff recommended it be commercial. They will work with Rosenberg for erosion
      control, mitigating the risk of being near the Virgin River.

      Councilmember Larkin stated she feels there needs to be further discussion of what
      takes place in floodplains; they are important for the eco system. She is concerned
      that the more is built in the floodplains, the water pushes into other areas.

      Councilmember McArthur noted a similar discussion took place regarding another
      parcel. He agreed with Councilmember Larkin – there needs to be further discussion
      and education.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman reminded the Council this has been the subject of a lot
      of discussion between the City and the property owners. He suggested Jared Bates
      explain how FEMA change this parcel from floodway to floodplain. The City owns
      property across the River.

      Councilmember Hughes noted when Jiffy Lube came before the Council, the
      developable areas were looked at during that time.

      Jared Bates with Rosenberg Associates explained there are two lines in the River –
      the floodplain and the floodway; he explained the difference. He noted there is a
      floodplain ordinance. Erosion protection would have to be put in place. He
      commented there is a lot of developable property in this area.

      After being asked by Councilmember Hughes, City Attorney Shawn Guzman
      explained the applicant could develop residential currently if they plan it to the City’s

      Mr. McKinley commented they still have a lot of meetings with City staff. They will
      need to meet the City’s floodplain requirements. Looking what the City has already
      approved along the River, he does not feel like they are proposing anything out of

      Mayor Pike noted that Councils cannot bind the hands of future Councils; many of
