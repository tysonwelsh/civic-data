                                           Emigration Canyon Monthly Report February 2026
                                           Calls for service: 37




                                          Crime/Police Stats:
In the month of February, Unified Police Department responded to 37 calls for service.

Incidents of Note:

Officer responded to a welfare check on a suicidal individual involving a gun. The officer was
able to locate the individual and speak with them about their situation. This individual was
appropriately cared for, and the weapon was secured and booked into UPD evidence for
safekeeping.

Officers responded to two another welfare checks on suicidal individuals, these individuals
were spoken with and dealt with appropriately.

Six people were found sleeping in their vehicles this month along the pullouts in the upper
canyon. One of the individuals was on probation and had several weapons offenses.

A DUI arrest was made where damage to property occurred.
