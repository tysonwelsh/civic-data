2.     ADOPT A RESOLUTION AMENDING SECTION IX.B.4 OF THE UTAH COUNTY OFFICE OF
       HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT RULES AND REGULATIONS TO CLARIFY SPOUSE
       ELIGIBILITY TO RECEIVE BENEFITS
(Track A, 04:30)
Commissioner Graves summarized a new benefit was added for county employees at the beginning of this year, and
this resolution adopts a policy to match that change. He asked why a policy is needed, and Utah County Deputy
Attorney Robert J. Moore outlined the discrepancies between benefits statements and county policy related to ongoing
UTAH COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

payments to spouses and children. Commissioner Graves mentioned the 26-year-old federal age compared to the 21-
year-old limit, and Utah County Human Resources Director Cammie Taylor noted it had not yet been presented as an
option. Mr. Moore explained the Board could make that decision as county policy, as there is no state law defining
the term. They also discussed the redlined draft of the resolution and differences in ages of children compared to
married and dependent children.

            Commissioner Lee made the motion to continue Regular Agenda Item No. 2,
