Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council held Tuesday, July 7, 2020, at the Lehi
City Administration Building, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at
approximately 4:00 PM.

Present:     Mark Johnson, Mayor
             Paige Albrecht, Council Member
             Chris Condie, Council Member
             Paul Hancock, Council Member
             Katie Koivisto, Council Member
             Mike Southwick, Council Member

Others Present:     Jason Walker, City Administrator; Cameron Boyle, Assistant City
Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney; Kim Struthers, Community Development Director;
Lorin Powell, City Engineer; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Steve Marchbanks, Parks
Superintendent; Beau Thomas, Assistant to the City Administrator; Shaye Ruitenbeek,
Management Analyst; Teisha Wilson, City Recorder

1. Welcome Breakfast
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Mayor
   Johnson offered the prayer.

2. Discussion or Round Up and Rodeo Celebration
   Due to the current conditions with the pandemic, the Rodeo Committee decided to cancel the
   event. The Council also decided to postpone the Round-Up Celebration week activities, but to
   see if it was possible to still have a parade as has been done in other communities.

3. Site Tour of North Marina with Saratoga Springs City Council (5:00 p.m. 2300 West
   Saratoga Road)
   This was hosted by Saratoga Springs.

4. Dinner and Discussion of Joint Planning Efforts for the North Marina (6:00 p.m.
   Saratoga Springs Police and Court Facility, 367 S Saratoga Road, Saratoga Springs)
   This was hosted by Saratoga Springs.

5. Adjournment. With no further business to come before the City Council at this time the
   meeting adjourned.
