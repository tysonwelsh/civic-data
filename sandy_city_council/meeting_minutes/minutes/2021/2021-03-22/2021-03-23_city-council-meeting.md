Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zoltanski spoke about the Central Wasatch Commission summit last
                    Friday. She asked for resident feedback and encouraged proactive community
                    engagement from residents to help guide the decision for a regional transportation plan.
                    The next CWC meeting is April 5th. She thanked Council Member Houseman for her
                    work on the CWC.



                    Council Member Robinson asked whether the recycling infographic on the city website
                    would be updated. He also asked for additional clarification regarding the recent
                    emergency declaration issued by the Governor.

                    Council Member Houseman spoke about a new program sponsored by the Sandy Club:
                    Sandy Club Gives Back. This month's service project included making fleece blankets
                    and donating the items to the Utah Humane Society. She acknowledged the Sandy
                    Senior Center for their innovative outreach efforts to keep our residents informed, engaged
                    and involved. Ms. Houseman thanked all who participated in the statewide food drive for
                    the Utah Food Bank. She spoke about the Central Wasatch Commission summit and the
                    shared priorities among the associated municipalities and invited residents to visit the
                    CWC website to learn more information.

                    Council Member Stroud encouraged all to attend the next Community Arts Guild play,
                    Frozen Jr. Please go to the website for more information.



Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Bradburn briefed the Council on the Covid vaccination process and provided details
                    about the Governor's recent emergency declaration. He spoke about the City's efforts to
                    reduce water usage and to educate the public on water-wise efforts to promote water
                    conservation. He also spoke about the Central Wasatch Commission and while the City
                    supports the CWC priorities, he felt it was premature to define transportation solutions
                    resulting in a multi-billion dollar investment without gathering more information from area
                    experts, businesses and the public.

CAO Report

                    Matt Huish, CAO, said the City is updating the infographic on the website. He thanked
                    Nick Duerksen for his efforts working with the Shops at South Town development. He also
                    mentioned the Parks and Recreation Department recently received $14,000 in grants.

2.         21-115                 Administrative Services leading a discussion of the cyber security
                                  landscape and emerging trends.
                   Attachments:   Cyber Security Update

                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director, gave a presentation on Cyber Security. He reviewed the
                                  latest trends used to infiltrate an organization's technology. Ransomware, phishing, and
                                  malware are some of the techniques used by cyber-attackers. Mr. Kelley reviewed
                                  cyber-attacks that have occurred in other municipalities and businesses and the need for
                                  cyber insurance. The City is taking these threats very seriously.
