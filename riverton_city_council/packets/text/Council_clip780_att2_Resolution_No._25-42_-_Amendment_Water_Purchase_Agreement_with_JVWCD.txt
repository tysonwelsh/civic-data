                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 25-42

A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE EXECUTION OF AN AMENDMENT TO WATER
   PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND PETITION WITH JORDAN VALLEY WATER
                    CONSERVANCY DISTRICT.

       WHEREAS, On January 6, 2015, the Riverton City Council approved Resolution 15-04,
authorizing the Mayor to execute a Water Purchase Agreement and Petition to receive wholesale
culinary water service from the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District (“District”); and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has continued to receive satisfactory culinary water service
from the District; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City’s culinary water demands continue to grow with its rising
population; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the Water Purchase
Agreement and Petition executed by the District needs to be amended to enable the City to
secure a minimum of 5,000 acre-feet of culinary water per year; and

       WHEREAS, an Amendment to Water Purchase Agreement and Petition has been
prepared to increase the minimum purchase amounts to be secured for Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City does hereby determine that it is in the
best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to approve the
Agreement and its terms.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby approves the terms of the Amendment to Water
          Purchase Agreement and Petition and authorizes the Mayor to execute the
          Agreement. The Mayor is authorized to Sign the Agreement upon verification that
          the same is approved as to legal form by the Riverton City Attorney. The effective
          date of the Agreement shall be upon the date it is approved by the Governing Board
          of the District.

       2. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.
