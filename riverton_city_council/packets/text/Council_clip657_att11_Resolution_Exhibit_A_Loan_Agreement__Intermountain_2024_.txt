                                        LOAN AGREEMENT

       THIS IS A LOAN AGREEMENT (this “Loan Agreement”) dated as of May 1, 2024, between
THE CITY OF RIVERTON, UTAH, a body politic and corporate and a political subdivision of the State
of Utah created and existing under and by virtue of the Constitution and laws of the State of Utah
(the “City”), and IHC HEALTH SERVICES, INC., a Utah nonprofit corporation (“Health Services”).

                                           RECITALS

        Reference is hereby made to the Bond Trust Indenture dated as of May 1, 2024 (the “Bond
Indenture”), between the City and Zions Bancorporation, N.A., a national banking association, as
bond trustee (the “Bond Trustee”), and to the Master Indenture (as hereinafter defined) for
definitions of various terms used herein.

        The Utah Industrial Facilities and Development Act, Chapter 17 of Title 11, Utah Code
Annotated 1953, as amended (the “Act”), authorizes municipalities in the State of Utah to issue
revenue bonds to finance the acquisition of projects, including hospital projects for corporations
engaged in health care services, for the purpose of protecting the health and welfare of the citizens
of the State of Utah, and to improve local health and the general welfare by inducing corporations
engaged in health care services to locate, relocate, modernize, or expand in the State of Utah.
Municipalities are also authorized under the Act and the Utah Refunding Bond Act, Chapter 27 of
Title 11, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended (the “Refunding Act”), to issue revenue
refunding bonds to refund revenue bonds previously issued under the Act.

        The Interlocal Cooperation Act, Chapter 13 of Title 11, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as
amended (the “Interlocal Cooperation Act”), authorizes cities, towns and counties in the State of
Utah (“Public Agencies”) to enter into contracts with one another in order to make the most
efficient use of their powers by enabling them to cooperate with each other on a basis of mutual
advantage and to provide the benefit of economies of scale for the overall promotion of the general
welfare of the State of Utah. Public Agencies are authorized under the Act to enter into interlocal
cooperation agreements to provide for the issuance of bonds under the Act on behalf of all of the
signatories to the interlocal cooperation agreement by one of the signatories to such interlocal
cooperation agreement for the financing or acquisition of projects qualifying as a project under the
Act or for the refinancing thereof.

         In order to accomplish the purposes set forth above and hereinafter further described, the
City, Box Elder County, Utah, Cache County, Utah, Davis County, Utah, Salt Lake County, Utah,
Summit County, Utah, Utah County, Utah, Washington County, Utah and Weber County, Utah
(the “Participants”), have entered into the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement dated as of April 1,
2007 (the “Interlocal Agreement”) to provide for the financing or refinancing of certain health care
facilities, and the City, pursuant to the terms of the Interlocal Agreement, has agreed to issue its
revenue bonds on behalf of the City and other Participants and loan the proceeds thereof Health
Services to accomplish such purposes.

       On May 15, 2012, the City issued its City of Riverton, Utah Hospital Revenue Refunding
Bonds, Series 2012A (IHC Health Services, Inc.) (the “Series 2012A Bonds”) in an aggregate
principal amount of $200,000,000 for the purpose of currently refunding all outstanding City of
Riverton, Utah Hospital Revenue Bonds, Series 2007A (IHC Health Services, Inc.) issued in an
aggregate principal amount of $200,000,000. The Series 2012A Bonds were issued pursuant to
the provisions of the Indenture of Trust and Pledge dated as of May 1, 2012, between the City and
Zions First National Bank (now known as Zions Bancorporation, N.A.), as trustee.

        The proceeds of the sale of the Series 2012A Bonds were loaned by the City to Health
Services pursuant to the Loan Agreement dated as of May 1, 2012, and such loan was evidenced
by the Series W Note (Riverton 2012A Financing) of Health Services (the “Series 2012A Note”),
issued under the Master Indenture hereinafter referred to.

         Health Services desires to prepay the Series 2012A Note and has requested the City to
assist it in the current refunding of all outstanding Series 2012A Bonds. Acting pursuant to the
Act, the Refunding Act and the Interlocal Cooperation Act, the City, as authorized by resolution
adopted by its governing body on April 16, 2024, is issuing its Hospital Revenue Refunding Bonds,
Series 2024 (Intermountain Health) (the “Bonds”) in the original aggregate principal amount of
$200,000,000.

       The proceeds of the Bonds will be loaned by the City to Health Services pursuant to this
Loan Agreement. In order to provide security for the repayment of the Bonds, the Obligated Group
is concurrently with the delivery hereof issuing to the Bond Trustee, as assignee of the City, the
Series QQQ Note (Riverton 2024 Financing) (the “Note”), dated the Date of Issuance, under
Supplemental Indenture Number 57 dated as of May 1, 2024 (the “Supplemental Indenture”),
among Intermountain Health Care, Inc. (the “Corporation”), Health Services, the other Obligated
Group Members and Zions Bancorporation, N.A., as successor trustee (the “Master Trustee”),
supplementing the Master Trust Indenture (Amended and Restated) dated as of November 15,
2003, as previously supplemented and amended (the “Master Indenture”), among the Corporation,
Health Services, the other Obligated Group Members and the Master Trustee.

       Pursuant to the Bond Indenture, the City will pledge and assign the Note and its rights
under this Loan Agreement (other than Unassigned Rights) to the Bond Trustee as security for the
Bonds. The Bonds will be payable from payments to be made by Health Services hereunder, from
amounts payable by the Obligated Group Members on the Note and from amounts held in certain
funds established under the Bond Indenture.

        In consideration of the premises, the respective representations and agreements contained
herein, the acceptance of the Note by the City to evidence said loan to Health Services and for
other good and valuable consideration, the receipt whereof is hereby acknowledged, and in order
to secure the payments to be made by Health Services described herein and the performance of all
the covenants of Health Services contained herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:
