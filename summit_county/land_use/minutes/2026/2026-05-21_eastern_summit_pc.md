3)
Continued Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for the Raduenz Commercial Stables, Riding Arena and Event Center, located at 388 East Boulderville Road, Summit County, UT; Parcel WMS-5. Applicant: Kim and Tim Raduenz; Administrative Process. Project #25-015.
Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner. (6:05 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Raduenz Commercial Stables and Riding Arena CUP - CONTINUED PUBLIC HEARING.pdf
Amir Caus,
AICP Senior Planner
, reintroduced the Raduenz Commercial Stables, Riding Arena, and Event Center CUP.  Planner Caus stated that, after hearing feedback from the public and the Commission, the request for an Event Center had been removed, and the number of daily visitors was now limited to 30 people. Staff recommended approval of the Conditional Use Permit as revised. (6:05 P.M.)
Applicant Kim Raduenz briefly addressed the commission at 6:07 p.m.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions and commented on the revised application. Planner Caus, the applicants, and Civil Deputy Attorney Helen Strachan responded to the concerns. (6:08 P.M.)
Commission Chair Peterson opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:31 p.m.
Augusta Clarke was called to speak at 6:31 p.m.
Melissa Band was called to speak at 6:34 p.m.
Gavin Cutler was called to speak at 6:36 p.m.
Stephanie Russell was called to speak at 6:39 p.m.
David McWilliams was called to speak at 640 p.m.
Kaylee Cassidy was called to speak at 643 p.m.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:44 p.m.
Commissioners asked final questions about the application to which Senior Planner Caus responded. (6:45 P.M.)
Bridget Hayes made a motion to approve the proposed Conditional Use Permit for parcel WMS-5 pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Paul Weller seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).

Planner
, presented an amendment to an existing CUP at 5357 W Browns Canyon Road, Parcel SS-76-9. Staff recommended approval of the Conditional Use Permit. (6:55 P.M.)
Commissioners asked clarifying questions to which Planner Osman, the applicant, Scott Schofield, Civil Engineer, Gus Sherry, and Principal Planner Ray Milliner responded. (6:58 P.M.)

No one came forward to speak.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the meeting for public hearing at 7:19 p.m.
Commissioners asked final questions regarding the recycling activities. Mr. Schofield and Planner Osman addressed the additional concerns. (7:20 P.M.)
Commission Chair Peterson asked if there was a motion to approve the amendment to the CUP with the change of striking condition number 14 and changing condition number 8 to be Monday through Sunday.
Bridget Hayes made a motion to approve the proposed amendment to the existing Conditional Use Permit for Parcel SS-76-9 pursuant to the findings of fact, modified conditions of approval and conclusions of law found in the staff report. Seth Bowen seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
