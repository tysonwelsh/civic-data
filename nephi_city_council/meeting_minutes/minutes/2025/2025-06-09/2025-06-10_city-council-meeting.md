                       NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                  June 10, 2025
                                     7:00 P.M
                                    21 E 100 N
                                Nephi, UT 84648



1. Abby Ivory and Stephanie Hardy shared an update with the council on the Taste of
   Central Utah Event held in May. Partners raised $21,877.64 in grants to put this on.
   They had provided educational classes once a month leading up to the event. The
   purpose of this was to help businesses commercialize their food business. The reach
   extended to 82 different businesses, 9 different counties and 2 different states. The
   event itself included 25 vendors from across the state and seven awards were given. It
   was well attended and vendors and attendees expressed interest in doing it again.


2. Seth Atkinson stated that the annexation request for property from 5 th south to 8th south
   on 4th west is now at a level 2. The request is to move it to level one. The city will need
   a concept plan from the requestee, Derrik Jacobson, and then will discuss the potential
   impact on city services and the infrastructure. Mr. Atkinson suggested a low-density
   zoning area, considering the current developments. The council decided to review the
   request.

3. The council reviewed 2 agreements:
          a. UORG Agreement
             The agreement is to award funding for the Jamestowne pump track park
             project.
          b. American Ramp Company Agreement
               This agreement states that we will pay the company for the work they are going to
               do on the track.
   The council decided to bring the agreements back for action consideration at the next council
   meeting.

4. Seth Atkinson also discussed the Mona City Gas Agreement detailing the services to be
   provided and the financial terms. The council agreed to review the agreement and
   consider it for action at the next meeting.
5. Seth Atkinson described the Utah Patriot Partner program and the mayor’s desire for the
   city to be involved. It is a pledge of support to Utah’s military members, veterans, and
   spouses to extend priority consideration in our employment practices. Lisa Brough
   mentioned the city’s existing military support which offers discounts on electrical bill for
   deployed military personnel. The council was interested in learning more and having
   someone come and do a presentation.

6. Mike Booth, Nephi City Golf Professional, wanted approval from the council to move
   forward with acquiring engineered plans for the new clubhouse design. The golf course
   will use funds from their capital budget for this purpose. Surplus funds will be used to
   fund this building. The council approved moving forward with the engineered plans.

7. Administrative and City Council Updates
         a. Mr. Atkinson shared the financial reports for fiscal year 2024-2025
         b. Previous to covid a scout troop would travel through Nephi and stay at our
             park. The troop requested permission to do so again June 21 and June 29.
         c. Mr. Atkinson shared the concerns of the craft fair organizer about conflict
             with the Farmer’s Market during the Ute Stampede. After discussing the
             potential impact the council decided to allow both events to proceed.
         d. Mr. Atkinson relayed the fact that there was a request to the city to add
             power to a billboard and create a digital sign. Our current code prohibits new
             billboards in Nephi. The planning and zoning committee was seeking
             clarification as to whether the council thought changing the sign to digital
             was considered a ‘new’ sign. The council decided to table the decision for
             further discussion.
         e. Mr. Atkinson stated that he had attended the Chamber of Commerce
             luncheon and delivered the State of the City Address.
         f. Lisa Brough stated that the parade permit had been acquired from UDOT for
             the Ute Stampede.
         g. Mrs. Brough announced that 5 candidates had registered to run for City
             Council in the upcoming election and all were in attendance tonight:
             Incumbent Skip Worwood, Incumbent Jeramie Callaway, Scott Jackson, Bart
             Miller, and Tate Douglas.
         h. Councilor Cowan stated that she was told Jamestowne, where the new pump
             track will be, has an ‘e’ at the end.
         i. Councilor Cowan mentioned that Emily Harris a former Miss Nephi would be
             competing in the Miss Utah pageant coming up.
              j. Councilor Parady stated that Jenson refrigeration is looking for Phase 2 and 3
                 of their project and would like to sit down and talk.
              k. Councilor Parady announced that Barrett thinks they own a rail spur. Mr.
                 Atkinson clarified that Nephi city has an easement.
              l. Councilor Parady explained that Brent Boswell, head of economic
                 development for Juab County, would like to be more involved in business
                 stuff for Nephi city.
              m. Councilor Callaway declared that he would be meeting with the Ute
                 Stampede committee and asked if anyone had anything they needed him to
                 discuss with them. The council expressed interest in having the Grand
                 Marshalls attend a council meeting and do the pledge.
              n. Councilor Skip Worwood attended the ribbon cutting for Desert Stream in the
                 place of the mayor. Councilor Cowan also attended.
