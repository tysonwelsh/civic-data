                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-24

          A RESOLUTION APPOINTING VOLUNTEER MEMBERS TO THE
          RIVERTON CITY EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS COMMITTEE

        WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance No. 18-07 and Resolution No.
18-34, the Mayor shall appoint with advice and consent of the City Council, residents of
Riverton City to serve on the Emergency Preparedness Committee; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the following volunteer
appointments: Steve Merrill, Todd Glover, James Huntington, Cameron Hess, Lynn Nunes, and
Terry Seguin;

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the appointees are
residents of Riverton City and are willing and capable of serving on the Emergency Preparedness
Committee; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the appointment of the Mayor's
nomination to the Emergency Preparedness Committee;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the appointments of Steve
          Merrill, Todd Glover, James Huntington, Cameron Hess, Lynn Nunes, and
          Terry Seguin to the Riverton City Emergency Preparedness Committee, which
          term shall end December 31, 2025.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 15th day of
March, 2022 by the following vote:
