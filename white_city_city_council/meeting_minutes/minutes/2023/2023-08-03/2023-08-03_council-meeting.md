                                           Workshop

Update on Engineering Projects

               Shane Ellis, Engineer, Salt Lake County Engineering and Flood Control, stated
Ski Connect is in its second phase. The new pedestrian ramp has been completed and the
sidewalk will be finished soon.

The Sego Lily project went out for bid. The contractor that won the bid plans to do the entire
project next year. There is not an official schedule, but it will be completed within four months.

The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) presented grant projects to the Board of
Trustees for its approval. The projects are the canal trail lights, 1300 E Sego Lily ADA compliant
crossing, and the sidewalk on Onyx Lane.

Justice Court, Funding of Projects, and ARPA Funds

               Mayor Flint stated the Council decided to move forward with finding a justice court
to contract with. Negotiations with Midvale City are underway, with the aim of finalizing this by
year-end.

                Rori Andreason stated Midvale City is working on how it will bill White City with
the option of billing quarterly. The draft agreement should be finished soon.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                Chief Ken Aldridge stated the UFA is actively updating its strategic plan.
Wildland fire activities in the area have been moderate, with recent storms significantly helping.
Wildland crews continue to help with fuel mitigation efforts throughout the UFA. He reviewed
retirements, promotions, and lateral hires.

Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Detective Josh Smith reviewed calls for service for the month of July. The
predominant calls were related to public order, including civil matters and welfare checks. There
has been an increase in transient activity. Speed and traffic enforcement efforts are ongoing with
the speed trailer being deployed at two locations. The Community Police Academy is scheduled
to start on August 29 and continue every Tuesday until November 14, 2023.

               Chief Randy Thomas stated UPD has made progress toward an overall
reorganization.

Amending the White City Metro Township Fee Schedule

              Erin O’Kelley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District,
reviewed the following resolution amending the White City Metro Township Fee Schedule to
include Zoning Fees previously included in Title 19:

RESOLUTION NO. 23-08-02                                                          Date: August 3, 2023

        A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP ADOPTING THE
        AMENDED 2023 FEE SCHEDULE

                                              RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) inherited the version of Salt
Lake County’s Code that was in existence of the date of White City’s incorporation in 2017
pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-414(3); and

       WHEREAS, as a result, Section 19.94.070 of the White City Metro Township Code (the
“Code”) set forth the civil penalties for zoning violation (the “Civil Penalties”) within the Code rather
than within the White City Fee Schedule; and

      WHEREAS, as part of White City’s recodification of its Code, the White City Council (the
“Council”) desires to move the Civil Penalties from Title 19 of the Code to the White City Fee
Schedule; and

       WHEREAS, when the Council adopted the 2023 White City Fee Schedule on December
21, 2022, the revisions to Title 19 were still in process and, as a result, the Council adopted the
fee schedule subject to the civil penalties remaining in Title 19 until such time as Title 19 could be
restated to remove the Civil Penalties; and

        WHEREAS, White City has adopted a final version of Title 19 that does not include the
Civil Penalties; and

       WHEREAS, because Title 19 no longer includes the Civil Penalties, the Council desires
to amend the 2023 Fee Schedule to include the Civil Penalties that were previously set forth in
Section 19.94.070; and


                                                   4
DATE            THURSDAY                          AUGUST                                    3, 2023

       WHEREAS, the Council also desires to revise language in Section 5.02.060 of the Code
regarding the fees White City applies to business license withdrawal fees to coincide with the
2023 Fee Schedule.

    NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL that effective immediately:

1.     The attached amended 2023 fee schedule is adopted: and

2.     Section 5.02.060.B of the Code is amended to read as follows:

        If, prior to a license being granted or issued, the application for a license is withdrawn by
the applicant for any reason, the license official shall retain a portion of the application fee if
authorized to do so in the metro township fee schedule. However, the mayor may, in their
discretion, authorize the license official to refund the entire application fee if they determine a
refund is appropriate under the circumstances.

3.    White City staff are instructed to file this resolution and the attached amended fee
schedule with the White City Metro Township Clerk; and

4.     Staff are instructed to post this resolution and the attached amended fee schedule 2023
fee schedule to the White City Metro Township website and to the “Municipal Resolutions Book”
within White City’s Municipal Code; and

5.     Pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-719, the amended 2023 fee schedule will go into effect on
the date this resolution is approved and adopted.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED this 3rd day of August 2023.

Council Reports

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)

               Mayor Flint stated the budgeting process is proceeding. All departments that
contract with the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) have presented their
budgets. The Justice Courts had an 86 percent increase in funding for the next year. There are
concerns regarding projects funded by the bond, as not all of them have gone out to bid, making
it uncertain how much these projects will ultimately cost.

Animal Services

               Council Member Price stated the Spayghetti and No Balls fundraiser will be held
on August 25, 2023. A $50,000 grant was awarded to proceed with a robo-cat trap. Animal

Mosquito Abatement District

               Council Member Price stated a new board member has been appointed. The
district has been using a treatment that is effective in heavily vegetative areas. The board will
determine whether it wants to invest in the equipment to continue the treatment. Davis County
has had seven pools of water tested positive for West Nile Virus. There have been no human
cases so far.
