RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only — the full ordinance text is not attached on PMN)

To be published on Friday, November 22, 2019, in the Salt Lake Tribune and Deseret News
City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption - On November 19, 2019, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1412, adopting the official Draper City General Plan, including elements of Moderate Income Housing, Master Transportation Plan, and Land Use Map. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder's Office and online at www.draper.ut.us. Published this 22nd day of November, 2019. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
