Citizen Public Input

                 Christy stated she is concerned about the crime in Kearns and how police officers
respond to individuals with PTSD and other mental health matters. She has been in a situation
with police officers where she has shut down and became non-responsive because of her PTSD.
The officer kept trying to get her to talk and it became a traumatizing situation. Some officers
may not have had training on how to deal with people with PTSD. She had to report an incident
a few weeks ago and it was frightening. She has spoken to Chief Levi Hughes and was supposed
to call him back. She would be happy to meet with Chief Hughes.

               Mayor Bush stated Chief Hughes is reaching out to the community to address
these types of situations; Christy’s experience could help the community.

CARES Act Update

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving an agreement between
Kearns and Salt Lake County to receive $1,015,500.82 from the Salt Lake County CARES Act
Fund for certain COVID relief activities:

RESOLUTION NO. 20-06-01                                                     Date: June 25, 2020

      A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
      AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN KEARNS AND SALT LAKE COUNTY TO RECEIVE
      $1,015,500.82 FROM THE SALT LAKE COUNTY CARES ACT FUND FOR
      CERTAIN COVID RELIEF ACTIVITIES

                                          RECITALS

   A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code Annotated
      (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq;

   B. The Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body for
      Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501;

   C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to UCA Title 17: COUNTIES;

   D. The CARES Act is a law passed by the Federal Government that provided for an
      appropriation of funds to the States and certain Counties for certain governmental
      emergency costs and economic relief efforts related to the COVID-19 pandemic;

   E. In May 2020, the State of Utah and Salt Lake County received their CARES Act
      appropriations from the Federal Government;

   F. In late May 2020, the County, taking a cue from the State regarding the management of
      CARES Act funds, and not compelled by the CARES Act to do so, decided it would send
      amounts of their appropriation to the municipalities of the County to fund and manage local
      COVID-19 efforts;

   G. Pursuant to the County’s desire to promote local engagement in the COVID0-19 relief
      effort, Kearns received the attached agreement on June 8, 2020 for consideration and
      possible approval of Kearns; and

   H. In addition to providing funding to municipalities, the County will retain a portion of the
      CARES Act funding it has received to operate a program that will assist businesses
      affected by COVID-19, including businesses within Kearns.

                                        RESOLUTION

      THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township effective immediately:

Kearns Metro Township Facebook Page

              Mayor Bush left the meeting and Council Member Snow chaired the remaining
item on the agenda.

                                               3
DATE            MONDAY                            JUNE                                         25, 2020


                Nathan Bracken stated issues with the Kearns Metro Township Facebook page
have arisen where individuals are making abusive comments against other residents and their
families. The Council has been given a draft social media policy but is waiting to adopt it until an
administrative assistant is hired. The administrative assistant will moderate the social media page.
If a government agency creates a public forum and starts weighing in on what people can or
cannot say, it may infringe on their 1st amendment rights. Without rules on what people can or
cannot say, it puts the Council in a difficult situation. Once the policy is in place, the Council should
not be the ones to make a judgement call whether a comment complies with the rules. The
administrative assistant will make the first call on whether the comment violates the policy or not.
If the commenter disagrees with the decision, then they can appeal to the MSD. If the commenter
disagrees with the MSD, they can take the issue to court. Until the social medial policy is finalized,
the Council may want to consider shutting the Facebook page down.

                 Council Member Snow stated she thinks the Facebook page should be shut down
until the social media policy is adopted. If a resident has a problem, they can attend the meetings
and voice their opinions.

                Council Member Butterfield stated the new Facebook page can be set up to push
information to residents and if there is an issue the resident can send a direct message with their
concern. The current Facebook page should be archived so if there is an issue, the Council can
reference back to it.

             Council Member Schaeffer stated not everyone knows about the Kearns website
and uses Facebook as their source of information.

               Council Member Snow stated the Facebook page should be taken down for 2
months until guidelines are in place. Rules cannot be put on the current Facebook page, a new
one will need to be created with the rules.

                Mr. Bracken stated social media can be subject to GRAMA. He also agrees that
the Facebook page should be archived. He is working on a records policy to reflect how the Metro
Township operates. He recommends the Council review the social media policy and the records
policy, when it is finalized.

                Council Member Snow asked if the Council has the legal right to shut the page
down.

                Mr. Bracken stated the Council does not have an obligation to have a Facebook
account.

             Council Member Schaeffer asked if a statement needs to be made to notify
members of the Facebook page that the page will be shut down.

               Mr. Bracken stated he can work with Council Member Snow to draft a statement
informing residents that the page is being shut down until the Council develops a clear social
media policy.

               Council Member Butterfield, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
temporarily suspend the Kearns Metro Township Official Facebook page until the development of
a social medial and records policy is adopted, direct Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, to work with
Council Member Snow to develop an informational statement to post on Facebook, and archive
the current Facebook page. The motion passed unanimously. Mayor Bush was absent for the
vote.
