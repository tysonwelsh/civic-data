                                ORDINANCE NO. 1554



AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLE 1 OF THE DRAPER
CITY MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING THE CITY SEAL AND OTHER CITY SYMBOLS.

      WHEREAS, Title 1 establishes the city seal; and

       WHEREAS, it is necessary from time to time to revise certain terms of the
Draper City Municipal Code to provide clarification and additional protections for
the city; and

      WHEREAS, the city has used additional symbols that should be protected and
used appropriately; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the proposed text amendment and
finds good cause to revise the terms and provisions of Title 1.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following
findings to amend Title 1 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) appropriate use of
the city seal should be defined and a custodian assigned; 2) the official city logo and
variations thereof should be identified, and a custodian assigned; 3) other visual
representations of the city should be identified and a custodian assigned; 4)
unauthorized use of these visual representations of the city should be defined and
the consequence for unauthorized use established; 5) the city has an interest in
ensuring city symbols are identified and protected to avoid likelihood of confusion;
and 6) the proposed amendment complies with state and federal law.

      Section 2. Revision. Section 1-1-030 of the Draper City Municipal Code is
hereby amended to read as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated
herein by reference.

      Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to
correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis errors prior to
submitting the Ordinance to the codifier.
       Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

      Section 5. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to
the date of final passage.




PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 2ND DAY OF AUGUST, 2022.
