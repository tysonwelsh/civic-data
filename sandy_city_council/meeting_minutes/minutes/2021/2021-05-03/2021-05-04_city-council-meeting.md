2.         21-178                 Council discussion and direction regarding the number of candidates
                                  available for rank on the ranked choice ballot.
                     Sponsors: Sharkey

                   Attachments:   RCV Rankings Memo
                                  RCV in Utah - Key stats from 2019

                                  Council Member Sharkey introduced the item and asked for feedback. Council had a
                                  discussion regarding the number of rankings that the City should consider using for the
                                  ranked choice voting election in November. RCV allows for up to ten rankings. The
                                  Council expressed a desire for the residents to have the full experience of ranked choice
                                  voting and were in favor of utilizing the full ten rankings the RVC system allows for. They
                                  also spoke about the need to educate the community about RCV and gather resident
                                  feedback after the election.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Houseman acknowledged Teacher Appreciation Week and she thanked
                    Ms. Stroud and educators for all they do to educate our children. She spoke about
                    Governor Cox's Executive Order that implements water conservation measures at all
                    state facilities and encourages local governments to take similar measures. She thanked
                    the city staff for the communication efforts to educate the residents on water conservation
                    and acknowledged all those who work so hard to ensure we have access to safe water.

                    Council Member Robinson thanked Ms. Stroud and acknowledged Teacher Appreciation
                    Week. He thanked the Parks and Recreation Department for their efforts coordinating the
                    Fiesta 5K event this past weekend. It was a great event and he encouraged the
                    community to participate in future 5K runs. He also discussed the end game bill taking
                    effect and the impact on the city's public meetings. He expressed a desire for the Council
                    to continue its discussion regarding in-person City Council meetings.

                    Council Member Zoltanski recently spent the morning with the Public Works mechanics
                    and staff and learned about their job and work environment. She spoke about the
                    important work provided by this department in maintaining the City's fleet. She thanked
                    Mayor Bradburn and the Public Works department for their time.

                    Council Member Stroud thanked the Fire Department for spending time with her this past
                    weekend and for taking her on a ride-a-long They provided a great deal of insight and
                    information regarding the impact speed bumps have on emergency vehicles.She
                    appreciated their time.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Bradburn spoke about the great job done by Public Works staff in the sign and
                    welding shop. He mentioned the city ordinances and community education programs
                    supporting water conservation efforts. He spoke about the impact of the End Game
                    legislation on the city and businesses. He mentioned that the City is applying for a $20
                    million dollar grant. He wished Chief Severson and Chief Cline a Happy Birthday.




CAO Report

                    Matt Huish, CAO, mentioned the Google Fiber contract has been executed and he
                    thanked City staff who were instrumental in working on the contract.
