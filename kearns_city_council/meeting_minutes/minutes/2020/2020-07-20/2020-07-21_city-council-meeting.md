CARES ACT Funding – Purchase of Cloth Masks for Community

                Mayor Kelly Bush stated she would like to purchase masks for the Kearns
community using some of the CARES Act Funding since all the schools and business are now
requiring that masks be worn. She has done some research with Council Member Snow looking
into the costs of these masks. The Governor’s Office gave them the name of two companies, one

                                                  4
DATE           MONDAY                         JULY                                      21, 2020

in Utah at $3.25 per mask and another one in New Jersey at $4.25 per mask. Other quotes she
has received are for $5 and $6 plus shipping.

                Council Member Snow stated the company that makes masks for $3.25 each are
willing to put a logo on it as well. The Council should order 10,000 masks on the first order
because schools are going to open. The Council could then go with smaller orders after that.

               Council Member Schaeffer stated the $3.25 per mask price sounds good. He did
not want to order so many that there would be a lot of left-over masks.

                Council Member Snow stated if the Council did not like the mask then it could
order from a different company on the next order. The masks come in small, medium, and large.
So, with the 10,000, the Council will be able to determine what size is mostly used and order more
of that the next time.

               Mayor Bush stated she did not know if the $3.25 price included shipping or not.

              Council Member Schaeffer stated if the company was local, he could pick them
up. He has a big truck.

              Mayor Bush asked if the Council wanted a logo on the mask. She suggested the
masks be black with green lettering that simply stated “Kearns Strong.” She felt the Council
should not use the masks to advertise but to make it about unity and health.

              Council Member Butterfield asked if the masks would be out of cotton. Cotton
masks are not as hot and heavy as other material. She liked the logo “Kearns Strong.”

             Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Scaheffer, moved to order
10,000 masks at a cost of $3.25 each. The motion passed unanimously.

Rental of Storage Unit

               Mayor Kelly Bush stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District will
discuss the storage of speed trailers for the Unified Police Department. So the Council did not
need to rent a storage unit as big as it discussed during the July 13, 2020, meeting. She has
called around and got prices for different sizes. A 5x5 unit would cost $47.00 a month; however,
she thought this might be too small. A 10x10 unit cost $89.00 a month, which would probably be
the size needed. The cost does not include insurance. The unit is available at Storage Etc.

              Council Member Butterfield suggested the Council go with the 10x10 unit and
see how that works. If a bigger unit is needed, then the Council could do that later.

               Council Member Butterfield, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
rent a 10x10 storage unit. The motion passed unanimously.

Additional CARES ACT Funding Discussion

                Mayor Kelly Bush stated the Unified Fire Authority had additional costs related to
the COVID-19 virus. It applied for grants but still fell short. UFA has estimated that it will need an
additional $630,000 by the end of the year. It was decided that UFA would be asking its members
to contribute some of their CARES Act Funding to cover this shortage. Kearns will be asked to
contribute $52,710 based on its population and percentage of calls, which is 8.75 percent. There
was a concern that UFA might need additional funds come the end of the year; however, by that
time CARES Act Funding would have been returned to the County. UFA stated if additional funds
were needed, it would approach the County to get CARES Act Funding. She did not want to have
to raise taxes come next year to help pay for this. The Agreement that Kearns will need to enter
into for this money will have to hold Kearns harmless and she wanted to make sure this was
included in the agreement before it was signed.

One of the smaller townships did not receive a large amount of CARES Act Funding and the
amount charged to them resulted is a $578 shortage. She was surprised and disappointed to see
that none of the bigger municipalities, who received large amounts of CARES Act Funding did not
