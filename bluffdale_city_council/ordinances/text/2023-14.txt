                                  ORDINANCE NO.2023-14




AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 5.10 OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY CODE
RELATED TO PUBLIC SAFETY CRIMES AND OFFENSES



WHEREAS the City ofBluffdale maintains criminal ordinances not covered by state code;
WHEREAS the City desires to protect the health and welfare ofthe community;
WHEREAS the City desires to add an ordinance to address the situation ofindividuals
interfering with employees performing public duties;
WHEREAS the City Council finds that the proposed amendment will further the public health,
safety, and general welfare;


NOW,THEREFORE,THE BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL HEREBY ORDAINS AS
FOLLOWS:


     SECTION 1. Adoption of Bluffdale City Code. Chapter 5.10.080: INTERFERENCE
WITH AN EMPLOYEE is adopted as follows:
