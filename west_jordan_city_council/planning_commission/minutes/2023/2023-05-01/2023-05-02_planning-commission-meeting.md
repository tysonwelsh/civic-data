2.                 Text Amendment-      Petting Zoos; Amend the 2009 West Jordan City Code Sections 13- 5C-
                   4 and 13- 5G- 3 adding" Petting Zoos" as a conditional use in the P- C ( Planned
                   Community) and P- F ( Public Facilities) zones; and amend Section 13- 2- 3 adding" Petting
                   Zoos" to definitions; City- wide applicability; City of West Jordan ( applicant)

Larry Gardner said this amendment would add" petting zoo" to the P- C and P- F zoning districts. As a
conditional use, the planning commission would have the ability to assign conditions to mitigate any
identified negative impacts. West Jordan has one existing petting zoo, which is in Gardner Village. That
property was originally zoned agricultural but was later rezoned to Planned Community. This
amendment would facilitate moving the existing petting zoo to other areas of the site in conjunction
with       another           user.




Based on the analysis and findings contained in the staff report, staff recommended that the Planning
Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for this application.

Kent Shelton said residential uses are allowed in a P- C zone, so his concerns were with potential smells
and noise that could be impactful to residential                                                    uses.


Larry Gardner said the conditional aspect of the ordinance would give the Planning Commission the
opportunity to impose conditions such as hours of operation, distance from future or existing
residential, requirements to keep the operation in a pristine condition, or other appropriate conditions on
a case- by- case basis. He said if the Commission felt that there would be impacts that could not be
mitigated with conditions, then they could forward a negative recommendation to the City Council for
the ordinance amendment. However, he thought that the Planning Commission had sufficient discretion
to mitigate               any impacts        with      conditions.

Matt Quinney said he had been to Gardner Village multiple times, and he did not have any concerns
with the current petting zoo location. He asked if the intent was for the existing petting zoo to remain at
Gardner Village but just move to a different location within the Village.


Larry Gardner said the petting zoo is currently at the extreme south of the property and next to the train
entertainment venue. The train business owner would like to move the petting zoo within the confines
of the entertainment village and to make nicer pens and a more convenient way to view the animals.
The current location is a separate parcel that was agricultural at the time of approval, so they are limited
in their location. The amendment would allow the petting zoo to move to a different location, but they
would have to obtain a conditional use permit.


Catherine Richardson asked if the area would be enclosed. She mentioned that there are roaming ducks
at Gardner Village that are not in an enclosed area.


Larry Gardner said the animals are in covered pens and the area where they are housed at night would
be reconfigured. There are wild mallard ducks that come and go at Gardner Village, but the ducks in the
zoo would be the domesticated Muscovy type.

Ammon Allen opened the public hearing.

Dirk Burton, West Jordan resident, stated for the record that he is a board member of the Farm at
Gardner Village.


Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

MOTION:         Jay Thomas moved, based on the information and findings set forth in the staff
                report and upon the evidence and explanations received today, to forward a
                positive recommendation to the City Council for the text amendment regarding
                 Petting Zoos." The motion was seconded by Matt Quinney and passed 7- 0 in
                favor.


MOTION:         Matt Quinney moved to adjourn.

The meeting adjourned at 6: 10 p.m.
