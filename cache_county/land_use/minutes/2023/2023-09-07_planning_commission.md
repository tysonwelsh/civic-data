#3 Public Hearing (5:35 p.m.) – Amending Title 16 Subdivision Regulations 
Nelson presented on the needed changes due to changes in state law for the Title 16 Subdivision 
Regulations. 
Staff and Commission discussed how pre-application meetings are currently handled, 15-day deadline 
requirement, final plat and plat notes requirements, the new subdivision improvement plan section, and 
the number of times an application can be reviewed. 
06:08:00 
Sands motioned to open the public hearing; Parker seconded; Passed 4, 0.
