 7.      An update regarding the Police Department's Flock Cameras (23-078) (01:29:48)

         Troy Beebe, Police Chief, presented.

         Chief Troy Beebe presented on the flock cameras. He said that is an awesome program.
         They have only had it up and running for three months and their data reflects that. He
         explained what a flock camera is and explained that it is a license plate recognition
         system. It reads the plate and the AI takes a photo of the back of the vehicle so that it can
         be searched for if needed. It can also take into consideration specific features of the
         vehicle. The AI does not take facial recognition. It meets the state’s laws. He said that
         they did a one-year contract with it to be cautious due to possible future implications.
         Overall, it will be good for the City. He said that it is proactive and that they are
         recovering stolen vehicles for the city and other cities. They are making arrests and helps
         criminals from not coming to Provo because they know they will be caught. The system
         is a deterrent for criminals.

         Ms. MacKay asked where the cameras are. Mr. Beebe said they are up around the city.
         Mr. Beebe has said the State has not agreed to contract with the flock cameras in Provo.
         For the three months of data, Mr. Beebe showed there has been 100 cases. It has helped
         the police department solve crimes quicker. He said that if they had had this technology
         in more places in Provo, they would have been able to solve significant traffic incidents
         and crimes that have occurred in the past.

         Mr. Beebe touched on retail theft. They are able to solve retail theft because they can go
         back on the cameras. He said they have been able to solve hit-and-run events. He said if
         they did not have these flock cameras, crimes would have taken a lot longer to solve. He
         also explained that people from other states are coming to the county to steal things.
         There have been many severe cases of theft in nearby cities. He said that when certain
         crimes happen in Provo, the flock cameras allow for them to be solved quickly. He
         shared the example of a man who was hit on Center Street at 5:00am and they were able
         to arrest the driver of the vehicle by 11:00am because of the flock cameras. Overall, Mr.
         Beebe said the flock cameras are amazing.

         Mr. Beebe shared the timeline of the flock cameras when trying to solve a crime. Within
         hours, crimes can be solved. He shared a personal story about a friend of his that resides
         in Provo. He said that there was a three page suicide note left. They did not share where
         they were going. But because of flock cameras, they were able to find a hint on flock
         cameras and tracked this person to the Uintas. The person had gone farther to Jackson
         Hole and found that on the flock cameras there. Police officers in Wyoming were able to
         track this person and stopped the suicide. They would not have been able to do that
         without the flock cameras.

         Rachel Whipple confirmed that they want to keep doing this. Mr. Beebe said they do
