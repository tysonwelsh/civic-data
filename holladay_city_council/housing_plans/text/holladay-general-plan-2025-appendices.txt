in order to “provide for the health, safety, and welfare” of each municipality and its

C. ENGAGEMENT SUMMARY
PHASE 1 ENGAGEMENT SUMMARY
This summary consists of all public engagement completed throughout the Phase 1
segment of the Holladay Horizons process. This phase focused on getting the word
out to the community through visual messaging using a variety of tools, events and
locations throughout the City. This phase also included one on one listening sessions,
two Steering Committee meetings and a Questionnaire to help guide the visioning
process. The demographic data represented in this summary, collected throughout
Phase 1, provides a baseline for the rest of the process by helping identify which groups
will need more targeted engagement opportunities in the future and where efforts
can be refocused to hear underrepresented groups.

HOW WE REACHED THE COMMUNITY
Holladay is using a multifaceted approach to engage with the community. One on one
interviews, pop up events, social media posts, a questionnaire, and a project webpage
invited the community into the process. This allowed the project team to gather initial
feedback on what the issues and opportunities are in the community, and provides
a basis for the next phases. Overall, the process reached hundreds of community
members with the intent to reach more throughout the process.

VISIONS AND VALUES EVENT

  KEY TAKEAWAY
  The community strongly values preserving Holladay’s distinct character, natural
  environment, and neighborhood identity while cautiously supporting thoughtful,
  small-scale development. Residents emphasized protecting mature trees,
  maintaining low-density zoning, and enhancing walkability and green space.
  There is broad support for policies that promote environmental stewardship,
  expand pedestrian and trail infrastructure, and strengthen local businesses.
  This reflects a desire for growth that is intentional, inclusive, and aligned with
  Holladay’s small-town feel.
The following section is a high-level executive summary of the key themes
gathered from the Vision and Values Workshop Series.
The Vision and Value workshop series was held over two days, Monday, February 24th
from 6pm to 8pm and Saturday, March 1st from 10:30am to 12:30pm. These dates and
times provided flexible participation options for residents. The event was attended by
50+ participants including the mayor of Holladay, business owners, and residents. The
event featured two activities, a Future Land Use mapping activity and a board activity.
The Future Land Use mapping activity included 5 maps of the different focus areas
of Holladay, including one general map of Holladay. This allowed residents to better
orient themselves and to focus on one area at a time. Residents added color strips that
coordinated with the proposed future land use categories to “draw” their own vision
for the future.
The board activity had three separate themes:
  1. Future Land Use: Boards included existing and proposed land use categories and
     future land use map. Participants were encouraged to leave notes about each
     designation, including what types of uses belong in them, and the character they
     should reflect.
  2. Vision Statements: Boards displayed the draft vision statement for each theme.
     Participants were encouraged to reflect on whether the statement aligned with
     their vision for Holladay by responding with a “yes,” “no,” or “maybe” using a
     sticker. They were also invited to provide open-ended feedback by answering the
     question: “What would you change?”
  3. Existing Policies: Boards included existing policies and actions aligned with the
     draft themes. Participants were encouraged to consider which policies the city
     should prioritize to support both the draft themes and the previously identified
     priorities from Questionnaire #1.
