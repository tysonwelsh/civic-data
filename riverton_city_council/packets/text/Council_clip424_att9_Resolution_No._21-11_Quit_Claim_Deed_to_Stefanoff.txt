                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-11


    A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE RELINQUISHMENT OF TITLE IN REAL
 PROPERTY BY QUIT CLAIM DEED TO DENNIS STEVE STEFANOFF AND DIANE
  MARIE STEFANOFF PRITZKAU AS SUCCESSOR TRUSTEES OF THE STEVE V.
 STEFANOFF TRUST DATED MARCH 17, 1981, OR ANY SUCCESSORS IN TRUST,
AND DENNIS STEVE STEFANOFF AND DIANE MARIE STEFANOFF PRITZKAU AS
SUCCESSOR TRUSTEES OF THE EILEEN V. STEFANOFF TRUST DATED MARCH
17, 1981, OR ANY SUCCESSORS IN TRUST, AN UNDIVIDED ONE-HALF INTEREST
           TO EACH OF SAID TRUSTS (Affecting Parcel No. 27-33-201-026)

                                               AND

 APPROVING THE RELINQUISHMENT OF TITLE IN REAL PROPERTY BY QUIT
    CLAIM DEED TO JAN S. HEMSBURY (Affecting Parcel No. 27-33-201-027)


         WHEREAS, on June 23, 2005 the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT)
conveyed a quit claim deed parcel No. 23-33-201-013 to "Steve V. Stefanoff, etal,”, conveying a
thin strip that is adjacent to the north side of property which fronts 12600 South Street; and

       WHEREAS, a title company assisting the current owners of property affected by this
deed have found and determined that no such record exists showing Riverton City conveyed this
same property to UDOT, prior to its attempted transfer of ground to “Steve V. Stefanoff, etal” as
grantee, which makes suspect the deed conveyance by UDOT; and

       WHEREAS, on June 23, 2005 UDOT conveyed by quit claim deed to "Scott T.
Hembury, etal" a thin strip that is adjacent to the North of the current tax parcel No. 27-33-201-
014 and adjacent to the south line of 12600 South; and

       WHEREAS, Scott Hembury was deceased at the time of this conveyance; the current
vested owner of the adjacent parcel to the south is "Scott T. Hembury and Jan S. Hembury,
husband and wife, as joint tenants,"; and

       WHEREAS, a title company assisting the current owners of property affected by this
deed have found and determined that no such record exists showing Riverton City conveyed this
same property to UDOT, prior to its attempted transfer of ground to “Scott T. Hembury, etal” as
grantee, which makes suspect the deed conveyance by UDOT; and

      WHEREAS, 12600 South was a city street at this location before jurisdiction over the
same was taken by UDOT; and

        WHEREAS, said title company and Riverton City officials have determined that the
most efficient means to clear any cloud to title of the parcels conveyed by UDOT as described in
this Resolution is for Riverton City to convey the same by quit claim deed to the original
recipients; and
       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that its interest in the
property to be conveyed by quit claim deed, if any, would be insufficient to justify obtaining
appraisal will help Riverton residents obtain clear title to property owned and occupied by said
residents, and conveyance by quit claim deed will not adversely affect existing public right of
way for 12600 South Street;


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby approves the quit claim deeds affecting Parcel Nos.
          27-33-201-026 and 27-33-201-027, attached as “Exhibit A”. The Mayor is
          authorized to sign each deed upon verification that the same are approved as to legal
          form by the Riverton City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.


       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 2nd day of
