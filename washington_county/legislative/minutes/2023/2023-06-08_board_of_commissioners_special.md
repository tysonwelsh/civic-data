SPECIAL WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JUNE 8, 2023

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 11:00 a.m. on June 8, 2023, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, County
Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

INVOCATION BY: Tim Martin, The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Susan Lewis
SWEARING IN OF ELECTED OFFICIAL:

Commissioner Snow swore in Clerk-Auditor Ryan Sullivan.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:15 a.m.

Lange yn Age.

SARAH LLOYD M SNOW
DEPUTY CLERK AIRMAN
