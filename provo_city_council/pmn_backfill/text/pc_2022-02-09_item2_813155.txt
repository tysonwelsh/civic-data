Gonzales

CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning
Commission. Key issues raised in written comments received subsequent to the Staff Report or public comment during
the public hearing included the following: None.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
   • Planning staff gave an overview of the proposed amendments and responded to questions regarding the content
       of some of the proposed changes. Staff also noted suggestions from the Planning Commission to improve certain
       code sections in the future.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   • Laurie Urquiaga asked specific questions regarding the images being removed from Section 14.34.295 and briefly
       discussed future changes to those design standards.
   • Brian Henrie and Dave Anderson both gave suggestions on how to make substantive improvements on a future
       revision on the parking lot landscaping code in Section 15.20.090.
   • Lisa Jensen clarified that the first set of redundant numbering of standard conditional uses are being removed
       from Section 14.34.250, but the text following that which repeats the conditional uses will remain.
