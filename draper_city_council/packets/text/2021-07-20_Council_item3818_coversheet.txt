From: Travis Van Ekelenburg
CC:  Date: July 20, 2021
Re: Ordinance #1494 Olson Residence Zoning Map Amendment Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment for approximately 1.84 acres located on the west side of Fort Street, at approximately 13764 S & 13770 S Fort Street. The properties are currently zoned RA1 . The applicant is requesting that a Zoning Map Amendment be approved to rezone 0.463 acres of the 1.84 acre property  from the RA1 zoning designation to the RA2 zoning designation as the applicant would like to create a 20,000 ft² lot for the existing home.
The Planning Commission reviewed this item on June 24, 2021 and forwarded a positive recommendation of 5-0 vote. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1.    That DCMC Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the zoning text and maps of the DCMC.
2.    That this map amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
For Denial:
1.     That this map amendment compromises one or more of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1). ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Ordinance #1494 7/13/2021 Cover Memo PC Staff Report 7/14/2021 Cover Memo
