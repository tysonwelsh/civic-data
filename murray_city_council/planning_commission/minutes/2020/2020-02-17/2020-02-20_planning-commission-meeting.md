UTAH EDUCATION ASSOCIATION — 886 East Pontiac Drive - Project #20-018

Brad Bartels was present to represent this request. Zac Smallwood reviewed the location and
request to construct a new corporate headquarters on the site at 875 East Pontiac Drive. The
property is located in the General Office Zone (G-O) Zone. In order to construct the new
headquarters, there are a series of six steps, some of which require Planning Commission

approval. The first step completed by the applicant in December of 2018 was the approval
process for a General Plan Amendment and Zone change. The second step is the partial
vacation of the Arrowhead Lane subdivision. The applicant is proposing to demolish a portion of
one unit of the four-unit condo building, then remove a portion of the land from the existing
Condominium Plat to create a new single lot subdivision. The intent is to build a sperate office
building on the newly created single lot for their own use. The applicant will withdraw about half
an acre from the plat which is currently used as parking. The third step is to create the Single-
Lot Subdivision per State Code. This would result in a single lot which meets all the
requirements of the G-O Zone. There are no front lot area or rear yard requirements. The lot
must have a width of ninety feet, it has a twenty-foot front setback and a corner side yard set
back of twenty feet. Step four, is to the Conditional Use Permit to allow the construction of the
new building. The building also meets the requirements of the G-O Zone for setbacks and the
30-foot height maximum. A proposed landscaping plan will be reviewed upon submittal to
ensure it meets landscaping requirements as well. The portion of the office space that the
applicant currently occupies will remain until the proposed office building is completed and then
it will be demolished. Currently, the property is parked at 295 stalls. The newly required number
of stalls for both the current and future building will be 253 stalls. The property will lose about
37 spaces with the addition of the new building however, the property will regain some of the
lost parking area when the small portion of office space is demolished, and that area is striped
for additional parking. The new proposed parking totals at that time will be 258 stalls and will
meet ordinance requirements. Steps five and six will require future Planning Commission
approval after they demolish the vacant portion of the building and begin the improvements
which will provide additional parking and landscaping, etc. Based on the analysis of the Partial
Withdrawal of the Arrowhead Lane Condo Plat, the Pontiac Single Lot Subdivision, and the
Conditional Use Permit for a new office building and survey of the surrounding area, Staff
recommend approval subject to conditions.

LAND USE ORDINANCE TEXT AMENDMENT — Section 17.140, R-N-B Zone, Addition of LU
#1112. Twin-Homes, as a Permitted Use - Project #19-119

Brad Reynolds was present to represent this request. Jared Hall reviewed the request to
amend the text of Section 17.140.020 of the Murray Land Use Ordinance to include Land Use
#1112, (Twin Homes) as a Permitted Use in the R-N-B, Residential Neighborhood Business
Zone only. Single Family Homes and Duplexes are allowed in the R-N-B Zone, but Twin Homes
are not. There are small distinctions between Duplexes and Twin Homes. Duplex is a single lot
with a two-family dwelling on that lot and can only be owned by a single person. A Twin Home is
configured just like a Duplex but with a property line through the middle of the building which
allows ownership of a single unit and the land that is associated with it. As proposed here a twin
home must meet area requirements of a 10,000 square foot lot minimum with 5,000 square foot
minimum for each dwelling. The allowance for a duplex in the R-N-B Zone is similar in that the
minimum area requirement is also a 10,000 sq. ft. lot. With the proposed amendment, Staff
would like to create the opportunity for the same thing that is already allowed in the R-N-B Zone
but have potential dual ownership allowances. Staff propose to add LU #1112 for Single Family
Dwellings Attached as specified for Twin Homes. Based on the background, analysis, and the
findings within this report, Staff recommends approval to the City Council to include Land Use

#1112, Twin Homes, as a permitted use in the R-N-B Zone by amending the text of Section
17.140.020(B) as follows:

(LU#1112) Single-family dwelling attached (twin-homes). Twin-homes shall meet the following
yard and area requirements: 10,000 square foot lot minimum (5,000 square feet, each dwelling).
