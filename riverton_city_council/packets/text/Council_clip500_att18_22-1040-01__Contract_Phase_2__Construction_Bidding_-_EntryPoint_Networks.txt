Proposed Scope of Services

EntryPoint Networks’ – Approach and Methodology

Phase 1 – Preliminary Work and Design

Preliminary Work – EntryPoint will work with city staff and the Riverton Broadband Committee to develop
preliminary conceptual design options to discuss city priorities and establish any additional scope as
approved by the City and confirm constructability.

High-Level Network Design – EntryPoint Networks, in partnership with Biarri Design Services, will take a
holistic approach to the design process recognizing that while the primary purpose of this proposal is to
complete a design for the installation of a city owned fiber optic network for municipal use, it has the
secondary objective of anticipating the possibility of a full Fiber-to-the-Premise (FTTP) project throughout
the City of Riverton. Including a pilot or proof of concept neighborhood that supports Treasury guidance for
use of American Recovery Plan Act funds.


EntryPoint Networks            Riverton, Utah – Network Design & Management Services – RFP #227-21       Page | 8
This EntryPoint/Biarri design approach will produce an initial feasibility design for the eight primary city
assets cited in the RFP solicitation. As an option, EntryPoint can provide a city-wide design – from which a
pilot neighborhood(s) can be identified. The optional city-wide design will include additional city assets that
can be included within the budget approved by City Council. These additional city assets could include City
Parks, Culinary Pump Houses, Secondary Pump Houses, Conservancy Pump Houses, Riverbend Golf Course,
and other strategic locations.

Upon notification of award, EntryPoint will host a kickoff meeting as outlined in the RFP with elected officials
and city staff to explore scenarios beyond the anchor locations and set expectations.

EntryPoint will obtain from the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) geospatial data relative to fiber
infrastructure owned by UDOT that might be accessed and used for portions of the city installation and for
middle mile backhaul for services. This information will also be provided as part of the design process.
This feasibility design will document the preferred architecture for City approval and result in a high-level bill
of materials and accompanying labor estimates.

Establish Final Scope of Work – This feasibility design and associated cost estimates will be used to
determine the cost to construct the infrastructure necessary to connect the eight (8) discrete locations listed
in the Request and clarify remaining budget that may be available for additional objectives, such as adding
additional locations, site preparation at fiber access locations for aggregation points to serve portions of the
city, and developing a small pilot or proof of concept area for FTTP.

Final Project Documents – Once the Riverton Broadband Committee selects and approves a specific design
for the project, EntryPoint will proceed with the following work in preparation for Phase 2:
     Present the selected project design along with projected schedules and costs to City Council for
      approval.
     Assist the city in coordinating and executing an interconnection agreement with UDOT for use of state-
      owned fiber assets as needed.
     Options and costs for design drawings and assist with procurement process
     Provide all documentation necessary for procurement of materials and labor, including specifications,
      blueprints, bid schedule, measurement and payment schedule, cost estimates, and any other
      necessary construction documents.

Phase 2 – Construction/Installation [Following Riverton City Council Approval of Design Work]

Bid Process Support: EntryPoint will assist the City’s Purchasing Department with materials and labor
procurement as needed. These activities are expected to include, but may not be limited to, providing a list
of suggested contractors, providing any necessary bid materials for city approval and use, conducting a pre-
bid meeting, and addressing any questions during the bidding process. Once bids are received EntryPoint
will assist the city in reviewing responses, make award recommendations and develop contract documents
for city approval and use.
