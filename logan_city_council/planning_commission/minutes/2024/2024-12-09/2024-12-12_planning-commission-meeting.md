PUBLIC HEARING:

PC 24-049 D&J Jones STR [Conditional Use Permit] Devin Jones, owner/authorized agent is
requesting a Conditional Use Permit to operate a three-bedroom short-term rental within the entire
home located at 28 Fairway Lane in the Mixed Residential Low (MR-12) zone; TIN 02-136-
0105 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

Staff: Russ Holley, Logan City Planner, reviewed the request for a permit to operate a 3-bedroom
short-term rental in a townhome located at 28 Fairway Lane. The proposed project meets all
parking, proximity, and neighborhood compatibility requirements in the Land Development Code.

Commission Peterson asked if it can be conditioned that the garage must remain available for
parking. R. Holley stated that the proposed parking is within the garage, with any additional parking
in front of the garage. If the garage was not available for parking the STR would be out of
compliance.

Proponent: Devin Jones said he is on the HOA (Home Owner’s Association) board for the
subdivision and the HOA has not received any noise complaints regarding his Short Term Rental.
