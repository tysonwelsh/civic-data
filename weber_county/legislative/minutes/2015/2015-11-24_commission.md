    5.   REQUEST FROM DEBORAH HOLDSWORTH, PARCEL #13-205-0006, FOR 2014 PRIMARY RESIDENTIAL STATUS

         Joe Olsen, of the County Assessor’s Office, representing the Tax Review Committee (Committee), stated
         that Ms. Holdsworth purchased this parcel on 10/2013. The previous owner had requested, by signed
         affidavit, a non-primary taxable status. The county identified the taxable status as non-primary in both
         the 2014 valuation notice and the tax bill, including the definition of the taxable status but Ms.
         Holdsworth did not appeal the 2014 taxable status. She appealed the 2015 taxable status, which was
         accepted. She attended the Committee’s meeting where the State Tax Commission’s requirement was
         identified that the property owner has the burden of proving that the property qualifies for the primary
         exemption. The county ordinance requires evidence to identify the primary residential status. The
         Committee, with input from county legal counsel, recommended that the request be denied for 2014. Ms.
         Holdsworth stated today that she paid the taxes without reviewing the tax notice carefully. After
         comparing her 2015 tax bill with a neighbor, she came to the county for information. Mr. Olsen stated
         that taxable status needs to be appealed by approximately September 15 and late Board of Equalization
         (BOE) appeals submitted through March 31 of the following year. The property owner was given proper
         notice and does not have standing. Courtlan Erickson, Deputy County Attorney, stated that this should
         have gone before the BOE by March 31, 2015 and the county needs to adhere to the statute. Ricky Hatch,
         County Clerk/Auditor, noted that the valuation notice issued in July contains the taxable status and the
         definition above it, underlined.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to deny the request by Deborah Holdsworth to change the 2014 primary
         residential status; Chair Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    2. 2014 LOCAL TRANSPORTATION FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH OGDEN CITY FOR FUND CAPACITY AND
       IMPROVEMENTS FOR THE NORTH STREET PROJECT– CONTRACT C2015-397

       Douglas Larsen, Weber County Economic Development Partnership Director, noted that this project came
