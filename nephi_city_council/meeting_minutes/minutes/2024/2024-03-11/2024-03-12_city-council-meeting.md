                             Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                      March 12, 2024

                                        21 E 100 N
                                     Nephi, UT 84648

1.       Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the Council an ordinance that had been reviewed by the
     Planning Commission. The ordinance deals with regulating matters regarding
     individuals who use a motorized passenger vehicle, or a recreational vehicle for
     residential or living purposes. The ordinance also deals with the appropriate places, and
     length of stay for individuals camping within the City.
        City Attorney Wright said that there would be language in the code that allowed
     reasonable accommodations for religious purposes that had been brought up by a
     resident at the last regular meeting.
         Councilor Parady asked for some clarification on whether you could have family
     members stay for a limited time at different times during the year. Mr. Atkinson said that
     the enforcement of the ordinance would be complaint driven.
        Councilor Travis Worwood asked what the penalties are for disobeying the law.
     Attorney Wright said it would be a Class B misdemeanor.
        Councilor Callaway didn’t like the fact that law-abiding citizens would be forced to
     break the law with the hope that a complaint would not be made.
        Officer Nisha King said that she has had to deal with individuals living in a RV. She
     investigated the Nephi City Code and in 2017 the code spelled out that you could not live
     in an RV on private property only in a designated RV Park with an active business
     license. But a new code was later adopted when the council adopted the new RV park
     rules and that part of the code had been left out.
        Mr. Atkinson said that it is still not allowed but is difficult to find and interpret and that
     is why we are trying to adopt a new ordinance that makes it clearer to people that living
     in RVs is not allowed.
        Code Enforcement Officer Andrea Holm said that she was called out on a call that day
     with someone living in an RV and there was a huge sanitation problem. She said that the
     short stay in RVs is not a problem but if you open the doors and allow any then it is very
     difficult to enforce.
        Chief of Police Morgan said his officers need teeth in the code to help deal with these
     types of problems. He said that he does not have the manpower to enforce how long
     someone is staying in an RV.

     PUBLIC COMMENT:

     that mentioned a designee and he wondered who the designee would be and thought it
     should have more specific language.

2.    The Council discussed increasing the sewer and solid waste collection rates and adding
     a stormwater utility. The council directed staff to bring back some recommendations for
     the next regular meeting.

3.    The Council reviewed an agreement with Mona City clarifying areas of responsibility for
     the natural gas line.

5.   Mr. Atkinson reported on a down pine tree in Pioneer Park due to a recent windstorm.
     He said that staff will be doing an assessment of all the old trees in the park, and some
     may need to come down for safety reasons. Councilor Cowan expressed her concern of
     making sure to replace the trees that are removed.

6.    Mr. Atkinson asked the Council if they would like to have a concept design made for
     Red Cliffs park so that when the road is constructed the proper infrastructure can be
     planned for. Councilor Parady said he has heard that some residents have a desire for a
     small dog park. The Council directed staff to contract for a concept design for Red Cliffs
     park.

7. Mr. Atkinson informed the Council that soon they will need to look at bonding for the
   electric 138 KV projects. In order to finance items that have been and will be soon
   purchased by the City for the 138 kV project, it will be necessary to pass a parameters
   resolution. These resolutions set upper limits on bond issuance. This upper limit often
   exceeds the estimated amount of the bond issuance to ensure that a revision to the
   parameters is not needed. The parameters resolution also specifies that items that have
   been purchased up to 60 days prior to the parameters resolution being passed are
   allowed to be financed by an eventual bond issuance. Since purchases are currently
   being made for the 138 kV project, a parameters resolution is recommended.

8. The Council directed staff to put the same two projects as last year on the CIB projects
   report. The two projects were the police station and bridge improvements.

9. The Council reviewed a revised On-Call Compensation Policy. The Council directed
   staff to bring the policy back to the next regular meeting for adoption.

10. Councilor Travis Worwood exited the meeting discussion on some proposed
    annexations from Wright Direction because of conflict of interest. Mr. Atkinson reviewed
    with the Council three different areas that Wright Direction would like to have annexed
    into the city. The Council prioritized what properties they would maybe consider for
    future annexation. Mayor Seely expressed his desire to control the land near the city
    boundaries instead of the growth happening in the county and controlled by the county.
    They directed staff to have the Planning Commission investigate where these properties
    are on the annexation map and what level they are at.
