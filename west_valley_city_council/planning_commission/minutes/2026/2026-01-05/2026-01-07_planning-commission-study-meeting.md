ZT-6-2025      Petition by West Valley City requesting an amendment to Sections 7-14-201 through 7-
14-206 of the Zoning Ordinance to update the Jordan River Overlay Zone to be consistent with the rest
of the Zoning Ordinance. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik discussed proposed changes to the Jordan River Overlay Zone, which had
been unchanged since 1998 due to ongoing litigation. Steve noted that while most of the original 12
items had been eliminated or moved, three key standards remained: architectural requirements for
building sites, screening requirements, and standards for downgraded buildings. The proposed updates
aim to streamline and clarify the zoning code by removing redundant or vague language, consolidating
standards, and simplifying the appeal process. The discussion concluded with Steve agreeing to research
whether similar language about building visibility standards was present in other overlay zones, such as
the 5600 Overlay Zone. The Commission reviewed how architectural requirements for building
visibility and uniformity across all sides would be maintained, with a focus on form standards rather
than specific site requirements.

ZT-7-2025     Petition by West Valley City to amend Section 7-6-202 of the zoning ordinance to modify
the RE Zone setback requirements for accessory buildings in rear yards. Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-
963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov.
Discussion: Brock Anderson presented a proposal to amend Section 7-6-202 of the Zoning Ordinance,
which deals with setback requirements for the Residential Estate Zone. The change would reduce the
setback requirement for accessory buildings from 10 feet to 1 foot, aligning it with other single-family
zones. Commissioner Durfee raised a question about the 1.5-story maximum height requirement for
accessory buildings in the RE Zone, suggesting it should be standardized across all zones. Brock agreed
this was a valid point. It was decided to postpone this application until January 28, 2026 so proper
noticing could be given to change both the setback and height requirements.

C-22-2025       Petition by YESCO, LLC requesting conditional use approval for a pole sign. The
property is located at 5219 South Upper Ridge Road on 36.22 acres, and is zoned M, Manufacturing.
(Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented a request for a pole sign at the Utah First Community Credit
Union Amphitheater, to be located in an area removed from the main road in a turnaround. The sign
would be owned by West Valley City Redevelopment Agency (RDA) to promote West Valley City
events and branding. Brock explained that the sign would be illuminated and follow City standards for
brightness and display speed. Commissioner Drozdek asked if the sign would have speakers and emit
sound. Brock responded that it would not have audio capabilities. The sign, which could be turned off
when not in use, was approved at a height of 20 feet tall despite the code allowing for a 35-foot tall sign.
Additionally, the applicant is requesting an increase in the electronic message panel size beyond the
standard 50% limit due to unique circumstances. Per 11-4-109(4) of the sign code, the Planning
Commission may allow alternate sign plans due to special circumstances. The proposed sign is unique in
that what will be displayed on the sign will be community events, which are always changing, so a
larger electronic message portion is being requested. There is an existing landscaped area surrounding
the sign. Due to the large area and difficult location to irrigate, the applicant is requesting that no live
plant material be required. The area will be re-done with a new decorative wall, and the ground will be
covered with rock mulch.

C-23-2025     Petition by A.D. Constructors requesting conditional use approval for an
office/warehouse with Outside Storage for Lot 4 of the Moody Subdivision. This property is located at
2258 S. Moody Circle on 0.67 acres, and zoned M, Manufacturing. (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-
3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented the A.D Constructors application for an office warehouse with
outside storage on Lot 4 of the Moody Subdivision. She detailed the building layout, the 5% landscaping
design requirement, and architectural enhancements to comply with the commercial design standards
towards the top of the building on the north elevation. The exterior would be constructed of ribbed metal
panels and CMU block wainscot. Outside storage would be hard surfaced, and screen from public view
by a 6 foot masonry wall on the east side, and a 6 foot slatted chain-link fence on the south side. Jody
agreed to provide more detailed information about building elevations and storage height restrictions at
the public hearing, as the Commission was concerned about the visibility of scaffolding and equipment
storage visibility from the street. Signage details were not provided at this time but will be reviewed
upon a future permit application.
