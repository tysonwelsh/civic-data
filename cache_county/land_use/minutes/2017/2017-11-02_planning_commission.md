Regular Action Items 
#1 Cache County Trails and Active Transportation Master Plan 
 
Dayton Crites updated the commission on the Cache County Trails and Active Transportation Master Plan. 
 
The Commission and staff discussed feedback from the community and surrounding areas. 
 
Sands motioned to recommend approval of the Cache County Trails and Active Transportation Master Plan 
to the County Council; Gunnell seconded; Passed 5, 0. 
 
05:54:00 
 
#2 Discussion: Code Changes 
 
Harrild reviewed the 2017 legislature changes that affect the Planning Commission. All codes are required 
to have language that is easy to read, understand, and use. Staff is working to update and rewrite any needed 
changes to the County Codes. 
 
06:05:00 
 
#3 2018 Meeting Dates and Application Deadlines 
 
Harrild reviewed the dates and application deadlines for 2018. July’s Planning Commission will be held 
July 12th  instead of July 5th. 
 
06:06:00 
 
#4 Upcoming: Elections for Chair and Vice Chair 
 
Elections will be held next month for Chair and Vice Chair.
