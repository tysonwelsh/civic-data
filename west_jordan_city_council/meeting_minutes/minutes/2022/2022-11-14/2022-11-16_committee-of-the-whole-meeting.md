Whitelock was excused.


 2. DISCUSSION TOPICS
         a. 2022 Drinking Water Master Plan Update
            Utilities Manager Greg Davenport answered questions provided by Chair Whitelock prior
            to the meeting. The City did not previously have a funding plan for water system
            rehabilitation and replacement costs. He recommended the Council not include funding
            for replacement in the budget until staff completed a replacement plan which was
            estimated to be completed by summer of 2023.

            Vice Chair Green wanted the Council to be able to start anticipating improvement costs.
            Vice Chair Green believed the Council needed to consider eliminating the Enterprise Fund
            transfer to help fund needed improvements. He would be uncomfortable raising water
            rates with money available in the Enterprise Fund. Mr. Davenport reported that the City
            would apply for grant funding for water system improvements when the update was
            complete.

            Mr. Davenport explained consequences of using the State standard for water
            storage/usage versus the number used in the last West Jordan Water Master Plan. He
            welcomed feedback from the Council and emphasized that the Master Plan was a planning
            document.

            Vice Chair Green asked how the Water Master Plan update would tie into the current
            General Plan update. Council Office Director Alan Anderson responded the General Plan
   was a guiding document that oversaw all of the master plans. The General Plan and the
   master plans should be compatible and consistent. Public Liaison & Policy Analyst Cassidy
   Hansen suggested that the General Plan include goals consistent with the Water Master
   Plan goals. Mr. Davenport noted that it was his understanding that each master plan
   became part of the General Plan.

   Responding to a question from Council Member Jacob, Mr. Davenport said he would
   research recent statements from the Governor regarding water rights. He also noted that
   he would be bringing back the plan for adoption at a future meeting. Vice Chair Green
   thanked Mr. Davenport for his time.

b. Business License Code Update
   Code Enforcement Manager Brock Hudson oriented the Council with proposed
   amendments to the Business License Code, Title 4 Chapter 1. Vice Chair Green believed
   additional amendments were needed. Vice Chair Green requested that staff determine
   what the purpose of business licenses was in West Jordan and outlined the following
   suggestions to simplify the Code:

      •   Determine if BCI background checks were really needed for all license types
      •   Update the Definition section (i.e., remove Shooting Gallery definition)
      •   Remove question of who signs checks in a company
      •   Remove requirement to post office/business hours
      •   Do not require US Citizenship

   Council Member Bloom referred to a reference in Code regarding individuals convicted
   of a Class B Misdemeanor or greater and commented there were many programs to help
   ex-convicts be successful in moving on with life. She did not want the Code to prevent that
   from happening in West Jordan. Mr. Hudson responded that language she referred to was
   eliminated with the proposed amendments. Mr. Hudson said the purpose of the update
   was to make Business License Code simpler and easier to use. Vice Chair Green suggested
   isolating necessary references to criminality in one specific section of the Code.

   Council Member McConnehey liked the proposed changes and would support moving
   forward with approval. He asked if language clarifying that convictions would only be
   taken into account if pertinent to the nature of the business, could be made clearer.
   Council Member McConnehey suggested changes to language on lines 840 (incorrect
   information), 1005 (“electronic” rather than “facsimile”), 1020 (licenses available upon
   request rather than posted), 1050-1051 (remove “online through the city’s payment
   portal” and allow all types of payments), 1052 (strike proration), 1179 (change in
   ownership percentage limits), and 1213 (eliminate curfew restrictions). Vice Chair Green
   agreed with the suggestion to eliminate curfew restrictions from the Business License
   Code.

   Vice Chair Green was uncomfortable with language beginning at line 1544 stating a
   holder of a City business license was strictly liable for any acts of employees. Mr. Hudson
   reported that staff would look at the language.
            All Council Members indicated agreement with changes discussed and work to make the
            process to obtain a Business License as simple as possible. The item will be brought back
            for further discussion at a future Work Session.
