1.     Public Hearing: Pheasant Hills Plat 4 Plat Amendment (Administrative Action)

On the request of Tom Hulbert, for a Plat Amendment of Lots 405 and 406 of the Pheasant Hills
Phase 4 Subdivision, located at 2019 East Graystone Court. The property is 0.974 acres in size and
zoned RA2. Application SUBD-146-2022. Staff contact is Jennifer Jastremsky, 801-576-6328,
Jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

Jennifer Jastremsky presented this project. The request is to adjust the property line between two
properties, and on the one of the properties to also reduce the open space easement. In 1993 the
Pheasant Hills Subdivision was approved as a PUD, this allowed the lot sizes in the development
to be about 10,000 sq.ft. Twenty percent of the development was required to be open space. With
the proposed adjustments to both properties, the development will still comply with the 20% of
open space. Because the City has rights to the open space easement, the request had to be reviewed
by the City Council. The Council approved the reduction in open space at a previous meeting. One
of the properties has a home built on it and the other property is under construction.

Vice Chairman Craig Hawker asked if the setbacks were not right and that is what brought this
request for the change.

Jennifer Jastremsky stated the home that is being built has been modified from what was originally
approved which shifted the house slightly, creating an encroachment with the setbacks. The PUD
approved setbacks of ten feet for the neighborhood. With the change both homes will have the
correct setbacks needed for the area.

Chairman Andrew Adams opened the public hearing for comments or concerns. He reminded
anyone who would like to speak on this to only speak for three minutes.

Ken Walters: resident of the Pheasant Hills neighborhood, and is a part of the HOA board Stated
he is in favor of this. He stated that the board had already approved this request on their end, and
hopes it is approved by the Planning Commission.

Chairman Andrew Adams closed the public hearing.

2.     Public Hearing: Siempre Events Center 1st Amendment Development Agreement
       (Legislative Action)

On the request of Randy Deschamps, representing Siempre Events Center, for an Amended
Development Agreement for 8.99 acres of property located at 1283 East Mike Weir Dr. in the
C-2 and RM zones. Application DA-134-2022. Staff contact is Jennifer Jastremsky, 801-
576-6328, jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, presented this request. The request is for a Development
Agreement for parking and overflow parking. The Original Development Agreement allowed for
a reduction in the number of approved parking spaces and required that 20 spaces be constructed
of geo-grass pavers to accommodate overflow parking. The total approved parking was 224 stalls
with 7 new stalls and 20 geo-grass paver stalls.

Ms. Jastremsky reviewed the proposed Development Agreement. The proposed agreement would
eliminate the 20 geo-grass paver parking spaces. The applicant installed 10 new parking spaces
with the construction making the new total without the pavers 207 stalls. In exchange for approving
the agreement, the applicant would agree to only hold one wedding event at a time. The code today
would require 153 parking spaces for the property.

Vice Chairman Craig Hawker asked about street parking. What is allowed and what is not. Does
the City govern the street parking or is it up to the business to do that? Eric Lundell., City
Engineer, stated that when there is a safety issue with parking the city will restrict parking on the
street.

Commissioner Susan Nixon asks about the new stalls along the east and they were not mentioned
in the agreement, are they part of the 207 or 224 stalls. Ms. Jastremsky stated they are not
calculated in the current 224.T

grass spaces if the Commission wants.

3.     Training: Subdivision Review Process

Jennifer Jastremsky and Eric Lundell provided a training on subdivision reviews. They reviewed
the steps taken in a review and what staff looks at when reviewing an application. Ms. Jastremsky
pointed out there are four different types of subdivision applications: Preliminary, Final, Amended,
and Minor. She then reviewed the application submission documents and review timeframes. All
subdivisions are reviewed by the Planning, Engineering, Fire, GIS, Building, Parks and Recreation
departments and sometimes by the Legal division. Ms. Jastremsky then went over the actual plans
that are reviewed for a subdivision, including a plat and tree plan.

Eric Lundell reviewed the documents that the Engineering Division reviews, including plats, plan
and profile sheets, and utility plans. He stated the city asked for a 3rd party surveyor, to make sure
the right of ways are accounted for. He also made sure the applicant has all the easements they
need to continue with their project properly. They made sure the setbacks are where they need to
be for all structures, they make sure the applicant is meeting all state and city regulations.

At the conclusion of the training, Jennifer Jastremsky told the Commissioners staff was thinking
of making some changes to staff reports by providing information in graphs and tables rather than
a written narrative.

Commissioner Susan Nixon stated that would be great. Commissioner Lisa Fowler said it would
be easier to take a quick glance at what they need, while in meetings to refresh the information.
