                                       TAYLORSVILLE,                 UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE             NO. 25- 16


  AN ORDINANCE           OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE                           ESTABLISHING           THE 2026
                TAYLORSVILLE             CITY COUNCIL MEETING SCHEDULE.


       WHEREAS,       the   Taylorsville
                                      City Council ( the " Council") met during its regularly
scheduled meeting on December 17, 2025, to consider, among other things, establishing the 2026
Taylorsville City Council Meeting Schedule; and

       WHEREAS,          UTAH   CODE      ANN. §        10- 3- 502( 1)    requires   that   the    council   of each

municipality shall by ordinance prescribe the time and place for holding its regular meeting( s) and
hold a regular meeting at least once each month; and

       WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. §           52- 4- 202( 2) further requires a public body which holds
regular meetings which are scheduled in advance over the course of a year to give public notice at
least once each year of its annual meeting schedule; and

       WHEREAS,       TAYLORSVILLE         MUNICIPAL         CODE §      2. 12. 040( A) requires    that the council

prescribe by ordinance the time and place of its regular meeting schedule and the City Recorder
shall give public notice at least once each year of the annual meeting schedule of the City Council;
and


       WHEREAS, the Council has deemed it appropriate to establish an annual meeting
schedule for 2026 for meetings of the City Council and other City Boards, Commissions, and
Committees.


        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
2026 Taylorsville City Council Meeting Schedule, attached hereto as Exhibit" A" and incorporated
herein by this reference, is hereby adopted. The City Recorder, or her designee, is hereby directed
and authorized to publish a copy of the attached 2024 Taylorsville City Council Meeting Schedule
pursuant to   UTAH CODE ANN. §       52- 4- 202( 2).


       This ordinance,    assigned    Ordinance        No. 25- 16, shall take effect as soon as it shall be

published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.


       PASSED AND APPROVED this 17th day of December 2025
