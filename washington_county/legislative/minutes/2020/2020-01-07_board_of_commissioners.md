WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JANUARY 7, 2020

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on January 7, 2020, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Brian Graf, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy County
Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Commissioner Cox was excused from the meeting.

PUBLIC HEARINGS CONSISTENT WITH STATE CODE REQUIREMENTS

AND TO CLARIFY PROCEDURE IN WHICH THE COUNTY COMMISSION

PUBLIC HEARING: REZONING A CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON

COUNTY NEAR VEYO FROM THE OST-20 (OPEN SPACE TRANSITION 20
ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE TO THE A-5 (AGRICULTURAL 5 ACRE

MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE

Mr. Messel explained that the applicants’ property is located north of Veyo. The property
owners desire to subdivide the property into two (2) parcels. The Planning Commission
recommended approval of the zone change.

Chairman Iverson opened the public hearing.

Slade Hughes spoke in favor of the zone change and mentioned that it would be a step
towards more attainable housing within the County.

Seeing no one else come forward, Chairman Iverson closed the public hearing.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Commissioner Almquist explained that the Sheriff's Office is in need of a storage building
for the Search and Rescue equipment. Bids have been received for that project and will be

Thursday, January 9, 2020.
