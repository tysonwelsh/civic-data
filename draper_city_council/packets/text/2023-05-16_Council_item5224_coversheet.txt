Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: May 16, 2023
Re: Resolution 23-22 Approving and Extending a Proclamation of Local Emergency Regarding a Landslide Comments: On April 24, 2023, Mayor Troy K. Walker, in his capacity as Draper City’s chief executive officer, issued a Proclamation of Local Emergency (“Proclamation”) declaring a state of local emergency in the City concerning a landslide and resulting debris field in the Hidden Canyon Estates Subdivision on Springtime Road in Draper (the “Landslide”), as authorized by the Disaster Response and Recovery Act, Utah Code Ann. 53-2a-201 et seq. (the “Act”).
Utah Code Ann. 53-2a-208(6), provides that a local emergency shall not be continued or renewed beyond the initial 30 days without consent of the governing body of the jurisdiction subject to the declaration.
Because (a) the Proclamation will expire on May 24, 2023, absent action of the City’s governing body (the “Council”) to extend it, and (b) the area remains a danger to the public because the slide area in unstable and plans are still being developed to stabilize the slope and access the debris field to remove debris from the slide area, the City Council met in regular session on May 16, 2023, to consider, among other things, approving and ratifying the Proclamation and extending its duration to October 1, 2023.
