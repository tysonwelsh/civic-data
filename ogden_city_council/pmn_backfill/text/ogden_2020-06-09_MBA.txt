Proposed Resolution 2020-1 adopting the annual budget of the Municipal
Building Authority for Fiscal Year 2020-2021
         A memo from Board staff regarding the proposed Fiscal Year 2020-2021 annual Municipal Building Authority (MBA)
budget came before the Board for consideration. The memo stated the current proposal is to adopt the fiscal year 2020-2021
MBA budget totaling $297,175 as proposed. A work session was held on May 19, 2020 to review and discuss the proposed
budget.
         Board Deputy Administrator Symes summarized the memo and used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize
the proposed MBA budget; the Fiscal Year 2021 budget is decreasing by nearly half when compared to Fiscal Year 2020 budget,
from $537,425 to $297.175. Expenditures in the budget relate to debt service for the 2007 Raptors Lindquist Stadium Bond and
the 2006 Public Works Facility Bond; the Stadium Bond was refunded in Fiscal Year 2020, and no debt service payment is
required. The Public Works Facility Bond has a principal remaining of $280,000 and the bond matures on June 15, 2021.
         Chair Choberka called for a public hearing on proposed Resolution 2020-1, entitled:
                  “A resolution of the Board of Trustees of the Ogden City Municipal Building Authority adopting the annual
                  budget for Fiscal Year 2020-2021.”
         The City Recorder presented written notice of said hearing and affidavit of the publication in the Standard-Examiner on
the 16th day of May, 2020 specifying the time and place of this meeting as the time and place when and where the proposed
resolution would be given a public hearing and be considered for final passage. The proof of publication was accepted and filed.
         There being no persons appearing to be heard, TRUSTEE HYER MOVED THE PUBLIC HEARING BE CLOSED.
THE MOTION WAS SECONDED BY TRUSTEE LOPEZ, ALL VOTING AYE. Trustee Nadolski was not present when
this vote was taken.

      TRUSTEE HYER MOVED THE RESOLUTION BE ADOPTED, AND THE CHAIR BE AUTHORIZED TO
SIGN THE SAME. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY VICE CHAIR BLAIR, WITH THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL
VOTE: VOTING AYE – TRUSTEES HYER, LOPEZ, STEPHENS, WHITE, VICE CHAIR BLAIR, AND CHAIR
CHOBERKA. VOTING NO – NONE. Trustee Nadolski was not present when this vote was taken.
