                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-30

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN INTERLOCAL
  COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND HERRIMAN
CITY FOR ASPHALT SURFACE TREATMENTS AND LANE RE-STRIPING OF 13400
                  SOUTH AND 13800 SOUTH STREETS

       WHEREAS, Riverton City and Herriman City each own and/or are responsible for
maintenance of portions of 13400 South Street and 13800 South Street within their respective
municipal boundaries; and

       WHEREAS, those roadways require maintenance including asphalt surface treatments
and lane re-striping; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City and Herriman City desire to jointly proceed with a roadway
maintenance project consisting of microsurface treatment for 13400 South Street, GSB Medium
treatment for 13800 South Street, and lane re-striping; and

        WHEREAS, Herriman City has solicited bids for the Project and will be responsible for
procuring, managing, and advancing the Project, with Riverton City reimbursing Herriman City
for Riverton’s proportionate share of project costs based upon actual billed amounts for treated
asphalt within the Riverton City boundary; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that it is in the best interest
of Riverton City to enter into the Interlocal Cooperative Agreement with Herriman City; and

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to execute the Interlocal Cooperative
          Agreement between Riverton City and Herriman City for Asphalt Surface Treatments
          and Lane Re-Striping of 13400 South and 13800 South Streets, attached hereto as
          Exhibit “A,” subject to such insertions and minor modifications as may be required
          for execution and approved by the City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
