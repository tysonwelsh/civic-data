                  Accessing the General Plan draft documents


The most current version of the draft General Plan document (Pace of Progress: Sandy City
General Plan 2025) is available on the project website (https://sandypaceofprogress.org/).
There is a link to the eight diﬀerent sections and two appendices included in the document.
The links are available on the main page of the website, but may not be visible until scrolling
down on that page. Most sections are a collection of multiple chapters. One appendix includes
individual chapters speciﬁc to each Station Area Plan and the other appendix includes individual
chapters speciﬁc to area plans for six of the City’s commercial areas (Neighborhood Activity
Centers).
Also available from the main page at https://sandypaceofprogress.org/, is an introductory video
by Mayor Zoltanski and comment box (“Leave a Comment”). The video provides a helpful
summary of the proposed General Plan. The project staﬀ will receive any comments left in the
comment box on the website. Written comments may also be provided to Jake Warner at
jwarner@sandy.utah.gov. Verbal comments can be made by attending upcoming Planning
Commission and City Council meetings throughout the months of October, November, and
December. To access an agenda for an upcoming Planning Commission or City Council meeting,
please visit https://sandyutah.legistar.com/Calendar.aspx.
