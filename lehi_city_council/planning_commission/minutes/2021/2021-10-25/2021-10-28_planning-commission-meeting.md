3.5) Public hearing and consideration of Rick Magness’ request for conditional use
approval of the Maverik Electronic Display Signs (EDS) Pylon and Monument signs
located at 2211 North 1200 West in a Commercial zone.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Rick Magnus with Yesco sign, was present to represent Gardner Company. He
stated the grade from I-15 is lower from North and South bound traffic and the visibility is
crucial to be able to see the sign. The topography to make safe ingress and egress is part of their
justification asking for the exception.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Mary Kelly stated she would be able to see the sign from her front yard.

Rebecca Wells stated she lives near where the sign will be. She stated there was already a lot of
light from other businesses and she would like to know how bright it would be and if the sign
would be stable.

Jacob stated in terms of the brightness they must be within the candles and lumens of the levels
for the code, which they meet when they submitted their application.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

The applicant stated the sign will be facing the freeway and the wind will most likely not affect
the sign. He also stated they are conforming to code with foot candles and measurements. When
they submit the building permit the calculations for the footings are submitted.

The Commissioners discussed the exception of the sign and whether it was warranted.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to approve Rick Magness’ request for
        conditional use approval of the Maverik Electronic Display Signs (EDS) Pylon and
        Monument signs located at 2211 North 1200 West in a Commercial zone; with the
        finding that it may provide better visibility therefore greater safety, and that the single
        pole is also allowed; including DRC comments and that the sign has been revised to be
        within code. Commissioner Hereth seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, no. The
        motion passed 4 – 2.

3.8) Public hearing and recommendation of Symphony Home’s request for review of the
Lehi Terrace zone change on 8.44-acres of property changing the zoning from TH-5
(transitional holding) to R-2 (medium density residential) located at approximately 1200
West 2900 North.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Russ Wilson, was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.


Shawn Goodwin asked about the green space and whether it was usable.

Mary Kelly asked about the fencing of the property.

Rebecca Wells asked about the driveways and the vehicles sticking out into the road. She also
asked about fencing and if it would still be installed. She stated she improves of the overall
concept if those elements are addressed.

Mike Shriner stated he was curious about the developer’s agreement and how he could find
clarification from that.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

The applicant addressed the open space and stated it would be provided as a buffer and a
playground had been discussed as being installed. Precast fences are being installed around the
perimeter. The driveways will be a sufficient depth and width and will be included in the
development agreement. Staff is working on drafting that agreement. He stated there will be an
HOA to maintain the open space area. Kim Struthers stated an agreement has been drafted and
sent to legal to review.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Symphony Home’s
        request for review of the Lehi Terrace zone change on 8.44-acres of property
        changing the zoning from TH-5 (transitional holding) to R-2 (medium density
        residential) located at approximately 1200 West 2900 North; with the findings that the
        proposed zone change is consistent with the goals and policies of the General Plan and
        will provide varying lot sizes that are compatible with the surrounding neighborhood,
        recommending the driveway length of 20 ft minimum. Including all DRC comments.
        Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The
        motion passed unanimously.

3.9) Public hearing and recommendation of Jewel Bullock’s request for review of R-1-15
(residential) zoning on the Neldon and Jewel Bullock Annexation, 1.33-acres of property
located at 578 West 2100 North.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Jewell Bullock, was present. She stated she would like her property to be annexed
into the City. She stated she would like to keep the property as A-1 and still annex into the City.
The Bullock's have been working with the City for years with the property to annex into the City.
She expressed her desire to preserve her animal rights.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing


Holly Bullock stated she is in support to the annexation and zone change for A-1.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing

    Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation of Jewel Bullock’s
    request for review of R-1-15 (residential) zoning on the Neldon and Jewel Bullock
    Annexation, 1.33-acres of property located at 578 West 2100 North; with the findings

    Commissioner Hereth; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The

3.10) Public hearing and recommendation of Matthew Jorgensen’s request for review of
the Beck’s Landing zone change on 10.48 acres of property changing the zoning from A-1
(agriculture) to R-1-22 (residential/agriculture) located at 1080 North 2300 West.

3.11) Public hearing and recommendation of Matthew Jorgensen’s request for review of
the Beck’s Landing concept, a 16-lot residential subdivision project located at 1080 North
2300 West.
Jacob Curtis presented items 3.10 and 3.11 simultaneously.

The applicant, Matthew Jorgensen, was present. He stated they have been working with Staff
carefully with the development.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for 3.10

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for 3.10

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for 3.11

Jenny Lee Finesse stated she was wondering if there was more than one concept. Jacob stated the
wrong image may have been sent out. She asked about the road that connects to 2300 West
because they have seen an increase in speed. She really liked the idea of single family homes.
She does not want Colony Pointe Drive to be a main construction thru fair.

Brooke Madsen stated when the original plat sent out there was not connectivity. He stated there
is a nub in the road he thinks will help with value for the property.

Stephanie Rover stated she received a notice and would like to reiterate her objection to the 2300
West connectivity through the neighborhood. There is a lot of traffic and she is concerned for the

kids in the area. When she looks at the map her property is on the edge of the development and
she would like to know what the drainage would look like.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for item 3.11

Ross Dinsdale stated the construction traffic will come from 2300 West. He stated there should
be an irrigation ditch near the easement and should be re-routed with the development. Colony
Point Drive does need to connect, and the majority of the traffic connects to 1500 North right
now. When a stub road is connected it will see more traffic and is an overall balance. Ross stated
stop signs will be installed where warranted.

The applicant clarified the jog that the jog in the road and that City code requires connectivity.
He stated the reality for the jog will cost more money and they are complying with green belt.

    Motion for 3.10: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation of Matthew
    Jorgensen’s request for review of the Beck’s Landing zone change on 10.48 acres of
    property changing the zoning from A-1 (agriculture) to R-1-22 (residential/agriculture)
    located at 1080 North 2300 West; with the findings that the proposed zone change is
    consistent with the Very Low Density Residential Agriculture designation of the General
    Plan, and that the proposed zone change is consistent with the goals and policies of the
    General Plan. Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
    Commissioner Hereth; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The
    motion passed unanimously.

    Motion for 3.11: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation for
    Matthew Jorgensen’s request for review of the Beck’s Landing concept, a 16-lot
    residential subdivision project located at 1080 North 2300 West; with the findings that
    the proposed concept will provide lots for single family homes and be consistent with the
    existing housing type in the neighborhood, the concept is consistent with the Lehi City
    Development Code, the concept conforms to the goals and policies of the General Plan.
    Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Hereth seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
    Commissioner Hereth; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The
    motion passed unanimously.
