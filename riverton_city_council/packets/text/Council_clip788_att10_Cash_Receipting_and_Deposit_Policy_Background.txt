Background

City Code establishes the duties of the City Treasurer, including serving as the custodian of City funds. In
addition, City Code serves as the City’s policy regarding cash receipting and deposits. To ensure
compliance with both City Code and state law, the Treasurer has developed procedures to ensure
compliance with both code and law.

The Treasurer, in collaboration with the Director of Administrative Services, has been working to
formalize these practices into a written policy. This policy will provide clear, consistent procedures for all
sites and employees who handle cash transactions and better aligns with the State Auditor’s Office
recommendations for a cash receipting and deposit policy. The policy does not replace City Code but
rather supplements it by offering more detailed guidance.

Risk Assessment Requirement

As part of the Risk Assessment required by the State Auditor’s Office, the governing board is expected to
formally approve policies related to cash receipting and deposits.

Recommendation

Staff recommends that the Riverton City Council approve the attached resolution adopting the City’s
Cash Handling and Receipting Policy.
