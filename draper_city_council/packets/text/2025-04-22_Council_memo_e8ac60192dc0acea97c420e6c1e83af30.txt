MEMO
To: City Council
From: Nicole Smedley, City Recorder
Date: 2025-04-22
Re: Approval of Resolution #25-15


Comments:


Background

Utah Code § 20A-5-400.1 allows municipalities to enter into interlocal agreements with
county clerk offices for the administration of local elections. Draper City has previously
partnered with the Salt Lake County Clerk’s Elections Division to conduct its municipal
elections and intends to continue that partnership for the 2025 municipal election cycle.

Discussion

Resolution #25-15 seeks City Council approval to enter into an interlocal agreement with
Salt Lake County for the administration of Draper City's 2025 municipal primary and
general elections. The agreement outlines the scope of services to be provided by the
County, including ballot layout and design, vote-by-mail administration, equipment
provision, election day operations, tabulation, reporting, and record maintenance.

Per the agreement, Draper City will be responsible for the actual costs of conducting the
election, which are based on factors such as voter turnout, number of jurisdictions
participating, and ballot content. A good-faith estimate of costs is included as Exhibit B to
the agreement. These costs may be adjusted depending on final participation levels or
events such as recounts.

Recommendation

Staff recommends approval of Resolution #25-15 to authorize the Mayor to execute the
Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with the Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office for election
administration services.
