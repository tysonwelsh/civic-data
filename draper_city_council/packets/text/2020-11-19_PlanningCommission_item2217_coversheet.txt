Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky
CC:  Date: November 19, 2020
Re: Draper Cove Phase 3 – Site Plan and Plat Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan and Plat Amendment for approximately 2.16 acres located at approximately 839 East Pioneer Rd. The property is currently zoned TC . The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved for a parking lot addition at the Draper Cove development.  Additionally the applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to remove two of the commercial units on the approved plat to allow for interior access to the new parking lot area and to amend the common area on the plat to include the new parking lot. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  The entire site part of a phased development plan, which includes a future fourth phase.
2.  The site plan and all phases conforms to applicable standards set forth in this title, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3.  The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
4.  The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
5.  The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns.
6.  There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 11/13/2020 Cover Memo
