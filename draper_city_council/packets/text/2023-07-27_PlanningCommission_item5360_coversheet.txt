Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: July 27, 2023
Re: Cross Point – Plat Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 22.58 acres located on the south side of Highland Dr., at approximately 166 East Highland Dr. The property is currently zoned CR and OS . The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to subdivide the property into two lots.
The property currently includes two parcels. One parcel is 17.28 acres in size and vacant. The second parcel is 5.29 acres in size and is currently developed as The Seasons mixed use building. Combined the properties are Lot 1 of the South Pointe Commerce Center Subdivision Plat. In 2007 Lot 1 was divided into two parcels outside of the City approval process. This plat will formalize the subdivision of the two parcels, creating Lots 4 and 5 of the South Pointe Commerce Center plat. The plat will also add a 0.40 acre strip of land located along the east side of the The Seasons parcel into the subdivision, included in Lot 4. This strip of land is owned by The Seasons and part of their development site. It ranges from 34-feet wide to 10-feet wide. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.   That there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended .
For denial:
