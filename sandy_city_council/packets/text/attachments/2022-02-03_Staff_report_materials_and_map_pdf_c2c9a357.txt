DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Ted Bernstein, is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to allow for a nursing
school in the Central Business District Office Subdistrict (CBD-O). (See Exhibit #1: Application
Material). A private or quasi-public school in the CBD-O Zone requires a Conditional Use
Permit as per Sandy City Code (21-8-2).

The subject area is 26,853 square feet of leased space located within an approximately 126,000
square foot building that is zoned Central Business District - Office Subdistrict (CBD-O). The
space comprises the entire fifth floor of the building. The applicant is proposing to utilize the
space for a nursing school. The proposed school operates on a hybrid model of in-person and
online learning with the in-person space utilized for labs and testing. (See Exhibit #2- Floor
Plan) The applicant is proposing to create three labs (3), a testing center, a student lounge, a
work room, three (3) shared offices, and fourteen (14) individual offices.

The property is located north of Sandy City Hall, east of Monroe Street, on Civic Center Drive.
Properties on the south and west sides are zoned CBD-O being an apartment complex and a
office building respectively. Properties on the north and east side are manufactured homes zoned
Manufactured Home Residential District (MH).

ANALYSIS

Compliance with Section 21-24-8(b) Parking Space Requirements
The Sandy City Land Development Code requires that private school parking requirements are
determined through Planning Commission review.

This property has two large office buildings with two multi-level parking garages. Based on the
size of the space the tenants will be occupying the lease agreement would allocate them 119
parking spaces. The applicant plans to enroll 48 students with 12-15 full time faculty and 10-12
part time faculty. This totals approximately 70 on the low end and 75 on the high end. At max
capacity they hope to enroll 72 students which would bring the total number of patrons using the
parking spaces to 99 which is a higher parking ratio than 1:1.

Dear Ms. Hague,

        Orbis Education in Partnership with Grand Canyon University is looking to bring our Bachelor’s in
Nursing Degree program to Salt Lake City. We have identified space at the building known as South Towne
Corporate Center located at 150 West Civic Center Drive and are currently finalizing lease terms on 26,853
square feet of space.

        The nursing degree we will be offering is a hybrid online program where all classwork is conducted
online and offsite. Students come in for weekly testing sessions, clinical training and simulations. Our
hours of operation are M-F, 8am-6pm however given the hybrid nature of the program students are not
onsite everyday and when they are onsite, they are not there all day.

         Our goal is to enroll 48 students per start however it may take time to get to that level. Our staff
will be comprised of administrative personal, full time and adjunct faculty. At full capacity we will have
12-15 full time and 10-12 part time employees.

        South Towne Corporate Center has two multi-level parking garages in addition to the surface
parking that encircles the property. As part of our lease agreement, we get 119 parking spaces within
the onsite parking facilities. As a general rule we do not schedule more than 72 students to be onsite at
the same time.

         If you would like to learn more about GCU’s nursing program and or Orbis Education, please visit
to the following websites: https://absn.gcu.edu/ and https://orbiseducation.com/

Please do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need additional information.
