GENERAL SESSION

Chair Tippetts called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. She
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

3.        CUP-22-30-403-049; 447-459 E Greenwood Ave; Amended Conditional Use Permit
          for 3 twin homes at 447-459 E Greenwood Ave.; SF-1 Zone with Duplex Overlay (SF-
          1/DO); Brandon Oquendo (Applicant); Elizabeth Arnold, Planner II (Staff).

Ms. Elizbeth Arnold stated that this a request for an amended conditional use permit. She added that when
it was first approved it had a stipulation that the residential structures were to be constructed as shown on
the submitted building elevations and site plan. This proposed amendment before the Planning
Commission today would be to change the elevations from those originally presented.

                                                    ***
APPLICANT:

The applicant was not present.

                                                    ***

PUBLIC HEARING:

Chair Tippetts opened the public hearing.

Mr. Lee Slusher, HOA president of Greenwood Village, asked if the new elevations would have a
sloped roof?

Ms. Arnold stated that as she understands it, the roofs would be flat.

Mr. Slusher asked if the only thing up for discussion is the building itself or if he could address
concerns with the site as it currently exists as people are dumping trash at this location?


PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION SEPTEMBER 15, 2021
Chair Tippetts stated that he should contact the code enforcement officer as that is a code
enforcement issue.

Mr. Slusher also asked if there was a start date for the construction to begin?

Chair Tippetts identified that the applicant was not represent to answer questions.

Ms. Patricia Potts asked if there is going to be something done with the trees and weeds? She stated
that she would like to request that the applicant remove the trees and weeds from the site.

Chair Tippetts stated that the issues presented will now be a part of public record and that the
concerns provided to the Planning Commission will be presented to the developer.

Mr. Doug Smalley, who resides just north of the proposed project, stated that he is concerned with
proposed flat roofs as it does not match the character of the nearby neighborhood. He stated he
and residents are not against new development but desire that new development in the area look
similar to the character of the existing neighborhood. He asks that the original rendering of the
development be followed and asks that the developer understand that this project was not
previously protested against because of the way the elevations were originally presented.

Chair Tippetts stated that because the applicant is not present the Commission is unable to address
some of the concerns of the residents with the applicant.

MOTION:        Commissioner Smallwood MOVED to close the public hearing. The motion
               was SECONDED by Commissioner Costello with all voting in favor.

                                                ***
DISCUSSION:

Commissioner Costello stated that she would like to have the applicant present so that items like

4.     Land Use Training

Mr. Wilcox presented to the Planning Commission a training on Land Use. The training went over
topics such as property rights and the legal protections that supplement them; the role of planning
and what it provides to the community; the role of the Planning Commission and Commissioner
duties; and legislative vs. administrative decisions.

                                               ***

STAFF UPDATE

5.     Upcoming Meetings and Projects.

Mr. Rockwood stated that there will not be a Planning Commission on September 22nd and that
the next meeting will be the regularly scheduled meeting in October. He added that the Planning
Commission will continue to see text amendments through the remainder of the year and that
development continues to come in from Jordan Bluffs and hopefully the City will begin to see
development along Main Street. Mr. Rockwood stated that he was working with Alex on the
general plan and some inconsistencies that exist in the code. He added that he will continue to
work with the current City staff and City Council on revising land use items in hopes of
developing a new general plan. Mr. Rockwood then introduced Elizabeth Arnold as the new
planner II and stated that she came to Midvale City after working in Wasatch County as a
planner. Mr. Rockwood also introduced the new planning director, Adam Olsen.

Mr. Adam Olsen stated that this is his second week working at Midvale. He previously worked
for American fork as their planning director. In his time, he has witnessed a lot of growth and
development and the challenges that presents. He is now excited to be with Midvale City to gain
experience in new projects that he would not have gotten the opportunity to experience at
American Fork. Lastly, he stated that he is very happy to be here and looks forward to working
with the Planning Commission in the future.
