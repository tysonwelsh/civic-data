                 1. Motion to convene Redevelopment Agency meeting.
                     Motion was made by Chairwoman Houseman to adjourn the meeting of the City Council
                     and convene the Sandy City Redevelopment Agency Meeting. Board Member Robinson
                     seconded the motion with all voting “yes”
                     The Redevelopment Agency Meeting commenced at 8:00 p.m.


                 2. Public Hearing. A Public Hearing was held for consideration of Resolution RD 22-02
                    regarding the adoption of the annual budget of the RDA of Sandy City, Utah for the fiscal
                    year beginning July 1, 2022, and ending June 30, 2023 as presented.

                     Chairwomen Houseman opened the hearing for public comment. Chairwoman Houseman
                     asked if anyone in the chamber or online wished to comment on the budget Resolution
                     item. There were no public comments made. Chairwoman Houseman agreed to continue
                     the Public Hearing until June 21,2022 so all budgets could be adopted the same night.

                 3. Kasey Dunlavy presented the board members with the approval of the RDA minutes for
                    May 17, 2022. Board member Scott Earl made a motion to adopt the Minutes for May
                    17, 2022. Board Member D’Sousa seconded the motion. The minutes were approved with
                    all voting yes.


                     Motion Passed: Yes 7 , No 0
