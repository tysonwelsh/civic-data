number of dogs outside does not matter. Condition #11 has been amended to state that minor 38 
modifications to the facility that are necessary in order to meet the minimum standards of the 39

required. 42

Staff and Commission discussed the conditions. There were questions regarding the number of 44

concerns with noise were discussed. If the dogs are brought to the property, and the applicant 47
