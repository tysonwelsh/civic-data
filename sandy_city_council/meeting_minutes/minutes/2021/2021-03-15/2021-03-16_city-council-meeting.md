5:15 Council Meeting
                       Council Chair Cyndi Sharkey welcomed those in attendance.

                       Chair Sharkey read a statement regarding the continuation of virtual meetings without an
                       anchor location.

Prayer / Pledge of Allegiance

                       Council Member Houseman offered the prayer.

                       Matt Huish, CAO, led the Pledge.

Non-voting Items

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                       Mike Applegarth, Council Executive Director, briefed the Council on upcoming agenda
                       items. Closed caption is now available on the zoom live meetings. The closed caption will
                       not be available on the video recording. The audio of the meeting remains the official
                       record of the meeting.

Council Member Business

                       Council Member Christensen mentioned the CDBG Committee is meeting tomorrow
                       evening at 5:00 pm. Hale Centre Theatre is seating at full capacity. She thanked Animal
                       Control for their help with a recent issue at Lone Peak Park.

                       Council Member Houseman thanked the Sandy Rotary Club for their efforts raising funds
                       for the Sandy Club. She also gave an update on the Central Wasatch Commission which
                       is holding a summit this Friday. The meeting is open to the public.

                       Council Member Zoltanski encouraged resident involvement in the Central Wasatch
                       Commission summit this Friday. She would like to hear feedback from Sandy residents.
                       Utah Division of Wildlife issued a reminder to keep your dogs on a leash at city parks
                       which is also a law in Sandy City.

                       Council Member Robinson spoke about the need for the Council and Administration to
                       work collaboratively regarding the allocation of any funds received from the federal
                       government through the American Rescue Act.

Mayor's Report

                       Mayor Bradburn provided some details regarding the process for the allocation of any
                       funds received from the American Rescue Act. He also mentioned that Sandy received a
                       $100,000 grant from Wasatch Front Regional Council.

                       Chief Severson thanked Pauline Lopez, Records Director, for 45 years of service to
                       Sandy City.

2.         21-106                 Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District requesting adoption of
                                  Resolution consenting to the reorganization of WFWRD as a local district.
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  WFWRD Executive Summary of Conversion from SSD to LD
                                  4840-1266-3261 v.1
                                  Resolution 21-11C
                                  Resolution 21-11C_Signed

                                  Council Member Kris Nicholl introduced Pam Roberts and Rachel Anderson with the
                                  Wasatch Front and Recycling District who provided the background and history of the
                                  formation of the WFWRD. They briefed the Council on the proposed reorganization of the
                                  WFWRD from a special services district to an independent local district. In order to
                                  successfully accomplish this transition, each municipality included within the WFWRD
                                  must consent to the reorganization. Three municipalities have already adopted the
                                  resolution. The WFWRD requested the City Council approve the Resolution 21-11C
                                  consenting to the reorganization of the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District as a
                                  local district.

                                  Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Brian McCuiston, Planning Director, presented on the Kasteler Annexation a property, a
                                  parcel of approximately 3.22 acres. The Planning Commission forwarded a positive
                                  recommendation to approve this annexation.The City is recommending an R-1-20A zone
                                  for this parcel. Council questions and comments followed. Mr. Kasteler spoke about the
                                  annexation and proposed development. Matt Huish spoke about the cost analysis
                                  process associated with proposed annexations.

                                  Public Comment opened.
                                  Public Comment closed.

                                  Following the vote on the motion, Council moved to Item 4 on the Agenda and heard
                                  General Citizen Comments.



                                  A motion was made by Kristin Coleman-Nicholl, seconded by Zach Robinson, to
                                  adopt Ordinance 21-10, an Ordinance annexing territory located at
                                  approximately 2576 East and 2590 East 10000 South and 10118 South Alta Villa
                                  Drive, in Salt Lake County, comprising approximately 3.22 acres into the
                                  municipality of Sandy City; establishing zoning for the annexed property; also
                                  providing a severance and effective date for the annexation.... The motion
