Public Comment (10:54)

Action Agenda

1.          A resolution approving a substantial amendment to the Program Year 2019 Annual Action Plan
            reprogramming CDBG-CV funds for Covid-19 public health response. (22-038) (12:26)

     Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-23, as currently constituted, has been
                  made by council rule.

Melissa McNalley, Planner, provided background on grants and funds CDBG receive in a year and for what
those funds are used.

Ms. McNalley asked Brian Jones if the agenda items needed to be discussed separately.

Mr. Jones responded that they could be discussed together but a vote would need to take place for each.

Vice-Chair Handley read the second agenda item.

Ms. McNalley continued to discuss the CDBG funds and the Consolidated Action Plan.

Councilor Shipley asked what percentage of the grants could be used for infrastructure.

Ms. McNalley responded that years ago, council decided 15% can go towards social security programs.
She reminded that the percentage could change based on council’s direction.

Vice-Chair Handley shared some reservations that he had regarding the amount of money that is
requested by and applicant versus the funding provided to them.

Ms. McNalley shared that the applicants might not receive the maximum amount they requested, but
they would be asked what the minimum would be to keep them operating.

Vice-Chair Handley suggested in the future that it would be helpful to see the context of what applicants
are requesting and what they are granted.

Ms. McNalley also said it might be helpful for the council to know the intentions of what the funds would
be used for.

Vice-Chair Handley opened public comment. With none, he closed public comment.

Vice-Chair Handley called for a vote on both agenda items.

     Vote:        The motion was approved 6:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, MacKay,
                  Shipley, and Whipple in favor. Councilor Hoban excused.

2.        A resolution approving the Year Three, Annual Action Plan of the 2020-2024 Five-Year
          Consolidated Plan. (22-038) (vote 25:43)

     Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-24, as currently constituted, has been
                  made by council rule.

     Vote:        The motion was approved 6:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, MacKay,
                  Shipley, and Whipple in favor. Councilor Hoban excused.

3.        A resolution tentatively adopting the tentative budget for Provo City Corporation for the
          fiscal year beginning July 1, 2022 and ending June 30, 2023. (22-016) (26:20)

     Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-25, as currently constituted, has been
                  made by council rule.

Kelsey Zarbock, Budget Officer, presented. She shared that this is not the final budget and that future
modifications could be made.

Councilor Ellsworth asked about ‘Exhibit A’ and if the tentative budget had been updated.

Ms. Zarbock shared that it had not be updated. She added that the updates would be presented at the
meeting on June 7th.

John Magness added that they are keeping a list of items council has discussed for revisions.

Brian Jones clarified what the intent of adopting the tentative budget.

Councilor Shipley asked if the revised version would include ARPA funds.

Vice-Chair Handley opened public comment. With none, he closed public comment.

Councilor Shipley suggested that in the future the council be presented with the running list of changes
prior to the meeting they adopt the tentative budget.

Mr. Jones added context regarding truth in taxation process.

Councilor Ellsworth asked Councilor Shipley’s for clarification on his suggestion.

Councilor Whipple asked what the reasoning would be to not adopt the tentative budget.

Mr. Jones provided information of the possible scenarios that could happen.

Vice-Chair Handley called for a vote.

     Vote:        The motion was approved 6:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, MacKay,
                  Shipley, and Whipple in favor. Councilor Hoban excused.

The Provo Municipal Council recessed and convened as the governing board of the Redevelopment Agency
of Provo with Chair Ellsworth conducting.

Redevelopment Agency Governing Board

4.        A resolution tentatively adopting a tentative budget for Redevelopment Agency of the
          Provo City Corporation for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2022 and ending June 30, 2023.
          (22-016) (42:49)

     Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-RDA-05-17-1, as currently constituted,
                  has been made by council rule.

Ms. Zarbock presented.

Chair Ellsworth opened public comment. With none, she closed public comment.

     Motion:      Board Member Handley made motion to tentatively adopt the tentative budget for
                  the Redevelopment Agency of Provo. Board Member Whipple seconded motion.

     Vote:        The motion was approved 6:0 with Board Members Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley,
                  MacKay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor. Board Member Hoban excused.

The Governing Board of the Redevelopment Agency of Provo City adjourned and convened as the
