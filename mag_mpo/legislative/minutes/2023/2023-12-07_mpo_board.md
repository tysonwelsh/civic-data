Public Comment (2:27) No public comment was received. Approve November 2, 2023 Meeting Minutes (2:35) Mayor Larsen moved to approve the November 2, 2023 meeting minutes as presented. Mayor Olsen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously . Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Modification (3:14) Bob Allen, MAG Senior Transportation Planner and Program Manager a.   Lindon Heritage Trail Phase 5 Lindon was awarded $1,589,850 in federal funds in 2018 to complete the final phase of the Lindon Heritage Trail from Murdock Canal Trail to the Lindon Boat Harbor. This phase of the project includes 2 railroad crossings and a corridor through an industrial area. The project is now ready to go out to bid. Due to permitting delays with the railroad and corridor acquisition as well as construction inflation, the project is now estimated at $2,677,101. Lindon is requesting additional funds to complete the project.   Original funds $1,589,850 Additional funds $1,087,251 New MAG total $2,677,101 Remaining Contingency Funds $4,696,216. Mayor Olsen moved to approve the addition of $1,087,251 be added to the Lindon Heritage Trail Phase 5. Mayor Johnson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. b.  Payson Main Street Bus Pullout MPO Board December 7, 2023 Page 3 Payson was awarded $292,000 in federal funds in 2020 to shift and improve a bus pullout near the intersection of Main Street and SR198.  Buses using the existing stop impede traﬃc and create problems at an already congested intersection. The project is now ready to go out to bid. Due to construction inflation the project is now estimated at $532,138.  UDOT has already added $119,768 in TSP funds to the project.  Payson is requesting $140,138 MAG funds be added to complete the project.   Original funds $272,232 Additional funds $140,138 New MAG total $432,138 UDOT funds $119,768* New project totals $532,138 *19,768 is the match on the original funds. Remaining Contingency Funds $4,556,078. Mayor Larsen moved to approve the addition of $140,138 to the Pason Bus Pullout project. Mayor Andersen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 2024 Legislative Project Prioritization Voting (11:44) LaNiece Davenport, MPO Director Ms. Davenport reminded the Board that the conversation for the meeting is to identify any possible one-time funding that the MPO could get from the legislature. Revenue at the state level is down from last year, and an increase is unlikely. Although funding is limited, the MPO Board coming together to have the conversations, and creating a process to identify priority projects to present to the legislature is valuable. An overview of the timeline and MPO Board approved voting procedures was provided. Mayor Johnson presented a map with proposed future corridors in Utah County. He explained that expanding item H36 to six lanes and having H37 tie into the Vineyard Connector will be the quickest solution to a lot of transportation issues. Mayor Johnson asked the committee to consider voting for H36 with a portion of H37 being added to it. Ms. Davenport stated that the revised projects 36 and 37 will be added to the ranked project list being voted on. She noted that MAG staff became aware of this request prior to the meeting and has not had an opportunity to vet the revisions being presented. Mayor Ostler moved that project H36 expand to six lane, and combine a portion of the Vineyard Connector project H37 to H36. Mayor Lundberg seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
