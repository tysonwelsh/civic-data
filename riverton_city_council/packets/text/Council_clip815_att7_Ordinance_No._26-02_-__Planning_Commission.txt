                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE NO. 26-02

      AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE SECTION TITLE 2.95.030
                     [PLANNING COMMISSION OFFICERS]

       WHEREAS, Utah Code section 10-20-301 requires the Riverton City Council to establish by
ordinance the Riverton City Planning Commission (“Planning Commission”); and

       WHEREAS, said ordinance is required to include procedures to select a chair and vice chair of the
Planning Commission; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Code section 2.95.030 addresses the selection of a chair and vice chair;
and

         WHEREAS, Mayor Buroker, after conferring with a representative of the Planning Commission,
has recommended that the officials appointed to serve as chair and vice chair of the Planning Commission
not be strictly confined to terms in office that last for a single year; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that in certain instances, the Planning
Commission may be better served if its chair and vice chair serve in these roles for periods of time which
exceed one year; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment to Riverton City
Code section 2.95.030 as presented by staff will provide greater flexibility to the Planning Commission in
selecting commissioners to serve as chair and vice chair;

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City as follows:

Section 1.       Amendment. The Riverton City Code Section 2.95.030 is hereby to read as
                 follows in the attached Exhibit A.

Section 2.       Adoption. This ordinance shall take effect on publication in compliance with Utah
                 Law.

§ 2.95.030 Officers.

The planning commission shall elect annually, during a regularly scheduled meeting, a chair and
a vice-chair of the commission, who may be allowed to succeed themselves. They will be elected
from among the members of the planning commission by a majority of the total membership.
The duties and responsibilities of the chair shall be to direct planning commission meetings and
to keep order within those meetings. In the event that the chair is not present at the planning
commission meeting it shall be the duty of the vice-chair to be responsible for the chair's duties.
The chair shall be a full voting member of the planning commission.

§ 2.95.030 Officers.
