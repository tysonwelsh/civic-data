Provo Orem Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) Frequently Asked Question and Provo Orem Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) Financing Recommendation - Andrew Jackson, Shawn Seager, Chad Eccles BRT is a 10.5 mile route that connects major employment centers, UVU, BYU, and FrontRunner. Ridership on the current Route 830 is about 3,000 a day, the projected ridership for BRT on the same route is 13,000 a day. The BRT provides high capacity, high-quality, 5 minute head-way during peak time, which increases ridership. The total cost for the project is estimated to be $150 million and half of that cost is anticipated to be paid for from a Federal Transit Administration (FTA) Small Starts Grant Program. The Small Starts Program requires a 50/50 match, making the local match $75 million. The value of right-of-way being used will account for approximately $10 million of potential in-kind value for the local match, with $3 million in engineering costs as match, leaving $65 million needed. MAG and UTA have worked with Lewis Young Robertson and Burningham to better refine the financing funding plan. Current projects already on the TIP would not be delayed. MPO, State, Region funds still have funds available. 2016 the Provo Lakeview Parkway project, $6.6 million has 3rd quarter funding. As a contingency plan and if there is not enough money is in the 3rd quarter, Provo in willing to delay this project to ensure there is funding to cover O&M in the short term. The county would bond against the 3rd quarter cent transportation tax; and O&M would also be paid with the county's 3rd quarter cent tax revenues. Then UTA will enter into a purchase agreement to purchase the BRT asset by 2028 with a lump sum payment to payback the 3rd quarter with interest and opportunity cost with 1st quarter funds, instead of a ten-year repayment schedule. The obligation of the bond will move from Utah County to UTA in 2028. BRT is expected to take off 30% of wear and tear of the roads. Need to plan for the future not just for today. As the county grows more transit options will be needed. Mayor Steve Lauritzen moved to approve the Provo Orem Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) Financing Plan as presented with the understanding that UTA purchase the BRT asset in totality in the year 2028 with a balloon payment. Regional Air Quality and its Correlation to Transportation Planning - Susan Hardy The Clean Air Act (CAA) requires in areas experiencing air quality problems, transportation planning must be consistent with air quality goals and be determined through Transportation conformity process.  Emission Sources  Point & Area, non-road engineers, on road vehicles, and miscellaneous  Major transportation-related criteria pollutants  Ozone (OZ) and its precursors volatile organic compounds (VOC) and oxides of nitrogen (NOX)  Particulate Matter PM10 and PM2.5; and Carbon Monoxide (CO)  Non-Attainment Areas: do not meet or previously have not met air quality standards  Maintenance Areas: fulfilled the requirements and demonstrated they do not exceed the standards Utah County is designated as Non-Attainment for PM10 and PM2.5 and Provo is designated as Maintenance for CO. There are five monitoring stations in Utah County: Highland, Lindon, North Provo, Provo, and Spanish Fork. Transportation Conformity  Demonstrates that emission from travel on the region's roads do not exceed the budget set for mobile sources in the State Implementation Plan (SIP)  Transportation bill links compliance with conformity requirement to continued FHWA and FTA funding for transportation plans, programs, and projects  It ensure that approval and funding goes to projects that are consistent with air quality goals Tier-3 Gasoline Resolution - Susan Hardy Tier-3 gasoline has a lower sulfur content that according to experts is an effective way to reduce pollution from vehicles. The production and selling of Tier-3 will be mandatory in 2017. The Wasatch Front air quality would benefit using Tier-3 gas. It will reduce particulate and ozone pollution from vehicles using it by 80%. The estimated cost to the consumer could be from one to twenty cents a gallon and will probably average out to ten cents a gallon.
