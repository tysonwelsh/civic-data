SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 6.09 acres located on the south side of 11400 S and west of Engelmann
Drive, at approximately 11492 S. Engelmann Drive (Exhibits B and C). The property is
currently zoned R3 and R4. The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat Amendment
be approved to allow for the development of the currently vacant site with 32 single-family
lots.

This property is part of the overall Big Willow Creek development. The Big Willow Creek
Development agreement was approved in January 2017 for 115 dwelling units on 49.75
acres. The approval included a Development Agreement limiting the density to a




Big Willow Phase 9                                                  App. # 2024-0026-SUB

maximum of 2.3 dwelling units per acre (du/ac), and required a variety of lot sizes between
10,000 square feet and 20,000 square feet in size. Preliminary plat approval for all 115 lots
was approved by the Planning Commission on July 18, 2017. Phases 1A and 1B obtained
final plat approval on September 18, 2018, with an amended plat approved for these
phases on November 21, 2019.

In April 2019 the applicant amended the development agreement (1st Amendment
Development Agreement) in order to add an additional 18.1 acres of property into the Big
Willow Creek Development. The 1st Amendment of the Development Agreement rezoned
some of the additional 18.1 acres of property to the RM2 zone, but limited any
development to a maximum of 9 du/ac. The remainder of the property was rezoned to R4.

On December 10, 2019 the applicant obtained a rezone and land use amendment for an
additional 4.12 acres of land located adjacent to the development. This area was not
included in the development agreements at the time of rezone. On May 5, 2020, the
Development Agreement was amended a second time (Second Amendment Development
Agreement) and added the 4.12 acres of property to the agreement, set density standards
for development on the additional properties added to the agreement, and set standards
for the approval and development of Phase 2.

Phase 1A was vacated by the City Council on April 6, 2020 to allow for the reconfiguration
of the access road. The applicant obtained final subdivision plat approval for Phase 2 on
August 27, 2020. Big Willow Creek Phase 6 was approved on April 7, 2021, as a minor
subdivision, and included reconfiguration of the access road from 11400 South. Phase 1B
obtained approval to reconfigure the existing roadway into a cul-de-sac on April 22, 2021.

The Phase 3 final plat was approved by the Planning Commission on August 26, 2021 after
another amendment to the development agreement (Third Amendment) was approved on
April 20, 2021. The Third Amendment to the development agreement which set the
standards for approval for the Phase 3 plat.

According to DCMC Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the R3 zone is to “permit medium density
residential development without special mitigation requirements. This zone is intended to
provide incentives to foster residential development with little impact on its surroundings and on
municipal services, and to generally preserve the semirural character called for in the density
element of the general plan.”

According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the R4
zone is to “permit medium to medium-high density residential developments with single-family
homes. These zones are intended to foster thriving and well maintained neighborhoods that
allow upscale single-family homes on smaller lots that require less maintenance and provide a
balanced lifestyle.”

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request and approve the application based on the findings listed
below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff notes the applicant is
required to comply with the following requirements pertaining to the plat and
development of the subdivision:

1.      That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
        Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
        and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
2.      That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
        development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
3.      If the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date of approval
        or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced,
        such approval shall be null and void. This time period may be extended by the
        Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6) month period for good cause
        shown. The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension prior to the
        expiration of the original one (1) year. See DCMC Section 17-9-070.
4.      The developer must provide a drainage report including detention, orifice, and
        pipe sizing calculations per 17-3-030(C)(9) and 17-3-030(F). The drainage report
        must meet the requirements of the City of Draper Drainage Design Criteria. LID
        (low-impact development) BMP’s are required in the overall drainage design.
        Report must be stamped and signed by a P.E. registered in Utah.
5.      The location of Clear View Triangles, per DCMC 9-27-080(F), for corner lots must be
        included on the Final Plat.
6.      Amend the plat to rename the typica’s shown to public utility easement details and
        not setback details shown.

       certification letter after subdivision construction is complete.

       triangles are 30 feet measured at the property line, i.e. rear of sidewalk, in each
       direction at intersection with a hypotenuse connecting the lines creating the clear
       view area. No objects above three feet permitted, including fencing.
