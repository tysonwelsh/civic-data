COMMISSIONER LEE - I'D LIKE TO MAKE A BRIEF STATEMENT: OUR FORM OF
GOVERNMENT IS ONE OF THE BEST IN THE WORLD BECAUSE WE BELIEVE IN THE
RULE OF LAW AND THAT MEANS THAT THE RULE OF LAW PROTECTS OUR
FRIENDS, AND IT PROTECTS THOSE WHO DISAGREE WITH US. WHEN THIS
AGENDA ITEM CAME UP, IT WAS FAIRLY NARROW, NARROW TO THE POINT OF
LET’S SEE IF THERE WERE SOME POLICIES IN THE COUNTY THAT WERE VIOLATED
WHEN IT CAME TO THE LEAKING OF DOCUMENTS AND LAST WEEK WHEN ALL 3
OF US WERE TALKING ABOUT THIS AND TRYING TO FIGURE OUT WHAT WAS
GOING ON, WE DID NOT KNOW. YOU SAW ALL 3 OF US THINKING THAT WE
NEEDED MORE INFORMATION AND HOW DO WE GET MORE? WE CAN ONLY DO
THAT EITHER BY OURSELVES OR WE DO THAT WITH SOMEBODY ASKING THOSE
QUESTIONS. WELL, IT DID NOT PROCEED FORWARD TO HAVE SOMEBODY ELSE
DO THAT AND SO WE WERE LEFT ON OUR OWN AS COMMISSIONERS TO SEEK OUT
WHAT WE COULD FIND AND WHAT WAS FOUND WAS THAT THIS NARROW SCOPE,
GOING BACK TO OUR PROCEDURES AND RULE OF LAW, THAT WE HAD THAT WE
WERE LOOKING AT IN THE WAY OF THAT THERE WAS SOMETHING THAT WAS
VIOLATED FELL APART. IT FELL APART BECAUSE THESE DOCUMENTS THAT THAT
WERE THE FOCUS HAD BEEN OUT THERE FOR AT LEAST 2 YEARS, SO HOW CAN
YOU LEAK SOMETHING THAT HAS BEEN OUT IN THE PUBLIC REALM FOR 2
YEARS? ONCE WE HAD THAT INFORMATION AND WE CONFERRED AND TALKED
ABOUT IT, AND IF ANYBODY WANTS TO FIND THAT THEMSELVES, YOU CAN JUST
GO ASK PROVO CITY BECAUSE THEY ARE THE ONES THAT HOUSE THOSE
DOCUMENTS. SO WITH THAT BEING THE CRUX OF THE WHOLE POSSIBLE
INVESTIGATION, FOR COUNTY PURPOSES, IT DOESN’T WARRANT FOR US TO
MOVE FORWARD.

TO CHRISTIE'S POINT, WE FELT IT VERY NEEDED AND NECESSARY TO LEAVE IT
OPEN BECAUSE IF SOMETHING DOES COME UP TO THE POINT TO WHERE THERE
NEEDS TO BE MORE INFORMATION GIVEN TO US OR AN INVESTIGATION, WE ARE
VERY OPEN TO LOOKING AT THAT AND SEEING WHAT IS NEEDED AND
NECESSARY SO THAT WE CAN GO FORWARD AND FIND WHATEVER IF IT COME
UPS. BELIEVE ME WHEN I SAY THIS - WE ARE TOLD AS COMMISSIONERS ALL
KINDS OF STUFF AT TIMES. NOT EVERYTHING THAT’S TOLD TO US DO WE
ACTUALLY GO OUT AND DO AN INVESTIGATION. IT HAS TO COME TO A LEVEL TO
WHERE ALL 3 OF US FEEL LIKE IT WARRANTS US TO MOVE FORWARD SO IN THE
WAY OF THE DOCUMENTS THEMSELVES, THE BOTTOM FELL OUT FROM
UNDERNEATH IT BECAUSE IT’S BEEN OUT THERE FOR SO LONG.
