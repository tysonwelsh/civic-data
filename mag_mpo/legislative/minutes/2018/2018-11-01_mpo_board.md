Recognition for Years of Service - Shawn Seager Steve Meyer, UTA and Brent Schvaneveldt, UDOT are both retiring and were presented with plaques in recognition of their years of service and many accomplishments. Provo Airport Future Planning - Dave Decker, Provo City Mayor Kaufusi introduced the Provo Airport Future Planning presentation and asked MAG to include air travel in their conversations and add mass transit connections to air travel facilities in the TransPlan 50. Dave Decker, Provo City Public Works Director, gave an update on the current status and amenities needed to sustain the projected growth of potential passengers. Flights are limited and facilities are at capacity because of the limited ramp space and single gate that creates a 2-hour gap between flights. 10 acres of concrete was laid this year to build a new ramp that will provide space to park aircraft. The project totaled $8M funded by FAA, EDA and Provo City. Utah Transit Authority 2019 Budget - Steve Meyer, UTA The UTA Board approved the tentative 2019 budget on October 25, 2018. Copies of the budget will be sent on flashcards. Steve Meyer presented an overview of the tentative 2019 budget and changes from the previous year. Strategic focus for 2019 will be on customer experience, leadership and advocacy, access to opportunity, strategic funding, and workforce of the future. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning Committee November 1, 2018 Page 3 Challenges UTA faces is population growth, air quality, access for all, disruptive technologies and sustainable funding. Lehi I-15 Tech Corridor Construction Update - Bob Westover, UDOT The emphasis on the I-15 Technology Corridor project has been the Triumph Bridge. I-15 has been lowered as traffic shifted east. The contractors are on schedule to have the bridge accepting traffic early November with 2-lanes in each direction. In mid-November construction will begin on 2100 North with a temporary DDI put in place. Pioneer Crossing and Redwood Road Construction Update - Matt Parker, UDOT The initial project was broken out into three projects so it could be finished faster and alleviate traffic congestions. Some of the current projects include the widening of Redwood Road, installing signals, and a CFI at Redwood Road and Pioneer Crossing. Bike safety provisions were put in place that include green thermal plastic and paint, bike boxes and an alternate lane to create safer ways to cross the intersection and increase active transportation. Exchange Program Process - Bob Allen, MAG The majority of MPO projects are funded with County Transportation Tax Funds and MAG Exchange Funds which require projects to follow the Joint Project Management Process. Projects funded with CMAQ, TAP and/or are a UDOT project, will follow the identified UDOT process. It was recommended to start the agreement process for 2018 TIP projects now, and begin talking to MAG staff for 2019 projects to get the process started to be prepared for the funding allocation. The accounting and invoice process were discussed. Update Reports from FHWA, UDOT, UTA and Transportation Commission Utah Department of Transportation: None UDOT Transportation Commission: None UTA: None FHWA: Steve Call reported that FHWA is operating under a continuing resolution that will provide two months of project funding.
