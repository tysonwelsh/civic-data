3.         22-372                 UDOT providing the council with an update on the Little Cottonwood 
                                  Canyon Environmental Impact Statement
                   Attachments:   Little Cottonwood Canyon EIS Presentation

6.         22-376                 The Administrative Services Department is recommending the adoption of 
                                  four resolutions as part of Fiscal Year 2023 Budget Carryover
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  FY 2023 Carryover Presentation
                                  22-51C General Fund (Community Events)
                                  22-49 C Capital Funds Carryover
                                  22-50 C Special Revenue Funds Carryover
                                  22-48 C Proprietary Funds Carryover
                                  Sharkey Memo and Alternate Resolution 22-48C.pdf

                                  Public Hearing: Brian Kelley, Finance Director. presented on the  Carryover Resolutions 
                                  proposed for the 2023 Budget Carryover for Council consideration and action. Mr. Kelly 
                                  reviewed the one-time FY 2023 requests. Council questions and comments followed. 

                                  Chair Robinson invited the public to comment on all resolutions listed with this item.

                                  Public comment opened:

                                  Mr. Corey Carter, Sandy City Fire Fighter, spoke about the memo submitted by Council 
                                  Member Cyndi Sharkey and thanked her. He spoke about call backs and the need to 
                                  address the long-term staffing needs of the Fire Department. He thanked and 
                                  acknowledged the Council for their support.

                                  Ms. Pat Jones is supportive of funding the fire department current 3-2 staffing levels. 

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren expressed support for the Sharkey proposal and is in favor of the 
                                  3-2 staffing.

                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Following Public Comments, Council Member Sharkey presented the Alternative to 
                                  Resolution 22-48C which provides funding for FY 2024 payroll management for Fire 
                                  Staffing. The proposal transfers funds of $1,173,773 to Payroll Managment for FY 2024 
                                  Fire Staffing from Capital Projects for FY24. 

                                  Council discussion followed. Mayor Zoltanski, the CAO, Finance Director, Fire Chief, and 
                                  City Attorney all weighed in on the discussion and provided feedback to the Council 
                                  regarding the Alternative to Resolution 22-48C. They spoke about the importance of 
                                  following the city's staffing plan and process and expressed some concerns with the 
                                  alternative resolution. After a lengthy discussion, Council Members Sharkey, Houseman, 
                                  Earl, D'Sousa and Stroud expressed support for the Alternative to Resolution 22-48C to 
                                  meet the current needs of the Fire Department staffing. Council Members Robinson and 
                                  Mecham expressed support for the Fire Department staffing needs, but was not 
                                  supportive of the Alternative to Resolution 22-48C as presented this evening.

                                  The motions and roll call votes on the Resolutions attached to this Agenda Item are as 
                                  follows:

                                  A motion was made by Ryan Mecham, seconded by Marci Houseman to approve 
                                  Resolution 22-51C, a resolution increasing total appropriations within the general

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Marci Houseman will be out of town for the next week and will not be at 
                      the October 25th City Council Meeting. She acknowledged the Parks Department for their 
                      efforts coordinating the Trunk or Treat event this weekend.

                      Council Member Alison Stroud spoke about the International Fire Fighter Challenge event 
                      that took place in Sandy City. She was very impressed with the dedication and strength 
                      of those competing. She just finished the Police Citizens Academy and thanked the 
                      Police Department for all their time and coordination of the traing - it was a great program.

                      Council Member Scott Earl thanked spoke about the Interfaith Luncheon. He thanked Ms. 
                      Edwards for coordinating the event. He also provided an update from the CDBG 
                      Committee.

                      Council Member Brooke D'Sousa thanked Scott Ellis with Public Utilities for presenting to 
                      the Sandy Youth Council at their first meeting. It was a great and informative meeting and 
                      the Youth Council enjoyed the night, especially exploring the Public Utilities garage and 
                      vehicles. She thanked Police for running the Police Citizens' Academy. She also enjoyed 
                      the recent Interfaith Luncheon. 

                      Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked the Fire Department staff for their efforts to 
                      coordinate the Fire Fighter Challenge event.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the recent Wasatch Choice 
                      Workshop. She also spoke about the Police Academy which gives the participants an 
                      opportunity to experience being tasered, pepper sprayed, or shot at with a pepper ball - 
                      Two of the Council Members participated in this memorable experience!

Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the playground installation schedule at Willow Creek Park. 
                      She spoke about her recent trip to Appleton, WI with the Fire Department staff where 
                      they went to the Pierce manufacturing facility and to complete the final inspection of the 
                      pumper engine purchased by the City. She shared her experience of the trip. It was a 
                      privilege to meet these employees who work so hard to produce the equipment. The 
                      employees expressed their thanks to the Mayor and Sandy City residents for their 
                      purchase.
