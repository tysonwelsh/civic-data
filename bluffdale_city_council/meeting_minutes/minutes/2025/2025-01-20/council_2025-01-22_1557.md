8.     Public Hearing Items.


       8.1     Resolution 2025-11 - Consideration and Vote to Declare as Surplus Property
               1.248 square feet and Related Easements at Bluffdale Citv Park for a Cellular
               Tower Lease to Verizon and Lease Approval. {Staff Presenters, Fred
               Donaldson and Bruce Kartchner\


Assistant City Manager, Bruce Kartchner, reported that Staff has been working on the lease since
2018. A memo explaining its history was included in the Meeting Packet. There has been poor
cell service in the area because it is in between antennas. They are excited that the agreement is
finally ready. The tower will look like an old-fashioned water tank and fits with the Old West
Days theme.


City Attomey,Fred Donaldson,reported that the surplus property is located at Bluffdale City Park
and will be used for a Verizon cell phone tower. The lease has a five-year initial term and four
five-year renewals. Verizon will pay an annual fee and maintain the equipment. City Staff worked
with Verizon to ensure that they were comfortable with the design and that adequate protections
will be put in place.


Council Member Austin asked about the tower's proximity to the basketball courts and any danger
to children climbing the wall to retrieve balls. Mayor Hall asked if anyone else would have access
or ifthey would need to call Verizon to retrieve them. Mr. Donaldson stated that he did not know
the answer to the question, but they would discuss the issue with Verizon and find a solution such
as posting a sign or obtaining a key to the area.


Council Member Austin stated that the utility rack and transformer are shown outside the wall in
the Site Plan and asked why they would not be located inside. Mr. Donaldson was unsure of the
reason for that placement but the City Engineer reviewed and approved the plan.
