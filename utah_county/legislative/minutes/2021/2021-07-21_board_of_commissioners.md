13. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE SIGNING OF AN AGREEMENT WITH UTAH
DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH FOR THE EPITRAX PROGRAM.
-Julie Dey, Health

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH - READING HERE IT SAYS EPITRAX MEANS THE
ELECTRONIC DISEASE SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM WHICH IS A WEB-BASED PRODUCT
USED BY THE STATE AND LOCAL HEALTH JURISDICTION SURVEILLANCE STAFF
FOR DISEASE SURVEILLANCE, CASE MANAGEMENT, INVESTIGATION ACTIVITIES.
SOME WERE CURIOUS ABOUT PRIVACY ISSUES.

CURTIS JONES, COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT BUREAU DIRECTOR OF
EPIDEMIOLOGY - WE PUT INFORMATION INTO EPITRAX. ONCE THE STATE HAS
THAT INFORMATION, THEY'RE THE ONLY ONES WHO HAVE ACCESS TO IT
BESIDES US AND THEN THEY STRIP ALL THAT DATA AND SEND IT TO THE CDC
(CENTER FOR DISEASE CONTROL) SO THERE’S ONLY TWO CHANNELS THAT HAVE
IT: THE LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS AND THE STATE HEALTH DEPARTMENT.
WE CAN SEE ALL THE UTAH COUNTY RESIDENTS, BUT THE OTHER COUNTIES
CANNOT. THIS IS FOR MONITORING AND WAY THAT THE INFECTIONS ARE
TRAVELING THROUGH THE COMMUNITY. FOCUS IS ON NUMBERS, NOT PEOPLE.


BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF UTAH COUNTY, UTAH
Public Meeting Minutes

July 21, 2021

4|

TYLER PLEWE, HEALTH DEPARTMENT DEPUTY DIRECTOR - THE OTHER
IMPORTANT THING WITH SURVEILLANCE IS IT’S BEEN HELPFUL FOR US TO FIND
OUTBREAKS. WITH OUR HEPATITIS A THAT WE HAD A COUPLE OF YEARS AGO,
WE’RE ABLE TO TRACK EVERYBODY AND ABLE TO SEE THAT UPTICK IN
HEPATITIS A AND THEN WE USE THAT SURVEILLANCE TO SEE WHERE THEIR
PATHS CROSS. WITH THAT WE’RE SOMETIMES ABLE TO PINPOINT WHERE AN
OUTBREAK DOES HAPPEN THEN WORK WITH BUSINESSES TO MITIGATE
PROCESSES TO ELIMINATE THE DISEASE FROM CONTINUING TO SPREAD WITHIN
OUR COMMUNITY.

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH - BASICALLY, WE’RE EMPHASIZING THAT THIS IS A
TRACKING OF BASIC INFORMATION, NOT INDIVIDUALS?

CURTIS JONES - CORRECT.
