                          MINUTES OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
                         REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY BOARD MEETING

                             Wednesday, September 8, 2021 – 7:00 pm
                                 Approved February 23, 2022

                 This meeting was held electronically via Zoom with the base location
                     at City Hall 8000 S Redwood Road • West Jordan, UT 84088


BOARD:        Chair Zach Jacob, Vice-Chair Kelvin Green, Chad Lamb, Christopher McConnehey, David Pack,
              Kayleen Whitelock (electronically), and Melissa Worthen (excused)

              Dirk Burton, Public Works Director Brian Clegg, City Administrator Korban Lee, IT

              Danyce Steck, and City Attorney Robert Wall

REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair Jacob called the meeting to order at 8:03 pm
2. CLOSED MEETING
At 8:04 pm, Board Member Christopher McConnehey moved that the Redevelopment Agency
Board recess to convene in a closed meeting to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real
property as provided by Utah Code Annotated §52-4-205 and adjourn from there.
Board Member Kelvin Green seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Council Chair Zach Jacob                    Yes
Council Vice Chair Kelvin Green             Yes
Council Member Chad Lamb                    Yes
Council Member Chris McConnehey             Yes
Council Member David Pack                   Yes
Council Member Kayleen Whitelock            Yes
Council Member Melissa Worthen              absent
The motion passed 6-0

Those present during the closed session included Board Members Kelvin Green, Chad Lamb,
Christopher McConnehey, David Pack, Kayleen Whitelock (via Zoom), and Chair Zach Jacob, Mayor Dirk
Burton, City Administrator Korban Lee, City Attorney Rob Wall, Economic Development Director Chris
Pengra, Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson, Council Office Clerk Cindy Quick, and Adam Long.

Board Members discussed the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property as follows:
Item #1: 8:13 pm to 8:37 pm

3. ADJOURN
Chair Zach Jacob moved to adjourn the closed session and Board Member Kelvin Green seconded the
motion. All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously 6-0.
