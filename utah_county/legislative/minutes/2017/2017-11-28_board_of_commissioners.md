                                         REGULAR AGENDA
4.     APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE AN AGREEMENT AND ASSOCIATED APPENDIX/TASK ORDER
       ATTACHMENTS WITH TEU SERVICES, INC. TO PROVIDE LED LIGHTING INSTALLATION
       AND MAINTENANCE SERVICES
(Track A, 04:30)
Utah County Deputy Attorney Benjamin C. Van Noy mentioned there are contractual issues with the proposal from
TEU Services, Inc., including who maintains possession of the bulbs. Per the current agreement, the vendor would
maintain possession of the bulbs; this causes potential legal issues for the county. Mr. Van Noy would also prefer
choice of law be changed from Texas to Utah in the event litigation is needed. Mr. Van Noy mentioned procurement
rules, and Commissioner Graves asked about the ‘Request for Proposals’ (RFP) process – he is skeptical of the
availability of other companies to offer the same service at no cost. Utah County Purchasing Manager Robert Baxter
noted there are anticipated costs of $10,000 monthly, and Commissioner Graves pointed out that would be paid
through the savings of the product. Mr. Baxter noted an RFP should still be completed, and the commissioners
discussed potential savings and ownership of bulbs with Mr. Van Noy. Commissioner Graves asked Mr. Baxter to
create an RFP by the end of December.

            Commissioner Graves made the motion to strike Regular Agenda Item No. 4,
            with the note that an RFP for these services should be produced by December 31,
            2017. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Ivie and carried with the

                                           PUBLIC COMMENTS
(Track A, 15:30)
Commissioner Graves took a moment of personal privilege to comment on the Utah County Human Resources Office.
He stated it is unacceptable that Human Resources is not making it a high priority to fully pay all county employees
on time; the ten elected officials have still not received their full pay from the previous week’s pay day, and there are
potential serious legal consequences. Commissioner Lee mentioned he was one of those impacted and Human
Resources is working on a solution; Commissioner Graves responded there are alternate options for the interim.
