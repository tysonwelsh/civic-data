COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER SAID SHE RECEIVED A LOT OF EMAILS AND
FEEDBACK ON THIS AND DOES NOT WANT TO DISCOUNT ANYONE'S VOICE. SHE
READ A STATEMENT THAT SHE HAD BEEN WORKING ON WITH THE SHERIFF.

WE BELIEVE IN BUILDING A COMMUNITY WHERE EVERY PERSON, NO MATTER WHERE
THEY COME FROM, CAN LIVE, WORK, PLAY, AND RAISE A FAMILY IN SAFETY AND DIGNITY.
THAT BELIEF GUIDED EVERY STEP OF OUR DECISION-MAKING PROCESS. IN THESE
CONTRACTS, WE DID NOT SIMPLY AGREE TO A BOILERPLATE AGREEMENT. WE
APPROACHED THIS WITH PRECISION, COMPASSION, AND CLEAREYED UNDERSTANDING OF
OUR OBLIGATIONS. OUR COMMITMENT IS TO PUBLIC SAFETY AND NOT FEAR. THAT'S WHY
WE MADE IT EXPLICITLY CLEAR. UTAH COUNTY WILL NOT PARTICIPATE IN LABOR RAIDS
OR IMMIGRATION RAIDS. PERIOD. WHILE THERE MAY BE ELEMENTS THAT ARE OUT OF OUR
CONTROL, WE WILL CONTINUE TO ADHERE TO THE PRINCIPLES THAT MAKE UTAH COUNTY
A WELCOMING PLACE FOR ALL WHO LIVE HERE AND WHO ARE OBEYING OUR LAWS. WE
WILL WORK WITH FEDERAL PARTNERS ONLY IN CASES WHERE IMMIGRANTS HAVE
COMMITTED CRIMES THAT THREATEN THE SAFETY OF OUR RESIDENTS. CRIMES AGAINST
PEOPLE. WE'VE SEEN THE REAL DANGER THAT ORGANIZED CRIMINAL NETWORKS CAN
POSE. A KNOWN HITMAN FOR MS-13 WAS ARRESTED IN OUR OWN COUNTY. WE'VE DEALT
WITH ORGANIZED THEFT RINGS. OUR RESPONSIBILITY IS TO PROTECT OUR COMMUNITIES
FROM VIOLENCE AND CRIMINAL EXPLOITATION. AT THE SAME TIME, WE FULLY RECOGNIZE
AND DEEPLY RESPECT THAT OUR ECONOMY DEPENDS ON THE CONTRIBUTIONS OF
HARDWORKING IMMIGRANTS. OUR FARMS, CONSTRUCTION SITES, HOTELS, AND
RESTAURANTS THRIVE BECAUSE OF THEM. THEY ARE OUR NEIGHBORS, AND THEIR
CHILDREN GO TO SCHOOL WITH OURS. THEY DESERVE TO FEEL SAFE AND SUPPORTED AND
NOT TARGETED OR AFRAID. WE DON'T WANT CHILDREN SCARED TO GO TO SCHOOL OR
PARENTS TERRIFIED TO GO TO WORK. THAT IS NOT WHO WE ARE AND THAT'S NOT THE
KIND OF COUNTY WE WANT TO BE. OUR APPROACH IS GROUNDED IN PRINCIPLE, LED BY
DATA, AND DRIVEN BY HUMANITY. WE ARE UPHOLDING THE LAW, PROTECTING OUR
PEOPLE, AND STANDING FIRM IN OUR VALUES. BECAUSE IN UTAH COUNTY, SAFETY AND
COMPASSION ARE NOT OPPOSING FORCES. THEY ARE TWIN PILLARS OF A STRONG AND
DECENT COMMUNITY.

SHE STATED THAT UTAH COUNTY WILL NOT PUT UP WITH CARTELS,
ORGANIZED RINGS, AND THEFT RINGS. THESE AGREEMENTS WITH ICE WILL
GIVE THE COUNTY THE TRANSPARENCY THAT THEY NEED.
