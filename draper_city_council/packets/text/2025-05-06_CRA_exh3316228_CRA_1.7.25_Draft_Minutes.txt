      2.b   CRA Resolution #25-01 between the Draper City Community
            Reinvestment Agency and the Clark and Christine Ivory
            Foundation (CCIF) regarding a workforce housing agreement



Community Reinvestment Agency DRAFT Meeting Minutes – January 7, 2025
            Assistant City Manager Kellie Challburg explained that with Resolution
            #25-01, Draper City would contribute $100,000 of CRA funds required
            to be spent on affordable housing, and the CCIF would sell seven units
            to Draper City and Canyon School District employees. Ms. Challburg said
            Phase 7 units would be ready in April 2025, and Phase 10 would be
            ready around December 2025. All other units in the neighborhood
            would be rental units. Most units would have 2-3 bedrooms, with a
            fourth bedroom option available. Qualifying employees would fit within
            a range of 80%-120% of Area Median Income (AMI). Units would be
            deed-restricted for 50 years, with appreciation capped at 4% annually,
            and the City would have the opportunity to buy back a unit if the
            employee wanted to sell.

            Board member T. Lowery said she was glad to see the opportunity come
            to fruition. Board member F. Lowry said his only wish would be to have
            more than seven units available. Chair Walker commended the CCIF for
            making the opportunity possible.

            Board member Green moved to approve CRA Resolution #25-01.
            Board member T. Lowery seconded the motion.

            Ashley Hatfield, representing CCIF, said City staff had been wonderful to
            work with, and said CCIF would love to do more within the community.

            A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
