Convene as the Board of Canvassers (4:40 PM)
Canice Harte made a motion to convene as the Board of Canvassers. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Chris Robinson, Roger Armstrong.
1)
Certify the election results are true, accurate, and final, for the General Election Canvass; Eve Furse (4:42 PM)
Eve Furse, Summit County Clerk, presented the ballot summary sheet and provided information on the number of ballots counted and not counted.
Council Chair Stevens asked a clarifying question about the ballot curing process to which Clerk Furse responded.
Clerk Furse presented the statistics prepared for the Lieutenant Governor’s Office, the Summary Results Report as well as the Precinct Table Report.
Council Members asked clarifying questions and provided comments for discussion.
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to certify the election results as true, accurate, and final for the General Election Canvass 2024 Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Chris Robinson, Roger Armstrong.
Attachment: Canvass Resolution 2024 General
Adjournment (5:06 PM)
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to adjourn. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Chris Robinson, Roger Armstrong.
