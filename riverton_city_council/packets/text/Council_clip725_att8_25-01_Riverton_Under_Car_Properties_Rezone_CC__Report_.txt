On December 12, 2024, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this
application. Minutes from that meeting are included below. The Planning Commission voted
to recommend approval, with the following recommended motion:

I move the City Council ADOPT Ordinance #25-01, “Riverton Under Car Properties Rezone,”
rezoning a 0.88-acre parcel located at 2284 West 12600 from RR-22 to C-G (Gateway Commercial).

Background


This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Thom Jakab, for two
parcels totaling about 0.88-acres located near 2284 West 12600 South. These properties are
on the north side of 12600 South Street just west of the existing Master Autotech building.
The property is currently zoned RR-22 which is a single-family residential zone with minimum
lot size of 1/2-acre, the requested zone is C-G which is Gateway Commercial. The General
Plan designation for this property is Business Park. To the north of the subject property is the
Country View PUD, which is a mix of single family and townhome units.

The subject property is made of two (2) rectangular parcels. The east parcel has a small
redbrick home, the western parcel has no structures. Both parcels have been used as a
vehicle storage and RV parking (as can be seen from the aerial images). The city has received
complaints about the parking and RV storage on a residential property. This rezone would only
resolve the zoning, the applicant would also need to get approval for a commercial site plan for
the property.

The subject property is zoned RR-22, a hold-over from long ago. The properties to the east
and west are zoned commercial and have commercial uses. Property to the east is zoned C-G.
Property to the west is zoned C-PO (Commercial Professional Office). The General Plan for the
north side of 12600 South, from the canal to 2700 West is Business Park which corresponds
with both C-G, and C-PO zoning.

The Planning Commission, in reviewing this application, forwarded it to the Council with a
recommendation for approval. Staff concurs with that recommendation.

2.    PUBLIC HEARINGS

      A.     “RIVERTON UNDER CAR PROPERTIES LLC,” PLZ-24-4009, AN
             APPLICATION FOR A REZONE FOR PROPERTY AT 2302 AND 2314
             WEST 12600 SOUTH FROM RR-22 (RESIDENTIAL, 0.5-ACRE LOTS) TO
             C-G (GATEWAY COMMERCIAL) FOR FUTURE COMMERCIAL
             DEVELOPMENT. APPLICANT – THOM JAKAB

City Planner, Tim Prestwich presented the Staff Report and reviewed aerial and zoning
maps of the subject parcels. He then discussed the history of the area. RR-22 is one of
the oldest zones in the City. Some properties in the area have since converted to
commercial, and the owner requested that the two subject parcels also be rezoned to
commercial. The General Plan designates the entire block from 2700 West to the canal
as Business Park, which corresponds with the Commercial Gateway (“C-G”), Commercial
Neighborhood (“C-N”), and Commercial Professional Office (“C-PO”) zones. All three
zones are present on this block. Master AutoTech, which adjoins the subject parcels and
is also owned by the Applicant, is zoned C-G. There is an existing fence between the
parcels and Country View subdivision, and no additional fencing was required.

Mr. Prestwich reported that recreational vehicles are currently parked on the properties
and Code Enforcement has been involved. If rezoning were approved, a Commercial
Site Plan would be required, and the issue would be rectified at that time. He noted that
the rezoning was in alignment with the General Plan and neighborhood.

Mr. Prestwich presented the Staff Report and reported that the subject property is on
South Redwood Road near 11800 South. The developer of Sycamore Glen acquired this
parcel from the Utah Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) and was requesting
rezoning to incorporate it into the project. Two five-plex townhome buildings were
approved for the adjoining property, and the applicant’s stated intention was to build the
same type of units on this property.

Notices were sent to all property owners within 1,000 feet of this site and posted on the
City’s webpage. One comment was received expressing concern about traffic impacts.

Aerial, Zoning, and General Plan maps were reviewed. Mr. Prestwich noted that the
subject parcel is currently zoned RR-22. The adjacent Sycamore Glen subdivision is an
S-D zone, and one special designation was to allow some smaller, single-family lots along
with the two five-plex townhome buildings. The applicant requested rezoning to allow two
more five-plex buildings on the subject parcel. The base zoning on the other parcel was
RM-6, but that zone no longer allows multifamily attached housing, so the request was to
rezone the parcel to RM-14-SD. The General Plan designated the parcel as Professional
Office, so a General Plan Amendment to High-Density Residential was also required.
