    MINUTES OF WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING
                            OCTOBER 15, 2025

THE WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION MET FOR STUDY SESSION ON
WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 15, 2025 AT 3:30 PM IN WEST VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600
CONSTITUTION BLVD, SUITE 220, WEST VALLEY CITY, UTAH. THE MEETING WAS
CALLED TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY CHAIR MATHEW LOVATO.
THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS WERE PRESENT:
     Mathew Lovato, Chair
     Martell Winters, Vice Chair
     Harold Woodruff, Commissioner
     Darrick Porter, Commissioner
     Nancy Drozdek, Commissioner
     Pauline Matagi, Commissioner

PLANNING DIVISION STAFF PRESENT:
     Steve Lehman, Planning Manager
     Jody Knapp, Zoning Administrator
     Brock Anderson, P&Z Senior Planner
     Julie Davis, P&Z Administrative Assistant

PRESENTATIONS:

GPZ- 4- 2025 Petition by Eloy Santana Perez requesting a General Plan change from Rural
Residential to Light Manufacturing and a zone change from A-1 (Agriculture, minimum lot size
1 acre) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 2720 S 6750 W on 1.09 acres. (Staff –
Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)
Continued from October 8, 2025 Public Hearing.

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented additional history and photos of the property as requested
by the Commission during the Continuance. The aerials show an increase in semitrucks and
small structures from 2017 to 2025. This overall area has been the subject of extensive Code
Enforcement actions and abatements. Zoning was also discussed outlining the more rural and
residential uses throughout this area as well as the regional wetland Park.

C-18-2025 Petition by Hale Center Theatre requesting conditional use approval for a
warehouse expansion with outside storage. The property is located at 2200 West Alexander St.
on 1.71 acres (.46 outside storage), and is zoned M, Manufacturing. (Staff – Jody Knapp at
801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)
Discussion: Jody Knapp presented a petition where the applicant is requesting a 5600 square
foot warehouse expansion and pave a trailer parking storage yard located in a former canal
area. There are easements that go along the entire corridor, so they have the correct fire
access. If side screening is something that the Commission is concerned about, they could
require screening along the front sides. The applicants were present, however there weren’t
any questions or concerns.
C-19-2025 Petition by Boyd Enterprises requesting conditional use approval for outside
storage at the Presidential Business Center, Phase 4. The property is located at 2195 S.
Presidents Dr. on 10.03 acres (.37 outside storage), and is zoned M, Manufacturing. (Staff –
Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)
Discussion: Jody Knapp presented a conditional use application for a trucking business. The
owner wants to maintain a slatted chain-link fence storage area in the rear parking lot for future
tenants' equipment and materials. The storage area is surrounded by Presidential Business
Park, with no other similar conditional use permits in the surrounding area. While the height of
stored equipment was considered, it was noted that semi-truck parking could mitigate any
screening concerns.

Commissioner Matagi asked what is being stored in the storage area. Jody responded that its
use is left open because they don’t currently have a tenant. Most likely it would be used for
rolling stock.


There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting
was adjourned at 3:50 PM.
