                                    SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL
                                          MINUTES
                                           Monday, October 5, 2020
                                          Approved October 14, 2020

The following are the minutes of the Special City Council Electronic Meeting of the Herriman City Council.
The meeting was held on Monday, October 5, 2020 at 5:30 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Council
Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required
by law, was posted in the City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and
interested citizens.

Presiding: Mayor David Watts

Councilmembers Present: Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith.

Staff Present: Interim City Manager Wendy Thomas, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Police Chief Troy
Carr, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, City Attorney Chase Andrizzi.

5:30 PM - SPECIAL MEETING:
This meeting will also be conducted electronically at www.herriman.org/agendas-and-minutes

Mayor David Watts called the meeting to order at 5:34 p.m.

1. Closed Session
     1.1. The Herriman City Council may temporarily recess the City Council meeting to convene in a
          closed session to discuss pending or reasonably imminent litigation, as provided by Utah Code
          Annotated §52-4-205
Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn moved to temporarily recess the City Council meeting at 5:35 p.m. to convene in a closed session to
discuss pending or reasonably imminent litigation, as provided by Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-205. Councilmember Steven
Shields seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
October 5, 2020 – Special City Council Minutes                                        Page 2 of 2
Mayor Watts             Aye
Councilmember Smith     Aye
Councilmember Shields   Aye
Councilmember Ohrn      Aye
Councilmember Henderson Aye

The motion passed unanimously.

The Council reconvened the meeting at 6:57 p.m.

2. Adjournment
Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn moved to adjourn the special meeting at 6:57 p.m. Councilmember Jared Henderson seconded the
motion and all voted aye.


I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a
true, accurate and complete record of the meeting held on October 5, 2020. This document constitutes the
official minutes for the Special Herriman City Council Meeting.




Jackie Nostrom, MMC
