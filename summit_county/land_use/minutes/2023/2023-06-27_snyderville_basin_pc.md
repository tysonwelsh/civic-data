Public hearing and action regarding a Low Impact Permit for a ~9,200 sq. ft. Owner: Beehive Court LLC (Seth and Jennifer Lansky). Administrative review. Project # 23-028
Amir Caus, Senior County Planner.  (6:10 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Lansky Pickle Ball Courts LIP Staff Report.pdf
Chair Carmichael corrected the Staff Report’s Criteria #11 to say "meets the minimum criteria for approval." (6:10 PM)
Amir Caus, Senior Planner, gave background on the application. (6:12 PM)
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the hearing for public input. (6:14 PM)
Sean Luebbers commented. (6:15 PM)
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the hearing for public input. (6:21 PM)
Commission Members asked questions. Mr. Caus responded. Commissioners then provided their input on the proposal. Lynda Viti, Deputy Attorney, responded to Commissioners’ questions. Seth Lansky, applicant, provided some additional background on intended uses. (6:24 PM)
The applicant’s representative, Sean Steinman, answered questions. (6:37 PM)
Christopher Conabee made a motion to recommend for approval a Low Impact Permit for a ~9,200 sq. ft. Recreation and athletic facilities, private use facility, located at 1201 Beehive Dr, Silver Creek Estates, Summit County, UT; Parcel SL-D-241 (2.43 acres) according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval included in the staff report with the additional condition of a prohibition on street parking for users of the facility and a reduction of the parking spots to 6 stalls, subject to the elimination of the turn around, and encouraging the applicant to use pervious surfaces where possible.. Tyann Mooney seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
