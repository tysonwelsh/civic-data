City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On June 20, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1585, adopting the
Budget including Compensation Schedules and adopting the Certified Tax Rate for
Fiscal Year 2023-2024. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s
Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 22nd day of June 2023. Laura
Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
