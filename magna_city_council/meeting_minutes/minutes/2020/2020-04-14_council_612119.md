Council Business

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

                Council Member Peel stated WFWRD curbside pickup is going to continue, but
area cleanup has been postponed indefinitely. A number of drivers are out, but WFWRD is still
covering its routes.

              Council Member Peay stated dumping has been happening three or four times a
week at Pleasant Green Park. He asked how that was being taken care of.

                Council Member Peel stated people could reach out to WFWRD to let it know
about the dumping and it would get a driver over there. The County’s Public Works Department
will pick up some of that stuff too. WFWRD is extending its voucher program, and if people would
take advantage of that instead of dumping, it would be much more beneficial. Vouchers are now
available at the Webster Center.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA) and Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                Council Member Hull stated the UFSA agreement was finalized, and everyone
signed off on it. Riverton City has requested to pull out of the UFSA, because it does not want to
be involved in a new bond for the new fire station. It will still continue as a member of the UFA.
The UFA is also working on its budget now for next year.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Magna Mosquito Abatement District

              Council Member Pierce stated some new legislation will require additional social
media or information to go out before the Mosquito Abatement District can spray. That is still
being worked out. Currently, the Mosquito Abatement District plans to spray in July, but that may
or may not happen. It is waiting to see how the legislation and the COVID virus play out.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

                  Mayor Peay stated the GSLMSD primarily covered the earthquake and the COVID
virus at its last meeting. At this point, things are at a standstill.

Resolution 20-04-01

               Paul Ashton reviewed Resolution 20-04-01 extending the State of Emergency
Declaration for COVID-19 Novel Coronavirus until June 30, 2020, to bring it more in line with what
the Legislature and the County have done.

RESOLUTION NO. 20-04-01                                                      Date: April 14, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL EXENDING
       THE STATE OF EMERGENCY DECLARATION FOR COVID-19 NOVEL
       CORONAVIRUS UNTIL JUNE 30, 2020

      WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
governing body for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501; and

      WHEREAS, Following the actions taken by Federal, State, and County Officials declaring
a 30-Day “State of Emergency” due to the infectious disease COVID-19 Novel Coronavirus
pandemic, the Mayor signed Magna’s 30-Day State of Emergency declaration on March 16, 2020;
and

       WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code § 53-2A-208, a Declaration of a State of Emergency
can only be extended past 30 days by a resolution of the governing body; and

      WHEREAS, On March 12, 2020 the Utah State Legislature passed HJR 24 extending the
Governors Declaration of a State of Emergency for COVID-19 until June 30, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, On April 7, 2020 the Salt Lake County Council extended the County Mayor’s
Declaration until July 6, 2020; and

     WHEREAS, Following the State’s lead and extending Magna’s State of Emergency for
COVID-19 to June 30, 2020 will avoid possible disruptions in service and relief efforts.

       NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved, by the Magna Metro Township Council, the State of
Emergency declared by the Mayor on March 16, 2020, due to the infectious disease COVID-19
Novel Coronavirus, is extended until June 30, 2020.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE MAGNA COUNCIL April 14, 2020.

Resolution 20-04-02

               Paul Ashton reviewed Resolution 20-04-02 extending the State of Emergency
Declaration for the March 18, 2020, Magna Earthquake until June 30, 2020.

RESOLUTION NO. 20-04-02                                                 Date: April 14, 2020

        03-18-2020 MAGNA EARTHQUAKE STATE OF EMERGENCY EXTENSION

        A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGAN METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL EXTENDING
        THE STATE OF EMERGENCY DECLARATION FOR THE 03-18-2020 MAGNA
        EARTHQUAKE UNTIL JUNE 30, 2020

      WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
governing body for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501; and

       WHEREAS, At 7:09 am on March 18, 2020, an earthquake measuring 5.7 on the Richter
scale occurred in Magna; and

       WHEREAS, Magna declared a “State of Emergency” based on the damages observed
from the effects of the earthquake; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County and the State also declared a “State of Emergency” for the
earthquake; and

       WHEREAS, On the afternoon of March 18, 2020 Magna began collecting information
regarding the damages to property; and

      WHERAS, Due to COVID-19, the State of Utah requested a 30-day extension from FEMA
on March 20, 2020 to collect and assess the damages from the earthquake; and

       WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code § 53-2A-208, a Declaration of a State of Emergency
can only be extended past 30 days by a resolution of the governing body; and

Resolution 20-04-03

                 Paul Ashton reviewed Resolution 20-04-03 recognizing and honoring the service
of the firefighters and police officers of the UFA and UPD during the COVID-19 and March 18,
2020, Magna Earthquake Emergencies.

RESOLUTION NO. 20-04-03                                                     Date: April 14, 2020

                            FIRST RESPONDER RECOGNITION

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       RECOGNIZING AND HONORING THE SERVICE OF THE FIREFIGHTERS AND
       POLICE OFFICERS OF THE UFA AND UPD DURING THE COVID-19 AND
       MARCH 18, 2020 MAGNA EARTHQUAKE EMERGENCIES

       WHEREAS, the Unified Fire Authority (“UFA”) is an Interlocal Cooperation Agency that
provides fire protection services to Magna; and

        WHEREAS, the Unified Police Department (“UPD”) is an Interlocal Cooperation Agency
that provides police services to Magna; and

       WHEREAS, on March 6, 2020 the Salt Lake County Mayor signed a “State of Emergency”
declaration due to the COVID-19 pandemic, thereby activating both UFA and UPD to conduct
event response to the emergency; and

        WHEREAS, on March 16, 2020 the Mayor signed a “State of Emergency” declaration as
a result of the declarations made by the County, State and Federal Government; and

                                               5
DATE           TUESDAY                                  APRIL                             14, 2020


        WHEREAS, on March 18, 2020 at 7:09 AM Magna experienced an earthquake measuring
5.7 on the Richter scale; and

       WHEREAS, immediately following the earthquake, a number of the officers of the UPD
deployed to Magna Main Street and began work to protect citizens from the falling debris and
unsafe conditions created by the earthquake; and

        WHEREAS, shortly behind the UPD, UFA’s Heavy Rescue and other emergency teams
arrived on site to work alongside the UPD to protect the impacted residents who lived in the Magna
Main Street Area; and

        WHEREAS, due to a toxic spill event that was happening near the Kennecott Smelter due
to the earthquake, both agencies had the added task of planning for a possible mass evacuation
of Magna that fortunately never came to pass; and

       WHEREAS, for the next three days, to protect the people and properties along Magna
Main Street, UPD maintained a 24-Hour a Day physical presence; and

       WHEREAS, during the heightened response to Magna’s time of need, the entities served
by the UFA and UPD saw no decrease in service response; and

       WHEREAS, though the earthquake event need has subsided, the COVID-19 event
continues to require a heightened response from both agencies; and

      WHEREAS, the exemplary service of the men and women of the UFA and UPD reflects
honorably upon them, the agencies, and the Magna Metro Township;

        NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Magna Metro Township Council that Magna
recognized and honors the firefighters and police officers of the Unified Fire Authority and Unified
Police Department for their exemplary service protecting the people of Magna during the ongoing
COVID-19 and Magna Earthquake emergency events; and be it further RESOLVED, a copy of
this resolution shall be provided to the UFA and UPD as a communications of Magna’s thanks for
their service in this time of community emergency.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE MAGNA COUNCIL April 14, 2020.
