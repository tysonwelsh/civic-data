City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1634

On February 18, 2025, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1634 providing
a one-time incentive payment to the Mayor in recognition of his outstanding service
and extensive involvement on various boards and commissions during the 2024-2025
period. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 18th day of February 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
