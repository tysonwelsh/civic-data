RESOLUTION 11-18-2025 – MASTER FEE RESOLUTION
      The rate stabilization fund would enable the city to take advantage of lower, but riskier, rates
      while still protecting residents from wild rate fluctuations that can occur in the market. UMPA
      has established a similar fund for electric rates.
      The city has a new hedge fund rate and would like to establish a small adjustment in the overall
      rate to begin funding the rate stabilization fund. The proposal is to adjust the base rates for
      General Service and Rural Service (both of whom benefit from hedged rates) by $0.03 per ccf
      (100 cubic feet. This rate assumes that $300,000 in the current natural gas fund balance would
      be dedicated to the rate stabilization fund. As the fund earns interest and begins to approach an
      amount that can be comfortably used in rate stabilization, the city can look to reduce this
      adjustment. Customers should still see a reduction in their bills this coming year due to a lower
      hedge rate.
      Council member Skip Worwood moved to approve Resolution 11-18-2025 – Master Fee
      Resolution. Council member Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed
      unanimously.
