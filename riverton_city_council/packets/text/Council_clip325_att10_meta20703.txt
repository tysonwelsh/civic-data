FROM:          Trace Robinson, Public Works Director

ITEM:          Approval of Cooperative Maintenance Agreement between the Utah Department
               of Transportation (UDOT) and the City of Riverton for the transfer of ownership
               and maintenance responsibilities from UDOT to the City of Riverton for the
               stormwater detention pond located at 12891 S and Redwood Road.
               Furthermore, UDOT will retain ownership and maintenance responsibilities for
               the detention pond located at 13571 S Redwood Road.

SUMMARY:

As part of the construction of the Redwood Road Widening Project, UDOT was required to build
two detention ponds to manage runoff. The City felt that the pond located at 12891 S Redwood
Road could be combined with adjacent properties and benefit the City for other uses. City Staff
worked with UDOT to provide an agreement that enables the transfer of ownership of the property
to the City. Execution of the agreement will require the City to maintain the physical appearance
and stormwater requirements for which the pond was constructed. In addition, City Staff felt there
was no benefit for the City to own and maintain the pond located at 13571 S and Redwood Road.
This pond will remain in the ownership of UDOT as well as all maintenance obligations.

        WHEREAS, the Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) has completed the construction
to widen Redwood Road from 12600 South to Bangerter Highway; and

       WHEREAS, as a part of the construction, UDOT provided two detention ponds to manage
stormwater runoff, the location of which are 12891 S Redwood Road and 13571 S Redwood Road; and

        WHEREAS, The City of Riverton sees a benefit to the City of owning and maintaining the pond
located at 12891 S Redwood Road; and

        WHEREAS, UDOT wishes to convey ownership and maintenance responsibilities of the said
pond to the City of Riverton; and

         WHEREAS The City of Riverton sees no beneficial use or has no desire to own or maintain the
detention pond located at 13571 S Redwood Road; and

        WHEREAS, UDOT has agreed to keep ownership and maintenance for the detention pond
located at 13571 S Redwood Road; and

        WHEREAS, execution of this Cooperative Agreement outlines the ownership and maintenance
responsibilities of both parties; and

      NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF RIVERTON
CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby approves Cooperative Agreement with UDOT which
          outlines ownership and maintenance responsibilities of both parties. Said Agreement, is
          attached hereto as Exhibit “A”.
       2. The Mayor is authorized to sign the approved Cooperative Agreement with UDOT.
       3. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

                COOPERATIVE MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT
       THIS COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT, made and entered into this                         day of
           , 2019, by and between the UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION, a state
agency, (“UDOT”) and RIVERTON CITY, a political subdivision of the State of Utah, (“City”).

                                          RECITALS

             WHEREAS, UDOT, as part of its project, S-0068(95)4; SR-68: Bangerter Hwy to
       12600 S. (“Project”), has constructed storm water detention ponds located at 12891 South
       Redwood Road and 13571 South Redwood Road; and

               WHEREAS, the City and UDOT desire to identify ownership, and maintenance of
       the constructed storm water detention ponds.

                                         AGREEMENT

      NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

       1. The City will own and maintain the storm water detention pond at 12891 South and
          Redwood Road, which will receive storm water runoff from both UDOT and the City.

       2. UDOT will own and maintain the storm water detention pond at 13571 South and
          Redwood Road, which will receive storm water runoff from both UDOT and the City.

       3. UDOT and City will comply with the required storm water permits, applicable laws, and
          regulations for the storm water detention ponds they own.

       4. The Parties shall complete inspections on any long-term storm water features using
          UDOT’s inspection form, as found on UDOT’s Long-term Storm water Management
          webpage https://www.udot.utah.gov/main/f?p=100:pg:0:::1:T,V:2652. The parties shall
          execute inspections according to the frequencies outlined on the inspection form.

       5. The terms of this Agreement shall continue as long as the Parties own and maintain the
          described storm water detention ponds.

       6. This Cooperative Agreement may not be assigned.
