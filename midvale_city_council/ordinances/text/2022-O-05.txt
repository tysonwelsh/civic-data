ORDINANCE NO. 2022-0-05
AN ORDINANCE REZONING APPROXIMATELY 0.17 ACRES
LOCATED AT 170 E 7200 S FROM THE SINGLE-FAMILY
RESIDENTIAL ZONE/DUPLEX OVERLAY/7200 S OVERLAY (SF-
1/DO/7200 S) TO THE MIXED USE/7200 S OVERLAY (MU/7200 S).

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Annotated Sections 10-9a-501 through 10-9a-503,
Midvale City (“the City”) has authority to make and amend a zoning plan which divides the City
into zoning districts and within those districts to regulate the erection, construction,
reconstruction, alteration, and uses of buildings and structures and the uses of land; and

WHEREAS, a request has been made for a change of zoning on the property described
in Exhibit A; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission held a public hearing on January 26, 2022, to
review the request for rezone and, after considering all of the information received, made a
recommendation to approve the rezone request thereon to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, the City Council of Midvale City, Utah held a public hearing on February
15, 2022; and

WHEREAS, after taking into consideration citizen testimony, planning and demographic
data, the desires of the owners of the property, and the Planning Commission’s recommendation
as part of its deliberations, the City Council determined the following:

1. The application is for a rezone to change from SF-1/DO/7200 S to MU/7200 S.

2. The subject property is on a total of 0.17 acres.

3. The rezone of the property will allow for light commercial mental health office.

4, The project complies with the rezone procedure outlined in Midvale City Code 17-3-

ICE.)

5. The applicable review departments have reviewed the project and provided a positive
recommendation.

6. The Planning Commission has reviewed the project and has recommended approval to
the City Council.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Midvale City, Utah
as follows:

Section 1. The zoning ordinance, which sets forth the zone districts within Midvale City
which portion of the said zoning ordinance is established by a zoning map, is hereby amended as
follows:

The property (Parcel No. 22-30-152-014) described in Exhibit A attached hereto and by
Ordinance No. 2022-O0-05 Page | of 4

this reference made a part hereof, which properties are located at 170 E 7200 S, Midvale, Utah,
and are currently zoned Single-Family Residential/Duplex Overlay/7200 S Overlay (SF-
1/DO/7200 S), shall be zoned Mixed Use/7200 S Overlay (MU/7200 S).

ZONING PRIOR TO EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS ORDINANCE:

Single-Family Residential/Duplex Overlay/7200 S Overlay (SF-1/DO/7200 S)

ZONING AFTER EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS ORDINANCE:

Mixed Use/7200 S Overlay (MU/7200 S).

Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect immediately.
