2) Other Business Items. (As Needed)
Discussed changing the meeting dates to the second Thursday of each month. Commissioners
agreed to move the meetings beginning January 2025.
Ryan Leike provided an update to his project.
3) MSD Planning staff will continue the Title 19 Code Update Project, including a review of the
Foothill and Canyons Overlay Zone as well as Site Development Standards. Planner(s): Matt
Starley and Brian Tucker (Discussion)
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding the updates previously made.
Discussed 19.38.060 Slope Protection - Slopes, slope waiver, and slope waiver provisions and
protection standards, retaining walls and structural elements. Bonding, posting, Environmental Impact
Study and mitigation, site screening, and completion. Planning commission may impose reasonable
conditions to mitigate the impacts and list what the conditions may be. Definition of what development
is and remove the word activities throughout.
Discussed 19.38.070 Grading Standards - Separate building pads for accessory buildings, change
international building code to current applicable building code, change the word “emulate” to
“replicate” a naturally formed depression, foundation wall versus retaining wall, foundation condition.
Staff to review Brighton’s language on retaining and foundation walls.
Discussed 19.38.080 Site Access – Change “city highway ordinance” to “Requirements of Title 14 and
Title 18”, adding Design and construction of streets. Find appropriate reference to fire authority and
change name of the regulation. In all crossings, remove the word eliminate. Confirm appropriate
definition to “private access road” and add to definitions. Change “Public Works Engineer” to “City
Engineer”.
Commissioner Harpst adjourned.
