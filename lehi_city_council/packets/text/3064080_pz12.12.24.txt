89   3.3) Public Hearing and recommendation of Kevin and Sarah Gordon’s request for review
90   of the Gordon Zone Change on 1.63-acres located at 860 North 2300 West changing the
91   zoning from A-1 (agriculture) to R-1-22 (residential/agriculture) and Neighborhood
92   Commercial.
93
94   Katie Bussell presented on this item. A previous zone change was approved on the condition that
95   only the proposed daycare would be placed within the zone. The necessary forms were provided
     Lehi City Planning Commission                    2                                 December 12, 2024
 96   by the applicant, but not until after the packet was created. The zone change is within the normal
 97   range. The DRC stated that the lot currently is storing trailers against code, and that those must
 98   be taken care of first.
 99
100   Commissioner Everett asked if the trailer enforcement concern had been taken care of. Katie
101   stated that it has not, but it is in progress. Commissioner Everett also asked about the status of
102   the daycare. Kim Struthers stated that it was still the only plan in place. Commissioner Eyre
103   asked if any other uses for the land were available. Struthers stated that it was only approved for
104   the planned daycare.
105
106   Commissioner Kunze asked if this is the same proposal that came before the Commission in
107   2022. Kim Struthers affirmed that it is. Commissioner Everett asked if there is a time frame on
108   the resolution of the code violation. Struthers stated that there is not a solid time frame yet, but
109   the applicants are working on it. The DRC comments recommend not rezoning until all the
110   trailers are moved.
111
112   Commissioner Eyre asked about the drive aisle, and if the driveway for the house and the
113   business are going to be shared access. Gary Ellis stated that it would be. Kevin and Sarah
114   Gordon, the applicants, were present. They believe that this is a better use of the land than solely
115   putting the daycare in place. The front of the lot will be dedicated to parking for the daycare, so
116   they don’t think the house’s driveway will be disturbed. Commissioner Eyre expressed concern
117   over drop-off and pick-up times. The Gordons explain that they intend on living in the house on
118   the property.
119
120   Commissioner Everett opened and closed public comment.
121
122   Commissioner Jones asked city staff if the trailers would still need to be moved before the zone
123   change happens if they provided a positive recommendation to the City Council. Kim Struthers
124   stated that if it is a requirement for the zone change, it will set a time frame, and that city staff is
125   comfortable with that. Commissioner Eyre asked staff if the property would require a right-in
126   right-out. Gary Ellis stated that Luke Seegmiller has been considering it, but there has not been a
127   decision yet. That is something that will be decided when a site plan is presented.
128
129   Commissioner Everett asked if there were any other properties with a shared driveway for a
130   home and a residential consumer business. Commissioner Kunze mentioned Hamilton Motors on
131   Main Street. Kim Struthers explained that the applicant would prefer to have two separate
132   driveways, but that Luke Seegmiller recommended one driveway to limit the number of tension
133   points on the street. The property is in a difficult position to connect, as it’s landlocked on three
134   sides.
135
136                   Motion: Commissioner Jones moved to give a positive recommendation to the
137                   City Council for the zone change, as the effect of the proposed amendment on the
138                   character of the surrounding area falls in line with the general plan, it will be
139                   consistent with the goals and policies of the general plan, as well as compatible
140                   with the proposed zones and land uses of nearby and adjoining properties, the
141                   suitability of the property for the uses of this land, and the overall community
142                   benefit of the proposed amendment. Kunze seconded. Commissioner Everett
143                   asked that the motion be amended to include all DRC comments, especially that it

152   3.4) Public Hearing and consideration of Kevin and Sarah Gordon’s request for approval
153   of a conditional use for a flag lot located at 860 North 2300 West.
154
155   Katie Bussell presented the item. The lot meets all size requirements for a flag lot. There are no
156   new driveways allowed on 2300 West at this time.
157
158   Commissioner Everett asked if the zoning is the only thing allowing this change to be made.
159   Gary Ellis said that it was, but if it wasn’t allowed due to zoning, there would be a way for the
160   applicants to request an exception.
161
162   Commissioner Eyre asked if it would be possible to move the recommended entrance to the
163   north. Kevin and Sarah Gordon, the applicants, were present. They provided an updated plan to
164   the Commission that had already moved the entrance from the south to the north.
165
166   Commissioner Everett opened and closed public comment.
167
168   Commissioner Everett suggested requiring the house to face away from the school. He suggested
169   requiring it to face to the west. Commissioners Eyre, Jones, and Lockhart all agreed that it made
170   the most sense with the orientation of the lot but felt uncomfortable about placing it as a
171   requirement to the applicant. They felt it was unnecessary.
172
173                   Motion: Commissioner Jones moved to approve with no conditions the proposed
174                   flag lot, as it meets the requirements for the R-1-22 zone and flag lot
175                   requirements, that the flag lot does not need conditions to mitigate against
176                   potential negative impacts, the proposed use will be located and conducted in a
177                   manner in compliance with the goals and policies of Lehi City General Plan and
178                   the purposes of the code. He included all DRC comments. Commissioner Eyre
179                   asked to stipulate that it is the updated plan with the northern entrance that is
180                   being approved. Commissioner Jones agreed. Commissioner Kunze asked to
181                   clarify that this is all dependent on item 3.3 passing. Commissioner Jones agreed.
182                   Commissioner Eyre seconded.
183
184                   Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes. Commissioner Lockhart, yes. Commissioner
185                   Eyre, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes. Commissioner Jones, yes.
186
187                   Motion passed unanimously.

189   3.5) Public Hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of the Public
190   Facility Rezone 2024 located at various locations.
191
      Lehi City Planning Commission                    4                                 December 12, 2024
192   Katie Bussell presented the item. The rezone is an effort to change all public property to the
193   Public Facility zoning designation. It was brought to the attention of the planners when they
194   noticed zoning irregularities in Family Park.
195
196   Commissioner Everett asked if the rezoning would require a General Plan Amendment. Katie
197   Bussell stated that it wouldn’t, as these are properties that just need to be redone. City staff had
198   no additional comments.
199
200   Commissioner Everett opened public comment.
201
202   Wayne Pearson came forward with questions about the rezoning. He lives close to the areas
203   being rezoned and wanted to know why the zone change was happening. Katie Bussell explained
204   that Family Park and the city cemetery are being rezoned to add a layer of protection and prevent
205   additional development from coming in. Kim Struthers added that the purpose is to recognize the
206   areas as public facilities. Pearson brought up a piece of property in front of his house that he’s
207   been trying to buy from the city for about 20 years. He’s been taking care of it, but it has never
208   been signed for. He wants to know if the zone change will affect that. Struthers pulled up the
209   map and determined that the area wouldn’t be affected by the zone change, and that the purchase
210   should still be possible. Pearson thanked the Commission and city staff for answering his
211   questions.
212
213   Bill Anderson asked if the zone change applied to private property. Commissioner Everett
214   explained that it will only affect properties already owned by the city. Anderson brought up a
215   note he got from the police department about a potential rezone. Kim Struthers explained that his
216   property will not be affected, he was just within the 300 foot radius of an adjacent property that
217   is being rezoned as a public facility. Katie Bussell clarified that no private property was being
218   rezoned at the meeting.
219
220   Commissioner Everett closed public comment.
221
222                   Motion: Commissioner Kunze moved to make a positive recommendation,
223                   finding that the effect of the proposed amendment on the character of the
224                   surrounding area is positive, that it is consistent with the goals and policies of the
225                   general plan, that it is consistent and compatible with the general plan land uses of
226                   nearby and adjoining properties, and that the suitability of the property of the land
227                   use is requested, and the suitability for the existing uses defined by the general
228                   plan are also positive. She included all DRC comments. Commissioner Lockhart
229                   seconded.
230
231                   Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes. Commissioner Lockhart, yes. Commissioner
232                   Eyre, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes. Commissioner Jones, yes.
233
234                   Motion passed unanimously.
