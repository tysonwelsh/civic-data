The purpose of the Joint Work Session was for the Council to receive annual training; discuss proposed street renaming —
Canyon Terrace Development, “1355 South” to “1425 South”; participate in continued discussion regarding Ogden School
District Properties; discuss proposal to create Gibson Community Reinvestment Area (CRA); Council/Board business; and hear
Council/Board comments.

Council Training

The City Council received presentations from various professionals regarding the following: Ethics/Conflicts; Open
Meetings; Cyber Security; Distracted Driving; Workplace Harassment; and Stormwater Pollution Prevention.

Proposed Street Renaming — Canyon Terrace Development, “1355
South” to "1425 South”

Planning Manager Brierley explained Ogden City Engineering requests a street name change from “1355 South” to
“1425 South” which is geographically more correct and identifies the street more with the existing Canyon Terrace development,
instead of the neighborhood street above it, 1350 South. He offered additional background and points of consideration:

e The Canyon Terrace Subdivision plat was recorded in 1985.

e =In March of 2022 Phase 2 was split into Phase 3 and 4.

e A building permit was issued, and construction began in September 2022.

e The proposed street name changes from “1355 South” to “1425 South” will tie in better with the Canyon
Terrace development.

e The street and buildings are currently under construction and there are no residents living on the street.

e The Police and Fire departments do not have a conflict with the proposed street rename.

Mr. Brierley concluded the Planning Commission has reviewed the proposal and forwarded a positive recommendation
to the Council. (  )

The Council then discussed various funding options that could be used to acquire the Grandview property; Council

property to open space, but to allow another buyer to acquire the property. Council member White stated she is undecided on the :
Grandview property; there is already a park near the Grandview site, and she would rather use the money that would be spent to
acquire the property to, instead, develop parks in other areas of the City where they are desperately needed.
Vice Chair Richey stated that it is important to recognize there is a great deal of public interest in this topic and he has
read every email he has received, and he thanked the senders for being involved and sharing their feelings. Chair Choberka agreed
and noted that she would like to pursue a broader study and plan for open space and parks needs in the community.
Proposed Gibson Community Reinvestment Area
Community and Economic Development Director Cooper explained City Administration proposes the establishment ofa
survey area for a potential Community Reinvestment Area (CRA) project area to be known as the Gibson CRA. He presented a
map to orient the Board to the general boundaries of the Gibson CRA between 12™ Street to 21 Street and between Wall Avenue
» and 750 West. Redevelopment Agency staff believe this area is a shoulder area to the Ogden River and is an area that will benefit
: from active reinvestment. Development objectives for the CRA include:
e Remove substandard buildings and encourage highest, best use of land.
e Eliminate vacant and underutilized land.
e Facilitate existing commercial expansion.
e Implement appropriate urban design principles where appropriate.
e Facilitate residential development that is complimentary to existing business and commercial areas.
e Upgrade infrastructure/streets.
e Strengthen tax base.
¢ Improve active transportation connections.
e Facilitate additional green/open space.
Mr. Cooper noted that eminent domain will not be used for the creation of the proposed CRA; the approval of a survey
area via resolution is the first step in creating a CRA. Once a survey area is established, Redevelopment Agency staff will develop
and present a draft plan and budget for the project area.
Board member Hyer wondered if the proposed boundary could be adjusted to include the 21* Street Pond; he referenced
past discussions about development concepts including the pond and suggested that inclusion in the Gibson CRA could be helpful
or could enhance any development concept. The Board supported the idea of adjusting the boundary as requested. The Board and
staff then discussed topics such as the population of the area included in the proposed CRA, existing land use and businesses in
the area, development currently underway in the project area and how such development will impact the taxable value of the area,
and the process/timing of necessary actions to create the CRA.
