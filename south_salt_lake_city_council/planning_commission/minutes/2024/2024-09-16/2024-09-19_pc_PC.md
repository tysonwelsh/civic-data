1.      PUBLIC MEETING
        An Application for a Preliminary Subdivision Plat Amendment to Consolidate Six (6)
        Parcels into One Lot. The Subject Property is located at 3535 South 500 West.

        ACTION ITEM
        Applicant: Daily’s Premium Meats, LLC.

City Planner, Jed Shum, presented the Staff Report and stated that this is a petition for approval of a
Preliminary Subdivision Plat Amendment to consolidate six unsubdivided parcels. The main purpose
is to provide parking by building a new parking lot on the southwest corner of the consolidated lot. The
South Salt Lake City Planning Commission Meeting                                               3

property is located at 3535 South 500 West and the zoning of the existing parcels is Flex. Large-scale
food processing is a permitted use in the zone. The Preliminary Plat was shared with the Commission.
Mr. Shum explained that this is an updated Preliminary Plat from the one in the Staff Report because
the one in the Staff Report was missing a few signature boxes for the utility company easements.

Images of the existing conditions were shared. Mr. Shum explained that the vacant area shown in the
photograph is where the new parking lot will be located. The Site Plan was reviewed. The proposed
parking lot will provide 51 total parking stalls, which will improve the parking situation on the site.
Some of the employees currently have to park at a separate location and walk over to the site. As for
the General Plan considerations, this application meets Economic Development Goal #3:

   •   Continue to support existing and future businesses through City partnership with the South Salt
       Lake Chamber of Commerce and a comprehensive business retention and recruitment program.
          o This plat application allows Daily’s Premium Meats to improve the current conditions
              of the business and to improve the employee parking situation.

Based on the information submitted in the application, supporting documentation, and analysis of the
relevant code sections and General Plan goals, Staff recommends the Planning Commission move to
approve the Preliminary Subdivision Plat Amendment to consolidate the six unsubdivided parcels
located at 3535 South 500 West, based on the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Conditions
of Approval for the subdivision that are listed in the Staff Report. The Planning Commission has the
option to approve, deny, or continue the application. The applicant is available to answer questions.
Commissioner Pechmann asked if there is any landscaping required by code. This was confirmed.

Chair Carter opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

Commissioner Holscher reported that she has been to this location before and parking has always been
an issue. She is pleased to see that something is being done to address the existing parking concerns.
