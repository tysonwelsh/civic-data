CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

OUTGOING CITY COUNCIL MEMBERS LARRY OSTLER AND NATHAN MEMMOTT HONORED:

   Mayor Seely read a list of past accomplishments and gave a tribute to Councilor Ostler and Councilor
Memmott who were vacating their city council seats. Councilor Memmott and Councilor Ostler both
were first elected to the council in 2016. . Mayor Seely expressed his appreciation to Councilor Ostler’s
for his knowledge and expertise on the utilities because of his experience of working for Nephi City’s gas
department. He said that Ostler had suffered many health challenges in the last few years, but seldom
missed a meeting. He stated that he appreciated Councilor Memmott for his efforts of studying and
researching the issues each week and adding his perspective to tough issues. Mayor Seely thanked both
councilors’ wives for their support of their husbands and said that it was an honor to serve each of
them. that he enjoyed working with them. Mayor Seely then presented them each with a plaque of
recognition and appreciation.
   Councilor Ostler said that he had enjoyed working with the Miss Nephi Pageant committee members
and director Jamie John. He thanked his wife and his sons for their support. He said he was proud of
the many projects that have been accomplished in the past eight years. He expressed his appreciation
for the people he had served with and said that he was glad for the experience.
   Councilor Memmott said that it had been a pleasure to serve on the council. He remembers in the
beginning of his term when he attended a land use training and with Councilor Ostler. He thanked the
council and said he appreciated Mayor Seely and his leadership, and he expressed his appreciation to
the staff . He stated that the City was in a good position to manage the growth that will happen in the
future. The council has had to work together, and even though the council members haven’t always
agreed, they were able to walk out the door friends. He said he appreciated his wife Stefanie for
encouraging and pushing him to run.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

 Juab School District Superintendent Kody Hughes expressed his appreciation to the council and staff for
their partnership with the school district. He said your kids are our kids and our kids are your kids. He
mentioned that he appreciated Councilor Memmott wanting to look at everything on the Hive project
from seven different angles. He said that Councilor Ostler had made him feel so welcome to the
community and even sent him a happy birthday text. He gave a Christmas gift to the council and staff.

Mr. Scott McKay representing the Juab/Millard Children’s Justice Center (CJC) asked for a financial
contribution to the CJC. He explained that the CJC is a calming facility which serves children and families
who are experiencing abuse. He stated that they are currently renting a building and now their goal is to
build their own building. The CJC is funded by a federal program that is experiencing funding cuts. The
State of Utah has tried to make up the difference and said he would appreciate if Nephi City would
consider kicking in money if they can.

Mr. Marty Barber introduced himself as the owner of Barco Construction, a company that has built
many of the steel buildings in the area. He said that he has recently built the Mad Dog Knives building
on the south end of the city. He said he has spent 1.2 million dollars on the project. He said that the
owners of Mad Dog Knives are not able to pay him for the work that has been completed and that he
thinks he can sell the building to Advantage Auto. He stated that he had closed the land deal that the
building is sitting on. He wanted to inform the council about what is happening with the project.

Mike Sperry said that moving forward there may be a need for a zone change on the property that Mr.
Barber was referencing.

Mr. John Phillipsen addressed the council and said that there had been a lot of thefts at his home and
the police department was not doing a good job investigating the theft. He stated that he had a Honda
generator stolen from his property. He referenced several incidents dating back ten to 15 years ago.

Mr. Travis Worwood thanked Councilor Memmott and Councilor Ostler for their service.

Mr. Phillipsen also thanked Ostler, Memmott and Mayor Seely for listening to him.

LOT 23 NORTH RIDGE PLAT B REAL ESTATE PURCHASE AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   The City Council approved an amendment to an original development agreement entered into with
Mt. Peak Development, developer of the North Ridge Estates in October of 2021. This allowed the city
to purchase a lot in the North Ridge Estates for the purposes of connecting future transportation
routes. This has been a goal of the City Council to maintain the grid transportation system as much as
possible as the city expands.

Plat B of the North Ridge Estates is completed, recorded, and lots are now for sale. The city has
proposed purchasing Lot 23, at market rates, of Plat B to connect transportation routes from
subdivisions to the south to the North Ridge Estates. The city has negotiated with the developer and
agreed upon a price to purchase Lot 23. The attached real estate purchase agreement (REPC) has been
drafted for this purpose.
