MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Nick Whittaker
Date: 2025-02-27
Re:

Public Hearing: Laura Hope Mason Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit Request
(Administrative item)



Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
for approximately 0.24 acres located on the east side of Tumbleweed Way at 14285 S.
Tumbleweed Way. The property is currently zoned RM (Multiple Dwelling Unit
Residential), and is subject to the South Mountain Development Agreement. The
applicant is requesting that a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved to
allow for a cottage business which creates and sells art.



Findings for approval:


  1. The proposal complies with the standards for approval found in DCMC Subsection
     9-5-080(E2-104(e) including the regulations in Section 9-4-104) of the 1999 DCMC,
     and potential negative impacts to adjacent properties and the public welfare are
     mitigated through the imposition of reasonable conditions necessary or desirable
     for the protection of the same.



Findings for denial:


  1. The proposal does not comply with one or more of the standards for approval
     found in Subsection 9-2-104(e) or the regulations of Section 9-4-104 of the 1999
     DCMC, and potential negative impacts to adjacent properties and the public welfare
     are not able to be mitigated through the imposition of reasonable conditions
     necessary or desirable for the protection of the same.
