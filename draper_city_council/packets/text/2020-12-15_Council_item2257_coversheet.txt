Re: Approval of Agreement # 20-180 Assessment in Lieu for Montague Residence Comments: In order to construct a residential dwelling on an existing parcel, Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) 10-3-020(C) requires the owner to construct public frontage improvements as a requirement of the building permit.  The process allows the owner to satisfy the requirement through the payment of an assessment in lieu of construction per DCMC 9-27-110(C).
The Montagues at 12838 S Boulter Street have constructed a new house and requested to pay the assessment in lieu of construction of the public frontage improvements.  There are no frontage improvements adjacent to the parcel on Boulter Street.  The City Engineer's construction cost estimate is $16,260.  Staff recommends approval of the assessment in lieu agreement to facilitate the future design and construction of Boulter Street by the city.
