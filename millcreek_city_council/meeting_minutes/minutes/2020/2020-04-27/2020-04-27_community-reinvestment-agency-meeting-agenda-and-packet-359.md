Minutes of the
Millcreek Community Reinvestment Agency
April 27, 2020
7:00 p.m.
Regular Meeting

The Community Reinvestment Agency of Millcreek, Utah, met in a regular public meeting on
April 27, 2020 at City Hall, located at 3330 S. 1300 E., Millcreek, UT 84106. The meeting was
conducted electronically per Millcreek State of Local Emergency Directive No. 2 of 2020 and
live streamed via the City’s website with an option for online public comment.

REGULAR MEETING — 7:00 p.m.
TIME COMMENCED: 8:27 p.m.

Chair Silvestrini called the meeting to order.

1. Presentation of Fiscal Year 2020-21 Community Reinvestment Agency Fund

Tentative Budget; Laurie Johnson, HR-Finance Director

Laurie Johnson said the budget had not changed from the Board’s meeting two weeks
ago. The budget included a loan from the West Millcreek Community Reinvestment
Area (CRA) to the Millcreek Center CRA in the amount of $721,800. The revenues
for both CRAs were just over $8.2 million. She said the $721,800 was reflected in the
debt interest in the expense budget for the Millcreek Center CRA. She said she would
update the tentative budget if new data came in before the final budget was adopted.

2. Discussion and Consideration of Resolution 20-03, Acknowledging Receipt of the
Community Reinvestment Agency (CRA) Tentative Budget on April 27, 2020;

and Tentatively Adopting the Tentative Budget for the Fiscal Year Beginning
July 1, 2020, and Ending June 30, 2021

Laurie Johnson asked the Board to approve the resolution. Chair Silvestrini asked for
public comment. There was none.

Board Member Jackson moved to adopt Resolution 20-03, Acknowledging Receipt of the
Community Reinvestment Agency (CRA) Tentative Budget on April 27, 2020; and
Tentatively Adopting the Tentative Budget for the Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2020, and
Ending June 30, 2021. Board Member Uipi seconded. The Recorder called for the vote. All
Board Members voted yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3. Approval of April 13, 2020 Meeting Minutes

Board Member Uipi moved to approve the minutes from April 13, 2020. Board Member
Jackson seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes. The
motion passed unanimously.

4. Closed Session per Utah Code Ann. §52-4-205(d) Strategy Sessions to Discuss the
Purchase, Exchange, or Lease of Real Property

Board Member Jackson moved to go into a closed session per Utah Code Ann. §52-4-205(d)
strategy sessions to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property at 8:31 p.m.
Board Member Uipi seconded. The Recorder called for the vote. All Board Members voted
yes. The motion passed unanimously.

Board Member Uipi moved to go back into the public meeting at 10:05 p.m. Board
Member Catten seconded. The Recorder called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes.
The motion passed unanimously.

ADJOURNED: Board Member Uipi moved to adjourn the meeting at 10:05 p.m. Board
Member Jackson seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted
yes. The motion passed unanimously.
