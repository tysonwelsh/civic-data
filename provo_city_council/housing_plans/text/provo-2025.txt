N
Provo plans to partner with the Provo City Housing Authority to build housing for City/District
Employees. Building is expected to begin in 2026 with completion by 2027. An active waitlist of
employees in the Police and Fire Departments was started in the Spring 2025.

P
Moderate Income Housing Set Aside for the Provo City Redevelopment Agency will be used in
the creation of workforce housing. This funding is expected to be utilized as collected. The
funding will be included in the Annual Report of the Redevelopment Agency and will be
identified in the the RDA financials as restricted Cash for the purpose of housing. Appropriations
for this budget item in the RDA budget is expect to begin in the 2027 Fiscal Year.

X
Provo City and the Provo City Redevelopment Agency will begin an application for a Housing
and Transit Reinvestment Zone by fall of 2025. This application will include identified properties
within the radius of the Frontrunner station with the benefit of an opportunity zone to expand the
radius.

A
Some rezoning will occur with the adoption of the Station Area Plans, which is expected by
December 2025.

B
Infrastructure continues to expand on the West side of Provo. West Provo is developing quickly
with mixed housing units including moderate income housing options. Without the added
infrastructure on the west side, housing would not be an option. More roadways, active
transportation trails and water and sewer lines will be added to new development by December
2025 and throughout 2026.

I
Ordinance Text Amendment to allow for group occupancy in the Professional Office zone in
Provo was passed on March 11, 2025 to allow for single room occupancy.

O
Provo City applied for HOME funding for down payment assistance program run by City staff.
HOME funding was granted for the 25-26 program year and is expected to assist between 6-10
moderate income households. The City will continue to apply for this funding annually.

G
Much of the land near transit hubs is zoned for mixed use and higher densities. The station area
plans (SAPs) will make further recommendations and are expected to be adopted by December
2025. On July 8 Provo City Council voted on the SAPs as to form for their review by the
Mountainland Association of Governments Station Area Plan Policy Committee.
