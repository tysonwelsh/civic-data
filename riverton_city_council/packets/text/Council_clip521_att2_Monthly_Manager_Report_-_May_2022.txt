                           Riverton Police Department Monthly Report

                                               May, 2022
    I wanted to highlight one of our officers this month who went above and beyond to save the life
    of a Herriman man after the man suffered a catastrophic medical emergency while riding his
    bicycle.

    On April 28, 2022, Officer Dallas Mosher was driving his personal vehicle with his family on
    Porter Rockwell Boulevard in Herriman when he noticed a cyclist laying on the ground on the
    side of the road with a group of people standing around him. Officer Mosher was off duty at the
    time, but he stopped to offer his assistance and immediately recognized the cyclist was in distress
    and required immediate medical care. Officer Mosher relied on his training to complete a quick
    assessment of the injured man and determined he was unconscious and in cardiac arrest.

    Officer Mosher began chest compressions and continued life-saving resuscitation efforts until
    paramedics arrived on scene several minutes later and relieved him. It was later determined the
    cyclist experienced a significant heart attack prior to crashing on his bicycle. The physicians at
    the hospital where the injured cyclist recovered after the incident believed the 53-year-old man
    would not have survived this traumatic medical episode without the timely life-saving efforts
    performed by officer Dallas Mosher.

    We are very proud of all our officers and the dedication they display every day, both on and off
    duty, to ensure the safety of our citizens.

    Also, I am pleased to note the number of vehicle burglaries reported last month was 6, the lowest
    monthly number we have had during the last couple of years. I credit our educational efforts and
    our focus on patrolling our neighborhoods at night for the dramatic reduction.
