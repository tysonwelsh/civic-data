II.     ACTION ITEM
        A.     ESTABLISH A DEADLINE FOR INTERESTED CANDIDATES AND SET
               DATE FOR CITY COUNCIL MEETING TO FILL MAYORAL VACANCY.
Matt Dahl said on Thursday, November 14, 2024, Mayor Marcus Stevenson resigned,
effective immediately, and vacated the office of the mayor. Municipal vacancies must be
filled by the process provided in Utah Code Ann. § 20A-1-510. This section requires that
the City Council fill the vacant office by appointing a qualified candidate to fill the
remainder of the mayor’s term of office within 30 days after the date of Mayor Stevenson’s
resignation. This would fall on Sunday, December 15.

Prior to the City Council filling the vacancy, the City Council is required to give public
notice at least 14 calendar days before the day on which the City Council fills the vacancy.
In addition to identifying the date, time, and place of the meeting where the vacancy will
be filled, the notice must also include how interested individuals must submit their names
for consideration and the deadline for submitting one’s name for consideration. The City
Council will then interview each individual who meets the qualifications for office in an
open meeting. From this pool of candidates, the City Council will appoint an individual to
fill the vacancy by majority vote.

City staff requests the City Council’s input on setting the two dates in order for staff to
provide proper notice for the Council meeting to fill the mayoral vacancy. Currently, there
is only one City Council meeting scheduled for the rest of the year. If the Council would
like to hold interviews and fill the vacancy during its regularly scheduled Council meeting
on December 3, staff will need to post public notice tonight.

City staff anticipates that there will already be an extensive agenda on December 3 for
regular City business. Staff is concerned that holding candidate interviews and filling the
vacancy during the December 3rd Council meeting may greatly extend this meeting. Staff
instead recommends that City Council consider holding a special Council meeting on
December 10 for the sole purpose of interviewing qualified individuals and appointing a
candidate to fill the office of mayor.

Regardless of the date selected by the City Council, City staff requests that the deadline
for individuals to submit their name for consideration be set on the preceding Thursday
at the close of business hours.
.
Council Member Dustin Gettel asked if it could be done before December 10th, suggesting
Wednesday December 4th or Thursday December 5th.

Matt Dahl said depending on when the notice of the vacancy is released, the meeting to
fill the vacancy could take place before December 10th, but state code requires a 14-day
notice. He said we could move the regular council meeting scheduled for December 3rd
to December 10th and fill the vacancy in a meeting on December 3rd.

Garrett Wilcox reminded everyone that the city offices will be closed an additional day
due to the Thanksgiving holiday on November 28th.

Council Members Dustin Gettel, Bonnie Billings, and Heidi Robinson all agreed that
December 10th was a good date to hold the meeting to fill the vacancy.

Matt Dahl talked about the number of residents that may be interested in the vacant
position. He also said that they can’t require additional documents, beyond what state
code requires, be submitted but they can suggest and will suggest that each candidate
submit a letter of interest that will be available to the public in the Council Packet. He
asked the Council what other information they would like to see from applicants.

Council Member Bonnie Billings said she would like to see a letter of interest, stating that
it will give her background information about the applicant up front.

Matt Dahl outlined that the application will be available online and in City Hall, and must
be submitted, in person, to Rori Andreason in the Recorder’s Office.

Mayor Pro-Tem Paul Glover asked if there was an application fee.

Garrett Wilcox said the state code doesn’t mention requiring a fee for any election other
than a traditional election. His understanding of state code is this election would not
require a fee.

Council Member Dustin Gettel agreed.

Matt Dahl explained the voting and appointment processes per state code. After much
discussion amongst the council members about the different nuances involved with the
process, he said that exactly how they will proceed will be discussed at the regular council
meeting on December 3rd.

Rori Andreason asked for the deadline to receive applications.

Matt Dahl said it is Thursday, December 5th by 5:00 pm.

Council Member Bryant Brown asked if anyone from the public has shown interest in
applying.

Mayor Pro-Tem Glover said he has heard from a couple of residents.
