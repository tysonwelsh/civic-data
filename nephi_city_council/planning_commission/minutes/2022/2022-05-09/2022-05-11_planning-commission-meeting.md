     2. NEBO MEADOWS - VICINITY PLAN (APPLICANT THE WRIGHT DIRECTION, LLC, COREY WRIGHT)
Mr. Atkinson explained that this vacant land is between the Hunters Meadows and the Nebo Heights
subdivision. It used to be owned by the Juab School District, and now The Wright Direction, LLC owns it
through a land swap for property in Mona. Forty-one lots are currently being proposed with an average
lot size of one-third acre lots, although this zone would allow as little as one-quarter acre lots. They are
currently proposing an elbowed street on 1450 North, but the city would rather they not install 1450 N.
and instead carry 200 East through to the North Ridge subdivision for better circulation, and to have
another North-South corridor other than Main Street for future transportation needs. The developer
has tentatively agreed to redesign going forward with this approach. At the time Mr. Messersmith did
the North Ridge subdivision; an agreement was reached to allow for this road going through either his
Lot 17 or Lot 18 depending on how this very piece was to develop. Also, of note, the city would like to
somehow secure, either with an easement, or with the outright purchase of the back of the lots, ground
to the east of this development that borders on the Nebo Heights subdivision. Utilities lie in the
backyards of the lots in the Nebo Heights subdivision, and servicing these lots has become a difficult
task. There is an easement in the back of the lots, but over the years, homeowners have encroached
into this easement. To avoid that happening in this new development, and to allow for servicing of lots
in the Nebo Heights development, the city would like to be able to actually fence this area off.
Commissioner Robertson expressed concern with the city fencing off the easement, and yet people still
having to pay taxes, yet not have access to their property. Mr. Atkinson explained that this is also being
looked at as an area to connect as a trail system to the canyon, so it may be best for these reasons to
purchase the ten feet.

Motion: The motion was made to approve the Nebo Meadows - Vicinity Plan.

Made by: Karl Brough             Second: Kent Heap                Outcome: Passed unanimously

    3. THREE BASINS - SITE PLAN (APPLICANT JEFFREY KAY)
Mr. Atkinson presented the site plan idea for Mr. Kay to put two triplexes on this lot that is zoned R-3
with a zero lot-line overlay. They will face south, and will each have their own individual water, sewer,
electrical, and gas meters. However, each bank of units will all be required to be under one ownership
as separate lots will not be created for each building. Mr. Kay needs to work with the city on the
placement of the meters. The city likes to put the gas and electricity as far apart as possible. Mr. Kay is
showing thirty-six parking spaces for six units (six stalls per unit). In the future he has talked about
putting some covered parking with storage for the units. Mr. Kay will need to show a light on his final

as-built plans if he is going to place a light pole at the entrance of the development on 100 West. The
sewer is very shallow in this location, but these triplexes will be slab-on-grade, and should work just fine.

Motion: The motion was made to approve the Three Basins Site plan.

Made by: Alan Hancock            Second: Kent Heap                 Outcome: Passed unanimously

    4. SIGN PERMIT FOR MAVERIK (APPLICANT YESCO, LLC SIGN COMPANY AND MAVERIK, INC.)

Mrs. Talbot explained that Maverik is proposing four signs to advertise their convenience store they are
building. One pylon sign will have two faces that are eighty-five feet in height with one face situated to
be seen from southbound I-15 traffic, and one for north-bound I-15 traffic. They will have a second
pylon sign advertising their pricing near the building, and on the frontage. And they will have two signs
affixed to their building one on the south side and one on the east side with their logo to label and
advertise the business. The need to update the city’s sign ordinance was discussed.

Motion: The motion was made to approve the sign permit for the Maverik.

Made by: Karl Brough             Second: Ann Peterson              Outcome: Passed unanimously

    6. DISCUSSION POSSIBLE CHANGES TO LAND USE CODE FOR FARM ANIMALS

City Administrator, Mr. Atkinson explained to the Commission that the city has been receiving feedback
from some citizens that they would like to have the ability to own a few chickens or other small farm
animals be allowed in other areas other than the animal rights overlay zone. He shared with them some
of the research he has gathered from other municipalities regarding this, and asked if they would like to
have him present an ordinance at the next meeting to consider regarding the allowance of chickens.
The Commissioners discussed the pros and cons of this possibility.

Motion: The motion was made to have Mr. Atkinson bring back a proposed ordinance for the allowance
of chickens in zones other than the animal overlay zone and on property less than half-an-acre, so the
Commission can decide if that would be the best for the city.

Motion: Ann Peterson             Second: Heather Robertson         Outcome: Passed unanimously
