Council Member Business

                    Council Member Marci Houseman mentioned that today marks the 80th anniversary of
                    the bombing of Pearl Harbor and she expressed gratitude to our veterans and those in
                    service to our nation. She spoke about those veterans honored at the event at
                    Rice-Eccles Stadium. She congratulated the Canyons School District - three of the
                    district's schools were identified as Top Schools by the U.S. News and World Reports.
                    She thanked all those who came out to the Sandy Club event a few weeks ago and
                    appreciated the Shops at South Town for their support of the Sandy Club through the
                    Giving Wall.

                    Council Member Brooke Christensen thanked Council Member Kris Nicholl for her eleven
                    years of service to Sandy City and wished her well in her future endeavors.

                    Council Member Zach Robinson thanked Council Member Nicholl for her service and work
                    for the city. He expressed appreciation for her comments and mentoring and enjoyed
                    serving with her and knows how much she cares about the City. He thanked the Public
                    Works department staff for the leaf clean-up program. Mr. Robinson thinks it is a great
                    service to the residents and loves the program. He also acknowledged Matt Huish and
                    Administration for their recent efforts to help a resident.

                    Council Member Monica Zoltanski recognized Council Member Nicholl and thanked her
                    for her leadership and service to the City and for her sensible compromises. Ms.
                    Zoltanski thanked the Parks department for coordinating the Light up the Cairns event
                    and offered a suggestion to the Administration to add favorite light displays to the city
                    social posts.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey thanked Council Member Nicholl for her eleven years of
                    service to Sandy City, serving on the Planning Commission and the City Council. She
                    respects Ms. Nicholl and will miss her advice and perspective. She thanked Ms. Nicholl
                    for all she has done and for her mentorship. We will miss you.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud thanked the Fire Department for inviting her to the Awards
                    luncheon. The Light Up the Cairns event was a great event. Ms. Stroud thanked Ms.
                    Nicholl for her service and help - she always took the time to offer explanations and
                    perspective on issues and was always willing to listen.

                    Council Member Houseman thanked Council Member Nicholl for her quiet confidence,
                    which comes from a desire to enrich the people around you.


Mayor's Report

                    Evelyn Everton, Deputy Mayor, thanked Council Member Nicholl on behalf of the Mayor
                    and Administration, for her service .

CAO Report

                    Matt Huish, CAO, mentioned the Covid response calls have doubled in the last month.
                    Dan Medina, Parks Director, read a thank you note received by River Oaks commending
                    the staff there.
