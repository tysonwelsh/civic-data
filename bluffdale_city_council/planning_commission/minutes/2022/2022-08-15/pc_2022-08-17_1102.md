4.     PUBLIC HEARING, CONSIDERATION, AND RECOMMENDATION on a Zoning
       Text and Development Agreement Application for The Centrum at Bluffdale Special
       District ("SD-X"), BCC 11.110.130, which is located at the Southwest Corner of 14600
       South and Porter Rockwell Boulevard, to Modify the Number of Apartment Units
       from 236 to 252 and to Clarify the Use of Architectural Concrete within the Project,
       LH Perry Investments, LLC, Applicants (Application 2022-34) - Staff Presenter,
       Grant Crowell.

City Planner/Economic Development Director, Grant Crowell , presented the staff report and stated
that in January 2022 a new Special District (SD-X) was created called The Centrum at Bluffdale.
He displayed an aerial view of the subject property and reported that it was originally an industrial
parcel that was subsequently changed to mixed-use. The original proposal included 236 apartments
and 17,500 square feet of retai l. If the City Council approves the revisions to the Development
Agreement ("DA"), there would be minor modifications to the DA to replace the original Exhibit B
with a new Exhibit B, which is the zoning ordinance.

As the applicants worked on the design and engineering plans, they realized that they could modify
the site plan to include 16 more apartment units. The overall view of the project would be
essentially the same. The parking ratio would remain the same as before at two spaces per unit.
There would be no changes to the types of units that were previously approved. The retail square
footage and commercial parking would be the same as previously approved .

Mr. Crowell next reviewed the widening of the road that would need to take place at the southeast
corner of the subject property, which was memorialized in the Development Agreement. That
expectation will remain unchanged to accommodate the needed intersection design for the proj ect.

Another aspect of the project the applicants had been working on was an update of the exterior
elevations as part of the proposal to include the use of architectural concrete in the bottom part of
the residential structure. If the proposal is approved, the SD-X ordinance would include updated
images of what the buildings would look like.

Mr. Crowell next reviewed the impact of adding 16 units. The applicants were required to have a
Traffic Engineer identify what that impact would be based on modeling and typical scenarios. The
applicants asserted that the proposed increase in units would be accommodated by the intersection
improvements that were previously discussed and agreed upon. As part of the site plan approval
process, Staff would seek to confer that the network would be able to handle the traffic. There
would also be a traffic light installed at Rising Star Way and Porter Rockwell Boulevard, as
warranted and approved by the Utah Department of Transportation ("UDOT").

Mr. Crowell reviewed the text of the revisions to the SD-X ordinance. In the residential building,
patterned and formed architectural concrete would be allowed as a primary exterior material on the
parking Jot levels. The accent and brick would still be required. The meeting packet included
current and proposed images for consideration. Mr. Crowell noted that the City Council has already
been receiving a lot of feedback about the project and many people are aware of the proposal.

5.     PUBLIC HEARING, CONSIDERATION, AND RECOMMENDATION on an Update
       to the Moderate-Income Housing Element of the City of Bluffdale General Plan.
       (Application 2022-36) - Staff Presenter, Ellen Oakman.

Associate City Plam1er, Ellen Oak.man, presented the staff report and noted that the Moderate-
Income Housing Plan is a requirement specified in the Utah Code Amendments HB 462. The new
General Plan, which was recently approved by the City Counci l, did not include the Moderate-
Income Housing Plan because of the incongruous timing between the State' s updates to its
requirements and the City's completion of the General Plan. Therefore, this element is now ready

required by the State Legislature to ensure the provision of affordable housing. This requirement
dates back to 20 19 with S.B. 34, Affordable Housing Requirements, which requires municipalities
to create a Moderate-Income Housing Plan with strategies or menu items to address.
