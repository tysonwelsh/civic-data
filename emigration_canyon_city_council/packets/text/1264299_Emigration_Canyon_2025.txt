OFFICIAL NOTICE
2025 MUNICIPAL GENERAL ELECTION
                                                                                                DIANA BAUN
                                                                                                 City Recorder
In accordance with Utah Code Section 20A-5-101(2), I, Diana Baun, City Recorder, give notice that the
following offices will be voted on within the City of Emigration Canyon in the 2025 General Election and the dates
for filing a declaration of candidacy for those offices:

2025 OFFICES TO BE VOTED ON

 Emigration Canyon
 Mayor                                                                                               4 year term

 Council Member                                     Council At-Large                   1 seat        4 year term




                       A primary election will be held, if necessary, on Tuesday, August 12, 2025.
                                The general election will be held on November 4, 2025.

CANDIDATE FILING PERIODS

Traditional Candidates
The Declaration of Candidacy filing period for qualified candidates begins on Monday, June 2, 2025 at 8:00 a.m. and
ends at 5:00 p.m. on Friday, June 6, 2025. Declarations of Candidacy must be made in person with Diana Baun,
Town Clerk, between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., Monday June 2 through Friday June 6 at the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District Offices, located at 860 W. Levoy Dr., Suite 300, Taylorsville, UT 84123.
A $50 Filing Fee will be collected during the declaration period.
For assistance, please call Diana Baun at (385) 377-9466, or email dbaun@msd.utah.gov
