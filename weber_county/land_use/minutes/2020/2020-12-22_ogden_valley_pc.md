OGDEN VALLEY PLANNING COMMISSION                                                               December 22, 2020


Minutes of the Ogden Valley Planning Commission for December 22, 2020. To join the meeting please navigate to the following web
link at the time of the meeting https://us02web.zoom.us/j/87034442502 commencing at 5:00 p.m.

       Present: John Lewis, Chair; Shanna Francis, Vice Chair, John Howell, Chris Hogge, Jeffry R. Burton, Ron Lackey, and Steve
       Waldrip
       Absent/Excused:
       Staff Present: Rick Grover, Planning Director; Scott Perkes, Planner; Courtlan Erickson, Legal Counsel and Angela Martin, Lead
       Office Specialist II

               Roll Call
               Pledge of Allegiance


            Consent Items

            CUP 2020-16: Consideration and action on a Bed and Breakfast Dwelling in the basement of an existing SFD located at
            4427 Powder Mountain Rd. in Eden. Applicant: Dennis & Kathy Longfellow; Staff Presenter: Scott Perkes

            Commissioner Howell made a motion to approve CUP 2020-16: Consideration and action on a Bed and Breakfast
            Dwelling in the basement of an existing SFD located at 4427 Powder Mountain Rd. in Eden. Commissioner Waldrip
            seconded the motion. Chair Lewis, Commissioners Hogge, Burton, Waldrip, Howell, Francis and Lackey all voted aye,
            motion carried.

            Director Grover commented on ADU Ordinance that the County Commission approved on Tuesday’s Commission
            Meeting.



            Adjourn


      Meeting Adjourned: The meeting adjourned at 5:15 pm
      Respectfully Submitted,
       Angela Martin, Lead Office Specialist
