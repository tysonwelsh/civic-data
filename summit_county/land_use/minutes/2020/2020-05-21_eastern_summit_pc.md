4. Public hearing and possible action regarding the amendments to the Promontory 
Equestrian Center Final Site Plan to modify the plan to include an employee housing 
structure (7 units) and to amend the facilities identified to better recognize the 
equestrian facilities as improved located at 6680 N Promontory Ranch Rd., Parcel SS-
51-C, no increase in facility capacity is being proposed, Shawn Potter representing 
Promontory Investments LLC, Applicant – Amir Caus, AICP, County Planner 
 
Commissioner Sonntag recused himself from this item. 
Planner Caus shared the background for this application. One public comment was 
received in opposition to this application from James and Sheila Ochowicz.  
On page one of the Staff report, the square-footage of the studios should show 400 square 
feet instead of 4,000. 
The project is within the gates of Promontory and meets the Development Agreement and 
affordable housing agreement. No additional capacity is being requested at the equestrian 
center. The building is pretty well screened from the main entrance. Materials will match 
the existing equestrian center. Staff recommends approval but asks for an additional 
Condition of Approval that deed restriction be finalized before final approval. 
Shawn Potter stated the housing units are intended for rental use with priority given to 
Promontory employees. Rent will be a maximum of 80 percent area median income (AMI), 
not to exceed 30 percent gross income of the renter. The renter can lock in for an annual 
lease term, but the rent will change on an annual basis. The County will review the terms 
every three years and Promontory will report annually. Attorney Strachan stated there 
will be a monthly rent roll showing who is renting, their income, and where they work. The 
rent roll is monitored by Jeff Jones.

Shawn Potter stated that the deed restriction is acceptable as a Condition of Approval and

Helen M Rose lives in Silver Gate, next to Promontory and stated that it seems the

Condition of Approval is that public transport is accessible. How is this being addressed?

Planner Caus stated that the project must adhere to Promontory guidelines and that the 
Promontory Design Review Committee and Promontory Conservancy approved the design. 
Shawn Potter stated that Promontory guidelines require the style of buildings fit in the 
common community. There are two existing units in the same style as proposed. 
Promontory has its own transportation system for employees near the trail system and the

Findings of Fact:  
1. The Promontory Development Agreement was approved on January 2, 2001.  
2. Promontory Investments LLC is the owner of record of Parcel SS-51-C (333.52 acres)  
3. Per Section 4.7.12 Employee Housing of the Promontory Development Agreement; The 
Developer shall provide for thirty-seven (37) employee households consisting of the 82 bedroom 
suites of employee housing. Approval of such employee housing units shall be subject to the 
final site plan approval process in this Agreement.  
4. The employee housing is a requirement of the Promontory Development Agreement and is not 
associated with the market rate density pool.  
5. The applicant is requesting to amend the Promontory Equestrian Center Final Site Plan to; • 
Add a new ~3,200 sq. ft. structure which will accommodate seven (7) employee housing units 
(~400 sq. ft. studios) at the Promontory Equestrian Center complex, located at 6680 N 
Promontory Ranch Rd. (Parcel SS-51-C), Promontory, Summit County, UT. • Amend facilities 
identified to better recognize the equestrian facilities as improved. o No increase in equestrian 
facility capacity is being proposed.  
6. The development parameters for this project are specifically set forth in the Promontory 
Development Agreement and Promontory Employee Housing Agreement.  
7. The proposed Final Site Plan Amendment is legally described as Promontory Equestrian 
Center Final Site Plan – First Amendment.  
8. Public notice of the public hearing was published in The Summit County News.  
9. Postcard notices announcing the public hearing were mailed to property owners within 1,000 
feet of the subject parcels.  
10. Service providers have reviewed the plats for compliance with applicable standards and no 
project issues have been identified that could not be mitigated.

11. Staff has reviewed the proposed plats for compliance with applicable Development Code 
standards and the Promontory Development Agreement standards.  
 
Conclusions of Law:  
1. The proposal meets the terms of the Promontory Development Agreement and associated 
Promontory Housing Agreement.  
2. The proposal meets the applicable standards of the Eastern Summit County Development 
Code.  
3. The proposal meets the applicable standards of the Eastern Summit County General Plan.  
 
Conditions of Approval:  
1. All service provider requirements shall be met.  
2. All necessary permits must be obtained and fees shall be paid prior to the commencement of 
any construction activity, including but not limited to the Summit County Engineering and the 
Summit County Building Departments. 
3. Deed restrictions must be finalized before final approval of the project. 
 
 
Commissioner Wilde made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the 
County Manager according to the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and Conditions 
of Approval in the Staff Report, with a change to page one of the staff Report to 400 
square foot studios rather than 4,000, and an additional Condition of Approval that 
deed restriction be finalized before final approval. Commissioner Wheaton seconded 
the motion. 
• MOTION CARRIED (6-0)
