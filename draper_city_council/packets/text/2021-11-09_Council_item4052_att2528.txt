                                        RESOLUTION NO. 21-60

          A RESOLUTION INDICATING THE INTENT OF DRAPER CITY TO ADJUST
          THE COMMON BOUNDARY BETWEEN DRAPER CITY AND LEHI CITY
          AFFECTING APPROXIMATELY 163 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED
          WITHIN PARCEL NUMBERS 110-150-007 AND 110-150-008 WITHIN DRAPER
          CITY.

       WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it is interested in reviewing a municipal
boundary adjustment with Lehi City; and

        WHEREAS, the boundary adjustment would adjust the common boundary as depicted in Exhibit
A attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference.

        WHEREAS, the boundary adjustment would relocate approximately 163 acres (“163 Acres”) of
property from Draper City into Lehi City; and

       WHEREAS, it is more efficient and economical for Lehi City to provide municipal services to the
163 Acres once they are developed; and

        WHEREAS, the City intends to hold a public hearing to review the boundary adjustment within
60 days after adopting this resolution; and

          WHEREAS, this Resolution complies with Utah State Code 10-2-419 requirements.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

        Section 1.      Intent to Adjust a Common Boundary. The City of Draper approves this
Resolution 21-60 indicating the intent to adjust the common municipal boundary with Lehi City as provided
in Exhibit A, otherwise known as the Draper City – Lehi City Common Boundary Adjustment.

        Section 2.        Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution and
all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3.       Effective Date.    This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE _____ DAY OF     , 2021.
