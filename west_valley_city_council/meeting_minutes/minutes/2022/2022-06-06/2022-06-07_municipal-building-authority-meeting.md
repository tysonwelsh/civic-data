    PUBLIC HEARINGS
    A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2021-
         2022 BUDGET
         Wayne Pyle informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
         Municipal Building Authority Meeting scheduled June 7, 2022, in order for the
         Authority to hear and consider public comments regarding re-opening the FY
         2021-2022 Budget.

           Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
           information as follows:

                  State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
                  allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The
                  West Valley City Municipal Building Authority holds, as needed on a
                  quarterly basis, public hearings to amend the annual budget.

                  A Public Notice was posted May 19, 2022 in public view at City Hall, the
                  Family Fitness Center and UCCC; also, posted on the Public Notice
                  Website and West Valley City’s Website. Notice was given that a public
                  hearing is to be held June 7, 2022 at 6:30 p.m., West Valley City Hall, 3600
                  Constitution Blvd., West Valley City, Utah.

           Vice Chairman Whetstone opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak
           in favor or opposition, Vice Chairman Whetstone closed the Public Hearing.

           ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 22-03, AMEND THE BUDGET OF
           WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY FOR THE
           FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2021 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2022
           TO REFLECT CHANGES IN THE BUDGET FROM INCREASED
           REVENUES AND AUTHORIZE THE DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
           The Municipal Building Authority previously held a public hearing regarding
           proposed Resolution 22-03 that would amend the budget of West Valley City
           Municipal Building Authority for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2021 and ending
           June 30, 2022 to reflect changes in the budget from increased revenues and
           authorize the disbursement of funds.

    B.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING THE FY 2022-2022 BUDGET
         Wayne Pyle informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
         Municipal Building Authority Meeting scheduled June 7, 2022, in order for the
         Board to hear and consider public comments regarding the FY 2022-2023 Budget.

         Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
         information as follows:

                This resolution adopts the final budget for the Municipal Building Authority
                of West Valley City for the 2022-2023 fiscal year after receiving public
                comment at a hearing on June 7, 2022.

                The Municipal Building Authority adopted a tentative budget that was made
                available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s
                Recorder’s office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment,
                before adopting this final budget for FY 2022-2023.

         Vice Chairman Whetstone opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak
         in favor or opposition, Vice Chairman Whetstone closed the Public Hearing.
