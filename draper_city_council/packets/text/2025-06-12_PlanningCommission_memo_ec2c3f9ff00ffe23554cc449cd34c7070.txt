MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2025-06-12
Re: Public Hearing: Alpine Hollow Development Agreement and Preliminary Subdivision
Plat Request (Legislative and Administrative Actions)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement and Preliminary
Subdivision Plat for approximately 115.2 acres located to the east of Suncrest Dr., at
approximately 3235 East 15780 South. The property is currently zoned RR-22 and RR-43.
The applicant is requesting that a Development Agreement and Preliminary Subdivision
Plat be approved to allow for a 50-lot single-family development.




The findings are as follows:

Development Agreement

For approval:


  1. The proposed Development Agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and
     (D).
  2. The addition roadway width is needed for the safety of future residents living in the
     proposed development.



For denial:


  1. The proposed Development Agreement does not comply with DCMC Section 9-5-
     200(C) and (D).
Preliminary Subdivision Plat

For approval:


  1. The proposed plat complies with DCMC Section 17-3-040(A).



For denial:


  1. The proposed plat does not comply with DCMC Section 17-3-040(A).
