RESOLUTION 06-16-2026-A – ADOPTION OF FY 2027 INTERIM BUDGET
       Seth Atkinson stated that the interim budget can be located on the city website. This budget
       was presented last June in a public hearing. The stormwater utility was added along with added
       accounts to the general fund for community utility advertising. Mr. Atkinson stated that the
       interim budget contains the proposed property tax increase and a separate revenue account for
       anticipated revenue associated with the increase. He provided details on the public hearing
       scheduled for August 11, 2026, and the final budget adoption at a council meeting in August
       following the public hearing.

RESOLUTION 06-16-2026-C – MASTER FEE RESOLUTION
      Mr. Atkinson explained the master fee resolution related to the electric rates, specifically the
      commercial demand charge. The recommendation was to move the rate to the large
      commercial rate. The change is to the demand charge only; the volume charges are unaffected.

SURPLUS EQUIPMENT FOR NATURAL GAS DEPARTMENT
      Seth Atkinson discussed the recommendation to surplus a backhoe used by the natural gas
      department. The backhoe is older and is used less frequently, and the gas department would
      like to list it as surplus equipment and list it for sale. Because the value is over $1000, council
      approval is needed according to the city’s surplus policy.
      Councilor JD Parady makes a motion to approve the backhoe as surplus to be sold. Councilor
      Tate Douglas seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.
