This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final decision
authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-25-4005, “Dansie
2025 Rezone,” by amending the General Plan to include both parcels in the “Low Density
Residential” General Plan designation and by rezoning the two subject properties to R-3
(single-family residential, 14,000-sf minimum lot size).

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of General Plan Amendment and
Rezone application PLZ-25-4005, “Dansie 2025 Rezone,” thus keeping the current General
Plan Designations and current zoning (RR-22).

Background

This is a public hearing for a General Plan Amendment and Rezone application brought by the
applicant, Alan Prince, representing MP&G, LLC. This request covers two (2) parcels, both of
which have the RR-22 Zoning Designation. If approved, this application would place all the
subject property in the same Zone of R-3.

The subject property is approximately 2.32-acres located near 3100 West and 13400 South, on
the north side of 13400 South. The two (2) parcels are identified as parcel numbers:

         Parcel Id #                                 Address                                 Acreage
        27-33-351-040                           3082 W. 13400 South                            0.93
        27-33-351-050                           3112 W. 13400 South                            1.39

Both subject parcels are currently zoned RR-22 (Rural Residential, ½-acre with animal rights).
Adjoing properties to the east and west also have the same RR-22 Zone. To the north the Dansie
Estates Subdivision (2017) has R-3 Zoning. The area south of 13400 South Street has R-4
 Zoning. It should be noted that although the three properties adjacent on the east side have
 RR-22 Zoning, none of the 3 lots meets the ½-acre minimum requirement to have large animals.

The General Plan designates these two properties differently. The western parcel (27-33-351-
050) is designated as “Low Density Residential” which corresponds with the R-3 zone, from there
the Low Density Residential designation extends west along 13400 South all the way to 3600 West.
The eastern parcel (27-33-351-040) is designated as “Estate Density Residential” which
corresponds with the RR-22 Zone. The Estate Density Residential designation extends east to
2700 West. Adjusting the General Plan designation to match the zoning is good practice, and an
adjustment of one parcel on the designation boundary keeps with the overall vision of the General
Plan for the area.

If the General Plan Amendment and Rezone were eventually approved by the City Council, the
applicant has expressed an interest in developing a small subdivision with a private lane. Issues
about the private lane, subdivision layout, drainage, irrigation water and fencing would be identified
and resolved during the subdivision process, and in future Planning Commission Meetings.

Dear Planning Commissioners,

I am writing about the proposed rezoning of property 3082 / 3112 West 13400 South from
RR-22 to R-3 Residential. Unfortunately, we have a long-planned vacation scheduled at the
same time as the public hearing, so we will be unable to attend. We would appreciate your
input and requests being noted this way.

My husband and I, and our son and daughter-in-law, are both adjacent property owners (to the
West) and do not object to the proposed rezoning as long as the development is developed in
the same manner as recent subdivisions in the area. We request that there be an 8-foot high
masonry wall separating the rezoned area from both our and our son's property. The
wall should match the other walls surrounding our property (running along the north
end of the property and also to the West of our home, which fronts 3200 West). This
would mean that an 8-foot masonry wall would need to run along the entire West side of
the rezoned development starting at 13400 South to the most northern corner of the
proposed rezoning. We still maintain farm animals (2 beef that we move between 2 pastures)
and the wall is necessary to protect the animals and our lifestyle from the new homes being
built. Raising beef has been part of the property and our lifestyle since we moved here in the
70s and is the only way to ensure we can continue our lifestyle and minimize the impact of the
new homes. We would appreciate your respect for the farm country that has always been in
Riverton - our acreage is a small piece that still retains that history and needs to be buffered
from new homes with a wall that matches the others.
