PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on February 27, 2019 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2019-02 repealing Ordinance No. 2017-07 authorizing the exemption to certain Impact Fees with respect to the development known as Anthem Center and authorizing the exemption of Roadway Facilities Impact Fee with respect to the WinCo to be Developed in the Anthem Center. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
