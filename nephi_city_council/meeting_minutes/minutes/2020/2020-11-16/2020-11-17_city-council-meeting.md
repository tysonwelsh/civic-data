Mayor Pro Tem Justin Seely called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  Myrna Trauntvein led the pledge of allegiance. Councilor Kent Jones spoke about the Juab County
Senior Citizen’s Program that feeds the senior every Wednesday. The program also delivers 52 meals on
wheels Monday through Thursday to senior in the community that are home bound.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes for
the regular council meeting held on 11-03-20, work session held on 11-3-20, the work session minutes
11-10-20, the sale of a burial right to Lisa Brindley, and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 11-
17-20 Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones reviewed a business license application for Nephi Oil, Inc. Councilor Jones moved to
approve the business licenses. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Jeanne Lewis, Chairperson for the Illuminate the Night activity, reported to the council about the
plans for the event. The event would be held on Saturday, November 28th from 5:00 to 6:30 pm. The
following activities were planned: reverse lighting parade along 1st East, Grinch contest, horse and buggy
rides, train rides, Santa Claus, characters from the movie Frozen, musical entertainment, hot chocolate
and donuts, and a countdown to the lighting of the park.

ANNEXATION POLICY PLAN REVISIONS ADOPTED:

  City Attorney Kasey Wright reviewed with the council some language changes to the annexation policy
plan. The changes revolved around annexations being contiguous to the city limits. The state statute
had changed allowing island or peninsula annexations. The proposed language changes are neutral
regarding contiguous parcels and included language in the Level 1 definition stating that annexation
request that are contiguous to city limits would be preferred over island or peninsula annexations.
Councilor Worwood moved to adopt the new revisions to the Annexation Policy Plan. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

UDOT STORMWATER PROJECT AGREEMENT:

   The council was informed that Nephi City has had a stormwater project on the list of funded projects
from the state’s Joint Highway Committee (JHC). The project would convey stormwater away from the
state highway to proper drainage systems thus avoiding flooding problems near the highway during
heavy storm events. When examining the options for these improvements, some obstacles arose
regarding where to convey the water. To overcome these obstacles, additional funding would be
needed. Recently, UDOT presented these issues to the JHC and secured $1 million of additional funding
to address these concerns. The upside to the city is the additional funding, but since a percentage
match is required from the city, the downside is the match requirement has also increased. Although
the price tag is higher, there is simply no way to get the extent of these improvements without the state
grant funding. The city would need to provide $212,552 in match money for this project.

   The necessary funds could be supplied from street funding, or the city’s capital improvement fund, or
a combination of both. While the funding could be supplied from these sources for this project, a way
to replenish these funds for the future should be explored. Councilor Ostler moved to approve the
UDOT Stormwater Project Agreement. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed
on a unanimous vote.
