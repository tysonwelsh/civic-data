                              RESOLUTION NO. 25-53

       A RESOLUTION APPROVING A REIMBURSEMENT AGREEMENT WITH
LLOYD’S TLC, LIMITED FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF PUBLIC IMPROVEMENTS IN THE
          AREA OF BANGERTER PARKWAY, 13800 SOUTH, AND 150 EAST

      WHEREAS, Lloyd’s TLC, Limited (“Developer”) intends to develop the real
property located at 13782 South 300 East in Draper, Utah (“Property”) into a
commercial shopping center (“Development”); and

       WHEREAS, the Development will generate additional traffic impacts on
surrounding public roads, including 13800 South, Bangerter Parkway, and 150 East,
necessitating certain infrastructure improvements attributable to the Development
(“Project Improvements”), which would ordinarily be the Developer’s responsibility to
fund and construct; and

       WHEREAS, independent of the Development, Draper City (“City”) has identified
preexisting deficiencies in its transportation system on the same surrounding public
roads (“System Deficiencies”), which are the City’s responsibility to remedy through
infrastructure improvements (“System Improvements”); and

       WHEREAS, the City is eligible for an appropriation of state funds in the amount
of $3,450,000 (“State Funds”) pursuant to Utah Code § 72-2-121(4)(k)(v), for
improvements to Bangerter Highway between 13800 South and I-15, subject to
revenue availability after distributions under § 72-2-121(4)(j), and anticipates
receiving such funds upon completion of the Improvements contemplated in this
Agreement. In the interim, the City has equivalent funds available to advance the
System Improvements, with the intention of replenishing its general fund upon
receipt of State Funds; and

       WHEREAS, in the ordinary course of business, the City would publicly bid the
System Improvements and select the lowest responsible bidder. However, the City
and the Developer agree that it is more efficient for the Developer to engage a
contractor to construct both the Project Improvements and the System
Improvements concurrently as part of the Development, with the City reimbursing
the Developer solely for the actual costs of the System Improvements. This
coordinated approach allows the Parties to share the costs of their respective
obligations in a manner that benefits the public and enhances the appearance,
function, and long-term durability of the improvements; and
      WHEREAS, developers are typically responsible for funding all Project
Improvements necessary for their proposed development, unless such
improvements address an existing deficiency. As an exception to standard practice
and as a benefit to both the Developer and the City, the City may, at its sole discretion,
reimburse certain costs of Project Improvements, but only if excess State Funds
remain after full reimbursement of Actual System Costs; and

      WHEREAS, the parties believe that coordinating the Project Improvements and
System Improvements will benefit the City, its residents, and the public by minimizing
disruptions, accelerating completion, reducing overall costs, improving quality, and
enhancing the appearance, function, and long-term life of the project; and

       WHEREAS, the Developer shall dedicate all rights-of-way it owns that are
necessary for the construction of the Improvements at no cost to the City. In the
event that condemnation is required to acquire property from third parties,
additional costs above the value of the property may be incurred, and if related to
the Bangerter Parkway improvements, may be included in the Actual System Costs
eligible for reimbursement under Utah Code § 72-2-121(4)(b); and

      WHEREAS, the Parties acknowledge that the Developer contributed to efforts
to secure State Funds, and that the Project Improvements, in conjunction with the
System Improvements, will enhance the safety and functionality of Bangerter
Parkway, providing significant public benefits to the City and its residents; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council entering into the Agreement with Lloyd’s TLC,
Limited is in the best interest of Draper City and promotes the health, safety and
welfare of its residents;

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER AS
FOLLOWS:

     Section 1. Approval of Agreement. The Reimbursement Agreement between
Draper City and Lloyd’s TLC, Limited, attached hereto as Exhibit A, is hereby
approved, and the Mayor is authorized to execute the same on behalf of Draper City.

       Section 2. De Minimus Changes. The City Manager is authorized to attach the
final exhibits to this Agreement and correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting,
clerical, or de minimus errors or omissions, provided such changes do not alter the
substantive terms of the Agreement as approved by the City Council.
