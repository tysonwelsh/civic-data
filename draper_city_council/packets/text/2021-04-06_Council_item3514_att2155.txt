                                ORDINANCE NO. 1486



     AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLE 3 OF THE
DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING GENERAL RULES FOR SUBORDINATE
MUNICIPAL BODIES AND MEMBERSHIP AND QUORUM REQUIREMENTS OF THE
HISTORIC PRESERVATION COMMISSION.



      WHEREAS, Title 3 establishes rules for subordinate bodies created by the city;
and

     WHEREAS, it is necessary from time to time to revise certain terms of the
Draper City Municipal Code; and

       WHEREAS, the city council has reviewed the proposed text amendment and
finds good cause to revise the terms and provisions of Title 3.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Findings. The city council of Draper City has made the following
findings to amend Title 3 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) The proposed
amendment is in compliance with the Utah Code Annotated. 2) The proposed
amendment is in the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens
of Draper.

      Section 2. Revision. Sections 3-1-150 and 3-1-160 of the Draper City Municipal
Code are hereby amended in whole, and Section 3-1-180 of the Draper City
Municipal Code is hereby amended in part to read as shown in Exhibit “A” attached
hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

      Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The city attorney is authorized to
correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis errors prior to
submitting the ordinance for publishing.

       Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this ordinance is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
ordinance shall be severable.

      Section 5. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to
the date of final passage.




PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 6TH DAY OF APRIL, 2021.

3-1-150: Subordinate Bodies: The city council may create any subordinate body
deemed necessary or appropriate for the administration of the city and shall
prescribe by resolution or ordinance the organization, powers and duties of such
bodies.

3-1-160: General Rules for Committees: Unless otherwise provided by law or these
ordinances, the following rules shall apply to all committees formed under this
chapter.

      A. Committees are advisory bodies only. Neither the committee nor its
         members have authority to either represent, act, negotiate or speak on
         behalf of the City, or to commit City funds for any purpose unless expressly
         authorized by the city council.

      B. Committees shall consist of members as the city council shall provide by
ordinance or resolution.

       C. Members. Committee members shall be appointed by the mayor with the
advice and consent of the city council. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter,
committee members shall serve four-year terms. When a new committee is created,
half of the members first appointed to the committee may be appointed to an initial
two-year term so that appointments to the committee are staggered.

      D. Chairperson. A chairperson shall oversee the proceedings and activities of
each committee. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, all chairpersons shall:

             1. be appointed by the mayor; and
             2. serve a two-year term.

        E. Vice-Chair or Temporary Chair. Each committee may appoint a vice-chair on
an annual basis or temporary chair on a per meeting basis to act as the chairperson
in in the event the chairperson is unable to attend or participate in the meeting.

      F. Committee members may be removed from office with or without cause by
majority vote of the city council.
      G. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, committee members shall
receive no compensation for their services, but may be reimbursed for reasonable
expenses incurred in the performance of their duties. Committee members shall be
deemed "volunteers" for purposes of city ordinances, rules and regulations
regarding personnel. However, they shall be included in the definition of “employee”
for purposes of the Utah Governmental Immunity Act as set forth in Utah Code
Annotated, as amended.

       H. Committees shall conduct meetings in accordance with the Utah Open and
Public Meetings Act. A committee shall keep a public record of its proceedings, and
all minutes of the meetings and decisions of a committee shall be filed in the Office
of the City Recorder as public records. Meetings of a committee may be called by the
chairperson, a majority of the members of a committee, the mayor, city manager,
city council or the department head assigned to work with the committee.

      I. Committees may receive funds by an annual appropriation in the city budget,
by government, corporate or private grants or donations, and from proceeds of
approved committee functions, which funds shall be held and managed as directed
by the city council.

      J. Advisor. The city manager shall designate a member of the city staff to serve
as an advisor to each committee. The designated staff member shall serve only to
advise and act as a liaison to the city administration and shall have no power to vote
on matters coming before the committee.

       K. Quorum. Three members of the committee shall constitute a quorum.
Three votes shall be the minimum number of votes required for a motion to pass
unless three votes does not constitute a majority of the quorum present; in which
case, a majority vote carries a motion.

       L. Electronic Meetings. Committees may conduct electronic meetings in
accordance with the Open and Public Meetings Act. To conform with the Open and
Public Meetings Act, a quorum must be present at the anchor location unless the
chairperson determines that a meeting with an anchor location presents a
substantial risk to the health or safety of the public.
