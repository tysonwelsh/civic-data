   1. GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT (Public Hearing) Legislative

       Consider a request to adopt the Downtown Area Plan which will serve as a vision document for our
       downtown. Case No. 2022-GPA-009 (Staff – Carol Davidson)

       Whitney Ward presented the following:

       Carol Davidson – We have a plan for you to recommend tonight. As you know we have been working
       on our General Plan and Downtown plan since the end of 2022. Just an FYI this Downtown Plan will be
       one of 10 – 15 plans for specific neighborhoods. We have been working with a 17-member stakeholder
       committee every month. I am going to turn the time over to Whitney Ward of VCBO, she and her staff
       have been working on the plan and she is going to present it to you. I wanted to mention that there are
       some citizen comments in the packet.

       Whitney Ward – We received comments from you as a Commission and from the public. You may
       want to discuss some of these comments, we want to hit the right balance. We are taking comments
       from both sides. Some think that they would like to push further and some think that we are losing some
       of that charm. There is a lot of policy work that needs to occur after the City accepts the vision and
       goals document. Whitney went through the presentation included in the packet. Whitney notated a
       correction to the Lively slide; they will be able to go up to 5 stories but will still need to step back after 2
       floors.

      Commissioner Andrus – The neighborhood areas have pretty rigid boundaries, are they meant to be?

      Whitney Ward – That is a great question. The idea with the boundaries was that looking at the existing
      character and the opportunities that is how the boundaries are decided. We did identify that we want to
      give the City the opportunity to look at the boundaries in a year. This is not one and done, it is a living
      document that can be modified as the City sees fit.

      Pro Tem Chair Kemp – I don’t want to see this plan create another layer of regulation; I think that from
      the perspective of a general plan the bubble flexes.

      Whitney Ward – We landed on harder lines because there were some definite ideas as to where these
      things should be. The concern with bubbles were that we weren’t giving enough direction to the City to
      make decisions.

      Pro Tem Chair Kemp – At the end of the day economics is going to drive what happens.

      Whitney Ward – With the next steps, the policy implementation of how do we rezone or what does a
      zone districts, how do they align with the character areas to help support that vision and goals. Then if
      somebody wants to come to you with a planned development or something that isn’t in the plan, they
      can still continue to do that.

      Pro Tem Chair Kemp – Will this add another layer will I have to get a Downtown Plan Amendment,
      then a General Plan Amendment and then a zone change?

      John Willis – No this will serve as the land use plan. It’s a little more specific. This is much more
      lenient in nature; the ordinance will be what drives this, it is the document that will give you the vision.

      Pro Tem Chair Kemp – So we are still only talking about a 2-step process. But we want the regulation
      to align with the vision.

      Jami Brackin– Yes, right now there is a question in the legislature that they want to make the General
      Plans regulatory, if that happens the hard lines will help with that. Right now, the General Plan isn’t
      regulatory. The Zoning Ordinances are, but they want to make them both regulatory.

      Commissioner Chapman – Where comes in the all the zoning changes and regulation requirements?

      John Willis – It will be a similar process where staff works with stakeholders to create the document.
      Zoning Regulation Amendment and you will have input on that. It will go to Planning Commission and
      City Council.

   2. ZONE CHANGE AMENDMENT (ZCA) (Public Hearing) Legislative

      Consider a request for a zone change amendment for the Desert Color PD (Planned Development). The
      amendment would allow the applicant to update the architectural elevations on approximately 10
      buildings in Sage Haven Phases 10 & 11. The proposed buildings are located generally north and east of
      the intersection of Owen Dr. and Jasper Ridge Dr. The applicant is Desert Color St. George, LLC and
      the representative is Bob Hermandson. The project is known as Visionary Sage Haven Phases 10 & 11.
      Case No. 2022-ZCA-043 (Staff – Dan Boles)

      Dan Boles presented the following:

      Dan Boles – There are 10 buildings with 42 units that are being affected by this application. The request
      is similar to one that we heard not too long ago. They are proposing to update the building elevations.
      The footprint, parking and layout all stay the same. Dan showed what was approved last year. They are
      asking for a desert territorial and a cottage which are both allowed by the Desert Color Board. Staff is
      recommending approval; it gives more variation to what was approved. The changes are minor and
      won’t affect the parking or roads.

      Commissioner Chapman – So will there be changes in bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage?

      Dan Boles – No.
