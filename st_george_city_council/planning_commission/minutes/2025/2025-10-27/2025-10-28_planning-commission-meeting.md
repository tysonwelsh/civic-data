MOTION:
     A motion was made by Planning Commission Member Casey to forward a
     positive recommendation to the City Council for the zone change for the St
     George Downtown Hyatt as presented. Including the staff recommendations
     that the height is increased to allow the additional height, as requested by
     the applicant and shown in the staff report. The applicant must combine the
     lots the site can be approved. And that a 15 foot landscape strip must be
     provided along the right of way on 600 East. And also that the use list be
     changed to only include Bar Establishment, Restaurant, Hotel/Motel.

SECOND:
     The motion was seconded by Planning Commission Member Fisher.

VOTE:
        Commission Chair Anderson called for a vote, as follows:

        Planning Commission Chair Anderson – aye

              Planning Commission Member Draper –aye

              The vote was unanimous. Motion carries.
