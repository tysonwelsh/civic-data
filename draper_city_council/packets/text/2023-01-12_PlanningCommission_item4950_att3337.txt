BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of an Amended Plat for approximately 3.66 acres
located at approximately 14886 South Traverse Ridge Rd. (Exhibit B & C). The property is
currently zoned C-2. The applicant is requesting that an Amended Plat be approved to
adjust the common property lines between Draper City and the Suncrest Homeowners
Association (HOA).

The application includes four parcels. One of the parcels houses the Ridge Restaurant,
another houses the HOA tennis/basketball courts, one is Draper City open space, and the
last one is undeveloped land owned by the HOA.

These properties are part of the Suncrest Development Agreement area and are subject to
the 1999 Draper City Municipal Code (1999 DCMC). The 1999 DCMC will be reviewed for
property standards in this application. That said, the 1999 DCMC does not have a plat
