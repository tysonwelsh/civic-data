A PROCLAMATION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL
HONORING COUNCIL MEMBER RICHARD SNELGROVE FOR 
HIS YEARS OF SERVICE AND EXPERTISE TO THE COUNTY
Page 1 of 18
County Council Meeting Minutes December 6, 2022
WHEREAS, Councilman Richard Snelgrove has served as a Salt Lake 
County Councilman since 2010 and was re -elected in 2016. He served as 
chair of the council in 2015 and 2019.
AND WHEREAS , Richard and his wife, JoLynn, have four children and 
their 10th grandchild is on the way. He is an accomplished business 
executive and entrepreneur, founding and managing several successful small 
businesses. He is president of Snelgrove Travel Centers Inc ., which recently 
celebrated 30 years in business. He also is involved in Snelgrove Ice Cream 
with his daughter, Lyndsay. 
AND WHEREAS , in his first year on the council he started holding Council 
on the Corner events at random times and at various county facilities. He 
engages voters who happen by to discuss a specific issue on which he wants 
input, or to just hear their general ideas and concerns. 
AND WHEREAS , he promised to withhold $ 150 million in county funding 
from UTA when its former leaders decided to close their board committee 
meetings to the public. Other council members joined the effort and the 
board reversed course. 
AND WHEREAS , Richard is known for saying, “What is the highest and 
best use?” and “Thank you for your service.”
AND WHEREAS , he dug into the County budget every year, asking his 
staff to spend significant time looking for ways to avoid a tax increase and 
improve efficiency. The Utah Taxpayers Association has recognized Richard 
with its prestigious Taxpayer Advocate of the Year Award "in recognition of 
his exemplary defense of taxpayers against tax increases and non -essential 
county spending." 
AND WHEREAS , Richard ’s fiscal conservatism has even carried over to 
other areas of his life. When one of his colleagues offered to take him to 
lunch, he chose Arctic Circle and was especially happy when there were 
coupons involved. 
AND WHEREAS , he was instrumental in organizing the Municipal Services 
District and served on its board for more than three years. He has also 
Page 2 of 18
County Council Meeting Minutes December 6, 2022
served on boards for the Jordan River Commission, Salt Lake County 
Library, Unified Fire Authority, Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District, 
Clark Planetarium, Utah Association of Counties and Salt Lake Council of 
Governments.
AND WHEREAS , Richard is an advocate of openness in government. He 
sponsored an ordinance requiring that bond elections be on the ballot only in 
general election years when voter turnout is greatest. He sponsored an 
ordinance requiring that a budget including any tax increase be presented 
before the general election. He proposed expanded citizen comment 
opportunities in Council meetings and spearheaded the new citizen call -in 
opportunity during Council meetings. 
AND WHEREAS , Richard is an outspoken advocate for clean air and 
water and recognizes these are vital for sustained quality of life. He planned 
and spearheaded Salt Lake County's participation with UTA for a "no fare " 
transit during the winter inversion months and sponsored a resolution in 
support of saving The Great Salt Lake. 
AND WHEREAS , Richard is an avid outdoorsman and loves to spend time 
in the canyons hiking and camping. He has advocated for open space and 
additional trails during his years of service. 
BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED  that the Salt Lake County Council 
celebrate Councilman Richard Snelgrove ’s 12 years of dedicated service and 
thank him for the impact he has made in Salt Lake County. He will be 
missed. Councilman Snelgrove, Thank YOU for YOUR service!
Dated this 6th day of December, 2022.

A PROCLAMATION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL
HONORING COUNCIL MEMBER STEVE DEBRY FOR HIS 
YEARS OF SERVICE AND EXPERTISE TO THE COUNTY
WHEREAS, Councilman Steve DeBry has served as a Salt Lake County 
Councilman since 2010, and was re -elected in 2012, 2014 and 2018. He 
served as chair of the council in 2013, 2017 and 2021.
 
AND WHEREAS , Steve and his wife, Lorraine, have three children and six 
grandchildren. A law enforcement professional for over four decades, Steve 
serves as Chief of the Millcreek Precinct of the Unified Police Department 
and has a career filled with public service as he has protected families, 
individuals and businesses.
AND WHEREAS , Steve graduated from the University of Utah in Political 
Science. Additionally, he has two associates degrees from Salt Lake 
Community College, graduating with honors. He also attended and graduated 
from the F .B.I. National Academy, F .B.I. Intermountain Executive Command 
College, West Point Leadership Academy, and the Utah Peace Officers 
Standards and Training Command College.
AND WHEREAS , Steve ’s knowledge of public safety has been an 
incredible asset to the county council. Whether it was during budget 
deliberations, or other policy decisions, his background and perspective 
were a valued part of the discussion. 
AND WHEREAS , when Salt Lake County had issues with their auditor a 
Page 4 of 18

decade ago, Steve was the council member who questioned adding 
additional employees to the budget and made the motion to remove the 
financial responsibilities from the auditor at the time, helping to transform that 
office in a positive way.
AND WHEREAS , he is known for his honesty, integrity, forthrightness, 
and putting the needs of others first. Steve is incredibly passionate, and 
deeply cares about the people he serves.
AND WHEREAS , Steve sometimes wears the coolest MacGyver leather 
jacket.
AND WHEREAS , he knew how to hire competent senior policy advisors . 
Brian Maxwell, Richard Jaussi and Kyler Brower provided Steve with 
valuable advice, but were also great assets to the council office and other 
council members. 
AND WHEREAS , Steve is a fiscal conservative through and through. He 
viewed his most important stewardship to be protecting precious and limited 
taxpayer dollars, ensuring an efficient, effective, and streamlined county 
government. During budget discussions he would propose ideas to save the 
county money, leverage resources in a more impactful way, and invest in 
items that would save taxpayer dollars over the long run.
AND WHEREAS , he was often heard using the phrase, “It’s nothing 
personal, it ’s business .” Steve knew what he was elected to do and though 
he sometimes had a tough exterior, he cared about people and was 
determined to represent his constituents in the best way possible.
Speaking of tough exterior …we are pretty certain Steve can bench press 
more than all the other council members combined. He is a regular at the 
gym. 
AND WHEREAS , Steve values responsible growth, public safety, 
transparent government, protection of our environment, and initiatives that 
improve the quality of life of families in the communities he represents.
AND WHEREAS , Steve always did things at the right time, for the right

was a big advocate for the west side of the valley and looked for ways to 
serve the cities he represented.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT PROCLAIMED : That the Salt Lake 
County Council honor Councilman Steve DeBry for his many years of 
service. He will be missed. He has served his constituents well and impacted 
Salt Lake County residents in a positive way during his tenure. Thank you, 
Councilman DeBry!

A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY 
COUNCIL REQUESTING RECERTIFICATION OF THE 
SALT LAKE COUNTY JUSTICE COURT.
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, Section 78A-7-103, UTAH CODE ANN ., requires Justice 
Courts be recertified at the end of each four-year term; and
Page 9 of 18
County Council Meeting Minutes December 6, 2022
WHEREAS, the current term of the Salt Lake County Justice Court shall 
expire in January 1, 2023; and 
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council has received an opinion letter 
from the District Attorney, which sets forth the requirements for the 
operation of a Justice Court and feasibility of continuing to maintain the 
same.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the County Council 
of Salt Lake County that it hereby requests recertification of the Salt Lake 
County Justice Court by the Justice Court Standards Committee and the 
Utah Judicial Council.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED by the County Council of Salt Lake County 
that it hereby affirms its willingness to continue to meet all requirements set 
forth by the Judicial Council for continued operation of the Salt Lake County 
Justice Court for the next four -year term of court, except as to any 
requirements waived by the Utah Judicial Council.  Nothing in this Resolution 
shall be construed to modify or amend the Council ’s previously stated intent 
to petition the Utah Legislature during the 2025 General Session to dissolve 
the Salt Lake County Justice Court and transfer its caseload to the Third 
Judicial District Court, as resolved in the Salt Lake County Council 
Resolution No 5986 passed and approved on June 21, 2022.

RECITALS
1. County owns a parcel of land located approximately at 1917 East 3300 
South, Salt Lake City, UT 84016, identified as Tax Id. No. 16-28-429-029 
(the “Property”), which was struck off to County after a tax sale on May 25, 
1988, and recorded on July 21, 1988, as Entry No. 4652412, in Book 6048,

2. Arthur Bruner (“Mr. Bruner), an adjacent landowner, has offered in 
writing to purchase the Property from the County for One Thousand Five 
Hundred Thirty Dollars ($1,530.00, the “Purchase Price ”), which amount has 
been approved by the Salt Lake County Real Estate Section as fair market 
value.
3. This offer is in the form of a Tax Salt Property Purchase Agreement 
“the “Agreement”), attached hereto as Exhibit a.
4. The County has determined that the Property is not in public use .  
Proceeds from the sale of the Property will e distributed in accordance with 
Utah Code Section 59-2-1351.5.

general public will be served by the sale and conveyance of the Property to 
Mr. Bruner.

statutes and County ordinances.

County Council that the Property, described in Exhibit 1 of the Agreement is 
hereby declared surplus property.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED by the Salt Lake County Council that the sale 
and conveyance of the Property by quitclaim deed to Arthur Bruner, as 
provided in the Agreement for the agreed appraised value of One Thousand 
Five Hundred Thirty Dollars ($1,530.00) is hereby approved; and the Mayor 
is hereby authorized to execute the Agreement, and the Mayor and County 
Clerk are herby authorized to execute the Quitclaim Deed, attached to the 
Agreement as Exhibit 2, and to deliver the fully executed documents to the 
County Real Estate Section for delivery to Arthur Bruner, upon payment of 
the agreed upon Purchase Price.
