Council Special Business

Salt Lake County Sheriff Presentation

                Sheriff Rosie Rivera delivered a PowerPoint presentation entitled: Re-
Established Law Enforcement Bureau (Model Contract for Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement
Service Area (SLVLESA). She reviewed transition goals of maintaining service levels and
taking care of the people providing services. She also spoke of the vision and SLVLESA-
specific goals.

              Chief Jason Ackerman, Sheriff’s Office, continued the presentation reviewing
general observations about the budget and the organizational chart; the SLVLESA model
contract budget; the proposed organizational chart for the re-established law enforcement
bureau; and the philosophy for partnership in contracting.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department Presentation

              Chief Jason Mazuran, Unified Police Department (UPD), delivered a PowerPoint
presentation entitled: Unified We Stand Stronger Together. He reviewed the Vision and Mission
Statement for Unified Police Greater Salt Lake (UPD); a draft of the current organizational chart
as of January 23, 2024; the importance of UPD providing local control; how UPD is expertise
based; SLVLESA Tax Revenue for 2023; and key reasons for working together with Copperton
Metro Township.

               Captain Del Craig, UPD, stated while UPD response times are not ideal, they
also are not bad considering the model under which UPD is working. Location and proximity drive
the issue. UPD has a commitment to continuing recent improvements.

Closed Session

               Council Member Clayton, seconded by Council Member Severson, moved to close

               Mayor Clayton stated during closed session, the Council decided to wait and
see what the final UPD Interlocal Agreement looks like. Once that has been reviewed, the
Copperton Metro Township Council will pass a resolution on February 21, 2024, to announce its
decision.

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated, for the record,
that there had been an Interlocal Drafting Committee meeting this morning that he attended
where removing SLVLESA as a member entity was discussed. The County has concerns about
SLVLESA being part of the interlocal agreement because it does not want to be responsible for
costs.
