Proposed Resolution 2025-13 amending the Budget for the Fiscal Year July
1, 2024 to June 30, 2025 by increasing the anticipated revenues and
appropriations for a gross increase of $3,358,000
          A memo from the City Finance Director regarding proposed amendments to the Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Redevelopment
Agency Budget came before the Board for consideration. The memo stated the proposed amendment includes recognizing
revenue to the Adams Community Reinvestment Area from bond proceeds ($3,358,000). The bond proceeds will be used to fund
issuance costs ($118,000) and the remaining amount due on the purchase of the Forest Service Building ($3,240,000). A prior
budget amendment authorized a reimbursement to the City of $360,000—the down payment from bond proceeds. The full amount
of bond proceeds totaled $3,718,000 – the purchase price of $3,600,000 plus the issuance costs ($118,000).
          Assistant Finance Director Jensen summarized the memo and presented a spreadsheet detailing the source of the revenue
for the proposed purchase of the Forest Service Building.
          Chair White introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2025-13, entitled:
                   “A resolution of the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency amending the budget for the Fiscal Year July 1, 2024
                   to June 30, 2025 by increasing the anticipated revenues for a gross increase of $3,358,000 from sources as
                   detailed in the body of this resolution; and increasing the appropriations for a gross increase of $3,358,000 as
                   detailed in the body of this resolution.”
          Chair White then opened the public hearing.
          Travis Pate stated he cares deeply about the Forest Service Building and the history of the building. He supports keeping
the building as an asset, preserving its history, and making it a vital part of the community.
          There being no further persons appearing to be heard, VICE CHAIR HYER MOVED THE PUBLIC HEARING BE
CLOSED. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER CHOBERKA, ALL VOTING AYE.
          ON A MOTION BY VICE CHAIR HYER AND SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER CHOBERKA,
RESOLUTION 2025-13 WAS ADOPTED UPON THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE – BOARD
MEMBERS BLAIR, CHOBERKA, GRAF, MYERS, RICHEY, VICE CHAIR HYER, AND CHAIR WHITE. VOTING
NO – NONE.
