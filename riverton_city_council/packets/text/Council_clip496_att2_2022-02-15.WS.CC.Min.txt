31            Administrative Law Judge Process – Ryan Carter, City Attorney
32
33   Ryan Carter, City Attorney, stated that he plans to adopt a whole new code for the
34   administrative law judge. He believes the City will see speedier results from using an ALJ. He
35   asked the Councilmembers to read Midvale’s code for an administrative law judge and said they
36   could expect something very much like it. He said he hopes the Council will adopt this new
37   code in March and he will then work with code enforcement to implement. He already has an
38   ALJ that has agreed to work with the City.
39
40   Mayor Staggs asked how the ALJ process differs from the criminal process. Mr. Carter
41   responded that code enforcement will have the ability to decide whether they treat the case as
42   an ALJ case or a criminal case. However, failure to comply with an ALJ order is a
43   misdemeanor.
44
45   Councilmember Buroker asked if there was more teeth from an ALJ order compared to a
46   criminal order. Mr. Carter explained that there is more attention placed on these cases which
47   usually results in more compliance. He explained that the ALJ would be used for property
48   maintenance issues, animal control issues and maybe stormwater.
49
50   Councilmember Buroker asked about sustainability. Mr. Carter said that the budget line item
51   will go up for the ALJ as the City grows into it.

 3            Riverton Police Department Presentation – Chief Don Hutson
 4
 5   Chief Don Hutson reported that six Riverton police officers who serve on the SWAT team were
 6   given a prestigious award by the South Jordan Police Department for their help in an incident in
 7   South Jordan. During this incident, a tactical team was needed to effect an arrest for a man that
 8   was significantly armed and opened fire on police when they approached in armored vehicles.
 9   The members of the Riverton SWAT team were instrumental in bringing this to a successful
10   resolution.
11
12   The South Valley SWAT team is a great example of shared services among communities.
13   There are members of Riverton, Herriman and South Jordan that serve on this SWAT team.
14   Last summer, the South Valley SWAT team competed in a competition and placed in the top
15   three. Over the Labor Day weekend, the South Valley SWAT team was able to help Unified
16   Police with an event where they needed some supplies and expertise that they did not have.
17   Riverton has a very capable SWAT team.
18
19   Mayor Staggs congratulated the Riverton officers that received this award and the picture of
20   these officers was shown on the screen. By leaving Unified Police Department, Riverton has
21   nine more officers for the same amount of money and was able to bring in some very capable
22   officers with a lot of experience and expertise.
23
24   Chief Hutson thanked the other departments in the City who have cooperated in dealing with
25   evidentiary and equipment issues. They have created two bays in the water department where
26   they can take vehicles to do investigations.
27
28   Chief Hutson said that vehicle burglaries went down during the month of January and that was
29   due to educating the public and patrolling the neighborhoods. They focused their efforts on
30   traffic citations and that can be seen in the number of citations that were issued during January.
31
32   Councilmember Stewart asked if there were officers patrolling on motorcycles. Chief Hutson
33   said that officers can enforce speed in a motorcycle or car. Councilmember Stewart also asked
34   about the kidnap line on the statistics. Chief Hutson said this was probably a misclassification
35   and he will find out.

17            Ordinance No. 22-08 – Vacating a Portion of Right-of-Way Located at
18            Approximately 3600 West 11820 South Consisting of 0.02 Acres (1,032 Sf) to the
19            Adjacent Property Owner, Proposed by Riverton City
20
21   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that this is the proposed vacation
22   of an unused portion of right-of-way on the west side of 3600 West just south of 11800 South.
23   Ivory Homes is developing the property adjacent to the right-of way on the west. The survey
24   work for that subdivision identified a gap between the back of sidewalk and the adjacent
25   property, which continues only for a short stretch before the property line moves to back of
26   sidewalk. This is not uncommon when survey work is updated. Neither the sidewalk nor the
27   travel lanes are affected, and as part of a dedicated public right-of-way, the resolution of this
28   situation    is   vacation    of    the    property    to    the    adjacent    land     owner.
29   The area to be dedicated consists of 1,032 square feet, or 0.02 acres.
30
31   Mayor Staggs called for public comments; there being none, he closed the Public Comment
32   period.
33
34   Councilmember McCay asked if it is traditional that the property line only go to the sidewalk so
35   that the sidewalk and the park strip is owned by the City. Mr. Lethbridge said the City’s right of
36   way begins at the back of the sidewalk. As part of the recreation of the plat for the rest of the
37   subdivision, that will be corrected so the line will shift inside the sidewalk and be part of the
38   public right of way.
39
40   Councilmember Stewart MOVED that the Riverton City Council adopt Ordinance No. 22-08
41   – Vacating a Portion of Right-of-Way Located at Approximately 3600 West 11820 South
42   Consisting of 0.02 Acres (1,032 Sf) to the Adjacent Property Owner. Councilmember
43   Buroker SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the motion. There
44   being none, he called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-yes, McCay-yes,
45   Stewart-yes and Wells-yes. The motion passed unanimously.
