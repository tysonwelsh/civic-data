Sara made a motion to approve the submittal of HAUC’s 5-Year Plan. April seconded 
the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
 
Each year HUD requires an annual plan be submitted on behalf of the agency. The annual 
plan must include updates on what the agency will be doing in the upcoming fiscal year. Every 
5th year HUD requires the agency to submit a 5-year plan to HUD; the updates are to include 
the addition or subtraction of any programs that are administered by the agency and are under 
HUD guidance and requirements. In addition, any administrative and operating functions that 
will be changing throughout the next 5 years. A narrative regarding the progress of the 
agency’s previous 5-year plan is also required to be submitted as part of the updated 5-year 
plan. A public comment period has been posted and the plan was made available to anyone 
wishing to review the agency’s 5-year plan. No public comment was received. The plan will be 
submitted to HUD within 45 days of the end of the fiscal year for the agency. 
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-10-03 Approve Cost of Living Adjustment 
Sara made a motion to approve the Cost of Living Adjustment. April seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC provides a Cost of Living Adjustment (COLA) to all staff annually when market 
conditions warrant and the Board of Commissioners approves. A COLA is a percentage of 
increase of the income designed to help staff keep up with the rising cost of goods and 
services due to inflation. The most common COLA in the United States is the Social Security 
Administration COLA which is announced in the fall of each year. At this time the 
announcement has not been officially announced, however, the increase is anticipated to be 
2.56%. It is the recommendation of the HAUC administration to support the same 2.56% 
COLA for all HAUC staff.
