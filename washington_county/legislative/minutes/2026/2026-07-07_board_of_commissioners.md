WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
JULY 7, 2026

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 9:00 a.m. on July 7, 2026, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist (via phone), Commissioner Victor
Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and
Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

INVOCATION BY: Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Bruce Miklautsch

CONSIDERATION OF CANVASS OF JUNE 23, 2026, PRIMARY ELECTION

Washington County Clerk/Auditor Ryan Sullivan read aloud the results from the June 23, 2026
Republican Primary Election. He reported 71,524 active voters, with 27,716 ballots cast for a voter
turnout of 38.75%.

(See attached report.)

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Accept the Primary Canvass as Reported.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3783, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
WASHINGTON _COUNTY’S GRANT OF CONSERVATION EASEMENT AND
PARTIAL RELEASE IN CONFLUENCE PARK AND AUTHORIZING EXPENDITURES
TO COMPLETE THE TRANSACTION

Jolene Rose, Wildlife Lands Specialist with the State of Utah, Department of Natural Resources,
Division of Wildlife Resources (UDWR) stated the UDWR holds a conservation easement on
Confluence Park. Washington County and UDWR previously entered into an agreement to release
approximately 1.457 acres of land from the conservation easement and add approximately 20.702
acres of land to the easement. This Resolution would finalize said conservation easement
transactions. Discussion was held regarding the language in the conservation easement and the
interpretation of each article within the agreement.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3783, A
Resolution Approving Washington County’s Grant of Conservation Easement
and Partial Release in Confluence Park and Authorizing Expenditures to
Complete the Transaction. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
