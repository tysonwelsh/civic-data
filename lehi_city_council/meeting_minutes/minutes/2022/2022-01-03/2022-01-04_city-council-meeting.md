Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council held Tuesday, January 4, 2022, at Lehi
City Hall, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at approximately 4:00 PM.

Present:     Mark Johnson, Mayor
             Paige Albrecht, Council Member
             Chris Condie, Council Member
             Paul Hancock, Council Member
             Katie Koivisto, Council Member
             Mike Southwick, Council Member

Others Present:     Jason Walker, City Administrator; Cameron Boyle, Assistant City
Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney; Kim Struthers, Community Development Director;
Lorin Powell, City Engineer; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Steve Marchbanks, Parks
Superintendent; Beau Thomas, Assistant to the City Administrator; Shaye Ruitenbeek,
Management Analyst; and Teisha Wilson, City Recorder;

1. Welcome and Opening Comment
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
   Koivisto attended virtually. Councilor Southwick offered the opening comment.

2. Swearing In Ceremony for Mayor Johnson, Councilor Condie and Councilor Hancock.
   The Mayor and Councilmembers were sworn in by Judge Cummings.

3. Discussion Items
   2.a. Draper Boundary Line Adjustment Discussion with DR Horton.
   DR Horton presented information on the annexation with Draper and Lehi. They will be re-
   submitting a plat with more information in that application. The Council and the applicant
   discussed the boundary adjustment and open space donation.

    2.b. General Plan Land Use Element Update
    Kim Struthers updated the Council members on the Comprehensive General Plan Amendment.
    He reported that anything dealing with a time limitation was removed from the text.

    Mike West, City Planer, explained the changes to the Transit Oriented Development zone and
    stated that they are choosing to allocate density to these zones.


3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time the meeting adjourned
   at approximately 5:45 p.m.
