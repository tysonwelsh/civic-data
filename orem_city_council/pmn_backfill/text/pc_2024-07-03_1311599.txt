 1                                 Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Julie Smith for the Planning Commission to forward
 2                                 a recommendation to the City Council to amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map
 3                                 of the City of Orem by rezoning the property located generally at 375 East 1600 North
 4                                 from the R8 zone to the PRD zone (approximately 2.30 acres) and amending Appendix
 5                                 KK of the Orem City code.
 6
 7                                 Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to rezone the current property from
 8                                 the R8 zone to the PRD zone to permit the building of 10 single family homes.
 9                                 Currently the parcel is zoned in the R8 zone, which is zoned to permit 7 lots instead of
10   the proposed 10. The proposed PRD zone would meet the requirements such as the height of the structures, the street
11   width, buffered sidewalks, parking stalls among other requirements as listed in the Orem City code. (Click here for
12   recording)
13
14   Recommendation: Staff Recommends the Planning Commission forward a recommendation to the City Council to
15   amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by rezoning the property located generally at 375
16   East 1600 North from the R8 zone to the PRD zone (approximately 2.30 acres) and amending Appendix KK of the
17   Orem City code.
18
