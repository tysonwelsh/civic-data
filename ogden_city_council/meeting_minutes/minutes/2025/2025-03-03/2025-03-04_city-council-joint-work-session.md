        The purpose of the joint work session was to receive the annual report from Ogden Downtown Alliance (ODA); receive a
zoning update and an administrative update; receive training regarding ethics, open meetings, and cyber security; discuss
Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board comments.

Agenda Review
        Chair White and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Ogden Downtown Alliance Annual Report
          The City Council has requested to receive information from local foundations and nonprofit organizations that have a
contractual agreement with Ogden City. These presentations help the Council to become more familiar with the objectives and
efforts of the organizations.
          Reide Thompson, Executive Director of Ogden Downtown Alliance (ODA), used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to
describe ODA’s organizational mission and goals, successes and challenges over the past year and any future plans for the
organization. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Brief discussion among the Council and Ms. Thompson centered on various special events held annually in the
community and the relationship between ODA and other community partners involved in producing those events.

          Mr. Rypien concluded the next steps in the Zone Ogden project are to continue monthly meetings with the Advisory
Committee and Planning Commission; refine special use chapters, development standards, and land divisions; proceed to final
draft of the document; and adopt the code updates. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the
meeting.

Administrative Update
          Mayor Nadolski provided an administrative update regarding communication between the City and leadership of the
Weber Housing Authority (WHA) regarding their proposed permanent housing project in Ogden City. He has discussed the
concept of Ogden City purchasing the subject property from WHA and WHA has asked for expediency relative to that element.
He asked Community Development Manager Smith to provide detailed information regarding the option to purchase the property.
Mr. Smith stated he is working on a formal transmittal to be submitted to the City Council regarding an option to purchase the
Aspen Care Center property from WHA; negotiations have been underway regarding the terms of the acquisition and funding that
has been set aside for infill development in this area of the City could be used for the acquisition. The transaction will be
expedited in order to allow WHA to protect the funding they have invested in the project; the City is seeking to ‘make them
whole’ so they can pursue other housing projects and provide services to the community.
          Discussion among the Council and Administration centered on which entity of the City will be the party to a real estate
purchase contract (REPC) for the property and the availability of Redevelopment Agency (RDA) funding for the acquisition.
          Chair White thanked Mayor Nadolski and other members of City Administration for their transparency and expediency
relative to this matter.

Council Training
          Open Meeting and Ethics: City Attorney Williams used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide the Council with
required training regarding the Open and Public Meetings Act (OPMA) and Ethics Act for the State of Utah. For a copy of the
presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Cyber Security: IT Manager Chapman used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide the Council with required
training regarding cyber security. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.


Council/Board Business
        Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reviewed the Council calendar for the month of March.

Council/Board Comments.
         Council member Choberka reported on her attendance at the Utah Renewable Energy Board Meeting yesterday; the
Board is still working, but the timeline for pursuing renewable energy measures has been pushed to April of 2026 and cities are
advised to include funding in their next fiscal year (FY) budget for community outreach regarding the measures.
