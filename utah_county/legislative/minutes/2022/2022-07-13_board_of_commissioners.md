Drone Update

2022 Season

History is repeating itself.

* In 2015 we came to the decision makers in the County with an idea for
testing mosquito population for West Nile and other mosquito borne
diseases.

* With a qPCR machine and the lab to support it we have been able to protect the
public in a timely fashion.

* Also, we have been able to develop innovative whys to use the machine to better
understand and control mosquitoes in the county.

* All at a savings of local tax dollars.

* We come to you again with an innovative idea for your consideration.

The idea we would like to explore with you is
The use of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles or Drones

* This innovation, that we are exploring with
you today, has the potential to:

* Save local tax dollars.

* Create efficiencies and effectiveness (ex:
reduce labor hours, better method of
delivery, etc.)

* Help us fulfill our mission as a Health
Department.

* Potentially help other departments in the
County improve services to the public.

2. REVIEW THE DRAFT EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT POLICY
-Rudy Livingston, Clerk/Auditor

Timestamp: 28:12

RUDY LIVINGSTON, BUDGET MANAGER - WHEN I STARTED WITH UTAH COUNTY
OF COUPLE OF YEARS AGO, I DID A TOUR OF SEVERAL OF THE DEPARTMENT
FACILITIES, AND IT BECAME VERY APPARENT THAT WE HAD A PROBLEM THAT
WE NEEDED TO ADDRESS AND THAT WAS THAT THERE WERE MANY ITEMS OF
EQUIPMENT THAT THERE WAS NO MONEY BEING SET ASIDE FOR FUTURE
REPLACEMENT. I BROUGHT THAT BACK TO OUR DEPARTMENT, AND WE
STARTED TALKING ABOUT THIS A BOUT AS WE GOT INTO OUR BUDGET
DISCUSSIONS AND WORKING WITH COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER WHO
WAS THE CLERK/AUDITOR AT THE TIME. WE TALKED ABOUT SOME WAYS THAT
WE CAN RESOLVE THIS PROBLEM AND WE DECIDED THAT IT WOULD BE
ADVANTAGEOUS FOR THE COUNTY TO PROVIDE FOR THE COUNTY TO PROVIDE
FOR THE FUTURE, REPLACEMENT OF ALL EQUIPMENT. WE DETERMINED THAT
WE HAVE SEVERAL DIFFERENT TYPES OF ASSETS (CAPITAL ASSETS WHICH WE’VE
ALREADY STARTED PUTTING TOGETHER A CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM,
VEHICLES, VARIOUS EQUIPMENT). SO, WE HAVE BEEN WORKING TO PUT
TOGETHER A POLICY THAT WOULD GOVERN THE OPERATIONS PROGRAM WHICH
HAS A FEW MAIN OBJECTIVES:

1. STANDARDIZE REPLACEMENT CRITERIA AND GUIDELINES FOR
EQUIPMENT,

2. MECHANISM BY WHICH FUNDING FOR EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT IS
AVAILABLE IN ADVANCE OF THE NEED AND THROUGH ESTABLISHMENT OF
A REPLACEMENT RESERVE IN THE INTERNAL SERVICE FUND,

3. PROVIDE DEPARTMENTS WITH RELIABLE AND UP-TO-DATE EQUIPMENT
TO ASSIST THEM IN MEETING THEIR NEEDS,

4. PROVIDE FOR A TIMELY ACQUISITION AND DISPOSING OF EQUIPMENT TO
MAXIMIZE RESOURCES AVAILABLE FOR MEETING THE EQUIPMENT NEEDS
AND TO SMOOTH THE OUTFLOW OF FUNDING FOR ESSENTIAL EQUIPMENT
AND THE ROTATION OF INCOME,

5. REDUCE THE MAINTENANCE COSTS BY ELIMINATING THE OLD EXPENSIVE
EQUIPMENT.

THIS WOULD APPLY TO ASSETS THAT INDIVIDUALLY COSTS OVER $5,000 AND
THAT HAVE A LIFESPAN OF 4 YEARS OF MORE AND THAT ARE NOT PURCHASED
