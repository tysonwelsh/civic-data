18.145.100 Disabled Accessible Parking.

(1) Disabled Accessible Parking Stalls to Be Provided. Accessible parking spaces Handicapped shall be
provided in off-street parking lots and shall count towards fulfilling the minimum requirements for
automobile parking.


(2) Location and Marking. Physically handicapped parking spaces shall be located as near as practical to
a primary building entrance with access ramps negotiable for all handicapped equipment. Each parking
space shall be identified by a permanently affixed reflective sign and/or surface identification painting
depicting the standard symbol for handicapped parking. Accessible parking spaces shall located, marked
and signed according to current requirements and standards of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).


(3) Number of Stalls. The number of handicapped accessible parking spaces shall conform to the current
minimum requirements of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) listed in the following table:
