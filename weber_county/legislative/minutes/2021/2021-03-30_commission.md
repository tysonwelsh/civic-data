G. ACTION ITEMS:

     1. AGREEMENT WITH SNOW CHRISTENSEN & MARTINEAU FOR LEGAL REPRESENTATION IN EMINENT
           DOMAIN CASES

           Courtlan Erickson, Deputy County Attorney, stated that this item related to four parcels along 3600
           West. Negotiations stalled and the Commission’s direction to the Attorney’s Office was to move
           forward with eminent domain. Mr. Erickson addressed Commissioner Froerer’s question stating that
           bids were received, the County Attorney’s Office had contacted three firms in accordance with the
           County Purchasing Code and determined that the firm of Snow Christensen & Martineau would best
           serve the county. This firm had worked for the county for eminent domain in the past. Chair Harvey
           indicated his distaste for eminent domain and stated that the county made every effort to work with
           the property owners and for a very long time.
           Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the agreement with Snow Christensen & Martineau for
           legal representation in eminent domain cases; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
           Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
