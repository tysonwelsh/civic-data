REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

PRESENTATION: 2021 WASHINGTON COUNTY FAIR SUMMARY

Washington County Fair Specialist Susi Lafaele presented a slide show of highlights from the 2021
Washington County Fair. The fair moved from August to April for the first time and overall was
very successful, with the demolition derby, boxing, MMA, and many concerts selling out. A new
carnival company was brought in which brought in almost $400,000 in revenue vs. $130,000 in
years past. Other changes highlighted included a change in charging a parking fee to charging a
gate admission, which doubled revenue.

PUBLIC HEARING: COMMUNITY _DEVELOPMENT_BLOCK GRANT (CDBG),
SECOND HEARING

Commissioner Almquist asked for a motion to open the Community Development Block Grant
(CDBG), second Hearing.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Open the Public Hearing to Receive
Comments on the Community Development Block Grant (CDBG), Second
Hearing. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

Commissioner Almquist opened the second public hearing for the CDBG program. Commissioner
Almquist stated that the hearing is called to allow all citizens to provide input concerning the project
that was awarded under the 2021 Community Development Block Grant Program. Washington
County has amended its capital investment plan, which is available for interested persons to review,
and has decided to apply for funds on behalf of the Angel Springs Special Service District, Jean
Wojtyla will be the project manager for this project.

The application was successful in the regional rating and ranking process and Angel Springs has
been awarded $44,686. Funds will be used for water system improvements for the Angel Springs
Special Service District. This project is on the Capital Improvements List, which is available at the

Washington County office.

Commissioner Almquist asked anyone with questions, comments or suggestions during the hearing
to please identify themselves by name before they speak and stated the clerk will include names in
the minutes, in order to specifically respond to questions and suggestions during the hearing.

After waiting and seeing no one else come forward to speak, Chairman Almquist asked for a motion
to close the Public Hearing.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to close the Public Hearing. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE __NO.___2021-1176-O, _AN _ORDIANANCE
ESTABLISHING TEMPORARY LAND USE REGULATION (MORATORIUM) ON
APPLICATIONS, APPROVALS OR PERMITTING OF “TOURIST HOMES” IN ALL
ZONES AND ALL UNINCORPORATED AREAS OF WASHINGTON COUNTY

Deputy Attorney Victoria Hales stated that the impact of tourist homes throughout Washington
County is very broad on both a zoning and economic standpoint. Ms. Hales further explained that
zoning regulations need to be updated to allow for greater management of property rights.

Commissioner Iverson stated there is a compelling interest to pass this ordinance.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2021-1176-O, An
Ordinance Establishing Temporary Land Use Regulation (Moratorium) On
Applications, Approvals or Permitting of “Tourist Homes” in All Zones and
All Unincorporated Areas of Washington County. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
