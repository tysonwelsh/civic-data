PUBLIC HEARINGS
A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION SV-2-2021,
     FILED BY WEST VALLEY CITY, REQUESTING A VACATION OF A
     PUBLIC ALLEY BETWEEN WARNOCK AVENUE AND STRATFORD
     AVENUE AT 1685 WEST
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled October 19, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear
     and consider public comments regarding application SV-2-2021, Filed by West
     Valley City, Requesting a Vacation of a Public Alley Between Warnock Avenue and
     Stratford Avenue at 1685 West.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              The West Valley City Engineering Division is requesting consideration to
              vacate the 16-foot alley located between Warnock Avenue and Stratford
              Avenue. The location of the alley is approximately 1685 West between
              2525 and 2565 South.

              The alley is located within the Chesterfield Plat. This plat was recorded
              with the County Recorder’s Office in July 1914. The plat identifies a 16-
              foot alley along the east property line of those properties fronting Redwood
              Road. It is presumed that this alley was to provide service capabilities for
              properties that would be used for commercial purposes.

              While the idea behind this was well intended, these alleys have not been
              utilized for that purpose. To staffs knowledge, these alleys have never been
              formally improved. As a common practice, many residents living adjacent
              to these alleys have fenced this area off. In some instances, residents have
              used the alley as part of their yard and for parking. The vacation of these
              alleys generally allows for property owners to take better care of these areas
              than has previously been done.

              Per State Law, when a street or alley is vacated, each abutting owner will
              end up with half of the vacated property. In this case, 8 feet of additional
              property would then belong to the property owners on the east as well as the
              west side of the alley. If a property owner did not want their portion, they
              could negotiate transferring their portion to the adjacent landowner.

              In the case of this particular alley, there is an existing storm drain line. The
              City will request that an easement be recorded that will accommodate the
              storm drain line. This will be done with the vacating ordinance that ends
              up being recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office.

          reviewed by the Planning Commission with a recommendation to the City
          Council.

   Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if there are any concerns regarding the alley as noted

   survey the property if the Council directs. Councilmember Huynh asked how much
   each property owner would add to their property. Steve replied just over 2,000 square

RESOLUTION 21-179: ADOPT THE 2021 ANNUAL REPORT ON THE
MODERATE INCOME HOUSING PLAN
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 21-179 that would adopt the 2021 Annual
Report on the Moderate Income Housing Plan.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

   Utah Code Section 10-9a-408 requires the legislative body of municipalities to
   annually prepare a report outlining a review of their Moderate Income Housing Plan.
   The report must address the following:

          “a revised estimate of the need for moderate income housing in the
           municipality for the next five years;
          a description of progress made within the municipality to provide moderate
           income housing, demonstrated by analyzing and publishing data on the
           number of housing units in the municipality that are at or below:
               o 80% of the adjusted median family income;
               o 50% of the adjusted median family income; and
               o 30% of the adjusted median family income;
          a description of any efforts made by the municipality to utilize a moderate
           income housing set-aside from a community reinvestment agency,
           redevelopment agency, or community development and renewal agency; and
          a description of how the municipality has implemented any of the
           recommendations related to moderate income housing described in Subsection
           10-9a-403(2)(b)(iii).”

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City
Council, and he called for a motion.

       Councilmember Christensen moved to approve Resolution 21-179.

       Councilmember Huynh seconded the motion.

        Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 21-28 that would authorize the City
        to enter into an Easement Purchase Agreement and accept a Perpetual Easement
        and a Temporary Construction Easement with and from Oscar and Refugio Perez
        for property located at 4245 South 2735 West.

        Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
        information as follows:

               The Kim Yoeun Yong and Sumala Yong parcel located at 4691 S.
               Woodgrove Drive is one of several parcels affected by the 4700 South
               Reconstruction Project (4000 West to 5600 West). This project is being
               constructed in cooperation with Kearns Metro Township. This project will
               reconstruct the pavement, curb, gutter, sidewalk and decorative street
               lighting on both sides of 4700 South and 4715 South to improve safety and
               ride quality of 4700 South and 4715 South. Compensation for the purchase
               of the Warranty Deed, Perpetual Easement and a Temporary Construction
               Easement and improvements is $11,700.00 based upon the Appraisal
               Report prepared by Van Drimmelen Associates, Inc.

               The project is being funded under the Surface Transportation Program
               (STP) with federal funds. Kearns Metro Township executed the Federal
               Aid Agreement with UDOT. West Valley City has signed an Interlocal
               Agreement with Kearns Metro Township. The city is responsible for 6.77%
               of all project costs, including right of way, within West Valley City
               jurisdictional limits. The total value for the Warranty Deed, Perpetual
               Easement and a Temporary Construction Easement is $11,700.00, making
               the West Valley City share of the acquisition $792.09.
