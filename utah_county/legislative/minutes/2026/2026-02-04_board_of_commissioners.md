1. RECOGNITION OF FEBRUARY 2026 EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH.

-P] Maughan, Human Resources

Commissioner Beltran announced Brandon Davis, from the Information Systems
Department, had been chosen as employee of the month. He read the following
statement.

Since joining Utah County in 2022, Brandon Davis has dedicated nearly four years to
providing exemplary service within the Information Systems Department. Brandon
started as a Project Manager working with stake holders, developers, and Information
Systems leadership to plan, track, and ensure projects were delivered on time and met
the needs of the stakeholders.

Recently promoted to Project Management Supervisor, Brandon now oversees the
broader portfolio of projects and mentors other project managers. Brandon has been
instrumental in developing the Project Management team into a more refined,
responsible, and capable team by bringing his experience and talents to the table.

Throughout his tenure, Brandon has maintained his Project Management certifications
and actively advocates for his team to pursue professional development and certification.

He demonstrates a strong commitment to hard work, communicates with
professionalism, and brings deep expertise to every project. His contributions improve
operational efficiency and strengthen the trust the citizens of Utah County place in our
work.

Brandon is an excellent organizer, but what truly sets him apart is his people-first,
practical approach. IT projects are often complex and full of technical details, yet
Brandon has arare talent for making the complicated feel simple. He explains things in
clear, plain language—never condescending—so everyone feels included and informed.
Both technical teams and stakeholders trust him and feel comfortable working with him.

I won't go into details, but his dance skills are top notch!

I nominate Brandon Davis, Project Management Supervisor from the Programming
division of Information Systems! His dedication, leadership, and commitment to
excellence make him an invaluable asset to Utah County.

-Nominated by Jeff Wilkinson, Patrick Wawro, and James Longhurst
