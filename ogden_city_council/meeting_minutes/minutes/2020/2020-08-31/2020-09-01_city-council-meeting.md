Proposed Zone Text Amendments - Allow online auto sales in the C-3
Zone
         Ogden City Planning is proposing to amend the Regional Commercial (C-3) zone to allow online automobile sales as a
permitted use. The proposed amendment includes standards that address parking and vehicle condition and which prohibit
signage, maintenance, and repair of vehicles. Currently, new and used auto sales are allowed in the C-3 zone as permitted and
conditional uses respectively.
         Planning Manager Montgomery used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize the proposed text amendments.
Staff has received several requests for the City to allow online car sales as a permitted use in the C-3 commercial zone to give
business owners increased options. The sale ofnew or used cars is prohibited in the C-1 and C-2 zones, but is pennitted in the C-3
zone. Car dealerships are experiencing an increase in online traffic and there is a demand for online sales. Staff feels this use could
be appropriate in the City if certain standards can be met, including the following:
               •   Office space shall be located only in a commercial building.
               •    Space for the vehicles shall be on the same site as the office and vehicles parked on site shall not have any
                   attached signage, writing on the windows, or advertising to promote the sale of the vehicle. The parking area
                   shall be treated as a normal office parking and shall not have any promotional banners or temporary signage as
                    defined in Title 18 of the Ogden Municipal Code.
               •   On-site parking for dealership owned vehicles shall be limited to a minimum of 3 stalls and a maximum of
                   6 stalls. On-site parking will be subject to meeting the provisions of chapter 12 of this title.
               •   On-site parking stalls shall not be used for automobile detailing, maintenance, or repair including but not
                    limited to paint, body and fender, brake, muffler, upholstery or transmission work.
               •    On-site parking stalls for the online sales shall be used to park functioning, operable vehicles for retail sales.
                   Vehicles parked on the site for sale or potential sale shall not have any appearance of damage and be in good
                    operating condition and physical appearance. No noticeable damage including but not limited to broken
                   windows, extensive body rust, flat tires, missing bumpers, or body damage is allowed.
         The Planning Commission considered whether the proposed ordinance amendment is consistent with General Plan and
zoning policies; they found consistency and that the amendment will fulfill the purpose of the C-3 zone, which is to provide for
the sale and supply of the complete range of retail and wholesale goods. However, they felt it appropriate to further defme
automobile maintenance and repair that is prohibited on-site. They voted unanimously to recommend approval of the ordinance
amendments to allow online sales with the five conditions outlined in the ordinance.
          Council discussion of the proposal centered on matters such as enforcement, licensing, and whether this type of use could
be a catalyst for other dealership models - such as a 'vehicle vending machine'. Council Deputy Director Symes indicated that
Council staff will work to publicly notice the matter for action later this month.

Engineering Standards
          Traffic flow, Refuse carts, Public right-of-way, As built project plans, Street lighting, and Storm water: Ogden City
Administration is proposing ordinance amendments to modify sight triangle (clear view triangle) standards that are used to prevent
visual obstructions at intersections, refuse cart placement requirements, and storm water regulations.
          Assistant City Engineer Nelson used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide the Council with an overview of the
proposed adjustments to the City's Engineering Standards. He noted that in 2019, the State Legislature amended State code to
require the City Council to hold a public hearing whenever the City is considering enacting or adjusting a land use regulation; this
includes adjustments to Engineering Standards. Relative to as-built plans, the recommendation is to amend the standards to
include a requirement for the plans to be submitted to the City. Staff also recommends adding a new requirement for the design,
construction, and inspection of streetlights to be installed. Relative to the sight triangle, the standards document will include
language calling for the review of potential traffic conflicts. Responsive to a State audit, the City is recommending stonn water
updates to the City ordinance and engineering standards to prohibit illegal discharges and to update the stonn water quality
permits requirements. Additional stonn water updates in the engineering standards include an added requirement for water
treatment for new developments, updated detention/retention facilities along with injection wells, low impact design
considerations, and added dispute resolution process related to low impact design items. Roadway design amendments include
updated winter work requirements, road width requirements consistent with the International Fire Code, and updated the term
"exclusive water/wastewater utility space". The fmal roadway design issue relates to collection of refuse carts; the following
language will be added to the standards: "provided that a customer located on a street with asphalt pavement width of less than
twenty (20) feet or on a street with a concrete apron or approach shall place the automated catt at the curb of the nearest street
with a pavement width of twenty feet or greater."
          Chair Choberka inquired as to the number of customers that will be impacted by the adjustment to refuse catt collection
standards. Mr. Nelson answered 133 residents on 19 separate roadways in the City. Chair Choberka asked how the homeowners

action on all other proposed amendments as soon as possible and then delay action on the refuse cart matter until after
