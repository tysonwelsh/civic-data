AMENDED PLAT (Administrative). Review a request for an amended plat for the Homespun
Village Subdivision near Leeds. The proposed plat amendment will affect Parcels HOSV-2-A-HV,

HOSV-3-HV and HOSV-4-HV. The applicant is Georgia Tanner.

years ago and stated that the subdivision is located near Leeds. He explained that the applicants
would like to remove a utility easement that is no longer in use. The easement was previously

and conditions of Staff. Ms. Hales verified with Mr. Edwards the location of the easement and
water tank.

ZONE CHANGE / PUBLIC HEARING (Legislative). Review and hold a public hearing for a zone

change request from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the A-5
(Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone for Parcel 4039-A-HV, generally located along Hidden
Valley Road near Leeds. The applicant is Don Parker.

Mr. Messel explained the location of the parcel, just outside of Leeds, near the cemetery. He
reported that the property is currently zoned A-20 but is not 20 acres in size. The applicants would
like to move forward with development of a vineyard and a conditional use permit in the future
for agritourism and therefore need to bring the property into zoning compliance. The applicant is
dedicating the portion of road that would be used for access to the parcel. The applicant has
acquired a small amount of property from the adjoining parcel to keep the parcel acreage at the
required 5 acres. Mr. Messel proceeded to read the findings and conditions of Staff.

ORDINANCE AMENDMENT / PUBLIC HEARING FOR PROPOSED OVERLAY ZONE IN THE

PINE VALLEY AREA (Legislative). Review, hold a public hearing and consider approval of a
proposed overlay zone in the Pine Valley area.

Mr. Messel explained that the residents of Pine Valley have formed a committee and have been
working with a private planner to draft a proposed overlay zone for the Pine Valley Area. The
original draft was presented to the Planning Commissioners for feedback and the draft is ready to
move forward with a public hearing at this time.

Ms. Nelson proceeded to give a presentation concerning the overlay and the resident’s desires
with the proposed overlay. She explained changes made to the draft requested by residents and
suggested by the Planning Commission.

Commissioner Anderson and Acting Chairman Owens thanked Ms. Nelson for her work and the
community’s work towards this ordinance. Discussion was had between Ms. Nelson and the
Planning Commissioners about the draft ordinance.

Acting Chairman Owens opened the Public Hearing.

Ms. Cathleen Neilson spoke about the growth and the history of the valley.

Ms. Maxine Davey spoke about Pine Valley’s uniqueness and history. She stated that she agrees

Mr. Bob Daley spoke about the minimum lot size, open space requirements, home size and the

Mr. Brian Davy spoke about the development review board and how the process is proposed to
work.

Mr. Bryan Thiriot, via Zoom, asked about previous applications and whether they will be processed
under the ordinance they were applied under. He also expressed support of the night sky.

Mr. Jack Rolf spoke about the openness of the process in drafting the proposed overlay and is
grateful for the effort put into it.

Mr. Todd Edwards, County Engineer, expressed concern about speed limits. State law states that
25 mph is lawful in any urban district and questions the legality of the 15 mph speed limit on the
dirt roads. He clarified that paving is done by the landowners and the county fully supports that
and will help as much as possible. Road maintenance was discussed.

Mr. Larry Gardner expressed support for the dirt roads and concern for dust control.
Mr. Steve Shakespeare commented on fire concerns in the Pine Valley area.

Mr. Jake Burningham stated concerns over the proposed 15 mph speed limit. He explained that
he is not in favor of regulations and feels left out of the drafting process by the committee.

Acting Chairman Owens closed the Public Hearing after all who wished to speak had the
opportunity to do so.

Commissioner Anderson agreed that the 15 mph speed is not needed. He stated speed limits are
controlled by engineering standards and may not be appropriate in Title 10, Zoning Regulations.

MOTION: Commissioner Anderson made a motion to RECOMMEND that the County

modifications. The motion was SECONDED by Commissioner Gaston. The motion PASSED.
