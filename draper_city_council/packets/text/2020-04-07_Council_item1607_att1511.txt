                                        ORDINANCE NO. 1425

         AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL LAND USE MAP OF DRAPER
         CITY FOR APPROXIMATELY 26.9 ACRES OF PROPERTY FROM
         RESIDENTIAL LOW/MEDIUM DENSITY AND COMMUNITY COMMERCIAL
         TO BUSINESS/LIGHT MANUFACTURING, LOCATED ON THE GREENFIELD
         FARMS SUBDIVISION (ALL OF WHEATFIELD CIRCLE; ALL OF
         WHEATFIELD WAY; 79, 101, & 123 W GOLDEN HARVEST ROAD) WITHIN
         DRAPER CITY, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE EPG PONY EXPRESS
         DEVELOPMENT LAND USE AMENDMENT.

        WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a General Plan and Land Use Map to
guide the future development within the City; and

        WHEREAS, from time to time it is necessary to review and amend the Land Use Map to keep
pace with development within the City and to ensure the provision of a variety of economic uses; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed land use change set forth herein has been reviewed by the Planning
Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been held in accordance with
Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed revisions to the Land Use Map; and

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to the City
Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Land Use Map of Draper City, and the City
Council has found the proposed land use change to be consistent with the City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

        Section 1.    Land Use Amendment. The following described real property located at the
Greenfield Farms Subdivision (all of Wheatfield Circle; all of Wheatfield Way; 79, 101, & 123 W Golden
Harvest Road) within Draper City, Salt Lake County, State of Utah, previously designated Residential
Low/Medium Density and Community Commercial as shown on the Draper City Land Use Map, as
depicted in Exhibit “A” hereto, are hereby changed to the Business/Light Manufacturing land use
designation:

       A part of the East Half of Section 36, Township 3 South, Range 1 West, Salt Lake Base and
Meridian, U.S. Survey in Salt Lake County, Utah:

                 Beginning at the Northeast Corner of Lot 1, Greenfield Farms Subdivision located
2332.04 feet South 0°04’18” West along the Section Line; and 155.40 feet North 89°57’42” West from
the Northeast Corner of said Section 36; said point of beginning is also Located 2329.52 feet South
0°13’21” West along the monument line; and 40.60 feet North 89°46’29” West from an existing Salt
Lake County monument marking the West Witness Corner to said Northeast Corner of Section 36; said
Witness Corner monument is Located 5309.76 feet North 0°13’21” East from an existing Salt Lake
County monument marking the West Witness Corner to the Southeast Corner of said Section 36; and
running thence South 0°12’42” West 983.14 feet along the East Line of said Subdivision to the Southeast
Corner thereof; thence North 89°49’42” West 832.619 feet along the South Line of said Subdivision to an
angle point; thence North 89°39’42” West 422.83 feet to the Easterly Line of the new alignment of road;
thence along said Easterly Line the following two courses: North 33°41’28” West 75.10 feet to a point of
curvature; and Northerly along the arc of a 712.18 foot radius curve to the right a distance of 12.68 feet
(Central Angle equals 1°01’12” and Long Chord bears North 33°10’51” West 12.68 feet) to a point on the
