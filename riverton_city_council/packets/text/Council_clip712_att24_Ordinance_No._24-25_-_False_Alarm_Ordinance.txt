                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   ORDINANCE NO. 24-25

   AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE TITLE 5, CHAPTER 30,
    SECTIONS 010 TO 140; RENUMBERING SAME TO TITLE 9, CHAPTER 35,
      SECTIONS 010 TO 140; AND AMENDING PROVISIONS RELATED TO
                ADMINISTRATION, FEES AND PENALTIES

                                    [CITY ALARM CODE]

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is charged with the duty to protect the health, safety and
welfare of its community; and

       WHEREAS, public safety services provided by Riverton City rely, in part, on receiving
communications from alarm service providers in the event an alarm is triggered in a Riverton
business or residence; and

        WHEREAS, conversely, public safety services provided by Riverton City are adversely
affected in the event a false alarm communication is relayed to the Riverton Police Department
from a Riverton business or residence; and

       WHEREAS, false alarm communications are often the product of malfunctioning alarm
systems which are not repaired in a timely manner, or repeated operator error, and should therefore
be regarded as avoidable problems; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Code Sections 5.30.010 to 140 (the City Alarm Code) regulates
the use of fire alarm systems which alert the Riverton Police Department through
telecommunications; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the City Alarm Code
needs to be updated to enable a renewed effort by the Riverton Police Department to reduce the
number of false alarm calls it receives annually; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the following action is
in the best interest of the City’s health, safety and general welfare.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

Section 1.     Amendment. The following Code Section is hereby amended:

               Title 5, Chapter 30, Sections 010 to 140 is hereby renumbered as Title 9,
               Chapter 35, Sections 010 to 140, respectively.

Section 2.     Adoption. Title 9, Chapter 35, Sections 010 to 140, is hereby reenacted and
               adopted as provided in the Attached Exhibit A.
