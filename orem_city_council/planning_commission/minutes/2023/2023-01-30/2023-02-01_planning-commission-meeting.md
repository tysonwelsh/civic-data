Chair Sakar asked Ms. Wagstaff to introduce Agenda Item 3.1: (click here for recording)

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by J5 Infrastructure Partners for AT&T for the City to approve the Conditional Use
Permit of an unmanned telecommunications facility to be collocated on a field light at Orem High School located at
175 South 400 East in the R8 zone.

Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to replace the existing field light pole at Orem High School with a
100’ tall monopole. The field light bank would be re-installed at the existing height on the new pole. AT&T
antennas and equipment would be installed at the top of the pole including a lightning rod. The installation would
also include an equipment shelter to be built near the base of the monopole.

The applicant has applied for a Conditional Use Permit due to the proposed pole being located in a residential zone
and the request for the increased height of the pole. Monopoles are allowed to be 75’ high, but in the circumstance
of need for greater coverage or to allow for collocation the height may be increase up to 110’. In this instance the
applicant is requesting a pole of 100’ be approved.

Monopoles are required to be located at least 115% of the poles height from any residential lot line. The proposed
location of this monopole at Orem High School is at least 400’ from the surrounding residential lots.

Based on the need for service determined by the applicant for this area, the location of the monopole was selected to
be collocated using a stadium light at Orem High School. As there is a pole already at this location, the proposed
monopole would be considered to be utilizing an existing structure per Orem City Code (22-13-9), which outlines
the priority given to locations for wireless telecommunication facilities. In considering the approval of a Conditional
Use Permit, the factors to be considered are outlines in Orem City Code (22-4-4).

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to
approve the Conditional Use Permit of an unmanned telecommunications facility to be collocated on a field light at
Orem High School located at 175 South 400 East in the R8 zone.

Chair Sakar asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Wagstaff.

Chair Sakar invited the applicant to come forward. Jason Evans, J5 Infrastructure Partners/AT&T, introduced himself.
Mr. Evans stated that he had been working with the school district to come up with a location for the tower. The school
has wanted a new concession stand for a while. The school district felt the location of the new cell tower above the
stadium lights was the best location for two reasons: 1) An existing concession stand will be removed and a new one
constructed. 2) Best location for service coverage taking into consideration the existing Verizon cell tower on the east
side of the football field. Public properties provide the best opportunities for the best coverage.

There was further discussion regarding the location and height of the new pole.

Chair Sakar asked if the Planning Commission had any more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did, he
called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Low moved to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to
approve the Conditional Use Permit for an AT&T telecommunication monopole facility located on a field light at
Orem High School located generally at 175 South 400 East in the R8 zone. Mr. Hawkes seconded the motion. Those
voting aye: Mike Carpenter, Gerald Crismon, James Hawkes, Helena Kleinlein, Madeline Komen, Murray Low and
Haysam Sakar. The motion passed unanimously.
