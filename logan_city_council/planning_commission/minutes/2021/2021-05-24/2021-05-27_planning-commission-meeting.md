                                  PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES
                                              Meeting of May 27, 2021
                            Logan City Council Chambers (290 N 100 W Logan, UT 84321) www.loganutah.org

Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session on
Thursday, May 27, 2021. Chair Dickinson called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Roylan Croshaw, Regina Dickinson, Sandy Goodlander, David Lewis

Commissioners Excused: Jessica Lucero, Jordy Guth, Ken Heare

Staff Present: Tanya Rice, Russ Holley, Ben Anderson, Aaron Smith, Kymber Housley, Bill
Young, Jeannie Simmonds (City Council Liaison)
Commissioner Goodlander moved to approve the minutes from the May 13, 2021 meeting as
submitted. Motion seconded by Commissioner Croshaw. The motion was approved unanimously.
PUBLIC HEARING:

 PC 21-023 SoLo VISTAS - Continued from the May 13th Meeting [Design Review &
 Conditional Use Permit] Steven Taylor/Jeff W. Ballard, authorized agent/owner, request a
 new 5-story mixed-use building consisting of commercial on the ground floor, and four upper
 floors of 62 residential condominiums. A Conditional Use Permit is requested for parking in
 the front of the complex along Main Street, located on a 2.48-acre lot at 790 South Main
 Street in the commercial (COM) zone; TIN 02-065-0043. (Woodruff Neighborhood)

STAFF: T. Rice reviewed the request for a design review and conditional use permit for a new 5-
story mixed-use building with commercial on the ground floor that includes leasable retail spaces
and commercial garages, and a public pool. The four upper floors are residential and an outdoor
garden/seating area on the rooftop. 80 East will extend from the north through the proposed
development and stub at the south side of the development to extend to Golf Course Road in the
future. Vehicular driveway access is proposed from the adjacent commercial property to the
northwest and two additional access points on 80 East. The proposed project meets setbacks,
density, lot coverage, and design standards. The height of the building as proposed is 60.8’. With
the increased setback of 40’, the code allows the Planning Commission discretion to adjust
building height up to a 10% increase from the 55’ maximum to 60.5’. The CUP is requesting
parking along the front of the building. There is a proposed landscaped 2’ tall berm between the
front parking area and Main Street. Including the proposed parking stalls in the front of the building
and the commercial garages, the project, as proposed, is 4 stalls short of the 167 required. The
proposal is also requesting a 6’ privacy wall surrounding the pool area, along 80 East.

The commercial zone requires all commercial uses on the ground floor. The pool can be
considered a commercial use if it is a public pool and not just for the residents. As a condition of
approval, the pool is required to obtain a business license. The 14 commercial garages cannot be
used for any type of self-storage in this zone.

Commissioner Croshaw asked for the definition of self-storage. R. Holley said that standard
storage units, mini storage units, or personal storage are not allowed in the commercial zone.


Commissioner Croshaw asked if the tenants in the retail spaces could use the garages as part of
their space. R. Holley said they could be used as a backroom for the business. T Rice said the
garages could also be used by any of the tenants as a parking garage.

Commissioner Goodlander asked about the 40’ setback. T. Rice said that the building is at least
40’ from property lines.

Commissioner Dickinson asked about the zoning and having residential in the commercial zone.
T. Rice said that there can be residential in the commercial zone if the ground floor is all
commercial. Commissioner Lewis asked about the pool and how the commercial use would be
enforced. T. Rice said that the use would be regulated with the business license.

PROPONENT: CJ with Design West Architects said they are trying to provide more green space
and bring something unique and memorable to Logan. Commissioner Croshaw asked why they
would want a public pool. CJ said it is an added amenity and they are trying to offer things to the
public that others do not. Commissioner Dickinson asked about having the commercial garages.
CJ said that from a retail standpoint, where there is no access from Main Street for deliveries, they
have choosen to create smaller retail spaces to offer to a larger variety of smaller retail shops or
startup business that would not require large deliveries, and in doing this they can offer the
commercial garages as another added amenity. Steve Taylor said their intention for the
commercial garages was back storage for the retail spaces or their parking. Commissioner
Goodlander asked if the garages are attached to the commercial spaces. Steve Taylor said that
they are connected, there is a walkway between the garages and the commercial spaces.

Commissioner Goodlander asked about people purchasing several of the condos and then renting
them out. Steve Taylor said that their intention is private ownership. Per the HOA, 52% must be
owner-occupied to receive financing.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Goodlander said that she doesn’t have a problem with approving
the additional height of 5.8’ to the building, or the 6’ wall around the pool. She asked about
Condition number 8 being commercial parking only. T. Rice said that it doesn’t matter who leases
the garages, it is considered a commercial leased space.

Commissioner Goodlander asked about condition number 4 and what the frontage is along 50
East. T. Rice said that the frontage is at 40% and the condition is to approve the reduction of the
10% from the 50% standard.

Commissioner Croshaw agreed with approving the height of the building and the wall around the
pool area. He said that parking should not be allowed in the front of the building. There was
discussion of surrounding buildings that have been allowed to have parking in the front.

Commissioner Dickinson said she is ok with the parking in the front but would like a condition
added that says they must have a landscaped berm. A. Smith stated that there is a condition
listed, number 2 (updated to 11 in the record) states that a dense landscaping buffer will be
constructed between the parking area and the sidewalk on Main Street. Commissioner Lewis
asked there is a height specified. A. Smith stated that the Type “C” Separation in the code states
that there shall be a minimum 4’ tall solid screen at installation. Commissioner Goodlander said
she is ok with the parking since the building is set back and there are only 9 spaces along Main
Street. Commissioner Lewis said it feels like a housing development in commercial space, and
that the 6’ wall surrounding the pool area along a busy street doesn’t seem like much of an


amenity. T. Rice asked if there should be some added clarification to condition number 6.
Commissioner Dickinson said she is fine leaving it as it is, Commissioner Goodlander agreed.

MOTION: Commissioner Goodlander moved to conditionally approve a design review &
conditional use permit as outlined in PC 21-023 with the amended conditions and findings for
approval as listed below. Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.

border with the Logan Medical Center Condominium property to achieve setback compliance along
this border. Within the Hospital Overlay Zone, a conditional use permit is needed to exceed the
base zone height of 40’ up to a maximum of 80’ The proposed building is approximately 67 feet
tall.

surrounding entrances. Brandon McBride said that they intend to meet the needs community,

Commissioner Goodlander asked about the order of the project. Steve Kelley said they will build

 PC 21-027 Land Development Code Amendment-Chapter 17.11 [Code Amendment]
 Logan City is requesting minor amendments to table 17.11.030 in chapter 17.11 “District and
 Corridor Zone Uses” to permit financial institutions, General Sales & Services, and Sales &
 Services in buildings with a footprint exceeding 1000,000 square feet as a conditional use in
 the Industrial Park (IP) zone.

STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request to modify the Commercial & Industrial Use Table
(17.11.030) in Chapter 17.11. The proposed amendment is to change “Banks, Savings & Loans,
Credit Unions”, “General Sales/Service” and “Sales and Service”, Footprint Greater than 100,000
sq. ft. (Big Box)” from not permitted to conditionally permitted in the Industrial zone. The purpose
of these changes is to encourage the transition of “big box” retailers from Main Street locations to
the westside of Logan where large, undeveloped tracts of land are available.

Commissioner Dickinson asked why banks were included in this change? R. Holley said some of
the other ancillary uses make sense to go with the big box stores. Commissioner Lewis mentioned
how banks tend to go next to or inside big grocery stores and talked about the potential concern


with the existing big box stores relocating to this zone and leaving empty buildings in the
commercial zone that are hard to fill. Commissioner Goodlander asked if there was a specific bank
that is planning to come. R. Holley said that he is not aware of any bank that is planning to come.
He spoke about how they are looking at the future long-term use and not just a specific project.
The IP zone is not strictly Industrial. We want a lot of employment opportunities in this zone and
with that comes more people and the need for additional accommodations. Commissioner
Croshaw asked if there would be any growth to the south of the IP zone. R. Holley said that the
south would be more commercial, residential, and recreation zones.

PROPONENT: No comments were made by the proponent.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Dickinson said the IP zone should be strictly industrial, it is on the
outskirts of town because it was meant to be only industrial. Commissioner Goodlander said that
the commercial businesses that are already in that area blend in well. R. Holley brought up how
big box stores have a lot of large semis that would have easier access through 1000 W. There
was talk about what type of commercial businesses are included in general sales and service and
sales and service that could end up in the IP zone. A. Smith said that the definition of large
footprint buildings (100,000 sq ft) is a free-standing industrial type building, that would not generally
meet commercial development standards. Commissioner Dickinson said that allowing CUP in this
zone will make the area too broad. Commissioner Lewis asked about tabling the project.
Commissioner Goodlander said she is ok with doing a CUP in this area, Commissioner Croshaw
agreed.

MOTION: Commissioner Goodlander moved to forward a recommendation for approval to the
City Council for a code amendment as outlined in PC 21-027 with the findings for approval as listed
below. Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1.    Utah State Law authorizes local Planning Commission to recommend ordinance changes to
      the legislative body (Municipal Council).
2.    The Code Amendments are done in conformance with the requirements of Title 17.51 of the
      Logan Municipal Code.
3.    The proposed Code Amendments are consistent with the Logan City General Plan.
4.    The proposed Code Amendments will provide additional flexibility in siting new land uses in
      Logan.
5.    No public comment has been received regarding the proposed amendments.

 PC 21-029 The Homes at The Knoll [Design Review Permit & Zone Change] Danny
 Macfarlane/Stan Checketts Properties LC, authorized agent/owner, request a new
 development of 32 two-story unit townhomes & 63 three-story units. The existing miniature
 golf course will be renovated, and a dog park and playground added. A rezone is requested
 of the 5.48-acre lot from Commercial (COM) to Mixed-Use Medium (MR-20), located at 1975
 South 800 West in the Commercial (COM) zone; TIN 03-006-0006. (Woodruff Neighborhood)


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a rezone and design review permit. The zone change
is for 5.48 acres from COM to MR-20 to construct 95 new townhomes, 32 are proposed as 2-story
with the remaining 63 proposed as three-story units, all with garages on the ground floor. The
project site contains an old mini-golf course and a dirt bike track. The surrounding zones are MR-
20 to the south, IP to the northwest, and a mix of NR-6, MR-9, & MR-12 further west.

R. Holley went over the new Utah law stating that some of the design standards on single-family
homes cannot be regulated. K. Housley said that this change is supposed to make homes more
affordable. R. Holley said that this project is not in line with the cities long-range plan, this area is
not designated for residential. Commissioner Dickinson asked how long this property has been
zoned commercial. R. Holley said it has been commercial for at least 15-20 years.

PROPONENT: Dany Macfarlane said they felt like this is a good location for residential due to the
close trail off 800 West, the miniature golf course, dog park, and playground, it is also neighboring
other residential areas. Commissioner Goodlander asked if there was a lot line adjustment made.
Dany Macfarlane said that they have applied for the boundary line adjustment through the Public
Works Department.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Goodlander said that this property has been COM for a long time
and continues to be COM in the Future Plan. Commissioner Croshaw mentioned the other
residential complexes currently being built in this area. There was discussion on the entrances to
this project and the future roads in this area connecting to the highway. Commissioner Lewis said
he is a little wary of letting go of the commercial space in this location. Commissioner Dickinson
agreed, this area was designated for COM and should technically remain COM, but with the parcel
to the south being changed to MR-20, she is ok with this zone change on this parcel. R. Holley
said the area to the south is designated MR-20 in the Future Plan. There was a small area that
was rezoned from COM to MR-20 for the Strata Project.

MOTION: Commissioner Goodlander moved to forward a recommendation for denial to the City
Council on a zone change as outlined in PC 21-029 with the conditions and findings for denial as
listed below. The design review project will be continued to the July 22, 2021 meeting pending the
City Councils' decision on the rezone request. Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.
FINDINGS FOR DENIAL
1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as COM.
2. The Woodruff Neighborhood Specific Plan does not anticipate residential uses on this property.
3. The Logan City General Plan does not anticipate residential uses on this property.
4. This area is one of the larger remaining semi-vacant COM areas along the HWY 89/91 corridor
   allowing for the possibility of a larger commercial use to be established.
5. The COM designation is intended for commercial uses serving city-wide and regional
   populations not townhomes.


Moved: S. Goodlander                   Seconded: R. Croshaw                    Approved: 3 -1

Yea: Goodlander, Croshaw, Lewis                Nay: Dickinson                  Abstain:
