CONVENTION & VISITOR’S BUREAU

JOEL RACKER, UTAH VALLEY CONVENTION & VISITORS BUREAU — THANK YOU.
AS WE WERE LOOKING AT THIS IN THE PAST, IT WAS ALWAYS VERY SIMPLE.
WE HAVE WORKED UP A BUDGET THAT BREAKS OUT THE DEPARTMENTS. WE
HAVE A VERY DETAILED BUDGET, BUT TYPICALLY IN THE PAST, WE’VE NEVER
TURNED THAT INTO THE COUNTY — WE’VE ALWAYS BEEN WILLING TO SIT
DOWN ONE ON ONE AND HAVE YOU LOOK AT IT. BUT IT IN ESSENCE IS OUR
COMPLETE STRATEGY FOR WHAT EVERYTHING LOOKS LIKE.

DISCUSSED AND EXPLAINED THAT THEY PREFER NOT TO HAVE THEIR BUDGET
AS PART OF THE COUNTY RECORD DUE TO THE COMPETITIVE NATURE OF THE
BUSINESS. HE PROVIDED A HANDOUT FOR THE COMMISSIONERS TO REVIEW.

JOEL RACKER INDICATED THAT THE SECOND PAGE OF THE HANDOUT
REPRESENTS THE COLLECTIONS AND THE BUDGET. THE FIGURES HE PROVIDES
ARE THE AVERAGE OF 3 YEARS.

DISCUSSED INVESTMENTS WHICH HAVE DONE WELL AND HAVE PERFORMED

DISCUSSED PARTNERSHIP INCOME — THAT’S THE REVENUE THAT GOES HAND IN

DISCUSSED THE BUDGET BY BREAKDOWN OF DEPARTMENTS. NO ADDITIONAL

DISCUSSED HOW THE CVB INTERFACES WITH HOTELS, TRANSPORTATION AND

JOEL RACKER HANDED OUT ANOTHER SHEET THAT SHOWS THEIR

TO THE FUTURE WITH VARIOUS GROUPS THAT THEY HAVE MINED
THEMSELVES.

DISCUSSED CALCULATED ECONOMIC IMPACT.

COMMISSIONER AINGE SHARED THOUGHTS REGARDING THE CVB AND
EXPLORE UTAH VALLEY AND WHAT GREAT ORGANIZATIONS THEY ARE,
HOWEVER, HE DOESN’T SEE THE RETURN ON INVESTMENT. HE IS PREPARED TO
CUT FUNDING FROM THE ORGANIZATION. HE SUGGESTS THAT THE GROUP
SALES SHOULD BE HOUSED TOTALLY IN THE CONVENTION CENTER. HE
DOESN’ T THINK IT IS EFFICIENT TO HAVE TWO DIFFERENT TEAMS, AT THE CVB
AND AT THE CONVENTION CENTER SELLING THE BUILDING AND SELLING THE
DESTINATIONS.

COMMISSIONER IVIE ASKED IF THE CVB HAD DATA THAT RELATED TO
RESTAURANTS THAT MAY HAVE BEEN USED IN CONJUNCTION WITH A
FUNCTION AT THE CONVENTION CENTER AND WHETHER CREDIT CARDS WERE
USED WITH ZIP CODES OUTSIDE THE AREA. COMMISSIONER IVIE IS TRYING TO
DETERMINE IF THERE IS TRACKING AS TO WHETHER THERE IS AN INCREASE IN
FOOD SALES. HE ALSO ASKS IF THERE IS THE SAME TYPE OF TRACKING WHEN
USING THE FACILITIES IN LEHI, THANKSGIVING POINT. ARE THEY JUSTIFYING
THEIR EXISTENCE COUNTY WIDE, NOT JUST TO THE CONVENTION CENTER.

JOEL RACKER INDICATED THAT YES, THEY GET FEEDBACK FROM VENDORS
THAT INDICATE THEY RECEIVED AN UPTICK.

A LOCAL BUSINESS OWNER SHARED HIS EXPERIENCE WORKING WITH THE CVB.

JOEL RACKER DISCUSSED HOW THE CVB IS THE LEAD CATCHER, AND THEN
THEY PLACE THE CLIENT WHERE THE CLIENT WANTS TO BE, WHETHER IT’S AT
THE CONVENTION CENTER OR THE PROVO MARRIOTT OR ANY OTHER OPTIONS
WITHIN THE COUNTY. THEY TRY TO PLACE THE PIECE OF BUSINESS WHERE IT
FITS BEST.

DISCUSSED THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL AND THE
CVB. BOTH PARTIES HAVE SALES TEAMS AND ARE COMPETITORS BUT IF THEY
CAN’T HANDLE A PIECE OF BUSINESS, THEY REFER IT TO THE OTHER.

DISCUSSED THE FACT THAT THE PROVO CITY LIBRARY HAS A 400-500 SEAT
BALLROOM WHICH IS AN ODDITY FOR A LIBRARY, BUT IT ALSO IS
COMPETITION.

COMMISSIONER IVIE INDICATES THAT HE LIKES THE FACT THAT HIS TAX
DOLLARS DO NOT HAVE TO SUBSIDIZE THE CONVENTION CENTER. THAT IT IS
BEING RUN EFFICIENTLY AND IS RUNNING IN THE BLACK.

DISCUSSED THE SALES TEAM AT THE CONVENTION CENTER — THEY ARE A
GREAT TEAM.

DISCUSSED HOW TO LAND UTAH COUNTY AS A GREAT DESTINATION AND HOW
TO BE FORWARD THINKING.

DISCUSSED HOW THE MARKET IS DOWN RIGHT NOW IN DIFFERENT AREAS OF
THE COUNTRY. WHILE EVERYONE ELSE IN THE WEST IS SUFFERING A
DOWNTURN, UTAH IS NOT.

DISCUSSED SUPPLY AND DEMAND.

COMMISSIONER AINGE DISCUSSED THE GREAT POSITIVE CONTRIBUTIONS OF
THE CBV, HOWEVER IT IS STILL A TINY DROP IN THE BUCKET... THAT WE HAVE
A STRONG ECONOMY, THAT WE HAVE TWO UNIVERSITIES IN THE CITY THAT
THE ENROLLMENT CONTINUES TO INCREASE AND PARENTS COME TO VISIT,
THAT WE HAVE A HIGH LEVEL OF PEOPLE MOVING TO THE COUNTY BECAUSE
WE ARE DOING WELL IN ALL AREAS AS A COUNTY, NOT JUST ON THE TOURISM
SIDE. HE ALSO INDICATED THAT UTAH COUNTY IS DISPROPORTIONATELY
OUTPACING THE REST OF THE STATE IN RELATION TO POPULATION GROWTH.

DISCUSSED CASE STUDIES IN DIFFERENT STATES THAT INDICATE IF YOU DROP
MARKETING AND ADVERTISING, TOURISM WILL STOP.

DISCUSSED THE NIGHTLY AIR B&B OFFERINGS IN THE AREA AND THE
COLLECTION OF THE TOURISM TAX.

DISCUSSED THAT THE 44,500 HOTEL ROOMS IN THE COUNTY — THE CVB IS THE
ONLY GROUP TO PROMOTE THEIR NON-PEAK NIGHTS. THE CONVENTION
CENTER DOES NOT.

COMMISSIONER AINGE THINKS THAT WITH LESS MONEY, THE CONVENTION
CENTER CAN DO MORE.

DISCUSSED WHETHER OR NOT THE CONVENTION CENTER WOULD BE ABLE TO
SELL AND PARTNER WITH THE UCCU CENTER, WITH SUNDANCE, WITH
THANKSGIVING POINT AND OTHER GROUPS, BECAUSE THAT IS CURRENTLY THE
ROLL OF THE CVB.

COMMISSIONER AINGE ISN’T AS CONCERNED WITH THE OTHER PROPERTIES AS
THE CONVENTION CENTER HAS THE BIGGER BOND PAYMENT AT THE MOMENT.
THAT HIS FEELING WITH THE OTHER PROPERTIES IS THAT THEY DO NOT
ANTICIPATE A BIG SHIFT IN THEIR USAGE BECAUSE THEY DON’T FEEL THEY
GET A LOT OF LEADS FROM THE CVB ANYWAYS.

COMMISSIONER IVIE ASKS WHAT PERCENTAGE OF CONVENTION OR LARGE
GROUPS LAND AT THE CONVENTION CENTER AND WHAT PERCENTAGE LANDS
OUTSIDE OF THE CONVENTION CENTER.

DISCUSSED UCCU AS BEING A BIG SPORTS VENUE THAT SUPPORTS ROOMS IN
THE OREM AND PROVO AREAS.

DISCUSSED THE FACT THAT THE CVB HELPS FACILITATE GROUPS THAT THEY
DON’T NECESSARILY TAKE CREDIT FOR BECAUSE THE GROUPS WOULD BE
COMING TO UTAH COUNTY REGARDLESS. THAT IS WHY IT IS HARD TO
MEASURE THE SUCCESS OF THE PIPELINE OR THE SUCCESS OF THE SALES
TEAM, BECAUSE THEY ARE A SHARED LEAD SOURCE WITH ONE OF THE
UNIVERSITIES.

COMMISSIONER AINGE INDICATES THAT THERE IS A STATUTORY OBLIGATION
TO PROMOTE TOURISM, HOWEVER IT WILL NOT ALL BE WITH THE CVB, AND
THAT HE THINKS THE COUNTY WILL GET A BETTER ROI ON THE PROMOTION
OF THE COUNTY.

COMMISSIONER LEE ASKS SOME IF/THEN STATEMENTS. IF THE FUNDS ARE
TAKEN AWAY FROM THE CVB, WHERE DO THEY NEED TO LAND SO THAT WE
AREN’T STARTING FROM SCRATCH. DISCUSSED WHAT THE PROPER USE OF THE
FUNDS WOULD BE.

DISCUSSED HOW LOCAL HOTELS ARE PROMOTING THEIR BUSINESS FOR
SMALL/MEDIUM CORPORATE EVENTS, BUT THE CVB IS PROMOTING UTAH
VALLEY FOR LEISURE TRAVEL FOR PEOPLE TO HAVE AN EXPERIENCE HERE.

COMMISSIONER LEE ASKS IF THERE CAN BE A HYBRID AS HE ALSO HAS
PROBLEMS WITH THE DUAL SALES FORCE.

DISCUSSED THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE SALES FORCE AT THE
CONVENTION CENTER VS THE CVB, AND THE FACT THAT THE CVB IS THE MAIN
LEAD CATCHER, THAT THEY BOUGHT THE DOMAIN NAME, AND THAT THEY
ALLOW THE COUNTY TO USE THE URL, BUT THE CVB MAINTAINS THE LEADS
GARNERED FROM THE SITE.

DISCUSSED THE HYBRID IDEA — THE CONVENTION CENTER NEEDS TO CONTINUE
DOING WHAT THEY ARE DOING. HOWEVER, HE DOES NOT THINK THEY CAN
STEP IN AND TAKE ON THE DUAL ROLE OF REPRESENTING THE COUNTY.

DISCUSSED THE CVB BEING THE ONLY ENTITY THAT IS WORKING TO KEEP
BUSINESS IN UTAH COUNTY — WHICH IN TURN BENEFITS THE CITIZENS OF THE
COUNTY AND INCREASE THEIR QUALITY OF LIFE.

DISCUSSED THE TRCC TAX (TOURISM, RECREATION, CULTURAL AND
CONVENTION TAX) AND WHETHER THE CITIZENS OF THE COUNTY WOULD
RATHER HAVE IT GO TOWARDS PARKS AND TRAILS, IMPROVING UTAH LAKE
ETC. OR PROMOTING CONVENTIONS.

DISCUSSED GETTING A BETTER MODEL FOR THE LEAD SOURCE GENERATION AS
FAR AS A POSSIBLE HYBRID IS CONCERNED.

COMMISSIONER LEE INDICATED THAT HE THOUGHT THE CONVENTION CENTER
SHOULD BE SOLD TO THE MARRIOTT OR SOME OTHER ENTITY BECAUSE THEN
THEY WOULDN’T BE COMPETITION.

COMMISSIONER AINGE DISCUSSED PROMOTIONAL DOLLARS — AND THAT THE
CONVENTION CENTER SALES DEPARTMENT WOULD HAVE TO BEEF UP TO THEIR
SALES FORCE, SOME OF THE MARKETING AND BRANDING EFFORTS CAN
CONTINUE AND COULD BE CONTINUED UNDER THE CVB OR MOVED TO THE
CONVENTION CENTER. DISCUSSED ECONOMIC IMPACT AND HOW
OPPORTUNITIES ARE SOMETIMES LOST IF WE ARE FOCUSED ON THE BOTTOM
LINE AT THE CONVENTION CENTER.

DISCUSSED THE MARKET STUDY ON THE CVB’S WIN AND CLOSE RATE WHICH
SUGGESTS THAT UTAH VALLEY IS NOT THE BEST FIT FOR SOME OF THE
LARGEST GROUPS RIGHT NOW. IT IS PROBABLY NOT THE BEST ROI TO GO
AFTER THESE ENORMOUS GROUPS.

DISCUSSED THE NEED FOR LEADS TO BE DEVELOPED EVEN IF THEY ARE WITHIN
THE STATE, NOT FROM LEAD SOURCES FROM OUTSIDE THE STATE.

COMMISSIONER IVIE SHARED A STORY REGARDING THE TWO HORSES — AND
THE PACK HORSE THAT HE HAD TO CUT LOOSE. HE ADVISES THAT SOME
ADDITIONAL DISTINCTION NEEDS TO BE MADE BETWEEN THE CVB AND THE
CONVENTION CENTER SO THAT THEY ALL DON’T DROWN, THAT THEY NEED TO
FIND A MORE EFFICIENT WAY TO RUN THE ORGANIZATION. HE ALSO
WONDERED IF THEY DID AWAY WITH THE FINANCIAL SUPPORT OF EXPLORE
UTAH VALLEY, WOULD THEY SOMEHOW OFFSET A SMALL PORTION OF THE
BURDEN THE TAXPAYERS WILL FEEL WITH THE PROPERTY TAX INCREASE IF
THEY ARE ABLE TO LESSEN THE RESTAURANT TAX OR THE HOTEL TAX. HE
ALSO STATES THAT THE 100,000’S OF EXTRA PEOPLE THAT WE DRIVE TO UTAH
COUNTY WILL BEAT UP OUR TRAILS, BEAT UP OUR ROADS, THEY WILL USE THE
COUNTY INFRASTRUCTURE, BUT THE COUNTY CANNOT USE THE TAX REVENUE
GENERATED TO OFFSET THE ABUSE OF THE INFRASTRUCTURE. IT IS HIS
FUNDAMENTAL PROBLEM WITH THE TRT TAX. HE FEELS THERE IS A FAILURE
IN THE LEGISLATURE TO GIVE THE COUNTY FLEXIBILITY WITH THIS TAX TO
OFFSET SOME OF THE BURDEN THAT THE INCREASED TOURISM BRINGS TO US.

DISCUSSED PLANNING FOR THE FUTURE, MAKING SURE WE HAVE EVENTS ON
THE BOOKS FOR THE FUTURE SO THAT IF LOCAL BUSINESS DRIES UP, THE
COMBINATION OF THE TWO WILL SOFTEN THE BLOW.
