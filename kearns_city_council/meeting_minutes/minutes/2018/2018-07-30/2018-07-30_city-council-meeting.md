                                      PUBLIC MEETING

Council Business

Carport

                Mayor Bush stated Curtis Woodward, Planner, Salt Lake County Planning and
Development Services Division, was working with Kearns residents that want to put a carport on
their property. The current ordinance states a carport cannot be built within eight feet from a
property line unless it is built behind an existing home. The Council can change the ordinance to
accommodate the residents but will add conditions; such as, the type of material used for the
carport.

Lake County, which has caused numerous problems. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services

Modifications of the Master Interlocal Agreement Regarding Financial Services

               The Council reviewed the following resolution authorizing the offering of a letter to
the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) seeking a modification of the Master
Interlocal Agreement between the GSLMSD, the Metro Townships, and Salt Lake County
regarding financial services provided by Salt Lake County.

RESOLUTION NO. 18-07-03                                                        Date: July 30, 2018

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE OFFERING OF A LETTER TO THE
       GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT (MSD) SEEKING A
       MODIFICATION OF THE MASTER INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BWEEN THE
       MSD, THE METRO TOWNSHIPS, AND SALT LAKE COUNTY REGARDING
       FINANCIAL SERVICES PROVIDED BY THE COUNTY

                                            RECITALS

   A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code Annotated
      (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of
      Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District pursuant
      to UCA §§ 17B-2a-1101 et al.

   D. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a county pursuant to Utah Ode Ann. Title 17 Counties,
      and is the contracted service provider of certain municipal-type service via the MSD.

   E. As a member entity of the MSD, who receives services from Salt Lake County via the
      Master Interlocal Agreement between the MSD, the Metro Townships and Salt Lake
      County (the “Agreement”), periodically it is necessary to propose certain amendments to
      the MSD to the Agreement to insure the services being provided are of a satisfactory
      quality and consistent with the needs of the Council and the Community with the rules in
      the agreement for handling such amendments being contained in Article II, section 2.4.

   F. Kearns feels the offered of a letter proposing certain amendments for consideration by the
      MSD greatly benefit Kearns and the MSD and improves effectiveness, efficiency, overall
      quality, and better control costs of services.

                                          RESOLUTION

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Kearns Metro Township Council approves the
offering of a letter to the MSD seeking a modification of the Master Interlocal Agreement between
the MSD, the Metro Townships, and Salt Lake County regarding the provision of financial services

Township, and

         BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the letter be composed by Council Staff to present to the
MSD at its Planning Meeting to be held on August 3, 2018 at 9:00 am as a possible agenda item
for their upcoming meeting on August 8, 2018.
