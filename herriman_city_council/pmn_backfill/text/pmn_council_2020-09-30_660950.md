Mayor Watts called the meeting to order at 5:44 p.m.


1. Discussion and Action Items
    1.1. Discussion and consideration of an ordinance adopting the Fire/EMS Impact Fee Facilities Plan
          and Impact Fee Analysis and imposing Fire/EMS Impact Fees; and providing for the calculation
          and collection of such fees - Chase Andrizzi, City Attorney
City Attorney Chase Andrizzi offered a brief history of the adoption of the Fire/EMS Impact Fee Facilities Plan
and Impact Fee Analysis public hearing. He explained upon approval of the enactment ordinance, impact fees
for new construction would start to be collected after the 90 day hiatus period which would essentially be
December 29, 2020.


Councilmember Ohrn explained the cost of building keeps rising, and highlighted this was a fee being collected
by the Unified Fire Service Area and now would start to be collected by Herriman City at the time a home started
construction. City Manager Wood explained the collection of these impact fees would stay within the
community, and would not subsidize other areas of the Unified Fire Service Area.


Councilmember Smith added the City would like to have this in place so there would not be a gap in collection
of those fees either from Unified Fire Service Area or Herriman City. This was confirmed.


Councilmember Smith moved to approve Ordinance No 2020-31 adopting an Impact Fee Facilities Plan and
Impact Fee Analysis and imposing the Fire/EMS Impact Fees. Councilmember Ohrn seconded the motion.


The vote was recorded as follows:
Councilmember Jared Henderson          Absent
Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn             Aye
Councilmember Steven Shields           Aye
Councilmember Clint Smith              Aye
Mayor David Watts                      Aye


The motion passed unanimously with Councilmember Henderson being absent.
