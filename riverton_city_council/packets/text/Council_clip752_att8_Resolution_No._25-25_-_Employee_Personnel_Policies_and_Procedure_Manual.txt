                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 25-25

      A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO THE RIVERTON CITY
              PERSONNEL POLICIES AND PROCEDURES MANUAL

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual (the “Manual”)
is used to set acceptable parameters for employment operations, and define employee benefits; and

      WHEREAS, the Manual is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in law or in
employment practices arise; and

       WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the manual and determined some terms need to be
updated to improve the administration of the retirement system and to remove Water Department
Director as a Merit employee; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, does hereby determine that it is in the best
interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City and City employees to
adopt revisions to the Manual;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual, Section 4.7, Retirement
          System and Section 2.6 Employment, Merit, attached to this Resolution is hereby
          amended as provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution. The Mayor is hereby directed to
          ensure that the Manual is accordingly revised and made available to all City employees
          within a reasonable time from adoption of this Resolution.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

        PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 15th day of
 April 2025 by the following vote:

2.6 EMPLOYMENT

MERIT
A merit employee is a full time employee who holds employment without limitation of time, and is not
subject to discharge, suspension of over two days without pay, or involuntary transfer to a position of
less remuneration, unless any of the aforementioned adverse employment actions are conducted in
accordance with predetermination and appeal procedures described in this Manual. The foregoing
notwithstanding, the following employees are not considered merit employees, unless the terms and
conditions of the employee’s employment contract state otherwise.

        City Manager
        City Attorney
        Public Works Director
        Administrative Services Director
        City Recorder
        Events and Operations Director
        City Treasurer
        Development Services Director
        Communications Director
        Executive Assistant/Policy Analyst
        Chief of Police


4.7 RETIREMENT SYSTEM

EMPLOYEES, OTHER THAN PUBLIC SAFETY SERVICE EMPLOYEES, WHO BECOME TIER II
MEMBERS OF URS BY GAINING EMPLOYMENT WITH RIVERTON CITY.
For purposes of URS coverage, the City classifies all full-time appointed (non-merit as defined in section
2.6) employees as eligible for retirement benefits. Eligibility for retirement coverage under URS shall be
administered in accordance with the statutory rules governing Utah Retirement Systems. URS arranges
individual retirement accounts for each Tier II Public Service Employee, and requires employers to
contribute annually to the employee’s retirement account. If an employee’s first employer is Riverton
City, then the employee shall be required to select whether to participate in what is known as a “hybrid
retirement” or a “defined contribution retirement” within one year from the employee’s date of hire.
Employees are directed to consult with URS to learn the benefits offered under both systems before
making a selection. Employees shall become members of the hybrid retirement system by default, if no
selection is made by the employee. Tier II Public Service employees who have already become a
member of hybrid retirement or a defined contribution retirement shall continue to be a member of
their same plan after coming to work for Riverton City.

In addition to URS benefits, Riverton City annually contributes to a 401(a) retirement program which is
managed by ICMA-RC. Riverton City will contribute to each employee’s ICMA-RC retirement account an
amount equal to 6.5% of an employee’s gross wage.

EMPLOYEES WORKING LESS THAN 20 HOURS PER WEEK FOR RIVERTON CITY.
Employees working less than 20 hours per week or who are temporary employees will have an amount
equal to 7.5% of the employee’s gross wage paid into a 457 plan for the employee which is managed by
URS. For purposes of URS coverage, the City classifies all elected officials and all part-time appointed
(Non-merit) positions, as part-time ineligible. This includes the Mayor, City Council, Planning
Commission members, youth council advisors, and any other part-time appointed officials that receive a
stipend. Eligibility for retirement coverage under Utah Retirement Systems shall be administered in
accordance with the statutory rules governing Utah Retirement Systems.
