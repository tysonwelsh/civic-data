TIP Modification – SR 92 Turn Lane Extension Project – Bob Allen, MAG (1:17) Created: 2021 Funds: $600,000 UDOT Funds Scope: Extend center turn lane on SR92 from Manor Dr to Spruce Dr in Highland. Proposal: UDOT determined that the center turn lane needs to be extended beyond Spruce Dr to Park Dr (4150 East). An additional $600,000 of UDOT funds would be added to the project bringing the new project total to $1,200,000. All of these funds are controlled by UDOT, but they do need to be added to the TIP. RTP TransPlan50 Kickoff – Shawn Eliot, MAG (7:10) MAG’s TransPlan50 is updated every four years in an effort to remain relevant to emerging trends and political realities. It incorporates the latest travel demand models for the area including socioeconomic and land-use data. MAG Staff: Shawn Eliot Melanie Haws Andrew Jackson LaNiece Davenport Michelle Carroll Robert Allen Andrew Wooley Shauna Mecham Chad Eccles Tim Hereth MPO Regional Planning Committee March 3, 2022 Page 3 The objective of the TransPlan50 is to address local transportation needs and educate local communities on the regional needs to address growth in the regional transportation system. By working together, communities can find sound solutions and create local plans that reflect regional needs. Dianne Olson with The Langdon Group (TLG) reported that work on the next update to the TransPlan50 has already begun and will be completed next year. TLG worked with MAG on the previous project and look forward to collaborating with the local decision makers and technical staff again. This year the strategy is to focus education. In late spring, MAG will conduct a series of Transportation Summits in the north, central, and south areas of the county and will invite a large group of decision makers and technical staff to these meetings for their respective areas. Invitees include mayors, county commissioners, city managers, city councils, planning commissions, city staff, and representatives from UDOT and UTA. Goals for theTransPlan50 update, including a timeline was presented. The goal of the meetings is to Educate, Engage and Inform on all things regional. Northwest Utah County Transit Study – Chad Eccles, MAG (33:22) The Northwest Utah County Transit Study looked at developing a transit system to better meet the needs of the communities of Eagle Mountain and Saratoga Springs as their population continues to grow. This study outlines near term steps for transit improvement and long-term steps for planning. The sales tax the two cities generate is a significant share of funding toward transit, yet services remain minimal. Results from the study and possible solutions to resolve the transit gaps were discussed. South County Transit Study – Mary De La Mare-Schaeffer (45:15) The county and cities of southern Utah County partnered with UTA, UDOT, and MAG to perform the South County Transit Study. The purpose of the study is to analyze the implementation of a fixed guideway transit system. UTA purchased a railroad right-of-way with the intent of developing a commuter rail or fixed guideway transit service. This study was the first step of the process for developing this service. Results from the study and possible solutions were discussed.
