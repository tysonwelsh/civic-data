                                        Meeting Minutes
                                      Work Session 2-11-20
                                            8:00 p.m.
                                   Nephi City Council Chambers



        1. Dr. Michael Clay from Brigham Young University addressed the council about
           contracting with a BYU student team to help with our general plan update. He indicated
           that the cost would be $15,000 with the petitioning the Community Impact Board (CIB)
           to contribute half of the cost.

        2.    Kalli Bower, who is developing a RV park on the south end of town, addressed the
             council about the possibility of changing some verbiage in the Nephi City code about the
             requirement of hard surface roads throughout the park.

        3. Electrical Department Superintendent Rust Finlinson requested from the council
           permission to send two employees to the Navajo Reservation to work alongside the
           Navajo Tribal Utility Authority (NTUA) and other volunteer power departments to bring
           electricity to families who have been waiting for many years to have electricity.

        4. Mr. Atkinson reported that two different property owners have approached him about
           the possibility of petitioning the city for annexation.

        5.    Mr. Atkinson discussed with the council about the need to prioritize some CIB projects that
             they would like listed on the county’s list of projects.

        6. Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn and to convene into executive session to discuss
           personnel issues. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
           unanimous vote.

        7. Meeting adjourned at 9:35

Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson (ABSENT), Justin Seely (ABSENT), Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Nathan
