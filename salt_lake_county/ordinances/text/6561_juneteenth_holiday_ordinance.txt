SALT LAKE COUNTY ORDINANCE 
 
ORDINANCE NO.__________      __________, 2021 
 
ESTABLISHING JUNETEENTH INDEPENDENCE DAY AS A LEGAL HOLIDAY OF 
SALT LAKE COUNTY 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE LEGISLATIVE BODY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY, 
UTAH, AMENDING SECTION  1.06.020 OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY 
CODE OF ORDINANC ES, 2001, ENTITLED “ HOLIDAYS” ESTABLISHING 
THE NINETEENTH DAY OF JUNE , JUNETEENTH INDEPENDENCE DAY, 
AS A LEGAL HOLIDAY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY  
 
The County Legislative Body of Salt Lake County ordains as follows: 
 
 SECTION I. The amendments made herein are designated by underlining the new 
substituted words. Words being deleted are designated by brackets and strike-through. 
SECTION II. Section 1.06.020, of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001 is 
hereby amended to read as follows: 
1.06.020 - Holidays.  
 
A. The following days are legal holidays during which County offices are closed: 
 
 
 
 
The First [d]Day of January 
 
 
New Year’s Day 
 
The Third Monday of January 
 
 
Martin Luther King Day 
 
 
The Third Monday of February 
 
 
Presidents’ Day 
 
The Last Monday in May 
 
Memorial Day 
 
 
The Nineteenth Day of June 
 
 
Juneteenth Independence Day 
 
 2 
 
The Fourth Day of July 
 
 
Independence Day 
 
The Twenty-fourth Day of July 
 
Pioneer Day 
 
 
The First Monday of September 
 
 
Labor Day 
 
The Eleventh Day of November 
 
Veterans Day 
 
 
The Fourth Thursday of November 
 
 
Thanksgiving 
 
The Fourth Friday of November 
 
 
Day After Thanksgiving 
 
The [Twenty Fifth] Twenty-fifth Day of 
December 
 
 
Christmas Day 
 
 
 
B. In the event that a holiday falls on a Saturday, it shall be observed on the preceding 
Friday. In the event that a holiday falls on a Sunday, it shall be observed on the following 
Monday.  
C. Where public business requires, some [c]County offices may be required to remain open 
for business on legal holidays.  
 
SECTION III. This ordinance shall become effective fifteen (15) days after its passage and 
upon at least one publication in a newspaper published and having general circulation in Salt 
Lake County.
