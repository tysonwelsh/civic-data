                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   ORDINANCE NO. 24-26

 AN ORDINANCE MAKING TECHNICAL REVISIONS TO ORDINANCE NO. 24-24,
                 ADOPTED ON NOVEMBER 12, 2024
                [RIVERTON CITY YOUTH COUNCIL]

      WHEREAS, on the date of November 12, 2024, the Riverton City Council adopted
Ordinance No. 24-24, which formally created the Riverton City Youth Council (“Youth Council”);
and

       WHEREAS, the ordinance presented to the Riverton City Council provided for a
governing structure at the level of youth participants by allowing for an election system which
would place popularly elected Youth Council participants in leadership offices, such as Youth
Council Mayor, etc.; and

        WHEREAS, during the course of discussing the merits of Ordinance No. 24-24, the
Riverton City Council determined that while the Youth Council would benefit from a leadership
structure at the level of youth participants, the program would be better served if students in
leadership were selected by administrative staff of the City; and

        WHEREAS, during the meeting of November 12, 2024 the Riverton City Council
amended Section 2.130.030(3) of Ordinance No. 24-24 to eliminate provisions related to popularly
electing Youth Council officers, and replaced the same with provisions which provided for their
appointment; and

        WHEREAS, upon further review of Ordinance No. 24-24, as amended, Riverton City staff
identified instances elsewhere in the ordinance which tacitly revered to Youth Council officers as
“elected”; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines Riverton City Code Sections
2.130.030(1), 2.130.040(1) and 2.130.040(3) should be amended to replace the word “elected”
with “appointed” or “appointment” to clean up inconsistencies on Ordinance No. 24-24; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the following action is
in the best interest of the City’s health, safety and general welfare.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

Section 1.     Amendment. The following Code Section is hereby adopted:

               Title 2, Chapter 130, Sections 030(1), 040(1) and 040(3) are hereby
               amended as provided in the attached Exhibit A.
