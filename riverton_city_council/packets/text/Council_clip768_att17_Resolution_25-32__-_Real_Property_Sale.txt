                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 25-32

  A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL AUTHORIZING SALE OF
                          REAL PROPERTY

        WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code §10-8-2 (1) (a) (iii) the Riverton City Council may
“purchase, receive, hold, sell, lease, convey, and dispose of real and personal property for the
benefit of the municipality, whether the property is within or without the municipality’s
corporate boundaries, if the action is in the public interest and complies with other law;” and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City owns certain real property located at the address of 4123
West, 12600 South (“Subject Property”);

        WHEREAS, on July 18 2023, the Riverton City Council adopted Resolution No. 23-62
declaring Subject Property to be surplus property; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council directed City staff to make the Subject Property available
to the public for sale and return to the Council with an appropriate agreement to sell the Property
has determined that the sale of the property will not adversely affect the City; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that staff has completed the
process to identify a purchaser for the Subject Property at market value, and presented an
appropriate Purchase and Sale Agreement to the Council for the sale of the Subject Property;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Purchase and Sale Agreement for the Subject Property attached hereto as Exhibit
           A is hereby approved. The Mayor is hereby authorized and directed to sign the
           Purchase and Sale Agreement, once the same has been approved as to legal form by
           the City Attorney.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 3rd day of
June, 2025 by the following vote:
