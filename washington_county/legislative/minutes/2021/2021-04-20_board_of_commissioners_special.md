WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
APRIL 20, 2021

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Almquist at 3:15 p.m. on April 20, 2021, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner
Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.
Others in attendance included: Zachary Renstrom

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION OF CAMPING REGULATIONS AT KOLOB

There have been ongoing issues with overuse of camping and unregulated fires in the
Kolob Reservoir area.

Commissioner Iverson explained that Washington County is in the process of securing two
(2) gators (ATV’s) for use by camp hosts around the campground area of Kolob.

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales explained that ordinances can be difficult to
enforce on private property, however, there are options in the form of a lease hold interest
from WCWCD to County. There was a discussion of what types of boat horsepower may
be allowed on the lake.

Water Conservancy Director Zachary Renstrom explained that the campground will be
available for use by Memorial Day of this year and will be by reservation only. Each
campsite will cost $50. WCWCD is in charge of fees and permits and the website.

Chairman Almquist clarified that camp hosts will be volunteers under Washington
County’s HR department.

Ms. Hales will continue to work with the Water District attorney to put together
agreements, an MOU or lease between Washington County and the Water District to
regulate camping in the area. County may need to draft Ordinances also.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
3:55 p.m.

E BENTLEY

DEPUTY CLERK
