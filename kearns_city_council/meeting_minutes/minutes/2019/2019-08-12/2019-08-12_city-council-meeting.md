Salt Lake County Engineering

              Madeline Francisco-Galang, Salt Lake County Engineering Division, requested
a time extension on Salt Lake County Contract #1758, which was originally signed on January
25, 2018, and ended on June 30, 2019. She is requesting that the contract be extended until
December 31, 2019. The three projects that are covered under this contract are:

    Way Finding to provide signs around Kearns to certain areas of significance. A consultant
     has been hired for this project and it should be completed by the end of September.

    Access across the railroad for Kearns High School students. A consultant will be hired to
     determine the best way to cross the railroad tracks for students attending Kearns High
     School. The study should be finished before the end of the year.

    Active Transportation Facility between Northwest Avenue and Cougar Lane. A consultant
     will be hired to look into this. The study should be done by the end of the year.

               Mayor Bush stated she wanted it on record that since the contract ended on June
30, 2019, that Salt Lake County still had the money available to complete the projects.

              Ms. Francisco-Galang stated the money is still available.

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Peterson, moved to extend
Contract No. 1758 until December 31, 2019. The motion passed unanimously.

Kearns Improvement District

              Pam Gill, Kearns Improvement District, updated the Council on the following:

    The two-million-gallon tank is 75 percent completed.

    The Cougar Pump Station is 95 percent completed.

    An outflow line was realigned at 5500 South where a waste water line had to be bypassed.
     This entailed working with Utah Transit Authority to go over the railroad tracks. This work
     was done over the weekend.

    A new eight-inch water main line was installed at 5015 South to 4820 West. Five new
     hydrants were also installed. The service laterals into the main water line should be
     completed in two weeks.

    A new main line will be constructed on 4720 West, which should be completed this year.

    Participated with Home Town Night and the Fire, Water, and Ice events.

Kearns Oquirrh Parks Fitness Center

               Patti Hansen, Kearns Oquirrh Parks Fitness Center (KOPFC), updated the

Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Chief Brian Lohrke updated the Council on the following:

    There were no problems at Home Town Days, the parade, and Fire, Water and Ice,
     celebrations.

    No event parking signs were put up to help with the parking problem during concerts held
     at the USANA amphitheater.

    UPD has been working with the Federal Task Force on drug houses. There has been
     quite a few this past year. A stash house was located in Kearns and a lot of drugs were
     confiscated. The investigation is ongoing.

    The gang unit has been working hard doing a lot of prevention tactics. It seems to be
     working because gang activity this summer as been low.

    UPD is conducting a social media campaign to remind drivers that school is starting and
     children will be out walking. The campaign reminds drivers to watch their speed and no
     distracted driving.

    An RFP was sent out to hire an architectural firm to perform the feasibility study for the
     new Kearns precinct building. UPD might need to bond for this project.

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting a personnel policy:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-08-01                                                   Date: August 12, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING
       A PERSONNEL POLICY

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-717(1)(c) authorizes the Kearns Metro Township
Council to adopt personnel policies and guidelines by resolution;

        WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) desires to adopt a
personnel policy pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-717(1)(c) and consistent with Part 11 of Title
10 of the Utah Code; and

       WHEREAS, the Council has determined that the personnel policy attached to this
Resolution will serve the best interests of the Kearns Metro Township and the general public.

        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Kearns Metro Township Council
that the personnel policy attached to this Resolution, is hereby approved effective immediately.

       APPROVED and ADOPTED this 12th day of August, 2019.
