into this.

Planning Commission

Heights Limits and Use provisions in the Manufacturing Zone (M-1)

                Kayla Mauldin, Senior Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (MSD), delivered a PowerPoint presentation on zoning considerations for Kearns’
Warehouse District. The PowerPoint highlighted the application; code update context; general
plan guidance; high-cube storage and its opportunities and challenges; other developments in the
area; and what various heights would look like in the warehouse district. She stated the questions
for the Council to consider include what height is appropriate in the warehouse district and is high-
cube storage a desired use in the warehouse district.

                Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated he is asking for
direction from the Council as far as the height limit for the code.

               Ms. Mauldin stated a site where high-cube storage is used could generate up to
$200,000 annually in tax benefits. However, cube-storage does increase traffic and has a
typically low employment rate since it is mostly automated.

               Mr. Bracken stated Utah has no high-cube storage facilities.

            Joe Hatch, Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission, stated the Planning
Commission only saw the 75-foot height and above options for high-cube storage.

                 Alex Rudowski, Municipal Services District, presented slides showing models of
infrastructure, landscaping, and existing conditions within the Kearns Metro Township. One of
the things that can be done with this software is to visualize what areas of the communities could
look like in the future. He then showed slides of what the warehouse district would look like using
different heights for the high-cube storage facilities. This tool is something that can be used to
look at Kearns as a whole, not just the warehouse district.

                Mr. Bracken stated an incomplete application has been received for a high-cube
facility. As the zoning ordinance stands right now, the application would go through the Planning
Commission for a conditional use permit.

                Mayor Bush stated she was not sure she would approve anything above 60 feet.

                 Mr. Bracken stated the best way to go about this would be to allow his office to
prepare a draft of the ordinance with some hard numbers and then bring it back before the
Planning Commission and Council. There might be a need to have two types of manufacturing
zones in Camp Kearns, which would allow different height limits. He will work on this ordinance
and bring it to the Planning Commission for approval and then the Council.

               Mayor Bush stated it might be better to hold a work session with the Planning
Commission and Council to discuss the height limit and come to agreement before it goes through
the process to adopt the ordinance.

               Mr. Bracken stated he would prepare a draft of the ordinance for discussion. The
draft would include two zones with different heights. The highest zone would allow for a 50-to-
60-foot height building.

Legal Updates

Valley Emergency Communications Center (VECC)

                Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated the Valley
Emergency Communications Center (VECC) is the dispatch center for the Kearns Metro
Township. Currently, all the metro townships are represented on the VECC Board of Trustees by
the Unified Fire Authority (UFA) and the Unified Police Department (UPD).

               Mayor Kelly Bush stated the UPD and UFA are representing a lot of different
interests on the VECC Board of Trustees. She would like to see each metro township have its
own representative on board and would like to present this issue to the other metro townships
during the upcoming Municipal Services District (MSD) meeting.

               Mr. Bracken stated some of the municipalities that use VECC have their own
representatives. If MSD agrees with this issue, then a letter would be written to the VECC Board
of Trustees requesting that each township have a seat on the board.

                All Council Members were okay with this being presented to the MSD board.
