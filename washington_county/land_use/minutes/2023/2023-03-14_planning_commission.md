Community Development




            Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                          March 14, 2023
The Regular Meeting of the Planning Commission of Washington County was called to order by Chairman
Kevin Jones at 1:30 p.m. on March 14, 2023, in the Washington County Commission Chambers and via
Zoom, at 197 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah. In attendance; Planning Commissioners Frank
Leuck, Mark Owens, Todd Jones, Kevin Jones, Brad Gaston, and Brian McMullin. County Staff in
attendance were Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Victoria Hales, Deputy Attorney; Todd
Edwards, County Engineer; and Reid Walkenhorst, Planning and Permits Specialist. Commissioner
Anderson was absent from today’s meeting.

       MEETING OPENING AND WELCOME.

       Chairman Jones opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. and welcomed everyone.

       PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE.

       Pledge of Allegiance led by Chairman Jones.

       ROLL CALL / STATEMENTS OF CONFLICT OF INTEREST.

       Roll Call:
       Chairman Jones: No Conflict
       Vice Chairman Owens: No Conflict
       Commissioner Todd Jones: No Conflict
       Commissioner Brad Gaston: No conflict
       Commissioner Kevin Jones: No Conflict
       Commissioner McMullin: No Conflict
       Commissioner Brandon Anderson: Absent

       Chairman Jones acknowledged Commissioner Leuck for his service and dedication as Planning
       Commission Chairman.

       1. REVIEW OF MINUTES OF FEBRUARY 14, 2023, PLANNING COMMISSION

       Reid will correct the record.

2. PLANNING COMMISSION TRAINING.
      I. Legislative vs. Administrative Decisions
     II. Public Hearings vs. Public Meetings
    III. Conditional Use Permits (Administrative Decision)
    IV.  Permitted with Standards (Administrative Decision)

Ms. Hales and Mr. Messel reviewed training items with the commissioners.


3. REVIEW & DISCUSS VARIOUS FUTURE CODE AMENDMENTS.
     I. Campgrounds (CUP vs. Permitted with Standards)
    II. Lighting

Ms. Hales and Mr. Messel reviewed potential regulation of campgrounds and lighting.

Commissioner Owens asked if a work meeting would be valuable to further draft a campground
ordinance.

The work meeting was set for April 4th at 12:00pm in the 226 conference room.

4. REVIEW OF DEVELOPMENT STAFF MEETING

Mr. Messel thanked the Commissioners for their involvement in attending JUD staff meetings.

5. COUNTY COMMISSION ACTION REVIEW.

Mr. Messel explained the County Commission denied the waiver of road improvements for Snow
Pine Subdivision.

ADJOURNMENT.

Commissioner Leuck made a MOTION to adjourn, and Commissioner McMullin
SECONDED the motion, and the Commission unanimously approved adjournment. The
meeting adjourned at 3:10p.m.
