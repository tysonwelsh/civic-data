     REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests approval of a conditional use drive through located at 1678 East Timpanogos Highway. The
new Swig will be going into the north part of the retail building where Hanger 15 bikes previously was located. The
retail building has a drive through that was constructed with the retail building. However, the drive through was
originally built for the southern portion of the building.

The Planning Commission held a public hearing and tabled this item on March 13, 2025. The applicant requested
more time to work with the Lehi City Traffic Engineer on the drive-through queuing. That queuing study has now
been completed.

Please consider the DRC comments in your motion.


     SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Drive-throughs are allowed uses that may have conditions put on them. The conditions are to mitigate against the
negative impacts that the proposal may have on surrounding properties and the community. The Planning Commission
may consider conditions and if they are reasonable.

The public may speak on this item. Comments should remain focused on the drive through and its potential impact in
the area. Comments that also include potential conditions that could mitigate against the impacts are the most helpful.

Swig Drinks Overview

Swig Drinks is a drive-through and walk-up soda-fountain chain founded in 2010 in St. George,
Utah. There are now over 141 stores in 14 states. Swig prioritizes fast, quality customer service
and serves up “happiness in a cup.” The featured product is known as a “dirty soda” created by
mixing a name-brand soda with flavored syrups, creams, and/or fruit purees. Swig stores also sell
non-soda beverages, energy drinks, and prepackaged snacks such as cookies, pretzel bites, and
suckers.

In addition to providing a quality product, Swig believes in giving back to the communities they
serve. They offer fundraiser options for charities, groups, individuals, and organizations. “Save
the Cups” is a fundraiser throughout the month of October raising money to help women pay off
bills from fighting cancer. The “Share the Love” campaign supports over 30 local charities chosen
by Swig employees, including domestic violence centers, canter treatment and research facilities
and nonprofits that provide accessible mental health services.

Project Overview

This Swig project proposes to remodel a 1,204-square-foot end-cap tenant space in a multi-tenant
building within a large shopping center. The building is addressed as 1678 Timpanagos Highway,
Unit K-101. The proposal requires approval of a Conditional Use Permit as the space was
previously used as a bicycle retail store since 2016.

Based on images from Google Earth, it appears construction of the shopping center was started
in 2009, and the subject tenant space building was constructed in 2011. There is a drive-through
lane around the building, however, no drive-through service window. This proposal involves
remodeling the interior tenant space, adding a drive-through window on the northern wall, and
adding a single door at the front of the store. This shop will be a drive-through only.

The 1,204 square-foot tenant space is located within the shopping center south of Timpanogos
Hwy and west of Catch Basin Road. Behind the building is a 10.5-foot-wide drive-through lane
that begins at the south end of the building by the Asian Bistro and Sushi Bar, continues north
behind the building, and turns west to the new Swig drive-thru window. The drive aisle behind the
building will provide the stacking and queuing for the Swig.

Queuing and Stacking

Approximately 180 feet of stacking space is available from the beginning of the drive-through lane
to the drive-through window providing queuing for nine (9) vehicles. Other Swig stores in Utah
have similar stacking space and operate without impacting adjacent drive-aisles or rights-of-way.

Signs

Signs proposed at the project site will conform to the City of Lehi code, and include wall, menu,
and directional signs. Colorful and visually interesting wall signs depicting the Swig Drinks logos
will be installed on the north and west elevations of the building. Free-standing menu boards are
proposed along the east side of the building between the building and drive-through lane. Signs
will be constructed with high-quality materials and properly installed under separate permits.

Operation

This location is anticipated to have five to seven employees and will accommodate drive-through
customers only. Swig does not use speaker boxes to take customer orders, rather staff members
are outside with handheld tablets taking orders that are immediately sent to the kitchen for
preparation. When customers arrive at the window, their order is ready, reducing the amount of
time spent in the queue and eliminating the noise created by a speaker box.

The typical business hours for Swig Drinks are 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., seven (7) days a week. Hours
of operation may be altered to best fit the needs of the particular community. This proposal requests
approval to operate 24-hours should the market demand continuous operation.

Comments

The queuing study points to several reasons why queuing won’t be as long at this site as the
Lehi State St and Saratoga Springs SWIG locations. None of these reasons are quantifiable,
rather they are theories of why some SWIG locations have more traffic than others based
on proximity to other SWIG locations and whether they have direct access to nearby
highways. This doesn’t make me confident that we won’t have a queuing problem here.



I have past experience when a cookie store with a drive-through proposed near the Lehi
SWIG which was regularly queuing into State St at the time. The expectation presented by
the applicant was that the opening of the Saratoga SWIG will draw traffic away from the
Lehi State St SWIG and remove the queuing issue. It arguably has, but nearly two years
after the Saratoga SWIG opened even this queuing study observed traffic queueing into
State St at that Lehi SWIG location. What may not be a queuing issue initially may develop
into one in the future.



Suggested Conditions



Approve with the condition that queuing from the drive through will not impact traffic flow
through the larger development and will not otherwise negatively impact other businesses.
In the case that these things happen, SWIG must modify the drive-through to
accommodate the longer queues or adjust their operation to be walk-in only during the
time periods when queuing is a problem.

It is important to note that the orders are typically taken by “runner employees” that moves back with the
queue taking the orders so the vehicles don’t have to actually reach the order board to place their orders and
this allows the maximum time by the server employees to produce the drinks. Often there are two runners
taking orders.

The proposed SWIG on Timpanogas Highway is also located 3.2 miles from the existing SWIG in Traverse
Mountain in the Harmons center and therefore this new site will likely serve the same customers as are
using that location. The proposed Timpanogas site has a 275 foot drive through lane which can
accommodate 11 vehicles. Additional queueing would then begin to block the drive isle lanes for the
shopping center but will not block public streets.

Some additional comments to consider are that how busy a store is varies depending on several factors.
Some of the most important ones is service area and density, proximity to similar services and convenience.
These SWIG facilities like many drive-thru facility have a very high percentage of pass-by related traffic
associated with them. Why the proposed SWIG is not likely to be as busy as the Lehi or Saratoga Springs
includes the following:

        The proposed Timpanogas SWIG is located in the rear of the shopping center with no direct access
         to the Highway, so this reduces impulse customers from the Highway. The customers of this
         proposed SWIG will likely be either customers who are making this location a destination or
         customers already in the Shopping Center. The State Street Swig has easy access on and off the
         highway which allows individuals traveling on State Street to be more impulsive as it has a more
         convenient access.
        The proposed SWIG is similar to the American Fork SWIG which is roughly 2.15 miles from the
         State Street SWIG, in a multi-tenant shopping center with 200 feet of queuing. The American
         Fork SWIG doesn’t seem to have the same queueing concerns likely because it is also not one
         block off a freeway interchange.
        Saratoga SWIG is likely a busy site because the next closest SWIG is 4.5 miles away, the Lehi
         State Street Swig which is the busiest SWIG is the area. The next closest is 6.5 miles away, the
         American Fork SWIG.
        The closer distance between the proposed Timpanogos SWIG and the existing Traverse Mountain
         SWIG (3.2 miles) means that this new SWIG will balance the demands and help siphon off
         customers from Traverse Mountain SWIG, not generate all new customers.

For this Timpanogas SWIG, the above factors is why it is not anticipated to generate the same queues seen
at the Lehi SWIG and also any congestion will be limited to within the site circulation of the commercial
parking fields as it is in Saratoga Springs, American Fork and Traverse Mountain and not back onto public
streets as the Lehi State Street site does at times.
