3. Text Amendment — Lift Stations; Amend the 2009 City Code Adding Section 9-4-4;

Sanitary Sewer Pumping and Lift Stations; City-wide applicability; City of West Jordan
(applicant)

Greg Davenport, Public Utility Director, explained the purpose of a sewer lift station that lifts sewer

from a lower elevation to a higher elevation. He explained all of the components that are involved in

the operation and maintenance of the system. West Jordan’s Sanitary Sewer Policy and Design

Criteria Manual currently has policies against them in the public sewer line. There are design

guidelines for private sewer lift stations. West Jordan does not have any public sewer lift stations.

There is one private lift station near the Jordan River. The HOA of that system hires a company to

monitor it 24/7. This code amendment was requested by the City Council to strengthen the design code

by adding it to the City municipal code. He read the new section of the code.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City

Council for this application.

George Winn asked how much it costs to build a sewer lift station.

Greg Davenport said there are pre-manufactured and on-site manufactured systems. The least

expensive type is a wet well with a submersible pump, and the other type has the dry pump outside of

the well. Depending on the amount of flow and the type of system he would guess that costs could be
between $50,000 and $100,000 for 50 homes. A public lift station could be anywhere between
$100,000 to millions of dollars.

Ammon Allen opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

MOTION: Kent Shelton moved, based on the information and findings set forth and the
evidence and explanations received today, to forward a positive recommendation
to the City Council for the Text Amendment adding Section 9-4-4 Sanitary Sewer
Pumping and Lift Stations to the 2009 City Code provided in the staff report. The
motion was seconded by Jay Thomas and passed 6-0 in favor. Trish Hatch was
absent.
