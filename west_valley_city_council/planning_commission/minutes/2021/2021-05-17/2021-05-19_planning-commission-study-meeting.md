ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-2-2021 Petition by ADAM MAHER requesting a General Plan change from Non-Retail
          Commercial to High Density Residential (12 to 20 units/acre) and a zone change
          from RB (Residential Business) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). The
          property is located at 3540 West 4700 South on 1.08 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik
          at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that the subject property and the parcel to the west were
              originally part of a two-lot subdivision. He said both lots have a shared access off of
              4700 South. The subject property has access to 3600 West through the neighboring
              property. Steve stated that there is an existing home on the east side of the property.
              Steve said there had been a discussion with Public Works, and they will not allow
              another access off of 4700 South, where a shared access already exists.

              Steve explained that this proposal was submitted requesting fourteen units with a
              General Plan request High Density Residential (12 to 20 units/acre). Steve said that

               not need to approve the request.

               Commissioner Lovato said there had been conversation amongst the Commission on
               how many of these developments the market can support.

               Commissioner Winters asked if the subject location is eligible to apply for RM
               zoning because of its access onto 4700 South. Steve said that is correct. To be
               eligible to apply for the RM zone, there needs to be access and frontage onto at least
               a 100-foot right-of-way, and 4700 South is a 106-foot right-of-way.

               Commissioner Winters said his only concern is this development will be surrounded
               mainly by single-family homes, and the adjacent residents would not welcome the
               development. He asked Steve if they were to develop single-family detached homes
               on the subject property would they need to request R-1-8 zoning. Steve said they
               could request R-1-8 or R-1-7 at this location.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-10-2021 Petition by EZEQUIEL MARTINEZ FLORES requesting conditional use approval
          for a shopping center. The property is located at 4936 W 3500 S on 2.47 acres and is
          zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
          brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Brock explained that the subject property has a school and then an American First
               Credit Union located to the west and a vacant building to the east, which at one time
               was a bank. He explained that on the east side of the subject property are existing
               shared access driveways allowing for only 1.63 acres to be developed. Brock said
               the access to the site is the shared access off of 4960 West. He pointed out that a
               portion of the property is not addressed in the site plan, and the applicant is working
               on revising the site plan to incorporate the missing section.

               Brock stated that the applicant is proposing a multi-tenant retail building. The
               applicant will have their bakery located in the main building, located on the
               southeast corner, and lease out the other tenant’s spaces. Brock explained the site

               applicant is planning to utilize the area not addressed on the site plan for part of the

               Brock said a pedestrian connection should be added to the subject property along

               Commissioner Wood asked what business would be using the drive-thru. Brock said
               it is to be used for a future tenant, and he is not sure who that will be at this time. He
               said he would follow up with the applicant.

               Commissioner Wood asked if the back of the building is what would be most visible
               when driving down 3500 South. Brock explained the south elevation would be
               considered the primary façade since it fronts onto 3500 South. Brock said the east
               elevation would also be visible from 3500 South, so it would be considered a
               secondary façade, and they need to add relief and design standards to that elevation.

               Commissioner Wood asked if all deliveries would be made on the same side of the
               building. Brock said he believes the doors on the rear of the building are for
               employees, but he will check. He stated the deliveries would all be coming in from
               the west side.

               Commissioner Wood asked if the vacant bank is part of this project. Brock said that
               the property has a different owner and is not part of this proposal.



               Commissioner Wood expressed a possible safety concerns with the school located
               north of the American First Credit Union. She explained that there is no bussing to
               or from the school, so parents pick up and drop off their children. She wonders if this
               proposal would increase traffic and safety risks.



There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:15 p.m.
