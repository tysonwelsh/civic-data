OTHER ITEMS:

© Legislative Update — Executive Buttars reported that SB136 will impact the way Cache
County can distribute the fourth quarter sales tax if the County decides to put the sales
tax option on the ballot. Cache County is on the verge of becoming a second class
county and the bill treats first and second class counties differently from third class
counties. Buttars is working with Runhaar to consider the various scenarios and is
seeking clarification from the Tax Commission.

© Cache County Fair Update — The Fair Chairman is Lane Parker and Cassidy Nemelka
has been working with vendors and advertising contracts for the fair and rodeo. This
year’s theme is “Celebrating the Past & Creating the Future.” The Events Center roof is
partially on.

OG Homeless Bill — Council member Merrill asked if the bill dealing with the homeless
passed and if it will affect the County? Attorney Swink said homeless shelter bills - HB
462 and SB 235 — do not appear to have an impact on counties.

ITEMS OF SPECIAL INTEREST

0° Proclamation ~ Child Abuse Prevention Month — The Family Place — David Zook,
Board Member for The Family Place, thanked the county for its support of The Family
Place. Their mission is to protect children and strengthen families. There were 364
substantiated cases of child abuse in 2017 in the region, which includes Rich County.
The Family Place has served over 11,000 adults and children this past year at their three
facilities - Logan, Hyrum and Smithfield. There is a shelter provided as well as classes
and counseling. Zook asked for the county’s help by continuing to have a member serve
on The Family Place Board and would like the Council to provide a liaison for them.
Zook invited the Council to the annual dinner April 13, 2018 at the Riverwoods
Conference Center and asked for their support of the proclamation.

Council member Merrill read the proclamation.

e Ordinance No. 2018-02 — Code Amendments (NO ACTION TAKEN) — Chris Harrild

CHILD ABUSE PREVENTION MONTH
PROCLAMATION

CACHE COUNTY’S GREATEST ASSET IS OUR CHILDREN,
NOW AND FOR THE FUTURE.

WHEREAS; ALL CHILDREN deserve to grow up in a safe and nurturing
environment to ensure they reach their full potential.

WHEREAS; CHILD ABUSE is a serious and growing problem affecting millions of
our nation’s children and thousands of children in Utah annually; and,

WHEREAS; CHILD ABUSE respects no racial, religious, class or geographic
boundaries; and,

WHEREAS; IT Is IMPORTANT for all citizens of Cache County to become more
aware of child abuse and the critical need for prevention within their respective
neighborhoods and community; and,

WHEREAS; DECREASING the occurrence of child abuse relies upon the efforts of

every individual in order to make a positive, substantial impact upon the children of today,
who will become the leaders of tomorrow;

THEREFORE; we, the Cache County Council, do hereby proclaim April 2018 as
Child Abuse Prevention Month. We support child abuse prevention efforts and education
and encourage all citizens to actively help protect our children and work to create strong
families within this community.

In witness whereof, I hereunto set my hand on this 13" day of March, 2018
