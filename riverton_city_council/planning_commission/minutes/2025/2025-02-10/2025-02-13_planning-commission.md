       A.     “FORD PARALLEL FENCE”, PLZ-25-2001, A CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT
              APPLICATION FOR A PARALLEL FENCE AT 13523 SOUTH REDWOOD
              ROAD. APPLICANT – ROBERT FORD.

Planner Lisa Halversen presented the Staff Report and stated that the request is for a
Conditional Use Permit (“CUP”) for Robert Ford who would like to install parallel fencing
along an existing fence line on his property. An aerial view of the lot was displayed. The
property is located in the southeast quadrant of the City near Riverwood Estates. The
property is approximately 2.8 acres in size and is zoned RR-22 as are the neighboring
properties. Mr. Ford is seeking a parallel fence, which is referred to in City Code as a fence
within a fence. The issue the applicant is facing is that there is an elevation difference
between the subject property and the surrounding properties. Mr. Ford’s property sits lower
in elevation. As a result, the existing six-foot fence does not provide Mr. Ford the privacy
he would like. He is proposing a taller fence along part of the north property line for
approximately 132 feet.

Ms. Halversen reported that Riverton City Ordinance allows for the proposed fence with
conditions, that were reviewed as follows:

       1.     The second fence must be constructed wholly on the applicant’s property.

       2.     The new fence cannot lean against an existing fence for support.

       3.     The applicant must be responsible for placing permanent weed control
              measures between the two fences.

       4.     The applicant is responsible for permanently maintaining the space between
              the two fences.

       5.     The new fence must meet all Riverton City Code requirements.

A diagram was presented showing what Mr. Ford plans to do between the two fences in
terms of the weed barrier and weed maintenance. He has agreed to take on that

that the Code requirements have been met. Staff recommended approval subject to the

Commissioner Cannon asked if the applicant was to install a fence on his property at his
expense if he would be limited in terms of colors that he could paint it. He also asked if the

new one is constructed. City Attorney, Ryan Carter did not believe there were any
restrictions on fencing color but stated that the purpose of tonight’s meeting was to discuss
the potential conditions and impacts of parallel fencing. That focuses on the space between
the two and how it is maintained. He did not believe there was any stipulation between two
residentially zoned properties on what the color of the fence should be.

In response to a question raised by Commissioner Cluff, it was clarified that the amount of
space between the two fences where the weed control barrier is to be placed was not
identified by the applicant or in the Code.

attractive and of earth color that looks nice. The fence will be engineered concrete. It will
be close enough to not interfere with the existing fence. He estimated a space of four inches

keep the weeds down. He stated that the existing fence is plastic and is not substantial. He
considered what is proposed to be safe. He stated that he is paying for the fence and offered
to remove the existing fence if desired by his neighbors. He also offered to paint both sides
of the fence. He did his best to ensure that they could participate if desired.

north side of the fence, there is a retaining wall. In that location, the ground is not resting

to the site plan they would have come back to the Commission.

Reception Center, which is permitted in the Commercial Gateway Zone. Chair Park reported
that Mrs. White has been operating a dance studio out of the facility for the past 14 years.

be located next door. The dance studio will remain.

      A.     MCKENZIE PARK ESTATES DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT (AN
             INTRODUCTION TO A PENDING DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT WHICH
             THE PLANNING COMMISSION WILL HEAR AS A PUBLIC HEARING ITEM
             IN AN UPCOMING MEETING).

Development Services Director, Jason Lethbridge, reported that the Development
Agreement is located between Bangerter Highway and 4000 West in a platted area. A
subdivision was approved in 2007 and the property was divided into individual lots with the
plat having been recorded. It went defunct in 2008 through various owners and legal
challenges, but due to recordation, a dedicated space for future public rights-of-way exists,
and there are one-third acre lots. One lot will remain a private lane and previously built
home but others will be converted from residential to commercial for the purpose of
Riverton City Planning Commission Meeting                                                13

constructing storage units. There are nearby storage units, and a Church of Jesus Christ of
Latter-day Saints (“LDS”), which borders the property on both the north and south sides.
The Development Agreement will limit the property use to storage facility use and serve to
rezone the property to commercial regional. More information will be presented during the
public hearing.

Krisel Travis, from DAI, reported that the storage units will be built in two phases. DAI has
completed several developments with Extra Space Storage in different locations across the
Wasatch Front and this location at 2299 South 4000 West will be partnered with Extra Space
Storage, as well. Ms. Travis addressed previous Staff concerns about stagnation during the
two phases. She stated that storage is in high demand. Phase 1 will include about 80,000
square feet of development of interior and exterior storage buildings in addition to RV
storage on a paved surface. All buildings are one story. She reiterated that Lots 1, 2, and
6 will remain as residential lots and will be part of the Master Development Agreement.
Phase 2 will include about 23,000 square feet to replace and reduce RV storage. They are
asking for a 15-year term for that development. There is the potential to expand in the future
from 106,000 square feet to 160,000 square feet by making some of the buildings multi-
storied. The building materials are Concrete Masonry (“CMU”) and stucco.

Ms. Travis stated that there is a proposal to hire an Onsite Manager to assist with security,
so there is an office residence within the site plans. There is also a need for extra signage
despite the City ordinance availability, which is included in the proposal. The LDS Church
has an existing fence on the north side of the proposed storage property, which the proposal
requests remain. There is also proposed fencing to be added in addition to gated enclosures
that would use CMU and precast fencing structures. There is exposure to Bangerter
Highway on the east side, and the proposal requests 96 feet of elevated signage on the
north, south, and east sides. The monument sign meets the City ordinance.

Commissioner Cluff asked if the residential property owners had been informed of the
development plans, to which Ms. Travis stated there have been agreements relating to
boundaries and facilitating the flag lot. The Development Agreement will address frontage
improvements. Mr. Lethbridge confirmed that communication from the developer, City
Council members from the area, and residents expressed acceptance of the project’s
proposed positive impacts on the neighborhood. Commissioner Cannon reported that he
has been part of previous proposals and plans for this area and approved of the current
proposal. Commissioner Rushton asked about the maximum height for buildout, to which
Ms. Travis stated that all buildings will be required to comply with the CR zoning heights,
which is four stories or within 80 feet, but the plans are for less. The setback requirements
increase as buildings increase in height.

Commissioner Rushton asked if all existing infrastructure will need to be removed.
Ms. Travis confirmed that was the case. She stated that they will also be handling the storm
drain detention basin and the City’s obligations to the LDS Church as it relates to the parcel.
Commissioner Rushton asked about street improvements on 4000 West. It was reported
that it will be realigned. The proposal will also improve the sidewalk and the curb and gutter
