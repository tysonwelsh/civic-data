                                    PUBLIC NOTICE

Please be advised that on March 20, 2025, the City Council of the City of St George
approved the following ordinance(s):


Ordinance No. 2025-020 amending the city zoning map by changing the zone from C-2
(Highway Commercial) to PD-C (Planned Development Commercial) on approximately 1.88
acres generally located on the southwest corner of 1470 East and 170 South.

Ordinance No. 2025-021 amending the Atkinville Interchange Area Zone Plan PD on
approximately 5.91 acres.

Ordinance No. 2025-022 amending a portion of Title 9- 13-4 of the City Code to prohibit
off-premises signs along SR-7 (State Route 7), also known as Southern Parkway.
