3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of DR Horton’s request for preliminary
subdivision review of Skye Plat A, a 382-unit residential development located at
approximately 3950 North Center Street in the Skye Planned Community.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item. Includes mixed use, medium and low
density, along with a civic site. Applicant has worked with Staff for pedestrian connectivity for
safe passage. The applicant is requesting an exception to block length while still providing
connectivity to pedestrian access. DRC had one redline comment for design for the final plat.

Commissioner Everett asked about pedestrian connectivity. Ross Dinsdale clarified the exception
they are requesting is in regard to block length due to grading. In an effort to try to minimize
grading, they are adding pedestrian access. The fire marshal has reviewed the exception and does
not have an issue with the request. They warrant the exception because of grade but are trying to
add pedestrian access also.

The applicant, Scott Bishop with DR Horton, was present. He stated they have talked about
pulling out single family homes and townhomes to allow for more parking. They have complied
with the new parking ordinance and providing more parking, particularly around the condensed
areas. Scott explained the clubhouse would be located outside of the plat that is shown and will
be in the annexed part of Draper that is now Lehi, which is not being shown as part of the site
plan tonight. The footprint will be green scape. If the requirements aren't met, a cost comparison
must be shown and met.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Brent Frampton – Had questions on the trailhead parking designation. Are their designated
phases that are in order.

Rachel Mince – Is there an ADA accessibility and why there should be an exception or
consideration. ADA laws change and whose responsibility should it be to fix it.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Ross Dinsdale responded to ADA compliance and safety. Scott Bishop addressed the order of
plats and can show that there is an order as they progress. Commissioner Jackson wanted to
clarify they have administrative issues and legislative issues they have authority to vote on.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of DR Horton’s request
        for preliminary subdivision review of Skye Plat A, a 382-unit residential development
        located at approximately 3950 North Center Street in the Skye Planned Community;
        including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report with the exception to the
        block length request. Noting a trigger for a timeframe. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

Cookies Academy General Plan Amendment, changing the designation from VLDRA (very
low-density residential agriculture) to NC (neighborhood commercial) on 2-acres located at
1910 West 900 North.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

have operated for 18 years. They would like to expand the daycare. The infrastructure is already
established. The home will be occupied as a daycare only. The applicants have a bus. There is a
pickup/drop off lane. Parents can drop their kids off and pick them up without having to leave their
vehicles. They typically avoid high traffic due to when the children are dropped off and picked up.
Hours of operation are anticipated 8:00AM to 6:00PM.

Matthew Jorgenson – Had been helping the landlord and can address parking and traffic. There is

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to send of positive recommendation of Jamie
        Barnes’ request for review of the Smart Cookies Academy General Plan Amendment,
