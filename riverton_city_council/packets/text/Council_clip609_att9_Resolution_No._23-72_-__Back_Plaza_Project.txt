                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 23-72

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
WITH BIG BEAR ELECTRIC FOR THE COMPLETION OF THE CITY HALL BACK
                         PLAZA PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, Purchasing bid out the City Hall Back Plaza Project and the bids came in
over budget; and

      WHEREAS, the Recreation Director would still like to proceed with this project; and

        WHEREAS, if approved, Council will need to approve $225,000.00 as an added expense
in a future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
      1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Big Bear
         Electric for the completion of the City Hall Back Plaza Project.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 5th day of
September 2023 by the following vote:
