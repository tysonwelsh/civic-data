19            SLR Update - Mr. Dan McCay
20
21   Mr. McCay presented an update regarding the Suburban Land Reserve (SLR) property as it
22   relates to the Center Cal Project. He said that Center Cal had to adjust their work schedule due
23   to COVID and that it was now moving forward on Phase Two. He also said that the nearby
24   Costco project had finalized the initial agreement phase and was now moving forward. He said
25   they were now entering into the new development phase with Edge Homes building an
26   anticipated 350 units, which were anticipated to be permitted in June. He said the area of land
27   connected with Herriman land, with an anticipated 1,500 units and three large parks there. He
28   said SLR had engaged Edge Homes to build on the Herriman side and ground had already
29   been broken. He said there was a great demand for housing in the community.
30
31   A discussion regarding the 16 acres of designated open space required in the Master
32   Development Agreement occurred. Councilmembers will discuss their preference for location
33   and use of these spaces. This discussion includes a possible joint venture with Jordan School
34   District for a swimming pool to be used by the three nearby high schools.
35
36   Mr. McCay stated that SLR was committed to the City’s open space requirements and said the
37   City needed to determine how and where the open spaces could be filled.
38
39   Mayor Staggs spoke of Jordan School District providing a community center, which had been
40   projected to have up to 3,400 residents; however, a community center plan anticipated by SLR
41   was projected to have up to 2,000 residents. Councilmembers said they would discuss the
42   proposed ideas for the open space.

 4            Ordinance No. 21-01 - Proposed Rezone of the Existing 1.4 Acre Commercial
 5            Development at 2998 West 12600 South from C-PO to C-G (Commercial Gateway) -
 6            Gorm Klungervik, Applicant
 7
 8   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained an application for rezone of
 9   property located at 2998 West 12600 S. He said the property was an approved commercial
10   development currently zoned C-PO. He said that a single building had been built, with several
11   open pads remaining. He said the project was approximately 1.4 acres, and the property to the
12   north and east was zoned RR-22, although a portion of the property to the east was the
13   Riverton High School site. He said the property to the west was zoned C-PO.
14
15   Mr. Lethbridge said the development was designed and approved with five pads intended for
16   medical office use. He said that following the construction of the existing building, the owner had
17   not had tenant interest sufficient to fill the other pad spaces; however, he approached the City
18   with potential tenants, but many had been more retail in nature than the Professional Office
19   zone allows. He said that, in response, the owner submitted an application requesting a zone
20   change from Professional Office to Commercial Gateway to expand the range of uses available
21   to the project.
22
23   Mr. Lethbridge said that on December 10, 2020, the Planning Commission held a public hearing
24   to consider this item and voted to recommend approval of the rezone application. He then
25   addressed comments and questions from the Council Members.
26
27   Mayor Staggs opened a Public Hearing and called for public comment; there being none, Mayor
28   Staggs declared the Public Hearing closed.
29
30   Councilmember McCay MOVED that the City Council adopt Ordinance No. 21-01, the
31   Klungervik Rezone, rezoning the subject properties located at 2998 West 12600 South
32   from C-PO (Commercial-Professional Office) to C-G (Commercial Gateway).
33   Councilmember Buroker SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the
34   motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as follows:
35   Councilmembers Buroker-Yes, McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The
36   motion passed unanimously.
