Approval of Minutes
         Board member Blair stated he had reviewed the minutes of the regular meeting of March 12, 2024, and found them to be
accurate to the best of his recollection.
         BOARD MEMBER BLAIR THEN MOVED TO APPROVE THE MINUTES AS PRESENTED. MOTION WAS
SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER CHOBERKA, ALL VOTING AYE.

Proposed Resolution 2024-1 regarding a budget amendment in the amount
of $66,064,650 to recognize and appropriate bond proceeds and additional
funds to the Redevelopment Agency General Fund, Housing Fund, Hinckley
Drive Redevelopment Area and Continental Redevelopment Area budgets.
         Chair Richey announced that there was an error in the noticing process for this proposed budget amendment. The Board
will consider tabling the public hearing and action on the budget amendment until the May 7, 2024, meeting; this will allow
sufficient time for proper noticing.
         BOARD MEMBER HYER MOVED TO TABLE PROPOSED FISCAL YEAR 2023-2024 BUDGET
AMENDMENT – SALES TAX AND TAX INCREMENT BONDS SERIES 2023 UNTIL MAY 7, 2024. MOTION WAS
SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER CHOBERKA, ALL VOTING AYE.


     There being no further business to come before the Agency, BOARD MEMBER GRAF MOVED THE MEETING
ADJOURN AT 8:35 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER HYER, ALL VOTING AYE.
