3. Change of Nonconforming Use, Landscaping business, 241 21° St

Petitioner Brandon Cundieff introduced himself and explained the proposal for a lawn care and
product provider. He highlighted the reduction in outside storage and the regular business hours,
emphasizing minimal noise and traffic. He also mentioned maintaining the front and back elevations
of the building and plans to clean up the landscaping and confirmed they will be working with the
Landlord to find a solution for the road easement.

Assistant Planning Director Joseph Simpson presented the considerations for the proposed
use, including compatibility with surrounding properties and site development requirements including
a history of the property, with previous uses and zoning changes, and outlined the proposed use as a
lawn care service. He discussed the potential impact of a road expansion and the abandonment of the
previous use and clarified that the second lot does not have non-conforming rights and cannot be
used for the same purpose.

COMISSIONER SANDAU MOVED TO APPROVE THE CONDITIONAL USE AMENDMENT
SUBJECT TO THE TWO RECOMMENDED CONDITIONS. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY
COMMISSIONER SOUTHWICK AND PASSED UPON BY THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE:
VOTING AYE — COMMISSIONERS SANDAU, SHALE, SHINODA, SOUTHWICK, WILLIAMS, AND
CHAIR SAFSTEN. VOTING NO — COMMISSIONER AABERG, STATING IT WOULD MAKE MORE
SENSE TO CHANGE THE ZONING AND COMBINE IT, AND COMMISSIONER ROSS, WHO FELT
ENOUGH TIME HAD ELAPSED SINCE THE PREVIOUS CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT AND THAT IT
DIDN'T MEET NEW ZONING CODES.

4. Special Exception, painting brick in East Central,539 24" St
Petitioner Craig Wilcox explained the need to paint the brick due to failing brick in several
areas. Ken Brothers, a donor and supporter of the Seager Memorial Clinic, supports the painting as
necessary to preserve the brick.
Planner Haylie Hale presents the staff's recommendation to deny the special exception, citing
the need for masonry repair rather than painting.

COMMISSIONER SOUTHWICK MOVED TO APPROVE STAFF SUGGESTIONS TO DENY THE
EXCEPTION. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COMMISSIONER ROSS AND PASSED UPON BY THE
FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE ~ COMMISSIONERS AABERG, ROSS, SANDAU, SHALE,
SHINODA, SOUTHWICK, WILLIAMS AND CHAIR SAFSTEN. VOTING NO — NONE
