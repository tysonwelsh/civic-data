ZONE TEXT APPLICATION
ZT-10-2020  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zone text change to allow outdoor event centers in
            conjunction with a regional shopping mall as a conditional use in the C-2 (General Commercial) zone.
            (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik explained that outdoor event centers, such as concert venues, are not allowed in the C-2
                 zone, and this proposal would allow for smaller outdoor events in the C-2 zone. Steve explained the City
                 has been working with the Valley Fair Mall on ways to have legitimate outdoor events in their parking
                 areas. Steve referenced the plaza on the west side of the mall entrance as another location where events
                 could be held. He also pointed out that they have had car shows closer to 3500 South. Steve explained
                 that with the conditional use permit; the Planning Commission can outline restrictions to minimize
                 impacts on adjoining properties hours of operation.

                 Chair Meaders asked if they would need to come back before the Commission for a conditional use for
                 each event. Steve said they would not if they follow the conditions of the conditional use. If they are
                 looking to do something outside the conditional use parameters, they would need to come back to the
                 Commission for approval.

                 Commissioner Winters feels holding these outdoor events would be a great addition to the community.
                 His only concern would be the noise level causing a disturbance. He asked if there was any way to place
                 a decibel requirement on the events. Steve said he was not aware of any sound caps that are imposed;
                 however, restrictions can be applied as to what location the event will be held and what time.
                 Commissioner Winters does not feel he knows the science of sound and asked if someone could
                 reference the best placement for louder events. Steve said most malls are not located next to residential
                 areas. He pointed out the places where we don't want to see events and outlined other areas that would be
                 okay.
GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
GPZ-8-2020  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a General Plan change from Very High Density
            Residential (over 20 units/acre) to High Density Residential (12 to 20 units/acre) and a zone change
            from CC (City Center) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) and R-2-8 (Two Unit Dwelling
            Residential, Minimum Lot Size 8,000 sq. ft.). The property is located at 3060 West 3650 South on 6.73
            acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545)

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik explained that the subject property is the Valley Fair Apartments and the five adjacent
                 duplex properties to the north. Currently, these properties are zoned CC, and the request is to change the
                 Valley Fair Apartments (on the County Housing Authority property) to RM and the duplex properties to
                 R-2-8. Steve explained that in 2016; the City rezoned the subject property to CC with the intent of
                 facilitating a redevelopment project where the County Housing Authority property would be redeveloped
                 along with the duplexes to the north. The County Housing Authority did not sign the development
                 agreement; therefore, the redevelopment project did not happen. The thought is to restore the original
                 zoning and keep everything as is.

                 Commissioner Woodruff asked if the City-owned the vacant lots on the south side of Lehman Ave. Steve
                 said the City does own that land, but the rest of the duplexes are privately owned.

                 Commissioner Woodruff asked if there has been communication with the County Housing Authority.
                 Steve said yes, he talked with Janice Campbell, who's the County Housing Authority director, just a few
                 weeks ago to let her know that the City is initiating the zone change.




  DISCUSSION ITEMS
  Neighborhood Health Audit

   Discussion:   Steve explained the benefit of this audit and asked the Commission if they would like access to the audit.

  Potential City initiated zone changes

  Discussion:    Steve stated that there are areas within the City that are zoned R-2-6.5 (4 parcels totaling 0.7 acres), R-2-
                 8 (51 parcels totaling 14.0 acres), R-4 (5 parcels totaling 4.3 acres), RM (19 parcels totaling 16.6 acres)
                 that are either vacant or not developed as any type of attached housing. Such parcels do not have a
                 development agreement or other approvals for attached housing and are designated for residential use. He
                 would like the Planning Commission to look at this information and decide if they feel the City should
                 rezone them. Steve presented slides of each property fitting the above description. The Commission
                 agreed they would like to come back to this discussion and look at each property to see if zone changes
                 should be applied.

  Application Action Log

  Discussion:    Steve gave an update on all the applications that go to both the Planning Commission and City Council
                 and pointed out where there was a voting difference between the two bodies.

                 Commissioner Winters asked a question related to Z-6-2020, a petition by DR Horton requesting a zone
                 change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential), R-1-
                 10 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 10,000 sq. ft.) and C-2 (General Commercial).
                 The property is located at 4600 S 5600 W on 11.0 acres. This proposal was approved by the Planning
                 Commission and denied by the City Council. Commissioner Winters felt like this would be a good
                 location for the townhomes because of the surrounding area. He asked if there was a shift in the
                 Council’s mindset or declaration of a stand. Steve said there were two reasons discussed by the City
                 Council that lead to the denial of this application. The Council had received many complaints from the
                 residents about the noise coming from the railroad tracks, and they did not want to put more residents in
                 that location. The second reason is they felt the townhomes proposed by Dr. Horton were not to the same
                 level as some of the other recently approved townhome projects.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:30 p.m.
