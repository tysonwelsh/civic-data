MEMO
To: City Council
From: David Dobbins, City Manager
Date: 2024-06-04
Re: Approval of Resolution #24-18


Comments:

Lehi City owns several tracts of real property located in Lehi City known as the Traverse
Mountain Property. The Lehi City Property is recognized as having significant scientific
and educational, wildlife habitat, ecological, scenic, riparian, watershed and open space
benefits to the public. Lehi City would like to protect the scenic and natural qualities of
the land by granting a conservation easement to a qualified holder. Draper City is a
qualified holder under state law and would like to assist Lehi City by holding the
conservation easement. Lehi City and Draper City have a mutual interest in preserving
and protecting Lehi's open space adjacent to Draper City open space.
