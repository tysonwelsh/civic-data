                     COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
                                 RESOLUTION #2020-09-01
                                     September 16, 2020
        A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
          APPOINTING JOEL BREIINHOLT TO THE COPPERTON METRO
                    TOWNSHIP PLANNING COMMISSION
                                          RECITALS
       WHEREAS, Section 19.05.010 of the Copperton Metro Township Code requires the
Copperton Metro Township Council to appoint members to the Copperton Metro Township
Planning Commission; and
        WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint Joel Breinholt to the Planning Commission.
       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED effectively immediately that Joel Breinholt is
appointed to the Coppperton Metro Township Planning Commission with a term ending on March
1, 2023.
        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Salt Lake County, Utah, this 16th day of September
2020.

                                                      Sean Clayton, Mayor
