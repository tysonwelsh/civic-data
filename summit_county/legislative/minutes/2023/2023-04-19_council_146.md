Meeting also conducted via Zoom.
Meeting with Congressman Blake Moore's Staff (12:10 PM)
Executive Conference Room of the Summit County Courthouse
Council Members Armstrong, Stevens, Robinson, and Hanson, along with Manager Shayne Scott, Karen Ballash, and Larry Ballash met with Anthony Coombs and Peter Jenks from Congressman Blake Moore’s office.
No motions were made or official action taken.
The following issues were discussed:
Childcare funding issues in Summit County/Statewide with the ending of ARPA funding. Summit County asked for ideas and potential assistance in understanding what federal legislation may be underway. Congressman Moore’s legislative staff will be offering assistance.
Thanks to Representative Moore's office for his support of High Valley Transit and the need for future transportation and Bus Rapid Transit Funding
Funding for the Weber River Water Resilience Project
The need for watershed protection and some deployment of the County's $50 million open space bond. This included indicating the County may need assistance with federal grants on the Ure Property
The unique nature of Summit County being represented by both Congressman Moore and Congressman Curtis
The County's flooding concerns—Congressman Moore’s Office is interested in understanding what federal assistance may be needed.
Summit County thanked Congressman Moore’s office for the support they have felt during his tenure.
The quorum was dissolved at 1:05 PM.
