2021 Tentative Budget

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting the Kearns Metro
Township tentative 2021 budget:

RESOLUTION NO. 20-11-01                                              Date: November 19, 2020

       A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP’S TENTATIVE
       2021 BUDGET

        WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality and political subdivision
of the state of Utah; and

      WHEREAS, Kearns is required to adopt a tentative budget for the upcoming fiscal year
pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Section 10-6-111; and

                                              1
DATE          THURSDAY                      NOVEMBER                                  19, 2020


        WHEREAS, Kearns prepared a budget for the upcoming 2021 fiscal year and desires to
adopt it as its Tentative 2021 Budget.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

   1. The attached budget is hereby adopted as Kearns’ tentative budget for the 2021 fiscal
      year.

   2. A public hearing shall be held on November 25, 2020, at 6:00 p.m. via WebEx and
      telephone, to receive public comments thereon, with notice thereof being duly provided
      pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-113.

   3. A copy of this tentative budget shall be made available for public inspection for a period
      of at least ten days prior to the public hearing, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-112.

   4. This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to adopt
Resolution No. 20-11-01. The motion passed unanimously.

CARES Act Resolution Amendment

RESOLUTION NO. 20-11-02                                                 Date: November 19, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL REPEALING
       AND REVISING PARAGRAP 4 OF RESOLUTION NO 20-06-01 TO AUTHORIZE
       THE USE OF CARES ACT FUNDING FOR CARES ACT-QUALIFIED EXPENSES
       INCURRED BY LOCAL DISTRICTS THAT PROVIDE SERVICES TO THE
       PUBLIC IN KEARNS BUT ARE NOT CONTRACTED SERVICE PROVIDERS

                                           RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.: and

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing
body for Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3B-501; and

     WHEREAS, Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to UCA Title 17:
COUNTIES; and

       WHEREAS, the CARES Act is a law passed by the Federal Government that provided for
an appropriation of funds to the States and certain Counties for certain governmental emergency
costs and economic relief efforts related to the COVID-19 pandemic; and

      WHEREAS, in May 2020, the State of Utah and Salt Lake County received CARES Act
appropriations from the Federal Government; and

      WHEREAS, on June 25, 2020, the Council adopted Resolution #20-06-01, authorizing
Kearns to enter into an agreement with the County by which Kearns would receive $1,015,500.82
in CARES Act funding from the County to assist with Kearns’ efforts in responding to COVID-19;
and

        WHEREAS, Paragraph 4 of Resolution #20-06-01 specified the uses for which the Council
will expend its CARES Act allocation, excluding expenses incurred by local districts that serve the
public within Kearns but are not contracted service providers; and


                                                3
DATE             THURSDAY                          NOVEMBER                                             19, 2020

        WHEREAS, Kearns’ agreement with the County originally required it to expend its CARES
Act allocation by October 1, 2020, but Kearns requested and received an extension from the
County that allows Kearns to make all qualifying purchases and process all reimbursements by
November 29, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, on September 2, 2020, following the Council’s adoption of Resolution #20-
06-01, the U.S. Department of the Treasury issued updated guidance1 on the CARES Act that
support the use of Kearns’ CARES Act allocation to assist local districts that serve Kearns but are
not contracted service providers; and

1 The guidance is available here: https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/136/Coronavirus-Relief-Fund-Guidance-for-
State-Territorial-Local-and-Tribal-Governments.pdf.

        WHEREAS, the Council desires to amend paragraph 4 of Resolution #20-06-01 to: (1)
specify that all CARES-Act qualified expenses must be incurred by November 29, 2020; and (2)
to allow Kearns’ to use its CARES Act funds to assist local districts that serve Kearns and have
incurred CARES Act-qualified expenses but are not contracted service providers for Kearns.

                                                RESOLUTION

      NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township that effective
immediately Paragraph 4 of Resolution #20-06-01 is repealed and amended to state as follows:

    1. To the extent possible, the Mayor and Council will expend Kearns’ CARES Act funds as
       follows, provided that all CARES-Act qualified purchases and reimbursements must be
       approved by the Council and finalized by 11:59 pm on November 29, 2020:

             a. Reimbursement of CARES Act-qualified expenses incurred by the Unified Fire
                Authority and Unified Police Department as Kearns’s first-responder agencies; and

             b. Reimbursement of CARES Act-qualified expenses incurred by the Greater Salt
                Lake Municipal Services District and its Contracted Service Providers as Kearns’s
                municipal-type service provider; and

             c. Purchase or reimbursement of documented CARES Act-qualified expenses
                requested by local district that provide services to the public within Kearns; and

             d. Reimbursement of any other Act-qualified expenses that Kearns incurs directly
                responding to the COVID-19 pandemic.

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 19th day of November, 2020.

KOPFC PPE Request

             Mayor Bush reviewed an agreement between Kearns Metro Township and
Oquirrh Recreation and Parks District to receive CARES Act funding.

               Nathan Bracken stated when the CARES Act funding was initially accepted, the
funds would pay for Kearns expenses that it directly incurred and that are contractually required
to pay. Kearns Recreation and Parks is not a contractual service provider. It was originally
understood that non-contractual service providers would need to request funds from Salt Lake
County. New guidelines have come out allowing Kearns Metro Township to distribute funds. In
addition, funds will be distributed for transactional costs associated with purchases.

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
approve the agreement. The motion passed unanimously.
