MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Nick Whittaker
Date: 2025-01-09
Re: Public Meeting: Danner I-ADU Deviation Request (Administrative Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Deviation from strict compliance with
geographical restrictions on I-ADU’s for approximately 0.19 acres located on the north
side of Hickenlooper Way, at approximately 1177 E. Hickenlooper Way. The property is
currently zoned RM (Residential Multiple-Family) and is subject to the South Mountain
Development Agreement. The applicant is requesting the Deviation be approved to allow
for the development of an I-ADU in the basement of the existing single-family dwelling.



Findings for Approval:


  1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least six thousand
     square feet (6,000 ft²);
  2. No D-ADU exists on the property;
  3. The proposed I-ADU can meet all of the requirements of sections 9-31-030 and 9-31-
     045, including other applicable requirements of this chapter before issuance of an
     ADU permit; and
  4. At least two (2) parking stalls meeting the requirements of 9-31-045(B)(2) are
     provided for use by occupants of the I-ADU.


Findings for Denial:


  1. The proposed I-ADU does not meet all of the requirements listed in DCMC
     sections 9-31-030 , 9-31-045, or other applicable requirements of Chapter 9-31.
