Mayor Lang asked how viruses are excluded from the backup files. Eric replied there is an
immutable backup. He explained that if a virus is sent to the backup servers and it takes
the City 30 days to detect it, the City is able to restore a portion of the data so everything
is not lost. Mayor Lang asked how much a petabyte is. Ken Cushing, IT, replied 1,000
terabytes. He noted that the proposal will provide 10 times more space which will likely
be filled over the course of the next 5 years.

COMMUNICATIONS
A.  BUDGET/PROGRAM MOD UPDATE
    Wayne Pyle, City Manager, stated that the pay gap initially calculated has been
    reduced. Wayne stated that because of this, the larger tax increase likely won’t need
    to be asked for but added that the Public Safety increase committed to in 2021 will
    still need to occur. Wayne discussed and detailed potential ongoing and one-time
    project program requests. Councilmember Whetstone asked how the potential
    network infrastructure increase would impact items like the backup server purchase
    previously discussed on this agenda. Wayne replied that the network backup system
    was requested as a lease because there is not a budget set aside for this currently.
    He indicated that if the Council chose to approve this program mod request, this
    type of purchase would come directly from the budget and would not be set up as
    a lease. Councilmember Huynh asked if increased maintenance for City facilities is
    a priority. Wayne replied the City does need additional custodial staff to service
    the new or expanded facilities that have developed over the last few years. He
    indicated that the City is getting by and can continue to do so but the longer
    maintenance is deferred, the more difficult it becomes to repair and bring things
    back to standard. Mayor Lang asked if staff could break down the Veteran’s Day
    Program cost. Wayne replied yes.

       The Mayor and Council had no further questions or concerns.
