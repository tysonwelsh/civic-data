Work Session to discuss and receive direction on proposed amendments to Chapter 1: Introduction and Chapter 2: Land Use of the Snyderville Basin General Plan. This item is for discussion and direction only; no recommendation or action will be taken by the Planning Commission.

Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner,
introduced amendments to Chapters 1 and 2 of the Snyderville Basin General Plan for work session discussion and then turned the time over to Planner Mustapha Osman for a brief presentation of the two chapters. (4:32 P.M.)
Mustapha Osman,
Planner
, introduced the 2026 General Plan for the Snyderville Basin stating the goal of the plan was to make the planning process clear, consistent, efficient and predictable. The Chapter 1 Introduction included the history of Snyderville Basin, demographics, and a population profile. Chapter 2 Land Use included existing conditions, land use goals, objectives, policies, and strategies, as well as future use land use maps. (4:33 P.M.)
Commissioners asked questions and provided comments regarding the proposed amendments to the general plan. A robust discussion followed covering many items such as climate resiliency, EV infrastructure, access, town centers, transportation, water, various maps, zone clarification, and policy 2.3 and entitlements. Planners Milliner and Mustapha, and Attorney Lynda Viti responded to concerns and contributed to the discussion. (4:39 P.M.)
Principal Planner Milliner asked for the Commission’s preference on how to proceed with future work sessions. Commissioners responded to his question. (5:59 P.M.)
