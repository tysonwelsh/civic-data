and Lindsay Grove Court (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently zoned RA2. The
applicant is requesting approval of a home occupation business for a candy making
business at the property in a proposed accessory structure.

The home was originally built in 1999 and is Lot 53 of the Akagi Farm Phase 1 residential

The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification. According to Draper City
Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low
density development with little impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to
generally preserve the character of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and
preserve conditions favorable to large-lot family life, including the keeping of limited
numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be
detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and
industrial uses.”

Home Occupation. The proposed business is a wholesale caramel business. There are no
customers coming to the home and all deliveries of the candies are made by the applicant
and his family. The applicant is proposing to construct a building in their rear yard area
that will be utilized as a commercial kitchen. The structure will have the appearance of a
cottage that is complimentary to the design of the house. The proposed building is
approximately 16 x 30 or 480 square feet. The applicant had initially proposed a 12 x 30
for 360 square foot building, but due to the location on the lot and required setbacks, the
location was moved. The new location allows the applicant to have a slightly larger
building. The building will still need to obtain building permits and all development
standards will be verified by Planning with that building permit. Because this is a corner lot
with streets on three sides of it, there will be minimal impact on the neighborhood. The
proposed accessory building is not different than other properties that have accessory
buildings.

STATEMENT OF COMPLIANCE WITH STANDARDS

In addition to the requirements listed as part of 9-5-080, our building will also abide by the requirements
laid out as part of our HOA’s CC&R, which include making the building appear similar to the main house
it is located behind. As such, our building will have white trim, a red brick color on the panels, and a grey
shingle roof. It will also be similar to the architectural style of our existing house at 1328 E. Hanna Ct.

Our business is a wholesale caramel business. As such we do not have customers visiting our property,
nor are we selling items from our home or this building. Its use will be strictly for the production of
caramels during regular business hours on days as needed, Sunday – Saturday.

The area that this building will be located in backs up to the street (1300 E). Therefore, any possible
nuisance behavior (and we can’t think of any that would be produced) will have no impact on our
neighbors.



OVERVIEW OF PROPOSED USE

We’d like to build a building to house the production of our caramels, which we sell wholesale to local
merchants (Angela’s Finest Caramels). The unit will be a fully finished building, including insulation,
wiring, lighting, and paneling as described by the Health code for commercial kitchens, and abiding by all
local codes and requirements. It will be 360 sq ft in size (12 x 30). We will run complete sewer, water,
and electrical lines to this building. It will resemble a small cottage in appearance (please see proposed
views in the other documents).

As mentioned above, the building will only be used during standard business hours on Sunday –
Saturday. Exceptions to these times will be minimal. Since the business is wholesale, we deliver the
caramels ourselves; there are no changes to parking or traffic patterns, and we do not have patrons
coming to our facility. The noise level will be minimal, maybe the occasional clanging of pots and pans.
We are locating the building on the portion of our property that backs up to 1300 E, and equidistant
from our neighbors on either side to mitigate any undesired noises.
