       5.2.        Allied Sign Site Plan Amendment

           Senior Planner Cache Hancey provided context on the application and discussed the
       compliance with the city code.

       Commissioner Caden Rhoton arrived at 6:02pm.

       Matt Phillips, the applicant, arrived at 6:03pm.

              Chair Steele requested locational context.

        Discussion ensued.

              Mr. Phillips noted his intention and the purpose for the amendment.

       Chair Steele asked about screening.

              Mr. Phillips noted that they will likely put up fencing.

       Discussion ensued.

       Public Works Director and City Engineer Naseem Ghandour entered at 6:08pm.

       There were no public comments.

              Chair Steele asked about code for screening.

       Senior Planner Hancey responding explaining no.

          Motion: COMMISSIONER RHOTON MOTOIONED TO APPROVE THE SITE
       PLAN AMENDMENT AS PRESENTED AT 6:09PM. COMMISSIONER FAGG
       SECONDED. ALL IN FAVOR VOTED YES: FAGG, PEARCE, STEELE, RHOTON,
       AND OSTLER. THE VOTE CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.
