1.      LOT LINE ADJUSTMENT. Review a proposal to move lot lines for zoning compliance reasons and for
        the purpose of building a cabin. Parcels are currently zoned A5(Agricultural 5-acre minimum lot size).
        Location is approximately 350 W 4000 N, Central Utah, 84782. The applicant is Troy Ence.

2.      ZONE CHANGE. Review a staff-initiated proposal to bring parcels 3276-D-HV, 3276-A-1-HV, 3276-C-
        HV, 3268-A-2-HV, 3278-HV and 3277-HV into zoning compliance. The parcels are currently zoned
        OST-20 (Open Space Transition 20-acre minimum lot size). Staff recommends this to be changed to a
        RE-40(Residential Estate 40,000 square feet minimum lot size) zone. The parcels are located along
        Hidden Valley Road in Leeds. The applicant is the Community Development Department.

3.      ZONE CHANGE. Review a staff-initiated proposal to bring parcels 4032-B-3-HV, 4032-B-2-HV, 4032-
        B-7-A-1-HV, 4033-A-1-D-6-HV, 4036-HV & 4032-B-1-HV into zoning compliance. The parcels are
        currently zoned A-20(Agricultural 20-acre minimum lot size). Staff recommends this to be changed to
        a RE-40(Residential Estate 40,000 square feet minimum lot size) zone. The parcels are located along
        Buckeye Reef Road in Leeds, Utah. The applicant is the Community Development Department.

4.      VARIANCE The applicant is seeking a Variance from existing Washington County zoning code 10-8D-
