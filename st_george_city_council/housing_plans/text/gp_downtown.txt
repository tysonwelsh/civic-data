Thriving Downtown
Downtown is the safe, vibrant, historic center of our City and region, offering unique employment, education, entertainment, and entrepreneurship opportunities. Downtown welcomes all members of the community to gather and thrive, consistent with its primary roles as the seat of government, home to residents in all stages of life, and showcase of local history, arts, and culture.
To achieve a Thriving Downtown, we will advance the following strategies in alignment with the Downtown Area Plan:
GUIDE DOWNTOWN GROWTH AND REINVESTMENT
a. Identify appropriate locations within Downtown to invite growth and revitalization.
b. Recognize and develop the distinctive and varied character of the downtown neighborhoods and activity centers.
c. Invest in public infrastructure and expand capacity Downtown to accommodate the desired growth potential.
d. Allow for a healthy mix of non-residential, single-family, and multi-family residential uses to provide the foundation for long-term vitality in Downtown.
e. Update the Downtown zoning to encourage development and align with plan goals.
EXPAND HOUSING SUPPLY AND MAINTAIN DESIRABLE NEIGHBORHOODS
a. Expand housing supply and residents living Downtown to support businesses, accommodate growth, and advance the Moderate Income Housing Plan.
b. Offer housing for residents of all ages and stages of life.
c. Maintain and develop safe and family-friendly neighborhoods.
d. Enhance complete neighborhoods with a range of convenient amenities and services for daily living such as parks, transit, and convenience retail.
RETAIN A REGIONAL ROLE AS A CENTRAL AND VITAL ECONOMIC NODE
a. Retain City facilities and services Downtown and encourage other governing agencies (County, State, Regional, Federal, etc.) to do so.
b. Identify and advance a distinctive niche for Downtown within the network of commercial and employment nodes citywide.
c. Promote and support a strong cluster of local and independent businesses Downtown.
d. Use landscape, street furnishings, and beautiful public spaces to enhance the sense of arrival and positive experience of being in the Downtown Arts District.
e. Enhance informal entertainment space Downtown that encourages patrons to increase visit duration and frequency.
f. Continue to offer visitor services and facilities Downtown and harness visitor spending to support more diverse shopping and dining options.
g. Promote expanded hours of operation of retail development to increase economic vitality downtown while enhancing the lively character.
STRENGTHEN DOWNTOWN AS THE CENTER OF HISTORY, ARTS, AND EVENTS WITHIN ST. GEORGE
a. Retain, promote, and protect historic assets and character.
b. Enhance and expand investment in the Downtown Arts District’s public spaces and public art.
c. Continue to offer diverse community-wide events Downtown, inviting all community members to gather in keeping with a small town, connected community feeling.
d. Prioritize the Downtown Arts District in allocating public funding for art and venues to concentrate a strong and vibrant arts cluster.
