                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-48

A RESOLUTION TO CONFIRM RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL SHALL NOT PROVIDE
          THE SERVICES PROPOSED TO BE PROVIDED BY THE
                   RIVERTON FIRE SERVICE AREA

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, as the legislative body of Riverton City
establishes municipal budgets, authorizes expenditures, and levies taxes and to pay for municipal
services; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City obtains first responder services from the Unified Fire
Authority under the terms of an Interlocal Agreement executed by and between Salt Lake
County, and several municipalities within Salt Lake County and Utah County; and

         WHEREAS, Historically, Riverton City residents have paid the Unified Fire Authority
for first responder services from ad valorem property tax revenues levied by the Unified Fire
Service Area (“UFSA”); and

        WHEREAS, by the end of the 2020 calendar year, the Riverton City Council anticipates
that the municipal territory of Riverton City shall be withdrawn from the UFSA service area
boundaries; and

        WHEREAS, on the date of April 21, 2020, the Riverton City Council adopted resolution
No. 20-31 which commenced the process to create a new first responder service area to be known
as the Riverton Fire Service Area (“RFSA”); and

        WHEREAS, as a part of the process to create a new first responder service area, Utah
Code §17B-1-212 requires the Riverton City Council to adopt a resolution indicating whether
Riverton City “will provide to the area of the proposed local district within its boundaries the
service proposed to be provided by the proposed local district;” and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code §17B-1-212 further requires said resolution to be adopted
within 60 days after the last hearing to consider adoption of RFSA, pursuant to Utah Code §17B-
1-210; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the last hearing to
consider creation of RFSA closed on the evening of June 16, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has considered this resolution in a public
meeting, following public notice required by law,
      NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
Follows:

      1. The Riverton City Council hereby confirms that it shall not provide the service of levying
         ad valorem property taxes to fund the cost for first responder service within the area
         proposed to be located within RFSA, to wit: the municipal territory of Riverton City.

      2. This Resolution is adopted in compliance with Utah Code § 17B-1-212.
