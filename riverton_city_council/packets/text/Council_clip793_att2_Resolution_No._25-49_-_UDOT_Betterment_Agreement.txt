                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 25-49

  A RESOLUTION APPROVING A BETTERMENT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE
    UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AND RIVERTON CITY FOR
  IMPROVEMENT OF AN INTERSECTION AT THE LOCATION OF BANGERTER
                    HIGHWAY AND 13400 SOUTH


       WHEREAS, the State of Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) is commencing
with construction to improve an intersection located at 13400 South and Bangerter Highway
(hereinafter referred to as the “Intersection Project”); and

       WHEREAS, the Intersection Project affects a broad variety of municipal facilities, such
as roads, utility lines, and park space; and

       WHEREAS, in the year 2023, the Riverton City Council approved Resolution No. 23-85
which authorized the Mayor to execute a Betterment Agreement with UDOT (the “2023
Betterment Agreement”) to identify improvements needed by the City to improve intersecting
City roads, which the City would fund as a part of the Intersection Project; and

       WHEREAS, following execution of the Betterment Agreement, the parties identified an
additional improvement to City streets which was not identified or funded under the 2023
Betterment Agreement; and

       WHEREAS, a new Betterment Agreement is therefore required to address construction
and funding for the additional improvement, and

        WHEREAS, said betterment Agreement has been prepared for approval and execution
by and between UDOT and Riverton City, which states the purposes thereof, and the extent of
the required participation of the parties and the rights, duties, responsibilities, and obligations of
the parties described in this Resolution.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1.      The Riverton City Council hereby approves the Betterment Agreement with UDOT,
               attached hereto as Exhibit “A”.
       2.      The Mayor is authorized to sign the approved Betterment Agreement with UDOT
               once the Betterment Agreement is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.
       3.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
