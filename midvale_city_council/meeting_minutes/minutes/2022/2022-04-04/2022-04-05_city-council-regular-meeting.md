Adam Olsen said the City proposes an amendment to the above listed chapters of the
Municipal Code. The amendment would stipulate that minimum on-site parking
requirements (including visitor parking) for multi-family residential projects-whether
stand-alone or part of an overall mixed-use development-not be assessed an additional
fee to tenants and/or residents outside of the base sale, lease, or rental price of a unit.
While all multi-family and mixed-use developments must provide a minimum base parking
count, some developments are charging a fee to use such parking; thereby creating
unintended spill-over of parking onto adjacent streets. Staff researched the issue and
found that Sandy City has a similar provision in their Code, prohibiting additional fees for
required parking counts. Staff worked closely with City Legal to craft the proposed
language included herein.

Public notice has been sentto all affected entities as required in 17-3-9.8 of the Municipal
Code. No objections have been received as of the writing of this report.

The Planning Commission reviewed the proposed amendment at their March 9,2022
meeting- After discussion, including questions for City Legal regarding possible avenues
of retroactive enforcement, a recommendation of approval was made, with a request for
Council consideration of a possible retroactive enforcement mechanism.

       B.       CONSIDER REDISTRICTING MAP OPTIONS FOR THE MIOVALE CITY
                COUNCIL DISTRICTS
Rori Andreason said ln accordance with Section 2.12.O3O of the Midvale Municipal Code,
the City Council members shall be elected from districts substantially equal in population.
To be elected from a district, the council member must be a qualified elector residing in
the district. To vote for the district candidate, the voter must be a resident of the district in
which the candidate resides.

The district lines are redrawn every ten (10) years following the completion of the U.S.
Census. Federal, State, and City codes stipulate that the districts must have substantially
equal populations. The city is divided into five (5) city council districts and one city council
member is elected from each city council district. Redistricting is done using U.S. Census
data, which is usually released around March 31 , 2021. However, this year it was not
released until September 30,2021 , due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

Utah Code 10-3-205.5 requires the City Council to make boundary adjustments where
necessary to maintain substantially equal populations, within six months after the
Legislature completes its redistricting process.

Utah enacted new congressional districts on November 12,2021 . Governor Spencer Cox
signed new state legislative dishicts for both chambers into law on November 16,2021.
The Midvale City Council decided on two map options to be considered. Two open houses
were held to receive public input:

        C.    FIREWORKSRESTRICTIONDISCUSSION
Matt Dahl said every year, the Unified Fire Authority (UFA) requests an updated fireworks
restriction map from their municipal partners. The updated municipal maps are then
uploaded into a central map showing where fireworks are prohibited throughout Salt Lake
County. UFA has requested that Midvale City provide an updated firework restriction map
by May 1,2022. lf we do not provide an updated map, UFA will default to our previously
approved map.

For many years, Midvale City's fireworks restrictions map prohibited fireworks along the
Jordan River and most of the Jordan Bluffs neighborhood (i.e., Holden Street to the
Jordan River, south of 7800 South). These restricted areas were based on historic
conditions as provided in State Code. Last year, due to a persistent drought and a
designated hazardous environmental condition, Midvale City approved ordinance 2021-
O-16, which expanded the firework restrictions to include all of Midvale. The ordinance

reverted back to original map.

seeking input from the City Council on the current firework restrictions map and
discussion regarding strategy for the summer if historic drought conditions persist. Staff
