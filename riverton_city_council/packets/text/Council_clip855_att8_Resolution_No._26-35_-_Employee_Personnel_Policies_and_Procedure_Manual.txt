                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 26-35

    A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO THE RIVERTON CITY
            PERSONNEL POLICIES AND PROCEDURES MANUAL

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual (the
“Manual”) is used to set standards for employment operations, and define employee benefits; and

       WHEREAS, the Manual is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in law or
in employment practices arise; and

       WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the manual and determined some terms need to be
updated to improve the standards in the Manual for tuition reimbursement and for selling back
vacation; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment to the
Manual as proposed by City staff would achieve the purposes herein described; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City and City
employees to adopt revisions to the Manual;

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual, section 4.6 Education,
          is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution.

       2. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual, section 5.3 Vacation,
          Selling Vacation, is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit B of this Resolution.

       3. The City Recorder is hereby directed to incorporate the amendments approved by
          sections 1. and 2. of this Resolution into the Riverton City Policies and Procedures
          Manual and republish the same.

       4. The Mayor is hereby directed to ensure that the Manual is accordingly revised and
          made available to all City employees within a reasonable time from adoption of this
          Resolution.

       5. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

4.6     EDUCATION
Employees are encouraged to obtain continuing education through attendance at job related seminars
and for the pursuit of higher education degrees such as Associates, Bachelors, Masters, etc. Requests
for attendance must be approved in advance by the supervisor, Human Resource designee and the City
Manager.

When Riverton City requires an employee to attend any education or training course, conference,
seminar, or certification course, Riverton City may provide the necessary time off with pay, as approved
by the City Manager. Riverton City will pay for all associated costs including tuition, registration fees,
authorized travel, meals, and lodging where applicable.

EMPLOYEES SEEKING HIGHER EDUCATION DEGREES (ASSOCIATES, BACHELORS, MASTERS,
PHD’S)
The purpose of this policy is to provide continuous full time Riverton City employees with equitable
financial assistance through education reimbursement for courses of study towards higher education
degrees or other approved educational or technical programs, which are consistent with their current
job and/or future Riverton City job activities and are beneficial to the City. It is the intent of Riverton
City to encourage its employees to improve job performance and increase career potential by taking
job-related or future Riverton City job-related courses.

Education is not an employee right. Approval for participation in education assistance shall take into
account the availability of funds, number of employees requesting assistance, management support for
the specific course work, employee workload, and successful performance.

ASSISTANCE
Education assistance will generally not exceed $1,500 1,750 per semester, for up to three semesters per
year. and nNo employee will receive more than the amount allowed by the Internal Revenue Service to
be exempt from inclusion as ordinary income within any calendar year unless approved by the City
Manager and only as funds are available.

Education assistance will be considered for classes that are directly related to the employee’s current
position or likely future positions. Employees are encouraged to use other available forms of tuition
assistance, waiver, scholarships or grants. In accepting assistance, employees are certifying that no
other tuition assistance such as scholarships or grants have been accepted for the classes and
reimbursement requested. Assistance is for tuition, books, and school and lab fees. Assistance will not
include travel, parking unless it is being required as a condition of employment as noted in Section XIV
(E) (1).

EDUCATION REIMBURSEMENT
The employee must have satisfactorily completed at least six (6) months of continuous full-time
employment, and have completed employment probation with Riverton City to qualify for participation
in the education reimbursement program. Education reimbursement will not be considered for



                                                      3
employees who have a current (within one year) disciplinary action on file. Any disciplinary action taken
during the period of course work will release the City from any assistance commitment.

Any applications for reimbursement must be received in Human Resources at the beginning of the
course. The applications can be found on-line or in the Human Resource department. The employee
must verify satisfactory completion of the course with a passing grade or better to receive
reimbursement. Where conventional letter-based grading is used to grade an employee-student’s
performance, a grade of “C” or better is required for reimbursement. Where other grading systems are
used, the Human Resource Department and the employee must meet and agree to what constitutes a
passing grade before the student enrolls. . To receive a reimbursement, the employee must first fill out
the reimbursement form and attach all documentation of payment and grades. If Riverton City has
agreements in place with a particular school to pay up front for tuition, the employee may do so with
the appropriate documentation. If the employee does not receive at least a passing grade at the end of
the class, the employee will be required to pay back the tuition which was paid on their behalf.
Employees receiving assistance must agree, in writing, to remain employed by Riverton City for a
specified time after receiving assistance. The agreement shall be as follows:

    1.   $0 to $500 annual contribution requires a 6 month commitment
    2.   $501 to $1,000 annual contribution requires a 12 month commitment
    3.   $1,001 to $1,500 annual contribution requires a 18 month commitment
    4.   $1,501 and higher annual contribution requires a 2 year commitment.

All commitment periods described above begin to run from the date on which the employee submits
proof of satisfactory completion of the education program.of the final contribution payment issued in a
calendar year. Any employee leaving employment with the City, either voluntarily or with cause, prior
to the expiration of the above mentioned employment commitment will be required to reimburse the
City for 100% of the tuition assistance received by the employee upon termination of employment. Any
employee who remains employed by the City but fails to satisfactorily complete any approved education
program (as in the case of an associates degree or other program requiring more than one year to
complete) shall be required to repay all City contributions to the employee’s education within two years
of the date final payment was received.

5.3     VACATION
ELIGIBILITY
Vacation leave is to be used by regular employees for scheduled absences. Vacation leave is available to
all full-time and regular part-time employees.

ACCRUAL
Vacation leave is accrued during each pay period. Full-time employees will receive vacation leave at the
following annual rates depending on longevity within Utah Retirement Systems, for Civilian employees,
or law enforcement equivalent experience for sworn officers. Law enforcement equivalent experience
is defined as one year for one year of direct law enforcement officer experience and one year for every
two years of other public safety experience such as corrections, jail or fire.

Regular vacation leave (employees excluding middle-level management and higher):
   1. 0-5 years 80 hours
   2. 5-10 years 120 hours
   3. 10-15 years 160 hours
   4. 15+ years 200 hours

Administrative vacation leave (middle-level management and higher):
   1. 0-5 years 120 hours
   2. 5-10 years 160 hours
   3. 10+ years 200 hours

Reduced- time employees accrue sick and vacation leave on a prorated scale, based upon the
employee’s regularly scheduled work hours.

New employees shall accrue vacation from the date of hire and shall be eligible to use this vacation as
soon as they have successfully completed probation, unless specified differently in the employment
offer letter as authorized by the City Manager.

Vacation leave accrual is capped at two hundred (200) hours. Vacation leave in excess of 200 hours, as
of the last payroll in the calendar year, shall be forfeited on March 31 of the following year.

Vacation in addition to this policy may be awarded as approved by the City Manager.

TERMINATION
An employee who is terminated or resigns voluntarily shall be compensated for unused accrued
vacation leave.




                                                    7
NOTIFICATION
In order to qualify for vacation leave use, vacation should be approved by the supervisor through the
“Request for Leave” form at least three (3) days prior to the leave date. Department Heads have
discretion to approve valid exceptions to the three day rule. All approvals must be made before the
vacation can be taken. If an excessive (excessive is defined as being the number of requests if granted
that would render the department or organization ineffective) amount of employees request leave for
the same time period it shall be granted at the discretion of the supervisor.

SELLING VACATION
Qualified Employees can sell back accrued vacation time. An employee is qualified to sell back accrued
vacation time if the employee has not received discipline in the form of suspension for two years prior
to the date sell back is requested. As used in this section, “Sell back” means an employee can turn in a
request to be paid for accrued vacation hours, in addition to the employee’s regular pay. Once
approved, the sell back request will be issued at the employee’s current hourly rate of pay. A “Request
for Selling Vacation” form must be turned into Payroll before the request can be processed. Employees
may not request to sell back vacation during the months of July or January. Properly approved requests
for vacation sell back will be processed during the subsequent payroll period.
