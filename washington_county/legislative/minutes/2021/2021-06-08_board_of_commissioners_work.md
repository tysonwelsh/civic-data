Acting Chairman Mark Owens at 1:30 p.m. on June 8, 2021, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers and via Zoom, at 197 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah.

PUBLIC HEARING ZONE CHANGE. Review and hold a public hearing for a zone
change request of Parcel STON-AG-HV from the RA-1 (Residential Agricultural 1 acre
minimum lot size) zone to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone. The
approximately 9 acre parcel is located at 5440 N. 100 E. in Pintura. The applicant is David
Morgan.

Mr. Messel explained that the applicant proposed a 6-lot subdivision approximately 2 years
ago and is now requesting a zone change for the parcel adjacent to the subdivision to
construct 17 greenhouses to grow agricultural product. Mr. Messel stated that the parcel
was designated as open space when the subdivision was created, and the septic system and
well are located on the open space parcel that is under review for zone change. A
conditional use request was previously denied because the RA zone does not allow for the
greenhouses as a conditional use. The A-5 zone would allow an agriculturally based
business as a conditional use. Staff has reviewed the request and is not in favor. Staff has
concerns about the access to the property and use of the designated open space. It is
currently open space but could possibly have negative impact on the property owners
because the 80-100 foot greenhouses could be a commercial use.

Commissioner Hughes asked how this recommendation plays into the county’s goal of
preserving agricultural use in Washington County. Mrs. Hales expressed concerns that the
plot was originally platted to provide water and septic services for the subdivision. This
area was platted as open space to support the sewer and water infrastructure and
commercial business on the parcel would hinder those uses from a legal standpoint.

correct legal way.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the requested zone change from the RA-1 (Residential Agriculture 1
acre minimum lot size) zone to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone and
recommends that the planning commission hold the public hearing. Staff is not in favor of
the proposal and recommends denial. However, the planning commission can determine
whether or not the project can move forward, and whether or not it can be recommended
to the County Commission for approval. If that is the recommendation, staff recommends
that it is based on the following finding and conditions:

1. The zone change and public hearing were properly noticed.

2. The public hearing was held on June 8, 2021, in the regularly scheduled Planning
Commission Meeting.

3. The zone change to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone would
allow the applicant a means to utilities their property for agricultural greenhouses
to produce commercially viable products, but applicant must propose a use that
does not negatively impact the water/septic use which supports the RA-1 lots.

4. Subject to the applicant proposing a legal solution for the water/septic use on the

same 9 acre agricultural parcel in perpetuity, under terms and with documentation

acceptable to the county in its sole discretion.

Subject to access to a public street that meets county standards.

6. The proposal is consistent with the General Plan.
