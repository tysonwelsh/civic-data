NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Mtg—June 7th @ 7pm.

       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          12 individuals from the City and surrounding areas shared their support for Mural
          Fest.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood talked about Mural Fest and its success despite the rainy weather. She
          stated how the proposed budget continues to have funding for Mural Fest. She said
          that Arts Director, Lesly Allen, will share some more on the topic.

              She acknowledged National Public Works Week and thanked the City’s Public Works
              staff for all the work they do and for when they come out in the middle of the night
              when things go wrong.

              Lesly Allen gave an update on Mural Fest. This year they received almost 600
              applications, with almost 60% of them being from artists outside of the country. She
              also shared some of the background work that staff do to make this happen.

              Kids 12 & under can submit names for the City’s new Hydro-X Truck.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Siwik expressed his full support for the Mural Fest and shared how
          arts are important to every community.

              Council Member Pinkney thanked everyone who attended and shared how she
              helped review Mural Fest applications this year. She said that arts are here to stay in

        6. INFORMATION.

                  a. Civilian Review Board Quarterly Report
                     CRB Chair, Joy Glad, presented the quarterly report to the Council. A copy of
                     the material is attached and incorporated by this reference.

                  b. Flood Preparation Update
                     Stormwater Division Manager, Corby Talbot, gave an update to the Council.
                     His staff is currently on 24-hour watches. The City has 33,000 sandbags that
                     are strategically placed in various at-risk areas or stored and ready to be
                     deployed for use. The creek is slowly increasing its flow but continues to be
                     well below flood level flow. City Engineer, Dennis Pay, added that the recent
                     cold and rainy weather pattern has helped to keep temperatures cool.
