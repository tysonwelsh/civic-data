                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 25-11

  AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE TITLE 5, CHAPTER 5 TO
                REVISE BUSINESS LICENSE ORDINANCE


       WHEREAS, Title 5, Chapter 5 of the Riverton City Code governs the licensure of
businesses within the territorial jurisdiction of Riverton City (hereinafter referred to as the
“Business License Ordinance”); and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City staff has undertaken a comprehensive review of the Business
License Ordinance to streamline processes related to application for several types of business
licenses; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, finds and determines that Riverton City Code
Title 5, Chapter 5 is in need of amendments as provided by City staff;


NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:


      Section 1.       Title 5, Chapter 5, of the Riverton City Code shall be, and hereby is,
                       amended to reflect the following changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

      Section 2.       This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 1st day of
