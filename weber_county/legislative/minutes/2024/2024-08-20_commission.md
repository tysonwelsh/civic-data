    3. APPROVE A DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR THE WINSTON PARK DEVELOPMENT.
         Charlie Ewert (Planning): Commissioners, you have all of the information and I can answer any questions
         you may have.

         Chair Harvey: Will there be grass on retention ponds? And fences and trees on property lines?

         Mr. Ewert: Grass in the retention ponds is not specifically mentioned in the Development Agreement but it
         can be added. It sounds like there are private agreements between the developer and lot owners for the fence
         and trees. There will be masonry wall along 1800 South with street trees.

         Chair Harvey: What about the street I asked for through Lot 108?

         Mr. Ewert: It appears the lot owner is not interested in selling but the developers are still happy to do a
         pathway that is already covered in the Development Agreement.

         Chair Harvey: This was a cluster subdivision some time ago. When it was sold to all the people, the
         disclosures said sometime the property behind them could be developed. For me, I feel misled on the
         original agreement. That is why I feel it is important to the street go through.

         Commissioner Froerer: Would you say the existing residents favor or do not favor this?

         Mr. Ewert: I can’t say favor or disfavor are terms that could be used. But from information we have
         received, they are aware and not fighting it.

         Commissioner Froerer: I have had concerns about granting land credit for a cluster then coming back and
         developing it. I know things have changed since the ordinance was in place. What is the benefit to the
         community and/or residents, or both, to letting this come out of open space?

         Mr. Ewert: The Planning Commission struggled with this as well. This land is without water and is not seen
         as viable farm acreage anymore. Technically it wasn’t a cluster but a PUD but they followed the same
         cluster subdivision rules. As far as getting density or bonus credits, they have happened under A1 zone but
         if they change to R1-15 there is no credit. The developer has agreed to a $7500 per lot donation to the park
         district to make improvements to the nearby park. This is covered in the development agreement.
