From: Jennifer Jastremsky

Re: Chandler Pointe at South Mountain – Amended Site Plan and Conditional Use Permit Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of an amended site plan and conditional use permit for approximately 614 square feet located at the east entrance to Chandler Pointe at South Mountain development, at approximately 14874 South Quarry Pointe Ln. The property is currently zoned RM2 . The applicant is requesting that an amended site plan and conditional use permit be approved to allow for the installation of an emergency access gate.   The findings are as follows: Conditional Use Permit:
The findings for approval as are follows:
1. The proposed gate complies with all International Fire Code standards.
2. The proposed gate complies with the standards found in DCMC Section 9-27-200(C).
3. That the proposed conditional use permit complies with the standards found in DCMC Section 9-5-080(E).
The findings for denial as are follows:
1. The proposed gate does not comply with all International Fire Code standards.
2. The proposed gate does not comply with the standards found in DCMC 9-27-200(C).
3. That the proposed conditional use permit does not comply with the standards found in DCMC Section 9-5-080(E).
Site Plan:
The findings for approval as are follows:
1. The proposed gate complies with all International Fire Code standards.
2. The proposed gate complies with the standards found in DCMC Section 9-27-200(E).
3. That the proposed amended site plan complies with DCMC Sections 9-5-090(E) and (H).
The findings for denial as are follows:
1. The proposed gate does not comply with all International Fire Code standards.
2. The proposed gate does not comply with the standards found in DCMC 9-27-200(E).
3. That the proposed amended site plan does not comply with the standards found in DCMC Sections 9-5-090(E) and (H). ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 12/3/2020 Cover Memo
