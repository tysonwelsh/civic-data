          REQUEST
          Dan Medina, Assistant Parks and Recreation Director,
          has submitted an application on behalf of Sandy City for
          a zone change of two areas, a total of 12 parcels and
          approximately 8.1 acres, owned by the City near the
          intersection of Wasatch Boulevard and Little Cottonwood
          Road. The proposal would change the zoning designation
          of the subject property from R-1-15 (Single Family
          Residential) to OS (Open Space).
