             Equipment and Mr. Chappell with Chappell Automotive approached Nephi City

   6. PUBLIC HEARING: Considering an ordinance updating Title 11 Land Use,
       Subdivision and Development Regulations (Currently Subdivisions), and Title 10
       Zoning (Currently Land Use): updating time frame requirements for city to meet
       regarding approvals per state code; creating a land use authority; updating
       standards and specifications; consolidating development code; and other
       modifications to both titles.

          a. Open public hearing
             TIME: 7:38 PM

          b. Staff presentation
                      Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented to the planning commission that the
             Planning Commission is trying to meet a minimum requirement that the state has
             set forward in their land use code, we're trying with this ordinance to come into
             compliance with the minimum requirements that the state has before us at this
             time, the due date is December 31, of this year. Added into the ordinance are
             definitions that match the states wording and definition. An additional Land use
             Authority is required, and the purpose of doing that is to move more
             administrative items to this meeting. No member of a city council can sit on the
             LUA (Land Use Authority), and legislative adjust the codes as needed. It splits
             the assignments into political and administrative items work separately. It brings
             more voice to the people, and less problems to the planning and city council.
                      One of the benefits is streamlining for developers, and the other is for the
             staff. Rezones would be legislative, and not administrative. Timing will be
             critical, with time limits on things that must be met and compliance met. Single-
             family, twin homes, multi-family homes will be under this jurisdiction. Civic-
             Review will also in place, to have everything transparent for anyone wanting to
             know fees. The idea is to help people move through the process better.

   7. Discussion item: Nephi City Annexation Policy Plan 2024 update
          a. Staff presentation
                Mr. Seth Atkinson presented that this is new to the Planning Commission
             tonight, the Policy Plan will normally come to the City Council. However, the
             state requires that whenever there is a proposed change to the map or plan, it
             comes to the Planning Commission for approval. The Annexation Plan is a guide
             for helping the City Council determine whether or not it is ready or prepared for
             annexation into a city. It looks at economic impact, tax base, infrastructure needs.
             It has a 14-point annexation analysis that has to be done every time, to help the
             council determine if the City Council would like to annex anything into the city.
                One of the changes is actually item number eight on this Annexation Policy, if
             anyone would like to annex into the city they will be required to show a concept
             plan. That needs to show intended uses, and potential street layout. It would need
             to meet zoning, current city limits, and assist us in making sure that the 14-point
             analysis gets done correctly and is an accurate representation of what will be
             happening if it is to move forward. So tonight, we would like to add the official
             request to have a concept plan shown as part of an annexation request. A map will
             be included, and it will show the levels of annexation. Level 1 is that the
             municipal services are there presently, or close by. Level 2 and 3 will be areas
             that are further out, level 3 being that services are further out and may not be
             presently annexed and may really think about services coming. The analysis will
             help with that.
                In addition, the other proposal is a change of boundaries. Each color has a
             certain criteria determination. Green is level one, Purple is level two, Orange is
             level three. The area that is in the orange in this area is proposed to the planning
             commission to be added into the annexation tonight, due to the proposed 138 kV
             line. It would follow the railroad tracks, and each of the properties surrounding
             would like electrical service. The staff recommends that the planning commission
             add the area as proposed on the map as level three. This other section on the map
             is proposed and recommended to level one, and this other area be moved back to
             level two. (Please see attachment A)
