was probably built ca. 1880-1900 and may have operated up until the 1920s.

widened, UDOT moved it onto the foundation it sites on today.

Commission.

shop through the preservation of historically significant structures provision of 
the Snyderville Basin Development Code.  As part of the proposal, the applicant 
is requesting to improve an existing shed into a restroom.

CONCLUSIONS OF LAW 
1. The adaptive reuse will require some physical changes to the building, but the 
features that are important in defining the overall historic character of the 
building and environment will be kept.    
2. The adaptive reuse is contained entirely within the historically significant 
structure with the exception of the exterior seating area. 
3. Significant archaeological resources affected by the project are protected and 
preserved by containing the improvements within the existing structure.

examples of craftsmanship that characterize the property have been preserved.
