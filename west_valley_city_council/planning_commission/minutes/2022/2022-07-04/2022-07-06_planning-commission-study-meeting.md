GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-5-2022 Petition by SCENIC DEVELOPMENT, INC. requesting a General Plan change
           from Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre) and Large Lot Residential (2 to 3
           units/acre) to General Commercial and a zone change from R-1-8 (Single Unit
           Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet), A (Agriculture,
           minimum lot size ½ acre), and A-2 (Agriculture, minimum lot size 2 acres) to C-2
           (General Commercial). The property is located at 3672 and 3690 South 5600
           West on 5.79 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that the applicant is requesting this General Plan/Zone
              Change intending to use the subject property for two medical office buildings. He
              pointed out the parcels located directly south of the subject location, where a
              hospital for the University of Utah will be located. Steve believes the construction
              of the hospital will be around 2026; however, he will need to double-check that
              date.

              Steve stated that staff has a couple of concerns he would like to address with the
              Commission if they feel the proposed zone change is appropriate in this location.
              He explained that when UDOT did the construction on 5600 West, they put the
              sidewalk right up against the curb. He said the city standard would have a park
              strip between the sidewalk and the curb, so staff would recommend that to the
              applicant. Steve noted that the City has tried to have power and utility lines buried
              where possible, which would be discussed with the applicant. He stated that when
              you have a commercial use next to a residential use, the code requires a masonry
              wall to be installed between the properties. The City would like the wall along the
              north and south sides of the subject property to match the wall installed for the
              University hospital.

              Chair Fuller asked Steve if the applicant wants approval of this proposal to build
              around the same time as the hospital. Steve said he would ask the applicant if they
              plan on waiting or starting construction once this proposal is approved.

              Commissioner Winters asked if there are any requirements regarding the noise of
              sirens on emergency vehicles near a residential zone. Steve stated that he does not
              believe emergency vehicle noise limitations exist. He explained that the
              University is a state entity and is not required to apply for a zone change. Steve
              asked Brandon Hill if his statement was correct. Brandon confirmed his
              information and added that historically the state had worked well with the City.

              Commissioner Lovato asked if the park strip would be five feet like the majority
              of 5600 West. Steve said yes, the park strip and the sidewalk would be five feet.

              Commissioner Wood asked Steve if there would be an impact on traffic along
              5600 West. Steve said he would investigate the traffic impact and report to the
              Commission. He said that when the Mountain View Corridor opened, it took
              some traffic away from 5600 West. Eventually, the corridor will become more
              like a full grade-separated freeway, which at that point would be able to handle
              even more traffic.

Z-10-2022     Petition by JOHN BAIRD requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture,
              minimum lot size 1/2 acre) to R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum
              lot size 8,000 square feet). The property is located at 3909 South 2200 West on
              0.52 acres. (Staff - Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-
              ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that the subject property has an existing house and
              detached garage. Steve noted that the proposed flag lot would place the existing
              home on one lot and the detached garage on the other lot. Therefore, it would no
              longer serve the current house.

              Steve stated that the landscaping and driveway need maintenance and repair. He
              noted that the existing structure would need to be repainted. Steve stated that the
              minimum frontage requirement for the R-1-8 zone is eighty feet, and, in this case,
              they would be one foot short of meeting the requirement. He said that the staff
              feels granting an exception for the one foot is a reasonable request. Steve said the
              applicant is proposing to meet the previous housing standards, which are higher
              standards previously stated in our ordinance. He noted that improvements would
              be added across the front edge of the subject property.

              Steve pointed out that if this zone change is approved, the existing detached
              garage would be on a separate lot than the existing home and most likely
              demolished to make room for a new house. He said that the county records
              showed this home was built in 1924, so our code would not require replacing the
              garage. However, the staff feels replacing the garage would be a reasonable
              consideration for the development agreement.

              Chair Fuller asked how close a detached garage could be to the property line.
              Steve said it could be within one foot of the side and rear property lines, as long
              as it is behind the house.
