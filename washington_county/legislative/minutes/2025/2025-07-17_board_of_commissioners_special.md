WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JULY 17, 2025

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 8:30 a.m. on July 17, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Commissioner Victor
Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin
Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Jerry Jaeger
REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:
PROCLAMATION NO. 2025-43, A PROCLAMATION HONORING WASHINGTON
COUNTY ATTORNEY ERIC CLARKE
Commissioner Iverson spoke about his relationship with Eric Clarke and his well wishes for the

future in his new position at the Utah Association of Counties, Commissioners Snow and Almquist
relayed their well wishes to Mr. Clarke.

Commissioner Snow read the Proclamation aloud.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow Approve Proclamation No. 2025-43, A
Proclamation Honoring Washington County Attorney Eric Clarke. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2025-1299-O, AN ORDINANCE UPDATING
COUNTY ELECTION CODE FOR SPECIAL ELECTION DISCLOSURE TO COMPLY

WITH STATE LAW

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained this Ordinance would bring County code into
compliance with recent state legislation regarding special election financial disclosures.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2025-1298-O, An
Ordinance Updating County Election Code for Special Election Disclosure to
Comply with State Law. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3557, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_ CONTRACT AMENDMENT WITH THE STATE OF UTAH FOR INCREASED
FUNDING FOR THE CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER

Children’s Justice Center Director Kristy Pike stated this contract is an annual renewal, with an
added contract amount allotted by the state.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3557, A
Resolution Approving a Contract Amendment with the State of Utah for
Increased Funding for the Children’s Justice Center. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 9:01 a.m.
