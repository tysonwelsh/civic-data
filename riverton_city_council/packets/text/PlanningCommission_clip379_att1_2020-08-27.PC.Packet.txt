This application is a public hearing and administrative action item.
In reviewing the application the Planning Commission is serving an administrative function,
decisions are based on substantial evidence.

Example Motion

I move the Planning Commission approve application PLZ 20-2027, Craig View Fencing
Conditional Use with the following condition:

      1. The developer shall install 6-ft vinyl fence along the west subdivision line, also along the
         north line of Lot 3 per City Ordinance 18.155. The east line of Lot 4 shall have no fence
         requirement. The remaining perimeter lot lines shall keep the existing fences in place.

Background

The PC approved this the Craig View Estates Subdivision on June 11, 2020. Craig View is a 4-
lot subdivision on 1.72 acres in an R-3 Zone (14,000 sf lots). In that approval, the example motion
provided by staff, included a condition for fencing which read:

          The developer shall install 6-ft rhinorock fence along the west subdivision line, also
          along the north line of lot 3 and the east line of lot 4 per City Ordinance 18.155. The
          remaining perimeter lot lines shall keep the existing fences in place.

The applicant is requesting the requirement for 6-ft rhinorock be removed and replaced with a
requirement for 6-ft vinyl fence on the west subdivision line and the north line of lot 3. And that
the fencing requirment be waived for the east line of Lot 4.

The west property is zoned RR-22 with animal rights which is incompatible with R-3 zoning in
Craig View. However, the General Plan designates all adjoining property as either Low or Medium
Density Residential which correspond with our R-3 and R-4 Zones. In other words the future land

Riverton Ordinance 18.155.80 requires 6-ft masonry fence between incompatible zoning or uses,
but also gives the commission the ability to approve an alternative fence type under certain
circumstances.

          18.155.080 (3)(a) Conditional Use. The planning commission may allow by conditional
          use permit alternative fence types and heights based on the following criteria:
                 (a) Hollow and foam core concrete or fiberglass/concrete mix fencing, or other
                 alternative fencing in keeping with the purpose of this title, and/or reduced height,
                 may be allowed where:
                         (i) Adjacent property is designated in the Riverton City general plan for a
                         land use that is determined by the commission to be compatible; or
                         (ii) Anticipated development patterns in the surrounding area are such that
                         required fencing would create an illogical or detrimental barrier between
                         properties; or
                         (iii) A parallel fence has been approved by conditional use which will
                         provide additional safety or screening in keeping with the purpose of this
                         title.

Also,

          18.155.080 (3)(b) Alternative fencing types may be allowed adjacent to properties with
          animals/animal rights and irrigation canals or other waterways provided the commission
          finds the proposed fence type is not detrimental to the safety and general welfare of the
          public.

The original approval was for an exception and designated Rhinorock fencing, the applicant is
requesting the requirement along the north and west lines be changed to 6-ft vinyl and that no
fence be required on the east property line.

The applicant is requesting no fence on the east line because he owns the property on the other
side and is in the process of cleaning it up. He would like to finish cleaning up the property and
build his house before installing a fence of his choice. Riverton Ordinance requires fencing to be
installed before building permits are issued, so he is asking for permission to waive the fence
requirement.

The staff report includes an example motion to approve as the applicant has requested.
