                                    RESOLUTION NO. 24-27

         A RESOLUTION APPOINTING A CITY COUNCILMEMBER AS DRAPER
 CITY’S REPRESENTATIVE TO THE BOARD ADMINISTERING THE INTERLOCAL
          AGREEMENT FOR THE CREATION OF A SCHOOL DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, Draper City entered into an interlocal agreement with the cities of Lehi,
Cedar Hills, Highland, Alpine, and American Fork (the “Interlocal”) for the creation of a new
school district; and

        WHEREAS, the Interlocal requires each participating city to appoint a representative to the
joint board administering the Interlocal; and

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council desires to appoint a Councilmember as its
representative on the joint board; and

       WHEREAS, the appointment of a Councilmember promotes the health, safety and welfare
of Draper City residents.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council hereby appoints Councilmember
________________________ as its representative on the joint board administering the Interlocal.

       Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held invalid
or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this
Resolution, and all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall be severable.

       Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 4th DAY OF JUNE 2024.
