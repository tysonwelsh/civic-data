JOINT DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT--SET SQUARE
       Seth Atkinson presented the joint development agreement with Set Square for a data center
       detailing the terms for power, water, natural gas, and sewer services. A summary of the
       provisions follows:
       1. The city is willing to allow the use of up to two hundred Megawatts of power through the
           new 138 kV line temporarily until a 345 kV line is constructed from the Clover Creek
           substation to service initial and future builds of data centers.
       2. The city would reserve capacity of up to two hundred Megawatts for a time while additional
           agreements are negotiated.
       3. The city would be paid $25,000 up front for the reserved capacity and $25,000 later after the
           project has been considered either a Designated Network Resource or a Designated Network
           Load.
       4. Utilities are provided for the data center facilities with a special municipal electrical rate that
           will be established. Utilities to a power generation facility are not part of this agreement.
       5. Compliance with UMPA policies is required.
       6. Additional 345 kV infrastructure will be required.
       7. Road infrastructure will be required according to terms set by both Nephi City and Juab
           County.
       8. Other legal requirements such as assignment, governing law, and confidentiality.
        Councilor JD Parady motioned to allow the mayor to sign the agreement once Set Square has
        signed. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous
        vote.

   2. Golf Course Project
          a. Seth Atkinson, City Administrator, and Mike Booth, Golf Director, provided an update on
              the golf course expansion including estimates for construction and the potential
              purchase of additional property.
          b. The total cost is estimated at $4.9 million, with 75% allocated to the golf course and the
              remainder for the property purchase.
          c. The city recently opened bids for the golf course expansion and most of the bids came
              back in the range that had been anticipated. After going through a quantitative and
              qualitative process to score the different bids with a committee made up of staff, a
              council member, and a member of the men’s league, a bid was selected for Council
              recommendation.
          d. Seth Atkinson presented earlier that the city has an option to apply for a grant from the
              Land & Water Conservation Fund for a sizable portion of the project cost. The city should
              know by mid-February if our project has made the state list for grant selection.
          e. The council decides to wait for the state’s list of grant recipients before making a final
              decision on the project.
   3. Reese’s Flat Plan
          a. Reese’s Flat is city owned property located east of the city. In a previous council meeting
              Craig Oswald, Recreation Director, and Seth Atkinson proposed the idea of putting a
              Competitive Mountain Bike Course there.
          b. The project was introduced again due to there being a grant available that would help
              cover a parking area, road improvements, and other access roads to the area.
          c. The council discussed potential benefits and challenges. It would bring revenue and
              other recreational activities to our community. However, it would negatively affect those
              who use the area for hunting.
          d. The council also discussed the possibility of using the area for other recreational
              purposes, such as a water tank or additional trails.
   4. Miscellaneous Items
          a. Councilor Shari Cowan stated that a common question the council members receive is
              when garbage day is on a holiday week. She noted that Spanish Fork does a magnet for
              their residents showing every month and holiday updates for trash pickup and thought
              that might be something Nephi City could do.
          b. Ms. Cowan had inquired about the cost from local business owner Amanda Livingston.
              For a five-by-seven magnet the cost would be about $1.50.
          c. The council proposes having magnets made up to give out at the information fair coming
              up on January 31. Any extras would then be available at the city office.
