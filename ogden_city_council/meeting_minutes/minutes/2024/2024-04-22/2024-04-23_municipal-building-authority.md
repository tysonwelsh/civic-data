Proposed Resolution 2024-1 regarding a budget amendment for
$61,258,700 in bond proceeds for the construction of the WonderBlock
parking structures and bond proceeds/costs
         A memo from the City Comptroller regarding a proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2023-2024 budget amendment came before
the Board for consideration. The memo stated the proposed budget amendment totals $61,258,700 and makes the following
changes:
             • Recognizes RDA Sales Tax and Tax Increment Bond Proceeds ($4,627,850) and bond premiums ($439,825)
                   and appropriates those funds for construction of parking structures at the WonderBlock project ($5,000,000)
                   and bond issuance costs ($67,675).
             • Recognizes MBA Lease Revenue Bond Proceeds ($55,075,800)* and premiums ($1,115,225) and appropriates
                   those funds for construction of parking structures at the WonderBlock project ($55,129,000) and issuance costs
                   ($1,062,025).
         Remaining bond proceeds of ~$9M will be held in restricted debt service and capitalized interest accounts for
appropriation at a later date.
         Deputy Comptroller Jensen summarized the memo and presented a spreadsheet detailing the source of funds and impacts
to revenues and appropriations included in the MBA budget. .
         Chair Richey introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2024-1, entitled:
                   “A resolution of Ogden City Municipal Building Authority amending the budget for the Fiscal Year July 1,
                   2023, to June 30, 2024, by increasing the anticipated revenues for a gross increase of $61,258,700 from sources
                   as detailed in the body of this resolution; and increasing the appropriations for a gross increase of $61,258,700
                   as detailed in the body of this resolution.”
         Chair Richey then opened the public hearing.
         There being no persons appearing to be heard, TRUSTEE HYER MOVED THE PUBLIC HEARING BE CLOSED.
MOTION WAS SECONDED BY TRUSTEE CHOBERKA, ALL VOTING AYE.
         ON A MOTION BY TRUSTEE CHOBERKA AND SECONDED BY TRUSTEE HYER, RESOLUTION 2024-1
WAS ADOPTED UPON THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE –TRUSTEES BLAIR, CHOBERKA,
GRAF, HYER, MYERS, VICE CHAIR WHITE, AND CHAIR RICHEY. VOTING NO – NONE.


     There being no further business to come before the Council, TRUSTEE HYER MOVED THE MEETING
ADJOURN AT 8:41 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY TRUSTEE CHOBERKA, ALL VOTING AYE.
