CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL MEETING
AUGUST 25, 2020

The Cache County Council convened in a regular session on August 25, 2020 at 5:00 p.m.
in the Cache County Council Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.

ATTENDANCE:

Chairman: Karl B. Ward

e Discussion — A request to allow unincorporated peninsulas in the annexation of
18.553 acres from the Unincorporated County into Hyrum City — Tayler Jensen

reviewed the request indicating statute requires the Council’s approval for an annexation
that creates an island or peninsula. Craig Rasmussen, Hyrum City Council member, said
Hyrum City is willing to enter into an agreement to maintain the roads.

e Resolution No. 2020-21 — Allowing unincorporated peninsulas in the annexation of
18.553 acres from the Unincorporated County into Hyrum City — The Council was

hesitant to approve the resolution because of concerns over road maintenance.

ACTION: Motion by Council member White to waive the rules and approve Resolution
No. 2020-21 — Allowing unincorporated peninsulas in the annexation of 18.553 acres from
the Unincorporated County into Hyrum City. Borup seconded the motion. The motion
failed, 3 aye — Borup, Ward & White and 4 nay — Erickson, Tidwell, Worthen & Zilles.

COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS

Karl Ward acknowledged the approaching retirement of Clerk/Auditor Zollinger and recognized
her as a stalwart, anchor and voice of reason and good judgment. He has appreciated her
guidance and wished her and her family the best.

Gordon Zilles said Zollinger has been classed as one of the very best clerks in the state of
Utah.

Dave Erickson asked Zollinger how many marriage licenses her office has issued. Zollinger
replied the office issues about 1,500 marriage licenses a year.

Gina Worthen thanked Zollinger for her service. It is appreciated.
Clerk/Auditor Zollinger thanked the Council for their support.

Craig Buttars noted there have been many election changes and all have gone smoothly.
Buttars said he has never had any reason to doubt the integrity of the elections under Zollinger’s

purview.

Barbara Tidwell stated most people don’t realize how much time is put in to serving in a
position such as Zollinger’s and doing it for thirty-one years says a lot about Zollinger’s
commitment.

Tidwell reminded the Council of the Cache Celebration of Women’s Suffrage tomorrow evening
at 6:30 p.m. on the east steps of the Historic Courthouse.

Paul Borup remarked he has all the confidence in the world that the mail-in voting in Cache
County is topnotch and he has appreciated Zollinger’s calm and steady leadership.

Jon White observed something needs to be done to address citizens’ concerns about the green
waste bins being used by nonresidents and the fact that Logan City provides them, but the cities
have to maintain them without compensation. The disparity in the fee Logan City is proposing
for Logan and other country residents also needs to be addressed.

MEMORANDUM

TO: Cache County Council

FROM: Dianna Schaeffer, Chief Deputy Auditor
DATE: August 25, 2020

SUBJECT: Request for Tax Relief Filing Extension

Every year Cache County processes hundreds of tax relief applications for various indigent abatements, exclusive
use exemptions, military exemptions, blind exemptions, settlements, and deferrals. The application deadline is
September 1" in most cases, but may be extended until December 31 if the county finds good cause.

e Low Income Abatement - State law §59-2-1107 through §59-2-1109 permits counties to abate taxes for
indigent persons subject to proper application, documentation, and deadlines. Allowable extension
§59-2-1109(2)(d).

e Blind Exemption - State law §59-2-1106 provides counties authority to abate taxes for the blind subject to
proper application, documentation, and deadlines. Allowable extension §59-2-1106(2)(b)(ii).

e Military Exemption - State law §59-2-1104 and §59-2-1105 allows counties to abate taxes for veterans and
active duty armed forces subject to proper application, documentation, and deadlines. Military applications
are allowed an “automatic” one year extension per UCA §59-2-1105(2)(b)(i).

e Non-Profit Use Exemption — State law §59-2-1102 provides counties authority to abate taxes for religious
charitable, and educational organizations. Applications were due by March 1st and have been processed.

e Settlement/Deferral - No deadlines are mandated in §59-2-1347.

After the tax notices are mailed at the end of October, the county will be besieged by citizens in dire need of tax
relief. We must be compliant with the law of the land while maintaining the intent of the law to help those in
need. Therefore, it is in the best interest of the community that the Cache County Council extends the filing
deadline for low income and blind tax relief to November 30, 2020. By extending the filing deadline, Cache
County can continue helping eligible citizens and remain compliant with the law in regards to applications for tax
relief. This would solve the problem of processing impoverished applicants while remaining legally compliant.

Request is: Approved “ Tabled Denied.
