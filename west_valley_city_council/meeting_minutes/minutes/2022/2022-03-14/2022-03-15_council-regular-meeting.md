ORDINANCE 22-10: AMEND TITLE 2, CHAPTER 3 OF THE WEST VALLEY
CITY MUNICIPAL CODE, MAKING ADJUSTMENTS IN THE WEST VALLEY
CITY COUNCIL DISTRICT BOUNDARIES
Wayne Pyle, City Manager, presented proposed Ordinance 22-10 that would amend Title
2, Chapter 3 of the West Valley City Municipal Code, making adjustments in the West Valley
City Council District Boundaries.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Section 10-3-205.5, Utah Code Annotated, requires all municipalities, with some or
       all council members being elected from districts, to “make any adjustments in the
       boundaries of the districts as may be required to maintain districts of substantially
       equal population.” In accordance with State law, City staff has evaluated existing
       council districts and has determined that adjustments are necessary.

       2020 Census data was utilized by staff to create two options for proposed council
       boundaries. The population of each proposed district varies by no more than 1% from
       the ideal population, which is one fourth of the total City population. In addition to
       balancing population between districts, staff also kept district boundaries to major
       streets. The table below lists the population of each proposed district for the two
       options and the percentage by which each district varies from the ideal population.

            District          Option 1         % Change            Option 2         % Change
                                               from Ideal                           from Ideal
                                               Population                           Population
               1                   35,018            -0.11%              34,907           -0.43%
               2                   34,761            -0.84%              35,209            0.43%
               3                   35,279             0.63%              34,942           -0.33%
               4                   35,172             0.33%              35,172            0.33%
