                                           AFFIDAVIT OF POSTING
                                     SYNOPSIS OF OGDEN CITY ORDINANCE



         I, Jason Gould, the duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, in and for the City of Ogden, Weber
County, State of Utah, do hereby certify the Ogden City Council at its December 19, 2023, meeting adopted
the following:

        ORDINANCE NO. 2023-71: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY, UTAH, AMENDING SECTION 8-1-4 OF
        THE OGDEN MUNICIPAL CODE TO ELIMINATE THE SUNSET PROVISION FOR THE AIRPORT
        ADVISORY COMMITTEE; AND BY PROVIDING THAT THIS ORDINANCE SHALL BECOME
        EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY UPON POSTING AFTER FINAL PASSAGE.

        Copies of all ordinances in their entirety are available for review in the Ogden City Recorder’s Office
located on the second floor of the Municipal Building, 2549 Washington Boulevard #210 during regular
business hours.

The undersigned, duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, does hereby certify that the above notice was
posted in accordance with Utah State law on this 28th day of December 2023.
