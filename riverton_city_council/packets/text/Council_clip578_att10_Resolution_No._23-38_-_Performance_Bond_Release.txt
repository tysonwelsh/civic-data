                                    RIVERTON, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-38

    RESOLUTION APPROVING RIVERTON CITY’S RELEASE OF BOND TO
 GUARANTEE PUBLIC IMPROVEMENTS WITHIN THE DALLING SUBDIVISION
                   (1801) DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, JORY DALLING (“Developer”) has engaged in the development of a
subdivision in the vicinity of 12750 SOUTH 2700 WEST Riverton, Utah, known as the
DALLING SUBDIVISION (1801) Subdivision;

       WHEREAS, Developer is required by Riverton City Code § 17.10.030 to post a bond to
guarantee construction of dedicated improvements in a manner satisfactory to Riverton City
standards and specifications;

        WHEREAS, upon completion of the project, 90% of Developer’s bond value is require
to be released pursuant to a bond agreement with developer and Riverton City Code § 17.10.030;
and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to inspection reports provided by City staff, the Riverton City
Council finds and determines that the DALLING SUBDIVISION (1801) Subdivision
improvements subject to Developer’s bond are constructed in a manner which meets Riverton
City standards and specifications, and therefore should be accepted by the City; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council further finds and determines that with acceptance
of said improvements, the remaining 10% of Developer’s bond value shall be retained by
Riverton City to insure that the improvements subject to Developer’s bond shall be maintained in
a state of good repair by Developer for a period of 12 months from the date of acceptance by
Riverton City; and

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. The improvements installed by Developer within the DALLING SUBDIVISION (1801)
      Subdivision and subject to Developer’s bond are hereby accepted.
   2. The City Manager is authorized to direct staff to release 90% of Developer’s bond held
      by Riverton to guarantee successful installation of aforesaid improvements in accordance
      with Riverton City standards and specifications.
   3. The remaining 10% of Developer’s bond shall be retained by Riverton City for a
      minimum of 12 months, and until released by the Riverton City Council.
   4. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
