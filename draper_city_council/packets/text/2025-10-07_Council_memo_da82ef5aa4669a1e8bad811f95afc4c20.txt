MEMO
To: City Council
From: Robert Markle
Date: 2025-10-07
Re: Resolution #25-51


Comments:

Draper City provides solid waste and recyclable material collection services (“Collection
Services”) to promote public health, safety, and welfare. Providing such services on
private streets increases liability and maintenance costs due to the city’s lack of control
over private road conditions.

Draper City has determined that it’s in the City’s best interests to contract for a third-party
provider to offer Collection Services. Draper City issued a Request For Proposal (RFP 25-
13) in compliance with applicable procurement laws to secure a qualified contractor for
the curbside collection, transportation, and disposal of solid waste and the collection and
processing of recyclable materials for subdivisions on private streets.

Following the review of proposals and negotiations, Draper City selected Ace Recycling
and Disposal Inc. as a city-approved service provider for said services. Draper City and
Ace Recycling and Disposal Inc. have negotiated an Agreement setting forth the terms,
scope of work, compensation, and liability protections for such services.
