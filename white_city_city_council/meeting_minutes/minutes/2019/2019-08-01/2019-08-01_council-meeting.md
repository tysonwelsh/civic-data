             THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to consider an
Ordinance creating Title 12: “Code Enforcement and Community Preservation”.

              Paul Ashton reviewed Title 12: “Code Enforcement and Community Preservation”
stating some additional amendments have been made.

            Council Member Flint, seconded by Council Member Dickerson, moved to adopt
the amendment 12.3.170 to Ordinance No. 19-07-01. The motion passed unanimously.

             Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Dickerson, moved to open
the public hearing to hear comments on Ordinance No. 19-07-01. The motion passed
unanimously.

               Scott Little stated the White City Metro Township Planning Commission reviewed
this ordinance on June 25 and tabled the item until July. On July 16, the White City Metro
Township Planning Commission was provided with a draft of the ordinance that is very different
from the one that was noticed and being presented today.

               Mr. Ashton stated the White City Metro Township Planning Commission was
given the ordinance for recommendation and input, it does not have say in what is approved.

                Mr. Little stated the ordinance that the White City Metro Township Planning
Commission reviewed gives a better due process and notice. He suggests that the Council look
at that draft and consider it for adoption.

                Mr. Ashton stated a consistent ordinance is being adopted across the metro
townships. Each metro township planning commission reviewed it and made suggestions. The
original draft will look different from the final draft.

                Mr. Little stated if a public hearing is going to be held with the White City Metro
Township Planning Commission then the Council should look at what it approved, especially if
the Council is going to state that they approved an ordinance based on what the White City Metro
Township Planning Commission reviewed.

               Mayor Flint stated she sends the White City Metro Township Planning
Commission a packet of the Councils meetings and there has been no comments made about
the ordinance.

              Mr. Ashton stated what is important is a consistent ordinance. The Council can
approve the ordinance, he will compare the two drafts and it can be amended next month or the
ordinance can be tabled until next month.

Resolution

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution to expend up to $5,000 from the
White City Administrative Funds account for the completion of a sidewalk project:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-08-01                                                      Date: August 1, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING THE EXPENDITURE OF UP TO $5,000 FROM THE WHITE CITY
       ADMINISTRATIVE FUNDS FUND BALANCE ACCOUNT TO FUND THE
       COMPLETION OF A SIDEWALK PROJECT

                                           RECITALS

A.     The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq.

B.     The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.


                                                4
DATE            THURSDAY                              AUGUST                               1, 2019

C.     Due to cost overruns, a small section of a sidewalk project was not going to be completed
as a part of the bigger capital project.

D.     Pursuant to Ordinance 18-03-01, White City Policy #2: “Purchasing, Contracting, and
Procurement” Article 3-1(a) allows the Mayor to make purchases up to $10,000 without bids of
any type.

E.     The estimate to complete the project is less than $5,000.

F.     On July 18, 2019 the Mayor Authorized Salt Lake County Public Works, the authorized
contractor for the White City sidewalk project to proceed with a price limitation of up to $5,000 to
complete the work – funds to be spent from the White City Administrative Fund Balance Account.

G.    Pursuant to fiscal best practices, to provide proper transparency regarding this
expenditure, this resolution serves to provide documentation to this transaction.

                                          RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council hereby
approves the Mayor’s expenditure of up to $5,000 to complete the sidewalk project; and be it
further

        RESOLVED, pursuant to the Mayor’s direction, the funding that shall be used to pay for
the project come from the White City Metro Township Administrative Fund Balance Account.

     APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 1st day of
AUGUST, 2019.

White City Metro Township Council Member Reports

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

               Council Member Cutler stated the 2019/2020 budget was adopted. The budget
is being separated by precinct which makes each precinct responsible for their budget. Litigation
with Riverton and Herriman cities is ongoing. The State Auditor reviewed the budget for
compliance and state statutes, compared line items with other documentation, and compared past
budgets with the present budget.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Authority Service Area (UFSA)

               Council Member Perry stated new liaisons will be assigned to four municipalities
with one of those being White City Metro Township. On August 15, ten candidates will be
interviewed for the four liaisons positions. UFA would like someone from the Council to attend
the interviews. Chief Peterson is reviewing all the interlocal agreements with the municipalities
and would like feedback from the Council. There were no major issues on the 4th of July. SLVLESA
received renderings for the Millcreek station. They are looking at a small station concept and a
large concept that would be modular. The stations will be a lot more modest than stations built in
the past.
