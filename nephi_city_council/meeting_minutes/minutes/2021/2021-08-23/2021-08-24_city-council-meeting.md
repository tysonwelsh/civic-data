Nephi City
 Work Session Minutes
August 24, 2021
7:00 p.m.
Nephi City Council Chambers



	•	 Barbara Finlinson was sworn in as the Nephi Justice Court Judge by City Recorder Lisa Brough.  Mayor Nielson read a biography of Ms. Finlinson’s education and accomplishments.  

	•	 Mr. Jody Adams addressed the council about his desire to build a mother-in-law apartment on his property.  City Administrator Seth Atkinson updated the council about recent legislation that allows internal accessory dwelling units (ADU) anywhere in the city.  The council discussed the idea of allowing external accessory dwelling units such as mother-in-law apartments.  Councilor Memmott said that it becomes difficult to manage external ADU and was hesitant to change code to allow them. Councilor Worwood, Councilor Ostler, and Councilor Seely would like to explore the idea of external ADU.  Councilor Jones had no comment.

	•	Mr. Atkinson reported on a preliminary draft of the city’s 10-year Capital Facility Master Plan for the electric system.  Due to this growth rate and the industrial customers expected to be added to the system, it is anticipated that the usage on the system will increase significantly in the next 10 years.  The council discussed the need for a second main connection for the entire electrical system.   Rocky Mountain Power had earlier indicated that they would bear most of the cost of building a secondary connection, but late in the day Power Superintendent Rust Finlinson received some information from Rocky Mountain Power that the cost to Nephi City would be much more than was anticipated.  Mr. Finlinson said that he would continue to pursue other avenues.

	•	Mr. Atkinson reported that with the untimely passing of Planning Administrator Glenn Greenhalgh, it had left a vacancy for the planning position.  Nephi is entering in an era of unprecedented growth that will need to be tracked and managed.  Mr. Atkinson asked the council if they thought Nephi needed a full-time planner.  He presented the cost estimate figures of hiring a full-time planner and some of the duties or the position.  The Mayor and all councilors agreed that a full-time planner was needed, and they directed staff to proceed in the hiring process of a full-time planner.

	•	The council discussed the need to acquire easements for drainage of storm water. As part of the UDOT project to convey stormwater drainage away from Highway 28 on the south end of the city, the city had proposed using existing drainage ditches in the area.   There is most likely a prescriptive easement on these ditches that can be used for drainage but challenges from property owners may still arise.   City Attorney Kasey Wright advised that the prescriptive easements are legally defensible.  Acquiring easements for these properties would be the safest route but there is no identified funding for stormwater purposes.  The council directed staff to pursue obtaining the easements.   
   
	•	City Administrator updates: Mr. Atkinson reported the current sales tax figures.  Mr. Atkinson reminded the council about the service project and 9/11 program at the park.  Mr. Atkinson informed the council about a couple of possible annexations that may be coming before the council. 

	•	City Council Updates:  Councilor Seely asked for a progress report on the Field House.  Councilor Jones gave an update on his health and apologized for not being able to attend the meetings the last couple of months. 

	•	Adjournment at 9:00 p.m.
