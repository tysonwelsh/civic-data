1.     RATIFY THE SIGNATURE OF THE COMMISSION CHAIR ON A LETTER TO PROVO CITY
       REGARDING THE PUBLIC USE TRANSPORTATION FEE
(Track A, 03:04)
Commissioner Graves read the following statement regarding Consent Agenda Item No. 1, asking it to be included
as his own opinion within the record:

          “The issue that Provo and Orem face is that the majority of residents in this county drive on
          their roads, and they do not have the funds to accommodate all of that traffic. The legislature
          created the ability to use sales tax dollars for roads. The allotment is easily calculated and is to
          show where people are spending their money. Just over forty percent of that ‘pie’ is spent in
          those two cities, yet only twenty-five percent of the most recent allocation was given to them
UTAH COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

Page 2

          through MAG (the Mountainland Association of Governments). MAG continues to gain
          strength and has pushed an agenda that is not always in the public’s best interest when utilizing
          tax dollars for transportation. Why do the cities not get representing figures to maintain their
          roads that Utah County residents are utilizing? The small remedy to be better partners for the
          county was to create a usage fee – the cost to the county being about $2,000 annually, even
          though our footprint is much greater. I’m asking that we as a Commission start trying to
          address that issue in MAG.”
