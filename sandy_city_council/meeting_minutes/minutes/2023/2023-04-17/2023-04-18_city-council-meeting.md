3.         23-148                 Parks and Recreation Department presenting the Council with interlocal 
                                  agreements between Sandy City and Salt Lake County related to TRCC 
                                  funds
                   Attachments:   ILA Packet.pdf

                                  Dan Medina, Parks & Recreation Director, presented an Interlocal Agreement (ILA) with 
                                  Salt Lake County regarding Tourism, Recreation, Cultural, Convention, and Airport 
                                  Facilities (TRCC) funding. The TRCC funding can be used for certain capital projects and 
                                  consulting work by public entities within the County. Sandy City submitted three 
                                  proposals for TRCC funding for capital projects: Amphitheater bathroom and Hillside Cafe, 
                                  Parks, Recreation, and Trails Master Plan Update, and Alta Canyon Sports Center 
                                  Architectural Designs. All three proposals were approved for funding. The ILA will be 
                                  presented to the Council for consideration and action at the May 2, 2023 Council 
                                  Meeting. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Council Member Ryan Mecham arrived.




4.         23-147                 Human Resources Department presenting the Council with a Fire 
                                  Department Step & Grade Pay Plan Proposal
                   Attachments:   Fire Department Pay Plan Presentation

                                  Katrina Frederick, Human Resource Director, presented the Step and Grade Pay Plan 
                                  Proposal for the Fire Department. She provided background information and timeline. She 
                                  detailed the many factors taken into consideration when developing this Fire Pay Plan 
                                  Proposal, which included the following: priorities identified by fire employees, number of 
                                  staff separations, pay scale, staffing needs and constraints, and the funding of short-term 
                                  needs. She reviewed recent employee turnover. In a 3 month time span - the Fire 
                                  Department lost 7 employees with a combined experience of 77 years, and she provided 
                                  pay comparisons with Class A cities similar to Sandy City. The Fire Compensation 
                                  Committee reviewed all the factors and comparisons and has recommended a number of 
                                  proposed revisions to the Fire Pay Plan including the following: reduction in the number of 
                                  steps for a variety of fire personnel positions, career advancement options for EMT's and 
                                  Paramedics, and increase pay to match the average of the second and third highest 
                                  cities in our class. The total of the proposed revisions to the Fire Pay Plan are estimated 
                                  at $635,000. Brian Kelley provided additional information and insight regarding the 
                                  proposal. Council questions and comments followed. Chief Bassett thanked the Council 
                                  and Administration for addressing the needs of the Fire Department. By addressing these 
                                  needs, the result is a highly trained fire department staff that can meet the needs of our 
                                  city's residents.

5.         23-145                 First Reading: Alta Canyon Sports Center Naming Rights Proposal

                     Sponsors: Sharkey, Earl and Robinson

                   Attachments:   Draft Naming Rights Proposal Package

                                  First Reading: Council Members Sharkey, Robinson, Earl presented a draft proposal 
                                  outlining the process for pursuing sponsorships and the sale of naming rights associated 
                                  with the Alta Canyon Sports Center. The City Council will act as the Alta Canyon Special

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson provided an update from the Sandy Arts Guild. The Arts 
                    Guild recent production of The Descendants was a wonderful performance and a very 
                    successful production.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham will be attending the Utah League of Cities and Towns 
                    spring conference in St. George later this week and will report on the conference at next 
                    week's Council Meeting.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey thanked the Public Works Department for inviting the 
                    Sandy Youth Council to their facility and teaching us about the Public Works Department 
                    and all their responsibilities and work they do for Sandy City. She also wanted to extend 
                    a special thanks to the Public Utilities staff for their continued efforts to clear brush in her 
                    neighborhood and around Sandy City and for the preventative work to keep our city safe 
                    from flooding this spring. We are all in this together and we have great people working 
                    here at the City. She also mentioned that Snowbird Sports Education Center has 
                    expressed an interest in utilizing Alta Canyon Sports Center for their sports training.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update from the Sandy Historic Committee 
                    and the Senior Center. She thanked Administration and Council Staff for all their help with 
                    today's school field trip for her third grade classes at East Sandy Elementary School.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski acknowledged all involved in today's field trip with East Sandy 
                    Elementary School. Today, Governor Cox declared a State of Emergency for flood risk. 
                    She encouraged residents to go to our Sandy website for additional information on flood 
                    risk, assessment and preparation. Our city departments are monitoring the potential flood 
                    risk around the clock. Sandy City is currently not in a state of emergency but is 
                    monitoring the situation very carefully. She extended heartfelt condolences from 
                    Administration, Council and on behalf of the entire City to the family of ABC 4 reporter 
                    Tracy Smith.

CAO Report

                    KIm Bell, Deputy Mayor extended Administration's appreciation to the emergency 
                    managment team for all their efforts to ensure the safety of our residents. 

                    Council moved to Item 10 on the Agenda.
