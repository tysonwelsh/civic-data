   1. ZONE CHANGE (ZC) (Public Hearing) Legislative

       Consider a request to change the Zoning from Agricultural, Minimum lot size 40,000 square feet (A-1)
       to Residential Estates, minimum lot size 20,000 square feet (RE-20) and Residential Estates, minimum
       lot size 37,500 square feet (RE-37.5) to create a new residential subdivision. The total property is
       approximately 16.47 acres. This property is generally located on the south side of 2450 South Street at
       Crimson Cliffs Way. The applicant is TJ Ventures LLC, and the representative is Bob Hermandson.
       The project will be known as TJ Ventures Estates. Case No. 2022-ZC-010. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

       Carol Davidson presented the following:

       Carol Davidson – Showed an aerial view of the location. The general plan is Low Density Residential.
       This area has a lot of agricultural land, a lot of Low Density Residential. The applicant is proposing to
       match the zones that are across the street on 2450. The portion in Washington City is slated to be R-1-
       10 so smaller residential lots. On the south side there is a planned pedestrian path that comes from the
       north there is also an equestrian trail planned. There is an irrigation easement on the south side. We
       will talk about those items with the preliminary plat. Staff does recommend approval.

         the Hillside Overlay. The property is generally located southwest of 475 Donlee Drive and is zoned
         Planned Development Mixed Use (PD-MU) and Open Space (OS). The applicant is Isaac Barlow,
         and the representative is Mike Bradshaw. Case No. 2022-HS-002. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

         Wes Jenkins presented the following:

         Wes Jenkins – When Tech Ridge wanted to develop we requested that they take their storm drain as
         much as possible to the west side where there are no buildings beneath it. They are trying to stay out

      A. Consider a request for a twenty-two (22) lot residential subdivision known as Desert Solace Phase 4
         Amended located along the future extension of Rim Runner Drive at approximately 4200 East in the
         Desert Canyons Development. The property is 10.4 acres and is zoned R-1-12. The applicant is
         DSG Engineering, representative Ken Miller. Case No. 2022-PP-007. (Staff – Wes Jenkins)

         Wes Jenkins presented the following:

         Wes Jenkins – Basically they are just adding one lot next to the existing wash that they piped
         underneath that road. We had the engineer look at it to make sure that lot 444 would not be
         impacted by floodwaters in a 100-year event. He has run a cross section that shows the elevation of
         the lot and the 100-year flood surface that comes through that wash. Then obviously they will do
         some rip rap to protect the lot from the erosion.
