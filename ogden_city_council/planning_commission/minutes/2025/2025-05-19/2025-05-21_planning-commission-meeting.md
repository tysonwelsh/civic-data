1. 24" Street Interchange, Master Plan Discussion
Senior Planner Brandon Rypien went over area coverage and the Land Use map. He also addressed
concerns of a possible roundabout at 25'* Street and D Avenue that was discussed in the May 7"
Planning Commission meeting and highlighted some of the benefits such as easy traffic flow and it
discouraging large truck traffic. He also touched on the Interchange bringing an ease to congestion
and increasing 24" Street capacity.

2. Zone Ogden Discussion, Development Standards
Presenters John Jensen and Jen Gardner focused on updates to the unified development code

and development standard chapters including improvements and infrastructure.

As there was no additional business to come before the Commission, COMMISSIONER
