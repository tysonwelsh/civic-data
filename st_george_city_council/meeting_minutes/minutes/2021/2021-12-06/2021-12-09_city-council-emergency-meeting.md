                        ST. GEORGE CITY COUNCIL MINUTES
                               EMERGENCY MEETING
                            DECEMBER 9, 2021 5:00 P.M.
                       ADMINISTRATIVE CONFERENCE ROOM

PRESENT:
     Mayor Michele Randall – via Zoom
     Councilmember Bryan Smethurst
     Councilmember Gregg McArthur
     Councilmember Dannielle Larkin
     Councilmember Vardell Curtis
     City Manager Adam Lenhard
     City Attorney Tani Pack Downing
     City Recorder Christina Fernandez

EXCUSED:
     Councilmember Jimmie Hughes

OPENING:
     Mayor Randall called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance.

ADJOURN TO A CLOSED SESSION :
     MOTION:
         A motion was made by Councilmember McArthur to adjourn to a closed
                session to discuss a property purchase.
     SECOND:
         The motion was seconded by Councilmember Smethurst.
     VOTE:
         Mayor Randall called for a roll call vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.

ADJOURN:
     MOTION:
         A motion was made by Councilmember Curtis to adjourn.
     SECOND:
         The motion was seconded by Councilmember Larkin.
     VOTE:
         Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.
