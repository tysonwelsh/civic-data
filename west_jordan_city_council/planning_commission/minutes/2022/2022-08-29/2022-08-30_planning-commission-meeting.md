 1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair Whitelock called the meeting to order at 5:30 pm.

 2. GENERAL PLAN DISCUSSION
         a. Discussion on a Draft of the 2022 General Plan for the City of West Jordan
            Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson provided a brief updated regarding the progress
            of the General Plan. Assistant City Attorney Duncan Murray outlined state law
            requirements and noted that the completion and update of the General Plan allows for
            other master plans to be updated as well.
            Council Member Zach Jacob arrived 5:42 pm
            At the request of Council Chair Kayleen Whitelock staff reviewed the proposed
            amendments to the Future Land Use Map and Council and Commission Members were
            asked to provide any additional requests or suggested amendments.

            At the request of Vice Chair Kelvin Green, an area east of 6400 W and north of 7800 S
            designated as Future Park (Dark Green) should be changed to Community Commercial.

            Council and Commission Members gave consensus that the northwest corner of City
            boundaries, west of U-111 should be left as is and not changed to match the zoning of the
            annexation policy as proposed.

            Vice Chair Green requested that the Bland property, located at approximately 7140 West

           were okay to designate it as medium density residential.
           Vice Chair Kelvin Green left at 6:49 pm
           Mr. McCandless reviewed the Southwest corner of the city pointing out the designations

           Council Member Melissa Worthen left at 7:02 pm
           Chair Whitelock reported that during discussions with General Plan Committee Chair
           Robert Lively it was noted that there were a lot of goals listed in the General Plan. She
           asked how they would work on achieving the goals. Mr. Lively wondered who would be
           assigned to each goal. She believed that unless there were steps to track and achieve the
           goals, that they should be removed.
           Council Member David Pack arrived 7:05 pm
           Mr. McCandless felt the goals were relied upon and believed they could be separated out.
           City Planner Larry Gardner believed there could be a process for the goals to be

           Council and Commission Members gave consensus to update the General Plan every five
           years and suggested breaking it out further into sections and updating certain sections of
           the plan more often. Referencing the Future Land Use Map Council and Commission
           Members requested that it be updated as often as possible to keep it current.

           Council Vice Chair Kelvin Green referencing the topic that development pays for
           infrastructure, he requested that the city explore the use of Public Infrastructure Districts
           to help with some of those impacts. Council Chair Kayleen Whitelock requested it be
           added to a future Work Session discussion.

           Commissioner Ammon Allen suggested that the goals throughout the General Plan may
           be better labeled as Guiding Principles where the General Plan is a guide book for other
           plans. All members agreed.

           Policy Analyst Cassidy Hansen made the suggestion that staff could review the goals to
           determine which goals should be included in the General Plan and which ones could be
           moved to the master plans. Additionally, she suggested including a table in the back
           referencing the goal, the action, the cost, the timing, and which department is over it. All
           members agreed to direct staff to do so.

       b. Discussion on Scheduling Future Land Use Training
          Council Chair Kayleen Whitelock referencing a book from Utah Leagues of Cities and
          Towns explained that leadership believed that it was a good reference for issues that
          needed to be addressed. She noted concern over how staff was providing information and
          a need to better understand their role. She felt that having a combined training could be
          very helpful. Planning Commission Chair Jay Thomas also desired additional land use
          trainings as well as more education regarding their roles and responsibilities.

           Referencing the book provided, Vice Chair Kelvin Green asked Commissioners to
           specifically review the dual role of a Planning Commission Member found on page 18.

            It was suggested the combined training could be held on a Planning Commission meeting
            night or on an opposite City Council night. It was suggested that City Attorney Rob Wall
            could provide a 45-minute training.

            Commission Member Matt Quinney suggested breaking the training up into two or three
            sessions as part of their monthly training and asked that it be done in January, so it
            involves new Planning Commission Members.

            Chair Thomas requested that the trainings be done sooner rather than later. Chair
            Whitelock clarified that she wanted the Council to attend the trainings as well. Council
            Member Pack suggested the trainings could be emailed ahead of the meeting and
            discussed when they meet together to save time. Chair Thomas felt in person interactions
            could not be duplicated online.

            Commissioner Ammon asked for further clarification as to what the City Council wanted
            from the Planning Commission in order to feel more confident with the body. Chair
            Whitelock believed a more in depth conversation was needed about processes. Planning
            Commissioners asked for more specific examples. Vice Chair Green clarified that he felt
            the Planning Commission Members should be making findings to determine whether a
            development was appropriate or not, rather than staff providing them with those
            findings. Commission Members felt that was already taking place. After a lengthy
            discussion, it was determined that City Attorney Rob Wall should work with Chair
            Thomas and staff to coordinate the preparation of reports presented to Planning
            Commission Members. It was also resolved that Commission Members could receive
            additional training during a future Planning Commission meeting.

 3. ADJOURN
Council Member Jacob moved to adjourn the meeting. Council Member Worthen seconded the motion.
All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously.

The meeting adjourned at 8:42 pm.
