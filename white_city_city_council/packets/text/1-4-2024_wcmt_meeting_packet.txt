                                             Workshop

Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Chief Jason Mazuran stated he has been tasked to reorganize the UPD. The plan
remains fluid and can be customized to fit the metro township's needs. For the purpose of
efficiency, certain services could be contracted through other agencies, including the Salt Lake
County Sheriff’s Office. It makes sense for jurisdictions to come together to pay for services.

               Paul Ashton asked if there was going to be an increase in the number of officers
in UPD.

               Chief Mazuran stated there will be a decrease in staff, but it will not be significant.
He is trying to make reasonable estimations to provide proper coverage. The finance chart
continues to be worked on to get the numbers to where they need to be.

               Council Member Cardenaz stated there may be sticker shock but there are going
to have to be some changes to make UPD work.

               Council Member Price asked what the deadline is to have the transition complete.

                Chief Mazuran stated the date discussed within UPD is July 1, 2024; however,
the legislature provided more time than that. To make the transition smooth, he anticipates moving
each group over one at a time.

Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA) Potential Tax Increase

               Rachel Anderson, District Administrator, UFSA, stated UFSA is proposing a tax
increase and with that, it must report to all member entities within the jurisdiction. White City is
unique in that it is completely within the boundaries of Sandy City. To get the most effective
services, UFA contracts with Sandy City Fire to provide services to White City residents. Tax
increases have been infrequent with the last one being in 2018. The board is proposing a 12
percent increase this year and 12 percent the following year.

               Tony Hill, Chief Financial Officer, Unified Fire Authority (UFA), delivered a
PowerPoint presentation reviewing the cost per average taxpayer, property tax rates, and
property tax revenue. The public hearing will be held on December 5, 2023, at 6:00 p.m.

Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Detective Josh Smith reviewed calls for service for September, noting increased
calls related to public order, civil matters, and welfare checks. Officers focused on general safety
around schools and school zones. There was an increase in traffic calls because officers were
asked to piggyback with crossing guards and provide education to drivers.

              Council Member Shelton stated he received an email regarding a crosswalk that
was taken out. The crosswalk is in Sandy City but borders White City.

               Detective Smith stated the crosswalk was removed when the road was repaved.
A member of the PTA contacted UPD to state her concerns about the crosswalk. She was
instructed to email Chief Randy Thomas, who then asked her to contact Canyons School District.
Canyons School District stated the crosswalk was not necessary, but it would revisit the issue.
Crossing guards are not supposed to cross children when there is not a crosswalk but are doing
it anyway to help get kids cross the street safely.

He also noted that on October 28, 2023, there will be a prescription round-up at the Midvale City
precinct.
