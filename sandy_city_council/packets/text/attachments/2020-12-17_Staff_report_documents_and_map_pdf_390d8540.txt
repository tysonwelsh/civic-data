DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Mary McKinley, is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to expand the operation
of an existing Child Care business from eight children per day to a maximum of 16 children on
the property located at 9694 South 575 East. (See Exhibit #1 – Application Materials). Any child
daycare desiring to operate with more than eight children per day is required to obtain a
Conditional Use Permit for a Category II home occupation from the Planning Commission.

The property is zoned R-1-8 and contains a 1260 square foot single-family home. Surrounding
properties to the north, west, east and south are single-family homes zoned R-1-8. The property
directly fronts on to 575 E and is 0.22 acres (9583 sq. ft.) (See Exhibit #2 – Vicinity Map).

The applicant is proposing to expand the operation of an existing childcare business which
provides children a well-rounded experience throughout their day and which caters to families in
the local area. The applicant has been operating a daycare at this location since 2018. She has
been licensed as a childcare provider since 2006.
McKinley Childcare – CUP-11-19-5946                                                     Page 2



The proposed daycare will operate with two employees, the applicant and one full-time
employee. The applicant has the capacity in both the indoor area of the home and the fully
fenced off backyard area to sustain having 16 different children at one time. The children range
in age from four through 12. The hours of operation are from 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Drop off and
pickup will occur along 575 E. with cars directed to come from 700 E. to the north, and exit to
the south onto Sego Lily Dr. (See Exhibit #3 – Traffic Pattern).

ANALYSIS
Section 21-11-05(F)(3) of the Sandy City Development Code indicates the maximum limit
granted by the Planning Commission when a child day care is expected to exceed eight children
at one time.
    a. A maximum of 16 children is permitted at any one time.
    b. A maximum of 18 children is permitted per day.
    c. These numbers shall include the licensee’s and any employee’s children if they are under
        six years of age under the care of the licensee at the time of the home occupation is
        conducted.
    d. A maximum of 24 vehicular stops per day for child drop off to pick up is permitted.

A neighborhood meeting was held on December 8, 2020 at 7:00 PM by the Community
Development Department. Four neighbors attended the meeting. A list of the concerns discussed
include:

   •   The level of traffic on 575 E.
   •   Where new customers will be coming i.e. anyone in the community or a local school
       specifically
   •   Cars blocking driveways during pick up and drop off times
   •   Whether 2 staff are enough to keep children distanced during the pandemic
   •   Whether the house is large enough to accommodate 16 children along with social
       distancing
   •   Whether the number of animals and the cleanliness of the property provide a quality
       environment for the children
   •   Whether 16 children at one time is just too many

2 neighbors plainly said that they are opposed to the increase

Notices were mailed to the property owners within a 500-foot radius for the Planning
Commission meeting.

CONCERNS
There are concerns with the number of animals located at the property. Staff was made aware
during the neighborhood meeting about several animals on site. The applicant has two turkeys,
along with eight chickens and three cats. A turkey is considered a small farm animal under the
city code, and therefore would not be allowed in the R-1-8 zone.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for Mary
McKinley for the property located at 9694 S. 575 E. to operate a Category II Home Occupation as
described in the application materials based on the following findings and subject to the
following conditions:

Findings

   1. The proposed use meets the intent of the home occupation section of the Sandy City Land
      Development Code.
   2. The applicant is aware of and is willing to comply with all regulations applicable to this
      type of home occupation.
McKinley Childcare – CUP-11-19-5946                                                                             Page 4


Conditions

      1. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes applicable
         to a child daycare.

      2. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City
         Development Code (including the removal of the turkeys), and all conditions of approval
         imposed by the Planning Commission.

      3. That the applicant or an aid assist at curbside with the directing of traffic and the
         loading/unloading of clients. Patrons shall not block driveway entrances to neighboring
         properties.

      4. That this Conditional Use Permit be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.
