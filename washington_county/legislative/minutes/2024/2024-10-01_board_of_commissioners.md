CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2024-1274-O, AN ORDINANCE EXPANDING
THE LIST OF SPECIES THAT MAY BE TAKEN WHILE SPOTLIGHTING

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that the Utah Division of Wildlife Resources
requested that the county’s ordinance regarding hunting and spotlighting be modified to allow for
the spotlighting and hunting of feral pigs, as they are an invasive species. This ordinance would
update the allowed species to be taken while spotlighting to be limited to coyotes, red foxes, striped
skunks, feral pigs, raccoons, and jackrabbits.

Kevin Bunnel with the Utah Division of Wildlife Resources stated the reasoning behind this
ordinance would be to eradicate any feral pigs that get into Utah and ensure there will not be an
established population within the county.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2024-1274-O, an
Ordinance Expanding the List of Species That May be Taken While
Spotlighting. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3361, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN ADJUSTMENT OF 2024 PROPERTY TAXES FOR CERTAIN PARCELS OWNED
BY RURAL HOUSING DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION DBA SELF-HELP HOMES

Washington County Commission

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated that state law allows County Commissioners to adjust
or defer property taxes if the Commission determines that the reduction is in the best human
interest and the interests of the state and the county. This request would cover approximately 112
lots.

Brad Bishop, Executive Director of Self-Help Homes explained that nine of the lots in the
Hurricane Heights subdivision were sold last week. He stated there are about 40 people that are
waiting on federal funding to close on the homes. One project will be in progress within the next
six to nine months, with the third project in La Verkin continuing to be a work in progress.

Commissioner Iverson encouraged the La Verkin property be rezoned to agricultural until the
property is ready to develop. He stated that he acknowledges the mission, but wants to ensure the
development of the properties as soon as possible. Commissioner Snow commended the mission
of Self-Help Homes, but questioned how long the tax burden should be shifted to the citizens of
Washington County. Mr. Bishop stated that Hurricane Heights and the other property in Hurricane
will be under construction within the year, with the La Verkin properties hopefully being finished
within the next two years. Commissioner Snow asked how long these properties have been owned,
with Mr. Bishop answering between 2019 and 2021.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3361, A
Resolution Approving an Adjustment of 2024 Property Taxes for Certain
Parcels Owned by Rural Housing Development Corporation DBA Self-Help
Homes with a Verbal Agreement that the La Verkin Property Be Moved into
Green Belt. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.
