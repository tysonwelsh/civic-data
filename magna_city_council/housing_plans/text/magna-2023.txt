New Neighborhood Mixed-use Zone: Adopted June 16th, 2023
The Magna Metro Township Council introduced a new Neighborhood Mixed-

Additionally, the Council has adopted revisions to the Medium and High-Density
Residential Zones in the municipality. These updates are designed to facilitate
the creation of 'Missing Middle Housing' styles in strategic areas. Updates
incentivize developers to construct multi-family residences with much-needed
smaller unit sizes by linking parking requirements to the number of bedrooms
per unit, rather than mandating a fixed two stalls per unit irrespective of unit
size.

To foster applications for changes in the Medium and High-Density Residential

New Neighborhood Mixed-use Zone: Adopted June 16th, 2023
Adopting the Neighborhood Mixed-Use Zone was motivated by a desire to
