 A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2019-
      2020 BUDGET
      Chairman Christensen informed a public hearing had been advertised for the
      Regular Redevelopment Agency Meeting scheduled April 7, 2020, in order for
      the Agency to hear and consider public comments regarding re-opening the FY
      2019-2020 Budget.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:

             State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
             allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The
             West Valley City Redevelopment Agency holds, as needed on a quarterly
             basis, public hearings to amend the annual budget.

             A Public Notice was posted March 12, 2020 in (the Salt Lake Tribune and
             the Deseret Morning News) general circulation to the general public.
             Notice was given that a public hearing is to be held October 1, 2019 at
             6:30 p.m., West Valley City Hall, 3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley
             City, Utah.

      Chairman Christensen opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in
      favor or opposition, Chairman Christensen closed the Public Hearing.

      ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 20-04, AMEND THE BUDGET OF
      WEST VALLEY CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY FOR THE FISCAL
      YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2019 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2020 TO
      REFLECT CHANGES IN THE BUDGET FROM INCREASED REVENUES
      AND AUTHORIZE THE DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
      The Redevelopment Agency previously held a public hearing regarding proposed
      Resolution 20-04 that would amend the budget of West Valley City Redevelopment
      Agency for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2019 and ending June 30, 2020 to
      reflect changes in the budget from increased revenues and authorize the
      disbursement of funds.

      Upon inquiry by Chairman Christensen there were no further questions from
      members of the Agency, and he called for a motion.

      Mr. Buhler moved to approve Resolution 20-04.

      Ms. Lang seconded the motion.

 RESOLUTION 20-05: AUTHORIZE THE REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF
 WEST VALLEY CITY TO REIMBURSE MTP-WVC INDUSTRIAL PARK FOR
 INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS IN THE SOUTHWEST ECONOMIC
 DEVELOPMENT AREA, TO PURCHASE PROPERTY LOCATED AT 2574
 SOUTH REDWOOD ROAD
 Mark Nord, RDA, presented proposed Resolution 20-05 that would authorize the
 Redevelopment Agency of West Valley City to reimburse MTP-WVC Industrial Park for
 infrastructure improvements in the Southwest Economic Development Area.

 Written documentation previously provided to the Redevelopment Agency included
 information as follows:

       MTP acquired 20 acres of property located at 7200 west and 5400 south to develop
       a flex industrial park to meet the high demand for fulfillment space. The property
       is located just outside the borders of the Southwest Economic Development area.
       In order to develop the site MTP needed to pull utilities from Mountain View
       Corridor. MTP has spent approximately $363,200 to pull utilities to their site. By
       pulling the utilities along the south side of 5400 South, they greatly enhance the
       ability if and when the gravel pits across from decide to develop.

       The Redevelopment Agency worked with MTP to select the site for development.
       Because of the expenses associated with developing this raw site, the West Valley
       City RDA proposed a reimbursement for some of the utility work seeing that it
       would benefit the EDA for future development.

 Upon inquiry by Chairman Christensen there were no further questions from members of
 the Agency, and he called for a motion.

       Mr. Fitisemanu moved to approve Resolution 20-05.
