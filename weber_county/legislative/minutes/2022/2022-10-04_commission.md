D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: Tonight at our main Weber County library we have the ‘The
Book That Changed Me’ event. I thought about the book that changed me and really it was a book first printed in 1970
Jonathan Livingston’s Seagull. Seagulls here are protected as our state bird. I associated seagulls with being garbage
birds after observing them at the local landfill and at the ocean. In the book, a seagull named Jonathan wanted to be
different. He observed a hawk flying high then diving down to hunt. So he thought he would try it but was unable to
control his descent and hit the ocean. He woke up at night and decided to go back to his flock. While flying at night,
he realized he was doing something unlike other seagulls. He eventually learns how to control himself in a dive, step-
by-step. Sometimes you’re going to try things in life and fail. A lot of times through failures, through being challenged,
we can find another pathway to a better state and improve ourselves.

     2. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH ROCKY MOUNTAIN                                                                                            MECHANICAL                        FOR          HVAC
        IMPROVEMENTS AT THE OGDEN VALLEY LIBRARY.
            Lynnda Wangsgard (Library Director): Existing units installed in 1995 and exceeded normal 15 year service
            life. Lennox discontinued circuit boards for these units and the R22 refrigerant is no longer being produced
            with a 400% cost increase since 2020. Three options have been explored. The first option stockpiles
         existing parts. It was learned that 70% of the units major components are no longer manufactured. One
         particular unit cannot be repaired and must be replaced in short order. Rigging and manpower costs to
         remove canopy support posts, disassemble/reassemble the screen will lead to $14,000 increase. Second
         option is to replace units as they fail. Recurring expenses of renting a crane, opening penthouses, ensuring
         each time the roofing membrane is not damaged, engaging in the bidding process with contractors every time
         a unit fails, escalating unit costs, and lack of heating/cooling in a portion of the library while unit is replaced
         all had to be considered; additionally, replacement units have up to a 41 week lead time. Option three is to
         replace all units now. Proposed new units have efficiency gains leading to possible $13,000 per year
         electricity savings each or $325,000 over the 25 year life of the new units. Total cost to replace all 27 units
         would be $214,100.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a contract with Rocky Mountain Mechanical for HVAC
         improvements at the Ogden Valley Library; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – N/A; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
