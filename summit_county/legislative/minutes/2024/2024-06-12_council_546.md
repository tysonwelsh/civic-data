Discussion regarding Lake Rockport Estates' request to have water supplied by Mountain Regional Special Service District; Andy Garland (3:09 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: MRW Staff Report re Lake Rockport Estates.pdf
Mountain Regional Water Special Service District General Manager Andy Garland presented a request to extend Mountain Regional’s service to Lake Rockport Estates. Council Members asked questions. General Manager Garland and Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas responded.
Seven Lake Rockport Estates community members attended the meeting and expressed their support for extending Mountain Regional’s service.

Project Manager Kendell, Manager Shayne Scott, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas, and Public Works Director John Angell responded.
