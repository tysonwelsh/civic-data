                                       Meeting Minutes
                                     Work Session 3-10-20
                                           6:00 p.m.
                         Nephi City Municipal Golf Course (Canyon Hills)



        1. City Council members took a brief tour of the clubhouse and eastern portion of the golf
           course.

        2.   Budget priorities/outcomes from the various city departments were presented to the City
             Council. Council members discussed which priorities/outcomes should be prioritized. City
             Council members scored the priorities and a list was generated.

        3. A brief executive session was held at 9:30 pm to discuss the character and competence of an
           individual.

        4. Meeting adjourned at 10:15

Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Nathan Memmott, Skip
Worwood, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, and Donald Ball.
