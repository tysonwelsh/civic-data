                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-08

   A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING SUPPORT TO GOVERNOR HERBERT FOR
 ENCOURAGING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES TO ALLOW MORE
          INTERNATIONAL REFUGEES TO RESETTLE IN UTAH

       WHEREAS, on September 26, 2019, the President of the United States issued Executive
Order No. 13888 relating to the policy of the United States in its acceptance and resettlement of
refugees; and

      WHEREAS, Executive Order No. 13888 states, “In resettling refugees into American
communities, it is the policy of the United States to cooperate and consult with state and local
governments, to take into account the preferences of State governments, and to provide a
pathway for refugees to become self-sufficient;” and

       WHEREAS, Executive Order No. 13888 required the Secretary of State to obtain
statements from state or local governments, to verify whether said state or local governments
would consent to the resettlement of refugees within the territory of said state or local
government; and

       WHEREAS, the world is currently facing a record number of people fleeing violence
and persecution creating the largest refugee crisis in human history; and

       WHEREAS, resettlement is the last resort for refugees who cannot return to their home
country and cannot rebuild their lives from where they fled; and

       WHEREAS, on the date of October 24, 2019, Governor Herbert issued a letter to the
President of the United States, encouraging the President to allow the state of Utah to accept
more international refuges; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Governor Herbert’s
position, expressed in his letter to the President of the United States on October 24, 2019, should
be supported by Riverton City.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council commends Governor Herbert for his leadership on the
          matter of opting to receive international refugees in the state of Utah.

       2. The Riverton City Council is open and welcoming to refugees, and also understands
          that refugees in our community have and will continue to make significant, positive,
          and lasting contributions.

       3. The Riverton City Council encourages state immigration officials under the direction
          of Governor Herbert to work with Riverton City staff to explore ways in which the
          City can support the position taken by Governor Herbert and expressed in his October
          24, 2019 letter to the President of the United States.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 21st day of
January 2020 by the following vote:
