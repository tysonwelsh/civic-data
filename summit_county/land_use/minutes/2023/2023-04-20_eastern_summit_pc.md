b.
Public Hearing and Action regarding the Amended Conditional Use Permit for the Grit Guest Ranch located on parcel SS-69-B-15, 8835 N. Park East Rd., Browns Canyons; Applicant: Kari Juip; Senior Planner: Tiffanie N. Robinson. Administrative Process. Project #23-010
(6:02 P.M.)
Tiffanie N. Robinson, Senior Planner, presented for public hearing and action regarding the amended CUP for the Grit Guest Ranch located in Browns Canyon.
Kari Juip, Applicant, and Terry Woodruff, Applicant, provided additional comments.
Commissioners asked questions. Ms. Juip, Mr. Woodruff, Ms. Robinson responded.
Commission Chair Wilde opened the meeting for public hearing.
(6:16 P.M.)
Richard Tanga commented.
Kelly McMullen commented.
Commission Chair Wilde closed the meeting for public hearing.
(6:20 P.M.)
Commissioners asked additional questions. Ms. Juip and Ms. Robinson responded.
Marion Wheaton made a motion to approve the amended Conditional Use Permit according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval outlined in staff report for the Grit Guest Ranch. Rich Sonntag seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).  Abstain: Alex Peterson, Don Sargent.
