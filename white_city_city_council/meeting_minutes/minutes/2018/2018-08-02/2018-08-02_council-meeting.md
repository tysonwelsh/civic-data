Discussion/Clarification of Agenda Items

              The Council reviewed the following agenda and future agenda items:

      Moderate Income Housing Plan
      Development of a long range Strategic Plan for White City Metro Township
      FY 2019 White City budget update
      Possible repeal of the Stormwater Impact Fee
      Sidewalk maintenance and replacement policy

              Council Member Perry, seconded by Council Dickerson, moved to close the staff
meeting. The motion passed unanimously.

White City Community Council Report

                Council Member Dickerson stated the Community Council discussed stolen
garbage cans and windows that were broken at an elementary school. The lower part of Carnation
Drive is going to be surveyed for sidewalks.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

               Mark West, Unified Fire Authority (UFA), reviewed reports from July 4. The UFA
is holding open recruitment for entry level firefighters in October, and the academy will start in
February 2019. The safety message for August is Back to School. Drivers and children need to
be aware of each other when crossing the street. Groups of firefighters are gearing up and are on
a 72-hour notice to respond to wildfires.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Officer Paula Stinson stated the Citizen Academy is starting on September 5 and
will be held every Wednesday until October 24.

Staff or Committee Reports

Interim Legislative Update

                Paul Ashton stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)
is accepting and moving forward with some of the metro townships recommendations. The
GSLMSD will begin the process of moving Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services
Division to the GSLMSD.

               Greg Schulz stated the Legislature will be looking at a potential bill to clean up the
incorporation process. A special legislative session was held to clean-up the Inland Port bill.

New Council Business

Public Hearing

              Paul Ashton stated Jake Hone, Developer, has submitted another application to
rezone property at 1160 East and 1186 East 10600 South. The White City Metro Township
Planning Commission has recommended denial.

               Greg Schulz stated the public hearing will be set for September 6. Approval of the
public hearing can be set subject to choosing a location that can accommodate a large group.

               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to set the
public hearing for application #30588 – Jake Hone, for September 6, 2018, at a location to be
decided upon at a later date. The motion passed unanimously.
