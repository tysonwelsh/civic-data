2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Olympic Park Neighborhood Trees Discussion.
Jessica Smith, City Arborist, and BJ Benson, Streets Manager, stated that there are concerns with
tree roots lifting sidewalks in the Olympic Park neighborhood. It’s creating a liability, so they
presented some options to the Council. Mr. Benson stated that many of the residents they spoke
with want the trees to be removed. The Council reached consensus to move forward with the 2nd
option which would be to remove all the trees and offer a tree reimbursement certificate to
residents. They would also begin making repairs to the concrete.

2.2 Lehi City Bike and Pedestrian Plan Mapping Facility
Kim Struthers and Mike West gave an update on the plan. They asked the Council to participate
in an exercise to help identify some needed connections and offer suggestions.

2.3 Update and Discussion of the Thanksgiving Point Transit Oriented Development Project.
Jonathan Bankhead gave an update on the project. He reported that they are working through the
process of an amendment to the development code. He said that they plan to submit smaller
sections of the Area Plan so they can work through the approvals on a smaller scale.
