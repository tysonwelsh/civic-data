The following are the minutes of the Planning Commission Meeting of the Herriman Planning Commission.
The meeting was held on Thursday, December 2, 2021 at 6:00 p.m. in the Fort Herriman Conference Room
and the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice
of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members
of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair Chris Berbert

Commissioners Present In Person: Brody Rypien, Heather Garcia, Andrea Bradford, Adam Jacobson, Andy
Powell, Forest Sickles and Darryl Fenn

Commissioners Excused: Jackson Ferguson

Staff Present: City Planner Michael Maloy, HPD Deputy Chief Cody Stromberg, Deputy City Recorder
Wendy Thorpe, Communications Specialist Mitch Davis, City Attorney Chase Andrizzi, Planner II Sheldon
Howa, Planner I Talia Wright, Staff Engineer III Josh Petersen, and Planning Manager Clint Spencer.



Chair Berbert called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.


City Planner Maloy explained no planning related matters were approved at the latest City Council meeting.


City Planner Maloy relayed a resident requested additional information, but no public hearing comments had
been received for item 3.1. The property had already been subdivided through Salt Lake County, with a metes
and bounds description, which was being formally recognized by the City.
Planner I Wright continued the potential daycare ordinance discussion, started previously by Planning Manager
Spencer. At a previous meeting, Commissioners requested limiting daycare sessions to two per day, with at
least thirty minutes between, and expanding hours of operation from 6:00 am to 7:00 pm for daycares only.
Commissioners debated allowing more than two sessions per day, the number of children per each, and traffic
impacts. They agreed to allow only two per day. Home occupation categories and parking requirements were
reviewed. Commissioners debated parking requirements, drop off and pickup plans. They stated some areas
were not conducive to home businesses and encouraged parking solutions on property. On street parking of
employees could create neighborhood conflicts. Planning Manager Spencer stated Herriman required four
parking stalls for each single-family home, two in the garage and two on property, which was more than most
communities. HPD Deputy Chief Stromberg cautioned against anything that would cause conflict with
snowplow plan, such as on street parking. Commissioners agreed employee parking should be onsite and not
on the street.


City Planner Maloy briefly reviewed annual training requirements for Commissioners. He emailed them online
land use training opportunities and requested they report their progress to him.

The work meeting adjourned by consensus at 7:02 p.m.


Chair Chris Berbert called the meeting to order at 7:04 p.m.


Paxton Guymon led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance.


Full Quorum Present.


No conflicts were offered.



Commissioner Garcia MOVED to approved Item 2.4 Approval of Minutes for the August 5, 2021 and September 2, 2021,
Planning Commission Meetings, Commissioner Jacobson SECONDED and all voted aye.
Chair Berbert reviewed the public hearing guidelines.

Planning Manager Spencer reviewed the amendment to further subdivide lot two of the Herriman Boulevard
Parkway Commercial subdivision and formalize the division of property. The amended plat included additional
cross access easements for future commercial development which would allow for internal circulation between
the different sites. The landscaping plan was to be reviewed with the site plan approval.

Applicant Paxton Guymon had nothing to add, he offered to answer questions. They were not under contract
to sell the smaller lot and were researching possible uses.

Chair Berbert opened the public hearing.

No comments were offered.

Chair Berbert closed the public hearing.

Staff Recommendation:
    1. Receive and agree to the recommendations from other City departments.
    2. Setbacks from residential properties to be as follows:
           a. 15’ from side yard (along south or west property line)
           b. 20’ from rear yard (along south or west property line)
    3. Receive landscaping approval from the City as park of the site plan process.
    4. Final plat to be reviewed and approved by the Engineering department.


Commissioner Powell MOVED to approve item 3.1 file number S2021-122 Request to Approve the Plat Amendment of
Herriman Boulevard Parkway Commercial at 12484 S Rosecrest Road in the C-2 Zone with staff recommendations,
Commissioner Sickles SECONDED the motion.

Commissioner Adam Jacobson           Aye
Commissioner Heather Garcia          Aye
Commissioner Andrea Bradford         Aye
Commissioner Brody Rypien            Aye
Commissioner Forest Sickles          Aye
Commissioner Andy Powell             Aye

The motion passed unanimously.


No comments were offered.
