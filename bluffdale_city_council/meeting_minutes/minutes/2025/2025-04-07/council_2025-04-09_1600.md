Bluffdale receives 10% of total property taxes so any City tax increases would only be to
Bluffdale's share oftaxes, not the entire assessed value. Other cities will have many ofthe same
taxing entities. What will change in comparison to Bluffdale is their specific tax rate and whether
they have a public safety tax.

The Certified Tax Rate("CTR")ensures that local governments do not obtain windfall revenues
as a result of revaluation or equalization projects. Taxing entities receive the same tax revenue
each year, so as home values increase, rates decrease. Bluffdale's historical CTR has declined
over the last several years, with public safety increases in 2017 and 2019. Ms. Thayer noted that
there have only been four tax increases in the City's history. Mr. Kartchner clarified that the CTR
is calculated by the State and noted that in 2008 and 2009 when property values declined,the CTR
increased even though the City did not increase taxes at that time. The market turned around in
2012 and values began increasing, which decreased the CTR. Because Bluffdale spans two
counties, CTR is normally received late in the fiscal year and should be provided in June 2025.
An estimated rate was included in the Tentative Budget.

Ms. Thayer compared Bluffdale taxes to those of a neighboring city and reported that the owner
of a home valued at $700,000 would pay $355 per year in City taxes. In a neighboring city, the
owner ofa home with the same value would pay $884 per year. A comparison chart oflocal city
tax rates was reviewed, and Ms. Thayer noted that the Public Safety Fee was included in
Bluffdale's tax rate to be more comparable to cities that have separate taxing entities for fire and
police. Eight nearby cities went through the Truth in Taxation process to increase property taxes
in 2024.

are higher than revenues, they meet with department heads and prioritize their needs. In 2024,the
City Council approved a Budget Review Committee, which provides materieds to aid the general
public in understanding the Budget. Public hearings are then held, after which the City Council
