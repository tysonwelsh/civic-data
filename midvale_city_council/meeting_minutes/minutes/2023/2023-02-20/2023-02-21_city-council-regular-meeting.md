B.   AUTHORIZATION FOR MAYOR STEVENSON TO SIGN A LETTER OF
     SUPPORT FOR THE 2023 RAISE GRANT APPLICATION
Wendelin Knobloch said Midvale City was invited by Wasatch Front Regional Council
(WFRC), the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT), and Utah Transit Authority
(UTA) to participate in the 2023 RAISE Grant Application for the FirsVLast Mile
Connections: lmproving Communities' Quality of Life through Access to Opportunities and
Healthy Transportation Connections Project.

This grant application for federal funds represents ten Utah cities, three counties, UDOT,
UTA, WFRC, and MAG and especially supports cities like Midvale with the time intensive
and costly application process. This is a rare opportunity, the last submittal of a pooled
application of similar magnitude occurred lorlhe 2O14 Tiger Grant, almost nine years ago.

The purpose of the grant application is to facilitate active transportation projects that will
enhance the safety, comfort and convenience for people who choose a walking or
bicycling trip, especially when utilizing transit to reach the destination, as detailed in the
attached letter.

The projects have been thoroughly vetted through the regional planning process
administered by WFRC and were chosen because they fit within the program criteria.
Please refer to attached detailed information for each project:

- Center Street Protected Bike Lane from Jordan River Parkway to Sandra Way
- Fort Union Trax Station to Maple Street Multi-Use Path
- 700 WMain StreeUHolden Street Bike Lane extending from the northern city boundary
  to Center Street
- Cottonwood Street Buffered Bike Lane.

The grant amount of $6,050,849 will cover 80% of the overall project costs, which leaves
a 201:o local match of $1,210,170 that will be due on 913012026. The local match can be
minimized by applying for other grants over the next three years that are accessible
through UDOT, the Utah Office of Outdoor Recreation and Salt Lake County. The
Planning Department has reached out to these agencies and received positive feedback.

Council Member Paul Glover asked when the funds would be available if the city does
get the grant?

C.     CONSIDER APPROVAL OF FINAL SUBDIVISION PLAT FOR A TWO-LOT
       SPLIT LOCATED AT 653 W THIRD AVE IN THE SINGLE FAMILY
       RESTDENTTAL 2 (SF-2) ZONE
Elizabeth Arnold stated Kyle Zack and Bryan Anderson request Final Subdivision
approval for a two-lot split located at 653 W Third Ave in the Single Family Residential-2
(SF-2) zone. This proposal has been reviewed by Planning Staff, the City Engineer, and
the Unified Fire Authority for compliance with the respective guidelines, policies,
standards, and codes. Staff finds the proposal complies with requirements outlined in
Midvale City Municipal Code for minor subdivisions (16.04.050) and the lot standards of
the SF-2 zone (17-7-2.3).

Public notice has been sent to property owners within 500 feet of the subject parcel. No
objections have been received as of the writing of this report.

The Planning Commission reviewed the request at its January 25,2023 meeting and
recommended approval of the final plat to the City Council.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION:
Based on compliance with the requirements of Chapter 16.04.050 and 17-7-2.3 of the
Midvale City Municipal Code demonstrated in the application or addressed by the
inclusion of conditions of approval, Staff recommended approval with the following
findings:
