                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 25-62

                 A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE REMOVAL OF THE
                        RIVERTON CITY SKATE FACILITY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council is empowered to manage the real property of
Riverton City, including “improve, protect, and do any other thing in relation to this property that
and individual could do;” pursuant to Utah Code § 10-8-2; and

      WHEREAS, Riverton City owns and operates the Riverton Skate Facility, located at
1435 West 12600 South; and

        WHEREAS, the Skate Facility has provided continual service to Riverton City residents
since its construction in the year 2003; and

       WHEREAS, on the date of December 2, 2025, the Riverton City Council found and
determined that after many years of service to the public, the Skate Facility had reached a
condition related to ordinary wear and tear which required that it be closed, pending further
discussion of what future plans should be since it has reached the end of its useful life; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City staff has explored options to repair areas of the Riverton
Skate Facility which have experienced age-related wear, and recommends that the original Skate
Facility should be removed in its entirety because the Skate Facility’s current facilities cannot be
salvaged or preserved in a cost-effective manner; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council therefore finds and determines that before the
City reaches a final decision on whether to move forward with future designs for a new skate
facility, the City should remove the existing Skate Facility to save costs incurred to secure the
Skate Facility from public entry; and

         WHEREAS, the Section 3.10 of the Riverton City Council Rules of Order and procedure
provide for the creation of subcommittees, comprised of up to three Council Members, to
“facilitate discussion, draft proposed policies and make recommendations to the full Council for
its consideration;” and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines the City Council should
create a subcommittee to evaluate the level of service it should continue to provide for skate
boarding, use of scooters, and the like, and to return to the Council with a recommendation as to
what the City’s level of service should be for skating-related activities in City parks, and what
facilities should be installed in support of the recommended level of service;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby declares the existing facilities which constitute the
          Riverton Skate Facility at 1435 West 12600 South should be removed in their
          entirety. The Riverton City Manager is authorized and directed to take whatever
           steps are necessary to remove said skate facilities in a manner compliant with City
           purchasing and construction policies and ordinances.

        2. The Riverton City Council hereby establishes a subcommittee, pursuant to Section
           3.10 of the Riverton City Council Rules of Order and Procedure, which shall consist
           of three members of the Council. Subcommittee members shall be appointed by
           nomination of the City Council during a regularly scheduled City Council Meeting, to
           occur no later than February 3, 2026. The Subcommittee shall consider the question
           of what level of service Riverton City should provide to skating-related activities in
           City Parks, and what skate facilities should be constructed in City Parks to meet this
           level of service. Said recommendations should include cost estimates and
           recommendations as to the appropriate location(s) for skating-related park facilities.

   3.      The Riverton City Council hereby directs the City Manager to provide support to the
           Council Subcommittee by supplying, without limitation, documents such as
           supportive studies, and cost estimates, and any other supportive information required
           for the subcommittee to consider in formulating recommendations described under
           this Resolution.

        4. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.

     PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 16th day of
December, 2025 by the following vote:
