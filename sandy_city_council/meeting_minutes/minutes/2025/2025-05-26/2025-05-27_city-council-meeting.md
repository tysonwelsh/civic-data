Prayer, Pledge of Allegiance, and Introductions

                       Council Chair Aaron Dekeyzer welcomed those in attendance.

                       Council Member Marci Houseman offered the Prayer.
                       Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer led the Pledge.

                       Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda.


General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                       Council Chair Aaron Dekeyzer invited the public to participate in General Citizen
                       Comment.

                       Public comment opened.

                       Ms. Kris Nicholl expressed a need for the Environmental Impact Study on Highland Drive
                       to be conducted. Traffic is getting worse and that study needs to be done. She suggested
                       redirecting funds for the city newsletter to cover the cost of the EIS study. She also
                       expressed concerns that the Mayor may be using taxpayer funds inappropriately for her
                       re-election bid and gave examples such as the Mayor's picture displayed on the
                       amphitheater marquee and giving gifts such as pins or stress balls out to the community
                       customized with red hats.

                       Mr. Steve McKinion mentioned that he saw two contractors dumping their garbage on a
                       Sandy street for bulk waste pick up. He wanted to notify the city that this was going on.

                       Public comment closed.

                       Council moved to Item 5 on the Agenda and convened a closed session.

1.         25-149                 Metropolitan Water District of Salt Lake and Sandy presenting their FY

                                  MWDSLS FY26 Tentative Budget

                                  Annalee Munsey, Metropolitan Water District of Salt Lake and Sandy Director, presented
                                  the Fiscal Year 2025-26 budget to the City Council. She introduced board members and
                                  staff. John Kirkham and Florence Reynolds are appointed to the MWDSLS Trustees by
                                  the Council. The MWDSLS was created 90 years ago. Sandy joined the district in 1990.
                                  Ms. Munsey provided an overview of the MWDSLS, the FY 2026 tentative budget, and the
                                  sources and uses of funds. She reviewed key infrastructure, metro water supply,
                                  proposed water rate increase, certified tax rate, bond issuance, FY 26 capital
                                  improvement projects, and agency fees. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Following the presentation, Council moved to the Consent Calendar.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robsinson mentioned that there will be a vacancy on the Sandy
                    Ambassador committee and asked the Council to think about someone to appoint to fill
                    the vacancy.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham announced that he will not be seeking re-election. He
                    was grateful for the experience and his service to the City.

                    Council Member Marci Houseman attended the Sandy Senior Center Memorial Day
                    barbecue and she thanked Council Member Sharkey for volunteering to serve at the
                    luncheon. She also attended the Memorial Day event at Larkin Cemetery and enjoyed the
                    service and is grateful to all those who have served our country.

                    Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer read a statement addressing and apologizing for his
                    demeanor while chairing the April 29th City Council meeting.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update from the CDBG Committee. The
                    committee received the HUD grant, which increased from the prior year's allocation. This
                    will have an even greater impact to the programs supported by the CDBG Committee.

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa provided an update from the Sandy Senior Center
                    Board. They had concerns about the glass recycling dumpster returning to the parking lot
                    by the center. Our Public Works director is aware of their concerns. The Senior Center
                    director, Charles Otis is retiring and we thank him for his long-time service to the senior
                    center.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski mentioned that Sandy City was just rated as one of the top cities to live
                    in, ranking at 109th. She attended the Beehive Academy graduation and spoke at the
                    event. She congratulated our Sandy Explorer program which began fifty years ago. She
                    thanked all the Veterans and their families for their important service to our nation.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, mentioned that the bulk waste clean up is going well and should be
                    finished next week.

                    Following the CAO report, Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

Adjournment
                    Council convened a closed session at 6:04 pm and adjourned the Council meeting
                    immediately following the closed session.
