3.         Ordinance 2022-04 amending Provo City School Board Districts. Citywide Application. (22-003)
           (40:20)

      Motion:      An implied motion to approve Ordinance 2022-04, as currently constituted, has been made by
                   council rule.

Cliff Strachan gave summary of criteria used to select final maps.

Chair Hoban opened public comment.

David Wright of Provo shared thoughts about population and voters in districts. He stated he prefers Map W
because it allows balance.

Terry McCabe of Provo and member of School Board shared that she disagreed with other school board members
and believes the westside needs its own district.

Eric Chase of Provo shared his disappointment in discussions council had about boundaries. He expressed his
concern for members of council being biased towards their own neighborhoods. He suggested council investigate
the statistics of low-income schools and residents and how they relate to the district maps.

Chair Hoban closed public comment.

Councilor Ellsworth shared the difference in Maps E and W. She also shared information about District 4’s map and
where candidates would be from. Her recommendation is that Map E be selected to allow for more potential
candidates.

      Motion:      Councilor Ellsworth made a motion that Map E be selected. Seconded by Councilor Handley.

Councilor Handley responded to Eric Chase’s comments. He communicated his desire to align with the school
board’s recommendations.

Councilor Whipple shared her dissatisfaction with all the maps presented and said she feels conflicted.

Councilor Ellsworth asked Councilor Whipple about how the title one schools would be impacted.

Councilor Whipple responded that the representation of schools is not balanced.

Councilor Handley disclosed that he does not think splitting the boundaries would result in less representation of
vulnerable schools.

Councilor MacKay shared her experience with her children living outside of boundary of district.

5.         Ordinance 2022-06 amending the zone map class. of approx. 1.39 acres of real property, from 46 W
           to 90 W on 800 N, from Residential Conservation (RC) to Campus Mixed Use (CMU) and authorizing
           a related development agreement. N. Park Nbrhd. (PLRZ20210112) (1:34:55)

      Motion:      An implied motion to approve Ordinance 2022-06, as currently constituted, has been made by
                   council rule.

Aaron Ardmore, Planner, presented the changes made from previous version. He discussed the development
agreement that is part of the Ordinance and summarized what is included in the agreement.

Councilor Ellsworth asked why they selected 10 years.

Aaron Ardmore responded.

Councilor Ellsworth asked what happens if the agreement had not been meet after 10 years.

Brian Jones added context of why 10 years was selected and what the development agreement would uphold.

Councilor Fillmore shared his support for this project and Ordinance.

Chair Hoban asked clarifying questions in which Mr. Jones replied.

Chair Hoban opened public comment.

Eric Chase of Provo expressed his support for this ordinance.

Chair Hoban closed public comment and invited Tim Metler to provide feedback.

Tim Metler of Metler Real Estate and the developer of the property shared his comments and answered questions.

Chair Hoban discussed his concern for parking and explained the study he conducted.

Councilor Handley shared his concerns regarding Chair Hoban’s study and the parking situation. He also spoke of
his support for this Ordinance.

Councilor Ellsworth said that parking will always be an issue around the university. She stated that she does not
believe this area meets the CMU standards and should be zoned as something else.

Councilor MacKay said that even if the students commit to using public transit, most of them have cars and those
cars will need to be parked somewhere.

Councilor Whipple shared her support for the development agreement along with the Ordinance.

Councilor Ellsworth asked about the current zoning of the property.

Chair Hoban asked why CMU was selected instead of another zone.

Mr. Ardmore shared the reasoning behind selecting CMU for both phases.

Chair Hoban asked why HRD is limited to a specific number of units.

Bill Peperone, Director of Development Services, responded to the density limit on certain zones.

Chair Hoban asked Councilor Ellsworth if she felt like the project was appropriate for that area based on the
response from Mr. Peperone.

Councilor Ellsworth provided an example and shared why she is still having reservations.

Councilor Whipple explained why Councilor Ellsworth’s example differs from the project at hand. She also shared
her support for phase one of the project, even if phase two is not completed.

Mr. Metler clarified the intended residents would be married couples based on the room sizes. He affirmed the
proposed zone is consistent with the area and appropriate for the project.

Councilor Fillmore recommended council take a vote.

      Motion:      Chair Hoban made substitute motion that the development agreement includes that there be
                   equivalent parking permits and parking stalls.

Mr. Jones proposed council vote on the implied motion since the development agreement had not been drafted
but suggested it could include amending the development agreement prior to execution to require that the
number of parking permits be equal to the number of stalls.

Chair Hoban agreed and said that was his intention. Councilor Fillmore seconded the motion.

Councilor Ellsworth asked for clarification on the motion.

Mr. Jones responded.

Chair Hoban called for a vote.

      Vote:     The motion was approved 6:1 with Councilors Fillmore, Handley, Hoban, MacKay, Shipley, and
                Whipple in favor. Councilor Ellsworth opposed.
