                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RDA RESOLUTION NO. 24-04

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A PARTICIPATION
AGREEMENT WITH OSMOND ENTERPRISES LLC FOR THE PARTICIPATION IN
           DEVELOPMENT OF THE PAIGE RETAIL PLAZA

       WHEREAS, Riverton City previously approved Ordinance No. 24-12 authorizing the
approval of a Development Agreement and to regulate development Paige Retail Plaza (the
“Project”) to be located at the southeast corner of 12830 South and Redwood Road; and

        WHEREAS, the Redevelopment Agency of Riverton City (the “RDA”) approved RDA
Resolution No. 22-04 which approved the Redwood Road Community Reinvestment Project Area
Plan (the “Project Area Plan”), in accordance with the Utah Community Reinvestment Agency Act
to support development of the Project with the use of tax increment and sales tax revenues; and

       WHEREAS, the RDA has recently acquired the following parcels of real property for the
purpose of participating in the Project: Parcel Nos. 27-34-251-027-0000, 27-34-251-036-0000, 27-
34-251-036-0000, and 27-34-251-035-0000 (the “RDA Parcels”); and

       WHEREAS, the RDA receives said tax increment directly from the taxing entities to help
reimburse for costs to install infrastructure which would be used by the public to access the site, such
as roads, sidewalks, and parking facilities; and

       WHEREAS, Osmond Enterprises, LLC. and affiliates (“Osmond”) are developers of
property within the Project, and have obtained development rights to real property adjacent to the
RDA Parcels; and

       WHEREAS, under the terms of the Project, Osmond shall install public infrastructure,
and continue to maintain the same in perpetuity, which creates the need for participation by the
RDA through donation of the RDA Parcels under the terms of a Participation Agreement; and

       WHEREAS, said Participation Agreement will benefit the citizens of Riverton City by
enabling development in accordance with the Redwood Road Community Reinvestment Project
Area Plan.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Participation Agreement between the RDA and Osmond Enterprises LLC is hereby
          approved. The Board Chair is hereby authorized to sign the Agreement, and may make
          any alterations thereto which do not affect the material terms of the Agreement, provided
          said Agreement and any alterations thereto are approved by counsel for the
          Redevelopment Agency of Riverton City.
      2. The Participation Agreement is made effective on the date the Agreement is signed by
         the Board Chair.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of Directors of Riverton City Redevelopment
