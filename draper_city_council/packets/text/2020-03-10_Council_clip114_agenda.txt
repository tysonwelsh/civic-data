                                    CITY COUNCIL AGENDA
    Notice is hereby given that the Draper City Council will hold a Business Meeting on Tuesday,
    March 10, 2020, at 5:30 PM, in the City Council Chambers at 1020 East Pioneer Road,
    Draper, Utah.

    The Agenda will be as follows:
    5:30 PM STUDY MEETING

    1.        Report: Fire Department 2019 Response Update and Strategic Plan, Chief
              Clint Smith
a
    2.        Introduction of Volunteers in Police Service (V.I.P.S.) members, Chief John
              Eining
a
    3.        Council/Manager Reports
a

    7:00 PM BUSINESS MEETING

    1.        Call to Order

    2.        Thought/Prayer and Pledge of Allegiance

    3.        Recognition: Marty Bodell, Cooper Gardiner, and Camryn Young as Finalists
              in the 2020 Competition for National Merit Scholarships
a
    4.        Recognition: Corner Canyon High School Swim Team
              The Corner Canyon High School Swim Team recently competed and placed 3rd
              overall in the 6A State Championship at Brigham Young University.
a

    5.        Public Comments
              To be considerate of everyone attending the meeting, public comments will be
              restricted to items that are not listed on this or a future agenda and limited to three
              minutes per person. Comments which cannot be made within these limits should be
              submitted in writing to the City Recorder prior to noon the day before the meeting.
              Comments pertaining to an item on the agenda should not be given at this time but
              should be held until that item is called.
    6.         Consent Items
               a.   Approval of February 11, 2020, and February 18, 2020, City Council
                    Meeting Minutes
               b.   Approval of February 28-29, 2020, City Council Retreat Meeting
                    Minutes
               c.   Approval of Resolution #20-13, approving an Interlocal Agreement with
                    Metro Fire
               d.   Approval of Draper Fire Department Board-Up Rotation Contract

    7.         Public Hearing: Approval of Ordinance #1410 and #1411: Offices on 7th Land
               Use Map Amendment, Zoning Map Amendment, and Development Agreement
               Request (Legislative Action)
               On the request of Keaton Morton for a land use map amendment from Residential
               Medium Density to Neighborhood Commercial (CN), a zoning map amendment from
               RA2 to CN, and a development agreement on 0.99 acres of property located at 681
               East Pioneer Rd and 12388 South 700 East. Staff report by Jennifer Jastremsky.
a
    8.         Action Item: Approval of Ordinance #1422, Nielsen's Frozen Custard
               Development Agreement Request (Legislative Action)
               On the request of Steve Nielsen, representing Nielsen's Frozen Custard and Diner, for
               a Development Agreement modifying Title 9 design standards on 0.70 acres of
               property located at 1462 East Draper Parkway. Staff report by Jennifer Jastremsky.
a
    9.         Public Hearing: Approval of Ordinance #1423, Draper City General Plan CR
               Text Amendment Request (Legislative Action)
               On the request of Draper City for a Text Amendment to the General Plan to add the
               Regional Commercial (CR) land use designation into the written plan document. Staff
               report by Jennifer Jastremsky.
a

    10.        Adjournment
