.




SUMMARY:
The Cooperative Wildfire System is administered by the Utah Division of Forestry, Fire and State Lands and
establishes the framework for wildfire suppression and cost-share responsibilities between the State and
participating municipalities. Participation in CWS ensures that when a participating entity remains in
compliance with the program requirements, the State assumes responsibility for wildfire suppression costs
within that jurisdiction.
Each year, participating entities complete a Participation Commitment that outlines the prevention,
preparedness, and mitigation activities that will be completed during the upcoming cycle. The attached PC
Statement reflects the calculated commitment amount for your jurisdiction, and the action items identify the
work UFA plans to accomplish in support of that commitment during the 2026 cycle.
