FROM:        Ryan Carter, City Attorney

ITEM:        Proposed Resolution No. 20-01 – Reappointment of Council Member Tish
             Buroker to serve on the Board of Trustees for the South Salt Lake Valley
             Mosquito Abatement District

SUMMARY:

The South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District (“District”) eradicates
mosquito populations in the southern region of Salt Lake County. Riverton City lies within
the boundaries of the District. The District is governed by a Board of Trustees, whose
members are selected from the governing bodies of the cities that receive mosquito
abatement service from the District. The term in office for a Board of Trustees member
is four years. Council Member Tish Buroker has served as a board member for the past
two years to finish the term in office of a prior council member. Council Member Buroker’s
current term in office is set to expire at the end of this year. Council Member Buroker is
both willing and eligible to be reappointed.

                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 20-01

  A RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING COUNCIL MEMBER BUROKER TO SERVE ON
   THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES FOR THE SOUTH SALT LAKE VALLEY MOSQUITO
                        ABATEMENT DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, the South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District (the "District") is
governed by a Board of Trustees, whose members represent the area in which the District
provides mosquito abatement service; and

       WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees are appointed to serve in office for a fixed term of
four years; and

       WHEREAS, Councilmember Tish Buroker was appointed in January of 2018 to serve
the remaining two years of a four-year term, after another member of the Council who served on
the Board of Trustees was voted out of office during the November 2017 municipal election; and

       WHEREAS, Councilmember Buroker has faithfully served Riverton City in her capacity
as a Board Trustee during her first term in office; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that reappointment of
Councilmember Tish Buroker to serve another term in office as a Board Trustee for the District
will aid in the protection of health and safety for Riverton City residents;

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City
as follows:
        1. The Riverton City Council hereby appoints of Councilmember Tish Buroker to
           serve as Riverton City's representative on the South Salt Valley Mosquito
           Abatement District Board of Board of Trustees with a four-year term to expire
           December 31, 2024.
        2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
