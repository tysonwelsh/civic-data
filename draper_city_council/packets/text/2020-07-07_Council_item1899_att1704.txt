                                    RESOLUTION NO. 20-33

       A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL DECLARING A
       PORTION OF A STREET KNOWN AS 12100 SOUTH LOCATED IN
       FRONT OF PARCEL # 28-30-283-002, NOW KNOW AS PARCEL # 28-30-
       283-003, AS SURPLUS PROPERTY AND AUTHORIZING THE SALE OF
       THE SAME.

       WHEREAS, Draper City owns approximately 2,391.84 square feet located generally at
690 East 12100 South in Draper, Salt Lake County, Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the City has previously vacated the public right-of-way from the property
with Ordinance No. 1416; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code, Section 3-3-160 describes the procedures to be
followed to surplus city-owned property; and

       WHEREAS, the City has complied with Section 3-3-160 to properly authorize the sale of
such property, including mailing notice to each owner of property owner within 300 feet and
providing reasonable notice of the proposed disposition at least 14 days prior to the hearing; setting
the matter for public comment; and receiving public comment; and

         WHEREAS, Draper City desires to declare the approximate 2,391.84 square feet of
property as surplus and to authorize its sale to best serve the interests of City residents and produce
a fair return.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

         Section 1. Declaration of Surplus Property. The properties described in Exhibit A and
Exhibit B are no longer needed by the City for a public purpose and can be declared surplus and
its sale is authorized.

        Section 2. Authorization to Sign Real Estate Agreement. The mayor is hereby
authorized to sign a Real Estate Purchase Agreement for said property and sell it pursuant to the
terms thereof.

        Section 3. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this resolution is held invalid
or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this resolution, and all provisions, clauses, and words of this resolution shall be severable.

       Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
passage.
PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF

UTAH, ON THE 7TH DAY OF JULY, 2020.
