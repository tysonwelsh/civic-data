Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding domestic animal waste in the watershed
and visitors with increased access trespassing on private land. Awareness should be raised on
current trespassing. Commissioners don’t want trails high in the canyon due to hikers and hunters
that scare away the large wildlife. The Trails plan was approved and adopted in 2007, limiting
access. There are not a lot of good access points and lead to dry hillsides that can catch on fire.
Commissioners suggest curtailing behavior of people accessing the canyon and will help to provide
information and ask not to dog walk and to park at trailheads. Can start with public lands and move
to preservation on some properties with a potential conservation easement. Having very little access
to green space will lead to a new zoning of green space and recreation. There has been camping in
Affleck Park and hunters camps in Pinecrest and at the top portions of Brigham Fork and the remote
areas. They do want to discourage camping. It was passed to designated areas and geared toward
more cars parked overnight on the road. Camping should not be allowed, and if people have a guest,
could be dealt with in a grace period with an RV on personal property. Too small an area, with
potential fire danger and water quality issues, and camping should not be allowed.
Commissioners and Staff discussed the Forestry Zone regarding conditional and permitted uses,
other uses regarding parks and quasi-public, should both be conditional and avoid barbeque pits.
Referred to public uses definition 19.04.070.139. Regardless of zoning district, if there are desired
uses, a rezone application can be submitted. Commissioners are extremely paranoid and afraid of
wildfire. FR-20 would give the right to build a house, leaving some properties in the 20-acre zone
or legal non-conforming. Could have a new zone named Open Space and eliminate recreation.
Requests elimination of Educational Facility with Residential Accommodation, and Planned Unit
Development. Briefly discussed Short-term Rentals with regards to septic systems and conditions.
Discussed wireless communications facilities noticing, and dark skies regarding the lighting on the
facilities.
Request a referral to Emigration Canyon in Limits of Disturbance rather than foothills and canyon
areas. Building height explanation of measurement is helpful, maybe increase building height to 35
feet on sloped site. Acquire accuracy on entity referred to in Water Quality that references the
applicable Utah administrative code.
Commissioners and staff agreed to skip Decembers planning commission meeting and meet in
January. Will then go to working session with the Council in March.
