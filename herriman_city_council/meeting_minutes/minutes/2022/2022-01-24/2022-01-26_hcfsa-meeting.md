           HERRIMAN CITY FIRE SERVICE AREA MINUTES
                                       Wednesday, January 26, 2022
                                       Approved February 25, 2022

The following are the minutes of the Herriman City Fire Service Area meeting. The meeting was held on
Wednesday, January 26, 2022 at 7:30 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman
Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on
the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair Lorin Palmer

Directors Present: Jared Henderson, Teddy Hodges, Sherrie Ohrn, and Steve Shields

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas, Assistant City
Manager Tami Moody, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Alan Rae, City Attorney Chase
Andrizzi, Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette, Police Chief Troy Carr, Deputy Chief of Police Cody
Stromberg, Public Works Director Justun Edwards, Operations Director Monte Johnson, Deputy Director of
Parks, Recreation and Events Anthony Teuscher, Planning Manager Clinton Spencer, and Building Official
Cathryn Nelson.

1. Call to Order (7:30 p.m. or as soon as possible thereafter)
Chair Palmer called the meeting to order at 7:54 p.m.

    1.1. Oath of Office for the Board of Trustees for the Herriman City Fire Service Area
City Recorder Jackie Nostrom conducted the oath of office ceremony for the Board of Trustees.

    1.2. Election for the Chair and Vice Chair for the Herriman City Fire Service Area
Trustee Shields moved to nominate Trustee Palmer as the Chair and Trustee Henderson as the Vice Chair of the Herriman City
Fire Service Area. Trustee Ohrn seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

    1.3. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes of
         January 26, 2022

Trustee Hodges moved to have the minutes of January 26, 2022 be prepared and distributed to each member of the Herriman City
Fire Service Area Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the clerk, if after ten days,
there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes are made
and the board is in agreement. Director Shields seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

2. Discussion and Action Items
    2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution appointing a Chief Executive
         Officer/District Manager, Chief Financial Officer, Budget Officer, Records Officer,
         District Clerk, District Treasurer, and Legal Counsel - Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
City Recorder Jackie Nostrom informed the Board all these items on the agenda were the creation documents
for the Board of Trustees. First, the resolution appointing various officers for District operations. Item 2.2
would establish Herriman City Hall to be the place of business for the District. Item 2.3 established the amount
of bond for the faithful performance of the members duties which would be set at $10,000. Item 2.4 outlined
the policies and procedures for the District, and Recorder Nostrom explained it was set up to operate similarly
to the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. Item 2.5 was consideration of an Interlocal Agreement with
Herriman City for administrative and legal costs for the District. Lastly, Item 2.6 was to adopt a formal seal for
the Herriman City Fire Service Area which would be used on all formal documents.

Trustee Shields moved to approve Resolution No. R01-22 appointing a Chief Executive Officer/District Manager, Chief
Financial Officer, Budget Officer, District Clerk, District Treasurer, and Legal Counsel. Trustee Ohrn seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Trustee Jared Henderson                                   Aye
Trustee Teddy Hodges                                      Aye
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn                                      Aye
Trustee Steve Shields                                     Aye
Chair Lorin Palmer                                        Aye
The motion passed unanimously.
