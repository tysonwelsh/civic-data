HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2022-17

22022-026 - ORDINANCE TO AMEND TITLE 10 OF CITY CODE TO
PERMIT FLAG LOTS ON LARGE LOTS ON MINOR COLLECTOR STREETS IN
AGRICULTURAL ZONES

WHEREAS, the City of Herriman, pursuant to Utah State Code, may adopt an ordinance to
establish land development regulations, which includes the adoption or amendment of zoning
regulations or map; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah State Code, the Planning Commission shall prepare and
recommend any zoning regulation amendment to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the City of Herriman Land Development Code, the Planning
Commission shall hold a public hearing and provide reasonable notice at least ten (10) days prior to
the said public hearing to recommend any zoning regulation amendments to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, a notice of a Planning Commission public hearing on a zoning regulation
amendment was posted in three public places on March 28, 2022, for a meeting to be held on April
7, 2022, at 7:00 p.m.; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission voted 6-0 to recommend approval of the zoning
regulation amendment in a public meeting held on April 7, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Council
Chamber; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Herriman City Code, it is the responsibility of the City Council to
consider the proposed zoning regulation amendment and Planning Commission recommendation ina
public meeting; and

WHEREAS, a City Council public meeting was held on April 27, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. to
discuss the zoning regulation amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is in the best interest of the citizens of Herriman
City to adopt the zoning regulation amendment, which was recommended by the Planning
Commission and documented in City File number Z2022-026;

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Herriman City Council as follows:

Title 10 of the Herriman City Code shall be amended as set forth in the attached Exhibit A,
and all other sections and provisions of Title 10 shall remain unaffected by this ordinance.

This Ordinance assigned Ordinance No. 2022-17 shall take immediate effect as soon as it shall be published
or posted as required by law and deposited and recorded in the office of the City’s recorder.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 27" day of April 2022.

Exhibit A
Title 10 Land Development Code
Chapter 10-29 Supplementary and Qualifying Regulations
10-29-18 Flag Lots

A. Purpose: Flag lots are intended to apply only to exceptionally deep or odd-shaped parcels that are
isolated from public streets and would be difficult to develop or utilize in any other way. An
applicant shall demonstrate that a flag lot is the most appropriate development option and will not
detract from the surrounding neighborhood.

B. Minimum Requirements: A flag lot shall meet the following minimum requirements:

1. The staff portion of a flag lot shall front on and be contiguous to a dedicated local public
street. Flag lots shall not front on a collector or arterial street.
a. Exception. Flag lots may front on a minor collector if the proposed subdivision contains two
acres or more, within an A-.25, A-.50 or A-1 Agricultural Zone and each subdivided lot shall
be a minimum of one-half acre.
