The following are the minutes of the Herriman Planning Commission meeting held on Thursday,
December 1, 2022, at 6:50 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main
Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City
Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Commission, media, and interested
citizens.
Presiding: Chair Chris Berbert
Commissioners Present: Andy Powell, Alternate Darryl Fenn, Heather Garcia, Alternate Forest
Sickles, Adam Jacobson, and Brody Rypien, and Andrea Bradford
Commissioners Excused: Jackson Ferguson
Staff Present: Communications Specialist lll Mitch Davis, Planning Manager Clint Spencer, City
Attorney Todd Sheeran, Deputy Recorder Wendy Thorpe, and Staff Engineer lll Josh Petersen
1. 6:30 PM - Work Meeting (Fort Herriman Conference Room)
Chair Chris Berbert called the meeting to order at 6:45 p.m.
1.1. Review of City Council Decisions
Planning Manager Spencer stated there had not been a City Council meeting since the latest PC meeting.
1.2. Review of Agenda Items — Michael Maloy, City planner
Commissioners discussed ideas for keeping track of items they have requested for future meetings, as
an item they had requested for a joint work meeting discussion was not included on the latest meeting.
Planning Manager Spencer responded those would be added to the agendas by himself or City Planner
Maloy. Commissioner Garcia recommended the creation of a Google Keep shared document or
something similar for keeping track of items PC would like discussed in Joint Meetings. City Attorney
Sheeran offered to look into options. Commissioner Garcia recommended a work meeting item focused
on Ordinances the PC wanted to work on. She was also in favor of more code review. Planning Manager
Spencer recommended prioritizing the items for the next Joint City Council/Planning Commission
meeting, which was scheduled for March 29, 202%. Commissioner Fenn requested a report from the
Police Department regarding code enforcement issues. Commissioner Jacobson requested to see all code
enforcement infractions or at least those with high prevalence items. Commissioner Fenn would like
