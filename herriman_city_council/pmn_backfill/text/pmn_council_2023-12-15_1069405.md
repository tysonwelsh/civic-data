                                EMERGENCY CITY COUNCIL
                                   MEETING MINUTES
                                     Friday, December 15, 2023
                                     Approved January 10, 2024

The following are the minutes of the City Council meeting of the Herriman City Council. The meeting
was held on Friday, December 15, 2023, at 9:00 a.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West
Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted
in the City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested
citizens.

Presiding: Mayor Lorin Palmer

Councilmembers Present: Teddy Hodges, Sherrie Ohrn (remote participation), Steven Shields

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas, City Recorder
Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Kyle Maurer, City Attorney Todd Sheeran, Communications Specialist
Destiny Skinner, Planning Manager Clint Spencer, Police Chief Troy Carr, Community Development
Director Blake Thomas, Public Works Director Justun Edwards, HPD Operations Commander Brent
Adamson.

Councilmembers Excused: Jared Henderson
                                                   ‘
9:00 AM:
   1. Call to Order
Mayor Palmer called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m. and excused Councilmember Henderson from the
meeting.

   2. Discussion and Action Items
          2.1. Discussion and consideration of a Resolution providing notice of a pending ordinance
               regulating aircraft landing and take off locations – Todd Sheeran, City Attorney
City Attorney Todd Sheeran presented the situation, explaining that the city received numerous
complaints about a helicopter landing on a residential property. Given the absence of specific
regulations in the City Code, he proposed a temporary land use regulation. This regulation, effective for
December 15, 2023 – Emergency City Council Meeting Minutes                                   Page 2 of 3

 180 days or until a permanent ordinance is enacted, outlined restrictions on helipads, including a
 prohibition in all zones except as a conditional use, a 1,000-foot distance requirement from residential
 buildings, and specific rules for emergency use and rooftop sites. He suggested roof top sit would be
 allowed for emergency use only.

 Councilmember Ohrn asked if the City had jurisdiction to limit this type of use. City Attorney Sheeran
 responded the City would have the zoning authority to determine where the helipad could be placed to
 control solely the landing and take-off of helicopters. He noted the City could have regulations in place
 as long as there are no conflicts with the FAA.

 Mayor Palmer opened public comment for this item.

 Tonya Earl noted she was at home yesterday when she heard a helicopter hovering over her yard
 which made her daughter frantic, and her house vibrated violently. She thought the helicopter was
 Lifeflight as it was red. Ms. Earl didn’t understand how people thought they could fly helicopters over
 neighborhoods outside of emergency and military usage.

 Tara Bennett noted she had moved to Herriman from out of state. Her previous home was located
 within a close proximity to a potential hospital that could install a potential helipad and she was
 required to sign an acknowledgement stating she was aware of the potential use of a helicopter in the
 area. Ms. Bennett also expressed concern of helicopters in residential areas could trigger PTDS for
 veterans.

 Steve Griffin indicated he lived in the home adjacent to the structure where the helicopter landed. He
 noted he had been in contact with Code Enforcement as well as the Building Department where he was
 informed the use had not been approved. He thanked the Council for taking action to mitigate this
 situation.

 Mayor Palmer closed public comment portion of the meeting.

 Attorney Sheeran explained there were some restrictions the Council could place on the use of a
 helipad for landing and take-off and acknowledged the FAA had jurisdiction of the airspace.
 Councilmember Hodges noted the FAA website specifies for people to check local municipality
 regulations.

 Councilmember Ohrn indicated the City was working with the land owner with the confusion on the
 “shade structure” and felt this situation was completely inappropriate as it crossed lines with the life,
 safety and wellness of the community.

 Councilmember Shields stated that landing a helicopter in a residential neighborhood was a poor
 decision. He indicated it was his duty to look for the betterment of the City as well as the safety of the
December 15, 2023 – Emergency City Council Meeting Minutes                                Page 3 of 3

 residents. This action would be to promote the safety, security, health and wellness of the community.
 Mayor Palmer agreed.

 Councilmember Shields moved to approve Ordinance No. 2023-22 approving a temporary land use
 regulation pertaining to the landing and take-off of aircrafts within Herriman City. Councilmember
 Hodges seconded the motion.

 The vote was recorded as follows:
 Councilmember Jared Henderson Absent
 Councilmember Teddy Hodges        Yes
 Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn        Yes
 Councilmember Steven Shields      Yes
 Mayor Lorin Palmer                Yes

 The motion passed unanimously with Councilmember Henderson being absent.

    3. Adjournment
 Councilmember Shields moved to adjourn the City Council meeting at 9:19 a.m.. Councilmember Hodges
 seconded the motion, and all present voted aye.
