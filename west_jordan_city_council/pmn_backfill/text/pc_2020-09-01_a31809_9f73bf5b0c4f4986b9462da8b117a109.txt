2.    Immaculate Used Cars; 8921 South Redwood Road; Conditional Use Permit for Motor
      Vehicle Sales; SC-2 Zone; Clark Automotive/Dennis Clark (applicant) [#22000; parcel 27-
      03-251-083-4001]

Dennis Clark, applicant, introduced his son Mike, who will be running the business.

Mike Clark stated that the car dealership will be located at the old Village Inn site. Improvements to
the building exterior and clean-up of the landscaping and parking area have begun. There will also be
some interior remodeling. There are 83 parking stalls. They intend to have a clean-operating
dealership. Shed sales that have been on the property should be vacated by the end of the month.

Larry Gardner said the building has been in place for 20 years. Good access is provided with the road
going to Home Depot with plenty of room to load and unload cars. This use fits nicely within the SC-2
zone.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the Conditional Use Permit for Immaculate
Used Cars; 8921 South Redwood Road; Conditional Use Permit for Motor Vehicle Sales; SC-2 Zone,
with the conditions of approval listed below:

1.     The use of the property shall conform to the submitted Site Plan, Letter of Intent, and the
       Application. Major deviations from these plans shall be reviewed by the City of West Jordan
       Planning Department.
2.     On-site vehicle and equipment repair shall not involve body work, engine or transmission
       rebuilding, installation of major accessories or any other repair work that falls under the
       definition of “Vehicle and Equipment Repair, General”, as defined in Section 13-2-3 and as
       mandated by the use table in Section 13-5E-3 of the West Jordan Municipal Code. Dismantling
       of any vehicle on site is prohibited.

3.     All vehicles shall be loaded or unloaded on the premises. Loading or unloading vehicles within
       any public right of way is prohibited.
4.     Vehicles associated with this business shall not be parked in a manner that fully or partially
       blocks any drive aisle.
5.     In association with this business the outside storage of any inoperable, wrecked, abandoned or
       junk vehicles on or near the premises is prohibited in accordance with Section 13-8-2 of the
       West Jordan Municipal Code.
6.     All current landscaping shall be irrigated, restored and maintained. Any changes to the
       approved landscaping shall require City approval.
7.     This business shall comply with all applicable noise ordinances of the Salt Lake County Health
       Department.
8.     All vehicles associated with this business shall be parked on a paved asphalt or concrete
       surface.
9.     Vehicles associated with this business shall not be parked or stored on any neighboring
       properties.
10.    Vehicles associated with this business shall be parked in an orderly manner and parked in a
       way that all vehicles can be accessed and moved without having to move another vehicle.
11.    Customer and employee parking associated with this business shall be provided on site and be
       clearly marked. No on-street parking is permitted for this business.
12.    Obtain permits for any permanent or temporary signs. All signage shall comply with 2009 City
       Code.
13.    Obtain and maintain a valid Business License with the City of West Jordan.

Ammon Allen said that lighting appears to be adequate and he asked what additional security measures
will be provided.

Mike Clark said they will install rope chains on 3-foot posts that prohibit vehicles from being driven
off of the site. There will be cameras as well as additional lighting in the parking lot.

Larry Gardner told the applicant that any new light poles will require a building permit and can only be
15 feet in height. They need to take care not to flood the residential property to the north with light.

Matt Quinney opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

Corbin England thought that this business is a great fit for the vacant building and property.

MOTION:        Corbin England moved, based on the information and findings set forth in the staff
               report and upon the evidence and explanations received tonight, to approve the
               Conditional Use Permit for Immaculate Used Cars; 8921 South Redwood Road;
               Clark Automotive/Dennis Clark (applicant) with the conditions of approval 1
               through 13 as listed in the staff report. The motion was seconded by Jay Thomas
               and passed 6-0 in favor. Kent Shelton was absent.

MOTION:        Jay Thomas moved to adjourn.
