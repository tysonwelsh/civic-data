                             NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                    Tuesday, May 9, 2023
                                          7:00 p.m.
                                         21 E. 100 N.
                                       Nephi, UT 84648

    1. Police Department Administrative Assistant Tiffiny Lund and Chief Michael Morgan
       reported to the council about the need for a Records Clerk/Evidence Technician. Ms.
       Lund reported about the many audits, compliance reports, and GRAMA requests she is
       required to do and every year the amount of time needed to accomplish her job has
       increased and her responsibilities have become more complicated. Ms. Lund offered
       some suggestions for raising revenue to help pay for this new position. The council
       agreed that Ms. Lund needs additional help in the office and they discussed possibly re-
       prioritizing some of the budget items to find the funds for this position.

    2. City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed two airport lease agreements with Jaren
       Lynn. The council directed staff to put the approval of these agreements on the next
       regular council meeting agenda.

    3.    New state code OHV/ATV rules were discussed. The council directed staff to change
         the Nephi City Code to coincide with the Utah State Code.
.
    4.    The council discussed the amendment to the Warburton Agreement. The council
         directed staff to bring the amended agreement to a regular council meeting for official
         action.

    5.   Mr. Atkinson reminded the council about the Six County Growth Summit.

    6.    Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the annual city administrator evaluations would
         be emailed to the council for them to complete.

    7.    City Attorney Kasey Wright reviewed with the council his role and who he represents.
         He clarified that he represents the council as a whole and as individuals. If there were
         ever a dispute between an employee and the council he would represent the council and
         not the employee. He would be loyal to the council as a whole and not favor one council
         member over another council member.

    8.     Councilor Parady expressed his concern about an organization not receiving an invoice
         for the use of the Hive. He stated that he had made many attempts to get an invoice
         and nothing had happened. City Recorder Lisa Brough said she would follow up with his
         concern.


    9.   The Mayor would like to see the water wheel at the Old Mill Park moving.
   10. Councilor Parady reported that the ribbon cuttings for two businesses were very well
      attended and very successful.

   11. Councilor Ostler reported that pavement is separating from the curb on 1250 North and
      Main Street. He also asked about the progress of the Crimson Flats Development that
      includes construction of ball fields. Mr. Atkinson answered that the city is ready to go out
      for bid on engineering and the traffic study completed for the road.

   12. Councilor JD Parady moved to convene into a regular meeting. Councilor Worwood
      seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.

   13. Worwood moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
       competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Parady seconded the motion.
       The motion passed unanimously. 8:45 p.m.

   14. Council reconvened into a regular work session at 9:25. Councilor Worwood moved to
       adjourn. Council Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
