   2. Citizen Review Board Ordinance Discussion. Council Chair Bynum said this is
      a rough draft of this Ordinance that was put together by City Attorney, Hannah
      Vickery. Ms. Vickery put this Ordinance together after she received information
      that was gathered from the City Council small group meetings.

       Ms. Vickery went over the draft Citizen Review Board ordinance. A copy is
       attached and incorporated by this reference. This draft will help as a guide for
       further discussion. She said the Council wanted a board that would be selected by
       the Council but due to South Salt Lake’s form of government the selection
       process will have to be different. Statutory board members are appointed by the
       Mayor with advice and consent by the Council. There is language in the draft
       ordinance that reflects the type of diversity the Council would like to see on this
       board.

       Council Chair Bynum asked for more clarification in the ordinance regarding the
       application process.

       Council Member Pinkney said she understands that the Mayor would select the
       members of the board but wondered if alternatively, a selection committee could
       assist with that process.

       Ms. Vickery said she would look into the possibility of a selection committee for
       the board selection process.

       Council Member Pinkney said she didn’t see in the ordinance anything about
       former police officers being excluded from the board and that is something she
       would like added.

       Ms. Vickery said there was not direction from a majority of the Council wanting
       to exclude former law enforcement from being voting members of the board. She
       indicated that the exclusion could be added upon direction of the Council and
       suggested there be a discussion.

       The Council had a lengthy discussion regarding training for the board.

       Council Member Siwik would like to see the review board review no knock
       warrants and property forfeitures.

       Council Member Pinkney asked that the Council have more discussion regarding
       former law enforcement serving on the board. She also had some concerns with
       the mediation process and is concerned about individuals that don’t feel
       comfortable filing a complaint against an officer to the police department.

       The Council had a lengthy discussion on whether or not former law enforcement
       should serve on the board.

       Council Member Pinkney said there will be a non-voting member of law
       enforcement on the board so she doesn’t think there is a need for any other person
       with law enforcement history on the board.

       Mayor Wood suggested that the ordinance could have language that stated that
       just one former police officer could serve on the board.

       Council Members deWolfe, Mila and Thomas all were okay with having a former
       police officer on the board and believe former law enforcement would bring
       added knowledge that would be helpful.

       Council Member Siwik agreed with Council Member Pinkney in not including
       any former law enforcement on the board.

       Council Chair Bynum was fine with having one former police officer on the board
