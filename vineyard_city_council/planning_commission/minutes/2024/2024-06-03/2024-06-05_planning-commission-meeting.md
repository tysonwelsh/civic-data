ALLEGIANCE

2.     PUBLIC COMMENTS
       There were no public comments to be made.

3.      BUSINESS ITEMS

       3.1.    Auto Zone - Site Plan Application
             Senior Planner Cache Hancey presented on the site plan application an Auto Zone
       by Colby Anderson ,with CIR Engineering. The Site is located at 243 N Geneva Road
       (Parcel ID:40:612:0201). The proposed site is located within the Geneva Road Mixed
       Use (GRMU) special purpose zoning district.

             Senior Planner Hancey presented the site plan and access in introduction to the
       project. He outlined the building and it parking, bicycle parking, trash, delivery, and
       sidewalks. He also included the landscaping. Planner Hancey noted that this meets the
       requirements for fencing and highlighted the access. He noted that these are revised
       elevations without color, but that it is laid out in the materials.

            Senior Planner Hancey noted that they have met all of the requirements but they are
       subject to the conditions: applicant pays any outstanding fees and makes redline
       corrections; applicant is subject to all federal, state, and local laws; applicant must
       observe all ADA requirements.

             Commissioner Graden Ostler asked about how the parking stalls were determined.

       Senior Planner Hancey described the calculation that is used to determine the number of
       parking stalls. He then briefed the business logistics including employees and hours of
       operation.

           Motion- COMMISSIONER NATHAN STEELE MOVED TO APPROVE THE
       AUTO ZONE SITE PLAN APPLICATION AS REQUESTED BY THE APPLICANT
       WITH THE CONDITIONS AT 6:12 PM. COMMISSIONER OSTLER SECONDED.
       THOSE IN FAVOR VOTED YES: NATHAN STEELE, BRYCE BRADY, AND
       GRADEN OSTLER.
