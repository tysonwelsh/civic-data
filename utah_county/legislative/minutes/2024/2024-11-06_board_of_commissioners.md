COMMISSIONER BELTRAN THANKED THE CLERK’S OFFICE FOR ALL THEIR HARD

County Assistant Election’s Director) HAS BEEN SELECTED AS EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH. HE

Nikki is incredibly deserving of an employee of the month.

I would like to nominate Nicole Stevens for employee of the month. Nikki is incredible and
the lifeblood of the elections department. I am honestly not sure what we would do
without her. She has an incredible knowledge of elections and code thanks in part to her
many years of great service to our county. When I have questions about code, I trust that
she knows the answer, or she knows that place to find it.

Nikki works incredibly hard. She answers lots of questions for those that are both upstairs
and down in the ballot center. In June she worked until 2am getting everything ready for
equipment to be sent to our vote centers. Nikki puts in the work to make sure that things
get done. Today as I am writing this she is working relentlessly to try and get all the help
that we need hired for the General Election.

Nikki is supportive and really cares about the people she works with. When I’ve worked
late with her, she will text to make sure I got home safely. She has empowered me to be
the best that | can be. I have caused her some stress recently quitting at such a busy time
but nevertheless Nikki has been incredibly kind and supportive.
