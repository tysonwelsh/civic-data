PC _ 24-017 CCN Holdings [Design Review Permit] Kent D Merrill/CCN Holdings, authorized
agent/owner is requesting a Design Review Permit for a new 20,790 square foot multi-tenant
industrial building located at 2041 North 600 West in the Industrial Park (IP) zone; TIN 04-079-0018
(Bridger Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley, Planner, reviewed the request for a new flex industrial building to be leased
out to smaller warehouse, contracting, or office types of businesses, located at 2041 North 600 West
in the Industrial Park (IP) zone.

The Land Development Code (LDC) requires a range of required parking based on individual land
uses. Based on the flexible nature of the proposed building and the mix of uses allowed in the IP
zone, staff has calculated that a total of 32 parking stalls would be the minimum parking stall
requirement. The proposal shows a total of 56 parking stalls, 24 more than the requirement. Parking
can be allowed in front of the building in the IP zone if it is needed for the functionality of the building.
In this case with proposed roll-up doors on the front fagade and the need for loading and unloading
through these doors, asphalt paving in front of the building is needed for functionality.

600 West is planned to be an 80-foot Collector Street. The City has requested that a 7-foot right of
way from 600 West be dedicated to the City for the future widening of 600 West. The 7-foot right of
way jeopardizes the compliance of the proposed site plan. The Commission will need to determine
where the 7 feet shall come from. The front setback can be reduced by 10% which would be an
additional 2 feet, and the 24-foot rear drive aisle can be reduced by 4 feet, the Fire Department
requires at least 20 feet for emergency access, gaining a total of 6 feet to use towards the right of
way.

Commissioner Peterson asked about reducing parking to make room for the additional right of way.


PROPONENT: Steve Mansfield said they are willing to make the width of their building one foot
smaller and move it back four feet if the commission will reduce the front setback by 10% or 2 feet.
This will get them to the 7-foot right of way.

Commissioner Lucero asked why they have so many parking stalls. Mr. Mansfield said that so many
businesses are under-parked, and the additional parking is a potential benefit for future businesses
at this location.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: The Commission discussed a 10% reduction to the front setback and determined
they are willing to allow a reduction in the front setback from 20 feet to 18 feet.

MOTION: Commissioner Peterson made a motion to conditionally approve a Design Review Permit
for PC 24-017 with the amended conditions as discussed and the findings for approval as listed
below. Commissioner Lucero seconded the motion.

PC 24-018 TEA [Zone Change] Erik Ashcroft/Erik C Ashcroft, Thomas Lee Hale & TEA Holdings
LLC, authorized agent/owners are requesting a zone change of three parcels from Traditional
Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) to Town Center One (TC-1) located at 345, 351 & 365 South 100
East; TIN 02-053-0026; -0027; -0028 (Wilson Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley, City Planner, reviewed the request for the rezoning of three properties totaling
.92- acres from Traditional Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) to Town Center One (TC-1) located at
345, 351, & 365 South 100 East. The property directly to the west was rezoned to TC-1 from
Commercial to convert the former hotel into a transitional housing complex. The rezone was needed
because the hotel was close to having 70 units per acre and that density is only allowed in the TC-1
zone. Once the property has completed all of its development it will be rezoned back to commercial
to prevent future redevelopment.

The TC-1 zone is the most intense zone the City has, it allows for the tallest heights and smallest
setbacks allowing for development that would only be associated with a downtown area. Staff feels

Planning Commission Minutes — April 11, 2024 6[Page

that the TC-1 zone would not fit in with this residential area and it is not what the neighborhood would
be anticipating. Staff is not recommending approval of the rezone request.

The Future Land Use Plan (FLUP) will be reviewed with the upcoming General Plan revision and
some of these fringe areas can be looked at to see if a different zoning would be more appropriate
with the changing residential and commercial needs.

PROPONENT: Erik Ashcroft explained that he planned to rezone the area to build townhomes that
would provide stable housing in the area. Mr. Ashcroft talked about the proposed site being a great
location for townhomes, close to downtown, shopping, and employment opportunities. Mr. Ashcroft
said that in speaking to his neighbors, they do not want to see more commercial development in this
area.

PUBLIC: Ryan Mernick who lives next to the proposed property asked what the timeline is for
revisions to the FLUP.

Josh Molitore asked if the applicant is planning on demolishing the existing homes.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Heare asked if there is a time frame for the General Plan revision.
Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director, said the General Plan revisions will start this
year and it will be an approximate two-year process.

Commissioner Heare asked the proponent if he was planning on demolishing the existing homes.
Mr. Ashcroft said they are planning to demolish the existing homes as they are older homes that lack
newer amenities.

Commissioner Heare spoke about alternate zoning for this location that would ensure housing is
developed at this location.

Commissioner Lucero asked if the FLUP could have a transition zone between commercial and
residential, possibly a Mixed Residential (MR) zoning.

Mike DeSimone said that to approve a rezone you have to be willing to accept everything allowed in
the proposed zone.

Commissioner Heare said the idea is good, but he cannot agree to a TC-1 rezone.

Commissioner Lewis suggested that the applicant talk to a City Planner to get some ideas of what
could be done, but TC-1 is not the right zone.

R. Holley said the applicant can move forward with this rezone request to the City Council for a final
decision, or they can choose to withdraw their request and reapply with a different zone.

Mr. Ashcroft said he did not want to continue with this request and withdrew his application.
