GENERAL PLAN/ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-15-2021 Petition by HAMLET DEVELOPMENT CORP. requesting a General Plan change
            from Rural Residential (1 to 2 units/acre) to Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre)
            and a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RE (Residential
            Estate). The property is located at 3702 and 3716 South 5450 West on 4.42 acres.
            (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:        Steve Pastorik explained that this proposal includes two lots with a home on each lot,
                   which they intend to retain. He pointed out the Reserve Subdivision located southeast
                   of the subject property, which was rezoned to RE in 2018. Steve noted that the
                   proposed subdivision would be similar to that subdivision. He said that the applicant
                   is proposing three flag lots. Steve pointed out that the property across the street does
                   have improvements; however, most of the road does not. Steve said that there is one
                   difference between this proposal and prior proposals for the Reserve. The homes will
                   be facing 5450 West.

                  Commissioner McEwen asked if this subdivision would include curb, gutter, and
                 sidewalk or if that would be discussed later. Steve said that where most of the street
                 does not have improvements, it was not discussed as part of this proposal. He
                 explained that the addition of curb, gutter, and sidewalk could be discussed as part of
                 the subdivision application.

                 Commissioner Winters and Commissioner Wood like the fact that the homes will be
                 facing the street. Commissioner Winter's only concern is that one building appears to
                 be much bigger than the surrounding homes. Commissioner Wood said that this
                 home sits much closer to the road, which might be why it sticks out. Commissioner
                 Winters asked Steve if there was a way to address this issue. Steve said that we could
                 speak to the developer about capping the height of the proposed structures.
                 Commissioner Winters feels it is better to give the option of a second story.

                 Commissioner Lovato asked how the Reserve fits into the community. Commissioner
                 Wood said that they have only finished two homes so far. However, she did not hear
                 anything negative about the development. She did have a conversation with one
                 gentleman who was concerned about the amount of water on the subject property.
                 Commissioner Winters said that the subject property is currently irrigated. Both
                 Commissioner Wood and Commissioner Winters said the homes are beautiful and are
                 an excellent addition to the neighborhood.

                 Steve mentioned that the applicant would be requesting a frontage exception to one or
                 two of the proposed lots. Commissioner Winters asked if they needed the exception
                 because of the existing homes, and Steve said that is correct; they are building around
                 those properties.

                 Commissioner Lovato asked why there is a private road in the middle of the
                 development and flag lots. Steve explained that this concept allows the applicant to
                 meet the average square footage lot requirement. He said that the flag lots include the
                 stem as part of the overall square footage, where an additional private road would
                 decrease the average size of the lots, causing them not to meet that requirement.

SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
PUD-2-2021   Petition by KEN MILNE requesting a reduction in the landscaping requirement for
             the Heather on Parkway PUD. Mr. Milne is requesting a reduction from 50% to
             44.4%. The request is related to the Heather Villas PUD, a senior living community
             that will be constructed at approximately 6087 West Parkway Boulevard. The
             property is approximately 13.8 acres and is zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling
             Residential). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Lehman explained that the applicant is requesting a reduction in the
                 landscaping requirement for the Heather on the Parkway PUD. Steve stated that Mr.
                 Milne does has preliminary approval. He said the existing development agreement
                 requires the unit size to be 1500 square feet and the applicant would like to develop
                 larger units, around 2,000 sq.ft. Mr. Milne feels the larger units will provide more
                 room for the tenants to have people visit comfortably. Steve noted that the applicant
              has submitted a letter that explains his reasons for requesting the landscaping
              reduction and this letter will be included in the Commissions packet. Steve did speak
              to the applicant about mixing different sized units to average the 1500 sq. ft.
              requirement

              Steve discussed the amenities that will be included in this senior development. He
              said there will be a large club house including many features, such as a fitness room,
              theater room and a sauna. There will also be a pickleball court and a walking trail.
              Steve stated that Mr. Milne feels offering larger units and a number of amenities will
              provide a higher level of comfort for future tenants and the landscape reduction will
              allow him to reach his goal.

PUD-6-2021    Petition by REJ HINTZE, representing Back Development, is requesting preliminary
              approval for the 4100 Commons Office Condominiums. The application will convert
              the old Granger Medical Office building into office condominiums. The property is
              located at 3745 West 4100 South. The property is approximately 7 acres in size and is
              zoned RB (Residential Business). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
              steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Lehman explained that the subject property used to be the Granger Medical
              Office Building until they relocated. He said the property has been left vacant,
              however the site has been kept well maintained. Steve stated that Mr. Hintze is
              wanting to covert the subject property into condominiums. This would divide both
              floors into smaller units for interested businesses to purchase.

              Steve said that he recently was informed of a transaction between Mr. Hintze and
              Kalen Howard, where Kalen purchased 2.76 % of Black Development. Steve said that
              Mr. Howard told him that his deal with Black Development had gone south. He
              explained to the Planning Commission that in speaking with Brandon Hill, Assistant
              City Attorney, it is fine for the City to move forward with this proposal. Steve
              mentioned this to the Commission because it is possible that this gentleman will be
              attending next week’s public hearing. If any further issues come up prior to the
              public hearing, Steve will inform the Commission.

              Chair Meaders asked Steve if he has any objections pertaining to this proposal. Steve
              said that he doesn’t know of any objections, and he feels that this gentleman could be
              trying to get assistance from the City to resolve his conflict with Black Development.

              Commissioner Winters feels this would be a great use for the property in question.

S-2-2021      Petition by MR. SHAUN JOHNSON, requesting revised preliminary plat approval
              for a portion of the 1st phase of the Weston Fields Subdivision. The revised
              preliminary plat will amend lots 1103, 1104 and 1105 of the Brookfield Subdivision
              and will adjust the north property lines of lots 110-112 of the Weston Fields
              Subdivision Phase 1. The property is located at approximately 4180 South 6740 West
              and is zoned R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square
                  feet). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:       Steve Lehman explained that during phase one of the Weston Fields Subdivision, it
                  was discovered that belonging to adjacent property owners were encroaching onto the
                  Subdivision. Shaun Johnson, the applicant, decided to amend his lots and adjust the
                  property line allowing the fences to remain in their current location. Steve said the
                  applicant did have preliminary approval and looking to move into the final plat when
                  it was realized that the fence belonging to the Brookfield Subdivision was
                  encroaching onto lots 110-112 of the Western Fields Subdivision. This proposal will
                  allow Mr. Johnson to adjust his property line and amend the affected lots of the
                  Brookfield Subdivision.
