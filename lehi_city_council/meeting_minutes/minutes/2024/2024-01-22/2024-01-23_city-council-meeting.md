3. Presentations and Reports
3.1 Timpanogos Special Service District's Westside Interceptor Project Update- Tess Fox
The Timpanogos Special Service District provided an overview of the West Side Interceptor
project, involving the installation of a 66-inch sewer line from Saratoga Road to Lehi Center
Street. The project aims to address increased capacity needs in west Lehi and Saratoga Springs.
The presentation covered challenges, such as materials, weather, and logistics, associated with
constructing a large-diameter pipeline. Divergent structures were discussed to manage flow and
allow maintenance on the 54-inch line. The use of fiberglass-reinforced polymer manholes was
highlighted for corrosion resistance. The presentation included details on the project's progress,
upcoming work, and estimated completion.

3.2 Timpanogos Special Service District User Rates Presentation
Richard Mickelson, Timpanogos Special Service District (TDDS) Executive Director, showed a
video on the master plan and future needs of the facility including the master plan phase one
improvements, outlining key projects and objectives. The plan addresses plant improvements
influenced by stakeholder input, regulatory requirements, and population growth. The total

3.3 PARC Tax Policy Discussion
Sierra Pierson and Jason Bond, Management Analysts and PARC tax committee members,
presented issues that were raised during the previous grant cycle, with suggestions to address
those concerns. The Council discussed potential changes to the policy including volunteer hours
as a matching component, and the perceived limitations imposed by the 50% funding cap on
organizations with modest budgets. Discussions revolved around exceptions to the policy for
granting larger funds to certain groups. Mayor Johnson proposed exemptions from cash match
requirements for groups with demonstrated community service records, while considerations
were raised regarding the allocation of grants for operational expenses versus specific projects.
The council also deliberated on the valuation of volunteer hours and donations. A proposal for
policy changes will be brought to the Council for consideration at the next meeting.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 South Freeway Corridor Study Presentation
Ryan Hales presented on the Lehi East-West Corridor Study that was recently completed by Hales
Engineering. He reviewed the alignment alternative which included a Pioneer Crossing expansion,
Pony Express Parkway (1900 S) expansion, and a north lake shore freeway construction. Mayor
Johnson expressed worries about the impact to residents and commercial property if Pioneer
Crossing were expanded. Planning efforts suggested aiming to optimize traffic flow and
connectivity while minimizing retrofitting costs. Options were evaluated based on future growth,
high-capacity transit routes, enhancing grid network capacity, and benefit to the city’s center. Mr.
Hales reviewed the regional impacts of each option. Concerns were raised about the negative
impact on Lehi if Pioneer Crossing were expanded to a freeway and suggestions were made to
involve the public in the decision-making processes. Mayor Johnson stressed the importance of
connecting North County cities through road expansion, while acknowledging disagreements
regarding project locations. Mayor Johnson voiced concerns about the potential disturbance caused
by two freeways. His preference would be for one freeway with multiple arterial roads to minimize
citizen disruption.

5. Consideration of a Plat Amendment to the West Lehi Elementary Subdivision creating
   five lots out of the three existing lots located at approximately 1100 West 1450 South in

   that the amendment will create five lots for new homes, which will be used by the school
   district's construction program for students. The program builds nice homes and is overseen
   by construction personnel who work for the district full-time.

    properly. He was also concerned with traffic and students parking on the road making it more
