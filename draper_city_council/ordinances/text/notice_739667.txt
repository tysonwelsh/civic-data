City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption - 
On March 1, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1526 amending the official zoning map of Draper City, otherwise known as the Airborne-Alcatraz Zoning Map Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder's Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 2nd day of March 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
