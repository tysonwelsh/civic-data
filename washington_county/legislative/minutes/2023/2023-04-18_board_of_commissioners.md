MEETING MINUTES
APRIL 18, 2023

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 4:00 p.m. on April 18, 2023, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah
Lloyd.

CONSENT AGENDA:

The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

PRESENTATION: IRONMAN WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP WINS 2022 EVENT OF THE
YEAR AT GOVERNOR’S STATE OF SPORTS AWARD

Sports and Adventure Manager Chad McWilliams with Greater Zion Tourism stated that the
IRONMAN World Championship that was held in May of 2022 was the first time the event has
been off of the island of Hawaii in forty-four (44) years. The Governor’s Office and the Utah
Sports Commission recognized the event as having a large economic impact on the state of Utah
and recognized it as the event of the year for sporting events that took place across the state of
Utah.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2023-1231-O, AN ORDINANCE AMENDING
VARIOUS TITLE 10 ZONING REGULATIONS REGARDING _DEFINITIONS,
CAMPGROUDS, CONDITIONAL USE PERMITS, PERMITTED WITH STANDARDS,
AND OTHER MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS DESCRIBED HEREIN

Mr. Messel related terms and definitions for commercial campgrounds were outdated as far as
where they are permitted and how dense they can be. The second part of this ordinance is regarding
permitted uses with standards, as they are becoming more common versus conditional use permits

Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3117, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A PROFESSIONAL SERVICE AGREEMENT FOR DRONE TORTOISE SURVEYS TO
BE CONDUCTED IN RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan stated one of the goals of the HCP is
to manage desert tortoises within the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve. In 2022, drone surveys were
conducted in Zone Six (6) and it is recommended that this year, drone surveys happen in Zones
Two through Five (2-5).

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3117,
A Resolution Approving a Professional Service Agreement for Drone Tortoise
Surveys to be Conducted in Red Cliffs Desert Reserve. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
