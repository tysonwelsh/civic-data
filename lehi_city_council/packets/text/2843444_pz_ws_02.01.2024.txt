30   2.1) Discussion of the 2100 North / Holy Cross Hospital Station Area Plan

31   Kim Struthers welcomed the applicants and introduced the items.

32   Jordan Swain and Tess Tanner were present via Teams to present the 2100 North Holy Cross
33   Station Area Plan. They shared a PowerPoint presentation along with an interactive presentation.
34   A community survey was presented that highlighted walkability and connectivity. Public
35   comments included in the survey focused on traffic, improvements, and connections to the rail
36   trail. Mixed-use development was proposed as an option.

37   Commissioner Roberts asked for clarification on existing buildings and the sanctuary.

38   Commissioner Lockhart expressed concern about the name "2100 North and I-15" and strongly
39   recommended choosing a different name. She also inquired about when the public would have a
