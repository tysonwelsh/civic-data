2.
Public hearing and recommendation regarding a Condominium Plat Amendment to combine existing suites with a section of common area within the building. 2750 Rasmussen Road, Summit County, Utah; Parcel SUMCC-101-K (0.646 acres). Applicant: David Nadler. Administrative review. Project #23-088. Madlyn McDonough, Summit County Planner (6:03 P.M.)
Madlyn McDonough,
County Planner,
provided a concise overview of the proposed Condominium Plat Amendment.
Commissioner Cooke asked a clarifying question regarding the square footage numbers between the staff report and the drawing provided.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:10 P.M.
No public comment was heard.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:10 P.M.
Christopher Conabee made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Manager pursuant to the following finds of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval listed in the staff report subject to the change discussed earlier of Finding of Fact No. 8 being changed to read 1274 square feet pursuant to Commissioner Cooke's comments. Thomas Cooke seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: DJ Hubler.
