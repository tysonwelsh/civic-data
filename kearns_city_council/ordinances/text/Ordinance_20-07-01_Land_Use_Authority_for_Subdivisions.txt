ORDINANCE NO. 2020-7-01                                     DATE: July 13, 2020

           AN ORDINANCE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
          DESIGNATING THE COUNCIL AS THE LAND USE AUTHORITY FOR
                              SUBDIVISIONS

                                          RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-2a-414(3) provides that a Salt Lake County ordinance will
remain in effect as the ordinance of the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) “until the metro
township council amends or repeals the ordinance;” and

       WHEREAS, Title 18 of the Kearns Metro Township Code (the “Code”) governs
subdivisions within Kearns; and

       WHEREAS, because the Salt Lake County originally adopted Title 18 prior to the
incorporation of the Kearns Metro Township, Title 18 as currently constituted is not specific to
Kearns and does not account for the metro township form of municipal government; and

        WHEREAS, on June 29, 2017, the Kearns Metro Township Council adopted Ordinance
No. 17-06-03 to create the Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission (the “Planning
Commission”) to enact chapter 19.05 of the Code, which specifies in Section 19.05.040(D) that
the Planning Commission is to “approve or deny…subdivision applications to the metro township
council;” and

        WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) desires to amend Title
18 and other applicable provisions of the Code to: (1) clarify that the Council as the land use
authority for Kearns for subdivisions consistent with the intent of Ordinance No. 17-06-03; (2) to
authorize the Planning Commission to review subdivision applications and make
recommendations regarding the same to the Council; (3) to make other corresponding changes to
applicable provisions of the Code; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-9a-602, the Kearns Metro Township Planning
Commission has reviewed and recommended the below revisions to Title 18 and other applicable
portions of the Code after providing notice consistent with Utah Code § 10-9a-205 and holding a
public hearing on July 13, 2020; and

        WHEREAS, the Council has determined that the attached revisions to Title 18 and other
portions of the Code will serve the best interests of the Kearns Metro Township and the general
public.

    NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:

12.       Section 18.08.010 is amended to read:

      18.08.010 Procedure Generally

      The council shall be the land use authority for subdivisions. In order to assure that each
      subdivision fully complies with the provisions of this title, the director or director's designee
      shall administer formal application and review procedures for subdivisions. An application
      shall not be deemed complete until the full application, fees and all required materials have
      been submitted. The payment of a partial fee and submission of preliminary plans for a pre-
      submittal review does not constitute a complete application.

      Each process shall include the following components:

      A. An application procedure, which shall include:




                                                   3
   1. Submission of an application form, as designed by the director or director's designee
      to clearly indicate the type of application, property address, applicant information, and
      other pertinent information;
   2. Submission of supplementary materials, including a legal description, property plat,
      the required number of plans/preliminary plats, and mailing labels (if required) for
      notifications;
   3. Payment of fees, as required under Title 3, Revenue and Finance.

B. A review procedure during which the director or director’s designee shall:

   1. Conduct an on-site review as provided by Utah Code § 17-27a-303;
   2. Review of the submitted site plan/preliminary plat for compliance with Kearns Metro
      Township land use ordinances;
   3. Refer the application and site plan/preliminary plat to any other government agency
      and/or affected entity which the director or director’s designee deems necessary to
      protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public and to ensure the project’s
      compliance with all applicable ordinances and codes;
   4. Process any exception requests that have been made in conjunction with the
      subdivision application.

C. A preliminary plat approval procedure during which the director or director’s designee
   shall:

   1. Confirm that all necessary agencies have responded to the requests for
      recommendation with a recommendation of approval or approval with conditions;
   2. Integrate recommendations from the other government agencies and affected entities
      involved above into the preliminary plat;
   3. Obtain recommendations from the planning staff and planning commission and provide
      the recommendations to the council;
   4. Approval of the preliminary plat by the council as outlined in Section 18.12.030.
   5. Issuance of a preliminary plat approval letter.

D. A final plat approval procedure, which shall include:

   1. Submission of a written explanation to the director or director’s designee by subdivider
      of all changes from the preliminary plat to the final plat seeking approval;
   2. An engineering review to ensure that the final plat complies with all conditions of
      approval of the preliminary plat and to ensure that the final plat complies with the
      design standards, codes, and ordinances and with minimum engineering/surveying
      requirements;
   3. A check of appropriate background information, such as: lot access, property title,
      record of survey, field boundary verification, etc.;
   4. Approval of the final plat and any related changes by the planning commission or the
      director or the director’s designee pursuant to Section 18.08.010(E).
   5. Payment of final fees and bond;
   6. The collection of the necessary approval signatures on the final plat;
   7. Recordation of the plat.
                                            4
   E. Final Plat Approval Authority

       1. The planning commission must approve in a duly noticed public meeting any proposed
          final plat which includes any change to the preliminary plat that involve: (1) increasing
          the number of lots; (2) lot access points; (3) the outer boundaries of the proposed
          subdivision; or (4) plans for required improvements identified pursuant to Chapter
          18.24. A public hearing is not required for planning commission approval of a final
          plat.
       2. The director or the director’s designee may approve a final plat if planning commission
          approval is not required under Section 18.08.010(E)(1). The director may, in their
          discretion, refer any change not addressed in Section 18.08.010(E)(1) to the planning
          commission to review for final plat approval.

13. Section 18.08.015 is amended to read:

   18.08.015 Time Limits

   Subdivision applications are subject to expiration according to the following schedule unless,
   for good cause shown, the applicant is granted an extension of time by the director or director's
   designee:

   A. A subdivision application shall expire if the applicant has not filed any of the required
      documents for preliminary plat approval within one year of the submission of a complete
      application.

   B. A subdivision application shall expire if the final plat is not submitted to planning and
      development services within one year of the preliminary plat approval.

   C. A subdivision application shall expire if the final plat has not been signed by the mayor
      within six months of the approval of the director or director's designee.

   D. A subdivision application shall expire if the final plat has not been recorded within six
      months of the date of the mayor’s signature on the plat.

       1. To enforce this section and to ensure that street light installation is completed in
          compliance with all of its requirements; and,
       2. To vary the standards referenced in this section and to approve alternative street light
          designs and locations when adverse topography, roadway geometrics and design, the
          presence of natural vegetation, or any other adverse conditions exist which would
          justify such variations and alternatives without being detrimental to the public safety
          or welfare.

   E. The council shall have the authority to waive or modify the requirement for street light
      installation in subdivisions upon finding that:

       1. The subdivision is located in an environmentally sensitive area; or,
       2. The subdivision will result in three or fewer new lots; or,
       3. The subdivision will not result in any other public street improvements.
