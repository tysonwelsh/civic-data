                                         Nephi City
                                   Work Session Minutes
                                      August 10, 2021
                                          7:00 p.m.
                                Nephi City Council Chambers




  1.    The council discussed a proposed amendment to the development agreement
       with Kenneth Jenson. This amendment would require a third corporation involved
       in the sale of the city property to Jenson to comply with the provisions of the real
       estate purchase contract and the development agreement. The Council directed
       staff to bring back the amendment to a regular council meeting for consideration.

  2.    The council discussed a written request from property owner Alex Johnson to
       consider his property as a blight area. After discussing recommendations on
       incentives for the Planning Commission, the Council directed Councilor Worwood,
       the liaison to the Planning Commission, to convey those recommendations to the
       next Planning Commission meeting. The council then directed staff to bring back
       the request from the property owner to the next regular meeting for consideration.

  3. City Administrator Seth Atkinson recommended that the Council may want to
     consider restructuring the Planning Commission so that the staff member is not a
     member of the commission and the chair position be another member of the
     commission. Resident Cory Thomson, who was in the audience, also voiced his
     support for the change and said that it fit the city’s code on the subject.

  4. Both the City Administrator and the City Council members provided updates on
     various projects in the city.

  5. Adjournment at 8:30 pm.



Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielsen, Councilor Larry Ostler, Councilor Justin Seely,
Councilor Kent Jones (Absent), Councilor Skip Worwood, and Councilor Nathan
Memmott, City Attorney Kasey Wright, City Administrator Seth Atkinson, City Recorder
Lisa Brough (absent), JD Parady, Cory Thomson, and Donald Ball.
