1. Stay Safe, Stay Home Directive Discussion

Mayor Silvestrini said Council Member Uipi was present at City Hall and Council Members
Catten, Marchant, and Jackson were participating in the meeting electronically. The purpose of
calling the meeting was to recap staff discussion held prior in the morning and to discuss the
County’s stay at home order that was issued yesterday. The mandatory order required everybody
in Salt Lake County to stay home unless they had a need to go out for necessary matters. The
order also required the closure of nonessential businesses. The Mayor encouraged any business
owners to review the emergency order on the City’s website. He said the order was needed
because people were not adhering to the guidelines, so recreational sports at the park and play
dates for kids would have to stop. He reported that half of the people hospitalized with the virus
were under the age of 30. Mayor Silvestrini said he had given direction to Unified Police
Department to enforce the County’s directive and those found violating it would be first given a
warning, for a second violation they would be guilty of a Class B misdemeanor, then the third
violation they would be guilty of a Class A misdemeanor. He said City Hall would remain open

issue yet. Council Member Marchant said the County issued a red, yellow, and green summary
on businesses that should be included the City’s e-newsletter. Rita Lund said the summary was
on the City’s website and social media. Mayor Silvestrini said he would keep the Council up to
date with any recent developments.
