Resolution

Legislative Lobbyist Services

                The Council reviewed the following resolution authorizing the Mayor to retain the
services of the firm Foxley and Pignanelli to assist White City for the purpose of providing
legislative lobbying services:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-07-01                                                      DATE: July 5, 2018

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO RETAIN THE SERVICES OF
       FIRM FOXLEY AND PIGNANELLI TO ASSIST WHITE CITY FOR THE PURPOSE
       OF PROVIDING LEGISLATIVE LOBBYING SERVICES

                                          RECITALS

                                               2
DATE           THURSDAY                                  JULY                             5, 2018



   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of
      White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. As an independent municipal entity under Utah Law, White City may be adversely affected
      by changes initiated by other entities to the state municipal code; and White City may
      identify certain law changes it needs to more efficiently, effectively, and equitably provide
      governance and manage the delivery of municipal-type services for White City.

   D. The government relations firm of Foxley and Pignanelli provides legislative monitoring and
      lobbying services.

   E. Foxley and Pignanelli, upon request of White City have provided a cost bid for White City
      to retain their services (Attachment A)

   F. Doug Foxley and Frank Pignanelli are recognized as experts by the State of Utah in
      providing these services.

                                         RESOLUTION

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council
authorizes the Mayor to retain the services of the firm Foxley and Pignanelli, to assist White City
for the purpose of providing legislative lobbying services for White City.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 5th day of July, 2018.

Resolution

Pipeline License Agreement

                                                3
DATE          THURSDAY                                  JULY                            5, 2018


             The Council reviewed the following resolution accepting the pipeline licensing
agreement with White City Water Improvement District:

RESOLUTION NO.18-07-02                                                     DATED: July 5, 2018

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP ACCEPTING THE
       PIPELINE LICENSING AGREEMENT WITH WHITE CITY WATER
       IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT

                                          RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (the “Metro Township”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
      Code Annotated §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Water Improvement District (the “Water District”) is a Local District
      pursuant to Utah Code Annotated §§ 17B-2a-401 et seq.

   C. The Water District desires to enter into a licensing agreement with the Metro Township to
      allow the Water District to perform necessary pipeline work for the conveyance of water.

   D. The Metro Township, as the grantor of such agreements also desires the Water District to
      perform the work described for the license.

   E. The attached pipeline license agreement (Attachment A) will allow the Water District to
      perform their work using a more effective, efficient, and equitable administrative process,
      thus being beneficial to the Metro Township and its residents.

   F. A possible conflict of interest may exist as the Mayor and Attorney serve both the Metro
      Township and the Water District, but based on the document being considered for
      approval being a template for other metro townships to use in creating such agreements,
      any perceived conflicts of interest are waived.

   G. The Metro Township and the Water District view the completion of water pipeline work as
      beneficial to meeting the public needs of the people of White City.

                                        RESOLUTION

       NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the White City Metro Township
Council approves the pipeline licensing agreement, Attachment “A,” for the White City Water
Improvement District.


APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this 5th
day of, July, 2018.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

              Council Member Perry stated a public hearing for the 2018/2019 budget was held
and then approved. Several employees retired this spring, which is causing an increase in
overtime being used. Chief Dan Peterson is trying to find a balance between using overtime and
hiring new employees. UFA is looking into hiring a lobbyist. Salt Lake County Surveyor Reid
Demman reviewed UFSA boundaries to make sure any changes with new developments are
captured.

South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

               Council Member Price stated the fogging process to control adult mosquitos has
started. West Nile has not been found this year; however, it is still early.

                               −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

              Council Member Flint stated the GSLMSD Board continues to work on getting its
own finance team and bank account. The Board hopes to have the finance team in place by
August. The Board is also working on a personnel policy.

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

recycled products, which has created the need to look at other options to recycle. WFWRD may
stop recycling certain products. Salt Lake County has agreed to keep the transfer station open
until a plan is decided on.
