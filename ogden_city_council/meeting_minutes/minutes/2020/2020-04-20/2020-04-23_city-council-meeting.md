         The purpose of the work session was to discuss the Marshall White Center.

Marshall White Center
          Chair Choberka invited input from representatives of the Young Men's Christian Association (YMCA) regarding
discussions they have been involved in relative to recreational endeavors and initiatives in the community at large. Rich West,
CEO for YMCA of Northern Utah, stated that the last time he addressed the Council, the YMCA had concluded its market
research to determine the community's ability to support a new YMCA facility. Since that time, he has been working on
development of a capital campaign aimed at raising the funds needed for the project; the YMCA has hired a consulting firm that
analyzes the viability of a capital campaign and they presented their findings concluding they feel the YMCA could raise
approximately $10 million for the project and then pursue a partnership with Ogden City for completion of the project. The
time line of the project is undefmed at this time, especially given the economic uncertainty that has been created by the COVID-19
pandemic. However, he hopes to be able to begin the campaign within a year's time; preparations are underway for launching that
campaign. For the first year, the campaign will be silent as the YMCA pursues larger donations from private partners; once a
certain amount has been secured, the campaign will become public. He then provided an overview of the services provided by the
YMCA; the YMCA provides much more than recreation programming. Discussion then centered on the timing of the design of a
YMCA facility, with Mr. West noting that it will be necessary to identify vital components that must be included in the facility,
but detailed design will not occur until after the capital campaign is concluded. Public Services Director Lowder discussed
Administration's analysis of the community-wide recreation needs and optional partnership models between the City, YMCA, and
the Boys and Girls Club of America for developing a larger recreation center in Ogden City.
          Discussion then shifted to the pricing of various YMCA membership options for Ogden City residents, after which
Council member Lopez asked ifit would be possible to build a new YMCA center around the Marshall White Center (MWC)
pool. Mr. West stated that planning for a future YMCA is based upon certain membership models and ongoing fundraising to
support annual operational costs. The determination has made thatrepurposing the existing MWC as a YMCA would be too costly
considering the age of the facility. Market research also showed that locating a new YMCA in a more central area would allow for
response to community demand. The goal of the YMCA is to impact as much of the City of Ogden as possible.
          Council member Hyer inquired as to the size of property the YMCA needs to build a community recreation center. Mr.
West stated that the facility would be considered 'medium' at 58,000 square feet; it would take up a quarter to half block
depending upon landscaping and parking requirements. Council member Hyer asked if it is necessary to locate the facility along a
defmed transit line. Mr. West stated that was not contemplated in the study.
          Discussion then shifted to the fluctuation in MWC attendance and fmancial data for Fiscal Years 2017-2020. Council
member Lopez stated that he is confused as to why the attendance numbers have increased if studies have shown that there is a not
a demand for recreational programming in the area of the community surrounding the MWC. Mr. Lowder stated there is a demand
