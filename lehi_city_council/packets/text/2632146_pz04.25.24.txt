 99   3.4) Consideration of DHI Communities' request for approval of the Ascend at Skye
100   concept plan including 302 apartment units located at approximately Center Street and
101   Aberdeen Way requesting a substitution of amenities.
102   Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.
103
104   The applicant, Brett with Wiggy Wash, was present and clarified questions for the
105   Commissioners.
106
107   Commission Chair Jackson opened the public hearing.
108
109   Jacob Curtis addressed the online comments which are submitted into the record.
110
111   Katlyn Kenney was opposed to the item and understood that the business had been approved.
112   She expressed that the intersection was already busy and congested. She felt that adding a turn
113   would be cause for concern. With the incentives that Lehi City offered with walkability and
114   conserving Lehi, it went against the purpose that Lehi was serving.
115
116   Kirk Evans was a property owner who was directly affected by what was happening with the
117   piece of property that was to be occupied by the car wash. The traffic was at a complete
118   standstill. There was a stoplight at the intersection, and there was bumper-to-bumper traffic for 5
119   to 6 hours a day, with the car wash expected to add to the congestion. When he tried to enter
120   Main Street, he found it impossible. He was fine with commercial business but not with high-
121   density housing. He asked that the commission think it through, especially considering the many
122   car washes surrounding the same area.
123
124   Commission Chair Jackson closed the public hearing.
125
126   The applicant wanted to clarify he had no intention of changing the zoning. The bordering
127   property to the house was residential. Commissioner Everett asked the applicant for the hours of
128   operation and water usage.
129
130   Commission Chair Jackson wanted to emphasize that there were zones and codes in place that
131   allowed property rights, permitting individuals to do as they chose with their property within the
132   set regulations. If it failed, Mr. Mignall took that gamble. Mr. Evans had the impression that
133   high-density housing was being considered, but it was not zoned that way. High density was
134   beyond a long shot and would require inconceivable changes. There were DRC comments that
135   would be implemented.

145   3.5) Public hearing and consideration of Mammoth Holdings request for approval of the
146   Wiggy Wash Concept Plan located at 26 South 2300 West, requesting exceptions to the
147   Development Code.
148   Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.
149
150   The applicant, Bret Wignall, was present. The wash and subdivision approval will be put in
151   simultaneously. They are divided into 4 pieces that total 4 acres. This is zoned commercial use.
152
153   Commission Chair Jackson opened the public hearing.
154
155   Jacob Curtis acknowledged the online comments.
156
157   Jared Hansen is concerned about the noise and traffic as well as the amount of car washes that are
158   already available.
159
160   Katelyn Kinney mentioned she resided in Lehi and noted that the area constituted a busy
161   intersection. The congestion near that intersection was already pronounced and raised concerns.
162   Within a three-mile radius, there were six car washes. Despite Lehi's incentives to become a more
163   walkable community and enhance its overall quality, it appeared not to be serving the interests of
164   Lehi or its citizens.
165
166   Kirk Evans, a property owner, was directly affected by this development. The traffic and
167   congestion were at a complete standstill at the intersection headed west. When he tried to access
168   Main Street, it was usually impossible. If this was approved, the traffic needed to be addressed,
169   and the property needed to be considered for future equity. He had not heard about this area being
170   HD until this meeting. He stated that this area was not zoned for HD, and it did not make sense for
171   it to be zoned as such.
172
173   Commission Chair Jackson closed the public hearing.
174
175   Commissioner Jackson wished to emphasize that there were established zones and a general plan
176   dictating future zoning changes, which governed property management. He observed that
177   comments were focused on the number of car washes in Lehi but stressed that it was within the
178   rights of private property owners, who assume the risk by adhering to property zones. The car
179   wash in question does comply with the zoning outlined in the general plan.
180
181           Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to grant approval of Mammoth Holdings request
182           for approval of the Wiggy Wash Concept Plan located at 26 South 2300 West,
183           requesting exceptions to the Development Code; including the exception of the setback

210   3.7) Public Hearing and consideration of Ivory Innovation’s request for approval of a plat
211   amendment and revised architectural elevations to the Innovation Park subdivision located
212   at approximately 2700 North Redwood Road.
213   Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.
214
215   The applicant representing Ivory Homes was present. They had decided to revise the design to
216   create a more consistent product and cost for the customer. The original design included the offset,
217   but the new design delayed the process and was not efficient for building townhomes. They opted
218   for straight lines for the interior to optimize the space. The price point was for rent because there
219   was no profit involved. They needed to be able to explain to the IRS that this property was viable
220   and sustainable. The City code increased construction costs.

228           stucco and balcony, as they met the development code. These findings were tied to the
