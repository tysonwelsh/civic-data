                                   MAGNA CITY COUNCIL

       RESOLUTION NO. R2026-37                                DATE: June 91h.2026

A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA CITY COUNCIL APPROVING AN AMENDMENT
  TO AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR GRANT
                                                 FUNDS

WHEREAS, Magna City is the subrecipient of pass-through funds from a U.S. Department of
Justice, Office of Justice Programs, Community Based Violence Intervention and Prevention
Initiative grant ("CVIPI grant") through the Salt Lake County Office of Homelessness and
Criminal Justice Reform ("OHCJR");

WHEREAS, Magna City and Salt Lake County, on behalf of its Young Services Division, entered
into an lnterlocal Cooperation Agreement, effective as of September I ,2023 (the "Agreement")
to build community capacity to address youth incidence of substance abuse and violence through
an integrated community, public health and public safety approach, funded by Magna City through
the CVIPI grant;

WHEREAS, the CVIPI grant has been extended through September 30,2026, and has 538,900.00
in awarded funding unexpended;

WHEREAS, an amendment to the Agreement has been prepared entitled "Amendment One ofthe
Interlocal Cooperation Agreement For The Safety Success Grant Programs," (the "Amendment")
attached hereto as "Attachment A," which extends the grant and adds the unexpended funding;

WHEREAS, the Magna City Council has determined that approval of the Amendment will
serve the best interests of Magna City and the general public.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MAGNA CITY COUNCIL:

               1.    Approval of the Amendment: The Amendment is hereby approved in the
       form attached as "Attachment A". The Amendment may be modified or revised prior to
       execution by the Mayor, provided that any such modifications or revisions do not
       substantially alter the material terms.

              2.       Direction to Mayor and Staff: The Mayor and staff are authorized and
   directed to take such steps as may be needed: (a) for this Resolution to become effective under
   Utah law; and (b) to finalize and post the Resolution to MuniCode, including but not limited
   to making non-substantive edits to correct any scrivener's, formatting, and numbering errors.

               3.     Severability. If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any part of
   this Resolution is unconstitutional or invalid, then such portion of this Resolution, or specific
   application ofthis resolution, shall be severed from the remainder, which shall continue in full
   force and effect.
            4.     Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 9th day of June,2026.

MAGNA CITY

This Amendment One ("Amendment") is entered into and shall be effective as of the signature of the
last party to sign below, by and between Magna City, formerly Magna Metro Township, ("Magna City")
and Salt Lake County, on behalf of its Youth Services Division, a body corporate and politic of the State
of Utah ("County"), collectively referred to as the "Parties."

                                              RECITALS

WHEREAS, Magna City is the subrecipient of pass-through funds from a U.S. Department of Justice,
Office of Justice Programs, Community Based Violence lntervention and Prevention Initiative grant
("CVIPI grant") through the Salt Lake County Office of Homelessness and Criminal Justice Reform
("OHCJR");

WHEREAS, the Parties entered into an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement, effective as of September l,
2023 (the "Agreement") to build community capacity to address youth incidence of substance abuse and
violence through an integrated community, public health and public safety approach, funded by Magna
City through the CVIPI grant;

WHEREAS, Magna City is no longer Magna Metro Township nor acting on behalf of Magna United
Communities That Care Coalition; and

WHEREAS, the CVIPI grant has been extended through September 30,2026, and has $38,900.00 in
awarded funding unexpended;

WHEREAS, Subparagraph 6.3 of the Agreement permits written changes to be made in the Agreement
upon the mutual consent of the Parties; and

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to extend the Agreement through September 30,2026, and amend the
Agreement to include the additional funding for 2026.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual desires of the Parties expressed herein, and other
good and valuable considerations, the receipt and sufficiency of which it is hereby acknowledged, the
Parties agree as follows:

  l. The reporting requirements outlined in Paragraph 2.3 shall be amended. Subparagraph 2.3.2 shall
     be renumbered to 2.3.3 and Subparagraphs 2.3.1 and2.3.2 shallbe amended to read as follows:



                                                    1
        2.3.1 Quarterly data shall be submitted by the lOth of every quarter (January l0th,
        April lOth, July l0th, and October l0th) to the Safety and Success Workgroup
        Coordinator which includes but is not limited to date of services provided, service
        hours, number and demographics of participants served, number and demographics
        of mentors, average case load of mentors, outcomes, and pre and post participant
        surveys.

        2.3.2 Quarterly narratives shall be submitted by the I Oth of every quarter (January
        10th, April lOth, July I Oth, and October lOth) to the Safety and Success
        Workgroup Coordinator which includes but is not limited to number of staff that
        received training and list of trainings/dates, type of strategies employed during the
        reporting period, types of direct services employed during the reporting period,
        descriptions on culturally specific interventions and services, descriptions of
        accomplishments highlighting one to two youth or program success stories,
        descriptions of problems or barriers and how overcame or assistance needed,
        collaboration ideas/needs/successes, and anything additional you would like to
        highlighted.

2. Paragraph 3, entitled Consideration and Paragraph 5, entitled Payment Schedule, are amended to
   include the additional funding of thirty-eight thousand nine hundred dollars (S38,900.00) for 2026.

3. Paragraph 6.1, entitled Term, is amended to extend the Agreement through September 30,2026.

4. All Parts, Paragraphs, Attachments and other provisions of the Agreement and any prior
  amendments thereof not specifically modified by this amendment shallbe the same and remain in
  full force and effect.
