                                       MID VALE CITY

                                 ORDINANCE No. 2012-14

                  AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING TITLE 11 OF THE CODE OF ORDINANCES
                   OF SALT LAKE COUNTY PERTAINING TO VEHICLES AND TRAFFIC

        WHEREAS, Section 10-8-84 et seq., Utah Code Annotated, 1953, as amended, grants the
authority to municipalities to enact ordinances that are necessary and proper to provide for the
safety and preserve the health, and promote the property, improve the morals, peace, and good
order, comfort and convenience of the City and its inhabitants; and

       WHEREAS, the regulation of vehicles and traffic is necessary to provide for the good
order of the City; and

       WHEREAS, Midvale City contracts with Unified Police Department ("UPD") for police
services and UPD occasionally uses Salt Lake County Traffic Code for vehicles and traffic
enforcement.

       WHEREAS, Midvale City Council hereby concludes that it is necessary to adopt Salt Lake
County's Title 11 pertaining to vehicles and traffic enforcement to allow UPD to use Title 11 of
Salt Lake County Traffic and Parking Code; and

         Now, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of the Midvale City, Utah as
follows:

                                              SECTION I

        That Salt Lake County's Code of Ordinances, Title 11 pertaining to vehicles and traffic
be adopted and enacted as set forth in Exhibit "A," attached hereto, and incorporated herein for
all purposes.

                                              SECTION II

         That Midvale City shall use the fee schedule currently in use by the Midvale Justice
Court.

                                             SECTION III

        In the event that any provision of newly adopted Title 11 is declared unconstitutional,
illegal or void, the remaining provisions shall be separate and remain in effect until similarly
ruled as such.
                                             SECTION IV

       This ordinance shall become effective upon passage, signature and notice of passage
published in a newspaper of general circulation.

         PASSED AND APPROVED this 10th day of July, 2012.
