Request
 The applicant, Greg Larsen with Sandy Oaks, LLC, is
 requesting preliminary subdivision approval for a 2-lot
 subdivision located at 8449 S. 300 E. This would be the
 fifth phase in the Sandy Oaks Subdivision and would
 result in connecting Sandy Oaks Drive to 300 East. The
 proposal will modify the dimensions of three existing
 parcels and provide street dedications to create the
 connection to 300 East. If the application is approved, it
 would allow for the development of two single-family
 homes on the properties (see Exhibit “A” for application
 materials). The subject properties contain approximately
 1.0 acre. The Planning Commission is the land-use
 authority for this review.

Background
 The proposed subdivision is surrounded by single-family
 homes. To the east and north, properties are zoned R-1-8.
 Properties to the west and south share the R-1-7.5(HS)
 zone. The creation of Sandy Oaks Drive has been a 20-
 year project intended to make efficient use of the excessively deep lots along 8400 South. Currently, there is a single-family
 home located between proposed lots 501 and 502 and an emergency vehicle turn-around required under the Phase 4
 approval on Parcel A.

Public Notice and Outreach
 This item was noticed to all property owners within 500 feet of the proposal. Staff held a virtual neighborhood meeting via
 Zoom on December 21, 2022. Attendees were generally favorable to the development as it will finally result in connecting
 Sandy Oaks Drive with 300 East. The only concerns expressed were around the timing and length of construction the
 project would require as it might create inconveniences for adjoining property owners.

Analysis
 This subdivision will be the fifth phase in the creation of Sandy Oaks Drive and result in connecting it to 300 East. It will
 create two buildable lots along Sandy Oaks Drive and remove the need for the emergency turn-around.

 Zoning Review
 The proposed subdivision is surrounded on all sides by single-family homes. All lots within this subdivision and to the
 South are zoned R-1-7.5(HS) while those to the north and across 300 East are zoned R-1-8. While it is preferable that lots
 created have lot lines that are substantially at right angles to the street line, staff has determined that the proposed lot
 dimensions are the most efficient use of the remaining land and meet all of the minimum requirements of the zoning
 district.

 The R-1-7.5(HS) zone is a traditional single-family zone specific to the Historic Sandy neighborhood. All of the standard
 requirements (setbacks, building height, lot size, etc.) of the zone are typical when compared to other similar zones. There
 are however additional design elements required through the Historic Sandy Neighborhood Plan that encourage a more
 “historic” character for new homes in the zone. These requirements are largely based on the Tier a home is located in. The
 proposed subdivision is located in Tier 3 – the least restrictive Tier. Architectural requirements for this Tier include the
 following:
 1.    Front porches
 2.    House entrance must face the street
 3.    Garage not to consume more than 40% of the house frontage
 4.    Minimum 4/12 roof pitch

 All of the design elements, setbacks and building height regulations will be enforced through the building permit process.
 All lots conform with the lot size and frontage requirements.

 Parcel A, as shown on the proposed plat, will not be a buildable lot. This area will be preserved for a future phase once the
 necessary land has been acquired to create new lots. An amended plat would be required to build anything on that parcel.

 The existing single-family home addressed as 8465 S. 300 E. must be demolished as the proposed revised property line will
 run through the middle of the home.

 Vehicle Access
 The gate currently located at the west end of Sandy Oaks Drive will be removed and the street will continue to tie directly
 into 300 East. The street width will be stepped down based on the proximity to the existing structures on 8445 South 300
 East. This step has been reviewed by the various departments and found to be acceptable.

 The first three (3) phases of the Sandy Oaks subdivision were approved with a 50-foot right-of-way, which is consistent
 with the City’s old residential street standard. This standard was changed to 52 feet to accommodate a five (5) foot
 sidewalk which meets current ADA requirements. The previous standard only called for a four (4) foot sidewalk. Sandy
 City Engineer, Ryan Kump recommended that the 50-foot profile used in previous phases be continued to Phase 5, except
 that the parkstrip would transition from five (5) feet to four (4) and the sidewalk from four (4) feet to five (5). During
 construction, that standard was not followed in Phase 4, and was built with a full width improvement, but lacked proper
 dedication. This current phase proposes to follow the previously approved road profile of a five (5) foot sidewalk and four
 (4) foot parkstrip on the south side of the Sandy Oaks Drive. The north side would not have sidewalk continue out to 300
 East. The proposed dedication of street right of way would be reduced to 42’ as it goes west.

  Pedestrian Access
  The sidewalk located on the south side of Sandy Oaks Drive will be continued and tie into the sidewalk running north to
  south along 300 East. The sidewalk running along the north side of Sandy Oaks Drive will end at the west property line of
  Parcel A. This is also driven by the location of existing structures at 8445 South 300 East.

  Staff Concerns
  Staff has no concerns with the proposal.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine preliminary review is complete for the Sandy Oaks Phase 5
  Subdivision located at 8449 S. 300 E. based on the following findings and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:

    1. That the proposed lot configuration is an efficient use of the land.

    2. That the proposed lot sizes and frontages conform to the requirements of the R-1-7.5(HS) Zone.

    3. That the various City Departments and Divisions have preliminarily approved the proposed subdivision plat.

Conditions:
   1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments and redlines throughout the final review process and
       that all issues be resolved before the subdivision can be recorded.

    2. That all City provisions, codes, and ordinances are adhered to during the review, construction, and operations
       process of this project.

    3. That previous approvals of the Planning Commission to reduce the right-of-way dedication required for the
       extension of Sandy Woods Drive to 300 East be honored. That a 42’ right-of-way dedication of Sandy Woods
       Drive be approved and built as per the applicant’s materials.

    4. That all residential lots comply with all requirements of the R-1-7.5(HS) zone.

    5. That parcel ‘A’ be a non-buildable parcel and future phase of development.

    6. That the existing home at 8465 S. 300 E. be demolished prior to recording of the subdivision plat.
