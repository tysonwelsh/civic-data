IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES AGREEMENT WITH IBM (FORMERLY AST).
-Jeremy Walker, Auditor

(Timestamp: 1:13:41)

have a new go live date 8 months from now.

Commissioner Beltran asked for further details regarding the extension. Jeremy
explained some of the key issues being, getting behind on what IBM was asking
the county to do, extreme county employee burnout, IBM replaced a project
manager, communication and expectation issues, and a new management change
to IBM.

Commissioner Powers Gardner said she has been through several other ERP
implementations and said in the end we should have a significantly easier time on
forecasting and our employees will be spending 20% less time doing manual
things, etc.

Rod Mann (UC Auditor) said it is not unusual for big ERP projects to exceed two or
three times their initial budgets. He thanked his team for all of their work.
