MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd Taylor
Date: 2024-06-13
Re: Public Hearing: Copper Creek Electric, LLC Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
(Administrative Action)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
for approximately 1.46 acres located at 13603 S. 300 E. The property is zoned RA1
(Residential Agricultural, 40,000 sq. ft. minimum lot size). The applicant is requesting that
a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow a home office with two
(2) non-family, non-resident employees coming to the home, and for the storage and use
of one (1) work vehicle as part of the business.

Findings for approval:
  1. Reasonable mitigation measures are placed on the conditional use to alleviate
     anticipated detrimental effects.

Findings for denial:
  1. Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to
     sufficiently alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
