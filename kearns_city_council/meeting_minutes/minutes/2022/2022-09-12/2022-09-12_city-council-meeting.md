Planning Commission

                 The Council reviewed the following Resolution honoring Jerry Wellman for his
service on the Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission and appointing Gray Thomas to
fulfil the remainder of the term:

RESOLUTION NO. 2022-09-01                                         DATE: September 12, 2022

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL HONORING
       JERRY WELLMAN FOR HIS SERVICE ON THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
       PLANNING COMMISSION AND APPOINTING GRAY THOMAS TO FULFILL
       THE REMAINDER OF JERRY WELLMAN’S TERM ON THE PLANNING
       COMMISSION

                                         RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Section 19.05.010 of the Kearns Metro Township Code requires the Kearns
Metro Township Council (the “Council”) to appoint members of the Kearns Metro Township
Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”); and


                                             1
DATE            MONDAY                      SEPTEMBER                                 12, 2022


     WHEREAS, Commissioner Jerry Wellman resigned as a regular member of the Planning
Commission on June 27, 2022; and

        WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint Gray Thomas as a regular member of the
Planning Commission to fulfill the remainder of Commissioner Wellman’s term pursuant to Section
II.A.10 of the Planning Commission’s Rules of Order and Procedure.

      NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Kearns Metro Township Council effective
immediately:

       1. The Council thanks Commissioner Jerry Wellman for his service to Kearns and the
          Planning Commission; and

       2. Gray Thomas is appointed as a regular member of the Planning Commission to fulfill
          the remainder of Commissioner Wellman’s term, which will end on March 31, 2022;
          and

       3. The Mayor and Kearns Metro Township staff are authorized to take any steps needed
          to implement Gray Thomas’s appointment to the Planning Commission

      APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Kearns Metro Township Council, on this 12th day of
September, 2022.

Stakeholder Updates/Information

Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Chief Levi Hughes updated the Council on the following:

    Introduced Sean Fausett as the new Sergeant in Kearns.
    Reviewed crime statistics for August.
    Presented a request to reallocate one Line Officer position in Kearns to a Sergeant
     position for the Community Oriented Police (C.O.P.) program.

                               −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

              Chief Wade Russell updated the Council on the following:

      Reviewed the Standards of Cover document.
      Kearns Metro Township patches that will be placed on UFA uniforms.
      UFA station construction updates.
      Update on the purchase of the ladder truck.
      Reviewed call statistics for August.

                               −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

              Renee Plant, Administrative Manager, WFWRD, stated the new website is up and
running and the kinks have been worked out. The seasonal cleanup program will be over in a
couple of weeks until next year.

                               −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Kearns Improvement District (KID)

              Greg Anderson, Kearns Improvement District, updated the Council on the
following:

     Utah Department of Water Quality and Salt Lake County Health Department water facility
      inspection.
     2023 budget.
     Public hearing to discuss impact fees will be held on October 18, 2022.
     Localscapes program.
     Conservation film.
     Water usage statistics.

Evidence 2 Success (E2S)

              Becky Guertler, My Kearns Community Coalition, updated the Council on the
following:

    Evidence 2 Success has changed its name to My Kearns Community Coalition.
    The process of becoming a 501(c)(3).
    Youth Council and $50,000 grant update.

              Josh Nielsen, My Kearns Community Coalition, updated the Council on the
following:

    Hiring of a coordinator.
    Members have the opportunity to attend a conference in Washington D.C.
    Hiring an artist to paint a mural in Kearns.

Other Business

Approval of Minutes

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
approve the minutes of the Kearns Metro Township Council meeting held on July 11, 2022. The
motion passed unanimously.

Future Agenda Business

               Mayor Bush stated the Council will need to hold a special meeting to adopt
amendments to the Moderate Income Housing Plan. She will include UPD’s request for a C.O.P.
Sergeant and the request to fund a mural. She suggested holding the meeting on September 27,
2022, at 6:00 pm.
