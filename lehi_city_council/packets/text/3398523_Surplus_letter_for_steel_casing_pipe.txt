                                                                                       June 4, 2025
To: Mayor and Council
From: Dave Norman
Subject: Surplus of Steel Casing Pipe

Mayor and Council,

I am requesting approval to surplus and sell up to 450 feet of 30-inch steel casing pipe, 100
feet of 36-inch steel casing pipe and 800 feet of 24-inch steel casing pipe.

The pipe is being used in the drilling of the new Flight Park well. The driller has pulled the 30-
inch pipe out of the ground after using it to drill a portion of the well. The pipe is used, but it is
in good enough condition to have some value. The 24-inch and 36-inch pipe will be pulled out
over the next several months as the project continues.

New 36-inch casing pipe is worth between $150 to $200 per foot; new 30-inch casing pipe is
worth between $125 to $150 per foot; and 24-inch casing pipe is worth between $75 to $100
per foot. The drilling contractor has offered the City a credit of $5,000 to haul the 30-inch pipe
off. I have reached out to the local metal recycler, and they are paying $70 per ton and the
30-inch pipe weighs approximately 32 tons, which is a value of $2,240.

We have found a contractor that would like to purchase 80 feet of the 30-inch pipe to use as
casing on a project and is willing to pay $5,000 for the 80 feet.

In accordance with Lehi City Code, once the surplus is approved, we will solicit bids from
other possible interested buyers through the City’s website and through other online markets.
We will also look to determine if the pipe can be used on City projects over the next couple of
years.

Please let me know if you have any questions or need further information. Your support in
moving this forward is greatly appreciated.

Sincerely,



Dave Norman
Lehi City Corporation
Public Works Director
