                                       SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL
                                             MINUTES
                                          Wednesday, May 6, 2020
                                           Approved May 27, 2020

The following are the minutes of the City Council Electronic Meeting of the Herriman City Council. The meeting
was held on Wednesday, May 6, 2020 at 4:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Fort Herriman Conference Room,
5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted
in the City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Mayor David Watts

Councilmembers Present: Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields and Clint Smith

Staff Present: City Manager Brett Wood, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Assistant City Manager Wendy
Thomas, Police Chief Troy Carr, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Alan Rae and Human Resources
Manager Travis Dunn.

4:00 PM – SPECIAL ELECTRONIC MEETING:
       1. Council Business
Mayor David Watts called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m. and welcomed those in attendance. He indicated there
was one item of business to discuss prior to entering into the Closed Session meeting as he turned the time over to
City Manager Brett Wood.

City Manager Wood indicated some cities in the south Salt Lake County area were looking to have a fireworks
celebration on May 23, 2020 to honor medical professionals, and asked the Council if they would be supportive of
the request. A short discussion ensued regarding the details surrounding the event. The Council relayed their support
for the celebration.

            1.1. Closed Session
                1.1.1. The Herriman City Council will temporarily recess the special City Council meeting to
                       convene in a closed session to discuss the character, professional competence, or physical
                       or mental health of an individual, as provided by Utah Code Annotated §52-4-205
May 6, 2020 – Special City Council Meeting Minutes                                                    Page 2 of 2
Councilmember Ohrn moved to temporarily recess the special City Council meeting to convene in a closed session to discuss the character,
professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual, as provided by Utah Code Annotated §52-4-205. Councilmember
Smith seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Councilmember Jared Henderson       Aye
Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn          Aye
Councilmember Steven Shields        Aye
Councilmember Clint Smith           Aye
Mayor David Watts                   Aye

The motion passed unanimously.

The Council reconvened the Special Meeting at 5:25 p.m.

         2. Adjournment
Councilmember Ohrn moved to adjourn the Special City Council meeting at 5:26 p.m. Councilmember Shields seconded the motion,
and all voted aye.

I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true,
accurate and complete record of the meeting held on May 6, 2020. This document constitutes the official minutes
for the Herriman City Council Meeting.




Jackie Nostrom, MMC
City Recorder
