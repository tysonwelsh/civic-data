Citizen Comments:
  Lawrence Horman – Murray resident
  Mr. Lawrence said without malice, he was frustrated and angry due to the battle he felt he was having with the
  City. After repeatedly trying to resolve his homeless issues, he believed the City was stonewalling him and
  withholding information that could improve his situation. He also felt that various agencies and departments
  were ignoring his requests to get power, water and sewer services installed at his current dwelling place.

Special Recognition:
  Murray City Employee of the Month, Tad Raban, Paramedic/Firefighter. Ms. Turner and Fire Chief Joey
  Mittelman presented Mr. Raban with a certificate and a $50 gift card, recognizing him for his strong leadership,
  loyal dedication, and heroic service as a Murray paramedic and firefighter.
