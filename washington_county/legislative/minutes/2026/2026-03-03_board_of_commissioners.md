County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3715, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE COUNCIL OF GOVERNMENTS RECOMMENDATIONS

Public Works Director Todd Edwards explained the Council of Governments (COG) recommends
the approval of the 2026 Master Priority Corridor Preservation Project List; the approval of a
Hurricane City request for $1,820,000 from the Preservation Fund to pay for acquisition of a right-
of-way for the 2800 West Street corridor, and the approval of a Washington County Request of
$150,000 from the Construction Grant Fund for additional funding for an alignment survey,
acquisition funding and road dedication plats for the Sheep Bridge Road corridor.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3715,
A Resolution Approving the Council of Governments Recommendations.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3716, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN EXPENDITURE OF TRT FUNDING FOR AN EVENT MARKETING AGREEMENT
WITH MATT’S OFF-ROAD RECOVERY

Greater Zion Sports and Adventure Marketing Manager Chad McWilliams stated the Tourism Tax
Advisory Board (TAB) recently voted recommending an Event Marketing Package of $25,000 for
the 2026 Matt’s Off-Road Games to be held at Sand Hollow State Park March 19-21, 2026.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3716, A
Resolution Approving an Expenditure of TRT Funding for an Event
Marketing Agreement with Matt’s Off-Road Recovery. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3717, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND SCHINDLER
ELEVATOR CORP. REGARDING CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER ELEVATORS

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained the Children’s Justice Center (CJC) is in need
of a maintenance agreement for their elevators and Schindler Elevator came in as the lowest
responsible bidder. This would be for a three year contract.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3717,
A Resolution Approving An Agreement Between Washington County and
Schindler Elevator Corp. Regarding Children’s Justice Center Elevators.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

APPOINTING BOARD MEMBERS TO THE HURRICANE VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated with the recent municipal election changes it is

Service District Administrative Control Board. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

Mr. Snow stated under state law the County is required to have an appeal process for employment
appeals. This Resolution would appoint an administrative law judge to hear employment appeals
in addition to the Career Service Council as required by State code.

Resolution Appointing an Administrative Law Judge for Employment
Appeals. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by

COMMISSIONER’S REPORT

Commissioner Iverson reported Washington D.C. and the judiciary branch continues to disappoint
due to the injunction on the Northern Corridor.

Commissioner Almquist explained several ordinances are being discussed that will be coming
before the Commission in the next several weeks.

Commissioner Snow stated water is almost back to average as of the last storms, and to continue
to pray for rain. He also explained the legislative session ends on Friday and thanked the legislators
from Washington County.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:25 p.m.
