Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Linda Peterson
CC: City Council Date: August 2, 2022
Re: Ordinance #1554 Comments: This proposed text amendment to Draper City Municipal Code is an effort to properly identify and protect key symbols associated with Draper City, such as the city logo, seal, flag and others from inappropriate use. Use of these symbols by those outside of the City organization can lead to confusion or contribute to the spread of disinformation. This ordinance designates custodians of the symbols to monitor use and determine if they are being used appropriately, based on the criteria outlined in the ordinance. It also establishes a penalty for inappropriate use.  ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Ord. 1554 7/29/2022 Cover Memo Exhibit A 7/29/2022 Cover Memo
