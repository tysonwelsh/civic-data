Subject: Council Member Stroud proposed amendment to the FY 2026 Tentative Budget –
         Hazardous Concrete, an additional Animal Services Officer, and Police Ammunition


Description: Appropriation of $100,000 in additional funding towards Hazardous Concrete
Repair, an additional Animal Services Officer, and Police Ammunition.
Proposal Summary:
This amendment proposes:
      1. A one-time appropriation of an additional $100,000 to hazardous concrete repair
         (Exhibit A).
      2. A new Animal Services Officer funded at $89,872 (Exhibit B)
      3. An ongoing increase of $5,000 for Police Ammunition (Exhibit C)
Narrative:
I want to emphasize the critical importance of allocating sufficient funding for hazardous
concrete repair in our upcoming budget. Beyond the obvious aesthetic concerns, failing
concrete infrastructure, such as crumbling sidewalks, poses a significant and immediate threat
to public safety. Investing now in these essential repairs is not merely a matter of maintenance;
it is a fundamental responsibility we owe to our constituents to ensure their safety and
maintain the structural integrity of our shared public spaces.
Hiring a new Animal Services Officer is a critical investment in the well-being and safety of our
entire community, both human and animal. This additional officer will enhance our capacity to
respond promptly to calls regarding stray, injured, or aggressive animals, reducing potential
public safety risks and ensuring humane treatment for animals in distress.
Ensuring our police department has access to the appropriate amount of ammunition is
fundamental to maintaining public safety and officer preparedness, particularly concerning
firearms training. Regular, realistic training with live ammunition is indispensable for officers to
