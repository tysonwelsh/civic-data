CONSIDERATION _ OF ORDINANCE NO. _ 2019-1139-O, AN ORDINANCE
IMPLEMENTING A SALES AND USE TAX FOR TRANSPORTATION
FACILITIES AND PUBLIC TRANSIT

Washington County Commission

Chairman Cox explained that the public road infrastructure is the most expensive asset that
is owned by Washington County. Due to the rising costs of materials over time, it has
become necessary to seek alternative funding sources as current sources have become
insufficient to maintain the needs of the growing County.

The State Legislature has provided a means to obtain funds in the form of tax
implementation which would be used to fund transportation facilities as well as a public
transportation system. Utah Code Section 59-12-2219 authorizes Washington County to
impose a .25% sales and use tax throughout the County.

Chairman Cox also mentioned that the County has received several letters of support from
Mayors throughout the County and read aloud a letter from Ivins Mayor Chris Hart.
Chairman Cox also mentioned that Ivins City has passed a resolution in favor of
implementing the transportation tax.

Commissioner Iverson explained that the County is considering this matter as it is
important to recognize that the County as a whole would benefit from a county-wide
transportation system as well as recognizing that funds will also be used to improve
existing roads.

Springdale Mayor Stan Smith stated that it is a necessary tax in order to further connect the
county via transit system. A transit system would help to fill jobs in the Springdale area as
it would provide a means of transportation for those that may not otherwise be able to
commute on a regular basis.

Hurricane Mayor John Bramall stated that the funds generated by the tax will largely be
funded by tourists visiting the County and spoke in favor of proposed tax.

Rockville Mayor Pam Leach spoke in favor of the tax.

Washington City Councilman Troy Belliston mentioned that it would be beneficial for a
County-wide transit system.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2019-1139-
O, An Ordinance Implementing a Sales and Use Tax for
Transportation Facilities and Public Transit. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2480, A RESOLUTION
ESTABLISHING WASHINGTON COUNTY'S GENERAL FEES AND GRAMA
FEES FOR SERVICES OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY RECORDS
MANAGEMENT/ARCHIVES DEPARTMENT

Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf explained that when GRAMA requests are received,
the Utah State Code allows the County to charge for staff time for the cost of the lowest
paid employee within the department qualified to work on the request.

Commissioner Almquist questioned how requests are handled if they are deemed frivolous
or broad.

Mr. Graf explained that the public has a right to inspect public documents. However, for
frivolous or over-burdensome requests, the county can require payment upfront if the costs
are expected to exceed fifty dollars ($50).
