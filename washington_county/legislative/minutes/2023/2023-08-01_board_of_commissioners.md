WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 1, 2023

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 4:00 p.m. on August 1, 2023, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Jessica
Jones.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Set. Pat Lisi, US Marine Corps

the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the

PRESENTATION: LIBERTY WEEK OVERVIEW

Dennis Leavitt, CEO of United We Pledge stated that Liberty Week is an annual event to remember
the purpose of Independence Day and the events that lead to the founding of the nation. He stated
over 33,000 people attended the weeks events with over 13,000 guests attending the exhibit held
in the County Administration Building.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3159, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A COMMENT LETTER IN SUPPORT OF THE BLM’S PROPOSED WITHDRAWL OF
CERTAIN LAND FROM BLM MANAGEMENT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that the federal government owns and manages
about seventy five percent (75%) of Washington County. The Bureau of Land Management (BLM)
is proposing giving approximately 15,000 acres to the School and Institutional Trust Lands
Administration (SITLA), and this letter would support that transfer.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3159, A
Resolution Approving a Comment Letter in Support of the BLM’s Proposed
Withdrawl of Certain Land From BLM Management. Motion seconded by

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3160, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE COMMUNICATIONS FRANCHISE AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON
COUNTY, UTAH AND BEEHIVE TELEPHONE COMPANY, INC (2023)

Mr. Snow stated there is a proposed franchise between Washington County and Beehive
Telephone Company that would be a ten (10) year agreement to use County right-of-ways to get
telephone and broadband service to Kolob.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3160,
A Resolution Approving the Communications Franchise Agreement Between
Washington County, Utah and Beehive Telephone Company, Inc. (2023), with
a direction to legal counsel to remove the bond requirement and to keep the
200-home threshold as stated in the agreement. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3161, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
LEASES FOR LOCAL CONGRESSIONAL OFFICES

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained Senators Mike Lee and Mitt Romney have
offices in Washington County, and desire to move their local offices to the Administration
Building 3" Floor.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3161, A
Resolution Approving Leases for Local Congressional Offices. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3162, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A LEASE OF A PORTION OF THE COUNTY BOULEVARD BUILDING TO THE
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

Ms. Felshaw stated this lease is regarding the federal courts. The County submitted an offer in
May of 2022, and the County has now determined they are willing to lease a portion of the building
once remodeling has been completed. The Justice Court will occupy one floor of the building once
remodeled, with the federal courts occupying the other two.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3162, a
Resolution Approving a Lease of a Portion of the County Boulevard Building
to the United States of America Subject to Attorney Review and Approval.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
