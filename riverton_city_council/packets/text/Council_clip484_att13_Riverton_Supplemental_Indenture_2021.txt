                     SUPPLEMENTAL INDENTURE OF TRUST

        This Supplemental Indenture of Trust, dated as of December 1, 2021, by and
between Riverton City, Utah, a political subdivision and body politic duly organized and
existing under the Constitution and laws of the State of Utah (the “City”) and Zions
Bancorporation, National Association, a national banking association duly organized and
existing under the laws of the United States of America, authorized by law to accept and
execute trusts and having its principal office in Salt Lake City, Utah (the “Trustee”);

                                 W I T N E S S E T H:

       WHEREAS, the City has entered into a General Indenture of Trust, dated as of
February 1, 2000, as heretofore supplemented (the “General Indenture”) with the Trustee,
as supplemented by this Supplemental Indenture of Trust (the “Supplemental Indenture”
and together with the General Indenture, the “Indenture”); and

        WHEREAS, the City previously issued its Water Revenue Bonds, Series 2020A in
the total principal amount of $5,491,000 (the “Series 2020A Bonds”) pursuant to the
General Indenture and an Eighth Supplemental Indenture of Trust dated as of January 1,
2020 by and between the City and the Trustee; and

        WHEREAS, the City has also previously issued its Water Revenue Bonds, Series
2020B in the total principal amount of $11,985,000 (the “Series 2020B Bonds”) pursuant
to the General Indenture and a Ninth Supplemental Indenture of Trust dated as of April 1,
2020 by and between the City and the Trustee; and

      WHEREAS, the Series 2020B Bonds were purchased by the State of Utah
Department of Natural Resources, Board of Water Resources (the “Board of Water
Resources”); and

       WHEREAS, the Series 2020A Bonds and Series 2020B Bonds were both issued to
fund the same project consisting of improvements to the City’s water system (the
“System”); and

       WHEREAS the City desires to reallocate funds which have been drawn between
the Series 2020A Bonds and Series 2020B Bonds and to redeem the outstanding Series
2020B Bonds after such reallocation; and

        WHEREAS, the Board of Water Resources is the Registered Owner of 100% in
aggregate Principal amount of the Outstanding Series 2020B Bonds and has consented to
the reallocation of the amounts which have been drawn on the Series 2020B Bonds and the
defeasance of the Series 2020B Bonds upon the terms and conditions herein set forth,
which consent is attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

        WHEREAS, Section 9.1 of the General Indenture provides that the City and Trustee
may enter into an indenture supplemental to the General Indenture for the purpose of
making a change that in the judgement of the Trustee, is not materially prejudicial to the
interests of the Registered Owners, the Trustee or any Reserve Interest Provider; and
       WHEREAS, in the judgement of the Trustee, this Supplemental Indenture is not
materially prejudicial to the interests of the Registered Owners, the Trustee or any Reserve
Instrument Provider; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to the Indenture, the Series 2020B Bonds may be redeemed
at any time, in whole or in part, at the election of the City; provided that the City meets the
notice requirements of the Indenture, and such notice has been waived by the Board of
Water Resources and such waiver is attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to a resolution of the City dated December 14, 2021, (the
“Resolution”), and the Indenture which authorizes the redemption and defeasance of the
Series 2020B Bonds, the City has the authority to redeem the Series 2020B Bonds for the
purposes set forth above;

       NOW, THEREFORE, THIS SUPPLEMENTAL INDENTURE OF TRUST
WITNESSETH, that to reallocate funds drawn between the Series 2020A Bonds and Series
2020B Bonds, and to redeem the Series 2020B Bonds, and intending to be legally bound
hereby, the City has executed and delivered this Supplemental Indenture of Trust.

                   SUPPLEMENTAL INDENTURE; DEFINITIONS; AMENDMENT

                     Supplemental Indenture.       This Supplemental Indenture is
supplemental to, and is executed in accordance with and pursuant to, Article IX of the
General Indenture.

                       Definitions. All terms which are defined in the Indenture, shall have
the meanings, respectively, when used herein (including the use thereof in the recitals and
the granting clauses thereof) unless expressly given a different meaning or unless the
context clearly otherwise requires. All terms used herein which are defined in the recitals
hereto shall have the meanings therein given to the same unless the context requires
otherwise and, in addition, the following terms shall have the meanings specified below:

         “Redemption Date” means December 15, 2021.

         “Series 2020A Bonds” means the City’s $5,491,000 Water Revenue Bonds, Series
2020A.

         “Series 2020B Bonds” means the City’s $11,985,000 Water Revenue Bonds, Series
2020B.

        “Series 2020B Redemption Account” means the account by that name established
in Section 2.1 herein.

                                    ARTICLE II

             APPLICATION OF PROCEEDS AND FUNDS AND ACCOUNTS

                       Creation of Series 2020B Redemption Account. There is hereby
established with the Trustee a Series 2020B Redemption Account within the Bond Fund.

                       Reallocation of Bond Draws. The Trustee is hereby instructed to
reallocate bond draws in the total amount of $2,765,971.45 from the Series 2020B Bonds
to the Series 2020A Bonds and transfer the matching amount of $2,765,971.45 from the
Series 2020A Construction Account to the Series 2020B Construction Account to
effectuate such reallocation.

                      Application of Funds in the Series 2020B Redemption Account.
The City shall deposit with the Trustee legally available funds in the amount of
$1,005,820.59, which shall be deposited to the Series 2020B Redemption Account, along
with $2,765,971.45 transferred from the Series 2020B Construction Account and
$234,749.07 transferred from the Series 2020B Account of the Bond Fund, for a total of
$4,006,541.11. The Trustee shall apply said moneys on December 15, 2021 (the
“Redemption Date”) to redeem the Series 2020B Bonds.

                        Redemption of the Series 2020B Bonds. Funds in the Series 2020B
Redemption Account are hereby irrevocably deposited with the Trustee in accordance with
Section 2.3 herein, which moneys, without regard to investment earnings thereon, are
sufficient to pay the principal of, premium, if any, and interest on, the Series 2020B Bonds
on the Redemption Date.

                                      ARTICLE III

                       CONFIRMATION OF GENERAL INDENTURE

        As supplemented by this Supplemental Indenture, and except as provided herein,
the General Indenture is in all respects ratified and confirmed, and the General Indenture
and this Supplemental Indenture shall be read, taken and construed as one and the same
instrument so that all of the rights, remedies, terms, conditions, covenants and agreements
of the General Indenture shall apply and remain in full force and effect with respect to this
Supplemental Indenture, and to any revenues, receipts and moneys to be derived therefrom.

                    Confirmation of Refunding of Series 2020B Bonds. The redemption
of the Series 2020B Bonds as described in Section 2.3 2.4 herein, is hereby ratified,

                        Severability. If any provision of this Supplemental Indenture shall
be held or deemed to be or shall, in fact, be inoperative or unenforceable as applied in any
particular case in any jurisdiction or in all jurisdictions, or in all cases because it conflicts
with any other provision or provisions hereof or any constitution or statute or rule of public
policy, or for any other reason, such circumstances shall not have the effect of rendering
the provision in question inoperative or unenforceable in any other case or circumstance,
or of rendering any other provision or provisions herein contained invalid, inoperative, or
unenforceable to any extent whatever. The invalidity of any one or more phrases,
sentences, clauses or sections in this Supplemental Indenture contained, shall not affect the
remaining portions of this Supplemental Indenture, or any part thereof.

                       Counterparts. This Supplemental Indenture may be simultaneously
executed in several counterparts, each of which shall be an original and all of which shall
constitute but one and the same instrument.

               Applicable Law. THIS SUPPLEMENTAL INDENTURE SHALL
BE GOVERNED EXCLUSIVELY BY THE APPLICABLE LAWS OF THE STATE OF
UTAH.

                    Effective Date. This Supplemental Indenture shall become effective
immediately upon execution.
