              SUMMARY:      Bryon Prince, Ivory Development, is requesting a zoning map amendment for the
                            property located at 4202 S. Riverboat Road from Industrial (ID) to Mixed-Use (MU).
                            The Taylorsville zoning map applies land use and intensity classifications established in
                            the Land Development Code (Title 13) to every property in the City. The City Council,
                            at their discretion, may amend the zoning map that it considers appropriate and that are
                            in the best interest of the City and its residents (per Section 13.05.010: Purpose).
                            The applicant is seeking a zoning map amendment with the intention to develop the
                            property with approximately 142 townhome units, a playground, multi-purpose field,
                            and pedestrian enhancements to the Jordan River Parkway trail adjacent to the project.
                            The applicant’s original proposal included two commercial buildings each with a 20,000
                            square foot footprint and a fitness/daycare center; however, the applicant has eliminated
                            the nonresidential components from the concept development plan. Exhibit E includes
                            the most recent development concept illustrating proposed development if this zoning
                            map amendment request were approved by the City Council.
                            The General Plan map designation for the subject property is currently “Business Park.”
                            The applicant has submitted a concurrent application (File #2G20) to amend the General
                            Plan map designation from Business Park to Medium-Density Mixed-Use. The proposed
                            concept development (Exhibit E) is not consistent with the current Business Park or
                            proposed Medium-Density Mixed-Use designations.

                            The Planning Commission forwarded a negative recommendation to the City Council
                            for the zoning map amendment request on September 8, 2020 (see Exhibit I). The
                            Planning Commission encouraged the applicant to work with city staff to submit an SSD
                            rezone request in lieu of using the Mixed-Use zoning designation.

 STAFF RECOMMENDATION:      Staff recommends a negative recommendation of Ordinance No. 21-04 based on the
                            following findings:

                                    •   The applicant’s proposed concept development plan (Exhibit E) does not
                                        include a nonresidential component; therefore, the Mixed-Use (MU) zoning
                                        district designation is not appropriate.

2021; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.

proposal to amend the City’s zoning map from Industrial (ID) to Mixed-Use (MU) for 24.30 acres
of property at 4202 S. Riverboat Road, a legal description of which is attached as Exhibit “A’ and
incorporated by this reference, is hereby approved.

Summary:
Bryon Prince, representing Ivory Development, is requesting a Zoning Map amendment to
rezone the property at 4202 S. Riverboat Road from Industrial (ID) to Mixed-Use (MU). The
applicant is seeking this Zone Map amendment to allow for the development of 142
townhome units, two commercial buildings each with a 20,000 square foot footprint, a
fitness/daycare center, a playground, and a multi-purpose field. A conceptual site plan
illustrating the proposal is included as an exhibit in this report.

Site Description
The subject property is 24.30 acres and is currently undeveloped. There are several
constraints impacting the potential future development of this property. A primary area of
concern for Taylorsville City and Unified Fire Authority is limited vehicle and pedestrian
access. The only vehicular access to the subject property is via Riverboat Road that
terminates at the southern edge of the property. There are few opportunities to improve
connectivity to the site because the property is bounded by the Jordan River to the north
and east and a Salt Lake County-owned golf course to the west. The subject property has
one means of ingress and egress via Riverboat Road posing a significant public safety
concern. Pedestrians may access the property via Riverboat Road or the Jordan River
Parkway. Prior to authorizing future development for this property, the City requires that a
second means of ingress/egress be provided.

The property is within in a “high potential” liquefaction zone and a FEMA 100-year flood
zone. Portions of the property immediately adjacent to the Jordan River are included in the
FEMA regulatory floodway. High-power transmission lines and an associated easement also
traverse the length of the parcel along the western edge. Surrounding zoning designations
are as follows:

 North                  South                   East                   West
 Murray City            RM-12                   Murray City            Open Space (OS) and
                                                                       Research and
                                                                       Development (RD)

General Plan Analysis
The Proposed Land Use Map in the General Plan sets the foundation for all land uses within
the City of Taylorsville. The land use plan identifies general land use classifications and in
some cases the intensity within the classification. Each land use classification on the
Proposed Land Use Map corresponds with compatible classifications on the official
Taylorsville Zoning Map.

The subject property is designated as “Business Park” on the Proposed Land Use Map. The
General Plan includes the following statement describing the intent of the Business Park
designation (pg 3-4):

       “The Business Park (BP) designation differs from Professional Office (PO) in
       that traditional industrial uses are permitted, often in “hybrid’ space for
       companies and organizations that do not fall neatly into one category of use.
       Uses may include headquarters management, research laboratories,
       prototype development, assembly and fabrication, light-manufacturing, and
       warehouse distribution facilities.”

The applicant’s proposal for a mixed-use development including commercial and residential
land uses is not consistent with the adopted policies and goals in the City’s General Plan for
this property. To address this, the applicant has submitted a separate General Plan Map
amendment application, concurrent with the rezone request, to amend the Proposed Land
Use Map designation from “Business Park” to “Medium-Density Mixed-Use.”

The General Plan includes the following statement describing the intent of the Mixed-Use
designation (pg 3-5):

       “Mixed use zones can contain a mix of land uses, such as residential,
       commercial and office within the same area. Land uses in mixed use zones
       may be varied either vertically (i.e. mix of uses in one building such as an
       apartment over a retail establishment) or horizontally (i.e. single use
       buildings with different uses placed next to each other). The Medium
       Density Mixed Use allows a residential density of up to 8.9 units per acre in a
       mixed use setting.”

While the development proposal meets the intent of the Medium-Density Mixed-Use
designation, the proposed Zoning Map amendment cannot be approved by the City Council
until the request is consistent with the City’s General Plan.

Additionally, the subject property is within one of several designated master plan study

                    4202 South Riverboat Road from “Industrial (ID)” to “Mixed-Use (MU).” Jim Spung/AICP/Senior
                    Planner)


      5.3 Mr. Spung presented this item. He stated that this is a similar request for the same property as Item #4 but is
          for a rezone from Industrial to Mixed Use. A negative recommendation is suggested because this change is
          not consistent with the adopted General Plan.

      5.4 FINDINGS:
          5.4.1. This application was initiated by Bryon Prince on behalf of Ivory Development.
          5.4.2  The applicant is requesting a zoning Map amendment to rezoning the property at 4202 S. Riverboat
                 Road (Parcel 21-02-226-007-0000) from Industrial (ID) to Mixed-Use (MU).

          5.4.3      The proposed concept plan includes 142 townhome units, two commercial buildings each with
                     20,000 square foot footprint, a stand-alone fitness/daycare center, a playground, and a multi-
                     purpose field.
          5.4.4      The subject property is within the “Business Park” designation in the City’s General Plan Proposed
                     Land Use Map.
          5.4.5      The proposed zoning Map amendment is not consistent with the adopted General Plan.
          5.4.6      The proposed concept plan does not comply with the Mixed-Use (MU) zoning district development
                     standards established in Section 13.23.260 of the Taylorsville Land Development Code.
          5.4.7      The City Council is the decision-making authority for a Zoning Map amendment request and may
                     adopt or reject the amendment as it deems appropriate pursuant to Section 13.05.030 and other
                     applicable sections of the Taylorsville City Code.

    5.5              STAFF RECOMMENDATION: Staff recommends the Planning Commission send a negative
                     recommendation to the City Council for File #8Z20 to amend the Zoning Map for the property at
                     4202 S. Riverboat Road from Industrial (ID) to Mixed-Use (MU), based on the Findings as outlined in
                     the Staff Report.

    5.6              Commissioner McElreath opened the public hearing and since no one came forward to speak in
                     favor or opposition, closed the public hearing and opened it for discussion or a motion by the
                     Planning Commission.

    5.7              MOTION: Commissioner Wendel - I will make a motion. I move that we send a negative
                     recommendation to the City Council for a zoning amendment for the property at 4202 South
                     Riverboat Road from Industrial (ID) to Mixed-Use (MU), File #8Z20, based on the Findings in the
                     Staff Report.
                     SECOND: Commissioner Barbieri
                     VOTE: Commissioner Barbieri – AYE, Commissioner Wright – AYE, Commissioner Quigley – AYE,
                     Commissioner McElreath – AYE, Commissioner Burggraaf – AYE, Commissioner Wendel – AYE,
                     Commissioner Willardson – AYE. Motion passes unanimously.

                                                    ZONING TEXT AMENDMENTS


     6.           10Z20 - Recommendation to the City Council for a Zoning Text Amendment to the Taylorsville Municipal
                  Code Sections 13.20.020; R-1 and R-2 Districts Table of Setbacks, Building Heights, Off-Street Parking;
                  and Section 13.26.080, Standards for Permanent Signs allowed Without a Permit. Jim
                  Spung/AICP/Senior Planner 3:05:09

   6.1 Mr. Spung presented this item. The proposed amendment will make changes to the Taylorsville Municipal
       Code as follows:

          6.1.1      Section 13.20.020: Reduce the minimum rear yard building setback for primary structures in the R-1-
                     10, R-1-8 R-1-7, R-1-6, R-1-5, R-2-10, and R-2-8 zoning districts from 20 feet to 15 feet.

          6.1.2      Section 13.26.080: Increase the maximum height for flagpoles in commercial and industrial zoning
                     districts from 60 feet, or the maximum height of a structure allowed in the zoning district, whichever
                     is less, to a fixed 80-foot maximum. It would also place the maximum height in the residential
                     districts at a maximum of 35 feet.

    6.2 FINDINGS:
        6.2.1  This application was initiated by the City of Taylorsville
        6.2.2  The City is requesting a text amendment to the Taylorsville Municipal Code Sections 13.20.020; R-1
               and R-2 Districts Table of Setbacks, Building Heights, Off-Street Parking; and Section 13.26.080:
               Standards for Permanent Signs Allowed Without Permit.
