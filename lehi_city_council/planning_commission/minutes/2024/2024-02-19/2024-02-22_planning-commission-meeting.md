3.1 Public hearing and consideration of James Whitmore’s requests for conditional use
approval of the Whitmore Vehicle Storage located at approximately 4300 North Digital
Drive in the Light Industrial zone.

Applicant James Whitmore was there for any questions.


Chairman Jackson- opened the public hearing.
Chairman Jackson- closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Eyre arrived late to the meeting and chose to abstain from this item since he was
unable to hear the information pertaining to this applicant’s request.

    Motion: Commissioner Lockhart moved to approve James Whitmore’s requests for
    conditional use approval of the Whitmore Vehicle Storage located at approximately 4300
    North Digital Drive in the Light Industrial zone; with the following that they provide protection
    against the existing power pole and that the vehicle storage use cannot extend outside this
    parcel boundary, that they maintain the fence/barb wire that they put up, and that all the
    vehicles stored on the planned community property be removed. That they also come back in
    a year to confirm that they have been compliant with the conditional use; including the DRC
    comments; with the findings as listed in the staff report. The motion was seconded by
    Commissioner Everett.

    Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Eyre, abstained. The motion passed unanimously.

3.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Mark Skousen’s request for review of the JD XIII
zone change on 1.01-acres located at approximately 1070 West State Street changing the
zoning from Mixed Use to C-H (Heavy Commercial).

Commissioner Kunze had a question about the parcel to the south and if it was an existing resident.
The staff confirmed that it is a house, and it would be right next to them. The area is zoned mixed
use but planned to change to commercial.

The applicant owns all the parcels that are subject to the zone change tonight.

Staff and Commissioners discussed the buffer and other concerns about changing the use of the
homes to the south that may be negatively impacted.

Josh Moffitt was there on behalf of the applicant and was available for questions.

Chairperson Jackson opened the public hearing.
Chairperson Jackson closed the public hearing.

There was discussion about a 4th smaller parcel that had been left off and if this was its own parcel
and why it was left off. It seems that there was no main reason for it being left off, it was just a
small parcel. The commissioners decided to include the small parcel in the zone change to not
create any islands or inconsistencies in the area.

    Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to send a positive recommendation for Mark Skousen’s
    request for review of the JD XIII zone change on 1.01-acres located at approximately 1070
    West State Street changing the zoning from Mixed Use to C-H (Heavy Commercial). Parcel
    120018-19 will also be included in this zone change to not create any islands in zoning;
    including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in the staff report. The motion was
    seconded by Commissioner Kunze.
    Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.11 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code amendment to Chapter 11, Application Requirements, to include all
common and limited common areas in condo and townhome projects as public utility
easements to allow access for fiber/telecommunications.

Chairperson Jackson opened the public hearing.
Chairperson Jackson closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Everett had a clarifying question for staff. They discussed adding to 9f "as long as
the structures don't encroach on any existing public utilities. "

Motion: Commissioner Lockhart moved to send a positive recommendation for Lehi City’s
request for review of a Development Code amendment to Chapter 11, Application Requirements,
to include all common and limited common areas in condo and townhome projects as public utility
easements to allow access for fiber/telecommunications; with the addition to 9f "as long as the
structures don't encroach on any existing public utilities;” including the DRC comments; with the
findings as listed in the staff report. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Kunze.

    Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
