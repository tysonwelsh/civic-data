ACTION ITEMS:

1. APPROVAL OF AN AGREEMENT WITH OGDEN VALLEY LAND TRUST FOR A DONATION

OF $50,000 TO THE OGDEN VALLEY LAND TRUST FOR THE PURCHASE OF A MONASTERY

CONSERVATION EASEMENT IN CONJUNCTION WITH A FEDERAL GRANT FROM THE

NRCS CONSERVATION EASEMENT PROGRAM ON APPROXIMATELY 1,080 ACRES THAT

WAS ONCE HOME TO THE MONASTERY KNOWN AS ABBEY OF OUR LADY OF THE HOLY

TRINITY.

John Bond, Weber County Treasurer, April 2020 meeting with members of the Board of Ogden Valley Land
Trust agreeing to pay remainder of needed $300k. Mother Teresa visited the abbey for a retreat; 7 years later
she would receive the Nobel Peace Prize. Weber County will donate $50k contingent on first raising the other
$250k by November 1, 2021. Over 257 donations received and the goal was met.

Ruby Raccasi, Ogden Valley Land Trust Chair, thanked the county for their support.

Greg Booth, Ogden Valley Land Trust, it’s an honor to represent the Land Trust. Without the $50k from the
County, preserving this open space would not be possible.

Commissioner Froerer: Land Trust does a great job preserving land in Weber County. “I was born and raised
a half mile from this site. Some of the monks I remember were some of the most kind and warm hearted people
that you could ever hope to meet. Truly benevolent people that I still remember to this day.”


Commissioner Froerer moved to approve an agreement with Ogden Valley Land Trust for a donation of $50,000
to the Ogden Valley Land Trust for the purchase of a Monastery Conservation Easement in conjunction with a
federal grant from the NRCS Conservation Easement Program on approximately 1,080 acres that was once
home to the monastery known as Abbey of Our Lady of the Holy Trinity; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye

2. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 37-2021 OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER
COUNTY ESTABLISHING A PUBLIC DEFENDER BOARD.

Bryan Baron, Attorney, explained background on discussions leading to formation of this new board that will
hire and oversee the Director of Public Defenders’s office.

Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve resolution 37-2021 of the County Commissioners of Weber County
establishing a Public Defender Board; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye
