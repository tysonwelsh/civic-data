AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL CREATING
CHAPTER 2.85 OF THE MAGNA CODE OF ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING AND
REGULATING THE USE OF ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES FOR THE EFFECTIVE
AND EFFICIENT PROCESSING OF CERTAIN DOCUMENTS FOR MAGNA

The Magna Metro Township Council does hereby ORDAIN as follows:

RECITALS

1. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
401 et seq.

2. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of
Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

3. Utah Code § 46-4-201 allows for the use of electronic signatures on legally binding
documents and contracts, unless otherwise disallowed.

4. The Utah State Legislature and Government Agencies uses electronic signatures for the
effective and efficient processing of legislation and other legal and administrative work of
the State.

5. Municipal Code Online is a Corporation that hosts the Magna Code of Ordinances, and
among the features of its software can allow for the secure use of electronic signatures in the
approval of Ordinances pertaining to changes, additions, and/or repeals of Magna’s Code.

6. Certain legal and business documents processed by the administrative agents of Magna
require the efficiency and effectiveness provided by using electronic signatures.

Chapter 2.85 — Electronic Signatures

7. It would be a benefit to Magna and its residents to use this regulate and use feature.
RESOLUTION
BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council does hereby adopt the
following ordinance, to be added to the Magna Metro Township Code of Ordinances:
CHAPTER 2.85 — ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES
2.85.010 — Purpose
The ordinance codified in this chapter regulates the use of electronic signatures by elected
officials and other parties in approving legislation, contracts, and/or other legally-binding
documents of Magna.
2.85.020 — Uses of Electronic Signatures Allowed
In any document accepted by Magna in which a signature is required or used, Magna may
authorize the use of an electronic signature so long as it complies with the requirements of the
most currently adopted version of Utah Code § 46-4: Uniform Electronic Transactions Act.
2.85.030 — Rules Regarding Force and Effect of Electronic Signatures
The use of an electronic signature shall have the same force and effect as the use of a “wet” or
manual signature if:
A. The signature is capable of verification;
B. The signature is under the sole control of the person using it; and
C. The signature is linked to the data in such a manner that it is readily ascertainable if the
data is changed after the signature is applied.
2.85.040 — Determination of Approved Technologies, Vendors, and Uses of Electronic
Signatures
Upon the recommendations of Staff, the Council shall determine and approve acceptable
technologies and vendors under this section consistent with industry best practices to ensure the

and recommend to the Council the documents for which Magna will accept electronic signatures.
2.85.050 — Enactment Date
