                       Chief Jason Mazuran, Unified Police Department (UPD), presented on the
reorganization of UPD. He explained that recent legislation removed the County Sheriff as CEO
of UPD. The Sheriff will go back to Salt Lake County and start a new law enforcement bureau. It
will have a Board and a CEO. He emphasized that the term “UPD 2.0” is misleading because
UPD has not been dissolved; it is being reorganized. The Board is anticipating a 7 percent
increase to offset the loss of the County. He showed a current organization chart, but stated it

Off Premises Beer Sales

                      Jennifer Bowen Crocket, Legal Counsel, stated part of Copperton’s Code
states any alcohol sales must comply with the State Code. Recently, the State decided there
would be no limits on when convenience stores and grocery stores could sell beer. If the Council
sees that as a problem, it needs to be addressed in the revisions. Otherwise, it can just go along
with the State Code. Essentially, Copperton can have laws that are more restrictive than the
State, but not more lenient.

                      Council Member Stitzer, seconded by Council Member Severson, moved
to keep the off-premises beer sales in concurrence with what is mandated by the State. The
motion passed unanimously.
