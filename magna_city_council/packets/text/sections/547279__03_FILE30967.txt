Planning Staff Recommendation: denial of proposed façade design


                                              PROJECT DESCRIPTION
This is a request for approval of a Conditional Use PUD Site Plan to allow for construction of a retail building and a
restaurant site. This property is part of the Flangas Crossing PUD subdivision, as well are the Qquirrh Hills Apartments, the
Burger King, the Family Dollar store and the vacant lots to the south of the Family Dollar along 8400 West.
****Review of north elevation façade along Magna Main Street.

                               SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located west of the Burger King property on the corner of Wikiup Drive and Magna Main Street.



                         PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS/RECOMMENDATION
At the October 10th, when this application received preliminary approval, the topic of the north facing façade along Magna
Main Street was discussed. The discussion was regarding whether the façade could be changed to look more like a store
front rather than the back of a building, since it is positioned along Magna Main Street. One of the main options that was
discussed was possibly adding faux windows to look like a store front side.

The applicant has come back with a very similar look as his original submission. Staff does not think that this is a significant
change to what the Planning Commission wanted.

Staff has designed a proposed look and thinks the Planning Commission should consider this for the façade.

Planning Staff is recommending denial of the façade proposed by the applicant.
