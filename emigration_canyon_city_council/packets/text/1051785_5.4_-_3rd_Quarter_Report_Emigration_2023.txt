SPAYGHETTI GALA
OUR SPAYGHETTI & NO BALLS GALA IS OUR
LARGEST ANNUAL FUNDRAISING EVENT TO
RAISE MONEY FOR OUR LIFE SAVING
PROGRAMS!

This year’s gala was held at the Salt Lake City Hilton
and featured:
   Adoptable puppies and kittens on site
   Photo booth with a professional Photographer
   Buffet style dinner
   Mini games & Pet Psychic
   Live & silent auction prizes
   Live entertainment

Through the support of our community, contract
partners, sponsors, and donors we raised nearly
$130,000!
PETAPALOOZA
IN PARTNERSHIP WITH BEST FRIENDS ANIMAL
SOCIETY ANIMAL SERVICES HOSTED OUR LARGEST
ANNUAL ADOPTION EVENT IN HOPES OF UNITING
HOMELESS PETS IN SHELTERS WITH THEIR
FOREVER HOMES.

This year’s Petapalooza adoption event was held at
Wheeler farm and featured:
   Hundreds of adoptable dogs, cats, & rabbits
   Over 50 vendors
   Live entertainment
   Food trucks
   Photo booth
   Course-A-Lure

This was our most successful Petapalooza as 173
pets from various shelters and rescues found their
forever homes!
