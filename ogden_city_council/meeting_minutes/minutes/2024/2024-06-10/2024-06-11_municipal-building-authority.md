Proposed Resolution 2024-2 adopting the budget for the Municipal Building
Authority for Fiscal Year 2024-2025
         A memo from the Management Services Department regarding the proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2024-2025 budget came
before the Board for consideration. The memo stated on May 14, 2024, the Administration presented the Trustees with a proposed
Fiscal Year 2024- 2025 Municipal Building Authority (MBA) budget. The board set a public hearing for consideration of this for
June 11, 2024.
         Board Policy Analyst Burton summarized the memo and stated the current proposal is for Trustees to consider adopting
the Fiscal Year 2024-2025 MBA budget totaling $3,678,500, as proposed. The budget maintains a balance of $5,000 for any
expenses related to financial reporting and the remainder is debt service for WonderBlock parking infrastructure.
          Chair Richey introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2024-2, entitled:
                  “A resolution of the Board of Trustees of the Ogden City Municipal Building Authority adopting the annual
                  budget for Fiscal year 2024-2025.”
         Chair Richey then opened the public hearing.
         There being no persons appearing to be heard, TRUSTEE HYER MOVED THE PUBLIC HEARING BE CLOSED.
MOTION WAS SECONDED BY TRUSTEE GRAF, ALL VOTING AYE.
