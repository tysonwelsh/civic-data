A. CONSIDER A PRELIMINARY SUBDIVISION PLAT FOR A 16-LOT
SUBDIVISION LOCATED AT 127-149 EAST 7100 SOUTH

Jana Ward said Chris Knapp, representing Ridgeway Construction, is requesting

approval of a preliminary subdivision plat approval to reconfigure four existing parcels

located at 127-149 East 7100 South into a sixteen-lot subdivision with four units in each
of the four new buildings. A Small Scale Master Plan for this project was approved by the

Planning Commission on May 13, 2020.

The property is zoned Single Family Residential with Duplex Overlay (SF-1/DO). The

combined parcels are approximately 1.2 acres with 280 feet of frontage along 7100 South.

There is an existing single-family home on each existing lot. This proposal will require the

demolition and removal of the existing homes and all accessory structures. All new

development will be required to comply with the standards of the SF-1/DO zone, and Title

16, Subdivisions.

Staff has found the proposed subdivision generally complies with the design standard

requirements of Title 16 Subdivisions, with the exceptions listed below.

1. Section 16.04.040(A)(3) requires that 2”-caliper deciduous street trees be planted

L with any new subdivision at a rate of one tree for every 30 feet of frontage. This
requirement will apply to the access road also. Based on this requirement, nine (9)
deciduous street trees are required along 7100 South, and two (2) trees on each
side of the access road. Trees must be planted or guaranteed by a cash bond at
the time new units receive the Certificates of Occupancy. Staff recommends
including this requirement as condition of approval.

as Section 16.04.040(A)(5) requires all new private streets comply with the street
lighting standards, including streetlights placed at intersections and no less than
one streetlight per every one hundred fifty feet of roadway. Based on this
requirement, one streetlight at the intersection with 7100 S and one streetlight at
the opposite end of the private street will be required to be added to the final plat.
Staff recommends including this requirement as condition of approval.

3. Section 16.04.040(F) requires that duty to serve letters from the water and sewer
providers (Midvale City and Midvalley Improvement District, respectively) be
provided and courtesy notices be sent regarding utility easements to Dominion
Energy, Rocky Mountain Power, Comcast Cable, Utopia, and CenturyLink prior to
final plat approval. Staff recommends including this requirement as a condition of

[ approval.

All major subdivisions require a review and recommendation from the Planning

approve the preliminary subdivision plat for the proposed subdivision with the following

conditions:

1. The applicant shall prepare a final subdivision plat to be reviewed and approved

development. The Final Subdivision shall include the following:

be guaranteed by a cash bond prior to the first certificate of occupancy
being issued for a unit.

b. The applicant shail provide evidence that courtesy notices has been sent to :
Dominion Energy, Rocky Mountain Power, Xfinity, Utopia, and Century Link
regarding the utility easements on the subdivision plat with the Final
Subdivision Plat application.

2. Prior to recording the Final Subdivision Plat, the applicant shall:

a. Obtain duty to serve letters for water and sewer service.

b. Removal of all the existing structures.

Upon City Council approval of the preliminary plat, the applicant will be required to
prepare a final plat accurately reflecting any conditions of approval imposed by the City

Council. The subdivision shall not be considered approved until final action by the City

Council on the final plat at a future date.
