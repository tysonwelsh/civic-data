Study Session

1.0   Training: Land Use Liability & Legislative Update

1.1   City Attorney Mike Barker provided training regarding land use liability,
      emphasized the importance of documenting information related to
      administrative land use decisions, and answered questions from the Council.
      Mr. Barker provided an update regarding the 2023 land use Legislation.

3.0   Closed Session

3.1   Councilmember T. Lowery moved to go into a closed session for the
      purpose of discussing litigation. Councilmember Roberts seconded the
      motion, which passed by unanimous vote (4-0).

Study Session Item 2.0 Council/Manager Reports

2.1   Kellie Challburg provided an update on the Mayor’s Bike Ride scheduled for
      May 13, 2023.



                                                                     Page 3 of 4
2.2   Councilmember T. Lowery and Parks and Recreation Director Rhett Ogden
      spoke of an upcoming Arbor Day celebration scheduled for May 5, 2023.

2.3   City Recorder Laura Oscarson spoke of required timeframes for providing
      information to candidates regarding campaign finance reports and explained
      changes to related State Code. A majority of the Council appeared to be
      comfortable with the City Recorder not sending out an additional electronic
      notice to candidates at the end of a filing period with a reminder about the
      additional 24 hours now allowed by the State.

2.4   Assistant City Manager Bret Millburn provided an update regarding
      preventative measures in anticipation of potential flooding and expressed
      appreciation to everyone involved in the effort.
