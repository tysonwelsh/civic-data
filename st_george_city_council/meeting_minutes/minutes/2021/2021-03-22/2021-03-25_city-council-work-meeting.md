UPDATE AND DISCUSSION ON CITY CAMPUS RENOVATION PROJECT:
     City Manager Adam Lenhard explained a couple of years ago, the City looked at
     expanding City Hall as there is no room for new employees. Plans were originally
     drawn to renovate City Hall, the Police Department, and the City Commons Building;
     however, when Covid began, the projects were put on hold. One of the original
     goals was to try keeping employees together as much as possible. As staff went
     through the plans, they felt this building would be full immediately after the
     renovation. He provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics:
     objectives; constraints; estimated costs; aerial view of the property; and operations
     to remain or relocate.

      The Council discussed the proposal with the consensus being to move forward.

DISCUSSION REGARDING THE NAMING OR DEDICATION OF A SPORTS FACILITY
IN MEMORY OF BILL AND MONA GIVEN:
     Councilmember Smethurst stated he is in favor of this as Mr. Given contributed so
     much to the softball community.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard explained the Canyons Complex has a very strong
      brand. He asked for suggestions on how to incorporate the Given name into the
      current name.

      Mr. McAffee suggested the possibility of naming the championship field after the
      Given’s as the Canyons Complex is a well-known facility. Staff discussed this idea
      with some of the Councilmembers, the consensus at that time was to rename the
      facility to the Bill and Mona Given Fields at the Canyons Complex.

      Councilmember McArthur commented that he is in favor of tying the Given’s name to
      the complex; however, he believes that is a long name.

      Councilmember Smethurst agreed that the name Canyons Complex should remain in
      the name since it is so well known.

      Mayor Randall asked if there are parts of the complex that can be named after the
      Givens, rather than the entire facility.

      Councilmember Hughes agreed with Councilmember McArthur regarding the length
      of the proposed name.

      Councilmember Curtis noted his concern is what precedence would the City be
      setting; however, this one is deserving and worthy of the consideration.

      The Council discussed the request; the consensus is to rename one of the fields to
      the Bill and Mona Givens Legacy Field and include a plaque outlining why the field
      was named after them, noting the field needs to look differently than the other
      fields.

DISCUSSION REGARDING HISTORICAL STATUES:
     Leisure Services Director Shane McAffee provided a PowerPoint presentation covering
     the following topics: historical statues being donated to the City; Carter statue
     desired location; McArthur statue desired location; Brooks statue desired location;
     map of potential locations; picture of statue at Town Square; and donation
     agreement.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman explained over the years, there were no statues in the
      downtown area. Art Around the Corner was instrumental in bringing in the art
      pieces. He mentioned there are other statues such as these in the downtown area.
      As some of the proposed property locations are not owned by the City, he does not
      think the statues will be allowed to be installed. He explained pieces that the City
      has paid to repair pieces placed by Art Around the Corner if they have been damaged

REVIEW OF PARKS PROJECTS:
     Leisure Services Director Shane McAffee provided a handout outlining upcoming park
     projects for Fiscal Years 2022, 2023 and 2024.

      Parks Planning Manager Jeff Peay explained these projects are funded with capital
      improvement funds, park impact funds, bonds, and grants. He outlined the handout
      and the Council discussed prioritizing the projects. With regard to the Halfway Wash
      Trail improvements, some residents of Paradise Canyon are opposed, they would like
      to keep it as is. He asked the Council to review the list and let him know if they
      want to move any of them and get them started sooner.
