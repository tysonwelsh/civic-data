ORDINANCE NO.__________ __________, 2022 
ORDINANCE MODIFYING MEMBERSHIP REQUIREMENTS FOR THE 
SALT LAKE COUNTY BOARD OF HEALTH AND MAKING RELATED 
CHANGES 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE LEGISLATIVE BODY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY, 
UTAH, AMENDING SECTION 9.04.030 OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY 
CODE OF ORDINANCES, 2001, ENTITLED “SALT LAKE COUNTY 
BOARD OF HEALTH – MEMBERSHIP” BY MODIFYING MEMBERSHIP 
REQUIREMENTS FOR THE SALT LAKE COUNTY BOARD OF HEALTH 
The County Legislative Body of Salt Lake County ordains as follows: 
SECTION I. The amendments made herein are designated by underlining the new 
substituted words. Words being deleted are designated by brackets and strike-through. 
SECTION II. Section 9.04.030, of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001 is 
hereby amended to read as follows: 
9.04.030 - Membership.  
The Salt Lake County board of health shall consist of fifteen members residing in Salt 
Lake County, including: a member of the [county council] Salt Lake County Council selected
by the County Council and fourteen other members, appointed by the [county mayor] Salt Lake 
County Mayor with advice and consent of the [c]County [c]Council.
A. Board members shall be appointed on a nonpartisan basis and include geographical
representation from [both the incorporated and unincorporated areas of] throughout the county. 
Members should have knowledge or an interest in public health and environmental matters and 
shall include:
1. A physician or surgeon licensed in the state;
2
2. A dentist licensed in the state;
3. A health care professional licensed in the state;
4. A non-health care professional licensed in the state;
5. Two representatives of the public not representing or connected with the
regulated business community;
6. A representative of the regulated business community;
7. A mayor representing a municipality in the county[;
8. A representative of the unincorporated county].
B. [Any potential conflict of interest of any member shall be disclosed in accordance with
applicable statutes and countywide policies and procedures] Board members shall comply with 
Salt Lake County Ethics Code, Salt Lake County Ordinance, Chapter 2.07, and countywide 
policies regarding conflicts of interest and disclosure statements. 
C. Board members shall serve for a term of three years, and their terms shall be structured,
as possible, so that one-third of the terms of office of those serving on the board expire each 
year. The board shall adopt bylaws governing the election selection of its officers, the 
transaction of its business, and all other necessary matters that are consistent with the Utah 
Local Health Department Act and Salt Lake County Ordinance. [committees, task forces and 
other advisory bodies to the board. The board member’ terms of service shall be governed by 
state law]  . 
SECTION III. This ordinance shall become effective fifteen (15) days after its passage and 
upon at least one publication in a newspaper published and having general circulation in Salt 
Lake County.
