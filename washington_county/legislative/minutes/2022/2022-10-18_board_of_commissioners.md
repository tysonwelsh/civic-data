CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-3010, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_INTERLOCAL _AGREEMENT_BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY _ AND

WASHINGTON CITY FOR TRT FUNDING TO ASSIST IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF
THE WASHINGTON CITY WHEELS PARK

Destination Development Manager Leslie Fonger explained that TRT funds can be used to fund
trails and parks within Washington County. Washington City is requesting funding to assist in the
construction of a non-motorized wheels park. The project is estimated to cost approximately $11
million dollars with $1 million coming from TRT funding.

Washington City Mayor Kress Staheli explained Washington City is preparing to construct a fifteen
(15) acre Washington City Wheels Park which includes a variety of skate features, BMX tracks, a
rock-climbing wall, playground, running track, splash pad, and open space. Construction will begin
in 2023.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Chairman Iverson stated that each local process is different and the Commissioners believe that
voter integrity is important.

Commissioner Almquist stated that the CVR reports could not be available unless the Governor is
seeking it. He expressed confidence that the Washington County elections are secure.

Commissioner Snow stated that the County must follow State laws.

Deputy Attorney Snow stated that you cannot utilize Government Records Access Management
Act (GRAMA) requests to obtain voting records as they are not public records. There are only two
(2) reasons why election records are reopened: in the event of a recount or if there is a judicial

challenge.

Chairman Iverson stated that public comment will continue to be worked through to make sure that
it is as productive as possible.

09/22/2022 @ 1734, Missing Person — Toquerville Falls — Man Hours — 18

Search and Rescue team members responded to a report of a male who had

gone missing after walking back to retrieve his jack from an individual who was
using it to fix their vehicle. The male returned to his vehicle before search

and rescue team members arrived at his location. He declined medical evaluation.

09/23/2022 @ 1910, Missing Person — Virgin River — Man Hours — 28

Search and Rescue Team members responded to SR-9 near the Virgin River

on report of two males who had gone missing after their raft popped while going
down the river. The males were located by SAR team members and returned to their
vehicle. They declined medical evaluation. All SAR members were accounted for

and cleared the scene.
09/24/2022 @ 1210, Injured Hiker — Bone Wash Trail - Man Hours — 20

Search and Rescue team members responded to a female who had a possible
broken ankle on Bone Wash Trail. The female was located and placed in a side by
side. She was then transported to Incident Command and loaded in an ambulance.
All Search and Rescue members were accounted for and cleared the scene.

09/26/2022 @ 1710, Lost Hiker — Red Butte Area, Kolob — Man Hours — 22

Search and Rescue and DPS helicopter Star 7 were deployed to locate a
lost hiker up in the Red Butte area. Star 7 was able to locate the lost party
and taxi them to their groups vehicle and other SAR members. There were no other

issues.

10/04/2022 @ 1031, Injured Hiker —- Lower Babylon — Man Hours — 34

SAR responded to a Garmin Spot activation on the Babylon Trail head. A 61 year old
female had fallen and dislocated her knee cap. SAR, Hurricane EMS, and BLM got her
on a Stokes and carried her back to the ambulance. She was taken to SGRH for

treatment.

10/07/2022 @ 1648, Lost Hikers - Oak Grove — Man Hours — 28

Search and Rescue team members responded to Oak Grove on a report of two

stranded hikers. The husband and wife were out of water and unable to find the trail out.
The hikers were located and assisted back to the trail head where they denied medical. All
SAR units were accounted for and cleared the scene.

10/11/2022 @ 1929, Lost Mtn Biker — Gooseberry Mesa — Man Hours — 22

SAR responded to a lost Mountain Biker on Gooseberry Mesa. IC was set up at the trail
head. SAR deployed and located the individual. They transported him to his camp site.
The individual refused medical services when offered.

44 year old female patient had a severe injury to her finger. I applied pressure to stop the
bleeding. The ambulance arrived and transported the patient to the hospital.

10/13/2022 @ 2258, Lost Hiker - Oak Grove — Man Hours — 26

female had fallen and dislocated her knee cap. SAR, Hurricane EMS, and BLM got her
on a Stokes and carried her back to the ambulance. She was taken to SGRH for

treatment.

10/07/2022 @ 1648, Lost Hikers - Oak Grove — Man Hours — 28

Search and Rescue team members responded to Oak Grove on a report of two

stranded hikers. The husband and wife were out of water and unable to find the trail out.
The hikers were located and assisted back to the trail head where they denied medical. All
SAR units were accounted for and cleared the scene.

10/11/2022 @ 1929, Lost Mtn Biker — Gooseberry Mesa — Man Hours — 22

SAR responded to a lost Mountain Biker on Gooseberry Mesa. IC was set up at the trail
head. SAR deployed and located the individual. They transported him to his camp site.
The individual refused medical services when offered.
10/13/2022 @ 1636, UTV Accident — Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 8

Deputies responded to a reported side by side rollover on Sand Mountain. The
44 year old female patient had a severe injury to her finger. I applied pressure to stop the
bleeding. The ambulance arrived and transported the patient to the hospital.

10/13/2022 @ 2258, Lost Hiker - Oak Grove — Man Hours — 26

SAR responded to a lost hiker in the Oak Grove area. The hiker said he had been hours
trying to find his way out. SAR located him and walked him back to the trail head. They
escorted him back to the main roadway. No medical was needed.
