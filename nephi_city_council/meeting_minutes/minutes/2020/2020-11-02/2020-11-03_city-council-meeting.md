North at 7:00 p.m. Tuesday, November 3, 2020.

Guests: Myrna Trauntvein (Press), Donald W. Ball, Carla Hancock, Al Robb, JD Parady, Jon Hadarlie CPA,
Steve Hart .

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then Councilor Seely
offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

   Al Robb, a veteran from the Vietnam War, led the pledge of allegiance. Mr. Robb then spoke about
his years in the service and his experience of participating in the Honor Flight back to Washington DC to
visit the wartime monuments. Mr. Robb then shared a poem that he had written shortly after 911 the
poem was entitled “I am Freedom”.

  Councilor Kent Jones presented 50 stamped envelopes with a two-dollar bill in each envelope and
invited those in attendance to take three or four envelopes to write a letter to a veteran to thank them
for their service.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes of the public hearings held on 10-27-20, regular council meeting held on 10-20-20, regular
council meeting held on 10-27-20, the work session minutes 10-20-20, the work session minutes 10-27-
20, and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 11-3-20 Councilor Seely seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones reviewed a business license application for Bowler Hat Group. Councilor Jones moved
to approve the business license. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

JON HADERLIE PRESENTED AUDIT REPORT FOR FY 2020 TO COUNCIL:

  Mr. Jon Haderlie, representing Larson and Company, presented to the city council and mayor the
certified annual audit report for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2020. He told of the audit procedures
and their conclusions. He said that there were no findings in the audit and they were offering a “clean”
opinion. The financial statements were free from material misstatement. He presented the mayor and
council with a copy of the financial statements and audit reports.

ORDINANCE 11-3-2020 GUN RANGE ANNEXATION ADOPTED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 11-3-2020: AN ORDINANCE ANNEXING CERTAIN
PROPERTIES TO NEPHI CITY AND ESTABLISHING ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR THE GUN RANGE
PROPERTY. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

  Carla Hancock commented that she appreciated the improvements that had been made at the gun
range. Steve Hart said that last time he was at the range there were people from out of state shooting.
Al Robb said he has had a very difficult time getting time on the range because of non-residents using
the gun range.

NEPHI SOUTH COMMERCIAL PARK PETITION RESCINDED AND NEW ONE ACCEPTED:

   City Recorder Lisa Brough presented a new annexation petition for the Nephi South Commercial Park
Annexation. The petition that was presented at the October 27, 2020 council meeting had been
rescinded. The new petition includes an additional parcel of land that would make the annexation
contiguous with the existing city limits. Councilor Memmott moved to accept the petition for further
consideration. Councilor Jones seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the gas pipeline project stating that the contractor is
finishing up the patching of the road on 2nd North in Mona.
   Mr. Atkinson stated that both water tanks are now nearly full. The south water tank was able to fill
on pressure alone. Street patching projects are being completed throughout the city.
   The Nebo Heights project is on hold until the staff determines if we have enough funding to proceed
with the water line project.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session
to discuss the character and competency of an individual and the acquisition and disposal of real
property. Councilor Jones seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:20 p.m.
