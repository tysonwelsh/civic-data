Subject:         Redline for amendments to the City Council Rules of Procedure




This Redline is in reference to Section “7” of the Sandy City Council Rules of Procedure as originally
adopted on February 21, 2023.



Proposed Amendments Below:



      7. Annual Biennial Training on and Review and Re-adoption of the Rules of Procedure

              a) The City Council shall receive training on and review and readopt its Rules of
                 Procedure, including any amendments, by majority vote, each evenly
                 numbered calendar year no later than the last regularly scheduled Business
                 Session Council Meeting in February.
