Request
 The applicant, Felix Guzman, on behalf of the
 property owner, Ron Mortimer, is requesting approval
 of a conditional use permit for increased building
 height and reduced setbacks for a property located at
 8383 S. Mesa Dr. The proposed structure is 747 square
 feet and 19 feet seven and a half inches in height. The
 proposed setback is three feet from the north side
 property line. The design of the exterior and roof is
 proposed to match the exterior of the main dwelling.
 See application materials for details in Exhibit A and
 Exhibit B.

Background
 The subject property is approximately .23 acres (9,878
 square feet) in the R-1-8 zone. The subject property is
 Lot 13 in the Sunrise Terrace subdivision. The
 property is located within the Sensitive Area Overlay
 Zone, but was developed in the prior to the
 establishment of those requirements for hillside
 protections. Properties to the north, south, east, and
 west are single family residential zoned R-1-8.

Analysis                                                                                                 Approximate location. See site plan in Exhibit B.
 The applicant is proposing to build an accessory structure in the rear yard
 that will be used as a garage and for storage space. The proposed structure is
 747 square feet and 19 feet seven and half inches in height. The structure is
 proposed to be three feet from the north side property line and seven feet
 from the east rear property line. There is an existing shed that will be torn
 down in the place of the new structure. The proposed structure will be
 approximately 22% of the rear yard. The applicant is proposing to match the
 exterior materials with the existing exterior of the main dwelling.

 The subject property is in the Sensitive Area Overlay (SAO) zone with
 slopes exceeding 30%. The property was developed prior to 1978 before city
 ordinances were in place and it has been determined the slopes are man-made and does not need to be protected. There
 will be cuts over 10 feet during construction and the applicant will need to ensure that protection is in place so as not to
 affect any neighboring properties.

 Building Height and Setbacks
 The proposed structure is 19 feet and seven and a half inches tall to peak of roof from finished grade. Properties in the R-
 1-8 zone are allowed to build up to 15 feet in height for an accessory structure. Section 21-11-2(a)(3)(c) of the Sandy City
 Development Code states that a building may be built taller, up to the maximum building height for a permitted dwelling
 within the zone in which it is located, upon receipt of a conditional use permit from the Planning Commission. In Section
 21-11-2(a)(3)(b) it states that a detached structure exceeding 15 feet in height shall increase the minimum setback one foot
 for each one foot of additional height, unless otherwise approved by Planning Commission. The proposed building would
 need to be to be setback seven feet from the rear property line and seven feet from the north side property line. The
 applicant is requesting to have the setback back on the north side property line reduced to three feet. The east rear setback
 would remain seven feet.

  Staff Concerns
  Staff is concerned about the methods of construction that will be used to ensure that the neighboring properties will not
  be negatively affected.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for increased height and reduced
  setbacks and as described in the staff report for the property located at 8383 S. Mesa Dr. based on the following findings
  and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. The proposed structure is consistent with rear yard area coverage for accessory structures.

    2. There is an existing shed that will be torn down.

    3. Staff finds that the proposed structure meets the intent of Section 21-11-2 provided the applicant complies with the
       proposed conditions


Conditions:
   1. That the structure be located as per the enclosed site plan and is built to 747 square feet and 19 feet and seven and a
       half inches high from finished grade to peak of roof. The structure will be sited three feet from the north side
       property line and seven feet from the rear property line.

    2. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.

    3.     That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City Development Code, and all
           conditions of approval imposed by the Planning Commission.

    4. That the property owner obtain temporary construction easements from the neighboring property owners to allow
       for over-excavation of the footing and foundations of the proposed structure; alternatively, the property owner and
       building contractor demonstrate with their building permit application appropriate shoring methods such that the
       construction will not cause damage or disturbance to those surrounding properties and that those methods be
       implemented during construction of the accessory building.
