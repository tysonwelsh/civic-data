COMMUNICATIONS
A.  FORM OF GOVERNMENT DISCUSSION
    Councilmember Whetstone asked if public hearings could be held before the
    passing of the resolution. Nichole Camac, City Recorder, replied yes.
    Councilmember Whetstone asked if those two meetings would count after the
    passing of the resolution. Nichole replied no and indicated that an additional two
    meetings would need to be held. Councilmember Huynh stated that the Council has
    considered controversial items before where many people attended meetings. He
    indicated that this item has been on the agenda for awhile and no one has attended
    the meetings. Councilmember Huynh stated that he plans to campaign beginning
    next week and will be pushing this.

       Councilmember Whetstone stated that the majority of the feedback he has received
       has been to keep the form of government as is. He indicated that he can’t in good
       conscience ignore the comments he has received from his constituents and agree to
       place this on the ballot. Councilmember Whetstone stated that he would like to hear
       more comments from residents and he is fearful that people will vote without
       understanding the full implications. He noted that he likes the current form of
       government personally but he does understand the benefit to allowing residents the
       option to choose but would like more time for education.

       Councilmember Fitisemanu stated that he has reached out to residents in his district
       and has also overwhelmingly heard that a majority of residents like the form of
       government as it is. He indicated that he would not be in favor of adding a resolution
       to the agenda and noted that the timeline is too quick. He indicated that it’s
       important that residents have time to do research, to be properly educated, and to
       be given ample opportunity to speak about the issue. Councilmember Fitisemanu
       stated that the Council has a big responsibility to ensure that a sustained, fair, non-
       objective campaign and educational process be conducted before allowing this type
       of change to be placed on the ballot.

       The Mayor and Council had no further questions or concerns.

NEW BUSINESS
