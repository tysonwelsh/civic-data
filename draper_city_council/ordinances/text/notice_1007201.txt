City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption - Ordinance #1661
On July 1, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1661 amending the text of Titles 6 and 9 of the Draper City Municipal Code related to seasonal farm stands, otherwise known as the City Initiated Seasonal Farm Stand Text Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder's Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
Published this 2nd day of July, 2025
