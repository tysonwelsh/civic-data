   3.1      PUBLIC HEARING – Tentative Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Budget
       The RDA Board will hear public comment concerning the adopted Tentative Fiscal Year

       subject of consideration in the Public Hearing. Approval of the final budget is tentatively
       scheduled for June 9, 2021.

Chair Fullmer called for a motion to open the public hearing.
