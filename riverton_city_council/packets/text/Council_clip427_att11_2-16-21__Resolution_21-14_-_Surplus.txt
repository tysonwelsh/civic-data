                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 21-14

A RESOLUTION TO DECLARE RS-12 AND RS-13 S/P AS SURPLUS, AUTHORIZING
     THE CITY MANAGER TO DISPOSE OF THESE ITEMS AS HE DEEMS
                         APPROPRIATE

       WHEREAS, the identified equipment has already been replaced and/or is no longer
needed; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets that are no
longer needed as surplus in a public meeting.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council declares RS-12 and RS-13 S/P as surplus, authorizing the
          City Manager to dispose of these items as he deems appropriate.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
February 2021 by the following vote:
