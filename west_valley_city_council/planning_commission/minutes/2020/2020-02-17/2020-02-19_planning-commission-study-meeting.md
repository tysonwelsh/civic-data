ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-2-2020 Petition by ERIK HOFFMAN requesting a zone text change to sections 7-1-103,
          7-6-201, 7-6-301 and 7-7 to add Automobile Emission Centers as a conditional
          use in the RB (Residential Business) Zone on properties over an acre. (Staff -
          Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497)

              Discussion: Jody explained that this applicant is looking to amend uses in the RB
              zone to allow automobile emissions and inspections. She explained that the
              emission and inspection use is currently included in the auto services definition.
              Jody explained the applicant is looking to amend the Code because he believes an
              Automobile Emission Testing Centers has less of an impact than other
              Automobile Service businesses. Jody explained another part of this application is
              use-specific regulations with the minimum lot size requirement being a big focus.
              Jody explained that this amendment will affect all RB zones and the agricultural
              and residential use table along with the commercial and manufacturing use table
              that will need to be amended. Commissioner Lovato explained there are different
              ways to perform the emissions and inspections and there is a possibility of noise
            being an issue.

            Jody stated Erik Hoffman does have a particular site in mind and that lot is
            currently zoned RB. The applicant is planning to demolish the existing structure
            and rebuild it. Commissioner McEwen asked Jody if fencing requirements will
            apply to this location. Jody explained approval of the use and site would need to
            come back to the Planning Commission to be reviewed as a conditional use if the
            text amendment were approved. The landscaping, fencing and other ordinances
            would apply at that time. There would be other RB zone type uses constructed on
            the site as well along with the Emmissions Center.

            Commissioner Woodruff asked Jody why there is a one-acre minimum lot size?
            Jody explained this requirement would further limit the locations this use could
            occupy in the RB Zone.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-2-2020 Petition by EA ARCHITECTURE requesting conditional use approval for an
         expansion of warehousing and manufacturing for Mapleleaf Cabinets. The property is
         located at 2554 South 3400 West on 2.5 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff -
         Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497)


            Discussion: Jody explained EA Architecture is looking to add a warehouse and
            workshop. Jody stated the new building additions will not be required to meet the
            Commercial Design Standards. Jody stated their current dust collection system is
            outside of the building and is quite noisy. This addition will place the dust
            collection system inside and help cut down on the noise.

            Jody explained she is adding a requirement to add more trees to meet code. She
            explained there is a gate that will close the back of the building off to the public
            and there is an existing wall separating this building from the surrounding
            residential area. Jody also explained the loading docks are on the east side of the
            building away from residential use and the overhead doors on the west side will
            be used for inventory only. Jody also explained there will not be any outside
            storage due to the product being weather sensitive. Commissioner Woodruff
            asked Jody if the fire department has been involved with this request. Jody
            explained they are involved and were present at the last development meeting.
