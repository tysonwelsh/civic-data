GP-1-2025 Petition by West Valley City requesting a change to the city’s General Plan to update the
City’s Moderate Income Housing Plan. Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-
ut.gov.

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented an update to the City's Moderate Income Housing Plan which is
required by state code and is updated to reflect changes in housing data. The City’s last update to the
plan was adopted in 2022 using 2020 U.S. Census Bureau data. The proposed amendments utilize 2023
Census data that reflect significant increases in home values, rents, and mortgage rates since 2020. Other
amendments include a comprehensive view of all income restricted housing units (not just tax credit
units), and an updated implementation plan to reflect anticipated actions over the next five years.


GP-2-2025 Petition by West Valley City requesting a change to the City’s General Plan to provide a
comprehensive update to the Major Street Plan. Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Steve Pastorik proposed an update to the City’s Major Street Plan based on travel demand
modeling performed by the transportation consulting firm, Parametrix. The reasons for the modeling
were (1) to verify that the street right-of-way widths recommended in the Major Street Plan were still

supported by the latest travel demand modeling; (2) to refresh the 2015 General Plan; and (3) for
updating the City’s transportation impact fees. The plan update includes the removal and addition of
intersection improvements, changes in road lane configurations, and the addition of new streets. The
unintended consequence of these changes would be a reduction in properties eligible for RM zoning, but
a modification to the zoning ordinance would reverse that. The conversation concluded with a
discussion on the fairness of the new plan and its potential impact on property owners.

SMI-3-2025 Grey Garza is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Grove North Subdivision. The
subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 8.8 acres. The proposed location is 7479 West 5400 South and is
zoned M. Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut-gov.

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented on behalf of Steve Lehman. He discussed a subdivision
application for a 2-lot subdivision on 5400 South. The lots would be approximately 4.5 and 4.3 acres in
size, with a new public right-of-way connecting the subdivision to a previously reviewed site. He
discussed the need for roadway improvements and a turnaround for fire access. The proposed site plan
includes industrial buildings, warehousing, and office spaces, and is expected to be used for flex light
industrial warehouse purposes.

There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting was
adjourned at 4:50 PM.
