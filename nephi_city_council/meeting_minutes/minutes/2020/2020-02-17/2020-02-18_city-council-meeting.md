PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Blair Painter representing American Legion Post 1. Mr. Painter
gave a brief history of the American Legion Post 1.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

   Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes of
the regular council meeting held on 2-4-20, work session minutes 2-4-20, work session minutes 2-11-20,
a list of claims on the warrant register dated 2-18-20, and the sale of burial rights to Tom Goble and
Walt Howard. City Attorney noted he was not present at the work session on 2-11-20 and requested
that correction to the work session minutes. that Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

ORDINANCE 02-18-2020 HOME OCCUPATION CODE CHANGES ADOPTED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed some changes to the home occupation code to allow
accessory buildings to be used for certain home occupation businesses that do not require a business
license. Ordinance 02-18-2020 made minor changes to both Title 3 and Title 10 that would legitimize
certain businesses that currently exist and expand the location options for new home-based businesses.
The ordinance also expands the definition of home-based businesses exempt from a business license to
those instructing in physical education courses. Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 02-18-
2020. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 02-18-2020-A CODE GOVERNING CANNABIS PRODUCTION ESTABLISHMENTS AND
PHARMACIES ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that due to the passage of a medical marijuana act by the
State of Utah, the need for local governments to adopt ordinances to locate potential future cannabis
facilities had become necessary. Ordinance 02-18-2020-A restricts where these facilities may locate.
The ordinance also restricts production facilities to industrial zones, requires a permit, restricts distances
from a community location, and establishes standards for cannabis pharmacies and production facilities.
The planning commission and city attorney had reviewed the ordinance. Councilor Jones moved to
adopt Ordinance 02-18-2020-A with a few minor changes by City Attorney Kasey Wright. Councilor
Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ELECTRIC DEPARTMENT OUT-OF-STATE TRAVEL APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed a request from Electric Department Superintendent, Rust Finlinson, for three
out-of-state travel requests. The first request was for the annual Intermountain Power Superintendents
Association in Mesquite, Nevada on March 3-5, 2020. The second request was for a Rocky Mountain
Electric Metering Association in Fort Collins, Colorado on March 16-19, 2020. The third request was for
the American Public Power Association conference in Long Beach, California in June. Councilor Ostler
moved to approve the out-of-state travel requests. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

ABANDONMENT OF EASMENTS APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that with the completion of two recent subdivision
developments, it was necessary to abandon some older easements for infrastructure that had been
replaced with updated infrastructure. CR Circle and Chapel View subdivisions had easements that
needed to be abandoned. Juab Title had prepared documents that will relinquish the easements.
Councilor Jones moved to approve the abandonment of easements for Chapel View and CR Circle
subdivision. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

 Mr. Atkinson reported that the ground-breaking ceremony for the Holiday Inn Express Hotel project
had taken place earlier in the day.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Councilor Jones reported that the RV park on the south end of town has begun moving large amounts
of dirt. He also reported that the meeting with Senator Mike Lee had been impressive.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character
and competence of an individual, litigation. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed on a
unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 7:53 p.m.
