Pre Council, 7:00 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilors
Albrecht and Koivisto attended virtually. Councilor Southwick gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Report
2.1 Tour of the Transportation Control Center
Luke Seegmiller, Traffic Engineer, took the Council on a tour of the new Transportation Control
Cetner.

2.2 HTRZ Funding Discussion
Marlin Eldred presented on the Thanksgiving Point preliminary plan and the process and expenses
that will be incurred during the process. HTRZ is a funding mechanism create by State legislation
that can be used for development near Frontrunner or Trax stations. They are trying to plan for the
infrastructure maintenance cost ahead of time.

2.3 Water Outlook Discussion
Dave Norman presented on the water outlook for this summer. He reported that it’s likely the
City will need to implement restrictions early.

3. Agenda Questions
None.

4. Administrative Report
Kim Struthers reported that the city is hosting an e-waste clean-up event on April 23rd.

5. Mayor and Council Reports
