SUMMARY:

Riverton City code includes a Table of Commercial Uses in Section 18.90.010. The Table of Uses lists
many Land Uses and lists whether each use is Permitted (P), Not Permitted (NP), or Conditional (C) in
each of the six commercial zones. From time to time the Table of Commercial Uses is updated to meet
the current needs of the City.

This amendment addresses "Assisted Living Facilities", "Building Trades", "Bus Terminals", "Landscape
Services", and "Dance Studios".
