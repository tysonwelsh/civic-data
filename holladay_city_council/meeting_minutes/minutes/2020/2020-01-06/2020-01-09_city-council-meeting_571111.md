I.    Welcome – Mayor Dahle.
Mayor Dahle called the meeting to order at 7:02 pm.

II.    Public Comments.
There were none.

III.      Consideration of Resolution 2020-01 Nomination and Appointment of a Mayor Pro-
         tem
Council Member Fotheringham nominated Sabrina Petersen as Mayor Pro-tem and moved to
approve Resolution 2020-01. Council Member Gibbons seconded the motion. The Council roll
call vote was as follows: Council Members Petersen, Durham, Fotheringham, Quinn, Gibbons,
and Mayor Dahle voted in favor. Resolution 2020-01 was approved by a unanimous vote.

IV.    City Manager Report – Manager Chamness
Manager Chamness reported that as of yesterday the Cotton Bottom was closed and the sign had
come down. The sign will be renovated. They will do a walk-through next week and get the
keys. Temporary fencing will also be installed.

V.      Council Reports and District Issues.
Council Member Petersen would like to set up a meeting with administration at Olympus High
and the city to discuss the transition to adding 9th graders. She also reported on an AirBNB on
her street. It is becoming an issue and one the city needs to tackle soon.

Council Member Fotheringham reported on Viking Night at Bonneville Jr. High.

Council Member Quinn reported that she and Chief Hoyal met with the principal at Morningside
Elementary to discuss the traffic/parking issues. Almost ¾ of the students that go there are out of
the area so there are a lot of parents dropping and picking up. They are working on options and
will also reach out the out to the school district.

Council Member Gibbons commented on the new intersection and 6200 S and Holladay Blvd
and how great that is for that area. He also asked about the stonework on the bridge to the west
of the intersection and if that could be matched on both sides of the bridge. Manager Chamness

Mayor Dahle reported that he will be meeting with Dr. Bates and Ben Horsely from the Granite

House on Jan. 18. Finally he thanked staff for the AV upgrades to the Chambers and Mt.
Olympus Room.

VI.   Recess to City Council Work Meeting.
Council Member Fotheringham moved to recess and reconvene in a Work Session. Council
Member Petersen seconded the motion. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the
Council. The Council convened in a work meeting at approximately 7:33 pm

        1.     Open Meetings and Ethics Training
City Attorney, Todd Godfrey provided the yearly open meetings and ethics training requirement
for the City Council. He noted that all email communication is subject to GRAMA as are text
conversations done in a group format.

       2.      Discussion on Committee Assignments for 202
The Council reviewed the list of committee assignments for the year and chose where they
would like to participate. There are two committees that need formal action- the Mosquito Board
and the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District. Council Member Fotheringham will finish
out his term on the Mosquito Board and Council Member Gibbons will be appointed to the
WFWR Board.

        3.      Discussion & Review of Rules of Procedure
        4.      Discussion & Review of Council Internal Policies
Manager Chamness went over the two documents. The Rules of Procedures is how a meeting is
run. The Council didn’t have any questions on that document. These internal policies provide
guidance on a wide variety of topics affecting City Council members, including benefits, actions
and roles. Staff has suggested a number of changes to these policies that are designed to clarify
existing policy for Council consideration. The Council asked for more time to review the
internal policies.

       5. Calendar – Schedule of Upcoming Meetings.
               Jan 23 – Council Mtg
               Jan. 30 – Council Photos & Joint Planning Comm. Work meeting
               Feb. 6 & 20 – Council Mtg
               March 5, 12 & 19 – Council Mtg
It was noted that the Retreat this year will be held in late March or April to align with the budget.

VII.   Closed Session pursuant to Utah Code Section 52-4-204 and 205 to Discuss Personnel
       Issues, Potential Litigation and Property Acquisition and Disposition.

VIII. Adjourn City Council Meeting.
