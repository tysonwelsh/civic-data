                                       Budget Workshop

Citizen Public Input

               Nicole Thompson stated her biggest issue with the budget is the wage increase
for Council Members. Citizens are still paying for two school districts, increased food, and gas
prices, among other inflation issues. The Council wants to increase its wages because it feels it
deserves it; however, she disagrees. She believes Council Members are going to meetings that
can be handled over email. Under council compensation, there is an allocation labeled
“miscellaneous” for $5,400. She is concerned with the lack of information for that allocation and
would like to know what it will be used for.

2023 White City Metro Township Budget Discussion

               Dave Sanderson stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)
provided a spreadsheet outlining the White City Metro Township budget. He has taken the
information provided by the MSD and created a line-item budget document.

               The Council reviewed the following budget items:

    Wages

                  Mr. Sanderson stated the miscellaneous line-item of $5,400 is for the Council to
utilize for training purposes.

               Council Member Perry stated at this point, those funds have not been used.

               Council Member Little asked where the miscellaneous line-item came from.

               Mr. Sanderson stated he included it in case the Council decides to use it. The
Council can eliminate the line-item.

               Council Member Little asked if the Council chooses to compensate for training,
does a resolution or ordinance need to be passed.

              Mayor Flint stated there are other allocations that will need to be increased that
the miscellaneous funds could be used for.

               The Council agreed to reduce the miscellaneous allocation to $0.00.

    Employee Benefits

               Mr. Sanderson stated this allocation is for taxes.

               Paul Ashton stated the miscellaneous allocation should be reduced to $0.00.

    Subscriptions & Memberships

                Mr. Ashton stated the American Institute of Certified Planners (AICP) is a
professional group for planning commissioners. The funds are allocated to pay for membership
fees to that group.

               Later in the meeting.

              Mayor Flint stated membership dues to the Utah League of Cities and Towns
needs to be added. The dues for White City Metro Township are $2,959.92.

    Printing and Publications

               Mayor Flint stated government entities cannot get a nonprofit bulk mailing rate.
The Council will need to decide how many mailers will need to be sent out to the community
during the year.

               Mr. Ashton asked how much mailers cost.

               Mayor Flint stated for printing and mailing is approximately $1,000.

               Council Member Little stated the White City Community Council’s function is to
keep the community updated. He thinks they would be able to send notices to the community and
get the nonprofit bulk mailing rate. The Council could then transfer the funds to pay for the mailer.

               Mayor Flint asked how many mailers will need to be sent out in the year.

               Council Member Cardenaz stated three mailers.

               Mayor Flint stated the cost will be approximately $800 per mailer.

               Council Member Price suggested increasing the line-item amount to $2,500.

    Office Expenses and Supplies

               This allocation will remain the same.

    Attorney – Civil & Land Use

               Paul Ashton stated when determining the budget for attorneys, the Council needs
to take in consideration his rate increase, the land use attorney, and the cost for the White City
Metro Township code codification. He recommended increasing the amount to $75,000.

    Training and Seminars

               This allocation will remain the same.

    Webpage Development

               This allocation will remain the same.

    Software/Streaming

            Council Member Price asked if funds need to be allocated to pay for
GoToMeetings.

               Mr. Ashton stated he has been paying for this service.

    Payroll Processing Fee

               This allocation will remain the same.

    Communications

                Mayor Flint stated she would like to move the newsletter funds to the contribution
line-item to cover the cost for the Community Council to produce the newsletter.

    Contributions/Special Events

                 Mr. Ashton stated the White City Community Council contribution has been split
into two line-items; administrative and Southeast Township Days.

               Mayor Flint stated these current numbers are not correct, the amount should
reflect $32,000.

             Council Member Price suggested allocating $30,000 to Southeast Township
Days and $8,000 for WCTCC Administrative.

    Credit Card and Bank

              Mr. Sanderson stated the amount listed is determined by the Greater Salt Lake

    Insurance

               This allocation will remain the same.

    Workers Comp Insurance

               This allocation will remain the same.

    Postage

               This allocation will remain the same.

    Professional and Technical

              Mr. Ashton stated these funds are for Rori Andreason’s contract, lobbyists,
miscellaneous items like, feasibility studies and a portion of James Woodward’s, UFA, salary. He
does not know why there is a public relations firm line-item.

                 Mayor Flint stated she wants to find out exactly what the public relations firm
allocation is for and if any invoices have been submitted for it.

               Counci Member Cardenaz stated public relations firm was budgeted for but not
used.

              Council Member Perry asked what the difference is between the allocations for
“UFA” for $9,000 and “UFA Emergency Services” for $10,000.

               Mayor Flint stated the $9,000 may be UFA’s CARES reimbursement.

              Mr. Ashton stated he recommended removing the public relations firm allocation,
leave the miscellaneous amount, and clarify what the UFA allocations are.

    UFA Emergency Services

            Mayor Flint stated she believes that this allocation is for a portion James
Woodward, UFA Emergency Services, salary.

    SL (Client) County

                Mr. Ashton stated these amounts are for Clerk services, Surveyor, and elections.
The elections amount will need to be increased to cover the 2023 elections, he suggested raising
the allocation to $15,000.

    Justice Courts Remediation

               Mr. Ashton suggested leaving this allocation as is, until dissolution of the justice
courts is determined.
