WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 16, 2023

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Iverson at | p.m. on August 16, 2023, in the Commission Overflow 111
East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Chairman Adam Snow,
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Steven Scott, County
Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Jayanne Lewis.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION ON PROPOSED COUNTY SALES AND USE TAX FOR EMERGENCY
MEDICAL SERVICES.

Commissioner Snow commenced the meeting discussion with his concerns regarding tax
increases to fund Fire and EMS Service needs throughout the county.

There was considerations about making the increases variable, allowing them to be
individualized for each area. However based on current legislation the bill does not allow
for individual tax rates.

It was questioned if there was interest in a unified fire service. There was little interest
expressed.

Another Work Meeting will be held to continue discussions on the matter.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
2:22 p.m.
