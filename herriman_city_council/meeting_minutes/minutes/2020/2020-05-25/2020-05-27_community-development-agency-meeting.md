                                  COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND
                                RENEWAL AGENCY OF HERRIMAN CITY
                                           MINUTES
                                                  Wednesday, May 27, 2020
                                                   Approved July 28, 2020

The following are the minutes of the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City. The
meeting was held on Wednesday, May 27, 2020 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West
Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City
Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair David Watts

Directors Present:          Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith

Staff Present: City Manager Brett Wood, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Assistant City Manager Wendy
               Thomas, Community Development Director Blake Thomas, Police Chief Troy Carr, City Recorder
               Jackie Nostrom, Public Works Director Justun Edwards, Communications Manager Jonathan
               LaFollette, City Planner Michael Maloy, Unified Fire Authority Precinct Chief Dominic Burchett,
               City Attorney Chase Andrizzi, and Economic Development Director Heather Upshaw

7:00 PM: (OR AS SOON AS POSSIBLE THEREAFTER)
   1. Call to Order
Chair Watts called the meeting to order at 7:08 p.m.

                  1.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes of May
                        27, 2020
Director Shields moved to have the minutes of May 27, 2020 be prepared and distributed to each member of the Community
Development and Renewal Agency Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary,
if after ten days, there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes
are made and the board is in agreement. Director Ohrn seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

    2. Public Hearing
          2.1. Public Hearing for the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City to
               discuss the adoption of the annual budget for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2020 and
               ending June 30, 2021 – Alan Rae, Finance Director
Finance Director Alan Director explained the Community Development and Renewal Agency’s budget has been
incorporated into the Herriman City budget; however, due to being a separate entity, the Board must approve the
budget separately. Three areas are included in this budget: Herriman Towne Centre, Herriman Industrial Park, and
Anthem.

Chair Watts opened the public hearing.

No Comments were offered.

Director Smith moved to close the public hearing. Director Ohrn seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

    3. Adjournment
Director Ohrn moved to adjourn the Community and Development Renewal Agency meeting at 7:31 p.m. Director Shields seconded the
motion, and all voted aye.


I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true, accurate
and complete record of the meeting held on May 27, 2020. This document constitutes the official minutes for the
Community Development and Renewal Agency Meeting.
