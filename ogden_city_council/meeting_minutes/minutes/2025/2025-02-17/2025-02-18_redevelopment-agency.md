Proposed Resolution 2025-1 authorizing the issuance and sale of the
agency's sales tax and tax increment revenue bonds
         A memo from the Management Services Department regarding a proposed Bond Parameters Resolution for the purchase
of the U.S. Forest Service Building came before the Board for consideration. The memo stated on December 10, 2024, the Ogden
City Redevelopment Agency Board approved several actions to authorize the purchase of the U.S. Forest Service Building located
at 507 25th Street. The Redevelopment Agency Board approved a resolution approving the Redevelopment Agency Executive
Director to purchase the Forest Service building for $3.6 million, a resolution declaring the intent of the City to reimburse the
Redevelopment Agency for expenditures incurred in connection with the building, and a budget amendment appropriating
$600,000 for earnest money and maintenance costs associated with the purchase of the building. The Agency put the earnest
money down as required by the purchase contract in December 2024.
         Financial Advisor Laura Lewis, representing Lewis Robertson Burningham, summarized the memo and stated the
proposal for the Board in this action is to approve bond parameters for the issuance of bonds for the full purchase price of the
building. As proposed, the Board resolution pledges Adams CRA tax increment revenue as the primary source for bond repayment
with City sales tax as a backstop. She summarized the bond parameters as follows:
              • Discount – three percent
              • Term – Not more than 16 Years
              • Rates – Not to exceed five percent
              • Maximum Par Amount - $4,400,000.
         Board member Graf asked Ms. Lewis to explain what the discount rate is. Ms. Lewis stated that purchasers of
government bonds care about the date the bond is due, the principal amount of the bond, and the interest rate; these things will be
printed on the bond certificate. If the rate is five percent, the bond amount can be multiplied by that rate and divide that by two to
determine the amount the bond purchaser will receive in payment every six months. However, bond counsel can communicate the
market rate for the bond, which may be lower than the determined rate, and this lower rate is used to adjust down the sale price of
the bond and this is known as the discount rate.
