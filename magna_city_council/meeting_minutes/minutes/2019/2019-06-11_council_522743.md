Ordinances

            THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to receive public
comment regarding the following requests:

              Paul Ashton reviewed the following amendments to the Magna Metro Township’s
Code of Ordinances:

Ordinance #19-05-01, repealing Title 17: “Flood Control and Water Quality” and replacing it with
Title 17: “City Flood Control and Water Quality.” The purpose of this is if the County amended its
ordinances in the future, the Magna Metro Township would not have to change its code of
ordinances.

Ordinance 19-05-02 changes Section 14.16.010, “Right of Way---Excavations and Structures
Prohibited When” to include language prohibiting excavation in Magna right of ways under certain
circumstances, and it establishes minimum standards for repairs following excavations in right of
ways.

Ordinance 19-05-03 amends Title 18: “Subdivisions,” Chapters 16, “Final Plant” and 24, “Required
Improvements” to add requirements for Geographic Information Systems (GIS ) files submission
as a part of the final subdivision plat approval process. This ordinance will help with planning and
zoning as well as record keeping. It should not be an additional burden because all subdivisions
have that data anyway.

ORDINANCE NO. 19-05-02                                                        Date: June 11, 2019

                      ROAD CUT REPAIR STANDARDS AMENDMENTS

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AMENDING
       MAGNA CODE SECTION 14.16.010 “RIGHT OF WAY – EXCAVATIONS AND
       STRUCTURES PROHIBITED WHEN” TO INCLUDE LANGUAGE PROHIBITING
       EXCAVATION IN MAGNA RIGHT OF WAYS UNDER CERTAIN
       CIRCUMSTANCES AND ESTABLISHES MINIMUM STANDARDS FOR
       REPAIRS FOLLOWING EXCAVATIONS IN RIGHT-OF-WAYS

      WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body for Magna pursuant to Utah Code 10-3b-501; and

      WHEREAS, current road cut standards for infrastructure being performed in Magna’s
roadways can cause the road surface to deteriorate and fail at an accelerated rate, requiring costly
and unplanned repairs and replacements to the road surface; and

        WHEREAS, establishing higher road cut repair standards, to include a time period during
which certain types of road cuts cannot be made to a new road surface can reduce or eliminate
the risks of early road surface replacement;

        NOW THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Council of Magna, in the State of Utah, as
follows:

        SECTION 1: AMENDMENT “14.16.010 Right-Of-Way – Excavation And Structures
Prohibited When” of the Magna Municipal Code is hereby amended as follows:

                                          AMENDMENT

14.16.010 Right-Of-Way – Excavation And Structures Prohibited When



                                                 5
DATE           TUESDAY                                  JUNE                               11, 2019

   A. No right-of-way of any countycity road shall be dug up or excavated and no approach,
      road, driveway, pole, pipeline, conduit, sewer, ditch, culvert, billboard, advertising sign, or
      other structure or object of any kind or character shall be placed, constructed or
      maintained within any such right-of-way except as permitted by and in accordance with
      the regulations of the countycity engineering division.

   B. Cuts or open excavations on newly constructed, paved, or overlaid rights-of-way are not
      allowed for three years. If an emergency cut or excavation occurs, the responsible persons
      shall comply with any special conditions imposed by the city regarding restoration of the
      roadway as detailed in Section C.

   C. Any person making any excavation, bore, or boring in or under any county right-of-way
      shall, at their own expense restore the surface to its same type and depth of pavement as
      that which is adjoining, including the gravel base material (at a minimum 3” Bituminous
      surface and 8” untreated base course). All restoration shall conform to city standards and
      all restorations shall be accomplished within seventy-two (72) hours from the time of the
      excavation, unless additional time is granted in writing by the city. If, the road has been
      constructed, reconstructed, or overlaid within the preceding three (3) years, or the
      pavement has been chip-sealed or slurry sealed with in the preceding two (2) years the
      permittee shall be responsible for the following: New Streets, Reconstructed Streets, or
      Overlaid Streets: The asphalt surface shall be milled down a minimum depth of two inches
      from curb (or edge of asphalt) to center of road and resurfaced with asphalt. If the cut
      extends through the centerline of the road the milling and paving will be from curb to curb
      (or edge of asphalt to edge of asphalt). Milling and paving shall extend to a length of 15’
      in each direction from each side of the cut. The City Engineer or designee reserves the
      right to require a length greater than fifteen feet in each direction and a depth greater than
      two inches if there are special circumstances. Slurried Streets or Chip-sealed Streets: The
      pavement shall be re-slurry sealed with an approved type II slurry from curb (or edge of
      asphalt) to center of road and a minimum of 15’ in each direction from each side of the
      cut. If the cut extends through the centerline of the road the application of slurry seal will
      be from curb to curb (or edge of asphalt to edge of asphalt). Excavation of city rights-of-
      way during the winter months (herein defined as October 15 to April 15) will be allowed
      only if the work is a new service connection, required maintenance, emergency or
      otherwise approved by the county. Permanent patching of a county right-of-way excavated
      in winter shall be delayed until April 15 or later and the outdoor ambient temperature is 50
      degrees and rising. The permittee will maintain a temporary patch until such time as the
      permanent surfacing is accomplished. The temporary patch will provide a smooth driving
      surface and be restored within 72 hours of being notified that repairs are required.

   D. Any person who violates the provisions of this section is guilty of a misdemeanor.

       SECTION 2: EFFECTIVE DATE This Ordinance shall be in full force and effect from July
10, 2019 and after the required approval and publication according to law.

ORDINANCE NO. 19-05-03                                                        Date: June 11, 2019

         GEOGRAPHIC INFORMATION SYSTEM (GIS) DATA REQUIREMENTS FOR
                         SUBDIVISION DEVELOPMENT

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AMENDING
       MAGNA CODE TITLE 18: “SUBDIVISIONS,” CHAPTERS 16: “FINAL PLAT” AND
       24: “REQUIRED IMPROVEMENTS” TO ADD REQUIREMENTS FOR
       GEOGRAPHIC INFORMATION SYSTEMS (GIS) FILES SUBMISSION AS A
       PART OF THE FINAL SUBDIVISION PLAT APPROVAL PROCESS

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality (“city”) pursuant to
Utah Code 10-2a-401, et seq; and

        WHEREAS, the use of Geographic Information System (“GIS”) is how municipalities map
their public infrastructure to identify and to provide upkeep and maintenance of these assets; and

        WHEREAS, requiring developers to provide Magna with the GIS information for new
subdivisions and developments allows Magna to identify future maintenance needs and costs as
the city grows;

        NOW THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Council of Magna, in the State of Utah, as
follows:

       SECTION 1: ADOPTION “18.16.020 Final Approval – GIS Data Required” of the Magna
Municipal Code is hereby added as follows:

                                           ADOPTION

18.16.020 Final Approval – GIS Data Required (Added)

A. Prior to the final approval and the issuance of any building permit associated with a subdivision
or development plan, or in the case of a single lot development, a single building permit, the owner
or developer shall provide to City a GIS data corresponding to the approved plans for all
improvements required by Chapter 18.16.020(B). Any changes during construction will require
additional GIS data to be submitted with the final approved as-built drawings. All GIS data shall
be submitted in the following format: 1. All GIS data shall be submitted in conformance with
County Policy 1013, “Standards for Geographic Information System” and the “Salt Lake County


                                                 7
DATE           TUESDAY                                  JUNE                             11, 2019

Public Works Engineering GIS Standards,” as approved by the Salt Lake County GIS Steering
Committee and on file with Salt Lake County Engineering. The City reserves the right to reject
any GIS data that is provided and is not in compliance with the above standards. 2. This shall be
done at the developer or owner’s expense. If a developer or owner does not provide the required
GIS data, the City may complete the work in the developer or owner’s behalf and the developer
or owner shall pay to the City the cost of completing the work at the hourly rate approved by the
Council for such work. If developer or owner fails to pay for such work, the City may pursue legal
action to recover these costs. 3. Developers with a cost as estimated by the public works
department of ten-thousand dollars ($10,000.00) or less may, prior to construction, petition the
Division for an exemption from the GIS requirements of this Chapter. The decision of the Public
Works Director shall be final. B. GIS data will be required for the following improvements: 1.
Roadway system: Regulatory signs, street signs, centerlines, curb and gutter, sidewalks,
crosswalks, ADA ramps, striping, road width, and monuments; Streetlights and signals (including
conduit and electrical boxes for streetlights and signals) 2. Storm drain system: Catch basins,
manholes, detention basin inlets and outlets, culverts, detention basin area, stormwater quality.

                                          AMENDMENT

18.24.070 Street Lighting

Except as provided for in subsection E below, adequate street lighting shall be provided for the
safety and welfare of residents and businesses located in Salt Lake Countythe City through the
installation of a street lighting system as part of subdivision development.

All street lights intended to illuminate the public street shall be installed in accordance with the

           1.   Appropriate distance or spacing;

           3.   Appropriate illumination at intersections;
           4.   Location upon the property;
           5.   Street light type and decorative style based on street classification;
           6.   Height based on location;
           7.   Installation methods and requirements; and

                Greg Schulz stated there has been a lot of misinformation about the Inland Port

4.7 percent market adjustment increase to total compensation in its budget. The UFA’s Board of
Directors wants to get in the top third in the state for compensation. Seismic stuff is moving
forward too. Magna’s old fire station will be retrofitted, but it is still on the list for replacement.
The UFA is preparing a bond for six new fire stations and Magna will be one of them.

               Council Member Peel asked if UFA paramedics were hired on as firefighters and
had training before becoming firefighters or if they were trained in house.

              Council Member Prokopis stated for the most part, paramedics are already
trained when they are hired, but the UFA trains too. When he was hired on there were only a
couple paramedic programs in the State, so the UFA trained its firefighters. Now, there are
programs everywhere.

               Council Member Peel asked if the paramedics also became firefighters.

                Council Member Prokopis stated everybody is a firefighter first and foremost,
and can specialize after that in areas, such as paramedics, hazardous materials, heavy rescue,
or be a driver.
