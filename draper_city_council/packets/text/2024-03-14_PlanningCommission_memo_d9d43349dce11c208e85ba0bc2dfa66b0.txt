MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
Date: 2024-03-14
Re: Public Hearing: Trailside Townhomes Plat Amendment (Administrative Item)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 19.37
acres located on corner of Brookings Drive and Suncrest Drive, at 2142 East Brookings
Drive (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently zoned RR-22, RR-43, and RM. These
zoning designations correspond with the 1999 Draper City Municipal Code and the
Development Agreement for Suncrest. The applicant is requesting to amend the existing
plat to consolidate the six parcels owned by the applicant into one large parcel. A
townhome project is proposed on the consolidated parcels.



The Planning Commission previously approved a plat amendment to consolidate the six
properties into one large property in 2022. The approval of that plat amendment expired
on January 29, 2024. The applicant has now submitted this subdivision plat amendment
application which is essentially the same as the prior approved plat amendment.



The findings for approval as are follows:

  1. There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
     way, or easement has been vacated or amended.



The findings for denial as are follows:

  1. There is not good cause for the vacation or amendment.
