  Dear City Recorder Carlson:

  Thank you for the opportunity to comment on the proposed budget amendment related to establishing
  affordable housing within the Cottonwood Mall/Holladay Hills Area.

  My husband and I recently purchased a home within the Elevate at Holladay Hills Community and are
  really enjoying the area. We are excited about progress being made within the development, including the
  recent opening of Trader Joes and upcoming opening of Lifetime Fitness. We also appreciate the City’s
  efforts to address housing needs in our community, and understand the importance of thoughtful planning
  as Holladay continues to grow.

  Because this proposal involves development within our neighborhood, we would appreciate clarity on
  how affordability will be defined, whether the housing will be mixed-income or 100% income restricted,
  what the income levels will be, and what design, traffic, and parking considerations are being evaluated.

  I also encourage the Agency to ensure that any affordable housing supported through this budget
  amendment is well-designed, professionally managed, and integrated in a way that supports
  neighborhood safety, infrastructure capacity, and long-term community stability.

  I look forward to continued opportunities for public input as more specific details become available, and I
  appreciate the City’s willingness to consider resident feedback as part of this process.

  Thank you for your time and leadership.
