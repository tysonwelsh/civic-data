1. Discussion         Regarding            Adoption        of a Fencing         Ordinance              While         Visiting     Sites in Millcreek
The tour began          at 3400 S. 1100 E. Participants                     walked       from       there west to Lorraine               Avenue      and
1100 East, then south along 1100 East to Carolyn                               Street, then south along Carolyn                          Street to
Fairclough         Drive,     then east to Christine             Street, then south along                    Christine      Street to Montclair
Drive,     then east along Montclair               Drive        to 1100 East, then north                 along 1100 East to 3400 South.
Coinmissioners              and staff observed           a variety   of existing         fences and vegetation                    in residential     front
and side yards.         After      the walk,     Commissioners              and staff spent 30 minutes                      discussing     the
appropriate        height      for front    yard fences,         the appropriate             distance        for clear view         requirements
from     a driveway         and an intersecting           street, and the extent               to which         existing        fences would       have
nonconforming           status once a new ordinance                  was adopted.
