MEMO
To: City Council
From: Nicole Smedley, City Recorder
Date: 2025-08-26
Re: Action Item: Resolution #25-42


Comments:


The Draper City Municipal Primary Election was held on August 12, 2025, for one At-Large
City Council seat for a two-year term. Pursuant to Utah Code § 20A-4-304, the Board of
Canvassers is required to certify the election results.


The Salt Lake County Elections Division will release the official results of the Primary
Election on August 26, 2025. Those documents will be provided at that time and attached
to the Resolution as Exhibit A for certification by the Board of Canvassers.
