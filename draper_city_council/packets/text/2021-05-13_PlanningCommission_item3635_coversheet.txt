Re: Ironhorse Run Plat Amendment - Plat Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for two existing subdivisions that total approximately 23.311 acres located on the south side of Pioneer Road, at approximately 2108 E. 1.  That there is good cause for the vacation or amendment.
