3.4 An Ordinance of the Legislative Body of Salt Lake County, 
Utah, Amending Section 19.90.020 of the Salt Lake County 
Code of Ordinances, 2001, Entitled “Hearing - Notice” to 
Require the Posting of Physical Signs Informing Members of 
the Public about Certain Land Use Hearings and Related 
Matters
21-0393
Page 2 of 8
Committee of the Whole Meeting Minutes March 30, 2021
Attachments: Staff Report
Public Notice Ordinance Draft_AATF
Public Notice Ordinance Draft - Clean Copy
Sponsors: Council Member Richard Snelgrove and Council Member Jim 
Bradley (Approx. 5 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Council Member Snelgrove stated this ordinance would require the County 
to post a 24 x 36-inch orange sign on property being rezoned.  The sign 
would contain all relevant information relating to the rezone.  The Greater Salt 
Lake Municipal Service District would be responsible for the posting signs.
Council Member DeBry asked what the time frame would be for posting of 
the sign. 
Mr. Jim McNulty , Municipal Services District, stated state statute requires a 
10-day notice; however, the MSD would likely post the signs at least two 
weeks before a hearing.
Council Member Snelgrove stated he asked Mitchell Park, Legal Counsel, 
Council Office, to do some research to see if the posting of physical signs 
has been implemented anywhere else.  
Mr. Mitchell Park , Legal Counsel, Council Office, stated his research 
showed that Sandy City has an ordinance already in place and he borrowed a 
lot of the text in this ordinance from Sandy's ordinance.  The Salt Lake 
County Planning Commission would need to recommend approval of this 
ordinance before the Council could adopt it.
A motion was made by Council Member Bradley, seconded by Council 
Member Bradshaw, that this agenda item be referred to the Planning 
Commission for review and recommendation.  The motion carried by a 
unanimous vote.  Council Member Stringham was absent for the vote.

3.6 Presentation on the Impacts of Utah Legislative Bills HB 294 
and SB 195 on Salt Lake County
21-0395
Attachments: Staff Report
210329.SB195.PresentationNotes
HB0294 Enrolled
210324 HB 294 Presentation
Sponsor: Council Chair Steve DeBry
Presenters: Ralph Chamness, Chief Deputy DA and Bridget Romano, Division 
Administrator (Approx. 30 mins). 
Informational
Mr. Ralph Chamness , Deputy District Attorney, stated these two bills are 
very complex and difficult to understand.
Ms. Bridgett Romano , Deputy District Attorney, stated HB 294 relates to 
the pandemic emergency powers and went into effect on March 24, 2021 and 
will sunset on July 1, 2021.  She then delivered a PowerPoint presentation 
highlighting the six principal things that HB 294 covers.
Council Member DeBry asked what would happen if the County Council 
Page 4 of 8
Committee of the Whole Meeting Minutes March 30, 2021
and Gary Edwards, Director, Salt Lake County Health Department, did not 
agree on the mask mandate.   
Mr. Sim Gill , District Attorney, stated if the Salt Lake County Council said 
it was time to end the mask mandate, but the Health Department did not 
agree, there would not be a mask mandate.  It falls on the County Council to 
make the final determination.  However, the Mayor can still mandate the 
wearing of masks within any County facility, and business owners can 
choose to do so as well.
Council Member Bradshaw stated he was concerned with the language 
terminating the emergency order and how that might affect the reimbursement 
the County would be eligible for from the Federal Emergency Management 
Act (FEMA).  
Mr. Mitchell Park , Legal Counsel, Council Office, stated the bill has a 
termination date; however, it indicates the emergency declaration may remain 
in effect. 
Ms. Romano then reviewed SB 195 stating this bill will go into effect on 
May 5, 2021.  It is very long and very complex.  It modifies the Emergency 
Management Act and addresses Public Health Emergencies.  
3.7 Update on Salt Lake County ’s Coordinated Response to 
COVID-19
The Council may take action, including votes on any 
necessary legislative matters related to the ongoing State of 
Emergency
21-0387
Attachments: Staff Report
County Council Briefing -- 03302021
Vaccination Briefing to Council -- 03302021
Sponsors: Mayor Jennifer Wilson; David Schuld, Intelligence Section Chief; 
Gary Edwards, Health Department Executive Director (Approx. 30 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Mayor Jennifer Wilson thanked employees who have worked through this 
crisis and have stepped in to assist wherever needed.
Page 5 of 8
Committee of the Whole Meeting Minutes March 30, 2021
Mr. Gary Edwards , Director, Salt Lake County Health Department, stated 
this week showed a decline of 31 new cases of COVID -19 as compared to 
last week.  Salt Lake County is below the state ’s threshold of 191 new cases 
per 100,000 residents. It has 187 cases per 100,000.  Testing has declined as 
well.  Positivity rates remain almost the same at 6.7 percent.  Hospitalization 
and ICU continue to improve with Salt Lake County down to 10.3 percent. 
Mr. David Schuld , Intelligence Section Chief for COVID -19, delivered a 
PowerPoint presentation.  He reviewed key numbers, the active case curve, 
an infection snapshot, zip code surveillance, current cases by age, the rolling 
14-day comparison, testing analysis, the percentage of known versus 
community cases, and potential sources of exposure.
Mr. Edwards delivered a PowerPoint presentation about vaccines.  Supply 
of vaccines continues to be limited with a slight bump in vaccine allocation 
expected. As of March 24, 2021, all residents over 16 years old are eligible 
for the vaccine.  He reviewed the priority groups, an administration report, 
number of first and second doses administered, outreach to vulnerable and 
diverse communities, and vaccine sites.
