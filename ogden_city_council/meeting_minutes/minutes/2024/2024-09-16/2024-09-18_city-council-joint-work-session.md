          The purpose of the joint work session was for the Council and Planning commission to receive annual land use training
specific to development agreements, and to discuss items of joint interest.

Annual Training – Development Agreements
       Planning Director Brierley, Assistant City Attorney Tanner, and Senior Policy Analyst Burton used the aid of a
PowerPoint presentation to provide training focused on development agreements, including the following:
            • What are Development Agreements? (What they are not, alternatives).
            • When to use them (When not to use them).
                     o Conditional Overlays.
                     o Pros and Cons.
            • Land Transfer Development Agreements.
            • Incentive Agreements.
            • Recent Trends.
            • State code changes around development agreements.
       For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
