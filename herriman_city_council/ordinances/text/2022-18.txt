HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2022-18

22022-038 - AMENDING THE HERRIMAN CITY OFFICIAL ZONING ORDINANCE,
TITLE 10, PERTAINING TO RENAMING RESIDENTIAL AND AGRICULTURAL
ZONES.

WHEREAS, the City of Herriman, pursuant to Utah State Code, may adopt an ordinance
to establish land development regulations, which includes the adoption or amendment of the
zoning text; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah State Code, the Planning Commission shall prepare and
recommend any zoning text amendments to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the City of Herriman Land Development Code, the Planning
Commission shall hold a public hearing and provide reasonable notice at least ten (10) days prior
to the said public hearing to recommend any zoning textg amendments to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, a notice of a Planning Commission public hearing on a zoning text
amendment was posted in three (3) public locations and mailed to affected entities on March 28,
2022, for a meeting to be held on April 7, 2022, at 7:00 p.m.; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission made a recommendation for the zoning text
amendment in a public meeting held on April 7, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Council Chamber;
and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Herriman City Code, it is the responsibility of the City Council
to consider the zoning text amendment and Planning Commission recommendation in a public
meeting; and

WHEREAS, a City Council public meeting was held on April 7, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. to
discuss the zoning text amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is in the best interest of the citizens of Herriman

City to adopt the zoning text amendment, which was recommended by the Planning Commission
and documented in City File number Z2022-038;

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Herriman City Council for the approval to
amend the Herriman City Official Zoning Ordinance, Title 10, to amend the names of the
agricultural and residential zones of the City.

This Ordinance assigned Ordinance No. 2022-18 shall take immediate effect as soon as it shall be published
or posted as required by law and deposited and recorded in the office of the City’s recorder.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 27" day of April 2022.

10-8-5: Clarification Of Zoning

Ambiguous Zone: Any property in the City which is not clearly zoned on the official zoning map shall be
deemed to be in the A-1-43 Zone.

10-8-6: Newly-Annexed Areas

The City Council shall assign a land use zone or a variety thereof to territory annexed to the City at the

time the territory is annexed. If the City Council fails to assign a land use zone at the time the territory is
annexed, the annexed territory shall be deemed to be in the A-1-43 Zone immediately upon annexation,
unless the property is located adjacent to the Hillside Overlay Zone, in which case it shall be zoned FR-5.

10-9-1: Purpose

A-1-10 Agricultural Single Family-25 Zone: The purpose of the A--251-10 Zone is to provide areas in the
City for low density residential development, together with limited agricultural uses.

A-1-21 Agricultural Single Family-5 Zone: The purpose of the A--51-21 Zone is to provide areas in the City
for low density residential development, together with limited agricultural uses.

A-1-43 Agricultural Single Family Zone: The purpose of the A-1-43 Zone is to provide areas in the City for
low density residential development and agricultural uses. (Ord. 2019-23, 7-10-2019)

10-9-6: Development Standards

Density In A-251-10 Zone: The baseline density in any residential development located in the A-.251-10
Zone shall be 1.8 lots per acre.
