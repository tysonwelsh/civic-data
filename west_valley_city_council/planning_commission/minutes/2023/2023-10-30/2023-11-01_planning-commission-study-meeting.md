ZONE TEXT APPLICATIONS
ZT-5-2023  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting amendments to the Zoning (Title 7)
           and Condominium (Title 9) Ordinances to comply with State code changes from HB
           406 and SB 174. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-
           ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that this item may look familiar to the Commission
              where it was on the agenda a few months ago and withdrawn. He stated that this
              is a request to change the zoning ordinance in response to two state code changes
              made during the last legislative session, HB 406, and SB 174.

              Steve explained that one of the changes to the State code is the City can no longer
              require a bond for landscape improvements. He noted that the second change is
              more significant, and it states that the City Council would no longer be part of the
              subdivision process for any single-family or townhome development. Steve stated
              that currently, a preliminary subdivision plat goes before the Planning
              Commission and the final plat to the City Council. He pointed out that if approved
              by City Council it would be considered an administrative approval. This means as
              long as the request meets all of the ordinance requirements, it would be approved.
              He noted that this provision would not take effect until February.

              Chair Fuller noted that the Commission reviews the zone change request before
              building a subdivision. He asked Steve if they would still see zone change
              applications. Steve stated that they would, and the preliminary plat would still go
              before the Commission, but the final plat would not go to the City Council.

              Commissioner Winters asked what the reasoning is to change the subdivision
              review process. Steve said there are two reasons. He pointed out how starting last
              year, there has been a lot of focus on affordable housing. He explained that
              simplifying the process would save time and cost, aiding housing affordability.
              Steve said that the legislative body, the City Council, would no longer be a part of
              an administrative decision.

              Commissioner McEwen asked if the staff could make changes to the final plat.
              Steve explained that, on occasion, changes are made between the preliminary and
              final plat. He said that staff are bound to what is in the ordinance. Steve stated that
              he would ask Steve Lehman, and if he has more information, he could speak
              about this in next week’s meeting.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-25-2023  Petition by GREY GARZA requesting conditional use approval for 18 townhome
           units. The property is located at 1854 W 4100 S on 1.54 acres and is zoned RM
           (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff- Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
           brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion:   Brock Anderson said that this application came before the Commission in
              September of 2022 and was approved. He stated that the approval expired before
              the applicant had their final plans approved or submitted for a building permit, so
              they are here today to renew the approval. Brock explained that the original
              application has mostly stayed the same. He noted that the amenity area and
              masonry wall are almost complete, and the landscaping is installed.

              Steve Pastorik asked Brock what type of fence is surrounding the pool area. Brock
              stated that it looks like it is made of metal, and there is a chain-linked fence
              behind it, but he isn’t sure why there are two of them.

              Brock said there would be two four-plex buildings and two five-plex. He noted
              that the applicant would need to update the plans for the fourplex structures
              because it doesn’t show the two-foot offset between units required via a
              development agreement.
               Chair Fuller asked Brock when they planned to develop the townhomes. Brock
               said he recently saw a for sale sign on the property, so he isn’t sure when that
               would start. He noted that if the property is sold, the development agreement
               would stay with the land, but he will follow up with the applicant.

               Commissioner Lovato asked if they sell the townhome property, would the new
               owner/developer be held to the proposed plans? Brock stated that they would need
               to follow the development agreement, and if they were planning to make any
               significant changes, those would need to come back to the Commission for
               approval.

               Chair Fuller pointed out that the applicant is proposing the amenities be shared
               between the townhomes and apartments. He asked if that would still apply if they
               sold the townhomes. Brock said it would, but he isn’t sure how the owners would
               handle that situation. Commissioner Lovato said he had a follow-up question to
               that. Wouldn’t the amenity requirement be affected if they had two different
               owners? Brock said that they would need to find a way to share them.

               Commissioner McEwen asked if this is the same applicant who, in a meeting, said
               they build townhomes and apartments to keep, not to sell. Brock stated that he did
               not speak to the applicant directly to confirm they are selling the property; he just
               noticed the sign, but he will find out.
