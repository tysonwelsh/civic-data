PUBLIC COMMENT:

  Mr. Rod Hansen addressed the council and said that he had visited the council back on August 17,
2021 and had asked the council to reconsider the out-of-town gun range fees. He said at that time
Mayor Nielson had asked him how much Fountain Green residents spent in Nephi. He said since that
day in August he had intentionally not spent a dime in Nephi. He listed the amount he would have
normally spent in Nephi and said that Nephi will miss out on approximately $19,000 a year in his
spending because of his frustration with the gun range fees.

NORTH RIDGE PHASE B FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that North Ridge Phase B Subdivision is seeking final approval. He
stated that the developers had agreed to an amendment of the original development agreement. This
amendment would include a provision to dedicate either lot #17 or lot #18 for a road connection shown
on the city’s master plan. Since it is unclear where the road will be placed and will depend on
development of the property to the south, the staff is recommending that the city purchase the other
lot from the developer so that the road could be established for the best interest of the city. Another
major concern with the development is to have the electric poles owned by Rocky Mountain Power
removed as part of the subdivision improvements. The agreement amendment specifies that permits
will not be issued for several lots until the electrical lines have been removed or relocated. The
amendment also specifies that the developer will meet requirements from the city and UDOT on the
closure of 1450 North in for for 1500 North to be connected to Main Street.
   Councilor Seely moved to approve the final plat for North Ridge Subdivision Phase B contingent on
the first amendment to the development agreement being signed by the developer. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following vote:

AIRPORT LEASE AGREEMENT WITH CODY AND LEO FINSTER APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson presented a draft of an airport lease agreement with Cody Finster who is wanting to
build four connected hangars west of the new airport apron. The hangars would be available for rent
after being built. Councilor Memmott moved to accept the airport lease agreement with the addition of
Leo Finster as a lessee along with Cody Finster. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote. After the vote Mr. Cody Finster asked the council if they would consider
letting them build a 5th hangar. The council agreed to change the agreement draft to build five hangars
instead of four. Mr. Atkinson said he would make sure that five hangars could fit on the ground before
making the change.

ELECTRIC MUTAL AID ASSISTANT AGREEMENT APPROVED:

 Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that the state of Utah has prompted a helpful response to the
problem of electric outages throughout the state. This has taken the form of a mutual aid agreement
between public and private electric providers during an emergency. The city has always had a mutual
aid agreement with other public providers, but this will now include cooperative agencies and private
companies. It allows, but does not require, an agency or company to request mutual aid during an
emergency. It does not obligate a company or agency to respond with mutual aid but if aid is offered,
certain costs are established, and conditions set forth.
   Councilor Worwood moved to accept the Electric Mutual Aid Assistant Agreement and to authorize
the Mayor to sign the agreement. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.
