Citizen Public Input

               Sara Van Roosendaal, Magna Metro Township Planning Commission, thanked
the Council for sending some planning commission members to the American Planning
Association (APA) Utah Chapter Fall Conference. One thing talked about was how out of date
conditional uses were. Once the Magna General Plan is updated, she would like to start updating
ordinances and conditional use policies.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
               Doug Crookston stated residents in the Millpoint Subdivision are concerned with
the development of the subdivision. The project has been turned over to D.R. Horton, and nothing
has been done since July. The concern is whether buildings will be built according to the plan –
a 55 and older development and with the same quality as the original buildings.

               Connie Stewart stated D.R. Horton, or another builder, has requested a zone
change for that development. She asked if that would come to the Council for a final okay.

            Council Member Peay stated a zone change request would first go to the
Planning Commission and then the Council.

            Council Member Peel stated residents’ best avenue is to go to the Planning
Commission with concerns.

              Max Johnson stated the Planning Commission will schedule that once it gets a
rezone application. Its meetings are held the second Thursday of each month.

Reports from Other Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department

                Chief Brent Atkinson, Unified Police Department (UPD), announced he was
retiring. His last day will be October 16th. Lieutenant Jason Ackerman will be the acting-in chief
until a new precinct chief is selected.

               Detective Buhler, Unified Police Department (UPD), requested the Council’s
permission to enforce parking along 2900 South on the north side of Brockbank Campus at
Cyprus High School. High school students are parking their vehicles in a way that blocks
residents at the home on the corner from using their driveway. This has been an ongoing issue
for a couple years despite issuing citations. He would like permission to paint the curb red and
put up signs that say no parking from 6:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. starting on the corner where the
house is down to 8650 West. That will enable the UPD to tow the vehicles.

                Council Member Ferguson asked if the curb could be painted a different color.
He did not want anyone mistaking it for a fire lane. He was also concerned about the residents’
visitors being towed.

               Matthew Stanley stated he is the homeowner and rarely had company during
those hours. Students were blocking his driveway and even parking on the property. He needed
a way to help law enforcement stop that from happening.

              Max Johnson, Planning & Development Division, Salt Lake County, stated if the
parking is along a public right of way, the County’s Public Works and Municipal Services
Department can take care of signing and painting.

             Paul Ashton suggested the Council contact the Public Works and Municipal
Services Department to find out what it can do. It may also have to amend its parking ordinances.

               Greg Schulz stated he will work with the County to make that happen.

Public Hearings

            THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to receive public
comment regarding the following requests:

Application #30739 – Shawn Hayward on behalf of the Raymond Construction Company and the
Loy W. Watts Family LLC requesting approval of a zone change from A-20 to M-2 for 3.34 acres
of property located at 7730 West U201.

               Max Johnson, Salt Lake County Planning and Development Division, reviewed
the request stating adjacent properties are M-2 and include a green waste recycling operation
and an outdoor toilet rental business. The property to the east is in application mode to become
an asphalt batch plant.

               Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to open the
public hearing for Application #30739. The motion passed unanimously.

               Steve Anderson, real estate broker for Shawn Hayward, reviewed the request

ORDINANCE NO. 18-10-02                                                      Date: October 9, 2018

       AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 19, ENTITLED “ZONING”, OF THE MAGNA
       METRO TOWNSHIP CODE OF ORDINANCES, 2017, BY RECLASSIFYING
       CERTAIN PROPERTY LOCATED IN MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP FROM THE
       A-20 (AGRICULTURAL – 20 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE TO THE M-2
       (HEAVY MANUFACTURING) ZONE

       The Magna Metro Township legislative body of Magna Metro Township, State of Utah,
ordains as follows:

      Section 1:     Section, 19.06.020, Zoning Maps of Magna Metro Township Code of
Ordinances 2017, is hereby amended, as follows:

        The property described in Application #30739 filed by Shawn Hayward located at 7730
West UTwo-O-One Highway within the Magna Metro Township (the “Property”), is hereby
reclassified from the A-20 (Agricultural – 20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the M-2 (Heavy
Industrial) zone.

The Property is more particularly described as follows:

PARCEL #14-21-176-004,

       COM 40 RDS W FR CEN SEC 21, T1S, R2W, SL MER, N 40 RDS; W 20RDS; S 40 RDS;
       E 20 RDS TO BEG. LESS STATE ROAD. 3.34 AC.

      Section 2:    The map showing such change shall be filed with the Magna Metro
Township Planning Commission in accordance with Section 19.06.020 of the Magna Metro
Township, Code of Ordinance, 2017.

        Section 3:     This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon
at least one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in Salt Lake
