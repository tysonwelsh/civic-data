CONSENT CALENDAR:
  a. Consider approval to purchase loose equipment for two (2) new fire
     apparatus from LN Curtis.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This request is to purchase loose equipment
      including hose, nozzles, tools, adaptors, fans, saws etc. for the two new fire
      apparatus that are on order. This is the majority of fire equipment required for these
      apparatus per the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) standards along with
      the Insurance Services Office (ISO) requirements and service delivery / response.
      Staff recommends purchase of the loose equipment from LN Curtis in the amount of
      $261,471.26.

   b. Consider approval of a SASO Agreement with Wairworthy, LLC at the St.
      George Regional Airport.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Wairworthy is an aviation company that
      designs equipment and clothing for pilots. Wairworthy just launched its first product,
      a flight bag which received much fanfare in the aviation community and generated
      over $50,000 in the first month. Primary use for the hangar is to store their
      aircraft. A small portion of the hangar will be used for office space to run their
      business. Staff recommends approval of the agreement.

   c. Consider approval of an Improvement Reimbursement Agreement between
      Ed Burgess (Ledges at Snow Canyon, LLC and Desert Valley Development,
      Inc) and the City of St. George for road improvements on Quarry Ridge
      Drive.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The developer of the Fieldstone subdivision
      is improving the east portion of Quarry Ridge Dr and the remaining west portion is
      the responsibility of the City. The City would like the developer to install the public
      improvements on the City's portion so that the full-width improvements are
      complete. The City's cost of improvements amounts to $273,032.78. Staff
      recommends approval of the agreement.

AMEND TITLES 3 AND 10 OF CITY CODE PERTAINING TO MOBILE
BUSINESS/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance amending portions of the City Code,
     including Title 3, Business and License Regulations, to add Mobile Business
     along with its operational requirements, time limits, and expiration date;
     and to amend portions of Title 10, Zoning Regulations, to add Mobile
     Business to be permitted in the C-2 and/or C-3 zone along with its specific
     standards, application, and parking requirements.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This request is to add mobile business to
      Title 3, Business and License Regulations, and to create specific standards in Title 10,
      Zoning Regulations, for these businesses. This item was presented to the City
      Council at a Work Meeting on October 27, 2022 for discussion on this topic and will
      be heard by the Planning Commission on November 15, 2022.

PLANNED DEVELOPMENT COMMERCIAL AMENDMENT/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance amending the Southern Hills West PD-C
     (Planned Development Commercial) to remove the carwash and amend the
     site layout for a new commercial development on approximately 5.1 acres
     located on the northeast corner of River Road and White Dome Drive for a
     project to be known as White Dome Commercial. Case No. 2022-ZCA-046

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: On March 4, 2021, the city council
      approved the Southern Hills West PD-C (Planned Development Commercial) zone as
      a part of the Southern Hills Master Plan. Then on December 16, 2021, this portion of
      the PD-C was increased from 4.5 acres to 5.1 acres by changing the zone on .60
      acres of the PD-R (Planned Development Residential) zone that is directly east of this
      property. The design and conceptual site plan of this project was also approved on
      this same date. This request is to make changes to the approved layout. The
      Planning Commission held a public hearing on this item on October 25, 2022 and
