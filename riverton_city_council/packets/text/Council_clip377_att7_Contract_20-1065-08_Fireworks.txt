INTRODUCTION:
Firestorm provides technical innovations with custom made pyrotechnics that will leave your spectators
in amazement. Everyone has seen standard, hand-lit fireworks shows or an electrically-fired pin board
show. We offer jaw-dropping, computer choreographed pyrotechnics that can fill the sky with color at
will. The Firestorm experience is an event your audience will never forget.

As Gale Bunker, Mayor of Delta, said after the first show we provided his city on January 1, 2012,
“Fireworks, we have had fireworks, lots of fireworks, but never magic fireworks!” And, like our
audiences in Mapleton, Utah for our past Pioneer Days performances, they will invite their out-of-town
friends in coming years.

We don’t do standard, old-school firework shows. Firestorm’s technology (as seen in Fig. 1) allows us to
paint the sky with color-coordinated fireworks of any size, from any number of locations wirelessly.
Because of Firestorm’s firing system we can detonate any size mortar or effect right on cue, by
calculating the rise times and initiating the shot so they detonate at exactly the desired moment.

FIREWORK DISPLAY OPITIONS
Show pricing is highly dependent on your individual preferences in theme, number of mortar field
locations, fall-out area sizes, and field of view for your guests. Please keep in mind that we can shoot
multiple firework displays, thousands of feet apart from one computer. For example, in St. George we
shot pyrotechnic effects 4” and smaller from a down town location and 4”- 12” pyrotechnic effects from
two locations on the surrounding mountains 4000’to 5000’ away. We can provide options for multiple
shoot locations if it will benefit you.


Proceed with caution with any vender that lists the exact type of product that will be used in your show at
this stage. We custom choreograph each shot to fit your music and theme. We cannot provide the exact
product we will use until we have the music and theme. This is a key defining factor in choosing the best
firework vendor. What we will quote is the minimum products we will use in you display.


There are many firework manufactures. The saying “you get what you pay for” is especially true in the
firework industry. Many firework companies buy the least expensive product; we refuse to do this for
safety and quality reasons. In addition, we want you to have the best products available. For example a 5”
Aqua Ghost Shell (http://youtu.be/FRMJgxxnU9E ), or 5” Triple color-changing Japanese shell
(http://youtu.be/7rnlnUzyxPE). These products can cost 4-8 times as much as a standard effect. If quantity
is all you care about, we are not that firework company.


Like Brian Wall, Mayor of Mapleton City, said. “There are going to be more shells, and larger shells, but
most of all it’s what they can do with it. They have a better paint brush.”


Firework companies quote different ways. We count one shell with multiple brakes as one shell. Some
vendors use a different method, be cautious, any other way is difficult to audit.


The following product quantities represent a quality firework display with multiple shot volleys of layered
matched colors and varied patterned explosions of color to paint the sky with wonder. We offer you an
unsurpassed quality that you can count on to be safe.
