Posted on Wednesday, January 17, 2024, on the Utah Public Notice Website https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html, Draper City Website https://www.draperutah.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?CID=13 and at City Hall, 1020 E. Pioneer Road, Draper, UT
City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption -
On January 16, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1601, amending the text of the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding to a new process for subdivision review and approval based on Senate Bill 174. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder's Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 17th day of January 2024. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
