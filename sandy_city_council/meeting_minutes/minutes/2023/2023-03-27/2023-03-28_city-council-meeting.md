2.         23-127                 Hale Centre Theatre presenting a review of the Cultural Celebration Series 
                                  and their 2022 performances.
                   Attachments:   Sandy City HCT Presentation - 2023

                                  Council Chair Stroud introduced Mark and Sally Dietlein, co-founders of the Hale Centre 
                                  Theatre, who provided a 2022 Year in Review of Hale Centre Theatre. In 2022, HCT had 
                                  889 sold out performances, sold 31, 542 season tickets, and had an economic impact of 
                                  over $41 million dollars. They also spoke about how the Hale Centre Theatre gives back 
                                  to the community by donating over 20,500 tickets, offering performances with audio 
                                  description and ASL, and producing sensory friendly performances. They also spoke 
                                  about the Hale Centre Theatre Cultural Celebration series and the upcoming 2023 
                                  performances planned to begin the beginning in May. 

                                  The HCT Arts and Education Program provided free tickets to over 15,000 youth with a 
                                  focus on Title 1 School students, presented education programming to 1,400 students, 
                                  30 camps, and 12 showcases. In 2022, HCT launched a new outbound program - The 
                                  Story Weavers.

                                  The Mountain America Arts & Education Beehive is expected to be completed in 2024 
                                  and will be a three level, 44,000 sq ft facility that is connected to the HCT. They expect to 
                                  break ground this spring and complete the facility in 2024. Council thanked the Dietleins 
                                  and team for their presentation.

6.         23-126                 Emergency Management Updating the Council on previous 
                                  accomplishments and upcoming goals for the program
                   Attachments:   Memo to Council re Emergency Management

                                  Amy DeNeff, Emergency Manager, reviewed her department accomplishments for the 
                                  past year. FEMA conducted an Integrated Emergency Management Training last summer 
                                  for Sandy City and she reviewed the training with the Council. Ms. DeNeff provided an 
                                  overview of new programs that Emergency Management will be launching this year, 
                                  upcoming tabletop exercises, and community trainings. She spoke about preparations for 
                                  potential flood management and our city's coordination with Salt Lake County Emergency 
                                  Managment. Council questions followed. Council thanked Ms. DeNeff for all her hard work 
                                  on behalf of Sandy City. Mayor Z acknowledged Ms. DeNeff's efforts on behalf of the City 
                                  and the excellent training received at the FEMA workshops.

7.         23-128                 Public Utilities presenting on the 2023 flood preparedness plan.

                   Attachments:   Sandy Flood Preparedness Update City Council - 3-23-23

                                  Tom Ward and Scott Ellis with Public Utilities presented an overview of the current years 
                                  snowpack, water supply, and flood runoff potential. They reviewed the different types of 
                                  flood hazards, the current conditions, the future outlook and flood risk, and the Sandy 
                                  City flood prevention and preparedness plan. They reviewed the steps the Public Utilities 
                                  crews and staff are taking to prepare for potential flooding and their outreach to the 
                                  community. Residents are encouraged to go to their website for up to date information. 
                                  They spoke about the cooperative efforts among the cities and county. Council questions 
                                  and comments followed. Council commended the crews in Public Utilities for their efforts 
                                  to keep our residents safe from potential flooding hazards.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski spoke about this past weekend's incident which involved an officer 
                    involved shooting.

                    Captain Jon Arnold provided details about the recent officer involved shooting this past 
                    weekend. Police protocols were immediately invoked and an objective and meaningful 
                    investigation was launched. The Police Department is following standard protocol. The 
                    Officer is currently on paid administration leave. This is a transparent process and the 
                    investigation is ongoing - that is all the information we have at this point.
