WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JULY 9, 2024

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on July 9, 2024, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.
INVOCATION BY: Lesa Sandberg
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Kyle Wilson
BOARD OF CANVASSERS MEETING:

Washington County Clerk/Auditor Ryan Sullivan read aloud the results from the June 25, 2024
Regular Primary Election. He reported that there are 69,026 registered Republican voters, and
there were 36,648 ballots that were cast for a voter turn out of 53.09%. 2,413 ballots were not
counted, which included 662 that were returned past deadline, 1370 were undeliverable, 342
uncured, 13 uncountable, and 26 provisional ballots that were not counted.

Mr. Sullivan commented on the issues with the United States Post Office, and requested that voters
use County drop boxes when available, or mailing ballots early to ensure they are postmarked
before Election Day.

(See attached report.)

Commissioner Almquist thanked the Clerk/Auditor staff and all volunteers for this election. He
further stated he met with the postmaster in St. George Utah, and spoke regarding improving the
election process when it comes to vote by mail. Commissioner Iverson discussed promoting the
ballot drop boxes with future elections.

Mr. Sullivan thanked his staff and other County employees for all the help and hard work regarding
the election.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Accept the Certification of the June 25, 2024
Regular Primary Election. Motion seconded by Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF STATEMENT ON CANVASSING

Commissioner Snow stated that the Commissioners would like a public statement regarding the
June 25 primary.
