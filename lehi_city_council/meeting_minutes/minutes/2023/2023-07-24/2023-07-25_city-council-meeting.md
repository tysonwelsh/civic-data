2. 20 Minute Citizen Input
Jane Rap, a resident of Cranberry Farms neighborhood, expressed her concerns about a rumored
punch-through off 2600 West. She mentioned that her neighborhood currently has only two ways
out, and adding a punch-through would worsen traffic issues in her neighborhood. She asked that
the Council expedite the Clubhouse Drive extension.

Jacob Sperry, another resident from Cranberry Farms neighborhood, shared his concerns about the
proposed Clubhouse Drive extension and a punch-through off 2600 West. He worried that routing
traffic through the neighborhood would be damaging and problematic, especially with many
children playing on the streets. He presented a matrix outlining the proposed traffic changes and
pointed out potential issues with the plan. He emphasized the difficulties residents face in getting
out of the neighborhood due to traffic congestion.

Erin Baker, another resident of Cranberry Farms, expressed her concern about the proposed traffic
changes that would affect the neighborhood. She believes the proposal would destroy the sense of
community, make the area less desirable for families, and create safety issues for children walking
to school. She suggested an alternative solution, moving the traffic by the Holiday gas station area.
She urged the Council not to create more traffic through Cranberry Farms.

4. Consideration of Resolution #2023-40 amending the Development Agreement for the
   Hansen Tennis Facility.
   Jacob Hansen was available to answer questions. He suggested a minor adjustment in the
   agreement regarding the development of the clay courts and the timing of the phases. He wants
   to ensure that once the clay courts are installed, they can operate as a permanent use even if
   the rest of the project is not completed.

    The Council discussed the wording of the agreement and agreed that if permanent facilities
    like bathrooms and parking are completed within two years, the clay courts can operate as a
    permanent use.

    Motion:     Councilor Southwick moved to approve Resolution #2023-40 amending the
                Development Agreement for the Hansen Tennis Facility; noting that the clay court
                use would not terminate within the 2 years. Councilor Koivisto seconded the
                motion.

5. Consideration of Resolution #2023-45 combining the culinary water and pressurized
   irrigation funds for external reporting purposes.
   Dean Lundell, Finance Director, proposed consolidating the culinary water fund and
   pressurized irrigation fund for a few reasons. The funds currently share debt and have
   employees working for both. Consolidation would make the financial statements more
   readable and easier to prepare reports for external purposes. He clarified that internally, they
   would still track the funds separately to monitor surplus or deficit. However, they wanted to
   combine them for reporting purposes.

    Motion:     Councilor Koivisto moved to approve Resolution #2023-45 combining the culinary
                water and pressurized irrigation funds for external reporting purposes. Councilor
                Albrecht seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Absent; Mayor Pro Tempore
    Hancock, Yes; Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed
    with 4 in favor and 1 absent.
