                              SPECIAL BOARD OF CANVASSERS
                                        MINUTES

                                      Approved December 8, 2021
The following are the minutes of the City Council Meeting of the Herriman City Council. The meeting was
held on Tuesday, November 16, 2021 at 5:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Council Chambers, 5355
West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was
posted in City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested
citizens.

       Presiding: Mayor David Watts

       Council Members Present: Jared Henderson (via phone), Sherrie Ohrn and Steve Shields

       Staff Present:City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, City Recorder

5:00 PM - SPECIAL MEETING:
   1. CALL TO ORDER
Mayor David Watts called the meeting to order at 5:01 p.m. and welcomed those in attendance. He excused
Councilmember Clint Smith from the meeting.

    2. RESULTS OF GENERAL ELECTION
             2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution finding and promulgating the results of
                  the 2021 Municipal General Election – Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
City Recorder Jackie Nostrom offered a brief overview of the General Election held November 2, 2021 and relayed
the official results as follows: For the Mayoral position, 63.21-percent of the votes were cast in favor of Lorin
Palmer. In City Council District #2 69.19-percent of the votes were in favor of Teddy Hodges. In City Council
District #3, 100-percents of the votes cast were in favor of Sherrie Ohrn. Recorder Nostrom declared the Mayor-
Elect would be Lorin Palmer, Council-Elect for District #2 would be Teddy Hodges, and Council-Elect for District
#3 would be Sherrie Ohrn.
