                                              MINUTES
                     OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER COUNTY
                                Tuesday, November 22, 2016 - 11:00 a.m.
                        Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
 In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons
 who appear and speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or
 purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

WEBER COUNTY BOARD OF CANVASSERS: James Ebert, County Commissioner; John Ulibarri, County
Assessor; and John Bond, County Treasurer.
OTHER STAFF PRESENT: Ricky D. Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Chris Crockett, Deputy County
Attorney; and Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME – Commissioner Ebert
B. ACTION ITEM:
   1. APPROVE THE WEBER COUNTY BOARD OF CANVASS - 2016 ELECTION
         Ryan Cowley, County Elections Director, stated that 22 of the 29 Utah counties voted by mail
         resulting in the highest number of votes cast thus far. It was a rather grueling election. Mr.
         Cowley praised the poll workers who dealt admirably with difficult situations and an inordinate
         number of provisional ballots (2,333) and registration issues. The communities pulled together
         and a lot of volunteers assisted in the process. Roy and Ogden Cities shared employees and
         resources to help alleviate the long lines, the Assessor’s Office provided employees, the
         Democratic Party provided water, chairs and snacks to voters while maintaining polling place
         neutrality, his staff worked tirelessly and made personal sacrifices, and many other county staff
         also assisted. An audit of the election was conducted and the results matched 100%. He said
         that 15% of the voters voted in person, over 73,000 voted by mail, and over 103,000 ballots were
         mailed out. He referred to the Election Summary Report before the board for all the county
         races.
         John, Ulibarri, commended Mr. Hatch and the Election staff for the many, many hours and the
         personal sacrifices and expressed thanks. Mr. Ulibarri’s staff that helped on election night had
         reported that it was a very professional and organized process. John Bond, County Treasurer,
         expressed appreciation for the Election staff’s efforts and said that Mr. Hatch’s comments in the
         Standard Examiner yesterday was very helpful and included an invitation to the community to
         come and see how the process works. Commissioner Ebert noted that voting by mail gets the
         information before the voters earlier and creates an overall better process. He commended Mr.
         Hatch for taking a lead on this process and for an amazing team that made it happen. Mr. Hatch
         thanked staff for the exhausting work of 15+ hour days for many weeks. He is proud of the
         county’s election process and said that the team does so much with so little.
         Mr. Bond moved to approve the Weber County Board of Canvass for the 2016 Election; Mr.
         Ulibarri seconded.
         Mr. Bond – aye; Mr. Ulibarri – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye
