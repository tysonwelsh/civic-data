5.   Reed Ridge Commercial Subdivision (6 lot)- Final Plat, applicant Jeff Parkinson

     a.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna Talbot indicated that this property is north of Maverick and that we are still
     waiting on the soil report.

     b.        Commission Discussion
     Brett Ludlow stated that there is a contingency on UDOT approval but, after waiting for 2
     months, he got an email today. He stated that we are getting closer. Heather asked
     about easements. Jeff Parkinson explained that easements have to be asphalt. He said
     the soil report should be here in about a week and that it just needs to be updated. Ann
     asked what the 1st report indicated, and Jeff replied that it showed collapsible soil. He
     also stated that the whole state of Utah has the same problem. Shauna said there is a
     contingency on the DRC notes and UDOT approval. Mr. Parkinson stated that he had
     emailed his disagreements to Shauna. Shauna told Jeff she would do whatever she
     could to help. Brett Ludlow said they discussed the contingency of the DRC notes and
     didn’t agree. Heather asked if there was an additional financial burden to Mr. Parkinson
     for the soil report. Shauna stated that the outcome would be the same, note or not. Brett
     stated that the contingency would change the whole plot. Mr. Parkinson asked multiple
     times where the extra lot is? Alan also asked where the parcel is, and Shauna confirmed
     that it is not shown on the plat map. It is south of Maverick and is called “Plat B.” Brett
     Ludlow asked why they are being burdened with this, if it’s never been done before. He
     said it has nothing to do with the application. Shauna said it’s needed to indicate a
     phased development, to which Mr. Parkinson replied that it is not a phased
     development. Cory pointed out that the parcels are not attached. Alan stated that he
     feels they are separate lots and that if “Plat B” is sold, the new owner would have to go
     through the process to subdivide it. It’s a “Buyer Beware” situation and the buyer would

     have to go through the commission first. Heather asked if anyone could explain the
     problems in Sanpete County concerning this subject. She understands that there have
     been illegally subdivided properties. Alan said that if we adopt Sanpete’s system, we’ll
     have a mess. Mr. Parkinson, property owner, agreed with Alan. Karl Brough asked what
     the rub would be in not adding the note. Mr. Parkinson stated he does not want to
     subdivide a “ghost property.” Heather agreed by stating this would make him do
     something he’s not responsible for. Shauna said that the Maverick property was not
     properly subdivided. Mr. Parkinson said that if he chooses to subdivide the property in
     question, he’ll come back to go through that process. He went on to say that he
     purchased it as 3 attached parcels in 2018. Alan advised that if “Plat B” is sold, the new
     owner will have to come to the commission for the subdivision process. Shauna stated
     that she is open for discussion, and this was an “ask” rather than a requirement. Much
     discussion was had on this subject (recording and script available for comments).

     c.     Action Item
     Motion: Cory Thomson Second: Fran Peterson Outcome: Passed Unanimously
     Contingent upon DRC notes and additional exemption note.

8.   National Vinyl Products (Expansion of Building) Site Plan

     a.    Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented the expansion of the NVP building site plan to the commision.

     b.       Commission Discussion
     Ann asked how big the building is right now. The developer responded that it is currently
     20,000 square feet and, with the expansion, it will be 50,000 square feet. He explained
     that the additional square footage will be used for individually packaging products and
     will not involve adding a lot more employees. He added that parking should not be an
     issue because of rotating shifts. He explained that there are 4-12 hour shifts, with 50
     employees per shift. Skip Worwood asked how long the shift overlap is. The developer
     said there is a 30 minute overlap between shifts, and the office staff is not included in the
     overlap. Alan brought up the fact that once construction starts, no one really knows
     what’s going on inside. He likened it to basement construction. Several commissioners
     voiced their concern about adequate parking. Karl asked if we still have code
     enforcement officers and Shauna confirmed that we do. Alan said he loves to see
     growth, but the standard needs to be the same across the board. Fran asked if the
     parking stalls needed to be lengthened or if the code needed to be changed. The
     developer stated he would like more spots if possible. Maybe they could dedicate
     certain stalls for certain vehicles, i.e. electric vehicles? Seth commented that the best
     best would be to change the code. Cory confirmed that the parking situation does meet
     code, as currently outlined.
     The developer asked for a definition from Nephi City as to what is deemed “floor area.”
     They have been using Juab County’s standard. Alan stated that there is a definite
     difference between city and county. Heather said that she liked that the developer
     brought up dedicating parking stalls to compact cars, etc. Ann pointed out an error on
     the plat plan, and advised the developer to change “NPV” to “NVP.”

     c.    Action Item
     Motion: Karl Brough Second: Cory Thomson Opposed: Alan Hancock
     Outcome: Motion carries Contingent on acceptance of DRC notes.

     Skip stated that he appreciates Alan’s concern, and that it was also brought up during
     city council. Karl stated that if the developer meets code, it is our responsibility to accept
     it. If they are not compliant, it becomes a code enforcement issue.

11.   Annexation Concept Zoning as a Land Use Element of the General Plan

      a.     Staff Presentation
      Seth presented the annexation of the concept zoning as a land use element of the city’s
      general plan. He stated that all changes made in previous discussions have been noted.

      b.      Public Hearing
      Ann opened the hearing at 8:43 p.m.
      Public comment and commissioner comments were both received during this time.

      Alan asked if higher density housing would cause law enforcement issues. Seth stated

      that he would like to see higher density housing that encourages ownership. Recording
      and script are available for additional commissioner comments and questions. Donald
      Ball commented that the plan needed more inclusion for low income housing. He also
      said that low income housing and ownership clash.
      Karl asked if the colors are coded in the legend of the map, to which Shauna confirmed.
      Alan asked how often the concept plan would be revisited. Seth stated that it would be at
      least every 10 years, and can be looked at any time. The general plan would need to be
      revisited at least every 5 years, given the growth of the city. Skip said that it will be nice
      to at least have the concept with a lot of ideas about types of industry. Recording and
      script are available for additional commissioner comments and questions.
      Shauna added that we need something preserved as an entrance to our city, similar to
      Harward Farms in Springville. We need to protect the bench for beautification.
      Shari Cowan asked if high density housing is really being proposed adjacent to the gun
      club.

      Public Hearing closed at 8:53 p.m.

      c.   Commission DIscussion
      Commission discussion was included in the public hearing, as noted above.

      d.    Action Item
      Motion: Alan Hancock Second: Karl Brough Outcome: Passed Unanimously
