Re: Text amendment to Personnel Policy Section 8040 - Holiday Leave Comments: Police and Fire receive all of their holiday leave at the beginning of each calendar year. The proposed amendment is to clarify that there is no year-end carryover of holiday leave. All holiday hours must be used before the end of the calendar year.
