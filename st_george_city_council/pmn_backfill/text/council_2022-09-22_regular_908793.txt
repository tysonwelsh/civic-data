CONSENT CALENDAR:
  a. Consider approval to purchase a budget development software from
     ClearGov.

       BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Development of the city's budget requires
       significant resources across the organization annually as we work to identify and
       evaluate the cost to provide services, long term needs of the community, and the
       funding available to pay for these needs. Data is gathered and evaluated for over
       3,400 accounts in over 40 different funds for over 130 different department divisions

      which culminates into a 400-page budget document. Staff currently rely on a myriad
      of products to help gather and analyze this information including Microsoft Excel,
      Google Sheets, Microsoft Word, and Adobe Acrobat. Due to the growing complexity

GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT/2022 MODERATE INCOME HOUSING
ELEMENT/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance amending the City General Plan to update
     the 2022 Moderate Income Housing (MIH) element of the General Plan.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City adopted the 2019 Moderate
      Income Housing (MIH) element of the General Plan per SB34 in Fall 2019. In 2022,
      HB462 required that Cities include implementation plans for each strategy. The 2022
      MIH Plan outlines six (6) strategies and associated implementation plans to provide
      realistic opportunities to meet the need for additional housing for families earning up
      to 80% of the Washington County median family income. The 2022 median family
      income for a family of four in Washington County is $83,900. 80% of the Washington
      County median family income is $67,120. The six (6) strategies were identified by
      the Planning Commission and City Council in a joint work meeting and
      implementation plan for each strategy was finalized by a working group that included
      two (2) members of the City Council and two (2) members of the Planning
      Commission. At their meeting held on September 13, 2022, the Planning
      Commission held a public hearing and recommended approval. No public comments
      were given at the public hearing.

      Link to presentation from Economic Vitality and Housing Director Shirlayne Quayle,

HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a Hillside Development Permit to adjust the location of
     the ridgeline and ridgeline setback on lot 89 of the South Rim at Foremaster
     Ridge Phase 4 final plat in anticipation of the construction of a residential
     home.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The ridgeline for the property located at
      1751 East Ridgeline Circle has been determined and is located on the South Rim at
      Foremaster Ridge Phase 4 final plat. Previously this year, requests have been made
      and granted to alter the 30' ridgeline setback on lots 82, 83, 84, 85, and 86. All of
      these requests were approved. This project will be known as South Rim at
      Foremaster Ridge Phase 4 - Lot 89. The he Hillside Review Board heard this case on
      August 24, 2022, and the Planning Commission held a public meeting for this case
      on September 13, 2022 and recommended approval with the following conditions: 1)
      The adjusted ridgeline is created by drawing a straight line between the two rear
      property line pins; and 2) An amended plat is filed and recorded for Lot 89 of the
      South Rim at Foremaster Ridge Phase 4 final plat with this new ridgeline location and
      associated 30’ setback on the plat.
