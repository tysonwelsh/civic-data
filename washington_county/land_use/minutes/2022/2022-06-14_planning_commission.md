4. PUBLIC HEARING / ZONE CHANGE. Review and hold a public hearing for a Zone Change
request of Parcel 8217-D-1-NW from the OST-20 (Open Space Transitional 20 acre minimum lot
size) zone to the RA-2.5 (Residential Agricultural 2.5 acre minimum lot size) zone. The
approximately 2.5 +/- acre parcel is generally located at 300 North Main Street in Gunlock. The
applicants are Justin and Tam Leavitt.

Mr. Messel explained the location of the property in Gunlock and that the applicant is desiring to
build a home on the parcel. The parcel currently does not meet the minimum lot size requirements
for the OST-20 zone and therefore, the applicant wishes to rezone to the RA-2.5 zone. Mr. Messel
proceeded to show a map of the area and a site plan submitted by the applicant. Zoning
designation and septic density in the area was discussed between Staff and the Planning
Commission.

DISCUSSION REGARDING PRIVATE RURAL ESTATE OVERLAY ZONE.

Mr. Messel went on to explain that zoning and density are always changing, and property owner’s
desires also change. He gave examples of some zoning and building requirements within the
county and spoke about the importance of mitigating detrimental effects of zoning. He explained
that the proposed overlay zone would be means for an applicant to request this overlay for
exemptions to the zoning requirements in the current zone. As an example of the proposed
overlay, an applicant could ask for an exception to height requirements if they could show a
mitigation of detrimental effects to the area or the neighbors. He gave examples of overlay zones
throughout the State that allow for exemptions to zoning regulations.

Ms. Hales gave hypothetical situations to applying for this overlay zone and how the process would
proceed for approval. She stated that the Planning Commission would be provided a draft of the
ordinance and Commissioner Hughes stated that he is in favor of any ordinance that gives property
owners more options for their land. Mr. Lueck asked how we protect property owners around the
applicant if the neighbor is opposed to the zone change. Ms. Hales explained that the overlay is a
discretionary decision and is subject to approval by the County Commission. Discussion continued
about how to mitigate effects on the adjacent landowners by requiring a minimum 20 or 40 acre
parcel for approval of the overlay. Vice Chairman Owens voiced concerns about fire protection and
Mr. Gardner verified that international building code would require fire protection.

Mr. Messel and Ms. Hales spoke concerning various upcoming changes to Title 10, Zoning
Regulations and needed code updates.
