1.      PUBLIC HEARING – A Petition for an Extension for the Final Plat Approval to
        Subdivide Eight Existing Parcels into a 61-Lot Subdivision Plat, Located at 547-617
        East 3900 South.

Action Item

Applicant:     C.W. Urban, Represented by Chase Freebairn
Address:       547-617 East 3900 South

City Planner, Tereza Bagdasarova, presented the Staff Report and explained that the application
was a petition for an extension of the Final Plat approval for property located at 547-617 East 3900
South. The intention was to subdivide eight existing parcels into a 61-lot subdivision plat. The
Monroe Townhomes Project had been before the Planning Commission in the past. On June 15,
2023, the Planning Commission approved the Final Subdivision Plat amendment for the C.W.
Urban three-lot plat. The approval came with the condition that the applicant record the final “The
Monroe” subdivision plat within 180 days of the approval.

On November 13, 2023, Chase Freebairn, representing C.W. Urban, submitted a plat application
to petition the Planning Commission for a one-year extension of the Final Subdivision Plat. The
plat extension application, along with supporting documentation and analysis of the pertinent Code
sections and General Plan goals, had remained unchanged since the previous Staff Report and
original application. Since approval of the Final Plat, the applicant has worked to fulfill the
Conditions of Approval, had applied for Building Permits, and had chosen to offer a 10% Warranty
South Salt Lake City Planning Commission Regular Meeting                                          3
February 1, 2024

Assurance. However, the plat could not be recorded until the right-of-way was complete, a process
that typically took six months. The applicant was working to ensure that conditions were met.

Ms. Bagdasarova shared images to illustrate the existing site conditions. She next presented the
Site Plan to the Planning Commission. Proposed renderings were reviewed. The top photo
highlighted the life/work building space and the bottom photo highlighted some of the amenities.
It was reiterated that the General Plan considerations had not changed since the last time the
application was reviewed. Ms. Bagdasarova noted that they were enumerated in the Staff Report.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the petition for an extension lasting
one year from the date of the request, focusing on the Final Subdivision Plat amendment to
subdivide eight existing parcels into a 61-lot subdivision plat, situated at 547-617 East 3900 South,
based on the information submitted by the applicant, the General Plan considerations and
ordinance analysis. Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval were
proposed. The extension fell within the provisions of 17.10.100(B)(7)(d) and could be granted
accordingly. The Commission had the option to approve, deny, or table discussion on the request.

Chair Carter noted that the extension would be based on the date of the request. He wondered if
that was the date the request was submitted to the City or the date of approval by the Planning
Commission. Ms. Bagdasarova clarified that it was based on the request submitted to the City,
which was November 13. There were no Commissioner questions for the applicant.

Chair Carter opened the public hearing.

James Smith gave his address as 3774 South 645 East. His main concern with the project was that
the property development in the area occurred in bits and pieces. As a 645 East resident, he wanted
to make clear that he opposed any attempt to extend 645 East onto the applicant's property.
Mr. Smith noted that there was not a lot of clarity about the road that went along the east side of
the property. The way it was presented, it looked like there would be a barrier there. He was
concerned about traffic in the area if there was not a barrier between the proposed subdivision and
the existing structures. Mr. Smith reiterated his concerns about traffic and access.

There were no further comments. The public hearing was closed.

Chair Carter asked about previous extension durations. Ms. Brass stated that it is typical to have
180 days as a Condition of Approval. By Code, it is not required but was included to move the
process along. Ms. Ungricht reported that for extensions, one year is the maximum. With more
recent extensions, there have been 360 or 365 days. Commissioner Ewell asked if 180 days was
sufficient to resolve the right-of-way issue. Ms. Brass was not certain about that. Mr. Freebairn
was present representing C.W. Urban and gave his address as 3451 Redtail Way in Layton. He
confirmed that 180 days was sufficient. The plan was to break ground next month. Demolition
was completed last month. The hope was to have the plat recorded in late spring or early summer.

Commissioner Slifka noted that she had not been part of the discussions that took place last year.
She asked about the concerns expressed during the public hearing regarding the layout. Ms. Brass
clarified that the petition was for the extension. The Final Subdivision had already been approved.

         Construct and Operate an Indoor Climate Controlled Storage Development, Located
         at 2385 South 300 West.

Action Item

Applicant:      A8-South Salt Lake, LLC, Represented by Jarod Hall
Address:        2385 South 300 West

Ms. Ungricht presented the Staff Report and explained that the application was for Final Plat
approval for a one-lot plat for property located at 2385 South 300 West. The matter was before
the Planning Commission at the last meeting. There were two separate applications associated
with the project including the Plat Application and Conditional Use Permit for an Indoor Climate
Controlled Storage Facility. Images of the existing conditions were shared. Ms. Ungricht reported
that there is an externally loaded storage building with 140 existing units on the site. The proposed
elevations were reviewed. As part of the project, an Indoor Climate Controlled Storage Facility
use was added. There were design standards associated with it that the building would meet. She
explained that there will be 40% windows on the primary façade and 20% windows on the side
and rear facades. The ALTA survey was shared. She reminded Commissioners that there was a
lengthier process due to the relocation of a storm drain. Currently, it is at the top of the property,
but the storm drain will be relocated to the south. She reminded the Commissioners that there is
a billboard on the site, which was outlined in orange. The billboard would remain, and the
proposed building would not conflict with the existing easement.

The Proposed Site Plan was shared. Ms. Ungricht explained that the red sections identify fire
access. There are seven parking stalls, which meet the Code as well as a loading truck area that is
separate from the customer parking area. That loading truck area was shown in the center, and it
would allow trucks and vehicles to pull in to load the items that would be stored. The Proposed
Fencing Plan was shared next. For the most part, the perimeter of the property will be fenced. The
building that fronts 300 West, which is not being changed as part of this application, will act as a
fence for a portion of the property. There are two automatic gates as well. One is on 300 West,
which will be for customers to use. There is also an access road where there will be a security
fence for fire access. The Final Plat was reviewed. It included right-of-way dedication that varied
from 0.5 feet to 0.63 feet along 300 West. The billboard easement was shown as well as the new
storm drain easement mentioned earlier.

and the addresses are 3535 South State Street, 130 East Winslow Avenue, and 3549-3555 South
200 East. She reported that the property was before the Planning Commission two weeks ago for

The Proposed Site Plan was shared. Ms. Ungricht explained that landscaping will be installed
