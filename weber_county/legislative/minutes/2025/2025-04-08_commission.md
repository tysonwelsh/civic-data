    3. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 21-2025 ADOPTING THE WEBER COUNTY BUSINESS
       RECRUITMENT, EXPANSION AND DEVELOPMENT POLICY.

         Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): Weber County has incredible partners in the County
         and the State. There are some unique bills passed this last session relative to economic development that has
         led to confusion on who has authority to do what in counties and cities related to state incentives and initiatives.
         We gathered our state partners together and developed a policy where we can all work cohesively together in
         unincorporated areas like west Weber County, specifically for business recruitment, expansion, and actual
         development projects. The goal was to establish a clear and structured economic development approach and
         to ensure cooperation between Weber County departments, regional economic organizations, and state
         partners. We need to make reference the Western Weber County General Plan when doing this type of business
         and recruitment. ... We use that Plan as the template and roadmap. The policy has several components. The
         first is the Weber County Economic Development Department and the Weber County Community
         Development Department will oversee implementation and administration. The second part is coordination
         with regional partners such as the Northern Utah Economic Alliance, Governor’s Office of Economic
         Opportunity, the Economic Development Corporation of Utah, and other relevant entities. Third part is public
         finance tools and incentives. The Weber County Economic Development Finance Committee shall be
         responsible for evaluation and recommending appropriate use of public finance tools including Public
         Infrastructure Districts (PIDs), Community Reinvestment Areas (CRAs), and Tax Increment Financing (TIF).
         Commissioner Froerer: This policy helps our state partners understand who they should talk to first before
         making decisions or promises that impact Weber County.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 21-2025 adopting the Weber County Business
         Recruitment, Expansion and Development Policy; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
