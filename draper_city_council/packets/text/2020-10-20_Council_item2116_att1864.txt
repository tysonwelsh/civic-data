Battalion Chief Kevin Holt is over the medical services provided by Draper City Fire Department with
responsibility over our certifications, medical training, and protocols for treatment. Our response to 911
calls during the COVID Pandemic has had to change to provide additional protection to both our
personnel, as well as the patients and their families. Battalion Chief Holt has worked tirelessly to update
or response protocols, in coordination with our Offline-Medical Control Doctor, and our Dispatch
Center. Kevin has also put department policies in place to safeguard our personnel from potential
exposure while at work through daily health and temperature screening, and ensuring we have proper
PPE for day-to-day operations and response. Kevin participates in several weekly conference calls to
discuss COVID infection and transmission rates and effective response measures. He also coordinates
the testing and proper reporting for our potentially exposed personnel. Battalion Chief Holt is an
invaluable member of the Draper Fire Executive Staff and a true fire and EMS professional.
