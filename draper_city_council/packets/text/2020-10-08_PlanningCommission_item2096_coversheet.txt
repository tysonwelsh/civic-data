Re: Sequoias – Concept Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Concept Plan for approximately 109.94 acres located at approximately 3235 East 15780 South. The property is currently zoned RR-22 and is located within the Suncrest Development Agreement Area . The applicant is requesting that a Concept Plan be approved to allow for future subdivision applications for a residential subdivision. The findings are as follows: For approval:
There are no findings for approval at this time.
For denial:
1. The DA approved Concept Plan for the Suncrest Development shows two public access points to the proposed development area.  The proposed Concept Plan only contains one public access point to the proposed development. Only 30 dwelling units are allowed on a single access point, regardless of the road being public or private, per International Fire Code Section D107.1 and the proposed Concept Plan shows 280 dwelling units. The Concept Plan cannot be approved with more units than allowed by the International Fire Code. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 10/2/2020 Cover Memo
