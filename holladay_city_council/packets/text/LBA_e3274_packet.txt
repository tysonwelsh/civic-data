                                  RESOLUTION LBA NO. 2026-02

                A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE LOCAL
                BUILDING AUTHORITY OF THE CITY OF HOLLADAY ACCEPTING
                AND TENTATIVELY ADOPTING THE 2026-2027 LBA TENTATIVE
                BUDGET
      WHEREAS, State law requires that the Local Building Authority of the City of Holladay
(“LBA”) adopt a budget for the receipt and use of LBA funds; and
        WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees desires to accept a tentative budget to provide a period
of study and comment on the eventual Final Budget for the LBA for the operating year 2026-2027;

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE LOCAL
BUILDING AUTHORITY OF THE CITY OF HOLLADAY as follows:
         (1) That the FY 2026-2027 Tentative Budget submitted by the Chair to the Board of
             Trustees is hereby accepted for further consideration by the Board.
         (2) That a public hearing to consider the proposed Final Budget be set for June 4, 2026, at
             6:00 p.m., or as soon thereafter as possible, at the Temporary City of Holladay Offices,
             3330 S 1300 E., Millcreek, Utah 84106.

       ADOPTED by the Board of Trustees of the Local Building Authority of the City of Holladay,
Utah this __ day of May, 2026.
