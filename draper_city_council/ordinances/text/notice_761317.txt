City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption - 
On June 7, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1542, amending the Draper City Municipal Code 15-1-075, pertaining to regulations for public property adjacent to parade routes. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder's Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 8th day of June 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
