                               MINUTES OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
                                     CITY COUNCIL MEETING

                                    Tuesday, May 09, 2023 – 6:00 PM
                                        Approved May 24, 2023

                                       Thomas M. Rees Justice Center
                               8040 S Redwood Road • West Jordan, UT 84088



CITY COUNCIL MEETING 6:00 PM
 1. CALL TO ORDER
     COUNCIL: Chair Christopher McConnehey, Vice-Chair Pamela Bloom, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, David
              Pack, Kayleen Whitelock, and Melissa Worthen
     STAFF:   Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson, Policy Analyst & Public Liaison Cassidy Hansen,
              City Administrator Korban Lee, Economic Development Director Chris Pengra,
              Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck

Chair McConnehey called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm and noted all council were present. He
asked for patience as they worked through technical difficulties with the audio and Zoom. He also
noted that Mayor Burton was participating via Zoom.

 2. PUBLIC COMMENT
Chair Council Chair Christopher McConnehey opened the public comment period at 6:02 pm

Citizen Comments:
Catherine Richardson resident of West Jordan voiced concern with the approval of a recent fee
waiver for the Day of Kids Community event. She felt the fee waiver showed partiality and favoritism
and was not fair.

Council Chair Christopher McConnehey closed public comment at 6:04 pm

 3. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Acceptance and receipt of the Mayor’s Tentative Budget for the City of West Jordan,
          Utah for Fiscal Year 2024.
          It was requested that Mayor Burton be given the opportunity to present the budget via
          zoom. However, due to technical difficulties it was not possible at the time.

 4. ADJOURN
Council Member Whitelock proposed temporarily recessing City Council meeting to convene in the
Fairway Estates meeting. Chair McConnehey agreed and called for a motion.

Council Member Green moved to recess the City Council meeting to convene in agency board
meetings. Council Member Whitelock seconded the motion.

Chair McConnehey clarified the motion. He explained that Council was holding two other meetings
for Fairway Estates and Redevelopment Agency boards. During those meetings they would be
holding discussions regarding those budgets. He called for a vote on the motion.

All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously (7-0).

At 6:09 pm the meeting recessed.

At 6:52 pm Chair McConnehey reconvened the meeting to revisit item 3a. Acceptance and receipt
of the Mayor’s Tentative Budget for the City of West Jordan, Utah for Fiscal Year 2024.

Council Member Green moved to formally accept the tentative budget for fiscal year 2024.
Council Member Whitelock seconded the motion. All voted in favor and the motion passed
unanimously (7-0).

Council Member Green moved to adjourn the meeting. Council Member Whitelock seconded
the motion. All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously (7-0).

The meeting adjourned at 6:56 pm
