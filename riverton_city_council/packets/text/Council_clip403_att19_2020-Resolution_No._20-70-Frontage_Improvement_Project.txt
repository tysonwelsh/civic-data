                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 20-70

      A RESOLUTION APPROVING A PO CONTRACT ISSUED TO BECK
  CONSTRUCTION TO COMPLETE THE FRONTAGE IMPROVEMENT PROJECT
                     (12400 SOUTH 1136 WEST)

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the City Engineer has determined that this project is needed.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

      1. The City Council approves a PO Contract issued to Beck Construction to complete
         the Frontage Improvement Project (12400 South 1136 West)

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 17th day of
November 2020 by the following vote:

                                          YES    NO
