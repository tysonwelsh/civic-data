    1.4     Consideration                 of ZT-21-003,                a Recommendation                           to Amend           the Millcreek                   Zoning
    Ordinance                to Enact           a City       Center        Overlay          - Development                  Agreement                Zone         Location:
    3332      S. 1300           E. Planner:              Francis        Lilly
    Francis         Lilly       said Robert            May     and John           Brems          assisted     on the ordinance                    proposal.           The
    purpose          of the proposed                zone would             be to further            advance          the goals           and vision         of the
    Millcreek           City      Center         Master       Plan,     to craft         more      specific         standards            pursuant         to a
    development                 agreement,             and to accommodate                    a unique         situation           into    the City         Center           and
    Common.             Lilly        said it was to create                 a vehicle        by which          a developer                could     propose            alternative
    standards.          He said typically                  an overlay           zoning       district       presents        stricter        standards            and criteria,
    but this would               be different            as a floating           zone      that would             get applied            by virtue        of a
    development                 agreement.           He presented               three      diagrams         of the City            Center:         one with           the
    underlying              zones,       one with          the City        Center        Overlay          Zone      (CCOZ),              and one with            the
    proposed          City       Center          Overlay       Zone        - Development                  Agreement           (CCOZ-DA).                   The        CCOZ          has
    three     districts         within         the city      center     with       different        standards:            the Neighborhood                   District,            Mill
    Center,         and Market             Place.        He showed           the Commission                   an updated            rendering             of Millcreek
    Common.


    Lilly     pointed           out that        an overlay          zone     with        a development               agreement             concept         was used in
    other     cities.        The process            allows        the city       some       flexibility           to leverage            variations         in existing
    zoning         standards           and obtain          public      goods.        For example,                 a developer            may      provide            schools,
    parks,     open         space,       plazas,        low-income              housing,         or money,           in exchange             for greater             flexibility
    in required             building           setbacks,      floor     heights,          lot area, parking               requirements,              number            of
    dwellings,           and other             minimum          standards.          The      CCOZ-DA                overlay         zone provides                a
    mechanism               for the applicant                and the city           to seek a particular                  development               in the City             Center,
Millcreek       Planning       Commission              Meeting           Minutes                        19 May        2021         Page 8 of 14



    and to have it regulated                 by conditions            in a development              agreement.       Each development
    agreement        (and therefore,           each zone)             would     be a unique         project,     and would         be regulated
    under       that development             agreement.         Where         a development           agreement         is silent     on a particular
    matter,      the base standards            of the CCOZ              and underlining             zone would          apply.     In order to give
    the City      maximum           discretion        to approve         or deny any proposed                  deviation      from      the CCOZ
    standards,      staff recornrnended               an additional            overlay      zone, where         the standards           would         be
    regulated      through       a development              agreement           that would        be crafted        specifically        for that
