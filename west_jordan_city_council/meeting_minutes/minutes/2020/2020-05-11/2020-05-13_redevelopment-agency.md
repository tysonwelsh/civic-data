DISCUSSION AND POSSIBLE ACTION REGARDING RESOLUTION NO. 221
Board Member Whitelock explained that she works part-time for the Woodbury
Corporation, and recused herself from this issue.

Paul Coates explained that the RDA owns property on the south side of 9000 South
between Mountain View Corridor and 5600 West. Jordan Starr LLC owns the parcel at the
northeast corner of the RDA parcel on which a Maverik fuel center and convenience store
is proposed to be developed. The proposed access agreement would allow access across
each parcel in order to access the other parcel – allowing for the eventual development of
both parcels. Cross access easements are common in situations with adjoining commercial
parcels. Jordan Starr LLC will be responsible for maintenance of the entire easement area
until the RDA parcel is developed. Once the RDA parcel is developed, each property owner
will maintain the portion of the cross-access easement area located on its respective parcel.
Proposed Resolution No. 221 would authorize the Executive Director to sign a Reciprocal
Access Agreement.

MOTION:             Board Member Green moved to accept Resolution No. 221, authorizing
                    the Executive Director to sign the Reciprocal Access Agreement. Board
                    Member Jacob seconded the motion.

A roll call vote was taken

Chair McConnehey                           Yes
