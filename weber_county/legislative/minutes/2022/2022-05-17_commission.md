         1.    Request for a motion to adjourn public meeting and convene public hearings.

         2.       Public Hearing for discussion and/or take action on a county-initiated ordinance to define specific
         zones in the Ogden Valley Planning Area where dwelling unit rights may be transferred from for the
         purposes of constructing detached accessory dwelling units, and to include general administrative and
         clerical amendments-ZTA 2021-11.
                  Scott Perkes (Planning): Changes address updates to existing accessory dwelling unit ordinance. No
                  STRs currently allowed in ADUs. Use of a detached ADU requires secondary density right,
                  necessitating transfer from another lot or parcel containing a surplus of density rights. Proposing
                  transfer of density rights for the purpose of constructing detached ADUs be allowed only from RE-
                  15, RE-20, AV-3, FV-3 and S-1 zones.

                  Commisisoner Froerer: This follows the existing General Plan to protect the valley floor. Part of the
                  Affordable Housing Plan required by the state and passed by the County.

         3.       Public Comments:
