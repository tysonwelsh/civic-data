SUBDIVISION REVIEWS – ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION

The Pointe @ 53rd Office Condos - 5217 South State Street - 1 year Extension of Preliminary
Subdivision Approval - Project # 23-005

Mr. Smallwood presented the application for The Pointe @ 53rd requesting an extension of a
preliminary Subdivision approval, which is allowed in this case. He reminded the Planning
Commission that they previously approved a subdivision amendment and relocated some lines
to create a specific parcel. It will be a 0.29 lot with a total of 16 office condominiums. The
property is located in the CMU zone. Mr. Smallwood briefly showed slides from the original
presentation. He noted that each floor will have a set of four units. The ground floor has four
units for commercial space, and then the additional floors will each have four units of office
space. The applicant is proposing 92 parking spaces. If this extension is approved, the applicant
will have until January 4, 2025, to apply for a final subdivision approval, or it becomes null and
void. They are only allowed to extend it by one year. Staff recommends that the Planning
Commission grant the extension for The Point @ 53rd, office building condo conversion, at the
property of 5217 South State Street subject to the nine conditions.

Chair Pehrson asked if there were questions. Seeing none, he invited the applicant, Chris
Johnson, to approach the podium. He asked if Mr. Johnson can comply with the conditions
listed, to which he replied yes.

Chair Pehrson opened and closed the public comment period, with no comments being made.

Commissioner Nay made a motion that the Planning Commission grant a one-year time
extension for final Subdivision approval for the point @ 53rd, office building condo conversion, on
the property located 5217 South State Street subjected to the nine conditions:
1. The project shall meet Murray City Engineering requirements including the following:
    a) Must complete and record the Point @ 53rd Amended plat.
    b) Meet City subdivision and condominium platting requirements.
2. The applicant shall meet all Wastewater Division requirements.
3. The applicant shall meet all Water Division requirements.
4. The applicant shall meet all Power Department requirements including:
    a) Review whether individual electrical meters or other changes to metering/service are
       required by the Power Department.
    b) Meet with Murray City Power to discuss planning any new power service(s) and future
       equipment placement to the building(s) with additional line extension costs to provide
       electrical service.
