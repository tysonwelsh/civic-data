Present                                          Absent

Councilmember Brett Clawson

Staff Present: City Attorney Jayme Blakesley, City Manager Eric Ellis, Community Development
Director Morgan Brim, Public Works Director Naseem Ghandour, City Recorder Pamela Spencer,
and Deputy Recorder Tony Lara



1.   CALL TO ORDER
     Mayor Fullmer opened the meeting at 5:01 PM.


2.   CLOSED SESSION
    Mayor Fullmer called for a motion to go into a closed session. Councilmember Holdaway
asked for clarification on what they could discuss before they went into a closed session. City
Attorney Jayme Blakesley explained the process and reason for going into closed sessions. A
discussion ensued.

    Motion: COUNCILMEMBER SIFUENTES MOVED TO GO INTO A CLOSED SESSION
IMMEDIATELY IN THE CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS TO HOLD A STRATEGY SESSION
TO DISCUSS PENDING OR REASONABLY IMMINENT LITIGATION. ROLL CALL WENT
AS FOLLOWS: MAYOR FULLMER, COUNCILMEMBERS CAMERON, CLAWSON, AND
SIFUENTES VOTED YES, COUNCILMEMBER HOLDAWAY VOTED NO. THE MOTION
CARRIED FOUR (4) TO ONE (1).
