Discussion and review of potential amendments to the Eastern Summit County General Plan. The Planning Commission will provide direction and examine the scope, intent and process for the updates, but will take no formal action.
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner, Mustapha Osman, County Planner. (6:11 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
East Side Staff Report Feb 5, 2026.pdf
Principal Planner Ray Milliner and Planner Mustapha Osman introduced the draft amendments to the Eastern Summit County General Plan for a work session discussion. They explained that the proposed amendments would be brought before the Commission over a series of future meetings. Elements of the plan discussed included Land Use, Housing, Transportation and Connectivity, Economic Development, Natural and Cultural Resources, Open Space, Community Health & Recreation, Water Use, Public Facilities and Services, Neighborhood Plans and Implementation. (6:12 PM)
Commissioners asked questions and gave feedback during a discussion on potential amendments to the Eastern Summit County General Plan. Commissioners proposed painting a picture of the general plan subjects, including public transportation, land use and area, affordable housing, wildfire and fire infrastructure, water, and density. Planners Milliner and Osman responded to the questions and outlined the process moving forward. (6:18 PM)
