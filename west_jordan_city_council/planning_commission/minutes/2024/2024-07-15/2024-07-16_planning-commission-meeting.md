2. QES Skyhawk; 4658 West Skyhawk Drive; Review of street-facing bay doors; M-1 (ZC)

Zone; DS Partnership LLC/Ken Worton (applicant) (#33115; parcel 21-19-302-006]

The applicant was not in attendance.

Tayler Jensen said that the Planning Commission is required to approve any street-facing bay doors, but

a public hearing is not required. One building will have bay doors facing south onto the public right-of-

way. The applicant has proposed landscaping with trees along the right-of-way to block the visual

appearance and bushes and rocks to screen the lower areas between the ground and the tree canopy.

There will probably only be industrial traffic passing by the property. He said that the Design Review

Committee reviewed the proposal and did not have any concerns.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the street-facing bay doors for QES Skyhawk

located at 4658 West Skyhawk Drive.

Trish Hatch thought that the code was unnecessary.

George Winn said he visited the site and thought that the bay doors would not cause concern to anyone,

because it is in a totally industrial area.

MOTION: _ Trish Hatch moved to approve the street-facing bay doors for QES Skyhawk located
at 4658 West Skyhawk Drive with the landscaping shown in the report. The motion
was seconded by Emily Gonzalez and passed 6-0 in favor. Jay Thomas was absent.

3. Jordan Valley Station; Continued from July 2, 2024; 3400 West 8600 South; Amended
Development Agreement for the purpose of extending the expiration date; P-C (TSOD)
Zone; Bangerter Station Associates, LLC [#33113]
Duncan Murray said under current state law, all development agreements and amendments are land use
regulations that require a recommendation to City Council from the Planning Commission in a public
hearing.
Tayler Jensen said the proposal is to amend the Jordan Valley Station development agreement by
extending the expiration date. He provided a history of the agreement. The current expiration date is
September 24, 2029, and the amendment would push it to 2039. Staff recommended that the Planning
Commission forward a positive recommendation to approve the proposed extension of the Jordan
Valley Station Development Agreement as presented.
George Winn opened the public hearing.
Alec Taylor, representing Boulder Ventures, said the real purpose of the extension is to allow them to
continue to provide a class A product in West Jordan like they have been doing for the past 30 years,
including the development of Jordan Landing. They are proud of the community that has been built at
Jordan Valley Station. Current developments include the Novi, Upper West, and Momentum
apartments and Starbucks, which are all quality products. The extension will give them time to
thoughtfully plan each of the next phases and to build something that everyone can be proud of.
Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.
George Winn asked what the current occupancy is and what is left to be done.
Alec Tayler said Novi has 270 units at 96% occupancy, Upper West 207 units at 95% occupancy, and
Momentum is about 50% occupied and should be fully leased out by the end of the year. The
remaining land will be built out in phases according to market demand and is entitled to 676 additional
residential units and 103,320 square feet of commercial/retail space.
MOTION: John Roberts moved that the Planning Commission forward a positive
recommendation to the City Council for the Amended Jordan Valley Station
Development Agreement as presented. The motion was seconded by Emily
Gonzalez and passed 6-0 in favor. Jay Thomas was absent.
