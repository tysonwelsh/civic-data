Kearns Metro Township 2020 Budget

              THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to hear public comment
on the 2020 recommended budget for the Kearns Metro Township.

              The Council reviewed the Kearns Metro Township 2020 Budget.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to open
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

             No one spoke in favor of or in opposition to the proposed 2020 Kearns Metro
Township budget.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to close the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to approve
the following Resolution adopting the final budget for the 2020 fiscal year:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-12-01                                              Date: December 20, 2019

       A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE FINAL BUDGET FOR THE 2020 FISCAL
       YEAR FOR THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP

       BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Kearns Metro Township Council (the
“Council”) held a public hearing on December 20, 2019, to take public comment regarding the
adoption of a final budget for the 2020 fiscal year beginning on January 1, 2020, and ending on
December 31, 2020.



                                              1

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the State of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-3c-202(2) specifies that the fiscal year for metro townships
within a municipal services district such as Kearns is the calendar year;

        WHEREAS, on December 20, 2019, the Council approved a tentative budget for Kearns
for the 2020 fiscal year and scheduled a public hearing for December 20, 2019 to receive public
comment regarding the adoption of the tentative budget pursuant to Utah Code § 10-5-108; and

       WHEREAS, the Council provided notice of the date, time, and place of the public hearing
in accordance with Utah Code § 10-5-108; and

       WHEREAS, the Council made the tentative budget available for public inspection pursuant
to Utah Code Ann. § 10-5-107 for a period of at least seven days prior to holding a public hearing
in accordance with; and

        WHEREAS, on December 20, 2019 at 6:00 p.m., the Council held a public hearing on the
tentative budget in accordance with Utah Code § 10-5-108 at 5658 South Cougar Lane Kearns,
Utah 84118.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

   1. The tentative budget, as amended (if amended) and attached hereto, is adopted as
      Kearns’s final budget for the 2020 fiscal year.

   2. The Mayor will file the final budget with the State Auditor no later than thirty (30) days after
      the date of this resolution.

   3. The effective date of this resolution is December 20, 2019.

       ADOPTED AND APPROVED by majority vote at a duly called public hearing of the Kearns
Metro Township Council on this 20th day of December 2019.

              THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to hear public comment
on the 2020 recommended budget for the Kearns Metro Township.

              The Council reviewed the Kearns Metro Township 2019 budget adjustments.
