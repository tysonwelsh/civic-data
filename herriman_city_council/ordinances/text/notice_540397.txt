NOTICE OF ADOPTION OF PROJECT AREA PLAN FOR THE HERRIMAN NORTH COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT PROJECT AREA 

On May 22, 2019, pursuant to Limited Purpose Local Government Entities - Community Reinvestment Agency Act in Utah Code Title 17C (the 'Act'), the City Council of Herriman City by its Ordinance No. 2019-10 ('Ordinance') adopted, designated and approved the Herriman North Community Reinvestment Project Area Plan (the 'Project Area Plan') dated April 2019, as the official project area plan for the Herriman North Community Reinvestment Project Area (the 'Project Area'). The Project Area consists of approximately 117.3 acres located along the western edge of the Mountain View Corridor and immediately north of 12600 South (Herriman Parkway). The Ordinance sets forth the legal description of the boundary of the Project Area and adopts the Project Area Plan as approved by the Redevelopment Agency of Herriman City. The Ordinance and the Project Area Plan shall become effective upon publication of this notice (the 'Effective Date'), at which time the Agency may proceed to implement the Project Area Plan. The Ordinance and the Project Area Plan are available for public inspection in the office of the City of Herriman Recorder and of the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman, 5355 West Herriman Main Street Herriman, Utah, Monday through Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., excluding holidays. For a period of 30 days after the Effective Date (the '30-Day Period'), any person in interest may contest the Plan or the procedures used to adopt it if the Plan or procedures fail to comply with applicable statutory requirements. After the 30-Day Period, no person may contest the Plan or procedures used to adopt the Plan for any cause.
