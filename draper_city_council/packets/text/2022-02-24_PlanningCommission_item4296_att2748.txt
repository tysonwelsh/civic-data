BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Zoning and Land Use Map Amendment for
approximately 1.44 acres located on the north side of 13800 South, at approximately 231
East 13800 South (Exhibit B). The properties are currently zoned RA1 and OR. The applicant
is requesting that a Zoning and Land Use Map Amendment be approved to allow for the
development of the currently vacant site for retail uses. A questionnaire from the applicant
outlining the current request can be found in Exhibit E.

The property includes seven parcels. There used to be three homes on the properties, but
these were demolished when the City improved the 13800 South corridor in 2017. All seven
properties are currently vacant.

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Office Service
land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is characterized as

The property has been assigned the RA1 and OR zoning classification (Exhibit D). According
to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA1 zone is to
“foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to
generally preserve the character of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and preserve
conditions favorable to large-lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals
and fowl. This zone is intended to promote a combination of retail and service facilities which, in
character and scale, meet day to day needs of nearby residents.”

DCMC Section 9-11-080(A) provides the purpose for the OR zone as “a district composed of
certain land and structures used primarily to provide administrative, clerical, and professional
offices, of a residential scale and character, to serve nearby residential and commercial areas, as
well as the city as a whole. These uses are characterized by low volume, direct daily customer
contact. This district also provides for accessory medium-high density residential (0 to 12 du/ac).
This district is designed to be a transitional zone, and should be used to buffer low density
residential uses from more intense land uses, districts, and heavily traveled transportation
routes. The property development standards, while strict in order to protect adjacent low density
residential uses, are designed to be flexible enough to allow experimentation in office and
housing design, and to allow housing constructed within this district to incorporate its own
protection from more intense adjacent uses.”

Zoning Map Amendment
A Text and Map Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City
Council and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation
to the City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in
Section 9-5-060(E) of the DCMC:

        1. Map Amendments:
            a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies
               of the city's general plan;
            b. Whether the proposed amendment is harmonious with the overall character of
               existing development in the vicinity of the subject property;

   1. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including but not

       Other than noted below, we are not aware of any inadequacies of the facilities intended
       to serve this property.

          a. The increase in density from one residential unit per acre to community
             commercial zoning constitutes a significant traffic increase to and from the
             subject area. One single family residential structure constitutes up to nine
             daily trips and two peak hour trips, based on industry standard vehicle trip
             tables. In the proposed community commercial zoning, there are several
             high intensity uses that will create high traffic trips to and from the subject
             parcels. This increase in traffic will impact will be exclusively to 13800 South
             whereas no access to Bangerter Highway is permitted by Utah Department
             of Transportation (UDOT). 13800 South is a major collector designed to
             accommodate the large volume. Traffic issues in the area are at peak travel
             times and will stack across the front of the subject parcels. Due to the
             extensive traffic, cross access shall be required with accent projects to limit
             the number of accesses onto 13800 South. A Traffic Impact Study may be
             required with a site plan or subdivision application based on the proposed
             use and projected traffic volume.

          b. Connectivity with the subject parcel to a public right-of-way is on 13800 South, a
             major collector street owned by Draper City. No access is permitted to Bangerter
             Highway. Access locations will be required to meet Draper City Standards and
             access management plan as outlined in our Master Transportation Plan.

          c. There are existing storm drain facilities available to the subject area. Provisions for
             onsite storm drainage will need to be addressed with any subdivision or site plan
             application, and shall comply with the provisions of the development
             requirements within the Draper City Municipal Code.

          d. Sanitary sewer facilities will be provided by South Valley Sewer District. Any
             subdivision application will require a commitment to serve from the Sewer District
             that facilities are adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.

Zone District

1. What is the present zoning of the property?

      Approximately 2/3 of the area of this property on the eastern side is zoned O-R (Office
       Residential) and the additional 1/3 on the west side (directly adjacent to Bangerter Hwy)
       is zoned RA1 (Residential Agricultural).

2. Is the proposed zoning consistent with the current land use designation? Please explain.

      Though O-R (Office Residential) is somewhat similar to the Office/Service designation,
       RA1 (Residential Agricultural) is not consistent with the current land use designation.
       Our proposal is to allow commercial retail and food service.

3. Is the proposed zoning similar or compatible to the current zoning in the same area? Please
explain.

      Our proposed zoning of Community Commercial is both similar and compatible to the
       current zoning in the area. The other three properties located on the corners of 13800 S
       and Bangerter Highway all have commercial zoning designations. Two of the corners are
       Community Commercial and the other is Regional Commercial.

4. Is the proposed zoning suitable for the proposed site? Please explain.

      Approving our proposed zoning change would create consistency at this intersection that
       would benefit the surrounding area and community. Considering current traffic levels and
       the surrounding areas, the Community Commercial zone makes sense for this location.

1. What is the present land use designation of the property?

      The present land use designation is Office/Service, our proposal is to change it to
       Community/Neighborhood Commercial.

2. Is the proposed land use designation similar or compatible with other land use designations in
the immediate area? Please explain.

      Yes, two of the other three corners of the intersection are already
       Community/Neighborhood Commercial with the remaining corner being designated as
       Regional Commercial.

3. What do you anticipate the land being used for?

      We anticipate this land to be used for food service and retail.

4. How would the proposed land use designation affect Draper City?

      Two of our parcels in the current proposal already do not comply with the land use
       designations so a change would allow for all of our parcels to be compliant. Considering
       the current traffic patterns of the area, a land use designation of
       Community/Neighborhood Commercial would have no adverse effects on Draper City.
