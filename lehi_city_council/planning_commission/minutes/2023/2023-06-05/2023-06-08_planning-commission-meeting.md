Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commissioner Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Consideration of Costco Wholesale’s request for approval of the Costco Fuel Station
Expansion located at 198 North 1200 East in the Commercial zone.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item. Administrative item; not a public
hearing item.

The applicants were present that were representing Costco. The application aims to improve the
wait time, the traffic flow, and the efficiency of the fuel facility. The project consists of six fuel
dispensary expansions. There was a recommendation for landscaping from Commissioner Eyre
for visual appeal and maintenance that the applicant was willing to change. Commissioner
Everett asked if they were going to be striping. The applicants hope the queuing striping
wouldn't be necessary as adding fuel pumps will help keep the flow moving. Fuel drops will be
about three times a day with 30,000 gallons and will be in the northern drop area. The new
dispensers are further away from the older dispensers so passing should be easier.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Costco Wholesale’s request for
        approval of the Costco Fuel Station Expansion located at 198 North 1200 East in the
        Commercial zone; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.2) Public hearing and consideration of Boyer Company’s request for conditional use
approval of the Holbrook Industrial Building 5 Site Plan located at approximately 4886
West Hudson Way.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Logan with the Boyer Company, was present to represent the Boyer Company.
The building will match what is in the area. Commissioner Everett asked about making the
adjustment from 75 feet to 50 feet as recommended by DRC. Logan said he would return it to the
project team for follow-up.

Commissioner Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Boyer Company’s request for
        conditional use approval of the Holbrook Industrial Building 5 Site Plan located at
        approximately 4886 West Hudson Way; including the DRC comments, that the
        proposed use, at the proposed location, will not be detrimental or injurious to property or
        improvements in the vicinity and will not be detrimental to the public health, safety,
        general welfare or convenience. The proposed use will not be located and conducted in a
        manner in compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General Plan and the
        purposes of this Code. That the property on which the use, building, or other structure is
        proposed to be established is of adequate size and dimensions to permit the construction
        of the facilities and the conduct of the use in such a manner that it will not be detrimental
        to adjoining properties and the area. Commissioner Bunker seconded. Amended by
        Commissioner Everett: strike will not be detrimental to the public. The second still
        stands.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Jeff Olsen’s request for review of a
Development Code amendment to Chapter 39, Definitions and the 05.030.B, Nonresidential
Table of Uses to add Concrete Manufacturing, Small Haul use and definition.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Randy Yates, and Jeff Olsen were present. They clarified Commissioner Eyre's
question for small scale by definition. The yardage was 2 yards of material or less and would

operate from 8:00 am to 4:30 pm. It was recommended by DRC for heavy commercial and light
industrial but would not be addressed as such tonight because it had not been noticed.

Commissioner Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Jeff Olsen’s request
        for review of a Development Code amendment to Chapter 39, Definitions and the
        05.030.B, Nonresidential Table of Uses to add Concrete Manufacturing, Small Haul
        use and definition; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

3. City Business

3.1) Presentation and discussion of Dry Creek Village Development (Mixed-use Overlay).
Mike West presented information on the mixed-use village overlay. The overlay zone is intended
to network open space and a livable community.

Tony Trane showed the Commissioners the community feel that they have been working with
the City. The piece itself has five different landowners. They intend to keep farming. The
concentration of phase one is owned by Flagship Homes. Making trail corridors and connectivity
in the community involves three D': diversity, density, and design. With the fourteen acres are
needed a concept and land use plan. The school district and landowners are involved with the
land use plan. The community will have a lot of walking and biking. Staff and Trane Engineering
have been working together on a concept to present to the property owners. A lot of the
recommendations from staff are being incorporated into the development.
