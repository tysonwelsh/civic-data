  1.    Subdivision lots and layout shall be consistent with the plans presented to the Planning
        Commission except where otherwise noted.
  2.    Final Plat must be recorded with Salt Lake County.



Background

This is a public hearing for amending several lots in the existing Riverton Cove subdivision.
Riverton Cove Subdivision was approved by the city in 2022, with 31 building lots and 7 shared
community spaces. The subdivision is in the RM-6 zone with detached houses on minimum lot
size of 5,000-sf.

This proposed plat amendment would convert portions of two (2) of the community spaces into
private yard space which would be added to the existing lots. No new lots will be created. No
community space that is required for storm drain or other utilities is being converted.

The applicant represents all the property owners involved. Each impacted property owner,
including the HOA signed Ownership Declarations, granting Isabel Jones the right to pursue this
application on their behalf. Additionally, all owners in the subdivision were notified of this public
hearing. Finally, after this hearing the amended plat will not be recorded unless each affected
owner signs the mylar in front of a notary.

The proposed changes increases the private yard space of Lots 1 thru 3, and Lots 24-29. The
shared community parcels known as Parcel B and Parcel G will be reduced in size.
