Vice Chair Bloom called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm, and being that she was joining the meeting
remotely, turned the responsibility of conducting the meeting over to Council Office Director Alan
Anderson. With no objection from the Council. Mr. Anderson put the names of Council Members
present in a hat to draw for Acting Chair until a new Chair was elected later in the evening. Assistant
City Attorney Patrick Boice drew out Chad Lamb’s name to be Acting Chair. Acting Chair Lamb
recognized former-Council Member Chris McConnehey as being present and thanked him for his
service on the Council.

 2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Chris McConnehey led participants in the Pledge of Allegiance.

 3. PUBLIC COMMENT
Acting Chair Lamb chose to postpone the election of a Council Chair and Vice Chair until Council
Member Bedore arrived.

Acting Chair Lamb opened the public comment period at 6:05 pm.

Comments:
Kathy McConnehey, West Jordan resident, requested the time frame for cleaning graves in the
cemetery move from one week to at least two weeks between November and March. She reported
having a wreath removed from her husband’s grave that she had used year after year and was sad to
lose the wreath holder that was made by her son. Ms. McConnehey appreciated the cleaning of the
cemetery, and asked if a two-week period could be given during months that no lawn care was done.

Chris McConnehey, West Jordan resident and former Council Member, seconded Ms. McConnehey’s

Ryan Wilkinson reported that he asked months ago whether West Jordan City had any liability for

Alexandra Eframo was disappointed the Council had not acknowledged 50 residents in attendance

Acting Chair Lamb closed public comments at 6:22 pm

Rob Bennett knew of other cities and entities with a text-based cell phone service that citizens could
sign up for to receive updates regarding meetings, weather, and other items of importance to the
community. He believed such a service would be appreciated by West Jordan citizens.

 4. COUNCIL BUSINESS
       a. Resolution No. 24-001 certifying the 2024 elections results for Council Chair and Vice
          Chair
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson instructed the Council regarding the election of a
          new Council Chair and Vice Chair. Council Members Zach Jacob and Kelvin Green
          expressed the desire to be considered for the position of Council Chair.

           Council Member Whitelock believed the Council Chair should be present as often as
           possible, send weekly or bi-weekly emails to the Council, be willing to commit to a yearly
           review of the Council Office Director, and get support from four or more Council Members
           prior to giving Council Office staff additional directions. She believed the position needed
           someone with time to dedicate, and the ability to help the Council run smoothly. Council
           Member Bedore echoed a lot of what Council Member Whitelock said, and expressed the
           opinion that the Council Chair position should not be one of “power”. He suggested a

           rotating Chair position that would change every six months. Council Member Whitelock
           asked for responses from both Council Members Jacob and Green.

           Council Member Jacob did not view the Chair as a position of power. He said he did serve
           on the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District Board and attending that meeting had
           occasionally resulted in his being late for Council meeting but would leave the JVWCD
           meetings earlier as needed if he were Council Chair. Council Member Jacob believed he
           had time to fill the position, and indicated he would be willing to commit to an annual
           review of the Council Office Director.

           Council Member Green said he had no conflicts that would cause him to be late for or miss
           Council meetings. He would work closely with the Council Office to get agendas prepared
           early, and make sure communication was across the board. Council Member Green said
           he would commit to an annual review of the Council Office Director.

           The Council voted electronically, with Zach Jacob receiving 57% of the votes, and Kelvin
           Green receiving 43%.

           Council Members Pamela Bloom, Chad Lamb, and Kelvin Green indicated desire to be
           considered for the Vice Chair position. The Council voted electronically, with Chad Lamb
           receiving 57% of the votes, Kelvin Green receiving 29%, and Pamela Bloom receiving
           14%.

           MOTION: Council Member Green moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 24-001

 6. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Ordinance No. 24-04 amending West Jordan City Code for Title 1 Administration,
          Chapters 4, 6, 8, 9 and 15
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson said staff were seeking Council direction on drafted
          amendments to Title 1 regarding Administration. Council Member Green asked that a
          document of questions and issues he sent via email to the Council be included in the
          record. Council Member Green believed the Title could be simplified and cleaned up and
          suggested referring the drafted amendments to an ad hoc committee of the Council.

           Council Member Lamb said between the proposed amendments and the suggestions
           drafted by Council Member Green, he believed the Council could direct staff to make
           changes and bring the ordinance back for approval. He expressed the opinion that a study
           session was not necessary.


City Council Meeting Minutes              Wednesday, January 10, 2024                          Page 4
           Council Member Whitelock disagreed. She felt the Council’s main job was to look at the
           Code. She did not support the way the Code was written, felt it needed to be discussed,
           and said she had three pages of notes for further discussion.

           Chair Jacob said he felt the discussion should take place in open meeting, and felt an email
           involving substantial comments on an agenda item sent to the Council prior to a meeting
           treaded on open meeting issues. Council Member Green believed he had heard multiple
           times that staff wanted to see information ahead of a meeting rather than being
           blindsided in a meeting. Council Member Whitelock said she wanted the discussion to go
           to a work session and did not want to waste time with an ad hoc committee.

           with the exception of the Budget and Audit Subcommittee, the subcommittees had been
           a waste of time and were not needed. Mr. Anderson said from his perspective the difficulty

       c. Ordinance No. 24-02 setting the 2024 Annual Meeting Schedule for City Council, and
          Agency Boards
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson presented a proposed Annual Meeting Schedule for
          2024. The Council discussed timing for work sessions and Council meetings. Council
          Member Whitelock believed separate work sessions were necessary. Council Member
          Green commented that one-hour work session discussions scheduled prior to Council
          meetings were often cut short to move to the next meeting. Council Member Green would
          be in favor of scheduling two meeting days per month, inclusive of two work sessions per
          month if meetings began at 2:00 or 3:00 in the afternoon.

           Vice Chair Lamb expressed concern about turn-around, and suggested a hybrid of the
           meeting schedule to improve agenda item turn-around. Council Member Shelton was in
           favor of keeping the calendar as proposed. Council Member Bedore suggested more than

City Council Meeting Minutes               Wednesday, January 10, 2024                      Page 6
           one work session per month would be needed, and suggested scheduling work sessions
           after Council meetings when possible.

           Mayor Burton mentioned that staff would support whatever decision the Council made.
           Mr. Anderson mentioned timing involved with public noticing requirements. Council
           Member Green suggested fewer staff members could attend Council meetings to lessen
           the impact on staff. The Council discussed including a Committee of the Whole on each
           agenda following regular Council meetings.

           Chair Jacob mentioned external board meetings that occasionally conflicted with West
           Jordan Council meetings, and suggested moving the first Council meeting of each month
           to the first Wednesday of each month instead of the second Wednesday. Council Member
           Green suggested moving Council meetings to Tuesdays instead of Wednesdays. Council
           Member Whitelock asked that whatever meeting schedule was adopted be consistent for
           the sake of residents. Mr. Anderson commented that the Planning Commission met on
           Tuesdays, and had already adopted their calendar for 2024.

           Council Member Shelton suggested it would be a disservice to an applicant to schedule a
           work session discussion for after a Council meeting and end up telling the applicant that
           the discussion would not happen because a Council meeting went long. Council Member
           Green suggested scheduling work session items that did not have external applicants for
           after Council meetings. Mr. Anderson confirmed the schedule could be changed by
           ordinance at any time.

           MOTION: Council Member Green moved to APPROVE Ordinance No. 24-02 as
                   proposed, setting the 2024 Annual Meeting Schedule for City Council and
                   Agency Boards.
                   Council Member Whitelock seconded the motion.
           The vote was recorded as follows:
           YES:       Bob Bedore, Pamela Bloom, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, Chad Lamb, Kent
                      Shelton, Kayleen Whitelock
           NO:
           ABSENT:
           The motion passed 7-0.
