                         MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

      This Memorandum of Understanding is made and entered into this ___ day of
______________, 2023, by and between the Jordan School District and Riverton City.

        WHEREAS, Utah state law requires preferred walking routes to and from schools
for students that live within 1.5 miles of an elementary school and 2.0 miles of a secondary
school and that municipalities provide crossing guards or shuttle service where necessary; and

        WHEREAS, students residing in the Western Springs neighborhood of Riverton City
live within 1.5 miles of Jordan School District’s elementary school and 2.0 miles of the
secondary school; and

       WHEREAS, because the infrastructure for the preferred walking route was incomplete at
the beginning of the 2019-2020 school year for the students living in the Western Springs
neighborhood, Jordan School District agreed to provide temporary bus routes for the Western
Springs neighborhood while the necessary infrastructure was being completed; and

       WHEREAS, the Western Springs bus routes were intended to be provided on a
temporary basis, because unlike most other bus routes, Jordan School District is not reimbursed
when busing students who live within 1.5 miles of an elementary school and 2.0 miles of a
secondary school; and

        WHEREAS, Jordan School District has provided four years of unfunded bus routes for
the Western Springs neighborhood pending completion of infrastructure improvements for the
preferred walking route; and

       WHEREAS, the implementation of the bus routes has saved Riverton City from
otherwise incurring costs to provide shuttle service across highways or to provide, train, and
supervise school crossing guards for the Western Springs walking routes; and

      WHEREAS, it is anticipated that infrastructure for the preferred safe walking route will
be completed within the next year; and

       WHEREAS, Jordan School District and Riverton City desire to provide bus routes for
the 2023-2024 school year so that the preferred walking route infrastructure can be completed.

        NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual agreements and covenants
herein contained, the parties agree with one another as follows:

      1.      Jordan School District will provide one more year of bus routes for the students in
the Western Springs neighborhood, consistent with prior years, for the 2023-2024 school year.

        2.      Riverton City will reimburse Jordan School District 50% of the costs of these bus
routes, but in no case will Riverton owe Jordan School District more than $40,000. Jordan
School District will invoice Riverton City for its share in November 2023.
