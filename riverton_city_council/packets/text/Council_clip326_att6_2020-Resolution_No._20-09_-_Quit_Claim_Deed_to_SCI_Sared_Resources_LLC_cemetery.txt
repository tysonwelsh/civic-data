                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO 20-09

    A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE TRANSFER OF 1.67 ACRES OF REAL
    PROPERTY LOCATED IN THE VICINITY OF 13001 SOUTH 3600 WEST TO
                 WASATCH LAND AND IMPROVEMENT

       WHEREAS, Wasatch Land and Improvement owns and operates the Riverton Cemetery
located at 13001 South 3600 West; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is the title owner of property identified as follows: Parcel
No. 27-32-400-053, is approximately .97 acres in size; and Parcel. No. 27-32-400-058 is
approximately .70 acres in size (the Subject Properties); and

       WHEREAS, the Subject Properties are located within the aggregated boundaries of
property owned by Wasatch Land and Improvement, which it uses for cemetery purposes; and

      WHEREAS, the Subject Properties were originally intended for roadway purposes
which will no longer be needed due to the use of surrounding properties for cemetery purposes;
and

       WHEREAS, Wasatch Land and Improvement desires to purchase the Subject Properties
from Riverton City for appraised value in order to incorporate the same into the Cemetery’s
operations; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that conveyance of said
Subject Properties to Wasatch Land and Improvement will not interfere with the future
transportation needs of Riverton City and will enable Wasatch Land and Improvement to operate
its cemetery without concern of trespass on City land.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council authorizes and directs the Mayor to transfer title to the
          Subject Propertyies as described in the Quit Claim Deed instruments attached to this
          Resolution, to Wasatch Land and Improvement.
       2. The transfer of title to the Subject Properties shall be administered and approved as to
          legal form by the Riverton City Attorney.
       3. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 21st day of
January 2020 by the following vote:
