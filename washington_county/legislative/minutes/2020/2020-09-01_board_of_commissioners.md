PRESENTATION: _2020_WASHINGTON COUNTY FAIR REPORT: SUSI
LAFAELE

Washington County Fair Director Susi LaFaele presented on the success of the 2020
County Fair. Although attendance was slightly down due to concerns from the COVID-19
pandemic, the Fair continued to be a great event that many families were able to enjoy.

Chairman Iverson mentioned the success of the Fair Parade in the host city of Washington.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2641, A RESOLUTION
APPOINTING A MEMBER TO THE HABITAT CONSERVATION ADVISORY

COMMITTEE

Commissioner Cox mentioned that Washington County manages the Red Cliffs Desert
Reserve and oversees the Habitat Conservation Advisory Committee (HCAC). The
Committee is composed of individuals who have the knowledge and the authority to make
recommendations to the County Commission in regards to the Reserve. Yvette Converse
has been appointed as the Field Manager of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service and is
recommended to replace acting Field Manager Laura Romin on the HCAC.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2641, A
Resolution Appointing Yvette Converse to the Habitat Conservation
Advisory Committee. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist,
and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2645, A RESOLUTION
SUPPORTING THE PREFERRED ALTERNATIVE REGARDING _THE
RENEWAL OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY DESERT __TORTOISE
INCIDENTAL TAKE PERMIT AND THE ISSUANCE OF A RIGHT OF WAY
FOR THE NORTHERN CORRIDOR

Commissioner Cox explained that a draft Environmental Assessment (EA) has been
released from the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) and is open for public comment
until September 10, 2020. In order to follow the National Environmental Protection
Agency’s (NEPA) statute, several alternatives have been explored for the best option in
regards to the Northern Corridor.

The preferred route identified by the Utah Office of Transportation (UDOT) and the
County starts at Red Hills Parkway near the southern border of the Red Cliffs Reserve and
connects to the Washington Parkway. The route is identified as Alternative B. The BLM
has selected Alternative B as its preferred route in the EA released for comment.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2645, A
Resolution Supporting the Preferred Alternative Regarding the
Renewal of the Washington County Desert Tortoise Incidental Take
Permit and the Issuance of a Right of Way for the Northern Corridor.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2647,_ A RESOLUTION
SUPPORTING THE LAKE POWELL PIPELINE AND APPROVING THE
WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMENT ON THE DRAFT ENVIRONMENTAL
IMPACT STATEMENT

Chairman Iverson stated that for over ten (10) years, Washington County, along with the
Washington County Water Conservancy District (WCWCD), have been studying the
feasibility of using a pipeline to transport water from the Colorado River to Washington
County. The proposed Lake Powell pipeline is expected to help the community thrive over
the coming years as additional water sources will be necessary to meet the area’s needs.
Washington County is in favor of the Pipeline and will submit a comment on the draft EA

before the deadline.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2647, A
Resolution Supporting the Lake Powell Pipeline and Approving the
Washington County Comment on the Draft Environmental Impact
Statement. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.
