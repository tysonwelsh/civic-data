                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-66

    A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL APPROVING AN
 EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT WITH DENNIS CRAIG GILES TO SERVE AS THE
               CITY MANAGER FOR RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has a need for a City Manager to assume and perform the
administrative responsibilities of the City; and

       WHEREAS, the City desires to enter into an Employment Agreement with Dennis Craig
Giles whereby he will serve Riverton City as the City Manager; and

        WHEREAS, the City’s governing body, including the Mayor, desires to establish by
execution of this Employment Agreement the City Manager’s employment salary, benefits, and
establish certain terms and conditions of employment in a manner consistent with Riverton City
Code and the associated employment description for the City Manager; and

        WHEREAS, the City’s Governing Body desires to (1) secure and retain the services of
the City Manager and to provide inducement for him to remain in such employment; (2) to make
possible full work productivity by assuring the City Manager’s morale and peace of mind with
respect to future security; and (3) to provide a just means for terminating the City Manager’s
services at such time as he may be unable to fully discharge his duties or when the City may
otherwise desire to terminate his employment; and

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1.     The Employment Agreement by and between Riverton City and Dennis Craig
              Giles, attached hereto is hereby approved. The Mayor is hereby authorized to
              sign, on behalf of the City, the Employment Agreement with Mr. Giles, upon
              verification that the Agreement has been approved as to legal form by the City
              Attorney.

       2.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

   PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 15th day of August
2023 by the following vote:
