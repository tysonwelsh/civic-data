                                 TOWN OF ALTA

                           RESOLUTION No. 2022-R-20

        A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE 2022-2023 FISCAL BUDGETS
                      FOR THE TOWN OF ALTA

       WHEREAS, the Town Council finds it necessary to amend certain departmental
budgets in the Town of Alta General Fund for the fiscal year 2022-2023,

       WHEREAS, the Town Council finds that there are unencumbered or unexpended
revenues sufficient to meet all departmental expenditures, and

        WHEREAS, the Town Council desires to appropriate such revenues for those
uses.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that in conformity with chapter 5 Title 10 of
Utah Code Annotated, the General Fund budget for the Town of Alta for fiscal year 2023
is amended as follows: Exhibit “A” – Amended Budget for the Town of Alta: 2022-
2023.


ADOPTED THIS 14th day of December, 2022.
