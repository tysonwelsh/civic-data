Study Session

1.0   Discussion: 1300 East and Pioneer Road Roundabout

1.1   City Engineer/Public Works Director Scott Cooley updated the Council on a
      current 1300 East project and spoke of plans for the roundabout at 1300 East
      and Pioneer Road.

1.2   Deputy Public Works Director/Deputy Engineer Robert Markle said the City
      had received many complaints about the existing roundabout at 1300 East and
      Pioneer Road and advised that improvements were needed in 2023. He
      showed plans for widening the roundabout and described the concrete that
      would be used. Mr. Markle said the project could be completed in two weeks
      if the roundabout were completely closed with a proposed detour, or
      completed in about one month with night closures and partial closures.

1.3   Mr. Cooley advised that a full closure would be safer. The Council and staff
      discussed the options available. Staff said a Request For Proposal (RFP) for
      prequalified contractors would need to go out soon. The Council asked for
      time to look at the information before making a decision.

5.0   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1576, an Ordinance amending the Official
      Zoning Map of Draper City for approximately 0.92 acres of property from
      RA1 (Residential Agricultural 40,000 ft² minimum lot size) to RA2
      (Residential Agricultural 20,000 ft² minimum lot size), located at
      approximately 275 East 13275 South, otherwise known as the Lyle
      McAllister Zoning Map Amendment

5.1   Planner Todd Draper explained the proposed rezone would enable a property
      line adjustment between neighbors. The proposed rezone would not allow a
      second residential lot to be created. Mr. Draper said the Planning Commission
      reviewed the request and forwarded a positive recommendation.

5.2   Lyle McAllister, applicant, said he planned to reduce the size of his lot (and
      increase the size of the neighboring lot) by .123 acres.

5.3   Mayor Pro Tem Roberts opened a public hearing and closed the public
      hearing seeing that no one wished to comment.

5.4   Councilmember F. Lowry moved to approve                  Ordinance     #1576.
      Councilmember T. Lowery seconded the motion.
