   Robertson               & Burningham,                        Inc.

   Inc.,    the City's            financial             consultants,             on how            to finance          a city       hall     without             a tax increase             to
   Millcreek            residents.              Jason       Burningham               said the City                issued       a sales tax revenue                       bond        back        in
   2019.      The security                 and bonding                  capacity          that has been               evaluated            is in the context                  of sales tax

5. Street       Re-Naming            Policy      Discussion;         Rita     Lund,       Communications                Director
   Mayor        Silvestrini      said the City had received                   multiple      requests         to rename       streets.     He said doing
   that costs money             and takes a considerable                   amount      of staff time to process.               The decision             to
   allow       it has resulted         in other consequences,                so staff would           like to set up a procedure                 and
   charge       a fee for it in the future.            He said it was not fair to impose                       the cost on the taxpayers
   when it only benefits                one street. John Miller               said the City's          ordinance        allows     for the renaming
   of street names.            He gave an example               that the recently           renamed          Gunn Avenue              to Villa        Vista
   created       a situation      where       there was an emergency                   service       call and the constituent              said they
   lived     on Gunn Avenue,               though      the road had been renamed,                     so the response            time was reduced.
   Rita Lund         said there is a $250 administrative                       fee from      the Salt Lake County                Addressing
   Office,      about a $200 cost for the street signs,                        and staff time to consider.                John Brems             said he
   draffed      an ordinance           that would        require       100%        of impacted        residents'     signatures          on the
   petition.      The current          ordinance       only required           70%.      There       would     also be a statement               on the
   petition      that the name change                could be a pain for residents                    to update     their     addresses,          a
   disclosure.          Streets should        be renamed           between         intersections       so there was not a portion                      of a
   street with       another      name. He also brought                    up the option         of having       real names or fake names
   for streets. The Council                 agreed with        100%         of neighbor       approval         and the petition           disclaimer.
   Council       Member         Uipi      brought     up fundraising           ideas for street renaming.                   She supported              John
   Brem's        ordinance       draft.     Council        Member          Jackson     said the McBride            petition       (in the regular
   meeting)        would       be subject        to the old ordinance.              Lund    pointed       out that there would                be a
   separate process            for the City         changing        street names.          One process          would       be ceremonial              and
   approved        by the City          Council.      The other would               be a city-initiated          change      that the City
   Engineer        would       approve.       The Council           gave staff direction             to present     them       with      a draft
   ordinance.
