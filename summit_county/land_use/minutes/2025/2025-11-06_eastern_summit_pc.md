Planner Ray Milliner presented a discussion/training- Review of Planning Commission Powers, Duties, and Bylaws. (6:15 PM)
The commissioners shared their comments and raised questions about the Powers, Duties, and Bylaws. There was an extensive discussion regarding the disclosure requirements for conversations between members of the public and commissioners, as well as discussions among commissioners themselves. It was emphasized that any biases must be disclosed. Additionally, commissioners sought clarification on the requirements for the continuation of a public meeting. Can the Commissioners change procedures? Asked for clarification on the membership of the Commission. Planner Milliner, Civil Attorney Helen Strachan and Community Development Director, Peter Barnes responded. (6:24 PM)
