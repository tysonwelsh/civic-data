26   2. Discussion and Presentation Items
27      2.a) HIVEs/Just for Kids appreciation presentation
28      Michelle Holbrook expressed her gratitude to Lehi City and presented a short PowerPoint
29      presentation to showcase the positive impact of their collaboration. They have been involved in
30      various sports and activities for individuals with disabilities, starting with adaptive swimming
31      and expanding into basketball, bowling, and volleyball programs and tournaments. The city has
32      supported them by providing spaces for their events and tournaments. She emphasized her
33      appreciation for Lehi City's support and mentioned various collaborative initiatives, including
34      educational activities for adults and community service projects. She also shared the group’s
35      excitement about a new Curtis Center that is nearing completion. The group expressed
36      overwhelming gratitude for the financial support received and concluded by sharing songs.
37
38      2.b.) CDBG application and review process discussion.
39      Jessica DeLora and Debra Lauret with Mountainland Association of Governments (MAG) led
40      a discussion on the Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) application process. The
41      proposal included setting priorities for public services, defining eligible criteria, and
42      recommending a vetting process for grant applications. The goal was to streamline the
43      application process and ensure that the grants align with the community's needs and priorities.
44
45      The conversation also touched on the challenges and benefits of funding various projects,
46      especially in balancing infrastructure development with public service projects. They
47      emphasized the importance of inclusivity, specifically in supporting those with special needs.
