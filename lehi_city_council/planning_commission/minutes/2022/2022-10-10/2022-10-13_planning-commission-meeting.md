3. Regular Agenda

3.1) Public hearing and consideration of Gatlin McBride’s request for approval of a
conditional use permit for an 8-foot fence located at 370 East 500 North.
Katie Bussell presented the item.

Chair Nielsen opened the public hearing

Brian Allred- they are okay with the fence. She wants the fence to extend all the way around the
property and does not want an 8 foot fence to only cover the south side and 20 feet up one side.

Jenny Allred- they have already had issues with the homeowner’s business.

Chair Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Chair Nielsen asked about the home occupation business. Kim Struthers responded that home
occupations can have vehicles, but they have to be parked off-street and that home occupations
can have off-site employees, they just can't frequent the site. Commissioner Jackson asked if the
conditional use was approved for the fence if it would impact the home occupation at all. Kim
said approving the fence would not indicate approval of the home occupation use. Commissioner
Eyre asked about the location of the fence.

Commissioner Jackson invited the Allred’s back up. He asked them about the history of this
property. The Allred’s stated there have been a lot of trucks in the back yard. They had a
conversation with the applicant that led to this application. Less cars have been parked in the
backyard now. More cars on the street recently.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved approve Gatlin McBride’s request for
        approval of a conditional use permit for an 8-foot fence located at 370 East 500
        North; with the conditions the fence, be of view of view obstructing construction.
        Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Carlson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes;
        Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson. The vote passed unanimously.

3.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Daniel Phillips' request for review of the Good
and the Beautiful general plan amendment on 9-acres of property located at 700 South Mill
Pond Road changing the land use designation from Mixed Use to Heavy Commercial.

3.5) Public hearing and recommendation of Daniel Phillips’ request for review of the Good
and the Beautiful concept, a commercial building located at approximately 700 South Mill
Pond Road.
Katie Bussell presented item 3.4

Commissioner Carlson asked about the mixed-use elements of the proposed use. Kim Struthers
provided a clarification on the mixture of uses.

Commissioner Eyre motioned to consider item 3.4 and 3.5 concurrently.

Katie Bussell presented information on Item 3.5.


Tony Trane represented the applicant. He clarified the extent of the General Plan Amendment.
The applicant incorporated features into their plan to work with the surrounding mixed-use. They
were originally going to try to get exceptions to mixed-use. Staff recommended they go to Heavy
Commercial. The use will not have many trucks. It will not be a typical office-warehouse
use. This proposal fits with the area.

Commission Chair Nielsen asked about the conflicting layouts. Tony responded that staff
directed they leave it how it is now, but they will clarify in the future. Right now, they are
overparked, but if they also have the auditorium full, they might need all of the parking. The
auditorium will have approximately 200 seats. There are no environmentally sensitive areas in
this land, but there are some closer to the pond. There are no environmental considerations in the
nine acres being considered. Commission Chair Nielsen asked about the parking. Tony
responded that they are overparked. Commissioner Eyre asked about the zoning map.

Commission Chair Nielsen opened public hearing on 3.4 and closed public hearing.

Commission Chair Nielsen opened public hearing on 3.5 and closed public hearing.

Commission Chair Nielsen asked about warehousing availability in commercial instead of heavy
commercial. Kim Struthers answered it allows small warehouse, but this proposed warehouse
exceeds the allowed size. And all the proposed uses will be allowed in the Heavy Commercial
zone.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to recommend approval of Daniel Phillips’
        request for review of the Good and the Beautiful general plan amendment; including
        all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes;
        Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson. The vote passed unanimously.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to approve Daniel Phillips’ request for review
        of the Good and the Beautiful concept, a commercial building located at
        approximately 700 South Mill Pond Road; including DRC Comments and that
        consideration be given to the design to preserve the environmentally sensitive areas of the
        site and look at reducing or moving the parking towards the road away from the pond,
        with findings in the report contingent on approval of the General Plan Amendment.
        Commission Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes;
        Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson. The vote passed unanimously.

3.6) Public hearing and consideration of Bastian Homes’ request for approval of a
conditional use permit for the Owen duplex located at 984 North 500 West in the R-2
(medium density residential) zone.
Katie Bussell presented the item.

Commission Chair Nielsen asked about the possibility of doing a flag lot. Staff responded that it
does not have the frontage required.

Devin Miller was present representing the applicant. The existing lot is currently just being used
for storage. The applicant wants to build something useful on the lot.

Commission Chair Nielsen opened the public hearing.


Peggy Briskle- lives next to the property. The applicant previously wanted to build a duplex but
could not because a duplex was being built across the street. She has concerns about how it will
fit in the back of the property. There is a lot of traffic on 500 West.

Raylynn Barnes- she wants to understand how the property will work and how far back it will go.
Also want to understand if it will just be one duplex or if another one could be built in the future.

Gaylynn Barnes- wants to understand what is happening and how the property can be more
presentable.

Devin Miller answered questions from the neighbors. They want the duplex to be close to the
street. Having a larger backyard is more desirable. Intend to have garages to park in. Intend to
meet all the setbacks required by Code.

Commission Chair Nielsen further clarified the location of the duplex on the lot.

Commission Eyre asked about the comment that they could not build another duplex because of a
lot across the street. Staff responded about the Downtown Spacing Overlay that does not allow a
mutli-family project within 400 feet of another. Commissioner Ellis asked about garages sticking
out. Kim Struthers responded that there are no requirements for standard zoning about the garage.
And spoke to the revised elevations, that it no longer looks like a direct mirror image.

Commission Chair Nielsen reopened public hearing. Peggy Briskle asked about the orientation of
the house. Chair Nielsen responded that that house would face towards 500 West. Commission
Chair Nielsen closed public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Bastian Homes’ request for approval
        of a conditional use permit for the Owen duplex located at 984 North 500 West in the
        R-2 (medium density residential) zone; including all DRC comments and findings stated
        in the report. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes;
        Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson. The vote passed unanimously.

3.7) Recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of Development Code amendments
pertaining to the Mixed-Use zone: Chapter 5, Chapter 15, Table of Uses, and Table of Bulk
and Intensity. (Tabled from the July 14, 2022 meeting)
Katie Bussell presented the item.

Commissioner Eyre asked about horizontal mixed-use and discouraging it. Kim Struthers

better to have it horizontal. Commissioner Eyre does not want to see separated uses.

Discussion between the Planning Commission and Staff.

Commissioner Ellis moved to recommend approval to City Council for Lehi City’s request for
review of Development Code amendments pertaining to the Mixed-Use zone: Chapter 5, Chapter
15, Table of Uses, and Table of Bulk and Intensity with the DRC comments, and the findings

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request for

        Chapter 5, Chapter 15, Table of Uses, and Table of Bulk and Intensity; including all
