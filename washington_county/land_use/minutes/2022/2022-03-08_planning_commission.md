Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
March 8, 2022

The Public Meeting of the Planning Commission of Washington County was called to order by Chairman
Frank Leuck at 1:30 p.m. on March 08, 2022, in the Washington County Commission Chambers and via
Zoom, at 197 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah. Commissioners in attendance were Brandon
Anderson, Todd Jones, Slade Hughes, Frank Leuck, Brad Gaston and Mark Owens. County Commissioners
in attendance were Victor Iverson, Chair; Gil Almquist, and Adam Snow. County Staff in attendance were
Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Victoria Hales, Deputy Attorney; Todd Edwards, County
Engineer; Laura Thurber, Planning and Permits Specialist; Kurt Gardner, Building Official; Kyle Layton,
County Building Inspector; Brent Cardall, Zoning and Code Compliance Specialist.

Chairman Leuck welcomed everyone to the meeting, led the Pledge of Allegiance and then explained
meeting protocol.

1.

DISCUSSION WITH ZACH RENSTROM, THE GENERAL MANAGER OF THE WASHINGTON

COUNTY WATER CONSERVANCY DISTRICT, REGARDING A PROPOSED ORDINANCE
FOR LANDSCAPING AND WATER CONSERVATION ON DEVELOPING LAND.

Mr. Messel introduced Mr. Renstrom and turned the floor over to him for a presentation
concerning landscaping and water conservation. Mr. Renstrom’s presentation included
information about water use in the county as it relates to zoning and residential development. He
discussed draft ordinance language regarding landscaping and water conservation for future
development, with examples of language adopted by many municipalities in the county. He
encouraged the county to pass similar legislation.

conditions:

Commission meeting.

Commissioner Todd Jones asked what had changed on this project plan since the last time this
was presented as a zone change. Mr. Watson, Principal Engineer of Watson Engineering, stated
that the size of the lots is the main difference. The lot sizes were changed to conform to the
approved 5 acre minimum for the RA-5 (Residential Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone.
The road layout was also adjusted to avoid double frontage lots. Notes on the final plat will also
state that no access to lots 1, 2, 3, and 4 will be from Highway 144.

MOTION: Commissioner Hughes made a motion to APPROVE the preliminary plat for
the Kolob Heights Subdivision with the findings and conditions presented by the county.
The motion was SECONDED by Commissioner Anderson. The motion PASSED
unanimously.

FINAL PLAT. Review a final plat request for the 13-lot Pinion Hills Phase 8 Subdivision in
Dammeron Valley on Parcel 7205-E-DVF. The parcels are currently zoned RE-40 (Residential
Estates 40,000 square foot minimum lot size) and located on the east side of the Pinion Hills
Development.

Mr. Messel went on to explain the Pinion Hills Phase 8 project plan and the adjustments that have
been made. It is proposed as a 13-lot subdivision with a cul-de-sac and two accesses from existing
roads. A common area is needed for storm water and has been called out on the preliminary plat
that will be owned and maintained by the HOA. The roads will be public roads and dedicated to
the county. He proceeded to read the finding and conditions from Staff and asked the Planning
Commissioners if they had any questions about the proposed plat.

Planning Commission Recommendation:
Staff has reviewed the proposed final plat and recommends that the Planning Commission approve
the final plat subject to the following findings and conditions:

1. That the address, including the house number and street name for each proposed parcel
shall be added to the final plat prior to recordation.

2. Thata current title report showing that the owner’s name on the plat matches the owner’s

name on the title report be submitted and approved before the final plat is recorded, with

notes and plat language to County’s satisfaction.

The final plat meets the applicable Washington County Codes and Standards.

The subdivision layout is consistent with the approved preliminary plat.

The proposed lots meet the minimum size requirement of the RE-40 (Residential Estates

40,000 square foot minimum lot size) zone.

PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING VARIOUS ADDITIONS AND REVISIONS TO TITLE 10,
ZONING REGULATIONS, INCLUDING ADDING PLANNED DEVELOPMENT - SHORT TERM
RENTAL PROVISIONS, ADDING WATER AND UTILITY REQUIREMENTS, REVISING
APPEAL _ PROVISIONS, _REVISING _ ENFORCEMENT __ PROVISIONS, _AND _ OTHER
MISCELLANEOUS CHANGES. Review, discuss and hold a public hearing regarding various
changes to Title 10, Zoning Regulations to plan for and allow planned developments with short
term rental uses and without an owner occupancy requirement, to plan for water and utility
infrastructure, to support zone changes and future development, to revise the appeal provisions
related to Title 10 appeals, to provide more clarity and certainty regarding the enforcement of
zoning regulations, and other miscellaneous changes.

Ms. Hales presented the changes to the previous draft that were suggested by Planning
Commission in the February meeting, and discussion was had about the various revisions. The
Planning Commission gave suggestions and added some clarification to the proposed ordinance.

Mr. Cardall also explained some of the code enforcement issues, his process, and how he works
with the landowners to resolve those issues. Discussion was had about the difference between
enforcement and working with landowners.

Ms. Hales finished her presentation and gave the floor back to Chairman Lueck.
MOTION: Chairman Leuck opened the Public Hearing at 4:06 p.m.

Mr. Jared Westhoff told the Planning Commission that he appreciates the efforts to create
this ordinance and as a developer, would be happy to work with the County on this. He
discussed some of the challenges the proposed ordinance would bring to developers and
gave suggestions for edits.

MOTION: Chairman Leuck closed the Public Hearing at 4:40 pm after all who wished to
speak had an opportunity.

Chairman Leuck asked how close the County is to having a final draft of this ordinance.
