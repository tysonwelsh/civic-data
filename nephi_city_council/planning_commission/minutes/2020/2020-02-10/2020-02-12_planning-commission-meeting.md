Nephi City Planning Commission Public Hearings
                          February 12, 2020

Chairman Greenhalgh welcomed those in attendance and opened the hearing at 7 p.m.
He explained the purpose of the hearing is to receive public comment on possible changes to the Home
Occupation ordinance. It is proposed Nephi Code 3-1-1B, along with 10-19-13B1, be changed to include
a more detailed description. Commission members reviewed possible changes. Chairman Greenhalgh
closed the hearing at 7:10 p.m.

Chairman Greenhalgh opened second public hearing at 7:10 pm. Purpose of this hearing is to receive
public comment on a request to remove hard surfacing requirements in RV parks. Kallie Bowers
addressed the Commission and options were explored.
Chairman Greenhalgh closed the hearing at 7:24 pm.
