2.
Introduction of the Director of Transportation Planning Administration. Carl Miller. (6:00 P.M.)
Carl Miller,
Transportation Planning Director,
introduced himself to the planning commission.
Commissioners welcomed Director Miller to the County.
3.
Public Hearing and Recommendation for a plat amendment to vacate a 60’ wide road between Lots 2 and 3, Brookside Estates Subdivision.Carol Wrenn, Applicant. Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner. (6:02 P.M.)
Jennifer Strader,
Senior Planner,
presented a plat amendment easement for public hearing and recommendation to the County Council.
Commission Chair Kucera opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:05 P.M.
No public input was offered.
Commission Chair Kucera closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:06 P.M.
Commissioners asked brief clarifying questions.
Thomas Cooke made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council for the plat amendment for the second amended Brookside Estates based on the findings of fact and conclusions of law. Bruce Carmichael seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
Attachment:
Staff Report - Brookside Estates Plat Amendment
4.
Public Hearing and Action for a conditional use permit for the Wasatch Crest Residential Treatment Facility at 241 N. Highland Drive. Jim Huffman, Applicant. Ray Milliner, Principal Planner. (6:08 P.M.)
Jim Huffman,
Applicant,
read a statement to commissioners, staff and members of the public.
Commission Chair Kucera opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:24 P.M.

Commission Chair Kucera closed the meeting for public hearing at 7:16 P.M.
The applicant, commissioners and staff responded to comments made during the public hearing followed by a lengthy discussion by the Commission.
Chris Conabee made a motion to approve the CUP per the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in both the staff report and the additional conditions and findings of fact and conclusions of law potentially listed during the meeting with the exclusion of the conditions of approval of number four (4) and number six (6) as found in the staff report. DJ Hubler seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
