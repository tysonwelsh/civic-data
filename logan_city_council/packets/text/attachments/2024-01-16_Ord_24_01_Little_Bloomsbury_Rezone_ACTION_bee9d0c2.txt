PC 23-074 Little Bloomsbury Rezone [Zone Change] Little Bloomsbury Inn & Gallery/Brenda C Sun Tr,
authorized agent/owner are requesting a zone change of the .13-acre parcel located at 181 North 200
East from Traditional Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) to Town Center Two (TC-2) zone; TIN 06-066-
0023 in the Adams Neighborhood.

STAFF: Russ Holley, Planner reviewed the request for a rezone of a .13-acre property located at 181
North 200 East from Traditional Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) to Town Center 2 (TC-2). The Future
Land Use Plan (FLUP) shows this entire block as Town Center (TC) zoning.  This request is in line with
the future plan and staff is recommending approval.

Commissioner Guth asked about the Historic District Overlay.  R. Holley said that this home is within the
Historic District and any proposed changes to the outside of the building would have to go through the
Historic Preservation Committee for review and approval.

PROPONENT: Brenda Sun gave thanks to the City staff and Planning Commission and spoke in support
of the future project.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Lucero said this makes sense to be transitional zoning and aligns with the
future plan.

Commissioner Doutre mentioned rezoning the remaining NR-6 properties on this street to Town Center.

MOTION: Commissioner Peterson made a motion to forward a recommendation of approval to the
Municipal Council on a zone change for PC 23-074 with the findings for approval as listed below.
Commissioner Guth seconded the motion.

RECOMMENDED FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1.  The surrounding zoning to the west is TC-2 and a rezone of this property will result in a contiguous
    zoning pattern on the block.
2.  The TC-2 zone is appropriate because it aligns with the adopted FLUP and fits into the existing land
    use patterns of the block.
3.  TC-2 zoning, and land uses are in close proximity to transportation choices, downtown commercial
    goods and services and employment centers.

---

4.  The majority of the surrounding existing land uses to the west are commercial in nature.
5.  The Conditional Use Permit process and LDC development standards will ensure a quality project that
    will be compatible with the surrounding neighborhood.

RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission forward a recommendation of
approval to the Municipal Council for a rezone of approximately 0.13 acres of property
located at 181 North 200 East (TIN# 06-066-0023) from Traditional Neighborhood
Residential (NR-6) to Town Center 2 (TC-2).

Land use adjoining the subject property
North:  NR-6: Residential Uses      East:  NR-6: Religious Uses
South:  NR-6: Residential Uses      West:  TC-2: Commercial Uses

PROJECT
The proponent is requesting to rezone the 0.13-acre property at 181 North 200 East from NR-6
to TC-2. The applicant is planning a future small boutique hotel and gallery for the property. If
the rezone is successful, a subsequent Conditional Use Permit will be requested to legally
establish those commercial uses. The property is currently developed with a single-family home,
built in 1909, and a detached garage in the rear yard. An open-ditch irrigation canal traverses
the property flowing northward and essentially bisects the parcel between the rear of the home
and the garage.

GENERAL PLAN
The Future Land Use Plan (FLUP), adopted in 2008, identifies this property as Town Center
(TC). The General Plan, a nonregulatory visioning plan, describes TC areas as being a hub of
the City and Valley and reflects a vibrant downtown, with a variety of commercial, mixed-use,
and institutionally uses and activities.  The TC designation covers land from 200 South to 500
North and staggers between 100 East and 200 East along the eastern side of Downtown Logan.
The TC designation staggers between 100 and 150 West along the west side of Downtown.

Figure 2 shows the current NR-6 zoning of the subject property 181 North 200 East.
[map/plat page 7 — aerial zoning map. Callout: "Project Location / ZONING = NR-6". Zone labels: TC-2, NR-6, NR-6, TC-2. Street labels: 200 N, 200 E]

Land Development Code (LDC) - Zoning
The TC-2, which encourages dense, walkable commercial and residential development in the
downtown area with unique and interesting building designs. This zone allows a density of up of
to 30 units per acre along with a mixture of commercial uses. The City went through a deliberate
and robust process in 2018 to redefine the Town Center zoning districts which culminated in the
adoption of Ordinance Number 18-07. This year-long zoning process established TC-2 in the
outer-ring areas of downtown as a way to buffer or transition the residential neighborhoods
further east and west of Downtown. The goal of the zoning strategy adopted in 2018 was to
further implement the visions expressed in the General Plan through a methodical transition of
uses and structure.

SUMMARY
Given the FLUP designation of TC and the current commercial land uses on the block, the
request to up-zone this parcel to TC-2 would be a predictable and expected zoning change.
With the LDS Temple located to the east of the block with extensive setbacks, TC-2
development and land uses on the subject property and the remaining 200 East block face
would be compatible and pose minimal impacts to the residential areas further east. Being a
core development proposal, infrastructure and utilities can be more efficiently utilized as
compared to outer fringe areas (sprawl) that excessively burden the transportation and
infrastructure networks with challenging logistics, higher vehicle miles driven, higher emissions,
and lengthier utility lines.

RECOMMENDED FINDINGS OF APPROVAL
The Planning Commission bases its decision on the following findings supported in the
administrative record for this project:
    1.  The surrounding zoning to the west is TC-2 and a rezone of this property will result in a
        contiguous zoning pattern on the block.
    2.  The TC-2 zone is appropriate because it aligns with the adopted FLUP and fits into the
        existing land use patterns of the block.
    3.  TC-2 zoning, and land uses are in close proximity to transportation choices, downtown
        commercial goods and services and employment centers.
    4.  The majority of the surrounding existing land uses to the west are commercial in nature.
    5.  The Conditional Use Permit process and LDC development standards will ensure a
        quality project that will be compatible with the surrounding neighborhood.
