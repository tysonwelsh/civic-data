Mountainland MPO Regional Planning Committee December 6, 2018 Page 3 Fourth Quarter Cent Sales Tax Presentation - Andrew Jackson A Public and Stakeholder Open House was held prior to the MPO Regional Planning Committee meeting to make sure there was an avenue for public comment as Utah County Commission postponed conversation on fourth quarter sales tax until December 11, 2018 so they could get through the budget. Andrew Jackson gave a brief overview of the Fourth quarter cent sales tax and discussed the projected revenue, when it can be expected and how it can be distributed once it is passed. If the County doesn’t adopt the fourth quarter sales tax by 2020 the cities and towns can adopt up to 2022. After that, there is no longer an avenue to adopt it. If it isn’t adopted as a whole, the Interlocal Agreement with UTA will not work. Mayor Brunst moved that the MPO Regional Planning Committee encourage and request the Utah County Commission enact the fourth quarter cent sales tax this year. Mayor Frost seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. UTA an UDOT recused themselves from the vote.
