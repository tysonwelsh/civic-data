                             KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP


RESOLUTION NO. 2022-09-01                                      DATE: September 12, 2022


        A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       HONORING JERRY WELLMAN FOR HIS SERVICE ON THE KEARNS
        METRO TOWNSHIP PLANNING COMMISSION AND APPOINTING
           GRAY THOMAS TO FULFILL THE REMAINDER OF JERRY
            WELLMAN’S TERM ON THE PLANNING COMMISSION

                                        RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Section 19.05.010 of the Kearns Metro Township Code requires the Kearns
Metro Township Council (the “Council”) to appoint members of the Kearns Metro Township
Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”); and

     WHEREAS, Commissioner Jerry Wellman resigned as a regular member of the Planning
Commission on June 27, 2022; and

       WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint Gray Thomas as a regular member of the
Planning Commission to fulfill the remainder of Commissioner Wellman’s term pursuant to
Section II.A.10 of the Planning Commission’s Rules of Order and Procedure.

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Kearns Metro Township Council
effective immediately:

       1.   The Council thanks Commissioner Jerry Wellman for his service to Kearns and the
Planning Commission; and

         2.     Gray Thomas is appointed as a regular member of the Planning Commission to
fulfill the remainder of Commissioner Wellman’s term, which will end on March 31, 2022; and

       3.     The Mayor and Kearns Metro Township staff are authorized to take any steps
needed to implement Gray Thomas’s appointment to the Planning Commission

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Kearns Metro Township Council, on this 12th day
of September, 2022.
