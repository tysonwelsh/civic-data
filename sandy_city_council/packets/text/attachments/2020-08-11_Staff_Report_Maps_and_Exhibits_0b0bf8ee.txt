    DESCRIPTION OF REQUESTS
    The applicant, Ryan Kump, Sandy City Engineer, is requesting a partial street vacation of
    9270 South. If vacated, a portion of 9270 South would then be transferred to the adjacent
    properties to the north and south. (See Exhibit #1 – 9270 South Vacation Exhibit).

    NOTICE
    Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to
    notify of the Planning Commission meeting. The property was also posted with a public
    notice sign on the property. No neighborhood meeting was required by the Community
    Development Director.

    ANALYSIS
    The reason for the partial street vacation is because this was presented as part of the
    original Stadium Block Master Plan and was viewed very favorably. This will be one of
    the first steps involved in the Master Plan development. (See Exhibit #2 – Vacation and
    Re-Routing Exhibit). The project will be done with an approximate 6.6 million dollar

   federal grant, and another matching 500,000 dollars by Sandy City. This realignment will
   serve two primary purposes. First, it will allow direct access for pedestrians from the light
   rail station to the stadium. Second, it will provide full signalized vehicular access through
   the intersection. Currently, 9270 South is not fully signalized. Also, 9270 South is less
   safe than the proposed realignment due to the vertical curve present that causes poor sight
   lines.

   The overall size of the parcel to be vacated is approximately 31,124 square feet. The
   vacated parcel will be transferred to the properties on the north and south. It is important
   to note that the official transfer of property will not be executed until traffic has been re-
   routed, so the City doesn’t find itself in an unfavorable position where we have vacated
   the property and then are unable for any reason to re-route traffic thereafter.


   REQUIRED DETERMINATIONS AND FINDINGS
   There are three determinations that need to be made by the Planning Commission and the
   City Council in considering a request for street vacation.

      1. Consent of the abutters. Sandy City staff have agreed to this vacation. The City
         has received no objection from any property owner that abuts the existing road
         segment.
      2. If good cause exists for the street vacation. The requested action will allow for
         future development for the Stadium Block Master Plan realignment. The Public
         Works Department has determined that this particular portion of property will no
         longer be needed for right-of-way or public use.
      3. That neither the public interest nor any person will be materially injured by the
         vacation. No injury to the public will occur due to this partial street vacation.

   STAFF RECOMMENDATION
   Staff recommends that the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to
   the City Council for the partial street right-of-way vacation of a portion of 9270 South
   Street as shown in the attached exhibit, based on the following findings and subject to the
   following conditions of approval:

   FINDINGS
   1. The partial street vacation action by the City will transfer the ownership of the subject
      property to the abutting property owners, who will use the area in future development
      of the property, or agree to own and maintain the property. Therefore, good cause for
      the action exists.
   2. That there is neither material injury to either the public interest nor any person by the
      requested vacation.
   3. Staff has not received any objection from the abutting property owners.

   CONDITIONS
   1. That the approval is conditioned upon the City proceeding through the vacation
      process through action by the City Council.
   2. That the recording of the street vacation ordinance be done with the Salt Lake County
      Recorder’s Office.
   3. That the official property transfer is not executed until traffic is re-routed using the
      new right-of-way.
