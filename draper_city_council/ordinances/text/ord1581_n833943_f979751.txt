City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On May 16, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1581, amending the text
of the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code relating to
IKEA parking and signage standards and concept plan. The complete ordinance is on
file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published
this 18th day of May 2023. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
