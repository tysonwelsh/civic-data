7:00 p.m. Councilor Hancock gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Report
2.1 Growth Summit Presentation- Curtis Blair
Mr. Blair invited the Mayor and Council to participate in the growth and prosperity summit in
October 2022, which is designed to bring the community together with different perspectives to
address how we manage the challenges and opportunities that come with growth. They formed a
compact which identified principles of prosperity. He asked that the Council review the compact
and sign it as a Council.

Ryan Beck, with Envision Utah, stated that they have been working on this visioning project to
determine what people want to see the valley look like in 2050. He reported that in a recent survey
they found that two in five believe that future growth will make Utah worse, which is up
significantly from 2014. He said we can’t stop the growth, but we need to make the best of it.

David Rowley stated that the report can be found at valleyvisioning.org and encouraged the
Council to sign the compact. He also asked the Mayor and Council attend the Growth and
Prosperity Summit.

2.2 Fireworks Discussion

3. Agenda Questions
None.

4. Administrative Report
Brad Kenison, Assistant City Engineer, informed the Council that they are utilizing MAG
(Mountainland Association of Governments) funds for many projects. The 2300 West road
expansion is on track to being this year. The extension of 1900 South also has support for
funding. They also have more property owners willing to sale for the road dedication.
