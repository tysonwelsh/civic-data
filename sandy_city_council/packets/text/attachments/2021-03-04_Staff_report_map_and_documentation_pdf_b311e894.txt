REQUEST
Comcast Corporation is requesting that the Planning Commission determine that a portion of the
existing Comcast headquarters building be allowed to be converted into a warehouse use, as
an ancillary and subordinate use to the primary use of the building as the regional
headquarters for Comcast Corporation.

BACKGROUND
American Telephone and Telegraph Company built the existing 129,556 square foot, four story
building at this location in 1999-2000. The Zoning at that time was Industrial (ID). The property
was later rezoned to the Research and Development (RD) zoning district, by City initiation over
desires to upgrade the appearance of the freeway fronting property along the west side of I-15.
Comcast Corporation is the successor to AT&T. Comcast has made a business decision to
centralize its activities in the Utah region to the Sandy headquarters location, including bringing
its maintenance and installation technicians, and all the support vehicles to this site. The
conversion of previously used office space to warehouse space is necessary to support the
technician activities at this site.

ANALYSIS
This determination is being considered under the Sandy City Development Code, section 21-23-
21-(O) (2) (d), Specific Ancillary Uses in the RD zone. The Planning Commission has the
authority under this section of the Sandy City Development Code to allow a warehouse use in
this zoning district upon review and a determination that the warehouse use is ancillary
and subordinate to the primary use of the building (Regional Headquarters and Offices).

The existing building contains 129,556 square feet on on four floors. The portion of the building
that is being converted to the warehouse use is 6,295 square feet, which represents 4.8 percent
of the total building floor area. The remodeling is internal to the building, except for two new
roll-up garage doors being cut into the building on the west side of the north wing. These garage
type doors will not be visible from the freeway, nor from the 300 West frontage street. (Please
see the attached letter from the applicant, which explains the uses and square footages of the
rest of the headquarters building.)

Staff has been working with Comcast representatives to address the site changes that are
necessary for the proposed changes to the outside site area to accomplish the intensification of
the regional headquarters needed by Comcast. In order to help Comcast meet its building and
operations constraints, three other Planning Commission actions are anticipated to be necessary
in the near future. These involve amendments to the development code, Site Plan Review and
subdivision amendment actions.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that the proposed introduction of a
warehouse use into the existing building is ancillary and subordinate to the primary use of the
Comcast regional headquarters building, based upon the following three findings and the
following three conditions:

FINDINGS.

  • That the warehouse use portion of the building represents only 4.8 percent of the total
    building square footage.
  • That the warehouse use will be internal to the existing building and will not be visible
    from the freeway or the 300 W. Street frontage.
  • That Comcast is a longstanding member of the Sandy City business community and the
    intensification of this building and site as a regional headquarters is valued by Sandy
    City and its residents.

CONDITIONS.

  1. That the remodeling complies with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety Codes
     applicable to this type of use.

As part of Comcast's request for a building permit to renovate the 1st floor of our Sandy building located at
9602 South 300 West Sandy Utah, we are asking for the inclusion of ancillary warehouse storage to our
permitted use of the building which is located in an R&D zone under ordinance 21-8-2 . Comcast is
providing the following additional information for general information to this request.
  1) The building encompasses a total of 129,556 Sq Ft of space over four floors
  2) The building is currently used by multiple departments and functions as follows:
       a. Comcast operates a 400-employee call center on a portion of the 2nd floor and all of the 3rd
          and 4th floors. This call center services Comcast customers across the western US from
          Minnesota to California. This Call Center equates to 82,230 Sq Ft or 63% of the building's
          footprint. This area's uses includes agent workstations, private offices, training rooms,
          meeting rooms, break rooms and restrooms.
       b. Comcast's Utah Area Operations is headquartered at this location as well. This accounts for
          an additional 142 employees, working in areas such as Technical Operations, Finance,
          Marketing, Residential Sales, Comcast Business Sales, External Affairs, HR, Security, and IT.
          These function teams occupy 35,033 Sq Ft or 27.6% of the buildings footprint These teams
          are located on a portion of the 1st and 2nd floors which is 18,587 Sq Ft on the 1st floor and
          16,446 Sq Ft on the 2nd floor. These areas include workstations, private offices, meeting
          rooms, break rooms and restrooms.
       c. The 1st floor also currently house's a data center of 3,377 Sq Ft or 2.6% of the total building
          footprint. This data center supports Comcast's operations in Utah, Arizona, and California.
       d. The 1st floor also includes building support areas such as UPS, communications room &
          mechanical rooms totaling 2,621 Sq Ft or 2% of the building footprint.
       e. The proposed warehouse space will contain 6,295 Sq Ft of the 1st floor or 4.8% of the total
          building footprint.
  3) With the addition of this warehouse space Comcast will add an additional 182 employees reporting
     to the Sandy location. Of those employees, 176 would be field technicians that either maintain our
     outside plant or install and service our customer's service throughout the Salt Lake Valley.

Comcast is asking for an expedited approval of this request as Comcast is working with two real estate
deadlines as it relates to this project. One being a lease expiration of our West Valley facility at the end of
June 2021. The second being the sale of our Miller facility to the developers for the Mill Creek
redevelopment project.
