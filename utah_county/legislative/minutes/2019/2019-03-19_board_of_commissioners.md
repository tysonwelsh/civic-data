COMMISSIONER AINGE, APPRICIATE WORK THAT IS GOING ON. ASKED QUESTION, CAN
YOU GIVE ME A GENERAL SENSE OF WHAT THESE AGREEMENTS ARE DOING?

RICHARD NELSON, PUBLIC WORKS, WE HAD THE FIRES THAT HAPPENED IN THE SOUTH
END OF THE VALLEY. THERE WAS ABOUT 130 ACRES AFFECTED. THERE IS A PROGRAM
FROM THE NATIONAL RESOURCES CONSERVATION SERVICE (NRCS) CALLED THE
EMERGENCY WATERSHED PROGRAM. THAT PROGRAM GIVES FUNDING FOR SOME OF
THE AFTER FIRE METIGATION WORK TO HELP WITH DRBREE FLOW THAT WILL COME
AFTER THE FIRE. THE NRCS HAS REQUESTED THAT THE COUNTY ACT AS SPONSORS ON
THIS RATHER THAN HAVE MULTIPLE APPLICANTS FROM ALL OF THE CITIES. WE
APPLIED IN OCTOBER AS THE SPONSOR FOR ABOUT 24 MILLION DOLLARS OF WORK.
THESE INTERLOCAL AGREEMENTS BRING THE CITIES IN TO PAY THEIR INDIVIDUAL 25%
MATCH FOR THE WORK BEING DONE IN THEIR AREA SO THE COUNTY IS NOT
RESPONSIBLE FOR THE WHOLE 25% MATCH. THERE IS ONE AGREEMENT WITH THE
STRAWBERRY WATER USER WICH IS FOR THE SAME THING. THEY HAVE WORK ON
CANALS IN PAYSON AND SPANISH FORK CANYONS.

COMMISSIONER AINGE, ASKED IF THE FUNDING THROUGH NRCS IS THE FUNDING AS
THE STATE APPROPRIATED FUNDS AND ARE THE FUNDS COMING DIRECTLY TO THE
COUNTY OR ARE, THEY GOING DIRECTLY TO THE CITIES?

RICHARD NELSON, IT IS A MIX. THE APPLICATION HAS BEEN MADE, WHICH STARTS A
CLOCK OF 220 DAYS TO HAVE DESIGNED AND BUILT. THAT IS WHY WE ARE TRYING TO
HAVE THE INTERLOCAL AGREEMENTS IN PLACE BEFORE THE 220 DAY WINDOW STARTS.

COMMISSIONER AINGE, ASKED TO THE AGREEMENTS WORK WHERE THE
MUNICIPALITIES ARE GETTING THE MONEY DIRECTLY?

RICHARD NELSON, ANSWERED YES, THE FUNDS ARE A REIMBURSEMENT FUNDS. THE
CITIES ARE RESPONSIBLE TO GET THE WORK DONE IN THEIR OWN CITIES. THEN WE
WOULD APPLY FOR THE NRCS FUNDS. WE REIMBURSE THE CITIES WHEN WE GET FUNDS
FROM NRCS.

COMMISISONER AINGE, SO THEY ARE JUST APPOINTING US AS THE CONDUIT. WE APPLY
FOR THE FUNDS THROUGH NCRS.

COMMISSIONER LEE, THIS IS AN IMPORTANT STEP BECAUSE THERE ARE PERCENTAGES
ATTACHED TO THEM. UTAH COUNTIES PERCENTAGE IS ABOUT 41% OF THE TOTAL
PROJECT. EACH CITY HAS A DIFFERENT PERCENTAGE. THE INTERLOCAL AGREEMENTS
ARE IMPORTANT TO HELP WITH COSTS AND CRITICAL THAT WE HAVE THEM SIGNED.

RICHARD NELSON, POINT OF INTEREST, THERE IS AN RFP IN PLACE TO HAVE A
CONSULTANT IN PLACE WHEN THE NRCS AGREEMENT IS DONE.

COMMISISONER AINGE ASKED WHAT HAPPENS IF THE CITIES DON’T AGEE?

RICHARD NELSON, ALL THE CITIES HAVE AGREED, JUST WAITING FOR SIGNED
DOCUMENT FROM WOODLAND HILLS.

COMMISSIONER LEE, ALONG WITH WHAT IS ON THE AGENDA WANTED TO NOTE THAT
SOME OF EXIHIBITS A AND B DID NOT GET UPLOADED AND ARE ON EACH INTERLOCAL
AGREEMENT.
