In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”

rain last night. Keep it in your prayers. Shooting and failed bombing in NYC today. Our society is getting to the point

work, and learning from failure.” We all need to get involved. I’m grateful when people come and talk to us about
their issues.
