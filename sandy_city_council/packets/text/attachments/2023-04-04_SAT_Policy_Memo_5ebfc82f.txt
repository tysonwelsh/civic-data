From:            Brooke D’Sousa, Council Member At-large

Subject:         Second Reading: Survey Approval Team Proposal



On February 21st and again on March 21st I came to the Council with a proposal to establish a Survey
Approval Team (SAT) to help ensure that both branches of government have equal opportunity to give
input on informational surveys before they are issued. The Council, Mayor, and staff all provided me
with great feedback on my proposal, and I’ve incorporated as much of that feedback as possible into the
Ordinance that I’m presenting you with this evening.

The purpose of this ordinace is to create a framework that allows for, and encourages collaboration
between the two branches to get better results and better data for better decision making.

Ordinance 23-06 adds a new chapter to the Sandy Legislative Code.

In conclusion, tonight I’m looking for the Council to take action and adopt Ordinance 23-06 as written
and attached to this agenda item.
