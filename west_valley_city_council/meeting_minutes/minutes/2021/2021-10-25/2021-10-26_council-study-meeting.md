CONSIDER APPLICATION SMI-5-2021, FILED BY MIKE GARZA,
REQUESTING FINAL PLAT APPROVAL FOR THE CREWE 2 LOT
SUBDIVISION LOCATED AT 3588 SOUTH 5450 WEST
Steve Lehman, CED, presented proposed application SMI-5-2021, Filed by Mike Garza,
Requesting Final Plat Approval for the Crewe 2 Lot Subdivision Located at 3588 South 5450
West.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Mr. Mark Garza, is requesting final plat approval for the Crewe 2 Lot Subdivision.
       The subject property currently has an existing dwelling that will be removed to
       accommodate two new single-family dwellings. The property is bordered on the
       north, east and south by existing residential uses along 5450 West and the Erin Hills
       PUD to the west.

       The applicant is the developer of the Erin Hills PUD and the owner of the lots
       proposed in this application. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on .42 acres.
       Although zoned R-1-8, both lots are approximately 9,250 square feet in size.
       During the rezoning process for the Erin Hills project, the developer requested an
       exception to the frontage for these two lots. An exception was granted by the City
       Council that each lot would be approved with 66 feet of frontage.

       Access to the subdivision will be gained from 5450 West. At the present time, this
       street is dedicated and improved with asphalt only. Although there are a few
       sections with curb, gutter and sidewalk, the majority of this street is void of those
       improvements. Staff has discussed the long-term vision of this right-of-way with
       the City Engineering Division. Future discussions are planned for the vision of
       5450 West and whether public improvements should be planned for this right-of-
       way. For the present time, the City will require a delay agreement for such
       improvements, but that could change based on discussions with the Public Works
       Department.

COMMUNICATIONS
A.  JUSTICE COURT REPORT
    Nicole Cottle, Assistant City Manager, stated that the Justice Court cannot legally
    be operated under the Legal Department’s jurisdiction. She explained that the
    Justice Court is governed by the Supreme Court so the City has limited ability to
    govern various rules and regulations. Nicole relayed how the Justice Court
    functioned under COVID-19 protocol and how the Court system has changed since
    the onset of the pandemic. She detailed how jury trials were backed up during the
    height of the pandemic and how they were, and are currently, being handled. Nicole
    detailed how the court will be moved to the current location of the Juvenile Court
    that is connected to City Hall in the near future. She discussed case numbers, tickets
    being processed, etc. Councilmember Buhler asked what the judges are and what
    types of cases they can process. Nicole replied that they are Justice Court judges
    and they can process everything below a Class A, and sometimes some Class A’s
    as well as small claims and infractions. Councilmember Buhler asked if they
    process Code Enforcement cases. Nicole replied no and noted that these are
    processed by Jody Knapp, an ALJ judge. Councilmember Buhler asked if there are
    any other ALJ’s employed by the City. Nicole replied that there are.
    Councilmember Buhler clarified rights of a jury trial and Nicole detailed the rules
    and regulations. Councilmember Buhler asked if more judges are needed. Nicole
    replied she does not believe that is necessary at this time but noted that it may be in
    the future. She added that the judges and clerks are operating at a 100% pace and
    do a great job at being effective and efficient. Councilmember Buhler asked if the
    judges are appointed by a judicial nominating committee. Nicole replied yes.
