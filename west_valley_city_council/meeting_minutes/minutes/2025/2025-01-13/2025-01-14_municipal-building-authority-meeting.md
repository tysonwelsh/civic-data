    ELECT CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FOR 2025
    Vice Chairman Huynh stated election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson for 2025 had
    been scheduled for consideration by the Board. He advised a rotation schedule had been
    used in the past to elect a Chair and Vice Chair, however this was an option for the Board
    and not mandatory.

    Vice Chairman Huynh called for a motion.

    Mr. Nordfelt moved to elect Tom Huynh as Chair of the Municipal Building Authority for
    2025 and Lars Nordfelt as Vice Chair of the Municipal Building Authority for 2025. Mr.
    Harmon seconded the motion.

    A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.


    MOTION TO ADJOURN
    Mr. Whetstone moved to adjourn at 7:02 PM.
