6.         22-172                 Community Development, Economic Development/RDA, Police/Animal 
                                  Services, and Fire Departments presenting their FY 2022-23 tentative 
                                  department budgets.
                   Attachments:   May 24th Presentations (ComDev, RDA, Police, Fire).pdf

                                  The following budget presentations were given to the Council:

                                  James Sorenson, Community Development Director, introduced staff recently promoted 
                                  to new positions within Community Development. He presented the FY 2023 proposed 
                                  budget to the Council. He provided highlights and accomplishments from his department 
                                  over the past year. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Kasey Dunlavy, Economic Development Director, presented the proposed Fiscal Year 
                                  budget for Economic Development/Redevelopment Agency. He reviewed the various 
                                  projects in EDA/RD. Council questions followed.

                                  Chief Severson, Police, introduced his Captains and Animal Services Director. He 
                                  presented the proposed Fiscal Year 2023 budget for Police. He thanked the Council and 
                                  Administration for funding the compensation request back in September. He provided 
                                  information on officer staffing and introduced three new officers in his department that 
                                  have come from out of state and have a great deal of expreience. Ian Williams, Animal 
                                  Services Director, provided an overview of his department. Council questions followed.

                                  Chief Cline, Fire, presented the proposed 2023 Fiscal Year budget. He recognized staff in 
                                  his department and spoke about the highly trained paramedics that have been able to go 
                                  to training because of the funds approved for his department by the Council. Our City has 
                                  received a Class 2 designation which saves residents approximately 3% to 6% on their 
                                  home insurance costs. He reviewed the department requests that are included in the FY 
                                  2023 budget. Council questions followed.




7.         22-179                 Administration presenting the council with an update and asking for 
                                  discussion on HB 440
                   Attachments:   HB440HomelessPP.ppt
                                  HB 440 Overview 3.24.pdf

                                  HB 440 - Homeless Services Amendment. She gave an overview of key provisions, 
                                  process, plan requirements, and site selection schedule. Council questions followed. 
                                  Council requested an additional update on the HB 440 process later in the summer.

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                                  Dustin Fratto, Executive Council Director, mentioned that we will have a meeting on May 
                                  31, 2022. He gave an overview of upcoming agenda items.

Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Brooke D'Sousa provided an update on the Sandy Youth Council 
                                  program. Our applicants for the 2022-23 program year represent 12 different high schools 
                                  in grades 9th through 12th. Our first meeting is June 9th.

                                  Council Member Ryan Mecham invited the public to attend the June 15th event in Historic 
                                  Sandy. He also provided an update of the Sister City program that he is currently working 
                                  with staff on reactivating the program. He is planning on visiting our Sister City, Riesa, 
                                  Germany this fall. 

                                  Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update from the Council of Governments 
                                  Committee (COG). She spoke about the transportation alternatives for Little Cottonwood 
                                  Canyon. The preferred alternative should be announced in the upcoming months. Ms. 
                                  Stroud thanked the Fire department staff for holding a wildland fire prevention event last 
                                  weekend.

                                  Council Member Cyndi Sharkey spoke about the recent Brookwood Elementary School 
                                  field trip to City Hall that she was involved with. The "kid" council had a lively discussion 
                                  on the "law" they were trying to pass which would "allow" unlimited video gaming on 
                                  Wednesdays. In the end, the new ordinance failed to pass but the kids had a great time 
                                  learning about the legislative process!

                                  Council Member Zach Robinson gave an update from the Senior Center Advisory Board 
                                  meeting. He mentioned that the senior center members have a great interest in water 
                                  conservation. The Center needs volunteers to help with projects. He also mentioned that 
                                  the Recreation department is in need of summer seasonal staff.

Mayor's Report

                   Mayor Zoltanski mentioned that  Alta Canyon pool opens this Saturday and the new Bell 
                   Canyon Preservation Trailhead ribbon cutting is also this Thursday. She thanked Council 
                   Member Stroud for attending the Wildland Fire Prevention event. Mayor Z also mentioned 
                   that the City is transitioning the coordination of the Healing Fields events to become a 
                   city sponsored and run community event.

CAO Report

                   Cliff Strachan, CAO, spoke about the city's efforts to respond to residents' concerns 
                   regarding speeders in Sandy City. The Mayor has directed Adminstration staff to create a 
                   task force focused on addressing issues related to speeding in the city. 

Adjournment
                   Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 8:15 pm.
