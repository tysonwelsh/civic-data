TIP Selection Process, Project Ideas and Study Approval Bob Allen, MAG (1:52) MAG is continuing the 2024 TIP selection process that began last year and will be completed in August. For the Project Idea Phase of the process, 50 projects were submitted with a total draft cost estimate of over $250M. On December 4th, MPO TAC met to review and give feedback for each project, and MAG staff reviewed and rated each project based on the adopted selection criteria. The purpose of this phase is to help projects determine whether to continue with the process. No feedback or rating precludes a project from moving to the Concept Phase Studies submitted through this process do not need to continue into the Concept Phase. The following studies are requesting to be funded. Mid Valley Highway Feasibility Eagle Mountain $250,000 Nebo Belt Corridor Salem $350,000 Spring Creek Corridor AT Provo/Springville $150,000 Independence Ave Connection Provo/Orem $165,000 Woodland Hills Trail Feasibility Woodland Hills $100,000 Total $1,015,000 MPO Technical Advisory Committee January 22, 2024 Page 3 Orem requested funds to update their Active Transportation Master Plan. MAG’s policy has been to fund an initial active transportation plan but allow each community to maintain and update that plan . Orem AT Plan Update Orem $325,000 Jill Spencer asked how studies scheduled to begin this year are funded. Bob Allen explained that MAG is conservative when it allocates projected funds to TIP projects. When revenue exceeds projections, it creates potential gaps in funding to cover the cost of studies. Money that is left over is used for the following selection process. Jason Bond moved to recommend that the five studies be funded as presented. Vern Keeslar seconded the motion. Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Modification Bob Allen, MAG Santaquin Main Street (11:17) Santaquin was awarded two separate phases of a project to widen Main Street from 3 lanes to 5 lanes. Phase 4 was awarded $4,450,000 to widen from 300 E to 100 W in 2018. Phase 5 was awarded $5,270,000 in 2020 to extend from 100 W to 600 W and complete the project. In an effort to reduce costs and construction impacts, Santaquin has combined the two phases into one project. The project is fully designed and initially went out to bid with only one response totaling over $20m. Since that time, Santaquin went out to bid again with better results. Construction inflation raised the total cost to $17,726,127 and is requesting additional funds be added to the project. UDOT has already added $753,000 to aid with signal work and pavement preservation. Original Funds $ 9,720,000 UDOT Funds $ 753,000 New Funding Needed $ 6,195,160 New Project Total $16,668,160 MPO Technical Advisory Committee January 22, 2024 Page 4 Brad Kenison moved to recommend that the requested funds be added to the project. Ben Hunter seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. UDOT Santaquin Main St; 100 W to 600 W, Phase 5 - Funding Addition UDOT Santaquin Main St Improvements Phase 4 - Funding Addition Eagle Mountain Airport RD (24:54) In 2022, Airport Road in Eagle Mountain was awarded county transportation tax funds to construct a new two-lane connection between SR73 and Eagle Mountain Town Center near Cedar Valley High School. This new project would add a new connection in a congested area. As the project was designed, it was determined that the intersection of SR73 needed to be modified to meet safety standards. In addition, cost estimates show additional funds are needed to cover construction inflation. Since the project was selected, a new school has been announced on the north side of the intersection. Eagle Mountain is requesting additional funds as well as a scope addition to accommodate the connection on the north side of SR73. Vern Keeslar moved to recommend that the Eagle Mountain Airport Rd project scope be modified as presented with the addition of $2,779,487. Jason Bond seconded the motion.
