                                    ORDINANCE NO. 1634

               AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL
         PROVIDING A ONE-TIME INCENTIVE PAYMENT TO THE MAYOR IN
          RECOGNITION FOR HIS OUTSTANDING SERVICE AND EXTENSIVE
         INVOLVEMENT ON VARIOUS BOARDS AND COMMISSIONS DURING
                           THE 2024-2025 PERIOD


        WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code (“DCMC”) 2-6-070 states “[e]lected and
appointed officers of the city shall receive such compensation for their services as the mayor and
city council may fix by ordinance…”; and

        WHEREAS, the powers and duties of the Mayor are described in DCMC 2-2-020 and
state law; and

        WHEREAS, Mayor Walker has demonstrated exceptional dedication to Draper City by
actively serving on multiple boards and commissions, including:

   •   President of Utah League of Cities and Towns (2024-2025)
          o Past-President in 2025-2026
          o 1st Vice President in 2023-2024
   •   Chair of Active Transportation Committee (ATC) – Wasatch Front Regional Committee
   •   Board Member for the Point of the Mountain State Land Authority (POMSLA)
   •   Local Advisory Council Member for Utah Transit Authority (UTA)- 2024 Chair
   •   Board Member for the Pete Suazo Utah Athletic Commission (PSUAC)
   •   Member of POST Council (Peace Officer Standards and Training)
   •   Board Member for the South Valley Chamber
   •   Board of Trustees Member for Lone Peak Hospital
   •   President of Municipal Building Authority (MBA) for Draper City
   •   Chair of Community Reinvestment Agency (CRA) for Draper City
   •   Member of COM (Conference of Mayors)
   •   Member of COG (Council of Governments)
   •   Gold Star Committee Member; and

        WHEREAS, Mayor Walker’s extensive service on these boards and commissions not
only exceeds his basic duties but has also been instrumental in safeguarding Draper City’s
interests. His leadership on the Point of the Mountain State Land Authority (POMSLA) is
particularly noteworthy, as it positions Draper to effectively address the significant impacts of
the state’s relocation of the prison and the development of The Point project, thereby ensuring
that the needs of our residents and businesses are prioritized in these crucial decisions; and

        WHEREAS, Mayor Walker has devoted countless hours representing Draper City; often
at the expense of time with his family and personal financial sacrifice as a self-employed
attorney; and

       WHEREAS, DCMC 2-6-070 requires the compensation for elected officials be set by
ordinance following a public hearing; and
       WHEREAS, the City Council has posted public notice and held the requisite public
hearing regarding a one-time incentive payment for Mayor Walker; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council has determined that a one-time incentive payment for Mayor
Walker is warranted to acknowledge the additional responsibilities he has assumed during 2024-
2025.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1.   One-time Incentive Payment. A one-time incentive payment in the amount
of $____________ for the Mayor is hereby authorized.

        Section 2.     Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 3.     Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final
passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE _____ DAY OF FEBRUARY 2025.
