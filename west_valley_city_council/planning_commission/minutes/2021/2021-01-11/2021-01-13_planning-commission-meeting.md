ZT-10-2020
West Valley City
A zone text change to allow outdoor event centers in conjunction with a regional shopping
mall as a conditional use in the C-2 zone.

The City’s Planning staff is requesting a zone text change to Section 7-7-110, which is the
Section of the Zoning Ordinance that addresses regulations specific to outdoor event centers.
The proposed change would allow outdoor event centers in conjunction with a regional shopping
mall as a conditional use in the C-2 zone. For reference, a regional shopping mall is defined as “a
Shopping Center located within 200 feet of Interstate 215 with a combined Building size over
500,000 square feet and a minimum site area of 50 acres.” By definition, the Valley Fair Mall is
the only regional shopping center in the City.
The Valley Fair Mall has held or is planning on holding outdoor events that fall into one of the
              two following categories:

      Third Party Vendor Events - these events are primarily hosted, organized, and operated
       by third party vendors. Examples of these events include car shows, breast cancer
       awareness, Bubble Bash, and friends for sight.

      Valley Fair Mall Sponsored Community Events - these events are concert series, outdoor
       movie nights, festivals, fairs, and multiple vendor booths. These are typically between
       200-500 people.

The mall is in the process of remodeling the outdoor plaza west of the main mall building. In
addition to making the plaza a more inviting place for patrons to linger, the remodel would also
facilitate small, outdoor events.

The proposed code change would allow the mall to apply for a conditional use permit to receive
approval to hold the outdoor, sponsored, community events like concerts. Through the
conditional use process, the Commission can impose conditions on items like hours of operation
and the place of sound emitting equipment to minimize impacts on adjoining properties.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval.

   2. Continuance.

   3. Denial.


Discussion:     Steve Pastorik presented the application. Commissioner Fuller asked Steve what
                would be the highest decibel level allowed for outdoor event centers.
                Steve explained the decibel level limitations could be addressed in the conditional
                use permit or the zoning ordinance.
                Casey Bulkley, General Manager of the Valley Fair Mall, addressed the Planning
                Commission. Casey stated that he has been working with Steve and he has taken
                note of all concerns. Casey pointed out a prior event at the Valley Fair Mall
                where the music was too loud; however, he is aware that is not the standard. He
                stated the goal of these events is to bring in customers for retail so that most
                events would be held during store hours. Casey explained they could also have
                small community events such as giving away backpacks to children for school or
                free eye exams. These events would be in the town center outside where the
                splash pad is with some music or something along those lines, but those events
                would be rare. Casey stated that he endorses the application and wants to have a
                decibel limitation as well as specified hours.
               Chair Meaders stated to the Commissioners that it sounds like the primary
               decision is if we want to handle the details with a conditional use or in the zoning
               ordinance.
               Commissioner Winters likes the idea of these outdoor events and would like to go
               the traditional route of a conditional use. Commissioner Winters explained that
               with a conditional use, we can look at the specifics and make more specific
               decisions. He stated he would be ready to make a motion.


Motion:        Commissioner Winters motioned to approve ZT-10-2020

GPZ-8-2020
West Valley City
3060 W 3650 S
General plan change from very high density residential to high density residential and a
zone change from CC to RM and R-2-8
6.73 acre

West Valley City staff has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for six parcels
totaling 6.73 acres located at 3060 W 3650 S. All six parcels are currently zoned CC (City
Center) with a General Plan designation of very high density residential (over 20 units/acre). The
proposed zoning is RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) for the 5.63-acre parcel to the south
and R-2-8 (Two Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 sq. ft.) for the 5 parcels,
which are 0.22 acres each, to the north. The proposed General Plan designation for all six parcels
is high density residential.

Surrounding zones include R-2-8 to the north; RM and R-2-8 to the west; CC, RM and R-2-8 to
the east; and RM and R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 sq. ft.) to
the south. Surrounding land uses include duplexes to the north, single family homes and
duplexes to the west, the City’s Promenade Park and condos to the east and single-family homes
to the south. The subject property to the south contains the Valley Fair Apartments owned by
Housing Connect, the Salt Lake County Housing Authority. The five parcels on the north are all
developed as duplexes.

The City actually initiated the General Plan/zone change application (GPZ-1-2016) that was
approved back in 2016 for the CC zone and the very high density residential General Plan
designation. At the time, the County Housing Authority was proposing to redevelop the Valley
Fair Apartments along with the duplexes to the north assuming the properties could be acquired
by the City similar to other properties within Fairbourne Station. The General Plan/zone change
application approval was granted subject to a development agreement. While the City Council
approved the development agreement, it was not signed by the County Housing Authority. At the
time, the County Housing Authority was unsure that project financing could be secured. For the
information of the Planning Commission, staff no longer takes a development agreement to the
City Council without the appropriate signatures.

While Housing Connect is still interested in redeveloping the Valley Fair Apartments given the
age of the units and Housing Connect’s desire to provide more units, the design will be different
from the concept forwarded in the 2016 development agreement. This is due in part to the duplex
properties no longer being considered as part of the project. Since the current zoning and General
Plan designation were put in place subject to a development agreement that is not valid, City
staff is recommending that the General Plan and zoning be changed back to what they were
before the change in 2016.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. The RM and R-2-8 zones recognize the existing development.

   2. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the
      public hearing.
