Chair Curt Cochran called the meeting to order at 6:30 p.m. and welcomed those in attendance. A
roll call was conducted wherein all council members were present, except for Council Member
Barbieri who was excused.

        1.1      Opening Ceremonies – Pledge/Reverence – Council Member Burgess (Opening
                 Ceremonies for May 15, 2024 to be arranged by the Council Member Barbieri)

       Council Member Burgess directed the Pledge of Allegiance, after which Council Member
Knudsen offered comments regarding the fact that when someone did something great or
noteworthy, it was rarely a singular effort. Instead, it was the result of a team of people working
well together for the greater good.

        1.2      Mayor’s Report

     Mayor Overson provided a comprehensive report covering various activities, which included
multiple ribbon-cutting ceremonies welcoming new businesses, ChamberWest's Women in
Business Annual Conference, the League of Cities and Towns Conference in St. George, the start
of the Taylorsville girls fast pitch softball season, a tour of the Jordan Valley Water Terminal
Reservoir, and CPR refresher courses for city staff. The Mayor also highlighted participation in
community-related events, distributed a budget presentation, and discussed the successful 28th
annual awards ceremony for Taylorsville employees.


        1.3      Citizen Comments

      Chair Cochran invited any member of the public who wished to address the City Council to
step forward and do so.

Karina McClellan, representing Ability's Inclusion Services, introduced her organization's efforts
in serving adults with disabilities and expressed interest in establishing a presence in Taylorsville.

Brian Miller registered complaints about the road conditions and noise pollution issues within the
city, suggesting stricter road noise regulations and maintenance improvements.

There were no additional citizen comments, and Chair Cochran closed the citizen comment period.

3.      REPORTS

        3.1      Taylorsville Police Department – Chief Cottam

             Chief Cottam delivered the quarterly police report. Special commendations were given
to officers Ryan Lutz, Noah McKee, and Garrett Birch for exceptional work ranging from detective
efforts to compassionate handling of difficult situations. The Chief also provided crime statistics,
discussed trends in different council districts, highlighted traffic incidents, and shared milestones
as the department approaches its third year.

     Referencing one report, Chair Cochran asked for clarification on what Public Order Free Text
referred to. Chief Cottam stated it was for general type cases that were not usually criminal cases.
       Runaways were mentioned and Chief Cottam stated that, the percentage for reuniting
runaways with families was high.

The Chief mentioned the number of records requests being received by his department and
indicated that the number would continue to rise. He then praised the City’s Code Enforcement
Department and mentioned that he was impressed with the amount of work they did as well as
their effective ability to patrol the city on a daily basis.

       3.2       West Valley Animal Services – Melanie Bennett

             Melanie Bennett of West Valley Animal Services shared a yearly update, emphasizing
the case increases in Taylorsville, adoption numbers, and the impact of volunteer efforts on animal
care. Reporting also included information on licensing compliance strategies, urban wildlife
challenges, education efforts, and fruitful events hosted during the past year.

        7.1      Resolution No. 24-07 - Consideration of a Resolution of the City of Taylorsville
                 Approving an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement Between the City and Salt Lake
                 County Regarding the Urban Wildlife Assistance Program – John Taylor

      John Taylor explained that Salt Lake County wished to enter into an Interlocal Cooperation
Agreement with Taylorsville regarding the Urban Wildlife Assistance Program. He stated it was
a new program. He stated the actual agreement is with Salt Lake County who has the contract
with the actual company involved. The cost would be $9,600.00 per year. John Taylor noted that
the cost had already been added to the tentative budget. He stated the contract would be renewed
tentatively every 5 years with no cost increase over that time.

     Council Member Harker asked how the raccoons and skunks were being disposed of in the
past. Mr. Taylor stated residents needed to dispose of the animals on their own. Clarification was
given for the full process of disposal.

MOTION:          Council Member Knudsen moved to approve Resolution No. 24-07, approving
                 an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Salt Lake County and the City
                 of Taylorsville regarding the Urban Wildlife Assistance Program. The motion
                 was seconded by Council Member Burgess.
