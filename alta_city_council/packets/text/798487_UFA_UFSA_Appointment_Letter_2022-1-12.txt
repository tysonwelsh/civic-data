The Alta Town Council hereby appoints Mayor Roger Bourke to serve on the Board of Trustees of the
UFSA and the UFA as the elected official to represent the Town of Alta. Mayor Bourke understands his
responsibility to participate in monthly board meetings and act in the best interest of the public as he
looks forward to the tasks of focusing on the Board issues and solution-based planning for the agency.

The Town Council would also like to renew the appointment of Elise Morgan as the alternate elected
official to represent the Town of Alta.

Voted by the Alta Town Council on January 12, 2022
