                                           PUBLIC HEARINGS
                                       Hearings began at – 6:35 p.m.

31120 – Wei Li is requesting approval to rezone approximately .30 acres of property from the R-1-6 (Single-
Family residential) zone to the R-1-5 (Single-Family residential) zone. Location: 3054 South 8850 West.
Planner: Todd A. Draper, AICP

Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services Planner Todd Draper provided an analysis of the
Staff Report.

Commissioner Richards asked the size of lot.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Wei Li
Address: 8381 Golden Cypress Avenue, Las Vegas, NV
Comments: Ms. Li said the purpose is to build another house. Her son lives in Salt Lake City, and works
at the County and rents an apartment and would like to build a house for him to live in.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Weight seconded that motion.

Speaker # 2: Citizen
Name: Victoria Crosby
Address: 3054 South 8850 West
Comments: Ms. Crosby said her concern was the house would be built too close, but now she understands
and would still want to rent to own the house she’s living in.

Speaker # 3: Citizen
Name: Rob Crosby
Address: 3054 South 8850 West
Comments: Mr. Crosby said the lot is more open and looks like more than two places open to that lot, the
one south next to it, nothings being done on it.

Commissioner Richards motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Weight seconded that motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Commissioners had a brief discussion regarding consistent with lots in the neighborhood and uniqueness
to Magna, but not the neighborhood.

Motion: to recommend application #31120 to the Magna Metro Township Council for approval as
presented.
       Motion by: Commissioner Elieson
       2nd by: Commissioner Weight

31085- Dominion Energy (Chris Balling) is requesting a Conditional Use approval for a Liquid Gas Facility
Acreage: 160 acres. Location: 1731 South 8000 West. Zone: M-2. Planner: Diana Martinez

Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the
Staff Report.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: HDR Engineering
Name: Brent Arnold
Address: 2825 East Cottonwood Parkway
Comments: Mr. Arnold said they have been on the project four years and early on did wetland studies.

Commissioner Richards asked why the women’s control room is half the size of the men’s. Mr. Arnold said
there is less frequent visits then the men working there.

Commissioner Weight motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion.

No one from the public was present to speak.

Commissioner Weight motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Richards seconded that
motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Motion: To approve application #31085 as presented with staff recommendations.
      Motion by: Commissioner Richards
      2nd by: Commissioner Elieson
      Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)

Commissioner Weight motioned to adjourn, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion.
