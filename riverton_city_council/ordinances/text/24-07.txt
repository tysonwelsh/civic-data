      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

           WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map from RR- 22 and C- PO/ EHOV/ SD to RM- 6/ SD for the
approximately 9. 26 acres located at 12701 South 3600 West.

           NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
                     Section 1.                  The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is, amended
                                                 to reflect the described changes as shown in Exhibit " A" and
                                                 described    in Exhibit ` B"


                     Section 2.                  The zoning includes the following Specific Development
                                                 Designations:
                                                 1.    Homestead Phases 1 &      2 shall be age restricted compliant
                                                       with all applicable laws to 55 and older for a minimum of
                                                       80%   of units.
                                                 2.    Units shall not front onto Water Street, and no individual
                                                       driveway access onto Water Street shall be allowed.
                                                 3.    All other approved elements for Homestead Phase 1 shall
                                                       remain in effect.


                     Section 3.                  This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.
