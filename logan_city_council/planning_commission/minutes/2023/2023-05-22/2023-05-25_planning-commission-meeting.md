PC_ 23-029 Spring View Estates Subdivision [Subdivision Permit] Paul P Burghardt/SHPB
Holdings LLC, Authorized agent/owner are requesting a subdivision permit to create 4 single-family
residential building lots on 1.48-acres located at 1900 South 1100 West in the Traditional
Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 03-142-0201 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley reviewed the request for a subdivision permit for a 4-lot single-family
subdivision. The property is surrounded by manufactured and mobile homes established from the
1980s up to the early 2000s. The proposed subdivision is considered an infill subdivision, as two of
the four proposed lots are not adjacent to streets. The Land Development Code requires a twenty-
foot wide paved driveway with a four-foot setback to the perimeter property line and an eight-foot
setback to the home on the front lot for a total of thirty-two feet of width to access the rear lot. As
proposed the subdivision meets that requirement. The Fire Code requires a second emergency
egress for subdivisions that have 30 homes or more. Where this subdivision connects to the Spring
View Mobile Home Park, their proposed shared driveway needs to run the length of the property so
it can be used as emergency egress for the homes to the north if needed. This should be added to
the conditions.

Commissioner Heare asked if this emergency access should have been completed in 1995 when
the Spring View Subdivision was created as shown on the plat map.

Commissioner Peterson asked if the residents can park on the shared drive. R. Holley said that the
shared driveway will have to remain clear, no parking will be allowed.

PROPONENT: No comments were made by the applicant.

PC 23-031 675 N 500 W Rezone [Zone Change] Tyler Burns/Renewvate Realty LLC, authorized
agent/owner are requesting a rezone of a 1.01-acre lot from Mobile Home (MH) to Mixed Residential
Low (MR-12) to develop a 12-unit apartment complex located at 675 North 500 West in the Mobile
Home (MH) zone; TIN 05-044-0019 (Bridger Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley reviewed the request to rezone a 1.01-acre lot from Mobile Home (MH) to Mixed
Residential Low (MR-12). This site has been used as a storage area and one caretaker home site
for the Palatial Living Mobile Home Park. The property was historically associated with the mobile
home park until recently when it was sold to a different property owner. The lot is accessed via 500
West or 725 North, both private streets within the mobile home park. The property is surrounded by
Mobile Homes to the northeast, Single Family to the southeast, Commercial to the southwest, and
Industrial to the west. The Future Land Use Plan (FLUP) lists this area as Detached Residential
(DR) with the nearest Mixed Residential (MR) zone located three blocks away along 1000 north.
Staff feels that MR-12 is not compatible with this area and the goals of the Bridger Neighborhood.
An option may be to amend the language in the MH zone to allow for more than one mobile home
at this location so the property can be better utilized. The existing ingress/egress would not meet
the requirements for a multi-family development at this location.

R. Holley referenced Land Development Code, Chapter 17.07.104 Mobile Homes (MH) which
states: “The Mobile Home zoning district is intended to accommodate existing mobile home parks.
Mobile home parks are developments in which the resident may own or rent the mobile home, but
rents the land on which the mobile home is located. New mobile home parks are not permitted.
Densities shall not exceed six (6) units per acre and the minimum lot size shall be 6,000 square
feet. Site development shall be consistent with original project approvals.”

M. DeSimone said that the code clearly states that new Mobile Home parks are not allowed, and
existing parks have to comply with their original approval. They cannot expand regardless of how
much additional land is available.

PROPONENT: Tyler Burns said that he just purchased this property and is trying to figure out what
he can do with it.

PUBLIC: James Merrill Bird who lives to the south on 500 West spoke about a twelve-plex bringing
in too much traffic for the private street.

COMMISSION: The commission discussed the possibility of developing this property for single-
family housing. Commissioner Doutre said that MR-12 seems more compatible and more of a
transitional zone from Industrial to Single Family. Commissioner Peterson said that this proposal
should be denied and the applicant should come back with a rezone request to a different zone or
an amendment to the Mobile Home zone to allow additional mobile homes.

MOTION: Commissioner Peterson moved to forward _a Recommendation of Denial to the
Municipal Council on a Zone Change for PC 23-031 with the findings for denial listed below.
Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.


Commissioner Doutre said that finding number four needs to be amended to remove single-family
homes and state that the majority of the surrounding area is mobile homes, industrial and multi-
family. The property is not surrounded by any single-family homes.

FINDINGS FOR DENIAL

1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as Detached Residential (DR).

2. The surrounding zoning is NR-6 and IP with the nearest multi-family zoning located
approximately 1200’ to the north

3. The MR-12 zone is not appropriate because the largest multi-family structure in the vicinity in a
4-plex to the south.

4. The majority of the surrounding area is mobile homes, industrial, and multi-family.

5. This site was developed as one dwelling unit and ancillary storage facilities to Palatial Living
Mobile Home Park and can continue as that use. Because the property was sold to a different
property owner does not automatically entitle the property to new uses.

Moved: E. Peterson Seconded: R. Croshaw Approved: 5-1
Yea: Heare, Lucero, Croshaw, Lewis, Peterson Nay: Doutre Abstain:
