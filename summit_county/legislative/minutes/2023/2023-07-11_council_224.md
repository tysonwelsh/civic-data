II.
Affordable housing discussion and updates
A.
Regionalization Feasibility Taskforce update
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Staff Report for July 11 Joint PCMC and Summit County Council Meeting.pdf
Attachment: Joint CC Meeting Housing Update Slides 7-11-23 jj.pdf
Jeff Jones, Summit County Economic Development and Housing Director, reported back to the Joint Councils about the research findings on creating a housing authority. Jason Glidden, Park City Affordable Housing Manager, provided the pros and cons of a joint housing authority. They sought Councils’ feedback. Council members shared their views. Daniel Nackerman, Salt Lake City Housing Authority Executive Director, answered questions. Council members approved continuing to pursue a joint housing authority.
B . Affordable housing project-specific updates (10:15 AM)
Manager Glidden updated the Councils on Park City’s affordable housing projects. Council members asked questions, and Mr. Glidden responded.
Director Jones updated the Councils on Summit County’s affordable housing projects. Council members asked questions, and Mr. Jones responded. (10:25 AM)
III.
3Kings Water Treatment Plant Overview (10:49 AM)
Mayor Nann Worel introduced the 3Kings Water Treatment Plant. Clint McAfee, Public Utilities Director, gave a presentation about Park City’s water history and the development of the plant. Michelle De Haan, Water Quality & Treatment Manager, supported the presentation.

IV.
Next joint meeting (11:03 AM)
The Councils agreed to meet again on September 12 at Park City Hall.
V. Water treatment plant site visit
Council members shuttled from Marsac to the 3Kings Water Treatment plant for an overview and walking tour.
