CONSENT CALENDAR:
  a. Consider approval to award bid to Newton Construction for Canyon View
     Substation in the amount of $936,895.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This substation construction includes site
      grading, concrete foundation installation, control enclosure and perimeter wall. Four
      (4) bids were received, and the bid summary sheet is included in the packet. The
      lowest bidder was selected. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Newton
      Construction in the amount of $936,895.

   b. Consider approval of an Agreement to Purchase Real Property from 3000
      East Holdings, LLC and reimburse 3000 East Holdings, LLC for installation of
      improvements.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City desires to acquire property (1.425
      acres) for a detention basin located on the southwest corner of 3000 East and
      Seegmiller Dr. The property is adjacent to and was originally part of the planned
      Arbors subdivision. The property was appraised and determined to have a value of
      $295,000. Improvement of the subject property, including design, will cost
      $455,000, for a total of $750,000 for the purchase price of the property and
      improvements. Additionally, the City bears some participation in the pavement costs
      in 3000 East, it being a major arterial roadway. This additional paving consists of 15
      feet along Arbors Ph 12, 13, and 15 in 3000 East for an amount of $34,861. Total
      property purchase and reimbursement amount in the agreement is $784,861. The
      Washington County Flood Control Authority has authorized an amount of $220,000
      to assist in purchasing the property, which will be reimbursed to the City. Staff
      recommends approval.

   l.   Consider approval of a Cost Sharing Agreement with Entrada Property
        Owners Association for asphalt replacement.

        BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City recently completed an irrigation
        pipeline project within Sinagua Trail in Entrada. This pipeline provides a redundant
        source of irrigation water to the Entrada, Ledges, and Sunbrook areas. JP
        Excavating was the City’s contractor. Staff met with the Entrada Property Owners
        Association (EPOA) and suggested replacing the entire road width’s asphalt, since the
        roadway is nearing the end of its useful life. The City would contribute the portion
        that would have been patched with our irrigation line project. The EPOA board met
        and approved the expenditures to replace the asphalt, with the City contributing the
        amount that would have been spent on the trench patching. Staff recommends
        approval of the agreement for the amount of $57,820.24.

PUBLIC HEARING/FISCAL YEAR 2022-2023 BUDGET:
     Public hearing to receive input on the Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Budget.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Preliminary Fiscal Year 2022-2023
      Budget was presented and discussed during the May 5th, May 12th, and May 26th City
      Council meetings and has been available on the City's website and in the City
      Recorder's Office. State Law requires the City to have at least one (1) public hearing
      to receive citizen input before formally adopting the City’s final annual budget, and
      also requires the budget be adopted on or before June 30th. This year the City will
      hold two (2) public hearings to take citizen input. This is the first hearing, and the
      second hearing will be held on June 16, 2022. Staff recommends holding a public
      hearing to receive input on the proposed Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Budget.

CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a conditional use permit for a cell tower to be known
     as Atlas Cell Tower on approximately .03 acres located at 1842 West Sunset
     Boulevard. Case No. 2022-CUP-004

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The general plan reflects commercial
      development, and the zoning is commercial for the proposed tower location. The
      item requires a conditional use permit because the applicant is proposing an 80'
