                                 RESOLUTION 23-78

           A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL INITIATING
         CHANGES TO DRAPER CITY CODE ADDRESSING INTERNAL AND
            DETACHED ACCESSORY DWELLING UNITS IN THE SOUTH
                            MOUNTAIN AREA.


      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council wishes to adopt land use regulations to
address the neighborhood and traffic impacts of unregulated internal and detached
accessory dwelling units in the South Mountain area; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council desires to initiate the formal process to
adopt land use regulations.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Resolution. The City Council hereby directs City staff to initiate
the formal process to amend the City’s Land Use and Development Code regarding
accessory dwelling units in the South Mountain area as designated on the map
attached hereto as Exhibit 1. City staff is directed to address parking issues and other
negative effects on the neighborhoods in the South Mountain area caused by the
proliferation of internal and detached accessory dwelling units and propose, for the
City Council’s consideration, solutions to these issues.

     Section 2. Effective Date.           This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THE 6TH DAY OF DECEMBER 2023.
