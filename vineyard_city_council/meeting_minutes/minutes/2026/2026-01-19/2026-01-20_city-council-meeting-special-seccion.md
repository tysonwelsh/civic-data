                • Opportunities to better involve faith-based and non-profit organizations in

5.    BUSINESS ITEMS
      5.1. 300 West Parking Updates Resolution 2026-03
           DISCUSSION AND ACTION: The Council will discuss existing no-parking and
           overnight parking restrictions along the full length of 300 West, including Vineyard
           Loop Road and South 300 West, and consideration of modifying or removing current
           signage to allow general on-street parking where capacity exists.

     Councilmember Wood presented the proposed changes.

     The council discussed the proposed changes and expressed their support for the resolution.

     Chief Deputy Holden Rockwell explained the current model regarding parking enforcement
and asked for a clarification on what the changes would mean for his department from an
enforcement perspective. A discussion ensued involving both Council and city staff. The result
of the discussion was that the resolution would be amended to include protection for emergency
parking as well as continued pool access for residents.

      5.2. Overnight Parking Permit Area at 100 West and 470 North (The Springs).
           Resolution 2026-02
           DISCUSSION AND ACTION: Discussion of the existing overnight parking permit
           and restricted parking designation at 100 West and 470 North in The Springs
           neighborhood, including consideration of modifying or removing the current permit
           area and associated signage. The council may act to adopt (or deny) the resolution.

     Councilmember Wood presented the proposed resolution.

     The council along with staff discussed the proposed changes. This included a discussion
around seeking input from the neighborhood the proposed changes would affect before a vote to
adopt any changes. Councilmember Wood offered to circulate a survey of those residents to
better inform the council.

   Motion: COUNCILMEMBER NAIR MOVED TO CONTINUE THE ITEM UNTIL
AFTER THE COUNCIL WAS ABLE TO SURVEY THE IMPACTED NEIGHBORHOOD.
COUNCILMEMBER LAURET SECONDED. COUNCILMEMBERS NAIR, LAURET,
WOOD, HOLDAWAY, MCCUMBER VOTED IN FAVOR. THE MOTION PASSED
UNANIMOUSLY.
