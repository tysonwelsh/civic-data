4.1 Request for approval of a special exception regarding setback requirements for the side
and rear yard at 5674 W Cottage Rose Cir in the R-1-15 (Residential) zone
Applicant: Pedro Martins
Acres: +0.39
File Number: P2022-106
Planner | Wright reviewed the request for a special exception to locate an accessory garage structure
on the pie shaped lot. The property qualified for a special exception due to the irregular lot shape. For
this size of lot, an accessory structure could be up to twenty-five feet high, but for every foot above
sixteen, one foot should be added to the existing three-foot setback. The applicant sought approval to
build up to twenty-five feet tall, which would normally need twelve-foot setbacks to meet City Code.
The applicant requested three feet setbacks at the rear and ten feet at the sides. He has consulted
with adjacent neighbors to determine the best placement location. Commissioner Fenn asked if the
earthen swell identified on the plat would affect the structure.
Applicant Martins responded the land was flat and there was no swell. The south side easement was
for the storm drain which exited onto the road. Utility easements would not be impacted by the
structure. Commissioner Fenn asked if drainage would be affected, the applicant responded it would
not.
Commissioners confirmed the front elevation faced the house/street. Commissioner Sickles offered the
opinion the structure was too high and should be moved away from the property line. Commissioner
Bradford agreed. Commissioners Rypien and Berbert were not concerned by the height, as the trail and
parking lot for Rosecrest park were separated by a fence. Commissioners debated the ideal size and

4.2 Consideration of a Conditional Use for a home daycare known as Bright Beginnings Child
Care located at 12549 S Pagano Lane in the R-2-10 (Residential) zone

Applicant: Astrid Arias

Acres: +0.15

File Number: C2022-099
Planner | Wright reviewed the request for a Home Occupation Daycare known as Bright Beginnings
Child Care. The daycare would be operated in the basement of the applicant’s residence. Care would be
provided for up to 12 children between the hours of 8:00 am and 4:00 pm, Monday through Friday. The
applicant had a fully fenced rear yard for daily outdoor activities. The resident had one car, which
allowed the second and third garage spaces for additional employees, should they hire some. The
driveway allowed space for drop off and pickup. It was an existing daycare, has been operating at the
home, and requested approval per the new standards to allow for 12 children and additional employees.
A comment alleged the applicant did not live there and was operating a commercial business at the
home. The applicant told staff they lived onsite. The home daycare would require HOA approval.
Applicant Astrid Arias stated she moved from South Jordan and she and her husband would be running
the business. They rent the home and have received approval from the owners to operate the home day
care. They had no plans to hire employees and stated they had no neighborhood issues.
Commissioner Rypien moved to approve item 4.2 Conditional use for a home daycare known as Bright
Beginnings Child Care located at 12349 S Pagano Lane in the R-2-10 (Residential) zone with the following
recommendations:
1. Comply with recommendations from other agencies, i.e. Fire Department, Health Department.
2. Subject to a review upon complaint.
3. Signage is limited to 3 square feet attached to and parallel to the dwelling.
4. Obtain a business license.

4.4 Consideration of a Conditional Use for Studio DeBroux at 14267 S Maple Run Circle in
the R-1-15 Residential zone
Applicant: Lindsey DeBroux
Acres: +0.18
File Number: C2022-111
Planner | Wright reviewed the request for home group Instruction to teach piano and vocal lessons to
groups of no more than 12 students. The applicant anticipated two to four students per group with no
more than two sessions per day, and sessions ending no later than 5:00 p.m. Notices were mailed to
neighboring properties within three hundred feet. Concerns were expressed regarding potential traffic
issues. The home included a driveway and R.V. pad for parking and drop-off. Per the applicant many
students lived nearby and would walk to lessons, which would mitigate parking issues.
Applicant Lindsey DeBroux confirmed classes would be small groups singing and/or playing the piano.
Recitals would take place at a hall in a neighboring city and would not be onsite.
Commissioner Jacobson moved to approve item 4.4 Consideration us of a Conditional Use for Studio
DeBroux at 14267 S Maple Run Circle in the R-1-15 Residential zone with staff recommendations:
1. Comply with recommendations from other agencies, i.e. Fire Department, Health
Department.
2. Subject to a review upon complaint.
3. Signage is limited to 3 square feet attached to and parallel to the dwelling.
4. Obtain a business license.
5. No on-street parking is allowed.
6. The instructor shall be a bona fide resident of the premises.
7. The number of classes shall be limited to two (2) sessions per day. The sessions must be
separated by at least thirty (50) minutes to allow adequate time for pick-up and drop-off.
8. Maximum of twelve (12) students allowed per session.
9. Home occupations involving pedestrian or vehicle traffic shall be conducted only
between the hours of seven o'clock (7:00) A.M. and seven o'clock (7:00) P.M.
