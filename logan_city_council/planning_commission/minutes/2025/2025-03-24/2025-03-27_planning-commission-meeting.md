PC 25-018 160 W STR [Conditional Use Permit] Nathan Larsen/Kenneth Stepp, authorized
agent/owner is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to operate a two-bedroom short-term rental
within the main floor of the home located at 160 West 600 South in the Traditional Neighborhood
Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 02-062-0012 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
    available in the Community Development Department.
2. If the owner-occupied CUP grantee changes their primary place of residence, the Conditional
    Use Permit (CUP) and business license may be revoked.

    occupying the main floor unit of a grandfathered duplex.

5. A minimum of three (3) parking stalls (2 for owner and 1 for STR) is required for the STR. All
    parking areas shall be located off-street, outside the front setback, and paved with an approved
    hard surface and striped and shall meet minimum LDC dimensions (9’x18’) per stall.
6. A short-term rental may not be the subject of multiple rental contracts by separate individuals,
    groups or parties for the same night or nights.

    STR shall be legally parked on-site and are not permitted to be parked on-street.
8. Rental contracts for the STR shall be for 30 days or less.
9. Rental contracts shall not be offered to the same individual, group, or party for more than two
    consecutive rental contracts in a six (6) month period. The owner shall maintain rental records
    available for review and inspection by Logan City.
10. Quiet times shall comply with the City noise ordinance of 10 pm to 8 am.
11. A Logan City building/fire inspection is required prior to use of the home as a Short-Term Rental.
    These inspections may result in building remodeling and building permits prior to the issuance
    of a business license.


12. A Logan City business license is required before an STR can be used.
13. The STR shall comply with local and state Building, Health, Fire, Safety, Maintenance and
    Nuisance Codes prior to operating.
14. The STR is subject to the City’s nuisance ordinances regarding noise, garbage, parking,
    occupancy, etc., and more than three (3) violations within a calendar year may result in the
    revocation of the short-term rental’s conditional use permit and business license.
15. The number of household pets is limited to the number allowed for a single-family home.
16. Exterior lighting shall be concealed source, down-cast and reviewed and approved prior to the
    issuance of a building permit and shall comply with current LDC regulations.
17. No signs are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All signage shall be approved and
    permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.
18. No fences are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All fences shall be approved and
    permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1. The proposed project conforms to the requirements of the Land Development Code.
2. This STR meets the City’s STR density and location parameters as specified in Chapter
   17.37.130.
3. As conditioned, the use is compatible with the surrounding land uses and will not interfere with
   the use and enjoyment of adjoining or area properties.
4. The streets providing access and other infrastructure to the subject property have adequate
   capacities and level of service for the conditional use.
5. The Planning Commission has substantiated the findings for a Conditional Use Permit in Chapter
   17.42.050.

PC 25-025 West Pointe Down Zone [Zone Change] Nathan Welker, property owner, is requesting
a zone change of the West Pointe Subdivision from Mixed Residential Medium (MR-20) to Mixed
Residential Low (MR-12), Located at 310 West 1400 North; TIN 05-134-0001; -0002; -0003; -0004;
-0005 (Bridger Neighborhood).

Staff: Aimee Egbert, City Planner, reviewed the proposal to rezone the West Point Subdivision
located at 310 West 1400 North. A. Egbert explained that in 2022 the property was rezoned from
Mixed Residential Low (MR-12) to Mixed Residential Medium (MR-20) to change the ownership
structure from condominium, where only the buildings are individually owned to a subdivision, where
an owner owns both the building and the land underneath. The rezoning was required to proceed

with the subdivision because the property was non-conforming due to the property exceeding current
allowable density. When the rezone was approved by the Municipal Council in 2022, a deed
restriction was recorded stating the property would not increase the density and that the owner would
rezone the property back to MR-12 once the final subdivision plat was recorded. The final plat was
recorded in June 2023, and the applicant is now applying for the rezone back to MR-12 as agreed
upon in the deed restriction.

Proponent: No comment was made by the proponent.

Public: No public comment was made

MOTION: Commissioner Heare made a motion to forward a recommendation of approval to the
Municipal Council on a Zone Change for PC 25-025 West Pointe Down Zone with the findings for
approval as listed below. Commissioner Guth seconded the motion. The motion was approved
unanimously.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as MR which is consistent with this request.
2. The area is contiguous with other MR-12 zoned properties on the same block and along
   collector streets and corridors as prescribed in the LDC.
3. The downzone to MR-12 will prevent the sell and redevelopment of this property into new multi-
   family development, which would be out of character for this block.
4. The development standards in the LDC for MR-12 zoning will ensure compatibility with
   properties located in the area.

PC 25-005 Naylor Construction -Continued from the February 27th meeting. [Conditional Use
Permit] Derek Naylor/Twin Peaks Construction LLC, authorized agent/owner, is requesting a
Conditional Use Permit for outdoor storage of equipment and stockpiles of aggregate located at 1302
West 600 North in the Commercial services (CS) zone; TIN 05-061-0004 (Ellis Neighborhood).

Staff: Russ Holley, Logan City Planner, reviewed the request for a Conditional Use Permit to operate
a construction business with outdoor storage located at 1302 West 600 North in the Commercial
Services (CS) zone. R. Holley explained that there are existing wetlands on the property that have
been delineated and submitted to the Army Corp of Engineers for review. The Conditional Use Permit
can be approved and conditioned based on the delineation, but if the Army Corp of Engineers makes
significant changes to the delineation, the applicant will have to return to the Planning Commission
for a new approval.

Until the Army Corp of Engineers approves wetland boundaries, the submitted wetland delineation
shall be used to determine wetland setbacks, and all outdoor material, equipment storage, and drive
isles shall be outside of the 50-foot wetland setback. All wetland setback areas must be landscaped
and planted/seeded with live plant materials.


Commissioner Peterson asked if there is a building associated with this business and if restrooms
are required. R. Holley stated that there is no building on the property and restrooms are not required
because there will be no customer traffic.


Proponent: Derek Naylor asked the Commission if they had any questions. Commissioner Doutre
asked the proponent if he will be able to meet the setbacks and landscaping requirements. Mr.
Naylor stated that he can meet all of the conditions.

Public: No public comment was made.

The proposed site plan shows a new parking area to the south and rear of the building with a drive-

In the TC-1 zone, buildings can be a maximum of 5 feet from the sidewalk, and 75% of the building
mass has to be frontage along Main Street and 400 North. The angled building meets the setback
requirement on the corner points of the building.

The proposed plans show exterior trellis-like features that extend to the east and south of the building,
creating gateways around the sidewalk entrances to meet the frontage requirement. If the trellises
are considered to be a part of the building, the frontage equals 75% on both streets. If the trellis

short of the minimum requirement.

stories. The proposal is for a 30-foot-tall one-story building. The Commission will need to determine
if the proposed 30-foot tall building that looks like two stories from the exterior meets the two-story
requirement.

Commission needs to determine if a drive-up ATM is still considered a drive-thru.
