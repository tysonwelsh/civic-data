                      SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND RELEASE

       This Settlement Agreement and Release (“Agreement”) is dated June ___, 2020, and is
between the Unified Police Department of Greater Salt Lake (“UPD”) and the City of Riverton.
(“Riverton”).

                                          RECITALS

       A.     UPD is an interlocal entity established by that certain Cooperative Agreement by
Public Agencies to Create a New Entity, to Consolidate Law Enforcement Service, and to Provide
Rules of Governance, dated on or about September 3, 2009 (the “Interlocal Agreement”).

       B.    Riverton was an original member of UPD but provided notice of its intent to
withdraw from UPD on or about July 19, 2018.

        C.      The Interlocal Agreement provides for an allocation of UPD assets and liabilities
upon the withdrawal of a member, and a dispute arose between Riverton and UPD over the proper
allocation of those assets and liabilities.

       D.      Riverton and UPD are parties to a lawsuit, Civil No. 190904012 (the “Lawsuit”),
concerning claims arising from the Interlocal Agreement, Riverton’s withdrawal from UPD, and
the proper allocation of UPD assets and liabilities pursuant to that withdrawal.

         E.      Riverton and UPD participated in a mediation on May 4, 2020, and now desire to
settle their dispute and dismiss the Lawsuit.

       F.      The UPD Board finds that entering into this Agreement is in the best interests of
UPD.

                                        AGREEMENT

Accordingly, the parties hereby agree as follows:

   1. Payment. UPD will pay Riverton a total settlement amount of $150,000.00 (the
      “Payment”). UPD’s Payment, in conjunction with the transfer of assets set forth below,
      will be for full and final resolution of this matter. UPD will make the Payment by no later
      than June 30, 2020. Payment will be made in the following manner: Payment will be made
      pursuant to wiring instructions provided to the UPD by Riverton.

   2. Transfer of Assets. (a) As soon as reasonably practicable following the execution of this
      Agreement, UPD shall transfer all of its right, title, interest, in, and possession of, the
      following items of personal property to Riverton:

            a. One (1) Faro brand laser surveying device, purchased by the UPD in or after the
               year 2013;




                                                1
       b. One (1) 2020 Harley Davidson, series FLHTP, motorcycle (not police equipped).

3. Contingent Liability for Special Assessment from URS for Net Pension Liability. At any
   time in the next five years following the date of this Agreement, should the UPD be subject
   to an extraordinary assessment or payment obligation from Utah Retirement Systems
   (“URS”) seeking payment of all or a portion of a GASB 68 net pension liability reported
   on the UPD’s financial statements from URS for the Pensions (defined below) which
   accrued during the time Riverton was a member of the UPD, then Riverton will pay 7.8%
   of such extraordinary assessment or payment obligation upon same terms that the UPD will
   pay its portion of such assessment or payment obligation. Regardless of the form or amount
   of said extraordinary assessment or payment obligation, Riverton shall not under any
   circumstance be obligated to make any payments to URS in performance of this Agreement
   following the expiration of five years from the date of this Agreement. To be clear, this
   extraordinary GASB 68 net pension liability assessment or payment obligation does not
   include the UPD’s regular pension funding requirements including any of those regular
   funding requirements derived from the UAAL, as that term is defined by URS and used in
   calculating regular pension funding requirements, regardless of whether those funding
   requirements increase or decrease in future years.

   The term “Pensions” means: (i) Tier 1 Noncontributory System; (2) Tier 1 Public Safety
   System; (3) Tier 2 Contributory System; and (3) Tier 2 Public Safety System.

4. Mutual Release. Riverton and UPD do hereby release and forever discharge each other,
   and each of their respective officers, employees, and agents, (collectively, the
   “Releasees”), from any and all existing or possible actions, causes of action, claims,
   demands, damages, costs, fees and expenses of any kind, on account of, or in any way
   arising from or related to the Interlocal Agreement, the withdrawal of Riverton from UPD,
   the allocation of assets and liabilities pursuant to that withdrawal, and the facts,
   transactions, or occurrences that were raised, or could have been raised, in the Lawsuit.
   Promptly following the execution of this Agreement, Riverton and UPD shall file a
   stipulated joint dismissal of the Lawsuit.

5. No Admission. The parties acknowledge and agree that this settlement is the compromise
   of doubtful and disputed claims, and no action made pursuant to this Agreement is to be
   construed as an admission of liability on the part of either party or any of the Releasees, by
   whom liability is expressly denied.

6. Costs and Fees. Riverton and UPD shall each pay its own attorney fees, costs, and other
   expenses incurred in connection with the Lawsuit and the underlying dispute regarding
   Riverton’s withdrawal from UPD. Riverton and UPD shall each pay one-half of the costs
   charged by the mediator.

7. Effectiveness. This Agreement will take effect upon execution by the authorized officers
   of UPD and Riverton, following approval by the UPD Board and Riverton City Council.
