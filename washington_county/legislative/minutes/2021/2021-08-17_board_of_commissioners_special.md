WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 17, 2021

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Almquist at 2:00 p.m. on August 17, 2021, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner
Adam Snow, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Others in attendance included: Jessica Kinross, Gary Bezzant and Cameron Rognan

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION __AND PERIODIC REVIEW __OF COUNTY RESOURCE
MANAGEMENT PLAN (RMP)

The sections that were reviewed included: Predator Control, Threatened and Endangered
Species, and Wildlife.

Each section was reviewed line by line and county specific revisions were suggested.
Cameron and Jessica will send the suggested wording and content updates for red lining.

Updates to the RMP will be brought before the Planning Commission for review and then
to the County Commission for final approval.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
3:00 p.m.
