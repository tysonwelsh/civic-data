2. PUBLIC HEARING
   a. Final Budget for the City of West Jordan Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year 2022
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck explained that the Board adopted the tentative
budget for FY 2022 on May 26, 2021. Revenues come from property tax in areas considered blighted.
An RDA is adopted over those areas to develop and re-incentivize that area. Some improvements
were streetscapes and water storage tanks to revitalize and spur economic development. The
purpose of the hearing was to receive public comment and the final adoption would take place on
June 23, 2021.

Chair Jacob opened a public hearing at 7:57 pm

None

   b. Amending the bylaws of the Redevelopment Agency, designating a location for agency
      meetings, and authorizing electronic agency meetings
Economic Development Director Chris Pengra addressed board regarding the amendments to the
bylaws.

Board Member McConnehey noted the modification regarding the Chair and Vice Chair serving for
a one-year term. He requested that it coincide with the calendar year and other Council assignments.
He felt that the language stated that if someone were to resign and that person were Chair, the new
person would be put in for a new term or for a term of one year. He requested there be language
stating that they would fulfill the remainder of the existing term for the calendar year and reflect
that change for both the Chair and the Vice Chair.

MOTION:         Board Member Green moved to table Resolution No. 226 amending the bylaws
                of the Redevelopment Agency until June 23, 2021 to make the appropriate
                amendments as requested
                Board Member McConnehey seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
