10.        21-436                 Public Utilities Department presenting the City Council with the Water
                                  Conservation Plan for adoption
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Memo - WCP to City Council.pdf
                                  Summary of Final Edits Based on Comments Received - Sandy
                                  Water Conservation Plan 2021.pdf
                                  Resolution 21-44C_Sandy Water Conservation Plan 2021.pdf
                                  Exhibit A_Sandy City Conservation Plan FINAL.pdf
                                  Submitted Written Comments.pdf
                                  Resolution_21-44C_Signed.pdf

                                  Ms. Abi Holt, with Public Utilities, presented the Sandy City Water Conservation Plan
                                  2021 for Council consideration and action. She provided a summary of the public
                                  comments received. Ms. Holt gave an overview of the historic per capita water use,
                                  reviewed conservation goals and the milestones met. The City has exceeded the state's
                                  goals. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Public comment opened.

                                  Mr. Zach Frenkle provided a document to the Council and Administration. He spoke about
                                  the water conservation goal for Sandy City. He expressed concern that the conservation
                                  goal is set too low and suggested a 2% water reduction goal.

                                  Ms. Claire Geddes explained that we have a finite amount of water. Water is the new
                                  gold. The Salt Lake is very low and she spoke about the impact of the drought on the
                                  environment. She expressed a need for our City to be much more aggressive in water
                                  conservation.

                                  Ms. Mary Beth Whittiker expressed concern about the Great Salt Lake, which she feels
                                  is in the process of dying. She would like all municipalities to increase their conservation
                                  efforts to reduce water usage and work together to save the Great Salt Lake.

                                  Ms. Shelly Filgo wants the city to take this drought seriously. She expressed a need to
                                  raise the the city's water conservation efforts to 2%.

                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Abi Holt spoke about the conservation efforts by the city and ongoing education. She
                                  urged all residents to conserve water.

                                  Tom Ward, Public Utilities Director, responded to Council questions and comments.
                                  Council and staff stressed that education and informed residents regarding water
                                  conservation is integral to the success of water conservation.

                                  Following the vote on the motion, Council recessed the meeting at 8:04 pm.
                                  Council reconvened the meeting at 8:10 pm.
                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Brooke Christensen, to
                                  adopt Resolution 21-44c, a resolution approving and adopting the 2021 Sandy
                                  City Water Conservation Plan..The motion carried by the following vote:
