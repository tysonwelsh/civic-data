CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION _NO._R-2020-2591,_ A_ RESOLUTION
APPROVING EXTENDED USE OF APPROPRIATED FUNDS COLLECTED
FROM THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS SALES TAX

Chairman Iverson explained that the deadline for use of grants awarded to cultural entities
for the 2020 disbursement cycle is being recommended for extension. This will allow for
entities affected by COVID-19 to utilize the funding at a later time as many cultural related
events have been cancelled or postponed due to the social distancing guidelines set forth
from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2591, A
Resolution Approving Extended Use of Appropriated Funds Collected
From the Recreation, Arts, and Parks Sales Tax. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF ADOPTION OF PUBLIC WORKS REPORT

Mr. Whitehead explained that a list of chip seal projects will be presented at a future
meeting. The Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) will be working on a
drainage project in the Santa Clara Wash area, as well as a project in the Gypsum Wash.

The Shooting Sports Park is currently working on the reconfiguration of the practical pistol
range.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Commissioner Almquist cautioned citizens to be mindful when recreating in the Smith
Mesa Road area as much of the land is private property.

providing essential services to the public during the COVID-19 pandemic and recognized
Commissioner Cox for his efforts to be present as well.
