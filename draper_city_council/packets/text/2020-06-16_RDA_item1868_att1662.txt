Business Meeting

1.0      Call to Order: Board Chair - Troy K. Walker

2.0      Motion: Approval of April 7, 2020, Redevelopment Agency Meeting Minutes

2.1      Boardmember Mike Green moved to approve the April 7, 2020, Redevelopment
         Agency Meeting Minutes. Boardmember Tasha Lowery seconded the motion.

2.2      A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts, and
         Vawdrey voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.0      Action Item: Resolution #RDA 20-02, approval of Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Tentative
         Budget and setting the public hearing date of June 2, 2020 to receive public comment on the
         Tentative Budget.
3.1   Bob Wylie, Finance Director, said that in the RDA budget 2021 was a balanced budget
      totaling $11,295,550. He said that the revenue source within the RDA was a tax increment
      that comes from the project areas. He said that there were no full-time employees (FTEs)
      within the RDA. The property tax was the majority revenue source. In the project areas
      West Freeway was an expired project area that still had a fund balance that would be
      transferred into the Capital Improvements Projects fund for two capital projects. The Sand
      Hills project had some development agreements, but staff were also transferring from this
      project into the CIP projects within the area. In regards to the Crescent project, the majority
      of the funds were a bond for the developer. The last large and active project was the
      Frontrunner project area with most of the expenditures for the project being developer
      agreement paybacks.

3.2   Boardmember Fred Lowry moved to approve Resolution #RDA 20-20, approval of
      the Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Tentative Budget. Boardmember Mike Green seconded
      the motion.

3.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts, and
      Vawdrey voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

      Boardmember Tasha Lowery seconded the motion.

4.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts, and
      Vawdrey voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.
