ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS

Application #: ZT-5-2024
Applicant:     West Valley City
Request:       An ordinance text change to amend Section 11-5-104 of the Sign Ordinance
               to allow monument signs for certain residential developments.

City staff are proposing an ordinance text change to amend Section 11-5-104 of the Sign
Ordinance to allow monument signs for certain residential developments.

The first change relates to monument signs for mobile home parks in the RMH zone. The City’s
Sign Ordinance currently prohibits monument signs for mobile home parks in the RMH
(Residential Mobile Home) zone. However, most mobile home parks in the RMH zone already
have a monument sign that was constructed under a different set of sign regulations. The table
below lists mobile home parks within the RMH zone that already have a monument sign.
        Name of Mobile Home Park                                   Address
Meadowbrook                                      1240 W Hummingbird St
Silo Farms                                       3655 S 1300 W
Riverside                                        1232 W Rock River Rd
Byde-A-Wyle                                      3455 S Byde A Wyle St
American Heritage                                3040 S Homecrest St
Windsor Estates                                  2800 Hampton Dr
Westcrest                                        3388 S Eastcrest Rd

This first change was prompted by the owners of the Town and Country mobile home park
located at 3751 S Redwood Rd. This mobile home park had a pole sign that was removed. The
owners would like to install a monument sign; however, the current sign code prohibits
monument signs for mobile home parks in the RMH zone.

Staff is recommending that monument signs be allowed for mobile home parks in the RMH zone
for the following reasons:
     most mobile home parks in the RMH zone already have a monument sign,
     monument signs emphasize the entrance of the community and help create community
         identity, and
     in the case of the Town and Country mobile home park, it is located on Redwood Road
         where monument signs are common.

The second change relates to monument signs for multi-family residential developments located
in zones other than the RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) zone. The City’s Sign Ordinance
currently allows monument signs for “multiple-unit dwellings” in the RM zone only. However,
there are several multi-family residential developments built in zones other than the RM zone that
already have a monument sign that was constructed under a different set of sign regulations. The
table below lists multi-family developments within zones other than RM that already have a
monument sign:

  Name of Development                 Address                    Zone              # of Units
Gearld L. Wright Villa         3375 W 3650 S              R-4                                79
Oxford Court                   3275 W 3650 S              R-2-6.5                            42
Willow Park                    3350 W 3800 S              R-4                                88
Reunion Woods                  3639 S 5600 W              R-1-8                              24
Granger                        3344 S 4000 W              R-2-8                              24
Kelly Benson                   3122 S 3600 W              RB                                 59
Harmony Gardens                3521 W 3100 S              R-1-8                              96
Compass Villa                  1466 W 3500 S              RB                                 55
Lakeside Village               1580 W Parliament Ave      R-1-8                             144

Staff is recommending that monument signs be allowed for residential developments with at least
two dwelling units per building, with at least 20 total dwelling units, and in zones other than the
RM zone for the following reasons:
     such developments already have a monument sign,
      monument signs help create community identity,
      the minimum of 20 total units prohibits monument signs for individual duplexes and
       fourplexes, and
      such signs are currently nonconforming and, as such, the code is unclear as to what
       standards apply when alterations are desired. This change solves this issue.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. The ordinance should be approved as proposed by staff.
   2. Continuance.
   3. Denial.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS

Application #:         C-8-2024
Applicant:             JM Seafood
Address:               3394 West 3500 South
Property Size:         C2 Zone (.42 Acres)

The applicant, JM Seafood, is requesting conditional use approval for a Grocery Store. This
property is zoned C2, General Commercial, and the West Valley City General Plan designates
this area as General Commercial. The surrounding zone is RM (Multi Unit Dwelling Residential)
to the North, and C2 (General Commercial) on all other sides. The residential Rosewood Gardens
Condos are to the north, O’Reily Auto Parts is to the west, a retail strip mall is to the east and
Panda Buffet is to the south across 3500 South.
This property is currently occupied by JM Seafood and operates as a Neighborhood Grocery
Store. At this time, they would like to expand the original building footprint (2,370 SF) to add
additional warehouse and freezer space (3,536 SF). This increases the total floor area to 5,915 SF
and is therefore now considered a Grocery Store by definition in the West Valley City Municipal
Code. The uses inside the building will include a retail sales area (3,040 SF) and warehouse
(2,592 SF). The C2 zone does not allow warehouse uses to exceed 50% of the square footage of
the buildings area. Due to the type of inventory, which is primarily frozen seafood products, there
are also several large freezers located in the retail section of the store. Delivery services are also
provided with two van type vehicles. These will remain on site and will be housed in the new
addition and not stored outside.

The building addition will have a stucco façade along the visible portion (east side) as well as the
north, and a metal façade on the west, which is not visible to the adjacent business. The addition
is over 50% of the original floor area and is therefore subject to the Commercial Design Standards
in the City Code and will be reviewed as a Secondary Façade.

The typical hours of operation for this use are 6:00 am – 6:00 pm, 7 days a week. There will be
four to six employees and approximately 12 parking spaces provided (7 required).

The site is adjacent to residential on the north side so the setbacks (10’), landscaping, and
masonry screening wall will also be completed. The existing vinyl fencing will remain along the
east side, which is the access for the residential development. The landscaping in this area will be
improved with trees and live plant material. The frontage of this property is along 3500 South,
which is a High Image Arterial. Currently due to the building location there is no landscaping
along that frontage. With this addition the east side of the parking must be setback 20’ and that
entrance area must be landscaped to comply with the 7-10 standards of the City Code. Also, 3500
South is a UDOT facility, and they have requested that the parking be setback as well.

The WVC Fire Department is currently reviewing the access plan and will provide requirements
as part of the plan review process for this development.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing, as well

as the following conditions:

       1. The use on site is approved for a Grocery Store and the Warehouse use shall
       not exceed 50% of the total floor area.

       2. There shall be no outside storage and no more than 2 delivery vans on site.

       3. The addition is subject to the Secondary Façade requirements in the
       Commercial Design Standards and must be architecturally integrated with the
       existing building.

       4. The site shall be improved and landscaped per the approved plans including
       landscaping along the east frontage to comply with the 7-10 standards of the
       WVC Municipal Code.

       5. The north boundary of the site shall be screened with a 6’ masonry wall.

       6. Must meet requirements of all affected departments and agencies including
       but not limited to UDOT and the WVC Fire Department.

       7. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing or to allow for
a revised site/landscape plans to be submitted.

Application #:         CA-3-2024
Applicant:             Granger Hunter Improvement District (GHID)
Address:               1629 West 2320 South
Property Size:         RM zone, 1.31 acres

The applicant, GHID, is requesting a conditional use amendment for an expansion of facilities at

the existing Anderson Well site at 1629 West 2320 South. The zoning for this property is RM,
Multi Unit Dwelling Residential, and the General Plan designation is Utility. The surrounding
zones are A, Agricultural to the west, R-1-8 (Single Unit Residential) to the east, C2, General
Commercial to the north and RM to the south. The surrounding uses include detached single unit
residential to the west, the Red Oaks Village PUD (ROV) to the south, Bank of Utah and vacant
land to the north and the Center Pointe Hollow PUD to the east.

This property was originally approved as a well site in 2002 (C-14-2002). It was developed in
coordination with the ROV PUD (PUD-1-2003) as shared open space. At this time GHID would
like to expand their site for a treatment plant and reconfigure the open space and parking areas for
Red Oaks.

As part of the initial review prior to submitting the formal application, GHID did an analysis of
the ROV open space and confirmed that are still in compliance with the 50% open space
requirement with this requested modification. This also includes a requirement that the existing
tot lot remain and be relocated as outlined in this submittal.
The new structure that is being built will match the existing structure in height and materials. The
fencing will remain as 6’ tall wrought iron and will enclose the entire area including the
expansion. The existing vinyl fence along the west side will remain.

Landscaping will be updated and enhanced including the 20’ setback along 2320 South. Staff also
recommends that the landscaping along the south side of the facility, adjacent to the new ROV
parking, be completed per 7-9 with a 5’ landscape strip and trees.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval subject to any issues raised at the public hearing as well as the following conditions:

1. The site shall be improved and landscaped per the approved plans including the following:
       a) Landscaping along 2320 South with 1 tree every 30’ and 50% live plant material
           coverage in the 20’ setback.
       b) A 5’ landscape strip along the south edge of the site adjacent to the extended parking
           area with 1 tree every 30’ and 50% live plant material coverage.
2. The building must be completed per the approved plans and be designed to compliment the
   existing building.
3. A tot lot for Red Oaks Village must be provided as shown on the submitted plans.
4. Must meet requirements of all affected departments and agencies, including but not limited to
   WVC Public Works.
5. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing.

Application #: CA-2-2024
Applicant:     Morelia Building II 4936 W 3500 S
Property Size: C-2 Zone (2.47 acres)

The applicant, Utah Construction Specialists, is requesting approval for a conditional use amendment
for a new building within a shopping center. The project is located at 4936 W 3500 S. The property is
zoned C-2, General Commercial, and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as
General Commercial. Zoning to the north, east, and west is C-2, while parcels to the south are zoned
R-1-8. The surrounding uses include a credit union to the west, charter school to the north, and a
vacant commercial building, as well as the former Shopko building, parking lot, and restaurant pad
sites to the east.

The applicant is proposing to construct a two-story, 3,700 sq. ft. building on the property that was
approved for a shopping center in 2021 (ZC 20210010). The original approval for the project included
a roughly 12,000 sq. ft. multi-tenant retail and restaurant building. This building was completed
earlier this year and is starting to be occupied by tenants. The current proposal is to remove 9 parking
stalls and landscaping in the north-west corner of the site to accommodate the new building.

At this time, the applicant has not indicated what types of businesses will occupy the new building. It
will be permitted as a building shell only, and the interior spaces will be reviewed later with tenant
improvement permits. Exterior materials for the new building will be EIFS, brick and metal siding,
which will match the materials on the existing building on the property. The south elevation is a
primary façade, and the east and west elevations are secondary facades. All facades shall comply with
the Commercial Design Standards outlined in 7-11-300P, which means that some modifications will
need to be made to the elevations from the plans submitted with the initial application.

The existing access points and parking lot circulation on the site will not change. Nine parking stalls
will be eliminated for the new building. Based strictly on retail and office uses going in the new
building, 11 parking stalls are required. The parcel extends quite far to the east into some existing
paved areas. This proposal has 22 more parking stalls added on the east side of the site to
accommodate parking for uses in both buildings. That brings the total parking stall count to 69 stalls
for the site. The 22 additional spaces on the east side will work, so long as a pedestrian connection is
provided across the site to the west side of the existing building. Overall parking requirements for the
site will be monitored as tenant improvement permits come in for review.

Fifteen percent landscaping is required on the site, and approximately 22% landscaping will remain
with the addition of the new building. Most of the landscaping was installed and completed with the
first phase of the project. Landscaping will be added or modified around the new building.

All signage on the property shall comply with the regulations in Title 11.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:
1. The site shall be constructed per the approved plan.
2. The building shall meet all the Commercial Design Standards outlined in 7-11-300P of the West
Valley City Code, with the south elevation being a primary façade and the east and west elevations
being secondary facades.
3. Parking on the property shall comply with all regulations found in 7-9 of the West Valley City
Code. The exact number of parking stalls required for both buildings will be evaluated and determined
as Tenant Improvement Permits are reviewed.
4. For the 22 additional parking stalls added on the east side of the property to be considered when
calculating minimum parking requirements on the site, a pedestrian connection must be provided to
the west side of the existing building.
5. There shall be at least 15% landscaping on the site, and the landscaping shall comply with all West
    Valley City Title 7 requirements.
6. Signage shall comply with Title 11 of the West Valley City Code.
7. Shall meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies.
8. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing.
