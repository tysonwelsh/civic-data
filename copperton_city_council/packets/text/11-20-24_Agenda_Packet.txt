        ORDINANCE NO. 2024-11-01                              DATE: November 20, 2024

AN ORDINANCE APPOINTING THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES
DISTRICT AS MUNICIPAL ENGINEER FOR THE TOWN OF COPPERTON

       WHEREAS, the Town of Copperton (“Copperton”) is a town class pursuant to Utah Code
§ 10-2-301(2)(f); and

     WHEREAS, Copperton is a member of the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(“MSD”), which provides administrative services to Copperton; and

      WHEREAS, since its incorporation in 2017, the MSD has contracted with Salt Lake
County to provide engineering services to Copperton and the MSD’s other municipal members;
and

       WHEREAS, the MSD has established an engineering department that includes many of
the engineers and staff from Salt Lake County’s engineering department that will provide
engineering services directly to the MSD’s municipal members, including Copperton; and

       WHEREAS, the MSD’s engineering department will become effective on January 1, 2024;
and

        WHEREAS, under Section 10-3-917 of the Utah Code, towns may by ordinance establish
the office of the municipal engineer; and

       WHEREAS, Section 17B-2a-1104(1)(b)(iv) of the Utah Code permits the MSD to provide
engineering services to its member entities; and

      WHEREAS, the Copperton Council desires to appoint the MSD to serve as
Copperton’s municipal engineer.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COPPERTON TOWN
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:

       1.      Effective January 1, 2025, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-917 and Utah Code
§ 17B-2a-1104(1)(b)(iv), the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District is hereby
appointed as the municipal engineer for the Town of Copperton to fulfill all applicable duties
and responsibilities set forth in the Utah Code, Copperton’s ordinances, and all other
applicable laws and regulations.

       2.      The Mayor and Copperton staff are authorized to take such steps as are
necessary to implement this ordinance.

                               PUBLIC NOTICE
                    TO FILL A MIDTERM VACANCY ON THE
                         COPPERTON TOWN COUNCIL

                            SALT LAKE COUNTY, UTAH

PUBLIC NOTICE is hereby given by the Copperton Town Council (“Council”) of a
midterm vacancy for Council Seat D, which is an at-large, elected position, due to the
resignation of Dave Olsen (the “Vacancy”), effective November 20, 2024.

Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-3-301 and 20A-1-510 the Council is seeking applicants
to fill the Vacancy. In accordance with Utah Code § 20A-9-203, qualified applicants must:
(1) be a United States citizen: (2) be a registered voter of Copperton; (3) maintain their
principal place of residence within Copperton, meaning that they have resided in Copperton
for twelve consecutive months before the date of the appointment; and (4) not be a
convicted felon, unless their right to hold elective office has been restored.

Persons interested in being considered for the Vacancy must submit a completed
declaration of candidacy, a resume, and statement of interest to the Copperton Clerk,
Nicole Smedley, via email at nsmedley@msd.utah.gov no later than 5:00 pm (MST) on
December 11, 2024. All documents submitted will become a public record subject to public
records requests. If you have questions about the Vacancy or the application process, please
contact Ms. Smedley via the above email address or at (385) 508-5944.

The Council will interview applicants and consider applications for the Vacancy during its
next regular meeting on December 18, 2024, at 6:30 PM, which will be open to the public
and held at the Bingham Canyon Lions Club, located at 8725 W. Hillcrest Street in
Copperton.

The person appointed to fill the Vacancy will serve the remainder of Councilmember
Olsen’s term, which will end on December 31, 2025. If the appointed person desires to
serve after that date, they will be required to file for candidacy in the 2025 general
municipal election.
