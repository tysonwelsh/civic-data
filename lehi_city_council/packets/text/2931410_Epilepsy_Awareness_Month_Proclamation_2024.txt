                                  Proclamation
                            EPILEPSY AWARENESS MONTH
                                  NOVEMBER 2024

Whereas, epilepsy is an incurable brain disorder that causes people to have recurring seizures;

Whereas, epilepsy is the third most common neurological condition, and one in twenty-six
people in the United States will develop epilepsy in his or her lifetime due to genetics, traumatic
brain injury, illness, unknown causes, and more;

Whereas, epilepsy afflicts 3.4 millions Americans, with nearly 34, 000 of them being Utahns.
Millions more are affected, such as families, caregivers, friends, teachers, and more.

Whereas, a widespread lack of understanding and knowledge about epilepsy often cause people,
particularly children and youth, to fear, isolate, stigmatize, and bully their peers who are afflicted
with epilepsy and other seizure disorders; and

Whereas, the city of Lehi joins ‘November 2024 as Epilepsy Awareness Month’, a global,
national, and state effort to increase awareness, educate residents of Lehi about epilepsy, and
lend support to people with epilepsy and their families in order to improve their quality of life.

Now, therefore, by proclamation of Mayor Mark Johnson and City Council members Paige
Albrecht, Chris Condie, Paul Hancock, Heather Newall, and Michelle Stallings.

Dated this 22th day of October, 2024
