Prayer, Pledge of Allegiance, and Introductions

                                  Council Chair Aaron Dekeyzer welcomed those in attendance.

Legislative Report

                                  Kim Bell, Deputy Mayor, provided an overview of the Legislative Session and reviewed
                                  many of the bills passed this session that will impact Sandy City.

General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                                  Council Chair Aaron Dekeyzer invited the public to participate in General Citizen
                                  Comment.

                                  Public comment opened.
                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren spoke about the proposed annexations to Sandy City and the

1.         25-101                 Parks and Recreation Department presenting an Interlocal Cooperation
                                  Agreement between Sandy City and Salt Lake County for TRCC Funding
                                  for Bicentennial Park
                   Attachments:   Memo Interlocal Agreements for TRCC Funding 2025

2.         25-099                 Economic Development Department Providing the City Council with
                                  Updates on Economic Development Projects and The Cairns Housing
                                  Transit Reinvestment Zone Proposal
                   Attachments:   April 2025 Council Update

                                  Kasey Dunlavy and Rob Sant, Economic Development, provided an update to the Council
                                  on Economic Development projects in Sandy City and the Cairns Housing Transit
                                  Reinvestment Zone proposal. Last year in Sandy City, there were over 700 new business
                                  licenses issued, 1,600 new jobs created, 3% unemployment and Sandy ranked in the top
                                  five cities for taxable sales of $3.3 billion. They provided project overviews of the following
                                  developments: The Compass located at 9295 S 255 W; Sandy Shulsen project located at
                                  10115 S Monroe Streeet; Centennial Village located at 215 W Sego Lily Drive; Redsky
                                  located at 10140 S Centennial Parkway; The Summit located at 10240 S Monroe Street;
                                  and the Utah Hockey Club located at 9835 S Monroe Street. They spoke about the
                                  Cairns Housing and Transit Reinvestment Zones (HRTZ), the key objectives and process.
                                  Council questions and comments followed.

Mayor's Report

                   Mayor Zoltanski congratulated all of the One Sandy Award recipients. She spoke about
                   the tour and beam signing event held at the Utah Hockey Club practice facility. The Fire
                   Department recently had their topping off ceremony for Fire Station 31. She attended a
                   kindness event at the University of Utah. She invited the community to attend the food
                   truck nights at the Sandy Amphitheater. She congratulated the Sandy Arts Guild and
                   Sandy Amphitheater on their Best of State Awards. She invited Chief Severson to speak
                   about an incident that occurred in Idaho involving an individual with autism.

                   Chief Severson spoke about the incident that occurred in Idaho. The Sandy Police
                   Department is well trained in identifying individuals with autism and in de-escalation
                   techniques. Each year the City and Police Department coordinate a community autism
                   event which was first brought to the City and sponsored by the City Council Office. It is a
                   family-friendly event and a great way to meet and interact with officers. This event will be
                   held in August.
