Unified Police Department (UPD) Proposed Budget Presentation

                Frank Nakamura, District Administrator, SLVLESA, stated he would like the
Council to consider approval of a 16.5 percent property tax increase. Compensation needs to be
raised for police officers to be able to retain and recruit new officers. The goal is to be in the top
5 agencies for compensation.


                                                  2
DATE           WEDNESDAY                          SEPTEMBER                          15, 2021


            Mayor Clayton stated truth-in-taxation would put the funds in next years budget.
What does UPD plan to do between now and then to retain officers.

              Mr. Nakamura stated because of the urgent situation truth-in-taxation would take
place in October or November.

              Chief Jason Ackerman stated all but three agencies have made significant
increases and of those three agencies, two of them will be making increases shortly.

               Council Member Stitzer asked if there is internal dialogue with other police
agencies to discuss wages or is the solution to raise taxes.

                Chief Ackerman stated the Governor talked to police Chief’s from various
agencies about a solution. Police agencies are competing for recruits from the same pool of
people that is unusually low.

              Mr. Nakamura stated there is a committee working to address compensation
issues through other avenues as well.

              Lisa Dudley, Chief Financial Officer, SLVLESA, reviewed the effects that a tax
increase would have on Copperton specifically, where budget cuts have been made, and the
shared services formula.

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution to consider approving a property
tax increase of 16.5% for fiscal year 2022:

RESOLUTION NO. 2021-09-01                                           Date: September 15, 2021

       A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING A DECISION, IF MADE BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTES OF THE
       SALT LAKE VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA (SLVLESA)
       FOLLOWING PROCESS REQUIRED BY UTAH LAW, TO INCREASE SLVLESA
       BUDGETED PROPRETY TAX REVENUES BY 16.5% FOR CALENDAR YEAR
       2022 WHICH WOULD RESULT IN THE LEVY OF A PROPERTY TAX RATE IN
       EXCESS OF THE CERTIFIED TAX RATE PURSUANT TO SECTION 17B-2a-903
       OF THE UTAH CODE

       WHEREAS, the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (“SLVLESA”) was
created by Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council dated August 18, 2009; and

        WHEREAS, the area of the Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is included in the
boundaries of SLVLESA for the funding and provision of law enforcement services to the areas
within the boundaries of SLVLESA; and

       WHEREAS, SLVLESA contracts with the Unified Police Department (the “UPD”) to
provide law enforcement services within its boundaries; and


                                              3
DATE            WEDNESDAY                              SEPTEMBER                            15, 2021


         WHEREAS, SLVLESA Board of Trustees, by a duly adopted Resolution dated August 19,
2021, stated its intent to increase its budgeted property tax revenues from prior year budgeted
property tax revenues by 16.5% or approximately $2,903,624 for calendar year 2022 which would
result in the levy of a property tax rate in excess of the certified rate as defined in Section 59-2-
924 of the Utah Code; and

        WHEREAS, a report dated August 19, 2021 (the “Report”), a copy of which is attached as
“Attachment 1,” was submitted to the Copperton Metro Township Council (the “Council”) pursuant
to section 17B-1-1003 of the Uta Code, providing the purposes for the increase in SLVLESA
budgeted property tax revenues; and

        WHEREAS, the purposes for the increase in SLVLESA budgeted property tax revenues
by 16.5% for calendar year 2022 as stated in the Report include the need to maintain the viability
of SLVLESA, increase compensation to be able to recruit and retain police officers, increase
staffing to meeting rising caseloads, pay for the increased costs of cars, equipment and supplies,
to ensure that the target reserves for SLVLESA are met, to hedge against future loss of revenues
and unexpected expenditures and generally to meet the costs of law enforcement services for
SLVLESA members; and

       WHEREAS, the Council allowed time during the duly noticed meeting on September 15,
2021 for comment on the proposed tax increase from members of the Council and the public; and

         WHEREAS, Section 17B-2a-903 of the Utah Code requires that before the SLVLESA
Board of Trustees can adopt a property tax levy for calendar year 2022 in excess of the certified
tax rate, it mush have prior approval of the property tax increase from the legislative bodies of any
municipality and county whose territory is located within SLVLESA or approval of a majority of the
legislative bodies of the municipalities and two-thirds (2/3) vote of the county legislative body; and

        WHEREAS, before the SLVLESA Board of Trustees considers levying a property tax rate
that exceeds the certified tax rate, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees shall hold a public hearing on
December 16, 2021, to provide to all interested persons the opportunity to be heard regarding the
proposed property tax increase; and

       WHEREAS, SLVLESA shall provide notices of the public hearing as required by Section
59-2-919 of the Utah Code; and

        WHEREAS, after considering all public input from the public hearings and all other
information available to them, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees will vote on whether to increase
its budgeted property tax revenues for calendar year 2022 by 16.5% that would result in the levy
of property tax rate that exceeds the certified tax rate.

    NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL as follows:

       1. The Copperton Metro Township Council hereby reaffirms its authorization of the
          SLVLESA Board of Trustees to impose property taxes on the areas within the


                                                  4
DATE           WEDNESDAY                           SEPTEMBER                           15, 2021

          boundaries of SLVLESA.

       2. Pursuant to Section 17B-2a-903 of the Utah Code, the Copperton Metro Township
          Council hereby approves a decision, if made by the SLVLESA Board of Trustees
          following the processes required by Utah law, to increase SLVLESA budgeted
          property tax revenues for calendar year 2022 in excess of prior year property tax
          revenues by 16.5% or approximately $2,903,624 and to levy a property tax rate in
          excess of the certified tax rate to generate the increase in its budgeted property tax
          revenues as defined in Section 59-2-924 of the Utah Code.

       3. This Resolution shall take effect immediately on passage and its publication on the
          Utah Public Notice Website pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §10-3-710(1).

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Salt Lake County, Utah, this 15th day of September, 2021.

Copperton Code Rewrite Update

                Nathan Bracken stated Title 3 of the Copperton Code covers financials,
purchasing policy and taxes. Since the code was written for Salt Lake County, this chapter will be
rewritten entirely to reflect Copperton’s policy. He provided examples of different municipality
codes.

from scratch.

easily accessible.
