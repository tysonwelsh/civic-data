Subdivision

 3.        6S23/SUB-000356-2023 – Tim and Misty Covington - Public Hearing and Consideration for a
           Subdivision Plat Amendment for the Property Located at 5758 South Canal Street, Taylorsville, Utah.
           (Terryne Bergeson/Planner 1)

3.1      Ms. Bergeson presented this item.          Tim and Misty Covington are requesting Planning Commission
         approval for a subdivision plat amendment to create Covington Hill Subdivision. The subdivision plat
         proposal will amend Lot 109 of the Kashmir Amended and Extended Subdivision by subdividing the property
         resulting in one flag lot.

         3.1.1     This proposal is here tonight because any change to a subdivision plat requires Planning
                   Commission approval. Because of the length of the staff portion of the flag lot being over that
                   allowed by Code, the Fire Marshall requires a turn around and “no parking” signs be posted along
                   the staff. Also being required is an additional fire hydrant being added.

         3.1.2     Mr. and Mrs. Covington completed a zoning amendment from R-1-40 to R-1-20 in preparation for
                   this subdivision and the City Council adopted Ordinance 23-08 to complete that. The existing lot
                   has enough square footage to be subdivided into two lots with a minimum area of 20,000 square
                   feet and a buildable area that meets all setback requirements for the Single-Family R-1-20 zone.
                   Both properties will remain with the “lot-density residential” designation in the City’s General Plan.

3.2      FINDINGS:
         3.2.1  This application was initiated by Tim and Misty Covington.
         3.2.2  The applicant is requesting to amend Lot #109 of the Kashmir Amended and Extended
                subdivision.
         3.2.4  The subject property is in the Single-Family Residential (R-1-20) zoning district.
         3.2.5  The proposed plat amendment complies with all subdivision and zoning standards in the City
                Code.
         3.2.6  The applicant has been working with Staff to address all City and Review Agency requirements.
         3.2.7  The Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office has reviewed the draft plat and provided comments.

         3.2.8     Property owners are responsible for securing the appropriate development code prior to any
                   construction activity.
         3.2.9     The Taylorsville Planning Commission is the decision-making body for subdivision amendment
                   requests.

3.3      CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:
         3.3.1  A storm water agreement shall be signed and recorded.
         3.3.2  The applicant shall continue working with City Staff and Review Agencies to address any
                outstanding review comments, prior to recording the plat.

3.4      APPLICANT ADDRESS: Tim and Misty Covington were on Zoom and thanked Staff for their presentation
         and said everything they had wanted to say had been covered.
