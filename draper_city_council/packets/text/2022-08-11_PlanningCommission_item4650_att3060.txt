BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.974
acres located at approximately 2019 East Graystone Ct. (Exhibit B & C). The property is
currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to
allow for the adjustment of the common property line between Lot 405 and Lot 406 and a
reduction in an open space easement.

Lot 406 has a home on it that was built in 1994. Lot 405 has a home that is partially
constructed on it. Phase 4 of the Pheasant Hills development was approved as a Planned
Unit Development (PUD) in 1993. At that time the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)
required PUD’s to provide open space. The Pheasant Hills development was approved with
20% overall open space, including 20% located within Phase 4. Open space areas that were

Lot 405 includes a 75-foot Home Owners Associations Easement and Draper City Open
Space Easement (Easement) along the north property line, with the easement to be
maintained by the owners association (Exhibit H). This area is a dry ravine that runs
through the subdivision. Twelve lots in the subdivision contain the easement along their
rear property lines.




Pheasant Hills Plat 4                                                 App. # SUBD-146-2022
Plat Amendment Request                          3
On August 2, 2022 the City Council approved a reduction in the width of the Easement from
75-feet to 40-feet on Lot 405. The Home Owners Association submitted a letter to the City
on May 25, 2022 in favor of the proposed reduction in easement width (Exhibit F). While
this reduction removes 2,945 square feet of area from the easement, it still maintains a
20% open space area within the Pheasant Hills development. It also allows the property
owners of Lot 405 to add a series of short retaining walls in the now available 35-feet of
area to handle some grading issues on the property. The proposed plat will formally reduce
the width of the easement to the approved 40-feet (Exhibit G).

The proposed plat will also adjust the common property line between Lots 405 and 406
and place 465 square feet of area within Lot 405.

Previous Conditions of Approval. The City Council placed the following conditions of approval
on the Open Space Easement Reduction on August 2, 2022:

    1. The location of the current toe slope of the current fill shall be considered the
       northern boundary of any future disturbance. All grading and construction shall be
       located south of this fill line.

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat
amendment request is found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the
standard of review for such requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing
        an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds
        that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
        way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application
based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff
report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

        1.      That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the
                site and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
        2.      That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
                development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
        3.      Provide a topographic map showing the current toe slope of the current fill
                prior to submitting a final mylar for review and signature.
        4.      This plat amendment does not modify the 50-foot Draper City Open Space
                and Flood Control Easement along Highland Dr. Any amendments to that
                easement will require a separate application.
        5.      Address the outstanding redlines in Exhibit A of this report prior to
                submitting a final mylar for review and signature.

The findings for approval as are follows:

        1.      There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street,
                right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

The findings for denial as are follows:

        1.      There is not good cause for the vacation or amendment.
