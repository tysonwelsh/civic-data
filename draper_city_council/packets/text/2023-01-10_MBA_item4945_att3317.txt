             MUNICPAL BUILDING AUTHORTY RESOLUTION NO. 23-01

       A RESOLUTION APPROVING A LEASE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE
       MUNICPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY OF DRAPER CITY AND NEW CINGULAR
       WIRELESS PCS, LLC FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A NEW
       COMMUNICATION FACILTY LOCATED AT 12375 S 550 W, DRAPER, UTAH.

        WHEREAS, the city has received a request from New Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC to
lease 1,600 square feet of Municipal Building Authority of Draper City property for the
construction, installation, and operation of a communications structure and associated equipment
to support a the new facility; and

       WHEREAS, there will be a significant improvement in the wireless service with the
addition of the wireless telecommunication facility; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has previously entered into a number of lease
agreements with cellular service providers throughout Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, the lease will provide for a continuous revenue stream; and

       WHEREAS, The Municipal Building Authority of Draper City has found the terms and
conditions thereof of the Memorandum of Lease Agreement (Exhibit “A”), acceptable to Draper
City.

       WHEREAS, The Municipal Building Authority of Draper City has found the terms and
conditions thereof of the Lease Agreement (Exhibit “B”), acceptable to Draper City.


    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MUNICIPAL BUIDLING
AUTHORITY OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       1. It does hereby approve the attached Lease Agreement between The Municipal Building
          Authority of Draper City and New Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC.
       2. The Honorable Troy K. Walker, Mayor of Draper City, is hereby authorized to execute
          the agreement on behalf of Draper City and to act in accordance with its terms.
       3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE MUNICIPAL BUIDLING AUTHROTY OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON THIS 10th DAY OF JANUARY, 2023.
