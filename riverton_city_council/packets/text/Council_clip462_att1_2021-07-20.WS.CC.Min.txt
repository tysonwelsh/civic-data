46            Amendments, Amendments Proposed by Riverton City.
47
     Page | 9 Minutes – City Council Meeting

 1   Jason Lethbridge said this is a public hearing for a Zoning Text Change proposed by Riverton
 2   City and the zone text change involves Section 18.225 Accessory Structures. He said the
 3   proposed amendments are primarily what staff would refer to as ‘housekeeping’ items, clarifying
 4   or correcting existing language and standards. He said one example of this in the proposed
 5   amendment is the current ordinance includes a prohibition on the use of storage or freight
 6   containers as accessory structures, but that language is located in the section relating to
 7   carports. He said this amendment relocates the language on storage containers to the General
 8   Requirements section.
 9   He said in addition to minor housekeeping changes the amendments consist of the following:
10   • The ordinance includes allowance for structures below 200 square feet to be located 1 foot
11   from property line. The amendment adds a limitation that the structure must also be single level
12   and below 16 feet in height, to minimize impacts to surrounding properties.
13   • Increase of side and rear setbacks to 15 feet where a second story is included.
14   • Clarification of requirements for unfinished storage areas under trusses, including limitation
15   to ladder access only.
16   • Clarification of height requirement and setback for agricultural structures, including minimum
17   15 foot setback where structure exceeds 20 feet
18   • Clarification of fencing and setback requirements for above ground pools, adding where such
19   pools have a capacity above 5,000 gallons fencing and setback standards apply.
20   • Addition of setback requirements for pool slides, diving boards, etc where such equipment
21   exceeds 36 inches in height.
22   • Deletion of maximum square footage limitation for internal accessory dwelling units, as Utah
23   State Code no prohibits such limitations.
24

27   ordinance.

11   Councilmember Stewart MOVED that the City Council direct staff to proceed with
12   development of final project boundaries, plans, budgets, including draft interlocal
13   agreements, and authorizing coordination with stakeholders and taxing agencies the
14   proposed 12600 South Community Redevelopment Area and the Redwood Road
15   Redevelopment Area as one, adding all of the land west of the Neighborhood Walmart to
16   1300 West to the 12600 South area, the Beans and Brews property, and the land to the
17   south boundary of the “McDougal Rezone” property to the Redwood Road Area.
18
19   Councilmember Wells asked if the redevelopment area including the McDougal property would
20   create problems in the future. Mr. Lethbridge said that it will not and everything will come back
21   to the Council for review.

23   Councilmember Buroker SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the
