Mr. Alan Orr stated the primary cause of congestion in Little Cottonwood Canyon is 
due to the weather and slick roads associated with snowfall.  Both alternatives would take 
years to complete.  He suggested short term solutions be put in place that would help with 
congestion.  These solutions included traction laws, snow removal earlier in the morning, 
and prohibiting roadside parking,

effect of both options.  They are both too expensive and exceeds the value in terms of 
return of investment.  More incremental solutions needed to be put in place such as 
traction requirements, dedicated a lane of egress, and increased bus and van services.

3.9 Updates to HR Policy 4-200 - Leave Policy 21-1027
Attachments: Staff Report
HR Policy Change request
4-200_Leave Practices_ZLL_20Aug21
Presenters: Kathleen Johnston, HR Director and Pashion Whales, EEO /ER 
Manager (Approx. 10 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Ms. Kathleen Johnston , Director, Human Resources Division, stated the 
proposed changes to this policy adds Juneteenth (June 19th) as an official 
County holiday.  It also makes changes in the wording in several sections and 
includes language to follow what is in the Family Medical Leave Act 
(FMLA). When the policy went before the Steering Committee for review, it 
recommended the policy remain gender neutral so "birthing mother" was 
replaced with "parent giving birth." The benefits provided under this policy 
have not changed. 
Council Member Bradshaw asked for an explanation from legal counsel as 
to why the term should be changed.  He thought this language was needed to 
Page 7 of 11
Council Work Session Meeting Minutes August 31, 2021
follow federal direction. 
Mr. Zachary Lancaster , Deputy District Attorney, stated a Supreme Court 
case defined sex to include sexual orientation and /or sexual identity.  If that is 
not changed, the policy would be in violation of Title 7.  
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  stated for the record she is a mother and loves 
mothers. 
A motion was made by Council Member Alvord, seconded by Council Member 
Theodore, that this agenda item be approved and forwarded to the 4:00 PM 
Council meeting for ratification, changing the wording in paragraph 2 (a) to read 
“birth mother,” instead of “birth parent.”
Council Member Alvord stated the definition is any person that carries a child during the 
gestational period. It is very important that it be noted that the County in no way plans to 
discriminate against any person.  
Council Member Bradshaw asked if this language was approved by the District 
Attorney’s Office.
Mr. Lancaster stated no, it has not been approved.
A substitute motion was made by Council Member Stringham, seconded by Council Member 
Bradshaw, that this agenda item be approved and forwarded to the 4:00 PM Council meeting 
for ratification, changing the wording in paragraph 2(a) to say “the eligible birth mother or 
parent giving birth."  The motion carried by the following vote:
Council Member Stringham, Council Member Bradley, Council 
Member Bradshaw, Council Member Winder Newton, and
