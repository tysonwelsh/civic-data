6. Public Comment
     Audience members may bring any item to the City Council’s attention. Comments will
     be limited to two minutes. State Law prohibits the Council from acting on items that
     do not appear on the agenda. Public comments for this meeting will also be
     conducted electronically. Any person interested in addressing the Council may submit
     a comment by emailing recorder@herriman.org or by visiting Herriman.org/agendas­
     and­minutes, where there is a link to fill out an online public comment form. Your
     statement will be read into the public record.
