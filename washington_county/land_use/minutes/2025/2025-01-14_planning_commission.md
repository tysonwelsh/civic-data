Community Development




              Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                           January 14, 2025
The Regular Meeting of the Planning Commission of Washington County was called to order by
Chairman Bradley Gaston at 1:30 p.m. on January 14, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers and via Zoom, at 111 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah. The Commissioners in
attendance were Brad Gaston, Brian McMullin, Kevin Jones, Mark Owens, Brandon Anderson, Keith
Kelsch and Bryan Simonsen. The Washington County Staff in attendance: Victoria Hales, Washington
County Deputy Attorney; Reid Walkenhorst, Planner; Emerson Rivera, Zoning & Code Compliance
Specialist; Sinalei Tutagalevao Administrative Permits Specialist.

1.     LOT LINE ADJUSTMENT (Public Meeting – Administrative). Review a proposed Lot Line
       Adjustment consolidating 6 parcels into 3 parcels near Diamond Valley, for the purpose of
       complying with acreage requirements for the OST-20 zone (Open Space Transition 20-acre
       minimum lot size). The proposed lot line adjustment will require approval by the Planning
       Commission due to access being made via flag lots from HWY 18. The applicants are Paul &
       Wyn Staheli and Mike Hukill.
       Staff Recommendation:

       Staff have reviewed the conditional use application and recommends that the Planning
       Commission approve South Central Broadband’s fiber optic cable installation project
       based on the following findings and conditions:

     1.    Public Utilities are a conditional use in all land use zones.
     2.    Notice of the proposed conditional use review was properly noticed.
     3.    The proposed conditional use meets the applicable standards of Washington County
           Code 10-18-A-6.
     4.    The proposal enriches the infrastructure network in Washington County.
     5.    The proposal meets the County General Plan.
     6.    The proposal meets the applicable Washington County Codes.
     7.    The detrimental effects of the conditional use have been mitigated, and the use will not
           result in an unreasonable risk to the safety of persons or property or interfere with the
           lawful use of surrounding property. The use will not create a need for services which
           cannot be met by local service providers.


      MOTION: Commissioner Anderson made a motion to APPROVE item 1 subject to staff
      recommendations.
      Commissioner Owen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.


2.    CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT (Public Meeting – Administrative). Review a Conditional
      Use Permit for installing fiber optic cable in the Blackridge Ranches subdivision in New
      Harmony. The installation is known as Phase 6. The applicant is South Central Broadband.
      Staff Recommendation:

      Staff has reviewed the Lot Line Adjustment request for parcels 7215-D-1-DA, 7215-F-1-
      A-DA, 7215-G-1-DA, 7215-C-DA, 7215-H-DA, and 7215-E-1-DA and recommends the
      Planning Commission review, and if comfortable approve the flag lots and the Lot Line
      Adjustment request based on the following findings and conditions:


      1.    The applicant has submitted a complete Lot Line Adjustment application and the
            supporting documentation, and two flag lots are proposed in the application.
      2.    A Professional Land Surveyor has documented the Lot Line Adjustment, flag lots, and
            new property descriptions.
      3.    A Notice of Approval will be prepared by the landowner, and signed by the Land Use
            Authority, for recording, with language acceptable to the County for compliance with
            State law.
      4.    Lot 1 shall be required to show road access adjacent to the flag stem on the survey plat
            map.

     5.   Conveyance documents shall be executed and recorded with language acceptable to
          the County.
     6.   An agreement regarding nonconforming access, delay of construction, and lot line

          requirements.


     MOTION: Commissioner Owen made a motion to APPROVE item # 2 subject to staff
