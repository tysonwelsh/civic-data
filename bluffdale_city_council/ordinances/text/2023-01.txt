                                   ORDINANCE NO.2023-01

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 6.30.010(A) OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY CODE RELATED
TO DETACHED TRAILER PARKING




WHEREAS the City of Bluffdale maintains a system of public roads and streets;

WHEREAS the City desires to allow parking on public streets when it does not create a hazard
or unsightly presence;

WHEREAS the City desires to modify and clarify its detached trailer parking ordinance to
facilitate enforcement of the ordinance; and

WHEREAS the City Council finds that the proposed amendment will further the public health,
safety, and general welfare.
NOW,THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH:

       SECTION 1. Amendment of Bluffdale City Code. Chapter 6.30 of the Bluffdale City Code is
hereby amended as follows:




06.30.010: General Restriction


   A. No person shall park a detached trailer(boat,camper trailer, semi-trailer, utility trailer,
      dump trailers, etc.) overnight on any public street or right-of-way. No person shallown
      orpossessa detached trailerparked overnightin violation ofthis ordinance. Ownership
       and possession mavbe demonstrated bv the currentormostrecentregistration, orany
       other admissible evidence. It is no defense to a charge that the detached trailer was
       illegallyparked bv another, unless the ownermakesa prima facie claim thatatthe time
       of violation, the detached trailer was used without the consent ofthe owner. Such a
       defense shall be considered an alibi defense and proper notice and witness lists.
       including the identity of the individual alieged to have parked the detached trailer
       withoutconsent mustbe given to the Citv asprovided in Utah Code Ann.§77-14-2.

       After the person raisessuch a prima facie claim, the Cityshallhave the burden to prove
       bevonda reasonable doubt thatthe trailer was used with legalconsent.

       Such a violation is an infraction.


   B. Whenever it becomes necessary for any person or persons engaged in construction
      work on or in a building longer than the legal limit in this Section, a permit shall be
         issued by the City Engineer. Notwithstanding,cones and other safety measures must be
         in place at all times.




         SECTION 2. Severabiilty. If any section, part or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Ordinance

       SECTION 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting as required by law.
