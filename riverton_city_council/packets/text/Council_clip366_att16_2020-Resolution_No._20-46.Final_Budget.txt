                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-46

            A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTION CITY COUNCIL
       ADOPTING THE FINAL BUDGETS FOR THE 2012-2021 FISCAL YEAR

       WHEREAS, the Governing Body of Riverton City has studied the Tentative Budgets
presented by the Mayor and adopted on May 5, 2020; and

      WHEREAS, the Governing Body of Riverton City has made additions or deletions to the
Mayor’s budget; and

       WHEREAS, the Governing body of Riverton City held the appropriate Public Hearings as
required by law on the proposed budgets for the 2020-2021 Fiscal Year;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, State of
Utah, as follows:

       1. The Budget, which has been approved by the City Council, is hereby adopted as
          the Final Budget for Riverton City for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2020 and
          ending June 30, 2021, as attached hereto.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 16th day of
June 2020 by the following vote:
