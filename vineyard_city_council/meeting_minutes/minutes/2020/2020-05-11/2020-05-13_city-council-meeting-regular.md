     8.4       PUBLIC HEARING – Zoning Code Text Amendment Ordinance 2020-04
        The applicant is proposing to amend Chapter 15.38.030(2)(c) regarding parking
        requirements for offices and medical offices/clinics. The request is to change the stall
        requirement from one (1) stall per 200 square feet of floor area to one (1) stall per 250
        square feet of floor area. The mayor and City Council will act to adopt (or deny) this
        request by ordinance.

Mayor Pro Tempore Judd turned the time over to Community Development Director Morgan
Brim.

Page 10 of 13; May 13, 2020 City Council Meeting Minutes
Mr. Brim explained that this zoning text amendment request was to accommodate a future
office/medical building by reducing the required parking from one space per 200 square feet to
one space per 250 square feet. He said that most office users would come with their own parking
requirements to accommodate their customers and employees. He explained that typically, when
parking issues did arise, they were within residential areas. He pointed out that residential and
commercial parking issues were very different. He said that the applicant had done a study that
they would be sharing. He added that Planning Commission and staff were recommending
approval. He then turned the time over to Joseph Browning with Hales Engineering.

Mr. Browning explained that the study focused on two core things: to do a parking count at a
similar medical site in American Fork and to compare the parking rates in Orem, Lehi, and
Draper. He said that they took the counts for peak demand from each of the areas and bumped
them up a bit. He mentioned that all three cities required one parking stall per 250 square feet of
building. He felt that based on the comparisons, one parking space per 250 square feet should be
adequate.

Mayor Pro Tempore Judd called for a motion to open the public hearing.

Motion: COUNCILMEMBER EARNEST MOVED TO OPEN THE PUBLIC HEARING AT
7:26 PM. COUNCILMEMBER WELSH SECONDED THE MOTION. ROLL CALL WENT
AS FOLLOWS: MAYOR PRO TEMPORE JUDD, COUNCILMEMBERS EARNEST, FLAKE,
AND WELSH VOTED AYE. MAYOR FULLMER WAS ABSENT. THE MOTION CARRIED
WITH ONE ABSENT.

Mayor Pro Tempore Judd called for public comments.

Councilmember Earnest expressed concern with the commercial parking being adjacent to the
high-density housing and felt that it was an issue. He said that he was comfortable voting for the
amendment with the understanding that there would be enough space as time went on. He
wanted the developer to know that they would be dealing with residents taking up parking stalls
in the commercial areas.

Mr. Lauret reminded the council that when the Yard B was approved in 2018, the developer
mentioned that there would be a shared parking agreement with other commercial developments.
He felt that if they lowered the requirement, then there would be fewer parking stalls to share.
Mr. Brim stated that it would be up to the developer how the parking was managed on the
commercial sites. He said that part of the hope was that the entertainment uses would have high
peaks at different times than a medical office building. He added that commercial uses would be
allowed to tow if they put up signs and enforced it. He asked X Development to explain how
they were going to manage the shared uses.

Eric Towner with X Development explained that they were developing projects in Lehi and
Draper with mixed uses. He felt that there was an opportunity to maximize building square
footage and have cross easements and cross parking. He added that they had negotiated cross
easements and parking with the owners of the Megaplex. He felt that a good baseline was one
stall for every 250 square feet of building. He said that tenants usually drive the parking need.

Mr. McHargue asked if this text amendment would be decreasing the requirements for all office
buildings and commercial developments. Mr. Brim replied that the development would not really
be getting a reduction in parking because of its was shared parking. In this instance every
