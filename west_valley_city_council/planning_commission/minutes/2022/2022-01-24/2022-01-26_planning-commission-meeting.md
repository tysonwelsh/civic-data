General plan change from neighborhood commercial to general

1.79 acres


Rick Magness with Anderson Wahlen and Associates has submitted a General Plan/zone change
application for two parcels totaling 1.79 acres located at 4065 S 5600 W and 5568 W 4100 S.
Both properties are currently zoned C-1 (Neighborhood Commercial) with a General Plan
designation of neighborhood commercial. The proposed zone is C-2 (General Commercial) and
the proposed General Plan designation is general commercial. For reference, a copy of the
commercial and manufacturing use table is included with this report. The C-1 zone allows fewer
uses than the C-2 zone. Both parcels are owned by America First Credit Union.

Surrounding zones include C-1 to the north and south, C-2 to the west, and RB (Residential
Business) to the east. Surrounding land uses include an AutoZone auto parts store to the north, a
CVS pharmacy to the south, a Walgreens pharmacy and Smiths grocery store to the west, and a
vacant parcel to the east. The subject property to the south is developed as a branch for American
First Credit Union. Aside from some access drives, the subject property to the north is vacant.

By way of history, the subject properties were rezoned from R-2-8 (Two Unit Dwelling
Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) to RB in 1998. They were then rezoned from
RB to C-1 in 2015.

Included with this report is a letter and concept plan from the applicant describing the proposed
use as well as reasons to support this application. The applicant is proposing to develop the
subject property to the north as a Starbucks coffee shop with a drive-thru. The C-2 zone allows
fast food establishments with drive-up windows as a permitted use; however, this use is
prohibited in the C-1 zone. The existing American First Credit Union branch is a permitted use
in both the C-1 and C-2 zones and would remain as developed.

If this application is approved, the requirements in Section 7-7-111 would apply to the east
property line as the RB zone is considered a residential zone. These requirements include the
following:

       “Where Fast Food Establishments with drive-up windows are located adjacent to a
       Residential Zone or Use, the order board, speakers, and pick-up window shall be located
       at least 50' from the nearest residential property line and any sound emitting from the
       order board, speakers, and pickup window shall not be audible from the nearest
       residential property line between 10 PM and 7 AM.”

Staff Alternatives:

      public hearing.

   3. Denial. The C-1 zone should remain.

SV-1-2022
4695 South Partial Street Vacation
4695 South 4110 West

BACKGROUND

The West Valley City Engineering Division is requesting a partial street vacation for a portion of
4695 South at approximately 4110 West. The purpose for the street vacation is to remove from
public record an old right-of-way that is no longer needed for public access.

Staff has been unable to locate a dedication plat or subdivision wherein this portion of 4695 would
have been dedicated, if dedicated at all. The street in question was likely built when the Academy
Park No. 1 Subdivision to the west was platted in 1960. Although this portion of 4695 South is
not part of the subdivision plat, it would have made sense for this connection coming off of 4700
South.

Because this portion of 4695 South would have been used by the public for access into the
subdivision, it would qualify as a public street by virtue of its perpetual use and maintenance by
West Valley City as a public right-of-way. The road only consisted of asphalt having no formal
improvements such as curb, gutter or sidewalk.

When the City approved the Valley Cove PUD in 1999, a new road was constructed for its access
along with 3 single family lots, all gaining access from 4695 South. The PUD dedicated
approximately ¾ of an acre for use as a City Park or trail head which is located immediately to the
north of 4695 South. The new road was dedicated and improved which effectively closed this road
for public use.

Per State Law, half of this right-of-way will go to West Valley City with the other half going to
the property owner to the south. They have been made aware of this proposal and look forward to
using the property for parking and/or a future expansion of their existing restaurant.

According to City Ordinance, streets and/or alley vacations shall be reviewed by the Planning
Commission with a recommendation to the City Council.

RECOMMENDATION

   1. Approval of the street vacation as requested.
   2. Continue the application due to issues raised at the public hearing.


Discussion: Steve Lehman presented the application. He explained that West Valley City and
            Salt Lake County could not find a plat that dedicated the right-of-way in question.
            Therefore, due to its use, and maintenance by the City, it was deemed a public
            right-of-way.

SD-1-2022
4715 South Dedication Plat
4715 South between 4130 and 4730 West


BACKGROUND:

The West Valley City Engineering Division is requesting consideration to dedicate a portion of 4715
South adjacent to the SL County linear park. The proposed application also dedicates a portion of Arlington
Park Drive, Dartmouth Street, and Vassar Street. The portion of 4715 south to be dedicated is located at
approximately 4715 South between 4130 4730 West.

ISSUES:

4715 South was platted under Salt Lake County jurisdiction with County subdivisions being
located south of 4715 South. It is not known why the entire right-of-way was not dedicated, but
staff believes the dedication only occurred to what is known as the Section Line. To provide some
background on this term, staff would like to provide the following:

        In order to facilitate the settlement of the western United States in the nineteenth
        century, the federal government broke the land up into “townships” that were
        generally 36-square mile blocks. Each township was then broken into “sections”
        of roughly one square mile, or 640 acres. Each section was further divided into
        “quarter sections,” and further into “quarter quarter sections” of 40 acres each.
Sometimes when the City boundary is adjacent to another jurisdiction, we have seen where roads
follow the section line, typically 30 or 40 feet on either side of the line. There is often no deed,
easement or dedication for these roads, as in this case for this situation.

During an initial assessment for the 4715 South improvement project to be undertaken by the City,
Kearns Metro Township, and Salt Lake County, the Engineering Division, in conjunction with
these other entities, noticed that full dedication did not take place when 4715 South was platted
and subsequently constructed. It was also noted that dedication for those smaller stub connections
(Arlington Park Drive, Dartmouth Street, and Vassar Street) did not occur. The purpose of this plat is to
dedicate all remaining right-of-way located on or adjacent to the north side of 4715 South.

The right-of-way increases in size from approximately 12-14 feet. Future improvements include
curb, gutter and sidewalk in certain locations for bus stops etc. The east/west pedestrian walkway
will continue to be gained from the existing walk in the linear park located within the County.

STAFF ALTERNATIVES:

        1.      Approval of the dedication plat, subject to West Valley City Engineering and Salt
                Lake County Surveyor’s approval.

        2.      Continuation, to address issues raised during the public hearing.

M-1-2022
Burger King Landscaping
Exception 5610 W 3500 S
C-2 Zone (0.59 Acres)

The applicant is requesting modifications to the requirements of Section 7-10-103 of Title 7,
Landscaping Standards Applicable to All High-Image Arterials. This property is one of many
that was impacted recently by UDOT’s widening of the intersection at 3500 South and 5600
West, in connection with the Mountain View Corridor project. Both streets are subject to the
regulations outlined in section 7-10 of the West Valley City Municipal Code, which defines
landscaping standards for properties that have frontage on high-image arterial streets. The
right-of-way improvements eliminated landscaping next to the sidewalk. The code requires a
minimum of a 20’ landscape buffer adjacent to the sidewalk, but there is currently less than 8’
remaining along 5600 West and about 18’ remaining on the west side of the drive approach
along 3500 South. More than 20’ remains on the south side of the building along 3500 South.
The Planning Commission can grant a modification to the 20’ landscaped buffer requirement
if the property owner can demonstrate that compliance would necessitate “elimination of
improvements required by other ordinances which could not be relocated elsewhere on the
site” (7-10-104(1)(b)).

All of the parking stalls would be eliminated on the east side of the building if the 20’
landscaped buffer is required on along 5600 West. One parking stall would also be lost if
the full 20’ is required on the west side of the drive approach on 3500 South. The parking
on the site would be insufficient for the business if these stalls are removed. The property
owner is requesting an exception to the 20’ requirement in both areas.

Due to the narrowness and grading of the remaining landscaped area along 5600 West, staff
recommends that lawn and trees should not be required, but there needs to be at least 50% live
plant material. All of the landscaping along 3500 South, besides the 20’ width requirement
west of the drive approach, shall meet all regulations outlined in 7-10-103. Staff recommends
that the applicant submits an updated landscape plan for the site so that compliance with all
regulations can be verified.

Staff Alternatives

Approval. Twenty feet of landscaping is not required next to the sidewalk along 5600 West,
and on the west side of the drive approach on 3500 South, but the site shall also meet the
following conditions:

   1. Lawn and trees shall not be required in the landscaping along 5600 West, but shall
       include at least 50% coverage of live plant material.
    2. The landscaping along 3500 South shall meet all requirements outlined in 7-10-103.
    3. A planting plan shall be provided to the West Valley City Planning Division and
       approved by staff before any work is done.
    4. Installation shall be completed by July 1, 2022.

 Continuance, for reasons determined at the hearing.
