PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT/PUBLIC HEARING:
     Consider a request for a Planned Development (PD) amendment to the Sun
     River Commons Master Sign Plan. Proposed changes include adding two 30
     ft. tall pylon signs, increasing monument sign height from 9 ft. to 10 ft. and
     from 5 ft. to 6.5 ft., and allowing all signs to have electronic message
     centers. The applicant is YESCO, and the representatives are Ray Draper and
     Ryan Cain. The project will be known as Sun River Commons Signs. Case No.
     2025-PDA-005 – PUBLIC HEARING. (Staff – Brett Hamilton).
