PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on September 29, 2021 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2021-28 vesting certain development rights and approving the execution of master development agreement and accepting portions of the master development agreement as municipal land use ordinances for the Olympia development. Authorizing a zoning map amendment and assigning the Olympia - MPC zone to approximately 933 acres of land known as Olympia located at approximately 12600 south 6800 west. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
