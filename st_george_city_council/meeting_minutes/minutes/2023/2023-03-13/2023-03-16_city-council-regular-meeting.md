CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval of a contract with Ash Excavating LLC for the
        construction of the Red Hills Debris Basin EWP Project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Two (2) bids were received for this
          project. Due to the flooding of residential property after the August 2020 severe
          storms in the area, the NRCS provided emergency funding to construct the
          planned basin to mitigate future flooding downstream. As the basin is located in
          the Snow Canyon State Park and the Desert Reserve, it took several months to
          secure the environmental permitting. St. George will pay a 25% match for this
          project. Staff recommends approval of the contract.

      b. Consider approval of an Improvement Reimbursement Agreement
         between the City of St. George and Riverfront Medical Holdings, LLC for
         storm drain improvements located along 1100 South Street.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The developer of parcel SG-5-2-31-
          12051 is required to install improvements along 1100 South. There is an existing
          public storm drain within 1100 South that is currently conveyed through a private
          single family residential lot. Developer has agreed to construct and install the
          storm drain improvements required as part of their development and to reroute
          the storm drain located on private property. Staff recommends approval of the
          agreement.

      c. Consider approval of an Improvement Reimbursement Agreement
         between Bright Ideas REI LLC and the City of St. George for road
         improvements on 2800 South Street.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The developer of parcel SG-5-3-9-4221
          is improving the north side of 2800 South Street along their frontage as well as
          the remaining south portion of the roadway. The City would like the developer to
          install the public improvements on the remaining portion of the roadway so that
          the full-width improvements are complete and so the storm drain can tie into
          River Road. The City's cost of improvements amounts to $373,370. Staff
          recommends approval of the agreement.

      d. Consider approval of Federal Aid Agreement with UDOT for MPO Regional
         Shared Use Path Standards.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City received some federal funding
           through the MPO to hire a consultant and prepare standards for design and
           construction of shared-use paths (i.e., trails) throughout the MPO region. This
           agreement with UDOT sets forth the amount of federal funds available for the
           project of $37,292 and the local match of $2,708, which is included in the current
           budget. Staff recommends approval of the agreement.

      e. Consider approval of Federal Aid Agreement with UDOT for Washington
         County Trail Counters.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City received some federal funding
           through the MPO for the purchase of some trail counters within the Washington
           County area. This agreement with UDOT sets forth the amount of federal funds
           available for the project of $74,584 and the local match of $5,416, which is
           included in the current budget. Staff recommends approval of the agreement.

      f.   Consider approval of Federal Aid Agreement with UDOT for the George
           Washington Blvd Extension Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The George Washington Blvd Extension
           Project is an important new roadway that extends from River Rd to Crosby Way.
           The project is split into two phases: Phase I for the roadway and Phase II for the
           bridge across the Virgin River. This project received federal funds of $2.1M
           through the MPO to help with the construction of the roadway portion of the
           project. UDOT is the recipient of federal funds and will administer the project.
           This agreement sets forth the federal aid amount and the local match of
           $152,494, which is included in the current budget. Staff recommends approval
           of the agreement.

      g. Consider approval of an event sponsorship for Brooks Block Party in the
         Main Street Alley on Monday, April 3, 2023.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This first-time event is organized by
           Utah Tech University and kicks of their "D" Week festivities for students. Main
           Street Alley. As part of the sponsorship, UTU is asking that the City waive the 45-
           day requirement, special event fee and rental fee for the use of the Alley. There
           is currently no fee schedule for the use of the Main Street Alley. In exchange,
           UTU will list the City as a sponsor in their online and printed materials for the
           event. The event is free to the public and approx. 300 students are expected to
           attend. UTU has contacted neighboring businesses and residents about the
           Monday night event and received positive feedback. They also meet City
           insurance requirements. Staff recommends approval.

INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH WASHINGTON
COUNTY/RESOLUTION:
     Consider approval of a resolution entering into an Interlocal Cooperation
     Agreement with Washington County for cost-sharing of communication
     services provided through Everbridge.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: St. George and Washington County
      currently share costs for public communications software and other services through
      Everbridge, which can be used for wildfire and emergency mass notifications, page
      outs for first responders, and community engagement communications. The total
      annual cost of the Services is $63,731.12. St. George City consolidated dispatch
      primarily uses paging and notification services and is responsible for $53,481.12 of
      the annual total. Washington County primarily utilizes the Alertus, Community
      Engagement, and Resident Connection functions and is responsible for the remaining
      annual amount of $10,250. City and County want to reduce this agreement to
      writing via the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement. Staff recommends approval of the
      resolution entering into the Interlocal Agreement.

ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance amending the city zoning map by
     amending the zone from A-20 (Agricultural 20-acre minimum lot size) to R-
     1-20 (Residential single family 20,000 sq ft minimum lot size) on
     approximately 20.50 acres located on the northeast corner of 2450 South

      remains undisturbed. At their meeting held on February 28, 2023. the Planning

TEMPORARY AMENDMENT TO TITLE 3, CHAPTER 10 OF CITY CODE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance temporarily suspending the permitting or
     processing of certain special event permits under Title 3, Chapter 10 of the
     City code.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Title 3, Chapter 10 of the City code governs
      the use of public facilities and permitting of special events at these facilities. It has
      been determined that the use of public facilities for special events has increased in
      recent years to the point that maintenance of the facilities is becoming more difficult.
      Public facilities should be available to the public and should not be over encumbered
      by special events. The proposed ordinance suspends new event applications in or on
      city facilities and changes the definition of "special event" and would be in effect
      immediately upon publication and/or posting as required by law and shall be
      effective for a period of no greater than six (6) months. During the temporary
      suspension, staff will study all impacts of events held at city facilities. Staff
      recommends approval of the ordinance temporarily suspending the permitting or
      processing of certain special event permits under Title 3, Chapter 10 of the City
      code.
