3.2 Public hearing and consideration of Innovation Pointe’s request for approval of the
Innovation Pointe Phase 3 concept, an 8-acre commercial development located at 1441 West
Innovation Way in a Commercial zone.
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Craig Chambers, Assistant City Attorney, wanted to clarify that the meeting tonight would
    not be accepting applicant narrative and was limited to questions only.

    The applicant, Kent England, was present.

    Commissioner Everett asked about the parking strip and the amount of parking.
    Commissioner Ellis also inquired as to why there was so much parking. Commissioner
    Nielsen stated that he was choosing to abstain from the vote on this agenda item because he
    disagrees with the project and goal of this project.

    The applicant responded to the Commissioners questions but was inaudible.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

    Commissioner Ellis asked if the Commission was looking at approving any exceptions.
    Commissioner Hemmert stated they were only giving recommendations. Commissioner
    Bunker stated he felt that Commissioner Nielsen should express his opinions and views
    where this would be a recommendation where his opinion is valued. Commissioner Nielsen
    stated the he doesn’t like the parking and landscaping and feels that it isn’t following the
    zone and code of the City. He expressed concern about the walkability and open space and
    doesn’t feel that exceptions should be made. Commissioner Ellis stated he hoped the park
    across the street was built and utilized. Commissioner Nielsen stated he appreciated the effort
    that has gone into this item but feels there are some things that can be addressed to improve
    the development.


Motion: Commissioner Ellis motioned to recommend approval of Innovation Pointe’s request
for approval of the Innovation Pointe Phase 3 concept, an 8-acre commercial development
located at 1441 West Innovation Way in a Commercial zone; including all DRC comments and
findings stated in the report, with the recommendation that the developer find ways to incorporate
more green space and reduce the parking. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, abstained vote; Commissioner Ellis,
yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, no. The
motion passed.

3.3 Public hearing and consideration of Rick Allen’s request for conditional use approval
of the Allen Flag Lot located at 2080 North 1200 East in an RA-1 (residential/agriculture)
zone.
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item. Sierra stated with this
    being conditional use this would be the time to make recommendations.

    Commissioner Bunker stated he observed the property and stated the development doesn’t
    meet the requirements for the flag lot. Ross Dinsdale, City Engineer, stated the property
    owner to the South, Gary Cooper, submitted comment for future development. Ross stated
    the flag lot stem can be built with curb, gutter and sidewalk and can be connected to the
    South property with a continuation of curb, gutter and sidewalk that would be paid for, or
    agreed upon, by the developer of the flag lot and property owner to the South. Commissioner
    Bunker stated he felt that was a good solution if the developer and Mr. Cooper were working
    together. Commissioner Everett asked if it met the code and asked for clarification on what
    exceptionally deep meant per the code. Commissioner Hemmert stated this is the first time he
    has seen a flag lot being asked as a conditional use where the property owner isn’t
    subdividing his own property and asked Kim Struthers why a flag lot would be requested.
    Kim Struthers, Community Development Director, stated the request was to provide more
    property options. He stated the new house would need to face the South for future
    development. Staff and Commission discussed the agenda item.

    The applicant, Rick Allen, was present and addressed questions that were brought up
    regarding the development. He stated he read Mr. Cooper’s public comment that was
    submitted and would be happy to work with him to find a solution for the properties.

    Commissioner Ellis asked about the removal of the fire hydrants and whose responsibility
    that would be. Ross Dinsdale stated that would be the developer’s responsibility.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Rick Allen’s request for conditional use
approval of the Allen Flag Lot located at 2080 North 1200 East in an RA-1
(residential/agriculture) zone, including all DRC comments and findings stated in the reports;
and including that the developer design access that can be utilized as a full street in the future
and work with the adjacent property owner top the South. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes. The motion
passed unanimously.

3.4 Public hearing and consideration of RMR LLC’s request for approval of the Reynolds
Daycare concept, a commercial daycare development located at approximately 2975 West
Executive Parkway.
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Nielsen had questions on the amount of parking.

    The applicant, Rob Reynolds, was present and addressed the questions that were asked about
    parking. He stated the parking exceeds the City requirements but does not exceed
    Thanksgiving Points requirements.

    Commissioner Nielsen responded to the applicant and had questions about a path and if other
    tenants that occupied the building would be ok with the parking and detention basin location.
    The applicant stated the detention basin would be closer to the tracks, making it further from
    the play area. The applicant stated in his experience tenants prefer their own property design
    and space. He stated it also makes the tenants closer to public transport.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker motioned to recommend approval of RMR LLC’s request for
approval of the Reynolds Daycare concept, a commercial daycare development located at
approximately 2975 West Executive Parkway, including all DRC comments and findings
stated in the report. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes. The motion
passed unanimously.

3.5 Public hearing and recommendation of Edge Home’s request for review of the Willow
Point concept, a 420-lot residential development located at approximately 4100 West
Hardman Way.
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Bunker stated there were several comments from the public and would like
    the applicant to address those comments. Most of the comments were geared towards historic
    parking. Commissioner Hemmert stated another concern expressed was the landscaping of
    the development. Commissioner Ellis asked if it was easier for the tenants to park in the
    street rather their garages. Commissioner Hereth asked if signal lights would be installed on
    Hardman Way. Ross Dinsdale stated there were signal lights that were being installed and
    future maintenance for the street was planned and UDOT would be responsible for a portion
    as well.

    The applicant, Edge Homes, was present. He stated the parking was to create walkability and
    felt they provided adequate parking at the clubhouse. The applicant stated the landscaping
    would be finalized with safety in mind. Edge Homes stated they are happy to have their HOA
    look at parking if it becomes an issue.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker motioned to recommend approval of Edge Home’s request for
review of the Willow Point concept, a 420-lot residential development located at
approximately 4100 West Hardman Way, including all DRC comments and findings stated in
the report. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes. The motion
passed unanimously.
