                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-07

     A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF RIVERTON CITY GIVING ITS
      ADVICE AND CONSENT TO THE APPOINTMENT OF CITY RECORDER

       WHEREAS, in accordance with Utah Code, the Mayor, with advice and consent of the
City Council, shall appoint individuals to fulfill statutory offices in Riverton City; and

       WHEREAS, due to the retirement of Virginia Loader, it has become necessary to
appoint a new City Recorder; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for appointment of Jamie
Larsen as the Riverton City Recorder; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the appointment of Jamie Larsen as
Riverton City Recorder;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Mayor's appointment of Jamie Larsen to the office of City Recorder, upon
          the retirement of the current City Recorder, Virginia Loader, is hereby made
          with the advice and consent of the City Council.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 19th day of
January 2021 by the following vote:
