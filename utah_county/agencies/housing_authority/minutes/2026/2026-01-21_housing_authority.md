I. Prayer 
An opening prayer was offered before the Board meeting commenced. 
II. Public Comments 
There were no public comments. 
III. DISCUSSION ONLY ITEMS 
A. 360 Management Review (Consultant) 
The HR consultant provided the Board with an overview of the 360-degree management 
review process. The consultant was requested to prepare and submit sample review 
questions for the Board’s consideration. 
 
B. HAUC Board member terms 
 
The Board reviewed the current board member terms and confirmed each member’s respective 
term status. 
 
C. Future potential members 
             The Board discussed the status of a current Board member and the possibility the member 
may become ineligible to continue. Two potential candidates to fill the position were discussed. 
The Board requested that a bio for the candidates be submitted for review at the next Board 
meeting. 
 
 
 

D. HAUC Board Elections 
            The Board was advised January constitutes the annual meeting and that Board elections would 
be conducted accordingly. 
 
IV. Executive Director’s Report 
The Executive Director reviewed multiple development projects, including a potential location 
for the Agency’s future main office. 
V. Commissioner’s Report 
No Commissioner’s report was provided. 
VI. Utah County Status Update (Claudia Barillas) 
Utah County’s management of the MAG grants was discussed. The MAG grants are currently 
open, and the County is in the process of finalizing its procedures. Additional information is 
expected by the end of January. 
VII. Financial Statement Report 
The Agency’s financial statements were reviewed with the Board. A question was raised 
regarding COC funding, and it was clarified that HUD will be issuing the grants as planned for 
one additional year due to a court order, and that the HUD funding reductions occurred within 
the Public Housing programs; therefore, the cuts do not affect the Agency 
 
VIII. Voucher Program Report 
The voucher program was reviewed with the Board, and changes to COC leasing procedures 
were discussed. The Board requested a waiting list report be prepared and presented at the 
next Board meeting for review. 
 
IX. Closed Meeting: Matters Pertaining to: 
 
Real Estate

Staff 
 
X. ACTION ITEMS 
 
A. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2026-01-01 Minutes Regular Board Meeting held 12-17-2025. 
April made a motion to approve the minutes from the regular board meeting. Suzanne  
seconded the request. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
B. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2026-01-02 HAUC Board Elections 
April made a motion to approve the 2026 Board Elections. Suzanne seconded the request. The 
motion passed unanimously 
During the Board elections, the responsibilities of the Board Chair and Vice Chair positions were 
reviewed and discussed. Following the discussion, the current Chair and Vice Chair were nominated and 
unanimously re-elected to continue serving in their respective roles. 
 
C. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2026-01-03 2026 Board Schedule 
Claudia made a motion to amend the board's meeting schedule to convene on the third 
Thursday of each month. Steve seconded the request. The motion passed unanimously.  
              Resolution 3 was not executed. The Board resolved to amend the meeting schedule to convene on the 
third Thursday of each month. The revised schedule will be reviewed and presented for approval at the 
next Board meeting. 
 
D. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2026-01-04 Lobby Hours 
April made a motion to approve the adjusted lobby hours. Suzanne seconded the request . The 
motion passed unanimously.  
 
             An analysis of lobby traffic was presented to the Board, along with supporting information gathered 
from other agencies regarding their lobby hours. The analysis recommended adjusting the Agency’s 
lobby hours to better align with actual usage patterns and to allow staff to more effectively focus on 
other projects and workload demands. 
 
E. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2026-01-05 Purchase Agreement Addendum #4 
Suzanne made a motion to approve the Purchase Agreement Addendum #4. April seconded the 
request. The motion passed unanimously.  
Additional time needed to address boundary line and survey issues on a potential development 
site were discussed. 
 
F. Ratification Resolution 2026-01-06 Landscape Contract 
Steve made a motion to approve the Ratification of the Landscape Contract. Claudia seconded 
the request. The motion passed unanimously.  
Due to the year-end and other timing constraints, the Executive Director sought Board approval via 
email to execute a landscape contract. The approval vote was unanimous, necessitating formal 
ratification at this Board meeting.
