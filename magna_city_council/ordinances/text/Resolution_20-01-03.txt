A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
APPROVING THE CONTRACT OF KMH CONSULTING LLC TO PERFORM
CERTAIN LEGISLATIVE LOBBYING WORK ON BEHALF OF MAGNA FOR
THE 2020 UTAH STATE LEGISLATURE GENERAL SESSION
RECITALS
A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401
et seq.
B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.
| C. With pending amendments upcoming during the 2020 Utah State Legislature General Session
for Utah Inland Port, Metro Townships, the MSD and other concerns, Magna desires to retain
the services of a Lobbying firm to work on their behalf on Capitol Hill to provide for the
outcomes Magna needs on these key pieces of legislation.
| D. During the 2019 Legislative Session, Magna retained the services of KMH Consulting LLC to
provide lobbying services for Magna.
E. Based on the work performed, Magna desires to retain KMH Consulting LLC to represent
them again for the 2020 Legislative Session,
F. KMH Consulting LLC has responded with the attached contract for services (Attachment

G. KMH Consulting LLC are highly qualified lobbyists who can professionally represent
Magna’s interests on these important pieces of legislation.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the contact between Magna
and KMH Consulting LLC, hereinafter known in Attachment A, and authorizes the Mayor to sign
the contract to affect the same.

Professional Services Agreement from KMH Consulting LLC with

This Professional Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into and effective this wg

day of January 2020, by and between KMH Consulting LLC with principal offices located at 4352
Rupp Court, Salt Lake City, 84129 and Magna Metro Township (MMT) located in Salt Lake
County.

Recitals

A. KMH Consulting LLC will provide lobbying services for Magna Metro Township (MMT) to
include interactions with the Utah State Legislature at the direction of MMT.

B. KMH Consulting LLC will work with the MMT Mayor and assigned Administrator to
increase the impact that MMT can have with legislative leaders in the state of Utah.

C. MMT will communicate the needs and requests to be made on their behalf by KMH
Consulting through the MMT Executive Director or MMT Mayor.

D. KMH Consulting has the knowledge and experience to lobby on behalf of MMT for the
limited purpose specified herein.

E. MMT wants to retain KMH Consulting to perform lobbying services subject to certain
terms and conditions

F. KMH Consulting wants to represent MMT for the limited purposes specified herein
subject to certain terms and conditions

G. MMT and KMH Consulting understand that due to the sensitivity of MMT’s overall
lobbying effort, cooperation between them is essential. It is, therefore, necessary for
MMT to provide consistent direction to KMH Consulting to avoid confusion with regard
to MMT lobbying needs.

H. MMT and KMH Consulting want to enter into an agreement to establish process and
parameters to enhance cooperation, including disclosure of potential conflicts and the
manner in which directions are provided to KMH Consulting.

IN CONSIDERATION of the mutual covenants provided herein, MMT and KMH Consulting

agree as follows:

1. SCOPE OF WORK
KMH Consulting is knowledgeable in the area of work to be performed for MMT. The
scope of the work to be performed by KMH is set forth in Exhibit “A” of this Agreement,
attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference. The Agreement may be
amended only by written instrument signed by both MMT and KMH Consulting. KMH
Consulting shall not represent MMT on any legislation without prior approval! from the
MMT designated Representative. KMH Consulting shall regularly communicate with

MMT’s designated representative during the 2020 Utah Legislative Session. KMH
Consulting shall preserve the confidentiality of the discussions with MMT’s designated

2. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
KMH Consulting shall be, at all times during this Agreement, an independent contractor

| and not an employee of MMT. KMH Consulting shall be solely responsible for reporting
| earning, income tax and social security to Federal and State agencies.

3. AUTHORIZATION TO PROCEED
Execution of this Agreement by the MMT and KMH Consulting will serve as
authorization for KMH Consulting to proceed with the services called for in the
Agreement

4. PAYMENT OF FEES
KMH Consulting will provide a billing to MMT in the sum of $6000.00 for the 2020

Payment of the retainer shall not be contingent on KMH Consulting securing any specific
legislation or policies to be adopted.

5. TERMINATION
The term of this Agreement shall be from January __/_, 2020 to December 31st, 2020.
This agreement may be terminated by MMT upon two calendar days written notice. In
the event of such a termination services will cease to be provided by KMH Consulting to
MMT.

6. OWNERSHIP of DOCUMENTS
All documents including notes, drawings, and reports generated by KMH Consulting
under this Agreement are property of MMT. Any reuse by MMT without written
authorization from KMH Consulting will be at MMT’s sole risk and without liability to
KMH Consulting. KMH Consulting may not use the work product for any other client or
purpose with out the express consent of MMT.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
KMH Consulting shall not disclose MMT records without MMT approval. KMH
Consulting shall treat as confidential MMT communications and records and not disclose
to others such communications or records during or subsequent to the term of this
Agreement, except as necessary to perform this Agreement hereunder and then only on
a confidential basis.

SCOPE OF WORK
Consultant shall perform the following services as directed by MMT:

A. Provide representation of MMT to legislators, governor’s office, lobbyists, and
business leaders for matters before the 2020 Utah State Legislature (the
“Session”).

B. Provide information and strategy for the General Legislative Session

C. Participate fully in the Session for MMT.

D. In order to avoid confusion and conflict with MMT lobbying efforts, KMH
Consulting must communicate frequently with an MMT designated
representative. Further, KMH Consulting shall receive approval from MMT
before KMH Consulting works on any legislation on behalf of MMT.

E. KMH Consulting shall meet with MMT at least weekly during the legislative
session and will meet as requested by MMT to discuss work being done by KMH
Consulting on behalf of MMT.
