- The council validator already documents this quirk ("THE MAYOR VOTES in Herriman… Corrects recon.md").

## Ranked fix list (severe first)

1. **[HIGH — data loss] PC vote extraction drops and mis-assigns motions.**
   ~75 of 440 named PC roll-call motions are missing from the db (**~17%**; by year: 2021=44, 2022=15, 2020=11, 2026=3). **7 PC meetings have recorded roll-call votes but ZERO db motions:** `2021-02-18, 2021-04-01, 2021-05-06, 2021-05-20, 2021-06-03, 2021-12-02, 2026-05-20`. Additionally, complex/duplicated multi-part meeting docs produce **merged, corrupted vote sets** — `motions 1621 & 1623 (2025-04-02)` carry 10 voters (roster max 9) with Nays that don't match the cited roll call, and `motion 1204 (2020-10-01)` paired item 2.4's motion text with item 3.1's "5 to 1" roll call while dropping items 3.1 & 3.2.
   *Fix:* re-extract PC handling uppercase "Commissioner X MOVED", per-item roll-call boundaries, and duplicated/two-session PC docs; re-run db + referrals (this also repairs the under-counted referral layer). Wire the PC validator's tally cross-check (currently "0/0 match", a blind spot).

2. **[MEDIUM-HIGH — entity pollution] db `person` table is ~165/195 junk entities.**
   The seconder parser captures the trailing word of the motion sentence: e.g. src "…Fiscal Year **Budget.** Councilmember Shields seconded" → seconder stored as **"Budget. Councilmember Shields"**. This created ~150 garbage person rows ("Offices. Trustee Shields", "Utah. Councilmember Henderson", …) plus unmerged real-name variants (`Adam Jacobosn`, `Henerson`, `Forrest Sickles`/`Sickle`, `Sheilds`, `Garica`, `Sheri Ohrn`). **Vote rows are unaffected** (0 votes point to junk; 21 clean persons), but `v_member_record` and any mover/seconder join is corrupt. Affects **153 council + 16 PC** flat-CSV seconder cells.
   *Fix:* correct the seconder regex to capture only "<Role> <Name>" after the motion sentence; add person-entity dedup/normalization; rebuild db.

3. **[MEDIUM — data loss] CDRA named roll calls dropped to tally-only.**
   All 16 CDRA motions are `names_recorded=0`, but ~4 have **named roll calls in source** (e.g. m28 2020-02-12: "The vote **is** recorded as follows: Director Jared Henderson Aye … Chair David Watts Aye"). The vote regex matches "was recorded" + Councilmember/Trustee/Mayor but misses **present-tense "is recorded"** and **Director/Chair** roles.
   *Fix:* extend the roll-call regex; re-extract the CDRA (`_CDA`) files.

4. **[LOW] Minor council roll-call deficits / tally note.** 2 council meetings show 1–2 more source roll calls than db motions (`2021-10-13`, `2022-10-12`); 1 unexplained tally-vs-count note flagged by the validator (`2021-09-08 m5`, source-ambiguous "3:2"). Hand-review; low impact.
