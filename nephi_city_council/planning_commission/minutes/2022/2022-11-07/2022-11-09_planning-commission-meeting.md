3.    Cornerstone Subdivision (1 lot) - Vicinity Plat, represented by Joe & Jaime Nielson
      and Josh & BreAnna Nixon

     a.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented the vicinity plat for the Cornerstone Subdivision. She said this is a 1
     lot subdivision but depending on how they set up the property, there may be 2 lots. This
     property is currently owned by Korey Wright, who purchased the land from Nephi City.
     This is the furthest south access on UDOT’s highway that we have before the freeway
     interchange. A boundary line agreement will need to be done prior to the preliminary
     plat. An affidavit has been obtained from Mr. Wright, allowing the developers to
     represent this portion of his parcel. Ownership is pending between Mr. Wright and the
     developers. The proposal will not be discussed at this time, but it will be a concurrent
     project. This is a very preliminary concept, but the minimum requirements have been
     met.

     b.       Commission Discussion
     Cory pointed out that a line on the plat shows the city boundary and asked if that was
     current. Seth answered that it had previously been annexed. Cory just wanted to make
     sure we weren’t approving a project outside of city boundaries. Shauna said she will
     make a note of that and make sure it gets updated. Karl asked if the proposed
     developments conform with highway commercial zoning, to which Shauna confirmed.
     Donald Ball asked about the access points, and if UDOT would approve two of them so
     close together. Shuana reminded him that this is a conceptual plan at this point, but the
     city is going to attempt to negotiate another access point with UDOT.

     c.     Action Item
     Motion made to accept the Vicinity Plat for the 1 lot Cornerstone Subdivision with the
     DRC notes.
     Made by: Alan Hancock Second: Cory Thomson Outcome: Passed Unanimously

4.   Planning Commission Bylaws Re-Review

     a.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented the changes and formatting that she made to the bylaws, as a result
     of the commission discussion during the prior meeting. Terms were changed from 4 to 5
     years, to reflect the city code. Shauna went through the entire document, noting the
     changes made and clarifying items that she inserted straight from the city code.

     b.      Commission Discussion
     Skip stated that certain items don’t need to be completely set in stone, allowing for some
     leeway. Cory mentioned that he likes to follow the rules and feels that if the rule is
     changed, then the bylaws need to reflect that. Much discussion was had about a
     commissioner “dropping off” every 5 years. Heather suggested that the verbiage just be
     removed, and Shauna agreed that it would be an easy fix. Cory stated that we are here
     to make sure other people follow the rules- so if we have rules and aren’t following them,
     it doesn’t look good. Shauna asked for input from the Commissioners regarding meeting
     minutes. She is wondering if we can share the minutes with the city council in a timely

     manner in order to facilitate a more streamlined process. Karl feels that it will help
     eliminate the same questions being asked over and over. Heather feels that it would be
     nice for the city council to see that thoughtful considerations are being made by the
     commission instead of just rushing through issues. Skip stated that the City Council
     doesn’t need to know every single thing discussed, but the major bullet points would be
     helpful. Ann pointed out that the document should have consistent capitalization of
     proper nouns, such as “Planning Commission.” Shauna said that she cut out much of
     what was listed under the requirement of the Secretary. A discussion was had about
     when the agenda will be available to the Commission to review prior to the next meeting.
     Ann feels that there needs to be a set window of time, in order to be able to review the
     agenda. If not, then those items shouldn’t be included in the agenda. Conflict of interest
     was discussed; Shauna said that a commissioner with a conflict of interest may
     participate in a discussion, but is not required to vote. Seth stated he learned at city
     council that if there is an ongoing conflict of interest, it needs to be documented in
     writing. Shauna noted that a 3 minute time limit was decided upon for public comment.
     Heather asked if that was the standard, or if there could be some variance. Skip
     confirmed that it can be decided on a case-by-case basis, and Shauna said it’s
     ultimately up to the person leading the meeting. Donald Ball stated that, at city council,
     the public is reminded that public comment does not include back and forth banter. He
     said the person “vents his spleen'' for 3 minutes, and the council listens. The sample
     agenda format for a public hearing was reviewed and confirmed. Shauna stated that she
     will make the necessary changes and bring it back at the next meeting for approval.

     Action Item:
     None needed at this time.

5.   General Plan Update

     a.      Staff Presentation
     Seth presented a draft of the general plan. He said they tried to structure it similarly to
     the prior general plan, with the exception of putting an executive summary at the
     beginning. The executive summary will make the plan easier to navigate. He talked
     about the city’s vision statement, and said that comments from the public well-being
     survey were factored into the document. Seth stated that everything the Commission
     does should tie to this vision. Shauna said that the implementation policies are things
     that are already being worked on by the Commission.

     b.      Commission Discussion
     Cory pointed out that his last name was spelled incorrectly, and asked for that to be
     fixed. Karl said this is an excellent draft, but has an issue with the graphic on page 36.
     He thinks it looks tacky and recommended using hexagons or octagons. He doesn’t like
     the colors or the percentages on the graphic. He proposed that the entire graphic be
     changed. Seth pointed out the demographic data included in the document and invited
     the commissioners to explore that information, if desired. Karl also pointed out that the

     dates on Figure 7 are confusing, and asked for clarification. Seth said he would ask
     Bruce to explain it a little better. Ann stated that part of their Commission requirements
     is to have training. She feels that reading and discussing this document should count
     towards that training; Shauna agreed. Ann also mentioned that it would be nice to have
     the new member appointed soon, and Seth said he hopes to have that taken care of by
     next Tuesday.

     c.    Action Item
     None needed at this time.

6.   Title 10 Updates

     a.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna said this is going to be short and sweet, but wants to bring something up to get
     the Commission’s opinion. She stated that the public hearing should be opened, but this
     will need to be continued because she has more research to do.

     b.     Public hearing
     Public hearing that was continued at the prior meeting was re-opened at 8:02
     pm.

     c.      Commission Discussion
     Shauna said that her question has to do with frontage. The type of frontage required is
     going to depend on whether the building is horizontal or vertical. Alan asked why that
     matters. Shauna explained that she is referring to the actual width of the building, not the
     frontage. Seth said that a narrow corridor to a home poses some public safety risks, to
     which Alan agreed. Heather brought up the fact that there are several town home
     designs to choose from. Shauna asked the Commissioners to think about it, and to also
     consider implementing a townhome/condominium zone. She could see doing that in the
     R3 zone and including a minimum width ordinance. Shauna said another thing that has
     come to light since we just passed CU-1 and CU-2 codes, is that there is a minimum
     square footage per residential unit, but none for commercial. She said that is fine, but
     wanted to get the Commission’s opinion regarding a property owner who is considering
     putting some residential units above his commercial buildings. Alan asked if we had
     already determined that the size of the commercial would be determined by the parking.
     He also asked if residential parking would eventually affect commercial parking? Shauna
     stated that the plan actually worked out nicely. She just wanted to confirm that we don't
     need additional acreage for the commercial, as long as he has 7,260 for the residential.
     Heather stated that it sounds like the parking took care of itself. She’s not as concerned
     with square footage as she is with parking. Shauna feels that it’s valuable to have
     minimum frontage and square footage required, especially for industrial and commercial.
     Heather stated that she doesn’t think she’s ever been worried about commercial
     buildings being too small.

     d.    Action Item
     Ann asked for a motion to continue the public hearing.
     Made by: Heather Robertson Second: Karl Brough Outcome: Passed unanimously
