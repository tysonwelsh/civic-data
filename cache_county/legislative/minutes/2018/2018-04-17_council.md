MINUTES FOLLOW-UP: Council member Merrill reiterated his uncomfortable feelings
regarding the RU-2 Zone and asked for a commitment of timing for the completion of the County
Master Plan or should the Council consider placing a moratorium on RU-2’s for six months?

Director Runhaar stated his office will begin work on the Master Plan at the beginning of the
fourth quarter this year. The most rapid completion would be twelve months with six months
after of adoptions, etc. He is estimating to have it completed somewhere towards the beginning
of 2020. He observed the Council does not need to Pass a moratorium on a zone. The Council
just needs to say it is not going to approve any RU-2’s or RU-5’s until the Master Plan is
completed similar to the policy memo put in place on the extension of road services. Runhaar
will formulate two or three alternatives on the issue to be presented to the Council at the
beginning of May. The Council can then decide on how to proceed.

Council member Tidwell stated support for Director Runhaar and his work on a Master Plan.

Opioid Epidemic Meeting with Jim Carroll, Federal Drug Czar — Executive Buttars

attended the meeting on April 11, 2018. The impact on rural communities where there
is only one or two doctors was the center of the discussion because it only takes one
doctor to overprescribe to have a problem. There are four counties in the state where it
is claimed there are more prescriptions than there are citizens in the county. Cache
County is not one of those counties, but the problem does exist here. In 2013 the county
had between fourteen and sixteen drug overdose deaths. It is important for the county
to be aware and work with health care providers to help solve the problem by a change
in prescribing policies. Executive Buttars remarked that farmers and rural residents
traditionally take care of themselves and have to keep working through pain, etc. which
may contribute to the problem in those areas.

¢ Elected Official and Local District Board Member Training — Executive Buttars told
Council members an email from the State Auditor's office indicates each elected official
and local district board member must commit in writing to abide by a standard of ethical
behavior and complete the state auditor’s online training at least once in the last four

training. Council members will receive a certificate at the completion of the training.

May 8, 2018 Council meeting.

¢ Discussion: Transitional Housing VAWA Grant Match — Terryl Warner said there is
a chance to apply for a one-time grant for housing funding to help victims of crimes. It is
up to $150,000.00 and there are ten grants available; however, since Warner asked for
this to be on the agenda, she has found out that CAPSA in Cache County is applying for
the grant. Warner now feels that since CAPSA services the same individuals that
Warner would be targeting with the grant, it will be better if she writes a letter of support
for CAPSA’s application instead of applying for the grant. The Council concurred.

¢ Discussion: 0.25% Local Option Sales Tax for Transportation — Director Runhaar

informed the Council the effective date of the legislation is May 8, 2018 so the Council
can take action that day or later and asked if there were any questions. Vice Chairman
Ward said, “Yes, everyone asks me why CVTD (Cache Valley Transit District) gets all
the money.” Runhaar said he will be Presenting two alternatives to the CVTD Board
tomorrow for their consideration:

1. Ask CVTD to turn back 33% of their 40% share of funding to a grant fund for
the ten cities they provide service to to be used for infrastructure resources the
cities don’t have such as sidewalk connections, ADA accessible ramps, bike
lanes, etc. — transit related purposes.

2. Continue to push for legislation that will change the transit district's
percentage to 10%. This money could be used for anything to do with roads.

Director Runhaar will be meeting with CVTD tomorrow at 5:45 p.m. at the west side of
Bridgerland Technical College. Runhaar recommends pushing for the legislation change
even if CVTD accepts Alternative 1.
