CC:  Date: April 8, 2021
Re: HMB Zoning Map Amendment Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment for approximately 0.99 acres located on the east side of 300 E, at approximately 13785 S 300 E. The property is currently zoned RA1 . The applicant is requesting that a Zoning Map Amendment be approved to change the zoning of the property from RA1 to RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 ft² lot minimum size) for the purpose of a future subdivision. The findings are as follows: For a positive recommendation:
1.   That DCMC Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the zoning text and maps of the DCMC.
2.   That this map amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
3.   That the proposed land use map amendment complies with DCMC Section 9-2-020(F).
For a negative recommendation:
1.   That this map amendment compromises one or more of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1). ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 4/5/2021 Cover Memo
