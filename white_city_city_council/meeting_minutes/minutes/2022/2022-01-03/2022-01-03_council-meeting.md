                                         WORKSHOP

Review of the 2021 White City Metro Township General Plan

              The Council reviewed the 2021 White City Metro Township General Plan and
made the following recommendations:

Chapter Six: Lifelong Communities

       Page 100 – Governance and Services

          •   In paragraph 2, remove the word “fun” and “such as the car show” in the sentence
              “They also support the White City Community Council, which plans fun events,
              such as the car show, that bring the community together.

       Page 101 – Economic Opportunities

          •   Add “approximately” where it refers to 119 households.

          •   Replace the word “without” with “no access” so that the statement is reflective.

          •   Paragraph 3, remove the word “increase” with language like “actively working to
              increase access”.

       Page 101 - Resilience and Sustainability

       •   Paragraph 1, remove the word “equitably”.

       •   Paragraph 2 talks about civil unrest; however, White City did not experience civil
           unrest. The language should say “may experience”

       •   Paragraph 3, remove the word “facilitate” to “encourage”.


   Page 110 – Who Provides What?

       •   Paragraph 1, clarify that there are five council members and a mayor that is elected
           based on the vote of the council.

       •   Should “Water: Jordan Valley Conservancy” be included in the list.

   Page 105 – Existing Conditions

       •   Bullet point 3, change the word “is” directly derived from… to “was” directly derived
           from…

       •   Bullet point 4, the description looks more like a history of Salt Lake County and not
           reflective of White City Metro Township.

       •   Bullet point 5, of the possible 50 zones, White City only uses approximately five.

   Page 108 – Examples of Age-Friendly and Accessible Places

       •   Under the picture titled “Example of a kitchen sink and cabinetry created to
           universal design standards. Photo credit: Kitchen and Bath Design News.” The
           word “kitchen is misspelled”.

   Page 109 – Economic Opportunities

       •   Add context to the SWOT and APAE Analyses paragraph.

   Page 109 – Existing Conditions

       •   Bullet point 7, should the sentence “None of White City access the highest
           broadband speeds” be changed since White City is getting Google Fiber.

   Page 111 – Case Study: Cup of Joes, Orangeville, Utah

       •   Remove the picture that has the “Cup of Joe” sign in the front yard.

   Page 112 – Tapestries: A Market Approach to White City’s Demographics

             scale home-based business”.

      Page 113 – Digital Infrastructure

         •   Edit language to indicate White City is moving forward to allow for higher speed
             broadband.
