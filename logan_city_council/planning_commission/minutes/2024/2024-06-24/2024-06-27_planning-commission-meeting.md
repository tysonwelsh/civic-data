PC 24-024 Schmidt Home STR [Conditional Use Permit] Thomas N & Megan S Schmidt, property
owners are requesting a conditional use permit to operate a 4-bedroom short-term rental using the
entire home located at 425 North 400 East in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone;
TIN 06-039-0003 (Adams Neighborhood).

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
available in the Community Development Department.

2. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a four (4) bedroom short-term rental occupying the full
single-family home.

3. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes two (2) people per bedroom with two (2) additional
individuals for a maximum of ten (10) occupants in the STR.

4. A short-term rental may not be the subject of multiple rental contracts by separate individuals,
groups, or parties for the same night or nights.

5. A minimum of four (4) parking stalls are required for the STR. All parking areas shall be
located off-street, outside the front and corner yard setback and paved with an approved hard
surface. The four (4) total parking stalls shall meet minimum LDC dimensions (9x18) per stall
and a minimum 12-foot-wide driveway. Two (2) cars parked side by side in the garage and
two (2) cars parked bumper to bumper in front of the garage, keeping the 25-foot front
setback. The driveway needs to be widened to meet the 12-foot minimum width, keeping at
least a 2-foot side setback.

6. Parking of recreational vehicles, trailers, boats, ATVs, or similar vehicles associated with the

STR shall be legally parked on-site and are not permitted to be parked on-street.

Rental contracts for the STR shall be for 30 days or less.

Rental contracts shall not be offered to the same individual, group, or party for more than two

consecutive rental contracts in a six (6) month period. The owner shall maintain rental records

available for review and inspection by Logan City.

9. Quiet times shall comply with the City noise ordinance of 10 pm to 8 am.

PC 24-023 Weston Hilton Home 2 -Continued from the June 13" meeting. [Design Review
Permit] Christian Wilson/Matthew Weston, 87 West Partners LLC, & Blue Creek Lodging LLC,
authorized agent/owner is requesting a design review permit for a new 61,496 square foot, four-story
Hilton Hotel with 107 guest rooms located at 70 West 1200 South in the Commercial (COM) zone;
TIN 02-088-0009; -0008; -0030 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

Staff: Russ Holley, Planner reviewed the request for a design review permit for a new hotel located
at 70 West 1200 South in the Commercial (COM) zone on a vacant parcel just south of Wal-Mart and
West of Les Schwab that borders Providence City along the south property line.

The Land Development Code (LDC) requires 111 parking stalls for the proposed project (one parking
stall per hotel room plus four additional parking stalls for employees) as proposed with 108 parking
stalls the project is 3 parking stalls short of meeting the requirement.


The hotel is a four-story building that is 43 feet tall and meets the design standards, fenestration,
setbacks, and open space requirements of the LDC.

The LDC requires the building frontage (building mass to lot width ratio) to be at a minimum of 50%
at the front setback line. Without considering the west fagade entry canopy structure, the building

frontage is proposed at 40%. If the Planning Commission considers the canopy, building frontage
would equal 55%.

R. Holley reviewed a comment received from the Providence City Manager regarding the sewer
system capacity in this area and requesting the addition of a new north-south street with the proposed
project. R. Holley explained that the sewer line in this area is shared in capacity, Logan City is
allocated a certain amount of capacity and Providence City is allocated a certain amount of capacity.
Logan City’s capacity is sufficient and can handle the addition of the proposed hotel to the sewer
system. R. Holley also explained that no new streets are proposed at this location in Logan City’s
Master Transportation Plan or Providence City’s Master Transportation Plan.

Commissioner Peterson asked R. Holley to go over condition number 5. R. Holley explained that
condition number 5 requires the sidewalk to continue through both proposed entrances to prioritize
pedestrian safety.

Commissioner Guth asked if both proposed accesses are approved with the proposed project. R.
Holley said that the second access to the west is approved if the future development to the west uses
that access.

Proponent: Christian Wilson, the architect on the proposed project, said they are willing to add the
three additional parking stalls and continue the sidewalk through both entrances. Mr. Wilson asked
the Commission to count the front canopy towards the frontage requirement.

Public: No public comments were made.

Commission: Commission Guth asked what the frontage is on the other businesses in the area.
Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director said that the only business in the area that have
frontage is the medical complex building on the corner of 1200 South and Main Street and the Truck
Ranch on the north side of 1200 South. There is no frontage along Main Street or directly across the
street from the proposed project on the Wal-Mart property. Commissioner Lucero said she is fine
with the proposed design and the front canopy counting toward the frontage.

Darren Farar, Logan City Engineer said there is a concern with the sewer main along 1200 South
and Providence City has an agreement with Logan City to upgrade the sewer line to meet their
capacity needs. D. Farar also spoke about the possibility of the commission adding a stub for a
future street.

The Commission determined that the west entrance canopy will count towards the frontage and that
a stub will not be required because no future streets are planned for this area.
