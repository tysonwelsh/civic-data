   5. PUBLIC HEARING: Considering changes to the City’s Annexation Area Policy
       Plan & Map:
          a. Open public hearing
             TIME: 7:36 PM

          b. Staff Presentation
             Mr. Seth Atkinson gave the presentation. The main change is coming from the
             City Council. This change will aid in an informed decision regarding expansion of
             the city's municipal boundaries, concept plans to show intended uses and a
             potential street layout must be presented to the city council, giving the council a
             better idea of what's being proposed if there's a developer involved, and helps us
             in that analysis on the level two.

              The map will show in better detail the changes and stages as proposed (Please see
              Attachment 1). Orange is level three, Purple is a level two, Green is a level one.
              Green meaning it has been through an analysis and is ready for petition of
              annexation. Purple or level two indicates that the analysis has not been done, and
              there may be some issues that will need to be worked out before moving to level
              one. These areas are typically closer to city boundaries. Orange or Level Three
              indicates that there are issues in topography making it harder to annex at this time.

              It is proposed that the lighter orange areas become additional areas to the
              annexation areas, due to coming extension of the infrastructure and making them
              more of a possibility. Currently the staff recommends that the indicated areas be
              at a level three. On the North side of the city the current boundaries, these areas
              the staff are recommending that they be moved into level two at the request of
