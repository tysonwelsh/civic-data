City Council Meeting Minutes April 9, 2024

Prayer, Pledge of Allegiance, and Introductions
Council Chair Ryan Mecham welcomed those in attendance.
Shane Pace, CAO, offered the Prayer.
Council Member Zach Robinson led the Pledge.
Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda.

General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)
Council Chair Ryan Mecham invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment.
Ms. Joyce Walker with the Dimple Dell Preservation Community invited the City Council,
Administration and the community to participate in the Annual Dimple Dell clean up effort
on April 27th. Volunteers will be spread across ten work zones. She provided additional
details about the event.
Mr. Dave Egelund expressed concerns related to emergency preparedness and is
supportive of increasing the staffing of the city emergency managment department
Ms. Kirby Croyle expressed her support of Ranked Choice Voting and asked the Council
to consider utilizing RVC in municipal elections.
Public comment closed.
Council moved to Standing Reports: Mayor's Report.

Council Business

Special Recognition

1. 24-136 U.S. Marshal's Service presenting Sandy City Police Department with the
Agency of the Year Award for the District of Utah.
Justin Martinez with the U.S. Marshals Service District of Utah presented Chief Severson
and the Sandy Police Department with the 2023 U.S. Marshals Task Force Agency of the
Year Award. This award was presented in recognition of their dedicated service to the
U.S. Marshals, District of Utah, and the Violent Fugitive Apprehension Strike Team. Their
experience, work ethic, teamwork, and dedication to protecting our communities has
been a great asset to VFAST. You have represented Sandy City with distinction, honor,
and integrity. Our partnership has resulted in safe communities, bringing fugitives to
justice, and friendships that will last a lifetime. Thank you for your service and sacrifice to
maintain law and order.
Chief Severson thanked the U.S. Marshals Office for this prestigious award and
expressed pride in his departments Task Force Team for their dedication, honor, and
service to our community. Mayor Zoltanski also acknowledged and thanked our police
task force and department for providing the highest level of commitment and excellence to
our community.

City Council Meeting Minutes April 9, 2024
Informational Items
2. 24-135 American West Symphony providing the Council with their annual update
Attachments: American West Symphony
Charlotte Jordan, Chair of the American West Symphony, provided a year in review of the
American West Symphony and Chorus. The AWSC is an all volunteer symphony and
choir providing free concerts and educational opportunities for our community youth, and
is now in its 36th season. The AWSC also participates in the Cultural Exchange with our
Sister City, Riesa, Germany and Ms. Jordan has been active as the Community Liaison
for that program for over a decade.
Council thanked Ms. Jordan for her many years of service to both the AWSC and the
Sister City program and honored her with flowers and a small gift. Mayor Zoltanski
honored and congratulated Ms. Jordan as the 2024 recipient of the One Sandy Award for
Arts and Cultural.

City Council Meeting Minutes April 9, 2024

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report
Dustin Fratto, Council Director, briefed the Council on upcoming agenda items. There is a
tour scheduled for April 23, 2024. Currently, the meeting on April 30th is cancelled
because it is the fifth meeting of the month.

Council Member Business
Council Member Cyndi Sharkey participated in a simulation coordinated by Salt Lake
County on homeless reintegration. It was a very eye opening and educational workshop
and experience. She provided an update from the Alta Canyon Advisory Committee and
appreciates their feedback and guidance. She expressed support for the PCG Consulting
firm and is looking forward to getting their full report in a few months.
Council Member Brooke D'Sousa thanked Administration for coordinating the tours of
recreation centers. She found the tours informative and helpful. She spoke about next
steps for Alta Canyon Recreation Center.
Council Member Alison Stroud thanked Mike Wilcox and Jake Warner for their help with
a recent concern.
Council Member Ryan Mecham also participated in the tours of recreation facilities and
found the tours and the insight from Martin Jensen, Deputy CAO very valuable. He met
with PCG Consulting and is looking forward to their report later this year.
Council Member Marci Houseman thanked lan Williams, Animal Services Director, for his
responsiveness to a recent issue in the community. She also thanked Tom Ward and
Mike Gladbach for their help with a resident's concern. She spoke about a proclamation
for the Week of they Young Child and asked the Council to consider involvement in the
HomeAid Utah summer diaper drive event that will take place on August 23, 2024.
Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledged James Sorenson for his help with a
resident's concern.
Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

Mayor's Report
Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the signing of the bond for the new Fire Station 31, and the
$21 million dollar commitment. She thanked the residents for their support of this
important project. The groundbreaking of the fire station will take place later this month.
She also spoke to Glacier Hills Elementary School students and thanked Council
Member Stroud for joining her.

CAO Report
Shane Pace, CAO, updated the Council on some upcoming Sandy City events: May 4th
is the Fiesta 5K. May 11th is the Wildland Open House. May 18th is the Sandy
Beautification Day. The community is invited to participate in all of these events.

Adjournment
Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:22 pm.
