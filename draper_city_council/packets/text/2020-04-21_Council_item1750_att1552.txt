                                     RESOLUTION NO. #20-20

                      A RESOLUTION OF DRAPER CITY ADOPTING
                THE 2019 SALT LAKE COUNTY HAZARD MITIGATION PLAN

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council recognizes the threat that natural hazards pose to people
and property within Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City has participated in the creation of a multi-hazard mitigation plan,
hereby known as the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan in accordance with the Disaster
Mitigation Act of 2000; and

        WHEREAS, the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan identifies mitigation goals and
actions to reduce or eliminate long-term risk to people and property in Draper City from the impacts of
future hazards and disasters; and

        WHEREAS, adoption by the Draper City Council demonstrates their commitment to hazard
mitigation and achieving the goals outlined in the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1.     The Draper City Council hereby adopts the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard
Mitigation Plan.

       Section 2.     This Resolution shall be effective upon passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THE _______ DAY OF ______________, 2020.
