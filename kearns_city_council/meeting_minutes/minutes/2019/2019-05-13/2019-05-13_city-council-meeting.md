Salt Lake County Library

                 Matt McLain, Salt Lake County Library Services Division, updated the Council on
the following:

    The new library is 8.25 percent completed. The old building has been demolished and the

    Story time at the Kearns Oquirrh Fitness Center has been changed to Wednesdays.

Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Chief Brian Lohrke updated the Council on the following:

      National Police Week honoring fallen officers is coming up, 371 names will be added to

      Kearns precinct is financially healthy at 12 percent under budget.

      A public hearing was held relating to body cameras where people expressed the desire
       to keep them. UPD has not decided yet, but is leaning towards keeping these cameras.

Council Business

Dog and Cat Ordinance

               Council Member Peterson stated the current ordinance does not limit the number
of cats or dogs a person may have if all animals are properly licensed and cared for. Surrounding
cities have established limits on the number of animals a person may own. The Council could
come up with its own limits and after a public hearing it could adopt a new ordinance.
