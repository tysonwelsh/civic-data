                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 20-11

  AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 18.70.070, 18.85.070, AND 18.100.070 OF
   THE RIVERTON CITY CODE, ADDRESSING SIDEWALK AND FRONTAGE
     IMPROVEMENTS IN CERTAIN COMMERCIAL ZONES, AMENDMENTS
                   PROPOSED BY RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed amendment to the Riverton City Code; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City Code Sections 18.70.070, 18.85.070, and 18.100.070 as
described in Exhibit “A”.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City Code shall be, and hereby is, amended as shown in Exhibit
       “A”

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 21st day of

Exhibit “A”

   C-G Zone, 18.70.070
   (3) Streetscape. The public right-of-way, which includes the back of curb, park strip, and sidewalk, shall
   be designed with a meandering straight sidewalk in the C-G zone. Within the park strip, one-and-one-
   half-inch caliper trees shall be planted every 25 feet on center according to the streetscape master
   plan. Behind the sidewalk, the landscaping plan is encouraged to include berms that rise one foot over
   every three feet in width.

   C-R Zone, 18.85.070
   (2) Streetscape. The public right-of-way, which includes the back of curb, park strip, and sidewalk, shall
   be designed with a meandering straight sidewalk in the C-R zone. Within the park strip, one-and-one-
   half-inch caliper trees shall be planted every 25 feet on center according to the streetscape master
   plan. Behind the sidewalk, the landscaping plan is encouraged to include berms that rise one foot over
   every three feet in width.

   C-R Zone, 18.100.070
   (2) Streetscape. The public right-of-way, which includes the back of curb, park strip, and sidewalk, shall
   be designed with a meandering straight sidewalk in the PCC zone. Within the park strip, one-and-one-
   half-inch-caliper trees shall be planted every 25 feet on center according to the streetscape master
   plan. Behind the sidewalk, the landscaping plan is encouraged to include berms that rise one foot over
   every three feet in width.
