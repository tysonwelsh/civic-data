                           Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                     April 9, 2024
                                       7:00 p.m.
                                      21 E 100 N
                                   Nephi, UT 84648

1.    Economic Develop Director Brent Boswell reviewed with the Council the following
     topics: data center recruitment, Utah Inland Port, and cottage businesses. Mr. Boswell
     and the Council discussed adopting an ordinance that would make it lawful to have retail
     food sales out of the home.

2.    Mr. Brandon Peterson addressed the council and stated that he has been working
     since October on organizing a Juab Community Orchestra. He has been having
     rehearsals at the JFAC auditorium in the Juab County Building. Each week they have
     had more participants, and he reported that they are really starting to sound like an
     orchestra. The group has been having trouble scheduling rehearsal time in the
     auditorium. Mr. Peterson’s goal is to provide a space for people that know how to play
     an instrument so they can continue to use their talents and to eventually be able to
     perform for the community. Recreation Director Craig Oswald has been working with
     Mr. Peterson to have the community orchestra be under the Nephi Recreation umbrella.
     The recreation department would provide advertisement and space for rehearsals. The
     name of the orchestra would be the Nephi City Orchestra. The Council agreed to lend
     their support and approval for this partnership and programming for the Nephi City
     Orchestra.

3. Pool/Facilities Manager Clark Wood introduced Mr. Kevin Parker from Stratton & Bratt.
   Mr. Parker explained and demonstrated a technology that uses salt, water, and
   electricity to produce chlorine for pools. This technology would replace the need to buy
   and store chlorine for the city pool. The equipment cost with the installation would be
   around $37,000 but would drastically reduce chemical costs. The life of the system is
   only 10 years, but the estimated payback is around six years. Mr. Parker did not
   recommend the use of this system for the splash pad but only for the pool. Mr. Wood
   was concerned about if the City was looking to build a new pool down the road it may
   not be wise to purchase a new system now. The council stated that they trusted the
   advice from staff on whether to purchase the new salt producing chlorine system.

4. Craig Oswald informed the Council that the contracting with Mr. Travis Story to run
   tournaments at the Hive had not been successful. Mr. Story has had to cancel several
   scheduled tournaments due to the lack of participation. Mr. Atkinson had spoken to Mr.
   Story and Mr. Story had stated that many outside events had conflicted with the
   scheduling of his tournaments. Mr. Oswald stated that he and his staff have talked
   about running some volleyball tournaments and even partnering with USA wrestling to
   run some of their tournaments. Mayor Seely expressed his concern about overwhelming
   the recreation department staff with more responsibility.

5. Mr. Atkinson said that he has had a request from an individual that wants to lease the
   final spot at the airport. The individual has asked for a thirty-year lease instead of our
   current term of twenty years. The council directed staff to keep with the twenty-year
   lease agreement.

6. Administration and City Council Updates:
     a. Mr. Atkinson gave the following updates:
             i.   He will not be to next weeks meeting.
            ii.   The gas department will be firing up the gas to the power plant this next
                  week.
           iii.   The holes in the old water tank have been filled with cement and UDOT
                  will put dirt on top of the tanks to prohibit access to the tanks.
     b. Mayor Seely reported that he had attended the LIC meeting and said that he had
         learned that early childhood physical and verbal development has been highly
         deterred because of too much screen time. He had asked Craig Oswald how
         Nephi Recreation could address this concern. Mr. Oswald said he does have
         some programming for preschoolers and could look to add programming that
         would help with physical activity among this age group.
     c. Councilor Travis Worwood reported on the meeting with the Juab County
         officials. He said that the City should be able to get the plat data we have
         requested from the county with us passing on the cost of the data to new
         development and then passing the funds collected onto the county.

7. Councilor Callaway moved to convene into regular meeting and then convene into
   executive session to discuss the character and competence of an individual. Councilor
   Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 8:15 p.m.

8.   9:15 p.m. work session reconvened.

9. Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the Council employee compensation market adjustments and
   inflationary adjustments for the police department and the electrical department.

10. The Council discussed raising the scholarship awards for the Miss Nephi Pageant.
    Mayor Seely proposed that the Nephi City enterprise funds sponsor some scholarships
    at the pageant.

11. Mr. Atkinson updated the Council about the storm water problem in Chapel View Estates
    and Ace Hardware store. He stated that Public Works Director Kyle Marchant has
    designed a new way to direct the stormwater to a new sump.

12. Councilor Travis Worwood moved to adjourn the work session Councilor Cowan
    seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote. The meeting adjourned
    at 9:45 p.m.
