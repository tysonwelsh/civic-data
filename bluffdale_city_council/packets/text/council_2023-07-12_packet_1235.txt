13   A question that always comes up when a property tax increase is proposed is whether the City
14   receives more taxes as the value of homes increases. Because of Truth in Taxation, Bluffdale is
15   prohibited from obtaining any windfall revenue as a result of reevaluation or equalization projects
16   according to State law. This means that the mathematical calculation is that when property values
17   rise, property tax rates must fall. The opposite is also true, which occurs occasionally. When
18   property values decrease, the property tax rate goes up. The adjusted tax rate each year is called
19   the Certified Tax Rate. In 2023, the Certified Rate was .001161, however, because property taxes
20   increased, it went down to .000976. In 2023, the Police and Fire fund did not exist, so the rate was
21   zero. This year, they are proposing the General Fund Certified Tax Rate be .000976, which is what
22   the State gave them. This means that there will be no property tax increase and no related Truth
23   in Taxation Hearing. The Fire and Police Protection Fund Certified Rate is.000260, which raises
24   just under $570,000.

BACKGROUND:
The City’s Redevelopment Agency is investing in an industrial park revitalization project from 14600 South to
Noell Nelson Drive which is facilitating the conversion of an existing private gravel road to a paved public street.
Phase 2 of the RDA project – converting 14730 South to a public road from 855 West to Noell Nelson Drive – is a
multi-faceted endeavor. This section has five property owners, a significant amount of property acquisition,
affects two operating businesses, and is being designed while an existing site plan application has already been
submitted for the subject project. The new public road project was requested by the City Council and RDA to be
wider than phase 1 to better accommodate heavy commercial traffic and has been designed with a 59 feet right
of way, expanding its historical footprint. The new public road will also bring necessary public utilities to a
currently unserved area of Bluffdale.

This development agreement request has been proposed to address development related issues resulting from
the road project impacts. The applicant’s current development proposal has been required to be modified due
to the road right of way expansion. Ongoing discussions between the City and the applicant identified several
land use provisions that need modifications to accommodate the road and additional real estate required.
Additionally, the City and RDA have an interest in redevelopment within the Eastern Bluffdale Economic
Development Area. General goals and objectives of the EDA are to incentivize the job growth and assessed
property tax revenue increases that a modern industrial park brings to the community, and to facilitate the
cleanup of a somewhat neglected corner of the community. The King’s Cross development has proposed
approximately 92,000 square feet of new heavy commercial and light industrial space to the community.

Because this project is complicated, a development agreement has been deemed an appropriate vehicle to
address several issues and concerns simultaneously to keep both the road and development project going.
Land use regulations, processing requirements, construction timing, and real estate acquisition provisions are
included within the proposed agreement. Utah State Code section 10-9a-532 addresses development
agreement processing requirements. When a development agreement proposes zoning modifications that
differ from the underlying zone, the agreement is treated as a land use ordinance amendment for noticing and
processing purposes. Development agreements are a tool which allows the City to address unique
circumstances based on findings for a particular property.

SUMMARY
The City’s Redevelopment Agency is investing in an industrial park revitalization project from 14600 South to
Noell Nelson Drive which is facilitating the conversion of an existing private gravel road to a paved public
street. Phase 1 of the project has been completed, obtaining right of way dedication and completing 855
West from 14600 S to 14730 S. This section of the project has been a catalyst for the new Covent Garden
industrial flex space project, which has been received quite well by the business community. Phase 2 of the
RDA project – converting 14730 South to a public road from 855 West to Noell Nelson Drive – is more
complicated. This section has five property owners, a significant amount of property acquisition, affects two
operating businesses, and is being designed while an existing site plan application has already been
submitted for the subject project. The existing 855 W gravel road resides within a historical 50 feet right of
way; however the new public road project was requested by the City Council and RDA to be wider to better
accommodate heavy commercial traffic. This next phase is designed with 59 feet right of way, expanding its
footprint. The new public road will also bring necessary public utilities to a currently unserved area of
Bluffdale.

This development agreement request has been proposed to address development related issues resulting
from the road project impacts. The applicant’s current development proposal has been required to be

                                                                           Application 2023-16: Kings Cross Brixton DA

modified due to the road right of way expansion. Ongoing discussions between the City and the applicant
identified several land use provisions that need modifications to accommodate the road and additional real
estate required. Additionally, the City and RDA have an interest in redevelopment within the Eastern
Bluffdale Economic Development Area. General goals and objectives of the EDA are to incentivize the job
growth and assessed property tax revenue increases that a modern industrial park brings to the community,
and to facilitate the cleanup of a somewhat neglected corner of the community. The King’s Cross
development has proposed approximately 92,000 square feet of new heavy commercial and light industrial
space to the community.

Because this project is complicated, a development agreement has been deemed an appropriate vehicle to
address several issues and concerns simultaneously to keep both the road and development project going.
Land use regulations, processing requirements, construction timing, and real estate acquisition provisions
are included within the proposed agreement. Utah State Code section 10-9a-532 addresses development
agreement processing requirements. When a development agreement proposes zoning modifications that
differ from the underlying zone, the agreement is treated as a land use ordinance amendment for noticing
and processing purposes. Development agreements are a tool which allows the City to address unique
circumstances based on findings for a particular property.

new development.

was improved in connection with Covent Garden, the City also desires to continue enhancing the
value of this area by improving and widening 14730 South. The improvements to 855 West and

accelerate the redevelopment efforts and attract more development and businesses into this area.

materially impact the Project’s ability to comply with the City development standards while
maintaining a viable first-class development. The Property’s location at the back of this
