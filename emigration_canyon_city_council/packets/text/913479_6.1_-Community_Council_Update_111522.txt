Community Council Update 111522
Three new members are joining the community council following last week’s election: Lesley
Bagley from Pinecrest, Karen Mowrer from Emigration Oaks, and Kurt Wagner from Killyons.
We’ve begun the process of filling council offices and jobs that have been vacant for some time.
With Steve Borst’s decision to remain on the council we’re down to two vacant memberships.

Last month’s roadside fuel reduction project in Killyons Canyon was halted prematurely when
the UFA fuels crew reached the end of its seasonal funding and disbanded. Perhaps two thirds
of the intended scope for this project remains to be completed when the crew reforms next
spring.

Our CERT relaunch still awaits a county decision on training class scheduling which is expected
to begin sometime after the first of the year. Sign-ups for the team are now up to 20 volunteers
and recruiting continues for member-trainers to assist UFA personnel during the training
sessions.

A joint planning session with UFA and the state Division of Forestry, Fire and State Lands has
been scheduled for January 10 to update the township’s Community Wildfire Preparedness
Plan and establish the work plan for 2023 fuel reduction projects.

Our Firewise recertification application will be submitted this week.

Bill Tobey
ECCC Chair
