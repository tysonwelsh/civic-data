2. Salt Lake         County         Public        Works        Operations           Snowplow               Presentation;           Steven      Kuhlmeier,
   Division        Director
   Steven Kuhlmeier                said the operations              team consisted                of 81 department           employees            operating
   60 large plow            trucks.      The team is on standby                  24 hours           a day to address         snow and ice events
   within      36 hours         after the end of a storm.               The county's               snow control          policy     establishes           priority
   roads: major           arterials,       minor     arterials,      collector         streets, remaining           streets,       and cul-de-sacs                  and
   dead ends. Dispatch                 monitors         weather      for prompt           action.      Personnel         are dispatched           when a
   roadway        hazard        is anticipated          (pre-storm       brine,        plowing,        and salt distribution).              He showed                 the
   plow      routes map and schedule                     for Millcreek          with      a main      shift (12am-3pm),               night     shift      (3ptn-
   12am),       and sidewalks              (12am-3pm).            The night       operations           focus on critical           routes      and are
   comprised         of reduced            crews.


   Kulhi'neier          addressed        the many         challenges       plow         drivers      face: cars parked            on the road, people
   blowing        snow back into the road, timing                       of storms           and traffic,        trees that are not trimmed,
   garbage       day, and storage               space for snow.          If cars are parked                 on the road, the drivers               will        not
   plow      it. He showed             a video      highlighting        the difficulty             of storing     snow in cul-de-sacs.                  He
   asked residents              to not park on the street, understand                            smaller     roads are lower          priority,      be
   patient      in large snow events,                not blow        snow in the road, put snow downhill                             when       cleaning
   driveways,           and understand             emergency         plowing        versus "pretty"             plowing.          There     are online
   tools for tracking             snowplows             and to report      concerns.             He reported        in 2023,       17,500       tons of salt
   was used for snow and ice removal.                             In 2022, it was 35,000.


   Mayor        Silvestrini       appreciated           the county's       work.         He asked about employee                     turnover.
