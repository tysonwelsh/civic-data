Chair Watts called the meeting to order at 7:06 p.m.

                        March 24, 2021

Development and Renewal Agency Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary,
if after ten days, there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes

    2. Discussion and Action Items
           2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution outlining the assignment of rights and
                obligations of the Herriman Business Center Participation Agreement – Blake Thomas,
                Community Development Director
Community Development Director Blake Thomas indicated this participation agreement currently allows funds
collected in the designated Herriman business Center project area to be accessed by the developer to install
infrastructure improvements to support new development. The infrastructure identified in the participation
agreement has mostly been installed. This agreement would require the City to reimburse actual costs incurred by
the developer. As per the Rosecrest Trust Agreement, they have requested to transfer their assignment of rights to
other entities. 7/8ths would need to be allocated to Sorensen Legacy Foundation and 1/8th to Sorensen Impact
Foundation.

Director Smith moved to approve Resolution R2021-01 agreeing to the assignment of rights and obligations under the participation
agreement for the Herriman Business Center Community Development Project Area. Director Ohrn seconded the motion.

The vote is recorded as follows:
Director Jared Henderson                          Absent
Director Sherrie Ohrn                             Aye
Director Steven Shields                           Aye
Director Clint Smith                              Aye
Chair David Watts                                 Aye
The motion passed unanimously with Director Henderson being absent.

           2.2. Discussion of a proposal to adjust the Herriman Business Center boundaries – Heather
                Upshaw, Economic Development Manager
Economic Development Manager Heather Upshaw explained there are two boundary adjustment proposals within
the Herriman Business Center Community Development Area (CDA). The first is for the REAL Salt Lake Training
Academy. The parcel was recently subdivided which ultimately caused a portion of the parcel to reside outside the
project area boundary which would cause issues with the City receiving Tax Increment Funding allocations.

The second adjustment proposal is due to the proposed land use changes with the Rosecrest Master Development
Area. As the development plans have progressed, the landowner has proposed to relocate a portion of the CDA to
an area more readily suited for commercial development. The current property is included in the R-2-15 zoning and
would be presented to the Planning Commission of April 1, 2021. This boundary change would also require a
public hearing to change the zoning from mixed use to commercial. She asked the Board if they were supportive of
relocating the commercial property and starting the process. The Board agreed.

    3. Adjournment
Director Ohrn moved to adjourn the Community and Development Renewal Agency meeting at 7:13 p.m. Director Smith seconded the
motion, and all voted aye.
