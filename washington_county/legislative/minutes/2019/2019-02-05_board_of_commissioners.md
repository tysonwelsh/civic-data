CONSIDERATION _OF_ ORDINANCE _ NO. _2019-1131-O, AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING TITLE 1, CHAPTER 15 OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY CODE

Washington County Deputy Attorney Eric Clarke explained that under state law, the
County has the option of adding additional insurance coverage or bonding for the potential
risk of elected officials performing any acts that are not in the best interest of the public.
The Utah Counties Indemnity Pool (UCIP) insurance ensures that the County is covered
under the limits of the law and the ordinance specifies the amounts that are being covered
for each elected official. The exception to this is the Treasurer, who is governed by the
State Money Management Act.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2019-1131-
O, An Ordinance Amending Title 1, Chapter 15 of the Washington
County Code. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and
Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION _NO._R-2019-2431, A RESOLUTION
IMPLEMENTING FRAUD PREVENTION RECOMMENDATIONS

Mr. Clarke explained that the Utah State Auditor’s Office issued a report on internal
controls for the Washington County government that contained five recommendations to
enhance the County’s fraud prevention efforts.

Recommendations include: Washington County Employees will be obligated to report an
abuse of power or fraud; there will be a button added to the Washington County
Clerk/Auditor website for citizens to report concerns; the County Commissioners will
administratively meet with the Clerk/Auditor and an external auditor to discuss any
findings; the Human Resources Department will ensure that County employees regularly
receive ethics training; and every four (4) years the Commissioners will take an elected
officials training provided by the State Auditor’s Office.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO._R-2019-2435, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON
COUNTY AND THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
REGARDING THE REBUILDING OF THE LOCAL ROAD UNDER I-15 AT MP

40 AND MP 42

Washington County Engineer Todd Edwards explained that that the Utah Department of
Transportation (UDOT) will be performing work in the I-15 area at Mile Post 40 and Mile
Post 42. The roadways that pass under the freeway have historically been owned by the
County, however, there have been questions about who should maintain them.

Due to the work that will be performed by UDOT, part of the County’s road would be
damaged in the process. UDOT will assist in the rebuilding of roads, after which, the roads
will then be maintained by the County.

Commissioner Almquist questioned who maintains the liability during the construction.

Mr. Clarke explained that the liability remains with the contractor or entity who performed
the action that would put someone at risk.

Mr. Edwards clarified that UDOT would be contracting for the work, and this agreement
is for reimbursement purposes only.

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2436, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH THE
UTAH DEPARTMENT OF NATURAL RESOURCES FOR RARE PLANT
SURVEYS IN WASHINGTON COUNTY

Washington County Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan
explained that the HCP has a fund that covers rare plant surveys in the County. Surveys
will be completed to monitor listed plants that are regulated under the Endangered Species
Act including Dwarf Bear Claw Poppy, Holmgren’s Milkvetch, Gierisch’s Globemallow,
Siler’s Cactus, and Shivwits Milkvetch.

The County will provide $12,000 under which the Utah Division of Natural Resources will
conduct the surveys, and monitor the rare plants and rare plant habitat.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-

Almquist voting aye.
