SUMMARY:

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicants, Marco Hermosillo and
Richard Ortiz with Benefactor Two, LLC. On their application they describe the proposed use of the
property as:

“Development of a modern automotive dealership. With a site over 1 acre of land, with a permanent
structure that will serve as a high-quality showroom, with enclosed service bays that will help prepare
vehicles for delivery with no heavy mechanical work, professional landscape and outdoor display areas.
 The dealership will be constructed with an emphasis on architectural quality, buffering from current
residential areas.”

The subject property is two existing parcels totaling approximately 1.16-acres located at 2630 and 2610
West 12600 South. These vacant parcels are on the north side of 12600 South, near the pedestrian
bridge. The properties form an “L” shape with about 270-ft of frontage on 126th, but only about 145-ft
touching on the north adjoining property in the Country View Condominium Project.

Area Zoning is mostly commercial along 126th South with a mix of Residential Zones in the area. To
the west is C-N (Commercial Neighborhood) zoning for a small office building and a Qwest Corporation
building. To the north, the Country View Condominium Project is zoned RM-8. To the northeast is a
non-conforming 4-plex on 0.45-acres zoned RR-22. To the east is the Taylor Bike Shop muti-tenant
commercial building with C-G (Commercial Gateway) zoning. South of 12600 South is mix of single-
family residential and commercial zoning and uses.

The General Plan for properties along 12600 South, from 2700 West eastward to the canal are mostly
designated as “Business Park”, with some “Community Commercial” near 2700 West. The requested C-
G zone is consistent with the “Business Park” designation.
