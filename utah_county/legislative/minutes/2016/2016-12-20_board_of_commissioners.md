16.    APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE AN AGREEMENT WITH THE UTAH VALLEY CONVENTION
       AND VISITORS BUREAU (UVCVB) FOR THE PROMOTION OF ACTIVITIES IN THE COUNTY
(Track A, 49:51)
Commissioner Graves suggested Paragraph 2, regarding an additional payment of ‘$300,000 from Utah County’s
transient room tax (TRT) and/or tourism, recreation, cultural and convention/restaurant tax (TRCC) revenues’ to the
Utah Valley Convention and Visitors Bureau, Inc. be removed from the contract. He clarified the additional amount
would come from the TRT fund, but would like to see separate agreements for the mandated amount versus the TRT
award. The commissioner discussed an upcoming presentation to be made in February 2017 to the Utah County
Tourism Tax Advisory Board, and Commissioner Ellertson mentioned the funds are subject to approval by the
commissioners. Commissioner Graves suggested a second contract could be approved following the February 2017
presentation in order to address the second clause.

                 Commissioner Graves made the motion to approve an agreement with the Utah
                 Valley Convention and Visitors Bureau (UVCVB), striking Paragraph 2 from
                 the proposed contract, as outlined in Regular Agenda Item No. 16. The motion
                 was seconded by Commissioner Lee, and discussion continued.

(Track A, 58:30)
John Garfield, General Manager of the Provo Marriott Hotel and Conference Center, came forward to comment on
TRT and TRCC designations for hotels and restaurants. He expressed concern about the Board placing restrictions
on the Convention and Visitors Bureau which would ultimately hinder business. Mr. Garfield pointed out fifteen- to
twenty percent of TRCC monies are from travelers. Commissioner Graves replied a business plan needs to be
presented for the UVCVB to be awarded the funds, just as they Board requires of any other entity making the same
request. He repeated the additional funds for the UVCVB should be established in a separate agreement.

Commissioner Lee advised against micro-managing when it comes to marketing issues, but acknowledged the need
to follow a process. Commissioner Graves stated there is not one-hundred percent agreement among the Board that
the money has been spent in the best practices and best use for Utah County. Commissioner Ellertson summarized
approximately $1,200,000 has already been budgeted for the UVCVB, and the current discussion is centered around
an additional $300,000 for tourism promotions. He talked about designation of the TRT/TRCC funds.

and additional business plan from the Convention and Visitors Bureau before any award of extra TRT/TRCC funds.

about the significant difference in pay for employees of the CVB and employees of the Utah Valley Convention
Center even though they are performing the exact same work. He is concerned about giving money for work
without holding the CVB accountable for those funds. Commissioner Graves noted he has similar concerns with the
Justice Court judges: fines are up, revenues are down, yet they are requesting increased budget and he fundamentally
has a problem with that. Commissioner Lee proposed a substitute motion:

                                           PUBLIC COMMENTS
(Track A, 2:17:50)
Being his last meeting as Chairman of the Board of Utah County Commissioners, Commissioner Ellertson expressed
appreciation for the opportunity to have served the county. He looks forward to continuing to help, as success will
be a team effort between the commissioners, elected officials, and entire workforce of the county. Commissioner
Ellertson encouraged everyone to set aside personal aspirations and instead work together towards the long-term
best interest of the county citizens. He noted he never wanted to be a politician, and advised anyone in the field to
do their best in being objective to the issues rather than ‘play politics.’ Bureaucracy and red tape should not exist to
hinder productivity, or bog down the process of governing the county.

Commissioner Ellertson repeated the need for teamwork in coming together and healing as a county. He quoted
Rotary International principles: “Of the things we think, say, or do, we should ask ourselves ~ Is it the truth? Is it
fair to all concerned? Will it build goodwill and better friendships? Will it be beneficial to all concerned?”
Commissioner Ellertson said he tries to live by those standards, and has tried to serve with those questions in mind;
he encourages the Board to do so as well as they make future decisions. He thanked the citizens for letting him be
their commissioner.
