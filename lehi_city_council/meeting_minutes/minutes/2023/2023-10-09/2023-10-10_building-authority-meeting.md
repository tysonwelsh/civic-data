Minutes of the Regular Session of the Lehi Local Building Authority held Tuesday, October
10, 2023, at 6:30 p.m. at the Lehi City Administration Building, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah.

Members Present:        Mark Johnson, Chairman
                        Paige Albrecht, Member
                        Mike Southwick, Member
                        Chris Condie, Member
                        Paul Hancock, Member
                        Katie Koivisto, Member


Others Present: Jason Walker, City Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney, Marlin Eldred,
Economic Development Director; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Teisha Wilson, City Recorder.

1. Welcome and Roll Call
   Chairman Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all members were present. Councilor
   Koivisto joined virtually.

2. Regular Agenda
   2.a) Public Hearing and Consideration of Resolution #L2023-01, approval of the Lehi City
   Local Building Authority Budget for FY 2024.
   Dean Lundell stated that the Council saw this in the Lehi City Budget when it was adopted in
   May, but stated that there needs to be a separate meeting to officially approve that budget.

   Mayor Johnson opened the public hearing. There were no comments.

   Motion:     Mr. Southwick moved to approve Resolution # Resolution #L2023-01, approval of
               the Lehi City Local Building Authority Budget for FY 2024. Ms. Koivisto seconded
               the motion.

   Roll Call Vote: Mr. Hancock, Yes; Mr. Southwick, Yes; Mr. Condie, Yes; Ms. Koivisto, Yes;
   Ms. Albrecht, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the Local Building Authority at this time, Mr.
   Southwick moved to adjourn the meeting. Mr. Hancock seconded the motion. The motion
   passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:40 p.m.
