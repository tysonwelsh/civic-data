                                      RESOLUTION NO. CRA 21-06

        A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE ADOPTED BUDGET OF THE COMMUNITY
        REINVESTMENT AGENCY OF DRAPER CITY BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL
        YEAR 2021-2022.


        WHEREAS, the Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City has adopted Resolution No.
CRA 21-04 which adopted the final budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2021 and ending June 30,
2022, in accordance with the requirements of the Utah Code Annotated; and

         WHEREAS, the Community Reinvestment Agency Board of Draper City wishes to amend the
fiscal year 2021-2022 budget; and

         WHEREAS, a public hearing to consider the appropriations has been noticed and held and all
interested persons were heard, for or against the appropriations; and

         WHEREAS, the Community Reinvestment Agency Board of Draper City hereby finds this action
in the best interest of the public’s health, safety and general welfare.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT
AGENCY OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1.      Purpose. The purpose of this Resolution is to amend the budget of the Community
Reinvestment Agency of Draper City, as approved and finalized by Community Reinvestment Agency of
Draper City Resolution No. RDA 21-04.

        Section 2.      Adoption of Amendments. The budget amendments attached hereto and made a
part of this Resolution shall be, and the same hereby are adopted and incorporated into the budget of
Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City, Utah for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2021 and
ending June 30, 2022, in accordance with the requirements of the Utah Code Annotated.

        Section 3.       Filing of copies of the Budget Amendments. The Budget Officer is authorized and
directed to certify and file a copy of said budget amendments in the office of the City Recorder which
amendments shall be available for public inspection.

         Section 4.        Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid, or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and
all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 5.        Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
passage.
