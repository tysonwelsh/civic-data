RESOLUTION NO. 6063
A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF SALT LAKE 
COUNTY APPROVING AND ADOPTING AN INTERLOCAL 
AGREEMENT EXECUTED BETWEEN THE COUNTY AND SALT 
LAKE CITY TO PROVIDE SIXTY -ONE THOUSAND DOLLARS 
($61,000) OF OPERATION AFGHAN RESETTLEMENT SUPPORT 
GRANT FUNDS TO SALT LAKE CITY TO SUPPORT AFGHAN 
REFUGEE RESETTLEMENT.
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County through its Office for New Americans applied 
for Grant Funding from the Open Society Foundation to provide support to 
refugees under the Operation Afghan Resettlement Support (“OARS”) 
Page 4 of 45
County Council Meeting Minutes February 28, 2023
program to assist with the resettlement of Afghan refugees in Salt Lake City 
and Salt Lake County.
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County desires to give a portion of the 
above-mentioned Grant Funding to Salt Lake City, specifically the Salt Lake 
City Know Your Neighbor Program, to assist with assimilation and 
welcoming the refugees.
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County and Salt Lake City have determined, through 
their governing bodies, that it would be in the best interest of the contracting 
governmental entities, and the inhabitants thereof, to enter into an interlocal 
agreement whereby Salt Lake County will provide sixty -one thousand dollars 
($61,000) of the OARS program Grant Funds to Salt Lake City of which the 
City shall use not more than sixty thousand dollars ($60,000) to hire a 
program coordinator and no more than one thousand dollars ($1,000) for 
program coordinator travel; 
WHEREAS, the Legislature of the State of Utah has provided at Utah Code 
11-13-202.5 for the adoption of interlocal agreements, pursuant to a 
resolution of the governing body of any political entity which is a party to 
such an agreement; 
WHEREAS, such an interlocal agreement has been prepared, is attached 
hereto, and is agreeable to the parties; 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED 
1. THAT the county council of Salt Lake County, Utah, hereby approves 
the attached interlocal agreement between Salt Lake County and Salt Lake 
City whereby sixty -one thousand dollars ($61,000) from the OARS grant 
funds will be provided to Salt Lake City for the purpose of supporting 
Afghan refugee resettlement as identified in the interlocal agreement.

regulations; and

Connector project; and

agreement with the City, which agreement is attached hereto as 
ATTACHMENT A  (the “Interlocal Agreement ”) to provide for the transfer
