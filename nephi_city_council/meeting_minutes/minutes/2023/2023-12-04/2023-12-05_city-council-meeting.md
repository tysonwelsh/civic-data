PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Shawny Allred, representing Branded Barber, led the pledge of allegiance and
reported on Branded Barber’s efforts to support veterans and the homeless community. Mayor Seely
complimented her on the new attractive store front for the barber shop on Main Street. Ms. Allred said
that she and the other barbers have worked together in the past and they have a desire to give back to
the community.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Parady moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting 11-21-23, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 12-5-23.
Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ABBY IVORY REPORTED ON THE GREATER NEPHI CHAMBER OF COMMERCE:

   Abby Ivory, Executive Director of the Greater Nephi Chamber of Commerce, reported to the Council
on the past six months of activity for the Chamber. Ms. Ivory had prepared a PowerPoint presentation
for the council which outlined many of the summer and fall events, upcoming events, and successes the
Chamber had accomplished. She stated that she has mentored other cities on how to have a successful
chamber. She reported that the Chamber now has 113 members. The Council thanked her for her
report and for her efforts in improving the business community in our area.

Work Session 8:30 pm

     1. Mr. Atkinson reported to the Council that city staff have been working with WCG to design the
        Crimson Flats development. Within that development, the city has plans for a police
        station. While the specifics of the building are still being worked through with the private
        partner in the Crimson Flats development, the interior of the building needs to be designed.
        The whole subdivision is in the design process. Mr. Atkinson asked the council if it would be
        okay if the City sent out an RFQ for design services for the police station. He stated that the
        capital projects fund has some funds saved up in the Government Buildings and Planning
        Projects accounts that could be used for this purpose. The council directed staff to move
        forward with the design process for the police station while the subdivision is also being
        designed and constructed.

     2.    The Council discussed the analysis of the Painter annexation request. The council would like to
          see the recommendations from the Planning Commission on the conceptual zoning plan for the
          area. The council expressed that they do not like to annex property that doesn’t have a
          definite development plan. One of the properties in the proposed annexation area does have a
          development plan but the council has not seen it and would like to before they change the
          level on the annexation area map. Councilor Worwood brought up the point of requiring water
          rights with any further annexations. Mr. Atkinson said he would work on a written policy
          requiring water rights with annexations.

     3. The council discussed the need for code governing short-term rentals. Councilor Worwood
        stated that the City should have a plan in place for when and if the Olympic games come to
        Utah again. Short-term rentals could be important to this area if the Olympic games end up in
        Utah.

     4. Mr. Atkinson explained to the Council that cities across the United States are dealing with an
        unprecedented situation with public camping. Nephi has also had issues with individuals
        setting up long-term camping on public property. With specific areas designated for camping
        in the canyon and areas around Nephi, city staff would like to propose a camping ordinance to
        encourage these activities toward designated camping areas. He presented a draft of the code
        governing camping on public and private property. Councilor Memmott did not think that
        paragraph C was clear in the way it was written. Councilor Callaway thought that prohibiting all
        sleeping in vehicles could have a negative effect on truck drivers that pull over to sleep and for
        people travelling long distances and needing to pull over and sleep to guard against drowsy
        driving. Mr. Atkinson said he would take the council’s suggestions and re-work the draft.
