BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately
1.72 acres located on the east side of 700 West, at approximately 623 W. Rolling
Hills Way (Exhibit C & D). The property is currently zoned R4. The applicant is
requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to increase the lot size of Lot 110 of
the Jenson Farms Phase 1A plat and decreasing the lot size of Lot 602 and Lot 603
of the Jenson Farms Phase 6 plat.

Lot 110 is vacant and Lots 602 & 603 have structures that are planned to be
demolished. The Phase 1A subdivision was recorded in 2019 and the Phase 6
subdivision was recorded in 2020.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the
Planning Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the
application based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed
within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they
include the following conditions of approval:

        1.      That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works,
                Building, Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the
                development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the
                site, including permitting.
        2.      That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied
                throughout the development of the site and the construction of all
                buildings on the site.
        3.      Section 17-9-070 of the Draper City Municipal Code specifies that the
                approval of a Plat Amendment expires one year from the date of
                approval if the plat has not been recorded or construction of the
