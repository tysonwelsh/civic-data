DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Patrick Romero, is requesting review for a special exception to the 30% protected
slope ordinance in the Sensitive Area Overlay Zone in order to build a gazebo and a mechanical
room, associated with a new pool, located slightly within the currently protected slope due to the
fact that the slope is not natural and has been previously disturbed.

BACKGROUND & SITE CONDITIONS
The property is located in the R-1-15 zone. The property is bordered on the north by North
Little Cottonwood Road. It is bordered on the west, south, and east by other single-family
homes in the R-1-15 zone. The subject property is approximately .34 acres in size and
currently contains an existing single-family home, as well as an accessory building already
located on the 30% sloped portion of the lot which was constructed while the property was
part of the County. The site also has an existing water feature that was installed by others,
prior to their purchase of the lot, on the 30% slope area.

The home is also located within the Sensitive Area Overlay (SAO) Zone. This zone’s purpose
is to protect the scenic character of sensitive areas, and also used to protect 30% or greater
slope areas.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject property as per
Sandy City Land Development Code requirements, to notify them of the Planning Commission
meeting. No neighborhood meeting was held for this application.

ANALYSIS
The SAO zone has a provision that states that dwellings and accessory structures must be
located an average of at least 20 feet away from the 30% slope area, with no portion of the
building or structure being closer than 10 feet from it. However, there is also a provision in
the code that authorizes the Planning Commission to grant a special exception should the
30% slope area be determined to be non-natural or previously disturbed. Additionally, it is
clear that the slope area was created by the cuts and fills required to construct North Little
Cottonwood Road.

The proposed mechanical room will encroach into the 30% slope area by approximately 4.5
feet. The applicant’s plan is to have a fully engineered retaining wall approximately four feet
tall to sufficiently locate that portion of the building within the previously disturbed slope
area.

Sec. 21-15-5. - Special Exceptions.

(b) Previously Disturbed or Developed Slopes. A property owner whose property contains or is
adjacent to 30 percent or greater slopes may request a special exception to allow
construction at reduced setbacks or no setback from the slope or on the slope. A property
owner may request the exception during the preliminary review or, upon individual lots, after
final development approval.

(1) Qualifications. Property which qualifies for the exception is limited to the following:

       a. The property contains or is adjacent to areas of 30 percent or greater slope;
       b. The slope was previously disturbed or altered;
       c. The disturbance or alteration was conducted legally either prior to the imposition of
       any sensitive area regulations on the property or was consistent with the sensitive
       area regulations in effect at the time the disturbance or alteration was conducted;
       d. The slope is stable and suitable for construction as determined by the City Engineer;
       e. Measures can be imposed which mitigate or eliminate hazards created by
       construction near to or additional disturbance or alteration of the slope;
       f. All development on the property complies with all other requirements of this title,
       such as driveway slopes and cuts and fills, maximum impervious coverage, etc.; and
       g. No other exceptions or any variances are requested or necessary.

The City Engineer has provided his recommendation for approving the special exception. The
recommendation is attached to this staff report.

   STAFF CONCERNS
   Staff has no concerns with the proposed special exception.

   STAFF RECOMMENDATION
   Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the special exception for
   encroaching into the previously disturbed 30% slope area by approximately 4.5 feet thereby
   eliminating the required setbacks at property located at 4183 E. Wildcreek Rd, based on the
   following findings and subject to the following conditions:

   Findings:
      1. That the City Engineer finds the slope to be stable and suitable for construction due to the
         amount of mature landscaping.

      2. That the City Engineer and Community Development Director recommend approval of
         the special exception.

   Conditions:
      1. That a building permit be obtained for the gazebo area and mechanical room.

      2. That all City provisions, codes and ordinances are adhered to during the
         review, construction, and operations process of this project, except as
         otherwise approved by special exceptions granted by the Planning
         Commission.

      3. That all accessory building setbacks and heights comply with all
         requirements of the R-1-15 zone.

      4. That the retaining wall be fully engineered and submitted for a building
         permit before construction begins.
