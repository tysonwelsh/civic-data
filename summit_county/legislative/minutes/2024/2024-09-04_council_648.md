Discussion and possible action regarding an appeal of a land use determination regarding the Fox Run North Lot 1 Plat Amendment; Appellant, Troy Anderson; Tiffanie Northrup-Robinson, and Helen Strachan (4:38 PM)

Tiffanie N. Robinson, Planner, provided background information and history regarding an appeal of a land use determination for the Fox Run North Lot 1 Plat amendment.
Troy Anderson and Roberta Anderson, Appellants, addressed the Council.

The item was continued to the September 25th Summit County Council meeting.
