2)
Continued public hearing from July 18, 2024, and possible action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for the property located at 7550 Lower Bowl Road, Browns Canyon, Summit County, Utah. The applicant is requesting the following uses; Rock quarries, gravel pits, and associated surface mining uses, including, but not limited to, filtering, sifting, and processing of soil, Recycling facility, Class II, Nursery/greenhouse, Contractor's yard. Parcel: SS-70-11 (44.59 acres) Applicant: Kylee Mimbach; Administrative Review. Project #23-194. Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner.
