Study Session

       Pledge of Allegiance led by Mayor Walker

       Discussion Regarding Station Area Plans
       Andrew Gruber, Wasatch Front Regional Council (WFRC) Executive Director,
       explained legislation behind station area planning, and the goal to create
       walkable city and town centers where residents could live, work, and play as
       the population continued to increase. Mr. Gruber said the four Draper Station
       Area Plans (SAPs) must be completed by the end of 2025. WFRC was tasked
       with verifying that SAPs met the objectives of the legislation. Responding to a
       question from the Council, Mr. Gruber said the State requirements were
       intended to ensure a good faith effort from cities throughout the State.

       Cameron Diehl, Utah League of Cities and Towns (ULCT) Executive Director,
       thanked Mayor Walker for his willingness to serve as the new ULCT President.
       Mr. Diehl gave a brief political history of station area plan legislation.
