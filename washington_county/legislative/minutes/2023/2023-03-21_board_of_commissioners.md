CONSIDERATION OF APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3101, A
RESOLUTION APPROVING THE COUNCIL OF GOVERNMENTS
RECOMMENDATIONS

Commissioner Iverson explained that the Council of Governments (COG) serves as a
recommending body to Washington County and oversees the expenditures of local transportation
corridor preservation funds. There are two (2) projects being recommended for funding which are:
The approval of a request to update the 2023 Master Priority Corridor Preservation Project List by
adding the following corridor: 400 West Street from 450 North to Old Dixie Highway 91. The
second request is the approval of an Ivins City funding request of $163,200 from the Construction
Grant Fund to acquire additional right-of-way for the Old Dixie Highway 91 corridor.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3101,
A Resolution Approving the Council of Governments Recommendations
Provided the Name be Changed to Old Dixie Highway 91. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3104. A
RESOLUTION APPROVING 2023 APPELLATE PUBLIC DEFENDER CONTRACTS

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that the current appellate public defender
contractor is much higher than in years past. Appellate cases require much more work than regular
cases. It is recommended to split the contract with the Indigent Defense Commission (IDC)
providing the extra moneys needed to split the contract in to two.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3104,
A Resolution Approving 2023 Appellate Public Defender Contracts. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
