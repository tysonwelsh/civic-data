Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: May 11, 2023
Re: Trailside Townhomes Plat Amendment Comments: On July 28, 2022, the Planning Commission granted approval of a plat amendment for Trailside Townhomes, located at approximately 2142-2318 E. Brookings Drive.  The application number is SUBD-111-2022.  Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 17-9-070 states:
If the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date of approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced, such approval shall be null and void.  This time period may be extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6) month period for good cause shown.  The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year.
To date, the property owner has not been able to record the plat due to the delay when: 1) the site plan application was being appealed, and 2) they are still waiting for approval form the Suncrest HOA Design Review Board for the site.  As such, the applicant is seeking a six-month extension to finalize the process and get the plat recorded.  Attached to this memo is the applicant’s written request for approval extension, the original approval letter, and Planning Commission staff report.  Staff received the request for extension on April 28, 2023, prior to the expiration of the plat amendment.
If approved, the site plan approval will be valid through January 29, 2023. The findings are as follows: Sample Motion for Approval – I move that we approve the plat amendment extension, as requested by John Wheatley, representing IKON Development for Trailside Townhomes, application SUBD-111-2022, based on the findings and subject to the conditions listed in the memo dated May 11, 2023.
1.   That the applicant has shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has occurred towards acquiring the signatures to record the mylar.
Sample Motion for Denial – I move that we deny the plat amendment extension, as requested by John Wheatley, representing IKON Development for Trailside Townhomes, application SUBD-111-2022, based on the findings and subject to the conditions listed in the memo dated May 11, 2023.
1.   That the applicant has not shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has not occurred towards acquiring the signatures to record the mylar.
