Proposed Updates to FY 2023 Town of Alta Capital Projects Plan

The Town of Alta Capital Projects Committee recommends the Alta Town Council
approve an updated FY 2023 Capital Projects Plan as part of the December Amendment
FY 2023 Budgets. Please see below for a list of significant updates to the FY 2023
Approved Capital Projects Plan.

   1. Updated Capital Projects Fund balance per November 30, 2022 Public
      Treasurer’s Investment Fund statement
          a. Please note that the spreadsheet calculates the budget for project 15,
               Future Community Center Phase II, by finding the sum of all other project
               budgets, and subtracting it from the Capital Projects Fund PTIF Account
               balance
   2. Updates to Community Center Roof Access, Town Office Window Replacement,
      and Firehouse Garage Ventilation projects to reflect completion and progress
   3. Updated description for Community Center Rear Yard Erosion Control project
   4. Updated budget for Alta Marshals Office Phase 2 Radio Upgrade project
   5. Inclusion of project 7 to purchase a Livescan fingerprint scanner for the Alta
      Marshals Office
   6. Inclusion of project 8 to purchase two new automated external defibrillators
      (AED’s) to replace obsolete equipment
   7. Inclusion of project 11 Town Park Improvements as a project to be completed in
      2023
   8. Moved Tom Moore Historic Structure project to 2024
   9. Updated Total 2023 Projects Budget—after adding and subtracting projects, the
      total is very similar to the amount in original approved 2023 plan.
