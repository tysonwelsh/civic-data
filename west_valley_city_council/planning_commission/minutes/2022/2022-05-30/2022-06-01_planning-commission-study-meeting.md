ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-1-2022    Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zoning ordinance text change to
             update the definition of hospital; prohibit hospitals in the BRP, MXD, C-3, LI,
             and M zones; prohibit community uses in the Decker Lake Station Overlay Zone;
             require windows and doors on homes within the RS zone to be Energy Star rated;
             and update the list of uses allowed as home occupations. (Staff – Steve Pastorik
             at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)



Discussion:      Steve Pastorik explained that the City staff is proposing an ordinance amendment
                 consisting of five changes. He explained that the first change would be updating the
                 definition of a hospital to include rehabilitation and detoxification services and not a
                 medical office or other use. Steve said the second change would prohibit hospitals in
                 certain zones and located near the residential areas they serve and commercial uses.
                 He explained that the third change would prohibit community uses in the Decker
                 Lake Station Overlay Zone. Steve explained several businesses within the Overlay
                 Zone serve alcohol, and recent State law will no longer allow a business serving
                 alcohol to change ownership if a community use was placed within the required
                 separation distance. He noted that if this change is approved, it would be the only
                 location where community uses are prohibited in the City. Steve stated that the
                 fourth change would require windows and doors on homes within the RS zone to be
                 Energy Star rated, which would make all standards within the RS zone meet or
                 exceed the Energy Star standards. The fifth and final change would update the list of
                 uses allowed as home occupations.

                 Commissioner Winters asked if the change regarding hospitals is intended to reduce
                 the potential impact on families, where the hospital traffic will be running all hours
                 of the day? Steve said that the main reasons are to place hospitals near retail services
                 and residents needing hospital services.

ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-8-2022   Petition by ARCADIA SP, INC. requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture,
           minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot
           size 8,000 square feet). The property is located at 3789 South 5200 West on 1.93 acres.
           (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik explained that the applicant proposes seven lots, four directly along
                 5200 West and three flag lots in the rear. He said there is an existing home on the
                 property, and the applicant wants to keep the house and remodel the inside. Steve
                 stated that the applicant requests an exception to the frontage of the four lots directly
                 off the street. He explained that they had looked into putting in a road to meet the
                 frontage requirement; however, that would create oddly shaped lots.
                 Steve reviewed the housing standards proposed by the developer to be part of a
                 development agreement, such as more insulation than required, nine-foot ceilings,
                 and all homes would have a variety of front elevations. He said they are also
                 proposing the old, higher-quality housing standards.
                 Commissioner Winters asked if the lots would be approximately ten-feet short of the
                 eighty-foot requirement. Steve said that is correct, the applicant wants to create a lot
                 for the existing house, and if they were to meet the frontage requirements, they
                 would lose one lot.
                 Commissioner Winters asked if granting the exception would create any issues.
                 Steve explained that a seventy-foot frontage is workable. However, any less than the
                 housing choices would be limited.
                 Commissioner Wood asked if they are proposing to keep some of the trees on the
                 subject property. Steve said he would discuss this with the developer and see if the
                 applicant is amenable to preserving some trees.

C-11-2022    Petition by CASTLEWOOD DEVELOPMENT requesting conditional use
             approval for a 330-unit apartment development. The property is located at 2940 W
             3650 S on 3.02 acres and is zoned CC (City Center) and R-2-8 (Two Unit
             Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). (Staff – Steve Pastorik
             at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov). (Continued from the May 25,
             2022, public hearing)

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik explained that this application had been continued twice. Once was at
                 the request of staff, and the applicant requested the second. He said they have
                 significantly improved their site plan and reviewed the updates. They have moved
                 the access to the south of the property providing two accesses to meet the fire
                 requirement. They are amenable to the request from Public Works regarding the
                 connection with the cul-de-sac. Steve explained the proposed metal screening for the
                 parking structure that provides proper ventilation. He noted that the applicant is still
                 working on sprucing up the portion of the building adjacent to the proposed park.
                 Steve reviewed amenities and the number of units.
                 Steve said he had spent much time working with the applicant on the landscaping.
                 He explained the conditions outlined in the staff report. The grade of the building
                 would be the same as the park, and the park needs to be filled in. There are
                 temporary parking spots that could be used for things such as grub hubs or potential
                 future tenants. Steve is working with the applicant to provide a buffer on the west
                 side of the subject property. He noted that they are proposing the addition of a new
                 vinyl fence.
                 Chair Fuller asked if the City has preliminary plans for the future work on lancer
                 way. Steve said they do, but it is very conceptual. Chair Fuller said it would be good
                 to have that plan to help with concerns about the increased traffic on Lancer Way.
                 Steve said he would follow up to see if they have anything to provide the public.
                 Commissioner Winters asked about the large curve/knuckle on the road connecting
                 to the proposed northern access. He feels this could cause a traffic concern if people
                 start parking there. Steve said he would ask the Public Works Department if the
                 curves would be sufficient or if something else should go there. Commissioner
                 Winters, have there been any complaints from the neighbors. Steve said not at this
                 time.
                 Commissioner Fuller asked if they had figured out the entrance to the adjacent
                 condominium. Steve said not yet; they are looking into options like an easement.


C-14-2022        Petition by WASATCH COMMERCIAL BUILDERS requesting conditional
                 use approval for outside storage. The property is located at 1820 W Printers
                 Row on 3.01 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock
                 Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)
Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that this application is a request for a conditional use for
              outside storage. He reviewed the history of the subject property and stated it has
              been a case for code enforcement. Brock stated that the storage areas would be
              located to the north of the property and if the applicant is intending to have
              permanent storage structures, they will need to work with our Building Inspections
              Department. He noted that the landscaping would need work to meet the
              requirements. Brock stated that they currently do not have a plan for the
              landscaping, but he can add a condition to the staff report for the improvements
              needed. He said that he has been working with the applicant on providing the final
              count of uses and parking stalls and another condition in the staff report would be
              that sufficient parking is to be provided. Brock discussed the various items that are
              stored on the property. He noted that the chain link fence would need to move in
              order to meet the landscaping requirements. Brock explained that the north side of
              the subject property currently isn’t screened, and it is visible from 201 so he
              recommends screening on the north side as well.

              Commissioner Winters asked if a different type of fence is required on the north side
              where it faces a high image arterial. Brock said that the property does not front on
              the high image arterial so, they would not need to meet that requirement.
C-15-2022     Petition by NEXT STEP WAREHOUSE requesting a conditional use for a
              Light Industrial Warehouse. The property is located at 3703 W. Parkway
              Blvd on 3.95 acres. The property is zoned M (Manufacturing) and is located
              in the Bangerter Highway Overlay Zone. (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-
              3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that this is a conditional use application for outside storage
              for a warehouse development. She said this application is coming before the
              Commission because it is within the Bangerter Highway Overlay Zone. Jody noted
              that there are many restrictions regarding what can be built on the subject property.

              Jody presented the proposed building types and noted that the structures need to
              meet the architectural standards of the overlay zone. She stated that the proposed
              buildings range from 8000 sq. ft. to 12000 sq. ft., and they do not have direct
              frontage onto Bangerter. Jody said that two different types of metal materials and
              garage doors are being proposed. She has recommended adding additional
              landscaping to building type A. She pointed out that the landscape plan shows a
              sidewalk; however, Jody does not believe they are adding a sidewalk.
