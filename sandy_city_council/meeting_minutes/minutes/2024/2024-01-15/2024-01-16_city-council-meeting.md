Ordinance 24-02 Meek Rezone
City Council Presentation
Jake Warner presented a rezone application on behalf of Robert Meek who is requesting
a rezone on his property located at 3761 E. Little Cottonwood Lane. The requested zone
change is for an R-1-8 zone from an R-1-15. The applicant intends to subdivide the
property, creating an additional lot from the rear of the property. The applicant spoke to
the Council regarding his request and application. He mentioned the feedback from the
neighbors is positive. Council discussion and comments followed. It was noted that there
was an error in the motion made by the Planning Commission, misstating the requested
zone, but that the intent of the Planning Commission was to forward a positive
recommendation for the rezone.

7. 24-019 Public Works Department reporting on the Fall 2023 Bulk Waste collection

Attachments: Bulk Waste Update to Council fall 2023

Mike Gladbach, Public Works Director, provided an update on the Fall 2023 Bulk Waste
collection program. He reviewed the program schedule, data collected regarding
violations, code enforcement process and the education and outreach campaign by the
Communications and Public Works Department. There has been tremendous progress
made by the community as a result of the communications campaign. The biggest issue
remains the placement of the bulk waste by the residents - too close to the curb. The
A-frames has been a very effective tool in communicating to residents. The public works
department plans on utilizing more of these in conjunction with the bulk waste program.
Code enforcement spoke to the process of how they handle residents who violate the
code. Mayor Zoltanski and Shane Pace spoke about the significant progress made with
educating the public about the do's and don't of the bulk waste program. Council is
supportive of the bulk waste program and of the purchase of additional A-frames for use in
this program. Council thanked Mr. Gladbach for his presentation and the crews who
handle the bulk waste program for the city.
Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda - General Citizen Comment.
