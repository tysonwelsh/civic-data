29        17.b       Resolution No. 20-27 - Accepting up to 2.35 acres of Property to be dedicated
30                   to Riverton City as part of the Proposed “River Springs Estates” Subdivision,
31                   Subject to Final Plat Approval by the Riverton City Planning Commission
32
33   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that in April of 2017 the Planning
34   Commission approved a Preliminary Plat for the proposed River Springs Estates Subdivision
35   located at 13702 South Lovers Lane. That project included land that had been defined by
36   Riverton City as a geologic hazard area, specifically being an area of impact should the canal to
37   the west breach or otherwise cause flooding. Riverton City adopted an ordinance to address
38   such situations, the Geologic Hazards Ordinance, which allows the City to protect areas of
39   potential impact, and includes tools the City can use to do so. He said it allows development lots
40   to be clustered away from areas of potential geologic hazard by keeping the lot count the same
41   but with smaller lot sizes.
42
43   Mr. Lethbridge further explained that the ordinance had been utilized with the proposed River
44   Springs Estates Subdivision. He said the western portion of the subdivision included 2.35 acres
45   that are within the defined hazard area. The preliminary plat shows that land dedicated to the
46   City and kept as open space, with the allowed 21 lots clustered into the remaining area. He said
47   the result was that many of the lots within the subdivision were below ½ acre in size, which is
48   consistent with the allowances in the Geologic Hazards Ordinance. However, the ordinance
49   requires that the City Council formally accept the land dedication of land before the Planning
50   Commission can approve the Final Plat of the subdivision. He said this issue was before the
51
     Page | 7 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     April 7, 2020



 1   Council, not to approve the subdivision itself, but to accept the dedication of the 2.35 acres,
 2   subject to the final approval of the subdivision by the Planning Commission.
 3
 4   Mr. Lethbridge said that Staff had reviewed the submitted material and found it to be compliant
 5   with the requirements of the ordinance. He also said that the proposed resolution included
 6   requirements that the property be fenced where adjacent to the proposed building lots on the
 7   east side.
