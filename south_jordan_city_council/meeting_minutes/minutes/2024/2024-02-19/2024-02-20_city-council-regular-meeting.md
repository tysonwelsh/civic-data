         G. Action Item
             G.1. Resolution R2024-18, Establishing the Healthy South Jordan Coalition.
             (Director of Recreation, Janell Payne)

Director Payne reviewed background information from the Council Report and Attachment A.

Council Member Shelton asked if this would establish another community committee, and if so,
would there be city staff or a council member assigned to it.

Director Payne explained it would be more similar to the Youth Council Program, rather than a
committee, and membership would not be limited. Council Members would be welcome to
attend, and there would be a staff member assigned as a program coordinator. Additionally there
would be a chair, vice chair, community liaison and a secretary. Any budget would be within the
Recreation Department, and it would be to pay for things like refreshments and meals. Many of
the initiatives here are not at additional cost to the city, and if those were identified that would go
through the regular budget process.

Council Member Harris believes this is a great idea and shared his experience at a breakout
session with the Utah League of Cities and Towns several years ago where they discussed the
concept of healthy cities and what they could do for their communities.

Mayor Ramsey likes the idea as well and talked about back in 2022 where she had the privilege
of presenting the Healthy South Jordan Hero Award to Bingham High School’s Hope Squad. She
also proposed considering a similar award once this comes together, finding ways to recognize
people who are being heroic in their efforts to create a healthy community.

         H. Staff Reports and Calendaring Items

Manager Lewis reminded everyone about the State of the City Address coming up on Thursday,
as well as the upcoming Budget Meeting on Thursday, February 28.

Attorney Loose encouraged council members to contact him if they are not getting all the
information he is sharing about the legislative session.

Council Member Johnson asked if there is some sort of wrap-up meeting with the council and
staff to discuss what happened and what is changing.

Attorney Loose responded that they can, and they usually go over specific issues that pertain to
South Jordan City. Generally, everything that happened is discussed at the League Conference in
April. There is always a standing invitation for them to visit, but staff has discussed our
legislators on a specific day to thank them for all their hard work and invite them to speak.

Mayor Ramsey noted they have also been discussing putting together an event, inviting our
legislators and some key legislative leadership to come and have a tour of the city to really show
them what’s going on in the city. She also expressed her concerns about the changes in the U-
111 funding and plans, and indicated that she would discuss that with staff at a later date.

Meeting. Council Member McGuire seconded the motion; vote was 4-0 unanimous in
favor. Council Member Zander was absent from the vote.
