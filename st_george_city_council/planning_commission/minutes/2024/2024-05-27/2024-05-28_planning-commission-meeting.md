REQUEST FOR PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT:
     Consider approval of a request for an ordinance amending an approved PD-C
     (Planned Development Commercial) on approximately 2.59 acres, located
     on the east side of 1470 East at approximately 300 South for the purpose of
     adding a 92-room hotel for a project to be known as @Home Best Western–
     Applicant – Rhett Beazer, Alpha Engineering – Case No. 2024-PDA-005 -
     PUBLIC HEARING.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: In 1998, the Planned Development
      Commercial zone was approved on this side as part of the approval of the Target and
      Lowes development on River Road. In 2004, 100South/1470 East was constructed
      from River Road and then extended to the subject property in 2008. Finally, in 2013,
      1470 East was constructed to Medical Center Drive. This application reviews the
      request for approval of a 92-room hotel which is a permitted use indicated in the
      approved Rim Rock Planned Development revised use list. Staff recommends approval
      of the application for an amendment to the PD for the Rim Rock PD in order to allow
      the @Home Best Western hotel to be constructed. 1. That the preliminary plat is
      approved in conjunction with this PD amendment. 2. That the Hillside Permit is
      approved in conjunction with this PD amendment. 3. That the height restriction placed

REQUEST FOR HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a request for a Hillside Development Permit to allow
     disturbance of areas in the 20-30%, 30-40%, and 40% and above slope areas
     and review possible construction in a rockfall hazard overlay – Applicant,
     Rhett Beazer, Alpha Engineering – Case No. 2024-HS-005.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: On February 28, 2024, the Hillside Review
      Board convened on the subject property and discussed the proposed hotel and the
      hillside issues related to such construction. The board requested additional
      documentation regarding 1:1 slopes. The applicant provided an updated geotechnical
      report and grading plan. The applicant is requesting to build on slope that exceeds
      20% and area that is in a rockfall study zone. The Board reconvened to review the
      updated geotechnical report and recommended approval of the application with the
      finding that there are some disturbed slopes greater than the 20-40% range but are
      non-contiguous could be disturbed (Staff – Dan Boles). Case No. 2024-HS-005 (Staff
      – Dan Boles).

REQUEST FOR PRELIMINARY PLAT:
     Consider approval of a four lot Preliminary Plat to be called @Home Best
     Western – Applicant – Rhett Beazer, Alpha Engineering – Case No. 2024-PP-
     010.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: The request at hand, if approved, would
      create four lots. One lot is proposed for a hotel while the other three are the remaining
      pieces that can further be divided in the future if desired. The anticipated that “Parcel
      #2” will be dedicated to the city as open space. With Parcel #3 the remaining property
      on the east side of 1470 East and Parcel #4 is the remainder on the west side. Staff
      recommends approval of this preliminary plat with the following conditions: 1. That a
      final plat is filed with and approved by the city prior to subdividing the property; 2.
      That lots 1, 3 & 4 are labeled as lots and not parcels and renumbered accordingly.

REQUEST FOR PRELIMINARY PLAT:
     Consider approval of a single lot preliminary plat to be called Soleil Ridge
     Preliminary Plat. This will be the property that the Soleil Ridge apartment
     complex will be built upon – Applicant – Josh Lyon, Wasatch Commercial
     Builders – Case No. 2024-PP-011.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: On June 27, 2023, the Planning Commission
      forwarded a positive recommendation to the City Council for the Soleil Ridge zone
      change to PD-R (Planned Development Residential) which would allow the construction
      of a 224-unit apartment development. On July 20,2023, the City Council approved the
      zone change. Staff recommends approval of this preliminary plat with the following
      conditions: 1. That a final plat is filed with and approved by the city prior to subdividing
      the property; 2. That an easement for public access is provided between 900 South
      and 800 South; 3. That the right of way vacation for 250 West north of 900 South be
      approved by the city prior to final plat recording. Case No. 2024-PP-011 (Staff – Dan
      Boles).
