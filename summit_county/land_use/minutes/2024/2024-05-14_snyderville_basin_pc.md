2)
Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for the Run-A-Muck Trailheads 1 & 2 and a Conditional Use Permit Amendment for the RTS Trailhead, located at 2387 and 2167 Olympic Parkway, Snyderville Basin, Summit County, UT; Parcel KJS-3-3&4LLA (88.61 acres); Applicant: Phares Gines, representing the Snyderville Basin Special Recreation District; Administrative Review. Project #23-003.
Amir Caus, Senior Planner, AICP. (6:01 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Run-A-Muk and RTS Trailheads CUP Staff Report.pdf
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner
, provided an overview and recommended approval of the proposed Conditional Use Permit for the Run-A-Muck Trailheads 1 & 2 as well as a Conditional Use Permit Amendment for the RTS Trailhead.
Phares Gines,
Snyderville Basin Recreation Trails & Open Space Manager
, addressed the Commissioners.
Commissioners asked brief clarifying questions to which Planner Caus, Attorney Viti, Director Putt and Phares Gines responded.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:11 P.M.
No public comment was offered.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:11 P.M.
Commissioners Cooke, Nagie, Carmichael, and Conabee provided additional comments and asked follow-up questions. Applicants, Phares Gines and Dana Jones provided additional comments to the discussion.
Thomas Cooke made a motion to approve the RTS trailhead based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval with an additional condition of approval that as the CUP is amended to include the associated restroom as outlined in the report the current RTS parking remain a permeable surface and not be repaved with asphalt. Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
Commissioner Cooke asked a clarifying question about trail signage.
Bruce Carmichael made a motion to approve the proposed Run-a-Muk Trailheads 1 & 2 conditional use permit pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in the staff report with an additional condition of approval that both parking lots remain permeable surfaces. Thomas Cooke seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
3)
Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for the Trailside Elementary School Expansion, located at 5700 Trailside Dr, Trailside, Snyderville Basin, Summit County, UT; Parcel SS-42-C-X (10.66 acres); Applicant: Todd Hansen, representing the Park City School District; Administrative Review. Project #24-037.
Amir Caus, Senior Planner, AICP. (6:28 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Trailside Elementary School Expansion CUP Staff Report.pdf
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner,
presented for approval a proposal for a small addition for Trailside Elementary School.
JD Simmons, representing the school district, briefly addressed the Commission.
Commissioners asked brief clarifying questions to which Mr. Simmons and Planner Caus responded.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:46 P.M.
No public comment was offered.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:46 P.M.
Commissioners provided brief additional comments on the project.
Makena Hawley made a motion to approve the Trailside Elementary Expansion CUP pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval in the staff report. DJ Hubler seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).  Abstain: Tyann Mooney. Recuse: Matthew Nagie.
Due to technical issues Commissioner Mooney’s vote was not heard.
