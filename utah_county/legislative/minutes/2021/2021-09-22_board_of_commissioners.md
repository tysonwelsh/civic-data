31. ADOPT AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND SECTION 6.04 RELATED TO APPROVAL OF
WATER AND SEWAGE DISPOSAL FACILITIES FOR DWELLINGS AND OTHER
APPLICABLE STRUCTURES AND USES, INCLUDING, BUT NOT LIMITED TO, THOSE
LOCATED IN THE HOBBLE CREEK CANYON AREA OF UNINCORPORATED UTAH
COUNTY

-Bryce Armstrong, Community Development

BRYCE ARMSTRONG, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATE DIRECTOR -
EARLIER IN THE YEAR WE HAD A WORK SESSION PRESENTATION FROM THE
STATE ENGINEER'S OFFICE THAT INDICATED A SIGNIFICANT OVER ALLOCATION,
OVER APPROPRIATION OF WATER IN UTAH COUNTY AND MOST OF THE STATE IS
SUBJECT TO WHAT THEY CALL A ‘GENERAL ADJUDICATION OF WATER RIGHTS’.
UTAH COUNTY IS SUBJECT TO ONE OF THOSE GENERAL ADJUDICATIONS MORE
SPECIFICALLY AND MORE CONCERNING WAS A CERTAIN AREA OF THE COUNTY,
HOBBLE CREEK CANYON, THAT HAD THE MOST CONCERN AND AS A RESULT, THE
UTAH STATE ENGINEER'S OFFICE ORDERED WHAT’S CALLED A ‘WATER
DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM’ WHICH TRIES TO HELP ADDRESS THIS OVER
ALLOCATION OF WATER RIGHTS STILL PENDING THE ADJUDICATION
COMPLETION THAT’S STILL ONGOING. IT’S NOT BEEN COMPLETED AND FROM
WHAT I UNDERSTAND - IT’S BEEN GOING ON FOR DECADES AND STILL MAYBE
YEARS BEFORE IT’S DETERMINED BUT IN THE INTERIM THIS DISTRIBUTION
SYSTEM HAS BEEN CREATED TO HELP MITIGATE SOME CONCERNS IN THE
HOBBLE CREEK CANYON AREA, ESTABLISHING A PRIORITY SYSTEM, BASED ON AN
APPROPRIATION PRIORITY DATE OF AN UNDERLYING WATER RIGHT. IT GETS
QUITE TECHNICAL BUT THE GIST OF THAT THE MESSAGE WE HEARD IN JANUARY
WAS THAT POTENTIALLY PEOPLE’S WATER SOURCES OR WATER WOULD BE
TURNED OFF AND THE REASON THEY’RE TURNED OFF BECAUSE THE WATER
LEVELS HAVE DROPPED SO SIGNIFICANTLY THAT IT’S NOT AVAILABLE. THIS
OBVIOUSLY CAUSES SOME CONCERN DUE TO A NUMBER OF RESIDENCES UP
THERE AND OTHER USES THE UTILIZE WATER AND SO THE COMMISSION
WANTED THE PLANNING COMMISSION TO REVIEW A POTENTIAL AMENDMENT
TO PERHAPS ADDRESS THIS POTENTIAL SITUATION. CURRENTLY WE HAVE AN
ORDINANCE THAT REQUIRES A PERSON TO GET THEIR WATER SOURCE AND
THEIR SEWAGE DISPOSAL SOURCE APPROVED PRIOR TO GETTING A BUILDING
PERMIT FOR A HOME OR OTHER USE. THIS AMENDS THAT SECTION THAT MAKES
IT A LITTLE MORE ROBUST IN ITS REVIEW AND ITS REQUIREMENTS. THE
PLANNING COMMISSION RECEIVED A PROPOSAL FROM THE COUNTY COMMISSION
IN APRIL AND HELD TWO PUBLIC HEARINGS THIS SUMMER AND MADE A
RECOMMENDATION BACK TO THE COUNTY COMMISSION. THEY AMENDED THAT
ORIGINAL RECOMMENDATION. IN SUMMARY, THE PROPOSAL THAT’S BEEN
RECOMMENDED BY THE PLANNING COMMISSION DOES SEVERAL THINGS:

* REQUIRES ANY PERSON WHO COMES IN FOR A BUILDING PERMIT THAT WILL BE
UTILIZING A WATER SOURCE, TO SIGN AND RECORD A WAIVER DISCLAIMER
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT FORM THAT ACKNOWLEDGES THEIR AWARENESS OF THIS
ADJUDICATION PROCESS AND ALSO RELEASES THE COUNTY FROM POTENTIAL
LIABILITY OF THEIR LOSS OF WATER AND ANY DAMAGES THAT MIGHT BE
ASSOCIATED WITH THAT.

* THE PROPOSAL SPELLS OUT SOME OF THE REPLACEMENT WATER
REQUIREMENTS IF A PROPERTY OWNER DOES IN FACT, LOSE THEIR WATER.
