Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Presentation and Discussion with Mayor Johnson

Mayor Johnson discussed the roles of the City Council Members and the Planning Commission
Members. Mayor Johnson discussed split votes on the agenda items.

Craig Johnson, the Lehi City Assistant Attorney, discussed the difference between being an
elected official and an appointed official. Craig addressed questions from the Commissioners and
Staff.

Ryan Wood, Lehi City Attorney, discussed Legislative and Administrative decisions,
Reasonably Debatable decisions, and Health, Safety, and Welfare of the Citizens of Lehi. Ryan

provided a list of resources for the Commission Members and addressed questions from the
Commissioners and Staff.

3. Regular Agenda

3.1 Public hearing and consideration of Yesco’s request for conditional use approval of the
Hunter’s Grove EMD Monument Sign located at 139 North Hunter Grove Lane in a
Commercial zone.

    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    The applicant was present. The applicant would like to push the sign as far North as possible.
    Kim Struthers stated DRC would need make sure it wouldn’t impact any utility easements
    and out of the site triangle.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged comments.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to approve Yesco’s request for conditional use
approval of the Hunter’s Grove EMD Monument Sign located at 139 North Hunter Grove
Lane in a Commercial zone; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report,
noting flexibility of the sign location as long as the sign stays out of the easement and site
triangle. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.3 Consideration of Wasatch Residential Group’s request for approval of the Lehi Block
Apartment site plan, a 232-unit development located at 2377 North 1200 West in a
Commercial zone.

    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item. The TOD was
    initially denied but was resubmitted with a different concept that met standards.

    The applicant, Adam with Wasatch Residential Group, was present.

    Commissioner Nielsen clarified that this was still be commercial. Commissioner Peterson
    asked if the proposal met all of the development requirements. Staff stated that it did.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Wasatch Residential Group’s request for
approval of the Lehi Block Apartment site plan, a 232-unit development located at 2377
North 1200 West in a Commercial zone; including all DRC comments and findings stated in
the report. Commissioner Peterson seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.5 Public hearing and recommendation of Boyer Company’s request for preliminary
subdivision review of Holbrook Farms Plat I, a 5-lot commercial subdivision located at
3600 West and Waterbury Drive.

    Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item

    The applicant, Spencer Moffit, was present.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged the comments.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.

    The traffic increase was discussed on Waterbury Drive. Ross Dinsdale stated there will be an
    increase, but it is well within what the road can handle. There is a traffic study being
    conducted and it will determine whether or not a full access or a right in, right out onto 3600
    West will be necessary. There is potential for lower traffic flow based on the types of
    businesses that develop.

Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Public hearing and
recommendation of Boyer Company’s request for preliminary subdivision review of
Holbrook Farms Plat I, a 5-lot commercial subdivision located at 3600 West and
Waterbury Drive; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner
Ellis seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
