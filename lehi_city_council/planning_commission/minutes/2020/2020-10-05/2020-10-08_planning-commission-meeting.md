1. Call to Order

Chairman Hemmert.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1. Public hearing and recommendation of Edge Homes’ request for preliminary
subdivision review of Willow Point, a 420-unit residential development located at
approximately 2100 North 4100 West.
    Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

    The applicant, Brandon Watson with Edge Homes, presented information on the units. The
    focal point is the community center on Hardman Way where a lot of the amenities will be a
    pool, clubhouse, pickleball and basketball courts, playgrounds, tot-lots, and greenspace. The
    applicant talked about 4100 West and the connectivity to 2100 North and what the traffic

    study had provided. Commissioner Hereth had questions about the traffic study and what
    analysis had come of it. Commissioner Nielsen asked about parking and pedestrian access.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged that both public comments were against the
    development, mostly because of traffic and density.

    The Commissioners discussed the pros and cons between commercial and residential.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Ross Dinsdale stated they didn’t ask them to do too much from 3600 West and 2100 North
    but we are working with UDOT on the traffic study to review how to decide how to get the
    most traffic in and out. It is constricted at 3600 West between the two Frontage roads so
    some work is going to be done there. Kim Struthers showed the development agreement that
    shows areas of future commercial and residential with overlay.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to forward a positive recommendation of Edge Homes’
request for preliminary subdivision review of Willow Point, a 420-unit residential
development located at approximately 2100 North 4100 West; including all DRC comments
in the September 16, 2020 meetings and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Ellis
seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Everett yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner
Eyre, yes. The motion passed 6-1.
