                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 20-24

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 9, ADDING CHAPTER 30 TO RIVERTON CITY
      CODE, REGULATING FREEDOMS TO PEACABLY ASSEMBLE AND
                     EXERCISE FREE SPEECH

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Code Title 9 of the Riverton City Code regulates the public
peace, morals, and welfare of Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, recent developments in the use of public demonstrations to exercise free
speech and assembly rights have demonstrated the need to create a legal framework by which
speech and peaceable assemblies can take place on facilities and grounds owned by Riverton
City; and

        WHEREAS, following these developments, Riverton City staff has drafted an ordinance,
adding Chapter 30 to Title 9 of Riverton City Code to enable the implementation of lawful
regulations relating to the time, place, and manner speech and peaceable assembly rights may be
exercised; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public meeting to consider such an addition to
Riverton City Code; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend and adopt the ordinance as described below.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
            Section 1. Riverton City Code, Title 9, shall be amended as described in the
                       attached Exhibit “A”.
            Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
