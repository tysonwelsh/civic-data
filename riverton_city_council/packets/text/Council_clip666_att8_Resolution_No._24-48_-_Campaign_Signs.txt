                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       RESOLUTION NO. 24-48

            A RESOLUTION REAFFIRMING THE POLICY OF RIVERTON CITY,
          UTAH, REGARDING THE PLACEMENT OF CAMPAIGN SIGNS ON PARK
               STRIPS LOCATED ADJACENT TO CITY-OWNED PROPERTY

        WHEREAS, Riverton City owns several parcels of land located within the territorial jurisdiction
of Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, the summer and fall of the year 2024 constitute an election season, with a general
election for several public offices to be held on the date of November 5, 2024; and

       WHEREAS, in the months leading up to election-day, candidates put forth a concerted effort to
communicate to the public regarding their candidacy for public office, oftentimes with the use of
campaign signs displayed in view of the public; and

         WHEREAS, Riverton City Code Section 18.150.190 governs addresses the placement of
political signs, including campaign signs, as follows:

        18.150.090 Exempt signs for which no sign permit is required:

        (21) Political Signs. Political signs may be displayed without permit and are allowed; provided,
        that the signs are not erected in such a manner as to constitute a roof sign, are not located on
        property in a way that constitutes a safety or visibility problem and are not erected on utility
        poles, street signs or in public rights-of-way. Political signs lawfully erected without a sign permit
        must be located on private property with the property owner’s consent and shall not be located
        within the city right-of-way or on any city-owned property or structure. All other provisions of
        this chapter shall continue to apply to political signs. Any sign placed in public right-of-way is
        subject to removal by city staff. When signs are removed for this reason, the responsible party
        shall be notified of the reason for the removal and the location. The sign will be made available
        for five calendar days to be picked up. After that time, the confiscated sign will be destroyed.

        and;

        WHEREAS, foregoing notwithstanding, The Utah Code Section 20A-17-103(2) states:

        A local government entity, a local government officer, a local government employee, or another
        person with authority or control over public property that posts or permits a person to post a
        political sign on public property:

                 (a)     shall permit any other person to post a political sign on the public property,
        subject to the same requirements and restrictions imposed on all other political signs permitted to
        be posted on the public property; and
                 (b)     may not impose a requirement or restriction on the posting of a political sign if
        the requirement or restriction is not politically neutral and content neutral.

        and;

        WHEREAS, in September of 2017, the Riverton City Council acting as a “local government
entity” with “control over public property” voted to permit candidates to place signs within public rights
of way which included park strips located adjacent to property which is owned by Riverton City, and
within park strips and medians which are maintained by Riverton City; and
       WHEREAS, the Council finds and determines that it should reconfirm its position on this matter,
owing to the length of time which has lapsed since this matter was first decided in the year 2017; and

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

       1.      The Riverton City Council hereby reaffirms its position that campaign signs are permitted
               within: a) park strips located adjacent to property which is owned by Riverton City; b)
               parks strips fronting public rights of way which are maintained by Riverton City; and c)
               medians found on City streets. To this end, Riverton City hereby waives enforcement of
               any provisions to the contrary found in Riverton City Code Section 18.150.090.

       2.      All remaining provisions found in Riverton City Code Section 18.150.090 not in conflict
               with Section 1 of this resolution remain in full force and effect. Placement of signs
               within park strips identified in Section 1 of this resolution cannot be placed in a manner
               which constitutes a safety or visibility problem, and may not be erected on utility poles,
               on street signs, or placed in a manner which protrudes into public rights of way beyond
               the bounds of a park strip.

       3.      This resolution shall become effective upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 21st day of May,
