    2. PUBLIC HEARING TO AMEND THE 2015 ADDENDUM TO THE 2002 ZONING DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR
       WOLF CREEK RESORT BY ADDING A CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT MAP FOR WOLF CREEK TO THE ADDENDUM

        Jim Gentry, with County Planning, stated that the 2/2015 addendum to the 2002 Wolf Creek Zoning
        Development Agreement showed the remaining number of units and the allocation of the units. However,
        the location of the units within Wolf Creek was not shown on the 2002 recorded agreement. One of the
        caveats of the Planning and County Commissions in approving the addendum was that Wolf Creek provide
        a conceptual drawing showing those units, parcels, and where they were allocated. The Planning
        Commission unanimously recommended approval of this item.
        John Lewis, with Wolf Creek Properties, referred to the bankruptcy problems of 2010 in Ogden Valley
        stating that the community has been restoring itself, and he expressed thanks for the county’s support. He
        stated that Wolf Creek needed to deliver a concept map for the 2002 agreement. He said that the open
        space has been retained and the density is no more than was approved.
    3. Public Comments: Chair Gibson invited public comments and Jan Fullmer, of Eden, noted that
       Commissioner Bell and Sean Wilkinson, County Community and Economic Development Director, had
       been at the 9/17/2014 meeting where there was tremendous consensus from 14 communities
       within/surrounding Wolf Creek on the community’s wishes and the overall emphasis was on maintaining as
       much open space as possible; this is consistent with the Ogden Valley General Plan’s current draft and is
       what Mr. Lewis has done—he has been consistent with what is documented, what is on the website, all the
       presentations, and the community’s input. She expressed gratitude to Mr. Wilkinson and his department
       for the tremendous assistance to the communities and posting all the information on the website. She fully
       supports clustered development to preserve open space.
    4.
       Commissioner Bell moved to close the public meeting and convene the public hearing; Commissioner
       Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
