MINUTES OF THE COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL WORK
SESSION HELD TUESDAY, OCTOBER 27, 2020, AT 5:00 P.M. THE MEETING WAS
HELD ELECTRONICALLY, WITHOUT A PHYSICAL LOCATION DUE TO THE
CURRENT COVID-19 PANDEMIC AND AS AUTHORIZED BY THE GOVERNOR’S
EXECUTIVE ORDER DATED 18 MARCH 2020

Members Present:         Mayor Mike Peterson, Council Member Scott Bracken, Council Member
                         Christine Mikell, Council Member Douglas Petersen

Staff Present:           City Manager Tim Tingey, City Attorney w. Shane Topham, Assistant Fire
                         Chief Riley Pilgrim, Records Culture and Human Resources Director Paula
                         Melgar

Excused:                 Council Member Tali Bruce

1.      Welcome – Mayor Pro Tempore, Scott Bracken.

Mayor Mike Peterson called the meeting to order at 5:02 p.m. and welcomed those listening. He
read in its entirety the determination giving the Council the authority to hold the meeting via Zoom,
pursuant to Utah Code Annotated §52-4-207(4).

2.      Staff Reports.

        a.       Police Policies and Practices – City Manager, Tim Tingey and Police Chief
                 Robby Russo.

Mayor Peterson reported that Council Member Bruce was unable to attend due to the death of a
close friend. Since she has expressed interest in the subject, she would like to be in attendance.
As a result, he entertained discussion regarding moving the meeting to a future date and time to be
determined. There was also information that was needed that was not yet available.

MOTION: Council Member Bracken moved to table the discussion and move it to a future date.
Council Member Petersen seconded the motion. Council Member Petersen-Aye, Council Member
Mikell-Aye, Council Member Bracken-Aye, Mayor Mike Peterson-Aye. The motion passed
unanimously.

3.      Review of Calendars and Upcoming Events.

        •        November 12 – 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. – Bengal Boulevard Open House in the City
                 Hall Community Room.

It was noted that the above event will likely be held remotely. Council Member Mikell asked if
boards will be posted at City Hall for those who are interested. She stated that in another
municipality they had similar types of meetings from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. where staff was
available to answer questions. She considered that to be a good way to run these types of meetings.
City Manager, Tim Tingey stated that the consultant has done some of these types of meetings
