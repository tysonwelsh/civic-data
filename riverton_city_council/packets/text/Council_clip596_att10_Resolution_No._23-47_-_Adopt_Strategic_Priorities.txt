                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 23-47

   A RESOLUTION ADOPTING AND AMENDING THE STRATEGIC PRIORITIES
                       FOR RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Strategic Priorities is used to chart a course by which
Riverton City may meet the needs of its citizens with available resources to provide with
municipal services; and

      WHEREAS, the Strategic Priorities are reviewed and updated from time to time and City
Council has evaluated and updated the Strategic Priorities; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment is
appropriate at this time to build stronger business connections between the City, as a market
participant, and local business enterprises; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to adopt the
Strategic Priorities.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Strategic Priorities attached to this Resolution is hereby adopted as
          provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution. .

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of
