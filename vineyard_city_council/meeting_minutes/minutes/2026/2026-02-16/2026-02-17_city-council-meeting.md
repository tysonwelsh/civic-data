     3.1 RDA Administration and Audit

     Councilmember Lauret presented on the item and updated the council on the previously discussed
proposals of hiring full time RDA Staff and contracting with Chris Harding for an additional audit.

      Mayor Stratton and the council discussed the RFP process that Mr. Harding had previously gone
through. This included discussions with staff that had worked on the previous RFQ and an overview of the
procurement process in general. Residents raised questions regarding the proposed audit and how it would
relate to the original scope of work.

     3.2. Commissions Discussion


    The council and staff discussed the current commissions and boards and if consolidation was
possible. The focus was on combining the Active Transportation Commission with Planning
Commission. There was also discussion regarding the attendance of the commissions and
overlapping duties of some of the commission members.

     3.3. Skate Park Discussion

     Councilmember McCumber led a discussion with council and staff on the ongoing plans for
the skate park. The discussion included comments and questions from residents concerned about
possible liability issues and how the city would go about mitigating those risks.

     3.4. Review Electric Mobility Devices Ordinance

    Chief Deputy Rockwell presented on the current ordinances regarding electric devices.

    The council discussed with staff what if any changes could be made to the current code to
address additional types of vehicles. Residents had questions around enforcement and
specifically helmet safety. There was discussion regarding the possibility of adding a helmet
requirement and the inclusion of the Communities That Care Commission to create an
educational program for schools.

Councilmember Holdaway was excused at 12:53pm

     3.5. 400 South Planning Discussion


     Councilmember Lauret presented work being proposed for 400 South.

     Mayor Stratton and the council discussed the proposed work as well as parts of the project
involving Orem city and where they were in the process of being completed. There was
additional clarification from staff in regards to presented plans and what had already been
approved and finalized. Staff indicated that a Public Hearing was planned for the Planning
Commission meeting on March 4th to discuss proposed changes to the development agreement as
well as impact fees, with the developers. The goal was to have it as an item for council
discussion during the March 10th, 2026 City Council meeting.
