SD-1-2026
Stegnger Way Extension
5892 W Parkway Blvd


BACKGROUND:

Vantrust Real Estate is requesting consideration to dedicate an extension of Stegner Way
(5990 West). The property to be dedicated is along the southern boundary of the parcel
located at 5892 W Parkway Boulevard, which is zoned M, Manufacturing.

Stegner Way currently extends north from Parkway Boulevard, just west of the Mountain View
Corridor, and dead ends into the Vantrust parcel. Wallace Stegner Academy is on the east
side of the street and a residential property is on the west. The proposed extension of the
street will create a knuckle intersection, and then the road will extend to the east along the
charter school’s northern boundary. The street extension is a requirement of the Development
Agreement for the parcel that was entered into with the City when the property was rezoned in
2024.

The developer will build the proposed road to the east edge of their parcel. The City is
currently looking to extend 5900 West between Begley Road and Parkway Boulevard. When
that project is built the City will finish the extension of Stegner way east to the new 5900 West
Street. The City anticipates advertising the road construction project for contractor bids in
June, and then the construction will most likely start at the end of this year and finish sometime
next year.

The new street will be built to a 60 ft. right-of-way, with curb and gutter, a 5 ft. park strip and 5
ft. sidewalk on both sides of the road. The developer is coordinating with the charter school for
a small area in the north-west corner of their property that will need to be dedicated for the new
right-of-way. Future phases of the Vantrust project will have access onto the new street. Per
the development agreement, industrial truck traffic cannot use Stegner Way to access
Parkway Blvd.


RECOMMENDATION
1. Approval of the street dedication subject to the staff report.
2. Continue the application due to issues raised at the public hearing.



Discussion: Brock Anderson presented the proposal.

The applicant Colby Anderson with CIR Engineering was present and mentioned that he
believed the dedication would be a great addition to the area from a traffic perspective. No
questions were asked.

Commissioner Durfee motioned to approve the application. Commissioner Woodruff
seconded. All were in favor.

                     UNANIMOUS—SD-1-2026 —Approval

C-4-2026
Godfrey West Valley Phase II
2466 S 5900 W
M Zone (16.54 acres)



Sansone Group is requesting conditional use approval for a warehouse building at 2466 S
5900 W. This property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley City General Plan
designates the area as Light Manufacturing. The zoning is also M for adjacent parcels on all
sides. The surrounding area is undeveloped to the north and south. There is a Rocky Mountain
Power Corridor to the east, and warehouse uses to the west.

The proposed development is the second phase of a larger Sansone project. Phase I was
approved in February of 2021 and the building was completed in September of 2022 (ZC
20210004). The Planning Commission reviewed a similar Conditional Use application on this

property in 2024 (ZC 20240002), but the approval expired, and the project was never built.
This proposal, nearly identical to the 2024 application, includes a 309,750 sq. ft. warehouse
building, which will accommodate up to four tenants. The building will be constructed primarily
of tilt-up concrete. This facility is not required to meet the Commercial Design Standards due to
the use being limited to a manufacturing zone, however, there are three design-related
requirements outlined in the development agreement for the property (see attached), which the
proposed building must meet.

The proposed site layout orients the building towards 5900 West. Customer and employee
parking will be on the east side of the building and the truck and trailer loading and parking will
be on the west side of the building, which mirrors the first phase. A new public road is being
constructed on the north side of the site, Nitro Lane. It is an extension of 2400 South and is
part of the Godfrey Industrial Park No. 3 Subdivision (ZS 20230003). Once the road is built, the
developer of that project will bond for the road and then record the subdivision plat. That road
needs to be dedicated as a public street for the two access points on the north end of this
project. The other two access points will be from Beagley Rd. The two access points on the
east sides of both streets will be for customers and employees and the two access points on
the west sides of the streets will be for truck access and parking. The applicant is proposing
194 customer and employee parking stalls on the site. The exact number of parking stalls
required is based on the proposed uses and building square footage, which will be monitored
as Tenant Improvement permits are reviewed.

Curb and gutter improvements were installed with the construction of Beagley Road and 5900
West, however the park strip and sidewalk portions of the right-of-way improvements need to
be installed in conjunction with this development. Along the east edge of the parcel there is
also the Lee Creek Channel. This area must remain as an open drainage ditch. The code
typically requires 20’ of formal landscaping next to the right-of-way and the area must also
have a 3’ tall landscape berm to screen the parking lot. The location of the drainage channel
makes this difficult to accomplish. 7-9-112(2) allows for the Planning Commission to consider
alternatives to the berm requirement for M-Zoned properties over 10 acres in size. The
applicant is proposing to leave the Lee Creek Channel area with native vegetation, which is
about 40’ wide in the narrowest section, and then do 10’ of formal landscaping next to the
parking lot. Staff suggests that in lieu of the berm requirement and 20’ of formal landscaping,
the applicant provides shrubs that will grow about 3’ tall within the 10’ landscaped area so that
they will create a hedge row to screen the parking lot. Street trees shall also be provided along
the entire frontage, with one tree provided every 30 ft.

The M zone requires 5% of the site to be landscaped. The current proposal provides 23%
landscaping on the site. On the conditional use approval for Phase I, the Planning Commission
required that the south side of the truck court area be screened with large evergreen trees. In
the current proposal for Phase II, it shows a retention basing going in this area, with minimal
plantings. Per the code there needs to be 20’ of formal landscaping. Some of the area can be
used for retention, but the code requirement still needs to be met, and large evergreen trees
should still be used for screening. This frontage should have street trees planted once every
30’ and the formally landscaped area should have 50% coverage of live plant material. The
south side of the building along the frontage shall also have trees spaced in the same manner.

On the north side of the property, the west side of the site, the site plan shows a retention
basin extending all the way up to the new right-of-way line for 2400 South. Most of the basin is
on the Phase I portion of the site. When that phase was approved, there wasn’t going to be a
road on the north side. With the addition of the road, the code requires that the 20’ required
setback from the street be landscaped. The north edge of the basin area should be redesigned
to accommodate 20’ of formal landscaping with street trees placed every thirty feet and 50%
coverage of live plant material, or they could potentially do 10’ of formal landscaping and trees,
with 100% coverage of live plant material within that 10’ if the Planning Commission finds that
reasonable. Street trees should also be continued in the same manner on the north side of the
building to create a cohesive streetscape.

The Public Works Department has indicated that there are some potential wetland issues and
floodplain issues on the site that may need to be addressed. The developer will need to work
through any such issues with the appropriate agencies.

No signage is being proposed currently. Any future signage on the property or building will
need to comply with Title 11.

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented the application.

Commissioner Durfee asked if there were any changes between this conditional use and the
original application. Brock responded that it is similar. The big change is that the first
application didn’t address the 2400 South Road Extension, but now there will be a public road
there.

Vice Chair Winters requested clarification regarding the Lee Creek Channel. He asked if at the
elbow the creek goes under the street and if it was piped from there. Brock said he believed it
was piped on the north end.

The applicants were present. Daniel Goodwin was there in person and Brad Chase was
online. Daniel responded to the question regarding the Lee Creek piping. He stated the pipe
parallels the roadway up to the new road that’s being constructed, up to Nitro Lane. There is a
48-inch pipe that runs down Nitro Lane and then it turns to the north. It’s a combination of
open channel and piped, and that still needs to be completed for that rerouting.
