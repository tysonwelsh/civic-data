Business Session

1.0   Call to Order: Mayor Troy K. Walker
2.0   Public Hearing: Refunding Bonds, Series 2023, proceeds from the sale of
      the bonds will be used to finance the refunding of all or a part of the
      Issuer’s outstanding sales tax revenue bonds in order to achieve a debt
      service savings, the proceeds of which were originally used to finance
      the acquisition of land and facilities for use as an aquarium and related
      improvements (the “Refunding”), (b) fund a deposit to a debt service
      reserve fund, if necessary, and (c) pay costs of issuance of the Bonds.
      The Project is located at 12033 Lone Peak Parkway, Draper, Utah, and
      leased by the Issuer to The Living Planet, Inc.

      or electronically, so Mayor Walker closed the public hearing.
