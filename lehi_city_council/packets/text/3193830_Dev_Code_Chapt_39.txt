  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests review and recommendation on a proposed amendment to Chapter 39 of the Development
Code, Definitions. The proposal will add clarification to the definition of Townhomes, Condos, and Dwelling Units.

The building department have had an increasing number of townhomes applying to have kitchens in their basements
with separate entrances, in essence creating an accessory dwelling unit (ADU) in a townhome. City Staff has concerns
with the impact on parking and utilities that these units may have. ADUs are only allowed in detached single-family
homes on lots over 6,000 square feet in size. The proposed amendment will add to the definition of both townhomes
and condominiums that only one kitchen is allowed.

The proposed amendment also adds clarification that each dwelling unit can only be occupied by one family as
defined in Chapter 39. This clarification is to help with enforcement of ADUs and occupancy in general.

Narrative

The building department has been receiving an increasing number of applications for
kitchens in their basements. We also have become aware that people are renting out these
basements as separate units. Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) are not allowed in
townhomes. Changing the definition to not allow multiple kitchens is an easier way to stop
these separate units than enforcement alone. People can still rent the basement, but the
renters would need to share the kitchen facility with the rest of the townhomes. This fits
under the definition of “Dwelling Unit” and “Family” already in the Code.

This change also seeks to clarify through language that a dwelling unit can contain one
family. This clarifies that only one family group as defined in the Development Code can
occupy a dwelling unit.
