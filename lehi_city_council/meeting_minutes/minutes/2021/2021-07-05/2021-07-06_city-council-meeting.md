1. Welcome and Opening Comment
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
   Hancock offered the opening comment.

2. Discussion Items
   2.a. Presentation of Lehi well-being survey results
   Professor Courtney Flint, with Utah State University, presented the survey results to the
   Council. She stated that the goal is to understand the wellbeing of Lehi residents and various
   perspectives on issues. She said that the previous survey was conducted pre-covid, and this
   survey was conducted in January and February 2021, so covid was still a concern then for many.
   As expected, the overall well-being did decline, but they would expect that to bounce back when
   they do the survey again. She noted that residents were commonly concerned about growth,
   infrastructure, and traffic.

    2.b. Water-share purchase for Willow Park
    Dean Lundell, Finance Director, informed the Council that the City will be purchasing water
    shares that will be used to maintain Willow Park. The cost is around $400,000 total for the
    shares. He also stated that the water shares will stay with the City, not the Park, so if the park
    is every sold or reverts to Utah County, the water shares will stay with Lehi City.

    2c. E-scooter policy
    Kim Struthers, Community Development Director, stated that they have had a few requests
    from various e-scooter companies that would like to operate in Lehi. Lime Scooter is the only
    company approved in Lehi right now, although that contract may need to be renewed. He
    inquired if the Council was willing to entertain the idea of entering into agreements with other
