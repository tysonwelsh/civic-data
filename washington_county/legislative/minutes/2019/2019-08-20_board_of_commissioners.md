AUGUST 20, 2019

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Acting Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on August 20, 2019, in the
Washington County Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy County

Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

EMERGENCY SERVICES REPORT: SHERIFF CORY PULSIPHER

Washington County Sheriff Cory Pulsipher stated that there have been nine (9) Search and
Rescue (SAR) incidents, with a total of 231 man-hours from July 16, 2019 to August 20,
2019.

Sheriff Pulsipher reminded citizens recreating in the backcountry area to plan well, be
prepared and alert others of travel plans.

CONSIDERATION _OF ORDINANCE NO. _2019-1145-O, AN ORDINANCE
INCLUDING THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AS AN EX

OFFICIO MEMBER _OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY COUNCIL OF
GOVERNMENTS WITH LIMITED VOTING PRIVILEGES

Acting Chairman Victor Iverson explained that the Utah Department of Transportation
(UDOT) has contributed $15 million dollars to the St. George to Springdale transit system
and has requested to be an ex-officio member of the Council of Governments (COG) with
voting privileges on public transportation matters.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2019-1145-
O, An Ordinance Including the Utah Department of Transportation as
an Ex Officio Member of the Washington County Council of
Governments with Limited Voting Privileges. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO._R-2019-2502, A RESOLUTION
CORRECTING TYPOGRAPHIC ERRORS IN A PROPERTY TAX ABATEMENT

APPROVED ON JULY 2, 2019

Washington County Deputy Attorney explained that there was an error with the previously
assigned tax abatement parcel number for Robert Brennan. The parcel number and the
amount of taxes, including penalties and interest was incorrect. The correct parcel number
with the adjusted tax burden is Parcel Number W-4204 in the amount forgiven of
$447,288.62, which is the tax owed including interest and fees.
