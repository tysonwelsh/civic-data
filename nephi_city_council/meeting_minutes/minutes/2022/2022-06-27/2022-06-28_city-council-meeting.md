PUBLIC HEARING:
Mayor Justin Seely opened the public hearing to accept public comment on the proposed fiscal year
2022 Amended Budget. City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed each account being amended.
No public comment was given.
Mayor Seely closed the hearing at 7:10 p.m.

WORK SESSION CONVENED AT 7:10 p.m.

    1.    The council discussed the Hive Agreement draft. Mr. Atkinson reviewed the building
         replacement schedule and costs. He estimates that it will require approximately $130,000
         annually to be put into the building replacement fund. These costs would be split between the
         school district and the city. Councilor Memmott suggested that a minimum amount be set in
         the agreement for this fund. Attorney Kasey Wright indicated that there were a few revisions
         that still needed to be worked through.

    2. The council discussed the Concept Zoning Map.

    3. The council discussed participation in the UMPA funded lawnmower replacement program. The
       council directed staff to use 40% of the funds for the lawnmower replacement program and 60%
       of the funds for LED street lighting.

    4. Mr. Tony Perides addressed the council about his son being bit by a dog running at large. The
       officer that helped him with this incident said that he felt he was not properly trained when
       dealing with vicious dogs. Mr. Perides went and visited with the animal control office Shirlene
       Buckley and she offered the idea of increasing the fines for violators whose dogs are running at
       large. The council directed staff to follow up if citations are being consistently written for dogs
       running at large.

    5. Mr. Atkinson asked the council about increasing swimming pool fees to help off-set the increase
       in the lifeguard and swimming pool worker wages. The council directed staff to bring back a
       master fee resolution with fee changes for the upcoming fiscal year.

    6. Councilor Callaway gave an update on the Mammoth Parade progress. He said he had notified
       the Grand Marshalls of the parade and they were very excited about the honor.

    7. Councilor Parady said that the Chamber of Commerce now has 74 paying members. City
       Recorder Lisa Brough said as soon as they reach 75 members, they can receive the remainder of
       the funding the City has pledged for their organization.

REGULAR COUNCIL MEETING CONVENED AT 8:00 p.m.

Mayor Seely opened the regular council meeting.

FISCAL YEAR 2022 AMENDED BUDGET ADOPTED:

  Councilor Parady moved to adopt the proposed Amended Budget for fiscal year ending June 30, 2022.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

MEETING ADJOURNED:
   Councilor Worwood moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual and the acquisition and disposition of real property. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Meeting adjourned at 8:02 p.m.
