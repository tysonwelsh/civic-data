2.6) Public hearing and recommendation of Gardner Plumb’s request for review of the
Main Street Meadows Zone Change from A-5 (agriculture) to R-1-22
(residential/agriculture) on 49 acres located at approximately 2500 West Main Street.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Howard Cook with Gardner Company, was present. There is not a site plan right
now as they are trying to rezone to agriculture.
Commissioner Newall opened the public hearing.

Todd Rees stated he has some concerns. He is pleased that Gardner is talking about lower density.
He is worried about the applicant changing the zone later. He is also concerned about the PUD
with 88 units, making areas denser because they must leave open space.

Jan Morris asked if there was a way it could be figured out before any changes were made and
then the community could vote on it, instead of the zone changes being made and then the density
being changed. She stated putting in a lot of homes in this section would be concerning because
there isn't a backroad in Jordan Willows. There are only three exits for Jordan Willows, and if
there was any kind of disaster, they are locked in. If more homes were put in that area there would
be issues. She thinks for the lot sizes that are there they wouldn't like any more people, especially
with an elementary school with more children.

Ron French is involved with ASD and the bond measure was turned down He would like to see
more schools before more homes are approved.

Lorie (inaudible) is concerned about the changes in the zone. She is concerned about the farm
industry being there. If the changes are being made now, will it change again and is her property
going to be protected.

Commissioner Newall acknowledged the online comments.

Commissioner Newall closed the public hearing.

Brittney addressed the zone change. If there is a zone change, the public will be noticed. The
applicant is not required to show staff what they are doing with the property if a zone change
application if filed. The lot configuration is determined by open space per a PUD. Connectivity
There is a road stub on the south end of Jordan Willows. 700 South is also a main route. It would
add more traffic. It would provide more ways in and out of the neighborhood. This has been part
of the General Plan and signals have been added to help. As a city, we cannot force or require ASD
to hold up development. The City does meet with ASD to collaborate, but over time we will have
to accommodate growth, hopefully voting for a bond. Commissioner Newall addressed the
concerns of the public as well as the staff.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to recommend approval of Gardner Plumb’s
        request for review of the Main Street Meadows Zone Change from A-5 (agriculture)
        to R-1-22 (residential/agriculture) on 49 acres located at approximately 2500 West
        Main Street; including all DRC comments and all findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Jackson seconded. All in favor.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Carlson,
        yes; Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

lifestyle changed. He is concerned about the noise and the buffering and the traffic. His request
would be to not accept, and to turn the carwash and do it the way the City has recommended and
turn it East and West, rather than North and South. He would hope these things would be taken
into consideration.

2.11) Recommendation of Reza Sanati’s request for review of the Cooper Hollow Phase 4
concept, a 12-unit townhome development located at 460 North 1200 East
*2.10 and 2.11 were heard simultaneously
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicants, Roger Dudley and Reza Sanati were present. The Cedar Hollow neighborhood
would like to combine the HOA and have a seemingly comparable home type. They stated they
would like the HDR as opposed to MDR. They are happy to enter into a development agreement.
Reza Sanati spoke about not having a specific number of homes and is willing to sign a
development agreement.

Commissioner Newall opened public hearing.

Bill Greenhalgh owns the property that is south of the proposed development. There are several
issues with the developer, and they have maintained the property line that is shared with the
applicant. Bill’s property is well kept. The high density is not good, and he doesn't think 1200 East
can accommodate anymore traffic.

Don Watts owns the corner lot in the HOA. He has had major issues with the HOA and so has his
father in law. The MDR does not apply, and he does not feel like Mr. Davies was forthcoming
with the HOA information. The historical nature of the lots and the historical use have changed
with flag lots have been beneficial. He asked to please follow the general zone and not change.

Mark Urry will be behind this property line and said that when zoning is changed it will have a
ripple effect. He would really like to see the property upgraded because it is an eyesore, but within
the zoning designation. He would respectively request that this is denied.

Jason Bothe stated is in favor of the Coopers HOA. They are still in cooperation with Mr. Zenati.


John Collier lives near the proposed development. He is concerned with the high density. He is
concerned about more lots being added after an approval. He would like the zoning to be left the
same.

Karyn Comish is concerned with the traffic on 1200 East and stated it is awful.

Karen Chappell owns a home near the development. She is the Assistant Principal at North Point
Elementary near 2300 West and 2100 North. She sees the same traffic patterns near Lowes and
1200 East and is concerned that this same issue will happen when more homes are added when the
traffic is already horrific. If these town homes are developed, there will be a lack of parking, which
will lead to on street parking. There are many single people in the community, and those units will
be cohabitating where more vehicles will be added to each unit.

Jason Bothe stated with an HOA you can't park on street due to HOA laws.

Karen Chappell stated there are people who will pay the fines to still park on the street and rent
even when they aren't supposed to.

Jason Adamson stated there is zero parking in that proposed plan, especially for trucks. 1200 East
has hardly any shoulder.

Sheri Dotson drives on 1200 East and is concerned about the traffic and the obstruction of vision.
She stated it would increase the problem and would like to see the sense of community that has
been expressed.

Commissioner Newall acknowledged the online comments.

Commissioner Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion 2.10: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend denial of Reza Sanati’s
        request for review of the Sanati General Plan Amendment on 1.50-acres of property
        located at 460 North 1200 East changing the land use designation from LDR (low
        density residential) to HDR (high density residential); including the findings that it is
        contrary to the general plan and DRC Comments. The minimum is not in conformance
        and not consistent to land use and the adjoining properties. Commissioner Jackson
        seconded. All in favor.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Carlson,
        yes; Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

        Motion 2.11: Commissioner Jackson moved to deny Reza Sanati’s request for review of
        the Cooper Hollow Phase 4 concept, a 12-unit townhome development located at 460
        North 1200 East, based on the findings that 2.10 received a negative recommendation.
        Commissioner Carlson seconded. All in favor.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Carlson,
        yes; Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.
