                                                            Proposed Budget Amendment




March 18, 2025



Honorable Mayor and Council,

The City’s Administrative Services department submits the following proposed amendments to the
2024-2025 budget for your consideration:


1.   Adjust Budget for 13800 S Sidewalk and Crosswalk -
        Reason – There has been ongoing discussion among staff, some elected officials, and citizens
        about accelerating the timeline for the 13800 S (3700 W – 3800 W) sidewalk construction
        project. Initially, the City had planned for this project in its long-range plan, with an expected
        budget of $150,000 in the 2025-2026 budget year. However, several citizens have directly
        reached out and some elected officials have requested that the project be moved to the 2024-
        2025 budget year (the current fiscal year).
        In addition to this project, the City has been working with Bluffdale to establish plans for a mid-
        block crosswalk across 13800 S at the West end of CR Hamilton Park. Staff recommends we
        complete this crosswalk at the same time as completing the sidewalk project. As the crosswalk is
        partially into the Bluffdale side of the road, staff is working on an agreement with Bluffdale to
        split the costs. The agreement would also include the construction of a traffic signal at the
        intersection of 13800 S and 3600 W and will come back to the City Council at a later date.
        Staff is recommending Council approve the proposed amendment for $170,000 for both the
        crosswalk and sidewalk construction.
        Funding – The City had planned on using the 470 CIP fund to pay for this project, which is
        expected to have a $500,000 fund balance at year-end. Therefore, staff recommends using the
        470 CIP fund to fund this project. This amendment is not due to overspending but is to allocate
        funds for a project not originally budgeted in the FY2024-2025 budget.
        Proposed Amendment:

                47303052-480300-25002 - Increase the budgeted expenditure by $170,000 to pay for
                 the project.
                First Class Roads Capital Projects Fund - Use of Fund Balance - Increase the budgeted use
                 of fund balance by $170,000 to pay for the project.
