Convene as the Governing Board of the North Summit Special Recreation District (2:55 PM)
1)
Notification of the North Summit Recreation Special Service District's intent to levy a tax rate which exceeds the District's certified tax rate in accordance with Utah Code Section 59-2-919(3)(a). The District intends to increase 2024 property taxes in the amount of $75,078.35, which is an approximate 70.49% increase, for the purpose of funding a full-time Director position, funding a part-time Assistant Director position, keeping up with the rising costs of goods and services, overseeing the construction of Beacon Hill Park phase one, and providing for the operations and maintenance of Beacon Hill Park (once opened); Charity Richins (2:55 PM)
Attachment: NS Recreation Notification of Intent-corrected.pdf
Deputy Attorney Ryan Stack explained the notice to increase the tax rate for the North Summit Recreation Special Service District. (2:55 PM)
Council Members asked questions. Deputy Stack and North Summit Recreation Special Service District Board Chair Charity Richins responded. (2:58 PM)
