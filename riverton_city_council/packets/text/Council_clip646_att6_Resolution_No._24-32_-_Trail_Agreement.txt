                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-32

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
   WITH THE WELBY JACOB WATER USERS COMPANY GRANTING PART OF
          THEIR PROPERTY FOR A PUBLIC RECREATION TRAIL

      WHEREAS, Riverton City is planning construction of a public recreation trail along the
Welby Jacob Canal and must be granted use of the property by the Welby Jacob Water Users
Company to do so; and

       WHEREAS, a public recreation trail will benefit public recreation and enjoyment; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that a Trail Agreement has
been prepared for approval and execution by and between the Welby Jacob Water Users
Company and Riverton City, which states the rights, duties, responsibilities, and obligations of
the parties described in this Resolution.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Welby Jacob
          Water Users Company, in substantially similar form to the agreement attached hereto,
          upon verification that the agreement has been approved as to legal form by the City
          Attorney.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 19th day of
March 2024 by the following vote:
