Chair Silvestrini called the meeting to order.

1. Discussion and Consideration of Resolution 19-15, Approving a Participation
Agreement with Clearwater Development; Mike Winder, Economic Development
Director
Mike Winder said the Agency considered the project initially in March. He highlighted a
map of the West Millcreek Community Development Area (CDA). He said the proposed
project would exceed open space requirements without including the park. He showed a table
with project cost estimates and said the total site improvement costs would be capped at
$3,356,646. Winder said the tax increment financing reimbursement would be calculated
annually as follows:
¢ Property taxes paid in a given year minus property taxes paid in 2019.
¢ 20% subtracted from that sum (since this CDA has an 80% pass through to the
agency and 20% must go to the taxing entities, per the original CDA agreement). This
gives us the TIF pass-through amount.
* 4% of the TIF pass-through amount must go to the agency for administration, per the
original CDA agreement

* 20% of the TIF pass-through amount must go to the Salt Lake County Affordable
Housing Fund, per the original CDA agreement

* The remaining 76% of the TIF pass-through shall go to Opus Green as reimbursement
for their park improvements as outlined once they are completed. The TIF
reimbursement amount will be capped at $3,356,646.

Winder said Millcreek would get a new 1.15 acres park along Big Cottonwood Creek paid
for by the tax increment of a new project (no general funds), tax increment dollars to
supplement administrative costs and to enhance the larger west Millcreek area, and a good
project made great because of partnership. The developers would get a 1.15-acre park
alongside their development that is maintained by the City which would be reimbursed by the
tax increment of their new project with interest. Chair Silvestrini said the project represents
an investment of $3.3 million in Millcreek. Winder said other properties in the area were
developing.

Micah Peters, Clearwater Homes CEO, said the billing to the City for the park would be at-
cost and the developers would not exceed the cost estimates.

Chair Silvestrini asked for public comment. There were no comments. John Brems clarified
that the total improvement cost estimate was $2.3 million plus $1 million for interest.

Board Member Jackson moved to adopt Resolution 19-15 approving a participation
agreement amended in paragraph 1.5 to correct the name to Big Cottonwood Creek with
Clearwater Development. Board Member Catten seconded. The Recorder called for the
vote. All Board Members voted yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2. Approval of June 10, 2019 Meeting Minutes

Board Member Uipi moved to approve item 2, approval of the minutes. Board Member
Jackson seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes. The
motion passed unanimously.

ADJOURNED: Board Member Uipi moved to adjourn the meeting at 8:12 p.m. Board
Member Jackson seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted
yes. The motion passed unanimously.
