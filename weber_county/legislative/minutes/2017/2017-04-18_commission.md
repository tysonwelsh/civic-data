E.1.PRESENTATION ON THE WEBER COUNTY CLERK/AUDITOR'S OFFICE
   Ricky Hatch, Weber County Clerk/Auditor, stated that the Clerk/Auditor’s Office (C/A) is a workhorse type
   of office. He outlined the tremendous amount of work done by the various divisions in his office that include
   Elections, auditing/budgeting and Clerk functions. He spoke of the critical work done by Elections, which is
   accomplished fairly, competently and accurately. Every year there are about 40 bills in the Utah Legislature
   specifically dealing with Elections. The auditing/budgeting arm encompasses many complex functions. The
   CAFR has won awards from the GFOA consistently for the past 23 years. The Clerk is the official record
   keeper for the county, handles GRAMA requests, minutes for different boards, marriage licenses (about
   2,500/year), passports (about 5,500/year), property tax relief applications (2,000+/year), property valuation
   appeals, conducts the tax sale, manages tax rates (over 50 entities in the county), handles all redevelopment
   agencies in the county, etc. Mr. Hatch stated that the office is based on three pillars of ideas: 1) The C/A
   strives to provide customers with world class customer service (he read a recent letter that is typical of others
   received and which spoke to the “extremely pleasurable experience” with the C/A’s Office), 2) Continuous
   Improvement, and 3) Communication and Team Work. Chair Ebert recognized Mr. Hatch who was recently
   selected as a panelist, and who presented at a public hearing at the federal level for the U.S. Elections
   Assistance Commission on Elections critical infrastructure, which he stated reflects on the leadership of the
   C/A. Chair Ebert noted that of about 3,100 counties, our County Clerk/Auditor was selected because of his
   amazing abilities, forward thinking, etc. He thanked Mr. Hatch for representing this county and state in such
   an amazing way.
   Chair Ebert stated that the County Commission started highlighting a county department once a month as a
   way to inform the community about the services that the departments provide. Along with this, a staff
   member is recognized for outstanding customer service and has use of Chair Ebert’s parking spot for the
   month. Mr. Hatch recognized Fátima Fernelius as someone who is steady, works hard, is competent, has
   tremendous knowledge of the services the county provides, and constantly asks how to help others. The
   commissioners expressed appreciation to the Clerk/Audit staff for everything that they do.
