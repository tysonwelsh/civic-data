17. DENY (OR APPROVE) AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND THE FOLLOWING SECTION
14.12.C.9 OF THE UTAH COUNTY LAND USE ORDINANCE; A REQUEST TO REMOVE THE
REQUIREMENT FOR PLANNED SUBDIVISIONS TO PROVIDE A CERTIFICATION THAT
LANDS BEING SUBDIVIDED ARE FREE FROM INVASIVE NOXIOUS WEEDS OR HAVE
UNDERGONE APPROPRIATE NOXIOUS WEED CONTROL TREATMENT.

-Greg Robinson, Community Development

GREG ROBINSON, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT PLANNER - THIS WAS CONTINUED
TO REVIEW SOME OF THE INFORMATION. I HOPE WE PROVIDED YOU ENOUGH
INFORMATION THROUGH THE WEEK TO ANSWER ANY OF THOSE QUESTIONS.

RICHARD NIELSON, DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC WORKS - I'VE TALKED BRIEFLY WITH
EACH OF YOU ON THIS. THE THINGS THAT WE’VE LOOKED AT FROM WHERE THE
WEEDS FUNCTION AS PART OF THE PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT, WE FEEL LIKE
THERE’S WAYS TO HELP THE LANDOWNERS FIND A WAY TO ACCOMPLISH WHAT
WE NEED TO DO TO BE WITHIN THE WEED ORDINANCE WITHOUT USING
CHEMICAL SPRAYS ON THEIR PROPERTY. THERE ARE OTHER ALTERNATIVES. WE
ARE HAPPY TO GO OUT AND MEET WITH THE INDIVIDUAL LANDOWNERS ON IT.
ONE OF THE THINGS THAT WE LOOK AT IF WE WERE TO CHANGE THIS
ORDINANCE AND NOT HAVE THE WEED CERTIFICATIONS IT CAN HAVE A
DETRIMENTAL EFFECT ON THE NEIGHBORING AGRICULTURAL USES, ESPECIALLY
THOSE THAT ARE FURTHER DOWN THE IRRIGATION DITCH. SOME OF THE AREAS
WE HAVE FLOOD IRRIGATION SO IF YOU HAVE A WHEAT INFESTATION
UPSTREAM FROM YOU, THOSE SEEDS GET IN THE DITCH AND GO ON DOWN AND
CREATE A PROBLEM ON SOMEONE ELSE’S PROPERTY AND GET INTO
PRODUCTION ISSUES.

COMMISSIONERS, RICHARD NIELSON AND GREG ROBINSON DISCUSSED SIMPLE
METHODS THAT COULD BE DONE TO TAKE DOWN THE WEEDS AND ASSIST WITH
THE CERTIFICATION AS WELL AS FOLLOWING THE GUIDELINES SET FORTH IN
THE STATE LAW.

ROBERT STEVENS, PROPERTY OWNER - I WOULD LIKE TO REQUEST THAT BRIAN
VOEKS (SENIOR POLICY ADVISOR TO COMMISSIONER LEE) SUBMIT A DOCUMENT
THAT I'VE WORKED ON ENTITLED, ‘45 ROADBLOCKS FOR SUBDIVISION
APPROVAL IN UNINCORPORATED UTAH COUNTY’ (AVAILABLE UPON REQUEST).
ACCORDING TO THE UTAH STATE NOXIOUS WEED ACT, WE ARE ALREADY
COMPLETELY IN COMPLIANCE BECAUSE WE CLEARED, SLOPED, LASER LEVELED
AND DISSED EVERY ONE OF OUR PARCELS. ON SOME OF THEM WE REPLANTED
WITH A SEED MIX, BUT BECAUSE OF THE DROUGHT UNFORTUNATELY THAT
GRASS DIED AND WEEDS TOOK OVER. YOU NEED TO UNDERSTAND WHAT WEEDS
- EACH PLANT IS CAPABLE TO PRODUCING THOUSANDS OF SEEDS AND MAY STAY
IN THE SOIL FOR YEARS. I'VE BEEN IN FREQUENT CONTACT WITH THE UTAH
STATE EXTENSION SERVICE AND THEIR SOLUTIONS ALL REQUIRE HERBICIDES
AND THESE ARE TOXIC HERBICIDES WHICH IS NOT A SOLUTION. I'VE BEEN IN
CONTACT WITH OUR RANCHER TED ALLEN WHO RENTS OUR PROPERTY. HE SAID
HE WILL CUT AND BAIL THE GRASS AND FEED IT TO HIS LIVESTOCK BECAUSE IT’S
HARD TO FIND ANYTHING ELSE RIGHT NOW WITH ALMOST EVERYTHING DEAD
AND HE THINKS THEY'LL EAT IT. WE DID ALL THIS EXCAVATION WORK IN

DECEMBER AND AGAIN IN APRIL AND THIS SEPTEMBER THE WEEDS FULLY
OCCUPIED THE LAND SO TO ASK SOMEBODY TO GO AND COMPLY WITH THE

COMMISSIONER GARDNER - I THINK THAT MR. STEVENS RAISES SOME GREAT
POINTS. I HAVE LIVED ON COUNTY LAND BEFORE AND SEEN SOME OF THE ISSUES
THAT CAN CAUSE DISCONTENT FOR THOSE OF US THAT HAVE LIVED ON COUNTY
LAND AND OWN PROPERTY ON COUNTY LAND. I THINK THAT MOVING FORWARD
AND LOOKING AT THIS, I THINK THAT ALL 3 OF US COMMISSIONERS ARE MORE
THAN WILLING TO TAKE A GOOD LOOK AT OUR ZONING LAWS AND OUR
REQUIREMENTS AND MAKE SURE THAT WE ARE BEING AS ACCOMMODATING AS
POSSIBLE. WHEN IT COMES TO A FEW THINGS LIKE THIS, WE ARE BOUND BY
STATE LAW. WE AS A COUNTY DERIVE OUR AUTHORITY FROM THE STATE. WE
ARE A POLITICAL SUBDIVISION OF THE STATE AND WE THEREFORE ARE
BEHOLDEN TO STATE LAW AND STATE LAW DOES REQUIRE THERE BY SOME
SORT OF A REQUIREMENT. I’M VERY HAPPY WITH WHAT I HEARD FROM OUR
PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT THAT THEY ARE WILLING TO COME OUT TO WORK
WITH YOU TO FIND WAYS TO REDUCE THE BURDEN ON THE HOMEOWNER AS
MUCH AS POSSIBLE. RIGHT NOW WE ARE BEHOLDEN TO STATE LAW SO WE HAVE
TO HAVE SOME REQUIREMENT. I THINK THAT UNTIL WE MOVE FORWARD AND
SEE IF WE CAN LESSEN SOME OF THESE BURDENS, THE LEAST WE CAN DO IS
WORK WITH HOMEOWNERS AND FIND A WAY TO MAKE THINGS HAPPEN AND
REDUCE THE BURDEN AS MUCH AS POSSIBLE. IT SOUNDS LIKE IF YOU HAVE A
RANCHER WHO’S WILLING TO MOVE AND GATHER THAT UP, LET’S SCHEDULE AN
INSPECTION FOR RIGHT AFTER HE DOES THAT AND THEN YOU'LL BE FINE.

COMMISSIONERS, ROBERT STEVENS, ROB MOORE (COUNTY ATTORNEY), BRYCE
ARMSTRONG (COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT), GREG ROBINSON AND RICHARD

MAINTAINING THE STATUE (4-17-112).
