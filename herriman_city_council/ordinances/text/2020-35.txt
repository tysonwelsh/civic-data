HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2020- 35

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF HERRIMAN AMENDING AND
ADOPTING CERTAIN CHANGES TO TITLE 10 OF THE HERRIMAN CITY CODE
TO INCLUDE WATER EFFICIENCY STANDARDS.

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Council (the “Council”) met in regular session on
December 9, 2020, to consider, among other things, an ordinance amending the City Code to
include changes to Chapter 23 regarding Landscaping and to add a new chapter on City-wide
water efficiency standards (the “Water Efficiency Standards”); and

WHEREAS, the City is authorized by law to exercise legislative powers and enact
ordinances establishing regulations for land use within the limits of the City; and

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”) has
provided the appropriate notice and held a public hearing regarding the proposed amendments to
Title 10, Chapter 23 and the addition of new Water Efficiency Standards (collectively the
“Proposed Amendments”) as required by Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-502; and

WHEREAS, the Council properly noticed and held a public hearing on the Proposed
Amendments and has considered the same as recommended by the Planning Commission; and

WHEREAS, the Council finds that it is in the best interest of the residents of Herriman
to adopt the Proposed Amendments as set forth herein.

NOW THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Herriman City Council as follows:

A. Title 10, Chapter 23, Sections 3 and 5 of the Herriman City Code be amended as set
forth in the Attached Exhibit A. All other sections and provisions of Title 10, Chapter 23 shall be
unaffected by this amendment.

B. Title 10 of the Herriman City Code shall be amended to add Section 32 as set forth in
the attached Exhibit B.

C. The Water Efficiency Standards, that may be administratively amended from time to
time, shall also be adopted as an Appendix to the Herriman City Engineering Standards Vol. 7.

D. This ordinance assigned no. 2020-35, shall take effect 90 days after approval.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Council this 9" day of December, 2020.

Me. Mayor —
